# TrimLine orders second AIDA servo press

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

TrimLine, an IATF 16949-approved U.S. automotive supplier and high-volume metal stamper specialising in the design and fabrication of high-precision tooling, has ordered its second AIDA 630-ton progressive die, direct-drive servo press (model DSF-M2-6300). Its first 630-ton AIDA servo press was installed at its LaGrange, Ohio, facility earlier this year.

"The benefits that TrimLine is reaping on its first DSF-M2-6300 press helped to justify a second duplicate servo press purchase to meet its customers' increased demand. This includes taking advantage of the servo press's pendulum motion capability to achieve a 25-30% spm increase over its previous mechanical press production method. TrimLine would be able to produce parts even faster at a rate of 51spm, if not for a stacking bottleneck at the end of the press as its AIDA servo press outperforms Trimline's material handling equipment," AIDA told ISMR.■
