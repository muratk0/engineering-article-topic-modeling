# Borgward orders servo press line

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

THE TRADITIONAL GERMAN car brand, Borgward has been successfully launched into the Chinese market with its BX7 model. To increase production capacity, Borgward Automotive ordered an XL servo press line from Schuler at the end of last year. From August 2018 onwards, the five-station servo press line, based in Beijing-Miyun, will produce body panels for the BX7, BX5 and BX6 models. The line, with a press force of 67,000 kilonewtons, comprises five presses and is fully supplied by Schuler, including the corresponding automation. The line features ServoDirect technology for better energy efficiency.

Up to 16 large parts per minute are produced by the Servoline 16. Double and quadruple parts can also be manufactured, as needed, in higher batch quantities. The simulation program, which is also included within the scope of the order, ensures that operators are provided with the right alignment of all movements of single presses and automation, including destackers, Crossbar feeders for parts transport and exit conveyor. Fast, fully automated replacement of dies and tooling produces the flexibility needed for rapid product changes.

Except for a few special components, Schuler manufactures and assembles the modules for the line – beds, uprights, slides and crowns – at its Chinese production facility in Dalian. See www.schulergroup.com and www.borgward.com
