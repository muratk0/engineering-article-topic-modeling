# Yih Shen Machinery Co., Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Established in 1979, YSM International (Yih Shen Machinery Co. Ltd.) in Taiwan has become a global specialist in stamping and bending manufacturing solutions. It delivers fully tooled-up manufacturing processes that are pre-tested and production-ready. This includes stamping and bending solutions integrated with operations needing a secondary process such as thread forming; welding; sub-part assembly; riveting etc. All these solutions are delivered on a single production line.

It has designed its stamping and forming machines to be modular (depending upon part/application complexity). Machines can be set up and each operation run independently so that several processes can be integrated into one machine.

"YSM-CNC 39T is our three-axis, servo forming machine which processes strip from 0.8-3mm thick (max. strip width of 60mm and wire diameter of Ø1.5-6.5mm). Due to its three-axis CNC structure, it is flexible for mould changes.

This type of CNC forming machine is good at keep the production line agile," it said.
