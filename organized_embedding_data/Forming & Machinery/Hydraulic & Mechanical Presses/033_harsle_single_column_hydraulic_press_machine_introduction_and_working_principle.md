**Working principle:**

A hydraulic press is a device that uses liquid to transmit pressure. When the liquid transmits pressure in a closed container, it follows Pascal's law.

The hydraulic transmission system of the hydraulic press is composed of power mechanism, control mechanism, executive mechanism, auxiliary mechanism and working medium.

Power mechanism Usually an oil pump is used as the power mechanism, generally a positive displacement oil pump. In order to meet the requirements of the moving speed of the actuator, one oil pump or multiple oil pumps are selected. Gear pump for low pressure; vane pump for medium pressure; plunger pump for high pressure.

Hydraulic machines usually refer to hydraulic pumps and hydraulic motors. Both hydraulic pumps and hydraulic motors are energy conversion devices in the hydraulic system. The difference is that the hydraulic pump converts the mechanical energy of the driving motor into the pressure energy of the oil, which is the power device in the hydraulic system. The hydraulic motor converts the pressure energy of the oil into mechanical energy and is the actuator in the hydraulic system.

The hydraulic pumps and hydraulic motors commonly used in hydraulic systems are both positive displacement, and their working principle is to use the change of the sealed volume to suck and press the oil. In terms of working principle, most hydraulic pumps and hydraulic motors are reciprocal, that is, when pressure oil is input, the hydraulic pump becomes a hydraulic motor, which can output speed and torque, but in terms of structure, hydraulic pumps and hydraulic motors are still Some differences.

The single-column hydraulic press has an independent power mechanism and electrical system. It adopts button centralized control. It can adjust the working pressure, pressing speed of the machine, manual and semi-automatic, and the stroke and range of no-load fast down and deceleration. The process needs to be adjusted, and the ejection process can be completed. There are three process methods: ejection process and stretching process. Each process is a fixed pressure and fixed process for selection. The fixed pressure forming process has an Ejection delay and automatic return. The single-column hydraulic press has a wide range of versatility and is suitable for the processing and forming of various plastic materials, such as extrusion, bending, hemming, stretching, etc. At the same time, the hydraulic press can also be used for the compression forming of various plastics and powder products. In addition, the single-column hydraulic press can also be used for product correction, pressing, and shaping.
