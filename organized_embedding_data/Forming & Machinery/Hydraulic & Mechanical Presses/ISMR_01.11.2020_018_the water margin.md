# THE WATER MARGIN

OMAX Corporation’s general manager, Mike Ruppenthal, outlines its latest new initiatives to ISMR.

ISMR SAYS:

“OMAX has launched a virtual trade show and a new OMAX TV service.”

Based in Kent, Washington, OMAX Corporation (a Hypertherm company) is a global specialist in advanced abrasive waterjet systems designed to cut most materials and thicknesses with speed and accuracy. The cold-cutting process of waterjet machining eliminates thermal stress in the workpiece material, preventing the hardening and warping that often occurs during conventional cutting processes.

Owner of the OMAX, MAXIEM, GlobalMAX and ProtoMAX brands, the manufacturer provides a comprehensive selection of JetMachining Centers which feature intuitive software controls and incorporate efficient pump technology.

The ISO 9001:2015 certified company designs, manufactures, assembles and tests components as a complete system. The company has also developed a comprehensive service and support network to keep its customers ahead of the manufacturing curve.

ISMR caught up with Mike Ruppenthal, OMAX Corporation’s general manager, who outlined its latest new initiatives to ISMR.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes or achievements and the reasons for this?

MR: OMAX is a customer-focused company resourced with a tenured waterjet engineering team and a research and development team of leading industry experts. Recently, our team received an award for best paper on “Particle Velocity in Abrasive Waterjets”. We are always trying to find ways to make waterjet more effective and useful for our end-users. Our abrasive waterjet technology enables shops to cut virtually any material with tight tolerances, no heat-affected zones and no extended set-up procedures.

We are delighted to announce that OMAX TV is now live at OMAX.com. OMAX TV offers a great way to access all the great video content that we have produced to demonstrate abrasive waterjet technology, waterjet applications and materials capabilities. The website originally went online in early July 2020 for testing before its official launch. OMAX TV is a wonderful, easy-to-use tool for anyone interested in learning about abrasive waterjet technology.

We also ran a virtual trade show on 1 October 2020 to highlight the benefits of waterjet technology to attendees.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals? What about the climate in 2021?

MR: We have seen a shift in our customers work from industries negatively impacted by COVID-19, such as travel, to industries that are thriving in the current climate, such as logistics and healthcare. As for 2021, we think that business will continue to evolve with the pandemic and the direction that it takes.

From what we have seen, the business climate tripped up in the early spring and summer months but has recovered well. We believe that we will continue to see a growth trend throughout 2021.

ISMR: How has the COVID-19 crisis affected your company’s operations and which steps have you taken in response?

MR: OMAX is going strong. Early on, OMAX was designated as an essential business and we quickly developed programmes to ensure the health and wellbeing of our employees, families and our community, as well as the continuity of our operations.

We run daily health checks for employees, supply PPE, provide daily cleaning procedures and offer vendor health check programmes. OMAX also has a cross-functional pandemic team that meets weekly to discuss local and state regulations.

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus for the company right now?

MR: OMAX was founded to provide precise, easy-to-use, cost-effective, standardised, reliable and high-quality abrasive-jet machining systems to the manufacturing industry, with strong aftersales services, and that focus has not changed.

We are investing in our customer service resources to improve the ownership experience for customers. OMAX also has several projects in place to improve core technologies, pumps, drive systems and software on our immediate roadmap.
