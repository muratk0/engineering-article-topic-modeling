# Peak Performer is a hit at Liebherr

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

A PEAK PERFORMER part levelling machine from KOHLER has been in operation at Liebherr-Hydraulikbagger GmbH since 2018, where it delivers level and low-stress steel blanks.

Hans Liebherr founded the company in 1949 and today, 70 years later, not only is Liebherr one of the biggest construction machinery manufacturers in the world, but the company is also successful in a number of other industries. Its wide product range now covers eleven business areas. Around 46,000 people work for the family-run group across more than 130 companies at sites on every continent. At the company's first site, in Kirchdorf an der Iller (between Lake Constance and Ulm), Liebherr-Hydraulikbagger GmbH manufactures an extensive range of hydraulic excavators, articulated dump trucks and material handlers.

"We need to ensure that our steel blanks, some of which are large and solid, are exactly level and low stress, to maintain the high quality standards of our products," explained Stefan Richter, Head of Steel Construction, Liebherr. Working with Project Manager Torben Handeck, who is in charge of programming and welding technology in the Steel Construction department, Stefan Richter set about finding a powerful and economical part levelling machine and chose a type 120P Peak Performer.

"The machine's hydraulic-free machine concept with direct drive, reversible levelling rollers and electromechanical levelling gap control tipped the balance," explained KOHLER. Steel workers in Stefan Richter and Torben Handeck's team level steel blanks (S355J2+N and S355MC) with thicknesses ranging from 8mm to 40mm on the machine. Reversible levelling rollers mean that the service life of the levelling rollers can be extended by up to 50%, particularly when levelling comparatively narrow parts.

Around 300 tons of laser, plasma and flame-cut steel is levelled on the machine each month for constructing the upper- and undercarriages of hydraulic excavators and material handlers – in a twoshift operation.

The advanced cleaning system developed by KOHLER also pays off for Liebherr-Hydraulikbagger GmbH. To clean the machine's rollers, first the machine operator moves the motor-driven top roller frame upwards. The operator can then slide either the top or the bottom guide plate with the supporting rollers and leveling rollers out of the machine, powered entirely by an electric motor. Particles of dirt, added KOHLER, can be removed quickly and easily.

Stefan Richter and Torben Handeck requested a second display for machine operation, allowing the machine to be operated from both sides of the roller path and shortening the distances for the machine operator which, in turn, speeds up the levelling passes. The machine can be operated intuitively, using an Expert Calculation System to set the levelling parameters.
