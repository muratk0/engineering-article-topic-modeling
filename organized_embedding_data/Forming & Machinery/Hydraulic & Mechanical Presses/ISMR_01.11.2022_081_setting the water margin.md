# Setting the water margin

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Since 1971, KMT Waterjet Systems has been a global specialist in waterjet technology and waterjet machinery. For decades, it has manufactured waterjet ultra-highpressure intensifier pumps (UHP) with water pressurised up to 90,000PSI (6.200 bar).

“Our line of waterjet intensifier pumps, pure water only or abrasive waterjet cutting nozzles, abrasive management systems and waterjet parts/components are integrated with OEM waterjet machines. KMT waterjet cutters are ideal for cutting metal; stone; glass; ceramic tile; granite; food; textiles; rubber and foam, along with many other solutions," it told ISMR.

At the recent EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, its PRO 50 pump unit was on display. It recently introduced the KMT Streamline PRO®90,000 PSI (6.200 bar) waterjet pump for rapid cutting applications. The PRO is available in various kilowatt pressures for a wide range of cutting applications: 37kW, 45kW and 93kW (which KMT Waterjet dubs "the world's fastest waterjet").

"The new STREAMLINE PRO®50 6.200- bar (37kW) pump is designed to significantly increase cutting productivity by up to 40% over industry standard 4.100 bar (37kW) cutting speeds," said KMT Waterjet.

“The PRO 6.200 bar (45kW) pump will significantly increase cutting productivity by 50-70% over industry standard 4.100 bar (37kW) cutting speeds. The PRO 6.200 bar (93kW) pump delivers twice the power to the abrasive waterjet tables by cutting products in up to half the time over conventional 4.100 bar (75kW) waterjet machining systems," it added.

The company's new NEO™60,000 PSI (4.100 bar) 50HP pump offers features such as proportional pressure control, a booster pump and soft-start motor to eliminate voltage spikes as well as threaded cylinder design, removable magnetic doors and patent-protected HP seal design.

KMT has also launched the new KMT Waterjet Cut Calculator 2.0 app with an on-demand webinar. The video is an informational session that showcases all the new features of the 2.0 version of the KMT waterjet calculator.

“The KMT Calculator now incorporates 25 materials, offers a wider range of substrate thicknesses, horsepower, orifice sizes and abrasive rates. A new calculator section demonstrates the features/benefits of cutting faster with an inside look at the exclusive KMT OEM Access segment. This shows the actual time differences cutting at 90,000PSI/6.200 bar vs. 60,000/4.100 bar, calculating speed/capacity; cost per part/job and abrasive consumption/savings. The KMT Cut Calculator app compares cutting at 6.200- bar to 4.100 bar to prove productivity levels," concluded the manufacturer.
