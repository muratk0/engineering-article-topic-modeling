# Bruderer launches two new presses

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Bruderer, a specialist manufacturer of highspeed presses, unveiled two new machines at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany, this November. Swiss stamping specialist, Bruderer (which is 80 years old), used the major industrial event to introduce the BSTA 710-220 and the BSTL 350-88 models (the latter designed to support customers involved in volume production of small and miniature components).

"As a fixed-stroke press, the BSTL comes into its own for precision as well as continuity and uses up to 30% less energy when compared to older machine models. Customers to our stand at Blechexpo were given a first look at the new technology that will help them remain competitive when tendering for mass production projects in the UK and overseas. They also saw how the press's control system can be accessed with different devices via a web browser, with the latest remote maintenance and cyber security," explained Bruderer.

Adrian Haller, Managing Director, Bruderer UK, commented: "The BSTL is the latest innovation from Bruderer and comes complete with a new-generation servo feed unit, which makes it the perfect press for customers involved in supplying electrification, communication and consumer electronics. Blechexpo was a great event to launch the machine and we have received significant interest so far. Thanks to its streamlined functions, the press offers an extremely strong investment-to-performance ratio, and this has been dominating conversations to date."

The second press launch is focused on delivering more space for complex stamping tools; the BSTA 710-220, said Bruderer, offers "the longest bed length (2.2 metres) of its machine type in the world." With automatic stroke adjustment and various options such as different stroke lengths, shut heights and press force monitoring (for example), the machine can be configured for individual customer requirements and is increasingly suitable for the emerging electrification world.

(L-R) Andreas Fischer, Adrian Haller and Reto Bruderer of Bruderer.

"The two launches are the perfect way to celebrate our 16th appearance at Blechexpo, one of the largest international events in our sector," said Reto Bruderer, CEO of Bruderer.

Bruderer provides precision stamping presses with press forces from 200 to 2500kN for stamping and forming simple and complex parts. The company has just started construction on its new 48,000 sq. ft. factory and showroom in Telford (UK).■
