Deep-hole drilling systems drill to depth up to 100 times diameter

Somex, a member of the Suhner Group, offers custommade deep-hole drilling systems capable of drilling up to 0.04 in. dia. and as deep as 100 times the diameter. These modules can be integrated directly into transfer machines, special-purpose machines, or conventional machines such as turning machines.

The deep-hole drilling process typically requires either a pilot drill 1.5 times the diameter or a drill guide bushing, which then is followed by the deep-hole drilling process. The design combines the guide bushing with a chip basket, allowing both items to travel in unison.

The movement of the guide bushing is air-driven, which helps to provide closer contact to the part while preventing the escape of the coolant medium.
