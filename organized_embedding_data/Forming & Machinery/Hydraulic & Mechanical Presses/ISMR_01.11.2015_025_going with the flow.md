# GOING WITH THE FLOW

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

At this year’s Blechexpo in Stuttgart, KMT Waterjet Systems introduced a new series of high pressure pumps for waterjet cutting – the STREAMLINE SL-VI.

ISMR SAYS

“ The demand for higher pressure waterjet cutting is increasing”

KMT Waterjet Systems is a leading multinational manufacturer of components for waterjet cutting systems. The company develops solutions for a wide range of industries and applications. Since 1971, it has been dedicated to long-term customer relationships. Continuous investment in innovation has made KMT Waterjet Systems a market leader in the waterjet cutting industry. High pressure pumps; cutting heads; abrasive systems; high pressure tubes; original spare parts and a global service network are among the key products and services of the company.

Over 40 years ago, KMT launched waterjet cutting with cutting nozzles and the Streamline SL-I pump delivering cutting pressure up to 55,000 PSI/3,800 bar. As waterjet technology demand grew, KMT released advanced precision cutting nozzles and pumps from the Streamline SL-II leading to its new Streamline SL-VI Series.

At this year’s Blechexpo in Stuttgart, it introduced a new series of high pressure pumps for waterjet cutting – the Streamline SL-VI. The new pump models are based on four frames in different sizes and give KMT customers a wide range of configuration possibilities. The series offers various configuration levels so that every pump can be tailored to the application. ISMR met Wolfgang Emrich, KMT’s sales and marketing director, and Vitus Pebalka, Director Product Management Aftermarket, at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart to catch up on the company’s latest initiatives.

## High pressure pump systems

“We offer the STREAMLINE SL-VI pump at three pressure levels. The PRO intensifier enables waterjet cutting at 6,200 bar. The model type PLUS operates at a pressure of 4,100 bar and the type STD allows for a pressure of 3,800 bar. The pumps will be available with different motor ratings. The configuration possibilities range from a model without electrical cabinet, doors and cover, for complete integration into the centralized control unit of an entire turnkey system, to basic stand-alone machines for occasional cutting needs and fully equipped pumps for the most demanding cutting applications,” Wolfgang Emrich, KMT’s sales and marketing director, told ISMR.

The first release of the Streamline® SL-VI Series is available with horsepower offerings of 30, 50 and 100 at 60,000 PSI. The SL-VI Series is a configurable designed water jet pump that can be custom-built based on a variety of options to meet different cutting requirements.

“The SL-VI is a modular series. We have a four frame concept – one is for the smaller 15hp pumps, the second is for pumps from 30-50hp, the third is for the SL-VI pump and the biggest one (frame four) is for the multiple intensifier pumps (125-200hp) which will be launched next year. The customer is free to choose the features he wants in the pumps because of the modular design – this also helps with lead times and offers more flexibility benefits,” continued Emrich.

## A customer focus

This year, KMT Waterjet Systems has been offering various new aftermarket services to support customers through the complete lifetime of their high pressure equipment and to ensure that they always get the best out of their products.

“We have decentralised our service structure so that we can be closer to the customer and quicker to respond to his needs. We support customers throughout a product’s service life for the highest possible uptimes. There has also been investment in additional manpower. We will be hiring a new service engineer in France and in the UK in the first half of next year. The company’s service offerings have been developed further to address individual needs even better than before. Our teams in different regions share their associated solutions for existing local demands so that customers can benefit,” explained Vitus Pebalka, Director Product Management Aftermarket.

“Waterjet is a clean technology. 6000bar technology is not only faster but also requires less energy per cut metre of material, less abrasive and less water so it is a cleaner process if you use higher pressures. Abrasive recycling systems also reduce the amount of waste e.g. 50% less dust and dirt etc. Of course, this also means cost savings for customers.”

KMT has seen improvements in European market economies this year and, it told ISMR , has achieved its European targets this year. Europe is one of the major markets for the company, while it also sells systems into other markets worldwide.

“The demand for higher pressure waterjet cutting is increasing. 6200 bar technology can increase cutting speeds by up to 50% and sometimes more. There is also a trend towards higher level cutting technologies for users who want higher reliability levels because they cannot afford any production interruptions. Conversely, however, we are also seeing requirements for lower-end technology from new market entrants who cannot afford highend solutions. We can supply these different ranges and now have more than 6000 installations across Europe,” Wolfgang Emrich told ISMR.
