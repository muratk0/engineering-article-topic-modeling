# Cutting, punching and bending

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

PRIMA POWER SOLUTIONS, that cover all stages of sheet metal processing, will be on show on its stand (Hall 3, booth 3504) at the forthcoming Blechexpo exhibition this November in Stuttgart. Visitors will be able to see:

- The servo-electric press brake with automatic tool-change storage, eP Genius 1030, making its worldwide debut.
- The 2D Laser Genius 1530 with 10kW fiber laser and Combo Tower Laser flexible storage system.
- The integrated punch-laser system, Combi Genius 1530, with LSR Loading and Stacking Robot.
- The servo-electric BCe Smart bending cell with new API (Advanced Profile Inspection) and DABA (Dynamic Adjustment of the Bending Angle) technologies.

## Bending

Making its world premiere at Blechexpo will be Prima Power's new bending solution, the eP Genius 1030, integrating the eP-1030 servo-electric press brake with automatic tool change storage. The result, it said, is a more versatile, faster and more reliable bending system, which eliminates manual set-up of the machine and allows the operator to commit his time and skills to preparation of the bending batches. This solution is especially suitable for small batches, where it is possible to estimate the machine set-up, and short cycle times become imperative to gain better market competitiveness.

The eP Genius press brake has 15 axes and a maximum tool capacity of up to 32 metres. The eP-1030 press brake included in the solution features a tonnage of 105 tons with a bending length of 3060mm; automatic CNC crowning; 5-axis back gauge and IRIS Plus angle control system on CNC-controlled motorised arms. The tool storage, which is the main innovation of this product, can accommodate a total of 32 metres of tools to be placed on eight tool-holder supports that move on three axes. It can handle tools (Wila-style) with a minimum length of 20mm up to 515mm. A rotating turret rotates the tools taken from storage for multiple processing possibilities.

## Laser and laser/punching technology

At Stuttgart, Prima Power will also display the Laser Genius 1530 fibre laser machine with 10kW laser, featuring piercing time of less than one second and new cutting nozzles to reduce gas consumption. The machine will be showcased with a Prima Power flexible storage system, integrating loading and unloading of the Combo Tower Laser.

“Laser Genius grows with customers' needs and can be integrated at later stages with other modules, such as the LST automatic sorting system to enhance the automation level of the production flow within the factory," explained Prima Power.

This year Prima Power celebrates 30 years of integrated laser-punch technology. At Blechexpo, it will showcase the Combi Genius 1530 with new eye safety protection mounted around the cutting head, which avoids the use of protective walls around the machine, resulting in greater accessibility and visibility of the system and simplified installation. It is available in two different performance categories: Pure, for efficient production with a lower level of investment, and Dynamic, displayed at the exhibition, for high productivity and efficiency.

“The turret of the Combi Genius can be customised and optimised for any requirement. Simultaneously, a record-breaking number of 384 tools can be inserted in the turret. Combi Genius features the Intelligent Ram, which shortens tool-change time and increases the number of tools in a turret, especially index tools, and can be equipped with Multi-Tool® stations to further increase the number of tools. Up to 300kN servo-electric punching force can be selected for the ram. The machine is equipped with a CF series 4kW fibre laser, developed and manufactured by Prima Power, and the LSR Loading and Stacking Robot for automatic material handling, up to stacking of workpieces onto pallets," the company told ISMR.

## Bending in focus

Prima Power will also exhibit the servo-electric BCe Smart bending cell at Blechexpo 2019. This is a servo-electric semi-automatic machine, suitable for single piece production; component kits; perforated material and sheet with large holes and embosses.

“This machine is designed for customers looking for a flexible and semi-automatic panel bender with a 'safety integrated' concept that allows the operator to focus only on value-added operations, aided by visual devices and sound signals. It comes with two new technologies - API, Advanced Profile Inspection (a camera device used to apply corrections to a bend to reach the target angle within the required tolerance) and DABA technology (Dynamic Adjustment of the Bending Angle), where corrective parameters are proposed in relation to the material variables allowing a considerable reduction in the time required to create a new panel," it told ISMR.

In an area of Prima Power's stand that is dedicated to software, Blechexpo visitors will be able to watch demonstrations of the new parametric functions of MasterBend, the new UI for Tulus Performance Reporting and the new cloud-based application Tulus Analytics, which offers customers a variety of analyses from machine data.

AT THE BLECHEXPO EXHIBITION in Stuttgart this November (Hall 8, Stand 8507), ROEMHELD will introduce an element for the fully automatic clamping of dies on the press ram, which can measure real-time data on clamping forces and transmit them to the machine control. The new series rapid clamping system, Flexline, is therefore suitable for predictive maintenance and use in Industry 4.0 applications.

The company will also showcase an overview of components for the efficient, rapid and safe transport and change of heavy dies. On display will be various transport carts, carrying consoles as well as roller or ball bars for machine tables. Clamping elements and components for optimising set-up times in sheet metal forming will also be on display. A selection of its latest clamping and changing technologies can also be seen live on an Andritz Kaiser press during the exhibition at the manufacturer's stand in the same hall.

Using a series of integrated sensors, the Flexline rapid clamping system determines process data on the press ram in real-time and forwards it to the machine control. The operator receives information on the real clamping force on the die at any time and is able to detect overloads on the clamping element, the occurrence of unusual tearing forces as well as die breakages and wear.
