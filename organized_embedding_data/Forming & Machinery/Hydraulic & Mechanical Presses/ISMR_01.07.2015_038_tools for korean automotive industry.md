# Tools for Korean automotive industry

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

WHEN IT WAS TIME to select a supplier of tools for press hardening, I.S. Tech, a Korean manufacturer of vehicle parts, decided on press manufacturer, AP&T. As a GM subcontractor, Korean-based I.S. Tech recognized the necessity of satisfying the growing demand for presshardened parts by increasing its own capacity with additional tools. Demand primarily pertained to B-pillars, C-pillars, tunnels, floor reinforcements and other structural parts.

The automotive industry is currently experiencing a sharp increase in the percentage of press-hardened parts in new car models. When it was time to select a supplier of tools, I.S. Tech chose AP&T.

AP&T received the first order for three tools at the beginning of 2014. An additional order was first placed for three more, and then an additional six in March 2015. A total of 12 tools have now been ordered, making this one of the largest individual tool orders ever in AP&T's history. AP&T's agent Dae Kyung International piloted the project through the entire process from tender to delivery on-site in Korea.

“Technically, the tool poses the greatest challenge in press hardening. The 900-degree blank is to be formed and cooled quickly and in a controlled manner to give the part the desired qualities. We have in-depth expertise of materials and methods as well as simulation and quality verification of the end product,”AP&T told ISMR.

## Eliminate ‘stock-outs’

FOSTER INDUSTRIAL has invested £15,000 to upgrade its Weldstock system, a sophisticated method of stock management designed to save time and money for its customers. The paperless system, which allows customers to hold their own stock of frequently used consumables such as welding wires and grinding discs, has been made faster and more flexible by the upgrade. As well as ending costly and inconvenient downtime caused by ‘stock outs’, it now has improved reporting and traceability functions to better predict requirements and react faster to changing needs.

“Weldstock was set up as a solution for customers who struggled to predict stock requirements in advance, leading to downtime and delays while they waited for deliveries. By analysing the most frequently used items and delivering them in a single consignment, we are able to ensure consistent stock levels while also providing cost savings,” explained Foster Industrial.
