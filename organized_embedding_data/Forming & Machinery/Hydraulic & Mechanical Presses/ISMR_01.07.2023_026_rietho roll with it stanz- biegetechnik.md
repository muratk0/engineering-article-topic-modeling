## Quality is machinedependent

There are many suppliers of slip bending rolls in the market, but RIETHO always chooses Schröder-Fasti for its spindle drives (without 1 follow-up movement) and solid, torsion-free machine bodies. Its recipe for success also lies in the roll geometry or, more precisely, in the type of crowning developed by Schröder-Fasti.

“As with crowning of the folding beam in a folding machine, crowning of the rolls helps to achieve even bending results thanks to the bending forces. Slip bending rolls are each specified for certain sheet thicknesses and radii because, if the crowning is incorrect, the sheet edges of a round bent sheet will diverge concavely (if the crowning is too small) or convexly (if the crowning is too large)," explained Schröder-Fasti.

One consequence of this is, for example, that an uneven tube cannot be welded cleanly. Slip bending rolls are therefore each suitable for a certain range of tasks. Schröder-Fasti clarifies this with its performance diagram, which shows which sheet thicknesses and folding lengths at which tensile strength values can reap high-quality results from a machine.

"In practice, users sometimes deviate from these specifications, depending upon the quality requirements of the workpiece, but ultimately the following applies.... only with the right machine can you achieve the requested radii in your sheet-metal parts," explained Schröder-Fasti.

## Two machines in one

RIETHO had been using a Fasti 125-10/1.0 with a 50mm top roll, a 136-25/2.5 with a 140mm top roll and another Schröder-Fasti slip bending roll with a working length of 1500mm and a roll diameter of 160mm for some time. The prospect of new orders and the need for a machine in the medium-performance range led the manufacturing service provider to order a slip bending roll Type 134-15/2 with a working length of 1500mm for 2mm thick sheets.

RIETHO normally processes steel sheets from 1-3mm in thickness. To ensure that already punched sheet metal and 1300N/mm² sheets for food technology products do not damage the rolls, the option of hardened polished rolls was chosen. Another equipment option was the change gearbox which enables the top roll to be exchanged.
