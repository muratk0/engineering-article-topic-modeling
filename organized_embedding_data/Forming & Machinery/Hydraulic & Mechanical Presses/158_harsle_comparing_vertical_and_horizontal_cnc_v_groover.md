### 1. Dimension
The dimensions of the two grooving machines are basically the same, but the vertical grooving machine is taller than the horizontal grooving machine, so the visual effect is slightly worse. Generally, store space is limited, and store customers tend to choose horizontal grooving machines.

### 2. Material Loading
2.1 First of all, since the front end of the vertical grooving machine is open, after the workpiece is processed, the back gauge will automatically.

The sheet metal is sent to the front end of the equipment, so it will be more convenient for the operator to get on and off the sheet material; secondly, because the workbench of the vertical grooving machine is narrow and the front support is designed with multiple universal balls, the movement of the sheet material on the platform is More flexible, more convenient and practical when processing thicker sheets.

2.2 Due to the large working platform of the horizontal grooving machine, when processing a whole plate or a relatively large sheet, loading and unloading will be more time-consuming and laborious; generally, there will be a protective film on the front of the stainless steel plate, so that when moving the sheet The protective film will form a friction barrier with the workbench surface. If thicker sheets are processed, moving the sheets will be extremely time-consuming and laborious.

### 3. Processing Range
Vertical grooving machine: processing 0.5-6mm thickness and 4000mm\*4000mm size sheets.

horizontal grooving machine: processing 0.5-4mm thickness, 4000mm\*1250mm size sheets.

### 4. Processing Speed
Since the tool holder of the vertical grooving machine is lighter, its running speed is relatively faster, and the vertical grooving machine has a double tool holder design, which will save a certain amount of man-hours when processing the density grooves of the entire board; due to the horizontal grooving machine The entire beam needs to move during processing, so the processing speed is lower than that of vertical grooving machines.

### 5. Energy Saving
Since the tool holder of the vertical grooving machine weighs about 300Kg, and the beam of the horizontal grooving machine weighs about 900kg, the power consumption of the main motor of the vertical grooving machine is lower than that of the horizontal grooving machine during processing.

### 6. Cost and Price
Since the vertical grooving machine has more parts, weight, processing technology, assembly technology, etc. than the horizontal grooving machine and is more complex, the cost price and sales price of the vertical grooving machine are higher than those of the horizontal grooving machine.

### 7. Workpieces and Grooves
7.1 The working principle of the vertical grooving machine is that the cross beam of the tool holder is fixed, and the sheet to be processed moves. During processing, the pressing plate is always pressed on the line where the sheet is processed. The working principle of the horizontal grooving machine is that the cross beam of the tool holder moves and is fixed by the processed sheet.

During processing, the side pressure plate and the front presser foot can only be pressed on one side and the planing end of the sheet. In this case, some laser cutting machines pre-cut sheets, irregular sheets or process skews, special-shaped grooves, etc., which cannot be completed by the horizontal grooving machine and can only be completed by the vertical grooving machine.

7.2 Since the tool holder of the vertical grooving machine is lighter than that of the horizontal grooving machine, and the working inertia between the two is also different, some fixed-point grooving tasks can only be completed by the vertical grooving machine.

7.3 Since the vertical grooving machine pulls or pushes the processed sheet material to move due to the back gauge, while the horizontal grooving machine processes the sheet material while it is fixed, so the horizontal grooving machine has certain advantages when processing the front and back grooving of the sheet material.
