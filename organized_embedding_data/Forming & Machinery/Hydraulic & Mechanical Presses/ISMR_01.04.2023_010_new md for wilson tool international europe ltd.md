# New MD for Wilson Tool International (Europe) Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

Andrew Blackwell has been appointed managing director of Wilson Tool International (Europe) Ltd. He started his career in mechanical engineering in the automotive sector and has held senior operational and commercial leadership positions in the aerospace, defence and industrial sectors.

"I'm excited to have joined a well-established company in Wilson Tool. It's well known in the punching and forming industries, focused on next day shipping of products across many OE ranges with high levels of customer service. Our aim is to keep increasing that range and expanding our press brake products with the same level of service that our customers expect and require in their own sectors," he explained.

Wilson Tool provides a wide range of tooling solutions for punching and bending around the globe with sales offices across Europe and press brake tooling manufacturing in Italy and the UK. Fifty years of growth has seen the company specialise in lean manufacturing processes and application training. Its Swindon headquarters has a Technical Centre with punching and bending facilities that customers can utilise to support development of their products and define their processes for challenging fabrication issues, with Wilson Tool engineering support.

Andrew takes over from Colin Blackwell who retires at the end of March after 41 years in the business.

"I would like to thank Colin on behalf of Wilson Tool for helping to build and grow the company into what it is today. It's a great legacy and one that I am proud to be guiding into the future," concluded Andrew Blackwell.
