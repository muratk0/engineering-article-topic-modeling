Gap-frame press designed for small-part stamping

Simpac America offers the CX series gap-frame press for production of small stamped parts for the appliance, automotive, and other general industries. Available as a manual or automated stand-alone press or as a fully automated tandem line, the press has a capacity of 80 to 300 tons.

The user-centered SPMS interface incorporates increased safety and expandability, as well as IIoT. The interface is freely movable with an integrated pendant and arm attachments. Progressive feeder sensors monitor quantitative measurements of refuels, injections, and adjustments in lubrication. New temperature sensors on the drive units help ensure preservation of the press and longevity of equipment lifespan.

Features include wide supporting four- and six-sided gibs and a wet-type clutch and brake assembly.

Electric upcut tube saws designed with quick-change mounting

Innovative Engineered Solutions Inc. (iES) has introduced a new line of electric upcut tube saws in two standard models. The eRB80 TS can cut tubes up to 90 mm dia., and the eRB150 TS can saw tubes up to 150 mm dia.

The saws are controlled with a standard Allen-Bradley or optional Siemens PLC/HMI and use an electric actuator for the saw blade, coupled with pneumatic clamping. The digital, Ethernet-based control system offers advanced diagnostics, maintenance reminders, and available production information. The saws are designed with quick-change mounting for rapid changeover of tube fixtures.

Robotic system offers fast, flexible press tending

ABB has introduced the IRB 760PT robotic press-tending system for press automation applications in the automotive industry. To help eliminate the risk of potential interference between the robot and the press, the system features a revised design that reduces interference in the robot’s fourth axis. This allows the robot to move freely without colliding with equipment such as press clamps, with no compromise for the overall working area and the robot’s freedom of movement.

Capable of handling loads up to 450 kg and changing tooling automatically, the system can be adapted and reprogrammed to handle new products without the need for re-engineering. It offers a 3.18-m reach.

Two robots can be combined to work in tandem across 10 axes to create the IRB 760 Twin Robot Xbar. With a production output rate of up to 16 parts/min., the system is suitable for high-speed press automation applications.

The company’s StampWare soware is available on the robot’s FlexPendant. It includes a graphical programming wizard that helps decrease the time needed to train the operator and robot programmer.

Technology tracks stamping press downtime, lubricant use

IRMCO has developed iNSIGHT, a patented technology that allows metal stampers to quantify their actual consumption of lubricant, control and document lubricant concentration, and monitor press line stoppages and efficiencies in real time, whether in-plant or remotely.

Users can track the different rates of chemical consumption for individual parts and compare this data to tool life to pinpoint the actual cost per part for each process in their facility.

Oxyfuel feature added to welding simulation tools

In collaboration with The Harris Products Group, Lincoln Electric has introduced the OxyFuel feature for its VRTEX 360 and Transport welding simulation tools. The new feature allows users to practice torch cutting safely and virtually. From setting up the torch to executing the cut, each task is designed to simulate real-world cutting applications.

The system offers the ability to connect to the user’s fuel of choice, propane or acetylene, and properly set fuel and fuel pressures in proper safety sequence. Users follow a step-by-step torch lighting sequence, following safety protocols and reinforcing the importance of reading the flame, and a proper torch shutdown sequence.
