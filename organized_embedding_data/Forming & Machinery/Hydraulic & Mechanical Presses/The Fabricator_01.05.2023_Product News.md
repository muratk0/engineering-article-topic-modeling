FMS combines laser/punching machine, panel bender, material handling

Salvagnini has unveiled a flexible manufacturing system incorporating the S1 combination machine, which can punch, form, tap, laser-cut, and separate parts, and the P4 panel bender. The combination has been designed to run kit or batch-one production—or process other series of parts that differ from each other—without work-in-process.

The S1’s multipress head can house up to 59 thick turret tools. The laser head moves along the Y axis with a 290-mm stroke. Software allows bidirectional communication between the S1 and P4. The new TML longitudinal device with two telescopic Cartesian manipulators automatically picks up the parts produced by the laser/punching machine and conveys them in-line to the panel bender or stacked on a pallet.

The P4-2520 panel bender is equipped with a blank holder that automatically adapts the length of the tool according to the size of the part to be produced, without the need for machine downtime or manual retooling.

End-of-arm tooling kits can be customized for vacuum pick and place

AutomationDirect offers end-of-arm tooling components for robot arm vacuum pick-and-place applications. Components can be purchased individually or in kits that include everything needed to create a single-beam or dual-beam end-of-arm tool.

Kits include structural rails (which support an optional internal vacuum), connectors, cup mounts, vacuum tubing, vacuum cups, fittings, a basic ejector, a flange for a Universal robot, and a blank robot flange.

The user cuts the structural rails to the desired lengths, determines the location of the suction cups, assembles the unit, and adds the compatible robot interface flange or drills one using the included blank robot flange.

System brings virtual planning to fabrication applications

Siemens offers the Sinumerik 840D sl CNC and its Virtual NC Kernel (VNCK). With Sinumerik Run MyVNCK, users can create their own control-specific virtual world by embedding the original CNC kernel into a virtual machine.

Users can plan, verify, and optimize their production steps in a virtual environment while avoiding possible programming errors and collisions. This technology can be used in applications such as laser cutting, waterjet, deburring, and bending.

TIG/stick inverter offers 10-to 200-amp output range

ESAB Corp. has introduced the Rogue ET 200iP Pro, a high-frequency TIG/stick welding inverter. The 21.2-lb. unit delivers a 200-amp TIG output at 25% duty cycle.

With a welding output range of 10 to 200 amps, the inverter provides arc control for delicate welds, as well as the power to run 5/32-in. stick electrodes. It has a pulsed DC TIG output of 0.1 to 500 Hz pulses per second.

The 115- to 230-V primary power enables users to work in a variety of locations. Flexible automatic input voltage compensation helps ensure a steady welding arc throughout the entire input power range.

Punch/fiber laser combination machine achieves high-speed, uninterrupted operation

AMADA AMERICA offers the EML-AJ series punch/fiber laser combination machine. It comes with all the punching functions inherent in single-function punching machines. Combined punches and dies are supplied with a Z turret with different diameters (1,010 mm upper and 1,200 mm lower) or a high-capacity turret of 1,200-mm-dia. upper and lower. Punching force is 300 kN.

The 3-kW fiber laser oscillator combines high oscillation efficiency with new optics. It employs assist gas rectifying technology and increases cutting speed by a factor of three compared to conventional machines, the manufacturer says.

A variety of automation options—automated punch and die changer, nozzle changer, laser scrap unloader, and cleaning function for cutting plates—allow for uninterrupted operation.

App delivers stand-alone measurement of high-power lasers

MKS Instruments Inc. has introduced the Ophir StarViewer iOS app, which delivers laser power and energy measurements wirelessly to iPhones and iPads. Data can be displayed in a time-based line graph, needle display, or large numeric display with statistics. Users also can capture a screenshot and share it, such as when a field technician needs to report results back to the lab.

The app is designed to work with the Ophir Ariel compact laser power meter for measuring high-power industrial lasers up to 8 kW. The system measures 440- to 550-nm green and blue lasers, used for copper welding; 900- to 1,100-nm fiber lasers for metal processing; 10.6-μm CO
