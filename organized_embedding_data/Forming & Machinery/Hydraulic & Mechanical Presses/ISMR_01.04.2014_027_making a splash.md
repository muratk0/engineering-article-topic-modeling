# Making a splash

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

RESATO Waterjet Technology from Holland is one of the few water jet cutting installations suppliers that develops and builds both the high pressure water jet pumps and the cutting tables. Its ACM waterjet cutters are available in different standard sizes and can be fitted, depending upon the size, with up to two independent cutting heads.

“Even with two cutting heads, the cutting area remains in 2D. The modular ACM waterjet cutting installation comes standard with direct linear driven motors on both main axes (X, Y). All standard options are easily installable as a module afterwards, enabling a machine upgrade to the customer’s wishes, for instance by mounting a second cutting head or replacing a 2D cutting head with a 2.5D cutting head,” explained the company.

The system features an independent measuring system (meaning no encoder measuring system). The Jetlift linear Z-axis is free of maintenance and features a height sensor and anti-collision detection. An electronic management system (EMS) enables unmanned operation and each waterjet cutting installation comes with IGEMS cutting software. See www.resato.nl ■
