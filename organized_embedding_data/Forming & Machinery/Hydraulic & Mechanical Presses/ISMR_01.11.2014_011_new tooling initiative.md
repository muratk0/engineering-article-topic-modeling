# New tooling initiative

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

AP&T IS intensifying its collaboration with Diversified Tooling Group (DTG), which is one of the largest manufacturers of press tools in the US. The goal is jointly to reinforce the companies’ expertise in tools and their customer offerings in press hardening on the North American market.

“Diversified Tooling Group has provided a full service tool and die process for numerous OEM North American automobile manufacturers for forty years. The cooperation between AP&T/DTG will now offer a full service hot stamping tool and die process for forming and cooling simulations, die design, die construction, die tryout and panel certification for the North American automobile industry,” said Mike Austin, Director – Manufacturing Engineering, DTG.

“Together we are continuing to develop the press hardening technique to be able to offer world-class solutions,” added Johan Friberg, Senior Advisor/Marketing and Sales, AP&T.

To develop, test and demonstrate tools and other components, a new press hardening line will be operational at DTG’s American Tooling Centre in Michigan at the beginning of next year. The line includes a 1,200 metric ton AP&T press, AP&T’s own furnace with feeding equipment (Multilayer Feeder Furnace) and additional automation for pre-production and to produce small volumes. The press hardening line is almost identical to the line at AP&T’s R&D Centre in Ulricehamn, Sweden. ■
