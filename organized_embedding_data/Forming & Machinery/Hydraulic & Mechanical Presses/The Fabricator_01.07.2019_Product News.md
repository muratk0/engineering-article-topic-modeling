System combines bending robot, press brake, tool changer

Amada offers the HG ARs, which integrates a bending robot with an HG series press brake and patented Automatic Tool Changer (ATC), for bending a variety of parts. It supports workpieces up to 39.3 by 31.4 in. and has a weight capacity of 44 lbs.

Four tool manipulators quickly load tooling from 15 punch stockers and 18 die stockers. The ATC stores 86 ft. of robot-loaded tooling.

A 6-axis robot with a range of motion capabilities performs all part loading, bending, and unloading operations. During bending, a potentiometer backgauge system with additional side gauge compensates for any deviation when the workpiece is placed on the die.

Brushes made entirely of stainless for corrosion protection

Pferd has introduced a new series of 100 percent stainless steel power brushes designated INOX-Total. All components are produced from 302 stainless (INOX) for corrosion protection, so they are suitable for use in environments highly sensitive to contamination.

Machining center handles parts up to 40 ft. long

FlexCNC has introduced the Flex-BEAM machining center for processing large or long parts, like structural steel beam, tube, and pipe, in a single setup. With an open-bed design that measures 40 by 2 ft., the machine can handle parts up to 40 ft. long. Alternately, the long open bed, coupled with a movable safety light curtain, allows multiple setups, so that while the unit is machining one part, another can be loaded or offloaded.

The machine has a monoblock welded steel frame with high rigidity and a T-slot machine bed. It features 3-axis interpolation, scribing, automatic hydraulic workholding, and automatic tool touch-off. A laser zero detector is standard, as is 6 x ATC, a hand pendant, and network with Ethernet cable.

Electric sideloader is compatible with Industry 4.0

Hubtex has updated its modular development platform for electric, multidirectional sideloaders with the introduction of the PhoeniX line. The first two models carry loads from 3.3 to 5.5 tons. They feature an ergonomic cabin, as well as improved maneuverability and all-around visibility. The new control system makes the trucks compatible with Industry 4.0.

The HIT3 operating terminal is equipped with a color display that gives drivers a clear picture of important settings and operating data. Electric power steering minimizes steering noise and helps reduce energy consumption.

Fiber laser delivers 12 kW of fiber power

The ByStar Fiber from Bystronic has been enhanced with a 12-kW fiber laser and a newly designed cutting head that enables the BeamShaper option to help ensure consistent cutting quality on various material qualities up to 1.125 in.

With the 12kW laser, the system’s cutting speeds increase up to 20 percent on average (when laser cutting with nitrogen) compared to the previously available 10-kW laser source. This increases productivity throughout the range of sheet thicknesses from 0.125 to 1.125 in., the manufacturer reports.

A slimmer design for the new cutting head decreases the possibility of contact with cut features during the cutting process. In addition, the company has reduced the number of different components and accommodated important technical functions in the interior of the cutting head.

Pretreatment protects precleaned surfaces from corrosion

Madison Chemical has introduced Ultra-Bond 2187, a dry-in-place pretreatment for precleaned metal. This zero-phosphorus solution forms a homogeneous organic/inorganic layer on pretreated metals, providing corrosion protection and enhancing paint adhesion for pretreated surfaces painted with powder or piqued paints.

The product is applied in a 1 to 3 percent concentration for 30 to 90 sec., and it does not require a postcoating rinse. It is suitable for use with ferrous, aluminum, zinc, and stainless alloys when used as directed.

PAPR system offers eye, respiratory protection for welders

Optrel has introduced a system that comprises a crystal2.0 welding helmet and an e3000 powered airpurifying respirator. The system delivers eye protection, visual clarity, and safety against airborne particulates for workers during welding or grinding operations.

The NIOSH-approved system protects welders from 99.8 percent of respiratory particulates including smoke, aerosol, and dust. Its adjustable air flow allows even distribution of fresh air in the helmet.

The helmet features the company’s patented Crystal Lens Technology and delivers 31 percent visible light transmission in its light state (protection level 2.0).

New sensors added to weld seam inspection system

Vitronic has added newly developed sensors to its Viro WSI automated weld seam inspection system for the automotive industry and its suppliers. With these sensors, the system is faster and more compact that its predecessor, reaching hard-to-access areas and diagnosing defects as small as 0.1 mm.

The sensors are available in two designs: a 30-mm-wide field of view for GMAW and brazed seams and a 14-mm-wide field of view for laser welded and brazed seams with their very finite seam geometries.

The interactive visualization stations display defective components and seams to the operator. The direct visualization on the line enables the system to intervene quickly in the upstream welding process.

Antiseize also offers corrosion prevention

Zyp Coatings Inc. has released Seize-Stop, a nonmetallic antiseize formulated with highly lubricating oil and powder lubricants. It provides antiseize protection in industrial applications that do not allow metal-containing products.

The antiseize can be used with most metals, including plain carbon steel, stainless steel, other alloy steels, titanium, nickel, aluminum, and soft metals. It prevents corrosion, rusting, and galling between metal parts and is recommended for service from ambient to 1,652 degrees F.

Capacitive discharge welders feature dual pulse output

Amada Miyachi America Inc. offers the CD-V series of capacitive discharge power supplies. Featuring a fully controllable dual pulse output and a color, 3.5-in. user interface, the welding machine provides consistent welding output for repeatable process results, the company reports.

Features include built-in peak current indication for immediate weld fire confirmation, pulse shaping with adjustable upslope to reduce expulsion during welds, an ultrafast rise time for conductive material welding, and the ability to go rapid fire between pulses for quick welding of adjacent locations.

It can store up to eight schedules at one time. A weld counter, programmable squeeze time, and a push-button rotary encoder contribute to ease of use.

Small internal mix spray nozzles coat, cool, treat, and paint

Exair’s new 1/8 NPT small internal mix spray nozzles atomize fluids up to 42 gal./hr. Internal mix atomizing nozzles mix the liquid and air inside the cap and produce fine atomization. They can be used on liquids with a viscosity up to 300 centipoise.

The nozzles, available in narrowangle round pattern, wide-angle round pattern, and flat fan pattern, are used to coat parts in containers, cool laminates, and apply paint and lubricant. They combine liquid and compressed air to create a coating of liquid that can be adjusted to meet the needs of the application.

Band saw introduced

Behringer Saws Inc. offers the HBE-321A automatic enclosed band saw. Designed to be an "everyday" saw, it features a servo-driven ball screw blade feeding system and pressure sensors, which provide constant downfeed control and adjustment. The ball screw-driven material feed gripper eliminates backlash and helps ensure precise material positioning and repeatability.

The machine is made of a vibration-dampening, gray cast-iron frame and components with cutting capacities up to 13.7 by 12.6 in.
