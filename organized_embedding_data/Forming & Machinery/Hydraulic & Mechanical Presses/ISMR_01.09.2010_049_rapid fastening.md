# RAPID FASTENING

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Sheet metal fastening specialist, Profil (UK) Ltd, has extended the versatility of its pierce and rivet assembly systems with the introduction of a battery-powered handgun capable of installing M6 or M8 anchor studs and nuts in materials of up to 3.00mm thick.

With a maximum setting force of six tonnes, the portable installation tool is suitable for low volume production applications, as well as prototype assemblies and general sheet metal fabrication.

“Measuring 440mm long by 430mm high, the handgun’s easily adjusted setting load and foolproof setting action ensure correct material piercing and fastener installation under all conditions. The tool will set up to 200 nuts or studs from a single battery charge and can be fully recharged in around 30 minutes,” said Profil.

“The handgun’s magnetic toolholder, combined with a 45mm maximum stroke, provide easy component and fastener location prior to setting. In addition, a nominal three-second setting cycle enables users to produce vibration resistant and watertight fixings at around five times the setting speeds of conventional fastening methods.”

Profil’s pierce studs and nuts are equally suitable for use with coated or uncoated materials, including high tensile, stainless and sandwich steels, aluminium panels and magnesium castings.
