# Rapid sorting of finished parts

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

THE HIGH SPEED of modern laser cutting systems call for productivity-enhancing and user-friendly systems for the manual sorting of prefabricated parts. With BeltFLEX, material flow specialist Remmert GmbH offers a fitting solution in its portfolio.

"With the integrated unloading option, finished parts can be sorted easily, quickly and in an ergonomic body posture. The component of the Remmert modular system integrates simply into existing layouts and can be used intuitively. In combination with other systems in our FLEX portfolio, suitable solutions for an agile material flow can be achieved," explained Remmert.

"BeltFLEX is a conveyor belt from which the operator can manually sort finished parts. The belt height is adjustable, between 765 and 875mm, to enable back-friendly working. BeltFLEX promotes ergonomic posture on the one hand and rapid manual sorting on the other. An even belt advance cycle ensures a faster unloading process than other variants of manual sorting. BeltFLEX is available in formats 3015 and 4020 and can transport sheets of up to 12mm in thickness. The permissible total weight of the finished parts to be transported is 1,600kg. By using BeltFLEX, material flow is significantly improved and users achieve measurable increases in productivity.

As a module of the FLEX family, BeltFLEX supports agile material flow. The integrated personal protection concept is designed for maximum safety at work: LED lights visualise the device status, faults and hazardous situations. The SMART Control system for FLEX components is simple and intuitive.

"The FLEX family is versatile, but all its components were designed with the aim of continuously improving the material flow in the respective area of application. With the LaserFLEX loading and unloading system, for example, laser cutting systems are optimally utilised," explained Frank Baudach, Sales Manager DACH, Remmert. "In combination with the BASIC Tower storage solution, used as a buffer for raw materials and finished parts, laser cutting systems can be operated day and night, which significantly increases the productivity of a system. By integrating FLEX modules, users can ensure optimum material flow." See www.remmert.de
