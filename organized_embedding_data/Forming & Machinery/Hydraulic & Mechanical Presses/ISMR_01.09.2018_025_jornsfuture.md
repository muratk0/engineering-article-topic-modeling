## SARONNI SpA www.saronni.it

SINCE 1950, Saronni has produced feed and cut-to-length lines and has global customers in sectors such as automotive, household appliances and electromechanical. An important area of its production are feeding lines for magnetic strip. Lines are manufactured to customers' requirements and can feature a controlled loop “free loop" or channeled loop "S loop".

"This year at EuroBLECH, we will be displaying an automatic coil feed line with HI precision straightener for magnetic strip and HI speed electronic feeder," it told ISMR.

"Some customers, to obtain high production performances, also require welding operations and strip accumulation in large coils loaded onto large-dimension decoilers. The heart of the lines is the HI precision straightening machine available for strip widths of 20 – 500mm, strip thicknesses of 0.2 – 0.8mm and speeds of 10 – 100m/min."

## SHUNG DAR INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD

www.shungdar.com.tw

MANUFACTURER Shung Dar, has over 35 years of experience in developing automation production facilities in the steel processing industry. It provides customised slitting line solutions for a range of material dimensions from hot-rolled steel and cold-rolled steel to stainless steel. With a dual-slitter system and rotating four-arm turnstile design, change-over time can be shortened.

Shung Dar's press blanking line has been designed to meet stringent requirements to process a wide variety of material, such as aluminum or high-strength steel and even surface sensitive material for the automotive and fabricating industries. ## SIMPAC EUROPE GmbH

https://news.simpac-europe.com

SIMPAC WILL launch a new servo press generation at EuroBLECH 2018. Since the forming of Simpac Europe GmbH in early 2017, the focus of its international team has been on research and development, sales and marketing. The MX series (3,000 – 10,000kN) and the SX series (8,000 - 30,000kN) are the initial results of this new, German-Korean cooperative venture, combining German engineering with Korean efficiency.

"The presses have been further developed at decisive points; the entire drive concept has been revised with regard to efficiency and dynamics. The very solid, low-stress annealed press bodies are FEM-calculated and optimised by individual, 'hot-spot analyses' in areas which are subject to higher loads. Key components have been replaced by products of German origin, which ensure maximum dynamics, long service life and a reliable supply of spare parts," commented the manufacturer.

## STAM SpA www.stam.it

## ITALIAN

MANUFACTURER, STAM, provides coil processing equipment and flexible rollforming lines. Its flexible rollforming lines for U, C, Z and Sigma profiles are complete production lines that are able to punch, roll form, cut and stack profiles automatically and flexibly. All adjustments are handled by computer, without the operator's direct intervention. These profiles can have variable sizes and thicknesses: the profile width varies from 100 to 400mm, the height from 40 to 10mm and the thicknesses from 1 to 4mm, with possible extensions of these ranges.

STAM rollforming lines are used in the automotive, building home appliances, energy, infrastructures and logistics sectors. They are flexible and production can be changed quickly from one type of profile to another, with no manual intervention. The production lines developed by STAM can punch, rollform, cut and stack these profiles in automatic and flexible mode. STAM also produces machines and complete equipment for cut-tolength and slitting lines for coils, as well as lines for flexible manufacturing systems. Its cutting lines cover a broad range of material thicknesses and types.
