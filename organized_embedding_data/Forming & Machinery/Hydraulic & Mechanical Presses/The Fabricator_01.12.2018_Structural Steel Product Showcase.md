Plasma cutting system cuts grating at high speeds

Soitaab’s plasma grate cutting system cuts interrupted surfaces such as steel and aluminum grating at high speeds. It uses a Kjellberg 360-amp Hi-Focus plasma torch and a patented Hotwire indirect plasma cutting technology. In this standard plasma torch with ohmic height sensing, a welding wire is fed automatically underneath the torch tip. This wire acts as the anode, completing the electrical circuit without a direct connection to the workpiece.

The wire simulates a continuous surface under the torch and the grate beneath is cut as if it were a flat plate. According to the company, there is no slow-down of the plasma torch, no preheating of the surface, and no loss of the plasma arc as the torch moves across the interrupted grate surface. The result is a clean, high-quality, accurate cut with little to no rework on the surface, the company reports.

Machine’s 3-D pipe cutting accuracy helps reduce welding, fitting time

HGG has introduced the ProCutter 600 for structural steel fabricators cutting complex piping constructions up to 24 in. dia. According to the company, the machine’s accuracy helps reduce grinding, fitting, and welding time after pipe profiles are cut.

Quality-certification programs for structural steel focus on error prevention

AISC offers quality-certification programs for the structural steel industry. The programs focus on the entire process of fabrication and erection. The goal is to build quality structures from the start by focusing on error prevention rather than error correction.

During the on-site audit, a company’s day-to-day operations are compared with the documented procedures from its quality management system. The program requires the company to be accountable for all facets of its organization, from management responsibility to employee training to material purchasing and handling to project-specific erection plans.

Purpose-built order picker handles long, awkward loads

Combilift offers the Combi-OP, which the company says is the first purpose-built order picker for long products. It offers efficient, noncompromised operation for easy picking of long profiles and handling of long, awkward loads, such as in the structural steel industry.

This man-up truck features a long platform that enables the operator to access long products stored at height. It is fitted with guide rollers, allowing it to operate in existing guided narrow aisles of just 56.5 in.

The truck has a roomy elevated platform and a capacity of 1,000 lbs. Its overhead guard, antislip floor, self-closing platform gate, and guardrails help keep the operator safe, and the steering angle automatically adjusts according to lift heights.
