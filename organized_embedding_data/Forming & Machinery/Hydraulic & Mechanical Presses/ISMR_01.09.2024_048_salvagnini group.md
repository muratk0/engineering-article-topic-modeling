# Salvagnini Group

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand G93 and D94

www.salvagninigroup.com

For over sixty years, Salvagnini has been designing, producing and selling flexible systems for sheet metal processing. Its panel benders; punching machines; press brakes; fibre laser cutting machines; FMS lines; automatic store towers and software have found applications in over 170 sectors.

Automatic set-up and tool change are two features of its B3.AU-TO press brake. The AU-TO automatic tool change device and the ATA automatic tool adjuster, which adjust the length of the stations, significantly reduce press brake re-tooling, increasing autonomy and productivity while reducing lead-time.

"Adopting scalable and modular automation in bending brings significant improvements in the machine's availability, extending its independence and flexibility," explained Salvagnini.

The new 2225 model of its P2 panel bender was unveiled worldwide last year at LAMIERA. Salvagnini has also developed its ADLU loading/unloading device and MCU automatic sorting device (in 6020 size) for processing sheet metal formats of up to six metres in length for its L3 fibre laser cutting system.

## SafanDarley BV

Stand A136

www.safandarley.com

SafanDarley, the Dutch inventor of the servo-electronic E-Brake bending system, is moving increasingly towards the digitally controlled factory with automated, modular and software solutions.

"The E-Brake is customisable, reliable and energy-efficient. We offer the largest range of E-Brake solutions in the market (available from 35-300 tons). They offer energy savings of up to 70%, shorter cycle times (up to 30% more productivity) and superior machine uptime. Users can configure their press brake and create a tailor-made machine that adapts to their specific needs. They can customise their bending capabilities with various options such as measurement systems and bending aids," outlined SafanDarley.

"The modular electronic press brake provides a flexible, cost-effective and future-proof solution for customers looking to invest in the most advanced technologies in the sheet-metalworking industry. They can even expand the E-Brake with the SafanDarley Robo Mate to create a lights-out production environment in the future," it added.

## SCHRÖDER Group

Stand A48

www.schroedergroup.eu/en

At EuroBLECH in Hanover, the SCHRÖDER Group will be showcasing a selection from its wide range of machines for folding, cutting, beading, flanging and rolling.

The highlight of this year's presentation will be the complete automation of folding heavy sheets. A MAK 4 Evolution UD will feature a robot that loads the machine with sheets of up to 6mm thick and 3000 x 1500mm in size, placing the finished product on a palletising station.

"Thanks to the pneumatic gauge in the machine's advanced handling system and the machine's up-and-down bending beam, the folding machine runs through the entire production process with no manual intervention. The entire automation solution is controlled via the Schröder control system. No knowledge of robotics is required. The solution presented is the result of a strategic partnership with STARMATIK, an established automation provider in the sheet metal industry," outlined the manufacturer.
