# FACE TO FACE

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

## Focus on energy efficiency

Ahmet Ozkayan also released new data on Ermaksan’s Evolution series hybrid press brakes.

“We created an environmentally and user friendly, long life precision machine with 60% low energy consumption, 80% low oil usage, 100% productivity and silent working features compared to ordinary press brakes,” he explained. “We also surprised our visitors with the second generation hybrid press brake - EVO II was launched after two years of R&D. This machine, which works with closed circuit servo-hydraulic systems, features zero maintenance cost and perfect precision at high speed.”

Hydraulic pistons are driven by AC servo motors without proportional direction valves on Evolution series hybrid press brakes. This means more precision and less maintenance as well as no pressure loss. Unlike conventional press brake systems, the only energy consumption that takes place on the Evolution series hybrid press brakes is when the operator pushes the foot pedal and moves the top beam for bending. This also reduces oil levels by an estimated 80%, Ermaksan told ISMR.

Mr. Ozkayan confirmed that the machine tool sector has been chosen as a strategic sector and many of its projects, driven by 40 expert engineers in its R&D department, have been approved by TUBITAK (The Scientific and Technological Research Council of Turkey) and TTGV (Technology Development Foundation of Turkey). His aim is to build a new structure for Ermaksan’s R&D centre.

“We have production capacity for many different sectors such as the defence and renewable energy industries. Our aim is to expand our capacity and play a leading role in the global industry. In addition to production of high-tech machines and software and electronic integrated products, we are working on projects for renewable energy industries like wind and solar energy,” he added.
