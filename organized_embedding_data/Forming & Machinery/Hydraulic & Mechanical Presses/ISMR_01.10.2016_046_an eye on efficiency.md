# An eye on efficiency

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

TAIWANESE MANUFACTURER Yih Shen Machinery (YSM) has been developing press forming machines since the 1980s and recently sold high-end press forming machines to Europe. The European market now takes up 20% of YSM's exports. 40% of YSM machines are sold into the automotive and motorcycle sectors, a further 40% is sold into the miniature circuit breaker sector with the remaining 20% spread across other sectors.

"The primary application of our machines is in the automotive and motorcycle sectors, such as tubularmounted components, brake components and headlight-mounted components," explained Vice-General Manager, Lin Ching-Tang. “For sheet forming, we do not adopt the continuous mould design but pre-press the sheet and then place the sheet onto machines for the forming process, which successfully reduces waste. Together with the all-in-one forming trend now prevalent in the industry, we have integrated presses, forming machines, tapping machines, welding equipment and automation into one and this design has helped our customers to keep their costs down."

The latest YSM CNC-39T 6.8-ton strip and wire forming/tapping machine can process 1.5-6.5mm wire diameters, 60mm max. strip widths and 0.8-3mm strip thicknesses at a 150ppm maximum forming speed. It can produce computer and electronic/lighting components as well as fastener parts in the automotive sector, using a PLC touch screen. The forming slides feature double cams - the integration of backward cams and slide sensors prevents forming tools from sticking, prolonging the lifetime of the equipment. The machine also integrates an Intercom automatic NC tapping system, made in Italy.
