# DEES Hydraulic Industrial Co., Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.deesgroup.com.tw

Stamping manufacturer, DEES, produces tandem lines as well as deep drawing; hot forming; hemming; die spotting; tryout; transfer; servo-controlled; C-frame and four-post hydraulic presses. With microprecision control and energy efficiency at the top of its R&D goals. DEES owns various patents and inventions to create customised presses for each customer.

DEES customers include Boeing; Honda; Toyota; Audi; Volkswagen; Mercedes-Benz; Volvo; Bosch Group Home Appliances; Electrolux; GE Home Appliances; Haier; Foxconn and more.

One focus at EuroBLECH will be its hydraulic tryout press (metalforming). It has installed a triple-action hydraulic tryout press at Volvo, with outer and inner slides plus cushion system for car-body part forming. It is equipped with an accumulator, to speed up production efficiency, and a moving bolster towards the front to facilitate die change.

Formed in 1976 in Taiwan, it has been present in China since 2000.

## Dimeco

www.dimeco.com

This French family-owned business, set up in early 1957, is a specialist in sheet-metal coil production systems. Stamping; punching; shearing; rollforming and bending solutions are available from coil for finished or semi-finished sheet-metal components. It also builds customised solutions.

Its factory at Besançon engineers and manufactures all types of press feed equipment, cut-to-length lines as well as “Flexilines" (LINAPUNCH flexible coil punching and LINACUT coil-fed fibre laser); equipment for press control and safety equipment plus quick die and coil-change equipment. Its Spanish factory manufactures flexible punching and rollforming equipment. Dimeco's Joint Venture in India (DKMFS) manufactures press feed equipment and cut-tolength lines for the Indian market.

The company has also developed application software to calculate coil/material/production parameters to optimise coilfed production.

"Standard, stainless and pre-painted steel, aluminium and any metal strip from coil can be processed using combined technologies such as punching, stamping, shearing, rollforming and bending," it said.
