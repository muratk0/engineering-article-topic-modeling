Plate processor offers optional bevel cutting, 12-station tool changing

Peddinghaus has introduced the HSFDB-C plate processor. It can perform drilling, scribing, countersinking, tapping, milling, oxyfuel cutting, and plasma cutting.

The system can feed material to the operating area of the machine as opposed to bringing the operating area of the machine to the material. The optional bevel cutting assembly maintains a single bevel cutting design for both the oxyfuel and plasma cutting torch.

The plate processor’s SignoMat hard stamp part marking system uses a single disc with 36 different characters for part numbering or layout requirements. The design rotates 360 degrees to accommodate mirrored nesting.

A 12-station rotary tool changer eliminates the need to change tooling manually during production. Each drilling unit is available as needed upon program command. The unit processes plate up to 4 in. thick and is available in 6-, 8-, and 10-ft. widths.

Laser cutting system processes tubes, pipes at high speeds

Mazak Optonics Corp. offers the Tube Gear 2-D laser cutting machine for tubes and pipes. It offers high-speed capabilities for thin-walled applications.

Available with a 2.5- or 4-kW resonator, the machine can process round and rectangular tube and pipe with diameters up to 6 in. With its standard 5-in. Servo Focus torch, it can make cuts at a rapid traverse rate of up to 6,299 IPM.

The machine is equipped with a triple chuck system to help minimize warp and material vibration, allowing processing of material up to 21.3 ft. long. It also includes a bundle material load/unload system that allows for large lot sizes up to 4.2 tons while still comprising a smaller footprint than similar machines.

Other standard features include an automated lens focal distance measurement system, automated calibration function, and a nozzle cleaning system.

Multiple-station machine finishes, inspects prehydroform tube

Haven has developed an integrated, multifunction machine for prehydroform tube machining, inspection, and identification for an automotive aluminum parts supplier.

The machine has seven stations working simultaneously to complete a finished tube every 5 sec. The sequence of operation is double end finishing (ID, OD, chamfer, and end face), chip removal, overall length measurement, diameter confirmation, wall thickness verification, straightness inspection, and full-length laser stenciling.

A complex program monitors the results of each operation and accepts or rejects a part based on extremely tight tolerance specifications. A walking beam-style transfer system helps ensure a smooth and complete progression of parts through the machine.

Precision-ground linear support and servo motion control allow for minimal setup time and a statistically repeatable performance. Laser sensor technology gives this machine a high level of accuracy and repeatability to help ensure zero defects reach the final product. The Ethernet operating system allows for integration with the existing plant data collection system. The machine downloads part recipes and part identification and uploads measurement data and part identification to the user’s data concentrator.

Welding tables are spatter-resistant

Forster America offers 3-D welding tables and accessories in the U.S. The tables come in gray cast or AlCu for stainless steels. Both versions are spatter-resistant.

The tabletop comprises beams that can be rearranged, moved, and adjusted at any time during the welding process to accommodate the needs of the welder.

Hydroforming press available in clamp forces from 250 to 5,000 tons

Interlaken Technology has introduced a four-column hydroforming press system for running high-volume production. It is available in clamp forces from 250 to 5,000 tons.

The multichannel, closed-loop control system is programmed to handle various sizes and materials. It also offers dynamic mode switching, which enables the user to switch among a variety of feedbacks such as force, position, and internal pressure.

Grinding wheel designed for demanding jobs

Walter Surface Technologies has introduced the Xcavator grinding wheel for tough jobs in metal fabrication, steel mills, mining, oil and gas, and shipbuilding. The heavy-duty wheel delivers the highest removal rate in the company’s line of grinding wheels.

The wheel, which features an aggressive, coarse front layer of enriched zirconium grain, has been tested on such hard resources as tungsten carbide, tempered steel, INCONEL® alloy, Hardox®, and boron steel.

Features include an extra-strong disc base/backing and the patented UHR multilayer manufacturing technology to prevent edge flakiness. Free of materials such as iron, sulfur, and chlorine, the wheel will not contaminate surfaces such as stainless steel.
