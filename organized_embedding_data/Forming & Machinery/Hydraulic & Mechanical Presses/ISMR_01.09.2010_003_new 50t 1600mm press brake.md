# NEW 50T 1600mm PRESS BRAKE

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Bystronic has launched a new small 50T 1600mm Bystronic press brake, which boasts a small footprint. In reducing the footprint, the machine also uses every millimetre of available capacity. The bed length of 1650mm is also the distance between frames so component lengths are not restricted by the depth of the machine’s throat.

The machine comes complete with euro upper tools, quick-to-change single vee lower dies, laser front guards and a Cybelec CNC 60 control. See www.bystronic.com
