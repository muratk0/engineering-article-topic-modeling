30-HP waterjet intensifier pump draws half the amperage of a 50-HP pump

Jet Edge Inc. has introduced the iP60-30, a 60,000-PSI, 30-HP hydraulic waterjet intensifier pump. Designed for waterjet shops with limited power, the unit draws 37 full-load amps at 460 VAC or 74 full-load amps at 230 VAC. It can produce 0.6 GPM of ultrahigh-pressure water at a continuous operating pressure of 55,000 PSI.

Like all of the company’s intensifier pumps, the unit features a tie-rod design that eliminates threads on the high-pressure cylinders, end caps, and hydraulic cylinders. This reduces the likelihood of cracking and increases component life. Matched metal components prevent galling of hydraulic components.

The pump includes a hydraulic accumulator to provide smooth hydraulic pressure, reduce spikes, and prolong hydraulic pump life, the company states.

Laser welding machine allows coil end welding on strip as thin as 0.003 in.

Guild Intl. has introduced the LHMA laser welding machine. It creates complete edge-to-edge welds, without the need for start and stop tabs, on strip as thin as 0.003 in. According to the company, this capability has been unavailable until now. Current technology employing GTAW machines allows for welding strips no thinner than 0.008 in. and requires start and stop tabs.

The machine uses a fiber laser to deliver the necessary energy for welding via a fiber-optic cable connected to a focusing head. The focusing head concentrates the energy to a very fine spot to weld steel, stainless steel, copper and brass alloys, and aluminum.

The machine includes a camera and large HMI touchscreen, allowing the operator to view the strip fit-up before welding. In 2 min., the process of shearing, aligning, and welding the two coil ends is complete.

Weld schedules can be stored for quick retrieval, and the parameters are set with the HMI touchscreen. An interlocked safety enclosure provides the required safety for the operator.

Updates made to shop floor CMM

Hexagon Metrology has introduced the updated 4.5.4 SF shop floor CMM. It now includes integrated workspace LED lighting to assist the operator in poor lighting conditions.

The bolt-hole pattern in the CMM’s granite base has been revised to accommodate a 300-mm kinematic plate for repeatable dedicated fixturing. Granite etching defines the measurement envelope in the X and Y axes. Optional programmable status lighting on the Z axis signals the machine’s progress in the measurement routine.

Power switches are located on the front of the machine. An integrated monitor and keyboard arm are available to replace the workstation upgrade option, adding increased flexibility for the operator while minimizing the footprint on the shop floor. The CMM can fit through a standard size door and go anywhere dimensional inspection is needed. It uses standard 110-/220-V outlets with no need for shop air.

Bending machine offers two heads that move vertically and horizontally

Schwarze-Robitec offers the CNC 100 DB Twin tube bending machine. Its two bend heads, which can be moved vertically and horizontally (1× left bending, 1× right bending), bend the tube alternately clockwise and counterclockwise.

This operation provides flexibility for such applications as manufacturing superheater coils. The tube serpentine does not need to be turned after each bend, allowing quick and economical production processes and the manufacture of long side lengths, the company states. As an option, both bend heads also can bend in the same bending direction. Different sizes of bending radii can be produced using various sizes of bend formers.

Designed for use in power plant construction, the bending machine is optimized for use on tubes made of high-temperature-resistant steels.
