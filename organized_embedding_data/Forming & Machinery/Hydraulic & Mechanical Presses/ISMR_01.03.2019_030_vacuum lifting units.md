# Vacuum lifting units

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

AERO-LIFT VAKUUMTECHNIK (Binsdorf, Germany) has introduced its new generation vacuum lifting system, the AERO ADVANCE vacuum lifter. It comes with a completely new look and features such as an intelligent warning device, built-in automatic power saving system and new safety spiral hoses that prevent cables from tangling when the unit is turned or adjusted. The design allows for a relaxed posture. An emergency stop provides maximum safety," added the company. See www.aero-lift.de

"The built-in, hot-dip galvanised, solid steel beams are particularly durable and offer permanent protection against corrosion. The LED lamps and symbols on the new plastic housing, with an aluminium look, indicate when the machine is ready for operation. Two high-quality vacuum pumps – just like all other components – are integrated in a pleasant housing box, protected against dust. If one pump fails, the other pump takes over for safety of operation. The new lifting device series is available in various versions with a lifting capacity of up to 1,200kg. As the pumps are enclosed, the unit is particularly silent," explained the manufacturer.

A single operator can lift large, heavy sheets via the generated vacuum 'without any exertion of force and without damaging the material'. The workpiece can then be conveyed for further processing, e.g. to a punching or laser cutting machine. The articulated and double spring-loaded suction plates provide for a cushioned, smooth placement and elevation of the lifting unit, even under load. Heavy plates can be separated using the articulated suction plates.

The suction plates can optionally be switched on or off via manual slide valves. WHAT WERE THE results when AP&T developed its award-winning production line for hot forming of aluminum?

"To meet increasingly strict government requirements regarding CO₂ emissions, the automotive industry has, for many years, been working to reduce fuel consumption and the climate impact of newly manufactured vehicles by taking various measures to reduce weight. New materials, new design and manufacturing methods have enabled lighter parts and components, resulting in reduced vehicle weight. In this, one of our core areas, press hardening of sheet steel has been of vital importance for the progress made so far. As we continue to improve and refine presshardening technology, we are also investing significant resources in developing production solutions for forming other materials and material combinations. One of the most interesting is high-strength aluminum, which is considerably lighter than steel but still associated with higher material costs and has properties that inflict other demands on forming methods and machinery," the Swedish press specialist told ISMR.

In 2013, AP&T began to investigate the possibility of developing a new process for hot forming complex designed parts in highstrength aluminum for use in industry. During the following three-year period, it ran and/or participated in a number of innovation projects with customers, universities and suppliers, primarily funded by the EU and other public stakeholders. The goal was to increase expertise by testing material, production processes and equipment (theoretically and practically). In 2016, the initiative resulted in an initial prototype line and a number of prototype components that showed great potential for the future.

A year later, its first full-scale production line for hot forming of high-strength aluminum – hot forming, w-temper and warm forming – was inaugurated at its Ulricehamn, Sweden, test centre.

"The first of its kind in the world, the solution offers new possibilities for a costefficient production process, enhanced manufacturability and increased functionality of lightweight car body components. Thanks to the high strength and low weight of the material, the body weight could potentially be reduced by up to 40 per cent by replacing conventional sheet steel with high-strength aluminum which can be made significantly thinner (down-gauging) while retaining its strength. This innovation won several prestigious awards, such as Altair Enlighten Award and SIQ Quality Innovation Award. Today, we have a fully commercial, scalable solution available to OEMs and Tier suppliers who wish to manufacture high-strength, lowweight, complex designed body part components," confirmed AP&T.

The production line includes high-speed destackers for blank feeding to the furnace; a furnace for quick and homogeneous heating, specially developed for 6xxx and 7xxx aluminum alloys; an intermediate cooling station (a step that provides the blank with better formability and corrosion resistance); high-speed transfer of the blank to the press; AP&T's high-speed, low energyconsuming servo-hydraulic press; forming and cooled tools and washing.

AP&T's chief technology officer, Dr. Christian Koroschetz, and manager, development forming processes & tooling, Dr. Michael Machhammer

"Due to the strong formability of the material, components that are currently manufactured by joining several different parts (such as car door rings) are manufactured as a single piece through one hot forming operation. This makes for a rational manufacturing process with fewer operations, simpler handling and logistics, fewer tools, better material utilisation and lower production costs per unit. To further streamline production, each operation in the process has been analysed and optimised. One of the challenges was to lower the time for what is referred to as the artificial ageing process," added the manufacturer.

AP&T uses an optimised ageing sequence to reduce cycle time by executing a pre-ageing process after forming and quenching the part to prepare the material for the subsequent paint baking process, after the car body is assembled. This pre-ageing step takes between five and 30 minutes, instead of several hours. The final hardening is carried out during paint baking which takes place after assembling the car body components.

"This leads to very ductile material behaviour during the final assembly of the component in the car body shop and results in good joinability. This enhanced ageing sequence was developed and evaluated by us in collaboration with Hydro Aluminium, which developed an aluminum alloy (AA6xx) which is tailored for this process," explained AP&T.
