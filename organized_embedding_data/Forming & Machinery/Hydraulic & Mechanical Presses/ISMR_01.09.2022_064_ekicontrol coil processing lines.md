# Ekicontrol Coil Processing Lines

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.ekicontrol.com

Ekicontrol Coil Processing Lines is a family company set up in 1986 and based in Urnieta, the Basque Country. It celebrates its 35th anniversary this year, with coil lines on four continents and over 20 countries.

"We design, manufacture and install lines for processing carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium metal coils. This includes longitudinal, transversal and multiple slitting lines, combined slitting lines for simultaneous slitting and coiling, complete press feed lines and lines with special requirements or with internal punching," it told ISMR.

Examples include its multi-blanking lines: TwinCut 20 for simultaneous slitting and cut-to-length cutting and the XS Line 1250 (for versatile cutting operations). Its OneCut 20 cut-to-length lines can work autonomously or continuously through a punching machine or sheet metal storage system. Its slitting lines can process coil weights from six to 20 tons (material thicknesses from 0.5 to 3.0mm).
