# Tight connections

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Nedschroef makes it possible to connect sheetmetal without pre-drilling by using flow-drilling metFlow®screws and precision robots (without compromising on strength). This process can be applied to highstrength sheet materials that are difficult to connect in multiple layers. This approach is also suitable for the pre-assembly of components.

"Various materials can be connected, such as steel and aluminium. Connections with CFRP are also possible. The connection with its metric thread is tight and durable and can withstand great stress, although it can be disassembled if necessary. Components do not require a pre-drilled hole or tapped mating thread, and tolerances can be easily compensated for (which saves cost)," it explained.

The Koninklijke Nedschroef Holding B.V. based in Helmond, the Netherlands, has developed connection technology worldwide for 125 years. Intelligent engineering, fasteners and individual forming technology solutions from Nedschroef are used in a range of industry sectors. Nedschroef employs 2,200 people in 11 countries and achieved sales of around 644 million euros in 2022.

Since 2014, Nedschroef has been part of the Chinese Shanghai Prime Machinery Company Ltd (PMC Group).
