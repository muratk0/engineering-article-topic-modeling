# Fine blanking press

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

FEINTOOL IS A LEADING manufacturer of fine blanking presses, marketed under the product family names HFA, HFT, XTRA and XFT. Its new hydraulic press, FB one, has been designed, the company told ISMR, to 'take cost-effectiveness and quality to a completely new level'. The FB one has been engineered with the aim of simplifying custom configuration, and enabling quick and easy modification in line with changing production requirements. Its modular design principle supports these goals. Press components can be easily combined and configured in line with specific needs. Components have been reduced in number and standardised (commonality for multiple systems), streamlining maintenance and parts management.

"Improved equipment efficiency (OEE) delivers up to 30 per cent lower power consumption, driving down costs per part and reducing the impact on the environment. It is simpler to operate, compatible with existing tools and offers support for automated processes as well as seamless integration with digitised production environments. The innovative hydraulic direct drive and radically new press design, combined with the new control platform, deliver unprecedented performance. It takes fine blanking to a new level," commented Feintool. See www.feintool.com
