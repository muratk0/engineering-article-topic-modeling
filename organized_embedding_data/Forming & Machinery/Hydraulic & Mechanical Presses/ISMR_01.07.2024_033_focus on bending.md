The ByBend Star 120 is the latest member of Bystronic's family of press brakes with minimal space requirement. The compact but rapid bending machine can process small to medium-sized parts in a variety of sheet thicknesses, up to a length of 2.05m (as well as materials in higher tensile-strength classes). Bending power is 120 tons.

"Operators can spare themselves elaborate bending angle measurements from now on. The LAMS function (Laser Angle Measuring System) takes care of these automatically. The 'FastBend Plus' function offers effective personal protection with maximum productivity and reduces set-up time. Users can start bending right away. Thanks to energy-efficient servo hydraulics, up to 30 percent can be saved in energy, with 20 per cent more speed in return," said the Swiss sheetmetal specialist.

The BySoft Cell Control Bend user interface relieves operators of much of the work. Taking account of material thickness and bending angle, BySoft Cell Control Bend determines the most suitable bending process and suggests relevant tools.

The ByBend Star 40 and 80 are two mobile press brakes with a new design and new drive system. The Dynamic Drive System concept allows for dynamic bending sequences without energy loss. Added to this are intelligent functions such as the Energy Saver and an automatic start-stop function.

Bystronic's ByBend Smart flexible, highspeed press brake increases the flexibility and speed of bending. The machine's main drive is only active when the machine is in operation. The Xpert 250-1000 (up to 2,000 tons in press capacity in a tandem configuration and up to 20m in bending length) has been designed for bending larger and more extreme applications. Bystronic's Xpress entry-level press brake is supported by intuitive BySoft Cell Control Bend software.

Bystronic's Xpert Pro press brake has now been equipped with the LAMS 4.1 angle measurement system's springback compensation for bending sheet metal. The Modular Tool Changer is an automation solution for the Xpert Pro press brake, designed to increase the productivity of the bending process “by up to 30 per cent." The Xpress press brake features the ByVision Bending user interface and ByMotion drive control. Options such as a mechanical crowning system and lower beam can be configured, if required.

The Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre 'plugand-play' press brake has already won a German design award and is designed for rapid, efficient parts production. A Mobile Bending Robot is available for feeding the Xpert 40, turning it into a compact, automated bending cell for producing 3D sheet metal components. The press brake also features optical tool detection.
