# High panel bender uptake

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

Machinery specialist, Salvagnini UK & Ireland, confirms that it is experiencing high order levels for panel benders from subcontractors based in the north of England and Scotland. In the past 12 months, no less than six of Salvagnini’s latest P2Xe and P4Xe panel benders with offline software have been installed at fabrication shops in Lancashire, Yorkshire, County Durham, Northumberland and Scotland.

“The modern UK trend for small batch, high product mix manufacturing means that set-up time has never been more critical to stay competitive and maximise profit margins. Zero set-up time using P2Xe and P4Xe panel benders is possible thanks to our ABA (automatic blank-holder system) technology, which can adjust the tooling in less than four seconds. Importantly however, this is done in ‘masked time’ i.e. before the previous cycle concludes,” Salvagnini told ISMR.

“All six of the recent Salvagnini panel bender installations at subcontractors in the UK’s north feature ABA blank-holders,” said Steve Williams, the company’s managing director. “Forward-thinking manufacturers now want to reduce WIP and run much leaner production. Using ABA, set-up time is a thing of the past.” See www.salvagninigroup.com
