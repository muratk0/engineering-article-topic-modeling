# Making its mark

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

INGYU PRECISION INDUSTRIES CO., Ltd. was established in 1970 and specialises in manufacturing high-speed and precision presses.

"We believe that design is the key to the quality. We keep the spirit of creating value for customers in mind and develop presses to meet our customers' requirements," the Taiwanese press specialist told ISMR.

INGYU's new Apex straightside, two-plunger, high-speed press has been designed to meet requirements for stamped components of high accuracy. The press features two-piece casting frames. The guiding post bushings form part of the bottom frame for high vertical accuracy.

"The new Apex, with automatic stroke adjustment, enables users to produce high accuracy sheet metal parts, increase the number of products produced per minute and reduce their tooling costs. Hydrostatic bushings decrease the friction area and suppress heat expansion to prevent guiding posts wearing out over the years. The quick access device enables the slide to move up 70mm in 30 seconds to help users quickly solve tooling problems. Eight different stroke lengths can be programmed through its SIEMENS control system. Shut-height and press speed adjust synchronously," added INGYU. Email info@ingyu.com or see www.ingyu.com/■
