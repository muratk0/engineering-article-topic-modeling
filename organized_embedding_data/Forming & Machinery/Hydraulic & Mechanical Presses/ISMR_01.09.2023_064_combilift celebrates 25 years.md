# Combilift celebrates 25 years

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Combilift, the Irish manufacturer and global supplier of customised handling solutions, is marking its 25th anniversary by introducing five new models throughout the year. Following the debut of the very compact Combi-CUBE in April and the 15,500lb-lift capacity Combi-CB15-5E in May, a further innovation will be unveiled on the first day of the FABTECH exhibition in Chicago, USA, at 11am on booth A2959.

"Our products, which have lift capacities from 2,000 to over 220,000lbs, are all designed for the safe, space-saving and productive handling and storage of the various loads typically handled in the fabricating sector, many of which are long and bulky. Our customised range is characterised by robust, straightforward design and is built for reliable long-life operation. All products are easy to service and maintain," said Combilift.

"The focus of the dozen exhibits spread across our two stands at FABTECH (A2959 and D4193) is on electric-powered products which are increasingly in demand from companies wanting to ensure that their carbon footprint is reduced whilst benefiting from powerful and productive performance alongside emission-free and quiet operation. Advances in battery technology have enabled us to offer increasing lift capacities, an example of which is the heavy-duty C10,000XLE with high ground clearance and roomy cab," it continued.

According to the manufacturer, there has been a move towards replacing ride-on forklifts with pedestrian-operated stacker trucks for safety reasons, particularly when working alongside pedestrians. Combilift will be exhibiting its Combi-WR4, multidirectional-reach stacker and the Combi-CS counterbalance stacker, which both incorporate a feature common to all Combilift walk-behind models; its patented offset tiller arm.

“This means that operators are no longer required to be in the dangerous 'crush zone' behind the truck but can now safely operate the truck from the side, where they also have excellent visibility of the load and any other people nearby," explained Combilift.■
