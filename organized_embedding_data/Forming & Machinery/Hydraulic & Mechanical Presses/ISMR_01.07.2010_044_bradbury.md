## LT COMBO

A combination laser cutting system for tube and sheet.

This 'two systems in one' allows the automatic laser cutting of tube from 16 to 225mm in diameter, and sheet with a choice in capacity from 3,000 x 1,500mm to 3,000 x 8,000mm

LT COMBO, by its versatile design, can be configured in a variety of formats to ideally match the application in terms of automation levels, productivity, capacity, available floor space etc.

This machine provides the perfect solution where laser cutting of both sheet and tube is required, but where the production volumes of both do not justify individual, dedicated machines — or where factory floor space is a restricting factor.
