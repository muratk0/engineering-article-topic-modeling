# TOOLING RETRACTION

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

In Vol. 12, No. 2 of ISMR, we stated that Mate Precision Tooling is the OEM tooling supplier to Salvagnini America. We would like to clarify to readers that Mate has not been an OEM tooling supplier to Salvagnini America for almost 20 years and apologise for any confusion caused.

Mate recently introduced its new patent pending Ultra IMT fully indexable multi tool for turret punch presses. Fully indexable multi tools can achieve any angle setting on the workpiece. Two versions are offered – the 3-Station (punching up to 6mm material with max. tonnage of 16 tonnes) and the 8-Station (punching up to 6mm material with max. tonnage of 7 tonnes).

"Ultra IMT is incredibly easy to use. Assembly and disassembly is very simple. No tools are required for punch, striker and stripper retention/removal," Mate explained.

It also features a drop-in quick tool change design that allows for fast tool changes.

See www.mate.com

## WELDING POWER

In 2009, French welding equipment and battery charger manufacturer GYS opened a new R&D Centre as part of the company’s R&D strategy. One major development from this investment has been the introduction of Power Factor Correction and Flexible Voltage technology across a range of its MMA, TIG and plasma machines.

“Power Factor Correction (PFC) technology offers the user a host of benefits, not least in reducing power consumption,” GYS told ISMR. “The requirement for less power also allows the user to weld at higher levels on a lower power supply. Using our PFC equipped MMA machines will allow high duty cycle welding at 160 amps (sufficient for 4mm rods) on a regular 13-Amp power supply, whereas normally one would expect to have to find a 16-Amp power supply. Normally, a 160A machine without PFC is capable of welding only 3.2mm electrodes at 125A using a 13-Amp fused plug.” GYS is also equipping machines with Flexible Voltage (FV) technology which can operate at any voltage in the range of 85V-265V and is suitable for 110V applications and wherever there is a likelihood of variations in voltage.

Currently, these technologies are incorporated into the GYS range of inverter MMA, TIG and plasma machines as well as the Plasma Cutter 31FV.
