# Flexible workpiece cleaning from Ecoclean

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Ecoclean has expanded its EcoCcompact workpiece cleaning machine to include the L and XL model variants to meet the diverse requirements of general industry. The new product family, with three working chamber sizes and batch weights of up to 150kg, enables capacity and cleaning performance to be adapted to company-specific requirements.

“An effective washing mechanism and high energy efficiency keep cleaning unit costs low. In addition, the compact plug-and-play solvent systems can be easily converted from hydrocarbon to modified alcohol," it told ISMR.

The diverse product range of the general industry includes workpieces produced by casting and machining, stamped, bent, pressed and deep-drawn parts, hydraulic and pneumatic components as well as fasteners. Depending upon the subsequent process or application, parts manufactured from different materials must meet different, but increasingly stringent, particulate and film cleanliness requirements.

Ecoclean EcoCcompact L.

“As with its smaller sister, it is easy to switch between hydrocarbons and modified alcohols (semi-polar solvents) during operation without any conversion work. The two new, compact plug-and-play solvent systems have working chambers with diameters of 650 (L) and 750mm (XL), enabling batch sizes of 650 x 470 x 300mm in the L version and 650 x 470 x 400mm in the XL version. The maximum batch weight for both systems is 150kg. Powerful, frequency-controlled flood pumps ensure fast filling and emptying of the working chamber. During standard injection flood washing, they generate a high mechanical cleaning effect," explained Ecoclean.

The systems can also be equipped with available process technologies for solvent cleaning, such as ultrasound and PPC. The vertical integration of the flood tanks reduces sump formation and prevents dirt pockets from forming. The result, said Ecoclean, is longer service life of the baths and a reduction in operating costs. Components made of different materials can be cleaned in one system.

"The EcoCcompact product family also features comparatively low energy consumption through optimised system technology. Flood tank two and the optional third tank are heated with heat recovered from the distillation process," added the manufacturer.
