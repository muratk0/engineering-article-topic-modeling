Waterjet cutting system has footprint less than 25 sq. ft.

WARDJet has introduced the A-0612 waterjet cutting system for industrial jobs. With a footprint of less than 25 sq. ft., the compact machine delivers the same performance as a full-size waterjet, the manufacturer reports.

The machine has a 2- by 4-ft. cutting envelope with the ability to cut materials up to 7 in. thick. The cutting head offers 4 in. of Z-travel to accommodate thick workpieces and adjust nozzle standoff distance.

It is powered by a 60,000-PSI pump that enables it to cut through any material in the same amount of time as a large-scale waterjet.

The industrial-grade rack-and-pinion drive system prevents slippage and stretching.

TRUMPF Inc. offers the TruBend series 3000 press brake. The closed-frame design provides high overall rigidity to help increase precision. The fastest machine in its class, the 3100 features a press force of 110 tons and 118 in. of bending length. The machine comes standard with a 2-axis backgauge, with the option to expand to a 4- or 5-axis backgauge.

Semiautomated positioning system introduced for ironworker punch station

TigerStop has developed a semiautomatic integration kit for the Piranha ironworker using its patented standard interconnect kit. The kit functions as a safety interconnect, preventing the ironworker from cycling while the positioning system is in motion and partially automating the punching or shearing process.

The kit moves the positioning system to the correct dimension within a cut list without the operator having to press start, but the operator still is able to reposition parts before the next punching or shearing cycle begins.

Portable magnetic drill features multiaxis positioning

CS Unitec’s new MAB 825 KTS portable magnetic drill features multiaxis positioning, allowing the drill to be adjusted up to 4¾ in. forward/backward and up to 4⅜ in. left/right after the magnet is adhered to the surface. The drill is suitable for job site drilling, boring, and slotting applications in which dismantling is not feasible.

With infinitely variable torque control and full-wave electronics, this heavy-duty drill cuts holes up to 3⅛ in. dia. with annular cutters in structural steel and other metals. The servoassisted drilling feature helps maintain feed pressure during operation. Additional capabilities include twist drilling up to 1¼ in. dia. and tapping up to 1 3/16 in. dia.

It offers a stroke length of 10 in.; a 16-amp, double-insulated, reversible motor; and an optional ¾-in. geared chuck.

Large-format dispensing system offered

The RD 2500 large-format dispensing table from Dispense Works Inc. combines a workbench with a built-in, precision, overhead servo XYZ gantry system. It is accessible from the front, sides, and top, requiring less operator reach to the center of the table.

The T-slot table base plate has 25 full-length slots to provide ample fixturing possibilities for most applications. Under this table surface, the machine base is fabricated from steel tubing and then is ground to a specified flatness.

This modular design provides a suitable foundation for mounting nearly any type of top surface, including T-slot tables, vacuum tables, panel conveyors, and custom plates.

The standard machine, which can travel at more than 32 IPS, is available in travels of 100 by 50 in. and 50 by 50 in.

Controller designed for fastener feed systems

Spirol has redesigned the Mark VI controller for its series 2000 fastener feed system. The controller has a 7-in. touchscreen display with 50-recipe storage capacity and Ethernet interface for remote access and control.

The controller monitors the feed system operation and continuously adjusts the output drive frequency to match the natural resonant frequency of the drive unit. The controller automatically adjusts the power level and synchronizes it with the two independent axes of motion within the drive unit to achieve maximum feeding efficiency, the company states.

Weld pads offer increased safety, comfort
