# Pressing solutions

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Schuler will be presenting a range of innovative solutions for increasing efficiency and flexibility in the press shop at the EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover from 25-28 October 2022.

"Under the trade show motto 'Innovative Solutions for a World in Motion, visitors to our booth 27-G82 will learn how turnkey lines can help them to open new fields of business and secure competitive advantage," said Schuler.

"We also support our customers in increasing the sustainability of their production and reducing their carbon footprint," emphasised Schuler CEO, Domenico Iacovelli. "With our numerous digital solutions, energy efficiency continues to improve. The shift to electromobility can also be mastered with our technology." Examples of Schuler's turnkey lines include the modular Triton single presses, as well as the newly developed press lines or Schuler's flexible laser blanking lines which, unlike conventional blanking lines, no longer require dies. Schuler will also be providing insights into the Smart Press Shop and its solutions for networking forming technology from the Digital Suite. These include solutions such as the “Track & Trace" system for seamless follow-up of components, the "Visual Die Protection" camerabased monitoring system or the augmented reality application “Schuler Connect" to support customers in the event of a service call. "Visual Quality Inspection" is a further application for the visual inspection of components which has recently been added.

For e-mobility, Schuler offers equipment for the production of electric motor laminations, as well as cylindrical or prismatic battery housings. The company's subsidiary, Aweba, supplies the appropriate dies for this application and others in sheet metal forming.

Sustainability is key. Solutions from Schuler's Digital Suite are designed to increase the productivity, efficiency and availability of the lines and reduce energy requirements. In the meantime, the press manufacturer also has plant technology for the large-scale production of bipolar plates for fuel cells in its product range, which convert hydrogen into electrical energy.

"The secret of our success has been our willingness to embark on new paths. It has taken many phases of change to transform our organisation from the small metalworking shop of the past to a global player in forming technology," it concluded.
