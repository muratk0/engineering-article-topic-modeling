# Tandem press line

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

CHIN FONG MACHINE has accumulated a number of sales records in tandem press lines for the automotive industry since 1986 with Toyota Motors. With experience amassed over years of technical cooperation with Europe and Japan, its A0 automated line has been manufactured up to 54000KN.

“Performance testimonials are available from customers such as Toyota, Ford, Honda, Nissan etc,” Chin Fong told ISMR.

The first press stage is deep drawing, following by trimming, piercing etc. The basic A0 line weighs 2400 tons with 800 tons of weight for the rest of the machines. To gain ground in production efficiency, Chin Fong replaced the conventional hydraulic press with a mechanical press featuring a link drive and long stroke.

“The hydraulic press has advantages in the deep-drawing process but production capacity is limited by the hydraulic system. By adopting a Chin Fong mechanical press, the link mechanism can slow down and maintain a steady speed for deep drawing during part forming, whilst accelerating the slide movement on the non-forming curve sections. In this way, production capacity can be increased by 30-40 per cent as opposed to using a hydraulic press. This layout is the popular practice in the field today and the A0 line can produce 8-12 pieces per minute with peripheral equipment,” the manufacturer explained.

The A0 line also includes de-stackers for material loading and a centring unit to position material accurately, a washing unit and conveyor, moving bolsters for tooling changes and a robot to transfer material. The automation unit has stacking, stripping, centring, washing, feeding and picking controls plus a die change system and clamping capability etc.

As the automotive industry shifts towards a “Go Green” mentality, materials such as light alloy and hard steels have been increasingly used in car components. Developing the right equipment for these material characteristics has become a major priority for stampers. By developing large servo mechanical presses, Chin Fong hopes to meet these challenges with its servo A0 line for automotive applications.
