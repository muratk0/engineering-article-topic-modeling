Automation system helps maximize cutting safety

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products offers AutoRex, a fully enclosed thermal cutting system that works with any of its automated cutting machines. The machine is combined with a shuttle table for automated loading/unloading and surrounded by an enclosure that blocks UV light emissions and reduces noise. The ventilation system captures all cutting smoke inside the cabin.

The shuttle table system features dual pallets. When one plate comes out for part and skeleton removal, the new plate is ready for exchange and immediate cutting. Loading/unloading outside the enclosure isolates workers from the cutting zone and enables the use of a crane without interrupting the cutting operation. The self-cleaning system moves all slag outside the enclosure and into a bin.

Shotblaster handles 3,000-lb. loads

Viking Blast & Wash Systems has released the 48T shotblaster with 48-in.- dia. capacity. Maximum work height is about 30 in. at the middle of the table and up to 34 in. at the edge.

The blast cabinet is constructed of 1/4-in.-thick AR 500 place steel reinforced with structural steel. It is lined with 1/4-in.-thick replaceable manganese plates in direct line with the stream.

Fiber laser machine offers extra-large capacity

LVD has introduced Taurus FL, a new large-format, gantry-style fiber laser cutting machine engineered for extra-large sheet metal cutting capacity. The modular machine has a 39-ft.-long bed that can be expanded in increments of 6.5 ft. to a maximum length of 131 ft.
