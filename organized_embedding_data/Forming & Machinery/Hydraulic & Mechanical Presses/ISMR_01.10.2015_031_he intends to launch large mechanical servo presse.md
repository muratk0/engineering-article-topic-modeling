# He intends to launch large mechanical servo presses at the end of this year with larger capacity and larger table areas

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

“Hot stamping press technology using boron steel is mainly used for automotive structural and safety components - it has become much more popular and we are seeing strong demand for it. The process ensures that steel is almost twice the strength of conventional steel which enables cars to withstand higher impacts over greater speeds so it is a very interesting process for automotive manufacturers. Nearly all the automotive OEMs are using this technology to manufacture the bodies of their vehicles,” he told ISMR.

“For cost reasons, boron steel (22MNB5) is not applied throughout the entire car body structure – instead, it is used in A pillars, B pillars and side impact areas to increase safety. Over the next five to ten years, I believe that every automotive OEM will adopt this technology.

“We supply most of the Japanese OEMs – Honda, Nissan, Toyota, Mitsubishi etc. – as well as North American customers such as GM, Ford, Chrysler etc.”

Patrick Wang has his eye firmly fixed on the future as he steers LCM towards new markets and new customers.
