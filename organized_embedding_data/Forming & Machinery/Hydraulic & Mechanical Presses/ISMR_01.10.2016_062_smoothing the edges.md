# Smoothing the edges

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

AT EUROBLECH 2016 (hall 11, stand J142), Heesemann will present its profiRounder, designed for dry mixed processing of steel, stainless steel, aluminium, titanium and copper sheet. It has been developed to meet a range of industrial applications on one machine: slag removal, edge rounding, oxide removal, surface sanding and glazing.

"The profiRounder is the first fully modular machine concept meeting all ATEX requirements for dry processing of all metals – a completely new generation of metal processing machines,' said Heesemann. "Completely modular design of the machine offers high flexibility with minimum space requirements. Contact rollers, brush rollers, disk brushes and the Fladder unit can easily be combined – even after installation, the machine base can be expanded by up to eight units by docking additional units onto the existing base.

The order of each module can be changed retroactively without major mechanical and electrical re-work (a plug-and-play concept). Fleece rollers and contact rollers (in special alignment in the machine) maximise abrasive lifetime. The fleece rollers are equipped with an automatic dressing device for increased service life and surface quality. Any wornout roller surface can be removed inside the machine itself, keeping maintenance for roller surface dressing as low as possible. Re-dressing can be repeated several times for a repeatable surface whilst also increasing the lifetime of the fleece roller.

Heesemann's RUT unit consists of several disk brushes moving on a framed path and rotating at a speed of up to 1,500rpm (radii of 2mm and more). The Fladder unit, which can be combined with any other Heesemann sanding and brush head, enables the profiRounder to remove very rough slag and oxide. Heesemann has also developed a quickchange-concept for the time-consuming replacement of transport belts and has redesigned the transport table to eliminate the accumulation of burr and the adherence of dust inside the table.
