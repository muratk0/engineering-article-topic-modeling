# New press launches from INGYU

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

The ApexKA series high-speed press.

## HV350-3000 multi-point, high-speed press

As the global wave of net-zero carbon emissions rises, the future of electric vehicles is quietly being rewritten. INGYU has therefore launched its HV350-3000 multi-point, high-speed press.

"The total machine clearance is extremely small and deflection of the machine body is within 0.05mm/m, ensuring the production accuracy of motor cores with a thickness of less than 0.25mm. Every second counts during the manufacturing process and our multi-point, highspeed presses are equipped with a slide quick-lift system that can quickly resolve mould issues without time-consuming recalibration, saving valuable production time. Siemens electronic controls are integrated with presses and servo feeders, allowing automatic adjustment of production settings and online support for troubleshooting, resulting in significant productivity gains," added INGYU.

INGYU is a Taiwanese precision stamping press manufacturer, established in 1970, which provides high-speed presses for automotive, can-making, motor lamination and complex forming applications. It has launched several new stamping presses as flexible solutions to meet new and changing challenges and applications.

## ApexKA adjustable-stroke press

“As the post-pandemic era approaches, we understand that customers need more flexible solutions to adapt to the changing market landscape. We therefore launched the INGYU ApexKA series of adjustable stroke, high-speed presses with stroke lengths ranging from 15-64mm. The ApexKA series is equipped with a bottom dead-centre compensation system that can reduce variation during the stamping process and ensure that the bottom dead-centre accuracy remains within three microns. This significantly increases product yield," INGYU told ISMR.

“We seamlessly integrate Siemens electronic controls with presses and servo feeders to enable the automatic adjustment of production settings, while also linking to an MES database for efficient collection of production and alarm data. With our online troubleshooting support, we aim to ensure efficient production lines," it continued.

## SVL series high-speed press

INYGU's latest SVL series high-speed press has a vertical accuracy of up to 0.001mm. It is tailor-made for plastic and metal materials as thin as 0.05mm.

“Featuring an extremely smooth mould, it achieves perfect cut edges during production. The speed of the slide is significantly reduced after reaching 120º, which not only reduces the impact force during stamping but also the noise level. The SVL highspeed press features servo-drive technology and an oil-free design, which makes it suitable for clean room production," explained INGYU.

The new SVL-10 is suitable for stamping ultra-thin plastic material as well as metals such as steel, brass, aluminium and stainless steel.■

KASTO, a German manufacturer of automated warehouses for storing and retrieving raw material (long stock and sheet) which also produces sawing machines for cutting to size, has been enhancing the connectivity and operational efficiency of its equipment.

"Whether it is a stockholder or a manufacturing facility using the storage systems and/or sawing machines, they benefit not only from higher productivity but also lower running costs through more economical use of electrical power. In addition, more data is available to drive endto-end decision-making, from order to delivery," it told ISMR.

"Manually executed processes no longer meet today's standards in the metal processing industry. Customers are offered sustainable concepts for automating and material to picking up the cut pieces. Processes include storage and retrieval; in-plant material handling; sawing; parts removal; marking; palletising and bundling," it continued.

Solutions from KASTO are designed to ensure continuously controlled, intelligent material flow. Machines, systems, goods and load carriers communicate autonomously to enable flexible, resource-efficient, economical storage and fabrication. In this way, said KASTO, users remain competitive despite the rising costs of energy and materials while at the same time improving their ecological performance. Digitisation and resource conservation are key components in the portfolio of the Achern-based company, which has a subsidiary in Milton Keynes serving Britain and Ireland.

## KASTOlogic

KASTOlogic is a modular warehouse management system (WMS) designed for bar and sheet-metal storage requirements. The software enables users to manage and monitor not just the processes in the warehouse itself but also upstream and downstream processes. Manually operated storage areas can also be integrated into the system using the mobile app.

The WMS software maximises energy efficiency using intelligent material flow algorithms and interfaces to all common ERP systems.
