# Industrial grinding and sanding

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

WITH THE ADDITION of nine new products, Chicago Pneumatic has created a comprehensive range of grinders and sanders. Its expanded CP3550 series are designed to offer 'high-quality finishes with minimal effort', it told ISMR.

"The expansion to a 17-model range allows perfect matching of applications and tools," explained Harald Odenman, Chicago Pneumatic's product marketing manager, material removal. "Their power creates greater torque for faster material removal rates, making users more productive. Efficiency is further boosted by easier tool handling and control, with better comfort. This allows rapid, precise work and reduces operator fatigue."

A 6" angle grinder has been added to the existing five models with 4-to-5" wheels. Four new die grinders – two standard and two extended – join the previous angle die grinder. These come complete with an Erickson 200 collet for attachment of ½" and 6mm mounted points and carbide burrs. The two current 5" angle sanders are now complemented by two new tools for use with 7" abrasive pads. The other two additions are low-speed sanders, whose gearboxes generate high torque with reduced speed (3,000rpm) for polishing or delicate applications. They operate with 7" or 9" sanding wheels or flapwheels and, like the angle sanders, offer a choice of models with 5/8" or M14 shaft threads.

Designed primarily for high-output work environments such as foundries, casting plants, fabrication shops and shipyards, CP3550 series tools are also suitable for production and maintenance operations involving cast iron, steel, stainless steel, aluminium, alloys and composites. Typical tasks include deburring; contouring; chamfering; fettling; weld seam removal; surface preparation; cleaning; polishing and finishing.

"Advanced features on all models include a rotating inlet swivel, which enables free manoeuvring and avoids hose knots. The streamlined safety lever found on each tool can be operated comfortably, even in thick gloves, and is easily directed downwards. Small-diameter handles give a comfortable grip, while a thermally insulated composite oversleeve avoids the discomfort of handling cold tools over long periods. Meanwhile, silencer foam technology reduces noise emissions," added the manufacturer.

Downtime for accessory changes on CP3550 angle grinders and angle sanders is reduced by a rapid and simple spindle lock feature requiring just one wrench. Keyless angle grinder guard adjustment enables quick changes, even when wearing gloves, to suit different work positions and to deflect sparks safely. See www.cp.com

## Fine abrasive waterjet cutting

CUTTING NARROW INCISIONS with precision in high-density materials using waterjet technology can be done using a specific waterjet cutting process: the Fine Abrasive Waterjet process (FAWJ). The first FAWJ cutting head was developed by Water Jet Sweden in 2008.

"Waterjet cutting is a common method for processing parts in high-density materials such as aluminium, stainless steel, titanium and carbon composites. The FAWJ cutting process bridges the gap between micro laser and EDM cutting and brings waterjet cutting into the field of micro part processing,' explained the Swedish waterjet specialist.

"To enable such levels of precision you need two things: (1) a cutting head and cutting process for FAWJ cutting, and (2) a machine built for extreme accuracy. The NCM 10 Micro fulfils both of those requirements," it added.

The micro waterjet cutting head was developed by Water Jet Sweden in 2008, enabling an abrasive jet diameter down to 0.2mm. The FAWJ cutting process requires very fine abrasives of 230-240 mesh and a special CNC-controlled dosage abrasive feeder. To reach the levels of accuracy required in micro part manufacturing, the NCM 10 water jet has a number of design features to create a rigid table that withstands temperature fluctuations.

The palletised cutting table makes it easy to install fixtures and presses for different types of machining. Maximum table size is 1x1m, which covers most cutting applications. The cutting table is fixed into the table frame to enable high precision cutting.

"There are many suppliers who state that they offer microcutting machines, but not many can offer a true micro part cutting tool with 0.2mm incision combined with ±0.008 accuracy," explained Tony Rydh, co-founder and CTO, Water Jet Sweden.
