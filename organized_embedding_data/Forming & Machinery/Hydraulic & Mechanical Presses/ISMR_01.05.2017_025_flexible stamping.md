# Flexible stamping

[TARİH: 01.05.2017 ISMR]

"IMAGINE THE VERSATILITY of a single press platform that can be configured for either a shell or cupping application wrapped up in a durable high-quality design – we are introducing the MP1-90 portal press, with the flexibility to do this as well as meet a wide variety of demanding applications," Nidec Minster Corporation told ISMR.

Rated at 90 metric (100 U.S.) tons, the MP1- 90 press is capable of 250 strokes per minute in shell applications and 150 strokes per minute in cupper mode. The press also features a 100mm (3.94") shut-height adjustment.

A massive cast iron frame provides low deflection and improved vibration absorption while a forged steel crankshaft also contributes to the lower deflection characteristics of the MP1-90. Oil film bearings give the press precision Bottom Dead Centre (BDC) repeatability while the combination hydrostatic/hydrodynamic guiding system enables precise slide guiding and improved part quality.

"We want to offer a low-cost, robust, durable and reliable press for this segment of the metal packaging industry," explained David Winch, CEO of the Nidec Minster Corporation. "The MP1-90 press is an economical multi-purpose press."

The MP1-90 press features a Minster pneumatic clutch and brake. A hydraulic overload feature in the slide protects the press frame and components in case of an overload condition. It is also supported by Nidec Minster's suite of customer service products.   
Nidec Minster exhibited at METPACK 2017 (2-5 May 2017) at the Messe Essen Fairgrounds in Essen, Germany. See www.minster.com

## Laser and resistance welding

AMADA MIYACHI EUROPE highlighted a range of laser and resistance welding equipment suitable for the medical industry at Medtec Europe in Germany recently. The spotlight was on the MIYACHI EAPRO Automatic Hypotube coating removal system, the MIYACHI UNITEK LMWS pulsed fibre laser welding system and the MacGregor DC613T linear DC spot welding power supply, which features a newly developed low-force dual weld head

"The MIYACHI EAPRO Automatic Hypotube coating removal system is suitable for removing biomedical coatings on round and cylindrical components such as hypotubes, springs, coils and guide wires. The laser ablation systems can also be used for advanced surface treatments, including discoloration, foaming and darkening or annealing. The clean non-contact laser process reduces or eliminates dust during manufacturing, and can be used to process most plastic and metal parts fed as a bundle or on a reel," explained the manufacturer.

The new LMWS pulsed fibre laser welding system is suitable for laser welding copper tabs to batteries, as well as welding thin wires such as thermocouples and guidewires. The LMWS, with the integrated 70-watt LMF70-HP pulsed fibre laser system, can be used for welding metals, plastics and dissimilar metals. By changing the settings, LMWS can also mark, engrave, cut, drill and ablate thin materials. Geared for low-volume production and research and development environments, it can also be transitioned to more automated systems when required.

"The MACGREGOR DC613T 1000 Amp precision linear DC spot welding power supply delivers ultra-fast 10 microsecond dynamic process response rates, with absolute closed loop 1-amp incremental accuracy and repeatability. The DC613T power supply was paired, at the show, with the MWH-Smart series of modular weld heads, a precision solution for quality controlled process monitoring applications. The weld head module can be fitted with optional force and digital displacement feedback transducers to ensure that welding force is always defined. The modules can be used with servo system-, pneumatic- or cable-driven actuators," concluded the manufacturer.
