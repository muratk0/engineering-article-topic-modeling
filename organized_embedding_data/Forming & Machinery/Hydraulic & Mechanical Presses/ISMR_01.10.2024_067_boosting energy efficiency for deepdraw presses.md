# Boosting energy efficiency for deepdraw presses

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

With its servo-hydraulic drive solution, Bosch Rexroth aims to increase efficiency in sheetmetal forming. It is one of the world's leading suppliers of drive and control technologies.

“Recuperation (energy recovery) can increase the energy efficiency of deep drawing presses by up to 30 per cent. The modular design simplifies engineering and installation for the machine manufacturer. End customers also benefit from reduced oil volume and smaller installation space. Sheet metal can be formed with increased productivity and quality. At the same time, the solution uncovers more opportunities for digitalisation. End users can minimise downtimes with condition monitoring, for example," outlined the manufacturer.

“The servo-hydraulic drive modules comprise a servo-hydraulic pump unit and control block which can be used to supply the die cushion and upper ram, as required. If both servo-hydraulic solutions are operated with a common electric drive system, a deep drawing press achieves energy savings of up to 30 percent compared to a conventional hydraulic press with valve control," explained Bosch Rexroth.

“The servo-hydraulic drive solution also unlocks additional possible savings by minimising the cooling requirements of the hydraulic unit. As a result, the power unit can be smaller and, in some cases, work without any cooling (which further reduces investment and operating costs)," it added.

The servo-hydraulic drive also enables databased analyses for process optimisation and condition monitoring to minimise downtimes.
