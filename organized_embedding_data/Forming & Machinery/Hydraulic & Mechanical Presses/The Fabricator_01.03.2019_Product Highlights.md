Tapping tool available in D station size

Wilson Tool International® has announced that the QuickTap™ tapping tool is available for the D station as well as the previously available C station size.

Manufactured for thick-turret punch press machines, the tool runs in the punch press, allowing users to eliminate secondary hole-threading operations. The tool taps a threaded hole with every machine stroke, at up to 200 holes/min. It can create strong, accurate threads at any programmed location on the sheet. Shavings from machine tapping are reduced or eliminated. The built-in tapping fluid injection system is synchronized with the machine stroke to help reduce wear and breakage. Its large tank enables the tapping of up to 40,000 holes before refilling.

Longitudinal seam welder includes material handling

MITUSA has developed a turnkey GMAW seam welding system that incorporates part loading and unloading for a medical equipment manufacturer.

The company engineered a motorized travel cart with hydraulic part lift for stainless steel shells weighing up to 1,500 lbs. in various diameters and lengths up to 72 in. The part lift has a roller assembly to allow the operator to position the seam manually in the exact location for welding. The system eliminates the need for a forklift or overhead crane. The operator uses a remote control to position and remove the part before and after welding.

Online strip width calculator updated with carbon steel tube and pipe categories

JMC Rollmasters Div. of Johnston Mfg. Co. Inc. has updated its Advanced Strip Width Calculator™ for tube and pipe producers. The free online calculator considers the effects of thinning caused by bending deformation, girth reduction in the fin passes, and the appropriate weld allowances to provide welded tube producers with estimated strip widths for their products.

The calculator’s material list now has three categories for carbon steel products: carbon steel standard mechanical tubing, carbon steel pipe products, and carbon steel DOM tubular products. These categories each use modified SyncIt™ values based on the varying forming stresses encountered and the weld quality requirements of each product. The answers provided for each value, however, remain unchanged from previous versions of the program.

Also updated is the calculator’s SyncIt value list, which allows users to synchronize the program more closely with their historical strip width tables and operational preferences. It now saves the manual settings on the user’s device and flags when manual values exist for each metal type. It also offers reference points on the drop-down list to allow comparison between manual and automatic settings.

Automated bending cell bends small parts at 4 sec. per bend

TRUMPF Inc. has released the TruBend Cell 7000, which the company says is the world’s fastest system for the automated bending of small parts at an average cycle time of 4 sec. per bend.

The cell comprises a TruBend 7036 Cell Edition press brake, the Bend-Master bending robot, the LoadMaster Bend material handling unit, a pallet system, and tracks, all within an enclosure that requires 69 sq. ft. of floor space.

The press brake offers a press force of 40 tons and high-speed acceleration. The ram is divided into two 20-in. sections to provide clearance for the grippers on the bending robot. The material handling unit’s multivacuum gripper prepares the bending part parallel to the current bending operation. A high-speed vision system identifies the exact orientation of the blanks. The flexible loading arrangement allows for up to 24 different blanks or 4,800 parts to be delivered to the machine.

Pipe sander/polisher suitable for small diameters

For surface finishing applications on smalldiameter pipe and handrails, CS Unitec’s new Pipe-Max Mini combination weld seam grinder and pipe sander renders weld seams invisible and can produce satin to mirror finishes on stainless steel, aluminum, and high-quality metals. With a 15-amp, variablespeed, 820- to 3,000-RPM electric motor that provides high torque, this 10.3-lb. tool deburrs, grinds, and polishes closed and open pipe constructions up to 31/4 in. dia.

The grinder features a continuous sanding belt speed adjustment from 10 to 31 FPS to accommodate the pipe material, as well as a continuous belt-tightening adjustment to suit the pipe diameter. The soft, elastic return roller gives the tool the flexibility to adapt to every pipe contour. Flat surfaces can be ground to remove spot welds and deep scratches, leaving no edges. The drive roller has replaceable rubber rings to help ensure the grinding belts will not slip.

Autonomous painting robot requires no teaching, programming

Omnirobotic offers OmniPainter ™, an autonomous robot that knows how to paint, without any teaching or programming. The company is developing AI so that robots can understand processes, not only repeat an instructed motion, which allows them to perform tasks on any products in real time.

The painting robot is adapted to continuous-flow finishing lines operation, supporting both powder and liquid coating application. It sees with a 3D camera, creates a digital model of the passing parts, uses the digital model combined with finishing knowledge to predict the best painting patterns, and then paints with control by AI.
