# FEATURED TECHNOLOGIES

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

## PRODUCTS & TECHNOLOGIES

More than 500 new product debuts are also expected to take place at FabTECH 2011.

AIDA-AMERICA CORP., Dayton, OH, (Booth 3125, South Building), will be exhibiting and demonstrating two servo presses from its extensive range of metal forming solutions. The first press on display will be a NS2-1600(D), 160-ton capacity, straightside servo press. This press will be part of a completely functional line, stamping parts will be presented at the show in cooperation with Formtek / CWP, Dayton Progress, AG Tool & Die and Anchor Danly. The press is provided courtesy of Royal Die & Stamping.

The second press in AIDA’s booth will be a NC1-1100(D), 110-ton, gap frame servo press demonstrating the servo press’s ability to reduce reverse tonnage. AIDA is a global leader in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metal forming automation equipment.

ATTEXOR CLINCH SYSTEMS is launching a new clinching portable machine: the SC 0101FS. Fast and light, for instantaneous sheet metal fastening, it is suitable for joining sheet metal profiles with small edges. Its rectangular toolkit allows rapid sliding along the clinched profiles. It avoids all the disadvantages of spot welding: no fumes, no sparks and no burning of the sheet metal coating.

THE BRADBURY CO., INC. will be featuring the e·Drive, a patent-pending advance in leveling technology, at its booth #2325.

“The e·Drive is the first hydraulic leveler that can penetrate the neutral axis of metal strips for complete stress removal. It levels material from 0.015" to 0.750". Rapid line speeds allow for a notably greater throughput than other leveling processes. Installation of e·Drive is simple, allowing it to enhance any cut-to-length line," the company told ISMR.

COHERENT’S METABEAM is a compact, powerful laser cutting system that is designed to be accurate, flexible and easy to use. Capable of handling a 50” x 50” (1.27m x 1.27m) sheet, it can cut a wide range of metals as well as plastics and organics. The METABEAM incorporates Coherent’s new low maintenance, sealed, pulsed 1000W CO laser.

FORMTEK MAINE will be displaying and running a complete 12” x 0.125” x 8,000lb press feed system. The feeder will have a new control platform that offers metal stampers a flexible, user-friendly operator interface. The new control has a 999-job memory with 11 inputs and 13 outputs (6 programmable) for today’s more complex stamping requirements.

KMT WATERJET has launched the ‘Future of Waterjet’ with the Streamline PRO 100,000psi pump. “In addition to increasing linear cutting by more than 100 per cent, the PRO Series of pumps cuts non-linear aluminium, titanium and stainless steel up to 75 per cent faster than standard 60,000psi waterjet cutting systems,” the company told ISMR.

“The PRO pump is the world’s fastest waterjet cutting solution when combined with KMT’s PRO series cutting heads: the IDE PRO and Autoline PRO.”

The KMT NEOLine 40i (40hp) pump uses KMT intensifier technology and is suitable for small job shops, sign and automotive shops requiring the versatility and flexibility found with waterjet cutting. The NEOLine 40i features 55,000psi operating pressure to cut steel, glass, plastic, aluminum, stone and much more.

MATE PRECISION TOOLING will exhibit its latest tooling system technology in booth 1330. EasyMark, EasySnap and SnapLock are just three of Mate’s broad line of special application tooling to be demonstrated. These tools mark parts for easier identification in downstream operations (EasyMark); create “shake and brake” joints for separating parts (EasySnap) and make interlocking tabs to lock parts together (SnapLock). Euromac customers can get up to triple grind life with Mate’s XMT24A two-piece punches for Euromac XMTE6 multi-tools. Mate’s ValuGrind precisely re-grinds punches and dies to maintain optimum condition for high quality parts production.

ROSENDAHL METAL TUBES & HOSES will present and share its latest advancements and technology highlights at FABtech 2011. Rosendahl Maschinen GmbH provides manufacturing solutions for smooth and corrugated tubes and hoses with diameters from 4mm to 200mm (walls of 0.1 to 4mm). Its main technological competencies are in metal forming, welding (TIG, laser and plasma welding), in-line heat-treatment, tube cold working and calibration and corrugation of circular round products.

TRUMPF’S new TruLaser 5030 Fibre is designed for high speed, efficient thin sheet metal and reflective material processing. Powered by the 3000-watt, fibre-guided TruDisk 3001 laser, the TruLaser 5030 Fibre can achieve triple the cutting speeds in certain applications and cost per part can be reduced by as much as 20 per cent. It is equipped with a universal cutting head for processing all sheet thicknesses.

The TruMatic 7000 combines the advantages of punch and laser processing and achieves high processing speeds. Active die technology enables scratch-free processing and advanced forming capabilities. The machine also features single-head cutting technology to process all material types and thicknesses. Automation options contribute to the high performance of the TruMatic 7000.

New functionality in UBECO rollforming software “Partial Project Add On” and “Partial Project Save As” allow users to create a new project by combining parts of old projects e.g. if a profile project with a new designed left half should be completed by a right half of an existing older project including the rolls. Further useful software extensions are the new arc type “Angle/Radius bending method” (compensating for material springback during flower pattern design).

VIBRO DYNAMICS now offers a full line of coil spring type isolators in a variety of sizes designed for use on stamping presses and other metal-forming equipment. ■
