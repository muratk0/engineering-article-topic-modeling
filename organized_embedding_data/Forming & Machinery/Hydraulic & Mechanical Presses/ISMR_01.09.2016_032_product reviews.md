## 5-axis waterjet cutting

JET EDGE, INC., a leading manufacturer of ultra-high pressure waterjet technology, is bringing its latest precision waterjet cutting technology to FABTECH (16-18 November 2016) on booth C25085.

During FABTECH, Jet Edge will perform live 5-axis abrasive waterjet cutting demonstrations on its X-Stream-powered EDGE X-5® 5-axis waterjet. The waterjet manufacturer also will introduce its new Permalign® V-Series Mini Hopper and show its 75,000psi (5200 bar) X-Stream® pressure intensifier pumps and closed loop water filtration system.

"Our EDGE X-5® 5-axis waterjet cuts precise taper-free parts from virtually any material. The system is capable of cutting sophisticated 3D parts, such as impellers and bevels, up to 50°. Its sturdy design separates the motion system from the catcher tank, eliminating vibration and ensuring maximum part quality. The ball screw-driven system features direct-couple AC brushless digital servo motors, providing repeatability at +/-.001". Productivity-enhancing options include a second cutting head with mirror cutting capabilities; a pneumatic drill for prepiercing materials that are prone to delamination; automatic submerged or above-water cutting capabilities and a proprietary laser plate mapping feature that allows precise nozzle-to-plate standoff," the manufacturer told /SMR.

Jet Edge's new variable aperture Permalign® V-Series mini hopper increases abrasive waterjet cutting productivity and profitability by allowing waterjet operators to easily make adjustments to abrasive flow, minimising abrasive consumption. Its 100hp (75kW) X-Stream® xP90-100 waterjet intensifier pump supports 75KSI (5200 bar) continuous operating pressure. The X-Stream, said the manufacturer, produces 50% more pressure than a 60KSI (4100 bar) intensifier pump, resulting in a 40-50% increase in productivity for many materials. See www.jetedge.com

## EDGE Connect CNC

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, is shipping a new CNC platform called EDGE® Connect. This next-generation CNC contains a number of enhancements for operator ease of use, reliability and customisable performance.

“New features include the latest version of Hypertherm's Phoenix™ software coupled with SureCut™ technology for ease of use and consistent performance for cutting applications. A software based operator's console and embedded PLC feature, called PLC Connect™, allows for greater customisation of a cutting machine with reduced costs. Enhanced CNC-based nesting for convenient operator programming with SureCut outcomes can be done via the ProNest™ CNC. Three new modern hardware options also improve performance, reduce weight and increase reliability," said the manufacturer.

Cutting machine customisation is further enhanced by quick and easy set-up through an EtherCAT communication platform and custom software configuration that allows for up to 12 axes through an online configurator. The hardware platform is the foundation and software selections enable any EDGE Connect to be set for various cutting applications. Cutting machine manufacturers can develop their own operator consoles and auxiliary programs using the Soft Op Con API and PLC Connect. For more information, please visit www.hypertherm.com

## Self-clinching pilot pins

PEM®self-clinching pilot pins from PennEngineering® are offered in a variety of designs to provide hardware solutions for positioning, alignment or pivot applications. They all install in thin metal sheets to become permanent parts of an assembly by being pressed into a pre-punched mounting hole of the proper size.

PEM Type TPS™ (300 Series stainless steel) pins install in steel or aluminium sheets and PEM Type TP4™(400 Series stainless steel) pins are designed for stainless steel assemblies. Both types install flush in sheets as thin as 0.040"/1mm or greater, feature a chamfered end to locate the mating hole quickly and easily, and are available in a variety of sizes and diameters.

PEM Type TPXS™ pins are engineered with a 15º tapered point to locate and engage the mating hole easily. The specially styled pin and proper captive retention screw wil meet ATCA PICMG 3.0 specification for 3mm alignment pins, said the company. These 16mm-long pins are made from 300 Series stainless steel and will install in aluminium or steel sheets as thin as 0.040"/1mm.

PEM Type MPP™ micro pins for compact consumer electronic assemblies can be supplied with diameters as small as 0.040"/1mm and in lengths as short as 2mm/0.080". They install into stainless steel or other sheet materials as thin as 0.020"/0.5mm. Upon installation, the pin's head mounts flush with the host panel to promote a smooth and clean appearance for the assembly. The pins can be installed automatically to speed production and eliminate handling issues. See www.pemnet.com
