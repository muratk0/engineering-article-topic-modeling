# Die changing cart

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

A NEW transport cart for safe and ergonomic handling and time-saving changes of moulds and dies of up to 500kg has been developed by Roemheld. The mobile cart has a height-adjustable table plate and a newly developed safety docking station.

The table plate is equipped with ball inserts which are lowered during transport so that the die is protected against movement. The die changing cart firmly docks to the transfer station at the machine table. By docking, the ball inserts on the table plate are lifted and the die can be easily inserted into the machine.

For bridging gaps, an additional ball location is available. After undocking, the balls lower automatically so that the die can be safely transported.

The new die changing cart is available in different versions with table sizes of up to 720 x 720mm and height adjustments of between 540mm and 1080mm. The hydraulic standard lifting column is adjusted by a single-lever foot operation. The new die changing cart was launched at the K-Messe in Düsseldorf and Blechexpo in Stuttgart. See www.hilma.de
