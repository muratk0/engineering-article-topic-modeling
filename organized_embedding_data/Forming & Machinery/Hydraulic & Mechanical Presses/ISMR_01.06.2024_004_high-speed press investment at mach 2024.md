# High-speed press investment at MACH 2024

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

A specialist supplier to F1, automotive and aerospace sectors aimed to increase its tool try-out capacity by purchasing its first Bruderer press at the MACH 2024 event in the UK recently. Alan Spargo Ltd, which will celebrate 50 years in business in 2025, has invested in a BSTA 40-tonne machine to help provide its growing list of customers with the option to test tooling solutions at high speed before they go into production.

"The technology can offer up to 1000 strokes per minute precision accuracy and will support the High Wycombe firm's desire to increase sales past the £3m mark this year. It builds on a long-term tooling relationship between the company and Bruderer UK, which has culminated in the development of a turnkey production line for MACH 2024 this week, the country's largest industrial show," Bruderer UK told ISMR.

“Specialist tooling from Alan Spargo has been developed in just six weeks to run on the Telford-based company's BSTL 350-88 highspeed press, the first time it has ever been run in the UK. The press comes into its own for precision and continuity and uses up to 30% less energy when compared to older machine models," added Bruderer UK.

Manufacturing precision components and complex tooling, Alan Spargo is a family business that supplies engineering solutions for customers in electronics, electric vehicles, solar panels, medical, defence and construction from its High Wycombe facility in the UK.

Adrian Haller, Managing Director, Bruderer UK, commented: "Alan Spargo's latest investment in a 40-tonne, high-speed press reinforces this position and gives it additional capacity to try out new tools at its Buckinghamshire facility, ensuring that the client can tweak stroke speeds and have complete confidence that, when it takes receipt of the tooling, it is going to run at high speed and with accuracy. We have worked together on several projects to build bespoke tooling that can be used on a number of our presses situated in some of the best engineering houses in the UK."

Peter Spargo, Director, Alan Spargo Ltd, added: "The last twelve months have been very good for us, and we've extended the workforce to 22 with the opportunity to recruit more staff, including new apprentices."

Bruderer's production line at MACH left Birmingham at the end of the exhibition and made its way to its multi-million facility currently being built at Hortonwood in Telford. It will form part of a demonstration facility that will be able to prove out machine tools, as well as being used as a potential training ground for future apprentices.

"The BSTL 350-88 high speed press, along with servo-feeder, decoiler and Alan Spargo tooling, represents a £500,000 investment," added Adrian Haller. "Construction work is progressing well, and we should be open for business in October, with up to six jobs set to be created across service, engineering and finance."■
