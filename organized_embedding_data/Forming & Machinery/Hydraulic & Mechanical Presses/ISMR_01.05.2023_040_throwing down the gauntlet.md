# Throwing down the gauntlet

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

At the Subcon exhibition in Birmingham (UK) this June, Treadstone Safety will be presenting its range of work safety gloves including the Premium Cut Resistant Atom1c engineering range; Hi Performance RazorPlex Cut resistant range and the PRO Assembly gloves. It specialises in cut-resistant gloves for engineering; assembly; metal handling; HVAC; oil industry; agriculture; manufacturing and impact protection as well as offering access to more gloves for a wide variety of industrial applications.

"Our flagship 'Atom1c' high-performance, cut-resistant glove range, for instance, features some of the best cut resistance/yarn diameter ratios. Our small, friendly and hard-working team aims to make Treadstone Safety one of the leading UK names in hand protection in the coming years. With a capable factory, producing some truly world-class gloves including an ever-growing portfolio of bespoke OEM gloves for distributors, the gauntlet has truly been thrown down...," the firm told ISMR.

The company is a member of the BSiF Registered Safety Supplier Scheme.■
