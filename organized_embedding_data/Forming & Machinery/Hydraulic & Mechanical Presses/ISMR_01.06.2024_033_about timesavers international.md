# About Timesavers International

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Timesavers International BV is a manufacturer of industrial wide belt grinding machines, with customers all over the world. Starting in 1939 as Linden Machine factory in Rotterdam with a focus on woodworking machines, it moved into metalworking machines in the late 1970s.

In 1965, the company moved to Goes in the province of Zeeland in the Netherlands and merged with Timesavers USA in 1996 to become Timesavers International. Today, the company is part of U.S. holding group; InCompass, Manufactured Growth Solutions. It specialises in deburring, edge rounding, finishing and precision grinding systems.

## About KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH

KOHLER develops and produces part levelling machines and strip feeding lines for presses and stamping machines as well as cut-to-length lines for steel service centres, mechanical engineering firms and for the automotive industry. At its levelling centre, the company levels sheet metal for contract clients and carries out levelling tests.

Its headquarters and production facility are in the southern German town of Lahr.
