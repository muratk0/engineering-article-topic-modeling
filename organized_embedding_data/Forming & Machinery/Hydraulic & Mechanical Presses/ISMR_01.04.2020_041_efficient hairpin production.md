# Efficient hairpin production

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

ON STAND F18 in Hall 10 at the WIRE exhibition in Germany later this year, Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG (Bihler) will present an efficient manufacturing solution for hairpins based on its servo technology.

"Electric mobility is gaining more and more momentum. Bihler has been involved intensively in the research and development of this new technology for several years and already offers groundbreaking automation solutions. Automotive suppliers use these solutions to produce battery cell and module connectors mounted in carrier frames, ZSB circuits for direct drives, shielding sleeves, busbars and hairpins for stators," it told ISMR.

The hairpin-like components are designed for safe and efficient power transmission in electric motors. They are manufactured directly from coils on a BIMERIC production system.

The servo system takes care of all process steps: starting with the dynamic, slip-free feeding of the wire, NC-controlled mechanical stripping of the coating with minimal crosssectional losses, simultaneous chamfering of the pin ends, pre-bending and 3D die bending, through to the final measurement of the component geometry and inline readjustment.

"Variant changes take place 'on the fly'. Depending upon the variant, the wire crosssection and the pin length, the output is between 60 and 120 hairpins per minute. The cycle speed is therefore around three times higher than with sequential systems, making the BIMERIC ideal for industrial production. Additional bending machines, cost-intensive peripheral equipment and space requirements can be reduced by using this complete system," added Bihler.
