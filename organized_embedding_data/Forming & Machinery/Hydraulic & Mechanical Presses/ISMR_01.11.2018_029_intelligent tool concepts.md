# INTELLIGENT TOOL CONCEPTS

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

What can tooling offer the operator in the age of Industry 4.0? Wilson Tool International shows ISMR the path to the future.

ISMR SAYS:

“Wilson Tool International brought a selection of new punch press and press brake tooling innovations to EuroBLECH 2018"

Wilson Tool International is the world's largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry. Products are manufactured at the company's worldwide headquarters in White Bear Lake, Minn. (USA), as well as manufacturing facilities in Canada, England, China, Brazil and Italy.

The company provides a wide range of tool solutions for bending, punching, stamping and tableting customers around the globe. For over 50 years, it has provided customised tooling solutions to help its customers to resolve challenging sheet metal manufacturing issues.

Wilson Tool International was at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover this October with a complete range of punching and bending tools for all major brands of punch press and press brake. ISMR sat down with Kelli Williams, Director of Marketing, and Helen Armitage, Senior Marketing Coordinator, Wilson Tool, for their views on the future of tooling in the age of Industry 4.0.

## A year of innovation

"It's been a really active year with a lot of exciting news and products. We acquired Thomas Engineering, a tablet tooling company in Illinois (U.S.A.), at the end of September 2018. We also announced the launch of a new additive manufacturing division in the USA in October. So, we now have four separate unique divisions – Punching (where we started 53 years ago), Bending ('Additive' fits within the press brake division), Stamping and Tablet Tooling," Kelli Williams told ISMR.

"From a worldwide perspective, we are seeing really strong growth levels. We stay close to our customers and talk to them regularly about factors that impact their manufacturing. We see a strong global economy and a great manufacturing climate right now. Our customers are busy and our tooling sales are growing across all the businesses. Across the board, the economy and industry look strong and we are optimistic about the future."

The company brought a selection of new punch press and press brake tooling innovations to EuroBLECH 2018. With a strong customer base in North America and Europe, it is very keen to grow its presence in markets such as the Middle East and has been increasing its attendance at international exhibitions. Challenges on the horizon include currency fluctuations and Brexit.

"Globally, much of our growth has come from acquisition. We are really focused on having one Wilson Tool worldwide, with the same approach, look, feel and branding whilst providing different levels of product offerings to customers. I feel that we are very well positioned to bring this to reality, at this point, as we continue to roll out the lean manufacturing philosophy in all our facilities. We are really rallied behind understanding the market as well as the needs of our customers, and we continue to find solutions (not necessarily tooling) to help them to be successful," added Kelli Williams.

## Additive manufacturing

Wilson Tool International recently announced the addition of a new division: Wilson Tool Additive™.

"On 1 October 2018, we launched a new additive manufacturing division, called Wilson Tool Additive. There are two different product lines – Bend 3D, our press brake tooling, and our Solv3D line. Our engineering capability and service is a real differentiator here – all our tooling technicians are expert engineers and can help to design and customise tooling to meet the needs of our customers. It puts us in a really great place from an additive perspective. We are initially launching this in the U.S. and North America and will then gradually roll this out worldwide," said Kelli Williams.

This new division will give Wilson Tool the capability to supply companies with made-to-order bending tools and fabrication support parts in hours – not days or weeks.

"Our Bend3D™ line of additively manufactured bending tools match the quality of traditional steel press brake tools used to bend sheet metal. They also work as forming tools, air-bending tools and mark-free bending solutions. The Solv3D™ line of additively manufactured support parts can replace end-use parts that have been traditionally manufactured out of steel or plastic. They can replace items in a shop that often require an expensive mould to be created. They can even replace jigs or fixtures that hold things together and can be used as a prototype," explained Kelli Williams.

## Press brake tooling

Wilson Tool manufactures a full selection of European and WILA/TRUMPF-Style precision press brake tooling and clamping systems that are designed to increase its customers productivity and efficiency. It launched its press brake tooling division in the USA 28 years ago and offers a one-stop shop from accessories to tooling to clamping.

"The European market has been very strong for us on press brake tooling," commented Helen Armitage, Wilson Tools' senior marketing coordinator. "We pride ourselves on being able to offer the whole range for our customers (European-style, TRUMPF style, Bystronic style etc.) as a one-stop shop. We are always improving on different tooling ranges and new concepts. New innovative tooling ideas, on display here at EuroBLECH, include the Corner Former and our Express Crowning™ system.

"The Corner Former is a game-changer – this tool eliminates the need for secondary operations such as extra welding and grinding. Operators can bend a four-sided pan or cover and form the corner in the same handling with staged tooling across the length of the brake – the Cornerformer will leave a nice finish so that no secondary operations are needed like welding and grinding. We design a lot of special tooling for specific applications. We are also keen to show our customers how they can form on a press brake so we are showcasing a tool here at EuroBLECH that has a press brake adaptor so that you can use TRUMPF-style punch press tooling in a pressbrake, and operators can use the press brake to its full advantage."

The Express Crowning™ product was originally launched in 2017 and is now being rolled out worldwide. It offers a feature that enables press brake operators to control deflection across the length of a bend and the ability to fine tune the deflection curve with micro-crown in much smaller sections.

## Packing a punch

The main new punch press tooling products on the company's booth at EuroBLECH were The Pen, a sheet metal marking tool with an automatic mechanical cap to maintain pen life, and the QuickTap™ PX tool for Pullmax PX punch presses.

"A lot of the time, our customers ask how we can help them to reduce specific secondary operations. So, for example, our QuickTap™ tooling has been developed to help our customers to save time (it can make up to 200 taps/minute). The Pen can be used to mark serial numbers, bend lines or assembly instructions directly on a component. Our sales engineers look at ways to improve processes when they see customers and maintain a constant dialogue with them. We also design a lot of special tooling for specific applications," explained Helen Armitage.

The QuickTap™ PX tool is an extension of the QuickTap™ line of tapping tools launched in the past 18-months. The QuickTap for thick turret punch press machines is available for D and C station sizes. Manufactured for thick turret punch press machines, QuickTap™ is among the fastest tapping tools available, with a short delivery time. It enables fabricators to eliminate time-consuming secondary operations related to creating threaded holes by running the tool right in the punch press. A built-in tapping fluid injection system is synchronised with the machine stroke to reduce wear and breakage. The tapping injection system's large tank enables the tapping of up to 40,000 holes before refilling.

The company's new QuickTap™ PX tapping tool is an extension to the QuickTap™ series that was launched last year for thick turret style machines. The new tool is designed to run on Strippit/LVD PX and Pullmax punch presses, and can tap at around 120 taps/minute, for substantial time savings.

"A lot of sheet metal contractors have a laser and a press brake and are starting to realise that they miss their turret punch press. We are consequently seeing an uptick in turret punch press sales and operators who want to figure out how to punch and form in the press brake. We now have this great new Brake Partner™ product that allows them to form in the press brake. We are seeing much greater demand for this. Laser cutting systems are very flexible, of course, but there are specific things that they cannot do. The application is certainly still there for turret tools and we see growth in this area," added Kelli Williams.

## Intelligent tooling

So, what is the future of tooling in the age of Industry 4.0?

"We are developing intelligent tooling that will suit our customer needs – for example, we have launched iSeries tooling that is compatible with AMADA AITS. This complies with Industry 4.0. We showcased an intelligent tooling cabinet at Blechexpo last year and are taking forward this concept - work is still in development," explained Kelli Williams.

"It is important to understand the changing and ageing workforce; we are designing our intelligent tooling to support the untrained and new workforce that is entering the sheet metal sector. There is a skills shortage in the industry – we need to make things as easy as possible for operators. Our R&D team is also looking at technologies, such as Augmented Reality (AR) and Artificial Intelligence (AI), for the future."

iSeries™, the company's line of thick turret tooling, is designed to be fully compatible with the AMADA ID Tooling System (AITS™) tool management system. Laser-marked with a data matrix code on each compatible tool, the iSeries™ product line brings sheet metal fabricators more thick turret tooling choices for their AITS machines. It is designed, Williams told ISMR, to deliver shorter lead times at competitive pricing. HPX® and HP2 tooling include a mechanically fastened guide retention feature for proper tool assembly during loading with pushbutton guide removal.

Wilson Tool has also opened its doors to a new generation of machine tool operators, and those still in education, to learn about manufacturing on Manufacturing Day each year. It shows high school students its manufacturing facilities and discusses the changing face of manufacturing with them, as well as the high-tech requirements of the sector. The company also offers apprenticeships and internships worldwide - many of its current team members started as apprentices.

"From what we are hearing, there is a need for more than tooling. To support our customers' need to educate their under-skilled workforce, our team has worked hard to produce application-specific problem-solution videos. It's broader than just tooling. We have launched around 25 new problem-solution focused videos and over 30 application videos on our website and on YouTube. In 2018, we launched a new video series called 'Technically Speaking' that is focused on technical applications and problems, prepared by our experts with step-by-step instructions. These are currently in English but will be translated into different languages. We have also done a lot of on-site and webinar education – it's all about instant access to knowledge..." added Kelli Williams.

Wilson Tool took the concept of 'Challenge and Solution' and used it as the theme of its booth presence at EuroBLECH 2018. All the videos running on the booth, viewed by ISMR, depicted challenges faced by its customers and solutions found for them by Wilson Tool. Specific customer solutions and special press brake/punch tooling developed for customers were also on display. It is clear that this tooling specialist has its sights firmly fixed on the future...
