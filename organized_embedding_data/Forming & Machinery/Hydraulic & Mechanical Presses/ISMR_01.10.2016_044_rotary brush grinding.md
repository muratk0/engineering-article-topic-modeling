# Rotary brush grinding

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

AT EUROBLECH 2016 (25-29 October 2016), Dutch company Timesavers will present a combination of its rotary brush machine, WRBW42, and a robot on booth K42 in hall 14. The workpieces, to be deburred or edge-rounded, pass the machine in a dedicated zone on the conveying belt; the robot identifies the parts, turns them and, for the final brushing operation, places them on the opposite zone of the conveying belt.

“Using this combination, rotary brushing makes enormous gains in profitability – only the loading and unloading of the WRBW42 needs to be carried out by the operator. The development also increases the level of integration into fully automatic processing lines, as expected from the forthcoming Industry 4.0 standard," explained the manufacturer. “Whether the parts are prepared by gas, plasma, laser or water jet cutting processes, or whether they are punched or machined (flat or embossed), our brushing process guarantees reliable deburring and edge rounding of steel; stainless steel; copper and aluminium – galvanized, coated, laminated or even printed."
