# Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG (Bihler)

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.bihler.de

As a leading system supplier in stamping, forming, welding and assembly technology, Bihler offers 'Made in Germany' technology. It provides turnkey solutions, design support, tooling, training and other services to its customers.

The family-owned company, with more than 1,000 employees, supplies manufacturers of stamping and forming parts, as well as assembly producers, with machine systems, flexible process modules and intuitive control technology combined with automation solutions. Its BM-HP 3000 servo production system is designed to provide efficiency for the industrial mass production of hairpins. Other products include the fully servo-controlled BIMERIC forming system and standardised LEANTOOL (radial/progressive tooling).

Bihler is also a provider of 3D technology software for the construction of stamping and forming tools.

The company was founded in 1953 by Otto Bihler, an aircraft mechanic who reportedly developed the first automatic universal springwinding machine (UFA 1).
