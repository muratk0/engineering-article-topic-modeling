Robotic plasma coping robot can be programmed in minutes

The Kraftech X-Calibre robotic plasma coping robot can process most structural steel profiles, including up to 47.5-in. I-beams, 16-in. rectangular and square hollow sections, 15-in. parallel flange channels, and 8- by 8-in. angle. Infeed and outfeed conveyor lengths are customizable. Larger-format machines are available, as well as optional plate and pipe processing. With a 3D CAD model, robot pathways are generated in minutes by offline programming and nesting software. Automatic pathways are created using DSTV-NC files, so the user can load the program and the machine does the rest.

Plate processing machine combines thermal and mechanical cutting

Tecoi North America offers the THOR (Tecoi hyper operative range) plate processing machine designed for heavy-duty thermal cutting applications. The system offers mechanical cutting with the capability to manufacture parts using multiple operations, including milling, drilling, tapping, countersinking, and marking.

It includes one or two cutting heads and an optional beveling system, as well as oxyfuel cutting with up to 12 torches and an auxiliary tube and profile lathe. The central XX axis has a path of 24 in. working on a robust double-beam design. It completes machining tasks with a fixed gantry and the option to fit up two machining heads.

Tecoi North America

www.tecoi.com/en

Four-roll plate bender’s CNC control center includes onboard material libraries

Carell Corp.’s VBH series four-roll plate bender offers greater bottom roller pinching force, higher drive torque, and a 3D CNC control center with onboard material libraries.

Linear sliding guides and a multiple bearing rolls support system maintain the necessary machine stiff-ness and geometry for bending operations. A high-torque reduction unit reduces the number of rolling passes.

Digital control of the roll parallelism with hydraulic proportional valves maintains a parallelism of 0.1 mm. Roll adjustment speeds and central lower roll pinching pressure are infinitely variable and controlled from the operator mobile panel.

The STRADIVARI control center was developed to be independent of operator skill. Utilizing native 3D-ROLLCAD software, the user can create the bending sequence automatically, starting from a drawing.

Carell Corp.

Robotic profile processor handles copes, holes, bevels in one pass

The V807 robotic profile processor from Voortman USA can thermally process nearly any kind of shape that needs to be produced with speed and accuracy. It can process copes, bolt holes, slots, notches, bevels, weld preparations, and layout marking in one pass with the ability to reach all four sides of the beam.

With fully modular and largely integrated components, the machine can be extended with more automated logistics, or complementary machines can be added to accommodate business growth.

Voortman USA

www.voortman.net

CNC cutting and drilling system also handles variety of milling jobs

DRM Max from United Precision Services is a heavy-duty CNC cutting, drilling, and milling machine with applications in offshore, wind tower, heavy vessel, column, reactor, and heavy-duty machinery segments.

The machine has a dedicated drilling/milling area with an independent subgantry and two retainers that allow milling up to 29 1/2 in. along the X axis. The robust design enables drilling up to 23/4 in. dia. and a variety of milling jobs such as large hole milling, face milling, pocket and slot milling, chamfer milling, thread milling, and counterboring.

Equipped with up to a 55-kW direct-drive spindle, the system can be equipped with other accessories enabling plate, pipe, beam, and dome cutting.

United Precision Services

www.unitedprecisionservices.com

Heavy-duty plate beveler offers portability, ergonomic design

The Steelmax BM 25 portable plate beveling machine produces machined bevels from 0 to 90 degrees with a maximum bevel width up to 1 in. using a high-speed rotary milling head with five replaceable carbide cutting inserts. Equipped with a variable-speed 2,200-W motor to help ensure efficient weld prep of steel plates, it offers an ergonomic design that allows each user to adjust grip and handle position for personal comfort.

The machine produces a milled finish that requires no additional grinding or secondary work, and plates are weld ready. It also can be used for 90-degree plate facing for square groove butt welds.

Steelmax Tools

steelmax.com

Robotic CNC cell automates every aspect of structural fabrication

Machitech offers BeamCut, a 6-axis robotic CNC cell that uses high-definition plasma technology to cut metal profiles for every structural steel and miscellaneous metals industry.

The system serves as an alternative to traditional angle lines, beam drill lines, band saws, hydraulic punches, ironworkers, and coping machines, producing up to 10 times faster than traditional cutting methods, according to the manufacturer.
