# DUST AND FUME EXTRACTION

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

Flextraction Ltd, supplier and manufacturer of Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) products, has launched a new range of self-supporting Hood Positioning Devices (HPD) - the Flextractor HPD, Extended HPD, Mini HPD and Flextender HPD. They all incorporate new mounting brackets, hoods and hoses for the capture and extraction of industrial dusts and fumes including hazardous and corrosive fumes at the point of source.

These HPDs have been designed to be used in a variety of industrial applications, including surface grinding, welding, product tipping and drum filling as well as all manufacturing applications where dust and fumes are emitted. They meet HSE requirements for the correct positioning and design of LEV hoods and are self-supporting mechanisms providing stability and accuracy in the carrying and positioning of a wide range of hoods in different sizes, weights and designs including individual customised hoods plus hose combinations. Without any internal obstructions, there is no wear, corrosion, flow restrictions or solids built up within the hoses.

Flextractor HPD’s are suitable for standard wall or stanchion mounting, as well as upward and downward swivel, joist and ceiling mountings and are available in five standard lengths between 1.5m and 4m. The Flextender HPD has a telescopic extraction arm giving the user a 1,200 - 1,650mm reach, especially useful in low headroom or limited space applications. A range of hoods is available in a wide variety of designs. For further information, e-mail: lkd@flextraction.co.uk
