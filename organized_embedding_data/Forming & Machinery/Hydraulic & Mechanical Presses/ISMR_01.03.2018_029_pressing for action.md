PRESSCARE IS A UK engineering company predominantly operating with power presses, coil handling equipment and automation systems. It offers a comprehensive range of services to the sheet metal industry including servicing; safety inspections; electrical & mechanical repairs/modifications; preventative maintenance programmes; machine moving; installation & commissioning; guarding solutions and CE marking.

"One of our specialities is the design and integration of machine control and safety systems, from minor safety upgrades to complete rewires. We can design, manufacture and integrate a new control and safety system to bring your press shop equipment into line with the latest safety standards," said the manufacturer.

PressCare is the UK/ROI agent for the SEYI Group, one of the world's leading manufacturers of power presses. It provides a comprehensive service from initial advice on the correct machine for an application to installation; guarding; commissioning; warranties; technical support; preventative maintenance and spare parts.
