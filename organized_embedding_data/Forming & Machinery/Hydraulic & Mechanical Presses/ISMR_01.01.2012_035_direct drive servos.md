# DIRECT DRIVE SERVOS

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

SEYI Europe GmbH will be exhibiting its 200-ton direct drive servo press, SD1-200, at EuroBLECH 2012. With its focus on environmental protection and cost saving efficiency, SEYI Europe GmbH will be showcasing new possibilities in production methods for stamping, bending, drawing and coining in one single workstation.

During EuroBLECH 2012 on booth B54 (Hall 27), SEYI’s working production line will be showcasing future servo press possibilities specifically for working with high tensile steel such as AHTSS in automotive sectors. SEYI, one of the world’s largest press manufacturers, has created a new benchmark in class with the innovative compact design of a press frame combined with its own motor design – a specially developed, low speed servomotor with high torque and low energy consumption.

“Combined with a logical ‘easy-to-operate’ free programmable control system, it is possible to achieve advantages such as dramatically reduced power consumption, improved productivity, extended tool life (5-10 times) and reduced tool noise up to 20%,” SEYI explained.
