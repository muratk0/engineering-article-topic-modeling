# New press repair contract

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Rhodes Interform, part of Group Rhodes, is a specialist in mechanical and hydraulic press manufacture and refurbishment. It has just announced another major project win in the aerospace industry.

The contract, recently completed successfully, is phase one of two for the refit of a 1989 vintage 3000-ton 'Chester Hydraulics' SPF press, at BAE Systems Samlesbury in the north-west of the UK. The press is a critical piece of equipment that manufactures major parts for the Typhoon (Eurofighter) aircraft.

Rhodes Interform's technical director, Peter Anderton, said: “This is a specialist repair to the 'hotbox' ensuring that the stringent temperature accuracies required during the manufacturing process are maintained. We won the contract based on our vast expertise in the manufacture, design and repair of SPF presses in the aerospace sector. The lockdown was obviously a difficult time for the industry, but over the past few months we have won several contracts for new machinery and equipment refurbishment. We are very optimistic for the continued recovery of the UK aerospace sector."

Rhodes Interform's machine refurbishments offer customers a solution to dramatically improve equipment life and increase productivity. The company provides a complete one-stop refurbishment service, from arranging where necessary for the machine to be transported to the company's maintenance facility in Wakefield, through to retrofitting new components and re-commissioning on-site.

Rhodes Interform's parent company Group Rhodes, based in West Yorkshire, boasts a 200- year metalforming history. The company has won Queen's Awards for both Innovation and International Trade in recent years, particularly for its work in the aerospace sector.
