# Mate to showcase new tooling solutions

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

MATE PRECISION Tooling will exhibit its latest tooling system technology at FABTECH 2012 (Booth 3720). Fabtech is North America’s largest fabricating, metal forming, finishing and welding event. The company will introduce RAPIDSET™ adjustable thick turret canisters for use with Mate Original Style and AMX™ punch press tooling.

RAPIDSET is also compatible with Amada Standard, Amada ABS and Wilson Standard tooling styles. Designed to provide quick tool changes and make punch length adjustment faster with existing tooling inventories, RAPIDSET feature a non-rotating clamping head with easily adjustable clamping screw for setting punch length, textured Gription™ canister feature for ease of handling and a pre-loaded, self-contained spring pack that provides consistent stripping pressure, die penetration and reduced slugpulling. It is available in A and B station canisters.

Triple punch grind life is promised with Mate’s Extended Life Punch for Euromac XMTE10 multi-tools. Hinge tooling, EasyMark™, Easy Snap™ and Snap Lock™ are just four of many special application-tooling systems to be shown at Fabtech that will improve productivity, eliminate secondary operations and improve product quality, Mate told ISMR.
