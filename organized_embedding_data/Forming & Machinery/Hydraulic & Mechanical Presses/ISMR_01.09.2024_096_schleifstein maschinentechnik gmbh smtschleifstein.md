# Schleifstein Maschinentechnik GmbH (smt|schleifstein)

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

https://schleifstein.de/en/company/

"smt|schleifstein is your global, reliable partner for equipment in the automotive industry, tank-head production and the shipbuilding industry. You will find us globally with locations in China and the USA, and sales offices in South Korea, India and central Asia," the company told ISMR.

With almost 130 years of experience in machine building and manufacturing, the family-owned company offers blank-and coil washing machines, dry cleaners, re-oiling systems and automation for the automotive industry. Its second strand is the supply of tank-head manufacturing equipment, shipbuilding presses and 2D/3D-segment forming presses. Related manipulators, crane automation, tools and dies for several forming operations, flanging machines and installation and integration equipment/ controls are also provided. Automation/conveyor systems can be added to machinery on-site.
