# Seat rails formed with hydraulic deepdrawing presses

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

Hydraulic deep-drawing presses are especially suited for deep parts, complicated shapes and special materials.

LASCO offers tailor-made presses and lines for sheet metal forming with its TZP series as well as numerous options and automation solutions.

## Top and bottom deburring machine

Machine for edge rounding and finishing of parts in a single pass.

The oscillating system is equipped with 12 brushes. The 6 top and 6 bottom, left and right rotating grinding flap brushes process the parts on both sides simultaneously

## Multi rotating brush machine

Machine for uniformly deburring, rounding edges and fishing of parts. For laser, punched and machined parts, or parts with pockets/protrusions in all directions. The rotating carrousel, equipped with 8 left and right rotating abrasive flap brushes, processes the parts in all directions, no matter how fine the edges of the work pieces are.
