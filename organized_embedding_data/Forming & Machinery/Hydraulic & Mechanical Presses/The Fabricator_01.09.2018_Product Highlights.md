Horizontal band saws designed for on-the-go applications

Dake Corp. has added two new benchtop horizontal band saws. The semiautomatic SE 5X6 RCT model comes with gravity down-feed and reverse cutting technology, while the SE 5×8 is a manual-feed system with a two-speed motor.

The model with reverse cutting technology runs clockwise, which throws chips away from the material and saw and provides a fine finish, according to the company.

The saws cut metal rounds, flats, and tubes in the field. With two handles on each base, they can be set on a cart and wheeled onto the production floor or placed in the back of a truck for cutting up to 6-in. rounds on a worksite. The saws feature single-phase 110V and a 2.5-HP motor; a constant speed regulator, which adjusts speed according to the amount of torque needed; variable speeds with 114 to 262 FPM; mitering up to 45 degrees; a blade tension indicator with clutch for proper blade tensioning; and a quick-release vise for fast setup and changeover.

Warm forming method streamlines fabrication of titanium parts

Quintus Technologies has developed High Pressure Warm Forming (HPWF), a process that combines high pressure with a moderately elevated temperature for forming titanium parts.

According to the company, the new process paves the way for more widespread adoption of Ti6Al4V, the grade of titanium used in many manufacturing sectors because of its high strength, light weight, formability, and high corrosion resistance. The process removes barriers to fabrication that previously limited the use of Ti6Al4V to specialty applications.

HPWF uses an induction heating system to warm the blank-and-tool package to about 520 degrees F—well below the temperature at which a protective gas is required—just before it enters the Quintus Flexform™ press. The press applies uniform high pressure (20,000 PSI) to a flexible rubber diaphragm in conjunction with a single rigid tool half to form complex sheet metal parts to final shape.

A complete cycle, from heating and loading to forming and unloading, takes less than five minutes. Forming at relatively low temperatures enables quick cooling. According to the manufacturer, the technology increases production throughput on Ti6Al4V parts by a factor of five over traditional hot forming processes.

Pusher stacker allows automatic blank processing

Biele Group offers a multimode pusher stacker, designed to enable fully automatic processing of tailor welded blanks for structural automobile components.

These pieces may come out of the press at a rate of one, two, or four pieces per stroke, either in line or in parallel. To manage these configurations, the conveyors between the press and the stacking zone, as well as the stacker belts, must run in start-stop mode.

Piece management in the stacking zone allows full flexibility in terms of possible piece combinations on the pallets, enabling stacks of up to 32 pieces on the same pallet with different row/column combinations. Left - and right-side pieces may be stacked automatically on the same pallet or on different pallets.

Abrasive saw cuts through INCONEL alloy in seconds

Kalamazoo Industries has custom-fabricated a saw that cuts 3-in.-dia. stock in 8 sec., 5-in.-dia. stock in 15 sec., and 7½-in.-dia. stock in 29 sec. The manufacturer equipped the saw with a 75-HP motor, a specially formulated wheel, and the newly developed TruArc™ oscillation system for increased cutting speed without burrs.
