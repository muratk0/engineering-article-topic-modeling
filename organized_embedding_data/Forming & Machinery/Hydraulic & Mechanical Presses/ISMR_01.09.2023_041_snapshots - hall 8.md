## AIDA

## www.aida-europe.com

At Blechexpo 2023, visitors to AIDAs stand can see DSF-NE2 monoblock servo presses as well as the entire AIDA press line series including servo presses, high-speed automatic presses for lamination and precision forming presses. "The AIDA DSF-NE2 is a monolithic two-point servo press. Equipped with our own low-RPM, high-torque servo motor, it can reach speeds of 120 strokes per minute. The presses guarantee low-noise, low-vibration, multiple varied-length forming with flexible applications and long die life spans. The DSF-NE2 series was awarded Product of the Year in the 'Metal Forming and Punching' category at the EuroBLECH Awards in 2022," said AIDA.

AIDA MSP lamination presses produce motors such as EV motors, HEV motors and energy-efficient home appliances' motors. AIDA MSP presses are suitable for producing parts with thicknesses of less than 0.20mm.

## Asservimenti Presse Srl

## www.asservimentipresse.it

Italian manufacturer, Asservimenti Presse Srl, produces equipment for press automation (decoilers; straighteners; cut-to-length lines; transfer and handling systems; robotics etc.) for sheet-metal working. With more than thirty years of experience, it currently exports its technology to over 20 countries. It has completed over 2500 installations globally and specialises in press automation, aiming to optimise the production cycles of its customers.

The manufacturer produces feeding lines with decoilers (up to 25-ton capacity with external diameter of 2000mm) which can process steel strip of up to 2000mm wide and 14mm in thickness. It has placed a firm focus on coil-processing lines that help to offset high material and energy costs. The company's latest new coil-cutting lines can process aluminium and high-tensile steel. It has installed many of these lines across Europe. Its latest developments also incorporate automatic coil-change on its lines and the new SMART HMI control panel.

## Gabella Macchine SpA

## www.gabella.it

Gabella Macchine is an Italian manufacturer with 60 years of experience in the production of cut-to-length (CTL) lines; slitting lines; multi-blanking lines; press brakes and other equipment to process sheet metal. Since the 1960s, a division of the company has been active in the manufacturing and sale of roofing and cladding materials.

"This has also led to the continuous improvements of our GP-26 slitter, with automatic slitting heads. It is designed to cut sheetmetal strip without the need for large sets of blades, rings and spacers. Material width management is handled by the line control panel, significantly reducing set-up time, with the option of saving width configurations and simultaneously moving the separators into automatic mode. [Cut quality is consistent] for all material thicknesses, with regulation of the cutting clearance to avoid burrs," it continued.

Today, Gabella Macchine remains a family-run company (second and third generations of the family).

## Roland Electronic GmbH

## www.roland-electronic.com

Roland Electronic manufactures double-sheet detection, weldseam detection and positioning systems as well as special products.

"We have been developing, producing and distributing highly specialised systems for factory automation and quality control since 1965. Our sensors and controllers solve tasks that are not solvable with standard sensors. Our systems also prevent damage to tools and manufacturing equipment and avoid the production of defective finished products and semi-finished products by using non-destructive testing and measurement methods," it commented.

Its SND40 weld seam detection system is a flexible measurement system based on the leakage flux method (magnetic flux) or the eddy current method. Depending upon the material, welding process, wall thickness etc., various options are possible for the measurement process and resulting program parameters. Roland's double-sheet detection system prevents two or more sheets/blanks from inadvertently entering the equipment. This avoids damage to tools and dies, stamping presses, expensive repairs and loss of production.

## Saronni Srl

## www.saronni.it

Saronni has expanded its range of precision straighteners with new performance levels, new designs and new technologies for coil processing. It has developed multi-roll straighteners (21 or 19) with reduced roll diameters, solutions to limit production bottlenecks and "Automatic Straightening Adjustment" (or ASA) software with high levels of automation.

Saronni, which celebrated its 70th anniversary in 2020, is a technologically advanced coil-feeding line producer. Its products include coil-handling equipment; compact feeding lines; traditional feeding lines (with loop); cut-to-length lines; embossing lines; coil-to-coil lines; straighteners; levellers; combi-straighteners and levellers with automatic straightening roll cassette-change; highspeed servo-feeders; zig-zag servo feeders; automatic disk cutting lines; coil welding units and more. It is based at Borgo Ticino (Italy), near Malpensa International Airport in Milan.

"Precision, accuracy and reliability underpin our developments and we continue to complete successful installations worldwide," it concluded.
