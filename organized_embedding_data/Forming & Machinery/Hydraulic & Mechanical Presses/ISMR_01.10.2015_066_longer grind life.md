# Longer grind life

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

WILSON TOOL INTERNATIONAL (www.wilsontool.com), the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, has launched the Dura-Die parting tool.

“The Dura-Die’s replaceable die inserts are made from our Ultima tool steel, which allows greater wear resistance to achieve maximum grind life. The die base is made from shock-resistant steel, allowing it to withstand even the highest punching force. Combined, these two steels give fabricators strong durability and performance,” commented the tooling specialist.

“Economical replaceable inserts, which are a lower-cost alternative to replacing the entire die, mean that fabricators can simply replace the die inserts for quick changeovers. By combining the Dura-Die parting tool with our Dura-Blade product, metal fabricators can greatly increase the life of parting tools. When a new die is necessary, the removable insert can easily be replaced with minimal downtime.”

Dura-Die is available for standard parting in C, D and E stations (close-to-clamp is available in D and E stations). It is also available with positive and SlugHugger2 relief options.
