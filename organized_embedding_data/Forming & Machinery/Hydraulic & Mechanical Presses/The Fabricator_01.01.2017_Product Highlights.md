Press brakes feature hydraulic crowning system

MegaFab has introduced the Piranha line of press brakes. The WPH and WPB models feature the Easy-Crown™ hydraulic crowning system, which eliminates guesswork and rework by compensating automatically for deflection through hydraulic cylinders in the brake’s bed. The operator programs the part, material type, thickness, and tools into the Delem control, and it automatically adjusts for accuracy.

The press brakes are available in four models, from 30 to 3,000 tons, including options for large-tonnage tandem brakes and electric servo-driven machines.

New housing, mixed-reality technology added to automated production systems

Wemo has updated the design concept of its automated production systems with a colorful, strong housing. It eliminates the need for a safety fence and protects the production line from dust, dirt, and other outside influences to help reduce failures, stoppages, and damage.

The housing also provides a clear view of the process, and the modular construction allows quick access to all parts of the machine for maintenance, service, and tool change.

The manufacturer also will add Microsoft HoloLens mixed-reality smart glasses to its machines to connect all available and useful data directly to machines and their operators. Relevant holograms are projected into the real world via the intelligent headset, creating a digital workplace. The operator gets immediate access to data for analysis, processes, and maintenance.

With the intelligent headset, an operator can control the machines from anywhere. Cameras placed inside the machines make it possible to view the production line process live from a distance.

Hardware/software system recognizes, picks randomly placed parts

MotoSight™ 3D CanonVision is a hardware/software system from Yaskawa Motoman that uses a single 3-D machine vision head to recognize and pick parts that are randomly placed in bins.

The system uses a Canon camera, which employs projected light patterns to locate the parts. Lighting is integrated as part of the camera unit, eliminating the need for additional lighting.

Single-step recognition reduces the need for multiple cameras. Once the part is recognized through 3-D CAD matching, the location and orientation data is transferred to the robot controller via Ethernet.

The system includes interface software for integration with the company’s robots, rack-mounted graphics workstation, calibration target, automatic bin locator, and empty bin detection. Up to 200 part models are supported. Three camera models are available to cover a variety of part and bin sizes.

Grinding wheel finishes with low vibration

Rex-Cut Abrasives offers the Sigma Green Max grinding wheel. Designed for aggressive grinding, it delivers high removal rates with less destructive finishes and reduced wear on operators, according to the manufacturer.

Suitable for use on metals such as stainless steel, mild steel, hard face, titanium, alloys, and aluminum, the grinding wheel can be used in such applications as foundries, tank fabrication, heavy weldment fabrication, shipbuilding, and power utilities. Featuring smooth, chatter-free operation, the wheel is available in 36 grit in 4-, 5-, and 7-in.-dia. sizes with ¼-in. thickness.

Fiber laser cuts steel up to ¾ in. thick

Cincinnati Incorporated offers the CL-960 fiber laser for fast processing of thin material and cutting of steel up to ¾ in. thick.

Features include air-assist cutting, linear-drive motors, touchscreen controls, 6,000-W light source, dual-pallet configuration, fiber resonator technology, and flexible glass fiber beam delivery.

Plasma machine offers 3-D plate cutting

Voortman has updated the V304 plate cutting machine for processing multiple types of material, such as mild steel, stainless steel, and aluminum. It can be equipped with up to two plasma (bevel) torches and up to six oxyfuel torches to allow for 3-D plasma bevel cutting, strip cutting, and weld preparation.

The machine is designed with gas valves as near as possible to the end process to help improve gas control and extend consumable lifetime. 3-D collision protection prevents damage to the torch when a collision occurs. It uses a magnet holder so that the torch can be mounted back in the holder to minimize machine downtime after a collision.

A dross inhibitor feature sprays oil at the piercing area just before piercing, which allows the slag to bounce off the oil layer so it will not attach to the plate.

Embedded PCs offer computing power for small DIN rail-mounted controllers

Beckhoff offers the CX8190 embedded PC for Ethernet communication, which provides increased computing power to small controllers. It is the first PCbased controller in an ultracompact bus coupler format for TwinCAT 3 automation software, says the company.

According to the manufacturer, the 32-bit, 600-MHz ARM Cortex™-A9 processor offers three times the CPU performance compared to the existing CX8000 series, as well as an eightfold memory increase with 512 MB of RAM.

The PC, which uses a Windows

®

Embedded Compact 7 operating system, comes with an Ethernet port and a two-port switch for real-time Ethernet or EtherCAT Automation Protocol (EAP). The small controller is programmed with TwinCAT 3 via the fieldbus interface or the additional Ethernet interface. TwinCAT 3 I/O software provides the basic run-time functionality, but further supplements can be added as options.
