# Yih Shen Machinery Co., Ltd. (YSM)

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand D39

https://ysminternational.com/

Established in 1979, YSM is a global specialist in stamping and bending manufacturing solutions. It has a workforce of over 150 people placed globally and, annually, produces upwards of 200 machines to be sent out to customers around the world.

"We are one-stop-shop solution providers in stamping and bending; process automation; welding systems; thread forming systems and inspection systems," the company explained. "We have been able to help our customers with cost reductions for new investments on projects by standardising our machines and making our stamping and bending machines modular in structure."

YSM has placed a strong focus on tool design, continuous innovation and customer service in its global operations. Its headquarters are in Taichung (Taiwan) with various support facilities around the world, including a Technology Centre in India. YSM has delivered various projects in Asia and Europe, designed to remove its customers' dependence on manual processes and operations by automating equipment.
