The Resato Job manager, Resato EMS monitoring software and the Job Manager module ensure that multiple cutting jobs can be programmed. The EMS monitoring software monitors the cutting process 24/7. Products also include the double intensifier pump and the abrasive continuous supply mini.

StM Waterjet GmbH is a provider of waterjet cutting systems with offices in Eben, Austria; Schweinfurt in Germany and Reiden in Switzerland. It has developed the OneClean modular system. The abrasives recycling module of the OneClean system makes it possible to recycle more than half of the garnet for additional cutting tasks, it told ISMR.

Its waterjet cutting systems include the StM MicroCut, StM EcoCut, StM MasterCut and StM PremiumCut. The StM TAC/12 bevelcut compensation cutting head is designed for precision, speed and the efficient use of resources in waterjet cutting.

Its EcoCut 4.0 waterjet cutting system features overall height of less than 2m, a working area accessible from three sides and above/below water cutting.

Swift-Cut Automation offers the Swift-Jet Pro, its waterjet cutting solution, along with the Pro, HP and HVAC Pro models. Its new Swift-Jet Pro 1500 compact waterjet made its first public appearance at its UK Open House this November.

TCI Cutting, a Spanish manufacturer of laser and waterjet cutting machines and intelligent automation systems, also develops intelligent production management software for autonomous and strategic decision-making (TCI Manager). This software improves production in the industrial sheetmetal cutting sector through intelligent communication systems machine-to-machine and machine-to-person, in an environment of integral automation and digital transformation.

Its BP-H COMBI cutting machine integrates, in a single machine, water cutting technology and HD plasma cutting. This machine incorporates the 5x Direct Drive 4.0 Infinite Loop® five-axis cutting head, which allows infinite rotation without rewinding in three-dimensional cuts, a height regulator, an anti-collision system and automatic sludge extraction, among other features.

TECHNIWaterjet manufactures water jet cutting solutions for the metals, composites, glass and stone industries. Now part of the GMM Group, it controls the entire waterjet process in-house with its own developed servo-driven pump and five-axis cutting head and holds several patents on its technologies. Its Electric Servo Pump is designed to be efficient and quiet.

"Our new Intec-G2®waterjet machine cuts parts up to 12" thick in most materials. Simple to use, its versatile cutting allows users to cut multiple material types on the same bed without having to change tooling," it explained. "Our Quantum NXT™ Series pump incorporates technology from the NASA space shuttle programme, making it 60% more efficient than standard hydraulic intensifiers."

The Intec™ G2 Abrasive Waterjet Series range includes the i35-G2; i510-G2; i713-G2; i815-G2; i1020-G2 and i1033-G2 machines. A remote-control pendant (MPG) allows users to manually wind forward or backward through a cutting path. Its PAC60™ five-axis cutting head is designed to produce parts with a true angle up to +/-60 degrees.

Uhde High Pressure Technologies GmbH (belonging to thyssenkrupp) has been designing and manufacturing ultra-highpressure pumps for pressures of up to 14,000 bar for industrial use for almost half a century. Its high-pressure pumps, the core element of the waterjet cutting system, with stepless working pressures of up to 6,000 bar are implemented in many industrial sectors, from the food industry to aerospace.

Water Jet Sweden, based in Ronneby (southern Sweden), is a well-known waterjet cutting specialist with over 100 different waterjet cutters from entry level 2D machines and micro processing to large machines for large and odd sizes of material, with its fiveyear performance warranty.

It develops, designs and builds industrial waterjet cutting machines starting from entry-level, with a wide range of optional functions, through to systems for highly bespoke waterjet cutting optimised to specific requirements. It celebrates its 30th anniversary this year.

TCI Cutting's BP-H COMBI waterjet/HD plasma cutting machine.

"From 1m², up to the largest at 6m x 15m, these are professional, heavy-duty waterjet cutting machines made to last with typically 20+ years of machine life. Available from single head up to six abrasive cutting heads or 12 pure water heads, there's a choice of cutting table configurations and Z axis units with linear motors for maximum flexibility," it commented.

The FiveX Ultra is its new full 3D, five-axis cutting machine (0-120° cutting) designed to manage precision cutting assignments. As the FiveX Ultra can be equipped with a FAWJ Micro Cutting Tool, it also manages extremely fine cuts down to 300 my, for precise and complex shapes. The Fine Abrasive Waterjet (FAWJ) cutting process bridges the gap between micro laser and EDM cutting and brings waterjet cutting into micro part processing. The NCM 10 Micro is its micro abrasive cutting model.

Its Alphajet, which it dubs 'the next generation of taper angle control and perpendicular waterjet cutting' is mechatronics based on compact design with a fixed TCP point. Up to four Alphajets can be fitted on the same machine, quadrupling the cutting capacity.

WJS has showcased an Augmented Reality, 3D version of one of its advanced waterjet machines, the FiveX, so that visitors could get closer to the operational cutter and look at it in 1:1, 1:4 or 1:10 scale, and then take it home with them on their smartphone or tablet.

The company's entry-level waterjet cutting systems include the T-model and H-model. Its Grand range is a heavy-duty waterjet cutting system with large tables sizes to manage large and odd-sized materials.

Italian manufacturer, Waterjet Corporation Srl, offers a range of new hybrid and electric servo intensifier pumps (such as EDrive, EDrive Pro and ServoDrive). Its Edge 5 cutting system features integrated ITC. Its machines include cantilever and bridge designs and five-axis flying bridge technology (Edge 5) which delivers significant productivity improvements. The company also offers a combination plasma and waterjet machine (the PlasmaJet model), as well as waterjet surface texturing technology.

Its waterjet cutting systems include the three-axis Prima and Practica; the three-/ five-axis RAM and Waterline as well as the Classica and Suprema. Its TopJet is an automatic waterjet cutting line.

Wazer is now offering what it calls 'the first desktop waterjet' at 864mm x 635mm x 534mm. The cutting area is 305mm x 460mm.
