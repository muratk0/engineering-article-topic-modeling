# New acquisition for Voortman Steel Machinery

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Voortman Steel Machinery, a well-known manufacturer of CNC steel processing machines, has acquired Müller Opladen GmbH, a German manufacturer of pipe processing machines. This acquisition expands Voortman's product portfolio, adding profile cutting machines for steel pipes, pressure vessels and structural steel.

Voortman is a family-owned manufacturer of steel processing machinery and software solutions for over 50 years. What started with the production of beam processing machines, followed by plate processing machines, has now evolved to pipe processing machines.

With a century-long market presence, Müller Opladen GmbH is a global specialist in 3D pipe-cutting machines for the small, middle and large range of profiles. It has customers in around 70 countries and has installed approximately 1,000 machines.

Voortman aims to provide increased value in growing markets, such as offshore & energy; and process equipment, by expanding its portfolio. This acquisition enables Voortman to broaden its product range with pipe processing technology. Müller Opladen GmbH will continue to operate in Germany.

"The integration of Müller Opladen is an important step forward in meeting the needs of our current and potential customers and offering a complete product portfolio for profile, plate and pipe processing. We are excited to welcome Müller Opladen to the Voortman family and are looking forward to jointly creating new business opportunities and driving future growth," concluded Mark Voortman, CEO of Voortman Steel Machinery.
