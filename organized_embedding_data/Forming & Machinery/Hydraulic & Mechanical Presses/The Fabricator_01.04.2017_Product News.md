Compact tool changer automates press brake

Bystronic has developed an integrated tool changer system for its Xpert press brake. The tool changer automatically sets up the press brake with the upper and lower tools that are required for the job. According to the company, during manual operation, this step takes up 70 percent of the entire bending time.

The 6-axis robotic arm integrated in the press brake accesses the tool magazine, which also is integrated within the machine and houses the bending tools. The entire automation system is located within the press brake, protected from dirt and ambient influences.

In combination with the tool changer system, the press brake offers a bending length of almost 10 ft. The available press capacity is 150 to 320 tons. As standard, the machine comes with an intelligent bending process control and fully automatic crowning. Both functions enable a variety of parts to be bent with high repetition accuracy.

App monitors sawing machines

Kasto has introduced an app for management of its sawing machines. Using smartphones and tablets, operators can query and monitor machine status at any time from anywhere on the company premises. If necessary, they can respond right away.

The app shows all important information at a glance. It displays the status of all Kasto sawing machines in the network that are equipped with the Easy-Control or ExpertControl systems.
