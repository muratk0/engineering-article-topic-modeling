# Press hardening for higher product quality

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

AP&T'S NEW PROCESS monitoring system for press hardening, called in-line process monitoring, is now being introduced to the market. It is designed for higher and more consistent quality of formed vehicle parts, leading to shorter cycle times and better control over the entire manufacturing process.

"Our monitoring equipment enables our customers to achieve an optimised and well-controlled process that gives the endproduct a higher level of quality," said AP&T Project Leader, Technology Development, Jörgen Theander. "The system was produced in cooperation with our customers and is designed in line with our specifications. It has been practically tested both externally and in our own press hardening line in Ulricehamn over a one-year period. The results have been excellent, and several of our customers have already decided to invest in the new system."

Press hardening technology is designed to enable sheet metal parts to be manufactured with low weight and high strength, resulting in lighter, safer and more energy-efficient cars. AP&T is one of the world's leading suppliers of complete production solutions for press hardening.

Pyrometers and infrared cameras are used to measure the material's temperature, with a high degree of precision, before and after pressing. The pyrometer registers the absolute temperature at a certain point, while the infrared cameras are used to read the heat distribution on the part's surface. All of this takes place without any of the equipment coming into physical contact with the material. It is crucial that the blank is heated up, formed and cooled down at exactly the right temperatures to ensure that the finished vehicle part has the desired material properties. The higher the degree of precision, the better and more consistent the result. See www.aptgroup.com
