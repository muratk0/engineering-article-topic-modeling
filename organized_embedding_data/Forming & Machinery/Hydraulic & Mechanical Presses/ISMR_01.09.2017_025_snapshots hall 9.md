## AGATHON www.agathon.ch

AGATHON, A supplier of high precision guiding elements and centring systems, provides standard and customised solutions for high performance tools. Two different roller types, as well as balls made from different material, are designed to fulfil tough requirements.

"E-Mobility requires high-performance tooling to increase the efficiency of the motors, batteries and more to boost the cruising range of electric vehicles. Cutting gaps and sheet thickness are being reduced to minimise loss, causing burrs on the punching parts. More efficient, more precise and stiffer guiding systems are a starting point to keep the precision, quality of the stamped parts and tool life high. In addition, maintenance is the key to success and therefore reassembling of the tool must be possible without losing precision and time," the company told ISMR.

## DREISTERN GmbH & Co. KG www.dreistern.com

"THANKS TO THEIR high production speed, roll forming systems are often not fully utilised with a single product. It is therefore appropriate to manufacture several different products on one system. Our production systems are designed for rapid tool change and frequent product change-overs," Dreistern told ISMR.

Another technology from Dreistern, multifunctional roll forming technology, considerably enhances this capability. It is based on standardised processing modules for punching, bending or welding that can be quickly integrated at any point on the roll forming machine. With universal compatibility and the related flexible control unit, individual processing modules can also be used on different machines.

## OMAB Srl

www.omab.eu

STARTED IN THE EARLY 1900s, OMAB specialises in the supply and manufacturing of complex metal components and assemblies using blanking, bending, drawing and assembling technology.

In addition to series manufacturing, OMAB offers a full tooling and tool maintenance shop. This includes a co-design service, engineering and prototyping up to production of the tool for serial production. Its manufacturing department has forty presses (Bruderer; Bihler; Balconi; Ompsa; Ross; Piovan and Invernizzi models) with tonnages ranging from 20 to 250 tons.

"We also provide our customers with a full complement of secondary operations with automatic, semi-automatic or manual processes such as riveting, contact welding, sub-assembly, machining, tapping and more. ISO TS 16949- and ISO-9001-certified, we operate in the automotive, electromechanical, electronics, fastening/clamping and appliances sectors. We produce a wide array of components such as terminals; springs and clips; reeled parts and frames; assembled parts; injectors components and small to large structural metal parts.
