# Fladder’s owner takes a long term view of the market and encourages a culture of continuous innovation

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Fladder Danmark A/S develops flexible deburring systems for the industry – its systems undergo a continuous process of improvement and development. This includes not only the mechanical parts and functions, but also the tools – the abrasive cylindrical brushes used in the machines. Fladder produces deburring systems to client requirements. For example, it has produced machines with working widths of up to 2m, which is the maximum width of sheets at the moment.

Fladder Danmark showcased its new FLADDER® 200/GYRO, which removes burrs and rounding edges, at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition. The abrasive tool in the FLADDER® deburring system is a flexible tool with a number of slats or strips of abrasive material which rub tightly against the part, closely following its contours

“Despite being our smallest machine, it offers the same features as the larger ones – the 200mm lock-it spindles with the latest 400mm brushes provide up to 40% more grinding. A newly developed vacuum turbine for fixation of work pieces has approximately 30% more effect. A new eco-system detects the flow of work-pieces and, when there is no flow, sets the machine on standby and finally shuts it off unless new work-pieces are put through. A touch-screen offers more intuitive operation of the machine,” the company told ISMR.

## A long term view

“Our philosophy is to be a long term group supplier and we are looking for long term business. We have a culture within the company of continuous improvement, in terms of product development, service etc. Our product line today is based on 100 current valid patents and we place a lot of emphasis on R&D and innovation,” the company’s owner, Keld Hundebøl, told ISMR.

“We started a venture with a second generation, German company (Heesemann) in another field of grinding technology (a calibrating technology) in which we had not yet been involved. This type of cooperation will benefit our customers because many of them need both types of grinding technology and we can support them more fully on this. Our relationship with this company goes back ten years or more.”

In fact, Fladder’s new Combi machine offers grinding and deburring in one pass – it is a combination of a belt grinding machine from its German partner, Heesemann and Fladder's own deburring technique.

“We have exhibited at EuroBLECH for the last 20 years. It is a very useful event because it is specialised in sheet metal and our products are particularly used in this industry. Visitor numbers are good here, people are actively looking for equipment and our feeling is that the business is improving (although activity has been relatively slow since 2008 and the global economic downturn),” added Mr Hundebøl.

“Nowadays, we are involved in the aerospace, train manufacturing, automotive and transportation equipment (eg truck) markets. We also see a future for our products in new areas which need a combination of materials (i.e. composites and metal etc.). When materials need to be joined together, a safe connection and careful surface preparation is needed. We see this as an opportunity for our products, particularly because 3D shapes are used and these can be complex to process.”

Fladder recently supplied large systems (up to 5 tonnes) for the deburring of heat transfer products used in nuclear power plants for a new US customer. Over the last few years, it has also marketed a new selflocking spindle system called Lock-It, featuring safe connection between spindle and tool. It is based on a new patent and enables tool changeover in just a few seconds. It can be used, Fladder told ISMR, not just on its own machines but also on packaging machines and other industrial machine tools from different manufacturers.
