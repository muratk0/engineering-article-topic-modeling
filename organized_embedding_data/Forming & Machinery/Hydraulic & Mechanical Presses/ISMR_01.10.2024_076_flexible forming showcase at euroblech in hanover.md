# Flexible forming showcase at EuroBLECH in Hanover

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

At its booth E104 co-hosted by Wirtschaftsförderung Heilbronn-Franken in Hall 27 at EuroBLECH 2024 (22-25 October 2024) in Hanover, Germany, DIEFFENBACHER will showcase hydraulic forming presses and complete press lines for sheet metal forming and the production of stainless-steel parts. Digitalisation and sustainability solutions, along with expanded services and automation, will be highlighted. The stamping specialist will also present its modernisation and conversion portfolio.

"One of our strengths is plant automation," explained Georg Obermaier, head of the Forming Business Unit, DIEFFENBACHER. "Our solutions with sophisticated gripper technology ensure short cycle times with coordinated processes. This increases productivity and ensures high part quality."

With extensive diagnostic options, the company's EVORIS digitalisation platform helps plant operators to save energy and raw materials. The EVORIS Reports app provides information about conspicuous deviations in production relating to individual process parameters, overall plant performance and cycle time. The app also displays consumption values for the entire plant, highlighting anomalies between production output and energy consumption.

DIEFFENBACHER's embossing press to stamp plates for stainless steel heat exchangers will also be showcased in Hanover at EuroBLECH 2024.

"Our TailoredPress, an economical all-rounder, can reduce energy use by up to 70% (depending upon the application). Variable-speed pump drives minimise unnecessary energy consumption which can occur, for example, during loading and unloading, set-up and maintenance of the press, and even in many phases of the normal press cycle. Control is fully automatic. The operator sets forces, paths and parameters as usual, and the required speed is automatically calculated and set. In this way, the pump always operates in an optimal range and works efficiently," explained the manufacturer.■
