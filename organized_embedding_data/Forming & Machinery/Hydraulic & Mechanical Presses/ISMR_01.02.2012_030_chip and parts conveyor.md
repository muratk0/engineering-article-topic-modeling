# Chip and parts conveyor

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

ERIEZ’S CHIP and parts conveyors employ a self-contained, continuous series of permanent magnets to automatically move and elevate ferrous turnings, chips, parts and small stampings along the outer surface of the stainless steel slider plate. They feature the Tuf-Trac internal track system to allow quiet, virtually maintenance-free operation and long service life.

The conveyors are designed using a heavy-duty, enclosed construction to prevent jamming or clogging of objects being transported. Custom-designed units can be submerged in coolant sumps or quench tanks, or may be located under punch presses or other equipment to move chips, parts or scrap. The working face is self-cleaning.
