# Ironworker packs a heavy punch

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

A complete range of general to heavy-duty metal fabrication machinery from Scotchman Industries will be presented on booths BC15301 and BC15401 at FABTECH in Atlanta. Live equipment demonstrations will showcase the features, benefits and cut quality.

Scotchman Ironworkers to be featured include: DO95 U.S.-made, dual-operator ironworker machine with hydraulic system designed using two pumps, allowing two operators to use the machine at the same time and ensuring that both operations have full hydraulic pressure and speed. The DO 95 has a 95-ton capacity punch and a 9" throat depth, which can punch a 1-3/16" hole in 1" material. Other workstations include a 24" flat bar shear, rectangle notcher, angle shear and a tool table.

Also on the booth will be the 5014-ET Ironworker, dubbed by Scotchman as "the world's first and only 50-ton hydraulic ironworker with a revolving three-station turret punch." The revolving turret accepts up to three different punch sizes that can be changed in seconds. It features 50 tons of pressure and can punch a 13/16" hole in a 3/4" plate. Other workstations include a 14" flat bar shear, rectangle notcher, angle shear and a tool table for using a large variety of additional tools.

The F185 85-ton ironworker with five fully integrated workstations will also be showcased, along with the 50514-EC 50-ton ironworker (with automated measuring system) and a number of hydraulic presses.
