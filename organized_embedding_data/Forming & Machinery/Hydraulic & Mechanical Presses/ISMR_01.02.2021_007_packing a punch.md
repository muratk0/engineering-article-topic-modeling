## U.S. power management product manufacturer, ASCO Power Technologies, has reduced programming time by 75% and increased efficiency with new nesting software.

ASCO Power Technologies, based in Welcome, North Carolina (USA), manufactures automatic transfer switches, switchgear and other power management products. The manufacturer has a long history of innovation and is on a constant drive to bring new technologies and new products to its customers to help them become more productive.

The company has used JETCAM Expert nesting software since receiving it alongside a Finn-Power 880 CNC punch press in 1997.

Its performance provided a stark contrast to the different nesting software systems being used to drive other machines in its facility at the time.

The manufacturer now uses JETCAM software, integrated with its MES Aquila DMM, to drive AMADA, Finn-Power, Wiedermann, Euromac and Cincinnati punch press and laser cutting machines.

## Challenges in focus

James Hunley, APT fabrication specialist, recalled: "We used to operate a range of nesting software systems supplied alongside machines in the early days, with varying issues. One was slow and not user-friendly - more of a CAD system than software designed for punching. Functions such as batch nesting were very cumbersome. We moved to another system, which resolved some of the issues of its predecessor but brought its own problems. Making changes often required you to start again - not convenient when you had just spent twenty minutes tooling a part."

Around 2005, the decision was made to standardise on JETCAM Expert for all new machines.
