# Folding technologies

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

The Schröder Group will present its broad portfolio of sheet-metalworking machines on stand A48 in Hall 11 at EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022).

Highlights will include the fully automatic folding beam tool-changer on its EVO Center motorised sheet-metal folder and presentation of the PowerBend Multi. The EVO Center has been developed for industrial series production with high repeatability and offers several automation options.

A completely redesigned version of the PowerBend Professional folder will also be on display, with the option of a rotating clamping beam. This machine has an increased clamping beam stroke of 500mm and now offers more space for bulky workpieces.

"At EuroBLECH, we will be focusing on the flexibility of the machine to efficiently produce single pieces and small batches. Whereas rapid automatic changes of up to 400mm-high tools on the clamping beam were already possible via two asynchronously movable gripper units, there is now another special feature to showcase. The EVO Center will be equipped with an automatic tool changer for the folding beam. Recesses (e.g. for loops that are not to be folded) can now be set, as required, by the program control," the bending specialist outlined.

Visitors to its stand will also see precision sheet-metalworking shears and slip-bending rolls. ■
