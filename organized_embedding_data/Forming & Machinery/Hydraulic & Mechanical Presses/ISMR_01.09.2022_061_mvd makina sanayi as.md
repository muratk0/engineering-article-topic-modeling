# MVD Makina Sanayi A.S.

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.mvd.com.tr

With over 70 years of experience, MVD is among the leading Turkish companies in the sheetmetal processing technology sector.

It is one of Turkey's top five machine tool exporters, sending machinery and systems to over 90 countries. Last year, it substantially increased its R&D activity.

Its products include the F8-6020 iLaser fibre laser cutting machine; E135-3100 and S40-1250 iBend servo press brakes; D320-4100 and B100-3100 iBend hydraulic press brakes and B08-3100 iShear guillotine shears. It also manufactures the iPunch (punch press); iPlasma (plasma-oxy cutting); iExpand (expanded metal press) and associated bending tool systems.

“We constantly advance our machine diversity and technologies with an innovative approach," it explained. "We will be exhibiting at MAKTEK Eurasia (Istanbul) in September and at EuroBLECH Hanover this year. Follow our website, social media accounts and Youtube channel for our latest news," it said.

## Senodis Technologies GmbH

www.senodis.io/en/

The Senodis team consists of four founders who cover a broad field of expertise in business, technology and software development. They are supported by a development team at the Fraunhofer IKTS, exploring new application areas for Senodis CeraCode®technology and the further development of inks.

"With CeraCode® marking technology (high-temperatureresistant, machine-readable marking), continuous component detection (as well as the connection of all elements of industrial production) can be enabled for hot-formed and press-hardened metal components," it continued.

Stand FO6E

"Our all-in-one-solution CeraCode®provides identification of each individual metal component from the raw material to the end product, trackable through every stage. The CeraCode®-system consists of a robust industrial printer including an ink supply, a unit consisting of a scanner and lighting for code registration plus a controller for data processing. Components are printed with a heat-resistant ceramic ink optimised for adhesion to metal surfaces," it explained.
