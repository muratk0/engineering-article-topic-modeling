“Melitron uses Mate EasySnap tool for breakaway tabs to eliminate secondary operations”

Putting the right tooling in a turret press can make a huge impact on productivity and part costs. In this challenging economy, the manufacturing experts at Melitron Corporation have proved how important the use of the right punch press tooling can be to the bottom line for them and their customer.

“We’re looking for more ways to deliver savings to our customers with our turret punching operations during these challenging times,” says David Smit, manufacturing engineer for Melitron. “By adding special purpose Mate tools, we’re eliminating fabricating steps, reducing set-up time and delivering a higher quality product to our customers. In times like these, efficiencies of this kind really matter and give us a competitive edge.

“For example, by using the Mate EasySnap tool, we’ve saved thousands of dollars in material costs and even more in reduced operator time. We’re able to reduce cycle times dramatically with minimal capital investment.”

## CYCLE TIME REDUCTIONS

Melitron is a complete, well-equipped, “end-to-end” metal fabricator using the latest in sheet metal fabricating technology. It boasts modern capabilities for sheet metal fabricating, metal turning, finishing and assembly.

“The reason for the company’s sustained growth, since it was founded in 1995, is a result of its continuous investment in advanced technologies, constant improvement and its team’s ability to change and adapt,” confirms Mr Smit.

“In punch press tooling, we believe that new tool designs can really contribute to the bottom line and give us a competitive edge. We’re constantly looking at new tool designs to see how they can improve things. That’s why we went with the Mate EasySnap and quick length-adjusting Ultra Tec tools. They gave us a way to reduce cycle times and thus saved thousands of dollars. At times like these, a tool change can generate substantial productivity improvements that are not possible any other way.”

Melitron fabricates cabinetry for business machines, computing, medical and networking applications, amongst others. These components and assemblies call for accurate punching and bending operations. Production runs vary from 100 to 500 and can include over 100 different product families that often incorporate 2,000 to 3,000 variations. When these parts require nonstandard processing, Melitron recently added the Mate EasySnap tool for a faster and surer way to overcome the difficulties presented by challenging designs.

Before using the EasySnap tool, Melitron used conventional slitting tools to punch breakaway tabs on part edges. An easy bend line was punched so that tabs would break away from the part once they had served their purpose. These punched bend line depressions were inconsistent so that the tabs often were either too rigid or too weak.

Without the required rigidity, the tabs were difficult to work with effectively. This slowed the fabrication process and risked errors and thus a scrapped part. In contrast, when alignment tabs were too rigid, breaking them free was often a difficult hand operation creating burrs on part edges and the need for a secondary grinding operation.

Above: Melitron Corporation’s 115,000-square foot facilities include automated Amada turret presses shown here. The company typically processes sheet metal ranging in thickness from 0.5mm up to 5mm. Part runs average between 100 to 500 pieces at speeds of up to 500spm.

In contrast, the EasySnap tool operates differently than a standard slitting tool. EasySnap has a linear V-line stencil machined onto the face of the upper and lower tools. As the tools penetrate the sheet, they create a consistent line of weakness (a snap line) in the upper and lower surfaces of the sheet material. The tool partially slits the sheet leaving just enough material to hold it together.

When desired, the part can be quickly and easily bent and snapped loose along the part line from the rest of the sheet without any tools. Part edges are consistently clean and there are no burrs or other unwanted “pips” such as those left by the micro joints.

For Melitron, EasySnap produces breakaway tabs that serve many useful purposes for subsequent processing, as Mr Smit confirms. “These tabs are stiff so they don’t bend when you don’t want them to. This makes working with them a lot more friendly and accurate.
