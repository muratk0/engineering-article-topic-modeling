# Coil processing systems

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

TOPTRAN MACHINERY CO., Ltd. (TOMAC), has designed a space-saving TLN7 3-in-1 servo straightener feeder featuring a decoiler (with inverter control), straightener and feeder in one seamless design. The system incorporates an electric eye loop control system; feed and work rolls with hard chrome-plated treatment; a hydraulic hold-down arm device with motorised wheel; adjustable feed line; hydraulic coil tip de-bending device; hydraulic in-feed pressing device and peeler extension support.

The company, which has production facilities in Taiwan, recently exhibited at the Mach Tool/ITM Poland exhibition in Poznan, Poland. See www.toptran.com.tw
