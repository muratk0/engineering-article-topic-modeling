Compliant angle grinder designed for robotic grinding, finishing

ATI Industrial Automation has introduced the CGV-900 compliant angle grinder to provide an automated alternative to grinding and finishing by hand. It offers built-in compliance, allowing the unit to compensate for irregularities in part surfaces and maintain contact with a workpiece. The compliance force is adjustable, enabling users to fine-tune their finishing processes in real time.

The unit also provides position sensing data to help indicate media wear. This lets users establish media replacement intervals, helping eliminate unexpected rework and downtime. Additionally, the data can be used for process verification.

The governed, vane-style motor provides a consistent speed during grinding and finishing processes to help ensure an even finish throughout the tool’s contact path.

Folder bends in two directions without manual turning

Bradbury offers the Biegemaster XBend folding machine, which performs automatic bending of material in two directions without requiring manual turning by an operator. It can handle parts up to 40 ft. long and up to 14 ga. thick.

Features include a BMS multitouch control system, precise positioning of the material, a robust cutter, part preprogramming, and the ability to archive all previously used projects on the machine.

Robotic welding system designed for shipbuilding

Comau and Fincantieri have introduced the MR4Weld mobile robotic welding system. The robot is being tested and will be subsequently used within Fincantieri shipyards to autonomously weld steel structures, with a possible three-fold increase compared to a manual process, the company states.

The system features a high-payload, 6-axis articulated robot fitted with a welding torch that is installed on a tracked undercarriage and equipped with an integrated vision system to autonomously identify welding joints. It is designed to help improve welding quality while reducing ergonomic risks.

The system can be managed by a single operator during transfer and welding activities.

Fronius offers the iWave with cold wire feed technology and TIG DynamicWire welding package, designed to help welders achieve perfect TIG welds, regardless of experience level, the company reports.

Ultrasonic thickness inspection with probe measures with 0.0004-in. accuracy

Verisurf Software Inc. has announced support for the Renishaw RUP1 (REVO ultrasonic probe) in the upcoming Verisurf 2024 software release. The probe measures metal parts from 0.039 to 0.787 in. thick with an accuracy of 0.0004 in. on parallel surfaces and 0.0039 in. on wedged surfaces with an angle of up to 10 degrees. Using the Dynamic Surface Points tool, Inspection Plans within the software now incorporate ultrasonic thickness measurements. These measurements are stored and presented through a graphical balloon, providing recipients of the report with a thickness map associated with the inspected component.
