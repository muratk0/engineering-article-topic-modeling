# Strip guiding rail

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

A LONGER STRIP guiding rail is necessary to completely cover cut-outs and therefore prevent the lifting-off of the strip, particularly for long side cut-outs. For this reason, Meusburger now offers its E 5636 Strip guiding rail in additional lengths of 60 and 100mm. With quick installation directly from the working plane, the time needed for assembly and removal of the stamping die can be significantly reduced.

"The strip guiding rails are hardened to 60 HRC and therefore offer excellent wear protection. The integrated infeed chamfers on all sides ensure easy entry of the strip, as well as making it possible to use both sides of the guiding rail. This prevents incorrect installation. The strip guiding rail is also available from stock in lengths of 30mm. For all lengths, there are various heights ranging from four to 20mm," Meusburger told ISMR.
