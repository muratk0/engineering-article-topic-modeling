# Stamping and forming

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

Busbars made of copper or aluminium are crucial components in high-performance battery systems and complete power distribution. Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik offers automation solutions for the mass production of these E-components.

"Users benefit from material savings of up to 50 per cent, flexible production with a wide range of variants and high productivity," it told ISMR.

A leading global automotive supplier now produces large numbers of busbar variants on a single Bihler GRM-NC servo stamping and forming machine, the manufacturer explained. It is equipped with six NC units and two 400kN presses, which are used individually or in combination.

Due to their geometries and dimensions, half of the busbar variants are manufactured using the LEANTOOL radial system. In these forming tools, the units with the punches are arranged in a circle around the forming centre. The raw material width corresponds to the part width. For busbars, which are made of 4mm thick pure copper, this saves up to 50 per cent in material costs compared to production with wide strip, said Bihler.

The other half of the busbars is produced with the LEANTOOL linear system due to its component length and the additional forming steps. For each of the variants, three L250 forming modules and two cutting frames are available in the two 400kN servo presses.

"Changeover times from one variant to another are one to one-and-a-half hours. Together with the two servo presses, process lengths of over 2,000mm can be achieved. Linear production saves up to 30 per cent of material, as only one carrier strip is required," explained the manufacturer.

The integrated LEANTOOL tooling system aims to significantly shorten the design and implementation times for new tools. This is due to the high proportion of standard parts in the highly standardised forming tools of up to 70 per cent as well as Bihler machine technology which enables movements from three sides.

"In addition, the Bihlerplanning WebApp supports planners and designers as early as the component planning, process determination and quotation preparation stages, as well as in the design of LEANTOOL radial and linear tools. All of this shortens time-to-market margins enormously. Users are therefore ideally equipped to meet the requirements of the future market of e-mobility and to play a significant role in shaping it with their own innovations," concluded the manufacturer.
