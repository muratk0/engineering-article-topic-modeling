# Smart four-roll plate bending system

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

HAEUSLER AG, a manufacturer of platebending machines, has introduced its VRM-NEO. This four-roll plate bending machine integrates the latest technologies and innovations for a wide range of applications.

“Whether wind towers in onshore and offshore operation, shipbuilding and mechanical engineering or vessel production, the VRM NEO masters the challenges of these industries like no other four-roll plate bending machine. With its numerous advantages and innovations, it gives manufacturers a clear competitive advantage in their production," it explained.

"The heart of the machine, the smart BENDtronic®software, is a user-friendly and intuitive control system that optimises both the movements of the machine and the bending result itself. Using this control technology, even less experienced operators can always achieve top results with minimal waste, which significantly reduces the amount of training required for new operators. Regardless of the requirements, the VRM NEO achieves consistently high quality with different shapes, materials or operators," it continued.

With its compact linear guide, in combination with a flexible exchangeable roller, the VRM NEO can also bend small diameters. The HAEUSLER design of the linear guide is aimed at stability and durability of the machine, which significantly reduces wear on the machine components.

“In combination with smart hydraulics and optimised oil management, for fast and smooth movement of multiple axes with minimal oil consumption, precision results are possible. These technologies contribute significantly to outstanding product quality, as they ensure a high level of positional accuracy during the entire bending process," added the manufacturer.

The HAEUSLER machine also features electric rotation, which reaches a standard speed of 8m/minute and enables higher speeds as an option. The machine's low noise level helps to reduce emissions and makes the VRM NEO one of the quietest machines in the industry.

"Our system, in combination with BENDtronic®, enables precise execution and accuracy in production. Reproducibility is easy to handle and the production results are always the same," concluded HAEUSLER.
