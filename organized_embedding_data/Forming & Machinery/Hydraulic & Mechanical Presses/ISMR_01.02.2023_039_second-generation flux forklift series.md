# Second-generation FluX forklift series

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

HUBTEX has given its FluX electric multidirectional counterbalance forklift truck some significant, innovative updates. The latest generation for load capacities of up to three tonnes is making its debut at LogiMAT 2023. Visitors to Stand 10B20 in Hall 10 at the LogiMAT trade fair will be able to see the new central driver's position, which provides higher-up, all-round visibility compared to its predecessor. Patented 360° HX steering has been added to the compact three-wheel running gear for the first time, designed to ensure smooth changes in direction from longitudinal to transverse travel.

“The new generation of our FluX handles both pallet and long-load transport in a single vehicle. This hybrid, used as a frontand sideloader, makes the new 3T FluX 30 suitable for a wide range of sectors (from the timber and metalworking industries through to the building materials trade). We have moved the driver's cab to the middle of the vehicle giving the operator a better allround view, including towards the load being carried," explained HUBTEX.

“This improved visibility is enhanced by the vehicle's new, more compact dimensions which include a new mast tilt feature. Being up to 20% smaller, the FluX is now more manoeuvrable and agile. The innovative design has enabled access to individual components, making them easier to access for servicing and programmed maintenance," it added.

The new FluX has retained the spoked steering wheel and joystick for user-friendly operation as well as the spacious driver's cab. In addition, patented HX steering has been developed further to enable fitting to all HUBTEX electric vehicles with a three-wheel running system. The HX steering mechanism allows smooth transitions between steering modes, reducing tyre wear.

The compact multi-directional truck is suitable for combined indoor and outdoor use in the warehouse with its soft elastic tyres and a maximum speed of 12km/h. Its large battery (48V, 930Ah) means it can outlast a whole shift. An optional lithium-ion battery provides rapid charging for multi-shift operation.

HUBTEX is an international manufacturer of sideloaders, order picking vehicles, purposebuilt industrial trucks and special equipment for transporting long, heavy and bulky goods.■
