# 3D waterjet cutting

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

CMS Metal Technology is dedicated to the production of metalworking machines and foam materials offering a wide range of complete three- and five-axis waterjet cutting systems, pressure intensifiers and dry or wet deburring and satin finishing machines. Since the 1990s, following its acquisition of Tecnocut and continuous internal developments, it has completed over 1,500 installations worldwide. It sells into industries in various divisions such as automotive, aerospace, machining, furniture and industrial architecture.

In 2019, it launched JD5, its new five-axis cutting head with “infinite rotation" (continuous rotation of the C-axis during consecutive cuts), which offers machining time savings and is designed to reduce the tolerance error of “mirror"-cut parts to zero. “Due to the intuitive and user-friendly programming, by starting with a 3D model, it is now possible to control every processing aspect (from the direction of the cutting head to actual head movements) to avoid collisions with the workpiece. The software's advanced functions allow users to intervene on the cutting path and improve the dynamics of the cutting head to achieve higher cutting quality and better precision," it outlined.

To accommodate more demanding applications, the cutting-head angle can reach up to ±60 degrees. Meet CMS at EuroBLECH in Hanover (25-28 October 2022) in Hall 12 on stand G164.■
