# Handling heavier forming dies

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Dies can be manoeuvred quickly and safely on and off the table of power presses using a wide range of carrying consoles from Roemheld. The maximum forming die weight that can be handled has increased by more than half (from 32 to 50 tonnes) with the introduction of a new console with electrically actuated chain drive and support feet.

It is intended for shortening set-up times when changing over batch runs in press shops where components are formed from large metal sheet, such as in the automotive supply chain. The equipment is either dedicated to one press and fixed into position, or supplied as a mobile unit on wheels to be easily transported between different presses. Special customised solutions can also be ordered, including for handling even heavier loads.

"Once at the machine, the carrying console's integrated docking system, which is equipped with a hydraulic lifting column and foot lever for easy adjustment, ensures that it is firmly attached to the press before a die is placed onto it by a forklift or crane.

The integrated chain drive moves the die at up to 2m/minute to its final position in the press via integrated roller and ball bars, positioning it with millimetre precision," Roemheld explained.

"The equipment is available in different versions suitable for almost any type of press. It may be factory-fitted for a new installation or retrofitted to existing presses. The compact design makes it particularly suitable for use when the press bed is low or where space is limited. By integrating sensors and stops, the console can be easily integrated into a partially or fully automated process, either via the power press control or a separate, optional control system," it concluded.

## ERP software

The software has become an industry-specific ERP software solution, specialising in all manufacturing industries. The capacity planning software has hundreds of UK users and is designed to support a business with its future growth and development.

“It will streamline operations across the entire workflow process and provide maximum efficiency along with an immediate ROI. It is easy to install, use and implement. It includes advanced features such as cost control; MRP; scheduling; reporting; quoting; inventory control and real-time tracking," the company explained.

A paperless approach is possible with tablets and mobile devices via kiosk or MIE Mobile Warehouse (a warehouse management application to control inventory; receive and ship items; select items from a pick list and clock in and out of timesheets). The app provides an intuitive graphical interface that communicates with the new MIE Platform, a web-services platform offering the same technical framework as MIE Trak Pro. MIE Mobile Warehouse is an Android app.

MIETrak Pro allows users to ensure that all quotes are consistent and that profit margins are maintained across the board. It gives the user purchasing power in the form of ERP, meaning that users take control of all purchases within the organisation efficiently and ensure that they are getting the most for their money," outlined MiE Solutions.

Once installed, the customer support team will offer support network and training either in-house, remotely by telephone and/or on-site at customer premises.
