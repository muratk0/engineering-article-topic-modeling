# Combi-CS pedestrian stacker wins IFOY Award

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

COMBILIFT WAS A winner at this year’s IFOY Awards 2020– one of the most hotly contested international awards in the materials handling industry which honours the best products and solutions of the year. The company won the Warehouse Truck Lowlifter category with its Combi-CS pedestrian counterbalance stacker.

“The Combi-CS is the only pedestrian counterbalance stacker that will operate in a conventional reach truck aisle for space saving and productive storage and handling. It features our internationally patented and award-winning multi-position tiller arm which can be turned to the left or right of the unit to position the rear drive wheel, allowing the operator to remain in the safest position (at the side of the machine, rather than at the rear as is the case with other pedestrian stackers). This ensures optimum visibility of the load and surroundings as well as guaranteeing maximum safety in areas where other personnel or members of the public may be present,” said the company.

Finalists’ products underwent stringent IFOY audit and innovation checks by industry experts. Journalists from leading logistics media from 19 countries also tested and evaluated the equipment for qualities such as technology, design, ergonomics, safety, marketability, customer benefit and sustainability.
