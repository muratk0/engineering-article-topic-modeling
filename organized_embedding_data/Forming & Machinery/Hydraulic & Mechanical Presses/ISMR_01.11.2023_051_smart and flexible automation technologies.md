# Smart and flexible automation technologies

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Turkish sheet-metal specialist, Ermaksan, featured a range of automated and flexible bending technologies, as well as solutions for smart manufacturing, at the Blechexpo/ Schweisstec trade show in Stuttgart (Germany) this November.

"Our showcased products not only meet current manufacturing demands but also anticipate the challenges of tomorrow. Our commitment to staying ahead of emerging trends and understanding customer expectations drives us to create environmentally friendly, energy-efficient machines, establishing a seamless balance between sustainability and technological advances," it told ISMR.

Featured technologies at the trade show included an automation line; an automatic cutting and bending system that Ermaksan terms its Flexible Cutting and Bending Line.

This integration of its FIBERMAK GEN-5 laser cutting machine with TOWERMAK loading system and the new SPEED BEND PRO press brake, enhanced with robotic applications, has been designed for productivity.

Ermaksan also introduced the FIBERTUBE-ECO cutting machine, equipped with a streamlined pipe-loading section, at the show. From handling pipes ranging from Ø20 to Ø200 to processing square and rectangular profiles, FIBERTUBE caters to applications across various sectors from defence and aerospace to furniture. The fibre optic laser pipe/profile cutting system was on display at the show in Stuttgart.

Ermaksan's products include fibre laser and plasma cutters; press brakes; punch presses; controllers; 3D printers; laser resonators and shears.■
