## TOOLING INNOVATIONS

New on the punching front from Wilson Tooling is an innovative automated tool grinding machine. The Wilson X-Sharp grinder is an automated punch and die grinding machine, designed with the objective of simplifying the process of sharpening punch press tools. With 40 years of experience, Wilson Tool provides punching solutions to metal fabricators around the world.

TRUMPF has also introduced new punching tools to enable the production of formed parts that are larger than ever before. Firstly, tool developers have created a new tool size they call “Size 5,” which is based on a new tool cartridge. For machines with the “active die” function, there are special tools for particularly large formed parts. Size 5 tools are compatible with all punching and combination machines – even those operating without additional options, like the active die option. This is made possible by a new kind of tool cartridge that allows for a different tool configuration. With an outer circle of up to 120 millimetres in the die, the tools thus offer multiple sheet metal forming options.

Tooling specialist Mate Precision Tooling now provides an expanded line of its LongLife Slitting Tool system to meet the needs of fabricators wanting greater versatility and flexibility. The Mate LongLife system combines premium tool steel punch and die inserts with robust punch and die holders for cost effective slitting tool applications.

The expanded line now includes variable width and length punch and die inserts. Four standard shapes are now available: rectangle, oval, trapezoid and dovetail. Also available are one-piece and two-piece die inserts. Added to the previously available rooftop shear are whisper, 1-way and flat shears.

Eleven standard discrete die clearances are now available – customers may also specify their own die clearances, if desired.

Last year, Mate also introduced its ValuGrind high precision grinder for easy punch and die maintenance. Designed by Mate, ValuGrind lets fabricators precisely regrind punches and dies to maintain optimum condition for the high quality parts production. Available for either Thick Turret or TRUMPF-style tooling, ValuGrind comes completely fixtured.

## ENERGY EFFICIENCY

The entire range of sheet metal working technology products and services known up to now as Prima Industrie or Finn Power will now be known as Prima Power. Over the years, the Prima and Finn Power product ranges have developed towards greater flexibility and operating economy through versatility, high automation levels and low energy and maintenance. The first Finn Power turret punch press featuring servo electric technology was introduced 12 years ago.

The heart of the new servo electric Shear Genius SGe from Prima Power, launched at EuroBLECH 2010, is an updated servo electric 30-tonne punching machine with 1,000 hpm stroke speed, 250 rpm index speed and 150 m/min sheet positioning speed. The right angle shear has a servo electric actuation system of its own, which makes shear movement at the same time both fast and fully controlled; this equals optimization for maximum productivity. Material thickness in shearing can be up to 5mm (Al), 4mm (mild steel) and 3mm (stainless steel). Automatic loading has been integrated and part removal and sorting are automatic.

Both punching and shearing units have water cooling and a central lubrication system. The SGe has also optimised tool changes; other auxiliary times have been minimised for best production output. Well known features such as programmable blade clearance setting are included to maintain high part surface quality. With the improvements made, it is possible to achieve 5–10 percent higher output compared with the previous hydraulic SG. The control system now features a new Prima Power PC-based NC control with Rexroth servo drives. The latest version of the Tulus operating system has a new online simulation view for follow-up and re-start. An integrated tool library with NC Express and servo electric punch makes tool set-up and adjustment easy and fast, which shortens set-up times dramatically.

The TruPunch 3000 introduces TRUMPF’s new electric punching head which brings several benefits. It is much quieter in operation and uses very little power in standby mode. And, when it’s punching, it is more energy efficient, using 2kW of power per hour less than its predecessor.

The new punching head is also more productive – tools are rotated at higher speed giving more benefits, one of which is that threads are formed quicker. There is a space saving, too. The absence of the hydraulic head allows the TruPunch 3000 to occupy a footprint that is smaller than the previous generation machine by five square metres (about 13%).

With the quality of processed surfaces becoming increasingly important, TruPunch 3000 has been designed to handle material as gently as possible. An important feature is the machine’s parts chute that ejects cut components horizontally into a collection bin, stacking them evenly. Workpieces measuring up to 500mm x 500mm are discharged, scratch-free, via the parts flap. It can also be operated with standard containers so material logistics can be uniform throughout the production process.

## AUTOMATION AND COMBINATION SYSTEMS

The TruPunch is also compatible with the TRUMPF SheetMaster which includes part picking and can automatically remove individual parts and stack them in a pre-determined layout. Further automated options include SortMaster Box Linear, a sorting unit for small finished parts and DisposeMaster that separates all process waste according to material type.

Another benefit of the TruPunch 3000 is the skeleton-free punching on concept. The result increases reliability in several areas. For example, parts will be cleanly separated from the skeleton, the operator no longer needs to remove leftover material from the table and the machine automatically separates finished parts from the scrap. Production costs are also reduced in the region of 10 per cent because less material is required, says TRUMPF.

One laser for two different machines is the concept behind the new TruMatic 3000 Fibre punch/laser combination machine. It shares a fibre-guided TruDisk laser with the TruLaser Robot 5020 cell in the TRUMPF Laser Network, an arrangement that significantly increases the utilisation rate of the beam source.

The most efficient method is undoubtedly to have the TruMatic 3000 Fibre and TruLaser Robot 5020 systems working in parallel. The fibre-guided TruDisk 3001 with its 3 kilowatts of laser power can switch from one application to the other in a matter of milliseconds.

The machines are supported by the intelligent LaserNetwork and are able to automatically make use of the laser beam as required. This means that whenever the combination machine is performing punching operations and the laser is not required, its energy is available to the robot cell; equally, when the cell is being prepared or adjusted, it relinquishes the laser to the TruMatic 3000.

In addition to sharing use of the laser, the two systems further complement each other. The combination machine offers a broad range of punch applications and forming variants and can be used for jobs such as preparing joint edges for welding. That simplifies jig and fixture engineering, making even smaller batches viable and extending the application scope of laser welding.

The Italian family-run coil processing specialist recently developed a new concept line for flexible punching and bending from coil (FPB). It is a flexible modular line that incorporates a punch press (with thick turret tooling), a part-turning device (after punching), positive and negative bends on the punched parts and can handle part dimensions ranging from 650-1500mm in width x 1500-3600mm in length.

Flexible, modular and compact, Salvagnini’s high-precision punching systems can be used as stand-alone machines or integrated with other systems to create autonomous production cells or become part of a complete and automated production system (AJS or Automated Job Shop). The multi-press head is one of the pioneering technological features that have contributed to the success of Salvagnini’s punching solutions. Its S2 system consists of a patented double punching multi-press head for rapid and precise symmetrical punching along the two centre lines. Its S4X punching and shearing system embodies the concept of flexible automation, uniting all the operations that used to require manual intervention into a single system that cuts, loads, unloads, stacks, separates and sorts. ■
