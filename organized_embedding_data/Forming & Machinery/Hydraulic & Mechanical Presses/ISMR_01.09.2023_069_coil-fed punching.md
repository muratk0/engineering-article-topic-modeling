# Coil-fed punching

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

The EXN is DALLAN's new powerful, coil-fed electric punching machine, offering greatly reduced energy consumption. The Dallan® Select servo-electric actuator is designed to unite power and energy efficiency.

"Thanks to ARC tool post arches, it is always possible to change the turret configuration and adapt the machine to other future production requirements. Maximum production efficiency is ensured by in-line machining, which achieves up to 100% optimisation of the material," DALLAN told ISMR.

"Apart from saving on raw materials, working from coil also enables customers to save a lot of energy (up to 70% electricity savings). It is a very fast machine compared to traditional sheet-fed machines using a fully automatic process. It meets the four main efficiency pillars; productivity, automation, flexibility and material/energy efficiency," it continued. The EXN can process material up to 1500mm wide and 4mm thick. Coil-fed operation improves efficiency levels and dramatically reduces scrap. The servoelectric punching head concentrates 20 tons of power on each punching tool and flexible tool configurations enable rapid production changeover without set-up time.

In the 2022 release of the Dallan D-Touch software, a new feature was added: the Coil Warehouse Management System (CWMS). Warehouse management data is also accessible to ERP systems. DALLAN adopted the DALCOS brand for coil punching and laser-cutting lines as the brand for all DALLAN coil-cutting technologies from 1 January 2023.

DALLAN SpA celebrates its 45th anniversary this year. It specialises in advanced systems to manufacture products from steel coil, including punching machines, rollformers, laser-cutting and packaging machines. The family-owned company operates worldwide on all five continents across 83 countries. Its machinery is used in the manufacture of profiles for applications in sectors such as interior finishing (false ceilings); sun shading (Venetian blinds and roller shutters); HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning); metal furniture and lighting, amongst others.■
