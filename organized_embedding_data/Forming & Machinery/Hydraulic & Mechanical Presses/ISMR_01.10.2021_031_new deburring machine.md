# New deburring machine

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

At this October's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany, Fladder Danmark A/S will be introducing its new CC deburring machine (Hall 5, stand 5412).

"The name CC refers to the cylindrical shape of the machine; the concentric movement of the abrasive tools and the circular transport unit," Fladder told ISMR. "The machine has a footprint of just 1.5m², low noise levels and is easy to move (no foundations or floor fixings). It is also easy to start, with low power consumption. Machine operation is very easy with a touchscreen control panel. Loading and unloading is from the same position, saving time and increasing efficiency."

The Fladder CC is built with a lower and upper cylindrical section. Between these sections, a circular vacuum table rotates, allowing workpieces to be fed manually or automatically. The displaced turning table will transport workpieces into the working area where they are deburred from multiple angles, with concentric movement of the tools.
