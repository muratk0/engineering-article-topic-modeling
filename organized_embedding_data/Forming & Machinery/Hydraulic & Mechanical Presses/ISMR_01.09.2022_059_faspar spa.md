# FASPAR SpA

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.faspar.it

FASPAR supplies its customers with coil processing equipment and aims to meet customers' needs and expectations by "mixing technological development with reliability", it told ISMR.

Its production range consists of coil processing equipment such as: slitting lines; packaging lines; robots for the automatic handling of cutters and spacers for slitting lines; levelling and cut-to-length lines; surface finishing lines; embossing lines; punching/bending lines; tension-levelling lines and single special machines and lines.

The Italian manufacturer also offers consultancy; service/ spare parts; retrofit and test/installation.

FASPAR is also a member of the TRAFOGRID Alliance, a group of machine tool and equipment producers with extensive experience in electric transformers.
