# A strong grip

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 ISMR]

DESTACO has announced another advance in its portfolio of lightweight, high-performance sheet metal grippers designed to improve operations in press shop applications. The new patented 84N5 Series sheet metal gripper expands the number of solutions available in DESTACO's high-speed portfolio of 84N family series of grippers. The 84N5 Series grippers are part of DESTACO's Accelerate Collection, which are designed to help users boost productivity in automotive manufacturing press shop stamping lines.

Operating at actuation speeds of 78 milliseconds (ms), the 84N5 grippers maintain a firm grip force of 1200N at 5 bar (72.5psi). They are designed to produce low wear that increases operational life with minimal field maintenance needed, while producing more stamped parts per hour.

"Uptime, faster throughput with reduced maintenance and costs are critical for our customers to stay competitive," said Gary Labadie, Global Product Director, Grippers for DESTACO. "This new gripper product delivers application metrics. The result is a universal highly configurable, fully adjustable gripper that is more durable, easy to reconfigure, reduces a customer's on-site inventory requirements and is very cost-effective."

The 84N5 grippers are available in fixed, double-open and 90-degree jaw configurations. The jaws are interchangeable and can be quickly adjusted in the field in ten minutes or less with a single Allen wrench. The jaws include a visual opening angle indicator and are adjustable anywhere from a 15- to 75-degree opening angle. A series of colour-coded tips lets the user visually know which sheet metal thickness can be gripped. The 84N5 jaws are universally designed to accept both single- and double-tip designs, which increases overall application flexibility.

Sensing solutions include in-jaw sensing that validates if a part is positioned between the jaws. The side-body sensing option includes two sensors that lets the user self-determine whether single blank, double blank, full open or full close operation is necessary.

"Because of its compact size, the 84N5 gripper has the versatility to fit into any press shop tooling and mounting configuration. The 84N5 gripper includes a fail-safe locking mechanism, preventing part drop in the event that the gripper loses air pressure. The parts remain securely gripped, which reduces scrap from an e-stop event," concluded the manufacturer.
