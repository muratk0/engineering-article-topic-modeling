# Simple combinations, flexible replacement

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

THE euroTECH MODULAR construction system allows users to combine a wide range of base plates with many different seals.

Vacuum suction plates in heavy use cannot be protected from wear. Various suction plates are vulcanised onto the base plate: once the seal is worn out, both need to be replaced although the base plate is technically still functional. The company offers a solution - a wide range of detachable seals, which can be combined with many different base plates. This, said the company, saves time and money and is better for the environment.

"If seals are vulcanised onto a base plate, the suction plate can no longer be separated. Wornout seals produce special waste due to the two very different materials used. Disassembling these suction plates is extremely timeconsuming. With euroTECH suction plates, the worn-out seal can simply be detached from the base plate and replaced within seconds. As seals only cost a fraction of the price of a complete suction plate, this solution considerably reduces the cost of the replacement. Only the worn-out seal is thrown away; the base plate remains in use," it explained.

The replaceable seals of three sizes can be combined with four different types of suspension, made from a wide range of materials including the three primary materials NBR black, NBR grey and silicone. There are a variety of possible combinations. This diversity of options helps users to establish the best conditions for lifting loads of different materials.
