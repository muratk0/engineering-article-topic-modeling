# Chin Fong Machine Industrial Co., Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand C164

www.chinfong.com

Founded in 1948, Chin Fong is one of the largest manufacturers of mechanical presses. The Taiwanese press specialist has placed smart machinery at the heart of its technological innovations. From a single-press smart solution, the company has moved onto multi-press connections and cloud computing, linking ERP management and AI computing systems to analyse Big Data. It offers its ‘iForming PMS-Intelligent Forming Productivity Management System' and has further boosted its sustainability credentials with SGS certification for its C-frame press products.

Chin Fong's Battery Shell solution is designed for the growing electric vehicle battery market.

"These press lines are customised for fabricating battery shells and covers, featuring automation commonly used by major manufacturers. We optimise press parameters for specific needs, considering maintenance and material requirements. The frame allows integration with devices like oil sprayers and cylinders, offering a cost-effective, high-value solution for battery shell production," it explained.
