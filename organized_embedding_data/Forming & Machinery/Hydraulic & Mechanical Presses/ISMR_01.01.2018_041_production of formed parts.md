# Production of formed parts

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

SCHULER has expanded its MSP series with the addition of a newly developed servo press. In addition to its 100- and 200-ton stamping presses, the forming technology specialist now also offers a machine that features 400 tons of press force and an innovative drive concept. The first type MSP 400 press will go to a customer in the automotive supplier industry.

"With the MSP 400, we're proving that the efficient production of blanked and formed parts is also possible for a low acquisition cost," commented executive director Markus Bieg from Schuler. "This new generation of presses is aimed at customers who are seeking a more balanced cost/benefit ratio without having to sacrifice the versatile ServoDirect technology or the convenience of simple operation."

Similar to Schuler's TwinServo technology, the MSP 400 utilises an opposing pair of drivetrains that feature electronic synchronisation. These drive trains consist of a highly dynamic servomotor, a brake module and an eccentric shaft. The shaft itself is made up of a connecting rod, a hinged joint for quiet and rigid operation, and a slide. The resulting benefits are similar to those delivered by the TwinServo technology. The pressure points are positioned well to the outside and improve the tilting rigidity, which allows greater eccentric loads.

"Compared with presses that use conventional drive technology, the oscillating stroke mode makes it possible to increase output with a customisable stroke height setting between 60- and 300mm. The clamping surface for the bed and slide measures 2,500 by 1,200mm. The slide - along with the monolithic press frame – consists of a welded steel structure that has undergone stress-relief annealing," explained Schuler.

With a touchscreen, operators can select from five pre-programmed slide movement curves for forming, blanking, embossing, bending and drawing. The movement sequences can be customised to fit specific process parameters, which improves the precision of the processed parts and the service life of the dies. The optionally available OptimizerPRO also allows full-range free programmability of the slide kinematics. Set-up and try-out functions reduce the time required to run-in the dies.

The MSP 400 can also be equipped with Smart Assist from Schuler to assist the press operator during set-up. An electronic overload protection device registers excessive press force immediately and can quickly reverse the main drive torque in the opposite direction to prevent damage to the press.
