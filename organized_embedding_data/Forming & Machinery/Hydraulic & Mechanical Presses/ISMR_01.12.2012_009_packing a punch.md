# PACKING A PUNCH

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

A 72.4 PER CENT increase in throughput means shorter delivery times for customers of BW Industries (Bridlington, UK). The company was looking to expand its range of high quality rolled steel sections and accessories for the domestic, construction, manufacturing and agricultural sectors. After extensive market research and discussions, it entered into an agreement with Gasparini S.p.A. to purchase a new production line.

The new Gasparini machine supplied through FICEP was a replacement for an existing Gasparini machine which had served the company for over 15 years. This new machine has now significantly increased throughput for the company’s product range up to 500mm x 6mm sections and the automatic changeover has also minimised downtime, resulting in further savings. The line can now produce plain and lipped channel sections, in sizes of 100mm x 2.0mm thick to 500mm x 6.0mm thick, with flying shearing and punching in one operation.

“Production analysis has shown that tooling changeover times from section to section have been reduced from 1 ½ - 2 hours to some 10 minutes. The production throughput has increased by 72.4 per cent because of the increased speed of changeover, production speed and online punching. The quality and accuracy of the sections also surpasses current industry standards,” BW Industries told ISMR. ■
