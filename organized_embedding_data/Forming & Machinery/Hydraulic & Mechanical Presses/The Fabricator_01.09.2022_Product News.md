Nibbler cuts 16-ga. mild steel, 22-ga. spiral duct seams

Milwaukee Tool has introduced the M12 FUEL 16-ga. variable-speed nibbler. Instead of a cutting blade, the compact, 12-V nibbler uses a punch and die to take small punches out of the material. This makes the tool suitable for making curved cuts, 90-degree cuts, and straight cuts, and for processing corrugated metals.

The nibbler cuts up to 7 FPM in 16-ga. mild steel when equipped with an M12 REDLITHIUM XC 4.0 battery. The POW-ERSTATE brushless motor provides the power to cut in heavy applications such as 22-ga. spiral duct seams. Features include a variable-speed dial; an LED work light; and a tool-free, 360-degree die holder rotation.

A chip-collection bag can be attached to the die holder to collect the metal punches for quick job site cleanup.

CAD/CAM nesting software updated for automated cutting

Hypertherm has announced a major version update to ProNest 2023, its CAD/CAM nesting software for automated cutting. This new release contains new features and ongoing enhancements designed to help customers improve their operational efficiency while reducing non value-added tasks, the company states.

The software includes full functionality for PlateSaver, a SureCut technology that combines the arc stability of X-Definition plasma with specialized software parameters to help increase material utilization when cutting mild steel. In addition, it contains enhancements to the Production Manager web-based module that integrates with the company’s EDGE Connect CNC to automatically capture real-time machine data.

Enhancements include the ability to edit production times for completed nests, set job status changes, and search for nests by customer. There is also a feature that merges XPR setups.

Entry guide table made for tube mill producing steel up to 2½ in. OD

T&H Lemont built a heavy-duty mill entry guide table for a tube manufacturer in the southeastern U.S. It replaces the OEM’s unit, which was badly worn and no longer controlled the infeed as tightly and precisely as necessary for predictable, stable production.

The unit was built for a mill that produces carbon steel tube from ½ to 2½ in. OD in wall thicknesses from 0.065 to 0.148 in.

CMMS software manages equipment, facilities maintenance

SMGlobal has released FastMaint CMMS software v.12., a maintenance management software for equipment and facilities. It helps maintenance managers schedule both preventive and corrective maintenance work, send work orders to the maintenance staff on their mobile phones, receive feedback from the maintenance workers, and track the progress of all outstanding and completed jobs. Software module enables automated quality control with cobots

Creaform, a business unit of Ametek Inc., has introduced the latest version of its VXscan-R software module for the VXelements 10 platform. The new version is compatible with a variety of robots, including collaborative ones.

Remote monitoring system introduced

ATC Diversified Electronics has introduced scalable technology that makes it possible to monitor critical processes remotely for a variety of conditions, including level, temperature, pressure, flow, current, vibration, and presence of voltage.

The SENSERT remote monitoring and alert system uses existing sensors, either hardwired directly to the base unit or connected wirelessly via remote I/O. Sensor data is monitored in real time and alerts are triggered based on thresholds that can be configured through a mobile app or web portal. Each channel can be configured for a high and low threshold value, as well as custom alerts.

Austenitic stainless steel designed for strength, corrosion resistance

The N’GENIUS Series of high-strength austenitic stainless steels is a reinvention of conventional austenitic stainless steels, designed to outperform most existing grades in the 300 series, according to the developer.

Dr. Ces Roscoe, the metallurgist behind the new material and inventor of the original 25Cr super duplex stainless steel, said, "Their strength properties are equivalent to duplex and super duplex stainless steels, whilst their ductility and toughness properties have all the advantages that are normally associated with austenitic stainless steels, particularly at sub-zero temperatures, but at the same time possessing far superior corrosion resistance."

The new material has been applied to an extensive range of corrosionresistant alloy line pipe grades. It can be manufactured in wrought and cast form; specified and used in a variety of markets, industries, and applications; and applied to all types of primary, secondary, engineered, and fabricated products, states the manufacturer.

90-ga. stretch wrap secures difficult loads

TAB Industries LLC, Reading, Pa., has introduced TWT-20120 extra-heavy-duty stretch wrap, designed to offer clarity and puncture resistance for extra-heavy pallet loads and unwieldy shapes. Manual clamps updated

Destaco has announced enhancements to three manual clamps in its 800 series. The 802-U clamp now includes an upgraded air cylinder that ensures the clamp’s exerting force is less than the maximum holding capacity when operated at normal pressure to help extend service life. New composite bearings on the trunnion mount and cylinder pivot point help eliminate metal-to-metal contact while requiring no lubrication, and a redesigned clevis bolt and increased rivet and prestop diameters help increase longevity.

The 807-U now features lubrication- and maintenancefree composite bearings on the trunnion mount, a redesigned clevis bolt, and a reconfigured base, and the 810-U offers new composite bearings as well.
