# A stamp in time

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

TAIWANESE PRESS MANUFACTURER, Lien Chieh Machinery Co., Ltd., specialises in the manufacture of various different types of hydraulic presses. Founded in 1947, for more than sixty years it has designed and developed a comprehensive range of presses. Examples include the LF-8000ton bolster size 2200mm x 1900mm, hydraulic hot forging press with fixed bolster (recently installed in Taiwan).

“From the very beginning, we have continued to focus on our ability to provide hydraulic presses with optimum features emphasising performance, quality, reliability and safety. Our ‘after-market’ support services are critically important to us and we maintain dedicated resources for this sole purpose,” the press specialist explained.
