# Mobile filter unit

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

The Fumex CMF is a high-performance filter unit with W3 certification for mobile use.

"Minimal pressure drop through the filter and externally arranged support arms on the extraction arm enable high air flow rates of roughly 1800 cubic metres per hour. This results in better extraction and cleaning of welding fumes and dust, for example, without high energy consumption," said the German specialist.

The cleanable unit has a simple control panel and is easy to manoeuvre. There are three models with W3 certification and three distinct cleaning methods:

CMF 10 – manual.

CMF 20 – automatic with an electric motor.

CMF 30 – automatic with an electric motor and compressed air.

If airflow is insufficient, an audible alarm serves as a warning and indicates that the filter cartridge needs to be cleaned or replaced. Dust particles which become detached from the filter cartridge during all cleaning procedures fall into the dust collection container.

"The filter cartridge removes more than 99% of the fumes and dust from welding work on high-alloy steels, for example steels with a nickel and chromium content of 30% and more. The fan wheel has backward-curved blades and is highly efficient.

It is designed to maintain uniform, high rates of airflow even when the filter cartridge is dirty. In combination with minimal pressure drop at the extraction arm, this makes the CMF filter unit particularly efficient," said the German filtration specialist.

"The extraction arm cover is designed for maximum suction power and optimised for the extraction of welding fumes. The flow of air is controlled by a manually-operated throttle valve on the cover mounting," it added.

The filter unit is supplied pre-assembled. Accessories include spark trap; silencer; LED lighting and HEPA filter (class H14). A clamp sensor for automatic start/stop and an extension hose are also available. Extraction hoses for special applications are also available on request.
