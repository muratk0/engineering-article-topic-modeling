The Group has also developed Long Folders™, which feature the Intelli-Fold™ Controller, made by its partners at Beck Automation. The Intelli-Fold™ Controller features resident programs and a graphic display that allows operators to save time by sketching a part profile and then entering its dimensions and angles. Screen prompts guide the process.

"Our sheet-metal folders are specifically designed for a wide range of industries and are suitable for light-duty applications such as box and pan, panels, flashing etc. We recommend them for metalworking fabricators in the HVAC, roofing and sign-making industries," it said.

Jorns AG is a Swiss manufacturer of folders, double folders, compact strip-cutting lines, rounding machines as well as accessories used for processing sheet metal. Its customers mainly work in roofing or architectural sheet metal. Products range from simple NC-controlled machines to sophisticated CNC-controlled sheet-metal bending systems and a J-Bend app, functional on tablets and mobile phones, to support the management and production of sheet metal profiles. In 2016, the company also launched its own CP200 touchscreen control panel.

The manufacturer will exhibit for the first time at EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover this October with Krasser GmbH (a manufacturer of sheet-metal cutting and coil processing systems) at their joint exhibition stand. Customers of the two machine-tool manufacturers are active in the tinsmith, roofing and façade construction sectors.

“There is clearly an increasing demand in the market for cutting and bending machines to 'communicate' with each other to increase productivity and reduce lead times, error rates and material consumption. We are pleased to be able to present our complete package to customers as an end-to-end solution in sheetmetal processing," explained Jorns.

The semi-automatic Jorns JB folder and fully automatic Jorns JDB double-bending folder can be configured in lengths of 3.2 to 12.2 metres and boast bending capacities for sheetmetal of up to 3mm.

“Jorns AG double bending is an efficient method of processing sheet metal in a slitterfolder machine. Our TwinMatic-Pro doublebending folders operate using both upper and lower bending beams to process sheet-metal profiles. The Norma-Line, Maxi-Line and Super-Line long-length slitter folders boast a modular design which allows owners to configure the machine in the way that suits their own bending requirements. Optional features include variable lengths (4m, 6m, 8m, 10m and 12.2m); CNC controls; shearing systems; hydraulic material thickness adjustment; depth stop and top beams," it explained.

"The area behind the clamping tool with a retraction of over 290mm for the bending tools offers above-average open space on the fully automatic Jorns JDB double bending machine. You can then produce bending profiles without turning them around or over – with an array of possible profile variations. Several parallel, tapered or overlapped profiles can be bent simultaneously, an innovation that increases the speed of an operator's work," Jorns told ISMR.

“The use of servo-technology, the automatic loading and unloading table as well as the individually driven clamping fingers allow maximum precision, flexibility and productivity. Thanks to two bending tools, material handling has also been drastically reduced. The consistent calculation of processing and stop positions according to the 'Shorten bending according to DIN 6935' method, intuitive graphic programming, high levels of angular accuracy and the uniformly closed water folds are all an integral part of the JDB," it added.

JPS Machinery's hydraulic long-beam folding machine produces building, roofing and cladding products in folded lengths of 3000mm up to 12000mm (material thicknesses of 0.7mm to 3mm). Products include flashings; cleats; lapping joints; standing seam/flatlock panels; rounds; hems; open hem profiles etc.

With an in-built shear, long folded arms can be 'trimmed' from 3000mm up to 12000mm, without requiring a second operation. This is suitable for thinner sheet material of between 0.5 and 0.8mm.

UK-based Morgan Rushworth offers a range of manual, hydraulic and CNC folders. Its HBP hydraulic box and pan folder uses an adjustable manual angle-setting device. Clamping and bending is controlled by a joystick or foot pedal or combination of the two, allowing easy positioning of the material for folding.

Its ESR range of CNC folders are designed to produce fast, accurate and consistent folded parts. Available in 1.5m to 4.2m bending lengths, all models are supplied with a 1m-powered back gauge, with optional longer back gauges (including U- and L-shaped configurations). All ESR models are supplied with a straight rail with optional high divided tools for box and panel-type operations.

"Our EXR range of CNC folders are manufactured with a rotating upper beam with one straight rail and one set of high divided tools that can both be utilised in the same folding program without any manual tool change. This versatile design enables high accuracy, high consistency levels and high productivity as there is only one initial tool setup to carry out. This is ideal for many folding operations, including rectangular panel work with return edges to the four sides," said the manufacturer.
