Swiss specialist Bruderer chose the recent MACH 2014 exhibition for the international launch of its latest high-precision fully-automated stamping press. The Bruderer BSTA 280-75 press was showcased with newly designed BPG planetary gearbox together with Leicht Stanzautomation drum decoiler and rewinder. The machine had already been purchased in a £500,000 deal and was transported directly to electronic component specialist Harwin Plc’s Portsmouth facility after the show.

Bruderer has also set up two new strategic partnerships with Zani Presses and Zehnder & Sommer. The latter’s high-speed servo feed units can operate up to 2000 strokes per minute (SPM).

The Chin Fong Group aims to achieve a NTD 10 billion (US$ 330 million) sales target by 2015.

Chin Fong’s new single point servo presses (iLS/DLS series) ranging from 80 – 300 tons; its double-point SDS series from 400 up to 1000 tonnes; the compound link LF and KW series for stamping-forging composite processes; the long stroke knuckle joint presses KL and warm-hot forging WF series have facilitated its slogan: “Intelligence of Forming, Vision of Forging”.

Chin Fong now has offices in Taiwan, China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and the USA as well as representatives in numerous countries around the world. Chin Fong’s A0 tandem press line for the automotive industry includes stages such as deep drawing, following by trimming, piercing etc. The basic A0 line weighs 2400 tons with 800 tons of weight for the rest of the machines. To gain ground in production efficiency, Chin Fong replaced the conventional hydraulic press with a mechanical press featuring a link drive and long stroke.

Interlaken Technology, a manufacturer of production press equipment for hydroforming, elevated temperature forming and hot stamping systems, has introduced a new four-column hydroforming press system for running highvolume production while reducing cycle time. Interlaken offers this new hydroforming press system in clamp forces ranging from 250 to 5000 tons.

Hydroformed parts are stronger and weigh less due to structural integrity and fewer welds or add-on pieces. In addition, costs are reduced and time is saved by eliminating secondary operations, reducing scrap, lowering material and manufacturing costs, and increasing design flexibility.

“The press’s multi-channel closed loop control system is easily programmed to handle various sizes and materials. It also offers dynamic mode switching which enables the user to switch between a variety of feedbacks such as force, position, internal pressure and other system variables,” Interlaken told ISMR.

Hot stamping is the process of evenly heating a steel blank, forming it at precise forming pressures and then cooling it at controlled temperatures in the die. A uniform “quenching” or hardening cooling process takes place while the hydraulic press dwells for a few seconds. Because the part is rapidly cooled in the die while the press is dwelling, springback is minimal.

Interlaken’s fully automated hydraulic press system can be easily configured to manufacture a variety of parts. With quick change dies and tooling, the Interlaken hot stamping press can be quickly configured to run a new part with very little down time.

“At room temperature, aluminium and magnesium perform poorly in challenging forming applications. Warm forming of aluminium and magnesium allows for tighter formed radii, better elongation and deep draws that cannot be achieved at room temperature. In some cases, aluminium elongations can be more than 100 per cent,” said the company.

It also produces transfer presses, progressive presses, cut-to-length lines and blank washers and has made use of servo press technology and NC hydraulic die cushions in its technical solutions.
