# GENERATION CNC

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

AT EMO in Hannover, FANUC Corporation exhibited the practical application of its latest control products and robots. This included its latest generation CNC drive products with major improvements and new high speed laser controls with a new laser series. FANUC also showcased a variety of automation concepts displaying integration of intelligent robots with machine tools.

The new FANUC CNC Series 30i/31i/32i MODEL B includes a large number of axes and multi-channel capability. Compared to previous models, the B Series feature significant advancements, numerous detail improvements and additional functions for punch presses and laser cutting machines. The new FANUC CNC Series 35i MODEL B is based on the technology of the 30i-B Series and has been developed primarily for transfer line and handling applications.

The new FANUC Power Motion i MODEL A offers CNC motion control for a wide range of applications, including press and stamping machines, loaders, filling and winding machines, wire saws, and for controlling servo motors as replacements for hydraulic cylinders. With integrated international “functional safety” features, it offers control of up to four paths and thirty two axes (four simultaneously) using either an LCD-mounted or stand-alone type CNC. The new FANUC Laser C-Series is available with 2kW-, 4kW- and 6kW-rated outputs.
