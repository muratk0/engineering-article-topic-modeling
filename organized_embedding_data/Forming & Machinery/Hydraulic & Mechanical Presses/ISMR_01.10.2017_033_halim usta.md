## ABOUT COMPANY

HÍDROLIKSAN was established in 1980 by Halim Usta and manufacturing hydraulic press in a site of which 8000 m² is closed and 10000 m² is open. The research and development activities are being conducted with a technical team and expert personnel in our factory and the quality of our products are increasing day by day.

HiDROLIKSAN provides the hydraulic press demands of Turkey as well as the presses produced are also being exported to many countries. We put emphasis on Research&Development, we attend national or international fairs throughout the world as well as we attend to the activities, seminars, and trainings and keep tabs on new developments. Our production facility has the product range of all types, dimensions, options, and quality.
