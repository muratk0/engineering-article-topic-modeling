# Production and assembly

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

AT THIS YEAR’S FABTECH tradeshow, Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik will present its new BIMERIC servo production and assembly system (booth S4784)

“The BIMERIC is the ideal platform for flexible, productive and cost-efficient sub-assembly production in stamping and forming as well as assembly technology. The modular servo system is particularly useful for small and medium batch sizes and for multiple part variants. With the powerful production flow from raw material (strip or wire) to installation-ready sub-assemblies, users can achieve a consistently high product quality. The latest servo and control technology ensures maximum process reliability and extremely short set-up times and enables users to react flexibly to customer requirements,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

With the same configuration of modules – material feeder, press unit, forming units and pick and place units – the BIMERIC standard version already covers a large variety of sub-assemblies for many industry sectors (automotive, electro/electronic, HVAC components etc.). For most product and version changes, just the component-specific active tool parts need to be changed on the respective units. Afterwards, all that remains is to use the production parameters programmed into the VariControl VC 1. See www.bihler.de
