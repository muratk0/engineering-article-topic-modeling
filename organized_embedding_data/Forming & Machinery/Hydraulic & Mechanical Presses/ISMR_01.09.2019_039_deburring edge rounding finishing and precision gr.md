# Deburring, edge rounding. finishing and precision grinding

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

TIMESAVERS WILL SHOWCASE its machines for metal deburring, edgerounding, finishing and precision grinding at this year's Blechexpo trade fair (5-8 November 2019) at the Landesmesse Stuttgart (Hall 1, booth 1803).

Its 42 series WWRBW Rotary brush machine handles deburring, uniform edgerounding to a radius of 2mm, finishing and the option to remove laser oxide. The 42 series HWRB Rotary brush machine on the Timesavers' stand will also feature the new Hammerhead™ for the removal of heavy slag.

"A more economical solution for deburring, edge rounding, finishing and the removal of laser oxide is our 32 series WRBW rotary brush machine. This machine will be presented with a new option: the automatic brush set-up, an optical system which continuously measures the brush length and automatically sets the brush to the exact settings specified by the machine user. As a result, manual calibration is no longer required. This option will also be available for the 32 and 42 series," the company told ISMR.

Its 22 series HW, also featuring the Hammerhead™ option for heavy slag removal and an abrasive belt for the removal of burrs and finishing of surfaces, will also be on display.

"We are also showcasing the 12 series WB for the deburring and finishing of small parts with a new design. Our 10 series Manual Grinder workhorse will also be presented with upgrades which make it a more interesting cost-saving device to deburr, edge round or finish small batches," it concluded, adding that every customer who buys a machine at the Blechexpo exhibition will receive a large Dutch Gouda cheese!
