# Strip and wire forming

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

MEET YIH SHEN Machinery (YSM) at Wire and Tube 2018 (Hall 11, Stand C06- 01). The latest YSM CNC-39T strip and wire forming machine can process 1.5-6.5mm wire diameter as well as 60mm maximum strip width and 0.8-3.0mm strip thicknesses. It also offers 150ppm of maximum forming speed. The machine size is 3500 x 1800 x 2.3mm, with a total weight of 6.8 tons.

"The machine can produce all kinds of computer parts and electronic/ lighting components as well as fastener parts for the automotive sector. The electronic control has been upgraded to a PLC touch-screen so that production information is shown on the screen, making all data and information crystal clear to users. The forming slides use double cams so that when the forming tool returns, it will be driven by the backwards cam," explained the manufacturer.

The integration of backward cams and slide sensors prevents forming tools from sticking, protecting the equipment and prolonging its lifetime. The machine also features an INTERCOM automatic NC tapping facility (made in Italy), providing a tapping solution for users. The CNC-39T can therefore carry out forming and tapping tasks.

40% of YSM machine tools are used in the automotive and motorcycle sectors and a further 40% is for the miniature circuit breaker sector – the remainder is spread across a variety of other sectors. Email info@ysm.com.tw or see www.ysm.com.tw
