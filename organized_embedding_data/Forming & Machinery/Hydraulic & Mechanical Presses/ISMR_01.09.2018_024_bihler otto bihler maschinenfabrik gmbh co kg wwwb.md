## IRON Srl

www.iron.it

IRON is a global specialist in customised production lines and a European leader in the production of coil feeding lines of all dimensions. Its range of products includes decoilers; straighteners; levellers; cut-to-length lines; slitting lines; punch/ bending lines and a laser line.

The company's IRONBEND automatic panel bending cell is a bending station with an electro-welded and stabilised structure. The panel bending operation is controlled by Siemens digital brushless motors that enable folding to a drawn profile; a six-axis handling robot integrated into the cell that can work with most types of panel and a CNC control unit (SINUMERIK 840 D SL plus 3D bending CAM software). This flexible bending system is managed by SIEMENS SINUMERIK digital solutions with colour interface, a 15.6"-wide-touch-screen and Siemens Operate operating system.

## PIVATIC OY www.pivatic.com

"EXPERIENCE PANEL bending in a new way at EuroBLECH 2018. Our new technological approach is a great innovation which takes forward our bending expertise heritage, which started in 1982. The new concept enables more complicated profiles, thicker materials and longer parts to be bent," Finnish bending specialist, Pivatic Oy, told ISMR.

"Our strong experience of wing benders has taught us a lot. With the new panel bender, we can expand bending capability and bend new types of profiles. This new bender allows us to take a huge leap in bending sheet thicknesses (of up to 3mm) and part lengths (of up to 8,000mm). It is a strongly built machine for heavy-duty applications that eliminates the need for a crowning device. There is also no need for separate clearance control between different material thicknesses. The bending angle can be measured for angle adjustment or quality reporting. This bender is most suitable for small and medium-size part batches."
