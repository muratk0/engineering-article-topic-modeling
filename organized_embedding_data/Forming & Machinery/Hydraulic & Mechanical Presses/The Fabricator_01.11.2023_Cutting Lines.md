# Cutting Lines

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 The Fabricator]

The NEW LINE 350 is a Cutting Line Suitable for 90° Cuts into Round Tubes, Square Tubes and Rectangular Tubes, which can be Automatically Loaded and Unloaded. Our Cutting Line is Equipped with a 2-Speed Motorized Cutting Head with Amperometric Absorption Control and Ground Oil-Bathed Reducing Gears for Heavy-Duty Production. This Cutting Line has a Bar Feed Unit supplied with Rollers and driven by a Gearmotor assisted by an Inverter controlling Rapid Feed Speed and the associated Acceleration and Deceleration Ramps for Bar Feeding.

OPTION 1:

The "CI" Type is Equipped with an Inclined Automatic Loader and Inclined Worktable Designed for Loading Hollow Tubes or Solid Bars with Round, Square, or Rectangular Sections.

OPTION 2:

The "CF" Type is Equipped with an Automatic Anti-Overlapping Bundle Loader, Designed to load Hollow Tubes or Solid Bars with Round Square, or Rectangular Sections.

New Programming Instrument with 5″ Backlit Graphic Display for the Management and Optimisation of Cutting Parameters and Settings.
