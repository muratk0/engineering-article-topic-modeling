# Handling heavy steel parts

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

GOUDSMIT MAGNETICS FROM Waalre (The Netherlands) has introduced a new magnetic power gripper, suitable for numerous applications. It serves both as a lifting magnet for picking up or gripping heavy ferromagnetic parts from 6mm (such as solid steel beams, grilles, plates or milled parts) and as a handling magnet in robotic applications or with plasmacutting machines in the metal industry.

"The permanent magnet can be pneumatically controlled using compressed air and a 5/3 valve. This makes it extremely safe, because the magnet retains the load even in the event of a loss of compressed air. To give an idea of the ratio of compactness to magnetic force, a 115mm power gripper lifts a load of about 175kg and has a holding force of 527kg. The magnet is maintenance-free, has a long life and is available in three sizes; Ø50mm, Ø75mm and Ø115mm," said the Dutch manufacturer.

The power gripper can pick up or grip solid, heavy steel parts or products in robot applications. The magnets can be switched on and off via a simple pneumatic control using a 5/2 or 5/3 valve. This allows the system to lift and transport steel parts from 6mm, one at a time. The on/off position is detectable by sensors in the slotted holes. If necessary, two or three grippers can be coupled together, while only two connection ports are required due to the internal air passage.

"In the second application, the switchable power gripper lifts solid steel beams, grilles, plates, milled or cast parts and more. It can also be used to hold steel objects for welding or sharpening. What makes this system so special is that it is very powerful when switched on and has absolutely no residual magnetism when switched off. Due to the high lifting force, even heavier objects can be moved with permanent magnets. The advantage of switchable permanent lifting magnets is that they do not require a back-up system for potential power failures and can therefore safely lift heavy loads. This means considerably lower investment and maintenance costs as well as a much simpler controller," the company told ISMR. See www.goudsmitmagnets.com

## Robust component marking

THE NEW 100-WATT fibre laser, FOBA Y.1000, operates 'up to 40 per cent faster than common 50-Watt lasers and marks substrates that are usually difficult to mark with other models', according to the manufacturer, FOBA.

"The 100-Watt fibre laser is suitable for marking and engraving automotive and aerospace components as the engraved marks are robust and ensure long-term legibility throughout the entire product life cycle. This applies to high-wear products such as engine components, ball bearings, break systems or extruded products and cables. This ensures reliable part traceability and protection against product forgery."

To meet different speed and contrast specifications for customised applications, four focal widths are available, allowing for adjustments to field marking dimensions as well as marking speeds. For on-the-fly-marking (marking on moving products in continuous processing lines), it is possible to obtain field marking dimensions of up to 498 x 367mm, said FOBA. Variable scan head tuning is available in two modes – for high-quality or high-speed purposes – for sharp marking results at line speeds of up to 600m/minute. The laser beam can be positioned at two angles.

"The air-cooled system requires hardly any laser source maintenance, in contrast to the extended maintenance requirements of water-cooled systems. The laser source's lifespan is also significantly longer compared to other highpower Nd:YAG-lasers with lamps or diodes, which need to be exchanged regularly," concluded FOBA.

See www.fobalaser.com
