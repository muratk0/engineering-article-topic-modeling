# Portable pipe bevelling

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

A lightweight, portable pipe bevelling tool that can bevel and flat-face sanitary tube and pipe simultaneously to help ensure high-integrity weld joints has been introduced by Esco Tool of Holliston, MA (USA).

The Esco Wart MILLHOG® Portable Pipe & Tube Beveller is a right-angle I.D. clamping tool for performing any angle of end prep on 19.1mm I.D. to 114.3mm O.D. sanitary pipe of different schedules. Allowing operators to bevel and flat face simultaneously, it produces square and bevelled ends with a reported ±0.0254mm accuracy which helps to ensure high-integrity weld joints.

"Suitable for end prepping sanitary pipe and tubing for fusion or orbital welding, the Esco Wart MILLHOG® Portable Pipe & Tube Beveller is robust and easy to operate. Available with electric or pneumatic motors, it requires no cutting fluids and is ideal for on-site applications. Featuring a three-blade holder, a wide range of TiN-coated cutting blades are offered," outlined Esco Tool.
