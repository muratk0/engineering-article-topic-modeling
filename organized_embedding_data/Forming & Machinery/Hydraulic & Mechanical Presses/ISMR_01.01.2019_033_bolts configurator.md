# Bolts configurator

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

IF USERS NEED bolts for a new design, suffer from a supply bottleneck or want to purchase spare parts, mbo Osswald’s bolt configurator is designed to help them find what they need quickly and easily.

"Whether users need sample parts, series parts or spare parts – the bolt configurator will find the right solution in a matter of moments. Alongside the proven DIN bolts, it is now also possible to configure bolts with groove in real-time," explained the manufacturer.

Combine the bolt form, material, shaft diameter and retainer version with the required, freely definable length or grip length. The configurator automatically calculates the required minimum dimensions, creates the corresponding dimensional drawing and displays the price and current delivery time. Users then enter the required quantity and place it in their shopping cart. The required quantity of the selected retainer type can also be added to the card and ordered by checking the corresponding box.

"Users can choose between bolts with or without head in steel and stainless steel. The all-round slot applied to the shaft can be configured for various standard retainer types:

Locking washer DIN 6799, retaining ring DIN 471, SL-retainer, KL-retainer and bayonet clip," added the company.
