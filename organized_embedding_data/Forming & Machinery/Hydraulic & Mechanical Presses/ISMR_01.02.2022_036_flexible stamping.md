# Flexible stamping

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

AIDA-America is introducing its newly designed NSX mechanical stamping press. This 110-metric ton, unitised frame press runs at speeds of 120 to 300 SPM with a stroke of 65mm.

"The NSX features a low deflection, one-piece frame and a low deflection cast iron slide. Accuracy and consistent high-volume production are assured using zero clearance roller-type slide guides and counter-rotating eccentric shafts. Press and die integrity are preserved with the inclusion of hydraulic overload protection," commented the press specialist.

"The press's floor mount design, intended to be paired with isolation mounts, as well as choice of plug and play Wintriss, Link or Rockwell Allen-Bradley controls provide ease of installation. Eight programmable limit switch channels are standard," it added.

The 110-metric ton unitised frame NSX press was locally engineered at AIDA-America in Dayton, Ohio (USA).
