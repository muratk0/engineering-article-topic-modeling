VLB Group's EB150CNC-UD fully electric tube bender can bend stainless steel tubes of up to 150mm for applications such as heavyduty vehicle exhaust production.

“The innovative design is compact yet robust, and the structure's integrity reduces to the minimum any deflection, even in the most demanding jobs. VLB Up-Down series ergonomic design guarantees operating comfort. Even with the most generous tooling stack, it keeps the lowest loading height with no need for a pit," said the manufacturer.

"The electric motion in all machine movements enables high production speed and low power consumption and maintenance. Using the booster and tangent booster systems means the production of highly complex parts with 1D CLR, zero distance between bends and very short ends. These machines are suitable for highvolume production batches that require high consistency," it added.

VLB 3D software prepares these machines for Industry 4.0 and integration into production systems with loading and unloading systems.

Voestalpine Sadef, with its 150-metre-long machine with 58 roll forming stations, is now able to produce very large rollformed tubes with bandwidths of up to 1,000mm and plate thicknesses of up to 8mm.

"We are totally convinced that with these new options we can offer products in sectors where large profiles are required, and where we ensure cost benefits. Think shelving, agricultural machinery, fencing, façade industry etc. We offer close cooperation and co-engineering with our customers and a component which is 'ready for assembly'," it explained.

WAFIOS single-head tube bending machines, EcoBend and TopBend 35, offer flexible customisation options.

"With its modular machine and tool concept, the machine can be easily adjusted to customer requirements. The EcoBend series features three sizes (25, 35 and 42). An intermediate size is now available between sizes 25 and 42 of the RBV series," WAFIOS told ISMR.

The fully integrated loading concept has various options (such as a high-angle conveyor, tube loader and loading unit). The camera system on the magazine checks markings, laser cut-outs and alignment of attachment parts 'almost within cycle time'.

"Innovations, such as the extension of the work range or the swivelling mandrel unit plus the modular, retrofittable machine body, enable project-specific investment while maintaining full flexibility for other applications. The machine can be retrofitted with options for other applications. A second bending direction can even be added, which turns it into a TopBend machine," it explained.
