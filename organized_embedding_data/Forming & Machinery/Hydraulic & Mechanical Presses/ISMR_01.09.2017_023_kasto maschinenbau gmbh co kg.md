# KASTO MASCHINENBAU GMBH & CO. KG

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

SAWING AND STORAGE technology specialist, KASTO will be presenting mechanical engineering highlights from both of its business segments at Blechexpo. Among the products shown will be the KASTOecostore space and energy-saving tower storage system and the KASTOwin line of multi-purpose band saws.

Above: The KASTOwin line of band saws

Besides being suitable for storing sheet metal in a wide range of sizes, the KASTOecostore can also hold Euro-pallets and skeleton containers. The standard version accommodates materials with a width of up to 1,524mm and a length of up to 4,000mm. The system height can be up to eight metres and can be flexibly selected as required - and the same applies to loading heights. Users can specify up to three different heights between 50 and 950mm per storage system. It comes as standard with the KASTO SmartControl machine control system, which has an intuitive user interface with a touchscreen.

KASTO will also present its flexible, multi-purpose KASTOwin line of band saws at Blechexpo. These fully automatic band saws are designed for the serial and production sawing of solid materials, tubes and sections. The KASTOwin series consists of five fully automatic band saws, covering a cutting range from 330 to 1,060mm. The most compact model, which can process materials of up to 330 millimetres in diameter, will be seen in action at Blechexpo. The KASTOwin is also available with a moveable material support table for heavy workpieces and in a special version for machining tubular material.

## INSPECVISION

HBIT Its 2D and 3D inspection machines at Blechexpo 2017. The company was formed by sheet metal manufacturers in 2004 to create a complete sheet metal inspection and reverse engineering solution. Its system automatically inspects or reverse-engineers sheet metal parts at every stage of production; flat, folded, welded or assembled.

Flat parts are measured with the Planar machine, which takes up to 40 million measurements in 0.1 seconds. The measurements are then automatically compared to a CAD file to create the inspection report. For folded and assembled parts, InspecVision developed the OptiScan.

"OptiScan is the only 3D scanner in the world which can measure surfaces and edges in 3D. 3D edges are critical when measuring sheet metal parts as their geometry is mostly defined by their edges, not by their surfaces," commented Dr. Jan Antonis, Managing Director, InspecVision.

Available in a range of flexible configurations, the Planar and OptiScan integrate to create a complete sheet metal inspection solution.
