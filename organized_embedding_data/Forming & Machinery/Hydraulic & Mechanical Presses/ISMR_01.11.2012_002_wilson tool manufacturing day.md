# Wilson Tool manufacturing day

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

TOOLING SPECIALIST Wilson Tool International opened its doors for National Manufacturing Day on 5 October 2012. US Senator Amy Klobuchar spoke during the event celebrating National Manufacturing Day at Wilson Tool International (www.wilsontool.com) at White Bear Lake, Minnesota (USA).

The event was intended to highlight the importance of manufacturing to the US economy and featured remarks by Sen. Klobuchar and a presentation by Wilson Tool executives followed by product demonstrations and a tour of the company’s manufacturing facility. Klobuchar chairs the Senate Subcommittee on Competitiveness, Innovation and Export Promotion, and has been a leader in the effort to boost US manufacturing and increase America’s competitiveness in the global economy.

Wilson Tool is the world’s largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes, and punch and die components for the stamping industry. The company prides itself on lean manufacturing. The facility tour and presentation were aimed at raising awareness of skilled career options in manufacturing.

Wilson Tool International expanded its global manufacturing capabilities recently with the acquisition of Italian press brake tooling manufacturer, Toolspress, a manufacturer of press brake tooling for the sheet metal fabrication industry in Italy. Established in 1984, Toolspress manufactures a complete range of tooling for most major press brake machines. The company operates a 5,200-square-metre manufacturing facility in Parma, Italy.

Top: Brian Robinson, CEO of Wilson Enterprises. Bottom: Senator Klobuchar chairs the US Senate Subcommittee on Competitiveness, Innovation and Export Promotion

“This merger makes it possible for Wilson Tool to expand our manufacturing capabilities in Europe while providing Toolspress with greater opportunities for growth,” said Brian Robinson, CEO of Wilson Enterprises. “There will be no immediate change in operations for either company. Toolspress employees and customers will continue to do business as usual.” ■

Welding skills for UK

Joe Cosgrove. Skillweld winner, with Adrian Hawkins, managing director of Weldability-Sif (right)

THIS YEAR, the finals of the UK SkillWeld competition, after a series of passive heats and semi-finals, took place at the TWI (The Welding Institute) in Cambridge earlier this year. Joe Cosgrove (19) from Hartlepool Doosan Power Systems was the winner, with second place going to Alistair Wilson (19) from Tillicoultry, Clackmannanshire and third place to Ryan Smith (20) from Belper, Derbyshire.

Joe said: “The competition was very enjoyable and I recommend it to others.” He was amongst 10 highest scoring competitors from the regional semi-finals to go through to the 3-day final.

The finalists were judged on over 50 different elements on test pieces which included a low carbon steel fabrication involving multi-process and various joint configurations, a stainless steel pipe butt joint (DC TIG) an aluminium fabrication with fully penetrated butt welds in varying positions and two butt joints in low carbon steel using two welding processes and subject to radiographic inspection.

Weldability-Sif, a UK-based “one stop source” is a proud industry sponsor of SkillWeld 2012, the UK’s premier welding competition, which has direct links to WorldSkills UK and WorldSkills International. Adrian Hawkins, managing director of Weldability-Sif, through the Weldability-Sif Foundation, is a keen advocate of the development and training of young people in the skills of welding. Over the last five years, he told ISMR, almost a quarter of all registered and certified welders in the UK have left the industry, representing a decline of 18,000 welders leaving only around 58,000 skilled UK welders. Deciding that the UK needed to educate and train a new generation of UK-based welders, the Weldability-Sif Foundation was set up. Since its formation, it has entered into partnership with EAL and TWI, which has so far helped over 20 colleges to install specialist welder training facilities, each with seven distinct and new EAL qualifications to help seal the gap in untrained welders.

Adrian said: “Recently we have seen UK manufacturing bucking the trend and, with major build programmes rolling out in the renewable and sustainable energy markets expecting to make the UK self-sufficient for energy, this project alone will require some 20,000 trained welders.” ■
