# Durmazlar Makina San. ve. Tic. A.S

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.durmazlar.com.tr

Durmazlar is a Turkish sheet-metalworking machinery specialist offering DURMA metalforming machines, configurations with autonomous and robotic technologies and special solutions, with Industry 4.0 applications. It is a founding (constituent) member of the Industry 4.0 platform in Turkey, it explained.

"D-Line is a line system where two or more metalforming machines work with automation systems. Efficient production is assured through high repeatability, high serial production levels and minimum error. D-Line is designed according to customer requirements by choosing machine automation systems in the most appropriate way for production," it told ISMR.

“D-Line features a sheet stacking system; punch-laser cutting machine; sorting unit and CNC panel bender," the manufacturer concluded.

## Faccin Group

www.faccingroup.com

## Faccin Group,

through its brands Faccin, Boldrini and Roundo, specialises in machines for rolling and bending metal sheet and profiles. Solutions include three- and four-roll automated plate rolling systems for heavier metal bending (steel bars, tubes, pipes and profiles with bending capacities of up to 18000cm³) and two-roll models for thin metal bending. Its systems can bend sheet/ plate with thicknesses of less than 1mm to over 300mm and useful working lengths exceeding 18m. Its portfolio includes dished end lines (dishing presses, manipulators and flanging machines).

“4HEL is the most precise and easiest to use series of four-roll bending rolls with total pre-bending. It ensures high productivity with a speed that is three-four times higher than a traditional pyramid bending roll. The 4HEL bending rolls can be effectively controlled through numerical control systems and can bend sheet/plate of less than 1m up to 10m with thicknesses from 5m to 150m," it said.
