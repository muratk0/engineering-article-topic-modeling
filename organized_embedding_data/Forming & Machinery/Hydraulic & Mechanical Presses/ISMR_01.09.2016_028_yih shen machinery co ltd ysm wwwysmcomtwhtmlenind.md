YSM'S stamping and bending machines take slide forming to a new level. 'Reaching For Excellence' is the driving force of this Taiwan-based manufacturer.

"Over the last few years, we have focused on making wellengineered, robust and cost-effective stamping and bending machines. Innovation and integration has further helped us to include several other independent operations such as threading, welding, riveting etc. into a single set-up. We are now accredited to offer CE-certified production lines meeting European standards. We also have a proactive approach to participating in every major trade show - this year is our third appearance at EuroBLECH."

YSM stamping and bending production lines cater to a wide spectrum of industries such as cell phone; household electrical switchgear and appliances; automotive; construction; hardware and general application sectors.
