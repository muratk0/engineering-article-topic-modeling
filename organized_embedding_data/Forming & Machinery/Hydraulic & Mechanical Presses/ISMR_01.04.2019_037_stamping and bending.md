# Stamping and bending

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

The YSM stand at EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover, Germany

Above: The new NC-26T servo stamping and bending machine

YIH SHEN MACHINERY Co., LTD. (YSM) in Taiwan has been in business for 40 years – it was set up in 1979 to manufacture machines for cutting, coiling and forming metal springs. Today, it is a global player in forming machinery, including stamping and bending lines.

"Our goal is delivering a seamless experience of manufacturing technology onto your shop floor with the latest hightech and low-investment manufacturing solutions," the Taiwanese manufacturer told ISMR.

At the TIMTOS exhibition in Taiwan recently, the company showcased its YSM "NC series" (Servo) cam-less, servo-driven stamping and bending machine. This new version is capable of speeds of up to 250 strokes/min. with slides that can be aligned in either radial or linear arrangements.

"Some of the key features of these manufacturing lines are faster tool set-up times; suitability for small batch quantities and cost-effective manufacturing. Independently driven servo slides; a servo stamping press and servo feeder makes set-up and use very simple and effective," explained YSM.

"We have made great inroads in Europe particularly in the automotive, electrical and general engineering industry. We have successfully integrated threading, welding, assembly and part inspection into complete 'in-line solutions' on our manufacturing lines. The electric vehicle industry and renewable energy are two areas where we have seen rapid growth, mainly due to special grants awarded by governments for this sector. This, in turn, has helped us to develop automated production lines to support the manufacturing of various components needed here."

The manufacturer has delivered total turnkey solutions for various industries, which involve the integration of several operations that were, up to now, done offline.

Some examples include:

■ Electrical – integration of operations such as welding, tapping and riveting into independent manufacturing cells for components used in circuit breakers.

■ Medical – YSM stamping and bending machines are used in the manufacture of 'self-activating safety clips' made of material as thin as 0.1mm spring steel strip, which require precise handling and accuracy of the finished component. These medical components are used to reduce the risk of infection when handling needles. Tools have been integrated with simple and quick-change inserts needed to accommodate a range of sizes to manufacture this product.

"The overall benefits include increased productivity, reduced production costs, improved product quality and greater process stability," concluded YSM. Visit www.ysm.com.tw/

## Intelligent manufacturing solution

To MEET THE Industry 4.0 intelligent machinery trend, Shieh Yih Machinery Industry Co., Ltd. (SEYI) has introduced its "SEYI Intelligent Manufacturing Solution, SIMS" with smart manufacturing blueprints at the TIMTOS 2019 (Taipei International Machine Tool Show) in March 2019.

"SIMS" integrates the press, production lines and factory management systems to achieve different layers of smart manufacturing, smart production application and smart management applications. The solution was demonstrated by live-streaming a remote simulation from SEYI's Taoyuan headquarters. According to the Taiwanese press specialist, SIMS has the following capabilities:

■ Smart Machinery Application: Equipped with flexible and expansive software modules, SEYI servo and mechanical presses can automatically monitor machine status and signal early warnings to boost production efficiency levels and reduce unexpected downtime.

■ Smart Production Application: "SIMS" can assist customers in making decisions by self-determining, deciding and executing functions during the process of feeding, transporting and replacing production dies after customers receive an order.

■ Smart Management Application: "SIMS" can, via the Internet, integrate different categories of production line signals. Machine status and production data, such as overall effectiveness and production status, are obtained via machine controllers and sensors. This solution provides management information of production reports and overall equipment effectiveness and integrates systems such as ERP and MES. Remote practical operations and management are designed to effectively reduce production costs to achieve smart management efficiency. In this way, said SEYI, 'we can collectively build a new form of intelligent stamping factory with man-machine collaboration'.

Shieh Yih Machinery Industry Co., Ltd is a leading manufacturer of medium-large servo presses. Servo technology, automation systems and intelligent manufacturing are the cores of its company strategy. It also invests in developing large-sized presses and automation system integration.

"We are confronting the challenges of Intelligent Machine (Industry 4.0) and Intelligent Factory, focusing on integration of communication, control and IoT (Internet of Things) to create a new intelligent manufacture and service mode. We will be building up win-win partnerships with various parties to fulfil customer requirements and optimise supplier chains," it told ISMR.

## Cooling off

NO. 807 IS a cooling chamber from Grieve, currently used for cooling oven trucks of steel parts at the customer's facility. Workspace dimensions of this cooling chamber measure 84" W x 96" D x 72" H. 7000 CFM with a 7-1/2-HP remote mounted exhaust blower to pull room air through load and exhaust.

"This Grieve cooling chamber has aluminised steel construction, a 1/4" plate floor and is sectioned to pass through a 72" wide doorway. No. 807 has twelve (12) 24" x 24" x 6"-thick HEPA filters installed at the top of the workspace to filter exhausted air," explained Grieve.

Grieve started in 1949 with one goal in mind – to create a line of industrial heat processing equipment.
