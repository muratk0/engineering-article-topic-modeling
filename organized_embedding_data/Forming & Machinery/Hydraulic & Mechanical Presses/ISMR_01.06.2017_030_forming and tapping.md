# Forming and tapping

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

YIH SHEN MACHINERY (YSM) has been producing press forming machines since the 1980s and has developed a solid foundation in machine tool R&D. The company has now sold its high-end press forming machines into the European market, which now takes up 20% of YSM's exports.

"The primary application for our machines is in the automotive and motorcycle sectors. They produce components such as tube-mounted components, brake components and headlight mounted components," said YSM Vice-General Manager, Lin Ching-Tang.

For sheet forming, YSM does not adopt the continuous mould design but pre-presses the sheet and places it into the presses for the forming process which, it says, successfully reduces waste. In line with the all-in-one forming trend now prevalent in the industry Mr. Lin and his R&D crews have integrated presses; forming machines; tapping machines; welding equipment and automation into one design to help YSM customers keep their costs down.

The latest YSM-36T strip and wire forming machine can process 0.6-3.2mm wire diameters; 30mm maximum strip width and 0.3-1.5mm strip thickness with 250ppm maximum forming speed. Machine size is 2100 x 1000 x 1900mm and it weighs 2.5 tons. YSM-36T processes include forming and tapping.

"The machine can produce all kinds of computer and electronic/lighting components, as well as fastener parts in the automotive sector. Production information is shown on the touch-screen, making all the information crystal clear to users. The forming slides use double cams so that, when the forming tool returns, it will be driven by the backward cam. The integration of backward cams and slide sensors prevents forming tools from sticking, protecting the equipment and prolonging its life. The machine also features INTERCOM automatic NC tapping (made in Italy)," explained YSM.

"While European machinery suppliers face tough competitions nowadays, we are confident that our equipment can meet our customers' requirements for four main reasons. Firstly, our stamping and bending lines are a competitive alternative to traditional high-speed presses, because this process significantly reduces material waste during production. This translates into a lower cost per part. Secondly, our production lines are very cost-effective because they are ideal for small batch production where tool-change time is minimal and tools are inexpensive so the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) is, consequently, very competitive.

"Thirdly, in today's world, versatility is key. Our simple 'modular tooling' adapts easily to end-user design and manufacturing activities. Finally, our 'easy-to-use' operating system does not require intensive training programmes and can be delivered on the shop floor," it concluded.

## Twenty-one years of Impax tooling

WILSON TOOL INTERNATIONAL, the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping and tableting industries, celebrated the stamping division's 21st birthday recently.

"Marking 21 years of tooling advancement for our stamping division is a tremendous milestone," said Chris Lawless, President, Wilson Tool International. "We are proud to celebrate past accomplishments and look forward to another 21 years of growth and stamping innovation."

The company's headquarters in White Bear Lake, Minn. (USA) celebrated with an all-day educational forum and birthday party to mark the anniversary. Presentations by Bohler Uddeholm and Special Springs featured discussions on tool steels and spring selections, respectively. Additional topics included advancements in tooling and industry insights. More than 50 stamping professionals from across the country joined Wilson Tool for the forum, which included plant tours and 21st birthday food and beverages.

Wilson Tool International® has been a leading provider of tooling solutions for sheet metal fabricating machinery worldwide. Products are manufactured at the company's global headquarters in White Bear Lake, Minn., USA, as well as manufacturing facilities in England, China, Brazil and Italy. Exacta product lines are manufactured in Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

## Sawing and storing material

ECOLOGY AND ECONOMY when sawing and storing material were the themes of an Open House at Kasto's UK headquarters and showroom, attended by ISMR, from 16-18 May 2017.

The firm's latest sawing machines and storage systems were launched in the UK, having been introduced last year by the German parent company. They included the new KASTOmicut, which offers higher levels of technology and performance at a lower price than the company's previous range of pivot-bow bandsaws. Also featured was the KASTOwin pro AC 5.6, a production bandsaw for use with either a bimetal or TCT (Tungsten Carbide Tipped) blade.

"This system is just as productive but less expensive than the KASTOtec, or even faster with the adaptive KASTOrespond system, and has the added advantages of lower power consumption for the hydraulics as well as energy recovery features that reduce electricity use and hence running costs," explained the company.

The occasion was also an opportunity for ISMR to hear about a new, modular, entry-level sheet storage system called KASTOecostore, which is lower priced than its predecessor, the KASTOunitower C.

KASTO also unveiled a recently developed energy recovery and storage system for the company's industrial warehouses, which are used globally for automated storage and retrieval of bar, tube, sheet, plate and other materials. The technology, which is optional on all of the company's storage systems and is also retrofittable, provides savings by enabling surplus kinetic energy to be converted into electricity and stored temporarily for later use. To achieve even greater efficiency, energy storage capacitors can be integrated. For further details, see www.kasto.com
