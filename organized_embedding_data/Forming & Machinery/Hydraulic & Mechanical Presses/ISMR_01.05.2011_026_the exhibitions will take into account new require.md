Haulick + Roos, based in Pforzheim, will showcase its range of precision stamping and forming presses, from 250 – 4000 kN compression force, including related peripheral devices. It also provides precision punching machines with peripherals, such as strip feeding, cutting tools, measuring and control equipment and tool change systems for materials purchased. Multiple operations, including punching, on one line are the speciality of manufacturers of customized lines such as IRON SpA. The Italian family-run coil processing specialist recently developed a new concept line for flexible punching and bending from coil (FPB). It is a flexible modular line that incorporates a punch press (with thick turret tooling), a part-turning device (after punching), positive and negative bends on the punched parts and can handle part dimensions ranging from 650 – 1500mm in width and 1500 – 3600mm in length.

Jetcam develops CAD/CAM nesting and material management software products for the sheet metal fabrication and aerospace/automotive composite industries, covering punching, profiling, routing and knife cutting technologies. It recently released new CrossTrack order, material and assembly management software suite, along with the latest version of the award-winning JETCAM Expert CADCAM and nesting software. JETCAM CrossTrack provides complete end-to-end management of orders, materials, parts, nests and, where applicable, assemblies. Kjellberg Finsterwalde will also be at the show. Its new plasma system HiFocus 161i cuts steel up to 50mm thick.

Komatsu will be promoting its press portfolio, including servo, gap, straightside and forging presses. Lankhorst Mouldings will showcase its KLP RollBlock coil storage system for storage of coils with diameters ranging from 500 to 2,500mm.

Leifeld Metal Spinning Ltd. has developed its new spinning machine generation, the SC series. Leifeld promotes the development of technologies such as flow-forming and profiling. The LVD Group is a leading manufacturer of a comprehensive range of sheet metal/plate working machine tools and software solutions, including laser cutting systems, punch presses, press brakes, guillotine shears and mid-level automation systems, and now incorporates a new Energy Reduction System (ERS) into its VX punch presses and PPEC series press brake products, helping to tackle the topical issue of rising energy costs.

Mate Precision Tooling will exhibit the latest in tooling system technology at BlechExpo (Hall 5, Booth 5311). Its Ultra IMT Fully Indexable Multi Tool can ‘achieve any angle setting on the workpiece’ and is designed to use Mate Ultra punches, strippers and Slug Free dies,

eliminating the need for specialized tooling inventory. Visitors will see how the Mate Ultra IMT maximises existing punch press output with features such as integral punch length adjustment, variable stripping force options, fully ported lubrication, easy active station identification etc. Mate’s new ULTRAFORM FX AND XT holders provide interchangeability with Mate’s ULTRAFORM insert system. These holders reduce holder complexity for machines with precise stroke control and for machine users who infrequently use or don’t want length adjustment.

Mate’s new Rollerball Deburr tool eliminates burrs and secondary operations on punched parts. The Rollerball Deburr pushes the burr away and creates a radius on the side of punched parts. Using a special ball in the upper and lower part of the tool, all part contours can be processed and deburred, even in tight corners. Rollerball Deburr is available for ThickTurret and TRUMPF Style applications.

Walter Nef GmbH is a leading manufacturer of hydraulic presses and production plants for all areas of forming.

Ophir Optronics was founded in 1976 and today is a leading global supplier of precision infrared components and laser measurement devices. The company develops, manufactures and markets infrared (IR) optics and laser measuring devices. The product range from Ophir’s Optics Group is the design and manufacture of optical elements for CO2 high power lasers used in industry for laser cutting and welding equipment.

Profil develops and manufactures pierce nuts and studs which are fastened to formed sheet metal parts with a riveting process. Safan is a specialist press brake and shear manufacturer for the thinner sheet metal segment, with its servo electric E-Brake, the I-Brake (the first electronic brake with integrated robots) and R-Brake.

Salvagnini designs, builds, sells and services flexible machines and systems for processing sheet metal. Products include the P4Xe and P2Xe hybrid panel benders; the S4Xe integrated punch-shearing systems; the S4+P4 complete panel production FMS lines; the L1Xe, L3 and L5 fibre laser cutting machines; the SL4 integrated punching-fibre laser cutting system; the E2 and B2 press-brakes, the ROBOformER® G2 robotic bending cells; automatic pack or tray store-towers and integrated systems for factory logistics. Its latest innovations are the L3 and L5 fibre lasers.

Schuler will be showcasing a range of press technologies on its stand at BlechExpo. Serapid specialises in quick die change-oriented manufacturing and, in recent years, has added new roller bars and die carts to its range of products to meet the demand for new die transportation and positioning tools.

For over sixty years, Timesavers has been providing widebelt abrasive machines for metal, wood, plastics and a wide range of other materials.

Grindingmaster machines are used for deburring, finishing, calibrating etc. TRUMPF’s core business is machine tools for flexible sheet metal processing for punching and forming, laser processing and bending. In the field of industrial lasers and laser systems, the company is headquartered near Stuttgart in Ditzingen and is a market leader, regularly releasing new products such as the new TruFiber fibre.

Weinbrenner will be promoting its B12 bending centre, press brakes, shears and tools whilst Dutch press brake tooling specialist, Wila, will have new products such as TIPS, a digital control system for press brake tools, on display.
