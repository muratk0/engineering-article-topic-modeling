# Packing a punch

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

MATE PRECISION TOOLING has introduced its new Roller Solutions™ tooling for increased productivity from thick turret punch presses.

“This new tooling family allows fabricators to quickly and cost effectively create rib, shear, offset and pincher features in sheet metal. Compared to traditional methods utilising progressive forming tools, Mate Roller Solutions dramatically increases productivity when creating detailed metal forms,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

Roller Solutions™ Tooling is available in configurations such as Rolling Rib tooling, Rolling Shear tooling (for slitting and nibbling operations), Rolling Offset tooling (to create offsets) and Rolling Pincher tooling (allowing parts to be quickly and cleanly separated from surrounding metal without burrs).

Designed for thick-turret B-station applications, all four Mate Roller Solutions tooling handle high-speed applications – equal to the programmed table travel speed. The tooling is suitable for stainless steel, mild steel and aluminium. See www.mate.com
