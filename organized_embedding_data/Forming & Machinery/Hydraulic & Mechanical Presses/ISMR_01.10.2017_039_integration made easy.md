From 2017 onwards, it aims to boost its sales and services in the Asian region – Russia, India, Japan and Southeast Asia will be LCM's latest prospective markets for introducing its new servo-mechanical tandem line.

After opening its Thailand office in 2015, LCM registered significant growth of 60-70%. This performance, said Wang, implies great potential in Southeast Asian countries. He estimates that the next market for drastic automotive growth outside China is in Southeast Asia – over 70% of LCM's customers are automotive manufacturers, while electronics and plywood industries share the remaining 20-30% customers. With its service centres, the company can respond to customers instantly, regardless of distance or time differences.

LCM has a positive outlook for 2017. At the beginning of this year, orders had already taken up half of the company's total capacity. LCM views 2017 as a promising year for global sheet metal formers and fabricators.

## Reaching for the next 70 years

The highlight for LCM this year is its latest servo-mechanical tandem line (2400-1000 tons).

"We are the first to develop this line in Taiwan," commented Patrick Wang, stressing that the production line is designed to meet the most advanced technology for the press industry.

"The production line enables users to benefit from, on one hand, high speed generated from mechanical presses and, on the other, energyefficiency from the servo-hydraulic presses. The reason for equipping the production line with a servo system is because it helps users to simulate production under a range of different circumstances, which fundamentally benefits those who need to diversify for modular, cross-industrial production. Under operational tests, the servo-mechanical tandem line can increase SPM from 18- 20 up to 30-40 times, thanks to its highly automated system. It therefore radically raises productivity," explained Patrick Wang.

"In achieving greater efficiency, versatility and precision, our production line broadens the potential for users to win more contracts as it fulfils a wide range of industrial requirements and specifications."

In the first 70 years of LCM's operation, it has achieved several milestones as a hydraulic press manufacturer. Its progress in the press sector, it told ISMR, shows that metal forming can move towards a zero-pollution industry. LCM's main objectives over the next few decades are therefore to boost its development of servo-hydraulic presses and mechanical presses as it believes that these will become indispensable to supply growing demands in high-tech and future industries.

## More connected and intelligent

The aspiration for LCM's next phase is to levelup new-generation hydraulic presses.

According to Patrick Wang: "Our goal from 2017-2020 is to develop more concepts that would contribute to higher performance servo-mechanical presses. The existing servomechanical tandem line has made huge process improvements in hot stamping press technology, as well as power units. Issues such as reducing the cycle time spent making components and eradicating extra energy loss have already been tackled and, in the future, the servo-operating system will be responsible for accurate communication between machines and operators – truly transforming the sheet metal forming industry into an intelligent industry."

To manufacture 'smartly', global manufacturers are choosing intelligent equipment to help them stay at the top of the field. LCM has been careful to ensure that the products machined by its presses are made of extremely high-strength material which is a prerequisite for high-end automotive manufacturing or high-tech (PCB) industries. LCM has a very high profile in the development of the PCB industry in Taiwan as the country has concentrated on becoming a high-tech island. It has supplied the industry with PCB laminator equipment, multi-layer lamination presses etc.

"I believe that, over the next few years, enhanced technologies in forging and hotforming will be introduced by our R&D team. LCM’s engineering expertise is continuously broadening its capacity to serve more industries," Patrick Wang concluded.

As it celebrates its 70th anniversary, LCM wishes to reconnect with old and new customers across its global service centres. With the same spirit passed down through its first- and second-generation managers, LCM aims to carry its message of mechanical excellence and technological dedication far into the future.
