Needle scalers remove coatings, corrosion

CS Unitec’s Trelawny Vibro-Lo VL series low-vibration needle scalers are designed to remove coatings, corrosion, and accumulated materials from metal and masonry surfaces. According to the company, the unit offers eight times less vibration than traditional needle scalers, which helps reduce operator fatigue and prevent injury.

An optional dust shroud helps protect workers from potentially hazardous dust and maintain a clean working environment when the tool is attached to a vacuum and appropriate PPE is used.

The pneumatic tool provides up to 3,000 blows per minute. It is suitable for irregular surfaces that are otherwise difficult to prepare. Other applications include cleaning and stress-relieving weld seams and steel joints.

Press brake cell offers average cycle time of 4 sec./bend

TRUMPF has introduced what it says is the world’s fastest system for the automated bending of small parts, the TruBend Cell 7000, with an average cycle time of 4 sec./bend. The system comprises a TruBend 7036 Cell Edition press brake, the BendMaster bending robot, the LoadMaster Bend material handling unit, a pallet system, and tracks within an enclosure that requires 69 sq. ft. of floor space.

The press brake delivers 40 tons of force and features an electric torque motor for high-speed acceleration. The ram is divided into two 20-in. sections to provide clearance for the grippers on the robot. The grippers can position components with a sheet thickness up to 0.3 in. The material handling unit’s multivacuum gripper prepares the bending part parallel to the current bending operation.

The flexible loading arrangement allows for up to 24 different blanks or 4,800 parts to be delivered to the machine. The standard system configuration includes unloading of finished parts to boxes or bins.

GTAW power source uses preset parameters

Miller Electric Mfg. LLC has introduced the Syncrowave 300 GTAW power source. It uses preset parameters and delivers a stable arc, the company reports.

Equipped with AC/DC GTAW, DC pulsed GTAW, and DC SMAW capabilities, the machine is suitable for general fabrication applications. It welds materials up to 3/8 in. thick with an output rating of 300 amps and 22 V at a 30% duty cycle.

The 178-lb. power source features simplified setup, and Pro-Set technology helps eliminate guesswork when setting GTAW parameters.

Automated bending system handles loads up to 44 lbs.

Amada’s HG 1003 ARs combines an ultraprecise press brake and a 7-axis robot to allow unattended processing and increased productivity. Designed to handle sheets up to 39 by 31 in., the robot can support loads up to 44 lbs.

The robot moves on a linear track to provide stage bending with the flexibility to process materials anywhere along the entire length of the machine. Additionally, the machine is equipped with an extended backgauge system that has two stops for adjusting the depth of the part and a third stop that provides for lateral alignment. If required, automatic readjustments are performed immediately in both the X and Y axes.

Parts can be moved forward and backward and rotated once they have reached the stop to ensure the correct bending position.

Portable wire feeder has IP44 protection class rating

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has introduced the Robust Feed Pro portable wire feed system. According to the manufacturer, it is the first product in its category with an IP44 protection class rating, which means it is protected against water splashes from all directions, as well as solid objects larger than 1 mm.

The completely sealed unit has a double-wall design with impact zones made from a more flexible material to absorb more impact energy. All controls and power and gas connections are protected inside the case, with a dedicated service cover providing access to the electronics.

The feeder is suitable for industrial fabrication operations such as marine and offshore, steel erection, structural steel fabrication, hardsurfacing and cladding, repair and maintenance, pressure vessels, and pipeline.

Aluminum housing air tools offer strength, light weight

Dynabrade Inc. has introduced a new line of aluminum housing air tools. The die grinders, disc sanders, and drills are manufactured with the rigidity, strength, and light weight of a housing machined from a single piece of aluminum bar stock.

Straight-line die grinders are available in 25,000 and 30,000 RPM for deburring and material removal applications. The straight-line design is suitable for applications in confined areas.

Right-angle die grinders are available in 12,000 and 20,000 RPM to provide high torque under load for aggressive material removal.
