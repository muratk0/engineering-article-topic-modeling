# WEARING IT DOWN

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

FEIN IS ADDING two compact models to its range of angle grinders. The WS 10 grinders, which feature an 800-watt motor, are easy to handle and offer optimum weight to balance for continuous use. They can be guided safely and comfortably with one hand and make light work of applications in metal construction, stainless steel machining or vehicle repairs. The new angle grinders with disc diameters of 115 or 125mm are designed for light-duty grinding jobs, deburring and removing tarnish or rust. They allow for safe and fatigue-free working, as they are lightweight, just 32cm long, and feature slim handle zones. The self-supporting motor design reduces the vibration of the FEIN angle grinder to just 2.5 to 5 m/s2. Users can therefore work continuously for up to eight hours; there is also no need for a special anti-vibration handle. The FEIN WSG 10-115 is an easy-to-handle compact angle grinder for light-duty deburring and grinding work. A small gear head ensures safe working even in tight spaces. The FEIN WSG 10-125 S is an angle grinder which is also suited to brushing and satin finishing applications. The two new angle grinders are equipped with a classic switch system and an M14 holder with spindle lock.
