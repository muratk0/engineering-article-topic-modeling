# Hydraulic press specialist

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

EVER SINCE it was set up in 1976, the basic philosophy of DEES Hydraulic has been to provide customers with "complete engineering, manufacturing and solutions". Certified to ISO 9001:2015 and CE, DEES designs safe and friendly interfaces for hydraulic presses in metalforming and hot forming of composite or alloy materials. Press capacity ranges from 10 to 6,000 tons, and the company has supplied over 4500 machines to 85 countries.

DEES Hydraulic Industrial Co., Ltd. supplies customers such as Bombardier; Boeing; Honda; Toyota; Audi; Volkswagen; Mercedes-Benz; BMW parts; Volvo; Jaguar; Land Rover; General Motors;

Bosch Group Home Appliances;   
Electrolux; GE Home Appliances; Haier;   
3M Group; Sony; Foxconn etc.

"We currently have a triple-action hydraulic tryout press in service with Volvo," it told ISMR. "With outer and inner slides and a cushion system for car body part forming, the press is also equipped with an accumulator to speed up production efficiency and a moving bolster to make die change easier."
