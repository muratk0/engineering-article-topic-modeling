# 'Three in One' technology

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

SINCE ITS ESTABLISHMENT in 1982, CHIEF Automation Technology Co., Ltd. has been actively engaged in R&D and innovation in the manufacture of stamping automation equipment. In recent years, in response to requirements for high-tensile steel sheet processing and challenges in special sheet processing, the company has developed stamping automation equipment designed to assist the stamping industry to improve production efficiency and quality. One example is its '3 in 1' compact servo-straightener feeder.

“We continue to increase our customers' capabilities with a range of services," it told ISMR. “The '3 in 1' compact servo-straightener feeder can save space and improve plant efficiency. Even if the material to be processed is thicker, the overall width would be around 5m or smaller.“

"An innovative mechanical structure minimises the space required for the entire machine. A variety of machine choices are suitable for different punch size and job requirements. Special order options are available for all press sizes e.g. it is possible to save material with the Zig Zag Electronic Roller Feeder," added the company.
