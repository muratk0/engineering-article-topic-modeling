# Servo blanking line

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

SHUNG DAR'S INDUSTRIAL CO. LTD'S servo blanking line provides a total processing solution for steel coil blanking and blank stacking operations. It features a Siemens servo control drive system and modular mechanisms for highly automated production, including automatic coil loading, threading and stacking. "Production line parameters are database-controlled. Mechanical adjustments can be made simultaneously via data downloading and confirmation and are designed to enhance operator safety and operational efficiency. Multiple coil carts and a dual stacking system make zero machine downtime possible, which also enhances safety," the Taiwanese manufacturer told /SMR.

"We were the first automation and peripherals company in Taiwan to obtain ISO9001 certification. We are also an A3 certified design consultant (Taiwan's Industrial Development Bureau) for automated machine design and development." See www.shungdar.com.tw ■
