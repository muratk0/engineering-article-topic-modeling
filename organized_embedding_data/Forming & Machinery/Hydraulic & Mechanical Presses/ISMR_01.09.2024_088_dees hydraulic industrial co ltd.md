# DEES Hydraulic Industrial Co., Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand G01

www.deesgroup.com.tw

Founded in 1976, Taiwanese manufacturer DEES Hydraulic Industrial Co., Ltd specialises in the production of large hydraulic machinery, unmanned production lines and hydraulic presses, hydraulic folding beds and hydraulic shears (supplying over 4500 customised hydraulic presses worldwide). Its main products include deep drawing; diespotting; die tryout; triple-action and C-Frame presses as well as tandem press lines. It has introduced a 2,500-ton tandem line, customised for automotive manufacturing.

At EuroBLECH 2024, it will showcase a 500-ton, fourpost hydraulic press for its Japanese client, JIS (including pump, bolts, valves, oil seal, regulations etc.) as an example of its custommade precision hydraulic presses. The press features a single main cylinder, a bolster (length: 1300mm x width:1800mm) and strokes (ascending: 120 mm/s, descending: 150mm/s and pressing: 7mm/s.).
