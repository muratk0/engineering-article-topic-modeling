# Power-packed angle grinder

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

INDUSTRIAL TOOL SPECIALIST, Fuji Air Tools, has launched a new and powerful series of 7" angle grinders with 1800W/2.4hp output - 20% higher power, it told ISMR, than alternative grinders in the same class.

"The FA-70 series is ergonomic, easy to use and provides extended service life, so users can increase productivity. The robust grinders can be used on various materials and are suitable for all heavy-duty applications performed in shipyards; bridge & building construction; mining; foundry; metalworking; rolling stock and oil and gas," explained the manufacturer.

"The FA-70 series has been designed with an improved motor and exhaust mechanism to ensure a wide 'high power output zone'. Optimised air flow within the body gives extra cooling on the gear leading to extended service life and reduced maintenance. The FA-70 has a new and improved blade material with higher wear resistance which also enables longer replacement intervals."

The angle grinders have been ergonomically designed with a thinner body than its predecessors and a low head to ensure easy operation. The grinders enable right- or left-hand handle configurations, giving the user added flexibility. The side handle has high stiffness to reduce vibration levels. See www.fujitools.com

## Quick change tooling

MATE PRECISION TOOLING will be on booth 1435 at FABTECH Mexico 2017 (2-4 May 2017 in Mexico City), exhibiting an expanded range of fabrication solutions including its new Ultra® QCT™ Quick Change Tooling, CleanBend™ Press Brake Tooling and 1 Micron Optics for fibre lasers. “Mate Ultra QCT tooling is simple and intuitive to set up and fully compatible with our Ultra® family of guides and standard canisters. Mate Ultra QCT is made from durable M4PM™ tool steel. It is coated with SuperMax™, our next-generation coating, to minimise wear and preserve fits. To set up, flip the latch to remove and snap the new insert into place. The punch is keyed at the perimeter of the punching tool – not the centre – providing superior angularity control. The shoulder of the punch remains securely guided by the guide when punching. Only one punch driver is needed for rounds or shapes," Mate told ISMR. Mate's new CleanBend™ forming technology for press brakes mimics a folding operation with 'minimal to no marking of the sheet metal'. CleanBend is designed for a wide range of applications including bending short flanges; bending a flange to a diagonal sharp or across existing holes; bending delicate or polished materials, including plastic coverings, and more. Mate 1 Micron Optics for fibre lasers feature fused silica lenses and mirrors. Mate's high reflection laser mirror coating is designed to maximise reflection, improving performance to prolong product life. See www.mate.com or email marketing@mate.com ■
