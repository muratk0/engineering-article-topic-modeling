# Heavy tonnage press development

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

SPANISH COMPANY GESTAMP AUTOMOCIÓN in Bielefeld (Germany) and press specialist Nidec ARISA from Logroño (Spain) are working together on a joint stamping press project whose outcome will mark a milestone in the long history shared by both companies. The heavy tonnage servo press, with 45,000kN force, and over 9 metres of die surface area will be delivered to Gestamp's facility in Bielefeld, Germany, at the conclusion of the project.

The single press, with two carriages and three uprights, is capable of levelling the eccentric loads of the die, with sufficient useful die dimensions for a wide range of present and future work. The installation will cover more than 37 metres in length, 20 metres in width, a height of almost 10 metres above ground and an underground depth of 6 metres. It features a weight of more than 1,350 tonnes, torque power of 9mW and 7mW of energy storage engines.

The servo press will be fed by a destacker, with several loading stations, and a conveyor belt system to feed the parts through the successive die stations at an extremely high rate. All offline programming will be done through Optiservo software developed by Nidec ARISA. The press has a "Full Size Energy Management" system that works through compensation engines, responsible for storing energy and transferring it to the press as necessary. A fully automated diechange system will optimise production of the installation and its handling.

One of the largest companies in the automotive sector worldwide, Gestamp Automoción is a business group with around 100 production plants spread all over the world. It is a leading player in the design, development and manufacture of metallic components for automobiles and continuously invests in research and development.

The relationship between Gestamp and Nidec ARISA goes back a long way. More than 100 presses and accessories have been supplied to the Spanish group since its creation in 1997. With presses supplied in Europe, Asia, Brazil and North America, the strong collaboration between these two companies extends worldwide.
