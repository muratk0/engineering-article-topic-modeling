# High-speed rotary shear line

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

SHUNG DAR INDUSTRIAL Co., Ltd's latest development, a high-speed rotary shear line which is commonly used in mass coil service centre production, showcases automated production.

"The compact line includes functions such as automatic coil loading, automatic coil threading and automatic levelling adjustment, cutting the rectangular, trapezoid and parallelogram sheet shapes, as well as stacking. The system adopts the uninterrupted cutting method, and omits some devices such as feed rolls which may indent the steel sheet and an arc guide device which may collide and leave marks on the steel sheet," the Taiwanese manufacturer told ISMR.

Compared with traditional shearing lines, the Shung Dar rotary shear line utilises a calculation unit which, when given the cutting length and the average feed rate of the sheet stock, can calculate synchronous speed and synchronisation length, ensuring that the sheet stock is fed to a rotary shear for flycutting via its rotating cutting edges.

"By using a servo motor, the shears can continuously cut and stack steel sheet at a maximum speed of 90M per minute. This feature can increase productivity as well as reduce production time and cost. The adoption of a dual levelling system, integrated with the tension levelling unit, enables levelling plasticity of up to 95%, creating high precision levelling for better usage of the processed blanks," concluded Shung Dar.
