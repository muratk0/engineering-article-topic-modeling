# Precision forming presses

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

AIDA, founded in 1917, develops and markets Direct Servo Forming (DSF®- Direct Servo-Former) servo presses, installing thousands of units worldwide to date.

"Our AIDA DSF® servo presses offer unrivalled operability, even with a reduced number of forming stages. Thanks to the 'soft-touch' function, which causes the punch to slow down when it comes into contact with the material, these presses guarantee a significant increase in the overall quality of the parts produced without compromising productivity," commented the Italian press specialist.

AIDA has developed an energy-control architecture that replaces the kinetic energy storage capacity of a flywheel with the electrical energy storage capacity of capacitors. The peak electrical energy required during the forming phase is predominantly provided by the electrical energy previously stored in the capacitors, leading to power demand from the grid comparable to that of a mechanical press according to the press manufacturer.

"All AIDA DSF® servo presses use proprietary AIDA servomotors developed specifically for forming operations, offering five times the performance of a standard commercial servomotor," outlined AIDA.

At the beginning of 2021, AIDA unveiled its new DSF-NE2, a monolithic two-point servopress that combines the high rigidity of the monoblock frame with the flexibility of the freely programmable servo motion of AIDA DSF® technology. The DSF-NE2 presses are equipped with AIDA's high-torque, low-speed servo motor. Each frame component is designed and verified with FEM systems and is manufactured to reduce deflection under load to less than 0.1mm/m.

"The DSF-NE2 series of presses guarantee multiple forming of various lengths, low noise and low vibration, optimising all production and maintenance operations while ensuring maximum application flexibility and long die life. This series of presses, capable of speeds of up to 120 strokes per minute, was awarded Product of the Year in the 'Stanz-und Umformtechnik' category at the EuroBLECH Awards 2022 in Hanover," confirmed AIDA.

The Blechexpo 2023 trade fair in Stuttgart (7-10 November 2023) will be an opportunity for AIDA to provide visitors with information on the DSF-NE2 series of presses and its entire series of presses (including servo presses, high-speed presses for motor core lamination and precision forming presses). These are suitable for the production of hightech e-mobility components such as bipolar plates, the cornerstone of hydrogen-powered cars.

For the production of EV/HEV engines, AIDA introduced its MSP series of highspeed lamination presses with a proprietary high-rigidity design and multiple suspension points in 2002. It recently launched the MSP-3000-370 high-speed press specifically to handle moulds of up to 3,700mm long, as required for double-row production.

The new AET Electronic Transfer, the solution developed by AIDA for the after-market, enables the handling of parts and components in multi-station or interconnected presses. This will also be showcased at Blechexpo in Stuttgart.

"AET transfer units integrate easily with all types of mechanical and servo presses from any manufacturer, and are perfectly suited for retrofitting to existing machines," concluded AIDA.■
