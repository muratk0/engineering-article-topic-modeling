# Cutting, rolling and drilling

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

At EuroBLECH 2024, Akyapak Machinery will launch three new machines and software on stand H42 in Hall 14 (22-25 October 2024).

"Engineered with future machining needs in mind, our HARE robotic cutting system is designed to minimise operational costs and significantly reduce processing times. With a robotic arm capable of moving vertically and adjusting to any angle, HARE boasts 6+2 axis control, allowing for comprehensive four-side processing," it told ISMR.

"Its enclosed cabin protects the robotic arm, while the robust design and secure clamping system ensure vibration-free cutting. HARE handles complex parts and extended profile lengths with ease, while offering real-time monitoring capabilities," it added.

The Next-Gen NG Series, a new platerolling innovation, removes the need for an underframe, resulting in a more stable and efficient body structure. The introduction of BYRED and NEXTROLL software promises a more intuitive user experience.

“BYRED's integrated system streamlines interactions between companies, suppliers and customers, optimising the entire supply chain. It allows seamless integration with third-party systems, providing a smoother operational flow from quoting to delivery. NEXTROLL, tailored for plate rolls, assists operators by offering a graphical preview of the bending process, ensuring accuracy and eliminating errors before bending begins. Both systems will be demonstrated at EuroBLECH," confirmed the manufacturer.

"Integrated with BYRED, our VENERA beam drilling machine offers real-time monitoring and production management. Rathole processing and chamfering capabilities have been improved to process a profile's corner chamfers. Part length measurements are now easier thanks to the scale added to the jaw pistons, which also helps to reduce waiting times by eliminating the need for full stroke operations," concluded the company.■
