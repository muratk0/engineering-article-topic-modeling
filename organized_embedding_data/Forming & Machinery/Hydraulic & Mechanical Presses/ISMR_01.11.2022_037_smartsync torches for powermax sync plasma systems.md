# SmartSYNC torches for Powermax SYNC plasma systems

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Hypertherm Associates has introduced four new SmartSYNC® torches for Hypertherm Powermax SYNC® plasma systems. The torches are available in two different lengths—0.6m (2') and 1.2 m (4')—with two different torch head angles (45 and 90 degrees).

Like other SmartSYNC torches, these new torches automatically set the correct amperage, air pressure and operating mode upon installation of the consumable cartridge and allow the operator to change the cartridge without returning to the power supply. The torches also come with Hypertherm's FastConnect feature to make switching between torches fast and easy.

Compared to the first generation Duramax® Hyamp long torch, the new SmartSYNC long torches are lighter, requiring 45 per cent less lifting force. They are even more robust and manufactured with a new flexible lead for improved ergonomics and manoeuvrability.

“Customers in a wide variety of industries have adopted the long torch since its introduction in 2014," said Jeff Hluchyj, a Powermax product manager. “These customers tell us they can get a lot more work done, especially if they were using an oxyfuel torch before. They also like the torch's ergonomic design and ability to stand up straight when cutting. Since operators do not need to bend over while using the long torch, they don't get tired as easily and can cut for longer.“

Hypertherm long torches are used for a wide variety of jobs including cutting coupons at steel mills and risers and runners at foundries; decommissioning or demolishing ships and nuclear facilities; scrapping metal for recycling and removing skeletons from cutting tables.

## Digitalisation and automation

"Our customers face the same challenges that we do i.e. supply chain disruption, rising inflation and prices, skilled labour shortages and an ageing workforce. How do we ensure that information and experience is transferred in a company? We help customers to automate the true expertise and experience in their companies so that they can become more profitable and look after their own customers," highlighted Marco Mostert.

The megatrends of digitalisation and automation are not new; both technologies have been in the marketplace for years. However, they have been thrown into sharp focus by the combination of challenges that manufacturers are facing right now. More companies, of all sizes, are now embracing these technologies as a result.

“In terms of the type and size of customers currently embracing digitalisation, we see a mix right now. I do not believe that digitalisation is solely confined now to high-end customers but is moving more towards 'continuous improvers' who wish to be elite performers in their manufacturing operations. Over the past couple of years, while interest rates have been low, owners of all sizes of companies have been investing in new technologies. They tend to be continuous improvers, perhaps new-generation management of family-owned companies that have grown up with digital technologies and are more comfortable bringing them into their operations. It is a definite trend that will continue to accelerate, in our opinion. We tap into this trend with our products and services to help our customers become more productive and profitable," explained Mostert.

Hypertherm intends to grow its waterjet technology share of EMEIA markets as it sees huge potential for high-end applications. It also aims to be increasingly multi-technology focused in terms of the cutting processes that it offers.

"Since 2019, waterjet now makes up more than 20% of our business. We expect to be able to do that in other adjacent technologies and build out that portfolio. We also want to increase our automation portfolio in that space (digital technologies enable all those cutting processes). In consulting services, we want to optimise total cutting value streams (no matter the technologies used) for our key and strategic accounts. We have been building out and investing in that capability for our customers," added Evan A. Smith.

## CAD/CAM nesting software

The latest release of ProNest® 2023, Hypertherm's advanced CAD/CAM nesting software for automated cutting, includes full functionality for PlateSaver, a SureCut technology that combines the arc stability of X-Definition® plasma with specialised software parameters to dramatically increase material utilisation when cutting mild steel. It also contains enhancements to ProNest's Production Manager, a web-based module that integrates with Hypertherm's EDGE Connect® CNC to automatically capture real-time machine data.

Enhancements include the ability to edit production times for completed nests, set job status changes and search for nests by customer. There is also a feature that merges XPR set-ups. The software will now automatically detect and merge changes made to the process parameters or bevel spreadsheets into a new set-up during installation. Users can choose to use their existing customisations or the latest factory cut charts.

Changes to the user interface include a new welcome screen and customisable ribbon. Our software developers have really focused on reducing the waste inherent in most cutting operations. Features like PlateSaver do this by maximizing the number of parts on a plate so customers can buy fewer plates and waste less metal. Tools like our Production Manager module will help customers look at their operations more holistically, providing insight to help them work smarter, faster and more profitably," added the company.

ProNest CAD/CAM nesting software enables seamless integration with SureCut, technology that applies optimised parameters to simplify cutting and reduces the need for operator intervention.

## Lessons from COVID-19

Hypertherm adapted to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions and created a hybrid way of working (using digital technologies) that, it told ISMR, "suited us, our partners and our customers and made us all more efficient."

"We took decisions remotely, ran more webinars and offered more video and online training. We also demonstrated our machine technologies virtually and communicated more on digital platforms. We intend to continue with some of these practices as they worked for everyone. There is now a lot more flexibility for our Associates to work from home for part of the week," confirmed Marco Mostert.

“The great resignation also affected the skilled labour market and accelerated the whole process. The pandemic taught us how to harness technologies and eliminate the less rewarding aspects of fabrication (such as lower value-added tasks) to attract new generations into manufacturing. A hybrid workforce or even fully remote workforce is pretty transformational," continued Evan A. Smith.

One of Hypertherm's USPs, he insisted, is its Associate-owned structure.

"We are owned by our Associates. This makes us different, as we are a company of owners. Every decision we make is viewed through that lens. We have a 'no layoff' philosophy through ups and downs; we keep our talent assets onboard and that degree of commitment, focus and loyalty means that we have the energy, creative powers and talents of everyone in our organisation. We adopt the Lean philosophy of 'total integration' and train every Associate on Lean process through Lego exercises and have them fully critique their own workstreams. We make everyone a problem solver and process optimiser in the business to harness every ounce of energy," he continued.

“Our Associates are very invested in Hypertherm's success which means that they have a very tight connection and high degree of engagement with customers. We think this sets us apart in the long term," he concluded.
