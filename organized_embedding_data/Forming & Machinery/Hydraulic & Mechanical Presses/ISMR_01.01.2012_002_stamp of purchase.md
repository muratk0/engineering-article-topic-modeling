# STAMP OF PURCHASE

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

INTERLAKEN TECHNOLOGY of Chaska, Minnesota (USA) has been purchased by Water Works Manufacturing, LLC of Cambridge, Minnesota (USA). Interlaken Technologies will become a division of Water Works Manufacturing, LLC and remain at its current location.

Interlaken Technology manufactures production press equipment for hydroforming, elevated temperature forming, hot stamping systems as well as precision testing machines for use in metal forming, leak testing, impulse testing and burst testing. Interlaken systems are computer-controlled hydraulic press systems with data acquisition capabilities.

Water Works Manufacturing, LLC’s core business segments include contract manufacturing and aftermarket ATV accessories. Capabilities include a full range of sheet metal and tube fabrication services such as laser cutting, stamping, tube bending, manual and robotic welding, powder coating, hydroforming, machining and final package assembly.

“With the support of Water Works Manufacturing LLC, Interlaken will now have the financial strength and flexibility to fund future growth plans to our advanced forming customers in the areas of research and development, engineering and design, FEA simulation, prototyping, capital equipment and serial production of parts,” the company told ISMR. www.interlaken.com

## SAIC flexes its muscles

SAIC Motor Corporation Limited has entered American Fortune magazine’s 2012 list for the global Fortune 500 for the eighth time. Its sales incomes totalled US$ 67.2548 billion in its consolidated statement, ranking 130th place by climbing 21 places from the previous year.

2012 is the first year of SAIC Motor’s public launch. During January to June this year, SAIC Motor domestic automobiles sales showed a year-on-year growth range of 11.4%. Amongst total sales, passenger vehicles registered a year-on-year growth range of 11.6%, reaching a historical height. Roewe and MG, self-dependent brands, achieved year-on-year sales growth rates of 12.2%. Joint ventures still maintain a steady growth.

Between January and June, Shanghai Volkswagen achieved sales of 636,700 with year-on-year growth exceeding 10%; Shanghai General Motor registered a sales volume of 671,500 with year-on-year growth exceeding 9.71%. Shanghai Commercial Vehicle plates extended the marketing share with a year-on-year growth rate of 11%. During the first half of this year, self-dependent brands successfully launched the two latest models (the Roewe 950 ‘chief administrative model’ and MG 5 ‘striding British sedan’).

In 2013, after completing the second term of the Roewe production line project, the SAIC Motor technological centre will become a research and development centre for finished automobiles, featuring the ‘largest scale, completed facilities in China’.

Industrialising new energy is an important part of SAIC Motor’s innovations – in April 2012, its E50 pure ‘electromobile’ and ‘Shanghai-branded’ fuel cell vehicle were displayed at Hannover Messe. In the second half of this year, SAIC Motor will launch two PHEV and BEV cars designed to save fuel by 50% on average. ■

## BRAZIL AND CHINA IN FOCUS

BRAZIL IS now South America’s leading economic power, responsible for 50% of regional GDP, and its strong growth and high interest rates make it an attractive destination for foreign investors.

A new report on Brazil’s sheet metalworking sector, from Brooks Reports, provides a general overview of the Brazilian infrastructure and current economic situation, with a particular focus on sheet metal manufacturing and processing.

“Generally regarded as the most significant global economic success story of the past 30 years, China’s economy is now reckoned to be the second largest in the world. World Bank data shows that investment in buildings, roads, bridges, railways, airports, industrial equipment and machinery rose to 45.4% of GDP from 36.3% between 2002 and 2010,” said Brooks Market Intelligence Reports, a division of Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd.

A new report from the division provides extensive information on the Chinese economy, infrastructure and the manufacturing industry and offers advice on how to do business with China. www.brooksreports.com
