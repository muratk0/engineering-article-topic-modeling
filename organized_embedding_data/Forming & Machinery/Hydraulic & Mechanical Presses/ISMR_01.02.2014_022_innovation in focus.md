# Innovation in focus

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

BRUDERER UK will be celebrating a £500,000 deal unveiling a ‘world first’ at MACH 2014. The company will be supplying a high-performance automatic stamping press to electronic component specialist, Harwin. The press will help the company to increase efficiencies and attack new opportunities in aerospace, instrumentation and industrial markets.

The new press, the BSTA 280-75 with a BSV-75 high-speed servo feeder, represents a world first in that it integrates a planetary gearbox into the shaft of the main motor. This will allow Harwin’s engineers to have full control of tooling development to produce test runs of new products.

Education and training is now well established at MACH with tours of young people, conducted by apprentices from MTA member companies, a familiar site around the halls. The E&T Zone is the centre of their visit, with exhibits such as the AMRC’s MANTRA and the Bloodhound Supersonic Car confirmed to take their places.

New for 2014 will be the Regional Heat of the Worldskills competitions in CNC turning, CNC milling, industrial electronics and CAD/CAM.

Rösler UK, the UK branch of the leading mass finishing and shot blasting equipment manufacturer, will be tripling its exhibition space at MACH 2014.

“MACH 2014 was the obvious exhibition to attend for us, as it is the leading exhibition for production equipment in the UK. We find the exhibition is the perfect opportunity to meet many of our existing customers as well as giving us an ideal opportunity to talk to prospective customers who have a need for surface preparation and/or finishing,” explained Rösler UK general manager, Steve Lewis-Brammer.

FICEP (Stand 4730. Hall 4) will be demonstrating two of its latest, high productivity machines from its extensive range of CNC machines at MACH 2014. The Gemini HD is an advanced, multi-function and flexible system for profile cutting, drilling, bevelling, machining and scribing with plasma bevelling and material cutting capability. Over 150 machines have been installed worldwide and now the latest HD machine, which features a plasma bevelling head, joins the Gemini range.

FICEP UK was recently appointed exclusive agents for the ERNST range of deburring machines. The machines compensate for surface height variations of up to 6mm and abrasive brush rollers are available for edge-rounding to comply with fabrication standards EN1090-2.

Renishaw (Stand 5730,Hall 5), the global engineering specialists, will be giving a UK exhibition premiere to its new SPRINT™ high-speed contact scanning system for CNC machine tools at MACH 2014. The system incorporates a new generation of on-machine scanning technology enabling fast and accurate form and profile data capture from both prismatic and complex 3D components.

An expanding sign maker invests in more sheet metal working machinery

Strong business growth at Blaze Neon, one of the UK’s largest manufacturers of signs for retail shop-fronts, banks and offices, has prompted the installation of a second flatbed laser profiler and two new CNC press brakes at its Broadstairs factory over the past 18 months. All machines were supplied by Bystronic UK.

Established in 1981 and now employing over 300 staff at its factories in Broadstairs and Birmingham, which also incorporates Cygnia Maintenance, Blaze is a long-term user of equipment from Bystronic, having purchased a larger laser profiler at the end of the 1990s and a water jet cutting machine in 2007.

## Extra capacity and support

“We decided to opt for machines from this Swiss manufacturer as, 14 years ago, they were the only supplier of top quality sheet metalworking equipment prepared to equip a CO2 laser cutting centre with extraction, filtration and ducting to allow us to profile perspex safely,” commented Blaze managing director, Clive Knight.

“The machine proved reliable in operation which encouraged us to standardise on this supplier for practically all of our sheet metal processing plant. We think that the machines are well produced and the manufacturer keeps up to date with modern sheet metalworking technology, which allows us to stay competitive. The extra equipment means that we normally complete our production during day shifts, seven days per week, so expensive night shifts are avoided.”

The original Bystar 4020 laser cutting machine accepts 4-metre by 2-metre sheet whereas the latest machine, a Bysprint 3015, has a capacity of 3 metres by 1.5 metres. A second bed at the rear allows a new sheet to be set up while the previous one is in the machining area.
