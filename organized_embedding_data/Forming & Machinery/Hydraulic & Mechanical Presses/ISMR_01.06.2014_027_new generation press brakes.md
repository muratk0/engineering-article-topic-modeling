# New generation press brakes

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

LVD COMPANY nv has launched the next generation of its Easy-Form® and PPEB Series of high-performance press brakes featuring a new, modern design that is more energy-efficient, easier to use and incorporates added safety features. The new generation press brakes also feature advanced control technology, servo drives and scanning sensors, ensuring more responsive operation and higher productivity.

An optional LED lighting system illuminates the back gauge and front work zone areas to provide better operator visibility and increased worker safety. Status lighting built into the covers of the press brake ram offers a visual indication of the machine’s operational status, enabling more effective shop management for higher throughput.

“The on-board LVD Energy Reduction System (ERS) intelligently manages energy consumption to ensure lowest possible energy usage in all modes of operation. ERS technology reduces power consumption by up to 45 per cent over conventional models, making Easy-Form and PPEB press brakes the most energy-efficient in their class,” LVD told ISMR.

The latest generation Easy-Form and PPEB press brakes also employ LVD’s advanced Touch-B control system, a graphical icon-driven touch screen control. Easy-Form press brakes are also equipped with LVD’s patented Easy-Form® Laser in-process angle monitoring and correction technology, ensuring consistency from the first to the last part. See www.lvdgroup.com ■

## Green laser for marking

TECHNIFOR has expanded its laser offering by integrating a new technology: the TG400 green laser. With its 532nm wavelength, this new marking solution enables marking of materials that do not interact with other classic laser equipment (using near-infrared, 1064nm) such as Yag, YVO4, Fiber or CO2.

Exceptionally fine (30µm), the beam delivers strong marking quality, suitable for small characters and Data Matrix™ codes. The TG400 laser enables surface marking with reduced heat-affected zone, avoiding material damage or distortion. Driving the TG400 green laser, software specifically developed by Gravotech Group helps the implementation of identification and traceability in production processes. See www.technifor.com ■

BYSTRONIC has equipped its BySprint Fiber with a new 6 kilowatt fiber laser, enabling even faster cutting speeds. The new laser source is available for the BySprint Fiber 3015 and the 4020 format version of the machine. “The 6kW laser considerably increases the BySprint Fiber’s cutting speed in the thin-to-medium range of sheet thicknesses. For example, in 3mm thick stainless steel, by up to 70 per cent compared to the 4kW fiber laser. The speed advantage is even more pronounced compared to the 6kW CO2 laser. In 3mm thick stainless steel, the new fiber laser is three times faster. Depending upon the material and the sheet thickness, the part output can thus be increased by up to 300 per cent,” explained Bystronic. Bystronic has also equipped the 6kW version of the BySprint Fiber with its Cut Control as a standard function to monitor the cutting process. When a cutting tear occurs, Cut Control automatically stops the laser. Subsequently, the cut is repeated. This allows the user to reduce the risk of miscuts. See www.bystronic.com ■

## Repel welding spatter

HENKEL’S AERODAG CERAMISHIELD has been re-branded and is now under the company’s Loctite® brand. Now known as the Loctite® SF 7900 Ceramic Shield, this protective coating is formulated to actively repel welding spatter. The quality of the weld seam is also enhanced as the shroud remains spatter-free and the gas flow is not interrupted.

“This dry film is easy to apply, dries in seconds and adheres well to contact tips, shrouds, welding nozzles and jigs. It is sprayed directly onto degreased surfaces from an aerosol can, creating a ceramic coating that prevents adhesion of welding spatter for up to eight hours without the need for re-application,” explained the company.

“The use of Loctite® SF (silicone-free) 7900 Ceramic Shield minimises the downtime that is traditionally needed to remove spatter build-up. Users report productivity gains of up to 10 per cent and a reduction in the cost of consumables by up to 70 per cent. After the shift, a gentle tap on the welding torch removes minor spatter build-up and the equipment is then ready for a top-up coat. There’s no need for expensive and environmental hazardous chemicals to release the spatter.” See www.loctite.co.uk ■

## CAD/CAM and nesting

ESAB, a specialist in welding and cutting technologies, has released its Columbus® III version 1.2, powerful CAD/CAM programming and nesting software for plasma, oxy-fuel, laser and waterjet cutting. This version offers many new features and updates to make programming easier and more efficient, improve material utilisation, increase productivity and streamline workflow.

“The new release provides improved CAD functionality with a standard shape library that makes it easier and faster to create part programs using an extensive collection of pre-defined part geometries,” said the company. “It enables parts to be imported from an existing assembly without the need for customised import filters, providing more efficient data exchange from Columbus to ERP, better production control and easier integration. The integration into complex ERP systems is no longer a costly customising process.”

The new software improves remnant creation by automatically optimising the remnant cut geometry on the existing layout. This simplifies the creation of remnant plates while maximising material utilisation. Three new bridge configurations enable continuous cutting of external contours with a single pierce and provide better links for bridges from part to part. The resulting part geometries feature negative-shaped bridges, which simplify the removal of parts from the skeleton. The software also improves cut quality and cost when bevelling by optimising the cutting path, even for multi-pass plasma cuts. See www.esab.co.uk ■

## Stud welding in focus

WITH ITS patented radial-symmetric magnetic field stud welding, combined with the newly developed HZ-1 universal stud with flat end face and centring tip, Heinz Soyer GmbH has developed innovative stud welding technology. This pioneering technique was initially limited to studs with a diameter of 10mm or less. The company has now made the leap to 16mm stud diameters, providing new welding solutions. The new technique even works on surfaces of thick plate and thin sheet metal that are not perfectly clean. No ceramic rings as welding aids are required so there is no ceramic ring waste and disposal.
