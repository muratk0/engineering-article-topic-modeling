# MetalMaster 2.0

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

HIGH PERFORMANCE capability in a small space characterises the new MetalMaster 2.0, explained Messer Cutting Systems GmbH. The system combines the advantages of plasma and oxyfuel cutting for metal plates.

“Most machines take up a lot of space in the production hall. We have reengineered our classic MetalMaster as a compact, but no less productive, alternative and extended it with useful functions,” explained Dipl.-Ing. (FH) Christian Jung, CEO, Messer Cutting Systems Europe. “Approximately 30 square metres is all that it needs to process plates of up to 3.0 x 1.5m economically using plasma or oxyfuel technology. The machine is compact, easily installed and ready to use.”

The torches can move over the plate at up to 35 metres per minute with the machine's twin-sided, high- powered drives. High cutting speeds are possible depending upon the material and process. The SmartLifter SL100 torch lifter helps to reduce cycle times with its rapid reaction times, requiring half a second for the full vertical stroke. Using the Global Control 2.0 touch-screen control, the user makes three clicks from loading the part program to the first cut.

“Our engineers have also worked on savings with the plasma process, reducing times by as much as five seconds per start. To protect the torch against damage from tilted parts, we have integrated a magnetic torch holder as collision protection,” continued Christian Jung. “Fume extraction, integrated into the table, with a fan/filter system meets all environmental requirements to reduce dust and smoke. Speciallydesigned slag containers significantly simplify cleaning of the table.”
