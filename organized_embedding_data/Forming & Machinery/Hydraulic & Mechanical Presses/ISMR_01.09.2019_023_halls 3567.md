## YIH SHEN MACHINERY CO., LTD. (YSM) HALL 6 www.ysm.com.tw

YIH SHEN MACHINERY Co., LTD. (YSM) was set up in 1979 to manufacture machines for cutting, coiling and forming metal springs. Today, it is a global specialist in forming machinery, including stamping and bending lines.

At Blechexpo this year, the company will display its YSM "NCseries" NC-26T strip and wire forming machine (Servo) cam-less, servo-driven stamping and bending machine. It can reach speeds of up to 250 strokes/min. with slides that can be aligned in either radial or linear arrangements

"40% of YSM machines are for the automotive and motorcycle sectors, and a further 40% is for the miniature circuit breaker sector. The remainder is for other sectors. Using our machines to meet increasing demand from Asia or new high-end, eco-friendly, global automotive trends will help European producers to level up their production quality and efficiency," it added.
