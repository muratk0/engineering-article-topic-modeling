# Fladder Danmark A/S

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand G135

www.fladder.dk/en/

Fladder Danmark will introduce a new HYBRID extension for its GYRO machines providing cold, calibrating grinding and deburring. The FLADDER 300/ GYRO HYBRID includes the GYRO part for deburring/ edge rounding (central oscillating and rotating gearhead with six counter-rotating spindles, equipped with FLADDER abrasive cylinders). The HYBRID section for grinding is an extension unit with a long-belt grinding unit, working across the machine's feeding direction.

Grinding pressure is generated by an oscillating contact wheel which presses the grinding belt towards the surface of the workpiece. Critical heat accumulation is avoided, said the company, because of the swift and short contact time on the workpiece.

"There is no workpiece discoloration or deformation. Damage to the grinding belt is not repeated on the workpiece surface. The GYRO and HYBRID functions work together at any feed speed," it said.

## GIZELIS SA

Stand A26

www.gizelis.com

GIZELIS SA, a manufacturer specialising in press brakes and shears since 1968, will unveil the ROBOBEND system—a solution integrating a robot into the press brake's body to create a highly productive, hybrid robotic cell. It can be operated by an operator for small batches or run fully automatically with non-stop 24/7 production for larger batches.

"We will introduce the cutting-edge G-HYBRID series, combining the robustness and versatility of a hydraulic machine with the energy efficiency, eco-friendliness and noise reduction of an electric machine. We will also showcase the patented, unique and fully electric G-ELECTROCUT 3006 shear," it said.

Additionally, the manufacturer will present solutions for machine interconnection, remote accessibility and IoT compatibility at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover.
