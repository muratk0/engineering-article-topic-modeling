The MACC TA Series Saws are Dedicated to Cutting Aluminum and Non-Ferrous Materials. This series has an RPM of 3,000 and are offered from Entry-Level to Fully Automatic. Due to the Precision of the TA Series and the Speed of the Blade Utilizing Carbide-Tip Blades, they produce a Cut that is so Clean, Accurate, and Precise! These Saws also use a Teflon Jaws System, allowing for the least amount of Marring, Unmatched Positioning, Accuracy, and Efficiency.

TA 400 A (FULLY AUTO)

1 AUTO MATERIAL FEED:

Streamlines production by automatically supplying material to the cutting area. This system enhances operational efficiency by keeping consistent material flow, reducing manual handling, and allowing for continuous operation. It’s ideal for high-volume manufacturing and repeatability.

2 PNEUMATIC VICES:

Equipped with Teflon jaws provide enhanced grip and protection when handling delicate materials. These vices ensure a secure hold without marring the surface of the workpiece, extending the life of both the jaws.

3 BLADE SPEED:

Features a precise speed adjustment capability, reaching up to 3,000 RPM. This high-speed setting enhances cutting efficiency, allowing for quick processing of materials with clean, precise cuts.
