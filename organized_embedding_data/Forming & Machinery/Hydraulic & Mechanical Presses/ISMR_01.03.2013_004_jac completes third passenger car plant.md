# JAC completes third passenger car plant

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

ON 5 JANUARY 2013, JAC held its third passenger car plant completion ceremony, as well as the new SUV (S5) offline ceremony. JAC's passenger car plant reportedly has invested 2.18 billion yuan in total, with an annual capacity of 240,000 units of cars for double shifts. The S5 is a new SUV, appearing in the 2012 Auto Guangzhou exhibition with a new design and interior trim.

JAC's third passenger car plant features world-class equipment and technology. Its pressing workshop includes a newly built, internationally advanced, totally automated pressing line, with a 2,500-ton servo press for high efficiency and automation.

The welding workshop is operated by 166 automatic robots, making it the first international advanced high-speed flexible welding line among Chinese automobile enterprises. It also can perform multi-vehicle crossover manufacturing, according to market requirements.

The paint workshop applies the high-level waterborne B1B2 free intermediate coating process, which can reduce VOC (volatile organic compounds) emissions whilst preserving the coating's anti-UV and anti-chip properties. This is a feature of the environmentally-friendly auto painting process. JAC's third passenger car plant is the first Chinese automobile plant to use this process.

The assembly workshop uses the L-shaped layout, and the ground source heat pump air conditioning system guarantees processing quality by maintaining constant temperature and humidity levels in the assembly environment.
