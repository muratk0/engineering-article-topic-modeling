# 60 years in the spotlight

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

THE CELEBRATIONS for Millutensil’s 60th anniversary coincided this year with EMO, the international machine tool trade fair, which took place in Milan from 5-10 October, and with BlechExpo which will be held in Stuttgart from 3-6 November 2015.

In July 1955, Luigia Assi founded Millutensil srl in Milan, Italy. The company manufactures three lines of products - die and mould spotting presses (Blue Line); coil processing lines (Green Line) and conveyors/conveying systems (Yellow Line).

The Millutensil Think Green design concept for a compact feed line with 5,000kg decoiler and 800mm wide straightener/feeder unit, for material thicknesses from 0.5mm to 4mm and material strengths of up to 1,200 Nmm2. This was presented at EuroBLECH last year.

“Today, thanks to a corporate policy characterised by the constant innovation of production lines, setting value on employees as well as showing professionalism and reliability towards customers, Millutensil srl is one of the leading companies in this sector,” the company told ISMR.

Run by women, the company’s vice-presidents are Beatrice and Veronica Just who have been supporting their mother in the management of the company for several years now, consolidating relationships with international customers. At EMO, Millutensil displayed a limited edition of machines featuring a new and innovative design. See www.millutensil.com
