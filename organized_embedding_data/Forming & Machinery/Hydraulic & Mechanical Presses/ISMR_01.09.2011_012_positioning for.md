# POSITIONING FOR

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

ISMR interviews Brian Robinson, CEO of Wilson Tool International, and Colin Blackwell, Managing Director of Wilson Tool European Operations, to gauge the company’s growth plans, targets and outlook for the future.

Since 1966, Wilson Tool International has been a leading provider of tooling solutions that enhance the performance of sheet metal fabricating machinery worldwide. Wilson Tool is the world’s largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry.

Products are manufactured at the company’s world headquarters in White Bear Lake, Minn. (USA), Toronto (Canada) as well as its European facility in Swindon (UK) and its site in Shanghai (China) which serves a growing customer base in that region. Technical sales centres have also been opened in Denmark, South Africa, France, Germany, Argentina and Mexico.

ISMR: PLEASE OUTLINE WHAT YOU CONSIDER TO BE YOUR GREATEST ACHIEVEMENT OVER THE LAST COUPLE OF YEARS?

BR Our main success is our recovery from the global economic downturn. We fared extremely well through the recovery process in most of our main markets. I consider it a major success that we have come out back on top.

CB I think weathering the recession as strongly as we did was a great achievement. In Europe, during that time, we took on an extra 2500 sqm manufacturing building next door to our Swindon facility where we also incorporated a training centre. We ran a recent Open House with Radan there – 43 customers from 23 companies over two days of training and demonstrations in our technology centre.

ISMR: WHAT WAS YOUR STRATEGY FOR SURVIVING THE GLOBAL ECONOMIC DOWNTURN? WHAT ACTIONS DID YOU HAVE TO TAKE?

CB We took sensible precautions from a cost perspective and worked extremely hard to gain market share during that time. Our European workforce remained largely intact during that time – we made no redundancies in Europe.

BR We also continued to invest in our R&D department in new product and patent development. In 2009 for example, in the depths of the recession, we launched more products than any year in Wilson Tool history. This means that we are well positioned to take advantage of the economic recovery. We also acquired a company called Exacta in Canada during that period. So we expanded our footprint during the recessionary period and also went after additional market share.

ISMR: WHAT ARE YOUR VIEWS ON THE CURRENT BUSINESS CLIMATE? WHAT FEEDBACK ARE YOU GETTING FROM YOUR CUSTOMERS?

BR Our order input with our customers seems to be pretty steady. In the US, they are talking growth numbers of roughly 20 per cent which would coincide with our business in North America. We see it continuing to be fairly strong through the second half of the year although not as strong as the first half of the year – the first two quarters of 2011 were very strong for us. However, we still expect double-digit growth for the second half of the year.

CB It’s a similar picture in Europe. It looks as if we will finish the year around 15 per cent plus. We are noticing that countries in Europe are coming out of the downturn at different rates – for example, Germany has done very well but countries such as Spain and Italy are moving more slowly. Overall, we anticipate a growth rate of 15 per cent this year.

ISMR: WHAT WAS THE GROUP TURNOVER FOR 2010? WHAT ARE YOU PROJECTING FOR 2011?

We did over US $150 million in 2010 for the group overall – double-digit growth for 2010. We anticipate a 15 per cent increase for 2011, in line with our projections.
