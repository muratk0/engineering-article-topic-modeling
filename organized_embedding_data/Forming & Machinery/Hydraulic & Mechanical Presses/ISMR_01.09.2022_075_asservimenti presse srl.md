# Asservimenti Presse Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.asservimentipresse.it

“Market trends indicate the increasing use of wider metal strip and higher capacity decoilers. Our products are well suited to these requirements," commented Asservimenti Presse, an Italian coil processing manufacturer.

The company designs, develops and build coil-feeding lines, incorporating decoilers with capacities of up to 30 t (external diameter of 2,000mm, maximum width of straightening machines equal to 2,000mm for up to 14-15mm material thicknesses).

"With an eye on constant innovation, during the pandemic we tried to listen to our customers' needs and develop specific solutions [to the challenges they faced]. Our coil-feeding equipment guarantees material, time and energy savings, whilst offering high levels of flexibility and performance," it told ISMR.

"We offer material savings via our zig-zag feeding line; automatic coil-change equipment that minimises production downtime and electronic pilot release for energy savings. Our flexible feeding line for servo presses provides high flexibility and performance," it concluded.
