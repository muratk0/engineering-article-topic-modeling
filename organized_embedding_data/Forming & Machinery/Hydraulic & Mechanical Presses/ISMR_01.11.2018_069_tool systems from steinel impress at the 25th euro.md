# Tool systems from STEINEL impress at the 25th EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

FROM 23–26 OCTOBER, decision-makers from the sheet metal processing industry met up for the 25th time in Hanover. STEINEL, the premium manufacturer of punching tool components, had its own stand at the EuroBLECH 2018 trade fair.

This year, process safety and costeffectiveness were the chief topics at this leading trade fair. It should therefore come as no surprise that the two tapping units and the modular tool system from STEINEL met with great interest.

The absolute highlight at this year's trade fair stand was provided by two tapping units, the S-Former Z and the S-Former E. Roughly one third of visitors to the stand asked for information about these smart solutions, which enable ultra-high-quality threads to be cut directly in the punching and bending process. As a purely mechanical solution, the S-Former Z operates fully synchronously with the tool right across the press stroke, requiring no electrical installation. The electrically powered S-Former E is driven by its own AC Servodrive, and is monitored continuously by a PLC control unit, making it ideally suited to the defect-free production of safety-relevant parts.

As an intelligent module, the standardised modular system is unbeatably economical and it enables ‘flying’ tool changes to be performed within 20 minutes – ideal for production runs involving a large variety of parts and small quantities.

"We are proud that our products met with such a high level of interest at this, the world’s largest trade fair in the sheet metal processing sector", stated Christian Pape, Sales & Marketing Director at STEINEL. "The feedback received from this sector demonstrates that our systems provide appropriate answers to topical questions, and meet the need for flexible, safe and costeffective solutions".

In total, over the four days of this trade fair, around 56,000 trade visitors found their way to Hanover. As a result, the STEINEL staff manning the trade fair stand had their hands full at all times. No fewer than 160 new contacts were made, with around one third coming from other European countries and from countries outside Europe. In 2020, EuroBLECH will once again be a definite fixture in the STEINEL trade fair calendar.
