# Sheet metal and tube cutting

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

BLM GROUP, A specialist in the metal tube processing sector, showcased its LS5 (sheet metal cutting) and LC5 (tube cutting) systems at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany. Both systems offer material shuttling modules for loading and unloading and automated vertical storage lift systems for flat sheet and tube.

"Modular configurations, high range functionality and exceptional performance are key characteristics of the LS5. To maximise throughput and expand capability, an automatic tube processing line can be integrated to the LS5 system to create LC5, a powerful combined system for tube and sheet metal laser cutting. It is unlike any system on the market," BLM Group told ISMR.

"LS5 and LC5 systems are compact to reduce floorspace requirements and minimise installation times. They offer a wide-access work area (for ease of use and maintenance) and are designed to be modular and versatile, with adaptable configurations, interconnected, with the ability to connect to other systems and communicate to process management systems in an Industry 4.0 environment."

For sheet metal cutting, LS5 and LC5 are available in different versions, from 1500 x 1300mm up to 2000 x 6000mm with the option to have longitudinal or transversal pallet change systems. For all versions, a complete range of automation solutions are available, which includes systems for loading and unloading and/ or systems for pallet and towers storage. An effective material handling configuration is when the pallet storage is on top of the pallet changer.

The LC5 tube processing system is an automatic system that is able to cut tubes in diameters of up to 120mm. Starting from a bundle, single tubes are automatically loaded, processed and unloaded one after another as finished pieces. The tube processing module is autonomous from the sheet metal processing module, only sharing the laser cutting head. It uses an independent operational panel to control the entire facility during tube processing.

"LS5 and LC5 are offered with a fibre laser source of up to 8kW. The system is equipped with a Zoom cutting head which adjusts the focal length to the material type, optimising the cutting process and results. The fully automatic nozzle change system is a time-saving resource when high automation levels are required for processing different material types and thicknesses. The part program dictates when the material characteristics have changed, and the nozzle is automatically exchanged," added the Italian manufacturer.
