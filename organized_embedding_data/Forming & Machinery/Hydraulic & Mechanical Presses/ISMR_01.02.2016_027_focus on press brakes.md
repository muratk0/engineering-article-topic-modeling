# FOCUS ON PRESS BRAKES

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

The next generation of LVD’s Easy-Form® and PPEB Series of high performance press brakes feature a new, modern design that is more energy-efficient, easier to use and incorporates added safety features. The ToolCell, LVD’s press brake with an integrated tool-changing system, significantly reduces press brake set-up time.

LVD is also now offering a 220-ton, 3-metre model with a large selection of top and bottom tooling stored in a tooling stadium/warehouse under the back gauge. An innovative gripper design is built into the machine’s back gauge finger serves as the tool-changer mechanism. When a job is complete, the press brake automatically changes the top and bottom tooling as required for the next job. All tool stations are precisely positioned, eliminating the need for manual adjustments.

Mate Precision Tooling showcased various press brake tooling solutions at the FABTECH 2015 exhibition in Chicago in November 2015. These included CleanBend™ forming technology and “Bevel Tang Style” press brake tooling.

“The new ‘Bevel Tang Style’ press brake tooling is for use in newer Amada style press brakes. Enabling faster and safer tool changes, the press brake tooling features a punch tang geometry that is compatible with punch clamping systems requiring a bevelled receiving surface,” the manufacturer explained.

Mate’s press brake tooling also includes a full array of European Precision and Wila TRUMPF-style punches, dies, standard specials, specials and accessories, available in segmented, full-length and half-length sizes.

Italian manufacturer, Prima Power, launched its eP2040 200-ton electric press brake at Blechexpo in Stuttgart in November 2015.

“This new press brake features crowning on the lower beam and a bending angle control system (laser measurement system) that we developed ourselves. It is a rare example of an electric press brake that can cover higher tonnage,” Claudio Banchi, Prima Power’s Executive VP of sales, told ISMR.

The eP2040 is a rapid, non-hydraulic bending solution with a new servo-electric drive system and CNC crowing system. The pulley belt system, actuated by Prima Electro servo drives and motors, distributes the bending force over the whole bending length for better movement control and accuracy. The Prima-Power eP-Brake is based on a rigid O-frame. This guarantees tool alignment even under stress deformation, since there is no horizontal displacement. The position of the upper beam, in relation to the lower beam, is measured by dual Y1 and Y2 linear encoders that are fixed independently of the machine frame and are bed-referenced. This design isolates ram positioning accuracy from any deflection in the side frames under load and maintains accurate positioning even during offcentre bending operations.

“We are also promoting an upgrade on our electric Fastbend panel bender. It has been redesigned and features a new system that can control the C-frame from both sides and is more accurate, which is better for bending delicate parts (3.2mm max. thickness),” added Claudio Banchi.

PunchBend is Prima Power’s compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials, which processes pre-cut material automatically into ready-bent, high quality components.

With 80 years of experience, it supplies a comprehensive range of press brake tools as well as the patented Rolla-V.

One of the new products on display at SafanDarley’s booth at Blechexpo in Stuttgart (November 2015) was the E-Brake 40 Ton 1600 Ergonomic. The E-Brake 150 Ton 3100 and M-Shear 310-6 (with cutting capacity of 6mm with a hybrid drive requiring just 22 litres of oil) were also showcased on the booth. The shear was equipped with an optional Return to Sender system.

The company has expanded its E-Brake Ergonomic press brake series, a compact, portable, high speed electric press brake, with a 40-ton model that offers higher capacity and a longer working length of 1600mm. The Ergonomic 40 Tons has an open height of 590mm standard and a stroke of 300mm standard. The ergonomic series is designed for bending small parts. Rapid acceleration and deceleration of the electrical servo-driven ram delivers approach speeds of up to 180mm/sec. The machine has a very compact footprint.

“Our new variant for the ergonomic 40-ton 1600mm E-brake press brake is easier to operate and portable for factory installation,” explained Wim van Kranen, sales and marketing director for SafanDarley, to ISMR during Blechexpo. “We also are showing an upgraded version of our laser angle measurement device with a new blue light instead of a red light. We switched to blue light because it is less influenced by light from the environment and therefore ensures a better result. The new system also takes much faster measurements.”

SafanDarley also showcased the latest developments in its Autopol offline software. With its 2014 acquisition of Swedish software maker, FCC Software (inventor of Autopol offline software), the company has taken an important step towards continuing to be a major player in the precision press brake market. It opened a new support office in Baltimore, USA, which started operations in January 2016.

The issue of bending and improving efficiency downstream from the cutting process is high on Italian manufacturer Salvagnini’s agenda. Its B3 ATA press brake is its benchmark for efficient sheet metal bending.

“The B3 is the only solution of its kind on the market and is the product of proprietary expertise in panel bending and press brake bending, combining the best of both worlds. Employing an original solution that teams design innovations with the latest technologies on the market, this press brake combines the power of a hydraulic press brake with the responsiveness and accuracy of an electric press brake, whilst maintaining the consumption levels associated with a hybrid system,” it told ISMR.

The B3 press brake in its ATA version employs automatic bending tool set-up to make the bending process even more efficient. Other Salvagnini bending solutions comprise panel benders such as the P4 P2 and P1 automatic panel benders in a range of 14 models and the FMS S4+P4 automatic panel production line.

Salvagnini also showcased presenting SaM, its new proprietary app which provides realtime information on Salvagnini machine status on smartphone, tablet or PC using remote monitoring. By implementing the philosophy of Industry 4.0, this new tool allows the user to keep up-to-date with the status of the system and to receive messages and statistics on current production.

Schiavi arrived at Blechexpo in Stuttgart last November with a real innovation – its new press brake, the LineAr (model 130.30 with 3m length was showcased on its booth). This press brake model features the patented Schiavi “Crowning Free System” which guarantees a constant angle along the bend and horizontal edge along the line of bend (for a perfectly straight bend). LineAr has three beams for the lower table and three for the upper and the bending force is distributed on the external beams so that the tool holding beams remain straight during the bending process.

Another highlight of its booth presence was its new CNC control, Atlas, integrated with the Moses system, which project all the information that the operator needs directly onto the frame of the press brake. This system is touch-screen so that the user can work directly on the press brake frame instead of walking backwards and forwards to the CNC next to the press brake.

The press brake has been built with no welds; the entire structure is bolted together. Schiavi offers the press brakes in three sizes. Small press brakes, up to 400 tons, feature 3- 4m bending length. The height of the lower tooling from the ground is reduced. Mediumsized (up 600-ton) press brakes have a 4-6m bending length. The foundation for the lower table is not necessary. Large (up to 1200-ton) press brakes feature a 6-12m bending length. The foundation for the lower table is smaller than ‘traditional’ ones.

“The LineAr 130.30 model improves upon the current LineAr specifications and features tables with smaller dimensions and smaller thicknesses, increased daylight opening to easily manufacture large and complicated components and a working plane for the operator which is closer to the ground,” Niccolo Zinetti, the company’s marketing and communications manager, told ISMR.

“The LineAr 130.30 is also equipped with an inverter that speeds up the bending cycle. A specially designed hydraulic system, with Start and Stop function, allows LineAr 130.30 to significantly improve efficiency by using maximum bending force only when in contact with the material, thus saving energy costs for the customer. The CNC control can also set a time after which the press brake will stop consuming electricity, whilst keeping all available data in memory.”

Schiavi has also patented and produced an electric-pneumatic angle measurement system to reduce bending times and obstructions, cut out springback issues and lower operational costs. All Schiavi press brakes are equipped with its LASER BEAM safety device solution.

Fitted with smart sensors, MagicShoe is one of the revolutionary components of the new TruBend Series 5000. Operators can use it to move around and initiate strokes. An array of additional features makes TRUMPF’s TruBend Series 5000 more productive and user-friendly. Using a new control concept, the machines are easy to operate. Two innovative angle measurement systems are also offered. The operator is assisted by a range of ergonomic components such as the MagicShoe, a work shoe fitted with smart sensors. This makes it easy for the operator to move around and initiate the stroke wherever they are standing at the machine.

TRUMPF’s improved ACB Wireless needs no cable and is easy to use. Measurements are taken via two sensor disks integrated into the upper tool. TRUMPF offers two angle measuring systems that complement one another to provide precise bending results. The ACB Laser system measures the bending angle regardless of the tool employed and is ready to operate with no set-up needed.

“These high-productivity, all-purpose TRUMPF machines come equipped with a new On-Demand Servo Drive that features 4- cylinder drive technology. The Servo Drive is highly dynamic and extremely quiet in operation, and delivers significantly more productivity than a conventional drive. At the same time, it remains eco-friendly since it consumes energy only during the bending process,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

“The new Touchpoint TruBend control is revolutionary in its simplicity and intuitive to use. Machine operators will quickly know how to use the different views, which focus on the essentials while still providing all the familiar options. An extremely realistic 3D visualization incorporating collision monitoring is also available to assist the operator. The user interface is engineered with the demands of everyday production in mind. The machine can be operated without a mouse, using a 21.5 inch monitor which, like a tablet, is equipped with the multi-touch function, and can even be used wearing work gloves.

Operators are free to swivel the monitor into whatever position is convenient for them, and have quick access to direct help whenever they need it,” added TRUMPF.

To cut down on walking distances and increase productivity at the same time, operators can also use an additional mobile control unit, the new MobileControl Pro. Operators can position the unit anywhere along the press beam – that way it is always within reach and saves them numerous trips to the fixed control panel. A 3.5-inch colour display enables them to input all the directions they might need for the bending process while standing directly at the machine, including instructions to switch to the next/previous bending step or to correct the angle and axis positions.

TCB (Thickness Controlled Bending) reduces angle errors caused by variations in sheet thickness, while those looking for ultimate angle accuracy are advised by TRUMPF to go with the ACB (Automatically Controlled Bending) angle measuring systems. These systems measure the actual angle during the bending process together with the spring-back and guide the press beam to achieve the desired angle. Two ACB angle measuring systems are available for the TruBend Series 5000.

With its new TruBend Series 8000, TRUMPF is offering press brakes to handle especially big jobs. They process large-format and thick components with precise results. Both large open heights and bending lengths, along with press power of up to 1,000 tons, make the machines in the new TruBend Series 8000 true powerhouses. Featuring lengths of up to eight metres and bending depths of up to 500mm, these machines are predestined for machining larger parts.

“Errors and test bends can be avoided, resulting in a substantial increase in productivity,” said WILA. “The New Standard pneumatic tool holders are based upon a unique, patented, self-locking clamping mechanism. Tools are clamped using air pressure. The main advantage of this approach is that tools can be clamped and unclamped extremely rapidly. This system further increases the speed of tool changes and results in significant increases in productivity.”

WILA B.V. has been specialising in tool holders (clamping, crowning, bottom tool holders), tooling and accessories for more than 80 years now, with the goal of reducing the adjustment time of the press brake and to further increase accuracy.

## The tool produces no distortion or marking on sheet metal

Wilson Tool International, the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, recently introduced the company’s new, complete line of press brake tooling called LVD Style.

“Available to the North American market, LVD Style press brake tooling is made in the USA, which means that we can provide shorter lead-times and rapid delivery on this type of tooling to the North American market. Manufacturers can additionally benefit from fast issue resolution and quick tooling replacements by ordering directly from Wilson Tool’s Minnesota plant, located in White Bear Lake,” said the tooling manufacturer.

At Blechexpo in November 2015, the company also showcased its V-Series Black press brake tooling.

“The tool produces no distortion or marking on sheet metal. The press brake dies incorporate replaceable inserts that decrease friction and enable better performance. Unlike traditional V-dies that can warp and distort material, V-Series Black dies produce clean, crisp bends with no distortion, making them the best solution to produce small flanges, small bend radii and bends close to holes,” Klaus Neumann, Wilson Tool’s sales director for Europe, told ISMR.

The rotating inserts used in V-Series Black dies also enable fabricators to bend a wide range of materials using the same die, reducing set-up time and minimising the amount of tooling required. Additional benefits include tighter tolerances during manufacturing which result, said Neumann, in increased bend accuracy and shorter lead times. V-Series Black press brake dies from Wilson Tool are available for virtually all major styles of press brake tooling, including American, European and WT Style.

“Customers want to have tooling that has more functionality e.g. QR codes on the tooling and software changes. Automation is also key – we are working with a customer now on automated press brake tooling. A future trend will be intelligent tooling i.e. selecting the right tooling for the application. The smart tooling concept includes a printer in the tool – the tool is connected to a network via Bluetooth so that you can programme logos and serial numbers and print it from the tool. There needs to be a driven application, and possibly even a servo motor, in the tool. The trend will be ‘a tool within a tool’,” explained Neumann. ■
