# Stamping its authority

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

The new 'Stamping Practice' reference book is an excerpt from the reference book 'Stanztechnik' and is available in English for the first time.

In close cooperation between Prof. Dr.-Ing. Matthias Kolbe (the Swiss manufacturer of Bruderer automatic stamping presses) and the German specialist for optical technologies, Jenoptik, the English-language reference book is now available from Springer Vieweg Publishers.

“The German reference book, 'Stanztechnik' has been an essential tool for professionals in stamping technology for over 50 years. The new English-language textbook provides an overview of the basics of stamping technology, practical tips for the design of tools and explanations of the high-performance stamping presses," commented Bruderer.

The reference book's content includes:

- Stamping technology processes and terms.
- Materials for stamped parts.
- Basic principles of shear cutting.
- Criteria and design rules for highperformance dies.
- Monitoring the punching tool and the press.
- Principles, control of highperformance stamping presses.

The book's targeted audience includes trainees and students of mechanical engineering and automotive engineering as well as practitioners and designers of production and stamping technology. This edition is based on the “Stanztechnik" textbook in German in its 13th edition (ISBN 978-3-658-30400-3, Springer Vieweg Verlag Wiesbaden, Germany, 2020).

The book's author, Prof. Dr.-Ing. Matthias Kolbe, is head of the Forming and Stamping Technology and High-Speed Forming professorship at the University of Applied Sciences in Zwickau, Germany.

Bruderer provides high-precision stamping presses with press forces ranging from 180 to 2500kN for stamping and forming simple and complex parts. Its product range includes servo, roll and gripper feeds as well as peripheral devices and services covering control technology, revisions and training.
