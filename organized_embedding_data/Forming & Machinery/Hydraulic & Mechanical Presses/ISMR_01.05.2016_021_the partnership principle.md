# THE PARTNERSHIP PRINCIPLE

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

Erick Rousseau, of Jean Perrot Industries, is forging new partnerships with press brake customers.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes, achievements, awards and the reasons for this?

ER: With our new award-winning press brake, the Maneo Premium LCS, we can now access new markets and new customers. This press brake, with its laser control system, won the innovation award at the Tolexpo sheet metal machinery exhibition in Paris last November. We have also developed a new robotised bending cell for a major aeronautical subcontractor in western France. This equipment was successfully installed at the beginning of 2015 and we are now focusing on new opportunities in this market. Press brake technology has now developed to such a degree that, in terms of performance, we are obliged to supply machines incorporating the latest measurement and autocontrol systems to ensure repeatable accuracy at high speed.

We have also built and installed two highcapacity press brakes working in tandem as one 1300-tonne capacity machine with 14m bending length. This machine, equipped with our patented Laser Control System, has been in service since June last year producing parts mainly for the agricultural sector.

We have been working on equipment designed to reduce time and effort when changing tools. In particular, we designed Toolbox which enables operators to change tools effortlessly with reduced downtime. This innovation has also generated much interest with our clients and we have already equipped several of our Maneo press brakes with it. On average, users are able to change a full set of tools in less than 10 minutes against 40 minutes without the system. Tool changes are time-consuming but are also a heavy and demanding operation for operators. To reduce the risk of musculoskeletal disorders (MSD) caused by the manipulation of heavy tools, Toolbox is installed on the side of the machine enabling the transfer of tools (punches and dies) to and from the machine to the Toolbox. No manual lifting of heavy tools is necessary. The system is manually operated, making it a costeffective investment compared to other motorised solutions available in the market. Based on two tool changes per day, a full return on investment is obtainable within the first year.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal/manufacturing professionals?

ER: In reply to a more demanding market where complex high value parts are required, customers require more efficient and accurate bending machinery. To meet this need, we have developed our press brake LCS Laser Control System (angular precision of bends to +/- 20mins).

Today, the market is extremely competitive and, to attract new customers, we are obliged to keep ahead of our main competitors by introducing new technology innovation and services. Customers are looking for full turnkey bending solutions; this has become our primary objective. For over 60 years, Jean Perrot has been supplying single machines and we will, of course, continue to do so. However, our key objective for the coming years will be to supply full bending packages.

Increasingly, our customers are looking for customised machines and peripheral equipment to suit their particular production challenges. With a team of more than 40 design engineers and draftsmen, we are able to respond by custom designing the machine and the relevant specific tools. We are also able to support our end-users with on-site service assistance when required.

We are also aiming to produce and sell large capacity (XXL) press brakes and tandem sets, particularly to export markets. As a wellestablished engineering company, we are familiar with design, logistics, on site installations etc. The Maneo Premium machines are fully designed and manufactured in France. We also have a longstanding partnership with a leading overseas supplier for other Jean Perrot products. Our engineers are also responsible for the design, quality and CE conformity of these imported Jean Perrot products.

ISMR: Please give a brief background to Jean Perrot Industries and explain your mission and objectives?

ER: Jean Perrot is a 100% French manufacturer of sheet metal machinery and has been a leading player in the market for over 60 years. We have produced some 4000 machines worldwide. Being an integral division of the PEI (Pinette Emidecau Industries) group, we have strong R&D support. Since 1998, our fabrication has been certified to ISO 9001 and, as a result, we offer full traceability of all parts and components. Our key focus is quality.

We are located in Burgundy – known for good wine, good food and good press brakes! We also have a global presence in Germany, Japan, China and USA. There are 120 employees in the company, 90 of whom are fully qualified engineers.

Our aim is to meet the needs of customers by giving high quality service as well as new service products, including a newly proposed maintenance contract. We have a team of 12 mobile service engineers, all of whom are multilingual. We also want to establish a close relationship with our customers so that we can face new challenges together, which drives innovation.

We are building a strong team of motivated people. All projects are managed by a project manager, backed by a dedicated project team and an operational manager. We are recruiting sales and after sales engineers and will also be reinforcing our R&D staff.

We also aim to develop more overseas orders which now make up 30% of our sales. You can find us at this year’s Metallobrabotka exhibition in Moscow where we will be showcasing the capabilities of our large tandem press brakes and Maneo Premium machine.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in metal bending markets?

ER: More accurate machines will always be needed. A new trend I see is customers wanting just one supplier for turnkey solutions. We are also focusing our efforts on new software innovations, traceability and a quality loop system. We will be making new software announcements shortly.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

ER: The challenges for us are to consolidate the French market and to keep ahead of our competitors with respect to quality and precision. We will also reinforce further our service support to customers.
