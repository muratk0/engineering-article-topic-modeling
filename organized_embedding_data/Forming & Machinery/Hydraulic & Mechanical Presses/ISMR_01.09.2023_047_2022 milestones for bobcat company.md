# 2022 milestones for Bobcat Company

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

January 2022: Bobcat unveiled its new, all-electric Bobcat T7X compact track loader at CES 2022.

April 2022: Walk-behind mower line-up unveiled in three width options (36-, 48- and 52 in.).

May 2022: Plans were announced for a new assembly plant in Rogers, Minn. This investment adds an additional 225,000 sq. ft. in production capacity and enables the company to streamline efficiencies across its manufacturing footprint in the Midwest region.

August 2022: Bobcat Company announced an exclusive platinum package available on select machines.

New telehandlers were announced (TL723 and TL923). Electric T7X, E32e and E10e were showcased at Bobcat Central's flagship store in Stockton, California.

September 2022: Bobcat unveiled its most powerful loaders ever produced with the introduction of the T86 compact track loader and the S86 skid-steer loader. The Bobcat loaders offer three hydraulic flow options in one machine. Bobcat North America wrapped up its annual Community Service volunteer effort with more than 425 employees dedicating their time and talents to support 35 community projects.

July 2022: Bobcat broke new ground with the PBR (Professional Bull Riders) as it sponsored the Carolina Cowboys, one of eight teams in the landmark new PBR Team Series bull riding league.

A new, 110,000-sq.-ft. Bobcat Aftermarket Parts Distribution Centre was opened in Reno, Nevada.

October 2022: The company celebrated the completion of its Statesville, N.C., manufacturing facility expansion. The US$ 70-million investment makes the location the company's largest manufacturing facility in North America and will add hundreds more jobs in Iredell County over the next five years.

Bobcat expanded into the light compaction equipment industry with the introduction of nine machines. The full range of Bobcat light compaction products included rammers, forward plates compactors, reversible plates compactors and trench rollers.

The company unveiled its autonomous mower concept at Equip Exposition in Louisville, Ky (USA).

Bobcat also showcased the job site of the future at bauma 2022, a global construction machine trade fair in Munich. Electrification was in the spotlight with the unveiling of the battery-electric E19e compact excavator.

November 2022: Bobcat and its employees donated more than US$ 250,000 to non-profit organisations focused on education, community prosperity and health resources. Doosan Bobcat matched nearly half of total employee donations to further amplify employee generosity.
