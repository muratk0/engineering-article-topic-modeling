## TOA DR ENTERPRISE CO., LTD

www.slitterline.com.tw

TOA DR ENTERPRISE Co. Ltd. Increasingly specialises in the manufacture of metalslitting machines, lines and equipment. The Taiwanese manufacturer has also registered several patent rights for its product lines, as a result of continuous research and development. Its TDS-700 Ultra Precision slitting line transforms coils into electronic connectors (copper alloy 0.1mmT x 30 strip capability).

"One of the leading manufacturers of copper and bronze alloys in Asia, who is also our client, used various well-known coil processing systems from several companies. It was impressed with TOA DR's slitting line, telling us it brought higher efficiency, better quality and user-friendly control design. With multiple mechanical designs and accessories, the TOA DR slitting line has become our client's main slitting system," the company told ISMR.

"We aim to maximise production efficiency, create extra profit for clients and supply total solutions for most types of materials such as Bimetal; Titanium; Nickel alloy; Silicon Steel and nonferrous alloys across industries.'

## YUNG HUA MACHINE WORKS CO., LTD

YUNG HUA Machine Works has been producing slitting lines and cut-tolength lines since 1945 (over 360 lines for coil centres in Taiwan, China, South East Asia, the Middle East and Central America)

"We have built our reputation on expertise, quality and automation for the past 72 years. Machinery safety is a key focus for us - a machine approved to ISO 13849-1was delivered in 2018. A further machine with CEMarking will be delivered in 2019," it told ISMR.

"Our full product range caters for material thicknesses ranging from 0.1mm to 12mm, a maximum slitting speed of 200m/ min. and maximum cut-to-length speed of 300 cuts/minute. Our machines, with the quality and automation required to meet European safety requirements, will become the new choice for European clients, in addition to traditional machines.'
