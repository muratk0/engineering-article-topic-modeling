# Vacuum lifting technology

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

THE TERM 'batch size 1' frequently comes up during the production and handling of plate goods. Eurotech has developed several applications for picking up plate and sheet materials, ranging from high-bay stores with integrated vacuum commissioning systems to individually customised solutions.

"Many items are ordered in small quantities. Picking up these plates manually poses a threat to quality and health. On the one hand, more employees are required to carry the plates; on the other hand, it is almost impossible to stack polished finishes manually without causing damage. A vacuum picking tool that uses a suction system to transport the plates can lift them without the items shifting or slipping. Not only does this ensure consistent quality, it also reduces the physical strain on employees who were previously picking up the plates manually," explained Eurotech.

Plates are picked up, equipped with slave boards and packaged automatically. Only one person is required to operate the equipment. If the business uses a cantilever rack, one additional employee is needed to load the various removal stations alternating with pallets of different plate goods. With Eurotech modular construction systems, components can be flexibly adjusted to meet individual customer requirements and spare parts can be installed. See www.euro-tech-vacuum.de

## Expanded fibre laser cutting range

AMADA's LCG-AJ series now includes 2-, 3-, 4-, 6- and 9kW fibre laser oscillators

AMADA'S LATEST LINE-UP of LCG-AJ flatbed laser cutting machines are equipped with AMADA developed 2-, 3-, 4-, 6- and 9kW fibre laser oscillators. Manufacturing and testing takes place at AMADA's Fujinomiya factory in Japan.

"Offering high cutting speed, low running costs and the ability to cut copper, brass and titanium, the LCG-AJ sets a new benchmark for performance and price at this level of investment. New machines have been added to the LCG-AJ line-up to make it a comprehensive range. The LCG-AJ range has a lightweight Y-axis carriage with a low centre of gravity, providing a 30% weight reduction. Most LCG-AJ fibre lasers incorporate an automatic nozzle changer for uninterrupted processing and all are compatible with AMADA's Digital Support System (ADSS) which allows customers to analyse and compare machine performance, as well as allowing AMADA to provide proactive service support," the Japanese manufacturer told ISMR.

With a positioning speed of 170m/min., they are also low energy consumers thanks to AMADA's fibre laser technology, according to the manufacturer. AMADA's latest AMNC 3i control enables simple operation with its smartphone-type 21.5" screen, including on-screen nesting, program scheduling and one-touch set-up features.

The LCG-3015AJ machines are fully compatible with AMADA's latest part picking system, the TK L. This enables high-speed removal and stacking of finished components, increasing productivity and minimising downtime between the cutting process and the next production step. All LCG-AJ machines can be equipped with load\unload systems for full productivity.

A FEW MONTHS after successfully launching its Webshop, WILA has launched the Tool Advisor; an engineering tool that assists customers in selecting the press brake tools which are best suited for their specific bending needs.

First introduced at the recent EuroBLECH and FabTECH exhibitions, the Tool Advisor is now available at the WILA Webshop. If you already have a Webshop account, you can immediately start using the Tool Advisor.

"To generate the best possible advice, the Tool Advisor allows you to enter additional data, such as the model and specifications of the press brake, the WILA tools that you already have and the type and thickness of the material to be formed. You can also create a two dimensional sketch of the sheet part," explained WILA.

"The Tool Advisor first checks whether tools in the existing WILA New Standard Premium range are suitable for the bending issue. In most cases, a variety of standard tools will be suggested and these possible tools can then be sorted by such factors as price level, maximum load, bending freedom and working height. If the required tool is not available as a standard solution, it is possible to immediately design a special tool and order it via our Webshop. Price and delivery time for all special tools are immediately given online."

After registration, the Tool Advisor is available via: https://webshop.wila.nl/tool-advisor/
