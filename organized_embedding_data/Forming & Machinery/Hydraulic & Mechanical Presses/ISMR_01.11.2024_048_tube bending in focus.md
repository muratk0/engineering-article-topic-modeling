# Tube bending in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Unison Ltd, a UK-based specialist in precision all-electric tube manipulation, will be presenting the capabilities of its range of all-electric and hybrid-electric tube bending machines at Southern Manufacturing & Electronics, along with technologies from the wider Unison family. The exhibition will be held from 4-6 February 2025 in Farnborough (UK).

Unison offers all-electric benders for tube diameters from 4-275mm.

"Available in single-stack, multi-stack, left/ right, pinball and twin-head versions, these machines are designed to provide rapid, automatic set-up, fast tooling changes and right-first-time repeatability, all supported by exceptional power and rigid mechanical design. Our hybrid-electric, dual-stack machines include 50mm and 80mm maximum tubediameter hybrid models," explained the tube bending specialist.

## Manually operated, CNC-controlled tube bending

On show and operational on Stand D215 at Southern Manufacturing & Electronics will be one of Unison's EvBend 1000 manually operated, CNC-controlled tube bending machines. Designed for low volume, precision production and prototyping, Unison EvBend models are used across aerospace and MRO, Formula 1 and the oil & gas industry for three-axis mandrel, multiplane bending.

“In addition to the EvBend 1000, which is designed for bending tube of up to 16mm in diameter (22mm in copper), a larger machine, the EvBend 2000, will also be displayed. Built to bend tube of up to 50mm in diameter, its bending function is servo-assisted. Both EvBend machines feature a 15-inch, PC-based touchscreen controlled by a CNC that can process up to 100 bends per component, storing infinite parts and connecting to both CAD and most tubemeasuring systems," outlined Unison Ltd.

## Laser cutting and wire manipulation

Visitors to Stand D215 will also be able to discover more about the machine tools available from Unison's sister companies, Nukon Lasers UK and wire-bending machinerymaker, Pneuform Machines Ltd.

Nukon Lasers UK recently launched a new high-performance machine range designed to make its laser cutting accessible to more UK subcontractors and in-house manufacturers (see more details below).

Unison Ltd acquired Pneuform, a manufacturer of wire forming and small-bore tube bending machines, in autumn 2023. As standard, all Pneuform machines are equipped with Yaskawa drives and Unison's software for easy programming and operation.

"We believe that our EvBend range of manually operated, CNC-controlled tube bending machines will be of particular interest to visitors from the aerospace sector," said Unison Ltd's joint managing director, Alan Pickering.“The latest advances to our Unibend CNC, tube bending simulation software, tube scanning solutions and tube bending application app will ensure that we have a great deal to talk about with existing and new customers."
