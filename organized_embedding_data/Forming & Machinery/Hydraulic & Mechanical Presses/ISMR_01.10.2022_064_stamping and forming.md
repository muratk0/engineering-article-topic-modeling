# Stamping and forming

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Bihler will introduce its new LM 2000-KT and LM 2000-NC linear machines at the EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition in Hanover, Germany (Hall 27, stand E104). These two machine platforms offer an array of new manufacturing dimensions using highly standardised, linear tool technology.

The LM 2000-KT and LM 2000-NC are the latest developments in Bihler's portfolio of stamping and forming machines. These are linear machine tools that use cams or servo units in the production of stamped and formed parts from strip material. The LM 2000-KT is designed for use in medium- and large-size batches, while the LM 2000-NC is for small- and medium-sized batches.

"Where classic mechanical stamping and forming machines are limited to the use of only individual tools, the LM 2000-KT and the LM 2000-NC are fully tool-compatible with standard linear tools—such as LEANTOOL Linear—or with parts from LEANTOOL Linear, or with the user's linear tool standard," outlined Bihler.

"This standardisation capability will benefit manufacturers who, for example, are producing medium-sized quantities of a job on the GRM-NC, but who now have to deliver higher volumes," explained managing director, Mathias Bihler. "In accordance with the 'plug & produce' concept, the manufacturer can simply transfer their stamping and forming tool, one-to-one, to the LM 2000-KT, and then immediately continue production."

Standard interfaces, along with quick tool-clamping capabilities on the LM 2000-KT and the LM 2000-NC, are designed for rapid and safe set-up, with 100% reproducible results.

The LM 2000-KT and the LM 2000-NC are designed to offer a wide range of applications and capability in stamping, forming and coining, while using "the simplest and most essential" basic machine configuration. The integral design of the compact machine body contains the infeed module; the press module; three forming modules (cam- or servo-controlled) and two central mandrels.

The machine body also contains the electrical hardware for the machine, the drive elements and the VC 1 control system. If needed, the LM 2000-KT and the LM 2000-NC can also be expanded to add up to five forming modules or to add welding, thread-forming and screw-joining processes.

“For added value, corresponding process modules can be seamlessly integrated into the basic machines and into the production sequence. A total machining length of 2,000mm is available on the machine plate, enabling fast set-up and rapid changeover," added Mathias Bihler.

New LM 2000-KT linear machine for the production of stamped and formed parts made from strip material in medium and large batch sizes (image: Bihler).

Meusburger standard cutting frames, types SBH and SBP, can be used in the standardised press installation space. Sensors that are integrated into all machine and processing components offer the possibility of predictive maintenance. All machining units are connected to a circulating oil lubrication system, and the use of direct drives offers more energy-efficient parts production.
