# XLC leveller

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Automatic Feed Company (AFCO) has received an order for an 'eXtra-large' capacity (XLC) leveller from a large southern Ontario service centre and coil processor serving automotive markets. Capable of handling coil widths of 2150mm (84"), the leveller will be integrated with customer-owned equipment in the summer of 2023.

Designed to accommodate a variety of material strengths and thicknesses using different cassettes, this XLC leveller will include two cassettes, each with different work roll diameters. The AFCO XLC can process a broad range of materials including surface-critical steel and aluminium, HSS, AHSS and UHSS.

“This system comes with an auto dualcassette change table, which holds up to two cassettes for change-out in less than four minutes. An independent cassette-style pinch roll is located at the exit end of the leveller. The pinch roll unit consists of two full-width chromed rolls in a modular assembly for rapid and easy cassette replacement," said AFCO.

"The leveller housing has a 140-ton capacity for work roll penetration of material at the leveller entry and exit. It is designed for a line running at a maximum speed of up to 90mpm. Our leveller calculator is also included, assisting operators in making the necessary leveller setting changes based on coil correction needed, helping to speed set-up and improved quality," it added.

The project scope also includes project management; installation supervision; customer plant start-up (commissioning); launch/production support and operator and maintenance training. This is the third XLC leveller order that Automatic Feed has received from this company.

Based in Napoleon, Ohio (USA), AFCO is a designer and manufacturer of coil-processing systems.
