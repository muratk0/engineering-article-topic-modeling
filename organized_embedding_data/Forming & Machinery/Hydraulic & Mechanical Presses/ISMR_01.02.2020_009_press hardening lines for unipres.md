# Press hardening lines for Unipres

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

UNIPRES SUPPLIES many of the world's major car manufacturers with car body parts made of pressed sheet metal. The global group has now decided to invest in two complete press hardening lines from AP&T. The first of the two lines will begin operating at Unipres's UK manufacturing centre in Sunderland at the start of 2020. The second line will be delivered to the group's French plant in Biache Saint Vaast in March 2020.

"We are naturally delighted to be entrusted with producing complete solutions that meet Unipres's high demands for short cycle times and extensive automation. Both lines feature advanced technology. For example, all the tooling and gripper changes are fully automated. The lines are also equipped with sophisticated press monitoring equipment, which ensures a consistently high level of quality for the manufactured parts," explained AP&T's technical sales manager, Peter Robisch.

AP&T's business model – One Responsible Partner® (which entails AP&T taking full responsibility for all production equipment and providing all required expertise in-house) also played an important role in the procurement. Another important aspect was AP&T's global operations, which allow the company to offer service and spare parts supply where customers operate. To date, AP&T has delivered and installed over 120 press hardening lines globally.
