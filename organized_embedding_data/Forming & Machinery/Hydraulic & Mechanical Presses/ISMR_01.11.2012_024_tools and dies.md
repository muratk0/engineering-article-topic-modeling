## Thick steel cutting

INCONSISTENT MATERIAL composition and surface quality can compromise the efficiency of laser cutting. It is also a particular characteristic of thick, low-carbon steel which can reach considerable temperatures near the point of cut.

This excessive heat can impede the cutting process, especially where filigree shapes are involved. If the kerf overheats, it can even set off a selfburning reaction where the material left in the cut burns and causes slag to stick to the rear surface of the sheet.

The new CoolLine option from TRUMPF helps prevent this from happening. It is a cooling device that keeps temperatures constant by spraying a fine water mist around the point of cut. The evaporation of just 30ml of water per minute provides one kilowatt of cooling; a highly effective measure.

The incorporation of CoolLine brings a wealth of production benefits. Tighter parts nesting and narrower skeletons result in better material utilisation and minimal wastage; a test run allowed 31% more parts, causing 14% less scrap as TRUMPF explained. CoolLine can be retrofitted to a variety of TRUMPF TruLaser models. www.trumpf.com

## Mini swivel bending

AT THE EuroBLECH 2012 trade fair in Hanover, German company Blech-Tec presented a revised model of its BT-150 mini swivel bending machine which can bend even delicate sheet metal components.

“This compact bending machine makes it possible to fabricate very small bent components easily and quickly – from single parts to pilot series to series in the low thousands. No special tools are required,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

The mini swivel bending machine is suitable for bending small parts such as springs, covers, contacts, brackets or housings for tool making, the electronics industry, thin sheet metal processing and many other applications. Benefits include high precision and repeat accuracy, so that even the most delicate components can be produced very quickly. Stop accuracy is 0.01mm and angular accuracy is 0.1 degrees, says Blech-Tec. Sheet metal with a thicknesses of 0.05 to 1.0mm can be processed.

“Operation of the CNC-controlled machine was kept simple, so that no specialised preliminary knowledge is required. The internal programme memory stores up to 100 programmes with 25 process steps each. Unlimited memory expansion is possible via a USB interface,” added the company.
