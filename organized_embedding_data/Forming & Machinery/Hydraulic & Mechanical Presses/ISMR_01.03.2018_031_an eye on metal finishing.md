# An eye on metal finishing

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

VISITORS TO STAND H20-740 at MACH 2018 will experience the world of metal finishing on offer from Ellesco. In addition to tapping into the knowledge available from Ellesco's team, visitors will also be able to see a wide range of equipment from some of the world's largest manufacturers of deburring, grinding and polishing systems.

Netherlands-based Timesavers, the 'world's largest manufacturer of deburring and flat surface finishing systems', will be represented by Ellesco. There will be four Timesavers machines on show at MACH 2018, ranging from the entry-level 10 series manual grinder through to the new 32 series 1100 WRB machine equipped with a combination of abrasive belt and rotary brushes for automated deburring.

The 10 series machine features a moveable/tiltable head onto which a grinding disc and flap brush are fitted. Rotating the head through 180 degrees allows both aggregate materials to be easily accessed. Larger still is the new Timesavers 22 Series metal finishing and deburring machine. The display machine has been updated to the new 'house style' of design to tie in with the Red Dot design award-winning 42 series machines. This new style creates cleaner lines to the machine and improved man/machine interface, including visual inspection capability for the abrasive medium. Available in three widths (900mm, 1100mm and 1300mm) the 22 Series can also be equipped with between one and three heads, combining contact roller style abrasive and rotary brush heads for metal finishing or deburring. It can deburr punched and laser cut metals as well as edge rounding and processing coated parts. The largest machine on show will be the Timesavers 32 series for deburring, edge rounding, edge deburring, smoothing and/or finishing flat metal parts from stainless steel, mild steel and aluminium.

For 3D components inside and out, Ellesco will continue the deburring focus by featuring the capabilities of WMS Engineering, a specialist in automated and robotic deburring systems. At MACH, WMS will be on the Ellesco stand highlighting the processes implemented and high-quality surface finishes, including sample parts, controls, robots and tooling that go to make up an automated deburring cell.

Ellesco will also exhibit machines that are suitable for weld dressing, re-finishing and polishing in the form of a KBM long-belt grinding machine for weld dressing and an Autopulit CNC polishing cell, which will highlight the level of surface finish that can be achieved using automated systems.
