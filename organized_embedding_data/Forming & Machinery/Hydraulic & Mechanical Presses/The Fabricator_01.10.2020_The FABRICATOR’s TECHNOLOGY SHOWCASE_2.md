Seamless Corners

Typically, fabricating a four-sided sheet metal pan is a time-consuming process requiring multiple bends on the press brake, followed by secondary welding and grinding operations to finish the corners. The corner forming tool from Wilson Tool makes it possible to bend and finish corners in the press brake in one handling. The Corner Former saves time by eliminating secondary operations while producing a higher quality part with a smoother finish, without the need for additional capital purchases.
