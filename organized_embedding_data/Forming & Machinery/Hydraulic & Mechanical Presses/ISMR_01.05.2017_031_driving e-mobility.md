# Driving e-mobility

[TARİH: 01.05.2017 ISMR]

E-MOBILITY IS ON everyone's lips. The range and performance of vehicles therefore play an important role. Vehicle range depends mainly on battery power and the efficiency of the motors. The electric motor consists, among other things, of the rotor and the stator, which are made up of many individual plates.

Agathon offers various solutions for new challenges in the rotor-stator tooling field. Guiding systems are available from stock or manufactured according to customer requirements. Stroke frequencies of up to 2500 strokes/minute are available, depending upon the guiding systems. Roller guides can be used for maximum stiffness and precision of the guides. This enables very narrow cutting gaps of a few µm.

Agathon guide elements are designed for simple installation and removal of the guide elements for maintenance without loss of accuracy and are significantly involved in increasing the maintenance intervals for strong reductions in maintenance costs. With the standard selected bearing steel 100Cr6 (1.3505), conditions are created to further increase the lifespan of the guide elements and reduce production costs.

"Through optimised production processes, the performance of the motors (and therefore the power consumption of the drive motors) can be reduced by up to 30%, compared to conventional production. Optimisation options include the reduction of sheet thickness, resulting in narrower cutting gaps. This requires significantly stiffer guides with high precision. Due to reduced sheet thickness, significantly higher numbers of strokes (lifespan) and stroke frequencies are necessary to maintain or even reduce the production time," explained Agathon.

"Tool maintenance gains in importance. Maintaining the cutting sharpness of active elements is fundamental to prevent the formation of burrs on the workpiece and to prevent power losses on the end-product. More frequent maintenance requires optimal guide elements, which will have no loss in accuracy during repeated installation and removal." See www.agathon.ch
