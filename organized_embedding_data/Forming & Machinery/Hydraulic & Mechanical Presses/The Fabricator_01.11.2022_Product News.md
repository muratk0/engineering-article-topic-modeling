Motorized seam orientation stand works on tube to 3.500-in. dia.

T&H Lemont has shipped a new, motorized seam orientation stand (SOS), model 280/6, which is designed to work on tube from 0.750- to 3.500-in. diameter and 0.028- to 0.250-in. wall thickness.

SOS units compensate for drift in the weld seam location. Using two vertical rolls that are adjusted by the operator, the SOS aligns the seam at a specified location on the circumference, usually top dead center, to align the weld seam with inductors used for inline annealing and sensors used for nondestructive testing. It also assists in maintaining the tube’s straightness and in complying with any customer requests for a specific seam location.

thlemont.com

Access can be controlled when sharing is no longer needed.

Servo-electric press brake offers CNC 4-axis backgauge

Kaast Machine Tools Inc. has introduced the HPB-E servoelectric CNC press brake. It features a CNC 4-axis (X, R, Z1, Z2) backgauge system run exclusively by AC servomotor.

Crowning is no longer necessary because of the consistent pressure across the beam. The beam downward movement is performed with the aid of belts, and its return is performed with springs, keeping the noise level to 63 dBs.

The machine offers quick setups and consistent bend angles, especially for air forming, the bending method preferred for these machines. With the linear bearings and the sliding front support arms that move right and left on the rail, the sheet can be controlled by the operator from the front.

Also, the length of the parts can be measured with the ruler mounted on the front support arms. The sheet can be fixed from the front side with the aid of the flip stop mounted inside the T slot.

Vacuums, dust extractors clean filter while unit is closed

Guardair Corp. has introduced the PulseAir line of vacuums and dust extractors. Powered by compressed air, these units incorporate a proprietary, button-activated system engineered to clean the vacuum filter while the unit remains closed.

On demand, a burst of compressed air is injected into the interior of the filter mounted underneath the vacuum lid. Dust particles clogging the outer filter surface are dislodged and settle within the closed container.

Coupled with HEPA filtration, the units enable end users to meet OSHA Silica Dust Regulation 29 CFR 1926.1153, the company reports.

The vacuum is suitable for general industrial housekeeping applications with high dust content, while the dust extractor is designed for source capture.

Coolant maintenance equipment reduces need to dump, refill fluid

Eriez SumpDoc coolant maintenance equipment combines both sump cleaning and tramp oil removal in one package. According to the company, the unit keeps coolant clean longer, decreasing the need to dump and refill fluid.

The machine serves as an alternative to a central fluid recycling system. It supplies complete coolant restoration and rejuvenation treatment of the metalworking fluids in the machine tool sump and requires minimal operator interface, says the manufacturer. The portable unit can be wheeled next to a machine tool, parts washer, or rinse tank to provide fluid reclamation.

Main components include a sump cleaner for removal of 50-μm and larger chips and solids, pleated bag filter and housing for removal of solids down to 10 μm, high-speed centrifuge for removal of emulsified tramp oil and fines down to 3 to 5 μm, an ozone generator for treatment of coolants, and electrical controls with a touchscreen dashboard.

Contactless power supply added to transport system

Beckhoff has developed No Cable Technology (NCT), an extension of its eXtended Transport System (XTS). NCT provides contactless power supply and synchronous real-time data communication directly to XTS movers. This enables individual movers to serve as mobile handling and processing stations, essentially turning the transport system into a flexible multirobot system.

The new technology adds a motor module and electronics that mount on the movers. The hardware required for the transmission technology is fully integrated into the motor module, and no additional connections or supply lines are needed.

This technology makes it possible to check the quality of products on the mover while the process is running. The TwinCAT control system enable easy connection of sensors and actuators. The data communication is real-time capable and can synchronize systemwide events.

GMAW guns offered with Eurostyle back-end connection

ESAB has updated its ST-21 and ST-16 GMAW guns with a Euro-style back-end connection that makes them compatible with the manufacturer’s feeders and other brands. They feature color-coded, quick connect/disconnect water lines and internal connections for shielding gas so they can be ready to weld in less than a minute, the company states.

Both guns have a 100% duty cycle at 600 amps and feature a design that can withstand the abusive environments of shipyards, mines, railcars, trailers, and construction and heavy fabrication sites. They are used for pulsed GMAW with 1/16-in. aluminum wires, tubular wires up to 7/64 in., and high-amperage spray and pulsed spray transfer applications.

The ST-16 EU has a slightly curved handle, a 55-degree gooseneck conductor tube, and a 10-ft. cable. The ST-21 EU has a pistol-grip/inline-style handle and comes with a 12-ft. cable.
