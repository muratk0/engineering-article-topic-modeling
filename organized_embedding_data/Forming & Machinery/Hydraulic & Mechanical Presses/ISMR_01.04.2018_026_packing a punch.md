# Packing a punch

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

MATE HAS ANNOUNCED immediate availability of its new QCT Metric™ A & B station punch drivers. These long stem punches are also known as Mate Original Style or Amada Style.

"Designed for easier, faster set-ups, these new A & B station punch drivers use our QCT™ punch retention system. It's simple and intuitive. QCT Metric A & B station punch drivers simplify tooling inventory. These universal drivers can be used on rounds or shapes," the manufacturer told SMR.

QCT punch inserts are made from Mate's M4PM™ long-lasting steel. They are keyed at the perimeter for improved angularity control and have a 0.770-inch (19,56 SBR grind life calculation) for added grind life. When punching, the shoulder of the punch remains securely guided. It comes standard with Mate's proprietary next-generation SuperMax™ coating for extended life. The Ultra QCT Metric A & B station design is fully compatible with:

- QCT Metric punch drivers
- Mate's Ultra® family of guides and standard canisters
- Mate's Original Style guides and Rapidset™ canisters.
- Mate's Original Style guides and spring packs.
- Fully interchangeable with Amada NCT, NEX and Z-Tooling systems as well as Wilson thick turret metric punches.
