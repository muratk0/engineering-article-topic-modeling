Dot peen markers don’t require PC or control box

Kwik Mark Inc. offers X series dot peen markers. The cantilevered marking head provides an open line of sight, while the marking head can retract to clear other tooling and equipment. Up to 6 axes are motion control-ready, and no PC or control box is required.

This dot peen marker features a twin rigid upright column leadscrew and ball slide design, brushless servomotors, noncontact limit home sensors, and a large T-slot table for fixturing. The X-12 model has a 12- by 6-in. marking area, while the X-24 model has a 24- by 6-in. marking area; both have a 12-in. height capacity. They also are available with a programmable Z axis.

System automates press brake tool changes

MC Machinery Systems offers press brakes equipped with its Automatic Tool Changer (ATC). The ATC includes 107.3 or 170.6 ft. of tooling storage. It can vary from high-runner V dies to specialized tooling, such as hemming or offset tools.

The tool changer automatically changes tooling, leaving the operator free to perform other tasks. Up to 2-in. V-die opening and 19.7-in. tooling lengths can be picked and placed because of the single, large-capacity tool manipulator. The manipulator is equipped with a die bed cleaner that runs automatically during tool changes.

Programs can be created offline using the DiamondBend bending software, at the controller, or imported from other MOS-controlled press brakes. The controller software chooses tooling sections based on inventory and availability.

Machines equipped with the ATC are not limited to using tools from the tool changer’s storage. The operator can use the machine as a stand-alone press brake, as well as an automated system.
