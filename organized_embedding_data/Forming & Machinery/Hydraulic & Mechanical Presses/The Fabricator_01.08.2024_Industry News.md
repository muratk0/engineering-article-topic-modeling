Feintool expands in Tennessee

Feintool continues to expand to meet customer demand for next-generation hybrid-capable transmissions. The $35 million, 47,000-sq.-ft. expansion is a 25% increase in space at the company’s Nashville, Tenn., facility, making room for a 1,600-ton direct servo transfer press that will go into service in Q1 2025.

"The expansion will create up to 50 new jobs to help meet demand for automotive transmissions, hubs, clutch baskets, and gear spiders," said Dan Swiger, vice president and general manager, Feintool Tennessee.

Additional presses and capabilities include two new 880-ton fineblanking presses, CNC lathes, laser welding, parts washers, and other secondary equipment.

Mack Iron Works announces Ohio expansion

The Mack Iron Works Co. has announced plans for a $300,000 expansion of its fabrication plant in Sandusky, Ohio. The project, slated for completion in December 2025, is expected to add three employees to the current 29-person staff.

The company’s capabilities include welding, burning and cutting, forming, turning, grinding, oxygen cleaning, and engineering.

Braner USA acquired by Holleway Capital Partners

Braner USA Inc., a designer and producer of flat-rolled coil processing and automated coil handling systems based in Schiller Park, Ill., has been acquired by private equity firm Holleway Capital Partners.

Braner’s systems process carbon steel, brass, copper, aluminum, stainless steel, and electrical steel.

In addition to Holleway, the investor group included Aldine Capital Partners, NewSpring Mezzanine Capital, and Braner CEO Doug Matsunaga.

"To set [Braner] up for continued success, we set out to find the right partner to provide the resources to grow our business while remaining independent," Matsunaga explained.

Process Service Specialists to build $3.5 million facility in Louisiana

Industrial mechanical contractor Process Service Specialists will invest $3.5 million over the next three years to establish a manufacturing facility at the Port of Lake Charles in Louisiana, creating 160 direct jobs.

The 11-acre fabrication shop will provide commercial and industrial steel, piping, and services to companies in the semiconductor, solar panel, petrochemical, and energy industries. Operations are expected to ramp up over the next two years.

CCAI FEF awards 2024 student scholarships

The Chemical Coaters Association International Finishing Education Foundation (CCAI FEF) has announced this year’s scholarship recipients. Ace Metal Crafts acquires Genesis Automation

Ace Metal Crafts, a manufacturer of sanitary stainless steel components with two locations in Bensenville, Ill., has announced its acquisition of Genesis Automation Inc.

H&S Industrial celebrates 75 years in business

Custom metal fabricator H&S Industrial, Manheim, Pa., is marking its 75th anniversary. Founded in 1949 as a small welding company, the company offers fabricating, process and utility piping fabrication and installation, rigging, cutting, forming, blasting, and painting services.

H&S Industrial specializes in customized and largescale industrial metal fabrication, from single applications to volume orders, for the food and beverage, pharmaceutical, transport and infrastructure, and other manufacturing sectors.

Pat Mooney Saws marks 75 years in business

Addison, Ill.-based Pat Mooney Saws is celebrating its 75th anniversary. Founded in 1949 by Patrick J. Mooney, the company introduced the first European-style miter band saws, as well as high-speed thin kerf carbide saws, to the U.S. and was one of the first companies to offer high-speed steel cold saw blades.

Today, the family-owned business is operated by the third- and fourth-generation lineage of Pat Mooney. Its 60,000-sq.-ft. facility houses service engineering and technical offices; a circular saw sharpening and service center; a training and demonstration room with powered saws; a warehouse for metal sawing machines, saw blades, and spare parts; a band saw blade welding center; and a fabrication and assembly shop for material handling equipment.

Heater Specialists acquires ParFab Industries

Tulsa, Okla.-based Heater Specialists LLC, a single-source manufacturer of refinery and petrochemical equipment, has acquired the fabrication business of ParFab Industries, a heater fabrication company in Inola, Okla.

Adding ParFab’s 170,000-sq.-ft. plant, HSI will own about 500,000 sq. ft. of indoor fabrication space serving companies in the power generation, petrochemical, refining, renewable, syngas, and energy industries.

HSI has offered continued employment to all ParFab Industries production employees in Inola. Upon completion of the acquisition, ParFab will operate its business under the HSI name. ParFab Field Services will continue operating as a stand-alone and separate company under its existing ownership.

Bobcat breaks ground for Mexico manufacturing facility

Worksite equipment manufacturer Bobcat Co. has broken ground for a $300 million manufacturing plant in Salinas Victoria, Mexico. The 700,000-sq.-ft. facility is expected to be operational in 2026.

The new location will create additional production capacity and manufacturing capabilities for select compact track and skid-steer loader models. The company’s compact loaders are also produced in the U.S. and the Czech Republic, where production will remain following the new facility’s opening.

The factory, which will seek LEED Silver certification, is expected to create 600 to 800 jobs.

Paradigm Manufacturing announces expansion

Paradigm Manufacturing, a provider of precision sheet metal fabrication, CNC machining, and cleanroom assembly, has announced plans to expand its headquarters in Hutto, Texas.

The $10 million project, which is scheduled for completion in April 2025, will add 60,000 sq. ft. to the existing facility, creating 50 to 75 jobs.

TMCO expands contract manufacturing capabilities in Nebraska

TMCO Inc. has leased 54,000 sq. ft. of industrial space in Lincoln, Neb., to expand its contract metal manufacturing capabilities. The company employs more than 230 people at its existing facility.

The new space will feature a 14-by 60-ft. powder coat paint booth and other associated equipment to accommodate large-scale welding, assembly, and powder coat paint projects.

Elite Tool, Swafford-Kientzy announce expansions in Missouri

Elite Tool and Swafford-Kientzy Machine & Fabrication (SKMF) have announced that they both will expand in Lincoln County, Mo., investing a combined total of more than $30.25 million and creating 36 jobs.

Contract manufacturer Elite Tool is planning a $30 million expansion with a new 120,000-sq.-ft. facility in Moscow Mills. The project is expected to create 32 jobs. SKMF, a provider of machining and custom metal fabrication services, will expand in Troy and increase its workforce by 25%.

Laurel Machine & Foundry expanding operations

Laurel Machine & Foundry is expanding its manufacturing services with the construction of a 20,000-sq.-ft. machining facility and a 7,500-sq.-ft. warehouse in Laurel, Miss.

The $3.1 million corporate investment, which also includes the purchase and installation of new equipment, will create at least seven jobs. Completion is expected within one year, with plans to fill the new jobs within three years.

The company offers a variety of industrial and manufacturing services, including CNC and manual machining, plate and structural fabrication, metal processing, and iron casting.

Alliance Fabrication and Manufacturing adds folding machine

Gary, Ind.-based Alliance Fabrication and Manufacturing has installed a Megabend updown folding machine from RAS Systems.

The machine performs updown bending on thick and thin materials and handles large, labor-intensive parts. It achieves straight bends in all material types with long or short flanges, whether bending in the center of the machine or on the side.

The folding system joins Alliance’s existing equipment for automated robotic welding, manual welding, metal forming, laser cutting, kitting, mechanical assembly, and stretcher leveling.

Carry-On Trailer to expand in Virginia

Carry-On Trailer, a manufacturer of steel and utility trailers, will invest $9.2 million to increase capacity at its facility in Westmoreland County, Va., and upgrade to a powder coat paint system. The expansion will create 60 jobs.

The company offers utility, cargo, aluminum, dump, equipment, and specialty trailers, along with a full replacement parts program. Based in Lavonia, Ga., Carry-On Trailer has seven manufacturing facilities throughout the U.S.

AEM expands campus, adds automated bending cell

American Engineering & Metalworking is adding a TRUMPF Flex Cell to its North Canton, Ohio, facility. The automated cell, designed to bend smaller laser-cut blanks quickly and autonomously, gives the company more capacity to produce complete fabrications and simple cut and bent parts.

AEM also recently added a TRUMPF 7020 panel bender for high-speed autonomous bending of large cut blanks less than 1/8 in. thick. The company currently is constructing a 24,000-sq.-ft. additional building on its 7-acre campus.

Hexagon enters strategic partnership with Paperless Parts in North America

Hexagon’s Manufacturing Intelligence division and Paperless Parts have announced a strategic partnership designed to accelerate the adoption of modern quoting software by custom part manufacturers.

Together, the companies will help job shops optimize processes across their operations, from quoting to downstream manufacturing and inspection, using software that features geometric interrogations, automated pricing and costing logic, AI, and advanced workflow technology.

The Paperless Parts software also will soon be available to Hexagon customers on the Nexus Digital Reality Platform, an open cloudbased platform designed to connect technologies from both Hexagon and its partner ecosystem.
