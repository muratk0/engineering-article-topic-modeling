# AIDA-America at FABTECH Mexico 2024

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA), demonstrated its press technology on booth 504 at FABTECH Mexico 2024.

Two AIDA presses were demonstrated at this year's FABTECH Mexico event, which took place from 7-9 May 2024 at Cintermex in Monterrey, Mexico. The first, an AIDA single-point mechanical press, is part of AIDA's gap-frame press line. These NC1 models range from 35-250 tons. A few of their standard features include:

- Rapid acting hydraulic overload protection (HOLP) at 10m/s.
- A highly rigid frame with low angular deflection.
- Tight clearances which minimize reverse tonnage.

The second press on display was AIDA's A Series single point, direct-drive, gap frame servo press. It showcased AIDA's exclusive Allen-Bradley control with standard features including user-friendly, swing-arm mounted, 12-inch colour touchscreen HMI (human-machine interface). It also included optical run buttons for ease of use; an Allen-Bradley servo-motion controller on ControlLogix® platform and 150-job recipe storage.

AIDA gap frame servo press with Allen-Bradley control.

Integrated tonnage monitor (peak only); step-feed (CNC handwheel) slide motion control for reduced set-up time and the display of actual and virtual crank angles are other features of the press.

Additional AIDA DSF-C1-A Series highlights at the show were:

- A next-generation servo motor with 5-10% higher speeds than AIDA's previous-generation gap frame servo presses.
- Capacitor-based Energy Conservation and Optimisation (ECO) system that reduces peak energy draw.
- Electronic Over Load Protection (EOLP) system that monitors applied torque throughout the complete stroke to prevent overloading.

This DSF-C1-A press blanked parts at the show with AIDA's optimised servo silent blanking stroke motion profile, which can only be achieved on a servo press. AIDA's DSF-C1-A Series product line is offered in a range of tonnages from 80 to 250.
