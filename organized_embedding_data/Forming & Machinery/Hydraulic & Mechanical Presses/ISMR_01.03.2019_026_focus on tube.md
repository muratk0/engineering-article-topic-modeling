The CNC 220 E TB MR gigantic electric multiple-radius tube bending system

A gigantic fully electric multiple-radius tube bending machine, featuring transport boost technology, was showcased recently at FABTECH 2018 in Atlanta (USA) and delivered to an automotive customer in the U.S. at the end of last year. The CNC 220 E-TB MR, manufactured by Schwarze-Robitec, can process complex tube geometries with diameters of up to eight inches. The CNC 220 E-TB MR processes tube lengths of up to 13 feet. A vertical height-adjustable pressure die moves to the required bending level in each individual case. This allows the requisite bending forces to be gauged to consistently apply the correct contact pressure to the tubes and pipes to be bent.

For particularly challenging components, Schwarze-Robitec combines the benefits of two different machines in the CNC 80/60 E TB MR. The Cologne-based manufacturer of tube bending machines has successfully commissioned two of these fully electric multiple-radius tube bending machines, featuring transport boost technology, at automotive supplier Eberspächer's site in Portugal. As an intermediate size, it features the bending head transmission and the bending capacity of a CNC 80. In addition, its vertical multiple-radius construction matches that of a more compact CNC 60 – as Eberspächer processes tubes with diameters of no more than 50 millimetres (1.97 inches). This enables the company to produce its exhaust technology components.

At the Offshore South East Asia (OSEA) in November 2018 in Singapore, Schwarze-Robitec presented its portfolio of tube bending machines for the offshore industry, focused on machines from its Heavy Duty series. The tube cold bending machines are specially designed for continuous use under major strain. High-strength materials and very large, thick- and thin-walled tubes can be formed in various bending radii.

With its 't forming' machines, German manufacturer Transfluid makes it possible to form tubes with an axial and rolling forming process. For instance, it is possible to perform accurate symmetrical and asymmetrical axial forming, with a variety of very specific materials.

"Our rotary forming machine can reduce or form the tubes partially or over a long distance. In both cases, the process guarantees outstanding precision. After all, we are working with tubes with an external diameter of between 1.5 and 8mm," said Stefanie Flaeper, sales and marketing director at Transfluid.

These fine tubes are used in endoscopy, emergency medicine and more. They also play an important role in hospital equipment, care equipment and sterilisation processes. As a very special connection technique is required in this case, it may be necessary to use lasers for the cutting during the forming of the tubes. To achieve this, Transfluid has completely re-engineered its 't form UMR' forming machine.

Tracto-Technik PBS is a leading specialist in the pipe bending sector. Its product range currently covers all-electric and electrohydraulic CNC pipe bending machines, semi-automatic pipe bending machines, machines for pipe end processing and hydraulic joining technology, pipe measuring systems as well as special solutions and software solutions for pipe processing.

Pipe Bending Systems (PBS) is aimed primarily for pipe processers with singlepart or small batch production and large product variety. The company's TUBOMAT has been fundamentally revised and modularly designed. This pipe bender (up to 48.3mm pipe OD) also optionally integrates the sawing, deburring, flaring and cuttingring assembly function. Various technical improvements have been made to all working functions, such as the tooling concept, handling and control techniques, workflow and energy consumption. The modern control concept provides program operation as well as a "4-fold bending angle pre-selection mode" and also enables bending data import from TT's own PIPEFAB software solution.

For pipe pre-fabrication, TT also offers its semi-automatic TUBOBEND pipe bender series and the fully automatic TUBOTRON CNC pipe bender series. The modular TUBOBEND machine prototypes are optionally available as right-hand or left-hand benders. The exposed bending head, which allows for large bending space, means that they can bend highly complex pipe shapes. The TUBOTRON 25 MR, an all-electric CNC pipe bending machine with multi-radii capability, bends pipes with an outside diameter of up to 25mm.

Two all-electric tube bending machines from Unison Ltd. are helping Ultibend Industries to increase the manufacturing productivity of stainless steel tube fittings for food and beverage processing systems at its manufacturing facility in Wellington, New Zealand. The machines automate the bending of tube fitting shapes, such as elbows and U-bends, in sizes from 1/2 to 4 inches in diameter.

Together, these benders are involved in making all Ultibend Industries bends of ½ inch to 4-inch diameters. Since installation, its 130mm single stack tube bender has produced 320,912 bends and the 80mm single stack tube bending machine bender that works on the smaller sizes has made 558,441 bends. Bends from these tube benders go to projects around New Zealand, Australia and the U.S., especially dairy factories, breweries and, in some cases, to the automotive industry.
