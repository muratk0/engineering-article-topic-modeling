MACC NON-FERROUS CUTTING SAWS

The MACC TA Series is specifically designed for cutting aluminum and non-ferrous materials. With a high blade speed of 3,000 RPM and models ranging from entry-level to fully automatic, this series delivers clean, accurate, and repeatable cuts using carbide-tipped blades. Each saw is equipped with a Teflon jaw system to minimize material marring while ensuring exceptional positioning accuracy and operational efficiency.

TA 400 A (FULLY AUTO)

Streamlines production by automatically feeding material into the cutting area. This system maintains consistent material flow, reduces manual handling, and supports uninterrupted cycles, making it ideal for high-volume manufacturing and repeatable cutting operations.

2 PNEUMATIC VICES

Equipped with Teflon-coated jaws, these vises provide enhanced grip and protection when handling delicate or finished materials. They ensure a secure hold without marring the surface of the workpiece, helping extend the lifespan of both the jaws and the material.
