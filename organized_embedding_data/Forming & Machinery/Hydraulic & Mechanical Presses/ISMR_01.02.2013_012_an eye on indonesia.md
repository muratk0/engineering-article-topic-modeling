# An eye on Indonesia

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

SOME of the largest metalworking machine tools ever imported into Indonesia were on the Baykal stand at Manufacturing and Machine Tool Indonesia from 5 – 8 December 2012.

“With 2-unit APHS, 4116 x 400-ton tandem dual CNC hydraulic press brakes, an APHS 4116 x 440-ton single-unit CNC hydraulic press brake and MGH 3100 x 16mm hydraulic swing-beam shears, this is the largest set of machines ever exhibited at the show and reflect the enormous changes being seen in Indonesia’s metalworking and engineering industries,” Baykal explained.

Ordered by PT. Burangkeng Maju Tehnik (BMT), a major Indonesian steel fabricating company, these machines will form the heart of its production line producing heavy equipment parts for the mining industry.

Founded in the early 1950s, Baykal is a Turkish manufacturer and global supplier of sheet metal working machinery. It specialises in the production of press brakes, shears, notchers, punching machines, laser cutting systems, plasma cutting machines, waterjet cutting and vertical machining centres.

With three factories and a combined production area of 60,000 square metres, Baykal has one of Europe’s largest facilities for the production of sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.
