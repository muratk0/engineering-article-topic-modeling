# Die spotting presses

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

SPC DIE SPOTTING PRESSES, from Taiwanese press specialist, DEES, are precision tools for the die-maker at the final stage of moulding which requires a high precision hydraulic press to further increase production efficiency and quality.

“Our machines are manufactured under strict quality control procedures to ensure accuracy and reliability,” said the manufacturer. “SPC die spotting presses can also relieve the operator from possible tension and fatigue, ensuring the safety of die press operations. With easy-to-use controls, the equipment also offers an optional 180° tilting slide to provide more accessibility,” explained Dees Hydraulic Industrial Co., Ltd.

The company’s main products include deep drawing presses, die spotting presses, press brakes and hydraulic presses. See www.deesgroup.com.tw
