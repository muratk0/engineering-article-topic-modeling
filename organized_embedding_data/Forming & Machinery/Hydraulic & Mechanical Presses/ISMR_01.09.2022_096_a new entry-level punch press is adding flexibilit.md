# A new entry-level punch press is adding flexibility and new efficiencies to the production of power distribution units (PDUs) at Olson Electronics, as well as unlocking new markets.

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“A TruPunch 1000 packs a punch in PDU production at Olson Electronics in the UK.'

Olson Electronics, a UK specialist in the design and manufacture of specialised power distribution units (PDUs), has invested in a new TruPunch 1000 entry-level CNC punching machine to replace manual manufacturing methods. The move is bringing new levels of flexibility and efficiency to this ambitious and highly competitive business.

Founded in 1961, family-run Olson Electronics is based at the company's ISO9001-accredited facility in Stanmore, London (UK). By adopting an in-house design and manufacturing strategy, Olson Electronics develops components, development methodologies and quality testing procedures.

Its power distribution units (PDUs) are fitted with multiple outputs designed to distribute electric power (especially to racks of computers and networking equipment in a data centre.) Many of them are rack-mounted for use in data centres and other IT applications. Desk-mounted PDUs are also popular i.e. for schools, colleges and laboratories.

Another important industry for the company is healthcare, in particular mobile trolleys for hospitals that require an electrical supply. PDUs manufactured by Olson Electronics are available to a variety of customers. Household consumers can even acquire them from Amazon or from Olson Electronics' website, with products shipped worldwide.

## Flexibility in focus

Olson Electronics' PDUs are supplied to various industries and large organisations worldwide. Alongside over thirty different standard catalogue product ranges and over 1,800 standard products, including intelligent PDUs with monitoring and switching capabilities, many of the products manufactured at Olson Electronics are custom-made. This provides important market differentiation and opens doors to new business. Olson Electronics has the design and manufacturing capability to produce custom solutions for individual requirements.

"We have a wealth of knowledge and experience in the manufacturing industry and have a fully equipped ISO 9001:2015 manufacturing assembly, which includes a wide range of metal fabrication machinery and our own powder-coating facility. Therefore, we have complete control over our entire manufacturing process which also enables us to offer our manufacturing services," explained the company.

Its production team can provide services including powder coating, subcontract manufacturing and a range of sheet metal fabrication solutions. These range from one-off custom designs or prototypes to full production batch runs. Its fabrication team offers services such as cutting/guillotine; bending/folding to 2m lengths; punching; spot welding and stud insertion. It also has a wide range of metal fabrication machinery including a 4-axis automated press brake.

## Packing a punch

Until recently, Olson Electronics relied on manual equipment such as power presses, shears and guillotines to produce the enclosures for its PDUs. However, the arrival of Mark Rebeiro as production manager sixteen months ago signalled a shift in philosophy.

"I had a CNC punching machine at my previous employer and knew the advantages it could bring," Mark Rebeiro explained. "While there was no problem with quality regarding our previous methods of manufacturing enclosures, it was all about gaining efficiencies and being more flexible. The previous process was quite rigid: if the equipment had a 50mm square tool, we got a 50mm square cut-out - that was it. By contrast, investing in a CNC punching machine would allow us to produce any size of cut-out using just one tool."

Olson Electronics shortlisted two CNC punching machines, but the company already had a TRUMPF TruBend 3066 CNC press brake that arrived a few years previously to replace manual sheet-metal bending, a factor that was set to influence the purchase decision.

“It made sense to invest in the TruPunch 1000, as well as TRUMPF tooling and software, to provide brand familiarity across the business," said Mark Rebeiro. "We now have everything that we need from one supplier."

Installed in April 2022, the TruPunch 1000 at Olson Electronics is predominantly processing galvanised steel or zintec from 0.9 to 1.5mm thick, sometimes up to 2mm thick.

"The compact entry-level TruPunch 1000 is extremely flexible, cost-effective and quick, processing sheet of up to 6.4mm thick at up to 600 strokes per minute. Importantly, Delta Drive dynamics eliminate the need for the sheet and support table to move in the Y axis, while full automation is possible in line with business development," commented TRUMPF.

## Flexibility in focus

Installed in April 2022, the TruPunch 1000 at Olson Electronics is predominantly processing galvanised steel or zintec from 0.9 to 1.5mm thick, sometimes up to 2mm thick. Batch sizes extend from one-offs up to thousands.

All enclosures are programmed using TRUMPF TruTops Boost software. The company creates models in 3D modelling software and exports them into TruTops Boost for automated disassembly. By activating the 'Boost' button, Olson Electronics can take advantage of a quicker process from geometry to NC program, while the software also allows more efficient material use via a shared order pool and 'Lean Nest' nesting processor.

“It's early days in terms of cycle time and process cost reductions. However, these are things that we can now measure as we get actual run times from the TruPunch 1000 for evaluation," explained Mark Rebeiro.

“For us, the investment is more about flexibility, particularly with the global supply chain issues that the entire manufacturing industry is currently witnessing. If a component we use regularly is unavailable, we can select an alternative safe in the knowledge that the TruPunch 1000 can produce different cut-out sizes in our enclosures without any need for new tooling. Previously, the components we specified were 'set in stone' because of the tooling we had available."

## Unlocking new markets

"We offer the largest range of punching tools on the market. Among the innovations is our MultiTool, which allows users to perform punching, forming and embossing processes highly productively. Up to ten different inserts in one tool adapter ensure shorter set-up and tool-change times," TRUMPF told ISMR.

LDU and Olson EV new product ranges have also been launched successfully by the addition of this new punch press. Clearly, the transition from manual equipment to CNC punching technology at Olson Electronics is yielding many benefits.

"Business is currently good; we're seeing steady growth year-on-year and increasing our headcount in certain areas," concluded Mark Rebeiro at Olson Electronics. "In addition, our new TruPunch 1000 has opened up other markets that we are now looking to penetrate."
