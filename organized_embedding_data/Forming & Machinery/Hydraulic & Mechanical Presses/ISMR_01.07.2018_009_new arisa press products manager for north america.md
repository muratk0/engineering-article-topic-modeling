# New Arisa press products manager for North America

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

NIDEC MINSTER SALES and project management veteran, Jim Schulte, has accepted the position of North American Arisa press product manager for the Nidec Press & Automation group.

Schulte has more than 20 years' experience in metal forming capital equipment. This includes positions in applications, project management and sales. He holds a BSc from Miami University and an MBA from Wright State University.

According to Schulte, the Arisa press products position is designed to provide local support for the global Nidec Arisa brand and was created due to strong Arisa product growth in North America.

Based in Spain, Arisa designs and manufactures some of the world's largest servo and mechanical presses. Arisa's engineering staff specialises in customised systems with transfer and material handling solutions, along with controls and software. The Nidec Press & Automation brand brings together Minster, Arisa, Kyori and Vamco products to the market. See www.minster.com

## Management changes at TRUMPF UK

A PERSONNEL CHANGE is taking place in the top-level management at TRUMPF UK. As of 1 July 2018, Annette Doyle left her post as managing director of TRUMPF UK to continue her career at TRUMPF U.S. in Farmington, CT. During the year 2016/2017, TRUMPF UK recorded an order intake of £57.1 million, an increase of 50 per cent on the previous fiscal year. The management team is now Lee Moakes as TRUMPF UK's new managing director, Gerry Jones as its new sales director and Juergen Herr becomes its new finance director.

"I am very confident handing TRUMPF UK over to a management team with a combined 60 years of significant TRUMPF experience in three countries. All three have an outstanding reputation and track record within TRUMPF, in their relevant fields and the entire industry. I am very much looking forward to witnessing the further positive development of TRUMPF UK under the leadership of Lee Moakes, with the support of Gerry and Juergen," Annette Doyle told ISMR.
