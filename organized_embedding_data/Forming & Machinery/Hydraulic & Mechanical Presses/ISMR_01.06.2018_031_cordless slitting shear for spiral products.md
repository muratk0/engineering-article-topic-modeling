# Cordless slitting shear for spiral products

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

TRUMPF HAS DEVELOPED its new slitting shear, TruTool C 200, with innovative 18 V lithium-ion rechargeable battery technology specifically for machining spiral pipes. The high-performance power tool is also suitable for cutting sheets of up to two millimetres in thickness. "Equipped with a 2 Ah rechargeable battery, it demonstrates its strengths, particularly when cutting spiral ducts, as it cuts seams with a thickness of up to 3.6mm in mild steel (400 N/mm2). So far this performance has only been achieved by the larger TruTool C 250 with an 18 V rechargeable battery and chip clipper," TRUMPF told ISMR.

Crucial for this is the steel cutting head. TRUMPF also uses corrugated cut guides, even in the basic version which, in combination with the durable knife, can cut even double metal sheets. The special geometry also ensures comfortable self-feed, which allows high working speeds of up to 10 m/min.

"Quick and easy retraction of the slitting shears from the metal sheet is equally important for machining spiral pipes. We achieve this with an open cutting head. The C 200 can even do without a chip clipper. In addition, the user has a good view of the marking, which enables accurate setting of the slitting shear," added TRUMPF.

For high application versatility of the new slitting shear, the tool has a rechargeable 18V battery and is compatible with a range of different cut guides and suitable cutters that are available in the respective accessory sets. In addition to the standard Spiro set, there are five other sets that focus on cutting quality, speed, easy turns or material compatibility respectively. See www.trumpf.com
