# Triple action presses

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

TAIWANESE PRESS SPECIALIST, SPC Dees Hydraulic, provides a full line of large capacity triple action presses to help customers drastically cut production costs. Established in 1976, the company is an experienced press manufacturer in Taiwan. It supplies hydraulic press machinery with easy-to-operate functionality and a range of professional support services for customers.

“The inner/outer slide can work individually or be inter-locked to achieve higher capacity or working force,” explained DEES. “We provide machines such as triple action presses which integrate a platform to the location of the presses to provide easier access for the operator.”

Meanwhile, the actual press, clamp and cushion capacity will be determined by the nature of manufacturing operations. SPC triple action presses units also offer user-friendly control systems to relieve unnecessary stress for operators. Email sales@spc.com.tw or see www.deesgroup.com.tw

## On the surface

PEITZMEIER MASCHINENBAU DESIGNS, constructs and produces machines for the surface processing of stainless steel, steel and aluminium. Its aim is to help users complete production processes in an easier, safer and more efficient manner.

“Our OMNI-GRIND modular system is one machine for various production technologies. OMNI-GRIND modules are available for grinding, polishing, deburring, edge rounding and bevelling. Changeover or additional installation of these processing modules is easy. With OMNI-GRIND, individual configuration for manufacturing processes is feasible, as the size is also configurable,” the German manufacturer told ISMR.

The table and machine size, tools and automation grade can be selected individually for the OMNI-GRIND modular system. The machines can also be upgraded and retrofitted with various technology modules. OMNI-GRIND Twinbelt grinding machines are also available in two different versions – the OMNI-Grind Twin for processing midsize parts and the OMNI-Grind Portal for automatic processing of large and heavy parts. See www.peitzmeier-maschinenbau.de
