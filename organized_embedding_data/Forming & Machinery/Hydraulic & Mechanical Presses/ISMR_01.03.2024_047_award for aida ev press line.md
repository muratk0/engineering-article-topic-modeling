# Award for AIDA EV press line

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA), has announced that its high-speed EV drive motor core press line received the 66th annual 'Ten Greatest New Products Award' from Nikkan Kogyo Shimbun (Japan Business & Technology Daily News).

"AIDA ENGINEERING Ltd., AIDA-America's parent company in Japan, received this award for the first ever independently developed and manufactured press system for forming EV drive motor cores. AIDA is also the first to achieve automated production in this field," AIDA told ISMR.

"To provide our customers with the optimal high-speed precision press line for producing difficult-to-form EV drive motor cores, we independently developed a complete array of peripheral equipment to achieve worldclass high-speed forming. This included an uncoiler; an S-loop device; a feeder; a core rotator and a scrap cutter. This system is equipped with the latest digital and AI (artificial intelligence) technologies, enabling us to provide customers with a comprehensive solution for an entire high-speed precision press line that incorporates DX (digital transformation) technology," it added.

The facial recognition system for form prevents erroneous operation by unauthorised personnel. AIDA has also developed an oil temperature management system to maintain precision forming as well as a predictive failure detection system.

AIDA is a global specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metalforming automation equipment. AIDA servo and mechanical stamping presses range in capacity from 30 to 4,000 tons, from one to 1,500 strokes per minute, from gap frame to straightside, high speed to cold forging, transfer and progressive die, material handling and coil-feeding equipment. With a worldwide network of sales and service locations in nearly 40 cities across 20 countries, AIDA press installations exceed 80,000 units in over 60 countries.■
