# Leak testing for mould-makers

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

MEUSBERGER, a manufacturer of standard parts, has developed its E 2175 Quick adapter set designed for efficient leak testing.

“The entire injection mould, whether it is cavity plate, mould insert or slide can be tested. The easy and tool-free installation (no additional mounting holes) in the cylindrical bores of the cooling circuits is, we believe, unique. The slim design enables a minimum distance from 14mm. The quick adapter allows mould-makers to pass on their product 100 percent leak tested to their customers. The E 2175 Quick adapter set is available from stock in two different diameters and supplied in a toolbox,” explained the manufacturer.
