# Multiple processes on one machine

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

VOORTMAN STEEL MACHINERY designs and develops high-precision steel plate processing machines.

“Manufacturers increasingly want to add additional operations or processes to their current production process, while automating and optimising business processes. We therefore offer high-end plate processing machines, such as the Voortman V310 and V325, capable of performing multiple processes on one machine (such as 3D plasma bevel cutting, oxy-fuel cutting, drilling, milling, tapping and countersinking),” it explained.

Parameters and technology for these operations are provided by Voortman, requiring minimal knowledge by users to set up these additional operations. These all-in-one machines are designed to increase flexibility while decreasing routing and intermediate stock in a workshop to shorten product lead times significantly.

“Ultimately, users will be able to create parts which are as complete as possible, while reducing operational costs per part. A combination of Voortman and SigmaNEST software solutions automate various data flows from works office to end product. This enables users to monitor part production status in real-time and manage load capacity in workshops,” it told ISMR. For further details, access www.voortman.net
