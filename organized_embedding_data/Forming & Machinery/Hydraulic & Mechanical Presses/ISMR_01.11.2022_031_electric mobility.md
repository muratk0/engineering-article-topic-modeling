# Electric mobility

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

The production of high-precision motor cores is at the basis of the transition to electric mobility. AIDA's high-speed presses are used for forming the motor cores of electric vehicles. Two product lines that are dedicated to the production of motor laminations are the AIDA HMX Series and AIDA MSP Series presses.

The AIDA MSP press enables users to produce motors such as EV motors, HEV motors and energy-efficient home appliance motors. The dynamic accuracy has been significantly improved due to its compact design and multiple suspension-point architecture. The areas that transmit the forming loads (such as the connecting rod bearings and the crankshaft bearings) are all made from special metals selected to avoid heat deformation. Precise dies that require a large bed area are necessary for the production of high-efficiency motors, such as motors for hybrid cars or for energy saving.
