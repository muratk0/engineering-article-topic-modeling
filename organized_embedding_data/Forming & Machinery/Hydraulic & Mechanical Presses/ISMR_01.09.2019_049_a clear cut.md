AT EMO 2019 Hannover, OMAX® Corporation will present its OMAX 5555 JetMachining Center® with a 40hp EnduroMAX® pump and Tilt-A-Jet®, as well as its latest in personal waterjets, the ProtoMAX® With a variety of products on display, visitors to the OMAX booth (Hall 16, stand D01) will be able to view waterjet demonstrations 'for every level of manufacturing'.

"The OMAX 5555 is suitable for shops needing an industrial machine with a smaller footprint capable of cutting 1397mm x 1397mm. With a completely sealed and protected ball screw drive system this robust and reliable workhorse is perfect for shops who are cutting projects that need high precision," the waterjet specialist told /SMR

"The Tilt-A-Jet lets your waterjet achieve virtually zero taper with most materials. It can position the nozzle at an angle calculated by the software to exactly offset the taper from the jet. Taper doesn't disappear – it just gets moved to the scrap part of the material, leaving your part with exactly square edges.

In addition to its industrial machines, OMAX will also demonstrate the new ProtoMAX personal abrasive waterjet system. ProtoMAX is a compact, selfcontained cutting system designed for prototyping and low-volume cutting of most material up to approximately 26mm thick. It is suitable for small job shops, engineering classrooms, 'makerspaces and personal use. It is also, said the company, a useful addition for large shops and fabricators that need occasional waterjet capabilities to complement their other cutting systems.

"Energy-effective direct drive pumps power both large machines. Our direct drive pumps use less electricity and water, while reducing component fatigue. These pumps also run quietly and cleanly, making them the ideal solution for shops that want to do more with less," explained OMAX.

OMAX's IntelliMAX control software was engineered specifically for use with abrasive waterjets and requires no special machine code knowledge to use. According to the company, someone with no CNC machine operation experience can learn to cut parts on an OMAX waterjet in a few hours. OMAX provides free online machine operation and maintenance training. IntelliMAX software is compatible with more than 90 different file formats, including all major CAD program file types, plus graphics file formats such as JPEG, GIF and PNG files.
