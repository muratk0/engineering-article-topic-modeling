With over 80 years of experience, Timesavers International is a global specialist in grinding machines for deburring; edgerounding (possible up to a 2mm radius); surface finishing; laser oxide and/or slag removal and precision grinding.

"We strive for the highest quality and best finish for sheet-metal products. Our metalworking solutions are built according to customer requirements and the possibilities are endless. The range of machines varies from small, entry-level deburring machines to large finishing and precision-grinding machines," explained the company.

"In addition, we offer training, service and provide worldwide support so that users can get the most out of their machines. Are you ready to produce more effectively and efficiently? If so, visit us at EuroBLECH and discover the 22 RB, 32 RB and 42 RB series, our popular rotary brush machines as well as the compact 12- series deburring machine and the 10 series Manual Grinder," it concluded.
