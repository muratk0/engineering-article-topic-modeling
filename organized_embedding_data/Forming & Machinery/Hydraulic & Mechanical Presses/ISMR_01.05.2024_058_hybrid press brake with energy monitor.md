# Hybrid press brake with energy monitor

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Automatic set-up and tool change are the two most valuable assets of B3.AU-TO, Salvagnini's press brake. Combining the best features of Salvagnini's electric and hydraulic solutions, the B3.AU-TO is designed to significantly extend the availability, autonomy and flexibility of the press brake.

The Salvagnini B3.AU-TO press brake has been built to relieve the pressure on companies dealing with manual tool set-up time and downtime for feeding, programming, part manipulation and controls.

"Available in four models, with bending lengths of up to three or four metres and maximum force of 170 or 220 tons, the B3.AU-TO enables companies to better cope with a dynamic market which demands increasingly smaller batches, kit productions or batch-ones with very short lead times. The AU-TO automatic tool-change device and the ATA automatic tool adjuster, which adjusts the length of the stations, significantly reduce press brake re-tooling, increasing autonomy and productivity as well as reducing lead time," explained Nicola Artuso, Salvagnini product manager for bending technologies.

## Features and capabilities

Distinctive features of the B3.AU-TO include the Salvagnini-patented upper and lower automatic tool-change unit.

"Additionally, AU-TO reduces set-up times and increases the availability of the press brake. The operations are rapid and in cycle, even in masked time, helping to increase the efficiency of the press brake. AU-TO makes the most out of the ATA devices, used to automatically adjust the upper and lower bending tool length (saving time and increasing production efficiency). AU-TO assures full flexibility for tools, because the press brake allows mixed tooling. After automatic tool change and set-up, the B3.AU-TO enables the manual integration of standard WILA tools or special tools, such as hemming tools, on the bend line," outlined Nicola Artuso.

"B3.AU-TO has the smallest footprint on the market. The tool store is covered in the rear of the B3. It can contain up to 24m of tools (up to 1000mm in length) without segmentation," he continued.

All the B3 press brakes are equipped with the following four features:

MAC3.0: A set of integrated adaptive technologies that make the system intelligent and cancel out waste and corrections.

S-Crowning: The mechanical crowning system that ensures consistent bending angles along the entire bending length: the effective curvature of the lower table is measured and compensated in real-time, without any operator intervention.

TFC2.0, Total Frame Control: This prevents deviations in the bending angle using an algorithm from the FEM study of each model. TFC2.0 compensates any in-cycle deformations of the press-brake structure according to variations in the characteristics of the part processed.

## AMS: This is a laser angle

measurement system that detects and corrects variations in the bending angle due to sheet metal springback. Regardless of the length of the press brake and of the part being bent, the angle is always measured in one single point; this approach helps to reduce the cycle time without compromising on precision.

"All these features make the B3.AU-TO suitable for companies with a high daily item turnover rate. The AU-TO and ATA devices reduce the cycle time, making it easier to forecast, plan and monitor," explained Nicola Artuso.

"B3 is a hybrid press brake. It achieves the typical tonnages of hydraulic press brakes, with the speed of electric ones. A Direct- Drive solution controls the ram movements: two independent Direct-Drive brushless motors, activated only when needed, allow the ram to reach fast approach and fast return with speeds up to 250mm/second (depending upon the chosen B3 model)," he added.

Impacts is Salvagnini's new environmentally focused tool that measures the consumption of electricity on the B3 press brake. It is the first step towards calculating volumes of CO₂ equivalent of each single manufactured component. Users can monitor their energy consumption so they can optimise and reduce it.
