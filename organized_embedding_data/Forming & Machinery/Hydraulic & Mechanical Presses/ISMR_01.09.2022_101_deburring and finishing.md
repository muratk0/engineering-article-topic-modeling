# Deburring and finishing

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

The Timesavers 22 series is an entry-level deburring machine for sheet metal products up to a width of 900mm, 1050mm or 1280mm. The machine removes upstanding burrs and applies a finish on metals such as stainless steel, steel, aluminium and copper.

“The machine can be equipped with a combination of wide abrasive belts (W), brushes (B), multi discs (MD) and/ or hammerheads (H). The 'configuration' describes the combinations of machine heads. For example, a 22 series HW is for slag removal and grinding or finishing, while a WMDMD is for deburring, edge breaking and finishing," explained the Dutch manufacturer, Timesavers International.

The hammerhead is Timesavers' solution for the easy removal of slag, also known as dross. Although the small, flexible pins hammer off the slag, the surface of the product is not harmed during the removal process.

“Because the 22 series has low operating costs, it is an excellent solution for companies who are looking for an efficient but economical deburring solution. Due to its small footprint and high production capacity, it can be placed in any size of company," concluded the surface-finishing specialist.
