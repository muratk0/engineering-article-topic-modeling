# Making its mark

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

AT BLECHEXPO 2015 THIS OCTOBER, Swiss press specialist BRUDERER will premiere two new high-performance stamping press models. The Swiss manufacturer will present the BSTA 410-110, a powerful press which complements its product line in the 40-ton press force range. The manufacturer will also offer a glimpse of the future of high speed stamping with a special high speed version of the BSTA 200-60 HS, equipped with a progressive 7-stage stamping tool made by Fritz Stepper GmbH & Co. KG. Up to 16,000 board-to-board connectors per minute can be stamped at a speed of over 2300spm, and this will be demonstrated live on the stand.

“The BRUDERER BSTA 410-110 features a main drive, brake, flywheel and clutch that are based on the BSTA 280, resulting in a correspondingly higher stroke speed of 1,600spm. The tool-loading area of 1,100mm corresponds to that of a BSTA 510, a generous size for stamping presses in this tonnage range, thereby allowing the use of progressive tools which are becoming increasingly popular as production complexity becomes even more important,” said the manufacturer.

The package is rounded off by BRUDERER feed technology. In its standard version, the BSTA 410-110 is equipped with a BBV 191 roller feed unit, the successor to the BBV 190. As optional attachments, various servo feeds or the BZV 61 gripper feed unit are available. Tools used on a BSTA 510-110 can be transferred to a BSTA 410-110 as the presses share the same size of tool-loading area. See the company on stand 7209 in Hall 7 or see www.bruderer.com
