# Cutting and bending in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

At EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, Germany, this October, sheet-metal specialist Salvagnini showcased its wide range of machinery, software and services on its booth.

## P-Robot

Salvagnini introduced P-Robot at EuroBLECH, a new application which combines a panel bender with a robot to produce kits, batches and single parts autonomously. It offers a compact and modular layout, is designed to make robot programming “as simple as possible" and allows for a certain level of customisation to specific production requirements. New functionality includes the ability to manage sheet separators to detach single parts produced, if necessary, and book-type stacking to pile parts vertically. At EuroBLECH, P-Robot was available for numerous different demos to highlight the different production strategies available.

"This P-Robot incorporates a P1, but we can also apply it to the P2 and P4. The P1 is our electric panel bender, with maximum consumption of 3kW and a footprint of just 8m². For the robot, we have chosen a partner which allows us to move around in proximity to the working area in complete safety: this translates into an extremely compact layout," Salvagnini told SMR.

“The robot is equipped with a flippable gripping device, fitted with suction cups on two sides, for picking up the bent part and immediately positioning the new part to be bent, in-cycle. This gripping device enables optimisation of the sheet loading/unloading cycles, improvement of production rates and a reduction in waiting times. We can also offer solutions with loading/unloading assistance only, or more complex systems providing for automatic movement of the pallets or thirdparty integrations (e.g. with work centres for labelling laser marking; riveting; welding and corner forming," it added.

## Laser in focus

A patented modern design, extensive automation portfolio and a range of advanced process control and efficiency solutions are the cornerstones on which Salvagnini has built its L5 laser. Salvagnini unveiled a new 8kW high power density source for the laser at EuroBLECH in Hanover. In the configuration at Hanover, L5 was fed from an LTWS store-tower, equipped with automated loading/unloading. The MCU automatic sorting device (with new full multi-gripping function) completed the layout and was used to stack parts of different shapes, sizes and weights. The L5 was also equipped with the APC2 adaptive process sensor which monitors piercing in real-time, for greater speed and higher quality.

"This new power source further improves the L5 performance on medium-thin sheet metal thicknesses, because the high-power density also allows significant improvements in cutting acceleration. The 8kW high power density source guarantees cutting speeds higher than any other traditional 8kW source, and at thicknesses of up to 6mm achieves cutting speeds even greater than those of a 10kW source," explained Salvagnini.

All the Group's lasers installed since 2021 have been equipped with the ACUT option (for cutting with suitably treated compressed air). Contributing to the sustainability of ACUT is APM2, the new compact, turnkey device which connects directly to the pneumatic system of the factory and ensures the pressure values required for the cutting process. Salvagnini's lasers can also be equipped with various artificial vision applications.

Salvagnini cutting program software is called STREAMLASER (part of the STREAM programming suite). A key feature is OPTI, the nesting algorithm which optimises the use of the metal sheet, minimizing scrap. Another is SAFE GRID, which automatically optimises the positioning of the parts with respect to the grid, preventing piercing operations, lead-ins and cuts from overlapping on the Y axis.

## Bending solutions

With over 40 years of experience in automatic bending, and more than 4000 installations in 76 countries worldwide, Salvagnini is a global panel-bending specialist. It presented an S1+P4 punching and panel bending line at Hanover (S1 punch-laser combi machine and P4-2520 panel bender). Salvagnini also presented a further extension of the concept of in-line processing, by integrating aB3.AU-TO press brake downstream of the S1+P4.

"Like all Salvagnini panel benders, the P2 natively combines productivity, with its automatic bending and handling cycles, with an average of 17 bends per minute, and flexibility, with its universal bending tools," commented Nicola Artuso, Salvagnini Product Manager for bending technologies.

"It doesn't need re-tooling, because the upper and lower blades, the counterblade and the blankholder are universal tools that are able to process the whole range of thicknesses and machinable materials. The ABA automatic blankholder adapts the tool length according to the size of the part being produced in-cycle, without machine down times or manual re-tooling. P2 is really the ideal solution for kit or singlebatch productions. Only the loading and unloading operations are performed by the operator, who positions the sheet metal on the worktable and collects the product after bending," he added.

The integrated advanced sensors measure the actual thickness and effective size of the blank, detecting any deformations caused by variations in temperature. MAC3.0 allows the P2 to adapt to the material being machined by measuring the tensile strength in real-time and comparing it to the reference values of some of the most common materials on the market. It is designed to drastically reduces the risk of obtaining faulty parts and eliminate reworking and material waste.

P2 comes in three models, which differ by maximum bending length (from 2180-2500mm) and maximum bending height (203mm or 254mm). Two devices from Salvagnini's P4 panel benders are now also available for the P2: CUT and RSU. The CUT tool is used to create narrow profiles; a cutting blade that works above the panel bender's lower bending blade. The RSU allows the P2 to use a number of special tools for deburring, ribbing or engraving the sheet metal.

“The CUT and RSU options can be installed together, along with all the other available options. The P2 on display at EuroBLECH, a 2120 model, is equipped with both tools with the addition of the DPM manipulator, a suction cup device that allows the handling of narrow parts or material-free frames with a minimum width of 105mm. The machine configuration is completed by the CLA option, auxiliary blades that are modular in length used to create upward or downward tabs that are shorter than the entire length of the panel being processed," Artuso concluded.

Automatic set-up and tool change are the two most obvious features ofB3.AU-TO, the Salvagnini press brake. By combining the features and benefits of electric and hydraulic solutions, B3.AU-TO significantly extends the availability, autonomy and flexibility of the press brake. It is available in four models, with maximum bending length of three or four metres and maximum force of 170 or 220 tons. At EuroBLECH, the B3 received some parts to complete from the S1+P4 line.

B3.AU-TO is suitable for companies with a high daily item turnover rate requiring a lot of re-toolings. The AU-TO (automatic tool change) and ATA (automatic tool adjuster) are designed to reduce the cycle time, making it easier to predict, plan and monitor. The option of using WILA tools for mixed re-tooling; integrated adaptive technologies; Direct Drive, KERS and new possibilities in the software further extend performance.

Direct Drive features two independent, direct-drive, brushless motors, activated only when needed, which allow the ram to reach fast approach and fast return with speeds up to 250 mm/second (depending upon the chosen B3 model). KERS recovers energy waste and reuses it in subsequent steps, accelerating and reaching higher speeds with the same consumption.

For all its B3 press brakes, Salvagnini offers MAC2.0 – a set of integrated adaptive technologies that make the system intelligent and cancel out waste and corrections. S-Crowning is a mechanical crowning system that ensures consistent bending angles along the entire bending length. AMS, the laser angle measurement system, detects and corrects variations in the bending angle due to sheet-metal springback.

STREAMFORMER and PARTS (part of STREAM, the Salvagnini programming software suite) enable users to automatically program the parts in a job list, reducing the number of re-toolings between different programs. This helps to reduce the total number of re-toolings. The B3 version of OPS, the modular software for managing Salvagnini production, has a feature that automatically optimises the production sequence to minimize overall re-tooling times. Exploiting OPS means improving press-brake productivity.

## Software and services

Salvagnini's OPS, Order Processing System is modular software for managing production that Salvagnini has been developing since 1993. LINKS is its IoT service that improves the overall effectiveness of Salvagnini systems, using the latest business intelligence technologies. It is employed to monitor the machine performance and access production data; logbooks; performance KPIs; telemetry and parameter monitoring, through the Condition Monitoring process.

STREAM is its integrated ecosystem for managing all activities in the office and on the factory floor. It is used to seamlessly program the various technologies required to produce a part. In addition to the five different CAM software packages, each associated with a specific technology, STREAM also includes VALUES and PARTS. VALUES provides an accurate estimation of product costs, while PARTS is used to manage the entire database of the company's products, defining the production flows for each part, centralising all the information and providing the programmer with a single point for controlling and performing programming activities.

ConceptS is Salvagnini expertise on analysing, simulating and optimising production flows. Its specialists analyse and simulate processes, whatever the production context, to define the best Salvagnini systems configuration. With its SIMs service, Salvagnini offers its customers the ability to improve the performance of every system and the addition of new functions after the first installation.

Salvagnini maintenance contracts are designed to improve system performance. Acty is Salvagnini's augmented reality application for Android/iOS devices that is designed to make remote support much more effective.
