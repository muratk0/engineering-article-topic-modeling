Compact filtration system protects users from fine dust during cutting processes

Kemper offers the Plasma-Fil Compact extraction system for combustion and plasma cutting applications. The stand-alone system can handle medium to large amounts of dust.

Its filtration technology separates more than 99 percent of captured hazardous material and the majority of ultrafine dust particles. Hazardous substances are cleaned automatically.

The filter is designed to prevent individual filter elements from sticking. One hundred percent of the filter area is always available when polluted air enters the system during the down-flow process, in which the polluted air is guided from top to bottom.

Waterjet cutting system cuts up to 500 IPM

Koike Aronson offers the K-Jet abrasive waterjet cutting system. It delivers cut speeds up to 500 IPM with standard cutting areas up to 72 by 144 in. and a table capacity up to 125 lbs./sq. ft. (3-in. mild steel).

Features include Hypertherm Hy-Precision 60,000-PSI intensifier pumps, Flash-Cut CNC with built-in CAD/CAM, and Ebbco filtration systems.

The machine offers fully enclosed, air-pressurized bellows; hidden linear ways; accessible grease fittings; and a heavy-duty, .-in. walled, floor-mounted table that reduces deflection. The slats and slat supports are easily removed, and the heavy-duty, 5/16-in. machined main beam and heavy-duty saddles help maintain smooth movement throughout the table.

Options include adjustable water height, clamping, air-over-oil hydraulic cooling, laser plate alignment, and laser height control.

Ultrasonic tester inspects tube weld zones online during continuous production

Designed to test for longitudinally oriented defects typical of the ERW process, Magnetic Analysis Corp.’s Echomac

®

WLD ultrasonic testing system has new operating conveniences for inspecting tube weld zones online during continuous production.

The system typically comprises four channels using both CW and CCW sounds modes for detecting ID and OD longitudinal defects between 5 and 10 percent of wall thickness. Additional channels can be added, depending on the application.

The system includes Echomac instrumentation and a test head incorporating transducer assemblies with irrigated shoes for constant coupling with the tube being tested. A joystick allows the operator to rotate the entire test head by means of a high-resolution stepper motor, maintaining optimal positioning of the transducer assemblies with respect to the weld zone. The operator can verify that the transducers are correctly positioned by observing a monitor showing a laser beam that must bisect the weld center.

The individual irrigated transducer shoes can be exchanged to accommodate tube size changes in 5 seconds using the system’s quick-disconnect mechanisms, states the company.

Facing machine’s floating collets handle standard, micro, and extended tube

The improved electric, lightweight TSM-4501M facing machine from Otto Arc Systems is designed to handle tube and pipe sizes from to 4 in. and can bevel up to a 0.375-in. wall thickness.

The machine design features floating collets, which allow users to employ standard, micro, or extended pipe or tube. The choice of a battery or electric variable-speed motor allows a constant speed for quick facing of multiple tubes. The upgraded clamping design improves facing quality on tube or pipe requiring square ends before welding, the manufacturer reports.
