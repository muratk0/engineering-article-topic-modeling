# New tube bending technologies at FÁBTECH

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Unison Tube LLC, the North American division of UK-based Unison Ltd (specialists in all-electric tube manipulation) will be presenting a range of tube and wirebending technologies at FABTECH in Orlando, FL (USA) from 15-17 October 2024.

Visitors to stand S34035 at Orlando's Orange County Convention Centre will be able to view one of Unison's recently launched Synergy hybrid-electric tube bending machines, a Unison EvBend 1000 manually operated, CNC-controlled tubebending machine and a Pneuform rotary head wire and tube bending machine.

"FABTECH is always a key event on our calendar," said Stuart Singleton, VP of Danville, VA-based Unison Tube LLC. "The 2024 event, however, is going to be extra special for us. Not only because 2024 marks the 30th anniversary of Unison Ltd launching the world's first all-electric tube bender, but because it's also the first year we'll be showcasing Pneuform wire- and small-bore tube bending machines."

Unison Ltd acquired Pneuform, a specialist in the manufacture of wireforming and small-bore tube bending machines, in Autumn 2023. Like all Pneuform machines, the model on show at FABTECH will be equipped with Yaskawa drives and Unison's software for programming and operation.

Unison Synergy hybrid-electric, dual-stack tube bending machines combine precise electric control with advanced, near-silent operation.

"Our Synergy models have proved highly popular with manufacturers and subcontractors who carry out repetitive tube bending tasks," continued Stuart Singleton. "As well as the 50mm model that we will have on show, there is also a larger 80mm machine. Both [feature] our user-friendly Unibend control and motors and drives. They offer exceptional power and rigid mechanical design."

Visitors to Unison's booth at FABTECH will also see the company's EvBend machines. Designed for low-volume production and prototyping, Unison EvBend models are used across the aerospace, MRO, Formula 1 and oil & gas industries.

"EvBend models provide CNC-controlled, manually operated, three-axis mandrel, multi-plane bending at a fraction of the cost of fully automated machines. While the EvBend 1000 has been designed for bending tube of up to 16mm in diameter (22mm in copper), a larger machine, the EvBend 2000, is also available. Built to bend tube of up to 50mm in diameter, its bending function is servo-assisted," the company told ISMR.

The company will also showcase the latest advances in its Unibend CNC, tubebending simulation software and advanced tube bending application app at FABTECH this October.

Available in single-stack, multi-stack and right/left varieties, Unison machines are delivered to more than 20 countries.

In 2023, Unison celebrated 50 years of intelligent tube technology.
