# High speed drilling line

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

THE RAPID from FICEP is a new CNC high speed drilling line for angles and flats. The machine’s powerful spindles, with new generation high performance tools, enables the use of cost-efficient Indexable Carbide Drilling tools to enhance productivity.

The CNC materials handling system loads the angles onto the conveyor track which then automatically clamps the workpiece in position and every process then takes place sequentially. The two drilling heads are equipped with very powerful direct drive spindles and an automatic tool-changer with six positions for each of the spindles. A new additional auxiliary axis of 200mm allows independent control of the two spindles in the “X” axis. The independent movement of the two auxiliary axes, while an angle is stationary, maximises the productivity achievable with each spindle within the stroke.

“RAPID CNC drilling lines are also modular and can incorporate scribing, hard stamp marking, single- or double-shearing or, alternatively, a high speed circular carbide saw. Optional hard stamping CNC marking units have eight selectable cassette types, each one including thirteen characters. Depending upon the RAPID model, rapid cutting is provided either by a single-cut hydraulic shearing unit or a high speed circular saw with hardened carbide inserts,” said FICEP. ## Safety structures in production

ARI METAL SRL, which specialises in the development of a modular Aluminium Profile System®, builds industrial safety structures for machine tools in accordance with European law.

“The safety structures are built with extruded profiles from our Aluminium Profile System®. The anodizing surface treatment (a minimum 10 microns) gives a fine finish to the profiles. Assembly is simple and fast and does not require specialised staff. Using hooks, panel assembly on the supports can be carried out by a single operator. After assembly, the safety structures require no maintenance and, if necessary, can be removed quickly,” explained Ari Metal.

The Italian manufacturer also offers accident prevention systems, both along the perimeter and mounted directly onto the machine. See www.arimetal.it ■

## Special steel in Europe

AT THE MADE IN STEEL exhibition in Milan (Italy), the SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH Group showcased the Group’s special products in the area of stainless, engineering and bright steel as well as tool steel. With more than 500 steel grades in dimensions ranging in size from 13 micrometres to 1,000 millimetres, it has strong product diversity in Europe.

SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH develops and produces special steel solutions for demanding applications and offers processing services on steel products and technical support. With nine steelworks worldwide and service companies in 38 countries, it tailors products to the requirements of local markets. In Italy, for example, SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH operates three service centres that cut, mill and grind the group's special steel to meet customer needs. See www.schmolz-bickenbach.com ■

## Kjellberg invests in India

KJELLBERG HAS NOW expanded its presence in the Indian market by establishing its subsidiary, Kjellberg Cutting and Welding India Pvt. Ltd, in Pune. The company offers products and technologies for thermal cutting, joining and processing.

“From 1 April 2015, the new subsidiary near Mumbai, in the federal state Maharashtra, will take care of all Kjellberg activities concerning sales and service in India with customers. The managing directors, Carsten Trentau and Falk Tzschichholz, aim to further improve Smart Focus 130, 200 and 300 will support our Indian customers in all service and sales affairs,” the company told ISMR.

Kjellberg’s new Smart Focus plasma cutting series is suitable for the Indian market and, says the company, has been positively received after the sales launch in February.

“The three compact plasma cutting units are user-friendly, easy to service and achieve their best cutting results in the range of 1 to 80mm with a convincing price-performance ratio. Users benefit from the patented Contour Cut technology for rapid and precise new Smart Focus plasma cutting series from 22- 25 April at BLECH India (stand E70),” concluded the manufacturer.
