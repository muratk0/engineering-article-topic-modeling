# Asservimenti Presse Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand C72

www.asservimentipresse.it

Italian manufacturer, Asservimenti Presse Srl, produces equipment for press automation (decoilers; straighteners; cut-to-length lines; transfer and handling systems; robotics etc.) for sheet metalworking. With over thirty years of experience, it currently exports its technology to over 20 countries. It has completed over 4000 installations globally and specialises in press automation, aiming to optimise the production cycles of its customers.

It produces feeding lines with decoilers (up to 25-ton capacity with external diameter of 2000mm) which can process steel strip of up to 2000mm wide and 14mm in thickness. The company has placed a firm focus on energyefficient lines that help to offset high material and energy costs. The new coil-cutting lines can process aluminium and high-tensile steel.

New developments incorporate automatic coil-change on its lines and a SMART HMI control panel, as well as a new straightener-feeder unit with electronic pilot release that can reach 110 strokes/min. with a step of 300mm.
