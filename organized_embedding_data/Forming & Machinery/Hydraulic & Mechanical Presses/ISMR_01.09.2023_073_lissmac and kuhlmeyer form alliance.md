# Lissmac and Kuhlmeyer form alliance

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

German companies Lissmac Maschinenbau GmbH from Bad Wurzach and Kuhlmeyer Maschinenbau GmbH from Bad Oeynhausen have launched a sales and service agreement (from 1 July 2023) whereby Lissmac co-represents and services Kuhlmeyer solutions.

"Together we are stronger and can better serve the market for sheet metalworking machines. To underline this cooperation, we will be exhibiting together in Hall 6 (booth 6F20) at the Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition in Essen, Germany, from 11-15 September 2023," they told ISMR.

Since 2003, Lissmac has developed a range of specially developed, patented steel brushing, grinding and deburring machines. Lissmac machines for doublesided processing of internal and external contours in a single pass are designed to save both processing time and energy costs compared to single-sided machining

Kuhlmeyer can look back on fifty years of innovation and specialises in machine construction for surface finishing, deburring, weld seam preparation and edge bevelling of three-dimensional sheetmetal workpieces or large-format plate material. Lissmac can rely on a worldwide network of dealers and a broad-based sales team, opening up new markets with Kuhlmeyer products.

"What unites Lissmac and Kuhlmeyer are the same values and similar corporate philosophies," said Daniel Keller, Managing Director, Lissmac Maschinenbau GmbH.
