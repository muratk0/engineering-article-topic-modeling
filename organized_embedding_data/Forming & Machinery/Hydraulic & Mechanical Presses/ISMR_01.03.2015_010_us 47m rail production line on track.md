# US$ 47m rail production line on track

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

EMERSON INDUSTRIAL AUTOMATION has been selected for products, solutions and services as part of a US$ 47 million investment at the Dabrowa Górnicza plant of steel giant, ArcelorMittal. The project is designed to take Poland’s railway infrastructure to the next level, and will result in the production of a new range of extra-long, cut-to-length rails.

An upgraded production line was completed in December 2013, forming part of the company’s rolling mill department. It produces 120-metre long steel rails – a four-fold increase over the maximum 30-metre long rails manufactured previously. The new length will reduce the number of joints between rails, significantly improving safety in railway transport.

Control Techniques and Leroy-Somer, both business units of Emerson Industrial Automation, provided variable speed drives and gearboxes which formed combined solutions for chain conveyors and roller tables. In total, 750 Leroy-Somer gearboxes are controlled by 440 Control Techniques variable speed drives.

“The combination of drives, gearboxes and service from a single source was a major consideration for ArcelorMittal. As a result of working with one single supplier, ArcelorMittal is able to achieve a far higher speed than the previous 30-metre line, greatly improving production efficiency and throughput,” explained Emerson Industrial Automation.

The new production line at ArcelorMittal’s Dąbrowa Górnicza was inaugurated officially in February 2015 by Janusz Piechociński, Poland’s Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Economy. See www.EmersonIndustrialAutomation.com ■
