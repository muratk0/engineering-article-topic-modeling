## Bihler (Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG)

## www.bihler.de

At Blechexpo 2023, Bihler will present its new LM 2000 platform, the latest addition to its portfolio of stamping and forming machines.

"This modular machine platform offers features highly standardised, linear tool technology. The machine platform is available in two variants, the LM 2000-KT and the LM 2000-NC. These two linear machines are driven either by cam- or servo-controlled units for the efficient production of stamped and formed parts from strip material," it said.

The LM 2000-KT is designed for use with medium- and largesized batches (up to 500 1/min.). The LM 2000-NC is intended for use with small- and medium-sized batches and high variant diversity. Both machines are tool-compatible with uniformly designed linear forming tools (up to 70 standard parts).

"Standard interfaces and hydraulic quick clamping systems enable rapid set-up and 100% reproducible tool changeover. A total machining length of 2,000mm is available on the machine plate for additional value-added processes," concluded Bihler.

## Nidec Press & Automation

## www.nidecpa.com

Nidec Press & Automation will be demonstrating its e-mobility manufacturing solutions at Blechexpo.

"The world of e-mobility is upon us and some of the world's leading manufacturers rely on our expertise and extensive turnkey solutions to quickly ramp up e-mobility production," it said.

"With six respected brands in the metalforming industry, we offer a one-stop solutions' source for electric vehicle OEMs and Tier One suppliers. Working with a single source affords strategic benefits in any production process, especially for products with rapidly evolving and highly complex technologies and manufacturing processes such as e-mobility components," it added.

NP&A's portfolio includes high-speed presses running at 2400 strokes per minute to 5000-ton servo presses, as well as automation (advanced highspeed feeds to custom-engineered turnkey systems) and high-tensile coil lines. It also offers a responsive customer service organisation.

## STEINEL

## www.steinel.com

STEINEL will present its product highlights at Blechexpo 2023. Over 157 square metres, it will be presenting its product portfolio for the punching and bending industry including guide units; nitrogen systems; die sets and die plates; active elements; tapping units; stamping tool modules and ready-to-work stamping tools.

"Our '100% made in Germany' products offer precision and durability. Besides standard and customised guide units, special highlights at Blechexpo will be the latest generation of STEINEL Manifold Plates and the latest version of S-Former E, the intelligent tapping unit," it told ISMR.

"We want to show visitors how they can improve their productivity by implementing the right components and technologies in their production process. We invite them to come to our booth with their most challenging projects and our engineers will present them with the best concepts and solutions," added Christian Borgert, Head of Sales and Marketing, STEINEL.
