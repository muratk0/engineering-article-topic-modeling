## DIMECO Group

## www.dimeco.com

A French family-owned business, founded in early 1957, DIMECO produces sheet metal coil production systems.

Staff at its headquarters engineer and manufacture press feed line equipment, cut-to-length lines as well as "Flexilines" (LINAPUNCH flexible coil punching and LINACUT coil-fed fibre laser system), equipment for press control and safety equipment plus quick die and coil-change equipment. This includes tool loading and storage as well as press safety and monitoring equipment. DIMECO has also developed an app, DIMECOIL, which calculates the coil/material/production parameters to optimise coil-fed production.

DIMECO's Spanish division has a new factory in Oiartzun (near San Sebastian) where it manufactures high production flexible punching and roll forming equipment. Its Joint Venture, DKMFS manufactures press feed equipment and cut-to-length lines in Pune (India) for the Indian market.

## InspecVision Ltd

## www.inspecvision.com

Award-winning InspecVision is a leading developer and manufacturer of 2D and 3D measurement systems for manufactured components. Its Planar automated inspection and reverse engineering measurement tool for 2D parts was designed specifically for high speed in the sheet metal industry.

"Creating real value for the customer is at the centre of everything we do," it told ISMR. "Our systems have saved companies hours of inspection time, improved part accuracy, increased the percentage of parts inspected, reduced waste and contributed to gaining new business and growing sales." Parts can be automatically inspected or reverse-engineered at every stage of production. With the addition of Opti-Scan 3D, the system offers non-contact 3D inspection capacity.

2020 saw InspecVision form a strategic partnership with TRUMPF to offer customers a future-proof measurement system that will be integrated into the TRUMPF Smart Factory world. InspecVision demo systems are installed at TRUMPF Smart Factories in Ditzingen and Chicago.

## Dreistern GmbH

Roll forming specialist, Dreistern, has focused on digitalisation and automation, what it dubs "roll forming 4.0", in the roll forming process.

"For us, Industry 4.0 is already a reality. We call it roll forming 4.0 or RF4.0 for short. We are presenting the latest evolutionary step – an integrated, intelligent roll forming process, which has reached another milestone with our new assistance system. The overriding goal of an intelligent roll forming machine is better control of the forming processes through specifically integrated sensors and the data obtained from them. These contribute to developing and implementing helpful assistance systems," it said.

Its SERVICES4.0 (replace, retrofit, modernise and service) will also be presented at Blechexpo. The latest generation of its DPMFlex double roll forming machines includes features such as the option of using pillar-frames. Its P3 series roll formers are for medium/heavy profiles.

"With the flexible P6/P7 series, overall measurements of U-, C-, C+- and Sigma profiles can be continuously varied and nonsymmetric variants produced," it added.

## Prima Power GmbH

## www.primapower.com

Italian manufacturer Prima Power offers 2D and 3D laser cutting; welding and drilling; punching; bending; combined technologies and automation machines and technologies for sheet metal. Its new Laser Genius+ 2D fibre laser machine has a trajectory speed of 180m/minute. The family includes 1530, 2040 and 2060 sizes which can be equipped with fibre laser powers ranging from 2 to 15kW.

"The new laser head has been designed to manage the heat required to melt thick sheets while staying cold and clean, using sensors that actively control the cutting process in real-time; hermetically isolated optics; simplified mechanics and a fume extraction system," it said. Three new on-board software modules are Optia (recovery of scrap sheet); Wizard, a simplified CAM for new/modified nesting processes and 2D Editor, a part program graphic editor.

Prima Power's has eight factories worldwide. Seven Technology Centres are available for technological consultation, feasibility studies, cycle time optimisation and demonstrations.

## Fladder Danmark A/S

## www.fladder.com

Fladder Danmark offers a wide range of manual, automatic and special machines for processing thin and thick sheet metal parts. These are used for deburring; oxide/dross removal; surface finishing; denibbing and intermediate sanding.

"The gear head in the machines, with the main motor and rotating motor, is mounted on a scissor suspension frame. The scissor is equipped with special hardened, grinded wheels and the machines create linear, oscillating movement across the conveyor belt. This, combined with the in-pair counter rotating spindles, results in uniform workpieces and uniform wear of the tools," Fladder told ISMR.

"Pressure on the workpiece is created by centrifugal force, through regulation of the RPM. FLADDER® technology is non-aggressive - the abrasive cylinders are flexible tools. This means that, besides flat workpieces, the machines can also process three-dimensional parts (which saves manual labour costs for deburring machined extrusions)," it added.

## KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH

## www.kohler-germany.com

KOHLER develops and produces strip feeding lines for presses and automatic stamping machines, as well as cut-to-length lines for steel service centres, mechanical engineering firms and for the automotive industry.

Its new generation of strip feeding lines features a modern drive concept for the levelling rollers. Direct drives, without cardan shafts, are designed to reduce the maintenance needed and increase energy efficiency.

"The new strip levelling machine requires 20% less floor space," explained KOHLER. "Precision strip levelling machines with direct drives can be delivered with up to 21 levelling rollers. The diameter can range between 40 and 65mm, depending upon the application. Systems with conventional drives can also be delivered with levelling rollers in the 18-30mm diameter range.

Its servo-electric Peak Performer part levelling machine levels and relieves stress from metal sheets and part blanks. Features include electromechanical levelling gap control (patented); an advanced cleaning system; reversible levelling rollers (patented) and predictive maintenance.
