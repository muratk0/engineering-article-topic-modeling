# Smart strip feeding

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

EBU UMFORMTECHNIK showcased a precise combined strip feeding/straightening system with all-roller drive and additional crosswise traversing axis at Blechexpo in Stuttgart (November 2019). The integrated zigzag movement displaces the strip sideways and produces parts punched at staggered positions. The result is reduced material consumption.

Motorised block placement of the straightening roller cassettes, central setting of strip edge guide rollers, lighting for the straightening unit and a special loop guide between driven coiler and straightening machine are integrated in the system. Besides ebu's STA automatic punching presses with their modular structure and various ebu-specific drive options, the company also supplies feeding technology. Its strip feeding lines and strip feeding systems are adapted to ebu machines and presses but can also be installed as an extension to presses from other manufacturers.

The ebu strip feeding line with Zigzag process, displayed at Blechexpo, was a space-saving and material-reducing solution. The strip feeding line features a zigzag strip feeding/straightening combination; strip feeding aid; unwinder; coil processing unit and coil handling cradle (CLW). The system is designed for a strip width of up to 1300mm, material thickness of up to 4mm and material cross-section of up to 3000mm². No coil cradle was necessary in this design.

The zigzag path, with which the combined strip feeding and straightening system (ebu URV 9.60) works, is also designed to save space. The strip is transported using a side displacement path. In this model, the path length is 500mm.

"The zigzag procedure means that substantially more material savings are made by arranging several narrow parts in such a way that the punching scrap is minimised. For example, the system that was presented in Stuttgart can later punch round blanks that are formed into compressed air containers. The blanks are punched at staggered positions from the coil, which also reduces set-up time," explained ebu.

The combined strip feeding/straightening system also has an all-roller drive that is synchronised via a precision distribution gear and mobile operator devices and sensors for reliable strip feed. The ebu EFH strip feed aid is operated hydraulically and has swivellable and expandable support arms and a powered take-up roller. The ebu HS 130 decoiler with frequency-regulated main drive with ultrasound loop control and the diversion aggregate for loop control include hydraulic expansion of the decoiler mandrel and hydraulically-operated pressure arms with a maximum pressing force of 850kg. Additional attachment segments increase the range of expansion.

Further components of the system are a decoiler positioning device (ebu HVE 120) and a coil handling cradle (ebu CLS 120) with tilt protection that can handle coils with a weight of up to 12,000kg and a width of 1,300mm at a lifting height of 300mm.
