# Tooling for Salvagnini punching systems

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

WILSON TOOL INTERNATIONAL (www.wilsontool.com) now offers tooling for Salvagnini precision punching systems. The company manufactures a complete range of punches, dies and strippers, including standard and custom shapes.

Designed to enhance the performance of Salvagnini punching systems, Wilson Tool’s punches and dies are manufactured with high-speed steel for long-lasting durability and resistance to galling. Salvagnini tooling is available for station sizes of 6mm to 90 x 90mm.

Wilson Tool International, a specialist in tooling for sheet metal fabrication, also recently launched a new version of its website (www.wilsontool.com/spanish) for Spanish-speaking customers. The website offers easy navigation to engaging, meaningful content on tooling solutions and services available locally.

In addition to Spanish, Wilson Tool’s website is available in the following languages: American and British English, French, German and Chinese.
