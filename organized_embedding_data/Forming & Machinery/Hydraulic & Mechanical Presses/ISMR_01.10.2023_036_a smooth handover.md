# A SMOOTH HANDOVER

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Retiring and new Timesavers managing directors, Paul Hartendorp and Frans Huvers, outline their hopes, vision and views of the company, the future and global market trends to ISMR.

ISMR SAYS:

“A new 12 series rotary brush deburring machine, as well as updated machinery models with new features for deburring; finishing; edge radiusing and laser oxide/heavy slag removal, will be showcased at Blechexpo 2023 in Stuttgart."

Timesavers International BV is a manufacturer of industrial wide belt grinding machines, with customers all over the world. Starting in 1939 as Linden Machine factory in Rotterdam with a focus on woodworking machines, it moved into metalworking machines in the late 1970s. In 1965, the company moved to Goes in the province of Zeeland in the Netherlands and merged with Timesavers USA in 1996 to become Timesavers International. Today, the company is part of U.S. holding group; InCompass, Manufactured Growth Solutions.

After 27 years of working at Timesavers International, 23 of them as managing director, Paul Hartendorp retired at the end of October. Frans Huvers, his successor, has picked up the baton and will be meeting customers, visitors and partners on Timesavers' stand number 5101 in Hall 5 at the forthcoming Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany, from 7-10 November 2023. He will be introducing a new rotary brush, dry deburring machine at the event, as well as updated machinery models with new features for deburring; finishing; edge radiusing and laser oxide/heavy slag removal.

Frans Huvers will also steer the company through its next phase of growth as it operates in various markets around the world. He aims to increase its market share as well as enlarge its existing markets. Both men sat down with ISMR to outline their hopes, vision and views of the company, the future and global market trends.

## Strategic targets

Paul Hartendorp, a dynamic entrepreneur, has always run Timesavers International as an SME family business.

"The fantastic American holding company has always given us room to develop and expand. Where else would you experience that?" he grinned. He believes that the timing is right for him to retire, leaving the company "in great hands and with numerous opportunities ahead." He has certainly left it in good shape, with a predicted 10% increase in turnover to around 34 million euros (compared to last year) and more percentage increases anticipated in 2025 as the company pursues further growth in the United States.

"I will never forget these years, and I cherish all the memories I have gained here," he told ISMR.

His successor, Frans Huvers, is a technician and engineer with a lot of international experience, including various management positions at machine-tool companies in the Netherlands. He started as a structural engineer, with Phillips' lighting division, on very thin sheet-metal deep drawing for contacts and battery housings. He then took over responsibility for the outsourcing of Phillips' engineering and lamp production machinery before moving onto management roles with various machine-tool manufacturing and engineering companies. He has known Timesavers for some time, having bought one of its deburring machines as a past customer. Like the company and most of its employees, he is a true Zeeuw (originally from Zeeland).

"Initially, I will be familiarising myself with the company and its activities and securing what has been achieved to date. We will then need to plan, roadmap and roll out the expansion in our production that will be needed to meet existing and new customer demands worldwide. To stay ahead of the competition, we need to strengthen our position and plan on value creation and innovation in all aspects of the business especially in products, services and processes. The third spearhead is to strive for excellence in order realization; manufacturing; logistics; service and development. These are all the big targets that I need to hit," Frans Huvers told ISMR.

Huvers will also be the first managing director of Timesavers International to be responsible for the worldwide sales of Timesavers International BV-produced machines. A few years ago, the company started producing its European rotary brush products at its factory in Goes for the U.S. market. Demand for these machines from the United States has since rocketed and Huvers plans to make these machines available for all U.S. dealers as well as customers worldwide.

All the company's different machine ranges have never been introduced in America before and he now sees the opportunity to do this.

"One of my major priorities will be to ensure that the ensuing production demand is fulfilled by our colleagues in Goes. The North American continent has around 40 metal dealers and this constitutes both a huge undertaking and a huge opportunity. It will also put greater demands on our production capacity in the Netherlands," continued Frans Huvers.

Huvers will therefore be focusing on streamlining and improving production in Goes to ready the company for new demands globally, including from several regions in the Far East.

## New launches at Blechexpo

At Blechexpo in Stuttgart this November, Timesavers will showcase its new, small 12RB series deburring machine with three stations and a working width of 300mm.

"It is the smallest machine on the market with rotary brush technology and we are the first to bring such a machine onto the market. We already have the larger 22, 32 and 42 machines in our product portfolio. This new machine will incorporate most of the features of the larger machines," Frans Huvers told ISMR.

Another innovation for Blechexpo is focused on minimising unnecessary energy consumption while maximising the simplicity, flexibility and connectivity of its machines. New features for the Timesavers' 42 series deburring; finishing; edge radiusing and laser oxide/heavy slag removal machine will also be rolled out for its 22 and 32 series machines. These include new frontages for the machines, providing all types of additional information for the operator including operational status and a more aesthetically pleasing appearance. The smart design includes a screen with a live view of the machine's exit. This is to ensure safer operations and reduce operator mistakes.

The new 12RB series deburrer will be launched at Blechexpo 2023 in Stuttgart, Germany.
