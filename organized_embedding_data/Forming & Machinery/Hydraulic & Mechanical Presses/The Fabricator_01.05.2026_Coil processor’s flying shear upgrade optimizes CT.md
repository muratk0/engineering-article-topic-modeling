# Coil processor’s flying shear upgrade optimizes CTL line performance

[TARİH: 01.05.2026 The Fabricator]

Applications

SITUATION

In today’s steel processing environment—driven by higher material performance, batch variability, and constant pressure on productivity—cutto-length (CTL) lines must deliver stable throughput, tight tolerances, and high equipment availability.

Within a CTL line, the flying shear is one of the most critical sections, as it directly impacts overall line productivity. Processing of heavy-gauge and high-strength steels increases mechanical loads, so a purpose-designed heavy-duty system is required that features properly dimensioned structural design and advanced control systems.

For this reason, downstream processors and steel service centers are increasingly focusing on targeted retrofit projects involving the upgrade of key components and machines within existing lines, with the goal of improving overall equipment effectiveness, increasing reliability, and reducing unplanned downtime and maintenance costs.

Netherlands-based Vogten Staal BV, a processor of hot-rolled products from coil, recently decided to do the same. The company developed a revamping initiative aimed at improving the reliability and performance of its cutting section, which plays a strategic role in ensuring process stability and overall productivity.

RESOLUTION

The company took installation of a new Flytronic flying shear from FIMIGroup, engineered to meet the specific production requirements of the company’s existing leveling and CTL line.

Made for heavy-gauge and high-strength steel applications, it handles steel in coil widths from 500 to 2,150 mm and thicknesses from 2.0 to 20.0 mm. The shear processes sheet lengths of 1,000 to 20,000 mm at up to 45 m/min.

The flying shear upgrade has allowed Vogten Staal to achieve a more stable and reliable cutting process, reducing operational issues and contributing to increased overall line efficiency.
