# Punching and shearing

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

BY COMBINING punching and shearing in one machine, Prima Power has set new standards in productivity and efficiency with its SGe5 Combi punch and right-angle shear. Utilising servo electric motors for very low power consumption and low noise levels (73.3dB compared with 84dB for a hydraulic machine), the concept is to operate without human intervention from the blank to automated sorting of the finished parts into up to 12 different bins.

With the option of part sorting and picking, up to 12 different component types are sorted along conveyors, which deliver them one by one into separate bins, while larger parts are recovered onto a table at the end of the conveyors.

“Suitable for short runs of components, which is becoming an increasingly common demand from customers, the SGe5 enables the mixing of different components on one sheet. For largely rectangular parts, the result can be zero or virtually zero material wastage as the right-angle shearing can split parts in a single stroke without the need for a skeleton. The shearing operation results in a very high quality edge without any nibbling marks,” Prima Power told ISMR.
