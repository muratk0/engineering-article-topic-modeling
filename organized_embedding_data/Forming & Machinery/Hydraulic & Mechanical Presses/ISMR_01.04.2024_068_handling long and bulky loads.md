# Handling long and bulky loads

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

“If manufacturers want to make better use of their storage space, speed up handling procedures and boost productivity and safety, they can visit our stand G15 in Hall 6 at Wire and Tube in Düsseldorf, Germany, for advice on how to achieve this," specialist forklift manufacturer, Combilift, told /SMR.

Established in Ireland in 1998 and with exports to over 85 countries, the company is a key player in the global materials handling sector. Its product range includes multidirectional forklifts and sideloaders; narrow aisle articulated trucks; pedestrianoperated models and straddle carriers which can move loads weighing 100 tonnes or more. The exhibits at this year's Wire and Tube event reflect the company's ability to provide solutions for the varied requirements of the tube and pipe sector.

To mark its 25th year in business in 2023, Combilift launched five new models (some of which will be on the stand in Düsseldorf) showcasing its new eco, hi-vis, green corporate livery.

"The Combi-CB70E, an addition to our growing electric range, boasts the distinction of being the shortest 7t capacity multidirectional counterbalance truck on the market, and ensures the versatile

space-saving handling of long and bulky loads, even in very confined spaces. The newly developed optional Auto Swivel Seat automatically engages and swivels the seat and armrest 15º to the right or left, to accord with the direction of travel selected by the operator. This reduces driver strain, particularly when travelling in reverse," explained Combilift.
