# Tube bending titan

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

A GIGANTIC FULLY electric multiple-radius tube bending machine, featuring transport boost technology, was showcased recently at FABTECH 2018 in Atlanta (USA) and delivered to an automotive customer in the U.S. at the end of last year. The CNC 220 E TB MR manufactured by Schwarze-Robitec, can process complex tube geometries with diameters of up to eight inches.

Schwarze-Robitec developed the tailor-made tube- and pipe-bending machine for one of its U.S. customers active in the automotive industry. Short cycle times and high speeds, as well as precision and flexibility, were required. The CNC 220 E TB MR processes tube lengths of up to 13 feet. A vertical height-adjustable pressure die moves to the required bending level in each individual case. This allows the requisite bending forces to be gauged to consistently apply the correct contact pressure to the tubes and pipes to be bent.

"Used in combination with individually adjustable tools, the multilevel technology integrated into the CNC 220 E TB MR allows tubes to be bent simply and precisely with very short lengths between bends," commented the German manufacturer.

The machine's NxG control system also contributes to the rapid cycle times. By improving time, travel stroke and speed on all CNC axes, production times can be reduced by up to 35%, said Schwarze-Robitec. The bend former and swing arm can be operated separately, enabling users to produce a diverse range of tube geometries while adjusting and positioning them during the bending process.

Schwarze-Robitec has already prepared the CNC 220 E TB MR for future Industry 4.0 developments. For example, the smart remote maintenance solution with several diagnosis functions is integrated into the gigantic machine. For further information, see www.schwarze-robitec.com
