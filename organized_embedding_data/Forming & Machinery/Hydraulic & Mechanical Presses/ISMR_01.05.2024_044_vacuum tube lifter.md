# Vacuum tube lifter

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

The new FIPALIFTswift vacuum tube lifter from FIPA GmbH lifts loads of up to 40kg and is used in a wide range of applications. It is designed for the rapid and frequent handling of loads, making heavy-duty work easier.

“Users now benefit from even faster handling. The new vacuum tube lifter is available in an ergonomic one-handed version. The user-friendly control handle enables intuitive, fast and smooth work, without the need for staff training," outlined the manufacturer. "Almost any object can be handled with the wide range of load-handling attachments from the range, such as the FORMHAND, a load hook or customised grippers with vacuum cups."

Right: The new FIPALIFTswift vacuum tube lifter.

Inset: The FORMHAND load-handling attachment.

At LogiMAT in Stuttgart, the trade show for internal material flow, FIPA presented a wide range of products for logistics. The focus was on lifting technology with the FIPALIFTswift and FIPALIFTmax vacuum tube lifters. Other highlights included the new Cobot Smart Gripper CSG, an area gripper specially designed for use with cobots and for palletising cartons, and the vacuum cups for bag handling which are optimised for handling bags and rapid "pick and place."

FIPA specialises in vacuum, gripping and lifting technology. FIPA GmbH was founded at its headquarters near Munich, Germany, in 1985 and employs around 100 members of staff worldwide. It also has a global presence, with its own subsidiary in the USA, a representative office in China and a worldwide partner network.■
