# THE PUNCHING FACTOR

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

ISMR profiles a selection of some of the latest new developments in punching, and combination punching, technologies

A punch press, used to cut holes in material, can be small and manually operated and hold one simple die set, or be very large with a multi-station turret and a much larger and complex die set. The choice of hole punched can be as simple as a circle or rectangle right through to special shapes to suit a specific cut-out design. By using a combination of single hits and overlapping geometries, complex sheet metal component shapes can be produced.

Recent innovations in punching technology have included more agile combination systems from a range of manufacturers, integrating, for example, laser cutting and bending operations into the punch processing. These are interesting advances, particularly as punching technology has traditionally been a more cost-effective process than, for example, laser processing operations.

In this article, we profile a selection of some of the latest new developments in punching, and combination punching, technologies.

## New punching innovations

Japanese machine tool specialist, Amada, has introduced a punch–fibre laser combination, LC-2515 C1 AJ, for flexible, eco-friendly production. Featuring a servo-electric drive and fibre laser technology, it has optimised quality, speed and energy efficiency. It also allows interruption of an ongoing production programme with, for example, an urgent job. It is designed to be highly productive, energy and cost-saving. The machine tool features a light-shielded open table and provides greater differentiation in the blanking process.

“With a compact footprint and ergonomic safety enclosure, this revolutionary machine is equipped with an Amada fibre laser beam engine and a multi-purpose tool turret for faster processing speeds, fewer secondary operations and lower running costs,” Amada told ISMR.

Amada’s turret punch press, the EM-3612 ZRB, has an automatic tool change system and can be connected with the ID TOGU tool grinding machine. It also boasts simplified tool setting for low skill operation and needs no repositioning for punching/tapping 3000 x 1500mm material.

Baust presented new coil-driven rotary punches with variable tool concepts at last year’s EuroBLECH exhibition. Its engineers designed two new punch cassettes that are flexible, easy to handle, productive, economical and use no motors.

Besides the coil drive principle, the new BRV features an easy product change, the variable tool concept. A pressurised air cylinder opens the tools wide enough to easily load a new coil/strip or remove a flange-mounted tool set and replace it with a set for a new punch pattern. The tools stay synchronised even when the tools are opened. The operator does not have to worry about tool alignment when mounting the new set. Now tool changes can be made in minutes. A pneumatic cylinder closes the tools even when the new strip is running at 60m/min. or more.

Baykal is currently one of the top ten sheet metal working machine manufacturers in the global market with manufacturing capacity for 3000 machines per year. It is one of the oldest machine tool companies in Turkey, founded in 1903. Its BPM-T punch press with turret can process large sheet metal sizes at high axis speeds and beat frequency. Automation can also be fitted for continuous operation.

Dalcos (the coil punching division of Dallan) launched a new coil-fed punching and laser cutting system at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover. The combo coil fed punch-laser has been designed to meet higher requirements for efficiency, productivity and flexibility in today’s manufacturing environments.

“The machine consists of an electric punching machine EXN 1000 attached to a laser cutting zone, ideal for making complex notches, special perforations and variable forms – in practice, a ‘laser tool’ that adds flexibility to the traditional productivity of the Dalcos EXN,” explained the manufacturer.

“One of the great advantages of this combo system is its flexibility – it is also possible to use just the punching or laser cutting zone, depending upon the specific process of the part and the available tools. The ELXN system can also include systems for fume treatment, connection for bottles for the laser gases, chiller etc,” explained Dalcos.

Turkish manufacturer Ermaksan has a range of punch press systems. Its ETP CNC turret punch press is designed to punch up to 6mm thick sheets. It is available in two versions – one with 33 and one with 27 stations. The 33-station model offers indexable D stations and may include 8A, 3B or 1C multi-tools upon request. Up to 54 tools can be housed and a hardened steel-coated tool adaptor system ensures a maintenance-free long working life. Several forming operations are possible with the functional tools perforating different diameters and shapes.

Its RPP rotative punch press is designed to allow economical sheet loading from three sides. Sheet processing can be done multi-directionally and flexibly for sheets of up to 4mm thickness. Ermaksan’s CPP (Combined Plasma Punch press) series combines HiFocus sensitive plasma cutting and hydraulic punching in one system. Cutting and perforating processes can be performed at high speed and accuracy, and the machine tool is optimised for mass production. Its FPP Fix Punch Press can handle multi-directional sheet processing on material of up to 6mm in thickness.

GEKA recently showcased its new GAMMA TRACTION 100 line, which processes angled sections with a traction system. This version is designed to satisfy the needs of punching, marking and cutting medium-sized angled sections.

CAM specialist JETCAM (www.jetcam.com) has released its v18 Expert software with new interface, nesting engine and automatic sheet processing for punching machines. The new user interface draws much of its styling from JETCAM’s CrossTrack product, and focuses on reducing the mouse movements a user has to perform during day-to-day operations. All file management and tabular data views have been improved with new controls, and general performance enhancements have been made throughout the interface.

Automatic Sheet Processing, available for all Expert Premium users, enables completely automatic skeleton destruction, unloading of parts, along with common punching/shearing. Version 18 includes many other features and enhancements across the application, including new Right Angle Shear logic enhancements and new routing functionality. Expert Lite users now also benefit from functionality previously only available in higher versions, such as CAD filtering.

As Industry 4.0 becomes increasingly relevant to the sheet metalworking industry, LVD has responded with a technology that gives shop managers critical production information in real-time. TOUCH-i4 is an industrial strength Windows-based tablet that provides an overview of the entire fabrication workshop. TOUCH-i4 generates and presents the live information needed to manage networked LVD laser, punching and bending machines.

The LVD Group is a leading manufacturer of a comprehensive range of sheet metal/plate working machine tools and software solutions, including laser cutting systems, punch presses, press brakes, guillotine shears and mid-level automation systems, integrated and supported by its CADMAN® PC based Windows® compatible software.

LVD provides the economy of P-Series punching machines to the high performance, full featured V-Series models. Punching machines are offered in stand-alone, automatic load/unload and full material handling configurations. Its new Strippit PX-Series, including the PX-1530, offers the flexibility to punch, form, bend and tap on a single machine to efficiently complete multiple processes, including the processing of complex, three-dimensional parts. The Strippit PX-1530 can bend small boxes, brackets, knockouts, louvres and countersinks with flange heights of up to 75mm. See www.lvdgroup.com

Mate Precision Tooling offers a range of punch press tooling. Mate’s new Versadie™ insert slitting die reduces material cost and waste for slitting operations by punching closely to the clamps, which is crucial when maximum sheet usage is required. This new type of die also allows users to replace die inserts without replacing the entire slitting die. Mate special assemblies are designed with high performance features for forming card guides, embosses, threadforms and hinges.

The company also recently introduced Roller Solutions™ tooling. This new tooling family allows fabricators to quickly create rib, shear, offset and pincher features in sheet metal. Compared to traditional methods utilising progressive forming tools, Mate Roller Solutions dramatically increases productivity when creating detailed metal forms, it told ISMR. Designed for thick-turret B-station applications, all four Mate Roller Solutions tooling handle high-speed applications – equal to the programmed table travel speed.

Meusburger has added the E 55301 mounting sleeve from pre-toughened tool steel 1.2312 to its selection of punching tools. The new product is complementary to the E 5530 bottle-neck cutting punch, which was specially developed for high punching and retracting forces. The special shape of the punch head prevents compression and the breaking of the head. The E 55301 mounting sleeve allows for easier insertion of the bottle neck contour into the punch retainer plate. Only a cylinder recess is required for the installation of the cutting punch.

PASS Stanztechnik AG is one of the largest punching tool manufacturers and specialists in special solutions for punching tools (TRUMPF, Salvagnini and thick turret systems). Its punch tooling portfolio includes form/blanking; cluster; embossing; marking; thread rolling and knockout tooling. It recently developed a new tool for producing threads – PS multi-thread with six positions which can be used for sheet thicknesses of up to 8mm (depending upon thread size).

The PivaPunch punching and cutting centre for coils, from Finnish manufacturer Pivatic, produces blanks and punched blanks which are cut both to length and to width straight from coil. Parts are produced according to the job orders created by the ERP system. Punching the second nest and cutting to width of the first nest takes place simultaneously – in a single pass, straight from the coil.

In the punching station, two parts are punched at the same time using thick turret tools set in two tool cassettes. Dedicated press tools can be used for perforation, standard hole patterns, large holes and for forming. Pivapunch can also be integrated with Pivatic’s PivaBend into one production line where finished parts are produced straight from the coil in one run. Parts can be roll formed where applicable.

Prima Power’s LPe6f punch laser cell delivers flexible and automatic production to customers. Indicators International recently invested in a Prima Power LPe6f servo-electric punch/fibre laser combination cell, and a Finn Power E6 servo-electric punching cell. Prima Power’s PSBB (short for punching, right angle shearing, buffering and bending) is a fully automatic manufacturing line for processing sheet into ready-bent components. The current offering includes many improvements and, with the new Shear Brilliance as the punching – shearing cell, raises manufacturing speed and productivity.

By combining punching and shearing in one machine, Prima Power has set new standards with its SGe5 Combi punch and right angle shear. Utilising servo electric motors for very low power consumption and low noise levels of 73.3dB, compared with 84dB for a hydraulic machine, the concept is to operate without human intervention from the blank to the automated sorting of the finished parts into up to 12 different bins. PunchBend is Prima Power’s new compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials.

Radpunch, from Radan, is software for programming punching machines. With over 35 years of punching experience, Radan drives thousands of punching machine tools worldwide.

“Radnest extends Radpunch functionality to provide true shaped nests that produce high utilisation, manufacturable nests from sheets, off-cuts and remnants delivering substantial savings in material,” the manufacturer, Vero Software, explained. “Designed to seamlessly integrate with Radprofile, the Radan punch/profile solution delivers optimisation for punch, profile and combination machine tools. This combination will expand with a customer to program all their future punch, laser, plasma, router and combination machine tool investments from one system.”

With its S4+P4 line, proposed in a new compact version, Salvagnini has designed an effective manufacturing flow of bent and punched parts aimed at increasing flexibility and production. The S4Xe+P4Xe is a Salvagnini solution for punching, shearing and bending sheet without any interruption in the process from sheet to finished product, and without any intermediate handling of the semi-finished parts.

With its new smart FlexCell, Salvagnini proposes a solution composed of stand-alone machines controlled by proprietary software, designed for efficient cell management and increased production effectiveness and ease of use.
