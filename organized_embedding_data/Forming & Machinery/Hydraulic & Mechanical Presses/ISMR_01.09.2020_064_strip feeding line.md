# Strip feeding line

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

SARONNI HAS BEEN manufacturing feed and cut-to-length lines since 1950 and it is one of the global leaders in coil processing technology. With around 4,500 systems installed worldwide by key OEMs and Tier manufacturers in the automotive and household appliance industries, Saronni aims to be a partner ‘for demanding customers looking for automated systems with high technological content’.

Its combi-type feeding line with automatic set-up is suitable for processing both aluminium and high-strength steel. It processes and feeds coils through the line to produce car body blanks. The line features a 6-roll leveller with automatic roll pack change and a telescopic microfeeder. Coil change and set-up are fully automatic.

Maximum coil weight is 30 tons, while maximum strip length on the line is 2150mm. The line can process strip thicknesses of between 0.5 and 3.5mm material, while maximum blank length is 5200mm.

“This line can process two materials with completely different requirements, namely aesthetic aluminum (the strip surface must not show any signs of scratches or shading) and high-strength steel. Given its extreme mechanical characteristics, high-strength steel requires special arrangements to be made during the decoiling phase and a leveller with a dedicated cassette to allow high penetration of the leveller rollers,” commented Saronni.
