# Smart drive solutions

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Baumüller, a motion control and automation specialist, will participate in FABTECH 2023 in Chicago, USA (11-14 September 2023). Baumueller-Nuermont Corp, Baumüller's subsidiary in the U.S., will present its latest technological solutions for sheet-metalworking machines on booth D41454.

The booth will focus on the overall Baumüller solution for the automation of servo eccentric presses. The machine cycle time, robustness, energy use and environmental compatibility can be optimised with high-performance direct drives and the compatible control unit. Baumüller has many years of experience providing solutions for servo eccentric presses.

“With ProSimulation, we offer simulation models of our drive components. The digital twins provide many advantages, especially during the engineering phase: development time, and therefore time to market, is shortened maximizing reliability and performance. In addition, we can undertake the complete design of a motion control solution for a sheet-metalworking machine," the company told ISMR.

"This offers numerous advantages, particularly for complex applications such as servo eccentric presses. It enables the selection of an efficient and resource-saving drive package. For example, the whole drive topology can be mapped in the software. This consists of the non-linear transfer ratio of the eccentric mechanics, gearing, high-torque motors, servo-drive technology and energy storage in conjunction with dynamic movement and force profiles. The simulation creates transparency in the power and energy profiles and describes the charging and discharging processes in detail. This enables efficient dimensioning of the energy store, and therefore the connected system load can be reduced. This saves resources, considerably relieves the power supply system and reduces the machine's footprint," it added.

The manufacturer's DST2-series high-torque motors provide the power for pressing processes. The motors can adapt to the press's requirements using different shaft and flange options.

At FABTECH 2023, Baumüller will present the b maXX 5000 product family for high-performance applications. The servo drives offer compact design, safety functions and an energy recovery option. Intelligent energy management and the use of capacitors to store energy can reduce the connected system load, saving energy and costs. The Box-PC b maXX PCC-04 machine control has been designed as a flexible and scalable platform. With its b maXX PLC mc control platform, Baumüller combines the properties of industrial PCs and PLC controls to optimise machine control. It also offers suitable HMI panels with touch display for user-friendly machine operation.■

Baumüller offers a complete system for servo eccentric presses consisting of the engineering, hardware and software.
