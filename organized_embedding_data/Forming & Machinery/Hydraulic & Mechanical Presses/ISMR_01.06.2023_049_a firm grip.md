# A firm grip

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Rivit brought half a century of history and experience to Milan for the LAMIERA sheetmetal exhibition this May. In addition to a comprehensive catalogue of fasteners and tools dedicated to the roofing, construction and industrial sectors, Rivit also offers a system for monitoring and verifying fasteners that is based on digital control. It is designed for companies of any size i.e. from artisans to Industry-4.0.

ControlRiv, the control system designed by the Emilia-based Rivit company, is designed to eliminate errors in fastening operations. The company describes ControlRiv as a “4.0 fastening system." At the LAMIERA exhibition, visitors were able to test the system and call on Rivit's customised consultancy services. One of the exhibition's themes was 'The Fastener Industry'.

“The system consists of a Primary module, which acts as the system's Master, and a series of Replica modules, so that up to 12 riveting tools can be connected and managed simultaneously. This brings numerous advantages to the production line, which is therefore more precise and repetitive. The ControlRiv makes it possible to identify the optimum curve for blind rivets and rivet nut fastening. In case of errors it stops all operations, allowing the operator to intervene to correct a fastener that is not in line with the required standard. This also helps to avoid costs related to any non-conformity," explained the manufacturer.
