# Servopresse equipment

[TARİH: 01.05.2017 ISMR]

Servopresse can build single machines, compact lines and special lines which feature:

- Decoilers: Single or double, with or without motors, and hydraulic self-centring expansion.
- Straighteners: Normal or feed straighteners, comprising a cast iron drafting head and hardened and ground steel straightening rolls.
- Electronic roll feeders: Suitable for automated lines.
- Traditional lines: These feature decoilers and straighteners to be used with an independent feeder.
- Compact lines: These include integrated decoilers and feeder straighteners.
- Special lines: These are designed to meet sophisticated working requirements such as zig-zag feeding lines; straightener cradles; bar feeders; complete cutting lines; micro-perforation and embossing coil line as well as automatic multi-disc cutting lines from coil.

Lara Cecchi, Marketing Manager, Servopresse Srl

Set up in 1966 as a producer of fast four-column mechanical presses, Faspar has expanded its range with the design and production of complete turnkey lines and equipment for the processing of coil and sheet metal. Faspar lines are able to process various types of material, although the company is increasingly specialising in lines which can process difficult materials such as non-ferrous and niche metals (galvanised; pre-painted; stainless steel; aluminium; titanium; copper; brass; titanium zinc; high-strength; magnetic sheet and tinplate).

Its 8,000 m² production plant turns out lines for longitudinal cutting; flattening and length cutting; surface finishing; coil punching and bending; flattening under tension; gate and foam panel lines and special lines. Over 300 Faspar machines are installed in Italy; Europe; Russia; Asia; North and South America and Africa with important customers who typically operate as steel service centres, or in the automotive, white goods and carpentry construction sectors.

ISMR: How did Servopresse and Faspar-Cimsa become partners?

LC: Of particular interest to us, to strengthen our marketing and production capacity, was the acquisition of the Cimsa brand by Faspar, a leading Italian company in the punching line and coil-bending sector.

This acquisition, in particular, enabled Faspar-Cimsa to start a partnership with Servopresse to jointly develop production lines for the processing of coil sheet metal. In fact, INPLAF had already installed some Cimsa equipment, powered by Servopresse lines, and, having learned of the acquisition of the Cimsa brand by Faspar and knowing that Faspar had also acquired specialised former Cimsa personnel, it welcomed the opportunity to acquire a line featuring the same technology that it had already installed when it turned to Faspar for its new blanking line.

Compared with the other two blanking lines that the customer bought, this new line is different. This is not only because of its new and more powerful programming software but also because of the concept, which is based on the selection of standard components. This is the result of a careful re-design of the machines, to increase the reliability of the line and to facilitate and speed up the retrieval of spare parts.

This was how the first partnership between Servopresse and Faspar-Cimsa came about, which led to the development of an advanced blanking line with a high degree of automation and flexibility. The line is made up of punching and shearing stations, using Faspar-Cimsa technology, whilst the coil line features are produced by Servopresse.

## INPLAF blanking line The main elements of the blanking line installed at INPLAF are:

Mobile motorised decoiler group with powered straightening group plus bench with ball recirculation axial sliding guides; a multi-head press with eight-cylinder configuration (recess 850); cutter group drive unit (recess 850); rotating station (recess 850) and a shearing station (cutting length 820mm, maximum sheet metal thickness 4mm). The mobile motorised decoiler group has a maximum capacity of 8,000kg per coil, the width of which can reach 806mm, with maximum outer diameter of 1,600mm and an inner minimum/maximum diameter of 470/560mm. The pressure arm is hydraulically driven and the loading saddle with jack is fixed to the base with the hydraulic shift of the coil.

The powered straightening group can process maximum widths of 806mm for minimum/ maximum thicknesses of 0.6/4mm (tensile strength 600N/mm²). The group consists of 11- counter-rolled type rollers (80mm diameter) and the head is opened via hydraulic cylinder to facilitate the introduction of the belt as well as the cleaning of the rollers. Straightening adjustment is done by hydraulic motors. A front hydraulic shield supports and guides the head of the coil in the straightening machine without manual operator intervention. Mounted self-centring guides enable incoming and outgoing belt alignment.

The multi-head press has an 8-cylinder configuration, six of which are of 20 tonnes and two are 30 tonnes, which bear cutter groups with the following diameters (four with maximum diameter of 31.2mm, two with maximum diameter of 50.8mm and four with maximum diameter of 88.9mm). The shearing station, with 1,450mm worktop, is configured with a conveyor belt for scrap + 45º collection belt; electrical equipment with control station; hydraulic equipment; 3-axis CNC SIEMENS 840 D and brushless motor.
