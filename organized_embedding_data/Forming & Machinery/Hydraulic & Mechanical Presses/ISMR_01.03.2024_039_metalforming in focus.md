# Metalforming in focus

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

Group Rhodes, a leading manufacturer of metal and composite forming machinery, will be demonstrating one of its metalforming presses in the MMMA Metal Working Village at MACH 2024 (15-19 April 2024 at the NEC in Birmingham, UK). Visitors will be able to find its stand 599 in Hall 6.

“The Rhodes Interform product on display was designed and manufactured in-house at our Wakefield (UK) factory as part of a full turnkey solution to produce a bespoke twin press facility for forging forklift truck components. The entire project, which consists of two Rhodes Interform multi-axis presses, a complete robotic handling line and an integrated conveying system, can process the largest forks at a rate of one part per 70 seconds," said the press specialist.

Group Rhodes will also be discussing the global patents that it has registered for its Superplastic Forming & Diffusion Bonding Technology (SPF/DB), including the territories of China, India, The United States and Europe. This solution is applied extensively to the lightweighting of components in the aerospace and automotive industries.

Operations Director, James Gascoigne, commented: "We are looking forward to attending MACH 2024, which will give us the opportunity to demonstrate our latest innovations in metalforming technology. This will be our first exhibition since we secured international patents for Superplastic Forming & Diffusion Bonding and will be a great chance for our team to discuss how this technology works and its potential applications globally.

"Given that our 19th-century patents were for improvements to the steam hammer, it is amazing to think that our most recent innovations are helping to form components for spacecraft! We are very proud to showcase these innovations, especially in our 200th anniversary year," he continued.

Alongside its SPF/DB technology, Group Rhodes' personnel will also discuss the company's portfolio of machinery for the hot and cold forming of metals and composites.

The four companies that make up Group Rhodes offer extensive capabilities including the design, development and manufacture of bespoke equipment to serve the metalforming, composite forming, heavy ceramics and material handling sectors. The parent company of Rhodes Interform, Group Rhodes, boasts a 200-year metalforming history. The company has won Queen's Awards for both Innovation and International Trade in recent years.■
