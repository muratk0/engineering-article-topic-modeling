# Peak Performer hits new highs

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

KOHLER HAS COMPREHENSIVELY upgraded its Peak Performer part levelling machine to provide a significant boost to the levelling capability of the machine family. Assuming the yield strength of the material is unchanged, the machine can now level, to the accustomed degree of precision, sheets that are up to 50% thicker than before - a significant development in the market.

As a leading global manufacturer of levelling solutions for the metalworking industry, KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH, based in the Black Forest area of Germany, also offers turnable levelling rollers (with extended service life) for the partleveller and a reworked drive concept. With its GAP Control for electro-mechanical levelling and its intelligent four-point overload protection system, the Peak Performer part levelling machine is designed for precision and efficiency in sheetmetal processing operations.

KOHLER has optimised the drive concept for part levelling machines in the Peak Performer 80P and higher series. The conventional approach, in which the drive shafts are driven by a single motor has been superseded by one in which several geared motors are located on the levelling rollers, which they then drive directly.

"This new drive concept reduces the extent to which the levelling rollers slip on the material as it is being levelled, resulting in a smoother surface finish. The overall tension in the machine is reduced, producing less wear and a demonstrably higher level of energy efficiency in the drive train. Another factor that benefits from the direct input of power into the levelling rollers is that higher material cross-sections can be levelled. The working range is significantly increased, particularly for wider sheets. The Peak Performer 80P can level S355-grade sheet steel that is up to 14mm thick and 1500mm wide," said the manufacturer.

A multi-finger touch display is now included as standard with the new Peak Performer generation. The "Expert Calculation System" simplifies and speeds up the material-specific adjustment of the part levelling machine. As an optional extra, KOHLER offers a barcode scanner that can be used to identify the parts to be levelled using, for example, the barcode printed on the job documents. The Peak Performer then automatically retrieves the associated levelling parameters and adjusts the axes accordingly.

## Bending innovations

BLECHEXPO WAS THE showcase selected by Salvagnini to present the global debut of its new, compact P2L-2120 panel bender capable of bending material of up to 2,180mm in length and 203mm in height.

"The new P2lean-2120 incorporates all the most evolved bending technology that, integrated with new technical devices, aligns this machine to sustainable development milestones that we have identified to make our solutions essential tools for the Industry 4.0 factory. P2lean-2120 is equipped with an ABA automatic blankholder and CLAN negative auxiliary blades that increase its versatility and allow production in kit and in sequence with masked time tool set-up (meaning no change-over time)," explained the Italian manufacturer.

P2lean-2120, like all panel benders in the Salvagnini range, implements MAC2.0 adaptive technology. The panel bender detects, in masked time, any variations in the material being processed. If relevant, they are automatically compensated for by the adjustment of the blade movements, guaranteeing constant quality of the bend even with variations in the material in the batch, and eliminating waste even for limited quantity production batches."

The global debut of its B3.ATA press brake, equipped with upper and lower automatic tool adjustment devices and MAC technology, was also celebrated at Blechexpo. B3.ATA is a native 4.0 hybrid press brake which can also be monitored remotely. B3 was equipped with the new iCON user interface, which was presented for the first time at Blechexpo. The ATA system eliminates re-tooling of the press and changes/ automatically adjusts the length of the tool in approximately five seconds, allowing for kit production even in press brakes.

"The new compact AMS angle measuring system uses an optic laser system to take more exact measurements. Our SCrowning system regulates and compensates for, in real-time, the centring value of the press brake based on the actual behaviour of the material. So, while SCrowning keeps the angle constant for the entire length of the bend, AMS brings the bend to the right angle. Thanks to the brand new TFC the closed-circuit control of structure deflections, bend consistency is autonomously guaranteed even with changes in the length and resistance of the material, and all this without the intervention of an operator," concluded the manufacturer.
