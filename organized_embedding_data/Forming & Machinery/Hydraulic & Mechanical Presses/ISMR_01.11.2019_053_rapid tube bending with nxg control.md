# Rapid tube bending with NxG control

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

WITH ITS NEW fully electric CNC 25 E-TB MR, Schwarze-Robitec has delivered a compact tube bending machine to China.

"For the first time, Changzhou Liangxu Vehicle Accessories Co., Ltd., is now able to bend smaller tube diameters rapidly and as part of a fully electric process – thanks to the NxG control system on the tube bending system. The Chinese automotive industry manufactures injection tubes and radiator outlet pipes for cars and trucks and is the main supplier of tube systems for China's third-largest truck manufacturer," explained the German tube specialist.

Schwarze-Robitec recently put a new CNC 25- E TB MR into operation at Changzhou Liangxu's headquarters in Changzhou on the Yangtze River in eastern China. The delivered machine can bend steel tubes with a diameter of up to ca. 1- inch and a wall thickness of ca. 0.08 inch as well as stainless steel tubes with a diameter of up to ca. 0.8 inch and a wall thickness of ca. 0.06 inch.

"This means that, for smaller tube diameters, Changzhou Liangxu Vehicle Accessories Co., Ltd., is using a fully electric machine with NxG control for the first time. It offers major benefits: depending upon the tube system, the NxG control system enables up to 50 per cent more output thanks to the simultaneous operation of the axes. The company is therefore preparing itself for the rise in demand brought on by the prolonged boom in China's automotive industry," added Schwarze-Robitec.

The CNC 25 E TB MR has been specially designed to process small tubes. It is available in both left/right bending versions as well as in a rotary variant, with the bending head capable of rotating 180 degrees. Tubes with complex geometries can be bent in one process step.

"Using intelligent scanners and a slim machine bonnet, the machine can be operated without any additional safety fences, allowing for available space to be optimally used. As the electrical technology is integrated into the machine body, the machine requires no additional control cabinet and can therefore be easily moved around the production area if necessary. In addition, the CNC 25 E TB MR is equipped with a freely stored bend former. The bending table and bend former can be operated independently of each other – a particular highlight of all our machines. This feature gives the user much more freedom when bending tubes and simplifies the entire bending process," explained Schwarze-Robitec.

While delivering the new product to Changzhou, Schwarze-Robitec also modified two additional machines and integrated the latest control system.
