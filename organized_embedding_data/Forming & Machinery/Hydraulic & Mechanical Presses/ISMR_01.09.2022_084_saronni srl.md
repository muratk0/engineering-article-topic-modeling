# Saronni Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.saronni.com

“For the latest in coilprocessing technology, visit our stand at EuroBLECH this year.We offer over 70 years of experience, with 1500 customers throughout our history and 4000 lines installed all over the world," Italian manufacturer, Saronni Srl, explained.

The company, which celebrated its 70th anniversary in 2020, produces feeding and cut-to-length lines and is a coil processing specialist. With lines installed all over the world and hundreds of customers active (mainly in the automotive and household appliance sectors), Saronni supplies customers looking for automated systems with high technological content.

Its main products include coil-feeding lines for presses (short and long versions) which incorporate decoilers; straighteners; levellers; feeders etc. It also offers blanking, cut-to-length, embossing and disc-cutting lines.

The manufacturer can adapt standard machines to customer requirements for line customisation and also offers retrofits and upgrades to Saronni equipment. Training, service and spare parts assistance are also supplied.
