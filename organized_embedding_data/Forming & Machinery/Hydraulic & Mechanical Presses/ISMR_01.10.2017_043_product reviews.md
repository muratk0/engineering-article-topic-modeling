Quick die change systems

Fully automatic die change cart for two dies of 40- tons each, die lifters and hydraulic clamping systems

Application with hydraulic removable flexible clamping system, die lifters and bolster extensions for loading and unloading the die

AUTOMATION WITH QUICK Die Change (QDC) systems is an important competitive factor for automotive and traditional manufacturing companies and is becoming increasingly important for small- and medium-sized companies around the world.

With the tremendous increase of new car models and the increased number of Electric Vehicles (EV), the automotive industry is also investing in more flexible and faster solutions to change moulds and dies automatically. The increasing diversity of different products requires flexible production systems to adjust production processes to meet customer needs without compromising on quality

"EAS mould and die change systems have, for many years, offered the industry simple and complete solutions for quick and fast die change on stamping presses as well as for rapid mould change on injection moulding machines," EAS told /SMR. "Changing a 50-ton tool is no longer a time-consuming job of hours, during which the press is not producing any parts, but can be done within a few minutes nowadays and, if required, fully automatically as well.

Die splitter for max. 20-ton dies for opening and maintenance of the die

The EAS strategy is to offer a complete solution from one international source by offering a product portfolio which features hydraulic as well as permanent electric magnetic clamping systems and a complete range of die-lifters to lift and position the tool. Bolster extensions or motorised pre-rollers, on which the tool can be loaded by forklift truck or by crane, are also offered.

For fully automatic tool change, EAS offers diechange tables and die change cars where the controls are combined with the machine controls. Tool weights of up to 200 tons can be handled. Die splitters/inspection units, in which the dies can be opened, turned, inspected and tested, are also part of its portfolio as well as transport cars. See www.EASchangesystems.com
