# Smart machines for pipes

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

At EUROBLECH 2012 (Hall 11, Booth D 54) Tracto-Technik (TT) will present innovative technology for bending and processing pipes and tubes; high-precision measuring systems for quality control and reverse engineering as well as intelligent software solutions for pipe shop automation, workflow optimisation and lifecycle management for piping.

“With our new servo-electric CNC multi-radii pipe bending machine, Tubotron 25 MR, pipes from 4 to 25mm (1'') OD can be rapidly precision bent. The machine has an exposed and compact bending head and enables production of complex pipe figures at a bending speed of 170°/s,” explained the manufacturer to ISMR. “The multi-level tooling concept enables bending of a pipe with different radii or of pipes of different diameter without intermediate tool exchange. The main applications are in aerospace technology as well as in the hydraulic, automotive and furniture industries.”

For pipe end processing, TT will showcase the new mobile Tuboform A, an axial-forming machine for the production of pipes up to 20mm OD in small- and medium-sized series. The forming tools (clamping jaws and forming punch) are individually manufactured according to the job. The unit is suitable for applications such as high-precision flaring of brake pipes or forming of corrugations for hose connections.

Visitors can also experience the Tuboscan S optical measuring system in operation. The tube to be measured is placed on a lighttransmitted surface and consecutively illuminated by several LEDs in different positions. The shadow images created this way are taken by a high-resolution CCD camera and evaluated by TeZetCAD tube software. Geometric tube data can be determined precisely within seconds. The Tuboscan S measuring system is suitable for quality control and bending data adjustment.

With its optical measuring cell, Tuboscan S, pipe figures can be measured precisely within seconds.

Finally Tracto-Technik will demonstrate how to optimise the manufacturing sequences in the pipe shop with the latest version of its Pipefab workflow management software. Using this practice-tailored software solution, all steps of the pipeline lifecycle – ranging from engineering, production, assembly and calculation to maintenance – can be captured and controlled digitally.
