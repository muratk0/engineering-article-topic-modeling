## Timesavers International
## www.timesaversint.com

BOOTH 1116

Timesaver’s top and bottom deburring machine, for the edge rounding and finishing of parts in a single pass, will be showcased at Blechexpo. The oscillating system is equipped with twelve brushes. The six top and six bottom left- and right-rotating grinding flap brushes simultaneously process the parts on both sides.

The manufacturer’s multi-rotating brush machine (for uniformly deburring, rounding edges and finishing parts) can be used for laser-, punched- and machined parts, or parts with pockets/protrusions in all directions. The rotating carousel, equipped with eight left- and rightrotating abrasive flap brushes, processes the parts in all directions, regardless of the fineness of the workpiece edges.

The company’s manual grinder is, it said, a cost-saving alternative to conventional grinding and deburring machines. Its head, consisting of a grinding wheel for pre-grinding and a flap brush, for edge rounding, can be turned by 180 degrees. The head can be moved on the workpiece by the stabilised arm for a uniformly rounded edge. Smaller parts can also be processed.

## WILA B.V.
## www.wila.nl

WILA B.V. has been specialising in tool holders (clamping and crowning), tooling and accessories for more than 80 years, with the goal of reducing the adjustment time of the press brake and to further increase its accuracy.

Two of WILA’s latest innovations, to be demonstrated at Blechexpo, are TIPS (Tooling Identification and Positioning System) and the New Standard Pneumatic tool holders.

“TIPS is an integrated part of the press brake control system and supports tooling identification and continuous monitoring of tooling position. Thanks to this precise recognition, both errors and test bends can be avoided, resulting in a substantial increase in productivity. The New Standard Pneumatic tool holders are based upon a unique and patented self-locking mechanism. Tools are clamped using air pressure. The main advantage of this approach is that tools can be clamped and unclamped very rapidly,” WILA told ISMR.

## InspecVision Ltd.

www.inspecvision.com

InspecVision was formed by sheet metal manufacturers in 2004 to create a complete sheet metal inspection and reverse engineering solution. Its system can automatically inspect or reverse-engineer sheet metal parts at every stage of production – flat, folded, welded or assembled.

Flat parts are measured with its Planar machine which takes up to 40 million measurements in 0.1 seconds. The measurements are then automatically compared to a CAD file to create the inspection report.

For folded and assembled parts, InspecVision has developed the OptiScan. “It is the only 3D scanner in the world which can measure surfaces and edges in 3D. 3D edges are critical when measuring sheet metal parts as their geometry is mostly defined by their edges, not by their surfaces,” commented Dr. Jan Antonis, Managing Director, InspecVision.

Available in a range of flexible configurations, the Planar and OptiScan integrate to create a complete sheet metal inspection solution.

## Goudsmit Magnetic Systems BV

www.goudsmit-magnetics.nl

At Blechexpo in Stuttgart, Goudsmit Magnetic Systems from Waalre, the Netherlands, will present a number of plate handling innovations. These include additions to its range of magnetic grippers for picking up or holding materials such as stamped steel parts, blanks and perforated plates.

The original design was suitable for plate thicknesses of up to 6mm – the new version can handle thicker objects and is three times as powerful. This gives a 100mm diameter gripper a shear force of 3,500 Newton. In addition, the grippers can be equipped with sensors that show when the magnet is on or inactive. This is especially important for robotisation because it allows better control.

“Magnetic grippers are a safe working method because they are permanently magnetic. There is no need for a back-up system to guarantee that the load remains attached. In addition, compared to traditional vacuum grippers, these types of combination grippers can handle perforated plates and small parts without any modification or change of tools,” said the company.

“Rising demand for higher quality makes workpiece demagnetisation increasingly important. We have developed a modular demagnetisation tunnel. This combines the installation-ease of demagnetisation plates with a tunnel’s efficiency. One advantage is that the transport belt is replaceable in one piece. There is no need to weld it to the machine or to run it through the tunnel twice, using extra guide rollers. This makes the option to demagnetise work pieces in grinding, sanding or brushing machines available to everyone.”
