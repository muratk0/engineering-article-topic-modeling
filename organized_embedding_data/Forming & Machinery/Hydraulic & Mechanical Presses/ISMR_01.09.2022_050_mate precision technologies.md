## Mate is an

international tooling specialist. Its sales engineers will demonstrate the features and benefits of its full line of solutions for punching on its EuroBLECH stand. This includes Mate QCT™ thick turret tooling, an insert-style punching system that features a tool-less punch retention mechanism so that the punch insert can be easily changed.

Ultra QCT and Metric QCT punches are fully interchangeable with Amada NCT, NEX and Z-Tooling systems, as well as Wilson Thick Turret Metric punches. Also available are AMX QCT™ for Amada air blow systems and MXC QCT™ for Wilson Series 90 systems.

"Versadie HD™ tooling improves the slitting of thicker materials that require more than 0.016" (0.406mm) total die clearance. There is no need to replace the entire die with replaceable die inserts. Versadie inserts are designed to allow for the greatest lengths currently available in the industry. All slitting die inserts are made from MPM82 tool steel," it said.
