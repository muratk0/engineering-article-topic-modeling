# 630-ton press installation

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA), recently installed a 630-metric-ton AIDA straightside mechanical press at Profile Metal Forming Inc. in McKenzie, Tennessee (USA). Profile has been supplying engineered components for the automotive industry for over 30 years.

The press options that it selected for its 630-ton AIDA press include a 3,650 x 1,500mm bed size; die area lights; front and rear light curtains with metal guards; four-channel forward/reverse tonnage monitor; automatic counterbalance control and automatic die-height adjustment control.■
