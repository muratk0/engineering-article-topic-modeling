# New CTL line for MSP 01

[TARİH: 01.04.2021 ISMR]

Mississippi Steel Processing (MSP), in Columbus, Mississippi (USA), recently installed a Red Bud Industries 0.625" (16mm) x 84" (2130mm) x 80,000lb (36,000kg) heavy gauge cut-to-length (CTL) line with 40' (12,000mm) stretcher leveller. The hybrid system has the ability to operate in start/stop mode when running thicker materials and in looping mode for 0.375" (9.5mm) and thinner material. Loop mode significantly increases productivity and throughput while stretching.

The stretcher leveller features Quick Draw technology to produce rapid cycle times. In addition, the unit also features Red Bud Industries' SUREGRIP long life metallic grippers that prevent slippage when running high-strength and/or oily material. The grippers typically do not require changing for up to a year or more, and the technology eliminates the need to use costly cardboard inserts.
