# Surface finishing for small parts

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

At the EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover this October, Q-Fin's F250 for finishing small sheet metal parts was awarded the prestigious EuroBLECH award in the 'Surface Technology' category.

"The F250, the successor to our F200, translates the advanced technology of our larger machines into a compact and versatile format especially designed for deburring, edge rounding and finishing small metal parts. This compact powerhouse impressed the jury and the public with its high level of automation and ease of use," Q-Fin told ISMR.

"With a belt width of 250mm and a maximum product height of 100mm, the F250 offers extensive possibilities for deburring, edge rounding and finishing small sheet metal parts. Each processing station is software-controlled, giving users complete control. The innovative vacuum system ensures that even the smallest parts, starting from 20 x 20mm, stay securely in place. The alternative magnet system allows safe processing of products as small as 10 x 10mm. A fixed table height and a movable inner frame add to the stability and make it easy to integrate the F250 into automation solutions," it added.

Using Qonnect+ software, the F250 can be connected to ERP systems for extensive process monitoring. The automatic height adjustment of the grinding belt optimises the process by preventing scratches on products when this station is idle. The electronic brush compensation extends the machine's lifespan by ensuring that brushes do not brush into the belt. A 10-inch HMI touchscreen provides intuitive and clear operation.
