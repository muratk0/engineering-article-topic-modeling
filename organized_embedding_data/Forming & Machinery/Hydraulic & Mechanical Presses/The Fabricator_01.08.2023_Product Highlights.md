Waterjet machine delivers rapid speed and acceleration/deceleration

Flow International Corp. has launched the Mach 200c waterjet cutting system. It features Pivot+ waterjet technology, enabling bevel and 5-axis cutting of almost any material, hard or soft, with up to 60 degrees of motion.

The system’s high-speed motors and drives allow for rapid speed and acceleration/deceleration, ultimately providing faster overall cut time, the company states. Additionally, the system features a load/unload table and a bridge that is canted back to allow for full table access.

With its variety of sizes and configurations, the machine can be paired with the manufacturer’s HyPlex Prime, MotoJet, and MotoJet X 60,000-PSI pumps.

Welding cobot works with existing equipment

Productive Robotics’ new 7-axis Blaze welding cobot can be paired with the user’s existing table, fixtures, and welding machine. It maneuvers around all sides of large or complex parts and fixtures to perform more weldments in a single setup.

The system offers the manufacturer’s proprietary teach-by-touch technology and is equipped to accommodate high- or low-mix volumes, short- or long-part runs, or jobs that require quick changeovers.

The cobot has a 61-in.-radius operating sphere and requires no new fixturing. The tablet controller can hold an unlimited library of weld recipes, giving the operator command of all welding parameters, including material type, voltage, wire feed, weld speed, and gas flow.

Productive Robotics LLC

www.productiverobotics.com

Tube lasers deliver straight-down cutting, beveling

HK Laser & Systems offers H series laser tube cutting systems, from regular tube cutting systems with full bundle loading to systems for cutting structural steels such as I beam, angle, and channel.

The systems feature a high-power cutting head for straight-down cutting (model Klinge) and beveling (model LA4) of tube. Based on multiaxis moving control, CNC algorithm, CAM cutting soft ware, and system interface development, these lasers also feature bus CNC systems with a 21.5-in. touchscreen with user-friendly HMI interface.

A variety of chuck systems are available depending on the application. The twochuck system (HTM, HTS, HTP, and HTX Plus models) offers pressure adjustment depending on different tube diameters and thicknesses. With the 3+1 twin chuck system (HTX model), chucks C3 and C4 are combined as one and can rotate or move synchronously but control claws opening and moving independently. And the fourchucks system (TL model) offers a big clamping force to handle ultraheavy tube.

Commercially compostable film brings corrosion protection back to nature

Cortec offers Eco-Corr Film, a rust-preventive plastic packaging that can go back to nature after use. This commercially compostable vapor corrosion inhibitor packaging material does not pose an ecotoxicity threat to plant growth, the company reports.

Metal parts packaged in the film receive ongoing protection against salt, excessive humidity, condensation, moisture, and aggressive industrial atmospheres. These include carbon steel, aluminum, galvanized steel, stainless steel, copper, and brass. After use, the film can be disposed in an industrial composting environment, giving it a final nonpolluting destination in the soil.

Workcell combines spot welding with automated cobot

With the new i5s system, Pro Spot Automation has integrated hand-held spot welding into an automated cobot welding workcell. The Auto Weld feature senses material thickness and resistance to adjust the weld profile automatically. It includes a built-in library for materials, weld caps, and thickness stackups for semiautomatic mode.

The intuitive touchscreen interface includes on-board training and user manuals, both in video and PDF forms. Via WiFi, the weld control software is kept up to date, and data logs can be exported to email for traceability and process control.

The user can create, edit, save, and load welding jobs with repeatable, traceable positions. The cobot works safely around humans without machine guarding.

Pro Spot Automation

www.prospotautomation.com
