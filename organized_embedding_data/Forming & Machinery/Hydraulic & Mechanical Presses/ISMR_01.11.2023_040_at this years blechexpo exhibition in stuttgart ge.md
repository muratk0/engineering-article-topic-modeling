# At this year's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany, Ottavio Albini from Asservimenti Presse highlighted the latest global trends and drivers for metal processing manufacturers.

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Italian manufacturer, Asservimenti Presse Srl, produces equipment for press automation (decoilers; straighteners; cut-to-length lines; transfer and handling systems; robotics etc.) for sheet metalworking. With more than thirty years of experience, it currently exports its technology to over 20 countries. It has completed over 4000 installations globally and specialises in press automation, aiming to optimise the production cycles of its customers.

The manufacturer produces feeding lines with decoilers (up to 25-ton capacity with external diameter of 2000mm) which can process steel strip of up to 2000mm wide and 14mm in thickness. It has placed a firm focus on energy-efficient lines that help to offset high material and energy costs. The company's latest new coil-cutting lines can process aluminium and high-tensile steel. It has installed many of these lines across Europe.

Its latest developments incorporate automatic coil-change on its lines and a new SMART HMI control panel, as well as a new straightener-feeder unit with electronic pilot release that can reach 110 strokes/min. with a step of 300mm. ISMR sat down with Ottavio Albini, Sales and Marketing Manager, Asservimenti Presse Srl, at the Blechexpo/ Schweisstec exhibition this November to discuss how industry developments are affecting the lines designed by the company.

## Network for success

"Since 1989 we have been designing complete lines for sheet metalworking and press automation. These include traditional feeding lines, featuring three machines with simple or double decoilers, straighteners and feeders. We also supply compact feeding lines, featuring two machines with simple or double decoilers and electronic straightener-feeder units. Customers also source other machines from us such as welding machines and cut-to-length systems. We now feature automatic coil-change on our coil-processing lines and we have introduced a new SMART HMI control panel, as well as a new straightener-feeder unit," Ottavio Albini told ISMR.

"Blechexpo/Schweisstec is a very interesting and important exhibition for us. We are pleased with our stand position and it's a great showcase for us to promote our new machines/lines that make parts for electric cars. Last year, we built lots of these types of lines. Personal contact and face-to-face relationships with customers, for example at exhibitions like this, are extremely important in a world where technology and automation have taken centre stage. We value our personal connections with customers; you can't do everything on MS Teams and email," he continued.

From the beginning, Asservimenti Presse's philosophy was to produce easy-to-operate, customised lines 'Made in Italy' working with steel and aluminium to counter the increasing skills and technician shortages in global markets. It offers remote monitoring capability on all its equipment and now has around 4000 machines operating in Europe, 40 in Asia, 30 in the U.S. and 20 in Africa. Its relationships with customers are longstanding and based on collaboration and knowledge sharing.

"Many of our Tier 1, 2 and 3 customers don't just have one line; some may have thirty lines or more. We collaborate with customers and other suppliers on building and customising lines for high-quality, rapid production and advise them, on an ongoing basis, to help with whatever they need. We want to grow with our customers and build strong relationships. You will find our machinery and equipment in a range of global industry sectors (automotive, agricultural, white goods, door/window production etc.)," added Ottavio Albini.

## Meeting new challenges

Asservimenti Presse is working more now on coil lines to process lightweight materials (such as high-strength steels, copper and aluminium) with quick changeover times. Coil-processing trends, in general, are pointing towards smaller, relatively uncomplicated lines that guarantee rapid changeovers. Albini has seen the increasing adoption of servo technology in many of these lines, as customers opt for more control and better productivity on their lines.

The move to electric and hybrid systems in the automotive market sector, as well as the prevalence of individual electric motors in cars, has seen the company's business grow. More of its lines are producing components for electric motors in cars for its customers.

Short changeover times help to ensure greater efficiency and productivity. Albini also sees more requirements for automation in lines, as well as a drive towards Industry 4.0.

"Material types are changing, as are the machines used on the line. I see much smaller production lines nowadays with high part mixes featuring rapid changeover to different part production. I also see more customers delivering 'Just in Time' with tight deadlines. Our machines are built for rapid production and quick changeover to meet these requirements," Ottavio Albini told ISMR.

However, a key focus for Asservimenti Presse has been on developing equipment that saves on material and energy costs. Spiralling inflation and energy prices are a real concern for manufacturers today so the company has changed from using hydraulic to servo-electric drives in its lines to pursue energy savings for its customers. The use of coil material in its steel and aluminium processing lines is an important time-, resource- and energy-saving development: better for material utilisation, minimising scrap levels and increasing efficiency.

"Our new electronic straightener-feeder unit with electronic pilot release allows users to completely clear the material using a numerical control system. Anticipating the future, and with sustainability in mind, we have created a machine that can reduce the opening and closing time of the straightener head; reduce the impact speed of the rolls on the material; save energy and reduce the use of lubricants," Ottavio Albini explained.

## An eye on markets

"Our biggest markets are Italy, Germany and Poland. The Italian government awarded important Industry 4.0 incentives last year to companies investing in capital equipment, which spurred the Italian market, but these have now been reduced to 20%. We are seeing dips in European markets currently, while the market in the United States is seeing growth. We often work with other partners/ companies to sell our press feeding lines and equipment in particular countries (in Germany, for example)," commented Ottavio Albini.

"After the COVID-19 pandemic, the market bounced back and we built a lot of new and tailored lines for customers. We often added automation to these lines. The last three years have been very good for us, as has this year. Our order book will sustain us until the middle

of next year. The challenge after that will be the last six months of next year. However, I believe that our turnover will be around 10% higher this year than last year," he told ISMR.

He highlighted current uncertainty in some industry sectors, such as automotive, where political decision-making is not marching in step with industry and technological developments. This is delaying important projects and line developments.

"Some politicians in Europe are not talking to industry or making the right decisions to enable the industry to move forward and technology to be developed for key industry sectors. It causes confusion: manufacturers need certainty before investing in new technologies. Clear roadmaps and technology pathways need to be set by governments to provide direction and certainty to industry. There are environmental deadlines approaching (such as the move away from petrol engines and rules on domestic heating systems) and parts need to be produced to meet these deadlines. Greater certainty is needed now for markets to move forward," he explained.

For manufacturers who wish to see the company's lines and machinery, Asservimenti Presse will be running an Open House at its headquarters in Massalengo (near Milan, Italy) before Christmas this year.

It will also be exhibiting at ITM Europe in Poznań (Poland), BIEMH (Spain), LAMIERA (Italy) and EuroBLECH (Germany) next year.
