# Stamping solutions for new materials

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

"WITH INCREASING TRENDS towards electric vehicles and lightweight materials, the most challenging issue for auto manufacturers is how to form parts made of new composite materials, alloys or high-strength steel. Our servo presses can overcome the limitations of traditional presses via a flexible and controllable servo system. Press hardening, cold forming, single-stage or multi-stage processes, progressive stamping and automation integration are all possible using SEYI servo presses," Taiwanese stamping specialist, SEYI, told ISMR.

"SEYI's Servo Presses feature a compact but efficient servo motor as well as an energy-saving mechanical and electrical design. The servo motor boasts low revolution speed, high torque, quick acceleration/deceleration rates and low power consumption. The press slide can run, said SEYI, at any position at different speeds and different slide motions can be selected. For instance, Hold Motion is suitable for special materials to dwell at BDC (Bottom Dead Centre) for extended times (BDC accuracy of 0.01mm).
