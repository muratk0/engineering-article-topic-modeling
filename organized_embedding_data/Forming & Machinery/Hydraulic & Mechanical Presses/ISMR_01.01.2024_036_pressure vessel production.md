# Pressure vessel production

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

Pressure vessels are essential components in various industries including oil and gas, petrochemical, chemical and food processing. These enclosed containers play a critical role in the storage and transportation of liquids and gases at pressures significantly higher or lower than the surrounding pressure. In the oil and gas industry, tanks and pressure vessels serve multiple purposes which make them invaluable assets for ensuring efficient operations.

"Pressure vessels are also essential for maintaining required pressure levels and preventing leaks or failures, so it is imperative that they are fabricated with high quality rolling and forming equipment (such as our solutions) to meet industry standards and ensure safety in operations," the Faccin Group told ISMR.

## Building pressure vessel shells

FACCIN's HAV three-roll variable axis plate rolls are advanced and powerful electronic three-roll bending rolls.

"The HAV series combines rolling power and maximum pre-bending precision, ensuring high performances in less time. The special variable geometry configuration makes it suitable for medium-heavy and heavy rolling processes for plates with thicknesses of up to 300mm. The plate roll HAV series delivers impressive results from processing a variety of high-tensile steels," explained the Italian manufacturer.

Four-roll plate bending machines, with linear or planetary guides, are also suitable for the construction of pressure vessel shells.

"The FACCIN 4HEL and 4HEP series are designed for greater productivity as they are rapid and precise as well as being easy to use. The four-roll plate rolls are the ideal solution for mass production, high volumes and repetitive production in automatic cycles," the Faccin Group highlighted.

All models can be equipped with the latest accessories and Siemens CNCs for 24/7 productivity.
