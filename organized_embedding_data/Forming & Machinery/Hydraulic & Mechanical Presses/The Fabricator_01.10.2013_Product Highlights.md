CNC pipe cutting system handles tube to 8 in. dia.

Lincoln Electric® Cutting Systems has announced plans to release the new Pipe-mate™ Profiler CNC pipe cutting system from VERNON Tool™.

Available in five sizes from 5 to 25 ft., the system cuts pipe lengths from 4 to 24 ft. Measuring 2 ft. wide, the automated machine processes round tube from 1 to 8 in. dia.

The adjustable ball transfer cradle simplifies movement of pipe on the bed. A standard, adjustable manual height control allows pipe beveling on straight cuts. The expandable pipe bed provides flexibility to easily increase work capacity while allowing for space limitations. The system cuts miters; saddles; centerline offsets; multiple intersections; gusset slots; and round, rectangular, and overlapping holes.

Ultrasonic cleaning system handles up to 1,800 parts/hr.

Cleaning Technologies Group LLC has introduced the Blackstone-NEY CleanVeyor ultrasonic parts cleaning system. Combining spray, immersion, and ultrasonic cleaning technologies, the system has been shown to achieve Six Sigma consistency in a number of applications requiring high production outputs and cleanliness levels, the manufacturer reports.

The modular system is suitable for final cleaning of parts with complex and internal geometries before assembly.

In the first process, a spray wash using a rotating brush module removes gross levels of contamination from parts. The second process uses ultrasonic technology in an immersion environment. In this process, the cavitation effect generates extreme local temperatures and pressures, helping to reduce chemical usage.

Spindle-blast system handles workcell finishing, surface prep

The RB-RXS-400 rotary indexing spindle-blast machine from Guyson Corp. incorporates a robot nozzle manipulator and two auxiliary axes of coordinated robotic motion of its servomotor-driven spindles. Designed for precision workcell finishing and surface preparation, the machine can be loaded and unloaded by a robot.

A 6-axis FANUC M-10iA robot is rigidly mounted on a pedestal at the rear of the 46- by 42-in. blast cabinet and fitted with protective laminated fabric that seals the back wall of the blasting enclosure and isolates the IP67 device from the potentially abrasive environment of the work chamber.

The end-of-arm tooling consists of a custom-designed gun bracket that secures multiple suction-blast guns and blowoff nozzles for a postblast air wash cycle. The 180-degree-indexing turntable design enables robotic unloading and loading during the automated blasting cycle. Rotation of both component-holding spindles is synchronized by the FANUC R-30iB robot controller.

Metalworking fluid contains 70 percent biobased content

Cortec®’s EcoLine® metalworking fluid, powered by Nano-VpCI®, is a biodegradable, multifunctional product formulated with biobased lubricants, additives, and emulsifiers. It can be used as an alternative to chlorinated products and mineral oils.

Containing 70 percent biobased content, the concentrate is formulated to work in a variety of soft and hard water qualities. It offers long-term corrosion protection for metalworking machines and processed metals.

The inherently stable fluid removes contaminants from parts while providing corrosion protection. It displaces water and oil from metal surfaces. According to the company, the fluid is effective for operations that require rapid cooling. The product is not susceptible to bacteria growth, and it does not contain any chlorinated compounds, chromates, or nitrites. Applications include heavy-duty rolling, grinding, extruding, stamping, cutting, and machining.

Abrasive flap discs made for aggressive performance

Weiler has introduced the Tiger Paw™ abrasive flap disc, designed for aggressive performance on edge-grinding applications. The 100 percent zirconium flap disc features high-performance grain and a poly-cotton backing for grain retention, the company reports.

The disc’s multiple layers of coated abrasive material expose sharp, fresh grains as they slowly wear away, and the backing prevents premature grain shedding on aggressive applications.

The phenolic-back disc is available in 4.5-, 5-, and 7-in. sizes, with or without a 5/8-11 nut, in Type 29 and 27 standard and Type 27 superhigh-density (4.5- and 7-in. only) configurations. The Type 29 configuration is made for tough, aggressive applications. The superhigh-density Type 27 configuration contains 35 to 40 percent more flaps.

Typical applications include pipe beveling, edge chamfering, weld blending, grinding, deburring, stock removal, surface finishing, and blending.

Punching machine also performs forming and bending

Haco Atlantic offers the Q-5 punching machines, which eliminate a number of secondary bending operations. With a bending height of 3.0 in. in a variety of angles and on parts that are nested in locations other than 0 to 45 degrees and 90 degrees, the machine operates as a multifunctional part-processing machine.

The punching machine can form up and bend sheet metal 3 in., allowing the user to punch and then bend or form the part and then drop it down the part chute.

All tools rotate (auto-indexing) at a speed of 250 RPM. An AC servomotor controls the hydraulics for tonnage, speed, and stroke of the punching head, allowing a maximum capacity of 1,200 hits/min.

All data for setting the hydraulic head is kept in the tool library for each tool. When the user loads a specific tool, the CNC knows how to set the punching head parameters.

Punching stroke and speed are programmable. During punching, the punch is set at the optimal height just above the sheet to help achieve minimum loss of travel and reach the maximum possible punching and nibbling rate. For forming operations such as embossing, louvering, bending, and rolling, form depth and height are fully programmable.

After tool regrinding, the punch head compensates for the loss of tool height without a need for shimming. The CNC automatically reads the tool height from the tool library and sets the punching head accordingly.
