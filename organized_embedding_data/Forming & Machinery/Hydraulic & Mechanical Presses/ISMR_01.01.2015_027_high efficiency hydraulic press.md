# High efficiency hydraulic press

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

“OUR DEES HD-FASTECH TECHNOLOGY, released in early 2012, has garnered excellent feedback from the stamping industry. The HD-FASTech hydraulic press has solved conventional deep drawing's low efficiency – raising the number of strokes per minute from 3SPM to 5-8SPM, and greatly improved production efficiency,” press manufacturer DEES told ISMR.

During the early stages of development, stability, reliability and safety were key research areas for the HD-FASTech technology. By using single acting cylinders for lifting and pressing, DEES effectively prevented unnecessary scratching of the cylinder during off-centre forming. The design of the cylinder cover enables maintenance or servicing to be easily performed without the removal of the entire cylinder, saving precious time.

“The slide return mechanism utilises DEES’s proprietary design – by featuring lifting bars and individual cylinders, slide fall is prevented. We also integrated the patented pipe bursting slide locking protection circuit for the protection of workers and tooling during forming. If a pipe or rubber hose bursts, the hydraulic circuit senses a loss in pressure which will lock the slide from a sudden downward drop," concluded DEES.

## Heavy duty hydraulic shearing

TAIWANESE MANUFACTURER Benign Enterprise Co. Ltd will be showcasing its heavy duty hydraulic shear on its booth J0408 at this year’s TIMTOS exhibition in Taipei. Able to process mild steel (4.4mm x 2450mm) and stainless steel (3.5mm x 2450mm), the 15hp, 3800kg shear features side gauges equipped with a metric/imperial scale and front setter.

“All motion controls are centralized on the same panel for easy operation,” explained the manufacturer. “Selection of jog, single or continuous cutting mode is easily accessible. The shear has a single position NC control unit, with read-out display, and protects the operator from any cutting hazards with a full view finger guard. Bell transfer plates on the table enable easier work-piece movement.”
