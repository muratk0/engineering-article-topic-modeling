# Precision cutting in focus

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

TECHNO, INC. CNC Routers has introduced its new HD series CNC router, a new precision cutting system. This all-steel constructed machine uses components such as precision THK rails and bearings, precision rack-n-pinion drives on the X- and Y-axes and a ballscrew-driven Z-axis.

The router also features brushless micro-stepper motors and controls, a 5-hp HSD high-frequency spindle, 6-zone adjustable vacuum table and a 10HP rotary vane vacuum pump. www.technocnc.com for further details.
