## ING YU PRECISION INDUSTRIES CO., Ltd. www.ingyu.com

INGYU will be presenting its new Saga machine at the EuroBLECH 2016 exhibition in Hanover from 25 to 29 October 2016. The precision stamping press manufacturer, in operation for the last 45 years, aims to provide high-precision machines for automotive, can-making, motor lamination and complex forming applications.

"The new Saga is a precision mechanical press with multislide motion for industries which need a variety of components made with higher accuracy. With a one-piece frame and rugged plunger and post guiding system, the machine's vertical accuracy is within 0.01mm for extended die life and improved part quality. There is a slide velocity decrease towards BDC to reduce the impact of stamping, noise and vibration. A special drive gear design enables lower power consumption and provides higher torque for press applications. Power savings of up to 30% are possible, compared to a C-frame press," confirmed INGYU.

"The new Saga enable users to produce high accuracy sheet metal parts, increase product rate per minute and reduce tooling costs. Three different slide motion modes are available for various product applications."

## IRON Srl
www.iron.it

IRoN produces coil processing systems for material widths from 200mm to 2000mm and thicknesses of between 0.5 and 12.5mm. Through a culture of continuous innovation, research and design, the company has transformed the cutting and handling of coil and sheet metal for the automotive, appliances, power and air conditioning sectors.

Today, IRON is a global leader in customised production lines and is a European leader in the production of coil feeding lines of all dimensions. Its range of products includes decoilers; straighteners; levellers; cut-to-length lines; slitting lines; punch/ bending lines and a laser line.
