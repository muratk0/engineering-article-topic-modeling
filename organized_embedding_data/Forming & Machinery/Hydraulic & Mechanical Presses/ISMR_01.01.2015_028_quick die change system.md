# Quick die change system

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

FORWELL’S quick die change system includes die clamps, die lifters, die arms, control box and power units. It is designed to reduce die change times, save on labour costs and increase safety.

“Our quick die change system is controlled by hydraulics. It’s easy and safe compared to traditional manufacturing methods. Although the power is off, the system can keep working for 48 hours. We are one of the best known manufacturers of quick die change systems and our ethos is focused on high quality and high service,” Forwell explained.
