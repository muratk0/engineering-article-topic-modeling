## Maintenance and service

The centralised lubrication system in the electric-drive 4HEP-MPE series also offers significant cost savings for maintenance. The hydraulic system requires a reduced tank size, resulting in a reported 70% decrease in the required oil volume. With a reduction in valves, hoses and components, this design creates ample space for maintenance tasks, allowing for efficient upkeep.

"This system not only improves the overall performance and longevity of the machine but also minimizes the frequency of oil replacement. Consequently, this leads to a significant reduction in the amount of oil waste," added Donatella Fumagalli.

This improvement in operational efficiency results in a reported 35% reduction in operating costs, according to Faccin. For the past three years, and in accordance with its focus on sustainability, Faccin Group has maintained the pricing of its electric-drive model at a level comparable to the hydraulic model.
