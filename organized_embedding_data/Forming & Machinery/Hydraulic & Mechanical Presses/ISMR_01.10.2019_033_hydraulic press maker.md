ISMR: Please explain your technical objectives going forward?

AL: From a technical point of view, we try to continuously improve our hydraulic press technology to give us an edge over our competitors. Most of our customers are in the automotive industry, with very stringent quality requirements. Over the past three years, for example, we have been focusing our efforts on building presses with stronger structures, better safety devices and higher speeds whilst fitting accumulators and servo feeds for fuelsaving. We are also developing the capability to produce different moulds on the same presses. Most of the press parts are made in DEES factories and every part is monitored by our QC engineers.

Our mission is to produce high-precision, rapid and quality presses. Options such as moving bolsters; 180-degree tilting slide tables; safety devices; servo valves and servo motors are just some of the options we can offer on hydraulic presses. We own many patents and spend a great deal on R&D to further develop our press technology.

Our general manager has just come back from the MF Tokyo metalforming show in Japan with some interesting ideas on new press technologies to develop for the future, so watch this space. The forthcoming Olympic 2020 Games in Tokyo will also bring us many anticipated projects from Japan.

ISMR: Are there any products/ technologies that you have released or are releasing this year that you would like to discuss?

AL: We talked last year about a major research project that we ran on hotforming. This year, I am delighted to confirm that we delivered a 1200-ton hot forming press line to an automotive customer (MAW in China), integrated with automated systems. Speed is very important for hot forming. To complete the forming and pressure holding before the parts cool down, timing is key.

At this November's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, on booth 6404 in Hall 6, we will be promoting our die spotting presses as 'the front line' for the manufacturing industry. These are crucial for the quality and accuracy of finished products for sectors such as aerospace; automotive; home appliances; electronics and any industry using die tooling.

Our die spotting press standard accuracy is ±0.05mm and we include an optional moving bolster for easy heavy die change. The 180-degree tilting table makes die fixing safer. We delivered a custom die spotting press to TOYOTA's with ±0.01mm slide repeatability as a DEES exclusive design.

We will also be exhibiting at FABTECH in Chicago (booth 46254) this year – come and meet us there!
