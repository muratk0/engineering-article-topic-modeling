# ADVANCED CNC CONTROL INNOVATIONS

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

FANUC CNC Europe is unveiling advanced CNC control innovations at AMB and BIMU. These include new CNC models Series 30i-B controllers, the Series 0i-D and 0i-Mate-D controllers with newly enhanced functionality and the new Series 35i-B CNC controller.

“The 30i-B Series offers newly enhanced features and functions with new advancements in operability and maintainability including USB memory port, enhancements in Dual Check Safety, new punch press and laser functionality,” the company told ISMR.

The 30i-B also features “High Speed SmoothTCP”, high speed multi-path PMC, enhanced tilted working plane and high speed smoothing functions, faster DSPs for advanced servo control such as multi-axis control and high speed current control and arbitrary speed threading. In addition, the 30i-B has enhanced FSSB and network performance and more diagnostic functions for MTTR reduction.

The CNC Series 0i-D features nano interpolation, AI contour II control, jerk control, tool management function, Dual Check Safety and grinding functions. See www.fanuccnc.eu

## SHADED EYE PROTECTION

Panther Spectacles and Resolution Goggles are two new products offering shaded eye protection from Weldability-SIF. Both these full CE En169-approved spectacles and goggles are ultra light, manufactured in polycarbonate, have anti-fog and anti-scratch lenses offering Shade 5 protection and are designed for environments where there are gas welding applications.

“The Panther Spectacles are suitable for industrial welding and fabrication applications as well as the automotive maintenance industry offering protection against infrared emissions, dust and small metal particles. They have an integrated nylon nose and soft PVC temple tips for optimum comfort. They also feature extendable side arms, which swivel and tilt for a better fit and come supplied with a neck cord,” the company explained.

The Resolution Goggles are designed for use with respiratory masks and prescription spectacles, whilst protecting the eyes from liquid splashes and the gas welding arc. They also feature a soft PVC frame and wide adjustable elastic strap with pivoting buckle for easy adjustment.

## NEW TOOLING ADVANCES

Mate Precision Tooling will exhibit the latest in tooling system technology at FABTECH INTERNATIONAL 2010, North America’s largest fabricating, metal forming and welding event.

“Unlike standard multi tools with just one angle setting, Mate’s Ultra IMT Fully Indexable Multi Tool can achieve any angle setting on the workpiece,” said Mate. “Patent pending, Ultra IMT is designed to use Mate Ultra punches, strippers and Slug Free dies eliminating the need for specialised tooling inventory. Assembly and disassembly is very simple – no tools are required for punch, striker and stripper retention/removal. Ultra IMT features a drop in quick tool change design that allows for fast tool changes, reducing machine downtime while increasing productivity.”

Show visitors will see how the Mate Ultra IMT maximizes existing punch press output with features such as integral punch length adjustment, variable stripping force options, fully ported lubrication, easy active station identification and ergonomic design. The tool is available in 3-station and 8-station versions.

Mate’s new ULTRAFORM FX AND XT holders provide affordable interchangeability with Mate’s ULTRAFORM insert system. These holders reduce holder complexity for machines with precise stroke control and for machine users who infrequently use or don’t want length adjustment.

Mate’s new Rollerball Deburr tool eliminates burrs and secondary operations on punched parts. The Rollerball Deburr pushes the burr away and creates a radius on the side of punched parts. Using a special ball in the upper and lower part of the tool, all part contours can be processed and deburred, even in tight corners. Rollerball Deburr is available for Thick Turret and TRUMPF-style applications.
