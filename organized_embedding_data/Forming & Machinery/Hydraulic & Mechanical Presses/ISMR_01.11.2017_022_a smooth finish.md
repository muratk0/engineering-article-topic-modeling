# A SMOOTH FINISH

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

"Timesavers stormed into Blechexpo with a new 32 RB series metalworking machine for deburring, edge rounding and finishing"

Timesavers' managing director unveiled its latest surface finishing innovations to ISMR at Blechexpo in Stuttgart.

When Arie van der Linden started a repair shop for woodworking machinery in Rotterdam (The Netherlands), Timesavers today specialises in a broad selection of machines for edge rounding, finishing, precision grinding and deburring metal parts. Operating from three facilities in the USA, the Netherlands and Taiwan, the organisation has placed a strong focus on innovation and R&D. In 2016, it won the prestigious Red Dot award for industrial design for its 42 series Rotary Brush (RB) machine.

With over 50,000 machines in the field today, and factory-trained independent dealers and agents, the Dutch grinding and deburring specialist stormed into Blechexpo in Stuttgart with a new 32 RB series metalworking machine for deburring, edge rounding and finishing. It also highlighted several other new innovations on its booth. ISMR caught up with its managing director, Paul Hartendorp, at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart to find out more.....

## New machine launch

"A key focus for us is our sheet metal customers, We have found, over the last few years, that edge rounding is becoming more important for our customers, versus deburring as is metal finishing. However, customers wanted to see these three important functions combined into one mid-sized machine (not just in a high-end version). This was the impetus for us to develop a new type of machine that combined all these benefits, so we are introducing the new 32RB series machine at this show," Hartendorp told ISMR.

"There is more technology now in these machines – some of them feature servotechnology and HMI/PLC touch-screen solutions. We developed our HMI (Human-Machine-Interface)/PLC (Programmable Logic Controller) touch-screen solutions in accordance with our customers' requirements, so they also feature push button layouts (at their request). We also included all kinds of features and options in these standard, cost-effective machines and introduced simple and effective innovations too, such as LED indication for machine functionality, automatic cleaning cycles in our vacuum process, a quick exchange system for tooling and very low noise levels and energy consumption."

The new 32 RB series machine, for uniform deburring, edge rounding and finishing, has a working width of 1100mm and four rotary brushes. More innovative options are available for the 32 series, which make it more versatile. Timesavers has designed it to be user-friendly, with a pre-programmable Siemens HMI touchscreen integrated into the machine, low tooling costs and minimum power requirements. The machine features a similar look as the Red Dot Award design-winning 42 RB series.

The new design has also been successfully implemented into Timesavers' 22 series machine, which also comes with a window so that users can see the deburring, edge rounding or finishing process taking place in the machine. The new design was shown for the first time at Blechexpo 2017, along with the 42 RB series WWRBW 1350mm and the company's 10- Series manual grinder.

## Preparing for Industry 4.0

"Industry 4.0 is a big issue now and we are on board with this. It is the reason why we equip all our standard machines with HMI and PLCs so that they are open platforms, giving users access to all the parameters in the machine for future connectivity in the production line. We work with two software platform vendors, one of which is Siemens (which is strongly ahead in Industry-4.0). We already use the Siemens' PLC/HMI for the 32 series' machine – the 42 series will also integrate a Siemens platform next year. We are positioning ourselves for Industry 4.0 in this way," explained Paul Hartendorp.

Deburring is not an automated process yet, but Timesavers has already shown the market that it is preparing for this by testing a machine with a robot at EuroBLECH in Hannover last year. It presented, for the first time, a combination of its 42 series WRBW rotary brush machine and a robot. The workpieces, to be deburred or edge-rounded, passed the machine in a dedicated zone on the conveyor belt; using modern vision technology, the robot identified the parts, turned them and, for the final brushing operation, placed them on the opposite zone of the conveyor belt.

Although manufacturers can integrate their own robots with deburring and edge rounding machines, the automated deburring concept is a vision that can be taken to the next level. However, the key to realising this concept, says Hartendorp, is developing more advanced vision technology that will enable users to identify sheet metal parts once they are inside the machine.

"We are currently working with a Dutch specialist in vision technology to allow us to take the next step in this process and make a deburring machine that is part of a completely automated process. This is still some years away - the overall cost of the process also needs to come down over that time. We believe that this is the right path to follow, in accordance with Industry 4.0," Hartendorp told ISMR.

## Looking to the future

"It has been a great year for us – we increased our volume in deburring by over 30%. It exploded in a lot of countries in Europe and the Far East. We are geographically spread quite widely, which has helped us to grow. The economies in Europe are growing now and confidence has increased. Those who held off investments over the past few years seem to have chosen 2017 as the year to invest – this bodes very well for the business climate next year," added Hartendorp. "I am particularly proud of our great team of people. The majority have been working for the company for a long time and are still very enthusiastic. They have really ensured the success of all our developments and I am very proud of them."

"Most of our customers are in Europe, with Germany the biggest market for us. New markets for us are in the Middle East and the Far East – we are actively investigating these markets. The construction industry is strong in those markets and The Middle East is diversifying away from oil markets now. We are proud to announce that we just opened our new office and a bigger showroom in Shanghai, China, which will create more opportunities for further growth in China. We have been selling there for 40 years, via our dealers and agencies, and have our own service people in China."

Timesavers has a strong history of diversifying both its geographical customer base and its product portfolio. With strong roots in the woodworking, and later metalworking, industry, it is also involved in the calibrating market for the machining industry. Hartendorp is looking for opportunities to transfer this rapidly-growing technology to the sheet metal market.
