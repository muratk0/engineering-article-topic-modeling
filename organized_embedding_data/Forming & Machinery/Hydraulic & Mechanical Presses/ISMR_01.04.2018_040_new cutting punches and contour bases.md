# New cutting punches and contour bases

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

MEUSBURGER HAS EXPANDED its range, adding three new cutting punches. It now offers additional contours for high flexibility in the configuration of cutting punches. Its E 55405 Cutting punch features a cylindrical head with a tolerance of +0/-0.02mm.

"Because of this tight tolerance, the cutting punch is ready to use and adjustment time is reduced to a minimum. This means savings in time and cost during the installation," explained the manufacturer.

"The E 5531 Cutting punch with bottleneck, with ejector, guarantees reliable removal of stamping waste and therefore ensures high process reliability in the stamping process. Thanks to its bottle-neck geometry, the new cutting punch is suitable for high stamping and retracting forces."

Meusberger's E 55402 Cutting punch with cylindrical head from 1.3344 PM (PM 23) features high adhesive and abrasive wear resistance through its homogeneous carbide structure.

In addition to the offset contours (round, oblong hole and rectangle), Meusburger now also offers a freely configurable hexagon contour as well as a round contour with flattening on both sides. These can be selected quickly using the cutting punches configurator in the digital catalogues. The configurable cutting punches are available within three working days.

## Robotic bending

FRENCH MANUFACTURER Jean Perrot, has announced the launch of the new 'JEAN PERROT ROBOTICS by CLEIA' brand.

"Bringing together Jean Perrot’s expertise in standard/customised sheet metal equipment, tooling and peripheral equipment and CLEIA's specialism for innovative robotics solutions, this is a compelling robotics solution for manufacturers," explained Jean Perrot.

Following its recent Bending 4.0 Open Day and the inauguration of the E COBOT BEND (collaborative bending cell) in France, Jean Perrot’s new announcement encompasses a robotic bending solution to improve material handling, in-process material flows and machine loading/unloading. Using vision systems and pattern recognition, the system is designed as an 'all-in-one' solution.

ISMR met Jean Perrot in Paris at the recent Tolexpo exhibition (part of the new Global Industrie event). Previous innovations from the French manufacturer include the Maneo Premium LCS press brake, the Tool Box and dynamic hydraulic crowning for the press brake. New 2018 versions of the E FOLLOW Tool and the Tool Box are now available, along with a new customised version of its HMI (Human Machine Interface) and a new barcode management system.
