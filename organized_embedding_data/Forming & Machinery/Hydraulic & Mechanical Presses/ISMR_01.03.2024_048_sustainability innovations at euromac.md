# Sustainability innovations at Euromac

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

At the Blechexpo sheet metal trade fair at Stuttgart in November 2023, Euromac focused on innovation and showcased a selection of machines that are designed to be efficient, reliable and energy-efficient.

"In recent years, energy cost increases have had a major impact on sheet metal companies. It has made us realise how vital energy is to our margins and business. Now the costs have come down, but we will never see the same prices we had three years ago so energy saving and efficiency have become essential keywords for any new sheet metalworking solution," it explained.

"It is the quest for energy efficiency that has led us to develop electric punching machines instead of getting into laser machines, such as the XT electric punching machine. With its loading/unloading station, the XT ensures an average energy consumption of 2.5kW. A classic Euromac innovation, the Digibend 400 horizontal bending machine, has an average energy consumption of 0.9kW," it added.

The quest for energy efficiency has also driven Euromac to adopt automation so that its customers can move to night-time production, when costs are lower. Its Fx Bend press brake, equipped with a robot, provides a reported average energy consumption of 1.8kW. This also encouraged Euromac to develop software for production optimisation.

More recently, Euromac has developed its XT independent grippers for the Euromac XT punching machine.

"Grippers can impede punching at the points where they hold the sheet metal. There are two solutions: waste time repositioning the sheet or resign yourself to wasting part of it. Independent grippers offer a third solution, allowing sheet metal and time to be saved while maintaining precision. Three grippers are each independent from the other and are capable of applying pressure of up to 50 bar. To use the entire sheet, operators can move one gripper to allow punching to continue. This is a quick and easy process that will save sheet metal without sacrificing precision," concluded Euromac.
