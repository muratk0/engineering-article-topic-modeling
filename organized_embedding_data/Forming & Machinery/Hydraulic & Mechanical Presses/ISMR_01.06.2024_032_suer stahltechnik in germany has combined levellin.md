# Suer Stahltechnik in Germany has combined levelling and deburring operations on a single production line to save time, space and energy costs.

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

With automation in mind, manufacturers increasingly wish to improve their operational processes.

In fact, it has become essential for companies who look to the future. Increasing demand for a part levelling machine combined with a deburring machine is therefore not an unexpected development.

This principle has been adopted by German manufacturer, Suer Stahltechnik, who has combined KOHLER's 85P.1600 Peak Performer part levelling machine on a single production line with Timesavers' 1,600mm-wide 42 RB series deburring and edge-rounding machine. One employee can operate both systems, saving time, space and energy costs.

As a service provider and processor of steel and other metals, Suer Stahltechnik supplies a wide range of sectors mainly in the construction industry. Its services cover, among other things, laser cutting, forming, refining metal surfaces, thread cutting and various welding-related work. Its customers largely come from the same German state: North Rhine-Westphalia. They mainly work in machine construction, steel and hall construction as well as railings/staircase construction and the automotive industry.

Above: Leonard Suer, Managing Director, Suer Stahltechnik. Right: Suer Stahltechnik's headquarters in Raesfeld, Germany.

## A clear customer focus

Suer Stahltechnik is a young company founded in Raesfeld, Germany, in 2020 by Leonard Suer. With a total of 12 employees, it provides high-quality sheet metal work such as laser cutting, deburring, edging and milling. All sheet metal processing is done at its Raesfeld facility. A sheet metal product that has gone through its laser cutter generally undergoes further processing.

"We want to deburr it, mill it, galvanise it and so on... We take over all the processing for our customers, so that they no longer have to do it themselves," it told ISMR.

The company is committed to its customers. It is still young and likes to take on challenges.

"If a customer comes to us, tells us where they want to go and asks if we want to be their partner, we say 'we'll do it'," explained Leonard Suer. For example, Suer recently purchased a machine to meet a customer need. The customer wanted a product that it could not initially make so Suer and its customer discussed what needed to be done, leading to an additional machine purchase.

## Deburring sheet metal parts

Speaking to several abrasive belt and brush manufacturers, Suer was pointed in the direction of Timesavers International. After having the initial conversation with its sales team, it decided to opt for technology from Dutch surface finishing specialist, Timesavers International. Its choice fell on a Timesavers 42 RB series deburring system with a working width of 1,350mm. It was later replaced by a machine of the same series, but with a working width of 1,600mm.

"The machine with rotating brushes is ideally suited for deburring and edge rounding to a radius of 2mm, laser oxide removal and finishing. Oxide removal is crucial for coating and galvanising. The applied layer adheres much better to a clean surface," outlined Timesavers.

1A-Maschinen GmbH, a Timesavers dealer, played a major role in the project. It provided the deburring machine in combination with AMI roller tables and Absaugwerk GmbH's dust extraction unit to Suer Stahltechnik.

## Deburring and levelling

The German company had been working with Timesavers' deburring machine for two years and were satisfied with it. Yet, it kept running into the same problem. The sheet metal parts that it ran through the deburring machine were not completely straight.

"When laser cutting, tension is introduced into the sheet metal. When that tension is released, the metal deforms. This can happen during follow-up processes such as welding or assembly. The post-processing that is then required can lead to higher costs. To save these costs, the stress should be removed from the sheet metal. Suer Stahltechnik therefore purchased the Peak Performer 85P.1600 part levelling machine from KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH," explained Timesavers.

KOHLER integrated the Timesavers deburring machine with its part levelling machine. The Peak Performer 85P.1600 part levelling machine levels sheet metal (steel, aluminum and stainless steel with dimensions of up to 1,500 x 3,000mm and a sheet thickness up to 28.0mm).

The part levelling machine has an advanced cleaning system, which allows the levelling rollers to be electrically moved out of the machine. The cleaning process is significantly simplified, especially after levelling black steel This is very important for Suer Stahltechnik as various metals such as aluminium, stainless steel and black steel are processed The machine is also equipped with an electromechanical gap control, displaying its full strength with varying cross-sections, to handle any changing forces.

The patented electromechanical gap control recognizes the changed force flow in milliseconds, compensates for it and counteracts it immediately. The gap control also remains constant during the levelling process, a key benefit designed for optimum levelling results especially with more complex components. The part levelling machine is also equipped with a barcode scanner, reversible levelling rollers for longer service life and functions such as edge and overload protection systems.

## An integrated operation

To save space, Suer wanted to link its new part levelling machine to its deburring machine to create one integrated process. The control panels of the KOHLER and Timesavers machine have now been integrated into one system, with three cameras on top. This allows one employee to manage and oversee the entire process.

"This saves time, space and energy. It also works very well," confirmed Leonard Suer.

KOHLER's part levelling machine is designed to ensure flat and low-stress sheet metal.

“Levelling involves more than just straightening a sheet. By reducing and balancing the tension in the material, further work becomes more process-reliable and efficient. The levelled part can then pass over the roller table into the deburring machine. Deburring, edge rounding, oxide removal and finishing then take place. At the touch of a button, the conveyor belt reverses once more so that the part is fed back to the front," explained Max Burgert, Product Manager, KOHLER.

In conclusion, KOHLER, Timesavers, 1A-Maschinen and Suer Stahltechnik cooperated successfully to go beyond just delivering a product. Ultimately, the goal was to deliver the optimum process for Suer Stahltechnik. Regular meetings between the partners were crucial during the development and completion of the production line.

"I really liked the fact that all the companies responded to every customer request. I was able to say, for example, 'I would like it to be this way and that way. Is that possible?'They tried our suggestions and made them work. They also worked very closely together on this project. I really liked that and it turned out very, very well for us," concluded Raoul Knoop, a machine operator and workshop leader at Suer Stahltechnik.
