# Roll grinding

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

"THE GEORG ULTRAGRIND 25 roll grinding machine, in the roll shop of Salzgitter AG's hot rolling mill, is the first in the global steel industry to use CBN abrasives. As well as achieving a grinding throughput three times greater than the machine previously used, it dramatically cuts set-up, testing and idle times," GEORG told ISMR.

"In roll shops, performance features such as high wear resistance and short grinding times matter more than the surface quality. Roll grinding machines in our ultragrind series carry out machining and measuring within one sequence without having to re-clamp the workpiece. This alone provides a significant reduction in machining times and higher efficiency, compared to conventional machines."

CBN abrasive tools are able to machine rolls of high-strength materials, such as HSS grades featuring a hardness of between 83 and 87 Shores C. The new machine at Salzgitter AG, which passed all acceptance tests in June 2018, can grind HSS work rolls with diameters of up to 1,000mm and weighing up to 25,000kg in a fully automatic process. Both conventional and CBN abrasives may be used.

"A major decrease in machining times can be attributed just to the higher grinding throughput achieved by CBN-based abrasive tools. With the new machine, average grinding times have been reduced by more than 30 per cent. Taking into account the time saved through the ability to perform measurements and crack testing online, roll grinding takes more than 50 per cent less time now. The grinding of HSS rolls with conventional abrasive tools takes 40 to 45 minutes on average. The new machine needs only 20 minutes," added GEORG.

The GEORG ultragrind 25 can also machine the rolls complete with chocks, including all types of pinch rolls. This avoids the need for frequent and tedious removals of chocks before roll grinding. For further information, see www.georg.com

## An eye on Industry 4.0

PRIMA POWER'S PRESENCE at EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover was aligned with the show's motto: "Step into the digital reality". All Prima Power innovations showcased in Hanover were designed for digital manufacturing and automated production.

Many innovations are showcased by the Italian manufacturer, including its new integrated robotic bending system with BCe-Smart panel bender and eP-0520 press brake, presented as a world premiere. Solutions from The Laser line were new products exhibited for the first time at a trade show: its new 3D fibre laser system Laser Next 2141, with its wide working envelope and different configurations, and the 2D fibre laser cutting machine, Laser Genius 1530, with automatic stacking robot LST and Combo Tower storage.

Its Combi Genius 1530 punching/fibre laser cutting system was showcased with a new solution which simplifies the installation and maintenance of the machine, as well as with the Compact Express loading/unloading system. The PSBB – Punching, Shearing, Buffering, Bending – flexible manufacturing line with the PCD (Picking & Centring Device) system processes blank sheets from automated storage into ready-bent components.

All of these technologies are supported by Prima Power's smart software family which connects machines, monitors the entire production flow and generates automatic processes from order to delivery. All products are 'Industry 4.0 Inside', the manufacturer told ISMR. For further information, see www.primapower.com
