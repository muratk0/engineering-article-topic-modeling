# THE RIGHT FINISH

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

The new Robusta Nova coil grinding line, from DEMIS, is designed to meet requirements from stainless steel companies and service centres for repeatable surface quality and better efficiency over long production lines. The direct drive, with high performance motor in each grinding head, and the cork grinding belt recently developed by Hermes Abrasives enable users to achieve a common brushing finish with a grinding belt.

Outokumpu was the first to use this grinding technology successfully at its service centre in Italy. It has ordered two additional grinding lines this year. One of these lines, which features three grinding heads, will replace an existing six-head brushing line and will use cork belts to imitate the brush finish,” DEMIS told ISMR.

With this new concept, the machine manufacturer DEMIS and the polishing belt producer Hermes Abrasives guarantee a production speed of more than 50 metres/minute by producing cork finish surfaces using a Hermes cork belt on the DEMIS Robusta Nova grinding machine.

“This newly developed cork belt can also be used in DEMIS sheet grinding machines, with a production rate of 20 metres/minute (compared with common finish on brushing rolls). The cork finish is the same as that achieved on a coil grinding machine,” said DEMIS. FOR MORE INFORMATION, SEE www.demis.biz
