Software helps metal formers with springback compensation

AutoForm Eng. GmbH has developed AutoForm-AutoComp software to help users evaluate and compare different springback compensation strategies and then select the one that is best suited to their needs. As a result, the final tool geometry and process setup can be defined efficiently, ensuring part geometry within the required tolerances and with a minimum number of correction loops in physical tryout, according to the company.

With the software, compensation loops are carried out automatically in the background. Users can select the number of iterations, check the status of these iterations, and visualize them on the screen.

AutoForm Eng. GmbH

www.autoform.com

Portable milling machine o’ers 30-mm chamfer length

CEVISA has launched the CHP-30G portable milling machine for producing chamfers with machined surfaces and angles between 15 and 70 degrees. It is suitable for processing thicknesses from 3 to 50 mm.

Advance and RPM are adjustable according to material requirements. Features include a 30-mm chamfer length and a pneumatic clamping system at 4 bar. Maximum chamfer in one pass is 28 mm.

CEVISA

www.cevisa.net

Cleaning kit quickly restores aluminum

Madison Chemical has introduced the Alum-Renew Kit, a combination of four cleaning and surfacepreparation products that remove various surface contaminants from aluminum substrates. Users can remove grease, oil, metal chips, coating carbide spots, light oxides, weld marks, carbonaceous films, and similar contaminants that commonly occur during the various stages of forming and fabricating aluminum.

Each kit contains a gallon of Viscoclean Green alkaline detergent, along with aluminum conditioners of varying intensities: Aluma-Safe Gel (mild), Aluma-Cling-Blue (moderate), and Treadbrite Edge Gel (aggressive). Each component of the kit is uniquely dyed and includes detailed instructions to simplify usage.

Stacking system protects blank surfaces, edges

Automatic Feed Co. has introduced the Edge Stacker, which transports blanks via an overhead conveyor system and drops them into ice cube stacks, regardless of the end use, while maintaining edge quality. This stacking system is designed for blanking or cut-to-length lines producing surface-sensitive steel, surface-sensitive aluminum, or blanks for laser welding.

The stacker is available as a single- or double-station stacker that can be equipped with either switchable permanent magnets, vacuum capability, or both. The overhead belt conveyors are servo-driven, providing blank support and precise delivery of parts from the press or shear to the stacking area.

Available modes of operation are single stacking, continuous mode stacking for nontrapezoidal blanks, alternate mode stacking for trapezoidal blanks, dual side-by-side part stacking, and dual end-to-end part stacking.

Automatic Feed Co.

www.automaticfeed.com

Self-shielded, flux-cored wire made for structural applications

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has introduced Coreshield 71T-8 OS, a self-shielded, flux-cored wire for all-position welding of critical structural applications, such as TKY joints in offshore construction, pipe structures, bridges, and storage tanks.

The wire features a diffusible hydrogen content lower than 8 ml/100 g and provides low-temperature impact toughness. As-welded mechanical properties include a Charpy V-notch strength of up to 127 ft.-lbs. at -40 degrees C and typical tensile strength of 521 MPa.

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products

esab.com

Compressed air capability added to laser cutting systems

BLM GROUP USA has announced that compressed air can be used as an assist gas on all of its flat-sheet and laser tube cutting systems.

When used in aluminum applications, high-pressure dry air produces nearly dross-free cuts while gaining up to 150% increases in speed on ⅛-in.-thick aluminum. It also can be an appropriate choice for mild steel applications, on which it has a lower cost yet comparable speed to nitrogen, the company states. The thickness of the materials that can be processed with compressed air varies depending on the laser power; materials up to ⅛ in. can be cut using a 3-kW laser or ¼ in. with 6-kW and higher lasers.

BLM GROUP USA

www.blmgroup.com
