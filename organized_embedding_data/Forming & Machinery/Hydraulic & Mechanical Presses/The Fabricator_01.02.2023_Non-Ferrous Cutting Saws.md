The MACC TA Series Saws sport Carbide-Tip Blade and are Dedicated to Cutting Aluminum and Non-Ferrous Materials. The TA Series is offered from Entry-Level to Fully Automatic; and with cutting speeds of 3,000 RPM, they produce Cuts that are Industry Clean and Accurate! These saws also use Teflon Jaws, allowing for the least amount of Marring on your material. This series can cut up to 45° Left and up to 45° Right.

The Blade Holder Shaft is on Bearings with Teflon Jaws to hold the in Place without Marring the material.

Two Pneumatic Vices and Bar Support Arm allow You to have Clean & Precise Cuts.

The TA Series is Dedicated to Cutting Tubes and Solids made of Aluminum or Non-Ferrous Materials.
