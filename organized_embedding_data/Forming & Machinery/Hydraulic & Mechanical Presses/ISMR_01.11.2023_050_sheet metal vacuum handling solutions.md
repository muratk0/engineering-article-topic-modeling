# Sheet metal vacuum handling solutions

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

At this November's Blechexpo in Stuttgart, AERO-LIFT Vakuumtechnik GmbH showcased its latest handling technology solutions for sheet metal and other materials. The company was founded in 1992.

In addition to its AERO-BASIC and AERO-ADVANCE sheet-metal lifting devices, AERO-LIFT also offers systems for swivelling and rotating sheet and plate materials. Mains-independent devices, such as the AERO-INDEPEND and AERO-SELECT (some of them are forklift-compatible), are part of the broad product portfolio of the southern German company.

"In addition to a particularly robust design and maximum durability, our lifting devices score with low operating costs and flexibility of the modular base system. The innovative and intuitive FORCE-LIFT tube lifter was demonstrated at Blechexpo, as were our flexible crane solutions," the manufacturer told ISMR.

AERO-LIFT Vakuumtechnik GmbH has been in the vacuum handling technology field for over 30 years. The family business was founded in Geislingen-Binsdorf/ Baden-Württemberg, Germany, where the company's headquarters are still located. It supplies tube lifters; vacuum lifters; area grippers for automation technology; components, crane systems and services.

"A wide variety of customers from construction and trades; automotive; chemicals; food; energy; logistics; metalworking and woodworking sectors benefit from our broad expertise and Swabian inventive spirit. Transported material is protected by vacuum cups during lifting and moving, a great advantage compared to conventional gripping and transport systems," concluded the company.
