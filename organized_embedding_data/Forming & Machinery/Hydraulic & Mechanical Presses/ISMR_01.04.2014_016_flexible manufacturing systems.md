# FLEXIBLE MANUFACTURING SYSTEMS

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

Prima Power’s compact FMS solution, PSBB (punching – shearing – buffering – bending), was also demonstrated during the recent technology days in Finland. Recent deliveries of this system have been made to Germany, Brazil, Russia and India.

PSBB is based on a Shear Genius® punching – shearing cell, an automatic Express Bender bending cell (both servo-electric) and dynamically managed material flow and buffering, as required by the application. Extreme system flexibility derives from the versatility of work stages and the availability of several operating modes. With buffering and the operating modes, maximum production efficiency can be ensured despite the difference in the cycle times of different work stages, Prima Power explained. Operational options, which can be used simultaneously, are:

- Direct, material flow from Shear Genius® to the bending cell.
- Use of the entire stacking area for buffering in direct connection.
- Simultaneous stacking on tables/wagons and retrieval of components from tables/wagons to bending cell.
- Use of the entire stacking area in unattended operation.

The PSR picking and stacking robot has a major role in system flexibility. It allows the re-organisation and optimisation of production flow between Shear Genius® and the Express Bender Cell, so that nesting can be improved for the right-angle shear.

With the robot, parts can be added to the production flow via storage connections or from other cells with wagon connections. In this way, the utilisation ratio of the bending cell can be considerably increased. Raw material can be loaded from wagons, the system can be integrated with a cut-to-length line and flat components can be brought from outside the system for automatic bending.

Tulus® Power Processing is a Prima Power software solution for controlling the entire production process, including order management, programming and machine time, scheduling all the way to the finished product with reporting facilities. It communicates with the enterprise resource planning system while operating as a manufacturing execution system.

“Tulus® Power Processing makes the production process transparent, because the status of production and the work stages of each part are always known. During the Technology Days, the versatile features of Tulus® Power Processing were demonstrated in practice. In the production environment created, the entire production chain was controlled from part preparation to the finished product and reporting. Visitors were able to follow the process with their smartphones,” explained the company.

The components of the product were manufactured on the Prima Power PSBB production line and assembled manually in the Tulus® Terminal work centre. The demonstration even included a situation where a part was damaged during the process. In this case, the software automatically places a new order if there are no ready parts in storage. ISMR previews the Mach Tool/ITM POLAND trade fair in Poznan

ISMR SAYS:

“This year’s Mach Tool/ITM POLAND exhibition will take place from 3-6 June 2014”

The next ITM Poland (Mach Tool) trade fair will take place in Poznan from 3-6 June 2014.

“As part of this year’s trade fair, for the first time, we will see exhibitors from India – the partner country for ITM Poland 2014. Indian industry, especially engineering, has been experiencing an upward trend. India produces, amongst others, various types of machine tools, power plant equipment, mining and textile machinery and electronic equipment. The Indian shipbuilding industry is rapidly growing as well as its production of transportation vehicles, railway rolling stock and motor cars. The Indian presence at ITM Poland will thus be an opportunity to commence promising business talks. The India Show, organised by EEPC, will bring more than 100 exhibitors to Poznan,” the event organiser told ISMR.

## Manufacturing innovations

For this year’s ITM Poland, the organiser has also announced other changes. For example, Pavilion 8a has now been opened after modernisation and refurbishment. It has been adapted for modern multi-storey stands – the permissible height of structures will rise to 10m.

This means that the “Four-Pack” of pavilions in which the ITM Poland exhibitors will showcase their technologies (the Mach-Tool lounge) has now extended its overall exhibition space to 12,000m2 gross. The Mach Tool lounge exhibition will feature special zones: Maintenance Repairs, Cax Innovation and CAD/CAM. This year’s trade fair in Poznan will also incorporate a new event – the FOCAST Cast Forum. It is dedicated to foundries, manufacturers of machinery and equipment, components suppliers, service providers and scientific representatives.

In Poznan, exhibitors are showcasing products and technologies in sectors such as engineering, automotive, armaments, energy and railway. Equipment and tools can be tested during the event in the following interactive spaces: Skills Training Ground (within the Surfex Surface Treatment Technologies lounge) and the Welding Academy (within the Welding lounge).

The ITM Poland exhibition will also feature the Second National Exhibition ‘Competitive Poland 1989-2014’. This event, which will celebrate 25 years of an independent Poland, will be held at the site of the Poznan International Fair between 2 and 15 June 2014 under the patronage of the President of the Republic of Poland, Bronisław Komorowski.

## ITM Poland 2013

ITM Poland 2013 attracted nearly 1000 exhibitors from 29 countries to 8 exhibition halls. The trade fair pulled in 16,017 visitors from in and outside Poland as well as over 200 journalists. Approximately 1,200 machines and devices for metal fabrication, hydraulics and pneumatics, welding and varnishing as well as industrial robots and manipulators were demonstrated live in operation.

Companies from outside Poland represented 47 per cent of participants at the 2013 exhibition. The trade fair included the products from companies from countries such as: Austria; Belgium; Brazil; Bulgaria; China; Czech Republic; Denmark; Estonia; Finland; France; Germany; Great Britain; Hungary; India; Italy; Japan; the Netherlands; Poland; Portugal; Russia; Slovakia; South Korea; Spain; Sweden; Switzerland; Taiwan; Turkey; Ukraine and the USA.

Turkey marked its presence in the Mach-Tool exhibition hall, featuring a wide range of companies from the sheet metal forming industry in Pavilion 5. The Baden-Württemberg stand (Pavilion 5A) acquainted visitors with nearly 20 tool producers. The Swiss Pavilion (Pavilion 8) – a joint initiative by the Polish-Swiss Chamber of Commerce and Swiss Business Hub Poland – made its second event appearance.

In addition, special events included Cax Innovation, workshops and live surface treatment demonstrations at the Skills Bootcamp (Poligon Umiejętności) as well as lectures and presentations in the special Welding Academy area, where the latest welding technologies, welding and fusion welding of thermoplastics were shown.

ITM Poland 2013 was honoured by the presence of the President of the Republic of Poland, Bronisław Komorowski, who visited Pavilion 5. The trade fair was also visited by representatives of the Aviation Valley Association. The trade fair was accompanied by the Subcontracting Bis fair as well as two-day co-operative Subcontracting ITM meetings.
