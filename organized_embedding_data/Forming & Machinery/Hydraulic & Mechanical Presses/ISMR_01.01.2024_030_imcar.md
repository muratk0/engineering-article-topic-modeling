## Wide range of solutions, in more than 70 years of history.

More than 70 years of activity with a constant goal: to satisfy our customers, often anticipating them, with innovative solutions and to be ready to solve their increasingly complex production problems. Saronni offers to its customers a wide range of machines useful to answer to every kind of need in the feeding line field. Besides the feeding and cut to length lines for presses, Saronni proposes also blanking lines, embossing lines, edges rounding and strip accumulation (SUPERCOIL) lines. Machines and automatic lines for discs cutting and stacking are added to the production.

Bending & Welding Technologies

Since 1955, IMCAR has been producing bending machines, benders, flanging machines, presses and much more. With its 68 years of experience, IMCAR is 100% made in Italy and designs, manufactures and exports machinery and technological solutions all over the world.
