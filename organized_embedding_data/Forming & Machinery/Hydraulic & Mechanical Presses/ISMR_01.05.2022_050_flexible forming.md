# Flexible forming

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Equipped with an innovative nine-point support design, a highly rigid ring frame, zero-clearance slide gibs and the freely programmable servo motion of the AIDA DSF® – Direct Servo Former technology, AIDA DSF-U presses are designed for high performance.

The slide does not move laterally during forming for greater flexibility of forming.

“With its single-crank design, DSF-U presses enable transfer forming, progressive forming and multi-stage cold forging. The overall height has been reduced by 30% by eliminating the connecting rods that had once been considered essential components of crank presses," explained AIDA.

The DSF-U series include a newly developed energysaving hydraulic power unit equipped with a flywheel.

“Multi-stage knockout functionality can be accommodated by mounting hydraulic cylinders in the bed on the left and right that actuate a central knockout bar. Basic operations can be performed from a central location at the front of the press, including confirmation of the crank angle, die height settings and load monitoring during forming,” added the Italian press specialist.

The DSF-U Series is available in multiple configurations of up to 25000kN. AIDA provides a wide range of options for DSF-U presses to meet the detailed requirements of its customers.■
