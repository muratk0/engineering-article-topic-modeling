# Magnetic core drill

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

FEIN, a power tools and accessories manufacturer, has released a new magnetic core drill, the KBE 32, to the UK metalworking market.

“The new KBE 32 magnetic core drill features a 1200-watt, high-power motor for drilling holes of up to 32mm. It is also the first tool on the market to combine a weight of 10.8kg with 1200-watt, offering a revolutionary power-to-weight ratio with high magnetic holding force. This means optimum control accessibility for those working in tight spaces," it explained.

"Easy to use, robust and extremely lightweight, the KBE 32 has been developed with a real focus on accessibility from size, weight and control standpoints. This was a key priority throughout the entire research and development process," it added.

With over 150 years of experience in manufacturing power tools, FEIN has a history of innovation providing application-based solutions for end-users in the metalworking and construction sectors.

"We pride ourselves on our application-based approach and providing trusted expertise to our customers, which ultimately enables them to source the best solution for each job they undertake. The KBE 32 has been designed with this philosophy in mind. Its entire development has focused on accessibility. It has been built to stand the test of time, while its mechanical components have been designed for up to 70,000 drill holes," concluded FEIN.
