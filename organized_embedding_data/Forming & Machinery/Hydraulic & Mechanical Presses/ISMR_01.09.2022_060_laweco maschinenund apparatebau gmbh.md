# LAWECO Maschinenund Apparatebau GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.laweco.de

LAWEco is a leading European designer and manufacturer of lifting systems. Its product range covers various fields of application. It offers flat-lift; spindle lift; push-chain lift; beltlift and loading lift platforms for various lifting requirements. The CARGO MASTER is its brand for aircraft ground-handling equipment. Established in 1979, the family-owned company also produces customised lifting systems.

"Whether in the rolling mill or for metal strip, we offer tailor-made solutions in areas where high process reliability is required,“ it highlighted.

Products include the coil-lifting carriage (for coil weights of up to 50 t); stacking lift platforms (load-bearing capacity up to 150 t) and special solutions (movable or stationary and with simple, tandem or double-scissor capability).

"In the infeed and outfeed areas of coil processing lines, coil-lifting carriages primarily handle the coil. In the stack areas, stationary and movable scissor lift platforms provide the required handling," it concluded.
