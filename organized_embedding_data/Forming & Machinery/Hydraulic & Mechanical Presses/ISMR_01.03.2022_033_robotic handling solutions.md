# Robotic handling solutions

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

ABB will showcase its latest advanced automated handling and robotics solutions at MACH 2022 (Birmingham, UK, 4-8 April 2022), including a high-payload collaborative robot, machine tending systems and a magnetic levitation-based conveyance system.

Stand 18-310 will feature an example of one of ABB's latest robotic machine-tending solutions. The ABB FlexLoader M for AGV is a fully automated feeder for automated guided vehicles (AGVs). Handling all types of workpieces, it can pick straight from the pallet brought to it by the AGV.

“Our complete range of FlexLoader M automated machine tending solutions will be shown on the stand in a series of videos and displays. Our FlexLoader M range enhances machine utilisation and flexibility while responding to batch size one, high-mix and low-volume production. Fast to program and user-friendly, no robotic expertise is required," ABB told ISMR.

There will also be a presentation on ABB FlexArc® robotic welding cells, complete robot systems that are available in several flexible, modular packages for custom-built welding.

“Our latest high-capacity collaborative robot, GoFa will be on the stand. It has a 5kg payload and 950mm reach. With a top speed of 2.2m/s, GoFa is faster than other cobots in its class while also offering easy programming and a high degree of built-in safety," said ABB.

Visitors to the stand will also see GoFa in action alongside ACOPOS 6D, a new handling system that uses magnetically levitating shuttles to carry products and materials to processing stations, eliminating mechanical wear and speeding up transit times. It uses a variety of magnetic shuttles to carry payloads of 0.6 to 14kg, reaching speeds of up to 2m/s. They can move freely in two-dimensional space, rotate and tilt along three axes and offer precise control over the height of levitation. Offering six degrees of motion control freedom, the shuttles can be precisely positioned to within ±5 μm, making ACOPOS 6D suitable for applications with strict positioning requirements.■

## Mobile die changing

The new mobile and space-saving die changing unit combines a standard console with an electric push chain drive and is suitable for loads up to 50 t. The image shows the drive and console in front of the machine table.

ROEMHELD has introduced a new manually movable changing console for dies of up to 50 t weight. The space-saving innovation extends the company's range of components for set-up time optimisation in sheet metal forming. It is designed for users who wish to change forming dies of all weight classes in a time- and force-saving way (e.g. automotive manufacturers and their suppliers.)

"The console, which is electrically driven via a push chain drive, has a particularly compact design and can be used on low press beds and in difficult-to-access environments. It can be mounted alternately on different presses and easily integrated into partially or fully automated processes," explained the manufacturer.

“It combines a standard die-changing console with an electric push chain drive. It is available in different versions and is suitable for most press types, both for new installations and retrofitting. Customised die set-up and changing systems can also be produced that feature special solutions," it added.

The new changing console is designed to enable users to change heavy dies 'easily and safely' on presses. The lowest support heights are possible, so it can also be used with deep press beds. The changing console is optionally available as a mobile unit with guide rollers and can be moved and transported between different plants. Once at the machine, an integrated docking system ensures that the console unit is firmly attached to the press.

The die-changing unit is available in different versions and suitable for most press types. In the image, the chain drive is mounted directly on the press bed and combined with a standard console.

“The die is then placed on the console by forklift or crane. The integrated push chain drive moves the die to its final position in the press via integrated roller and ball bars and positions it there with millimetre precision, even against fixed stops," said Roemheld.

Optionally, the die can be inserted semi- or fully automatically; speeds of up to 2m/minute are possible. The degree of automation of the die change can be freely determined, individual sensors and stops can be integrated into the control. The changing console is operated either via the press machine control or a separate control, which is optionally available.

Roemheld is a specialist in die clamping technology for sheet metal forming. Its group of companies addresses most clamping tasks in sheet metal forming, plastics and rubber processing with an extensive portfolio of die clamping technology. This includes magnetic, hydraulic and electro-mechanical clamping systems. Products for die change (such as die changing carts, roller bars and driven carrying consoles) complete the product range.■
