MACC’s Semi-Automatic Band Saws offer a fast and efficient cutting solut ion, ideal for boosting production speed in applications focused on single cuts. With semi-automatic operation, these saws simplify the cutting process, reducing manual effort while maintaining consistent performance. For best results, they are used with infeed and outfeed tables along with a high-quality measuring device, helping ensure accurate lengths and smooth handling throughout the sawing process. This setup allows for greater precision and ease of use, making the sawing process more controlled and productive.

Band saws are equipped with a built-in recirculating coolant system and an adjustable material stop for efficient, repeatable cutting.

Each band saw includes a single saw blade and a vertical hydraulic clamp for secure material holding during cutting.

Fully-automatic band saws feature a high-capacity variable speed control motor with inverter technology.

Enables precise angular cuts ranging from 0° to 60° on both the left and right sides.
