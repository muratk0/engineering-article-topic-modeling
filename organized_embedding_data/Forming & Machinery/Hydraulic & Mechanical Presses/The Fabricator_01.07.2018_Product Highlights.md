Lubricant mixing system can support several machines

Industrial Innovations has announced the availability of its portable Pro-Mix™ I MS proportioning system. It provides a mixture of lubricant or coolant concentrate with water on demand at adjustable ratios from 4-to-1 to 40-to-1.

Positioned at the point of use, the system eliminates the practice of manual hand mixing and transporting pails of mixed lube across the plant floor. The small footprint of 19 in. wide by 26 in. deep by 62 in. tall, equipped with casters, allows the unit to be placed to support one or more presses running the same lubricant or coolant dilution ratio.

A pneumatic paddle wheel agitator continuously mixes the diluted mixture in the 30-gal. stainless steel reservoir. Sensors detect liquid levels that automatically initiate the mixing system on demand as the mixed lube is distributed to the point of application.

Multidirectional wet bench designed for high-volume production

Magnaflux has introduced the Universal WE multidirectional magnetic particle wet bench. It is designed to completely magnetize the surface of parts up to 35 in. long with a single shot. This cuts the time for magnetizing, bathing, and inspecting the part in half with no need for additional coil shots.

The AC-only wet bench can be set up for automatic sequencing mode.

Large-angle grinder offered in 18-V version

Milwaukee Tool has introduced the M18 FUEL™ 7-/9-in. cordless grinder, which the company says is the first 18-V large-angle grinder. It generates the power of a 15-amp corded LAG and has a 9-in. grinding capacity.

The tool combines the POWERSTATE™ brushless motor, REDLITHIUM™ battery pack, and REDLINK PLUS™ intelligence. It features a trigger switch, ⅝-11 spindle, overload protection, line lockout, electronic clutch, and antivibration handle. The 11.95-lb. grinder reaches 6,600 RPM.

Plate roller can bend thick plate, conical shapes

The MG 4100 T is the latest plate rolling machine from MG Srl. Built for an Indian fabricating company that supplies parts to agricultural equipment manufacturers and structural steel companies, the machine can bend plate up to 100 mm (3.94 in.) and has a working width of 4,200 mm (165.4 in.).

The machine is 5 m (16.4 ft.) tall. The plate roller has three rolls, with the top roll having a diameter of 850 mm (33.4 in.).

The machine has the hydraulic capability to bend conical shapes. Two supports of 5,000 kg (11,023 lbs.) each, one central and one lateral with double joint, are in constant contact with the plate during the bending process.

This same type of plate rolling technology also is being used in the construction of oil and gas pipelines, silos for agricultural customers, and tanks for companies in the pharmaceutical and food processing industries. Recently the company also has seen an increase in applications involving various aluminum alloys.

High-power laser delivers 150 W of blue light

Nuburu offers the AO-150 high-power blue laser, which delivers 150 W of blue light. According to the company, the light is absorbed about 10 times better than infrared light in copper and most yellow metals, which leads to a wide process window for laser welding and allows both conduction mode and keyhole welding.

Because the welding process is under tighter control, the weld quality is enhanced, with no spatter and minimal voids. The high absorption leads to faster welding speeds than infrared and ultrasonic welding, says the manufacturer.

The modular-design laser produces high-quality welds in stainless steel and aluminum and can be used to weld dissimilar metals.
