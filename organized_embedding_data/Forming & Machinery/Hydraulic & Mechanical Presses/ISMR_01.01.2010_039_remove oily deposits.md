# REMOVE OILY DEPOSITS

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

Daimer’s new Super Max 15500SP steam-only systems offer pressures levels of 1000 psi and high temperatures for cleaning surfaces and equipment in metal fabrication and processing facilities.

The new fuel-heated, electric-powered Super Max 15500SP combines a high flow rate, low pressure levels and new Super High-Temperature Optimized Technology (Super-HOT). The steam-only system is engineered to remove grease from a variety of surfaces and machinery within metalworking and metal manufacturing plants.

“Our new stationary Super-HOT pressure washers offer dedicated wet-steam for removing oily deposits,” said Matthew Baratta, Daimer.com spokesman. “This technology also eliminates confusion for users because it offers a single, high-temperature cleaning option.”

Daimer’s Super-HOT commercial pressure washer systems have been manufactured to produce only wet steam with temperatures approaching 330ºF. The 15500SP pressure washers turn cold water to steam in as little as 30 seconds. The equipment can be heated with a variety of fuels, including #1 or #2 heating oils, diesel, kerosene, natural gas or even propane.
