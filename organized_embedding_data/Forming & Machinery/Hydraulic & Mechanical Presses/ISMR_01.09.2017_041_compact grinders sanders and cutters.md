# Compact grinders, sanders and cutters

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

INDUSTRIAL TOOL SPECIALIST, Fuji Air Tools, has launched a new series of compact angle grinders, sanders and a cutter designed to deliver improved productivity in industrial metalworking applications.

"The new FA-45 Series offers a superior power-to-weight ratio a lower profile head than previous models, making these tools ideal for deburring, weld bead removal or basic chamfering in the construction, petrochemical, foundry and metalworking industries, as well as shipbuilding, bridge-building and rolling stock assembly," Fuji Air Tools told /SMR.

"FA-45 Series tools are highly efficient, being up to 27% more powerful than preceding models in the Fuji portfolio. Durability is assured with a new, robust design for the body, exhaust and throttle lever, as well as air-cooled gears to extend service life. Their ergonomic design also reduces noise and vibration to ensure operator comfort."

There are six models of FA-45 Series 5" angle grinders to choose from, delivering between 870W - 1100W (1.17hp – 1.48hp) and weighing no more than 2.1kg (4.6lb). Each grinder is equipped with a swivel silencer that minimises the effect of freezing during operation. FA-45 series angle grinders also feature a low grinder head, measuring 60.4mm (2.38") in depth, which makes them particularly suitable for corner grinding.

The FA-45-8 5" angle cutter features a similarly low-profile head and has a maximum power output of 1100W (1.48hp) Its 5" diamond cutter makes it suitable for use in a variety of metal cutting applications.

Completing the FA-45 Series are three models of 7" angle sanders. Equipped with an 870W (1.17hp) motor and delivering a free speed of 7000rpm, these sanders are designed to tackle tough surface preparation jobs. See www.fujitools.com
