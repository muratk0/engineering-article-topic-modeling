# Grinding and deburring

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

On stand K06 in Hall 14 at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022), Lissmac will present its SBM series and SMD 3 series machines for grinding and deburring. Both systems can be customised for a wide range of customer requirements.

"Our customers have the option of choosing between two machine concepts: double-sided processing in one operation and single-sided processing—dry on one side or wet on the other. At EuroBLECH, we will present three models in the SBM series. In the dry machining process, efficient machining of all cutting contours on both sides of sheet metal i.e. the outer and inner contours, can be done in just one operational process. Synchronous machining on both sides offers maximum productivity and machining results. All machines in this series offer energy savings and quick/easy tool change," said the manufacturer.

The Lissmac equipment portfolio comprises four machine series (SBM-XS, SBM-M, SBM-L or SBM-XL series). The models differ in terms of working widths, as well as the number and type of units for workpiece machining. Lissmac will showcase its SBM-XS 300 ALU MIX small parts deburring machine with a loading robot and “simple and intuitive machine operation." It is designed for deburring and edge rounding of steel, stainless steel and aluminium with sheet thicknesses from 1-15mm and part sizes from 45 x 45 × 1mm up to a maximum of 200 × 200 × 15mm. Inner and outer contours are machined equally.

"The integrated turning station eliminates the need for time-consuming turning and reprocessing of parts. Folding away the turning station allows parts with an edge length of more than 200mm to be processed unilaterally," said the surface-finishing specialist.

The SMD3 series can be used for different materials, part sizes and sheet thicknesses (from edge rounding R2 to finishing).

The SBM-L machines are designed for deburring and edge rounding of workpieces of up to 50mm sheet thickness.

For deburring and edge rounding of the inner and outer contours of stamped parts or punched parts, as well as for laser- or fine plasma-cut workpieces, the two dry-operating SBM-L 1000 G1S2 and SBM-L 1500 G1S2 machines for removal of spatter on the sheet surface will be shown. Extraction and filter technology significantly reduce dust, dirt and noise pollution. Optional smart features include automatic tool wear compensation, automatic sheet thickness measurement, a barcode scanner and other tools.

“The deburring of often very heavy workpieces on both sides saves costintensive turning or machining of the parts twice. The processing units are individually electrically adjustable or can be switched on and off. The transverse machining principle ensures optimum tool utilisation over the entire working width and the modular, compact construction in modern machine design requires only a small amount of space," said Lissmac.

Lissmac's range of singlesided dry grinding machines includes the entry-level models of the SMD 1 series. Applications range from deburring and all-round edge rounding to the removal of heavy plasma or flame-cut slag.

At EuroBLECH, Lissmac will present the universal entrylevel SMD 123 RE machine for machining steel, stainless steel and aluminium. It is suitable for removing burrs that occur during punching or laser/plasma cutting.

"The two rotor heads enable uniform edge rounding on all sides and also a directionless finish. The creation of a surface grind is possible without time-consuming set-up work. Components can also be machined by forming. The SMD 123- RE has a small footprint and can be operated intuitively via a touch panel. Other features include separate on/off switching of the processing units, continuously adjustable sanding belt speed and a quick/easy toolchange," added Lissmac.

“Our SMD 3 series is used for efficient part processing from 50 × 50mm, regardless of the part geometry. Applications range from slag removal, deburring and uniform edge rounding up to a radius of 2.0mm to surface finishing and small parts machining. The sophisticated tool arrangement ensures a uniform machining result over the entire working width. A quick tool-change system reduces set-up times to a minimum," commented the manufacturer.

"The production of high-end ground patterns and strong edge rounding are strengths of the SMD 3 series," LISSMAC told ISMR.

In Hanover, the SMD 345 REER (S-Edition) will be on display for applications such as edge rounding R2 to finishing. Featuring several round brushes, it is suitable for processing sheet metal with coating, foiling, embossing or through-passages, as well as materials of different thicknesses. The SMD 335 DRE (P-Edition), which will also be presented, is suitable for machining thick sheet and offers cool-grinding. Mechanical slag removal reduces tooling costs for users.

In cooperation with straightening technology specialist, Kohler Maschinenbau GmbH, Lissmac will also be presenting a processing line for deburring and part straightening in a single operation on its exhibition stand.

"Interlinking of the two processes ensures efficient production and material flow, significantly reducing the effort required for material handling and logistics," concluded Lissmac.■
