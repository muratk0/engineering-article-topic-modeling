### Lubrication method:
According to the working conditions of the plate rolling machine, box lubrication and regular refueling lubrication are used respectively. The work roll bearing and sliding guide surface are lubricated by a manual pressure injection gun. The hydraulic transmission device uses box lubrication.

Lubricant Selection:

1. Lithium base lubricating oil is used at the roller bearing and sliding guide plate.

2. The hydraulic transmission device adopts hydraulic oil of the same type as the hydraulic system (32# or 46#).

### Lubrication Precautions:
1. The sliding guide surface of the frame can be refueled once per shift with the pressure injection gun.

2.Before each shift, oil should be injected into each lubrication point to check whether the hydraulic oil in the tank is at the specified oil level.

After the hydraulic system works for 150 hours, the oil should be changed once, and then the oil should be changed once every 1500 hours or a year. The entire lubrication system should be cleaned 150 hours after the machine starts working. If the machine is in a continuous full load state for a long time, clean it once every six months.
