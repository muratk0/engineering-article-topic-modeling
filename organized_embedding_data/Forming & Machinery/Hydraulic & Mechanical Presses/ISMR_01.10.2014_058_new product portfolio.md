# New product portfolio

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

The E 1940 automatic ejector set lock Photo: Meusburger

BY AUTOMATIC locking and unlocking, Meusburger's new E 1940 ejector set lock secures the ejector set against any unwanted movement – with no additional work steps required. The lock is automatically deactivated as soon as the mould is installed on the machine and reactivated when the mould is removed.

The new product is also flexible – it can be installed not only inside the risers but also in the centre, then serving as a support pillar. For maintenance on the ejector set, the E 1940 can be quickly and easily unlocked and is now available from stock.

See www.meusburger.com

The manufacturer of high precision standard parts has also expanded its range of consumables with adhesives for various applications. For locking threads, VBA 2M43 and VBA 2M70 – both usable up to 150°C – are a suitable choice. They prevent the self-loosening of threads and enable permanent sealing. The retaining compounds VBA 6M03 and VBA 6M48 are highly resistant to chemicals and are usable up to +175°C. VBA 5M42 provides permanent sealing of threads up to bursting pressure, while the new, solvent-free adhesive/sealant VBA 5M77 is designed to seal pipe threads.

The company recently introduced a new 'modular concept' product range. Customers are offered high precision standard components which enable the optimal guiding of modules. Using Meusburger’s selection guide, they can find the most convenient combination of guide pillars with centre flange, ball cages and guide bushes for any specific application. The E 5078 guide pillar with premium surface quality Ra 0.1 (Superfinish) is suitable for very high stroke rates. ■
