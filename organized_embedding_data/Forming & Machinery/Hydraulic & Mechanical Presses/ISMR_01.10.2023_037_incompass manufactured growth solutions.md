## About InCompass™

The InCompass™ group specialises in material processing solutions. Its industrial legacy brands include machine design, maintenance and custom finishing systems. The brands under the group's umbrella are:

- Timesavers International: Deburring, edge rounding, finishing and precision grinding.
- Dubois Equipment: Equipment for the finishing industry.
- Randbright: Machines/systems for metal centreless deburring, edge rounding, finishing, polishing, grinding and cleaning.
- Clausing Industrial: Machinery for drilling, milling, turning, grinding and sawing.
- Colchester Machine Tool Solutions: Turning machines and products for metalworking applications.
- Bourn & Koch Inc.: Precision automated machine tool solutions.
- Mollart Machinery: Gun drilling systems, deep hole boring and deep hole finishing systems.
- Blanchard: Vertical rotary surface grinding systems.
- Fellows: Precision gear shaping technology.
- Bridgeport: Milling machinery.

## Global market trends

So, how does Timesavers view the global business climate for sheet-metal professionals?

"The market is slowing down right now, but I think this is temporary. I strongly believe, in the coming months, that people will use their remaining budgets to invest in machinery again (typically towards the end of the year). I believe that the market will still grow and there will be a large increase of laser sales, and therefore edge-rounding machines. The European market slowed down over the summer but we still have a huge backlog to fulfil. Having said that, we feel that the market will increase in the fourth quarter. On a worldwide scale, we are also focusing on Far Eastern and North American markets to diversify our market opportunities. This gives us a much more stable growth path in the industry," Paul Hartendorp told /SMR.

Both men pinpointed the fact that labour is expensive and hard to get so machines need to be easy to set up and operate (by fewer people) more independently. Customers are also, confirmed both men, requesting robots around their machines.

At FABTECH 2022 in the USA, Timesavers paired its 32 series flat-part finishing machine and a cobot, which handled material pick-up and placement of parts onto the conveyor feeding the machine. Because the cobot is designed to work in close proximity to humans, typical machine guarding with fences was not required for this type of application. Robot and vision technology were demonstrated in action at the exhibition.

Trade in China has now picked up for Timesavers and it sees further opportunities in Asian markets.

"The other trend that we see is increased connectivity or loT. A lot of customers are asking us to provide data from the machine so that they can do whatever they wish with it. That calls for an open architecture to provide this kind of data to customers. They also want higher productivity and faster speeds," confirmed Frans Huvers.

## Service and support

"We are also increasing our efforts with partners (dust extractor systems, ground conveyors etc.) as well as our efforts on service. The idea is to provide a bigger portfolio of products, not just the machine but also maintenance, training and education services for operators. We aim to bring these to market next year," confirmed Frans Huvers.

In the spirit of digitalisation and Industry 4.0, both men also confirmed plans to develop a customer portal, which will incorporate a web shop (for parts purchase). This portal will also feature a menu of options, such as video training and machine operation instructions as well as best practice advice. Timesavers already offers training at its headquarters for customers' machine operators.

"Service and support is a crucial part of our offering," concluded Frans Huvers. “That should never be forgotten. Our customers rely on us for support and we take that responsibility very seriously."■
