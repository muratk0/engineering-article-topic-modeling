# Versatile rollforming

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

AT R + T In Stuttgart, Dreistern will be presenting a new rollforming manufacturing concept for roller shutter slats and roller shutter boxes. The concepts are highly flexible manufacturing solutions that enable the cost-effective manufacture of the smallest batch sizes thanks to very short set-up times on a product change.

“Up until now, a product change could easily take two hours or even longer on machines for roller shutter slats. Many manufacturers do not therefore change tools at all – instead, they produce different products on different machines. With this new system, the effort required for a complete product change can be reduced to less than 30 minutes. Old machines can also be upgraded with the new system,” Dreistern explained.

## Steel pipe cutting

FINNISH COMPANY, Exact Tools Oy, has developed a new method of cutting pipes by launching a range of steel pipecutting systems along with cutting accessories. The award-winning innovative PipeCut range of pipe cutting systems is one of the fastest and most precise ways to cut and bevel pipes and tubes on-site producing a straight ready-to-install finished surface in a multitude of diameters and materials and eliminating, says the manufacturer, many of the problems associated with pipe cutting.

Each model can be supplied with a choice of 3 different blades – TCT (tungsten carbide tip) blades for general use in cutting steels, copper, aluminium, plastic and multilayer materials. The Cermet model features ceramic tips for heavy duty applications such as cutting stainless steel and acidproof steel and Diamond discs are available for cutting cast iron pipes.

Exact pipe cutting systems are suitable for cutting a wide range of tubes and pipes including spiral duct tube ranging from 75mm – 1000mm in diameter with wall thicknesses ranging from 1.5 – 6mm.

The cutter firmly grips the pipe to be cut with the blade automatically positioned to cut correctly. The entire weight of the cutter rests on the pipe. When started, the blade chips the pipe surface rather than grinding it, producing a burr-free cut surface that is ready for installation. The operation is dust-free and does not produce any sparks.

Tools on modern rollforming machines for the manufacture of foam-filled roller shutters have numerous adjustment features. The new generation of Dreistern tools only use adjustment features that are necessary for day-to-day production, reducing scrap levels by an estimated 30%.

A new bending process enables the production of an impressive variety of box shapes. Painted aluminium surfaces are susceptible to damage. Dreistern’s solution is provided by raw material that is coated with a protective film. The protective film passes through the entire manufacturing process and is only removed on-site.

“The great versatility of the new system concept is not at the expense of productivity. Set-up time, in case of product change, is a few minutes. Every 40 seconds, a complete roller shutter box leaves the machine,” concluded the manufacturer. See www.dreistern.com

## The final cut

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of advanced metal cutting systems, has announced the launch of an expanded line of Centricut brand consumables for Kjellberg systems. The new consumables are designed for high amperage stainless steel cutting using Kjellberg’s FineFocus 800, FineFocus 800 Plus, FineFocus 900, PA-S7 or PA-S75 systems. “They include features that eliminate leaking compared to the OEM version and better cooling technology in the form of Hypertherm’s patented CoolFlow nozzle. This guarantees longer consumable life, enabling customers to lower their operating costs without sacrificing cut quality or productivity,” said Hypertherm. See www.hypertherm.com

## Metals analysis

THE LATEST generation of SPECTROMAXx from SPECTRO Analytical Instruments delivers the analytical performance of a laboratory-based optical emission system.

“The SPECTROMAXx is one of the most successful OES metal analysers on the market, with thousands installed worldwide. All the important elements encountered in the metals industry can be determined with the SPECTROMAXx, including traces of carbon, phosphorus, sulphur and nitrogen – SPECTROMAXx can now measure nitrogen down to 10ppm,” said the manufacturer.

Sample excitation is digitally controlled, giving exactly defined plasma conditions and better analytical precision. A range of special sample adaptors for small samples is also available. The spark stand has been optimised for low argon consumption, but a new Argon Saving Module takes this further. Depending on the instrument duty cycle, savings of up to 40% in argon consumption can be realised. The high-resolution, temperature-stabilised optics can be configured to suit a customer’s application over an available range from 140 to 670nm and use the latest multiple CCD detectors.

## PRODUCT REVIEWS

Fibre cutting in focus HIGHER LASER power and automatic adjustment of the focus position make the BySprint Fiber more flexible. Since the beginning of the year, Bystronic has been offering its BySprint Fiber laser cutting machine with two-kilowatt laser power and now a three-kilowatt laser source. The new Fiber 3000 laser source increases the maximum thickness of sheets to be processed from 12 to 20mm with steel, from 6 to 8mm for stainless steel, from 6 to 12mm for aluminium and for copper and brass to 6mm.

A further innovation with fibre laser cutting is the ByPos Fiber, on which the focus position can be automatically adjusted. ByPos Fiber will be fitted as standard to all BySprint Fiber systems that are ordered after the New Year, independent of the selected laser power.
