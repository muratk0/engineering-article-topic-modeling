## Hans Weber

Maschinenfabrik GmbH (WEBER) has over 100 years of experience in mechanical engineering solutions. It offers grinding technology, extruder technology, additive manufacturing, robotics and automation.

"Our innovative products are tailored to our customers' needs and supported by continuous research and development. Customers can trust our comprehensive portfolio and the highest quality standards for advanced mechanical engineering solutions," it explained.

It will showcase a metal grinding machine for laser, punching and nibbling parts. The NLC series is the WEBER metal grinding machine using wet grinding technology. It can be used for special types of materials, when the material is heated excessively and for processing very oily metal sheets.

"Wet grinding with the NLC makes deburring, rounding, descaling and surface grinding easy and safe. The arrangement of up to five different processing stations can be freely selected," WEBER explained.
