Capital Machine Technologies, Inc. drills into the Vertical Machine Market with Flex Machine Tools

Flex Machine Tools

Capital Machine Technologies, Inc. is excited to announce our new partnership with Flex Machine Tools. For nearly 50 years Flex Machine Tools has been building innovative and high-performing machine tool solutions, spanning over three generations.

Capital will exclusively distribute the FlexCNC Vertical Machine Center (VMC) line within our twelve-state territory, including Florida, Georgia, Texas, Alabama, Mississippi, Louisiana, Arkansas, Oklahoma, Virginia, Tennessee, North Carolina, and South Carolina.

The FlexCNC VMC ranges in size from 10′ to 80′ long. In addition to their standard sizes, they also design and build custom machines to suit customer needs. These machines are capable of high-speed drilling and milling of large parts in one setup with less repositioning.

This new partnership puts more boots on the ground for servicing Flex’s machines through Capital’s service department of 24 factorytrained Service Engineers. Both Capital Machine and Flex Machine Tools customers are given the "
