[TARİH: 01.05.2017 ISMR]

ISMR discusses new partnerships, new products and a focus on automation with Servopresse's marketing manager, Lara Cecchi.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements and the reasons for this?

LC: A new partnership between Servopresse and Faspar-Cimsa has led to the creation of a highly automated coil blanking line, now installed at an Italian company in Tuscany for the production of customised pressure-bent profiles. This new coil blanking line was recently installed at INPLAF's factory, an Italian company that has been engaged in the manufacture of pressure-bent profiles, for a wide variety of applications, for about 35 years.

Servopresse, represented by myself and Faspar-Cimsa, represented by sales manager Maddalena Aime, are two family-run companies that have developed along similar lines, with complementary production operations geared towards similar application sectors. Today, this long-term relationship, which combines industrial expertise and friendship, between our two families has become a long-term working relationship. I hope that we will continue this successful partnership long into the future.

ISMR: How has your production evolved and shifted?

LC: Over time, the original production of industrial sheet metal has been strengthened by the production of profiles for metal carpentry, finding a niche in sectors such as the production of frames for housing modules; mobile homes and elements for photovoltaic systems, industrial doors and more.

The installation of this new blanking line responds to the new production and commercial objectives that INPLAF's young and dynamic management has set. It is a major investment that has started a synergistic partnership between two Italian companies which is set to continue over time, given the excellent results from this first experience.

ISMR: Please give a brief background to both your companies and explain your relationship, as well as your mission and objectives?

LC: Servopresse was founded by the Cecchi brothers in 1970 and employs around twenty staff at its factories (5,000 m²) in Milan and Nice. It specialises in the design and manufacture of automation lines for presses, shears, profiles and other machines using sheet metal coils. Today, Servopresse is run by the second generation of the Cecchi family - Dario, Lara and Paolo.

90% of Servopresse production is destined for overseas markets, and it has a significant presence in the United Kingdom where, in recent years, it has delivered fifteen coil lines (with up to 15 tonne capacity for coils ranging from 1,000 to 1,600mm wide). The UK customer is one of the most important toolmakers in the automotive industry worldwide, Sertec, an OEM for the Jaguar-Land Rover Group (JLR) and many others. Servopresse also has a significant presence in Hungary; Poland; Scandinavia; South Africa; United States; Mexico; Brazil; Argentina; Chile; Russia; Australia and India, with over 5,000 high-performance coil lines produced since 1970.

The secret of our success is that we are a manufacturer, not just a simple assembler. Around 85% of mechanical components are manufactured in-house and we are able to offer special "made-to-measure" machines which can satisfy even the most sophisticated, diverse and complex manufacturing requirements. Our biggest customers are the automotive stampers but we also supply to sectors such as home appliances, furniture and rollforming.
