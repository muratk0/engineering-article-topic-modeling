# Keeping control of the feed

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

DIMECO's new RF1 numerical control, fitted to its electronic feeders for press feeders, is designed to get straight to the point....

"The RF1 enables users to stamp parts with enhanced safety and optimum productivity. It is ergonomic, safe and easy to use," the French manufacturer explained. The family-owned business, founded in early 1957, is a specialist in sheet-metal coil production systems.

"RF1 is an operator-focused system. The compact and user-friendly unit lets users work with precision. The feeders are ergonomically designed for fast and simple operation, with an intuitive interface featuring contextsensitive menus for immediate familiarisation. Several accesses are managed by passwords, depending upon the user (production or maintenance). With rapid training, operators can immediately control their feeder and production line from reels," it added.

This new generation of numerical control has been designed with safety in mind. It interfaces with emergency stops, safety zones in an existing line and safe speed for manual introduction of the coil as close as possible to the tooling. A safety PLC is integrated into the RF1.

"Micro-correction of the pitch during production; part counter; pilot release control etc.—all the essential functions for feeding a stamping press can be found in the RF1," added Dimeco.

To complete these functions, RF1 feeders can be fitted with programmable electronic cams, with three inputs and six outputs.
