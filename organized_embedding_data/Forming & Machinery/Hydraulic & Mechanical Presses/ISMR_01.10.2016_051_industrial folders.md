# Industrial folders

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

SCHRÖDER GROUP will be exhibiting at EuroBLECH 2016 in hall 11, booth A48, as a leading manufacturer of folding machines for the industrial sector. At this year's exhibition, it will present a powerful and newly developed folding machine, PowerBend Industrial, which is positioned between the PowerBend-Series and the high-end machines of the Evolution-Series. Along with other motorised folding machines, the manufacturer will also be showing a large selection of shears.

The new PowerBend Industrial bends steel sheets of up to 6mm and is suitable for semi-automated operations. With options such as the Up-and-Down folding beam and a suction gauge, as an extension of the back gauge, the number of manual actions can be minimised. The combination of both options allows the alignment of metal sheets against the horizontally-positioned folding beam, fixing it with the suction gauge. Suction cups position the sheet alongside the folding beam for the folding process, using the Up-and-Down folding beam with bends and counterbends, without any intervention needed by the operator.

The MAK 4 Evolution UD and SPB Evolution UD machines will also be showcased at the exhibition. They include an automatic tool changer and suction gauge - important requirements for automation in industrial sheet metal processing. The PowerBend Professional is an entry-level machine for industrial sheet metal processing. Also on display on the booth will be the MHSU motorised shear, PDC-NC as well as the PowerShear Professional with a sheet support and unloading station for the final product (positioned on the back of the machine). Manual folders, such as the ASK 3 and Type 114 and 134, will also be on display.
