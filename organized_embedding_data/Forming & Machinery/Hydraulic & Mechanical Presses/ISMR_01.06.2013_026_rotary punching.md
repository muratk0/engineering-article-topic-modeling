# ROTARY PUNCHING

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

SAMCO MACHINERY, a specialist in rollforming technology, has introduced its latest rotary machine innovation into the Canadian and world marketplace.

“We feel the Vortex is our most versatile rotary punch machine,” said a Samco company spokesman. “Not only is it user friendly and interchangeable, the rotary motion is fully balanced providing less vibration, less wear on punch and die, and a longer die life.”

In addition to operating at speeds of up to 500fpm, the Vortex takes up less space (64' x 32'), does not require hydraulics to operate and offers more throughput.

“The Vortex can be retro-fitted to existing Samco lines,” he continued, “making it easy to add to any production line with less production downtime.”
