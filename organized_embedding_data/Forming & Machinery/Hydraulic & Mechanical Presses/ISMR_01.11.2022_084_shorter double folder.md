# Shorter double folder

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Thalmann, part of the CIDAN Group, has been a pioneer in the development and manufacturing of long folding machines since 1960. It has now developed a shorter Thalmann TD200 3.2 metre and TD150 4.0-metre double folder for users that need a small footprint and more automation for the forming of metal trim. This new innovation is designed to be smooth, fast and quiet, Thalmann told ISMR.

“The new double folder is based on our unique kinetic control shaft technology, ensuring a high level of performance and synchronous force distribution over the entire length of the machine. This means faster and smoother folding processes, without any loss of folding precision, that are guaranteed by the exclusive dynamic folding technology (DFT)," the folding specialist explained.

The double folder features a narrow folding blade for tight Z bends and a 10mm carrier blade for maximum capacity of the machine. A 3-degree upper beam means more free space and a curved lower beam is for deep F and J channels. The folder boasts a 1250mm back gauge and 700mm table extender. It can be used to form taper parts and automation can be added to the machine i.e. side loaders, part flippers and unloaders.■
