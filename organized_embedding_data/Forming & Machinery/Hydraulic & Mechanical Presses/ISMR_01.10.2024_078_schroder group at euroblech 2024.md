# Schröder Group at EuroBLECH 2024

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

From 22-25 October 2024, the Schröder Group will be exhibiting a selection of sheetmetal working machines on booth 11-A48 at EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany. With a slip bending role, a motorised shear and the flexible PowerBend Professional UD folding machine, the company will be presenting solutions tailored to the needs of the sheetmetal working industry.

The focus of this year's exhibition appearance will be on industrial sheetmetal working machines and automation. The highlight: a MAK 4 Evolution UD with robot module in the control system, which processes heavy sheets automatically with a robot.

The Schröder Group has teamed up with automation specialist, STARMATIK, to present a system at EuroBLECH that can be integrated into fully automated industrial production processes. A MAK 4 Evolution UD has been equipped with a Fanuc robot for this purpose. It loads the machine with up to 6mm thick sheets (3000 × 1500mm in size) and places the finished bent product on a palletising station.

Using the pneumatic stop in the Advanced Handling System and the up-and-down folding beam, the folding machine itself runs through the production process without any intervention from the robot. The entire automation solution is controlled via Schröder software. No knowledge of robotics is required, confirmed the manufacturer. The robot module in the Schröder POS 3000 control system will be available in future for all machines in the Evolution class as well as the PowerBend series.

The Schröder Group, which is celebrating its 75th anniversary this year, has also redesigned its PowerBend Industrial UD folding machine. The machine (the bi-directional folding version at EuroBLECH processes thick steel sheets of up to 5mm thick to a length of 4000mm) has been equipped with improved servo motors throughout.

"Automation and digitalisation are definitely part of our vision for the future," explained Thomas Ostermair, Sales Manager, Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH. "However, automation and flexibility can be opposites which is why, in most cases, partial automation, tool changers and rotating clamping beam (as well as individual convenience functions) are better suited to the requirements. Small batches and individual pieces are the real challenge, especially in the trade and for suppliers."

Another Schröder highlight at EuroBLECH will be the PowerBend Professional UD folding machine for up to 3mm thick sheet metal, designed to provide flexibility for plumbing companies (for example). It is equipped with an up-and-down folding beam, rotating clamping beam for two tool stations, radius step bending function and suction plate stop. This equipment enables semi-automated production and quick set-up for individual pieces.

The PDC-NC, a robust motorised sheetmetal shear with NC positioning gauge, motorised blade gap adjustment and pneumatically controlled sheet holding-down device, will also be part of Schröder's EuroBLECH display. The version at the show cuts steel sheets of up to 2mm thick but is also suitable for other materials. The pneumatic hold-down device can be deactivated for very soft materials.

"With the series cutting function, up to 99 different or repetitive stop dimensions can be preset. This means that several stop dimensions can be processed in succession or repetitions can be specified for individual dimensions," concluded the company.

The Schröder Group consists of Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH in Wessobrunn-Forst (Germany), SCHRÖDER-FASTI Technologie GmbH (Wermelskirchen, Germany) and SMU GmbH (Leinburg-Weißenbrunn, Germany).
