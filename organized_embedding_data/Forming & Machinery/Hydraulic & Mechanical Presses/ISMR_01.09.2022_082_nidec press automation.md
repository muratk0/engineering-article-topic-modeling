"The world of e-mobility is upon us. Some of the world's leading manufacturers are relying on our expertise and extensive turnkey solutions to quickly ramp up e-mobility production,"Nidec Press & Automation told /SMR.

Nidec Press & Automation offers a one-stop solutions source for electric vehicle OEMs and Tier One suppliers. Working with a single source affords strategic benefits in any production process, especially for products with rapidly evolving and highly complex technologies and manufacturing processes, such as for e-mobility components. Its latest integrated manufacturing solutions for the e-mobility market include presses, coil lines, automation systems, controls etc.

Nidec Press & Automation combines the experience and knowledge of six metalforming brands: Minster; Arisa; Kyori; Vamco; SYS and CHS.

"While each brand offers distinct e-mobility production solutions, the combined synergies create a more efficient and cost-effective path for manufacturers looking towards increased production capabilities and profits," it added.
