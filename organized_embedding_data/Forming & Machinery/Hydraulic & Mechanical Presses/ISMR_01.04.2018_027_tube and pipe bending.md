# Tube and pipe bending

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

UNISON LTD. WILL be presenting its tube and pipe bending machinery, associated tooling and software capabilities at TUBE (the International Tube and Pipe fair in Düsseldorf, Germany) from 16-20 April 2018. It will also be launching its end-facing machine for tube and pipe with multiple capabilities. Features include:

■ Medium, high and ultra-high cone & thread tube end preparation (6mm – 38mm depending upon tube size and pressure rating). 
■ Square edge tube end preparation >50.8mm. 
■ Plain V bevelling >22mm.
■ Compound bevelling 22mm - 50.8mm.   
■ Flat land bevelling >19mm.   
■ Flat land bevelling (19mm - 50.8mm).   
■ J type bevelling <50.8m.

"In addition to our new product, we will also be showcasing the new and improved EB1000, a manually-operated, 3-axis CNC precision tube bending machine. New features include mechanical design improvements for increased reliability and ease of use. It includes compatibility with Coordinate Measuring Machines (CMMs). The EB1000 is an ideal solution for small or complex tubes made from exotic materials typically found in motor sport, marine and aerospace components," added the manufacturer.

"We will also have a 38mm Breeze Twin-Head tube bending machine on the stand which is specifically designed for the highspeed manufacture of symmetrical shapes in tube or square section. Applications include the production of parts for supermarket trolleys, wheelbarrows, furniture and automotive parts."

See Unison Ltd. on stand 5E10 at Messe Düsseldorf or see www.unisonltd.com■
