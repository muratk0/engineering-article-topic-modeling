# YSM innovations

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

YSM's product range includes stamping and bending solutions integrated with operations needing a secondary process such as thread forming, welding, sub-part assembly, riveting etc. All these solutions are delivered on a single production line able to produce parts with speeds of up to 250 parts per minute.

Its modular approach means that, despite the complexity of the part or the application, its machines can be set up to run each operation independently of each other (thereby simplifying the process and reducing tool complexities and costs). For example, the slides can be adjusted to align in either radial or linear operation, so that users have the flexibility to integrate several processes into one machine with minimal adjustments.

The company launched the YSM 39T-CNC, a combination line with a set of radial (mechanical) and linear (servo/ NC-driven) slides in 2020 and, in 2022, a similar model with 100% mechanically driven linear and radial slides.

“This proved popular in the e-mobility industry where customers need to perform several complex operations on a single machine (reducing the need for large, complex stamping tools)," YSM told ISMR.

Easily configurable slides enabling either linear or radial operations, new safety features and Industry 4.0-compliant/IoT-enabled software are just some of the innovations that YSM has introduced over the last two years.

ISMR:Which issues are of prime importance for your customers? How are you addressing these issues for them?

GW: Process automation and process integration have gained new relevance due to the current skilled manpower shortage issue. Companies that are heavily dependent upon manual processes have been affected the most. They have realised the importance of reducing their dependency on manual processes and have now begun to automate and integrate these processes.

We, as a company, have delivered several projects that have removed the dependency of manual processes/operations and automated these. This has benefited customers by reducing their dependency on people while ensuring the higher productivity, consistency and reliability of processes. We are all familiar with the earlier misconception that automation would replace human workers. This is not entirely true, but automation is now able to save and even create better quality jobs after re-skilling people.

Customers are also focused on cheaper and faster manufacturing. Our new servo machines can deliver high production speeds, coupled with accuracy. Today's market calls for ever-smaller runs and shorter delivery times, especially in the e-Mobility space where we see the number of EVs gradually increasing.

Our new servo-driven machines are the answer to those market requirements. The modular nature of these machines enabled us to configure them to specific "special requirements" that the customer may have (e.g. larger and higher-tonnage presses with more space for stamping). We can custom build to customers' requirements.

Key benefits of the servo technology are faster set-up times, lower tooling costs and suitability for small-batch production and prototyping. They can also be easily set up in linear or radial configuration.
