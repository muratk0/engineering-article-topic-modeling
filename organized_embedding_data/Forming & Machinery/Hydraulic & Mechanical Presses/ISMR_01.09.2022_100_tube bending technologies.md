# Tube bending technologies

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Unison Tube LLC, the North American division of UK-based Unison Ltd, will present several innovative tube manipulation technologies at FABTECH (North America's largest metalforming, fabricating, welding and finishing event) from 8-10 November 2022.

Unison machines on show at Booth C10217 will include an all-electric Breeze Twin-Head tube bending machine and one of the company's new Synergy hybrid, multi-stack tube benders. Unison will also use FABTECH to launch the most advanced version of its Opt2Sim tube bending simulation software, with several free software licenses up for grabs.

"Equipped with the recently upgraded Unibend control, an operating system offering speed enhancements of up to 25%, the all-electric Unison Twin Head 1 1/2" (maximum tube diameter) machines will precision-bend two ends of a tube simultaneously. This capability makes them well suited to the high-speed manufacture of symmetrical tubular shapes typically found in automotive, agricultural and furniture applications," Unison told ISMR.

Officially launched at the MACH machine tool show in the UK earlier this year, Unison's new Synergy machines combine electric and hydraulic operation with high levels of control. Developed for organisations that carry out high-volume, repetitive tube bending tasks, Synergy models are available in 2" (50mm) and 3" (80mm) versions. They feature a servo-driven bend arm, carriage, plane of bend and carriage side shift, with a hydraulically controlled mandrel, pressure die and clamping system.

In the latest iteration of its Opt2Sim tube manipulation software, Unison makes it easier to import and extract tube data and includes versions for simulating the bending process, creating batch files and scanning tube coordinates. Opt2Sim provides users with a complete end-to-end solution, from taking 3D STEP files to bending tube within a single software suite. Delegates visiting Unison's FABTECH booth will have the chance to win one of several Opt2Sim software licenses.

"We are delighted to be returning to FABTECH with such inspiring tube-bending technologies," commented Unison Tube's Vice President, Stuart Singleton. "We are particularly excited to be unveiling our latest Opt2Sim software, as this really will revolutionise tube bending simulation for organisations of all sizes."■
