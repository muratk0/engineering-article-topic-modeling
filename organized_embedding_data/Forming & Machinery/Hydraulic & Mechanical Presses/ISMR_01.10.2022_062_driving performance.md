# Driving performance

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

At EuroBLECH 2022, Voith Turbo will be presenting its compact variable-speed pump drives, DrivAx IQ4 and DrivAx RQ4. The new drives expand the performance spectrum of the DrivAx family.

Other highlights at the drive specialist's booth include the self-contained servo drives DrivAx CLDP, whose ten-year history is being continued with new sizes, and the DrivAx CLCP, developed for robot applications. The specially developed DrivAx PDSC and DrivAx PSH press drives are suitable for use in presses from 500kN upwards.

“DrivAx PSH is our resource-saving servohydraulic press drive for almost unlimited high forces with high productivity. In the DrivAx PSH press-drive, the variable-speed DrivAx IPS pump drives replace the valve and control technology. This allows optimum adaptation of force and speed to the press process and simplifies the design of the drive," explained Voith Turbo.

"The intelligent control allows very flexible use of the press. Productivity increases and the parts produced are of exceptionally high quality. The complete system has a modular design and includes the aggregate technology, control technology and software as well as sensors and safety modules," it added.

Founded in 1867, the company today has around 20,000 employees, sales of €4.3 billion and locations in over 60 countries worldwide. It is one of the larger family-owned companies in Europe. The Group division, Voith Turbo, is part of the Voith Group.
