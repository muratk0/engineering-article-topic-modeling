# P2Xe: COMPACT HYBRID PANEL BENDER.

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

## PERFECT BALANCE BETWEEN SPACE AND CONSUMPTION, PRODUCTIVITY AND FLEXIBILITY. WITHIN EVERYBODY’S REACH.

With its compact and modular design, the P2Xe is a true concentration of innovation that is capable of making up to 165 mm high and 2180 mm long bends on a surface of only 18 square meters. Power consumption is less than 9 kW. The P2Xe is user friendly. It is equipped with proprietary numerical control system, intuitive graphical interface and simple programming. Thanks to the automatic blankholder set-up, it minimizes operator handling, enabling fast production of small batch sizes.
