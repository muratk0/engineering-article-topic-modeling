# Magnetic timing belt for steel sheets

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

At EuroBLECH, Goudsmit Magnetics will be demonstrating a magnetic timing belt with high speed steel sheets transported to and from a press

FOR THE RAPID and precise placement of steel sheets in a press, Goudsmit Magnetics recently developed a magnetic timing belt that transports steel sheets to and from the press. At the EuroBLECH trade fair (Hall 12, Stand A16) in Hanover, the company will give a demonstration of this timing belt in operation.

Transport to and fro is done with an acceleration speed of 6m/s2. If desired, the belt can be provided with permanent or switchable electro magnets. In this latter case, the magnetic force can be switched on and off electrically, with the option for a robot to take over the sheets. The belt is suitable for a sheet thickness of 0.7 to 2.5mm. To avoid the possibility of two sheets being picked up at the same time, the system is equipped with two compact, pneumatically-switched sheet separators. Magnetic grippers, with a friction pad, handle the sheets without damaging them and, where necessary, provide sufficient friction.

The newly developed magnetic sheet separators are made of very strong Neodymium Iron Boron magnets and can be switched on and off pneumatically. Pneumatic separators separate stacked steel sheets, which makes it possible for a robot to lift them one by one from the stack. Magnetic grippers, with or without a rubber pad, lift up the steel sheets and handle them without displacement or damage. These grippers are switched on and off pneumatically and can handle sheet material including perforated sheets.
