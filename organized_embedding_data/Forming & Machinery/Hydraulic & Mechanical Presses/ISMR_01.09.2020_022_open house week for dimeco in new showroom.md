# Open House week for DIMECO in new showroom

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

SHEET METAL SPECIALIST DIMECO reacted quickly to the postponement of EUROBLECH by organising an open house week to welcome customers and partners in Pirey, France, from 12-16 October 2020, almost the same week that EUROBLECH 2020 was originally planned.

“We thought it would be a smart move to introduce our new technologies and new solutions such as the LINACUT (coil-fed laser cutting line) and the LINAPUNCH (coil-fed punching line) on which parts will be produced, as well as some amazing heavy-duty decoilers (up to 35 tons) and high-capacity straighteners. Also on show during the event will be some 6-ton capacity ROTOBLOC STACKMATIK quick toolchange trucks, fitted with push-pull devices and other ROTOBLOC quick die-change and quick coil-change systems, the core of our SMED (Single-Minute Exchange of Dies) methodology, which brings users high returns on investment,” the French manufacturer told ISMR.

“We see this as an opportunity to show more equipment than we had originally planned for EUROBLECH with more space in our new showroom next to our main manufacturing site in France.”

Established in 1957, DIMECO is a French family-owned company specialising in coilfed flexible fabricating lines as well as coil handling and press feeding lines, with users all over the world.
