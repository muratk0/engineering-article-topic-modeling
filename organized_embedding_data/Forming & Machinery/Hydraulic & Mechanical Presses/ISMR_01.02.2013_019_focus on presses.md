Interlaken offers turnkey fully automated hot stamping press systems. Hot stamping is the process of evenly heating a steel blank, forming it at precise forming pressures and then cooling it at controlled temperatures in the die. A uniform "quenching" or hardening cooling process takes place while the hydraulic press dwells for a few seconds. Because the part is rapidly cooled in the die while the press is dwelling, springback is minimal.

The fully automated hydraulic press system can be easily configured to manufacture a variety of parts. With quick change dies and tooling, the Interlaken hot stamping press can be quickly configured to run a new part with very little down time.

Interlaken Technology Corporation delivered a warm forming press system to General Motors in Warren, Michigan. This gives GM the capability to develop new ways to form aluminium, magnesium and steel panels into revolutionary shapes that could enhance design capabilities and lightweight vehicles for improved fuel efficiency. Standard Interlaken warm forming systems are capable of forming aluminium and magnesium at elevated temperatures to 350°C. Higher temperature solutions are available.

“At room temperature, aluminium and magnesium perform poorly in challenging forming applications. Warm forming of aluminium and magnesium allows for tighter formed radii, better elongation and deep draws that cannot be achieved at room temperature. In some cases, aluminium elongations can be more than 100 per cent,” it told ISMR.

Automobile-related capital investment has been rising in Asian countries, such as Indonesia, Thailand, and India, as well as in Mexico, Brazil and Russia.

Established in 1937, Chinese pressmaker JIER has produced a variety of mechanical presses from C-frame to straight-sided presses (60t to 7,500t) including automated high speed press lines. It also produces transfer presses, progressive presses, cut-to-length lines and blank washers and has made use of servo press technology and NC hydraulic die cushions in its technical solutions.

Komatsu designs large presses for forming automobile roofs and doors. Its large presses are represented by a tandem press for a variety of wide applications and a transfer press for high productivity. Its hybrid AC servo press, H1F, achieves high performance by combining advanced CNC controls and a hybrid drive mechanism.

“Servos offer a variety of benefits, including a major increase in forming performance and productivity due to its free motion mechanism. The accurate linear sensor maintains high bottom dead centre accuracy over a long period of time for strong performance in blanking and precision forming of sheet metal. As a clutch is used, a major reduction in maintenance costs can be achieved. Thanks to the newly developed hybrid drive mechanism, large outputs from small motors have been gained and power costs are very low,” said the manufacturer.

Its large high speed transfer blanking press for processing car body panels improves productivity by 75 per cent, it told ISMR. Komatsu also provides blanking presses and coil lines.

The Schuler Group posted excellent figures for the past fiscal year 2011/12 (ending 30 September) with strong growth in sales and earnings. The press manufacturer raised sales by 27.9 per cent to € 1.23 billion, almost doubling revenues within two years. The Group profit more than doubled year-on-year to € 51.8 million.

“The past fiscal year was the best in our 173-year history,” explained Schuler CEO, Stefan Klebert, on presentation of the company’s annual results. Schuler once again displayed strong growth of 29.9 percent to € 401.2 million in Asia, which accounts for the largest share of sales.

In Germany, the company posted sales of € 394.9 million, representing growth of 33 per cent. The rest of Europe contributed € 245.7 million (+61.2 percent) to sales. The company anticipates sales of around € 1.2 billion in fiscal year 2012/13.

Schuler has launched its largest investment programme of the last ten years with more than € 80 million for the past and current fiscal years. A major focus area is China, which is already the world’s largest market for car sales with tremendous growth potential. The company is currently tripling production capacity at its Dalian facility to a total floor space of 16,000 square metres. The factory expansion is expected to be completed in spring 2013. Preparations are also under way to expand sales, procurement, and local development capacities at the Shanghai site. Additional service support centres will also be established in several Chinese cities.

The company unveiled its new TwinServo Technology press generation - the new press line is more compact, flexible and silent than most conventional presses as well as more economical in its energy consumption, said Dipl.-Ing., MBA Stefan Klebert, Chief Executive Officer of Schuler. In addition to energy efficiency, a further future area for the Group is lightweight construction. The company is developing new markets, such as presses for packaging, large pipes and railway wheels.

THE MINSTER Machine Company showcased its NTCH-25 notching press and an articulated robot producing lamination rotors and stators during EuroBLECH 2012. The NTCH features Minster technologies such as single-point eccentric steel crankshaft, oil film bearings and hydraulic quick-lift.

In addition, a slide shut-height adjustment lock-up, oversized wrist pin and high tensile connection provide a 75 per cent snap-through rating. Also as standard is a fully-programmable precision direct drive servo indexer designed for high torque and high speed precision.

Nidec Minster Corporation recently introduced the MMC press line of mechanical stamping presses to markets in North and South America. The machines will be manufactured by Ningbo CFG Machinery Company at its factory in Ningbo, China.

“The MMC press line offers metal stampers a wide range of cost-effective solutions for their mechanical press needs; including single and two-point gap and unitised frame straightsides, and a full complement of tie rod construction straightside machines with crankshaft or eccentric geared drives in two or four-point configurations up to 1,200 tons capacity,” it told ISMR.

All machines are equipped with a variety of features to help improve productivity, including wet clutch & brake (most models), hydraulic overload protection, monitored automatic lubrication and Wintriss brand controls as standard. In addition, many straightside presses feature double-geared drives for high torque and working energy, and feature full 8- point gibbing for maximum tooling life and improved part quality.

Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG presented its new NC production system (GRM-NC) at EuroBLECH. The machine is designed to handle a large range of stamping and forming parts made of wire and strip, with very short tool set-up times, rapid response to customers’ requests and easy operation. All tools for the GRM 50 and 80 models can be adapted and optimised on the GRM-NC.

Founded in 1962, Shieh Yih Machinery Co., Ltd. (SEYI), with headquarters in Taiwan, is one of the world’s largest press manufacturers. Today, it supplies customers in industry sectors such as automotive/transportation, 3C (consumer electronics, computers and communications), home appliances, metal hardware, architecture and medical instruments. It employs 550 people (45 per cent in Taiwan, 55 per cent in China) and boasts total factory space of 34,500 square metres (12,500 in Taiwan/ 22,000 in China). At a ceremony attended by ISMR, SEYI celebrated its 50th anniversary at its global headquarters in Taoyuan, Taiwan on 2 November 2012.

SEYI manufactures mechanical presses (straightside and servo presses) ranging in size from 25 to 2400 tons, at facilities located in Taiwan and the People’s Republic of China. At its original location in Taoyuan, Taiwan, SEYI operates a 12,500-square metre facility and, in 2003, began production in Mainland China at an 11,000-square metre facility in Kunshan, Jiangsu Province. Combined production capacity at the Taoyuan and Kunshan plants approaches 4,000 presses annually.

Construction of a second 11,000-square metre facility in Kunshan was completed in 2009 and began production in 2011. The new facility in Kunshan can manufacture up to 300 presses annually, ranging in size from 300 to 4,000 tons. On 14 August 2012, SEYI commenced construction of a new office facility in China. SEYI also recently announced that the company had established a German subsidiary in Frankfurt.

Despite strong sales headwinds, SEYI reported increased profits and margins. Net income for the six months ending 30 June 2012 was TWD 92.1 million (US$ 3.1 million), a 6.7 per cent increase from last year’s net income of TWD 86.3 million (US$ 2.9 million). SEYI Europe GmbH exhibited its 200-ton direct drive servo press SD1-200, its latest and most updated technology, at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover this October. With its focus on environmental protection and cost saving efficiency, SEYI Europe GmbH unveiled new production methods for stamping, bending, drawing and coining from one single workstation via the SEYI direct-drive servo press SDseries. The line can be manufactured with 80-500 ton press forces. ■
