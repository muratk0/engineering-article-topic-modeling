# Strip and wire forming

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

WHILE EUROPEAN MACHINERY suppliers face tough competition nowadays, we are confident that our equipment can meet customers requirements," Taiwanese manufacturer, Yih Shen Machinery Co. Ltd. (YSM), told ISMR.

"There are three major reasons for this. Firstly, stamping and bending lines are a competitive alternative to traditional high-speed presses because this process significantly reduces material waste. This translates into a lower cost per part. Secondly, our production lines are very cost-effective because they are ideal for small-batch production where tool-change time is minimal, tools are inexpensive and so the total cost of ownership (TCO) is very competitive. Thirdly, in today's world, versatility is key. Our simple 'modular tooling' enables designing and manufacturing to be easily adapted by the enduser. The 'easy-to-use' operating system does not need an intensive training programme and can be delivered straight to the shop floor."

YSM will be exhibiting its YSM NC-26T strip and wire forming machine at MACH Tool in Poland (Hall 6, booth 34). It is a full servo automatic forming system with 11 axes (up to 16 axes can be used). Each forming axis is operated by a servo, replacing the traditional cam-driven style, to increase the efficiency of mould changes, suitable for small-scale production.

"40% of our machines are used in the automotive and motorcycle sectors, and a further 40 per cent is employed in the miniature circuit breaker sector – the rest is for other sectors. Using our machines to meet increasing demand from Asia, or high-end, eco-friendly automotive trends globally, will help European producers to level up their production quality and efficiency," concluded YSM.
