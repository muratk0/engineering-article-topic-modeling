Plasma cutting system cuts grating at high speeds

Soitaab’s plasma grate cutting system cuts interrupted surfaces such as steel and aluminum grating at high speeds. It uses a Kjellberg 360-amp Hi-Focus plasma torch and a patented Hotwire indirect plasma cutting technology. In this standard plasma torch with ohmic height sensing, a welding wire is fed automatically underneath the torch tip. This wire acts as the anode, completing the electrical circuit without a direct connection to the workpiece.

The wire simulates a continuous surface under the torch and the grate beneath is cut as if it were a flat plate. There is no slow-down of the plasma torch, no preheating of the surface, and no loss of the plasma arc as the torch moves across the interrupted grate surface. The result is a clean, high-quality, accurate cut with little to no rework on the surface, the company reports.

Bending system combines press brake and panel bending

Salvagnini’s FlexCell is an automated kit blanking and short-run bending system that combines press brake and panel bending technologies.

Cut and laser-marked blanks are received from a fiber laser or S4 punch+shear machine. Bar codes on the blanks then are scanned into the system. Part sequencing is optimized automatically, and the panel bender and press brake are set up automatically for bending.
