# GLA-WEL GmbH has taken significant strides towards creating an automated, digitalised workflow after integrating LVD bending technology and software into its production processes.

[TARİH: 01.03.2021 ISMR]

COMPANY GLA-WEL GmbH

WEBSITE www.gla-wel.de

WORKS WITH Mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium

INDUSTRY

Sheet metalworking subcontractor to a wide range of industries including wastewater technology, automotive industry, sports equipment, machines and special systems.

## EQUIPMENT

Easy-Form 135/30 press brake. Easy-Form 320/45 press brake. ToolCell 220 Plus/40 press brake CADMAN-SDI, CADMAN-B, CADMAN-JOB software.

Based in Melle, Lower Saxony (Germany) GLA-WEL is a medium-sized, familyrun business focused on sheet metal cutting, forming and fabrication. What sets it apart from its peers is its own software house, Octoflex, which has created an ERP system that is currently being used by about 100 customers.

## An eye on flexibility

GLA-WEL runs three LVD bending systems, a 220t ToolCell with automatic tool changer and two Easy-Form press brakes (135 and 320 tons). It is also making extensive use of LVD's CADMAN®-SDI file importing and calculating software, CADMAN-B offline programming software and CADMAN-JOB workflow software.

GLA-WEL commercial director, Marcel Kemner, confirmed that, as a family business GLA-WEL is run in a very person-to-person way, which means it can be very flexible and responsive.

Stephan Glahs and Simon Welkener are two of the managing directors of the business, and also head Octoflex.

"We do a lot of small series work to short timescales, so this is important. But the bigger we got, the less flexible we became. So, it became vital to make our processes leaner so that we could manufacture more quickly and efficiently. This is where digitalisation comes into play. Building on our Octoflex ERP system, we are creating a digital process flow so that we can automate a lot of our operations and administrative tasks," he explained.

He added that the decision to work with LVD came down to the ability of its software to support this.

"We deal with a lot of customers in the sheet metal sector and have to integrate our ERP system with different manufacturers' software. We saw that the connectivity of the LVD software could significantly improve our manufacturing capabilities. For example it would also allow us to connect to non-LVD laser cutting machines," explained Stephan Glahs.

## Significant time savings

Stephan Glahs added that, in terms of digitalisation and integration with ERP, in the past, the focus was always just on the lasercutting machines. Bending was an external process that relied on shop floor data input by the operator to link it back into the ERP system. This meant that costs, times etc. had to be estimated at the planning stage, rather than based on hard data. This reliance on a manual skilled process was a major challenge for GLA-WEL.

"Now, with CADMAN-SDI, when you put a job into the ERP system, the files are automatically transferred to SDI in the background and you immediately get back the production time and the development for the laser-cut blank, So, two steps that would previously have been done more or less manually are now carried out completely automatically," explained Simon Welkener.

"You can't simply measure the throughput time based on how long the part spends in the bending department. It starts when the customer places the order and ends when you deliver it. The bending itself is only a very small part of that. The cost comes as much from the preparation time as from the bending itself. Before we had the LVD pressbrakes, the customer placed an order, the sales administrator created an order in the system, then production planning set out the production stages. Next, the order went into design engineering where they calculated the laser blank geometry. Then, it went back to Production Planning to release it to the shop floor," he added.

That system has now changed with its new LVD purchases.

GLA-WEL confirmed that, on the first of the LVD press brakes to be installed, it has seen an 18.52% increase in productivity.

"Now, when we get an order from a customer, if they can supply STEP files we can go straight into production. In the past, we needed two days just for administration before we could start making an item. We can now eliminate these two days. We only need to put the order in the system, check that the drawings are OK and we can then send it straight into manufacturing. We have saved 50% of our administration time," clarified Welkener.

The next significant set of time savings come from automated offline programming in CADMAN-B.

"We used to program at the machine, so you can imagine how that has improved our production times and capacity," said Stephan Glahs. "Now, we simply import the STEP file and it is done. The amount of time saved across all these areas is enormous."

CADMAN-JOB comes into play when the order goes into production.

"Because you define the flow of jobs that have to be completed on a machine in CADMAN-JOB, you can re-arrange them to suit current requirements and machine availability and minimise non-productive time," explained Stephan Glahs.

The original intention had just been to buy the ToolCell press brake, which was suitable for automating fast turnaround and small batch work, but, according to Marcel Kemner, the company bought three machines because of the Easy-Form® Laser angle measurement system for accuracy and further time savings. "In the past, we would bend the part, take it out, measure it and bend it again (sometimes three or four times). With the Easy-Form you bend it, take it out and it is finished," added Kemner.

Overall, GLA-WEL confirmed that, on the first of the LVD press brakes to be installed, it has seen an 18.52% increase in productivity, compared to a competitor's two-year-old machine.

Octoflex customer portal

The next step is to take digitalisation even further, said Stephan Glahs.

"We have a customer portal where the customer can upload his drawings, get a quote and order complete laser-cut and formed parts. These come through automatically into our production. Now, we have also started to offer the Octoflex package to other sheet metalworking companies so that they can use the ERP system and portal for their customers," offered Glahs.
