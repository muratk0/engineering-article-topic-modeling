# Plate straightening purchase

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

On 15 September 2023, Miilux Oy officially signed the purchase agreement for an advanced Faccin R9-2500x30 platestraightening machine. Miilux's decision to choose Faccin once again is a testament to its experience with the Faccin R series machine, which was successfully installed at its facility in Turkey in 2015. This new machine will effectively replace an outdated existing one, further enhancing Miilux's operational efficiency.

Miilux Oy is a top-tier service provider catering to the needs of metal, mining and stone industries, as well as earthmoving and mechanical engineering companies in Finland and globally. Its production facilities are in Raahe, Finland (a hub that offers convenient access by sea and road) with two branches in Poland and Turkey. Miilux is always on the lookout for ways to improve its production capabilities and invest in the latest technology.

The Finnish Steel Service Centre found itself in a predicament; its trusty plate leveller, a loyal workhorse for over five decades, was showing signs of wear and tear. Recognizing the need for improvement, the company decided it was time to seek a replacement.

"We decided to invest in a new straightening machine to replace the current old one. The technical life of our actual straightening roller was approaching its end. Hard and strong Miilux steels require more power from the machine. We wanted to invest in product quality. With this investment, the level properties of Miilux steels will be top class," explained Olli Mattila, Sales and Production Director, Miilux Oy.

## Making the selection

After careful consideration that took over three years, the decision-making process reached its conclusion with the selection of two brands for this procurement.

"During the evaluation of various options, we took into account the successful implementation of a Faccin plate straightening machine at the Miilux branch in Turkey four years ago. The outcome of this installation was highly satisfactory, demonstrating the effectiveness of the machine in terms of reliability and high performance," Miilux confirmed.

"We waited for technical reports from our factory in Turkey, where the straightening machine has been functioning for four years. The operator feedback was very positive. The Vossi Group, Faccin Group's agent, has been our partner for several of our machine investments. We have a long partnership with both the Faccin and Vossi Groups; the technical assistance provided is always toplevel. [We made this decision because of a host of benefits such as quality, reliability, price, compliance with our needs etc.]" listed Mattila.

The efficiency of the maintenance service and promptness of response time greatly influenced Miilux's decision-making when it came to new investments.

"Often, the purchase price represents just a small part of the lifecycle cost of the machine. Companies invest in machines for fabrication needs and, when they work, they generate invaluable incomes for the company. It is of the utmost importance that the maintenance of the machines respects high-quality standards and that any technical problem is resolved quickly. From our experience, we know that Faccin Group and Vossi Group have invested in these issues," added Mattila.

According to Mattila, the substantial cost savings achieved through the implementation of the Faccin plate leveller solution at the Miilux Turkish branch can be attributed to its energy efficiency and production capabilities. As a result, the Faccin Group has been chosen once again to provide the new acquisition for the Finnish headquarters.

"In our case, the challenge was the limited space available, which affected this machine purchase. Faccin Group addressed the challenges relating to the machine location, designing the so-called 'Miilux Style' model for us, the Faccin R9-2500x30 plate straightening machine. The machine will be placed as an extension of our tempering line i.e. almost all tempering plates are run through the straightening roll. The straightening capacity of the new machine is up to 20mm material thickness when it comes to wear steels. This increase in quality gives our sales managers a new opportunity to sell more wear steels to more destinations. We are looking forward to next summer when the new plate straightening machine will be part of our production process here in Raahe," concluded Mattila.

## A customised solution

"A couple of years ago, we acquired a Faccin 4HEL series four-roll plate bending machine. Previously, we had a Faccin HAV series three-roll variable geometry bending machine in use for a long time, so we have a lot of expertise with Faccin equipment. Our operators are satisfied with these userfriendly and reliable machines (both located in Finland)," confirmed Mattila.

"Thanks to the new Faccin solution, a more efficient roll will be achieved and better correction for larger and harder material strengths," concluded Miilux.
