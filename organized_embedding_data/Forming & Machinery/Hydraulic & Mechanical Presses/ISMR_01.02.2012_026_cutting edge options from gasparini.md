# Cutting edge options from Gasparini

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

AT GASPARINI’S recent Open House, new ranges of shears and plasma cutters were launched.

Completely redesigned to include a large number of innovative solutions, Gasparini’s shears have shearing capacities of up to 30mm with cut lengths ranging from 2000 to 10000mm. Gasparini’s new X-Cut guillotine shearing machines feature a blade gap adjustment system that guide the precise positioning of the blades every 200mm along the entire length, an anti-twist device and a newly developed hydraulic system that improves operational efficiency.

A new LED lighting system for the cutting line provides a view of the edges of the sheet, thus reducing operator fatigue. New accessories include the pneumatic sheet support with three operating modes: standard, scrap and small piece collection box, and frontal piece return. Innovative solutions for the Gasparini plasma cutting system include modularity, a single trolley for all installable accessories and a mobile fume hood. The numeric control unit developed by Gasparini integrates the simplicity of onboard machine use with sophisticated off-line management, nesting and automatic programming systems.

The new waste collection and conveyance system is also designed to facilitate work activities and has been improved to limit the use of compressed air (energy savings) and manoeuvring space: two square metres behind the machine. Another new feature is the new portal upon which the torch is mounted, which provides more room for standard applications (marking, oxy cutting etc.), as well as for special applications. Gasparini plasma cutting equipment can be equipped with a vision system for determining the origin of cut. ■
