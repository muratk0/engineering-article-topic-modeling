# New ‘combined' bending line

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Jorns AG, as a Swiss manufacturer of folders/bending systems, exhibited for the first time at EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover with Krasser GmbH (a manufacturer of sheet-metal cutting and coil-processing systems) and SPÓLKA STO-LARCZYK. Customers of the manufacturers are active in the tinsmith, roofing and façade construction sectors.

"There is clearly an increasing demand in the market for cutting and bending machines and, to a certain extent, the decoiling system and recoiling system, to be combined and 'communicate' with each other to increase capacity, improve productivity and reduce lead times and error rates as well as material consumption," explained Jorns.

The three manufacturers decided to close this gap under the motto “Together we are STRONG" and offered a “total package" as a solution on a few square metres at the exhibition in Germany.

## New JC100 control

Jorns also introduced its new JC100 control to the general public for the first time at the show. It expects the Jorns JDB double-bending machine to be delivered with the new JC100 from the beginning of January 2023. The semi-automatic Jorns JB folder and fully automatic Jorns JDB double bending folder can be configured in lengths of 3.2 to 12.2 metres and boast bending capacities for sheet metal of up to 3mm.

"The new JC100 machine control offers a direct interface to the Bendex Enterprise Suite from MicroSea. The direct integration allows a bending profile (consisting of bends; notches; holes; mitre cuts etc.) to be obtained from the JC100 control directly from the higher-level Bendex Enterprise Suite system. A manual and file-based data export in a transfer format, such as XML, is therefore no longer necessary," explained Jorns.

For customers who do not use the interface to the Bendex Enterprise Suite platform from MicroSea, Jorns and Krasser offer a direct interface.

## Cutting and feeding

The SE2 roll shears are electrically driven with an adjustable roller shear head where the air gap and overlap rate of the rollers can be adjusted according to the quality and thickness of the material. They run on a precision guideway that is mounted on the machine frame. The system is driven by a servo motor and positive transmission via toothed belt. The cutting length is programmed using the control system.

The roll shears are fitted with a multibeam monitoring laser. The module checks the initial situation in semi-automatic mode and brings the shears to a stop if a beam is interrupted during the cutting process. Cutting speed on the new line is approx. 2000mm/s forward and 3000mm/s in reverse in fully automatic mode, restricted to 1350mm/s in semi-automatic mode for both directions (for 2mm steel, 3mm aluminium and 1.5mm stainless steel).

To increase the overall productivity of the Jorns double bender through an automated loading process, the Jorns JS side infeed is an option. Sheets with a thickness of up to 3mm steel and a width of up to 1000mm can be transported (by 12 vacuum cups) from a stacking table on the left of the machine and drawn into the machine at a speed of up to 2000mm/s. A stack of sheets up to a height of 150mm can be placed on the stacking table, ready for processing. Jorns double bending machines, which are equipped with a divided clamping system and a JS side infeed, can automatically load and bend up to four identical bending profiles per bending cycle.

The new Jorns JPM profile manipulator uses vacuum suction units for picking up the flat sheet or the already bent bending profile. For the finished bending profiles, users can choose between two solutions: the Jorns JR Ramp output system (manual) or the Jorns JF conveyor system (automatic output system).

To feed pre-cut sheet-metal strip, up to a width of 1250mm, from the side onto the infeed tables, Jorns offers an optional transfer table which can be mounted on the left and/or right of the machine.

## Cleaning, deburring and edge rounding

Wöhler's modular cleaning machines and deburring machines have been specially developed for metalworking to meet specific requirements for removing slag, burrs and oxide. The EdgeRacer portfolio of machines processes steel, stainless steel and aluminium in mixed mode as a modern component in modern sheet metal processing.

Depending upon the material, thickness and intended use of the sheet to be processed, possible applications for EdgeRacer machines in sheet-metal processing are very diverse. These range from removing slag from flame-cut parts to deburring and edge rounding on inner and outer edges to finishing the sheet surface. EdgeRacer also offers suitable machines for the removal of cutting oils before deburring and for dust removal before further processing.

“EdgeRacer also sets completely new standards in energy efficiency. EdgeRacer machines require up to 90% less energy than comparable machines. This is very important for every manufacturer in view of continuously rising energy costs and leads to rapid amortisation," Wöhler told ISMR.

The EdgeRacer range of machines include:

- EdgeRacer the Washer: This removes cutting oils and contaminants before further processing.

- EdgeRacer the Grinder: A universal machine for removing slag, burr and oxide as well as for edge rounding.

- EdgeRacer E Series: Single-sided deburring machine for removing burr and oxide layer.

- EdgeRacer D Series: Deburring machine working on both sides for removing burr and oxide layer.

- EdgeRacer the Finisher: This machine gives a parallel brushed surface finish to sheet metal.

- EdgeRacer the Deduster: This technology removes the dust of upstream processing.

## EdgeRacer the Washer

EdgeRacer the Washer removes the oils used in laser cutting or punching as well as any resulting contamination.

"Manufacturers of sheet-metal parts like to use oil to extend the service life of their tools and to increase the performance of modern laser-cutting systems. After that, grease, oil and adhering dirt are undesirable. EdgeRacer the Washer removes these oil or grease residues and production-typical contaminants in a feed-through operation. EdgeRacer the Washer can be operated stand-alone or in line with EdgeRacer deburring machines or deburring machines from other manufacturers," explained Wöhler.

“Operators appreciate the clean and dry parts that leave EdgeRacer the Washer, which are perfectly prepared for further processing. This also applies to parts with 3D contours. Continuous separation of oil and solids from the water-based cleaning medium extends the change intervals of the cleaning medium," it added.

Soiled parts are introduced by the infeed conveyor into the washer and are cleaned on both sides in a feed-through operation. A sprayed cleaning agent separates the oil from the sheet metal. Two brushes then remove grease, oil and solids for clean surfaces. Finally, rollers push and squeeze off the last water residues. The workpieces leave EdgeRacer the Washer clean and dry, as well as prepared for direct further processing. The cleaning agent preserves the surfaces for subsequent processing.

Two models (EdgeRacerW 300 and EdgeRacer W 1500) offer working widths of 300mm or 1500mm, respectively. They clean and dry sheets on both sides, in one pass, and also clean metal sheet with 3D contours

"Environmentally friendly, due to their oil and solids separator, they also guarantee no sludge formation," added Wöhler.

## EdgeRacer the Grinder

EdgeRacer the Grinder is the universal machine from the EdgeRacer family for all sheet-metalworking applications, from removing slag to deburring and rounding edges and removing oxide layers. It can be equipped with different types of brushes to perform all grinding tasks.

“EdgeRacer the Grinder removes the slag produced on the underside during plasmacutting or oxyfuel cutting. As it processes the incoming parts from below, there is no need for tedious turning of the parts. The disc brushes are adjusted so that they almost touch the underside. The steel pins then knock off the slag. The machine also removes stubborn punch burrs and laser beads as well as laser splatter. Operators can determine the rounding at the edges via the brush setting," Wöhler told ISMR.

It can also deburr fine sheet or sheet with cut-outs (3D workpieces). The grinding brushes can be advanced to above transport level so that the complete workpiece edge is processed. The oxide disc is used to remove oxide layers on the inner and outer edges.

“Depending on the width of the parts to be processed, manufacturers can choose between two processing modes. Choose single-row operation to take full advantage of the working width, whether for wide parts or for parallel feeding. To optimise wear on the tools, the brushes change position automatically. For narrower workpieces, choose double-row machining. This way, you achieve twice the output in just one pass," outlined Wöhler.

The machine offers a working width of 1500mm, with speeds of 0.3-3.0m/minute, and a space requirement of 3100 x 1300 x 2500mm. It can accept part heights of ≤102mm and part lengths of ≥385mm (shorter on pallets).
