# THE FINISHING TOUCH

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

ISMR caught up with Piet Kooman, Timesavers’ sales and marketing director, to discover its latest new initiatives and product launches.

“With the newly launched 22 series rotary brush machine, a fully standardised machine portfolio and a new digital and training strategy in hand, Timesavers had a lot to tell ISMR….”

DUTCH SURFACE finishing specialist, Timesavers, specialises in a broad selection of rotary brush machines for precision grinding, finishing, edge rounding and deburring metal plates. Operating from three facilities in the USA, the Netherlands and Taiwan, the organisation has placed a strong focus on innovation and R&D. In 2016, it won the prestigious Red Dot award for industrial design for its 42 series Rotary Brush (RB) machine.

ISMR caught up with Piet Kooman, its sales and marketing director, to discover its latest new initiatives and product launches.

## metal professionals? What about the climate in 2021?

PK: The first reaction was shock – as lockdown was imposed. After two weeks, people started settling down but our business levels were still good and customers were still ordering machines. We have seen a small sales dip in September, but this is to be expected – a normal pattern around exhibitions and Summer holidays. I expect business to pick up again in October and November, as usual.

The real issue is what will happen in 2021 and nobody really knows the answer to this. Much depends upon finding a vaccine for COVID as well as the creativity and resilience of manufacturers. Many have now adjusted to lower, but perhaps smarter, working hours compared to the incredibly busy year for all of us in 2019.

We are certainly debating internally how we will approach trade show attendance in the future. We generally take part in thirty-five exhibitions per year and many of these may not now be necessary. I think we will need to be more selective about the exhibitions that we choose, participating only in the ones that we feel would bring the most revenue benefit for us, and then supplementing them with digital events in the future.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements?

PK: We have now completed the full standardisation of our deburring, edge rounding and surface finishing machine family (600-1600). We are currently building our new, smaller 22 series 600 WRBW rotary brush machine and have already received first orders for it. It is our latest new launch this year. We are very proud of this compact 22 series machine (600mm x 1100mm x 1600mm). At the beginning of the year, we sold the largest customised precision grinding machine we have ever sold to a Dutch company, Van Geenen. This customer bought his first machine from us in 1984 and this will now be his fourth Timesavers’ machine. The machine’s table size is 2.1m x 8.5m and it can process a wide range of materials such as stainless steel, steel, aluminium and plastics. The machine will be an important source of revenue for Van Geenen. We also plan to take other customers to see this large machine as they may wish to outsource work of this type and magnitude to Van Geenen.

ISMR: How has the COVID-19 crisis affected your company’s operations?

PK: We have optimised all our processes over the course of this year, and especially during lockdown, to improve customer relationships and offer extra support to them.

As far as our internal operations are concerned, we build our machines in a cell (with just one person) so social distancing was not an issue for us in the factory. It was more of an issue for the personnel in our offices, so we had to implement social distancing procedures there. Fifty per cent of our office staff is now working from home and the remainder are working at the company. All our production staff are back at work now.

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus for the company right now?

PK: There are three different strategies that we are employing right now. The first is standardising the design of our product range, with the new 22 series 600 machine.

Secondly, we now have a dedicated operation focusing specifically on stainless steel polishing sales. This is a key market for us and we want to continue increasing that business in Europe. We already have a lot of stainless steel polishing business in China.

The third strategy is to build up a closer relationship with our mother company in the USA. We are currently integrating our product range more closely now with the machine portfolio in the U.S. Traditionally, demand for our machines has always been higher in Europe but we are now seeing the U.S. take crucial next steps, so we are stepping up our portfolio presence there. We have 90-100 staff in Europe and around 70-80 people in the U.S. At the moment, in the metal business, 60% of our turnover is in Europe, with 40% in the USA.

Of course, we also continue to work in tandem with Industry 4.0 requirements and refine our 4.0 strategy. In this way, we are giving our customers the opportunity to integrate our machines into fully automatic processing lines. Our machines run on our own software but they are also compatible with software from other manufacturers. We also offer remote diagnostics on machines so that downtime can be avoided.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers right now and how are you addressing these issues for them?

PK: We have placed a real focus this year, during lockdown, on improving our marketing and customer support. This includes customer training. There is a lack of skilled operators in the industry so training becomes key. This year, during the COVID-19 lockdown, we produced a comprehensive step-by-step guidebook on how to use our machines. It can also be accessed digitally on phones, tablets etc. It explains how, in ten steps, an operator can set up our machines and start using them. We have fine-tuned the guide and now supply it with every machine.

## A complete rotation brush range

With over 70 years of experience and a huge customer base worldwide, Timesavers recognises the need for continuous development and innovation. Its rotation brush machines offer deburring, edge rounding and finishing of different metal parts and thicknesses for each working width and capacity need.

Its latest development is the 22 series 600 WRBW rotary brush machine. This compact 600mm wide machine contains all the features of the larger 32 and 42 series deburring machines, such as uniform and even results (with even wear of the tooling), high production capacity and Industry 4.0 functionalities.

“This machine enables customers with smaller production demands and associated budgets to deburr and round parts. It, therefore, completes the Timesavers RB range: deburring and rounding at every budget and capacity,” explained Piet Kooman, sales and marketing director, Timesavers.

In addition to this new machine, the company also offers its 42 series WWRBW and the 42 series HWRB rotary brush machines for deburring, edge rounding up to 2mm radius parts, finishing and laser oxide removal. The HWRB can remove heavy slag on plasma- or flame-cut parts. The 42 RB series machines are available in working widths of 1350mm and 1600mm. For parts of up to 1100mm wide, the compact 32 series WRBW can deburr, edge round and finish parts as well as remove laser oxide.
