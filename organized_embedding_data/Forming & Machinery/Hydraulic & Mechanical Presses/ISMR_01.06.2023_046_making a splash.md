# Making a splash

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

CMS is expanding its offer with Tecnocut Proline, a waterjet cutting system designed as a machining centre. Featuring a compact and sturdy monolithic structure, it is suitable for intensive use or where cutting tolerances and repeatability are requested.

"This innovative, easy configurable concept not only has the 330kg abrasive tank installed on-board, but the electric high-pressure pump (4,150- or 6,200 bar) and the dredging system for abrasive removal are integrated with the machine frame, a big saving in terms of overall layout," explained CMS.

The system is suitable for both manufacturing processes, starting from a wide range of materials, and for mechanical machining processes, where an initial stage of waterjet roughing is requested to bring down the cost of tool-machine finishing. Thanks to its numerous sensors and IoT protocols, Tecnocut Proline can convey information about the machine and its machining status in real-time, on the CMS Connect cloud platform.

CMS connect can offer personalised micro services through the use of the IoT Apps that support the everyday activities of industry operators, improving the availability and use of the machines and systems. The platform views, analyses and monitors all the data emitted by connected machines. The data gathered by the machines in real-time becomes useful information to increase productivity and reduce operating/ maintenance costs as well as energy costs.
