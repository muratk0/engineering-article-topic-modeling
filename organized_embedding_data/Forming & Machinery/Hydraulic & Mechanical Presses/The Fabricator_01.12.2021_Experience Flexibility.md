Flex Machine Tools

Flex Machine Tools is centered in Ohio and was founded on the humble beginnings of Richard Kennedy. The original business started as Midwest Specialties Inc. – a government contracting manufacturing business in 1971. To help with some manufacturing projects, he designed a tapping arm to increase speed and efficiency when tapping holes.

As he perfected the design of the unit, it later became what is known as the FlexArm. Now, over 50 years & three generations later, Flex Machine Tools is still building innovative & high-performing machine tool solutions.
