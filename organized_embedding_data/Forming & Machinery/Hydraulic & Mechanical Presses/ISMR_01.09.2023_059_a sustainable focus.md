# A sustainable focus

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

MVD Makina Sanayi A.S. (MVD) is among the leading Turkish companies in the sheetmetal processing technology sector. It offers a range of press brakes such as the B100-3100 iBend hydraulic bending system and D320-4100 press brake, its H135-3100 (hydraulic and electric) model and the 100% electric E100-3100 iBend press brake.

MVD also offers its F8-3015 laser-cutting system, with a special bridge developed for high-speed movements, the iLaser fibre laser-cutting machine and its B06-3100 iShear guillotine shear model.

"The MVD family fully adopts the principles of sustainability in the light of continuous education and continuous development. Our principles of respect for people and nature are closely linked not just to our machine development but also to issues such as sustainability, improving our company culture and reducing our carbon footprint. In this context, we have made major improvements and implemented various projects and investments across all our production stages, and will continue to do so," it told ISMR.

MVD also manufactures the iPunch (punch press); iPlasma (plasma-oxy cutting); iExpand (expanded metal press) and associated bending tool systems. It has also developed digital services as part of its digital transformation remit

"At MVD Academy, we provide vocational training to new workers and teach them about our technologies and deep-rooted company culture. Trainees who successfully complete their training can then join specialist colleagues in our teams," it added.■
