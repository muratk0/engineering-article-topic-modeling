# Boschert GmbH & Co. KG

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.boschert.de

Boschert GmbH & Co KG has specialised in notching, punching, laser, press brakes and copper processing since 1946. At EuroBLECH, it will display the latest generation of its Boschert FiberLaser with optimised control system, which reduces the internal working and reaction times to the nanosecond range for faster switching times.

"The new control system also regulates the CombiLaser Multipunch. Designed as a machine with two separate punching and fibre laser units, it offers the option of economical, flexible and productive punching and fibre laser processing of sheet metal up to super format. The punching unit, which is built on its own C-frame, has a tool changer with eight stations and a further 64 continuously indexable tools in the magazine," explained the company.

It will also present solutions for copper processing as well as its range of notching and bending machines.
