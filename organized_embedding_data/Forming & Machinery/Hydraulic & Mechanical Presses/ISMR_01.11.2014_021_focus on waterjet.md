# FOCUS ON WATERJET

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

With a 4,000 bar operating pressure yet low energy consumption, the new Woma EcoJet 70M 4000/4 is an ultra-high pressure, direct-drive pump for waterjet cutting system operators. Its compact dimensions mean that the unit with its plunger pump is easily installed in new or existing systems.

The EcoJet 70M 4000/4’s plunger pump uses very little oil and runs very quietly with a noise rating of just 80dBA. The direct drive uses up to 30% less energy, says the manufacturer. These are significant improvements on the performance of pressure intensifier units. The EcoJet is suitable for abrasive and pure water cutting and can also be retrofitted into waterjet cutting systems for metal, stone and plastics.

The EcoJet is barely heated with the use of water, so the process is also suitable for use with heat-sensitive materials that would melt or change in shape if they were sawn.

Woma’s new WTS Eco water treatment system is installed after the extraction facility and the sedimentation tank and adds a flocculant to the wastewater with its suspended matter and oil-and-water emulsion. The flocculant ensures that they are bound and caught in the sand filter of the discharge system. The purified water now fulfils the requirements for solids and suspended matter and can be discharged into the sewage.

The system can be adapted for the size of the waterjet cutting facility. With four cutting heads, for example, a discharge system with a maximum throughput of 800 litres an hour is required. For larger cutting systems several water treatment systems can be fitted in parallel. In this way, operators of waterjet cutting systems can ensure that their wastewater disposal is always eco-friendly and complies with statutory requirements. See www.woma.com. ■

Spinforming can improve the quality of tube-formed products and lower production costs

With an increasing demand for quality tube-shaped products, space-saving solutions and cost savings in production processes, it is important to select the production process that best fits your business model. This article will explain how a spinforming machine can improve the quality of tube-formed products and lower production costs.

Oblique OST spinformed application

## The spinning process

Currently, spinforming technology has progressed to modern CNC-controlled machines. The primary objective of spinforming is to create a symmetrical shape or form from flat blanks or tube without creating a major reduction in the thickness of the parent material.

Tube spinning is a more difficult process compared to spinforming blanks, as an inner mandrel is not present to create the final spun form. From tube shapes that are round and oval, a cylindrical reduced diameter of the shell can be spun. Additionally, spun shapes can be combined with cast, drawn, forged or machined parts to reduce costs and increase productivity. Spinning the tube ends can be done concentrically, eccentrically and obliquely.

## Concentric spinning

Concentric spinforming machines form a reduced end profile that is concentrically aligned with the tube's central axis. A concentric spinner can automatically control the part length without trimming, in most cases. Dynamic Length Control provides accurate part length compensation for varying material parameters. A concentric spinner can be placed in a horizontal or vertical design, in which the vertical spinner uses less floor space.

## EST-OST spinning

The Eccentric Spinning Technology (EST) and Oblique Spinning Technology (OST) spinning processes form a reduced end profile that is eccentric to, or at an oblique angle to, the tube’s central axis, without rotating the part. Using advanced mechanical design and machine control techniques, spinforming complex transition angles, such as eccentric and oblique, can be achieved.

Using different types of angles is a frequently occurring requirement, especially in the automotive industry, for space-saving reasons. Various complex shapes can be more easily connected with other components. For example, catalytic converters can be formed EST/OST to save space restrictions in the engine compartment.

First, the shell is loaded into the workholding clamp of the EST/OST spinning machine, where the datum positions the part at the correct depth. The shell is static and the two forming rollers rotate around the part to form the end in multiple steps. Forming different angles requires a movement in several directions, within a 4-axis machine. The shell is moved by workholder in a horizontal direction and fed in and out multiple times to form the end (Z-axis). Together with the movement of the two rollers (X-axis), the shell can be spinformed. The shell is moved off-centre by workholder to form an eccentric (Y-axis), and angularly rotated by the workholder to form an oblique shape (A-axis). The EST-OST spinning machine can also feature automatic trimming of the shell ends.

In summary, the EST and OST spinning process forms a reduced end profile that is eccentric or at an oblique angle from the tube’s central axis, typical for applications where space is limited or restricted. Spinforming technologies are capable of producing one-piece products, without rotating the part, which eliminates the need for additional parts such as welded end cones and their associated costs.

This spinning method makes part development and product changes possible in a fraction of the time and cost when compared to the hours and expense of re-designing and building cone die tooling. All Fontijne Formitt spinformers use CNC control systems for easy programming and rapid part profile changeover.

## Benefits

In general, spinning can be more efficient than other metalforming processes.

## No welding

Spinforming technology eliminates the need for welded tube end-pieces e.g. two circumferential welds for one-piece cone ends and six (2 x longitudinal and 4 x axial) welds for two-piece cone ends. This enhances overall component quality and reliability, as possible leaks from weld processes are almost eliminated. Indoor pollution created by welding is also eliminated as there are no weld gases.

## Scrap rate

One-piece end cones, which are commonly made by dies in combination with a press for concentric end cones or a hydrostatic process for eccentric and oblique end cones, generate between 20% and 40% more scrap compared to spinforming the shell ends. The minor material waste is caused by the subsequent trimming of the shell end after spinforming.

## Handling

As only one part is required for spinforming, instead of multiple parts for welded end cones, the handling time (such as positioning the parts in a welding fixture) is reduced, which lowers expensive labour costs or automation.

## Space-saving

Industries are looking for space-saving solutions which fit current design changes.

## Flexibility

If prototypes or a new product needs to be produced, the investment (for example, in dies for presses) is not required. The spinforming machine typically will not require changes in tooling unless the diameter changes, in which case only the part clamp insert changes.

## Tooling maintenance

The maintenance and replacement costs of multiple dies used in combination with a press to form the end cones for welding are significantly higher compared to the forming rollers used in a spinforming machine. This is mainly driven by the number of various dies required and the complexity of the dies, compared to the basic forming rollers of the spinning machine.

## Summary

Using spinforming machines to form tube-shaped products leads to a more efficient production process. Significant savings on material and labour costs, and the elimination of welding, produces a higher quality product, making the spinformed tube product more reliable.

Spinforming equipment can process both ferrous and non-ferrous metals including stainless and high-alloy steels to create hollow symmetrical and asymmetrical shapes (eccentric and oblique), fabrications and assemblies. Potential applications include:

• Catalytic converters

• Mufflers/resonators

• Water jacketed assemblies

• Bulk transfer/large hose couplings

• Motorcycle suspension components

• Housings for fuel cells

• Filtration pipes

• Filtration canisters

• Sterile floor drains for the construction industry. ■
