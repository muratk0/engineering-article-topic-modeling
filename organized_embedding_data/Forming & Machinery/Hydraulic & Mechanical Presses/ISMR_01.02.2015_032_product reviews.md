## Stamped and formed parts

THE NEW servo-controlled RM-NC stamping and forming machine is designed for efficient manufacture of classical stamped and formed parts in small and medium batch sizes. It enables a very rapid, requirements-focused response to customer demands.

“Rapid, 100% reproducible set-up and re-tooling operations are the crucial strengths of the system. The VariControl VC 1 machine and process controller enables easy programming and operation with set-ups almost nine times faster, with 300% more output,” said the company.

“The cost for new tools is significantly reduced and existing tools from the RM series can be easily integrated and optimized for use on the NC machine. In this way, the RM-NC can replace a number of mechanical machines and allow users to boost output while simultaneously reacting more quickly to individual customer requirements.”

Compared to the RM series of mechanical stamping and forming machines, output can be increased by more than 300%, batches can be processed in half the time and re-tooling times cut by up to a factor of nine, confirmed the company. www.bihler.de

## Industrial tablet

AS INDUSTRY 4.0 becomes increasingly relevant to the sheet metalworking industry, LVD has responded with a technology that gives shop managers critical production information in real-time. TOUCH-i4 is a Windows-based tablet that provides an overview of the entire fabrication workshop, generating and presenting the live information needed to manage networked LVD laser, punching and bending machines.

“The real-time TOUCH-i4 system collects information from LVD machines and a centralised CADMAN database that can be viewed and managed wirelessly on the shop floor, allowing the user to make informed decisions based on lead-time, cost, technology and individual machine workload. It can be used to help users to streamline production orders ready for the next operation,” explained LVD. www.lvdgroup.com

## Packing a punch

MEUSBURGER has recently extended its range of punching tool components with its E 5590 stripper. This is made of an elastomer to prevent damage to the surface of the punched part.

“The contour on the cutting shaft is cut with the first stroke. This ensures zero-play fitting and prevents accidental drawing-in of the strip. The stripper encases the cutting punch, increasing its buckling resistance. The very high contact pressure acting on the strip ensures that it is held down during the punching process,” explained the company.

For further information, www.meusburger.com The E 5590 Elastomer stripper

## Hardox Tube 500

THE NEW Hardox Tube 500 range from SSAB has now been launched. The new range provides the same hardness and toughness as Hardox wear plate, but in circular form.

“The new Hardox Tube 500 range completes the existing Hardox family product range. We have long experience of developing wear-resistant steel tubes. The final properties are set after tube rolling and welding, which guarantees consistency as well as high hardness and toughness qualities,” said Fredrik Mikaelsson, manager for tubes and sections at SSAB.

“Hardox Tube 500 is suitable for transporting all types of abrasive materials such as wet concrete, soil, gravel and ore slurry. Its extreme wear resistance allows for a lighter product with a long service life.”

It is available from stock in 88.9-133mm diameters and different material thicknesses. Custom tubes in thinner materials and other diameters are also available. www.ssab.com
