# 》 Deburring and sawing with a difference

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

“RSA is the only company worldwide to manufacture both deburring machines and their high performance brushes,” Thomas Berg explained to ISMR. “We are a complete systems provider with 40 years of experience offering not only a system of attuned modules for production, but also the corresponding tools, brushes and saw blades. That way, our customers can be sure that all the components work seamlessly together. We are also the only manufacturer of a cold circular saw that can cut 300mm solid bar (most manufacturers can only cut 260mm). We are aiming to increase our turnover this year to 20 million euros, which is around a 15-20 per cent increase on last year."

Whether single or multiple cuts, steel or stainless steel parts or non-ferrous metals, RSA offers a range of saws from entry models to complete modular lines. All feature short set-up times and easy operation with high levels of precision and efficiency.

At this year’s Tube exhibition, the company launched two new innovative sawing centres. The RASACUT SH 300 is a high-performance cold circular saw suitable for thick-walled tubes and solid bars up to 300mm diameter and stock lengths up to 14m or more. Another new development is the RASACUT CC 90 saw family with gripper feed. This all-purpose single cut saw is now available for tubes and sections with diameters up to 90mm. The saw is suitable for small batches as well as for mass production and has very short set-up times. A dynamic gripper feed ensures a high rate of reproduction and output. In addition, RSA offers the RASACUT CC 90-2 concept, a double-cut solution for tubes and sections up to 45mm diameter.

## A modular approach

Deburring tubes, sections, bar ends and sheet metal after cutting is essential. RSA offers deburring systems that are adapted to the properties and quality of the workpiece material. Its modular and customised approach to system design effectively allows users to ‘bolt on’ modules (such as cleaning or stacking systems) that they need in their production lines. As Berg explains, time is money in today’s competitive manufacturing landscape and any residual time spent on ancillary operations loses money for a user.

“We have launched new flexible integrated systems for high speed cutting of any steel material, reducing ancillary times to increase overall process times. These feature dynamic gripper feed systems, high performance servo drives and very short set-up times. We also offer a flexible and broad range of peripherals for customised solutions because we see increasing demand in the market for highly integrated solutions, where processes such as deburring, chamfering, measuring, cleaning, stacking etc. are completed and customers are left with a finished product that is ready for the next processing stage. Our vision is to continue reducing the ancillary time spent on cutting to bring down overall cutting times as much as possible," added Berg.

“We have also devised a new human machine interface for all our machines, with touch-screen capability. This user interface makes it easy for even inexperienced operators to use, with no need for long training periods. An operator needs no more than two clicks to get to the information he needs, with 90% of machine data visible at any time. We aim to deliver real added value for our customers by establishing what they really need.”

RSA is already working on new product lines with new material diameter cutting ranges to deliver to customers across a broad range of industry sectors including automotive OEMs, steel centres, furniture manufacturers etc. 70% of its customers are in the automotive sector. The rest of its business is shared between tube service centres/traders and tube manufacturers. The rest is shared by furniture manufacturers, the steel trade, steel service centres, scaffolding etc. The company wishes to reduce its dependence on large automotive sectors in the future.

## New horizons

RSA has now relocated its current head office and assembly plant from Lüdenscheid to a 10,000-square metre area with almost 4,000 square metres of production space at Schwerte in Germany. With an eye on environmentally-friendly construction, geothermal energy heats the flooring in the office and the adjacent assembly plant.

The logistical success was the relocation of the machine fleet with its large sawing systems. 75 employees followed and their number has now grown to more than 100, as the new building offers higher capacity.

Besides RSA’s large sawing systems for the metalforming industry, measurement, washing, chamfering and deburring machines and modules will also be produced at the new facility. Manually-controlled deburring machines for pipe and metal sheet as well as brushes for drilling machines and angle grinders will also be made at the plant as well as saw blade production, using RSA’s own sharpening service.

“We wanted to expand our assembly facility and have more room for product development,” Thomas Berg told ISMR. “We have now doubled our assembly capacity and have a new demonstration area and dedicated showroom. We have kept an eye on energy costs with geothermal heating of the new plant. Our staffing levels have increased to 100 and we have launched a number of new products and product improvements, some of which can be viewed at the forthcoming EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (Hall 11, stand G29).”
