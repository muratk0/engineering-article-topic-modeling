# Schuler's Hessdorf site turns 60

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Hermann Schleicher was a pioneer of pneumatic gripper feed technology. He died in 2005 after starting his own business 60 years ago in a small garage in Erlangen (Germany) and founding the machine factory named after him. In 1990, his company joined Schuler and, today, transfer systems for presses as well as coil lines and laser blanking lines, are still being produced at the Louis-Schuler-Strasse in Heßdorf. Around 250 employees celebrated the milestone 60th birthday of their site with a big party on 30 June 2023 and ceremony for long service on the previous evening.

"Schuler can look back on a long tradition in Heßdorf," said Frank Klingemann, managing director of the Industry Division. "The coil lines, transfers and blanking lines also continue to provide our customers around the world with increased output, efficiency and flexibility."

Schuler offers customised technology in all areas of forming (from the networked press to press shop planning). Founded in 1839 at its headquarters in Göppingen, Germany, Schuler has approximately 5,000 employees at production sites in Europe, China and the Americas, and service companies in more than 40 countries.
