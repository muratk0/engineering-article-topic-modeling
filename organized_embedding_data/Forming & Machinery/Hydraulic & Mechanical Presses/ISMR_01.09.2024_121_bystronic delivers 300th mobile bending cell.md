# Bystronic delivers 300th Mobile Bending Cell

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Swiss sheet metal specialist, Bystronic, recently delivered its 300th Mobile Bending Cell. The buyer is the toboggan run and slide manufacturer, Josef Wiegand GmbH & Co KG, from Rasdorf in Germany. The smallest bending cell from Bystronic, the Mobile Bending Cell incorporates a ByBend Star 40 or 80 and a bending robot.

The Wiegand company, well known for its toboggan runs, children's slides, event slides and water slides (delivered internationally), chose a Mobile Bending Cell with a ByBend Star 80 and a mobile bending robot.

350 employees manufacture the leisure equipment in Rasdorf, partly by hand, for Josef Wiegand GmbH & Co KG. The company has now decided to automate some of its manufacturing processes to be able to respond better and more quickly to customer requests.

"The Mobile Bending Cell offers an easy entry into automation, especially for small and medium-sized companies with limited space. This is because the bending solution has a footprint of just around five square metres (ByBend Star 80 approx. 3.5m², mobile bending robot approx. 1.8m²). A bending cell with a permanently installed robot, on the other hand, [can be] ten times as large," outlined Bystronic.

"The Mobile Bending Cell works according to the 'Plug in & Bend' principle. It can be set up in almost any environment, put into operation in five minutes and quickly repositioned, if required. Simply plug it in and start bending!" it added.

Bystronic focuses on automation of the complete material and data flow of the cutting and bending sheet metal process chain. The intelligent connectivity of lasercutting systems and press brakes with innovative automation, software and service solutions is the key to comprehensive digitalisation in the sheet metal industry, it told ISMR.

Bystronic's headquarters are at Niederönz (Switzerland). Additional development and production locations are at Sulgen (Switzerland); Gotha (Germany); Cazzago San Martino and San Giuliano Milanese (Italy); Tianjin and Shenzhen (China), and Hoffman Estates (USA).■
