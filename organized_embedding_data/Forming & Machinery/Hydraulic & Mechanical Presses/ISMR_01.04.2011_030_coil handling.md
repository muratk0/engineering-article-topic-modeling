# COIL HANDLING

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

Wimo is a specialist for the development and manufacture of innovative load bearing equipment for lifting, turning, transporting and storing goods of all types. It offers custom-made solutions that allow users to optimise the flow of materials. Wimo has developed a range of load carrying equipment for smooth coil handling. These include coil hooks, coil/sheet metal tongs, crane hooks, spreaders, turning devices and coil containers.

Wimo’s coil hook with bob weight uses crane suspension via swivelling suspension bolts. A hinged and vertically adjustable synthetic approaching plate is mounted on the inside of the hook, which also has a synthetic back plate. The coil hook is fitted with a rounded supporting rail to protect coils during handling. For extra safety, the bob weight is not only butt-welded to the front of the coil hook, but also saddled over the hook.

Special features on the coil hook include a powered horizontal drive unit or a spring-compensated coil hook suspended on a motorised rotary Wimo crane hook. It also offers coil hooks with the adapter suspended on a telescopic Wimo coil tong for transporting bundles of slit strips. Coil hooks are also offered with small prong cross-sections for insertion into capstan wheels and reels. They can also be fitted with adapters for lifting coil container tongs.

The coil turning hook is for turning slit metal strips from a vertical to a horizontal axis position. Additional features include synthetic prong support for protecting contact surfaces, a steel or synthetic approaching plate to protect the sides of the coil during lifting and depositing operations and a protector on the tip of the prong for protecting the loaded coil during manoeuvering operations.
