# Stamping 'first' at MACH 2024

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

“A 'UK first' will take place from 15-19 April when we run our BSTL 350-88 live at MACH 2024," Bruderer UK told ISMR recently. The manufacturer of high-speed presses will set-up a full production line on stand 6-482 to showcase the machine's capability for the volume production of small and miniature components.

"The BSTL comes into its own for precision and continuity and uses up to 30% less energy when compared to older machine models. For the first time at a major exhibition, a turnkey line will be set up with a state-of-the-art pallet decoiler and winder with purposebuilt 'MACH' tooling set to produce commemorative giveaways for visitors to the stand," it continued.

"MACH 2024 will be a massive moment for our UK business and will be the first time that anyone will have seen the BSTL 350-88 produce products at a major manufacturing event," added Adrian Haller, Managing Director, Bruderer UK. "This press will also utilise Bruderer's latest control system that can be accessed with different devices via a web browser, with remote maintenance and the highest cyber security."

Bruderer UK, which is currently building a new 48,000 sq. ft. factory and showroom in Telford, UK, is also launching a second new model, the BSTA 710-220, at MACH 2024. This press delivers more space for complex stamping tools with a long bed length (2.2 metres). Featuring automatic stroke adjustment and various options, such as different stroke lengths, shut heights and press force monitoring, the press can be configured for individual customer requirements and is, said Bruderer UK, increasingly suitable for the emerging electrification world.
