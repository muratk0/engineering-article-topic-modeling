### What is Punching Machine Automatic Feeder

The working principle of the punch press The design principle of the punch press is to convert circular motion into linear motion. The main motor generates power to drive the flywheel, and the clutch drives the gear, crankshaft (or eccentric gear), connecting rod, etc., to achieve the linear motion of the slider. The movement from the main motor to the connecting rod is circular. Between the connecting rod and the sliding block, there needs to be a transition point for circular motion and linear motion.

There are roughly two mechanisms in its design, one is ball type, the other is pin type (cylindrical type), through which circular motion Converted into linear motion of the slider. The punch presses the material to deform it plastically to obtain the required shape and accuracy. Therefore, it must be matched with a set of molds (upper and lower molds), the material is placed in between, and the machine applies pressure to deform it, The reaction force caused by the force applied to the material during processing is absorbed by the punch machine body.

**Productivity:** It can be processed continuously in multiple projects.

**High speed:** up to 600 times per minute.

**Versatility:**Regardless of the width and thickness of the material, just adjust the feeder to match the mold. Simple structure, economical and practical, low failure rate, easy maintenance.

**Form**:

**Single type:** suitable for coil material (thickness above 0.05mm), single product or continuous weighing product.

**Duplex:**suitable for roll materials (thickness less than 0.05mm), short-length materials, single products or continuous weighing products.

**Feeding accuracy:**

It depends on the number of revolutions and the length of the feed. Generally, the accuracy is 0.05mm. If the guide pin is used for positioning, and the accuracy of ±0.01mm can be obtained. Four characteristics of the combination:

**One-way bearing**

**Scroll wheel**

**Disc brake**

The high-grade clutch is used to make the two sides of the plate completely contact, with long life, good stability, and high precision.

**Reverse device**

(1) The structure is the same as the one-way device. It is believed that the control of the lower wheel in the scenic spot will not cause the lower wheel to regress after a long time of use. Stability and precision are quite high.
