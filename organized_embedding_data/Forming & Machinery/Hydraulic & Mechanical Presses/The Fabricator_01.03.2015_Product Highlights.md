GMAW consumables’ contact tips have shielding gas ports for cooling

Tweco

®

, an ESAB brand, has introduced the patent-pending Velocity™ GMAW consumables platform. The contact tips feature shielding gas ports that keep the tip 30 percent cooler than other consumables, which improves parts life by up to five times or more in some applications, the manufacturer says. The company has integrated the consumables on its Spray Master

®

GMAW guns and offers retrofit kits for existing Spray Master guns and the Classic Number series.

The cool-running contact tips reduce the ability for spatter to stick. The threadless, drop-in-style contact tips can be exchanged without the use of tools. This style eliminates problems with burnbacks as well.

Traditionally threaded nozzles are available, as well as a slip-adjustable nozzle with an indexed collar that enables the welder to define the contact tip-to-nozzle relationship—protruding, flush, or recessed.

Electrochemical system cleans GMAW/GTAW welds

Walter Surface Technologies has released the new Surfox 304 GMAW and GTAW cleaning system. According to the company, it is the only electrochemical system on the market that cleans impurities from GMAW and GTAW welds. It involves no hazardous materials and will not alter the parent material’s surface while forming a passivation layer.

The system features a patent-pending quick-change system that allows users to switch from one brush to another within seconds, without the need for added tools. It comes with a flow-through liquid system that delivers the exact amount of electrolytic solution to the brush, preventing the risk of burning and delivering consistent cleaning, the manufacturer states. The system comes with three brush designs: a triangular sleeve for GMAW welds, a cylindrical sleeve for spot welds and tight corners, and a microwand with a cylindrical sleeve for intricate areas.

Nonferrous cross-cutting system puts large-shop automation in reach

TigerStop’s TigerSaw 1000 is a fully automated and integrated crosscutting system for aluminum and other nonferrous metals. It features a heavy-duty build, 18-in. saw paired with a pusher system for single-piece flow or pack sawing applications up to 26 ft.

The system uses Dynamic Optimization™ automated lineal cutting software, as well as a lube mister and flow controls for controlled saw-stroke cycle. It performs cross-cutting automation and material optimization functions typically found only in larger systems. Features include positioning accuracy of ±0.006 in. and 240 SPM variable.

sheet fanners feature a magnetic circuit to fan and separate semisticky, oily, bent, and heavy-gauge sheet steel for presentation to presses, press brakes, and shears. No prying is required, and doubles are eliminated. They do not mar painted or polished surfaces.
