# Making its mark

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA), will demonstrate its press technology on booth 3909 at FABTECH®Mexico 2023. Two AIDA presses, along with AIDA's Allen-Bradley based control for larger servo presses, will be demonstrated at this year's FABTECH®Mexico from 16-18 May 2023 at Centro Citibanamex in Mexico City.

The first, an AIDA single-point, mechanical gap frame press, is part of AIDA's gap frame press line, with models ranging from 35 to 250 tons. A rapid hydraulic overload protection (HOLP) at 10m/s, a highly rigid frame with low angular deflection and tight clearances (to minimise reverse tonnage) are a few standard features of these NC1 Series presses.

The second is AIDA's A Series single point, direct-drive gap frame servo press, showcasing AIDA's Allen-Bradley control.

“Standard DSF-C1-A Allen-Bradley control features include a swing-arm mounted 12 in. colour touch-screen HMI; an Allen-Bradley servo motion controller on ControlLogix® platform; 150 job recipe storage and an integrated tonnage monitor (peak only). Other features include a step-feed (CNC handwheel) slide motion control for ease of die set-up; eight PLS cams; eight-channel die protection with PNP/NPN signals; display of actual/virtual crank angles and ergonomically designed optical run buttons to provide the highest level of safety while helping to reduce operator fatigue," explained AIDA.

"Additional AIDA DSF-C1-A series highlights are a next-generation servo motor with 5-10% higher speeds than our previous generation gap frame servo presses; a capacitor based Energy Conservation and Optimisation (ECO) system that reduces peak energy draw and a modern Electronic Over Load Protection (EOLP) system that monitors applied torque through the complete stroke to prevent overloading," it added.

This press will be blanking parts with AIDA's optimised servo silent blanking stroke motion profile, which can only be achieved on a servo press. The new DSF-C1-A Series product line is offered in a range of tonnages from 80 to 250 tons.

Additionally, AIDA will be exhibiting its Allen-Bradley-based servo press control for servo presses from 315 – 3,500 tons. This operational and interactive 19 in. PC touch-screen Allen-Bradley control will demonstrate several servo press stroke motion profiles in real-time on a virtual press. Control features include direct access to a PDF of the press manual and display of actual and virtual crank angles.

AIDA is a global specialist in the design; manufacture; sale, service and support; refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metalforming automation equipment. AIDA servo and mechanical stamping presses range in capacity from 30 to 4,000 tons and from 1-2,000 strokes per minute; from gap frame to straightside, high speed to cold forging, transfer and progressive die press models; material handling and coil feeding equipment.■
