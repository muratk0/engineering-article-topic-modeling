# A stamping showcase

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Italian stamping specialist, AIDA, will be promoting its range of press technologies at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, Germany, from 22-25 October 2024 (stand E52 in Hall 27). This includes its DSF-NE2 monoblock servo presses as well as the entire AIDA press line series with servo presses, highspeed automatic presses for lamination and precision forming presses.

## AIDA DSF-NE2

The AIDA DSF-NE2 is a monolithic two-point servo press that combines the high rigidity of the monoblock frame with the flexibility of the freely programmable servo movement of AIDA DSF® technology.

“Equipped with our own low-RPM, high-torque servo motor, this series of presses offers unmatched performance in metalforming applications. Each frame component is designed and verified with FEM systems and manufactured to reduce deflection under load below 0.1mm/m," explained AIDA.

“The DSF-NE2 presses guarantees low-noise, low-vibration, multiple varied-length forming, optimising all manufacturing and maintenance operations and ensuring flexible applications and long die life spans," it continued.

“This series of presses reaches speeds of 120 strokes per minute. The DSF-NE2 series was awarded ‘Product of the Year' in the 'Metal Forming and Punching' category at the EuroBLECH Awards 2022," it added.

## AIDA MSP lamination press

The AIDA MSP lamination press is designed for production of precision and high value-added motors such as EV motors, HEV motors and energy-efficient home appliance motors.

“Dynamic accuracy is significantly improved thanks to the compact design and multiple suspension points architecture of the press. Balance between high rigidity and wide bed area is brought to a higher level using the latest technologies. Moreover, the areas that transmit the press forming loads, such as the connecting rod bearings and the crankshaft bearings, are all made from special metals selected to avoid heat deformations. MSP presses are ideal for the production of parts with thicknesses of less than 0.20mm," highlighted AIDA.

The MSP-4000-430 high-speed press was launched in early 2024, specifically for die lengths of up to 4,300mm as required for triple-row production. The MSP-4000-430 features the same technology. Several hundred units of MSP presses are installed worldwide in the production plants of major players in the automotive market.

AIDA is a leading supplier of servo and mechanical metal stamping presses, automation equipment and complete metalforming systems. It was founded in Honjo, Tokyo, in 1917.■
