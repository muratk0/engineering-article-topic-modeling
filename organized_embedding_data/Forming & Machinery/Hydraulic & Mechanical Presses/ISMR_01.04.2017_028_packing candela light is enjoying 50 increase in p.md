# PACKING Candela Light is enjoying 50% increase in production punching speeds and throughput with its new TruPunch 1000

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Candela no longer has to remove parts from a skeleton frame or perform deburring operations”

TRUMPF’s TruPunch 1000 CNC punching machine from TRUMPF, UK lighting manufacturer, Candela Light, is enjoying a 50% increase in production speed (and hence throughput) over its two previous turret punch presses. The machine was purchased alongside a TRUMPF TruBend 3066 CNC press brake and the latest TRUMPF TruTops Boost software that allows the company to generate programs at the touch of a button.

For over 30 years, Candela Light has played a key role in numerous major decorative lighting schemes in the private and public sector. The company, which is one of the independent lighting manufacturers in the UK, supports projects from early proposals and lighting designs, right through to commissioning and production. It celebrated its 30th anniversary in 2016. Candela Light, based in Birmingham, is an ISO9001:2008/AS 9100-accredited manufacturer, providing contemporary and heritage style street lighting, bollards and interior lighting internationally.

"Being one of the few truly independent lighting companies in the UK, our flexibility and ability to meet the complex needs of the architect and consultant is unsurpassed. All of our products benefit from nearly 20 years of experience in the field and our team of product designers and engineers is continuously developing. Our 3D CAD systems enable rapid development of new products and 'specials'," the company told ISMR.

## Investing for success

Having experienced strong growth over the past 12 months (Candela now employs 28 people), the company decided to re-invest some of its profits back into the business. Of particular focus was Candela's turret punch presses, which were ageing and had become a little unreliable, according to the company.

"We knew that technology had moved on, so we decided to assess the main players in the market to see about buying a replacement," explained the company's managing director, Nigel Astley. "At first, we thought about replacing the turret punches with a fibre laser profiling centre, but the TRUMPF representative explained that we would be much better off with a TruPunch machine based on the type of parts that we process. As a result, we invested in the TruPunch 1000.

In addition to pure punching, the forming of threads, making of extrusions and bending of flanges are also possible with the TruPunch 1000 which is designed for cost-efficient, complete processing in a single clamping.

"Unlike our previous turret punches, which only had a couple of auto-index stations, all tools on the TruPunch 1000 — including TRUMPF's MultiTools - can be rotated through 360°," explained Mr. Astley. "Furthermore, the machine is 50% faster than our previous capability and we no longer have to remove parts from a skeleton frame and perform deburring."

In terms of eliminating secondary operations, the TRUMPF machine at Candela was ordered with almost all of the extra functions available, including MultiTool, MultiShear, MultiBend, Tapping (with breakage control for tapper), Marking/Rapid Beading, Engraving, Deburring and Roller Technology.

Since its installation in June 2016, the machine has been set to work producing the metalwork for Candela's extensive range of contemporary and heritage lighting products lanterns, columns and brackets. Although the TruPunch 1000 can process sheets of up to 6.4mm thick, Candela typically processes aluminium of up to 4mm and copper and brass of up to 1.2mm. The company also manufactures parts from stainless steel. Candela makes to order, rather than for stock, and batch sizes can range from 20- to 300-off.

## Press brake and manufacturing software

Candela also asked TRUMPF to provide a replacement press brake and new manufacturing software.

"We wanted a single supplier so that we would only require one training programme and one service contract," said Mr. Astley.

"With this in mind, we invested in a new TRUMPF TruBend 3066 CNC press brake and TruTops Boost software."

Mr. Astley added that the downstroking functionality of the TruBend 3066, as opposed to the upstroking configuration of the company's previous press brake, means that Candela can use the machine's bigger throat to accommodate larger parts.

"The 3D visualisation technology offered by the TruBend machine means that we can also use unskilled labour," he explained.

"Candela also uses TruTops Boost, a recently-introduced software solution that merges all the steps needed to generate sheet metal manufacturing programs. The enhanced automation offered by TruTops Boost means that creating NC programs for laser profiling punching and bending is quick and easy, since the calculations are carried out automatically. The software also includes full 3D CAD design functionality," added TRUMPF.

The new TRUMPF technologies at Candela Light are helping to meet increasing demand for the company's range of street architectural, LED and heritage lighting. Recent landmark projects include exterior LED lighting for the £50 million Park Regis Hotel development in Birmingham, the Edgbaston Canal tunnel LED lighting refurbishment project and the supply of heritage lanterns to the Edgbaston Priory Club (a leading leisure club).

Candela's facilities include product engineering, graphic and lighting design, photo realisation, structural calculations, full ingress protection certification and luminaire construction certification. It works in all materials from plastics to stainless steel; copper; brass; aluminium; cast iron; cast aluminium and glass.

"Three members of staff have attended the TRUMPF training course and they have picked things up fairly quickly, considering we have never used TRUMPF technology before," said Mr. Astley. "Of course, we will continue to learn as we move forwards, but we've hit the ground running, which is important for a busy, growing company such as ours."
