# PACKING A PUNCH

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

S FI Inc. is an award-winning, comprehensive value-added precision sheet metal manufacturer in the US. It fabricates a broad range of components and assemblies for the military, telecommunications, medical and food service industries.

The company offers product design and engineering services; punching and fabricating; painting; silk screening; rubber stamping; welding; QC inspection and fully finished components. For its punching operations, it uses a Murata Wiedemann Motorum 2048-LT-CNC with four auto-index stations and 44 tool stations, and a Murata Wiedemann Motorum 2044-CNC punch press with four auto-index stations and 30 tool stations. Both presses are tooled with Mate Marathon Plus™ tooling.

## Tooling in focus

Supplier relationships are extremely important for business success. For SFI Inc., one reason that its fabricating operations have excelled is because of its strong relationship with tooling specialist, Mate Precision Tooling. Bud Sano, SFI’s President, has attributed increased uptime and fast tooling changeover to its longterm relationship with Mate.

"We started our company in 1978 and began with Mate's Marathon® tooling for our first press, a Wiedemann 2040," Mr. Sano explained. "We began building a knowledge base with Mate, listening to its recommendations on how to best use the tooling for a particular job. As projects became more diverse and challenging, Mate helped us to choose the right tooling to integrate into our processes.”

Producing more parts in less time is extremely important to SFI, which has a high incidence of producing parts in small volumes of one to five.

"We started off fabricating many prototypes and it's still a mainstay for us," added Mr Sano.

"Today, a long run would be about 5000 parts a week. And we continue to produce many prototypes. They are very important to our customers and give us an edge in getting the longer-run work."

With such a wide range of projects and quantity requirements, the right tooling and tooling changeover is a priority to Mr Sano.

"We want anything that can help us cut time off our processes for our equipment. Punching is key to our operation, and it's the tooling that lets our punch presses perform like new."

SFI's long history with Mate includes working with it as a Beta test site for trying out and experimenting with new tooling. One particular tool, that Sano recalls, worked so well that it didn't want to give it up after testing. It tried to persuade Vidal Rodriquez, Mate’s sales engineer, to keep the tooling once the testing was completed. Those tools were early versions of Mate's Marathon Plus™ tooling system.

In the turret, Mate Marathon Plus tooling nibbles parts at up to 600 HPM and punches at up to 220 HPM. Full length guiding provides tool alignment and stability at these top speeds. The tooling is a suitable match for Murata Weidemann's servo motor-run drive design, resulting in higher productivity.

Marathon Plus is an enhanced version of Mate’s Marathon tooling that has all of the original benefits plus a unique way to adjust the punch length. Instead of taking the tooling apart to change the punch length - which can take ten minutes or more - Marathon Plus tooling allows the operator to click and turn the outside barrel of the punch holder, thereby adjusting the length. This eliminates the need for disassembling the tool. Key to very precise punching, the external length adjustment feature provides very precise tool length setting in 0.005-in. increments. In addition, this design eliminates the need for shims or adjusting tools, further minimising punch press downtime.

Marathon Plus also features fully guided, long-lasting M-2 high-speed steel punches for excellent edge quality and spring-loaded strippers for holding sheets flat and hole accuracy. Full length guiding from the shank, shoulder and punch tip provides strong tool alignment and stability. Combining premium steel with a fully guided system allows customers to attain a lower cost per hole when compared to other tooling systems. In fact, customers frequently report producing over 1 million hits before needing to sharpen. Also, punch holders are fully hardened and ground for maximum precision and long life,” Mate told ISMR.

Another key feature is the self-releasing ‘pop-up’ stripper that eliminates tools during punch change out. Simply twist the retainer to release the stripper; there’s no need to use a wrench to lock the plate. The hardened steel strippers ensure long life, while Mate Slug® Free dies eliminate slug pulling for improved piece part quality and increased tool life.

Other Marathon Plus features include: multiple angle settings; an internal lubrication system; single draw bolt lock for positive punch retention and minute corner radii to reduce chipping and extend tool life. Many of these tool design features are a result of input from fabricators such as SFI.

## Refining the process

“Testing new tool features like these at SFI with Marathon Plus gives great feedback," Mr Rodriquez explained. "We can evaluate tolerances, premature wear, ease of use, downtime reduction, quality of holes and nibbling edge blending from hit to hit. Once that information is collected, Mate uses the data to determine if tool design refinements or improvements are needed or desired."

With the extended hit rates that Marathon Plus delivers, press longevity is also extended, according to Mr Sano.

"We typically get well over a million hits on a given Mate tool before sharpening or replacement is needed," he said. "We can punch out parts virtually to perfection with no burrs and no sheet marking. The steel strippers have a wide holding surface that prevents cornering issues, ensuring stable punching and smooth blank removal. By using Marathon Plus, we are getting more productivity out of our presses and we're extending their useful life. That is owed in large measure to the relationship we have with Mate and how they help us use tooling more productively."
