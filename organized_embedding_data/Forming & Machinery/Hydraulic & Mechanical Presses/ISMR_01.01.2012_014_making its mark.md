[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

Since its establishment in 1948, Chin Fong Machine Industrial Co., Ltd., the largest maker of mechanical power presses in Taiwan, has developed a reputation for innovative R&D capability. ISMR spoke to its President, Jack Chao.

Chin Fong Machine Industrial Co., Ltd., established since 1948, is the largest maker of mechanical power presses in Taiwan. Chin Fong’s quality policy is ‘committed to business sustainability based on customer satisfaction, by practicing personal accountability, and to pursue quality perfection through professional dedication and continuous improvement’.

Chin Fong’s product portfolio includes servo presses, straight side presses, C-type presses, transfer presses, automated tandem press lines and heavy stamping presses along with a range of associated automation. It has also formally announced a commitment to

## Aims and objectives

The Chin Fong Group aims to achieve a NTD 10 billion (US$ 330 million) sales target by 2015.

“To achieve this," says its President, Jack Chao, “a business model transformation from ‘press manufacturer’ to ‘total solution provider to the metal forming industry’ is our primary objective through plotted strategy targets: to develop and complete new product lines of servo presses and forging presses, to expand even deeper into the mainland China market and to diversify risk to global markets.”

Chin Fong’s new single point servo presses (iLS/DLS series) ranging from 80 – 300 tons; its double-point SDS series from 400 up to 1000 tonnes; the compound link LF and KW series for stamping-forging composite processes; the long stroke knuckle joint presses KL and warm-hot forging WF series have facilitated its slogan: “Intelligence of Forming, Vision of Forging”.

## Sheet metal trends

Chao sees sheet metal and machinery market trending towards ‘high-end domination’ on technologies, values and applications.

“Taiwan’s machine tool export volumes rank third in the world right after Germany and Japan,” he told ISMR. “In our position, the right and sustainable direction to follow is to strive and compete with technology forerunners with better cost / performance ratio in the emerging market, rather than sinking and dying with low cost, low quality rivals in the middle-bottom levels of the market pyramid.”

In terms of high-end technology, he underlines the trend towards high precision, high efficiency and high intelligence machine tools pointing to the need for high rigidity frame structure on machine tools for near net shape forming or fine blanking applications. He also sees a trend for high output ratio machine tools such as multi-stage transfer presses and automatic multi-press tandem lines in all tonnages as well as a move towards highly intelligent servo-driven presses which offer users great flexibility of forming applications either in die try-out or production.

“On high-end value, we are moving closely towards trends for ‘large in size and quantity and ‘system-integrated’ which require all-inone manufacturing capacity, rapid responses to commercial and technical inquiries and excellence of supply chain management that is able to bring customised, built-to-order projects faster than customers’ expectations as well as standard machines to satisfy ever-changing market demands,” he explained.

“Urgent demands for press line automation and system integration are rising along with the labour costs in previously low cost countries such as China and Mexico. Now customers tend to evaluate and negotiate with us on total cost of ownership and return of investment, rather than dollar-per-kilo, and this challenges us to increase added-value by thinking, promoting, making and learning faster than our customers about their requirements for process planning, press model design and optimisation, and tooling and automation solutions.”

At the high end of the market, he says, modern metal forming applications processes are trending towards high precision owing to the popularity of mobile and handheld devices that bring high forming thresholds by featuring light alloy materials with high tensile strength.

“In the automotive sector, thanks to the fashionable design of new cars, tooling size is increasing while the shapes to be formed are becoming more and more versatile and complex, engendering a need for high process complexity,” he added. “These trends in the sheet metal forming market are challenges as well as opportunities for us in terms of growth in technologies, expertise and sales revenues.”

to manufacture the EH series of high speed presses in mainland China. Chin Fong now has offices in Taiwan, China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and the USA as well as representatives in numerous countries around the world.

However, Chin Fong President Jack Chao is keeping a wary eye on market developments in different geographic regions.

“Our turnover reached USD200 million in 2010 and dropped 9% to USD182 million in 2011. We project our 2012 result to mirror 2010. The uncertainties of the debt crisis in Europe, doubts on the US fiscal cliff and sluggish economic growth in China and other emerging countries are obvious signs of a global slowdown in terms of fixed asset investment,” he told ISMR.

“The 3C industry (Computer, Communication and Consumer Electronics) has long played an important role at Chin Fong’s market sectors, especially TFT-LCD manufacturing, in recent years. Due to over-expansion in the past, we see little opportunity of further investment in production capacities in this sector at this moment. Meanwhile, the vast popularity of mobile and handheld devices such as tablet PCs and smartphones severely impacts desktop PC and laptop markets and consequently shrinks the production equipment requirement for middle to small tonnage presses. Overall, we are adopting a prudent and conservative attitude to the current business climate.

Nevertheless, he sees silver edges in the dark clouds.

“In China, sluggish economic movement still means huge market demand for us, particularly in the automotive sector. 18.5 million cars were produced in 2011 while 20 million are expected by the end of 2012. Should the tendency curve extend onwards, we can forecast this figure as somewhere between 35 to 40 million by 2020 and this has actually inspired our expansion plan in China. In the US, the Obama administration is fighting back against the high unemployment ratio and related issues by invoking a ‘Return to Manufacturing’ policy and we have seen this somewhat stimulate the growth of our sales in the US/Mexico market.

“The Brazilian market is the third largest in our export segment and we expect strong demand in the near future thanks to FIFA 2014 and Olympics 2016. Japanese domestic demand remains on a downturn, but signs show that Japanese-based FDI in Southeast Asia, India and China is expanding owing to natural disasters, such as the 311 earthquakes and Thai flood, which strongly impacted existing logistical and supply chains. We can therefore conclude that the global economic trend will bottom out by the end of 2012 and slightly recover in the second quarter of 2013.”

## Brand recognition

Chin Fong has won numerous product, quality and operational awards, as Jack Chao explained to ISMR.

“Business achievements and awards are the results of continuous improvement and our pursuit for excellence. As we continuously upgrade our competency to improve competitive advantages on R&D innovations, operational performance, global marketing and talent acquisition, we have won a number of awards, plaudits and recognition from external organisations,” he told ISMR.

“On R&D innovations, Chin Fong’s iLS1-160 intelligent link drive single point servo control press received the Award of Eminence in March 2011 at the Taipei International Machine Tool Show (TIMTOS 2011). The transmission system was developed with an energy-saving, 6-link drive mechanism which effectively decreased 75% of the servo motor torque requirement. In tandem with the speed reduction mechanism, the servo motor power required has been reduced 40% compared to that required by direct servo drive systems. The “i-Motion” intelligent servo control system not only broadens the machine’s versatility but also adds value to the entire process chain by improving product precision, stability, production efficiency improvement and prolonging die life through low noise and low vibration processes.”

The Chin Fong DLS Series intelligent link drive single point servo press also received the Ringier Technology Innovation Awards for the Metalworking Industry in June 2011 at the Metalworking and CNC Machine Tool Show 2011 (MWCS 2011). As well as the energy-saving link drive mechanism and servo control, the system incorporates a plunger guide and optical linear gauge onto the slide assembly to precisely guide the slide movement and ensure Bottom Dead Centre (BDC) repeatability, all to bring sheet metal forming technology to the next level of in terms of motion and accuracy control.
