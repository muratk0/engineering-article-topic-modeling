# Maintaining a grip

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

The VDF FEDERN Group is an alliance for spring, punching and bending technology. VDF VOGTLAND and RENZING Federntechnik, as well as KREUTZER Stanz-Biegetechnik, specialise in technical springs made of wire and strip as well as corresponding assemblies and components.

Captive nuts have been added to the Group's product range as a new, intelligent fastening solution in accordance with customer specifications. Captive nuts are used, above all, in the production of control cabinets. There are also numerous applications in the automotive and household appliance industries.

"When retrofitting components to what are, in fact, thin sheets, captive nuts make it possible to provide a friction-locked nut. Thanks to the clip function, the nut can also be positioned in difficult-to-access places. The economic production of large batches takes place directly on the machine following the cutting and punch-bending process," explained the Group.
