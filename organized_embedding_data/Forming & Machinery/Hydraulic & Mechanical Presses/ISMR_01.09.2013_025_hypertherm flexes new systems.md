# Hypertherm flexes new systems

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM®, a manufacturer of advanced cutting systems, has introduced a new Powermax® plasma cutting and gouging system, the most powerful Powermax system ever shown, and recently demonstrated its advanced technologies, True Bevel™, Rapid Part™ and True Hole™ . These technologies are part of Hypertherm’s Built for Business™ Integrated Cutting Solutions platform – a combination of hardware and CAM nesting software.

True Bevel technology for HPRXD plasma systems is a new performance application for mild steel that simplifies the bevel-cutting process. Parameter values for several processes are available. Output values for angle compensation, kerf, cut height, cut speed and arc voltage are set which makes set-up for new jobs fast and results accurate.

With Rapid Part technology, Hypertherm products integrate seamlessly to significantly increase productivity, without operator intervention. Motion optimisation techniques are featured in the part program and are automatically executed by the computer numerical control (CNC).

“True Hole cutting technology for mild steel produces significantly better hole quality than previously possible using plasma – it is also delivered automatically without operator intervention,” said Hypertherm. www.hypertherm.com

## EFFICIENT TUBE BENDING

GERMAN SPECIALIST Transfluid offers a sophisticated portfolio starting with high-power mobile bending machines up to efficient mandrel bending machines and tube bending machines for large tubes of up to 25mm wall thickness and 300mm diameter.

The company provides efficient variants of tube bending machines with servoelectric, hydraulic and hybrid drives as well as semi-automatic or fully automatic systems with freeform options, bending direction right or left as right-/ left bender with a handling system or robots for loading and removal.

Transfluid also provides a high-power software solution ‘t’ control for the import of data from established CAD systems. www.tube-processing-machines.com
