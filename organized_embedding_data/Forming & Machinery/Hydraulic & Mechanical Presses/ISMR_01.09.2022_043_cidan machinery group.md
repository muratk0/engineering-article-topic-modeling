# CIDAN Machinery Group

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.cidanmachinery.com

The globally active CIDAN Machinery Group will showcase modern and automated sheet metal processing and sheetmetal profile production on an area of around 360m², featuring a line-up of folding machines, coil processing and automation software.

"The new nuLINK bending software generation, where users can draw as if on a tablet and easily enter parameters, and the even faster CIDAN FORMA Z folding machine (with unique geometry) will be on display. We will also showcase a large multiple coil line from FORSTNER, where users can switch between six different coil types, and the innovative THALMANN TD double folder which, thanks to three modular and integrable automation units (side loading unit, flipping unit and sheet-unloading unit) simplifies the complex sheet profile production process and sustainably increases production efficiency. Valuable time and high costs can be saved, particularly as the entire system can be operated by just one employee," explained CIDAN.

THALMANN is now part of the CIDAN Group.

## Dallan SpA

www.dallan.com

Since 1978, northern Italian manufacturer, Dallan, has been producing compact complete systems for the manufacture of products from metal coil including punching, rollforming and packaging. It offers a wide range of machines for operations such as rollforming, punching and laser cutting.

“Dallan EXN is our new coil-fed, powerful electric punching machine, offering greatly reduced energy consumption. The Dallan®Select servo-electric actuator unites power and energy efficiency. Thanks to ARC tool post arches, it is always possible to change the turret configuration and adapt the machine to other future production requirements. Maximum production efficiency is ensured by in-line machining, which achieves up to 100% optimisation of the material," it told ISMR.

## EFB

www.efb.de

The EFB (Europäische Forschungsgesellschaft für Blechverarbeitung e. V.) organises industrial research on sheet-metal processing. Experts from industry and science work on a variety of joint projects. These are presented, discussed and evaluated in specialist working groups.

The EFB advises and initiates future-oriented research roadmaps of industries. It ensures the knowledge transfer of publicly funded project results through various channels, such as events and trade fairs. Contacts between business and research partners, as well as users and suppliers, are developed continuously and for specific purposes.

"On the EFB 'TechnologieForum Blechverarbeitung' stand, research institutes and innovative companies will present new developments on pressing issues in sheet-metal processing to users," it told ISMR.

"Our BLE-X Navigator tool enables users to search EFB publications from more than 40 years of research on sheetmetal processing technologies. It provides a context-based overview of the content and structure of the documents," it added.
