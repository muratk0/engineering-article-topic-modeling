Measuring table allows quick tube length checks

Bewo Cutting Systems has developed the Solitair, a measuring table for controlling the length of cut and processed tubes and solids. The unit measures products up to 3,000 mm with a diameter up to 115 mm. It is based on the same concept as the manufacturer’s MT measuring table, but with a modular design and using the latest production techniques.

According to the company, the table offers absolute measurement results with minimal deviation, making the table suitable for checking whether processed products have the desired length. It is equipped with a printer for making measurement reports. The user can draft measurement reports with self-dictated text and give bundled measurement reports to customers.

Electric stroke controls added to ironworker

Scotchman Industries has introduced the 5014-ET, a 50-ton, three-station turret hydraulic ironworker. The unit, based on the 5014-TM ironworker, now is equipped with standard electric stroke control to help improve accuracy.

The electric stroke eliminates almost all moving parts that exist in a mechanical system, helping to simplify maintenance and troubleshooting. It also eliminates human error from this portion of the metal fabrication process, the company states.

Measurement system delivers instant, noncontact 3-D scan of wide area

Keyence Corp. has released a new model of its VR-3000 series 3-D measurement system. The machine allows an instant, noncontact 3-D scan of a wide area. It can measure rough surfaces, flatness, warping, and planarity. The user places the sample on the stage and clicks a button for measurement.

The machine can perform batch analysis among multiple samples simultaneously under the same condition. It compares measurement results directly to CAD data. The user can compare visual and numerical differences between design data and an actual sample, then export the scan back into CAD data.

With pass/fail inspection, the unit automatically determines if a part is good or bad based on user-defined tolerances.

Multiproduct roll forming line is hands-free

Samco Machinery has introduced a hands-free, multiproduct roll forming line for users performing JIT production of multiple profiles. The machine can perform at variable speeds up to 60 FPM, depending on the punching pattern and part lengths.

The process is automated from the moment coil leaves the uncoiler until finished product exits the cutoff. Features include 10K double-ended uncoiler with hydraulic mandrel expansion, universal bottom-driven roll former with side-by-side tooling for JIT production, auto prepunch/notch press selection system with guiding system, and AMS controller.

Closed-loop length control systems maintain sufficient coil stock in the line. A stop-and-go cutoff hydraulic press mounted on the roll former base includes three sets of cutoff dies with an automatic die change system.

Servo-drive press brake features two angle measuring systems

TRUMPF Inc. offers the new TruBend series 5000 press brake equipped with on-demand servo drive with four-cylinder drive technology. The servo drive offers quiet operation and consumes energy only during the bending process.

The machine features two angle measuring systems. Automatically controlled bending (ACB), which takes measurements through sensors integrated into the upper tool, is suitable for bending short sides and reflective surfaces and for multipoint measurement. The new ACB laser system projects a laser line onto the sheet and measures the angle with a camera, making it compatible with any tooling. It is suitable for use when processing acute and open angles, as well as thick sheet metal.

Caliper set simplifies critical quality control measurements

TigerStop has introduced TigerSPC, a configurable caliper set designed to help simplify critical quality control measurements for parts up to 16 ft. long. Accurate to ±0.006 in., it has snap-on jaws to accommodate end-to-end, miter-to-miter, hole-to-hole, hole-to-center, and hole-to-end measurements without the need for calibration.

The caliper set is available with an optional table system for working lengths of 4 to 16 ft. Standard and custom attachments are available to configure each caliper set to the user’s critical quality control measurements.
