# Meusburger at Moulding Expo 2017

[TARİH: 01.05.2017 ISMR]

MEUSBURGER WILL PRESENT its products and numerous innovations at Moulding Expo (30 May –2 June 2017). The highlights on its stand, 6C17, will be products to try out hands-on, a demonstration table for surface finishing equipment and the Meusburger technical tip video clips.

In mould-making, new items such as the E 8630 Gear unit for stack moulds and the self-developed IsoTemp® high temperature hose will be presented. Die-makers will see new products for active parts and cutting elements. For the selection of the appropriate cutting punch, the standard parts manufacturer offers a special configurator which is equipped with new configuration options.

"Our multifunctional H 1000 Clamping system enables precise clamping of plates with repeatable accuracy in the μ-range. The H 4062 Assembly table can also be tried out at the Meusburger stand. Air cushions on the underside of the moveable plates enable light and easy movement of the mould or die halves. Both mould or die halves are freely accessible without having to re-position or turn them around, enabling efficient operation," the manufacturer told /SMR.

At the product demonstration table, application engineers will be available for questions, requests and discussions on the topic of surface finishing equipment. See www.meusburger.com
