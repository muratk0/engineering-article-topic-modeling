THE YSM NC-26T is a full servo automatic forming machine with 11 axes (with the option of up to 16 axes)

"Each forming axis is operated by a servo, to replace the traditional cam-driven style, to increase the efficiency of mould change and make it suitable for small-scale production. Using our machines for the increasing demand from Asia, or for meeting the high-end eco-friendly automotive trend globally, will help European producers to levelup their production quality and efficiency," explained YSM.

"Stamping and bending lines are a competitive alternative to traditional high-speed presses because this process significantly reduces the waste of material in production. This translates into lower cost/part. Our production lines are very cost-effective because they are suitable for small-batch production where tool-change time is minimal, tools are inexpensive and therefore the total cost of ownership (TCO) is very competitive. In today's world, versatility is key. Our simple 'modular tooling', where designing and manufacturing become easily adaptable by the end-user, does not call for intense training programmes and can be delivered on the shop floor."

40% of YSM machines are for the automotive and motorcycle sectors, and a further 40% is for the miniature circuit breaker sector; the remainder is for other sectors.
