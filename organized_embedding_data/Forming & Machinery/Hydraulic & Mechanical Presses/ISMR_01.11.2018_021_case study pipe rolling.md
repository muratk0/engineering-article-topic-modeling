# CASE STUDY: Pipe Rolling

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

Faccin S.p.A. has many years of experience in the design and manufacture of special machines, standard and customised, for pipeline production. It has just finished another HAV-2P project; NC-controlled pipe mill bending rolls, suitable for pipe production, that is designed to deliver productivity, repeatability and automation of the entire process.

"These pipe bending rolls are built from sturdy electro-welded structures and are fitted with three rolls with a special electronically-controlled anti-deflection system. HAV-2P long bending rolls are designed for rolling plates from 5 – 45mm thick, suitable for producing pipes up to 18m long with diameters from 15 to over 60 inches," explained FACCIN.

To further increase production speed and automation levels, Faccin has also designed a post-bending machine that prepares the pipe for welding with a series of outboard motorised guide rollers that control the movement of the pipe from the HAV-2P system to the post-bending machine and successive working stations.
