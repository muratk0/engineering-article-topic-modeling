# THE RIGHT CONNECTIONS

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

TT’s new TUBOMAT generation, showcased at this year’s Blechexpo, is designed for customised piping and hydraulic pipe processing, in particular

For more than five decades, TRACTO-TECHNIK has been developing pipe/tube processing technology in Germany. Its system for processing media-bearing pipes, ‘PIPE BENDING SYSTEMS’, incorporates smart system solutions for the processing of pipes (including systems for single-part and small-series production) into a wide variety of tubes, pipes and profiles.

ISMR sat down with its sales manager, Frank Dobbener, during this November’s Blechexpo in Stuttgart, to see the latest technology innovations on its booth.

We also offer a software solution for the organisation and workflow optimisation of the pipe shop (PIPEFAB) as well as our TUBOSCAN pipe measurement systems,” Frank Dobbener, TT’s sales manager, told ISMR.

We have also focused on the ergonomics and designed it as a completely modular structure. We just sold a machine at this exhibition to a German customer for delivery at the end of the year. Fundamentally revised, modularly designed, functionally and ergonomically optimised and robust, TT’s new TUBOMAT generation is designed for hydraulic pipe processing. The modern control concept allows program operation as well as an approved 4-fold bending angle pre-selection mode and also offers the option of importing pipe bending data from TT’s PIPEFAB software. Numerous detail improvements and optimisation of the workflows round off the new TUBOMAT working centre.

“We sell our pipe bending systems into a wide range of industry sectors – from hydraulic industry and machine construction; shipbuilding and offshore industry; plant engineering and pipeline installations to HVAC; automotive; aviation/aerospace and many other sectors,” Dobbener told ISMR. “Our main market is in Europe and we will be attending the Tube Dusseldörf exhibition from 4-8 April 2016 (Hall 5, booth E21).”

## Making hydraulic connections

Leak-proof connections are indispensable to guarantee a functioning hydraulic system. To connect the hydraulic pipes to other components of the hydraulic system, 24-degree cone connectors or 37-degree flared connectors are often applied. Both methods are widely used and internationally standardized.

For more than 40 years, TRACTO-TECHNIK has been developing practical assembly devices for the pre-assembly of cutting-rings as well as for flaring of pipe ends. Earlier this year, it launched a new and compact combined device, called TUBOFORM C (= Connect), to manage both connection methods.

The hydraulically-driven machine is equipped with a universal seating device. Depending upon the connection method in use, customers can choose from four different assembly heads (for cutting-ring assembly with manual pressure adjustment, cutting-ring assembly with automatic pressure adjustment, 37-degree flaring and 10-degree flaring), which are easily inserted into this seating device.

“The most reliable way for assembling cutting-rings is to use the automatic pressure adjustment of the machine and the corresponding assembly head,” confirmed Frank Dobbener. “In this case, the specific working pressure is automatically taken from a pressure table in the machine control by inserting the coded counter support plate. This way of operating saves times and improves process reliability because incorrect working pressure for assembling the cutting-rings is virtually impossible. This has been very well received by our customers.”

Each counter support plate corresponds to a specific working pressure, which is stored in the machine control. Depending upon the type of cutting-ring in use, customer-specific adaptations or modifications of the working pressure can be made by reprogramming the pressure table of the machine control.

The TUBOFORM C working range comprises steel and stainless steel pipes from 6 to 42mm outside diameters. The machine can process single parts as well as small and medium-sized tube serial production. A piece counter shows the number of the parts yet to be manufactured. The working process itself – whether cutting-ring assembly or flaring – takes a few seconds. By adjusting the cylinder stroke limiter, the working stroke and the return stroke can be optimised, saving time and money especially when producing in larger batches.

The separation between the hydraulic power unit, with the universal seating device as the basic machine, and several assembly heads, with the corresponding tooling as additional accessories, has proved highly successful in practice, said TT.

## Software and measurement systems

Intelligent and modularly-structured software solutions facilitate planning and organisation in all areas of pipeline construction. The core of TT’s software offering takes the form of two products: PIPEFAB BE (Bending Edition) pipebending software and PIPEFAB HE (Hydraulic Edition) workflow management software.

TT’s pipe measuring systems can be used for individual bends, complex pipe systems or a planned pipe connection that needs to be measured. They can also be used for the purposes of reverse engineering. Its pipe measurement products include:

■ TUBOSCAN S: Optical pipe measuring systems.

■ TUBOSCAN: Measuring arm systems with tactile sensors, laser scanner or pipe measuring fork.

■ TUBOCONTROL: Pipe measuring table for angle and length measurement.

■ SCOPELINK: Isometric system for customised pipe connections (template pipes).
