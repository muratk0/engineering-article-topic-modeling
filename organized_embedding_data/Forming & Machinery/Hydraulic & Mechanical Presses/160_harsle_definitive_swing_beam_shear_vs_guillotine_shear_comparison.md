## Overview
A hydraulic shearing machine is a type of industrial machinery designed for cutting sheet metal and plate material with the help of hydraulic force. It utilizes hydraulic cylinders to generate the force required for the cutting action. When we choose hydraulic shearing machine, we will be confused by swing beam shearing machine and guillotine shearing machine sometimes, we can not decide which one is suitable for our working purpose, today we will share some knowledge about the differences between swing beam shearing machine and guillotine shearing machine.

## Blade Cutting Edge
Guillotine shear: 4 cutting edges for upper blade and 4 cutting edges for bottom blade.

Swing beam shear: 2 cutting edges for upper blade and 4 cutting edges for bottom blade

.

## Cutting Method
Guillotine shear: upper blade moves up and down uprightly and bottom blade is fixed on the worktable.

Swing beam shear: upper blade moves in arc motion and bottom blade is fixed on the worktable.

## Blade Gap Adjustment
Guillotine shear: blade gap can be set by motor.

Swing beam shear: blade gap can only be set by manual crank.

## Cutting Angle Adjustment
Guillotine shear: cutting angle cut be set step by step on controller.

Swing beam shear: cutting angle adjustment is unavailable.

## Applications of Swing Beam Shear vs Guillotine Shear

- **Swing Beam Shear Applications:** Due to its versatility and ability to handle thicker materials, swing beam shears are commonly used in industries like steel fabrication, construction, and shipbuilding. They are ideal for cutting large sheets of metal into manageable sizes, especially for heavy-duty applications.
- **Guillotine Shear Applications:** Guillotine shears are widely used in sheet metal fabrication shops, particularly for cutting thin to medium thickness sheets of steel, aluminum, and other metals. Industries such as automotive, aerospace, and metalworking rely on guillotine shears for their fast and precise cutting capabilities.

## Which One Should You Choose?

The decision between a **Swing Beam Shear vs Guillotine Shear** depends largely on the nature of your projects and the materials you work with.

- If you need to cut **thicker sheets of metal** or need a machine that can handle various material types with high versatility, the **Swing Beam Shear** is the better choice.
- If **speed, precision, and high-volume production** are your priorities, particularly for **thin to medium thickness metals**, then the **Guillotine Shear** will be more suitable for your needs.
