# 3D waterjet cutting

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

CMS Metal Technology is dedicated to the production of metalworking machines and foam materials, offering a wide range of complete 3- and 5-axis waterjet cutting systems, pressure intensifiers and dry or wet deburring and satin finishing machines. Since the 90s, thanks to the acquisition of Tecnocut and continuous internal developments, it boasts more than 1,500 installations worldwide.

In 2019, CMS Metal Technology launched JD5, its new 5-axis cutting head with infinite rotation (continuous rotation of the C-axis during consecutive cuts) which, it told SMR, guarantees important savings on machining times and reduces the tolerance error of mirror-cut parts to zero."

“Thanks to intuitive and user-friendly programming, by starting with a 3D model, it is now possible to control every processing aspect from the direction of the cutting head to actual head movements to avoid collisions with the workpiece. The software's advanced functions allow users to intervene on the cutting path, to improve the dynamics of the cutting head for higher cutting quality and better precision," it explained.

To accommodate more demanding applications, the cutting head angle can reach ±60 degrees.

Earlier this year, CMS launched its new solution for removing slag in thermal cutting operations: the Hammerbolt Deslagging Unit.

"This unit operates using replaceable flexible bolts that resemble small hammers. These bolts oscillate to dislodge the metal slag that forms as a result of thermal cutting while the workpiece is being fed into the unit," it told ISMR.■
