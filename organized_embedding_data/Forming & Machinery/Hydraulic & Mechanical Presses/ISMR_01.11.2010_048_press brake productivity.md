# PRESS BRAKE PRODUCTIVITY

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

Wila premiered its new step in ICT-controlled press brake productivity at its Press Brake Productivity Centre during EuroBLECH 2010.

“By using this new technology in both clamping and tooling, there is less possibility of errors. Because of the constant information transfer between tooling and clamping, the press brake will be optimally controlled,” Wila told ISMR. “At our Press Brake Productivity Centre at EuroBLECH, there was huge interest for detailed information about this innovation that has been made market ready in cooperation with TRUMPF.”

At EuroBLECH, WILA brought the two Premium and PRO product lines to the attention of visitors.

Wila has also developed the New Standard Pro range of press brake tools and accessories. This range derives directly from its New Standard Premium range of press brake tooling.

At the exhibition, Wila also offered visitors the opportunity to enter the Wila Fast Changeover Competition. The daily winner was rewarded with an iPod. The fastest time ever was achieved during EuroBLECH by Marcin Rejzerewicz from HSW (Poland).
