# BRAKE APPLIED TO PRESS BRAKE DISTRIBUTION

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

Finn-Power Oy (a company in the Prima Industrie Group) and Safan BV have decided to terminate their current press brake distribution agreement after eight years of cooperation. Termination will be effective from 1 January 2011.

Finn-Power Oy will therefore develop and manufacture its new line of electric and hydraulic press brakes which will be distributed worldwide by the Prima/Finn-Power sales organization.

Safan BV will distribute worldwide its E-Brake electric press brakes, H-Brake hydraulic press brakes, shears and robotised press brakes through its own network of subsidiaries, agents and dealers. The Finn-Power sales network will be active on Safan’s products until the end of the year, with deliveries of machines sold in 2010 which may occur in the first half of 2011.

Finn-Power will continue to render an aftersale service on the hundreds of press brakes installed so far and Safan will cooperate with a spare parts policy.
