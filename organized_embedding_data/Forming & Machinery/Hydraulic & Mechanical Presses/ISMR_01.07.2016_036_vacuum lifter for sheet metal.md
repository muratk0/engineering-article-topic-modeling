# Vacuum lifter for sheet metal

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

FIPA (www.fipa.com) entered the lifting technology market in mid-2015. The company's new FIPA Spider is a vacuum crossbeam that, with its lightweight aluminium structure, is easy to handle.

"Spider's modular construction and extensive accessories mean that it can be customised. The plug & play concept means that installation takes no time at all so that handling flat workpieces in sheet metal and timber processing is very easy," said the company.

"As we have done with End-of-Arm-Tooling, we are positioning ourselves in this segment with a highly flexible product. Modularity for adaptation to suit customer needs is our core aim. The point at which special production begins for others has long been standard for us," commented CEO, Rainer Mehrer, on the addition to the range.

"The new vacuum crossbeam, also known as a vacuum lifter, comes in two designs: up to 250kg and up to 500kg. It has an uncomplicated workflow - attach the vacuum cup, activate the hand slide valve and then lift after the vacuum has built up. A large selection of FIPA vacuum cups guarantees safe handling of panels and sheets without leakage issues. For sheet metal processing, for example, we offer low-marking vacuum cups to ensure gentle handling before painting or gluing processes," added FIPA.

The FIPALIFT tube lifter can cope with a variety of workpieces. The FIPA Spider is designed for use on a chain hoist and can lift weights of up to 500kg.
