# Milestone for Addit and SafanDarley

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

SAFANDARLEY HAS, in its capacity as a 'fixed supplier' to Addit, supplied its fiftieth press brake to this rapidly expanding customer in Poland.

SafanDarley supplied Addit in Poland with a CNC-driven, H-Brake 400-ton, five-metre press brake with semi-automatic tool changer, while Addit in Venlo took delivery of an E-brake 150-3100 ATC. The system in Poland has storage for 144 tools with 12 different die combinations. This intelligent storage system enables heavy tools to be changed quickly and safely.

Addit is a supplier of precision sheet metal work and a system supplier. It opened a branch in Poland twenty years ago and is now a worldclass supplier with 600 employees and an annual turnover of around 40 million euros.

Over recent years, huge investments have been made in the organisation leading to sustained growth. Laser welding and new powder-coating and wet-paint installations have also been installed. The company has transferred to SAP for its ERP software.

This year in the Polish Wegrow, a new hall (covering six thousand square metres) was set up to process stainless steel and aluminium, taking the company's overall floor space to 35,000m². To meet lean manufacturing requirements and the step-by-step implementation of Quick Response Manufacturing, its multifunctional press brakes can produce a huge variety of parts quickly and efficiently.
