# New straightenerfeeder unit

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Asservimenti Presse's new straightenerfeeder unit with electronic pilot release can reach 110 strokes/min. with a step of 300mm.

“With this straightener you can decrease your energy consumption by 30%-40% compared to traditional machine types (hydraulic, mechanical etc.). The range spans a minimum width of 200mm up to a maximum width of 2000mm," commented the manufacturer.

“We worked very closely on this new straightener-feeder unit with the enduser. We incorporated its feedback and suggestions into the system design and built various test models until we perfected the final configuration," it added.

Electronic straightener-feeder units, via the open upper head, enable cleaning of the rolls making the material easier to introduce and allowing material release for the pilot centring (especially useful for greater material thicknesses).
