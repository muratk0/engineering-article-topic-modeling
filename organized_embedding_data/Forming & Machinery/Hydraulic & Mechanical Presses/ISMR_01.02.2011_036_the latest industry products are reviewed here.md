VACUUM GRIPPERS SAVE TIME

AT THE EUROBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Goudsmit Magnetic Systems BV exhibited the newly re-designed MagVacu Combigripper.

“This is a conventional vacuum lifting system which uses magnetic force; it doubles the gripping power, allowing robots to move faster. This increases the robots’ handling speed, reducing production time, with time being gained during production processes. This integrated system is also twice as powerful and safer than conventional vacuum systems. Considering the simplicity of the system, there is no wear and therefore no maintenance required,” Goudsmit told ISMR. The system handles (perforated) steel, aluminium or stainless steel sheet, up to 12mm thick. Since change-overs are not required for different products, it enables a larger range of products to be handled on unmanned machines. The magnet pulls the steel sheet onto the vacuum suction cup, ensuring a neat fit.

## SURFACE TEMPERATURE VARIATIONS

THERMEX IS AN economical thin paper that can be used in virtually any application that involves heated contacting surfaces ranging from 200° to 300°F (93° to 149°C). Invasive intolerant environments that aren’t easily accessed with traditional temperature indicators and infrared thermometers are among the candidates for Thermex,” Sensor Products Inc. told ISMR.

Upon exposure to heat, Thermex changes colour instantaneously and permanently to reveal temperature distribution between any two contacting surfaces. The intensity of this colour change directly relates to the temperature to which it was exposed, enabling Thermex to reveal spot high or low temperature zones and minute surface variations. Common applications include heat sealing, lamination/press, converting, metallurgical welding, ultrasonic welding, heat sinking and clutch/brake interfaces.

A sheet of Thermex (available in 35.6 by 43.2cm) format can be trimmed for smaller applications or custom-cut to defined dimensions.
