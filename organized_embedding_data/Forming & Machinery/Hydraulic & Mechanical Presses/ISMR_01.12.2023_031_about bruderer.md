# About Bruderer

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Swiss stamping specialist, Bruderer, was founded in 1943 by Egon Bruderer. It employs some 460 people all over the world, 370 of whom work at the Frasnacht site in Switzerland where all its stamping presses are produced. Over 95% of the company's products are manufactured for the export market. Sales and service organisations all over the world combine with the company's own centres of competence, for example in Asia, to ensure direct contact with customers.

“The main reasons for the long life and consistently high precision of the BSTA high-performance stamping presses are the unique lever system, which distributes the load acting on the ram, the ram guiding concept and many other features. Because of this technology, stamping tools can operate with the smallest of tolerances and minimum wear, resulting in a very long tool service life," it told ISMR.
