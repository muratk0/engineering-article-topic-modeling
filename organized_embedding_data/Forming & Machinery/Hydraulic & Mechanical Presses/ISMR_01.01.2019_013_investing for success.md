# INVESTING FOR SUCCESS

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

Grégory Conraud, managing director of AMB Picot, explains how AI is revolutionising the roll bending process.

ISMR SAYS:

"AMB Picot stormed into EuroBLECH with intelligent rolling software, incorporating AI, to simplify the roll bending process"

A manufacturer of machine tools for more than 150 years, stormed into the EuroBLECH exhibition with new intelligent rolling software, called easyroll. The company's portfolio includes a wide range of plate roll bending machines including roll-profile bending machines and special bending machines developed along four key principles – reliability; quality; ease of use and profitability of production resources.

Thanks to their versatility, roll bending and pre-bending machines are used around the world in sectors such as transport; public works; aerospace; energy and construction. They can be used for one-off as well as large or small-batch production runs. AMB Picot continuously develops new products and improves its range using 3D design software and finite element analysis. From mechanical structures to Numerical Controls, everything is designed by its research department in Lentilly (France).

Grégory Conraud, its managing director bought AMB Picot in 2008 and celebrated this 10-year anniversary in September 2018 with his team. He met ISMR at EuroBLECH in Hanover in October 2018 to discuss trends, technology development and new initiatives in roll bending markets.

ISMR: Please tell us about AMB Picot and its roll bending systems?

GC: AMB Picot is a 150-year-old French designer and manufacturer of roll bending machines, driven by innovation. I have been heading the company for ten years now and we have placed our focus firmly on the development of roll bending technology, particularly two-roll, three-roll and four-roll bending. We are driven by the needs of our customers and, more specifically, on making the process more user-friendly. Over the past few years, we have focused on the development of three-roll bending technology that enables better performance and enlarges our customers' scope of activity. Our vision is 100% customer success.

Innovation is in our DNA – we never sit back and relax. We always strive to improve every aspect of our offering – technologies, products, service. Every single component in our roll bending machines is made in our workshops at Lentilly, France.

ISMR: What are you launching at EuroBLECH this year?

GC: We launched our new easyroll 2.0 software here at EuroBLECH. It is Artificial Intelligence software for roll bending machines, based on springback prediction and material simulation, to enable our customers to reach their expected rolling diameters on the first attempt, with no corrections needed. Now, rolling really is 'a piece of cake'.

The roll bending machine on our booth, which incorporates this software, is a new design, following extensive consultation with our customers and agents. We have aimed to make it as user-friendly as possible. Simplicity is key.

At this year's EuroBLECH, we are also presenting our 100% Serenity package. Customers want an internal process to help them manage roll bending costs, logistics, cycle times, delivery etc. The Serenity, or worry-free, package helps them with this so that they can focus on their productivity. We can also offer them worldwide training, 24-hour technical support and financial help (i.e. machine buy-back solutions etc.). Machine buy-back represents real added value for customers in case they have a short-term market requirement or if they are hesitating to invest. This package provides peace of mind to customers, who can be assured that they will be supported throughout the roll bending process, with all the elements there for success.

ISMR: Tell us how you have incorporated Artificial Intelligence into the roll bending process.

GC: Easyroll is a major achievement for the company. It has taken eight-ten years of development, during which we have tested different approaches to make roll bending easy. The software now features Artificial Intelligence for the roll bending process. We use simulation, combined with algorithms, to predict and minimise deformation on a sheet. Each sheet is different, so the software needs to adapt. We continuously update the algorithms and offer updates every three months to customers. I would encourage roll formers to consider this breakthrough technology. After just one year, over 50% of our sales are now in easyroll technology.

Easyroll is smart technology for experts, as well as beginners. This intelligent rolling software is designed to enable our customers to reach their expected rolling diameters on their first attempt, with no corrections needed. Incorporating a smart material database, it predicts material behaviour, according to material characteristics (e.g. thickness, springback, mechanical characteristics etc.) and continuously learns from experience. It is as efficient for bending special parts (in manual mode) as it is for large batches (assisted programming) and keeps customers on track for higher productivity.

The software is continuously being developed for different applications (i.e. aluminium, composite, high-tensile strength materials, 3D bars etc.). We own the software and all the electronics - the machine is designed and produced by us - so we are very flexible and can bring any new ideas or development into play. This gives us an extra element of control.

ISMR: Are you working on any other new initiatives?

GC: Yes, we have also been working on new two-roll technology, with another roll forming company. We are also investigating the development of electric machines to meet the specific needs of particular customers. Some industry sectors, for example, process smaller diameter material or need different machine response times or better control levels, so hydraulic technology may not be appropriate for these requirements. We may be able to announce more on these developments next year.

ISMR: What is your approach to Industry 4.0?

GC: Industry 4.0 is the fourth industrial revolution. For AMB Picot, Industry 4.0 means connected, intelligent, smart and efficient manufacturing and this all means profit for the customer. We are moving towards the Industry 4.0 goal by implementing Artificial Intelligence in easyroll technology. We have also initiated other new developments using robots, in line with Industry 4.0 principles. Digitalisation is crucial now for every manufacturer.

ISMR: What is your view of the business climate for sheet metal professionals?

GC: I think the business climate is good, but tough. However, customers are more open to new technology, which is a great sign. For the last two years, we have been promoting innovation. There is a great start-up culture in France, particularly in IT, and we have taken advantage of this to drive our own technology and IT developments. It propels innovation, encourages the development of new technology and makes us drive our business more efficiently. I believe that this also opens new doors to those who are prepared to innovate.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance to your customers and how are you addressing those issues?

GC: Customers need experienced operators to replace those who retire. There is a skills shortage worldwide and this filters down to roll bending. Our customers therefore want our technologies to help them bridge this skills gap, which is why we have focused on making the roll bending process easier to use. I was in India a few weeks ago, and there were similar concerns from customers looking for experienced bending operators – this issue is a global one. We have also responded to this.
