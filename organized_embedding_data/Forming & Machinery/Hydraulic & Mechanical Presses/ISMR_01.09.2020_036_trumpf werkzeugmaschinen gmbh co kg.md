## Voortman Steel Machinery www.voortman.net

“The V310 utilises a heavy steel bridge for increased feed rates and extended consumable life during drilling. The 10-fold tool changer moves along with the bridge so it remains close to the processing area and tools can be changed quickly and even whilst the V310 is cutting,” said the manufacturer.

VOORTMAN is among the leading producers of steel plate processing machines for the manufacturing industry. It developed the Voortman V310 as a “do-it-all” machine capable of plasma cutting, drilling, marking and more on one process list. All Voortman plate cutting machines come equipped with the SigmaNEST advanced nesting engine. The company offers a comprehensive portfolio of automation solutions.

## Waterjet Corporation S.r.l. www.waterjetcorp.com

WATERJET Corporation was set up in 1991 and operates as a manufacturer of NC machine tools for high-pressure waterjet cutting and the finishing of stone, composites, glass, steel, alloys and other materials.

“Waterjet solutions stand out for their innovative approach, cutting accuracy, reliability and strong customisation capability. Production and R&D are carried out in-house by our highly qualified personnel (now more than 60 people). Sales are handled by our widespread sales network. An international network guarantees quality, as well as rapid and efficient after-sales support,” it told ISMR.
