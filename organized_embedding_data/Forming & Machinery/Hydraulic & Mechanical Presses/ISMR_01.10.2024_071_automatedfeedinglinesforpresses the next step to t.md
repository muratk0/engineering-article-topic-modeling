Italian sheet metal specialist, Salvagnini, has chosen EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, Germany (22-25 October 2024) as the venue for launching the global preview of its Generation 4 technology. It will offer pioneering solutions to tackle urgent market challenges: labour shortages and the need to maximize productivity and ensure increasingly high product quality, reduce errors and consumption.

At booth D94 in Hall 11 at EuroBLECH 2024, Salvagnini will present two highly automated factory solutions that work in stand-alone and collaborative mode. These solutions are designed to improve the easy use and repeatability of jobs, eliminating low added-value activities, reducing lead times and optimising production flows. The aim, said the manufacturer, is to guarantee increasingly low unit costs and increasingly high competitiveness.

## Flexible Smart Line S4+P4

The first solution is a Flexible Smart Line (FSL) S4+P4, a flexible production system integrating machines, people and Big Data in a single ecosystem. FSL analyses and interprets data to implement smart production flows and reduce scrap, waste and consumption. The MD single-sheet store-tower feeds the S4 punching-shearing machine, while the MC Cartesian manipulator unloads and stacks the parts, feeding the P4 panel bender and balancing the production rate. FLOW software manages the machinery independently, optimising the results according to the chosen production strategy.

"FSL introduces the new S4.G4 punchingshearing machine with low consumption, versatility and simple construction. The advanced hybrid actuators reduce average consumption by up to 20% while eliminating the need for maintenance. MC is the automatic sorting device used to easily stack parts with different strategies, eliminating the geometric constraints from punching nests. NEXUS software generates the sorting program automatically, guaranteeing full process control and predictability, thanks to the integrated 3D simulator," explained Salvagnini.

## Flexible Smart Job Shop

The second solution is a Flexible Smart Job-Shop (FSJ), the combination of three different stand-alone systems which work together, when required, to handle converging production jobs. The various systems are connected through the software, as well as by transfers and AMRs. Flexible Smart Job-Shop can also integrate intermediate stores or other machining stations upstream and downstream.

The FSJ features the new L3.G4 laser, in a fully automated configuration with the LTWS store-tower, MCU sorting device and belt transfer; the P-Robot, combining a P2-2120. G4 panel bender and a six-axis robot; plus the B3 press brake with ATA devices for upper and lower bending tool set-up.

“L3.G4 offers an innovative gantry solution guaranteeing full accessibility to the working range, greater strength and machining precision. P-Robot introduces the innovative RVS artificial vision system, which recognizes the parts arriving on the belt and, whatever their position, manages their loading with no operator intervention or centring," added Salvagnini.

A new architecture that combines high accessibility with high rigidity and an 8kW high power density that reduces consumption and achieves the typical performance of 10kW sources are just two of the key features of the new L3.G4 fibre laser cutting system. A wide range of advanced process control and efficiency solutions are available such as the ACUT option (for cutting with suitably treated compressed air), APM2 (a compact turnkey device which connects directly to the pneumatic system of the factory to ensure the pressure values required for the cutting process) and various artificial vision applications based upon neural networks.

## Panel bending showcase

At EuroBLECH 2024, Salvagnini will also present its new P2-1620 panel bender, in addition to four models already in the Salvagnini portfolio, which completes its range of compact panel benders.

It can bend 3.2mm thick sheet metal up to 1600mm long and 203mm high. The P2-1620 panel bender combines productivity (automatic bending and handling cycles with an average of 17 bends per minute) and flexibility (universal bending tools). No re-tooling is required because the upper and lower blades, counterblade and blank-holder are universal tools that can process the whole range of thicknesses and machinable materials. The ABA automatic blank-holder adapts the tool length according to the size of the part being produced in-cycle, without machine downtimes or manual re-tooling," continued Salvagnini.

The RSU option mounted on the P2 on display in Hanover will be equipped with a tool for scoring plastic film, which protects delicate materials such as stainless steel, making it easier to remove in areas subject to subsequent welding or assembly operations.

"P2-1620 also guarantees zero waste production, adapting autonomously to the variations in the mechanical and geometrical characteristics of the sheet metal being machined, as well as to the external environment. Its advanced sensors measure the actual thickness and effective size of the blank, detecting any deformations caused by variations in temperature. MAC3.0 allows it to adapt to the material being machined by measuring the tensile strength in real-time," concluded Salvagnini.
