# LVD IS ON FORM

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

At this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, LVD showcased its new Sirius 3015 Plus CO laser cutting system, debuted the new Strippit VX-1225 CNC turret punch press and also unveiled its new Compact Series of PPEC precision press brakes in 35 and 50 ton models.

New product introductions were complemented by mid-level automation solutions for LVD laser cutting and punching equipment. The company featured a new 10-pallet Compact Tower (CT) system for material loading and unloading, and storage of raw material and finished parts. Also displayed was the new Strippit PA-1225 automatic load/unload unit, a compact, single-side system shown to reduce manual worksheet handling time.

LVD’s Easy-Form® Laser in process angle correction and monitoring system also generated high interest at EuroBLECH. LVD’s latest addition to its offline ‘integration’ solution, Cadman OEE, offers users analysis tools to analyse and optimise machine efficiency.
