# Productive material handling

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

"IF MANUFACTURERS want to make better use of their storage space, speed up handling procedures and boost productivity, our awardwinning customised products, exported to over 85 countries, ensure the very safe handling of long and bulky products, palletised goods, containers and oversized loads," Combilift told /SMR.

Specialist forklift manufacturer, Combilift, has a range which includes multidirectional forklifts and sideloaders, narrow aisle articulated trucks and a growing number of pedestrian-operated models as well as straddle carriers which can move loads weighing up to 100 tonnes. These can be used to handle and transport materials such as tube and pipe.

"Pedestrian trucks are an increasingly popular option as they can ensure a higher level of safety, where other members of the workforce are present, compared to ride-on forklifts, particularly in busy manufacturing facilities. Our pedestrian range includes counterbalance stackers, multidirectional reach stackers and powered pallet trucks. These all feature the patented multi-position tiller arm which can be turned to the left or right to position the rear drive wheel, allowing the operator to remain at the side of the machine rather than at the rear. This safer position reduces the risk of incidents in narrow aisles and allows for full visibility of the load, the forks and the surroundings," added Combilift.

Combilift's original multidirectional C-Series trucks work inside and out, and on uneven terrain in all weathers, for universal applications in the yard and warehouse. Sideways travel with long loads resting on the platform also offers greater stability and safer manoeuvring. More electric-powered models have been added to the range, such as the 8,000kg capacity C8000ET.

## Tube cutting and welding

L&R INDUSTRIES, INC. (“L&R”), a U.S. specialist in steel fabrication, has again selected Thermatool as its equipment supplier for a new mill in its mechanical steel tube division in Mountain Grove, MO.

Thermatool provided a 300kW CFI High-Frequency Welder and a new Alpha Mach 2 High Speed Flying Shear Cutoff, which were both installed in late 2019. It also installed a non-contact laser velocimeter for L&R, a new option for the Alpha Cutoff that offers 1mm (or less) in cut length accuracy.

“L&R experienced such accurate product cut lengths that no second or third handling of final product has been required, significantly improving the quality control of products produced. Tube is ready to ship right off the mill, a hallmark of Alpha Cutoff value," commented Thermatool.

In addition, L&R opted for Thermatool's ThermaView™ product, an advanced diagnostic software package that allows for control and feedback of the HF Weld power settings of the CFI Welder, enabling L&R to service demanding customers with quick turnarounds and repeatability.

“We chose Thermatool products because we found them to be the most competitive in both lowering costs and leveraging efficiency,” said Kurt Windsor, Plant Manager, L&R Industries, Inc. “The products that we chose increased our production by 30% and allowed for direct shipping to customers, which saves us time and operating costs while reducing scrap."

With over 60 years of experience in the tube and pipe industry, Thermatool offers a line of HF welding, heating and cutting equipment, which can be customised, in addition to service and support.

The ISO 9001-certified manufacturer has been in the high-frequency welding field for more than 60 years, bringing to market innovations such as ThermaView™, HAZControl™, AutoMatch™ and other patented technologies designed to provide better welding, heat treating and production equipment for high production throughputs. Services include process development and metallurgical capabilities, complete engineering design and equipment manufacturing, a research and development centre, customer training and an aftermarket support and service group.
