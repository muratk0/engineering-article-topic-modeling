# HYDROFORMING

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

Vehicle manufacturers can help save weight by using hydroformed parts in their designs, lowering fuel consumption and CO2 emissions. The hydroforming process is very repeatable. Many manufacturers use hydroforming equipment to produce parts that require very tight tolerances.

The hydroforming process is simple. The workpiece - a straight or curved pipe section – is inserted into a tool consisting of an upper and lower die. The mould is closed and the tube ends sealed with two straight punches.

During forming, the two axial plungers press against the pipe ends, while the emulsion (with pressure up to 4000 bar) presses the wall of the tube to the shape of the tool. Significant weight reductions are possible using this method. In addition, many components and manufacturing steps can be saved by the complex geometries produced from other manufacturing processes.
