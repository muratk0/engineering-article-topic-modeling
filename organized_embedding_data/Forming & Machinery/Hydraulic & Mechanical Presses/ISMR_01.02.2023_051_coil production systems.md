# Coil production systems

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

Dimeco, a French family-owned business set up in early 1957, is a specialist in sheetmetal coil production systems. Stamping; punching; shearing; rollforming and bending solutions are available from coil for finished or semi-finished sheetmetal components. It also builds customised solutions.

Its factory at Besançon engineers and manufactures all types of press feed equipment, cut-to-length lines as well as "Flexilines"(LINAPUNCH flexible coil punching and LINACUT coil-fed fibre laser), equipment for press control and safety equipment; quickdie and coil-change equipment. Its Spanish factory manufactures flexible punching and rollforming equipment.

Dimeco's Joint Venture in India (DKMFS) manufactures press feed equipment and cut-to-length lines for the Indian market. The company has also developed application software to calculate coil/material/production parameters to optimise coil-fed production.

"Standard, stainless and pre-painted steel, aluminium and any metal strip from coil can be processed using combined technologies such as punching, stamping, shearing, rollforming and bending," it told ISMR.■
