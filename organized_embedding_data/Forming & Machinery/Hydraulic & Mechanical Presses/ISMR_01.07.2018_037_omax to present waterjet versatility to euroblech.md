# OMAX to present waterjet versatility to EuroBLECH 2018

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

AT EUROBLECH 2018 in Hanover (Germany), OMAX® Corporation will present its OMAX 55100 JetMachining® Center with a Tilt-A-Jet®, MAXIEM® 1530 with an A-Jet® as well as its new ProtoMAX®.

"With a variety of products on display, visitors to the OMAX booth (Hall 12/ Stand H80) will experience versatility and high precision, proving there is a waterjet for every level of manufacturing," it told ISMR.

"The OMAX 55100's Tilt-A-Jet cutting head demonstrates how OMAX machines achieve taper-free cutting without slowing down cutting speed. The Tilt-A-Jet positions the nozzle at a precise angle calculated by the Intelli-MAX® software to exactly offset the natural taper from the abrasive jet. Taper doesn't disappear. It is moved to the scrap part of the material, leaving the part with square, taper-free edges," it added.

The MAXIEM 1530's A-Jet can cut bevelled edges and angled sides, as well as create complex 3D shapes. The A-Jet is a software-controlled multi-axis cutting head that greatly increases the versatility of the OMAX JetMachining Center. It can cut parts that require no secondary finishing, significantly reducing part production time.

Energy-effective direct drive pumps will power both large machines at EuroBLECH; a 50 HP EnduroMAX® on the 55100 and a 40 HP pump on the 1530. In addition, OMAX will demonstrate its new ProtoMAX personal abrasive waterjet system. ProtoMAX is a compact, self-contained cutting system which is suitable for prototyping and low-volume cutting of almost any material of approximately 1" thick. "Our easy-to-operate control software, Intelli-MAX, was engineered specifically for use with abrasive waterjets and no special machine code knowledge is required to use it. A person unfamiliar with CNC machine operation can learn to cut parts on an OMAX waterjet in just a few hours. Online training for machine operation and maintenance is provided free, so customers can access training information whenever they need it and at their own pace. Intelli-MAX software is compatible with more than 90 different file formats, including all major CAD program file types, plus graphics file formats such as .jpg, .gif, and .png files. This means almost any 2D or 3D part file can be imported directly into an OMAX waterjet controller and turned into a real part," concluded the manufacturer.
