# Material handling and transport

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Irish specialist manufacturer, Combilift, will be exhibiting a selection of models from its extensive range of material-handling solutions at EuroBLECH in Hanover (Germany) and FABTECH (USA). Products range from small pedestrian stacker trucks to the Combi-SC Straddle Carrier which can lift loads of over 100t.

"Whatever you need to move around production facilities, yards or warehouses, we will have a suitable solution, designed and engineered to our ethos of safe, space-saving and productive handling," it highlighted.

The machines on its stand will reflect the diversity of its portfolio: the original multidirectional forklifts of the C-Series, with lift capacities of 2-25t, are joined by the Combi-CB (a compact, counterbalance design, multidirectional truck).

“Pedestrian-operated trucks are now a popular replacement for ride-on forklifts and we offer a number of these, all of which feature our patented, award-winning, multiposition tiller arm which enables safer operation, maximum operator visibility and narrow aisle performance," it added.

Combilift is a large global manufacturer of multi-directional forklifts and a specialist in long-load handling solutions. It exports to more than 85 countries and boasts more than 60,000 forklift trucks in use worldwide.

Meet Combilift at EuroBLECH on stand F156 (Hall 12) or at FABTECH on stand BC15415.
