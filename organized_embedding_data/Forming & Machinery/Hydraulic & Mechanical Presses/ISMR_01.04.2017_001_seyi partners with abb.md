# SEYI partners with ABB

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

IN THE EMERGING era of intelligent manufacturing, Taiwanese metal stamping machine manufacturer SEYI (Shieh Yih) Machinery has partnered with international industrial robotics supplier, ABB, to offer customers metal stamping automation solutions.

Besides improving the designs and functions of its mechanical and servo presses, SEYI Machinery is currently working strategically with ABB to meet their clients' needs. Offering solutions such as one press with one robot, one press with multiple robots or multiple presses with multiple robots will allow customers to complete automatic production solutions. In addition, SEYI will be applying its "Intelligent Stamping Line Control System", developed inhouse, to offer more advanced metal stamping solutions for intelligent production.

"In this transformative age of manufacturing, it is unavoidable for operators to work with industrial robots. Robots spare operators from repeated and risky work, whilst greatly increasing the quality of stamped parts and productivity. Through the years, ABB has been an important partner to SEYI in developing automation systems. With increasing customer requirements for automation we have solid faith that win-win outcomes can be expected based on the cooperation between ABB and our company," Chairman and CEO, Ms. Claire Kuo, told ISMR.

SEYI customers can now experience live stamping automation. A demonstration cell has been set up at the company's headquarters, in Taoyuan City, featuring SEYI's most advanced servo press with ABB robots.

SEYI is a leading manufacturer of mediumlarge servo presses. It is confronting the challenges of Intelligent Machine (Industry 4.0) and Intelligent Factory, focusing on the integration of communication, control and loT (Internet of Things) to create a new intelligent manufacture and service mode. It will continue to create partnerships to fulfil customer requirements and optimise its supplier chain.
