AIDA is a leading supplier of servo and mechanical metal stamping presses, automation equipment and complete metalforming systems. Founded in Honjo, Tokyo, in 1917, it has a worldwide network of sales and service locations in nearly 40 cities across 20 countries and press installations exceeding 80,000 units in over 60 countries. It has manufacturing facilities in Japan; the United States; Italy; Malaysia and China.

At the beginning of 2021, AIDA launched its DSF-NE2, a monolithic two-point servo press that combines the high rigidity structure of the monobloc frame with the flexibility of the freely programmable servo movement of the proprietary AIDA DSF® technology; Direct Servo Former®.

At EuroBLECH, AIDA will provide information on the new DSF-NE2 monoblock servo press as well as the AIDA press line series including transfer, progressive and lamination presses. AIDA's new AET (AIDA Electronic Transfer) solution enables the movement of blanks and parts in multi-station or interconnected presses.
