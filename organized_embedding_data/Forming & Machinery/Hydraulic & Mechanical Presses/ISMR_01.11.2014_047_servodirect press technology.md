# ServoDirect press technology

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

SCHULER BEUTLER automatic blanking press is ‘the world’s first line with two electrically coupled and freely programmable pressure points’, according to the company.

50 per cent less energy consumption, longer die service lives and greater precision: these are the benefits offered by Schuler Beutler’s newly developed MSC-2000 automatic blanking press with ServoDirect Technology. The new machine generation features two electrically-coupled, freely-programmable pressure points without gear transmission. By linking the pressure points in the press uprights, the machine’s rigidity has been significantly increased.

“The MSC-2000’s highly precise and efficient part production opens up new perspectives for blanking and forming businesses – while at the same time being extremely good value for money,” explained Joachim Beyer, the Chief Technology Officer of Schuler AG. “The drive concept represents a leap in innovation, as it offers the complete functionality of ServoDirect Technology and allows flexible adjustment to all conceivable die functions and forming processes.”

The play-free and lubrication-free drivetrain consists of a highly dynamic servo motor, brake module and eccentric shaft with conrods, knuckle-joint and slide. The new MSC-2000 automatic blanking press uses two such drivetrains facing each other – synchronised via the drive control – which deliver a press force of 2,000 kilonewtons.

“There are pre-programmed profiles for knuckle-joint standard, blanking, coining, bending and drawing processes,” explained Adrian Achermann, general manager of Schuler subsidiary Beutler Nova. After adapting the slide motion to the workpiece, the servo direct drive also enables the user to raise the speed and thus the output performance in oscillating mode. Thanks to the freely programmable slide movement, dies can also be set up quickly in production.

The drivetrain’s reduced mass moment of inertia increases the dynamic response of the slide movement. Together with the lack of gear transmission and the use of roller bearings, this cuts electrical power consumption by more than 50 per cent. See www.schuler.com. ■

## SSAB launches Laser Plus

LASER PLUS is a new generation of steels for laser cutting that comes with guarantees such as maximum flatness deviation of 3mm/m after laser cutting and bending radius of 0-1.5 x the material thickness. These features make it possible to meet very challenging requirements in automated production environments.

“The dead flat process gives optimum flatness and minimum internal stress, which allows us to guarantee a maximum deviation of 3mm/m after laser cutting Laser Plus. The flatness tests on Laser Plus show unique results. Strict thickness tolerances and constant mechanical properties for each and every delivery result in trouble-free production. In addition, the smooth surface quality of Laser Plus steel guarantees a higher cutting speed and superior surface quality of the cut edge. Cutting complex and high precision components and narrow contours can be done without rework. This actually takes steels intended for laser cutting to a new dimension,” said Robert Wesdijk, Product Group Manager, Hot Rolled Strip products, Western Europe, SSAB.

The production process of Laser Plus steels results in a surface that is covered with a thin micro-scale. This enables extremely efficient laser, water and plasma cutting. The quality of the cut edge remains excellent, even up to a thickness of 30mm, said the company. ■

## WELDING SIMULATION

SIMUFACT.WELDING 4, the new version of the welding simulation software, celebrated its premiere at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover. New features included the integration of a new simplification method to substantially shorten simulation time, an optional available meshing tool for complex geometries on a CAD basis and a row of functions that streamline the software’s usability.

“For simulation of sheet metal forming and mechanical joining processes, our latest software version of Simufact.forming 12 provides increased user friendliness and new AFS technology. This new technology enables the use of process-related software functions for specific fields of application,” said the manufacturer.

“Simufact.forming comes with volume-hexahedral-meshing. The user can expect high result accuracy from the simulation, especially the geometry after the forming process: sheet thickness changes, inner and outer radii, as well as spring-back effects. The Simufact.forming and Simufact.welding product lines not only pave the way for simulating individual production steps of sheet metal forming, welding or mechanical joining. By coupling the products, users can consistently simulate their process chain without interfaces.” See www.simufact.de. ■
