Robots and Cobots

FANUC offers the industry’s widest range of industrial robots and cobots for arc, spot and laser welding. We have a robotic solution for any size job, at any reach and payload to help fit your specific needs.

New! M-800iA/60 High-Accuracy Robot

FANUC’s new M-800iA/60 is ideally suited for processes that require extreme precision. A robust, six-axis construction and high rigidity makes the M-800iA/60 able to resist deflection and maximize accuracy for applications such as drilling, 3D laser cutting and water jet cutting.

R-2000 Robot Series

The R-2000 robot series is one of FANUC’s most popular. Featuring high performance capabilities and payload capacities ranging from 100 to 270 kg, it makes the perfect solution for material handling and spot welding applications.

Paint Robot Series

FANUC’s paint robots are ideal for automating applications that are ergonomically challenging, hazardous and labor intensive. Our paint robots and door openers (all designed and built in the U.S.) are used by a diverse range of industries including automotive, aerospace, agricultural products, recreational vehicles and boats, furniture, appliance, medical devices, and more.

Factory Automation Solutions at FABTECH

FANUC offers industrial PCs providing a powerful platform for custom developments in a wide variety of applications. Available in a number of different sizes and options, our industrial PCs are fast, secure and easy to use.

Our new CNC laser solutions provide a seamless connection to laser machines. FANUC’s dedicated CNCs for lasers are highly synchronized with axis control to achieve high-precision processing with various laser sources.

FANUC’s new CNC Retrofit Kits upgrade machine tools to improve the performance of legacy machines. For those interested in getting started, download the CNC Retrofit App to help collect and submit relevant machine information.

The new ROBODRILL Plus vertical machining center features boosted speed and performance capabilities. Powered by the new FANUC Plus Series 31i B5 control, the ROBODRILL Plus is easier to use and has 3, 4 and 5-axis operational enhancements, unleashing unbeatable cycle times.
