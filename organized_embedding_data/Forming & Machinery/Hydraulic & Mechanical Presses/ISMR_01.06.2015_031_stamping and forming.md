# Stamping and forming

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

THE NEW RM-NC servo-stamping and forming machine from Bihler is designed for efficient manufacture of classical stamped and formed parts in small and medium batches.

“Rapid, 100 per cent reproducible set-up and re-tooling operations are the crucial strengths of the system. This is the result of combining servo technology with the latest control technology. The result is reliable production, while the use of the VariControl VC 1 means that the system is extremely easy to program and operate,” Bihler told ISMR. “Machine production cycles of up to 300 1/min. can be achieved,” it added. The costs for new tools are significantly reduced and existing tools from the mechanical RM stamping and forming machines can be integrated for use on the RM-NC. Compared to the mechanical RM series, output can be increased more than 300 per cent, batches can be processed in half the time and re-tooling times cut by up to a factor of 9. See www.bihler.de
