# KGN Pillinger flexes its muscle

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

KGN PILLINGER has invested in the latest laser cutting and press brake machines for fabrication of all stainless steel components, providing precision engineering and bespoke products. The Amada F0 MK2 rotary index laser cutter automatically pierces and cuts stainless steel tube, angle, box section and plate to exact size and specification whilst the HD Series press brake delivers 170 tonnes of pressure to fold sheet metal and process a large mix of materials.

“Both of these exciting machines enable us to maintain our policy of providing precision engineering and products to our customers, whilst forming new levels of productivity and efficiency,” said Jeff Norman, KGN Pillinger MD. “Our foresight in investing in the latest technology is one of the major reasons for our continued success.” ■
