# Ninth dual servo blanking line for Daimler China

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

AUTOMOTIVE GIANT DAIMLER AG has once again collaborated with Fagor Arrasate on a new dual servo blanking line for steel and aluminium. The line was installed at the Beijing Benz facility in Beijing, China. After rigorous testing, the line was placed into full production in January 2018.

"The 800-ton servo press with Linkdrive 180mm stroke and speeds of up to 85 strokes per minute, is supported by FAGOR entry and exit equipment. The equipment is capable of handling steel of up to 950N/mm² and series 5000 and 6000 aluminium. The entry terminal equipment is capable of handling 30 metric ton coils. The line is configured with entry coil storage; a coil car; movable decoiler; threading table; feed rolls capable of handling dry or oily material; hydraulic crop shear; cleaning brushes and a FAGOR 19-roll cassette corrective leveller. The stacker is a combination magnetic/vacuum to allow for quick changeover between steel and aluminium products," Fagor Arrasate told ISMR.

The entire line was designed and produced at the FAGOR facility in Arrasate, Spain. The Spanish manufacturer will supply a tenth dual servo blanking line for DAIMLER at its Bremen, Germany, facility in the fourth quarter of 2018. See www.fagorarrasate.com
