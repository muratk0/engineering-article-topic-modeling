LEACH STRUCTURAL STEELWORK LTD. recently invested in a new, fully automatic CNC sawing system. The VB1250 band saw with spindle, from the innovative Dutch manufacturer Voortman, has added another dimension to its complete range of band saws. Because the machine makes use of servo technology and a spindle, it has fewer moving parts, which reduces wear. This saves Leach considerable costs as the band saw blade lasts longer.

By linking the new technology to the in-house developed VACAM control, it offers advantages like longer maintenance intervals, a higher degree of automation and more possibilities for flexible process control.

To achieve the best possible efficiency and production, the VB1250 band sawing machine is installed in a split configuration with the V630M rapid drilling system, by Voortman.

The tip of a ball point pen provided the inspiration for the new TRUMPF foil slitting tool. Light in weight and movable in all directions, it glides over the surface of the sheet metal and scores all kinds of foils without damaging the metal itself. Even intricate shapes can be easily separated from protective foil after the slitting process. Its ceramic stylus makes it extremely tough and durable. Depending on the type of foil, this new ball point offers a service life five to eight times longer than its brass predecessor. It is suitable for use on any TRUMPF TruPunch machine.

To ensure that metal surfaces are scratch-free, many manufacturers currently score foils by hand, a tedious and time-consuming task. The new TRUMPF foil slitting tool eliminates the need for this manual process.

## PACKING A PUNCH

PRESS AND SHEAR MACHINERY LTD has been selected as sole distributor in the UK and Ireland for the Yawei-Nisshinbo range of sheet metal machinery. One of the most widely recognised names associated with the manufacture of turret punch presses, Nisshinbo has a large existing user base in the UK.

In 2007, Nisshinbo set up a Sino-Japanese joint venture with Yawei, one of China’s largest manufacturers of sheet metal machinery producing over 3,000 machines per year at its 260,000-square metre facility in Jiangsu province. There are four models of turret punch press, HPH, HPI, HPQ and HPC, all of which offer different combinations of configuration and speed to suit the application and budget. For example, the high-end, 30-ton HPQ press can process sheet sizes from 1,250mm x 1,250mm up to 1,500mm x 5,000mm at hit rates of up to 1,750 per minute. The mid-range HPI machine offers the same standard features but has a maximum hit rate of 1,000 per minute.

Standard equipment includes sheet and tool lubrication, auto index stations, brush or ball tables, a bushed turret, a choice of 26, 36, 38 or 40 stations and quick die change. Among the options available are Wilson multi-tools and wheel technology, programmable sheet clamps, load/unload systems and Jetcam offline programming software.
