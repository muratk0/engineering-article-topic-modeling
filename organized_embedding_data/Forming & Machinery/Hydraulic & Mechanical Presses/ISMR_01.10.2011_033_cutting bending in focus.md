# CUTTING & BENDING IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

AMADA’S JAPANESE PRODUCTION philosophy and its software is developed to handle today´s demands for efficient production and minimises the environmental effects of production. At its stand (B70) at the BLECH Nordic exhibition in Sweden, it will also be launching a new global version of its FM-B ball screw-driven press brakes, which is adapted to the European market.

“The machine that will appear at BLECH Nordic is an FM-B 3613 G NT. This machine has a bending force of 36 tons and can bend parts with lengths up to 1200mm with incredible precision. The FM-B is equipped with Amada's NC control, AMNC PC. The FM-B can be programmed off-line by our BendCAM software. FM-B is also equipped with several features for improved operator ergonomics,” Amada explained.

Amada will also be showcasing its latest technologies in the laser cutting of sheet metal and tubes. Its FOM2-R machine has two cutting tables for sheet metal and a rotary index for cutting pipes up to a diameter of 220mm and a length of 6 metres. It is also equipped with an automatic nozzle changer which enables it to produce in several different material types and thicknesses with no manual set-up by the operator.

“In today's tough business climate, the accent is more on optimising production flow and minimising material wastage. It is important to produce correctly from the first part. There are no margins in the manufacturing industry for mistakes. Therefore, we choose to show users how we can help them optimise the flow in their factory,” Amada told ISMR.

“During BLECH Nordic, we will use our stand as a factory. We start from a 3D design, a penholder which includes laser cutting and bending. We will prepare the design quickly and efficiently with our software for bending and laser cutting machines. After preparation, everything is saved to our database SDD where can retrieve program information to the different machines.”

## CONVEYOR SYSTEMS

CONVEYOR MANUFACTURER Vibro Industries, Inc. manufactures a line of pneumatic vibratory transporters. The Vibro air-operated transporter is 100% air-operated. It can transport objects along an assembly line or remove scrap from beneath machine tools, even at close quarters where conventional conveyors cannot be installed.

It is suitable for linear scrap removal from punch presses, lathes and other automatic multi-functional machine tools. It eliminates scrap removal downtime usually associated with conveying systems and, because it is adaptable for multi-tray installation, can replace more than one conventional belt conveyor. Optional features include a cover guard for harsh environments, quick clamps for fast tray removal, adjustable bearing support for trays and a motion sensor that automatically shuts down machinery if air supply is ever interrupted. ## WATER JET CUTTING HEAD

ACCUSTREAM HAS INTRODUCED what it claims to be the industry’s first customisable waterjet cutting head. The wide range of adapter configurations available makes it easy to use the Dialine cutting head with any type of waterjet system.

Dialine offers users the facility for customisation to meet specific operational needs and to operate seamlessly with any waterjet cutting system at peak performance. Users can customise the Dialine’s abrasive body, the inlet for cutting abrasive feed lines and the nozzle. There is then a choice of standard or swivel adapters, diamond or ruby orifices and thimble filters for added protection.

Abrasive bodies are available in 0.281”, 0.300” or 0.281” dual inlet versions. The new adapter head connections provide the facility to rotate the abrasive inlet, enabling correct placement. The abrasive inlet option includes a 3/16” abrasive inlet for connection to smaller abrasive feed lines. An optional dual inlet makes vacuum-assisted cutting possible when low pressure piercing of brittle materials is required.

New nozzle options include three nozzle nut options to enable customised cutting head configurations whilst the nozzle guard design significantly reduces wear and protects the cutting head. A second replaceable carbide-lined nut provides maximum protection and wear resistance. Suitable for retrofitting to all major waterjet cutting systems, the Dialine comes with a 600-hour cutting time warranty and can provide up to 1000 hours of high performance cutting.
