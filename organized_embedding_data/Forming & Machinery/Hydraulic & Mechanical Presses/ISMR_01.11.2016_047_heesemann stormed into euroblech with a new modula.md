# Heesemann stormed into EuroBLECH with a new, modular design for its deburring and edge-rounding machine

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

KambH & Co. KG presented a new generation of deburring and edge-rounding machines at EuroBLECH 2016 (25-29 October 2016). This new modular machine series, profiRounder, offers a broad and specific range of technologies and, the manufacturer told ISMR, is able to replace more than one machine at once.

"Our focus lies on solutions for our customers' current and future requirements - the machines are adaptable and modular so that processing units can be attached or units can be exchanged. EuroBLECH is the global premiere of the profiRounder machine - we have already sold the first machines in Germany and also to the UK," Norbert Ledwig, Heesemann's sales manager for deburring and grinding, told ISMR in Hanover.

Heesemann has been a market leader in the woodworking market for over 80 years – this is a relatively new step for the company into the sheet metal market. The profiRounder was well received at EuroBLECH in Hanover, nominated for the award shortlist by the editorial staff of "Blechnet" and "Maschinenmarkt" magazines.

## A new modular concept

The profiRounder combines minimalistic design, glass bead blasted stainless steel and anthracite-coloured floor-to-ceiling windows. It has been designed for dry mixed processing of steel; stainless steel; aluminum; titanium and copper sheets and meets a range of different industrial applications on one machine: slag removal; edge rounding; oxide removal; deburring; surface sanding and glazing.

"The design of profiRounder is almost maintenance-free. Any remaining service work has been reduced and simplified to a minimum. For example, we developed a quick-change-over concept for the time-consuming replacement of transport belts - with profiRounder, this should only be a matter of a few minutes. The conveyor belt is removed sideways from the vacuum table," explained the manufacturer.

"By re-designing the transport table, we managed to eliminate the accumulation of burr and the adherence of dust inside the table to the extent that the common cause of fire - inherent to dry sanding machines - has been eliminated," it added.

A completely modular design offers flexibility to the customer. The concept allows the machine to grow with changing user requirements.

"If the customers buys a machine with two units today, the machine can be rapidly expanded with further modules tomorrow."

without any difficulty. Expansion modules are connected to the existing installation using plug-and-play solutions. The sequential order of the modules can be changed with minimal effort at any time. Contact rollers, brush rollers, disk brushes and Fladder units can be combined in the base machine as well as in the expansion modules without constraints. The control unit in the base machine automatically detects the units mounted in the expansion modules and displays them on the operating terminal," explained Heesemann.

Fleece rollers and contact rollers' special alignment in the machine maximizes the life-time of the abrasives. Fleece rollers are equipped with an automatic dressing device for increased service life and surface quality. Any worn roller surface is removed inside the machine, keeping maintenance for dressing of the roller surface as low as possible. Redressing can be repeated several times, securing a repeatable surface and drastically increased life-time of the fleece roller.

"The main advantage is flexibility," added Norbert Ledwig. "This is a modular machine so you start with two units of the base machine and you can expand it as you go. Users are free to choose the units that they need i.e. disk brush units, sanders, brush rollers, nylon brushes etc. We are the only company in the world to integrate Fladder units into the machine, for rough slag and oxide removal. This is a flexible machine that can be adapted to meet a customer's requirement. The maximum material thickness it can process is about 160mm."

The profiRounder deburring machine is controlled by a 15.6-inch touch panel displaying up to eight units. The user interface adapts automatically to any extension to the machine base. Using plain and simple imagery and virtually no text displayed, it enables simple and intuitive operation. If the machine is embedded in a production network, Heesemann service can remotely run diagnostics to fix any parameter and programming issues.

## AN EYE ON PRECISION

CAM software helps KMF Precision Sheet Metal to convert a design into machineable parts for its punches, lasers and press brakes

ISMR SAYS:

“KMF tries to maximise sheet utilisation for any batch quantity"

To manufacture components for diverse products such as premium class aircraft seating, coffee vending machines and electrical distribution boards, specialist CAM software is vital to nest a range of parts for several customers on the same sheet, according to the UK's largest sheet metal subcontractor.

Operating from a 120,000 square foot facility in Staffordshire (UK), KMF Precision Sheet Metal Ltd ships around one million piece parts every month. Programming manager, Stephen Gardener, told ISMR that the Radnest function within its Radan sheet metal CAD/CAM system from Vero Software saves time and reduces scrap.

"It plays a large part in our day-to-day business, and we use it in two different ways.

Firstly, it consolidates large assemblies where we want to produce an exact amount of parts to match the Bill of Materials. A number of our assembly jobs consist of 100 or more parts, and the Project Nesting function means that we can nest different materials and thicknesses on different-sized sheets. These would typically be saved as a project, with all nests saved under one job number.

As our batch sizes can vary from one-off to thousands-off, we have a full-time nesting office analysing all jobs to see if the quantity justifies running its own nest program.

"And, secondly, it gives us the ability to nest small batch quantities. If a customer wants five-off, we can nest them on sheets with components for other customers. But, if we had to run a full sheet program for just five parts, it could leave us with about 20 per cent waste.

"As our batch sizes can vary from one-off to thousands-off, we have a full-time nesting office analysing all jobs to see if the quantity justifies running its own nest program. If it doesn't, the parts will be separated and nested with parts for other customers. This way we can maximise sheet utilisation for any batch quantity," confirmed Gardener.

## Keeping control

KMF has used Radan for many years to drive its range of TRUMPF punch presses, punch/ laser combination machine tools and lasers. But a manual press brake operation for the bending process caused a considerable bottleneck with a typical set-up taking around 45 minutes. Gardener says that investing in Radan's specialist Radbend module for programming press brakes has slashed set-up time by at least half. It also means that they can frequently set up a job using just one part, saving four or five parts on one set-up.

"And Radbend minimises waste by simulating the full bending sequence before starting to fold metal. Operators simply call up the program and view it in 3D on their machine console."
