# New product lines from SCHUNK

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

SCHUNK, A SPECIALIST in gripping systems and clamping technology, will be at Southern Manufacturing 2017 (Farnborough, UK) on stand S110 from 21-23 March 2017. The family-owned company will be showing a variety of new and established product lines.

Its ROTA-S Plus chuck features an optimised wedge bar drive system, a jaw change time of less than 60 seconds and an improved lubricant system for a consistently high clamping force. Higher rotational speeds and cutting speeds are possible and users can apply more efficient cutting strategies that shorten manufacturing time.

From its toolholding stable, SCHUNK will be emphasising the benefits of its TENDO E compact hydraulic expansion toolholder. Its Industry 4.0 developments and automation products will also be on display at Southern Manufacturing 2017. One such product is the new BSWS-MPG-plus standardised finger blanks, featuring an integrated quick-change jaw system that shortens the time for a jaw change in small parts grippers. The BSWS-MPG-Plus reduces jaw change times in grippers to less than 30 seconds. The entire length of the fingers can be adapted to the individual workpiece without having to take into account the jaw change and the required screw connections.
