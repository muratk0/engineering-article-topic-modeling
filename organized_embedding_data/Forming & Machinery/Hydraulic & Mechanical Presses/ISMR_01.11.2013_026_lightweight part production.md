# LIGHTWEIGHT PART PRODUCTION

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

SCHULER will present its latest presses and automation components for manufacturing lightweight parts at the forthcoming Blechexpo exhibition from 5-8 November 2013. Visitors to Stand 8205 in Hall 8 of Messe Stuttgart will not only see Schuler’s new FLEXline C-frame press and crossbar robot for part transport, but also interactive 3D models of its recently unveiled laser blanking line and TwinServo press. Experts from Schuler’s demonstration centre in Göppingen will also be reporting their initial experience with the newly launched MSD 250 servo press for the part supplier industry.

There is still strong demand from car manufacturers and their suppliers for press hardened parts, for which hydraulic presses are used. By greatly increasing the contact pressure and optimising the die, Schuler has succeeded in drastically reducing cycle times (“Pressure Controlled Hardening” or PCH). Schuler’s mechanical presses with ServoDirect Technology are highly economical. The power rating of the newly developed MSD 250 servo press is up to 30 per cent lower than its predecessor (based on comparable process parameters). See www.schuler.com
