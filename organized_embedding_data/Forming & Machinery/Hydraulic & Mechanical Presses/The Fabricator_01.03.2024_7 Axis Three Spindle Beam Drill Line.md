3 ADM ECO

The 3 ADM ECO is an affordable drill line with 3 spindles, ideal for fabricators who are on a tight budget but still want to remain competitive. In this model, the material is moved after each horizontal drilling process (on the z-axis). The high-speed drill heads work separately on three sides of the material with great precision. This machine can multitask: While one side of the flange is being processed and the material is held steady, it can also carry out other functions like drilling, tapping and countersinking independently on the web and the opposite flange.
