# An eye on material handling

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

FLEXQUBE has received an order for material handling trolleys and Liftrunner B-frames from Mann+Hummel Filtration Technology US LLC. The total order value is US$ 400,000 and the order will be delivered in the first quarter of 2020 to the Mann+Hummel plant in Gastonia, NC (USA).

"This is the first larger order we have received after signing the distributor agreement with STILL GmbH in March 2019, for the Liftrunner product line. There is potential for additional orders for others Mann+Hummel plants going forward, especially in the USA and Mexico where we can offer the FlexQube carts together with the Liftrunner system," the company told SMR.

"We are very happy to see that the Liftrunner and FlexQube combination is a preferred solution with clear cost and efficiency benefits compared to both the current forklift driven process, but also compared to other competing tugger train manufacturers and competitors for material handling carts in the marketplace, "Anders Fogelberg, CEO FlexQube AB (publ), commented.

The Mann+Hummel Group is a German manufacturing group headquartered in Ludwigsburg within the metropolitan area of Stuttgart. The company develops, produces and distributes liquid and air filter systems, intake systems and thermal management components.
