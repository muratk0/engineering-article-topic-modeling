# HYBRID grinding and deburring machine

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Fladder in Denmark offers a wide range of machines for deburring, surface finishing, edge rounding and lacquer grinding (including manual, automatic and specialised machines). Surface treatment machines include processes such as grinding, brushing, polishing and deburring. Its automatic machines are equipped with advanced features such as programmable control systems on several models, energy-saving system options and the ability to add multiple workstations to its HYBRID and LS machines.

“This makes it possible to perform multiple steps of the process on a single machine, saving users time and money," explained the company.

The Fladder® HYBRID grinding and deburring machine is designed for grinding workpieces that require calibration grinding on the surface and rounding of edges. Workpieces are advanced on a common conveyor belt through the two grinding processes.

“To achieve optimum cycle times for the two very different grinding processes and avoid unintended heating of the workpiece, the calibration grinding is carried out by a transverse grinding belt which is briefly brought into contact with the workpiece surface by reciprocating contact wheels," the manufacturer told /SMR.

"The short contact time with the workpiece protects the workpieces from inappropriate heating and ensures optimal utilisation of the sanding belt. The workpieces can then be introduced to the conveyor belt at a pace which is solely determined by the extent of the subsequent edge rounding," it continued.

To produce true uniform radii on all edges, it is a prerequisite to use radial flexible grinding cylinders (the structure and density of which must be selected based on the task at hand). Depending upon the desired process time and the amount of edge rounding required, it is important to be able to freely choose the spindle RPM (in the range from 300-2000 RPM) which is possible with the oscillated Fladder 300/gearhead.

Fladder was founded in 1974 and is headquartered at Ansager in Denmark.
