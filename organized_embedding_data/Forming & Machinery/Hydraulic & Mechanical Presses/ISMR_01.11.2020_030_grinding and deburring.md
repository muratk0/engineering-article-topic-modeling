# Grinding and deburring

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

WEBER manufactures grinding and deburring machines for sheet metal processing. The grinding machines are used for deburring, edge rounding of 2mm, descaling and surface grinding. WEBER offers these solutions in various machine series.

At the EuroBLECH Digital Innovation Summit, WEBER presented innovations for three series. The TTS series has been redesigned and can be equipped with various WEBER brushing techniques.

“Different methods can also be combined, according to requirements. For example, edge rounding with a radius of 2mm is increasingly of interest for customers. The new WEBER TTS solves this by combining the R2 brushstation and the P2 planetary head,” said WEBER.

The design of the WEBER brush heads, with several tool carriers arranged side by side, is space-saving and variable in width. The tool carriers are arranged in such a way that they interlock and overlap widely. As a result, said WEBER, the processing result is the same over the working width and is both constant and gapless. Elaborate mechanical oscillation of the brush units is not necessary. As an alternative, the assembly with two P2 planetary heads or a P6 planetary head is available.

WEBER’s series of TN compact wet grinding machines has also been extended by modern brushing technology. In addition to the abrasive belt station and round brushes, the P6 planetary head can now also be used in the series.

“This means that perfect edge rounding, according to today’s criteria, is also possible after pregrinding with the grinding belt.

With an optional second grinding roller, a corresponding finish can be achieved,” said WEBER.

For mass punched parts and precise fine blanked parts, the NLCA series is equipped with a compact addition to TNA, which can be furnished with up to three planetary head stations. It features a magnetic clamping table, demagnetisation and control with a SIEMENS TouchPanel. The dimensions are compact to allow for easy chaining into narrow production lines.
