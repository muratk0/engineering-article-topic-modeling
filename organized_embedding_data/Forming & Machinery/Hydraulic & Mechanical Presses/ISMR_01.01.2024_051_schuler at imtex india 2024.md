# Schuler at IMTEX India 2024

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

Schuler, a global specialist in metalforming solutions, will be exhibiting at the IMTEX India 2024 trade show from 19-23 January 2024 in Bangalore.

"Visitors to stand A105 in Hall 5 will be able to explore a wide array of new technologies tailored to elevate customer processes," outlined Nitin Kulkarni, Managing Director, Schuler India Private Limited.

"Highlights of our showcase at the event will include hot stamping innovations as an important part of automotive lightweight construction. Visitors can discover the new FAST Technology for aluminium which not only reduces the space requirements, but also significantly lowers investment and operating costs," added the manufacturer.

Schuler's new press line family includes the Servo MAX, Servo NEXT and Mech NEXT for sheet-metal forming of outer skin parts as well as structural parts. Schuler Service is also offered to its customers as well as its Digital Suite applications including 'Visual Die Protection' or 'Track and Trace' to transform and optimise the metalforming workflow.

Schuler offers customised technology in all areas of forming—from the networked press to press shop planning. Presses from the Schuler Group mint coins for more than 180 countries. Its products also include automation; dies; process expertise and service for the entire metalworking industry. Founded in 1839 at Göppingen, Germany, Schuler has approximately 5,000 employees at production sites in Europe, China and the Americas, as well as service companies in over 40 countries. The company is part of the international technology group, ANDRITZ.■
