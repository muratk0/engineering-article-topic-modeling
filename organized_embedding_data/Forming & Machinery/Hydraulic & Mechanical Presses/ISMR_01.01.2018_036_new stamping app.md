# New stamping app

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

FIELDHAWK IS a cloud-based communications mobile application designed to communicate with Nidec Minster stamping presses from iOS or Android mobile devices. Cloud-based, secured communications enable all authorised users to check machinery status from wherever there is a phone service and/or an internet connection.

"With Fieldhawk, users receive real-time updates for press status; operating condition; production data and more. Up-to-the minute communications keeps manufacturers in control of their operations so that they stay informed and stay productive," said the U.S. press manufacturer.

Service alerts delivered on a mobile device from the Minster Production Management Control (PMC) press control panel keeps operators informed of the forthcoming service intervals required, allowing them to proactively schedule Minster parts and service to reduce downtime.

"Remotely monitoring your stamping press activities with FieldHawk allows for improved productivity; proactive maintenance reminders; predictive analytics; reduced maintenance costs and a better return on your valuable capital machinery investments," concluded Nidec Minster. See www.minster.com
