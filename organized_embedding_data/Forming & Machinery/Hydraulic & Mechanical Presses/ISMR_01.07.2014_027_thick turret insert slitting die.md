# Thick turret insert slitting die

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

MATE PRECISION TOOLING has introduced the new VERSADIE™ thick turret insert slitting die for D and E Stations. The VERSADIE design takes clamp clearing operations to a new level by reducing material cost and waste for slitting operations by punching closely to the clamps and allowing users to replace die inserts without replacing the entire slitting die.

Mate’s new VERSADIE features replaceable die inserts that accommodate lengths up to 4.560 inches (115.82mm) for E stations and 3.560 inches (90.42mm) for D stations. These are currently the longest available in the industry, says Mate. The die body is easily shimmed after the insert is sharpened during routine maintenance – there is no need for special insert shims. Mate’s SlugFree® die design also prevents slug pulling and stops damage to the piece part and the tooling.

“Clamp clearing dies are necessary when maximum sheet usage is required,” reported John Galich, Mate’s marketing manager. “VERSADIE now provides the ability to punch as close to the clamps as possible,

“VERSADIE is stronger than ordinary slitting tool dies and withstands the most demanding punching operations. The die insert is precision manufactured from MPM82 tool steel. This high speed, particle metallurgy steel allows for sharper edges on the die opening, giving VERSADIE high value and long lasting versatility – ideal for slitting operations. It also has tighter tolerances of the insert to the holder. Together, these design features ensure greater machine uptime, lower tooling costs and overall lower fabricating costs. The die body itself is made of S7 shock resistant tool steel,” added the manufacturer. See www.mate.com ■

## Section and plate bending

SCHWARZE-ROBITEC will be showing its latest solutions for the forming of plates and sections in Hall 6 (booth C06) at the EuroBLECH exhibition (21-25 October 2014 in Hannover, Germany). Two new bending machines (Series Platebender PB for plates and Sectionbender SB for sections) will be displayed. The bending specialist will also be presenting a new control for its tube bending machines and the TorqueFormer (to form sections with complex isometrics torsion superimposed and three-dimensional).

The new PB plate bending machine processes plates with a material thickness of just a few millimetres all the way to 125 millimetres. They are equipped optionally with three or four rollers. The machines already have eleven axes and therefore create a larger bandwidth for bending contours. As the SB machines process both flat or angle irons as well as T-, I-, C- and special sections in the most varied formats and dimensions, the technology is used in the most diverse sectors of the industry.

See www.schwarze-robitec.com

## PRODUCT REVIEWS

JET EDGE, INC., a leading manufacturer of ultra-high pressure water jet technology, will be exhibiting its latest 5-axis precision water jet cutting system at the EuroBLECH International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition (21-25 October in Hanover, Germany). Look for Jet Edge in Hall 16, Stand G72. During EuroBLECH, Jet Edge will perform live 5-axis abrasive waterjet cutting demonstrations on its premiere 90,000 psi (6200 bar) EDGE X-5™ 5-axis waterjet with Aquavision Di control. It will also showcase its 90,000 psi (6200 bar) X-Stream intensifier pumps and eco-friendly 55,000 psi (3800 bar) Eco-Jet direct drive pumps.

“Our EDGE X-5 5-axis waterjet cuts precise taper-free parts from virtually any material. The system is capable of cutting sophisticated 3D parts such as impellers and bevels up to 50°. Its sturdy design separates the motion system from the catcher tank, eliminating vibration and ensuring maximum part quality. It is available in many sizes and there is an optional second cutting head to double productivity,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

Jet Edge’s 100hp, 90KSI (75kw, 6200 bar), X-Stream xP90-100 waterjet intensifier pump is capable of producing 90KSI (6200 bar) and supports 75KSI (5200 bar) continuous operating pressure. The X-Stream produces 50 per cent more pressure than a 60KSI (4100 bar) intensifier pump, resulting in a 40-50 per cent increase in productivity for many materials. Compared to a 60KSI (4100 bar) pump, typical operating pressures of 75KSI (5200 bar) use 30 per cent less water, 30 per cent less power and up to 50 per cent less abrasive, resulting in a 40 per cent reduction in operating costs, says Jet Edge.

The 30hp, 55KSI (22kw, 3800 bar) Jet Edge ECO-JET waterjet pump features an efficient direct drive pump design that consumes up to 40 per cent less electricity than a 50hp (37kw) hydraulic intensifier pump, but produces the same output, according to the company. See www.jetedge.com ■
