Avure Technologies is a key global provider of ultra-high pressure systems utilised to process advanced materials and to form sheet metal. It has installed over 1,700 high-pressure systems worldwide. It recently supplied a hydroforming deep-draw press to the Kale Pratt & Whitney joint venture to manufacture aircraft engine components in Turkey.

In late 2014 a grant was awarded to Avure Technologies to support the demand for new low weight material within the aerospace industry. The project will focus on the development of an economical fabrication process for titanium sheet metal forming by using high-pressure, combined with a gentle temperature elevation. The complete project is scheduled to be completed in 2016.

At its headquarters in Frasnacht, Switzerland, Bruderer develops complete solutions which are exported around the globe. EuroBLECH 2014 saw the launch of its new, fully-automatic, stamping press, BSTA 510-150, with a tool-loading area of 1500mm.

Bruderer has set up two new strategic partnerships with Zani Presses and Zehnder & Sommer. The latter’s high speed servo-feed units can operate up to 2000 strokes per minute (SPM).

Chin Fong’s new single point servo presses (iLS/DLS series) ranging from 80 – 300 tons; its double-point SDS series from 400 up to 1000 tonnes; the compound link LF and KW series for stamping-forging composite processes; the long stroke knuckle joint presses KL and warm-hot forging WF series have facilitated its slogan: “Intelligence of Forming, Vision of Forging”.
