# Blank punching solutions

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Pivatic has expanded its product portfolio to include flexible integrated sheet metal blank punching solutions. Its offering now also includes storage solutions from Remmert (www.remmert.de); the integrated PivaPunch PCB (Punching Centre for Blanks) sheet punching centre; PivaBend bending lines; flexible Pivatic MPB (Multitool Panel Bender) benders and RAS (www.ras-systems.com) panel bending solutions.

A general focus for Pivatic has been to increase production line flexibility where smaller batches and kit production is possible with minimum set-up times or a buffer storage between process stages. Punched and bent parts sit next to each other during the working process and are automatically transported to the next process stage.

Sheet metal blanks can be produced on the PivaCut blanking line before reaching the punching machine. The PivaCut line features coil storage, decoiler, straightener and shear for longitudinal cutting.

PivaCut Cut-to-Size solution shears all edges of the blank, This way, any possible camber is removed and the sheet cross dimensions are accurate. It is also optional to include dynamic and/or parametric sheet nesting with a solution like this," Pivatic told SMR.

Applications for integrated Pivatic line solutions include steel doors; cable ducts; elevator doors; façade claddings; electrical enclosures and HVAC parts. Meet Pivatic at Blechexpo in Stuttgart this October in Hall 1, stand 1108.
