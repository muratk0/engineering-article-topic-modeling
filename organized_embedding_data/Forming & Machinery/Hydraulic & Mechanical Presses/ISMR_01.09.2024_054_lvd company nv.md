# LVD Company NV

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand F104

www.lvdgroup.com

LVD will highlight solutions for smart manufacturing at EuroBLECH. On display will be 20kW laser cutting as well as bending automation, folding and software technology.

This will include a new low-cost fibre laser; high-tonnage E press brake; robotic bending cell; panel bender and the latest CADMAN® software. LVD will also unveil a new, "ultrapractical" laser cutting machine offering, it told ISMR, low investment and operating costs for customers.

"The Easy-Form 9 400/40 press brake will be the largest press brake LVD has ever exhibited at EuroBLECH. The 400-ton machine features numerous configurable options for precision bending of large/long parts. Easy-Cell 80/25 combines an Easy-Form 80-ton press brake with a robot to automate bending of parts from 100 x 100mm up to 1600 x 1200mm weighing up to 25kg," outlined the manufacturer.

"LVD's Multifold 2111 panel bender demonstrates automatic loading, folding and stacking of workpieces. CADMAN-Flow offers a single point of entry to the CADMAN software suite and easier, faster programming," it concluded.
