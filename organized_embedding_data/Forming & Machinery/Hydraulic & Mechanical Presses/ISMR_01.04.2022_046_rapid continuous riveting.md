# Rapid, continuous riveting

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Zygology has launched a new Z-AR Auto Feed riveting system to speed up the riveting assembly process.

"One of the most frequent questions we are asked is how to speed up riveting assembly processes, but not at the cost of efficiency or quality control. Our new Z-AR Auto Feed riveting system is designed to reduce manufacturing lead times, minimise downtime and speed up assembly," the manufacturer explained.

"The most significant and common factor that slows down the assembly process is human. The repeated requirement is for the operator to reach into a box, pick up a rivet, insert it into the nose of the tool and then install it. The new Z-AR system removes that continuous requirement," it added.

The Z-AR's bowl feeder holds up to 1,000 rivets at a time. By utilising specially adapted Stanley XT1 or XT2 tools attached to the Z-AR bowl feeder and control unit, rivets are fed to the tool nose tip at the rate of 60 or more per minute. Rivet handling by the tool operator can be almost eliminated, and downtime is significantly reduced.

"After each rivet is installed, the PLC controlled mechanism feeds a new rivet from the bowl feeder to the nose tip almost instantly. The speed of assembly is limited only by the dexterity of the tool operator," continued Zygology.

"Spent stems are automatically ejected from the tool and collected in a storage container in the control unit for quick and easy disposal or recycling. The system features Stanley XT1 and XT2 tools, the transfer mechanism is easy to remove from the tool and, as the tools use readily available parts, routine maintenance is quick, cost-effective and straightforward," it said.

The Z-AR system can accommodate most standard rivet types of between 3 and 5mm in diameter, and rivet materials can be accommodated including aluminium, copper, steel and A2 and A4 stainless steel. The flexible umbilical enables 360° riveting in any direction.
