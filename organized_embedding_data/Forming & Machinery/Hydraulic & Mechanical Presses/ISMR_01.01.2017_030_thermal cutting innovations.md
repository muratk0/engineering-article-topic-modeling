# Thermal cutting innovations

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

MEET KOIKE, the Japanese thermal cutting solutions specialist, at SteelFab 2017 in Sharjah, UAE from 16-19 January 2017. The global specialist in oxy fuel, plasma and laser cutting machines with high-end technologies can be found on booth 1100 in Hall 4 at the show.

Koike Middle East (the local sales and service support office for the Middle East with a technology centre and spare parts support in the UAE) will be present at SteelFab. Koike has local support partners in all Middle Eastern countries, it told SMR, with a strong customer base of structural fabricators; offshore, oil and gas fabrication and general fabrication manufacturers in the Middle East.

"We will be showing many new innovations at SteelFab and will give live demonstrations on the Deltatec and PNC-12 EXTREME CNC cutting machines. The PNC-12 Extreme with CutFire 100i and new D420 control will also be demonstrated live in Sharjah. A completely new range of mechanised portable welding machines will also be introduced at the show," explained the manufacturer.

The PNC-12 EXTREME is an innovative new portable CNC cutting solution which is suitable for both oxy-fuel and plasma cutting. The assembly and set-up of all components (such as machine body, rail, and cross bar) do not require any special measuring tools. It is pre-configured for different sizes. The oxy-fuel package comes with an oxy-fuel torch set with integrated automated piercing sequence programme and gas on/off solenoid valves on all gas lines. The plasma cutting package comes with Initial Torch Height Sensor (IHS), Arc Voltage height Control (AVC), a torch break-away system and a 35mm diameter plasma torch holder.

Koike will be organising a special seminar at SteelFab 2017 on its new smart plasma bevel system, inkjet printer system, portable cutting machines and new mechanised portable welding machines on 17 January 2017 from 12.00-12.30pm. See www.koike.ae or www.koike-europe.com

## Self-clinching stand-off fasteners

NEW PEM® SELF-CLINCHING stainless steel stand-off fasteners with bright nickel plating provide corrosion resistance and, according to manufacturer PennEngineering®, contribute to an attractive finish in stainless steel assemblies. These 400 Series stainless steel threaded fasteners enable the mounting, spacing or stacking of panels, boards or components.

"They install into thin, stainless host sheets by being pressing into pre-punched and properly-sized round mounting holes. After installation into sheets with hardnesses of up to HRB 88/HB 183, they become permanent parts of an assembly and will not loosen or fall out. A mating screw completes the attachment process," said the manufacturer.

“This nickel-plated stainless product line includes Type SO4™ through-hold threaded stand-offs, Type BSO4™ blind threaded stand-offs (whose closed threads enable a flush appearance on the back of the host sheet) and Type TSO4™ threaded stand-offs engineered for installation into especially thin stainless sheets."

Type SO4 and Type BSO4 stand-offs are available in thread sizes from #4-40 to #8-32/M3 through to M5 and will install in stainless sheets as thin as 0.040"/1mm. Type TSO4 standoffs are offered in thread sizes from #2-56 to #6-32/M2.5 through to M3.5 and will install in stainless sheets as thin as 0.025"/0.63mm.

Detailed specifications and performance data (Bulletin SONP), fastener drawings and models can be accessed at www.pemnet.com ■
