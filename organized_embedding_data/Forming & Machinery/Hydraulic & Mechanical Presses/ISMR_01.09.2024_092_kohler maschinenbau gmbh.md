# KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand D71 (also 11-B13)

www.kohler-germany.com

At EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, KOHLER will showcase a part levelling machine and a precision leveller with horizontal coiler for levelling strip material for connectors, plugs and electronic components.

KOHLER develops and produces part levelling machines and strip feeding lines, for presses and automatic stamping machines, as well as cut-to-length lines for steel service centres, mechanical engineering firms and for the automotive industry. The KOHLER product range offers solutions from individual machines to linked and automated production lines.

"We offer innovative solutions, such as trendsetting drive concepts or dispensing with hydraulic systems on our levelling machines. The Peak Performer part-levelling machine levels and relieves stress from sheets and part blanks. There is no need for time-consuming, manual rework," it highlighted.

## Nidec Press & Automation

Stand J90

www.nidecpa.com

The Nidec Press & Automation (NP&A) brand brings together Minster; Arisa; Kyori; Vamco; CHS; SYS and, most recently, Automatic Feed Company products to the market, allowing combined synergies to offer efficient solutions to manufacturers. NP&A has established a single-source solution for machinery, services and technology. The group operates a shared global customer service network with major service centres in Minster, Ohio (USA); Mexico; Germany, Spain and China.

"We are positioned to offer turnkey integrated manufacturing systems featuring the quality and innovation of the world's leading metal forming companies. NPA-branded press line components offer full OEM integration, prior to installation, increasing operator ease of use as well as uptime and reducing required shop space. Our shared manufacturing facilities produce responsive lead times and lower costs for pressrooms," it outlined.
