Beam drill works quickly, eliminates postprocessing

Next to accuracy, speed is one of the most important conditions in the steel industry. The Peddi-SubX-1120 from Peddinghaus can process a beam with milled block copes on both flanges in 90 sec. without any rework or grinding. Eliminating the need for grinding or any other type of postprocessing helps restore labor hours to maximize profits, the manufacturer reports.

The beam drill line performs drilling, milling, coping, and layout marking within a 1911/16-in. operating window in seconds.

Plate sorting system identifies, stores parts automatically

The Voortman Plate Sorter is designed to identify flat parts automatically and store them in a vertical storage system. Production planning is informed if any parts are missing for upcoming fabrication, giving them time to create the part packages just in time for fabrication.

The system comprises a high-end 3D camera, storage tower, robot, and automated pallet stacking unit.

VOORTMAN USA LLC

www.voortman.net

Portable CNC plasma cutting system simple to program

At 35 lbs. and 20 by 14.5 by 10 in., the ArcDroid CNC plasma machine is portable for use in shops and job sites. With its Simple Trace custom operating system, the machine can reproduce the user’s designs, delivering accurate, repeatable plasma-cut parts.

The user traces a template point to point or freehand, and the machine creates a cut file instantly, says the company. It also can import .dxf files. The system is compatible with Fusion 360, Pronest, and SheetCAM.

Multifunction cutting machine configured for part removal system

The K5200xmc plasma cutting, oxyfuel cutting, and milling system from Kinetic Cutting Systems Inc. is identical to the K5000xmc but comes configured for the addition of an automated system that removes finished parts from the machine.

The low-profile, automated carriage lets the part removal system pick, place, and palletize parts and sort them by part, work order, or batch number. It can travel under the cutting gantry, so there is no need to stop the machine to remove parts.

Hydraulic angle roll bends large pipe, beams

RMT—Revolution Machine Tools offers the A-FAB HD 433 angle roll. This heavy-duty machine offers hydraulic bottom rolls adjustment, includes universal tooling, and bends up to 4-in. Sch. 40 pipe with 40 in. ID. The machine also bends I beams, H beams, and nearly any other structural steel shape.

Cutting machine supports two laser or plasma heads

The new Element 400L Unitized cutting machine from Messer Cutting Systems is a large-format laser cutting up to 20 kW. It supports plasma and laser beveling and marking.

This U.S.-manufactured cutting machine has acceleration and speeds that can support two laser or plasma heads with no loss of performance, the company states.

The new Bevel-U is a high-precision bevel unit that supports laser beveling up to 50 degrees. The higher acceleration of the cutting machine provides improved cut quality with vertical or bevel cutting.

Multiprofile plasma cutter combines fabrication steps into one process

The EL-SQRD from Electro-Mechanical Integrators is a CNC plasma cutting machine that processes round, square, rectangle, angle, channel, and flat stock to 24 ft. long. Features include manual loading, two auto supports, and the PieceMaker software package.

In one smooth process, the machine can miter cut, profile, cope, and burn holes and slots in multiple profiles. It reduces or eliminates manual measuring, sawing, notching, drilling, deburring, and repetitive handling.

Three-roll bender has 2½-in. capacity

Eagle Bending Machines offers the BA55 CNC double-pinch section bending machine. Its three driven rolls are managed by the servo-controlled ProBending system. The machine can be used to create variable-radii shapes and polycentric forms from almost any material type.

The unit has a 2½-in. capacity, full CNC direct radius input controls, and horizontal and vertical operation. It can be used in full automatic, semiautomatic, or manual mode.

The user inputs part length, radius, bend degree, arc length, and other information and the CNC automatically creates the 2D graphics and the program.

Software allows user to control CNC plate roll bending results

BENDtronic CNC plate rolling software from Haeusler AG allows anyone to control not only the movements of the machine but also the bending result. The software is graphically supported and enables bending in one or several moves. In addition, it can be used for any type of product shape, such as cylinders and tubes, ovals, tanks, and complex geometries up to S-shaped workpieces.

With the help of the system’s intelligent information feedback, a fully automatic bending process is possible from the first sheet with little rework and no scrap, says the company.

Synergy plate bending rolls from Trilogy Machinery Inc. are designed to offer economical bending for small to intermediate-size fabricators and job shops. Distance between roll bender’s lower roller shafts adjustable from 11 to 37 in.

J&S Machine will display the Clomea CRMX70 roll bender. It features three independently driven drive rolls for bending up to 5½-in. tube, 2½-ft. square bars, or 3-in. solid round bar. Distance between the lower roller shafts can be adjusted from 11 to 37 in. to ensure best roll positioning for the material type and radius required.

This machine and the company’s other benders can be equipped with different controls, from manual to highly technical CNC. One bender can be powered by six different control models, allowing a fabricator to purchase a manual control and upgrade to a more powerful control as its needs and capability requirements grow.

Plasma cutter reaches speeds to 1,500 IPM

Park Industries’ Kano HD 510 plasma cutting system has a unitized design and heavy-duty steel construction with a table up to 10 by 24 ft. The machine reaches speeds to 1,500 IPM and offers a cutting area of 64 by 124 in.

The system can be configured with a Hypertherm Powermax air plasma, Maxpro200 oxygen plasma, or an XPR high-definition plasma system. An oxyfuel torch also is available.
