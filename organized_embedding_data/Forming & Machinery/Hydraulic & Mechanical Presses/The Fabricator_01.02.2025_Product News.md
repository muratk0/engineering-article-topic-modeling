Controls packages automate sheet metal cutting machines

The Eckelmann Group offers E°CUT Controls for sheet metal cutting machines including plasma, laser, oxyfuel, and waterjet. The automation packages are available in three choices: E°CUT Elements for the light industrial segment, E°CUT ECO for high-definition cutting machines, and E°CUT PRO for high-end and combination machines. Each all-in-one package includes CNC, CAD/CAM software, servo drives and servomotors, industrial PC, and HMI software.

Compact electric press brake suitable for fabricating small sheet metal components

AMADA AMERICA has debuted the EGB 4010e compact electric press brake for producing small, precise sheet metal components. The tool cabinet provides quick access to tooling to help improve setup time, while the Tool Setup Assistant helps boost productivity, the company reports.

Punches and dies slide easily into the bed to help improve loading efficiency. Dies also can be placed into the bed. Extra storage for tooling is provided at the bottom of the cabinet.

Press brake tooling line expanded

Mate Precision Technologies has added more than 500 items across press brake tooling lines.

Amada Fixed Height Style profiles are suitable for bending operations with small production lots and a variety of projects. They provide a consistent height for all punch profiles to eliminate the need for multiple setups to account for uneven heights. They also eliminate the need to make frequent adjustments to the safety beam.

The company also has expanded its range of punch and die profiles for its Wila Trumpf Style and patented American Precision Style with Maglock press brake tooling. Using rotating supports to bend up to 40 degrees without marking, the new Wila Trumpf Style version of Clean-Bend is now available in eight sizes.

Compact automated bending system fits almost any production environment

Bystronic has launched the ByCell Bend Star M. It is based on the recently introduced ByBend Star 120 press brake and fills the gap between small mobile bending robots and large bending cells.

With its compact, modular layout, the fully automatic bending cell is suitable for small and large companies, processing small batches up to large series. Together, the press brake and robot complete bending jobs automatically and without errors, according to the company.

Featuring a footprint of 21.32 by 20.34 ft., the bending cell is equipped with RF-AC tooling, enabling automatic tool changing by the robot. The vision system eliminates the need for referencing.

Remote support service program launched for automated laser cutting machines

TRUMPF has introduced Remote Operations Support, a new service for automated laser cutting machine customers. The service connects experts in Germany with U.S.-based customers operating the TruLaser Center, a system that automates loading of sheet metal, laser cutting, part removal, and sorting.

This service reduces the need for additional on-site staff to monitor the night shift, as a team of TRUMPF experts in Neukirch, Germany, remotely monitors the machinery in the U.S. and can intervene and resolve errors to keep the laser cutting system producing parts.

TRUMPF Inc.

www.trumpf.com

Waterjet system cuts complex parts out of most materials

Hypertherm offers the OMAX MAXIEM 1530 JetMachining Center equipped with an A-Jet cutting head and a 40-HP direct-drive pump that generates output pressure of 50,000 PSI.

With a cutting envelope of 10 ft. by 5 ft. 2 in., the waterjet system cuts complex parts out of most materials, generating surface finishes that often do not require secondary machining. The system comes standard with the company’s IntelliMAX software suite.
