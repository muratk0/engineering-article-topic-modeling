## Bihler (Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik GmbH)

## www.bihler.de

Bihler offer automated manufacturing solutions in stamping, forming, welding and assembly technology.

"Electromobility is in the fast lane. Our automation solutions for e-components are pushing this trend further. Among other highlights, we will be presenting our new BM-HP 3000 hairpin machine at this year's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart," it told ISMR.

"This compact servo production system unlocks new possibilities for the industrial mass production of all common hairpin types. To this end, the BM-HP 3000 combines all process steps into one efficient complete solution. This starts with the dynamic, slip-free feeding of the wire; precision cutting; NC-controlled mechanical stripping of the coating; simultaneous chamfering of the pin ends; 2D pre-bending and 3D die bending all the way to removal and mono-component storage. Users benefit from cycle rates that are three times higher than with sequential systems, easy and rapid variant changes 'on the fly' and consistently high production reliability," it added.

## G.RAU GmbH

## www.g-rau.de

At Blechexpo 2021, G.RAU will present its NITINOL precise strip with homogeneous mechanical and physical properties.

"This super-elastic strip has a shape memory sensor and actuator. Users can produce long single strip lengths for a cost-saving processing option, such as reel-to-reel processing. The standard dimension range starts at a strip thickness of 0.10mm and ends at approximately 2.00mm (sheet thickness tolerance of +/- 0.01mm)," said the German manufacturer.

As a standard for super-elastic applications, it uses ASTM F2063-compliant NITINOL. It delivers the strip in widths of up to approximately 35mm.

On request, it also provides packaging (including special packaging); labelling; coding and sorting. Its customers include small and medium-sized enterprises as well as global players such as Amphenol; Bosch; Philips; Siemens, Wieland and ZF.

## Meusburger Georg GmbH

## www.meusburger.com

Product innovations from Meusburger at this October's Blechexpo will include its high-precision standard components for the modular concept.

"The modular concept in stamping dies has been part of our standard practice for years. A universally usable basis die, also called a base set, is made into a customised stamping die with the help of slide-in die modules," it explained to /SMR.

"Choosing the right gas spring among the wide range of products currently available on the market is not easy. Often questions arise such as: 'How much power do I need? Is this the right size? What about installation or maintenance?' This is exactly where we wish to support customers," it added.

It offers SB stamping and forming die sets for 200kN, 300kN and 400kN Bihler presses. The die sets are available from stock in lengths of up to 596mm and as type SBH 'tunnel cut' or 'SBP spring-loaded guiding plate'.

## Zehnder & Sommer AG

## www.zehndersommer.com

With 50 years of experience and advanced press feeding technology, Zehnder & Sommer is a partner for press automation and stamping/forming parts production.

"Precision, speed and reliability are key for press feeding technology. This includes the manufacture of components or precision parts in various industries such as automotive; electrical and electronics; packaging; household; white goods; cosmetics; aerosols; armaments; medical technology or rotor/stator lamination," explained the Swiss specialist.

Its EV XG performance roll feed offers a feed velocity of up to 400m/minute (fast pilot releasing for production of up to 600 strokes/minute). The feed has a direct drive (no gears) and features synchronously driven rolls over torsion-free performance coupling. Feeding precision is +/-0.02mm. Band widths are up to 320, 480 and 640mm, and strip cross section is up to 2000mm².

"Focus on product developments, we have strengthened our global team over the last few months," it added.
