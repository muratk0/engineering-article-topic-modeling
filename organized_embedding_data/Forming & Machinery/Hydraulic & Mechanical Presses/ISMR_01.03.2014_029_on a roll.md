# On a roll

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

FORMIT LTD specialises in rollforming machines and coil-handling equipment and has been manufacturing for over 25 years. Over recent years, it has developed a new breed of hydraulic flexible manufacturing systems for punching, forming and cropping, with quick-change tooling, for various different products in several material thicknesses and types.

## PRODUCT REVIEWS

“We have received orders for machinery and tooling worth over £1 million, which we believe reflects the improvement in the economic climate over the past two years, and we hope that this success will continue,” the company explained.

Formit Ltd also specialises in refurbishment and retrofitting to customers existing equipment. It will be exhibiting at the MACH 2014 exhibition in the UK on Stand 4534 in Hall 4 (MMMA Village). See www.formit.co.uk ■
