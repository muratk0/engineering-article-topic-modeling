## LASCO UMFORMTECHNIK GmbH

www.lasco.com

LASCO supplies machine tools and production lines for forming tasks. It develops, designs and manufactures individual solutions for industrial users in more than 60 countries.

"In servo drive technology, servo motors are directly coupled with hydraulic pumps. Positioning of the hydraulic pistons, adjustment of the pressing speed as well as the setting of force values can be made without using control or proportional valves. This drive technology is particularly effective at positioning tasks. The value of the actual position of the moving press ram is transferred to the axis control position controller. The servo drive then moves the hydraulic piston to its required position by feeding the exact quantity of hydraulic oil into the cylinder," LASCO told ISMR.

## SCHULER AG

www.schulergroup.com

IN THE AGE of the Industrial Internet, machines that communicate are no longer something to aspire to in the future. At the EuroBLECH trade fair, Schuler's "Smart Press Shop" concept will demonstrate how networking solutions in forming technology can increase process reliability and cost-effectiveness in production. Many Schuler lines already have the necessary interfaces for comprehensive networking. Simulations also provide valuable information for the virtual optimisation of the entire system.

Schuler offers tools for optimising output, helping to considerably reduce the time required for commissioning. The systems also provide data measured by sensors installed at numerous points, for example to monitor the press force. If the press force progression deviates from a particular pattern, this indicates irregularities in the process. The information can be used to maintain the line, thereby preventing damage to the machine and tool.

"In nine out of ten cases, problems can be solved remotely," explained the German press specialist.
