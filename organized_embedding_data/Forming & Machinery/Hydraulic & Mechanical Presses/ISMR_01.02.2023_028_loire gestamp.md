# Loire Gestamp

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

Loire Gestamp, a branch of the Gestamp group, designs and manufactures all types of hydraulic presses and can adapt products to the specific needs of each project. Processes include hot stamping, cold stamping (deep drawing presses), hydroforming and try-out tooling.

Situated in Hernani, Gipuzkoa (Spain), since 1962, Loire Gestamp is one of Europe's largest producers of hydraulic presses. Loire Gestamp installed its first fully monitored servo-mechanical line at Gestamp Navarra plant in 2020 to ensure greater production and flexibility with lower energy consumption.

It also has internal capabilities for the development and manufacture of dies, covering the entire value chain: design; machining; construction; tuning; prototyping and monitoring. In this way, Gestamp keeps its stamping process experience in the group.

## Looking ahead

With 25 years of experience, Gestamp has moved from being a small local stamping company to a global company operating in the main automotive manufacturing hubs. The company is present in 24 countries with more than 100 production plants, 13 R&D centres and a workforce of nearly 40,000 employees worldwide.

The company offers processes such as hot stamping; cold stamping; rollforming; hydroforming; welding (including laser welding); assembly and patchwork blanking. It has embraced ongoing Industry-4.0 initiatives that cover different production processes from hot stamping, cold stamping or arc welding to auxiliary processes (such as maintenance, logistics, quality or energy) in its march towards the Smart Factory.

Its turnover in 2021 was 8,093 million euros. In November 2022, Gestamp presented its results for the first nine months of 2022.

Francisco J. Riberas, Executive Chairman of Gestamp, confirmed: "Our results continue to reflect Gestamp's strong positioning and the success of our strategy. Looking ahead, market conditions remain difficult due to high inflationary pressures, rising interest rates and global geopolitical challenges. In this context, the company will continue focusing on preserving profitability and its balance sheet position for future growth."

During the first nine months of 2022, Gestamp's revenues increased by 30.9% in reported terms, reaching €7,697 million. EBITDA stood at €847 million, registering an increase of 20.9% compared to the first nine months of 2021. Regarding profitability and liquidity, EBITDA margin stood at 11 % (12.4% excluding raw material impact) in the period and the company generated a free cash flow of €65 million from July to September 2022. Gestamp recorded a net profit of €183 million over the first nine months of the year.

Investments have reached levels of 7.5% of revenues for the period, €581 million in absolute terms. As the company is focused on electrification trends, over 50% of its first nine months of 2022 tangible CAPEX is related to electric vehicles (EV). As electrification trends accelerate, Gestamp continues to focus on the development of projects that help its clients to accelerate this transition, such as battery box and extreme size parts.

## Cleaner and greener

Gestamp aims to provide solutions that make mobility cleaner and safer by adding new products to its portfolio and covering its clients' EV needs. For example, the recent resolution of the Spanish Ministry of Industry regarding the development of the Electric and Connected Vehicle (PERTE VEC) is a boost for the Spanish automotive industry to develop projects that promote electric vehicles. Gestamp is present in three of the five projects led by the OEMs.

The company is continuously working on achieving sustainability goals that “are part of its DNA," focusing on a “greener" automotive industry and creating awareness of the importance of the circular economy. For example, it is trying to increase the sustainable origin and efficient use of the energy required for its production. In Brazil, Gestamp has just signed an agreement with Cemig to supply its electricity at the plants from renewable energy sources.

"The agreement will ensure that, from 2024, all our plants and our R&D centre in Brazil will work with 100% renewable energy and propel the company ahead in sustainable operations as Gestamp becomes the first automotive components company in Brazil to operate with electricity generated in a completely renewable way. With this new contract in Brazil, we are expanding an ongoing strategy that started in Spain with an agreement with Naturgy, aiming at the decarbonization of all our activities," commented Gestamp.

It is aligned with the Six Sustainable Development Goals of the United Nations 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development and has accordingly developed its Sustainability Policy and Strategic Plan 2021.

## FOCUS ON STAMPING

Fourth hot stamping line in India

Ges/Multistep is the latest generation of Gestamp's hot stamping technology, where ultra-high-strength steels parts are made through a rapid succession of consecutive stamping steps. During the process, parts pass through several pressing stations in quick succession and experience rapid but gradual cooling. This way, Gestamp manages to obtain sophisticated geometry for its parts as well as a better crash behaviour and lightweight performance.

In January 2023 at the Auto Expo 2023-Components exhibition in New Delhi, India, Gestamp announced its fourth hot stamping line in India. The company was showcasing its range of products and innovations for mobility such as its extreme size parts; battery boxes; cell-to-pack concepts; joining technologies and chassis innovative lightweight solutions or 'Active Frunks'.

The Indian automotive sector has grown exponentially to become the third largest automotive market in the world. Gestamp is looking ahead to 2023 in India with a new hotstamping line to be opened by the end of the year, in addition to the two existing ones in Pune and another in Chennai.

“Since its creation in 2006, Gestamp India has followed a strategy (as it does worldwide) to be close to its customers by co-developing solutions in close proximity to manufacturers. Thanks to its strong R&D capabilities, it can co-develop projects together with Indian customers and help them on the road to electrification," commented Gestamp.

Gestamp's third business unit, under the Edscha brand, was present for the first time in India at Auto Expo 2023- Components. In May 2022, Edscha signed a joint venture agreement with the Indian automotive supplier Aditya Auto Products & Engineering, based in Chakan. The cooperation extends to the development, manufacture and distribution of hinge systems, door controls, latches and parking brakes, and this Spring will see initial production of module hinges at the Pune plant.

## Mobility solutions

Gestamp presented its technologies for connected and electric mobility in Wolfsburg (October 2022) at the International Suppliers Fair (IZB). This included extreme size parts; battery boxes; cell-to-pack concepts; joining technologies; chassis innovative lightweight solutions and Active Frunks or power doors.

Aspects such as safety, lightweight construction and a reduction in CO₂ emissions continue to play a decisive role. Against this background, Gestamp's R&D teams showcased core innovations and technologies from its three business areas at the IZB: Body, Chassis and Mechanisms (Edscha).

## Body in White (BiW)

Extreme size parts is a new generation of body parts that integrate multiple functions as one-piece solutions in giga-stamping.

Ignacio Martin (Chief Technology and Innovation Officer, Gestamp) explained: “This new product family offers a unique combination of increased crash safety, light weight, lower costs and CO₂ reduction in a single product. This hot-stamped steel solution with different material properties and thickness combinations allows our OEMs to offer a significant reduction in assembly time. Extreme size parts represent an optimal solution for the design of new electric vehicles and make it possible to largely cope with crash safety in the vehicle body and therefore reduce the complexity of the battery box."

Gestamp also develops safer and lighter battery electric vehicle (BEV) solutions specifically designed for urban mobility. It showed, for the first time, a multi-material battery box made of fibre-reinforced plastic FRP (100% reusable and circularly recyclable) and aluminium. This product could represent a solution for small electric vehicles. This intelligent concept for urban mobility is part of another product family of battery concepts developed by Gestamp R&D.

"Battery concepts fit seamlessly into the extreme size body concepts, allowing us to offer our customers less complex and more efficient solutions. We presented some new developments for the mobility requirements of tomorrow such as engineered products and related technologies like Multistep, BKT (Bending Kinematic Technology) laser technology and an insight into aluminium competencies," explained the company.

## Gestamp expands plant in Japan

Gestamp is one of the world's largest manufacturers of automotive hot stamping parts. Gestamp Hotstamping Japan has decided to expand with a new plant building on its premises in Matsusaka. This decision is part of Gestamps expansion in Asia, which is key for its global strategy. The expansion meets increasing demand for parts from Japanese automobile manufacturers. The new expansion of the Matsusaka plant is scheduled to be operational by June 2023.

Hot stamping entails heating the material until it becomes malleable. It is subsequently subjected to a formation and rapid cooling process to produce a transformed and hardened material. This process requires fewer steps than the traditional stamping process to achieve lighter and more resistant products.

As Gestamp's first Japanese base, Gestamp Hotstamping Japan has been supplying parts to domestic and overseas automakers. It will introduce a next-generation hotstamping line that combines a large press with an environmentally friendly electric furnace to avoid burning gas to heat the blanks. This will enable Gestamp Hotstamping Japan to supply automotive manufacturers with one-piece, high-strength, lightweight parts, contributing to the Japanese automotive industry's accelerating shift to EVs.

## Chassis

Ulf Sudowe, R&D Chassis Products Director, Gestamp, commented: “Front and rear axles are complex and load-bearing components that are in the lower part of the vehicle. They carry the engine connection and are subjected to high loads and forces, while playing an important role in the safety of the vehicle.

That is why it is so important to develop different ideas and solutions for modern and future-oriented components that meet current and future e-mobility requirements in the chassis."

Gestamp delivers fully validated chassis components, from research into advanced technologies, first concepts with design and development and prototyping to complex component testing. The fully integrated production processes in the concept phase include flexible platform solutions for different vehicle powertrains (electric vehicles, plugin hybrid vehicles and internal combustion engines), with minimum weight and low CO₂ impact.

The introduction of "HyperTubes" (highstrength, complex-phase steel tubes) in the development of rear axle supports has resulted in an improvement, especially in electric vehicles, by increasing crash performance while reducing weight.

## Mechanisms

Gestamp's third business unit, under the brand name Edscha, presented the second generation of its Power Door to the public for the first time at IZB. The actuator is now in the door check area and Edscha offers a comprehensive system around the power door, including intelligent sensor technology that detects obstacles in the vicinity of the door and can stop the door in time before a collision.

It also supplies the control unit (ECU) which is used to control the actuator through specially developed software and is the link to the vehicle environment monitoring system, which also detects static and dynamic obstacles such as pedestrians and cyclists.

The second generation Power Door also has a servo function. This means that even heavy doors can be moved manually with virtually no effort (no matter the position of the car). The power door is also stepless, meaning it has no stop notches but keeps the door firmly in place in any position.

"With the Active Frunk," Edscha CEO, César Pontvianne de la Maza, explained, “we offer a modern solution specially tailored to electric vehicles. Where there is still a combustion engine in most vehicles today, space is freed in the electric vehicle that can be used as an additional luggage compartment, for example." The Active Frunk combines an actuator for fully automatic opening and closing of the front lid with a hinge for active pedestrian protection.
