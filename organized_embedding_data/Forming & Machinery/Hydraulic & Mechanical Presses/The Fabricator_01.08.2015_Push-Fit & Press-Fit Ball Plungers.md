For use in vertical and overhead applications. The unique o-ring design holds the plunger in place and can be easily inserted by hand or assembly tool. Body, spring and ball are stainless steel. The o-ring is Nitrile butadiene rubber (NBR).

Plastic or Stainless Steel Body With Stainless Steel or Plastic Ball

Small and lightweight for use in sensitive and light duty applications. Available with black thermoplastic or stainless steel bodies. The spring and ball are stainless steel or white plastic.

Shoulderless Knurled Press-Fit Steel and Stainless Steel Bodies Stainless Steel and Nylon Balls

These plungers are designed for simple, easy installation into wood, soft plastic or aluminum. Ideal where a threaded plunger is impractical or when threaded adjustments are not required. Nylon ball resists marring. Designed without a shoulder for insertion from either end.
