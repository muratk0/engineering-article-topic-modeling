# Crane Master Store

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

POLISH MANUFACTURER, PoWer-Tech, has focused on full automation of the production process, integrating its Eagle iNspire or Eagle eVision laser cutting system, Crane Master for loading and unloading and the sheet metal storage system, TwinTower, into one system.

“Users of this type of integrated Crane Master Store system can produce more parts in a shorter time, significantly increasing productivity and production capacity," Power-Tech explained. “It also saves on space and operator/ management time."

The TwinTower storage, from a selected shelf, loads the materials. The Crane Master receives the metal sheet from the storage system, transports and loads them onto the laser cutter's removable table. After cutting the pieces, Crane Master receives the processed material from the table and transports it onto the unloading point, from where it later be delivered to the end-user or transported to the warehouse.
