# Portable dust extraction solution

[TARİH: 01.03.2021 ISMR]

Chicago Pneumatic has introduced a new all-in-one dust extraction solution designed to make metalworking applications safer and more efficient.

"The compact and portable solution, comprising a powerful CPA5000 pneumatic vacuum cleaner and a lightweight shroud, is designed for use with traditional pneumatic hand tools to enable dust-free drilling, grinding and sanding. The solution is suitable for a wide range of operating environments, including hard-to-reach locations in shipyard, aerospace, rolling stock, machinery and renewables applications," the manufacturer told ISMR.

"Metal dust poses a serious risk to workers' health, if inhaled. It can also put the surrounding facility and machinery at risk, as it is ten times more combustible than wood dust," added Damien Feller, Global Product Marketing Manager, Spares & Solutions, at Chicago Pneumatic. "That is why it is vital to ensure effective mobile dust extraction is successful, especially in applications where no central vacuum is available. We want to help managers address these issues while maintaining productivity. The effective removal of dust particles protects workers' health and helps the workplace comply with clean air regulation. With less dust impairing visibility and no need to pause work for cleaning, workers can turn their full attention to delivering the best possible results."

Designed for ease of use, the vacuum cleaner weighs 1.3kg. Its lightweight and compact size makes it easy for workers to carry with them to the next job. The shrouds are light yet durable, and simple to adapt to existing hand tools. They feature clear windows to enable 225-degree visibility, allowing workers to monitor the progress of the task at all times.

"The same airline is used to power both the hand tool and the vacuum cleaner, simplifying use. The hose features a one-inch interface to optimise flow for effective and quick dust extraction, delivered with high efficiency, using cyclonic action. To increase safety, the solution features a HEPA filter, which can capture very small dust particles," explained Chicago Pneumatic.

The new solution extends Chicago Pneumatic's existing portfolio of dust extraction accessories. The range includes a selection of accessories for angle sanders and angle grinders; a dust extraction hose, a bag and a complete dust collection system for self-vacuum sanders as well as a dust extraction hose to connect central vacuum sanders to an external central vacuum.
