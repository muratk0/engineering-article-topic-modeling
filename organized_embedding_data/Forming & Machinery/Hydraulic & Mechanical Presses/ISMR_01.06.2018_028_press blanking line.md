# Press blanking line

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

SHUNG DAR INDUSTRIAL CO. Ltd's press blanking line has been designed to meet the stringent requirements of the automotive and fabricating industries, including suppliers of surface-exposed panels and other auxiliary sectors.

"Our press blanking line, providing a total processing solution for steel coil blanking and blank-stacking operations, features a Siemens servo control drive system and a modular design. This enables highly automated production, including automatic coil loading, threading and stacking. Production line parameters are database-controlled and mechanical action adjustments can be made whilst downloading data and confirmation, providing greater operator safety and operational efficiency," the Taiwanese manufacturer told ISMR.

Multiple coil carts, and a dual stacking system, make zero machine downtime possible. In addition, lines using this technology are equipped to process wide varieties of material, such as aluminium or high-strength steel. Even surface-sensitive material can be processed safely on the same line at the highest production rates, explained Shung Dar Industrial Co. Ltd.

With its R&D technology and expertise in automatic stamping and feeding equipment, Shung Dar has made its mark on blank stamping lines in Taiwan.
