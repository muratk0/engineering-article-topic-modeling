# On the surface

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

DANISH MANUFACTURER, FLADDER DANMARK A/S, will be focusing on its FLADDER GYRO deburring machine, now available with accessories that reduce power costs and machine wear, at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover.

"All motors and the vacuum system are regulated by frequency converters. An energy-saving system shuts down the machine automatically, in three steps, when the machine is no longer needed. The first step is a standby function, where new workpieces wait a certain amount of time. If no further workpieces are placed on the conveyor belt, the machine switches off step by step. The second step switches off the vacuum system and the third step handles the extraction system," explained the company.
