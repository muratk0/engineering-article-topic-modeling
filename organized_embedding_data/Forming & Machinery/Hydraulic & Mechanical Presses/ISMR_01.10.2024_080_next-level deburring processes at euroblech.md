# Next-level deburring processes at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Dutch surface finishing specialist, Timesavers International BV, is placing a spotlight on increased efficiency and productivity for its deburring machines on its stand L41 in Hall 14 at EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany (22-25 October 2024).

Visitors to Timesavers' stand at EuroBLECH will see an intelligent and operator-free solution: an automated deburring process that employs universal techniques, vision technology and smart features. This machine adapts to the correct parameters automatically, removing the need for manual intervention or an operator, making it highly flexible for most environments.

The improved 42 RB series deburring system.

Its improved 42 RB series machine features the newly designed Top Brush. It can automatically adjust the grinding belt and brushes without operator intervention and is compatible with Level 2 and ERP systems, making it a step towards the factory of the future.

The 10 series manual grinder.

"Our popular 32 RB series with four large rotary brushes will also be on display. This grinding machine is suitable for deburring, edge rounding up to a 2mm radius, laser oxide removal and finishing. Last, but not least, we will present our 10 series manual grinder which deburrs three to four times faster than a standard angle grinder," it continued.

Timesavers also offers training, maintenance and broad application knowledge to its customers. The company specialises in deburring, edge rounding, finishing and precision grinding systems.
