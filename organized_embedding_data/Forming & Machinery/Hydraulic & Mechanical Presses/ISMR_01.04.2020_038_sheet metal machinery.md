# Sheet metal machinery

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

MORGAN RUSHWORTH is celebrating its 148th year in business with the launch of an updated range of sheet metal machinery.

"We believe that embracing new technology, while retaining traditional robust structural designs, results in high quality and efficient machinery for today's production environment," it told ISMR.

2020 will see the UK launch of both new and refreshed models including its FR Compact and XR production fibre lasercutting systems, a range of XPE servoelectric press brakes and the EXR Multi-Bend CNC folders.

"The compact lasers are suitable for R&D work or for starting to bring jobs in-house, while the production models are able to meet the most demanding applications. Our servo-electric press brake greatly reduces energy consumption and increases cycle times, along with almost noiseless operation. The multi-bend CNC folders offer programming direct from 3D drawing files with even complex forms produced without a tool change," explained the company.

"Our popular ACP high-definition plasma range now features the latest Hypertherm XPR technology. Our PBXR press brake builds on the success of our PBXS model with a height-adjustable control and high approach and return speeds."
