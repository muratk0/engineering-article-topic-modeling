# 800-ton Triton servo press for Fulterer AG

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Pull-out systems from Fulterer AG are used, among other things, for cabinet drawers in the catering industry, commercial vehicles and various other businesses. The manufacturer, which has production sites in Lustenau near Bregenz in Austria and in neighbouring St. Margrethen in Switzerland, has ordered an 800-ton Triton servo press from Schuler for the first time. The system has an electronic, highly dynamic, three-axis transfer system as well as the Ecoform package and several digital solutions.

“For example, the DigiSim simulation software enables a virtual clearance analysis and stroke rate optimisation, even before the press goes into operation. The scope of supply also includes machine apps for production data and press force evaluation as well as for the analysis of the drive system, cooling and lubrication circuit. The Ecoform package ensures a sustainable and efficient forming process that conserves resources, reduces energy costs and significantly minimises the CO₂ footprint," outlined press specialist, Schuler.

Schuler first presented the modular Triton press concept to the public at the Blechexpo 2021 trade fair in Germany. The machines, available with mechanical or servo drive, already feature the prerequisites for digital solutions. Many Triton systems also use the same components, increasing the availability of spare parts.

Schuler is supplying the 800-ton servo press for Fulterer in tie-rod design with a 4.60m-long and 2m-wide table for production of the pull-out systems, which offer a load capacity of 600kg. Fulterer employs 250 people at eight locations in six countries and is family-owned. The company was founded in 1956 by Dr. Karl Fulterer. Since 2019, the company has also been represented in China and Mexico and, since 2022, in Brazil.

Schuler offers customised technology in all areas of forming, from the networked press to press shop planning. Its products also include automation, dies, process expertise and service for the metalworking industry. The company is part of the international technology group, ANDRITZ.■
