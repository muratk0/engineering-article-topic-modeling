# The HST press series

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

The HST series of straightside highspeed precision presses is equipped with an intuitive graphical colour human-machine interface (HMI) and an intelligent protection system. AIoT intelligent networking enhances overall production process data management and analysis, improving humanmachine interaction modes. The HST series also employs multiple safety loop protection devices, using visual monitoring systems and sensors to monitor the production environment in real-time. Abnormalities trigger immediate alarms to protect operators' safety, ensure product stability and increase production efficiency.

The multi-point plunger guide design significantly reduces eccentric load and deflection during multi-station continuous stamping, increasing product precision. The frame is entirely made of FCD ductile cast iron, absorbing high-speed stamping vibrations. It undergoes annealing treatment to eliminate internal stresses, ensuring structural stability. The HST series features eight-sided, highhardness bearing steel guide plates, roller bearings in the slide guide paths and extended bearing support distances, ensuring stability during high-speed reciprocating movements, surpassing JIS Super Grade static and dynamic precision.

The machine structure design is optimised using “Finite Element Analysis, complemented by a constant temperature lubrication cooling system to reduce the impact of thermal deformation on equipment precision. This ensures the rigidity required for the stamping process and meets the forming characteristics of special materials. The HST series is suitable for thinner silicon steel stator and rotor products, enhancing new energy motor performance and reducing eddy current losses.
