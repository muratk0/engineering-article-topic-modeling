# AN EYE ON SIMPLICITY

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

TRANSFLUID SPECIALISTS have developed an economical system for the automatic production of closed tube frames with up to six bends. The frame ends are reduced and merged during the bending process. The solution includes an additional pressing device in which the fabricated tube frames are inserted automatically.

“The challenge was to create an optimal process for our customer, which ensured efficient and rapid results, with a cycle time of just 15 seconds,” explained Gerd Nöker, managing director of Transfluid. The entire system, says the manufacturer, can be operated for up to one hour without the need for personnel.

The solution has a tube bunker that can accommodate 240 tubes with a diameter of 30mm. After the separation process, lengths are re-measured and one tube end is reduced automatically. The bending process is on a Transfluid double-head bending machine, which is equipped with two axially servo-electric positioned heads. Finally, automatic removal and feeding into the pressing device takes place. Various embossing punches fix the frame. ■
