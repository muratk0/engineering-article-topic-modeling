# Energy-efficient press brake control

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Bosch Rexroth aims to increase the costeffectiveness of press brakes, for sheetmetal bending operations, with two new solutions for down-stroking drives.

"Manufacturers can benefit from an innovative valve control or an extremely energy-efficient pump control, depending upon their requirements. Both drive packages are characterised by high energy efficiency and control quality for repeatable processes. We can also assist with selecting the best solution for the respective application using the latest simulation tools," it told /SMR.

Various requirements are placed on pressbrake drives for sheet metal processing. Manufacturers are increasingly paying more attention to energy efficiency and carbon footprint when selecting the type of drive. Bosch Rexroth now offers two new solutions for valve-controlled and pump-controlled drives.

## Pump-controlled drive

"Our new complete solution for a pumpcontrolled drive offers high energy efficiency with minimal operating costs, especially for press brakes with high utilisation rates up to 24/7 operation. The control unit is a complete power unit supplied ready for installation, which combines a highly dynamic servo motor and a four-quadrant axial piston pump. A safety block with end position-monitored on/off valves is also part of the package," it explained.

Above: Pump-controlled or valve-controlled? With two new drive solutions from Bosch Rexroth, energy-efficient press brakes can be designed and modernised for different applications.

The motor-pump unit, which can be managed using a servo controller, supplies the connected drive cylinder with the required hydraulic power at every operating point. The upper piston can be moved, in an energyefficient manner, using the motor speed and torque control outputs.

"Comparisons using simulation technology show that, compared to conventional drives with throttle control, the new solution offers high potential for saving energy. On the one hand, the intelligent power-on-demand drive achieves a higher level of efficiency with lower system power while, on the other hand, exact metering reduces the total amount of hydraulic oil required," continued the manufacturer.

## Valve-controlled drive

To optimise valve-controlled drives, Bosch Rexroth is expanding its portfolio of components for press brakes with a 3D cylinder block.

"Using a new manufacturing process that allows virtually unlimited shaping based on a 3D-printed sand core, the pre-fill valve that was previously mounted on the block is now integrated into the cylinder block. This delivers considerable benefits in terms of energy efficiency and installation space, as well as a reduction in variants. Compared to conventionally manufactured cylinder blocks, the new manufacturing process results in a weight saving of around 35 per cent," confirmed Bosch Rexroth.

The two new solutions for valve-controlled and pump-controlled drives operate at a maximum operating pressure of 350 bar with standard pressing forces of up to 3,200 t (valve-controlled) and 2,500 t (pumpcontrolled). Higher pressing forces are possible. Both variants are also suitable for technical retrofitting.■
