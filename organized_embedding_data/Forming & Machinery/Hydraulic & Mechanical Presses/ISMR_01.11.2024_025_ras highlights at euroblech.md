# RAS highlights at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Highlights on the RAS booth at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover this October were the XLTbend with Tool Support, MEGAbend with MetalLift for heavy parts and the introduction of the new MinibendCenter 2.

The optional Tool Support system, available for both the RAS XLTbend and RAS MEGAbend machines, significantly simplifies the handling of upper beam tools used in the production of large box heights. It enables “effortless” handling of upper beam tools up to 300mm (XLTbend) and 400mm (MEGAbend), designed to accommodate large box heights. This system enables tools to be moved to the desired position or stored in the machine's integrated tool magazine.

“It allows operators to move large and heavy tools without physical effort. By minimizing the required effort, the operator's workload is significantly reduced, enhancing overall comfort. In addition, this reduces fatigue and importantly lowers the risk of injury from repetitive heavy lifting,” said RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH.

The XLTbend is suitable for forming complex precision sheet metal parts such as cassettes, panels and boxes (even in small batches). It is a flexible UpDown folding machine, featuring Bendex software for automatic programming of the bending sequence, front and rear operating options; ViN laser loading assistance and a hybrid gauging system.

The newly introduced RAS MetalLift expands the ergonomic benefits of the MEGAbend and allows the handling of heavy bent parts in up to 6mm mild steel. The innovative lifting system is designed to make it easy to load and unload heavy and large blanks without physical effort. In addition to the automatic bending process on the MEGAbend, the MetalLift relieves the operator during loading and unloading by fully supporting the weight of the workpieces.
