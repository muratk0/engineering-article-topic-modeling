# THE HEART OF THE MATTER

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

FLADDER DANMARK A/S is upgrading the ‘heart’ of its deburring machines to increase capacity by more than 40% as part of a process of continuous improvement and development. The development includes not only the mechanical parts and functionality, but also the tools – the efficient abrasive cylindrical brushes.

This recent development concerns the very ‘heart’ of the machines - the strong, compact gear head with a total of 6 spindles, mounted in counter-rotating pairs. During the process, the entire head and 6 spindles rotate and oscillate across the surface of the parts so that workpieces are continuously ”attacked” from every possible direction during the deburring process. This ensures a uniform finish of edges and surfaces – on straight edges, inside or outside edges, curves, circular or square holes etc. Fladder Danmark A/S has changed the embedding of the bearings and the spindle construction so that its new Ø400 mm abrasive cylinders can now be used. The most commonly used abrasive cylinders are Ø300mm respectively Ø350 mm, but the change to Ø400 offers significant benefits, the company told ISMR.

“New abrasive cylinders contain 22m2 abrasive material i.e. 42% increased capacity,” it explained. “The most common tool used by our customers is the current Ø300mm abrasive cylinder which contains 15.5m2 abrasive material. With 22m2 in the new Ø400 mm cylinder, it means an increase in capacity of more than 40%.”

Further capacity can be offered by changing the density of the abrasive cylinder. The new abrasive cylinders are mounted on Fladder’s new LOCK-IT spindle system which enables users to move to the next (Ø200) spindle size. The spindles are mounted on a cone which – together with a modification of the bedding of the bearings – makes it possible to use the Ø200mm LOCK-IT spindles without the risk of harmful vibrations. The new abrasive cylinders occupy most of the space inside the machine.

One important parameter in the development process was to keep the outside dimension of the machine so as not to use more floor space than necessary.

“The new compact design of the heart has given us the opportunity to increase the movement across the surface of the workpieces, resulting in even more uniform quality of the workpieces. The new ‘heart’ is being offered to our old customers. The possibility of implementing this new technology into old machines means that customers who, for example, have a 10-year old machine may now upgrade their machines to the latest technology at a reasonable cost,” the company added. www.fladder.com
