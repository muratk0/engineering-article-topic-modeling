[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. At EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover last October, it launched three new press brakes.

"Our green FX Servo press brake uses electric technology – there are no hydraulics. Users can dramatically improve their energy efficiency and make massive power savings with the press brake, which can be used with standard controllers and is very user-friendly for press brake operators. It has been designed for more uptime, less maintenance and a special focus on low energy consumption (approximately 50% lower compared to hydraulic press brakes). More accurate bending results can also be achieved due to equal bending force along the ram," Ermaksan told ISMR.

The FX servo green press brake utilises servo motors and has a number of interesting features, including a 4-axis back gauge. Full-length bends can be made between side columns, with no obstructions, and the machine is almost silent in operation. Sliding front support arms assist the operator for easier material handling.

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the Evolution II (hybrid), Speed Bend Pro, the Power Bend Pro, the Power-Bend Falcon, the Micro-Bend, the Eco-Bend Expert and the Heavy-Duty press brake (featuring a hydrodynamic hybrid crowning system, 6-axis servomotor back-gauge system and special top and bottom tools).

Italian company F.lli Vimercati has developed e.TERNA, a hybrid bending system. Energy savings of 30-40% and 30% decreases in machine cycle times, it says, are key results. This system can be applied to all the bending models in its range, without limits in stroke, power or dimensions.

"The BMS Multi-Touch Control features a multi-touch screen, rather like those you see on an iPad. Using one finger on the screen, you can make profiles larger or smaller and angles wider or shorter in preparation for the bending process. Users can open existing profiles with one click. In Europe, there are fewer skilled workers now so it is important that the folder is easy to operate. The software is easy to use and operate, even for someone who has never used a folder before or bent a metal sheet," explained Mario Traub, who handles the marketing for Biegemaster.

Other user-friendly and time-saving features have been designed into the software, such as a tapered back-gauge feature with a 'stick-together' function. This is helpful for operators who are bending, for example, several 4m sheets and wish to process them together for faster installation, rather than bending them one by one. One side of the sheet is slightly smaller and fits into the wider side of the other profile, for significant time savings.

"A new laser-pointing solution for taper bending (which highlights the point where the sheet must be inserted) is another innovation. Our engineers devised this solution to ensure the accuracy of the process – users can see video of our double-folder with tapered back gauge on our YouTube channel (www.youtube.com/channel/UCmUvA_ZZY3Y7aeEEtE76t5Q). There is also a loading position tool on the folder, to help load sheet into the folder. It might need two or three people, depending upon the length and weight of the sheet, to manually load material into the folder. With this solution, the bending beam rises to a 90-degree angle (like a little table in front of the folder) so that it is easier to load the sheet. It is a simple innovation but it makes an operator's work much easier," added Traub.

## Wilson Tool International

(www.wilsontool.com), the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, offers easier part removal in press brake operations with swing ear sets.

"Suitable for bending boxes with four sides and return flanges, swing ears are available in most profiles for American, European and WT style punches. With the addition of swing ears, forcing the punch out of the box when making the final bend is no longer necessary, because the ears simply swing out of the way," said the manufacturer.

It also introduced its Express Crowning deflection control system to help control deflection, or "the canoe effect", in pressbrake applications over four feet long. Part of the company's line of bending solutions, the new Express Crowning system gives fabricators the flexibility to fine-tune the deflection-compensating curve of the unit to overcome the nuances built into the machine. Press brake operators can now crown the lower beam with manual or electric options and secure dies with manual or hydraulic power.

Wilson's ExpressRail™ 2000 hydraulic clamping system reduces set-up time with a wireless pendant control that clamps and seats press brake tooling in seconds, giving fabricators more open height and extended clamp life. It is compatible with existing WT and American style press brakes.
