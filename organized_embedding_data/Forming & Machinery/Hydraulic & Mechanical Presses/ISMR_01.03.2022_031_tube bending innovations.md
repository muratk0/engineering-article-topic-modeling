# Tube bending innovations

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

Unison Ltd, a manufacturer of all-electric CNC tube bending machines, will be presenting several new technologies at the forthcoming MACH 2022 exhibition in the UK (4-8 April 2022).

“These systems will help manufacturers and subcontractors to accelerate their efficiency and productivity as demand returns to prepandemic levels," it told ISMR.

On its stand 630, Hall 6, at the show will be an all-electric Unison Breeze 16mm (maximum tube diameter) multi-stack tubebending machine. It will be equipped with the company's newly upgraded Unibend control, an operating system providing speed enhancements in the region of 25% when bending at full power.

It will also showcase a Unison EvBend 1000, developed to reduce the entry cost to precision rotary draw tube bending by combining manually operated feed, tube rotation and bending force. It features CNC-controlled braking on the carriage feed, rotation and bend arm axes. Suitable for prototypes and one offs, the EvBend 1000 is widely used in the Formula One and aerospace industries.

Visitors to the show can also be updated on Unison's newly launched range of Synergy HBM (hybrid, multi-stack) range of machines. Unison Ltd offers more than 30 all-electric tube and pipe bending machines for diameters ranging from 4mm (5/32") to 275mm (10" schedule pipe).

“Each model delivers rapid set-up; fast tool changes; exceptional power; rigid mechanical design and all-electric control for right-firsttime repeat subcontract work, or immediately after producing a single trial part. These are all attributes that we believe make our Breeze models the ultimate tube manipulation machines for businesses specialising in small batch production runs," explained the tube bending specialist.

Above: Electric Unison Breeze 16mm (maximum tube diameter) multi-stack tube bending machine.

Available in 50mm and 80mm versions from launch, Unison's new Synergy hybrid range combines electric and hydraulic operation. As with Breeze models, the bend arm, carriage, plane of bend and carriage side shift are servo-driven, while mandrel, pressure die and clamping system are hydraulically controlled. This, said the manufacturer, makes Synergy machines suitable for high-volume, repetitive tube manipulation operations that do not require rapid set-up times and all-electric control provided by Breeze machines.
