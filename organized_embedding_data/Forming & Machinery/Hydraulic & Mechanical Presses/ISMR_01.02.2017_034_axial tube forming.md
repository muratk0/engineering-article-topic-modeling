# Axial tube FORMING

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

TRANSFLUID MASCHINENBAU GMBH has developed a solution incorporating automatic tool detection for tube forming. Its new axial tube forming machine, REB 645, enables tube end-forming with rapid tool changes for complex tube geometries and extreme degrees of forming.

The REB 645 enables axial forming of tubes with diameters from 6 to 45mm. Using a servomotor, the tool sequence is positioned horizontally and vertically. In addition, rolling stations for specific forming processes can be integrated. Upon request, the machine can be equipped with electric or hydraulic numeric drives.

For extremely short cycle times, these forming procedures can also be carried out gradually in transfer systems. See www.tube-processing-machines.com
