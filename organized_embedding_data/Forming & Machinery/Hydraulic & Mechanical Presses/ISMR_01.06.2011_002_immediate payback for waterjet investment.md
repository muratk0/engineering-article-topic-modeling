# IMMEDIATE PAYBACK FOR WATERJET INVESTMENT

[TARİH: 01.06.2011 ISMR]

Wightman Stewart has supplied a turnkey waterjet cutting system to CML Alloys. It includes an AccuStream AS Series intensifier, Dialine diamond cutting heads and a WARDJet waterjet cutting table with a twin-screen CNC controller.

The decision to invest in the latest waterjet cutting technology enables CML Alloys to cut nickel alloys and titanium with a high degree of accuracy and minimal waste of material. The company has also improved lead times and extended its range of services using the water jet cutting process to provide fully machined parts to exacting customer specifications.

The AccuStream AS 60 100hp intensifier sits adjacent to the WARDJet ZX-2543 waterjet cutting table. Its build quality, cost of ownership, PLC-based diagnostic control and twin intensifier design were important considerations in its specification. AIT (Advanced Intensifier Technology) ensures easy simple maintenance, prolonged component life and high levels of reliability.

The WARDJet ZX-2543 has been custom designed with a large 2.5 x 4.3-metre cutting table to accommodate large sheets of titanium. Whilst most of the titanium cutting involves straight-line cutting, the 2.5-metre deep table gives CML Alloys the facility to cut components such as large dished heads. The AccuStream Dialine cutting heads feature diamonds that increase machine uptime with an estimated life cycle of 1000 hours. The two independent Z axes incorporate height sensors which are important when cutting expensive materials.

CML Alloys is cutting titanium sheet to finished tolerances of (+0.0/-0.2mm). The WARDJet machine is laser mapped and ball-barred to comfortably achieve this.
