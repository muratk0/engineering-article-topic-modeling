# KEEPING CONNECTED

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

MVD Makina Sanayi AS has Industry 4.0 in its sights as it gears up to meet its customers' future needs for connectivity.

MVD Makina Sanayi AS has been manufacturing machines since 1950 and sheet metal processing machine tools, such as press brakes, shears, punching, plasma and laser cutting systems, since 1971. In 1994, it told ISMR, it produced the first tandem press brake in Turkey.

The company has been CE-compliant since 2001 and is ISO 9001-certified. It now exports to over 80 countries and aims to be one of the largest manufacturers of sheet metal machine tools in the European market.

ISMR spoke with MVD's sales manager, Taner Ercal, on the company's booth at this November's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany, to discover its latest initiatives.

## Fibre laser

MVD showcased a 2kW iLaser fibre laser cutting system, with exchange table, on its booth at Blechexpo. ISMR also watched a demonstration of its 2m x 6m iPlasma plasma cutting machine with XPR 300 generators and oxycutting head.

The iLaser featured an IPG laser source, a Precitec Procutter laser cutting head, wide touchscreen, loading/unloading table, camera monitoring system for operators, dust collection filter and advanced CNC controller. Features of the CNC control panel include Fly-Cut functions for same direction high-speed cutting and 'No Piercing' technology for thin material processing. Enhanced sheet metal recognition software, referencing and sheet alignment functions and a workpiece job order search function/recall are also included.

MVD showcased a 2kW iLaser fibre laser cutting system, with exchange table, on its booth at Blechexpo

MVD presented its first laser cutting and plasma cutting systems to the market in 2011. The iPlasma cutting system on its booth at Blechexpo featured servo motors, a Hypertherm cutting head and source as well as a dust collector filter system and oxycutting head. The system can also be equipped with a 5-axis bevelling head, multi-plasma heads and pipe cutting functionality. Automatic torch height control continuously monitors the surface of the sheet so that torch height is kept at the required level. The maximum thickness of sheet metal that can be processed is up to 80mm mild steel on MVD's plasma cutting systems.

"A key highlight for us is the addition of servo electric drives to our press brake range – they offer more control, and higher energy savings to our customers. On the laser side, we are also promoting new loading/unloading systems for our laser cutting systems which should be ready next year for launch," MVD's sales manager, Taner Ercal, told ISMR.

The move to servo-electric drives has characterised the company's press brake development. Its range of iBend hydraulic press brakes sport numerous features, such as large throat depth and stroke, a height-adjustable control panel and automatic height-adjusted front support system. The company started hybrid press brake manufacturing in 2015 and launched its first servo press brakes in 2018.

"We are currently promoting two types of servo-electric press brake – that is our main bending focus," added Taner Ercal. "On press brakes, customers want to improve the speed and cycle of production – servo drives help them to achieve this. The main benefits, of course, are the energy savings and repetitional accuracy that they bring."

In combination with this, MVD has also investigated reducing the level of noise as well as fume/dust from its machines. Both the laser and plasma cutting systems on its booth at Blechexpo boasted dust collection features.

## Industry 4.0 and automation

Industry 4.0 is also a key area for the company. It is discussing, with customers and software suppliers, how best to meet the future needs of its users for connectivity. It is already offering more software options on its machines and has been developing robotic applications on its laser and bending machines, with programmed production.

"We have looked at our customers' Industry-4.0 requirements and introduced robotic systems with our press brakes. We intend to increase the number of robotic bending systems in the future to meet our customers' requirements. This eliminates operators from the process and is suitable for specific markets where labour is expensive and the speed/efficiency of production is important. We see Industry 4.0 as a major trend – linking press brakes and laser cutting systems together, for example," explained Taner Ercal.

"We also receive enquiries for large tonnage press brakes, as well as tandem press brakes, but the main enquiries we receive from customers are for 4.0 connectivity. Speed and increased productivity and efficiency of production are the main issues for customers. Our laser customers are now cutting different types of materials, including titanium, so our machines are geared up for these new requirements. We are able to supply laser cutting systems from 2kW up to 10kW."

MVD incorporates high-end European components, that are manufactured in Germany, Italy, Switzerland and the Netherlands, in its machines. All machines are designed and analysed with 3D-supported computer-aided programs.

## Growth prospects

"In 2019, we increased our sales and turnover with a double-digit increase, compared to 2018. The growth was generated by orders from recently established western European markets as well as new additions to our Asian markets and from the Russian market. Europe remains our largest market but, this year, we have been trying to open new channels in Asian markets such as Indonesia, Thailand and South Korea. We already have established distributors in those markets," Ercal told ISMR.

In terms of strategic objectives, MVD intends to increase its market share across these sectors worldwide.

"We are looking for new distributors in some Asian countries but our main challenge, rather than finding new distributors, is to increase the number of machines sold by existing distributors. Our factories are all in Turkey but we only have one overseas office in the USA (Chicago). We have grown our presence in North American markets. We have also seen growth this year in markets across Europe and Asia, but the Asian market has been particularly strong for us," continued Ercal.

Machine users can meet MVD at the Riga (Latvia) exhibition. The company also exhibited at FABTECH in Chicago, showcasing its fibre laser and press brake technologies. It will also be attending the SteelFab exhibition in Dubai, where it is searching for a new distributor.
