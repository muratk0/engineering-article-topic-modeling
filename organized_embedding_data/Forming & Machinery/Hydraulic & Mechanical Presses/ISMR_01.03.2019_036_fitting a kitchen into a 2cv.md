# Fitting a kitchen into a 2CV...

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

WHEN ITALIAN AUTOMOBILE designer, Flaminio Bertoni, first conceived the Citroen 2CV (or, in French Deux Chevaux – which literally means "two horses") in 1936, it was to provide some relief from the heavy workload of French farmers. It is doubtful whether he ever conceived the vehicle being re-engineered as the world's smallest food truck. Yet, at UK-based manufacturer Parry Stainless Steel Products, that is precisely what has been created…

Affectionately nicknamed Dolly, and the brainchild of "Super Chef" Andrew Mellon, the 2CV has been painstakingly converted from a basic passenger car to a mobile catering unit, fully fabricated by Parry in stainless steel and fitted at its Draycot factory. Andrew chose to work with Parry because he wanted the vehicle equipment fabricated, manufactured and assembled in the UK and had used Parry's kitchen equipment for many years. Dolly was born!

The first concept drawings were created by project designers, GEC Anderson. 3M also joined the project, agreeing to wrap the vehicle once the engineering process had been completed, ready for Dolly's public debut. The bespoke installation, all in fabricated stainless steel, includes a double sink from which Dolly produces her own hot water (to comply with health and safety standards), a set of four drawers to hold produce, with a pull-out work surface above on which to prep food.

A large drawer to the rear holds cooking equipment including an 1860 single fryer, a CHU single hob with a 3022 heated base, PGF800 gas griddle and an AG2H two ring gas burner. A further drawer holds cooking utensils and condiments and there is even a cocktail stand which can sit at the rear of the units or stand alone. Atop is a bespoke one-piece cover which sits over all the fabrication to provide a further stainless steel worktop.

"The initial fabrications were largely 'experimental' as we had no real reference point for a project of this nature. After all, it had never been done before! Our team really relished the opportunity to engineer such a unique and special assignment, which now stands as a testament to its, and the company's, skill and expertise...," said Parry.

One of the main priorities of Dolly's design brief was to create a compact catering vehicle that minimised food waste. Parry's carbon neutral operation helped contribute to the green credentials of the overall endeavour. More than creating simply a small catering van, the team had a genuine challenge to engineer the sheer volume of equipment into Dolly's petite proportions and "unique" contours.

Dolly was earmarked as one of only three headline vehicles at the 2018 London Motor Show and was unveiled at the Bicester Heritage site in May 2018. Further appearances were scheduled for Dolly, including live cooking displays, as she embarked upon her first British tour.
