# Prima Power to launch new line at Blechexpo

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

How can industrial manufacturers level up their production processes in a scenario that calls for continuous dynamism to combine high production rates and flexible use? Prima Power offers answers to this question at the 16th Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metalworking in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023), where it will showcase its modular and scalable integrated solutions.

Prima Power will be demonstrating how its targeted solutions level up customers' production processes, combining multiple work stages as one and handling a wide material range to automatic, finished bent products. Show visitors can find its booth 3201 in Hall 3 at Blechexpo.

## Launch of new line

As a world premiere, it will present a manufacturing line that features a combined punching and laser-cutting machine (Combi Genius) with an LSR loading, unloading and stacking robot; a servo-electric press brake (eP Genius 1030) with integrated tool-change storage and a seven-axis industrial robot.

"With its profitable combination of productivity and flexibility, this system is a perfect solution for a wide range of materials and complex product manufacturing. Designed to be an efficient process solver and challenge-ready manufacturing line, it handles the tighter bends, the thicker materials and the most complex assemblies.

These features make it the perfect tool for several industries, such as machinery and yellow goods," stated Prima Power.

Visitors to Blechexpo will also discover the Prima Power software ecosystem "as if they were in a real factory," said the manufacturer.

"They will experience the highly efficient seamless flow from ERP order to machine production in a few clicks; the complete overview; easy and clear production planning, control and diagnostics; and the faster reaction to production changes," it added.

## Punching and laser cutting

The line on display on the Prima Power stand at Blechexpo will rely on the next-level Combi Genius for combined punching and laser cutting. This advanced model features a larger combined working area, better cutting performance via 6kW laser power with new cutting head and efficient gas mixer, and improved robot performance.

“The resulting main benefits are a strong reduction of cycle times (thanks to a 25% cutting speed increase), a 25% saving in gas consumption and huge overall improvement in the cutting quality of highly sensitive materials such as aluminium. The enhanced performance of the Combi Genius adds to the flexibility of this automatic multi-purpose solution, which integrates different working stages to speed up production and reduce manufacturing costs," outlined Prima Power.

The LSR loading, unloading and stacking robot loads sheets onto the machine and stacks finished workpieces onto stacking pallets.

## Bending in focus

After the parts are punched and cut with the Combi Genius and handled by LSR, they can be bent by the eP Genius 1030 pressbrake with an integrated seven-axis industrial robot. The eP1030 servo-electric press brake comes with automatic tool-change storage that can hold 28 metres of upper and lower bending tools (dies and punches) on seven racks. This press brake eliminates the need for manual machine set-up.

"The real innovation of the eP Genius to be presented at Blechexpo is the addition of the anthropomorphic robot for loading and unloading operations. This makes it possible to work completely unmanned, freeing the operator's time for value-added activities such as preparing and testing production programs," explained the manufacturer.

In direct mode, the robot picks up the punched/cut parts released by the LSR of the Combi Genius on a roller table, places them onto the press brake to be bent and unloads them on pallets positioned on the floor. The robot can also work in a temporary buffer mode i.e. in a continuous process with a temporary waiting condition.

The robotic cell is configured with a Sheet Reverser, which allows the robot to turn over the part to be bent or the component before stacking it, whenever this is required by the operating program. Gripper storage can accommodate three different grippers which are automatically stored by the robot when they are not in use.
