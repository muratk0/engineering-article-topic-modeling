# An eye on tube

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

MANUFACTURING SOPHISTICATED CUSTOMER-SPECIFIC pipework in large quantities at competitive prices represents a serious challenge. Intelligent manufacturing solutions are required for the economic fabrication of complex tube and pipe geometries. The modular tube manufacturing system from the PIPE BENDING SYSTEMS (PBS) division of TRACTO-TECHNIK supports the workflow of tubes and pipes in an efficient and flexible manner with individual system modules - bending machine, measuring technique and software - working together.

Dr.-Ing. Christan Gerlach, head of the PBS business area, explained: "There are several examples of our high-quality tube production for machines for special applications. Rarely does a tube geometry appear twice, most of them are unique, one-of-a-kind products. Today, one of the most important manufacturers of food processing lines worldwide produces tubes for the daily assembly of his equipment almost on a Just In Time basis (during the year, in one single plant, several hundred kilometres of tubes are installed). Of course, each bend or elbow must be suitable."

System workflow is managed by a software module named PIPEFAB, starting from the supply of pipe, followed by machining operations – marking, sawing, bending, deburring, welding - up to the finished part.

"If the customer owns several plants, PIPEFAB can propose the most efficient site for the production of each part worldwide. We at TRACTO-TECHNIK are creating the basis for global applications of digital engineering and manufacturing of piping network within the framework of Industry 4.0. Our system can be run independently of installing connecting pipes or manual/automated manufacturing in the workshop," resumed Dr. Gerlach.
