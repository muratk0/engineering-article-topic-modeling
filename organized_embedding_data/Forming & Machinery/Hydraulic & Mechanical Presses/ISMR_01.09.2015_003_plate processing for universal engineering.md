# Plate processing for Universal Engineering

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

UNIVERSAL ENGINEERING has increased its productivity with a major investment in the purchase of the new FICEP Gemini 36 HD Plate Processing Centre.

UE designs, develops and manufactures close-tolerance complex assemblies and equipment for the sub-sea, oil and gas, defence and aerospace industry sectors. Its need to increase productivity of components and reduce lead times for its diversified manufacturing processes has led to a major investment in the new FICEP Gemini 36 HD Gantry Style Plate Processing Centre. This system can machine, drill, tap, bevel, mark and thermal cut with oxy fuel and 400-amp plasma components from plate with accuracy and speed in one operation.

“The Gemini enables the company to manufacture completed components in just one process, eliminating the need for multiple material handling operations and external subcontractors. This has significantly reduced lead times and saved on transport costs. Universal Engineering can now also process larger and profiled components in heavy steel plate internally to deliver even more savings, as previously the processing of these components had to be outsourced,” explained FICEP.
