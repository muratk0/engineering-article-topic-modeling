# Smart panel bender

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

AFTER ITS SUCCESSFUL international debut at EuroBLECH in Hannover, Prima Power's new servo-electric panel bender (BCe Smart) will be showcased at the 13th edition of SteelFab, the Middle Eastern trade show for the metal working industry, from 16 to 19 January 2017.

"Today, machines must be increasingly smart and productive," explained Roberto De Rossi, Product Manager for Press Brakes, Prima Power. "Ease of use, ergonomics, flexibility, active safety and reliability are the key requirements to face the new challenges imposed by the market. BCe Smart 2220 is our new panel bending machine designed to meet these requirements with simple but innovative and intelligent solutions. This is why we called it 'Smart'."

The large working table, enabling loading and unloading in a single sequence, increases productivity. The panel bender, says the Italian manufacturer, is extremely easy to use and does not require skilled operators. Using a barcode reader and the ATC system, its set-up is automatic (taking place in masked time and activating dynamic production queues). BCe Smart is also flexible and suitable for producing small volumes, kits of components and individual parts with, Prima Power told /SMR, high-quality bending and high repeatability.

“High ergonomics and safety levels are obtained through a series of characteristics such as the absence of physical barriers for manual loading; warning lights and acoustic signals to synchronize loading and unloading; programmable luminous reference for the simple positioning of the pieces to be machined and an additional interactive display that guides the operator on the working phases to be carried out. The working table side sections can also be easily lowered to allow the operator to get closer for loading or unloading medium-sized sheets," explained Prima Power.

"Other features include compact layout, low maintenance and quick and easy installation. The servo-electric actuators enable the significant reduction of energy consumption and maintenance costs."

BCe Smart is equipped with a range of options to adapt it to specific applications such as automatic tool change, additional short blades for symmetrical or asymmetrical positioning, UBC to minimise the bending profile width or piece-centring devices with asymmetric notches. There is also an additional screen for self-learning mode. See www.primapower.com ■
