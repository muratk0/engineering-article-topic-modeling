# World Cup cutting and bending

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

TURKISH sheet meet specialist, Baykal Machinery (which has been working on a project to be used in the construction of the seven sports stadiums for the 2022 Qatar Fifa World Cup for nearly a year) has pioneered a first with its fully automatic pole cutting and bending system. There is no fully automated system in the world of this size, Baykal told ISMR, adding that existing systems require manual intervention.

“The fully automatic cutting line (26m x 60m) has a cutting size of 2.5m x 14m and saves up to 50 per cent of total processing time. The line not only prevents working hazards but also eliminates labour costs. We completed transportation of the line to Qatar in December last year and it will become operational within a few months,” Baykal told ISMR.

Sports stadiums will be built in seven cities in Qatar as part of the preparations for the 2022 World Cup. Nine of the stadiums will be newly built and three will be renovated. The stadiums built in Al-Daayen, Al-Khor, Al-Rayyan, Al-Shamal, Al-Wakrah, Doha and Umm Slal will be dismantled after the World Cup and sent to developing countries. They will be built by the German architecture company ‘Albert Sheer & Partner’ whose headquarters is in Shanghai.

Baykal Machinery’s director of brand marketing, Murat Bayazıt, confirmed that Baykal received the order about a year ago (the line includes a plasma cutting table, loading / unloading unit, bending press and plasma pipe cutting). He also added that the company has established a plant in Qatar for the fully automated pole cutting and bending line and will start shipments in approximately one month’s time, aiming to become operational by the end of this year.

There will be unmanned production in the lines that will build steel constructions and lighting poles for the 2022 Fifa World Cup. The lines will also be used for the construction of the roads, hotels and facilities required for the World Cup organisation. Orders for the system have been received from the US, Italy and Middle Eastern countries. ■
