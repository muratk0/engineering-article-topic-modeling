# Tube bending

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

AFTER A PERIOD of declining oil prices and a difficult trading environment, Asia's oil and gas industry is now experiencing an upturn, according to experts. In November 2018, the Offshore South East Asia (OSEA) 2018 exhibition was held in Singapore. At the event, Schwarze-Robitec presented its portfolio of tube bending machines for the offshore industry. It focused on machines from its Heavy Duty series.

"This is the third time that Schwarze-Robitec has exhibited at OSEA - Singapore is the ideal location for the trade fair, with the country seen as a hub for the Asian oil industry. We have been a reliable partner for oil and gas companies in the Asian market for many decades. The tube cold bending machines from our Heavy Duty series are powerful solutions for the fast, precise and efficient bending of complex tube geometries. They enable users from the offshore industry to significantly increase production efficiency. The tube cold bending machines are specifically designed for continuous use under major strain. High-strength materials and very large, thick- and thin-walled tubes can be formed in various bending radii," the German manufacturer told ISMR.
