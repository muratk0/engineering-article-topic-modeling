# Making a splash

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

WATER JET SWEDEN has launched Alphajet, which it dubs ‘the next generation of taper angle control and perpendicular waterjet cutting’.

“Alphajet is mechatronics based on a compact design with a fixed TCP point. Extraordinary cutting result can be achieved with carefully selected machine elements and small precise movements at an angular accuracy of within ±0.05mm,” explained the Swedish waterjet system manufacturer.

“The new Alphajet has a compact design, which means less weight and fewer limitations on the work area. This is extra beneficial for those who want to arm their machine with more than one cutting tool. You can fit up to four Alphajets on the same machine, thereby quadrupling the cutting capacity,” added Tony Rydh, founder and technical manager at Water Jet Sweden.

Less movement, explained Rydh, leads to higher accuracy. The new Alphajet has a fixed TCP, so that the machine's X and Y axes don’t need to move to handle the taper angle control. Alphajet’s gearbox design means that 45-30-30 angles require very small movements in the A and B axes when cutting, and the choice to put the toolholder very low on the cutting head provides a low rotation point with small, more accurate movements.

Changing the cutting head is quick and easy – it is automatically centred with the v-block tool holder and there is no need for extra calibration after changing the focusing tube. This both saves time and maintains precision of the cutting head. No special CAD/CAM programs are required to use Alphajet.

All of Water Jet Sweden’s different cutting heads designs fit the new Alphajet. Spray protection minimizes water spray when piercing and the collision sensor protects the nozzle from breaking in the event of a collision. An online height sensor is also available.
