# FOCUS ON WATERJET CUTTING

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

In thermal/mechanical separation technologies, ANT Applied New Technologies picked up a 2017 Blechexpo Award (presented by Blechnet and MM Maschinenmarkt) for its "Consus", an abrasive remixer that enables faster shaving and savings of the abrasive. To produce an air-free jet, the suspension is pressurised in a high-pressure vessel and forced out of the nozzle via a hose.

"Tests prove that the ANT suspension jet cuts two to three times faster than conventional waterjet cutting methods and at less than half the pressure. The reason for this is the composition of the waterjet – a suspension made of water and an abrasive, which is pressurised without air before being squeezed out of the nozzle. The omission of air allows the jet to cut much more efficiently. ConSus combines all of these benefits in a single unit, which can be integrated into any existing water jet cutting system," ANT told ISMR.

At the 2017 Blechexpo exhibition, BTG GmBH showcased two high-pressure pumps in its ECOTRON range – the 40.37+ and the 40.45+.

Above: The sunburst view is one of several status reports in Bystronic's ByCockpit software. It displays the condition of an entire factory at a glance.

Swiss sheet metalworking machinery manufacturer, Bystronic, has introduced a twin-head ByJet Flex for waterjet cutting, which can accept up to two newly developed, 3D cutting heads for producing bevelled edges on components. Upgrading from 2D to 3D technology takes less than an hour, said the manufacturer.

"The ByJet Flex platform can be integrated into a wide variety of production environments and used for both 2D and 3D applications, operating with two CNC heads for straight or bevelled cutting. The basic version is equipped with all prerequisites that allow it to be upgraded with functions such as 3D cutting heads; drill spindles; automatic parts holding during cutting (FixMaster); precise positioning of the cutting head (PositionPointer) and intelligent fine adjustment for rapid, multi-head cutting (PrecisionAligner)," Bystronic told ISMR.

The manufacturer has also introduced a new app that monitors, in real-time, all processes taking place within its laser cutting machines, press brakes and waterjet cutting systems. Called ByCockpit, the software fits neatly with the digital networking requirements of Industry 4.0. It evaluates the collected data and generates status reports, allowing factory output to be optimised.

Eckelmann AG, now over 40 years old, released a CNC user interface for cutting as well as integrated computer vision. Below: The new Mach 500 waterjet cutting system from Flow International Corporation

Flow International Corporation, a leading developer and manufacturer of ultra-highpressure waterjet machines for cutting applications, has released its new Mach 500 waterjet cutting system in Europe.

"Together, the latest generation of FlowXpert® 3-D solid modelling CAD/CAM software and the Compass™ 5-axis contour following and collision sensing solution enable the Mach 500 to make a quantum leap in terms of speed, precision and reliability, significantly boosting productivity," Flow told ISMR.

"With the Mach 500, we have successfully doubled acceleration versus previous models. This means a 15 to 30 per cent reduction in cycle time to the Mach 3b, our best-selling waterjet cutting system worldwide. The Mach 500's Z travel height is 50 per cent larger (305mm) and repeatability (0.03mm) has been doubled. The modern electrical system improves reliability of the overall system and reduces complexity. The latest generation of FlowXpert® CAD/CAM software is capable not only of designing complete 3D models and assemblies but also of simultaneously calculating optimum cutting paths," it added.

"We are delighted that the system has not only captured the imagination of our customers but also that of the expert panel for the Austrian State Prize for Innovation," said STM's CEO, Jürgen Moser. To cut narrow incisions in hard materials with high precision requires a specially developed waterjet cutting process. FAWJ stands for Fine Abrasive Water Jet (micro waterjet, finecut) and was launched in 2008 by Water Jet Sweden. Since then, the technique has been applied to specially developed precision machinery designed to manufacture micro-components. Water Jet Sweden has now broadened the possibilities and launched The FAWJ Micro Head Package for its ordinary machine models. It is designed for customers who wish to cut fine parts with narrow incisions and high precision, without the need for the most extreme micro part tolerances. Its "Micro" (NCM 10) is specially developed for the FAWJ process and is used for the manufacturing of micro components. Micro waterjet cutting machine is a popular alternative to Micro-EDM and Laser.

Its machines include cantilever and bridge designs and 5-axis flying bridge technology (Edge 5) which delivers significant productivity improvements. The company also offers combination plasma and waterjet machines, as well as waterjet surface texturing technology.
