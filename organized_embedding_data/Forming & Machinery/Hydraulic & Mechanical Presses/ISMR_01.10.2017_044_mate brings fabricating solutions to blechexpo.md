# Mate brings fabricating solutions to Blechexpo

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

THE MATE PRECISION Tooling exhibit (stand 1704-1, Hall 1) at Blechexpo in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2017) will display Mate's expanded fabricating solutions, including its new Thick Turret metric QCT™ Quick Change Tooling for A and B stations, new MPM82 material for Thick Turret A and B station dies and more.

Mate patent-pending QCT insert-style punching system is now available in metric (long stem) versions for thick turret A and B stations The QCT system is simple and intuitive to set up, said Mate, as users can flip the durable latch to remove and snap the new insert into place. Original Style QCT is fully compatible with Mate's Ultra® family of guides and canisters, and Mate's Original Style guides, spring packs and Rapidset™ canisters. These punches are also fully interchangeable with Amada NCT, NEX and Z-Tooling systems, as well as Wilson Thick Turret Metric punches.

"Mate QCT punch inserts are made from M4PM™ tool steel, proven to be the most durable, longest lasting tool steel in the industry. Punch drivers are coated with SuperMax™, our next-generation coating, to minimise wear and preserve fits. Only one punch driver is needed for rounds or shapes," explained the manufacturer.

Mate's Thick Turret A and B station dies now feature new optional MPM82 tool steel. Designed for use in high-performance tooling applications, Mate's MPM82 is a high-speed, particle metallurgy steel designed to provide value and versatility for demanding punching applications. Mate will also be exhibiting its broad line of TRUMPF-style tooling in Original Style, Quicklock™ and Next™ versions. See www.mate.com or email: marketing@mate.com■
