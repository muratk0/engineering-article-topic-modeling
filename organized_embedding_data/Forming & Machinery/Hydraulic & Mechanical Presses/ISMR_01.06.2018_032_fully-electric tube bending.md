# Fully-electric tube bending

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

BAE SYSTEMS, ONE of the largest defence contractors in the world, has chosen AMOB as the supplier for its new tube bending cell. The eMOB-100 fully-electric CNC pipe bending machine was installed at the company's pipe shop in Scotstoun, Glasgow (Scotland) to bend all of the piping on the forthcoming Type 26 frigate, which is used to hunt submarines. As the system includes 49 different tool sets, AMOB's installation and training team was with BAE Systems for just under two weeks.

"The customer's requirements were clear and detailed – a fully-electric machine with all available add-ons, to give it the most up-todate system possible. The eMOB-100 was the perfect machine for the job. The entire project – from request for quote through to delivery – was just under two years long, which included visits to the manufacturing facility in Porto, Portugal, as well as existing customers (notably the Jaguar Land Rover plant in Gaydon) and a three-day factory acceptance test (FAT) in early 2018," AMOB told ISMR.

"The FAT test went smoothly with BAE signing off on delivery of the machine there and then. The machine was then delivered 30 days later and installed over a weekend to minimise production downtime. Week one of the two-week training schedule consisted of getting the six pipe-fitters up to speed with the eMOB-100 and production test parts, while week two involved production assistance to smooth out any issues between training and full production, which started the following week. By the end of the second week, BAE was up and running to full speed and in production."

The eMOB-100 covers a range of sizes from 21.3mm x 1.6mm all the way to 88.9mm x 7.1mm. For BAE, the material ranged from extra heavy-duty carbon steel and stainless steel through to 90/10 copper nickel. The multistack capability of the machine allowed it to produce 2D and 3D bends in a single length of tube, something it did not have the capacity to do before. The eMOB-100 also gives it a new processing capability in variable radius roll bending, something it has already been utilising and incorporating into the design of the Type 26.
