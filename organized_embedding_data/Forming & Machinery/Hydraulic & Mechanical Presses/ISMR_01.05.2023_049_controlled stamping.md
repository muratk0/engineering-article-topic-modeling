Founded in 1917, AIDA is a leading global manufacturer of mechanical and servo presses for sheet metal forming, automation equipment and complete metalforming systems.

“AIDA was the first company in the world to develop and market direct-drive servo presses (DSF®- Direct Servo Former, our proprietary servo technology), installing, to date, thousands of units worldwide," it told ISMR.

AIDA presses are used to produce a wide range of items including automotive chassis and components; electric motor laminations; application parts and structural elements; electrical and electronic components; bi-polar plates and battery cases. The company, which continues to evolve and grow, now boasts production facilities in Asia, Europe and North America, with a global presence in more than 60 countries.

## Electric mobility

The production of high-precision motor cores is at the basis of the transition to electric mobility. AIDA's high-speed presses are used for forming the motor cores of electric vehicles.

“Our MSP presses allow users to produce high-precision and high value-added motors such as EV motors, HEV motors and energy-efficient, home appliances motors. The dynamic accuracy of this series of presses is significantly improved thanks to the compact design and multiple suspension points architecture," AIDA explained.

“Balance between high rigidity and wide bed area is brought to a higher level using the latest technologies. The areas that transmit the press forming loads, such as the connecting rod bearings and the crankshaft bearings, are all made from special metals selected to avoid heat deformations. Ultra-precision dies that require a large bed area are necessary for the production of high-efficiency motors, such as motors for hybrid cars or for energy saving," it added.

Responding to these needs, AIDA applied innovative technologies to achieve increased precision with its MSP Series High Speed Stamping Presses, which are also particularly suitable for the production of parts with thicknesses of less than 0.20mm.

## Direct Servo Former (DSF) technology

Bi-polar plates are the core component of PEM fuel cells. They control not only hydrogen and air supply but also the emission of water vapour, along with heat and electrical energy. The production of bi-polar plates requires high-precision forming presses.

Equipped with an innovative nine-point support design, a highly rigid ring frame, zero-clearance slide gibs and the freely programmable servo motion of the AIDA DSF® – Direct Servo Former technology, AIDA DSF-U presses are designed for high manufacturing performances. The slide does not move laterally during forming operations, enabling versatile forming operations. Combined with the single crank design, DSF-U presses enable transfer forming, progressive forming and multi-stage cold forging.

“The overall height has been reduced by 30% by eliminating the connecting rods that had previously been considered essential components of crank presses. The DSF-U series includes a newly developed energysaving hydraulic power unit equipped with a flywheel. Multi-stage knockout functionality can be accommodated by mounting hydraulic cylinders in the bed on the left and right that actuate a central knockout bar. Basic operations can be performed from a central location at the front of the press, including confirmation of the crank angle, die height settings and load monitoring during forming. We provide a wide array of options for DSF-U presses to meet detailed customer requirements," highlighted AIDA.

## DSF-NE2 servo press

Battery cases are containers for chemical reagents and must resist highly corrosive electrolytes. Together with electric motors, they represent the base of e-mobility. The production of battery cases requires highthroughput presses.

In early 2021, AIDA unveiled its new DSF-NE2, a monolithic two-point servo press that combines the high rigidity of one-piece frame with the flexibility of the fully programmable servo motion of AIDA DSF® technology. DSF-NE2 presses are equipped with AIDA's high-torque, low-speed servo motor for metal forming and stamping. Every frame component is designed and verified with FEM systems.

"The zero-clearance roller guide requires no lubrication and contributes significantly to longer die life. The DSF-NE2 series of presses provide low-noise, low-vibration precision forming, optimising all production and maintenance operations while ensuring maximum application flexibility and long die life. This series of presses is capable of speeds of 120 strokes per minute," commented AIDA.

The DSF-NE2 series was awarded Product of the Year in the "Metal Forming and Punching" category at the EuroBLECH Awards 2022.

"AIDA DSF® (Direct Servo Former) servo presses offer unparalleled operability, even with a reduced number of forming stages. Thanks to the 'soft touch' function, which causes the punch to slow down when it comes into contact with the material, they can guarantee a significant increase in the overall quality of the parts produced without compromising on productivity," continued AIDA.

The manufacturer has developed an energy control architecture that replaces the kinetic energy storage capacity of a flywheel with the electrical energy storage capacity of capacitors. In fact, the peak electrical energy required during the forming phase is predominantly provided by the electrical energy previously stored in capacitors, leading to "an energy demand from the power grid comparable to that of a mechanical press."

"All AIDA DSF® servo presses use proprietary AIDA servo motors specifically developed for forming operations, capable of offering five times the performance of a standard commercial servo motor," concluded the press specialist.
