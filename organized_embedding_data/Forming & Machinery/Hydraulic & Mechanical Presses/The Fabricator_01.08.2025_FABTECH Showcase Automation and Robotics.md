Automation That Moves The Future: FANUC America at FABTECH 2025

In an era defined by workforce challenges, supply chain volatility, and rising demands for efficiency, automation has become a strategic priority for fabricators. At FABTECH 2025, FANUC America is showcasing a comprehensive suite of advanced automation technologies designed to empower fabricators with greater precision, adaptability, and operational resilience.

FANUC will highlight how automation not only alleviates workforce constraints but also enhances productivity and quality. From welding to wet machining, the company will demonstrate its latest robot and cobot solutions in its Main Booth (B17010), many powered by FANUC’s new R-50iA controller. In the Finishing Pavillion (D40121), FANUC’s innovative CRX Paint Cobot and industrial paint robots will showcase real-world applications.

Highlights of the exhibits include:

Collaborative Robots Perform Live Welding Assembly

In an all-new work cell featuring coordinated motion live welding, the CRX-30 iA uses 3DVision to pick raw washers and nuts before a CRX-10 iA live welds, with the prior cobot cleaning and sanding them to a finished part.

Flexible Multi-Arm Welding

In a dynamic welding display, the high-capacity FANUC M-950 robot simulates lifting an excavator bucket and presenting it to a duo of ARC Mate welding robots performing synchronized welds.

Flexible Robotic Wet Machining

In a wet machining demonstration, FANUC’s high-precision M-800/60-20B industrial robot mills and drills aluminum stock using a variety of tools and accommodating various mounting positions. Built to withstand harsh elements like water and debris, this 6-axis model, known for its rigidity and accuracy, offers a larger work envelope compared to similarly priced CNC machines.

Dual Cobot Paint Application with Load and Unload

At the Paint Booth, FANUC uses two cobots, visual identification and advanced line tracking to identify, load, paint and unload electric guitar bodies. Employing a CRX-10 iA/L Paint Cobot with FANUC 3DV camera and iRVision, the system visually tracks and simulates painting of the guitar bodies. Meanwhile, a CRX-20iA/L Cobot will leverage a fixed FANUC 3DV iRVision sensor and iRPickTool software in showcasing its impressive reach and line tracking capability to load and unload guitar bodies from a conveyor to a rack.

For metal fabricators seeking to enhance operational performance and remain competitive in a shifting landscape, FANUC underscores the value of automation as a cornerstone for sustainable growth and innovation.
