## DREISTERN

www.dreistern.com

REQUIREMENTS FOR ROLLFORMING equipment become fuzzier due to changing customer requirements.

Dreistern’s multifunctional roll forming technology offers a new way to cope with this challenge.

A “multifunctional machine” can be configured within a few minutes for completely different parts with different process requirements by placing specialised work modules, like a flying punch or a laser welding module, onto the rollforming machine just like an ordinary rollforming tool. Every work module has its own mobile control which can freely be moved. This enables the exchange of work modules even between different production lines. Different lines can share work modules which cannot be fully utilised by one line.

“The benefits of multifunctional rollforming include 30% to 50% reduced foot print, 10% to 30% reduced investment cost for complex parts and expandability from an ordinary rollformer to a highly flexible sheet metal production cell at any time,“ explained the manufacturer.

## STEINEL NORMALIEN AG

www.steinel.com

ELECTRICAL AND MECHANICAL tapping units from STEINEL ensure that threads can be formed directly during the punching and bending process. The electrical as well as the mechanical tapping unit can be integrated directly into the punching and bending tool.

“Compared to a subsequent, non-integrated tapping process, the quality of the parts, process reliability and profitability in production can be noticeably improved. The tapping units, which were specifically developed for this application, can be easily integrated into the most progressive tools. The advantages of process-integrated tapping can be enjoyed fully through the different tapping units,“ STEINEL explained.

With the electric version (S-Former E) of the tapping unit, threads from M1 to M26 can be formed using complete process monitoring. The mechanical tapping unit (S-Former Z) is force-controlled through the press stroke and forms threads from M4 to M8.
