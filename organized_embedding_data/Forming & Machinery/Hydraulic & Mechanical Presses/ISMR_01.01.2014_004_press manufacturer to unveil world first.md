# Press manufacturer to unveil ‘world first’

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

BRUDERER WILL be celebrating a £500,000 deal at MACH 2014 to deliver a ‘world first’. The company will be supplying a high-performance automatic stamping press to electronic component specialist, Harwin. The press will help Harwin to increase efficiencies and attack new opportunities in aerospace, instrumentation and industrial markets.

“The new press, the BSTA 280-75 with a BSV-75 high-speed servo-feeder, represents a world first in that it integrates a planetary gearbox into the shaft of the main motor. This will allow Harwin’s engineers to have full control of tooling development to produce test runs of new products,” Bruderer told ISMR. ■
