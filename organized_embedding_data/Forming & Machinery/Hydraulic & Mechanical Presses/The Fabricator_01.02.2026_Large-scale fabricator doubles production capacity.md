# Large-scale fabricator doubles production capacity with tandem press brakes

[TARİH: 01.02.2026 The Fabricator]

SITUATION

Operating from two northern Ohio facilities totaling more than 130,000 sq. ft., Vicon Fabricating Co. is built for large-scale, high-precision fabrication. Specializing in long lengths and thick gauges, the company handles a full range of heavy-duty steel fabrication and secondary operations.

Working with domestic and foreign high-strength steels, the company provides fabrications and formed steel components for demanding applications in the heavy truck and trailer chassis, utility, train, and construction industries. Vicon also provides replacement frames, repair services, and custom fabrications for in-service vehicles and equipment.

In addition to its laser cutting, plasma cutting, punching, drilling, shot blasting, and powder coating capabilities, the company’s equipment lineup includes a 750-ton by 26 ft. CNC press brake with backgauge, as well as two CNC press brakes from Cincinnati Incorporated: a 4,000-ton by 50-ft. machine, and a 2,500-ton by 40-ft. press brake with backgauge.

When growing demand began to push the limits of its metal forming capacity, the fabricator turned to CI once again.

RESOLUTION

Vicon acquired two CI 1,250-ton Proform press brakes, each able to operate independently for standard jobs or in synchronized tandem for long, heavy parts requiring precise, multipoint bending.

When configured in tandem, the machines make a 2,500-ton by 40-ft. flexible, long-bed CNC forming system that can handle short-run to production components.

Together, the press brakes provide extended forming length for large assemblies, high tonnage per foot for thicker materials, and faster cycle times with synchronized hydraulic control, the company reports.

The configuration lets operators run two short jobs or one long one without interrupting workflow. The touchscreen controls make switching modes quick and intuitive.

With the new tandem system fully online, forming capacity has effectively doubled. The combination of higher tonnages with longer beds allows operators to move large and complex jobs through the department at unprecedented speed, the company states. Lead times have shortened, scheduling is more flexible, and the company can respond faster to both large OEM orders and one-off repair jobs.
