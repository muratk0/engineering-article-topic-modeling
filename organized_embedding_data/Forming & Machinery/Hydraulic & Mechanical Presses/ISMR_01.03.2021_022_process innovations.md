# Process innovations

[TARİH: 01.03.2021 ISMR]

MVD Machinery has been developing machines for sheet metal processing and forming since 1950. Exporting to 82 countries such as Japan, Chile, USA and New Zealand, the company produces CNC press brakes, guillotine shears, punch presses, plasma cutting and laser cutting machines. It currently offers more than 50 machine types. The Ministry of Industry and Technology in Turkey granted it the status of an R&D centre in 2017.

MVD's iLaser is a fibre laser cutting system of aluminium and composite construction, designed to increase cutting speed, quality and precision using servomotors.

"Faster, more precise, higher quality and lower cost cutting results have been achieved with the iLaser. Regardless of material type and thickness, automatic adjustment of the operating parameter values (with no intervention) makes it easier to operate the machine and save valuable time," MVD told /SMR.

MVD has carried out several studies aimed at manufacturing more products in shorter times, especially with the development of new cost-reducing techniques with the system. The system incorporates a range of innovative new features.

With a light and rigid bridge design, the speed of the machine was increased from 150,000mm/min. to 220,000mm/min. and its acceleration went from 2.5G to 4G.

With the increase in speed, production efficiency increased by 20%.

Cutting precision is less than 3%.

Positioning sensibility has been reduced to less than 3%.

The operator is better protected from harmful rays emitted by laser and the particles formed during cutting.

By applying a continuously accelerating motion, instead of a continuous speed movement, the cutting time has been further shortened.

MVD hopes that the approach it has taken to the development of its machinery range will also inspire companies that manufacture similar machines and provide technological infrastructure.

"We try to keep up with the latest technologies, continuously improve machine design and be an innovative solution partner for our customers throughout the process (from the project stage to product launch). We aim to produce high value-added products that will provide design and cost benefits to our customers and we will work with them throughout the product development phase, producing environmentally friendly solutions. Verification and testing processes take place at different stages of production," added MVD Machinery. See www.mvd.com.tr
