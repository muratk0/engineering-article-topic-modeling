# Part and strip levelling

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH will once again be returning to the Blechexpo trade show in Stuttgart this November with its own booth 5502 in Hall 5. It will showcase its latest innovations in part and strip levelling, including a solution for automated part levelling, during live demonstrations at the exhibition. The solution is designed to improve efficiency and reduce ongoing costs with pre-programmed operation.

"We not only ensure process optimisation through level and low-tension sheets, but also through contemporary coworking. Loading and unloading small parts, in particular, is an activity that can be easily automated with a cobot, especially when large batch sizes are involved. This is because the actual part levelling often only takes a few seconds whereas over the levelling process as a whole, the most time is taken up by parts handling," it explained.

The surface-processing specialist highlighted the various benefits of using a cobot. With bin picking, or "reaching into the box", the cobot can use a 3D camera to remove parts that are randomly arranged in a bin and feed them, in the correct orientation, for further processing. Bin picking, it told ISMR, replaces tedious manual tasks, leaving skilled personnel to concentrate on their core duties. A cobot that enables close cooperation with humans, without a separating guard, can also take over an additional work shift or, if necessary, compensate for anticipated staff shortages.

At Blechexpo, KOHLER will also be presenting a compact precision levelling machine (CPL) specifically for levelling strip material (even from thin coil material) for plugs, connectors and electronic components for the connector industry. The robust levelling machine features 19 hard chrome-coated levelling rollers, supported by a large number of supporting rollers.

"The small roller diameter means that even thin strips can be precision-levelled. The CPL as a complete system, with horizontal coiler for different coil weights, can also be found on the stand belonging to Leicht Stanzautomation GmbH (stand 6207, Hall 6) at Blechexpo in Stuttgart," concluded KOHLER.

KOHLER develops and produces part levelling machines and strip feeding lines for presses and stamping machines as well as cut-to-length lines for steel service centres, mechanical engineering firms and for the automotive industry. At its levelling centre, the company levels sheet metal for contract clients and carries out levelling tests. Its headquarters and production facility are in the southern German town of Lahr. KOHLER also has sales and service sites in Shanghai (China); Moscow (Russia) and Salt Lake City (USA).
