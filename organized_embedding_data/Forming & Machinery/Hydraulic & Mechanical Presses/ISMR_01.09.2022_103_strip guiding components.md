# Strip guiding components

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

“Many stamping die components, such as strip guiding, are usually produced in small quantities which is quite expensive and time-consuming. Using standardised components is a good option. Advantages include immediate availability, the combination possibilities of the different types of strip-guiding within the stamping die, and CAD data provided for the design," explained Meusburger, which offers an increasing number of strip-guiding solutions.

Its E 5632 round spring-loaded, strip-guiding pin with collar can be used for different strip thicknesses and is protected against wear by a hardened design. For dies with small installation space, the spring can be built into the bore from a diameter of 10mm.

Its E 5634 spring-loaded, strip guide (block-shaped) is mainly used when there are side cut-outs and therefore too little contact surface available. The strip guide can be mounted directly from the working plane. Using the centrally-positioned shoulder screw, only a small amount of installation space is required within the stamping die. The length of the shoulder screw also determines the spring displacement of the strip guide. The symmetrical design allows the block-shaped strip guide to be used on both sides.

“Our E 5636 strip-guiding rail is always used with a strip lifter. Thanks to the screw connection on the top, it can be directly installed from the working plane. The hardened design ensures wear protection and the integrated infeed chamfers allow safe insertion of the punching strip. It can be used on both sides because of its symmetrical design," added Meusberger.

The small-diameter versions of its round E 5640 strip lifter are suitable for dies with limited installation space. The integrated initial bore enables the introduction of a clearance hole for a pilot pin. The lengths of the strip lifter with collar are matched to the strip-guiding pin with collar, making it easy to combine these two elements.

## Corrosion control

Sandblasting, water blasting and grinding are common methods of restoration and surface preparation for rusty metal parts or structures. However, for areas where these techniques cannot be used (for a variety of reasons), Cortec® offers a surface preparation alternative to abrasive blasting or grinding in the CorrVerter® Rust Converter Primer.

"CorrVerter® is a fast-drying, water-based one-component primer for rusty surfaces. It contains a unique formulation of chelating agents combined with a high solids waterborne latex that has extremely low water vapour permeability. The combination of these materials converts surface rust into a hydrophobic passive layer, creating a specialty primer with excellent protection against re-rusting of metal surfaces," it said.

It can be used almost anywhere that a tight layer of surface rust is present. Rather than requiring special equipment or abrasion.

"The large contact surface of the blockshaped E 5644 strip lifter guarantees reliable lifting and forward pushing of the metal strip, even for large cut-outs. The E 5644 also has an integrated initial bore for the introduction of a clearance hole for the pilot pin and all functional dimensions are matched to the respective plate thicknesses," continued Meusberger.

"Our E 5654 block-shaped, spring-loaded strip positioner can be used either springloaded on both sides for centring or rotated at 90° and fixed on one side for aligning to an outer edge. It is available from stock in three different sizes," it concluded.

## Tube laser cutting

BLM Group USA has announced the availability of its new LT7F2 lasertube featuring a 2D cutting head. It is suitable for fabricators processing round tubes, square, rectangular and open profiles or special sections requiring straight vertical-type cutting.

"This machine offers the benefits of our versatile LT7, with 3D cutting head, at a value price point. The LT7F2 can handle tubes from 0.5" to 6" and up to 15.4lbs/ft., and is available with either a 3kW or 4kW fibre laser," it told ISMR.

Similar to the original LT7, the LT7F2 features automated changeover. At the end of each batch, the loader, spindle, steady rest and intermediate supports automatically adapt to the new section to be cut without any interruption of the production cycle.

Additionally, the LT7F2 offers flexible loading options that can be optimised according to production needs. Special and or/open profiles, short or long parts can be unloaded (front or back) while scrap is automatically managed. Users can pause the workflow to produce small batches, loading one or a few bars on the fly. Additionally, robotic loaders or automated storage systems can be integrated with the lasertube for continuous flow production.

As with all BLM lasertube systems, the LT7F2 comes with BLM GROUP's software interface and Active Tools, which are patented solutions that automatically adapt process parameters (independent of operator experience).
