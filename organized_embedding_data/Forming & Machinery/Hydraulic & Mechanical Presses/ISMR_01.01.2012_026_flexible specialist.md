Multiple operations on specialised lines, including punching, are IRON’s speciality

Since 1969, when it was founded, Italian manufacturer IRON SpA has continued to meet challenges in technology, quality and innovation. Specialised, customised and upgraded coil processing lines for punching, bending and cutting are its hallmark. It supplies to a range of sheet metal customers in sectors such as lighting equipment; electrical cabinets and switchboards; elevators; supermarket equipment; automotive parts; boilers and burners; safes; air conditioners; industrial and domestic kitchens plus vending machines.

Amongst IRON’s successful machine tool models, some have represented important moments in the evolution of automation. Its pneumatic feeder and subsequent hydraulic version, using orbital motors, were innovative automation systems for press feeding in the automotive sector and were chosen by major automotive OEMs such as Alfa Romeo.

## Automation in focus

“In general, Europe needs to reduce its overall manufacturing costs and therefore needs to think of new technical solutions and, more specifically, of automation,” Massimo Dalla Pace, IRON export manager, told ISMR.

“European manufacturers must put their focus on innovation and not be followers, but leaders, and find new solutions to the challenges that they face. There is also a need for new higher performance, reliable machine tools to replace older ones.”

To remain on the crest of the wave of innovation, IRON has set up a department dedicated to research and development, backed by its founder and staff who have amassed considerable expertise over the years at meeting ever-increasing technological requirements. New solutions such as structural-mechanical, electronic and electromechanical analysis of line control systems and management software, with the aim of improving and facilitating the work of the operator as well as an online support service that guarantees client assistance directly from IRON’s offices, have been tested (and proved) in a model known as IRON online.

“Extensive experience has enabled us to introduce various innovations into the mass production process. This includes a touchscreen operating system and the introduction of high tech materials such as a total digital control system. IRON is committed to continuous research and to the constant improvement of advanced technologies to guarantee its clients unsurpassed production performance,” Dalla Pace explained.

The company uses robots for welding metal parts, latest generation 5-axis machining centres to help guarantee the quality of its systems, quality assurance workshops (where precision tests are carried out on the materials, the quality and tolerance of the welds, processes, painting and assembly etc.) as well as employing a comprehensive series of checks and inspections.
