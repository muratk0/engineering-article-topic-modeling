# Automated press line for sheet metal forming

[TARİH: 01.01.2020 ISMR]

AS AN IMPORTANT automotive production location in Asia, Thailand has great investment potential. As one of Thailand's leading automotive parts manufacturers, the Thai Summit Group plays an important role in developing Thailand as an automotive manufacturing and export location. It is a sheet metal forming industry specialist.

Thai Summit Automotive Co., Ltd. (www.thaisummit.co.th/) operates a press line for sheet metal forming at its headquarters. To operate the system, which features six fully automated presses, the automotive parts manufacturer has been using FIPA gripper systems. The lines are automated, from infeed up to the finished part, which minimises handling while increasing output.

“The presses are operated consecutively and connected in a series. Metal is automatically moved through a series of presses and formed into a sheet. Employees no longer need to manually load the metal sheet into the first press. After the pressing process a robot, positioned beside the press and equipped with a gripper, removes the sheet and feeds it into the next press. Depending upon the number of presses working in the series, this process is repeated until the last press. The entire handling process between the presses is fully automated," explained the company.

FIPA's SM-B series bellows vacuum cups, made of a wear-resistant and oil-resistant NBR material, were optimised for highly dynamic gripping processes. Robust anti-slip cleats give the vacuum cups for sheet metal handling high slip resistance, even when metal sheets are oily.

FIPA equipped each of the six robot cells with an automatic tool changer to reduce set-up times. These are suitable for flexible, cross-tool applications. The grippers can be changed automatically, quickly and easily to process five different car body parts in the same cell or machine. The new fully automated press line allows Thai Summit to process 900 metal sheets per hour, compared to only 600 previously.
