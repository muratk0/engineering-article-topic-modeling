# Tooling innovation at FABTECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

WILSON TOOL INTERNATIONAL showcased innovative tooling solutions at FABTECH 2016. EXP Punch technology for Salvagnini offers a standard holder with truly universal punches for high-precision punching systems. Its comprehensive EXP line is designed for thick turret, thin turret and multi-tools, and is now available to enhance the performance of Salvagnini punching systems. EXP punch technology is manufactured with high-speed steel for long-lasting durability and resistance to galling.

Swing Ear Sets bring easier part removal, suitable for bending boxes with four sides and return flanges. Forcing the punch out of the box when making the final bend is no longer necessary, because the ears simply swing out of the way. They are available in most profiles for American, European and WTstyle punches.

Wilson Tool's ExpressRail™M2000 Hydraulic Clamping System reduces set-up time with a wireless pendant control that clamps and seats press brake tooling in seconds, giving fabricators more open height and extended clamp life. It is compatible with existing WTand American-style press brakes.
