# All around the press

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

MILLUTENSIL IS A specialist manufacturer in spotting and maintenance, providing ergonomic, productive and easy-to-use technologies to help streamline the checking and testing of moulds and dies. Meet the company on stand C113, hall 27, at this year's EuroBLECH (25-29 October 2016).

Its die-splitter was developed following its customers' requests to easily handle large dies in the sheet metal sector. It is used for splitting, adjusting, maintenance and try-out operations on dies.

"Benefits include the considerable reduction of die production costs, thanks to time and workforce efficiency; extremely easy die handling (without the use of cranes, forklift trucks or lifting equipment); longer service life; ergonomic positions to work on dies (using swivelling and tilting plates) and streamlined operation through the intuitive controls of the Touch Panel," Millutensil explained.

Millutensil has positioned itself to meet customer needs "all around the press" for those who need feeding lines, one or more lubrication systems and conveyor belts for the transport of scrap and parts.

At EuroBLECH 2016, Millutensil will showcase its recent "Z-Zig-Zag" programme, which features different solutions to save material in manufacturing. These include Zig-Zag automatic lines for cutting discs with a press; Zig-Zag feeder machines, Zig-Zag automatic lines for cutting discs with a multi-disc shear and Zig-Zag automatic lines for cutting discs with a laser.

The systems for manufacturing discs from coil, using presses, can be built as a long line with Zig-Zag feeder machine, as a compact line with straightener/Zig-zag feeder or as a Zig-Zag feeder. Millutensil manufactures zig-zag automatic lines combined with multi-disc shears for cutting discs. A multi disc shear ensures cutting flexibility for the diameters of the discs to be manufactured. The machine enables the manufacture of discs with different diameters with no need for moulds. In Millutensil's laser cutting Zig-Zag automatic lines, the feed line features a decoiler and straightener machine, belt advance system, laser and a stacker consisting of single or double stacking boards.
