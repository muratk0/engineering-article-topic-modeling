# Fabricating solutions at FABTECH

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

MATE PRECISION TOOLING will be exhibiting at this year's FABTECH in Las Vegas (booth C36069) from 16 – 18 November 2016. Its greatly expanded one-source fabricating solutions include the new Ultra® QCT™ Quick Change tooling, CleanBend™ press brake tooling, One-Micron fibre laser lenses and more.

“Our Ultra QCT tooling is simple and intuitive to set up, and fully compatible with our Ultra® family of guides and standard canisters. The punch is keyed at the perimeter of the tool – not the centre – providing superior angularity control. The shoulder of the punch remains securely guided by the guide when punching. Only one punch driver is needed for rounds or shapes," Mate told ISMR.

“Made from M4PM™ tool steel, Mate Ultra QCT is proven to be the most durable tool steel in the industry. And it's coated with SuperMax™, our nextgeneration coating, to minimize wear and preserve fits."

Designed to accommodate any shape up to 0.492" (12.50mm) diameter, Mate's new Salvagnini “P0" fully guided perforating tool features tight stripper clearance to prevent lateral punch movement to help ensure accurate hole punching. MPM82 powdered tool steel has strong wear resistance for long tool life. The fully guided tool is designed for smaller hole sizes in the 7-ton stations of Salvagnini punch presses. “Our new CleanBend forming technology for press brakes enables high quality sheet metal forming with minimal to no marking of the metal. Using rotating supports, the process mimics a folding operation," explained the manufacturer.

Mate also continues to expand its laser optics offerings with the introduction of 1 Micron Optics for fibre lasers. 1 Micron Optics are approved for use in leading brand fibre lasers. See www.mate.com
