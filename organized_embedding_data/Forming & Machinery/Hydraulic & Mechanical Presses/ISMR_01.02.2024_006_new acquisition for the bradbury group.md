# New acquisition for The Bradbury Group

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

The Bradbury Group, a global specialist in the design and manufacture of roll-forming and coil-processing equipment, has acquired the assets of Press Room Equipment Co., a provider of press-feed and coil-handling equipment based in Springfield, MO (USA).

Press Room Equipment Co. (PRE) has over four decades of experience designing and manufacturing equipment for the metalstamping and fabrication industries. The company offers a wide range of products including stock reels; pallet decoilers; straighteners; coil upenders; press feed systems; high-speed precision servo feeds and automated processing lines. In addition to the PRE brand, the acquisition includes the Norwalk and Waddington Electronic brands.

This acquisition marks a significant step in the longstanding collaborative relationship between The Bradbury Group and PRE, with PRE supplying equipment for Bradbury's roll-forming lines for several decades. This new phase is expected to enhance the companies' offerings and bolster their collaborative efforts.

Above: David Cox (L), CEO of The Bradbury Group, and Gary Moore (R), General Manager of Press Room Equipment Co.

Gary Moore, General Manager, Press Room Equipment Co. added: “We are thrilled to be part of The Bradbury Group.

Bradbury has been an established customer of ours, and we have great respect for its roll-forming lines and engineering excellence. By joining its team, we will share resources and industry expertise enabling us to offer cutting-edge solutions to the stamping and coil processing markets."

The Bradbury Group plans to maintain the operation of Press Room Equipment Co., LLC as an independent entity, with no alterations to its management, staff or location. It will, however, provide technological and engineering support to facilitate PRE's growth and business expansion.

The Bradbury Group is a global family of manufacturing companies dedicated to producing metal-processing equipment. Collectively, the companies have equipment in over 80 countries with 700+ employees around the world.■
