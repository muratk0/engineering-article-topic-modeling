# Wilson Tool

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand A127

https://wilsontool.com/en-gb

Wilson Tool is the world's largest independent manufacturer of CNC punching and press brake tools. An extensive range of accessories, tool grinding machines and spare parts for punching machines and pressbrakes are also included in its scope of supply.

The company celebrated 55 years in business in 2021. Products are manufactured at Wilson Tool's global headquarters at White Bear Lake, Minn., USA, as well as at manufacturing facilities in England; Canada; Brazil and Italy. It has also developed further services for customers such as lean manufacturing seminars, webinars, application training and industry fora.

"Wilson Tool has a wide range of tools for punch presses and press brakes. We offer our customers tailor-made tooling solutions for the most difficult requirements. The Wilson Tool name represents more than 50 years of experience providing innovative tooling solutions that help our customers with their toughest manufacturing problems," said the tooling specialist.

## Yamazaki Mazak

Stand D26

www.mazakeu.com

Yamazaki Mazak is set to give a European debut to its entrylevel laser processing machine, the OPTIPLEX 3015 Ez, at EuroBLECH 2024.

“Our entry-level model is designed to offer customers a user-friendly experience, affordability and exceptional performance. The 6kW machine on display at EuroBLECH will be exhibited as part of a fully integrated cell, which takes the workpiece from raw material through to the finished parts," Mazak told ISMR.

In addition to the OPTIPLEX Ez, which is equipped with a three-pallet changer to optimise cycle time, the cell comprises a Mazak CST 3015 storage automation system featuring eight shelves (six for loading raw materials and two for unloading remnant sheets). It also features a Mazak SMART MANUFACTURING CELL, a robotised sorting system to separate the finished parts and place the parts on a pallet, ready for the next process.
