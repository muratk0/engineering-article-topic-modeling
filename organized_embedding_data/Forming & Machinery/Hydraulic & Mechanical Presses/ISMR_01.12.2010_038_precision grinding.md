# PRECISION GRINDING

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

Mate Precision Tooling has introduced its ValuGrind high precision grinder for easy punch and die maintenance. Designed by Mate, ValuGrind lets fabricators precisely regrind punches and dies to maintain optimum condition for high quality parts production.

Available for either Thick Turret or TRUMPF-style tooling, ValuGrind comes completely fixtured. Features include an extra large 165mm diameter diamond grinding wheel for optimal punch and die finish, inner wheel cooling system places coolant directly where needed (reducing excessive heat build-up that could damage the tooling) and a 1050-watt motor delivers 4050 RPM for high speed, precise surface finishes.

“Precise grind depth control is easily set in 0.02mm increments for a precise finish to avoid over-grinding and a compact, portable tabletop design (820 x 520 x 675mm) makes ValuGrind easy to operate where space is limited,” Mate confirmed. See: www.mate.com/valugrind
