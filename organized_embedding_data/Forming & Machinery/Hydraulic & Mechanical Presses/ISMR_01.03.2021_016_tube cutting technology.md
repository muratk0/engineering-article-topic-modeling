# Tube cutting technology

[TARİH: 01.03.2021 ISMR]

The t-cut tube cutting machines from mechanical engineering company, transfluid, in Schmallenberg uses orbital cutting technology for tube with a diameter of up to 80mm.

"Perfect cuts are one of the key production benefits of this technology, as well as rapid cycle times and clean processes. The RTO 628 for tube diameters of 6-28mm and the RTO 2080 for tube diameters of 20-80mm cut tubes with great precision, including steel and stainless steel tubes," commented transfluid director, Stefanie Flaeper.

The intuitive controls of the system integrate with production processes and can be combined with different concepts for loading tube or flexible unloading systems, if required. Speed and flexibility, said Flaeper, are inherent in the machine, used as either a stand-alone system or as part of a production cell.

"We built the t-cut tube cutting system with a focus on economy and maximum efficiency. This starts with the durable cutting blade and continues with its clean cutting results, with no further steps required on a workpiece. With optional software available to optimise the cutting process, scrap and any associated waste is a thing of the past," added Flaeper.

As an all-in-one solution, the t-cut can manufacture medium and large volumes with different cutting options, using a cut-through, pull or pull-/and break method. Changing machine set-up for other tube sizes or lengths takes very little time.

In addition to the orbital cutting technique, transfluid also offers the blade-cutting method. If needed, the cutting step can be integrated with the process on the bending machine. The bending and cutting process can be combined, said transfluid, with very little chip generation. See www.transfluid.net/en
