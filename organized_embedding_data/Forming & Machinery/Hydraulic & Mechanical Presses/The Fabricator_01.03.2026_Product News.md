Gas mixers offer enhanced connectivity

Witt Gas has introduced its latest generation of the MEM+ gas mixer series, which now features enhanced connectivity. The mixers offer seamless integration into existing systems through a choice of seven distinct interfaces. Standard options include Analog 4-20 mA, Ethernet, CanBus, and OPC UA; optional interfaces include Profinet, RS232, and Analog 0-10V.

The mixers provide mixing of two or three nonflammable gases, such as nitrogen, CO

2

, or oxygen. Electronically operated, motorized proportional valves enable mixture adjustments in 0.1% increments. The desired gas mixture typically is achieved in less than three seconds, the company reports.

Wire drive pairs with power source and variety of robotic arms

Miller Electric Mfg. LLC has launched the Auto Elite Wire Drive, designed to pair seamlessly with the Auto Deltaweld power source to create an automation-ready welding system.

Built for custom welding automation systems, the drive offers simplified integration and compatibility with a variety of robotic arms and automation setups. Mounting bracket options support a variety of robot arms and system setups. A quick-release bracket speeds up liner changes.

Graphic designs added to welding helmet line

Lincoln Electric has introduced three new graphic designs to its Viking 3350 ADV welding helmet line. The new designs are Mythical (neon hues and a dragon centerpiece), Red Strike (a deep crimson base with electrified lightning accents), and Graffiti (black-and-white, graffiti-inspired artwork).

The helmet offers the company’s 4C lens technology for optical clarity.

Portable multiprocess power source offers MIG, stick, and TIG welding outputs

ESAB offers the Rogue EMP 210 Pro compact, multiprocess welding power source. The unit is suitable for use in light fabrication and light construction, auto repair and restoration, metal art, maintenance, and farm/agriculture applications.

With the Synergic Control feature, welders set the wire type, wire diameter, and shielding gas mix, and the machine automatically sets and maintains optimized welding parameters throughout the range of material thicknesses. The 37.3-lb. unit provides a top MIG output of 210 amps, a stick output of 180 amps, and a TIG output of 210 amps, all at 30% duty cycle.
