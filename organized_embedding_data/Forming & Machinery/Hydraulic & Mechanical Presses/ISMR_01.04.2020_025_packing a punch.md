# PACKING A PUNCH

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

Kintech has saved on subcontract costs with a new punch press.

Kintech, a UK specialist in the fabrication of engineered parts, is benefiting from the installation of its first TRUMPF punch press to replace the company's long-established machine. Based in Hull, Kintech is using the automated TRUMPF TruPunch 3000 to perform lights-out production of parts for the caravan, leisure, retail and medical sectors, eliminating the cost of outsourcing this activity.

Although only established in 2009, the company was formed from the ashes of former Hull-based fabrication business, John A Kinnersley Co. Ltd., which has been in business for forty years. The assets of Kinnersley were acquired by current Kintech chairman, Carl Canty.

"Although the machines were perfectly operational, it was clear that the last ten years of business at Kinnersley had seen minimal investment," explained Carl Canty. "As a result, since the establishment of Kintech, we have steadily been updating machinery, which has resulted in more orders and revenue growth."

## Making a new choice

Today, ISO9001-accredited Kintech has 60 employees and has enjoyed year-on-year growth since its formation a decade ago. Core business at the company is the punching, bending and powder coating of sheet and tube.

Kintech supplies products to some of the largest caravan parks and manufacturers in the UK. The company's portfolio includes caravan steps; beds and tables; storage units; siting equipment; flues; roof plates and the manufacture of assisted-living aids for the care industry.

The latest machine tool to come under scrutiny, in terms of potential replacement, was Kintech's punch press.

"Our punch press was old and could no longer keep pace with growing sales," said Carl Canty. "In fact, we had started to subcontract our punching work, which was having an obvious impact on bottom line profitability. With this in mind, we decided to replace the machine."

Canty had been a long-time user of one particular supplier of sheet metal processing machinery but, this time, conscious of being thorough, he decided to examine the entire market. What he found proved to be a surprise.

"Although I thought that my previous machine tool supplier would be hard to beat, the TruPunch from TRUMPF was more attractive, both technically and financially," he told ISMR.

To make sure, he duly accepted invitations to both TRUMPF in Germany and the company's UK headquarters in Luton.

## An automated option

“TRUMPF went the extra mile and was very professional from the outset," he reported. "I originally thought the TruPunch 1000 would be ideal for our requirements but, following advice from TRUMPF, I could see that the 3000 model was far more suitable. This was primarily because we intended to introduce lights-out manufacturing and the availability of more automation options on the TruPunch 3000," explained Canty.

Kintech produces various parts from large panels, particularly for its customers in the caravan and leisure industries. A number of these sheets have short punching times of 2-3 minutes. These jobs can be labour-intensive, so automation provides a very attractive alternative. Subsequently, Kintech requested that the TruPunch 3000 be accompanied by a full-sized TRUMPF SheetMaster load/unload system.

Installed in July 2018, the automated TruPunch 3000 has been busy producing large panels for caravans and sunbed equipment, largely from 1.0 to 1.5mm thick sheet. Typical materials processed on the machine include galvanised steel, zintec and aluminium. Kintech accommodates batch runs of up to 1500, but more usually around 200-off. Many of the longer-running repeat jobs are processed unmanned overnight and at weekends, with more intricate jobs completed during the day.

“There are many aspects of the machine that we like," added Carl Canty. "Aside from the obvious business benefits of running lights-out, we enjoy the high speed of the machine and the fact that we can set up the tooling while it is running. We are also saving on nesting, labour and materials, not to mention subcontract costs. Furthermore, with its brush table, the machine is quieter than our previous model. Indeed, we have now sold that machine, so we are totally reliant on TRUMPF for our punching requirements."

To support its investment in the TruPunch 3000, Kintech also acquired TRUMPF TruTops Boost software for 2D/3D design and programming.

“In my experience, investing in proprietary software for your machine tool – rather than third-party software – always pays dividends, and this has proved no exception," explained Carl Canty. "We have found TruTops Boost to be highly beneficial, and the operators have really warmed to it. In fact, as first-time users, we found the learning curve to be a lot shorter than expected. Within two weeks, we were feeling very confident on the system which is very easy to use. We already have a number of special tools for the TruPunch 3000 and they are readily supported by TruTops Boost."

The company has been able to innovate much of its product design due to the versatility and advanced capability of the TRUMPF TruPunch 3000. Kintech's investment in the automated TruPunch 3000 and TruTops Boost software package represents the company's largest ever investment as it looks to offer its customers a more reliable, faster and more cost-effective in-house punching resource.

"We have a good set-up now with the new TRUMPF TruPunch," concluded Carl Canty. "Our throughput has increased in line with orders and the machine is delivering exactly what we expected. Having in-house control over manufacturing is a real benefit – we can even process emergency jobs, if necessary.

Of course, the saving on our previous subcontract requirements has helped to justify the investment, which means that our ROI will be relatively short."

## INVESTING FOR SUCCESS

MARCEGAGLIA

Marcegaglia has launched a 600 million euro plan of investments over five years to strengthen activities at its main plants.

Coil coating operations

Marcegaglia is the largest independent global operator in the steel processing industry, processing 6.2 million tons per annum. With a turnover of more than 5.5 billion euros, Marcegaglia is a leading global player in the transformation of steel.

"In 2019, we enjoyed stable production and sales volumes in a market which is declining. In 2020, we aim to consolidate by continuing our growth path both internally and externally. Accordingly, we have launched a 600 million-euro plan of investments over five years to strengthen activities at our main plants," the Italian materials specialist told ISMR.

In 2018, Marcegaglia acquired 80% of Novero (a cold-drawn tube mill). In December 2019, the Marcegaglia Group, through Marcegaglia Plates, acquired (from Evraz Group SA) 100% of the share capital of Evraz Palini & Bertoli, expanding its entire range of heavy quarto plates, with total production of about one million tons per year.

Expansion and development

In 2019, the Marcegaglia Group signed a seven-year loan agreement with the European Investment Bank (EIB) for 100 million euros. This will cover the costs of digitisation (56%) and improved energy efficiency (44%) at its production plants to support its expansion and development activities.

As far as internal CAPEX is concerned, Marcegaglia is placing its focus on value-added products and more efficiency in production. Key projects underway include its new highly specialised cold rolling mill, two cogeneration plants, stainless and carbon steel tube production and stainless steel finishing as well as the improvement of logistical capabilities at Marcegaglia Ravenna. Ravenna is the largest of the Marcegaglia plants and one of Europe's biggest service centres for finishing, cutting and automated packaging of strip and plate.

Marcegaglia Ravenna - spread over 540,000 square metres (225,000 of which indoors) – is equipped with innovative plant engineering facilities and advanced production technologies for chemical pickling, cold rolling, static annealing, galvanizing and pre-painting of coils.

“Our expertise in the transformation of carbon steel flat products has led to the creation of pre-painted steel products manufactured in the Marcegaglia Ravenna plant. The process is enabled by an innovative coil coating process which is continuously evolving to produce better products more quickly, with a lower environmental impact," Marcegaglia told ISMR.

Its painting lines for the organic coil coating of steels, which comply with EN-10169, are designed for efficiency and production flexibility. A large galva-painting line – with combined installation length of 200 metres – is dedicated to HSLA steels and low carbon content material.
