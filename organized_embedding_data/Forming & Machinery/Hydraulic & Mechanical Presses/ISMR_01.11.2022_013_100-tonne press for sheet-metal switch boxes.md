# 100-tonne press for sheet-metal switch boxes

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Ottemeier Werkzeug- und Maschinentechnik GmbH from Verl, Germany, has ordered a TailoredPress from Dieffenbacher to be delivered to a German customer.

The 100-tonne TailoredPress, which will replace a more than 40-year-old Dieffenbacher press, is part of an Ottemeier switch-box manufacturing line for producing sheet-metal parts automatically in several steps, from coil to stacking. Ottemeier will also deliver and integrate conveyor systems and robots at the factory.

"Since 1981, our customer has had only positive experiences with the Dieffenbacher press," commented Christian Wölki, Managing Partner, Ottemeier. "It was no surprise to us that once again we were asked to supply a press from Dieffenbacher as part of our complete automated line."

The TailoredPress for metalforming offers press forces from 1,000 to 10,000kN. The demand-oriented servo drives underpin its “quiet and energy-efficient" performance. Dieffenbacher delivered the press (including servo drives, die clamping and sound insulation) to Ottemeier in November 2022.

The scope of supply also includes assembly and pre-commissioning at Ottemeier, as well as final assembly and commissioning for the end customer.■
