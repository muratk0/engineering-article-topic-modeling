Fully automated punch/laser combination machine made for connected manufacturing

TRUMPF has introduced its TruMatic 5000 manufacturing cell with the new SheetMaster for material handling to the North American market. The system offers laser cutting, punching, and forming capabilities with a fully automated flow of materials, from loading/unloading the machine to removing finished parts.

The combination machine is equipped with a 6-kW fiber laser that saves energy while cutting sheet metal parts fast, the company says. It comes with a descending die that prevents parts from being scratched during the punching process. The punching head also comes with the company’s patented electric Delta Drive, which uses about 30% less energy.

The loading/unloading system uses grippers to handle parts in the manufacturing cell. Monitored by sensors, the suction cups on the grippers can be activated individually for flexibility. In addition, the system can be connected to an automated STOPA storage system, which automatically supplies the manufacturing cell with raw material. The system also palletizes and sorts parts without human intervention.

System integrates large-size turret punching and laser cutting into compact space

Murata Machinery USA Inc. (Muratec) offers the MF30510HL, which combines turret punch press and fiber laser processes. It offers a work size of 5 by 10 ft. and a punching capacity of 30 metric tons.

The system merges punching, laser cutting, forming, tapping, and deburring. Its 4.0-kW fiber laser output enables the efficient processing of a variety of sheet materials and thicknesses, the company states. It also can be integrated with the manufacturer’s automation systems, including the FG series parts sorting system.

An integrated four-station rigid tapping unit allows for high-speed tapping operations. The fiber laser remains securely mounted on a dedicated frame to help eliminate potential vibration interference during cutting and punching. Forming dies retract when not in use to allow the machine to perform laser cutting and punching to reduce the chance of scratching and catching while making adjacent punch stations available.

Cobot plasma cutting system automates high-mix production

Hypertherm Associates offers the Powermax105 SYNC air plasma cutter with cobot operation for manufacturers that are considering automating their welding operations, especially in high-mix production.

According to the company, the addition of the cobot provides users with rapid, consistent, and safe cutting and gouging, along with minimized secondary operations.

Fully electric steel plate roll machine delivers 0.05-in. positioning accuracy

Davi Inc. offers the fully electric e-Power plate roll machine. According to the manufacturer, the compact unit delivers quiet operation, energy efficiency, ease of maintenance, and simple installation.

Because it is electric, the machine achieves a positioning accuracy of 0.05 in. to help ensure repeatable and reliable results for a variety of applications. Its smooth and simultaneous movement across multiple axes allows the machine to execute multiple tasks concurrently.

The system has a single actuator component that can be replaced by a licensed electrician and shipped to the manufacturer for rebuilding. The design of the plate roll machine helps minimize the need for interventions and eliminates typical points of leakage.

Two-in-one cordless tool performs beveling, weld shaving

Heck Industries/Woodward Fab has introduced the model 2700, a batteryoperated two-in-one beveler/weld shaver.

The tool includes both a V-block bevel attachment and a weld shaver shoe plate. It creates 45-degree bevels up to 5/16 in. deep (5/8 in. wide) and removes weld beads flush up to 5/8 in. wide in a single pass.

The 15-lb. unit uses Dewalt Max 60-V batteries, which are not included.

Vertical band saw provides low torque and amp usage at start up

Jet Tools, a JPW Industries company, has introduced its continuous variable- speed vertical band saws. These 12-, 18-, and 20-in. saws can be used for cutting metals including copper, aluminum, brass, ferrous metals, and carbide stainless steel.

Continuous speed changes eliminate the need to shift from low to high speeds. An internal chip blower continuously clears metal shavings, and a laser guides cutting operations. Additional axis increases cutting head reach capability to 90 degrees

Tecoi USA’s BevelArc 3D has an additional axis, increasing the cutting reach capability of the line of plasma, oxyfuel, and laser plate processing machinery. With it, cutting heads now can be positioned from 0 degrees to 90 degrees from a vertical position for cutting and fabricating pressure vessel tank heads, as well as difficult shapes and profiles from beams, plates, and tubes. Users can perform continuous beveling by tilting, without unnecessary torch turning. Since the torch does not have to move, it is not necessary to make loops to recover torch position, except in rare cases when the finishing of the corners requires it.

Features include automatic restart after collision, automatic proportional height control, and high-precision roller runners.

Bending Cell Offers Both Automated and Manual Operation

AMADA’s Collaborative Robot System, paired with the 88-ton HRB 8025 press brake, allows for seamless transition between automated and manual operation. The cobot system is designed to transform a standalone press brake into an automated bending system capable of 24/7 operation.

The system comes equipped with a tablet-based, drag-and-drop programming interface that allows operators to quickly set up programs at the machine and streamline the bending process. Smart templates combine multiple movements for faster, easier programming.

Blanks can be delivered on a standard pallet and picked up directly with the cobot, without having to hand-load blanks onto a gauging table. Vacuum grippers accommodate small to large parts, and multiple part-palletizing configurations are available.

New controls automate orbital stretch wrapping

New Genius Controls from TAB Industries LLC transform the company’s semiautomated TAB Wrapper Tornado orbital wrapper into a fully automated pallet wrapping machine. This technology enables one forklift driver to trigger the orbital wrapper to stretch-wrap a pallet load automatically in 30 seconds or less without pressing any buttons, handling plastic film rolls, using remote controls, or leaving the seat of the lift truck. As the lift truck driver approaches the orbital wrapping ring with a pallet load, the wrapper equipped with the controls automatically recognizes its presence, scans the load dimensions, determines the appropriate amount of stretch wrap, applies the stretch wrap 360 degrees around and under the pallet and load, and cuts the stretch wrap.
