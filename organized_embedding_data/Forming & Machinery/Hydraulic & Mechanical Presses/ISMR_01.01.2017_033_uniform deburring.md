# Uniform deburring

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

FLADDER DANMARK A/S produces a variety of deburring systems that are suitable for processing laser-cut, punched or watercut workpieces.

“Brushes ensure uniform deburring - the gear head, with the main motor and rotating motor, is mounted on a scissor suspension frame. The scissor is equipped with special hardened, grinded wheels. This enables the linear, oscillating movement across the conveyor belt which, combined with in-pair counter-rotating spindles, results in uniform workpieces and uniform wear of the tools. The abrasive cylinders are flexible tools so that flat, as well as bent, shaped and three-dimensional workpieces, can all be processed," the Danish manufacturer told ISMR.

The FLADDER® 300/GYRO is equipped with a powerful gear head mounted with six cone spindles on which different kinds of spindles can be used. Using a powerful vacuum system, even small workpieces can be secured during the deburring process. The control panel includes a touch-screen and the machine body itself is powder coated with strong shields that offer a view of the deburring process through the large windows.

“We are focused on saving energy. As an option, the vacuum motor can be controlled by a frequency inverter. When processing large pieces, the vacuum effect can be set to half, thereby saving energy. Another option is an energy-saving system which uses a laser beam to detect the flow of workpieces in the machine. If the flow of workpieces stops, the machine will shut down. This saves energy and avoid unnecessary wear of the tools and the machine," added FLADDER.

The FLADDER® 400/GYRO is developed for heavy-duty work and the option of using different kinds of tools to increase capacity. See www.fladder.dk
