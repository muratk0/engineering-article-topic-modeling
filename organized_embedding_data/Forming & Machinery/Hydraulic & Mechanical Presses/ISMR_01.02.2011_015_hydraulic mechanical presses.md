Founded in 1962, SEYI (Shieh Yih Machinery) in Taiwan has established a strong position in the press building industry over the past 48 years. SEYI mechanical presses are suitable for both blanking and progressive die tooling. Press products include servo presses, single/double crank presses, straightside presses, high speed presses and a variety of press ancillaries. Current joint ventures with Japanese press maker Amino, H & F (Hitachi Zosen Fukui Corp.) & LEM in developing hydraulic, straightside and precision high speed presses add to SEYI’s technical abilities.

“We will continue to develop the servo technology on our presses,” Claire Kuo, SEYI CEO, told ISMR. “In fact, we will announce new servo technology developments at the next TIMTOS exhibition in Taiwan in March 2011. Asia is still very much a volume market using a lot of traditional mechanical presses but customers there are starting to look seriously at servo technology now.

“We have seen more of a rise in metal fabrication, as opposed to metal forming, over the last year or so and our customers are becoming more interested in software to improve the efficiency of their press operations.”

The company has placed an emphasis on features such as high forming ability, productivity and safety levels on its presses. In keeping with the increasing move towards environmentally-friendly technologies, it has also examined issues such as energy savings to incorporate into its product line.

## AUTOMATION AND PRESS HARDENING

AP&T is currently a world leader in press hardening, and has completed around 30 installations since 2005. As one aspect of its product development, AP&T has produced a new generation of press hardening tools that help cut lead times and promote more even cooling.

Turnkey production solutions are a central element of AP&T’s range and the area is growing. For example, demand for manufacturing solutions for heat exchanger plates has greatly increased. This is an area in which AP&T is developing complete solutions and introducing part innovations. For example, an energy-enhancing press function has been produced that reduces energy consumption by up to 50 per cent.

Production solutions for manufacturing roof drainage line parts is another area where AP&T is increasing its sales. AP&T’s concept includes blank feeding with cutting, press feeding, hydraulic press, press tools, outfeed and seaming, bending and curling. Over the last year, it has delivered this type of solution to Budmat and Plast-Dach in Poland, for example.

The Minster Machine Company will be relocating its expanding Automation Division from Beaufort, South Carolina to its global manufacturing headquarters in Minster, Ohio. “The decision was made to facilitate our Automation Initiative, which includes expanding our existing technologies and product offerings to include robotic-assisted part and blank handling, intelligent assembly and welding automation capabilities that were added to the organisation earlier in 2010,” said David Winch of Minster.

The Automation Division includes a comprehensive and growing line of coil and die handling machinery designed and manufactured by The Minster Machine Company. This product line includes the recently acquired Dynamic Products offering of specialised coil handling machinery. The recent addition of flexible robotic technology adds the capacity for blank and part handling, intelligent part assembly and welding (spot, arc and laser).

The combination and expansion of these resources is expected to be complete by the second quarter of 2011. The Minster Machine Company celebrated the opening of its subsidiary in Ningbo, China, with a ribbon-cutting ceremony on 8 December 2010. ■
