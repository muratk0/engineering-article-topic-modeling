# Racer3 steams ahead

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

COMAU’S RACER3 won the "Product of the Year" award in the "Industrial Robots" category during the thirteenth edition of a contest organized by Control Engineering Poland with the aim of rewarding the best technology and innovations.

The award presented during the Industry 4.0 Conference in Warsaw was preceded by two voting phases lasting six months. In the first phase registered products were screened by a jury whose members represented universities, technical institutes, media and industry professionals. The product selection was then made available to Control Engineering’s readers who voted Racer3 to victory.

“Built entirely in aluminium and magnesium Racer3 weighs 30 kg and features a maximum reach of 630 mm and payload of 3 kg. Thanks to its compact size and extreme velocity the robot is suitable for applications such as assembly; material handling; machine tending; dispensing; pick & place etc. which require maximum precision and velocity in small work spaces” explained Comau ■
