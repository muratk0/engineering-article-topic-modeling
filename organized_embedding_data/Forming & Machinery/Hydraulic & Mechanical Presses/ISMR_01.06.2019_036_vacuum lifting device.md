# Vacuum lifting device

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 ISMR]

THE ET-HOVER-UNIVAC range of vacuum lifting devices is expanding – it now includes the new eT-Hover-univac-line.

"The eT-Hover-univac is designed as an attachment for crane and lifting equipment. It is suitable for lifting and moving large surface loads made of glass, wood, metal, stone or plastic. The new vacuum lifting device is also an attachment for crane and lifting equipment and compatible with the same materials. It is more compact and lighter than its predecessor, making it even more convenient for building areas," euroTECH told ISMR.

"It is suitable for all applications that involve lifting and moving narrow loads," added euroTECH's product manager, Andreas Beuter. "The new lifting devices allows you to handle very narrow loads effortlessly."

It also has a manual turning function and can be turned continuously by 360°, with an automatic locking option at steps of 45°. Manual pivoting of the device by up to 90º is also possible. Like the eT-Hover-univac, the device is operated using buttons on the case. A wireless remote control is optionally available. Two handles make the device easy to position and guide. An electric carrier arm for automatic pivoting is also optionally available.

The new eT-Hover-univacline has a two-circuit system and four suction plates arranged in line. In vertical mode, the lifting device can lift loads with a weight of up to 360kg; in horizontal mode, up to 500kg. These loads can have dimensions of up to 2,500 x 1,000mm. An extended main beam can increase the maximum dimensions to 4,500mm. Multiple security and warning systems, designed in accordance with DIN EN 13155, aim to prevent operation errors and hazards.
