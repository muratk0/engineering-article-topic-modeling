Modular system offers waterjet deburring, cleaning in single step

Ecoclean has introduced EcoCvelox, a flexible, modular system for high-pressure waterjet deburring and cleaning in a single procedure. The configurable, expandable machine with an integrated linear transport system processes parts up to 200 by 200 by 200 mm with cycle times of 15 sec. per pallet in the one-piece flow.

An integrated CAD/CAM interface allows for quick programming of the high-pressure deburring process, either with a single spindle or a highpressure turret with up to five tools. Component cleaning and drying can be carried out by means of injection flood washing, spraying, controlled rinsing, and ultrasound, as well as high-speed blowing and vacuum drying. The system can be loaded automatically with a robot or portal system, as well as manually.

Drop-probe device aids welding machine safety

Rockford Systems has introduced the Detect-A-Finger

®

Gen II dropprobe device to safeguard operators of riveting and welding machinery from finger and hand injuries.

The RKC008 and RKC009 models feature an increased stroke of 1.85 in., unified right- and left-handed operation, a pre-punched enclosure to achieve faster electrical connections, and a soft drop-probe rod for template development before forming the steel drop probe.

The devices work by gravity. When a probe drops and encounters a finger or workpiece in its way, it will not complete its downward travel and the machine will not start a cycle or stroke. A spring returns the probe to its starting or up position.

Plasma transferred arc welding system offers simple integration

Mavrix Welding Automation Inc. has launched a new, stand-alone plasma transferred arc (PTA) system. It can function independently or be integrated with any motion system.

The PTA system permits the user to develop specific timed sequencing of the gas, powder, pilot, and main arc through a user-friendly, 7-in. color HMI/PLC. Gas and water flow monitors automatically shut down the system when needed to protect the torch.

Autofeed band saw includes pneumatic clamping system

Tormach Inc. has introduced the AF50 autofeed band saw. The programmable, fully automatic machine combines a pneumatic clamping system and hydraulic feed control to provide consistent, repeatable cuts.

It operates on 115-VAC, single-phase power and cuts lengths from 0.157 to 50 in. The pneumatic clamping system features an adjustable soft-squeeze, which holds material from thin-walled tubing to 7-in. round steel bars.

Solid-carbide drill requires less torque

Walter USA has introduced the DC160 X·treme Evo Advance solid-carbide universal drill. It has a deeper and larger chip gash at the point, requiring less torque and feed force. This also moves the second margin forward, allowing it to engage sooner and improving true position of the hole.

The four-margin drill provides improved stability in uneven entrances and exits, as well as cross holes or interruptions.

New-formulation welding wire, electrode deliver spatter-free deposits

Castolin Eutectic has introduced En-DOtec

®

DO∗327, a new-formulation welding wire, as well as the new electrode EutecTrode 6327 XHD for rebuilding and wear-facing applications.
