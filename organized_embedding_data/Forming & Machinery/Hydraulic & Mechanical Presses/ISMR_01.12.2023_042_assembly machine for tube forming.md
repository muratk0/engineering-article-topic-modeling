# Assembly machine for tube forming

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Danfoss Power Solutions has launched its new WalformConnect tube-forming machine for steel and stainless steel tubing in sizes ranging from 6-42mm.

"With an array of digital features, data tracking and cloud connectivity, the WalformConnect machine enables simple and safe hydraulic tube forming within the Walform®system, a cold-forming, metric, tubefitting system," it said.

The new machine reduces the risk of assembly errors with a barcode scanner that verifies the correct tooling to use. It also automatically indicates the compatible clamping jaw part number and tube parameters. The machine captures a range of real-time data (such as cycle times, machine temperature and tool-clamping status). Operators and supervisors can access information on what proportion of a production batch is complete, cycle time per order etc.

"The ability to monitor multiple machines in the cloud enables easier operational planning. In addition, historical production data and information on past orders can be saved on an individual machine or in the cloud, enabling easy local or remote analysis to further improve production," said the manufacturer.■
