# Grinding within reach

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

STRONG HAND TOOLS®, a manufacturer of welding clamps, magnets, pliers and BuildPro™ welding tables, has introduced a new grinder rest for convenient table-top, or table-edge, storage of handheld (4-1/2” or 5”) grinders.

The compact grinder rest holds the grinder, wrench and spare discs. A cable wrap hook enables safe storage of the Grinder electrical cord. The spare disc hook keeps discs within easy reach for quick change-outs and helps to prevent discs from getting lost or damaged. The grinder rest bracket has an open design that allows it to be easily placed for storage, yet retrieved quickly when needed. The four-hole wrench storage pattern accommodates a wide variety of wrenches.

“The grinder rest is suitable for keeping heavily used handheld grinders, and grinder components, safely stored near the work area for greater efficiency whether the work area is in the shop, or out in the field,” said the company. Two models are available – one with a strong magnetic base for placement on any flat steel surface, such as a worktable, or the universal C-clamp base for mounting on a table-edge, or table-leg.

## From top floor to shop floor

EPICOR SOFTWARE CORPORATION, a specialist in business software solutions for manufacturing, distribution, retail and services organisations, has announced the integration of its Epicor Mattec manufacturing execution system (MES) and next-generation enterprise resource planning (ERP) solution, Epicor ERP version 10. Out-of-the-box integration radically simplifies deployment and delivers faster time to value so users can quickly unlock the power of a closed-loop enterprise system that connects the top floor to the shop floor in real-time.

Epicor Mattec MES is an out-of-the-box configurable MES solution designed to meet operational challenges in manufacturing. The solution offers a comprehensive set of MES capabilities for production scheduling, machine operation and maintenance, quality management and real-time analytics to monitor machines and analyse machine-related data such as overall equipment effectiveness (OEE), run rates, scrap, yield and energy consumption. The system captures data directly from machines and operators, and delivers real-time production metrics and real-time operations analytics in a visual manner.

“One of the common challenges that manufacturing organisations face is the need to effectively connect planning and scheduling data from their ERP system with shop floor realities in one centralised system,” said Tom Muth, Senior Manager, Product Marketing, Epicor MES. “With the integration of Epicor ERP and Mattec MES, customers can achieve a holistic view of their business processes and core production, and better utilise shop floor data that is crucial for improving operational efficiency and maximising profitable manufacturing outcomes.”
