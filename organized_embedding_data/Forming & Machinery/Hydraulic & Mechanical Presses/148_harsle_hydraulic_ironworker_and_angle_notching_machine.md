## Definition
The Combined Punching and Shearing Machine is a machine tool that combines various functions such as metal cutting, punching, shearing, bending, etc. Hydraulic Ironworker It has the advantages of simple operation, low energy consumption ,and low maintenance cost.

The Combined Punching and Shearing Machine are divided into two types: Hydraulic Ironworker and Mechanical Ironworker. Among them, the hydraulic type is the most widely used.

## Characteristics
The Combined Punching and Shearing Machine are generally suitable for machining small workpieces and can work at multiple stations at the same time.

The left footswitch controls the punching station separately, and the right footswitch controls other operations at the same time.
