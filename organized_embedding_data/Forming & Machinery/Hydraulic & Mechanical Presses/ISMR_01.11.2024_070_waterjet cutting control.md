# Waterjet cutting control

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

IGEMS in Sweden offers products such as CAD/CAM & Nest software for waterjet cutting in both 2D and 3D (ranging from one-axis cutting to six-axis bevel cutting on a rotation axis), as well as a modern CNC for waterjets (together with Beckhoff).

Its software's key features include:

- A user-friendly interface: IGEMS is designed for ease of use, allowing operators of all skill levels to quickly learn and navigate the software.

- Advanced nesting capabilities: Nesting algorithms are designed to optimise material usage, minimise waste and maximise efficiency.

- Comprehensive toolpath generation: IGEMS provides precise toolpath generation, designed for high-quality cuts and reducing the need for manual adjustments.

- Integration with machines: The software is compatible with a wide range of waterjet cutting machines, making it versatile for different operational needs.

Customisable options: Users can tailor settings and workflows to fit specific project requirements to enhance flexibility.

"We offer customer support and extensive training resources, helping users to get the most out of the software. The software is regularly updated with new features and improvements, ensuring it stays current with industry trends. Overall, IGEMS combines ease of use, efficiency and adaptability for waterjet cutting operations," the company explained.

Its Tilter is a versatile 5x kinematic mechanism because it can handle 45° bevels and taper angle compensation. The IGEMS Rounder measures, calculates and compensates for deviation in five-axis waterjet cutting, calibrating to the cutting head.

"The IGEMS CNC Snapper takes detailed and accurate photos of the cutting table. The image provides the basis for optimal positioning in the NC program, saving time and minimizing material use. The IGEMS Straighter is a handy and advanced tool that clips onto the machine's mixing tube. It's a matter of seconds until the five-axis kinematic perpendicular angle is as straight as an arrow," it concluded.
