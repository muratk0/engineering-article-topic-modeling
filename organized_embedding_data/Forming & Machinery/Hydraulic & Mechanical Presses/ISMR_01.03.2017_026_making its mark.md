# Making its mark

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

TAIWANESE PRESS SPECIALIST, Lien Chieh Machinery Co., Ltd., will celebrate its 70th anniversary, as well as the first anniversary of its servo mechanical tandem press line, this year.

With tonnages ranging from 2400 to 1000 tons and a bolster area of 5,000 x 2,500mm, the press line is used in the automotive sheet metal industry. Designed to overcome the limits of traditional hydraulic presses (in terms of speed etc.), the manufacturer launched its first servo mechanical tandem line in 2016.

"Each hydraulic press that we design is in line with the special needs of our customers - we believe in tailored products and problem-solving. We therefore continuously conduct research and development. In 2015, we opened a new branch in Thailand and, in the future, intend to set up branch offices in Japan, and move towards the establishment of an international Press Group," it explained.
