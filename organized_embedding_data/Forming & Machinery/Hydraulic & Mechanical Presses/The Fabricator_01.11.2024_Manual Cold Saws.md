# Manual Cold Saws

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 The Fabricator]

The MACC Entry-level Manual Cold Saws are Equipped with a High Torque Electric Motor with Some Models offering Gear Reduction Units and Variable Speed Drives to Reduce the Saw Blade’s Rotational Speed while Maintaining Consistent Speed for the Heaviest of Loads. The NEW 300 EDV & NEW 350 EDV can cut up to 45° Left and up to 45° Right.

The Jet Separation System Keeps the Blade Oiled properly.

The Reduction Unit is in an Oil Bath with a Double Hinged Pin that has an Eccentric Bush.

Equipped with a Dual Quick-Locking Vice and a Sliding Counter-Vice for Longitudinal Cutting.
