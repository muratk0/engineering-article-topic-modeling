# 2500 ton tandem lines for automotive manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

A 2500-TON HD tandem press line, developed for the automotive industry by DEES Hydraulic Industrial Co, Ltd., is designed for easy maintenance, powered by the motor and pump set.

"Moving bolsters with Q.D.S. (quick die change system) enable easier and faster heavy die change. The tandem line offers pressing speeds of between 30 and 150mm/s, controlled by the accumulator, and approaching speeds of 800 mm/s or faster as well as return speeds of 600mm/s or faster. A cushion system knocks out the parts. All speeds and strokes, including the slide and cushion systems, are adjustable (repetitive is within ±0.05mm)," the Taiwanese press specialist told SMR.

DEES self-developed HD-FASTech technology is designed to resolve deep drawing's low efficiency, greatly improving production efficiency. The tandem line pictured is operational 24/7 and is being used by a major customer (over 10% market share) which, said DEES, is one of the top three automotive manufacturers. DEES supplies the custom hydraulic presses which are designed to be strong and robust enough for long operations.

"Ever since we started in 1976, the basic philosophy of DEES Hydraulic has been to provide customers with 'complete engineering, manufacturing and solutions'. Certified to ISO 9001 and CE, we produce safe and user-friendly interfaces for our hydraulic presses in metalforming working environments. Our hydraulic press capacity ranges from 10 to 6,000 tons, and we have supplied over 4500 customised hydraulic presses to 80 countries worldwide," added DEES.
