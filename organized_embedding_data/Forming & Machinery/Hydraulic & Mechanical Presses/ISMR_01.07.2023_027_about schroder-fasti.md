# About Schröder-Fasti

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Schröder-Fasti, part of the Schröder Group, creates innovative sheet-metal processing technologies. Its portfolio includes round bending machines; flaring machines; hydraulic plate shears; press brakes and pivoting bending machines. Branch offices are in Germany and Denmark.

Together, the companies Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH, Schröder-Fasti Technologie GmbH and Schröder-Fasti Scandinavia A/S form the umbrella brand Schröder Group.

The 134/15/2 slip bending roll.

The 134 can continuously bend tighter or wider radii and also produce tapered objects utilising individual control of the three rolls at Schröder-Fasti, RIETHO received a very interesting inquiry for components with tighter radii. As a result, an 80mm roll was ordered along with the standard 100mm roll.

"RIETHO therefore received two machines in one from us," said Christian Jarosch, sales representative at Schröder-Fasti Technologie GmbH from Wermelskirchen in North Rhine-Westphalia. "It is now possible to bend either full width 2mm steel sheets to about a 60mm radius or 1.5mm sheets to about a 50mm radius. We can only give approximate guide values, as every material behaves differently."

## The machine memory

Different sheet-metal materials and even differences between batches and roll directions are the subtleties to which a user must adjust when bending. The springback behaviour of stainless steel is particularly worth mentioning.

Florian Kuderer, one of the employees at RIETHO who works most frequently with the Schröder-Fasti machines, appreciates the CR80 PLC software control of the slip bending roll 134, the bending program memory and the incremental dimension correction.

"Many customers order the same products again and again. Setting up the machine for this is very quick because the bending program is stored and can be automatically adapted to the current material characteristics," he explained.

One of these recurring orders, for example, is a one-third shell with a 90mm radius which is required for the cladding of revolving doors.

As a machine operator of Schröder-Fasti slipbending rolls of various generations, Florian Kuderer knows the benefits such as the asymmetrical three-roll arrangement (which enables start-up and finish rounding in the machine, particularly short straight ends, and high concentricity of the tube closure).

He also appreciates progress in the software control. For example, the 134 can continuously bend tighter or wider radii and also produce tapered objects utilising individual control of the three rolls. The funnel-shaped tablet chutes for a medical technology company, which he produced with the machine, was only a small order (in terms of quantity) but also an example of the level of flexibility in sheet-metal forming with this latest addition to RIETHO's slipbending rolls.■
