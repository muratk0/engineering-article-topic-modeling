# Precision spray oiling

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

SPRAY OILING of work pieces in metal working has become increasingly important in the last few years. The downside of this new process is a high level of contamination of the machinery and pollution of ambient air due

to the formation of heavy spray mist. As a consequence, intricate extraction systems have to be installed.

With spray.xact, Technotrans AG has unveiled a flexible and easily adaptable technology that is used for the precise, automatic spray oiling of work pieces in metal working and pressing applications, thereby preventing spray mists. The modular system has a simple and compact design. It can be easily retrofitted into punching, cutting or deep-drawing machines.

“Economical and precise dosing of the oil is ensured by a valve that pulsates at a high frequency and is equipped with a single-product spray nozzle. The spray jet is generated without compressed air. In standard systems, compressed air encourages the formation of spray mist. In the Technotrans system, compressed air is only used for the media supply via a dosing piston pump. As a result, the energy consuming and cost intensive consumption of compressed air is reduced to a minimum. The nozzles can be actuated individually,” added the company.

“The beginning and end of the spraying process as well as quantity and frequency are controlled by the control system in a jobrelated manner. These parameters can be modified even during the running process. This enables the spraying of patterns and the nearly loss-free oiling of asymmetric work pieces. Extraction is not required. The simple design of the spray.xact system is particularly eye-catching: all of the valves of a spray bar are supplied via a common supply line and connected in series using hoses. There is no need for sophisticated hose and cable packages. Individual nozzles and valves can be replaced easily and without the use of tools.” ■
