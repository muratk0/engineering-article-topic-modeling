# Rapid die change

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

FORWELL'S QUICK DIE Change System (QDCS) is an automatic die changing system that can be specifically designed for hydraulic and mechanical power presses and press production lines. This system automates the die changing process and is designed to provide increased speed, efficiency and productivity to a workshop. The Quick Die Change System not only reduces die change time, but also promotes diverse small batch production runs and streamlines production management.

"Our Quick Die Change System (QDCS) includes die clamps, dielifters, die arms, control box and power units. It is designed to reduce die change time, saving labour costs and increasing safety. The QDCS works in conjunction with various mould clamping systems for automatic punching presses; injection moulding machines; die casting machines; PCB fine piercing and die clamping systems," the Taiwanese manufacturer told ISMR.

"It provides maximum flexibility to accommodate diversification in presses and dies with its serialised design concept of components and equipment. All dies can be changed without any modification, enabling the use of standardised die and press equipment, also saving cost. Multiple presses can share a single Quick Die Change System, further economising on costs and resulting in higher efficiency and productivity. The Quick Die Change System is controlled by hydraulics. Even if the power is off, the system can keep working for 48 hours."
