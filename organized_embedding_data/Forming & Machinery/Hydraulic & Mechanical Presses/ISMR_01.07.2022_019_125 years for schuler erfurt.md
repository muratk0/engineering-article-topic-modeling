# 125 years for Schuler Erfurt

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Press specialist Schuler's Erfurt site is celebrating a key anniversary: 125 years ago, businessman Henry Pels started selling machine tools there. This was followed in 1899 by construction of the new factory at Schwerborner Strasse, where Schuler's central production plant in Europe is still based.

The press manufacturer celebrated its birthday with a large summer party. It all started in 1897 with machine tool distribution. Today, Schuler's central production plant for presses in Europe is in eastern Germany.■

The Erfurt factory in Germany produces many different presses for the Schuler Group today, from press lines to automatic stamping presses. © Schuler.
