# AIDA-America at FABTECH 2023

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

## Servo near-fine blanking

AIDA-America will also partner with Dallas Industries; Pax Products, Inc; Pacesetter Systems and Tower Metalworking Fluids to demonstrate its new DSF-N2-3000AB, 300-ton straightside servo press as part of a full progressive die production line blanking near-fine parts.

"The demonstration will show how servo near-fine blanking can produce a 90% + burnished surface with a straight edge, exceeding the typical 70%-80% burnished edge requirement of many applications. This two-point, direct-drive DSF-N2-AB servo press includes the exclusive AIDA/Allen-Bradley servo control system (locally developed, manufactured and supported by a team of AIDA Controls engineers based in Dayton, OH)," said AIDA.

"Control system standard features include Common Industrial Protocol (CIP) safe technology and a userfriendly, ergonomically designed, swing-arm mounted, 12-inch colour touch-screen HMI with optical run buttons for ease of use. A handheld step-feed (CNC handwheel) slide motion control is also included for reduced set-up time. DSF-N2-AB Series servo presses combine the rigidity of a unitised frame with the flexibility of freely programmable servo motion," added AIDA. Like all AIDA servo presses, the DSF-N2-3000AB press includes an AIDA-designed and built low-speed, high-torque servo motor specifically manufactured for stamping press applications. Additional standard DSF-N2-AB features include NEMA 13 rated floorstanding cabinets with flexible placement options; a pneumatic safety brake integrated with the GuardLogix safety controller for an additional, redundant layer of safety; a full-length, six-point slide guide system and a recirculating oil lubrication system. DSF-N2-AB Series presses are offered in 200- and 300-ton models.

## Direct-drive, gap frame servo press

AIDA's DSF-C1-1500A direct-drive, gapframe servo press will be blanking parts as well as demonstrating AIDA servo press programmability and flexibility. Visitors to AIDA's booth will see the optimised servo silent blanking stroke motion, which can only be done on a servo press. This 150-ton DSF-C1-A press will showcase the exclusive AIDA/Allen-Bradley control, with many of the same features as the DSF-N2-AB Series control system.

Additional DSF-C1-A Series press features are a servo motor with 5-10% higher speeds than AIDA's previousgeneration, gap frame servo presses; a capacitor-based Energy Conservation and Optimisation (ECO) system that reduces peak energy draw and an Electronic Overload Protection (EOLP) system that monitors applied torque throughout the stroke to prevent overloading. DSF-C1-A Series presses are offered in a range of tonnages from 80 to 250.

The AIDA/Allen-Bradley-based servo press control for servo presses from 315-3,500 tons will also be on display. This fully operational and interactive 19-inch, PC touch-screen Allen-Bradley control will demonstrate several servo press stroke motion profiles in real-time on a virtual press.
