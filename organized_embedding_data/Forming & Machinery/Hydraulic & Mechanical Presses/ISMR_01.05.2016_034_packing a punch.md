# Packing a punch

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

MEUSBURGER has launched a new cutting punch of high-quality H40S carbide steel, manufactured according to DIN 9861.

“The material presents a compromise between hardness and fracture toughness to enable long service life. The best dimensional accuracy is guaranteed through the precision-ground shank of the cutting punch. The soldered head of steel allows for easy finishing without special tools,” said the company.

The E 5543 Cutting punch is available in diameters from 1 to 5mm and a length of 71mm and covers a wide application range. See www.meusburger.com ■
