# About Worcester Presses

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Worcester Presses is a leading UK provider of mechanical presses; hydraulic presses; processing equipment and press transfer robot lines. It also provides its own-manufactured Worcester mechanical presses; Chin Fong mechanical power presses; Yeh Chiun hydraulic presses and Tomac coil processing equipment, as well as a variety of press shop ancillary equipment.

Its sales team can specify individual machines and full turnkey packages, tailored to specific requirements. The service department provides technical backup, routine preventative maintenance and service contracts with emphasis on rapid response. Central stores enables it to issue most spares from stock.

The GTX 500-D is the latest installation in what has been one of Worcester Presses' busiest years, supplying more than 20 machines all over the UK. This has seen its turnover accelerate towards £4 million and driven the need for new appointments in its administrative and engineering teams. Investment in a new CNC machine has given the Dudley-based company the capability to manufacture replacement parts for its presses quickly and efficiently.
