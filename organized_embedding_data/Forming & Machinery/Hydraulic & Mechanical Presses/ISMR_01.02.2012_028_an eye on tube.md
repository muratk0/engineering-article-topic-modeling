# An eye on tube

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

GERMAN MECHANICAL engineering company TRACTO-TECHNIK (TT) develops and manufactures machines for trenchless pipeline installation and renewal as well as machines, measuring techniques and software solutions for tube fabrication. Established in 1962, the company presently has about 500 employees at national and international locations.

Company founder Paul Schmidt originally invented such a machine for handling hydraulic tubes. The device was named "TUBOMAT" and integrated all functions necessary for hydraulic tubing in one flexible and mobile unit: sawing, internal and external deburring, bending, flaring, assembling of cutting-rings and chamfering of tubes from 6 to 42mm outside diameters.

Today TT's tube fabrication product range comprises allelectric and electro-hydraulic CNC tube bending machines, semi-automatic tube benders, tube end formers, assembly machines for hydraulic tubing and tube measuring systems as well as software solutions for workflow management of tube & pipe shops. Its latest developments are the TUBOTRON VARIO 40 RL tube bending machine and the TUBOSCAN S optical tube measuring system.

The TUBOTRON VARIO 40 RL all-electric, multi-stack CNC tube bending machine is able to do right-and-left hand bending, integrates rotary-draw bending and freeform bending and is suitable for bending complex tube and pipe shapes in series production. The TUBOSCAN S optical measuring device is able to precisely determine geometric tube data within a matter of seconds. ## The new TBA 400 Ecoline

GEORG’S TRANSFORMER division has recognized the growth in energy markets, particularly in Asia, Africa, South- and Middle America, and launched a new lamination cutting line. The TBA400 Ecoline has been designed for the efficient production of low-loss step-lap transformer cores.

Production cost savings of 35% have been achieved, says Georg, adding that for this line model, there is no necessity for special machine foundations or a loop pit. The standardised line is equipped with a double head decoiler, one hole punch and one V-notch as well as two fixed shears and two optional basic sheet stopping systems (RS/T and HS/T). The TBA400 Ecoline is designed for sheet widths of between 40 to 440mm.

More than 900 production lines have been supplied by Georg to the transformer industry worldwide. Its advanced coil processing lines, special purpose machine tools and production lines for the transformer industry are in operation globally. www.Georg.com
