# Volkswagen Commercial Vehicles starts new press line

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

VOLKSWAGEN COMMERCIAL VEHICLES has officially put into operation a new Schuler servo press line in Hanover, Germany. The line consists of six individual presses and has a total press force of 91,000 kilonewtons. It is driven by 15 torque motors with a total torque of 600,000 newton meters.

With a speed of 17 strokes per minute, the servo press line will be used, for example, to produce parts for the T6 Volkswagen bus. When producing four parts per stroke, the line has a maximum output performance of 68 parts per minute. At the head of the line are two robots - they alternately remove blanks from the stack and place them on a conveyor belt. The camera of an optical centring station calculates the exact position of the blanks so that the Crossbar Feeder can place them accurately into the first press.

“As soon as the die opens after the first forming stage, the next Crossbar Feeder glides in to pick up and remove the part using its suction cups – just fractions of a second before the next blank falls into the die. The parts can therefore be transported within seconds from one press to the next as they gradually develop their final shape,” said Schuler.

The Schuler line features pause and standby mode, but also a device to regulate the temperature of cooling water and a usage-based compressor control system designed to reduce energy needs. The press operators can centrally monitor and control all consumption levels. This also includes power consumption.

With a total weight of 3,300 metric tons, a width of 24 metres and a length of 94 metres, it took six months to assemble the complete line in Hanover – in a space in which 215 Volkswagen Amaroks could easily be parked.

## WILA B.V. opens sales office in China

IN ADDITION to offices in the Netherlands and USA, press brake tooling specialist WILA recently opened a third sales office in China. The office in Taicang, Jiangsu Province will focus on further expanding WILA's market position in China.

“Increasing demand for higher productivity in the sheet metal industry, in combination with our commitment to serve our global customers, has led to the establishment of Jiangsu WILA Press Brake Tooling Systems CO.,LTD. We have been specializing in tool holders (clamping, crowning, bottom tool holders), tooling and accessories for more than 80 years now, with the goal of reducing set-up time and improving the accuracy of the press brake,” WILA told ISMR. An eye on EMO

FROM 5 TO 10 OCTOBER 2015, the machine tool industry gathered in Milan, Italy to attend the EMO Milano exhibition. Around 1600 companies presented their latest technology on 120,000 square metres of

exhibition area, and the metalworking world was shown from every angle including breakthrough technologies such as additive manufacturing.

Promoted by CECIMO, the European Association of Machine Tool Industries, and organised by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, EMO MILANO 2015 registered 155,362 visits over the six exhibition days, from visitors representing 120 countries. 51% of the visitors were from outside Italy.

Institutional and B2B meetings, presentations, press conferences, conventions and technical seminars also took place during EMO, which was held alongside EXPO, the Universal exhibition.
