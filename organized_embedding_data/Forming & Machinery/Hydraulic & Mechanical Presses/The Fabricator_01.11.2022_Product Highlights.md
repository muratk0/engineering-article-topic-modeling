Angle grinders feature optimized armature for faster heat dissipation

Metabo Corp. has introduced three models of its new MBrush angle grinder. The tools are made for use in production welding and fabricating environments. Each features a 1,900-W and 14.5-amp motor with a newly optimized armature for increased power and faster heat dissipation, says the company.

The 6-in. WEP 19-150 Quick M-Brush has a no-load speed of 9,600 RPM and comes with a standard guard and a clip-on type 1 cutting wheel guard. The 5-in. WEPBA 19-125 Quick DS M-Brush has a no-load speed of 11,000 RPM, while the 6-in. WEPBA 19-150 Quick DS M-Brush no-load speed is 9,600 RPM. These two tools include auto-balance technology.

High-duty-cycle lift tables offer extended time between maintenance

For applications that require frequent cycling (lifting and lowering) of lift tables, Southworth offers high-duty-cycle packages that help increase recommended maintenance intervals from 50,000 cycles to 200,000 cycles.

The increase in cycles is attributed to upgraded, more durable components, such as high-strength, oversized alloy shafts and pins that are turned, ground, and polished; hardened, regreaseable cam followers; steel roller wear strips; and an upper limit switch.

These tables are suitable for integration with other handling systems, like conveyors and sortation systems, when standard maintenance intervals could create unnecessary system downtime and production bottlenecks. They also are designed for use in congested areas or where maintenance access is limited or difficult.

Southworth Products

www.southworthproducts.com

Compact, enclosed waterjet system delivers 5-axis parts

Flow has developed the all-new EchoJet in the 1313-sized format configured with the HyPlex Prime direct-drive pump. The fully enclosed waterjet system is designed to help improve operator efficiency while protecting the surrounding environment from water and abrasive. This compact unit mirrors the traditional machine tool look and feel, with the flexibility to cut various materials and deliver 5-axis parts.

The gantry design helps optimize the work envelope while minimizing overall footprint. Other features include improved part accessibility, easy-close sliding doors, and worktable lighting.

Flow

www.flowwaterjet.com

Automated material storage and retrieval systems designed for tube lasers

BLM GROUP USA has introduced a new automated tube storage and retrieval system that integrates with its tube laser systems using cassettes that can be loaded directly inside the machine.

The storage system automatically retrieves the correct material, brings it to the machine, and automatically loads it. When a job is complete, the system automatically removes the used cassette with any remaining stock to be placed back into the tower. The system automatically checks the stock level and updates the warehouse management software.

For additional flexibility, the manufacturer offers a second retrieval system style for use with a central warehouse. With this system, the desired bundle is extracted automatically from the storage tower and staged near the machine in a queue for either crane or forklift loading.

Updates made to offline robot programming software

Hypertherm Associates introduced Robotmaster 2023, a version update of its offline programming software for robots. The software integrates seamlessly with the user’s chosen CAD/CAM system, eliminating the need to import and sync data and reducing programming time and errors.

The new version allows for the creation of libraries to store and re-use robotic cell components. Users can preview and select components within the library and save modified or proprietary components for later use.

Improvements have been added that support the latest CAD formats and include enhancements to the CAM modules, user interface, and user experience to facilitate the creation of trajectories and further improve programming.

Bio-based fluid offers nontoxic corrosion protection

Renewable Lubricants has introduced Bio-Rust Preventative, a bio-based fluid that provides nontoxic, VOC-free, sustainable corrosion prevention on ferrous and galvanized steel. The nonstaining, water-displacing formulation provides a waxy film that protects ferrous materials in salt and acid-fume (hydrochloric acid) environments and against humidity.

The biodegradable fluid meets the corrosion protection film form MILPRF-16173, Class II, Grade 3, according to the company. It provides more than 120 hours of protection in a HCI fume test and more than 80 hours in a 5% salt spray (fog) ASTM D117 test.

In addition to corrosion protection, it can be used as a nonchlorinated metalworking fluid for stamping and drawing applications.
