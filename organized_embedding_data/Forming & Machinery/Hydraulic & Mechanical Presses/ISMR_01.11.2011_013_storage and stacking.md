## AUTOMATED STORAGE SYSTEMS

Automated storage systems cater for the entire internal logistics of sheet metal from semi-automatic, compact storage systems up to high-performance storage centres including handling units and conveyors for automatic supply of sheet metal to processing machines such as turret punch presses, laser profilers, plasma cutting centres and waterjet cutting machines.

Compact Lift is a simple, semi-automatic sheet storage system for small, medium and large format sheet. Sheet can be stored either on pallets or on cantilever arms. Ergonomic, simple operating elements promote safety and ease of use.

Tower Storage Systems are also compact, designed as a single or double tower to store relatively small quantities, and may be used as a buffer in flexible manufacturing systems. The towers have a large storage to footprint ratio to heights of 20 metres. Up to 100 storage locations accept loads totalling up to five tonnes. Flat and bar products as well as pallets can be handled and stored in addition to sheet.

Up to 30 automatic picks per hour results in highly productive material supply to machine tools or dispatch. Longitudinal or lateral stations with carts can be installed for handling, as well as an unpacking or commissioning table.

Gantry storage systems, due to their flexible arrangement and the design of the storage, are suitable for small-to-medium size industrial facilities. The gantry design, with an operating crane moving over the top of the store, allows easy and economic extension when capacity needs to be increased quickly. System heights of three to eight metres are possible and almost any number of levels may be specified.

Longitudinal storage systems, such as KASTO’s Uniflex, are designed as a single- or double-sided facility starting with around 200 single-deep storage locations for pallets of all sheet sizes. These systems can be built either as a rack-supported silo-construction up to 25 metres high with roof and wall cladding, or as a stand-alone storage unit in an existing building.

Honeycomb system storage is mainly used in steel and metal distribution centres as for water jet or laser processing of large sheets. The characteristics of these automatic, high-performance cassette systems are high storage density, synchronous vertical and horizontal movement of the gantry crane and optimised, intelligent storage according to whether the stock is fast, medium or slow moving.

As an example of machine-supported storage and stacking systems, Bystronic’s new ByTower storage tower permits optimal use of a machine’s capacity by automating the work process and enabling lightly manned production. This means that the flow of material proceeds without the need for operator intervention and the machine’s shuttle table still remains completely accessible.

However, the user benefits not only from the autonomous execution of work processes but also in the form of considerably more flexible sheet metal processing. This is because various material types and thicknesses are available in the shortest possible time. Cut material is simply returned to the ByTower for storage. The user can decide how many cassettes are to be used for storing the material and how many for the return transfer of cut parts. The metal sheets can be stored in the cassettes either with or without the wooden pallets. All Bystronic laser cutting systems with a cutting area of three metres by one and a half metres, can be equipped with the ByTower.

At BlechExpo 2011, the ByTower was demonstrated with eleven cassettes, which together with the ByTrans Extended and the automated nozzle centering, transforms a BySpeed Pro laser cutting system into a fully automated laser cutting cell. ■
