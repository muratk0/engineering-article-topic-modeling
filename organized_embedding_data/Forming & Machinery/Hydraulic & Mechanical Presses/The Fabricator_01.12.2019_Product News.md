Hand-held analyzer measures carbon and equivalents

SciAps has introduced the hand-held Z-200 LIBS analyzer, which it says is the first LIBS unit that differentiates steels and stainless by carbon content and verifies carbon equivalents for weldability of metals.

With this tool, the laboratory technique of laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy has been miniaturized into a 4-lb., battery-operated, hand-held unit. The analyzer reports a carbon result, other alloying elements, and carbon equivalents in 5 to 6 sec. It also verifies low-carbon grades of stainless and nickel alloys and analyzes cast irons and cast steels.

SH3636 Blasting machine from Viking allows for continuous part processing

Viking has introduced the SH3636 Spinner Hanger blaster. The unit has a 36- by 36-in. working area chamber and two VK PowerMax 15-HP blast wheels running at 3,525 RPM. The wheel has a capacity of 310 lbs. of abrasive per minute with a velocity of about 295 FPS.

The blast cabinet rotates a primary drum with two compartments to allow for continuous processing of parts for manual loading and unloading of the front compartment without system shutdown. The drum is lined with ¼-in. manganese alloy on the sides and ½-in. cast chrome/moly directly in front of the blast wheels. Each compartment contains one fixture in front of the blast wheels with a hook capacity of 750 lbs.

Internal mix atomizing spray nozzles conserve liquid

Exair’s new 1/2 NPT No Drip internal mix atomizing spray nozzles positively stop liquid flow when compressed air is shut off, eliminating the possibility of drips. Liquid and air mix inside the nozzle, producing fine atomization of liquids up to 300 centipoise.

The nozzles are available in four patterns: narrow-angle round, wide-angle round, flat fan, and 360-degree hollow circular. They are suitable for pressure-fed applications that don’t require independent air and liquid control.

Exair Corp.

www.exair.com

Automated digital hardness testers offer accuracy at all test loads to 0.5 percent

The L.S. Starrett Co. has introduced two new digital Rockwell/superficial Rockwell benchtop hardness testers (models 3823 and 3824) with fully automated load/unload procedures. The testers use a closed-loop control unit with a load cell, a DC motor, and an electronic measurement and control unit instead of traditional dead weights, enabling high-accuracy measurements at all test loads up to 0.5 percent.

Model 3824 has an automated Z axis. The user presses the Start key, and the entire test process is completed automatically. The testers feature programmable scale conversions, dwell times, statistical capabilities, and a test counter. They can test in 30 different Rockwell hardness scales for carbon steel, alloy steel, cast iron, and nonferrous metals.

Thermomechanical treatment for steel helps improve material properties

Steeltec has developed Xtreme Performance (XTP

®

) thermomechanical treatment for steel. It imparts a fine-grained steel microstructure and improved material properties without the need for alloying elements or additional heat treatment, reports the company.

Using this technology, the manufacturer can modify standard steels to give them improved low-temperature toughness and strength. Precipitation-hardened ferrite-pearlite steels can be used at temperatures down to -60 degrees C, while bainite steels can handle temperatures as low as -100 degrees C.

Hand-held resistance weld checker offered

Amada Miyachi America Inc.’s MM-410A hand-held resistance weld checker is suitable for process monitoring and troubleshooting. The compact unit supports a variety of resistance welding technologies, including AC, DC inverter, AC inverter, transistor, and capacitive discharge.

Featuring a simple and intuitive user interface and color touch panel display, the unit provides information on current, voltage, weld time, and force. It can be used to correlate waveform and numeric data with process results and provide detailed weld data for process optimization and validation.

If used continuously in a production environment, it reduces scrap by detecting drifts in the weld process and alerting operators before process failure. The unit also reduces the frequency of destructive testing and provides an independent way of monitoring the welding power supply by detecting any drifts in welding power supply calibration.

Flexovit USA Inc. has introduced Capstone™, a patent-pending cupwheel grinding wheel developed as an alternative product to Type 6 and Type 11 cupwheels. Portable, automated DCC CMM introduced for shop floor inspection

zCAT and Verisurf Software Inc. have introduced a portable and automated system for quality inspection and reporting. It combines the compact zCAT DCC CMM with Verisurf model-based measurement and inspection software.

The 30-lb. CMM is designed for in-process inspection, so the same person making the part can measure the part. Users can teach the machine what they want to measure and then permit the device to take over with an accuracy comparable to that of larger, nonportable CMMs, according to the company.

Orbital wrapper features multipallet accumulation zones

TAB Industries LLC has introduced multipallet accumulation zones as an option on its fully automated TAB Wrapper Tornado Perfect Storm wrapping systems.

The zones set modular, powered conveyors at the infeed with a proprietary sensor array that recognizes the presence of a pallet load to be wrapped. The wrapper automatically feeds the pallet load from the accumulation zone onto the infeed conveyor and then into the center ring for wrapping and to discharge in a continuous process, automatically adjusting the wrapping to the dimensions of the pallet load.

With multiple accumulation zone extensions available, the orbital wrapping system enables lift truck operators to unload and stage multiple pallet loads for wrapping, then tend to other tasks without waiting for loads in process to be wrapped and without stopping to run the system.

WLAN and mobile devices establish wireless communications via PC USB port

The new CU8210 series WLAN and mobile devices from Beckhoff Automation establish wireless communication via the USB port of an industrial PC. They also provide all necessary physical protection for components that stick out of control cabinets.

The C8210-M001 cabinet dome features an IP66 protection rating when installed. The components inside the housing dome, such as the USB-2.0 stick for wireless connection, are completely protected against physical contact, dust, and moisture in industrial environments. The cabinet dome can therefore be mounted both in the panel of the control cabinet and directly on the machine or outside of control cabinets.
