# Fladder Danmark A/S

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.fladder.com

At EuroBLECH this year, Danish deburring manufacturer, Fladder, will be presenting its GYRO deburring machine series and showcasing some new developments: the FLADDER®CC deburring system with concentric unit, carousel bed and stationary, manual 400/GS machine and free abrasive tool. It is suitable for use with a robot.

“FLADDER®GYRO can be used for multiple applications: traditional deburring with abrasive brushes and the removal of oxides via spring-threaded cylinders. It is controlled by frequency converters that reduce energy costs for manufacturers. An energy-saving system is optional; it shuts down the machine automatically in three steps to prevent it from running idle. Uniform deburring is possible due to the oscillating and rotating gearhead with the six in-pair counter-rotating spindles," said the deburring specialist.

## Pivatic Oy

## Pivatic Oy

manufactures punching and bending lines for automatic sheetmetal production. The company's focus is "to deliver tailored, high-performance, reliable solutions to ensure the most productivity and customer satisfaction."

"For EuroBLECH this year, we will be showcasing a fully automated servo-electric coil punching, cutting and pressing line on the stand. Highly efficient and indexable Thick Turret punching, combined with a separate cutting station and a press tool station of 1200kN punching force, allows jobs (such as hole group, embossing, corner notching and trimming) to be flexibly produced," explained the Finnish manufacturer.

“Energy consumption for the full line is between 12-18 kWh, depending upon the use of the big press, which is 60-70% less compared to traditional systems. This supports our low-energy product solutions towards global sustainable development," it concluded.

## GIZELIS SA

www.gizelis.com/en/

GIZELIS SA, set up in 1968, produces machines and solutions for the sheet metal industry and will present, at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition, two new innovations.

"We are proud to combine innovation and productivity in these systems; our patented electric shear, G-ELECTROCUT, as well as our range of press brakes, G-BEND PLUS and G-FLEX. We will also demonstrate full compatibility and connectivity with Industry 4.0 protocols," it explained.

G-BEND PLUS press brake features a single-axis back gauge system and bending length of 3400mm/4400mm. The G-FLEX press brake has a hydraulic system, two-axis back gauge system and CNC crowning. The new G-ELECTROCUT hydraulic swing beam shears feature a sheet support system that uses a hybrid belt/pneumatic drive to bring full-length pieces to the front of the shear to increase productivity.

In 2004, Gizelis S.A. formed a strategic alliance with Boschert GmbH & Co KG (see separate Boschert entry in this hall section).

## Produtech Srl

www.produtech.it/en

## Produtech is an

Italian sheet-metal punching and laser cutting machine supplier, which has specialised since 1993 in the production of coil-fed machines.

"Using an innovative approach, we were able to understand the market's need for more flexible and productive lines. For this reason, we decided to manufacture machines with coil-fed processing mode, rather than with traditional metal sheet feeding. Competence, reliability and technical expertise, with a great focus on customer needs, are our core business values which have allowed us to grow and strengthen our presence worldwide," it told ISMR.

“Our lines match laser-cutting and/or punching-forming technologies with coil-fed working modes, offering productivity benefits over traditional sheet-fed lines. Each line is a unique solution and is customised according to users' productivity needs," it added.
