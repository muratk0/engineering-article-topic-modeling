# Double-sheet detection

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

The lightweight SHX sensor bracket has been designed by Roland Electronic GmbH for the effective integration of double-sheet sensors in vacuum tooling. SHX is also suitable for retrofitting.

“Utilising a central spring combined with a belt, the bracket does not need any guides. As a result, this solution offers almost ideal operating behaviour. Placement on sloping TWB stacks is easy to manage; placing is convenient, so that collisions of the sensor or the sensor plug with the upper plate are a thing of the past, even with the most sloping stacks," explained the manufacturer.

“An essential property of a good sensor bracket is the mobility of the suction plate. In the extended state, this is a basic requirement for performing reliable doublesheet control. However, this mobility [must be offset against the requirement to be] as stiff as possible in the extended state. The spring-belt combination meant that this challenge was solved with the SHX sensor bracket. A huge benefit is that guiding is not necessary," it added.

Wear and tear, as well as jamming, are things of the past, according to Roland Electronic GmbH. Significant weight saving is possible, as the spring-belt system has a small mass. In the extended state, the bracket is designed for acceleration in the horizontal 2g direction. Component-transfer is therefore no longer a limiting factor.

“The SHX sensor bracket, in combination with the high-performance sensors of our E20 double-sheet control system, finally solves known application issues, so that reliable double-sheet control is guaranteed. This avoids costly damage and downtime," concluded the manufacturer.
