# Punching process productivity

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

“Nitrogen cylinders score over mechanical springs when you need large batches and precision production. If it needs to be even smaller, precise, faster and more durable, manifold plates are the best choice," tooling specialist, STEINEL, told /SMR.

Nitrogen cylinders are used when processing thin material and narrow strip in production, as they require less installation space and offer constant hold-down forces to ensure consistently high production quality. STEINEL nitrogen cylinders are designed to ensure long service life using innovative design details such as lifetime lubrication and two-point piston support.

"Several nitrogen cylinders can be combined to form composite tube systems, which always ensure a balanced force ratio for greater process reliability. Composite plate systems offer the same advantages with a more robust and compact design. They connect nitrogen cylinders and control fittings via holes in the composite plate. The increased nitrogen volume results in an even flatter increase in force. To make the right forces available in the right place, composite plate systems can have several circuits with different pressures," explained STEINEL.

Detail of a manifold plate with three independent pressure circuits.

## Manifold plates

"Our manifold plates are suitable for complex tools with high long-life quality. The design of the manifold plate is always tool-specific. It features a very low increase in force and a constant maximum force, which leads to consistent production quality and extremely long lifetime of the tool itself and the active elements. Manifold plates are also easy to maintain and install, which minimises downtime," Christian Borgert, Head of Sales/Marketing, STEINEL, emphasised.

The development and manufacture of these systems require specialised expertise. STEINEL engineers develop and optimise each manifold plate using the finite element method (FEM) to precisely calculate the influence of heat and forces. As one of the few global manufacturers of manifold plates, STEINEL adheres to strict safety and quality standards, and offers a consulting service to determine the right nitrogen concept to meet respective requirements.

"In summary, manifold plates offer a superior solution for applications that require high packing density, maximum process reliability and long service life. They are a key component in modern punching and bending processes, especially for the production of precision parts in large batches," concluded Christian Borgert.
