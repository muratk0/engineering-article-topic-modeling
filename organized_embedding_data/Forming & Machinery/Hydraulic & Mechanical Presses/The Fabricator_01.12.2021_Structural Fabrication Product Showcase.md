Plasma cutting system includes smart automation

Kinetic offers the K5200XMC plasma cutting system, which the company says delivers best-inclass speed and accuracy. With plasma cutting, oxyfuel cutting, beveling, and a vertical machining center built into the cutting gantry, the system can cut, mill, drill, and tap in a single setup.

With the addition of an automated part unloading system, it can process material day after day with minimal downtime. While the machine cuts at one end of the table, the smart unloading system can sort and palletize parts at the other end.

Magnets for fabrication, metalworking, lifting

Industrial Magnetics Inc. manufactures magnetic assemblies and tools for a variety of industrial steel applications using rareearth magnets, ceramic magnets, and electromagnets.

Its end-of-arm tooling magnets use rare-earth permanent magnet circuitry to pick and place metal parts, providing material handling safety while minimizing shop air costs. The magnets often are used in automated transfer and robotic pick-andplace systems or lift-assist material handling applications.

The Transporter models can handle a variety of stamped, forged, hydroformed, and assembled parts. Transporter LPs are designed to replace vacuum cups with minor tooling and adjustments. Permanent, electromagnet, and combination air knife fanners provide steel fanning and separation for stacking and destacking applications, reducing the likelihood of die-damaging double-blanking problems in automated sheet handling operations.

Industrial Magnetics Inc.

Machine delivers unattended plate processing

The V310 from Voortman Steel Machinery is a machine that can drill, cut, mark, tap, and mill in one run and at one station. It offers fully automated or unattended plate processing, including milling small or large slotted holes where cutting is not an option or a variety of shapes with a range of milling depths.

A dashboard shows the operator when to be at the machine for manual intervention; otherwise, the machine runs unattended, and the operator is free to work in other areas of the shop.

Press brakes offer bending lengths to 60 ft. in singlemachine configuration

The Optima series press brakes from Ursviken range from 400 to 5,000+ tons up to 60 ft. long in a single-machine configuration. With tandem machines, even longer bending lengths are available.

Automated tooling changing, CNC variable die tools, angle measurement systems, and material handling automation are engineered to help increase machine efficiency for heavy-plate applications.

For applications that require perfect fitment such as crane arms, the patented FlexiCrown system allows for the fine CNC adjustment of each wedge for a perfect crown every time, states the company.

Cutting machine includes new onboard controller

Messer Cutting Systems offers the Element 400 cutting machine, which includes the Global Connect onboard controller with redesigned HMI software and integrated OmniFab compatibility. This configuration helps improve real-time transparency on maintenance, downtime status, and production reports that are transmitted through the controller, via a PC, or tablet.

This machine can be equipped with options such as plasma bevel cutting, oxyfuel cutting, marking, and cutting tables.

Plate bending rolls can be equipped with upgraded NC

Cole-Tu v e ’ s Sahinler Metal Makina fourroll plate bending rolls can be equipped with an upgraded numerical control as an alternative to the more expensive CNC. Elliptical shapes can be rolled with the upgraded NC because it comes with additional pumps and software that keep the rolls moving throughout the bending process, thereby eliminating flat spots.

The standard machines are featured with three LED readouts, and the NC, upgraded NC, and CNC are available as optional equipment.

Cole-Tuve is the exclusive North American distributor for Sahinler and sells a complete line of fabricating machinery.

Disposable liners keep heads dry in hard hats

NoSweat has developed disposable liners for hard hats that absorb sweat instantly. The thin, peel-and-stick liners offer a universal fit in all brands and makes of hard hats and bump caps and comply with major safety standards requirements.

SweatLock technology locks up to 2 oz. of moisture inside the liner. Depending on the person and activities being performed, each liner can last anywhere from one use up to two weeks.

Electric forklift offers environmentally friendly lifting to 5 tons

Combilift has launched the Combi-XLE electric, multidirectional forklift with up to 5-ton lifting capacity. It incorporates high ground clearance; large, cushioned front and rear tires; and a spacious cab.

The forklift features patented all-wheel traction to help reduce tire wear and load swing and enhance braking. Also included is a newly developed, patented Eco-Steer system for a small turning radius.
