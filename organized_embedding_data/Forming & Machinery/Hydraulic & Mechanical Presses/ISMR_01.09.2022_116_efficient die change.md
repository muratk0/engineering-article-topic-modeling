# Efficient die change

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Where forming presses and punches are at work, the entire process chain is trimmed for maximum output. But with decreasing batch sizes, the number of interruptions due to die or mould changes is increasing. A way to how to handle these efficiently will be shown by Roemheld at Stand K65 in Hall 27 at the EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition in Hanover, Germany, from 25-28 October 2022. The manufacturer is a market specialist in dieclamping technology for sheet metal forming.

"Consistently high productivity is enabled by individual complete systems for efficient die change. A new feature is a pull-push system that moves dies weighing up to 50t in and out of the press via a chain drive. It is screwed directly to the press bed, where the chain transports the die at a speed of 33mm/s. Faster versions with a maximum of 200mm/s are also available. A separate guide is usually not required. The system is supplied without control as standard; the control is available as an option," explained Roemheld.

There are different versions for different spatial situations in the press. If the press bed is high enough, the chain is mounted hanging downwards in the storage unit. Since the chain box is open at the bottom, it is also insensitive to dirt. However, if the press bed height is too low to store the chain, magazine storage is available where the chain is stored in a spiral. The system offers additional flexibility because it can be mounted in front of, or behind, the press bed or on mobile consoles.

“The new pull-push system fits seamlessly into a comprehensive range of complete solutions for die or mould changes on punching and forming lines. Our extensive product range allows us to offer a complete solution for almost every situation. It starts with the rack systems. Using different types, they can be adapted to the respective requirements of the production site. Ball or roller bars in the racks and changing carts, as well as in the transport consoles, ensure consistently safe, quick and efficient die change," continued Roemheld.

“The changing carts transport the dies between the storage area and the press. There, employees can bring them into the machine using consoles. Using a uniform docking system for all racks, changing carts and consoles, the dies can only be moved when all components are safely coupled. This significantly increases safety for employees during handling," it added.

Solutions for the positioning, centring and clamping of dies complete Roemheld's overall concept. The clamping systems are available in mechanical, hydraulic, magnetic and electromechanical versions.

"The die-change process becomes particularly efficient when using magnetic clamping technology. Clamping takes just a few seconds; operators no longer have to intervene manually. Magnetic clamping plates are suitable for all ferromagnetic dies, regardless of geometry, size and dimensions. Standardisation is not necessary," concluded the manufacturer.
