# DOUBLE FOLDER

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

“THE THALMANN TD double folder, with its dynamic folding technology, enables the movement of several machine axes simultaneously. The folding beams are always placed in optimum position to the part, reducing repositioning and idle times to a minimum. Overlapping tool movements result in a fluent folding process and a significant increase in productivity and capacity,” Thalmann told ISMR.

A further improvement is the multi-zone gripping system which allows simultaneous independent handling of several parts e.g. inserting two three-metre sheet metal strips on a six-metre machine and, after bending, removing the two profiles separately without one falling off.

The ZR single folder series offers features such as dynamic crowning. The special tool geometry of the ZR models unlocks new possibilities for manufacturing complex parts. Thalmann folders are equipped with dynamic control shaft technology. This kinetic drive system distributes the power equally to all machine stands, clamping and bending beam. This results in strong bending angle accuracy and parallelism of the bent parts.

The Swiss technologies will be on display and in operation from 18–21 February 2014 at Roof and Timber in Cologne (Hall 8, stand 8603). www.thalmann-ag.ch ■

## ROFIN and IMRA America announce license agreement

ROFIN-SINAR Technologies Inc, one of the world's leading developers and manufacturers of laser sources and laser-based solutions, and IMRA America, Inc., a specialist in the research, development, manufacturing and application of ultrafast fibre lasers, have entered into a license agreement for the use of IMRA’s patent rights for the patent family including US patents RE37,585 and 5,656,186, “Method for controlling configuration of laser induced breakdown and ablation”.

This patent family was invented by Gerard Mourou and colleagues while at the University of Michigan and is licensed to IMRA from the University of Michigan. The invention is concerned with ultra-fast lasers for micromachining. The inventors recognized that ultra-short pulses are shorter than the time needed for most energy transfer processes within the material. Therefore deterministic, ultra-precise material processing could be achieved. This method is sometimes referred to as “cold” or “athermal”.

ROFIN’s product portfolio ranges from single laser-beam sources to highly complex systems, covering all of the key laser technologies such as CO lasers, fibre, solid-state and diode lasers, and the entire power spectrum, from single-digit watts up to multi-kilowatts, as well as a comprehensive spectrum of wavelengths and an extensive range of laser components. ■

## New tooling solutions

MATE PRECISION TOOLING brings an even bigger presence to this year’s FABTECH Canada, Canada’s largest fabricating, metal forming, finishing and welding event, from 18-20 March 2014. On its booth 1217 in Toronto, it will display its VariBend flexible, multi-purpose tool that bends sheet metal tabs at any angle up to 90° eliminating the time-consuming secondary operations usually performed on a press brake.

“VariBend’s bending angle is adjustable in 1° increments and will handle the specific material types and thickness for which it was designed. VariBend will produce form heights of approximately 0.300 inch to 0.700 inch (7.60mm-17.75mm). Programmed as a normal forming tool (regular forming or as an upforming tool), VariBend operates with CNC punch press accuracy and with little or no sheet marking,” Mate told ISMR.

Mate’s VariBend is suitable for punch presses with upforming capabilities and especially machines with stroke control. For thick turret machines without stroke control, Mate’s Ultraform® holder can be used for length adjustment. VariBend is available in many tooling styles and station sizes.

Mate’s Premia™ is an automated tool sharpening system. It features an innovative touch-screen interface that simplifies all tool set-up and maintenance operations. Everything is menu-driven, with three standard programs for small, medium and large tools, plus two more that are completely customisable. These programs ensure sharpening consistency, no matter who operates the machine.

Mate’s UltraTEC® A and B punches are also now available in optional M4PM steel for high performance punching applications. M4PM steel has at least 50 per cent or greater wear resistance according to independent metallurgical experts.

New for TRUMPF press users is Mate’s support for special shape tooling and forming applications. Mate now provides complete set-up and Trutops programming information for special shapes and forming applications for all of its TRUMPF customers at no added cost.
