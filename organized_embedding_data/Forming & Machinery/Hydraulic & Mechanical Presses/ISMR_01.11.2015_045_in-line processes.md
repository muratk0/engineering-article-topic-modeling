# IN-LINE PROCESSES

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

Family-owned manufacturer, WEMO, has launched a range of standard machines, with a new high speed panel bending line, and has just opened an office in the U.S.

ISMR SAYS:

“WEMO intends to double its turnover in five years”

Dutch machine manufacturer, WEMO, believes in a world in which man and machines work and learn together in harmony to achieve the highest levels of productivity and efficiency. To do this, it develops smart, flexible and rapid solutions for coil or sheet metal working using a range of modules, with some engineering to order, enabling Wemo to solve complex production challenges.

Its Plug and Produce line concept spans a variety of feeding (in/out) punching, bending and joining lines where production lines can easily be expanded in the event of growth.

Last year, WEMO celebrated its 51st anniversary. The company currently has 110 employees and focuses above all, as it told ISMR, on customer needs and quality. ISMR caught up with WEMO’s managing director, Paul Hoekstra, between the Blechexpo and FABTECH trade fairs recently.

## High speed panel bending

“We introduced the WEBEND PRO high speed panel bender line last year and sold the line to a Turkish customer in two minutes at Blechexpo in Stuttgart,” Hoekstra told ISMR. “We are known in the market for high output and high quality lines but the world is changing and customer requirements are moving increasingly towards lower entry-level machines. We have therefore started developing lower entry-level standard machines and automatic bending and punching machines. We have also produced standardised machines for coil rollforming, with shearing, combined with roll bending e.g. for production of roller shutter boxes. We intend to grow alongside our customers' financial growth and extend their lines over time, adding more modules for punching, bending, joining/clinching, welding etc. when required.

“Our strength is still in punching, bending, joining and handling equipment/lines but we are also working with partners on other line specialties such as welding, rollforming and clinching. We still want to stay a company that makes special solutions using a modular way of building. We have lowered the entry level for more spin-off equipment and are increasingly combining modules. We intend to double our turnover in five years.”

The WEBEND PRO high speed panel bender from WEMO has been designed as a Plug and Produce system for rapid part output and quick Return on Investment (RoI). With a 10-year warranty on all solid parts, the panel bender comprises a feeding station, a longitudinal bending station, a head-bending station, a take-off station and fully automated control.

The system can accommodate sheet metal blanks - Ferritic steel or (pre-painted) Steel DC01 - from 0.5mm to 1.3mm thick. Blank widths range from 245mm – 900mm, with lengths ranging from 400mm – 2275mm.

“Product tolerance is ± 0.2mm (depending upon your pre-punched blanks),” said Paul Hoekstra. “It is easy to extend the line with extra features and we believe the line’s output is 2.5 times faster than our competitors’ production lines. We have sold many standard WEBEND PRO lines and five of the new WEBEND PRO machines (high flexibility, coil-fed rollforming with roll-bending in one machine, programmable for each Vbend/roll-bend). It can process material from 0.7 to 1.2mm in variable lengths (400mm min. to 7000mm). WEPRESS PRO can also handle aluminium without scratching the material surface (with or without protective film).”

## An eye on markets

WEMO sells production machines into the U.S., Europe, the Middle East and Asia. Three months ago, it sold three lines to a Chinese customer to manufacture landing doors for elevators (every seven seconds, one landing door). A few weeks ago, Hoekstra told ISMR, the company sold a panel line for ship cabins and hotel cabins to a Chinese customer and is actively researching the market in the MINT countries (Mexico, Indonesia, Nigeria and Turkey). He also confirmed the recent UK sale of a high speed line for producing ceiling panels. Hoekstra has his sights firmly set on the U.S. market.

“We have fourteen lines in the U.S. already and opened an office in the Atlanta area a few weeks ago,” Hoekstra explained. “We see a lot of potential opportunities in the U.S. market – its production methods are very labour-intensive so there is scope for automation. Customers want the same high quality levels but at a lower cost. We introduced the WEBEND PRO XL panel bender line at FABTECH. It was an excellent event for us – we sold some enclosure

production lines there. I believe that we could eventually start our own production there but not for ten years or so. We are already expanding our sales force in the U.S.”

WEMO launched a new value chain (Virtual World) which examines, in depth, the markets that interest the company. It purely focuses on the needs of its customers and not on the machine. It then develops a Product Market Combination (PMC), launches and markets it. It also looks across industry sectors to find other applications and customers for its products (e.g. aerospace, healthcare, food etc.). The company currently supplies into industry sectors such as white goods, construction, automotive and storage.

“We look critically at what we do and try to find new markets for our equipment. We are also trying to become leaner so that we can reduce lead times for our own machines for customers. So far, we have reduced internal lead times by 50% already. We want more turnover with the same level of employees so a more modular level of building is needed, hence our approach,” explained Paul Hoekstra.

He identified several common market trends and customer requirements to ISMR.

“Customers tend to want high quality equipment that need fewer people to operate, less overhead, no more stock, one-off and flexible batches and more material savings. In certain sectors (such as office furniture etc.), and in certain markets (like China), pre-painted materials can be more popular. In the automotive sector, high tensile steel and aluminium is used. We can adjust bending within the operating bending process in our lines to suit the materials being processed - much depends on the product,” Hoekstra told ISMR.

“Our focus at Blechexpo was on the standard panel bender line and our new website and logo. Our new website is totally different - its focus is on the customer and it is industry-specific. We have just developed a full electrical bending (CNC and swivel bending) line. We are launching a servo swivel bending module for standard machines – these are generally hydraulic. We are also in the process of developing a module for corner forming pre-painted material with thicker material for wall cladding – we will be bringing this out in the first quarter of next year. We are also finishing a study to prove that our way of bending for high quality output using hightensile steel is more suitable than roll forming (for example, in the automotive industry)."

The refurbishment of used WEMO lines has also been a revenue generator for the company. It just sold a kitchen panel line from a customer in Switzerland as a refurbished ceiling tile line to a UK customer.
