Luvata is a leader in metal fabrication, component manufacturing and related engineering and design services. It began by developing methods of casting long cannon barrels for warships in 1580.

Solving the problem of casting a long tube with a perfectly concentric hole increased the range and accuracy of cannons and stopped ships from blowing themselves up in the 16th century – but it also led to new developments in many, more constructive areas of technology.

Since then, Luvata has pioneered a diverse range of processes and products including everything from roofing and facades to supermarket chiller cabinets, from zips on trousers to particle accelerators that will recreate the origins of the universe. Luvata offers coils, sheets and strip in a wide range of alloys, widths, thicknesses and lengths to exact specifications.

## SETTING A RECORD

Cluster punch tools multiply the number of holes per hit. New ways for maximising their use by Luvata reveal how much cluster tools can contribute to increased productivity and overall quality.

Luvata, the world’s largest coils-and-cooler manufacturer, recently set a new record for cluster punching without re-sharpening the punch. Luvata recorded 4,100,000 hits using a Mate UltraTec® 4-hole cluster punch. The punched parts become end plates for air-conditioning coils.

The record was set punching 0.3875 inch diameter holes using a 0.010 die clearance on four different material thicknesses – 0.037, 0.046, 0.058 and 0.071- inch thick galvanised steel sheet.

Achieving a very high number of quality hits without sharpening is important to Luvata (as it is to every fabricator looking to extend tool life) because it eliminates the non value-added time spent sharpening. It also enables the company to reduce machine set-up time by an average of 4 to 6 hours a week while reducing excess part inventory.

“To get the most productivity out of our machines, we need superior performance from our punch tooling,” reports Barry Rounsaville, methods analyst for Luvata’s fabricating operations in Grenada, Mississippi. “We continually look for ways to extend tool life, maximise quality and eliminate set-up time. Mate UltraTec tooling, and especially Mate’s cluster tooling, allows us to do that.”

Luvata is the leading global manufacturer of finned pack heat exchangers, playing a prominent role in the design and production of air units for the refrigeration and air conditioning sectors as well as AC refilling and maintenance equipment for the automotive industry. Automated punching is a key process in the metal fabrication of these Luvata heat exchangers.

## TOOL MANAGEMENT IN FOCUS

Luvata’s disciplined tool management process is key to getting maximum productivity out of its presses and tooling.

“We require tight hole tolerances for our air conditioning coils,” says Mr. Rounsaville. “We carefully control all punching conditions, not just with the tools, but with all variables. These variables include type of material and thickness, press speed, cleanliness of the machine turret, tools and material and operator training with the process.”

Luvata worked its way up to the 4,100,000 hit count without sharpening by steadily improving use of its cluster tools. Initially, it was getting up to 2,000,000 hits with these tools while maintaining hole quality. Mate reports that a cluster punch will average 500,000 hits during normal use before sharpening is required. Luvata was exceeding this average by a wide margin through the careful management of the variables described previously.

In addition, Mate recommended treating a replacement cluster tool with its Maxima coating. This coating increases the lubricity of the punch points, to resist wear for cleanly punched holes. The additional coating gave the new tool the added boost for maintaining hole quality up to the 4,100,000 hit level.

## LONGER TOOL LIFE

Extended tool life and punching quality is inherent in the design of Mate’s UltraTec tooling. According to Mr. Rounsaville, the UltraTec tool design results in much longer tool life on slitting, nibbling and other punching operations, not only in the cluster punching operations.

“We punch an average of 216,000 lbs. of galvanised and galvannealed material and 50,000 lbs. per aluminium a month,” said Mr. Rounsaville. “That includes 0.037, 0.046, 0.058 and 0.071 galvanised steel on one of our Finn Power NightTrain presses.

“On our second NightTrain press, we punch 0.040, 0.050, 0.063, 0.080 and 0.090 alloy aluminium (3003 and 5052). Part sizes vary from as small as 1.00 x 4.00 inches to 48 x 96 inches. For all of these materials and sizes, Mate tooling gives us the best results. We regularly test other tooling and have never had another brand come close to Mate’s tool life or quality performance. The key is that Mate tools do last longer and, with our fabricating knowledge and experience, we make those tools last even longer.” ■
