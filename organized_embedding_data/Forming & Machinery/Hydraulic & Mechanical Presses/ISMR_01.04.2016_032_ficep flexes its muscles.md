# FICEP flexes its muscles

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

FICEP showcased its full range of steel plate, beam and angle processing machinery at the MACH show in the UK, including its best-selling Endeavour which, it told ISMR, was ‘the fastest ever automatic CNC line for drilling, milling and marking beams with additional auxiliary axis movement’. The company manufactures a wide range of machines available for fabrication with options to punch, mark, drill, scribe, machine and shear or thermal cut.

Its latest GEMINI plate processing machine can drill, mark, tap, countersink, mill and cut with plasma or gas and has been further improved with faster positioning and increased tool change capability, offering users more options.

“There are systems for every steel processing application from a stand-alone band-saw to a fully automatic production line and material-handling system. These are all controlled with the very latest steel construction software by our own software company, Steel Projects, presenting the latest PLM software (Production Lifecycle Management),” the company told ISMR at the MACH exhibition.

On the FICEP exhibition stand was a live demonstration of the Endeavour 603 DD, the advanced CNC drilling, milling and scribing equipment for beams, plus features on the new options for milling copes rather than thermal processes.

Ficep S.p.A. (Varese, Italy) has become a leading supplier of structural steel and plate fabrication equipment over the past 85 years. According to the company, it currently controls approximately 50% of the world market for structural steel and plate processing systems. Established in 1930 and located at the foot of the Alps in northern Italy, 16km from the border with Switzerland, it focuses on the engineering and manufacturing of machine tools for the metalworking industry. Its portfolio of machinery includes steel section and plate fabrication equipment, automated handling and forging systems.

In February 2000, Ficep S.p.A. established Ficep UK to address the sales and service requirements of the UK market after previously working with agents. Since then, Ficep UK has sold hundreds of CNC lines to the UK structural industry. ISMR caught up with Mark Jones, Ficep UK’s managing director, for his outlook on 2016 just before MACH and you can read the interview with him in the March issue of ISMR.
