Two-hand control panels available

AutomationDirect has added Schmersal two-hand control panels to its line of safety products. Two-hand control systems are used with a special control monitoring relay and require an operator to use both hands simultaneously when initiating a hazardous operation. This forces operators to keep their hands on the two-hand control actuator and out of an unsafe spot when a system is performing a hazardous operation.

Panels are available in aluminum or plastic and with or without push buttons. Replacement push buttons and a legend plate also are available.

Tool grinder includes 16-station wheel changer

Rollomatic has introduced the GrindSmart model 630XW3 CNC tool grinder with a 16-station wheel changer. It incorporates six fully interpolated axes (three rotary and three linear) and linear axis drive technology.

The machine’s footprint remains small, as the wheel changer adds only 30 in. to its width. It offers simultaneous change of wheel packs and associated coolant manifolds with coolant nozzles. To shorten cycle time, tool unload/load happens simultaneously during wheel change, and embedded intelligence keeps frequently used wheel packs in front.

The wheel arbors are HSK-type with a proprietary attachment design that ensures less than 0.00005-in. TIR over the life of the grinding spindle, according to the manufacturer.

Rollomatic

www.rollomaticusa.com

Wire compacting unit provides fast adjustment after electrode exchange

Amada Weld Tech has introduced a redesigned wire compacting unit for its line of modular resistance welders. Suitable for stranded wires, the plug-andplay modular compacting unit provides precise, fast, and reproducible adjustment after electrode exchange, the company reports.

The machine replaces the previous open C-frame box-type unit, and its closed-box design eliminates flexing due to lateral forces. Ball bushings guide the ceramic slide, and a new cross roller guide facilitates adjustment of the electrode position.

Using a micrometer screw, operators can now lock the ceramic and electrode position without affecting adjustment. As a result, the adjustment time has been reduced from 30 min. down to five.

Amada Weld Tech

PAPR system works alone or with helmet

Optrel has introduced its new Swiss Air powered air-purifying respirator (PAPR) that provides fresh, breathable air with maximum protection in a lightweight, comfortable system that welders can use alone or with their favorite helmet or hood.

The system is suitable for welding, grinding, plasma cutting, and metalworking applications. Vacuum bags provide longterm metal parts protection

Cortec Corp. has released VpCI-126 vacuum bags. They add an extra barrier against moisture and oxygen to basic VpCI-126 film to help inhibit corrosion on metal parts during long-term storage or harsh shipping conditions.

Metals packaged inside the bags receive continuous protection against salt, excessive humidity, condensation, moisture, aggressive industrial atmospheres, and dissimilar-metal corrosion. The VpCI molecules in the film vaporize and condense on metal surfaces in the enclosed package, forming a protective molecular film that does not need to be cleaned off before the contents can be used.

Weldcote has introduced its Striker 200 welding inverter, a digital AC/ DC welding machine for GTAW and SMAW. It offers pulse width modulation, as well as insulated gate bipolar transistors.

IIoT system detects and prevents potential incidents

Siemens has introduced Sitrans SCM IQ, a new industrial internet of things (IIoT) system for smart condition monitoring. It enables potential incidents to be detected and prevented at an early stage.

The wireless Sitrans MS200 multisensors form the hardware basis for installation on machinery such as pumps, gear units, compressors, and drivetrains, where they collect vibration and temperature data. Via a Bluetooth connection, this data is sent to the Sitrans CC220 industry gateway, where it is encrypted before being transmitted from there to the cloud. The anomaly detection is based on machine learning. It continuously monitors and analyzes all sensor values and detects deviations from the normal operating state in advance.

Siemens

www.siemens.com

Hand-held LIBS analyzer can identify carbon

Rigaku Analytical Devices has introduced the KT-500, a hand-held, laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy (LIBS) analyzer. Featuring a carbon-capable, miniaturized, highresolution echelle spectrometer, the unit can identify elements such as carbon that were difficult to see with previous-generation hand-held LIBS analyzers. This gives the user the ability to analyze ferrous and nonferrous alloys on one device.

The analyzer identifies low-alloy steels, stainless steels, nickel and cobalt alloys, and carbon steels. Its carbon analysis, along with low detection limits for other key elements, makes it suitable for determining carbon equivalence and for solving key applications required in petrochemical facilities, including sulfidation, corrosion, and residual elements.

Rigaku

www.rigakuanalytical.com

Low-silicate GMAW system helps reduce postweld cleanup

Lincoln Electric has introduced a low-silicate GMAW system that delivers high-speed, low-spatter welds to help reduce postweld cleanup and improve paint adhesion.

The system combines a Rapid X LS waveform, designed to deliver lowspatter operation, with a SuperArc XLS filler metal that produces GMAW welds with significantly reduced surface silicates. In corrosion tests with identical pretreatment and paint coating types, the filler metal demonstrated improved corrosion resistance both on the weld bead and at the weld toes, according to the company.

Regulator helps improve weld when using shielding gas

The Harris Products Group "zero" compensated flowmeter regulator for use with shielding gas protects the weld puddle against contamination from atmospheric air and other elements that can cause weld defects.

The device uses a ball float and a variable area flowmeter to yield precise control of the welding gas. The control valve is placed on the upstream side of the flowmeter, which means that it can operate accurately at virtually any inlet pressure, the company states. This design also reduces the surge associated with pressure-compensated flowmeters, allowing it also to function as a gas-saving device.

Two versions are available: one for pipeline applications up to 200-PSI inlet pressure and one for cylinder applications up to 3,000-PSI inlet pressure.

Harris Products Group

www.harrisproductsgroup.com

Torch withstands push welding of aluminum

D/F Machine Specialties has released the NCC P-38 curved, watercooled, hand-held GMAW gun for push welding aluminum. The lightweight yet heavy-duty gun is designed to withstand the demands and heat of aluminum welding.

The gun can be used with all manufacturers’ wire feeders that have provision for water cooling. It accommodates a slip-in tip fastened by a collet-action nut. The nozzle assembly water chamber and inner body water chamber are serially connected to each other with sliding, removable connectors, which simplifies detachment of the nozzle assembly and eliminates the need for gaskets in the water passage system.

The 50-degree curved metallic front body allows for operation in tight, hard-to-reach areas. The torch has a copper gas nozzle, which threads into the copper front end of the torch for heat dissipation.

www.dfmachinespecialties.com
