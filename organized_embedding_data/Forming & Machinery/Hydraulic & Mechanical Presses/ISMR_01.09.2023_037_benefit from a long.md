Many plate processors appreciate the benefits of a double-gantry plate processing system. ## Industrieverband Blechumformung e.V. (IBU)

## www.industrieverband-blechumformung.de

As a national association in Germany, the IBU represents companies in the sheet-metal industry and their partners. Its 240-plus members are predominantly family-owned SMEs, with a high degree of specialisation and experience, and are mainly suppliers to sectors such as automotive; electronics; machine and plant builders; furniture; construction and medical technology. The IBU also represents the interests of members in politics, customer groups and academia.

The association offers technical seminars, practical working groups and regular information services for corporate practice as well as professional advice on matters of law and business through its network of experts as well as technical exchanges between members. Members are integrated into the network of the business association for steel and sheet-metal working party (WSM), the working group of suppliers (ArGeZ), the Federation of German Industry (BDI) and the ICOSPA network.

## Moeller Precision Tool LLC

## https://moellerpunch.com/

Moeller Precision Tool is a U.S.-based manufacturing specialist in the punch and die component space, with headquarters in Wixom, Michigan (USA). In business since 1965, it produces stamping and die components. It has a large inventory of standard and special die components. This includes die punches; die buttons; die springs; punch retainers; custom dies; accessory components; advanced precision tooling and performance coatings.

The company recently expanded outside the United States with facilities in Mexico; Canada; United Kingdom and, most recently, Italy. The Italian purchase in Milan includes immediate plans to triple production space and add advanced machinery and highly skilled operators.

"Our rich history of comprehensive punch and die solutions, in conjunction with future expansion, will offer enhanced services, exceptional quality and uncompromising value to our European partners," said Frank DuQuet, President of Moeller Precision Tool.
