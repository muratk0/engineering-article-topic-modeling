# Automated fastener insertion

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

SWITCHING FROM MANUAL insertion of self-clinching fasteners to an automated system can be a big change, but it can have some surprising benefits as sheet metal specialists, CIL Precision, discovered.

CIL agreed to conduct a six-month trial using PENN’s PEMSERTER Series 3000® automatic feed, 71.2kN fastener installation press in its fabrication plant at Cappoquin, Ireland. The plan was for CIL to use the PEMSERTER press for low volume runs of sheet metal enclosures and cabinets for customers in the quality orientated medical sector. The installation was completed within two hours and the PEMSERTER press was ready to start installing PEM® Brand self-clinch nuts into the sheet metal enclosures.

“Initially, the operators were unsure about the automated process,” explained Martin McGrath, Production Manager, CIL, “But they found that it was easier to use than the manual system in which they have to hold the parts in one hand and load them with the other hand. It took each employee about 20 minutes to learn to use the PEMSERTER press. The information on the display screen was very intuitive and employees quickly become comfortable using it.”

The touch-screen display also gave CIL some additional features which were not possible with a manual process. For example, the PEMSERTER press uses a laser to locate the position of the fastener and includes a counter to ensure that the correct number of fasteners have been attached to each work unit. If a piece of hardware is missing, the PEMSERTER press automatically alerts the operator. This allows the missing piece of hardware to be fitted before the work moves to the next stage of the manufacturing process. During the trial, CIL also found that adding the hardware to the flat sheets was an easier process than adding them to the pre-folded sheets.

“I knew that automated insertion would be faster than a manual process, even on short runs, and the PEMSERTER press proved to be 30% to 40% faster than our usual methods,” added McGrath.

There were also other benefits, such as the elimination of hydraulic fluids in the PEMSERTER Series 3000® press which would help CIL to meet the high quality standards demanded by the medical industry and could also help it to minimise the environmental impact of its work. The rapid changeover of setups on the PEMSERTER press would also enable CIL to replace two machines used for manual insertion with a single PEMSERTER press.

“With reduced tooling costs, there are sound business reasons for using an automated process which can provide savings and a fast return on investment,” concluded McGrath.
