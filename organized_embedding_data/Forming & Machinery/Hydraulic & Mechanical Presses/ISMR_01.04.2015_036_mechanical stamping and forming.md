# Mechanical stamping and forming

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

BIHLER’S GRM 80P mechanical stamping and forming machine is designed for the mass production of stamped and formed parts as well as complete assemblies.

“The machine offers a high stroke rate of 250 parts per minute and plenty of space for extensive tool concepts,” the manufacturer told ISMR. “In combination with the separate 400kN press, a machining path of more than 2100mm is available for fully automated manufacturing solutions with integrated processes such as contact welding, thread forming, screw inserting etc. This means that larger and bulkier wire and strip material precision parts and complex assemblies can be produced more economically.”

The VC1 flexible control platform enables operators to control even complex automation solutions on the GRM 80P. The machine’s “bASSIST” diagnostic and online help system also assists machine operators with control issues. See www.bihler.de ■
