# Smart tool systems

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

FROM 23-26 October 2018, decisionmakers from the sheet metal processing industry met at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover. STEINEL, a manufacturer of punching tool components, had its own stand at the trade fair and showcased its two tapping units and modular tool system.

"Our highlights at the trade fair stand were two tapping units, the S-Former Z and the S-Former E. Around one third of visitors to the stand asked for information about these smart solutions, which enable ultrahigh-quality threads to be cut directly in the punching and bending process. As a purely mechanical solution, the S-Former Z operates fully synchronously with the tool right across the press stroke, requiring no electrical installation. The electrically powered S-Former E is driven by its own AC Servodrive and is monitored continuously by a PLC control unit, making it ideally suited to the defect-free production of safety-relevant parts," STEINEL told ISMR.

As an intelligent module, the standardised modular system enables 'flying' tool changes to be performed within 20 minutes, and is suitable for production runs involving a large variety of parts and small quantities.

"We are proud that our products met with such a high level of interest at the world’s largest trade fair in the sheet metal processing sector," stated Christian Pape, Sales & Marketing Director, STEINEL. "The feedback received from this sector demonstrates that our systems provide appropriate answers to topical questions, and meet the need for flexible, safe and costeffective solutions."

STEINEL reported making around 160 new contacts at EuroBLECH, with around one third coming from other European countries and from countries outside Europe. In 2020, EuroBLECH will once again be a definite fixture in the STEINEL trade fair calendar. See www.steinel.com
