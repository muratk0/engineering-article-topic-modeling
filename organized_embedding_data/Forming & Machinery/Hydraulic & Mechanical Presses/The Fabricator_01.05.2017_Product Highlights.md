Axial tube end forming machine features automatic tool detection

The new REB 645 axial tube end forming machine from transfluid features automatic tool detection, allowing fast tool changes for complex geometries and extreme degrees of forming. A bar code system reads the operation data of a tube with an individual bar code. All tools of the machine, which is equipped with six forming stations, have RFID sensors to help ensure the right program and appropriate tools are available.

If the automatic inspection unit detects an incorrect tool, the operating panel with data memory displays this information immediately and indicates the correct tool for the change.

The machine performs axial forming on tubes with diameters from 0.24 to 1.77 in. A servomotor helps position the tool sequence horizontally and vertically. Rolling stations for specific forming processes can be integrated. Upon request, the machine can be equipped with electric or hydraulic numeric drives.

Fiber laser cutting systems reach G-level acceleration

Haco Atlantic North America has introduced the FL 3015 LU and FL 4015 LU fiber laser cutting systems. The carbon cutting head assembly offers rigidity combined with high dynamics, and the company’s "parallel kinematics technology" has allowed accelerations to reach G-levels.

The automatic loading station contains a gripper that exists out of a vertically moving frame, equipped with suction cups, a separator unit, doublesheet detector, and separation airblow. When combined with the automatic unloading system, it allows unattended production.

Three CNC retractable clamps keep the sheet stable. The cutting head is liquid-cooled.

Roll benders can produce pipes more than 59 ft. long

Faccin SpA offers the HAV-2P series of NC pipe mill bending rolls for pipe production. The machines are constructed with sturdy electrowelded structures and fitted with three rolls in thermally treated steel alloys.

The rolls are moved independently—the top roll moves vertically, the lower rolls move horizontally—allowing the power of the machine to be used fully, depending on the material thickness. For the production of long pipes with relatively small diameters, the lower rolls are supported by backups for their entire length. The top roll is preloaded to compensate for flexing during plate bending and can be replaced to extend the range of diameters and thicknesses that can be obtained by rolling.

The long bending rolls are designed for rolling plates up to 13/4 in. thick and suitable for producing pipes more than 59 ft. long. The roll bender can be paired with a postbending machine that prepares the pipe for welding, as well as a series of outboard motorized guide rollers to move the pipe to the postbending machine.

Portable dust collection vacuums for electric tools are self-cleaning

CS Unitec’s line of HEPA dust extraction wet/dry vacuums with 11-amp motors offer 130-CFM airflow and an electromagnetic pulse filter cleaning system. Two HEPA filters capture 99.99 percent of particles down to 0.3 micron.

The line includes the CS 1445 H with a 9-gal. tank capacity, the CS 1225 H with a 6.6-gal. capacity, and the CS 1500 H with a 13-gal. capacity. The electromagnetic pulse cleaning system automatically shakes the HEPA filters to remove debris during operation. The vacuum senses fine dusts and cycles the filters more frequently to keep them clean during the work cycle.
