Electric servo waterjet pump generates 88,000 PSI max

Techni Waterjet has introduced the Ultimate Series electric servo pump by Quantum SPT. It is designed to generate hyper-pressure (60,000 to 88,000 PSI) without significantly increasing operating costs, the company reports.

The check valves and seals been redesigned to withstand the higher pressure for increased service life. The cartridge-style check valves are readily accessible and can be changed quickly, and no special tools are required to access and replace any of the hyper-pressure components.

The high cutting speed results in lower abrasive requirements and costs, since the job is completed more quickly using a higher-power-density cutting stream. Features include a heavy-duty frame, integrated LP water filters, built-in router for remote support, and advanced diagnostics.

Beveling technology automatically prepares edges for welding during laser cutting

TRUMPF has introduced the EdgeLine Bevel, which automatically prepares parts for welding during the cutting process. Compatible with the manufacturer’s standard laser cutting machines, this technology allows users to create beveled edges while cutting out the contours of each part. Liquid metal flows into these bevels during welding to help ensure a high-quality weld seam.

The technology also can be used to create bevels on a part’s inner contours, and it can automatically make the countersinks required to screw parts together, eliminating downstream processes.

The beveling technology is suitable for companies in the sheet metal fabrication sector that use joining processes on the shop floor or require countersinks for screw connections. And because it features quick and easy programming, it also can be used by companies that only occasionally fabricate parts with bevels and countersinks.

Turret punch press features 200-in. X axis

LVD North America has introduced the Strippit V30 1550, a heavy-duty, 33-ton turret punch press featuring a 200-in. X axis. According to the manufacturer, it is the only punching machine of its kind engineered to process worksheets up to 200 in. long by 60 in. wide without multiple sheet repositions, excessive sheet handling, or extra shearing operations. A rack-and-pinion design delivers precise motion and rigidity over the full length of the X axis. The X-axis traverse speed is 3,150 IPM, with a combined axis traverse speed of 4,095 IPM. The system has a maximum hit rate of 440 hits/min. on 1-in. centers in material thicknesses up to 0.25 in.

Integrated technologies added to panel bender

Salvagnini has updated its P2 panel bender with MAC3.0, a set of integrated technologies—sensors, formulas, and algorithms—to make the machine even more intelligent, according to the manufacturer.

In addition to adapting the bending force to the mechanical characteristics of the sheet metal being machined, which was possible with the MAC2.0 version, the new function extends the range of machinable materials and allows new ones to be defined simply and intuitively if necessary. The user loads the sheet metal onto the worktable, and the new function measures the material’s strength in real time. This reference value is saved so the new material can be called up as needed. The panel bender adapts the bending force as necessary to keep working within the parameters. The function calculates tensile strength in about 0.3 second. The measurement is always made on the first bend, and if the value measured differs from the reference value and the bending force has to be modified, the reworking time is less than one second.

Software adds analytical capabilities to digital microscope

Olympus has introduced new software that adds measurement capabilities and usability upgrades to its DSX1000 digital microscope. Automatic edge detection is available on in-plane and profile measurements, enabling users to measure features and defects more easily during QA/QC inspections. Other new functions include measuring the difference between two data points, analysis templates that enable users to automate analysis tasks, stitched images with a wider field of view and higher resolution, and time-lapse imaging.
