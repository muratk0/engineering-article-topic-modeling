Wrench-free nibbler features free-floating punch design

Kett Tool Co. has introduced the KL-2020, a 14-ga. nibbler. The lightweight, wrench-free tool features a rotating head and a die design that allows maneuverability for cutting sheet metal.

The 4-amp nibbler can be locked into four different positions in 90-degree increments. The angle of the die is changed with a twist of the hand, while the free-floating punch design extends time between replacements, the manufacturer states.

It cuts 14-ga. cold-rolled mild steel and most grades of stainless to 18 ga. According to the company, the 10.5-in., 4-lb. nibbler delivers a clean, burr-free cut of flat and corrugated sheet metal up to 65 IPM.

Custom CNC at heart of flying-optics laser cutting system

Favor Laser has used NUM’s Flexium 68 CNC system to develop its XO laser cutting system, which can produce very small or very large parts from sheet metal with a feed rate of up to 196.85 FPM.

The flying-optics machine has four axes, but it uses two motors for the X axis. Horizontal positioning of the cutting head is handled by the X and Y axes, while the Z axis controls the height of the head above the surface of the workpiece, and the fourth axis is used for loading and unloading material.

The CNC’s motion control hardware includes built-in configuration flexibility for future enhancements. The proprietary dynamic operator function controls the gap between the cutting head and workpiece dynamically to maintain beam focus and cutting efficiency. This enables event-driven machine cycles to be integrated into the real-time CNC kernel.

The machine also uses NUM Ethercat I/O terminals and a dual-processor Flexium FS152i operator panel with a 15-in. flat screen and hard drive running the Windows® operating system.

App aids tool steel selection

Lindquist Steels has introduced a Tool Steel Selection Guide app for mobile devices. Its build function allows users to search several hundred common applications and choose the material that best fits their production needs.

Whether it be a forming die, a rifle barrel, or an injection mold cavity, once the user selects the application, the build function provides a variety of options in different steel grades from lowest to highest quality with its respective technical data sheet. A single platform does product research, quoting, and ordering. The app is available for free download from the iTunes App store or the company’s website.

Fiber laser offers 140 W of output power

JDSU has introduced the ST series fiber-coupled diode pump laser. Powered by the company’s next-generation diode laser chip, it offers 140 W of output power through a 106.5-µm fiber. The ultracompact unit is a highly integrated device that reduces the number of components needed to produce a kilowatt-class fiber laser.

The multimode pump module offers high brightness, small size, and simplified thermal management. The diodes operate independently as distributed heat sources, allowing reliable air- or water-cooled architectures, the manufacturer reports.

The laser has been tested for endurance and power stability to withstand prolonged use in manufacturing settings, according to the company.

Process demagnetizes large pipes

Schuler, in cooperation with Maurer Magnetic, has developed a process that can demagnetize large pipes during their production. According to the manufacturer, this improves weld quality, enabling pipelines to withstand much greater pressures.

In the patented process, the welded pipes are pushed through an electromagnetic coil and demagnetized, with no delay in production. The large pipes are completely demagnetized by an alternating magnetic field, which can reach a field strength of up to 80 kA/m in the coil.

The method is suitable for spirally welded pipes with a wall thickness of up to 1.18 in. and a diameter of 4.67 to 11.48 ft. Pipes with longitudinal welds can have diameters of about 8.2 ft. and a wall thickness of 0.79 to 1.57 in.

Oil-free liquid prevents slag buildup

Tower Oil & Technology Co. offers Kleneslat-RP. The synthetic, oil-free, nonflammable, nontoxic liquid prevents hot slag and splatter from sticking to support slats and workpieces during laser, plasma, or flame cutting operations.

When sprayed or painted lightly onto laser slats and areas where laser slag buildup is a concern, the product helps simplify the slag removal, reduce downtime, and maximize equipment efficiency, according to the manufacturer. The liquid does not contain silicones or ozone-depleting chemicals.

The product does not inhibit secondary operations such as welding, painting, or galvanizing, and its ingredients do not cause hazardous fumes or discomfort to operators and surrounding workers.
