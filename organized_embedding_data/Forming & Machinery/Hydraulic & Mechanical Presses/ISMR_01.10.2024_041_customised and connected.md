# CUSTOMISED AND CONNECTED

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Spanish manufacturer, GEKA Ironworkers, outlined its latest innovations and strategic/technical focus to ISMR. It pinpointed industry trends, market predictions, new product launches and areas of opportunity and growth.

ISMR SAYS:

GEKA has over a century of experience in fabricating steel working machines, and particularly specialises in hydraulic ironworkers and CNC solutions. The company manufactures machines that are designed to be versatile and durable, serving industries such as construction, steel structures, telecommunications and metal workshops.

Its machinery and systems include a range of ironworkers (for shearing profiles, angles, bars and plates); hydraulic punching machines; bending technologies; CNC feeders/ positioners; consumables (punches, dies and blades) and standard/optional accessories. The company machines all its parts and tools in-house.

With a presence in over 90 countries and a client base of more than 100,000 international customers, GEKA has become a specialist in the metalworking sector.

The Spanish manufacturer outlined its latest innovations and strategic/technical focus to ISMR, along with industry trends affecting its equipment design, market predictions and areas of opportunity and growth.

## An eye on innovation

Over the past two years, GEKA has continued to expand its CNC solutions, providing tailor-made machinery for tasks such as punching, shearing and drilling steel and metal. These CNC lines (which can work with angles, flat bars and beams) are designed to meet customers' specific needs, offering precision and automation.

GEKA has also been focusing on innovation, launching new models (such as its DSB drilling and sawing line) and enhancing its press-brake and laser solutions.

“We take pride in maintaining high European manufacturing standards, constantly evolving our product line to match industry demands. This commitment to quality and technological advancement has solidified our reputation for providing durable, reliable equipment," Eneko Conde, GEKA's sales manager, told ISMR.

## Current business climate

According to Eneko Conde, the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals is marked by a mix of challenges and opportunities driven by economic changes, technological advances and evolving industry demands. He broke down some of the key factors that he believes are influencing international markets.

1. Laser and CNC machining: Advanced machinery, such as laser cutters and CNC machines, is in high demand.

2. Rising demand in key sectors: Despite the challenges, demand for sheet metal products remains robust particularly in industries such as renewable energy (solar panels), construction and telecommunications (5G infrastructure). This is fuelling growth opportunities, especially for those who can innovate and adapt quickly.

## 3. Supply chain disruptions and costs:

The industry has faced significant disruptions in global supply chains due to the COVID-19 pandemic and geopolitical factors, which have driven up the costs of raw materials such as steel and aluminium.

This has put pressure on margins, making it critical for companies to streamline their operations and better manage inventory.

4. Labour shortages: The industry faces a significant skills gap, especially in regions where manufacturing labour is declining. Companies are investing in training programmes and apprenticeships to build the next generation of skilled workers and automate processes.

## Industry trends and challenges

Eneko Conde outlined important industry trends and challenges that he has identified whilst working with various metalworking customers around the world.

"In terms of trends, there is a significant push towards automation and smart manufacturing technologies, enabling increased efficiency and reduced production costs. Customisation and flexibility are key trends: customers are increasingly requesting tailored solutions, necessitating adaptable manufacturing processes that can handle small batch sizes or unique specifications," he highlighted.

“Digitalisation is another major consideration. The integration of digital tools for design, manufacturing and supply chain management is becoming crucial for competitiveness and responsiveness," he continued.

The industry faces challenges in attracting and retaining skilled workers, which is critical for precision manufacturing. Ongoing global supply chain issues can affect the availability of materials and components, leading to delays and increased costs.

"To counter these challenges, GEKA is investing in advanced manufacturing technologies, such as robotics and AI-driven systems, to enhance productivity and reduce human error. We also offer flexible manufacturing solutions and have adapted our production capabilities to offer more customisation options, allowing for greater flexibility in meeting specific client needs," outlined Eneko Conde.

To address the skilled labour shortages, GEKA has plans to implement training programmes and partnerships with educational institutions to cultivate a skilled workforce.

Global supply chain issues can also lead to delays in sourcing materials, affecting project timelines. GEKA has moved to address this concern for its customers by offering adaptable and customisable equipment.

"In this way, we can help fabricators to respond to changes in supply chain dynamics, allowing for quicker adjustments to production needs," explained Eneko Conde.

## New product launches

GEKA will be introducing three new innovations at the EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition in Hanover, Germany, from 22-25 October 2024.

“We will be introducing our drill & saw beamline, our tube laser cutting machine and a new electric press brake product line, marking a significant milestone in sheet metal," Eneko Conde told ISMR.

“The drill & saw beamline is engineered to enhance structural steel processing, offering precise drilling and cutting for beams and profiles and significantly improving efficiency and reducing downtime. Our tube laser cutting machine provides high-speed, accurate laser cutting for various tube and pipe shapes, catering to industries that demand complex, precision cuts with minimal material waste. We are also excited to debut our latest pressbrake product line, a novelty in the press-brake industry. This innovative line offers enhanced flexibility and precision for bending applications. These new products highlight GEKA's commitment to innovation, addressing the evolving needs of modern fabricators and enhancing productivity and precision across the industry," announced Eneko Conde.

## Leveraging new technologies

GEKA is examining and implementing technologies such as IoT (Big Data), robotics and artificial intelligence into its business and machinery.

“In the 'Age of Digitalisation', data analytics is key. By collecting and analysing large volumes of production data, we can identify trends, improve process efficiencies and make data-driven decisions. Big Data analytics can optimise inventory levels and improve supplychain visibility, helping to mitigate disruptions and manage costs," outlined Eneko Conde.

“Artificial intelligence (AI) is also used in process optimisation. AI can help to optimise cutting and bending processes by analysing production data, allowing for adjustments that enhance precision and reduce waste," he added.

GEKA integrates IoT (Internet of Things) technology into its machines, enabling realtime data collection and analysis for improved operational insights. It also incorporates robotics and automated systems in its manufacturing processes that are designed to reduce labour costs and increase precision and speed.

"We offer flexible manufacturing systems and can adapt our machinery to handle various tasks and product types, allowing for greater customisation and responsiveness to market demands. GEKA's systems are designed to work with other technologies, facilitating interoperability across production lines and supply chains which is essential for Industry-4.0," Eneko Conde concluded.
