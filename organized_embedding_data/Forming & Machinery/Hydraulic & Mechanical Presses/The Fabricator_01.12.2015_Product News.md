Annual machine safety checkup

Omron Safety Services offers manufacturers in the metal fabrication and forming industries an inspection service to help ensure that machines such as press brakes, presses, shears, and cutting machines are safeguarded to applicable regulations.

With this service, an Omron safety system field inspector checks the machine’s safeguarding systems, including safety device applications, fixed and movable guards and compliance, visibility of hazards, point-of-operation and perimeter guards, energy-isolation devices, emergency stop, mechanical power, and transmission guards. The inspector also reviews any modifications to the machine’s location, uses, and addition/removal of peripheral or auxiliary equipment.

After the inspection is complete, a report is generated with pass/fail compliance for each inspection checkpoint, along with corrective action recommendations for failed checkpoints. All machines that are compliant with the current safeguarding regulations receive a dated validation sticker to be placed on the machine.

Torque-free stainless steel clamping levers

J.W. Winco Inc. offers GN 927.7 stainless steel clamping levers with eccentrical cam with stainless steel contact plate, tapped type, in inch and metric sizes. The levers are offered in Type A with adjustable contact plate or Type B with fixed contact plate.

The RoHS-compliant clamping levers are used for rapid clamping and releasing operations. They permit torque-free clamping via a combined downward vertical and cam-actuated motion. Designed to ensure that their movement cannot exceed the maximum clamping position, they deliver thrust forces of up to 8 kN.

With the Type A version, the distance between the eccentrical cam and the contact surface is adjustable by means of a fine-threaded knurled nut. This permits the maximum clamping force to be set by a simple adjustment. In addition, this also permits the selection of a preferred lever position in relation to the clamping lever pin.

Automated punch press loaders

Murata Machinery USA Inc. has introduced the FG series of automated punch press loaders. They provide lights-out, unattended operation during breaks, shift changes, lunch, and after hours.

The servo-controlled systems feature an axis drive, programmable parts stacking patterns, and an integrated FANUC-based FMS control. These cell loaders remove finished punched or laser-cut parts and sort and stack them on the finished parts pallet.

Multipads on the loader arm pick up finished parts, including small, hole-intensive, nonferrous, and differently shaped parts. They also enable programmable, flexible stacking patterns. Loading, unloading, and skeleton stations located on the right side of the punch press give the cell loaders a compact floor plan design. Worksheets also can be loaded and unloaded manually from the left side of the punch press.

The FG Tower series provides flexible full-sheet operation that can be left unattended longer, with blank and finished part storage. Material and part exchanges can be completed with the stocking elevator while the system is running. Material pallets can be scheduled for finished parts after they are emptied; if a material pallet empties before the job is complete, the system will continue to the next scheduled job.
