# WILA celebrates 90 years

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

On 1 October 2022, tooling specialist, WILA, celebrated its 90th anniversary.

On 1 October 1932, Mr. J.A.J. Willemsen set up a forge (WILA) in Laren. Over the years, the forge developed into a machine factory. Today, WILA B.V. employs 350 people, has two factories in the Netherlands as well as subsidiaries in the United States and China. It has developed into a leading manufacturer of tooling systems for sheet-metal bending applications.

"Many productivity-improving innovations have been developed at our facility in Lochem and are being used as standard industrial components by modern machine builders as well as users of these machines. For instance, mechanical crowning; the Safety-Click in New Standard Tooling; hydraulic or pneumatic clamps; robotbased tool changes or 'smart' tool data management," it told ISMR.

“We are a strategic partner that focuses on long-term relationships and partnerships; a position that is based on knowledge, stability, transparency and trust. It also leads to considerable growth each year and requires major investment. Stakeholders are confident that the future has been taken into account and that the continuity of the company and its clients is given the attention it deserves.

That is why owner, Hans Willemsen, has decided to gradually transfer leadership and ownership to the current Board which, on the one hand, knows the company inside out after many years and, on the other hand, is young and fresh enough to introduce new ideas," it added.■
