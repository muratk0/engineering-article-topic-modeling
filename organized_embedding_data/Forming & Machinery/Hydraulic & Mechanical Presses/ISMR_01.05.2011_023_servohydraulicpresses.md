# SERVO&HYDRAULICPRESSES

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

Since the market launch of servo drive technology in 2007, the Schuler Group has systematically been adding new application fields. The advantages of manufacturing with knuckle-joint presses are obvious: the so-called time-motion curve can be individually programmed. Servo drive presses are not only faster, they also enable highly precise forming operations and can be adapted to the respective material and automation process. In total, there are fewer mechanical components in the drive compared to conventional knuckle-joint presses. This makes them much more service-friendly.
