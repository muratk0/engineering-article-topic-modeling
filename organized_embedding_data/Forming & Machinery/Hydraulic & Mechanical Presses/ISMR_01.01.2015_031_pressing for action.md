# Pressing for action

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

TAIWANESE PRESS PRODUCER, Lien Chieh Machinery Co., Ltd., has specialised in the manufacture of different types of hydraulic presses for over sixty years. Founded in 1947, it has focused on the development of a comprehensive range of technically advanced hydraulic, hydroforming, deep drawing and forging presses designed to offer high levels of performance. It now turns its attention to a range of new hot forming and mechanical press developments.

“LCM has had great success with sales of its large single-cylinder forging presses (8000 tonnes to 15,000 tonnes) to automotive customers for aluminium wheel manufacturing. Our customers sell to German and Italian automotive OEM customers. We have been making these large forging presses since 2008, although we have been developing smaller forging presses since the 1990s to customers making components for the aerospace industry. Our 8000-tonne model was launched two years ago. We have also been very successful at exporting our products – approximately 95% of our production is exported to around 1000 customers around the world,” the company told ISMR.
