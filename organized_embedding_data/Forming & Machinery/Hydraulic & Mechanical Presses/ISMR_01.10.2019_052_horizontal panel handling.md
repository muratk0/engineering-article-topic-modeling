# Horizontal panel handling

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

AT THE BLECHEXPO trade fair in Stuttgart, Germany (5-8 November 2019), Eurotech will present new lifting devices in its eT-Hover-panel series, as well as two other new systems.

Eurotech is a specialist in standardised and customised handling and transport solutions for vacuum technology. In addition to a new eT-Hover-panel series lifting device for the removal of narrow high-bay drawer racks, the vacuum specialist has expanded its product portfolio with other eT-Hover-modesty series lifting devices. Another focus of this year's Blechexpo will be the electronic handheld suction device, type Nemo, which will also be available for testing.

Blechexpo visitors can see and test the new products and solutions, developed for the sheet metal processing industry, in Hall 3 on our booth 3412. The eT-Hover devices are used for lifting vacuumsealed, flat loads, such as glass panes, sheet metal and plastic or wooden boards. They start at a load capacity of 250kg. Depending upon the base frame and equipment, such as longitudinal cross beam and suction plate selection, a maximum load capacity of up to 4,000kg can be achieved," it told ISMR.

"As an extension of this series, a very flat version was designed for removal from narrow high-bay drawer racks. Things are made easier by a telescopic and swivelling handle with operating panel, which is also used in other models. The operating panel can be used to control all relevant processes, if the user wishes. A vacuum pump with an integrated non-return valve generates the vacuum. Devices are currently available with a load capacity of 500 or 750kg."

The eT-Hover-modesty series lifting devices serve as an alternative to similar, more extensively equipped devices. Devices are equipped with two handles for easy manoeuvring and positioning. The devices can be rotated pneumatically or rotated manually. The new feature of this series is a version that pneumatically enables swivelling of the workpieces by 90°. Lifting devices are operated entirely with compressed air; an electrical connection is not necessary. Due to their compact design and vacuum generation by an ejector, these lifting devices have a very low dead weight.

The Nemo type electronic suction device, modified by Eurotech, is a mobile, electronic, handheld suction device. With a holding force of up to 170kg, the Nemo can be used on most surfaces.

"The vacuum pump installed in the handle generates the necessary vacuum to hold the workpiece steadily. The installed vacuum meter with red/green area is used for monitoring. The vacuum pump is equipped with an economy circuit. Once the pump reaches safe negative pressure, it will be switched off. If the vacuum in the suction device drops, it will automatically switch on again. In all sectors, where heavy lifting is the order of the day, this powerful tool minimises the risk of damaging expensive materials. Nemo maximises productivity while reducing the physical workload," added the company.
