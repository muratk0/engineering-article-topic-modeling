# Launch of RWS rail-guided, die-changing table

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Roemheld has introduced a new, rail-guided, RWS die-changing table for transporting heavy dies and moulds. The solution is available in three versions for loads of up to 15 tonnes, 25 tonnes or 40 tonnes per die. RWS has been launched with a transport and handling company from the German region of East Westphalia.

"Electrically driven, the system is equipped with two different pull-push systems for the safe and automatic transport of dies to set-up presses. It can be used to change dies and moulds on all types of machines, including vertical forming and injection moulding machines. RWS is available as a single or tandem cart and can be used flexibly in a wide range of production scenarios. It also enables several presses to be connected to just one changing table. This means that the presses are free during preparation and can produce right up to die change," explained Roemheld.

"RWS is our answer to today's trend where loads in die and mould-making are increasing. This solution helps car manufacturers to integrate numerous individual parts into increasingly large castings, for example in megacasting for frame or body manufacturing. They are processed with heavier dies in larger presses and punches. We understand these new requirements and are expanding our range of die weights to accommodate heavier loads," it continued.

The die-changing table is guided on floor-level, round rails. Loads of up to five tonnes can be moved manually by one person. In addition, the rails are designed to facilitate precise carriage guidance and prevent dirt deposits and tripping hazards on the floor. Consoles bridge the distance between the press bed and the die-changing cart. RWS has an integrated pull-push system that then moves the dies onto and off the press bed via roller rails. A safety mechanism ensures that the loads can only be moved when the RWS die-changing table is firmly attached to the press or the storage space.

“Dies of different sizes can be changed with one system because a standardised base plate is used. Semi-automatic and fully automatic die changes are possible thanks to the control system that is integrated in the cart and precise positioning. Other available features include integration with the press control and the ability to combine with a rapid clamping system for dies," outlined Roemheld.

The amount of time needed for actual changeover is also minimised because the die-changing table is equipped with integrated lifting bars and a pull-push system. In the semi-automatic system version, the cabled remote control (wireless also available) enables simple and centralised control of the cart.

"Compared to free-moving transport systems, the rail-guided die-changing table offers numerous advantages: maximum repeatability and precision of up to ±0.3mm, lower energy consumption and space requirements, and greater availability. In addition, installation of floor-level round rails generally places fewer demands on the factory floor," concluded Roemheld.
