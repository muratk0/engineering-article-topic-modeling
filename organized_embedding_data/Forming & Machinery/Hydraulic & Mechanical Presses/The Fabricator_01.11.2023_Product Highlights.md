Belt sander can be used in multiple positions

Kalamazoo Industries has launched the Kalamazoo 1SMVP 1- by 42-in. multiposition belt sander. It can be used vertically, horizontally, and anywhere in between to handle various shaping, smoothing, and finishing projects.

The unit delivers consistent power, even during demanding tasks, and the variable-speed control helps ensure optimal results across different materials and applications, the company reports.

The precision tracking system ensures the belt stays aligned, minimizing the need for constant adjustments and enhancing overall accuracy. Safety features include a removable belt platen, boxed pulley guard, worktable, and easy-toreach toggle on/off switch.

Robotic cell picks, fits-up, and welds small parts autonomously

Path Robotics has introduced its new Autonomous Fit-up robotic system, a robotic cell that uses proprietary computer vision and AI to autonomously pick, fit-up, and weld small parts.

The user loads in raw components, hits Go, and comes back to a batch of welded parts. The cell runs autonomously for hours with no human intervention.

As part of its Robots-as-a-Service offering, the company provides manufacturers with 24/7 production assistance via its Mission Control command center.

Cincinnati Incorporated has developed the Xform press brake with the new, ergonomic Omni-Arm pendant arm and an expanded console. The controls can be positioned anywhere in the workplace.

Protective ear buds include Bluetooth functions

Milwaukee Tool has introduced REDLITHIUM USB Bluetooth Jobsite Ear Buds. They provide passive hearing protection, delivering noise reduction rating tips in foam or silicone. Bluetooth functions provide phone call and music capabilities.

The Jobsite Aware Mode enhances situational awareness by actively sensing external noises and reduces volume pass-through of loud sounds until levels reach a designated listening volume.

The wireless, in-ear system is resistant to water, dust, and impact and offers up to 10 hours of run-time on a single charge.

Compact machine integrates turret punching, laser cutting

Murata Machinery USA Inc. has launched the Muratec MF-3048HL combination punch press and fiber laser for performing precise cutting, punching, tapping, and forming operations on a variety of materials.

While the large turret capacity cuts down on tool changeouts, the fiber laser performs contour cutting and addresses the low-volume punch tool requirements. Added standard features like controlled punch stroke, quick-change tools, scribing, and tapping increase production transitions from one job to another for flexibility and shorter production times.

With a press capacity of 33 tons and a rated output of 4.0 kW, this hybrid machine handles a maximum sheet thickness of 1/4 in. and sheet sizes of 4 by 8 ft. or 5 by 10 ft. It delivers 510 hits/min. and a table speed of 4,921 IPM.

Sensor provides optical seam tracking for arc and laser welding

Scansonic has introduced the TH6D-Advanced optical seam sensor, offering 25- μm resolution and scalable pattern recognition algorithms. The sensor is suitable for use in automated welding with arc or laser and other processes requiring reliable guidance along a defined geometry.

The sensor projects three lines onto the edge to be machined. These lines refract at the edge and pass on information about the seam position, gap dimension, and edge offset at the joint to the control system, as well as correction data for the orientation and positioning of the tool. In this way, the unit enables dynamic correction of the tool center point in real time.
