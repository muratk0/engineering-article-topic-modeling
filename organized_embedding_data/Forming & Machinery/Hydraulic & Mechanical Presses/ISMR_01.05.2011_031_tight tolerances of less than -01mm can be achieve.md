# Tight tolerances of less than +/-0.1mm can be achieved and there are virtually no limits on intricacy or hole size restrictions

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

FROM MICRO TO MACRO In the water jet cutting segment, Bystronic’s Microwaterjet made its debut late last year. This system is used for producing (very) small precision parts and will open the door to new, trend-setting markets, believes Bystronic. Over the whole working area of 1000 by 600mm, even bars with a width of 0.2mm can be produced with a roughness of up to N6.

“The Microwaterjet is the only water jet cutting system with which a reproducible manufacturing tolerance of plus/minus one hundredth of a millimetre can be achieved,” it told ISMR.

Also new from the company is the ultra-high pressure pump ByPump Ultra, with which a pressure of up to 5300 bar can be generated.

The pump is suitable for users who occupy a lucrative niche and specifically acquire orders for cutting extra thick materials, for example metal sheets of more than around 150mm.

However, central themes for Bystronic remain economic efficiency and productivity covering a comprehensive range of materials and thickness spectra. This means a recommended operating pressure of 3600 bar in conjunction with multi-head cutting. Thus, at EuroBLECH 2010, the PrecisionAligner was presented for the first time which enables multi-head cutting even for an individual part. Other innovations are the PositionPointer and the FixMaster. The PositionPointer allows the cutting head to be positioned more precisely and the material to be used more effectively. The FixMaster, on the other hand, is used to fix cut parts during the cutting process so that they are protected from damage.

Bystronic’s Byjet 4030 Classic L features two cutting heads. It is versatile enough to produce one-off to full production runs, with the ability to switch from one material to another with minimum downtime, and with the capability of lights-out operation. This means greater capacity for users. The cutting heads feature CNC head distance allowing complex parts to be cut in the same nest moved by the CNC control.

NEXT GENERATION CUTTING Flow International has introduced the Paser 4 abrasive cutting head system. The Paser 4 cutting head is now available for Flow Abrasive waterjet shape cutting systems operating at either 60ksi or 87ksi pressures.

The Paser 4 consists of the Paser 4 cutting head, the Paser 4 abrasive metering valve and the new Low Profile On/Off valve. These new products can easily be retrofitted to existing installed Flow Water jet machines so operators can immediately begin to use patent-pending technology to significantly extend component life. This means lower cost of operation since fewer parts and abrasive are consumed and time between maintenance intervals is greatly reduced. Orifice component life is increased 3-6 times over earlier generations and competing cutting heads.

Flow’s Paser 4 Metering Valve has been newly designed to significantly reduce abrasive clogging and surging for more consistent abrasive flow, resulting in more efficient and optimised delivery of abrasive. Accuracy and stability in the abrasive flow is important to ensure that highest cutting performance is achieved.

The new design has enhanced the robustness of the metering valve for longer component life. Abrasive is the single greatest component of operating cost in abrasive water jet cutting applications. The Paser 4 Metering Valve ensures the correct amount of abrasive is used and eliminates waste.

Flow International Corporation conducted live demonstrations of the latest advances in waterjet technology at Houstex 2011. The Flow booth featured the Mach 4 cutting system that combines Flow’s HyperJet® pump, rated at 94,000 psi, and Dynamic Waterjet® XD, high-precision flat stock and 3D cutting technology. This combination improves part cycle time and accuracy two to four times over standard non-Dynamic systems.

Flow invented Dynamic Waterjet in 2001, bringing accuracy and speed to 2D cutting. Now Flow brings that technology to bevel cutting and 3D parts with the patented Dynamic XD, enabling manufacturers to accurately cut a wide variety of intricate designs, often eliminating any secondary processing.

Flow’s HyperJet pump is rated at 94,000 psi, delivering a stream of water at nearly four times the speed of sound. Pressure equals productivity: water jets equipped with HyperPressure systems, running continuously at 87,000 psi, cut up to 50 per cent faster while using less abrasive than conventional waterjets. Parts are cut with a lower cost per part, and more jobs can be completed in less time.
