# 3D FABRI GEAR 400 III

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

The 3D FABRI GEAR 400 III machine is designed for an expanded range of lasercut tube, pipe and structural applications. It cuts a wide variety of tubes and pipes including round, square, rectangular and triangular. It can also process I and H beams, C-channel, angle iron and additional user-defined shapes from mild or stainless steel.

Featuring a powerful, high-precision 6-axis laser, the 3D FABRI GEAR 400 III can handle larger, longer, thicker and heavier material due to its rigid workpiece handling system incorporating a four-chuck design. It also has improved processing speeds with tighter tolerances and can be used for drilling and tapping.
