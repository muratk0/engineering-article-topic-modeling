# Phoenix Tooling & Development Ltd rises to the 'proving out' challenge with a £750,000 press line investment via Worcester Presses.

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Established in 1990, Phoenix Tooling & Development Ltd is a specialist in progression, transfer and multi-slide press tooling. It has collaborated with many leading international automatic machinery manufacturers. The UK precision press tool designer and manufacturer is targeting a £3 million turnover by 2025 after it completed a £1.1 million investment drive that included the installation of a new press line.

To ensure that all new tooling enters full production without any issues, the company's latest acquisitions include a Chin Fong GTX 500-D mechanical press and Tomac TLF4 coil handling equipment that allows for fully simulated mass production stamping. The new equipment enables trial tooling of up to 3.5 metres in length, whilst the tonnage monitoring apparatus captures data from each stage of the press cycle.

Phoenix Tooling & Development Ltd (which supports customers in the automotive, construction, white goods, EV and medical sectors) has extended its longstanding relationship with Worcester Presses to purchase the new 500-tonne Chin Fong press and full coil line. The GTX 500-D delivers the longest tool bed (3.4m x 1.5m) in its history and is equipped with a three-in-one Tomac decoiler, straightener and feeder line that allows the business to build larger tools and, more importantly, 'prove out' complex tooling projects.

## Tooling in focus

This is a major development for the Aldridge-based business, as the company is now able to give its growing customer base full confidence that the tooling produced will stamp components to the required customer production rates, quality and consistency before it arrives at their respective factories. The business also has the equipment needed to simulate full production testing and support a seamless handover process to its clients.

"We relocated to Shenstone Drive last year and had to make the decision whether to persevere with our 1930s Wilkins and Mitchell 300-tonne press," explained Martin Mulvey, Managing Director, Phoenix Tooling & Development. "After careful deliberation, we took the decision to use the move to our new home as the perfect time to search for a new, more powerful press that had better control as well as for ancillary equipment that could speed up our try-out & commissioning processes and give us the ‘proving out' capacity that we wanted.

"Worcester Presses has been superb. Russell Hartill and his team spent lots of time exploring our requirements, challenging our approach and then identifying possible solutions. The best one is the impressive Chin Fong GTX 500-D and Tomac three-in-one TLF4- 800 coil line," he added.

Phoenix Tooling & Development, which has also invested in a new GF+ Mikron machining centre with 'WorkPartner' automated palletising, pushed the £750,000 button on the press investment and started to plan for its installation.

"The new 500-tonne (3.4m bed length) press line, with roll feed and coil handling, will help support our tool hand-over procedures to customers. The aim is to supply a full detailed report for each tool supplied. The report would include information such as the force results for each stage of the tool, the angle of the forces within the cycle, the speed achieved etc. We aim to be able to test our tooling within a production environment to ensure a smooth, clear buy-off and hand-over procedure to our customers," added the company.

## Installation and logistics

Midlands Machinery was appointed by Worcester Presses to put the Chin Fong press in place. This required taking the roof partially off Phoenix Tooling & Development for two days, removing part of the outside verge to accommodate the crane and building a one-foot-deep pit in which to house it. Installation took place in December 2022.

“The press, which weighs about 55 tonnes without the ram, would just about fit under the roof if we moved the air receivers to the side (something that Worcester Presses was able to facilitate)," explained Martin Mulvey, who started his working life with Phoenix Tooling & Development's sister business, Cooke Brothers.

"It took about two weeks from start to finish to get the press in place, commissioned and ready to accommodate our ongoing press tooling projects. Five of our staff have been trained on how to get the most out of it, giving us full flexibility on the shop floor," he continued.

Russell Hartill, Managing Director, Worcester Presses, added his support: “This is such an impressive purchase for Phoenix Tooling & Development, opening over £1 million of new opportunities for the business. The Chin Fong press delivers expert control, precision quality and, with the GTX 500-D, 30 strokes per minute. It features front and rear light guards for enhanced safety and to give engineers the chance to work around the tool.

"Tool monitoring and protection has been fitted to improve tool life, whilst the Tomac automatic coil feed replaces the need to manually feed the straight strip into the press. It is a game-changer for the team," he added.

## Press tryout and commissioning

The experienced team at Phoenix carries out extensive try-out testing and commissioning on all new projects. Its over £1 million investment in new equipment is designed to ensure that all new tooling and commissioning meets the exacting standards required, with each component verified against its CAD data.

Customers can view the progress of their tooling trials.

To ensure that all new tooling enters full production without any issues, the company's latest acquisitions include the Chin Fong GTX 500-D and a Tomac TLF4 coil handling equipment that allows for fully simulated mass production stamping. The new equipment enables trial tooling of up to 3.5 metres in length, whilst the tonnage monitoring apparatus captures data from each stage of the press cycle.

Multi-slide tooling is tested and commissioned on either the company's Bihler GRM50 or RM40 dedicated try-out machines. The in-house equipment facilitates the handling of coil material enabling Phoenix to run first-batch volume production to ensure handover of new tooling to its customers.

All tools pass through an internal commissioning phase, where tools are fully tested and prepared ready for production. Alternatively, where required, tools can be commissioned at the customer's own premises.

## Prototype manufacture

Prototype tooling and low volume manufacturing are key areas of engineering where the Phoenix team has a longstanding and proven track record of delivering rapid turnaround solutions.

"With our fully equipped in-house facilities, we can keep lead-times to a minimum. As all processes from 3D design, CNC manufacture, CNC laser cutting, pressing capacity up to 500 tonnes and the latest laser scanning are all [carried out] on-site, each project can be controlled more effectively as the risk of relying on subcontract processes is removed," explained the tooling specialist. Phoenix has completed many low-volume projects where soft tooling has been manufactured, laser cuts made and parts formed. Typical prototype volumes have ranged from 50 to over 10,000 parts produced and supplied in accordance with customer timelines. In many cases, Phoenix has received production tooling orders with the additional requirement of supplying "off form stage" prototypes prior to full tool completion. These useful additional services can be managed in parallel with full tool manufacture to ensure that the customer receives both pre-build prototypes and full production tooling within the agreed time scales.

Phoenix also offers press tool repairs, servicing and modification. In line with Phoenix's continued investment in the latest processing, the company's line-up of TRUMPF 600L Twin Head Combination Laser punch presses are designed to create flat components and the strip layouts required for the development stages of each new tooling project.
