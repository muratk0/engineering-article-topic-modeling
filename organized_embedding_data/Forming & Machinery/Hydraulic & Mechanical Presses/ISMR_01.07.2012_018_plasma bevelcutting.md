# Plasma bevelcutting

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

ECKELMANN LAUNCHED its innovative plasma bevel-cutting aggregate (German Pavillion, Stand: E1436) at the Welding & Cutting exhibition in Beijing from 4-7 June 2012. The device – with optional z-axis mechatronics – can be added to any machine gantry. In conjunction with the Eckelmann CNC-for-cutting controls, this robust unit offers precision whilst distance regulation is handled by the CNC-for-cutting controls.

“A common problem with beveling is the necessity for highly dynamic tool tracking and correction with the entire machine. The required dynamics of the axes in the handling machine are only possible with costly drive engineering. We have reversed the perspective and developed a bevel-cutting aggregate that makes the tool tip the focus. Via a fixed Tool-Centre-Point (TCP) made popular by robotic kinematics, the tool tip remains in the same position in relation to the workpiece during the entire process. As a result, the tool coordinates system moves with the tool,” explained Eckelmann.

“The burner is tracked around a C-axis: with a turning speed of 120 degrees/second and at an angle rotation of up to ±540°. The plasma burner can be swiveled at ±45° (a-axis) at a speed of 100 degrees/second. The z-axis integrated in the beveling unit corrects the distance to the surface of the workpiece at a speed of 125mm/s. Altogether, the unit achieves precision of ±0.5mm to the Tool-Centre-Point. The bevel-cutting aggregate is well equipped for the harsh conditions of plasma cutting.” www.eckelmann.de
