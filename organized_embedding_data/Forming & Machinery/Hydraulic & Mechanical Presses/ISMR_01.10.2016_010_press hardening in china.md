# Press hardening in China

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

USING EQUIPMENT FROM AP&T, China's Yifeng Automotive Technology Group has developed its manufacturing of press hardened vehicle parts 'from scratch' in just four years. The company is now investing in several press hardening lines to increase its capacity. Yifeng will be a huge player in the industry, and is now AP&T's single largest customer.

"Yifeng has been ordering a large number of production lines for press hardening from us since 2012. Our cooperation has deepened each year. The most recent order also reinforces our leading position in press hardening – not only in China, but globally, " said AP&T's CEO and managing director, Magnus Baarman.

(Left) Su Yujun, manager of Yifeng's plant in Ningbo. On the right is AP&T's general manager in China, Kari Rannisto.

Yifeng is China's largest manufacturer of press hardened vehicle parts and tooling for pressing body parts. The company delivers to most of the car manufacturers active in the Chinese market, as well as to manufacturers in Europe and the US. The Group has five development centres and production plants in ten Chinese cities. The most recent order to AP&T includes press lines for Ningbo, Shanghai, Xiangtan and Guangzhou. The Ningbo plant alone has capacity for 5.4 million strokes a year for manufacturing press hardened parts, and the number will continue to increase with the new lines in place.

Yifeng's vigorous investment in press hardening is a good example of the quick development that is currently ongoing in the Chinese car industry. Over 20 million cars are currently being produced in the country every year, and a rising share of them has an increasing percentage of press hardened parts. As is the case in the rest of the world, the underlying driving force is the increasingly stringent demand for safer, lighter and more fuel-efficient cars that have lower environmental impact.

AP&T's equipment is used for manufacturing body parts such as A-pillars, B-pillars and different types of reinforcements.

"Our focus is on leading the development of lightweight solutions in the Chinese car industry, which is why we are vigorously investing in press hardening technology from a partner that shares our lofty ambitions. We are very pleased with both the equipment and the service, and our continuous exchange of knowledge is rewarding for both parties, " said Su Yujun, manager of Yifeng's plant in Ningbo.

He added that demand for press hardened parts continues to be strong.

"The trend is clear. An increasing number of car manufacturers are starting to use press hardened parts, and opportunities for usage continue to grow. In the future, it will be possible to replace an increasing number of cold-formed parts with press hardened parts."

Since 2012, Yifeng has ordered a large number of complete press hardening lines from AP&T - the most recent order was delivered in 2016. The equipment is used for manufacturing body parts such as A-pillars, B-pillars and different types of reinforcements. The deliveries include line design, presses, automation, line control systems, integration etc. AP&T's organisation in Shanghai provides service and support.
