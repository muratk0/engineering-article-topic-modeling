# PACKING A PUNCH

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

profiles a selection of some of the latest new developments in punching, and combination punching, technologies

ISMR SAYS: “ Recent innovations in punching technology have included more agile combination systems”

A PUNCH PRESS, used to cut holes in material, can be small and manually operated and hold one simple die set, or be very large, CNC-operated, with a multi-station turret and hold a much larger and complex die set.

Its die set consists of a set of punches (male) and dies (female) which, when pressed together, form a hole in a workpiece (and may also deform the workpiece in some manner). The punches and dies are removable, with the punch attached to the ram during the punching process. The ram moves up and down in a vertically linear motion, forcing the punch through the material into the die.

Recent innovations in punching technology have included more agile combination systems, from a range of manufacturers, integrating, for example, laser cutting and bending operations into the punch processing. These are interesting advances, particularly as punching technology has traditionally been a more cost-effective process than, for example, laser processing operations.

## New technology advances

In this section, we profile a selection of some of the latest new developments in punching, and combination punching, technologies.

Japanese machine tool specialist, Amada, has introduced a punch–fiber laser combination, LC-2515 C1 AJ, for flexible, eco-friendly production. Featuring a servo-electric drive and fiber laser technology, it offers optimised quality, speed and energy efficiency. It also allows interruption of an ongoing production programme with, for example, an urgent job.

“The machine is highly productive, features energy-saving benefits and lower running costs while maintaining high speed and efficient processing of thin and reflective sheet metals,” Amada told ISMR. “Its ID Tool system ensures continued quality of processing tools while the automation of part picking and sorting reduces production time and drastically reduces energy consumption.”

Amada’s turret punch press, the EM-3612 ZRB, has an automatic tool change system and can be connected with the ID TOGU tool grinding machine. It also boasts simplified tool setting for low skill operation and needs no repositioning for punching/tapping 3000 x 1500mm material.

Baykal is currently one of the top ten sheet metal working machine manufacturers in the global market with manufacturing capacity for 3000 machines per year. It is one of the oldest machine tool companies in Turkey, founded in 1903. Its BPM-T turret punch press can process large sheet metal sizes at high axis speeds and beat frequency. Automation can also be fitted for continuous operation.

Baust presented new coil-driven rotary punches with variable tool concepts at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition. Its engineers designed two new punch cassettes that are flexible, easy to handle, productive, economical and use no motors.

Besides the coil drive principle, the new BRV features very easy product change through its variable tool concept. A pressurized air cylinder opens the tools wide enough to easily load a new coil /strip, or remove a flange mounted tool set and replace it with a set for a new punch pattern. The tools stay synchronised even when the tools are opened. The operator does not have to worry about tool alignment when mounting the new set. Now tool changes can be made in minutes. A pneumatic cylinder comfortably closes the tools even when the new strip is running at 60m/min. or more.

Dalcos (the coil punching division of Dallan) launched a new coil-fed punching and laser cutting system at the recent EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover. The combo coil fed punch-laser has been designed to meet higher requirements for efficiency, productivity and flexibility in today’s manufacturing environments.

“The machine consists of an electric punching machine EXN 1000 attached to a laser cutting zone, ideal for making complex notches, special perforations and variable forms: in practice, a ‘laser tool’ that adds flexibility to the traditional productivity of the Dalcos EXN,” explained the manufacturer.

The first part of the process is carried out by the electric coil-fed punching machine that uses standard thick turret tools with A, B and C dimensions. This part of the machine can also be equipped with punching tools with larger diameters, for perforating and cluster operations, up to the F dimension (6 inches in diameter) as well as Dalcos G-Tool rotating tools.

The punching zone uses a moving servo-electric hammer that generates 20 tons in force, inserted in a heavy duty H-frame structure that allows the processing of thin as well as thick materials up to 4mm. Once the material is punched and completed with embossing and deformations, it is delivered to the laser cutting zone for completion of notches or special cutting.

“One of the great advantages of this combo system is its flexibility: it is also possible to use just the punching or laser cutting zone, depending upon the specific process of the part and the available tools. The ELXN system can also include systems for fume treatment, connection for bottles for the laser gases, chiller etc,” explained Dalcos.

Turkish manufacturer Ermaksan has a range of punch press systems. Its ETP CNC turret punch press is designed to punch up to 6mm thick sheets. It is available in two versions – one with 33 and one with 27 stations. The 33-station model offers indexable D stations and may include 8A, 3B or 1C multi-tools upon request. Up to 54 tools can be housed and a hardened steel-coated tool adaptor system ensures a maintenance-free long working life. Several forming operations are possible with the functional tools perforating different diameters and shapes.

Its RPP rotative punch press is designed to allow economical sheet loading from three sides. Sheet processing can be done multi-directionally and flexibly for sheets of up to 4mm thickness. Ermaksan’s CPP (Combined Plasma Punch Press) series combines HiFocus sensitive plasma cutting and hydraulic punching in one system. Cutting and perforating processes can be performed at high speed and accuracy, and the machine tool is optimised for mass production. Its FPP Fix Punch Press can handle multi-directional sheet processing on material of up to 6mm in thickness.

GEKA recently showcased its new GAMMA TRACTION 100 line, which processes angled sections with a traction system. This version is designed to satisfy requirements for punching, marking and cutting medium-sized angled sections.

LVD featured its latest and most versatile punching technology at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover. Its new Strippit PX-Series, including the PX-1530, offers the flexibility to punch, form, bend and tap on a single machine to efficiently complete multiple processes, including the processing of complex, three-dimensional parts. The Strippit PX-1530 can bend small boxes, brackets, knockouts, louvres and countersinks with flange heights of up to 75mm. TOUCH-i4 generates and presents live information needed to manage networked LVD laser, punching and bending machines.

“The Strippit PX-1530 provides large all-tool rotation capacity, high hit rates, large workpiece handling and is available with modular automation options,” LVD told ISMR.

“It can be paired with Compact Tower (CT-P) automation which provides increased productivity by facilitating optimal material flow and unattended operation for uninterrupted punching.”

The CT-P system provides full capabilities for loading, unloading and storage of raw material and finished parts, enabling automated production from stored raw material to stacked finished parts, as well as providing increased storage capacity. The unit facilitates unmanned, “lights out” production in a compact cell environment.

LVD also highlighted the new features of the latest version of its CADMAN® offline programming software for punching, bending and laser cutting applications at EuroBLECH. LVD also offers the module CADMAN-JOB, software that helps users to analyse and optimise their workshop’s throughput.

A new innovation for LVD at EuroBLECH was its TOUCH-i4, an industrial strength Windows-based tablet that provides an overview of the entire fabrication workshop. TOUCH-i4 generates and presents live information needed to manage networked LVD laser, punching and bending machines.

“The real-time TOUCH-i4 system collects information from LVD machines and the centralised CADMAN database to offer insight and flexibility on daily operations. This information can be viewed and managed wirelessly on the shop floor with TOUCH-i4, allowing the user to make informed decisions based on lead-time, cost, technology and individual machine workload,” explained LVD.

Mate Precision Tooling offers a range of specialised punch press tooling. Its new European and Wila TRUMPF Style tooling includes a full array of punches, dies, standard specials, specials and accessories, available in segmented, full-length and half-length sizes.

Mate’s new Versadie™ insert slitting die takes clamp clearing solutions to a new level. It reduces material cost and waste for slitting operations by punching closely to the clamps, which is crucial when maximum sheet usage is required. This new type of die also allows users to replace die inserts without replacing the entire slitting die. Mate special assemblies are designed with high performance features for forming card guides, embosses, threadforms and hinges.

Faster cycle times and high quality production help fabricators to achieve higher output with less waste.

In punching, speed and precision are the key issues. Particularly for large quantities, it is important that the cost for the individual part is as low as possible to be competitive. Austrian manufacturer Meusburger Georg GmbH & Co KG provides standard tool concepts and standardised solutions, ensuring flexibility.

PASS Stanztechnik AG is one of the largest punching tool manufacturers and specialist in special solutions for punching tools (TRUMPF, Salvagnini and thick turret systems). Its punch tooling portfolio includes form/blanking; cluster; embossing; marking; thread rolling and knockout tooling. It recently developed a new tool for producing threads – ps multi-thread with six positions which can be used for sheet thicknesses of up to 8mm (depending upon thread size).

The PivaPunch punching and cutting centre for coils, from Finnish manufacturer Pivatic, produces blanks and punched blanks which are cut both to length and to width straight from coil. Parts are produced according to the job orders created by the ERP system.

Punching the second nest and cutting to width of the first nest takes place simultaneously – in a single pass, straight from the coil. In the punching station, two parts are punched at the same time using thick turret tools set in two tool cassettes. Dedicated press tools can be used for perforation, standard hole patterns, large holes and for forming.

Pivapunch can also be integrated with Pivatic’s PivaBend into one production line where finished parts are produced straight from the coil in one run. Parts can be rollformed where applicable.

Prima Power’s PSBB (short for punching, right angle shearing, buffering and bending) is a fully automatic manufacturing line for processing sheet into ready-bent, high quality components. The current offering includes many improvements and, with the new Shear Brilliance as the punching-shearing cell, raises manufacturing speed and productivity to a new level.

“The vast majority of all fabricated sheet metal components are rectangular, so a highly economical method to produce them is to perform first punching and then shear the components loose in the same automatic process with an integrated right angle shear. Also, parts with two or three straight edges are perfect for fabrication with a right angle shear,” explained the Italian manufacturer.

“The new, fully servo-electric Shear Brilliance features linear drive technology for fast sheet positioning. With long travel of the 4,070mm coordinate table and a full 3,100mm working area for punching and can be used without repositioning, accurately and at great speed. The sheets are pre-positioned during machine operation which reduces load time considerably.”

The rapid 1,300hpm punching speed adds productivity. Shear Brilliance offers a huge tooling capacity in a 24- or 30-station turret which ensures minimum set-up times and maximum tool quantity in single set-up. Tool sizes can be chosen by the customer, which adds the flexibility required in modern production.

Up to 35 tons of servo-electric ram force – the highest available in servo-electric punching – enables very complex contours, using one hit instead of two. As fewer hits are needed production is faster. Despite the ram force, speed has not been compromised.

Equipped with COMBO storage, the PSBB line is a suitable solution for lights-out production intricate components from a variety of materials, which can be changed, as programmed, automatically. The PSBB can also be integrated in Night Train FMS® solutions.

By combining punching and shearing in one machine, Prima Power has set new standards with its SGe5 Combi punch and right angle shear. Utilising servo electric motors for very low power consumption and low noise levels of 73.3dB, compared with 84dB for a hydraulic machine, the concept is to operate without human intervention from the blank to the automated sorting of the finished parts into up to 12 different bins.

“Suitable for short runs of components, which is becoming an increasingly common demand from customers, the SGe5 enables the mixing of different components on one sheet. For largely rectangular parts, the result can be zero or virtually zero material wastage as the right angle shearing can split parts in a single stroke without the need for a skeleton. The shearing operation results in a very high quality edge without any nibbling marks,” explained Prima Power.

PunchBend is Prima Power’s new compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials.

This year at Hanover, the central theme proposed by Italian manufacturer Salvagnini was process efficiency, expressed in different and original ways. First of all with the S4+P4 line, proposed in a new compact version, designed for an effective manufacturing flow of bent and punched parts and aimed at increasing flexibility and production.

With the new smart FlexCell, Salvagnini proposes a solution composed of stand-alone machines controlled by proprietary software, which guarantees efficient cell management and increases production effectiveness and ease of use.

German sheet metal specialist, TRUMPF, has a range of dedicated punching technology in its TruPunch range, which can be twinned with SheetMaster Compact automation for continuous operation. It also offers its TruMatic 7000 punch laser combination machines.

TRUMPF presented a wealth of innovations at the EuroBLECH 2014 exhibition. Among them was the TruMatic 6000 fiber. This combined punching and laser cutting machine exploits the benefits of the solid-state laser in a new performance class. High feed rates in thin sheet metal as well as great variety in the materials to be worked distinguish this high-productivity machine.

“The TruMatic 6000 fiber joins the advantages of the solid-state laser with high quality punching technology, making it a productive punch laser machine when working thin sheet metal. It uses the high cutting speeds possible with the TruDisk 3001 and combines its 180 kilonewtons of punching force, at 1,000 strokes per minute, with highly dynamic drives. It rapidly cuts, punches and shapes sheet metal up to 6.4mm thick,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

“When engraving, it marks parts at 2,800 strokes per minute. The strengths of the solid-state laser come to bear especially in galvanized mild steel and film-covered stainless steel. In this application area, the advancing speeds are as much as six times those of a CO2 laser of the same power.”

The solid-state laser also expands the range of materials that the TruMatic 6000 fiber can work. If desired, automatic loading and unloading can be handled by the SheetMaster, using individually controlled vacuum grippers. The SheetMaster sorts and positions the parts on carts or pallets. The machine discharges small items directly, via part removal flaps and into receiving containers. Tool changing is quick, thanks to the linear magazine.

Wilson Tool offers a comprehensive tooling line for punch presses, whether standard or special shapes; thick turret, thin turret or TRUMPF-style tooling. Its Optima® coating, Ultima® premium tool steel and EXP® punch technology are a few examples of its punch expertise. The company also offers a range of accessories for punch presses.

“Our EXP™ punch technology is the first tooling in the industry to offer a standard holder with truly universal punches. This innovative product increases productivity, reduces manufacturing waste, saves storage space while delivering the same performance, grind life and durability as our HP punch press tooling at a lower cost,” the tooling specialist explained.
