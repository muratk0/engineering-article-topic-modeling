# Universal gripper for robot stackers

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

EXHIBITING AT this year's EuroBLECH exhibition, Biele Group is a specialist in the manufacture of stacking lines for automotive components which incorporate robots. Over the last few years, the corporate group has supplied more than one hundred stackers for specific applications in response to the high standards of quality required by automotive OEMs, Tier 1s and Metal Service Centres.

In 2016, Biele Group made a great leap forward in the evolution of robot stackers with the development of a universal motor-driven gripper, adding new value to the robot stacker. Previously, the robot stacker used to force the utilisation of a gripper and a pre-centering set for each type of piece to be stacked. This led to recurrent costs and the need for a storage zone.

The universal gripper implemented on new robot stackers adapts them for any structural component, regardless of the shape and size. This gripper is automatically synchronized with the pre-centering system. Thus, investment in new grippers and storage for these is no longer required, with the resulting space and recurrent cost savings. The motor-driven universal grippers designed by Biele Group allow rapid and fully automatic change between references. The same gripper may be exchanged on any installation. Biele displayed the capabilities of its products on its booth at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition.
