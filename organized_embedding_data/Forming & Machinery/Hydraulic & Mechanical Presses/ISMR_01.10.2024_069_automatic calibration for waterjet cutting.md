# Automatic calibration for waterjet cutting

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

In precision waterjet cutting, tool change is a critical step. When a cutting tool is to be changed, careful calibration is required to ensure that precision is maintained. With Water Jet Sweden's new Auto TCP Calibration Tool, calibration can be performed automatically.

"It takes a fraction of the time, requires no special skills and always provides accurate results," WJS told ISMR.

The Auto TCP Calibration Tool consists of special software that plugs into the control system, a measuring probe with a base plate and a calibration ball. The product is used for all types of angled cutting i.e. machines that are equipped with Alphajet, Beveljet or FiveX cutting heads.

"For an experienced operator, manual calibration normally takes 15-30 minutes," explained Stefan Johansson, System Developer, Water Jet Sweden. "If you are unaccustomed to it, it can take even longer. With the new Auto TCP Calibration Tool, the measurement takes just three-five minutes but above all, you avoid the human factor and minimise the risk of errors. All changes are verified in a log that is saved.“

The Auto TCP Calibration Tool will be launched in the USA at the FABTECH trade fair in Orlando (15-17 October 2024) on stand S11170 (Hall North/South) The following week, it will be released in Europe (Hall 12, stand H116) at the EuroBLECH trade show in Hanover, Germany, on 22–25 October 2024.
