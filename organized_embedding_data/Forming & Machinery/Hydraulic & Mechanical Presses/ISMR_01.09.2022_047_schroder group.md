# Schröder Group

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.schroedergroup.eu

Schröder Group is taking its latest sheet-metal working machines as well as advanced automation solutions to this year's EuroBLECH in Hanover. Visitors to its stand will see precision sheet-metalworking shears and slip-bending rolls as well as folders for the sophisticated handicraft and industrial sectors.

"Our main stand highlight will be the EVO Center motorised sheet-metal folder with automatic clamping beam and folding beam tool-changer. Thanks to our in-house developed software control, POS 3000, this machine enables fully automatic processing of the entire folding program with no manual intervention," it outlined.

"Another highlight on our booth will be the PowerBend Professional sheet-metal folder with a working length of 3,200mm and sheet thickness of up to 3.0mm. This popular machine has an increased clamping beam stroke of 500mm and now offers more space for bulky workpieces," added the manufacturer.
