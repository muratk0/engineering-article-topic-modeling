# Multifunctional rollforming

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

DREISTERN’S multifunctional rollforming technology transforms the rollforming machine into a universal sheet metal processing cell. For many years, supplementary processes such as notching or embossing have been integrated into its rollforming lines.

“Users of multifunctional rollforming technology can quickly integrate almost any supplementary process or add forming stations for advanced product designs. This capability does not have to be paid for by higher initial cost (as is the case with conventional rollforming machines),” explained Dreistern.

The concept of multifunctional rollforming is based upon individual work modules, designed as mobile units with standardised interfaces and an independent control system. This allows positioning at any place in the rollformer within a few minutes. The punching modules are designed for both flying and Start-Stop® operation which allows elevated tolerances and high production speed.

“All work modules can be utilised on different machines. This is especially advantageous for costly work modules such as laser welding cells. Since laser welding can be used now on any rollforming line, it has become much more economical,” said Dreistern.

Dreistern will be exhibiting a multifunctional rollforming machine, in full operation, at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (21-25 October 2014).
