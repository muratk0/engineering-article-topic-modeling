# Launch of new 30kg collaborative robot

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Universal Robots, the Danish manufacturer of collaborative robots (cobots), expanded its product portfolio with a new 30kg payload cobot at the end of last year. UR30 is the second in Universal Robot's new series of next-generation cobots and is built on the same architecture as its awardwinning UR20.

"Despite its compact size, UR30 offers extraordinary lift and its superior motion control ensures the perfect placement of large payloads, allowing it to work at higher speeds and lift heavier loads. This makes UR30 ideal for several applications including machine tending, material handling and high-torque screwdriving. For machine tending, the high payload brings new possibilities as it allows the cobot to use multiple grippers at the same time. This means that it can remove finished parts and load more material in one single pass, shortening changeover times and maximizing productivity," said Universal Robots.

UR30 will also effectively support hightorque screwdriving as it can handle larger and higher-output torque tools and, using a steady mode feature, is designed for straight and consistent screwdriving. The 30kg payload also makes UR30 suitable for material handling and palletising of heavy products across all industries, with the small footprint enabling it to fit into most workspaces (relieving humans of heavy lifting). Weighing 63.5kg, it can be moved between work cells.

"The higher payload and greater flexibility underpin a new era in automation. Industries around the world are embracing more agile manufacturing and modularity in production. Part of achieving that modularity and agility is about mobility, and this cobot delivers that [regardless of] its payload," commented the company's President, Kim Povlsen.
