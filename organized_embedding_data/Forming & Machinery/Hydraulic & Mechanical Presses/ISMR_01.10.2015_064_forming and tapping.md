# Forming and tapping

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

YIH SHEN MACHINERY (YSM) has been developing forming machines for metal sheet and wire rod since the 1980s. The European market currently takes up 20 per cent of YSM's exports. 40 per cent of its machines are for the automotive and motorcycle sectors, a further 40 per cent is for the miniature circuit breaker sector and the rest is spread across other sectors.

"The primary application for our machines is in the automotive and motorcycle sectors for parts such as tube-mounted components, brake components and headlight-mounted components," declared Vice-General Manager, Lin Ching-Tang. “For sheet forming, we do not use the continual mould design but pre-press the sheet, then place it onto machines for the forming process which successfully reduces waste. With the all-in-one forming trend now prevalent in the industry, we have integrated presses, forming machines, tapping machines, welding equipment and automation in one and this design has helped our customers to keep their costs down.”

The YSM-36T strip and wire forming machine can process strip thicknesses from 0.3-1.6mm (max. strip width of 40mm) and wire diameters of Ø0.6 – 3.2mm. The machine can produce electronic/lighting components and fastener parts in the automotive sector. The forming slides use double cams and the integration of backward cams and slide sensors prevent forming tools from sticking, protecting the equipment and prolonging the life of the equipment. The YSM-36T forming and tapping machine can also be installed with INTERCOM automatic NC tapping made in Italy, providing a tapping solution for users. See www.ysm.com.tw/
