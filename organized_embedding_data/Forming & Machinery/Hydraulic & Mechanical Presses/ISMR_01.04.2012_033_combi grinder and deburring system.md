# 'COMBI' Grinder and Deburring System

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

FLADDER DANMARK A/S and Karl Heesemann Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG have launched a 'combi' machine for grinding and deburring. Both companies have cooperated with each other for many years delivering machines for automated lines, particularly in the North American market.

The calibrated grinding technology from Heesemann has been refined and developed through two generations. Deburring technology from Fladder Danmark has been developed and marketed since 1983.

"As a result of this cooperation, we are now able to launch a new machine that combines the two technologies into one machine that can perform grinding and the deburring in one operation. The first process is a belt grinding with the Heesemann MFA unit that can be equipped with up to two rubber-coated contact rollers to remove larger burrs and superfluous material as well as apply a finish to the surface."

The other process is deburring using the Fladder deburring unit with its rotating and oscillating gear head with counter-rotating spindles. Flexible abrasive cylinders remove burrs. The workpieces are brushed from different angles, regardless of where and how they are positioned on the conveyor belt. Because of the abrasive brush movements, edges are rounded uniformly.
