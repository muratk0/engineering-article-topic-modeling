# All-in-one forming

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

YIN SHEN MACHINERY CO., LTD was established in 1979, manufacturing machines for cutting, coiling and forming metal springs. The Taiwanese manufacturer has been working on press forming machines since the 1980s and recently sold its high-end press forming machines to the European market. The European market now takes up approximately 20% of YSM's exports.

"The primary application for our machines is in automotive and motorcycle sectors e.g. parts such as tube-mounted, brake- and headlight-mounted components," declared Vice General Manager, Lin Ching-Tang. For sheet forming, YSM does not adopt the continuous mould design, but pre-presses the sheet and puts it onto machines for the forming process which reduces waste. Together with the all-in-one forming trend now prevalent in the industry, Mr. Lin and his R&D crews have integrated presses, forming machines, tapping machines, welding equipment and automation into one design which has helped YSM customers to keep their costs down.

The YSM-36T strip and wire forming machine can process strip thicknesses of 0.3 – 1.6mm, up to a maximum strip width of 40mm. The machine can produce, for example, electronic lighting components and fastener parts in the automotive sector. It also features a PLC touch screen and forming slides with double cams. The integration of backward cams and slide sensors prevents forming tools from sticking, protecting the equipment and prolonging the lifetime of the equipment. The machine is installed with INTERCOM automatic NC tapping.
