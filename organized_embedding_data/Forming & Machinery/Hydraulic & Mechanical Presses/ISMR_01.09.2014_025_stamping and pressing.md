Interlaken Technology, a manufacturer of production press equipment for hydroforming, elevated temperature forming and hot stamping systems, has introduced a new four-column hydroforming press system for running highvolume production while reducing cycle time. Interlaken offers this new hydroforming press system in clamp forces ranging from 250 to 5,000 tons. Hydroformed parts are stronger and weigh less due to structural integrity and fewer welds or add-on pieces. In addition, costs are reduced and time is saved by eliminating secondary operations, reducing scrap, lowering material and manufacturing costs, and increasing design flexibility.

Established in 1937, JIER, the largest pressmaker in China, has been engaged in producing a variety of mechanical presses from C-frame to straightside presses (110t to 7,500t) including automated high speed press lines. JIER also supplies a package of tailored solutions to customers as well as transfer presses, progressive presses, cut-to-length lines, blanking lines and blank washers etc. It has also applied servo press/NC hydraulic die cushion technologies in its technical solutions. JIER recently provided high speed press lines to FORD.

Komatsu designs large presses for forming car roofs and doors. Its large presses are represented by a tandem press for a variety of wide applications and a transfer press for high productivity. Its hybrid AC servo press, H1F, combines advanced CNC controls and a hybrid drive mechanism (pat.p). Its large high speed transfer blanking press for processing car body panels improves productivity by 75 per cent, it told ISMR. Komatsu also provides blanking presses and coil lines.

LOIRE SAFE is a leading Spanish manufacturer of press hardening and hot stamping solutions. From its first line supplied with this technology in 1998, LOIRE SAFE has specialised in the manufacturing of complete installation turnkey lines.

“We have designed, manufactured and supplied more than 30 per cent of the lines installed all around the world. Our manufacturing programme also includes press production lines for manufacturing mainly automotive parts and hydraulic tryout presses,” it explained.

Mossini SpA offers solutions for sheet metal forming – conventional eccentric presses; mechanical presses with Link Drive slow motion; eccentric presses with programmable slow motion Desmodrive; hydraulic presses; triple-effect hydraulic presses; die spotting presses; multi-slide hydraulic presses and electric presses. Presses and lines for hot stamping, hot forging and rolling of rings complete its production range.

NEFF Presses supplies concepts for complete lines for pressure hardening as well as energy-efficient power units for hydraulic presses. Walter NEFF GmbH, based in Karlsruhe, was founded in 1948. Over 6,000 of its presses – from 25 to 30,000kN – are in service worldwide. The company’s product range comprises hydraulic presses for metal cold- and hot forming (press hardening process); pulsating blank holder (PBH®) technology; plastic forming plus monitored and documented assembly processes. In addition to the design and development of new machines and systems with accompanying technical support, Neff offers services such as official safety inspections, preventative maintenance, assembly and repair work, upgrades and modifications to all hydraulic presses.

Nidec Minster Corporation introduced its MMC press line of mechanical stamping presses to markets in North and South America. The machines are manufactured by Ningbo CFG Machinery Company in its factory located in Ningbo, China.

OMET is a partner for sheet metal stamping operations and specialises in the production of new presses, mechanical presses up to 1,200 tonnes and hydraulic presses of up to 500 tonnes. It supplies complete sheet metal stamping lines, presses, feeder lines, transfers etc. It overhauls, maintains and restores used presses of any type. OMET is also present in Poland as Omet Poland Sp.zo.o.

Founded in 1962, SEYI has established a strong position in the metalforming industry over the past 50 years. It offers mechanical presses, ranging in size from 25 to 4,000 tons, at facilities located in Taiwan and mainland China. It is expanding its Total Solution Service emphasising peripherals such as feed mechanisms, transfer equipment and other ancillaries to maximize SEYI press productivity.

SEYI’s products are sold to customers in over 40 countries around the world and have received numerous quality and industry awards. It has also supplied the world’s leading automotive companies. The aviation machinery and medical equipment industries are expected to be future areas of growth for the company.

At EuroBLECH 2014, German press manufacturer Schuler will present new processes, technologies and innovative solutions to increase energy efficiency. The specialist in metal forming, celebrating its 175th anniversary this year, has reduced energy consumption of its presses and automation systems. Visitors will see a blanking press with a new drive technology and a press force of 200 tons. Schuler will present new exhibits at the exhibition, including carbon parts of the BMW i3 and other car body parts which have been formed on its presses.

VACCAR screw presses are used by some of the world’s largest companies for forging steel, aluminium, brass, copper, titanium and special alloys. VACCARI designs and provides integrated solutions with manipulators, feeders, transfer machines and robots, allowing for the automation of industrial processes associated with forging, coining and calibrating.

ZANI is a pioneer in developing sophisticated mechanical presses (up to 30,000kN). Its press products include straightside, 1/2/4 con rods; Motion Master (modified double knuckle-joint drive motion for ram speed reduction); Servo Master (servo motors in connection with knuckle-joint kinematics) and Knuckle-Joint.
