Established in 1984, Jet Edge is a global designer and manufacturer of water jet systems for precision cutting, surface preparation and coating removal.

High pressure pumps, cutting heads, abrasive systems, high pressure tubes, original spare parts and a global service network are among the key products and services of KMT Waterjet Systems. KMT is working on the next generation of waterjet pumps and is already testing the Streamline PRO-II pump, which features a pressure range of up to 7,000 bar. This new development includes a revised pressure intensifier which uses fewer parts, thus further simplifying the service. Advancements have also been made in the life of internal components to provide greater durability of common consumable parts. The tests carried out so far have once again shown a considerable leap in productivity. After an extensive global testing phase, this innovation will probably be available in the fourth quarter of 2011.

The company has a strong catalogue of successful products. KMT has implemented its development plan to further enhance the product range. This process has seen the company release a ground-breaking waterjet cutter that has literally ‘raised the bar’. Its high performance Streamline PRO Range is capable of 6,200 bar – and marks a 50 per cent increase in pressure capability compared to KMT’s previous SL V system.

Right: High Flow Abrasivejet Cutting Head

KMT’s pump with the most powerful performance in this segment is the STREAMLINE PRO-I 125D high pressure pump. At maximum pressure, its 93 kW (125 PS) engine allows for a flow rate of 5.8 l/min. Compared to the STREAMLINE PRO-I 60S, it can operate twice as many cutting heads simultaneously. Therefore, the user is able to produce twice as many pieces at the same time and with almost the same floor space. Additionally, the high flow rate makes it possible to assemble a cutting head with an orifice size of up to 0.38mm in diameter so cutting extremely thick or solid materials is possible.

The cutting pressure of 6,200 bar is produced with the help of two pressure intensifiers operated with a phase shift that pump the cutting water to the cutting heads. This way, the pressure drop resulting from the shifting of the plungers can be almost levelled out. Combined with the interconnected 1.6 l pressure accumulator, this means virtually continuous pressure signal.

The inspiration for KMT’s Streamline PRO Range came from the growing demand for increased productivity from a large range of industries.

Peter Longman of KMT Waterjet Systems says: “Our strategy is to focus on making the strongest and most reliable high pressure water jet equipment whilst supporting it around the world.

“The horse power in the pumps hasn’t changed significantly, but by increasing the pressure and decreasing the jet diameter, the energy density hitting and cutting the material is much stronger and more efficient than before. This brings an extended list of benefits to the user – including higher cutting speeds, increased pressure flexibility, reduced use of abrasive and the ability to cut new materials.”

Cutting and piercing speeds are increased substantially and this gives a marked benefit to the edge quality and cutting taper. The Streamline PRO Range can operate with a variable pressure between 500 – 6,200 bar and can run multiple cutting heads from either of the 60hp or 125hp pumps.

“In addition to increasing linear cutting by more than 100 per cent, the second generation of the KMT Streamline PRO® Series of Ultra High Pressure Pumps cuts non-linear aluminum, steel, titanium, and stainless steel up to 75 per cent faster than standard waterjet systems cutting at 60,000 psi/4,137 bar,” said the company.

## WaterJet Sweden has announced

another addition to its precision waterjet cutting machine specification. Linear Drives now comes as standard on all models. The advantage of the programmable positioning of independent cutting heads, that comes with having linear drives, allows users to increase the scope and speed of production. This has also enabled the company to increase the traverse speed, on the X axis, by an impressive 200 per cent to a maximum of 30 m/min.

Linear drives, coupled with a new ‘super fast’ height sensing system, provide efficiencies for material nesting and quicker processing times. The ability to control nozzle wear offset for each individual cutting head allows even more precise common line cutting to get the best results.

WJS is the largest abrasive water jet cutting company in Europe with 9 x multi-head CNC water jet cutting machines including the largest and highest specification waterjet machines for aerospace profiling in Europe 12000mm x 3000mm. The scale and variation of machines enable The Water Jet Group able to cope with large volumes and quick turnaround. ■
