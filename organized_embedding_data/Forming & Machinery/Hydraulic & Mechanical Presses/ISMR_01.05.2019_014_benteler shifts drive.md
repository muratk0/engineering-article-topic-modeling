# BENTELER shifts drive

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

Ralf Göttel, CEO of BENTELER International AG

## BENTELER PRESENTED

ITS new Electric Drive System 2.0 at the Shanghai Auto Show. This platform solution for electric vehicles is now series-ready. It has evolved from the first rolling chassis prototype which had its world premiere in Shanghai in 2017. It is a showcase for the company's entire engineering and metal processing competence: from components and modules for chassis, body-in-white and engine & exhaust applications to modular e-mobility system solutions.

"The BENTELER Electric Drive System 2.0 is now ready for immediate implementation for our customers as it now includes tested and validated crash management and tuned axle systems for high driving comfort and handling. All interfaces to the car body are optimally defined. It saves our customers several years of development time and supports fast growth acceleration. We are offering entire system engineering and programme management from concept to start of production (SOP). We support our customers in contracted manufacturing or with a license for in-house production. The industrialisation concept is designed for cost-efficient production," BENTELER told SMR.

"Electric Drive System 2.0 is weight-optimised, adjustable to any customer requirement and scalable for numerous vehicle segments. It is specially designed for e-mobility and integrates several functions which can be ordered as a complete solution or as individual modules (such as integrated crash management; flexible and scalable battery storage system; electrified chassis solutions etc.). In cooperation with a well-known technology company, we recently proved the platform functionality and performance in real conditions. It was connected to a car body and has been driving on roads."

Despite the turbulence in the global economy and within the sector in 2018, BENTELER's revenue topped €8 billion for the year. However, the transformation of the automotive industry and the investments associated with this – gave rise to costs above plan. Added to this were external factors such as the trade conflict between the U.S. and China which started with punitive tariffs, and the introduction of the testing procedure for exhaust emissions, WLTP. In combination, this led to an EBIT of €119.3 million for BENTELER. The result of operating activities was thus €66 million below that of 2017 (adjusted for a special effect from a land sale).

At the same time, BENTELER adjusted its strategic orientation.

"We are placing our focus on balanced growth and increased profitability. In future, we will be concentrating even more closely on our core business," explained Ralf Göttel, CEO of BENTELER International AG. "In addition, we will invest in a targeted way in selected areas that fit with our core competence – for example, in electromobility."
