# Roll bending – from order to installation

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

PROFILE-BENDING MACHINERY manufacturer, AMOB, faced a tough technical challenge last year when two Taiwanese construction giants expressed interest in its products. The job, which required a machine that could manipulate the HEA800 beam 'the hard way' to a minimum radius of 20m (amongst other profiles with similar geometry) would enable the customers to manufacture new coal storage sites.

"With our Portugal-based facilities with in-house manufacturing capacity, dedicated bespoke project department and vast experience with similar projects all over the world, we won the bid. Once the decision was confirmed, manufacturing started immediately. Just like all our products, every aspect of the two MAH-700AC roll bending machines was made under one roof - from small design alterations, tailoring the machine to exact requirements, cutting the plate, welding the frame, machining the shafts and assembling all the components to painting, full testing and sign-off. Our capacity to produce everything in-house was one of the key reasons the customer chose us to complete the order – leading to reduced delivery times and no need for outsourcing," AMOB told ISMR.

As soon as the machines had rolled off the production line, both customers were invited to AMOB for acceptance tests. Once the machines were delivered, AMOB's training and commissioning teams flew out to commence installation and conduct start-up and advanced training. Following this was technical and maintenance guidance so that the customers could feel confident and self-sufficient.

AMOB S.A. provides one of the largest ranges of specialised equipment for the tube and pipe bending industry worldwide. See www.amob.pt
