# Dallan SpA

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.dallan.com/en/

Family-owned DALLAN SpA celebrated its 45th anniversary in 2023. It offers advanced systems to manufacture products from steel coil including punching machines; roll formers; laser cutting and packaging machines.

With more than 3,000 machines/systems installed and over 10,000 profiles made, DALLAN has specialised since 1978 in roll forming machines for thin and prefinished materials (e.g. aluminum shutter profiles and major metal ceiling profiles such as T-bar profiles).

The manufacturer has also introduced training tools and Masterclass webinars for engineers, production managers and technicians in the sheet metalworking sector, as well as a 'Manufacturing Efficiency' blog. The EXN is DALLAN's powerful, coil-fed electric punching machine, offering greatly reduced energy consumption. EXN II is the natural evolution of EXN. DALLAN's LXN Revolution is a coil-fed, laser cutting system.
