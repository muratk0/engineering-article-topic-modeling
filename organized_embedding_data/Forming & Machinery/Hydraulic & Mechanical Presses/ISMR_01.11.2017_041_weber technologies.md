# WEBER technologies

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

For years, WEBER has been using its planetary head technology for edge rounding. The tools are arranged in a compact machining station in groups on planetary heads. The ideal tool arrangement ensures evenness in the rounding process. The multi-rotation brush system machines parts with rim holes and slots from all sides, with even machining results along the entire machine width. Both systems are also used for scale removal in heavy plate machining.

Where large pieces require even surface structures, WEBER machines use a large tool carrier with various machining heads. The machines are controlled via a touch screen with an intuitive user interface. WEBER I-Touch enables systematic navigation and direct input.
