# MAGNETIC DRILLING MACHINE

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

SPECIALIST METALWORKING solutions supplier, JEI Solutions, unveiled the latest additions to its flagship MAGBEAST® range of portable magnetic drilling machines at MACH 2016 in the UK. Taking centre stage was its new MAGBEAST 5 – billed by the company as ‘the world’s largest portable magnetic drilling machine designed to solve the most challenging hole-making applications’. It can tap holes up to M52 and cut holes of up to 200mm in diameter.
