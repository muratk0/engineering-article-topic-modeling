# Accelerate manufacturing performance

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

VOORTMAN WILL SHOWCASE its latest innovations in machine and processing automation for the steel fabrication and manufacturing industries at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart this November. Its latest V310 CNC plate processing machine will be on display, featuring a drill spindle and plasma/oxy cutting capability. A heavy steel bridge ensures a higher allowable feed rate and provides extended consumable life with minimal vibration during processing. Its tool-changer moves along with the bridge which ensures instant tool changes of up to ten tools ‘with minimum increase in production time’.

“This machine offers diversity and flexibility in processing where cutting, drilling and marking are carried out in one process. It can also drill holes of up to 40mm. Manufacturers can speak with one of our experts about their production goals on our booth 5107 (Hall 5) at Blechexpo and find out how our robust product catalogue can upgrade their processing capabilities, improve part quality and save on labour costs, backed by our service and support,” commented Voortman.

“The Voortman V310 uses the fastest height control in the market. Combined with the reduced cut-to-cut cycle time of our Instant Cut Technology, users can make a 30% improvement in processing times for each product. Because Instant Cut Technology is built into our VACAM 4.0 control software, it remains productive by letting the software create the optimal production mix of speed and quality,” it continued.

When processing thick material, the V310 uses an oxy-fuel torch. This new torch is equipped with standard GCE nozzles and can be exchanged with a bayonet mount for minimum downtime. The ignition and height control systems are integrated with automatic height control during cutting, creating one solid torch. A built-in pressure gauge indicates the pressure during cutting for easy adjustment.

Voortman Steel Machinery has designed and developed machines and solutions for steel fabrication and manufacturing industries for 50 years. Its CNC plate processing machines integrate several functions designed to optimise workflow and are designed to reduce cost per part, including 3D precision plasma bevel cutting, oxy-fuel cutting, drilling and marking.

## PRODUCT REVIEWS

WITH THE BYTUBE 130, Bystronic is launching a new laser cutting system that offers manufacturers an easy entry into tube processing.

"In the past, many metal processing jobshops and original equipment manufacturers have limited their operations to the processing of sheet metal. In doing so, they have missed out on sales opportunities. With the new ByTube 130 laser cutting system, manufacturers can now gain access to new customer groups. After all, the fields of application for tubes are very diverse: from the automotive and construction industries right through to furniture, machine and equipment manufacturers. Exploiting this market potential is critical to success in the metal processing business," the Swiss sheet metal specialist told ISMR.

“The ByTube 130 automated system reduces the need for manual interventions to a minimum and makes entry into tube processing particularly easy. The machine covers a very wide range of requirements. Since 85 per cent of the market potential lies in the small tube segment, the ByTube 130 is geared toward the processing of tubes with diameters of between 10 and 130mm.

The machine has a loading capacity of 17kg per metre. The 2D cutting head allows a large proportion of customer requests to be processed, since vertical cuts account for 90 per cent of the market," it continued.

The wide processing spectrum offers users the flexibility required to process a diverse range of orders. In addition, the ByTube 130 has the potential to substitute complex and cost-intensive processing steps. A growing number of manufacturing companies are discovering laser cutting as an alternative to the two separate processes of sawing and drilling. The fibre laser performs both at once - and considerably faster.

"With clean cutting edges, deburring is also a thing of the past. This not only results in reduced labour costs, the higher throughput speeds also reduce the costs per part," added the manufacturer.

"Available in two performance levels – two or three kilowatts – the fibre laser aggregate of the ByTube 130 excels with outstanding energy efficiency. While fibre laser technology has already established itself for the cutting of sheet metal, it is now also gaining popularity for tube processing with thin and thicker material thicknesses. The consistent cutting quality is another compelling argument in favour of fibre laser technology. Due to its shorter wavelength, compared to CO₂ laser technology, it has no problems with highly reflective non-ferrous metals, such as copper and brass," explained Bystronic.

With the new ByVision Tube user interface, Bystronic combines all the functions relating to the laser cutting of tubes on a single touchscreen. Even entry-level users without extensive experience can immediately start producing with the ByTube 130. Cutting jobs are set up rapidly, and the interface is highly intuitive.

"With our ByTube 130, we are the only supplier in the world to offer a laser cutting system with a 'mirrored' configuration. This allows customers to choose the optimal layout for their specific production flow. Although this solution is simple at first glance the mirroring offers a number of benefits. Loading and unloading areas no longer get in the way of each other. This not only reduces the logistical workload, but also saves space and manpower. And faster throughput times result in a significant increase of productivity,' it added.
