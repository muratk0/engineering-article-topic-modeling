# Fourth press hardening line

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Xincheng Automotive Industrial Co., Ltd., a manufacturer of chassis parts for several Chinese and international car manufacturers, is ordering its fourth press hardening line since 2019 from press specialist, AP&T.

During the last three years, AP&T has received orders for three press hardening lines for Xincheng Automotive Industrials' manufacturing plants in Wuhan and Jingjiang, China. The first and second lines became operational at the beginning and end of 2021, respectively, and the third will complete SAT delivery in March 2022. Xincheng Automotive Industrial has now ordered another line for its Wuhan factory.

“Our decision to make this investment was spurred on by increasing demand for press-hardened components from many of our customers. By 2023, the new line will greatly increase our production capacity," said Huang Yinquan, Vice General Manager, Xincheng Automotive Industrial.

These orders from Xincheng Automotive Industrial strengthens AP&T's position in press hardening in the Chinese market. AP&T's established organisation in China has helped collaboration to continue throughout the pandemic.

“AP&T holds a significant share of the market and has a good reputation for press hardening in China. Its technology makes for efficient, stable production and it provides excellent service. These are vital factors for us," added Huang Yinquan.
