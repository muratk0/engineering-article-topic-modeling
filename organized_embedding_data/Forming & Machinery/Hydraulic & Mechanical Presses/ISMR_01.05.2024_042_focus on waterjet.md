# FOCUS ON WATERJET

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Intermac (part of the Biesse Group) offers its Primus range of waterjet-cutting systems. The five-axis cutting head is fitted with the patented Intermac infinite rotating C-axis system for making tilted cuts (+/-60°) on complex profiles, with no limits. This system, says Intermac, eliminates the errors due to rotating axis reverse clearance in traditional methods, and also guarantees a constant supply of abrasive material.

Jet Edge, Inc. manufactures complete systems for precision waterjet cutting, mobile waterjet cutting and UHP cleaning and surface preparation applications, as well as accessories. Models include the Edge X-3 mid-rail gantry waterjet, the Edge X-5 five-axis, mid-rail waterjet and the Edge dual-carriage, mid-rail waterjet with Aquavision DI controller. Its JetStart series waterjets feature HERO 50 intensifier pumps. Its Edge X3-HR (three-axis, high-rail waterjet) and Edge X5-HR (five-axis, high-rail waterjet) also feature the Aquavision DI controller.

It also offers a wide range of waterjet pumps, including hydraulic intensifier pumps and direct-drive pumps.

Jet Star International Ltd. is a European supplier of waterjet products such as garnet abrasive and spare parts designed for all types of waterjet machines.

Kerf Developments offers the Optima range of waterjet cutting machines. The Optima waterjet series has the option of a 3-, 4- or 5-axis cutting head. It is offered with an X-axis of 2-, 3- or 4m and a Y-axis span of 1-, 2-, 4- or 6m with bespoke dimensions available upon request. The Z-axis stroke enables cutting of material up to 180mm thick with precision levels of 0.1mm. The Optima 320 twin-head system cuts materials of up to 125mm thick with a cutting envelope of 3m x 2m.

The machines come with single or multiple cutting heads that feature automatic height adjustment, including anti-collision systems, and offer a range of optional extra items such as dual cutting heads using a spacer bar, dual independent heads, a rotary tilting head and GRS abrasive removal systems.

Kimtech Waterjet Machines offers three XY (small, medium and large) waterjet machines that cover most areas of application, as well as fully customised machines. The Swedish manufacturer has delivered over 300 waterjet cutting machines since start of production over 40 years ago. Its XY range of machines features abrasive waterjet cutting and comes with a variety of accessories.

KMT Waterjet celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2021. It specialises in the design and manufacture of waterjet-cutting pumps and technology, and specifically ultra-high pressure ('UHP') pump technology with water pressurised up to 90,000 PSI/6.200 bar. Its line of waterjet intensifier pumps, pure water only or abrasive waterjet cutting nozzles, abrasive management systems and waterjet parts/components are integrated with OEM waterjet machines. With its STREAMLINE models, the company incorporates IoT technology into its portfolio.

There are a variety of waterjet machine cutting systems including one-dimensional multi-head conveyor cutting; two-dimensional XY cutting; three-dimensional XYZ cutting (including Bevel & Taper control cutting); five-axis waterjet cutting and six-axis robotic pure water or abrasive waterjet cutting.

The KMT Streamline PRO® 90,000 PSI (6.200 bar) waterjet pump is designed for rapid cutting applications. The PRO 6.200 bar/50 HP Pump, it told /SMR, "will significantly increase cutting productivity by up to 40% over industry standard 4.137 bar/50hp cutting speeds." The company's NEO™ 60,000 PSI (4.100 bar) 50HP pump offers features such as proportional pressure control, a booster pump and soft-start motor to eliminate voltage spikes as well as threaded cylinder design, removable magnetic doors and patent-protected HP seal design.

Metallbau Müller GmbH (MBM) has developed a combination of abrasive sludge suction and cutting water treatment, which is also suitable for smaller waterjet cutters and complies, it told /SMR, with European wastewater introduction regulations.

The KMT Waterjet Cut Calculator 2.0 App, with an on-demand webinar, incorporates 25 materials and offers a wider range of substrate thicknesses, horsepower, orifice sizes and abrasive rates.

MicroStep, a manufacturer of CNC-controlled cutting systems, celebrated its thirty-year anniversary in 2021. The company develops and produces plasma, oxyfuel, laser and waterjet cutting systems. Its waterjet cutters include the AquaCut and WaterCut series. It also offers tested automation solutions, from material handling around the cutting system to picking and sorting of cut parts.

Muototerä produces FinJet waterjet-cutting machines as well as the new Tamcut waterjet-cutting system with 2000 bar direct-drive high-pressure pump. It also supplies high-pressure pumps, garnet abrasive and spare parts. The company is based in Tampere, Finland.

OMAX® Corporation is a manufacturer of rapid multi-axis abrasive waterjet systems, cutting 2D and 3D parts from a range of materials and thicknesses. Exemplified by the OMAX, OptiMAX; MAXIEM®, GlobalMAX®, MicroMAX and ProtoMAX® brands, the company designs waterjet systems with intuitive software, direct-drive pump technology and accessories. In April 2019, OMAX merged with Hypertherm.

In March 2022, Hypertherm's OMAX Corporation introduced its most advanced waterjet, the OptiMAX, a new generation of waterjet designed to quickly turn prints into parts while relying less on highly experienced operators. At EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, Germany, OMAX showcased its OptiMAX waterjet along with the ProtoMAX, a compact, self-contained cutting system suitable for prototyping and low-volume cutting. OMAX also featured its highly intuitive IntelliMAX software at the exhibition.

OMAX direct-drive pumps are designed to be compact, quiet, efficient, easy to maintain and inexpensive to operate. They include the EnduroMAX direct drive pump, MAXIEM (for the MAXIEM line of waterjets) and GlobalMAX. The DynaMAX intensifier pump is suitable for water-only (or pure waterjet) applications, multi-cutting head systems and etching. In March 2023, Hypertherm announced the release of its ProNest® LT 2023 version 15.1 nesting software, which is now available on a subscription basis for the waterjet cutting market.

Perndorfer Maschinenbau KG produces waterjet-cutting systems to customer requirements, as well as special machines and its WJC range. Another mainstay is mechanical contract manufacturing. Everything is manufactured and produced in Austria at its Kallham site.

Projet System is a supplier of pure waterjet spare parts; abrasive waterjet spare parts; high-pressure pumps; coils; high-pressure tubing and high-pressure coupling.

Rainford Precision's Finepart micro abrasive WMC500 II waterjet system is manufactured in Sweden and features a micro-fine jet of just 0.2mm. This, said the manufacturer, provides a positional accuracy of +/-2.5 microns, a repeatability of +/-2 microns and an adjustable jet pressure from 500 to 4000 bar.

Resato International's product range includes high-pressure technology waterjet-cutting systems as well as components and systems for testing, injection and control that operate up to 14,000 bars. Its modular waterjet cutting solutions include the ACM-2015 waterjet cutting machine. This machine is equipped with a three- or five-axis cutting head and is powered by a waterjet intensifier pump (type PJ4-4000).

The Resato Job manager, Resato EMS monitoring software and the Job Manager module ensure that multiple cutting jobs can be programmed. The EMS monitoring software monitors the cutting process 24/7. Products also include the double intensifier pump and the abrasive continuous supply mini.

StM Waterjet GmbH is a provider of waterjet cutting systems with offices in Eben, Austria; Schweinfurt in Germany and Reiden in Switzerland. It has developed the OneClean modular system. The abrasives recycling module of the OneClean system makes it possible to recycle more than half of the garnet for additional cutting tasks, it told /SMR. Its waterjet cutting systems include the STM MicroCut, STM EcoCut, STM MasterCut and STM PremiumCut. The STM TAC/12 bevel-cut compensation cutting head is designed for precision, speed and the efficient use of resources in waterjet cutting. The cutting head with integrated Taper Angle Control (TAC) and a swivel range of up to

12º compensates for angular error, enabling precise production even with high feed rates. Its EcoCut 4.0 waterjet-cutting system features overall height of less than 2m; a working area accessible from three sides and above/below water cutting.

Swift-Cut Automation offers the Swift-Jet Pro as its waterjet cutting solution. Its SJP 1500 and SJP 3000 models can cut metal of up to 50mm thick and low-density materials of up to 100mm thick.

TCI Cutting, a Spanish manufacturer of laser and waterjet cutting machines and intelligent automation systems, also develops intelligent production management software for autonomous and strategic decision-making (TCI Manager). It offers a wide range of waterjet cutting systems. Its BP-H COMBI cutting machine integrates, in a single machine, water cutting technology and HD plasma cutting. This machine incorporates the 5x Direct Drive 4.0 Infinite Loop® five-axis cutting head, which allows infinite rotation without rewinding in three-dimensional cuts, a height regulator, an anti-collision system and automatic sludge extraction, among others.

WJS Tech Day last October. Delegates viewed the combination of Alphajet TVL cutting tool with Micro cutting head from Water Jet Sweden.

TECHNI Waterjet manufactures waterjetcutting solutions for the metals, composites, glass and stone industries. Now part of the GMM Group, it controls the entire waterjet process in-house, with its own developed electric servo-driven pump (Quantum®Series Water Jet Pump) and five-axis cutting head. It holds several patents on its technologies.

"Our new Intec-G2® waterjet machine cuts parts up to 12" thick in most materials. Simple to use, its versatile cutting allows users to cut multiple material types on the same bed without having to change tooling," it explained. "Our Quantum NXT™ Series pump incorporates technology from the NASA space shuttle programme, making it 60% more efficient than standard hydraulic intensifiers."

The Intec™MG2 Abrasive Waterjet Series range includes the i35-G2; i510-G2; i713-G2; i815-G2; i1020-G2 and i1033-G2 machines. A remote-control pendant (MPG) allows users to manually wind forward or backward through a cutting path. Its PAC60™m five-axis cutting head is designed to produce parts with a true angle up to +/-60 degrees.

Uhde High Pressure Technologies GmbH (belonging to thyssenkrupp) has been designing and manufacturing ultra-highpressure pumps for pressures of up to 14,000 bar for industrial use for almost half a century. Its high-pressure pumps—the core element of the waterjet-cutting system—with stepless working pressures of up to 6,000 bar are implemented in many industrial sectors, from the food industry to aerospace.

Water Jet Sweden, based in Ronneby (southern Sweden) is a well-known waterjetcutting specialist with over 100 different waterjet cutters from entry level 2D machines and micro processing to big machines for large and odd sizes of material, with its five-year performance warranty. It develops, designs and builds industrial waterjet cutting machines starting from entry-level, with a wide range of optional functions, through to systems for highly bespoke waterjet cutting optimised to specific requirements. It celebrated its 30th anniversary last year.

"From 1m², up to the largest at 6m x 15m, these are professional, heavy-duty waterjetcutting machines made to last with typically 20+ years of machine life. Available from single-head up to six abrasive cutting heads, or 12 pure water heads, there's a choice of cutting table configurations and Z axis units with linear motors for maximum flexibility," it commented.

The FiveX Ultra is its new full 3D, five-axis cutting machine (0-120° cutting) designed to manage precision cutting assignments. It can be equipped with a FAWJ Micro Cutting Tool for extremely fine cuts and complex shapes. The Fine Abrasive Waterjet (FAWJ) cutting process bridges the gap between micro laser and EDM cutting and brings waterjet cutting into micro part processing. The NCM 10 Micro is its micro abrasive cutting model.

Its Alphajet, which it dubs 'the next generation of taper angle control and perpendicular waterjet cutting' is mechatronics based on compact design with a fixed TCP point. Up to four Alphajets can be fitted on the same machine, quadrupling the cutting capacity. Its multi-head CNC-controlled Premium cutting system is for 2D and 3D cutting.

WJS has showcased an Augmented Reality, 3D version of one of its advanced waterjet machines, the FiveX, so that visitors could get closer to the operational cutter and look at it in 1:1, 1:4 or 1:10 scale, and then take it home with them on their smartphone or tablet. The company's entry-level waterjet cutting systems include the T-model and H-model. Its Grand range is a heavy-duty waterjet cutting system with large tables sizes to manage large and odd-sized materials.

The five-axis Suprema 3D cutting system from Waterjet Corporation.

Italian manufacturer, Waterjet Corporation Srl, offers a range of new hybrid and electric servo intensifier pumps (such as EDRIVE, EDRIVE PRO and SERVODRIVE). Its EDGE 5 cutting system features integrated ITC. Its machines include cantilever and bridge designs and five-axis flying bridge technology (EDGE-5) which delivers significant productivity improvements. The company also offers a combination plasma and waterjet machine (the PlasmaJet model), as well as waterjet surfacetexturing technology.

Its waterjet-cutting systems include the three-axis Prima and Practica; the 3-5-axis RAM and Waterline as well as the Classica and Suprema. Its TopJet is an automatic waterjetcutting line.
