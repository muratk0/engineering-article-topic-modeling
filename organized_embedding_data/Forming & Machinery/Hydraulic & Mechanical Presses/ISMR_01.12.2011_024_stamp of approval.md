Thomas Kern of Dreher outlines the press specialist's strategic vision.

ISMR SAYS

"Dreher pinpoints component trends in automotive manufacturing which, it says, will trigger stronger demand for fully automated production lines for press-hardening"

ISMR: PLEASE OUTLINE YOUR MAIN ACHIEVEMENTS AND ACTIVITY TO DATE?

TK During the past five years, we have developed the company from a supplier of automation equipment, SA coil lines, transfer and feeder automation to a supplier of complete systems for the sheet metal forming industry. Although we do not build presses by ourselves, especially for the automotive industry, we are now an accepted supplier of complete stamping lines.

For complete lines, we cooperate with press makers worldwide and act as their supplier, their customer or as a project partner. Putting automation as the central focus of projects in the sheet metal industry is our major goal to provide our customers with the highest possible availability and reliability of complex stamping and production lines in the sheet metal industry.

ISMR: WHAT ARE YOUR VIEWS ON THE CURRENT BUSINESS CLIMATE? HOW DID THE GLOBAL ECONOMIC DOWNTURN AFFECT YOUR BUSINESS?

TK We are pleased with our current order situation. We read a lot of information about a possible global downturn of the industry in the newspapers, but neither we nor our customers can currently see any effect of this news on the real economy. We weathered the global economic crisis three years ago, thanks to some large projects from the automotive industry, without problems. We are also proud that, despite the difficult times, we were able to employ people due to the order backlog we had.
