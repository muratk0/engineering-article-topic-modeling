# Magnetic lifting system

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Alfra GmbH presented its TML 250 magnetic lifting system at the Schweissen and Schneiden trade show in Essen, Germany, this September.

"It has been designed for strong performance on thin-walled materials. Its dead weight is just 3.5kg so it is up

to 70 per cent lighter than magnets with similar performance. Maximum load capacity is 250kg (with 3:1 safety factor) and tear-off force is >750kg from 10mm steel (S 235). It offers 360 degrees rotatable load swivel and one-handed activation is possible (even between the flanges of a T-beam)," the manufacturer explained.

Alfra manufactures and distributes electrical devices and tools for punching and drilling that are suitable for use on materials with thicknesses ranging from 0.8mm to 100mm. It also offers a wide range of applications in patented magnetics and lifting technology.

The German manufacturer has two factory locations at Hockenheim and Berlin-Stahnsdorf.
