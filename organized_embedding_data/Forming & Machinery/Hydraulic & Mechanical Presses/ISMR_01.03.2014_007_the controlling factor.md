# The controlling factor

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

STEERTEC IN RAUFOSS, Norway, recently modernised its production equipment with help from press specialist, AP&T. The company specialises in the manufacture of cold formed aluminium parts for passenger cars, or more specifically parts for the steering system. Some of Steertec’s customers include German luxury car brands such as BMW, Daimler, Porsche and Audi.

After initial discussions with AP&T, which had previously supplied automation equipment to Steertec, the company decided to upgrade and rebuild five old hydraulic presses in the line: one 400-ton, one 200-ton and three 100-ton presses. At the same time, it ordered a new and customized AP&T press with capacity for one million parts per year. All of the new equipment was installed and fully operational by summer 2013.

“The new equipment has entailed unprecedented improvement. We obtained a stable and reliable process and cycle times have been shortened. The press line's production capacity has significantly increased," Steertec told ISMR. “The customised press has a separate pump for each of the seven cylinder functions/axes. The individual control allows several operations to be performed at the same time, completely independently of each other and without impacting each other." AP&T will be providing insight into the latest development in press hardening on its booth 4543 in Hall 4 at MACH 2014 in Birmingham (7-11 April 2014). For the first time in the UK, AP&T is presenting the press hardening process using advanced 3D technology for a wider audience. The simulations allow visitors to study in detail parts of the process that are otherwise impossible to demonstrate. ■
