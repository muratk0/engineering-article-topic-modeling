2-D and 3-D machine inspection systems offered

MC Machinery Systems Inc., a subsidiary of Mitsubishi Corp., has partnered with InspecVision Planar US to offer high-speed, integrated 2-D and 3-D measurement systems.

InspecVision Planar 2D can be used to measure or reverse-engineer flat parts that have been manufactured on laser, plasma, and waterjet cutting systems, as well as punched and some stamped parts.

The InspecVision Opti-Scan 3D uses a high-speed, high-resolution industrial camera and an LED DLP projector to scan the surfaces of an object. Patterns of light are beamed from the projector onto the part, and these patterns are recorded by the camera and used to create surface measurements.

This system also features a back-light table to capture the silhouette of a part. This silhouette image then is converted into 3-D edge measurements using the patented Planar Edge 3D™ technology. 3-D edges are essential when measuring the critical features of sheet metal parts, including the hole size and position, which are defined by the part’s edges, not its surfaces.

Saw designed for additive manufacturing

Kasto Maschinenbau GmbH & Co. KG has developed the KASTOwin amc automatic band saw for individual off cuts of components made by additive manufacturing (AM). It features an integrated 180-degree turning device and intelligent control system.

The saw separates components made with AM from their base plates. With the turning device rotating parts upside down, wear on the saw blade is reduced because only small quantities of chips are drawn through the cutting channel.

The base plate with AM parts is bolted to the fixture by means of a handling unit or crane. Alternatively, it can be fixed in position with an optional quick-clamping system. The machine is prepared for a suction unit so that components can be turned and sawed in automatic mode with minimum dust generation.

The operator enters the thickness of the base plate via the SmartControl controller and its job wizard. The upper section of the saw, which has a ball screw spindle drive, then moves to the required height, and the saw automatically separates the parts with the required tolerance.
