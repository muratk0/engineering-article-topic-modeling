# Accent on automation

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

With the order for a SpeedFeeder to be delivered in Spring 2015, C&K Johansen will complete expansion to a fully automated 4-press line

IN 2007, C&K JOHANSEN in Denmark began gradually expanding an existing press into a complete 4-press line with a shuttle and three SpeedFeeders from AP&T. Three of the presses had been previously delivered by AP&T. The smallest one, at 250 metric tons, was completely renovated to meet demands for high productivity and availability.

The company has now placed an order for another SpeedFeeder to complete the installation. This means that the flow between the presses will be much quicker, and it will be easier to apply new parts in production. The new SpeedFeeder will also make it easier to handle blanks. A double gripper was previously used which frequently needed to be exchanged to cope with several operations with the same feeder. Delivery and installation of the last SpeedFeeder is expected to be completed by spring 2015.

Part of C&K Johansen's long term strategy is to continue automating production.

"Efficient and flexible production with a high degree of availability for the machinery is a prerequisite for our continued competitiveness," said Peter Møller, President, C&K Johansen AS Denmark. The company is a leading supplier of deep-drawn sheet metal parts in long and short batches to most industries. Some of the products formed by the company include brake components, engine parts for trucks and mountings and parts for farm machinery. ■
