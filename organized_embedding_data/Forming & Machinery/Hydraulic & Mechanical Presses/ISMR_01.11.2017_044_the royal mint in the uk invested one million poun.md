# The Royal Mint in the UK invested one million pounds on a new Bruderer press for the new one pound coin.

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

Stamping technology is playing a crucial role in the production of the new one pound coin. Bruderer UK, which has nearly 50 years' experience of manufacturing precision high-speed presses, has installed a new press into the Royal Mint's Llantrisant facility in Wales (UK), giving it additional speed, capacity and flexibility.

The Royal Mint is the world's leading export mint, making coins and medals for an average of 60 countries every year. However, its first responsibility is to make and distribute United Kingdom coins as well as to supply blanks and official medals. The Royal Mint is the largest integrated and most technically advanced minting facility in the world. It occupies a 13-hectare (35-acre) site and employs over 900 people.

## Packing a punch

"Capable of up to 825 strokes per minute, the BSTA 1600-117B2 is responsible for creating the hard-cut blanks that form one of the first processes in the manufacture of the new 'pound' coin. The coining press has been specified with an 1170mm press bed length and is capable of feeding material of up to 500mm wide x 12mm thick - ideal for tooling for different types of currency production now and in the future," the press specialist told ISMR.

It also comes equipped with the latest B2 control system, meaning everything can be controlled from the Human Machine Interface (HMI), including full operational setting of the feed and speed, together with monitoring control of the entire stamping operation.

"At an investment of over £1m, this is the largest single order ever placed with Bruderer UK and certainly one of the most prestigious, in recent years," explained Adrian Haller, Managing Director, Bruderer UK.

"Our relationship with the Royal Mint stretches back nearly 40 years and, as part of its latest round of capital investment, we were asked to tender for a new press. After spending time with the production/engineering team, we identified the Bruderer BSTA 1600-117B2 as the ideal machine to meet its requirements for greater speed and power – taking them from 125 tons up to a special execution of 180 tons' press capacity."
