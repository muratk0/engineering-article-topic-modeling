# Gripping solutions from Schunk

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

AT THE ADVANCED Manufacturing Show at the Birmingham NEC (UK) from 2-4 June 2015, Schunk will be demonstrating the Schunk SVH servo-electric 5-finger gripping hand complete with the Schunk lightweight arm, the LWA that will be controlled with a tablet via a Wi-Fi connection.

The innovative new hand will be introduced at the show to support the rapidly growing automation demands of the UK manufacturing sector. Replicating the movements and characteristics of the human hand with its four fingers and a thumb configuration, the SVH has a total of nine drives that permit various gripping operations to be executed with high sensitivity. Schunk will also be demonstrating the capabilities of its MGP-plus 2-finger gripper for small components.

Schunk will also be showing a number of toolholding systems on Stand A404 to highlight the diversity of the product line. This includes the TENDO-E compact toolholding system which can accommodate tool shank diameters of up to 20mm and the Tribos system (to ensure maximum tool life). Built for high-speed cutting at up to 60,000rpm, the Tribos has high radial rigidity and strong vibration. Schunk will also be presenting examples of its Vero-S range of quick-change pallet modules and KONTEC KSC centric clamping vice.
