Fagor Arrasate engineers and manufactures lines for cutting and processing metal coil; presses and complete turnkey installations for cold and hot stamping; special lines for manufacturing complex sheet-metal parts and systems for cold, warm and hot forging. Lines include transfer presses, conventional mechanical presses, hydraulic presses and servo presses.

"We operate globally with customers including OEMs and Tier Ones in the automotive sector; processors of coils and blanks in steel or aluminium; forged parts or electric rolled steel producers as well as manufacturers of home appliances and metallic furniture," it told /SMR.

Its high-speed press line (WaveLine concept) has been developed to transfer large, stamped parts (such as body side, hood, roof and double-door units) through large tandem press lines. They deliver output ratings of up to 17spm in XL-type press lines and up to 21spm in L-type ones. Its press-blanking lines enable complex geometries to be cut and stacked at a high rate.

## Jorns AG

Jorns AG, as a Swiss manufacturer of folders/bending systems, will exhibit for the first time at EuroBLECH 2022 with Krasser GmbH (a manufacturer of sheet-metal cutting and coil processing systems) at their joint exhibition stand H132.

Customers of the two machine-tool manufacturers are active in the tinsmith, roofing and façade construction sectors.

“There is clearly an increasing demand in the market for cutting and bending machines to 'communicate' with each other to increase productivity and reduce lead times, error rates and material consumption. We are pleased to be able to present our complete package to customers as an endto-end solution in sheet-metal processing," explained Jorns.

The semi-automatic Jorns JB folder and fully automatic Jorns JDB double bending folder can be configured in lengths of 3.2 to 12.2 metres and boast bending capacities for sheet metal of up to 3mm.
