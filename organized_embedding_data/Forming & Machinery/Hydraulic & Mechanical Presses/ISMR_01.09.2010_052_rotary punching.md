# ROTARY PUNCHING

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Rotary punching has steadily gained in importance in the manufacturing of drywall construction profiles following decades of domination by the conventional reciprocating stamping process. Until recently, the manufacturing bottleneck was the production speed of the punching process.

“However, today the rotary punching process can almost quadruple the speed of the punching operation. As a result, more manufacturers in Europe are converting to the technology. Speeds of up to 200 m/min. for the technology we have developed are much more efficient,” said Baust Stanztechnologie.

Material is fed into Baust’s Rotary Punch BRS 650 from the coil, punched and then formed in the profiling unit. After a simple tool change, framework profiles can be produced on the same unit complete with punched holes typically used for electrical installations. Various lengths of framework profile and a variable hole pitch can be processed. The required framework length is input via the control; the framework is then slowed and /or accelerated as required and at the appropriate moment. Using this method, the hole pitch can be accurately and flexibly programmed. Profiles for shadow joints and suspended ceiling profiles can also be produced on this type of machine.

“The Baust rotary punch Duo BRS 650 enables optimum application for base profiles with various product widths,” explained the company. “Two punching units are mounted, one in front of the other and can be infinitely adjusted to the various products. The adjustment for each product width can be carried out manually or automatically. UA profiles with high material thickness of 2.00mm can also be produced on the Baust Rotary Punch BRS 900. It is now possible to reach a production speed of 80 m/min. and significantly reduce manufacturing workpiece cost.”

## VERSATILE TIG WELDERS

Designed for a wide range of TIG welding applications, the Murex Tradestig AC220 and AC220a compact welding units are designed for use by the craftsman welder. These Tradestigs are extremely robust with an impact-resistant polymer casing, making them suitable for use in tough industrial environments including the metal fabrication industry. They are light and easy to carry and with IP23 protection can be used both outside and indoors.

“The Tradestigs are designed for quality TIG welding with all types of material of thicknesses up to five millimetres. They feature QWave technology for high arc stability and low noise levels in AC mode. True MMA welding characteristics are provided in both AC and DC modes including hot start, arc force and polarity switch (DC). A two-program function allows pre-programming and program change during welding operations. An electrode preheating facility ensures excellent weld starts and extended electrode life,” said Murex.

With the Tradestig AC220, the user simply needs to set the plate thickness on the control panel and the machine will do the rest. Users can still, however, control the welding current, slope down and post-gas manually. The AC220A control panel has several advanced functions including Pulse TIG, AC balance, to control arc cleaning and penetration and AC frequency to control arc width.
