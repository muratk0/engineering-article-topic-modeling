# Schuler supplies presses for U.S. and Mexico the auto

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

Schuler has received a major order from a U.S. automotive supplier. The world's largest press manufacturer will deliver a total of 15 machines, including automation, with a press force of between 400 and 2,000 tons, to the customer's sites in the U.S. and Mexico. The package will include transfer, ProgDie and stamping presses. The Schuler Weingarten site played a substantial role in concluding the major project.

This is the fourth order for Schuler from this customer in two years. In 2015 and 2016, the U.S. supplier ordered several transfer presses, including automation.

Schuler provides process expertise and service for the metalworking industry and lightweight automobile construction. Schuler is also a specialist in coin minting presses and implements system solutions for the aerospace, rail transport and large-dimension pipe manufacturing sectors. In the 2016 fiscal year, Schuler generated sales of 1,174.2 million euros.

After acquiring toolmaker AWEBA and the majority stake in Chinese press manufacturer, Yadon, Schuler is now present in 40 countries with around 6,600 employees. Schuler is majority-owned by the Austrian ANDRITZ Group.
