Turkish manufacturer, Baykal Machinery, is a leading sheet-metal processing machine manufacturer with 650 team members and 80 R&D engineers in three facilities (70,000m² total production area). The company specialises in the production of press brakes; shears; lasercutting systems, tube cutting and plasma cutting machines.

"We are increasing our R&D investments into the latest technological developments and further innovation. We offer, for example, a range of eco-friendly electrical models. Baykal machines are used by more than 50,000 customers worldwide including Porsche, BMW, Mercedes, Boeing, Siemens and HP," it told ISMR.

"Our wide range of Industry 4.0-ready press brakes are designed for accuracy; our bending solutions can be customised to fit different requirements. Our biggest press brake can bend 50mm stainless steel; a testament to our 'Think Big' motto," it continued.

Baykal fibre laser cutting systems are designed for robust continuous operation as well as accuracy, efficiency and speed. Since its first step into this area in 2007, Baykal has now become a major manufacturer of fibre laser cutting systems in Turkey.
