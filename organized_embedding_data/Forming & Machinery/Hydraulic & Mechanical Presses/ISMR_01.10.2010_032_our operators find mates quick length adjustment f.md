## SET-UP TIME SAVINGS

Melitron is also phasing in Mate’s Ultra Tec tooling system because it eliminates the need for measuring and shimming punches, a slow process which requires hand tools. In contrast, if conventional tools require exchange when dulling occurs, often in the middle of a part run, operators need to be adept at the tool shimming and set-up process to minimise machine downtime. All of that is eliminated with the Ultra Tec tool system.

“Our operators find Mate’s quick length adjustment feature easy and a real time saver, especially when setting up forming tools,” reports Mr Smit. “The tooling’s indexing mechanism lets the operator know how much shorter or longer they are setting the punch. This is done without disassembling the tooling or removing the tooling from the turret. So, there are two-fold time savings. Mate’s consistent die height allows our programmers and operators the versatility to put more forming tools side-by-side in the turrets.”

Mate Ultra Tec tooling disassembles with a touch of a button and goes back together just as fast. Tool length is set in five seconds without shimming. The tooling’s push-button design allows for rapid and easy setting of overall length and die penetration in precise increments without the need for measuring tools (0.006 inch for ½-inch A station tools and 0.0008 inch for larger station tools). The other components are easily disassembled by hand and locked up rapidly using Mate’s pre-set 75 ft/lb torque wrench.
