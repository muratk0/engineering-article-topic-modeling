LDM has over 40 years of experience in designing, manufacturing, installing and set-up of coil processing machines; slitting and cut-to-length lines; coil rewinding lines and revamping used lines with the latest technologies.

"We are recognised for the quality of our equipment and client-oriented approach. Our main investment policy is the continuous quality increase of our machinery using the most up-to-date technologies and design solutions," it outlined.

LDM also builds several types of shears (material thicknesses from 0.3mm to 16mm max. and material width of 2100mm). "For cut-to-length lines, we provide fixed shears with cut-off controlled by the power drive along with clutch and brake. We also supply flying shears mounted on a movable base with recirculating screw balls and motor-

gearbox drive with inverter for accuracy of cut-off. For slitting lines, we offer rotary blades as well as rotary blade shears for coil edge trimming," it added.
