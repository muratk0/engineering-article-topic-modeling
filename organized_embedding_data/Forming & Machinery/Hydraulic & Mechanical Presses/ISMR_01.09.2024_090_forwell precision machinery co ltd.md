“If you want to automate your press line; enjoy a personalised service; save on production times; improve safety and integrate your business into the world of Industry 4.0, FORWELL can help you," the Taiwanese manufacturer told ISMR.

"Our Automated Die Change System (QDCS) has been designed to prevent workplace accidents and human error while speeding up production by reducing die change from one hour of work to ten simple minutes. In addition, its automation is easy to use; at the push of a button on the control screen, QDCS saves 90% of the time it takes to change a die as well as saving 90% of production costs," it continued.

Forwell's Quick Die Change System includes a power unit; die clamps; die lifters; die arms and a control panel. Once fitted to a hydraulic press, the manufacturer promises reduced die change time; diverse products in small batches; reduced inventory; improved productivity and quality as well as reduced labour costs.
