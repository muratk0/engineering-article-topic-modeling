# Punching and bending systems

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Pivatic Oy manufactures punching and bending lines for automatic sheet-metal production. The company's focus is “to deliver tailored high-performance, reliable solutions to ensure the most productivity and customer satisfaction."

“For EuroBLECH this year, we will be showcasing a fully automated servo-electric coil punching, cutting and pressing line on the stand. Highly efficient and indexable Thick Turret punching, combined with a separate cutting station and a press tool station of 1200kN punching force, allows jobs (such as hole group, embossing, corner notching and trimming) to be flexibly produced," explained the Finnish manufacturer.

“Energy consumption for the full line is between 12-18kWh, depending upon the use of the big press, which is 60-70% lower compared to traditional systems. This supports our low-energy product solutions towards global sustainable development," it concluded.

The company was founded in 1975. Its production facilities and headquarters are at Hyvinkää, Finland. Pivatic also has a worldwide sales and partner network of more than 30 representatives. It is a member of The Ursviken Group, a global provider of solutions for sheet-metal manufacturers.

Meet Pivatic on its stand E136 in Hall 11 at the EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition from 25-28 October 2022 in Hanover, Germany.
