Avdel® a leading brand from STANLEY Engineered Fastening, recently introduced the Infalok® range of large diameter, high tensile lockbolt fasteners. These exceptionally strong fasteners are capable of withstanding up to 30 tons of tensile load – and to install them an equally heavy duty placing tool is required. The new Avdel “AV™” range of placement tools was also launched with a complimentary range of pump units for placing high strength Infalok® lockbolts as well as larger sizes of Avbolt® structural blind fasteners.

The company’s UK design engineers have also been developing a new hand-held pneumatic riveting tool for installing smaller Avbolt® fasteners and Avdelok® lockbolts. The 73200 tool with its “clean sheet” design is fast and light. STANLEY Engineered Fastening belongs to the Industrial group of Stanley Black & Decker, an S&P 500 company.

Mate’s new hybrid Threadform forming tool for punch presses provides a new solution for fastening two pieces of sheet metal together without tapping or secondary operations. Designed for use when the material thickness is greater than the screw pitch, Mate’s Threadform forming tool thins the material in the centre of the form, creating a threadform helix so that the material fits between the pitch of the screw head in just one operation.

Benefits of using the forming tool include the elimination of tapping and other labour-intensive secondary operations such as welding fasteners to sheet metal. The tool also eliminates debris when tapping and reduces component cost by eliminating the need for special fasteners.

Northern Precision Ltd is a leading European supplier of specialist fasteners for sheet metal, inserts for plastics, installation equipment, as well as bespoke bar turned and cold headed parts. Its core product ranges include self-clinching fasteners; rivet bushes; blind rivet nuts, blind rivets; cage nuts; weld studs and inserts for plastics. It is also an authorised distributor in the UK for Arnold & Shinjo Fastener Systems and its range of branded nuts, studs and installation systems and LCM Machine Tools with a range of fastener installation presses and bespoke tooling solutions.

PEM® self-clinching fasteners, from Penn Engineering, for use in stainless steel assemblies install reliably and permanently into thin stainless steel sheets and enable lighter designs by minimising hardware parts count. This growing family of special hardened stainless fasteners includes a wide range of types and styles with some providing the added benefit of strong corrosion resistance.

Depending upon type, the fasteners are produced from precipitation hardening grade stainless, 400 Series stainless or A286 stainless steels. Most are designed to install in stainless sheets as thin as 0.030”/0.8mm. With the fastener harder than the host stainless sheet, successful clinching results in their becoming integral parts of an assembly without risk of loosening hardware.

PROFIL develops pierce nuts and studs which are fastened to metal shaped parts by means of a riveting process. It is a system supplier that also constructs automated feeding equipment which is customised to meet individual production requirements. The company is a pioneer in mechanically joined fastener technology and has steadily continued its development throughout the last decades.

All major European automobile manufacturers and their suppliers are its customers and PROFIL fasteners can be found not only in the car body, but also in the subsystems closures, seats, instrument panels, pedals etc.

From small beginnings, TR Fastenings has grown into a multi-million pound business, supplying over 150 million components every day to thousands of customers. From its own-brand self-clinch sheet metal fasteners to its new range of plastic fasteners and cable management products, TR invests heavily into the research and development of new products.

The company has refined and developed its versatile Binx Nut to create a new all-metal, self-locking nut suitable for use across numerous industry sectors. No bigger than a standard full nut, the Binx Nut’s design incorporates two opposing cantilevers which lock into the mating part with inward and downward pressure, thus minimising thread wear and enabling the nut to be removed and replaced repeatedly whilst retaining torque resistance.

These pioneering new fasteners are unaffected by temperature fluctuations or contaminants, making them suitable for a range of applications, such as electrical and domestic appliances and locomotive. The Binx Nut is one of a wide range of self-locking components offered by TR Fastenings, which also includes Nylon insert nuts, Tri 5 and Tri 6 nuts, Hank-loc and Wun-Loc nuts and materials such as mild steel, brass and stainless steel. ■
