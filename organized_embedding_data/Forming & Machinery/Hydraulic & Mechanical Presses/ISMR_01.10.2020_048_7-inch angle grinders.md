# 7-inch angle grinders

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

FUJI HAS INTRODUCED a new series of 7-inch angle grinders to help metalworkers improve productivity across a range of demanding applications. Compact and lightweight, but durable, the FA-67 series combines power with a user-friendly design to make time-consuming grinding tasks easier to perform. Suitable for use in harsh environments, the series can be used for a range of heavy metal applications, including foundry, bridge and road construction, shipbuilding and rolling stock.

“The FA-67 series has been built with ergonomics in mind. The tools are 25 per cent lighter and 15 per cent smaller than traditional 7-inch grinders, yet they deliver the same performance. The tools are easy to handle, even over long periods, and the 2.1hp motor maintains rotation speed to ensure optimal grinding performance at all times,” said Fuji.

“The compact and lightweight body is designed to ensure easy manoeuvrability. The tools are equipped with an easy-to-use trigger that can be adapted to markets, user habits and safety requirements. For enhanced safety, the series has a flange design which transmits the power to the disk, reducing vibration levels for operators. The compact head of the grinder enables excellent visibility over the piece being worked on to help improve precision and achieve a quality finish,” it added.

The angle grinders are protected by a full metal housing. Innovative design enables a smoother flow through the bevel gear to extend the tool’s working life with air cooling and lubrication. See www.fujitools.com
