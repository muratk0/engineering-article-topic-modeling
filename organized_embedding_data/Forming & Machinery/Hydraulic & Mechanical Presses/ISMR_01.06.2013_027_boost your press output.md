# Boost your press output

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

NEW SCHULER PRESSES have been successfully using ServoDirect technology for several years now. With the aid of Schuler’s patented Dualdrive technology, it is now also possible to retrofit conventional flywheel presses with this new technology. These retrofits can boost the productivity of existing presses by up to 50 per cent, Schuler told ISMR.

The servo enables users to freely program the slide speed before and after the forming phase. The slide is only moved by the existing flywheel drive during the forming phase. This shortens cycle times whilst expanding the range of possible product variants. To make the best use of this increased performance, the machine’s peripheral equipment can also be adapted and modernised.

“Despite the additional servo motor, the modernised press boasts improved energy efficiency: the rapid increase in productivity reduces energy consumption per part produced.

"As the flywheel drive is still used for the forming operation, existing dies and tooling can continue to be used without adjustment – as too can the control system, to a large extent. Our service experts simply enhance it with the addition of a servo module and adapt the drive control and feed. The investment quickly pays for itself,” Schuler added.
