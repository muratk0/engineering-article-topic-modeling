# Semi-Auto Cold Saws

[TARİH: 01.06.2026 The Fabricator]

MACC SEMI-AUTOMATIC COLD SAWS

The MACC Semi-Automatic Cold Saw series has similar features to that of the manual style saws but with added capabilities. By a press of a pedal, the machine clamps, cuts, and returns automatically. This ensures consistent cut pressure, extending blade life and significantly reduces operator fatigue during production runs. the up and down speed movement with a flow control descent valve ensures seamless, repeatable cuts.

MOVABLE CUTTING HEAD

Provides wide-angle head articulation for versatile cut positioning, with pivoting motion up to 45° left and right (and up to 60° right on select double-column machines), enabling angled cuts without manual repositioning of the workpiece.

DOUBLE QUICK-LOCKING VICE

Securely clamps round, square, and rectangular stock with a rapid lock mechanism, ensuring stable holding during cutting and quick, repeatable setups between parts.

COOLANT SYSTEM

Maintains cutting performance and extends tool life with a power-driven coolant pump. Includes a coolant filter and optional LED separation system to keep the blade lubricated and free of debris.

ANTI-BURR CONTROL

Enhances cut quality and reduces post-cut cleanup with anti-burr vice devices and chip breakers on double-column models. These features help keep the blade clean and deliver smoother finishes.

PEDAL-ACTIVATED SEMI-AUTO CYCLE

Enables controlled semi-automatic operation via foot pedal. The system initiates vice closure, blade engagement, down-stroke cutting, automatic return, and vice release-reducing operator fatigue and ensuring consistent productivity.

SUPPORT ARM AND BAR STOP

Provides enhanced stability and precision during cutting. These features enable quick material handling and ensure consistent, accurate cuts across various projects.

SUPPORT ARM: Provides long secure and stable material support.

BAR STOP: Allows for precise, repeatable length adjustments for greater efficiency.
