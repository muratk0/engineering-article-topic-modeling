# We examine a selection of new coil processing trends and initiatives with an eye on the future

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

Coil processing lines have to operate at optimum efficiency or they can turn into money-losing operations. Margins can be so thin that even a 1% drop in efficiency could dramatically impact a manufacturer’s profit margin. Here, we preview some of the latest new global innovations and technology developments in coil processing and handling technologies.

## Innovation in focus

Founded in 1928, ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH is a roller levelling and press feeding specialist. Its product range comprises precision levellers for parts, cut-to-length and slitting lines, press feeding lines and coil preparation lines for rollformers. Its latest innovations include the new Arvis 4.0 interface (demonstrated on its HiCap® leveller) for full visualisation of all process steps in preparation for Industry 4.0, and the EdgeBreaker and EdgeRacer deburring machines.

The Bradbury Co., Inc. was awarded Patent #8893537 for its advanced e•Drive® roller levelling technology. Bradbury has produced over 50 levelling machines equipped with this controlled tension technology, in gauges ranging between 0.015” to 1”. Bradbury Group Australia, working with Bradbury Group Pu.ma., recently designed a low cost solution for PU insulated panel manufacturing. The entry-level solution, named BGA IntroPlant™ 100, will produce insulated roof and wall panels of up to 100mm thick.

The Bradbury Process Line Group also recently completed commissioning of a new 3/4” x 96 cut to length line at Aceros Tepotzotlan in Monclova, Mexico. Its sister company, Aceros Dondisch, in Mexico City was the first company in Mexico to decide on Bradbury e-drive® technology in 2008. The line itself was produced by Bradbury company, Athader, in Spain while the e-drive® levellers were supplied from Bradbury USA.

Companies in The Bradbury Group are leading manufacturers and worldwide suppliers of roll forming and coil processing equipment for a wide range of industries. Athader is a leading European supplier of coil processing lines. It is based in the city of Renteria in one of the most highly industrialised areas of Spain, the Basque Country. Its product range includes automatic slitting lines, cut-to-length lines, combined lines (slitting and cut-to-length) and multiblanking lines.

Continuous development since 1940 has made Bronx International a global innovator in the design, manufacture and technology of colour (coil) coating lines; galvanizing and metal coating lines; combination lines; chemical and paint coaters; tension levelling lines and strip joiners. It successfully completed the installation of a compact warehouse coil paint line in Minsk (Belarus) in October 2015. The process line has a maximum line process speed of 40m/min. Bronx has also formed an agreement with the Galfan Technology Centre to become the exclusive agent for Galfan® coatings in sheet and coil applications.

French manufacturer, Dimeco Alipresse, offers a wide product range including stock decoilers, straighteners, NC feeders and shears combined in short line, space-saver or rear loop arrangements. Dimeco also develops tailormade, flexible metalforming production systems to process coil in steel, pre-painted aluminium or stainless steel. Its Flexilines (Automatic Flexible Forming Lines) integrate complex technologies in feeding production lines such as coils, bending and DNC punching units, rollforming and laser assembling equipment.

At its booth at the 2015 Tolexpo show, the company exhibited a zig-zag press feed line including coil handling and coil feeding/deburring. At FABTECH in November 2015, it exhibited a coil-fed fibre laser line while at Blechexpo 2015, in Stuttgart, it displayed a coil-fed flexible punching line.

Award-winning Firmac exhibited its Drive Cleat Duct and STR-750, Square to Round Duct Former, at the Big 5 International Building and Construction Show in Dubai in November 2015. The company also produces a wide range of coil handling and cut-to-length systems.

Fagor Arrasate is a specialist in the design and manufacture of sheet metal cutting and processing lines, as well as in systems for manufacturing tubes and rollformed steel. It manufactures cutting lines for processing coils with widths of up to 2,500mm and thicknesses of between 0.05mm and 30mm. The coils can weigh up to 50,000kg. Installations for processing of blanks are also produced. Very large dimensions can be achieved in tube and rollforming manufacturing lines.

Vetchberry, the coil processing division of ThyssenKrupp Materials (UK) Ltd, is employing a new flagship 1850mm wide coil slitting line at its Birmingham (UK) Service Centre following successful trials by manufacturer Heinrich Georg GmbH. The line, which can handle coils of up to 28 tonnes, has the capacity to process stainless steel from 0.3mm to 4mm thick and aluminium of up to 5mm thick, setting new standards in edge and strand quality together with significantly longer production runs yielding larger finished coils. Georg UK has invested in its machine shop as it plots further growth following a 40 per cent rise in turnover in 2014.

Italian manufacturer, IRON SpA, has supplied a wide range of customised equipment for coil handling since 1969. Innovations include its TEMNOS blanking line and the electric version of its Punchpress Bender. The company manufactures coil feeding and handling lines; levelling (transversal cutting/blanking lines); slitting lines; laser cutting from coil lines and customised systems. It has also built a new levelling and cut-to-length line using the PLC Siemens 1500 TA PORTAL and ‘Touch-Screen KP700’ console display for coil widths of 1500mm (thicknesses of 3mm).

Marcegaglia’s stainless steel business has been expanded with the production of flat materials (coil and cut-to-length sheet) at its Gazoldo degli Ippoliti plant in Italy, which manufactures hot-rolled steel (1D finish) from 2 to 6mm thickness and cold-rolled steel (2B finish). These coil processing lines can also supply in-line edge slitting. Downstream, Marcegaglia can count on the integrated production of stainless sheet and strip but also flat and angle bars. The cut-to-length line can work both hot-rolled and cold-rolled materials with optional polishing (220-240-320 grit), scotch brite brushing and paper or laser film protection on each sheet.

Marcegaglia has also increased its flexibility in zinc coating up to Z800 (also allowing a different coating between sides), and new grades have been developed such as dual phase DP600, DP800 and DP1000. The coil colour-coating units can count on more than 25 standard painting cycles that can be combined for specific surface requirements using more than 1200 colours. The production programme of heavy (quarto) plates was also enriched and a wide array of end uses are now covered, with specific certifications for boilers, pressure vessels and naval construction (ASTM 131, grades A, B, D, RINA approval) among others.

Since 1960, Millutensil has been representing the German company FIBRO for standard mould elements, distributing the rapid mould and coil change systems of the French company ROTOBLOC-PSP on the Italian market.
