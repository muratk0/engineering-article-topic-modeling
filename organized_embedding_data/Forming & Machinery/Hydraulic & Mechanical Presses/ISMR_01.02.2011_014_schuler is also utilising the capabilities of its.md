Germany’s leading car manufacturers rely on Schuler’s automation expertise for the production of blanks for body panels and structural parts. BMW in Regensburg, for example, has been using a combination of external pre-cropping station and dual decoiler since 2009 to optimise its coil preparation process.

In Autumn 2009, BMW’s Regensburg facility launched production on a new blanking line from Schuler. It can convert up to 400 metric tons of coil per day into blanks. The line includes the world’s first-ever cutting press to feature ServoDirect Technology and is designed for the processing of high-strength materials.
