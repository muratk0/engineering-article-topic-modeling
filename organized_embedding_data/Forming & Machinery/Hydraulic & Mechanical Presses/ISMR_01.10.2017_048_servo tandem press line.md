# Servo tandem press line

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

TAIWANESE PRESS MANUFACTURER, Lien Chieh Machinery Co., Ltd., is supplying a new servo-mechanical tandem line for press tonnages ranging from 1,200-600 tonnes with a bolster area of 3,200 x 2,000mm. Lien Chieh Machinery started in 1947 and now specialises mainly in hydraulic presses. Its current capacity is for presses ranging from 50 tonnes to 15,000 tonnes. The manufacturer supplies various industry sectors such as aerospace, marine, automotive and electronics.

In the early days of its operations, the company manufactured basic hydraulic equipment (e.g. table lifters for plywood) and later turned its hand to exploring hot forming technology. Today, LCM's hydraulic presses have extended metal forming techniques for higher level performance, especially in hydroforming and deep drawing.

"We have a strong global outlook, aimed at meeting different customer structures and requirements with our mechanical engineering expertise. Our international service centres are set up across continents and, from 2017 onwards, we aim to boost sales and services in the Asian region. Russia, India, Japan and Southeast Asia will be our latest prospective markets for introducing the servo mechanical tandem line," it told /SMR. See our interview with Lien Chieh on page 86.
