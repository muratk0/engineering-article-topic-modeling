German specialist TRUMPF has now enabled quick and easy calculation of bending parameters for its TruBend press brakes, which can now be done on the iPhone, iPad and all other smartphones. The new TRUMPF BendGuide App also provides access to current brochures and any TRUMPF news concerning bending. BendGuide has been introduced in six languages and is available free of charge from Apple and Android app stores. It can also be downloaded for use on a PC.

This new app enables the user to calculate the most important bending parameters. These include press force, nesting height, side length, die width, installation height mounting, bending inner radius, sheet thickness, tool and sheet weight. Changing from metric to Imperial measurement can be done in the setting menu.

TRUMPF’s TruBend Cell 7000 with automatic tool changer runs an average cycle time of just 4 seconds per bend, with its parts flow rate ‘twice that of a conventional bending cell’, it told ISMR.

The Variobend double bender and long folding machines offer efficiency and flexibility for metal roofing companies. The patented bending system is capable of bending profiles with working lengths from 4-12m and bending capacities from 1-3mm for steel. Variobend also has new touchscreen control for wireless data-transfer from the building site directly to the machine.

Wila will introduce a new line of New Standard Premium heavy duty clamping and crowning tooling at EuroBLECH 2012 to boost productivity of higher-tonnage press brakes.

Wilson Tool International will be presenting a full selection of European-Style and Wila / TRUMPF-Style precision press brake tooling and clamping technology at EuroBLECH which significantly reduces machine set-up times, increasing productivity and efficiency.

## Precise cut-outs

FEIN new cordless nibblers make precise cuts in trapezoid and corrugated sheet metals and profiles. The company has updated and expanded its metalwork range by launching a range of nibblers. For the first time, the range comes complete with lithium-ion batteries as well as re-designed housing and motors.

“Nibblers are suitable for jobs where trapezoid, corrugated sheets, single rolled sheets or profiles need to be cut at speed, without deformation or when a precise cut-out is required. The cutting tool comprises a punch, die and cutting head.

FEIN nibblers are built to be curve-compatible and compact, making work in difficult-to-reach spots much easier,” said the company.

FEIN cordless nibblers feature high quality 14.4 V lithium-ion batteries. Both FEIN’s cordless and mains-powered tools feature a QuickIN clamping system for replacing punches and cutting heads quickly and tool-free. The cutting direction can also be changed by up to 360° without the need for tools, allowing for left and right-handed operation. The nibblers are capable of cutting 30m of 0.75mm thick sheet steel with just one battery charge and powerful enough to cut overlaps in spiral seam pipes (used in ducting).

FEIN is a metalworking specialist with a range of power tools for various materials, applications and professions in the metalworking sector. www.fein.de/
