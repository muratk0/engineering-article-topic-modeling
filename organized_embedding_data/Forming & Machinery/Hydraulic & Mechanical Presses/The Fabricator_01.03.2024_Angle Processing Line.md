# Angle Processing Line

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 The Fabricator]

AKD

Speed and accuracy meet technology

The AKD is designed to punch, mark and cut angle and flat bars. Fully computer-controlled, it can process L-angle bars ranging in size. The automated infeed and outfeed system assures a continuous production flow and operator safety. With its sturdy frame, the AKD offers stability and durability for demanding steel requirements, making it the optimal choice for utility pole and tower production, power plant operations, and various steel construction projects.
