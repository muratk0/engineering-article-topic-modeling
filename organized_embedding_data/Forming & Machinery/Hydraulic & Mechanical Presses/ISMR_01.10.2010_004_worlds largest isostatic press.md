# WORLD'S LARGEST ISOSTATIC PRESS

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

June 18th saw the inauguration of the world's largest isostatic press at Kinzuko-giken in Himeji, just outside Kyoto, Japan. The press – which was manufactured by Avure in Västerås, Sweden – was sold and installed by AP&T's Japanese organization.

“This deal anchors our position in the Japanese isostatic pressing market,” said Tord Andersson, CEO of AP&T. AP&T has been established in Japan since 2002 and this is the second press that the company has delivered to Kinzuko-giken.

Isostatic pressing is used to compress metal, which improves its strength and extends its service life. Examples of products manufactured with isostatic pressing include blades for jet engines and hip joints.

AP&T will organise a number of seminars during the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover this October where issues such as automation and press hardening will be discussed under the headings of Advanced Forming, Lean Production and Emerging Technologies.
