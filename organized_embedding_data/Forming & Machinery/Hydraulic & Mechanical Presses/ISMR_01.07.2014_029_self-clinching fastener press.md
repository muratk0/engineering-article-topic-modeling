# Self-clinching fastener press

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

THE PEMSERTER® Series 4® press for installing self-clinching fasteners has been newly upgraded with an optical sensor feature designed to promote faster work set-up. The newly engineered Optical Sensor Indicator (OSI) has been integrated as standard into the built-in, point-of-operation safety of the press to signal when the safety is properly set for each job.

The OSI activates during set-up mode when the press ram/punch is being adjusted onto the work-piece before fastener installation. The process is set in motion when the operator unlocks the ram-jam nut and then begins to adjust the ram clockwise. The OSI then changes colour (from black to orange) at the exact point that the safety is successfully engaged, eliminating the potential for operator error.

“The PEMSERTER Series 4 press from PennEngineering® offers a cost-effective and low-maintenance solution for installing self-clinching fasteners quickly and reliably. The press achieves adjustable insertion force from 1.8kN to 53.4kN/400 lbs to six tons and features 45.7cm/18” throat depth providing clearance for a wide variety of chassis configurations,” said Penn Engineering.
