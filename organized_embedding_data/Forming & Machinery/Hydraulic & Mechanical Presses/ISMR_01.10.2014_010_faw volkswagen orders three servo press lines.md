# FAW Volkswagen orders three servo press lines

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

IN ITS ANNIVERSARY year, Schuler AG has landed one of the largest orders in its 175-year history. FAW Volkswagen Automotive Co. Ltd. has ordered three press lines with ServoDirect Technology and three tryout presses for testing and setting up dies for its facilities in China. The servo press lines will be used mostly to produce car body parts, such as hoods and doors. The order is worth over € 150 million.

It is the first time that FAW Volkswagen has invested in equipment with ServoDirect Technology. Compared to conventional mechanical press lines, productivity is around 20 per cent higher while energy consumption is significantly lower, Schuler told ISMR. “Thanks to electronically-regulated motors, presses equipped with ServoDirect drive technology not only guarantee high output performance, but also increase flexibility and energy efficiency as well as reduce part costs.”

Over 9,800 employees work at FAW-Volkswagen Automotive Co. Ltd., a joint operation by FAW, Volkswagen and Audi. Following plant expansion in 2004 (Car Plant 2), up to 600,000 vehicles can now be manufactured there each year; these include Jetta, Jetta A2, Bora, Golf and Passat VW models as well as Audi models A4 and A6. ■
