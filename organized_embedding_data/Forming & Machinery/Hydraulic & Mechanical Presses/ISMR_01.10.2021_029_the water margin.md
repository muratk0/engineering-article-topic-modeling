# The water margin

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

CMS Metal Technology offers a wide range of complete waterjet cutting systems, pressure intensifiers and dry or wet deburring and satin finishing machines. Since the 1990s, thanks to the acquisition of Tecnocut and continuous internal developments, it now boasts more than 1,500 installations worldwide across various industry sectors such as automotive; aerospace; machining; furniture and construction.

The company has now launched its CMS Tecnocut Proline, a compact, sturdy single-block design waterjet cutting system. Designed for high productivity and versatility, as well as accuracy and repeatability, the system also focuses on operator safety in the working environment.

"Tecnocut Proline features a stainless steel tank that can include an optional retrofit dredge; pipe rotary axis; automatic water level system and 5-axis calibration TCP. Front and rear automated hatches protect the operator and create a cleaner working environment. A laser line projector easily and rapidly checks the alignment of the material with respect to the cutting X-axis. A video camera, integrated into the Z-axis, captures the cutting area to accurately determine the origin of the work," explained CMS.

"The machine is accurate with positioning precision and axes repeatability of +/- 0.035mm and +/- 0.025mm, respectively, to meet the most demanding cutting applications," it added. "It also guarantees 35% less floor space due to the electric intensifier style pump integrated into the machine frame, and 33% shorter cutting lead times with the JD5 5-axis head. We also offer a reduction in costs to remove and dispose of used abrasive via our new dredge. The system features up to two 4150 bar (60K PSI) integrated electric intensifiers for maximum cutting versatility," it added.

## High-speed blanking

The Balconi Press model 2DMRV-315 (3150kN) is designed for the high-speed blanking of high-strength steel parts. Offering high structural rigidity to reduce wear on punches and dies, it blanks material thicknesses of up to 7mm at a speed of 200spm.

"Since the blanking presses use a reduced ram stroke (in this case, 70mm), we have equipped this machine with a hydraulic device for quick ram opening of 100mm (Quick-Lift). This allows the operator to check the condition of the die and remove any scrap material without having to unload the tool or adjust the ram," said the Italian stamping specialist.

There are two separate lubrication systems, one for the crown and the other for the ram guides. The press features a Balconi electronic feed system and a pilot release controlled by a servomotor. A mechanical shear, connected to the press shaft, cuts the skeleton at each press stroke or after a certain number of strokes programmed by the operator.

"Industry 4.0 standard software enables preventive diagnostics, remote diagnostics and Big Data exchange. Optional accessories include spring shock-absorbers; a quick die change system and sensors for dynamic control of blanking," added Balconi.
