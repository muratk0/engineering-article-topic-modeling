# THE TOOLING CONCEPT

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Dutch tooling specialist, Wila B.V., has been specialising in tool holders (clamping and crowning), tooling and accessories for more than 80 years. With continuous innovations, it has been able to reduce the adjustment time of the press brake and to further increase accuracy. Three of WILA's latest innovations – E2M® (Easy to Move), Smart Tool Locator® and V-Lock – were showcased at its booth at EuroBLECH.

“Our vision has not changed. Our core business is still to increase our customers’ productivity by providing innovative solutions for their press brakes. What you see on our booth today is our easy to move (E2M) concept for press brake tooling – this makes lighter work for the operator and saves time on tool change-over. Tool changes are becoming more frequent these days, as job batches become smaller so production needs to become more flexible as jobs change all the time. Rapid tool change has therefore become more important,” explained Michiel Wensing, Director, Sales and Marketing, WILA to ISMR.

E2M® has been developed so that (heavier) New Standard tools can be replaced in a convenient, safe and efficient manner. Besides fast and safe tool change-over, it also offers major improvements in productivity.

Another new innovation is WILA's V-lock for bottom tools. It has applied a pin and groove system across its bottom tooling ranges to prevent any movement of the bottom tools in a press brake and also as an additional safety/accuracy measure. The V-Lock makes it possible to further improve bottom-tool positioning. A groove at the back of the tool is used to automatically align bottom tools in X and Y directions, and to firmly clamp them in place. This, for instance, prevents tools from sliding or being pulled upwards during the bending process. The V-Lock therefore helps to significantly improve accuracy and safety in the bending process.

WILA's Smart Tool locator was also on display on its booth – it indicates where to place tooling and is connected to the control of the press brake so that the controller can decide which bending order to follow. It makes it instantly clear where the tooling should be positioned in the clamp. The ease and speed at which tools can be replaced also increase with this innovation.

We work very closely with the press brake manufacturers to give us a good understanding of how new developments will impact our tooling. Software has also progressed hugely and become a key differentiator. We are starting to implement more and more software features and functionalities.
