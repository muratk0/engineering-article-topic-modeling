# The water margin

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 ISMR]

A NEW SMALLER-FORMAT waterjet cutting machine has been introduced by AXYZ Automation Group. Called the WARDJet A-0612, it forms part of the A-Series of machines and shares all the same design and performance characteristics of the larger X-Series, but at a lower cost.

"The A-0612 will process a diverse range of materials which traditional CNC routing/ cutting machines cannot handle. These include stainless steel and other non-ferrous metals, cast and wrought iron, solid rubber and rubber composites, ceramic, glass and fibreglass. With a comparatively small 213 x 124cm footprint, the A-0612 will fit perfectly into any engineering shop or machining centre where space is at a premium. Its compact size also enables easier transportation, installation and portability in comparison to full-size waterjet machines," explained the UK manufacturer.

"A key benefit of the A-0612 is that, whilst similarly configured small-format waterjet machines operate at pressures ranging from 30,000 to 45,000psi, the A-0612 will operate at 60,000psi. The ability to operate at this much higher pressure equates to the cutting power of full-size machines and radically reduces the amount of abrasive required for the cutting process, contributing to the machine's cost effectiveness.

As with the larger-format WARDJet machines, the A-0612 is based on a lightweight and rigidly constructed modular solid steel frame gantry that will support robust machine operation for heavier and thicker materials. It also incorporates multiple cutting tool heads, an industrial-grade rack and pinion drive system for cutting accuracy and an optional water level control system and cutting table enclosure. These latter features combine to reduce splashback and conserve water and machine noise within the workplace.

"In common with all WARDJet machines, the A-0612 is supported by proprietary Move motion control software. This has been designed specifically for waterjet cutting applications and is part of the purchasing package," concluded AXYZ.
