## Bystronic

Stand B66

www.bystronic.com

At EuroBLECH 2024, Swiss sheet metal specialist Bystronic will be focusing on the automation of sheet metal processing. The manufacturer helps sheet metal processors to automate, accelerate and optimise their production.

"The ByCell Bend Star M mid-sized bending cell is celebrating its premiere. It fills the gap between a small mobile bending robot and a large bending cell, offering the benefits of a fully-fledged bending cell but with the advantage of a compact, modular layout. Parts of up to 30kg and a width of 1200mm are possible," commented Bystronic.

Further highlights at EuroBLECH will be its two laser cutting cells with automation. One cell features a 30kW laser cutter, third shuttle table and combined loading/unloading system including ByTower Compact storage tower. The other cell features a ByCut Eco laser cutter and the ByLoader Flex variable loading/unloading system. The BySoft Suite software package and comprehensive service packages round off the Bystronic range at EuroBLECH.
