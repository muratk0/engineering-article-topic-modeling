# Calculating the water margin

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

SWIFT-CUT AUTOMATION recently introduced Swift-Jet, its new waterjet cutting solution. Offering precision cutting, this smart machine can be used to cut metals; stone; composites; plastics; tile; glass; leather; rubber; textiles; foam; food; paper and more.

"The Swift-Jet, with its easy-to-use software, and powered by Hypertherm HyPrecision pumps, can cut a wide range of materials and is the answer to affordable, high precision, high-quality, in-house cutting," said the manufacturer.

The Swift-Jet package provides componentry; pump; software; installation and post-sales technical support and training. With a choice of cutting bed sizes and 30HP and 50HP pump options, all Swift-Jet machines use Touchscreen Control Consoles, OMRON servo motors, 32mm ball screws on the X,Y,Z axes and are calibrated using the latest Renishaw software and ballbar testing equipment.

"With the rise of a variety of industries seeking in-house capabilities to design, develop and create their own projects and wanting to avoid expensive outsourcing, owning a waterjet profiling system is becoming an essential part of their business," Swift-Cut Automation told ISMR.
