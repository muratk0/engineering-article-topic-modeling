# Faccin Group

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand D07

## www.faccingroup.com

Faccin Group, a metalforming machine manufacturer, will use EuroBLECH as the springboard for the global launch of its latest innovations: the Faccin four-roll electric driver plateroll with linear guides and a CNC console with free mobility concept (with live demonstrations).

"The Faccin 4EDL series is a cost-effective, high-productivity rolling solution guaranteeing automation, repeatability and user-friendly bending of thin and thin-medium plates. Highspeed rolling up to 10m/minute, a 35% decrease in energy consumption, 70% reduction in oil waste and silent operation with 10-15dB noise reduction is possible," said Faccin Group.

"The new CNC console boasts features designed to enhance automation, improve machining productivity, deliver exceptional finishes and ensure part consistency. It includes a revolving, high-resolution 19" screen, ergonomic design, larger joysticks and an advanced wireless radio-control for controlling all machine accessories," it added.

The Group will also exhibit the premium Roundo angle roll line for tube and profile bending.
