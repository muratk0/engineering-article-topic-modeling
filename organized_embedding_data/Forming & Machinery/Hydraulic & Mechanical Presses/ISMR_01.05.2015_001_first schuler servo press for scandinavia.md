# First Schuler servo press for Scandinavia

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

SCHULER HAS HANDED over a monoblock servo press to Swedish automotive supplier, GNOTEC Kinnared AB. The type MSD-400 machine is the first servo press that Schuler has ever delivered to Scandinavia. The press manufacturer also supplied a coil line and roll feed which, with the MSD-400, comprise a complete forming system. With a force of 400 metric tons, the servo press replaces a mechanical eccentric press previously used by GNOTEC Kinnared AB.

“The new machine will significantly boost our productivity,” said Magnus Stagh of GNOTEC Kinnared AB: “Compared to the previous model, the output performance has doubled. What’s more, the new servo press enables us to produce parts of a much higher quality than before.”

“The MSD-400 will also make GNOTEC Kinnared AB’s manufacturing more flexible and efficient. Servo presses enable manufacturers to individually program the stroke height and forming speed. At the same time, there is a dramatic reduction in energy consumption – at the same output level – and a significant increase in die service lives,” added Schuler.

GNOTEC Kinnared AB manufactures a wide variety of metal parts, mainly for the automotive industry. Founded in 1933, the company with its head office in Gnosjö, southern Sweden, employs around 500 people at its six facilities in Sweden, Slovakia and China. In 2013, the GNOTEC Group became part of the Frauenthal Group.
