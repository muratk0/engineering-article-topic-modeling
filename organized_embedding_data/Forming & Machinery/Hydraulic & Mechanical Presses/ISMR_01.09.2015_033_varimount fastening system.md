# VariMount™ fastening system

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

THE NEW PEM® VariMount™ fastening system (Type VM) from PennEngineering® utilises self-clinching technology, paired with a round steel or stainless steel base plate, to offer a clean and ready-made assembly for mounting into any rigid material or panel (including composites, plastics and metals).

“Multiple radial holes in the base plate and a generous footprint combine to promote effective mounting of the assembly, whether the method is mould-in or laminate with layers, adhesives, standard fasteners or spotwelding. Mounting can be performed either on the front or through the back of a panel,” explained the manufacturer.

A variety of PEM self-clinching fastener types for accepting mating hardware in the application can be specified for VariMount assemblies. Standard offerings available, in a range of metric and unified thread sizes and lengths, include steel and stainless steel nuts, steel blind nuts, steel standoffs and steel and stainless steel threaded studs. Beyond these standard offerings, other PEM fastener types and sizes can also be supplied pre-installed into base plates to suit particular application requirements.

## Sawing and storage

AT THE EMO 2015 international machine tool exhibition, to be held in Milan from 5-10 October 2015, KASTO will present numerous innovations from the two sides of its business – sawing and storage (Hall 18 / Stand no. A18 B09). Visitors will have a chance to assess, amongst other things, the KASTOunitower long material storage system, the new KASTOwin series of bandsaws and the KASTOsort robotic sorting system.

“The KASTOunitower tower storage system is available in three variants and is suitable for bar materials, sections, sheet and plate, as well as euro-pallets, boxes and other items. Its height means that it can hold a given amount of stock in a smaller space than floor or cantilever arm storage systems and therefore is more economical,” KASTO told ISMR.

The horizontal bandsaws in the KASTOtec series are designed for processing large workpieces and materials that are difficult to cut. KASTO will be exhibiting the fully automatic KASTOtec AC 4 carbide specification bandsaw with a cutting range of 430mm. The KASTOwin series, introduced in 2014, comprises five automatic bandsaws with cutting ranges of up to 1,060mm. The KASTOwin A 3.3, with a cutting range of 330mm, and the KASTOwin A 8.6 whose cutting range is 860mm, will also be demonstrated on the KASTO stand.

In addition, the company will exhibit the KASTOspeed high-performance automatic circular sawing machine in combination with a KASTOsort robotic sorting system. KASTOsort integrates industrial robots with suitable gripping systems. Cut pieces can be removed under automatic control and stacked for packing density, cleaned, deburred, weighed, marked and/or sent to further processing equipment.

## Making a splash

AT EMO MILANO 2015, taking place in Milan from 5-10 October 2015, OMAX® Corporation and its distributor partner, ITEK, will jointly showcase the OMAX 60120 and MAXIEM® 1515 JetMachining® Centers with direct drive pumps, advanced cutting heads and the latest Intelli-MAX® Software Suite (Hall 18, Stand C03).

“The bridge-style OMAX 60120 JetMachining Center can cut complex part sizes of up to 1.52m x 3.05m from a variety of materials and thicknesses. The abrasive waterjet system’s performance is a direct result of our Intelli-MAX Software Suite and EnduroMAX® Pump technology. The user-friendly software automatically optimises the machine’s tool path for consistent quality and repeatable results, while the direct drive pump provides faster cutting speeds per unit of input than intensifier designs,” OMAX told ISMR.

Available in 22-, 30- and 37kW options, the EnduroMAX Pump runs continuously at 4,100 bar. Through the control software, the Tilt-A-Jet automatically calculates and adjusts the angle of the nozzle to remove the natural taper from the finished part by transferring it to the scrap part of the material while maintaining high cutting rates.

The MAXIEM 1515 JetMachining Center has a cutting area that fits up to 1.5m x 1.5m of material stock and can cut a broad range of materials and thicknesses. The 1515 employs a 3,450 bar direct drive pump that regulates the electricity input to the motor to reduce overall energy costs. This increased efficiency enables the pump to deliver more power at the cutting nozzle, regardless of the pressure, than intensifier designs at any pump power rating, said the manufacturer. MAXIEM direct drive pumps are available in 15-, 22- and 30-kW options, with the 30kW version on display at EMO 2015. See www.omax.com

## Tube and hollow profile production

TAILOR-MADE TUBE bending cells enable automatic production of tube and profiled semi-finished products – from tube separation and weld seam positioning via the bending process to integrated quality control, reshaping or separation systems and unmanned transport of the semi-finished products. They are used, for example, in the series production of injection pipes, coolant and axle tubes, tie rods as well as several body, exhaust gas system and chassis components.

Cologne-based tube bending machine manufacturer, Schwarze-Robitec, offers various mandrel bending machines for mass production. The machines of its CNC MR series have been optimised for the efficient production of tubes with different radii or bend-inbend geometries. Fitted with stacked bending tools, they are designed for minimal set-up times.

To ensure that fault-free bent tubes are further processed exclusively, the company also integrates measuring systems for quality control upon request. Its freeform bending process enables the efficient machining of large radii and complex geometries with different bending radii as well as highstrength materials. Hollow profiles, with radii of over 6 x D, are fitted in cars and commercial vehicles. See www.schwarzerobitec.com
