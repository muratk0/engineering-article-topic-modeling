# SERVO ELECTRIC PUNCHING

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

THE FINN-POWER E5X is a turret punch press which extends the company’s wide range of servo electric machine tools. It features easy operation, a high degree of productivity and accuracy as well as low energy and maintenance costs, says the company. The E5x comes with the TULUS software suite developed by Finn-Power. It makes the machine easy to use even for operators without prior experience of Finn-Power technology.

“The number of rotating tools can be increased by using indexable multi-tools. Thus programming is simplified and set-up times are shortened. New quick-change tool holders can be used for even shorter set-up. A major contribution to operating efficiency is made by easy load mechanism and automatic clamp positioning and closing function as well as a large 500 mm x 500 mm work chute for components. A lifting brush table mechanism provides extra protection for materials with a sensitive surface,” Finn Power told ISMR.

“A three–stage system makes the machine safe to operate and minimises downtime in case of failure. The clamps are equipped with sensors which automatically stop the machine if the sheet comes loose. As options, sensors with a similar function for sheet distortion or tool jamming can be added. Instantaneous machine stop prevents further damage to the machine and makes re-start rapid and simple.

“The use of servo electric technology in punching is inherently economical in operation and maintenance, but it also opens new possibilities to develop further ways to reduce costs. The E5x has a new idle operation mode, into which it switches automatically if not operated for a while. Index tool rotation speed is 133 rpm and maximum nibbling speed 700 hpm. Work area without repositioning is 1,250 mm x 2,500 mm, which can be enlarged by re-clamping,” added the manufacturer.

The Compact Express automation solution is also available for the E5x. Sheets are loaded and unloaded by equipment which is highly compact. An essential element in productivity is the speed of loading and unloading sequences which shortens cycle times and adds to capacity.
