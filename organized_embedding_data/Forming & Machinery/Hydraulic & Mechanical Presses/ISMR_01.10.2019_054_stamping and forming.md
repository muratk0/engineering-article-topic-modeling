# Stamping and forming

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

AT THIS YEAR’S BLECHEXPO in Stuttgart (5-8 November 2019), the Meusburger stand (Hall 6, stand 6107) will feature the new standardised stamping and forming die set from Meusburger and Bihler. WBI and Segoni will also offer a glimpse into their product range.

“A special highlight at this year’s Blechexpo will be the standardised stamping and forming die set, which is the first of its kind and was developed in cooperation with Bihler for Bihler NC presses. The new die sets (‘tunnel cut’ or ‘spring-loaded guiding plate’ versions) are available for stamping presses of up to 400kN and will bring enormous cost and time savings for Meusburger and Bihler customers in the future,” explained Meusburger.

The newly developed SB stamping and forming die sets are offered for Bihler NC presses with stamping forces of 200kN, 300kN and 400kN. The die sets are available in lengths of up to 596mm and in ‘SBH tunnel cut’ or ‘SBP spring-loaded guiding plate’ versions. While the tunnel cut version is primarily used for prototypes and small batches, the version with spring-loaded guiding plate is suitable for producing precision parts and large batches.

“The standardisation of stamping and forming die sets has great benefits: they are significantly more cost-effective and are available from stock. The die sets themselves also offer many product advantages such as: precision alignment edges and practical anti-rotation protection, as well as helpful mounting threads in the plates for pull handles, eye bolts and spacer discs, and useful vent holes,” added the company.

In addition to Meusburger, associated companies WBI and Segoni will also be presenting their range of products for mould and die makers to Blechexpo visitors. WBI Knowledge Management will introduce its software for knowledge management to collect, share, further develop and save important expertise. Segoni will show a live presentation of the latest version of its software for production planning which focuses on companies with individual production structures active in moulds and dies as well as jigs and fixtures construction.
