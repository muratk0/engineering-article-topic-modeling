# Automation and sustainability

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

In this era of digital transformation and the current volatile market scenario, the sheet metalworking industry is focusing more and more on key issues such as automation and sustainable production.

“This is a perfect backdrop to our exhibits at the forthcoming LAMIERA trade show in Milan (10-13 May 2023), where we will be presenting a new P2 panel bender, an L3 laser and a B3 press brake, in addition to a wide portfolio of services and software solutions," Tommaso Bonuzzi, Sales Director Salvagnini, told ISMR.

## P2 panel bender

Salvagnini's new 2225 model of its P2 panel bender will be unveiled for the very first time worldwide at LAMIERA in Milan.

"This new size confirms the characteristics of productivity; flexibility; precision; high technological content; reduced energy consumption and compact layout that have always made the P2 particularly attractive, and also offers a maximum bending height of 254mm. P2, with its universal bending tools and automatic bending and handling cycles, combines productivity and flexibility, completing an average of 17 bends per minute," explained Tommaso Bonuzzi.

No re-tooling is required because the upper and lower blades, counterblade and blankholder are universal tools that can process a range of thicknesses and machinable materials. The automatic blankholder adapts the tool length according to the size of the part being produced incycle, without machine downtimes or manual re-tooling. A wide range of options extends its application horizons: the DPM device available on the panel bender on display in Milan, for example, enables the handling of narrow parts or material-free frames with a minimum width of 135mm.

"P2 is an ideal solution for batch-one and kit production. Its advanced sensor technology measures the actual thickness and effective size of the blank, detecting any deformations caused by variations in temperature. MAC3.0 allows the P2 to adapt to the material being machined by measuring the tensile strength in real-time and automatically compensating for any variations in the range ±25% of the reference value. MAC3.0 greatly reduces the risk of obtaining faulty parts and eliminates reworking and material waste, increasing the financial sustainability," added Bonuzzi.

## A multi-purpose laser

Salvagnini will also be presenting its L3 fibre laser at LAMIERA, designed for reduced energy consumption and transversal use (regardless of application, material and thickness) as it is equipped with a single laser head which allows workable thicknesses and materials to be cut.

"Equipped with a 10kW power source, L3 is an agile laser with numerous proprietary functions that allow the operator to solve any problems in their day-to-day work. L3 is fed by ADC, compact automation for sheet metal loading and unloading. Its strengths are its compact footprint, rapid cycle times and fast installation. This configuration embraces financial sustainability: the L3 is equipped with the ACUT option, which allows users to cut thicknesses of up to 20mm with appropriately treated compressed air, while ADC's gripping devices are equipped with new AVC (adaptive vacuum control) technology. This new technology continuously monitors the vacuum degree present in the gripping suction cups, and vacuum generation is activated only when required (resulting in a reduction in compressed air consumption of around 90%)," concluded Bonuzzi.■
