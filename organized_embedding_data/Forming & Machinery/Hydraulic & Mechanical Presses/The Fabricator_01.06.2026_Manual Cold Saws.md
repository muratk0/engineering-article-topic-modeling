# Manual Cold Saws

[TARİH: 01.06.2026 The Fabricator]

COLD SAW - THE FABRICATION ESSENTIAL

MACC entry-level manual cold saws are equipped with a high-torque electric motor and a double-hinged pin with eccentric bush. The operator manually pulls the head down. Ideal for prototyping, maintenance shops, and low-volume varied cuts. They maintain consistent blade speed for smooth, accurate cuts everytime.

MANUAL LEVER

Allows smooth, controlled positioning of the saw head. An integrated button provides quick start/stop control of the blade’s rotation during cutting.

DURABLE VISE

The double-locking vise creates a rigid hold on both sides of the blade, preventing burrs even on miter cuts.

COOLANT FILTER

Filters out debris and metal particles to keep coolant clean, supporting better heat dissipation, smoother cutting performance, and extended tool life.

MITERING

Enables quick, precise angled cuts for mitering and other fabrication needs. Adjustable settings let operators easily set and lock the desired cutting angle, ensuring smooth and accurate results every time.

ECCENTRIC BUSHING

Enables fine adjustment of the blade’s position for improved cutting accuracy. By minimizing wear, it extends the lifespan of both the blade and the saw.

HEAD CONTROLS

The MACC Cold Saw features a user-friendly control panel with manual blade speed control (RPM adjustment), an emergency stop, and a main power switch. The saw head also includes an oil filler opening and an easy-to-read sight glass for quick oil level monitoring.

SUPPORT ARM AND BAR STOP

Provides enhanced stability and precision during cutting. These features enable quick material handling and ensure consistent, accurate cuts across various projects. The SUPPORT ARM Provides secure and stable material support during cutting. The BAR STOP Allows for precise, repeatable length adjustments for greater efficiency.
