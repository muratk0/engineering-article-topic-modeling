# Rapid mobile bending cell

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

The new Flex Cell is an automation solution for TRUMPF's TruBend 7050 machine. In a few simple steps, an operative can dock the mobile robot cell onto the bending machine for automatic operation. The machine will then run autonomously for several hours. This, said sheet-metal specialist TRUMPF, helps companies to handle order surges and combat shortages of skilled labour.

TRUMPF is launching the Flex Cell at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023), on its stand 1404 in Hall 1.

"The TruBend 7050 is our fastest bending machine," said TRUMPF product manager, Wolfgang Radler. "With the Flex Cell, we're offering users a fast and simple solution to automate this machine. This shows once again that we are focusing on customers' needs."

Together with the Flex Cell, the TruBend 7050 has a footprint of under 10 square metres and a double-sized material buffer. This ensures that it has enough material to feed the TruBend 7050 throughout a night shift.

"A vacuum combination gripper on the robot arm enables fast and safe loading and unloading. Using TRUMPF TecZone Bend software, a bending program can be created in just a few seconds. The operative is then able to program the robot using an app and the teach panel. This makes the TruBend 7050 quick and easy to set up. Thanks to automatic double-sheet detection, the Flex Cell is also able to recognize if unprocessed material has stuck together during loading and then eject it. This reduces rejects and saves resources," explained TRUMPF.

The Flex Cell is suitable for components with a format ranging from 600 x 400mm to 70 x 50mm. It can process sheet metal of between 0.7 and 6mm in thickness.

"In combination with the TruBend 7050, the Flex Cell is suitable for all companies that are suffering from a shortage of skilled workers and would like to increase their productivity through automation. Companies with fluctuating orders for the manufacture of small and simple parts will particularly benefit from this solution," concluded TRUMPF.

TRUMPF was founded in 1923 as a series of mechanical workshops and has since developed into one of the world's leading companies for machine tools, laser technology and electronics for industrial applications.■
