# Wilson Tool acquires Brazilian tool manufacturer

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

WILSON TOOL International has expanded its global manufacturing capabilities with the acquisition of Brazilian punch press tooling manufacturer, Milling Puncionadeiras, based in Sao Paolo.

"Wilson Tool has been doing business in Brazil and throughout Latin America for more than fifteen years," said Brian Robinson, CEO of Wilson Enterprises. "This merger will allow us to augment our manufacturing capabilities around the world and better serve customers in one of the industry's fastest growing markets."

Milling Puncionadeiras is a privately owned company founded in 1982 by Juscelino Aleixo dos Anjos to manufacture high-quality punch press tooling and accessories for the fabrication industry. "As the fastest growing economy in Latin America, Brazil's sheet metal industry is poised for significant growth," said Jorge Martinez, Latin American Manager for Wilson Tool.

There will be no immediate change in operations for the companies. However, as the integration process moves forward, the new business entity will be known as Milling Puncionadeiras, a Wilson Tool Company. Juscelino Anjos will continue to oversee operations at Milling Puncionadeiras.

With the acquisition of Milling Puncionadeiras, Wilson Tool now operates manufacturing facilities at the company's world headquarters in White Bear Lake, Minn. USA, as well as England, China, Canada and Brazil. www.wilsontool.com

## Major contract wins for ESAB

ESAB, a global leader in welding and cutting technologies, is extremely active in South America where ESAB Argentina recently won contracts valued at over US$ 1.75 million.

Within the automotive sector, ESAB Argentina has sold standard welding equipment to two Tier-1 suppliers, while significant orders for welding automation systems have been received from customers in the wind power industry – and ESAB Argentina is currently pursuing further business opportunities in this sector worth over US$ 8 million. As well as these contracts for welding equipment and automation, two separate orders have been received for automated profile cutting machines, together worth US$ 200,000.

A further important development in Argentina is a contract to supply special electrodes to a customer in the nuclear industry. The same contract also covers trials of other materials for use in this highly demanding sector which, the company told ISMR, could lead to additional contracts in the future. In total the business won by ESAB Argentina during the summer of 2011 is worth over US$ 1.75 million, with additional multi-million-dollar opportunities being actively pursued.

"ESAB invested substantially in new technology in wind tower products and this is helping our customers develop competitive advantages in difficult markets.

These customers will also be able to apply the new ESAB ICETM process to further improve welding speed," says Nazmi Adams, ESAB's Global Head of Consumables.

ESAB recently reported expansion of its activities in Mexico with three main areas of growth - supply chain, a new customer care centre in Monterrey and a re-launch of the arc equipment product line. Equipment sales in 2011 make up for 10% of the total ESAB Mexico sales and the goal is to reach 30% by 2013. ■

## Asian hot stamping alliance

## TO ENHANCE the

competitiveness of the domestic automotive industry in Asia, China Steel Corporation (CSC) invited a number of related companies and academic institutions to form a new Research Alliance for Hot Stamping and held a founding conference on 9 December 2011. The conference was hosted by CSC's Technology Vice President, Yu-Soong Chen. Hundreds of people attended the conference, including the vice CEO Chia-Min Wei of Metal Industries R&D Centre (MIRDC), members of the alliance and experts from related industrial and academic fields.

Yu-Soong Chen indicated that meeting the global trend of "energy saving and environmental protection", less weight and higher safety for the vehicle has become the main objective to pursue for carmakers. The Hot Stamping forming process has been widely used by European and American auto manufacturers in auto parts production such as B-pillar, bumpers and door reinforced beams because of its dimensional stability, fewer passes in the forming process and ultra-high strength. The use of hot stamping parts in the global automotive industry will reach a total number of 350 million and the production line will increase from 140 to 400~500 by 2015, said CSC.

## LASERSHAPE ADDS CO2 LASERS TO FIBRE CUTTERS

UK SUBCONTRACTOR Lasershape Ltd. has added to the two TRUMPF TruLaser 5030 Fiber laser profiling centres it purchased in early 2011, by installing a further two TRUMPF machines to provide extra capacity for thicker sheet – a TruLaser 3030 with 5kW resonator and a TruLaser 3040.

"The fibre machines were a new avenue for us in terms of more efficient and productive cutting of thinner sheet materials, typically below 6mm," says director, Tim Leam. "For processing stainless steels between 6 and 15mm, and mild steels between 6 and 20mm, we've been at capacity for nearly two years hence the need to invest in additional CO2 laser cutters."

The investment has coincided with a move to new premises in Nottingham as both space and electricity supply at the company's previous facility had reached their limit. The new building is three times larger and features its own power sub-station.

"We're seeing growth across the whole spectrum of industries we serve," explained Mr Leam, "and we attribute this to factors such as our rapid delivery strategy, our people and, of course, our technology." ■
