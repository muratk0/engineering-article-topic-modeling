# CAUSING A STIR

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“AST's average welding time for one metre of weld seam has been reduced from 20 to 25 minutes down to five to six minutes with EWM's tigSpeed wire feeder"

Something needs to be stirred, on an industrial scale, in the chemical and pharmaceutical industries as well as in food technology. AST Apparatebau GmbH, from Wilnsdorf in Germany, manufactures agitator vessels of this type.

"The media in agitator vessels aren't just stirred - they're also heated or cooled depending upon the respective contents. In wax production, for example, different media are further processed at different temperatures in agitator vessels. In the pharmaceutical industry, jackets even cool the contents of the agitator vessels all the way down to minus 80 degrees Celsius," explained the company.

## Weld seams in agitator vessels

AST worked with EWM AG, one of Germany's largest manufacturers of arc welding equipment, on TIG welding of these agitator vessels.

AST reduces TIG welding time, by up to 75 per cent, to five-six minutes with EWM.

Heating or cooling in agitator vessels is carried out with a half-pipe coil. It is usually welded onto the vessel wall in a spiral formation. Halfpipes are pipes cut longitudinally. The top of the vessel is also the bottom of the pipe. The cooling or heating liquid flows inside this, and there is a direct exchange of heat between the liquid and the vessel. This is a suitable way to harness the heat, which is then supplied or dissipated via the vessel wall directly.

The diameter of an agitator vessel is typically between 80 centimetres and 2.5 metres, while the length of the weld seams created can range from almost ten metres to several hundred metres in total.

Demands on these weld seams are high. The industrial standards of major chemical companies prescribe specific weld preparations for half-pipes as well as differentiated versions. For example, the half-pipes must have a bevel range of between 30 degrees and 45 degrees on the inside and be pointed towards the bottom. Root pass welding is conducted in a protective gas atmosphere for forming. A clearly recognisable golden yellow iridescent root forms from the inside. Following this, an endoscope is used to determine whether requirements have been met.

Typical joining processes for seams like this are TIG welding for the root pass and MAG welding for the cover pass. The average welding time for one metre of weld seam is reduced from 20 to 25 minutes down to five-six minutes.

## TIG welding

After cooperating with welding machine manufacturer, EWM (in Mündersbach, Germany), AST saw a significant increase in productivity. Rudolf Federrath, from its Siegen office, provided welders with the tigSpeed wire feeder from EWM.

"For MIG/MAG welding, wire feed systems come as standard for the respective process. TigSpeed, on the other hand, has been specially designed and constructed for TIG welding, as it enables both cold wire feeding and work with hot wire. The filler wire is pre-heated for this."
