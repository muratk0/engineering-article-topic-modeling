## Spot welding control

GYS, the French manufacturer of car body repair equipment, has announced the launch of a major advance in functionality in its latest range of spot welders. Dubbed ‘Energy Dynamic Control’ (EDC), this technology provides fully automatic setting of the machine requiring no parameter input from the operator.

The new proprietary technology (patent-pending) will set the spot welding parameters, automatically taking into account metal thickness and the resistance variations of high strength metals. Benefits include ease of use but also, importantly, a reduction in the likelihood of incorrect settings being applied to the weld. This technology is fitted to the range of GYS C Clamp spot welders which carry vehicle manufacturer approvals.

This launch coincides with a change in distribution strategy by GYS, which is now supporting a network of equipment, paint and parts distributors directly from the GYS UK headquarters in Warwick. GYS manufactures its spot welding range in France and is one of the largest providers of spot welding equipment to bodyshops across Europe. It is now expanding its UK operations. See www.gys.fr ■

## Linear motion products

HEPCOMOTION has taken two stands at the MACH 2014 exhibition (4584 and 4567), demonstrating the depth of its product ranges on offer to designers of automation, production and process machinery. The first stand focuses on the company’s core linear motion technology and the second on complementary system components.

HepcoMotion originated the V guide system now used worldwide for the control of linear, rotary and continuous motion. Many of its leading products will be featured at MACH 2014 including the flagship GV3 Precision Linear Motion and Transmission System and the DTS (Driven Track System). The PRT2 Precision Ring and Track System will also be included, demonstrating Hepco’s ability to manufacture complete 360° ring guide systems and continuous motion circuits of various sizes and variations. The company can also manufacture complete rings of virtually any size.

HepcoMotion timing belt rings fall into the same product group and, for the first time at a MACH exhibition, visitors will see the PRT2 “1-Track” continuous motion system giving designers complete freedom in 2D system. Completing the exhibits on this stand will be the HepcoMotion Linear Measuring Indicator (LMI) that provides precise length measurement and can also be adapted for rotary applications where angular measurement is required.

The company’s second stand provides a taster of the extensive range of products available to meet system needs. These include the ZIMM Screw Jack Building Block System for loads from 1kg to 100 tonnes, Winkel’s high load combined bearing & rail systems, HTS Telescopic Ball Bearing Slides, BSP Ball Screw Premier Series, the LBG Linear Ball Guides and the Pacific Simplicity® range of plane bearings. ■

## Hydroforming in focus

INTERLAKEN TECHNOLOGY, a manufacturer of production press equipment for hydroforming, elevated temperature forming and hot stamping systems, has introduced a new four-column hydroforming press system for running high-volume production while reducing cycle time. Interlaken offers this new hydroforming press system with clamp forces from 250 to 5000 tons.

Hydroformed parts are stronger and weigh less due to structural integrity and fewer welds or add-on pieces. In addition, costs are reduced and time is saved by eliminating secondary operations, reducing scrap, lowering material and manufacturing costs, and increasing design flexibility. Interlaken’s hydroforming press provides greater overall quality of formed parts.

The multi-channel closed loop control system is easily programmed to handle various sizes and materials. It also offers dynamic mode switching which enables the user to switch between a variety of feedbacks such as force, position, internal pressure and other system variables. See www.interlaken.com ■
