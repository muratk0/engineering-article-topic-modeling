# Strong Asian growth for laser welding

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

SCANLAB AG’S subsidiary, Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH, a specialist in remote laser welding solutions, has announced the establishment of a sales and service centre in Shanghai, China. The company’s growth strategy and closer proximity to existing customers and installations were key motivators in creating a Chinese subsidiary. On 1 March 2015, Derek Wang began organizing the site as Vice President Sales and Technical Support Asia, Blackbird Robotics Asia.

“In recent years, remote laser welding has become an integral production technology for automakers and their suppliers. Application areas include doors, tailgates, seats and countless other components,” said the company.

Blackbird’s system solutions combine control technology, a broad platform of 3D scan optics and seam tracking techniques. These enable substantially increased productivity for robot-assisted laser welding applications. Blackbird’s decision to establish a Chinese presence will benefit local market development, as well as technical support and spare parts supply in China and neighbouring Asian countries such as Japan and Korea.

“Many of our customers operate production facilities in China. To provide them with the expected local support and prompt solutions to technical challenges, we’ve been planning an Asian presence for some time. With Derek Wang on board, we now have the seasoned industry expert we need to start quickly in China,” explained Dr. Wolfgang Vogl, CEO of Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH. See www.scanlab.de ■
