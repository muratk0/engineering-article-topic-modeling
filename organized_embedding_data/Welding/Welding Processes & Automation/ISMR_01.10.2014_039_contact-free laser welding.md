# CONTACT-FREE LASER WELDING

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

For more than 25 years, the ROFIN Macro Group has been providing laser system solutions for the welding of pipes and profiles. Welding pipes with the laser results in very narrow weld seams of fine-grained structure and very small heat-affected zones. In contrast to conventional WIG welding, contact-free laser welding can achieve significantly higher process speeds. The welding seams still meet extreme demands.

The ROFIN Profile Welding System consists of a beam-guiding system and a sensor system, for process welding, with computer-controlled linear axes. All necessary functions for the operation of the laser system are controlled centrally via an integrated SPS-Controller. This includes the supply of process gas, the monitoring of the optical safety circle and the complete system monitoring of the laser, including the cooling unit.

“The modular design of the PWS and standardised interfaces enables easy integration (including with existing systems). The beam-guiding systems ensure secure operation under industrial conditions. The PWS operates almost maintenance-free and is resistant to oil, dirt, dust, moisture and electrical interference – essential prerequisites for operation in harsh industrial environments,” ROFIN told ISMR.

The CO2 Slab Lasers of the ROFIN DC-Series, with a power output up to 8kW and beam quality of K = 0.95, are suitable for welding seam qualities at medium-to-thick wall thicknesses. ROFIN´s CO2 Slab Lasers are low in maintenance with minimal gas consumption.
