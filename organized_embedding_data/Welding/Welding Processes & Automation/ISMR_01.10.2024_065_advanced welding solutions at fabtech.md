# Advanced welding solutions at FABTECH

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Robotic welding specialist, CLOOS, has announced its product line-up for FABTECH 2024 (15-17 October 2024) in Orlando, Florida (USA) on stand W4829.

“Visitors to FABTECH will discover our ExperT Modular system, a versatile, scalable solution for welding automation. Tailored to individual production needs, it offers seamless integration, precision and enhanced productivity—ideal for evolving manufacturing environments," CLOOS told ISMR.

FABTECH visitors will also see the CS-3 all-in-one system. This seven-axis, overhead-mounted welding robot features integrated headstock/tailstock positioners and an indexing transporter. Suitable for large-scale robotic welding, it offers enhanced torch accessibility and a compact, all-in-one design ready to be configured with CLOOS's welding processes.

"Compact and portable, our ArcBoT cobot welding system is a ready-to-weld unit with smart programming software—no robot programming is needed. Seam tracking and touch-sensing features ensure precision and quality in every weld, allowing set-up and welding within hours of delivery," outlined CLOOS.

“Precision and power are offered by our manual welding equipment. Our MIG/MAG and TIG systems, ranging from 400A to 600A, deliver production-grade performance for automation and manual applications. They are fully equipped and ready to use. Users can also optimise their production with our SC-1 robotic welding system. It features a compact indexing turntable and modular weld tabletops for efficient two-station operation. Designed for cost-efficient and easy fixturing, this ready-to-ship system ensures seamless integration and increased productivity," it added.

FABTECH visitors will be able to watch live demonstrations of CLOOS welding systems. Hands-on demos are also offered.

“Strike an arc with our manual welding equipment or try out our ArcBoT and score a pair of exclusive welding gloves. Plus, be entered for a chance to win great prizes," concluded CLOOS.

Carl Cloos Schweißtechnik GmbH (CLOOS) has been a specialist in welding technology since 1919. With 950 employees worldwide, its process competence is in the welding and cutting of various ferrous and nonferrous metals. It offers customers individual solutions which are optimised and adapted specifically to their product and production requirements.

Its portfolio includes QINEO welding power sources for a variety of welding processes and QIROX robots, positioners and fixtures. From welding technology to robot mechanics and control to positioners, software and sensors, CLOOS supplies everything from a single source. The company boasts nine production sites, 12 subsidiaries and 50 sales and service centres worldwide.■
