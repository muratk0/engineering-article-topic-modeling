# Oxygen monitor for welding

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

INERT GAS WELDING requires the elimination of oxygen from the weld zone during the welding of reactive metals, including stainless steel. Before the welding process begins, the oxygen level must be reduced to a required minimum, which is less than 100ppm when welding stainless steel.

Weld purging is the act of removing, from the vicinity of the joint, oxygen, water vapour and any other gases or vapours that might be harmful to a welding joint. Such gases may combine with the metal to form undesirable compounds which may reduce corrosion resistance or be instrumental in creating cracks or other structural defects in metals.

The hand-held, battery operated PurgEye® 100, designed and developed by Huntingdon Fusion Techniques HFT®, has been designed to obtain zero colour, oxide-free welds in stainless steel, reading from atmospheric oxygen level (20.94%) down to 100ppm (0.01%).

"The PurgEye® 100 is IP65-rated and comes with long-life sensor, leak-tight push buttons, auto-calibration and a vacuum-sealed leak-tight probe assembly. The monitor has a clear LCD screen with features such as a low battery icon as well as the low sensor icon. When the monitor is not in use, an automatic sleep mode activates to conserve battery life," explained HFT.

The Weld Purge Monitor® was invented by HFT® in the 1970s and, with over 40 years of innovation, design and manufacturing experience, the company now offers a family of PurgEye® Weld Purge Monitors® to measure oxygen levels from atmospheric content (20.94%) down to 1ppm.
