SOYER has specialised in high-speed fastening technology for over 50 years. It offers stud-welding products from a single source and its products have won numerous national and international awards and prizes/ certifications over the years.

"With over 52 years of experience and trade-specific expertise, we offer the entire world of stud welding technology from a single source, 'Made in Germany', straight from the manufacturer. Our product range encompasses everything from the latest portable stud-welding devices to fully automated high-tech, CNC-controlled, stud-welding centres. All welding accessories and welding elements of all kinds are also available from our production plant in Wörthsee," SOYER told ISMR.

"The extremely lightweight BMS-9 ACCU stud welder with high-end battery technology is ideal for mobile working where there is no access to electric-grid power i.e. installation work. The unit's rechargeable battery can be quickly and swiftly replaced and re-charged using an external charging device," it added.
