# Patented airflow design for welding helmet

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

Lincoln Electric's VIKING™ 3350 XG PAPR welding helmet, complete with exterior grind control, features a powered air purifying respirator. The helmet also features various benefits designed to improve the welding experience.

The VIKING™ 3350 XG PAPR features a low-profile external button to switch between weld and grind mode, a smart blower system and an extra-large 12.5 sq. in. auto-darkening lens with our exclusive 4C® Lens technology. Featuring a lens with superior 1/1/1/1 optical clarity, extreme colour accuracy, light weight and an even shade from any angle, this feature increases operator comfort for long hours of welding. Lincoln Electric told /SMR.

Chase Rutti, its product manager for accessories, added: "When welding must be performed in enclosed or other areas, where it's difficult to access effective ventilation or control exposure to welding fumes, the PAPR system provides a critical layer of protection for the individual operator. Our VIKING™ 3350 XG PAPR system, with a popular exterior grind control, helps the operator to save time with easy access to the most common fabricator activities – welding and grinding for pre- or post-joint preparation."

The helmet also features a patented airflow design. The airduct is integrated into the top of the headgear and features two air flow baffles, which the user can adjust to change the direction and distribution of the set air flow. This feature helps to maximise operator comfort and prevent eye dryness.

"With the system's battery life, operators can work a full shift without interruption, improving efficiency. We also offer an extended battery which can operate for up to 16 hours, providing flexibility when working overtime and functioning as a safeguard if the battery is not charged overnight," added the manufacturer.

The VIKING™ 3350 XG PAPR bundle includes the helmet; a blower unit; a Lithium Ion battery; 120V battery charger; padded belt (29-52 in.); shoulder straps; two HEPA filters; a pre-filter; spark screen; a hose with cover; head covering and duffle bag.
