# PORTABLE MIG/MAG WELDING

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

ESAB has launched a new addition to its Caddy family of welding machines, the Caddy Mig C160i, which has been optimised for welding mild steel with 0.8mm wire. Suitable for assembly, maintenance and repair operations in fabrication or repair workshops, as well as on-site, the Caddy Mig C160i is compact, lightweight (11.4kg) and rugged.

It features a built-in wire feeder that accepts 200mm spools (approximately 5kg) and the polarity can be changed to enable operators to use either solid or gas-less cored wires. Portability, an important feature of the Caddy Mig C160i, has now been enhanced by a welding cable management system. The new design also utilises a range of advanced materials for a lightweight, durable and impact-resistant housing.

“The user simply sets the plate thickness between 0.5 and 4mm and a heat adjustment knob enables welds to be made hotter or colder if desired. Operation from either a single-phase power supply or a portable generator means that it can be used virtually anywhere. The Caddy Mig C160i delivers 30 per cent more power from the mains with the same fuse. At a 35 per cent duty cycle the machine delivers 150A/21.5V; at a 100 per cent duty cycle, it still achieves an impressive 100A/19V. The current setting range is 30-160A and the speed of the integral wire feed can be set from 2-11m/minute,” said ESAB.
