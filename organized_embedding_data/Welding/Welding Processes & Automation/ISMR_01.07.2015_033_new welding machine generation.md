# New welding machine generation

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

CARL CLOOS SCHWEISSTECHNIK GMBH presented a wide range of innovative welding technology for different applications at this year’s Beijing Essen Welding and Cutting exhibition. It has expanded its product portfolio with its QinTron inverter welding machines.

The welding specialists also offered technologies from entry to premium level and from manual machines to automated robot systems.

The focus of its exhibition booth was on the new QinTron product series. The welding machines, with adjustable voltage pre-selection, are available in capacity classes of 400A, 500A and 600A. Together with Chinese manufacturer, Shenzhen Riland Industry Co., LTD, CLOOS developed the new inverter welding machines at record speed.

"There is enormous market potential between the high-tech and high-end products of CLOOS and our portfolio which is targeted more at entry level and lower middle level,” emphasised Qiu Guang, founder and managing director of SHENZHEN RILAND. RILAND is now producing the machines in China under the quality control auspices of CLOOS. At the Beijing exhibition, CLOOS presented the new inverter welding machines to the Chinese market for the first time.

CLOOS also displayed the wide QINEO model range of welding machines for manual and automated welding. Another highlight was the new QIROX QRH-280 welding robot suitable for CLOOS cell and compact system solutions.
