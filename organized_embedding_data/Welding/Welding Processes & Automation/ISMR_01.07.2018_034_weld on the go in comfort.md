# Weld 'on the go' in comfort

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

The new Pico 160 set from EWM includes the welding machine, workpiece lead, electrode holder and additional carrying handle for the welding machine and dirt filter

The new Pico 160 set from EWM

EWM AG, A GERMAN manufacturer of arc welding technology, now supplies its MMA welding inverters Pico 160 and Pico 160 cel puls as a set in conjunction with a practical and robust transport case and components from the accessories range.

"For welding work on the road, a lot of work equipment has to be carried to the welding site. With the new Pico 160 set, the MMA welding machine, workpiece lead, electrode holder and even electrodes, chipping hammer and wire brush, for instance, can be taken to the location in one trip – everything is properly stowed away. The plastic case from the L-BOXX® range, with its ergonomically-shaped carrying handle, has been specially produced for EWM, is intelligently partitioned and exceptionally robust. Its practical click system also makes it possible to connect multiple cases by stacking one on top of the other," explained EWM.

With the Pico 160 set, EWM supplies a complete package with the Pico 160 or Pico 160 cel puls MMA welding machine, L-BOXX® in EWM design, workpiece lead and electrode holder. The Pico in the set is also equipped with a carrying handle and a dirt filter for work in a dusty environment. There is enough storage space for further special welding tools.

"Both MMA inverters from the latest Pico 160 development stage are characterised by excellent welding properties. In particular, the Pico 160 cel puls makes precise MMA and TIG welding easy; with welding characteristics (Arcforce) for all electrode types, users can achieve any seam – even vertical-down welds with cellulose electrodes," explained EWM.

"The pulse function makes pulsing possible when welding with stick electrodes or when TIG welding. This way, for example, MMA vertical-up welds can be produced quickly and without the need for the time-consuming 'Christmas tree' technique. The bridging of air gaps is also made easier. Alongside the pulsing ability when TIG welding, the arc can also be ignited easily with touch-starting technology, and ended cleanly using optimised control and a deliberate torch movement without lengthening the arc. The result is TIG welded seams of the highest quality. The robust aluminium and plastic housing and a dead weight of just 4.7kg make the Pico 160 cel puls the ideal mobile working unit."
