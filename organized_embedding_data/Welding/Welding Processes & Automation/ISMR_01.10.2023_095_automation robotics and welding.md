# Automation, robotics and welding

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Robust manual welding technology, collaborative robotics, scalable automation solutions and innovative data management solutions were showcased by CLOOS at this September's SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN exhibition in Essen, Germany.

"A highlight on our exhibition booth was the automated high-tech production line, in which automated welding technology was integrated with handling and grinding applications in one production line. By combining innovative welding technology with upstream and downstream production processes, users can close automation gaps and significantly increase the efficiency of their welding production," CLOOS explained.

Its QIROX system includes the robot technology, positioners, safety technology, software, sensors and interface to the process technology. The CLOOS portfolio comprises simple, compact standard systems as well as complex, chained systems with automated workpiece identification and loading and unloading processes. The modular design provides users with scalable solutions that are adapted to different applications and individual production requirements.

CLOOS also presented the QINEO ArcBoT which offers an easy entry into automated welding technology and is designed for consistent, reproducible welding quality. Its high-tech MIG/MAG welding power source and Cobot complement each other.

## Manual welding

During live manual welding demonstrations, trade visitors saw the range of QINEO welding power sources from entry to premium.

"The QINEO StarT enables an easy start into the world of modern MIG/MAG welding technology. Users can weld any workpiece economically. The high-tech QINEO NexT MIG/MAG welding power source offers excellent arc characteristics for high welding quality. The modular design enables various utilisation possibilities from the basic welding machine for manual welding to the multi-process welding machine for automated robot welding," detailed CLOOS.

"In addition, the QINEO QuesT welding power source complements our portfolio for high-end TIG applications and is particularly suitable for demanding welding connections and quality requirements. QINEO welding power sources are characterised by digital connectivity, maximum energy efficiency and the option of integrating new welding processes," CLOOS told ISMR.

At SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN 2023, CLOOS demonstrated how automated welding technology can be integrated with handling and grinding applications in a production line.

## Welding processes

CLOOS also displayed new and proved welding processes for high productivity and quality.

"With the new Mix Weld process, users can solve demanding welding tasks even with heat-sensitive materials. Vertical-up welds and horizontal-vertical positions can also be welded linearly with this process, which saves significant time in the process sequence," outlined the welding specialist.

"MoTion Weld is particularly suitable for fine plate applications and applications in the lowest capacity ranges. This welding process is well suited to meeting special requirements for weld surface and appearance," it continued.

With Tandem Weld, the high deposition rate can be converted into both welding speed and volume filling which is beneficial for thin and thick sheet thicknesses. According to CLOOS, users can enjoy shortened welding times and reduced rework "thanks to minimised component distortion, low heat input and good gapbridging ability."

## Digitisation solutions for future production

CLOOS software solutions increase the performance of welding power sources and robot systems. With its C-Gate loT platform, users can access information from their welding production in real time. Central data acquisition and processing enables users to monitor and control their production processes. C-Gate consists of several modules which users can activate, depending upon their individual digitalisation strategy, from the manual welding power source to the fully automated production line.

CLOOS RoboPlan software enables offline programming of automated robot welding systems, including the generation of welding and travel paths as well as sensor routines for 3D models and their direct transfer into the robot controller. This increases system utilisation, optimises the production process and makes welding more flexible.

CLOOS also presented its new digitisation platform, QNECT, for the first time at SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN. In the future, all digital solutions from CLOOS will be integrated in QNECT so that users have all the information available at a glance.
