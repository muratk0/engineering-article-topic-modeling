# 'REAL, RAW AND RADICAL'

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

"Integration and connectivity is essential in future welding'

Kemppi explains its ‘real, raw and radical objectives to ISMR, with Welding 4.0 goals in mind...

A basement in Düsseldorf recently, Kemppi showcased future welding - an ecosystem approach that comprises advanced machines, automation, application software and human skills. Kemppi's vision encompasses the full potential of the Internet of Welding with connectivity, cloud-enabled solutions and software.

"Forty years ago, we introduced the first inverter power source and continued to pioneer the industry ever since. For us, future welding incorporates advanced technology with IIoT enabled digital solutions, quality management, process expertise and ultimate usability," said Frederic Lanz, Vice President, Sales Europe and Global Projects, Kemppi Oy. "We chose our stand design according to the three Rs - Real, Raw and Radical - because it also perfectly describes our technology. We want to highlight the contrast between the real, tough working environment for welders and the latest technology available now for welding.'

ISMR visited Kemppi's booth to see its latest innovations and discuss Kemppi's vision and objectives for current and future welding, centred around Welding 4.0 requirements. Headquartered in Lahti, Finland, Kemppi, employs over 600 welding experts in 13 countries and has a revenue of more than 110 million euros. Its offering includes welding solutions – intelligent equipment, welding management software and expert services - for demanding industrial applications and ready-to-weld needs.

The Control Pad, the brains of the system, serves many purposes. It is an advanced and practical user interface to set and adjust parameters, control welding and view WPS details.

Kemppi's flagship product, X8 MIG Welder, is a multi-process solution which, combined with native connectivity to WeldEye welding management software, makes it a powerful tool for demanding industrial welding. The digital WPSs and wireless Control Pad enhance the solution's usability and make printed WPS documents unnecessary. It can be used for MIG/MAG welding, stick (MMA) welding, MIG brazing cladding and heavy-duty gouging. The solution consists of Kemppi's high-duty and upgradeable power source with newly designed wire feeder, ergonomic welding guns, intelligent software and an intuitive wireless Control Pad for total welding control.

The X8 MIG Welder's intelligent power source and five available Wise processes and functions are designed for optimised arc characteristics for any welding application. In addition to high speed and aluminium welding (WiseFusion), the arc characteristics include narrow gap (RGT), root pass (WiseRoot+), thin sheet (WiseThin+) and low spatter welding in globular transfer mode (WiseSteel). It has power of up to 600 A, using the most common electrical network voltages.

X8 MIG Welder's native connectivity to WeldEye software module Welding Procedure and Qualification Management allows for the use of digital WPS (dWPS) and makes printed WPS documents unnecessary.

"We have the X8 as a focus on our booth as a state-of-the-art MIG system - it is still the definition of extreme welding. We are taking huge steps towards IoT and Industry 4.0 and our mantra is to do things the right way and not just talk about it. Software has been included in the focus of our product development for over ten years, with the idea that the product ties to the software for welding. That is what we are highlighting here on our stand," Frederic Lanz told ISMR.

## An open approach

Integration and connectivity are essential in future welding. Kemppi's universal, cloud-based WeldEye software is compatible with any brand's welding equipment, accessible anywhere, and always up-to-date with support to ISO, ASME and AWS standards, Kemppi told ISMR. At the exhibition, Kemppi introduced a new WeldEye module, Welding Production Analysis, for companies looking to benefit from a solution for monitoring and analysing welding production efficiency.

WeldEye for Welding Production Analysis tracks and measures the arc time of welding stations, and standard times in serial production. It also provides information on the time spent on non-welding activities with reason codes. The software's data mining tool arranges the data into user-friendly visualisations and provides, for example, comparisons between planned and actual welding time per machine teams, shifts, locations or projects. This enables production managers to identify improvement potential in processes or working methods that have an impact on welding. By applying continuous production monitoring, it is possible to substantially increase arc-on time and shorten welding production lead times. The WeldEye for Welding Production Analysis module also enables third party data connections via cloud APIs.

"The new Production Analysis module for the WeldEye software is an easy solution, particularly for companies that need a light and easy-to-set-up solution for monitoring welding productivity. Companies can buy the entire WeldEye package, or just selected modules," Frederic Lanz told ISMR.

The data input for WeldEye for Welding Production Analysis is currently managed with a hand-held device. For future applications, Kemppi is developing sensor technology to measure welding time based on magnetic field detection together with Suunto. Suunto is a global specialist in sports precision instruments. This R&D cooperation focuses on the use of a small and cost-efficient battery-powered wireless sensor solution that utilises Suunto Movesense technology.

"Welding used to be a very manual task, documentation and management was also very manual. This has changed with our WeldEye software, which has revolutionised the entire welding management software market. One year ago, as part of our Welding 4.0 initiative at EuroBLECH, we announced that we would open the API (Application Programming Interface) for the WeldEye software. This was a major undertaking for us – it basically means that we opened the WeldEye management software as open-source software to other manufacturers so that third party suppliers can supply data to the software. The data is crucial – including it in our WeldEye software is a true IoT approach and not just Welding 4.0 – it truly enables Industry 4.0. You no longer need different software to manage different welding production functions – you just have one dashboard to manage all the data," explained Frederic Lanz.

"This year, we have taken one step further – through the Open API, we can utilise all elements of a metal workshop and visualise them in our WeldEye cockpit. This is just the start and we are looking forward to including more elements in the future. It is now larger-scale than just the welding and there is much more to come."

One example of this 4.0 application is Kemppi's collaboration with German manufacturer, Kemper, where air quality data from Kemper's air monitoring systems are sent to the WeldEye dashboard. ISMR was shown examples of this on the WeldEye dashboard.

"We have a strong vision and a forward-thinking philosophy – we are transferring our vision into reality and creating business opportunities for our customers. The cooperation with Kemper has been very fruitful. The unique sensor technology of Kemper's AirWatch provides the required data and complements WeldEye’s dashboard with real-time air quality data. Data has to be visualised and it has to be manageable," concluded Lanz.

## An eye on the future

In the complete welding ecosystem, the A7 MIG Welder represents Kemppi's expertise in Industry 4.0 enabled robotic arc welding. The system can be integrated with any robot brand. The power source's Wise process packages improve welding productivity beyond regular MIG welding. The A7 MIG Welder now has a new user interface, plug and play capability and an operational process where the wire can be 'pulled back' when igniting for better arc seam performance. Also on display on the booth was its Gamma GTH3 helmet.

"We are very much in a growth phase and expect double-digit growth by the end of this year. Europe is performing over target and so are regions like Asia for us. Business has really been accelerating since our EuroBLECH launches last year. We have picked up momentum, especially as customers now understand our ideas and focus on X8 and Industry 4.0. We have a new CEO now, with new and fresh ideas, and also some great new products coming up. We recently announced a partnership with robot specialist, KUKA, on a joint welding package and have now successfully introduced the X8 to China," continued Frederic Lanz.

"In terms of environmental responsibility, we are working on several projects and programmes aimed at improving sustainability. We try to put the welder at the heart of all our technological developments and support young welders on the WorldSkills Europe competition, for example."

On the main screen of Kemppi’s booth at Schweissen and Schneiden, a video showed Kemppi's vision of welding in the next 20 years with its focus on IoT, Welding 4.0 and robotic welding. 3D welding, Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) also feature in this vision – Frederic Lanz sees AR becoming a future tool for predictive maintenance. However, he stressed that the human welder will always remain at the heart of Kemppi's vision.

"Even though there is so much new technology and automation now, the welder always remains at the centre of our vision. Humans have creativity and vision – robots do not. Our software and products are focused on their needs, now and in the future," he concluded.
