# ESAB completes brand transition to EXATON™ for speciality alloys

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

ESAB WELDING & Cutting Products has finalised the transition to the Exaton™ brand name for all welding filler metals involved with its 2018 acquisition of Sandvik Welding Consumables. Globally, all products now carry the Exaton name. For the EU, Middle East and Asia-Pacific regions, the new naming conventions are as follows:

■ For stainless products, the Sandvik name has been replaced with the Exaton, followed by the EN-ISO classification of the product. For example, Sandvik 25.10.4.L is now Exaton 25.10.4.L.

The nickel-based consumables, previously sold under the Sanicro name, have been renamed with “Exaton Ni" in front of the current Sanicro number. For example, Sandvik Sanicro 72HP is now Exaton Ni72HP.
