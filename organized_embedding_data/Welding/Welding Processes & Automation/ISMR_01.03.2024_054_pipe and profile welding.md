# Pipe and profile welding

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

North Bavarian manufacturer, XELLAR Technologies, is celebrating its debut at the Tube trade fair in Germany with its flexible and powerful “X-Weld" module for welding, pipes and profiles. It can be used for microplasma, TIG, laser and high-frequency welding, as required. It can reach speeds of up to 35m/minute for most processes and 200m/minute for the high-frequency version. The weld seams meet the specifications of European standard EN 10217-7 DIN for welded steel pipes under pressure loads.

“X-Weld is suitable for pipes of various cross-sections with diameters of between 3-127mm and wall thicknesses of between 0.1-3mm. Larger dimensions are possible on request with special solutions. It can also weld profiles with dimensions from 10-100mm side length, even with complex geometries," it told ISMR.

The welding module can be used for a wide range of materials from low-alloy to high-strength plain, stainless and duplex steels, as well as for aluminium, manganese, copper, brass and titanium. It can process both cold and hot-rolled, galvanized and nongalvanized, pickled and unpickled surfaces.

Longitudinally welded high-pressure pipes for pumps and battery cells can be processed with “X-Weld” as can pipes with high surface requirements. These include pipes for filling and desalination plants. The main target sectors are the automotive, construction and food industries.

The “X-Weld” element can be flexibly integrated into the modular XELLAR rollforming systems, which are designed for material widths of 200mm, 300mm and 400mm. Like all of the modules, it is completely enclosed for occupational safety and environmental protection.

Modular XELLAR variable roll forming systems can be retrofitted at any time. In addition to X-Weld, there are modules for roll forming, punching and cutting to length which can be combined with each other as required, via plug & play, and retrofitted at any time. Other processes such as plastic extrusion or foaming, adhesive bonding and packaging of profiles are implemented on a customer-specific basis.

XELLAR will be presenting the “X-Weld" module at the Tube exhibition in Düsseldorf, Germany, from 14-19 April 2024 on booth A14 in Hall 6.

XELLAR Technologies GmbH, formerly Profilmetall Engineering GmbH, together with Profilmetall GmbH form the PROFILMETALL Group (a specialist in roll-formed metal profiles and roll forming systems in Germany).■
