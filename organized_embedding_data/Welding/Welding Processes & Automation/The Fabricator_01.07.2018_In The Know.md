Test-drive Cutting, Bending, and Welding at This Hands-on Workshop

This three-day workshop offers the opportunity to improve your knowledge and functional skill of sheet metal fabrication concepts, equipment, and technology. Two sessions will be offered: July 23-25, 2018, or July 30-Aug. 1, 2018, at Harper College in Palatine (Chicago), Ill.

Hands-on demonstrations and practice will be emphasized on the following equipment: Mitsubishi CO

2

laser, Amada 89-ton press brake, Panasonic robotic welding cell, plate roller, ironworker, welding equipment, TRUMPF TruBend 7036, TRUMPF TruPunch 2000, and ring roller. You’ll also have the option of taking FMA’s Fundamentals of Metal Fabrication certificate exam on the last day of the workshop.

This workshop is designed for technical educators and trainers looking to expand their skill set; engineers, designers, and programmers who desire cross-training on job shop equipment; welders who need exposure to the full range of fabrication concepts and equipment; and front-line operators in need of additional training for company advancement.

To be eligible for camp grant funding, schools must be able to accommodate at least 12 students at their facility, direct an entrepreneurship camp curriculum, introduce manufacturing processes (e.g., metal fabricating, CNC, machining, welding) by guiding students through the design and production of a take-home project, and provide opportunities to tour local manufacturing plants during the camp week.

NBT is striving to support 100 camps and bring at least one program to every state. If you don’t see your state listed above, consider reaching out to the local community college or technical high school with a manufacturing-focused curriculum in your area and encourage them to apply for an NBT manufacturing camp grant. Donation Supports 12 Camps in Wisconsin

This year 12 camps in Wisconsin are being funded by Miller Electric Mfg. Co. LLC, headquartered in Appleton, Wis. Miller Electric is investing in the area’s future skilled workforce with a $10,000 donation to support NBT’s summer manufacturing camp program.

"Miller Electric Mfg. Co. believes welding is a key process to the future of manufacturing. We are proud to provide camp and scholarship donations for the young people to connect with a career in such fields. Welding is a key part of the manufacturing economy, and we need these future leaders to keep it going," said Bruce Albrecht, vice president, global innovation and technology, Miller Electric Mfg. Co.
