# Welding ventilation

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

EXTRACTABILITY, a division of Weldability Sif, is a UK-based “one-stop source” for welding fume extraction solutions. The company has announced that its is now selling a new range of Vent-To-Atmosphere (VTA) systems, as packages consisting of suction arms, cranes and hoses.

VTA systems extract welding fumes at the point of source, and include a range of suction and collecting elements. Each extraction arm includes a hood installed at the tip of every suction element to ensure efficient extraction and capture the fumes at the point of source. Extractability’s suction arms can be purchased with external and tubed mechanisms which can then be used on other extraction applications other than welding.

Extraction arms are available for wall or ceiling attachment, or connected directly to the filter unit, and are fully self-supporting. Telescopic suction arms are also available, designed for workplaces, training and education centres with limited headspace.

## Die spotting press

DEES HYDRAULIC has long been committed to integrating new technologies and has successfully developed a closed loop, hydraulic, servo-controlled die spotting press.

With micro-precision control and energy efficiency at the top of its R&D goals, the servo system’s fixed-flow control, as the core technology used in the hydraulic spotting press, can further improve the precision of the conventional design or overcome the ball-screw’s wear and tear to reduce overall maintenance costs.

“The energy-efficient servo motor can save up to 30% energy. Adopting a pulse wave signal handwheel jog down for micro precision of 0.01mm can prevent damage when spotting the punch and die. The closed loop, servo-hydraulic system can reach high slide repeatability levels of +/- 0.02mm,” explained the manufacturer.

The company supplies customers such as BOSCH; Audi; Volkswagen; Mercedes-Benz; Volvo; Honda; Toyota; BMW and more. See www.deesgroup.com.tw
