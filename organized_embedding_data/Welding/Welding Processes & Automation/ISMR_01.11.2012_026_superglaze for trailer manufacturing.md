# SuperGlaze for trailer manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

LINCOLN ELECTRIC has introduced SuperGlaze® 5356 TM™, an engineered aluminium alloy GMAW wire designed specifically for applications in the trailer manufacturing industry.

The Lincoln Electric Company is a specialist in the design, development and manufacture of arc- welding products, robotic arc welding systems, fume control systems, plasma and oxyfuel cutting equipment and has a leading global position in the brazing and soldering alloys market.

“The engineered alloy system provides strong puddle clarity and arc action, as well as excellent wetting for both semi-automatic and high productivity automatic applications,” the company explained. “Proprietary manufacturing processes provide a smooth surface finish, making it suitable for automatic applications on formed truck panels.”

This aluminium GMAW wire is particularly suited for high-speed groove welds in formed truck panel applications, as well as robotic fillet welds on trailer tanks, requiring minimal post-weld clean up. It is available in standard diameters of 0.035 in., 3/64 in. and 1/16 in. wound on 1 lb. and 20 lb. plastic spools, as well as 16 lb. steel spools. www.lincolnelectric.com

TECHNO, INC. CNC Routers has unveiled its the new HD Mini Series CNC router, a new precision cutting system.

Developed by a team of engineers using global manufacturing techniques, the HD-Mini is a heavy-duty machine designed to be an affordable CNC work platform. It is suitable for any size shop requiring a compact machine to run small routing jobs or to be used as an educational tool.

The HD-Mini features ball screw drive on all three axes and is controlled using a Techno hand-held micro-stepper controller. The controller can run G-Code or dxf file formats. Techno’s HD Mini CNC router also features brushless micro-stepper motors and controls and a 2 HP HSD high-frequency collet spindle. www.technocnc.com

## A REAM IN TIME

TWO NEW reamers from Sandvik Coromant were launched on 1 October. Solid carbide reamers from the CoroReamer family are designed to meet requirements for high surface quality finish when close tolerances are required.With dedicated geometries and internal coolant, they deliver fine hole quality and process security, the company told ISMR.

“CoroReamer 435 is a versatile and high performance reamer, suitable for a wide range of different materials. CoroReamer 835 is for steel and stainless materials when optimum performance is needed,” it explained.

The reamers are available in spiral flute design for through holes and straight flute design for blind holes.The diameter range is 3.97-20 mm (0.156-0.787 inch) as standard with an achievable hole tolerance of IT7. These complement the CoroReamer 830 that is used in larger diameter applications. www.sandvik.coromant.com
