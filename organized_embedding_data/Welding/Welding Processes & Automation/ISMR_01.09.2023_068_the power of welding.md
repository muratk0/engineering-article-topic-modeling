# The power of welding

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Fronius Perfect Welding is a specialist in arc welding and robot-assisted welding. As a systems provider, the Fronius Welding Automation division also implements customised automated complete welding solutions for the construction of containers or offshore cladding, for example. The range is rounded off by power sources for manual applications, welding accessories and a broad spectrum of services.

At this September's Schweissen & Schneiden trade show in Essen, Germany, it will be showcasing a variety of high-end welding solutions that make particularly efficient use of energy, material and time.

"We are also tackling the shortage of skilled workers in the joining technology sector. We combine new technologies, the advantages of digitalisation and ease of use, so that all welders can reliably achieve the best welding results," it explained.

The WeldCube Navigator software solution supports users step-by-step in production. The guidance and job pre-setting functions can shorten the training and familiarisation period for new welding specialists and help to minimise the risk of errors.

The Fronius iWave Multiprocess PRO is a single system to cover every welding process from TIG and MIG/MAG through to MMA and every process variant.

“This system is also future-proof because it allows users to accommodate all the requirements of Industry 4.0," outlined the welding specialist.

## Welding innovations

Welding technologies on display at the exhibition will include its TIG DynamicWire welding package for the iWave welding machines. It is designed for "smart and simple" cold wire TIG welding.

"Even amateurs can achieve perfect TIG welds with ease. Compared to a conventional continuous wire feed in cold wire systems, TIG DynamicWire actively adjusts the wire speed to the welding behavior, torch position and current conditions. It even compensates automatically for component tolerances of up to 30%," commented Fronius.

Fronius will also showcase its Cwc-S cobot welding cell for high levels of flexibility when welding different components (from batch size one to small series).

"Operation can be learned in just a few hours and no knowledge of programming is required. Trainees can learn how to use the CWC-S in no time, allowing them to take much of the strain off your specialist welders. Precision welding torch movements create high-quality reproducible welds every time. The protective cabin and welding fume extraction system provide safety," added Fronius.

The WeldCube Navigator is a real-time feedback solution for welding specialists. High-quality welds are essential for many products. Faulty or missed welded joints lead to high manufacturing costs. The earlier these faults are identified in the manufacturing process, the quicker it is to respond to them.

## Health and safety

Fronius will also highlight two health and safety products at Schweissen and Schneiden. Its Exento extraction devices, combined with extraction arms or fume extraction torches, are used to extract welding fumes and (depending on the model) are suitable for temporary and permanent jobs. Their high mobility and 360-degree usage radius make them particularly flexible for changing workplaces.

"The Exento HighVac mobile high-vacuum extraction system removes more than 99.9 % of captured welding fume particles, resulting in significantly lower welding fume exposure. In combination with the powerful Exento fume extraction torch, the high-vacuum system captures the welding fumes directly at the source. It is equipped with an extralarge, vertically mounted filter cartridge for particularly effective cleaning," commented Fronius.

"For respiratory protection, we will also showcase the Vizor Air/3X PAPR system. It features a powered air-purifying respirator, interconnecting hose and carry strap. Combined with one of its matching welding helmets (e.g. Vizor Air/3X Connect), this system filters up to 99.8% of welding fume particles via a TH3P-R-SL particle filter (maximum classification). It then routes the cleaned air through the interconnecting hose and directly back into the inside of the filters and up to and out of the welding helmet," it added.

Vizor Air/3X is a protection system for the eyes and airways offering glare protection technology and an air flow system in the welding helmet with variable air distribution between the forehead and breathing zones. The lithium-ion battery delivers an operating time of up to 18 hours. An acoustic and visual alarm is triggered if the battery is low, the filter is clogged or missing, or in the event of low air flow.
