# Welding and cutting

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

ESAB OFFERS customer-driven solutions to meet most welding or cutting needs. It will showcase a number of innovations at Blechexpo Hall 5, stand 5115.

“Our new WeldCloud™ on-line weld data management platform provides the process transparency and product traceability necessary to drive continuous productivity and quality improvements. Accurate, near real-time welding data collection enables a wealth of analytics. Data is stored securely in one comprehensive database, located within a user’s firewalls. See WeldCloud in action with Aristo® Mig 4004i Pulse, a multi-process power source,” said ESAB.

“New Cutmaster® portable air plasma cutting systems range in output from 20 to 120 amps and cut material ranging from 6mm to 40mm. Among the lightest and most compact in the industry, Cutmaster systems are portable and easy to store. With Cutmaster, the recommended cut capacity equals the system’s true cut capacity. Cutmaster delivers more output when needed, offering a maximum cut thickness up to 150% greater than the recommended cut.”

The SGX automated plasma-oxy-fuel cutting machine is suitable for smaller shops looking to automate as well as fabricators who wish to improve cut quality and productivity. SGX will showcase ESAB’s m3 Plasma® system for precision cutting up to 50mm mild steel.

The latest release of ESAB's Columbus® CAD/CAM nesting software offers new features to make programming easier and more efficient. See www.esab.com

For automated plasma cutting solutions, visit Thermal Dynamics (an ESAB brand) in Hall 5, Stand 5211-2 at Blechexpo.
