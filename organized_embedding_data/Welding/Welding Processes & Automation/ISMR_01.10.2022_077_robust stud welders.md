# Robust stud welders

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

At the EuroBLECH 2022 trade fair in Hanover (25-28 October 2022), Heinz Soyer Bolzenschweißtechnik GmbH (SOYER), a specialist in fastening and joining technologies, will showcase its new generation of robust stud welders, designed for use under tough conditions.

“The new BMK-20i device model with efficient inverter technology is part of the successful SRM-EcoWeld® product line. It is particularly suitable for welding large-diameter weld studs up to M16. This unit has been specifically designed for an invariably high welding capacity, even with uninterrupted continuous operation, and just requires a mains connection of 32A. It is a compact, robust and powerful aid for daily use, especially on construction sites. Due to its low weight, this stud-welding device can also be transported over longer distances," the company told ISMR.

“Further benefits for the user include smooth and gentle welding, even under challenging conditions such as out-of-position welding, the elimination of ceramic rings as aids and no or only minimal reworking at the welded joint," it added. ■
