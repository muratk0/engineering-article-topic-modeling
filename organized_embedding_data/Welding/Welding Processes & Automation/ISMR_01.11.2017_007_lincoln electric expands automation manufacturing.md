# LINCOLN ELECTRIC expands automation manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

LINCOLN ELECTRIC AUTOMATION has announced the expansion of its Wayne Trail, Fort Loramie, Ohio (USA) automation facility, which specialises in automated manufacturing solutions for metal forming, fabricating and metal joining processes.

The investment nearly doubles the facility's size to support larger-scale projects, future growth and improved operational effectiveness. In partnership with JobsOhio, Lincoln Electric Automation expects to create 50 additional full-time, advanced manufacturing positions to support the larger operation.

Lincoln Electric's new Welding Technology & Training Centre in Ohio, USA

Commemorating the 100th anniversary of its legacy welding school, Lincoln Electric® will open its new Welding Technology & Training Centre, on its Euclid, Ohio, campus in January. Classes begin in the new facility on 8 January 2018. The centre's opening maintains the company's role of running 'the oldest continuously-operating welding school in the world'.

The two-story, 100,000-square-feet-plus facility doubles Lincoln's welding education capacity. The building features a virtual reality training lab with 10 VRTEX Virtual Reality Web Simulation Trainers, 166 welding and cutting booths, six seminar rooms, 13 welding school classrooms, a 100-seat auditorium, an atrium and a reception area.

Lincoln Electric is a specialist in the design, development and manufacture of arc welding products, robotic arc welding systems, plasma and oxyfuel cutting equipment and has a leading global position in the brazing and soldering alloys market. Headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio (USA), Lincoln has 63 manufacturing locations, including operations and joint ventures in 23 countries as well as a worldwide network of distributors and sales offices covering more than 160 countries. ## An eye on deburring

"THIS TRADE FAIR is attended exclusively by expert visitors who are looking for a solution to a deburring problem or for optimised surface finishing," was the conclusion of many of the 151 exhibitors at the second DeburringEXPO (10-12 October 2017 in Karlsruhe, Germany)

Approximately 2000 visitors from 38 countries travelled to Karlsruhe for the 2nd trade fair for deburring technology and precision surface finishing. With more than 1000 participants, the expert forum, which was held in two languages for the first time, was also one of the trade fair's highlights.

With an exhibition profile that is consistently aligned to deburring, rounding and the production of precision surface finishes, DeburringEXPO covers a range of processes which are becoming more and more important in manufacturing. Visitor numbers were at the same level as for the premiere event i.e. roughly 2000. 23% of the visitors came from outside Germany and journeyed to the trade fair from a total of 38 countries. In addition to Germany, the best represented countries were Switzerland, France, Italy, Austria, the Netherlands and Belgium.

For nearly 30% of the visitors, the programme offered by the DeburringEXPO Forum influenced the day on which they attended the event. The presentations dealing with various issues covered all aspects of deburring, the production of precision surface finishes and cleaning after deburring were simultaneously interpreted for the first time (German to English).

The next DeburringEXPO will take place at the Karlsruhe Exhibition Centre from 8-10 October 2019. See www.deburring-expo.de

## EMO Hannover boosts orders for Germany

IN THE THIRD quarter of 2017, order bookings for the German machine tool industry rose by 13 per cent compared to the previous year's equivalent period. Domestic orders were up by 31 per cent and exports increased by five per cent. In the first three quarters of 2017, total order bookings increased by three per cent. Domestic orders were down by two per cent. Order bookings from outside Germany rose by five per cent, with metal cutting and forming technologies performing equally well.

"In September, orders in all three categories showed a double-digit increase, recording the steepest growth in the ongoing year. Domestic orders, in particular, soared by 46 per cent, compensating in almost one single month for the losses recorded during 2017 so far. This means that the turnaround we expected for the year's second half is well under way. What we are seeing is not least the effects of a highly successful EMO Hannover, which triggered a substantial volume of orders," commented Dr Wilfried Schafer, Executive Director, VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association), who anticipates a production rise of three per cent in 2017.

The eurozone is continuing its sustained upturn, driven by the markets of Southern Europe such as Spain, France and Italy. The major lead markets of China and the USA are also doing well, according to the VDW.
