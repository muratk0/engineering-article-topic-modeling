# ITI welcomes new staff

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

Joe Potesta, of Indianapolis, and Craig Reehten, of St. Louis, were selected earlier this year for two positions on the field staff of the International Training Institute (ITI), the education arm of the unionised sheet metal, air conditioning and welding industry (in the USA).

Potesta will serve on the welding team, working to educate instructors and training directors across the country as well as with Accredited Test Facilities (ATFs) in the Northeast Welding Region. Reehten will provide curriculum support and training to educators at the training facilities across the United States and Canada, in addition to working on Ventilation Verification and indoor air quality initiatives.

Potesta, a second-generation sheet metal worker, was previously the director of training at Sheet Metal Workers Local 20 in Indianapolis and has been active with the local for more than two decades. In addition to an associate degree in applied science from Ivy Tech Community College and a Bachelor's Degree in Education from the National Labour College, he holds multiple industry certifications. For the past several years, Potesta has been a consultant for ITI as an OSHA instructor.

Reehten has been a member of Sheet Metal Workers Local 36 in St. Louis since 2004 after serving a decade in the U.S. Navy. After working as a foreman and testing, adjusting and balancing (TAB) technician and supervisor, Reehten was hired by Local 36 to become an apprenticeship programme instructor. He earned an associate degree from Southwestern Illinois College and maintains industry certifications.

In 2021, Reehten was elected vice president of Local 36.
