# Social responsibility, with an accent on health and safety as well as sustainability, are important elements of Kemppi's modern welding philosophy

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Left: Kemppi welding equipment.

Below: The Flexlite GF handle grip.

Kemppi Oy is a design specialist in the arc welding industry. It supplies advanced products, digital solutions and services for professionals from industrial welding companies to single contractors. It operates with a highly skilled partner network covering over 70 countries. Headquartered in Lahti, Finland, Kemppi employs close to 800 professionals in 17 countries and has a revenue of 140 million euros.

A user-centric design approach, focused on reliable, sustainable products to improve the welding experience, drives Kemppi's research and development. Equipment must also be practical; functional; ergonomic; safe; reliable and cost-effective - while still maintaining an aesthetic appeal, Kemppi told /SMR. Its new slogan, 'Designed for Welders' crystallises this commitment. Around ten per cent of its annual turnover is devoted to R&D initiatives.

However, this is not the full story. Social responsibility, with an accent on health and safety as well as sustainability, are important elements of the Finnish welding specialist's philosophy. /SMR spoke with Florian Hegemann, commercial director of Kemppi's guns and torches business unit, to find out more....

## The business climate

Hegemann outlined several welding trends and global market movements that are affecting Kemppi's business.

"In the last couple of years, the welding industry has been driven by increases in disposable incomes in emerging markets and there still is a strong demand for four- and two-wheeler vehicles. Nevertheless, in the automotive industry and according to Tier One/Two suppliers, we see changes in demand (such as welding lighter and different parts) because of the move towards e-mobility," he explained.

Florian Hegemann, Commercial Director, Guns and Torches, Kemppi.

Construction manufacturing industries have experienced high growth in the last couple of years and rapid industrialisation in developing countries has also driven welding growth. We believe that global infrastructure programmes/ projects and logistics will be key drivers for our market. Cost reductions; the need for increasing productivity; reduction in resources; the use of more lightweight materials and more efficient product designs are key trends facing our customers. Markets are becoming more unpredictable. COVID-19; the slowdown in globalisation; current supply chain issues; higher raw material prices and material shortages are all challenges right now for our customers," he continued.

Although Kemppi has experience with digital tools, it relied more on them during the pandemic. A lot of its meetings take place online now and have become part of its daily routine. They have also helped to reduce unnecessary travel although meeting customers, said Hegemann, is 'an essential part of what we do....

One of the greatest challenges in the welding market globally is the lack of skilled workers and this, Hegemann pointed out, will worsen as experienced welders retire from the market in the next few years.

"This is an issue of concern and will push the need for automation further up the agenda. We currently work with many different partners on robotics and automation projects," he commented. "So far, 2021 has been a good year for us. The market seems to have recovered quickly. For 2022, if COVID-19 can be managed, I see strong potential."

## Safety and ergonomics

Factors such as health and safety, as well as ergonomics and usability, play a key part in Kemppi's design focus.

"Our focus on product development has been on streamlining Kemppi guns and torches, making them lighter, more efficient easier to use and more ergonomic. All our MIG/MAG and TIG torches are designed with this in mind. We try to set new standards in ergonomics, Kemppi's design focuses on developing arc stability and welding properties as well as improving the welding experience of individual welders," Florian Hegemann told ISMR.

"We also wanted to strengthen our position in global aftersales markets which is why we bought Trafimet, an Italian torch manufacturer, in 2019. That was a very strategic asset for us," he added.

Flexlite is a series of welding torches for both MIG/MAG welding and TIG welding. Flexlite torches are available as gas and water cooled versions in alternative power classes, lengths and value levels. Flexlite products feature specific user values, including light weight, balance and reduced wrist loading. Silicon rubber material and innovative design gives a firm grip and allows the welder to concentrate on producing 'the perfect weld in any welding position.' It also offers consumables with increased durability and extra-long lifetime.

"We launched the Flexlite GX MIG/MAG welding gun in 2019 and this set a new standard in ergonomics and handling. It has a detachable pistol grip handle from the welding gun - this makes it much more comfortable for the welder to use and eliminates muscle pain when welding. This grip also reduces the workload on the welder by up to 64%, depending upon welding position," added Hegemann.

"This year, we brought out our new Flexlite GF fume extraction gun for higher safety levels. The ergonomics and weight are close to our standard GX torch. We tried to reduce its weight and make a lightweight fumigation torch. It offers up to 95% efficiency.* The Flexlite GC product range contains five lightweight and robust entry-level welding gun models for MIG/MAG welding processes. Our consumables are appropriate for our overall MIG range and most consumables can be used with all Flexlite MIG guns, providing extended consumable parts life," he continued.

Flexlite GC 'entry line' guns, with a specially designed handle requiring no bending support (hence better torch cooling and longer consumable life), can also be accessorised with a detachable pistol grip handle. Another GC accessory is a rotatable neck giving easy access to challenging workpieces.

Occupational lung diseases are the number one cause of work-related illnesses. In welding, workers are exposed to greater risk because the welding processes produce smoke containing harmful metal fumes and gases. Respiratory protection equipment, featuring a positive pressure principle, is an efficient way of preventing occupational health problems. Kemppi therefore launched its Flexlite GF MIG welding gun product family for efficient fume extraction.
