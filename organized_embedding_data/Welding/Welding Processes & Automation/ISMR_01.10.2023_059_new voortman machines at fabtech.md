# New Voortman machines at FABTECH

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Voortman Steel Machinery again returned to Chicago to present its latest machines at FABTECH 2023, the largest metalforming, fabricating, welding and finishing event in North America (11-14 September 2023). On its interactive booth 3575, in Hall A, visitors experienced live demos of the Voortman Fabricator automated welding system and the latest additions to Voortman's pipecutting range as well as its plate- and beam processing machines.

"The new Voortman Fabricator automated welding system has been designed specifically to benefit the steel fabrication industry. Operators can choose on the fly between Fit & Weld or only Fit. By offering the quickest ROI, maximum efficiency in space utilisation and choice-based flexibility, we support every workplace hungry for automation to combat the shortage of skilled personnel," Voortman told ISMR.

With the acquisition of Müller Opladen, and the subsequent onboarding of its pipecutting machines, Voortman has taken a major step to further expand into current and new markets. Voortman also brought its MO Compact pipe-cutting machine to FABTECH to further highlight its new pipe-processing capabilities in live demonstrations.

In addition to live demos of the Voortman Fabricator and Voortman MO Compact, visitors also saw live demos of the Voortman V310 (the complete plateprocessing machine) as well as the Voortman V633, its beam drilling, milling and marking machine (equipped with four sub-axes).
