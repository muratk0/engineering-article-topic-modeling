# Weld parameter app

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

LINCOLN Electric has introduced a mobile application designed to provide common weld settings for SMAW, GMAW/FCAW and GTAW processes. Its Weld Parameter Guide mobile application is available for Apple® iOS devices and Android™-enabled devices.

“Welders can use the app to find the correct welding parameters for their projects, welding process, material and thickness. Designed for welders in the shop or in the field, the Lincoln Electric Weld Parameter Guide provides convenient access to information that will help them set up their weld operations on the go, with no Internet or Wi-Fi connection required,” the company told ISMR.

The app’s key features include an easy-to-use graphical interface featuring icon-based menus; dynamic “smart” look-up tables; US and metric settings option; typical machine settings for SMAW, GMAW/FCAW and GTAW welding processes and a notes section that allows the user to add additional weld set-up information. Steel and stainless steel material machines settings for SMAW as well as aluminium, steel and stainless steel material machine settings for GMAW/FCAW and GTAW are also featured.

The Weld Parameter Guide is available at no cost to smartphone and tablet users through the Apple iTunes® App Store and the Google Play Store™. The app is compatible with Apple iOS devices (Apple iOS Version 6 and newer) and Android™-enabled devices (Android™ Version 2.2 and newer). See www.lincolnelectric.com ■
