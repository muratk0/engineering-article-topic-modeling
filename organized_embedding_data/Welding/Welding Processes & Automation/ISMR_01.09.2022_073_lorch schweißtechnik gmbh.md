[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Lorch Schweißtechnik will be presenting its entire range of welding solutions at EuroBLECH from the latest welding systems to cobot/robot solutions and a new welding helmet generation. The new R series, for MIG-MAG welding in crafts and light industry, will make its trade fair première.

"Our new compact welding cell for robot welding, developed with Yaskawa, will also be presented in Hanover," explained Lorch.

The Lorch MicorMIG can be used for conventional MIG-MAG welding as well as pulse welding and as an alternative for more elaborate TIG welding. The internet-based Lorch Connect solution can be connected to any Lorch welding unit for quick retrieval of data for documentation or analysis.

With the APR 900, Lorch is presenting a new generation of auto darkening filters in Hanover. These helmets are designed to reduce welder stress, offering adjustment options and high-end glare protection.
