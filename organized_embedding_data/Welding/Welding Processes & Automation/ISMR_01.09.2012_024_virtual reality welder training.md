# Virtual Reality Welder Training

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

LEARNING TO weld can be a very complex and costly exercise, involving much practical work using live welding on expensive equipment, power, consumables and materials, so MIG/MAG, MMA and TIG welding process training in a virtual reality environment from welding equipment manufacturers, Kemppi (UK) Ltd, is a cost effective way to help train tomorrow’s welders.

“The Kemppi ProTrainer is designed to manage the costs of learning by reducing material, consumable and power costs using a virtual welding and learning experience. Designed for use in educational establishments and company welder training facilities, Protrainer can increase the speed of the students’ learning curve by as much as 50% and reduce the cost of the training by up to 40%,” Kemppi told ISMR.

“ProTrainer offers a very realistic virtual welding environment and simulates the welding process and parameters necessary to weld successfully. It uses real welding guns and torches, so gives the student a real feel for welding, but without the restrictions of welding helmets, thereby making communication between the student and teacher that much easier.”

The system features a central server to manage communications between the ‘trainer module’ and ‘trainee module’. The ‘trainer module provides user management tools, real-time follow up for monitoring and regulating the student’s progression, performance and results by both the teacher and student. The ‘trainee module’, available as either a WORKBENCH or a LITE version, allows each student to select and work through defined welding exercises and to visualise and analyse their own personal work.

The WORKBENCH version is the hardware tool of ProTrainer with a high resolution wide screen display and comes equipped with real welding tools, while the LITE version, also provided with real welding tools, is a lightweight mobile trainer for use between training sites.

## EURO RIVET BUSH

TR FASTENINGS is set to capture new European business with the launch of its latest innovation, the Euro Rivet Bush. TR has been manufacturing the Hank rivet bush for 30 years and is a key supplier in the UK market of rivet bushes. Designed, developed and manufactured at TR’s production facility in the UK, this new product offers an alternative to the Kerb-Konus and PSM rivet bush ranges. The Euro Rivet Bush is produced using multi-spindle machines.

“Designed to offer a permanent female thread in sheet steel that is too thin to be tapped by conventional methods, the Euro Rivet Bush is easily installed,” TR Fastenings added.

Available in sizes M3-M8 in steel self-finish or plated, the product is offered in a number of different spigot lengths to suit specific applications. However, TR also offers reduced lead times on any non-standard diameters or spigot lengths.

The genuine Euro and Hank rivet bush products can be used in a number of industry sectors, including sheet metal fabricators, white goods, automotive, electronic and telecommunications etc. TR Fastenings offers a wide range of other sheet metal products, including; Hank self-clinch, weld nuts, blind rivets, K-series, blind rivet nuts and weld studs.
