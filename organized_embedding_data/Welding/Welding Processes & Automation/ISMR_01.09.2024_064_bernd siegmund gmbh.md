Bernd Siegmund GmbH, based in Oberottmarshausen near Augsburg (Germany), is a specialist in the design and manufacture of welding tables. The functional and patented products are used in machine, plant, metal and vehicle construction as well as with robotics.

At EuroBLECH, the company will be displaying its new welding cell, new workbench and other technologies/ products on more than 480m² of stand space. The new laser welding cell, for manual welding, provides mandatory protection from scattered radiation and reflections for surrounding people and objects. It also reduces interference caused by external factors such as airflows or dirt particles.

"We are combining the qualities of a common workbench with the strengths of our Siegmund welding tables and the extensive modular system. This results in an innovative workbench for everyone; the Siegmund workbench," it explained. The workbench is available in different versions: with a solid steel plate for welding work or with an aluminium table.
