# WELDING FILLET JOINTS

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

WELDABILITY-SIF has launched a new series of rutilecoated electrodes SifChrome 308L, SifChrome 312, SifChrome 316L and SifChrome 316L CRYO from its Sifbronze brand of welding products. SifChrome is suitable for welding fillet joints in the horizontal/vertical position, as the titanium oxide in the electrode coating makes these electrodes easy to strike and produce a smooth arc with little spatter, together with a good weld appearance and self-releasing slag.

Sifbronze has been manufacturing welding consumables and servicing the welding industry for 87 years and is now the only, UK-owned source of welding consumables.

Weldability-Sif supplies MIG, TIG, MMA, spot and oxy/fuel welding and plasma cutting machines, torches, accessories, consumables and personal protective equipment. It distributes over 4,000 tons of mild steel MIG welding wire per annum to the market. ■
