Welded components fabricator increases productivity by 65% with high-performance welding system

The company realized that to produce the desired quantities, it didn’t need to add more robot cells but replace the existing welding systems.

SITUATION

Painco, based in Rio das Pedras in São Paulo, Brazil, is a supplier of welded components and assemblies made from carbon steel plates. The company serves a variety of industries, in particular the yellow goods and commercial transportation sectors, which require processing of metal thicknesses that pose significant challenges.

To assist with production, Painco incorporated robot-assisted welding cells, which had the potential to increase productivity substantially. However, the company quickly discovered that using welding systems with just 350 amps resulted in subpar seam quality and welding speed. Rejects increased, and production capacity stagnated.

RESOLUTION

The company realized that to produce the desired quantities, it didn’t need to add more robot cells but replace the existing welding systems. Painco chose the Fronius TPS 600i power source, with a peak power of up to 600 amps, and upgraded to a Pulse Multi Control (PMC) welding package.

In its newly equipped production facility, the company found that the pulsed arc significantly improved material transfer, increasing welding speeds substantially. Two beneficial side effects also emerged.

"We were able to detect significantly lower heat input and a reduction in the welding spatter," said Operations Manager Rafael Severino. "This resulted in flawless weld seams with far fewer rejects, which enabled us to increase our productivity even further—all in all by up to 65%. This saves us about 120 working hours per month.

"At the same time, investing in the new devices was much cheaper than purchasing additional robot cells," he concluded.
