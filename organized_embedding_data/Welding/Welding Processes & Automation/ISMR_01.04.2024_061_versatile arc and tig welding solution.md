# Versatile arc and TIG welding solution

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

The new Fronius Ignis 150/180 series is designed to be robust and easy to handle. The durable housing keeps environmental factors such as dust and moisture at bay, while the technology inside offers a range of functions designed to facilitate the welding process.

The compact Ignis is available in the 150 A and 180 A power categories. There are also TIG variants for both models for those looking for a mobile TIG welding solution. The Ignis 180 is also available in country-specific versions and can be used in regions with different mains voltages.

"The first requirement for quality welding is a reliable arc. The Ignis 150/180 delivers this even in the event of voltage fluctuations, during generator-powered operation or with up to 100 metre-long grid leads. The integrated Power Factor Correction technology uses the existing mains voltage, improving the power factor. Not only does this make the Ignis extremely energy-efficient, but it also increases the welder's operating range. The Ignis is therefore suitable for use on construction sites, for repair and maintenance work in industry and agriculture or in a home workshop," explained welding specialist, Fronius.

## A wide range of functions

"Although the Ignis 150 (6.5kg) and the Ignis 180 (8.8kg) are particularly lightweight and easy to handle, they contain a host of features to make everyday welding easier. Both systems feature an easy-to-use menu operated via a rotary and push button, which allows users to select their desired parameters, and the seven-segment display clearly displays the settings at all times," commented Fronius.

The Ignis also features SoftStart, HotStart and TrackingArc functions to assist welders when they start welding. SoftStart was specially developed for basic electrodes that require a low welding current. HotStart, on the other hand, increases the current for a fraction of a second at the start to enable the electrode to ignite more easily which is useful for rutile and cellulose electrodes.

Like a flashlight, TrackingArc helps to find the exact starting position, for example, when re-igniting after an interruption or after an electrode change. This is especially useful when using non-automatic darkening welding helmets. It also helps to prevent welding errors, such as end-craters or notches, and saves time-consuming rework.

## Pulse function

The MMA Pulse function is designed for highquality welding at speed and is also suitable for challenging welding tasks in tight spaces. TIG pulse is also available for the TIG version of the Ignis 180. The pulsed TIG arc primarily facilitates the welding of very thin materials, since less heat is introduced into the base material.
