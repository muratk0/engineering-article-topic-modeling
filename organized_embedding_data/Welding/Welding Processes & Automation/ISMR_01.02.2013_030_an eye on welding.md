# An eye on welding

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

CEMSA SPA has recently widened its offer of welding solutions for the assembly of shelves, cabinets and metallic doors. Its solutions range includes single-head machines, double-head machines, multi-head machines, manual, semi-automatic, automatic machines and robotised cells.

It can offer either single independent welding units or welding machines integrated in working lines with downstream profiling, bending or panelling lines.

“Our units are designed to satisfy the needs in several demanding fields, ranging from the production of shelves to metallic furniture, reinforced doors to panels and metallic containers.” E-mail: info@cemsa.it
