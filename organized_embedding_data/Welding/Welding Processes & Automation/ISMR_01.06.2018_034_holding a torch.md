# Holding a torch

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

Right: The grip's ergonomic design with rubber inserts and the new anti-kink device facilitate a comfortable, fatigue-free work process, even when carrying out positional welding

EWM AG HAS expanded its range of MIG/MAG welding torches to include the PM series for professional users. The newly designed grip aims to guarantee a comfortable hold, even over long periods, and illuminates the working area with its motionactivated LED lighting.

Function torches are optionally available; they make it possible to control welding parameters remotely

The integrated colour display ensures that the welding parameters are easy to read and provides the welder with information on the current welding task.

"Weld spatters can be considerably reduced - this saves time on finishing work. The use of consumables can also be reduced, compared to commercially available welding torches, providing major potential for savings. Standard welding torches in the PM series are - as are all EWM welding torches – suitable for use on any commercially available welding machine with Euro torch connector," EWM told ISMR.

Additional comfort in the tough daily welding routine is always welcomed. With the new PM torch, EWM delivers relief for work. Its grip with rubber inserts is shaped ergonomically so that man and machine almost form one unified organic unit. With an innovative anti-kink device, balanced design and reduced weight, the new development from Mündersbach also contributes to a fatigue-free work process, even during positional welding.

The new PM welding torch from EWM makes its debut in combination with the new MIG/MAG Titan XQ multi-process welding machine. But it can also be used in the standard torch version together with all other MIG/MAG welding machines – thanks to the Euro torch connector.

"On the standard torch, the torch trigger can optionally be installed at the top. You also have the option of selecting function torches which make remote control of welding parameters possible. The OLED graphic display shows the welding parameters in a user-friendly manner at the welding site. With the high-end RD3X version, the welder can use ewm Xnet and component management to access a welding sequence plan. The welding parameters are set automatically by the system from the stored WPS. The setting process is less complex and constant quality is ensured - this way, Industry 4.0 can be integrated into everyday welding," added EWM.

The new PM welding torch from EWM is available in numerous variants. Customers can select the model which best matches their individual applications. The choice includes gas or water-cooling as well as short or long necks. The welding current range extends to 550 A. All torches are available as function torches.

"It lowers the error rate using an interference-free wire guide, excellent shielding gas coverage of the arc range and safe current contacting. At the same time, the use of consumables is also reduced because of the large material cross-section of the contact tips (M7/M9 instead of M6/ M8, as is common in the industry). Substantially better heat dissipation prevents overheating and extends service life. Shielding gas consumption remains low because gas losses are prevented and less finishing work is required because of less spatter," concluded EWM.
