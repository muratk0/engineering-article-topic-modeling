# Angles and edges

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

FEIN IS extending its stainless steel range to include a powerful fillet weld grinder. The flat tool head, which can be swivelled without the need for additional tooling, can quickly and efficiently machine fillet welds on metal constructions without damaging neighbouring surfaces, even in acute angles. The KS 10-38E was developed to meet the requirements of niche metal workers including specialist interior craftsmen, industrial tank constructors, ship and vehicle builders.

Fillet welds occur automatically when two metal work pieces are welded together at an angle. Metalworkers have to grind these often difficult-to-reach spots, to satisfy customers’ finish and corrosion resistance requirements. FEIN has developed a sophisticated solution especially for this niche application. With a head just 33 millimetres long, the powerful fillet weld grinder reaches even very narrow fillet welds. The head, which was developed by FEIN, can be smoothly swivelled through 180 degrees and fixed to the neck of the machine without the need for tools, which shortens the overall length of the machine and makes even work on corners and work offset to the side possible. The spark hood can be adjusted or removed entirely for different working positions.

“The fillet weld grinder features an 800W motor with permanent overload capacity.

Double gear reduction delivers a very high torque. The speed range can be electronically adjusted between 1,350 and 3,750 rpm. Even when subjected to continuous use, neither the machine nor the head will overheat – allowing for safe working and avoiding unnecessary interruptions,” said FEIN. See www.fein-uk.co.uk
