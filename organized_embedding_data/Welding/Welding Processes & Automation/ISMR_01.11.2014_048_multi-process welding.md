# MULTI-PROCESS WELDING

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products showcased Warrior™, a multi-process welding machine, at EuroBLECH 2014 in Hanover. Warrior is designed for GMAW (MIG/MAG), FCAW (Flux-Cored), SMAW (Stick), and GTAW (TIG) welding, as well as Arc Gouging. It delivers up to 500 amps at 60% duty cycle in a rugged package. This welding machine welds 0.8 – 1.6mm flux-cored and solid wires, has a full range of SMAW (Stick) capabilities and carbon arc gouges up to 10mm diameters.

“Warrior’s inverter-based design provides strong arc performance and energy savings, making inverter technology affordable for every welding application. Features such as power compensation, parameter adjustment close to the operator and ESAB’s TrueArcVoltage™ for constant and true arc voltage all day long, considerably reduce energy consumption compared to old technology SCR power sources,” explained ESAB.

“A high-duty cycle allows for long runs across a variety of applications. Warrior’s air tunnel design keeps critical electronics free from dirt, oil and foreign objects. A user-friendly design places all controls – including the power switch – on the front panel.

Warrior’s simple and clear user interface makes it easy for anyone to get started welding within a few seconds. The sun-friendly display makes the digital meters easy to read outdoors.”

A durable external body withstands the elements. Warrior’s IP23 rating is designed to endure harsh outdoor environments. It is also generator-compatible, making it suitable for remote job sites. In addition to the Warrior power source, the Warrior Feed 304 is a new rugged, user-friendly wire feeder. It features a high-torque 4-roll drive and includes all the controls needed to get the job done, including remote voltage, gas purge, inch wire and wire feed speed (WFS), trigger lock and slow run-in.

At EuroBLECH, ESAB also announced the release of its Columbus® III version 1.2, powerful CAD/CAM programming and nesting software for plasma, oxy-fuel, laser and waterjet cutting. This latest version of Columbus III offers many new features and updates to make programming easier and more efficient, improve material utilisation, increase productivity and streamline workflow.
