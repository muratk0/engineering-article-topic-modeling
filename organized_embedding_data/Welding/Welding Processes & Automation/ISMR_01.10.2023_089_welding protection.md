# Welding protection

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

The 3M™Adflo™powered air purifying respirator system, with 3M™ Speedglas™ G5-01 Series welding helmet, has been designed for workers in heavy-duty environments. The system provides particulate respiratory protection for welders in a robust, compact design with ergonomic, adjustable head suspension. A flip front welding shield allows for a clear, wide-view protective visor for grinding and set-up.

"The system has a high-efficiency particulate filter that can be 'stacked' onto an optional 3M™Adflo™Filter (not included) for additional protection against certain vapours or a nuisance odour pad assembly. Audible and visual alarms signal low airflow and low battery charge," commented 3M.

It is available in two different autodarkening lens options (VC/TW). A G5 Series storage/carry bag is also included.
