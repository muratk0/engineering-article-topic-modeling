# QinTron with Pulse function

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

A YEAR AGO, CLOOS presented its QinTron inverter welding machine for the first time. The welding specialists from Haiger have now expanded the new product line with a Pulse function for MIG/MAG welding with a pulsed arc.

"The QinTron welding machines, with infinitely adjustable voltage pre-selection, are available in the 400A, 500A and 600A capacity classes. This modern inverter technology offers great energy-saving potential at an optimum degree of efficiency. The multi-process system is flexibly applicable for the MIG/MAG, TIG (Liftstart), MMA and gouging processes," CLOOS told ISMR.

Now the QinTron is also available with the Rapid Weld and Vari Weld high capacity welding processes for MIG/MAG welding with a pulsed arc. Rapid Weld provides advantages wherever high penetration depths and a safe root fusion are required. The special control generates a focused stable arc with a very high arc pressure. By means of special electrical parameters, it is possible to modulate the penetration profile. Due to the very small opening angle, the quantity of filler material and shielded gas can be considerably reduced.

"The main advantage of Vari Weld is a very directionally stable and powerful arc. Thus, relatively high welding speeds with deep penetration can be achieved with Vari Weld. The DuoPulse option completes the welding process package. This function extends the energy control possibility. With alternating 'hot and cold' pulses, the weld pool is modulated by the welding capacity and thus the heat input into the component to be welded can be varied. The new welding processes open up new fields for different applications," added CLOOS.
