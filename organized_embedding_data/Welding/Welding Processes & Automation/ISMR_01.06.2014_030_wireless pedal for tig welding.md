# Wireless pedal for TIG welding

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

LINCOLN ELECTRIC has introduced a wireless pedal that delivers an enhanced level of freedom and mobility for TIG welding applications. The K3127-1 wireless pedal reduces clutter and improves workplace safety by eliminating cords that can lead to tripping hazards in the shop environment. The system includes the foot pedal (K3350-1) and receiver (K3351-1), which operate on a number of Lincoln Electric power sources. The pedal makes it easier to access hard-to-reach areas. The receiver features an LED display that indicates the status of foot pedal battery life, welding output and signal integrity.

It also includes a power cable adapter requiring 115V service. Extension legs adapt the pedal for use at an angle or on irregular surfaces. Email: www.lincolnelectric.com ■
