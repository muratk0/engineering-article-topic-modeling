# TiG and stick welding

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

THE NEW Square Wave® TIG 200 welding machine from Lincoln Electric offers a multi-process TIG and stick welding experience for craftsmen, small shop fabricators, makers, motorsports enthusiasts and hobbyists. Users can perform AC TIG welding on aluminium and DC TIG welding on steel, stainless and chrome-moly when precision and bead appearance are important. Users can also switch to stick welding when working with thicker materials or welding outdoors.

The Square Wave TIG 200 combines an easy-to-use interface for rapid set-up with built-in advanced features. A pulsed TIG feature provides a drumbeat-like rhythm for filler metal deposition.

AC frequency control allows users to adjust the machine for a wider or more narrow arc profile when TIG welding on aluminium. Adjustable AC balance provides more cleaning action on aged aluminium and helps to maximize penetration on thicker aluminium materials.

The portable machine plugs into standard 120V or 230V input circuits and weighs 46lb (21kg). The TIG output range is 10-200 amps when operating on 230V input, and 10-125 amps when operating on 120V input. The stick output range is 10-170 amps when operating on 230V input, and 10-90 amps when operating on 120V input. See www.lincolnelectric.com ■

## Scrap metal removal

PREMITEC’S MULTI-TRAY scrap shaker, sometimes referred to as the vibratory scrap shaker or electro-mechanical scrap shaker, has been designed for linear scrap removal from many types of presses. It utilises the 'tablecloth effect’, a slow acceleration in the forward stroke (material is transported forwards) and a rapid return stroke (material will remain stationary), ensuring movement in one direction only.

“Due to its compact design, the vibratory scrap shaker may be installed in confined, congested areas. Unlike many other transporters, shuttles or traditional conveyors, it does not need to be located in close proximity to the tool. The scrap removal trays can be located close to the ‘action’ but the vibratory conveyor power unit can be a safe distance away,” explained the company.

The linear scrap transporter can be used with a long single tray stretching some 10 metres or more, depending upon width, but is also adaptable for multi-tray installations such as multiple trays. The vibratory scrap conveyor is adaptable to requirements and a selection of trays can be mounted on the unit. Common ‘low profile’ trays can be used for different tools and applications. If using trays measuring over three metres, linear bearing supports can be provided. Using a 30-degree angle in the scrap trays, material can be made to be transported to the left or right, enabling part sorting to be carried out. See http://premitec.co.uk/work/scrap-shaker/ ■

## Create and optimise laser jobs

SCANLAB AG is releasing the latest version of its laserDESK program to create and execute professional laser marking and material processing jobs. Available immediately, Version 1.2 offers numerous new features including a "Parameter Assistant" to optimise all laser and scanner parameters for any processing task. Deeper integration with SCANLAB's SCANalign calibration solution enables precisely-positioned marking on every workpiece. LaserDESK 1.2 can be used in normal mode, or remotely for automated execution of laser jobs or even offline as a pure ‘Office Version’.

New remote commands enable the program's integration into system control as well as ‘slave’ or as ‘master’, allowing fully automated complex marking operations and dependencies. Additionally, integration with the SCANalign image processing solution has been further enhanced. Markings can be precisely positioned on every workpiece thanks to automatic image data transfer from the calibration system.
