## CARL CLOOS SCHWEISSTECHNIK GmbH www.cloos.de

CLOOS WILL present several innovations and further developments in manual and automated welding at EuroBLECH 2018.

Along with live demonstrations of manual welding, show visitors will see a range of CLOOS welding power sources, from entry to premium.

The focus will be on the QINEO NexT, a new high-tech inverter welding power source with, says CLOOS, excellent arc characteristics.

"We will also present MoTion Weld, a new process family for automated MIG/MAG welding. Here, welding processes in the short arc range are combined with a mechanical system which draws the wire backwards and forwards at high frequency. Due to low heat input into the workpiece and minimised spatter formation at high welding speeds, stable welding processes are particularly suitable for thin sheet applications," the company told ISMR.

The CLOOS portfolio features simple, compact systems as well as complex, chained systems with automated workpiece identification and loading/ unloading processes. CLOOS is a single-source supplier for associated robotic and welding technologies.

## IGM ROBOTERSYSTEME AG www.igm.at

'STRONGLY COMPACT' is the guiding theme of igm Robotersysteme AG's presentation at EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover. It develops robot systems for heavy-duty welding applications, designed for a minimum footprint and strong flexibility.

"The robot that will be on display at our booth offers a complete solution for workpieces of up to 3,000kg, covering just 30m² of ground. Nevertheless, the parts remain accessible all around and can be welded without removing and reloading. This is done by an L-shaped manipulator with a hollow shaft in the face plate; the robot can therefore access the workpiece from the back to reach inner seams e.g. of excavator car bodies," explained the company.

A rotating column with cross and vertical slide considerably enlarges the working range of the robot. The vertical slide is of telescopic design, large stroke and reduced build length. The displayed unit saves approximately 1,000mm in height if workshop height is limited.
