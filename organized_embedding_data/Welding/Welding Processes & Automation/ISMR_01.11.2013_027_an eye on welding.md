# An EYE on welding

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

GYS, the French manufacturer of welding machines and battery support equipment, has reported another successful year at the Schweissen and Schneiden show at Essen. A highlight from the show was its new Pearl 150.2, a 150A inverter MIG. This two-in-one gas/gas-less machine, manufactured in France, welds steel, stainless steel and aluminium and will be available from November 2013.

It is also showcased the award-winning TIG 250 AC DC. GYS’s continued investment in research and development resulted in the launch of this fan-less, advanced liquid-cooling three-phase TIG, marking a significant transition into the high performance industrial segment for GYS.
