# Record order intake at CLOOS

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

CLOOS produces complex and chained robot systems for automated welding

Welding specialist Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH, with its headquarters in Haiger (Germany), has registered an order intake of more than 160 million euros. Next year, CLOOS will celebrate its 100th anniversary – the engineer Carl Cloos founded the company in 1919 in Siegen.

"Highly automated and intelligent robot systems are in great demand in our German core market, but also all over the world. In future, we wish to clearly differentiate ourselves from our competitors with manual and automated high-tech welding products," commented CLOOS managing director, Sieghard Thomas.

The welding specialist has placed its emphasis on digitalisation and connectivity to support customers on the way to Industry 4.0. The new CLOOS Gateway, for example, is a complete information and communication tool. Users can view the performance and efficiency of their welding systems, localise shortages, avoid errors with preventive measures and increase overall production efficiency.

Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH has also considerably expanded its production capacities. Its new production hall in Haiger (total area of around 3,000 sqm) is now ready. CLOOS intends to further expand its international offices. CLOOS China moved to a new location with a considerably larger production area in May 2018. The CLOOS Czech subsidiary in Prague is also planning to move to a bigger location in 2019. Further expansion, for example at its production site in Poland, are planned for the short term.
