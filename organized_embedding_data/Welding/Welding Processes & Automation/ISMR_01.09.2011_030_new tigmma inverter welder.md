# NEW TIG/MMA INVERTER WELDER

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

THE TIG150HF from Weldability-Sif, the UK-based supplier of MIG, TIG, MMA, spot and oxy/fuel welding and plasma cutting machines, torches, accessories, consumables and personal protective equipment, is a new lightweight (12kg), single phase 230V, 13A, DC TIG/MMA inverter-based welding machine featuring IGBT (Insulated Gate Bipolar Transistor) technology, TIG lift arc and HF (High Frequency) arc ignition.

“This easy-to-operate machine can be switched between TIG and MMA welding and between HF and lift arc ignition at the turn of two quick selection switches on the front panel. The high 93V OCV (Open Circuit Voltage) and the arc force control feature makes this unit suitable for MMA welding using a range of different electrodes including low hydrogen, cellulosic and aluminium electrodes. It has a current range of between 5 – 130A and is rated 130A @ 15 per cent duty cycle, whilst the built-in gas solenoid, TIG torch switch socket and remote foot control option make it an excellent unit for the user just starting with TIG welding on mild steel, stainless steel and copper,” said the supplier.

Another TIG welding feature offered by this machine is an adjustable slope-down ‘crater fill’ current control. Overall, it has an ‘ingress protection rating’ of IP21 and includes protection against under and over-voltage, over current and thermal overload. ## TUBE PROCESSING SOLUTIONS

TRANSFLUID CREATES improved processes for tube bending and forming. Transfluid Maschinenbau GmbH develops free-of-interface automation systems from a single source for its customers to ensure optimum set-up for the entire tube processing process.

To quicken the tube bending process whilst reducing handling costs, Transfluid developed a solution that forms one side of the aluminium tube before bending. A 4-axle handling system takes over loading the fully electric bending machine. The safe inward transfer of the 80 to 800mm long tubes is guaranteed by loading from a step-feeding system with a capacity of up to 500 workpieces. To ensure quality upon bending, the length of every tube is checked before feeding. The bent tubes are then removed by a robot and fed into a combined Transfluid forming machine. The chipless cutting of over lengths then takes place as well as simultaneous axial forming. Subsequently, the robot feeds the single tubes to an optical measurement facility to control the forming geometries and surfaces. The integrated Transfluid automation system (t motion) can also process very short parts that, on customer request, are to be end-formed on both ends after bending. For this, the forming machine is equipped with an automatic clamping jaw changer. This allows quick change between two different form clamping pieces, according to user requirements.

## OILING THE WAY

SHELL LUBRICANTS has launched a new range of Shell Gadus greases. “The range is designed to meet the challenges of the biggest, fastest and largest equipment in operation today, in some of the coldest, hottest and most extreme conditions,” it told ISMR.

Shell’s steel customers include Tata/Corus, Europe’s second largest steel producer, Guangdong Shaogang Songshan in China, one of the country’s largest manufacturers of pig iron, steel and steel products, and others in other key steel manufacturing markets.

The Gadus range of greases have been specifically designed to lubricate bearings in some of the most arduous conditions seen in today’s steel manufacturing, such as the bearings used in continuous casting machines in steel plants. The bearings in a continuous caster have to cope with very high temperatures in excess of 180°C, often in the presence of dust and water. The grease is carefully designed to reduce excessive component wear and risks to production. It also increases safety in heavy-duty, slow moving bearings that face severe loading while experiencing some of the hottest working temperatures seen in steel manufacturing.

Shell Gadus S3 T460 and Shell Gadus S5 T460 (previously Stamina 0511 and Stamina HDS) are extreme-temperature, extreme-pressure greases that make up part of the new Shell Gadus range. The greases use the latest technology diurea thickener and additives to reduce the impact of oxidation, while increasing anti-wear and anti-corrosion properties, even when subjected to extreme heat.

Shell Gadus has also made it simpler for customers to compare product benefits and performance while taking into account other variables such as compatible thickeners, base oil viscosity, NLGI (National Lubricating Grease Institute) grade and any other special performance characteristics that make a grease suited for a specific or unique customer challenge. To aid the selection process, the range is based around key tiers, each offering increasing levels of performance and protection, including: Entry, Mainline, Premium and Advanced, which uses the latest synthetic, high performance technology.

## Smart crowning System

LVD COMPANY nv now offers Smart crowning, a programmable crowning V axis system for its line of PPEB-H Series and Easy-Form Series press brakes. Smart crowning provides an additional independent crowning axis on top of a conventional V-axis to automatically compensate for asymmetric variations in material thickness and material properties and thus assure bending accuracy over the entire length of a workpiece.

LVD’s Smart crowning system uses Smart Segments and Smart actuators that operate individually to provide independent compensation. Smart Segments are mechanically connected in a ‘caterpillar’ linking configuration so that it is impossible to program an inappropriate step in crowning which prevents damage to the crowning table and tooling.

In automatic mode, Smart crowning in conjunction with Easy-Form laser, measures angles at several points along the length of the sheet under pressure, up to a maximum of 16 points. Material spring-back is then measured in the centre of the press brake and values for individual Smart crowning actuators are identified (see Figure 1).
