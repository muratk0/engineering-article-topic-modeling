# Simulations of sheet metal structures

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

SIMUFACT, AN MSC Software company, has announced the release of Simufact Welding 7, which includes significant new functionality to its welding simulation software. New functionalities include performance and functionality/ease-of-use enhancements.

Rapid simulation of thin-walled sheet parts is possible - assemblies made of thin metal sheets can be calculated more quickly because the required number of elements in the model drops significantly due to the new solid shell elements feature. The segment-to-segment method, implemented in the new solver, is designed for faster and more efficient calculation of large models with many contact surfaces. A faster calculation method for resistance spot welding processes is also available, with shorter computing time for complex and large assemblies through the newly implemented Thermal Cycle method.

Easy definition of new welding robots with multiple welding paths, heat sources and fillet seams is also possible.

"Users can now take into account possible rotations and movements of the tools along the surface to predict the real clamping behaviour," added the company. "Users will now be able to compare their simulated model with their target design or with 3D measurement data as a reference model. Simulated deformations can be evaluated relative to the reference geometry. UNV files can now be imported, improving the compatibility of the welding software with third-party software and interoperability in the process chain."
