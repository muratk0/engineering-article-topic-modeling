E-book explores welding gases, gas detection equipment

CO2Meter.com

has released the ebook "Your Guide to Understanding Gas Safety & Analysis Across Welding Applications." It presents the gases used in welding, the importance of gas detection equipment in the industry, and the proper steps to protect staff working around hazardous gases.

Low-VOC cleaner compliant in 50 states

Walter Surface Technologies has introduced reduced-VOC SLAP SHOT heavyduty cleaner and degreaser. It dissolves and removes grease, oil, and grime on contact without leaving residue.

Designed to clean tough contaminants, the product delivers heavy-duty performance inspired by the original SLAP SHOT but with less than 10% VOCs, the manufacturer states.

The parts and brake cleaner is compliant in 50 states and adapted to meet California’s tight VOC standards and Canada’s 2024 VOC regulations.

Hand-held XRF analyzer designed for ease of use

The Vanta hand-held X-ray fluorescence (XRF) analyzer from Olympus provides rapid elemental analysis and alloy identification. Designed for durability in challenging industrial environments, the unit has an intuitive user interface and customizable software to help minimize training.

The unit is enabled for cloud technology, and optional wireless LAN and Bluetooth allow real-time data sharing.

Compact cordless angle grinder fits in small spaces

Ingersoll Rand offers the G5351 IQV20 cordless angle grinder and cutoff tool. The lightweight, compact unit offers a 1.0-HP output with up to 8,000 RPM.

The slim, balanced grinder helps make it easy to work in enclosed spaces while reducing fatigue, the company states. It is available in a kit that includes one grinder, two guards, one side handle, two batteries, and one battery charger.

Round bumpers protect machines and workpieces

Fairlane Products offers rubber and polyurethane bumpers designed to guard, stop, align, and protect a machine or workpiece during the manufacturing process. The round bumpers are available in inch and metric sizes in a variety of styles.

The RB-516 bumpers come with mounting options such as tapped hex shoulder and threaded, and stud mount. They come in black neoprene, white nitrile, or colored urethane. Diameters are 3/4 to 21/4 in. and 19 to 57 mm.

Also available are steel plate-mounted round bumpers that come in diameters and lengths from 2 to 4 in., with the plate sizes from 21/2 to 5 in. These round bumpers are available in 40, 60, or 80 durometers.

Combination forming, punching, laser cutting machine handles 6- by 12-ft. sheets

Boschert has announced the availability of its new CombiLaser 4020 combination forming, punching, and laser cutting machine, which accommodates sheets up to 6 by 12 ft.

The system offers IPG fiber lasers of 1, 2, 3, or 4 kW with Precitec cutting heads for processing a variety of materials and thicknesses. It also features 360-degree rotation to all tools and an automatic tool changer with capacity for up to 96 tools.

The upper punching head handles typical punching with full rotation, 36 tons of force, and up to 1,100 SPM. Also included is a lower cylinder for punching from under the sheet or forming. The lower cylinder also has 36 tons of force, fully synchronized rotation, a 25-mm stroke, and up to 250 SPM.

Counterbalance truck handles long, bulky loads

Combilift has unveiled the Combi-CB70E high-capacity electric counterbalance truck. It offers multidirectional ability for space-saving handling of long and bulky loads up to 15,500 lbs.

Features in the gas strut suspension cab include a tilting steering column, hydraulic steering, and a Grammer MSG65 seat.

An automatic swivel seat is available as an option; it automatically engages and swivels the seat and armrest 15 degrees to the right or left to match the direction of travel selected by the operator.

The truck also features independent electric traction, which provides all front and rear drive wheels with 100% traction control. This negates the need for differential lock on slippery surfaces and helps reduce long-load momentum twisting when traveling sideways.
