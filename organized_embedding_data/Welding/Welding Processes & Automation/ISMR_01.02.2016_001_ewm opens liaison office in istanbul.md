# EWM opens liaison office in Istanbul

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

Above: The EWM team in Istanbul. The new team joins Bernd Szczesny, President of the Executive Board of EWM AG (fourth from right) and Robert Stöckl, EWM Executive Board Member Sales (third from left), in celebrating the opening of the new liaison office in Turkey. (Photo: EWM AG)

GERMANY'S largest manufacturer of arc welding systems and welding machines sees great growth potential in Turkey. EWM AG opened a new site in Istanbul at the beginning of December 2015. EWM AG Türkiye Irtibat Bürosu is centrally situated in an industrial park with excellent transport connections near the airport and the harbour district. It targets workshops and industrial enterprises.

“Growth in production and, with it, the increasing demand for innovative welding technology in Turkey display exciting potential. The country is also highly attractive in terms of its position as a geographical gateway between East and West,” Mehmet Yildiz, export manager at EWM, told ISMR.

Above: New EWM liaison office in Turkey. The arc welding systems manufacturer entered the Turkish market with a liaison office in Istanbul in December 2015. In addition to sales with a specialist market concept, the new branch offers a comprehensive range of services. (Photo: EWM AG)

The new office includes a showroom and a technical training centre. Besides consulting and training, EWM also aims to establish itself as a port of call for all welding-related queries. The liaison office will issue regular invitations to information events as a result.
