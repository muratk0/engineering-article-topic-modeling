# Laser welding workstation

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

AMADA MIYACHI EUROPE has announced the availability of its MIYACHI EAPRO NOVA3 laser welding workstations for precision spot- or seam welding of metal parts (including medical, automotive, electronic and aerospace components).

"The NOVA3 is suitable for seam sealing medical devices and high-frequency aerospace radar components, spot welding small mechanical parts and battery housings, as well as hermetic seam welding of sensors, batteries and battery packages. The MIYACHI laser welders can join a wide range of stainless steels, nickel alloys, titanium, aluminium and copper," explained the manufacturer.

"The modular, flexible, lean-manufacturing-ready laser welding workstations feature CE-approved Class 1 eye-safe enclosures. An integrated touchscreen enables easy programming."

The NOVA3 can be customised to specific production requirements and equipped with pulsed Nd-YAG lasers (up to 600W), continuous wave fibre lasers up to 5000W and quasi-continuous wave (pulsed) fibre lasers up to 600W average power. Manual, pneumatic and motorised door configurations are available, with horizontal or vertical opening directions. The NOVA3 offers servo motor-driven axis (XYZ) CNC motion.
