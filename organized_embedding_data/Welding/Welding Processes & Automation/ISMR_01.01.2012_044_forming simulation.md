# Forming simulation

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

SIMUFACT ENGINEERING will present new versions of its simulation software, Simufact.forming and Simufact.welding, at this year's EuroBLECH 2012 exhibition, from 23-27 October in Hanover, Germany. The new versions provide more functionality for a broader scope of applications while offering simplified usability – the new software releases are used for the design and optimisation of manufacturing techniques in metal processing. Simufact will showcase its product innovations at the joint stand of EFB in hall 11, booth E08 at EuroBLECH.

Simufact's simulation solutions cover all essential production processes for the production and processing of blank sheet metal – from rolling to sheet metal forming, welding and mechanical joining. Standard interfaces support the integration of Simufact software in existing CAD/CAE environments.

A new functionality – the coupling of Simufact.welding with Simufact.forming – smoothly links process and welding simulation. Thus, processes taking place before or after welding can be efficiently integrated in the numerical approach. Easy data transfer not only allows a user to consider the forming history, but also the strength analyses of the simulated welding seams. Step by step, this intelligent connection of Simufact solutions turns the simulation of entire process chains into virtual reality.

"The integration of material data in the simulation of forming and joining processes offers the user a multitude of new, experimental material data and also provides access to high quality analytical material data in combination with JMatPro®, thus ensuring accurate simulation results," the company told ISMR. Simufact forming is an established simulation solution for forming and joining processes. By offering improved meshing and report capabilities, Simufact.forming 11 eases the engineer's daily work in tool and process development. The new optimisation feature allows the comfortable testing of process options and ensures substantial time savings for the definition of best process design. Highlights include the flexible description of thermo-physical boundary and initial conditions, and the automated reporting in pre- and post-processing.

Simufact.welding 3.1 enables the simulation of various welding methods. The latest solver technology and a userfriendly overall concept allow for an effective calculation of welding sequences and realistic prediction of component distortion, whilst taking into account microstructural transformations. Calculation of phase transformation and phase volume fractions, as well as the automatic mapping of weld filler metals, complement the new features. See www.simufact.com
