# From office to workplace

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

The ewm Xnet base module streamlines production processes

WITH ITS WELDING 4.0 welding management system ewm Xnet, EWM AG brings the added value of Industry 4.0 to welding production. By digitally linking planning, controlling and production operations, the modular software opens up new opportunities for companies of all sizes to enhance their efficiency.

"The new highlight of ewm Xnet is component management; it expedites the entire value-added chain and offers maximum transparency. Together, the three ewm Xnet modules and the Xbutton function help to utilise savings potential, proactively prevent production errors and automate previously time-consuming documentation," explained the manufacturer.

"By managing components, creating welding sequence plans and assigning WPS, the new component management system makes welding businesses more efficient, from planning to production. It quickly pays off thanks to cutting production costs, reducing non-productive time, minimising errors, producing clear WPS for every single run, guaranteeing quality and defining ideal welding parameters at all times. It also offers users more transparency. The welding coordinator can see all relevant production and quality data throughout the entire production process with no extra effort required."

ewm Xnet records welding data in realtime, manages the data and sends all consumption values straight to the office: from the welding current and welding consumables to gas and, of course, a major cost factor – effective arc time. In this way, unutilised potential can be quickly and clearly identified. When devising and managing welding procedure specifications and allocating welders, WPQ-X Manager from ewm Xnet is a graphics editor which displays all runs and seams quickly and precisely. Welding procedure specifications are transferred directly from the office to the welding machine via LAN or WiFi and can easily be accessed by welders. The WPQ-X Manager is also available as a stand-alone software module, independent of ewm Xnet.
