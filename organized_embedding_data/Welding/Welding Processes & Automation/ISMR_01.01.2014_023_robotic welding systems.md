# ROBOTIC WELDING SYSTEMS

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

KEMPPI OY, a Finnish welding solution provider, has joined forces with ABB Robotics to develop fully equipped robotic welding systems/packages.

ABB has indicated that the design of the “technologically innovative” welding packages will be modular, cost-effective projects for companies to easily install or to upgrade into a robotic welding system. Kemppi equipment will be featured at all certified ABB Robotics’ welding lab locations enabling customers to conduct robotic welding trials on the latest equipment available. These joint business ventures will cover Europe, Asia, Africa and South America and the co-operation is non-exclusive.

ABB FlexArc® robotic welding cells are complete robot systems available in several flexible and versatile standard modular packages. The KempArc welding system automation range allows flexible configuration to suit customers’ specific demands/welding applications. The range is designed for automated MIG/MAG welding and there are two power classes available, 350 and 450 amp. Each power source offers a wide range of welding curves for Pulse or Synergic MIG/MAG welding, thus ensuring quick set-up for busy working conditions.

Wise welding processes for automation products also offer solutions to meet challenging welding applications. See www.kemppi.com ■

HYGIENE-COMPATIBLE COMPONENTS Significant improvements to hygiene management can be made with components engineered for hygiene compatibility. Solvaro, the metal components manufacturer, supplies a number of companies whose top priority is hygiene. Designed on a project-specific basis, the product range includes stainless-steel ageing racks for cheese, dishwasher filters and additional components for industrial plant engineering.

For example, Solvaro manufactures ageing racks for Studer Maschinenbau AG, a high quality supplier to the cheese industry. The ageing racks have an Rv-5-8 perforation which allows the excess liquid to drain while exposing the cheese to enough air to allow the microorganisms to do their work. The ageing racks have a diameter of 854cm and are made of 1.4301 stainless steel which provides suitable hygiene properties and prevents the build-up of germs, with no risk of rust.

The production of cheese – and many other foods – requires highly specialised machines and facilities that are customengineered for the production process. As a logical consequence, companies need small quantities and quick turnarounds are common. ■
