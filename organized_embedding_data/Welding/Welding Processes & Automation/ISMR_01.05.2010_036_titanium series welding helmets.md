# TITANIUM SERIES WELDING HELMETS

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

Miller Electric Mfg. Co. has launched its line of Arc Armor Titanium Series welding helmets, designed for industrial protection, performance and comfort. The Titanium Series is built upon a shell design that is lightweight for extended wear, reflects radiant heat and features a quick-release front cover lens to reduce welder downtime.

Featuring Miller's lenses and an exclusive aluminum heat shield secured within a durable shockabsorbing gasket, the Titanium Series is made to withstand the rugged abuse commonly associated with industrial welding.

All three Titanium autodarkening models (9400, 9400i and 7300) feature Auto-On/Auto-Off, grind mode, independent arc sensors, lowamperage TIG performance (5 amps), magnifying lens holder and meet ANSI Z87.1-2003 (High Impact) standards.
