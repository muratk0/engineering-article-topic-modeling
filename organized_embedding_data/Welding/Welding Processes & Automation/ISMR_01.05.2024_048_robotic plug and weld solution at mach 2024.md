# Robotic 'plug and weld' solution at MACH 2024

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Pemamek showcased its compact robotic welding station, PEMA Skytrack, at the MACH exhibition in Birmingham, UK, this April. The PEMA Skytrack station integrates robotics, intelligent software and advanced welding technology.

"Robotic welding offers a solution to challenges in production where many companies are struggling to find talented welders/workforce and goals for productivity and efficiency are high. A well-designed robotic welding system increases safety and quality and enhances a company's competitiveness," explained Pemamek.

"Our PEMA robot station is designed to bring improvements and efficiency to heavy manufacturing processes. All welds are designed in PEMAWeldControl for smooth and easy programming of the welds," it added.

All the company's robotic welding solutions are custom-made to fit production and specific needs. PEMA Skytrack is an advanced robotic welding station designed to optimise welding processes across various industries. With a small factory footprint, and two-ton payload, it can be rapidly installed and integrated into production routines for "plug and weld."

"Robotic welding increases productivity and flexibility in production. One PEMA welding robot typically does the job of three-five welders. The welder becomes an operator and can perform multiple welds at the same time," concluded Pemamek.
