# CAPITAL VICES By Dipl.-Ing. Walter Lutz

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Vices form part of the basic equipment of every workshop but workshop fitters are under enormous pricing pressure due to cheap overseas competition. To rationalise its production and maintain constant quality, tooling supplier Ridge Tool has invested in two Cloos robot welding cells, which are optimally integrated into the production process.

To achieve constant quality with the corresponding quantities, Ridge Tool in Gevelsberg replaced its previous welding technology with two turnkey, standardised Z-series compact cells from Cloos (based in Haiger) and are now producing 300 vices on them per day.

“These cells offer us a lot of advantages, with robots, power supply and welding technology working together perfectly with no problems,” says works director, Herbert Lorbach. If necessary, they can be transported by forklift within the production area and are quickly ready for operation again.

## DOUBLE PRODUCTIVITY

“The investment in flexible robot cells is important, as we are working with continuous improvement processes,” Lorbach adds. “This is why we are twice as productive compared with other companies.”

On a tour through the factory – with a vice tradition which is over 100 years old – it becomes clear what the managing director, who is full of ideas, is talking about: other machines

and work stations are arranged around the two welding cells, so that the employees do not have to move too far unnecessarily. The material flow from the semi-finished parts via the welded assemblies to the finished vices runs perfectly locked ‘hand in hand’.

“In this way, we can easily achieve an annual output of 60,000 vices with constant quality, using just 13 production employees,” says Lorbach. Since the US tooling company Ridge Tool of Elyria (Ohio) took over the Gevelsberg manufacturer, Peddinghaus, in 1996, production has been consistently improved through a focused Lean Manufacturing Strategy. The manufacturer is consequently operating very competitively today in the world market producing quality vices – four models in different designs.

“The specialist trade of workshop fitters to industry and the craft trade are our customers, where our vices are in daily use,” says Herbert Lorbach. He also offers special solutions such as the vice with integrated tube chuck jaws, for the fixing of rods and tubes or the quick action chuck for more efficiency on the building site.
