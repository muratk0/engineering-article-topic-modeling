## An eye on FABTECH

AWS partners with the Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME), Precision Metalworking Association (PMA) and the Fabricators and Manufacturers Association (FMA) to hold the annual FABTECH exhibition, the largest event in North America dedicated to showcasing a full spectrum of metal forming, fabricating, tube and pipe, and welding equipment and technology. This year, FABTECH returns to the McCormick Center in Chicago from 9-12 November 2015 – please see ISMR’s FABTECH preview on page 34 for further details.

“This year’s event is expected to be one of the best ever with more than 1,500 exhibitors and an expanded show floor with 650,000 net sq. ft. of innovation. More than 40,000 attendees will converge here to experience four days of equipment buying and selling, networking and professional development,” AWS told ISMR.

American Welding Society (AWS) highlights during FABTECH 2015 include:

## ■ ‘So You’re the New Welding Engineer Conference

This two-day conference is for managers, engineers, designers and others with welding responsibility but limited exposure/background in welding. The conference explains how to get up to speed quickly and ask the right questions to get the results needed and save time and money.

## ■ RWMA Resistance Welding School

A two-day resistance welding school sponsored by the American Welding Society and the Resistance Welding Manufacturing Alliance, and conducted by industry specialists. The basics of resistance welding and real-life applications of the process are covered. Participants learn at their own pace and discuss specific welding concerns with the instructors. Attendees can bring their own samples for discussion.

## ■ The AWS Professional Welders Competition

Professional welders can sign up on-site to compete for a US$ 2,500 first prize, a US$ 1,000 second prize and US$ 500 third prize. Contestants will make a single-pass SMAW weld with E7018 on low-carbon steel. Speed and quality will be the criteria.

## ■ Welding Wars Competition

Teams of three student welders (over 16 years of age) will fabricate the weldment on a supplied print using GTAW, GMAW and plasma cutting, with sheared and cut material provided within a time limit of two hours. A panel of certified welding inspectors will evaluate all weldments to AWS D1.1 based on the accuracy of project of print specifications, weld size, overall weldment appearance, craftsmanship, professionalism and safety.
