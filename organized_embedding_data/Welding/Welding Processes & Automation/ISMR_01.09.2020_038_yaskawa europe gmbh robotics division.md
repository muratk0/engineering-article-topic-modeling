# YASKAWA Europe GmbH (Robotics Division)

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

www.yaskawa.eu.com

YASKAWA manufactures new compact ArcWorld cells for robot-based arc welding, designed to increase throughput ‘flexibly and cost-effectively’.

“The ArcWorld cell formula for success is simple: the MOTOMAN robot, positioner, controller and power source are grouped on one single platform – saving space and immediately ready for use. This allows the robot cell to be moved quickly and easily as required. The cell is also very easy to install and operate,” commented the robotics specialist.

## XLase GmbH

www.xlase.de

“WE OFFER customers increased performance for the laser cutting of tube and sheet. Our fully automatic TS65 is a system for the high-performance processing of tubes and profiles. New, adaptive pneumatic chucks, with an efficient pressure feedback system, and the intelligent combination of distance and height control ensure maximum production reliability, allowing the shortest travel distances and process times,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

“The successful GH-Series for cutting sheet metal is entering its fourth generation now and achieves extremely high machine dynamics, thanks to an even stiffer machine frame and consistently implemented lightweight construction in the motion unit,” it added.

## Yu-Chiun Precision Corp. www.yuchiun.com.tw

YU-CHIUN Precision Corp was established in 1983 in Shulin City, Taipei County. It specialises in the multimanufacture of progressive stamping dies and complex parts (to drawings).

“With more than 30 years of experience, Yu-Chiun uses advanced equipment to meet the needs of our clients. Our service and offering includes progressive stamping dies; plastic mould components; CNC optical profile grinder processing and complex parts (according to drawings),” it told ISMR.
