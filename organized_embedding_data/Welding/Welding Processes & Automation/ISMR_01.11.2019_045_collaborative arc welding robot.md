# Collaborative arc welding robot

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

FANUC AMERICA, a leading supplier of robotics, CNCs, ROBOMACHINEs and Industrial loT solutions, demonstrated its new line of collaborative arc welding robots at FABTECH 2019 in Chicago, USA, recently.

"A built-in sensor allows the welding cobot to work safely alongside people without the need for expensive guarding. Easy to use, even for those who have never used a robot, our arc welding programming interface supports simple and complex applications, including advanced features such as Weaving, iRVision, Seam-Tracking, Thru-Arm Seam Tracking (TAST) and Multi-pass," FANUC told ISMR.

Both of FANUC's new welding cobots provide six axes of motion. The CR-15iA for ARC offers a 15kg payload and a 1441mm reach. The CR-7iA/L for ARC has a 7kg payload and 911mm reach. High payload capabilities, combined with the large motion range, increases system flexibility to process a broad range of parts.

FANUC's arc welding robot family also supports newer applications such as wire arc additive manufacturing (WAAM). At FABTECH, the new FANUC CR-15iA for ARC cobot, equipped with the Lincoln POWER WAVE® R450 robotic power source, carried out live welding to build an aerospace jet turbine component.

"To produce the part, the robot deposited material layer by layer, so that only the required material is used. Compared to traditional machining, additive manufacturing can significantly reduce material costs and waste. In addition, it is easy to make production changes or build prototype parts as required with minimal time delays," explained FANUC.

Both cobots for ARC offer floor, ceiling and angle mounting options. CR-15iA has a hollow wrist for seamless integration of weld package dress outs, third-party utilities and sensor cables. The hand guidance function is available for all FANUC CR collaborative robots, enabling users to teach their collaborative robot by leading it through paths. The latest R-30iB Plus controller features an intuitive iPendant with enhanced screen resolution and more processing capability compared to previous versions.
