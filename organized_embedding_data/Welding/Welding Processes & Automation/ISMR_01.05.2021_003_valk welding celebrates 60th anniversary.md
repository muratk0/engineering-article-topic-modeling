# Valk Welding celebrates 60th anniversary

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

Valk Welding is 60 years old this year, a milestone for the European welding robot integrator. With the establishment of "LAS Verkoopmaatschappij", Remco H. Valk's father started the company on 22 March 1961 and it has grown into one of the largest welding robot integrators in Europe.

"The automation of welding technology has always been an important part of our business, alongside the sale of welding wire and welding equipment," said Remco H. Valk. This led to the delivery of its first welding robot in the late 1970s.
