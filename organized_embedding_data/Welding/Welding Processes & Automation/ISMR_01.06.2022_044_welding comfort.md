# Welding comfort

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

Swiss manufacturer Optrel joins welding helmet safety, performance and comfort with precisely engineered design.

“For field of view and eye protection, our Panoramaxx series helmets offer allday comfort, with fully adjustable IsoFit® headgear," it explained.

Optrel has introduced the Position Locker and new headgear features designed to take the Panoramaxx series of welding helmets and IsoFit® to “a new level in practicality and comfort, it told ISMR.

The Position Locker is a small, two-piece component which, said Optrel, plays a key role in enhanced helmet function and comfort.

“With the Position Locker, any unintentional dropping of the visor is a thing of the past. Lock the visor closed for welding work or open it. The Position Locker keeps the helmet visor fixed upright," explained Optrel.

"IsoFit® headgear adjusts in width, length and height to fit the wearer's head for individual comfort, even in extreme working positions. To optimise the headgear for wearability and durability, the IsoFit® is now fitted with a front sweatband. Its wide comfort pads are improved for long-lasting and hard-wearing weight distribution, enhancing all-day comfort," it continued.

The Position Locker and new IsoFit® headgear features are now standard on all Panoramaxx series welding helmets.

In 1986, Optrel was a pioneer in automatically darkening welding helmets. Today, it is a leading global supplier of face and head protection products, breathing protection solutions and active sunglasses. The original start-up has long since become a global player. The Optrel group includes highly specialised companies.■
