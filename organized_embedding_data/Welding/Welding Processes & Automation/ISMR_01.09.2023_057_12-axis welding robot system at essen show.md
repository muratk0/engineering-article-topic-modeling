# 12-axis welding robot system at Essen show

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

European welding robot integrator, Valk Welding, will present a large 12-axis welding robot system for the robotic welding of heavy and long products of up to 20 tons and workpiece lengths of up to 25m at the Schweissen & Schneiden trade fair (Essen, Germany) this September. As a trade fair highlight, it is offering the welding robot system for sale from now with the option of installing it at the buyer's premises shortly after the trade fair. It therefore offers a solution for companies with a short-term need for an automation solution that can weld very large workpieces in very small batches (up to single pieces).

"Of course, the large welding robot installation is also intended to show what we can do in terms of welding robotisation for companies with high-mix, low-volume production. In fact, the cell is equipped with the latest developments in welding seam tracking with Arc-Eye Adaptive that adjusts welding parameters in real-time in case of seam deviations as well as automatic wire change; integrated fume extraction; full offline programming and CE protection," explained CCO, Peter Pittomvils.

"We have developed innovative solutions so that we can [offer] a continuous process. Companies that make bulky parts in small numbers and large variations for offshore, heavy transport and heavy machine building, among others, have mostly already automated the welding production of large batches, but want to be able to do the same for small numbers. This is where we distinguish ourselves from traditional welding robot integrators," he added.

Valk Welding serves small and large companies, as demonstrated with its compact FRAME-E welding robot cell. With two side-by-side workstations of 3100mm on a torsion-free frame, this concept is one of its most widely used systems designed for low-downtime workpiece changes.

At previous Schweissen & Schneiden trade shows, visitors could learn about the integrator's Offsite Teaching, in which the welder manually programs workpieces using VR (virtual reality) glasses. During the trade fair, Valk Welding will demonstrate the latest version of its Offsite Teaching powered by VRPS. Its new ARP (Automatic Robot Programming) automatically programs the robot (including searching) after users click on the weld seams.

As welding consumables largely determine the continuity of the robotised welding process, Valk Welding will also showcase its latest developments such as welding wire; wire transport; robotic torches; cable assemblies and custom solutions at the exhibition on booth 3B67 in Hall 3.■
