## MEUSBURGER GEORG GmbH & Co KG

www.meusburger.com

"MOULD-MAKERS HAVE used our multifunctional H 1000 clamping system for many years. Now our innovative H 3000 system, which has been designed and built to precisely match the standard hole pattern of Meusburger die sets, is also available for die-making. The high precision of the H 3000 and the accuracy of the Meusburger standard die sets result in an exact and dimensionally perfect stamping die," Meusburger told ISMR.

Due to very tight tolerances of the mounting holes and the clamping elements, the die plates can be clamped and machined with repeatable accuracy in the μ range using the Meusburger H 3000 clamping system. This, says the company, saves valuable time and money in production.

## MICRONORM Woronka GmbH www.micronorm.de

THE TRUECAM PILLAR Cam is used in large dies for automotive stamping technology for applications in which aerial or die mount cams cannot be used for punching holes or coining contours at different working angles. Micronorm has expanded its TrueCam Pillar Cam range with its "SMALL" version, which will be on its booth at Blechexpo 2017.

"With its compact design and stable guide system, the TrueCam SMALL Pillar Cam is ideal for applications in progressive dies, where punching, bending or coining is required at different working angles of up to 45°. By using the TrueCam SMALL Pillar Cam, you save space in the dies as well as reduce effort and cost during design, manufacturing and handling. It can also be used in large automotive dies and as an extension of the existing Pillar Cam program with smaller sizes. The program is designed to be used with standard shank diameters for shoulder punches of 10 to 32mm. Pillar diameters 28, 40 and 50mm are offered as standard." it told ISMR.

## CISAM IMPIANTI Srl

www.cisamimpianti.it

CISAM IMPIANTI'S NARROW strip winding lines allow the "turn beside turn" rewinding of steel or non-ferrous strip from coils weighing up to 5000kg. The material head/tail is join-welded and rewound directly onto the winding machine mandrel, spools or disposable cardboard.

They are mainly used by narrow strip producers and service centres or combined with galvanic sheet/plate equipment for strip surface treatment. The company's narrow strip edge-profiling and coil processing lines differ from traditional coil processing lines as they allow edge-profiling and subsequent calibration through skin-pass of the strip to be processed. Strip head/tail welding can be performed by flash, TIG or laser welders, depending upon the quality and dimensions of the strip to be welded. On request, a tempering system of the welded area can be integrated for the processing of high carbon content strip.
