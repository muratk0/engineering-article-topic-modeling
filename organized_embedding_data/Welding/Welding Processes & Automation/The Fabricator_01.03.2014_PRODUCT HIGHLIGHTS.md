Monitoring tool captures real-time arc-on, deposition, and welding output info

A new Internet-based industrial welding information management system from Miller Electric Mfg. Co. provides real-time data from the weld cell. Insight Core™ automatically collects and reports arc-on time, arc starts, and quality performance based on amperage and voltage.

The system is available in two configurations: one for the Miller® Axcess® family and one for Miller 14-pin-compliant power sources. A field-installed retrofit module is designed for the manufacturer’s power sources already in use.

With the system, manufacturers can assess welding productivity and quality in real time from anywhere via the Internet. A preconfigured dashboard reports a daily summary of key performance metrics. Manufacturers can use the dashboard to monitor total arc-on-time percentage; get real-time visibility of welds outside of predetermined threshold limits for amperage and voltage; and set goals to raise the bar on corporate welding performance.

2-kW fiber laser cutting system delivers same power as conventional 4-kW fiber laser

Amada, in collaboration with JDSU, has introduced the ENSIS 3015 AJ fiber laser cutting system. A 2-kW fiber laser oscillator and optical components are engineered to work in synergy, allowing the system to produce the power and beam efficiency equivalent to that of a conventional 4-kW fiber laser, according to the company.

The system automatically provides the optimal beam configuration based on the material being cut. The oscillator is designed as an integrated module, resulting in a size reduction of 50 percent. This integrated design allows the system to deliver a high-power, high-quality laser beam in a small footprint, states the manufacturer.

Punching machine also bends, forms, taps

LVD Strippit has introduced the PX-1530 punching machine. It can punch, form, bend, and tap on a single machine to process complex, 3-D parts.

With its closed-welded frame and heat-treated, stress-free construction, the machine provides large all-tool rotation capacity, high hit rates, and large workpiece handling. It is available with modular automation options.

The system allows for bending of small boxes, brackets, knockouts, louvers, and countersinks with flange heights up to 3 in. Parts at any angle on the sheet can be formed, punched, and bent.

Tooling line lightens load on stamping robots, personnel

De-Sta-Co has introduced the Accelerate collection of lightweight tooling. According to the manufacturer, the end effectors are up to 50 percent lighter than their predecessors, allowing line speed gains of 25 to 30 percent.

Made of high-strength, aircraft-grade aluminum, the tooling maintains strength while clamping wall thickness and overall weight are reduced. Weight savings come from thin-wall extruded tube for the end effector frames rather than heavier solid bars or thicker-wall tubing.

The modular tooling is customizable and sized to global standards.

Vision-guided technology designed for robotics applications

ABB Robotics has introduced Integrated Vision, a smart camera system for 2-D vision-guided robotics applications.

Powered by Cognex, the system features the patented PatMax® algorithm for advanced part location. According to the manufacturer, the result is robust inspection, defect detection, guidance, alignment, and measurement to help ensure products meet quality standards.

The company’s RobotStudio PC-based programming tool provides the platform for creating vision tasks. Operators can select features, set parameters, and operate under real-world conditions from a library of common vision applications. The smart camera offers a variety of built-in communication protocols that interface directly with the robot.

Three-in-one welding machine handles light-duty projects

Victor Technologies™ has introduced the Tweco® Fabricator® 141i three-in-one welding machine, a multiprocess unit that runs off 115-V household power. It offers 10 to 140 amps of power for GMAW and lift GTAW and 90 amps for SMAW, making it suitable for light-duty welding projects in motorsports, auto repair, farm/ranch, metal art, and maintenance applications.

Push-button controls, LED displays, and a quick-start guide enable first-time welders to set up the unit. A setup chart inside the side panel guides users with diagrams and welding parameter recommendations.

The 32.2-lb. unit, which incorporates a built-in gas solenoid valve and wire feed system for GMAW, accepts 4- or 8-in. spools of wire.
