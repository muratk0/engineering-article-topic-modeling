# Welding in focus

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

EWM, Germany's largest manufacturer of arc welding technology, is a family-run business founded in 1957. Today, it has over 400 sales and service support outlets across the globe, making it one of the world's leading producers of welding technology (including sustainable solutions).

## Tetrix XQ for TIG welding

Powerful, durable and easy to use, the new Tetrix XQ 230 from EWM delivers TIG welding at a high level.

"We put particular emphasis on improving user-friendliness. The new controls with a 7-inch colour display are particularly well-arranged and intuitive to use. Welding tasks can be individually programmed and accessed again at any time. The Tetrix XQ 230 is incredibly powerful and can be run directly from a single-phase 230 V power socket, which makes it very versatile. An ergonomic and robust casing and compatible accessories round out the innovative concept," said EWM.

The welding machine enables continuous operation even on weak grids, with long mains leads or when in generator operation. Depending upon the welding task, the user can choose from various TIG and MMA functions. Up to four pulse variants are available. It is available in DC and AC/DC variants, so covers a broad spectrum of applications and materials.

"The Tetrix XQ 230 can be optionally equipped with a cooling unit, welding torch and trolley for transport at the construction site or in the workshop. The water-based cooling system, Cool XQ, enables continuous use of the welding torch even at high welding currents, under extreme workloads or unfavourable ambient conditions," added EWM.

## Expandable MIG/MAG welding machines

Different working environments present different challenges when welding. EWM has therefore developed new modular versions of its Phoenix XQ puls, Taurus XQ Synergic and Taurus XQ Basic MIG/MAG welding machines, providing welders with more convenience and customisation. The modular machines are also space-saving and lightweight, offering more mobility and flexibility. This new solution, EWM told ISMR, allows users to put together exactly the right additional components for their daily work activities.

Users can pick and choose which wire feeder or what cooling they prefer to use for their daily tasks. They can also retrofit their machines as needed, gradually completing their set-up with new modules. The wide range of user-oriented accessories – including a toolbox and cover lens for the control – also gives users the freedom to adapt their modular XQ series machines to any working area. Modular versions of the XQ welding machines are also compatible with the ewm Xnet welding management software.

## Digital welding data management

The ewm Xnet digital welding 4.0 management software solution supports, documents and analyses the entire production process, from work preparation to final costing, all completely paperless. This makes quality assurance even easier and allows users to extensively evaluate any welding parameters used for a job after the fact.

"ewm Xnet records all the welding data in real-time, manages it and communicates the consumption values. The software provides support in component management, creating welding sequence plans and allocating welding procedure specifications. You can also assign access permissions individually and keep track of each welder's test certificates," explained EWM.

It recognises changes to the parameters using real-time welding data recording and analysis, signalling these to users and helping them to prevent errors. Now, both EWM power sources and welding machines from any manufacturer can be integrated into the network, making a company's entire welding production transparent. Production-related quality assurance is becoming more and more important. Not only does it ensure only high-quality products, it also plays an important role in product liability.

Order numbers, assemblies, batches, serial numbers etc. can be recorded when entered using an industry scanner and assigned to the welding process. The welding management system also provides data on consumption and efficiency.

As ewm Xnet works in an open SQL database, various systems can read and write data for the purpose of digitalisation.

## Xnet Universal logger

With the ewm Xnet Universal logger, EWM is now extending the benefits of its welding management system to companies that use welding machines from several different manufacturers in their production line. This, said EWM, will ensure transparency throughout the entire production line, allowing users to fulfil all quality and product liability requirements in welding production.

“Even EWM welding machines without a digital interface and machines from any other manufacturer can now be integrated into ewm Xnet. In particular, this will give companies with few power sources, who still value a future-proof quality management system for welding, a great chance to integrate their existing machines into the Industry 4.0 environment," it added.

In combination with the ewm Xnet plug-and-play set, Universal logger users can choose to use ewm Xnet functions even on individual machines. It also makes using ewm Xnet for robotic welding, and other automated systems, easier.
