# Support for Japanese laser welding applications

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

On the right, Yukihiro Okumura, President Intech CO., Ltd. On the left, Karl Christian Messer, Managing Director, Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH

REMOTE LASER WELDING specialist, Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH, has intensified its presence in the Japanese market. The German manufacturer exhibited at the Japan International Welding Show in Tokyo, from 25–28 April 2018, with its Japanese sales agent, Intech CO., Ltd. Intech operates as a one-stop shop – offering all-in-one welding solutions that include the laser source, a scan system and a control system. In cooperation with Blackbird, Intech supports users by configuring individual welding solutions for flexible and efficient manufacturing processes.

"Japan's manufacturing industry – and especially its automotive sector – need innovative, robot-assisted laser welding systems to increase flexibility, process safety and productivity," Blackbird told ISMR.

Blackbird Robotics, with its associated company SCANLAB, supplies high-power scan heads in a range of laser welding systems. The product offer with 2D and 3D scanners include features such as spiral welding and on-the-fly capabilities, as well as laser beam oscillation and optical zoom for flexible car body assembly and parts attachment. The intelliSCAN FT 2D scan head, for example, is specifically designed for easy integration into robot and gantry systems. With full industrial suitability, this scan system is suitable for welding flat components and other electromobility applications.

The growing demand for in-process quality control led Blackbird to introduce to Japan its latest laser welding solution with a built-in OCT (optical coherence tomography) scanner. The system provides integrated edge tracking and seam topology measurement. Unlike other measurement methods, OCT-based distance measuring evaluates detailed data with full flexibility ahead of, within and after the laser welding process itself. The acquired data allows careful assessment of seam quality as well as detection and recording of welding defects and can be used in a clock-period-neutral manner for quality assurance procedures.
