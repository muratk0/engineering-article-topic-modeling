# Stud welding without separate ground clamps

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

AT EUROBLECH 2016, Heinz Soyer Bolzenschweißtechnik will be showcasing its portfolio of SRM® stud welding products. This includes new lightweight and mobile stud welding inverters designed for smooth welding in all positions, even under challenging conditions such as out-ofposition welding.

Using integrated SRM Technology® (Pat. No.: DE 10 2004 051389), the use of separate earth clamps can be dispensed with when welding. This offers the user substantial practical advantages, because the timeconsuming and cumbersome work step of fastening two separate and symmetrically arranged earth clamps to the workpiece is no longer necessary. Grounding now takes place directly via a contact ring which is springmounted onto the welding gun's support tube. This prevents the dreaded 'blowing' effect with weld bead accumulations on one side and unsightly scorching on the workpiece.

"With practical welding demonstrations at the trade show, the advantages of this patentpending, innovative welding technique will be shown in Hanover," the manufacturer told ISMR. Meet the company in hall 13, booth F08 or see www.soyer.com
