# KJELLBERG FINSTERWALDE

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]
www.kjellberg.de

AT EUROBLECH 2016, Kjellberg Finsterwalde will be presenting new products and technologies for plasma cutting and welding, as well as carrying out live welding and cutting demonstrations. Different technologies will be deployed, such as Contour Cut for cutting small holes and fine contours or cutting with air as plasma gas. Kjellberg's display at EuroBLECH 2016 will be completed by its XFocus fibre laser.

"With practical welding demonstrations, we will showcase the application range of our InFocus technology. It achieves process-stable and reproducible results in fully or partly automated production processes. A broad range of steels and non-ferrous metals can be welded or soldered. InFocus produces high-quality joints, fulfilling the most demanding industry standards," Kjellberg told ISMR.

## RIVIT Srl
www.rivit.it

ITALIAN MANUFACTURER, Rivit, will be showcasing its RIV606, an automatic feeder for blind rivets, and the RIV616, an automatic feeder for rivet nuts. RIV606 is an automatic feeder for standard blind rivets (from dia. 2.4mm - 6.4mm) and can be used with tools with mandrel suction, such as models RIV502, RIV503 and RIV505. RIV616 is an automatic feeder for rivet nuts from M3 to M8 with cylindrical or hexagonal shank, dome or reduced/countersunk head. RIV616 can be used with tools such as RIV938, RIV912 and RIV942.

"The reduction of riveting time is an essential goal for manufacturers and is especially true in the automotive sector," Rivit explained. This is why the manufacturer has designed a pneumatic/electric unit that loads the rivet, or the rivet nut, directly onto the head of the riveting tool - this is designed to halve installation time, improve productivity and improve safety (by eliminating the need for contact, when loading, between the operator's hand and the body of the fasteners).
