# Arc welding productivity

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

X5 FASTMIG IS an industrial multiprocess welding system developed in Finland by Kemppi, in collaboration with professional welders.

“The X5 FastMig is a versatile arc welding powerhouse that is built with energy-efficient inverter technology. Non-short-circuit ignition minimises spatter and creates high-quality reliable welds. Available in Manual and Synergic 400A and 500A models, X5 FastMig significantly enhances welding productivity and enables increases in arc-on time by improving weld quality, ergonomics and user experience,” said the welding specialist.

## Weld quality counts and ergonomics

“With X5 FastMig’s latest ignition technology, Touch Sense Ignition, the spatters are remarkably reduced which minimises the need for after-treatment. This allows the welder to fully focus on welding and increases arc-on time,” added Kemppi.

The optimised arc control can be further enhanced with Wise special processes developed at Kemppi.

X5 FastMig enables increases in arc-on time by improving ergonomics, weld quality and user experience.

X5 FastMig can be easily connected to the WeldEye welding management cloud service, which helps to manage the everyday tasks of welding quality and production departments more quickly and easily. It offers a simple solution for a faster welding documentation process, reduced weld repair rates and a 360-degree view of the welding production and project status.

“WeldEye allows for 100% traceable compliance with welding procedure specifications, as well as tracking of arc-on time,” added Kemppi.

The modular system with accessories is designed to enable higher arc-on time. As a multi-process welding system, X5 FastMig is suitable for MIG/MAG, MMA, gouging and TIG welding.

“Overall health and safety in the factory are the responsibility of the supervisor. It is not possible to control everything on the workshop floor, but it is easy to control the equipment used by the welders. We had the welders’ ergonomics in mind when designing X5 FastMig. The result is welding equipment that is safe and easy to use in different welding applications. Ergonomics in welding is more than a ‘comfy’ feeling – it ensures that welders stay safe and well, for instance by reducing unnecessary lifting and moving heavy items. The number of days that employees are out sick can be greatly reduced by a timely investment in ergonomics,” explained the Finnish welding specialist.

The four-wheeled cart features a pivoting gas cylinder plate that allows safe cylinder loading at the floor level without extra lifting.

The ergonomically designed, top-loading wire feeder saves space and improves occupational safety in changing wire spools. Hanging the wire feeder up to an adjustable angle, even in dual set-up, allows for easy accessibility. The handle’s internal structures are enforced to sustain temporary hanging in crane transfers.

## Accessories for any application

Flexlite GX welding guns feature flexible cable sets and ball-jointed cable protection that reduce wrist loading. The innovative neck structure adds to welding comfort with more efficient cooling, which also extends the lifetime of consumables. On-torch GXR10 remote control allows parameter adjustment at the weld joint.

The wide range of accessories have been developed to improve welding ergonomics and streamline production processes. With the help of X5 Selector, an online configuration tool, users can choose the set-up that best fits the everyday needs of welding production.

## User experience

The X5 FastMig’s wide range of accessories include transport units, interconnection cables of various lengths and wireless/ wired remote controls.

“When the user interface is intuitive and easy to learn, setting the welding parameters is smooth and effortless. The automatic parameter setting, by simply adjusting wire feed speed, is a helpful feature. With X5 FastMig, the basics of the user interface are easily grasped within ten minutes. This usability results in an increase in valuable arc-on time, especially in companies that employ outsourced staff, or in companies that have several welders sharing their welding fleet,” added Kemppi. The user interface is available with a digital panel or with a traditional two-knob system.
