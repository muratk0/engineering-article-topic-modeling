# Welding and cladding

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

WELDING AND CLADDING specialist, Polysoude, has organised various Open Days in Germany, France, Italy and India, showcasing its technical welding and cladding solutions. Welding technicians, decisionmakers and project managers from all over the world have attended presentations and live demonstrations.

A key event in November 2015 was its participation at the Stainless Steel World conference and exhibition in Maastricht in the Netherlands. Polysoude, invited by KCl, took part in the event as an exhibitor and also provided a technical presentation, given by Kees Meurs, sales manager for The Netherlands. This was a key opportunity for the company to present its process designed particularly for tubing with corrosion resistant alloys - TIGer technology.

"The principles and numerous technical and economic advantages of the TIGer welding solution were highlighted during the conference," Polysoude told ISMR. "From a technical standpoint, the quality of the deposit, low dilution rate and flawless control of the cladding thickness are all crucial points for any companies aiming for zero defect welds. When calculating the profitability of a project, productivity gains can be made due to the high deposit rate, absence of need for post-cladding machining and infinite repeatability of operations as a result of automation - all acutely advantageous economic aspects."

In 2015, Polysoude also witnessed higher demand for full scale tests directly on-site. With the development of its "Services" department, it decided to provide support to its clients, from the initial stage of machine installation, by offering training from equipment use and maintenance to equipment rental (either for trial before a purchase decision or during a production peak).
