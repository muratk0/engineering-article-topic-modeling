# Augmented Reality in welding

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

COMBINING AUGMENTED REALITY with the real welding process – this was the idea that Alexander Atzberger used to convince the expert jury from the German Welding Society (DVS) and EWM AG. The EWM "Physics of Welding Award" was given to a project by the young scientist from Munich at the opening ceremony of SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN, a leading trade show for welding and cutting. EWM promoted the idea as Germany's largest welding technology manufacturer, offering a prize with the award of €30,000.

In collaboration with the DVS, EWM called for entries to the contest for the Innovative Approaches Award in welding technology for the fifth time. This year, Alexander Atzberger received support for his pioneering project. The 27-year-old junior researcher at the Bundeswehr University Munich plans to make knowledge transfer in welding easier through his research project. The approach is to exchange movements and process parameters in welding through an Augmented Reality environment between two users anywhere in the world and in real-time. The technology could then be used in many cases in practice, which include, besides occupational and advanced training, the analysis and optimisation of the welding process with customers.

Prof Dr.-Ing. Michael Rethmeier of the Federal Institute for Material Research and Testing (BAM) recognised the future-oriented approach of the young scientist: "Alexander Atzberger's idea combines welding technology with state-of-the-art technologies and opens up entirely new possibilities to welders around the world," he said.
