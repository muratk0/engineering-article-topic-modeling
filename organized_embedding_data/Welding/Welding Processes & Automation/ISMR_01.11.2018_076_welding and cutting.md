# Welding and cutting

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

ESAB WELDING & CUTTING PRODUCTS showcased its complete portfolio of solutions for manual and automated welding and cutting products at EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany, this October.

ESAB's Digital Solutions platform provides several capabilities (ESAB WeldCloud, ESAB CutCloud and more to come) that connect digital ecosystems for welding and mechanised cutting, respectively. At EuroBLECH, ESAB presented new architecture, powered by Microsoft Azure IoT and utilising the ThingWorx®Industrial Innovation Platform from PTC, that allows for on-premise, hybrid and cloud-based architectures.

The welding specialist also launched its new modularised system for SAW, GMAW and gouging featuring the Versotrac EWT 1000 tractor, Versoarc EWH 1000 welding head and EAC 10 controller. The new Railtrac™mB42V is a universal rail-mounted tractor for welding and cutting that operates in flat, horizontal or vertical positions. Powered by 42V from the welding system or an optional 18V battery, the Railtrac B42V features the new W8000 remote weaving unit for making weave welds of up to 80mm wide and for weld path tracking.

ESAB also showed its new Miggytrac™mB5001, a batteryoperated compact, programmable tractor for MIG/MAG welding using a standard torch, along with two new enhanced torches for manual MIG/MAG welding. ESAB also demonstrated mechanised plasma cutting and bevelling with its iSeries plasma cutters, Combirex cutting table and DMX Automated Plasma Beveller, a next-generation bevelling system. For mechanised cutting, ESAB introduced its Columbus®III version1.6 CAD/CAM software.

"For heavy duty protection from welding fume and particulates when welding, gouging and grinding, combine an ESAB welding helmet with the new ESAB PAPR (Powered Air Purifying Respirator) system. The ESAB PAPR features the latest control technology, allowing the operator to adjust air flow between 170 and 220 L/min to suit the environment and application," it told ISMR.

Arcair, an ESAB brand, also introduced the AirPro X4000 torch and cable for manual carbon arc gouging. Where previous torch models required 27lbs. of grip pressure to open the torch, the AirPro X4000 uses the compressed air already flowing through the torch.

ATHADER RECENTLY INSTALLED its first coil processing line with a Bradbury e-Drive leveller in Spain. The CTL-1600 x 3 (63" x 0.120") cut-to-length and levelling line includes advanced roller levelling technology, producing "laser" quality flat sheets.

"Our e-Drive technology was developed and has been in the market for the last ten years with customers, mainly in the United States and Mexico, with more than 70 machines installed. Acceptance tests for the line included laser cutting of sheets in different directions to verify the quality of flatness. The results certificated the reduction and homogenisation of internal stresses," explained Bradbury.

Bradbury's patented e-Drive leveller system creates a torque differential between entry and exit rolls. The drive system is designed to reach maximum tension differential within the leveller but will monitor and automatically control any slippage. Both features working together are designed to ensure "laser flat" material.

The e-Drive leveller features hydraulic adjustment and double drive of the levelling rolls. The line also includes automatic coil loading with flexible storage station; a rotary shear of up to 80mpm (260 FPM); a double station combined stacker; sheet inspection (without stopping the line); an automatic packaging line; inspection cameras and monitoring data capture with the "Matrix" system developed by Bradbury software technicians.
