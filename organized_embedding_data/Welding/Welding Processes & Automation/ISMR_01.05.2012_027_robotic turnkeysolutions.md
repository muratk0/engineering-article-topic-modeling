# Robotic turnkeysolutions

[TARİH: 01.05.2012 ISMR]

CLOOS INTRODUCED numerous product innovations at the AUTOMATICA 2012 exhibition. Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH presented new welding robots in the QIROX® series and recently developed, synergy-controlled high-end power sources.

A special highlight of the trade fair was the QIROX automation concept with a new CLOOS robot generation. The new Type 350 welding robots in the medium capacity class were showcased in two versions: the classic version, QIROX Classic (QRC), as well as the hollow shaft version QRH where the cable assembly is integrated in the robot body and with an integrated wire drive unit.

The robot types featuring a modular design are suitable for loads of up to 15kg. An optional 7th axis integrated in the robot base is able to provide an extension of the working radius by 550mm (to a total working envelope of 1100mm) which enables a greater degree of flexibility in workpiece processing.

## Multi-process welding

THE FLEXTEC 650 multiprocess welder from Lincoln Electric delivers up to 650 amps of welding power. Its FLEXible TEChnology allows welders to use a variety of processes – stick, Touch Start TIG (DC), MIG, flux-cored CV submerged arc and arc gouging – on numerous applications, including heavy equipment and transportation, construction and welding education.

The inverter-based Flextec 650 is rated at 650 amps at 100 per cent duty cycle with a robust amperage range of 10-815 amps, weighing in at 165 pounds in a case that measures 21.83 inches high by 16.3 wide x 29.33 deep. Lincoln Electric Ready-Pak and One-Pak packages configured with Lincoln Electric wire feeders and accessories are structured for easy ordering. Job site-ready rack packages are also set for future release.

Flextec 650’s features include flexible multi-process capability for handling a wide range of welding applications, low power consumption, selectable hot start for extra starting amperage, faster arc response with variable arc control and remote control operated by a foot pedal or hand control up to 100 feet away.
