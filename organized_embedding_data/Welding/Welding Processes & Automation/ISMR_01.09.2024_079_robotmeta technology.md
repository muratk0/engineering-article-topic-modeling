# RobotMeta Technology

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.robot-meta.com

Founded in 2017, RobotMeta offers mature products and solutions for robotic MIG/ MAG and laser welding.

With the original impetus to make industrial robots easy to use, it aims to bring robotics to metal fabricators and SMEs worldwide.

"We develop and supply easy-to-use welding robots, and ready-to-weld robotic MIG/MAG and laser welding packages, the company told ISMR.

"With CE certification, our welding robots are now installed in over 30 countries. Our products are easy to learn and easy to use with no sacrifice in functionality, efficiency and reliability. Our team has a great deal of experience in welding and robotic welding projects," it added.

At EuroBLECH, it will highlight its CE-certified, 'easy-to-use' welding robot and 'ready-to-weld' robotic MIG/MAG and laser welding packages. This also includes hollow-wrist industrial robots, MIG/MAG or laser source/torch as well as torch cleaning stations, positioners or rails as external axes and relevant safety equipment.
