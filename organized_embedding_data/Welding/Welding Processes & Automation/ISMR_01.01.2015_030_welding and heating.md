# Welding and heating

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

DA JIE ELECTRICITY MACHINERY INDUSTRIAL CO. LTD was founded in 1973 in Taichung city, in the centre of Taiwan, with 40 employees and strong R&D and sales teams. The company has sold its welding and heating products to over 100 countries. Its focus, it told ISMR, is on rapid delivery and high quality for its customers.

Da Jie is one of the professional spot welding machine suppliers in Taiwan. Its other welding products include a multi-spot welder, seam welding machine and a condenser welding projection welder. It also manufactures microcomputer precision control resistance welding machines, air hydraulic flash butt welding machines, inverter spot welding machines and seam welding machines.

“Other products in our portfolio include column-type, multi-spot, mesh welding machines and hydraulic multi-spot, grid board welding machines. Electrical heating machines are also manufactured. We also supply equipment for stainless steel water tanks and electric fan guards,” the company explained. “Good tools are prerequisite to the successful execution of a job."
