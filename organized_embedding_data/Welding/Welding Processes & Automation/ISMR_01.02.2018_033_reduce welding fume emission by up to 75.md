# Reduce welding fume emission by up to 75%

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

"ACCORDING TO SCIENTIFIC studies, digitally modified EWM coldArc, forceArc and forceArc puls welding processes significantly reduce welding fume emissions and substantially minimise hazards for welders and operators. In light of the new limit on alveolar dust that will come into effect by the end of 2018 and calls for a reduction from 3mg/m³ to 1.25mg/m³, EWM technologies make it easier for customers to comply with the new limit," the German welding specialist told ISMR.

"Welding fumes feature a high percentage of alveolar dust. To meet the required limits and protect the welder from alveolar dust, welding dust must be substantially reduced. With our innovative welding processes, up to 75% less welding dust is produced (compared to conventional welding processes)," added EWM.

Scientists from RWTH Aachen University carried out extensive tests on welding fume emissions from short arcs, spray arcs and pulsed arcs and compared them with results from corresponding coldArc, forceArc and forceArc puls welding processes from EWM – at the same deposition rate. The results reportedly showed that the use of digitally modified, innovative processes can significantly decrease emissions and considerably reduce the risk for welders and users.

"The formation mechanisms of welding fumes differ significantly between short and spray arcs, as tests on unalloyed and low-alloy steels showed. With coldArc, the short arc is dissolved with very low output, which reduces welding fume emission by up to 75%. The forceArc welding process has a shorter arc length than conventional spray arcs and, therefore, higher arc force. The arc penetrates the parent metal deeper, raising the amount of fume deposition on the material surface – welding fume emissions are reduced by 40% because they never enter the air," explained EWM.

"Tests on high-alloy chrome-nickel steels with the forceArc puls pulse arc welding process also show highly reduced emissions due to high process stability. If the rate of emissions at low wire feed speeds is still comparable with conventional pulse arc welding processes, the emissions rate of the forceArc puls process at wire feed speeds of 13m/min. is reduced by a factor of 4.5 compared to pulsed arcs."
