# Simufact acquired by MSC Software

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

MSC SOFTWARE announced the acquisition of Simufact Engineering this February. Simufact is a specialist in the simulation of metal forming and joining processes. Based on nonlinear simulation software technology, Simufact.forming and Simufact.welding are metal forming and welding process simulation tools used by the aerospace, automotive and machinery industries, their parts and materials suppliers, as well as major OEMs.

"It is tremendously exciting to make the leap to a global company," said Michael Wohlmuth, Co-founder and CEO of Simufact. "Our team eagerly anticipates replicating our success with new companies and in more countries." See www.simufact.de ■
