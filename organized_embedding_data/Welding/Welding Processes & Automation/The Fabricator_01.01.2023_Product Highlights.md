Deburring machine settings adjusted automatically with software

ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH has introduced the EdgeBreaker 6000 deburring machine, designed to meet the requirements of laser job shops. Its vacuum conveyor belt and rotary brushes enable extremely uniform edge rounding, the company reports. Other processing modules include a grinding belt for deburring and a finishing unit for surface finishing. ARKU Wizard software can be added to the machine to adjust processing settings automatically during deburring. The operator enters a few parameters—burr size, material type and thickness, desired edge rounding after deburring—and further information comes from the automatic wear measurement of the rotary brushes. The software then calculates the appropriate tools and the correct machine settings.

Mat prevents welder fatigue, withstands splatter and sparks

The Weld-Diamond mat, an antifatigue mat for welders and fabricators, is available from First Mats. It features a flame-retardant diamond tread surface to withstand welding bays and other demanding industrial environments. It is slip-resistant, and its beveled edges help prevent trips and falls.

The mat has a foam center, but it is fusion-bonded to a vinyl top surface that’s been fire tested to BS EN 13501-1 Class Cfl - s1. This means the mat will withstand welding splatter and spills of other hot materials. It is available in various standard sizes starting at 60 by 90 cm and in custom sizes to 18.3 m long.

Compact system combines robot, panel bender

Salvagnini has introduced P-Robot, which combines a panel bender with a robot to produce kits, batches, and single parts autonomously. It offers a compact and modular layout, as well as simplified programming, and allows for a certain level of customization for specific production requirements.

The P1 electric panel bender has a maximum consumption of 3 kW and an 8-m

60-ton electric-drive press brake bends up to 25 mm/sec.

LVD has introduced the Dyna-Press 60/20 high-speed, electric-drive press brake for air bending, coining, hemming, and bottoming.

The 60-ton machine provides 2 m of working length and a 5-axis backgauge, offering bending speeds up to 25 mm/sec. The Pro model is equipped with the company’s Easy-Form laser system for greater control of the bending process. It also offers an optional custom-made crowning system.

The press brake consumes low electricity and delivers fast cycle times to bend more parts per minute more efficiently at a lower cost per part, the company states. A streamlined design uses two heavy-duty ballscrews to drive the force transmission from the servomotors to the pressing force of the ram.

Press features all-electric actuation system

Beckwood Press Co. has launched the patent-pending Linear Servo Press (LSP), which uses an all-electric actuation system.

Available in tonnages from 125 to 500, the press offers fully programmable speeds, pressures, and positions. Because it is fully electric, it has no hydraulic power units or centralized lubrication systems and up to 70% fewer components than servomechanical and hydraulic presses.

Brush hits both sides of the weld root

Osborn has introduced the Honey Badger double stringer bead brush for the pipeline, welding, and metal fabricating markets. The brush is built with two sets of wire knots on a unique face plate and nut, says the company. Because of how it’s constructed, the brush can hit both sides of the root, or hot pass, while cleaning out any slag or debris of a weld.
