# WELDING FLUX PASTE

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

WELDABILITY-SIF, the UK-based supplier of MIG, TIG, MMA, spot and oxy/fuel welding and plasma cutting machines, torches, accessories, consumables and personal protective equipment, has launched Sifbronze ECO paste flux. This boric-acid free solution can be applied directly to the weld joint or as a ‘deep dip’ to the filler rod to aid cleaning and consumable flow when oxy/acetylene brazing and for other welding applications on mild steel, copper alloys and cast iron.

The patented paste flux is ecologically clean (it does not contain the traditional noxious ingredients normally associated with brazing fluxes and meets the European Union REACH regulations). It can be used in a wide range of applications or by itself, where three fluxes would have been used in the past.

This pre-mixed flux paste, which has a melting point that starts from 420°C, can be applied directly to the weld joint and used as a deep dip to fully coat the filler rod. It can be used with multi-purpose Sifbronze No.1 and Sifbronze No. 101 brazing rods on mild steel and copper alloys. It can also be used on copper welding applications with SifsilCopper No 7 brazing rods for full fusion welding of de-oxidised copper sheet as well as in cast iron welding applications with Sif Super Silicon No. 9 brazing rods.

## AUTOMATIC COIL LOADERS

RED BUD INDUSTRIES (USA) has introduced a fully automated coil pre-stage and load system for its heavy gauge coil processing lines. This system automatically advances pre-staged coils and loads them onto a dual stub decoiler.

The system comprises an in-line coil pre-staging system, integrated coil car and dual stub arbor decoiler. These components operate in concert to eliminate the need for operator attention or intervention when loading coils.

At the push of a button, the coil pre-stage and load system’s integrated coil car retrieves the coil to be processed. The system will then measure the outside and inside diameters of the coil. After measuring, the coil car will position and raise the coil as required automatically for loading onto the line’s decoiler. The dual-mandrel decoiler will then spear the coil and centre it automatically on the line.

The system can also incorporate a coil reject station. This supplementary coil staging area allows the process order of the coils to be changed when required. It also allows for partially processed coils to be removed and stored off-line, eliminating the need for an overhead crane to remove a coil before the next coil can be processed. This allows for quick change-over to the next coil to be processed.

## NEW PLASMA CUTTER

GYS LTD, the French welding equipment and battery charger manufacturer, has launched a new lightweight single phase 230V plasma cutter 21KF with a built-in 60/70dB air compressor and dehumidifier. These features make it suitable for use in workshops and on-site in agricultural, construction, quarrying and engineering applications, where no compressed air supply may be available.

Utilising inverter technology, it is designed to cut at up to 20A using a 13Amp fuse plug, producing low intensity cuts on 6mm thick steel, stainless steel and cast iron sheets as well as on 4mm aluminium and copper sheets (including painted surfaces) without distortion.

“The GYS ‘Pilot Arc’ system enables the arc to start without any contact with the sheet being cut, making it very easy to use. Starting without any HF avoids any electromagnetic interference (phones, computers radios, etc.),” GYS told ISMR.

Other features include a 4m air-cooled torch with a safety trigger to avoid any accidental starting of the unit, reinforced protection for use with a generator, over voltage tested up to 400V and IP23 protection against rain and dust impregnation.

## WELDING PRODUCTIVITY

ESAB IS INTRODUCING new two-axis and three-axis positioners for manipulating workpieces during welding.

“Not only do the positioners improve ergonomics for the welding operator, but they also help to maximise productivity and quality by maintaining a good welding position throughout the weld run. Applications for the positioners range from pipe production and vessel manufacture to cladding of end dishes and fabrication of fuel tanks,” ESAB explained.

Two series of two-axis positioners are available, giving operators control over tilt and rotation. The RT250 and RT500 have load capacities of 250kg and 500kg, respectively, while the seven models in the larger series, designated RT1 to RT35 units, have load capacities of 1-35 tonnes. All models operate from a multi-voltage three-phase supply. 24V DC remote control units give operators full control over all the positioner functions.

ESAB’s new TAP 1T and TAP 3T three-axis positioners enable workpieces to be rotated, tilted and raised. Load capacities are 1000kg and 3000kg, respectively. Rotation is controlled via an inverter-driven AC motor, whereas height and tilt are controlled hydraulically.
