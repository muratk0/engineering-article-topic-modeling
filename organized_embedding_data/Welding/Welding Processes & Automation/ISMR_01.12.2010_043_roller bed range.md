# ROLLER BED RANGE

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

ESAB has launched a comprehensive range of roller beds to help improve the productivity and quality of round fabrications such as wind turbine towers, pipelines and vessels.

“All of these new welding automation products are robust and high quality, having been designed to withstand the harsh conditions often encountered in fabrication shops. Customers have an extensive choice, with sizes and features to suit a broad array of applications and the roller beds can be used in conjunction with our A2-A6 process controller PEK system for submerged arc welding, GMAW welding and arc gouging.

“Depending upon the model selected, the maximum load capacity ranges from 2.5 to 60 tons per section and the roller beds can handle component diameters from approximately 150mm to 8.4m,” ESAB told ISMR. E-mail: info@esab.co.uk
