# Metal forming and welding software

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

SIMUFACT ENGINEERING launched new versions of its simulation software, Simufact.forming and Simufact.welding, at the EuroBLECH exhibition in October 2012. Providing more functionality for a broader range of applications, with simplified usability, they are supported by standard interfaces in existing CAD/CAE environments.

Simufact’s simulation solutions cover all essential production processes for the processing of blank sheet metal – from rolling to sheet metal forming, welding and mechanical joining. New functionality – the coupling of Simufact.welding with Simufact.forming – smoothly links process and welding simulation.

“Processes taking place before or after welding can be efficiently integrated into the numerical approach. Step by step, this intelligent connection of Simufact solutions turns the simulation of entire process chains into virtual reality,” the manufacturer explained.

“The integration of material data into the simulation of forming and joining processes gives the user a multitude of new, experimental material data and provides access to analytical material data on combination with JMatPro to ensure accurate simulation results.” For more information, see www.simufact.com ■
