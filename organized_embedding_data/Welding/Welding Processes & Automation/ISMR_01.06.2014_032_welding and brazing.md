# Welding and brazing

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

GYS, a major European manufacturer of bodyshop welding equipment, has launched a new multi-torch MIG welding machine with brazing capability. The M3GYS Auto is a single-phase welder featuring a 3-torch system, designed to be set up simultaneously for brazing, steel and aluminium via a spool gun.

It features GYS synergic control offering optimal wire speed selection and will accommodate 1kg, 5kg and 15kg wire reels. The GYS car body MIG range also includes single torch machines suitable for brazing as well as Pulse MIG machines. See www.gys.fr ■
