Maximize shop flexibility with Yaskawa’s new pre-engineered, compact ArcWorld

®

solutions that deliver powerful arc welding performance for small- to medium-size parts in medium volume fabrication settings. Ideal for replacing or supplementing manual welding, each space-saving workcell enables fast and easy installation or relocation, as well as quick changeover.

ArcWorld HS

Extremely compact solution that features a hinged-station (HS) design and only requires 1.4 square meters (15 sq ft) of floorspace. Featuring a Yaskawa AR900 arc welding robot and a single fixture table, this workcell processes parts up to 550 × 500 × 1,050 mm in size.

ArcWorld RS

Slightly larger model that only needs 2.3 square meters (25 sq ft) of space. The rotating station (RS) features two fixture tables and a high-performance AR900 robot to process parts up to 425 × 700 x 990 mm in size.

ArcWorld HC

Portable human-collaborative (HC) job shop features a direct teach, plug and play HC10DTP human-collaborative (HC) robot with Power and Force Limiting (PFL) technology, enabling safe collaboration in a fenceless environment, as well as an IP67 exterior. Its 1,200 mm x 2,400 mm fixture table with a 2,000 lb payload capacity facilitates processing of parts up to 500 × 2,000 × 800 (HxWxD) mm in size.

Featured workcells come complete with fume mitigation, arc protection and interior LED lighting. Power supply processes are accessed by Yaskawa’s easy-to-use digital Universal Weldcom Interface (UWI).
