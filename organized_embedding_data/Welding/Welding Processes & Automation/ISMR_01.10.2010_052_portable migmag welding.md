# PORTABLE MIG/MAG WELDING

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

ESAB has introduced a new addition to its Caddy family of welding machines - the Caddy Mig C200i. Suitable for a wide variety of assembly, maintenance and repair operations, the Caddy Mig C200i is compact and lightweight and features a built-in wire feeder that accepts 200mm spools. Ergonomically designed and rugged, the new machine can be used to weld steel, aluminium and stainless steel, and is suitable for Mig brazing. Both solid and gasless cored wires can be used.

A key advantage offered by this new inverter power source is ESAB's QSet automatic setting technology for strong welding performance with the minimum of effort from the user. Once the material type and thickness have been entered via the intuitive operator interface, the user performs a five-second test weld and the QSet system automatically optimises the short arc welding parameters. QSet then maintains a constant arc to ensure consistent quality and ease of use, whether the operator is working in a production environment, repair workshop or on-site.

The 12kg weight means that the Caddy Mig C200i is easy to carry and can be operated from either a single-phase power supply or a portable generator. The Caddy Mig C200i delivers 30 per cent more power despite still being equipped with a 16A fuse, says ESAB. At a 20 per cent duty cycle, the machine delivers 180A/23V; at a 100 per cent duty cycle, it achieves 100A/19V. The current setting range is 30-200A, the speed on the integral wire feed can be set from 1-12m/min, and wire diameters from 0.8-1.0mm can be used. E-mail: info@esab.co.uk
