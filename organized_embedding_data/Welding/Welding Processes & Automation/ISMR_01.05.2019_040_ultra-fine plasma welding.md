# Ultra-fine plasma welding

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

Above: The new Microplasma by EWM is for delicate plasma welding of workpieces with material thickness down to 0.1mm

WITH THE TITAN XQ 400 AC puls, Germany's largest manufacturer of welding machines, EWM, aims to revolutionise the MIG welding of aluminium. Its new acArc puls XQ alternating current welding process for manual and automated welding can even be applied to extremely thin sheets. The weld seam is, said the company, 'as pure as a diamond'. Alongside the new MIG-AC welding process "acArc puls XQ", all the established and optimised EWM DC welding processes (such as the forceArc puls XQ, rootArc XQ or coldArc XQ) are included in the machine at no additional cost.

EWM AG has introduced its Microplasma plasma welding machine for delicate welding when working with materials as thin as 0.1 millimetres. It features digital control technology, inverter technology and compact plasma torches designed for welding work 'to the finest detail'. Areas of application include medical and dental technology, aviation, pneumatic components and welding of various metals to thermocouples.

The Microplasma is offered in three power variants. The current for the plasma arc is adjustable in fine 0.1 ampere steps from 0.3 up to 20, 50 or 100 A, depending upon the machine.

"Plasma technology offers advantages over TIG welding in that the pilot arc is adjustable, affording 100% ignition reliability, and the plasma arc is extremely constricted, allowing the user to precision-concentrate the water-cooled plasma torch upon the desired point of the workpiece at a temperature of up to 22,000 °C. Various plasma nozzle sizes make it possible to direct the heat input to an exactly-defined area," commented EWM AG.

"The pilot arc is a special feature of plasma welding, as it ionises the part directed towards the workpiece, ensuring reliable ignition of the main arc. This is a key advantage, particularly in partially mechanised and automated production, in which reproducible results are required. The pilot arc current can be adjusted at four operating points during the welding process (before, during and after welding and during pauses in welding). The current for the pilot arc is adjustable between a range of 2 and 15 A."

For operation of the machine, EWM has integrated the latest digital control unit, Comfort 2.0, with a visual display of the plasma welding sequence parameters that allows users to flexibly set as many as 100 JOBs. All standard parameters are preconfigured, for plasma, TIG and pulse welding. Automated pulse functionality keeps the pulse frequency automatically adjusted in line with the welding current level.

The gas metering unit is another standard feature that makes working easier on a practical, day-to-day level. A control knob enables fine and highly-precise regulation of the gas quantity. This reduces gas consumption while enhancing weld quality and efficiency.

"Our manual and automated welding torches are compact, making them extremely handy to use. They are designed to enable precision work, guaranteeing optimal heat dissipation and featuring a sophisticated cooling functionality that increases the service life of consumables. This allows exact tack welding and welding of films, drive components, membranes, sieves and filters made of low- and high-alloy steel, for example, as well as bi-metal electrical and thermocouples," added the company.

In the new Microplasma, the compact power source is connected to the cooling unit without the need for tools. A cooling aggregate is optionally available for mechanised continuous use situations. A trolley allows users to wheel the Microplasma, along with all its accessories, over to the place of operation and offers space for the plasma gas and shielding gas cylinders. Microplasma has an array of ports for connecting accessories, such as remote controls. An external gateway allows users to digitally network the machine with the ewm Xnet 2.0 welding management system. The Microplasma uses a simple 230 V mains connection for its power supply.
