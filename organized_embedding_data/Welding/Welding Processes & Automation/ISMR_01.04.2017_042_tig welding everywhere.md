# TIG welding everywhere

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

EWM AG IS launching two newly developed products in its range of portable TIG welding machines: the Tetrix 230 and Tetrix 300 Comfort 2.0 puls, both available in DC and AC/DC versions. This compact machine series features the new EWM system housing, which can be connected to a cooling unit and transport cart without needing any tools.

"A number of concepts to improve user-friendliness have been successfully incorporated in the development of the new machine generation. The real highlight is the new Comfort 2.0 control, equipped with an additional display and illuminated symbols for improved presentation of the machine's functions. This enables welders to see crucial information at a glance, giving them full control over the welding process. Handling is also simplified by the intuitive single-hand operation concept featuring a large, central click wheel and a smart menu control with visual user guidance and logical up/down menu. In the AC versions, users also have full access to the AC parameters when welding in set-up mode, making it easier to determine the optimum setting for changing welding tasks," explained EWM.

The newly designed average value pulse function allows users to simply implement elements such as WPS requirements, even in pulsed mode. In the Tetrix 300 versions, another feature comprises the digital interface offering connectivity to ewm Xnet. This quality management software enables quality-related data to be easily recorded providing full quality control (even when used on-site at customer facilities).

With a standby power-saving function, EWM's inverter technology is designed to ensure efficient performance and conserve resources. The machine's high mains voltage tolerances ensure smooth operation with generators and enable welding with mains connection leads of up to 100m in length.

## Fume and dust extraction

NEDERMAN HAS LAUNCHED its new range of FX2 articulated extraction arms that are small, manoeuvrable and easy to use, for applications where extraction of small volumes of gases, dust and fume is required within a limited area. They are designed to capture pollutants as close to their source as possible.

"A high capture rate is achieved with minimum energy use, and any hazardous airborne substances are removed before they reach an operator's breathing zone or contaminate premises and goods. From an end-user perspective, the new Nederman FX2 is packed with benefits that include low noise levels and improved energy efficiency, stable positioning with easy manoeuvrability, an aesthetically pleasing userfriendly design and complete freedom when planning installations," said the manufacturer.

The low noise level and improved energy efficiency of the FX2 arms was achieved using computerised simulations during the design phase. The result is a highly improved air flow with lower pressure loss, meaning smaller fans can be used which consume less energy. Complementary products such as Nederman fans, ducting and control units can increase energy efficiency even further. The FX2 arms are also highly flexible and easy to position, extend and retract. The two outermost arm joints can rotate 360°, making it possible to position the hood exactly where required. Once correctly positioned, the hood can be locked into place using two friction devices in the arm joints.

For the FX2 arms, Nederman has three different models that cater for a wide spectrum of needs. With a long upper arm section, the FX2 PLUS swivel and accessories can be placed high up or above a false ceiling. The Nederman FX2 can be mounted on ceilings, walls and floors or even fixed to worktops. Cut2fit aluminium profile extensions can be cut to any length and assembled with the classic bracket for mounting arms to the ceiling or wall. See www.nederman.co.uk
