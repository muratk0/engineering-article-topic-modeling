# Fronius launches new TIG series

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

The MagicWave 230i can communicate and be networked with other devices via Bluetooth, WLAN and NFC technology

AUSTRIAN TECHNOLOGY SPECIALIST, Fronius, has expanded its portfolio of manual power sources with three new devices for Tungsten Inert Gas (TIG) welding.

"The MagicWave 230i, 190 and TransTig 230i are practical, powerful and quiet. Thanks to their rapid signal processors, they always achieve the exact current curve that allows for maximum arc stability with the lowest possible noise emissions. With intuitive menu navigation, users can access the correct parameters in a few steps and can then concentrate fully on achieving a clean weld seam," the manufacturer told ISMR.

Whether in pipeline or container construction, for processing stainless steel or vehicle repair and maintenance, the new Fronius TIG welding systems have a range of applications. The most powerful source is the MagicWave 230i for AC welding. It can communicate with other devices via Bluetooth, WLAN and NFC technology, and can be networked with them. The MagicWave 190 is suitable for welding materials such as steel, stainless steel or aluminium. The AC power source features gas cooling.

An integral part of the new series is the TransTig 230i for DC welding. Weighing 15.9kg, it is especially light. This manual power source is suitable for all welding applications apart from aluminium. As with the MagicWave 230i, the power source can also be supplied with an optional water cooling system. Using Bluetooth, WLAN and NFC, the TransTig 230i can also connect wirelessly to devices such as an intelligent welding helmet, remote control or even a smartphone.

"The multilingual operating concept of the manual power source with a 4.3-inch full-colour display allows for intuitive and extremely simple operation. Using the favourites button, welders can easily retrieve and set parameters that they often use. The modular design is a particular advantage; users can expand these devices as desired with different function packages. USB ports allow software updates to be installed and welding data to be documented," added Fronius.

Every device is available as a multi-voltage version. Using the Fronius Power Plug - a lockable plug connector on the rear of the power source - the mains cable or plug can be changed quickly and easily, depending upon where the device is being used. This means it can be used anywhere in the world, even with different mains voltages. Fronius has also redesigned the welding torch: an ergonomic grip combined with a torch-changing system increases comfort and convenience, and allows for more precise handling. See www.fronius.com
