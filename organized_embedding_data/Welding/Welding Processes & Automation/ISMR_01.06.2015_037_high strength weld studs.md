# High strength weld studs

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

Bolzenschweißtechnik GmbH told ISMR.

As previously used welding studs of unalloyed steel with property class 4.8 offer comparatively low load capacity, the company has now introduced a new high strength weld stud generation with property class 8.8. The Black Series is manufactured in cold processing and is distinguished by its completely black colour marking achieved with an environmentally friendly black finishing. This eliminates any danger of confusion with similar weld studs with lower property classes. All process steps, including storage, transport and processing, can be secured verifiably by visual inspection.

These features of the new weld stud generation, for applications such as steel and metal construction, vehicle, container, equipment and system engineering, have been registered as utility patent under registration number 20 2015 100 234.3.
