# Remote laser welding

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH, a manufacturer of system solutions for remote laser welding, presented new functionality for industrial 'on-the-fly' welding processes at the Laser World of Photonics 2023 exhibition in Munich this June.

OTF MotionSync enables automated manufacturing processes with high throughput, as required e.g. for electromobility. The special feature provided is the new software, designed to enable welding processes to be carried out precisely, even though the scan system is moved at the same time or the components themselves are transported under the scanner on a conveyor belt. This ensures that manufacturing processes with extreme accuracy requirements, such as the welding of batteries or bipolar plates for fuel cells, can be controlled efficiently and be traceable in the future.

"Our core competence for many years has been precise laser welding with scan systems that are permanently mounted or guided by industrial robots. We are a pioneer in 'mobile' laser welding, called on-the-fly (OTF). By using the new OTF MotionSync software, this knowledge is now transferred to the precise processing of moving components. The software determines the exact position of the scanner and component by reading the high-frequency position data from up to two channels. The data are processed during the laser process and compared to the welding geometry. Therefore, the software can always guarantee precise welded joints," commented Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH.

The new software solution makes it possible to implement rapid component sequences and high throughput, for example in battery or bipolar plate production. Conveyor systems with up to two linear axes can be used for the welding process.

OTF MotionSync, which can be combined with all 2D and 3D scan systems offered by Blackbird's sister company SCANLAB, were showcased at Munich at the end of June. Beta versions for industrial-scale test installations are also available.
