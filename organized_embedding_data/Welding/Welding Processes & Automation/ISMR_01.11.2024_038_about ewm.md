# About EWM

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

For over 65 years, EWM has developed arc welding technology made in Germany. The family company was founded in Mündersbach, in Germany's Westerwald region, in 1957. Today, it has over 400 sales and service support outlets across the globe, making it one of the world's leading producers of welding technology. In addition to smart machines, it develops and produces all necessary components (such as welding torches for manual and automated applications).

Many EWM welding processes are patented and designed to save energy, materials and time. Sustainability is a key factor. EWM also offers comprehensive, targeted service and consultancy.

## Automated welding for higher productivity

"We are very satisfied with the welding technology and the optimised process," emphasised Florian Pfeifer. “In its role as a full-service provider, EWM also offers us comprehensive, competent advice and support in project implementation. We really appreciate that."

It is not just implementation and quality of the welding machines that is important to Alwitra, but solution-oriented advice and individual service.

“With EWM, we exchange ideas on an equal footing and benefit from short, uncomplicated communication channels. This makes it easy to work with EWM," concluded Florian Pfeifer.

As Alwitra continues to adapt its product range to market requirements, there will be a growing focus on process automation in the future. With EWM React, the flat roof system specialist expects to take its productivity to new levels.
