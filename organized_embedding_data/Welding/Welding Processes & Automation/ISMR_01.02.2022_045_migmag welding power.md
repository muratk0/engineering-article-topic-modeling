# MIG/MAG welding power

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

CLOOS is presenting a new version of its MIG/ MAG welding power source, QINEO StarT, with integrated wire drive for manual welding. The operating module, a powerful wire drive unit and the support for a 15kg wire coil are integrated in a compact housing together with the power unit.

"The QINEO StarT offers easy entry into the world of modern welding technology. Users can weld any workpiece economically. The heart of the QINEO StarT is an inverter power unit. Outstanding arc control guarantees excellent welding results. The maximum quality standard makes the QINEO StarT a long-lasting and robust welding machine," CLOOS told ISMR.

The configuration possibilities of the QINEO StarT are flexible and versatile, as are the welding applications. The consistently modular product concept means that, from the capacity class to the wire tip, each QINEO StarT is customised. Users can adapt the Eco, Master and Premium versions to make the QINEO StarT their individual welding system.

"The compact version of the QINEO StarT offers easy, quick and intuitive operation. Manual welders benefit from the comfortable operating concept that they can adapt to their individual requirements," added CLOOS.

In addition to the standard processes, the QINEO StarT offers further CLOOS welding processes with pre-set parameters, depending upon the variant. Manual welders can therefore get started right away without a timeconsuming search for parameters. With the QINEO StarT 406, operators can also use the 'Fine Weld' energy-reduced, current-controlled MIG/MAG short arc process. Due to minimised spatter formation, Fine Weld is particularly suitable for thin, coated plates and fine visible weld seams.

Since 1919, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH has been one of the leading companies in welding technology with more than 800 employees all over the world. From welding technology, robot mechanics and controller to positioners, software and sensors – CLOOS supplies everything from a single source.■
