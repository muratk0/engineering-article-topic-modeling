# Orbital tube welding

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

AT THE TUBE 2018 trade show in Düsseldorf, Germany, Polysoude will present its orbital tube welding solutions and TIG bi-cathode technology (TIGer), which is designed to provide significant productivity gains. Find the company in Hall 6 on stand G03.

"Regulations and standards that govern the tube sector are imposing stricter quality on manufacturers. Based on this observation, for several years we have been developing orbital welding solutions using the TIG process. This is a stable and reliable process, with or without filler wire, and can be used on all sorts of materials. Combined with a wide variety of Polysoude welding heads and programmable power sources, it covers all tube sectors," explained Polysoude.

As part of its orbital welding range, Polysoude recently developed the MW34 for highproductivity applications without wire filler. It is fitted with titanium tube clamp inserts (TCI™), which adapt to tube dimension tolerance variations. With an operating range from 6 to 34mm, the tool is suitable for the aeronautics, bio-chemistry and micro-electronics sectors, as well as pharmaceutical, food and conventional chemistry sectors.

"For clean room welding applications, we can complete the installation with its P4 power source and its remote touchscreen option. The operator can thus select the welding procedure, programme and monitor the welds without leaving the cleanroom, thus minimising the risk of contamination," said Polysoude.

"We have also added the TS 8/75-2 welding head to our range. Benefits include its high duty cycle and its pneumatic clamping system controlled via its ergonomic handle. Its integrated rotating wire feeder is also a significant advantage as the wire does not become twisted and no longer has to endure variations in speed; this results in productivity and quality gains. Suitable for all drilling pitches, thanks to its adjustable stand-off legs and with a wide range of tooling available, the TS 8/75-2 is a suitable welding head for all types of tubular heat exchangers consisting of tubes with an inner diameter of 10 to 60mm."

Polysoude's TIGer bi-cathode technology is designed especially for cladding to allow lower ferrite levels (<5%) and deposition rates of above 6kg/h with a speed of 1000mm/min, while preserving the quality of conventional TIG. Polysoude can now propose its TIGer process for SPX heads for continuous high-yield weld overlay.
