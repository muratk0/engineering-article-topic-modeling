# Additive manufacturing study

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

THE FRAUNHOFER ILT, together with the Fraunhofer IPT and KEX, is planning a second round for a Consortium Study on Additive Manufacturing (AM). This industry-focused project is scheduled to start on 24 March 2015 and end on 9 December 2015.

The main objective of this study is to obtain a detailed overview on technological and market related trends in AM. The results will be achieved by analysis of future challenges and R&D trends for AM, market structures and future product requirements as well as crossindustry synergies. For further information, or to participate, see www.ilt.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ilt/de/documents/zentren-und-cluster/Consortium-Study-on-Additive-Manufacturing.pdf ■
