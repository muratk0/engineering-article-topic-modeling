Dual-weld-head friction stir welding machine delivers tight weld flatness tolerance

Precision Technologies Group offers the Powerstir dual-weld-head friction stir welding machine, designed to help ensure a tight weld flatness tolerance in the volume production of automotive battery tray floor assemblies from extruded aluminum panels.

That tight tolerance is essential to ensure that each electric vehicle battery cell sits level within its housing. The unit’s matched dual-force control systems and balanced upper and lower head welding parameters contribute to stable friction stir welding by both the upper and lower weld heads. This produces matched weld seams with balanced heat input, minimizing postweld distortion.

Technology doubles cut speed in automated plasma cutting

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has launched SquareCut Technology to help improve the productivity of automated plasma cutting operations. This technology tilts the torch to compensate for the 1- to 3-degree edge angle inherent in high-precision plasma cutting.

The system offers High-Speed Mode, which cuts up to two times faster without increasing bevel angle; Quality Mode, which creates a cut surface with a 0- to 1-degree bevel; and Improved Enhanced Hole Technology to cut small holes with improved cylindricity.

The technology works with the company’s Columbus CAD/CAM software, Vision T5 CNC, the DMX 5-axis beveler, and iSeries high-precision power source. It works at cutting outputs from 100 to 400 amps when cutting mild steel plate from 0.25 to 1.5 in. thick.

Sheet metal software simplifies import of part data

Lantek has released the latest version of its sheet metal software, Lantek Global Release 2021. New features in the import of CAD models into Lantek Expert allow drag and drop and the detection of duplicate parts, making the import of part data simpler and quicker. The software can import 3D models as assemblies and sets of parts from nearly every CAD system on the market.

With the assembly to nesting importer, it can unfold all the sheet metal parts in the model, taking into account the bending tool to be used, to arrive at a 2D shape ready for nesting and cutting. Furthermore, new capabilities for managing draft parts make it possible to flag up parts that need further design approval before manufacture.

New Clean Cut technology can be applied to those contours where no lead-ins or microjoints should be placed to prevent potential imperfections in the final cut.

Software includes more condition monitoring functions

TwinCAT Analytics software from Beckhoff offers a complete workflow from acquisition of data to its storage and analysis to dashboards for continuous machine monitoring. Traditional condition monitoring algorithms are available in the software using simple configuration with no programming requirements. This addition optimizes both the standard workflow and spontaneous measurements, such as during machine commissioning or service, says the company.

The numerous algorithms support moment coefficients, discrete classification, and vibration assessment according to the ISO standard, in addition to functions such as magnitude spectrum, envelope spectrum, zoom FFT, power cepstrum, and multiple RMS calculations. All functions are available as standard PLC function blocks, which means that condition monitoring functions integrate directly and easily into the machine controller.
