## WISE WELDING

Welding entered the digital technology age in 1993, when Kemppi introduced its first ‘Pro’ MIG/MAG welding products. Since then, digital processing techniques have significantly increased welding productivity and weld quality.

Today, Kemppi’s new Wise welding software products can either be pre-loaded to new Kemppi equipment, including the new FastMig Pulse 350/450, or added later using DataGun, a compact automatic field program device, the size of a mobile phone, for delivering Kemppi’s software programs and updates.

The MinarcMig Adaptive 170

“WisePenetration is a tailored process for delivering constant welding power to the weld pool regardless of changes in the distance between the welding gun nozzle and the workpiece. This reduces the risk of lack of penetration and the need for welding parameter adjustments. Because of the reductions in weld defects, it saves time and rework costs, including filler material costs,” Kemppi told ISMR.

WiseRoot is a tailored cold arc process for root pass welding, designed for the closure of root joint areas. It is three times faster in root closure than TIG welding, Kemppi told ISMR.

The WiseFusion process creates and maintains the correct arc length parameters in manual (WiseFusion) and automated (WiseFusion-A) pulsed MIG/MAG welding applications. WiseFusion, once set, eliminates the need for regular parameter adjustments by ensuring consistent weld pool control in all positions.

WiseThin is a tailored cold arc process for manual (WiseThin) and automated (WiseThin-A) thin sheet welding and brazing.

## SPEED AND PRECISION

QINEO is the new modular product line from Cloos welding machines – operating concept, design, welding and ignition characteristics, wire drive unit and ease of maintenance have been considerably improved, Cloos told ISMR.

“From capacity class to additional equipment, each individual QINEO is specifically manufactured. This saves a great deal of time during maintenance and also simplifies upgrades and retrofits: so that in future you are able to weld as precisely as your job requires,” Cloos explained.

QIROX is the new product brand from Cloos for automated welding and cutting. Its modular design allows users to build scalable solutions that are optimized to their own production requirements.

Lorch unveiled a number of innovations at Schweisstec, including the SpeedPulse, a new process for MIG/MAG pulse welding for aluminium and stainless steel.

“With the S-series, you can weld 1mm plates with 1.2 wire and draw through seamlessly without interruption and without wire change up to the maximum current,” it told ISMR.

Up to 48% process acceleration for steel welding and more than 30% for stainless steel are the result, says Lorch. The speed benefit of the new S-Series can be converted to additional metres of welding seams by professional welders. There is therefore enormous potential for increased productivity, particularly for steel welding, for specialised welding companies and industrial enterprises.

Motoman’s new powerful 7-axis VS50 “Versatile Spot” spot welding robot is designed to optimize automotive applications using DC spot guns with compact servo actuators. Fast and agile, the VS50 robot can be placed in close proximity to work pieces and other robots to create flexible, high-density layouts, eliminating multiple stations and resulting in shorter production lines and smaller spot welding workcells for reduced cycle times.

Another robot manufacturer, Panasonic Factory Solutions Company of America (PFSA), has also showcased its TAWERS-Aluminum MIG, part of the continuous evolution of the TAWERS robot family. Suitable for high-performance push-pull aluminum feeding, the TAWERS-Aluminum MIG uses a three-motor system to ensure quick and accurate wire feed.

Panasonic also addressed the common challenge of locating joints for extrusion applications and for thin to thick welding applications. The syncro-weaving function and servo feeder provide real-time parameter adjustment from thick to thin materials - for penetration on the thick and no burn through on the thin. TAWERS-Aluminum MIG can also use high voltage touch sensing through aluminum oxide, which provides a fast response and prevents wire bending.
