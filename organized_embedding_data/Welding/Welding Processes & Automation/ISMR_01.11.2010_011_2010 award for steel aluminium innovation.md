# 2010 AWARD FOR STEEL ALUMINIUM INNOVATION

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

The jointly developed steel aluminium blank, the result of groundbreaking research by voestalpine Stahl GmbH and Fronius GmbH, was awarded the Innovation Prize by Autorevue on 20 October 2010 at a gala at Casino Baden.

Although the welding of steel and aluminium has been considered virtually impossible for decades due to the materials’ different material properties, a research team at voestalpine’s Linz site achieved the breakthrough. The key to this combination is an innovative form of joint edge with strong results for pull, pressure and deep drawing tests and excellent anti-corrosion properties.

From a technical point of view, the steel and aluminium combination is ready for series production. Actual implementation options are currently being discussed with automobile manufacturers. The advantage of the metallurgic combination using a CMT welding and soldering process is that the hybrid blanks can be reformed into construction parts after the soldering process – in the same way as with conventionally laser-welded steel blanks. The front carriage, cabin and roof are possible areas where the blanks could be used in vehicle production.

“Our work is based on the principles of specialisation, innovation and technology. The aluminium and steel bond reflects all of these principles in one piece of work,” commented Wolfgang Eder,

CEO of voestalpine AG, who celebrated the award with the two successful researchers Enno Arenholz and Alois Leitner.

The advantage of metallurgical joining with the MIG brazing-welding process is that hybrid blanks can be formed into components after being joined as are conventional laser-welded steel blanks.
