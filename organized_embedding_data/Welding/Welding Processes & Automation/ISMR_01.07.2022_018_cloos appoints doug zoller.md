# CLOOS appoints Doug Zoller

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Doug Zoller took over the management of CLOOS Robotic Welding Inc. in the USA and Mexico at the end of June 2022. He replaces Hartmut Boegel, who retired from the company on 30 June 2022 after more than 40 years of service.

“Hartmut Bögel has shaped the successful development of our subsidiaries in the USA and Mexico over the past 25 years," said Stephan Pittner, CEO of the CLOOS Group. "We would like to express our heartfelt thanks to him for his professional expertise and commitment."

CLOOS Robotic Welding Inc. was founded in 1985 as a subsidiary of the CLOOS Group. From locations in Schaumburg, Illinois, and Monterrey, Mexico, CLOOS Robotic Welding offers customer-specific, turnkey welding solutions for the U.S. and Mexican markets.

The welding engineer and MBA has extensive expertise in welding and automation technology as well as many years of leadership experience in technology, project management and sales. Most recently, he held various senior positions at welding equipment manufacturer, ESAB.

"With his experience, Doug Zoller will optimally support us in achieving our growth targets in the North American market," emphasised Alexander Veidt, CFO of the CLOOS Group. "We look forward to

Doug Zoller, GM, CLOOS Robotic Welding, Inc.

successful cooperation and wish him a good start and much success in his new position."

Since 1919, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH has been one of the leading companies in welding technology. It has over 900 employees worldwide and offers QINEO welding power sources and QIROX robots for automated welding systems. In addition to its headquarters in Haiger, CLOOS has nine production sites, 14 subsidiaries and 64 sales and service points around the world.■
