# COMPACT WELDING STATION

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

FOR MANUFACTURERS involved in small and medium batch laser welding, the new TRUMPF TruLaser Station 3003 offers a flexible, economic and ergonomic solution. The design of this machine allows the optimum beam source to be selected for the application. The TRUMPF TruPulse laser is typically used for welding temperature sensitive components.

TRUMPF disk, diode and fibre lasers up to an output of 1kW can be specified for applications such as welding lap joints in heat exchangers. And for tasks such as layer removal and stripping of semiconductors, the TRUMPF TruMicro microprocessing laser would be the ideal choice.

The TruLaser 3003 is economical to run and is characterised by its minimal auxiliary processing times thanks to its programmable focusing optics. An integrated scanner optic makes this possible; neither the workpiece nor the optic need to be moved for processing. Linear axes that expand the working range are available as an option.

Despite its compact dimensions of 860 x 2000 x 1310mm, the TruLaser Station 3003 has a large working range of 300 x 300 x 500mm. Its automatic swing doors provide fast and ergonomic loading of workpieces. See www.trumpf.com

## PERFORATED SHEET PERFORMANCE

SERVERS AND THE IT networks they control are at the heart of every company, so they require a high level of protection. Varistar server racks from Schroff GmbH, a leading electronics packaging systems developer and manufacturer, meet the special requirements of these high-tech devices. The cooling system creates the proper climate so that servers can operate continuously at a constant temperature. And for all-round hardware and software protection, these racks are also resistant to extreme mechanical and electromagnetic limit loads.

The perforated sheet that Solvaro developed for the Varistar series delivers stability by means of a honeycomb perforation and optimises ventilation with a special perforation for the rear walls. In addition, the perforation pattern provides an unobstructed view of the rack interior.

Schrofo Varistar server racks have to meet stringent requirements. Even the doors have to meet strict specifications: reliable heat removal, stability and low tolerances are just as necessary as an unobstructed view of the hardware and wiring. Solvaro blocks out the outer contours and the areas for handles and hinges with a special technology for perforated sheet – developed in-house – that delivers the lowest tolerances possible. Precision machining of the material enables the company to ship ready-to-install plates. This means that customers are able to eliminate a complete automated further-processing step.

Solvaro honeycomb perforation uses the mechanical advantages that natural honeycombs possess: a high degree of stability with low material utilisation. With an 80 per cent-free cross section, the perforated sheet guards server rack interiors against overheating and dirt particles, while delivering the heat exchange required. It also allows an unobstructed view so the hardware and wiring can be monitored for proper functioning even when the door is closed.

See www.solvaro.com

## MATERIAL STORAGE AND RETRIEVAL

LVD COMPANY N.V. has introduced a compact material storage and retrieval tower (CT-P) for Strippit turret punch presses, including Strippit V, VT, M and VX-Series models. The space-saving CT-P system provides increased productivity by facilitating optimal material flow and unattended operation for uninterrupted punching.

The CT-P system provides full capabilities for loading, unloading and storage of raw material and finished parts, thus enabling automated production from stored raw material to stacked finished parts, as well as providing increased storage capacity. The unit facilitates unmanned “lights out” production in a compact cell environment.

THE ALESTA® POWDER coating system from DuPont CoatingSolutions is partially automated and features flexible transport facilities, a waste water-free two-chamber pre-treatment plant, a retained water dryer, an enclosed manual powder coating cabin and an enamelling furnace with a heat recycling system.

Rippert, Haug Chemie and DuPont CoatingSolutions carried out a comprehensive series of tests together to bring the process up to its customer’s (Elba’s) required anti-corrosion standards. The result showed that a combination product with multi-metal capabilities, comprising a water-based acidic cleaning agent and iron phosphate solution, provides an optimised pre-treatment process for galvanised steel, aluminium and black steel substrates.

Adhesion and anti-corrosion properties as well as the outdoor properties of the used liquid paints formed the basis for the choice of the new powder coating system. Sample sheets of the substrates used by Elbag were coated in liquid paint and subjected to corresponding tests to obtain exact information about their properties. DuPont CoatingSolutions developed two different powder coating layered structures based on the results.

## THE RIGHT PROFILE

VERNON TOOL COMPANY, a Lincoln Electric subsidiary, is now offering pipe and tube cutting and profiling solutions for use in the handrail and fencing fabrication market. The Vernon Tool MasterTube Cutter (MTC) and MasterPipe Profiler (MPP) models are suitable for small- to medium-sized shops with a need to efficiently cut and profile tube or pipe for improved joining preparation.

The 5-axis MTC offers the capacity for 1 – 8 in. round (1 – 12 in. outside diameter) and rectangular (7.5 in. cross section) pipe and tube cutting with either plasma or oxyfuel cutting. With a servo-driven rotating chuck, the MTC provides rotation and longitudinal motion and its two additional profiling axes allow for torch angle and linear motion. This system is suitable for high-production/high-volume handrail and fencing fabricators.

The 2-axis MPP is capable of handling cutting and profiling operations for pipe with an outside diameter of 1 – 12 in. and a length of up to 40 ft. This solution is suitable for both small shops and fabricators. See www.vernontool.com
