# NEW RANGE OF INVERTER WELDERS

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

KEMPPI (UK) Ltd has announced the launch of the Minarc Evo range of lightweight inverter welders following the international success of the awardwinning Minarc range, first launched in 2001. The new Minarc Evo product range offers a choice of MMA, MIG/MAG and DC TIG welding equipment which is easy to use and light to carry.

All the machines in the Minarc Evo range include Power Factor Correction (PFC) technology for ultimate energy efficiency, delivering their maximum output current at 35% duty cycle from any single phase 230V, 16A industrial power network and are compliant with the latest European EMC Harmonics Directive, IEC6000-3-12.

They are specifically designed for professional industrial repair and maintenance work, including agricultural and horticultural applications, welder training colleges as well as DIY applications and they can be used with generator power supplies, including with extra-long 100m plus extension cables, making them suitable for use on the shop floor and on-site.

Minarc Evo 150 is an MMA/TIG machine rated 140A @35 per cent duty cycle for MMA welding and 150A @ 35 per cent duty cycle for TIG welding. MinarcMig Evo 200 is a MIG/MAG machine with a large welding capacity and options include either Automatic or Manual mode set-up for precise weld quality and arc ignition, monitored and controlled by Kemppi’s adaptive arc regulation system. Rated 200A @ 35 per cent duty cycle, it has a large LCD graphical interface for easy user set-up. FOR MORE INFORMATION, PLEASE VISIT www.kemppi.com
