# Seamless cored wires

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

BÖHLER WELDING OFFERS its diamondspark brand to cover a full range of seamless cored wires.

“They are a great choice for welding applications with stringent requirements for productivity, safety and weld quality, such as robotic serial manufacturing and mechanised welding, and are suitable for high- and ultra-high strength steel welding, and for hydrogen-critical applications,” said the manufacturer.

“Benefits include a high deposition rate. Compared to solid wires with equal wire diameters, the current conducting cross-section of diamondspark wires is smaller and, therefore, resistance heating in the sheath is higher at the same welding current. This translates into a higher wire melt-off rate and higher deposition rates than with solid wires,” it added.

Hermetically sealed and ultra-dry, diamondspark wires are produced with low levels of diffusible hydrogen – typically 2-3ml/100g weld metal for rutile types and even lower for metal-cored and basic wires. The diamondspark laser-sealed metalcored wires are even ultra-dry with average initial hydrogen levels of below 2ml/100g weld metal. According to the manufacturer, they maintain this property until the moment of welding, regardless of the duration of storage and time of exposure at the work site.

“The seamless, copper-coated wire design adds sufficient stiffness and glide to overcome friction in liners, welding guns and contact tips for problemfree feeding,” added the welding specialist.
