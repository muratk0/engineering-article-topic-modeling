System automatically supplies laser machines

Remmert’s Laser Flex is fitted with two automated units for load and unload of laser systems. After sheet metal is cut, the system’s fork unit lifts the processed metal sheet from the laser’s changing table. At the same time, the vacuum unit picks up a new sheet from the connected storage system. As soon as the remover places the sheet back down or sets it next to the machine, the supplier attaches the new sheet to the laser.

This 60-second process helps keep downtime of connected machines to near zero, even lasers with high processing speeds.

Glass fiber tape reduces weld oxidation

Argweld

®

Weld Backing Tape™ from Huntingdon Fusion Techniques HFT

®

is a glass fiber tape that resists high temperatures and supports weld roots. It eliminates the creation of slag while removing contact with air to help reduce oxidation in manual and automatic welding. The tape has no true melting point, so it works with GTAW and GMAW. Four grades are available for use up to 80, 160, 240, or 600 amps.

In the center of each 3-in.-wide aluminum adhesive tape is a 1-in.-wide band of woven glass fiber. The glass fiber matting, which has differing thicknesses to match the welding current in use, is positioned under the center of the weld joint. The welding torch argon flow has sufficient space to move between the pores of the matting so the arc can stabilize and start forming the weld root.
