# SOYER (Heinz Soyer Bolzenschweißtechnik GmbH)

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]
www.soyer.com

AS A SPECIALIST in fastening and joining technology, Heinz Soyer Bolzenschweißtechnik GmbH (SOYER) will be displaying its new SRM® stud welding inverter BMK-8i with single-phase 230V connection at EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover. Weighing 9.5kg, it does not need high-voltage current and was developed to be mobile.

"Thanks to patented SRM Technology®, the lightweight stud welding device enables the energy-efficient, safe and clean welding of studs made of steel and stainless steel up to M10," the manufacturer told ISMR. "The SOYER BMK-8i entry-level model uses a welding current of 300A."

The new PH-9 SRM12+G welding gun, with integrated ground contact, complements the BMK-8i stud welder. Equipped with a new generation of SRM coil, gas consumption can now be reduced by 90%, said SOYER. There is no need for separate earth clamps when welding as mass transmission now takes place via a contact ring which is spring-mounted onto the support tube. This patent-pending welding gun has been specifically developed for M12 weld studs and recently won the 2016 Federal Innovation Prize.
