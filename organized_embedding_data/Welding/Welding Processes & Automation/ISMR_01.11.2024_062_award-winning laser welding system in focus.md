# Award-winning laser welding system in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

WeilTechnology received the EuroBLECH Award 2024 in the joining technology category at the EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover, Germany, on Tuesday 22 October 2024. Its LWC-RT4-BPP laser welding system picked up the award for the production of metallic bipolar plates.

"Our welding module for the production of bipolar plates for fuel cells and electrolysers achieves very high absolute welding speeds of 1m/second with a large usable working range. It precision joins thin metal foils to form bipolar plates," explained the manufacturer.

"The static structure between the optics and clamping technology guarantees accuracy and reproducibility. The butterfly weld process ensures outstanding weld seam quality, minimised distortion and low waste. The double field scanner technology used achieves extremely high process stability by increasing the required welding speed between two scanners. This enables annual production of several million bipolar plates," it continued.

The manufacturer has been developing machines and modular systems for over 35 years. Its systems are characterised by the combination of laser, clamping and automation technologies to enable versatile production steps in forming, cutting and joining processes.■
