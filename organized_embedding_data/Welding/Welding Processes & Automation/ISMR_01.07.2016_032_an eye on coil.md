# An eye on coil

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

IN DECEMBER 2015, Italian coil feeding equipment manufacturer, Servopresse Srl, celebrated its 45th anniversary. It is currently in the process of delivering a machine to a key rollforming manufacturer with a factory in India. The self-adjusting line features decoiling, straightening and welding of band head-tail

The coil-handling equipment has been designed to reduce time and costs on the cutting and profiling lines. The line can handle coils of up to 15 tons with an outside diameter of Ø2000mm for a max. width of 900mm. The welding head-tail unit features a hydraulic guillotine shear (double- or singleblade) to trim the bands that will be welded. Torch support is completed with a manual cross slide and joints to a fine welding point regulation. The TIG welding power source is 300 Ampere, inverter controlled at 40%. The power source also features a welding parameter device control; an automatic water cooling TIG torch; a fan cooling system; a gas pressure regulator and a spare parts kit.

"The advantages of the head-tail welding system are numerous, especially on complicated progressive moulds and transfer systems. This is because the stop, re-start and coil change functions in the production line can be significantly reduced," said Servopresse, which produces a complete range of coil lines for use with various types of machine tools such as presses, shears, bending presses, punching machines, profile-forming machines etc.
