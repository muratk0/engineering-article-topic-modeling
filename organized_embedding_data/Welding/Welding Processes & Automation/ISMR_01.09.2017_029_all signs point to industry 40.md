# All signs point to Industry 4.0

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

HOW ARE COMPANIES preparing their welding technology to face a digital future? How can they optimise their processes with Industry 4.0 in mind? At the Schweissen & Schneiden trade fair in Düsseldorf, EWM AG will provide answers on stand A60 in Hall 10.

In keeping with the slogan "Welding 4.0", the company will present the latest generation of its MIG/MAG welding machines designed for efficient and innovative welding processes. Enhanced EWM Xnet quality management software performs the complete analysis and management of data produced in the welding process. EWM has also introduced new functionality to the software in the form of digital component management. The opening of the trade fair will coincide with the presentations of the fifth EWM Award, a project aimed at promoting young scientists which carries prize money of 30,000 euros.

EWM will also offer trade visitors their first glimpse of a completely new machine concept. Germany's largest manufacturer of arc welding technology will underline the importance of digitalisation and Industry 4.0 in the optimisation of welding processes. The MIG/MAG welding system offers maximum flexibility, it told ISMR, while the new generation of machines is aimed at manual, mechanised and automated welding processes.

Alongside high efficiency and a high duty cycle, sustainability was the main consideration for EWM in the development of established digital inverter technology. It has redesigned a clear control unit for the new machine series which promises welders faster and simple access to all the main machine settings. Visitors at the trade fair will be able to see the new welding system and other technological innovations, with live demonstrations on the EWM stand.

## Servo mechanical press line

PRESS SPECIALIST, Lien Chieh Machinery Co., Ltd. was founded in 1947 and, this year, is celebrating the 70th anniversary of the launch of its servo mechanical tandem press line. The line is extensively used in the automotive sheet metal industry - tonnage ranges from 2400 to 1000 tons and the bolster area is 5,000 x 2,500mm.

"We were the first to develop this line in Taiwan. The production line has been designed to meet advanced press industry technology requirements, enabling producers to benefit from the high speed generated from mechanical presses as well as energy efficiency from the servo-hydraulic presses. Servo systems help manufacturers to simulate production under different circumstances, which fundamentally benefits users who need modular and diversified cross-industrial production. In operational tests, the servo mechanical tandem line can increase the SPM from 18-20 to 30-40 times, with its highly automated system. It therefore raises productivity radically," the company told ISMR.
