# Welding technology for future production

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

THE HANNOVER MESSE will take place from 23–27 April 2018 in Hanover, Germany. At the show, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH will present numerous future-oriented innovations for manual and automated welding technology. The focus of its exhibition booth will be on connectivity and digitalisation. Using an interactive presentation, the welding specialist will demonstrate a completely connected robot system.

Whether for individual component marking; offline programming; 3D measurement; automated operation and production monitoring; remote service and others, CLOOS offers a variety of solutions for Industry 4.0 in welding technology. The CLOOS portfolio includes simple, compact systems as well as complex, chained systems with automated workpiece identification and loading and unloading processes.

"At the exhibition, we will show that tool carriers, robots, power sources and master controllers successfully communicate with each other today in CLOOS automation systems all over the world," said CLOOS managing director, Sieghard Thomas. The company will celebrate its 100th anniversary next year. From welding technology, robot mechanics and controller to positioners, software and sensors, CLOOS supplies everything from a single source, CLOOS told ISMR.
