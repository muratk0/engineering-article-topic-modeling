## Sensors and software

The robots at the three final welding stations are fitted with a high-tech laser sensor which compensates for the tolerances between the programmed lines and the actual positioning of the work-pieces. Torch position, as well as various process parameters, are continuously adjusted to achieve optimum welding results.

Offline programming of the robot systems is done using Cloos RoboPlan software. While the system is in production, a new program can be produced in RoboPlan. The software enables welding, search, travel paths and tools to be defined on 3-D models to which the welding parameters and further functions required to run the program are then added, with the resulting program only then transferred via a network connection to the robot controller and optimised at the workplace. This process is less time-consuming than producing a whole new program in the system.

As well as RoboPlan, Process Data Monitoring software (PDM) is used to monitor program performance, generate error messages and monitor welding parameters. Remote Diagnostics Software (RSM) enables remote maintenance of the robots.

## Automation partner

Investment in the latest robot welding systems has enabled the company to significantly accelerate its production processes and achieve precisely reproducible welding results. AGCO Fendt is now able to meet the demands of its customers for increased productivity and quality.

Employees also benefit from better conditions as the robots undertake the physically heavy work and the general hazards of arc radiation and welding fumes are also reduced. They can therefore concentrate more on process monitoring.

The high level of redundancy of the robot and welding technology as well as the software in the production line also enable increases in capacity at short notice. Capacities of up to 20 per cent can immediately be implemented. For both manual and automated welding, Fendt has relied for many years on Cloos technologies.

"We benefit a great deal from the expertise and experience of our partner. Cloos supplies all the technologies we need, single-sourced,” concluded Baumgarten.

In the meantime, more than 30 Cloos robot cells are in use at Asbach-Bäumenheim for the welding of individual assemblies to entire safety cabs. The oldest Cloos robot system at Fendt has been operating successfully for over 20 years and over 100,000 operating hours. ■

## SteelFab 2015 will be a catalyst of growth for regional steel fabrication and metal working in the Middle East

Meet ISMR on its booth at next year’s SteelFab exhibition in Sharjah, UAE – the regional steel fabrication and metal working industry’s well-established trade event. The 2015 show, from 26-29 January at Expo Centre Sharjah in the United Arab Emirates, will feature greater participation from steel fabricators, steel contractors and manufacturers. Exhibitors in this segment can expect to meet visitors such as main contractors, construction companies, oil & gas industry, shipbuilding industry and heavy manufacturing industries.

“Suppliers of steel fabrication and metal working equipment and technology are expecting a significant rise in demand from the region’s resurgent construction sector, one of the key demand drivers for the fabrication industry. With industry reports that the US$ 2.5 trillion regional construction sector appears to be returning to near full-capacity, leading manufacturers and suppliers are set to converge on a regional platform to push up their sales and begin the New Year on a positive note,” the organiser told ISMR.

SteelFab 2015 will include new features such as the Steel-Tech conference, which will tap into rising demand by bringing together primary steel manufacturing and secondary processing technology for the regional steel industry. It will feature suppliers of turnkey steel plants, furnaces, rolling mills, coil handling machinery, galvanising/annealing lines etc. It is expected to attract visitors from the region's steel producers and steel mills, primary steel processors, tube and pipe manufacturers and steel wire manufacturers.

## About SteelFab 2015

The 11th SteelFab will be organised and hosted by Expo Centre Sharjah with the support of the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry from 26-29 January 2015. It will showcase machinery and equipment in metal forming, surface preparation and finishing, grinding and cutting, machining and other allied engineering disciplines. In 2014, SteelFab hosted 300 exhibitors over 24,000 sq. m. of exhibition space and attracted 9,022 trade visitors. See www.steelfabme.com.

## Middle Eastern metal markets

“We enjoyed unprecedented growth for the previous edition of the show, in terms of exhibition space sold, the number of exhibitors we hosted or the visitor turnout. We are expecting to do better this year with about 15% growth given that the market fundamentals are pretty strong and growth drivers are intact,” said Mr Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, CEO, Expo Centre Sharjah.

According to latest industry reports on the GCC construction market, Saudi Arabia continues to have the highest market share of 44.3 per cent with projects valued at US$ 1.1 trillion. The UAE is in the second place at 30.3 per cent with US$ 717.7bn project value and Qatar has projects worth US$ 270.1 billion, accounting for 11.2 per cent.

“While this takes into account all the projects that are currently under way, the volume of announced and planned projects in the UAE in 2014 alone is expected to be valued at US$ 315 billion, with the government announcing a number of major development projects and stepping up spending on social infrastructure development,” added the organiser.

“While a buoyant market does generate better demand, latest technological developments too are a key factor that drives the market. For example, demand for automation in steel fabrication is increasing at a fast pace. From automatic robotic welding and surface treatment solutions to automatic material handling and fully automatic steel fabrication factories, market requirements are changing quickly. SteelFab 2015 will be the region’s one-stop shop for all the requirements of the fabrication industry,” added Naveen Fernandes, Group Exhibition Manager. The sheet metal sector is also seeing quick adaptation to new technology with more lasers selling (CO2 and fiber) and exhibitors such as TRUMPF, Durma, Ermak and Bystronic expected to display lasers.

SteelFab 2015 will focus on specific industry segments. The show will continue with focus areas such as Welding & Cutting, Material Handling, Metal-Cutting and Pipe & Tube Machinery which are popular in the regional industry. The Metal-Cutting segment has seen strong growth with several international manufacturers represented on the show floor.

“SteelFab is also known for its strong participation by leading manufacturers and dealers from foreign countries, with Taiwan, Germany, Italy, Turkey, India and China and other countries either having special pavilions or facilitating participation of exhibitors from their respective countries during the previous edition of the show,” added Mr Fernandes.

With two more months to go, the show has already secured the support of UCIMU SISTEMI PER PRODURRE – The Association Of Italian Manufacturers of Machine Tools, Robots, Automation Systems and Ancillary Products; the Official German Pavilion, Taiwan Pavilion and the UAE Contractors Association.

“Foreign participation in SteelFab has been increasing with each passing year, and our partnership with several industry associations and government entities have ensured that visitors have access to the widest range of products from across the world,” added Mr Fernandes.

Visitors can also take advantage of an extensive line-up of technical seminars and product presentations that will be held alongside the show.
