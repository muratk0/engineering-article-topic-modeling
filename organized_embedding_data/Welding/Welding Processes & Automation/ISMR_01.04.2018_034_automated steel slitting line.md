# Automated steel slitting line

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

"At Hannover Messe, we will present our new control cabinet generation QIROX Controller QC 2. Independently from chained systems with MES connections, the new QC 2 robot controller generation enables the bidirectional data transmission to host computer systems via PC-UA. The new servo technology ensures dynamic movement and high path accuracy of the robot during welding."

Other highlights will be the new QIROX Operating System (QOS) with the new QIROX Technology Interface (QTI) and new QIROX QWP operating software. With QINEO Data Manager, Process Data Monitoring and RoboPlan, CLOOS offers further software solutions for connected production. It has also developed a complete information and communication tool, PC Suite, to centrally record and save all incoming and outgoing data and regulate access.

Finally, CLOOS will showcase new welding processes designed for high productivity and quality. This includes Tandem Weld, where two separate welding wires are fed and melted simultaneously in one molten pool for high deposition rate, high welding speed and low heat input. The laser MIG/MAG Hybrid process, a combination of a laser beam with a MIG/MAG welding process in one common process zone, will also be demonstrated.

Steel thicknesses are from 0.3mm to 12.0mm and steel widths range from 1220mm to 2000mm. With over 35 years of experience in developing automated production facilities, its advanced line design is customised to customers requirements.

"Thanks to the dual slitter system design, changeover time on our slitting lines has been shortened and productivity has been doubled. Double seats enable an offline blade arrangement while the line is running, which is key for production time savings," explained the Taiwanese manufacturer.

"With a rotating four-arm turnstile device, slit strips can be temporarily stored on the heavy-duty turnstile arm to enable offmandrel strapping and allow operators to select any coil, in any order. Greater convenience and efficiency are enhanced because of the equipment design."
