AWS offers a number of widely recognised certification programmes to assist the industry in identifying qualified welding personnel and to provide opportunities for welding professionals. The main AWS certification is the Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) programme. Established in 1976, this AWS service has certified more than 60,000 welding inspectors and currently about 25,000 are certified. It has been a major contributor to improved weld quality and reduced inspection costs, AWS told ISMR. The Society also offers additional certification programmes for welding supervisors; engineers; radiographic interpreters; educators; sales representatives; robotic arc welding operators and technicians; fabricators and more.
