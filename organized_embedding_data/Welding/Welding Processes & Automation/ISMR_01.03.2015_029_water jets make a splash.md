## Analyse, control and manage welding processes

“DISCOVERING POTENTIAL savings, ensuring cost-effective production and documenting every weld are three requirements which couldn't be more different. Our modular quality management software, ewm Xnet, helps to combine these three tasks in a single solution which small welding firms and major companies can use. It is compatible with Industry 4.0 and already supports networking for complex manufacturing sequences today – from mass production to a one-off single item,” explained ewm.

“ewm Xnet continuously captures and records all welding parameters and machine data to meet quality assurance criteria and documentation requirements. The software allows users to compare the duty cycle and effective welding time of every machine. A low ratio between these two values indicates a significant amount of non-productive time. By precisely analysing this non-productive time, processes can be optimised in a targeted manner.”

Wear is a subtle process. Replacing parts too soon pushes up costs; replacing them too late can mean production downtime and wastage. Ideally, parts should be replaced in time but not prematurely – potential savings for any company. ewm Xnet determines the optimum moment based upon the recorded machine data. It also features maintenance alerts. Before starting an order, the counter readings on the machines used are set to zero for the following consumption items: welding consumables, shielding gas and electricity. Once work is complete, ewm Xnet automatically displays the cumulative values. The software is web-based and compatible with all operating systems. It runs on fixed PCs, laptops, tablets and smartphones. See www.ewm-group.com ■
