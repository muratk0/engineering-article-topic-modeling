# Weld fume extractor

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

LINCOLN ELECTRIC has enhanced its next-generation Miniflex weld fume control system. Created for the welder ‘on-the-go’, the Miniflex removes and filters welding fumes wherever welding operators go, including confined and other hard-to-reach spaces.

Unlike other bulkier welding fume extractors, the Miniflex features standard wheels, handle and lightweight, synthetic materials. It offers a dual-filtration system made up of a primary LongLife®-H filter and a HEPA output filter. Two key new features are a carbon brush early warning system (to alert the user when 80 per cent of the carbon brushes have been consumed) and a system airflow check (to check the extraction performance of the unit and indicates when a filter is saturated and requires replacement).

Lincoln Electric is specialist in the design, development and manufacture of arc welding products, robotic arc welding systems, plasma and oxy-fuel cutting equipment and has a leading global position in the brazing and soldering alloys market.
