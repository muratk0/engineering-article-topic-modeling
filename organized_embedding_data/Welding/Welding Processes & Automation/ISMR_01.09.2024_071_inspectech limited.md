InspecTech has been in business for over 41 years providing NDT solutions using eddy current, flux leakage and ultrasonic technologies.

"We partner with our customers to increase product reliability and productivity by providing integrated, quality, custom-designed solutions in non-destructive testing," it commented.

The InspecTech Model 12-07 system utilises magnetic flux leakage test techniques to test the integrity of the weld seam in carbon steel blanks. The carbon steel blanks are conveyed by the welding machine with their edges butted and are joined by laser welding. The 12-07 system is installed directly in the process line.

"Our MFL system is specially designed to test welded blanks. We have over 50 MFL inspection systems for testing welded blanks around the world," it added.
