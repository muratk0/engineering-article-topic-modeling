# CLOOSer to the future

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

Above: QN MIG MAG welding power source

AT THE SCHWEISSEN and Scheiden exhibition this September in Düsseldorf, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH presented various product innovations for manual and automated welding. The focus of its booth was on connectivity and digitalisation. The strength of the company, which will celebrate its 100th anniversary in two years, is its widely spread expertise – CLOOS supplies everything from a single source (from the welding technology, robot mechanics and controller to positioners, software and sensors).

During live demonstrations of manual welding, booth visitors saw a wide range of CLOOS welding power sources, from entry to premium. One CLOOS focus was on its new QINEO NexT MIG/MAG welding power source series. The modular design enables many applications, from the basic welding machine for manual welding to the multi-process welding machine for automated robot welding. CLOOS has also further developed its QINEO Pulse and QINEO Champ welding power sources. The new Proversion offers optimised functions such as an Ethernet interface as standard.

With its QIROX robots, positioners and special purpose machines, CLOOS develops and manufactures automated welding systems to meet the specific requirements of its customers. At the exhibition, CLOOS presented, for the first time, its new QIROX QRC-290 welding robot for, it said, 'easy entry into automated welding'. Other booth highlights were its new QIROX Operating System (QOS) and the new programming surface QIROX Technology Interface (QTI) 1 for robots.

CLOOS also presented its new process family, MoTion Weld, for automated MIG/MAG welding. The new process is particularly suitable for thin plate applications. For laserwelding, CLOOS also offered individual and future-oriented solutions. Software solutions on display aimed to increase the performance of welding power sources and robot systems. The RoboPlan offline programming software is designed to support the user to efficiently structure production runs.
