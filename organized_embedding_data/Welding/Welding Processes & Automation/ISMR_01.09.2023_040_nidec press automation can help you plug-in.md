## Carl Cloos Schweißtechnik GmbH (CLOOS)

## www.cloos.de

Carl Cloos Schweißtechnik GmbH (CLOOS) has been a specialist in welding technology since 1919. With 950 employees worldwide, its process competence is in the welding and cutting of various ferrous and non-ferrous metals. It offers customers individual solutions which are optimised and adapted specifically to their product and production requirements.

Its portfolio includes QINEO welding power sources for a variety of welding processes and QIROX robots, positioners and fixtures. From welding technology to robot mechanics and control to positioners, software and sensors, CLOOS supplies everything from a single source. The QIROX ExperT is its new product line for standard components in the automation sector. The range includes predefined compact cells and compact systems as well as a modular construction kit concept for robot systems.

The company boasts nine production sites, 12 subsidiaries and 50 sales and service centres worldwide.

## Optrel AG

## www.optrel.com

In 1986, Optrel started working on automatically darkening welding helmets. Today, it is a global supplier of face and head protection products, breathing protection solutions and active sunglasses. Its products include welding, respiratory and face protection such as respiratory welding helmet systems, the TH3 blower respiratory protection system and respiratory grinding helmet systems.

"Every year, we deliver well over one hundred thousand occupational safety products, and we are not a mass producer. Almost every product is unique; tailored to the needs of the customer. We are still shaped by the special culture of a family business. Strong values and sustainable action make us a reliable partner. In everything we do, we and our customers are united by a common goal. We want to sustainably improve their processes," it commented.

## REHM GmbH & Co. KG

## www.rehm-online.de

REHM manufactures TIG welding equipment; MIG/MAG welding equipment; manual welding equipment and plasma-cutting equipment.

"Our welding units are easy to use and suitable for demanding applications," REHM highlighted.

Its new FOCUS.ARC P MIG/MAG pulse welding machine features the new SIRIUS application which enables machine and process data to be digitised and communicated to all networks. Production progress, welding data and consumption values can therefore be documented and used for production planning. The focused arc has also been further developed and improved.

The REHM INVERTIG i is its next generation of three-phase TIG devices, featuring new AC waveforms; SPOT for DC and AC; PULS DC up to 17,500Hz and PULS AC up to 17,500Hz. The TIGER® digital welding unit is its mobile solution. The TIG inverter TIGER® is equipped with up to eight process variants for weld seams.
