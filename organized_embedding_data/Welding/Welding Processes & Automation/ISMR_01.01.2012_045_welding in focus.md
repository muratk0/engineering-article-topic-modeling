# Welding in focus

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

FROM 23-27 Oct. 2012, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH will showcase its latest highlights at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover. In Hall 13 on stand D25,

With its new QINEO® Pulse and Tandem welding torch, Cloos combines the advantages of the QINEO welding machine series with its high-capacity Tandem technology for high deposition rates and high welding speeds. Cloos will also introduce the QINEO Tronic Pulse, a further development in inverter technology. Pulsed arc welding is now possible even for smaller component size with higher efficiency levels, it told ISMR.

Cloos has extended its scope of supply with the new small inverter machine series particularly for welding on-site or in a workshop. The series comprises the portable MIG/MAG welding machines (QINEO Micro and QINEO Micro Pulse) as well as welding machines for electrode manual welding and TIG welding. The portable plasma cutting system, QINEO Plasma Cut 30, completes the Cloos small machine series.

"Our plasma powder arc welding system (PPAW) QINEO TIG Tronic 350 DC was particularly developed for plasma powder joint welding. The main advantages of this process are its high weld quality and good accessibility. The QINEO Champ allows welding by using the heat-reduced Cold Weld AC process. The new power source technology also enables users to change different processes within a weld seam several times without arc interruption. Thus, the welding process can be perfectly matched to the joining task," Cloos added.

Cloos QIROX® welding robots will also be on display at EuroBLECH. See www.cloos.de
