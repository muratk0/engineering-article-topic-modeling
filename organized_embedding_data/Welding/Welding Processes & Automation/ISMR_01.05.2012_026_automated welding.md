# Automated welding

[TARİH: 01.05.2012 ISMR]

CEMSA’S WELDING machine, DELTA S TVR, is a ‘new concept in fully automated working centres’.

The machine is equipped with an indexing rotating table with stations, normally six. The machine shown in the picture is a projection welding machine for three components, featuring three magazines, to produce door locks. The machine can also be equipped with a standard single-phase transformer, in the range of 150 kVA, and a Medium Frequency inverter (1000 Hz).

Standard production rate is one piece every 2.5 seconds with practically no marks on the workpiece face. www.cemsa.it
