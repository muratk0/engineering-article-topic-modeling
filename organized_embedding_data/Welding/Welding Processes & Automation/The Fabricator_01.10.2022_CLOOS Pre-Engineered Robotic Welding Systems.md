Since 1985, CLOOS has been supplying robotic welding systems from our North American headquarters in the Chicago area. They have found success welding large, complicated parts in industries like Agriculture, Construction, and Mining. CLOOS has taken this experience and developed a full range of pre-engineered cells that provides the same high-quality solutions to almost every size company in any industry. All the Pre-Engineered systems come with integrated CLOOS weld power sources for seamless welding performance and the robust German design built to last.

The QINEO ArcBoT

is the newest addition to the CLOOS lineup. It is designed to work in the situations where there is a high mix of simple parts. With the intuitive, leadthrough programming approach, it is a perfect addition to the shop floor to help meet production needs. All equipment is mounted under the welding table so it can easily be transported to the location it is needed most.

The QIROX Compact Cells

are the perfect solution for small to medium part production needs. All six models are designed for 2-station productivity. Their small footprint has a minimal impact on the production floor and the cells are pallet-mounted, which allows them to be moved easily with almost no disassembly.

The most popular Compact Cell design is the CC-4. It provides the versatility needed for high production automation.

The CS-30 is the workhorse of the Compact System lineup. It has the capacity to handle parts up to 3.5m in length.

The QIROX Compact Systems

are designed to handle the medium and larger parts. As with the Compact Cells, these systems are fully engineered for 2-station operation and quick, efficient set-up. There are 10 different designs to fit individual customer parts and floor space.
