Touchscreen panel available in two sizes for weld monitoring

AMADA WELD TECH has introduced the TP-AWS3-A touchscreen panel in standard and large sizes for weld monitoring. When used in tandem with a welding system, the panel provides real-time dynamic measurement, cataloging, and analysis of all welding variables.

The unit features two independent measurement channels on screen, providing expanded oversight. Users can view voltage oscillations with the panel’s oscilloscope functions, which include zoom and cursor modes.

With full on-screen SPC capability, quality measurement is shown in real time. An integrated time and date system helps simplify weld reporting and traceability efforts. The panel stores up to 99 schedules.

Weld camera provides clear, remote view of open arc welding processes

Xiris Automation has introduced the XVC-750 weld camera with 140+ dB high dynamic range capability in the near infrared spectrum. Designed for GMAW and GTAW processes, the camera integrates seamlessly with welding automation equipment.

The camera provides a clear, remote view of open arc welding processes during setup or run-time. Its image contrast of the melt pool and cooling weld bead enables advanced machine vision analysis to make precise geometric measurements and to detect defects in the weld process.

Featuring a variety of angled optics configurations, the camera is compatible with different lenses for versatility in many welding applications.

Buffer scheduling feature added to ERP software

Cetec ERP has introduced a new scheduling feature that allows users to estimate static buffer time occurring before or after work is completed at a given work location. Allowing this buffer time lets users ensure ample time is given on the workorder, without impacting labor time, estimates, or cost. The new field can be managed in two ways: Defaults can be set from a table in the Data Maintenance page named "OrdlineStatus," or they can be set manually or overridden in a part’s labor plan.
