# Welding power and processes

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

FRONIUS UK LTD will be presenting its welding capabilities for manual, robotic and automated solutions, including all its latest welding power sources, at MACH 2018 (9-13 April 2018) in Birmingham, UK. The Fronius team will be on stand H20-957 asking visitors about their welding challenges and offering solutions or alternative ways of welding to meet those challenges.

The single-phase TransSteel 2200 manual power source offers, Fronius told ISMR, multi-process capability.

It is the first single-phase, 3-in-1 welding machine for MIG/MAG, TIG and MMA welding applications with a multi-voltage option available, giving welders greater flexibility. Visitors to MACH will be able to see the TransSteel 2200 live at work in the demo bay as well as on the stand. For the first time at a UK exhibition, we will also show our newly modified TIG process, ArcTig. Developed to help welders work more efficiently and reduce costs, ArcTig is an innovative mechanised joint welding process that allows users to weld up to 10mm thick alloy sheets and pipes without extensive seam preparation and reworking," explained Fronius.

Manual TIG welding at Fronius has seen a refresh with the launch of two new power sources – the MagicWave 230i and the TransTig 230i. Both TIG machines are the first from Fronius that can communicate with other devices via Bluetooth, WLAN and NFC. The Fronius MagicWave 230i is an AC/DC power source with Active Wave technology, making AC TIG welding much quieter, whereas the TransTig 230i is a DC power source.

Alongside the new products, Fronius will be showcasing its wide range of manual, robotic and automated welding solutions, including the intelligent TPSi range with Cold Metal Transfer (CMT) technology for low heat input with stable arc, the WeldCube data management system for gathering and analysing weld data, the TransPocket 150 and 180 (including the multi-voltage capabilities of the TransPocket 180) and more.
