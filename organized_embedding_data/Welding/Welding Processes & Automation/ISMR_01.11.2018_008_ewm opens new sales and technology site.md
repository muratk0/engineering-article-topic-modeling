# EWM opens new sales and technology site

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

EWM AG has invested 3.8 million euros into the new sales and technology centre in Wittstock

EWM AG, ONE of Germany's largest supplier of arc welding technology, has opened another site in Wittstock (Brandenburg) aimed at meeting the high demand for welding technology in North-East Germany. In the sales and technology centre, the family business provides a comprehensive range of welding technology as well as extensive welding consultancy services.

"As a technology partner for customers, we want to develop innovative welding processes and individual solutions for all welding production processes in a large area of application technology. Furthermore, we are planning regular trade events and demonstrations of modern and future-thinking welding processes at the new site," outlined the German welding specialist.

"We want to be the greatest specialist welding shop in East Germany," added Toralf Pekrul, manager of the EWM site in Wittstock.

At present, the family-run business already has a site in Brandenburg in the town of Rathenow. With the opening of the large sales and technology centre (over 2,000 square metres in Wittstock), the welding technology manufacturer continues to expand. It currently has 14 sites in Germany.

Alongside a transport network and 1,150 square metres of storage space, the new premises in Wittstock provides lots of space for new technology. In addition to the latest EWM welding machines, there are also welding robots and automation components for practical applications. As such, EWM is developing individual solutions with customers to optimise complex welding manufacturing processes and systems. The welding manufacturer has also set up a large area for service and repair

EWM AG has acquired a stake in H. Euen GmbH. The news was announced at EuroBLECH by Bernd Szczesny (right), Chairman, EWM AG, and Andreas Euen, Managing Director, H. Euen GmbH

"In Wittstock, there will soon be training sessions, demonstrations and robotised welding days on a regular basis to continually provide customers with the right service package and the latest technology. We are initially starting with a team of 14 employees at the new site, with a medium-term plan to expand the number of workers up to 40," concluded the company.

EWM AG recently acquired a stake in H. Euen GmbH, a systems vendor with complete solutions for robot automation, mechanisation and the manufacture of machine construction components for use in welding. The two long-term partners announced the news at the EuroBLECH trade fair. The new company arising from the relationship will trade under the name of EWM-EUEN GmbH.

For the opening of the new site, EWM offered a varied programme with an in-house exhibition, practical welding demonstrations and specialist talks

## New President and CEO of Bentley Motors, Inc.

Christophe Georges

BENTLEY MOTORS HAS announced that Christophe Georges will become President and CEO of Bentley Motors, Inc. – the regional office for Bentley Motors in the Americas – on 1 December 2018. He succeeds Mark Del Rosso, who was recently appointed as President of Audi of America.

Georges, who will lead the Americas region for the second time – previously between 2007 and 2015 – leaves his role as director of product and marketing for Bentley's global organisation. He will continue to report directly to Chris Craft, Bentley's Member of the Board for sales, marketing and aftersales.

Craft commented: "I am delighted to welcome Christophe back to the Americas region. He brings a wealth of knowledge of the ultra-luxury market and a strong understanding of the Bentley brand. As we embark on the most intensive period of new model introductions in Bentley's history, I am confident that Christophe will continue to drive the company's success in this key market. I would also like to thank Mark for the significant contribution he made in his time at Bentley, playing a crucial role in reinforcing the company's position in the region."

Georges joined Bentley Motors in 1998 and led the European and Americas regions before taking responsibility for the company's marketing and product activities.

"I look forward to re-establishing my close relationships with our customers and retailers in the region, driving the brand and network presence further and introducing our next generation of products to the market, starting with the all-new Continental GT next year," commented Georges.

There are 61 Bentley retailers throughout the Americas with corporate headquarters based in Reston, Virginia.   
The region is one of Bentley's most important markets, accounting for more than 20 per cent of global sales.

## New regional VP for Olympic Steel

OLYMPIC STEEL INC., a leading metals service centre in the U.S., has announced the promotion of Andrew F. Wolfort to the position of Regional Vice President. He joined Olympic Steel in 2007 as an inside sales representative for the company's Chicago office and was then promoted to outside sales representative. In 2010, he relocated to Olympic Steel's Bettendorf, Iowa facility where he was promoted to inside sales manager. The following year, he assumed the position of regional sales manager at the company's Gary, Indiana facility and, in 2015 Wolfort was promoted to the position of general manager – Chicago Division.

"Andrew's leadership has been instrumental in the success and recent expansion of our Chicago facility," said Andy Markowitz, President – Specialty Metals. "His diverse commercial and operations experience, in both the flat rolled and specialty metals market segments provide an excellent foundation to increase sales and market share throughout the Chicago and Northwest Indiana region."

In this expanded role, Wolfort remains general manager of Olympic Steel's Chicago Division, while assuming additional oversight responsibility for the company's Gary, Indiana facility. Scott Besunder, general manager – Gary, will report directly to Wolfort. In his new role, Wolfort will report to Markowitz.

Wolfort attended the University of Colorado Boulder, Leeds School of Business where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration and Finance. He is also a graduate of the Metals Service Center Institute's Strategic Metals Management programme at the Olin Business School at Washington University in St. Louis, Missouri. Wolfort is President of the MSCI Central States Chapter and has been a chapter board member since 2015.
