Welding helmet features wide fit, external controls

The new ORBIT welding helmet from Walter Surface Technologies offers three high-definition, auto-darkening lenses; anti-scratch cover plates; and a 24-sq.-in. viewing area. HD technology allows the user to see colors well and have a clear, defined view, the company states.

The helmet is designed with a wide fit to allow the user to wear gear, glasses, or other accessories underneath it. External controls allow for shade adjustment and grind mode selection without having to lift the hood. It is solar powered and has a USB charger for backup charging.

Brinell hardness measuring system includes configurable display

Ametek Newage Testing Instruments has launched a new version of its Brinell Optical Scanning System (B.O.S.S.). The system uses a compact scanning head attached to a computer to measure impression diameters in seconds and yields a measurement resolution of 0.01 mm.

The software sets up the criteria for the reading and performs the necessary Brinell Hardness Number calculations to ASTM standards. The system takes into account such anomalies as impression roundness and surface finish of samples.

The user interface has been upgraded with a new configurable display that shows the 10 most recent readings and features customizable measurement screens. An updated demonstration mode allows for multiple impressions.

Ametek Newage Testing Instruments

Automated semi-narrow groove welding system handles thick plate

Pemamek LLC has developed an automated semi-narrow groove welding system for welding thick plates from 2 to 6.5 in. This submerged arc welding process uses less consumables over the length of the operation, according to the manufacturer.

The tiltable welding head, equipped with W-in. wire, provides control over the fusion process and weld bead creation. It also helps direct the energy into the side walls of the semi-narrow groove for full penetration. Any unconsumed flux can be vacuumed, circulated, and recycled.

Full welds are possible from the root weld to the surface. For tubular structures, longitudinal and circular milling machines are used to mill the groove root; a 16-degree included angle is standard with an 8-degree radius on the base.

Collaborative welding system offers simple programming, changeover

Miller Electric Mfg. LLC has introduced the Copilot collaborative welding system. This entry-level cobot is designed for shops and welders who are new to robotic welding and looking for ways to keep up with demand, the company states.

Welders input the material thickness and wire diameter, and IntelliSet technology configures the ideal settings for the best results. This allows for quick changeovers for highmix, low-volume parts. Powered by the Auto-Continuum 350 welding power source and featuring Accu-Pulse, Versa-Pulse, and Regulated Metal Deposition welding processes, the system will grow with businesses and operations as they take on more projects, says the company.

Flame-resistant cotton shirt suitable for light welding applications

Black Stallion has introduced the FS9-PGY gray plaid flame-resistant cotton work shirt. Constructed of 7-oz. treated cotton and recommended for light welding applications, it conforms to ASTM D6413 flame resistance through 50 home washings.

Adjustable metal wrist snaps allow for a tighter or looser fit as needed. The two chest pockets include protective flaps to keep out welding sparks and debris. These flaps can be secured with the hidden snaps. The left chest pocket includes a pencil slot for quick access.

Long-life, shank-mounted, aluminumoxide flap wheels offer aggressive cut rates

A-Prime aluminum-oxide, shank-mounted flap wheels are available from Weldcote, a division of Zika Group. Intended for use on pneumatic air tools, the flap wheels are available in long-life and premium-cut versions for use on stainless steel, steel, alloyed steel, nonferrous metals, aluminum, and titanium.

According to the company, the long-life flap wheel offers aggressive cut rates and is designed to work in small, difficult-to-reach areas, while the premium-cut version uses a flexible X-weight material with a premium initial cut rate.

The flap wheels are available in ¾- to 3-in. sizes in grits from 40 to 320. The shanks measure ¼ in.
