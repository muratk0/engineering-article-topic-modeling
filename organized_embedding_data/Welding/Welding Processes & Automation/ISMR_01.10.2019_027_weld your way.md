# WELD YOUR WAY

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

As CLOOS celebrates its 100th year anniversary, its managing director, Sieghard Thomas, outlines market trends in robotic welding markets, its new strategic initiatives and latest technology launches to ISMR.

Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH (CLOOS) has been one of the leading manufacturers of welding technology. Over 800 CLOOS employees all over the world offer production solutions in welding and robot technology for industries such as construction machinery; railway technology; energy; agriculture and the automotive industry.

CLOOS welding power sources in the QINEO series are used for a variety of welding processes. With its QIROX robots, positioners and special purpose machines, CLOOS also develops and manufactures automated welding systems to meet the specific requirements of its customers.

As CLOOS celebrates its 100th year anniversary, its managing director, Sieghard Thomas, outlines market trends in robotic welding markets, new strategic initiatives and latest technology launches to ISMR.

ISMR: What is your strategic focus/ vision over the next few years?

ST: For many years now, robotics has made its way into various industrial sectors, resulting in ever faster innovations, higher investment sums and shorter life cycles. We are at the dawn of a new era in digitisation and we are preparing for it with foresight because, in everything we do, the sustainable, economic success of our customers is always at the centre of our activities.

It is on this basis that the CLOOS group has now become part of Estun Automation Co. Ltd., capturing the potential of the largest robotic market of the world, China. Estun is a listed family company which was founded in Nanjing (China) in 1993. Today, Estun develops and produces a broad product portfolio which includes drive electronics and servomotors as well as industrial robots and ranges from motion controls to "turnkey" automatic production lines. Because of our partnership with Estun, we want to further expand the business for automated arc welding.

Sieghard Thomas, Managing Director, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH (CLOOS)

Estun and CLOOS are both family companies who share common values and requirements on product and quality goals. We are glad that we have found such an innovative partner, with whom we will continue to develop significantly.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in robotic welding markets?

ST: Demand for highly automated and intelligent robot systems is growing worldwide. Here, the individual components of a welding system and higher-level systems are networked. That is why we are increasingly focusing on digitisation in product development. The portfolio comprises simple, compact systems as well as complex, chained systems with automated workpiece identification and loading and unloading processes.

It is increasingly difficult to find qualified manual welders - not only in high-wage countries, but also in emerging markets. This provides additional impetus to automate welding, even for small batches up to batch size 1. CLOOS develops and manufactures automated welding systems to meet the specific requirements of our customers.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes or achievements and the reasons for this?

ST: For 100 years, CLOOS has pushed forward pioneering achievements in welding technology. Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH celebrated its 100-year company anniversary with a week of festivities from 6-11 May this year. During this celebration, we presented visitors with more than thirty new products.

We are proud to belong to the two per cent of German companies who are more than 100 years old. With our motivated, well-trained employees and innovative technologies, we believe that we are well placed for the next 100 years!

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals?

ST: Although the general economy has slowed down slightly at the moment, we are benefitting from the positive economic development and good order situation of the last few months.

Thanks to our broad positioning in the welding technology sector, we are not solely dependent on one industry. Difficult developments in the automotive industry, for example, have hardly affected us. The strong order situation has enabled us to significantly expand our production capacities in recent years.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers at the moment and how are you addressing these issues for them?

ST: From welding technology to robot mechanics and control to positioners, software and sensors, CLOOS supplies everything from a single source. Our customers expect flexible solutions for maximum efficiency. We offer a one-stop shop for high-tech solutions in manual and automated welding.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

ST: Developments around Industry 4.0 enable a much higher level of automation in production itself and further optimisation of the entire value chain. That is why the demand for intelligent manufacturing systems in our market environment is continuously increasing.

Digitisation offers enormous potential to optimise performance and increase plant availability, particularly for large complex welding systems. That is why we are strongly investing in laser scanners and offline programming systems. We want the welding program to be visualised completely on the digital twin.

ISMR: Are there any products/ technologies that you have released or are releasing this year that you would like to discuss?

ST: At Blechexpo in Stuttgart this November, visitors can expect a multitude of new products and innovations for a quicker, more economic and more flexible welding production. We will offer technologies from entry to premium level and from manual welding machines to automated robot systems from a single source.

Visitors will be able to watch live demonstrations of our wide QINEO welding machine range at Blechexpo in Stuttgart. The focus will be on the new QINEO NexT. This high-tech MIG/MAG welding power source features excellent arc characteristics for the highest welding quality. The modular design allows different applications from the basic welding machine for manual welding to the multi-process welding machine for automated robot welding.

We will also highlight new and proven welding processes for maximum productivity and quality at Blechexpo in Stuttgart. The new process family, MoTion Weld for automated MIG/MAG welding, is the main focus. Because of the controllable heat input into the workpiece and minimised spatter formation at high welding speeds, MoTion Weld processes are particularly suitable for thin plate applications.

We will also showcase the possibilities of additive welding at Blechexpo for the first time. A multitude of different components can be produced economically and resource-efficiently. Another exhibition highlight is the new Gateway C-Gate. This enables demand-based management of welding and robot data. All information is entered and processed centrally into an integrated information and communication tool. The new system consists of system-related hardware and different software modules. With the production module, users can see the performance and efficiency levels of their robot systems, localise shortages and increase efficiency.

Blechexpo visitors can also look forward to seeing our new Instant Robot Programming System (IRPS) for efficient welding with minimum programming effort. IRPS automatically allocates the welding program out of a database after a 3D scan of the workpiece. Last, but not least, our new QIROX Micro Cell offers a basic solution for rapid and easy entry into automated welding technology.
