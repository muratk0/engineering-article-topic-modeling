# NEW MD FOR CLOOS

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH has appointed a new managing director, Dr.-Ing. Robert Buchmann. Dr. Buchmann will be in charge of the operational management of the company, together with Dipl.-Kfm. Ralf Pulverich.

The company presented a wide range of innovative welding technology at the last EuroBLECH trade show in Hanover which included novelties in the field of robotics as well as newly developed welding power sources and process technologies for varied applications. Its QIROX welding robots are designed for floor and overhead mounting and can be combined with various different Cloos sensors.

The newly developed QINEOTRONIC inverter power source (350 – 600 A) can be used for both manual and automated welding. Its new MSG narrow gap welding technology, an automated welding process variant using industrial robots, is particular efficient for welding thick-walled component parts.
