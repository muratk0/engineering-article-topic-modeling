# An eye on tube and profiles

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

AT THE RECENT Tube Düsseldorf show from 4 to 8 April 2016, ROFIN demonstrated various options for laser welding tubes and profiles. This year, it showcased solutions for welding thin-wall composite tubes as well as classic stainless steel tubes.

Its Profile Welding System (PWS) opens up a wide range of possibilities for welding tubes and profiles. The PWS is a complete laser welding system with an integrated process sensor for welding gap detection and tracking. Using the “Weld Sensor”, the user can observe the welding process while the integrated controller corrects the welding position automatically. The operator control panel offers easy operation of all important system features. System parameters and functions, such as laser power, moving axes or gap monitoring at the work-piece, can be centrally set and controlled. With the new motorized axes (Z/X/Y), the operation becomes even more user-friendly and, depending upon customer requirements, connection to product databases can be made.

“The real-time working sensors and rapid, precise linear actuators ensure high production reliability by positioning the laser beam within a few μm of the seam gap while achieving welding speeds of up to 60 m/min. The integration of a seam inspector or a beam and process analysis tool is optionally available,” it told ISMR.

The PWS can be combined with different laser beam sources from ROFIN, such as the fibre lasers of its FL Series or the CO lasers of its DC Series with output power of up to 8kW, to adapt to customer requirements. See www.rofin.com
