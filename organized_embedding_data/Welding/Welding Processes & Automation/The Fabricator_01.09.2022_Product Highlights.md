Positioners made for assembly, arc welding applications

Pemamek Oy and Pemamek LLC have introduced Skyhook Pro positioners. Controlled by a RC32H wired remote control and intelligent software, the positioners can lift loads up to 15 tons. They can be rotated ±180 degrees for optimal positioning.

The user can preprogram and modify movements as needed, eliminating the need for clamping. Additionally, because the programs are generated ahead of time by more experienced welders, those new to the trade can continue operations using just the remote control and a pedal.

An electrical cabinet on the backside of the positioner contains a proprietary communication system connected by Wi-Fi to mobile phones, tablets, and laptops for programming, diagnostics, and maintenance schedules, providing a vehicle for importing or exporting work cycle information to other Skyhook machines.

Pemamek LLC

www.pemamek.com

Welding helmets include integrated LED

light Lincoln Electric has launched the new VIKING 2450 ADV series welding helmets. The autodarkening helmets come standard with an integrated LED light that adjusts to the environment to help improve the user’s visibility of the workpiece and weld.

The operator can activate or deactivate the light using a low-profile external control. When activated, the light illuminates the work area for increased workspace visibility during arc-off time. It automatically turns off when the operator strikes an arc and turns back on when the arc is extinguished.

The optics offer 4C lens technology and a 9.3-sq.-in. viewing area.

The Lincoln Electric Co.

www.lincolnelectric.com

Cobot welding cell features intuitive programming

Fronius offers the CWC-S cobot welding cell. Its ±-0.04-mm repeatability helps ensure weld reproducibility, even in series if necessary. The company’s intelligent welding technology controls the welding process and records all welding data as required. WeldCube data management software, available as an option with the welding cell, stores and evaluates the data. Programming is intuitive, so it can be learned quickly by any welder that does not have programming experience. The operator guides the welding torch manually to the starting points, position points, and end points on the component and confirms these by pressing a button. The software uses this information to calculate the welding contours and welding sequence, while the required welding parameters also can be selected quickly using predefined characteristics.

Fronius

www.fronius.com

Buffing wheels require minimal buffing compound

Saint-Gobain Abrasives has introduced Norton FAB (fixed abrasive buff) buffing wheels that nearly eliminate the need to use buffing compounds. The wheels feature a patentpending design that incorporates abrasive grains into the buffing wheel for results with Ra values from 1 to 5.

The wheels are tear-resistant and waterproof. Their silicon carbide abrasive is dispersed uniformly and applied to both sides of the cloth for consistent buffing. Applications include automatic or semiautomatic buffing, cut buffing, and mush buffing of hard alloys and soft metals such as aluminum and brass.

Saint-Gobain Abrasives

www.nortonabrasives.com

Vision system added to cobot

Productive Robotics has introduced a new, automated vision system for its 7-axis OB7 collaborative robot. The operator shows the cobot what to pick up, pushes one button, and the OB Vision learns to recognize the object and pick it up in one automated step.

The sighted cobot eliminates the need for fixtures and staging parts for pickup, and it enables manufacturers to produce more complex parts, the company states. It can perform vision-based bin picking and provide sensitive gripping for handling delicate parts.

Productive Robotics Inc.

www.productiverobotics.com

Actuator designed for heavy loads

Bosch Rexroth has introduced the EMC-HP series compact, low-maintenance electromechanical cylinder for loads up to 100 kN. Featuring a planetary screw assembly, it has oil-bath lubrication and optional water cooling directly on the screw to help provide continuous power even under harsh conditions.

The actuator comes in three sizes with protection class IP65 for heavy-duty applications with strokes up to 1,500 mm, from pressing, forming, and riveting to tensioning, positioning, and testing.

Bosch Rexroth

www.boschrexroth-us.com

DoAll robotics light from Smart Vision Lights combines six light types, 32 control channels

Smart Vision Lights offers the patented DoAll machine vision light for robotic inspection and complex assembly applications. The light combines a dome light, two angles of dark field illumination, NIR and RGBW ring lights, and a four-quadrant multispectral ring light with 32 control channels.

Initially designed to be mounted at the end of a robotic arm, the light includes multiple ports for connecting auxiliary lights, such as spots or linear lights. It is part of an illumination system that includes a sequencing controller, a four-zone multidrive LED driver, and cables.

Smart Vision Lights

smartvisionlights.com

MKS Instruments’ Ophir BeamPeek measurement system includes cooling cartridge

MKS Instruments Inc. has introduced the Ophir BeamPeek, an integrated beam analysis and power measurement system for real-time measurement of lasers in additive manufacturing chambers. It provides simultaneous beam profiling, focal spot analysis, and power measurement in 3 seconds. There is no need for water or fan cooling, as the system includes a replaceable passive cooling beam dump tray that eliminates downtime between measurement sessions.

The system is suitable for service testing of additive manufacturing chamber powder beds. It can withstand chamber conditions, including the presence of metal powder residuals and when cooling agents or airflow connection points are not available.

MKS Instruments Inc.

www.ophiropt.com
