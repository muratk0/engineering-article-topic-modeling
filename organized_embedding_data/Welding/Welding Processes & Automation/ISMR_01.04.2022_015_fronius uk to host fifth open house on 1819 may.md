# Fronius UK to host fifth Open House on 18/19 May

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Fronius UK will be opening the doors to its Technology Centre in Milton Keynes to unveil its latest welding technology as it hosts its fifth Open House on 18/19 May 2022 (from 9:30am until 3:30pm on both days). It has put together a wide variety of demonstrations across its range of products, solutions and applications and a programme of talks from specially selected guest speakers and industry partners.

“This is a real opportunity to see up close the most recent advances in welding technology, including the new iWave Multiprocess Pro, launched this January. In addition to all the TIG functions, users can weld all MIG/MAG process variants with a single system. If they want to weld faster, with less rework, they can try out the TransSteel Pulse range of MIG/MAG welding machines and never have problems welding those hard to reach areas again with the new Fronius Flex-Drive. Visitors will hear from an expert on the development and possibilities of Wire+Arc Additive Manufacturing (WAAM), plus see some automation and robotic welding solutions," Fronius explained.

The new Magic Cleaner 150/300, for reworking of TIG weld seams and stainless steel surfaces in a single step, will also be showcased. The electrochemical cleaning devices make it possible for users to polish welds to a lustrous finish and permanently print logos and serial numbers on workpieces.

“The day will also incorporate our other two business units: Solar Energy and Perfect Charging. With the imminent increase in energy prices, visitors will be able to look at our solar products and services. These include the hybrid solar inverter that recently won first place for efficiency in Energy Storage Inspection 2021 and our robust project inverter for high yields, the Fronius Tauro. If an operation uses any electric forklifts or warehouse trucks, our intelligent battery chargers can reduce energy costs and CO₂ emissions," added the manufacturer.
