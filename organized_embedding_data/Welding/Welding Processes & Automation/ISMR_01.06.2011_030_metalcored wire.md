# METALCORED WIRE

[TARİH: 01.06.2011 ISMR]

COREWELD 46 LS is a new generation metal cored wire based on ESAB’s cored wire surface technology. It has been developed for the welding of thin plate with a minimum thickness of 1.0mm and provides fabricators with minimal silica islands and a substantially faster and higher quality welding solution compared to solid MAG wire.

“The many advantages relative to solid wire relate to the extremely wide spray arc parameter envelope that starts as low as 160A. Switching from solid wire to Coreweld 46 LS will, in most cases, require no change in the positioning of the welding gun so the conversion time is limited to the optimisation of welding parameters,” said the company.

Coreweld 46 LS is available in 225kg Marathon Pac bulk drums for downtime savings compared with using standard 300mm diameter spools. FOR MORE INFORMATION, e-mail info@esab.co.uk
