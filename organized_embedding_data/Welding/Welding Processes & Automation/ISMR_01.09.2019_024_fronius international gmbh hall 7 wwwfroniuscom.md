# FRONIUS INTERNATIONAL GmbH HALL 7 www.fronius.com

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

FRONIUS WILL BE launching its TPS/i Steel Edition for manual steel welding at Blechexpo in Stuttgart.

"The Steel Edition enables users to carry out welding tasks on steel workpieces with material thicknesses ranging from one millimetre and above. A highlight is the Pulse Controlled Spray Arc feature, which is particularly suited to small gaps, fillet welds and root passes. It causes considerably less welding spatter which, in turn, significantly reduces the amount of rework required," explained Fronius.

The WPS package is designed to eliminate the time-consuming process of creating welding procedure specifications. This is particularly beneficial for companies who must comply with the European standard, DIN EN 1090. To document the welding process, the TPS/i Steel Edition is also equipped with the WeldCube Light data recording and analysis system, which records information about every weld.

## KEMPPI OY HALL 7 www.kemppi.com/en-US/discover/mastertig/

KEMPPI HAS LAUNCHED a renewed MasterTig product family for AC and DC TIG and MMA welding. The new product range includes power variants in 230 and 300 A models, which will be complemented with 400 and 500 A models in 2020.

"The modular structure allows users to build the perfect set-up for their requirements, choosing from a range of power models, user interfaces, remote controls, transport carts and our new Flexlite TX TIG torches," said the Finnish welding specialist

"The MasterTig's high-technology features are designed to enhance TIG welding. For example, the MicroTack function is ideal for multiple, repetitive tacking on thin sheet applications, where a consistent and controlled appearance is paramount. The Double Pulse function combines alternative pulse cycles and improves welding speed by up to 30%. Optima AC is a customised waveform that improves welding quality and reduces the noise level in AC TIG welding by 20%," it added.
