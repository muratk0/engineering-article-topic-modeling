# MIG/MAG wire feeder

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

THE drive 4X wire feeder is a new development by EWM Group, the Mündersbach-based welding machine manufacturer. With this innovation, the user receives all components of the MIG/MAG welding process chain from one source: from the power source to the wire feeder with newly developed wire feed mechanism to the welding torch. This includes the filler materials.

“If the quality of all the components is aligned, this means the highest quality for the weld seam. The 4X drive uses innovative MULTIMATRIX technology designed to save money, secure jobs and protect the environment,” EWM explained.

The core is the new wire feed mechanism. Each wire feed roller is driven by its own cogwheel. As these four cogwheels are interlocked with the drive shaft, the wire feed rollers run synchronously. The driving shafts of the four wire feed rollers are each mounted on two ball bearings and are therefore especially long-life and fatigue-endurable, compared to the generally used wear-prone sliding bearings.

Two set-screws can be used to adjust the contact pressure of the roller pair simply and individually – to match the material used. With a diameter of 37mm, each wire feed roller is comparably large. The wire-supporting areas are also large and the pressure on the wire is very even. This stabilises the transport.

Different filler metals and different wire diameters require appropriate wire feed roller sets which each consist of four wire feed rollers of the same kind. The wire feed rollers are colour-coded and available for wire diameters from 0.8 to 1.6mm. V-grooves are suitable for steel, stainless steel, flux cored and solder wires. U-grooves are suitable for feeding aluminium materials. The colour codes allow a clear distinction between the ten different roller packages to rule out any confusion. The wire is always fed correctly and precisely.

“The roller exchange is a special highlight. Rollers can be replaced safely and without tools. As nothing is dismantled, there are no parts that can be lost. The roller holder is unlocked and folded open without removing it from the wire feeder. The rollers are then exchanged and the roller holder is closed and locked. Thanks to interior lighting, this can also be done where lighting conditions are poor,” commented EWM.

As standard, the drive 4X is equipped with sliding rails. The optional wheel-mounting kit enables the device to run on wheels. A lifting eye is provided for transport with a crane – with an additional protective plate for especially rough use.

All connections for the wire feed mechanism are easily accessible from the outside, shielded by a protective flap. Intermediate hose packages are connected without tools and without the need for expert personnel. This is of particular benefit at construction sites, for frequent re-tooling and when transporting the device.

With the drive 4X, there is just one machine-independent, universal control unit for all MULTIMATRIX MIG/MAG welding machines of EWM’s alpha Q, Phoenix and Taurus Synergic S series. The MULTIMATRIX control unit has a stand-by mode that saves energy during welding breaks. Essential information, such as the job number that indicates the current welding job, the kilowatt indication for calculation of the energy input per unit length and the motor current of the wire feed mechanism for improved operational safety, is visible at a glance.
