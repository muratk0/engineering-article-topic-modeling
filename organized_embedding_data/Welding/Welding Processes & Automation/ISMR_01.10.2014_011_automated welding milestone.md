# Automated welding milestone

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

OVER 150 guests attended the festive opening of AWL-Techniek CZ Inc.’s new premises on 18 September 2014. The building in Napajedla, Czech Republic, bears a noticeable resemblance to the company’s headquarters in Harderwijk, the Netherlands.

AWL’s Chief Executive Officer, Piet Mosterd, said: “This new building means that AWL CZ is ready for the future, and we are another step further in our quest to put our customers at the forefront of automated welding. AWL CZ will be able to make a substantial contribution to the growth of the organisation.”

Totalling more than 21,500 sq. ft., the fully equipped offices and workshop accommodate everything from cell and fixture assembly to 3D measurement, from PLC and robot software integration to electrical machine controllers and welding cell solutions. In addition, it has a mobile laboratory for testing welding quality.

Just like its headquarters in the Netherlands and AWL CN in China, AWL CZ applies the LEAN philosophy and 5S principle. As a result, the building’s layout is highly efficient, which both minimises waste and ensures well-regulated operations. Plans to build a second workshop are now underway and should be completed in 2015 or 2016. ■
