# FOCUS ON SUSTAINABILITY

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

The Flexlite GF product series consists of several MIG/MAG welding guns with a fume extraction function. The vacuum system extracts the welding fumes directly from the gun nozzle without disturbing the shielding gas flow. The MIG gun collects welding fumes as soon as they are generated and before they reach the welder's breathing zone.

Lower fatigue has a positive effect on welder's work and results in better welding quality since the welder spends around 20% of his/her working day holding a welding gun The Flexlite GF welding gun's anatomically designed shape and balance reduce wrist loading, is designed to allow the welder to achieve 'the perfect weld', according to Kemppi.

Flexlite TX welding torches are designed for use with MasterTig welding equipment. The torch range includes several neck models. It won the Red Dot award for industrial design in 2020. Available in alternative power classes and lengths, the torch range includes different neck versions for water and gas-cooled models. On-torch remote control options enable accurate and immediate welding current adjustment at a distance.

## An accent on sustainability

Meeting our own needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs is a key tenet of sustainability. This has become even more important with the march to Net Zero, climate change goals and the focus on global emissions reductions.

"Sustainability is very important to us. We focus on longer lifecycles and the higher quality/durability of our products. We create a win-win situation by doing this. On the one hand, this creates long-term cost savings for our customers (as they need to buy less equipment) and it also creates material and resource savings. We have a responsibility towards our environment - we live in a very "throwaway society and we do our best not to be part of that," Hegemann told ISMR.

"We chose this sustainable design approach for our guns and torches a long time ago and believe even more strongly now that this was the right choice. Although price pressure in the market is steadily increasing and it can be easier to develop a new product that can be thrown away, this is not a sustainable approach and we do not endorse it. Long lifecycle and high quality are part of our DNA and will continue to drive our innovation and product development in the future," he added.

Sustainability is also a personal goal for Hegemann, who studied the subject as part of his Master's thesis. It relies on three principles that are ecological, economic and social.

"With a 'build to last' approach, our equipment pays off for our customers who can also increase their productivity by using wellperforming, high-end equipment whilst saving resources. Together with our customers, we are trying to build a better future for society, commented Hegemann.

## The next generation of welders

Europe is the main market for Kemppi's guns and torches business unit. Hegemann also highlighted Australian and Asian markets, with China as an important, but also competitive, market.

"Target industry sectors for us include construction; shipyards; general engineering and infrastructure projects. In terms of growing markets, we see strong potential in the renewables sector, and the need for more lightweight TIG-welded parts. We also see future growth in the automotive market, with its shift towards e-mobility and lighter welded parts," commented Hegemann.

However, social responsibility and the need to attract a younger workforce to the welding industry also fuels Kemppi initiatives. It supports, as a partner, the EuroSkills welding skills competition which takes place every two years. The Finnish specialist also has a long history of teaming with schools, colleges and universities across the world to help train a new generation of welders.

"With the current lack of skilled welders globally, we also feel that it is part of our responsibility to motivate young people to join this business and become welders. We can supply the equipment to make their job safe and easy. Welding is a great industry - you see the tangible results of what you create every day. Wages are also lucrative. The industry offers great career prospects. I see this as a goal for our business unit. There will always be a future for manual welders; robotics and automation will not be able to do every job," concluded Hegemann.

## The Flexlite family

Flexlite GX is a series of MIG/MAG welding guns for professionals. Flexlite GX welding guns are available in alternative power classes and lengths, including different neck types. Flexlite TX is a series of welding torches for TIG welding. Its innovative design and ergonomic handles are shaped to the hand. Flexlite TX welding torches are available in different neck types, which provide good ergonomics for welding in all positions. The Flexlite GF product series consists of several MIG/MAG welding guns with a fume extraction function. The Flexlite GC product series contains five lightweight and robust entry-level welding gun models for MIG/MAG welding processes.

## Kemppi supports EuroSkills competition

Kemppi was a partner at the EuroSkills vocational competition, presenting the achievements of young, talented and skilled welding professionals (up to 26 years old). The event took place in Graz, Austria, in September, attracting 450 participants. Kemppi provided the welding equipment and had several stands around the venue area, guiding and hosting the competitors and participants during the event. "We support EuroSkills 2021 because it is a great way of encouraging young people and the event audience into the welding profession using Kemppi Red Dot award-winning equipment," said Kemppi.
