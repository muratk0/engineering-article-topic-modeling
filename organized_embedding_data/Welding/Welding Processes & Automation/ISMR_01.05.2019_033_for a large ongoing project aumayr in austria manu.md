Aumayr, based in Austria, specialises in custom-made ventilation pipe systems. The company has been involved in a number of prestigious projects, such as 'Europe's most modern music theatre' in Linz. For a large ongoing project, Aumayr manufactured around 1000 running metres of ventilation pipe components – an impressive quantity handled with the help of the ArcRover 22 from Fronius.

Aumayr is one of Austria's most well-known manufacturers of ventilation pipe systems, with approximately 300 employees working at three locations in Austria and the Czech Republic. The family-run company, founded in Linz in 1965, has a pool of equipment for metalworking, featuring computer-controlled laser cutting and stamping machines; servoelectric bending machines and other high-tech systems. Its equipment, together with its in-house design and development department, enables the company to develop and produce custom-made products 'from scratch'.

This innovative metalworking approach means that Aumayr can manufacture ventilation pipes that, it told ISMR, 'conventional ventilation manufacturers might struggle to produce'. At the start of 2018, the company received a large order for welded stainless steel ventilation pipes. Even as the order was being calculated, it became clear that new alternatives to its previously manual welding process would be required due to the hundreds of running metres of weld seam and the customer's stringent quality demands. Above all, sheet metal thicknesses of up to four millimetres had to be welded without warping and with consistently high quality to create ventilation pipe components that were sometimes as long as six metres, as high as 2.5 metres and as wide as 1.5 metres.

## An eye on quality and cost

The key aims, when the ventilation components were being manufactured, were to employ a mechanised welding process, achieve perfect weld seam quality in all components and optimise costs by eliminating the need for reworking and working at a higher welding speed.

Fronius Perfect Welding is a specialist in arc and resistance spot welding and is a global market leader for robot assisted welding. As a system provider, Fronius Welding Automation also offers customer-specific automated complete welding solutions in areas such as container construction up to cladding for the offshore sector. Power sources for manual applications, welding accessories and services are also in its portfolio.

Fronius International GmbH is an Austrian company with headquarters in Pettenbach and other sites in Wels, Thalheim, Steinhaus and Sattledt. With 4,550 employees worldwide, the company is active in welding technology, photovoltaics and battery charging technology. 91% of its products are exported through 30 international Fronius subsidiaries and sales partners/ representatives in over 60 countries.
