# New Siegmund Workstation

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 The Fabricator]

Perfectly suitable for working with cobots and robots

The expanded working surface is suitable for welding with cobots and robots. You can easily store the welding and robot accessories in the Workstation and have enough space on the surface for your welding projects.

NEW

Isolation Plate

Made of hard paper for insulation between Workstation and robot. Suitable for system 16 and 28 – Imperial Series. On the top side the robot foot-specific drilling pattern can be attached itself – so the insulation plate fits for any robot or cobot. Plate dimensions: 7-5/8″ × 9-7/16″ × 1″

Bigger dimension of the worktop

The dimension of the Workstation plate of 4’x4’ provides you plenty of space to place a robot or cobot on it.

The worktop is plasma nitrided and perforated. The grid on the surface is 2″ × 2″ and the boreholes Ø 16 mm (5/8″).
