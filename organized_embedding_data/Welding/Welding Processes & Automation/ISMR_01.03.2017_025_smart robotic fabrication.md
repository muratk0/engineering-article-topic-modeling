# Smart robotic fabrication

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

DOPPELMAYR HAS BEEN using IGM robots for ropeway construction for many years. When it reconstructed its production hall, the manufacturing system for the automatic welding of its transport frame products was re-specified. The focus was placed on simplified system construction, a reduction of manpower/cycle times and an increase in fabrication quality.

"The previous robot cell was only set up for automatic welding. Assembling, tacking and feeding was done manually. These manufacturing steps were therefore replaced by a fully automatic Industry 4.0-ready overall solution featuring one handling and two welding robots. To reduce the cycle time and improve the quality of the end product, a re-design of the transport frames as well as a reduction in the variation of frame types had to be arranged. The changes led to fewer component parts and simpler, more continuous welding seams (fewer start-/stop-points), as well as shorter search drives," IGM told ISMR.

Using smart robotic fabrication, it was possible for Doppelmayr to manufacture five different frame types. To handle the different geometries of the various components, a fully automatic gripper exchange system enabled the application of five different gripper types. After the welding stages, an automatic measurement stage was integrated for quality control, where each part is checked against its given tolerance range. Finally, welding spatters were removed and the complete part can be palletised."

In this project, a semi-automatic manufacturing process has been replaced by a fully automated process which is programmable offline. Doppelmayr's production rate, said IGM, has risen to 2500 transport frames per year. The reduction of manpower (two hours per part), it added, has led to an overall reduction of 5000 hours per year.
