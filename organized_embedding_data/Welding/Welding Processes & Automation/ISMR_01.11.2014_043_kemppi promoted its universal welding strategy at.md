# Kemppi promoted its universal welding strategy at this year’s EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Kemppi is a global welding technology company manufacturing arc welding equipment and providing solutions for highly productive welding. In 65 years of operation, Kemppi has introduced new innovations to the market, pioneering the development of welding technology and solutions. In 2013, the company posted global revenues of 111 million euros.

Kemppi production plants are located in Finland and India. The company has over 650 employees. It is also the first manufacturer of welding solutions in the world certified to ISO 3834-2.

## Universal welding strategy

Management of welding procedures, welder qualifications and welding equipment fleet results in high welding production performance, EBIT growth, cost competitiveness and a reduction in lead time. Kemppi ARC System 3 is a new modular software solution to reach a new level of welding production management. The tailored toolbox enables the customer to choose modules for developing any part of the production process e.g. quality, project/documentation management or productivity. The system can be installed in arc welding machines of any make or brand. Access to all relevant welding data enables better decision-making and improvements in quality and productivity.

Kemppi ARC System 3 is part of Kemppi’s new TWXM total welding management solution concept. TWXM enables welding quality, safety and productivity management according to customer specific needs. The offering was completed with WeldEye®, welding quality and documentation software by Norwegian Weldindustry AS, acquired by Kemppi in December 2013.

“In general, we are promoting our universal welding strategy here on our EuroBLECH booth,” explained Frederic Lanz, VP Marketing and Sales, Kemppi to ISMR. “Customers appreciate our strategy of focusing on a higher scale of development, not just hardware but also software. Whether you are talking about equipment, WPSs or validation of welding equipment, the Kemppi ARC System 3 is a unique welding management system that can work with Kemppi equipment as well as a range of other brands. You will therefore, unusually, see a range of other manufacturers’ equipment on our booth here at EuroBLECH to demonstrate this level of interoperability. ARC System 3 is based on open source software – it is the ‘Android’ of welding software.

“The return on investment for the ARC System 3 software is usually about six months and operators then benefit from further cost savings. Rather than guessing at welding productivity levels, the software manages welding production in a professional way to record all information about welding operations – how many welds are made, qualification-type, when does a welder have to re-qualify, how are the WPSs managed, quality levels etc. A manager can see straight away, on a tablet, if a welder is struggling. We keep it simple and easy to enable welding production, and welders, to be managed in a way that really makes a difference to costs and overall welding quality and productivity. This strategy differentiates us from our competitors and sends a strong message to our customers.”

## An eye on 2014

Kemppi is enjoying a good year in 2014. It has 15 subsidiaries globally and is not dependent upon any single markets. It is continuously developing its new software versions with a large choice of new modules to accommodate different process requirements from its customers. Lanz sees strong growth for the company in Asia Pacific markets (India, China, South East Asia, Australia etc.) although its home market is in Europe.

“Recently, we won the biggest manual welding contract in China for a large shipyard – 551 welding power sources. It is thought to be the biggest manual welding deal in China this year so we were delighted to win that. We have already started delivery across three phases for this contract.”

Kemppi has also upgraded its powerful Minarc Evo equipment range. Minarc Evo 180 now delivers more MMA/TIG welding power than its predecessor, the Minarc Evo 150, serving ‘welders on the move’. The portable 5.8 kg machine delivers 170 amps maximum output current for MMA welding at 30% duty cycle and 180 amps for DC TIG welding at 35% duty cycle. When fitted with a 10-amp power supply plug (market dependent), the Minarc Evo 180 still delivers 140 amps MMA welding power at 28% duty cycle.

In a separate initiative, Kemppi Oy also joined forces with ABB Robotics to develop fully equipped robotic welding systems/packages. ABB has indicated that the design of the welding packages are ‘modular, cost-effective projects for companies to easily install or upgrade into a robotic welding system’. Kemppi equipment will be featured at all certified ABB Robotics welding lab locations, enabling customers to conduct robotic welding trials on the latest equipment available. These joint business ventures cover Europe, Asia, Africa and South America.
