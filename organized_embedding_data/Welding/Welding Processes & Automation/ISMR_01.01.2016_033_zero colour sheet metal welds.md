# Zero colour sheet metal welds

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

FOR WELDING sheet metal joints on titanium and stainless steels, Trailing Shields® can be attached to the welding torch to leave a bright shiny weld underneath. These accessories, designed and manufactured by Huntingdon Fusion Techniques HFT®, can eliminate scrap and prevent the need for expensive and timeconsuming cleaning processes.

“Trailing Shields® are small devices that easily connect to any TIG, MIG or Plasma (GTAW, GMAW, PAW) welding torch. These extra lightweight devices are easily carried along with the welding torch without any drag. The silicone side shields simply seal against the metal to prevent argon escaping and to prevent the entry of any oxygen or nitrogen from the surrounding atmosphere,” said the manufacturer. As well as the inert argon gas coming through the weld torch nozzle, there is an extra argon supply to the Trailing Shield® which prevents oxidation or discolouration of the welded joint and its adjacent heataffected zone. The use of Trailing Shields® ensure that the weld stays under a protective argon gas coverage, while the hot weld metal is cooling, preventing it from oxidising on contact with air. Titanium and stainless steels are chosen for their corrosion-resistant properties and, if they are then allowed to oxidise during the weld cycle, expensive post welding cleaning techniques become necessary.

“Our range of Trailing Shields® are available in flat format for sheet metal welding or in curved format for welding circular items like tubes, pipes and tanks. Use of Argweld® Trailing Shields® will allow much faster welding, offering savings in production costs. One simple low cost Trailing Shield® will prevent oxidation, reduce gas costs and eliminate expensive post weld cleaning, giving payback in less than one weld,” explained Georgia Gascoyne, CEO, Huntingdon Fusion Techniques HFT®.

As well as the shields for manual welding there are longer and wider versions for automatic welding. These allow faster welding without losing any weld-shielding properties. ■

## Sheet metal forming from concept to production

ESI GROUP, a solutions provider in Virtual Prototyping for manufacturing industries, has launched the latest version of its sheet metal forming simulation and die face design solution, PAM-STAMP 2015. Addressing the needs of OEMs and tier-suppliers in the automotive, aerospace and heavy industries, PAM-STAMP predicts the outcome of complete sheet metal forming processes.

“For manufacturers, this means saved time and cost throughout the entire product development cycle – from conceptual design to try-out and production. For tooling suppliers, PAM-STAMP enables production die engineering by providing high quality, predictive simulation to support styling of outer panels and also the development of new lightweight structural parts,” explained Harald Porzner, Director of Virtual Manufacturing Product Management, ESI Group.

PAM-STAMP 2015 features enhanced topology checking and geometry clean-up and repair to enable efficient geometry-based dieface design. With Die Starter, a new technology to generate the die surfaces needed to create a part, it is possible (in the styling and design phase) to automatically generate the die tools and validate the forming results. The same technology can also be used in the concept development phase to automatically generate a starting point for refined die face design, saving a lot of iterations.

“By accurately accounting for all aspects of the stamping process, PAM-STAMP enables full feasibility assessment early in the process. It supports tooling and design engineers from concept through to selection of tooling methods to try-out and final production, predicting the outcome of every step in the manufacturing chain before making any real prototypes. PAM-STAMP’s results can be easily transported to the ESI Virtual Performance Solution so that product performance models properly reflect the ‘as manufactured’ components,” concluded the manufacturer, ESI. ■
