# 50 year anniversary for igm Robotersysteme AG

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

AUSTRIAN WELDING ROBOT specialist, igm Robotersysteme AG, is celebrating its 50th anniversary this year, with subsidiaries and partner companies in more than 20 countries on five continents. Over 700 employees in sales and production facilities look after the development, sales and maintenance of customised welding robot systems (from single robot systems to complex FMS assembly lines). Offering single robot systems up to complex FMS lines as well as pick-and-place machines, its portfolio covers the spectrum of robotic systems.

Fifty years ago, in 1967, the company was founded as a trading house for welding products. One year later, it started building custom machines for automated MIG welding in small workshops in Vienna, followed by relocation to a building at its current site in Wiener Neudorf, Austria in 1975. It then decided to build its own automated welding system with computer control. Within a year, it had developed the "Limat 2000" welding robot with continuous path control.

In 1982/83, it developed its first 5- and 6-axis articulated arm robot and was awarded with the Austrian "Staatswappen" ("National Coat of Arms") for this development. In 2011, it launched a new RTi400 robot series, with six to eight rotational axes and even faster slim arms, built for MIG/MAG 1- and 2-wire welding or for TIG high-performance or narrow-gap welding. Milestones include the development of the first articulated robot (RT280) in 1983 as well as the new RTi robot generation with K5 PC-based control in 2000. In 2008, the iCAM laser camera system was launched and, in 2011, the development of the RTi400 modular robot series followed.

"We will keep working on new developments. Our offline-programming system, with modular software packages, will be further developed in the future to minimise the programming effort for a more timesaving process. In addition, new market opportunities, in regions such as Mexico and the Middle East, will be investigated," explained the company. See www.igm.at
