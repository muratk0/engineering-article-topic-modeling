Adaptive resistance welding controller offers fast commissioning

PRC7000 high-speed automated welding controllers from Bosch Rexroth are designed for automotive manufacturers and others using high-throughput automated welding systems.

According to the manufacturer, the controllers provide reliable, high weld spot quality and precise adjustment of the welding current to help eliminate spatter and reworking. Software tools and a modular design help enable commissioning up to 90% faster than other systems, states the company.

The units can store up to 10,000 welding programs that can be easily selected and built via a drag-and-drop graphical interface to program more sophisticated welding sequences that can have 100 or more separate heat blocks. Each heat block can be configured to handle specific weld criteria.

The system also features a new, Industry 4.0-ready dual-processor architecture for both process control and communications with higher-level automation systems. This design enables high data transfer rates without affecting the process control.

Software advances CAD/CAM for metal fabricating

Cambrio has introduced version 22 of its SigmaNEST software suite. It includes improvements to all areas of the SigmaSUITE product portfolio, including CAD/CAM nesting for router, bevel, punch, and cut-to-length, and it also expands Connected Shop capabilities for metal fabricators.

The redesigned installer detects all existing products for upgrading. Import capabilities have been improved for the latest native CAD formats, such as sketch association to SigmaNEST operations for NX and 3D part property mapping for Solid-Works. In addition, the SigmaNEST shape library has been improved to enable fast parametric geometry edits for more efficient CAD programming.

The software offers options to change the sheet names in the feature tree, granular control over hatching density for reporting visibility, and multiview layout sequencing. Users also can select the correct machine bed drawing directly from the machine parameters config menu to see their stock in relation to the bed size and slat locations.

A graphical indicator lets users validate tab locations for the entire nest before sending the NC program to production. Additionally, part reporting capabilities have been extended with support for part process parameters such as amperage and gas type, improved visual aids for nest detail text, and the ability to highlight mirrored parts with hatching styles.

GTAW power source simplifies switching among welding tasks

The iWave, a modular GTAW power source from Fronius, offers multiprocess capability on all weldable materials for switching among different welding tasks. The series is available from 190 to 500 amps.

The CycleTIG option provides for maximum control over the arc and targeted heat input. Other features include improved ignition control and greater ease of use with intuitive operation. The Multiprocess PRO option gives the user unlimited access to all GMAW processes in power categories from 300 amps.

The unit can be upgraded to cold metal transfer, pulse multicontrol, and low spatter control welding packages.

Fronius

www.fronius.com

Intensifier pump delivers 95% uptime

Flow Intl. Corp. has introduced the MotoJet X intensifier pump for its waterjet cutting systems. According to the company, the pump achieves 95% uptime and long life.

With its sound-deadening design, the pump operates quietly at 60,000 PSI with both 30- and 50-HP options. Features include quick-remove side panels, an airassisted lid, and an automatic interior light. A light built into the lid handle shows the IoT-enabled pump’s operating status.

Flow Intl. Corp.

www.flowwaterjet.com

Biodegradable penetrant frees frozen parts without VOCs

Renewable Lubricants offers Bio-Blast, a fast-acting, biodegradable penetrant that eradicates rust and dirt to free corroded nuts, bolts, and other frozen components quickly. A patented blend of additives and biosolvents provides a fast-penetrating, nonstaining catalyst for ferrous and nonferrous metals.

Fast capillary wetting action reduces surface tension, freeing up frozen parts quickly. There is no direct loss of the penetrant into the water, soil, or work environment. The nonflammable, noncombustible, nontoxic formulation contains no hazardous VOCs.

AI In Welding: The Beginning of a New Era

Sponsored by:

In this webinar, Soroush Karimzadeh, CEO & Co-Founder of Novarc Technologies shows you how NovEye™, Novarc’s AI-powered weld monitoring and control software further improves pipe welding quality and productivity by increasing the autonomy of their welding cobot.

By monitoring and making real-time corrections to the welding operation, NovEye increases the autonomy of the cobots, freeing the operator to work on other tasks.

Using years of data collection and machine learning, this giant piece of software ties into the control system of the cobot and controls the weld pool the same way human welders would, allowing the boring, repetitive welds that don’t require the higher skill of experienced welders to be delegated to the robot.
