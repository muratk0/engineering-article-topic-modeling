Online strip width calculator designed for welded tube and pipe

JMC Rollmasters Div. of Johnston Mfg. Co. Inc. has developed the Advanced Strip Width Calculator™ for tube and pipe producers. The online calculator uses mathematic-and metallurgic-based algorithms to estimate thinning caused by bending deformation, as well as girth reduction effects in the fin passes.

The calculator’s SyncIt™ feature allows the user to synchronize the formula with specific product requirements and operational preferences. Once set, editable parameters are saved automatically for future reference. Internal text files within the program help the user get started. Clickable question buttons provide answers to SyncIt questions.

Newly developed steel offers high yield, tensile strengths

Ovako has introduced Hybrid Steel

®

, which offers the properties of tool steel, maraging steel, and stainless steel, as well as the production economy of engineering steel. It takes its name from the simultaneous hardening by both carbides and intermetallic precipitations.

According to the company, the steel has three times the yield and tensile strengths of conventional steel at temperatures up to 500 degrees C. These properties make the material suitable for use in highly stressed applications such as engine components, bearings, and tools.

Because the steel develops its full properties through heat treatment at temperatures that cause a very low distortion, a manufacturer can machine a component to its final dimensions with the material in a softer condition and then heat treat it to achieve its full strength.

Upgrades added to automated barrier panel

Rite-Hite Machine Guarding has reintroduced its Vertiguard automated barrier panel with several new upgrades. The bottom-up pneumatic panel provides physical separation between machine operators and manufacturing processes. It is suitable for small interaction points with single or double robotic cells, part assembly, and conveyor openings.

The panel offers a compact footprint and protects workers from application-driven hazards. The pneumatically operated, vertically rising system also allows for overhead crane loading. In addition, the low-profile design helps prevent back injuries, since it requires minimal reach-in to load parts.

The standard size has been increased to 110 by 96 in. (from 72 by 86 in.). Other upgrades include improved finger guard, new brand cylinder and flange mount with center support, improved sliding panel design, improved interlock switch brackets and additional switch options, and galvanized steel back panel and cylinder cover.

Plate bender can function like four-roll or three-roll machine

C Marshall Fabrication Machinery offers the MG Synthesis System four-roll plate bending machine. It includes the standard motorization of the two central rolls; however, the side rolls can turn freely like a standard four-roll machine or be motorized like a three-roll machine as required.

On a traditional three-roll bending machine, without the fourth roll securely pinching the plate, the rotation reference point may be lost because of plate slippage. On this new machine, however, the lower central roll ascends and pinches the plate to prevent slippage.

On traditional four-roll machines, during the rerolling phase, the lower central roll is not used and only the top roll remains powered while the side rolls spin freely. However, on the new machine, the operator can engage the sideroll motorization with the turn of a key and work with the top and side rolls powered.
