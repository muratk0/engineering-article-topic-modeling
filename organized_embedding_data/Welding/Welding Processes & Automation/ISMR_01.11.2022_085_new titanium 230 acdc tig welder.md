# New Titanium 230 AC/DC TIG welder

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Following the successful launch of the Titanium AC/DC TIG welder, a sister product has now been launched by GYS, the singlephase Titanium 230 AC/DC. Intelligent and connected, it offers precise arc control and industrial welding performance of up to 230A (16A fuse) using integrated power factor correction. It also has a duty cycle of 60% at plus or minus 40 degrees Celsius and is equipped with a degauss feature to demagnetise metal parts.

"The Titanium 400 was the most advanced TIG welding machine that we had ever produced and one of the most capable machines on the market. The new Titanium 230 brings the same array of advanced welding functionality, performance and excellent user experience to single-phase welding," said GYS.

"It offers users six TIG welding modes including standard, pulse, fast pulse and a mix of AC and DC to increase the welding speed. It also features a unique TIG Wizard, enabling the creation of bespoke welding cycles and the optimisation of the type of material to be welded. It also offers five types of assembly and a patented synergic mode for maximum productivity," continued GYS.

Equipped with a 5-inch colour interface, which is intuitive, multilingual and customisable, users can choose from three interface options: Easy, Expert and Advanced. Offering full connectivity, it measures energy usage and provides traceability of welding data. It is also connectable for use with robots via an optional kit. The user mode includes the option to share the product with several different operators. Back-up of user jobs as well as machine configuration are both possible via a USB key, as are new and revised welding synergies and software updates.

A free tool for interface customisation is available online. Users can customise the machine by saving their welding settings and personal preferences. This is beneficial when shift workers use the same machine. Hire and rental companies can also customise the machine's settings for each customer to restrict access to certain modes and features.■
