# Miniature stud welder

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

AS A SPECIALIST in fasteners and connection technology with over 45 years of experience, Heinz Soyer Bolzenschweißtechnik GmbH will be exhibiting its BMK-12i stud welding inverter with SRM-Technologie® (Studwelding in Radially Symmetrical Magnetic Field) at Schweisstec 2015 in Stuttgart. After its launch in 2011, and following years of intensive research and development work, the second generation stud welder, in miniature format, is now ready for the market and series production.

“With its GS mark for certified quality and safety by TÜV-Rheinland, it has been possible to completely fulfil the requirements for health and safety in the workplace for this range of devices,” explained SOYER.

"This small, light and strong stud welder in pocket format weighs just 6.8kg and, with a welding capacity of 800 amps, is suitable for weld studs up to a diameter of 12mm. It does not require ceramic rings when welding and produces high-strength welded joints without annoying flanges, welding beads or welding splatter by using conventional inert gases (e.g. mixed gas 82 per cent argon/18 per cent CO2). This enables the user to use the stud thread right up to the base material.”
