# Protection and visibility

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Kemppi has announced the launch of its new Zeta welding and grinding helmet product range for full-face protection with lightweight design and advanced visibility features. Designed for multifunctional use where the face and eyes need protection, the Zeta helmets can be used for welding, grinding, inspection and cutting processes.

The Zeta product range includes four narrow, lightweight, stand-alone helmet models equipped with the advanced auto-darkening filter for welding or a wide visor for grinding, along with other practical features:

- Zeta G200 grinding helmet.
- Zeta G200x grinding helmet with integrated LED work lights.
- Zeta W200 welding helmet with automatic darkening welding filter (ADF).
- Zeta W200x welding helmet with ADF and integrated LED work lights.

"We have placed special focus on improved visibility and eye protection. We can see this in the significantly large viewing area of the ADF filter on the Zeta welding helmet, measuring 110mm in width and 60mm in height. The Zeta grinding helmet also features a large 200mm-high visor with a 180° horizontal view," explained Roger Rasmussen, Head, Kemppi Safety Business Unit.

The Zeta ADF is an improved version with VISION+ technology designed to increase the quality of vision and work accuracy.

"The ADF DIN 2.5 light-state means that users can enjoy sharp colours and visibility through the filter when the welding arc is inactive," explained the Finnish welding specialist.

Integrated work lights feature an automatic function that turns off the working lights when users are working in bright lighting conditions. When the arc goes off and in poor lighting conditions, the work lights automatically turn on.

"The battery pack is on the neckband, which further improves the balance of the helmet. It can be charged with a USB charger. Power levels can be checked by pushing the button outside the shell. The completely smooth inner surface of the Zeta helmet makes it easy to clean," concluded Kemppi.■
