# Protection for welders

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

THE LATEST IN personal protection for welders, the Gamma product family is now available from Kemppi Safety Product dealers. Offering protection for the eyes, face and respiratory system, Gamma also integrates work lights for clear vision and safety in low light working conditions. It aims to reduce work fatigue, increase comfort and protect from welding-related health hazards. "Our new Gamma product series is on its own level in terms of overall protection," said John Frost, product manager for personal protection equipment at Kemppi Oy.

"When used with the new powered filter unit, Gamma meets the highest European Total Inward Leakage (TIL) classification for respiratory performance, class TH3. This means that it protects users from 99.8 % of airborne fumes and particulate."

Headquartered in Lahti, Finland, Kemppi employs over 800 welding industry specialists in 17 countries and has a revenue of more than 150 MEUR. Its offering includes welding solutions – intelligent equipment, welding management software and expert services – for demanding industrial applications and ready-to-weld needs. Local expertise is available via its global partner network covering over 60 countries.

"Gamma products are designed by welding professionals for welding professionals. Model options range from standard face and eye protection to advanced high-class respiratory solutions, including a model with integrated LED work lights. They can be used in all arc-based processes, including welding, cutting and gouging, as well as in grinding and inspection work," added the company.
