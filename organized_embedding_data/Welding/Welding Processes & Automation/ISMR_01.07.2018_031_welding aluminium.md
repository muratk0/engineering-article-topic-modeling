# Welding aluminium

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

FOR MORE THAN ten years, ULMA Construction has successfully used DINSE welding torch sets for various processes in structural steel and aluminium. When welding aluminium on robotic systems, push-pull systems are of key importance for precise feeding of the welding wire which, in turn, affects the texture of the welding seam. They transport the filler material. ULMA works with the DINSE 4-roller drive, DIX FD 200M.

"The electronically-coupled drives in the wire feeder, and directly at the welding torch, ensure that the wire is reproducibly fed through the torch set. That way, especially soft and sensitive wires are protected against buckling. This ensures a stable arc and a smooth production process. The compact dimensions ensure a wide range of use, with easy component access. Weighing 1.65kg, the DIX FD 200 M is suitable for robots with low bearing capacity or hollow wrist robots with low payload," DINSE told ISMR.

"The DINSE two-circuit cooling system is particularly effective and ensures a long service life without expensive production stops. These factors provide us with a stable process," said ULMA Construction.

ULMA decided to complete the system with the liquid-cooled power sources (DIX PI 500). They can be used in combination with conventional as well as hollow wrist robots. The existing welding torch equipment can also be used. ULMA achieves good results with the special PowerPulse process, where two wire feed speeds with freely selectable frequencies can be changed periodically. By alternating between high and low energy input, an easy-to-control weld puddle is achieved. This minimises pore formation during aluminium welding and enables regular weld ripples and straightforward welding of visually appealing seams. ULMA can now supply many materials in large batches and in short production times.

"We tested and discarded numerous torch systems in advance. DINSE offered us very good cooling performance, stable wire feeding and geometries which allow us to reach difficult welding areas. The shield gas protection was also significantly better than with comparative products. With the DIX PI power sources, we have the right technology for each production section," added ULMA.
