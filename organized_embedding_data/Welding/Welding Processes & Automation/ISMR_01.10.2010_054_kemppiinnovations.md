# KEMPPIINNOVATIONS

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

The Kemppi DataStore 2 and DataGun system have revolutionised procurement of welding machines and their welding software profiles. Customers can now purchase customised welding machines without the need for unwanted pre-installed welding programs or processes.

Kemppi’s MagTrac welding tractor packages provide simple, reliable mechanisation for longitudinal welding applications. MagTrac packages include and integrate the welding power source, distance filler wire delivery system, plus applied process control as the customer wishes, through Kemppi’s Wise product portfolio and DataStore.

KempArc Pulse TCS (Tandem Control System) is designed for heavy deposition, high speed welding in robotised applications. It provides strong arc process control so that heavy, complex fabrication shapes can be welded quickly with high levels of accuracy. New Wise TCS arc software solutions have also been developed to further enhance performance.

Minarc Evo models now include 150 amps MMA, 200 amps MIG/MAG and 180 amps DC TIG models. Kemppi has also harmonised its global warranty promise - the new 2+ warranty cover includes all welding equipment manufactured and sold by Kemppi after 1 July 2010 (excluding the wearing parts) for two years from date of sale. Local warranty service is available in any country where Kemppi has representation. Cover includes parts and labour.
