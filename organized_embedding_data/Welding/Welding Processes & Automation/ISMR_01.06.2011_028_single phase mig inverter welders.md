# SINGLE PHASE MIG INVERTER WELDERS

[TARİH: 01.06.2011 ISMR]

PEARL AND PEARL XL are two new single phase, 110V/230V 13A, 50/60Hz, 6kVA, compact, portable MIG high specification welding inverters using synergic welding technology from GYS Ltd, the French welding equipment and battery charger manufacturer.

Featuring advanced GYS power management technology, both integrate Flexible Voltage (which automatically adapts to any voltage in the range from 85-265V) and Power Factor Correction (PFC) technology which substantially increases efficiency. In combination, these features overcome power supply problems caused by imperfect generators or long extension leads.

“A feature of these machines is the synergic welding technology from GYS, which includes a controlled welding current to give a very stable arc producing a smooth weld bead requiring no further finishing. To achieve this, all the welder has to do is program the current and sheet thickness, then weld. If he/she wishes, traditional manual setting of the welding parameters can be selected. On the front panel, two control knobs and a large digital screen allow the welder to easily set the required parameters,” said GYS.

The specification also extends to both machines being supplied with a Euro removable welding torch and earth clamp plus a fixed power cable. Also unusually, for such a machine, the Pearl XL will accommodate a large 15kg wire reel, feeding it through a 4-roller system.

Both machines are designed for small to medium-sized companies undertaking light fabrication and maintenance work, craftsmen and DIY enthusiasts and are suitable for welding mild steel, aluminium and stainless steel using standard MIG or Flux Cored welding wires. ## GRINDING & DEBURRING

THE DANISH COMPANY Fladder Danmark A/S and Karl Heesemann Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG from Germany have launched a “combi” compact machine for grinding and deburring.

The first process in the new machine is a belt grinding operation with the Heesemann MFA Unit, which can be equipped with up to two rubber-coated contact rollers. The purpose is to remove larger burrs and superfluous material and apply a smooth finish to the surface. The other process is deburring by the Fladder deburring unit with its rotating and oscillating gear head with the counter rotating spindles.

“The workpieces are brushed from a variety of different angles, regardless of where and how they are positioned on the conveyor belt. Because of the abrasive brush movements, the edges are rounded uniformly whether they are inside or outside edges, straight or curved,” the companies told ISMR.
