## process approach

The welding experts opted for a pulse process with an average amperage of 300 amperes to achieve the right root formation when welding the approximately 4mm thick side walls of the ventilation components. This approach ensures the necessary heat input. The somewhat "cooler" Fronius CMT (Cold Metal Transfer) mixed process was used for section welding the flange plates and bracing ribs in longitudinal and transverse directions.

"The mixed process adjusts the ratio of hot pulse process cycles to cool CMT process phases, as required for the specific application, resulting in perfectly welded, virtually spatterfree metal connections with zero distortion," Fronius told ISMR.

"The constant speed of the welding carriage, combined with the right welding processes, were the key factors in achieving the consistently reproducible high quality of the weld seams," concluded Erwin Kunst.

"Ultimately, it vastly exceeded our expectations. The Fronius welding experts deserve special thanks for their advice and support right from the start. Together, we were able to significantly optimise welding quality as well as production times."■
