# Flexible welding with the MicorMIG mobile from Lorch

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

The MicorMIG series by Lorch Schweißtechnik, for MIG-MAG welding, combines the welding power/features of the MicorMIG series with a compact and portable build to expand the machine's areas of use, featuring its new MicorMIG mobile in the 300- and 350-ampere performance classes.

"The new MicorMIG mobile is 63.5cm long, 31.1cm wide and 54.7cm high. It weighs barely 35 kilograms. Sporting two handles, it can be ergonomically carried by two people, while its stable housing will securely protect it at falls from heights of up to 35cm. The MicorMIG mobile's compact build is suitable for flexible use in assembly, on the construction site, on ships or simply for quick repairs in line and group manufacture. It can also be used as a full, stationary workshop machine," commented Lorch.

"The interaction of a stable arc and intuitive operation enables quick and simple mobile welding with the MicorMIG for beginners and pros. Combined with dynamic control technology and pre-defined Synergic welding programmes, all other parameters can be set automatically once material/wire/gas and the welding process have been selected. Each welder can adjust the arc dynamics from hard to soft via the dials, while the ControlPro display shows all relevant information," it said.

The system's update capacity makes it customisable in its configuration and allows it to grow along with its tasks. Additional welding procedures, welding programmes and pre-defined welding jobs can be installed using NFC technology. This technology also makes it possible to set up personalised access management for operating the systems. Parameters can be limited, access restricted or the system can be locked entirely this way.

"A well-structured display, simple operation and stable arc: this system is suitable even for beginners," said Lorch.

"The full-process version provides the welder with all Lorch Speed processes which are available with the MicorMIG. This not only increases speed during MIG-MAG welding, but also optimises weld seams and prevents workpiece distortion using high-quality process control. Welding fume emission and noise are clearly reduced as well. The Micor-Twin process is a current highlight here, allowing much faster and simpler welding of demanding exposed welds by using the MIG-MAG procedure instead of the time-consuming TIG procedure," added Lorch.

The MicorMIG mobile is installed in a robust steel and plastic housing. Easier transport of the system can be facilitated by screwing it on various trolleys or carts using an integrated holding system. Lateral eyelets also enable transport by crane.

Lorch Schweißtechnik GmbH is a manufacturer of arc welding systems for industrial applications as well as for use with robots and collaborative robot systems. It also develops helmet and torch systems.■
