Parameters database for 100 torches optimizes fume extraction

Kemper has developed a torch database for effective torch extraction. The data pool, with the parameters of about 100 torch models, provides the basis for the optimum capture of substances by the VacuFil i series of high-vacuum extraction units.

The manufacturer developed a test station and, on the basis of the international standard DIN EN ISO 21904, specified the relevant parameters for optimum welding fume extraction, such as the required vacuum for the air volume flow and the resultant extraction capacity. The data, which varies according to the extraction torch, is incorporated in the database.

The stored parameters automatically define the required extraction capacity of the extraction units. When welders connect the extraction unit to an extraction torch, they select the connected torch via the touchscreen, and the device automatically and permanently regulates the optimum extraction capacity, even adjusting it to changes in external conditions.

Metal-cored wire helps reduce postweld cleaning

Hobart has introduced its new Fab-COR Edge XP metal-cored wire. The wire is formulated to migrate silicon to large islands at the centerline of the weld, where they can be identified and are accessible for removal. This helps reduce downtime for postweld cleaning that can be critical for subsequent operations.

The silicon gathering is best on clean materials, but the wire also can be used to weld over mill scale. Its wetting action allows for excellent weld bead appearance and contour in these applications, the company reports.

The wire can be used for welding on a variety of joint angles and material thicknesses.

Film carriage helps improve stretch-wrapper efficiency

Orion Packaging Systems, a division of ProMach, has introduced the newly redesigned S-Carriage InstaThread Prestretch Film Carriage. At the heart of the design are two prestretched rollers. Film travels in an S pattern around the rollers, providing more than 180 degrees of film contact to help reduce slippage and neckdown. The result is a consistent prestretch of 260% at different tensions and 1 in. less neckdown for fast wrapping and improved film yield, the manufacturer reports.

To load a new roll of film, the operator opens the dual lock carriage door, pulls the film through the middle of the rollers, and closes the door.
