# Weld seam detection system

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

Finding a weld seam on tubes is becoming more and more important, especially during automated manufacturing processes and is ultimately of increasing importance for the quality of the final product. Optimising the welding processes does not make this complex challenge any easier. In the case of longitudinally welded tubes, for example, weld seams can be increasingly difficult or impossible to detect optically.

Roland Electronic GmbH has been successfully dealing with the issue of localising weld seams on tubes for many years. Its SND40 Weld Seam Detection System is a measurement system based on the leakage flux method (magnetic flux) or the eddy current method. It is characterised by a very high grade of flexibility. Depending on the material, welding process, wall thickness etc., various options are possible for the measuring process and resulting program parameters.

“An intuitive menu navigation feature (Fig. 2) makes it possible to find the optimum parameters for localising weld seams on tubes, depending upon the product, without in-depth knowledge of the measuring device or the measuring method," explained the manufacturer. "The wizard mode minimizes the effort required to set up the system. Just enter the tube diameter, wall thickness, material type, information on post-treatment and the required target angle for the weld seam positioning (Fig. 3)," it added.

These parameters are queried step by step. From this, suitable “recipes" are calculated, which are verified and optimised in a subsequent automated process. The database created by Roland Electronic has been continuously maintained over the years.

"Our SND40 weld seam detection system therefore guarantees reliable detection, even of weld seams that are not visually recognizable. It ensures optimum and required weld seam position (Fig. 4) for safe further processing of tubes and avoids process and product errors. It guarantees an increase in quality and productivity as well as sustainable protection of the customer's resources," concluded Roland Electronic GmbH.■
