# COBOT WeldSpace 4.0

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

In the future, advances in digitalisation and automation will also change welding production processes. Particularly for the manual welding of classic sheet metal components, says Demmeler, new welding technology using collaborative robots can relieve valuable skilled workers of tiring routine and repetitive tasks.

"With our COBOT WeldSpace 4.0, even small and medium-sized companies are guaranteed a quick start in automated and professional welding production thanks to rapid commissioning and extremely simple programming. Whether it is individual parts, series or small batch sizes with a large variety of parts, they can save time in welding and reworking. The fully equipped welding cell with welding robot and welding technology offers modularity and flexibility in the smallest possible space with four workspaces: SPACE A, A1-A2 and Space B. The COBOT WeldSpace 4.0 can be operated and programmed without welding expertise, said Demmeler.

The automatic movement of the enclosure from Space A to Space B by 900mm creates around 3m² of additional working space and enables work to be carried out in shuttle mode. While the robot is welding on one side, the operator (protected by retractable roller doors with aluminium armour and integrated welding protection glass) can set up and re-clamp on the DEMMELER 3D welding table on the other side in parallel (two-station operation). Further division of workspace Space A into A1 and A2, by means of a partition, can even create an additional workstation (three-station operation).

"Optionally, Space B can be equipped with a new type of two-axis manipulator. For the best welding result, the powerful two-axis manipulator positions the workpieces (weighing up to 500kg) in the trough position and without re-clamping. Thanks to the manipulator's rotary axis, even circumferential seams can be welded without problems," concluded Demmeler.
