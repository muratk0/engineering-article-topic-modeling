ETS WELD SEAM CLEANING, POLISHING & MARKING SOLUTIONS

ETS EUROPEAN TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS develops electrochemical cleaning systems for efficient weld seam cleaning, polishing, marking, and surface treatment. With over 35 years of international experience in the welding industry and in-depth expertise in electrochemical cleaning, ets focuses on high-quality products, practical operation, and solutions built for demanding modern fabrication applications. The SOLIDCLEAN X SERIES combines state-of-the-art technology, compact machine design, and intuitive digital operation to help shops clean and finish stainless steel weld seams with confidence. Available in multiple power levels, including SOLIDCLEAN X 80, X 100, and X 150 AUTO, the system gives fabricators a flexible platform for cleaning, polishing, and marking while supporting reliable day-to-day use in workshops, production floors, and industrial fabrication environments.

ELECTROCHEMICAL WELD CLEANING

ETS SOLIDCLEAN X systems are built for weld seam cleaning and surface treatment, helping shops clean, Polish, and mark stainless steel weld seams with ONE device.

COMPACT DESIGN

The SOLIDCLEAN X SERIES is designed with a compact machine footprint, making it practical for workshops, production floors, construction sites, and other dynamic work environments.

DIGITAL OPERATION

SOLIDCLEAN X features intuitive operation through a digital display and accessible controls, helping operators work efficiently without overcomplicating the cleaning process.

CARBON BRUSH TECHNOLOGY

ETS uses carbon brush technology to support cleaning in corners, edges, and hard-to-reach areas, making the system useful for detailed stainless steel fabrication work.

COMPLETE SYSTEM SETUP

Each SOLIDCLEAN X device set includes core accessories for cleaning and polishing, including a carbon brush, adjustable teflon sleeve, ground cable, teflon handle, electrolyte container, spray bottle, ETS cleaner, and ETS polisher.
