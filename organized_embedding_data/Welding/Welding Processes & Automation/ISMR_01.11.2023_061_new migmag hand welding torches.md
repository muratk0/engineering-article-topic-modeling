# New MIG/MAG hand welding torches

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

MECHAFIN's Push-Pull unit is an intermediate drive unit to which the conventional welding torch can be connected.

The new MEX torch series from Swiss manufacturer, MECHAFIN AG, has been designed to withstand tough conditions in shipyards; oil platforms; coal mines and in the automotive or railway sectors.

“It provides the operator with maximum comfort for a fatigue-free welding process. The adapted ergonomics and placement of the probe, as well as its design in combination with the twocomponent handle and the ball joint, ensure comfort in every working position. [This means high-quality welds] and increased work safety,"said the Swiss welding specialist.

"Thanks to the quick-release fastener on the handles, the torches are extremely easy to service. A torch neck can be replaced with ease, should this be necessary. This saves time and reduces service costs. The torches feature long service life of wear parts. Optionally, LED lights or push buttons are available for controlling the welding system, which further increases welding comfort," it continued.

MEX welding torches also offer "optimised button position for fatigue-free, safe operation", as well as high mechanical load capacity. Designed for easy maintenance and serviceability, they also feature a separate shielding gas hose for improved weld pool coverage.
