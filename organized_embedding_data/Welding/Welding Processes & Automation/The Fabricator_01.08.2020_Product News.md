System offers automated pipe cutting option

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has introduced the SmartPipe system for automated plasma or oxyfuel cutting of pipe alongside a table for cutting flat plate. The standard line covers pipe in diameters from 2 to 12 in., lengths up to 20 ft., and weights up to 2,200 lbs. The heavy-duty model can handle pipe diameters up to 10 ft. (including 36-, 48-, and 72-in. diameters) and lengths up to 60 ft.

The system comprises a pipe turning unit with a manually adjustable chuck and a CNC turning axis, two or more adjustable-position pipe supports, and a support track. A typical configuration would situate the turning unit between the cutting table and gantry rail (oriented parallel to the rail), but it can be located behind the table as well (parallel to the gantry).

Graphical programming software creates programs for straight or bevel cuts for any pipe application, such as saddles, holes, slots, or weld cut preparations. The software, which runs on the company’s Vision controller, generates a 3D rendering of the finished pipe so users can confirm details before cutting.

Filter removes vapors, contaminants

The .01 Micron SuperStar Filter from Walmec North America is designed to provide clean, dry, compressed air, resulting in the removal of any vapors and contaminants down to 0.01 micron.

With flow ranges of 50, 75, or 100 SCFM, the four-stage filtration system can handle pressure ratings of up to 250 PSI. It works best when installed at or near the point of use.

The first- and second-stage filters remove moisture, liquids, dust, rust, scale, and other contaminants to 5 microns. The third and fourth stages remove any remaining particles down to 0.01 micron and absorb any remaining vapors.

Pull allows hands-free door opening

In response to the coronavirus pandemic, Industrial Magnetics Inc. (IMI) has developed the Arm-Pull, a hands-free door opener that allows users to open swing-style doors without using their hands or fingertips.

The new pull attaches to latchless, swing-style doors and allows people to pull the door open using their forearm, wrist, or elbow when entering or exiting a room.

Suitable for public restrooms, retail stores, office buildings, and shop floors, the product can be used on metal or wood doors using the magnetic or through-hole mounting options. It is constructed from heavyduty stainless steel as a one-piece laser-cut and precision-formed unit.

The company also has devised a way for the pull to work on latching doors with lever-style handles by incorporating a J-hook magnetic assembly. The J-hook holds the door handle in the open position, converting the door into a latchless style that can be used in conjunction with the pull.

BLM GROUP USA has introduced the LT360 all-in-one 3D cutting system. It features a fiber laser cutting head mounted on a 6-axis robot working in tandem with a part handling robot to produce complex geometries in any 3D piece within a single process.

400-amp cutting system upgraded

Thermal Dynamics Automation has upgraded its 400-amp plasma cutting system—featuring Ultra-Cut 400 XT high-precision power source, XT torch with QuickPierce consumables, and iCNC Performance Controller—to reduce piercing time by up to 40% on mild steel thicknesses from 1.25 to 2.0 in.

Pierce-on-the-fly routines have been added to the control. They start moving the torch after about one-third of the total pierce time. The groove behind the torch provides an evacuation path for the molten metal, reducing its impact on the consumables. Pierce-puddle-avoidance routines also have been added, which prevent the front end of the torch from touching the puddle during arc start..

Thermal Dynamics
