# TIG welding powerhouse

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

EWM offers TIG welders a compact, powerful inverter welding machine, the new Picotig 220 puls DC. Weighing in at just ten kilograms, the welding machine has a robust, lightweight design making it suitable for workshops and construction sites.

“Lightweight and easily portable for maximum efficiency, we have expanded the Picotig series with a new model: the Picotig 220 puls DC. The integrated Power Factor Correction (PFC) ensures an increased power output while the machine's high efficiency and standby function help to save both power and costs. Users can achieve welding currents of 220 A with 40% duty cycle for TIG welding and currents of 190 A with 35% duty cycle for MMA welding. This is a significant increase in performance in comparison to the previous model, explained EWM.

The welding technology manufacturer has made the welding machines in its Picotig series more convenient to operate. With a mains lead measuring 3.5m, the Picotig 220 puls DC offers a wide working radius with no need for an extension cable. The sturdy carrying handle also comes with a cable holder for the mains cable and built-in storage compartment for wear parts. The robust casing provides protection for all the connections on the rear side. A protective cap on the front side protects the controls against damage and dirt.

“The Comfort 3.0 mini control allows users to select all pulse, standard and innovative welding processes, or users can set tack points with precision using the spotArc® and spotmatic functions. The Picotig 220 puls DC is also suitable for MMA welding and is compatible with cellulose electrodes. The control is extremely easy to operate, supporting users with even the most complex of welding tasks. Users now have multiple options when choosing a welding torch, as the Picotig 220 puls DC can be used with both conventional and digital EWM multi-functional welding torches with display and remote-control functions," highlighted EWM.

The new Picotig 220 puls DC from EWM weighs just ten kilograms.

The five-pole torch connection is also compatible with EWM X technology. Whether it's the TIG Retox XQ welding torch, spotArc® welding torch or a welding torch used with the previous Picotig 200 puls DC model, the user is free to choose and can adapt the welding equipment to suit the task at hand. A wide range of accessories is also available.
