Welding machine/generator suitable for indoor, outdoor SMAW

Miller Electric Mfg. Co. has introduced the new Fusion 160 SMAW machine/generator. At 222 lbs., it is portable for outdoor work, and PowerShift technology allows it to be plugged into 120-/240-V utility power for inside use. The machine’s welder delivers up to 160 amps of stick welding power, and the generator produces up to 6,500 W of power for electric tools, making it useful for outdoor work.

According to the company, the multivoltage plug (MVP™) makes it easy to connect the machine, with no tools required to switch between either type of current.

Company engineers have designed the power source to delivers a smooth, stable stick arc, which should result in less spatter and less time spent on cleanup.

Deburring machine accessory produces palm sander finish

AM Machinery Sales offers a new rotary orbital finishing disk system accessory head from Loewer. The device, available as an optional accessory on all Discmaster machines, allows users to produce a finish like a palm sander in a through-feed machine, for use in situations where a palm sander would not be efficient. It creates a finish similarto a DA pattern—known in architectural metal shops as "angel hair"—on sheet metal.

The orbital finishing disk allows the user to remove the marks created during a bending operation without having to finish the entire part after forming. The forgiving nature of the random scratch allows for faster blending after bending and welding.

This system can be retrofitted on all existing machines.

Servo hydraulic press cuts energy consumption in half

AP&T has introduced a servo hydraulic press for cold forming and hot forming. It combines the consistently high press force of a hydraulic press with the energy efficiency and speed control possibilities offered by servomechanical presses.

According to the company, the press is quicker and twice as energy-efficient as a conventional hydraulic press and requires a third as much maintenance.

The pressing process can be controlled with a high degree of parallelism between the press table and slide, which helps decrease tooling wear. The force remains consistently high during the entire press cycle and can be distributed precisely, even over small surfaces. The cushion can be pre-accelerated when forming sensitive materials.

Acceleration force and deceleration force are up to 250 percent higher than that of a conventional hydraulic press, the manufacturer states. Process and machine data is controlled via internet-based systems.

Energy can be stored internally in the machine, which enables higher output with lower installed effect. The need for cooling is reduced, and when the press is idle, it does not consume any energy. The noise level is low, and the press is designed to be easily recyclable.

Coating forms permanent bond with galvanized steel to prevent corrosion

Chemcoaters Inc. has introduced InterCoat

®

ChemGuard, a new type of corrosion protection for galvanized steel. The coating, which is applied over a light layer of zinc, reacts with the zinc to form a permanent, covalent bond on the surface of the metal. The bond is formed at the molecular level and cannot be washed or worn off.

This process allows bending, stamping, rolling, postpainting, and shearing while providing self-healing characteristics that help protect zinc exposed naturally during secondary processing.

The RoHS-compliant product has a low coefficient of friction and is weldable. It is designed to be applied on the galvanizing line or, for custom formulas, by the original coil coater.

The process reduces the need for zinc coatings heavier than G30 and eliminates the need for temporary corrosion protection coatings used in shipping and materials storage. It is applicable to many industries, including automotive, aerospace, construction, electrical conduit, wall studs, furniture, fixtures, appliances, outdoor and highway railing, agricultural, and lawn and garden.

Tapping tool creates up to 200 threaded holes per minute

Wilson Tool International

®

has introduced the QuickTap™ tapping tool to help eliminate secondary operations related to the creation of tapped holes.

The tool taps a threaded hole with every machine stroke, completing up to 200 holes per minute depending on material type/thickness and tap size. According to the company, the tool produces accurate threads at any programmed location on the sheet.

A built-in tapping fluid injection system is synchronized with the machine stroke to help reduce wear and breakage. Its large tank enables tapping of up to 40,000 holes before refilling.
