Compact workcells integrate robotics into weld processes

Yaskawa Motoman has introduced the ArcWorld RS and ArcWorld HS pre-engineered robotic arc welding workcells as compact options for integrating robotics into current weld processes. Suitable for replacing or supplementing manual welding, such as pre-assembly before welding in larger robotic workcells, each system is designed for small to medium-size part production for medium-volume fabrication.

Requiring 25 sq. ft. of floor space, the RS uses a rotating station manually indexed by the operator, featuring two fixture tables with a 100-kg payload capacity per side. It processes parts up to 425 by 700 by 990 mm. The HS needs 15 sq. ft. of floor space and offers a hinged station that allows easy access for loading/unloading parts. Its single fixture table has a 100-kg payload capacity for processing parts up to 550 by 500 by 1,050 mm.

18.5-in. display with full HD resolution stands up to industrial use

Canvys - Visual Technology Solutions has introduced an 18.5-in. custom monitor with full HD resolution for use in industrial applications. This display is a base model for OEMs to create customized HMIs through custom product development or monitor integration.

The true flat display is available with a protective screen or PCAP touch. The PCAP version is a 10-point touchscreen. The monitor features an antiglare coating to prevent fingerprints but retains good gliding capabilities. The OSD menu is integrated into the front glass and works via PCAP touch. The sealed plastic housing is designed without ventilation holes to keep dust and liquids out of the unit.

Shot blasting machine handles large, complex steel weldments

The RHBD-K continuous-feed spinner hanger shot blast machine from Rösler Oberflächentechnik GmbH is designed for the automatic processing of large, heavy, complex steel weldments used in the production of rail cars and agricultural and construction equipment.

The machine cleans, descales, and derusts components such as frame sections, base frames, wheel suspensions, and cross beams to prepare them for painting. It can deliver surface finishes of SA 2.5 with surface roughness readings from 6 to 12 microns. The modular equipment allows customization of size and technical features to fit the finishing job, whether it’s as a stand-alone unit or integrated into a manufacturing line.

Networked weld monitor simplifies resistance welding data capture, analysis

AMADA WELD TECH INC. has released the IIoT-ready WM-200A networked resistance weld monitor, which helps simplify data capture, storage, and analysis on a networked platform.

The unit monitors all aspects of the resistance welding process, providing visual and statistical feedback in R&D and production environments. It also provides immediate feedback to the weld station by monitoring key aspects of the process and sending good/no good information to the process controller instantaneously during production.

Whether connected to a stand-alone resistance welding station or implemented in a fully automated system, the unit enables users to collect large amounts of high-resolution process data to be used for manufacturing traceability, statistical data analysis, equipment efficiency, and health.

Pneumatic clamps updated with magnetic piston, T-slot

Winco has updated its pneumatic clamps with new features to help increase reliability and lifespan. The pneumatic cylinder now operates with a magnetic piston and has a T-slot on the housing. The slot simplifies sensor attachment, while the magnetic piston permits more precise querying of the current position by the new GN 3380 sensor, which entirely replaces the previous sensors. Other sensors also can be installed in the slot.

GN 860 series pneumatic clamps now have hardened, ground bearing bushings and tempered bearing pins for reduced wear. No meaningful alterations were observed in testing for more than 250,000 cycles, says the company. Structural reinforcement of the sheet steel housing reduces wearinducing vibrations during operation, and the new, one-way restrictor in the cylinder enables smooth travel to help extend the lifespan of the piston.

3D simulation software update offers increased programming automation

Cenit North America has released an updated version of its 3D simulation software. FASTSUITE E2 enables manufacturers to optimize their use of human and material resources, and its hardware vendor neutrality gives users flexibility in cell and factory planning.

The new release offers increased automation in programming activities, improved simulation quality, standardized support for touch-sense operations, increased flexibility for multirobot cells or multimachine setups, automatic tool path optimization for 5-axis laser machines, and improvements in weld seam programming for arc welding.
