# New Chair for The Welding Society of Finland

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Reetta Verho, DI, IWE, has been appointed The Welding Society of Finland's (SHY) Chairman of the Board as of 1 January 2023.

Verho has acted as Member of the Board since 2012 and as Deputy Chairman for the past five years. Since 2017, she has also acted as Member of Welding Coordinator Club and Chairman of the Board since 2019. She also acts as Deputy Member of the Board of SHY Lahti.

Reetta Verho has an IWE degree in welding and solid work experience in the welding field. She has worked for nine years as “Manager, Welding Service Team“ at Finnish welding specialist, Kemppi Oy.

“The Welding Society of Finland supports and develops professional expertise and increasing awareness of the welding industry e.g. among young people. SHY enables welding professionals to network across company boundaries. This is how we develop the welding industry together. I am very happy that my employer, Kemppi, wants to support and develop SHY's operations and contribute to pioneering the industry," she said.

The Welding Society of Finland was established in 1949 to promote welding expertise and interactions between professionals in welding, allied processes and NDT.

SHY's membership consists of approximately 1,800 welding and thermal spraying professionals and approximately 140 company and community members. The association has 13 local branches. It offers members information, support, events and training," added Kemppi.■
