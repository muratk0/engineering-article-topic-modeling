# NEW WELDING CATALOGUE

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

GYS HAS launched its 2012 welding catalogue which introduces ten new welding machines. The emphasis in the 2012 catalogue is on MIG/MAG with five new machines addressing three market sectors. Neomig 270 is a 3-phase MIG pulse inverter due later this year. Magys 350 and 450 are fully synergic industrial specification welders, available in compact or separate configurations. And, in single phase, the successful SMARTmig range launched in 2011 has been expanded with the introduction of SMARTmig 180 and SMARTmig 182 - two full-size 180A models sporting the ‘SMART’ control panel.

The TIG range has also expanded with the launch of GYS’s first 3-phase TIG welder. The TIG 250 DC/HF is a 250A machine featuring an advanced control panel and pulse mode. The new TIG 168 DC/HF is a single-phase 160A TIG machine also with advanced control panel and pulse mode. Finally, new to the 2012 catalogue is the Gysmi 200P, a 200A MMA single phase welding machine. e-mail: uk@gys.fr

## PRODUCT REVIEWS

IN THE last years, Dreistern has contributed considerably to the development of new processes such as laser welding of high-strength steel, integration of additional production processes like piercing, embossing or notching into tube manufacturing lines or the production of completely new types of tubes such as multi-layer composite tubing. Tests at Dreistern with slightly modified equipment have proven that existing systems can also produce multilayer pipes with layers of copper or stainless steel in lieu of aluminium.

“Stainless steel looks exceptionally promising. These new ‘specie’ of multilayer pipe could become a low-cost version for ornamental or chemical tubing. In order to keep cost low, the thickness of the stainless steel layer can be as small as only 100 Microns or even less. An inner or outer plastic tube supplies the necessary strength. Best of all, these new members to the family could still exhibit the advantages that have gained their older siblings a hefty market share within plumbing and radiant heating markets in the first place,” said Dreistern.

A new concept from Dreistern engineers is based on a highly modular machine base, which can incorporate almost any tube processing technology at any position of a tube production line. For a given production task, only such processing units as are needed for the task are temporarily integrated into the line. The machine control provides the same flexibility for incorporating additional functions using an easily-extendable bus system. The German machine builder already supplied the first few machines which can produce standard tubing and complex-shaped automotive parts within an integrated line concept close to this modular design. www.dreistern.com

## FLEXIBLE BENDING

LVD COMPANY NV now offers the Vari-VEE V30-140 CNC adjustable Vee die for its complete range of PPEB and Easy-Form precision press brakes. The Vari-VEE V30-140 complements LVD’s heavy-duty Vari-VEE option. This smaller version Vee die offers the benefits of a variable and adjustable bottom Vee die to a broader range of LVD press brake models from 320 to 1000 tons and up to 8m in length.

LVD’s Vari-VEE die provides automatic adjustment of the V-width in increments of 10mm, reducing set-up time and providing flexibility for bending operations. The variable die enables the bending of sharp angles of up to 50 degrees for the maximum V-width. This is especially critical when forming high-strength materials because of significant springback. The radius on the V-die is a hardened insert which is machined with LVD’s patented STONE compound curve for optimal rolling and friction reducing properties.

LVD’s Vari-VEE V30-140 is suitable for applications that demand bending of different thicknesses of materials; frequent die changes; bending thick materials with large Vee widths or different radii on the same component, as well as bending high strength materials which tend to crack while bending. www.lvdgroup.com
