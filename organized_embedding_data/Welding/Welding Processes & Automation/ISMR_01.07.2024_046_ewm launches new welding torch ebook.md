# EWM launches new welding torch eBook

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

Welding torches can often be underestimated, yet they are so important to the welding process. The user must decide whether he/she wants to accept low performance and possibly high wear on components when using a lower-quality welding torch or whether to opt for quality.

"In our new eBook, we shed light on how to obtain optimal welding results while still achieving maximum productivity," EWM told ISMR. "When it is time to purchase welding torches, the main focus is often on the price. Low-cost versions are preferred over higher quality, but more expensive, ones. This is a serious mistake, because you cannot create a good weld seam with an inferior welding torch."

In its new free eBook, 'Cost-efficient and reliable welding: the importance of the welding torch to the overall process', EWM provides valuable tips and demonstrates the advantages of optimally harmonising all the welding components.

So, what characterises a good welding torch? EWM is quick to explain.

"The welding torch should have a long service life. The parts subject to heavy wear should be wear-resistant. The welding result should not require any reworking and the welding torch should enable comfortable and ergonomically optimised operation. All these requirements are met by high-performance, high-quality welding torches which not only improve the quality of the weld seams, but also reduce operating costs in the long term," it said.

"The basic requirement for high weld seam quality is a stable process, which avoids increased spatter and pore formation and prevents a lack of fusion. If these problems occur, it is important to find the cause. Often, it is not the power source that is the cause but the welding torch, an inadequate shielding gas supply or the poor quality of the filler material. It is important to detect and eliminate potential interference in the welding process," continued EWM.

For over 65 years, EWM has developed arc welding technology in Germany. The family company was founded in Mündersbach, in Germany's Westerwald region, in 1957. Today, it has over 400 sales and service support outlets worldwide.

In addition to smart machines, EWM develops and produces all necessary components such as welding torches for manual and automated applications. Many EWM welding processes are patented and developed to save energy, materials and time. EWM also offers customer-oriented service and consultancy.
