# Cutting and welding

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

The MSS Group Ltd will be at the MACH 2022 exhibition (4-8 April 2022, Birmingham, UK) on its 160m² stand number 400, in Hall 17, to showcase a range of machinery.

"Visitors will be able to see the efficient MSS Nitrocube™ (nitrogen generation system), which combines the highest quality components in our unique cube enclosure with sophisticated MSS intelligent monitoring and controls. All systems provide 300bar nitrogen with 99.98% and 99.999% gas purity as standard with a huge range of flow rates. Storage capacities are available to suit most applications," it told ISMR.

New to the UK will be the MSS Nitr02 gas mixer panel, which has been specially developed for use with high-power fibre laser cutting systems and enables on-line variable gas mixing technology.

"Extremely compact, the system can be installed with minimum floor space without the need for any large storage tanks. This technology allows significant increases in fibre laser cutting speed and quality. Special show launch offers will be available," explained the manufacturer.

Above: The Penta Swing 6kW fibre laser cutter.

MSS will also be showcasing the Penta Swing 6kW fibre laser cutting system with a Precitec cutting head and IPG laser sources. Available with laser powers from 2-40kW, and cutting bed sizes of up to 12m, the Penta range is, said MSS, "an industrial work horse to suit all laser cutting types."

Another new system to the UK, the 1-2kW Penta Swan laser welding system, can process a range of materials from mild steel to aluminium. Special MACH show launch offers will also be available for this product, confirmed MSS.■
