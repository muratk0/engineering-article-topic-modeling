# Process simulation for cold forming

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

SIMUFACT ENGINEERING, an MSC Software company, unveiled the latest features of its simulation solutions, Simufact.forming and Simufact.welding, at the Wire show recently in Duesseldorf. With its current product versions, Simufact has expanded the range of application fields by including additional manufacturing processes.

This includes new options for the simulation of pressure welding processes, in which work-pieces are joined by applying heat (through the introduction of electrical current or due to friction) and are then subsequently joined by applying pressure. Pressure welding processes include resistance spot welding, friction welding and friction spot welding among others. Another new feature is the option to simulate the intermediate steps in resistance heating which allows for the reduction of strain hardening effects.

A special topic at the Simufact stand was the Industry 4.0- related linking of process simulation with press force monitoring to improve tool life – Simufact presented the results of a pilot project with its partners Prokos and Möhling.

Simufact.forming and Simufact.welding are widely used software solutions in the metal forming market for the design and optimisation of manufacturing processes in metalprocessing. Based on Simufact software solutions, users can simulate a wide range of forming and joining techniques, including the processes involved in cold forming. The new application module pressure welding adds value to the coldforming manufacturing process chain.

Simufact.welding is welding simulation software that enables the calculation of distortions, residual stresses and the material conditions within welding processes from a single user interface. In addition to simulating arc welding and laser beam welding, the current version (Simufact.welding 5) now also covers Resistance Spot Welding (RSW). See www.simufact.de
