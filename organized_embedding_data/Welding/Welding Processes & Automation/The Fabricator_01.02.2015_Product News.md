Portable wire feeder

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has introduced the MobileFeed 201AVS, a suitcase-style, portable wire feeder for industrial welding applications. The unit, designed to fit through a 16-in. manhole, weighs less than 30 lbs.

The voltage-sensing wire feeder can be used with any CV or CC welding power supply. It is powered off the arc, so only the welding cable is required for basic operation. Its high duty cycle of 450 amps at 60 percent helps ensure trouble-free operation for high-amperage applications, the company reports. A quick-change, four-roll feed unit provides dependable wire feeding and quick, toolless wire changeover. Digital meters for both voltage and wire feed speed help save labor for critical welds using specific welding parameters.

The impact-resistant, flame-retardant case protects against dirt, metal grit, and moisture, as well as extreme abuse from grinding sparks, corrosive chemicals, and drops. An optional remote control allows voltage to be adjusted away from the power supply.

Inline spark trap

Nordfab Ducting LLC has added an inline spark trap to its Quick-Fit™ ducting product line for welding and grinding applications. When installed in a dust collection system, the spark trap helps reduce the possibility of fire in cyclones/collectors by decreasing the number of sparks that reach these units through the ductwork.

The spark trap mounts into the company’s clamp-together ducting systems, which require no rivets, screws, or welding. They also can be mounted to other ducting systems with the use of an adapter. No calibration or other assistance from a factory technician is required.

Hole saws

CS Unitec’s new hole saws are suitable for cutting in tight spaces where a magnetic drill cannot fit and for cutting material too thin for a magnetic drill to attach. Designed for use with hand-held drills, the tungsten carbide-tipped (TCT) hole saws cut clean, fast holes without grinding the core. The 1-series hole saws include a center pilot drill for cutting holes up to 1 in. deep, with diameters from 0.5625 to 4 in. Cutters up to 3.25 in. dia. feature a 0.375-in. shank, while cutters with diameters of 3.3125 and larger feature a 0.5-in. shank.

The 2-series heavy-duty hole saws feature a spring-loaded pilot pin to eject the slug. The milled 3-flat shank fits 0.5-in. chucks. The 2-series hole saws are available in diameters from 0.5625 to 1.375 in. and drill up to 0.5625 in. deep.

Press brake controller

Haco has introduced a new concept for press brake controllers: SyncView, a dynamic display located on the beam that moves horizontally to the correct bending location. The tablet screen is positioned automatically where the workpiece is being worked on by the operator, allowing the operator to watch both the screen and the workpiece simultaneously during the bending process.

The 3-D image of the bending steps is always right in front of the user rather than yards away. This allows the user to understand and carry out bending steps more quickly during the bending sequence.

The 3-D images on the screen can be rotated without the operator having to leave the work position, enabling fast evaluation of complex pieces. The operator looks in the same direction at both the screen and workpiece, reducing constant head turning between the screen and the place where the workpiece must be positioned.

Arc welding robot

Yaskawa Motoman’s new 6-axis MA2010 arc welding robot features a 22-lb. payload, up from 6.6 lbs. on the previous MA1900 model, as well as a 23 percent larger wrist hole for utilities and a 6 percent longer reach.

The robot, introduced with the DX200 controller, features a contoured arm structure that reduces interference by enlarging the close-range motion area of the robot, making it suitable for welding large workpieces and reaching over jigs. Its hollow upper arm maintains the optimum bend radius of the welding torch cable. The Taxis can rotate the torch ±210 degrees without cable interference.

The robot has a 6.6-ft. horizontal reach, 11.9-ft. vertical reach, and ±0.003-in. repeatability. Integrated, through-the-arm torch cabling eliminates cable interference and helps reduce cable wear.

Lightweight, cordless tools

TRUMPF Inc. has added new models to its TruTool line of compact, lightweight, lithium-ion cordless power tools: the PN 130 profile nibbler, S 160 and S 114 shears, and C 160 cutting shears.

The battery-optimized aluminum gear head is designed for the low voltage of the battery used in the cordless tools to help achieve above-average operating time. For all the new cordless power tools, the manufacturer uses 10.8 lithium-ion battery technology for the standard 2-Ah battery and optional 4-Ah battery. This enables users to work for extended periods of time without charging the battery.
