"WE ARE WELDING" is the guiding theme of EWM AG (Mündersbach, Germany) and reflects its commitment to welding on a daily basis – with its staff, suppliers and partners and in cooperation with its customers. The group of companies is now inviting all interested visitors to come to its booth (stand E82 in hall 13) at the EuroBLECH 2016 exhibition in Hanover (25-29 October 2016).

EWM will be presenting its top innovation in the form of the forceArc puls MIG/MAG welding process, which combines the advantages of forceArc with the benefits of pulse welding. This process, which will be showcased in a live demonstration at the group's exhibition stand, offers potential overall savings of up to 30 per cent, EWM told ISMR.

## Getting a firm grip

STRONG HAND Tools®, a manufacturer of welding clamps, magnets, pliers and BuildPro® welding tables, has introduced its Inside/Outside angle magnets.

“For the optimal set-up of each unique substructure, you can use either the inside or outside surfaces of the Inside/Outside angle magnets. Hold stock from the outside, leaving clearance for inside welds, for outside welds. Hold and position sheet metal, round pipes, square angled or flat stock. Two independent magnets inside + double plates provide maximum pull force. The Inside/Outside angle magnets are fully enclosed for easier cleaning. The narrow profile makes them ideal for use in tight spots," said the US manufacturer.

“The adjustable Inside/Outside angle magnets [MLA Series] can be adjusted from 30° - 270°. Use an allen wrench to set and lock the angle. Use the mounting hole to link two or more magnets together for use as fixturing elements. Insert a wrench into the hex hole on the magnets to provide a convenient breakaway force for easy removal from the finished workpiece." See www.stronghandtools.com ■
