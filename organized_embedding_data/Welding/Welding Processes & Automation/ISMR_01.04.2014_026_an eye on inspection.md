# An eye on inspection

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

OCSA S.p.A., an Italian company which is part of the Gabrielli group, is a leading manufacturer of cold rolled precision steel strips. ISRA VISION PARSYTEC, a well-known supplier of surface inspection systems for coil products, was chosen by OCSA to install a new SURFACE MASTER inspection system that can inspect the top and bottom of narrow strip steel.

The system uses matrix cameras with a resolution of 170µm as well as bright and dark field lighting to detect any surface defect. By using the integrated Instant Classifier, a software module that includes a pre-programmed defect catalogue with a comprehensive range of information about typical defect types, it ensures that defects are reliably categorised. SURFACE MASTER also detects unwanted stripes on the metal surface. See www.isravision.com ■

## Welding in focus

CARL Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH has produced a new fully automated welding tip and gas nozzle changer that helps to avoid costly production downtime thanks to the manual replacement of robot welding torch consumables. When the welding robot needs new nozzles, the changer automatically takes the wearing parts of the torch and replaces them for new ones, with no manual intervention, and the use of the integrated torch cleaner and measurement module guarantee strong welding results with shorter production times.

Due to its compact and modular design, the SpareMatic can be integrated into both new and existing systems. These systems are employed in industries such as power plant technology, construction machinery, railway technology and the automotive industry. Cloos can also offer all the relevant core competencies for modern QINEO welding machines and QIROX robots, positioners and handling systems.
