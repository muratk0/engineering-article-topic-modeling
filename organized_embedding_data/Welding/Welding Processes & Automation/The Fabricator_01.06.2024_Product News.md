Engine-driven welding machines include integrated air compressors

Miller Electric Mfg. LLC has released the Bobcat 265 Air Pak and Trailblazer 330 Air Pak engine-driven welding machines with integrated industrial rotary-screw air compressors. The compact all-in-one machines also offer multiprocess weld quality, auxiliary power, and battery charge/crank assist for maintenance, repair operations, and field fabrication.

Both machines allow operators to run air impact wrenches and give 100% deliverable air. The compressor produces 30 CFM of air at 100% duty cycle and delivers 80 to 160 PSI of air with an optional storage tank. Auto-Set technology helps simplify weld parameter setup. Front panel predictive maintenance displays alert the operator when equipment needs to be serviced.

Compact 2D laser machine designed for smaller shops

Prima Power has introduced the Platino Linear 2D laser, a compact machine designed for small to medium-sized businesses. It is equipped with a maintenance-free linear drive system to help minimize downtime.

The cantilever architecture and open-design cabin provide complete access to the work area, while the synthetic granite frame delivers vibration absorption and thermal stability. According to the manufacturer, an aluminum carriage minimizes the weight of moving components without sacrificing rigidity.

The Smart Cut and Gas Mixer Cut features help reduce cycle times up to 30%, while ECO-TOUCH Cut reduces gas consumption by up to 40%, reports the company. Adaptive Cut adjusts focus and beam diameter for different materials automatically. The Safe Impact Protection System prevents damage from collisions.

Automated storage systems are available to minimize manual intervention and optimize space.

Wheel line expanded to accommodate heavy-duty industries

Hamilton Caster has announced the expansion of its Nylast wheel line to include sizes up to 24 in. dia., tripling its current load capacity of the series. Crafted from solid-cast nylon, the wheels offer high impact strength for industries with demanding conditions.

The wheel material is formulated with finely divided particles of molybdenum disulfide for enhanced load-bearing capability while preserving the impact resistance inherent to nylon. From the face to the bore, the wheels are CNC machined to strict tolerances to ensure consistency, the company states.

The larger wheel diameters enable the company’s Super Duty and Extreme Duty caster series to handle heavier loads, such as those in the aerospace and defense, ship- and bridgebuilding, and construction sectors.

The wheels withstand high load capacity while their lighter weight imparts less overall PSI on the contact area to prevent damaging floors. Their low rolling resistance when moving heavy loads helps minimize friction and save energy.

Push-feeding saw station cuts nonferrous metals

The TigerSaw 2000 Compact is a fully automatic optimizing and push-feeding saw station for cutting nonferrous metals including aluminum and copper. The compact saw is suitable for use in a variety of industries, including prefabrication, mechanical construction, aluminum processing, curtainwall, storefront, and fenestration.

The saw offers extensive guarding and a fully enclosed saw blade with a cutting envelope of 1 by 9.75 in. to 3 by 7.75 in., as well as 3.5-in.-OD tube. Compatible with 14- and 16-in. saw blades, it features a Spray Mist Coolant System and a 4.8-HP motor.

Optional upgrades include cut list downloading software, dynamic optimization and dynamic pack optimization software, simulation and statistics reporting, and label printing.

Plasma cutting guide helps ensure straight, accurate bevels

Heck Industries/Woodward Fab has introduced the PB200 Plasma-Pal beveling guide. The cast-aluminum guide has an adjustable torch head and roller ball guides to help the user set up a plasma machine to do straight, accurate bevels or parting on plate steel.

The guide is designed for use with any common handheld plasma cutting machine with torch head cup diameters of 7/8 to 2 in. Head angle is adjustable to 30, 45, or 90 degrees, and distance from the plate also is adjustable. A ball-bearing roller allows for smooth movement during cutting.
