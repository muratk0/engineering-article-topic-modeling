# Weld purge monitoring

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Global weld purging specialist, Huntingdon Fusion Techniques HFT®, has unveiled its updated PurgEye® 100, with new features for the weld purge monitor.

Weld purging is the act of removing (from the vicinity of the joint) oxygen, water vapour and any other gases or vapours that might be harmful to a welding joint. Such gases may combine with the metal to form undesirable compounds that may reduce corrosion resistance or be instrumental in creating cracks or other structural defects in metals.

"Weld purging requires the removal of oxygen and it is important that welders know exactly when it is safe to start welding to ensure that welds remain free from oxidation. This latest Weld Purge Monitor® accurately reads down to 100ppm, suitable for welding stainless steel," explained the company.

HFT technical sales manager, Luke Keane, added: “Guesswork is eliminated with the monitor and welders can now know the exact oxygen content before, during and after welding."

The hand-held PurgEye®100 now features a metal housing, upgraded from a plastic one. Readings are from atmospheric oxygen level (20.94%) down to 100ppm (0.01 %) of oxygen. The PurgEye®- 100 is IP65-rated and comes with leak-tight push buttons, autocalibration features, vacuum-sealed leaktight probe assembly and wrist/neck strap.

The PurgEye® 100's display features a low battery icon as well as the low sensor icon. When the monitor is not in use, automatic sleep mode activates to conserve battery life.

The Weld Purge Monitor® was invented by HFT® in the 1970s. The company's Argweld® product line offers a complete family of PurgEye® Weld Purge Monitors® to measure oxygen levels from atmospheric content (20.94%) down to 1ppm (accurate to 10ppm) for all applications and budgets.■
