# EWM expands Picomig family

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Suitable for construction sites and production, new welding machines from EWM's Picomig family are robust, compact all-rounders that are designed for greater efficiency.

“Both the Picomig 220 and Picomig 225 can be used to achieve welding currents of 220 A, and with a 230 V connection. Users benefit from low power consumption and reduced CO₂ emissions," said the welding specialist.

EWM is expanding its welding machine series with the Picomig 220 and Picomig 225 models designed for wire spool sizes of D200/5kg and D300/15kg, respectively. The multi-process inverter welding machines feature welding processes for pulsed MIG/ MAG welding, standard MIG/MAG welding and TIG as well as MMA welding. All necessary characteristics for steel, CrNi, aluminium, CuSi and flux-cored wires are included at no extra charge.

The new Picomig 220 and Picomig 225 multi-process inverter welding machines.

"The welding machines in our Picomig family are easy to operate and allow users to get started right away without time-consuming training. The machines are even suitable for standard mains connections. Because of their modern control and tailored accessories, they can be individually adapted to the welding task at hand. Portable machines are intended for mobile welding applications but can also be quickly and easily mounted onto a trolley with various systems available for users," outlined EWM.

"The high efficiency of the inverter technology also ensures more energy efficiency in operation while optimised ignition characteristics provide for the best welding results. This not only reduces the welding times, but also helps users to significantly lower their energy consumption and effectively cut down CO₂ emissions," it added.
