# YASKAWA Europe GmbH, Robotics Division

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.yaskawa.eu.com

YASKAWA is a global technology supplier of robotics, drive and control technology, as well as solutions in the energy sector.

It will be spotlighting the new MOTOMAN GA50 robot for 3D laser welding and cutting at high speeds at EuroBLECH 2022. It is particularly suited to 3D welding of complex contours and workpieces.

It has also introduced a new SP series of energy-efficient spot-welding robots that cover a payload range of 80 to 235kg. The MOTOMAN GP Shelf-type series now includes five shelf-type robot models with extended downward reach e.g. for elevated placement on steel structures.

It will also present its revised cobot portfolio with a new Ecosystem partner programme so that entry to robot automation becomes smarter and easier. YASKAWA has also expanded its compact cobot welding solution, Weld4Me, into the CE-compliant Weld4Me CE complete cell.

AIDA

www.aida-europe.com

Stamping specialist AIDA will be promoting its new DSF-NE2 servo press as well as the entire AIDA press line series (including servo presses, high-speed automatic presses for lamination and precision-forming presses). The company now has production facilities in Asia, Europe and North America, with a global presence in over 60 countries.

AIDA will also carry out live demos of ADMS (AIDA Digital Motion System), the solution it developed to analyse and optimise the management of transfer units for servo and mechanical presses.

The DSF-NE2 is a monolithic, two-point servo press that "combines the high rigidity of the monoblock frame with the flexibility of the freely programmable servo movement of AIDA DSF® technology.

"The DSF-NE2 presses series guarantees low-noise, low-vibration, multiple, varied-length forming, optimising all manufacturing/ maintenance operations and ensuring flexible applications and long die life spans. This press series can reach speeds of 120 strokes per minute," AIDA told ISMR.

AP&T

www.aptgroup.com

Under the theme of 'One Responsible Partner®', press specialist AP&T will be presenting its total offering to the sheet-metalforming industry. Its portfolio encompasses everything from turnkey production lines; advanced automation and AP&T's servo-hydraulic press to support packages; optimisation; maintenance; remote support; training; spare parts supply and other value-creating services.

"Our products and business models provide customers with the best conditions for high lifetime performance and sustainable profitability," it explained. "At EuroBLECH, visitors can also gain insight into the opportunities that we can offer as an employer."

AP&T offers turnkey solutions for fibre and sheetmetalforming based on its extensive knowledge of processes and machines as well as long-term dedication to its customers' business. Its global offering includes complete production lines with presses; automation; tools; heat-treatment systems and services such as maintenance; spare part provision; optimisation and rebuilds.
