Ultralow gas lens helps reduce torch size

Arc-Zone has released the Stubby Slam’d ultralow gas lens to help fabricators shrink the front end of their 17, 18, 26, and 3 series GTAW torches. The lens delivers the gas coverage needed to get good GTAW results without the bulk and weight of a standard gas lens collet body.

The lens helps reduce torch size for improved torch control in tight, restricted spaces, the company states.

Pipe sawing system makes clean, square cuts at the job site

RIDGID, a part of Emerson’s professional tools portfolio, has introduced the Pipe Saw for cutting pipe quickly in the shop or in the field. It is configured to cut steel and stainless steel, and with a quick switch to one of the optional blades it can cut plastic and cast steel.

The saw secures to the pipe with a three-point roller system that sets up and cuts up to 12-in. pipe with up to ½-in. wall thickness in minutes. Handlebars and feed rate speed monitoring with LED interface let the operator control rotation from an ergonomic position.

RIDGID

ridgid.com

Magnetic measuring system handles linear, angular measurement

Elesa offers the MPI-R10 magnetic measuring system for hands-free linear and angular measurement in metalworking production. It handles cutoff and positioning tasks for assembly processes that require stand-alone operation. The system’s accuracy and repeatability help minimize machining procedures, the company reports.

The device measures relative to a static frame-mounted magnetic band and plastic adhesive cover strip, with electronics housed in an IP54 or IP67 protected case, making it suitable for manual or semiautomatic workstations even in dirty, wet environments.

Elesa

www.elesa.com

Sheet followers hold materials up to 1,000 tons

Fab Supply has introduced a sheet following system for press brakes from 90 to 1,000 tons. Designed to handle large sheets and heavy parts, the system is available in three models: standard capacity (500 lbs./arm), heavy duty (700 lbs./arm), and maximum duty (1,200 lbs./arm). Each is customizable with additional arms, wider work surfaces, and scratch-resistant surfaces.

Features include programmable height adjustment from bend to bend, the ability to support a part bent to an included angle of 40 degrees, and overload protection.

Optional 10-kW capability offered for sheet lasers

BLM GROUP USA now offers an optional 10-kW fiber laser source with its LS5 and LC5 flat sheet laser cutters. These machines can cut steel, stainless steel, iron, copper, brass, and aluminum sheets in thicknesses from 0.039 to 1.37 in., as well as double sheets, depending on the material. Users can specify the power level, from 2 to 10 kW, that is best suited for their needs.

The systems are available in bed sizes of 10 by 5 ft., 13 by 6.5 ft., and 20 by 6.5 ft., all equipped with dual shelves and automatic loading/unloading and changeover. They offer longitudinal or transversal configuration, simultaneous axis speed up to 196 m/min., swift acceleration, and a rigid mechanical structure.

The LC5 also accommodates a tube processing module, with the sheet and tube functioning autonomously and sharing the cutting head. The tube processing module works with tubes up to 120 mm.
