# TWO INTO ONE

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

WELDING TECHNOLOGY specialist Fronius International presented its innovative tandem welding process CMT Twin to a wider public at the EuroBLECH 2012 exhibition. This novel twin-wire solution lets users exploit two cold-metal-transfer (CMT) processes, or combine a CMT process with a GMA pulsed-arc welding process, all within one single system.

“CMT Twin comes with two digital power sources that work completely separately from one another. This allows the weld processes to be individually adjusted to the different application requirements and also means that – within the given physical limitations – any wirefeeder can be chosen. Because it is possible to set widely diverging wirefeed speeds, as well as allowing great flexibility in the choice of welding processes, users can now precisely control the thermal input and deposition rate. This, in turn, lets them achieve the optimum welding speed and seam quality with virtually any welding position and combination of materials,” Fronius explained.

Because CMT uses the CMT process – in which the electrode is moved back and forwards in a controlled manner during welding to manually assist droplet detachment – it inputs considerably less heat than a conventional dip-transfer arc. Other advantages of CMT are that hardly any spattering occurs, and the process is even more stable since arc length is unaffected by the surface of the workpiece or the welding speed. As a result, it is possible to achieve high welding speeds; for a fillet weld that is welded at the lap joint of two 2mm steel sheets, these can be as fast as 4m/min.

To ensure a perfect weld from the very beginning, in CMT Twin the ‘trail’ electrode does not start work until the ‘lead’ electrode process has stabilised and the power source for the ‘trail’ electrode has received a start-up signal. CMT Twin combines the advantages of TimeTwin and CMT in one system. It is particularly well suited to welding light-gauge sheets at high speed, and for positional welding with high deposition rates without exceeding the optimum thermal input. www.fronius.com

OMAX Corporation showcased the high-speed, high-precision cutting performance of its OMAX and MAXIEM® machines and accessories at the EuroBLECH 2012 exhibition in Hannover. The company also debuted its new Intelli-VISOR™ system monitoring package to the European market.

EuroBLECH attendees saw live cutting demonstrations of the OMAX 60120 JetMachining® centre with a 36kW (50hp) EnduroMAX® Pump, Rotary Axis head and Tilt-A-Jet® nozzle, as well as the versatile MAXIEM 1515 JetCutting centre with a 30kW (40hp) direct drive pump and A-Jet® accessory.

The new Intelli-VISOR System Monitoring Package enables users to monitor every part of their machining process, from pump pressure to abrasive levels. With the addition of this new system, OMAX customers will experience even higher levels of productivity, said the manufacturer. OMAX and MAXIEM machines can rapidly cut a wide range of materials, from advanced composites and exotic alloys to traditional metals, rubber and glass.

“The OMAX 60120 is a bridgestyle abrasive jet machine featuring a table size of 3.7m x 2m and X-Y cutting travel of 3.2m x 1.6m. The machine’s 36kW (50hp) EnduroMAX Pump provides double the operating life and makes for faster part processing, lower operating costs and easier maintenance. The 60120 features our intuitive Intelli-MAX® software suite that calculates the precision of the velocity of a tool path at over 2,000 points per inch, allowing for complete control over the motion of an abrasive jet and enabling accurate, fast machining. The Rotary Axis head paired with the Tilt-A-Jet enable the 60120 to cut XYZ paths, creating complex 3D parts with virtually zero taper,” said OMAX. www.omax.com

## Weld thin sheet metal faster

A NEW “cold” MIG/MAG process from Lorch ensures more productivity when welding thin sheet metal. At EuroBLECH 2012, Lorch Schweißtechnik presented the latest addition to its MIG/MAG-Speed processes. This time, it focused on thin sheet metal and, with the SpeedCold, has developed an energyreduced i.e. “cold” process.

“The new process guarantees high weld quality, however it also enables the welder to weld sheet metal with a material thickness of between 0.5 and 3mm up to 40% faster,” Lorch explained.

The productivity increases through the SpeedCold process occur because Lorch technology, in all process sections, only introduces precisely the amount of energy that is required for an optimal welding result. A standard short arc reaches its limits for steel and CrNi stainless steel when welding 1mm metal sheets; with the SpeedCold, sheet metal thicknesses of 0.5mm can be achieved.
