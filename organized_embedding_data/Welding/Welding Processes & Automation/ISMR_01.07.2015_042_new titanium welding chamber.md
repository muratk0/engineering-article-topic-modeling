# New titanium welding chamber

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

High-precision radar for the steel industry

Researchers at Fraunhofer FHR install the radar at a strip mill in Eisenhüttenstadt. © Fraunhofer FHR

WEST COUNTRY WELDING SUPPLIES has launched its off-the-shelf welding chamber. Partnered with German manufacturer Mbraun, it has designed the welding chamber for the titanium and special metals welding market. “Aerospace, semiconductor, F1 racing, medical, scientific and nuclear industries have a demand for high quality welded components. Increasing standards have forced these industries to look again at their welding techniques and processes to achieve the perfect Titanium weld. We believe we have the solution – the 10/10 chamber is the future for welding Titanium and specialist materials,” said the company.

“Successful Titanium welds need to be carried out with minimal oxygen in the weld area. This purpose-designed chamber will reach 10ppm in around 10 minutes. This saves gas and money compared with oversized or old leaky chambers. These chambers are manufactured from high quality stainless steel with a full epoxy coating.”

The welding workstations have an ergonomic design for greater operator comfort with features such as shaded sliding eye shield and internal lighting as well as a fast flow gas purge system and insulated torch feedthrough. See www.westcoweld.co.uk

STEEL IS a key material in vehicle and machinery construction. Large quantities of offcuts and scraps are left over from rolling and milling crude steel into strip steel. A new radar system from Fraunhofer researchers in Wachtberg measures the width of the strip during fabrication to an accuracy of micrometres and helps to minimize scrap. This enables the rolling facility to self-adjust so that less scrap is produced with considerable savings in costs.

Two radar sensors mounted at the side of the rolls measure the distance to the edge of the steel. In principle, the system can be compared with echo-locating by bats.

“Our radar sends out continuous electromagnetic signals that are reflected by the right and left edges of the strip. The transmitted and received signals are then compared to each other with the help of numeric algorithms. The width of the sheet can be calculated from this comparison,” said Prof. Nils Pohl, scientist and head of department at FHR. The radar measures very quickly – 5000 times per second and operates even under severe conditions of dust, heat, steam and fog.”

In future, the high-frequency radar, which operates with electromagnetic waves of above 30GHz, will be mass produced.

## Press forming and tapping

TAIWANESE MANUFACTURER, YSM (YIH SHEN MACHINERY CO., LTD.) has been producing press forming machines since the 1980s and has developed a solid expertise in machinery R&D. The company recently sold its high-end press forming machines to the European market. The European market now takes up 20% of YSM's exports.

"The primary application of our machines is in the automotive and motorcycle sectors, such as tube- mounted components, brake components and headlight mount components," said Vice-General Manager, Lin Ching-Tang.

YSM machines for sheet as well as wire rods are known by customers from the automotive industry. For sheet forming, YSM does not adopt the continual mould design but pre-presses the sheet and has the sheet put onto machines for the forming process, which successfully reduces waste. Together with the all-in-one forming trend now prevalent in the industry, Mr. Lin and his R&D crews have integrated presses, forming machines, tapping machines, welding equipment and automation into one and this design has helped YSM customers to keep their costs down.

40% of YSM machines are for automotive and motorcycle sectors, a further 40% is for the Miniature Circuit Breaker sector and the remainder is for other sectors. Its showcased YSM-36T Strip & Wire Forming Machine handles strip thicknesses of 0.3-1.6mm, up to a maximum strip width of 40mm; wire diameter is Ø0.6 – 3.2mm. The YSM-36T is set up for two primary applications – forming and tapping.

The machine can produce all kinds of computer and electronic/ lighting components as well as fastener parts for the automotive sector. A PLC touch-screen displays production information on the screen. The forming slides feature double cams so that, when the forming tool returns, it will be driven by backward cam. The integration of backward cams and slide sensors prevents forming tools from sticking, protecting the equipment and prolonging the lifetime of the equipment. The machine is installed with INTERCOM automatic NC tapping made in Italy, providing a tapping solution for users. See www.ysm.com.tw
