# WELDING HIGH STRENGTH STEELS

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

Hobart Brothers has introduced a new welding wire designed for welding high strength steels that require good low temperature impact toughness. The new Hobart FabCO 107G all-position, gas-shielded flux-cored wire offers a tensile strength in the 100 to 110 ksi range in the as-welded or the post-weld heat-treated (PWHT) condition.

It is suitable for welding A515, A710, EQ56 and similar HSLA and Q&T steels found in offshore and structural applications. The wire can also be used to weld 4130 chrome-moly or 8630 nickel-chrome-moly pipes.

“The FabCO 107G wire has a smooth, stable arc that produces an excellent bead profile and helps to minimize downtime for rework associated with quality issues," Hobart told ISMR. "The wire also produces low spatter levels and has an easy-to-remove slag to help increase productivity and reduce post-weld clean-up."
