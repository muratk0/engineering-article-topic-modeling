Hobart has introduced its new IronMan 230 all-in-one MIG welder for mild steel material ranging from 24 ga. to 1/2-in. This 250-amp industrial grade unit replaces Hobart’s IronMan 210 and 250 models and features 12 voltage taps for greater precision control; suitable for those who need to weld with both light-gauge and thick materials.

Twelve voltage taps provide more precise arc control, while the option of a DP 3545-20 spool gun for aluminum welding makes the IronMan 230 a versatile performer for light industrial, MRO, body shop, farm and hobby applications. This DC/CV, single-phase unit is designed for ease of use.
