# ESAB wins Industrie Lyon award for WeldCloud

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

ESAB FRANCE SA has won the Innovation Award in the "Digital Tools" category at Industrie Lyon 2017, held from 4-7 April 2017 in Lyon, France.

"Beating over 160 innovations presented, ESAB's WeldCloud™ online data management system took the winning spot for its ability to provide digital solutions that optimise welding and associated manufacturing activities," said the company. "This innovation was driven by our need to help customers automate activities that surround welding processes and enable them to drive continuous improvements in productivity, quality and machine efficiency."

Industrie Lyon is held every two years, hosting 21,000 visitors and 900 exhibitors. The exhibition showcases a global range of manufacturing equipment and services – it has presented the Innovation Award for the past 15 years.

"WeldCloud data resides in one comprehensive database, located within a user's firewalls and not in a proprietary or provider-controlled cloud. Users have more peace of mind when they control their own data. WeldCloud features an open-architecture design for communication and analytics.
