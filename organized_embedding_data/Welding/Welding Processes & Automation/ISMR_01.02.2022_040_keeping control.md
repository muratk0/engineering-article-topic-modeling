# Keeping control

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

Welding specialist, Schnelldorfer Maschinenbau GmbH, has spent more than three years developing the redesigned neXt machine controller. The controller enjoyed its world premiere at the Blechexpo/ Schweisstec trade show in October 2021.

"This important development adds to our continuous further development in standardisation targeted at revolutionising welding automation, as well as expertise gained from more than 30 years of welding machine production and feedback from customers on more than 1000 successfully completed projects," it told ISMR.

The new generation of neXt controllers is an important building block in the manufacturer's portfolio of welding machines.
