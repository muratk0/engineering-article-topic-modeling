# Future welding ecosystem

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

THE WORLD IS changing fast, with digitalization reshaping industries and touching practically every sphere of life. While technological innovation plays a major part in the development of welding, it is no longer enough to focus on individual machines or processes.

"Future welding requires a holistic approach to the entire welding ecosystem, comprised of advanced machines, automation, application software, management systems and human skill. It takes advantage of the full potential of the Internet of Welding with cloud-enabled solutions and software. Future welding is the combination of all these elements that create customer value," Finnish welding specialist, Kemppi Oy, told ISMR.

"Kemppi is changing welding from dirty dull and dangerous into cool, clean and clever. We are rethinking future welding as a multifaceted whole that incorporates advanced technology with smart quality management process expertise and ultimate usability. We are continuously developing our offering to meet the evolving demands of the industry, constantly enabling safe infrastructures in the world." said Frederic Lanz, Vice President, Sales Europe and Global Projects, Kemppi Oy. "By re-identifying welders and understanding our customers value propositions to their end clients, we are building a welding ecosystem that can offer solutions to all welding needs."

The company's flagship product, the X8 MIG Welder, is a multi-process system which combines a focused and intense arc, upgradeable power source, usability and native connectivity with WeldEye welding management software into a versatile tool for industrial welding. The X8 MIG Welder can be used for MIG/MAG welding, stick (MMA) welding, MIG brazing, cladding and heavyduty gouging. The digital WPSs and wireless Control Pad enhance the solution's usability, making printed WPS documents unnecessary.

Integration and connectivity is essential in future welding. The universal WeldEye software is compatible with any brand's welding equipment. As a cloud-based service, it is always up-to-date according ISO, ASME and AWS standards, is accessible on any computer or hand-held device and available globally. "For welding production, this means more flexibility and increased process insight, even on a global scale," explained Kemppi. Kemppi's A7 MIG Welder can be integrated with any robot brand. Wise process packages are designed to improve welding productivity beyond regular MIG processes. The A7 MIG Welder's web-based interface allows the user to set up and control the power source from a regular PC or tablet. Welding curves can be updated later from the Kemppi DataStore.

"Our Gamma GTH3 helmet features new welding face shields and a fresh air respirator. The Gamma helmet has the highest TH3 level of protection, allowing welders to work with full confidence in their gear and focus completely on the task at hand," concluded Kemppi.
