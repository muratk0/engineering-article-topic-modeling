Welding robot made for high-density layouts

Yaskawa Motoman offers the 7-axis Motoman

®

VA1400 II welding robot. Located in the lower arm, the seventh axis acts as an elbow, simplifying torch access into tight places and allowing the robot to reach both sides of a part.

Available in floor-, wall-, and ceiling-mounted configurations, the robot is suitable for high-density layouts. It is compatible with the DX200 controller. The robot has a horizontal reach of 4.7 ft., vertical reach of 8.1 ft., and ±0.003-in. repeatability. It features integrated through-the-arm torch cabling, and power cable and welding utilities can be routed through the robot base.

Tool library added to design software

IronCAD has introduced the IronCAD Mechanical add-on, which offers a library of tools such as standard fasteners, steel sections, pipes, and flanges for use in the design process. Because knowledge and rules are built directly into the tools, typical day-to-day tasks such as bill of materials generation, component placement, and sizing are simplified, helping to reduce design time and effort.

A specialized mechanical dialog addresses the details required to create custom or standard components and assemblies. Users have new dialog forms for all standard components (such as screws, bearings, and gears) that graphically guide fabricators in the creation of assemblies with all appropriate settings like plate holes and clearances.

Tubing system delivers abrasive finishing to small-diameter pipe interiors

Pferd Inc. has introduced Polistar-Tube, a multistage tubing system that allows Polistar flexible abrasive stars to reach bore and pipe interiors. To prevent corrosion on stainless steel pipes, the system is manufactured using stainless steel rivets.

The abrasive stars are suitable for small-diameter bores and pipes from ¼ to 1.5 in. dia., performing cleaning, fine grinding, and very fine grinding of bores; removal of heat discoloration in stainless steel after welding; inlet and outlet radiusing of bores; light deburring; and deburring in cross bores.

The multistage internal fin-grinding system, used with the appropriate flexible shaft and motor drive, allows the abrasive stars to be used on difficult-to-access inner surfaces of pipes, pipe bends, and deep bores.

Laser cutting system reduces power consumption

Fonon Corp. has updated its TITAN™ flat-bed, multipurpose laser cutting machine, integrating multiple subsystems and introducing several changes to help minimize floor space requirements, reduce power consumption, and increase productivity.

Designed to consume less than 5 percent of the power required by common CO

2

laser systems, the TITAN FX turnkey laser cutting system has an integrated laser source and heat exchanger. Its modular laser is available in 1-, 2-, or 4-kW increments, allowing users to increase the laser cutting power up to 8 kW.

The machine can adjust dynamically to slight variations in thickness across the metal plate. Its sealed feedback sensor allows operation in harsh industrial environments. The unit’s frictionless, direct-drive, linear-motion platform; software-controlled orthogonality; and lightweight bridge enable high acceleration and on-the-fly cutting capabilities.

The gantry can be calibrated at the press of a button to help ensure the material is in alignment, free of seizures and unplanned machine failures. Power wattage upgrades to the laser do not require a specialized field engineer.

Portable, suction-based vacuum clamping protects surfaces

Festool USA has introduced the Vac Sys portable, suction-based clamping system. The system allows users to clamp nonporous material for machining and finishing while protecting delicate surfaces from clamp damage.

Each clamping module is equipped with a suction base that adheres to nonporous surfaces with a quick-release valve and can adapt to many surface sizes based on interchangeable vacuum cups. The system allows access to all sides without moving or manipulating clamps.

Material can be rotated 360 degrees or tilted from 0 to 90 degrees for greater access, putting large, awkward pieces within reach.
