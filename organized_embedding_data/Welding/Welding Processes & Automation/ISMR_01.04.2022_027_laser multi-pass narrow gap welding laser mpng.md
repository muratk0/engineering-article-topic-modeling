# Laser Multi-Pass Narrow Gap Welding (Laser MPNG)

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

The laser is positioned at the joint between the two sheet edges to be welded, while a filler metal is added. The energy of the laser beam melts the edges of the workpieces, as well as the filler metal from the wire, which then fills the void between the two pieces and creates a high-quality weld seam. The process can be used to weld typical joint configurations in steel construction.

The sheet edges are plasma-cut and the joint sometimes has gaps of up to 2mm-wide, which the laser welding process can bridge. When welding either a web plate (T-joint) or a butt joint, the process ensures that the joint is complete i.e. that the two parts are joined across the entire contact area. In conventional steel construction, this is not always 100 per cent guaranteed due to the process involved. There are also technological limits, particularly when using T-joints.
