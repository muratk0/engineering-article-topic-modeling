## FLEXIBLE AUTOMATION SOLUTIONS

SALVAGNINI HAS been an important benchmark for the flexible automation sector ever since 1963.The modularity of the upgradeable and flexible highperformance systems that Salvagnini designs and manufactures in-house offer a number of advantages.

The range of systems currently comprises: the P4Xe and P2Xe hybrid panel benders; the S4Xe integrated punch-shearing system; the S4+P4 flexible, integrated punching and bending system; the L3 and L5 high-dynamic fiber laser cutting machines; the new SL4 integrated punching and fiber laser cutting system; the E2, B2 and B3 KinEtic press-brakes; the ROBOformER® robotic cell and factory logistics solutions for automatic feeding from compact or extended pack store-towers.

At the Lamiera exhibition, Salvagnini showcased some of these technologies. ■

## Hybrid Panel Benders

THE P4XE is the first in its new generation of adaptive hybrid panel benders, offering maximum automation and productivity yet featuring consumption reduced to less than 11 kW, teamed with the new, ergonomic HPT feeding and unloading system. The P4Xe’s manipulator, clamp and rotator have been redesigned to maximise the machine’s versatility. For example, the new clamp descent control allows cycle times to be reduced and the clamp closing force to be adjusted.

The mean in-cycle electrical consumption of the 2116 model is less than 11 kW. On request, the panel bender can be equipped with an automatic crowning system. Sophisticated modifications recently made to the bladeholder allow the P4Xe to compensate automatically for flexure and deflection that occur during the cycle. ■
