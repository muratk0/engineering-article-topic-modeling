# AN EYE ON INNOVATION

[TARİH: 01.01.2020 ISMR]

Kemppi welding offers welding solutions (intelligent equipment, welding management software and expert services) for demanding industrial applications and ready-to-weld needs. Its global partner network covers over 60 countries.

Founded in 1949, the family-run company numbers over 800 employees. It also owns the Norwegian software company, WeldIndustry AS, and the Italian welding group, Trafimet Spa, which operate as independent brands within their area of specialty.

John Frost, Kemppi Group Product Manager Portable & Compact Equipment & Safety, outlines the company's technical focus and its latest new technology innovations to ISMR.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers at the moment and how are you addressing these issues for them?

JF: Kemppi customers expect high levels of performance; it's what the Kemppi brand defines. Performance can, of course, be measured simply from the product experience itself. But today's physical product or service performance is only one element in the measurement of overall customer satisfaction. Customer partnerships demand interactive support and communication, on either side of 'physical product' deliverables or service supply, pre- and post-purchase. Kemppi and its partners are there to deliver customer satisfaction.

ISMR: What is your technical focus this year? Please elaborate

JF: Today, Kemppi is focused on increasing the 'cadence and flow' of new value product to market, high-performance welding products and service solutions. As a Kemppi Group product manager, my specific area of responsibility focus is on portable, compact and safety product solutions. 2019 has been a particularly busy year, in terms of new product releases.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in welding markets?

JF: Price is an area of increasing competition, but the lowest price carries the highest risk for the customer and inevitably fails to deliver value over the expected product lifecycle. 'Soft product elements' become negatively impacted and those, in the end, count towards the overall brand value measurement and postpurchase evaluation. Kemppi focuses on value.

ISMR: Which new products and technologies have you launched over the last few months?

JF: 2019 has seen the launch of exciting new SAFETY products, new TIG welding equipment and new TIG torches, all separately offering refreshing user value innovation.

For example, Kemppi personal respiratory systems have created a 'big noise' in the markets since launch. Protecting welders and welding related trades from the harmful effects of airborne contamination, from welding and related processes, we now offer something truly new for the protection of workers' health. Practical advantages include excellent respiratory protection standards and innovations such as integrated LED work lights powered from the LiFE+ rechargeable PAPR battery packs, featured in the GAMMA XFA product series.

True innovation is, of course, a result of investment in research and development and it doesn't come for free. With over 100 Kemppi personnel dedicated to future innovation projects and reliable maintenance of existing models, the Kemppi brand defines its positioning as 'the most investable welding brand' choice. Investment security is important for our customers and we take that responsibility seriously.

ISMR: Please outline the main benefits of the MASTERTIG, FLEXLITE and GAMMA Safety product ranges? How would you summarise these for customers?

JF: The new MASTERTIG is, firstly, a great welding machine - its welding arc control and stability is, again, a step beyond that of previous competitive models. But it is in usability, productivity and environmental areas where customers will enjoy extended value from the new MASTERTIG machines, including 60% faster set-up times, 30% increased welding speed (application-specific) and more than 20% reduction in audible noise levels.

For example, new MASTERTIG is 60% faster to set up, using the WELDASSIST auto set-up feature. By selecting WELDASSIST, users input the material, thickness, joint type and welding position and Kemppi WELDASSIST pre-sets the machine parameters so that users are welding in seconds!

WELDASSIST is great for professionals and students. In fact, students can instantly see how the variable TIG process set-up parameters differ between alternative materials, joint type and welding position. This supports their learning and technical knowledge transfer, making the 'young guns' of our industry quickly more productive and highly skilled.

FLEXLITE is a new family of welding guns and TIG torches. Our development focus was in 'key' measurable areas. Firstly, the user experiences the benefit of 'improved balance' in the hand, reducing wrist loading and operator fatigue. Plus, the lifecycle of changeable parts, such as contact tips, is increased, through to improved cooling efficiency that reduces contact tip and nozzle temperatures, extending life and welding performance.

SAFETY protection has long been part of the Kemppi offering, but 2019 witnessed a major offering gear change for our customers, with the introduction of new welding helmets and respiratory protection solutions for welding and related trades. Kemppi's respiratory family now includes, BETA, GAMMA and DELTA models.

Work-related health risks really came into focus for the welding industry during 2019, with the IARC (International Agency for Research on Cancer) defining all welding as a carcinogenic process. Therefore, depending upon the type and concentrations of welding fumes, users and employers must select the appropriate respiratory protection to work safely and protect health. Work-related respiratory disease is preventable, of course, if the appropriate protection is worn and correctly maintained. We now provide alternative high-end respiratory protection models, including GAMMA XFA models, featuring integrated LED work lights and respiratory protection (offering 99.8% efficiency).

ISMR: Are there any products/technologies that are releasing next year that you would like to discuss?

We will launch more new SAFETY models in January 2020, including new welding face-shields and welders' respirators, that are the lightest physical weight in their class! New MIG/MAG welding guns and welding machines will also be released during 2020 but you'll have to wait and see!

JF: Kemppi is a solid and high-performance business. We aim to deliver on our commitments and expect the same from our partners, on behalf of our customers around the world.

Having worked for Kemppi for thirty years, I can guarantee that our intentions and commitments run deep and our integrity whole. We're not perfect, but we aim to be...

As we are even more focused on the ‘cadence and flow' of new innovation to market, you can expect new releases, new innovations and new performance solutions from us.
