## Platform for product launches

Simufact.welding was selected as one of the most innovative exhibits at the show. Simufact's welding simulation software (Simufact.welding) was nominated as one of the three most innovative exhibits for the shortlist of the EuroBLECH Award –‘joining’ category. The EuroBLECH Award is given by Blechnet and MM MaschinenMarkt. Award winners were Grenzebach Maschinenbau and Fronius Deutschland.

After an eight-year development period, Simufact’s new version 4 comes very close to the demands of the manufacturing industry: an efficient and comfortable model design, consideration of special welding-specific boundary conditions, adjustable meshing and re-meshing, a material data bank with complete data sets for all welding relevant material classes, practical and application-oriented handling and pre-settings.

Many manufacturers use the EuroBLECH exhibition as an opportunity to launch new products, technologies, software and processes. A selection of new product launches are highlighted here.

AWL-Techniek showcased various new developments including its Quick Motion spot welding cell (that significantly shortens weld-toweld time and has a smaller footprint), laser exhaust welding using laser hybrid welding and clamping technique improvements (via localised air nozzles with compressed air).

BAUST presented new coil-driven rotary punches with variable tool concepts at EuroBLECH. Its engineers designed two new punch cassettes that are flexible, easy to handle, productive, economical and use no motors.

“BRC punch cassettes are mechanically driven by the coil. A very precise set of male/female punch tools interacts like a sprocket wheel. At any moment, more than one punch is fully engaged with the die. With its rotating action, the punch tool transforms the force pulling the web into punch force. We call this 'coil-driven' punching. Today, roll forming machines generate enough pulling power to drive rotary punches by the web/coil, making the compact BRC suitable for integration on roll forming mills,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

Besides the coil drive principle, the new BRV features easy product change using its variable tool concept. A pressurized air cylinder opens the tools wide enough to easily load a new coil/strip, or, remove a flange mounted tool set and replace it with a set for new punch pattern. The tools stay synchronised even when the tools are opened. The operator does not have to worry about tool alignment when mounting the new set. Now tool changes can be made in minutes. A pneumatic cylinder comfortably closes the tools even when the new strip is running at 60m/min. or more.

Bolzenschweißtechnik GmbH, the German stud welding machine manufacturer, presented its new machine and weld stud catalogue for 2015/2016 at EUROBLECH 2014. This catalogue, with over 100 pages, contains a complete range of stud welding machines from simple handheld devices to CNC controlled stud welding systems. Also included are numerous innovative welding systems and welding accessories such as the award-winning mobile stud welding station, the HesoMatic-9, which unites energy source, control and feeding units into one single and compact housing.

Among its new products, the new SK-5AKS multifunctional automatic welding head (patent- pending DE 10 2013 201 463 A1) was launched at the trade fair. This welding head unites all the elements for gap and contact welding into one compact housing and can be switched instantly from one operation to the next in one hand movement, eliminating painstaking conversion or the need to switch the welding head. This represents not just a practical but also an economic gain for the user, as in future there will be no need to acquire a second welding head and the cost of purchase is significantly reduced.

KASTO exhibited products from its sawing and storage portfolio at EuroBLECH in Hanover. Its new bandsawing machine range, KASTOwin, was on display, consisting of five automatic bandsaw models which cover a cutting range from 330 to 1060mm. These machine tools are suitable for universal use and all types of material, representing a flexible solution for multiple applications.

The fully automatic pivot-type workshop saw KASTOpractical A2 was also on display. This model has been designed for efficient cutting and mitring of profile, tube and solid bar. A two-speed drive unit delivering 35m/min. or 70m/min. and a fully hydraulic feed are just two features.

Automated storage was also be represented at the show with the KASTOuniline. It is a longitudinal design and is best suited to long and narrow spaces, up to 25 metres in height. It is particularly suited to all sheet format pallets from small to large and extra-large as well as for cassettes holding tube and profile and cubic products.

KASTOuniline can be connected directly to processing machinery such as laser cutting machines and press brakes. The exhibition model at EuroBLECH demonstrated integrated robotic sorting from the cut nesting program for unattended production in a typical laser processing company.

Voortman Steel Machinery showcased its solution to bevel without the use of corner loops, which does not increase the waste rate and still creates high quality corners. During the motion of the first bevel, the torch already turns to the right angle for the next bevel. In a situation that requires two V-bevels, the torch will protrude the plasma arc during the first V-bevel and then drag the plasma arc during the second V-bevel.

“Because corner loops are no longer needed, the total cutting length per nesting is shorter which is equivalent to a decrease in costs. Based on test results, waste reductions of 20% are possible with our new bevel solution,” Voortman told ISMR.
