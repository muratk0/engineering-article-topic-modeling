# Portable fume extractor

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

EXTRACTABILITY, a division of Weldability-Sif, is a UK-based “one-stop source” for welding fume extraction solutions. The company has just launched the new ProtectoTwin unit, a twin high-pressure turbine with adjustable speed control designed to extract and filter the metal particulate fumes created in welding processes.

“The ProtectoTwin is portable, weighing 25kg, and is supplied on swivel castors. Its small dimensions (300mm x 300mm x 670mm) mean that it is easily manoeuvrable and so suitable for a number of applications. These include small fabrication shops, single welding bays with on-torch fume extraction and maintenance welding,” explained the company.

“The unit is supplied with a 2.5m hose and magnetic foot welding nozzle, and is fitted with a BIA M filter cartridge that filters hazardous substances. Dual ports facilitate local extraction via hood and torch extraction from a single unit. The filter is automatically cleaned with compressed air, providing a long filter life. The particles are collected in a dust collection drawer and can be easily removed from the unit.”
