# Industrial welding in focus

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

KEMPPI’S FASTMIG X product series offers strong welding quality for demanding industrial applications. It features a series of alternative high-end set-ups for different purposes:

■ FastMig X Regular for MIG/MAG pulse welding.
■ FastMig X Pipe for pipe and root welding.
■ FastMig X Intelligent for diversified welding applications and for all metals and processes.
■ FastMig X Tailored versions to suit customer specific needs.

The new FastMig X series (pictured above) is a modular welding system that can be optimised for specific professional welding applications by choosing suitable components and software package options. Arc Mobile Control software brings a new and flexible way to use and control the welding machine for production management and for welders.

“When the ArcVoltage feature is enabled, FastMig X automatically shows the actual arc voltage on the display during welding. This means that you can see exactly the right voltage from the welding arc, and can easily keep your welding values inside the pre-defined range regardless of the size and length of the cables,” explained Kemppi.

Kemppi’s ArcValidator (below) is ‘the first ever automated and universal solution for welding equipment validation’, the Finnish manufacturer told ISMR. It offers fast, consistent welding equipment validation, meeting ISO 3834 and EN 50504 standards. Among the benefits it offers are up to 80 per cent faster validations, reporting and standards-compliant documentation and certificate creation. It is compatible with all brands and models of welding machines and welding processes with absolute unit meters.
