# Welding and cutting focus

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

ESAB, A SPECIALIST in welding and cutting technologies, will be on stand number A116 in Hall 4 at Industrie Lyon, which takes place from 4-7 April 2017 at EUROEXPO LYON, France. ESAB will be exhibiting several products to visitors seeking new welding and cutting systems.

Its main focus will be the Rebel EMP215iC, a MIG/TIG/Stick welder and 120V - 230V, CC/CV welding system, providing multi-process arc performance, location flexibility, lightweight portability and an operator interface with LCD/TFT display. Also featured will be the Rebel EMP 235iC and EM235iC, bigger machines which hold 15kg wire. Alongside the Rebel will be the new Sentinel™ A50 automatic welding helmet.

"The helmet's radical new design incorporates ergonomic headgear and maximum adjustability to increase productivity, functionality and usability.

The Sentinel A50's high-tech features include adjustable 5-point headgear, a 100 x 60mm viewing area, an externally activated Grind Button, a colour touch-screen control panel and a frontloading convex cover lens that comes in different colour options and changes in ten seconds," said ESAB.

Also on display will be the new Renegade ES300i MMA (SMAW)/LiveTIG (GTAW) welder. The inverter-based MMA/TIG machine weighs 15kg, measures 460 x 200 x 320mm and produces a top output of 300 amps at 40% duty cycle. The MA25 Pulse, supported by the AristoMig 4004i pulsed GMAW (MIG/ MAG) system, will also be on display.

The system includes the new, intuitive MA25 Pulse Panel, and the upgraded Aristo MIG 4004i Pulse power source. The Aristo MIG 4004i Pulse now incorporates quicker responding hardware and new software controls for better arc starts, especially with aluminium and stainless steel. See www.esab.com
