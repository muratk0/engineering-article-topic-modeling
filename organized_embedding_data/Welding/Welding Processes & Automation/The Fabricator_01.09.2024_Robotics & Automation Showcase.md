SCALABLEARC™ BY SCALABLE ROBOTICS

ScalableArc™ is the NO CAD, NO CODE, point-and-click solution for industrial welding that reduces programming time by 10X! ScalableArc™ allows welders to operate industrial robots with zero prior experience. It’s as simple as scan, click, weld! The robot starts by scanning the workpiece and creating a 3D model, capturing everything the welder sees, including all brackets and fixtures. After the scan, the welder uses our wand to simply point and click the weld locations. These teaching points are then captured and validated in the 3D model. Next, the robot automatically fine-tunes teaching points, while allowing the welder to make final torch adjustments through our user-friendly app. That’s it! Now the robot is ready to weld.

ScalableArc™ automatically adjusts for seam variation during production, ensuring first-pass, high-quality welds. This eliminates the need for highly precise parts and costly fixturing.

ScalableArc™ supports all the functions of an industrial welding robot including multi-pass welding, stitch welding, seam tracking, external axes, tracks, gantries, etc.

Installation takes just a couple of hours, and within minutes, welders are ‘click welding’. ScalableArc’s™ easy teaching method can be installed on new or existing robot systems (including cobots) to turn your robot into a high mix robotic welding solution.
