# New welding innovations

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

IN HALL 3 on stand J113 at the forthcoming Schweissen and Schneiden exhibition, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH will showcase a wide range of welding processes as well as new automation solutions. Visitors can expect a completely automated production system and live demonstrations on different materials.

“The focus of the Cloos exhibition stand is, amongst others, the improved Tandem Weld process with a new torch and perfectly matched QINEO Tandem welding power sources. For Tandem Weld, two separate welding wires are fed and melted simultaneously in one molten pool. The advantages of the Tandem Weld process include a high deposition rate, a high process speed and low heat input,” Cloos told ISMR.

With Cold Weld, Cloos offers a process for extremely low heat input into the component. The QINEO CHAMP AC generates an alternating current with a special pulse form for control of the welding process. Benefits of the process include low spatter formation, high welding speeds and a good gap-bridging ability.

“The Rapid Weld process is used when high deposition rates and optimum penetration depths at high process speeds are required. Even full penetration through joints is possible with wall thicknesses of up to approx. 6-8mm so that it is not necessary to weld the back,” explained Cloos.

Cloos will also present laser hybrid welding and cutting at the show. This process has many advantages such as a minimum weld preparation and post processing, very short production times and fewer working steps. The laser also marks the components as well as drilling boreholes. With its chained robot welding system, including automated loading and unloading of robot stations using QIROX robots and QINEO power sources, the automated system will be demonstrated at the exhibition on work pieces from different industries.

Cloos will also display modern software to program robot systems and document welding technology. Its welding simulator WELDPLUS, for training welders, will be on its show booth. Cloos experts will also give presentations during the "66th IIW International Conference" and the "Welding Congress 2013" at the exhibition.
