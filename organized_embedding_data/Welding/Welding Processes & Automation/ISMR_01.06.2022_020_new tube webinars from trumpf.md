# New tube webinars from TRUMPF

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

Designers across the manufacturing industry are increasingly taking advantage of the benefits of metal tube and profiles which are laser cut and welded. This, in turn, is opening up huge commercial potential for the metalworking sector.

Through the development of its own range of TruLaser tube machines, TRUMPF will be running two free webinars from 10am on 14 June 2022 and 2pm on 15 June 2022 to show participants how to programme tube parts using newly enhanced TRUMPF programming tube software.

It links directly to the new TRUMPF TruLaser Tube 3000, on which the parts will be cut during the webinars. TRUMPF specialists will also answer questions on all aspects of laser tube and profile cutting as well as their applications.
