# The future of welding

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

ISMR took a tour of ABICOR BINZEL's booth at this September's Schweissen and Schneiden exhibition (Stuttgart, Germany) to view its latest welding innovations. Under the motto 'People, Planet, Progress', it showcased its complete torch portfolio. This ranged from manual torches for extraction at source to the new automatic extraction torch, xFUME® AUT, and the robot extraction torch, xFUME® ROBO (with matching extraction system).

The xFUME® AUT extraction torch is suitable for welders who weld individual component types in high volumes and with simple, partially automated processes or for environments where liquid or gaseous media is transported through pipes. It can be used for processes such as pipe, beam and plate welding.

The xFUME® ROBO extraction torches with externally guided cable assembly (OA for over arm) and xFUME® extraction system is suitable for fume extraction in robotic welding, welding cells or production halls. The extraction tube can be adjusted to the torch bending angle, the torch bending radius and the torch neck length (according to the application).

"Our manual torches have an even better grip, while our robot torches play in the top league in terms of cooling capacity, gas coverage and stability. When welding torches can be used for longer periods of time, it is not only worthwhile economically for users but also for the environment as a whole," explained the welding manufacturer.

The future of robotic welding was also highlighted on the booth with demonstrations of its iBOT® one welding cobot for an easy start into automation, as well as further system solutions around the robotic welding cell and pipe welding. Its new iROB® power source line (up to 500 amps), for simple to demanding processes, was also on display.

"With its self-explanatory, intuitive/ easy operation and repeat accuracy of +/-0.02mm in every application as well as its high flexibility, the iBOT® one will soon be indispensable in welding production. Operators can use the recorded path to move the robot from weld to weld with no collisions. Small batches and individual parts can now be produced in an automated process, depending upon the welding task," explained the manufacturer.

The ABICELL is a robotic plug and play solution that comes with fully equipped welding products and carries CE certification.

With more than 35 subsidiaries, 20 exclusive sales partners and over 1200 employees worldwide, welding specialist ABICOR BINZEL Group is represented in over 50 countries.
