# LINCOLN ELECTRIC acquires Tennessee Rand, Inc.

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

LINCOLN ELECTRIC HOLDINGS, INC. has acquired the privately-held automated systems and tooling manufacturer, Tennessee Rand, Inc. Tennessee Rand is a US specialist in the design and manufacture of tooling and robotic systems for welding applications. The company serves a wide base of automotive and metal fabrication customers.

"Tennessee Rand strengthens our already strong position as a market leader in welding automation in North America," said Christopher L. Mapes, Chief Executive Officer. "It brings extensive tool design, system building and machining capabilities that will enable us to further expand our welding automation business. It is a nice complement to our recent acquisition of Wayne Trail Technologies."

Tennessee Rand, Inc., with headquarters and manufacturing operations in Chattanooga, Tennessee (USA) has annual sales of approximately US$ 35 million. Lincoln Electric focuses on the design, development and manufacture of arc welding products, robotic arc welding systems, plasma and oxyfuel cutting equipment and has a leading global position in the brazing and soldering alloys market.
