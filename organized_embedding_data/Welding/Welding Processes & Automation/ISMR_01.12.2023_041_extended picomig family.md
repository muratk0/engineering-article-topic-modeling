# Extended Picomig family

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

New welding machines from EWM's Picomig family are designed as robust, compact allrounders. Both the Picomig 220 and Picomig 225 can be used to achieve welding currents of 220A with a 230V connection.

"Users benefit from low power consumption and reduced CO₂ emissions. We are expanding our welding machine series with the Picomig 220 and Picomig 225 models, designed for wire spool sizes of D200/5kg and D300/15kg respectively. The multi-process inverter welding machines feature welding processes for pulsed MIG/MAG welding and standard MIG/MAG welding as well as TIG and MMA welding. All necessary characteristics for steel, CrNi, aluminium, CuSi and flux-cored wires are included at no extra charge," explained EWM.

"The welding machines in the Picomig family are easy to operate and allow users to get started right away without time-consuming training. They are even suitable for standard mains connections. The machines can be individually adapted to the relevant welding task at hand. The portable machines are intended for mobile welding applications but can also be quickly and easily mounted onto a trolley with various systems available for users to do so. High efficiency levels of the inverter technology also ensure more energy efficiency in operation while optimised ignition characteristics provide for the best welding results.
