Small GTAW torch

The CK Micro Torch is the smallest GTAW torch on the market, according to manufacturer CK Worldwide. With three different angled heads, the torch can be used in many tightly spaced applications.

A clear Pyrex® nozzle and a low-profile torch body allow the operator to see the weld puddle. The water cooling jacket lets the torch reach its highest amperage possible, even with a 3/32-in. tungsten electrode.

The torch is available in 70-amp gas-cooled and 140-amp water-cooled versions. Each torch comes with 45-, 90-, and 180-degree heads; three presharpened tungsten electrodes; a tungsten stickout gauge; two Pyrex nozzles; one alumina nozzle; and Super Flex® cables.

Parts washing system

Walter Surface Technologies has added the CleanBox Flow to the BIOCIRCLE® line of manual industrial parts washing systems. Suitable for the bulk cleaning of small to medium-sized parts, the compact unit is designed for use with Bio-Circle CB 100, a natural, water-based solvent that is biodegradable and VOC-free.

The parts washer comprises a sink and flow-through cleaning brush, a basin, and a heavy-duty pump that circulates through heavy oils, greases, and contaminants. When paired with the solvent, the washer enables the quick removal of hardened greases, oils, and residues from parts.

Features include an air gun to facilitate the quick drying of industrial parts, a two-level cart that puts the washer at a suitable height for working and makes the system mobile, and a lid to prevent contaminants from entering the system.

The solvent contains Nature Boost, a raw material derived from vegetable extracts that acts as a natural solvent with water. The solvent removes greases, ink, rubber marks, tar, wax, carbon, soot, and pastes. It cleans at room temperature in manual, immersion, presoaking, and ultrasonic applications. The noncorrosive, biodegradable, NSF-certified solvent is compatible with steel, stainless steel, aluminum, and nonferrous metals.

Brazing system

In collaboration with the joining technology development department at Audi AG, Fronius has introduced CMT Braze+ with a new, patented gas nozzle shape that allows high brazing speeds.

According to the manufacturer, initial applications of the new process in Audi AG’s autobody shops have demonstrated that under real series-production conditions, robot-assisted brazing speeds of up to 9.84 FPM are feasible.

The extremely narrow, conical shape of the newly developed gas nozzle causes the shielding gas to emerge at high speed. This, in turn, constricts the arc, allowing brazing to be carried out more rapidly. At the same time, gas consumption decreases to 0.79 to 1.32 GPM.
