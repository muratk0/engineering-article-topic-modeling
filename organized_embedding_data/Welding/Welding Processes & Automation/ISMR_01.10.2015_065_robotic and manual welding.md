FROM 3-6 NOVEMBER 2015, at the trade fair duo of Blechexpo and Schweisstec in Stuttgart, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik will present a wide range of manual and automated welding on stand 6407 (Hall 6).

“The focus of our exhibition stand will be on an automated robot system equipped with an automated torch-changing system. We will present the new Tandem Weld generation for process-safe and quick and comfortable welding. With the Cold Weld process, an alternating current produces a very special pulse form, which brings about extremely low heat input. The Laser Hybrid Weld process is the combination of a laser beam with a MIG/MAG welding process in one common process zone, using the advantages of both processes. Narrow Gap Weld technology offers particular economic efficiency for welding thick-walled component parts,” explained CLOOS.

“Another highlight will be the new fully automated current tip and gas nozzle changer, SpareMatic, which allows non-stop welding with operative components. When the welding robot needs new nozzles, the automatic current tip and gas nozzle changer comes into play. The changer takes the torch consumables and replaces them for new ones. Due to its compact, modular design, the SpareMatic can be integrated into both new and existing systems.”

CLOOS will also present the wide QINEO model range of welding machines for manual and automated welding such as the new QinTron inverter welding machine. There will be live demonstrations of manual welding on its booth at the exhibition. See www.cloos.de

DEES HYDRAULIC INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD. is a well-known hydraulic press manufacturer with factories in Taiwan and China. Its main products include deep drawing and die spotting presses as well as related hydraulic press technology. It now has a full product portfolio that serves the entire automotive industry from tandem lines, die spotting and tryout presses to hemming presses. Its specialty, however, is hydraulic presses. It currently utilises servo motors to increase the production capacity of its hydraulic presses and is adding accumulators to increase press speed (on its tandem lines, for example).

Its HD series presses are pre-engineered to fit most applications or can be customised to suit specific applications. Features of its new automotive tandem press line include a computerized frame design using Finite Element Analysis; cylinder tube and piston row with one-piece design; maintenance-free and adjustable eight-point, oil-less guiding plates and overload protection valves.

“An efficiently designed hydraulic circuit block with fewer pipes and connections and accessible open side-frame suitable for automation equipment (front to back or left to right) are also features of the line. There is an adjustable daylight speed change-point cushion stroke for easy operation and rapid set-up via limit switches control and ram lock device. Other features include a Quick Die Change System (QDC), moving bolster and auto load and unload system. Accessories include a colour NC touch-screen with linear transducer and stand-alone oil heat exchanger,” added the company. See www.deesgroup.com.tw
