# Welding in focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

Portable cordless MMA welding machine

AT EUROBLECH 2016, Hall 13, stand F126, Kemppi will launch three new solutions aimed at redefining the performance and usability, health and safety of welders as well as welding management capabilities in extreme industrial welding production.

Kemppi will unveil a multifunctional welding solution designed to increase welding performance and quality using a focused and efficient arc, an upgradeable power source and a practical wireless user interface that features full integration with welding management tools, including digital welding procedure specifications WPSs.

For the health and comfort of welders, Kemppi will also introduce its latest personal protective equipment, including respirators offering TH3 levels of respiratory protection. The new welding face shields and FreshAir respirators feature new innovations, including Comflex system and integrated LED work lights. In ready-to-weld portables, Kemppi will introduce a convenient cordless MMA cell pack for 'off grid' welding.

Together with Weldindustry, Kemppi recently launched the new WeldEye welding management software. WeldEye gives users real-time insight into the performance of welders and the progress of projects, as well as traceable compliance with WPSs and international welding standards, regardless of which brand's welding equipment. This makes it easier to reduce costs, improve performance and conduct quality controls. It is designed to streamline welding production as well as to cut overhead costs due to less manual work, giving (says Kemppi) 70 per cent faster documentation processes. At the end of a project, Kemppi explained, the time spent on creating documentation can be cut by weeks or months.

“New unified WeldEye software dramatically decreases the time spent on documentation and gives everyone involved in welding processes unprecedented insight into the work being done. Gone are the days of manually compiled documentation in binders or Excel sheets – with WeldEye, all the information you need is available at the push of a button," Kemppi told ISMR.
