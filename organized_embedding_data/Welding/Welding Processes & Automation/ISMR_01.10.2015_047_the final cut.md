# The final cut

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

AT THIS YEAR’S Blechexpo on 3-6 November 2015 in Stuttgart, Germany, Kjellberg will showcase its extensive expertise in welding, plasma and laser cutting as well as mechanical engineering. Its focus is on the presentation of two new power sources.

“The Smart Focus series for plasma cutting is extended by a new model which also uses patented Contour Cut technology to achieve very high cut quality. Furthermore, a new power source for plasma arc welding will be on show for the first time. Its potential for joint welding and hardfacing will be demonstrated through its user-friendly process control. The very high cutting and welding quality and speed of our ‘Made in Germany’ products, such as the new TIG technology InFocus, can also be seen during live demonstrations on our stand 5110 in Hall 5 during Blechexpo,” Kjellberg told ISMR.

Kjellberg’s Blechexpo showcase will be completed by its new InFocus welding torch, the HiFocus plasma cutting series, the XFocus laser and various mechanical engineering services. See the company at Blechexpo in Hall 5, stand 5110 or visit www.kjellberg.de
