# Welding 4.0 in action

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

IN KEEPING WITH the "Welding 4.0" slogan, EWM is introducing its solution to the urgent challenge of Industry 4.0 for the welding industry - the new multi-process MIG/MAG pulse welding machine series, Titan XQ puls.

"It has comprehensive digital network capabilities and can therefore unlock significant untapped potential for savings throughout the entire process chain. In addition, it also includes efficiency-enhancing, innovative welding processes from EWM as standard in the machines at no extra charge. Titan XQ puls offers durability, robust design and high power reserves," EWM told /SMR.

In addition to other digital functions, the machines are network-compatible via the Welding 4.0 welding management system, ewm Xnet. Welding procedure specifications can be transmitted directly from the planning office to the Titan XQ puls - paperless and including all parameters. It is able to deal with a large part of the previously time-consuming welding documentation automatically with ewm Xnet.

The 350 A, 400 A, 500 A and 600 A power variants are initially offered as compact versions with the separate lightweight 13kg wire feeder, which has also been newly developed. Titan XQ puls inverters are designed for hard continuous operation in three-shift operation.

The company guarantees a duty cycle of 80% (Titan XQ puls 350, 400 and 500).

There are now four versions of the new PM welding torch with control buttons, graphic display and LED illumination of the working area as well as the new, ergonomically-shaped grip with rubber insert. There are also three controllers to choose from, which EWM is presenting with Titan XQ puls. The premium model is called Expert XQ 2.0. See www.ewm-group.com
