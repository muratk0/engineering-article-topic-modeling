# The welding process chain

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

GERMANY’S LARGEST arc welding technology manufacturer, EWM AG, will be showcasing numerous innovations relating to its Multimatrix welding technology at the DVS Expo in Germany (14-17 September 2015).

“Users report cost savings of up to 60 per cent in practice with Multimatrix technology. This is achieved by reducing the consumption of energy and materials, which also significantly decreases the time taken to perform welding work and increases productivity,” explained EWM AG. “The free EN 1090 WPQR package not only includes diverse welding procedure specifications relevant for practical use, but also replaces the welding procedure tests required for the EN 1090 certification.”

EWM’s forceArc puls is a new MIG/MAG welding process with straightforward handling. Benefits include minimal finishing work thanks to a virtually spatter-free arc, reduced heat input and higher welding speed. EWM’s WPS-Tool software allows welding procedure specifications to be created quickly and easily managed for different workflows. Used in combination with the Xnet quality management software, this data can be directly stored on the corresponding welding machines and individual access rights to the data assigned for each user. This is designed to ensure welding quality and helps with the management of welding tasks.

Welding ferromagnetic workpieces can be difficult, because of their magnetic fields. EWM’s degauss 600 degaussing power source eliminates the magnetic field from the workpiece, not just before but also during the welding process. This effectively prevents magnetisation of the workpiece, resulting in high quality weld seams on ferromagnetic materials. See www.ewm-group.com

## Forging a path

LIEN CHIEH MACHINERY CO. LTD. was established in 1947. For more than 65 years, it has accumulated practical experience in developing a comprehensive spectrum of presses. Its product portfolio includes die spotting presses, die tryout presses, drawing presses, forging presses, hemming presses, hydraulic presses and others.

Lien Chieh has supplied hydraulic presses to Ford.

The company’s 6,000 – 15,000 ton high capacity, single cylinder hydraulic forging press is designed for the manufacture of wheels for commercial vehicles and automotive industries (press bolster area of 2700 x 2500mm).

The Taiwanese pressmaker has sold hydraulic presses directly to Ford Motor, Toyota Motor and Honda Motor. See www.lienchieh.com
