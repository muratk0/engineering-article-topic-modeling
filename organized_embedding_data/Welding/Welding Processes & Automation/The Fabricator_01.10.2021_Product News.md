Machine saves space in light production fastener welding applications

CenterLine (Windsor) Ltd. has introduced the model N to its CLCS Flex- Fast line of configured stationary welding machines. It features a narrow upper arm and lower knee with a 152- by 300-mm tooling platen. The base model supports one OHMA air-over-oil weld cylinder.

The machine is designed to save space and is suitable for lighter production fastener welding applications. The controller offers control of the machine sequence and up to two feeders. The VeriFast LVDT weld body, together with upper weld cylinder position monitoring, provides reliable fastener detection to monitor the quality of the projection welding process.

Split-frame pipe cutter now features hydraulic motor

Esco Tool now offers its COHOG split-frame pipe cutting machine with a hydraulic motor that permits low-speed operation with high torque for chatter-free cutting of superduplex and other highly alloyed pipe. Suitable for jobs requiring a high volume of end preps, the clamshell machine has a stationary ring machined from 7075 aluminum alloy that is hard-coated and supports a custom-steel-forged, heattreated, nickel-plated rotating ring.

Available in eight sizes for pipe ODs from 2 to 24 in., the machine is available with interchangeable electric, pneumatic, and hydraulic motors. Requiring a 10- to 15-GPM pump at 1,500 PSI, which can be rented or supplied by the user, the hydraulic motor provides variable speeds from 1 to 20 RPM.

Hybrid tube benders designed for high-volume, repetitive work

Unison Ltd. has launched Synergy HBM, a new line of hybrid, multistack, all-electric tube bending machines. Available in versions to handle 50- and 80-mm maximum tube diameters, they feature the company’s latest Unibend Lite control system.

The benders combine electric and hydraulic operation. The bend arm, carriage, plane of bend, and carriage side shift are servo-driven, while the mandrel, pressure die, and clamping system are hydraulically controlled. This makes the machines suitable for high-volume, repetitive tube manipulation operations that don’t require rapid setup times and all-electric control.

Multipurpose band saw blades cut variety of materials, shapes

The L.S. Starrett Co. offers the new Intenss bimetal band saw blades for general-purpose metal sawing of a variety of materials and shapes. Featuring the manufacturer’s patented bimetal unique® technology that provides 170% more weld contact with the teeth, the blades are resistant to tooth strippage, which reduces fracture and breakage. In addition, M-42 teeth with a multiedge tooth design help produce split chips for faster cutting.

The blades are available in 1/2-, 3/4-, and 1-in. widths and 0.025- and 0.035-in. thicknesses. Blades with 1¼- or 1½-in. widths and 0.035- and 0.042-in. thicknesses also are available, but without the bimetal unique feature.

Instructive guide highlights oxyfuel safety

For operators using oxyfuel equipment to cut, weld, braze, or heat metals, Harris Products Group has published a safety manual to help prevent accidents and injuries. This 12-page instructive guide is suitable for use by both new and veteran operators, providing basic information about the elements of different oxyfuel processes and guidelines for safe, proper equipment operation.

Topics include the combustion process, types of gases and cylinders, key hazards, attaching a high-pressure oxygen regulator, safety devices such as check valves and flashback arresters, and PPE. The manual is available in English, French, and Spanish.

Software connects tools to automate planning, production for fabrication

New manufacturing project management software from Hexagon’s Manufacturing Intelligence Division enables sheet metal fabricators to connect data from planning, quoting, and manufacturing systems to help improve decision-making and productivity.
