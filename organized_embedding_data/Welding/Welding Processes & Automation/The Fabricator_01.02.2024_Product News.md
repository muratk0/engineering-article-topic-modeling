Cordless shear modified for better ergonomics

The TruTool S250 LiHD 18V battery-powered shear from TRUMPF, which cuts through 0.10-in.-thick mild steel and 0.06-in.-thick stainless steel, has been made more ergonomic, even in energy-intense applications, reports the company. The geometries of the tool’s cutting table and blade have been reworked. The blade is now a rhombus shape with a pointed wedge angle. This design helps ensure users have to apply very little force and can work for longer periods without getting tired. The tool also has been optimized to cut even 0.79-in. radii easily, the manufacturer states.

Cobot offers 35% more reach and capacity for automated welding

ESAB offers a second cobot option for MIG and pulsed MIG welding applications on steel, stainless steel, and aluminum alloys. The new 5- by 10-ft. table with UR20 robot arm provides 35% more reach and a 67% higher payload to accommodate larger parts, the company reports.

The new system uses the same software app that runs on a standard smart phone or tablet and Smart Puck to hand-guide the torch and record its position with the push of a button.

Modular, automatic bending cell adapts to sheet metal fabricators’ needs

Bystronic has enhanced the Xpert Pro press brake to create a fully automatic bending cell that offers efficiency and flexibility for small and large manufacturing companies. The modular design allows the bending cell to be adapted to the user’s requirements, and it is suitable both for processing long job lists and for changing orders—from small batch sizes to large series.

While the 7-axis robot autonomously changes the grippers and bending tools, the intelligent software autonomously determines the optimal sequence of the bending tools for every job list, saving up to 30% of the processing time, according to the manufacturer.

AI-based simulation software generates robotic welding paths automatically

Cenit’s FastSuite E2 release 2023.2 3D simulation software for robot welding applications features numerous new functions and enhancements.

AI-based offline programming with automated path optimization generates robot paths automatically and analyzes them to identify collisions, axis limits, and singularities. The algorithm then automatically generates error-free programs.

The automatic seam identification and tracing function enables automated generation and optimization of robot welding paths.

The robot programs can self-adjust to account for geometry deviations within the components and tacking arrangements.

System stores up to 75 coils for easy access

Cidan Machinery Group has launched Forstner Coilfarm, an adaptable system designed for sheet metal fabricators serving the architectural and industrial sectors with a variety of material thicknesses and color variants.

With up to 75 coils, the machine allows immediate access to a range of material types, thicknesses, and colors. This allows even large projects to be run at the same time. Coil loading and change are quick and safe, the company reports.

Integrated zero-line alignment (fixed edge) helps ensure that the materials are aligned every time to reduce errors and waste.

Flux-cored wire designed for variety of structural applications

Weldcote, a division of Zika Group, offers Mighty Arc flux gas-shielded and mild steel flux-cored wire. Designed for single, multiple-pass, and all-position welding, the line includes carbon baked steel; carbon steel; and low-alloy, high-strength wires.

Offering low spatter, low fume emissions, and a smooth arc, the flux-cored wires can be used for a variety of applications, including structures like buildings, ships, bridges, machinery, and vehicles, the company reports.
