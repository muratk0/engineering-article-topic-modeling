# Multi-process TIG welding

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

Fronius is launching the iWave, an intelligent, high-end TIG welding series for different metals that is available in power categories from 190A to 500A.

"The innovative Option CycleTIG provides for maximum control over the arc and targeted heat input. Further highlights include improved ignition control and greater ease of use with intuitive operation. It features sophisticated TIG technology and is ideally suited to manual metal arc welding, even with cellulose electrodes. The Multiprocess PRO option gives the user unlimited access to all MIG/MAG processes in power categories from 300A," explained Fronius.

"Customers can individually choose the specific features that they need and add to the range of functions (as and when needed). Starting with standard TIG or standard MIG/MAG applications, the iWave enables upgrades to the full Fronius range, including the Cold Metal Transfer (CMT), Pulse Multi Control (PMC) and Low Spatter Control (LSC) welding packages," it added.

According to the welding specialist, the iWave supports the main communication standards and is ready for Industry 4.0. Modern wireless connections give welders greater freedom and security. Peripheral devices, such as a remote control or the high-tech Vizor Connect welding helmet, can be wirelessly connected via Bluetooth. WLAN enables communication with other devices in the same network so that the user can enjoy a host of digital benefits, such as central user management, real-time data transfer and rapid updates.

"It is the first TIG power source on the market with a Real Energy (kJ) display and analysis feature. In addition, Power Factor Correction (PFC) provides for efficient energy consumption and no-load power consumption of the device is always below 50W," concluded Fronius.
