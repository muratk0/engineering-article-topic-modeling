## Gain even more benefits from process simulation in sheet metal working with Simufact solutions:

Simufact.forming works with a volume hexahedron mesher providing the highest accuracy for simulation results, especially related to the geometry after the forming process: sheet thickness changes, inner and outer radii, and spring-back. Our sheet mesh algorithm increases calculation speed and reduces hardware requirements. In combination with Simufact.welding you are able to connect the simulation of forming and welding processes and transfer results from step to step (process chain).
