Yaskawa is a global technology supplier of robotics and drive/control technology. For 40 years, it has been supplying robot-based turnkey welding systems from its own European system construction. It is drawing attention to this anniversary at EuroBLECH with a complex arc welding cell. The latest Motoman robot models for spot, laser hybrid, arc and cobot welding, as well as for handling tasks and control technology, will also be on stand D56.

"We offer a technical solution for feeding robot and track/ gantry/positioner braking energy back into the power supply system, as standard and without additional hardware. All larger Motoman robots, with payloads of 35kg or more, and the current YRC1000 robot controller can convert kinetic energy from downward and sideways motions directly into the power supply system. Depending upon the motion profile, this considerably reduces the energy requirements of the robot," outlined Yaskawa, who will also be showcasing the Weld4Me CE cell, Motoman robot servo drives and the iCube Control automation platform at EuroBLECH 2024.
