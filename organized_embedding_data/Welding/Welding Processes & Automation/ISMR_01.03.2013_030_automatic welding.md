# AUTOMATIC WELDING

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

CEMSA SPA, a specialist in resistance welding technology, has launched a new model in its CNC machine series, Roboseam, for the automatic welding of sinks and similar products. Compared with previous versions, this new model has been designed for a wider field of applications.

“One of the most innovative features of this new Roboseam is its compactness enabling, as an important advantage for customers, lower transport costs all over the world,” said the manufacturer.

“This new machine has a working speed that is considerably higher than the one reached by previous models; it is characterised by simplified programming in a Windows environment that enables, as well as standard welding operations on stainless steel sinks, different applications for products requiring two-dimensional welding or welding on parallel planes.” E-mail: info@cemsa.it
