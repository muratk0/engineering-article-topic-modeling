# Resistance welding

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

MIYACHI Europe, a leading supplier of laser and resistance welding systems and laser marking products, showcased its solutions at Salon Industrie 2013 in Lyon this April. On display was its “newhorizon” resistance welding equipment and ML–7350C high speed laser marker as well as the new MG3 hot bar monitoring system.

“Miyachi Pico’s newhorizon line of modular resistance welders allows users to choose from among 24 modular components of weld heads and weld pincers to configure the right solution for their application. With hundreds of possible configurations, users can run their applications whilst reducing maintenance and doubling process flexibility when ordering and using the products,” the company told ISMR.

Also on show was Miyachi Unitek’s new 50 Watt ML–7350C Yb: fiber laser marker designed for high speed laser marking, laser engraving, laser cutting and laser ablation. The ML–7350C makes high contrast marks on both plastics and metals. 50W of power means the marker can provide distinct, rapid and versatile markings of letters, numbers and graphics.
