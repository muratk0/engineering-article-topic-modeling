Refinery expansion fuels need for fume collector

Situation

Wyoming Refining Co. is a 15,000-barrel-per-day oil refinery in Newcastle, Wyo., that has been in operation since the 1940s. The firm uses many types of equipment, including reactors, exchangers, pumps, and towers, each of which requires piping to deliver materials to the next stage of production.

“There’s a lot of welding for piping and supports,” said Maintenance Supervisor Clay Iacovetto. “We wanted to give our welders a better work environment.”

Concern for the well-being of employees prompted the company to look for a ventilation system as it expanded operations with a new 40- by 60-ft. welding shop.

Resolution

When planning the ventilation system, Iacovetto worked with Wyoming Mechanical Supply (WMS), Gillette, Wyo., to install a Farr Gold Series® fume collector with HEPA filtration, manufactured by Camfil APC.

“We took a look at what kind of products they were working with,” said Kyle Norris, president of WMS. “It was primarily carbon steel and some stainless steel. We needed to take into account the dangerous dust output from that operation. We did that with the integrated HEPA filter.”

Source-capture points were placed around the new shop. “After we reviewed their processes, we decided to go with a 6-in. arm. On a few of the capture points, we added a 10-ft. boom extension for more flexibility to reach through the shop,” Norris continued.

The build also included dampers and intake louvers that direct filtered air back into the space during the winter and exhaust it in the summer, resulting in energy savings and cleaner outside air.

Since its installation in 2012, the fume collector has lived up to expectations. “The welders are happier,” said Iacovetto. “The unit has been reliable. We haven’t done anything to it since we put it in.”

Orbital stretch wrapper reduces load-wrapping labor requirements for tank manufacturer

Situation

Baker Tankhead is a manufacturer of ASME and standard tank heads, hot-formed tank heads, cones, manways, and rolled shells, located in Fort Worth, Texas. Varying in size and material, the heavy tank heads measure 8.625 in. to 16 ft. dia. The company ships about 27,000 lbs. of product in about 40 pallets daily throughout the U.S. and Mexico.

The company was hand-wrapping its pallets. Each wrapping job required three employees, who were at risk of injury when they passed under the heavy load to pass the film. In addition, the film rolls were heavy and could cause back injuries.

Resolution

Baker purchased the Yellow Jacket 87M orbital stretch wrapper from Muller LCS. With the machine, just one employee can wrap the same loads that previously required three employees, in a fraction of the time. The unit allows for uniform tension and wrap to help ensure a consistently secured load.

To meet high wrapping demands, the firm runs the wrapper for six hours out of every 24 without much delay. The 110-VAC unit comes in manual and semiautomatic versions, requiring a footprint of about 8 by 9 ft.

Truck cab manufacturer uses carts to move aluminum stringers without damage

Situation

A manufacturer of commercial truck cabs recently had concerns about potential damage to aluminum stringers it needed to transport from its warehouse kitting operations to production lines. The aluminum stringers are 2- and 4-ft. slender products and susceptible to damage if not handled properly.

The company decided to look for carts that would allow simple loading and unloading of the stringers, as well as safe transport through the plant.

Resolution

Creform Corp. custom-designed an A-frame cart that met the company’s requirements. The cart is stable for transporting the stringers, while its tilted shelves provide good visual inventory management and ease of parts loading and unloading. After the cart is filled with product in the warehouse, it is moved directly to the production floor, holding one shift’s worth of inventory.

The cart’s main structure is built from 42-mm plastic-coated steel pipe; 28-mm pipe holds the shelves. Its A-frame design adds stability to the cart and tilts the 0.5-in. HDPE shelves to help protect the inventory while making sure it stays securely in the storage bay. Three wide shelves hold the 4-ft. stringers, while five narrow shelves hold the 2-ft. stringers.

The cart, which has a 500-lb. capacity, is 57 by 63 by 59 in. Assembled with metal joints, it can be modified to handle other sizes of products as the company’s needs change. The modular design allows for the addition of accessories such as hitches for towing with either manned vehicles or Creform AGVs.
