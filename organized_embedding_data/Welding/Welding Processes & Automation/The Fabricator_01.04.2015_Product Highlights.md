Push/pull welding torch offers improved thermal dissipation

The PullMig welding torch from Fronius is a lightweight push/pull unit that can survive a fall from almost 10 ft. The stainless steel outer tube and antitwist device in the zero-degree position help ensure that the torch neck, which rotates 360 degrees, remains extremely stable, even in the event of harsh impacts with the workpiece.

Several structural changes have improved the thermal dissipation while also reducing the absorption of arc radiant heat. As a result, overall the welding torch remains cooler than its predecessor, and the gas nozzle can be handled safely without gloves about 1.5 min. after welding, the company states.

The wire feeding of the new torch has been made more precise, and the user no longer needs to open the handle to thread a new wire. Synchronization of the feeder motors is handled automatically by the power source.

CNC plasma tube cutter adjusts quickly to various material shapes

Electro-Mechanical Integrators (EMI) offers the Tube Processing Center (TPC), a CNC machine for cutting any combination of intersecting angles, copes, slots, and holes with no tooling changes. Changes to material sizes and shapes are accomplished in a matter of minutes with only an Allen wrench.

Standard machines cut lengths up to 24 ft. They cut weld-ready components from rounds up to 5.5 in. dia., squares to 4 by 4 by 0.25 in., angles to 3.5-in. legs, flats to 6 in., and channels to 4 in. wide.

The machine is designed with optional modules to accommodate either high-volume/auto-load or small-volume/manual-load operations. Custom turnkey machines are built to handle other material sizes and special applications.

Deburring machine addresses finishing challenges

The new Loewer DiscMaster SF series top and bottom deburring machine from AM Machinery Sales Inc. has been designed to address the problem areas most precision sheet metal and stamping shops have such as deburring up-formed parts and removing oxidation.

The machine is equipped with oscillating disc stations on top and bottom. The discs have many different abrasive types available, including wire brushes for oxidation removal. This new design eliminates the use of conveyor belts; it uses feed rollers instead to allow the deburring brush to penetrate beyond the material thickness, which allows the abrasive to sand the edges to meet edge-rounding requirements. The ability to control all aspects of the machine allows for deburring of galvanized coated or laminate-coated material without harming the protective coating.

Extra features added to horizontal band saw

Behringer has added more standard features to its HBE Dynamic series horizontal band saws. The saws now include automatic precision servo downfeed control and blade protection sensor technology.

A sensor-driven cutting pressure control is located at the back of the saw blade. Positioning the sensor directly at the saw blade helps ensure that pressure adjustments are quick and accurate, the company reports. The control automatically adjusts blade pressure to create the optimum balance between feed rate and blade pressure.

Automatic feed control automatically adjusts cutting parameters such as blade speed and downfeed rate to the material grade and shape. This permits operators to create flexible material feed programs, optimizing cutting data based on specific material grade. Additionally, a fault/alert history may be viewed at any time by the operator. The detailed information displayed in plain text is useful for both planning and analysis of past projects.
