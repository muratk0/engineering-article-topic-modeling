# Laser and precision joining

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

MIYACHI EUROPE, which develops, builds and services machines and components for laser and resistance welding, marking and hot bar systems, has announced a few new additions to its precision joining solutions’ range.

The Miyachi EAPRO is a compact hot bar new horizon ACF laminating system that features pulsed heat and constant heat temperature controllers, as well as low and high-force bonding heads. The Miyachi UNITEK is a 500W fiber laser welder which, together with the MIYACHI EAPRO Laserline filter unit, offers the precise control needed for various small metallic component and penetration welding.

Miyachi Europe’s Remote Services adapt to high-end resistance welding modules, such as AWS3 and MG3, and are suitable for customers with multiple locations and high uptime system demands as they allow for time and travel cost savings. See www.miyachieurope.com ■

## PRODUCT REVIEWS

ESAB Welding and Cutting Products specialises in offering turnkey solutions and fully integrated, optimised cutting systems. It also plays an active role in the implementation and consultation for safety-related cutting standards.

ISO (The International Organisation for Standardisation) is the world’s largest developer of voluntary International Standards and ESAB has helped to spearhead a new European EN ISO standard in worker safety and has effectively implemented the new standard 17916, ahead of industry-wide implementation later this year. This new EN ISO standard requires all OEMs to conduct their own evaluations and tests around the safety of their thermal cutting machines and defines potential risks and prevention measures for cutting machine operators.

“We are currently the only cutting systems manufacturer to be fully compliant with the new standard. All the cutting systems we manufacture at our German facility will now meet the new safety requirements,” it explained.

For further details see www.esab.co.uk ■
