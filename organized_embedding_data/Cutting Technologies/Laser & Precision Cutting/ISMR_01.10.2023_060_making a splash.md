# Making a splash

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

WARDJET waterjet cutting systems are a range of tailored solutions designed and manufactured by WARDJET, an Ohio-based company (USA) with over 30 years of experience in the industry. Its precision waterjet systems cut a range of materials such as metals, composites, stone, glass and more.

"With a comprehensive materials database, users can program parts allowing for precise cuts for intricate designs and complex shapes. One of the key features that set us apart is the robust construction and thoughtful design of our waterjet systems. Each component is carefully crafted to withstand the rigours of industrial applications, ensuring maximum durability and longevity. The gantry system provides stability and rigidity during cutting operations, contributing to the system's precision," explained the cutting specialist.

A wide range of customisation options for waterjet systems are offered including various cutting heads, abrasive delivery systems and control software.

"WARDJET waterjet systems are userfriendly and efficient. The intuitive interface allows operators to create and modify cutting patterns easily. The advanced software supports various file formats, enabling seamless integration with design software such as CAD/CAM applications. The software optimises cutting paths, minimising material waste and maximising productivity," it explained.

"With cutting speeds of up to 900 inches per minute, these machines can significantly reduce production time, making them valuable assets for high-volume manufacturing. The ability to perform rapid and accurate cuts allows businesses to meet tight deadlines and enhance their overall productivity," it added.

WARDJET waterjet systems are also designed to minimise material waste by utilising an optional advanced recirculation system. The system filters and recirculates water and abrasive materials, significantly reducing water consumption and abrasive usage. This eco-friendly approach not only reduces operating costs but also contributes to a more sustainable manufacturing process.

The systems are also equipped with multiple safety features such as safety interlocks, emergency stop buttons and comprehensive guarding to prevent accidents and injuries during operation. Remote monitoring capabilities allow operators to keep track of machine performance and status in real time. Users can access WARDJET's dedicated support team for assistance.

"The WARDJET A-Series is a compact and affordable waterjet system that can fit on any shop floor. It is ideal for small to medium-sized projects that require high-quality cutting. The A-Series has a built-in, water-level control system that allows for super-quiet underwater cutting. The M-Series is a modular waterjet system that can handle any size or shape of material. It is suitable for heavy-duty projects that require high productivity and efficiency. It also has a robust gantry design that can support multiple cutting heads and accessories and a modular design that can be easily expanded or upgraded," explained the U.S. manufacturer.

Its X-Series reaches traversal speeds of 900 inches per minute, with its advanced drive system and structural aluminium crossbeam. A five-axis waterjet cutting head can also be integrated. This empowers users to cut intricate 3D shapes and includes taper angle control.

The Water-Only Series is a specialised waterjet system that can cut soft or delicate materials without abrasive. It is suitable for applications that require pure water cutting, such as rubber or foam. The water-only series machines are designed with purpose-built tanks featuring a stainless-steel floor and optional washdown system to remove kerf material. It also has a variety of nozzles and orifices designed to optimise cutting performance.
