Punch/fiber laser combination machine achieves high-speed, uninterrupted operation

AMADA AMERICA offers the EML-AJ series punch/fiber laser combination machine. It comes with all the punching functions inherent in single-function punching machines. Combined punches and dies are supplied with a Z turret with different diameters (1,010 mm upper and 1,200 mm lower) or a high-capacity turret of 1,200-mm-dia. upper and lower. Punching force is 300 kN.

The 3-kW fiber laser oscillator combines high oscillation efficiency with new optics. It employs assist gas rectifying technology and increases cutting speed by a factor of three compared to conventional machines, the manufacturer says.

A variety of automation options—automated punch and die changer, nozzle changer, laser scrap unloader, and cleaning function for cutting plates—allow for uninterrupted operation.

Beveler for sanitary tubing performs end preps for orbital welding

Esco Tool offers the C-HOG MILLHOG OD-clamping end prep tool. It mounts to the outside of sanitary tubing to maintain the integrity of the tube interior and operates without cutting fluids to prevent contamination.

Designed for use on thin-wall tubing, the tool evenly distributes holding power along the tube exterior. It fits tube and pipe from 0.5-in. ID to 3-in. OD, and the consistency of the land makes it suitable for orbital welding end preps.

Available with cutter blades for performing any angle of prep, including J-preps, the tool can bevel, face, and bore simultaneously. Featuring a 1.75-in.-wide body and a 120- or 220- to 240-V electric motor, it has an attached feed wrench.

Software helps optimize BiW assembly process

AutoForm Engineering GmbH has unveiled new software version AutoForm Assembly R10. With its new features, the software helps users optimize the BiW assembly process and ensure the desired level of quality early in the process chain.

The software allows for the evaluation of the assembly process from early feasibility through to production support, including process engineering and initial tryout support. Users can import nominal CAD geometry in a variety of formats, assign all single parts a suitable material selected from an extensive material database, and define a range of alternative assembly processes by varying critical aspects.

Process adjustments can be made virtually, allowing users to define which clamps and joins should be considered for shimming and teaching. By running multiple simulations, the software helps users determine the optimal parameters to be applied to resolve accuracy issues in the assembly.

Profile benders capable of vertical, horizontal operation

Kaast Machine Tools Inc. offers PBM and PBH series angle rollers for circular bending of metal profiles, including angles and pipes. Featuring vertical and horizontal operation capabilities, the machines include a standard set of modular dies suitable for bending square, flat, and angle iron. Custom dies for rounded or other profiles also are available.

The PBM series features a dualspeed motor for the two nonmodular bottom rolls and a manually adjustable top roll with a millimeter scale. The optional digital readout assists with repeatability. The 30 model handles 1.5- in square tubing, while the 50 model handles 2 in.

The PBH series differs in that its top roll is moved hydraulically, not manually. The 50, 65, and 80 models feature three driven rolls and two hydraulically adjustable bottom rollers.

Also included with these models is a digital display of two axes and mechanical guide rollers adjustable in three axes.

Portable sump cleaner generates up to 110 gal./min. suction

Eriez has increased the vacuum flow rate on its portable sump cleaner. The unit now generates up to 110 gal./min. of suction to eliminate sludge and chips. According to the company, the unit cleans a typical CNC machine tool sump of sludge in 10 to 20 min.

The cleaner removes the cutting fluid, sludge, and chips from the sump; filters the sludge and chips from the fluid; and returns the filtered fluid to the sump or transports it to a coolant recycling system for processing.

The machine is available in capacities from 50 to 1,000 gal. It features a discharge nozzle that can be used like a pressure washer to rinse out sludge and break it apart for removal.
