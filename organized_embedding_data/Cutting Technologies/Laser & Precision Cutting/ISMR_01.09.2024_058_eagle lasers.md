# Eagle Lasers

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand F242

https://eagle-group.eu/en/

Eagle Lasers is a technology and R&D company specialising in fibre laser systems and automation. It has branches in Poland, Italy, Germany, Scandinavia and France, with a strong global presence through sales and service distributors.

At EuroBLECH 2024, Eagle Lasers will showcase a new continuous operational concept. The FlowIN system ensures continuous and simultaneous material flow, cutting and sorting, addressing various challenges in the sheet metal cutting market.

"This system streamlines production, resolving many sheet metal cutting market challenges," said the manufacturer. "We will also display the latest iNspire 2.0 fibre laser cutter and its advanced automation solutions. The CraneMaster system efficiently loads sheets onto the cutting table and simultaneously unloads cut parts to the unloading station or storage. The eTower smart modular storage system features shelves that serve as both loading and unloading points, further optimising workflow and storage capabilities."
