# PRIMA SETS STRATEGY

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

PRIMA North America, Inc., the North American subsidiary of PRIMA Industrie S.p.A, a leading manufacturer of industrial lasers and laser systems, has announced the promotion of Carl Bryant to the position of sales and marketing manager for PRIMA 3D Laser Systems and to the newly created position of automotive market manager for North America. Mr Bryant was previously North American sales manager for LASERDYNE SYSTEMS.

In announcing the promotion, Terry L. VanderWert (president of PRIMA North America) said that Mr. Bryant's position is part of the company's realignment of laser system sales to focus on specific industries. “Mr. Bryant's new responsibilities are part of our strategic goal for the continued growth of LASERDYNE and PRIMA sales in North America," Mr. VanderWert stated. "The RAPIDO is the standard for cutting and trimming of hot formed steel in automotive applications across the world. The OPTIMO, which is used for cutting and welding applications primarily in car body and aerospace component manufacturing, is the largest work envelope, standard 3D laser system available on the market."
