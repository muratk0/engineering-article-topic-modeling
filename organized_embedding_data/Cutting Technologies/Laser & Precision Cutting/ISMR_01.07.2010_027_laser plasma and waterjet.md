# LASER, PLASMA AND WATERJET

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

## The versatility appeals to companies looking to cut thick and thinner metal profiles as well as organisations working with a range of hard and soft materials

Jet Edge, Inc. is a leading manufacturer of ultra-high pressure waterjet and abrasive jet systems for precision cutting, coating removal and surface preparation. Its Mid Rail Gantry waterjet cutting machine is designed to ‘cut virtually any material’ and was showcased and demonstrated at the 2010 WJTA-IMCA Expo on 17-19 May in Houston, USA.

Powered by a 60,000lb/in2 or 90,000lb/in2 Jet Edge waterjet intensifier pump (available in 30-200hp models), the Mid Rail Gantry machine features an exposed tank that can accommodate overhead loading. It uses an industrial PC controller and can be configured so that all three axes are fully programmable (Z optional). It also features direct-couple AV brushless digital servo motors and single or double carriages.

KMT Waterjet Systems is a leading global manufacturer of waterjet cutting systems, Ultra High Pressure pumps and components for waterjet systems. KMT Robotic Solutions recently launched its JetTool 60,000psi robotic waterjet cutting system.

KMT Waterjet Systems’ Pro series of highpressure waterjet cutting products can be used with 4100 and 6200 bar working pressures. This allows users to select the optimum working pressure for specific applications and the versatility appeals to companies looking to cut thick and thinner metal profiles as well as organisations working with a range of hard and soft materials.

“The high pressure operation of the 6,200 bar Pro series gives users improved energy density for cutting with 50 per cent more pressure through the cutting head orifice and focusing tube. This provides higher cutting speeds, improved cutting quality and less abrasive wear. With the 125HP version of the pump, multi-head cutting can also be undertaken,” the company told ISMR.

The Pro series pumps can be connected via an interface to the internet for remote diagnostics and monitoring of pump performance. The life of key components, such as valves, can also be monitored. Because waterjet abrasive is the most expensive part of using waterjet systems, the KMT Waterjet Abrasive Management System is designed to reduce costs through precision control and consistent flow of the abrasive.

Water Jet Sweden has entered the microprecision machining centre market with Finecut AB, which has developed a range of ‘fine abrasive waterjet’ (FAW) models. WJS and Finecut aim to capture small, fine abrasive waterjet applications, including production of miniature components that require minute cutting techniques.

Water Jet Sweden has also introduced the Panelone human-machine interface (HMI) for waterjet cutting machine customers. According to Tony Ryd, WJS director, customers will no longer need to rely on operators having specific numerical control knowledge as Panelone presents a simple interface from which all aspects of the profiler can be intuitively controlled.

He added that the same basic platform will now be used as the standard for all waterjet profilers made by WJS across its entire range, right up to the full five-axis models. This will not only enable the company to hold down manufacturing costs but will also benefit customers requiring operator training at the factory. The same factory training course will apply to all; multiple customers can attend together without the need to arrange special one-of sessions for individual customer machines.

Above: WJS systems have an anti-collision feature

Customers requiring physical parts without drawings or electronic files can use the digitizing capability of a Wardjet waterjet to reverse engineer the parts. If the parts are large, they can be placed on the waterjet table and the waterjet can memorise the part profile rather than having to use a tape measure. With the X-Series controller, the waterjet cutting head, or a laser alignment guide mounted near the cutting head, can record the X, Y and the Z co-ordinates of a part.
