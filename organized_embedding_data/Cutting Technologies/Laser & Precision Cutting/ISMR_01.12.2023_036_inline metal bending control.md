# Inline metal bending control

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

At the EuroBLECH trade show in Hanover Vision Components showcased its laser profiler for angle measurement and inline control of sheet-metal bending processes. The VC nano3D-Z laser triangulation sensor operates autonomously, without an external computing unit, and is fully calibrated for angle measurement.

Also new are a mounting kit and other accessories for easy commissioning. The rugged IP67-grade laser profiler is suitable for die widths from V6 to V200 and heights from 55 to 150mm. Versions for larger dies are also available on request.

"The sensor delivers results even with reflective or brushed surfaces as well as perforated sheet metal. We have developed it specifically as an OEM product and, in addition to the turnkey complete solution, also offer a modular kit for individual further development. The integrated embedded vision system and high-power blue laser (class 2) are perfectly matched. Press-brake manufacturers will receive industrial-grade components with assured long-term availability," it added.

"It enables therefore an increase of throughput and efficiency. The measurement resolution is better than +/- 0.05°. The easy-to-mount sensor connects to any controller/PLC and directly outputs the bending angle in degrees. The sensor is already integrated in DELEM and – new – CYBELEC controllers," said the manufacturer.

Vision Components is a leading international manufacturer of embedded vision systems. It also offers software libraries and develops customised solutions on request. The company, based in Ettlingen in southwestern Germany, was founded in 1996 by Michael Engel, inventor of the industrial-grade intelligent camera.

More world premieres followed, including "the world's first" intelligent vision sensors and the “first-ever" embedded 3D laser profiler. Today, Vision Components has sales offices in the United States and Japan and works with local partners in over 25 countries.■
