Metal cutting band saw offers variable blade speeds from 55 to 255 FPM

Grizzly Industrial Inc. has introduced the South Bend SB1128 7- by 93/8-in. 1-HP metal cutting band saw. The heavy-duty band saw, designed to handle tough cutting jobs, has a single-phase motor and variable blade speeds from 55 to 255 FPM.

The quick-release/-lock swivel mast, which allows the blade to adjust from 45 degrees left to 60 degrees right, allows for angled cuts without workpiece or vise repositioning. Other features include a quick-lock/-release vise, cutting fluid system, sliding-position front vise, automatic blade shutoff, integrated splash pan, and centralized controls.

Nitrogen generator helps shops meet fiber laser cutting requirements

NitroCube, offered by Bystronic Inc. and MSS Nitrogen Inc., is a compact nitrogen generator designed to meet fiber laser cutting requirements. According to the companies, the system typically generates nitrogen that is 99.999% pure, and it is sized based on user consumption figures to help ensure correct flow and storage capacity.

Delivering pressures at 4,350 PSI, all models are modular and expandable retroactively. Standard models provide volumes from 11,000 to 42,000 cu. ft./day. Highflow models provide volumes from 59,000 to 169,500 cu. ft./day (and up to 378,000 cu. ft./day with combined units) while maintaining purity levels.

Compact automated bending system handles midsize sheet metal range

SafanDarley North America LLC has introduced the Multi Cell, an automated bending system for products up to 500 by 2,000 mm and 40 to 125 tons of pressing force. This compact system is suitable for bending a variety of products and provides flexible, robotized automation for the midsize sheet metal range.

The system is retrofit-capable, allowing the integration of new technology and features to help ensure long-term investment value. Key features include compatibility with servo-electronic or hydraulic press brakes, a 70-kg FANUC robot, customizable software and electronics, and options for feedin/retake stations, angle measurement, and sheet thickness.

The modular design includes safety fencing, automatic doors, and AGV support for automated feed-in/-out. The centrally positioned robot helps enhance flexibility by maximizing reach and adaptability to any production setup.

Eyewear options protect welders

Bollé Safety offers protective eye-wear for welder safety. Its Fusion+ welding helmet features a variable welding shade (4/5-8/9-13), 100- by 53-mm field of vision, and spare lenses. Its Universal welding goggles are vented and feature a shade 5 antifog, an-tiscratch coated lens.

Inverter-based MIG welding machine offers energy-saving features

ESAB has unveiled the Rustler EM 280 Pro, an inverter-based MIG welding machine for industrial fabrication applications. It features a maximum welding output of 280 amps and rated output of 200 amps at 60% duty cycle.

The unit connects to 208-V/230-V single-phase primary power ± 15% and features Power Factor Correction to lower the primary power draw. It can provide its full output range on a 30-amp breaker and offers an energy efficiency of 82%. After two minutes of idle time, the machine enters an energy saver mode to further reduce power consumption.

The machine comes with factory-optimized arc programs for carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminum, silicon bronze, and fluxcored wires from 0.023 to 0.045 in. It can perform spray transfer welding with 0.045-in. solid and flux-cored wires and 0.047-in. aluminum wires. It can weld up to ½-in. steel and 3/8-in. aluminum in a single pass.

Fully automatic panel bender produces numerous bend types with one tooling set

LVD’s Multifold 2111 panel bender offers fully automatic loading, folding, and stacking of workpieces up to a maximum bend length of 2,100 mm in material thicknesses up to 2-mm cold-rolled steel with bend heights up to 110 mm.

A flexible alternative to die bending for many applications, the panel bender requires only one set of tooling—a universal set of servo-operated up-and-down folding blades—to produce numerous profiles and various types of bends.
