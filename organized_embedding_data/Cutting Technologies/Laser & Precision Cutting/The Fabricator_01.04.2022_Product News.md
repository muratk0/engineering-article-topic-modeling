Compact, 3-kW fiber laser cutting system has 3D cutting capabilities

Amada offers the Brevis 1212 AJ compact, high-precision, 3-kW fiber laser. Engineered for manufacturing versatility, it features high-speed laser cutting, easy material loading, and a flexible Z axis to help ensure efficient laser cutting of assembled or preformed parts, according to the company. The cutting system handles everything from prototyping to full-scale production.

With its 4- by 4-ft. footprint, the machine saves shop floor space. Doors on both sides of the machine slide open, maximizing accessibility for material handling. Cutting pallets are easily pulled out for efficient material loading. In addition, 4- by 8-ft. sheets can be loaded to process two 4- by 4-ft. workpieces sequentially. The flexible Z axis allows precise laser cutting on 3D materials, such as boxes or covers with a height up to 7.8 in. Accuracy is maintained because there is no deformation when cutting close to bend lines.

Other features include the company’s AMNC 3i control with an easy-to-navigate graphical interface and a high-speed processor, the i-Camera assist system, and an automatic nozzle cleaner.

Technology helps users maximize parts per plate for plasma cutting

Hypertherm has introduced PlateSaver, a new SureCut technology that automatically maximizes the number of parts on a plate. It is available through the company’s ProNest and other authorized software for fabricators cutting mild steel on a CNC table using X-Definition plasma.

The technology combines the arc stability of X-Definition plasma with specialized software parameters to help increase material utilization. Those parameters cause the technology to use shorter leads when starting and finishing a cut for less wasted space between parts. In addition to shorter lead-ins and lead-outs, it takes molten metal into account by using moving pierces that cause molten splatter to fall within a predictable "splash zone."

Programmable limit switch offers five additional cam timing options

Wintriss Controls Group has introduced a new ProCamPAC Pro programmable limit switch (PLS) for its SmartPAC Pro press automation controller. Building on the functionality of the previous PLS, the Pro model features five additional cam timing options for flexibility and functionality.

A new toggle cam output performs an asymmetrical toggle function, turning an output off or on for different stroke amounts. The new on/off counter cam output controls functions that need to operate at a preset interval instead of on every machine cycle. Operating similarly, a new pulsed counter cam output is programmed with an "on angle" and a dwell time instead of an on/off output.

Useful when running configurable tooling, a new channel-always-on cam output will turn on and stay on until a new tool is loaded. And the triggered cam output actuates a precision, onetime cam timing signal based on a preset trigger such as an external input from ancillary equipment.
