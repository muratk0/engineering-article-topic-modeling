# 30 PER CENT FASTER ON THIN SHEET

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

A KEY BENEFIT of the TRUMPF L20 flat bed laser is its universal cutting head. This feature can eliminate downtime associated with changing the laser head to correspond with a different thickness of material, a characteristic of previous generations of TRUMPF flat bed lasers.

Eminox, a UK manufacturer of high performance stainless steel exhausts and emission control systems for commercial vehicles, invested in TRUMPF’s new flatbed laser. To ensure it could optimise production of its thin gauge components, Eminox specified its new TRUMPF L20 with two cutting heads, one universal and the other, high speed. The company machines mild steel up to 15mm thick but also stainless steel in 16 or 18 gauge. For applications where high productivity for thin sheet cutting is a pre-requisite, TRUMPF also offers an optional high speed cutting head.

“Whereas the head change on the TruLaser 2530 took several minutes, on the TRUMPF L20 it takes seconds,” John Rogers of Eminox explained.

Eminox estimates that it will achieve a 30 per cent increase in productivity on its TRUMPF L20. It has also been able to include the production of flanges on this machine, a process that previously required subcontracted secondary drilling. This is made possible by the TRUMPF feature ContourLine, a technique for achieving the best quality holes. Its pulsed cutting allows holes to be created that are much smaller in diameter than the material thickness.

To further boost productivity, Eminox also plans to exploit the larger bed size of the TRUMPF L20. John Rogers concludes, “This increased bed allows us to contemplate the use of larger stock sizes and experiment with improved sheet utilisation. This will help us boost cost-efficiency even more.” FOR MORE INFORMATION, SEE www.trumpf.com
