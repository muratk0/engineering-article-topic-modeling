# CYLASER Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.cy-laser.com

CYLASER is a global specialist and early pioneer in the manufacture and supply of fibre optic laser cutting systems. It produced its first fibre laser cutting machine in 2005 and regularly introduces new accessories/cutting processes for continuous productivity improvements. Based in Schio, Italy, it has a branch facility/Technology Centre at Sterling Heights, MI (USA).

“At EuroBLECH, we plan to exhibit our CY2D EL3015, a gantry-style robust machine with 12kW power generator with high-precision rack and pinion. The system is easy to transport, quick to install and ready for its use in a few days. It can be equipped with options such as the EVO V HP cutting head; CY FAST-MARK (marking head); VORTEX (cutting process to improve stainless steel quality) and VEGA (cutting process to improve quality cut on thick material)," it continued.

Also on display will be its LM model (with linear motors), the HL model (aeroplane structure) and automation options.
