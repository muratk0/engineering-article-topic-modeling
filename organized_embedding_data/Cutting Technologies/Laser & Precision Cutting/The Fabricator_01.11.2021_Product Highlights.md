Waterjet design allows for multiple head configurations on single gantry

WARDJet Tailored Waterjet Solutions has introduced the Mseries CNC waterjet. Its design allows for multiple head configurations on a single gantry, with the ability to park heads outside of the cutting zone. The high-speed, high-acceleration, large-format machine handles large- and multiple-sheet production. Designed for industry-standard sheet sizes, it maximizes access to all sides of the cutting area. The machine features the company’s Move touch control software, as well as a hardened helical rack-and-pinion drive that allows up to 1,400-IPM accelerations while maintaining accuracy.

The system can accept several types of cutting heads for water-only or abrasive cutting, 5-axis cutting, and 2D capabilities. Multiple heads or spreader bars can be added for increased throughput.

Fiber laser designed for high-volume shops

Cincinnati Inc. has introduced the CLX fiber laser machine, designed to help improve functionality in a lights-out environment. The automation-ready, 3- by 1.5-m and 4- by 2-m dual-pallet powerhouse has half the total frame weight of its previous iterations while maintaining robustness. According to the company, the machine features improved intelligence that allows it to take care of itself and keep cutting material.

Features include dual 22-in. touchscreen monitors, four interior cameras with different aspect ratios, an nLight 15-kW Corona laser, predictive technology, Beckhoff IoT integration, a Laser Mechanisms software package, and remote communication.

Waterjet deburring system provides deburring, cleaning in one process

The FlexJet HD high-pressure waterjet deburring system from Karl Roll performs deburring and cleaning in one work step. The individually adapted nozzle systems help ensure a high degree of workpiece flexibility and high precision for the removal or burrs, chips, welding beads, and layers of paint and scale.

The system delivers water pressure of about 500 bar. The rotary table integrated in the treatment chamber with pneumatic connections for automatic workpiece clamping devices is designed for components with a diameter of up to 450 mm and a maximum height of 550 mm. The maximum workpiece weight including the clamping device is 100 kg. The workpiece can be rotated during the process and moved along the X axis, while the nozzles can be moved along the Y and Z axes.

Alkaline parts cleaner protects against corrosion

Kyzen offers Metalnox M6314CP, a heavy-duty alkaline cleaner/inhibitor. Safe for use on aluminum, the cleaner is formulated to remove a variety of manufacturing oils used in machining, stamping, and deep-draw operations. It includes steel corrosion inhibitors to provide long-term protection for both mild and cast steel.

The product prevents flash rusting common in aqueous processing of ferrous metals. It’s also suitable for use on nonferrous metals, stainless steel, chrome steel, aluminum, magnesium, and highspeed steel.

Resistance welder can be configured for many different applications

Weld Systems Integrators Inc. offers the ValuePress resistance welder, configurable for many different applications. It features the company’s exclusive lightweight ram for faster follow-up required for projection welding. The welder can be configured for AC, AC inverter, and MFDC.

Features include a rigid frame with minimal deflection, full-size ram with preloaded linear bearings, solid copper secondary with no flexible stranded cables, reversible T-slotted platens for ease of mounting tooling, silver-plated secondary surfaces for MFDC applications, open construction, and industry-standard components.

Desktop resistance weld checker monitors, manages key welding variables

Amada Weld Tech has introduced the MM-400A desktop resistance weld checker. It allows operators to monitor and manage key welding variables that result in changes in weld heat, including current, voltage, time, force, and displacement. The compact unit supports AC, DC inverter, AC inverter, transistor, and capacitive discharge resistance welding technologies.

The unit is suitable for use in process development, where it can correlate waveform and numeric data with process results and provide detailed weld data for process optimization and validation. In a production environment, it reduces scrap by detecting drifts in the weld process and alerting operators before process failure. The machine also reduces the frequency of destructive testing and provides an independent way of monitoring the welding power supply.
