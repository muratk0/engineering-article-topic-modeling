# Plasma in focus

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

Above (left to right): Peter René Hiltebrand, Peter Hiltebrand Senior and grandson, Peter Michael Hiltebrand

## ARTISANAL METALWORKING SHOP,

Peter Hiltebrand AG in Höri, manufactures parts efficiently and economically using Boschert's Ergo Cut flatbed plasma cutting machine and its new controller with touch-screen operation. It bought the machine in 2016.

"It has a portal design and a working range of 1,530 x 3,030mm. The machine is equipped with a Kjellberg HiFocus plasma source and automatic cutting head height adjustment (two replaceable heads) and is able to cut sheets with thicknesses of between 0.5 and 50mm, depending upon the material. It operates at working and cutting speeds of between 200 and 8,000mm per minute, while machine positioning takes place at 30 metres per minute," Boschert told ISMR.

"The compact machine design features a traveling suction container and includes a movable worktable to make it easier to load and unload the sheets. Because it is possible to use different plasma gases, depending upon the requirements and application (for example, marking), the machine can be used to cut a wide range of materials such as steel, aluminium and non-ferrous metals."

The Swiss firm, which was initially mostly active in jobbing and contract work, made a name for itself by manufacturing playground equipment. It then developed a wide product range comprising, for example, window grilles, decorative grilles, fences, railings and gates. The Hiltebrand portfolio now includes, for example, house signs; mailboxes; flower stands and fireplace accessories. The company has also invested in machines, equipment and premises. The traditional blacksmith's forge is complemented by presses for forming, shears for cutting the metal sheets, punch presses and, of course, several welding stations. After a short introductory phase, the plasma cutting system is now used to cut individual parts or short runs of up to 100 parts as required.

Summing up, Peter René Hiltebrand explained: "This solution is vastly easier than the optically-based oxyacetylene cutting of the past. What is more, we are now extremely flexible when it comes to materials and thicknesses and we can manufacture the required parts immediately as a function of their priority. We started by manufacturing 10mm-thick parts for burial crosses, a rapidly growing sector for artisanal metal work. The trials with the corresponding sheets were successful and validated our decision to purchase a Boschert Ergo Cut 3015.

"Because we also work in metal construction, the plasma cutting system supports us in many different ways by allowing us to manufacture all the parts we require quickly and as the need arises. One important aspect is that Internet sales are bringing an increasing volume of business for items such as mailboxes, signs, sun dials, flower stands, murals, lamps or wine racks, with the result that we are actually now using the plasma cutting system more than we originally planned."
