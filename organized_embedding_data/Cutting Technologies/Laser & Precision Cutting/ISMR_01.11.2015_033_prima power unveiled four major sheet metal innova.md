# Prima Power unveiled four major sheet metal innovations at this year’s Blechexpo in Stuttgart

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

With manufacturing facilities in Italy, Finland, USA and China, it delivers machines and systems all over the world. At present, its installed base totals more than 10,000 systems. All its products are developed according to its “Green Means” concept, combining sustainability and productivity.

Prima Power is the Machinery Division of the Prima Industrie Group. It specialises in machines and systems for sheet metal working covering laser processing; punching; shearing; bending and automation.

Prima Power launched four major technology innovations, across a range of sheet metal applications, at this year’s Blechexpo in Stuttgart. ISMR met with Claudio Banchi, Prima Power’s executive VP of sales, at the show to gauge his views on the market trends that have been driving the company’s innovations in this area.

## Laser technology in focus

Prima Power has a long history in laser technology development and has, to date, installed around 2000 flat laser machines worldwide.

“At Blechexpo, we really see the technology developments that cannot wait until the next EuroBLECH. At this show, we are launching a new 2D fibre laser machine, Genius, with linear drives and a higher range with greater power and performance than our Platinum machine (6kW). At the same time, we have also developed our new CombiGenius combination machine - punching and fibre laser – with a very compact design and patentpending intelligent ram system. We can make the tool and ram rotate. This intelligent ram will shortly be available on all our punching machines but is being launched here at Blechexpo,” Claudio Banchi told ISMR.

The new Laser Genius - the spearhead of Prima Power 2D fibre laser cutting technology – features high dynamic linear motors for a 15% increase in productivity compared with conventional drive systems. The machine has a comprehensive range of automation modules and CNC proprietary management guarantees accuracy in cutting and head positioning.

The Prima Power fibre cutting head employs a single lens strategy, an impact protection system and a highly dynamic focal axis. The lens drawer has a quick alignment system (OPC) and protection glass drawer for easy inspection. A series of options are dedicated to different production needs, Prima told ISMR - SMART Cut for rapid cutting of thin sheets (up to 5mm) allows a reduction of cycle times by up to 30%, MAX Cut for the fast cutting of medium-thick gauge sheets, reducing processing times by up to 40% and NIGHT Cut, for intensive production and higher piercing and cutting process safety.

At Blechexpo, Prima Power also introduced the punching unit of its new Combi Genius punching – laser cutting cell. The new technology is also available in Punch Genius turret punch presses. Numerous improvements have been made not only in the punching unit but also on the ergonomics, making the cell very easy to operate. Enhanced productivity is also complemented by a new industrial design.

Its flexible turret concept allows operation of up to 384 tools or 128 index tools simultaneously in the turret. A new option is the rotary ram which shortens tool-change time and increases the number of tools in a turret, especially index tooling. More value can be added to the end product by using special tools for tapping, bending, rollforming, marking etc. As the laser source, a fibre resonator of either 3kW or 4kW can be chosen.

## The bending equation

“Our third Blechexpo launch is the eP2040 200-ton electric press brake. It features crowning on the lower beam and a bending angle control system (laser measurement system) that we developed ourselves. It is a rare example of an electric press brake that can cover higher tonnage,” added Claudio Banchi.

The eP2040 is a rapid, non-hydraulic bending solution with a new servo-electric drive system and CNC crowning system. The pulley belt system, actuated by Prima Electro servo drives and motors, distributes the bending force over the whole bending length for better movement control and accuracy.

The Prima-Power eP-Brake is based on a rigid O-frame. This guarantees tool alignment even under stress deformation, since there is no horizontal displacement. The position of the upper beam, in relation to the lower beam, is measured by dual Y1 and Y2 linear encoders that are fixed independently of the machine frame and are bed-referenced. This design isolates ram positioning accuracy from any deflection in the side frames under load and maintains accurate positioning even during off-centre bending operations.

“We are also promoting an upgrade on our electric Fastbend panel bender. It has been redesigned and features a new system that can control the C-frame from both sides and is more accurate, which is better for bending delicate parts (3.2mm max. thickness),” explained Claudio Banchi.

The new generation of the Fast Bend panel bender focuses on set-up rather than material handling (only loading, rotation and unloading are manual). The new Tulus Bend HMI speeds up operations for component handling and self-learning is also supported. The new CAM, Master BendCam, performs 3D simulation of part and machine and realistic visualization of various manufacturing processes.

“Fast Bend is efficient and sustainable, with an average energy consumption similar to the press brakes (7.5kWh) and reduced maintenance costs (-65%). Three models with different maximum bending lengths are available: FBe4 (2.250mm), FBe5 (2.750mm) and FBe6 (3.350mm),” added Banchi.

## Sheet metal market trends

“We see that fibre laser is gaining more of a foothold in the laser cutting market. At the moment, we are looking to increase power on our fibre laser machines. There are a lot of manufacturers in this area. We expect the fibre laser market to segment and we want to be present in all segments but especially towards the top end of the market,” Banchi told ISMR.

“Press brake and laser markets are competitive (the technology is relatively simpler), whereas the panel bending and combination machine markets are less competitive (the technology is more complex).”

He also pointed out that Industry 4.0 is not a new concept for Prima Power – many of its machines are already connected to systems on coil lines, for example.

“We have had to face Industry 4.0 issues for a long time. We are in a better position than suppliers with stand-alone machines and limited connectivity. We have developed software which can connect to a customer’s ERP with machine data and is customisable.

Prima Power also introduced the punching unit of its new Combi Genius punching laser cutting cell.

We will continue to develop this. Today, customers in Europe want short series and even one-off batches and software must keep pace with these requirements,” he explained.

Prima Industrie has been present in China since the 1990s and recently made a significant US$ 5.6 million investment to strengthen its direct presence in the Chinese market through the construction of a manufacturing plant in Suzhou (70km from Shanghai) for the production of laser, punching and press brakes machines for the Chinese market.
