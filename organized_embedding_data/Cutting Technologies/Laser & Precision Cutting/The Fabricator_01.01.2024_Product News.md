Fiber laser designed for ease of use, cost efficiency

ByCut Eco from Bystronic is designed to simplify users’ first steps in fiber laser cutting. Intuitive process control enables a quick start in laser cutting sheet metal for both longtime and less-experienced users.

Featuring a Fiber 6000 power source, the system cuts thick and thin sheet metal without interruption. A high-efficiency fiber laser source and fiber optic beam delivery provide low operating costs. The BySoft Cell Control Cut user interface with 22-in. HD touchscreen controls the entire manufacturing process. The software helps minimize material consumption by helping arrange the molds for the parts to be cut. The software also offers user support when creating cutting plans and setting parameters.

Automated cutting machine offers travel speeds to 1,400 IPM

ESAB has introduced the Combirex Pro for automated plasma and oxyfuel cutting and beveling. New features include dual linear rails on the crossbeam and heavy-duty crane rails mounted on H beams on both sides of the machine for X-axis movement.

A redesigned side carriage helps increase maximum travel speeds to 1,400 IPM. The system offers an additional 19.7 in. of cross travel, which enables users to cut a 10-ft. plate even if the machine is equipped with the manufacturer’s DMX beveler and a water table.

The machine is available in five different width options from 5 to 10 ft. with rail lengths from 16.4 to 65.6 ft. It can be equipped with four tools, including one plasma torch or up to four gas torches, as well as marking tools.

Orbital wrapping machines feature textured powder coating as standard

TAB Industries LLC has introduced new powder-coated steel machine guarding for its TAB Wrapper Tornado line of orbital wrapping machines. Included as standard, the powder-coated guarding has a textured gray finish that resists scratches and scuffs and hides dust and fingerprints to maintain a professional quality appearance.

The powder-coated machine safety guarding features laser-cut, 14-ga. steel for durability. It uses interchangeable parts to help promote quick assembly and permit retrofit on existing machines in the field.

Deburring tool’s new features help workers generate high-quality edges

TRUMPF has introduced a new TruTool TKA 1500 edge milling tool with updated features to create sheet metal edges with repeatable accuracy and without any rework. The redesigned deburring tool is now available with a cutting mount and guide fence for increased applications and safety.

The unit comes with a standard 45-degree multiedge cutter mount. Operators also can choose optional multiedge or radius multiedge cutter mounts in 30-, 60-, and 37.5-degree angle positions. The tool is suitable for deburring, rounding, or chamfering end edges. It also can be used to prepare parts for powder and paint coatings, as well as welded seams.

The bevel length can be adjusted without tools, and the new design provides an unrestricted view of the dial ring.
