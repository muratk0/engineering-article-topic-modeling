# Prima Power packs a punch

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

MANY NEW TECHNOLOGIES and solutions by Prima Power had their world premiere at Blechexpo, the international trade fair for sheet metal working held in Stuttgart from 7–10 November 2017. Its 2D laser cutting machine, Laser Genius 1530 with the Combo Tower Laser automation system, was showcased for the first time with a 10kW fibre laser source by IPG Photonics, which further boosts its performance especially for high material thicknesses. Laser Genius is managed by the Prima Power Open CNC and the HMI framework is the new Prima Power Tulus Laser 2D, a modern and userfriendly interface, applying the existing Tulus platform and its ecosystem to 2D lasercutting machines.

For the first time at an exhibition, Prima Power showcased its LPBB (Laser cutting - Punching - Buffering - Bending) manufacturing line. It features fibre laser cutting, punching and automatic bending enhanced with LSR loading and a stacking robot. As a world premiere, a new generation laser marker was also introduced, integrated into the production line. The LPBB line making its debut at Blechexpo 2017 featured the new combined punching/laser system Combi Genius 1530, the Express Bender EBe 5-3 panel bender and the LSR loading and stacking robot, creating a high-end automation solution from a blank sheet to a ready produced sheet metal part.

The eP-1030, the most versatile servoelectric press brake of its eP family, is now available with the new version of the bending follower option, enabling simpler and faster positioning of large and heavy parts. The eP-press brake showcased in Stuttgart was also equipped with a flattening table to increase productivity and facilitate operation. Wila-style upper and lower hydraulic tool clamping by Prima Power and an IRIS PLUS device for real-time correction of the angle make it suitable for producing high-quality complex components.

A software area was dedicated to live demonstrations and presentations of its full array of products for digital manufacturing. See www.primapower.com
