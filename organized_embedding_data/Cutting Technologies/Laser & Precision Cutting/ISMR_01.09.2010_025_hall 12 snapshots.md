## ADIRA S.A.

P – Hall 12, Stand D30
Website: www.adira.pt

ADIRA is an Iberian solutions provider for the cutting and bending of sheet with 54 years‘ experience. It also produces high speed laser cutting machines. The laser resonators are SLAB-type or optical fibre and the machines can be fitted with options such as tube cutting and automation. Adira also manufactures a wide range of shears and press brakes from 600mm to 12 metres and from 16 tons to 2000 tons, including robotic bending cells and complementing its offer with standard machines from the Guimadira line.

Left: ADIRA cutting and bending solutions

## CYBELEC S.A.

CH – Hall 12, Stand F39
Website: www.cybelec.ch

CYBELEC SA, founded in 1970, is a market leader in metal forming technology. Annual production has today reached over 6000 units and more than 60,000 machines have already been equipped with its units. For its 40th anniversary, Cybelec is launching a new generation of numerical controls for press brakes and shears. Visit the Cybelec stand and discover the new High End VisiTouch and the new Entry CybTouch Numerical Controls.

Above: Cybelec’s numerical controls

## DARLEY B.V.

NL – Hall 12, Stand A44
Website: www.darley.nl

At EuroBLECH, Darley will feature its latest innovative integrated sheet metal working solutions, including the new EHP TL press brake range, the new CNC-Touch screen control from Delem and the Laser Angle Control System. Darley will also exhibit the Multifold E-Drive bending automat to automatically produce a wide variety of panels, doors, plates, steel furniture and electrical cabinets on a medium-sized machine. Another new highlight will be the completely re-styled guillotine shear with the new 17”touchscreen control.

## INNOMAX AG

## JOUANEL INDUSTRIE

## F – Hall 12, Stand F36

## Website: www.jouanel.com

JOUANEL Industrie is a French company that has been manufacturing machines for thin metal since 1948 and is involved in the roofing, HVAC, insulation and metal construction industries. It offers a full line of bending rolls, shearing, folding, swaging, punching, rollforming, Pittsburg or Snaplock machinery and a range of hand tools for sheet metal. It also supplies automatic lines for uncoiling, adjustable punching, longitudinal and rear end beading and rollforming.

## SIMASV S.R.L.

## I – Hall 12, Stand D07

SIMASV will present its new CNC notching machine at EuroBLECH. The CNC variable angle 30°>140° AV226/PA-CNC notching machine is characterised by cutting accuracy (0.1°); max. cutting capability 220*6mm; a punching head; switch selecting; punching/cutting mode; a shearing head; selfadjustment of blades clearance (patented); solid structure; workbench with conical grooves; hydraulic locking of blade stroke; remote control and a wide choice of optional accessories.

## WEINBRENNER MASCHINENBAU GMBH

## D– Hall 12, Stand D36
