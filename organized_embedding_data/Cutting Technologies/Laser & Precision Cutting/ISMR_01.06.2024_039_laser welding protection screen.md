# Laser welding protection screen

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Shaver Industries, a specialist in industrial safety solutions, announced the European launch of its LZRTect laser welding protection systems this April at the MACH 2024 exhibition in Birmingham, UK. LZRTect can be pulled or retracted for steady protection during welding. The operator walks the guard from the roller tube to the J-hooks on the opposite side of the work cell.

“The retractable laser weld screens are designed to provide reliable protection against lasers, sparks, heat, ultraviolet light and flying debris. The key feature of LZRTect is its versatility, allowing for quick and efficient movement to any welding location within a workspace," said the manufacturer.

The retractable system extends up to 20 feet in length, automatically retracting with a spring tension roller. This design allows for multiple mounting locations, facilitating top access and ensuring a clear path for overhead cranes when rolled up. Portable and durable, LZRTect's screens can be swiftly moved to any welding location, eliminating the need for large stationary guards.■
