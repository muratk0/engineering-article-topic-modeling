Direct-diode laser provides high cutting speeds in thin to medium worksheets

Mazak Optonics offers the Optiplex 3015 DDL directdiode laser—the first of its kind, according to the manufacturer. This platform delivers higher performance and reliability than traditional fiber or other solid-state laser generation systems, the company states.

The PreviewG control provides high speed and accuracy for increased productivity. It offers optimum acceleration and deceleration for the reduction of cutting time.

Dome-loaded back-pressure regulator suitable for almost all gases

Witt Gas Controls has introduced the BPR2 domeloaded back-pressure regulator, which maintains constant gas pressure in processes, system components, and tanks, regardless of gas volume or flow fluctuations.

Using a dome diaphragm, the unit regulates a higher inlet pressure by opening up only as much as necessary to achieve the desired pressure at the inlet. It is suitable for almost all technical gases, including oxygen, from 7 to 290 PSI and temperatures from -22 to 122 degrees F.

Press brake automation software flattens 3-D model for bending in one click

SigmaTEK offers SigmaBEND 3-D press brake automation software. Designed in partnership with some of the largest press brake manufacturers, the software takes a 3-D model or flat-pattern CAD drawing through tool selection and bend sequencing automatically. According to the company, it takes the guesswork out of running a press brake, including bend-by-bend reports for the shop floor.

With one click, the software flattens a 3-D model and readies it for bend processing. Using its database of material K-factors and an extensive tool catalog, the software applies the proper tool and die combinations for every part, including calculating station setup and detecting possible collisions. User input can prioritize different factors, and the software will produce several bend scenarios from which the user can choose.

Once the bends are sequenced, with another click, a flat-pattern DXF can be exported to be cut in SigmaNEST, an NC file sent to the press brake, and a shop floor report printed.

Automated system loads/unloads in 60 seconds

The Bystronic ByTrans Extended is a compact material handling system that loads and unloads in 60 seconds. It integrates into any ByVision-controlled machine, and the short cycle time is independent of the thickness of the metal sheet.

Solid-state disk laser designed for Industry 4.0

TRUMPF has introduced the latest generation of its TruDisk disk laser. The compact, energy-efficient machine offers the ability to carry out condition monitoring and predictive maintenance, a basic prerequisite for successful implementation of lasers in digitalized and connected manufacturing environments.

Condition-based services are a modular component of TruConnect, the company’s technology for connected manufacturing and Industry 4.0. They increase the availability and productivity of connected laser systems while identifying potential cost savings.

The built-in CPX control system collects all condition data and process parameters. During processing, sensors measure multiple parameters, including the actual laser output at microsecond intervals, all internal and external signal characteristics, the utilization rate of the beam source, and the condition of additional components. The Precision Time Protocol feature synchronizes all the sensors and provides them with an identical time stamp.

GMAW consumables offer cool contact tips

Tweco, an ESAB brand, has announced that its Classic Numbered series GMAW guns now are available with Tweco Velocity™ consumables. The consumables feature gas-ported contact tips that keep the tip 30 percent cooler, which the company says improves parts life by up to 500 percent or more in gasshielded flux-cored, spray transfer, and pulsed spray transfer applications. In short-circuit GMAW applications, the consumables typically last two to three times longer than conventional consumables, states the manufacturer.

The consumables feature threadless, drop-in-style contact tips and integrate the contact tip seating area directly into the conductor tube. With the mediumand heavy-duty consumables, the shielding gas flows from the conductor tube through four ports machined into the base of the contact tip. The cooler contact tip helps reduce the ability for spatter to stick.
