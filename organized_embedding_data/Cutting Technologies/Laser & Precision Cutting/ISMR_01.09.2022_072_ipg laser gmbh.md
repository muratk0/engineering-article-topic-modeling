# IPG Laser GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

https://lasersystems.ipgphotonics.com/de-DE

IPG Photonics is a global specialist leader in fibre laser technology. It offers a diverse selection of fibre laser families across all modes of operation including continuous wave lasers; quasi-CW lasers; nanosecond pulsed lasers; ultrafast pico- and femtosecond pulsed lasers. IPG fibre lasers span the wavelength range from ultraviolet to Mid-IR and provide output powers from a few to many kilowatts.

"This technology enables greater precision, higher speed processing, more flexible production methods and enhanced productivity across multiple applications and industry," it highlighted.

"Diverse lines of low-, medium- and high-power lasers and amplifiers are used in materials processing; communications; entertainment; medical; biotechnology; scientific and advanced applications with many new products displacing traditional technologies. IPG products are available globally for OEMs, system integrators and end-users across a wide range of industries," it told ISMR.

IPG has over 6,000 employees in offices throughout the Americas, Asia and Europe.

## WECO Srl

www.weco.it/de/

Italian welding specialist, WECO, produces systems at its new production facility in Cittadella (north-east Italy) offering machines, systems and software solutions for the professional welding market (TIG, MIG and MMA welding).

At EuroBLECH, it will showcase its Power Pulse Digital MIG/MAG and MIG/MAG Pulsed welding machine for processing common and stainless steels, aluminium and alloys. Also on display will be the Micro Pulse 225, a portable, very light (16kg) multi-process welding machine.

"The Micropulse 225 provides all MIG/MAG Standard Synergic, MIG/MAG Pulsed, MMA and TIG DC Lift processes. It also includes the double pulsed function for those alternative MIG/MAG applications," WECO told ISMR.

The Plasma Voyager 105 is a power source that has been designed for high-performance plasma cutting. It can offer processes such as Synergic Cut, Manual Cut and gauging.

"Every year, we invest over 10% of our turnover in the design and development of technologies capable of innovating our products," added WECO.
