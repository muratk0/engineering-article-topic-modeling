# New innovations at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

AMADA PRESENTED ITS latest highlights from the world of sheet metal processing at EuroBLECH 2018, with a special focus on laser technologies and production benefits delivered by IoT. Recent developments in AMADA's ENSIS laser technology, powered by in-house developed fibre oscillators, were also showcased. ENSIS technology uses variable beam control to provide high speed and high quality for the processing of flat sheet and tube and even for welding.

New machine and automation options were presented, such as the AMADA VENTIS-3015AJ 4kW new fibre laser generation with 4kW laser power and one laser module and the AMADA ENSIS-3015AJ 9kW fibre laser cutting machine (equipped with Variable Beam Control technology and new Auto Collimation mechanism). AMADA also introduced a 6kW variant at EuroBLECH 2018, using AMADA's inhouse developed and built fibre laser oscillator. It combined Variable Beam Control technology and new Auto Collimation mechanism.

EuroBLECH 2018 was also the world premiere of the EML-2515AJ servo-electric punch and fibre laser cutting combination machine. Equipped with AMADA's own 3kW fibre laser oscillator and 300kN punching capacity, it boasted several special features. The PDC (Punch and Die Changer) unit can hold 220 punch tools and 440 dies, allowing automatic tool changing in-cycle to reduce set-up and down times. A four-station automatic nozzle changer, MPT (Multi-Purpose Turret) with four-station tapping unit and RMP-3015N automatic load/ unload system were also featured.

AMADA FLW-ENSIS3000 M5 fibre laser welding system and ENSIS-3015RI 3 kW fibre laser cutting machine for flat sheet and tube processing were also highlighted on its booth at Hanover. Bending innovations on display also included the AMADA HFE3i-1003L Press Brake, HG-2204ATC Press Brake with automatic tool changer and EG-4010 servo-electric press brake.

A further focus was on the next generation of AMADA's networkbased machine surveillance concept with two new features, allowing monitoring – anytime, anywhere. My V-factory visualises the entire connected production environment at a glance. The optional new IoT-Support is the second element providing a direct-response customer service concept for error prevention and immediate support.
