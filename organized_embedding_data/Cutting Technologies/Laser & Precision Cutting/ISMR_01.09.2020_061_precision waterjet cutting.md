# Precision waterjet cutting

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

OMAX CORP®, AN international leader in abrasive waterjet technology, will showcase its extensive product line at EuroBLECH 2021 (9-12 March 2021) in Hall 12 (stand H80). Included in the lineup will be its ProtoMAX® and OMAX® 55100.

“ProtoMAX is a compact cutting tool ideal for prototyping and low-volume abrasive waterjet cutting. With a cutting window of 305mm by 305mm, the light industrial waterjet cuts nearly any material up to 26mm thick. The ProtoMAX is suitable for small job shops, engineering classrooms, makerspaces and personal use,” said the manufacturer.

“A much more robust machine, the OMAX 55100 JetMachining Center®, is the largest of the OMAX cantileverstyle machines. With the addition of a Tilt-a-Jet® and a 40hp EnduroMAX® pump, the OMAX waterjet has become a powerful and versatile tool to add to a shop floor. The Tilt-A-Jet positions the nozzle at a precise angle calculated by IntelliMAX® software to exactly offset the natural taper from the abrasive jet.”

OMAX’s easy-to-operate control software, IntelliMAX, was engineered specifically for use with abrasive waterjets and no special machine code knowledge is required to use it. Someone unfamiliar with CNC machine operation can learn to cut parts on an OMAX waterjet in just a few hours, OMAX told ISMR.

“Online training for machine operation and maintenance is provided free, so customers can access training information whenever they need it and at their own pace. IntelliMAX software is compatible with more than 90 different file formats, including all major CAD program file types, plus graphics file formats such as .jpg, .gif, and .png files. This means that almost any 2D or 3D part file can be imported directly into an OMAX waterjet controller and turned into a real part,” concluded OMAX.

Through its distribution network of 30 partners worldwide, OMAX Corporation has expanded its global reach and increased the availability of its precisionengineered technology.
