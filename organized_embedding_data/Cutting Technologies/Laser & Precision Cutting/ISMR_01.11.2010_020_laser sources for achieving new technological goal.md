# LASER SOURCES FOR ACHIEVING NEW TECHNOLOGICAL GOALS

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

■ Reduced running costs, thanks to the drastic reduction in power consumption required by the source; 70% when cutting if compared with a 3000 W CO2 source and 8 times less when the machine is on stand-by

■ Elimination of maintenance costs, due to the simplicity of the power source

■ The absence of an optical path and the need for laser gas as the electronic power source does not require periodical maintenance and its life is the same as that of the laser machine itself. Advanced beam reflection control systems guarantee integrity and safe operation

■ Higher cutting speeds and rapid piercing due to extremely high beam density

■ Wider range of machinable materials thanks to the characteristic fibre wavelength which makes it possible to cut highly reflective materials such as aluminium, copper and brass efficiently; high-quality processing is also achievable with galvanized and zinc coated steel

■ Elimination of source warm-up times and costs.
