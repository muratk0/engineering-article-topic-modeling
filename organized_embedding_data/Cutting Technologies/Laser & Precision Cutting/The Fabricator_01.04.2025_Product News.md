Fiber laser cutting machine cuts mild steel up to 4 in. thick

Machitech has introduced the new Fiber Prime S laser cutting machine. It combines high-speed 4G acceleration with 12 to 60 kW for cutting mild steel up to 4 in. thick.

The machine, which handles a variety of heavy-duty applications, includes a Precitec cutting head that provides integrated gas mixing and focused compensation. The system also features the optional Machitech bevel head for creating complex bevel cuts, including V, X, Y, and K, with custom angles in a single operation.

Low-profile magnetic drill fits in tight workspaces

Blair Equipment Co. has launched the BFD-KX+ right-angle magnetic drill, designed for accessibility in tight workspaces. It enables operators to drill in areas that are hard to reach without compromising on performance or durability, the company states.

At 615/32 in. tall, the drill is suitable for confined spaces where standard drills cannot operate. The high-torque motor helps ensure consistent drilling performance on tough materials. It uses Karbon X+ annular cutters and features a magnetic base for steady drilling operations. The simple, intuitive control panel allows for easy operation, the manufacturer reports.

Welding gauge has three functions in one

Weldersgauge.com

offers the Pro Welders Gauge for professionals and students. Made of 0.035-in. stainless steel, the tool is three gauges in one, measuring material thickness from ½ to 16 ga.; electrode, rod, and wire from 1/16 to ½ in.; and fillet welds from 3/16 to ⅜ in.

The size of a credit card, the gauge fits in a wallet and has a hole for use with a lanyard. Relevant information is laser-etched into the gauge, and the etching is darkened with ink for readability.

Software lets users manage workflow in multiple robotic workcells

Pemamek Ltd. has launched the Pema CellControl software program for Pema robot stations. Through its browser-based touchscreen interface, the operator can manage workflow in a single or multiple robotic workcells.

The software features drag-and-drop job queue and product database functions, the latter of which allow operators to build their own product directories. This helps simplify management of preprogrammed welding jobs and repetitive tasks. The live status monitoring and built-in alarms provide instant feedback so operators can make adjustments on the fly.

Large-format fiber laser features processing area up to 120 ft.

The V353 large-format fiber laser cutting machine from Voortman provides fully autonomous plate processing. Powered by a 15-kW laser, the system delivers bevel cuts up to 45 degrees with minimal operator input.

With its modular design and processing area of up to 120 ft., the machine handles plates up to 10 by 20 ft. and 1.5 in. thick. Advanced plate buffering and automatic nozzle changers help the machine transition smoothly between materials and thicknesses.

Zirconia flap discs feature flexible cotton backing for intricate contours

Weldcote, a division of Zika Group, has introduced its line of Z-Cotton zirconia flap discs with a flexible cotton backing suitable for fine finishing work on detailed contours.

Designed for applications on carbon-based steels and stainless steel that require high conformability and a softer feel, the discs are constructed of zirconia aluminum oxide grains. The discs are available in both Type 27 and Type 29 4-in. sizes with a choice of backing material density: regular, XL, and XXL.

Smart pendant software streamlines programming for robot welding

Yaskawa America Inc. has launched Weld Builder, a new smart pendant software package designed to streamline the welding programming process for robot integrators and end users.

The software features a user-friendly interface with a step-by-step guided process for creating weld jobs through the Weld Wizard. This tool is designed to help users, especially those new to robot welding, by guiding them through the weld job and helping them select appropriate welding parameters. The Weld Advisor feature suggests potential welding parameters based on user-defined welds.
