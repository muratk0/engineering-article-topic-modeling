# Silent Cut with plasma

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

DUE TO high energy density and flow velocity during high-quality plasma cutting, sound levels can reach over 100dB(A), depending upon the cutting task. With new Silent Cut technology, Kjellberg Finsterwalde now offers a solution to reduce the noise exposure, which can be disturbing for human hearing (particularly in the upper frequency range).

"As a further development of our patented Contour Cut technology, Silent Cut reduces the sound pressure level by up to 15dB(A) during plasma cutting of mild steels with a material thickness of between four- and 30mm in the current range between 60- and 160 A. Even high frequencies perceived as particularly disturbing are considerably reduced. The reduction of sound levels by 10 decibels is already perceived by human hearing as a halving of the loudness. Silent Cut therefore makes an important contribution to health and safety at work whilst ensuring high cut quality," Kjellberg told ISMR.

Kjellberg's intensive development work forms the foundation of Silent Cut technology. The results are a combination of new consumables and new cutting data, allowing the user to cut holes, bars and contours with similarly high-quality as Contour Cut and to benefit from considerably reduced sound pressure levels. The technology can also be used for existing systems.
