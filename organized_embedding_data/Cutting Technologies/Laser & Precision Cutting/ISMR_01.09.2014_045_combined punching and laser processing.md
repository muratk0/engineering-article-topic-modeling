# Combined punching and laser processing

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

NUMEROUS WORLD premieres and many innovations along the entire process chain will be on exhibit in the TRUMPF booth at EuroBLECH 2014. Appearing in almost every product sector are innovations for economical and profitable laser cutting, welding and marking, along with punching and bending – more than TRUMPF has ever unveiled before at a trade show. Design and programming software, the new generation in the TruBend Series 5000 and – with the semi-automated TruBend Center – a new member of the TRUMPF press brake line.

“Also celebrating its premiere will be the TruPunch 5000, which has raised productivity standards in punching. The TruMark 5050, thanks to higher mean laser power, can mark products even faster and still at outstanding quality. Numerous innovations also make the TruLaser 2D laser cutting machines a highlight. Also new is the TruMatic 6000 fiber, a combined punch laser machine,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

“The TruMatic 6000 fiber combines the advantages of the solid-state laser with high quality punching technology, making it a productive punch laser machine when working thin sheet metal. It uses the high cutting speeds possible with the TruDisk 3001 and combines its 180 kilonewtons of punching force, at 1000 strokes per minute, with highly dynamic drives. It rapidly cuts, punches and shapes sheet metal of up to 6.4mm thick. When engraving, it marks parts at 2800 strokes per minute.”

When considering productivity, the strengths of the solid-state laser come to bear especially in galvanized mild steel and film-covered stainless steel. In this application area, the advancing speeds are as much as six times those of a CO2 laser of the same power ratio, said TRUMPF. A descending die, a laser guard with its own NC axis and part removal flaps equipped with brushes ensure gentle processing of the materials and high part quality. To ensure that delicate curves and tight radii are cut with quality at both edges and corners, the machine is also equipped with a sophisticated laser output control system. It can process mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium as well as nonferrous metals like copper and brass.

“Guided by the laser light cable, the beam is directed to the point where it is needed. This might be the cutting head at the TruMatic 6000 fiber, a welding cell or another TRUMPF machine that uses the laser as a tool. This is made possible by the LaserNetwork. Here, multiple machines can share the TruDisk 3001 and thus optimise laser use. This concept is especially attractive to companies that are making their entry into laser welding. The laser also uses minimal energy thanks to high efficiency levels,” TRUMPF added.

TRUMPF offers myriad options to keep down non-productive times for the TruMatic 6000 fiber. Among them are automation for the material flow and tool change, along with smart functions determining whether a need for action has arisen and then taking that action automatically. If desired, automatic loading and unloading can be handled by the SheetMaster, using individually-controlled vacuum grippers. The SheetMaster can be augmented with a tool changer providing storage for 40 additional tool stations. As an alternate, the ToolMaster can store and change from 40 to 70 tools. See www.trumpf.com ■
