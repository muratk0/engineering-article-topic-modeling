# Laser cutting machine market in the U.S.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

The U.S. laser cutting machine market size is expected to reach US$ 1.95 billion by 2030, registering a CAGR of 5.9% from 2024 to 2030, according to new research from ResearchAndMarkets.com. The estimated market value in 2023 was US$ 1.32 billion.

"The growing trend of automation in the manufacturing sector and the rising demand for end-use industry will lead to market growth. Laser cutting machines have become increasingly popular in various industries such as metal fabrication; automotive; pharmaceuticals; electronics; aerospace and others. The market is projected to experience rapid growth due to the rising adoption of Industry-4.0 technologies, including the Internet of Things (IoT), data analytics and automation," said the report, 'U.S. Laser Cutting Machines Market Size, Share & Trends Analysis Report 2024-2030.'

These technologies facilitate real-time information exchange for laser cutting machines, which operators monitor and manage to increase output. Laser cutting machines feature sensors, control systems and data analysis platforms that enable realtime monitoring and predictive maintenance. The growing trend of automation in the manufacturing sector is also fuelling demand for laser cutting machines.

According to the report, the flame cutting segment held the largest market share of 44.1% in 2023. Cutting and finishing precision, as well as rapid cutting speed, have led to high demand for flame cutting machines. Flame cutting is used to cut and process steel and high-thickness materials.

"The high-power, laser cutting machines segment held the largest market share in 2023. Cutting multiple layers and

large quantities of metal sheets is easier with laser cutting machines that provide enhanced power. The fibre laser segment held the largest share in 2023. Fibre lasers capacity to achieve faster cutting speeds enhances productivity and contributes to cost savings and improved turnaround times in manufacturing processes," outlined the report.

The automotive sector held the largest sector share in 2023. Laser cutting tools offer advantages including reduced errors, consistent high-quality cuts and the ability to create complex shapes and designs efficiently.
