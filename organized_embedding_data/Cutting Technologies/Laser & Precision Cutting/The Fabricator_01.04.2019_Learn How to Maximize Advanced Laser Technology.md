# Learn How to Maximize Advanced Laser Technology

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 The Fabricator]

Laser technology is changing rapidly. Advancements in robotic laser heads, smaller and more accurate sensors, increased power, and other developments are making lasers more affordable and adaptable than ever before. That’s why it’s more important than ever to learn how to maximize the capabilities of innovative laser applications.

FMA’s 26th annual Advanced Laser Applications Workshop (ALAW), the longest-running conference in the industry, is developed specifically for manufacturers, job shops, and suppliers to the automotive, aerospace, agriculture, and construction industries. Twenty-five educational sessions are offered, including one from Bernard Swiecki from CAR who is back by popular demand, to address current changes in the automotive market. Other topics for the educational sessions cover everything from laser welding to additive manufacturing. Case studies will share how lasers offer flexible, dependable, and costeffective solutions for batteries, seats, powertrain, body-in-white, e-mobility, and other automotive applications.

Leading experts, distinguished keynote speakers, and company representatives will all be there with knowledge to share for three days of learning, networking, and cutting-edge technologies you won’t find anywhere else.
