# New plasma power source

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Kjellberg is expanding its portfolio for plasma cutting with its new K 200 power source which was presented live at the Schweißen & Schneiden exhibition in Essen, Germany, this September.

“With the K 200, users can focus on the essentials of plasma cutting: optimal and reliable cutting results with easy operation, low investment and operating costs," commented Kjellberg.

The power source cuts with oxygen, air and nitrogen in the material thickness range from 1-60mm, with a maximum cutting current of 200 A. The K 200 has a marking function and also features integrated automatic gas control and a torch with direct connection. A manual torch extends the range of application to manual cutting and gouging.

"Overall, the user benefits from low investment costs and easy installation, operation and maintenance of the system,"

The K 200 can be fully integrated into CNC guidance systems or controlled and monitored via smartphone. The accompanying app also allows the user to see all parameters and settings as well as customise the cutting data.

Kjellberg held live mild steel plasmacutting demonstrations on its booth at the Essen show, using its latest technology. The focus was on rectangular, smooth cut surfaces and precise holes. Presentation of Kjellberg's entire portfolio for automated plasma cutting (in the material thickness range from 0.5 to 160mm) at the exhibition was complemented by insight into the real-time analysis, monitoring and control of process data during the plasma-cutting process.

Kjellberg Cutting also showcased the capabilities of its Q plasma power sources/ systems, and other new products, at the FABTECH exhibition in Chicago, USA, in September.

For 100 years, Kjellberg Finsterwalde has developed and manufactured sustainable plant equipment for the metalworking industry (made in Germany). Its portfolio includes products and technologies for plasma cutting, welding and customised special-purpose machines. For more than 60 years, it has been one of the market leaders in plasma cutting.
