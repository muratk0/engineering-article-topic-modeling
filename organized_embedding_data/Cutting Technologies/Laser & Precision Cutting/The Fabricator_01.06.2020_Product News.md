Nontoxic rust converter can be applied to damp surfaces

NanoRustX offers NanoPrime, which it says is the first multipurpose, zero-VOC, 100% nontoxic rust converter. The water-based, singlecomponent product converts iron oxide into iron phosphate and creates high-strength adhesion to rusty, clean, painted, or galvanized steel. It also delivers excellent single-coat adhesion and corrosion protection when used on aluminum, the company states.

Comprising nanopolymers that create a chemically bonded layer, the product prevents under-film corrosion when used with low-VOC topcoats. It can be applied to damp surfaces, requires no sandblasting, prevents surface cracking, requires minimal protective equipment when applying, and isn’t sensitive to chlorides.

Combination punch/fiber laser system offered

TRUMPF has developed the TruMatic 1000 fiber combination punch/laser machine, a compact system that offers a working range of 5 by 10 ft.

The combination is achieved by adding a 3-kW TruDisk solid-state laser and retrofitting the TruPunch 1000 with a laser cutting system, a laser evacuation unit, and a beam guard system.

This machine is designed with two sorting options. Processed parts up to 7 by 7 in. are sent down a chute to be sorted into a series of four boxes positioned below the machine for easy removal. Alternatively, an additional extra-large, flexible parts flap can sort parts up to 16 by 48 in.

System combines waterjet deburring, cleaning, drying

Ecoclean has developed the modular EcoCvelox system, which combines 5-axis, high-pressure deburring with various part cleaning and drying processes in a custom configuration. According to the company, it can achieve cycle times of 15 seconds per pallet.

Features include a CAD/CAM interface for rapid, offline programming of the high-pressure deburring function, as well as dynamic part handling technology.

With the modular design, a combination with other products from the manufacturer, such as solvent cleaning before deburring in applications involving high oil input, also is possible. The diverse standard modules of the system are rated for parts measuring 200 by 200 by 200 mm, which are supplied on pallets. This format covers most general industry applications.

Power clamps save space

Destaco has introduced its model 84A40-1/84A50-1 power clamps. Despite their very compact doublearm design, these pneumatically powered clamps produce high force and tolerance compensation for firmly holding sheet metal together during various critical assembly applications, such as welding.

The standard opening angle of the clamp is 90 degrees, but the opening angle of both arms is independently adjustable. This enables space-saving installation and adds flexibility.

Three different sets of arms are available for the smaller 84A40-1 model and four for the 84A50-1. They have two-part sensing and are available in three different sensor variants, such as PNP and NPN.

Mobile sales app gives real-time overview of service center inventory, sales

Invera has released Invex-Go, a real-time mobile sales app for the metal industry. Management and sales teams can get their metal business KPIs and metrics while on the go, in real time. The app provides an overview of a metal service center’s current sales activity, inventory levels, and customer sales-related information.

The app is a suite of mobile dashboards that provide both historical analysis and open transactions. Information is presented in the form of graphs, tables, and charts.

The Stock Available tab gives a realtime inventory position; the Customer 360 tab provides a comprehensive customer profile; and the Scoreboard shows open quotes, bookings, and sales analysis. Other dashboards are Daily Snapshot company overview and Salesperson 360.

Gaylord tilter simplifies loading of conveyors, sorters

A new gaylord tilter from Southworth Products Corp. helps operators move items from gaylords and other large containers onto conveyors and automated sortation systems.

The model GTU-500 tilts loads up to 110 degrees to provide controlled emptying of container contents. It features a panstyle platform that sits essentially flush with the floor when lowered, allowing gaylords to be placed and retrieved by a hand pallet truck. A steel retention bar prevents gaylords from falling even when the unit is tilted to the full 110 degrees. It has a 500-lb. capacity when fully tilted. The 50- by 50-in. platform accommodates gaylords on 48- by 48-in. pallets.

Fully electric rolling device added to end former

BLM GROUP USA has announced the enhancement of its EForm tube end forming machine with a fully CNC electric rolling device. The punches of the end former reach up to 10 tons of force, molding tubes up to 30 mm dia. of various materials such as copper, aluminum, iron, and stainless steel.

The machine has nine available processing stations for shaping, rolling, butting, and cutting that can be configured to the user’s needs.

The absence of obstacles in the top part of the machine allows closing previously shaped tubes in the clamp, even those with very complex 3D geometries. Since all of these operations can be performed in a single pass without having to withdraw material from the machine, cycle times are reduced.

Fiber laser cuts at up to 5,500 IPM

Kaast has introduced the Lasersonic B for sheet metal fiber laser cutting. Producing 1,000 to 12,000 W of laser power, it offers a maximum acceleration of 1 g and a maximum speed of 5,500 IPM. Positioning accuracy of the X/Y axis is ±0.0004 in./12 in., and X/Y axis worktable repeatability is 0.0007 in.

Offering cutting capacities from 10 by 5 ft. to 12 by 6 ft., the honeycomb welded structure of the machine incorporates a gantry double-drive control system, large inertia servomotor, and high-precision gear and rack. Cypcut software measures real-time frequency and power curves, and laser power is adjusted automatically according to the speed of movement of the cutting head.
