# Fibre laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

LVD now offers YSD LaserONE, which it bills as ‘a new cost-effective laser cutting machine designed to put the advantages of fibre laser technology within easy reach of sheetmetal fabricators by eliminating the extras that increase machine cost and complexity’. LaserONE is offered with a 2- or 4kW laser power source in a 3000 x 1500mm table size and with optional Load-Assist automation.

LaserONE is designed, manufactured, sold and serviced by LVD. Engineered and tested by LVD Company in Belgium, it cuts a variety of materials and thicknesses with the flexibility of fibre laser, offers low operating and maintenance costs and, said the company, provides a quick return on investment. The machine features a Precitec cutting head with NC-focus; automatic focal adjustment and crash protection; Raycus laser source; Siemens control; servo motors and drives.

“The YSD LaserONE is equipped with an automatic pallet changer for rapid interchanging of the shuttle tables over a cycle time of approximately 30 seconds. An optional Load-Assist automation system offers easy loading/unloading,” LVD explained to ISMR.

YSD is an established brand which is owned and used by one of LVD’s joint venture companies. The YSD LaserONE made its debut at LVD’s INSIGHTS 2020 in Virtual Tech Sessions. For details, go to: www.lvdgroup-insights.com/en or see www.lvdgroup.com n
