# Mild steel plasma cutting

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

"Visitors to Schweißen & Schneiden 2023 (Essen, Germany) this September will be able to see the excellent results of plasma cutting live at Kjellberg Finsterwalde's stand 6B21 (Hall 6). Several times a day, there will be live demonstrations of mild steel plasma cutting using the latest technology. The focus will be on rectangular, smooth cut surfaces and precise holes," Kjellberg Cutting told ISMR.

The presentation of Kjellberg's entire portfolio for automated plasma cutting (in the material thickness range from 0.5 to 160mm) at the exhibition will be rounded off by insight into the real-time analysis, monitoring and control of process data during the plasma-cutting process.

Kjellberg Cutting will also be exhibiting at the FABTECH show at McCormick Place in Chicago, USA, from 11-14 September 2023. Its U.S.-based team will be showcasing the capabilities of its Q plasma power sources/ systems, and other new products, on booth A5880.

At EuroBLECH 2022, Kjellberg Finsterwalde presented the Q 4500 plasma power source with a cutting current range of up to 450A. It combines plasma cutting and marking, enabling users to make plasma cuts of up to 120mm.

Left: Exact plasma cuts in mild steel will be live on Kjellberg Finsterwalde's stand 6B21 at Schweißen & Schneiden 2023.

For 100 years, Kjellberg Finsterwalde has developed and manufactured sustainable plant equipment for the metalworking industry (made in Germany). Its portfolio includes products and technologies for plasma cutting, welding and customised special-purpose machines. For more than 60 years, it has been one of the market leaders in plasma cutting.■
