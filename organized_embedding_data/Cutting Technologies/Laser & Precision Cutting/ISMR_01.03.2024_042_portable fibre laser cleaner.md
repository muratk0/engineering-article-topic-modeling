# Portable fibre laser cleaner

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

The MobiLase® Laser Cleaner is a portable, all-in-one fibre laser cleaner housed in a 60lb suitcase.

"Users can rapidly remove rust, paint, oil, grease, dirt and more. The powerful 100W laser cleans and treats moulds, tools and other surfaces with precision wherever a clean surface is needed," explained the manufacturer, Rocklin Manufacturing Co.

"There is no need for grinding, blasting, solvents, water or chemicals used in lessefficient cleaning processes. It is a portable solution: users can bring the all-in-one suitcase wherever cleaning is needed. The short pulse duration and small output beam diameter delivers precise surfacelevel cleaning and there are few settings to master (either offline or remotely via wireless control)," it added.

Laser glasses and gloves are included along with safety guidance and optional fume extractors. The system is backed by a one-year warranty and local support.
