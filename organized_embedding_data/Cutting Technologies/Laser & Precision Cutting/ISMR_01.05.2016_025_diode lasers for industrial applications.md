# Diode lasers for industrial applications

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

LASERLINE will present its diode lasers for industrial applications at the forthcoming Lasys exhibition in Stuttgart (Hall 4, booth 4B51) from 31 May – 2 June 2016.

Its exhibition highlight will be the fibrecoupled high power diode laser, LDF 11.000-60 with 11kW laser power and 60mm mrad beam quality. The laser has a compact design and is suitable for typical metal processing such as welding, hardening, cladding or brazing.

Its new water-air-cooler, Laserline CCA12, for lasers of the LDF series will also be exhibited for the first time at the Lasys. This device guarantees cooling of the laser and beam-shaping optics. Thanks to its integration into the system control of the laser, it can be easily configured and monitored.

Laserline will also present its LDF 4000-4 diode laser with beam converter and diode laser module, LDM 4000-100, at Lasys. The LDF 4000-4 was developed for applications with high demand for beam quality – such as, for example, remote welding – and offers up to 4kW power at a beam quality of 4mm mrad. In contrast, the modular LDM 4000-100 was created as a 19” rack mount and also reaches a high laser power of 4kW at 100mm mrad beam quality.

The company's presence will be rounded off by exhibits on cladding and parts for welding of tailored blanks. Additionally, Laserline will show optics for welding and hardening processes as well as samples from various application areas
