# CUT AND PUNCH

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Produtech's CEO, Fabrizio Mologni, outlined its latest technology developments, strategic objectives, market views and production philosophy to ISMR...

Produtech was founded in 1993 and specialises in sheet-metal processing, coil-fed systems with a high degree of customisation. Based in Albano Sant'Alessandro in the province of Bergamo, Italy, it produces sheet-metal processing (punching and laser cutting) machines and systems which feed material directly from coil. Systems include combined punch-laser systems and special equipment, such as punching lines for processing metal bars and beading machines.

Its latest innovations include an optical fibre, coil-fed laser system and its EffiCOIL coil-fed combination fibre laser cutting and punching machine, which will be on display at the EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022).

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes, awards and achievements along with the reasons for this.

FM: Produtech specialises in the production of coil-fed systems for sheet-metal cutting and forming. Research/development is an important investment item in the company budget and has led to the creation of a wide range of innovative machines protected by international patents. Our customer portfolio today counts a prevalence of overseas customers over Italian ones, an indicator of our extensive presence in the main global markets.

We have received numerous awards from Italian government agencies, such as Invitalia, for the enhancement and development of our patents. Awards have also been given by the European Community under the Horizon 2020 programme, with several certificates of excellence awarded to us for the development and implementation of machines and innovative solutions. Competence, reliability and exclusive technological expertise, combined with great attention to customer needs, are the values that characterise Produtech.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for coil-processing professionals?

FM: Production from coil, by coil-fed machines and systems, enable users to obtain finished products that do not need further, or various disparate, production processes. However, coil processing is not as prevalent in global markets as, for example, processes that initially deliver finished workpieces from flat sheet-metal feeding.

The production of metal workpieces directly from metal sheet is a mainstream process in the market, despite the great production benefits of feeding metal direct from coil. Many major global sheet-metalworking machinery manufacturers and brands offer machines and systems that feed flat sheetmetal. Our experience with coil processing equipment has convinced us of the benefits of production using coil-fed machines, eliminating all preliminary processes. We are therefore working on expanding the range of our operating equipment and machine accessories to offer completely automated solutions using coil in the production of metal components.

ISMR: Please explain Produtech's strategic and technical objectives?

The goal is to reproduce the same conditions as those for sheet feeding with the material change to be processed, while maintaining all the benefits of coil. This development will make it advantageous in all respects to use Produtech systems that produce metal components by feeding directly from the coil, even with material thicknesses of up to 8mm.

Investments are also underway in new machine tools to strengthen our internal production capacity, a new organizational chart (underlining our need for highly skilled and specialised professionals) and the installation of powerful management software that enables manufacturers to organise and control the entire production process.

ISMR: Which trends and growth areas do you see developing in global coil-processing markets?

FM: Manufacturers in various production sectors (such as the 'white' household appliances sector; air conditioning; clean rooms; automotive; grain silos; metal furniture and shelving) have already expressed a strong interest in systems and machines that produce from coil. They see, in this production method, many opportunities relating to the flexibility offered by our coil-fed equipment which can carry out complex cutting and deformation processes automatically on one single machine, without reworking and without intermediate tooling.

The ability to complete 'just in time' orders responds to the needs of the market, which increasingly calls for rapid solutions to complex requirements. Technological innovations, the result of extensive research and design efforts, have increased our expertise and enabled us to compete in challenging, but certainly rewarding, markets such as in the USA, Germany and India etc. For these reasons, we believe that the market for machines that feed directly from coil is destined to expand and that there are excellent opportunities for the positive development of coil-processing equipment.

FM: Our ability to respond to customer needs with innovative and high-tech products has enabled us to extend our presence in many overseas markets. We decided to create new solutions aimed at mitigating any production challenges. Systems are being designed that make coil change easy, rapid and automated, with the automatic insertion of the coil head into the production machines. All this is done through a cycle managed by the CNC, to process low- and high-thickness materials and enable all processing via coil feeding. It encompasses the entire range of material thicknesses needed by the production sector, overcoming the difficulty of producing components from different materials that require complicated coil-change operations.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers? How are you addressing these issues for them?

FM: The requirements that our customers highlight to us are mainly linked to the challenge of responding quickly to the production needs of the market and, therefore, require strong flexibility. More and more “just in time" production is requested as well as the ability to produce different workpieces that make up individual orders without machine tooling, raw material waste or the need for heavy labour – all of which greatly affect production costs.

We offer coil-fed systems that work with different technologies combined on a single machine which enable, through nesting, the production of components distributed from coil with unlimited length, no head/tail waste and no need for continuous sheet-metal part loading. Laser-cutting systems and mechanical tools can be operated on the same machine so that different workpieces can be produced and unloaded automatically with no need for the continuous presence of operators and with limited raw material waste.

All this translates into speed of response to customer needs, production flexibility and strong economic savings. Our coil-processing systems, with various servo machines for the management of the sheet-metal roll and the unloading and palletising of the workpieces produced, enable the fully automated production of metal components. This means no downtime as well as cost-, space- and time savings. These systems meet customer needs for extreme flexibility in relation to high productivity performance, offering different power versions with maximum widths of the coil to be processed.

ISMR: What is your strategic focus/vision over the next few years?

FM: We aim to develop new versions of our machines and innovations. After developing the first coil-fed punching machines, covered by international patents (which use mechanical tools), we have now developed a new optical fibre, coil-fed laser system.

More recent is the design and development of a unique combination machine which combines the two technologies (fibre laser cutting and punching/deformation with mechanical tools) with direct coil feeding. It is our strategic objective to enhance this production method, obtain finished components with the required processes (starting from the coil and on a single machine) without reworking and intermediate processing. This offers important benefits in terms of space occupied; reduced production times; no need for maintenance or machine tooling; raw material and energy savings; reduction of manpower; better environmental impact and substantial cost savings.
