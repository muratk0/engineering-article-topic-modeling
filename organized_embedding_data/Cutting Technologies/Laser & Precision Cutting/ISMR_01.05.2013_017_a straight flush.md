# A STRAIGHT FLUSH

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

Water jets can enhance productivity, versatility and profitability for sheet metal subcontractors

ISMR SAYS:

“Water jet can leave an excellent crisp quality cut, often with a burr-free finish”

Water jet cutting technology is one of the fastest growing major machine tool processes in the world due to its versatility and ease of operation. Its main advantage over other methods is that it is a cold cutting technology and therefore used primarily in applications where conventional cutting, chipremoving or thermal processes fail to produce the desired cutting quality. In contrast to thermal cutting technologies, water jet cutting does not cause any thermal stress in the material. This effectively prevents material hardening and warping. In addition, no hazardous fumes, molten by-products or splashes are produced.

Demands on industrial cutting have risen dramatically over the last few years. Apart from high productivity and rapid cutting speeds, operators wish to cut complex shapes with precision and good cutting edge quality. Water jet cutting does not apply any tangential forces on the material. Although the kinetic energy of the water jet is very high, it does not cause changes in the structure of the material along the cut edge. This method enables precision cutting with smooth cut edges and no burrs.

As the cut edge quality is generally excellent, there is normally no need to rework the parts. The surface of the material remains undamaged. The very fine water jet produces an equally narrow cutting gap. This means that no material is wasted and the method can be further enhanced with narrowmesh nesting software, which in turn offers even more cost savings to operators.

## Why waterjet?

Water jet cutting is particularly advantageous when it comes to the production of complex cutting shapes. The cold cutting method enables the cutting of virtually any shape in any material. Manufacturers who need to constantly change materials and cutting contours can benefit from the flexibility offered by water jet cutting. Water jet cutting can be combined with other cutting technologies to produce sharp corners, bevelled edges and extremely small radii which are generally difficult to achieve with other methods.

As cuts can be started from any point along the contour, it is a highly versatile technology for all types of materials. Metals that can be cut using the water jet process include aluminium; brass; bronze; carbon; copper; stainless steel; nickel alloys and titanium.

However, a range of non-metals (such as carbon fibre, composites, laminates, glass and plastics) plus a variety of soft materials (like foamed rubber and plastics, elastomers and synthetic fibres) can also be cut using the technology.

The main benefits and advantages of cutting metals with waterjet include: no material deformation caused by heat; no hardening of the material along the cut edge; no changes to the metal structure; no heat-affected zones; no hazardous fumes and vapours and no or only minimal need for re-working. Water jet cutting can be used to cut thicker materials than can be processed with laser and offers greater precision than plasma cutting.

Set-up times are generally short as tangential forces are minimal: work pieces need normally not be clamped. High cutting speeds can be generated and there is no need to re-sharpen tools. From an environmental perspective, the method does not produce vapours or dust or require costly coolants. It is a suitable method for the production of prototypes and production processes that require high flexibility and is successfully used in large series production.

The main benefits of the water jet cutting process for manufacturers are chronicled here.

■ FLEXIBILITY AND VERSATILITY: Abrasive water jet can profile cut virtually any material and is a very versatile cutting technique. It is therefore used in a wide range of industry sectors.

■ COLD CUTTING PROCESS WITH NO HEAT DISTORTION: There is no heat input with water jet cutting. Aluminium, stainless steel and mild steel can be cut without warping or distortion of parts, and heat- affected zones associated with laser and plasma are eliminated. This is often a requirement in the aerospace and automotive industries. Cut edges are not hardened by water jet: you can tap holes and machine edges straight away without blunting or damaging your tooling.

■ CLEAN CUTTING: Water jet can leave an excellent crisp quality cut, often with a burr-free finish – unlike laser and plasma which can leave sharp burrs and slag.

■ PRODUCTIVITY BENEFITS: Production cutting software tightly nests parts on the parent material, which can pass on substantial material cost savings. Water jet cutting can be faster and more cost effective than machining, CNC routing and EDM: scrap material does not end up on the factory floor as swarf and can be re-used for other parts.

■ TIGHT TOLERANCES: Tight tolerances of less than +/-0.1mm can be achieved with water jet cutting. If you require a higher tolerance for your application, consider water jet for cutting away the bulk of the part and finish with a minimal amount of machining – this can be a real cost saver, especially with thicker materials. The water jet cut finish can be tailored to suit individual requirements and budgets. When high tolerances are achieved, secondary processes such as machining or drilling are often not required. »
