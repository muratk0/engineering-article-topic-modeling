# Traceability and process control

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

"ANYONE INTENT ON making industry 4.0 happen must keep an eye on the detail. The smart factory requires the products themselves to communicate next steps in the production process or their position in the supply chain and the most efficient method is laser marking. This is why we are predominantly focusing on this technology at the MACH 2018 exhibition in the UK from 9-13 April 2018." explained sheet metal specialist, TRUMPF.

TRUMPF marking systems come in different formats, sizes and power classes so that manufacturers can find the most suitable device for each marking task, regardless of component size or batch quantity and whether it is for traceability or process control.

"All stand-alone TRUMPF laser marking systems are simple and safe to use. Great emphasis is placed on ergonomic operation. They are certified to laser Class 1, just like a commercially available DVD player. These systems can therefore be used in the factory without any additional safety measures," added TRUMPF.

The marking system appearing on TRUMPF's stand 34 (Hall 19) at the MACH exhibition is a typical example. The TruMark Station 3000 is for users who need to ensure traceability in small and medium-sized production runs and who wish to integrate the marking process into their own manufacturing. Its large interior space accepts components of up to 440mm x 200mm x 350mm in size, weighing a maximum of 12kg. If necessary, the transfer flaps installed at the sides of the case can be removed, allowing the workstation to be integrated into an assembly line or enabling it to mark even larger components.

The TruMark Station can also be supplied with a number of options such as a rotary axis, additional focusing lenses, pilot laser and integrated exhaust system. This product is designed for a wide range of manufacturing industries from medical and electronics to automotive and general fabrication.

Also featured will be the new TRUMPF 18V brushless range of cordless fabrication tools. Shears, slitting shears, nibblers, profile nibblers, seam lockers and power fasteners are now joined by new models to extend the range. All incorporate a new battery-optimised gear head which, in combination with 18V Li-ion technology and brushless low-wear motors, provide up to 60% longer run-time after each full charge. Alongside the conventional 10.8V products, the new 18V models give fabricators an extended choice that allows, for example, the cordless cutting of sheets from 2mm to 2.5mm.
