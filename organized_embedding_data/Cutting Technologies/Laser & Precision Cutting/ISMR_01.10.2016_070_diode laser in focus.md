# Diode laser in focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

THIS YEAR'S EUROBLECH presentation from Laserline concentrates on diode laser applications in joining and surface treatment processes. Its main focus is on welding and brazing applications, as well as cladding and additive manufacturing. Meet the company in Hanover on stand C34 in hall 16.

Various samples will show the process-optimising use of diode lasers in complex welding applications such as tailored blank welding, multilayer narrow gap welding and thick plate welding with 50kW laser power. A video installation will demonstrate brazing with triple spot optics - an innovative processing technique, especially suitable for joining hot-dip galvanized steel sheets.

For surface treatment, Laserline will demonstrate, using a powder-coated shaft by the Fraunhofer-Institute for Material and Beam Technology (IWS), the advantages of cladding with diode lasers. With the help of specific components, the use of diode lasers will also be showcased for additive manufacturing – beginning with the integration of the laser beam source over a five-axis milling machine to the flexible interplay between laser-based powder coating and milling.

At EuroBLECH, Laserline will also introduce its diode laser beam sources which are particularly suitable for welding applications. Amongst others, the LDF 5000-30 (5kW), which is suitable for all common welding applications, and the compact 4kW industrial laser (the 19" plug in module LDM 4000-100) will be presented. Also on display will be the LDF 4000-4, a 4kW laser with a beam converter that was developed for applications requiring high beam quality (e.g. remote welding). Laserline will also show optics for welding and cladding, as well as the Laserline triple spot optic module for brazing applications. See www.laserline.de
