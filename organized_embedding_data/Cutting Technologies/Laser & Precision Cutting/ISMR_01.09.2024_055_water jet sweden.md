# Water Jet Sweden

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.waterjetsweden.com

Water Jet Sweden develops, manufactures and markets solutions for waterjet cutting and provides a complete aftermarket offering with service and spare parts. It celebrated its 30-year anniversary in 2023.

"We serve customers in more than 40 countries. Our broad range of products covers all kind of applications from basic 2D cutting to advanced free form shaping, from making tiny micro parts to large ship constructions," it told ISMR.

"Our service team is highly specialised and appreciated for fast, efficient support, online trouble-shooting and personal commitment. Our technical expertise is available on a 24/7 basis, and we always ship your spare parts on the very same day that you order them," it added.
