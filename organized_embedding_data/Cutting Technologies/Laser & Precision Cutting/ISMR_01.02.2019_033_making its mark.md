# Making its mark

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

FOBA LASER MARKING + ENGRAVING is attending two international trade shows in February to present its direct part marking solutions. At both Intec in Leipzig, Germany (5-8 February 2019) and MD&M West in Anaheim, USA (5-7 February 2019), a FOBA M2000 laser marking system will be showcased.

"Its trade show presence also includes recently introduced technology, FOBA Mosaic™, for fixture-less laser marking. FOBA Mosaic™ works with a camera that is integrated directly in the marking head.

"The vision-system creates a view of the entire marking field without optical distortion and enables automated laser mark alignment. Expensive product fixtures therefore become unnecessary, which means essential cost savings," FOBA told ISMR.

The FOBA M2000-P laser marking machine will be equipped with a 20-Watt fibre laser and MarkUS 2.12 integrated software, including FOBA Mosaic™. At Intec, FOBA will also present the FOBA Y.1000, a 100-Watt lasermarker for integration in high-throughput production lines that complies with high-speed manufacturing requirements.

FOBA is also hosting two lectures at Intec. On 7 February 2019, Dr. Faycal Benayad-Cherif will be speaking about the FOBA Mosaic™ marking solution (2:30p.m in English). On 8 February 2019, medical market expert, Christian Söhner, will discuss UDIcompliant laser marking of medical products (11:20a.m. in German).

Meet FOBA at Intec in Hall 2 on booth B59 and at MD&M West in Hall E on booth 360. See www.fobalaser.com
