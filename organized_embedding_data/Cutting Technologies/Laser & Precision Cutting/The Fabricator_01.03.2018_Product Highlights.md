Portable work light withstands tough conditions

Big Ass Light, a division of Big Ass Fans, has introduced the battery-powered LED Light Bar work light. It is designed to survive being doused and dropped, making it suitable for use on tough jobs.

The work light features six brightness settings, from 80 to 5,000 lumens; 270-degree light angle, which allows the light to illuminate entire rooms; a high-capacity, rechargeable battery that provides 1.5 hours of illumination at the brightest setting and 48 hours at the dimmest; and an octagonal shape that prevents the light from rolling away when set down or dropped.

The light carries an IP67 rating, which means it is sealed against dust, dirt, and liquid. It can be submerged in up to 3 ft. of water for 30 min. It also can be sprayed off with a hose or cleaning chemicals when it gets dirty.

Cutoff saw customizable for cutting superalloys

The model K20SW semiautomatic wet cutoff saw from Kalamazoo Industries Inc. can be customized to cut nearly any superalloy rapidly with a 20-in. abrasive wheel formulated for the alloy. The saw features an air-over-oil precise feed control with a 0 to 12-FPM feed rate, automatic end-of-cut head return, and manual stock feed.

Additional features include an air vise, cast-iron upper arm/trunnions, a 27- by 30-in. steel saw top, a heavy steel floor stand with tray-top for coolant collection, and a ⅓-HP coolant pump with 10-gal. reservoir and dual coolant hoses. A 15-HP TEFC, 220-/440-V, three-phase motor is standard, with higher-horsepower motors available as well as oscillation, enabling faster cutting without scorching.

Abrasive belt machine available with American flag design

The Dynafile abrasive belt machine from Dynabrade now is available with an American flag design on the housing.

The air tool grinds, deburrs, blends, and finishes usually inaccessible areas on metal and composites. It is suitable for light corner-weld removal and blending of welds.

The 20,000-RPM, 3-lb. tool uses 24-in.-long coated abrasive belts in widths of ½, ¼, and ⅛ in. The air motor is adjustable so the operator can set the most comfortable throttle lever position.

Vertical plate saw features optional NC measuring device

The new compact, heavy-duty LPS-T plate saw with vertical band saw blade is available from Behringer. It comes with a linear guided material table with a support surface that provides a cutting capacity of so in. long, 24.4 in. wide, and 24 in. high. T-slots in the table enable flexible positioning and clamping of the material.

A new feature is the optional NC measuring device, which helps simplify setup. The operator enters the required cutting depth, and the measuring device then enables automatic positioning. The material can be placed and aligned quickly.

Both the saw and the NC measuring device are controlled by an ergonomically designed BT65 touch control panel with a symbol-based menu system.

The saw can run either bimetal or carbide saw blades. It is driven with a 5.4-HP motor and runs over large, wearproof band wheels. All blade guidance parts are made of vibrationproof gray cast iron. Band saw changeover can be completed in about two minutes by a single operator without the aid of tools, the company states.
