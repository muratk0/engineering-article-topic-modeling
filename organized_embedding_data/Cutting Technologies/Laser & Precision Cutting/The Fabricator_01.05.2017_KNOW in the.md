Coil Processing Certificate Course

June 21-22, 2017 East Chicago, Ind.

Master the fundamentals of coil processing with an understanding of the machines, the processes, and tools of the trade

This two-day seminar will teach the theory of operation behind the slitting, leveling, shearing, and blanking processes. You will receive a comprehensive overview of the coil slitting and leveling process with an in-depth look at techniques and practices that will enhance your coil processing operations. FMA’s expert instructor will guide you through the fundamentals of coil processing using interactive instruction and videos of line operation to help you gain knowledge on the following concepts:

Related coil processing terminology

Function of common machines

Material properties

Slitting, leveling, shearing, and blanking processes

Designed for:

New and minimally skilled coil processing operators.

Experienced operators, engineers, designers, setup personnel, quality control personnel, and managers who want to improve their coil processing knowledge and skills.

Lasers 101 Certificate Course

June 5, 2017 Wixom, Mich.

Test your knowledge on laser basics, cutting, and welding

This one-day certificate course focuses on the basics of lasers. The curriculum will address how a laser works, the role of the resonator, what types of lasers are out there, an overview of what each type of laser can do, and the different applications that a laser can perform. Through easy-to-understand exercises, the course instructor will break down how the laser works, so you will get maximum productivity from your laser without sacrificing part quality.

The schedule includes classroom training and certificate testing at the end of the day.

Designed for:

All levels of laser operators and supervisors.

Owners, operators, managers, safety personnel, and anyone who wants to gain a better understanding of how a laser works.

$395 FMA Members $595 General

Record-Breaking Number of NBT Camps in 2017!

Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

, the foundation of FMA, announces an all-time high number of camps taking place this summer. From coast to coast 70 camps are scheduled at various locations throughout the U.S. Find a complete list of summer camps on the NBT website:

nutsandboltsfoundation.org/camps

.

NBT provides camp grants to community colleges, technical colleges, and high schools to host summer camps for students 12-16. Grants range in amounts from $1,000 to $2,500 for camps in their first, second, or third year of the camp cycle. After three years, a camp can become an affiliate camp or change the program and start the cycle over.

Students who participate in the NBT summer camps are introduced to the fascinating, high-tech career choices available to them in today’s automated manufacturing industry. Through local company tours, guest speakers, highly skilled educators, and access to specialized technical equipment, students are exposed to a variety of skills during the week at summer camp. In some cases, these are once-in-a-lifetime experiences that students may never get a chance to otherwise have.

The programs of NBT are successful because of generous donors and participation in the foundation events. Save the date for the next fundraising event in August! More information at
