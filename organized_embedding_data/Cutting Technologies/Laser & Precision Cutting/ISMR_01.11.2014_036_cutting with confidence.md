# CUTTING WITH CONFIDENCE

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Hypertherm hits the spot at EuroBLECH

Hypertherm’s booth at this year’s EuroBLECH showcased its broad portfolio of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting products and software, introducing a new 3D application short torch for HPRXD® systems. EuroBLECH was also the first international exhibition where Hypertherm demonstrated the capabilities of its HyPrecision waterjet. ISMR took a tour of the booth with its regional director, EMEA, Theo Cornielje.

“Our trade show booth at EuroBLECH is a good reflection of the direction that Hypertherm is taking strategically. Hypertherm is very well known in the cutting industry, especially for plasma cutting. Throughout the years, however, our focus has broadened from a component supplier to an industrial cutting solutions provider. So we are getting more involved in the performance of the systems rather than just designing and manufacturing them. The market is changing and end-users demands are much higher, so the performance of our systems is more important,” he told ISMR.

“We are therefore trying to get closer to the users of the product, in close cooperation with our OEMs who integrate our technology onto their systems, to keep them more informed and ensure that the performance of our systems truly reflects the quality of the products that they manufacture. We are doing lots of after-sales initiatives to get closer to the marketplace and guarantee performance.”

Hypertherm’s product portfolio has widened from just plasma technology into multi-technology i.e. a full range of plasma cutting, laser cutting and waterjet cutting (the most recent) technologies. In combination with its automation and software products, Hypertherm now offers fully integrated cutting solutions to customers.

“There are now many more cutting applications,” explained Theo Cornielje. “Traditionally, the cutting has mostly been on a flat bed. We are now in a better position to offer 3D, profile, pipe and construction steel cutting with our new lines. This capability is also reflected on our booth at EuroBLECH. We are moving deeper into cutting markets, beyond plasma and meta (waterjets can, of course, cut many other materials).”

This year, Hypertherm transitioned to become a 100% associate-owned company. This, said Cornielje, sent a very strong signal to the marketplace that Hypertherm is committed to its associates, its customers, its products and marketplaces. It is also, he says, looking long term… another theme for its presence at this year’s EuroBLECH.

## Cutting the cost of cutting

Cornnielje’s vision is for Hypertherm is to move forward in a strong and positive way in cutting.

“Software is a strong contributor to the machine’s quality – it also affects the cost. It is really the centre point of cutting operations. We acquired a company, MTC Software (now rebranded to CAM Solutions), three years ago to enable us to offer strong software solutions for end-users. One of the outcomes was our TrueHole technology, which has been a game-changer. The vision is to be a strong partner in the development of cutting solutions. While we are still 100% committed to the channel partner model, we are also working on getting closer to the end-users to ensure that our offering in terms of systems, services and training ties very closely to their needs. You could call it a push-pull strategy, where we inform the end-users of current standards and technologies and then work with our channel partners to ensure that they get it.

“Our vision is also a commitment to cutting the cost of cutting operations. It’s a very challenging period because new technologies require new training and new staff (we are actively hiring this year). Our commitment to expansion into after-sales means that, from cradle to cradle (the full lifecycle of the product), we want to ensure that the user gets the best possible support from us. That means developing new teams in Hypertherm, which we are building with product specialists and channel partners to optimise our offering. That is a big step forward for us.

“We have also brought out new software applications to optimise the speed of cutting (RapidParts technology) including a recent one for bevel applications (TrueBevel technology). The top end of our solutions that we are presenting at EuroBLECH is our new ProNest 2015 software for optimised bevel cutting.”

To date, this has been a very good year for Hypertherm in the EMEA region, he added, despite uncertainty in regions such as Russia. Machine uptime, he says, is as critical as ever. It also means a move for Hypertherm into proactive ‘service at the machine’, upgrading and retrofitting, for example.

“Flexibility and quality are our watchwords. We are still proud to be the market leader in plasma cutting,” concluded Cornielje.
