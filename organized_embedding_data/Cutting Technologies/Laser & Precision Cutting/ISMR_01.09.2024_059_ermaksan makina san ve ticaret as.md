# Ermaksan Makina San. VE Ticaret A.Ş.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand A52

www.ermaksan.com.tr/

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, offers products and solutions for sheet metal processing machines, additive manufacturing and optoelectronics with technology production experience of more than half a century.

"Our strong R&D and over 800 qualified staff produce high-quality machines in modern production facilities extending to a 100,000m² area. We continue to work on future oriented R&D such as fibre laser technologies; new machine models; Industry-4.0 applications and 3D printers. Machines produced by Ermaksan now operate in more than 120 countries," outlined the manufacturer.

Ermaksan's products include fibre laser and plasma cutters; press brakes; punch presses; controllers; 3D printers; laser resonators and shears.
