A major advantage of waterjet technology is its ability to cut virtually any material as well as fibre-reinforced materials, reflective materials, uneven surfaces and stacked layers of different materials. It does not superheat the area adjacent to the cut, so material integrity is left intact. No frayed edges or burrs are left after cutting, producing excellent material edge quality and largely eliminating the need for secondary finishing processes.

As an effective alternative to conventional machining operations, abrasive waterjet cutting provides an efficient way to process parts from virtually any material and a wide range of thicknesses, with no heat-affected zone Waterjet cutting is especially advantageous for cutting complex shapes. Materials can be cut into almost any shape - sharp corners, bevels, pierce holes and shapes with minimal inner radii are all possible. Stacking, nesting and tabbing optimises material and can significantly reduce cutting times.

## New waterjet innovations

Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, has introduced a twin-head ByJet Flex for waterjet cutting, which can accept up to two newly developed, 3D cutting heads for producing bevelled edges on components. Upgrading from 2D to 3D technology takes less than an hour, said the manufacturer.

"The ByJet Flex platform can be integrated into a wide variety of production environments and used for both 2D and 3D applications, operating with two CNC heads for straight or bevelled cutting.
