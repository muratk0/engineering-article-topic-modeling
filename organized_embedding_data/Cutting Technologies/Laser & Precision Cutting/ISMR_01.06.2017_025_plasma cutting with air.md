# Plasma cutting with air

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

THE COMPACT CUTTING inverters in Kjellberg's CutFire series are being used in a range of applications for cutting thin sheets. Kjellberg Finsterwalde is now offering a new and compact cutting inverter, CutFire 100i, to further increase cutting performance and reduce the cost per cutting metre for plasma cutting with air. Power sources built from 2017 onwards are now equipped with the new machine torch, Flash, and use air as well as swirl gas. Equipped with new consumables, cutting speeds have been increased, piercing safety has been enhanced and the lifetime of consumables has been improved.

"The air that rotates around the plasma beam stabilises the plasma beam and makes it possible for users to pierce up to a maximum of 20mm. Air is used as swirl gas, ensuring better protection of the torch and more effective cooling. Consumable lifetime can be increased many times over and the cutting speed can also be increased," the company told ISMR.

"CutFire 100i delivers reliable cut results for material thicknesses ranging between one and 30mm, at low investment costs. In addition to straight and profile cuts, the air-cooled inverter with the new Flash 101 torch can also be used for bevel cutting up to 30° and marking with 20A. For these purposes, no additional equipment or software is required. Adaption to CNC-controlled and other automatic guiding systems can be done quickly and easily. The costs per cutting metre are low due to its high cutting speeds and efficient use of air as well as plasma and swirl gas."

Kjellberg Finsterwalde will present the new plasma cutting inverter, CutFire100i, with its swirl gas torch (Flash 101) at ITM Poznań (Poland) in Hall 5 (stand 38). See www.kjellberg.de■
