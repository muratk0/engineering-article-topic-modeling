# Plasma cutting in focus

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

FOR THE CUTTING of its dished ends and sheets, Slawinski & Co. GmbH (a specialist in bottom and specially formed parts), uses a plasma cutting machine from MicroStep with an exceptional gantry width for cutting bevels and holes in plates and dished ends.

At the EuroBLECH 2014 trade fair, MicroStep presented an optimised version of its multifunctional MG plasma cutting production series for processing sheets, pipes, beams and dished ends. Its new ABP® (Additional Bevelling Process) patented technology, with which subsequent welding seam preparations can be made on already cut parts, was also presented for the first time at the exhibition. In cooperation with the Slawinski company, MicroStep developed an innovative system for the flexible and precise processing of sheets and dished ends in special dimensions.

A special dished end cutting table makes the processing of bottoms from 500mm up to 5,500mm diameter possible. A special version of the MicroStep R5 rotator, for example, enables bevel cutting for welding seam preparation up to 120°. Sheet processing is possible with the same gantry on a work surface of 14,000 x 6,000mm and it replaces the initially inserted plate-cutting machine. The integrated ABP® laser scanner will scan the entire surface of the dished end – the 3D scan is compared to the cutting plan and fluctuations in the raw material are automatically corrected.
