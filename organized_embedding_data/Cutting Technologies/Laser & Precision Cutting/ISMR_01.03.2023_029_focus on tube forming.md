# FOCUS ON TUBE FORMING

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

Hans Laser Smart Equipment Group Co,. Ltd offers a tube laser cutting machine (from 1-4kW) and various loading/unloading options.

Harco Metal Products, Inc. uses a variety of tube-forming processes to meet a range of applications, with over 14 different endforming machines to produce most shapes (from simple to extremely complex). It provides tubular fabrication processes to form almost any shape including: CNC rotary arm bending (mandrel-supported), ram or compression bending, roll bending and more.

Hypertherm's Rotary Tube Pro™ software is designed to make it "fast and easy for anyone to design and cut tube and pipe parts." Designed for mechanised rotary cutting, it supports stand-alone units and cutting table add-ons including plasma, laser, waterjet and oxyfuel machines.

The family-owned JustLaser GmbH, in Austria, specialises in laser systems for cutting and engraving various materials, especially metals (also pipes).

Established in 1987, Kayseri Metal Centre serves as a steel service centre in Gebze and a profile manufacturer at its Izmir facility (Meko Metal).

“We mainly focus on the greenhouse, automotive, solar, construction and HVAC industries. We have our own R&D department and long experience in profiling. Besides standard tubes and hollow sections, we specialise in special profiles for any kind of industry worldwide (starting from 0.50mm thickness up to 8.00mm, with a total of 26 rollforming lines)," it said.

Polish manufacturer, Kimla, offers a hybrid laser for tube and sheet cutting. Its Flashcut linear laser, with linear drives, can cut sheet and tube (6m or 12m). The system automatically unloads the tubes. The Kimla combination flat sheet-tube laser incorporates a tube laser cutting system which, says the company, ‘is totally smart in its extraction of the cut part'.

A special type of palette is used in the Kimla fibre laser, with an additional steering axis. It enables the cutting out of shapes and cutting of pipes with diameters up to 200mm. The maximum tube length depends on the laser model (3,000mm for the LF1530). The pipe to be cut rests freely during the cutting process, with no system of grips that block cutting at the pipe ends. This minimises waste.

Software specialist, Lantek, includes the Lantek Flex3d Tubes as a CAD/CAM software system for designing, nesting and cutting parts of tubes and pipes in its portfolio. The 2020 update also includes collision avoidance solutions in Lantek Expert and Lantek Flex3d modules “that can avoid up to 80 per cent of downtime."

Through its partnership with TRUMPF, a key industrial group in the machine-tool sector, it now has the necessary resources to pursue new investments.

At EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, LVD showcased its TL 8525 XXL Tube Laser (processing tube of up to 250mm).

The TL 8525 is a feature-rich tube laser cutting machine designed for high productivity. It handles tube lengths of up to 8200mm and diameters of up to 250mm (round or square). Optimised features include a bevel cutting head; automatic front loader; 2kW laser power and a large X-axis cutting head range of 1190mm for maximum material usage. Maximum tube length is 8200mm, with 20-250mm tube diameter range and 45-degree 3D tilt cutting head.

The TL 2665-FL tube laser is built to handle a range of tube and profile cutting. Automatic setup and run functions provide a high level of process reliability. A seven-position front loader allows for semi-automatic tube loading for high-volume production. It can cut round, square or rectangular tube and pipe, C-channel and angle iron. It is available in 3kW or 4kW fibre laser versions (7925mm maximum tube length, 165mm diameter). It features automated nesting; a seam detection system and CADMAN-T embedded advanced programming software.

Mazak launched its FG-400 NEO (a new fibre laser cutting system for tube) last year. It handles large sizes (up to 406mm diameter for round tube and 305 × 305mm for square tube). Beam-shaping technology allows users to modulate the shape of the laser beam based on the cutting conditions e.g. material type, material thickness, type of contour etc.

Mazak has increased the cutting and movement speeds and the machine has a new rotary-tool spindle unit. The laser cutter performs multiple processes including cutting, drilling, flow-drilling and tapping, all in the same machine.

The system is suitable for large-sized tubes and pipes, including round, square and rectangular cross-sections, as well as H, I and L beams. By combining a new 3D laser head with A-axis, B-axis and fibre laser technology, it can facilitate the stable machining of complex shapes and highly reflective materials, such as copper and brass. The 4-chucks system provides additional stability by preventing material distortion during processing. Mazak also offers a remote diagnostic service via iCONNECT for all its machines.

Other tube processing machines from Mazak include the FT-150 FIBER, a compact laser processing machine for tube, with the operator area, loading station and unloading station all on the same side. It is designed for high-speed cutting of small and medium tubes up to 152.4mm diameter in large batches. It is equipped with a 3kW fibre resonator and has an additional U-axis in the cutting head, which facilitates ultra-high-speed cutting, as well as a tilting laser head for bevel cutting. The FG-220 DDL laser cutter can process both thin and thick materials, as well as high reflectance material such as copper and brass. The machine is equipped with a 4kW DDL resonator.

MTC Bamieh Srl's main products are slitting and cut-to-length lines; welded pipe lines and pipe packaging machine; steel tape coil preparation systems; serrated bar production in coil or bar shape; profiling machines; stamping presses and other metal equipment. It can also supply pole and high mast production lines.

Memoli sas di Vergani Roberto & C offers the Memoli tube bender ETM series, designed to bend with or without mandrel tubes and profiles, and capable of bending in both right and left directions. It bends tubes with diameters from 8mm up to 150mm and can make curves of over 180° for a minimum bending radius of 2.5/3 x the tube diameter.

Messer Cutting Systems GmbH offers a range of sheet metal, pipe and tube cutting systems. Its pipe and tube cutting system processes square and round tube. It includes two tube and pipe support carts. There are multiple manual chuck options for rectangular tube, square and round tube and pipe.

The SicoMat® is designed for high productivity and robustness; characteristics which are required particularly in shipbuilding and wind tower manufacture. With bevel cutting heads for oxyfuel cutting and plasma cutting, a wide spectrum of marking processes and the option of a sandblasting or grinding head, the SicoMat® offers a high level of automation (even the weld edge geometry can be programmed and set automatically).

Metalix CAD/CAM Ltd. offers tube CNC software such as cncKad, AutoNest and MBend. cncKad and AutoNest include a job estimation module; support for 3D tube parts; smart common cuts for rectangular nesting; multiple cut sheets; suction force calculations for pickup and AutoCAD 2018 DXF support. Its MTube software provides new support for Tube Bevel, and enhanced support for Tube commands—tube assemblies (construction), connections, intersections, bend cuts etc.

MURGESI Srl specialises in turnkey manufacturing of production lines for polygonal and conical round steel poles for public lighting and electrical power lines and manufacturing lines for road manufactured products. Its new profile curving machine with rapid and high curving quality makes it suitable for pole and profile production.

MVD Inan is an experienced manufacturer of press brakes, laser machines, guillotine shears, plasma machines, turret punch presses and expanded metal presses. Its plasmacutting solutions offer tube cutting options. For example, its iPlasma cutting system can also be equipped with a five-axis bevelling head, multi-plasma heads and pipe-cutting functionality.

Nukon Lazer Makina Metal Sanayi Vetic. AS offers the REX 2D/3D Sheet Fibre Laser/ Tube Laser Combine Mac which cuts pipe and profile (3000 x 1500mm), with power ranging from 1-8kW. Its VENTO FLEX 2D/3D Sheet/ Tube laser combined machine combines pipe and profile with sheet-metal cutting.

Its NKT-160 Pipe and Profile Fibre Laser system is used to process pipes or profiles produced in different shapes and materials across various industry sectors. Available with automatic loading/unloading systems, it offers up to 4kW of power.
