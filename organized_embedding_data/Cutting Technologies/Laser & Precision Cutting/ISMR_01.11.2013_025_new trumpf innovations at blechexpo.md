# New TRUMPF innovations at Blechexpo

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

INTRODUCING two new world innovations in the TruLaser Series 5000 at the 2013 Blechexpo trade fair, TRUMPF will showcase a new CO2 and a solid-state laser machine for all-purpose 2D quality laser cutting. Both technologies can carry out all the standard assignments in 2D laser cutting. This includes cutting mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium of up to 25mm thick.

“This means that the solid-state laser has reached an application spectrum that had previously been confined to the CO2 laser.

The CO2 laser is more energy-efficient and, in this respect, is catching up with the solid-state laser,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

The new TruLaser 5030 fiber, a 2D laser cutting machine with a 5-kilowatt solid-state laser, uses the BrightLine fiber function to cut stainless steel as thick as 25mm. The new TruLaser 5030 has a particularly energy-efficient CO2 laser, TRUMPF told ISMR.

At Blechexpo, TRUMPF will also be introducing various intelligent functions and software-based solutions for its machine tools. LensLine with condition checking determines the status of the lens in a laser machine. DetectLine ensures automatic adjustment of the focal position. Appearing for the first time at Blechexpo will be the TCB (thickness-controlled bending) function for the press brakes in TRUMPF’s TruBend Series 5000. This and the BendGuard option, together with the optical setup and positioning aid, form the backbone of TRUMPF’s smart bending package.

The new TruMatic 6000 punching and laser cutting machine will also be presented to the public at Blechexpo. It has numerous smart functions in its range of features including smart loading and unloading functions. TRUMPF’s new TruLaser Station 5005, which will make its public debut at Blechexpo 2013, is designed for laser welding. At Blechexpo, the TruLaser Station 5005 will demonstrate its welding capabilities in a process for the sheet metal manufacture of a coffee capsule holder.
