# PRECISION ENGINEERING

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

ISMR: WHICH PROCESS INNOVATIONS HAVE HELPED YOU THE MOST?

HJ The investment in the TruMatic 3000 with a SheetMaster. We could not do laser cutting before that purchase, and the machine is now fully automated. But this kind of process innovation also shows the other side of the situation. Jobs requiring low skills levels are those that fall by the wayside. The situation is aggravated when employees are unable or unwilling to extend their skills.

The next steps are already in the offing. In Europe, we will have no choice other than to consistently implement automation. This is a task that falls first to machinery manufacturers. There is a real need for everything that can be automated while achieving high functional capacities and good process reliability.

ISMR: DO YOU THINK THE MANUFACTURERS ARE ON THE RIGHT PATH?

HJ In fulfilling his own specific role, the manufacturer determines which potentials are available. The target is to reduce personnel costs for the entire manufacturing process. Automation should not simply expand to occupy unused floor area and steal space unnecessarily. Nor should changes be made simply for their own sake. Investments must always be seen in the context of the whole. Because of that, consulting services provided by the sales staff are in greater demand than ever before.
