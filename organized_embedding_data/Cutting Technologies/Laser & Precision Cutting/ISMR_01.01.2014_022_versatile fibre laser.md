# Versatile fibre laser

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

THE FIBRE laser’s range of applications is rapidly expanding. Until recently, cutting systems with fibre lasers were used primarily in the thin sheet metal range because, for sheet thicknesses above 8mm, the advantages of CO2 laser systems prevailed. Now this is changing. Bystronic is offering various solutions for its BySprint Fiber which significantly improve the cutting characteristics and overall efficiency of the fibre laser right through to medium sheet thicknesses.

The Cut Control function is now available for the BySprint Fiber with at least 3 kilowatts. Cut Control improves the monitoring of the cutting process and enables precise edge detection. This function is a long-established technology used in CO2 laser cutting systems from Bystronic. Thanks to integrated process monitoring, the machine automatically stops when a cutting tear occurs and returns to where the tear started. Subsequently, the cut is repeated. This reduces the cut part rejection rate. Edge detection enables the position of a loaded metal sheet to be precisely determined. This results in an optimal degree of utilisation, particularly with long and straight cuts that run close to the edge of the metal sheet.

“The automatic nozzle changer is another new function of the BySprint Fiber. The nozzle change takes only 15 seconds. This is faster than a manual change and increases process reliability. With the magazine inventory, the BySprint Fiber can be pre-equipped for cutting orders with varying sheet metals,” added Bystronic.

In addition to the standard 3015 format, Bystronic also offers the BySprint Fiber in the 4020 large format with a working area of 4 x 2 metres because, on large metal sheets, more cut parts can be placed and nested even more efficiently. In addition, with the increased volume of parts per metal sheet, the period during which the machine is able to cut without interruptions is increased so that non-productive time decreases and part output increases.

“We now offer the BySprint Fiber with a 2-, 3- and 4-kilowatt laser source. In the most powerful 4-kilowatt version, the BySprint Fiber achieves almost comparable cutting speeds to a CO2 laser, right into medium sheet thicknesses. If you take into account the energy efficiency and the lower maintenance costs of a fibre laser, the BySprint Fiber with 4 kilowatts is more economical than a CO2 laser system in the thin and medium range of sheet thicknesses,” explained Bystronic.

“With an energy efficiency of approximately 30 per cent, the fibre laser is considerably more efficient than a CO2 laser. Overall, this results in lower energy consumption. For many users, this is the main advantage of the fibre laser. The maintenance costs are also lower in comparison to CO2 laser cutting systems. The BySprint Fiber’s resonator is virtually maintenance-free and does not require any laser gas at all. Elaborate and high-maintenance deflection optics in the beam path are eliminated in the BySprint Fiber because now the laser beam is guided to the cutting head via fibre optics. Users benefit from long operating times and long maintenance intervals.”

Shear Genius

DURING THE Steel Fab Exhibition, Prima Power will invite customers and visitors to its Open House event arranged at its Sharjah premises from 20-23 January 2014. In operation will be Prima Power’s punching – shearing cell range, Shear Genius® SGe6, for max. sheet sizes of 3000mm x 1500mm.

“As a company, we have been present in the Middle East for several years,” said general manager, Michael Shahidiar, “and everything indicates that the integrated punching – right angle shearing concept championed by Prima Power has a great future there too.”

Prima Power has a history of 26 years with this technology, introducing the first product in 1987. Development over the years has been continuous and today’s solutions offer high productivity levels.

“The fully servo-electric Shear Genius® features rapid and reliable punching and shearing, high component quality, edges without burrs, savings in material and reliable automatic handling of sheared parts. Extra work due to micro joints and sheet skeletons is eliminated. Power supply requirement as well as energy consumption and maintenance costs are remarkably low,” the company told ISMR.

Servo-electric technology enables accurate forming, roll forming, tapping etc. Low noise level is a further benefit. The 84dB level of earlier hydraulic models has been brought down by 10dB.

“Savings in energy and maintenance are paralleled by those achieved in raw material consumption – meaning extremely competitive costs per component. With skeleton-free processing, the Shear Genius® needs 10-15 per cent less material for the same production level of a stand-alone machine. 100 per cent sheet utilisation can be achieved when shearing rectangular components,” added Prima Power.

The integrated Shear Genius® is not just an automatic, flexible manufacturing cell (which can be equipped for long periods of unmanned production and upgraded from a wide range of Prima Power material handling modules) but also a flexible and versatile punching solution.

“When the job at hand requires only punching and forming etc., it can be used just as a high-performance turret punch press,” Michael Shahidiar explained. “It has often been chosen as the heart of factory-wide FMS solutions, designed for 24/7 and lights-out production.” ■

## SELF-CLINCHING CAPTIVE PANEL SCREWS

PEM® TYPE PFC4™ self-clinching captive panel screws now feature fastener solutions engineered to install permanently and reliably into stainless steel assemblies. They can be specified for use in stainless sheets as thin as 0.060”/1.53mm with hardness of up to HRB 88 on the Rockwell “B” scale. Their integrated captive screw in a spring-loaded assembly reduces loose hardware and minimises parts count; fully recessed heads in fastened position allow tool-only access and the fasteners comply with UL 60950 access standards.

“Designers can also specify MAThread® anti cross-thread technology as a safeguard to correct potential off-angle thread mating and promote proper alignment during installation. A Torx® recess can be supplied in place of the standard Phillips drive. The fasteners are manufactured from 400 Series stainless steel offering corrosion resistance comparable to zinc-plated steel. They are available in assorted screw lengths and in thread sizes #4-40 up to #10-32 and M3 through M5. All Type PFC4 captive panel screws are RoHS-compliant,” explained Penn Engineering.

Installation (using a PEMSERTER® or other standard press) is completed by inserting the fastener into a punched or drilled hole, placing the fastener into the hole of the anvil and positioning the mounting hole over the fastener’s shank. Sufficient squeezing force is then applied until the shoulder of the retainer comes into contact with the sheet material. The fastener ultimately will be flush on the side of the sheet opposite installation to enhance cosmetics.
