# ASIAN FIBRE LASER

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

The first 15kW ByStar Fiber 8025 fibre laser cutting system in Asia was installed recently at Leon Fuat Metal Sdn. Bhd in Malaysia.

ISMR SAYS:

“The new machine, with higher cutting power, will increase the manufacturer's production output and enable it to offer shorter delivery lead times.

A large-format, high-powered laser cutting machine was installed recently in Malaysia to Leon Fuat Metal Sdn. Bhd., earning it the title of first 15kW ByStar Fiber 8025 in Asia.

"As a steadfast brand advocate for Bystronic, Leon Fuat's new investment will empower it to become a major player in the industry," Bystronic told ISMR.

Chinhan Ooi, general manager of Bystronic Malaysia and south-east Asia sales director, confirmed: "This milestone not only signifies an important, strategic starting point in the APAC region, but also marks huge potential in Malaysia for sales of Bystronic high-powered solutions. We are especially proud to announce that Leon Fuat Group, who previously owned mainly CO₂ laser cutting systems from another manufacturer, has selected Bystronic for its business transformation."

## Long experience in the industry

With more than 38 years of experience serving various industries, Leon Fuat specialises in the trade and processing of steel products, with a diverse portfolio comprising various steel materials (including carbon steel, stainless steel and alloy steel). It also trades in special steel materials such as tool steel and non-ferrous metal products such as bronze, brass, aluminium and copper.

The company is also involved in the processing of steel products, which complements its trading operations. It has a wide portfolio of steel products which include flat products such as coil; plate; sheet; welded tubes and pipes; welded rectangular and square sections as well as long products such as bars; rods; shafts; sections; angles and channels plus seamless tubes and pipes.

Leon Fuat provides various value-added services in the form of metal cutting; levelling; shearing; profiling; bending and finishing, as well as the production of expanded metal to meet the requirements of its customers.

As part of its long-term business enhancement strategy, investments were made in the downstream production plant of welded steel pipe manufacturing in Port Klang, Selangor (Malaysia). This enabled Leon Fuat to cater to demands from existing customers and the mass market.

Leon Fuat Metal Sdn. Bhd. is part of the Leon Fuat Bhd Group. The Group has a wide customer base of over 3,000 built up over decades. Key customers include those from the manufacturing sector such as producers of metal products and components to fabricators of machinery, equipment and metal structures. The Group's other customers include those from the construction industry's infrastructure and building segments, as well as hardware wholesalers and retailers. Besides Malaysia, the Group's customers are from Thailand; Singapore; Vietnam; Philippines and Indonesia.

## An eye on fibre

Calvin Ooi, executive director of Leon Fuat, shared his purchasing roadmap: "Before this new machine, we had installed one 10kW ByStar Fiber 4020 machine in the factory. We were already impressed by the cutting speed, which is significantly faster than the CO₂ laser cutting machine. The new machine, with higher cutting power, will increase our production output and [enable us to] offer shorter delivery lead times," he commented.

"In its early days, Leon Fuat Group owned eleven CO₂ machines from another manufacturer. In 2018, with the upgrade to its first Bystronic ByStar Fiber 4020, previous production constraints and high operating costs were eliminated. Even with just five CO₂ laser cutting machines left in operation, it was able to increase productivity and revenue with our fibre laser machine," added Bystronic.

The Leon Fuat Group has also invested in another Bystronic machine; a 6kW ByStar Fiber 3015 laser cutting system. However, this machine was installed at its sister company, Supreme Steel Makers Sdn. Bhd, to process applications for thinner sheet metal materials.

## Looking ahead

Looking into the future, Leon Fuat believes that the ByStar Fiber 8025 will offset the limitations of its current laser cutting machines. For example, the company can now load larger-sized raw materials to produce bigger parts. Bystronic's ByVision Cutting intuitive and easy-to-operate software also enables it to integrate the solution into any future business endeavour.

Emphasizing that speed is of the essence, Calvin Ooi admits that with this new set-up, the company will be able to produce more and deliver faster, giving it greater competitive advantage in a challenging business environment. In addition, he said, the new laser cutting system will help to lower overall operational costs, including electricity and gas consumption, which is vital to the success of the business.■

## Environmental Stewardship

“As a steel processing and trading company, we understand that our operations have an impact on the environment. As part of our commitment towards sustainability, we regularly monitor and are transparent about our waste generation, energy and water consumption, and noise pollution. We have invested in solutions to optimise and manage the use of these resources, therefore minimising adverse environmental impacts," commented Leon Fuat Bhd in its 2020 Annual Report.

The Group strives to minimise greenhouse gas emissions by maximising fuel efficiency and using green diesel for delivery trucks. Scheduled and non-scheduled waste generation is recorded at LF Metal. It also monitors and records the effluent discharged to ensure it is below the permissible limits set by the Environmental Quality Act 1974 (EQA 1974).

In tandem with legislative requirements, it has also organised a ‘Noise Monitoring and Initiative Programme for 2020' at LF Metal to educate its workforce on adopting safe work practices.

The Leon Fuat Group monitors electricity and fuel consumption, and began its reporting initiatives of both fuel and electricity in FY2017.
