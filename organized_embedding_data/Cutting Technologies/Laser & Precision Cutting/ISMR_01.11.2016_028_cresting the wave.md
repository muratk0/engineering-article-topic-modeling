## We highlight a selection of new and innovative waterjet technologies for sheet metal manufacturers

A pump can pump up to 3 litres of water per minute through a 0.28mm water nozzle. This creates a fine jet with extremely high energy density for cutting all kinds of materials.

Pure water cutting is extremely precise: the water jet can be finer than 0.1mm and enables the cutting of fine contours, sharp angles and tight radii. A waterjet cutting machine also cuts hard materials such as metal, stone, glass or ceramics. For this, the waterjet must be mixed with a cutting medium (abrasive) - for example, garnet sand. This procedure uses the abrasive jet as the separating device. The waterjet speeds up the abrasive particles. Waterjet/abrasivejet cutting routinely cuts precision parts with tolerances as close as ± 0.005" and better, when required. Many job shops add waterjets to their operations alongside other cutting technologies such as EDM, laser, milling and plasma.

A major advantage of waterjet technology is its ability to cut virtually any material as well as fibre-reinforced materials, reflective materials, uneven surfaces and stacked layers of different materials. It does not superheat the area adjacent to the cut, so material integrity is left intact. No frayed edges or burrs are left after cutting, producing excellent material edge quality and largely eliminating the need for secondary finishing processes. Waterjet is considered a "green" technology - it produces no hazardous waste, reducing waste disposal costs. Large pieces of reusable scrap material can be cut which might have been lost using traditional cutting methods. Parts can also be closely nested to maximize material use, and the waterjet saves material by creating very little kerf.

Waterjet cutting is especially advantageous for cutting complex shapes. Materials can be cut into almost any shape. Sharp corners, bevels, pierce holes and shapes with minimal inner radii are all possible. Stacking, nesting and tabbing optimises material and can significantly reduce cutting times. ## New waterjet innovations

Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, has introduced a twin-head ByJet Flex for waterjet cutting, which has the ability to accept up to two newly developed, 3D cutting heads for producing bevelled edges on components. Upgrading from 2D to 3D technology takes less than an hour, said the manufacturer.

"The ByJet Flex platform can be integrated into a wide variety of production environments and used for both 2D and 3D applications, operating with two CNC heads for straight or bevelled cutting. The basic version is equipped with all prerequisites that allow it to be upgraded with functions such as 3D cutting heads, drill spindles, automatic parts holding during cutting (FixMaster), precise positioning of the cutting head (PositionPointer) and intelligent fine adjustment for rapid, multi-head cutting (PrecisionAligner)," Bystronic told ISMR.

Eckert's COMBO waterjet cutting machine uses two modern cut technologies - plasma and water. Compass automatically makes real-time cutting head height adjustments to allow precision cutting of non-flat or stress-relieving material. The collision sensor protects the machine from damage and downtime.
