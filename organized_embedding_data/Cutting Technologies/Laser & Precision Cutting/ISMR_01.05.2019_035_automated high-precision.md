# AUTOMATED HIGH-PRECISION

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

This article highlights some of the historic developments in highprecision plasma cutting and discusses some of the essentials for maximum productivity today.

By Dirk Ott, VP — Global Plasma Automation, Thermal Dynamics

Fabricators can have it good, fast or cheap – pick two. High-precision plasma cutting used to be that way, too, but the options were cut speed, quality and parts life.

Today, fabricators enjoy the best of all worlds. On 6mm steel, top cutting speeds reach 100 ipm. A high-precision system can achieve a cut accuracy of ±0.25 to 1.2mm and kerf widths as narrow as 1.2mm. Consumables can last from 1,300 to more
