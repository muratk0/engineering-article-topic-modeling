## New manager for lasers and materials processing

WITH EFFECT from 1 January 2014, Dr. Mario Ledig is now head of the Lasers business unit within Jenoptik’s Lasers & Material Processing division. In this position, he is responsible for the entire business unit on a global level. He was also appointed as managing director holding operational responsibility for JENOPTIK Laser GmbH – one of the two companies within this business unit.

Dr. Ledig earned his doctorate in laser physics at the Friedrich Schiller University in Jena and has more than 20 years of professional experience in optics/photonics. He has held various executive positions at Qioptiq all over the world.

Jenoptik is one of the few suppliers worldwide to mass-produce the entire technology chain in laser sources – from semiconductor material and diode lasers up to solid-state lasers. In the Laser Processing Systems business unit, Jenoptik develops and manufactures laser machines which are integrated into its customers’ production lines as a part of their process optimisation and automation. They are used for the processing of plastics, metals and glass in connection with the processing of thin films. ■

## JCB bids farewell to Blake

JCB staff bid a fond farewell to JCB chief executive officer, Alan Blake, who retired from the company after almost a quarter of a century of service at the end of last year. Alan Blake became JCB CEO on 1 January 2010 – only the fifth person to hold the position. Dozens of friends and colleagues gathered at JCB World HQ recently to say goodbye at a special presentation.

The new chief executive officer, Graeme Macdonald, who took over as CEO on 1 January 2014, said: “Alan has contributed a huge amount to the growth and success of JCB over many years. The expansion projects that Alan led enabled JCB to double its production from 36,000 to 72,000 machines over a five-year period.

## New Board Member for Alcoa

ALCOA’S BOARD of Directors has elected Carol L. Roberts as a director of the company and member of the Audit Committee of the Board. She holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Mechanical Engineering from Yale University.

Ms. Roberts, 54, is senior vice president and chief financial

officer of International Paper Company (IP), a global leader in packaging and paper with manufacturing operations in 24 countries and 2012 net sales of US$ 28 billion. Her career spans engineering, manufacturing, business management, human resources and finance, bringing a strong set of cross-functional experiences to the Alcoa Board.

Alcoa is a global specialist in lightweight metals, products and solutions. It pioneered the modern-day aluminium industry 125 years ago and today delivers value-add products from a range of lightweight metals and flat-rolled aluminium. Alcoa is also a leading producer of primary aluminium, as well as the world’s largest miner of bauxite and refiner of alumina. See www.alcoa.com. ■

On behalf of the 11,000 JCB employees around the world, our dealers and customers, we wish him a very long and happy retirement.” ■

## Stamp of approval at Stadco

Stadco has announced the appointment of Paul Wickes to the position of UK operations director. Over the last year, he has held responsibility for the Powys facility, overseeing significant improvement in operational and financial performance. In his new role, Paul has full responsibility for all operational aspects of the company’s four UK business units, as well as purchasing, materials, planning and logistics.

Stadco Automotive Ltd. is an international Tier 1 supplier of automotive Body-in-White products and services. Its core capabilities are aluminium and steel stampings, assemblies and facility design. The company has gone through a period of extensive development and growth, recently announcing a multi-million pound investment in the latest stamping technology at its new facility in Telford. The two 2000-tonne transfer presses, due to be installed in 2014, will be amongst the largest in Europe. ■

## MD appointed for Schuler’s Systems division

THE SUPERVISORY BOARD of Schuler Pressen GmbH has appointed a new managing director and head of the Systems Division: Johannes Linden, currently still managing director of metal packaging specialist, Mall + Herlan. The company is one of the leading manufacturers of machines for producing monobloc metal packaging products. The 44-year-old engineering graduate will join press and stamping specialist, Schuler, on 1 March 2014.

“We are delighted to have gained such an acclaimed expert in metal packaging as Johannes Linden,” stated Schuler CEO, Stefan Klebert. “His expertise will help strengthen our position in the field of packaging – a key segment for us with considerable potential.”

The demand for metal packaging continues to grow around the world. Schuler offers a number of presses which can be used, for example, to produce aerosol and beverage cans. ■
