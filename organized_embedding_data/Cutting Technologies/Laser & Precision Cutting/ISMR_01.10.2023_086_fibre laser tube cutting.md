# Fibre laser tube cutting

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

BLM GRoUP opened the doors recently to its facilities in Levico Terme (Trento, Italy) to offer its customers the chance to get up close to its technology during 'Innova 2023'. This year, attendees gained firsthand access to its new Lasertube LT12: a fibre laser cutting system for tubes and metal profiles that can cut tubes from 25-305mm in diameter.

"LT12 can be equipped with different laser powers and is capable of processing tubes of up to 62kg/m. The structure is configured with a mandrel at the tail and a steady rest that supports the tube being cut. One of the innovations of the Lasertube system is 'interpenetration' where the mandrel enters the steady rest to cut the end of the bar for minimal waste," explained BLM GROUP.

"The tube is supported throughout the processing line for rapid laser cutting on both small-diameter tubes and long, heavy tubes, while maintaining speed and precision over a range of diameters. This is a flexible system that caters to multiple sectors but is of particular interest to those industries where it is common to find open and closed profiles that can be up to 305mm in diameter," it continued.

The new Lasertube system is modular in both loading and unloading lengths. Two sizes for loading (8.5m and 12.5m) and several sizes for unloading are available. The option of loading bar lengths of up to 12.5m was made to elevate processing through nesting, which arranges each cut on the bar to make the most of the material while minimising unused bar sections.

'Drill & tap' is an additional function that allows drilling and tapping with the additional option of employing FlowDrill technology directly in the Lasertube system. The machining unit is equipped with an 8- or 16-position tool magazine that allows the wear rate of each tool to be monitored. For the first time on a Lasertube from ADIGE-SYS, Active Scan has been introduced. This technology (developed by BLM GROUP) makes it possible to autocorrect the positioning of geometries on the tubes to be cut.
