# Boschert GmbH & Co. KG

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

https://boschert.de/en/

Boschert GmbH & Co KG was founded in 1946 as a small workshop for the textile industry. Today, it builds notching; punching; bending; plasma; laser and copper processing machines. It is currently celebrating its 75th anniversary in the industry.

Two of its latest innovations will be on display at EuroBLECH in Hanover: the Boschert CombiLaser generation and the Boschert Fiber Laser generation. 'Compatibility & Connectivity' with Industry 4.0 protocols will also be highlighted on its booth.

Fifteen years ago, Boschert established a joint venture company in a new factory in Greece with Gizelis SA. Together, both companies offered a new production range of pressbrakes and shears to the market. See separate GIZELIS entry in this hall section.
