# Lasers crucial for medical technology

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

Sheet-metal specialist TRUMPF has become an important partner for medical technology manufacturers.

“Innovative medical devices cannot be manufactured without laser technology. With our lasers, medical technology manufacturers can continue to improve their products. This enables even gentler treatment options for patients," said Bernd Block, Medical Technology Industry Manager, TRUMPF. The high-tech company will showcase various laser applications for medical technology at its Medical Photonics in-house trade show.

“Our laser systems enable manufacturers of medical devices and medical components to process even the smallest of components precisely, flexibly and with consistently high quality. They also enable the use of advanced and biocompatible materials. Medical device manufacturers implement a whole range of applications with technology from TRUMPF such as the welding of pacemakers, cutting of stents and cannulas, marking of reusable surgical instruments or 3D printing of artificial joints and dental implants," it told ISMR.

The sheet-metal specialist not only supplies lasers to the medical technology industry, but also provides ongoing support, advising medical technology manufacturers on every phase of product development, from concept to series production.

“Medical technology manufacturers can test their applications in TRUMPF's laser application centres, for example, and do not have to build their own pilot plants for this purpose," said Block. He went on to add that mature manufacturing processes were important. The medical and healthcare industry places high demands on products. For example, medical products must be particularly robust and functional, while complying with numerous material specifications and legal requirements and standards.

A major trend in the medical technology industry is the automation of in-house manufacturing. “This is particularly evident in the production of high-risk products such as pacemakers. Medical technology manufacturers need standard machines that can be automated, such as those offered by us," explained Block.

For example, medical technology manufacturers can very easily combine the company's laser systems with cobots in their production. These robots, which can work with humans without a protective enclosure, load and unload the laser systems with components. Monotonous and physically strenuous movements are therefore eliminated for the machine operators.

## Chemical etching for components

Advanced Chemical Etching Ltd (ACE), in the UK, uses innovative chemical etching processes to manufacture precision metal components at volume. It supplies the aerospace, automotive, electronics and communications sectors with precision metal etching and 'chemi' etching. It also manufactures precision metal components from chemically etched; pressed; wire eroded; laser- and waterjet-cut blanks with varying complexity, thickness, form and finish.

The manufacturer exhibited at the recent Southern Manufacturing & Controls exhibition in Farnborough, UK. It confirmed rapid increases in volume for the automotive and aerospace sectors, as well as new opportunities for its titanium, copper and aluminium etching services in the race for electrification and hydrogen fuel cell systems. ACE's expansion continued last year with a £500K investment into two new etching machines.

The company specialises in the development of prototype components, pre-production and volume fulfilment to customers in aerospace; space; precision engineering; automotive; electronics; medical, telecoms and renewables sectors. Its scope of activities stretches from safety-critical components for aircraft and F1 cars to meshes; electric connectors; battery interconnectors; fuel cell bipolar plates and heat exchangers.

## Scrap metal bins

“Scrap metal is an asset for your business, so containment facilities and collection should be at the heart of your working processes, especially in manufacturing," Alchemy Metals told ISMR.

To ensure that collection and containment runs as efficiently and seamlessly as possible, it designs and manufactures its own scrap metal bins.

"We have a specialist team whose job is to manufacture and maintain our scrap metal bins, stillages and sealed stillages. It ensures that they are clean and regularly spray painted. Our fleet will deliver and collect different sizes and types of scrap metal bin. Full containers are exchanged with replacements. Larger scrap metal bins and skips have forklift guides to ensure safe, secure movement around a site. Many are stackable, to help keep their footprint to a minimum," added the manufacturer.

Security is an important consideration, because of the threat of metal theft. Alchemy Metals' scrap metal bins are lidded, lockable and SmartWater-labelled to help deter theft. The company can also provide security tags to allow users to 'lock' the container to ensure secure transportation. Each security tag has its own special number to allow it to be tracked from door to door.

"Sealed scrap metal bins are available for the effective containment and transport of fluids and solutions. They are regularly leak tested to guarantee performance. In addition, our stillages are fitted with drainage taps to allow for the effective recycling of coolants," concluded Alchemy Metals.
