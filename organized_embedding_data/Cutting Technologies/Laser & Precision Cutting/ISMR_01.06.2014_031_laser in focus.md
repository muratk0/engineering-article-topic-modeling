# Laser in focus

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

ROFIN offers a comprehensive portfolio of laser sources, systems and complete solutions for the entire range of industrial laser material processing applications. With a servo-controlled axis system, completely re-engineered CNC system and now also with fiber laser, the new Select Fiber manual welding laser from ROFIN is celebrating its première at this year’s LASYS.

MPS, its modular laser work-station, for high-precision welding, cutting, drilling or structuring applications, is now available in three additional product versions: MPS Compact, MPS Rotary and MPS Advanced for high-precision µm-exact machining, with a working envelope of 1000 x 600mm.

ROFIN is also introducing an extended variant of its CombiLine Basic marking solution with practical rotary table at the LASYS tradeshow as well as a full-scale CAD extension of its EasyMark desktop laser marker. From its broad fiber laser portfolio, it now offers three laser sources from different power classes for a wide range of applications: the new long pulse fiber laser LFS 300, the new FL 015 C fiber laser and the FL 060 fiber laser.
