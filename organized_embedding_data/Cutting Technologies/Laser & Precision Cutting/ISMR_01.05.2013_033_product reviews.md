LIMO from Dortmund has developed a beam shaping module for its L³ LIMO line laser system which significantly accelerates annealing processes. Nine fiber-coupled laser modules produce a homogeneous, very intense line.

"It must be a linear source which scans over the surface and enables uniform and continuous processing of the irradiated areas," said Dirk Hauschild, Director, Thin-Film Activation at LIMO. L³ LIMO line laser systems have been developed as an alternative to typical punctual working lasers i.e. nine fiber-coupled laser modules. In combination with a LIMO beam shaping module, they produce a homogeneous line (80mm x 0.2mm, inhomogeneity <2.5 per cent) with very high intensity (10kW/cm²). The laser beam can directly irradiate the object or be displayed onto the target plane via a projection lens.

The system is suitable for operations such as: short-term tempering (modifying surfaces and coatings through rapid heating); selective tempering (selective heating of certain surface materials); rapid thermal annealing (annealing of crystal defects within milliseconds) and rapid thermal processing (producing a new material or new material characteristics from two deposited materials) etc.

## Advanced waterjet technology

OMAX CORPORATION, in conjunction with its distribution partner Henko Machine Tools, demonstrated the versatility of the MAXIEM 1515 JetCutting Centre at the MTA exhibition in April at the Singapore Expo. MTA introduced high precision engineering solutions to Singapore’s rapidly growing medical technology industry.

The MAXIEM 1515 features complex cutting capabilities in a compact design making it suitable for fabrication shops serving the medical industry. With a table size of 2.3 metres x 1.8 metres and a maximum table load of 600kg/square metre, the abrasive waterjet cuts a wide variety of materials without creating heat-affected zones or mechanical stresses. By cutting parts directly from the plate, the MAXIEM 1515 also dramatically reduces set-up time and material usage.

Engineered for efficiency, the machine’s direct-drive pump delivers more than 90 per cent of the electrical input power to the cutting nozzle, allowing for high cutting speeds. It also incorporates the company’s intuitive Intelli-MAX® Software Suite that provides higher cutting speeds and greater precision. The Intelli-MAX Software, which runs on the Windows® operating system, precisely calculates the velocity of a tool path at over 2,000 points per inch for complete control over the motion of an abrasivejet.

See www.maxiemwaterjets.com
