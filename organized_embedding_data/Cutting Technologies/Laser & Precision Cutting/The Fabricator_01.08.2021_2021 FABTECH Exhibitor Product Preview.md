A combination of abrasive belts, oscillating discs, and rotating brushes helps the user grind, remove burr or slag, round edges, grain, and polish a product in a single pass. Machines are available in widths from 13 to 64 in. Available in a variety of configurations including top, bottom, and top/bottom in either a wet or dry operation, they can process parts as small as a business card.

Apex will showcase a new feature allowing the easy replacement of a machine’s working head from a disc station for edge rounding and deburring to a Scotch-Brite-style barrel brush for straight grain finishing—all in one modular machine.

Application specialists will be available to provide machine demos and answer specific application questions.

Automated bending systems to be highlighted

In addition to stand-alone machines, SafanDarley will present two automated systems.

The R-Brake 130T bending cell is based on the fully electric, environmentally friendly Ultra press brake. Its overhead robot design delivers the benefits of a big cell inside a small space. The operator has 100% access to use the brake in manual mode by moving the robot to the side, and the machine switches back to fully automated in seconds. The brake can be equipped with an automated tool- and gripper-changing system, making it suitable for smaller batch sizes.

The E-Brake 35T Mini Cell is a compact, automated system for large batches of smaller sheet metal parts. It will be shown with an optional pallet dispenser to automatically load new pallets and unload finished parts.
