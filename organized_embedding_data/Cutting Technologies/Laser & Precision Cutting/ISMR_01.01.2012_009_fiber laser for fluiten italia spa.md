# Fiber laser for Fluiten Italia S.p.A.

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

PRIMA POWER Laserdyne, a leader in precision multi-axis laser machining systems, recently confirmed that Fluiten Italia S.p.A. of Milan Italy took delivery of a Laserdyne 430 four-axis fiber laser system.

Founded in 1962, Fluiten is a premier European designer and manufacturer of mechanical seals used in a broad range of applications including nuclear reactor columns, naval vessels, pharmaceutical processing systems and petro chemical refineries. The newly acquired Laserdyne 430 will be used to precision weld high-pressure and temperature-resistant components used in the manufacture of Fluiten seals.

The Laserdyne 430 is a four-axis system designed for precision cutting, welding and drilling two- and three-dimensional components. The system operates at speeds of up to 800 inches/min. in the X, Y and Z axis (0-20 m/min.) with bi-directional accuracy of 0.0005 inch (12.7 micrometres). This accuracy is throughout the system’s 585 x 408 x 508mm work envelope, making it suitable for laser processing a broad range of Fluiten components manufactured to UNI EN 12756 (ex DIN 24960) standards. These include laser processing helical spring seals, bi-directional seals and seals with integral flanged and jacketed casings.

The 430 system features Laserdyne's latest and most powerful system controller, the S94P. The Laserdyne 430 includes features such as BreakThrough(r) Detection (BTD) and Laserdyne's patented Automatic Focus Control(r) (AFC). These features have been employed by system users to laser process complex electronic, medical and turbine engine components. See: www.primapower.com
