STAND 5303

## www.efb.de

The EFB (Europäische Forschungsgesellschaft für Blechverarbeitung e. V.) organises industrial research on sheet-metal processing. Experts from industry and science work on a variety of joint projects. These are presented, discussed and evaluated in specialist working groups.

The EFB advises and initiates future-oriented research roadmaps of industries. It ensures the knowledge transfer of publicly funded project results through various channels such as events and trade fairs. Contacts between business and research partners, as well as users and suppliers, are developed continuously and for specific purposes.

"Our BLE-X Navigator tool enables users to search EFB publications from more than 40 years of research on sheet-metal processing technologies. It provides a context-based overview of the content and structure of the documents," it explained.

## Fladder Danmark A/S

## www.fladder.com

Fladder Danmark offers a wide range of manual, automatic and special machines for processing thin and thick sheet-metal parts. These are used for deburring; oxide/dross removal; surface finishing; denibbing and intermediate sanding.

"The gear head in the machines, with the main motor and rotating motor, is mounted on a scissor suspension frame. The scissor is equipped with special hardened, ground wheels and the machines create linear, oscillating movement across the conveyor belt. This, combined with the in-pair counter rotating spindles, results in uniform workpieces and uniform wear of the tools," Fladder told ISMR.

"Pressure on the workpiece is created by centrifugal force, through regulation of the RPM. FLADDER® technology is nonaggressive. The abrasive cylinders are flexible tools. This means that, besides flat workpieces, the machines can also process three-dimensional parts (which saves manual labour costs for deburring machined extrusions)."

## MSS Group Ltd.

## www.msslasers.com

MSS Group Ltd will be showcasing its Nitrocube, Nitr02, Puregas and Flowbox laser-cutting gas technologies.

"The MSS Nitrocube™ nitrogen-generation system has been designed for high-pressure laser cutting gas. Featuring MSS intelligent controls, all systems provide 300bar nitrogen and purities of 99.5% to 99.999%. There is a range of flow rates and modular gas storage options available. MSS Nitr02 is an innovative gas mixing system for online variable gas mixing without the need for bulky tanks. It is designed to significantly increase fibre laser cutting quality and speed with greater flexibility for cutting with various gases."

The MSS Puregas laser-cutting gas filtration system features nitrogen, oxygen and compressed gas filtration and oil vapour removal to protect against particle damage or oil contamination for laser-cutting systems. The MSS Flowbox measures and monitors laser gas flow for laser systems to help ensure operating efficiency and eliminate leaks.

## Timesavers International B.V.

## www.timesaversint.com

With over 80 years of experience, Timesavers International is a worldwide specialist in suitable solutions for businesses that require deburring; edge rounding; removal of laser oxide and slag; finishing and precision grinding of sheet metal.

The 42 RB series is its largest deburring machine with an available working width of 1350mm and 1600mm. It deburrs large and small parts. The updated version has an ECO-mode. The 32 RB series is equipped with hammerheads that remove the slag quickly and easily. In addition, a wet dust separator from Absaugwerk GmbH will be installed, to ensure dust collection, at Blechexpo.

Its 22 Series WRB (Wide Rotary Brush) machine, with its Industry-4.0 functionalities, is suitable for deburring, edgerounding, finishing and the removal of laser oxide.

The new version of its 22 RB series has pictogram control and a user-friendly interface. It also offers an entry-level deburring machine, the 10-Series Manual Grinder.

"We will also display a new machine series, with the rotary brush, for processing small parts. Applications include deburring, rounding and finishing," it said.
