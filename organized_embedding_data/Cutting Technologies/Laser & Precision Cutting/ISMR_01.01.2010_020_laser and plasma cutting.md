## SIMPLE, AFFORDABLE LASER CUTTING

TRUMPF has just launched its low weight TruLaser 1030, a space-saving, entry level machine that is easy to operate and has a footprint of 25 square metres.

Simple operation is key to this model and it introduces a special concept that allows the customer to choose between ‘beginner’ and ‘professional’ levels. For those new to the technology, the touch panel only shows the keys that are needed for fast component cutting. Functions such as start, stop and pause are similar to those found on a home entertainment CD player. The user is guided intuitively through the programme with technology tables running automatically in the background.

At the professional level, the operator has the option of adapting all of the technology values. And, with integrated workshop programming, he or she can move from the geometry file to the finished cutting programme in just five steps.

The TruLaser 1030 combines a rugged drive system and machine construction with a TRUMPF TruCoax 2kW diffusion-cooled RF laser resonator. With a simultaneous axis speed of 85m/min, the TruLaser 1030 can cut mild steel in thicknesses of up to 15mm.
