## Pressing analysis

AT THIS YEAR'S EuroBLECH in Hanover (25-29 October 2016) in hall 27, stand J104, fine blanking specialist Feintool will present Feinmonitoring, its contribution to Industry 4.0 and to maximum press availability.

Feinmonitoring is a web-based maintenance and analysis tool which continuously monitors the technical status of Feintool's fine blanking presses and analyses the data acquired. Feintool has significantly expanded this data analysis function in recent months. Feinmonitoring is part of, and the basis for, the Smart Maintenance concept.

"Feinmonitoring makes it possible to detect problems early. Smart Maintenance exploits this advantage to increase plant availability using needs-based maintenance and to guarantee predictable maintenance costs," explained Marc Schneeberger, Business Development Manager, Feintool.

Sensors built into the fine blanking presses continuously measure different values such as power consumption, particles in the oil, temperature or pressure at various points. The data are transferred via a mobile connection to the Feinmonitoring database, where they are collected, analysed and evaluated. If these measured values are in a critical range, the customer receives an automatic message.

Feinmonitoring also makes an important contribution to the productivity of the fine blanking press. The continuously recorded data provide a valuable overview of the operating status of the Feintool press. This includes details such as the level of use, downtime and run-time, which can be displayed at any time via PC, tablet or smartphone. This enables trends to be detected and systematic production problems to be corrected. Users can also reduce disturbance variables and reproduce functioning settings.

The Feintool Smart Maintenance service is, in turn, linked with Feinmonitoring and intervenes precisely at the critical points identified by it. This means, for example, that Feinmonitoring makes it possible to promptly detect problems with parts subject to wear. Feintool service engineers can then independently carry out the preventive replacement of these parts. Feinmonitoring is now standard on Feintool presses.

See www.feintool.com
