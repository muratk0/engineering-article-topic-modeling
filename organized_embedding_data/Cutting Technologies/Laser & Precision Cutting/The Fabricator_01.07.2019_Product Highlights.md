New software added to laser processing systems

Prima Power Laserdyne offers Version 2.5.1 of S94P software, now included on new shipments of LASERDYNE

®

795, 606D, and 430 multiaxis laser processing systems. It is designed to help reduce part cycle times; improve efficiency; and provide additional capability for laser welding, drilling, and cutting processes.

In the new version, SmartComp™, added to the Smart Techniques™, corrects for part placement and part variation when processing rotary parts on a vertical axis. Enhanced support for OFC2 Absolute™ software and hardware makes it easier to keep the nozzle properly spaced from the part surface.

The software also helps enhance crash protection when using OFC and AFC for large holes and gaps within the part, provides additional real-time control of the pulses being sent to the workpiece, and shortens overall processing time when there are large gaps between holes, cuts, and weld features.

Ironworker features five workstations

Kaast offers the PS ironworker, which offers five workstations for punching, shearing, and notching operations. Featuring tonnage capacity from 45 to 175 tons, the DP two-cylinder machine style allows parallel work on the punch, as well as a second workstation.

Ironworkers from 65 tons and up include an electric trigger switch up to 40 in. for quick flow of repetitive work. Each workstation has a jog mode for tool/workpiece positioning and is available with optional CNC material feed stock for the punch function, 12- or 20-ft. material table, or pneumatic holding devices.

Software automates processes for simplified work flow

Hexagon’s Manufacturing Intelligence division offers a new release of Radan CAD/CAM software for the sheet metal industry.

Radan 2020.0 includes a new quick-nest mode that provides a more visual interface, with limited upfront setup for projects, and a redesigned project dialog allows users to start nesting more quickly. Machine-specific templates contain automation; for example, a fiber laser template will switch to the correct laser machine and put in all necessary settings.

Features include reduced mouse travel and an autosave function.

Plasma cutter pierces 5/8-in. metal

Thermal Dynamics

®

, an ESAB brand, has upgraded the Cutmaster

®

58 hand-held air plasma cutting system to include the SL60 QD 1Torch™ as standard. QD stands for quick disconnect, which enables selective replacement of either the torch handle or cable.

The 60-amp, 43-lb. unit can pierce ⅝-in. mild steel, stainless, or aluminum and has a maximum sever capacity of 1¼-in. Its tubular roll bar protects the front and rear of the power supply and makes the unit easy to carry. Color-coded LEDs on the front panel indicate gas pressure status and setup errors. The on/off switch is on the front panel. The system accepts 1- or 3-phase, 208- to 480-V primary power.

The unit offers a purge mode for clearing the gas line and setting gas pressure, as well as three process modes: standard cutting mode; trigger latch mode, which allows the operator to release the trigger to prevent hand fatigue during longer cuts; and expanded metal mode, which instantly reignites the pilot arc while cutting expanded metals such as grates and chain link fences.

Mobile fume extractor protects operator’s breathing zone

SAS has introduced the model 300 RuggedAir mobile fume extractor. It removes airborne fumes and particulates produced by welding, metalworking, powder handling, and solvents and chemicals. The system features a cleanable Micro-Pleat Series 2 particulate filter that can be cleaned with compressed air while in the unit’s chassis.

Features include a digital display with a Magnehelic

®

gauge and hour counter. The gauge allows the operator to monitor the filter saturation, and the counter can be reset to allow for consistent preventive maintenance schedules.
