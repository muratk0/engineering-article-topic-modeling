## Laser cutting in focus

The new Dalcos LXN 1500 system features automation with a robot to unload the workpieces. It is equipped with a 3kW fibre laser source and can process strip material from 0.5 to 3mm thick (coil) and up to 5mm sheet materials. Strip with widths of up to 1550mm (from coil) and up to 14 metres in length can be processed. The working range of 2500mm allows complex machining with precision facilitated by the Dalcos HD vision system, which checks the positioning of workpieces in the machine, for fully automatic production.

"There is no waste when cutting from coil as the machine can cut coil of widths of up to 1500mm. Continuous automatic production; a sequential process and flexibility for handling coil/sheet are key features of this new machine. It can also process long products/profiles of up to 5m with the Cartesian automatic stacker (or long products/profiles up to 14m if the stacker is not used). This system has a wide range of applications. Samples include construction/ automotive parts, road signs, electrical cabinets, door parts, fridges, home appliances, light fixtures and air conditioning parts," explained Daniele Fratini, Dallan's marketing manager.

"The best application is where you have products with the same width but different lengths. The machine can optimise this type of production. There is no need to change the coil to produce parts of different length during production which maximises uptime and minimises waste."

Since its inception, the company has expanded from fire suppression to a wide array of products including exhaust, pollution control, fire suppression and prevention, and utility distribution. Its integrated kitchen ventilation packages include hoods; exhaust fans; electrical controls; direct-fired heaters; grease duct systems; fire suppression systems; grease filters; utility distribution systems and internet-based control systems.

## Punch and laser systems

ELLECI, Dallan's Pordenone-based customer, took delivery of a 2kW Dalcos ELXN combination servo-electric punch/fibre laser line just over a year ago. Separate laser and punching areas in the machine are designed to improve the flexibility of the line as well as overall production times. When the punching system is processing material, the laser system can cut material at the same time.

"ELLECI's material utilisation rate compares with sheet/coil production systems and improves it by 15.9%. The machine has also improved production speed by 100% because when ELLECI works with sheet, it needs to load the machine with each sheet. However, with coil-fed in-line production systems, production time is continuous. There is no need to load or unload the machine. ELLECI can use sheet or coil on the same machine, taking advantage of the best of both working processes," explained Daniele Fratini of Dallan.

"There is no waste when you process the coil because the material is cut to the same width of the coil (end-to-end). Using coil also reduces the space needed for the machine (e.g. no need for large sheet stores). We can therefore minimise work in progress, waste and set-up tasks. ELLECI told us that, in general, it can save up to 200,000 euros per year. The 2kW fibre laser cutting system can process a wide variety of material up to 3mm thick. On the punching system, we have lots of tooling and you can set up the machine for different types of production without changing the tooling on the machine."

Presented at the last EuroBLECH exhibition as the 'world's first combo coil-fed punch-laser', the line at ELLECI can handle 4mm max. thickness (0.3mm/min.) using coil or sheet. The ELXN consists of an electric punching machine (EXN 1000) in line with a laser cutting zone, for making complex notchings, special perforations and variable forms. The system features a decoiler loading 6000kg. The 8-roller straightener eliminates the curve due to winding. At the head of the ELXN system is the patented Vega gripper feeder.

"We installed the ELXN line in January 2016 at ELLECI, which has been operating a Dalcos hydraulic punching line for 12 years. It is making parts for electrical cabinets on the punch/laser. Productivity increases (which include optimised raw material usage) of up to 200% have been registered with the new line," explained Michela Dallan, Sales Assistant Manager, DALLAN S.p.A.

The line at ELLECI can handle 4mm max. thickness (0.3mm/min.) using coil or sheet. It can also process a range of materials such as stainless steel, aluminium, mild steel and prepainted material.

"We manufacture air conditioning units for electrical cabinets on the combined punch/ laser machine – we are able to produce parts of different lengths e.g. 1000mm and up to 4000mm on the machine. The in-line punching operation is done first, followed by laser cutting with a 2kW fibre laser. An integrated vision system then checks the parts," ELLECI told ISMR.

## An eye on rollforming

ISMR also took a tour of the production line at Dallan. A new 30m rollforming machine with automation and packaging systems, designed to make equipment to feed chickens, was being built for an Italian customer in Padova.

"Our customer wants to cut material to different lengths. He will supply the entire chicken feeding system with belts (1000m) to his customer. The line can process ten workpieces per minute with the robot. The customer has bought four lines from us (each for a dedicated profile)," explained Michela Dallan.

A rollforming machine for a German customer, designed to make roller shutters and slats (cut to length), was also on the shop floor and due for delivery the following week. The customer has bought five of these machines.

"Our high degree of specialisation means that we can offer customers not only the forming machines and lines (with all the equipment, tools and machinery to obtain the finished product) and the complete integrated production system with automatic packaging, but also the original design of the product as well as expertise on sourcing the right raw material suppliers," concluded Michela Dallan.
