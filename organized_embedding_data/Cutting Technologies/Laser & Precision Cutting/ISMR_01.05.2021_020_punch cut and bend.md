# Punch, cut and bend

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

Durmazlar (Durma) is a global manufacturer of sheet metal processing machinery, with headquarters in Turkey, 1500 employees, a wide production line equipped with the latest technology and modern facilities covering 150,000 square metres. Today, it exports to 120 countries and counts 80 distributors as business partners - approximately 80% of its machinery is sent to buyers in developed industrial countries such as the USA, members of the European Union and Australia.

Its wide product portfolio includes lasercutting systems; press brakes; punch presses; bending centres; Smart Line solutions; plasma cutting machines; guillotine shears; plate rollers and profile bending machines. It recently launched what it dubs 'the first 15kW fibre laser cutting system in Turkey'.

"The benefits of 15kW laser for users include rapid and precise cutting; lower operating cost (for both thin and thick sheets); maximum productivity and smooth cutting edges," it told /SMR.

The manufacturer also plans to shortly launch a new automated cell with punching, laser cutting and bending capability.

"Combining the features of a Durma punch/laser and panel bender, it is an ideal solution for getting the best out of bending with a rapid and efficient return on investment (ROI); flexibility and fully automatic operation," it explained.

Durma's new automated punching, cutting and bending cell will be launched shortly.

The cell offers a tower storage system for sheet metal and a punch loading-unloading system. The Combi Punch-Laser system features part and scrap conveyor systems; buffering systems; stackers; a turntable system; panel bender; pick & place and a part evacuation system.
