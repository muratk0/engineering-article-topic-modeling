# Intelligent plasma-cutting processes

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Kjellberg Finsterwalde Plasma und Maschinen GmbH has developed products made in Germany for 100 years. It has been producing plasma-cutting systems for a wide range of cutting tasks for over 60 years.

“These products make it possible to achieve excellent results for automated, mechanised or manual cutting. Our latest products include the Q-Series inverter power source. It combines precision plasmacutting with complex requirements. The Kjellberg-developed, browser-based HMI Q-Desk provides realtime process data and information from all system components on standard devices, regardless of location. Users can transfer and process all data via the MQTT protocol using the Ethernet interface," said the company.

The Q-Series plasma power source with Q-Gas. Electrically conductive materials can be cut.

As the smallest model in the Q-series, the Q 1500 plasma cuts material thicknesses of up to 60mm with a maximum cutting current of 150A. The Q 3000 and Q 3000 plus plasma-cutting systems cut and mark material thicknesses of up to 80mm with a maximum cutting current of 300A. Because of their modular design, machine performance of the Q 1500 plus and Q 3000 plus can be upgraded to suit customer requirements.

“Q-Hole technology enables users to make precise holes in mild steel with a diameterto-material thickness ratio of 0.75:1. Our patent-pending 'ProPierce' process is offered exclusively for the Q series. It increases the previous maximum piercing capacity by 10mm in mild steel and by up to 30mm in stainlesssteel and aluminium. The new technological process offers the user more flexibility. No process change, additional equipment or new consumables are required," confirmed the manufacturer.

With its carbon footprint, Kjellberg's Q plasma power source makes a demonstrable contribution to climate protection. Daily live demonstrations of the system can be seen at Kjellberg's stand D112 in Hall 13 at the EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022).
