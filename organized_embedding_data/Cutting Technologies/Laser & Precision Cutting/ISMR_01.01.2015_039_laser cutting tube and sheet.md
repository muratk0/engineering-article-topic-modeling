# Laser cutting tube and sheet

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

BOTH FLAT sheet and tube sections can be processed on the new Adige LC5 laser cutting system. Available from BLM GROUP, this versatile combination machine can switch from flat sheet processing to tube processing, and vice versa, immediately and automatically without the need for re-tooling.

Its capacity includes the ability to process flat sheet measuring up to 1500mm x 3000mm (which can be increased), and round, square, rectangular and oval section tube up to a maximum diameter of 120mm. The end-user has the option of choosing either a 4.5kW CO2 laser source or a 3kW fiber laser, which enables the option to process reflective metals such as copper, brass and aluminium.

A key element in the design of the LC5 was to keep the footprint as small as possible. Adige has achieved this by incorporating all of the electrics/electronics within the machine itself. Installation is greatly simplified by this approach. Ease of machine access and operation is created by the use of wide openings to the working area and two touch-screen operator panels. ■
