# PLASMA SUCCESS IN BRAZIL

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

ESAB HAS sold its first bevel cutting system in Brazil. A VBA Expert Pro system has been integrated into a SUPRAREX plasma cutting machine for a customer in the general fabrication market. The fabricator will be able to take advantage of the VBA Expert Pro’s ability to undertake precision bevelling cuts at angles of +/- 45° with a plasma torch. The rotating range is up to +/- 540° and the working area is suitable for material thicknesses from 6 to 38mm.

The VBA Expert Pro integrates simply into the SUPRAREX CNC plasma cutting machine. The plasma torch is programmed to move in three axes via servomotors and this ensures high precision chamfer tolerances. With over 100 years of experience, a presence in over 80 countries and more than 8,000 employees worldwide, ESAB is a recognised brand in welding and cutting.

## TITANIUM TUBE MILLS

TITANIUM TUBES are used in airline engines or frames, chemical and nuclear power plants as they are highly corrosion-resistant, lightweight and strong.

“There is currently a marked trend towards production of seam welded titanium tubes in the market,” Rosendahl told ISMR recently. “For each nuclear powerplant, a volume of 300 tons of titanium tube is required – there is therefore a lack of titanium tube production capacity at the moment. The raw material costs of titanium are 10 to 20 times higher than those of comparable steels.

“We believe that stainless tube mills are not able to combine the necessary levels of tube quality, production reliability and production speeds at the lowest scrap rates for titanium tube manufacturing.”

Rosendahl has developed its new titanium tube mill series TH-G Ti for the production of tubes for high security-relevant applications such as heat exchanger products for power generation. The dimension range for the TH-G Ti Series goes up to 120mm in diameter and can cover wall thickness values of between 0.2 and 3mm.

“Rosendahl hybrid-forming for titanium tube production combines the advantages of moveable and non-moveable tooling. Higher performance levels have also been introduced in titanium tube manufacturing by using high-end TIG-plasma- or laser welding technology. There is a fully integrated quality control system including continuous thermographical analysis of the welding seam as well as STOP and GO production for zero defect welding (to minimise scrap rates).
