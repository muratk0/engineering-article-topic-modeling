# TECOI Corte SL

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand B117 and 13-B20

https://tecoi.com/en

Spanish machine tool and software manufacturer, TECOI, produces laser cutting, plasma cutting, tube/ profile cutting and heavy-duty machining equipment.

“Developed for fibre laser cutting of large formats and thicknesses, our LS-CF machine is ideal for intensive use requiring precision and repeatability. With a laser power of up to 20kW, this model is equipped with the TWIN DISK dual-head system and the CAP automatic pallet changeover system, designed exclusively by TECOI, which allows it to eliminate downtime and further extend its performance," explained the company.

The TWIN DISK system, featuring two independent heads simultaneously working on the same gantry, is designed to double the productivity of a laser cutting system. Another TECOI development is its DRILTEC PLUS, a multi-tool machining head able to carry out drilling, tapping and countersinking operations.
