# LASER SHARP® – a new range of structural sheets for laser applications

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

Marcegaglia has launched LASER SHARP®, a new range of structural sheets for laser applications which are suitable for special finishes.

Key features are a well-maintained surface appearance and flatness after a 3mm/mt. linear cut. High execution speed and cut precision, especially on thin material thicknesses, save unit costs.

The laser cutting process simultaneously provides a highdefinition cut, with the ability to produce openings and complex cuts with well-defined square edges so that the product is burrfree and machining tolerances can be contained to a tenth of a millimetre, explained Marcegaglia.

The range consists of LASER SHARP® 200; LASER SHARP® 240 (CE marking available); LASER SHARP® 355 (CEmarking available) and LASER SHARP® 360. It is suitable for:

■ Cutting thin thicknesses. ■ High precision work (plus or minus 0.1mm).

■Cutting particularly elaborate profiles.

■ An immediate finished product, without further machining. ■ Smaller parts (less than 200mm).
