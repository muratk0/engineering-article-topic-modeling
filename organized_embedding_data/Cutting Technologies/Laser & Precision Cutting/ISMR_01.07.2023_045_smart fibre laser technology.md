# Smart fibre laser technology

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

From the BySmart Fiber to the ByCut Smart, Bystronic presents its new generation of laser cutting machines from the “Smart" series. The new flexible ByCut Smart 3015 cuts steel; aluminium; stainless steel; brass and copper using high laser power (three to 15 kilowatts), an optimised cutting process and smart features. It boasts a sharp and modern design to significantly increase user friendliness. Different access door configurations provide flexibility, including front and/or side access as well as multiple windows with direct views of the cutting process. Overall, the new design offers better access to the interior space.

"Laser power levels from three to 15kW ensure excellent cutting performance and high parts output for thin to medium sheet-metal thicknesses. Depending upon the production range and customer request, the ByCut Smart 3015 is available in a selection of different laser powers of 3-, 4-, 6-, 8-, 10-, 12- and 15 kilowatts. Non-ferrous metals can also be processed," explained Swiss sheet-metal specialist, Bystronic.

Smart features such as the Nozzle Control Tool (NCT) and KerfScan, the Parameter Wizard and cutting with MixGas, in addition to more laser power, are designed for higher cutting quality as well as increased productivity.

"The NCT provides automatic nozzle centring at the beginning of a cutting plan and after collisions. As a result, manual interaction from the operator is no longer required, reducing time expenditure by up to 95%. Centring is completed within seconds, rather than minutes," outlined Bystronic.

“KerfScan also serves as a monitoring function, increasing process reliability. Through greater autonomy, customers benefit by producing less scrap due to incorrect settings, poor material quality or hot sheet metal, which reduces waste costs and makes production with flame cutting more cost-effective and reliable," it added.

The Parameter Wizard determines the best parameters with N₂ and MixGas for steel with thicknesses of 4-15mm. It optimises the entire cutting process and ensures its smooth functioning, which improves production time without downtime.

“MixGas combines the benefits of O₂ and N₂ and is an alternative to cutting with pure nitrogen or oxygen. Cutting with MixGas (a mix of nitrogen and oxygen) massively improves cutting quality in thicker steel at increased cutting speeds. MixGas also contributes to achieving better cutting results with varying qualities of steel. The higher the laser power, the greater the advantage," Bystronic told ISMR.

With the new shuttle table concept, users can load their ByCut Smart up to 40mm over the entire surface on both tables or 60mm over the entire surface on one table.

"Bystronic software and automation solutions integrate the ByCut Smart into sheet-metal production. Loading and unloading solutions can be selected in a variety of designs and degrees of automation. Our systems organise the material flow fully and semi-automatically according to the order situation, and provide enough flexibility to process smaller orders manually," added Bystronic.

"A fast cutting process and extremely low maintenance requirements guarantee high machine uptime and longevity, and therefore more profit per part," it concluded.
