NEW PRODUCT RELEASE

HF-LU Series Laser Cutting Machine

Hymson Laser was founded in 2008 to design and build integrated laser cutting and automation systems. As one of the largest manufacturers of laser cutting machines with intelligent technologies in China, its products include sheet metal laser cutting, tube laser cutting and 3D cutting systems. Its customers are in various industries, including construction, automotive, rail and aerospace sectors. It is also a leading supplier of automation systems for new energy automobile, consumer electronics and metal fabrication industries.

Its product portfolio is particularly flexible. It also provides a broad range of intelligent system options in drilling, marking, welding and other applications to address lithium battery manufacturing, sapphire and glasses processing, PCB & FPC processing and metal sheet & tube processing. Hymson Laser has sales and service teams in around 40 countries and is currently setting up a new operations centre in northern Italy to service European customers.

At this October's EuroBLECH exhibition, Hymson Laser showcased its new HF-LU series fibre laser cutting system with automated loading and unloading system. ISMR went to its booth to see how the company is moving its technology into European markets.

## The laser equation

"Hymson Laser has experienced rapid rates of growth, since it was founded in 2008. The three founders enjoyed long experience in the sheet metal industry and have steered the company towards new, international markets. In 2017, we opened our last two smart factories. These two factories are operating but they will be ready, by the end of next year, for full production. In 2010, we started our metal forming operations and, in 2014, our new energy/battery division. Our philosophy is to try to get as close to customers as we can," explained Vincent Yang, Hymson's marketing manager.

The company's headquarters are at Shenzhen (Guangdong) and four additional development, production, sales and service subsidiaries are located in Guangzhou (Guangdong); Jiangmen (Guangdong); Changzhou (Jiangsu) and Anshan (Liaoning).

Its factories in Jiangmen and Changzhou were designed as two of the most advanced and largest smart factories among laser manufacturers in China, actively promoting "Made in China 2025", "IoT" and "Industry-4.0" principles.

Although it is well known in China, Hymson Laser aims to increase its brand presence in Europe and has accordingly opened a new office and factory near Vicenza in northern Italy to address these new markets. It features a new showroom, along with R&D engineering, service and spares.

"We believe that it is important to focus on innovation and strive to be the best, especially in a competitive environment. We aim to be a truly international company and are recruiting new experts worldwide. We also have a new global strategy, starting with Hymson Italy (near Vicenza). Next will be Hymson USA and Hymson south-east Asia. We want to develop a global footprint in every region," confirmed Yang.

## Laser in focus

"We aim to bring out a new machine every year for metal fabrication. We will tread our own path and keep focusing on R&D and innovation. We are putting a lot of investment into these areas, including the right skilled personnel. Over 1600 people work for Hymson (800 of whom are engineers)," Vincent Yang told ISMR.

In accordance with these principles, Hymson Laser showcased its newly launched HF-LU series fibre laser cutting machine with automated loading and unloading system. The machine features a patented kinematic system (high dynamic movement and precision cutting) for the cutting head and double cooling system for the lens and body. It has been designed for lightweight sheet metal laser cutting. The cutting table has retractable clamps, controlled by CNC, for stable movement of the workpiece. The system is suitable for cutting reflective materials such as aluminium and copper and has been designed for lower energy consumption.

Hymson Laser independently developed a new cutting process for its fibre laser cutting machines which solved the issue of burrs when cutting aluminium. The process achieves burr- and wrinkle-free, high-precision cuts without secondary processing (saving time and processing costs).

"Our major highlight here at EuroBLECH is the new model LU series (loading system storage tower), all designed in Italy. This is a compact fibre laser cutting system, newly designed, for thin material (thickness of less than 10mm). We can offer 3-4kW power with this model. We invented the cutting head which can move rapidly, especially on thin material (400 holes per minute)," explained Yang.

Yang also showed ISMR Hymson's HP-D series fibre laser cutting system for tube, on the booth at EuroBLECH, featuring automated loading and unloading system.

## Spotting new trends

Most of Hymson Laser's customers are sheet metal contractors, working across a range of industry sectors. It has consequently seen constantly changing shifts and patterns in sheet metal cutting markets.

"I see three major trends," Yang told ISMR. "Firstly, the trend towards higher power in fibre laser markets. A lot of factories are looking for 15kW+ solutions for fibre laser, however it is still quite a young technology and you do not necessarily need higher power sources to achieve the desired cutting results."

"Secondly, I see a move towards larger working ranges. 3 x 1.5m was the original working range but now it is 4 x 2m or 6 x 2m, which is becoming more popular. We are now working on a 12 x 3m working table for a customer in China which should be ready next year. The third trend is towards higher automation. The fibre laser cutting machine is just one part of the product line – our motto is 'Laser Technology and Automation Technology' to reflect our experience in automation. We are now assembling more automation on lasers. We use our own equipment and also work with automation partners. Sheet metal is embracing Industry 4.0 so this is a natural progression. We can offer a range of automation options for customers." he added.

Sustainability is Hymson Laser's guiding principle, supporting its daily operations and ensuring its long-term growth. The company has successfully launched 'Green & Smart Manufacturing' to decrease waste of electricity, water, gas and energy, and to raise utilisation rates through 'intellectualisation, digitalisation and automation'. This eco-philosophy, it believes, is a key pillar of its growth.

"Our ultimate aim is to create long-term partnerships. We want to be a reliable partner to our customers, one who listens to their requirements and helps them to create value for the long term. This is where we see the future for Hymson Laser," concluded Vincent Yang.
