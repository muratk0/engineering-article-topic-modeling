# Laser cutting head

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

EAGLE OFFERS AN innovative solution for the steel industry – the eVa cutting head. The laser cutting head enables laser cutting power of up to 12kW and the processing of materials up to 60mm.

“This cutting head is our engineers' answer to the growing demand for efficient, durable and reliable solutions in sheet metal processing. The device has been prepared with a focus on the durability of its components. The result is competitive laser power, faster production, high efficiency and lower costs of operation and maintenance," said the manufacturer.

What distinguishes this type of cutting head is the lack of moving parts through the laser beam and relatively simple construction design. Engineers prepared the cutting head with just two fixed lenses. Despite this, the cutting head continues to provide change of focus diameter and angle of the beam to the material. It is protected by five patents.

In addition, the cover glass is located further away from the cutting process so that it is less susceptible to the contamination generated during cutting. Protective glass, mounted over a collimating lens, prevents contamination on the lens.

Benefits of the new cutting head, said Eagle, are that it is four times less sensitive to dirt; can lower maintenance costs; carry out faster piercings; cut material with a thickness from 1 to 60mm; offers the option of automatic nozzle exchange and ensures automatic centring and automatic checking of the nozzle quality.
