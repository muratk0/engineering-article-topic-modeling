# Multiple operations in one

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

YAMAZAKI MAZAK launched its new Optiplex laser cutting machine at MACH 2012, offering laser users the ability to combine multiple machine operations without the need for costly operator intervention or downtime.

The new machine has been designed to offer greater production flexibility and increased productivity with a variety of automated set-up functions for optimised cutting even during extended unmanned operation. In effect, this automation enables the Optiplex to implement non-stop cutting of different metals, regardless of metal thicknesses, eliminating the need for multiple laser machines and material nesting.

Optiplex is equipped with a constant-beam delivery system which uses a U-axis design to compensate for table axis movements, in turn delivering consistent beam power anywhere on the table for more stable cutting conditions and better accuracy. It uses six external mirrors, five fewer mirrors than previous designs, which significantly increases the power to the torch.

“Productivity is further increased with rapid axes movement (feed rate of 120m/min. and acceleration of 1.2G) and rapid pallet change, provided by a new high speed twin-pallet system which is capable of changing pallets in 19 seconds and operates simultaneously with the automated set-up function. The new Optiplex 4kW is also equipped with a number of intelligent machine functions designed to further enhance productivity, reduce pierce times and maintain cut performance, including piercing, burning and plasma detection,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

An Eco-mode resonator delivers reduced electricity consumption (5 per cent) during the cutting operation, a 50 per cent reduction in laser gas consumption to 15L/hr and 43 per cent reduction in air consumption to 500L/min. The Optiplex also features the new MAZATROL Preview 2 CNC ‘Touchscreen’ control system which automatically determines the cutting speed and acceleration from each point to the next from the program contour. ON A ROLL
