# FOCAL POINT

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

Subcontractors and job shops have quickly found that the fibre laser’s expanded cutting capabilities have extended their reach into new markets and new customers

ISMR SAYS: “We highlight a selection of the latest new fibre laser innovations in the market today”

A relatively new innovation in laser cutting thicker, sheet metal has been the introduction of the fibre laser which can cut not just steel materials but also non-ferrous materials such as copper, brass and titanium. Linear drives enable rapid positioning and cutting speeds to be achieved. Fibre laser sales have cut significantly into overall CO2 laser cutting market sales this year for many laser cutting machine tool manufacturers.

The use of fibres eliminates the need for elaborate and costly optics for beam guidance and the space requirement can be downsized.

This also makes fibre lasers very stable and easy to use. The beam quality is high and continually getting more powerful. Fibre lasers are also very efficient and easy to cool, converting around 70-80% of the power from the pump source. This is better for power consumption, but also means that less power is being converted to heat.

Fibre lasers can be comfortably used for the efficient processing of gauge and fractional materials and fine feature cutting. Cut capability on steel is from thin gauge to approximately 20mm. A range of global manufacturers have invested heavily in fibre laser offerings to the global market. Subcontractors and job shops have quickly found that the fibre laser’s expanded cutting capabilities have extended their reach into new markets and new customers. ## An eye on fibre

“On our ENSIS laser cutting machine, operators can cut 20/25mm mild steel on the fibre laser – people just don’t believe it or believe that the machine is only 2kW,” Japanese specialist Amada told ISMR at last year’s EuroBLECH exhibition. “Traditionally, 20mm mild steel is cut on a 4kW CO2 machine. The ENSIS just uses 25% of the power of a 4kW CO2 machine. That has really opened a lot of customers’ eyes.”
