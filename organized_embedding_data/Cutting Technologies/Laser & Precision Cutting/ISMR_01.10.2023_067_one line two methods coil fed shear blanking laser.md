Laser ISSE was founded in 2003 as a pioneering laser job shop in Turkey, with laser engraving and welding systems, to service and support the rapidly expanding use of laser technology in a wide range of industries. Through European suppliers, it also became a spare parts vendor for a range of equipment including laser lamps; filters; mirrors; protective windows; lenses; flow tubes; fibre optic cables and goggles for laser machines.

Since 2008, it has been producing and providing engraving, cutting and welding systems such as 2D laser engraving and cutting; coil-fed laser cutting; robotic applications and turnkey solutions. These feature different types of lasers such as fibre; diode pumped solid state; Nd:YAG; UV; disc and CO₂. Its international team has delivered, and continues to run, more than 1700 laser systems worldwide (exported to 56 countries over the past 20 years) with over 200 different applications.

"We provide 'end-to-end' custom-made laser solutions from the design stage

through to on-site installation including software; automation; all stages of production (plus fixtures); laser safety etc., ensuring a comprehensive 'A-to-Z' service with no outsourcing. We specialise in coil-fed laser cutting, automated laser welding and cleaning lines (including robotic loading and unloading)," it explained.

## Coil-fed shear blanking and laser cutting line

The manufacturer's coil-fed shear blanking and laser cutting line offers a cutting process that is clean-edged, precise and consistently repeatable. Laser ISSE works with material thicknesses from 0.2-10mm, while accommodating material widths that range from 30-2000mm. This flexibility helps it to provide tailor-made solutions for a range of different projects.

"This system offers maximum flexibility in material cutting, accommodating a wide range of materials including high-strength steel; stainless steel; aluminium; copper and more, making it a versatile solution for diverse manufacturing requirements. The blanking line cuts custom blanks direct from the coil with our turnkey solutions. It utilises automation for feeding, levelling, cutting and stacking all cut parts. Shear cutting provides rapid cutting of blanks of any size whilst laser cutting offers high-speed cutting for straight lines, trapezoid and differently shaped blanks for various production requirements," it told ISMR.

"The low-cost maintenance system is designed to enable users to significantly reduce scrap through its seamless integration of continuous cutting and nesting capabilities. It also reduces floor space requirements for raw material leading to reduced electricity and labour costs as well as enhanced cost efficiencies. Rapid and uninterrupted production ensures maximum efficiency and output," it added.
