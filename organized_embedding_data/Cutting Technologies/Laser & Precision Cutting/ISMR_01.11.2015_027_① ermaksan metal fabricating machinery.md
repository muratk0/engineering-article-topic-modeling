# ① ERMAKSAN METAL FABRICATING MACHINERY

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, has forged itself a solid profile in the sheet metal working industry. The company has been producing sheet metal fabricating machines since 1980. It exports 80% of its production through agents, under the ERMAKSAN brand, to over 70 countries in the world and has a branch office in Chicago, USA for its marketing, sales and after-sales services for the North American market.

Ermaksan has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. The company currently manufactures guillotine shears, hydraulic/hybrid press brakes, laser cutting machines, punch presses, plasma cutters and assorted metalworking machinery.

Last year, it won two awards in the industrial category of the GoodDesign awards for its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine. The Fibermak machine also won IF’s Best Design Award. This year, the family-run company celebrates 50 years in the industry and unveiled a new fibre laser machine at this year’s Blechexpo at Stuttgart, where ISMR caught up with its sales manager, Ersin Yildiz.

## Fibre laser in focus

“We unveiled the Fibermak Momentum Gen-3 G-Force fibre laser here at the show,” he told ISMR . “We started the fibre lasers with model Gen 1 and it was superseded by Gen 2 after a few years. At the beginning of 2015, we unveiled the latest version, the Gen 3 fibre laser model. Now we have an optional model, the Gen-3 G-Force. The new machine features an advanced servo system where acceleration speeds can be increased up to 2.5G. There is a need for this in the market. We are also providing this upgrade for new and existing customers along with free software upgrades. We produce our own open-source software in-house, which gives us more control.”

Fibermak features several servo motors for all axial movements. These are the latest technology single cable servo motors. Power and process data are transmitted in one standard motor cable, significantly increasing speeds. This technology also gives more accurate positioning and more geometrically accurate parts. Laser units of between 500W up to 6kW can be selected for the product family.

The new machine is designed for ultra-low energy consumption and very rapid cutting capability with low maintenance costs. The fibre laser can cut different thicknesses and types of materials such as steel, stainless steel, aluminium, brass, copper and galvanized steel.

“We recently opened a new laser factory in Bursa (10,000 square metres) and a new R&D centre (8000 square metres). We invested 30 million euros into the R&D centre. The laser centre will have its own showroom and display fibre and CO2 laser production. We are putting our main focus onto laser machine production next year and aim to produce 50 laser machines per month (we are currently producing 20 machines per month). We will be launching a lot of new laser products next year,” added Ersin Yildiz.

Nine months ago, Ermaksan unveiled the new Gen-3 Fibermak fibre laser – the Gen-3 G-Force followed this launch. The company has a strong laser history, having produced the first linear drive flying optic laser cutting machine in Turkey. Fibre lasers are suitable for different applications such as welding, hybrid welding, remote welding, 2D and 3D remote cutting, heat treatment and cladding.

“Our real focus next year will be on fibre laser although we also produce other technologies such as press brakes, plasma cutters, punch presses and shears. Fibre laser technology has more potential in the market than CO laser technology although CO laser technology will always be on the table for thick materials. Fibre laser machines are very easy to operate and are becoming more and more flexible and versatile. Experienced CO operators are hard to find – it can take just one week to train a fibre laser operator,” explained Yildiz.

## A long term view

Yildiz confirmed that 2015 had been a challenging year but is optimistic about the future. The majority of Ermaksan’s customers are in Turkey, North America, South East Asia and Europe. The company aims to extend its global distributor network next year.

“The advantage of being a family-run business is that we can afford to take a long term view of the market,” explained Yildiz. “This means that we can focus on properly developing our new products and keep an eye on market trends and developments.”

The right marketing strategy, Yildiz told ISMR, is key. He sees growing market needs for combination machines and energy-saving/ green solutions.

“Customers want flexibility and less downtime – there is an increasing need for automation. For press brakes, the trend for higher durability is increasing,” he continued.

With a strong capacity for innovation and collaboration with partners such as universities in the design and development of products, Ermaksan is moving confidently into the future. See www.ermaksan.com.tr ■
