# Cutting and punching combi from coil

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

EfficoIL is a coil-fed, combined punchlaser machine from Italian coil processing specialist, Produtech Srl.

"The line is equipped with two compact and independent working heads (electric punching unit and optical fibre laser cutting unit) running aligned, via an innovative electronic system. The units are perfectly synchronised and run on the same work area, with precision and speed. Processing from coil offers great production benefits and substantial cost, space and manpower savings (eliminating all preliminary processes)," Produtech explained.

"EffiCOIL is an innovative and flexible solution in the coilfed, laser punching machine market because it cuts and forms coil sheets on a single line. Interpolation of the machine's working axis allows it to cut, punch and form any shape or geometric figure for producing parts directly from coil. The technological value, innovation and environmental sustainability of this laser-punching machine were ratified by the European Commission, who financed the design and production of the first prototype via Horizon 2020 project funds," it added.

Produtech systems also include special equipment, such as punching lines for processing metal bars and beading machines, and a new AutoCOIL automation project.■
