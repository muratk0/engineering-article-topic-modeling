# Powering the plasma cut

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Hypertherm Associates, a U.S.-based manufacturer of industrial cutting systems and software, has announced the launch of CorePlus™, the gas connect console for XPR® X-Definition® plasma cutting systems.

The CorePlus gas connect console is positioned between the current Core™ and VWI™ (Vented Water Injection™) consoles and introduces argon gas capability for users that primarily cut mild steel. The gas console offers all Core console capabilities plus argon marking and an increase in piercing thickness with argon-assist enhanced piercing capability.

"With the introduction of argon gas, customers gain valuable capabilities from their XPR plasma cutting system which deliver better outcomes in production," said Marty Geheran, Hypertherm's product marketing manager for heavy industrial plasma.

The ability to mark with argon significantly improves the quality of marks made with the XPR system. With the Core gas connect console, users are limited to marking with nitrogen gas only.

"With argon-assist technology, customers can pierce 10% thicker mild steel at 170A and 300A compared to standard production pierce capacity. Like the Core, the CorePlus console offers full X-Definition capability on mild steel and entry-level capability on stainless steel and aluminium with nitrogen as its plasma gas.

This includes particularly good results on thin stainless steel through the Hypertherm HDi cutting process. Like the other XPR gas consoles, the CorePlus console can be fully controlled through the CNC for high productivity and ease of use," concluded Hypertherm.
