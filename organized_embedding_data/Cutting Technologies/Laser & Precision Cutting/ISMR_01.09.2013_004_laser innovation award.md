# LASER INNOVATION AWARD

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

DEVELOPERS and researchers working with laser technology are eligible to apply for the Berthold Leibinger Innovationspreis laser innovation award. The deadline for applications and nominations is 31 December 2013. This international prize, from the Berthold Leibinger Stiftung nonprofit foundation, has been offered since 2000 for innovations in the application of lasers as well as laser devices.

At the award ceremony on 26 September 2014 in Ditzingen, Germany, 30,000 euros for the first prize, 20,000 euros for the second prize and 10,000 euros for the third prize will be awarded. The prestigious Berthold Leibinger Zukunftspreis, a prize for laser research which is judged by the jury on the basis of secret nominations, will also be awarded with a prize fund of 30,000 euros.

Eight finalists will be honoured as winners of the first evaluation process. They will be invited to the jury session in April 2014 to present their innovations and be presented with their award. Eligible to apply, or be proposed, are individuals and project groups worldwide whose main development efforts and market potential lies in the application of laser light or the development of laser devices. See www.leibinger-stiftung.de
