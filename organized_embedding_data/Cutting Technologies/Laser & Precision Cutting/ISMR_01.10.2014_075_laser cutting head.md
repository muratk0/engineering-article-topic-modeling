# Laser cutting head

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

HIGHYAG, a subsidiary of II-VI Incorporated, will be showcasing its laser cutting head, BIMO-FSC with machine-controlled adjustment of focus position and focus diameter, at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition at Hanover (21-25 October 2014).

“The BIMO-FSC can cut different sheet thicknesses, of up to 25mm, without any manual intervention so that maximum productivity in manufacturing can be achieved,” HIGHYAG explained. “In addition to productivity, the cut quality is of particular importance. One determining factor of cut quality is the optical design. The optical design of the BIMO-FSC enables a reduction of the laser power-induced focus shift as well as for diffraction-limited imaging quality as suitable conditions for best cut quality.”

See HIGHYAG on booth G68 in Hall 11 at EuroBLECH or see www.highyag.com ■
