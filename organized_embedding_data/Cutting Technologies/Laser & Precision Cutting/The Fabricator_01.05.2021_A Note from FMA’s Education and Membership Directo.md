Fabricators & Manufacturers Association

The longest-running laser conference in the industry is almost here with both in-person and virtual attendance options. The Advanced Laser Applications Workshop (ALAW0) is a premier event developed specifically for manufacturers, job shops, and suppliers to immerse themselves in the latest advancements and applications of industrial lasers.

The in-person and virtual attendance options enable you to choose how you’ll get invaluable insights from a multitude of educational sessions to fully optimize advanced laser applications. Plus, discover the latest laser advancements in the automotive, aerospace, agriculture, and general fabrication sectors.

We are excited to get back to business and see as many of you as we can in person. FMA is dedicated to providing a safe and clean conference environment to everyone who attends. We look forward to seeing all of you in June.

Spark Creativity and Innovation at ALAW

June 1-3, 2021

Attending ALAW has never been easier! Whether you join us in Plymouth, Michigan, or jump online, get ready to engage with and learn from the greatest minds in industrial lasers.

Hear from these noteworthy industry experts who will share the latest trends.

Automotive Market Update Bernard Swiecki Center for Automotive Research

Bernard will provide a high-level outlook of the automotive industry — the state-of-the-industry now and where he sees it going in the future.

The Future of e-Mobility Henrikki Pantsar TRUMPF Laser Technology Center

With e-Mohility being the way of the future, it brings new materials and raises new engineering challenges. Henrikki will share his insights into how lasers can contribute to the future growth of e-Mobility.

The Next Frontier in Metal AM Brian Victor nLIGHT

Brian will introduce a new fiber laser beam shaping technology. It’s capable of a single-mode Gaussian beam for printing fine features and a small multimode ring beam for building bulk features faster.

Plus, connect with industry professionals and experience cutting-edge technology demos from [MIGHT, Coherent, and Fraunhofer USA, and 20+ exhibitors promoting the latest technology and innovations.
