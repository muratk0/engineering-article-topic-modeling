## Eagle fiber laser flies high

MSS LASERS is expanding its capabilities by offering the Eagle range of fiber laser machines, sharing its knowledge and experience in a close partnership with the manufacturer.

“Machines offered come as standard with linear drives in all axes, acceleration up to 6g and cutting speeds of over 100m/min. A combination of high speed processing and table-change times of less than 10 seconds make the Eagle machines one of the most advanced and productive fiber laser cutting machines available,” the company told ISMR.

“For maximum versatility, customers can choose laser power from 2kW to 6kW and bed sizes of up to 6m x 2.5m. Accuracy is assured through the rigidity of a carbon fiber bridge and the dynamic polymer concrete frame, minimising vibration and heat-related deflection of the machine geometry.”
