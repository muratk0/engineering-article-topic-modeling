## Cutting across sectors

"We are equally at home cutting components for local general engineering clients, as we are working on intricate decorative designs for interior designers and architects or producing parts to the demanding specifications and industry guidelines set by our global, blue chip defence customers," said Lasered Components Ltd.

For example, in the architecture and construction sector, its flourishing partnerships with architects, interior designers, interior specialists, engineers, fitters and fabricators is based on an intimate understanding of their needs and an eye for laser cutting innovation.

"We have laser cut everything from one-off decorative metalwork pieces and artworks; signage and panelling; components for staircases and balustrading, communal entrance doors to vaulted ceilings and external building cladding. Lasered Components' work features in some of the UK's most iconic buildings, restaurants, hotels, shops and offices, including The Savoy, The Shard, London Olympic 2012 projects, all of London's airports and from shopping centres to social housing projects," added the company.

From laser cutting stainless steel parts for prototypes within the mobility sector, to small laser cutting batch work for motor sport and high-performance vehicles, to tail lifts, truck bodies and componentry used in agriculture, heavy engineering, marine, military and rail transport, the company has just finished its financial year, recording a 27% increase on the previous 12-month period, which in turn was 40% up on the year before.

Lasered Components produces high-integrity close-tolerance laser-cut components. It also has an established customer base in the electronics and security industries and is a preferred laser cutting partner to contractors and subcontractors in the energy and water industries.

"One of our niche specialities is laser cutting parts for global providers of advanced equipment and systems for the food processing industry. Our work in the medical sector is many and varied, from producing components used in the manufacture of hospital medicine trollies through to vital monitoring equipment where safety critical applications are of paramount importance," added the company.

The manufacturer also cuts parts for the furniture, lighting, retail and consumer goods sectors.
