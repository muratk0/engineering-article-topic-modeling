LED illuminates surfaces for powder coating

Nordson Industrial Coating Systems

has introduced nLighten™, an LED light that can be added to the company’s Encore manual powder-spray gun. It illuminates the surface being coated to help improve powder application accuracy and allow inspection and approval of work on-the-fly.

The light helps the user highlight and fix light powder areas on surfaces, deep recesses, and cavities. The increased light helps enable precisely targeted powder application to help reduce waste and save on material costs. The product is rated for use in all powder coating environments.

Torch for carbon arc gouging features pneumatic jaw

Arcair

®

, an

ESAB

brand, has introduced the AirPro™ X4000 torch and cable. When the operator pushes the rocker switch, compressed air already flowing through the torch opens the torch jaws to allow insertion, adjustment, and removal of carbons. The lever assembly is eliminated, creating a lower torch profile.

The rocker switch also controls the compressed air on/off function so that no air flows through the torch or cable without the operator initiating the flow. When in use, a latch mechanism locks the rocker switch in position to help reduce hand fatigue. The no-leak air valve saves electricity and maintenance costs of the shop air compressor, says the company.

The torch and cable are rated for use with 1,000-amp systems. The torch accepts 3/16- to ½-in. pointed round, ⅜-in. and ⅝-in. flat, and ⅝-in. half-round carbons.

Sheet plate bending machine accommodates four-, three-roll bending

MG SRL offers the K series sheet plate bending machine for four-roll or three-roll bending. The minimal straight part on the machine—1.8 times the thickness of the sheet processed—now is even shorter. The machine is a variant of the PH model, with variable geometry.

The machine is equipped with the NC Touch Command EVO control, which monitors the bending process. The operator enters the sheet parameters using the touchscreen, and the control generates the program. The system monitors the coordinates to ensure they are executed accurately and corrects any errors.

For multidiameter processes, the speeds of the interpolated movements are selected automatically and controlled in all conditions in real time. The control allows wireless communication, interconnection, data collection and analysis, and the visualization of the desired shape in 3D graphics.

Industrial dust collector handles fine, heavy dusts

The new Gold Series X-Flo industrial dust collector from

Camfil APC

is suitable for industrial applications that produce or process fine, fibrous, and heavy dusts and fumes.

The unit is designed in modules, each of which handles airflows up to 6,000 CFM using four Gold Cone™ X-Flo filter cartridges. The cartridges have more pleated media and surface area than before, so they can move more air and process more dust without increasing the collector’s overall footprint.

The collector also features a newly engineered inlet and baffle configuration that creates a more uniform airflow, which extends the life of the filters. When the filters are pulse-cleaned, more dust is channeled directly into the hopper instead of into the adjacent filters.

Fiber laser cuts thin, thick materials without lens change

The Ensis series uses

Amada’s

proprietary fiber laser technology to process both thin and thick materials without a cutting lens change or manual setup. The 9-kW fiber engine incorporates technology that optimizes the laser mode and beam parameter product based on the material thickness being processed. The fiber laser achieves high-speed cutting in thin material and can process thick plate efficiently, says the company.

The AMS 3015 CL provides flexible automation that reduces unnecessary laser machine downtime caused by manual material loading.
