Hypertherm's President and CEO, Evan A. Smith, and regional director Europe, Theo Cornielje, outlined key initiatives to ISMR at EuroBLECH.

Hypertherm introduced a new generation cutting system in its Powermax® line of light industrial plasma cutters, advanced technologies for high-end automated plasma cutting and a new company vision "Shaping Possibility" at its stand at EuroBLECH 2016 this October.

All the creations and winners can be viewed on Hypertherm's EMEA Facebook page.

ISMR took the opportunity, at this year's EuroBLECH, to discover more about its company philosophy with its President and CEO, Evan A. Smith, and regional director, Theo Cornielje.

## Industry 4.0 in focus

Hypertherm designs and manufactures advanced cutting products for use in a variety of industries such as shipbuilding, manufacturing, and automotive repair. Its product line includes plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, in addition to CNC motion and height controls, CAM nesting software, robotic software and consumables. The 100 per cent associate-owned company has more than 1,400 associates along with operations and partner representation worldwide.

"EuroBLECH 2016 was an innovative and very successful event for us" said Theo Cornielje, Regional Director, EMEA region. "We integrated our new company vision 'Shaping Possibility' into our new stand including advanced live and online connectivity with our regional headquarters in The Netherlands, as well as interactive tables on our stand. These innovative technologies make it possible to explain our technologies in depth, show the system capabilities and guide visitors to the solution that best fits their cutting needs by using the principles of industry 4.0."

Advanced HyPerformance® plasma technologies, including SureCut™ applications like True Hole® and True Bevel™ as well as HyPrecision™ Waterjet cutting, were demonstrated by making use of advanced connectivity options that support Industry 4.0 applications. A live connection with Hypertherm's Cutting Technology Centre at its EMEA headquarters in The Netherlands, via High Definition live stream, was initiated. In addition to the cutting schedule, visitors could also request demonstrations.

"We tried to think of ways that we could demonstrate cutting in an innovative way. These live, interactive demonstrations were sparked by a Russian customer who asked if we could arrange a remote cutting demonstration, live, for him from our base in the Netherlands. He asked questions during the demonstration, it was a very interactive experience and nobody needed to travel. We have brought that concept here to our booth at EuroBLECH, showing cutting demonstrations live from our demo centre on our screen here. It's an exciting way to demonstrate our capability, in line with the digital age and Industry 4.0, which are the buzz words at EuroBLECH this year."

Delivering 6.5 kilowatts of output power, the newly launched Powermax45 XP plasma cutting system is extra powerful, allowing it to cleanly cut material of up to 22mm in thickness, and sever material up to 31mm. A new low-amp process makes it easy to mark, score and make precision gouges, while the lighter design makes it more portable. Eleven different torch styles for handheld and mechanized use, plus a variety of consumable types for specialised cutting and gouging applications, are available.

The Powermax45 XP plasma cutting system comes with innovations such as a new Duramax™ Lock torch with a 'torch disable' switch for quick replacement of consumables, a FastConnect™ torch to quickly switch between different torches and Smart Sense™ technology that automatically adjusts gas pressure and flow.

"We continue to upgrade our cutting systems – waterjet, plasma or automated plasma – and you can see this here on our booth at EuroBLECH," explained Evan A. Smith, Hypertherm President and CEO. "We are also expanding applications for the small versatile Powermax45 XP (such as marking, gouging and special applications). Our heavy automated plasma systems will receive a generational upgrade next year.

Users want greater flexibility in their equipment

"Beyond those core generational renewals, the themes on our booth here include application expansion. Users want greater flexibility in their equipment – the key benefit of plasma is that it is the lowest operating cost cutting technology available. Bringing that operating cost advantage to a broader set of applications, in environments where customers are more focused on competitiveness, is key."

Besides all the new products and technologies, the company also featured its HyPrecision Waterjet cutting products, CAM nesting software line, automation products as well as torch and consumable technologies from Hypertherm and its Centricut® and AccuStream® brands. The company has also introduced a lower-amp SilverPlus® electrode. This new electrode, for the HyPerformance family of plasma cutting systems, is designed for cutting at 80 amps.

## Robotics and software

"Our emphasis on solutions toolkits for users means helping customers to actually apply the technology and, increasingly, there is a software emphasis here. You can see this with our robotic software for offline programming. Some of the biggest barriers to integrating different cutting technologies with robotics is complex curve following and the difficulty and tediousness of programming robot arms to apply cutting technology with the right parameters. That is a key area of investment for us and we hope to accelerate that application development with our end users and key partners," explained Evan A. Smith.

Hypertherm is also shipping a new CNC platform, called EDGE® Connect. This next-generation CNC contains a number of enhancements for operator ease of use and customised performance. New features include the latest version of Hypertherm's Phoenix™ software (coupled with SureCut technology) and a software-based operator's console and embedded PLC feature called PLC Connect™ for greater customisation. Enhanced CNC-based nesting enables convenient operator programming with SureCut outcomes via ProNest™ CNC and three new modern hardware options are designed to improve performance, reduce weight and increase reliability.

## An eye on the future

"We are a US-headquartered company so our largest market is North America, including Mexico and Canada. EMEA is our second largest market (about a quarter of our turnover), followed by Asia, China and South America," explained Evan A. Smith.

"A good expansion area for us this year has been waterjet, as customers continue to cut a variety of materials (thin and thick), including high-strength materials. We are very involved with the end-users of all our products as it is important for us to understand their needs and requirements, now and in the future. It helps us to decide where we should be focusing and improving our technology, as well as understand the applications that customers are using and how to improve them. We take a solutions approach and try to gauge what customers actually need, rather than just trying to sell them products," concluded Smith.
