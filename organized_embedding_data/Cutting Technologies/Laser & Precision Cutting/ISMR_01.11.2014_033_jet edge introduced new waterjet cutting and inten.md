# Jet Edge introduced new waterjet cutting and intensifier pump products to the European market at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Founded in 1984, Jet Edge has grown to become one of the largest manufacturers of ultra-high pressure waterjet technology in the world. Jet Edge waterjets are used in a wide range of industries around the world, including automotive, aerospace, defence, industrial manufacturing, food production, and machine and job shops. Its extensive product line includes precision waterjet cutting systems, waterjet pumps, and mobile waterjet cutting and surface preparation systems. This year, it is celebrating its 30th anniversary.

Minnesota Business magazine recently awarded it the ‘2014 Minnesota Manufacturing – Best in Class Small Company Award’. Jet Edge was one of three manufacturers contending for the award, which recognizes a small company (1-50 employees) for overall excellence in the manufacturing industry in Minnesota.

## Reaching new markets

“Our main message is that we are one of the few waterjet manufacturers that can supply a customised solution tailored to their application. We are willing to customise and integrate pumps onto any other machines (not just our own brand of machines).

We are also differentiated because we make our own waterjet machines and pumps,” explained Nancy Lauseng, Marketing Manager, Jet Edge.

“We have had a very successful year this year and have been working for the last few years to develop distributorships around the world in key markets – this is really starting to take off now. Europe, Asia and the Middle East are key regions. As this year is also our 30th anniversary, we invited our customers and international distributors to our ribbon-cutting event at JetEdge headquarters. We are a family-owned company and have always tried to focus on ease of operation, maintenance and training for our customers. We offer free training every other month to our customers.”

To celebrate its 30th anniversary, Jet Edge refreshed its website to offer web content in four languages. Its goal for next year is to expand the number of languages for its website to French, Spanish and Japanese. It is expanding its operations across the world and wants to reach as many new markets as possible.

At EuroBLECH this year, Jet Edge introduced a new 6200-bar ultra HP tensile bar pump with a 37kW motor, capable of producing 2.6 litres/minute, to the European market. It also makes a 100hp (75kW) pump that can produce 5.1 litres/minute.

“This is the latest technology in waterjet cutting which, combined with our A-Jet Star motion system with 5-axis head and taper compensation, gives us the best edge possible at the fastest cutting and feed rates (we believe it is the highest performance available at the market),” explained David J Anderson, International Sales Director, Jet Edge. “It also reduces the cost of consumables because of the high pressure involved (i.e. the amount of garnet required to make the cut) to between 30 and 40% reduction in garnet consumption and between 30-40% increase in cutting speed across a range of materials.”

Jet Edge's EDGE X-5® 5-axis waterjet system with Aquavision Di Controller cuts complex taper-free and 3D parts from virtually any material. Featuring its innovative Permalign EDGE cutting head technology, the EDGE X-5 dramatically increases waterjet cutting capabilities by allowing users to produce chamfers, weld bevels and sophisticated 3D parts such as impeller blades with a taper-free finish. The EuroBLECH debut was the first time that Jet Edge had introduced the X-5 to the European market.

“We are continuously trying to increase the longevity of our components. We are making significant changes to our intensifier pumps to improve their longevity,” explained David J Anderson. “We recently received a US patent for metal-on-metal seal technology for the development of seal life for our Xtreme pump (eliminating the number of seals that need to be replaced). We will eventually retrofit this to all our 60,000psi pump lines.”

“We are also doing everything we can to reach customers in other countries by tailoring our website in other languages, offering local representation, good service/spare parts availability, training and after-sales service. Our mission is to help our customers solve their manufacturing challenges and achieve their highest level of productivity, precision and accuracy.”

For further information, see www.jetedge.com. ■

ISMR talks to Christoph Neudörffer, managing director of STEINEL Normalien AG, about the company’s presence at EuroBLECH

The rebranded STEINEL was present at EuroBLECH 2014 with a booth showcasing its tooling, tapping and guide unit products and technologies. ISMR caught up with Christoph Neudörffer, managing director of STEINEL Normalien AG, on its booth in Hanover.

“We are offering the complete range of tooling products necessary for toolmakers in stamping and pressing industries, such as guiding units and highend tooling for high speed presses,” he told ISMR. “We consider ourselves as a system provider of complete solutions for a range of customer needs and are no longer just offering products from a catalogue. It’s about offering added value to our customers. Just selling a simple product from a catalogue is not enough. We have therefore changed our strategy so that we offer complete solutions to customers.

“We have developed from a supplier of standard parts to a system provider of punching and bending technology. We are still viewed as provider of high quality standard parts but now also as an experienced partner when it comes to design and construction of complete tools. Our customers attach importance to our expert opinion and advice, which makes us especially happy.”

## Solutions, not products

STEINEL’s booth at EuroBLECH made use of descriptive exhibit presentations, videos and 3D animations. It also advertised its new partnership with Bihler. Its most important achievement this year, said Neudörffer, was Christian Pape, Head of Sales and Marketing, STEINEL Normalien AG, and Christoph Neudörffer, Managing Director, STEINEL Normalien AG, (left to right) the integration of the ‘complete tool chain divisions’ of the company into the rest of the company.

“This has led us to a new philosophy in the company – solution-finding, not just provision of parts. We wanted to integrate the different parts of the company into one entity, STEINEL, so that we present just one integrated face to the customer (rather than Steinel Normalien etc.). We are still refining this and focusing strongly on our products, but I believe we will reach more customers this year.”

In addition to high-quality standard parts, STEINEL also offers nitrogen cylinders, manifold plates and tapping units. It also produces sophisticated active elements and tool assemblies at its secondary location in Buching. This is additional evidence that the transition to becoming a system provider has been completed. During the design and construction of modular technology progressive tools for its SP series, Bihler relied on STEINEL experience and expertise. The machine builder also recommends the experts from Villingen-Schwenningen and Buching to its customers when it comes to tool adaptations of existing tools to the new NC machines.

“As our technologies are getting increasingly complex, 3D animations and videos on our EuroBLECH booth are more effective in conveying the services we provide, rather than demonstrations at the machine,” said sales manager, Christian Pape. “By using animations, we wanted to give our visitors exciting insights that they could not get from a machine in operation, and we succeeded in doing that. We noticed, for example, that even though guide units are part of our customers' daily operations, our 3D animations were able to provide information on the special features of STEINEL guide units with which non-STEINEL customers, in particular, were not familiar. A STEINEL tool in an SP500 servo press could be seen live at the Bihler booth.

"We were spot on with our trade show emphasis on modular technology and tool adaptation. There was tremendous interest in our modular tool concept that we presented for the first time at the EuroBLECH two years ago. The module systems, which have been perfected since then, have successfully been implemented by various companies and help them to reduce design, construction and set-up times while preventing set-up errors. We were able to demonstrate how that works with the help of a 3D animation and exhibited tools. There was also a lot of interest in the adaptation of existing tools to the new Bihler NC machines. In this context, I think we were able to convince trade fair visitors of our expertise in this area and get them excited about our service package."

## Looking to the future

“It has been a good year for STEINEL this year in terms of sales,” concluded Christoph Neudörffer. “We have established a lot of good relationships for the future and we have some strong products in the pipeline. I am optimistic about next year’s prospects. Our main target is not quick levels of growth – we are more interested in sustainable, steady and solid levels of business.
