# An eye on tube

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

BLM GROUP is appearing at EuroBLECH 2016 with a range of technology in tube and sheet metal manufacturing and fabrication (stand B36, hall 12).

The new ADIGE-SYS LC5 machine is a combined cutting system for metal sheet and tubes. LC5 can be further enhanced with fully automatic loading and unloading systems. The LT8.10 lasertube machine features both a fibre laser source and tube cutting head for, says BLM, 'extremely fast and accurate 3D cutting'. The machine is supplied with "Active Tools", a range of special functions that are specifically programmed to automatically solve challenging problems.

The concept of "All-In-One" is encapsulated in BLM's ELECT80 bending system, the 5-axis LT-FREE laser cutting system which can perform a laser process on an already bent piece.

"In our special demo room, we will feature our proprietary suite of software entitled 'BLMelements', which is a set of tools for designing parts, estimating production times, creating part programs, planning production and, if need be, integrating with other BLM GROUP machines. The software enables remote machine monitoring, the collection of operating statistics and receives realtime notifications of events that may require attention," said BLM.

BLM GROUP is a global specialist in tube and sheet metal processing solutions. Its product line includes laser cutting, cold sawing, bending, end machining and wirebending machines.
