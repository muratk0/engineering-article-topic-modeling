Fluid reclamation machine combines sump cleaning, tramp oil removal

The Eriez redesigned SumpDoc™ portable, inline fluid reclamation machine combines sump cleaning and tramp oil removal in one package. It serves as an alternative to a central fluid recycling system or batch processing, supplying coolant restoration and rejuvenation treatment of metalworking fluids in a machine tool sump with minimal operator interaction.

There is no interruption in the production cycle or need to transport fluids. The portable unit can be wheeled next to a machine tool, parts washer, or rinse tank to provide full-service fluid reclamation in a two-step process.

Fiber laser sequentially powers two workstations

The Coherent HighLight™ FL-ARM with Fiber-Fiber-Switch is a 2- to 8-kW, dual- fiber-output laser that can sequentially power two separate workstations or processes. Dual-fiber output delivers increased throughput in high-volume welding applications, particularly in automotive manufacturing, such as welding of doors, hang-on parts, ultrahigh-strength steel components, and aluminum body frames. Alternately, the second fiber can act as a redundant backup for the first, thus minimizing service downtime when required.

The process fibers use the manufacturer’s Adjustable Ring Mode configuration, comprising a dual-core delivery fiber that produces a central spot surrounded by another concentric ring of laser light. The power levels in the center and the ring can be independently adjusted on demand from 1 to 100%.

Automation system add-on helps process individual laser-cut parts

Bystronic has introduced the BySort add-on for the ByTrans Cross automation system for loading/unloading laser cutting systems. The add-on is designed for processing large series of individual cut parts that need to be unloaded and sorted separately according to jobs.
