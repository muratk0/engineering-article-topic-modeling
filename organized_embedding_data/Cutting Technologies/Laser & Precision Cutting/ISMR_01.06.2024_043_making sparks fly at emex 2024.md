# Making sparks fly at EMEX 2024

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Two leading businesses in the laser-cutting industry converged at New Zealand's largest industrial trade event, EMEX 2024, to offer a holistic solution that cuts through inefficiencies for their metal fabrication job shop. International machinery dealer, Intermach, was at EMEX Stand 3090 from 28-30 May 2024 at Auckland Showgrounds.

Intermach is a versatile New Zealand-based machinery trading company, specialising in both new and used laser-cutting, fabrication and engineering machinery from leading brands. Australian-based laser cutting quoting company, Tempus Tools, was also on the Intermach stand for customers who have decided to purchase a new machine and want to look at software to streamline operations and increase their quoting efficiency.

Tempus Tools is the designer of ToolBox, its cloud-based laser cutting quoting software. ToolBox can be set up with information on material price, cutting time, labour and other relevant information to generate quotes quickly. ToolBox, said Tempus Tools, typically cuts the time taken to respond to quotes by more than half, and frees up skilled staff for other production priorities.

With more than 100 years of combined experience in its leadership team, extending from the job shop floor to the formation of entire laser-cutting enterprises, Tempus Tools designed ToolBox to not only increase laser-cutting quoting efficiency but also to integrate with other software programs and to perform a range of functions in one, saving the need to purchase multiple subscriptions or programs.■
