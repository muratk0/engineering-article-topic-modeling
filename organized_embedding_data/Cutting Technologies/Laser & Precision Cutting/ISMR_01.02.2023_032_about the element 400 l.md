# About the ELEMENT 400 L

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

The ELEMENT L is a flexible solution for laser cutting applications which can process XXL sheets using little manpower. With the Bevel-U, precise and repeatable bevel parts can be produced which is advantageous for edge preparation and especially for automated welding.

“With the Element 400 L, working widths of between 1,600mm and 5,100mm and a working length of up to 25,000mm are possible. It cuts sheet steel up to a thickness of 40mm. With positioning speeds of up to 140m/min. and high acceleration (combination of X and Y axes), it is very fast and, thanks to the linear guides in the X and Y axes, has a high level of positioning accuracy," Messer Cutting Systems told ISMR.

“Up to six tools can be integrated into the ELEMENT 400 L, including automatic tool selection and positioning. The user can also combine the latest laser technology (maximum of two stations) with advanced plasma cutting technology," it added. The Laser Nozzle Control (LNC) quickly automates set-up work for every material, thickness and process - without manual intervention. Nozzle quality is regularly checked, and the nozzle cleaned to ensure high process reliability. In the event of a defect or a different sheet thickness, the nozzle is automatically replaced. The concentricity of the laser beam to the nozzle is checked and scanning is automatically calibrated for increased cutting quality and reduced set-up times.

“Global Connect control software can process production orders directly, especially in combination with the products from the OmniFab family. It offers a simple and clear user interface and protection against operating errors. It also provides fast access to a wide range of production data, minimizing unplanned downtime and operator errors, reducing paper and material handling inefficiencies and labour," concluded Messer Cutting Systems.
