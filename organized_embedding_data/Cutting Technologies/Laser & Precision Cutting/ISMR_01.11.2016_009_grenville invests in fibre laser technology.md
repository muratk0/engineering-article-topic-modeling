# Grenville invests in fibre laser technology

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

UK-BASED GRENVILLE ENGINEERING HAS invested in the Bystronic BySprint 3015 fibre laser to provide an enhanced service offering for its customers. Acquiring the new fibre laser unlocks new capabilities and the option of cutting new materials such as brass and copper. The Bystronic laser can cut up to 20mm mild steel, 12mm stainless and aluminium including 6mm in brass and copper.

The fibre laser will be supported by a second investment of two CNC press brakes to be installed early in December. The company is expected to reach a £4m turnover by the end of 2016 with further growth in the region of £5m - £6m predicted over the next three years.

Established over 30 years ago, Grenville Engineering Ltd offers design and prototyping; metal cutting; machining; drilling; pressing and forming; welding; assembly; grinding; deburring; final assembly and delivery. See www.grenville-engineering.co.uk

Nucor completes new acquisition

Above: The Grenville Engineering team with its BySprint 3015 fibre laser

NUCOR CORPORATION HAS closed on its acquisition of Independence Tube Corporation for approximately US$ 435 million.

“We are pleased to welcome the 335 team-mates at Independence Tube to the Nucor family," said John Ferriola, Chairman, CEO and President of Nucor Corporation.

Independence Tube is a leading manufacturer of Hollow Structural Section (HSS) steel tubing and has the second highest market share in HSS, selling its products primarily through service centres, which are also an important channel to market for Nucor products. Doug Jellison will serve as President of Nucor Tubular Products.■
