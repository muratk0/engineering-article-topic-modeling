The laser cutting machine forever changed the way sheet metal parts were fabricated. Whereas the first CNC punching machines delivered newfound efficiencies that few could have ever believed possible at the time, the laser revolutionized the industry. No tooling. No waiting. No turning back.

Today, the laser cutting machine is the workhorse of the modern metal fabricating shop. Nothing can match its combination of cutting speed and quality.

The technology also continues to change the way fabricating companies think about how they go about their business. With the increase in fiber laser power sources, more shops are using their laser cutting machines to process plate, not just sheet metal.
