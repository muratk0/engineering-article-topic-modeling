# Innovations in tube cutting

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

CYTUBE is CY-Laser's fibre-laser system designed for cutting tubes with different profiles (round, rectangular, square or other standard profiles with a diameter from a minimum of 15mm to a maximum of 225mm).

“In its new version, CYTUBE has become a modular system that allows customer to solve issues related to available space, avoiding the need to choose a customised machine with particularly high costs. The choice to equip CYT5 and CYT9, the two versions of CYTUBE, with a modular structure means, in fact, reducing costs not only for the customer but also for the manufacturer, while maintaining high quality and reliability," said the Italian manufacturer.

Its main features include automatic scrap conveyor; automatic setting of the clamps with adjustment of the jaws for reductions in production change-over times; adaptive grip of the tube with automatic adjustment for more effective closure on materials of different thicknesses and more.

"The heart of CYTUBE is the process cell, while tube handling is its key automation feature since it allows users to manipulate the tube in the process cell by guiding it with the movements necessary to complete all planned processes," confirmed the Italian laser-cutting specialist.■
