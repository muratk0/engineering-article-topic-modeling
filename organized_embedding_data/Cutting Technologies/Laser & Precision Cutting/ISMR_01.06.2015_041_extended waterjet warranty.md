# Extended waterjet warranty

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

WATER JET SWEDEN has introduced an extended machine warranty. It guarantees brand new machine performance after five years of usage.

“Our new warranty is unique for the industry,” said Lennart Svensson, CEO of Water Jet Sweden AB. “It is made possible by our world patent guide system and robust machine design. This makes our machines unique. In practice, we promise something that our machines have proved for decades.

“No frame can be made 100 per cent straight and parallel. When the X-beam moves along the Y-axis, irregularities and thermal impact will have a harmful effect on the cutting result and the life span of the machine. This influence will increase with the length of the cutting table. Traditional designs try to cope with this problem by tolerating a compensating slack along the guide rail – thereby still compromising both cutting accuracy and the life span of the machine.”

The patented solution used for all WJS machines eliminates this problem by using a parallel-axes system, where one side of the machine is fixed and the other will adjust itself continuously. Wear on mechanical parts is reduced considerably, calibration is facilitated and consistent and strong cutting quality can be obtained, said WJS, regardless of machine size.

The extended warranty covers all Water Jet Sweden’s gantry machine models ordered after 1 May 2015 and covers the frame and moving parts of the mechanical system. It spans five years or 10,000 hours of operation plus service and yearly maintenance by technicians authorised by Water Jet Sweden.
