# Contour Cut technology

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

AT THE EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Kjellberg Finsterwalde demonstrated precision plasma cutting of small contours with Contour Cut, and of large contours up to 50 per cent faster using Contour Cut Speed. Contour Cut Speed is the latest, patented plasma cutting technology from Kjellberg Finsterwalde for the cutting of outer contours and larger inner contours in steel. Contour Cut technology cuts smaller contours, in particular holes with a diameter/material thickness ration of 1:1 to high quality levels.

“CCS uses the same consumables and enriches the existing Contour Cut technology with a number of significant economic advantages, as there are reduced cost per metre cuts as a result of higher cutting speeds in the quality area, very low heat-affected zone, high quality in terms of contour accuracy, perpendicularity and surface quality as well as further reduction of angular deviation for inner and outer contours and excellent repeat and dimensional accuracy,” said the manufacturer.

Contour Cut Speed is integrated as a standard feature of all new plasma cutting units in the HiFocus series. Users who already have a system with Contour Cut can also use CCS by getting an update. Older HiFocus systems can also be retrofitted with the new plasma torches of the PerCut 200 and 450 series to take advantage of Contour Cut technology. www.kjellberg.de

## PASS Clean Cut

SLITTING PERFORATIONS with no undesirable nibbling marks are now possible with a new slitting tool, PASS Clean Cut, developed by PASS Stanztechnik AG. This solution can be used on TRUMPF machines as well as thick turret punching tool systems, regardless of machine tool manufacturer.

Undesirable nibbling marks occur with standard conventional slitting tools. For producing long slitting lines, nibbling marks can be prevented through continuous shear slitting over a series of hits. In the first action of the tool, the punched material will remain attached.

During the process of cutting long slitting lines, a shear-style cutting action is used so that the punched material will be punched free at the side but remains as a sheared part on the sheet. The punched material is bent with the PASS Clean Cut and will be transferred into a second section of the die where a separate shear cuts the material into a small slug. The sheared slugs are transferred directly through the die. On the last pass of the slitting line, the cutting tool will cut the remaining material completely from the sheet at the desired position. The tool works progressively forward without time-wasting back-hits to complete the process. Slitting is possible up to 2mm near the sheet edge.

PASS Clean Cut can be used on aluminium sheets of up to 2mm, steel up to 1.5mm and stainless steel of up to 1.2mm.
