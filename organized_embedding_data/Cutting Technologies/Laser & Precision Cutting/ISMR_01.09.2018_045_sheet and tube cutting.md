# Sheet and tube cutting

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

POLISH MANUFACTURER, KIMLA, designs and produces a range of rapid CNC machines (such as fibre laser cutting systems and waterjet cutting systems) which, it told ISMR, are stable and easy to operate. With a 23-year history, it has installed more than 2500 machines globally. "Our flatbed laser is now available in a hybrid machine. Offering tube cutting as well as full flatbed production, this machine is suitable for manufacturers who do not have the space to install two separate machines. With a single laser source and cutting head, the switch between flatbed cutting and tube is seamless and as quick as a normal pallet change. Designed with a special material feeding system and separate receiving pallet, it allows automatic unloading of finished workpieces. The overall design is very compact with one of the smallest footprints on the market today," added Kimla.
