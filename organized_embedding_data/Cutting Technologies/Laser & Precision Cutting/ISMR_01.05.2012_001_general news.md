## Jean Gallay SA orders fiber laser

PRIMA POWER Laserdyne, a supplier of precision multi-axis laser machining systems, confirmed that Jean Gallay SA (Geneva, Switzerland) has ordered a LASERDYNE 795XL BeamDirector system equipped with a fiber laser.

“By choosing the LASERDYNE 795XL system with the fiber laser, Jean Gallay is making a breakthrough in laser processing,” reported Madam Laurence de la Serna, CEO. “The investment was the most important capital investment in 2011 for us. It demonstrates the desire of our company to grow, especially in the aerospace market, which is forecasted to show strong growth in Europe for several years, despite the recent downturn in commerce in European countries.”

Jean Gallay products are used in industrial gas turbines, aircraft engines, helicopter turbines and nuclear reactors. The company will use the LASERDYNE 795XL equipped with a 2kW fiber laser for 2D and 3D processing of heat-resistant alloys (nickel and cobalt), stainless steel alloys, titanium and titanium alloys. Typical parts include challenging designs for combustion chambers, diffusers, burners for gas turbines and many others. A LASERDYNE 795XL system is capable of controlling seven axes of motion with a 2m x 1m x 1m (80 inch x 40 inch x 40 inch) work envelope that can include two rotary tables and LASERDYNE’s latest controller, the S94P.
