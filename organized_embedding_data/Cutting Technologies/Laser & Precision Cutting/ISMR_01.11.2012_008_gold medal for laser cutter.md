# Gold medal for laser cutter

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

A FLATBED laser cutting machine integrated with a 400W fiber laser from JK Lasers has been awarded a gold medal and diploma at the 2012 International Technical Fair, Plovdiv, Bulgaria. Designed and manufactured by ISL Photonics, a division of IKIS, the Gladus400 impressed show judges with its manufacturing performance.

The Gladus400 can be fitted with any JK fiber laser to precision cut sheet metals such as aluminium and steel at speed. It also features an integrated chiller, filter and extraction system, making it easy to install into most industrial premises. The size of the cutting table can be adapted to meet individual needs.

“Our new ISL Photonics division is now integrating JK fiber lasers into a range of general purpose flatbed cutting machines that we hope will serve many markets for years to come” said IKIS.

JK Lasers is a global company that provides industrial laser solutions for the medical device, aerospace, automotive, electronics and semiconductor industries. IKIS is a manufacturer of light fittings and has been established for almost 15 years.

This year’s International Technical Fair, held in Plovdiv, Bulgaria between 24 and 29 September, showcased the latest developments from a range of companies, including those specialising in the machine building, electronics, electrical engineering and construction industries.
