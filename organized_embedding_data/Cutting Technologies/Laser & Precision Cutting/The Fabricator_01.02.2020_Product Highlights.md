Part sorting automation available for laser-cut parts

Mazak Optonics Corp. has introduced an automated part sorting system for laser-processed parts. From changing pallets to sorting finished parts, the system helps improve material flow through the fabrication production line.

Available for 3015 and 4020 laser machine formats, it has a modular design that allows for expansion and customized unloading positions. The system can be added to an existing Laser Flex automation system. Finished parts are stacked on pallets by order or by part type.

Available functions and enhancements include unloading conveyors, over/under carts, mini unload tower, and labelers.

Fiber laser processes complex contours and thin to thick sheets

TRUMPF offers the TruLaser 5030 fiber, equipped with the 10-kW TruDisk, to process mild steel up to 1.2 in., stainless steel up to 1.5 in., and aluminum up to 1.2 in. The machine’s patented High-speed Eco cutting technology helps minimize assist gas consumption using the company’s patented touchdown nozzle.

The machine processes complex contours and thin to thick sheets. The BrightLine fiber, CoolLine, and PierceLine functions help ensure high-quality laser processing in thicker materials. The Drop&Cut feature lets operators use part skeletons for additional production. They see a live image of the machine’s interior on the control unit’s user interface. From the control unit, the operator clicks or taps the screen to drag the programmed contour to the desired location on the projected sheet and initiates the cut.

The Smart Nozzle Automation cleans and inspects the nozzle before use. The new QuickSwap feature lets operators replenish nozzles during the cutting process without interruption via a drawer that extends outside of the machine. This helps minimize setup time and operator intervention.

Suction and blowoff robots eliminate water pockets during finishing prep

LestaUSA has developed suction and blowoff robots for eliminating moisture on parts during the finishing preparation process. The robots eliminate water pockets before the parts enter the dryoff oven.

The robots do not require complicated programming or teach pendants. While in a weightless learning mode, a finishing line attendant guides the robot through the appropriate sequence to remove moisture, while the software creates its own robotic code of every movement.

Mechanized welding system helps lower cost per joint

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has launched its Pipeweld Orbiter mechanized welding system and a complete line of Pipeweld consumables. Designed for welding pipeline tie-ins and special sections such as road and river crossings, the system can lower cost per joint by 40%, according to the company.

After completing the root and hot pass with cellulosic electrodes, the system automates the fill and cap passes using flux-cored electrodes. Another procedure is to use downhill, low-hydrogen electrodes for the first fill pass, then the welding system for the remaining passes. The mechanized system completes joints faster because uphill flux-cored welding offers deposition rates up to 9 lbs. per hour.

The system comprises a lightweight bug and a fast-fit band that clamps on pipe with diameters greater than 8 in. The bug contains the controls, wire spool holder, wire feed mechanism, torch, bug drive mechanism, and cables and hoses, all connected as one unit. The same bug works on both sides of the pipe.

Fiber laser cutting system equipped with beam-shaping technology

Amada has introduced what it says is the first fiber laser cutting system equipped with beam-shaping technology. The 4-kW Ventis 3015 AJ fiber laser cutting system uses Locus Beam Control (LBC) to achieve infinite locus patterns, helping ensure optimum beam-shape control for a variety of materials and thicknesses.

LBC technology enables cutting speeds up to three times faster than a conventional 4-kW fiber laser, says the company. By combining two mirrors and a collimation system that uses high-velocity vibration, the technology achieves high-speed, high-quality cutting.
