June 6-8, 2016, The Inn at St. John’s Conference Center, Plymouth (Detroit), Mich.

The theme for this year’s ALAW is Lasers: Efficiency and Strength for Lightweighting. As the use of lasers becomes more widespread, product designers and engineers are using various metals to lightweight end products during the laser welding process. They must also learn how to design components, assemblies, and systems for the successful implementation of these lightweight parts. These topics are addressed in depth, along with practical examples and case studies to help build a foundation in laser design and implementation.

This workshop is designed for metal fabrication job shops, manufacturers, suppliers, automotive and aerospace engineers, and anyone interested in learning more about laser material processing systems to reduce costs, improve quality, save material and reduce weight, and provide flexible laser manufacturing.

In addition to keynote presentations by the steel industry on Tuesday and aluminum industry on Wednesday, there will be over 20 presentations on the latest topics in laser welding of multimaterials, remote laser welding, lightweighting, green lasers, hybrid manufacturing, and laser additive manufacturing, just to name a few.

There will be the ALAW tradition of two world-class open houses featuring live welding and cutting demos at Fraunhofer USA Center for Laser Applications and TRUMPF’s Laser Technology Center. Registration for both open houses is free to ALAW attendees.

New this year are plant tours of Laser Mechanisms Inc. in Novi and Precitec in Wixom. Both tours on Monday are sponsored by nLIGHT with transportation provided. Registration for both tours is free to ALAW attendees and students. This will be the second year for the popular Lasers 101 certificate course held on Monday, prior to the plant tours.

Attendees will also have the opportunity to network during the receptions and visit the following tabletop exhibitors (confirmed as of 3/18/16) during the refreshment breaks: Blackbird Robotics, Fraunhofer USA, Genesis Systems Group, KUKA Systems USA, Laserline Inc., Laser Mechanisms Inc., Ophir Laser Measurement Group, Plasmo USA, Precitec Inc., and TRUMPF USA.
