Established in 1984, Jet Edge is a global designer and manufacturer of waterjet systems for precision cutting, surface preparation and coating removal. In March 2015, it introduced its new iP60-30 60,000psi (4100bar), 30hp (22kw) hydraulic waterjet intensifier pump. The iP60-30 draws 37 full load amps at 460vac (60Hz) or 74 full load amps at 230vac (60Hz). It is capable of producing 0.6gpm (2.3 L/m) of ultra-high pressure water at a continuous operating pressure of 55,000 psi (3800 bar).

It features a tie-rod design that eliminates threads on the high pressure cylinders, end caps and hydraulic cylinders. This reduces the likelihood of cracking and increases component life. Matched metal components prevent no reduction in cutting speed for the precision cutting of parts with square, taper-free edges, including interlocking pieces and dovetail fittings. Through the control software, the Tilt-A-Jet automatically calculates and adjusts the angle of the nozzle to remove the natural taper from the finished part by transferring it to the scrap part of the material. OMAX Corporation also offers a range of table sizes, pumps, accessories and software.

For routing high pressure water from the pump to the cutting head, most waterjet cutting systems use rigid stainless steel tubing. This consists of a lot of pipe sections and connectors and normally is not flexible – it can be if a semi-rigid steel material is used, but such a construction needs a lot of space. As an alternative, KMT Waterjet Systems offers steel-reinforced high pressure hoses which – compared to steel piping – allow for a higher degree of flexibility when setting up high pressure lines.

Often, the use of flexible high pressure hoses, which can be installed up to a maximum pressure of 4,000 bar, facilitates the high pressure water supply for waterjet cutting systems. The installation is less complex compared to a rigid tubing and leaves a certain degree of flexibility of the hoses even when they are set under pressure.

OMAX Corporation’s 60120 JetMachining Centre can cut complex parts, measuring up to 1.5m x 3.0m (5’ x 10’), from materials that range from advanced composites, exotic alloys and glass to traditional metals, rubber and stone. Featuring an X-Y travel of 3.2m x 1.5m (10’ 6" x 5’ 2"), the 60120 comes standard with the company’s Intelli-MAX® 21 Software Suite, which runs on the Windows® operating system. The software precisely calculates the velocity of a tool path at over 2,000 points per inch, allowing for complete control over the motion of an abrasivejet and enabling precise, rapid machining.

The software automates most programming and tool set-up work, virtually eliminating the need for extensive operator training, and meets the growing trend of manufacturers processing 3D components by supporting up to six axes of coordinated motion. Free software upgrades are available for the life of the machine.

The 60120 has been demonstrated with a 50hp EnduroMAX® Pump, which is also available in 30hp, 40hp and 100hp options. The EnduroMAX Pump increases the part-processing speeds and lowers the operating costs of the 60120 as well as all other OMAX-brand abrasive waterjet machines.

At the 2014 EuroBLECH exhibition, Dutch waterjet manufacturer Resato presented its new Flex-zone.

“Most traditional waterjets use light screens surrounding the cutting table to comply with safety regulations. We have found a way for these light screens to be placed onto the bridge where they move along with the cutting heads, to provide necessary safety only where needed – in the cutting zone. The Flex-zone option allows for safely loading and unloading of material while the waterjet cuts. This reduces downtime to a minimum, meaning an up to 40 per cent increase in productive hours,” the manufacturer explained.

With this innovation, Resato

further reduces the competitive gap between waterjet cutters and both laser and plasma cutters.

WARDJet Inc. is a manufacturer of over 30 standard size waterjet cutting machines in the USA. It designs and builds custom waterjets integrated into complex processes. Standard waterjets range from 2’ x 2’ to 40’ x 17’ with custom sizes available. It has built machines with fifteen cutting heads on one cross beam, and can install as many as nine 5-axis cutting heads on a single cross beam. Its patented WARD Pro is an abrasive recycling system.

With over 30 years of experience in water cutting, Water Jet Sweden is one of Europe’s largest manufacturers of waterjet cutting machines. Its first machine delivery was in Sweden 1994 and, in Europe, in 1996. It has delivered over 600 machines to 38 countries worldwide.

Its latest innovation is the first abrasive waterjet machine with eighteen different axes moving simultaneously, as well as four standard cutting heads and two 5-axis “Beveljet 60” heads, adding up to six individually-controlled cutting units.

“We manufacture systems in all sizes and for all applications, from integrated compact machines to systems with operating ranges of 4 x 18m or larger, as well as abrasive 5-axis waterjet cutting heads. Our cutting systems can be converted to multi-head operation at any time,” explained WJS.
