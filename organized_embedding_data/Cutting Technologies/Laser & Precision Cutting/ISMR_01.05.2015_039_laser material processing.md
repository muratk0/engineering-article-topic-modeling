# Laser material processing

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

HIGHYAG Lasertechnologie GmbH will present its latest generation products for material processing with solid state lasers at this year’s LASER World of PHOTONICS in Munich (22-25 June 2015). Laser processing heads for cutting, welding and brazing as well as laser light cables and fibre-to-fibre couplers can be seen at HIGHYAG booth 411 in hall A3.

“HIGHYAG laser light cables with their advanced connectors are designed for diverse industrial applications. The new QBH-compatible connector, the LLK-Q, meets demanding requirements for dynamic use in cutting machines. It guides the light to the processing head with almost no power or beam quality losses, including the ability to withstand unwanted radiation. The laser light cable portfolio also includes the automotive-standard connector, the LLK-Auto, as well as the connectors, LLK-HP for high-power applications and LLK-LP for low-power applications. Scattered light sensors are available as an option for all connectors,” explained the company.

“Our improved laser cutting head, the BIMO-FSC, features new compact electronics with more interface options as well as a new and optimised cutting attachment. The BIMO-FSC is available in

HIGHYAG’s latest generation remote laser welding head, RLSK, can freely position the focus in a three-dimensional workspace using scanner technology. With even faster adjustability of the focus position, the time between weld stitches can be minimised and the robot utilization and the laser utilization can be increased.

The manufacturer also has “RLSK-Studio” software with new features for process control. The RLSK comes with new optics enabling lower laser-induced focus shift and improved process monitoring due to increased light efficiency in the camera port. It features more control interfaces and more flexible mounting options of the cross jet. The RLSK will be presented in a “welding-on-the-fly” application with the Yaskawa Motoman MC2000 robot at the HIGHYAG booth.
