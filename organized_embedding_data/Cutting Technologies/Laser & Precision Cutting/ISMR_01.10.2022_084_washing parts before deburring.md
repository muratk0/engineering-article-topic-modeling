# Washing parts before deburring

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Manufacturers of sheet-metal parts normally use oil to extend the service life of their tools and increase the performance of modern laserand fibre laser-cutting systems. These residues on metal parts become undesirable after the cutting operation.

“EdgeRacer 'the Washer' removes these oil residues and production-typical contamination in a continuous process. Operators appreciate the resulting clean and dry parts (even with 3D contours), that are ready for further processing. The continuous separation of oil and solids from the water-based cleaning medium extends change intervals. EdgeRacer 'the Washer' can be operated stand-alone or in line with EdgeRacer or any other deburring machine," explained Wöhler Brush Tech GmbH.

"Dirty parts are fed into 'the Washer' via conveyor where they are cleaned on both sides in one pass. Sprayed-on cleaning agent separates oil from the sheet metal. Two brushes then remove oil and solids for clean surfaces. Finally, rollers press off the last water residues. The workpieces leave the machine clean and dry, prepared for further processing. The cleaning agent preserves the surfaces for subsequent processing," it added.

EdgeRacer 'the Washer' is available in working widths of 1500mm (59") and 300mm (11.8") for cleaning smaller parts.
