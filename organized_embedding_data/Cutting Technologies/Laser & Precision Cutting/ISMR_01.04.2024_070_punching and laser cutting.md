# Punching and laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Prima Power's Combi Genius offers versatile and flexible punching and laser cutting, combining multiple operations in one machine. It can process maximum sheet thicknesses of 8mm (aluminium, mild steel and stainless steel).

Prima Power's Combi Genius.

"This Lean Manufacturing style reduces cost per part; parts are made rapidly, easily and accurately on one machine by one operator using a single operating and programming system. The Combi machine works with numerically controlled, servo-electric axes providing high-speed and energy-efficient operations with low maintenance requirements and production costs," explained Prima Power.

A high brilliance fibre laser is integrated into the Combi Genius to process a wide range of materials. A 3- or 4kW Prima Power fibre laser resonator offers manufacturing capacity for a large range of sheet metal products. Prima Power's ECOPUNCH® concept in the machine has been designed to save energy consumption. The control panel is integrated in the electric cabinet for easy cabinet placement and access to the machine controls. This multitasking control enables work preparation, program background editing and set-up while the machine is running.

23-ton and 30-ton punching force on the machine is standard, depending upon the selected performance (Pure/Dynamic). Up to 400 standard or 384 index tools can be simultaneously available in the turret for active use. The turret is compatible with AMADA® tools.

"The Punch Laser combination, equipped with servo-electric punching, provides rapid nibbling speeds and the possibility of using low ram speeds when using forming tools or for reducing the noise level with largediameter tools. The fully programmable stroke length offers a variety of possibilities for forming operations. Three models are available: CG1225, CG1530 and CG1540," confirmed Prima Power.■
