# Laser cutting and process automation

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

EDGETEC AND PIERCETEC, Precitec's latest laser cutting technology packages, will be introduced at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (25-29 October 2016).

"These packages contain much more than simply hardware and a focus on high-quality laser cutting processes. EdgeTec enables fast cuts with smooth edges for all materials - from thin metal sheets to stainless steel thicknesses of over 30mm – with a single laser cutting head. PierceTec carries out the piercing process independently, shortens cycle time and optimises cut quality," explained the manufacturer.

Disk laser technology is characterised by high performance stability, easy servicing and low costs. Following a strategic cooperative agreement with a leading laser manufacturer, Precitec now offers the "All-In-Light" package worldwide. This features a laser power source, cutting head and process fibre in power classes ranging from 1-8kW.

With the inclusion of the entire optical chain, starting from the laser source through to the processing head, Precitec's portfolio has been expanded over the past few months. Specific adjustments in the laser sources' hard- and software, as well as extensive modifications to the cutting head, ProCutter, coupled with a new method of process control, has led to the introduction of the new EdgeTec technology package.

"The technology is designed to enable significant quality improvements, especially for the nitrogen cutting of steel and aluminium. With EdgeTec, laser cutting machines can now even process metal sheets of 30mm and more," Precitec told ISMR.

Precitec's PierceTec technology package is designed for rapid, high-quality autonomous piercing. The sensor measures and evaluates process data online and takes over the laser control. Piercing progress is constantly monitored in a complex control process, where laser parameters are optimised in real-time based on the received data. The heat-affected zone, as well as piercing holes, are minimised.
