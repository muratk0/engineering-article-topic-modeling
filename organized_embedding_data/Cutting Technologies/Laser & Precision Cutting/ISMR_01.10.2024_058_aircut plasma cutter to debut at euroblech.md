# AirCut plasma cutter to debut at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

TEKA Absaug-und Entsorgungstechnologie GmbH, headquartered in Coesfeld (Germany), will be presenting a product premiere at EuroBLECH in Hanover (22-25 October 2024) on stand F58 in Hall 13. A complete plasmacutting system from TEKA will be on show for the first time at the trade fair.

"The new AirCut series is an integral solution in which all components are harmonised with one another: plasma cutting head; flame cutting table; EcoCube filter system; CAD/CAM software; component library and Industry 4.0 connection. One advantage of the new complete solution is that it can be installed, ready for cutting, within one day according to the plug & play principle. The new systems are aimed particularly at the needs of small and medium-sized metalworking companies. They combine precision, robustness, user-friendliness and moderate costs," explained TEKA.

TEKA offers the new AirCut plasma cutting systems via various sales channels. On its neighbouring stand at EuroBLECH, Eckert Cutting (Hall 13, Stand E52), a large-format AirCut system will be showcased.

The new plasma cutting systems from TEKA are available in two sizes at launch: as an AirCut medium-format system with an effective cutting area of 1250mm x 2500mm and as an AirCut large-format system with a cutting area of 1520mm x 3060mm. They offer a maximum positioning speed of up to 40,000mm/minute and a positioning accuracy of ≤0.15mm/metre.

"A flame-cutting table made of welded structural steel ensures maximum stability, and the precise rack and pinion drive for the X and Y axes of the guide machine is a design element that guarantees not just accuracy but also durability. Ease of operation and user-friendliness were also among the key requirements for our developers,"added TEKA.

A free-standing operating terminal with Intel Core i5 processor and 19-inch screen is included as part of the AirCut system. The CAD/CAM software supplied, including a component library, controls the cutting processes. A Panasonic FB-I4C InfoHub module, with cloud access and networking options via M2M communication protocols (such as OPC UA and MQTT) is designed for Industry-4.0 connectivity.

To ensure a clean working environment, TEKA has equipped the automatic extraction table with sectional control. An integrated interface allows for remote start and power supply of the filter system. For the AirCut series, the developers have chosen the W3- certified EcoCube filter system.

“EcoCube filter systems follow the plugand-play principle. They can be installed and ready for operation on-site in less than half an hour. In the AirCut series, the EcoCube filter systems are available in two performance levels: EcoCube 5.5kW (medium format) and EcoCube 7.5kW (large format). They actively adjust the required suction power during the cutting process," concluded TEKA.
