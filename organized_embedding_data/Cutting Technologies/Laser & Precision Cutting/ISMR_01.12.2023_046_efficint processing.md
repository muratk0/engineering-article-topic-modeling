# Efficint processing

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

At the EuroBLECH sheet-metal exhibition in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022), AMADA presented a range of new solutions with a focus on increasing productivity while simultaneously boosting cost- and resource-efficiency.

It presented its latest generation of laser-cutting systems with more powerful beam sources, which are designed to be energy-efficient and can machine an even wider range of materials. Features such as the AMADA LIS system enable intelligent, highly automated cutting, while new automation systems make it possible to achieve considerable productivity increases.

AMADA also presented its new ASR 3015 PR storage and part picking system. This fully featured system enabled the autonomous loading and unloading of raw material, parts sorting and the storage of components and sheet skeletons. Material loading/unloading and part removal runs in parallel without interrupting machine operation and there is no variation in sheet load/unload cycle times.

As featured in our November issue, AMADA has now brought its new auto crowning device and enhanced flexibility to its mid-range of press brakes (the new HRB Auto Crowning mid-range model). This was previously only available on high-end press brakes such as the AMADA HG.

AMADA My V-factory Industry 4.0 solutions, IoT Remote Support and a new line control solution also demonstrated how users could manufacture more intelligently and boost their productivity and cost-effectiveness.

## Fibre laser cutting

“To keep our customers profitable in the increasingly competitive laser cutting market, we showcased the new 6kW VENTIS fibre laser featuring our Locus Beam Control (LBC) technology, highlighting its ability to perform like an 8-10kW machine but with lower running costs," the Japanese manufacturer told /SMR. "This higher power variant will be especially relevant considering energy costs are rapidly increasing. So far, almost 200 orders have been received for the 4kW version in Europe."

Higher cutting efficiency has been enabled by AMADA's LBC technology and its single-diode module, high-brightness, fibre laser engines. This combination, said AMADA, provides “efficient material removal and higher cutting speeds versus a standard fibre laser of equivalent power." The VENTIS series fibre laser cutting machine is available in 3015 and 4020 sizes, with 4kW or 6kW power sources.

There are three main processing modes in LBC technology: these are Productivity Mode; Quality Mode, providing dross-free stainless steel up to 15mm (4kW) and 20mm (6kW) to reduce secondary processes, and Kerf Control mode (particularly useful when combined with AMADA's TK part picking systems, allowing the cutting kerf to be widened up to 1mm for automatic removal of components from the cut sheet).

"LBC Flash Cut was another revolutionary development shown at EuroBLECH 2022. This processes the shape by moving only the laser beam without moving the axis, so it can process at an unprecedented speed that surpasses conventional laser machines," AMADA added.

The new 6kW VENTIS was shown with an all-electric pallet changer, while a 4kW VENTIS was exhibited in a fully automated cell to highlight the 'Productivity, Not Power' theme.

"Consisting of the new double tower ASF-EU II Twin load/unload system and TK3015EU part picker, this cell is capable of higher productivity over a 24-hour period than a 15kW standalone laser, with significantly lower average cost-per-part," continued AMADA.

## Fibre laser welding

AMADA has also increased the laser power of the FLW-ENSIS fibre laser welding cells to 6kW. The 6kW fibre laser increases the penetration depth in steel and stainless steel to 12mm and in aluminium to 8mm.

"As a result, components can be fully welded at extremely high speeds without the need for time-consuming weld seam preparation. These machines are suitable for all applications because the FLW-ENSIS fibre laser welding cells are also exceptionally well suited for soldering processes. All users of these welding cells benefit from innovative ENSIS technology. The mode of the laser beam can be automatically changed to best suit the application and material being processed," explained AMADA.

Another feature is the beam weaving technology, in which the integrated rotating optics cause a circular movement of the laser beam. It is now possible to bridge over larger gap sizes than were previously possible using laser welding processes, explained AMADA. The FLW-ENSIS systems are equipped with a filler wire function to cope with particularly large gaps.

## Automated bending cell

AMADA developed the RBR fully automatic bending solution to produce a wide range of part sizes with a rapid bending cycle. It is possible to schedule a large production run of different parts.

“The bending cell is equipped with devices such as the automatic tool, gripper changers and the motorised regripping units to support part manipulation by the bending robot. From the press-brake side, the HRB-ATC series (available both as 100 tonnes x 3 metres and 220 tonnes x 4 metres) offers the advantages of fully automated production using the automatic tool changer (ATC). The ATC, able to reduce set-up time of the press brake by up to 80% compared to manual tool loading, lets the operator schedule requested production without any tool layout restriction," explained AMADA.

The AMADA HRB-ATC series also features a punch reverse function and automatic crowning – compensating for deflections in the upper and lower beams when pressure is applied – which comes as standard to boost precision for users.

The AMADA RBR series is available with either a 50kg or 200kg payload robot. The flexibility of the solution is exemplified by a wide range of part sizes, from a minimum of 300 x 200mm for either robot, up to 2000 x 1000mm for the 50kg robot and 3000 × 1500mm for the 200kg version.

This high level of automation is possible using the offline programming module VPSS 3i ARBEND and the CELL MANAGER scheduling system. Another option is the AMADA Bi system for in-cycle angle measurement and adjustment which helps to eliminate scrap parts.

## Smart networking

With its new Blank-to-Bend concept, AMADA linked punching and cutting systems plus automatic press brakes via a warehouse storage system to form a highly automated end-to-end solution.

"It is possible to perform all cutting and bending operations, including material handling, in a single, highly automated, end-to-end system for the first time. The concept revolves around the use of the AMADA CSII warehouse storage system. This is connected, for example, on one side to an AMADA punching machine or punching/fibre laser combination machine which creates the cut blank. On the other side, an AMADA HRB-ATC with RBR bending robot solution performs the fully automated bending operations necessary to produce the finished part," concluded the manufacturer.
