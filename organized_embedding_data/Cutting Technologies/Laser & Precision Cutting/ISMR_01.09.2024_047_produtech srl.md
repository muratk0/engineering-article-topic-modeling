# Produtech Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.produtech.it/en

Produtech is a metal punching and laser cutting machine supplier, specialised since 1993 in the manufacturing of coil-fed machines. It designs and produces lines for sheet metal processing.

"In particular, our lines match laser cutting and/ or punching-forming technologies with the coil-fed working mode, providing various productive advantages over traditional sheet-fed mode; each line is a unique solution in the market and is customised to user needs. Competence, reliability and exclusive technical expertise, with a great focus on customer needs, are our core values. These have allowed us to grow and strengthen our presence worldwide," the Italian coil processing specialist told ISMR.

Produtech manufactures three different models of sheet metal punching machines and the ISEO laser cutting machine. It also manufactures a combined punching and laser cutting machine, EffiCOIL.
