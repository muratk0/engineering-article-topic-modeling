# BOOSTING PRODUCTIVITY

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

A t this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany (23-27 October 2012), a number of manufacturers will be launching and showcasing several bending innovations and technologies for sheet metal working. We highlight a selection of these and what they may mean for future bending operations.

BLM will launch its Lasertube and bending machine in an automated cell. The combination of laser and bending processes in one production cell enables high repeatability as well as a simplified production process. Further piece machining on additional equipment is not required; a guarantee of flexibility for further different uses of the system.

ISMR SAYS:

“We previewsome of the latestnewbending technologies on offer”

Swiss manufacturer Bystronic is one of the leading suppliers of machine systems and associated services for the processing of sheet metal. Its product portfolio comprises laser cutting systems, waterjet cutting systems and press brakes as well as software and automation solutions. Bystronic will be launching a new machine for bending at EuroBLECH; a press brake which, according to the message from Niederönz, “will absolutely electrify users”.

“The machine will be extremely rapid so that the machine operator is in a position to bend more parts per unit time than on a comparable press brake. We will also present another innovation at EuroBLECH that makes the operation of press brakes even simpler…..” the manufacturer told ISMR.

Codatto International will introduce the latest version of its EVA automatic bending system.

“EVA can produce a wide range of panels which go beyond the capacity of a conventional bending system and can process ‘just in time’ production of very small batches,” it explained.

In line with its strategy based on continuous innovation, Gasparini Industries will display the X-PRESS ECO at EuroBLECH, a press brake range with energy-saving features, high speed, extreme precision and noiselessness.

"This is just the first step inaugurating the ECO CONCEPT, the stream of innovation that will distinguish the systems that we will develop from now on. We will also present the PHOENI-X, our new controller, at EuroBLECH," the Italian manufacturer told ISMR.

LVD will unveil a new bending system as well as new laser cutting, punching and software products at EuroBLECH. It will celebrate its 60th anniversary with the unveiling of an innovative press brake automation system and introduction of the high-speed Electra-FL fiber laser system at the show. Also on display will be the LVD Pullmax 720 punch press able to punch, mark, bend, tap and form up to 75mm. Visitors will see the new TouchTM-Series touch screen control and user interface and the award-winning Easy-Form® Laser adaptive forming system at work on a 135-ton/3-metre press brake.
