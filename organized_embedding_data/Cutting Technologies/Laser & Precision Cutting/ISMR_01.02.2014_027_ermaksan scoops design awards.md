# ERMAKSAN SCOOPS DESIGN AWARDS

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

ERMAKSAN, a leading Turkish manufacturer of sheet metal working machinery, has scooped three awards in two different international design competitions – the GoodDesign and IF Design Awards.

Over 700 products from 38 countries were entered into the GoodDesign competition and Ermaksan was the only company that succeeded in winning awards in the industrial section with its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine. Ermaksan picked up IF’s Best Design Award, which has international prestige, with its Fibermak laser cutting machine as well as winning two awards from GoodDesign.

Ahmet Özkayan, Ermaksan’s general manager, commented that design played a large part in the success of its machine tools. He added that it is not just quality and rigidity that makes a difference but now also ergonomics and aesthetics. www.ermaksan.com.tr ■
