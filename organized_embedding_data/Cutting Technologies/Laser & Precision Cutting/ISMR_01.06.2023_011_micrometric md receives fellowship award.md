# Micrometric MD receives Fellowship Award

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Neil Main, managing director of Lincoln-based Micrometric, in the UK, has received an award for his outstanding lifetime contribution to the industrial use of lasers. Founded in 1981, Micrometric Ltd specialises in laser material processing of metals and is a European specialist in the use of lasers for the production of fine parts and precision components from rapid prototype through to volume production.

He was honoured with the Fellowship Award at the AILU ILAS 2023 Symposium Dinner, an event which celebrated all things related to industrial lasers and featured a range of award categories. The Fellowship Award recognises an individual AILU member who has made an outstanding lifetime contribution to the industrial use of lasers.

"I have been involved with AILU since its inception, and it's a great way of bringing all stages of laser processing together and building the knowledge around laser manufacturing. I am very pleased to accept this Fellowship Award and am honoured that my work and time as president are being recognised in this way," commented Neil Main.

Left to right: Christian Dini (Innovation Award), Jack Gabzdyl (2021 Innovation Award), Adam Clare (President's Award) and Neil Main (AILU Fellowship Award).

AILU Committee President, Adam Clare, added: "Neil Main has been actively working in the laser industry since 1982. He was instrumental in starting AILU and has been involved with the association since its inception. He was even voted President of AILU in 2013 for a two-year tenure. The encouragement, persistence and goodwill demonstrated by Neil has been invaluable to AILU over more than 25 years."

The recipient of the Fellowship Award receives AILU Life Membership. One new Fellow is awarded at each Industrial Laser Applications Symposium (ILAS) which takes place every other year. Previous AILU Award winners automatically become AILU Fellows.
