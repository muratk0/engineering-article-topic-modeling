# TRUMPF innovations at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

The new SortMaster Station and SortMaster Vision solutions from TRUMPF enable fully automatic sorting without programming.

At EuroBLECH2024 in Hanover (Germany) this October, TRUMPF presented a new solution for the automated sorting of 2D laser-cut parts.

"With the SortMaster Station and the SortMaster Vision, customers will be able to sort parts particularly reliably and without any programming effort. The SortMaster Station removes the parts from the sheetmetal in the first step, regardless of their geometry. The machine operator then sorts the separated parts manually or has them removed and palletised fully automatically by the SortMaster Vision. Meanwhile, the laser cutting machine is already processing the next sheet," the sheet metal specialist explained.

“The automated sorting of parts is one of the greatest challenges in the sheet metal world. Conventional methods are particularly suitable for simple components with medium sheet thicknesses of up to 12mm. For many companies, their common automation solutions are too inflexible and timeconsuming to program, so they have their parts sorted by hand. With the SortMaster Station and the SortMaster Vision, we enable automated sorting of any sheet metal parts," said TRUMPF CTO for Machine Tools, Thomas Schneider. TRUMPF offers the new solution in a 3m format for laser cutting machines in the 3000 and 5000 series.

The solution uses the LiftMaster Compact loading and unloading automation system from TRUMPF. It removes the cut sheet from the pallet changer of the cutting machine and transfers it to the SortMaster station. This separates the components from the scrap skeleton in a few seconds. The separated parts are then conveyed to the sorting area.

"With our solution, we have decoupled the cutting, separating and sorting processes from each other for the first time. This allows users to better utilise the laser machine and increase their throughput," explained Schneider. A third pallet station also enables parallel manual sorting, for example for special formats.

Automated sorting with SortMaster Station and SortMaster Vision from TRUMPF. Using adaptive robotic functions, all programming of the sorting process is eliminated.

## Sorting with cameras and intelligent software

The SortMaster Vision is used for automated sorting.

“Partnering for several years with Intrinsic, a leading AI and robotics software company in the Alphabet Group, we have introduced adaptive robotics capabilities to SortMaster Vision, including automated perception and robot path planning. This enables a robot to autonomously recognize the separated parts using AI-supported image recognition," said Schneider.

The software autonomously calculates the robot's motion plan including the gripping points. The SortMaster Vision independently obtains all the part information from the cutting program. It uses this to simulate various removals in advance and selects the most suitable one at runtime. This eliminates the need for any programming of the sorting process. The process creates flexibility with regard to the range of parts and batch sizes.

“Thanks to this innovative process and partnership with Intrinsic, customers can now automatically sort parts that could not be removed using conventional solutions, even with a batch size of one," outlined Schneider. These include small or complex parts that are prone to tipping over or tilting in the scrap skeleton. However, even thicker parts can be separated out and automatically sorted by the sorting process.

The two sorting modules can also be retrofitted at a later date which, said TRUMPF, reduces the initial investment and allows the degree of automation to be increased step by step.

## New laser cutting machine

TRUMPF also launched a new laser cutting machine at EuroBLECH which, it said, is particularly suitable for users with smaller production volumes who are looking for a machine with low investment and operating costs. The TruLaser Series 1000 Lean Edition offers the same quality standard as TRUMPF's high-end models but, said the company, is significantly more affordable.

For the new TruLaser Series 1000 Lean Edition, TRUMPF has adapted the range of functions of its TruLaser Series 1000. The machine does without various functions that are primarily relevant for users with high throughput.

“When companies have a low production volume, it is often not worthwhile to have their own sheet metal processing unit, because they cannot utilise conventional machines to full capacity. With the new TruLaser Series 1000 Lean Edition, these kinds of users will be able to produce the parts they need themselves in the future. This provides flexibility and independence," explained TRUMPF product manager, Patrick Schüle, adding that, with this machine, users get the same access to TRUMPF's customer service.

The TruLaser Series 1000 Lean Edition retains the universal cutting unit with reversible collision protection. If the cutting unit collides with a component that has been cut free, it deflects and the operator can then push it back into the holder by hand.

"To prevent collisions from happening in the first place, the TruLaser Series 1000 Lean Edition also features the nanojoint technology we developed in which components are fixed with the smallest of holding points and are, therefore, particularly easy to remove after cutting," added TRUMPF, highlighting the machine's ease of operation and an online monitoring function for the cutting unit.

At EuroBLECH 2024, TRUMPF also highlighted its improved machine diagnostics to increase productivity and lower service costs as well as its new TRUMPF TruLaser Series 3000 Bevel Cut Edition. This automatically prepares components for welding and can produce bevelled cutting edges (chamfers) on the top and bottom edge of the component while cutting, saving an otherwise necessary downstream work step (see the October issue of ISMR).
