# Loading for lasers

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

WITH THE BYTRANS Cross, Swiss manufacturer Bystronic has developed a loading and unloading solution for fibre laser cutting. It is designed to flexibly adapt to varying order situations and manufacturing rhythms in laser cutting.

"The key is the loading and unloading unit's modular design – this unlocks a variety of application scenarios," explained Bystronic. "As an automation bridge, the ByTrans Cross can be integrated between the laser cutting system and the warehouse to channel the material flow. However, the ByTrans Cross is equally well suited for use as a standalone solution, without a connection to the warehouse, to ensure the supply of raw metal sheet in various thicknesses and materials to the laser cutting system. The basic version of the ByTrans Cross comes with two loading carriages, which can be used to store raw material when used as a stand-alone solution."

With the newly developed BySort upgrade, unloading with the ByTrans Cross becomes more versatile. An additional bridge can be installed on the ByTrans Cross system to accommodate up to two sorting heads.

These heads are automatically tooled using the available gripper modules. The BySort is designed to deposit the completed parts on an additional unloading position next to the laser cutting system, which offers ByTrans Cross users a higher degree of flexibility. It can also increases the speed of the entire unloading process.

Both the BySort and the ByTrans Cross are controlled using the laser cutting system's touch-screen. Bystronic developed the ByVision Cutting user interface, which is already being used with the latest generation of Bystronic's fibre lasers.
