# Sheet metal in focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

## New software for the sheet-metal processing industry; greater performance and smart features on its laser-cutting machines; a new press brake and a consistent focus on sustainability along the entire cutting and bending process chain are Bystronic's highlights at EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, Germany. The Swiss sheet-metal specialist will be showcasing its technology in Hall 12, stand A66/B66, at the world's largest sheetmetal exhibition from 25-28 October 2022.

Bystronic will be presenting BySoft Suite; digitalisation from quotation to delivery. This software solution for the sheet-metal industry extends its range of BySoft software products.

"With the new software solution, customers can fully digitalise their business and significantly increase their efficiency- a step towards the Smart Factory and beyond," Bystronic explained.

"The ByCut Star 4020 is the successor to the ByStar Fiber and offers impressive laser output with a new, modern design. Additional smart features such as the 'Parameter Wizard' and the optional 'Automatic BeamShaper' (ABS) optimise the entire cutting process. The ByCut Star is optionally available in 3 x 1.5-metre and 4 x 2-metre formats," it continued.

Bystronic's BySmart Fiber 3015 with 12 kilowatts offers its customers “smart access to laser-cutting technology." The compact fibre laser (BySmart Fiber 3015 and BySmart Fiber 4020) offers optional features such as “ParameterWizard" and “BeamShaper" to provide additional support for sheet-metal processing. The BySmart Fiber is now available with even more laser power, designed for greater performance and efficiency.

The ByTube Star 130 – Bystronic's solution for tube processing- is now available with a laser output of four kilowatts. This allows users to process tubes of up to 8.5 metres in length even more quickly.

"A fully automatic set-up, with open profiles and ellipses, leads to more flexibility and quality in production. Additional options such as 'Laserscan' (real-time compensation of tube bending to improve cutting precision) and 'QuickCut' (more speed for better performance by way of an additional linear axis) further increase efficiency," Bystronic told ISMR.

With the new ByBend Star 120, Bystronic will present its press brake for the small to medium-sized parts segment. Strong and compact, it is designed for a wide range of sheet thicknesses as well as materials in higher tensile strength classes.

“The 'Laser Angle Measuring System' (LAMS) function guarantees the perfect bending result from the very first part. With the 'FastBend Plus' function, there is also no need for time-consuming bend angle measurements. The system does this automatically and with extreme precision. Bending users now save up to 30% energy and get 20% more speed in return – thanks to the energy-efficient servo hydraulics," added the manufacturer.

In recent months Bystronic has invested heavily in the optimisation of its production, products, supply chains and peripheral performance optimisers. It has placed its focus firmly on sustainability as a central element of its corporate strategy. The goal is to shape a sustainable future with sheetmetal.

To this end, Bystronic has developed an ambitious sustainability strategy that is being consistently implemented across all areas. The company's first sustainability report is now available and will also be presented at EuroBLECH.
