# ‘On the go’ cutting

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, has introduced the Powermax30® AIR, a new portable air plasma system and internal compressor in one.

“With a built-in compressor and the ability to operate on both 120- or 240-volt lines, the Powermax30 AIR is a versatile system that enables metal cutting just about anywhere. Weighing 13.5 kg, it provides 30 amps of output giving it the highest power-to-weight ratio of any system in its class,” said the manufacturer.

With a recommended cut capacity of 8mm and a severance capacity of 16mm, the system can cut a variety of metal types and thicknesses making it a suitable entry-level cutting tool. The high power-to-weight ratio is due in part to several engineering advances including a new patent-pending consumable design that optimises air flow from the compact internal compressor. This design directly contributes to the efficiency of the system for consistent cut performance. See www.Hypertherm.com ■
