# SPI invests for future

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

SPI Lasers, a leading designer and manufacturer of fiber lasers, has confirmed that it is increasing investment in people, machinery and sales channels following significant growth in demand for its products.

This, combined with further new product releases planned in each quarter of 2010, has warranted a doubling of manufacturing floor space, recruiting across operational, engineering and sales functions and investment in sales and application development facilities.

In addition to doubling its manufacturing capacity capability, SPI is continuing to invest in the sales channel with a move to a new and larger facility in the USA (Santa Clara) which expands the applications development capability and improves customer meeting areas and office facilities. Further investments include improved stocking and product support facilities in Asia and the opening of a second application and customer suite in the UK.

At Photonics West, SPI Lasers will showcase new products including the redENERGY single-moded pulsed laser, redENERGY 40W (20kW peak power) pulsed laser and an M2=4 Low-moded 400W microwelding laser, all launched in June 2009.

At the CEO Forum held on 14 January 2010 at the Yeouido Korea Exchange, POSCO announced that it produced 29.53 million cubic tonnes of crude steel and that its total sales reached 26.954 trillion KRW in 2009. Operating profit was 3.148 trillion KRW and net profit was recorded at 3.172 trillion KRW.

Last year saw a huge drop in crude steel production and product sales amid the slumping steel industry worldwide, as well as a decline in operating profit and sales due to weaker product prices. However, POSCO entered a company-wide emergency management system including shortening the quarterly management cycle to a monthly cycle after Chairman Joonyang Chung was inaugurated in February. The company managed one of the quickest turnarounds among global steelmakers through the reduction of 1.3595 trillion KRW in costs by strengthening its domestic and overseas market base.

“Unlike overseas steelmakers who reduced steel production by more than 40%, POSCO was able to overcome the crisis by only reducing production by 20% in the first half of the year. Especially in the second half, the market situation improved while the decrease of raw material costs was reflected in Q4 sales which recovered to the 7 trillion KRW level,” the company told ISMR. “Operating profits also recovered to the 1 trillion KRW level in Q3 and reached 1.587 trillion KRW in the Q4.”

POSCO has set this year's steel production and sales goals to 34.4 million tonnes and 29.535 trillion KRW, an increase of 16.6% and 9.3% respectively. Consolidated steel production and sales goals increased 16.1% and 16.2% respectively at 36.1 million tonnes and 43 trillion KRW.
