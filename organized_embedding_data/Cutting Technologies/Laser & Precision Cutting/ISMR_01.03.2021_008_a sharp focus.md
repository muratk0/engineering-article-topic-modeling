# A SHARP FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.03.2021 ISMR]

JSR Precision Engineers' purchase of a new Mazak laser cutting system enables it to fulfil complex orders more quickly, allowing the business to grow further.

When JSR Precision Engineers, a prominent, Sheffield-based precision engineering company in the UK, needed to replace a laser processing machine, it turned to Yamazaki Mazak to maintain the high standards synonymous with the Steel City of Sheffield.

"In 40 years of business, there aren't many industries which we haven't supplied," said Simon Pepper, Sales and Business Development Manager, JSR Precision Engineers Ltd. (JSR). "We have an exceptionally diverse customer base and provide services and precision parts across a wide spectrum of industries including, among others, the construction, automotive, nuclear, medical and railway sectors."

The company chose a new Mazak OPTIPLEX NEXUS 3015 6kW Fiber for its bespoke steel laser cutting services. With a maximum bed size of 3000 x 1500mm the new machine is helping to maximise the company's performance and production levels.

## A culture of continuous investment

Founded in 1981 as a family-run business, JSR offers subcontract sheet metal work, machining, laser cutting and part manufacturing services, supplying everything from one-offs to small batches and high volumes from two sites in Sheffield. The company has grown considerably since its establishment, forging its reputation in the steel and engineering industry through continued investment in the latest laser cutting, CNC milling and turning technology.

Examples of investment projects include the company's own laser cutting facilities, as well as in-house forging and heat treatment plus induction brazing facilities. Further investment in CNC milling and turning centres has seen quality standards maintained. Continued investment in the company means that it can offer engineered products from design through to finished items without the need to outsource services from other suppliers.

"We recognise the importance of reducing time to market and the value of continuing to develop a close liaison between our engineers and our customers to reduce lead times and cost. Based upon our extensive investment in equipment, employees and systems, we will strive to continuously improve our productivity to maintain our competitive edge in an increasingly demanding marketplace," Pepper told ISMR.

"We are also increasing the skill base of our employees," added the company, which celebrates its 40th anniversary this year.

JSR's continued success saw it become a limited company in 2006 and gain ISO 9001 accreditation in 2008. It now employs 20 members of staff and enjoys a turnover of over £2 million per annum and has retained its ISO standard since it was first awarded. JSR is also an accredited license holder of the 'Made in Sheffield' trademark - an internationally famous mark of quality for manufactured products.

"From making changes to existing products or development from new, we are always looking for new ways to meet and exceed our customer expectations. As a business, we are continuously working on how we can use our engineering machining skills to positively contribute to both local and UK-based engineering and manufacturing industries. With our industry experience we combine our knowledge, abilities and skills to produce bespoke or one-off components through small batch runs to prototype products," said Pepper.

## Laser cutting operations

JSR is keen to avoid any situation where it would need to outsource services from external suppliers. This ethos was put under strain with new success, as JSR began to supply increasing quantities of components to the agricultural market. As enquiries for laser cutting work grew even further, so too did JSR's reliance on subcontracting its laser cutting work out to other suppliers.

Simon Pepper continued: "Our rapid success in the agricultural sector meant we were continuously purchasing parts to fulfil our order book, to the extent that it became clear that investing in our own laser would be a much more cost-effective course of action. This also gave us the added benefit of being able to reduce lead times and costs for our customers, as we no longer had to rely on third-party sources."

## Quality and customer satisfaction is key.

Above and Left: A laser-cut Stainless Steel private sign, Knight and Harry Potter badge from JSR.

"As a company, we pride ourselves on putting quality and customer satisfaction at the forefront of everything we do," explained Simon Pepper. "We've grown year-on-year through continued investment in training and equipment, and now operate over 30 machines on our premises. This includes milling, drilling and turning centres, as well as sawing and laser cutting equipment."

"While purchasing our first laser was originally seen as a way to streamline our processes, it soon developed into the company establishing a separate, rapidly expanding laser cutting division," he added. "Servicing the increasing demands of our agricultural customers meant this aspect of the business became even more vital, so when our laser experienced a number of faults we decided the best course of action would be to cut our losses, sell it and replace it with a new machine."

Having previously purchased two Yamazaki Mazak VTC 20-C CNC turning centres and another FJV-25 machining centre, the team at JSR noticed Mazak's OPTIPLEX NEXUS 3015 FIBER laser processing solution during a visit to Mazak's European Technology Centre in Worcester, UK. As this model was similar in specification to JSR's existing laser, the company believed it could be suitable for machining its products.

The latest machine in the series, the OPTIPLEX NEXUS 3015 FIBER uses flying optics and a helical rack and pinion positioning system that enables high cutting speeds, maximum throughput and rugged construction. Its flexible design also allows for stable cutting of highly reflective materials such as copper, brass and aluminium, and higher productivity when cutting thin-to-medium-thickness material, which proved beneficial to JSR.

"Not only did the new machine seamlessly replace our old laser with no compromise in quality, the increased reliability of the OPTIPLEX NEXUS meant there was less downtime than our previous model," said Simon Pepper. "This, combined with the faster cutting times, means we can fulfil complex orders even more quickly, allowing the business to grow further."

One such example of a difficult project made easier by the OPTIPLEX NEXUS' innovative design was a recent enquiry for 1500 mild steel parts, made up of 190 drawings of components ranging in thickness from 5mm to 20mm. Utilising JSR's programming software to record off-cuts that could be repurposed, the OPTIPLEX NEXUS was able to quickly machine parts while keeping waste material to a minimum. As a result, JSR's customers were able to save money while enjoying precise processing work and a lead time of just three days from initial enquiry to order delivery.

Its new Mazak laser, it told ISMR, is 'three times faster than an ordinary CO2 laser cutting system and has increased efficiency and production in the business, allowing us to provide increased capacity and a faster turnaround for our customers'.

Laser cutting services have become increasingly more popular over recent years, said the company. JSR can deliver one-off products, batch or mass-produced parts. Its sales and design team works with customers throughout the process to ensure that a specification is correct.

The new laser cutting system is suitable for cutting stainless steel; aluminium; brass; bronze; copper; thin gauge steel and steel plate with a high-quality finished edge.

JSR's laser cutting facilities can cut sheet dimensions of up to 3000mm × 1500mm.

"Our new fibre laser eliminates mirrors and other components of the beam delivery path by using fibre optics to significantly reduce the cost of maintenance, allowing us and our customers to benefit from production cost efficiency. Equipped with sensors that monitor and adjust the laser focus and calibration for additional accuracy, the OPTIPLEX NEXUS 3015 FIBER includes additional intelligent cutting functions which optimise cutting conditions to provide quality surface finishes during high-speed cutting, so that there is no further requirement for secondary finishing," said Simon Pepper.

He concluded: "The innovative design of the new laser processing machine means it has fitted seamlessly into our existing operations and become an integral part in a very important and continually expanding part of the business."
