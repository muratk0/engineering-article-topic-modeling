# MULTIPLE PART CUTTING

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

TOTALLY UNMANNED ‘lights-out’ production is underway at a precision sheet metal component manufacturer in Queensland, Australia. Since adding a Prima Power LC6 with laser sorting robot to its plant line-up, Samin Sheet Metal has upped production by about 30 per cent and ensured greater control of its production accuracy. Samin invested in this equipment to gain faster output for its accuracy-critical markets while the doors are shut at nights and its production staff is at home in bed. Managing director, Wade Samin, says it is a significant move forward for the company which, until now, had been operating purely on day shifts using a sizeable amount of manual labour.

“We’re using the LC6 now to run an unmanned operation through the night which is greatly increasing our output,” said Mr Samin. “Before the LC6, we were just running a normal day shift with production staff loading the machine for laser cutting and unloading.” By day, the Prima Power LC6 is manned while it completes more complex or once-off jobs that may require human intervention. By night, the machine cuts multiple parts or completes custom jobs. Mr Samin confirmed that it has enabled the company to increase productivity without increasing staff.

“It’s allowed us to take on extra work and get more product out of the door,” he said.
