# Laser ablation system

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

AMADA MIYACHI EUROPE has launched the MIYACHI EAPRO Automatic hypotube coating removal system, a laser ablation system for removal of a range of biomedical coatings on round and cylindrical components including hypotubes, springs, coils and guide wires. The laser ablation systems may also be used for advanced surface treatments including roughening, discolouration, foaming and darkening or annealing. The clean non-contact laser process reduces or eliminates dust during manufacturing. It can be used to process most plastic and metal parts fed as a bundle or on a reel.

"The system provides a fully automated process that saves time and improves production processes, enabling higher throughput. The laser process creates a cleaner cut with smoother edges when compared to grinding, as well as a much cleaner surface after processing," explained the manufacturer.

With the standard system as the starting point, AMADA MIYACHI EUROPE collaborates with customers to redesign or modify the system to suit specific applications. Customers may also select the correct laser for their process. Other system options are available, including automatic OK/NOK selection for output bin, in-line on-the-fly wire diameter measurement and automatic wire length measurement.
