# Laser in focus

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

AT this year’s LASYS, the international trade fair for system solutions in the laser material processing segment from 31 May to 2 June 2016 in Stuttgart, ROFIN will shine a spotlight on application-oriented system solutions (Hall 4, booth B31).

“The ROFIN Performance Unlimited manual welding laser, which makes its debut at the exhibition, will show visitors just how easily and simply long or bulky parts, such as those used in the manufacture of surgical instruments or endoscopes, can be welded. We will also demonstrate precision welding of micro contours at our flexible laser workstation, the MPS Compact, using the StarFiber P long pulse fibre laser,” the company explained.

“Visitors to the CombiLine Basic laser workstation will be able to experience laser marking of free-form surfaces live. A selection of laser beam sources across the entire technology spectrum – from picosecond and femtosecond lasers to lowpower sealed-off CO lasers, and diode lasers to high-power fibre lasers – round off the laser solutions offered at the ROFIN exhibition stand.”
