# MAKING ITS MARK

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

XFocus 500 fiber laser system for cutting and marking

WITH its new fiber laser system XFocus 500, Kjellberg Finsterwalde extends its range of products for the laser cutting and marking of alloyed and unalloyed steels as well as aluminium. In addition to the XFocus 1000, another fiber laser system is now available for cutting thin sheet. The compact XFocus 500 system, with a laser performance of 500W, is suitable for marking and cutting mild steel from 0.5 to 5mm thick, stainless steel from 0.3 to 5mm thick and aluminium from 1 to 3mm thick.

“The optimum cutting parameters for different applications and quality requirements are provided by an integrated technology database. In total, up to nine different cutting speed values are available for each type of material and material thickness. The focus position of the laser head, as well as the gas pressure, is also adjusted automatically via the database,” explained the manufacturer.

Like the XFocus 1000 fiber laser system, the XFocus 500 features an integrated cooling unit, axis with height control and a laser head with motor-controlled focus position adjustment as well as an automatic gas supply. XFocus fiber laser systems can also be used with all common guidance systems, either individually or alternating with a plasma cutting unit, as a complement to plasma cutting thin sheet.

At the Essen trade fair for welding and cutting, Kjellberg Finsterwalde showcased new electrodes for both tasks – the Garant X for welding and the Kjelcut for cutting. See www.kjellberg.de
