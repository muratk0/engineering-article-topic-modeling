# Fibre laser cutting in focus

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Eagle Lasers' iNspire 2.01530 is the latest version of the Polish manufacturer's flagship fibre lasercutter with power of up to 40kW, a maximum positioning speed of 350m/minute and a maximum cutting speed of 150m/minute. It is fitted with a carbon fibre traverse and Eagle-manufactured linear motors on all axes.

"The new 2.0 includes a redesigned 24-inch 'Full HD' vertical multi-touch screen panel equipped with a supersensitive override step potentiometer, intelligent illuminated buttons and hand gesture recognition.

Additionally, the iNspire 2.0 features Eagle Eye, an advanced monitoring system enabling remote fault detection for seamless operation. Enhanced machine parameters offer augmented process control and flexibility, with broader customisation and adaptability," the laser-cutting specialist told ISMR.

The latest upgrade of Eagle's patented eVa cutting head with automatic nozzle changer technology is designed to ensure faster performance. Scanners are strategically placed on the pallet changer to maximize safety and efficiency, automatically resetting the machine upon detecting any issues.

"The iNspire 2.0 is an important step in the continuous refinement of what, at Eagle, we call 'The Ideal Machine'," said Camillo Brena, Eagle's marketing director.

"It is a goal that we will intentionally never reach because there will always be room to innovate and adapt to new requirements," he continued.

In today's industrial landscape, where efficiency and productivity are paramount, fibre laser technology has emerged as a game-changer. Its widespread adoption is fuelled by technological advances; enhanced reliability; reduced space requirements; simplified operation and lower energy consumption compared to its predecessors.
