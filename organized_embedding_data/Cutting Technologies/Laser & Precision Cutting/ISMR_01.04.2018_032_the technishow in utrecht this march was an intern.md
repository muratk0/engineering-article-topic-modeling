THE TECHNISHOW IN Utrecht this March was an international showcase for industrial production technology. Coherent/ROFIN displayed its extensive selection of application-specific laser processing solutions at the exhibition.

Performance manual welding system

"The Coherent/ROFIN HighLight FL3000C is the company's first fibre laser with 3kW of output power generated from a single fibre laser module. The compact laser can be equipped with either a 50μm (15m length) or 100μm (20m length) fibre. The combination of various power levels and the range of available fibre core diameters can match specific application requirements for a broad range of processing tasks. The lasers can operate in either CW or pulsed mode, at repetition rates of up to 5kHz. The 1μm output wavelength is strongly absorbed in many materials and is especially suitable for processing highly reflective materials," said the manufacturer.

"All lasers in the HighLight FL Series, which range from 500 - 10,000 watt output power, are easily field-serviced, modular pumping units, helping to reduce operating and maintenance costs."

Coherent/ROFIN's welding product portfolio includes the manual Desktop and Performance welding systems frequently used as workhorses in rough workshop environments. Now in its 7th generation, the Performance offers welding in a wide field of applications.

"Controls such as a large touch-screen, dynamic foot switch and weld assist functions simplify everyday work. The patented SweetSpot resonator guarantees consistent weld quality and constant depth of focus from the first laser pulse to the last. The integrated TrueView hardware eliminates any parallax misalignments and enables a 100% hit rate, even when working out of focus," added the company.

Coherent/ROFIN's laser workstation CombiLine Basic is a solution for efficient laser marking: a floor standing workstation with a spacious working chamber, an integrated laser marker and smart operation technology. It can be equipped with a broad selection of Coherent/ROFIN laser sources for marking and micro material processing, such as fibre and solid-state lasers in different power ranges and wavelengths, for a wide range of applications like corrosionfree marking of metal, precise engraving of delicate structures as well as marking of various plastics.
