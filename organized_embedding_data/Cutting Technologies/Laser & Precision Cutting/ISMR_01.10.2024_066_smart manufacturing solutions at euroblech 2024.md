# Smart manufacturing solutions at EuroBLECH 2024

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Belgian sheet metal specialist, LVD Company NV, will be highlighting productivity solutions for smart manufacturing on stand F104 in Hall 12 at EuroBLECH 2024 (22-25 October 2024 in Hanover, Germany). On display will be 20kW laser cutting solutions; bending automation; folding and software technology (including a new fibre laser); a high-tonnage Easy-Form press brake; Easy-Cell robotic bending cell; fully automatic panel bender and the latest CADMAN® software.

“We will unveil a new laser cutting machine offering low investment and operating costs. An ultra-practical machine, it provides reliable cutting minus costly extra features. The new laser is available in three configurations to meet price/performance needs and deliver the lowest cost per part manufacturing. It can be equipped with a MOVit Load-Assist automation system," said the manufacturer.

LVD will demonstrate the diversity of its laser cutting technology using a LaserCube. This compact system will feature highpower 20kW cutting, bevel cutting at a maximum bevel angle of 50 degrees, mixed gas cutting and 19" Touch-L control. It will showcase smart cutting with optimised sheet utilisation; nano joints; micro welding; remnant creation and collision avoidance.

## An eye on bending

The Easy-Form 9 400/40 press brake will be the largest high-tonnage press brake that LVD has ever exhibited at EuroBLECH. The 400-ton machine is floor-mounted with no foundation required. It features a six-axis modular back gauge for high application versatility and a total of ten axes for precise bending of large and long parts. A built-in Easy-Form® Laser adaptive bending system has been designed for repeatable accuracy for quality assurance.

Easy-Cell 80/25 combines an Easy-Form 80-ton press brake with KUKA industrial robot to automate bending of parts from 100 x 100mm up to 1600 x 1200mm, weighing up to 25kg. The press brake features a 2500mm bend length, five-axis back gauge and 2500mm crowning table for a wide scope of applications. Easy-Cell features an adaptive bending system which is designed for bend angle accuracy from the first part. LVD CADMAN® software provides rapid, simple offline programming of both press brake and robot.

LVD's Multifold 2111 panel bender will demonstrate fully automatic loading, folding and stacking of workpieces up to a maximum bend length of 2100mm in material thicknesses of up to 2mm cold rolled steel with bend heights of up to 110mm. A flexible and time-efficient alternative to die bending for many applications, Multifold requires only one set of tooling (a universal set of servo-operated, up-and-down folding blades) to produce numerous profiles and various types of bends.

## Software for process flow

LVD's latest software releases will also be demonstrated, including CADMAN®-FLOW.

"CADMAN-Flow introduces a new generation of CADMAN offering improved process overview, smarter tool optimisation, easy ERP connection and faster, more efficient bend solutions. CADMAN-FLOW improves the speed and flexibility of the process flow from a 3D file to machine code to final product, offering significant gains in productivity," concluded LVD.

LVD is a well-known manufacturer of sheet-metalworking equipment including laser-cutting systems; punch presses; pressbrakes; automatic and semi-automatic panelbenders; guillotine shears and automation systems integrated to and supported by its CADMAN®software suite.
