# HyPrecision waterjet

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of advanced plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, has announced the introduction of its HyPrecision Waterjet, a complete line of waterjet products. These products include pumps ranging from 15 to 150 horsepower, precision cutting heads and abrasive delivery equipment. This announcement is the result of Hypertherm’s acquisition of AccuStream.

The systems feature Advanced Intensifier Technology to extend maintenance intervals, reduce maintenance time and maximize performance. Hypertherm estimates the total cost of ownership for a HyPrecision system is up to 20 per cent less than competitive systems, resulting in increased profitability for customers.

Examples of the practical engineering found only in HyPrecision waterjet pumps include optimized plunger length and diameter, combined with long bearing engagement for better alignment, resulting in maximum component life with minimum pressure fluctuation and placement of hydraulic rod seals and plunger bearings outside the hydraulic end cap for easier access and servicing. The addition of an advanced piston design minimises seal wear and greatly extends part life. See www.hypertherm.com/waterjet ■
