# Lasertube LT12 for rapid processing

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

BLM Group's Lasertube LT12 is a new laser cutting system, designed to meet the challenging needs of companies in the tube and profile processing industry. The new Lasertube, presented in the second half of 2023 at the Innova Open House, offers versatility and high performance. LT12 was also showcased at the Tube trade show in Düsseldorf, Germany, supported by an innovative application of artificial intelligence which intrigued booth visitors.

“The goal of this new system is to fill a gap in our Lasertube system offering, which is manifested between two products: the LT8.20, which focuses on flexibility and performance, and the LT14, which is dedicated to processing long and heavy profiles. LT12 is the solution for processing tubes, profiles and beams, keeping the focus on versatility and productivity. Many times, customers have asked us for a product larger than LT8.20, but the jump to LT14 was too great both in terms of footprint size and performance across the lower range. LT12 was created precisely to give many customers the opportunity to process 'light' tubes with great performance, but also to be able to process larger tubes when needed," explained product manager, Gianbattista Mazzoleni.

An interesting sector for the Lasertube LT12 is agricultural machinery.

“This is a vibrant and dynamic world, but one in which laser tube cutting technology has not yet shown its full potential as it has in other sectors. We see great opportunities since square tubes, rectangles and even open profiles (including 'C', 'L' and 'H' profiles whose size easily comes to around 300mm diameter) [fit] the size range of the LT12. There are also many structural assemblies in this sector that would benefit significantly in terms of reduced manufacturing cost from the use of a Lasertube system. Of course, this new product is also very interesting in light construction architecture, for making canopies or small sheds, with tubes or beams of up to 6-8mm thick and lengths typically longer than the traditional 6m," added BLM Group.

The machine concept is similar to the LT8.20 with a mandrel in the tail and a chuck to support the tube in the cutting zone. LT12 integrates the interpenetrating mandrel in the chuck, so that minimal scrap is guaranteed without the need for chuck or mandrel movement. Particularly on large tubes, this results in time savings in the scrap reduction stage and is consistent with the desire to have a larger machine but with strong processing time performance. LT12 is equipped with a chain-loading system for tubes from 8.5m up to 12.5m (fully automatic unloading is possible for lengths of up to 12.5m).

A special inverted smart template has been added that operates from above to hold the tube in place, reducing wear and tear on the support itself. There are also Active functions that characterise Lasertube systems and, in particular, the Active Scan, which quickly allows the correct position and size of the tube being cut to be verified.

The focusing head is another special feature consisting of a Tube Cutter with a longer focal length that allows it to process higher thicknesses than the LT8.20 (the machine can process tubes of up to 62kg/m in weight) and is able to tilt up to 50° from vertical, a full 5° more than the LT8.20 and LT14. Active Piercing and Active Focus are also available.

"The machine offers excellent accessibility and a relatively small footprint compared to the LT14. It is equipped with four cameras, which are necessary to see the cutting area that is inaccessible due to the fibre source, allowing complete visibility of the entire workpiece being processed," added the company.

LT12 is equipped with a mechanical machining unit capable of making threaded holes from M4 to M12. A 16-tool magazine with tool integrity control allows these machining operations to be fully automatic, programmed directly in the CAD/CAM design phase with ArTube.
