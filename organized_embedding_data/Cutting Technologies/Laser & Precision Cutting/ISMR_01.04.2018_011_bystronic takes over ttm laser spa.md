# Bystronic takes over TTM Laser S.p.A.

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

"SINCE NOVEMBER 2017, Bystronic and TTM Laser S.p.A. have partnered on joint sales activities. Since then, positive feedback from existing customers has shown that there is a strong demand in many markets for an even closer collaboration between Bystronic and TTM Laser S.p.A. Hence, both partners have now agreed on a full takeover of TTM Laser S.p.A. by Bystronic," the Swiss manufacturer told ISMR.

Bystronic offers system solutions for the cutting and bending of sheet metal. In addition to automated and networked solutions for sheet processing, the supplementary processing of tubes and profiles is an important step for many customers that allows them to position themselves more successfully on the market. Being able to purchase all these production solutions from a single source creates added value for customers.

With TTM Laser S.p.A.'s technologies, Bystronic is deepening its existing portfolio for the laser cutting process step with applications for tubes with diameters of between 12 and 815mm and for profiles. In addition, Bystronic is expanding its portfolio with solutions for the laser welding of large-format metal sheets.

TTM Laser S.p.A. is headquartered in Cazzago San Martino (Province of Brescia, Italy). The company, which has been active on the market since 2001, specialises in the development of 2D and 3D laser systems for cutting tubes and profiles and for the welding of large-format metal sheets. In 2017, with some 40 employees, it achieved a turnover of approximately 14 million euros.
