HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, has announced the launch of the Powermax30® XP, a new plasma cutting system that delivers high performance in a small portable package.

“The new system is 50 per cent more powerful than its predecessor, yet maintains the Powermax30’s light weight and small size. It features a two-in-one design for high power cutting on thick metal and detailed cutting on thin metal using FineCut® consumables. The recommended cut capacity is 10mm with a severance capacity of 16mm,” the company explained.

The system is easy to use with simple controls. In addition, it is rugged and reliable with Hypertherm Certified™ reliability to perform in the most demanding environments.

## Going linear

ARI METAL, a company specialising in the development of modular aluminium profile systems, offers a complete range of linear guides, as well as accessories, for linear material handling. Linear guides are increasingly used in the automation and construction of machine tools.

Over the years, ARI Metal has designed and manufactured various types of linear guides and has now designed a standard linear guide. The linear guides are made from structural aluminium profile systems, the core business of the company. These systems are based on modular anodised aluminium structural sections which are more flexible for mechanical engineering systems. For further details, see www.arimetal.it ■

DIVERSIFIED TECHNOLOGY COMPANY 3M has extended its Cubitron II range of abrasive solutions. The new Cubitron II Cut and Grind Wheels by 3M offer particular benefits for processes such as cutting, notching, gouging, fillet weld removal and bevelling of both mild and stainless steel. The wheels can remove the same amount of material with less pressure, making it easier for the operator to use.

David Atherton, senior marketing executive for the 3M Abrasive Systems Division, explained: “Due to the popularity of the Cubitron II range of products and the innovative new technologies that back them, we have been able to extend our product range to include a more specific cut and grind wheel. This means they cut faster and last longer. Tests show that the Cubitron II Cut and Grind Wheels achieve significantly increased output with less pressure required from the user.”

See www.3M.co.uk/abrasives
