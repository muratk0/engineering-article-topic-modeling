# TRUMPF acquires manufacturer of ultra-short pulsed lasers

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

SHEET METAL SPECIALIST TRUMPF acquired the laser manufacturer, AMPHOS, on 9 January 2018. This acquisition strengthens the TRUMPF portfolio of laser products and technologies.

AMPHOS was founded in 2010 as a spin-off of RWTH Aachen and the Fraunhofer Institute for Laser Technology ILT. It develops and produces ultra-short pulsed lasers with high output power for manufacturing and research applications. Ultra-short pulsed lasers are especially important in electronics manufacturing, where they are used to produce items such as printed circuit boards, displays and cover glass.

## An eye on BIEMH

MORE THAN 1,300 international leading companies from 20 countries have confirmed their attendance at the 30th BIEMH, International Machine Tool Biennial, which will take place from 28 May to 1 June 2018.

The key to AMPHOS lasers is InnoSlab technology, which was codeveloped by the company's founders while still a part of ILT. InnoSlab will allow TRUMPF to open up an entirely new range of parameters for its ultra-short pulsed lasers. AMPHOS develops ultra-short pulsed lasers that feature output power of between 200 and 400 watts. The company also offers high-power lasers for research applications that have an output as high as 1.5 kilowatts.
