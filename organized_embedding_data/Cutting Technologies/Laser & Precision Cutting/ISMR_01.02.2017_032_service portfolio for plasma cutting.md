# Service portfolio for plasma cutting

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

AS ONE OF the oldest manufacturers of plasma cutting systems in the market, Kjellberg Finsterwalde offers not only the latest plasma cutting techniques but also an extensive service portfolio. These include:

• Technical support via telephone and email as well as a 24-hour hotline for technical questions and problems.

• Individual technology and service training for users and service technicians.

• Customer-specific cutting demonstrations and cost calculations at Kjellberg Finsterwalde’ s application centre.

• Assistance on commissioning, production monitoring, maintenance and repair.

The Kjellberg service packages are the latest additions to the portfolio – individual maintenance services combined in profitable packages. Corresponding with the operating year or the operating hours of the plasma cutting unit, respectively, the packages include high-quality original spare parts and consumables, in line with the plan for the change of components. Regular maintenance measures help improve equipment availability permanently and reduce downtime and repair costs,” the plasma specialist told ISMR.
