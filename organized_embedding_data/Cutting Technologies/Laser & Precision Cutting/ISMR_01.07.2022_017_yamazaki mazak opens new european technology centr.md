# Yamazaki Mazak opens new European Technology Centre Laser in Milan

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Yamazaki Mazak opened a new European Technology Centre (ETC) Laser in Milan, Italy, in May.

Yamazaki Mazak opened new headquarters for its European Laser Business in Milan, Italy, and hosted an Open House event for its European Technology Centre (ETC) Laser on 26-28 May 2022.

Established more than a century ago in Japan, Yamazaki Mazak is a global specialist in laser-cutting equipment. Over its long history, the company has set up ten production plants and multiple sales subsidiaries worldwide, with 14 of them in Europe.

“The rising importance of our European subsidiaries, as well as new developments in laser-cutting technology, has ultimately led to the creation of a dedicated site, our new European Technology Centre (ETC) Laser alongside the Italian subsidiary, in Cerro Maggiore near Milan," Mazak explained.

The new facility features a large showroom where a selection of the company's flagship laser-cutting machines were in operation and a Clean Room, for maintenance operations on optical components (such as cutting heads and other technological solutions related to laser-cutting machines). The showroom also includes a nitrogen generator that, with a gas mixer, enables Mazak's engineers to develop new cutting technologies and processes. A CAD/CAM corner demonstrated Mazak software technology.

“Customers are able to enjoy multiple events and open days to better understand our technologies. This ample floor space hosts a complete line-up of machinery to enhance technical training courses with hands-on experience," added Mazak.

Mazak laser-cutting machine users also benefited from access to its European Parts Centre in Leuven, Belgium. Able to process 20,000 parts per month, 365 days per year, the warehouse can reportedly deliver 97% of the spare parts for all Mazak machines within 24 hours across Europe.

## Inaugurating the ETC Laser

During the exclusive three-day Open House event, customers saw an extensive selection of Mazak's laser-cutting machine range under one roof.

Two new machines were the stars of the show: the OPTIPLEX 3015 NEO S-15 and the FG-220, with fibre technology. The OPTIPLEX 3015 NEO S-15, a 2D fibre laser-cutting machine, features innovative Beam Shaping Mode technology for enhanced control of beam shape and diameter. It features a new MCT3 cutting head and new MAZATROL Smooth Lx CNC control system. Large front and side access doors, an adjustable control panel and easy accessibility to the workpiece make it suitable for versatile operations.

"The FG-220 is our latest fibre laser machine for round, square, rectangular and open profile (I, L, and H) structural material cutting. Integrating multiple production processes into one single machine means reduced set-up time, reduced lead time, fewer operators and reduced floor space requirements," Mazak told ISMR.

Four independent rotating chucks prevent the material from distorting, for precision even when working with complex contours.

The OPTIPLEX 3015 NEXUS FIBER 6kW and the OPTIPLEX 3015 FIBER III 10kW with CST Automation technology were also on display. As well as a suite of laser solutions, visitors to the new ETC Laser also saw models from the INTEGREX range of multi-tasking machines, as well as the new VARIAXIS C-600 simultaneous 5-axis vertical machining centre and QTE-200M entry-level turning centre.

Gaetano Lo Guzzo, Director Laser Business Europe, Yamazaki Mazak, commented: "Mazak has a rich heritage in the development and delivery of revolutionary laser-cutting technologies, and we welcomed laser customers from all over Europe to our new facility. It will not only act as a fantastic demonstration space but will also play a fundamental role in the overall operational life of many Mazak laser machines — from applications engineering and aftersales support through to the repair and maintenance of laser cutting heads.
