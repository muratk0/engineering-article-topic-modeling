# ACCENT ON AUTOMATION

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Automated work, which is gaining in importance and sophistication, will be one of TRUMPF’s highlights at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition. TRUMPF will present new solutions for all technologies, such as efficient laser cutting of varying sheet thicknesses – the primary task of the TruLaser 3030 – in booth C32/1-3 in Hall 11.

To demonstrate its efficiency in a lights-out operation, in October 2010 TRUMPF will launch its PalletMaster Tower. The pallet changer, with an integrated intermediate buffer, was developed by TRUMPF Japan. It converts the TruLaser 3030 into a complete automation cell. It can be controlled using the laser’s standard software, saving and processing different material types fully automatically.

The PalletMaster Tower, which can be installed in as little as a week, is designed for sheet formats of up to 3 x 1.5 metres and sheet thicknesses of 0.8 to 25mm; it is available for 6, 10, 15 or 20 pallets. While it is hardly larger than a regular pallet changer, it can be up to six metres high, depending on the number of pallets stacked on top of each other. Since all sheets and cut panels are on separate pallets, even sensitive materials will be safe from scratches.

When it comes to processing different materials, the TruLaser 3030 offers yet another innovation. At the EuroBLECH show, it will also be available with a sturdy 6-kW CO2 laser. This will make it faster compared to the current 5-kW version – up to 10 per cent for construction steel and 15 per cent for stainless steel. But it will also increase the flexibility of the laser, allowing even thicker sheets to be cut. See: www.trumpf.com
