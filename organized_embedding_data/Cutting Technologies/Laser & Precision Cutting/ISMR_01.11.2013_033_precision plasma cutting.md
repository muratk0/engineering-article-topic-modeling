# PRECISION plasma cutting

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

VICTOR THERMAL DYNAMICS announced the European launch of the Ultra-Cut XT series power sources for automated plasma cutting, as part of its integrated system of components that deliver higher productivity and lower cutting costs, at the recent Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition in Germany.

Ultra-Cut XT systems enable parts to go directly from the cutting table to welding, painting or assembly without expensive secondary operations. It delivers ISO 9013:2002 Class 3 or better cuts on any material up to 50mm thick, noticeably reducing bevel and the need for post-cut finishing.

“Consistently producing quality holes with an automated plasma cutting system can be a big challenge for fabricators. The best results can be obtained with a fully integrated system that allows the CNC controller to intelligently control the capabilities of an advanced high precision power source, such as a Victor Thermal Dynamics Ultra-Cut® system and iCNC™ XT controllers,” explained the manufacturer.

Victor Thermal Dynamics has developed its Diameter PRO software technology which facilitates this integration of components allowing the Ultra-Cut® system plasma systems to cut bolt-ready holes with minimum-to-no-taper. As soon as the holes are cut, the work piece is ready for finishing or assembly. See www.victortechnologies.eu
