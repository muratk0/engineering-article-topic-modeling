ISMR viewed TRUMPF's new TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber, combining the high speeds of a solid-state laser with a broad range of processing capabilities, at the German sheet metal specialist's IN-TECH show in Ditzingen, Germany. The new machine can cut tubes and profiles with diameters of up to 254mm and materials as thick as 10mm. In addition, it is designed to make high-quality bevel cuts up to 45 degrees. At the heart of the machine is a four-kilowatt TruDisk 4001, a high-power disk laser manufactured by TRUMPF. A RapidCut function further increases productivity.

Two all-electric tube bending machines from Unison Ltd are helping Ultibend Industries to increase the manufacturing productivity of stainless steel tube fittings for the food and beverage processing systems at its manufacturing facility in Wellington, New Zealand. The machines automate the bending of tube fitting shapes, such as elbows and U-bends, in sizes from 1/2 to 4 inches in diameter.

Together, these benders are involved in making all Ultibend Industries bends in the sizes ½ inch to 4-inch diameter. Since installation, its 130mm single stack tube bender has produced 320,912 bends and the 80mm single stack tube bending machine bender that works on the smaller sizes has made 558,441 bends. Bends from these tube benders go to projects around New Zealand, Australia and the U.S., especially dairy factories, breweries and, in some cases, to the automotive industry.

Tracto-Technik PBS is a leading specialist in the pipe bending sector. Its product range currently covers all-electric and electrohydraulic CNC pipe bending machines, semi-automatic pipe bending machines, machines for pipe end processing and hydraulic joining technology, pipe measuring systems as well as special solutions and software solutions for pipe processing.

Pipe Bending Systems (PBS) is aimed primarily for pipe processers with singlepart or small batch production and large product variety. The company's TUBOMAT has been fundamentally revised and modularly designed. This pipe bender (up to 48.3mm pipe OD) also optionally integrates the sawing, deburring, flaring and cutting-ring assembly functions. Various technical improvements have been made to all working functions, such as the tooling concept, handling and control techniques, workflow and energy consumption. The modern control concept provides program operation as well as a "4-fold bending angle pre-selection mode" and also enables bending data import from TT's own PIPEFAB software solution.

For pipe pre-fabrication, TT also offers its semi-automatic TUBOBEND pipe bender series and the fully automatic TUBOTRON CNC pipe bender series. The modular TUBOBEND machine prototypes are optionally available as right-hand or left-hand benders. The exposed bending head, which allows for large bending space, means that they can bend highly complex pipe shapes. The TUBOTRON 25 MR, an all-electric CNC pipe bending machine with multi-radii capability, bends pipes with an outside diameter of up to 25mm.
