# LASYS

[TARİH: 01.05.2012 ISMR]

The focal points of the biannual LASYS exhibition at Stuttgart Messe (Germany) are machines, processes and services, including laser-specific machine subsystems. Cross-industry orientation covers an extensive range of materials at what has become a wellrecognised European industrial show for laser material processing.

The LASYS exhibition is targeted towards all those users looking for material processing solutions using lasers. Applications include:-

■ Mechanical engineering and automobile production

■ Optical industry

■ Metalworking and processing industry

■ Plant and apparatus construction

■ Electrical engineering and electronics industry

■ Medical technology industry

■ Precision engineering

■ Plastics industry

■ Semiconductor industry.

4000 visitors, across 10,000 m2 of exhibition space, are expected by the show organisers who also anticipate 200 exhibitors (35% from abroad). Due to their versatility, flexibility, high precision and reliability, lasers are now often preferred to other processes. The application areas for lasers therefore include inscribing, marking, cutting, welding, drilling, removing material, structuring, hardening and coating/finishing, at times both in macro processing and micro processing. More and more new applications are being added e.g. cleaning of sculptures or high-gloss polishing in toolmaking and mouldmaking. A light beam is also a match for the widest possible range of materials: metal, plastic, semiconductors, glass, ceramics, wood, textiles or paper.

## Highlights for 2012

LASYS 2012 has a new promotional supporter, the Working Group for Lasers and Laser Systems for Material Processing from the German Engineering Federation (VDMA).

There will be a number of new highlights at this year's LASYS exhibition including a new special show "Megatrends – made by Laser", a Solution Centre "Meet the Experts", the Stuttgart Laser Technology Forum 2012 on 13/14 June (SLT), the Stuttgart Laser Marketplace, the Specialist Forum "Lasers in Action" and a short course on "Basic Knowledge of Lasers and Laser Material Processing".

Around 350 participants attended the last SLT 2010 presentations, given by over 60 speakers. What makes the Stuttgart Laser Technology Forum special is the synergy effect it creates when "science meets the laser industry". In accordance with the philosophy of the organiser, the Institut für Strahlwerkzeuge (IFSW), everything from the laser source to beam control, right down to the application, is covered.

The Stuttgart Laser Technology Forum 2012 will be organised under the management of Professor Thomas Graf, Director of the ISFW and Dr. Rudolf Weber, Head of Process Development at IFSW.

The focus of SLT'12 will be on removing precision micro material processing (such as drilling and structuring), process understanding in high-performance macro material processing (such as welding and cutting), system technology for process safety as well as user experiences of modern laser sources such as disc and fibre lasers.

Baden-Württemberg is a leading location for optical technologies. The Stuttgart region is home to important customers for laser technology and various synergies have been created by running parallel events (such as the automotive trade fairs from UKIP Media & Events Ltd., plus O&S). The Stuttgart Trade Fair Centre is located next door to Stuttgart Airport and just off the A8 motorway.
