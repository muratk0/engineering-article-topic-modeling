PRC Laser Corporation

PRC Laser Corporation is pleased to debut two Laser innovations in expandng niche markets. Over the years, many customers have expressed interest in longer sheet cutting capability with no need for wider sheets; since most sheets are 4′ and 5′ wide. However, a growing number of shops are ordering sheets longer than 8′ or 10 feet. The PRC M513 Falcon (extended length unit) offers 13 foot cutting capacity on a 5 foot wide table without the price premium of most 6′ × 12′ or 4020 models.

Perhaps even more exciting is the new PRC Apex 4×4 Laser with a Tilt-Table innovation. PRC "has your back" with a motorized gear drive that brings the table to a vertical or apex position, similar to a draw bridge. The Apex provides a safe and convenient way to load 4×4 sheets with minimal strain. The PRC Apex 4×4 has a sheet weight capacity of over 400 pounds and utilizes helical rack and pinion drives along with AC Servos.
