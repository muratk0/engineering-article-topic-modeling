# Innovative 3D laser metal cutting

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

JENOPTIK’S LASER PROCESSING SYSTEMS business unit of its Lasers & Material Processing division received the 2013 Vision Award for the JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM. This machine is designed for cutting complex 3D metal parts.

The Jenoptik division had presented the JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM (Beam in Motion) laser cutting machine live in the US for the first time in Autumn 2012. The machine combines a stationary laser with robot-assisted motions. The core is a laser robot with integrated beam guide.

“The robot arm is unique because the laser beam path is fully integrated and can be controlled at extreme repeat accuracy of +100 µm,” Christon Manzella, Director - North American Sales of Jenoptik’s Laser Processing Systems business unit, explained.

With the new machine concept, laser outputs of up to, at present, 5 kilowatts can be used. The benefits of a robot system, such as high mobility and accessibility, precision and dynamics of the system, are not lost. The JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM is used for cutting 3D metal parts, particularly in the automotive industry for example, for complex 3D body and structural components or hydroformed pipes for exhaust systems and car body components. Contours and functional holes in white goods, tanks and vessels can be produced more easily. Other potential applications include the processing of very thin metal parts and challenging shapes such as those of an extraction unit or an exhaust unit.

Christon Manzella added: “The movements of the robot arm’s axes are up to 60 per cent faster than the norm in metal processing with laser and therefore allow shorter cycle times.” For more information, see www.jenoptik.com/en-lasermachines-laser-cutting-metal ■
