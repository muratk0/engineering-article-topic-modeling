# Waterjet Sweden to unveil new Ultra

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

At 10am on 25 October 2022, at this year's EuroBLECH trade show, Water Jet Sweden will launch its new Ultra machine concept for “advanced and flexible waterjet cutting." Its stand F164 is at the entrance of Hall 12 at the Hannover Messe.

"The market for waterjet cutting has developed and customers' requirements for waterjet cutting are increasing. More companies are discovering new areas for waterjet-cutting technology to meet new and complex manufacturing needs. The new machine we are launching is designed to meet today's requirements and future needs," outlined Lennart Svensson, CEO of Water Jet Sweden.

In 2020, the company released its Alphajet TVL cutting tool offering a new level of angle compensation. Since then, it has launched new systems for abrasive delivery and sludge management.

Water Jet Sweden develops, manufactures and markets advanced solutions for waterjet cutting as well as service and parts.
