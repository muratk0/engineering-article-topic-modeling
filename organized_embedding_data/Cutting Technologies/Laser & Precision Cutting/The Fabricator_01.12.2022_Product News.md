Automated station performs drilling, tapping, countersinking

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has introduced its Auto Drill 30 fully automated drilling system. It can drill holes with diameters up to 30 mm through plate thicknesses up to 50 mm, depending on material type.

The new system uses a completely new spindle design to expand drilling diameter and add capabilities. Equipped with a seven-tool changer to accommodate seven different tools within the same program, the system can also tap, countersink, and counterbore.

This option is available for the Suprarex HDX and NUMOREX automated cutting machines. It can be used in combination with stations for plasma beveling, oxyfuel cutting, inkjet marking, and pin stamping.

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products

www.esab.com

Fiber laser cutting machine designed for cost efficiency

LVD has introduced the Puma fiber laser cutting machine designed to provide high-technology features and performance at a lower total cost of ownership. Offered with a 3-, 6-, or 12-kW laser in 3,050- by 1,525-mm, 4,065- by 2,035-mm, and 6,160- by 2,035-mm formats, the automation-ready system handles a variety of cutting applications.

The welded steel, monoframe construction provides stability to achieve quality cutting at high feed rates, says the company. The cutting head incorporates crash protection, motorized focus-position adjustment, and capacitive height sensing, as well as automated functions for sheet referencing, cutting gas selection, pressure control, and nozzle cleaning.

The shuttle table system allows one table to be loaded or unloaded while cutting on the other table. Table changeover takes 35 seconds.

LVD

www.lvdgroup.com

Automated GMAW system provides high deposition rates

Miller Electric Mfg. LLC, Hobart Filler Metals, and Tregaskiss have announced the release of the Hercules single-wire, high-deposition, automated GMAW system. It delivers a gain of up to 30% or more in deposition rates and corresponding productivity with no loss of weld quality, according to the manufacturer.

The system is designed for medium to large manufacturing operations with robotic welding, specifically for those with long welds in the flat and horizontal welding positions. It comprises a Miller power source and wire feed technology, a Tregaskiss torch, and Hobart metal-cored wire.

Torches automatically set correct amperage, air pressure

Hypertherm Associates has introduced four new SmartSYNC torches for its Powermax SYNC plasma systems. The torches are available in 2- and 4-ft. lengths with torch head angles of 35 and 90 degrees.

The torches automatically set the correct amperage, air pressure, and operating mode upon installation of the consumable cartridge and allow the operator to change the cartridge without returning to the power supply.

CNC/CMM simulator offers training flexibility

Hexagon’s Manufacturing Intelligence division has introduced the HxGN Machine Trainer, a multiaxis CNC and CMM simulator. Manufacturers, educators, and students can use the operator-programmer trainer within a minimal physical footprint on the factory floor or in the classroom.

The simulator presents realistic machine preparation scenarios and provides interchangeable CNC controls to cover various types of machine language mentoring.

Brush keeps tooth gullets clean for optimal cutting results

The M. K. Morse Co. has released the Tooth Brush, a magnetic chip brush that helps to ensure optimal cutting conditions with industrial band saw blades. The patent-pending design features 11 brush configurations to fit almost any saw and clear chips from blade teeth gullets. The brass brush is designed for simple installation on all machines. The assembly includes a 4-in., 300-μm brass brush; two 50-lb. magnets with antiskid covers; and a pack of hardware including bolts, nuts, washers, and a sleeve.

The M. K. Morse Co.

www.mkmorse.com

GTAW package brings cold-wire components to welding machine

Fronius offers GTAW cold-wire components for its iWave. The patented, new TIG DynamicWire welding package actively adjusts the wire speed to the welder’s behavior, torch position, and current conditions, including automatically compensating for component tolerances of up to 30%.

The iWave power categories from 300i to 500i can be upgraded to the cold-wire system. The welding package is ready for use immediately by means of software activation and can also be retrofitted to existing systems at any time.

The wire feeder is set once and then remains in a stable position. The flexible tungsten holder is lightweight, and there are no vibrations at the handle.
