# Powermax45 hits 100,000th unit

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, is marking a major milestone with the production of its 100,000th Powermax45® plasma cutting and gouging system.

“This milestone makes the Powermax45 our bestselling plasma system. It easily beats our previous record set in 2007, when we announced we had sold 50,000 Powermax600® units,” the company told ISMR.

Introduced in 2008, the Powermax45 is portable — weighing in at 16kg — but powerful enough to cut 12mm thick metal and sever 25mm thick metal, if needed. The system is designed for handheld or mechanized cutting and gouging.

The 100,000th system was outfitted with a special commemorative cover signed by dozens of Hypertherm Associate-owners, including company founder Dick Couch, and shipped to Savannah, Georgia for the Gases and Welding Distributors Association (GAWDA) Spring Management Conference, which began on 3 April 2016.

Conference attendees were given the opportunity to bid on the system during a silent auction. The winning bid was made by Dave Walker, treasurer at GAWDA, who offered US$ 2,525 for the system. Proceeds from the auction were donated to GAWDA Gives Back, which provides funding to in-need non-profit organisations in the city of the GAWDA Annual Convention. ■
