# The path to greater productivity

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Sheet metal specialist, Prima Power, will be showcasing new modular and scalable technologies at EuroBLECH 2024 (22- 25 October 2024 in Hanover, Germany). Reflecting this year's EuroBLECH motto 'The Power of Productivity', Prima Power will demonstrate dynamic solutions designed to level up the manufacturing processes of its customers on stand B146 in Hall 12.

## Platino Linear with Compact Server

The Platino Linear 1530 2D laser machine, the new 2D laser solution, is equipped with an 8kW fibre laser. The Compact Server loading/unloading system further optimises overall throughput per square metre.

"At EuroBLECH 2024, the Platino Linear will be showcased with features designed to further increase its overall profitability. The maintenance-free linear drive enhances machine uptime and reduces operating costs due to its high energy efficiency. With its compact footprint, the Platino Linear achieves high throughput per square metre with a 22% reduced footprint. It also offers lower cost per part with gas savings of up to 40%, reduced energy consumption and up to 96% sheet utilisation. Advanced cutting options, such as the GAS MIXER Cut and ECO TOUCH Cut, optimise cutting processes by enhancing speed, reducing gas consumption and minimising burrs in a variety of materials," explained the Italian manufacturer.

## Robotic press brake bending cell

The Prima Power robotic press brake bending cell in Hanover will integrate the eP Genius 1030 press brake, with automatic tool-changing system, and a seven-axis anthropomorphic robot which connects the press brake to high-end Night Train® storage (creating a fully automated flexible manufacturing system).

“The robotic press brake bending cell is profitable for small and large batches due to its easy part programming, automatic gripper change and modular automatic tool change (up to 30% faster than the market average). Its efficiency in unmanned production enables fully automated operations, eliminating all manual intervention except for the removal of finished parts from the work area," said Prima Power.

## PSBB flexible manufacturing system

The PSBB (Punch-Shear-Buffer-Bend) line is a fully integrated system for manufacturing rectangular parts and thin materials. The system to be showcased at EuroBLECH 2024 includes the Shear Genius 1530 EVO combined punch and shear system and the Express Bender EBe 2726 FM panel bender.

“The PSBB line provides integrated automation, enabling 24/7 automated production for kit, batch and mass production. By reducing low added-value manual operations, PSBB results in four times higher production efficiency compared to conventional manufacturing cells. The Shear Genius 1530 EVO is designed for square parts and panels. It combines various processes, (including punching, tapping, forming, marking and shearing) with up to 600 tools available inside the machine without additional tool storage. It delivers truly sustainable production through high sheet optimisation (20% better than conventional technologies), low power consumption (25% compared to conventional technologies) and reduced maintenance requirements. The integration of the Fast-Loading Storage sheet-bysheet loading device provides an efficient and economical solution when production demands require quick raw material changes," added the company.

The bending solution integrated into the PSBB line on show at EuroBLECH will be the Express Bender EBe2726 FM fully servo-electric panel bender, with a new bending height of 264mm. The DNP (device for narrow parts) option enables the bending of narrow parts and profiles. The Express Bender on display in Hanover will include advanced angle control systems, such as the thickness measurement system (TMS) and patented advanced profile inspection (API).

## All-in-one software solutions

Prima Power's all-in-one software suite, featuring NC Express®and Tulus®Office, is designed to improve continuous and integrated production flow. NC Express is a versatile CAM software designed to program a wide range of Prima Power machines, with advanced toolpath generation and optimisation algorithms to enhance automatic programming.

"EuroBLECH 2024 will see the world premiere of the new NC Express BEND, designed specifically for panel bending operations. It streamlines programming, reduces set-up time and boosts productivity. Serving as an integrated MES platform for comprehensive production planning and monitoring, Tulus Office provides real-time insights and data analytics to optimise manufacturing efficiency," concluded Prima Power.■
