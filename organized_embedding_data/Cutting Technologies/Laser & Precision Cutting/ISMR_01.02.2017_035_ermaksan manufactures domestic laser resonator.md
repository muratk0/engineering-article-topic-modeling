# Ermaksan manufactures domestic laser resonator

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

ERMAKSAN MAKINE, ONE of the leading manufacturers in the sheet metal processing sector, has set up ‘Turkey’s first private sector semi-conductor plant’ and started exporting the machines it produces using a domestically developed national fibre laser beam source, national laser chip and controller systems.

The latest sheet metal processing technology (fibre laser cutting systems, press brakes, shears etc.) from Ermaksan was exhibited on its stand number 105 at the recent Metal Processing Technology Fair, held at the TÜYAP Bursa Fair Centre in Turkey.

The domestically-produced laser beam sources have been put into operation as has the first national laser chip, developed by Optoelectronic AR-GE Centre, Ermaksan’s R&D Centre established with its own engineering experience,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

Ermaksan Makine’s general director, Ahmet Özkayan, commented on Ermaksan Optoelectronic technological products (such as semi-conductors, laser beams/resonators, laser chips, software and CNC controllers as well as sheet- and pipe cutting machines, plasma, punch, bending and guillotine shears): “We manufacture at our 96,000 sq. metre hightech production facility in the Bursa Organized Industrial Zone. Ermaksan currently employs 800 people. We continue with AR-GE and innovation activities to improve the capabilities of the machines we produce, meet the demands of the global market, continuously improve customer satisfaction and reduce the dependency of our country on machine production,” he said.

With the contribution of our Optoelectronic R&D Centre, today we are proud to be among the world’s most important laser chip manufacturers. The next most important technological production after the chip was the laser beam source (resonator). This YGL-3000 fibre laser beam source is the first to be 100% produced in Turkey, by Turkish engineers.”

Özkayan added that environmental awareness is increasing day by day and, accordingly, Ermaksan has designed this awareness into its machine tools.

In the sheet processing machine sector, an average of 300 litres of hydraulic oil is used in most of the machines that are produced. This oil needs to be changed and disposed of every three years. We have completely removed the use of hydraulic oil with the press brake, that we call the Green Press, which is designed using servo-electric motor technology.”
