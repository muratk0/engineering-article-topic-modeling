Machine offers practical, simplified fiber laser cutting

LVD Strippit offers the YSD LaserOne fiber laser cutting system, designed for simplicity and cost-effectiveness. It is offered with a 2- or 4-kW laser power source and 120- by 60-in. table with optional Load-Assist automation.

Automated program for 90-degree cuts developed for sawing system

With the addition of the optional Auto-90 program feature, Scotchman’s SUP-600 NF AngleMaster saw runs completely automatically for 90-degree cuts only. The system feeds materials and makes 90-degree cuts continuously.

This upcut circular cold saw is designed for cutting nonferrous material such as aluminum and copper. It features a 24-in., 132-tooth carbide blade that can produce round cuts up to 8 in. dia. and rectangular cuts up to 9 by 5 in. at 90 degrees. When coupled with the AngleMaster digital material positioning system, it serves as a semiautomatic, programmable saw system that automatically rotates to any cut angle, pushes material into the saw, and automatically rotates to the next desired angle.

3D hand-held scanners achieve 0.2-mm resolution

Exact Metrology has increased the capability of its Artec 3D hand-held scanners with a new HD mode. With this artificial intelligence-powered scanning technology, users can obtain sharp 3D scans with a resolution of up to 0.2 mm.

Trained on hundreds of thousands of carefully selected samples, the engine’s neural network detects familiar patterns, surface details, and shapes. This allows the 3D scanner to reconstruct a higher number of polygons per frame, resulting in dense, high-quality 3D data, the company reports.

Laser system measures tube ODs

Marposs has introduced the Aeroel Barline.X system for measuring the OD of products manufactured in cut lengths, such as steel drawn tubes and copper tubing. With online gauging and continuous through-feed measurement, the system provides real-time grinder regulation, automatically preventing the production of out-of-tolerance products.

The system can measure diameter and ovality of stock from 0.00394 to 5.86 in. using either a single- or dual-axis (XY) laser gauge, depending on the diameter range and the type of product. The noncontact system is impervious to product vibration and movement, enabling continuous inspection.

In most cases, the sensor can be installed just after the grinding wheel and before the oiling device so that the use of oil doesn’t interfere with gauging accuracy. In this position, the tube can be cleaned easily by blowing away the emulsion.

The system features a patented self-calibration device that helps ensure permanent gauge accuracy with no remastering required. Software offers guided menus to simplify use, even for untrained staff. Factory preprogramming makes the system immediately operative in its basic functions.

Pipe cutter integrates automated material handling system

HGG offers the small-footprint ProCutter900 RB for cutting precision profiles from 2- to 36-in.-dia. pipe up to 42 ft. The machine integrates a totally automated material handling system on a small footprint. With the infeed conveyor, raw material is fed continuously into the machine, cut and profiled with a pantograph cutting arm, and then systematically moved out parallel to the cutting table.

Features include a two-point fume extraction system, proprietary software for 3D parameter-based part profile programming and work preparation, a three-jaw chuck for secure pipe positioning, a pipe support and cutting bed with roller bed for loading/unloading, a cutting trolley with biaxial cutting head using plasma, and an operator interface with graphic touchscreen navigation.
