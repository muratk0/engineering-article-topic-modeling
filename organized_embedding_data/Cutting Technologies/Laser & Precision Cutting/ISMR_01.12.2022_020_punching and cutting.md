# Punching and cutting

[TARİH: 01.01.2022 ISMR]

EffiCOlL is a coil-fed combination punch laser system from Produtech Srl. The line is equipped with two compact and independent working heads (electric punching unit and fibre-optic laser cutting unit) which run aligned, via an innovative electronic system, on a single line. It can cut, punch and form any shape or geometric figure, producing parts directly from coil.

"The units are perfectly synchronised and run on the same work area, with the highest precision and speed," said Produtech. "EffiCOlL is an innovative and flexible solution which cuts and forms coil sheet."

Punch laser technology meets the production requirements of different sectors, offering high efficiency rates with reduced environmental and economic impact. It enables energy and raw material savings and reductions in processing time, labour, wearable components, workspace and emissions.

"The technological value, innovation levels and environmental sustainability of this laser-punching machine were ratified by the European Commission, who financed the design and production of the first prototype with Horizon 2020 project funds," added the Italian coil processing specialist.

This coil-fed machine enables two complementary production technologies to interact in a single working area. The technology is used to produce structures for household appliances; steel furniture; shelving and work tools' drawer units; electrical equipment and lighting components, as well as panels and structures for building construction. It has given a significant boost to the growth of the Produtech brand globally.
