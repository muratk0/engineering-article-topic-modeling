# Laser or HF induction welding?

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

DREISTERN’S shop floor is presently displaying two roll forming lines for small welded profiles. The product of both lines is the same – window spacer bars with a sheet metal thickness of 0.2 to 0.3mm. But all other characteristics of these lines are very different.

“The induction line produces 200 metres of aluminium profiles per minute, while the laser line supplies 60 metres per minute. The laser line processes zinc-coated steel and features much lower material costs compared to aluminium. In addition, the laser line can also process stainless steel which provides 35 per cent better heat insulation than aluminium spacer bars. The laser line can also be upgraded for the production of plastic-steel composite profiles.

The option to produce cost-effective steel and efficient stainless steel or composite profiles makes the laser line a very attractive alternative to HF induction lines," said the manufacturer.

Both lines are equipped with a new tooling concept which reduces the tooling cost by almost 50% compared to the present solution. The reduced tooling cost and short change-over time enables economical production of a high number of different product sizes. The choice between the laser and the induction alternatives still remains a challenging task, concluded Dreistern.
