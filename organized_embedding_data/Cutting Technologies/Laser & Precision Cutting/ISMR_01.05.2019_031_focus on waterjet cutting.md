Resato International's product range includes high-pressure technology waterjet cutting systems as well as components and systems for testing, injection and control that operate up to 14,000 bars. STM is a leading provider of waterjet cutting systems with its head office in Eben, Austria, and Schweinfurt, Germany. It has developed the OneClean modular system. The abrasives recycling module of the OneClean system makes it possible to recycle more than half of the garnet for additional cutting tasks. This saves procurement, disposal and transportation costs which, said the company, make amortisation possible within about three – five years at an approx. annual abrasive consumption of 50 tons.

Swift-Cut Automation has introduced Swift-Jet, its new waterjet cutting solution. Offering precision cutting, this smart machine can be used to cut metals; stone; composites; plastics; tile; glass; leather; rubber; textiles; foam; food; paper and more.

"The Swift-Jet, with its easy-to-use software, and powered by Hypertherm HyPrecision pumps, can cut a wide range materials and is the answer to affordable, high precision, high-quality, in-house cutting," said the manufacturer.

The Swift-Jet package provides componentry; pump; software; installation and post-sales technical support and training. With a choice of cutting bed sizes and 30HP and 50HP pump options, all Swift-Jet machines use Touchscreen Control Consoles, OMRON Servo Motors, 32mm ball screws on the X,Y,Z axes and are calibrated using the latest Renishaw software and ballbar testing equipment.

Its Electric Servo Pump is designed to be efficient and quiet.

At FABTECH2018, Water Jet Sweden showcased an Augmented Reality, 3D version of one of its most advanced waterjet machines.

"With the 3D AR version of our FiveX machine at FABTECH, visitors were able to not only get closer to an operational cutter than ever before. They were also able to look at it in 1:1, 1:4 or 1:10 scale, set it up wherever they want to and then take it home with them on their smartphone or tablet," explained Lennart Svensson, CEO & President of Water Jet Sweden.

Water Jet Sweden has one of the broadest waterjet product portfolios in the world. Its FiveX machine system is the latest machine model in the portfolio. With a Z-movement of 750-1500mm and a ±0-91º cutting angle, it is specifically designed to manage high-precision 3D abrasive cutting in space. All FiveX machines are designed with full size walls and automated front and back protection.

It features a new cost-efficient sensor function, fully integrated in the PanelOne HMI. When started, the safety lid opens, it automatically identifies the 3D workpiece placement and sets the zero point. Then, without halting, the program goes directly into cutting mode.

To cut narrow incisions in hard materials with high precision requires a specially developed waterjet cutting process. FAWJ stands for Fine Abrasive Water Jet (micro waterjet, finecut) and was launched in 2008 by Water Jet Sweden. Since then, the technique has been applied to specially developed precision machinery designed to manufacture micro-components. Water Jet Sweden has now broadened the possibilities and launched The FAWJ Micro Head Package for its ordinary machine models. It is designed for customers who wish to cut fine parts with narrow incisions and high precision, without the need for the most extreme micro part tolerances. Its "Micro" (NCM 10) is specially developed for the FAWJ-process and is used for the manufacturing of micro components. Micro waterjet cutting machine is a popular alternative to Micro-EDM and Laser.

Italian manufacturer, Waterjet Corporation Srl, has showcased its EDGE 5 cutting system with integrated ITC. Its machines include cantilever and bridge designs and 5-axis flying bridge technology (Edge 5) which delivers significant productivity improvements. The company also offers combination plasma and waterjet machines, as well as waterjet surface texturing technology.
