# Placing the focus on fibre laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

AMADA HAS LAUNCHED its ENSIS-3015 AJ fibre laser CNC cutting machine, featuring a 3kW source.

"At 3kW, the machine offers the industry's largest single-diode module size. Unlike other machines, there is no diode module beam combiner improving reliability and increasing machine uptime. Two machines have already been sold into the UK market," the Japanese manufacturer told /SMR. AMADA's patented ENSIS technology can change the laser beam mode, not just the focal spot size and focus position, for better beam control in thin and thick sheet processing. The machine also offers single-lens processing for all materials and thicknesses, and features a large-capacity nozzle changer to cover the full range automatically.

"The machine is capable of cutting up to 25mm thick mild steel, which is equivalent to the performance levels of a standard 6kW fibre laser, but uses just half the power to do the same job. With energy prices proving a major overhead for profiling and fabrication shops, this presents the opportunity to make significant savings," explained AMADA.

"The ENSIS technology provides a high-speed pierce in 20mm mild steel, and faster cutting than a 4kW fibre laser. This performance is achieved by instantaneously changing the beam mode between pierce and cut. For piercing materials at the thicker end of the material spectrum, oil-shot functionality is available."

The 3kW ENSIS also offers WACS II, the latest version of AMADA's proprietary Water-Assisted Cutting System. This device allows the amount of water to be varied in two stages, providing further assistance when processing thicker mild steel. Across a cutting range of 3000 x 1500 x 100mm in the X, Y and Z axes, processing feed rates of up to 100m/min. are possible, AMADA told /SMR. See www.amada.co.uk
