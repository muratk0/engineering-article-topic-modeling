# Cutting and marking

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

MESSER CUTTING SYSTEMS will be on booth C98 in Hall 13 at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (25-29 October 2016). Its MultiTherm® cutting machine combines three processes - laser cutting, plasma cutting and ink-jet marking - within one nested plan.

“So, for example, internal contours can be precisely cut with the fibre laser and external contours can be rapidly and economically cut using the plasma torch. Markings and charge numbers etc. are added by the inkjet with no damage to the material. This simplifies the cataloguing of individual parts and offers traceability benefits,” explained the manufacturer.

Its large MetalMaster 2.0 cutting system will be presented on other partner stands at EuroBLECH with live demonstrations of plasma and oxyfuel cutting under the motto “Compact cutting technology". Messer Cutting Systems offers its customers an interface for machine status, downtime and cutting times, with machine data captured with “Global Reporter“ software on its own brand control, Global Control, which can also be displayed on a smart device.

Messer has also been crafting an extensive service concept. Customers can pick and mix their requirements under individual Service Level Agreements for their machine for the long term. Its virtual service mode is dubbed ´Remote-Service 2.0’. See www.messer-cs.com
