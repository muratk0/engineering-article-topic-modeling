# High-end laser cutting in the XXL format

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

BYSTRONIC IS EXPANDING its ByStar Fiber laser cutting system with the extra-large 6225 and 8025 formats. This is designed to increase the productivity of fibre laser cutting and the diversity of cutting applications.

"More sheet metal, more parts, more variety. Extra-large-format laser cutting systems enable users to stand out from their competitors with an expanded processing portfolio. To support sheet metal processing companies with a large selection of machine formats, we are expanding the ByStar Fiber high-end laser cutting system to include the 6225 and 8025 formats. The machine can be equipped with laser outputs from three to 10 kilowatts, depending upon the users requirements," said Bystronic.

With the new extra-large formats, users can process metal sheets on the ByStar Fiber with a working area of up to 8 by 2.5 metres. This increases the machine's productivity, because large metal sheets allow the parts that are to be cut to be nested more efficiently and thus significantly reduce undesirable raw material offcuts. The BySoft 7 software supports users with smart nesting procedures that enable optimal cutting plans to be created for large formats.

"Frequently the key to success is to increase the variety of cut parts. With the ByStar Fiber's new 6225 and 8025 formats, this is no problem. Large-format metal sheets allow large parts to be cut in addition to diverse small parts, without requiring the machine to interrupt the laser cutting process during long cutting paths. This provides a competitive advantage that laser cutting systems in the common standard formats cannot offer," added Bystronic.

It is also possible to process several smallerformat sheets in one 'go'. The various metal sheets can be positioned on the ByStar Fiber's cutting table, as required. This enables users to increase the level of machine utilisation by reducing non-productive times and allowing the laser cutting system to operate for longer in an uninterrupted cutting cycle. As standard, all ByStar Fiber machines, including the new extra-large formats, are equipped with the latest generation Bystronic cutting head.
