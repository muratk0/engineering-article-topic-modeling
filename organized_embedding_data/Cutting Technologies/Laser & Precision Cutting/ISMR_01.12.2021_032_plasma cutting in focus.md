# Plasma cutting in focus

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

The Torchmate 4510 is the third addition to the Torchmate 4000 series product line.

Lincoln Electric® has introduced a new CNC plasma cutting table, the Torchmate 4510. Building on the success of the Torchmate 4400 and 4800, with years of use in the field by customers worldwide, Lincoln Electric has added a new CNC plasma cutting table to the 4000 series product line – the Torchmate 4510.

Built with the same design as the Torchmate 4000 series tables, the Torchmate 4510 is expanded to a five-foot by ten-foot (5x10) configuration and Lincoln Electric remains the single-source provider of the plasma table. The new CNC machine keeps all the premium components of the 4000 series tables, including FlexCut® plasma cutters, motion controls, a touchscreen and Torchmate CAD software. The machine is delivered fully assembled, enabling the machine to be ready to cut within 30 minutes of delivery.

“We designed the Torchmate 4510 table for improved production capabilities for those looking to optimise metal cutting. This machine offers the ability to accommodate a full 5 x 10 sheet of metal up to 1” thick when equipped with the optional FlexCut 125 power supply. Using 5 x 10 sheets of metal provides 36% more material capacity than a 4 x 8 sheet, maximising the number of parts that can be cut out and reducing material handling time,” said the manufacturer.

Manufactured in Reno, Nevada (USA), Torchmate machines consistently cut metal parts for a variety of customers. The Torchmate 4000 series is a line of CNC plasma cutting tables. Extensive training options include a complete virtual training programme. Torchmate 4000 series tables come with a two-year warranty.
