ISMR flew to Turkey in March to witness the launch of Ermaksan’s latest laser cutting machine – the Fibermak fiber laser cutter – and Evolution (EVO II) hybrid press brake. Ermaksan hosted 200 visitors from 40 countries at its Bursa factory for the launch.

## Rapid cutting operations

“Fibermak is a peerless machine in laser cutting technology with its strong design, ultra-low energy consumption, rapid cutting capacity and almost zero maintenance cost,” Ermaksan told ISMR. “It transmits the laser beam onto the sheet metal by fiber cables and its cutting quality with high beam density is well suited to thin sheet compared to other alternatives. Fiber laser cutting technology ensures high quality cuts at rapid speeds. It consumes 70% less energy than CO2 lasers, making it a green technology. Cutting reflective materials (such as aluminium, copper, brass etc.) is also easier.”

Fiber lasers are suitable for different applications such as welding, hybrid welding, remote welding, 2D and 3D remote cutting, heat treatment and cladding. Fibermak, says Ermaksan, can rapidly and efficiently process thin sheet. There is no need for a laser gas mixture or optical components as laser transmission is done by fiber cable. There is also no need for elements such as beam path, folding mirror, quartz tube, bellows and turbo blower etc. Users, said the company, can save 50% per each part cost through efficient 24-hour daily production.

Laser presentations were also given from Precitec (HPL SSL laser cutting head) and IPG Photonics (fiber laser). The HP SSL cutting head is suitable for use in flat bed systems and pipe-cutting machines with fiber-coupled lasers.
