# Laser measurement systems in focus

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

GL Control GmbH develops 360° laser sensors and systems for checking the internal geometries of pipe ends; ovality; roundness; welding inspection; surface defects etc. Its sensors can be used for the quality assurance of forming processes and weld seams. Weld seams and bends or radii can be measured and logged for quality management systems.

"Our sensors deliver precise data to the production control and can be integrated into automated production. For Industry 4.0, complex internal contours can be measured and recorded with our laser measurement technology," it explained.
