# HIGH PRESSURE CUT

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

With headquarters in Baxter Springs, Kansas (USA), KMT Waterjet started its business in the late 1960s. KMT delivers the entire high pressure line of a waterjet cutting system including the cutting head and abrasive supply chain – all its high pressure waterjet components are produced in-house. The equipment can be integrated onto any type of waterjet cutting system including standard-sized tables, robot applications, online cutting installations or other special machinery.

Waterjet’s cutting advantages for sheet metal formers and fabricators are well documented – no heat deformation of material, clean and burr-free edges with no secondary operations needed. Waterjets can also cut a wide range of different materials (thicknesses of 300mm and above) as well as multiple layers of material with no complex adjustments.

“We are constantly working on product improvements and new projects. With around 300 employees worldwide, we maintain a tight global sales and service network,” Wolfgang Emrich of KMT Waterjet told ISMR.

“Our objective is to keep developing waterjet cutting technology further and to make the technology available for a wider range of customers,” said Emrich. “Increasing the water pressure to the next level of 7,000 bar is very close to being introduced into the market. New materials are constantly being developed and cutting methods need to be made available for them. It is very difficult to find any material that waterjet cannot cut.”

## NEW HORIZONS

The company specialises in high pressure technology which primarily focuses on the waterjet cutting business sector, but it also produces waterblasting equipment which is used for surface cleaning and surface preparation as well as for high pressure testing. KMT shares partnerships with system manufacturers so that end-users can always find the right system to fit their needs.

“Like every other company in the machine tool industry, KMT was certainly impacted by the global economic downturn,” confirmed Emrich. “Sales slowed down and the company had to adapt to this new situation. Even before the downturn, customers were looking at the costs that they have to cover. This general behaviour has not changed, but customers are often looking for more than just lowest cost; product quality and highly developed aftermarket services with on time-delivery and 24h-service hotline gives them flexibility and helps them to secure their production.”

With about 7,000 KMT system installations worldwide, the majority of KMT’s customers are operating in industries such as metal shops, aerospace and job shops. Stone & Tile, automotive and other industries that machine soft materials cover the rest.

“Our major milestones to date are the 2006 launch of Streamline SL-V200 the largest pump in the industry, the 2007 launch of our new abrasive cutting heads (Active Autoline II and Active Ide II) and the 2009 launch of Streamline Pro-1 which operates at 6,200 bar,” Wolfgang Emrich told ISMR.

“7,000 bar high pressure systems will contribute to a further expansion of waterjet technology, since productivity will again increase and even more materials and thicknesses can be cut.”
