# Cook Compression chooses Kimla laser cutter

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

COMPRESSOR SOLUTIONS SUPPLIER, Cook Compression, has revolutionised its metal fabrication output by installing a Kimla LF1530 fibre laser cutting machine, replacing two of its CO₂ machines and reducing electricity usage by almost £1,000 per month. Installation of the new equipment, supplied by UK-based MBA Engineering, has enabled Cook Compression to significantly enhance its laser cutting services.

"The precision of the Kimla technology – the machine cuts to an accuracy of 0.03mm – has eliminated the need for Cook Compression's engineers to utilise additional metal finishing processes to effectively complete a project, saving on costs and valuable manpower," said MBA Engineering.

Although two machines have been replaced by one, Cook Compression's capacity has increased by 30% since the installation of the Kimla machine, enabling the compressor expert to manufacture an increased number of parts more efficiently. The Kimla technology can also cut metal with a thickness of up to 15mm, enabling Cook Compression to enhance the services it can offer to its customers.

Richard Wignall, managing director of Cook Compression, added: "Since installing the Kimla machine from MBA Engineering, we have experienced unprecedented results. During a recent project, we managed to cut components in two minutes 44 seconds, a tenth of the time it would have taken previously. We were concerned that the speed of the cutting process would affect the quality of the finished product, but we couldn't have been more wrong – all parts were of superior quality and cut with precision accuracy."
