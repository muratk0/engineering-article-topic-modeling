Laser Genius

by Prima Power—maximum performance on all materials and thicknesses

Laser Genius is the high-performance 2D fiber laser machine by Prima Power, world-class supplier in the high-tech field of laser and sheet metal working machinery. The machine offers superior productivity, quality and flexibility on the entire range of thicknesses.

The high dynamic linear drives, together with the effective CNC management, increase productivity and cutting quality on all materials and thicknesses compared with conventional drive systems. Innovative materials like stonecast and carbon fiber for machine frame and carriage increase machine performance and reliability.

The proprietary fiber laser up to 10 kW features low operating costs thanks to high energy efficiency and reduced maintenance and allows high process versatility. Laser Genius fiber laser head has adaptive optics for the automatic management of focal position and diameter and for fast, reactive and accurate stand-off measurement.

In order to best meet all production needs, Laser Genius can be integrated with several suites of options (SMART Cut, MAX Cut, NIGHT Cut, CONTROL Cut, NOZZLE CHECK and OPC BY CAMERA) and with Prima Power automation systems, including: Compact Server, Combo Tower Laser, automatic stacking robot LST, and connection to Night Train FMS.
