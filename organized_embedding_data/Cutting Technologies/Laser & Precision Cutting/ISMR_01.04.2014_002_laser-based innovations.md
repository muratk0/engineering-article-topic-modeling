# Laser-based innovations

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

LASHARE is the acronym for a European project, involving more than 30 SMEs from across Europe, large industrial enterprises and six well known laser research institutes. It is being coordinated by the Fraunhofer Institute for Laser Technology (ILT).

Its main objectives are to turn innovative prototypes into robust solutions, which can be transferred to industry and bring laser-based innovations to the market more quickly and reliably. Normally, innovative laser-based products take a long time before they are ready for industry. Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) developing technologies may sometimes lack the expertise and resources to match the technologies to the demands of industry and so investors may consider these technologies to be too risky. As a consequence, many innovative ideas never make it beyond the development level or small series production.

Laser Zentrum Hannover e.V. (LZH) is running a LASHARE subproject, based around cross-cutting. The project examines the relevant assessment criteria for developing new laser-based products and, based upon these, SMEs can evaluate their developments more easily and provide potential investors with more security. LZH is also further developing three technologies.

Other current sub-projects include CUDE (a 2kW diode laser with high beam quality for metal processing is being developed and integrated into a laser cutting machine); MOBILAS (research into a mobile processing unit for shipbuilding and large steel constructions to weld, cut and ablate corrosion and paint) and TWOMICRO (a 2µm laser and beam analysis system is being further developed and tested under industrial conditions). ## BrightLine fiber:

Through thick and thin with the solid-state laser: the new BrightLine fiber option now lets you achieve excellent cut quality when processing thick sheets and the ultimate in productivity when processing thin sheets. Using one and the same machine to process your entire range of parts, you can look forward to impressive edge quality with stainless steel up to 25 mm thick, the tiniest contours, much easier removal of parts – combined with maximum productivity when cutting thin sheets.
