# Feel the G-Force

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

SIX MONTHS AGO, Ermaksan unveiled the latest version of its fibre laser cutting machine, the Fibermak Gen-3 G-FORCE. First showcased at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart last November, it offers acceleration of up to 2.5G using a servo motor system.

"This is faster than regular linear motor speeds. We achieved this by developing the system with advanced servo motors that were compatible with the complete system. G-Force was later displayed at several shows and there are now a significant number of end-users running the machine and enjoying the benefits" said Ersin Yildiz, Ermaksan's international sales manager.

The Fibermak G-Force was on display at the Lamiera exhibition in Bologna, Italy (May 2016) and the Technishow in Utrecht, The Netherlands (March 2016). It will also be in display at the Mach-Tool (ITM Poland) event in Poznań (Poland).

"Fibermak features several single cable servo motors for all axial movements. Power and process data are transmitted in one standard motor cable, significantly increasing speeds. This technology also gives more accurate positioning and more geometrically accurate parts. Laser units of between 500W up to 6kW can be selected for the product family," confirmed the family-run Turkish manufacturer which, last year, celebrated 50 years in the industry. Ermaksan has a strong history in laser cutting, having produced the first linear drive flying optic laser cutting machine in Turkey.

The new machine is designed for ultra-low energy consumption and rapid cutting capability with low maintenance costs. The fibre laser can cut different thicknesses and types of materials such as steel, stainless steel, aluminium, brass, copper and galvanized steel. Fibre lasers are suitable for different applications such as welding, hybrid welding, remote welding, 2D and 3D remote cutting, heat treatment and cladding.
