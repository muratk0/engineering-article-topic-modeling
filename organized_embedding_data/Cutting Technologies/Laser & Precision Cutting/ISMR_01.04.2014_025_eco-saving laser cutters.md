# Eco-saving laser cutters

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

MITSUBISHI Electric has launched a new eX Series laser cutting machine with three options – 2.7kW, 3.5kW and 6.0kW – which features patented cross-flow laser design, lower running costs and high cutting speeds. The design helps to eliminate the need for expensive quartz tubes and turbine blower systems and, according to the company, requires 2-5 times less maintenance than other laser equipment on the market.

There are a few reasons that Mitsubishi feels that this machine reduces costs. The ‘just-on-time’ discharge system of the eX Series laser, by automatically switching to ‘eco-mode’ during standby, cuts running costs by up to 90 per cent during standby. A single resonator charge of fresh gas enables 23-hour continuous operation without replenishment, reducing running costs and eliminating bottle replacement work.

Processing times are quicker as the eX Series integrates Mitsubishi’s latest Jet Pierce technology. Thick mild steel plate (up to 27mm) and medium thick plate processing times are increased by 30 per cent and thin sheets by approximately 20 per cent.
