Fiber laser processes thin sheets with 6g acceleration

Prima Power has introduced the Syncrono fiber laser, featuring 6g acceleration for processing thin sheets.

The system is two synchronized machines in one: one small, light head with high dynamics, and one larger machine with a wide working area. The two machines are coordinated by patented algorithms for optimum distribution of movements throughout the axes. An active compensation cancels the vibrations deriving from the high dynamics.

The machine can process such materials as high-reflecting brass and copper. Electricity consumption is reduced through the user of lower power and high source efficiency, without a turbine, optical chain, or filters.

It includes 5- and 7.5-in. lenses. The focal position is controlled automatically, with no material adjustment required from one material to another.

LED lights in hydraulic clamping system show press brake operator where to position tooling

Wila® USA has introduced the Smart Tool Locator, offered as an option on the company’s New Standard Premium Clamping System for press brakes. The locator uses LED lights integrated in the clamping system to show the operator where to position the tooling.

The lights are controlled by the press brake CNC. The locator also indicates to the operator where to make the next bend, following the bend sequence of the part that is being formed.

The lights are mounted behind the ruler inside the cover strip of the clamping system. This ensures the locator is protected from damage and does not interfere with the sheet metal part being formed.

Lightweight, low-profile magnetic drill suitable for drilling in close quarters

Fein Power Tools Inc. has introduced the new Slugger® JHM Short Slugger portable magnetic drill. The 22-lb., low-profile drill is designed for portable hole-making in fabricating, construction, and specialty metalworking applications.

With its 430-W, 680-RPM motor, the drill can make 1.1875-in.-dia., 2-in.-DOC holes quickly. With an 11-in. height, the drill can fit in tight places. It is suitable for use in overhead and upside-down drilling because the gravity-fed coolant bottle can be removed. The aluminum frame has high-impact plastic inserts to withstand high-production holemaking. All motor wiring is contained to prevent snagging or getting caught in tight places.

Software allows real-time information sharing across entire company

Lantek has introduced Lantek Factory software. Information sharing in real time is at the heart of the concept, working from a common database across the whole company.

The sales team can prepare quotations and deliver them in minutes, producing accurate pricing from live information about material costs, production times, and expected profit margins. Based on current work load, the sales team can offer realistic delivery times.

Once accepted, the sales order is processed and passed to production planning, where the production order is placed. Next the production engineers create the program, allocating the machine and the material, and nest the parts. Shared information goes to the warehouse, where material is delivered to the allocated machine ready for manufacture, and stock levels are updated, triggering reordering of material when necessary.

On the machine, the operator logs start and finish times to record the status of the job and the manufacturing times as the parts are being produced. This information goes to the administration team, where delivery notes and invoices are raised, information about the cost of the job consolidated, and instructions for shipment of the parts issued to the warehouse.

Managers have an overview of the complete process so that they can monitor production and the status of individual jobs.

Tube and pipe cutting software drives multiple 3-D laser cutting machines

SigmaTEK Systems LLC has released SigmaTUBE® tube and pipe cutting software for round, square, rectangular, and triangular tube and pipe. It also supports structural materials such as I-beams, H-beams, C-channel, and angle iron. Custom programs are available for use with Mazak FabriGear, TRUMPF®, BLM, Bystronic, and Amada 3-D tube and pipe laser cutting machines.

The software can drive multiple machines, and it supports multiple assembly model configurations. Model refresh provides updated design revisions. A cutting technology table sets the cutting condition parameters. The software operates seamlessly within the SolidWorks® environment.

Welding system travels on magnet-secured, adjustable track

Koike Aronson Inc./Ran-some offers the IK-72W, a lightweight welding system that travels on an adjustable track secured by magnets. The system is suitable for welding on curved plate, in channels, and on angled steel members.

The track can be extended or curved as required, and the magnets allow for vertical use. The system is compatible with most GMAW systems. A communication cable sends the signal to the wire feeder using the trigger circuitry. Various adapters allow use with various trigger plug configurations. A standard curved or 180-degree torch can be mounted.
