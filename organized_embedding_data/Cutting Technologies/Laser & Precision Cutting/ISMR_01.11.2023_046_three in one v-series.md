# Three in one V-series

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

At the Productronica trade fair this November, FOBA presented its M1000 compact laser workstation with integrated 3W UV/5W UV/10W green laser. All three wattages are combined in one product for minimum space requirements with flexibility. The easy and space-saving integration of the multifunctional UV and green laser head was the main focus at the FOBA booth.

"Our customers have signalled that saving space in production is an important issue for them. Therefore, we are very pleased to introduce this next generation of the FOBA V-Series; it is so compact that the laser head fits into our smallest laser workstation," outlined Philipp Febel, Director Product Management, FOBA.

Due to the wavelength and the corresponding low heat input during marking, 355 and 532nm lasers are suitable for high-contrast laser marking of delicate and sensitive materials. Visitors to Productronica in Munich also saw a live demonstration of the Mosaic software feature on the FOBA M2000 stand-alone laser marking machine.

"FOBA Mosaic enables marking without a fixture, regardless of the position under the marking laser. This allows components to be precisely marked independent of their placement in the laser marking machine," explained FOBA.

The new folding machine at Blechexpo: EVO DuoBend with two independently operated folding beams.

The Schröder Group exhibited a wide range of sheet-metalworking machines at Blechexpo in Germany (7-10 November 2023). It showcased folding machines for all sheet thicknesses as well as automation solutions for a wide range of sheet-metal applications.

"Highlights were the MAK 4 Evolution UD, which bends 6mm thick sheets on a 3200mm working length, the PowerBend Multi with the option of conical bending and the new EVO DuoBend, a folding machine with two folding beams," the company told /SMR. "Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH was nominated for the 'Blechexpo best-Award 2023' innovation award with the EVO DuoBend.

"The top model in our range of industrial folding machines is the MAK 4 Evolution UD for up to 6mm thick sheets. The folder has a generous clamping beam stroke of 1090mm and features an up-and-down folding beam and a back gauge with suction cups. Radius Step Bending was shown as a special function of the POS 3000 3D graphic control," it continued.

The EVO DuoBend made its first public appearance at Blechexpo. This new motorised folding machine (a working length of 4000mm for sheet thicknesses of up to 4mm) works with two independently operating folding beams. As well as its up-and-down folding beam, Schröder has introduced a second option for manufacturing large-format products with counter bends without having to turn the sheet.

"A negative bend is made by the top folding beam and a positive bend by the bottom folding beam. This eliminates the need to bypass already bent product sides, which means that there are no longer any restrictions due to the maximum bypass radius of less than 200mm.

The EVO DuoBend appeared at Blechexpo with a fully automatic tool changer which has featured many times on the MAK 4 Evolution UD with up to 500mm-high tools," added the bending specialist.

With the option of a rotating clamping beam, the Schröder Group offers users the ability to quickly switch between tool stations and geometries with different clearances. Both the PowerBend Professional and the PowerBend Multi were shown at the exhibition with improved rotating clamping beams. The PowerBend Multi also has a new squeeze function that folds hems entirely by bending with the folding beam. The swivel range of the folding beam has been extended to 190° for this purpose.

"The PowerBend Multi also offers conical bending. With the two-axis backgauge, precise pluggable profiles can be produced quickly and easily in the tenth-of-a-millimetre range. This eliminates the need for manual reworking in applications where edges are not parallel such as parapet or wall copings; roof edge trim; verge plates and cladding with a slope," explained the Schröder Group.

The slip bending rolls (Schröder-Fasti brand) were also on display at Blechexpo. Their roll geometry with crowning enables particularly uniform radii. Beading and flanging machines were also on the booth, as well as the ASK 3 segmented manual folding machine and the two powerful motorised shears, MHSU and PDC, with an NC positioning gauge.

The Schröder Group consists of Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH, SCHRÖDER-FASTI Technologie GmbH and tool manufacturer, SMU GmbH. Familyowned Hans Schröder Maschinenbau specialises in the development of modern machine concepts for bending and cutting sheet metal. The Schröder Group today is one of the leading providers of machines for bending; cutting; beading; flanging and circular bending all types of sheet metal.■
