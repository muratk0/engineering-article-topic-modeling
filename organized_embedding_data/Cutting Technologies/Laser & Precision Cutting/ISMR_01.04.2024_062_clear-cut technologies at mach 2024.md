# Clear-cut technologies at MACH 2024

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

WARDJET, a global specialist in waterjetcutting technology, and AXYZ, a pioneer in CNC router solutions, will be exhibiting at MACH 2024, the UK event for engineering based manufacturing technologies (15-19 April 2024) at the NEC in Birmingham, UK.

Visitors to MACH will see the WARDJET A-Series waterjet cutter on stand #17-9.

"The WARDJET A-Series waterjet cutter is a versatile and compact machine that can cut through any material, from titanium to concrete. It does this in a small, enclosed footprint, making it ideal for businesses with limited floor space. Despite its small size it is incredibly powerful, with the most power per square foot of any machine in its class. The A-Series is also the only machine in its class to offer a standard 60k psi pump, enabling cutting of materials up to 178mm in thickness. It can be used to cut intricate designs, create custom parts or even create prototypes quickly and efficiently," commented WARDJET.

On stand #17-140, show visitors will also be able to witness the AXYZ METALWORKER in action. This CNC router comes with standard features optimised to cut aluminium, copper, zinc, metal composites and mild steel. It has a rigid frame and large stress-relieved gantry to from low RPM, high torque spindle options to ultra-fast 40K spindles for continuous operation. It is specifically engineered for precision part production in nested-based CNC operations. The router was designed exclusively for end-users looking to process small to large-sized sheets of material. Operators will benefit from standard features that include high-performance servo motors and helical drive systems, an industrial 22" touch-screen and more," explained AXYZ.
