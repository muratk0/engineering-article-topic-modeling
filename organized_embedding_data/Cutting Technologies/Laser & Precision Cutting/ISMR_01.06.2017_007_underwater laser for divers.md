# Underwater laser for divers

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

WORKING UNDER WATER is personnel- and time-intensive. The Laser Zentrum Hannover e.V. (LZH) is therefore developing a laserbased, automated process for cutting sheet piling under water, with the Institute of Materials Science of the Leibniz Universität Hannover.

Sheet piling protects fortified shore areas, or can be used to dry out these areas if repairs are necessary. If the sheet piling needs to be dismantled, divers must cut the walls into smaller pieces using a cutting torch. Normally, a diver can cut about 20 metres per day, which corresponds to a speed of about 0.07 metres per minute. In the LuWaPro project, scientists at the LZH have now developed a process which uses a disc laser for torch cutting. The diver therefore only performs a supervisory role. The process can be used to separate the metal sheets, which are usually 10mm thick for sheet piling, at speeds of up to 0.9m/min.

"Poor visibility, currents or uneven surfaces make working underwater more difficult. The scientists in the Underwater Technology Group achieved a position tolerance of about 2mm in their process, enabling the system to react robustly to possible impacts during underwater operation. The process was tested with two scenarios. On the one hand, stand-alone metal sheets were cut underwater. On the other hand, the sheets were backfilled with concrete. In the second scenario, leaking of the molten material through the cutting kerf is achieved by tilting the laser head at an angle of 20 degrees," explained LZH.

A further major benefit, in comparison to the hand-guided process, is the ability to monitor the process. Sensors determine whether a cut is completed or not. Otherwise, the diver often cannot see this due to poor visibility underwater. The laser safety necessary for this process can be guaranteed if the diver wears conventional safety goggles under the helmet, said LZH.

The "Laser cutting under water for high productivity – LuWaPro" project was supported by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy. The Laser Zentrum Hannover e.V.
