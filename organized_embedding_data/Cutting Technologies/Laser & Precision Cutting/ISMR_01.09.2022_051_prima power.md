## Schiavi Macchine International

## www.schiavimacchine.it

Schiavi Macchine International is an Italian metal-bending specialist.

"We have been delivering innovative bending solutions since 1958 with our own TASK numerical control. From the 3D drawing, ATLAS by TASK can program the entire automation cell from the patented, crowning-free, hybrid press brake for high bending accuracy and the connected 7-axis robot. This software is fully integrated into a complete production system in partnership with enterprise-level material tracking from CrossTrack and nesting software from JETCAM. We are integrated into ZoomFab: a comprehensive productivity management system with machine monitoring and extended data collection," it told ISMR.

Schiavi also offers several solutions to ensure automation of material handling for 2D lasers. It has successfully integrated installations with ASTES4, an advanced parts sorting system. Multi-tower storage systems can improve logistics.
