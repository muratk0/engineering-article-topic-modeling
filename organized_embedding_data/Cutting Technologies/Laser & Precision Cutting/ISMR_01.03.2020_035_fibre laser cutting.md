# Fibre laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

THE HACO GROUP, an established manufacturer of sheet metal machinery, is now launching a range of fibre laser cutting systems, ending a thirty-year period offering CO2 laser cutting machines whilst expanding its range of fully automated fibre laser systems.

“With a wide range of solutions in sheet metal, structural steel, aluminium milling and woodworking machinery, we are set to further expand our solutions for the global market with our new product range. The systems are aimed at small and medium-sized companies looking to enter the fibre laser arena," commented Frank Havegeer (CEO, HACO Group).

The HACO FL-series offers a fully enclosed fibre laser cutting system, based on a rigid frame, with low inertia moving parts to enable high acceleration and increased production output. The dual-pallet exchange table offers high productivity with short cycle times. A choice of fibre power (from 2kW up to 12kW), Precitech® cutting heads and Beckhoff® control is available. Cutting table dimensions are offered from 3000 x 1500mm up to 8000 x 2500mm.

“The HFL-series is ideal for companies looking to expand their capabilities with a moderate budget yet high performance laser cutting machine," added HACO.
