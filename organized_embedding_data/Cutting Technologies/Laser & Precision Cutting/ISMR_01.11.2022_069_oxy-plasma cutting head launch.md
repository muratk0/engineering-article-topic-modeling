# Oxy-plasma cutting head launch

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

At this year's EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover, Polish manufacturer Eckert launched its Dual Flex 3D cutting head for plasma-oxygen cutting, with two exchangeable torches.

“Do you need a tool allowing users to cut and bevel various types of metal, in a wide range of thicknesses, for welding preparation? With Dual Flex 3D, you can cut and bevel material of up to 100mm thick using plasma or oxygen torches (even for advanced contours). Dual Flex 3D is a universal production tool," commented Eckert, a 30-year-old manufacturer which has installed over 1,800 CNC cutting machines in 36 countries.

"Material utilisation; energy consumption; noise levels and pollution during welding preparation are reduced to a minimum, compared to manual work. Dual Flex 3D can also cut profiles and even tube," added Eckert.

For 3D plasma cutting, cutting head angle deflector of +/-52° provides a powerful bevelling tool in three dimensions. The ability to machine at an angle of +/-45°, and the revolutionary oxygen cutting system, provides extra wide ranges of welding preparation for oxygen cutting. Dual Flex is available in two versions; 2D and 3D with Kjellberg and Hypertherm plasma power sources. Assembly options are limited to Eckert's Jantar, Sapphire, Chrome and Onyx oxygen-plasma cutting machines.
