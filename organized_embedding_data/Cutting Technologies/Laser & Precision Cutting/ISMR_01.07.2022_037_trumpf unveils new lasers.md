# TRUMPF unveils new lasers

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

High-tech company TRUMPF unveiled a host of new lasers at the LASER -World of Photonics show in Munich, Germany.

## TruMicro USPs

The next generation and new lines of TRUMPF's TruMicro ultrashort pulse lasers (USP) were launched at the show. The two TruMicro 6000 and TruMicro 2000 product families feature new technology platforms to boost power and enhance versatility. Ultrashort pulsed lasers see frequent use in electronics manufacturing.

"With the benefit of powerful infrared lasers, we can convert both the TruMicro 2000 and the TruMicro 6000 to green and ultraviolet wavelengths while maintaining high beam quality. This enables us to offer the USP laser best suited to the customer's use case. With 100 watts, the next-generation TruMicro 2000 is the most powerful industrial ultrashort pulse fibre laser. The TruMicro 6000 offers up to 200 watts at pulses in the femtosecond range for even higher performance," said Steffen Rübling, the product manager responsible for TRUMPF's TruMicro lasers.

Based on slab technology, the next-generation TruMicro 6000 can be deployed in various industries for use cases ranging from cutting to drilling. The active medium, a crystal that amplifies laser light, looks like a tile or slab, hence the name. The laser light travels through the slab, zigzagging between its two end faces. The slab's advantage is that this active medium is very conducive to cooling, which enables high pulse energies at high repetition rates. Ultrashort pulses reduce thermal stress on highly sensitive materials.

The next-generation compact and lightweight TruMicro 2000 has an average power output of up to 100 watts which accelerates the machining process. The fibre-based TruMicro 2000's parameters, such as pulse duration, repetition rate and pulse energy, can be variably adjusted within milliseconds to suit a wide range of use cases. These pulses can serve many purposes, e.g. to cut highly sensitive medical products such as stents, to machine surfaces and to mark a wide variety of materials.

The new lasers may be used with an LLK-U, a fibre optic cable for ultrashort pulse lasers based on hollow-core fibres. This type of cable makes it much easier to apply ultrashort pulses in industrial use cases.

## TruDisk lasers for thick sheet

TRUMPF also presented the latest generation of its TruDisk high-power, solidstate lasers at LASER -World of Photonics. The powerful industrial disk lasers deliver a hefty 24 kilowatts of output power. Suitable for cutting, welding and machining surfaces, these new TruDisk lasers are suitable for jobs that require a combination of high-power output and maximum beam quality – for example, to cut thick sheet metal and steel, and to weld thick sheets.

"With this powerful new TruDisk generation, we are primarily addressing the heavy industry's welding needs," said Evangelos Papastathopoulos, the TRUMPF product manager responsible for these new disk lasers. High-power lasers of this sort see frequent use in hybrid applications that combine laser and arc welding. "The new TruDisk generation is also great for cutting sheet metal and steel," added Papastathopoulos. "TRUMPF's

BrightLine technology gives customers so much flexibility that they can cut thick and thin materials almost at the touch of a button."

At the heart of BrightLine technology is a two-in-one laser light cable (LLK) with an inner and an outer fibre core patented by TRUMPF. A TruDisk disk laser couples the laser light into the LLK and distributes the laser power to the inner and outer cores using a device known as a wedge switch. This affords the user greater latitude and precision in adjusting laser power and the beam profile to the given sheet's thickness. When cutting and welding, BrightLine distributes the laser beam and its energy on the part to achieve strong results for the given material and thickness. As a cutting tool, it is designed to produce smooth edges. As a welding tool, it reduces spatter and offers a higher-quality seam.

This sixth TruDisk generation also comes with a beam guidance system. It enables users to plug cables into multiple outputs to use the laser light for several systems without compromising power and beam quality. Active Power Control keeps high power output and process stable. With the patented TruDisk resonator and the newly designed LLK-X connector, this laser is also able to machine highly reflective materials without disruptions caused by reflected light.

## TruFiber 2000 P

TRUMPF also presented a new 2kW TruFiber 2000 P fibre laser at the LASYS exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany. It is suitable for applications ranging from laser cutting to laser welding and additive manufacturing. Optical laser cable length reaches 10 metres for enhanced flexibility.

The TruControl laser controller provides convenient start-up of the laser, definition and storage of up to 1000 different laser programs as well as all relevant interface buses.

“The TruFiber 2000 P has an inherent power stability over an eight-hour shift. This can be further improved over the same time using the Automatic Laser Power Control option which eliminates the effects of diode ageing," explained Mark Richmond, Product Manager CW Laser, TRUMPF.
