# Launch of new XPR460 plasma cutter

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

Hypertherm Associates, a U.S.-based manufacturer of industrial cutting systems and software, has announced the release of its latest innovation: the Hypertherm®XPR460® plasma cutting system. Positioned as the latest addition to the XPR line with X-Definition® technology, this advanced plasma system has been designed for versatility, productivity and precision according to the company.

"The system offers superior cutting versatility for mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium and performs multiple applications with SureCutTMtechnology for bolt-ready holes and smooth 45-degree bevels. It is suitable for XY and robotic cutting applications," outlined Hypertherm Associates.

"In terms of productivity, cutting speed is increased by an average of 12% through to 50mm (two-inch) mild steel with piercing of up to 64mm (2.5 inches) mild steel. The system is future-enabled for Smart Factory integration and connectivity. It also offers an increase of 12% in consumable life versus HPR400XD®. The XPR460®plasma cutting system provides high cut quality for thicker metal (providing cleaner, sharper and more consistent edge quality) as well as excellent part-to-part consistency over the life of the consumable, ensuring quality from the first cut to the last," it added.

With Arc response technology, the XPR460 is also engineered for system optimisation. Ramp-down error protection significantly increases realized consumable life, and torch protection reduces the impact of catastrophic

electrode blowouts which can damage the torch at high current levels.

Designed for seamless operation, the XPR460 features Argon-assist technology for extended piercing capability and edgestarting on thick mild steel and stainless steel. Its advanced process control mechanisms prioritise plate protection and consumable efficiency, minimizing downtime and maximizing output.

"With four gas-connect console options, the XPR460 offers flexibility and control over the cutting process. Each console is seamlessly integrated with the CNC interface, ensuring ease of use and high productivity. CorePlus, VWI and OptiMix gas-connect consoles enable improved marking applications, enhancing the system's versatility and efficiency," concluded the manufacturer.

In addition to its plasma and waterjet cutting systems, the company creates CNCs and software for increased productivity and profitability. Founded in 1968, Hypertherm Associates is a 100-per cent Associate-owned company, employing approximately 2,000 Associates, with operations and partner representation worldwide.■
