# Cutting and marking

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

CY-LASER specialises in the production of fibre laser cutting and welding systems. The Italian manufacturer will exhibit at the LAMIERA 2022 exhibition in Milan (18-21 May 2022) and introduce its new CY-FAST MARK galvo marking head which combines the marking process with cutting.

"This head, independent from the laser cutting source, is ideal for rapidly marking barcodes/QR codes and images, even on film-coated materials. It is designed for very high-speed marking. Compared to a traditional system, it offers time savings of over 40%. Customised marking levels and the ability to adapt to every marking requirements makes it a very useful accessory that fits perfectly into an Industry 4.0 framework. For contractors, in fact, part tracking is very important and CY-FAST MARK definitely represents added value," it told ISMR.

The CY-FAST MARK, with the EVO V HP proprietary cutting head for high powers, will be integrated onto CY2D RP3015 fibre laser cutting systems on the manufacturer's booth at LAMIERA 2022.

The exhibition will also be an opportunity for CY-LASER to present other innovations such as the CYTUBE modular system for cutting tubes with different profiles, the 5-axis cutting head with variable chamfer for bevel cutting and the CYWELD fibre laser welding system. The CYWELD is designed for the automatic welding of two overlapping sheets and processing of a semi-finished product which can be used for plate heat exchangers and conditioned silos.
