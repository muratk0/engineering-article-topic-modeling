# Laser-tube processing

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

The LT12 is the latest addition to the BLM GROUP laser-tube offering.

BLM GROUP presented its "the Solution" theme at WIRE & TUBE 2024, the international trade fair for tube and wire processing, from 15-19 April 2024 at the Düsseldorf exhibition centre in Germany.

A highlight of its booth was an interactive 3D AI model of BLM GROUP's new LT12, a laser-tube system with fibre source capable of processing tubes of up to 305mm in diameter and 12.5m in length. Visitors to the booth were able to communicate with the 3D model, which is powered by AI, and receive answers to questions on the equipment capability.

"The LT12 features a 3D laser cutting head and can cut tubes, beams and profiles of various open or closed sections with a speed directly comparable to other laser-tube machines of smaller size. This is well-suited to the mechanical and alternative energy industries, where there is a need to expand the maximum diameter of light alloy tubes that can be cut with fibre lasers," explained BLM.

BLM GROUP's ELECT150 demonstrated its ability to perform highly complex shapes on curved profiles. The ELECT150 is a fully electric tube bender for tubes of up to 150mm in diameter. At the exhibition, it was shown bending a 110mm-diameter tube with an average radius of 90mm.

The company's LT8.20 Lasertube is a versatile laser-tube cutting system working in a wide range of industries. It offers a working range from 12-240mm diameter, equipped with 3D laser cutting capabilities, a dual loading system, Active Tools technology and a fibre laser source of up to 5kW. The LT8.20 is configurable to meet specific customer needs and can be connected to an automated warehouse to optimise the entire production process.

BLM GROUP also featured a fully automated solution at the exhibition, incorporating two manipulator robots that handle parts between the processes of

cutting and shaping; bending with core; nut insertion; shaping on the second end of the tube and in-line dimensional control. This solution integrates 3-RUNNER bar-loading; SMART; AST8-VE systems and a telecentric measuring system.

Its Lasertube and Bender Workcell is a work cell and tube bender using robots and AGVs. This solution uses data matrix scanning on the part to help identify the type of bending program to be used. The system, composed of an E-TURN tube bender and Lasertube LT7 system, utilises a manipulator robot and an AGV for tube transport. This solution can adapt to "on the fly" batch production without special intervention by external operators.

BLM GROUP also highlighted its range of services and software offerings at WIRE & TUBE 2024 in Germany, along with its machining solutions.
