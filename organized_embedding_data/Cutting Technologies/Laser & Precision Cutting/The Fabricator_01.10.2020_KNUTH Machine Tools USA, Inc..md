The KNUTH Machine Tools USA Journey to Assemble Fiber Lasers in the U.S.

Aristotle said, "The whole is greater than the sum of its parts." In other words, when individual parts are connected together to form one entity, they are worth more than the individual parts.

This is how manufacturers create value - and one prominent German company is adding final assembly operations to its North American headquarters in Lincolnshire, Ill. For years, KNUTH Machine Tools, a global machine tool manufacturer and supplier, has engineered, developed, and manufactured cutting systems (laser, plasma, and waterjet) at its worldwide headquarters in northern Germany. Now the company is expanding its manufacturing and assembly capabilities in the U.S.

KNUTH Machine Tools USA now assembles its ACE Laser Cutting Systems in Lincolnshire. The highest quality components are sourced from all around the world, with a focus on major components purchased from USA vendors such as the IPG laser source, safety glass, filtration unit/dust collector and many other small components made in the U.S. Future plans call for expanding the assembly process to include full build-out of the electrical cabinet, sourcing U.S.-made chillers, additional engineering on the PC-based control system, and the fabrication of material handling systems.

The ultimate goal is to bring more manufacturing stateside, adding value, reducing costs, and improving delivery time and serviceability, all while ensuring the highest level of safety is delivered to customers.

In addition to a long tradition of innovation and quality, KNUTH has also built its reputation on safety. Following stringent European and USA safety standards on the products they offer, the ACE Laser Cutting System is no exception. All systems are Class 1 certified, offering the highest level of safety under all conditions of normal usage.

The first units are already installed in the field and are being used to fabricate high volumes of air moving and air filtration products for use across many industries.

KNUTH Machine Tools USA currently offers two table sizes, equipped with automatic shuttle table systems and laser power up to 8 KW.
