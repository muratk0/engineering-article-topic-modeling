There’s still time to attend the premier industrial laser industry event: the 25th annual ALAW

®

Advanced Laser Applications Workshop, June 4-6 in Plymouth (Detroit), Mich.

As the use of lasers becomes more widespread, OEMs, manufacturers, job shops, and suppliers need to gain familiarity with the properties, advantages, and applicability of the laser welding process. In addition to keynote presentations on industry issues, over 25 presentations will be offered on laser welding of multimaterials, remote laser welding, lightweighting, green lasers, hybrid manufacturing, and laser additive manufacturing.

ALAW continues the tradition of offering world-class open houses featuring live laser welding and cutting demos at Fraunhofer USA - Center for Laser Applications, TRUMPF’s Laser Technology Center, and IPG Photonics. In addition, laser technology demos will be hosted at Laser Mechanisms Inc. in Novi and Precitec in Wixom. Registration for all open houses is free to ALAW attendees.

KEYNOTES

Tuesday, June 5

Sarah Kleinbaum

Sarah (Ollila) Kleinbaum is the technology manager for joining of dissimilar materials in the Department of Energy (DOE)’s Vehicle Technology Office. In this role, she assesses the challenges facing the automotive industry to implement lightweight materials in vehicles and sponsors technical research projects including academia, national laboratories, and industry partners to address those challenges. Sarah also serves as co-director of the LightMAT consortium, which seeks to enhance the collaboration between industry and national labs to accelerate the development and implementation of lightweight automotive materials. Before her work at the DOE, Sarah managed the Materials Analysis and Approval Laboratory for North America at Whirlpool Corp.

Wednesday, June 6

Bernard Swiecki

Bernard Swiecki serves as director of the Automotive Communities Partnership (ACP) at the Center for Automotive Research (CAR), as well as a senior automotive analyst in CAR’s Industry, Labor, and Economics (ILE) group. He joined CAR in 1994. Bernard has written and managed research in a diverse array of automotive fields pertaining to automakers, suppliers, electric and alternative powertrain vehicles, and other subjects. He manages CAR’s analysis of vehicle sales, production, and segmentation data.
