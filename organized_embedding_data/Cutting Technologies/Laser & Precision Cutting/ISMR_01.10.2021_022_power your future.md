# Power your future

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

EAGLE LASERS is one of the pioneers of fibre laser technology and an early launcher of efficient 6kW, 12kW, 15kW and 20kW fibre laser cutting machines. This October at the Blechexpo 2021 exhibition, it will be presenting its new 30kW iNspire fibre laser cutting machine (#iNspiretheworld).

Fibre laser cutting technology has grown continuously over the last few years and has become a very efficient solution for many companies. Its benefits are indisputable; reduced production time, less energy consumption and, most importantly, lower cost per part providing a new level of productivity and efficiency.

"We knew from the beginning that this technology was the future and we invested in it. We researched and developed key technologies based on the concept of the ideal fibre laser machine. Unlike other companies with diversified portfolios, we did not adapt existing systems to fibre laser technology. Instead, we created a completely new concept from the start," explained Ivan Melicher, CEO of EAGLE LASERS Germany.

The result is a rapid fibre laser cutting system (regarding performance, acceleration and material handling). EAGLE LASERS' machines have linear motors in all axes, a composite material body, carbon fibre traverse and the brand's own patented eVa cutting head. The systems are designed to enhance productivity, efficiency and reliability.

"We are very proud to be able to deliver the fastest and most reliable fibre laser cutting system with controlled 30kW power on the market today. When we started in 2006, we believed that this could be possible. Today, we have turned this vision into reality." said Melicher.

The EAGLE LASERS iNspire series, with 30kW of power, 6G acceleration and a system capable of running 24/7, can cut nine holes per second (rates normally reserved for a punch). With breakthrough positioning speed of nearly 350 metres per minute, it is also optimised for automation/warehouse systems and full reporting.

"The 30kW iNspire series has an unmatched nine seconds' pallet changer, the newly updated eVa cutting head and a full dynamic control system. It represents the future for this market. We will be presenting the new 30kW fibre laser cutting machine during the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart this October (Hall 5, stand 5305). Come to visit us and see for yourself how to power your future!" concluded Melicher.

## Within five years electric cars can be fossil free.

There is more to this than meets the eye. Electric cars have zero emissions, but they are made of steel. For every tonne of steel produced in the world, almost two tonnes of CO₂ are emitted as well. One car might not make much of a difference. But think of all the cars across the globe. All the wind turbines. All the trains.

The good news is, this is all about to change. As the largest Nordic steel company, we at SSAB together with our partners LKAB and Vattenfall have come up with HYBRIT, a way to produce steel without emitting CO2.
