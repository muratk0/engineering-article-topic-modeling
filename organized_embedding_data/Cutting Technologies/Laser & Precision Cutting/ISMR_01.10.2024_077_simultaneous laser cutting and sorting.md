# Simultaneous laser cutting and sorting

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

“Automated sorting in laser cutting is often viewed as the ultimate goal, but we don't believe any current system on the market truly delivers," outlined Marcin Ejma, Founder and CEO of Eagle Lasers.

“We realized that improving sorting wasn't enough. Instead, we needed to rethink the cutting process itself. Rather than cutting parts directly, we decided to cut the scrap around them, leaving only the parts to be sorted," he added.

This idea led to the creation of FlowIN, Eagle Lasers' new integrated system that is designed to ensure simultaneous material cutting and sorting. It will debut at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, Germany (22-25 October 2024) on Eagle's stand F242 (Hall 13).

“FlowIN is more than just a cutting system—it's a full production solution. It cuts and sorts at the same time, ensuring only finished parts exit the machine with no leftover skeleton. The metal sheet is fed onto a brush table, where parts are cut, separated from the scrap and conveyed on a belt-driven table. Sorting outside the machine is streamlined into a simple pick-and-place process, as the machine already separates parts during cutting. The skeleton is cut into smaller pieces for easier waste management and better material utilisation. The system's design also eliminates the need for a pallet changer," explained Eagle Lasers.

"FlowIN's brush table has no comb-related issues such as cleaning, exchange, part scratches, debris and time lost removing contaminants. Its automatic separation of cut parts from both the skeleton and each other simplifies the workflow, removing the need for microwelds, microjoints or nanojoints. With power capabilities of over 40kW, FlowIN is also coil-cutting ready and optimises material efficiency-driven nesting, ensuring minimal gaps between parts while preparing them for sorting," it continued.

The system features Eagle's components including the award-winning eVa Cutting Head; linear motors on all axes; a carbon fibre traverse; a polymer concrete machine body and Eagle Eye fault detection technology. FlowIN's core purpose is to "free" parts and enhance production flow, aiming to make a significant industry impact. Its fibre technology has been designed to deliver energy savings, shorter lead times, lower costs per part and improved profit margins for operators, Eagle Lasers told ISMR.

“Our system frees parts from the surrounding scrap. Most machines [in the market] still leave a skeleton, making sorting difficult and time-consuming. No matter the industry, clients who recognize the benefits of FlowIN will find it highly profitable," concluded Marcin Ejma.■
