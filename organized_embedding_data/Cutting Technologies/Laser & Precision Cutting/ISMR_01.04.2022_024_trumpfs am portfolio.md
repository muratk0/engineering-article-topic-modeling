# TRUMPF's AM portfolio

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

TRUMPF offers both relevant laser technologies for additive manufacturing from a single source: laser metal fusion (LMF) and laser metal deposition (LMD). New innovations to the range include green laser technology for 3D printing of copper and other precious metals.

TRUMPF also strengthened its Additive Manufacturing (AM) division by taking over the joint venture TRUMPF SISMA S.R.L. completely from its Italian partner, SISMA S.p.A. It will continue SISMA’s LMF (Laser Metal Fusion) business in the industrial, dental and medical markets.

The aim was to provide an additive support role for Smithstown's medical device customers, specifically for R&D work.

"We looked at many potential machines, before designing our own benchmark and sending it to suppliers of interest," said Kevin Kelly, a Smithstown manufacturing engineer. “It was the quality of the benchmark produced by TRUMPF, using its TruPrint 2000, which caught our attention. In addition, the volume of the machine was perfect for our needs and the price was competitive."

Installed at the end of 2020, Smithstown is using its new TruPrint 2000 to produce printed parts from metal. The company's new Additive Centre also houses a machine for printing polymer components.

"Additive manufacturing is ideal for medical work because of the complex geometries involved," explained Kevin Kelly. "Without 3D printing, several processes would be required, taking many hours. Additive manufacturing also offers the potential for individual customisation, which has obvious benefits for products such as implants."

## Prototypes and samples

Since installation, the machine has been busy producing prototypes and samples for medical device customers in 17-4 and 316 stainless steel.

"In the medical sector, it can take years to move from the design and test stage to validated production, but we're now in a position to help expedite this process and bring customer ideas to life," outlined Kelly. "Upon reaching the production phase of current projects, we could well need several 3D printing systems so that we can dedicate machines to a single material. We will not hesitate to invest in more machines if the demand is there."

With its small 55μm diameter laser beam, the TruPrint 2000 provides a high-quality printing result. Two TRUMPF 300W fibre lasers deliver high productivity over the entire cylindrical build volume of 200mm diameter x 200mm high. Among notable options that help to ensure high quality standards is melt pool monitoring. With this function, deviations in the laser metal fusion process are detectable early via sensors, and critical areas of the component can be visualised.

Users can also monitor all weld pools in parallel.

TRUMPF development engineers reduced the TruPrint 2000 laser's focal diameter to 55 micrometers to print components with smoother surfaces, enhanced quality and intricate grid structures. The TruPrint 2000 prints parts out of titanium, a material that figures prominently in medical devices.

"Users enjoy the benefits of automated powder bed and melt pool quality monitoring. Another benefit is an end-to-end documentation trail that corroborates the quality of the printing process. This is a key prerequisite for the additive manufacturing of medical devices," TRUMPF told ISMR.

## An eye on the future

Moving forward, Smithstown's business strategy is to focus more on high-volume precision components, rather than simply tooling and small-batch parts.

"Some of our competitors have 3D printers, but the TruPrint 2000 definitely gives us an edge," said Kelly. “We also have a dedicated team of experts who can design parts specifically to leverage the benefits of additive manufacturing, helping to minimise costs without compromising quality. Designing a part for additive is quite different to designing a part for traditional machining."

Smithstown - which carries certifications that include ISO9001 (quality), ISO13485 (medical devices) and ISO14001 (environmental) – has recently secured several new projects that are driving the need for more staff recruitment. Alongside its investment in the latest manufacturing technologies, ongoing growth is assured at this business.

"We are really happy with the quality and build speed of the TruPrint 2000, which is backed-up with good support from TRUMPF who have listened to our needs throughout," concluded King. “As a result, we have not experienced any unexpected issues whatsoever, which is impressive considering that this is our first venture into additive manufacturing."

We highlight trends, growth drivers and challenges for the global household appliances industry, as well as smart homes.

Home appliances refer to a range of devices, generally operated on electricity and used in the home for chores such as cooking, cleaning or preserving cold food. Also termed 'domestic appliances', these mainly involve white goods such as refrigerators; air conditioners; dishwashers; clothes dryers; drying cabinets; freezers; kitchen stoves; water heaters; washing machines etc.

Major appliances are characterised as large machines capable of completing a routine household job, the purposes of which include cooking or refrigeration in home, commercial, industrial or institutional locations. They are generally in a set position and are considered part of the home in the real estate market as they are typically included in the property when other furnishings are not. They frequently require a generous amount of electricity to function properly. The most prominent products in the major appliance industry are ovens, ranges, refrigerators, freezers, washers and dryers. Dishwashers and air conditioning units are also manufactured.

This multi-billion-dollar industry is run by some huge brands. Increasing urbanisation, a rising middle-class population and new product development are just some of the factors driving growth in this market. One major driver of this industry is the rise in per capita income in developing countries, which has greatly spurred consumer spending.

## Household appliance market

Product innovation and advances leading to portfolio extension and 'product premiumisation' is a key factor driving market growth for the global household appliance market according to analyst, Technavio. Fierce rivalry in the global home appliance industry has prompted vendors to invest heavily in R&D and innovation to stay afloat in such a competitive market. Technavio forecasts market growth for the sector of US$ 89.50 billion from 2020 to 2025 in its latest market research report.

One major driver of this industry is the rise in per capita income in developing countries, which has greatly spurred consumer spending.

“The household appliance market structure is expected to remain fragmented during the forecast period. Vendors are deploying different organic and inorganic growth strategies to compete in the market. Various players in the household appliance market are engaging in strategic partnerships and joint ventures, coupled with launching differentiated products to up their game," it explained.

Refrigeration and cooling appliances (such as refrigerators; freezers; fridge freezers and beverage coolers) are common household appliances.

The worldwide home appliance market is seeing an increase in the number of multi-functional solutions.

"Refrigerators, air conditioners and washing machines saw the largest sales in APAC, owing to rising disposable income and changing lifestyles among the population, as well as rising urbanisation. The growing middle class in APAC acquired high-end goods," said the analyst.

"Because Western European markets are established, manufacturers are focusing their efforts on Eastern European consumers. As a result, during the projected period, positive sales growth in the major household appliance market in Europe is expected," it added.

Specialty stores, department stores, hypermarkets, supermarkets, convenience stores and clubhouse stores are all part of the offline distribution channel. Because of the shift in consumer preference towards online shopping, sales through the offline distribution channel have been gradually falling over time. To fuel sales through offline channels, vendors are managing their sales through local retail entities. They also have strategic partnerships with professional chains of home appliance shops, according to Technavio's report.

"APAC will contribute to 53% of the market growth during the forecast period. China and Japan are the key markets for household appliances in APAC. The growth of this region can be attributed to the growing middle-class population and their rising income, which empowered their spending power on household needs and appliances," said the report.

In terms of country-wise market growth, the report outlines that China, USA, Japan, Germany and France will contribute to the highest market growth levels.

“Another element driving the home appliance market share rise is the rising use of multi-functional and innovative products. Multi-functional products on the market can serve multiple household applications, resulting in excellent value for money. Customers believe that multifunctional products save them time and money. They also eliminate the requirement for multiple items for different applications," explained the report.

"Vendors are always innovating to stay competitive, maintain high customer attention, extend their customer base and generate demand for their products to meet the growing demand for multi-functional household appliances. The worldwide home appliance market is seeing an increase in the number of multifunctional solutions, which is driving acceptance of these devices," it added.

Product innovation and quick adoption for technologically advanced appliances are further adding to the growth of the small domestic appliances market in Europe.

## Small domestic appliances

The global small domestic appliances market size was valued at US$ 176,804.3 million in 2020 and is estimated to reach US$ 581,657.6 million by 2030, registering a CAGR of 13.0% from 2021 to 2030. This is according to new analysis on ResearchandMarkets.com.

"A growing urbanised population creates huge demand for small domestic appliances as people living in metro cities seek a high standard of living and look for advanced and smart domestic appliances during the forecast period. Increasing personal disposable income and affordable credit facilities are promoting people to improve and design their living rooms and kitchens. A surge in innovation for home appliances has prompted people to replace their old model appliances with new smart appliances," said the report.

High price and comparative popularity of residential and commercial small domestic appliances brands have led to the advent of counterfeit brands. Counterfeit brands are generally prevalent in developing economies where customers are highly price-sensitive. This factor restricts the sale of the existing original small domestic appliances brands in these regions.

Connectivity has emerged as a high-end novelty across a wide range of home devices.

“The outbreak of COVID-19 has positively impacted the overall growth of the small domestic appliances market. During lockdown periods, people were choosing to replace their old appliances with smart and advanced appliances. Furthermore, due to the increased number of COVID-19 cases, hospitals, clinics, railway stations and airports were given top priority for cleaning and hygiene," said the report.

The market is segmented into cooking/baking and heating appliances; kitchen appliances; personal and animal care; textile handling appliances; cleaning appliances; air-treatment appliances and others.

“Europe gained a significant share in the global small domestic appliances market in 2020 and is expected to sustain its share during the forecast period. A considerable growth rate is anticipated for new, advanced and smart residential and commercial appliances for the residential and commercial segment. In addition, there has been a huge increase in demand from major European countries such as France, Spain, the UK and Russia," continued the report.

“The increasing preference for hassle-free lifestyles and the growing industrial application of air purifiers, vacuum cleaners and dishwashers are supporting the growth of small domestic appliances in the region. Product innovation and quick adoption for technologically advanced appliances are further adding to the growth of the small domestic appliances market in Europe," it added.

## The smart home

Smart homes not only perform basic functions, such as turning off or dimming lights and closing curtains, but are also capable of performing complex functions such as automatically lighting up exit routes in the event of a fire alarm. Connectivity has emerged as a high-end novelty across a wide range of home devices (including security cameras, utility meters and thermostats). In the coming years, connectivity is likely to become a more pervasive trend to the extent of being an embedded standard in every household device and equipment virtually.

With access to home products from remote locations, users can switch off the lights of their house if left on. They can also keep a watch on any activity taking place within the house through video surveillance systems. Smart locks allow end users to receive notifications regarding any abnormal activity in or around the house. Homeowners can also remotely set the temperature of the room as well as switch the HVAC and other electrical appliances on and off, according to their requirements.

The use of data analytics software and solutions, along with home automation systems, helps homeowners to determine if the electric equipment in the home is operating efficiently and achieving the targeted energy usage goals. This enables residents to identify the areas that require improvement and energy efficiency upgrades. The improved features and convenience of monitoring homes from remote locations are expected to boost the growth of the smart home market in the coming years.

The global smart homes market is highly competitive and consists of several major players. In terms of market share, a few of the major players currently dominate the market. The market appears to be moderately fragmented.

Based on type, the smart home appliance is expected to be the most lucrative segment. The availability of wireless solutions (that have enabled smart appliances to link wirelessly with smartphones and tablets via the Internet or Bluetooth) as well as wireless connectivity have propelled market expansion. The ability to handle smart home appliances remotely has reduced energy usage and reduced the cost and time necessary for chores. The demand for home appliances is expected to rise in the near future as a result of these advantages.

"Home automation and smart homes are two ambiguous terms used in reference to a wide range of monitoring solutions, controlling and automating functions in a home. Unlike simple home automation solutions (which could range from motor-operated garage doors to automated security systems), smart home systems require a web portal or a smartphone application as a user interface to interact with an automated system," commented analyst, Mordor Intelligence.

"Globally, the increasing importance of the need to counter security issues is anticipated to fuel the demand for smart and connected homes. Moreover, innovative wireless technologies, including security and access regulators, entertainment controls and HVAC controllers, are expected to foster market growth," it added.

Smart homes, it told /SMR, are on the rise, with nearly 30 million U.S. households projected to add smart home technology in the near future. The products that consumers are looking to add to their homes include connected cameras (highest demand); video doorbells (second highest demand); connected light bulbs; smart locks and smart speakers.

## Smart home market

According to a new report on MarketsandMarkets, smart home market size is expected to reach US$ 138.9 billion by 2026 from US$84.5 billion in 2021, at a CAGR of 10.4%.

The increasing number of internet users and the growing adoption of smart devices, along with growing needs for energy-saving and low carbon emission-oriented solutions, are the key drivers in the market. In addition to this, factors such as the increasing importance of home monitoring in remote locations and increasing disposable incomes of people in developing countries have contributed to the growth of the smart home market.

"Demand for smart home products, such as smart speaker and home healthcare, has increased due to the COVID-19 pandemic, as the majority of the population is working from home. The demand for home healthcare products (such as fitness trackers and health status monitors) are on the rise as people have become more conscious about monitoring heart rate, oxygen levels and other fitness-related activities regularly to avoid COVID-19 risk factors. However, global sales are expected to decline by 5–10% in this fiscal year, depending upon the progression of the virus spread," said the report.

Shipments of smart home products depend on the utilisation and requirements of homeowners. Consumers usually prefer products that offer convenience and energy efficiency. The cost of smart home products is high and involves other upfront costs such as installation and maintenance costs. The cost factor influences the market negatively.

“The concept of home automation is not new in economically advanced countries in North America and Europe. Most of the residences in North American countries are already equipped with smart devices. Several residents have been using smart thermostats, smart meters, HVAC controls and lighting controls for a long time. As the switching cost of these products is high, consumers do not replace them soon after installation. Even European countries, such as the UK and Germany, have a considerable number of smart homes. Therefore, the market for home automation products, which had a substantial penetration rate over the past three years, is expected to grow at a slower pace during the forecast period," said the report.

In the wake of COVID-19, home security solutions, such as smart locks and cameras, are facilitating contactless deliveries. Home healthcare solutions for the elderly to live a safe and independent lifestyle, such as remote chronic health monitoring, are gaining momentum. Work-from-home solutions are being used to maximize productivity (such as smart speakers/hubs to keep track of meetings and tasks).

"More entertainment needs centred around health, spirituality and personal needs are emerging owing to which there is increased adoption of smart TVs, home theatres and entertainment systems," added the report.

However, compatibility issues between various devices is a challenge for the growth of this market. As a result, end-users regularly install units which can only be controlled by the manufacturer's app. Multiple control spots is also an issue.

“In recent years, the demand for smart kitchen products, like smart ovens, is on the rise due to growing demand for frozen food and ready-to-cook foods. Unmonitored cooking is one of the leading factors responsible for kitchen fires. As a result, advanced sensors are now being developed, which enables contextual data acquisition around oven alarming for fire hazards to users. Such safety features are anticipated to further fuel demand for smart ovens in the forecast, and thereby contribute to the growth of the market. All these factors have led to the increased adoption of smart kitchen appliances," said the report.

"COVID-19 has triggered a huge increase in the demand for smart home products like smart wearables such as smartwatches, smart bands and finger rings. The data from wearable devices can also be utilised, along with self-reported symptoms, for identifying cases of COVID-19. There has therefore been a surge in the sales of smart wearable devices, especially through online channels. Rapidly growing digitalisation and expansion of e-commerce, especially among the young and millennial population, at the global level are driving online sales of the product across the world," said the report.

The market in APAC is expected to grow at the highest CAGR during the forecast period. Factors such as changing lifestyles; increasing disposable income; rising geriatric population; growing focus on smart building infrastructure in developing economies of the region; the presence of a huge customer base and rapid technological advances in smart home products are driving the growth of the market in APAC. ■
