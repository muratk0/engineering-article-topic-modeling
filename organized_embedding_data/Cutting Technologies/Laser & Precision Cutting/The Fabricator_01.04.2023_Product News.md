Panel bender delivers 17 bends/min.

Salvagnini offers the P2 panel bender with a variety of evolving options, suitable for kit or single-batch production. The machine natively combines productivity, with its automatic bending and handling cycles (an average of 17 bends/min.), and flexibility, with its universal bending tools.

The bender doesn’t need retooling, because the upper and lower blades, the counterblade, and the blank holder process the whole range of thicknesses and machinable materials. The ABA automatic blank holder adapts the tool length according to the size of the part being produced incycle, without machine downtime. The operator performs only loading and unloading, positioning the sheet metal on the worktable and collecting the bent product.

Fiber laser achieves optimum beam shape control

The Ventis 3015 AJe fiber laser uses AMADA’s Locus Beam Control to achieve infinite locus patterns, ensuring optimum beam shape control for each cutting application. According to the manufacturer, the technology allows cutting up to three times faster than with a conventional fiber laser.

A high-brightness fiber laser oscillator helps ensure optimal quality of the high-energy-density laser beam, delivering nearly dross-free cutting of a variety of materials and thicknesses, including stainless steel and aluminum. Surface roughness is reduced by about 50% when compared to a conventional machine, and dross is reduced to less than 10 μm, the company states.

Spring calculator app includes additional material types

Newcomb Spring Corp. has launched the Springulator 2.0, an advanced version of its spring calculator app. Available on Google Play and the App Store, the app is a tool for engineers and designers who wish to check the stress of a design, calculate spring functions, and test spring capabilities.

The app allows users to calculate an array of information with minimal data input. With reference diagrams and stress charting, it provides data for compression springs, extension springs, and torsion springs.

The new version includes additional material types for all spring styles, finish-type specification for all spring styles, choice of standard or crossover hooks for extension springs, and suggested body length display for crossover hooks for extension springs. Users can spec out coil gap for extension springs and leg length for torsion springs.

Work glove’s palm coating adapts grip to multiple environments

Magid’s new VersaTek work glove coating adapts to almost any environment to amplify the worker’s grip when working in wet, dry, slick, abrasive, or oily conditions. This prevents workers from having to change gloves when moving from one application to another.

While the palm coating delivers above the minimum abrasion resistance for the highest Level 6 abrasion level protection, it’s also designed to allow natural hand movements for precise handling of small parts.

Updated app checks feasibility of new tube bending projects

Unison Ltd. has launched a new version of its Tube Bending Application app, designed to help tube bender operators establish the required tooling type, mandrel style, tube size and material, bend torque, and machine size.

An indication of application difficulty—the difficulty of bending almost any metal, wall thickness, and diameter—also is provided. For users that want to bend new components or start new projects but do not have the correct tooling or machinery, the app provides a feasibility/viability check, detailing essential data regarding the equipment they will need to use.

New features include a project-specific filing system and a new dashboard for instant access to tube data, project notes, tooling setup, mandrel setup, and machine power. There also is an updated conversion tool for transforming XYZ coordinates into YBC bending data.

www.unisonltd.com

Device maintains clean internal tube finish during laser cutting

BLM GROUP has announced the availability of Clean Tube Technology for its Lasertube 8-in. and below machines to help keep the inside of the tubes free of slag, debris, and dust.

The probe is inserted into pipes during the cutting process and automatically adjusts to the tube diameter. It uses suction to capture spatter, gases, and vapors, leaving a clean, dross-free internal finish. This technology also helps stop the laser beam from reaching the opposite side of the tube wall.

Capturing the debris during the cutting process eliminates the need for a cleaning step after cutting and before secondary processes, such as tube bending with a mandrel. The cleaner the inside of the tube, the longer the life of the mandrel.
