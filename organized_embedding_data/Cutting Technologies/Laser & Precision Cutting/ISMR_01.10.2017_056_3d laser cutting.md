# 3D laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

JENOPTIK RECENTLY SHOWCASED its 3D laser metalcutting technology, JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM, at the Schweissen and Schneiden trade fair for joining, cutting and surfacing in Düsseldorf, Germany. At its joint stand with the Messer Group and Messer Cutting Systems, visitors could watch live demonstrations of JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM, its laser robot system for 3D metalcutting of chassis components for the automotive industry.

"Compared to conventional machining methods, the noncontact laser process minimises machining times by eliminating the set-up time and also reducing the costs associated with tool wear. The JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM laser cutting machine is a virtually wear-free tool, perfect for a host of metal working applications. With a repeat accuracy of up to 50μm in optimal conditions, this laser machine ranks among the most precise systems on the market and is comparable to conventional Cartesian systems. It cuts a circle or other standard contour in just one second, meaning its cycle time is significantly shorter than other robot systems," said the manufacturer.

The concept is based on a beam guide inside the robot. This means that laser sources with a power of up to 4kW can be used without the need for a complex external beam guide. The equidistant internal beam path is designed for stable beam quality. Several laser robots cutting in parallel can also be integrated into a single machine to achieve a higher output in significantly reduced floor space. The robot-based machines also enable complete line integration of the laser cutting process and can be integrated into production lines or combined processing units.

The JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM is used in the automotive industry for cutting complex chassis components made of materials such as hot-formed and ultra-high strength steels as well as cast aluminum. Hydroformed tubes can also be trimmed for use in battery or electric motor carrier units for electric cars. See www.jenoptik.com

## Deep drawing press line

LIEN CHIEH HYDRAULIC Industrial Co., Ltd. (LCH) now offers its high-speed deep drawing tandem press line with plate washer and robot automation, model type LCH-LDH. The automated line incorporates a 3D vision system, 6-axis robotic arms at every station, a robot automatic feeding system and plate washer. It is designed to increase productivity and reduce manpower.
