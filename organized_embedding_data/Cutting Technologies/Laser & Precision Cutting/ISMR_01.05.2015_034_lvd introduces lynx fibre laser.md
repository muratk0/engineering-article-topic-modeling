# LVD introduces Lynx fibre laser

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

LVD COMPANY NV has expanded its fibre laser cutting systems product line with the introduction of the Lynx FL, which made its debut at CIMT, Beijing in April.

“Lynx offers users the ability to enter the world of fibre laser cutting with the flexibility to process a wide variety of material types and thicknesses all within a modest and cost effective budget,” LVD told ISMR.

“Powered by a high efficiency IPG (2kW) fibre laser source, it provides dynamic, accurate thin sheet processing of traditional sheet metal materials such as mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium with the added versatility to efficiently process metals such as copper and brass. Increased beam absorption of the 1m wave length laser beam by the material provides processing speeds of up to three times faster than CO2 laser sources in thin sheet metal.”

Lynx maximises uptime with an integrated shuttle table system that allows one table to be loaded while the machine is cutting on the other table. Table-change time is 35 seconds. It also features an integrated Siemens IU CNC control and drive package and the new Precitec “Light-Cutter” cutting head with protective glass cartridge change, temperature and constant distance control plus a crash-protection system.
