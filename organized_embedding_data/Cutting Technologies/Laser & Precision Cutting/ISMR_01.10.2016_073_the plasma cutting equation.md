# The plasma cutting equation

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM HAS launched its new Powermax45 XP® — successor to its bestselling Powermax® plasma cutting system which offers, it says, the most power and versatility in its class.

"Delivering 6.5 kilowatts of output power, the new system is extra powerful and can cleanly cut material up to 16mm in thickness as well as sever material of nearly twice that thickness," said the manufacturer.

A new low-amp process makes it easy to mark, score and make precision gouges, while the lighter design makes it more portable. Versatility is further enhanced by the availability of 11 different torch styles for handheld and mechanized use, plus a variety of consumable types for specialised cutting and gouging applications.

The system comes with a new Duramax™ Lock torch, which has a torch disable switch for quick replacement of consumables, a FastConnect™ torch to quickly switch between different torches and Smart Sense™ technology which automatically adjusts gas pressure and flow for higher performance.

Hypertherm is also introducing a lower-amp SilverPlus® electrode. This new electrode, for its HyPerformance family of plasma cutting systems, is designed for cutting at 80 amps.

"Like all SilverPlus electrodes, the new 80-amp electrode is proven in our laboratory testing to last more than twice as long as a standard copper electrode.

Instead of replacing the electrode at a pit depth of 0.040 of an inch, as recommended with standard electrodes, SilverPlus electrodes are designed to last to a pit depth of 0.080 of an inch. In addition, the electrode delivers a very consistent range 4 and 5 cut quality throughout its life, allowing owners to lower their operating cost, with no impact on cut quality or speed," explained Hypertherm.

The electrodes, protected by patent, are created by fusing a completely solid silver tip onto a copper electrode base. A hafnium pin is then inserted into the tip. This dramatically slows the wear rate for both the electrode and nozzle, as silver disperses heat better than copper. A strong bond between the copper and silver results in more consistent wear rates, deeper pit depths and more robustness to ramp down errors and hard failures, said the manufacturer. Hypertherm also offers 130-, 200-, 260- and 400-amp versions with similar performance.
