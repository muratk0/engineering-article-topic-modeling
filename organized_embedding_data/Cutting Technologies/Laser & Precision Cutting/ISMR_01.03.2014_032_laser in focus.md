# Laser in focus

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

ADVANCED LASER TECHNOLOGY (ALT) provides laser processing technology that can be easily integrated into production lines for use in original equipment manufacturing and remanufacturing plants.

Most of its activities are concentrated on producing high quality cladding using metal powders backed up with metallurgical expertise to surface engineer key industrial components with increased corrosion resistance and hardness. Other laser processes in development are cladding using powder gels and metal strips, laser welding and hardening techniques.

“Processing with laser technology provides excellent benefits for manufacturing equipment, such as improved product lifetime, lower replacement costs and equipment restored to its original performance capability,” ALT told ISMR. “ALT offers added value from its design and consultancy expertise; the formulation of new powder and gel-based recipes; and laser head and process design.” See www.altlaser.co.uk ■
