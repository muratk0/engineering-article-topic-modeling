## OVER 100 ATTEND LASERSHAPE OPEN HOUSE

SUBCONTRACT LASER and waterjet-profiling specialist, Lasershape, is celebrating a successful recent open house at its new Nottingham headquarters in the UK, where the company played host to more than 100 people, both existing and new customers.

Held on 13 July 2012, Lasershape hosted a champagne reception followed by a day of factory tours on the hour, demonstrating the latest laser and waterjet technology in action. Machines on-site at the £3 million, 24/7 facility include four CNC laser profiling centres from TRUMPF (two fibre and two CO2 models) and a high specification WaterJet Sweden NC4060Q. Visitors were also treated to demonstrations of the latest SigmaMRP software installed recently at Lasershape to further improve factory workflow.

With its new 38,000 sq. ft. plant already receiving the latest AS9100 Rev C quality management accreditation for the aerospace industry and full Rolls-Royce supplier approval, the company makes components for almost every industry sector from shop fitting, architectural, telecommunications and medical through to transport, yellow goods and military applications. Batch sizes from one-off parts to hundreds of thousands can be accommodated.

Lasershape has secured several new customers including a major aerospace supplier, a fire prevention systems company and a supplier to the cosmetics industry. It has also secured a new phase of a major architectural project in London valued at around £250,000. ■
