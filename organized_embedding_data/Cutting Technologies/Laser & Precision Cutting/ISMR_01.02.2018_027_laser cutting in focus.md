# Laser cutting in focus

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

SALVAGNINI IS EXPANDING its product range with the L3-6020, its new laser cutting system for large-format metal sheet. The L3-6020 can process sheet metal of a maximum length of 6096mm and maximum width of 2032mm, while retaining the features of the smaller L3 models; high speed and great flexibility over a wide range of materials and thicknesses, Salvagnini told ISMR.

"Our patented load-bearing beam principle with lightened airplane manipulator has been revised and implemented in this new model, for an extremely rigid structure with even greater stability, without compromising speed and precise, easy positioning. The six sliding doors on the long side provide easy access to the working area while the large windows, approved for safe use with solid-state lasers, along with the position of the new control console, offer maximum visibility of the cutting area," said the Italian manufacturer.

Salvagnini has paid particular attention to the new electrical pallet changeover (CPE) system: this rapid system, regardless of the size and weight of the sheet, has been designed to minimise the risk of issues while transferring the machined material below the raw one. The L3-6020 is equipped with a single optics head that cuts across a range of workable thicknesses for rapid production changes, eliminating adjustment times.

"Our patented Dry Cooling technology involves cooling the optics without the use of gas or liquids, and offers real-time control of the lens temperature. The L3 is also equipped with two native cutting functions, Standard and PowerCut, that allow you to choose the operating mode best suited to different production requirements. Standard mode guarantees greater safety in unmanned manufacturing, while PowerCut offers reactivity and greater operational speed. Both can be easily activated using a toggle switch," commented Salvagnini.
