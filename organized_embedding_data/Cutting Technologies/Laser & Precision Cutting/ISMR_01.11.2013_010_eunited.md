## RAISING THE BAR

ISMR profiles a selection of the latest new water jet cutting technologies, highlighting some of the innovations and features that distinguish them in the market

ISMR SAYS:

“Manufacturers who need to constantly change materials and cutting contours can benefit from the flexibility offered by water jet cutting”

Water jets can enhance the productivity, versatility and profitability of sheet metal subcontractors. They require few secondary operations; produce net-shaped parts with no heat-affected zone, heat distortion or mechanical stresses caused by other cutting methods; can cut with a narrow kerf and make better usage of raw material since parts can be tightly nested. As the cut edge quality is generally excellent, there is normally no need to re-work the parts.

Because there is no heat-affected zone (HAZ), water jets can cut more materials and eliminate grinding off hardened material and slag. No dust, fumes or gases are released during the process. The method allows for high precision cutting with smooth cut edges and no burrs. Water jets can cut 1/16 inch to 12 inches or more of whatever material is chosen. They can also replace and complement other machine tool processes in the workshop such as EDM, milling and laser cutting.

Abrasive water jets can machine materials that lasers cannot (such as copper). Precision abrasive jet machines can also obtain the same or higher tolerances than lasers, especially as material thickness increases.

## Innovation in focus

Water jet cutting is particularly advantageous when it comes to the production of complex cut shapes. The cold cutting method enables the cutting of virtually any shape in any material. Manufacturers who need to constantly change materials and cutting contours can benefit from the flexibility offered by water jet cutting. Water jet cutting can be combined with other cutting technologies to produce sharp corners, bevelled edges and extremely small radii which are generally difficult to achieve with other methods.

Machine tool and product manufacturers are continuously making improvements and upgrades to their water jet cutting technologies. Here, ISMR profiles a selection of the latest new water jet cutting technologies, highlighting some of the innovations and features that distinguish them in the market.

Swiss manufacturer Bystronic supports current sheet metal processing trends with innovations in software, bending and laser and water jet cutting. Its Byjet series of water jet cutting systems have served the market for many years. The latest ByJet Smart features an innovatively-designed cutting bridge, a precision-manufactured machine frame made of highly shock-absorbing composite material and a dynamic high-pressure pump with extremely short pressurisation and de-pressurisation periods.

Depending upon requirements, the ByJet Smart can be equipped with components that enhance its performance. The PositionPointer allows the cutting head to be positioned precisely. During processing, this results in a minimum waste of raw material. FixMaster eliminates the need for micro joints. Cut parts can be fixed to the sheet skeleton with soluble drops of adhesive.

PrecisionAligner is a fine adjustment tool that enables cutting with two cutting heads on a single part. There is also an option to equip the cutting heads with a drill spindle.

Flow International Corporation, a leading developer and manufacturer of industrial water jet machines for cutting and cleaning applications, has entered into a definitive agreement for acquisition by American Industrial Partners (AIP), a middlemarket private equity firm focused on North American-headquartered industrial businesses, in a transaction valued at over US$ 200 million.

Under the terms of the merger agreement, AIP will acquire all of the outstanding shares of Flow common stock for US$ 4.05 per share in cash. This represents an 11.3 times multiple of enterprise value to trailing four quarter reported EBITDA of US$ 17.8 million as of 31 July 2013. The Flow Board of Directors has unanimously approved the merger agreement.

Flow recently introduced the Paser 4 abrasive cutting head system, now available for Flow abrasive water jet shape-cutting systems operating at pressures of either 60ksi or 87ksi. The newly designed cutting head establishes a new industry benchmark with rapid cutting speeds and low operating costs.

Flow continues to raise the bar in performance by incorporating new patent-pending technology to significantly extend component life. This means lower cost of operation since fewer parts and abrasive are consumed and time between maintenance intervals is greatly reduced. Orifice component life is increased 3-6 times over earlier generations and competing cutting heads, it told ISMR.

For the first time since the acquisition of Accustream in early 2013, Hypertherm demonstrated water jet cutting products on its stand (including pumps, abrasive regulation and cutting heads) at the recent Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition in Essen.

“The acquisition advanced us in our strategy to provide customers with the optimum cutting technology. Whether plasma, laser or waterjet is required for their particular cutting application, we can offer the solution,” explained Theo Cornielje, Hypertherm’s regional director for the EMEA region.

Its HyPrecision™ water jet pumps range in horsepower from 15 to 150. The advanced intensifier technology extends maintenance intervals, reduces maintenance time and maximises performance.

Jet Edge, Inc. will introduce its new EDGE X-5 5-axis water jet cutting system at the FABTECH 2013 exhibition in Chicago (booth S2762) from 18-21 November 2013. During FABTECH, Jet Edge will conduct live 5-axis water jet cutting demonstrations on its 90KSI EDGE X-5 water jet system. The water jet manufacturer will also exhibit several ultra-high pressure water jet pumps, including its 90KSI X-Stream hydraulic intensifier pumps and the eco-friendly ECO-JET direct drive pump.

The US manufacturer’s EDGE X-5 5-axis water jet system cuts precise taper-free parts from virtually any material. The system is capable of cutting sophisticated 3D parts such as impellers and bevels up to 50°. Its sturdy design separates the motion system from the catcher tank, eliminating vibration and ensuring high part quality. The EDGE X-5 utilises an industrial PC controller. The machine is available in many sizes and an optional second cutting head can be added to double productivity.

“Our 100hp, 90KSI (75kw, 6200 bar), X-Stream xP90-100 water jet intensifier pump is capable of producing 90KSI (6200 bar) and supports 75KSI (5200 bar) continuous operating pressure. The X-Stream achieves much faster cutting speeds and drastically lowers operating costs compared to traditional 60KSI (4100 bar) water jet pumps, enabling users to increase productivity and reduce part costs. The X-Stream produces 50% more pressure than a 60KSI (4100 bar) intensifier pump, resulting in a 40-50% increase in productivity for many materials. Compared to a 60KSI (4100 bar) pump, typical operating pressures of 75KSI (5200 bar) use 30% less water, 30% less power and up to 50% less abrasive resulting in a 40% reduction in operating cost,” Jet Edge told ISMR.

Nordstjernan recently signed a definitive agreement to sell 100% of the shares in water jet specialist, KMT Group, to American Industrial Partners. KMT is a global specialist in the design and manufacture of products utilising ultra-high pressure (“UHP”) pump technology in industrial manufacturing, surface preparation and food processing applications. AIP will acquire KMT for an enterprise value of US$ 147.5 million.

KMT Waterjet Systems has a network of professional OEMs (Original Equipment Manufacturers) who specialise in water jet machine technology. These OEMs will design and manufacture the waterjet cutting table system that works best for a cutting application. Options include abrasive machine cutting; pure water only cutting; entry level, small and large format, taper control, bevel head, cantilever, gantry or bridge style tables; CNC software; 2-axis to 6-axis robotic systems and combination water jet/plasma/ waterjet/SawJet systems. KMT water jet products also include pumps, cutting nozzles and abrasive transfer systems.

Mitsubishi has broadened its supply chain offering with the introduction of an entry-level water jet machine, the MW3. This new machine is suitable for production environments and customers that are new to water jet. Its solid base construction and cantilever head design make it reliable and affordable, the company told ISMR.

The Mitsubishi 720 CNC control delivers advanced programming capabilities. The unit is controlled on a 15-inch waterproof screen, with improved graphics and NC design to simplify operation. Easy-to-use menu and help functions guide the operator through both programming and work piece set-up.

MW3 comes standard with a high pressure S-50 KMT pump, which provides 60,000 PSI of water traveling up to MACH 2 with 1.09 GPM of water consumption through a maximum orifice size of 0.013”. The reciprocating hydraulic piston to ceramic water piston ratio is 20:1, pushing the water into an accumulator that maintains a smooth and even flow to the cutting nozzle. Other Mitsubishi models include the 2-3 axis Classica, 4-axis Suprema and 5-axis Evolution X5.

At EMO 2013, OMAX Corporation showcased how advanced abrasive water jet technology cuts simple to complex shapes from virtually any material quickly and accurately. The company unveiled its advancements in micro-machining as well as demonstrating the precision of its OMAX JetMachining® Centers, the versatility of its MAXIEM® JetCutting Centers and innovative features of its cutting head accessories.

OMAX has been making great strides in water jet-based micromachining, targeting manufacturing segments that produce parts or part features smaller than 300 microns. The company shared many of its recent achievements in micro-abrasive waterjet technology at EMO, highlighting how it is a viable process with competitive advantages.

The 60120 exemplified the JetMachining Center line-up at EMO 2013, demonstrating how the company’s intuitive Intelli-MAX® Software Suite, which OMAX features on all its JetMachining Centers, provides high cutting speeds and great precision. With a Tilt-A-Jet® cutting head accessory, the 60120 is able to achieve zero taper for precision cutting of high tolerance parts with square edges, including interlocking pieces and dovetail fittings. Through the Intelli-MAX control software, the Tilt-A-Jet automatically calculates and adjusts the angle of the cutting nozzle to accurately remove the natural taper from the finished part by transferring it to the scrap material while maintaining extremely high cutting speeds.

With an A-Jet cutting head accessory, OMAX’s MAXIEM 1515 JetCutting Center is able to make precise angled cuts and bevels, as well as automatically correct for the taper normally found in an abrasive water jet cut. The method allows for high precision cutting with smooth cut edges and no burrs. Supplied with a MAXJET® 5i, a long-life diamond integrated nozzle, the A-Jet cuts bevelled edges at angles determined by either the machine operator or part program.

The A-Jet adds two motion axes enabling the nozzle to tilt over a range of 0 degrees to 60 degrees from the vertical position. It can also be used to easily cut countersunk holes as well as machine parts with complex geometries. With its additional axes of motion, the A-Jet allows production of bevelled edges in preparation for welding operations. Use of this accessory eliminates the need for many secondary machining and grinding operations.

TECHNI Waterjet’s reputation has been strengthened with the ANCA Group electing to take up an option to increase its stake in the company. The strategic alliance will help it to continue to increase production to meet demand for its products such as the new Quantum NXT ESP electric servo pump.

“The backing of a leading global machine tool manufacturer, such as the ANCA Group, will help to grow the business, enhance wider recognition of our products and strengthen and protect our unique Intellectual Property IP portfolio,” the company told ISMR.

The TECHNI Waterjet™ Quantum ESP™ is a new concept in water jet cutting. It incorporates a servo motor directly enveloping a high load, precision ball screw. The ball screw directly houses the ceramic plungers which reciprocate back and forth to create the pumping action, in much the same way as a hydraulic cylinder works on an intensifier pump. Water jet cutting is particularly advantageous when it comes to the production of complex cut shapes.

Feedback to ensure accuracy of motion. By mapping the entire cutting area with a lasercalibrated digital encoder, the motion system can deliver positional accuracy of 0.0005” (0.01mm) and high cutting speeds of up to1000ipm or 25m/min. It won the “Excellence in Product Design” Award at the Manufacturing Innovation Excellence Awards on its introduction into the market.

Every Techjet-X3® comes standard with Softec water jet software. At the heart of Softec is Tru-Cut Technology, a sophisticated algorithms database that determines the optimum cutting speeds and machine ramping characteristics.

WARDJet is a leading US manufacturer of over 30 water jet and abrasive water jet cutting machines. The company recently began setting up the 10’ steel surrounds for an exciting new project on its shop floor that is a radical departure from any of the water jets in its current product line. This new enterprise features WARDJet water jet technology seamlessly integrated into a system designed to open up new possibilities in automated production. By partnering with a global industry leader to create a water jet ‘unlike anything else it has ever done’, WARDJet hopes to provide a high quality solution suitable for many custom applications.

With over 100 different water jet models in its portfolio (2D, 3D BevelJet, pure water, finecut or abrasive water jet cutting technology) Water Jet Sweden AB can tailor solutions to meet precise needs. It has, to date, delivered over 600 machines to 38 countries.

“Our own worldwide patent for the integrated guide system, high performance cutting heads and valves, as well as collaborative work with our system control suppliers, G E Fanuc and Siemens, has made it possible for us to manufacture a full range of 2D and 3D water jet machinery with maximum accuracy and performance,” the company told ISMR.

At the MACH Tool exhibition this June, it ran live demos on pure water jet cutting, Beveljet 3D-cutting and tube-cutting. At its Open House in April this year, it launched its new machine category, the NCM 10 Micro – a new water jet cutting system developed for the micro component industry. ■

The Techjet-X3® incorporates Linear Scale cutting machine. The infinite control of the servo motor and precision of the ball screw enable accurate control over the output pressure and volume of the water displaced and eliminate pressure spikes when deadheading. This infinite control gives an operator the ability to program virtually any pressure and flow rate from zero to full capacity, while only using the power displaced at the cutting head.

The Techjet-X3® series is the latest in the company’s evolution of products to create a precision, easy-to-operate, safe and reliable water jet.
