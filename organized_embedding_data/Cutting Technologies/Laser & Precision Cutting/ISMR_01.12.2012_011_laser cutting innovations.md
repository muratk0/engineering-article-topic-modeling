# Laser cutting innovations

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

No less than three of the innovations at EuroBLECH came from the laser cutting segment. Innovation number one: the BySprint Pro 3015 with six kilowatts. Since the system now offers even more laser power, it is more versatile as a high speed system for thin to medium-thick metal sheets as well as an all-rounder with which a user can cut sheets of up to 25mm thick.

A similar development took place with the second innovation: the fiber laser cutting system, BySprint Fiber, which is now available in the 4020 format. Bystronic says that a four by two metre sheet allows 78 per cent more parts to be nested than a sheet with a format of three by one and a half metres. The time during which the machine can cut without interruption increases by the same ratio. BySprint Fiber is now available with both three and four kilowatts of power and hence approaches the sheet metal thickness range that was, up to now, limited to CO2 laser cutting systems and provides the user with even greater flexibility.

Innovation number three: the ByAutonom, which was developed from the beginning as an autonomously working machine which, together with automation modules such as ByTrans, ByTower and Bycell, can be used for reduced-manning operations. This is also applicable if the type and thickness of the raw material has to be changed. The machine sets the applicable focal length and selects the appropriate nozzle, centres automatically and continues cutting. It received an award during the EuroBLECH exhibition.

“Higher output means that the cost per part is reduced,” explained Bystronic. Even thick material can be cut faster because of its special 9-inch cutting head which comes as standard equipment and is better suited to such orders than a 7.5-inch cutting head.

ISMR: Can you give any indication of Bystronic results for the year?

FT: It is a little early to say but I assume that we will achieve a growth rate of 5-10 per cent this year because the markets are very different. The US market is booming, so is the Korean market. We also find the UK market very interesting at the moment. The Russian and Turkish markets are going well. Some southern European markets are struggling, as expected with the current euro crisis.

We see good growth in some south-east Asian markets and predict that our sales in China will be lower this year than last year. However, we feel this will come back. Interestingly, the Chinese market has no experience of decline whereas European markets have experienced downturn before.

Next year will be a difficult year. We expect smaller growth in 2013 than in 2012. Europe remains an unstable market although we do see growth pockets in countries like Russia and China plus regions like eastern Europe.

## FACE TO FACE

ISMR: You launched nine new products at EuroBLECH. What is your R&D philosophy?

FT: 10 per cent of Bystronic’s staff work in R&D. On new products today, half of the ideas come from customers and various players in the market and half come directly from our R&D activities. Every two years, we have an idea workshop in Switzerland where we invite ideas from customers as well as a certain number of customers and Bystronic staff plus a number of young people, with no pre-conceived ideas about our business, who can think ‘out of the box’ e.g. students. They suggest novel ideas like controlling machines by iPhone and the voice control concept we recently adopted on our high-end press brakes. We try to make these ideas reality within three years so that customers can reap the maximum advantage.
