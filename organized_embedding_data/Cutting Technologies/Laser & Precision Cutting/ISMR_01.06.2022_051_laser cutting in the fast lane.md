# Laser cutting in the fast lane

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

The flagship of Bystronic's laser cutting systems is now available with 20 kilowatts of laser power and an optimised cutting process. Its latest generation cutting head was developed for consistently high quality with high laser outputs and demanding cutting applications.

"Compared to 15 kilowatts, the productivity of the 20-kilowatt ByStar Fiber is on average 40% in mild steel and stainless steel of 4mm to 20mm. This means that sheet metal processing companies can benefit from higher productivity at low unit costs because the new ByStar Fiber cuts steel, aluminium and stainless steel precisely. The 20 kilowatts of laser power thus enable maximum flexibility for both large series and urgent customer orders," said Bystronic.

"Whether cutting aluminium, non-ferrous metals or steel, the high-performance Bystronic cutting head excels with maximum precision in both thin and thick sheets and profiles. The 20 kilowatts open up extended applications in steel and aluminium of up to 50mm. The optional 'Parameter Wizard' ensures that the correct quality of the cut parts is always selected by obtaining the perfect parameter within minutes. The new power level is available for the ByStar 3015, 4020, 6225 and 8025," it added.

The fibre laser cutting system is controlled using ByVision Cutting software via a 21.5-inch touch screen. Operating the machine, said the manufacturer, is “as simple as using a smartphone."
