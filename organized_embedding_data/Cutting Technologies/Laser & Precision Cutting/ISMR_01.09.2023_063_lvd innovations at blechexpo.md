# LVD innovations at Blechexpo

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Belgian sheet-metal specialist, LVD, will address the challenges of metal fabrication with new laser- cutting, bending automation and software integration solutions at Blechexpo in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023). On its stand 1808 in Hall 1 at the exhibition, visitors will find the new Phoenix FL-6525 laser with a large cutting range of up to 6510 x 2600mm and bevelcutting option.

"Its large format makes cutting oversized sheets easy and efficient; high dynamics ensure high-quality cutting results. The bevelcutting head provides precise control of the angle and depth of the bevel to cut intricate contours or for weld-prep operations. The Phoenix FL-6525 at Blechexpo will feature a 12kW laser with bevel head,"LVD told ISMR.

Making its debut at the show will be the Easy-Cell 80/25. LVD's latest robotic bending cell, which combines an Easy-Form 80/25 press brake with KUKA robot, automates the bending of a range of part geometries up to 1600 x 1200mm (weighing up to 25kg). LVD's Easy-Form® Laser adaptive bending system is designed to ensure accuracy from the first bend. Easy-Cell accelerates "art to part" production by automatically generating bending and robot program, with no robot teaching required.

"We power the smart factory with the new CADMAN-Flow. CADMAN-Flow uses a single point of entry to a complete software suite: software for (robotic) bending, laser cutting and punching, Manufacturing Execution System (MES) and Smart Drawing Importer. Via one portal, users can import and verify parts and assemblies, create CAM programs, import production orders and retrieve production information from the touch controls, improving the speed and flexibility of the process flow," added the company.

LVD is a leading manufacturer of sheet metalworking equipment including lasercutting systems, punch presses, press brakes, guillotine shears and automation systems, integrated to and supported by its CADMAN® software suite. Its Industry 4.0-ready products and technology make smart manufacturing possible.■
