# AUTOMATION IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.06.2011 ISMR]

MODERN LASER CUTTING machines, such as the TruLaser 5030 Fiber, aim for the highest productivity possible in certain segments, such as thin sheet processing. The resulting short sheet feed times mean faster sorting of parts and sheet skeletons to avoid bottlenecks at the machine. To meet this demand, TRUMPF offers a new and ergonomic solution: the PartMaster. It complements the LiftMaster Compact automation component for laser flatbed machines.

A conveyor belt moves the cut parts into an ergonomically-correct position that is comfortable for the operator. This allows the operator to sort them easily while additional sheets are being processed, and means he/she does not tire as quickly and can work more productively. For completely automated operation overnight, the conveyor belt can also be replaced by a regular finished parts pallet.

Tests with sample parts of varying thicknesses and geometry indicated time savings of up to 34 per cent compared to parts sorted directly from the pallet changer, says TRUMPF. The finished parts move automatically and in an orderly fashion to the sorting station.

The PartMaster is a modular component in the LiftMaster Compact that can also be installed into existing systems.
