# Flexible laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

PRECITEC presented its latest generation of cutting heads at the recent EuroBLECH exhibition. Its new compact "ProCutter" was developed specifically for laser-based fusion cutting of thin and medium material thicknesses in the 1µm wavelength range. With an integrated sensor system, three different designs and individual configuration options, this new development offers optimized flexibility.

The ProCutter owes its flexibility to a concept which combines three different product versions with individually adjustable configurations. The cutting head is available as a straight or offset version (90° exit to the left or right-hand side) and can be adapted to the machine concept concerned. In addition, users can choose between the ProCutter version with motorized focal position adjustment or the manual version which adjusts the focal position manually.

The ProCutter Zoom version offers manufacturers, who want to machine a broad range of sheet thicknesses, the option of varying the focal position and focal diameter independently of one another. With an adjustable zoom of between 1.25x and 2.5x, the laser spot size directed at the work-piece can be adjusted up to a factor of 2. Precitec offers a further configuration with the CutMonitor version to monitor the piercing process and start the cutting process precisely when the material has been completely penetrated.

The product design of the ProCutter range enables material machining with laser power of up to 6kW in the near-infrared range, with small installation space and low weight. See www.precitec.com ■

## Process efficiency

IN 2014, SALVAGNINI followed Industry 4.0 guidelines and presented new solutions designed to take process efficiency to the next level.

“The S4+P4 line is one of our most successful solutions, allowing you to punch, shear and bend sheet metal in a non-stop process that transitions seamlessly from blank to finished part. It has been designed to run kit and batch-one production, and to process series of parts where each is different from the next, in an efficient flow, without work-in-process. This does away with the need for intermediate sheet handling, thanks to automatic blankholder set-ups and the multipress head, with no set-up times,” explained Salvagnini.

Its S4Xe integrated punching and shearing centre is a flexible sheet metal processing system capable of punching, cutting and processing blanks automatically. All the operations that used to require manual intervention – such as cutting, feeding, unloading, stacking, separating and sorting – are combined and automated in this single system. S4Xe pairs a patented multi-press head with an independent-blade angle shear – both are fitted in a single structure and work in concert with each other.

The S4Xe Punch&Cut function recognizes the punches belonging to each individual part, groups them together accordingly and processes them separately, balancing kit or multiple productions as generally required on FMS lines. The new manipulator features nine independently opening pincers which are controlled by an intelligent path optimization algorithm.

The Salvagnini P4Xe panel bender produces sheet metal panels from notched blanks without any need for handling or operator intervention. It works with one universal tool capable of forming all manner of bends, while the ABA blankholder ensures that the composition is adjusted automatically and instantly in-process, resulting in zero set-up times. The new CLA SIM option has been designed so that the length of the auxiliary blades can also be set up automatically. Each P4Xe also comes with the ABC feature, a device to allow automatic programmable bending unit crowning, for improved bend quality and repeatability.

P4Xe measures basic material characteristics throughout part processing to automatically initiate corrective machine movements. The new MAC 2.0, a proprietary technology, calculates spring-back effect and automatically modifies the program to minimize scrap and assure quality and operational repeatability.
