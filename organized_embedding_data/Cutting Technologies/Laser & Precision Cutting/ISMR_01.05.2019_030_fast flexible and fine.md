# FAST, FLEXIBLE AND FINE

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

We outline a selection of new and innovative waterjet cutting technologies for sheet metal manufacturers.

ISMR SAYS:

“Waterjet cutting produces excellent edge quality which, in many cases, eliminates the need for secondary finishing processes

From metal to carving intricate 3D designs in marble, the waterjet has numerous applications and is one of the most versatile cutting systems in the manufacturing industry today. The introduction of 5-axis cutting heads has triggered massive growth in waterjet cutting. No longer confined to cutting out flat objects, waterjets are now able to produce continuous bevels, revolve around the workpiece and cut at angles from 0-90 degrees.

Despite the high kinetic energy in waterjet cutting during the working of a high-pressure waterjet cutting machine, part deformation is avoided and cutting accuracy can be achieved without leaving any frayed edges or burrs. This produces excellent edge quality which, in many cases, eliminates the need for secondary finishing processes.

Waterjet cutting is referred to as a "coldcutting" process because it requires no extra heat. In fact, as only high-pressure water and abrasives are used, waterjet cutting generates very little heat, even from friction, so there is no risk of burning, melting or affecting the material in any way (no heataffected zone). Sharp corners, bevels, pierce holes and shapes with minimal inner radii are all possible. Cold cutting also reduces overall production time because there is little or no need for clamping or bracing materials during set-up. Operators do not have to wait for materials to cool down before moving onto the next step in production.

Cold cutting also offers a high level of precision. As pressurised water is flowing through a very small area, users have greater control over the cuts being made. Cold cutting is often used for more detailed cuts, such as geometric shapes or designs. It can also be used to cut a wider variety of materials as well as more irregular surfaces.

To produce an air-free jet, the suspension is pressurised in a highpressure vessel and forced out of the nozzle via a hose.

The X-Series incorporates a re-engineered lightweight and rigidly constructed modular gantry to hold multiple cutting heads and components and an optional integrated variable water level control system.
