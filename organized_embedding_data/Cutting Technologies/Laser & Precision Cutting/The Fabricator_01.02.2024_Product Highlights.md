Portable fiber laser cleaner cleans material without grinding, solvents

Rocklin Manufacturing has introduced MobiLase, a portable fiber laser cleaner housed in an all-in-one, 60-lb. rolling suitcase. The 100-W fiber laser rapidly removes rust, paint, oil, grease, and dirt by cleaning the material or coating while not disrupting the part itself.

With this process, there is no need for grinding, blasting, solvents, water, or chemicals. At the double-click of the trigger, the 1.4-lb., hand-held applicator delivers a short pulse duration and small-diameter output beam for precise surface-level cleaning wherever it’s needed.

Settings on the accompanying wireless device enable simple programming either wirelessly or offline. Laser glasses, gloves, and fume extraction options are available for added safety protection. The fiber laser source is rated for up to 100,000 hours of use with minimal maintenance required.

Panel benders process large, complex geometries in high volumes

LVD has added panel benders, including high-speed systems, to its line of sheet metal processing equipment. The panel benders can process large and complex geometries quickly and efficiently in medium to high volumes, the company reports.

Three models are available to handle panel sizes up to 3,200 by 1,250 mm, and the machines can be customized to expand capacity and performance. Their servo-driven design requires minimal service and maintenance. A universal set of folding blades permits processing of a variety of profiles and bends. Most profiles can be formed using a single set of tooling.

The panel benders are suitable for producing large electrical cabinets, panels and cabinet components, shelving, clean rooms, steel furniture, architectural building panels, photovoltaic components, trailer side panels, lighting, and other industrial equipment panels featuring complex geometries with radius bends, short side lengths, and narrow profiles.
