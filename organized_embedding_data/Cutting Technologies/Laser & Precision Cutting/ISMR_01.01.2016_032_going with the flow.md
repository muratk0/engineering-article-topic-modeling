# Going with the flow

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

WATER JET SWEDEN’S new standard product range opens up new markets for the waterjet cutting systems manufacturer. It has launched a new product range with standard machines offering, it told ISMR, high quality and performance but at a lower price.

“We have identified an increased demand for our machines but with features and pricing for more fundamental cutting needs,” commented Lennart Svensson, CEO, Water Jet Sweden (WJS).

To supply the market with these types of machines, WJS has been developing a completely new machine platform. It has tested different designs, materials and components in the quest to find a solution that fits high quality demands, but is less expensive to produce and install. The new machine systems can be purchased for less than €100,000 which is a completely new price level for Water Jet Sweden.

“This gives us a perfect product range for a more price-sensitive segment of the market,” explained Lennart Svensson. “There are many countries where we can see substantial growth potential, thanks to the new machines. We have a strong and established brand around the world and now we can offer one of the most complete product portfolios in the market.”

The machine systems are sold as complete packages where everything is included - machine system, installation and the training that is required to operate the machines. See www.waterjetsweden.com ■
