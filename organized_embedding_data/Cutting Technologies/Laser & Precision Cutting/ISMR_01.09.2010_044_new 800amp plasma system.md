# NEW 800AMP PLASMA SYSTEM

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Hypertherm has announced a new 800 amp HyPerformance Plasma system for cutting thick stainless steel and aluminum. In addition to its ability to production pierce 75mm and sever 160mm thick metal, the system also cuts thin metal - down to 1 to 2mm - without the need to switch torches.

“The new HyPerformance HPR800XD system is able to pierce thick metal due to PowerPierce technology. This patent-pending technology eliminates the need for predrilling and minimizes damage to the torch by using a liquid cooled shield to repel molten metal during piercing and cutting. In testing, we found there was no damage to its consumables or lessening of cut quality, even after piercing 75mm thick stainless steel 400 times,” said Hypertherm.

From 30 to 400 amps, operators can use HyPerformance Plasma processes to mark, bevel and cut mild steel, stainless steel and aluminum. This versatility is extended up to 800 amps so operators can also cut thick stainless steel and aluminum with the same system. Other benefits of the new Hypertherm technology include an optional auto gas console for increased productivity and ease of use; the ability to cut, bevel and mark using the same set of consumables and a single quick disconnect torch for fast and efficient consumable changeovers.
