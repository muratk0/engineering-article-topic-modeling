# New laser for Outokumpu

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

GLOBAL SPECIALIST IN stainless steel manufacturing, Outokumpu, has significantly increased production after replacing two lasers with one bespoke Kimla laser cutting machine.

For the past 15 years, Outokumpu has been running three CO₂ laser machines to fulfil its cutting needs. Working with MBA Engineering, a supplier of laser cutting and metal fabrication equipment, the team identified a requirement to update its metal cutting capabilities function.

After discussing its cutting capacity and turnaround with MBA, Outokumpu came to the conclusion that it could increase production with just the one machine. It made the decision to replace two of its three machines with one Kimla laser cutting system after a visit to the Kimla facility in Poland with MBA Engineering.

With main aims being to increase production, reduce running costs and free valuable floor space, Kimla provided a bespoke, fully automated fibre laser cutting machine to the Finnish company. Outokumpu was directly involved in the design of the automation system for the Kimla ProCut 20 60 LF 6kW, 6m x 2m, ensuring that it was built to the specifications required by the team.

Rob Swift, Project and Technical Sales Manager, Outokumpu, explained: "When we decided to update our laser cutting processes, we had clear objectives in mind. MBA Engineering surpassed our expectations with a machine that can cut a part in ten minutes – this previously took an hour on our old machines. In an industry where time is money, it's great to have this kind of technology at the heart of our business and we're very much looking forward to seeing the potential we have unlocked for Outokumpu with this investment."

Bradley McBain, Managing Director, MBA Engineering, added: "Outokumpu is one of the biggest names in the stainless steel industry, so we're immensely proud to provide the company with a bespoke fibre laser. The feedback we have received from Outokumpu's operators has been great, with claims that the machine cuts 5mm stainless as quickly as 1mm on high power."

Established in 2009, MBA Engineering is the exclusive distributor of Kimla fibre laser cutting equipment in the UK. See www.mba-eng.co.uk
