15-kW fiber laser increases cutting speed up to 50%

Bystronic has introduced the 15-kW Bystar Fiber laser for the ByStar 3015 and ByStar 4020. According to the company, cutting speed is increased by up to 50% (when cutting with nitrogen) compared to a 10-kW laser source. The laser enables extended applications in stainless steel and aluminum up to 1.5 in. thick.

Whether cutting aluminum, nonferrous metals, or steel, the Bystronic SSC cutting head achieves precision in both thin and thick sheets and profiles, states the manufacturer. The variable telescoping cutting head automatically adjusts the focal length and spot size, and the focal position is set automatically for each material.

The optional BeamShaper function helps ensure smooth cutting edges and increased speeds in carbon steel materials with fluctuating material characteristics from 0.750 to 1.125 in. thick.

Bystronic

www.bystronicusa.com

Curved, water-cooled GMAW gun handles aluminum

D/F Machine Specialties has introduced the NCC P-38 curved, watercooled, hand-held GMAW gun. The lightweight, heavy-duty unit is intended for push welding of aluminum.

The gun is designed for use with all manufacturers’ wire feeders that have provision for water cooling. It accommodates a slip-in tip fastened by a collet action nut.

The nozzle assembly water chamber and inner body water chamber are serially connected to each other with sliding, removable connectors. This simplifies detachment of the nozzle assembly, eliminating the need for gaskets in the water passage system.

D/F Machine Specialties

www.dfmachinespecialties.com

Portable dust extraction system helps metalworkers stay safe

Chicago Pneumatic has introduced an all-in-one dust extraction system that comprises a CPA5000 pneumatic vacuum cleaner and lightweight shroud. This compact, portable system is designed for use with traditional pneumatic hand tools to enable dust-free drilling, grinding, and sanding, no matter where the work takes place.

The 2.8-lb. vacuum cleaner can be carried from job to job, while the lightweight, durable shroud is simple to adapt to existing hand tools. Clear windows enable 225-degree visibility.

The same air line is used to power both the hand tool and the vacuum cleaner. The hose features a 1-in. interface to help optimize flow.

PAPR helmet offers heavy-duty protection from welding fumes

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has developed the Savage A40 powered air purifying respirator helmet. It is suitable for use in situations where welding fumes and particulates are a concern, such as for some cored wires, working in poorly ventilated spaces, and for protecting operators from hexavalent chromium fumes.

The unit has three settings that adjust airflow between 170 and 230 l/min. to suit the application. It operates at 70 dB and features a high-capacity, rechargeable lithium-ion battery that offers up to eight hours of performance between charges.

Laser microscope offers smart features for fast experiment work flows

Olympus offers the LEXT OLS5100 laser microscope for submicron 3D observation and surface roughness measurement. It includes smart features to help make materials science experiment work flows faster and more efficient, the company states.

The microscope’s Smart Experiment Manager helps users manage the planning, acquisition, and analysis phases of their experiments. The microscope scans samples according to a customized experiment plan generated by the software, helping to prevent users from missing data or redoing work. During analysis, the soft-ware’s trend-visualization tools help users spot problems.

The Smart Lens Advisor rates objective lenses based on the application, so users know they are using the correct objective.

Olympus

www.olympus-ims.com

Welding carriage features multifunction drive system

Koike Aronson Ransome has introduced the Wel-Handy Multi Next portable welding carriage for semiautomated welding. It features a multifunction drive system that allows operators to perform straight line, stitch, and weave welds with the same carriage.

The welding carriage is constructed with a single-piece aluminum die-cast body protected by aluminum and stainless steel heat shields to resist slag penetration.

The 120-ft.-lb. pull force magnet delivers traction control to help ensure precise, consistent results for vertical and horizontal welding.

Esco Tool has introduced the COHOG clamshell split-frame machine for beveling and end prepping tube and pipe. Wire offers expanded mechanical properties

Hobart has announced that its FabCO Triple 7 gas-shielded flux-cored wire now meets the requirements for an AWS E71T-9 C/M H8 classification, in addition to the AWS E71T-1 C/M H8 classification the wire previously held.

The T-9 designation provides greater impact strengths at low temperatures—20 ft.-lbs. at -20 degrees F—for critical jobs in manufacturing, construction, fabrication, ship, railcar, and structural steel.

Cordless nibbler and shear cut curved, straight metal surfaces

Metabo Corp. offers an 18-V nibbler and 18-V shear. The Metabo NIV 18 LTX BL 1.6 nibbler comes with a punch, die, and combination key. Cutting speed is adjustable, and the brushless motor delivers 600 to 2,360 SPM. The tool can cut through 16-ga. mild steel, 18-ga. stainless steel, and 14-ga. aluminum, with a minimum curve radius of ⅝ in.

The SCV 18 LTX BL 1.6 shear provides 1,300 to 4,840 SPM. The tool can cut through 14-ga. aluminum or nonferrous metals, 16-ga. carbon steel, and 18-ga. alloy/stainless steel, with a minimum curve radius of 19/16 in. The tool includes a guard for hand protection, a speed selector, two blades, two hexagon wrenches, and a feeler gauge.

Nonwoven file belts finish complex parts

Saint-Gobain Abrasives has introduced Norton Vortex Rapid Prep nonwoven file belts for finishing tight, intricate, and complex parts. The belts cut like a coarse grit size while producing finishes similar to a finer-grit abrasive on a variety of metals. The proprietary Clean Bond resin system helps ensure a smear-free finish.

The belts can be used in combination with coolants or in dry applications. They are available in several sizes, including widths of ¼, ½, and ¾ in., with lengths from 12 to 24 in. depending on belt width.

Fiber laser handles sheet, tube

The Haco HFL-E series fiber laser combines a flatbed and tube laser in one machine. It offers a power output from 1 to 6 kW and flatbed table sizes up to 6 by 2 m.

Handling a maximum tube length of 6.5 m, the machine can cut tubes as small as 20 to 230 mm depending on the model. The digitally controlled pneumatic chucks help ensure precise movement while cutting without compromising speed, the company states.

Weld Systems Integrators Inc. (WSI) has introduced a 12,000-joule capacitive discharge welder series. They are suitable for projection welding fasteners, with standard or ring-type projections, to hot-stamped, aluminum-silicate-coated materials; galvanized metals; and other coated materials.

The integrated welder design includes the company’s fast follow-up head and an active displacement system for graphing displacement for every weld.
