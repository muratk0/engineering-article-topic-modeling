# Launch of new fibre laser

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

High-tech company TRUMPF has developed a particularly versatile fibre laser range for electric motors and hydrogen fuel cells.

“With the new TruFiber P fiber lasers, users can manufacture core components of electric drives and hydrogen fuel cells quickly and particularly reliably,” said Mark Richmond, product manager responsible for fibre lasers at TRUMPF. The company presented the TruFiber P at the LASER – World of Photonics trade fair in Munich this June.

TRUMPF recently extended the TruFiber P range to include laser power levels from 500-2000W, with single mode and multimode variants in a smaller format to complement the existing higher power, 3000-6000W multimode lasers.

The single-mode fibre, which can be up to ten metres long, allows users to design their production more flexibly, as the beam source does not have to be directly next to the laser cell.

It is becoming more and more important that complex machines and software are easy to operate because of the lack of skilled workers. Another important trend is sustainability, both for the companies themselves and for their products. In addition, partnerships with other companies become increasingly important e.g. automation solutions for the entire sheet-metal process chain.

We will continue to drive forward the most important future trends in the world of sheet metal, such as digitalisation and automation. We are also working on further solutions that support users with the help of artificial intelligence to make their production even more efficient.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

MM: We see opportunities primarily in automation, software solutions and more and more data-driven remote and predictive services. These concepts can support users in difficult tasks and massively increase machine uptime, decreasing maintenance costs. TRUMPF has a strong focus in these areas.

I also see challenges in the increased cost levels and very high interest rates, which make investments more onerous for our customers. Fortunately, we can support them with our financial services.

ISMR: What is your approach to Industry 4.0 and enabling technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR)?

MM: TRUMPF is the leading supplier and user of digitally connected manufacturing solutions. We digitally connect not just our own factories but also our customers' factories. For example, our Oseon software controls production and automatically organises material transport. Manufacturing therefore flows well and productivity increases by around 20 per cent.

AI, VR and AR are also becoming increasingly important. With our Sorting Guide, for example, we offer a solution that sorts parts with the help of AI. These technologies are rapidly gaining more importance as they enable automated processes and can therefore partially address the challenges of finding qualified workers.

ISMR: Are there any products/technologies that you have released or are releasing this year that you would like to discuss?

MM: At this year's Blechexpo, we will be showing several new machines and technologies for automated bending. We will also present a punch-laser combination machine that users can seamlessly integrate into their smart factory.

This Autumn, we launched a welding robot that programs itself by using a smart sensor. A range of entry-level machines are also now available for our customers (in, for example, the bending field).

ISMR: How has TRUMPF embraced sustainability in its products, processes and operations?

MM: As a 100-year-old family business, sustainability is part of the DNA of our corporate culture. TRUMPF solutions have always been sustainable. The machines are particularly durable, can be easily repaired and come with a 15-year warranty for spare components (even if the machine is no longer produced).

On the other hand, we see developing sustainable technologies for our customers as a major lever for greater climate protection. In addition to energy savings, material savings also hold enormous potential for companies to improve their carbon footprint. TRUMPF solutions support customers in saving material and energy along the sheet-metal process chain. For example, we offer one of the most efficient algorithms on the market for nesting and a laser blanking system which processes material directly from coil. This saves material and increases climate protection.
