# EAGLE fibre laser technology at EuroBLECH 2018

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

Polish fibre laser specialist, EAGLE, introduced two world premieres at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Germany (October 2018) – a new 15kW fibre laser cutting system, with automated loading and unloading unit, as well as a new 3D printer.

It has taken great care in developing its lightweight eVa cutting head. It has developed and patented the eVa to handle ultra-high power, up to 15kW, in busy production environments. According to the Polish manufacturer, the dual-body design within the cutting head transfers the power, up to 15kW, without risk of damage. Its light weight enables high G manoeuvrability. It features rapid linear motor lift and drop. eVa also boasts few optics and a protective glass design to protect against contamination.

"Our new laser cutter, with a 15000W (15kW) fibre laser source, gives us even greater efficiency then before and enables better cutting quality at a lower cutting cost. This innovative machine was showcased at EuroBLECH with automation - a CraneMasterStore system, with loading and unloading unit combined with MonoTower automated storage. Our first 15kW fibre laser features 6G acceleration and 350m/min. rapid traverse speed. Our patented eVa cutting head also solves the problems of ultra-high-power laser cutting, delivering reliable operation at these power levels," the company told ISMR.

Ermaksan Makine, one of the leading manufacturers in the sheet metal processing sector, has set up 'Turkey's first private sector semi-conductor plant' and started exporting the machines it produces using a domestically developed national fibre laser beam source, national laser chip and controller systems.

"The domestically-produced laser beam sources have been put into operation as has the first national laser chip, developed by Optoelectronic AR-GE Centre, Ermaksan's R&D Centre established with its own engineering experience. The YGL-3000 fibre laser beam source is the first to be 100% produced in Turkey, by Turkish engineers," the manufacturer told ISMR.

HAWK is a fibre laser cutting machine that it has brought to market. It is Ermaksan's budget fibre laser cutting machine solution with a 3 x 1.5m bed size (without shuttle table). Its small footprint, rapid installation and easy-to-use interface make it suitable for job shops who cut thin material. At Blechexpo and FABTECH 2017, its flagship laser cutting system Fibermak, was also showcased with a new 4G optional upgrade. This offers 4G acceleration to users and is now available for the Fibermak Momentum series.

Polish innovator Eckert launched an award-winning innovative Piercing Control System (PCS) for its Diamond Fiber Laser 2 cutting machine, which substantially reduces piercing time from 10 seconds to two seconds, as well as cutting gas/energy costs.

Highyag/II-VI presented its product portfolio for high-power laser materials processing applications at EuroBLECH in Hanover (October 2018). II-VI's laser optics, including aspheres, variable radius mirrors and protective windows, were featured along with advanced 1μm laser processing heads and beam delivery solutions. Unveiled at EuroBLECH was a new laser cutting head, a new 2D remote welding head and a new laser welding head with wobbling function. II-VI's laser sources were also presented. The portfolio includes individual laser diode chips, laser bars, stacks and direct-diode laser engines.

The company's new fibre laser combiners enable fibre laser systems to scale to 10kW by combining the output of multiple kilowatt-class fibre lasers to achieve faster cutting or welding speeds, or to process thicker sheets of metal.

Its new high brightness laser diodes feature a 190μm wide output facet designed to achieve coupling efficiency into widely used 200μm core fibres. The increased output power per emitter enables fibre laser manufacturers to generate higher power levels with a reduced number of emitters. The company's BIMO-FSC laser cutting head is available in different versions for focus control.

Chinese manufacturer, Hymson Laser has established a new centre of operations in Italy, as it brings its fibre laser technology to Europe. As one of the largest manufacturers of laser cutting machines with intelligent technologies in China, its products include sheet metal laser cutting, tube laser cutting and 3D cutting systems.

At the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (October 2018), Hymson Laser showcased its new HFLU- series fibre laser cutting system with automated loading and unloading system. The machine featured a patented kinematic system (high dynamic movement and precision).

The cutting table has retractable clamps, controlled by CNC, for stable movement of the workpiece. The system is suitable for cutting reflective materials such as aluminium and copper and has been designed for lower energy consumption.

"Our major highlight at EuroBLECH was the new model LU series (loading system storage tower), all designed in Italy. This is a compact fibre laser cutting system, newly designed, for thin material (thickness of less than 10mm). We can offer 3-4kW power with this model. We invented the cutting head which can move rapidly, especially on thin material (400 holes per minute)," explained Vincent Yang of Hymson Laser.

Hymson Laser independently developed a new cutting process for its fibre laser cutting machines which solved the issue of burrs when cutting aluminium. Yang also showed Hymson's HP-D series fibre laser cutting system for tube, on its booth at EuroBLECH, featuring an automated loading and unloading system.

"Fibre laser cutting systems have several advantages over CO₂ laser systems as they require virtually no maintenance, are more energy-efficient and take up less space. The power supply is much smaller than that of a CO₂ laser, yet fibre delivery enables the beam to travel greater distances allowing for installation on larger tables," explained fibre laser machine manufacturer, Hypertherm, which produces the HyIntensity™ Fiber Laser.

Hypertherm's product line includes cutting systems, in addition to CNC motion and height controls, CAM nesting software, robotic software and consumables.

Polish manufacturer, Kimla, has innovation at the heart of its company, demonstrated in both its control systems and machine tool production. More recently, this was highlighted in its laser cutting machines with linear drive technology, which were showcased at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover. With more than 3,000 machines installed globally and employing over 300 people, Kimla is constantly developing new solutions to help increase productivity for its customers.

"Linear drives can double the speed of laser cutting machines; this is because mechanical drives have limitations due to the heat generated via friction and part wear. Linear drives, however, exhibit no friction and magnetic fields do not wear, resulting in a machine that can be two or even three times more efficient than standard drive systems," Kimla told ISMR.

"At EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover, we showcased our new Extremecut linear flatbed laser, enabling quick and efficient cutting of any shape out of sheet metal. In addition, we displayed our hybrid fibre laser system, which can cut sheet and tube (6m or 12m) and automatically unloads the tubes. Fibre lasers are three to four times cheaper and three times more efficient (in terms of cutting cost) than CO₂ lasers - this is especially true for thin sheet."

Kjellberg's XFocus 4000 is a compact system with a 4,000 Watt fibre laser source for cutting and marking thin sheets of mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium with common guiding systems that are suitable for plasma cutting. The laser system can be operated on the same guiding system with a plasma cutting unit, either in change mode or separately. The fibre laser XFocus series are complete all-in-one solutions for cutting and marking material of up to 20mm thickness.

Belgian sheet metal machinery specialist, LVD Company NV, showcased its Electra FL-3015 fibre-laser cutting system with new 10kW fibre laser at EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover (October 2018). It can process ferrous and non-ferrous materials (plate thicknesses of up to 30mm) with acceleration of 2G – or 20m/s². Electra uses a lightweight and rigid cast aluminium gantry, controlled by specially tuned servo drives. At EuroBLECH, the Electra-FL featured a 10-shelf Compact Tower (CT-L). The CT-L keeps pace with the Electra to load, unload and store parts and material.

"The Electra was built for speed – the rigidity of its frame means that you can maintain the rate of acceleration, even when working at really high feed rates with very high acceleration. We can even, by utilising the core power benefits of 10kW, maintain 2G acceleration whilst cutting. A part in 8mm material cut on a 10kW fibre increases productivity by 520% compared to a 6kW CO2 laser," Matt Fowles of LVD told ISMR at EuroBLECH.

EuroBLECH 2018 was also the European debut for LVD's TL 2665-FL tube laser cutting machine. It processes round, rectangular square or other standard tube profiles up to 7925mm long in a range of materials using a 2kW fibre laser. It is equipped with a standard seven-position, magazine-type automatic tube loader, automatic set-up and run, bow detection and twist compensation features which are designed to minimise downtime and increase tube cutting output.

LVD also produces the Phoenix and Lynx fibre laser cutting systems. The Phoenix FL 4020 and Phoenix FL 6020 laser cutting machines are designed to handle sheet dimensions of 4000 x 2000mm and 6000 x 2000 mm respectively. They are available in 3-, 4- and 6kW versions.

Mazak's OPTIPLEX 3015 Fiber offers stable cut performance and greater throughput for applications using stainless steel; copper; brass; bronze; aluminium; hastelloy; Inconel; titanium; thin-gauge mild steel and other exotic metals. There is no need for additional surface cleaning when painting mild steel cut with nitrogen.

Mazak showcased a 10kW version of its OPTIPLEX 3015 Fiber III at EuroBLECH 2018.

OPTIPLEX 3015 Fiber incorporates Mazak's Intelligent Technology to directly increase the productivity of the end-user by allowing the machine to optimise the torch set-up automatically per program. This can dramatically improve cut speeds, increase throughput and require less operator intervention, delivering more predictable processing. The combination of fibre beam diameter and focus control provides a wide range of improved cut performance in all material types and thicknesses. The eight-position nozzle changer facilitates unattended operation.

The OPTIPLEX 3015 Fiber also features the new Preview 3 control with a 15" touch screen and straightforward user interface to simplify machine operation. The graphical interface visualises the cutting path and automatically determines process conditions including the required lens, nozzle, feed rate and laser output for different materials and thicknesses. The OPTIPLEX Fiber III series machines are available in 2.0kW, 4.0kW, 6.0kW, 8kW and 10kW configurations.

Many new technologies and solutions from Italian manufacturer, Prima Power, had their world premiere at EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover (October 2018). Many innovations were showcased by the Italian manufacturer, including solutions from The Laser line. New products exhibited for the first time at a trade show included its new 3D fibre laser system Laser Next 2141, with its wide working envelope and different configurations, and the 2D fibre laser cutting machine, Laser Genius 1530, with automatic stacking robot LST and Combo-Tower storage. Its Combi-Genius 1530 punching/fibre laser cutting system was showcased with a new solution which simplifies the installation and maintenance of the machine, as well as with the Compact Express loading/unloading system. All of these technologies are supported by Prima Power's smart software family which connects machines, monitors the entire production flow and generates automatic processes from order to delivery. All products are 'Industry 4.0 Inside', the manufacturer told ISMR.

Other fibre laser cutting systems from Prima Power include the Platino® Fiber laser platform, with Laser Piercing Monitor to give real-time feedback and check when piercing is completed, and Prima Power Fiber Plasma Monitoring (detecting problems with the cutting and formation of plasma when cutting is lost). At EuroBLECH 2018, innovations were presented in the L5, the high-dynamic laser now managed by a new trajectory planner. L5 was connected to LTWS, the new tower store, equipped with a new sorting device.

Salvagnini's automatic FlexCell ZERO cell is a smart and flexible FMC (Flexible-Manufacturing Centre) which integrates three smart machines with OPS-FlexCell integration software. The Flexcell on display at EuroBLECH featured an integrated L5 laser cutting system, a P2lean panel bender and a B3 ATA press brake. Salvagnini's L5 high-dynamic fibrelaser system cuts across a large range of thicknesses using its compass, a mechanical drive system and the single-optic cutting head with dry cooling feature.

TRUMPF's fibre-laser cutting machine, the TruLaser 2030 fiber, is equipped with up to four kilowatts of laser power. It is also capable of fully automatic manufacturing. "Just 7.8 x 5.9m of installation space is required for the TruLaser 2030 fiber, including pallet changer. Important components such as the switch cabinet, suction system and laser are built into the machine frame. The 3m working area is easily accessible along its whole length and is designed for large formats. Large windows and LED illumination provide optimum views inside the machine," the German manufacturer told ISMR.

"TruLaser 2030 fiber is available with 2-, 3- or 4- kilowatts of laser power. With its TruDisk solid-state laser, the TruLaser 2030 fiber can cut highly reflective materials such as copper, even using nitrogen. All materials and sheet thicknesses can be processed with the same cutting unit. This saves set-up time, as there is no need to change the lens and cutting head. Along with other parameters, the focal position of the laser also adjusts automatically to the respective material. The cutting head's collision protection and an automatic nozzle changer with space for eight nozzles reduce set-up times even further."

To increase the capacity utilisation of the laser and to reduce the investment costs, the machine can also be operated in a laser network. To this end, the TruDisk laser has an additional output as an option. In this way, the laser can supply an additional machine or welding cell as well as the TruLaser 2030 fiber. This reduces procurement costs for the second laser and facilitates entry into new technologies such as laser welding or laser tube cutting.

"The TruLaser 5030 fiber, TruLaser 5040 fiber and TruLaser 5060 fiber offer high processing speeds and reproducible, high part quality – even for complex contours. Excellent feed rates are possible thanks to the solid-state laser. Intelligent assistant systems make the complete machining process more productive and reliable than ever. Operator involvement is low; non-productive times are minimal," explained TRUMPF.

The fibre laser-based TruMark Series 5000 offers high power, high frequencies and adjustable pulse duration. The marking laser is designed for demanding applications with high power requirements and short cycle times. The TruMicro Mark 2000 is an all-around turnkey solution for an ultrashort pulse laser in a marking station.

"The fibre-based ultrashort pulse lasers in the TruMicro Series 2000 are distinguished by their compact and light design. Its moderate average power and high beam quality are designed to open up applications in new areas such as medical technology and foil processing," explained the German specialist.

TRUMPF's TruFiber with 500W or 1kW laser power is a tool for fine cutting and welding work with basic-mode beam quality.

"The fibre offers high process speeds, slim welding seams and narrow kerfs. The excellent beam quality is also beneficial for wobble welding on difficult materials," added TRUMPF.
