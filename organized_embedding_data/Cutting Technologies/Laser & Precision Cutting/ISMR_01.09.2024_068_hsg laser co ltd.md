# HSG Laser Co. Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.hsglaser.com

HSG Laser is a high-tech enterprise dedicated to providing intelligent laser equipment solutions to customers all over the world.

"We focus on laser plate cutting, pipe cutting, welding and automated production lines with the concept of efficient, intelligent, environmental and compatible product development. We will showcase heavy tube laser cutting models and a 30kW super-fast sheet metal laser cutting system on our booth at EuroBLECH in Hanover," confirmed the manufacturer.

Its G3015V 30KW laser cutter is a highly dynamic, high-load laser cutting system with a double-rail structure to assist stable processing. It features, said HSG Laser, a special machine bed for high power and offers eco-friendly processing.

The manufacturer set up an overseas manufacturing base in Thailand earlier this year for anticipated annual production capacity of around 2,000 laser cutting machines. The company has now established five production bases worldwide, with a total annual production reported capacity of 10,000 machines.
