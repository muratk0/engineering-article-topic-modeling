# FOCUS ON WATERJETS

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

With 41 years of development experience, Eckelmann AG offers all-in-one solutions for plasma, oxyfuel, laser and waterjet cutting. At EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, its focus was on E°CUT, its user-friendly E°CUT HMI and new CAD/CAM functionality: visitors could test the interface on a 3D animation. Systems automated with E°CUT cut both thin sheet material and large sheets, for sectors such as the construction industry or shipbuilding.

Eckert AS Sp. z o.o. is a Polish manufacturer of CNC cutting machines, working across three technologies (plasma, oxyfuel and waterjet cutting) or combined technologies. Its Opal Waterjet cutting system features a wide range of equipment options, including a powerful ProX 3D cutting head. It is suitable for most materials up to 250mm in thickness.

Its OpalWaterjet Combo is a patented, rapid CNC waterjet for plasma and waterjet cutting. Equipped with the Pro-X 3D waterjetcutting head, it combines the advantages of ultra-fast plasma cutting and precision waterjet cutting.

"Our Opal Waterjet Combo is, we believe, the world's fastest waterjet, through a unique combination of waterjet and plasma technology. Optionally configurable with 2D and 3D cutting heads, depending upon the application, it cuts materials of up to 250mm thick," Eckert explained.

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products offers its Hydrocut™HDX large gantry waterjetcutting system which combines waterjet with other cutting and marking processes such as plasma cutting; plasma marking; oxy-fuel cutting or plate lettering or marking tools. The Hydrocut HDX can be equipped with up to four waterjet cutting heads, two oxy-fuel stations and up to two plasma stations, including ESAB's DMX Plasma Beveller. Floor mounted crane rails and a rigid gantry are combined with digital AC drive amplifiers, brushless AC motors and precision gearboxes.

At this October's EuroBLECH exhibition, ESAB's cutting software specialists provided demonstrations of Columbus™ version 2.4, a powerful CAD/CAM programming and nesting software optimised for plasma, oxy-fuel, laser and waterjet cutting. This latest version of Columbus offers various new features and updates designed to make programming easier and more efficient, improve material utilisation, increase productivity and streamline workflow.

Flexicon's JET-FEED™ garnet delivery system automatically feeds garnet directly from bulk bags to waterjet cutters.

With decades of experience and over 13,000 systems installed, Flow International Corporation is a global specialist in the development, engineering and marketing of ultra high-pressure pumps and complete 2D and 3D waterjet cutting systems. 2024 marks a significant milestone for Flow Waterjet as it celebrates 50 years of innovation in waterjet technology.

At this year's IMTS exhibition in the USA, Flow announced the debut of its Mach 150 waterjet cutting system, designed for entrylevel users. Its Mach 200c system was also on display, featuring an innovative canted bridge design for full work envelope usage, distinguishing it from standard open-gantry systems.

Its flagship Mach 500 was also showcased with live cutting demonstrations, as was its EchoJet, a fully enclosed waterjet solution (with five-axis capabilities) engineered to boost operator efficiency while protecting the environment from water and abrasive. Its compact design allows it to fit in smaller spaces, where a smaller footprint is required.

The NanoJet is a small-format waterjet machining centre and fully enclosed, all-in-one solution. Flow's Motojet X intensifier pump technology includes its Hyplex Prime pump, its next generation of energy-efficient, direct drive, ultra high-pressure waterjet pumps.

H.G. RIDDER Automatisierungs GmbH (founded in 1980) has produced the 2D and 3D WARICUT waterjet cutting machines, in a wide variety of designs (including the WARICUT micro-max water jet micro cutting) since 1991. It also produces energy-efficient, high-pressure pumps. Its WARICUT micromax is a micro waterjet cutting machine in both 2D and 3D versions.

IGEMS (Sweden) offers products such as CAD/CAM & Nest software for waterjet cutting in both 2D and 3D (ranging from one-axis cutting to six-axis bevel cutting on a rotation axis), as well as a modern CNC for waterjets (together with Beckhoff). Its Tilter is a versatile 5x kinematic mechanism because it can handle 45° bevels and taper angle compensation. The IGEMS Rounder measures, calculates and compensates for deviation in five-axis waterjet cutting, calibrating to the cutting head.

“The IGEMS CNC Snapper takes detailed and accurate photos of the cutting table. The image provides the basis for optimal positioning in the NC program, saving time and minimizing material use. The IGEMS Straighter is a handy and advanced tool that clips onto the machine's mixing tube. It's a matter of seconds until the five-axis kinematic perpendicular angle is as straight as an arrow," it explained.

Intermac (part of the Biesse Group) offers its Primus range of waterjet-cutting systems. The five-axis cutting head is fitted with the patented Intermac infinite rotating C-axis system for making tilted cuts (+/-60°) on complex profiles, with no limits. This system, says Intermac, eliminates the errors due to rotating axis reverse clearance in traditional methods, and also guarantees a constant supply of abrasive material.

Jet Edge, Inc. manufactures complete systems for precision waterjet cutting, mobile waterjet cutting and UHP cleaning and surface preparation applications, as well as accessories. Models include the Edge-X-3 mid-rail gantry waterjet, the Edge X-5 five-axis, mid-rail waterjet and the Edge Dual Carriage Mid Rail WaterJet with Aquavision DI Controller. Its JetStart series waterjets feature HERO 50 intensifier pumps. Its Edge X3-HR (three-axis High Rail WaterJet) and Edge X5- HR (five-axis High Rail WaterJet) also feature the Aquavision DI Controller. It also offers a wide range of waterjet pumps.

Jet Star International Ltd. is a European supplier of waterjet products such as garnet abrasive and spare parts designed for all types of waterjet machines.

Kerf Developments offers its Optima range of waterjet cutting machines. The machines come with single or multiple cutting heads that feature automatic height adjustment, including anti-collision systems. A range of optional items include dual cutting heads using a spacer bar, dual independent heads, a rotary tilting head and GRS abrasive removal systems.

Kimtech AB offers three XY (small medium and large) waterjet machines that cover most areas of application, as well as fully customised machines. The Swedish manufacturer has delivered over 300 waterjet cutting machines since start of production over 40 years ago. Its XY range of machines (small, medium and large) features abrasive waterjet cutting and come with a range of accessories. Its new control system is designed to optimise operation, monitoring, remote support and updating options.

KMT Waterjet Systems celebrated its 50th anniversary in 2021. It specialises in the design and manufacture of waterjet cutting pumps and technology, and specifically ultrahigh-pressure ('UHP') pump technology with water pressurised up to 90,000 PSI/6.200 bar. KMT pumps range from 90,000 psi to 55,000 psi, with installations available in over 100 countries.

Its lines of waterjet intensifier pumps, pure water only or abrasive waterjet cutting nozzles, abrasive management systems/ hoppers, meters and waterjet parts/ components are integrated with OEM waterjet machines. With its STREAMLINE models, the company incorporates IoT technology into its portfolio.

The KMT Waterjet Cut Calculator 2.0 App, with an on-demand webinar, incorporates 25 materials and offers a wider range of substrate thicknesses, horsepower, orifice sizes and abrasive rates. It is available at Google Play and the Apple Store for free.

Libellula offers CAD/CAM systems for laser, plasma, oxy-fuel and waterjet cutting machines. With Libellula.CUT, operators can program waterjet machines, saving material and reducing time.

Metallbau Müller GmbH (MBM) has developed a combination of abrasive sludge suction and cutting water treatment, which is also suitable for smaller waterjet cutters and complies, it told /SMR, with European wastewater introduction regulations. Its MBM MEWAB is a combination sludge extraction system for waterjet cutting systems with a new filter unit. It has been developed primarily for small cutting systems with an abrasive consumption of up to 500g/minute.

MicroStep, a manufacturer of CNC-controlled cutting systems, celebrated its thirty-year anniversary in 2021. The company develops and produces plasma, oxy-fuel, laser and waterjet cutting systems. Its waterjet cutters include the AquaCut and WaterCut series. It also offers tested automation solutions, from material handling around the cutting system to the picking and sorting of cut parts.

At EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, it presented its new WaterCut waterjet cutting system, equipped with a 3D rotator, enabling precise bevel cuts of up to 45° on a variety of materials. It also displayed a newly developed 2D cutting head with ABC technology, enabling cone compensation of ±5°.

Muototerä is a family-owned Finnish company, specialising in waterjet cutting technology through its FinJet and TamCut brands. The FinJet R series covers all robotic integrated waterjet cutting systems. The cutting system has two main work principles: either the robot has an integrated waterjet-cutting nozzle or the part is treated under a static waterjet. TamCut is a compact waterjet-cutting machine that can cut most materials. The company also supplies high-pressure pumps, garnet abrasive and spare parts.

OMAX® Corporation is a manufacturer of rapid multi-axis abrasive waterjet systems, cutting 2D and 3D parts from a range of materials and thicknesses. OMAX has already installed over 7,000 waterjet cutting systems worldwide and uses the experience gained to continuously improve. Owner of the OMAX, OptiMAX, MAXIEM®, GlobalMAX®, MicroMAX and ProtoMAX® brands, the company designs waterjet systems that feature intuitive software, efficient direct drive pump technology and a wide range of accessories. In April 2019, OMAX merged with Hypertherm.

In March 2022, Hypertherm's OMAX Corporation introduced the OptiMAX, a new generation of waterjet designed to quickly turn prints into parts while relying less on highly experienced operators. The ProtoMAX is a compact, self-contained cutting system suitable for prototyping and low-volume cutting. OMAX also offers highly intuitive IntelliMAX software.

OMAX direct drive pumps are designed to be compact, quiet, efficient, easy to maintain and inexpensive to operate. They include the EnduroMAX direct drive pump, MAXIEM (for the MAXIEM line of waterjets) and GlobalMAX. The DynaMAX intensifier pump is suitable for water-only (or pure waterjet) applications, multi-cutting head systems and etching.

In May 2024, Hypertherm announced a major version update to ProNest® 2025 version 16.0 nesting software, which includes synchronised features for IntelliMAX software.

Perndorfer Maschinenbau KG produces waterjet cutting systems to customer requirements, as well as special machines and its WJC range. Another mainstay is mechanical contract manufacturing. Everything is manufactured and produced in Austria at its Kallham site.

At EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover this October, it highlighted its new product, the HTRS H2O Re-use. This is a resource-saving cutting water circulation system, with an absolute separation rate of 0.02μm, designed for the safety of high-pressure pumps and components.

Projet System is a supplier of pure waterjet spare parts; abrasive waterjet spare parts; high-pressure pumps; coils; high-pressure tubing and high-pressure coupling.

Rainford Precision's Finepart microabrasive WMC500 II waterjet system is manufactured in Sweden and features a micro-fine jet of just 0.2mm. This, said the manufacturer, provides a positional accuracy of +/-2.5 microns, a repeatability of +/- 2 microns and an adjustable jet pressure from 500 to 4000 bar.

Resato International's product range includes high-pressure technology waterjet-cutting systems as well as components and systems for testing, injection and control that operate up to 14,000 bars. Its modular waterjet cutting solutions include the ACM-2015 waterjet cutting machine. This machine is equipped with a three- or five-axis cutting head and is powered by a waterjet intensifier pump (type PJ4-4000).

The Resato Job manager, Resato EMS monitoring software and the Job Manager module ensure that multiple cutting jobs can be programmed. EMS monitoring software monitors the cutting process 24/7. Products also include a double intensifier pump and the abrasive continuous supply mini.

StM Waterjet Group is a provider of waterjet cutting systems with offices in Eben, Austria; Schweinfurt in Germany and Reiden in Switzerland. Each of its systems can be used for pure water, abrasive or combined cutting processes so that all machines can cut a wide variety of materials without conversion. Parallel cutting (2D) with up to twelve cutting heads is also possible. Its systems are manufactured in series, pre-installed, tested and usually delivered in container format.

“For almost 50 years, we have been developing future-oriented production solutions at our company headquarters in Eben im Pongau, Austria," it told /SMR.

Its waterjet cutting systems include the STM MicroCut 4.0, STM EcoCut, STM MasterCut, ST PremiumCut; ST PremiumCut IFC and STM Cube. With STM OneClean modules, water and abrasive can not only be separated from each other, but also largely cleaned and reused for the cutting process. STM BasicClean is designed for abrasive rinsing and offers a fully automatic and space-saving system with a large bag for collecting the settled abrasive sand.

Swift-Cut Automation offers the Swift-Jet Pro as its waterjet cutting solution. The underwater cutting system (adjustable water level) means less noise and less mess. It also features high-/low- pressure control for piercing brittle material. It comes with a touchscreen HMI with optical mouse control.

TCI Cutting, a Spanish manufacturer of laser and waterjet cutting machines and intelligent automation systems, also develops intelligent production management software for autonomous and strategic decision-making (TCI Manager). It offers a wide range of waterjet cutting systems. Its BP-H COMBI cutting machine integrates, in a single machine, waterjet cutting technology and HD plasma cutting. This machine incorporates the 5x Direct Drive 4.0 Infinite Loop® five-axis cutting head, which allows infinite rotation without rewinding in three-dimensional cuts, a height regulator, an anti-collision system and automatic sludge extraction, among others.

TECHNIWaterjet manufactures waterjet-cutting solutions for the metals, composites, glass and stone industries. Now part of the GMM Group, it controls the entire waterjet process in-house, with its own developed electric servo-driven pump (Quantum® Series Water Jet Pump) and five-axis cutting head. It holds several patents on its technologies.

"Our new Intec-G2® waterjet machine cuts parts of up to 12" thick in most materials. Simple to use, its versatile cutting allows users to cut multiple material types on the same bed without having to change tooling," it explained. “Our Quantum NXTTM Series pump incorporates technology from the NASA space shuttle programme, making it 60% more efficient than standard hydraulic intensifiers."

The Intec™ MG2 Abrasive Waterjet Series range includes the i35-G2; i510-G2; i713-G2; i815-G2; i1020-G2 and i1033-G2 machines. A remote-control pendant (MPG) allows users to manually wind forwards or backwards through a cutting path. Its PAC60™ five-axis cutting head is designed to produce parts with a true angle up to +/-60 degrees.

TeenKing CNC Machinery Co. Limited offers five-axis dynamic and 3D waterjet cutting machines, as well as a six-axis robotic abrasive/pure waterjet cutting system.

## Uhde High Pressure Technologies

GmbH (belonging to thyssenkrupp) has been designing and manufacturing ultra-highpressure pumps for pressures of up to 14,000 bar for industrial use for almost half a century. Its high-pressure pumps, the core element of the waterjet cutting system, with stepless working pressures of up to 6,000 bar are implemented in many industrial sectors, from the food industry to aerospace.

Water Jet Sweden, based in Ronneby (southern Sweden), is a well-known waterjetcutting specialist with over 100 different waterjet cutters from entry-level 2D machines and micro processing to large machines for large and odd sizes of material, with its fiveyear performance warranty. It also produces systems for highly bespoke waterjetcutting optimised to specific requirements. It celebrated its 30th anniversary last year.

When a cutting tool is to be changed, careful calibration is required to ensure that precision is maintained. With Water Jet Sweden's new Auto TCP Calibration Tool, calibration can be performed automatically. It consists of special software that plugs into the control system, a measuring probe with a base plate and a calibration ball. The product is used for all types of angled cutting i.e. machines that are equipped with Alphajet, Beveljet or FiveX cutting heads. It is designed to improve operational performance for perpendicular cutting, bevel cutting and freeform 3D cutting.

The FiveX Ultra is its full 3D, five-axis cutting machine designed to manage precision cutting assignment. It can be equipped with a FAWJ Micro Cutting Tool for extremely fine cuts and complex shapes.

WJS's Alphajet, which it dubs 'the next generation of taper angle control and perpendicular waterjet cutting', is mechatronics based on compact design with a fixed TCP point. Up to four Alphajets can be fitted on the same machine, quadrupling the cutting capacity. Its multi-head, CNCcontrolled Premium cutting system is for 2D and 3D cutting.

Users can cut delicate, precise parts in metals, ceramics and other high-tech materials with WJS Fine Abrasive Waterjet technology (0.2mm cuts at ±0.2μaccuracy). The company's entry-level waterjet cutting systems include the T-model and H-model. Its Grand range is a heavy-duty waterjet cutting system with large table sizes to manage large and odd-sized materials.

On 1 June 2024, Water Jet Sweden took over service responsibility for Bystronic's waterjet customers including service, customer support and spare parts management in all Nordic and Baltic countries.

Italian manufacturer, Waterjet Corporation Srl, offers a range of new hybrid and electric servo intensifier pumps (such as EDRIVE, EDRIVE PRO and SERVODRIVE). Its EDGE 5 cutting system features integrated ITC. Its machines include cantilever and bridge designs and five-axis flying bridge technology which deliver significant productivity improvements. The company also offers a combination plasma and waterjet machine (the PlasmaJet model), as well as waterjet surface texturing technology.

Its waterjet cutting systems include the three-axis Prima and Practica; the 3-5-axis RAM and Waterline as well as the Classica and Suprema. Its TopJet is an automatic waterjet cutting line.

WAZER offers a 'desktop waterjet' at 864mm x 635mm x 534mm. The cutting area is 305mm x 460mm.

In June 2024, it launched WAZER Pro, its most powerful and productive waterjet cutting system. It cuts material two to four times faster than WAZER Desktop.■
