# Kjellberg Finsterwalde

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.kjellberg.de

Kjellberg offers products and technologies for plasma and laser cutting, welding and customised special-purpose machines. For over 60 years, it has been a market specialist in plasma cutting. Its extensive range of cutting technologies are designed to deliver strong cutting and marking results (e.g. Q-Hole technology for precise holes in mild steel with a diameter-to-material-thickness ratio of 0.75:1.).

At EuroBLECH, Kjellberg will showcase its Q-Series (intelligent plasma cutting for digitised production). The Kjellberg-developed, browser-based HMI Q-Desk provides real-time process data and information from all system components on standard devices (regardless of location). Users can transfer and process all data via MQTT protocol using the Ethernet interface.

“The Q-series plasma cutting system combines precise, high-level plasma cutting and marking. Thanks to the modular design, performance of the Q 1500 plus and the Q 3000 plus can be further upgraded and customised to requirements," it outlined.

## MicroStep Europa GmbH

https://microstep.com

MicroStep is a leading manufacturer of CNCcontrolled cutting systems. The company develops and produces plasma, oxyfuel, laser and waterjet cutting systems. This ranges from small CNC machines for schools and workshops to customised production lines for shipyards, steel centres or the automotive and aerospace industries. It also offers automation solutions (from material handling around the cutting system to the commissioning and sorting of cut parts).

“Our systems include 2D and 3D cutting systems with plasma technology or oxyfuel-cutting technology; from simple 2D cutting systems to automated and multi-functional cutting systems," it told ISMR. "Our 3D cutting systems also feature laser technology e.g. a flexible 3D laser system with automatic material handling and additional technologies for drilling, scanning or marking. We also offer 2D and 3D waterjet-cutting systems.

"We aim to offer the right solution for every cutting task in sheet metal, tube, profile or dome," it added.
