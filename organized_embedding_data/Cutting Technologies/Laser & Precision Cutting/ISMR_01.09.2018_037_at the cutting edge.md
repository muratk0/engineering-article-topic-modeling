# At the cutting edge

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, A MANUFACTURER of industrial cutting systems and software, will be exhibiting at the EuroBLECH 2018 exhibition (23-26 October 2018) in Hanover, Germany. The company will introduce a new member to its X-Definition™ plasma family and a new HyPrecision™ Waterjet pump, with predictive maintenance capabilities, in Hall 13, Stand E108.

"Our X-Definition plasma is the latest form of plasma technology, building on high-definition plasma for cutting mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium. The result is squarer-cut edges, markedly less angularity and excellent surface finish on non-ferrous metals such as aluminium and stainless steel. At this year's EuroBLECH, visitors will meet the latest member of the XPR® plasma product family and see the cutting results," the company told ISMR.

A new HyPrecision Waterjet pump will be introduced at EuroBLECH 2018. One of the innovations in this pump is seal maintenance technology designed to ensure that the system continuously performs at peak level.

"This year is a special one for us as we celebrate our 50th anniversary. We are very proud of everything that we have achieved over the last five decades. We have grown from a local manufacturer of plasma systems to a global provider of cutting solutions and are focused on building longterm relationships with our customers, founded on technology and service leadership. 'Shaping Possibility' is our company's vision that we will further build on together with our customers, partners and associates," said Marco Mostert, Regional Director, Hypertherm EMEA.

The company will also feature its Powermax® line of light industrial plasma cutters; CAM nesting software line; robotic software; automation products and torch and consumable technologies at the exhibition.

EUROTECH WILL BE showcasing a couple of new vacuum lifting devices at this year's EuroBLECH in Hanover. Visitors to booth A02 in Hall 15 will be able to view and try the new eT-Hover-univac-line and a new eT-Hover-modesty.

"The new eT-Hover-univac-line vacuum lifting device gets its name from a linear arrangement of suction plates. It is equipped with four BSP 400 RL suction cups and transports flat, vacuum-tight panels made from sheet metal, glass, wood or plastic horizontally as well as vertically. In vertical mode, it can lift panels with a weight of up to 360kg; in horizontal mode, up to 500kg. The manual turning function can turn loads continuously in either direction. The lifting device can be pivoted by up to 90°. A wireless remote-control unit allows the user to adjust all main functions. With its two-circle system, the eT-Hover-univac-line is designed for use on construction sites. This makes it a suitable addition to the univac series," explained EuroTECH.

Stand visitors will also discover a lifting device from the eT-Hover-loop series - these are used for turning flat loads weighing up to 500kg. The exhibit on show at EuroBLECH can turn loads by up to 180°. Using a linear actuator, the device turns flat, vacuum-tight panels made of sheet metal, glass, wood or plastic with dimensions of between 3.5 x 2.0 metres and 14.0 x 2.0 metres.

The third lifting device on display will be the eT-Hover-modesty series. These lifting devices are used for lifting vacuum-tight, flat loads such as sheet metal, glass panes and plastic or wooden boards weighing up to 150kg. They have a simple design and are used as cost-saving alternatives to similar, more extensively equipped devices. The device can be turned and pivoted manually or, optionally, turned pneumatically. This makes it suitable for loading and removing items from warehouses or processing machines.

"Several safety and alarm systems ensure the necessary level of safety to prevent faults and risks in all Eurotech lifting devices, as prescribed by DIN EN 13 155," added the manufacturer. "We will also be presenting our range of vacuum components, including everything from suction plates to flat suction cups and bellow suction cups," concluded EuroTECH.
