# MAKING THE CUT

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

## ISMIR SAYS: “The cutting equipment had to meet seven stringent requirements from DELA.“

Already in its second generation, with expertise accumulated over five decades, German family-owned manufacturer, DELA GmbH & Co. KG, has been successfully competing in the production of flame-cut blanks and flanges for many years. A cleanly produced flame cut is a prerequisite for accurate processing without wasting resources, whether for steel construction or for turned and milled parts, and this also considerably enhances the productivity of the tools used in processing.

"We can cut all common flanges according to DIN, ANSI, EN 1092-1, special flanges, shaped cuts and flame cuts according to customer drawings. We specialise in the manufacture and processing of steel products, according to customer requirements. Our plasma cutting machines are able to flame cut to very tight tolerances, creating the optimal basis for mechanical processing," it explained. Offering a combination of laser-, plasma- and oxyfuel cutting services, DELA also offers mechanical machining and surface treatment processes (such as deburring, grinding and hot-dip galvanizing) for small or large batches. It also uses modern test facilities and has created its own quality management system.

## Investing for success

Customer satisfaction has always been at the forefront for DELA and it prides itself on upholding high-quality standards and deadlines for its domestic and international customers. To enable the manufacture of both reproducible series and individual items in reliable processes, Stefan Debus (owner and CEO of DELA) decided to invest in the future of the company and meet growing requirements for more modern plasma cutting technologies.

His focus was on the following attributes: reliability and precision; user-friendliness; reliable processes; use of the latest technologies in CNC plasma and autogenous cutting systems; high-end CAD/CAM and nesting software; automated vibratory conveyor tables that convey slag and components produced during cutting operations to the end of the table; high-efficiency extraction systems and Industry-4.0-enabled technologies.

The solution for DELA was supplied by Eckert Cutting Technology GmbH, with over 30 years of experience in all cutting technologies. The decision was taken in March 2020, enabling the starting gun to be fired as early as October 2020. Systems supplied by Eckert are equipped with high-quality components from well-known manufacturers and are individually tailored and delivered to customers' specific requirements. Two out of a total of five new systems are already in operation.

## Precision and speed

DELA Flanschen was the first to start production with two new Sapphire plasma-cutting systems from Eckert, each with a 4,000mm x 12,000mm working area. They were equipped for parallel cutting with one HD 3000 and one Vortex 3D FL plasma torch support per system and four Q-series plasma power sources from longstanding German plasma specialist, Kjellberg.

Following the successful commissioning of all five Eckert systems - two plasma and three autogenous - all systems at DELA Flanschen will be integrated into a common Industry 4.0 infrastructure using Eckert ConnECKt 4.0. This is designed to ensure that there are no limits on system availability or production efficiency. "Our latest Q plasma cutting system series brought strong added value; it is designed for reliable and precise cutting quality with digital production. From now on, DELA will be using the Industry 4.0-enabled Q 3000 inverter power source for 2D cutting and the Q 3000 plus for 3D contours. Since DELA only cuts structural steel, the Q-Gas O2 gas box and the Q-Torch 4510 plasma torch are the perfect combination for these special customer requirements," Kjellberg told ISMR.

"Our Contour Cut and Contour Cut Speed technologies were also important for the required quality cuts at high cutting speeds - as was the expectation of an excellent marking result, which it was also possible to meet with our Q-Mark technology," it added.

"Since the commissioning of the first two systems, we have been absolutely convinced of the correct decision to cooperate with Eckert, as well as Kjellberg (not least because our own high-quality standards for our products have been surpassed and even taken to a new level). We're thrilled with the results and are looking forward to the next three systems, which are already in the starting blocks," confirmed Stefan Debus, owner of DELA GmbH & Co. KG.
