Laser cutting machine also performs drilling and tapping

Tecoi’s new LS series laser and drilling machine incorporates the choice of either one or two drilling heads, maximizing its large-dimension laser processing cutting station capability for steel service centers, manufacturers, and fabricators.

Each drill head includes up to six tools, with tool change in as fast as 0.8 second. Options include Twin-Disk double-head cutting technology, a double-core fiber system for thick and thin plate, and automatic beveling.

With Driltec Plus, the machine combines laser cutting of areas from 10 by 20 ft. to 20 by 230 ft. with drilling and tapping of smaller holes to reduce the need for material handling. Users can drill holes up to 7/8 in. and tap holes up to 5/8 in.

The machine has a fully automated mobile system design coordinated with the movement of the machine along its entire length. It also features an automatic production control system that enables operators to program tasks along the entire cutting area.

Hypertherm Associates has introduced the Powermax45 Sync plasma cutter, which offers high cutting power for gouging, cutting, and marking. It uses a patented, single-piece Powermax cartridge that helps simplify operation and eliminate operator error while offering increased consumable life.

Features include advanced torch communication for automated setup, an optional CNC interface and FastConnect torch connection that simplify setup for mechanized use, and a variety of specialty cartridges and torches to expand application capabilities.

Caster reduces push/pull forces by up to 50%

Caster Concepts Inc. has introduced the TWERGO Xtreme caster for heavy-duty material handling demands in the AGV and aerospace sectors. According to the manufacturer, when compared to traditional products used in these applications, the caster reduces push/pull forces by up to 50%.

Compact swing chamber shot blast machine features robotic workpiece handling

Rösler offers the compact RWK 6/12- 2 swing chamber shot blast machine. Equipped with automated workpiece handling, it can be integrated into linked manufacturing lines. The furnished digitization software package helps minimize overall personnel requirements.

The machine can be used for all kinds of shot blast applications like surface cleaning, desanding and descaling, surface homogenization, and shot peening. Workpieces treated in this machine include castings and forgings, machined components, welded sheet metal parts, and technical springs.

In its standard version, the unit can handle workpieces with diameters of up to 600 mm and lengths up to 1,200 mm. The dual-chamber design allows workpiece loading/unloading in one chamber while the shot blast process takes place in the second chamber.
