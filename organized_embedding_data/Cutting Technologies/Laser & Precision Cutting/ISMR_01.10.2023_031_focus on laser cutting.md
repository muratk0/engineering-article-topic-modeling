# FOCUS ON LASER CUTTING

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

## An eye on precision

"We process all conceivable materials in all thicknesses and quantities for our very broad clientele. With the new 20kW fibre laser cutting machine, we were able to double our performance portfolio from the previous 25mm sheet thicknesses. Because we can also operate the fibre laser with the additionally installed mixed gas system if required, we can offer our customers even more performance diversity in the thin and thick sheet range. They greatly appreciate this and now place orders with us that up to now could not be handled economically by any supplier," confirmed Alexandre Herren, commenting on the new purchase.

"On the other hand, using our 20kW fibre laser, sheet-metal parts are precision-cut (in terms of quality) with very high system performance which often makes time-consuming finishing work, such as deburring edges or grinding surfaces, unnecessary. Finally, with automated loading and unloading, we can now process orders fully automatically (in most cases), while significantly reducing parts handling and the associated personnel costs," he continued.

Alexandre Herren also confirmed that, because of its latest acquisition, the company now also saves between 50 and 70 per cent in energy costs and consumables (compared to using CO₂ laser systems).

"After one year in production, we can confidently state that with the new laser cutting machine we once again have a unique selling point in western Switzerland. We are very proud of this; it provides our customers with real added value," concluded Alexandre Herren.■
