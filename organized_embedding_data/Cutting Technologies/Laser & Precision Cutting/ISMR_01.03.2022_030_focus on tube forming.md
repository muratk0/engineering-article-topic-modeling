Prada Nargesa SL, from Spain, has over 50 years of experience in manufacturing industrial machinery. Its portfolio includes hydraulic press brakes; shears; non-mandrel tube benders; hydraulic punching machines; horizontal presses and bar twisting machines. It also offers technical support to carry out operations such as cutting, punching and folding metal sheet, tube and profile bending.

Preco's RT1000 is designed for precision laser cutting small diameter tubes for medical devices such as surgical instruments, stents and other implantable devices. It incorporates a granite base, precision servo drives, rigid work holding, fibre-delivered laser sources and Preco's fine cutting head.

PLATINO® is the general purpose 2D laser cutting machine by Prima Power which cuts a broad range of materials and thicknesses. Change-over to tube processing can be achieved with zero set-up time, said the Italian manufacturer. It is available in two sizes (1530 and 2040), and with laser powers ranging from 2500W to 5000W.

Platino Fiber Evo is now available with high brilliance, energy-efficient fibre lasers with 2-, 3-, 4- or 6kW power. The new fibre laser cutting head with adaptive collimator, designed and manufactured by Prima Power, is equipped with a single focus lens.

SAY-MAK MAKINE IMALAT VE DIS TIC. LTD. STI. in Turkey offers sheet metal working machines; direct-gear motorised guillotines; hydraulic and motorised plate bending machines; hydraulic and motorised section and pipe bending machines; hydraulic workshop presses; hand-lever guillotines; manual folding machines and more.

SigmaTEK Systems is a leading global provider of end-to-end CAD/CAM software solutions.

"Our product suite covers the full range of fabrication workflow; from quoting to shipping, including robust integration with a wide range of business systems and the ability to program all major laser, waterjet, oxyfuel, plasma, punch, router, knife, press brake and tube machines," it said.

Swiss manufacturer Spiro International SA produces air duct systems for the HVAC and sheet metal industry.

"As the inventor of the spiral duct machine - the Tubeformer – we have built a sound reputation in the HVAC and sheet metal industry over the last six decades," it told ISMR.

SVR Ltd. manufactures ventilation and chimney machines as well as systems for metal laser welding and the cutting of blanks and tubes based on fibre sources (specifically developed for the requirements of HVAC and metal shop manufacturing).

The t-cut tube cutting machines from mechanical engineering company Transfluid enables the use of orbital cutting technology for tubes with a diameter of up to 80mm.

Sheet metal specialist, TRUMPF, offers a range of tube cutting solutions such as the TruLaser Tube 3000 fiber, TruLaser Tube 5000 fiber, TruLaser Tube 5000, TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber and TruLaser Tube 7000. TruTops Tube software specialises in the programming of tube and profile processes.

"With the TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber, the solidstate laser has a broad processing spectrum. Thanks to RapidCut, the high feed rates of the laser come into their own, even on smaller contours. Above all for thinner materials, this is a huge productivity advantage. The open machine concept gives the TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber optimal accessibility during loading and unloading. This means the machine is efficient from a batch size of 1. Using the Central Link interface, the machine is also optimally equipped for Industry 4.0. It can cut tubes with a diameter of up to 254mm and wall thicknesses of up to 10mm for mild steel," TRUMPF told ISMR.

The bevel cutting option enables high-quality bevel cuts of up to 45°. TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber can also optionally take over further process steps, such as tapping threads.

TRUMPF recently acquired an 80 per cent stake in Active Fiber Systems GmbH (AFS), a company based in Jena, Germany, that specialises in fibre lasers.

TT Engineering now has a cutting and welding unit, re-designed to meet the latest requirements of the market. Working with a laser source, its new stand-alone TOP cuts pipes with diameters up to 400 and 500mm, with a thickness range from 0.4 to 3mm and a maximum pipe length of 1,550mm.

The software developers from UBEco aim to enable users of 64bit computers to benefit from higher processing speed. This becomes apparent in computationally expensive program parts e.g. output to CAD or creating the files for the Finite Element Analysis with LS-Dyna. The roll database and the profile catalogue have been developed as a fast SQL database.

Voestalpine Sadef, with its 150-metre-long machine with 58 roll forming stations is now able to produce very large rollformed tubes with bandwidths of up to 1,000mm and plate thicknesses of up to 8mm.

"We are totally convinced that with these new options we can offer products in sectors where large profiles are required, and where we ensure cost benefits. Think shelving, agricultural machinery, fencing, façade industry etc. We offer close cooperation and co-engineering with our customers and a component which is 'ready for assembly'," it explained.

WAFIOS single-head tube bending machines, EcoBend and TopBend 35, offer flexible customisation options.

"With its modular machine and tool concept, the machine can be easily adjusted to customer requirements. The EcoBend series features three sizes (25, 35 and 42). An intermediate size is now available between sizes 25 and 42 of the RBV series," WAFIOS told ISMR.

The fully integrated loading concept has various options (such as a high-angle conveyor, tube loader and loading unit). The camera system on the magazine checks markings, laser cut-outs and alignment of attachment parts 'almost within cycle time'.

"Innovations, such as the extension of the work range or the swivelling mandrel unit plus the modular, retrofittable machine body, enable project-specific investment while maintaining full flexibility for other applications. The machine can be retrofitted with options for other applications. A second bending direction can even be added, which turns it into a TopBend machine," it explained.

Warcom is a leading Italian manufacturer of press brakes, shears, plasma cutting machines and fibre laser cutting machines. Its new W-Power Tube, for plasma cutting of tube, is equipped with a bevel torch for 3D cutting.

XLase GmbH offers laser cutting systems for tube and sheet. The fully automatic TS65 is a system for high-performance processing of tubes and profiles. New, adaptive pneumatic chucks with an efficient pressure feedback system and the intelligent combination of distance and height control enable short travel distances and process times.

Zopf Biegemaschinen GmbH produces bending solutions, including conventional ring bending machines for tubes and profiles as well as semi-automatic and CNC-controlled machines. Tube bending machines, with and without mandrel, together with horizontal bending machines for bar complete its portfolio.■
