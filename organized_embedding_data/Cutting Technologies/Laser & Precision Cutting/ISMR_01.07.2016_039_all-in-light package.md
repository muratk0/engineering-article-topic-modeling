# 'All-In-Light’ package

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

WITH ITS new 'All-In-Light' package, Precitec – a solutions provider in laser material processing – supplies the complete optical chain – from the laser and the laser light fibre to the cutting head. This package is made possible through strategic cooperation with the laser specialist, TRUMPF. It makes disc laser technology available for manufacturers of flatbed and tube cutting machines. At the same time, Precitec will continue to maintain its independence and cooperate closely with all laser source manufacturers in the interest of its customers.

“Our customers benefit from the combination of state-of-the-art laser technology and innovative cutting head technology plus the additional benefits provided by a ‘One-Stop-Shop' giving a reduction in complexity and easier sourcing and purchasing of parts. For this reason, we chose the name 'All-In-One', i.e. 'All-In-Light', for the project," explained Precitec CEO, Dr. Thilo Wersborg.

For several years now, disc laser technology has been proving its worth in industrial laser cutting and welding. This technology is characterised by high performance stability, easy servicing and low costs. Precitec offers the 'All-In-Light' cutting package internationally with power ranging from 1- to 6kW. 8kW are currently being tested and solutions for up to 10kW are already being developed. Precitec has also been successful in significantly improving the cutting quality in recent months, especially for nitrogen cutting of stainless steel with thicknesses exceeding 30mm.

## Bent part removal

WILSON TOOL International (www.wilsontool.com), the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes, and punch and die components for the stamping industry, offers easier part removal in press brake operations with swing ear sets.

"Suitable for bending boxes with four sides and return flanges, swing ears are available in most profiles for American, European and WT style punches. With the addition of swing ears, forcing the punch out of the box when making the final bend is no longer necessary, because the ears simply swing out of the way," said the manufacturer.

Standard swing ear sets are available in 6" lengths, and non-standard profiles and custom lengths are available as made-to-order options for any punch profile. Each set includes a left- and right-hand swing ear section.
