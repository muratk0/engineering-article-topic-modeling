Bandage includes liquid-indicating gauze

DrySee, a medical technology company dedicated to improving wound care, has introduced a patented waterproof bandage with liquid intrusion alert. If water or other liquids make their way into the bandage, the gauze barrier around the perimeter of the bandage will turn from a light blue to dark blue. The color change alerts the user to change the bandage, knowing that the perimeter seal is broken.

If liquid passes through the second adhesive ring and reaches the wound site, or if the wound seeps fluid, the internal gauze pad will turn a dark-blue color, alerting that a bandage change is necessary.

Aluminum grinding wheel redesigned for faster grinding

The Aluminator Grinding Wheel from Rex-Cut Abrasives has been reformulated to grind 2.2 times more aluminum weld per minute than its original version and to last 46% longer.

The cotton fiber wheel grinds and blends aluminum without clogging or loading. It naturally breaks down as heat is generated, resulting in constant sharp grit being exposed to the aluminum workpiece, eliminating loading and the need for wax or grinding aids. It offers increased grinding action, smooth operator control, very low vibration, and an RA finish of 200, the company states.

The wheel is available in original thickness and a ¼-in. thickness. Diameters are 4.5, 5, 6, and 7 in. in 36 grit.

Multipurpose welding machine includes power factor corrector

Weldcote, a division of Zika Group, has introduced the MIG 200 Striker multipurpose welding machine for steel, stainless steel, and aluminum. Designed for GMAW, GTAW, and SMAW, the compact, dual-voltage machine with digital readouts automatically identifies the power grid voltage and operates accordingly.

The machine features power factor corrector, which increases power efficiency, reduces input power requirements, and stabilizes the welding current to help maximize the efficiency of extension cords and generators.
