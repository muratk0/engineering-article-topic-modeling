# Laser marking in focus

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

LASER LINES IS presenting the Datalogic Arex 400 range of fibre laser marking systems, suitable for users in advanced manufacturing.

"Designed to fit in with robotic operations on a production line, the solution is flexible and versatile with an ultra-compact footprint. The Arex 400 systems have a high resistance single conduit head connection and the capability to operate at up to 40 degrees Centigrade, at 100% power as standard," said Laser Lines.

"The new Arex 400 systems now offer the smallest laser head size presently available, which makes them suitable for those who need to integrate a solution into a production line, where there is little space. The head is also extremely light so can be held by a simple-to-fit bracket and includes the useful 'Green-Spot' feature, which gives the operator a visible feedback on the completion of a successful marking action. This range is particularly suited to advanced manufacturing systems where traceability, part ID and brand markings are paramount, such as the automotive market."

The Datalogic Arex 400 laser controller offers enhanced embedded PC performance and further improved connectivity, with low noise operation and washable filters with improved dust and oil protection. Extended repetition rates have also been added to the Datalogic range, making the systems more suitable for marking plastics and heat-sensitive materials. The Arex 400 range now comes with IP64 protection. The range includes 10W, 20W, 20W MOPA, 30W and 50W power options.
