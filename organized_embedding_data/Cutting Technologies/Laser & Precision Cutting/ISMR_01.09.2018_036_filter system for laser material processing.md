# Filter system for laser material processing

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

FOR 40 YEARS, Herding Filtertechnik has developed solutions for industrial filtration technology and is associated with the Herding® sinter-plate-filter. In laser processes, it offers experience in the efficient filtration of fumes, condensates and finest particles.

"The centrepiece of this technology for Herding is the globally patented Herding® sinter-plate filter, based on pure surface filtration. High separation performance (<0.1 mg/m³); constant operating conditions and pressure loss; extremely long service life and optional recirculation are the outstanding characteristics of this technology. We will be exhibiting this technology at EuroBLECH in Hanover (booth D22 in Hall 15)," it told ISMR.
