# Messer Cutting Systems

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.messer-cutting.com

Messer's product range includes oxyfuel, plasma and laser cutting systems from hand-guided machines up to special machines for shipbuilding. This encompasses oxyfuel equipment for soldering and heating. Intelligent software and automation solutions, as well as services, round off its portfolio.

"Cutting, digitalised workflows and optimised processes— at EuroBLECH 2024, Messer Cutting Systems will be showcasing future-proof ways to make the world of sheet metal processing even more productive and reliable. A new laser cutting system will celebrate its premiere in the live demo. Visitors will also discover how they can increase productivity with automation in all aspects of production. Live applications with hydrogen will also focus on health and safety at work and reducing the CO₂ footprint on stand 13-B128," said the manufacturer.

A second stand in Hall 15 (A05) will focus on automation: a sorting system will be demonstrated live, with ICSORT, as well as tube processing with the fibre laser.

www.akyapak.com/en

At EuroBLECH, Akyapak Machinery will launch three new machines and software.

"Visitors to EuroBLECH will see our new Hare robotic cutting system, our Venera Beam Drill line and next-generation NG 4 Roll-Plate Roll as well as our BYRED software," it told ISMR.

"For over 60 years, our products and services have been helping to improve people's lives around the world. Customers use our products to build infrastructure and structures that help people to access energy, bridges, hospitals, airports and much more. In doing so, our machines enable sustainable, safe and efficient manufacturing by reducing waste, maximizing safety and creating more output with fewer resources," it continued.

Akyapak provides automated machinery/metal fabrication solutions for various industries, designed to enhance productivity and efficiency when combined.

In January 2023, it completed its renewable energy project, a solar power plant now operational atop its production facility, underscoring its ongoing commitment to sustainability.
