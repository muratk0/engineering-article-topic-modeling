# Energy-efficient welding and bending

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

TRUMPF displayed several major technologies at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, all highly innovative in nature and design.

TRUMPF’s new diode direct laser, the TruDiode 3006, was presented in combination with the welding cell, TruLaser Robot 5020. The laser is suitable for deep welding and heat conduction welding and acts as a substitute for conventional welding techniques. In the deep welding process, the laser can even join materials that are several millimetres thick, achieving very high strength at the joint. During heat conduction welding, the new diode lasers create a cleaner and more attractive welding seam than with TruDisk lasers. Passive cooling and the extended service lives of the diode laser modules also promise good availability and long life cycles.

“The special advantage of the diode direct laser is its relatively high efficiency level – as much as 40 per cent. This makes it superbly thrifty in its use of energy. Additional cost savings are achieved by the laser due to its space-saving form and resulting small footprint. Comparing laser welding with conventional procedures shows that the costs per item can be reduced by up to 80 per cent. At EuroBLECH, the robot cell was equipped with a TruDiode 3006 delivering 3 kilowatts of power. Depending upon the demands of the operation, the laser system can also be equipped with a 2-kilowatt or a 4-kilowatt TruDiode laser,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

TRUMPF is also now entering the world of panel bending: its TruBend Center 5025 has a bending length of 2.5 metres and the TruBend Center 5030 boasts a bending length of 3 metres. The new TruBend Center machines are semi-automatic bending centres. Operators just need to insert the sheet by placing it against the backgauge. These provide a suitable complement to the company's bending portfolio.

Even using standard tools, panel bending technology offers an extremely quick way to form positive and negative bends as well as folds. It also allows for circular bends with a variety of radii – a significant advantage, since this cuts set-up times and reduces tool costs. The TRUMPF machines offer a wide variety of parts that go far beyond the traditional range of panel bending components. They are designed to produce complex components with productivity and precision, from filigree edges to large-scale forms.

The machine also automatically takes care of tool changing to eliminate set-up time for the machine operator. During bending operations, the ToolMaster Bend to the left and right of the machine remains in its resting position. When the next job comes along, it re-equips the blank holders automatically. Operators can use this time to get new material ready, for instance. These features make the machine particularly userfriendly. Automation of workpiece handling and tool changing also enhances productivity.

The result is an extremely rapid machine, even for small and medium-size batches and the manufacture of single parts.

“With our TruBend Center, we are alone in the industry in offering a panel bending machine with an additional vertical axis for the part manipulator. This makes for unprecedented flexibility, since the part manipulator can fix parts at a range of positions. Without this additional feature, many components would be impossible to manufacture. The vertical axis also ensures that the operator can easily remove parts that have downward edges,” explained TRUMPF.

Even with standard tools, the TruBend Center machine concept allows for a whole range of shapes and formats – for aluminium and mild steel sheets up to 3mm thick and stainless steel sheets up to 2.2mm thick. Possibilities include not just short side lengths, deep boxes and small profiles, but also parts with formed sections, recesses and parts with diverse radii. The machine can also process galvanized and painted sheet metal.

High axis speeds and acceleration rates coupled with innovative operating aids lend the TruBend Series 5000 great productivity. These all-purpose TRUMPF machines come equipped with a new on-demand servo drive that features 4-cylinder drive technology. The servo drive is highly dynamic and extremely quiet in operation, and delivers significantly more productivity than a conventional drive. At the same time, it remains eco-friendly since it consumes energy only during the bending process.

The operator is assisted by a range of ergonomic components, for instance the MagicShoe which revolutionizes stroke triggering. TRUMPF also offers two innovative angle measuring systems for precise bending results that complement one another.

The new Touchpoint TruBend control is simple and intuitive to use. An extremely realistic 3D visualisation incorporating collision monitoring is also available to assist the operator. The user interface is engineered with the demands of everyday production in mind. The machine can be operated without a mouse, using a 21.5-inch monitor which, like a tablet, is equipped with the multi-touch function, and can even be used wearing work gloves. Operators are free to swivel the monitor into whatever position is convenient for them, and have quick access to direct help whenever they need it.

Operators can also use an additional mobile control unit, the new MobileControl Pro.

TruTops Boost, the new software solution developed by TRUMPF, merges into a single system all the steps needed to generate sheet metal manufacturing programs. ■
