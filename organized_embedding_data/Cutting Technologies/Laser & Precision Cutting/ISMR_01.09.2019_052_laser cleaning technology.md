# Laser cleaning technology

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

LASER PHOTONICS, WHICH develops fibre and CO2 laser machines for marking, cleaning, cutting and engraving applications, has launched what it calls 'the first 2000W handheld laser cleaning machine that is fully capable of meeting health and environmental regulations in manufacturing environments'. This system is designed to clean with rapid speed while maintain laser cleaning efficiency for eco-friendly material processing.

"Laser cleaning is new disruptive technology which helps to minimise chemical and abrasive usage in industrial environments to meet increasing compliance with OSHA and EPA requirements worldwide. Stricter compliance regulations demand safer practices in addressing pollution and health concerns," said the company.

The laser cleaning system, called Cleantech LPC 2000CTH, uses a high-powered, energy-efficient laser to clean a variety of materials. It is suitable for industrial manufacturers operating with components designed to withstand high-temperature environments such as maintenance and repair facilities for military, shipbuilding and aircraft repair facilities as well as turbines, jet engines and combustion motors. This disruptive technology has seen a wave of adoption by key players in numerous industries.

## The process

"Laser cleaning works by directing a high-powered laser beam at a material ready for cleaning; rust removal; corrosion removal; decontamination; de-painting; pre-welding or other surface preparation processes. This process can be used to substitute or replace industrial cleaning processes such as abrasive blasting, sand blasting or wet chemical processes for decontamination or corrosion removal. The LPC 2000 CTH cleans materials made of aluminium; anodized aluminium; alloy metals; stainless steel; mild steel; copper; brass; non-transparent plastics; plaques and more," said the company.

"We believe that the LPC 2000 CTH is the fastest laser cleaner in the market that does not damage the base material of components. Different modes of operations allow the laser to remove charring, rust or paint at high speeds and volumes, yet is gentle enough to also clean small, delicate parts," it concluded.
