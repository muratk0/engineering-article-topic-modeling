# JK Lasers launches 3kW fiber laser

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

A POWERFUL 3kW fiber laser has been launched by global industrial laser manufacturer JK Lasers, part of the GSI Group. The new addition to the company’s Watt-to-kiloWatt fiber laser range will help enhance manufacturing productivity by processing thicker section metals at faster speeds and to higher standards.

Delivering stable output power from switch-on, the JK3000FL features a consistent focused spot size and beam profile over the complete power range.

It is fitted with JK Lasers’ detachable ‘plug in, pre-aligned’ (PIPA) beam delivery fibres, offering core diameters from 100µm, for maximum versatility when processing difficult-to-access areas.

The launch of JK Lasers’ JK3000FL has helped to open up new applications for the company, including remote laser welding. Typically used in high volume automotive assemblies, this application has previously been dominated by CO2 lasers that use a high brightness laser source and scan head to project the focused spot over a few metres.

“Compared to Nd:YAG lasers of a similar power level, the JK3000FL is up to ten times more energy efficient. It is also highly reliable, with the 300,000-hour meantime to failure (MTTF) of the individual laser pump sources contributing to extended system lifetimes,” the manufacturer explained.
