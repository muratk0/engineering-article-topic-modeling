# FLEXIBLE TUBE CUTTING

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

A tube inside a tube - that might be your initial reaction when you see the new TruLaser Tube 7000 in action. This laser cutting system processes tubes inside a tunnel-like safety enclosure and then, depending on size, moves them forward to the brush table or ejects them to a parts container behind the machine.

TRUMPF offers a laser cutting system featuring heavy-duty drives engineered for heavy tubes, a broad chucking range and a high degree of flexibility. This new machine can cut tubes and profiles with large diameters and wall thicknesses without sacrificing productivity. It is able to process tubes up to 30 feet long and can handle a maximum weight of 496 pounds. The system's 0.6 to 10-inch clamping range enables new applications. Available with up to 3.6 kilowatts of laser power, it is suitable for a wide range of applications.
