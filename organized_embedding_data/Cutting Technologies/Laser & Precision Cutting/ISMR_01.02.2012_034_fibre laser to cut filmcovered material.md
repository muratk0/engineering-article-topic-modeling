# Fibre laser to cut filmcovered material

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

AT THE recent TolExpo and Fabtech exhibitions in Paris and Chicago, Salvagnini introduced an important innovation in laser cutting to satisfy the increasing number of fibre cutting laser users who require quality cutting on plastic film-covered thin stainless steel.

As a result of its collaboration with two important manufacturers of protective film for sheet metal - Novacell from France and Polyfilm from America - Salvagnini demonstrated cutting filmcovered sheet metal in a single step on its laser systems. Polyolefins UV treated 4228REF film, manufactured by Novacell and presented at TolExpo, has already been tested by a number of Salvagnini customers. It can be used with fibre and CO lasers.

“For our L3 and L5 laser clients, winning features are flexibility, thanks to the wide range of materials that can be processed, stable cutting parameters, the ease of production changes between one material and thickness and another plus high-quality cutting and low power consumption,” Salvagnini told ISMR.

“Fabtech was the showcase for Z3 film, manufactured by Polyfilm, and also capable of excellent results in terms of quality and performance.” www.salvagninigroup.com

## SURFACE INSPECTION

ISRA PARSYTEC, a leader in the supply of surface inspection systems for the metal industry, has been commissioned to install the 49th surface inspection system at the POSCO plant (Pohang Iron and Steel Company) in Kwangyang, South Korea. The inspection system is to be used in a galvanizing line. The company’s Dual Sensor offers two orthogonal types of illumination to cover quite different types of surface defects. Direct light enhances topographic surface imperfections while diffuse light smoothes these kind of defects to make even lower contrast defects visible. The new inspection system will ensure 100% monitoring of surfaces at high production speeds on the steel production line. www.parsytech.com
