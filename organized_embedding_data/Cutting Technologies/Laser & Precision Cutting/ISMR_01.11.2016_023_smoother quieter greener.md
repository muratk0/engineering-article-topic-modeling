# SMOOTHER, QUIETER GREENER

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

Ermaksan placed a strong emphasis on energy efficiency and smooth operation for its product launches at this year's EuroBLECH

With 50 years of technological investment/experience and an innovative R&D department, Turkish manufacturer Ermaksan has become one of the leading manufacturers in the sheet metal fabrication machinery industry. Its products include laser and plasma cutters, press brakes, punch presses and shears. With a 100,000 m² modern production facility and a qualified team of 800 employees, it offers technical support to customers in 70 countries.

It has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. In 2014, it won two awards in the industrial category of the GoodDesign awards for its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine. In 2015, the family-run company celebrated 50 years in operation.

"We aim to continuously follow customer expectations and design, produce and offer environmentally-friendly, high-technology and energy-saving machines with strong added value," it told ISMR at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany. "We are therefore delighted to launch a range of new products at EuroBLECH this year."

## A launch pad for new products

Ermaksan has forged itself a solid profile in the sheet metal working industry. The company has been producing sheet metal fabricating machines since 1980. It exports 80% of its production through agents, under the Ermaksan brand, and has a branch office in Chicago, USA for its marketing, sales and after-sales services for the North American market.

At this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (25-29 October 2016), it launched three new press brakes, one new turret punch press (ETP Servo Series) and a guillotine shear with a slightly different configuration. Its Fibermak SL-G Force fibre laser cutting system also sported a sideloading fibre laser.

"EuroBLECH is the most popular sheet metal exhibition in the world so we are using this opportunity to showcase some of our new products here," Ersin Yildiz, International Sales Manager, Ermaksan, told ISMR. "We have a new 'green' electric press brake and another 'green' machine for servo punching. We are also demonstrating robotic bending on our booth."

Ermaksan also displayed its new PowerBend Pro press brake for processing thin and small parts of up to 1.27m. An operator can comfortably sit using a height-adjustable CNC pendant arm to control the press brake. ISMR viewed a bending demonstration on this new model at Ermaksan's stand.

"The green FX Servo press brake uses electric technology - there are no hydraulics. Users can dramatically improve their energy efficiency and make massive power savings with the press brake, which can be used with standard controllers and is very user-friendly for press brake operators. It has been designed for more uptime, less maintenance and a special focus on low energy consumption (approximately 50% lower compared to hydraulic press brakes). More accurate bending results can also be achieved due to equal bending force along the ram," he added.

The FX servo green press brake utilises servo motors and has a number of interesting features, including a 4-axis back gauge. Full-length bends can be made between side columns, with no obstructions, and the machine is almost silent in operation. Sliding front support arms assist the operator for easier material handling.

Other existing Ermaksan press brake models include the Evolution II (hybrid), Speed Bend Pro, the Power Bend Pro, the Power-Bend Falcon, the Micro-Bend and the Heavy-Duty press brake (featuring a hydro-dynamic hybrid crowning system, 6-axis servo motor backgauge system and special top and bottom tools).

"We are also showcasing a new innovation on our Fibermak SL-G Force fibre laser. This now offers a side-loading feature for workshops with limited space. Here, on the booth, we feature a 2kW version with various automation options," added Ersin Yildiz.

## Focus on innovation

Yildiz confirmed that, despite recent political upheaval in Turkey, it has been a good year for Ermaksan with turnover expected to grow by 10%.

"In terms of market regions, we have been focusing more on South America (e.g. Brazil) and Asia (e.g. India). We see good growth potential in these areas," he told ISMR.

"We are also building Industry 4.0 interconnectivity into our machines. For example, we use QR codes/barcodes on the products for users to scan with Smartphones. They can then check the machine status from their phones. However, this is just the beginning - this knowledge can be extended, for example, to other machines that can also process this information, as part of a production line, and react accordingly i.e. initiate production, inform the customer by email etc. This is an ongoing process of innovation."

He also had two other interesting innovations to share with ISMR.

"We are now making our own controllers for the guillotines – the next step will be producing controllers for our other products. We are also investing 50 million euros into a new development, which we hope to be able to unveil at next year's LAMIERA exhibition in Milan so, watch this space..."
