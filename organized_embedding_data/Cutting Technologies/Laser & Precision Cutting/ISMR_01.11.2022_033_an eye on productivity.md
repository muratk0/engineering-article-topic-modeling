# An eye on productivity

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

The Element 400 L processes working widths from 1,600mm to 5,100mm and a working length of up to 25,000mm are possible. It can cut sheets of mild steel, stainless steel and Aluminium of up to 40mm thick. It does this with positioning speeds of up to 140 m/min. and high acceleration (combination X-/Y axes) with, via linear guides, high positioning accuracy.

“High-speed lifters and modern process optimisation ensure a significant increase in productivity. Independent, servo-driven tools offer versatile processing options. Set-up times can be reduced by automatic positioning of multiple tools from the part program or the control (optional). Plasma and laser is combined; up to six torch stations can be integrated in the ELEMENT 400 L (including automatic torch spacing and programmable torch selection)," said Messer Cutting Systems.

“The highlight of the Element 400 L is the bevel-cutting head specially developed for the laser process, the Bevel-U. It is suitable for the production of precise and repeatable bevel parts – a great advantage in weld preparation for automated robot welding. The bevel angle is continuously adjusted during the lasercutting process. Magnetic collision protection for the cutting head is part of the design. The Laser Nozzle Control (LNC) automates set-up tasks quickly with no manual intervention," it added.

ISMR: Are there any products/technologies that you have released, or are releasing this year (including at EuroBLECH), that you would like to discuss?

JM: What we can see today is a major leap in laser technology performance. Rapidly increasing laser power, different bevel shapes, new gas mixtures – for laser-cutting machines to be able to meet current demands, innovative solutions are required to make applications more effective and transparent.

Our new laser-cutting machine, ELEMENT 400 L, is the latest member of our ELEMENT family and had been developed to meet these requirements. The highlight is the bevel-cutting head, Bevel-U. It is perfectly suited for the production of precise and repeatable bevel parts – a great advantage in weld preparation for automated robot welding.

Bearing in mind the increasing lack of skilled personnel, automated processes are also extremely important. That is why we also kept the focus on automatic nozzle management, as well as material loading and unloading during the development process. The combination of various features and powerful software ensure maximum productivity and performance. The ELEMENT 400 L therefore becomes a seamless total solution for metal processing in just a few steps. All the system benefits were demonstrated live at EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany, this year.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet-metal cutting markets?

JM: The themes of “automation and digitalisation of processes and equipment" will still be the focus for the next few years. Additionally, the trend towards laser will continue, in our opinion, because of ever-higher laser powers as well as precision in cut quality. In most cases, no finishing is required after laser cutting.

I would like to touch briefly on global climate change. With the effects of the COVID-19 pandemic and the Ukraine war, it feels as if this has slipped out of the spotlight. However, to be able to achieve climate-protection goals, CO₂ emissions must be reduced along the entire value-added chain. Engineering and plant construction play an important role here; with new technologies and process optimisation, we can achieve the prerequisites for more process and energy efficiency, and hence sustainability.

The new, easy-to-operate Global Connect CNC control equipped with 18.5" touchscreen helps to save paper and provides a clear overview of all production data in digital form.

ISMR: What is your approach to Industry 4.0/Big Data and technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality?

JM: Messer Cutting Systems focuses on providing solutions beyond machines. Our focus is to embed Messer cutting machines as tightly as possible into our customers' business and production processes. This is the focus of our Industry 4.0 strategy. We offer a modular portfolio of software components and applications that bridge the customers' ERP systems and the machines with solutions for quotation management, production planning, automation and material handling as well as business analysis. This includes tight integration with Global Connect, our next-generation control to simplify workflows with the operators of Messer cutting machines in automated, as well as semi-automated, scenarios.

With our solutions, customers can achieve completely paperless digital production.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

JM: Increasing prices and difficulties in energy supply are a real stress test for engineering. We await positive signals and support from the politicians. But we can also do something ourselves; to counteract the current bottlenecks, it is important that we broaden our supply chain. We know the customer requirements in the various markets and can implement individual solutions. Quality and customer satisfaction are our highest priorities.

It is important that we also remain competitive in the future. In uncertain times, companies tend to reduce research and development. We must master this tendency in our company as further development is important, especially in times of transformation. Every crisis also offers opportunities. We must recognise these and implement them consistently.

EDITOR'S NOTE

At EuroBLECH 2022, Messer's gases division also showcased process optimisation solutions with gases across the spheres of welding and cutting. The joint stand, shared with Messer Cutting Systems, placed a focus on sustainability, energy efficiency, cost reduction and digitalisation.

As laser-cutting machines become increasingly powerful, they require correspondingly large quantities of gas. This also increases the potential risk of particle contamination. Messer is working with Messer Cutting Systems, as a manufacturer of laser-cutting machines, to integrate the filter units and ensure that replacing the filters is a straightforward process. Filter solutions are also available for machines that have already been installed.
