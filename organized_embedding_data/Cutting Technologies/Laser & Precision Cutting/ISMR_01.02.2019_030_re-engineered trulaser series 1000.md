# Re-engineered TruLaser Series 1000

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

TRUMPF HAS RE-ENGINEERED its TruLaser Series 1000. These machines can now be automated across the board. The TruLaser Series 1000 also handles many cutting-related processes autonomously to drive down part costs.

"Equipped with a robust laser and functions such as collision protection, these machines cut sheet metal in a process-reliable way - the intuitive operating concept reduces training time. Beyond that, we have added new features such as protective glass monitoring and BrightLine fiber. Previously available only in higher 2D laser classes, they improve processes and boost machine efficiency," the German sheet metal specialist told ISMR.

The new series consists of two types of machine, the TruLaser 1030 fiber and the TruLaser 1040 fiber with work areas of three and four metres long, respectively. The TruDisk solid-state laser operates at constant power and is not affected by reflections off copper, brass and other materials that reflect when cut.

Machines with a four-kilowatt laser are equipped with BrightLine fiber to cut thick mild steel. In the event of a collision with tilted parts, a special protective mechanism deflects the cutting head to prevent damage to the cutting head and the machine. The TruLaser Series 1000 deposits slag and small parts in five pull-out drawers below the machine's frame.

"Positioning speed is up from 85 to 140 metres per minute. The machine cuts all materials and sheet thicknesses with the same cutting head. It changes nozzles automatically using the optional nozzle changer. Sensors monitor the optics' protective glass and let the operator know when it is time for a replacement. Visual inspections are a thing of the past. All this reduces the machine's set-up time. The PierceLine feature reduces piercing time by up to 80 per cent and protects the machine and material. These machines operate very energy-efficiently with an average power consumption of 13 kilowatts for the 3-kilowatt laser and 14 kilowatts for the 4-kilowatt laser," concluded TRUMPF.

The TruLaser Series 1000 features a new 18.5-inch multi-touch control panel. The MobileControl app operates the TruLaser Series 1000 using a touchpad situated near the machine. TruTops Monitor software tracks the machine's status continuously and notifies the operator in the event of a malfunction. The TruLaser Series 1000 affords users the opportunity to access the machine's digital enhancement. It contains five apps that assess machine data and deliver the results to users, providing an insight into tool and material usage and displaying the current machine status to users 'on the go'.
