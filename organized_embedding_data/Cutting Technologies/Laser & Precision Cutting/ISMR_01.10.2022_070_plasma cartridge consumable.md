# Plasma cartridge consumable

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

“Until now, plasma consumables have all worked the same way. You'd grab five consumables, put them in the right order and screw them onto your torch. It sounds simple, but in real life it isn't. You had to find the right consumables; double check part numbers; assemble your stack and then carefully install onto the torch. Not too loose, not too tight. Everything needed to be just right," Dutch cutting specialist, Hypertherm, explained.

Now, this is no longer necessary as Hypertherm recently introduced its cartridge consumable. Instead of five parts, there is now just one colour-coded part. Instead of spending several minutes changing out consumables, it can now be done in a few seconds (according to the manufacturer).

"Our new cartridge combines a traditional five-piece stack-up into a single-piece cartridge, making change-outs faster, ordering easier and inventory tracking simpler. It replaces small part numbers with easy-to-see colours so consumable mix-ups don't occur. It also lasts longer than standard, handheld cutting consumables and, when paired with our new SmartSYNC™ torch, lets you know when a new cartridge is needed. There is no more guessing," explained Hypertherm.

In addition, cartridges used with the SmartSYNC torch feature RFID technology. The system will automatically choose the right amperage and process based on the cartridge type just installed. If a gouging cartridge is installed, the system will automatically go into gouging mode.

"Another first is that the torches have controls on them. You can adjust the amperage without walking back to the power supply. You can turn everything off and change your cartridge on the spot. Finally, as you're cutting or gouging, the torch is recording and sending information – starts and stops, arc-on time etc. – back to your system," concluded the manufacturer.■
