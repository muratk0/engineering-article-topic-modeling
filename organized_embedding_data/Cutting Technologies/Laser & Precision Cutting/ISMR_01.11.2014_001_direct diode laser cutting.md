# Direct diode laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

AMADA President and CEO, Mitsuo Okamoto, introduced its groundbreaking new laser ExC (ExaC) technology, implementing the sheet metal direct diode laser (DDL) cutting process, at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover.

“Until now, DDL technology has been applied to processes such as welding or hardening because of the compactness and high oscillation power efficiency of the oscillator, in which excitation source diodes are used directly for laser processing. However, this technology could not be used in cutting processes because of lower beam quality and less focus ability under high power conditions.

We reconsidered DDL technology in collaboration with US-based company, JDSU, and succeeded in developing a 2kW oscillator with the level of performance needed for laser cutting,” AMADA told ISMR.

“The two main features of this new technology are that, through optimised energy efficiency, the oscillation efficiency has reached 40% and it is the first time in the world that a manufacturer of processing machinery has succeeded in developing DDL cutting. In addition, there is less need for maintenance operations such as part replacement.” See our interview on page 74 for more details. ■
