Portable fiber laser marking unit brings marking to the part

Rocklin Manufacturing has introduced the MobiLase Battery, a battery-powered version of its all-in-one, portable fiber laser marking unit housed in a 45-lb. rolling suitcase. The lightweight system allows industrial-strength laser marking to be brought directly to the parts, wherever they may be. Featuring a 30-W fiber laser and a large, 100- by 100-mm marking window, the system delivers fast, durable laser markings on nearly any material.

The 7-lb., hand-held applicator is equipped with a trigger to start marking, plus two bracket options for marking various part shapes. These brackets also enable rapid focusing from a consistent, repeatable distance, which is further optimized by the laser pointer simulator. An optional laser stand offers rapid conversion from portable to tabletop marking when desired.

User-friendly software on the touchscreen PC embedded in the suitcase empowers a variety of easily programmable and potentially intricate markings, including numbers, letters, and characters; time and date; bar codes, 2D codes, and QR codes; and images, logos, and symbols.

The lithium-ion battery enables prolonged use and a rapid recharge, and the laser source features a marking lifetime up to 100,000 hours.

Cobot cell automates GTAW

Cincinnati Incorporated has introduced the CINCINNATI Cobotic 100T welding cell for GTAW. With a working range of about 4 by 8 ft., the automated system is suitable for large or small production batches. The compact cell contains advanced safety devices, including continuous robot elbow monitoring.

The robot can be programmed using a tablet and buttons on the torch. It autonomously detects sheet metal deformation without operator intervention. Dual work zones allow operators to weld in one zone while safely unloading parts in the other.

Servo-electric press brake incorporates automated tool storage

Prima Power offers the eP Genius 1030 flexible automated bending system, which integrates an eP-1030 servo-electric press brake with an automatic tool storage unit. With automated machine setup and shortened cycle times, the machine is suitable for smaller batches.

Features include a 105-ton capacity, 3,060-mm bending length, automatic CNC crowning, 5-axis backgauge, and an Iris Plus angle control system on CNC motorized arms. The tool magazine houses 32 m of tools on eight toolholders that move on three axes. A turret allows the tools to be rotated, increasing machine flexibility.

The user interface allows intuitive programming, both on the machine and offline, as well as automatic management of machine setup.

Electric press brake suitable for high-mix, low-volume parts

AMADA AMERICA has introduced the EGB 6020 ATCe electric press brake. According to the manufacturer, the machine reduces power consumption while providing automated tool setup and repeatable bending accuracy and eliminating gauging errors and part locating fixtures.

The high-speed press brake is suitable for processing high-mix, low-volume parts, even if the operator lacks experience.

Automatic panel bender processes tall, thick, complex parts

TRUMPF has unveiled a new, fully automatic panel bending machine for the North American market. Designed to process complex parts, the new TruBend Center 7020 can bend parts with side lengths of up to 13.8 in. in material thicknesses up to 0.15-in. aluminum, 0.12-in. steel, and 0.08-in. stainless steel, extending over the entire bending length.

According to the manufacturer, the bender works up to 80% faster than a manual press brake, depending on the specific part and bending operations involved. A new rotary part manipulator can move the part closer to the bending line, helping to reduce cycle time by eliminating the need to reposition the grippers during processing. Workers can load/unload the bender from the side without having to lean into the interior, which also saves time.

The ToolMaster system helps ensure that the blank holder tools, which hold the part in place during the bending process, are correctly matched to each workpiece, helping to reduce setup time.

Software helps job shops turn PDF drawings into clean CAD files

Tempus Tools has developed the PDF-2Parts PDF-to-CAD conversion tool. With one click, the user can isolate all of the cutting geometries of a part. The tool delivers a clean, scaled CAD file with no need to make further adjustments or clean up dimensions, annotations, and extra views from the file after conversion.

While viewing the PDF, the user can enter data in a floating panel, and that data is delivered in a ZIP file along with the drawings at the end of the conversion. Data captured includes part name, height, and width; and material type, thickness, and quantity. Bending lines are delivered in a different layer to separate them from cutting lines.

The tool is suitable for use in laser, plasma, waterjet, and oxyfuel cutting applications, as well as milling and turning.
