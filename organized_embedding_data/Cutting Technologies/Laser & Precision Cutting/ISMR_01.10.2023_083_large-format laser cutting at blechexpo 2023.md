# Large-format laser cutting at Blechexpo 2023

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Spanish machine-tool and software manufacturer, TECOI Advanced Plate Processing Systems, will be showcasing its latest advances in large-format laser cutting at the Blechexpo 2023 exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023).

On the company's stand 1108-2 in Hall 1, visitors will be able to see innovations such as its exclusive TWIN DISK system, featuring two independent heads simultaneously working on the same gantry, to double the productivity of a laser cutting system.

“The combination of TWIN DISK with our five-axis bevelling system (BEVEL ARC) makes it possible to produce several parts with complex geometries in extremely short times, breaking production records for a large-dimension laser machine," highlighted the company.

Another TECOI development is its exclusive DRILTEC PLUS, a multi-tool machining head able to carry out drilling, tapping and countersinking operations. It was developed by TECOI to extend the versatility of its laser cutting stations. TECOI’s LS and MB-L series machines have been designed for the solid state/fibre laser cutting of sheet metal, whereas its FL series is for tube and profile cutting using solid state lasers.

Although the manufacturer's presence at Blechexpo will focus mainly on its laser developments, its stand at Blechexpo will also highlight TECOI’s latest plasma cutting and heavy-duty machining equipment. The company also offers a range of ancillary equipment such as automated plate storage systems, cutting tables, tube lathes and waste disposal/fume extraction systems.■
