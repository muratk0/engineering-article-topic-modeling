# HIGHYAG celebrates 20th anniversary

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

HIGHYAG LASERTECHNOLOGIE GmbH (HIGHYAG) celebrated its 20th anniversary this year with a corporate event for employees and their families in September. Photos from the early years of the company and guided tours of the manufacturing facility gave an insight into the company’s history and its products for the laser industry.

Since its founding in 1995, HIGHYAG has focused on technological excellence in laser material processing. An important milestone in its twenty-year history was the development of a laser welding head for welding with solidstate lasers in fully automated production lines in the automotive industry. Laser cutting heads, laser light cables and beam couplers complete its product portfolio of tools for laser material processing. It introduced the BIMO-FSC cutting head in 2012.

Since 2013, HIGHYAG has been a wholly owned subsidiary of II-VI Incorporated. In January 2014, it moved to its current corporate building in Kleinmachnow, just outside Berlin. Laser processing heads and beam delivery systems are manufactured and marketed worldwide from here. See www.highyag.com

A LEADING ALUMINIUM body builder for commercial vehicles has improved efficiency and saved energy by installing Miller welding equipment, supplied by Foster Industrial, throughout its entire factory. Wilcox has spent around £110,000 on twelve Miller XR MIG push pull systems and seven Dynasty 350 TIG systems as part of its expansion plans.

Wilcox Commercial Vehicles is one of the UK’s leading manufacturers of aluminium bodies for rigid and tipping trailers, building around 700 units each year. In January 2015, the firm invested over £3 million in an additional 3,000 sq. ft. factory and, as a result, business is booming. Since January, Wilcox has seen a 40% increase in productivity.

A combination of a broad product range and increasing demand from the construction sector means the order book is full and turnover is heading towards £16 million. The 100- strong team needs to be able to complete jobs quickly but without compromising on quality of finish. Installing Miller welding equipment, which has been developed specifically for use with aluminium, has enabled the team to improve efficiency while producing better quality welds.

“Welding aluminium presents a number of challenges which can make it more difficult and time-consuming – such as the metal’s high thermal conductivity and thermal expansion – but the output and behaviour of the Miller equipment has been especially designed for this. It means that we get consistently good results which, in a business renowned for the quality of its products, is incredibly important,” said Vito Ronzano, Managing Director, Wilcox Commercial Vehicles.
