# THE FIRST CUT

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

Suitable for retrofitting to all major waterjet cutting systems, the Accustream Dialine precision waterjet cutting head features a removable diamond and comes with a 600-hour cutting time warranty for up to 1,000 hours of cutting.

“Companies wishing to upgrade their waterjet cutting system with the Dialine cutting head will benefit from improved edge quality, higher cutting speeds and improved abrasive nozzle life of up to 15%. The diamond orifice securely and precisely engages the diamond to produce a concentric stream alignment to the cutting nozzle,” said Wightman Stewart.

The mixing chamber is manufactured from high wear-resistant material designed for use with the abrasive waterjet process. In some instances, the life of the diamond orifice surpasses that of the mixing chamber, which if necessary can be easily replaced to maximise the diamond’s life. A ruby can also be used with Dialine and will offer the same quality of cut but with a shorter working life. See: www.Accustream.com
