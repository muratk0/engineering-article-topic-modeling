# Plasma cutting milestone

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Hypertherm Associates, the U.S.-based manufacturer of industrial cutting systems and software, has announced the assembly of the 10,000th Hypertherm MAXPRO200® plasmacutting system. It credits the milestone to "thousands of manufacturers and fabricators worldwide who depend on it," it told ISMR.

"We built a plasma cutter that delivers high productivity with low operating costs," said Tyler Kipp, marketing programme manager for Hypertherm Associates. "Job shops, steel centres, heavy equipment manufacturers — anyone needing solid cutting power and capacity without requiring high-definition cutting — have proved this."

Serving thousands of customers across diverse industries and applications, Hypertherm Associates focuses on how the economic landscape and market trends affect manufacturing and production floors of all sizes. Customer pain points are front and centre, it explained, as engineering considers "the right tool for the job" when designing a new machine or technology.

"Customer business goals inspire our design innovations," added Kipp.

Hypertherm Associates is a manufacturer of industrial cutting products and software. Its products include Hypertherm plasma and OMAX waterjet systems as well as CNCs and software.

Founded in 1968, Hypertherm Associates is a 100 per cent Associate-owned company, employing approximately 2,000 Associates, with operations and partner representation worldwide.■
