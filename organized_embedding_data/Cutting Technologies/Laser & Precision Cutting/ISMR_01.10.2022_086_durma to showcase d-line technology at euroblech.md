# DURMA to showcase D-Line technology at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Durmazlar Makina San ve Tic. A.Ş.(DURMA), a Turkish sheet-metal machinery manufacturer, will be showcasing its D-Line solutions (designed for high quality, high repeatability and mass production) at EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, Germany (stand G48, Hall 11).

“Our D-Line at EuroBLECH 2022 will feature a rapid sheet stacking/feeding system; 20kW fibre laser; sorting robot; electrical press brake with automatic tool changer; bending robot and autonomous guided vehicle system," it told ISMR.

“The rapid stacking/feeding system (with 5000kg stacking capacity) transfers sheet metal to a fibre laser-cutting machine and provides around 10% energy savings. Compared to traditional fibre laser-cutting machines, it makes the cutting process 20% faster. Workpieces are cut on a 20kW fibre laser (sizes: min. 80 x 100mm and max. 1000 x 500mm) and are transferred to autonomous guided vehicles with the help of a robotic sorting system. They are then bent in the electric press brake by robots, a process which offers 70% energy savings (in comparison to traditional hydraulic pressbrakes)," it added.

DURMA produces metalforming machines and configurations with autonomous and robotic technologies and special solutions, with Industry 4.0 applications. It is a founding (constituent) member of the Industry 4.0 platform in Turkey, it explained.
