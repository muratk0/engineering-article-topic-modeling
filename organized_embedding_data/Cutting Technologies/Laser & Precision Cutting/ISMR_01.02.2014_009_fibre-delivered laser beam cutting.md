# FIBRE-DELIVERED LASER BEAM CUTTING

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

AN EXCITING NEW European project is creating a novel µm wavelength specific laser cutting head, capable of cutting up to 15mm thick stainless steel, with cut quality similar or better than that currently achieved with CO2 laser cutting systems. The aim of the project is also to increase available cutting speeds in the thickness range of between 3mm and 15mm.

These goals are being achieved through the development of new beam-forming optics and supersonic assist gas nozzles. Key benefits will include higher laser cutting speeds and improved cut quality when using a 1µm laser source.

Results from the Fibre-delivered Laser beam Cutting Optimisation project (FILCO) will be used to enable high quality 1µm laser cutting of metallic plates of up to 15mm in thickness while delivering higher processing speeds. This will allow end-user job shop SMEs to enhance productivity and significantly reduce operating costs.

Consortium partners TWI Ltd; Fraunhofer-ILT; Precitec KG; Optolita UAB; Laser Expertise Ltd; Q-Sys BV and Metalmark Ltd are working together on the project. The research leading to these results has received funding from the European Union Seventh Framework Programme [FP7/2007-2013] under grant agreement 315405.
