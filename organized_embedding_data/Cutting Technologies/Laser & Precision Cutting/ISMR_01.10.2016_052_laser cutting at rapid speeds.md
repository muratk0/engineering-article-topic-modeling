# Laser cutting at rapid speeds

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

IN TIME FOR the EuroBLECH trade fair, Bystronic is upgrading its ByStar Fiber laser cutting system with new features. A new 10kW laser accelerates the cutting process to rapid speeds. The high-end system therefore offers the highest level of laser power in Bystronic's portfolio.

“The new laser essentially provides the ByStar Fiber with the equivalent of a turbo ignition stage. Compared with the industry standard 4kW fibre lasers, the 10kW aggregate increases the cutting speeds on the ByStar Fiber by as much as a factor of four. This enables users to cut four times the number of sheet metal parts in the same time as most of their competitors. The greatest speed benefits can be achieved in material thicknesses of between 3 and 12mm. However, the 10kW fibre laser also achieves incredible speeds in sheet thicknesses of up to 30mm," Bystronic told ISMR. The ByStar Fiber is also being equipped with a new cutting head.

Bystronic developed and designed this cutting head in-house.

“The ByStar Fiber cuts stainless steel, aluminium, non-ferrous metals and mild steel from the thin to thick range of materials. To consistently achieve the best cutting quality with this broad material mix, the cutting head must automatically and flexibly adapt the cutting process to the changing materials and their characteristics. We have therefore equipped the cutting head with the Spot Control function which shapes the laser beam. Depending upon sheet thickness and material, Spot Control adjusts the focal point with high precision. This enables the ByStar Fiber to consistently achieve optimal processing quality, in spite of varying sheet thicknesses and materials, and all this at high speed," said Bystronic.

Bystronic can upgrade the ByStar Fiber with suitable automation solutions such as the ByTrans Extended and the ByTrans Cross loading and unloading systems. At the EuroBLECH exhibition, Bystronic will show a possible combination with the ByTrans Extended as a stand-alone solution. In this configuration, the ByTrans Extended handles the entire loading and unloading of materials, loading blank sheets and removing the finished parts and residual sheets after the cutting process. The automation solution offers diverse material handling possibilities.

“The ByStar Fiber is now also available in the 4020 large format. This enables users to cut metal sheets up to 4 by 2 metres. On the one hand, this allows more parts to be cut from a single metal sheet and increases the productive phase during the cutting process. On the other, it also enables large cut parts to be manufactured, which would not fit on standard-sized metal sheets of 3 by 1.5 metres. This allows users to expand their range of products. And this, in turn, is an important aspect of achieving a competitive edge," concluded Bystronic. See www.bystronic.com ■
