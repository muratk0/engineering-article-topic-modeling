# "Bending from the beginning'

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Sheet-metal bending specialist, Weinbrenner, has been "bending from the beginning" since 1954. With a long pedigree in bending systems, it now offers a range of bending, automation and laser-cutting products to the market.

"Since our foundation in 1954, we have always focused on technical progress, high quality and productivity. Our production was initially focused on special bending machines but, in the light of technological developments in the sheet-metal processing industry, we formed Weinbrenner Maschinenbau GmbH to serve the international market. Fibre laser cutting machines and automation systems for both bending and cutting processes have now been added to our portfolio," it told ISMR.

## Bending portfolio

"Our flagship model is the WPH Series Hybrid Press Bake with robust O-frame design. This unique design, using hybrid technology, ensures faster speeds and generates less energy consumption compared to traditional hydraulic CNC pressbrakes," the manufacturer continued.

With 70 years of production under its belt, Weinbrenner has honed the flexibility and precision of its systems. Its latest products and technologies have been designed with energy efficiency, higher speeds and sustainability in mind. Its portfolio includes a wide range of 100% electric press brakes ranging from 20-130 tons.

"Both our ball-screw and belt-pulley-driven press brakes offer immediate acceleration. This reduces cycle times, in comparison to hydraulic press brakes, by more than 30%. Our WPS electric and WPH Hybrid models feature more rigid O-type mechanical construction. The frames do not deform with high-pressure applications," explained Weinbrenner.

"In short, our environmentally friendly servo-electric and hybrid machines offer accuracy and high speeds as well as better productivity; lower power consumption; shorter cycle times and low maintenance costs," it told ISMR. "Advanced robotic automation combines mechanisation and automation to form sheet-metal parts with accuracy and reliability, as well as significantly shorter cycle times."

## Cutting in focus

Weinbrenner has now added fibre laser-cutting machines to its product portfolio, and its new WFL range of high-power fibre laser-cutting systems for greater speed and acceleration.

"This new linear system is designed to take advantage of high laser powers for greater speeds while cutting thin- and medium-thickness material. The laser power range starts from 6000 up to 20,000 Watts on a 1500 x 3000mm cutting table," outlined the manufacturer.

"With its high strength, the low-weight gantry ensures stiffness and acceleration of up to 4.0G. High acceleration, when processing small radius arcs and sharp corners on sheet-metal material at high speeds, leads to accurate cut parts. Advanced linear motors are particularly suitable for dynamic movement where high acceleration is required over short distances," it added.
