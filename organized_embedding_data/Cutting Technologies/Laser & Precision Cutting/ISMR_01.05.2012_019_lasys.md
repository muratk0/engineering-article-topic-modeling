ISMR SAYS: “Canada's Metalcraft Technology has made key investments in punch press tooling”

Investing millions of dollars in the latest automated equipment and hundreds of hours in advanced training, Metalcraft Technology in Canada proves that the best technology, wisely used, results in quality, productivity and success. In the competitive fabricating industry, smart use of the best equipment often wins the race.

One of Metalcraft Technology’s key investments is punch press tooling, but the investment goes beyond the tooling itself. Metalcraft Technology trains its operators in its use and utilises the tooling supplier’s knowledge to maximise these tooling resources to the maximum. When it started in 1997, the company standardised on one tooling style, Mate Ultra Tec®, and has stayed committed to it for 18 years.
