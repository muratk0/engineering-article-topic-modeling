# We highlight a selection of the latest new fibre laser cutting innovations in the market today.

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

Fibre lasers are a key choice for diverse applications in laser precision cutting and welding, as well as in micro-materials processing and laser marking. In laser precision welding, fibre lasers enable users to create slim seams and achieve narrow kerfs in laser precision cutting – even with wide working distances. In thin materials, the high level of irradiance on the workpiece can be converted directly into a high process speed.

The fibre laser is a relatively new innovation in laser cutting systems for machining thin, and now thicker, sheet metal (including non-ferrous materials such as copper, brass and titanium). Fibre lasers work with low amplitude noise, are extremely resistant to heavy environments and offer low maintenance costs. The technology has quickly been embraced by subcontractors and job shops as its extended cutting.

The use of fibres eliminates the need for elaborate and costly optics for beam guidance and the space requirement can be downsized.
