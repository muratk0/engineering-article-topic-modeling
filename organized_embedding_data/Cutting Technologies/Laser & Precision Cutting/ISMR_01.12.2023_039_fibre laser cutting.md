# Fibre laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Hymson's new HF3015 IT fibre-laser cutting system was highlighted at the EuroBLECH sheet-metal exhibition in Hanover recently.

"The key components of the system are entirely made in Italy (from numerical control to motors, servomotors and drives including software and Industry-4.0 interface)," Hymson told ISMR. "The numerical control system has been developed by Italian manufacturer, ESA. It communicates with Hymson Italydeveloped software."

An automatic nozzle-change option caters for frequent changes of materials or material thicknesses. Automatic nozzle centring capability is designed to eliminate possible inaccuracies and significantly reduce faults, scrap levels and emergency operations. Laser powers for this model range from 1500kW to 12kW (with further powers of 15kw and 20kW possible).

"The system also features a fiveaxis bevelling head for flat sheet-metal processing and automatic CNC loading and unloading systems. The modular design of our laser machines responds to market demands for customisation," added Hymson.

The laser specialist has five production sites worldwide, four R&D centres and over forty overseas partners.
