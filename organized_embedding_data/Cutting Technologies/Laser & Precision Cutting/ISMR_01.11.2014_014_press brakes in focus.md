# Press brakes in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

SHEET METALWORKING machine supplier, Bystronic UK, welcomed over 80 visitors to its latest open house, held at the company’s Coventry headquarters and technical centre from 16-18 September 2014. The focus this time was on the company’s range of press brakes, with a morning presentation each day explaining the productivity, accuracy and repeatability advantages of the machines and programming software.

Afternoon sessions looked at the range of other innovative machines and technology being demonstrated. They included a BySprint Fiber 4kW fibre laser profiling machine, a ByAutonom 6 kW CO laser cell with full automation and a ByJet Smart waterjet cutting machine, as well as Bystronic software, plate storage, CNC plate rolls and a deburring machine for plate and sheet metal parts. ■
