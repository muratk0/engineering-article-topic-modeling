# Three TruLaser Center 7030 fibre laser cutting systems are now running unmanned 24-hour shifts at VDL Technics in The Netherlands. The machines cut, independently remove sheet metal parts, stack them on pallets and bring them into storage.

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

VDL Technics, based in Boxtel, Netherlands, is a subsidiary of the VDL Group and specialises in the production and serial assembly of complex metal components. Its customer base includes companies from the agricultural, transport and mechanical engineering sectors. VDL manufactures the components, some of them highly complex, in batch sizes of 20 up to 1,500. Design consultancy as well as laser cutting and welding, punching and bending are all part of the company's portfolio.

"Our customers expect quality and short delivery times. This can only be achieved with automation and digitalisation," explained Hans Sanders, Managing Director, VDL Technics. VDL Technics has pursued both intensively in recent years. Sanders believes that they are the key to the company's success.

VDL Technics works in three shifts.

However, employees are only there during the day. At night and on public holidays, the machines take over, including three TruLaser Center 7030 fibre laser cutting systems from TRUMPF. The all-rounders take care of the entire machining process, from loading the raw material to the completely sorted component, virtually independently.

Because of highly automated machines like the TruLaser Center 7030, Hans Sanders can increase his productivity.

## Unmanned 24-hour shifts

Up to 2021, a 4kW TruLaser 3030, a 6kW TruLaser 5030 and an 8kW TruLaser 5030 fiber were connected to the STOPA storage system at VDL Technics.

"After these highly productive machines had worked all weekend, lasering around 150

hours, my staff had the task of tidying away and storing the finished parts in the STOPA storage system every Monday," explained Hans Sanders. “That took another 125 to 150 hours, which meant we lost the time advantage gained by quick cutting. In addition, it was very monotonous work; skilled workers are much too good for that," he added.

He told his contact at sheet metal specialist, TRUMPF, a few years ago, how much he would appreciate a machine that not only cuts but also independently removes the parts, stacks them on pallets and brings them into the store. However, when TRUMPF presented him with the TruLaser Center 7030 which can do exactly that, Sanders still waited. He wanted to see how the machine proved itself in practice.

But the time came in 2021 when the first fullservice laser machine was put into operation at VDL Technics.

When Sanders placed the order, it was under the proviso that TRUMPF install a 12kW laser instead of the standard 6kW laser. That was provided and around ten weeks later, the machine was running its first unmanned 24-hour shifts. Today, five TruLaser Center 7030 systems operate across the entire VDL Group. Sanders now has three full-service laser machines in his production, and his colleagues from sister companies VDL Industrial Modules and VDL NSA Metaal now rely on the fullservice machines.

The all-rounders take care of the entire machining process, from loading the raw material to the completely sorted component, virtually independently.

## Easy operation

The three TruLaser Center 7030 full-service laser machines, as well as another laser system and a bending machine from TRUMPF, are connected to a STOPA high-bay storage rack. This will soon be followed by a 24kW laser system.

"The only thing that is still done manually is storing raw sheets in the STOPA store," explained Sanders proudly. However, none of the other machines work as fully autonomously as his three TruLaser Center 7030 machines.

"We program the systems offline using the TruTops Boost programming software and then that's it. From that point on, the machines take care of everything automatically," outlined Sanders.

Programming is easier than with conventional automated laser cutting systems because of the high degree of automation of the TruLaser Center 7030.

"My programmers can handle the systems and we have a fully finished part at the end of the process," maintained Sanders.

## Process reliability

Hans Sanders believes that logistics are the most important element in production.

"What is the point if the laser cutting process is fast but the downstream processes come to a halt due to delays caused by manual removal?" he asked. The process reliability of his machines is much more important to him.

"We have a problem if a part is tilted somewhere along the line during unmanned cutting," he added. The TruLaser Center 7030 is equipped with numerous functions to prevent this from happening. For example, SmartGate integrated in the brush tables prevents parts from overturning. It consists of two carriages that move synchronously to the cutting head and support the sheet during the cutting process. An ejector cylinder fitted on the cutting head ejects the metal parts downwards automatically.

"That makes microjoints superfluous. We receive top-quality parts straightaway without any post-processing. Punching slugs and scrap fall into a container without appreciably interrupting the cutting process," said Sanders, and added: "I just think that is fascinating."

"Depending upon the material thickness and the type of component, a full-service laser machine processes up to 850kg of material every hour. With three machines working over the weekend, this is a lot of material. But we don't have to worry about that anymore," outlined Sanders.

## Reducing staff workload

But the TruLaser Center 7030 does not just take care of automatic unloading. At VDL, the SortMaster Speed stacks parts on up to three pallets and places them in the deposit position.

“They are then automatically taken directly to the TruBend Cell 5170 for bending, which is the next process step. Thanks to the three TruLaser Center 7030 systems, we can increase our order volume by 20-25 per cent. The work that a system like that takes on for our staff not only reduces their workload, but massively accelerates our processes," highlighted Hans Sanders.

This is a key point for Hans Sanders, who is very impressed by the machine. "The TruLaser Center 7030 is a unique system; there is nothing comparable in the market. It is perfect for any supplier who wants to process a lot of parts quickly, safely and to the best quality standard," he explained.

He is also certain that machines, such as the TruLaser Center 7030, have great potential due to increasing customer requirements and the ongoing lack of skilled workers in the industry.■
