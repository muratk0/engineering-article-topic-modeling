Laser cutting machine generates bevels while cutting

The TruLaser Series 3000 Bevel Cut Edition from TRUMPF can produce beveled cutting edges (chamfers) on the top and bottom edge of sheet metal components while cutting. This helps ensure optimum preparation of the welding edges and reduces downstream processes.

The machine can apply chamfers to sheets up to 1 in. thick and handles complex bevel geometries with ease, the company reports. It is suitable for mild and stainless steel.

The cutting unit features two additional rotary axes, ultracompact drive motors, and a nozzle developed specifically for bevel cutting. The cutting head can be tilted in all directions up to an angle of 50 degrees. As usual, the cutting unit regulates the distance to the sheet, minimizing the risk of collision despite the steep angle.

Abrasive belts offer flexibility to conform to contoured surfaces

Saint-Gobain Abrasives has introduced its next-generation Norton NORaX N889 microstructured abrasive belts. With a flexible design, they conform to contoured surfaces to provide a consistent, fine finish while reducing the number of process steps.

The belts feature a micron-grade aluminum oxide grain structured in a fine 3D pyramid pattern that is suitable for lower-pressure (0 to 8 PSI) applications that require belt flexibility. The engineered abrasive slowly erodes during grinding, exposing new, sharp abrasive grains.

Saint-Gobain Abrasives

Fiber laser cutting system delivers medium-thick plate cutting efficiency

The new GV series fiber laser cutting machine from HSG Laser provides up to 60 kW of power to meet a wide range of fabrication needs. The system delivers high-production cutting of materials up to 3.9 in. thick, making it a viable alternative to plasma and flame cutting, the manufacturer reports.

The exchange table supports up to 11,023 lbs., accommodating a variety of applications, and laser power can be tailored to the user’s primary material thickness and future growth trajectory. With high-power output, the system significantly increases the efficiency of cutting medium-thick plates; the 30-kW system provides cutting speeds that are up to 400% faster than traditional 12-kW systems, according to the company.

With a maximum acceleration of 6G, the system reaches speeds of 984 FPM. Optimized piercing efficiency delivers zero-second piercing for medium-thickness plates. Features include an intelligent anti-collision function, versatile edge detection, the company’s Perfect Finishing Cutting Function, a smart cutting head, and visual remnant utilization.

Cloud-based gas monitoring software tracks complete gas consumption

InduSuite, ESAB’s platform of software applications for welding, cutting, and robotics, has introduced FloCloud gas monitoring software for tracking gas process parameters, analyzing gas consumption, and identifying gas leaks and overconsumption. It uses InduSuite’s WeldCloud software application platform and mesh WiFi-enabled pressure and flow sensors that communicate data to the FloCloud online application. The software features an analytics dashboard and reporting system that tracks every end user point and sends notifications if gas pressure drops or if gas flow is outside of WPS parameters. It also tracks complete gas consumption per welding job, per welding operator, and per overall project.

ERP system’s time tracking feature helps improve labor time/cost estimates

Cetec ERP provides actual labor cost on work orders through time tracking as part of its standard ERP system. Customized labor plans are created for jobs with the routing steps for the various areas of production.

Using a printed traveler/router and a bar code scanner, the operator can scan the order into the system using a unique order bar code for accurate logging of time started and stopped. Time can also be logged by clicking a button on the work order screen.

Knowing the actual time spent on jobs and work orders helps manufacturers determine labor and overhead costs accurately. Built-in reports give the ability to compare estimated labor cost versus actual labor cost, as well as employee and job efficiency. This data then can be used to reinforce better labor time/cost estimates to drive proper product resale markups and more accurate scheduling.

Cetec ERP

www.cetecerp.com

Two features added to offline robotic welding programming software

Pemamek Ltd.’s software offline programming system for heavy robotic welding automation, Weld-Control 300, now includes two new add-on features.

The PathPlanner feature automates the creation of collision-free paths. It optimizes robot movements from approach to welding to exit to help ensure smooth workflow even on complex workpieces.

Its compatibility with existing 3D models helps ensure simple integration to pave the way for advanced welding automation.

The AutoScan feature enables adaptive automated welding with minimal user input. Using a combination of software and scanning technology, it creates data-driven, accurate welding paths that can then be used on all subsequent workpieces. It is fully compatible with the Pema FMS.
