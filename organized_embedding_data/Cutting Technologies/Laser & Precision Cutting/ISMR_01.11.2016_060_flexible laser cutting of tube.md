# Flexible laser cutting of tube

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

LT-FREE IS a 5-axial laser cutting machine developed by BLM Group to offer flexibility and simplicity for the laser cutting of bent pipes, flat pipes, shaped sheets, hydroformed elements, collapsible elements and welded elements.

"Starting from simple and effective off-line programming, the LT-FREE machine reduces the number of semi-finished products and manufactures finished products within a short time, in a very inexpensive process. LT-FREE is a system created with various applications in mind, from prototyping to serial production, in sectors such as automotive, aerospace, motorcycles, household appliances, HVAC, furniture and steel structures," explained BLM.

To satisfy the needs of individual clients, four different configuration options are available – Entry Level, Piece Value, Mid-Flex and High Flex. The High Flex and Value options also include the Bin Packing unit, a powerful station for automatic loading/ unloading, providing the LT-FREE unit with totally independent operation. The application for a fibre laser with power of between one and 2kW gives the LT-FREE the ability to cut a wide range of materials, while saving energy and maintenance costs.

“Active Piercing makes programming holecutting easy, without any need to worry about the material's thickness, which is often variable from area to area in many elements processed on the LT-FREE unit. This function also manages hole-cutting within the shortest possible time, without damaging the wall of the pipe resting on the processed wall," said BLM. See www.blmgroup.com
