STEP Ahead Awards Recognize Women for Excellence in Manufacturing

The Manufacturing Institute’s STEP (science, technology, engineering, and production) Ahead Award program is honoring 130 women who have demonstrated excellence and leadership in their careers and have made an impact on their companies and communities. A reception and program being held in Washington, D.C., on April 20 will highlight each honoree’s story, including their leadership and accomplishments in manufacturing.

Congratulations to the following FMA members who will be recognized at this event.

Lori Tapani

Co-President, Wyoming Machine Inc., Stacy, Minn.

"People are my passion! In manufacturing, we use processes and technology/innovation to transform raw materials into final products. That transformation is powered by people with diverse skill sets working collaboratively! Identifying and harnessing the unique gifts and talents that each person brings to the team is where the magic happens."

Robin Tindall

Environmental Stewardship Manager, Hypertherm Inc., Hanover, N.H.

"Being able to design and make something is empowering and exciting. It takes an amazing amount of teamwork, creativity, and hard work to convert an idea to a design, and the design to a product that is useful and reliable. The joy is in the collaboration."

Karla Valdes

Quality Manager, Commercial Vehicle Group Inc., New Albany, Ohio

"I love seeing the transformation and progressive changes; they never stop. Experiencing the challenges and solutions that manufacturing offers keeps me motivated to strive for a better environment, not only for the business, but for the people which are our most important assets."

The STEP Ahead initiative was launched to examine and promote the role of women in the manufacturing industry through recognition, research, and leadership for attracting, advancing, and retaining strong female talent.

Master the fundamentals of precision sheet metal bending

with an understanding of the math, the equipment, and the tools of the trade in this intensive two-day seminar. You’ll understand the principles behind quality sheet metal bending through interactive instruction and sample work problems throughout the course. Through easy-to-understand exercises, you will learn how to calculate accurate bend deductions, select the best tooling for the job, and determine the correct V-die opening to avoid part distortion. FMA’s expert instructor will guide you through the fundamentals of press brake operation and you’ll gain knowledge in these basic concepts:

related metal fabrication terminology

blueprint interpretation

different forming methods and how each relates to bend type

proper use of hand tools

inspection and quality practices

The Future of Fabrication & Welding Training 2017 Summer Workshop

will focus on professional development for advanced technology faculty, administrators, and company trainers in the trenches. NBT Auction Raises Over $17,000 to Support Manufacturing Students

Another successful silent auction to benefit Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT) took place at FMA’s 2017 Annual Meeting in New Orleans. Over 300 conference attendees had the opportunity to outbid each other on some of the most unique and interesting donated items during the opening welcome reception on March 8.

The room was abuzz with energy and excitement as colleagues from coast to coast gathered to network and learn about the industry, new technologies, the current economy, and market forecasts. Auction participants could place bids on-site at The Ritz-Carlton reception or online on the NBT website.

As the night progressed, several items caught the attention of the crowd. Some of the most sought-after items were two steel tables fabricated at Fox Valley Technical College’s all-girls manufacturing camp, a GoPro HERO Bundle, an Apple Watch, a two-day pheasant hunting trip, and a bottle of Pappy Van Winkle bourbon. Other popular items included tickets to "Dancing with the Stars," a signed mini helmet and rookie card from Chicago Cubs’ Kris Bryant, and a Philco Model 84 refurbished radio, circa 1934, refurbished by FMAC Publisher Dave Brambert.
