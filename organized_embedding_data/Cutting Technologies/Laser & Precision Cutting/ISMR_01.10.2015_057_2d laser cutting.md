# 2D laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

TRUMPF’S new 2D laser cutting system, TruLaser 2030 fiber, features intuitive controls, minimal space requirements and quick and simple installation. The machine, powered by the TruDisk solid-state laser, rapidly cuts thin sheet metal (in particular).

“The machine’s enhanced dynamics and the motion unit with gantry drives also contribute to high productivity. Numerous functions, such as the one-cutting-head strategy and an optional nozzle changer, have been adopted to achieve shorter, non-productive periods. Standard equipment for the TruLaser 2030 fiber is the TruDisk 3001 with threekilowatt laser power. The four-kilowatt version TruDisk 4001 is optional,” explained TRUMPF.

“The machine’s strength is in sheet metal thicknesses of up to 12mm, where it is highly productive. It also offers the flexibility to process thicker materials. At three kilowatts of power, it can cut mild steel of up to 20mm, stainless steel of up to 16mm and aluminium of up to 12mm thick. At four kilowatts, the boundary is raised to 20mm of stainless steel and mild steel as well as 16mm in aluminium. Non-ferrous metals are also handled by this line. Depending upon the laser power selected, the system can cut copper and brass sheet of up to 6 or 8mm.”

All materials and thicknesses can be separated with a single cutting unit. This saves set-up time, since it eliminates the need to change lenses or cutting heads. The collision protection built into the cutting head and an automatic nozzle changer, with space for up to eight nozzles, can further reduce non-productive time.
