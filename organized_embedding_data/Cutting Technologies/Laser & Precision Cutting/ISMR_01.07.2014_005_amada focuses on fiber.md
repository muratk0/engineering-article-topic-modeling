# AMADA focuses on fiber

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

JAPANESE machine tool manufacturer, AMADA, has completed a large-scale, clean-room workspace for the mass production of fiber laser oscillators in the Assembly floor (Plant 5) of its Fujimomiya Works in Shizuoka (Japan). The maximum production capacity of laser oscillators will be 40 units per month (8 booths at 5 operations per month each) by the clean-room, 2.5 times greater than its previous production capacity. The total investment value was approximately 400 million yen for the new 1208 sq. m. facilities.

“Total sales for our laser machines in 2013 were approximately 1,000 units, more than a 10 per cent increase on the previous year,” AMADA told ISMR. “Looking ahead, demand for laser machines (including combination machines) is expected to more than double, with fiber laser-equipped machines accounting for more than 30 per cent of total laser machine sales. Fiber laser oscillator production volume was 14 units/month in FY2013 and 26 units/month in FY2014. We expect even more rapid growth in the years to come.”

A total of 14 booths in the new clean-room are now ready. Amongst these, 11 booths are for fiber lasers (eight for production, two for development and one for maintenance). The development booths will be for development of next-generation oscillators with an eye on the future evolution of optical technology. “With this development at our Fujinomiya Works we have establish a basic system for supplying and maintaining fiber laser oscillators, through which a customer’s factory can maintain production with no downtime. The next plan is to form a similar fiber laser oscillator supply and maintenance system based on this one as the supplying base to European and American markets at Brea Plant, AMADA’s subsidiary in California, USA. In line with the global expansion of fiber lasers and future rising demand, we will establish national and international supply systems for the appropriate volumes at the appropriate locations and continue to develop a rapid and fine-tuned maintenance system.”

“Additionally, the development of fiber laser oscillators has now been upgraded to the next generation, with miniaturisation improvements enabling built-in installation possibilities as well as improving the quality of beam. It is now possible to achieve higher quality processing with lower output power. In the future, we plan to continue producing our 2kW and 4kW oscillators and also begin manufacturing a 6kW version. We will continue to strive to develop the next generation of oscillators using the latest technologies,” AMADA explained.
