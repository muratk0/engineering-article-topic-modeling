# LASER SOURCES IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

COHERENT, INC. showcased its new kilowatt class portfolio of laser sources at the FABTECH trade show in November 2011. The company updated its three-pronged kilowatt class strategy, addressing the high power materials processing market, which includes cutting, welding, cladding and heat treating applications.

At the show, it presented a kilowatt version of its E-Series CO laser called the DIAMOND E-1000 (requires no consumables and features rapid cutting). Its HIGHLIGHT 8000D is a kilowatt-class direct diode system introduced in June 2011 with output powers of up to 8kW. Completing the strategy is the HighLight 1000FL fiber laser, primarily targeted at cutting and welding of metals such as mild and stainless steels, copper, aluminium, brass, titanium and aluminium magnesium. www.coherent.com

## Heat stress protection

MILLER ELECTRIC Mfg. Co. has extended its Arc Armor line of welding protection products with the release of two new welding helmet cooling accessories designed to reduce heat stress, leading to increased operator comfort, productivity, weld quality and safety. The two new Arc Armor Heat Stress accessories include:

■ The CoolBelt is a lightweight belt-mounted cooling system that integrates into most Miller welding helmets. Designed for extreme industrial environments, the CoolBelt offers multiple air speeds with directional air controls to provide air movement over the user’s face and head. The CoolBelt can lower temperature underneath the hood up to 17oF while also removing stagnant air and decreasing lens fog. A rechargeable battery pack provides up to five hours of battery life and extra battery packs are available.

■ The CoolBand is an integrated headgear cooling system that fits exclusively into most Miller Arc Armor helmets, it provides up to six hours of continuous cooling on one battery charge. Downward and upward air vents provide constant air movement over the user’s face and head, removing stagnant air and decreasing lens fog.
