# Compressed air system adds flexibility to metal distributor’s nitrogen generation

[TARİH: 01.11.2025 The Fabricator]

Applications

Situation

Bemidji Steel, founded in 1983, is a national distributor of steel and aluminum products, including sheet, plate, tubing, beams, rods, and structural steel. In 2017, the company started offering value-added services, including precision laser cutting and forming. To keep up with growing demand, it built a new fabrication facility to house its equipment and production lines.

In addition to metal forming equipment and pneumatic tools such as air hammers, the new facility runs four lasers for cutting flat sheet and tube. The laser equipment relies on high-purity, high-pressure nitrogen to prevent oxidation and maintain cut quality. Bemidji Steel generates that nitrogen with a NitroCube5 membrane generator capable of producing up to 1,200 m

3

of high-purity nitrogen per day.

To meet nitrogen purity requirements, the company needs a consistent supply of clean, dry compressed air going into the nitrogen generator. Bemidji Steel wanted a compressed air system that would ensure air quality and have enough redundancy to prevent unplanned downtime and support future growth.

Resolution

The company brought in Fluid-Aire Dynamics at the beginning of the new build to design the compressed air system from the ground up for optimal efficiency and performance, including compressor selection, storage, air treatment, and distribution system.

The system relies on two 60-HP PneuTech RK-60 rotary screw air compressors. The company can run all of its air-using processes, including nitrogen generation, off one of the compressors, so it cycles between the two compressors to extend the service life of both. This ensures there is always plenty of air to run operations, even while one machine is offline for maintenance.

Air treatment is an important consideration for nitrogen generation. Air coming out of the compressor goes first into an oil/water separator and then into a desiccant air dryer (Great Lakes GPS-500-116), which can achieve dewpoints as low as -40 degrees F. Inline filtration before and after the air dryer removes particulates and oil carryover for optimal air purity. A dry air receiver tank installed after the desiccant dryers helps ensure consistent supply and pressure for nitrogen generation and air tools.

The compressors are housed in a dedicated compressor room; waste heat is captured to offset facility heating costs in the winter. An aluminum loop-style distribution system from Unipipe carries air where it is needed. Unipipe piping also delivers nitrogen from the membrane generator to the laser cutters.

The new system provides the reliability and performance needed to power Bemidji Steel’s operations today while giving the company room to grow in the future. With two 60-HP compressors, it can add more lasers or a more powerful nitrogen generator without adding compressed air capacity immediately. The system is modular, and there is enough room in the compressor room to eventually add a third compressor if needed. The distribution system also can be reconfigured and air drops added if Bemidji expands its production lines.
