# Messer Cutting Systems GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.messer-cutting.com

Messer's product range includes oxyfuel, plasma and lasercutting systems from hand-guided machines up to special machines for shipbuilding. This encompasses machines and equipment for oxyfuel welding; cutting; brazing; soldering and heating. Spare parts; repairs; retrofit; maintenance/service; environmental technology and in-house software solutions are also offered.

At EuroBLECH 2022, it will focus on new levels of productivity with ELEMENT (its latest plasma bevel technology) and ELEMENT L (a new, flexible solution for precision laser bevel cutting, including XXL sheet). It will also demonstrate how its latest solutions for digitisation in production and Industry 4.0 will work. The entire process (from setting up sales quotes; nesting plans; production planning; material handling; machine controls and business process analysis) will be shown live. The digital network of machines and systems will provide the relevant production data in real-time.
