# FOCUS ON LASER CUTTING

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

Franz Langen (on the right) in conversation with the operator of the ByStar Fiber 8025. The quality of the cutting edges is essential for the further processing of the parts. A high-quality laser cut eliminates the need for costly reworking. (Photo: Daniel Kunzfeld)

Above: Langen managing directors, Wilfried and Franz Langen, have been relying upon Bystronic cutting technology for 20 years

Being able to cut sheet metal parts up to a length of 8 metres transforms the entire material handling process. The team at Langen CNC Metalltechnik has adapted all workflows upstream and downstream of the laser cutting process to the new cutting dimensions.

For laser cutting, Langen CNC Metalltechnik uses the BySoft 7 software from Bystronic. The program automatically nests the parts that are to be cut on the metal sheet to minimise offcuts.

With the ByStar Fiber 8025, Langen cuts around 200 metric tons of metal per week. If required, the available capacity would permit them to process even higher volumes.
