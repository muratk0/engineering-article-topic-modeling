# 2D/3D combination cutting

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

## Durmazlar (Durma) is a global
manufacturer of sheet-metal processing machinery, established in 1956, with headquarters in Turkey. Its wide product portfolio includes laser cutting systems; press brakes; punch presses; bending centres; Smart Line solutions; plasmacutting machines; guillotine shears; plate rollers and profile bending machines.

"We have now integrated our 2D sheet laser cutting machines with our tubecutting machine to offer a comprehensive combination solution. Our HD-F 4020 COMBI machine is designed to meet all laser cutting requirements in a single unit, allowing for automatic loading and cutting of tubes ranging from Ø12 to Ø170mm," the manufacturer told ISMR.

"The Durma HD-F 4020 COMBI also offers the convenience of loading eight tubes at once as an option. In addition to being able to load different types of tubes at once, automatic measurement of the lengths of all the loaded tubes on the cutting line is just one of the many features offered by this combination machine," it added.

The machine is equipped with a measuring device that detects incorrect tube loading while the chuck is clamping the tube, immediately stopping the loading operation and sounding an alarm. This functionality helps prevent wasted time and production losses in the manufacturing process.

As a founder member of Turkey's Industry-4.0 platform, Durma also designs machines that can communicate with each other through customised cloud technology, designed in-house.
