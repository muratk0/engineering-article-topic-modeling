# THE POWER OF PLASMA

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

There have been a number of significant advances in plasma cutting technology in 2013. ISMR showcases a selection of the latest developments.

Advantages of the plasma cutting process include high flexibility when cutting all electrically conductive materials; low requirements on material quality and working environment; high cutting speeds when cutting thin and medium-sized plates (up to 10 times higher compared to oxy-fuel) and higher cutting speeds when cutting medium-sized and thick materials compared to laser.

Materials can be cut nearly dross-free (no after-treatment is required) and the process is excellent for cutting medium-sized and thick high-alloyed steel and aluminium plates as well as thin and medium-sized mild steel (up to 30mm).

## Latest plasma technologies

There have been a number of significant advances in plasma cutting technology this year – here, we showcase a selection of the latest developments.

Turkish manufacturer Ermaksan has launched a new plasma cutting machine for flexible working and high quality cutting with different automation solutions.

ESAB Cutting Systems now offers m3 Plasma™, its most advanced plasma system that combines plasma cutting and marking capabilities in an integrated package suitable for a wide variety of materials. The m3 Plasma, demonstrated recently at the FABTECH exhibition in the US, incorporates the latest technology for efficiency in high-speed, low-cost standard plasma cutting, precision cutting and marking and high-current, thick-plate cutting.

“The m3 Plasma system features our next-generation EPP Precision Plasmarc® power sources and new XR Series nozzles. EPP-202/362 power supplies feature a simpler design with the ability to communicate directly with the CNC. The new XR nozzles provide the versatility to cut a variety of material thicknesses at high speeds with the same nozzle, thus lowering operating and energy consumption costs,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

In addition, m3 Plasma features ESAB’s precision hole technology which automatically processes bolt-ready holes in mild steel up to 1” down to a 1:1 hole-to-thickness ratio, eliminating the need for a secondary drilling operation. Smart Voltage Height Control automatically adjusts the voltage height control to maximise consumable life while ensuring consistent cut quality.

A novel demonstration at last year’s FABTECH exhibition was ESAB’s new Hydrocut™ LX waterjet shape cutting machine, a combined waterjet and plasma cutting system. High precision contours can be cut with waterjet, while non-critical contours can be cut with plasma.

ESAB also showcased a new integrated robotic plasma cutting cell at FABTECH 2013. It partnered with leading robot manufacturer, KUKA, and automation manufacturing expert, Arc Specialties, to design and develop the advanced robotic plasma cutting cell which features robotic cutting software that ensures accurate cuts, creates true bevel angles, compensates for kerf and eliminates complicated robot programming.

ESAB also exhibited its complete range of PowerCut® manual plasma cutting systems at FABTECH 2013.

Hypertherm®, a manufacturer of advanced cutting systems, has just launched a new website for Hypertherm’s mechanised HPR® and HPRXD® end users to learn more about HyPerformance Plasma technology and the capabilities of Hypertherm’s HPRXD power sources – see http://emeahprxd.hypertherm.com

At the recent Schweissen & Schneiden 2013 exhibition in Germany, the company demonstrated several of the latest enhanced capabilities for its HyPerformance® HPRXD® product line as well as its True Bevel™, Rapid Part™ and True Hole™ technologies that are part of its Built for Business™ Integrated Cutting Solutions platform – a combination of hardware and CAM nesting software.

True Bevel technology for HPRXD plasma systems is a new performance application for mild steel that simplifies the bevel-cutting process. Parameter values for several processes are available. Output values for angle compensation, kerf, cut height, cut speed and arc voltage are set which makes set-up for new jobs fast and results accurate. With Rapid Part technology, Hypertherm products integrate seamlessly to significantly increase productivity, without operator intervention.

Hypertherm also recently introduced new centricut brand consumables for Kjellberg HiFocus plasma systems with new PerCut 210M and 450M torches.

The complete Powermax® family was also on display in Essen with the addition of a new plasma cutting and gouging system which made its global debut at Schweissen & Schneiden 2013.

In December 2013, Hypertherm launched its Powermax125®, a new cutting and gouging system delivering, it says, ‘the most power of any portable air plasma available on the market’.

At the same exhibition, Kjellberg Finsterwalde presented a new plasma cutting system for efficient cutting, HiFocus 600i neo, for precise cuts and a flexible cutting range at low costs per cutting metre. It cuts materials such as mild steel and stainless steel with a thickness from 0.5mm to 160mm, making the plasma cutting system highly versatile for the user. Marking, bevelling and underwater plasma cutting applications are also possible.

At the same trade fair for welding and cutting in Essen, Kjellberg Finsterwalde’s new coated rod electrode Kjelcut was demonstrated for cutting and hole piercing. Designed for a variety of applications, it also cuts thicker plates of all alloyed and unalloyed steels, non-ferrous metals, cast iron and cast steel.

Earlier in 2013, Techno Inc. launched its CNC plasma accessory: the TechProbe Digitizer. The new digitising probe mounts easily to any Techno CNC plasma machine. The TechProbe software will digitise an object with simple or complex geometric shapes, input the object, scan-area dimensions and desired stepresolution into the software, and the program automatically collects the data. Most of the software’s default settings, such as speeds and resolutions, will not have to be re-configured.

The Ultra-Cut XT system, launched in Europe at the recent Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition, from Victor Technologies enables parts to go directly from the cutting table to welding, painting or assembly without expensive secondary operations. It delivers ISO 9013:2002 Class 3 or better cuts on any material up to 50mm thick, noticeably reducing bevel and the need for post-cut finishing.

Victor Thermal Dynamics has also developed Diameter PRO technology which facilitates this integration of components allowing the Ultra-Cut® system plasma systems to cut bolt-ready holes with minimum-to-notaper. As soon as the holes are cut, the work piece is ready for finishing or assembly, saving time and money by eliminating secondary processes and thereby increasing productivity.

With the ability to cut 25mm thick mild steel at 2000mm per minute and 50mm thick mild steel at 760mm per minute, the Ultra-Cut 400 XT can lower the cost per cut and makes it competitive with the oxy-fuel process.

At the recent BlechExpo exhibition in Germany, Voortman unveiled its latest innovations in plasma straight cutting and plasma 3D bevelling as well as oxy-fuel cutting of steel plates. Its VCS range enables the combination of oxy-fuel and plasma cutting processes to cover all commonly occurring tasks such as multi-torch gas cutting, straight plasma cutting, bevel plasma cutting, marking or a combination of all. VCS cutting systems can process steel, stainless steel and aluminium.

In December 2013, Voortman started production of its new V304 plate processing system at its production facility in Rijssen, the Netherlands. The V304, capable of multi-torch oxy-fuel cutting, plasma straight cutting and plasma 3D beveling, is one of the successors of the VCS Compact, the VCS Thermo and the VCS Multi. ■
