# Vigan

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 The Fabricator]

Snapshot

Belgium-based company VIGAN is a leading manufacturer of pneumatic unloaders, used for unloading ships in ports worldwide. Pneumatic unloaders lift products by vacuum out of the hatch of the vessel to fill trucks or to transfer on a conveyor belt to storage silos.

VIGAN handles the entire process, from concept and engineering to production and pre-assembly of the unloaders on site. Each machine is perfectly tailored to meet the customer’s requirements.

Recently, VIGAN invested in a

Phoenix FL-6020

laser cutting machine to cut larger pieces, eliminate welding work and add capacity for future growth. They also installed two

Easy-Form

320/40 press brakes synchronized for bending in tandem up to 8 meters. With the complete

CADMAN

®

software suite the company has full control over its production flow.

VIGAN is part of the Van De Wiele group.
