Fiber laser with rotary index offers high-speed processing of flat, shaped materials

The ENSIS 3015 RI from Amada is equipped with a 3-kW fiber laser and an integrated rotary index located on one of three shuttle pallets, allowing the operator to switch from flat sheet cutting to tube or pipe cutting in as little as two min.

The system uses a variable beam control unit that automatically adjusts the laser beam’s properties to cut a variety of thin to thick materials without a lens change. Optimal beam control enables the machine to process thin materials up to four times faster than a CO2 laser, the company reports.

Collector removes ultrafine airborne coolant mists

Camfil APC offers the EM Expert coolant mist collector for applications that use cooling lubricants such as turning, drilling, milling, roughing, and grinding. CAD/CAM software includes enhancements to part unfolding

VISI 2020.0 CAD/CAM from Hexagon Manufacturing Intelligence’s Production Software business helps improve mold and progressive-die design processes and features an enhanced Reverse module.

New part unfolding technology lets the user work directly on the original solid part without needing to extract the model’s skin. Sheet metal part recognition provides an improved graphical representation of the part analyzed by identifying bends, planar faces, and features.

The software’s associativity with the original model during the die design process allows the original part to be edited and changes to be made automatically on the study of the part. Also, the blank calculation will completely unfold all the detected features and coining faces.

Wet dust collector redesigned

Midwest Automation has introduced a newly redesigned line of Niagara wet dust collectors recently updated to clean or filter contaminated air in a variety of metal grinding, deburring, and sanding operations. Dust particles are separated from the air by mixing the contaminated air using water as a filtration media.

Baffles create turbulence and divergent forces that cause the metal dust to settle to the bottom of the tank as sludge. A stainless steel secondary filter helps prevent moisture-laden air or "mist" from entering the plant.

The wet dust collectors are available in three series with 15 models.
