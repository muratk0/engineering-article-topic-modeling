# Fibre laser with intelligent functions

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

Process reliability: Cut Control monitors the cutting process

FIBRE LASER TECHNOLOGY is an important technology for sheet metal processing companies and will remain so in the foreseeable future. Fibre laser cutting systems enable excellent cutting results, not only for thin sheet metal but also in the medium thickness range. This can be a decisive competitive advantage. If companies can cut rapidly with high quality, they are able to increase their parts’ output and hence produce more successfully.

To achieve the performance required for fast and high quality laser cuts, Bystronic has equipped its BySprint Fiber laser cutting system with a 6kW laser source. The powerful laser source considerably increases the BySprint Fiber's cutting speed by up to 70 per cent compared to the 4kW fibre laser when cutting 3mm thick stainless steel. The speed advantage is even more pronounced when compared to the 6kW CO laser – in 3mm thick stainless steel, the 6kW fibre laser cuts three times faster.

Bystronic has also provided intelligent additional functions for its laser cutting systems. One of these is Cut Control which monitors the entire cutting process on the BySprint Fiber and reduces the risk of mis-cuts. In the event of poor cutting results or cutting tears, Cut Control automatically stops the cutting process. Subsequently, the cutting head reverses and repeats the cut.

“With the ByOptimizer, we offer an online service that rapidly generates optimized cutting plans. The time-consuming tasks of programming and manually positioning the parts to be cut on the metal sheet are a thing of the past,” Bystronic told ISMR.

“ByOptimizer is based upon newly developed cluster technology, which automatically groups parts to be cut on the metal sheet. They are grouped so closely that the gaps, which represent raw material waste, are reduced to a minimum. This reduces the cutting paths required to subsequently cut the parts out of the metal sheet. The new online service can be used with both fibre lasers and CO lasers from Bystronic.”

The ByTrans Extended system is an automation unit from Bystronic that handles the entire loading and unloading process on the shuttle table of laser cutting systems. This enables users to speed up the material flow throughout all the processes associated with laser cutting to a maximum. ■
