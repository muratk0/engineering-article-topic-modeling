# Laser metal processing

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

JENOPTIK’S LASERS & Material Processing division and five companies in the Messer World demonstrated their latest breakthroughs in laser metal processing at EuroBLECH 2012 in Hanover, Germany, from 23 to 27 October 2012.

At EuroBLECH 2012, Jenoptik displayed its laser source JenLas® fiber cw 1000 which is a highly brilliant single-mode high power fibre laser system for rapid processing and consistently high productivity. This laser system is a continuous wave laser with an output power of 1,000 Watts.

“Also showcased at EuroBLECH was our robot-based 3D metal cutting laser machine, JENOPTIK-VOTAN® BIM. Its laser robot arm is special in that the optical fibre is located only in the stationary robot shoulder and the laser beam path is totally integrated. During the cutting process, the laser light is guided by mirrors. Due to the immobility of the laser fibre, its condition is preserved which eliminates any costly maintenance and replacement of the fibre. The slim and 5kg-light light laser cutting head also contributes to the accessibility into tight locations, high path accuracies and high speed 3D metal processing. Both features reduce the collision probability during the process. With a repeatability of ±100 µm, our laser machine is one of the most accurate on the market,” the manufacturer explained.

Other possible applications for the JENOPTIK-VOTAN BIM are 3D laser cutting of thin metal pieces, sophisticated profiles e.g. of the extraction system or an exhausted air system.
