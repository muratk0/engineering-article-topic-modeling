# Smart and connected solutions

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

At EuroBLECH2024 in Hanover (Germany) from 22-25 October 2024, Messer Cutting Systems presented a range of technologies. The manufacturer offers complete solutions from a single source that automate and optimise the entire material flow and information processes in production. Networked systems and machines work together, providing relevant information from production in real time.

Flexible and automated processes, it told ISMR, offer ideal conditions for mastering current and future challenges such as the shortage of skilled labour, energy transition and scarcity of resources.

## FIBERBLADE GU fibre laser cutter

The new FIBERBLADE GU, an entry-level machine for laser cutting, is a compact and powerful fibre laser cutting system. With a robust design, flexible application options and user-friendly operation, it has been designed to be efficient, rapid and economical.

"With the FIBERBLADE GU, we offer an economical but powerful solution for precise 2D cutting of standard sheetmetal. With three selectable laser powers (12kW, 20kW and 30kW) and four available sizes (3015, 4020, 6025 and 8025), the machine adapts to different production requirements," explained Abraham Balendran, Product Management, Messer Cutting Systems.

Mild steel, stainless steel or aluminium thicknesses, depending upon power selected, of up to 60mm can be processed. The different models cover cutting ranges from 3,000 × 1,500mm to 8,000 × 2,500mm. Components include the Precitec ProCutter-2.0 cutting head.

"The one-piece aluminium bridge construction gives the FIBERBLADE GU high rigidity combined with low weight for dynamic processing and increased cutting performance. The machine works with high acceleration and positioning speed for high efficiency and productivity, even for demanding cutting tasks. The parallel shuttle table also ensures smooth material changeover, significantly reducing production time," added the company.

A user-friendly user interface includes a large touchscreen for intuitive operation and simple control of the system. The 21.5-inch screen for the four process and room-monitoring cameras provides a view of the work area and an overview of the cutting process. The FIBERBLADE GU is compatible with OmniWin software for seamless integration into existing production processes.

"A special feature of the FIBERBLADE GU is its durability and ease of maintenance. Easy access to the components via large inspection hatches makes maintenance work considerably easier. Automatic lubrication of the sliding surfaces keeps the machine ready for use for longer and enables high performance. The sectional extraction system on both sides ensures a clean working environment," outlined Messer Cutting Systems.

## Digitalisation solutions

Under the motto 'Smart, Efficient, Connected', Messer Cutting Systems presented digitalisation solutions for the future of sheet metal processing. The cutting specialists from Groß-Umstadt demonstrated a fully connected production environment with seamlessly integrated production control, from part costing and quotation generation to automated and optimised production planning. At EuroBLECH 2024, the focus was Messer's newly integrated Advanced Planning and Scheduling System (APS).

"APS provides a precise overview of orders for efficient resource utilisation. The advanced calculation module, OmniFab Sales Quote, allows for accurate quotation costing, helping companies to analyse their material and production costs and remain competitive. Connected to the ERP system, quotations are calculated automatically, quickly and reliably. At a time when efficiency in manufacturing is becoming increasingly important, precise calculation of materials, components and processes is essential to stay competitive," said the manufacturer.

"Another highlight at EuroBLECH was the OmniWin 2024 CAD/CAM software. OmniWin is an easy-to-learn, clear design and nesting software that adapts intelligently to the machines and individual requirements. It handles all cutting tasks tailored to orderrelated production with CNC-controlled cutting machines. The latest version offers numerous innovations, including a revamped 3D component import, extended features (such as the ability to copy nesting plans) and improved work ergonomics through Dark Mode," it continued.

## Bevel cutting with BEVEL-U

"Rapidly increasing laser power, different bevel shapes, new cutting gases; innovative solutions are needed to ensure that lasercutting machines can meet today's demands for productivity and dynamics. We responded to these developments with the innovative ELEMENT L laser cutting machine. The centrepiece of the machine is the Bevel-U bevel unit, which was specially developed for the laser process," added the manufacturer.

Messer's ELEMENT L laser machine combines uniform piercing and faster cuts in laser precision cutting. It cuts mild steel, stainless steel and aluminium in working widths of between 1,600mm and 5,100mm and a working length of up to 25,000mm with O₂ and N₂ as well as an optional automatic gas mixing unit. A central component of the machine concept is the Bevel-U bevelling unit.

The Bevel-U enables bevel angles of between -50° and +50° as well as different edge shapes (I, V, Y, X, K) on the workpiece for a subsequent welding process. The bevel angle is infinitely adjustable during the laser cutting process. Magnetic collision protection for the cutting head is as much a part of the design of the bevelling unit as a manual control panel for all drives so that adjustments can be made directly on the machine.

“Even with material thicknesses of 50mm, Bevel-U can produce precise and repeatable bevelled parts with a wide variety of bevel contours in the highest cutting quality thanks to its unique design, a major advantage in edge preparation for automated robot welding. An automatic testing and calibration routine ensures consistently high quality, even after a nozzle change. Combined with the automatic nozzle changer Laser Nozzle Control (LNC), productivity can reach a new level thanks to fully automatic management of the cutting nozzles," outlined the cutting specialist.

## Automated material handling

With its ICSORT system, Messer Cutting Systems also presented a material handling solution for sheet metal processing at this year's EuroBLECH. In combination with Messer's cutting systems and own software, ICSORT creates an efficient production environment. During the live demonstration of ICSORT at the trade fair stand, trade visitors were able to see the integrated software and hardware solutions in practice.

"The ICSORT system automates all loading, unloading and sorting tasks. This allows the user to maximise machine utilisation. Due to its modular design, the system can be customised to individual customer requirements. It is suitable for precisely positioning large raw materials on the cutting table and for sorting small cut parts quickly and efficiently. This is made possible by 40 individually controllable magnets which can be flexibly adapted to all mild steel moulds and ensure the safe movement of parts," explained the manufacturer.

"With two bridges and the powerful magnet units, ICSORT can handle even large components (regardless of their dimensions or material thickness). The specially developed OmniFab software package also allows work processes to be optimised and controlled from the workstation," it concluded.
