# LASYS 2022 returns to Stuttgart

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

The LASYS trade fair in Stuttgart, Germany, focuses on the innovative strength present in laser material processing. Laser systems, services, methods and materials all come under the spotlight as LASYS showcases the entire range of applications for lasers in material processing. From 21-23 June 2022, trade visitors will once again be able to experience the event live in person.

“The transformation of the automotive industry towards e-mobility is creating huge changes in terms of the way the industry works. Even if many components (such as the bodywork and interior) remain the same, new markets are opening for which material processing using lasers represents the only efficient method of series production. For example, lasers can cut ultra-thin copper and aluminium films for the batteries, weld the copper coils for the electric motor and electronics together and cut an exceptionally wide range of materials such as aluminium alloys, plastics and lightweight materials to size," commented the show organiser.

"We are responding to the changes in the industry with innovations for the manufacture of high-quality batteries, electric motors and power electronics as well as lightweight construction. Laser material processing is a technology that enables us to offer precise and efficient solutions for almost all manufacturing processes," added Thomas Fehn, Chief Sales and Service Officer, TRUMPF Laser Technology (which will be exhibiting at the show).

Based at Stuttgart's trade fair grounds (Hall 4), LASYS will also host the Stuttgart Laser Days (SLT) conference on 21 and 22

June 2022. The SLT days are organised by the IFSW Institute for Laser Technology at the University of Stuttgart, which pursues holistic research approach ranging from laser beam sources to application in manufacturing processes. Alongside the SLT, the technical forum “Lasers in Action“ will run three days of practice-based lectures live in the exhibition hall.■
