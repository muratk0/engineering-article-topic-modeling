# Plasma cutting and gouging

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

VICTOR® THERMAL DYNAMICS, a producer of plasma cutting systems, has launched its new PAK-200i manual plasma cutting and gouging system, designed to meet demanding manual plasma cutting applications.

The PAK-200i provides 200 Amperes of cutting power for quality cuts on up to 40mm mild steel, with a maximum cut capacity of 70mm. With a 100 per cent duty-cycle at full output, this system is capable of operating at full power all day. A liquid-cooled torch provides significantly longer parts life, and the Tip Saver™ circuit ensures accidental contact between the tip and workpiece at high power levels will not result in damage. This system features a dual-gas capability, for cutting stainless steel with an argon-hydrogen (H35) plasma gas and nitrogen shield gas, to produce weld-ready cuts.

“Plasma gouging with the PAK-200i offers faster removal rates – up to 11.3kg per hour compared with conventional air-carbon arc gouging. The operator has a clear view of the gouging area and benefits from reduced noise and smoke emissions. A range of specialised gouging torch consumables maximises parts life during gouging operations,” the company explained.

Plasma gouging is suitable for a wide variety of applications, including weld back-gouging, removing hard-facing materials and preparing worn joints for re-welding. The PAK-200i is available with either the manual PCH-200 torch, with a choice of 70° or 90° torch head, or with the 180° PCM-200 mechanised torch, for automatic cutting and gouging operations.
