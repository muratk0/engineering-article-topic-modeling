# NEW FIBRE LASER

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

At the EuroBLECH 2010 exhibition in Hanover this October 2010, Hypertherm featured its latest technology including the company’s first fibre laser system and two of the most versatile Powermax systems ever made. These were demonstrated live on its stand.

“Hypertherm’s new HyIntensity Fibre Laser HFL015 system is unique in that all of the components are engineered and designed to work together as one complete cutting system. Unlike products from other fibre laser manufacturers, Hypertherm’s system includes the power source, cutting head, gas supply, operator interface consoles, motion controls and software. Additionally, the system operates on familiar Hypertherm control platforms, with pre-developed cutting processes, for significantly simplified table integration and operation,” said the company.

In addition, two new Powermax systems made their EuroBLECH debut. Hypertherm’s Powermax65 and Powermax85 come with more torch options and a more rugged torch design for greater durability in difficult cutting environments. Available torch options are an ergonomic 75-degree hand torch, a 15-degree hand torch for gouging and cutting in tight locations, a 38cm full-length machine torch and a 15cm short barreled machine torch for robotic and pipe-cutting applications.

## E-F@CTORY

To remain competitive, the manufacturing industry is employing more IT systems, increasing the amount of shop floor information that has to be integrated into wider company systems; from advanced plant control and monitoring, through to integrated production, accounts and purchasing systems. Without a unified solution, which provides a transparent view of the business and manufacturing systems, the sheer volume and diversity of data required can sometimes overwhelm system users.

Mitsubishi Electric’s e-F@ctory is its vision for manufacturing that unifies its leading control hardware and network technologies with enterprise IT systems offered by strategic partner companies. The e-F@ctory concept was born out of the expertise Mitsubishi developed in-house through facing its own challenges as a global manufacturing enterprise. The technology provides the tools to immediately improve almost any enterprise’s bottom line through increased productivity, shorter lead-times and reduced waste.

e-F@ctory integrates all Mitsubishi Electric’s factory automation products; stores and processes data from existing controller systems; makes the Shop Floor to Top Floor principle a reality; is modular and scalable and provides tailor-made modular solutions, geared to the individual needs of a specific automation task.
