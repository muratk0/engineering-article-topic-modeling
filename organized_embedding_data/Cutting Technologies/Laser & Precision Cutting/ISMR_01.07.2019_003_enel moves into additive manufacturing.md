# Enel moves into additive manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

THIS APRIL IN Cavriglia (Italy), at Enel Produzione's Engineering and Technical Support Centre in Santa Barbara, Enel and Prima Additive officially inaugurated the new Laserdyne® 795. The machine, recently installed at the factory, will enable the production and repair of metal components.

"Laserdyne® 795 is a machine equipped with Laser Metal Deposition technology, developed by Prima Additive and customised to the requirements of the energy production sector, in cooperation with Enel, the main energy producer and supplier in Italy.

"Enel's choice of a direct energy deposition machine was determined by the flexibility of this solution, in terms of production and repair. This type of application enables it to reduce warehouse time, reduce downtime and save money, allowing the repair and re-use of the same component. Before the purchase, Enel evaluated several case studies relating to the efficiency of components and spare parts on the efficiency and availability of power plants, in addition to the number, average stock, economic value and types of damage to parts. This tool, embracing the main concepts of the Circular Economy, Industry 4.0 and sustainability, allows it to repair and revamp damaged components, as well as create complex shapes and re-engineered parts," Prima Additive told ISMR.

The Laserdyne® 795 features the 5-axis BeamDirector® laser head, combined with a roto-tilting table, to produce parts using up to seven degrees of freedom. This system offers two different deposition modes; the first involves the rotation of the part during the process, while the second involves the laser head moving along the entire profile of the element. The working area is 1000x1000x1000mm.

"The laser head has been developed specifically for direct energy deposition applications, and is equipped with four separate nozzles which guarantee uniform distribution of the powder at the melting point. The external powder supply system allows up to two materials to be processed simultaneously," added Prima Additive.
