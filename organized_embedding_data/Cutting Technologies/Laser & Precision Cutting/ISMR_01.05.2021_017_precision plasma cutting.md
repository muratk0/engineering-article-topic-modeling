# Precision plasma cutting

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

Sheet metal, plate cutting and profiling experts, Kerf Developments, has an extensive range of oxy-fuel, plasma and waterjet cutting technology. The 2020 Version of its UltraSharp plasma cutting technology utilises proven material databases to calculate appropriate cutting strategies, identifying the most suitable speeds and feeds, gas pressures and applying appropriate lead-in and lead-out strategies.

"Calculating and combining all these strategies, UltraSharp offers customers high cutting speeds, consistent cut quality, excellent edge finish and downstream productivity benefits. The latest enhancements will appeal to those involved in structural steelwork and general fabricators that need 'bolt-ready' holes, slots and high-quality profile cut edges," said the UK manufacturer. Kerf's RUR2500GP is a combination machine that offers precision plasma cutting via a Lincoln Electric Spirit 400amp system with UltraSharp cut quality. This is complimented by an oxy-fuel cutting head for cutting material up to 150mm thick.

"Our Plasmaster 3015 compact profiling machine bridges the gap between our high-end RUR machines and our refurbished machines. Significantly improved with new technology since its introduction, the enhanced Plasmaster 3015 targets smaller businesses wanting to cut everything from thin materials, such as ductwork, through to heavy-duty fabricators needing to cut thicker materials up to 25mm," it told ISMR.

The Plasmaster machine consists of a heavy-duty plate support system that contains individual dross bins that can be easily accessed to remove waste material. It supports a plate size of 3.0m x 1.5m and up to 25mm thick. The engineers at Kerf designed the base to be free-standing and not connected to the machine frame. This eliminates any potential shock loading issues and is designed to significantly enhance process reliability.
