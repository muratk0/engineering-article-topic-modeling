The waterjet’s ability to process a wide range of materials and thicknesses makes it a flexible tool in today’s workshops

Waterjet cutting technology is one of the fastest growing major machine tool processes in the world due to its versatility and ease of operation. Manufacturers are swiftly realizing that there are few limits to what waterjets can cut.

The waterjet’s ability to process a wide range of materials from metals to non-metals and soft materials makes it a flexible tool in today’s workshops. The major benefits of water jet cutting include no heat-generated structural changes in the material being cut; virtually burr-free cut edges (for reduced finishing operations); no material discoloration; no toxic or fume discharge; high material yield (for greater cost savings) and multi-head operation (for competitive cutting of flat material).

## A WATERPROOF INVESTMENT

Waterjet is a cold cutting process – edges do not harden, stresses do not form and there is no discoloration to the material. The systems can handle large material thicknesses, with no melting (leaving crisp sharp corners and a burr-free finish) and there are no restrictions on hole sizes or intricate work. In addition, waterjet only removes the jet width of material (1mm) - full removal of material is no longer required, saving time, material and costs. Its accuracy, as a process, is a testament to the fact that secondary processes are not usually required.

The ByJet Classic 3015 waterjet cutting system, which was launched in July last year, is Bystronic’s answer to its customers’ margins that have come under pressure as a result of the economic crisis.

“The ByJet Classic 3015 will be of interest to all existing as well as new waterjet cutting machine users who require high functionality of the system coupled with precise cutting results and who simultaneously place value on a manageable investment level,” the company told ISMR.

The Byjet Classic 3015 is a highly functional and precise waterjet cutting system for all standard applications (cutting range 3048 x 1524mm). The permanent nozzle distance regulation and the integrated collision sensor help to secure an economical, reliable and high-quality cutting process. A CNC-controlled drill spindle allows the pre-drilling of expensive composite materials and other laminated materials - this effectively avoids delaminations.

Flow International has just launched its newest Mach 4 waterjet system, the flagship of the Mach series, which combines Flow’s HyperJet pump, rated at 94,000 psi, and Dynamic Waterjet XD, high-precision flat stock and 3D cutting technology, to improve part cycle time and accuracy ‘two to four times over standard non-dynamic systems’, according to the manufacturer.

Flow invented Dynamic Waterjet to virtually eliminate stream lag and taper produced by all waterjets. Flow’s JetPlex 55,000 psi pump and FlowMaster software is specifically designed to optimize waterjet cutting. A (6-) six-axes pedestal robot, designed by Kuka (Flow’s partner), will be on display at this year’s IMTS exhibition outfitted with a Flow Abrasive Waterjet end-effector that is suitable for three-dimensional trimming and drilling.

Flow International Corporation has also just launched the Paser 4 Ultra Component Life (UCL) abrasive waterjet cutting head. The Paser 4 UCL is designed to improve cutting performance and lower operating cost.

“Orifice life is increased 3 to 5 times while abrasive consumption is reduced, resulting in substantial savings. Offered as an upgrade on most waterjet machines, the Paser 4 is available with a variety of orifice and mixing tube combinations to match today’s common applications,” Flow told ISMR.

International Waterjet Machines has just launched the WPCxxyyA5 series machine - a versatile waterjet pipe cutting system. It cuts pipes using high pressure water jet.

The wall thickness of pipe that can be cut, and cutting speed, depends on the type of material. The waterjet pump is 50 hp and, when cutting with abrasive, the maximum thickness of mild steel is 60mm. The pump requires 3-phase electricity power supply of 220V - 480V, 50 or 60 Hz (specified by the customer). The pressurized water is fed to the cutting head through high-pressure whip tubing.
