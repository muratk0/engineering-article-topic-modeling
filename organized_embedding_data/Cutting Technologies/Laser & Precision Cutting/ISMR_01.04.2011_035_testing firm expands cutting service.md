# TESTING FIRM EXPANDS CUTTING SERVICE

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

EXOVA, THE GLOBAL testing and advisory business, has expanded its metallurgy testing service after investing in new cutting machinery. The Exova Edinburgh laboratory has invested over £15,000 in a new Buehler Powermet 3000 automatic cut-off machine which is capable of accurately producing 1mm sampling of macro and micro specimens. The installation of the robust cutter now means that the Edinburgh facility can offer this service for the first time. The machinery can be used in a wide range of applications in metallographic and for cutting requirements in pipeline production.

“This new machinery allows pipes to be cut up into micro slices so that the exact location of the welding defect can be identified,” said the manufacturer.

This new testing service enhances Exova’s on-site capabilities which include non destructive evaluation; positive materials identification; ferritescope; replication; hardness testing; portable hardness testing; metallographic; witness welder qualification; expert witness services; metallurgical consultancy services; plant life assessment; stress analysis and risk-based maintenance.

## METAL SPRAYING ONLINE

METALLISATION LTD has published a series of videos demonstrating the capabilities and diverse applications of metal spraying. The videos are available to view, free of charge, on the Metallisation website www.metallisation.com.

The first five videos: ‘Flame Spraying’; ‘Arc Spraying’; ‘ARC140 deflected Extension’; ‘20m cored Wire Spraying’ and ‘deflected Spray Extension – Zinc on RNLI chassis’, are available to view now with other videos planned for the future. The video library is intended to show a range of products and applications. These include a summary of the main thermal spray processes, a 20m extended supplies pack, ARC140 system spraying cored wires and a deflected flame spray extension applying zinc to difficult access areas on vehicle chassis.

## ELECTRIC CHAIN HOIST

## APRIL 2011

A NEW RANGE of electric chain hoists, in capacities ranging from 125 to 5000 kg has been launched by Street Crane Company, the UK’s largest crane manufacturer. Designed for durability and high productivity, the LX electric chain hoist gives precise and safe lifting for a range of industries.

“The new hoists are aimed at the volume market and are ideal for localised workstations and production line applications where they might typically be used on jibs, monorails and light track crane systems,” said Street Crane managing director, Andrew Pimblet.

Safety is a key consideration. Overload protection is provided by a slipping clutch and models feature high performance disk brakes. Plug and socket electrical connections are an option to simplify final installation. Where required, the range of lateral travel can be controlled by the use of limit switches.

“Low headroom hoists have a chain diverter so that the hook rises to the side of the motor gearbox to give an exceptionally low profile. Where headroom or the available operating envelope for the hoist is a more serious issue, hoists can be installed between the beams of a double beam crane to create a greater clear working height. Control options include 48Volt standard pendant or remote operation by radio device. Control units can be set-up for solo operation or to work twin hoists for tandem lifts,” said the company.
