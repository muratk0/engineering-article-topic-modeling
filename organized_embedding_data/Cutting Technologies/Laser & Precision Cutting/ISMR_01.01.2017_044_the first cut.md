# The first cut

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

Given a suitable interface, Messer customers can collect the machine data with the 'Global Reporter' on Messer's own brand CNC, the Global Control, and display it on a smart device.

MESSER'S MULTITHERM® CUTTING machine combines three processes - laser cutting, plasma cutting and ink-jet marking within one nested plan.

"For example, internal contours can be precisely cut with the fibre laser and external contours can be cut rapidly and economically with the plasma torch. Markings, charge numbers etc. are added with the inkjet with no damage to the material. This simplifies cataloguing the individual parts and produces advantages for traceability," Messer told ISMR. The company's MetalMaster 2.0 cutting machine can be integrated into tight spaces, requiring an area of just 30m². With its combination of plasma and oxyfuel cutting, it is suitable for a wide range of material types and thicknesses.

"Short production cycles, the highest quality standards and increasing cost pressures demand continuous readiness to innovate, to keep up with current and future demands. Many users wish to be able to call up the machine status from any given location at any time, regardless of whether from PC, tablet or smartphone. In this way, machine downtimes can be recognised immediately and corrected more rapidly. The evaluation of the actual cutting times gives planning reliability and yields data about the general productivity of the processes. We give our customers the appropriate interface for this and can show how the machine data is captured with the 'Global Reporter' software on their own brand control Global Control and displayed on a smart device," said Messer Cutting Systems.
