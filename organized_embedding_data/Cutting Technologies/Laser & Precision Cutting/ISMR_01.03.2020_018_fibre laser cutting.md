The use of fibres eliminates the need for elaborate and costly optics for beam guidance and the space requirement can be downsized. This also makes fibre lasers very stable and easy to use. The beam quality is high and continuously becoming more powerful. Fibre lasers are also very efficient and easy to cool, converting around 70-80% of the power from the pump source. This is better for power consumption, but also means that less power is being converted to heat. Fibre lasers can be comfortably used for the efficient processing of gauge and fractional materials and fine feature cutting. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new fibre laser cutting innovations in the market today.

## Fibre in focus

CYLASER has been pioneering the application of solid-state fibre optic laser technology for industrial cutting applications since 2004. Today, and hundreds of installations later, it has refined and improved its original vision of a robust, precise, efficient and productive fibre laser cutting system with two laser cutting processes, Vortex and Vega (designed to offer excellent cut quality). It showcased a new machine with linear motors at the LAMIERA exhibition in Milan in 2019 (linear motors give users more control over the cutting speed). It is also in the process of developing a new bevel head to increase cutting possibilities.

The Vega cutting process exploits laser beam diameter and a Programmable Shape laser beam enables users to automatically select the laser spot dimension with the same optics set-up. It is designed to increase the cutting quality for any metal, substantially reduce the cutting roughness on stainless steel and increase the quality of the cut on thicker materials. This includes enhanced cut quality on stainless steel and aluminium. Vortex is a process that is designed to enable users to obtain higher cutting quality on >8mm material thicknesses. With the Vortex feature, the user can take full advantage of fibre laser's potential through beam emission frequency modulation and in-kerf gas flow management. Benefits include lower roughness on stainless steel cutting edge; reduced gas consumption in comparison to standard parameters; lower material heating (so that most complicated patterns can be cut) and easier part extraction from scrap.

CYLASER's wide fibre laser cutting product portfolio includes 2D laser cutting machines (some with tube processing capabilities); tube laser cutting systems and 3D lasercutting machines. Machines range from 2.5m x 1.25m to 9m x 3m. All CYLASER machines are powered with up to 12kW fibre optic generators. The company also offers a variety of Load Unload Automation Systems and Material Storage Towers with automatic material retrieval, loading and unloading functions for lights-out operation.
