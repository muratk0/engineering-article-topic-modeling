# Laser-cutting solutions

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

BLM GROUP presented its latest sheet laser-cutting system at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany, in October 2022. The new LS7 is a fully redesigned and optimised system to process sheet metal that makes a clean break with the recent past of ADIGE-SYS, featuring combined systems to process sheet and tube.

“The goal was to supply superior performance at the lowest possible price and it has been achieved by focusing on sheet-metal cutting functions only and eliminating all the elements previously necessary to integrate tube processing. In an increasingly competitive market, the part cost is a fundamental aspect and it is necessary to have the right machine for each specific production process. Only in this way can costs be truly optimised," commented Mauricio Gutierrez Matta, Global Business Development Manager, Sheet Metal Lasers, BLM GROUP.

The LS7 was designed as an entry-level product in the high-end system segment. It has a conventional aluminium mobile gantry structure actuated by a rack and pinion system, which slides on a strong metal structure, filled with polymer concrete to ensure high rigidity even with high accelerations of up to 2G.

The cutting area has been designed to protect the bellows from the glowing sparks thrown in all directions, especially during piercing. Access is only from the short side and the entire machine remains completely enclosed for safety reasons related to the fibre laser source. The cutting process can be viewed by a camera, with the image visible remotely, on the operator console and on an additional external monitor.

“Pallet change takes place on the long side in just nine seconds using a smart mechanical solution which anticipates the simultaneous exchange of the two tables. Handling is fully electric, without pneumatic or hydraulic actuators. LS7 is available in both 3000 × 1500 and 4000 x 2000 versions. Additional options include various loading-unloading automation solutions with one or two towers. The system is designed for easy transport. Installation takes just one week, including training," added the Italian sheet-metal specialist.

At EuroBLECH, along with the LS7, BLM GROUP also exhibited a wide range of technological solutions, including the LT8.20 Lasertube; the LS5 sheet-metal, laser-cutting machine and the LT360 flexible and compact robot laser-cutting cell.
