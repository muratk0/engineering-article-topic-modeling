# More cutting power

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

TRUE TO ITS MOTTO, “More power for cutting”, ROFIN will present the DC 060, a CO2 slab laser with 6kW cutting performance for the first time at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition (Hall 11, Booth A46). ROFIN is continuing to develop its product portfolio for the cutting market towards higher cutting performances for thicker sheet metals and, it told ISMR, is the only laser manufacturer that offers CO2 as well as fiber lasers in this high power range.

“We are glad to enlarge our CO2 product range with this new model,” stated Thorsten Frauenpreiß, Managing Director of ROFIN-SINAR Laser GmbH. “This laser provides new possibilities for all cutting applications and is an ideal complement to our high power fiber laser portfolio. We are convinced that we can further expand and strengthen our market position in laser cutting.”

The laser operates with only a few extremely durable components and does not need conventional gas recirculation – leading to a considerable reduction of lifetime costs. The energy-saving standby operation leads to minimal energy consumption. The entire DC Series is provided with the “Rofin Control Unit” (RCU) which enables extended monitoring and control functions, resulting in improved pulse-to-pulse stability, manifold control and log functions including E-Service. ■

## ON A ROLL

THE FEA (Finite Element Analysis) simulation of the roll forming process is becoming more and more important if the designed rolls have to be checked before manufacturing, to check whether they are able to produce the desired product. For this purpose, UBECO PROFIL roll form design software now has an interface to the FEA system, LS-DYNA. A new feature of the software version is a powerful postprocessor that enables a user to evaluate the results of the FEA simulation.

Sectional views of the sheet within the rolls, at any machine position or after leaving the machine, are created by key-press. The rolls can be inserted (as 2D cross section or as 3D solids) to check the position of the sheet within the roll tools. Inserting the designed flower pattern enables target/actual comparison. Stress and strain are shown by colours in the FEA view. PROFIL also creates graphs of the stress, strain and thickness reduction as a function of the sheet position in the machine. These functions are then tailored to the special needs of the roll form designer and help to evaluate the FEA result.

FEA simulation is a part of the three-step quality management concept for roll form tool designers. Other steps are: “Stress of Band Edge” that works simultaneously while designing the flower pattern and “Profile Stress Analysis” (PSA) for calculating the estimated stress within the whole profile cross-section.
