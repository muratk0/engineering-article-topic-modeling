Fiber laser cutting machine cuts mild steel up to 4 in. thick

Machitech has introduced the new Fiber Prime S laser cutting machine. It combines high-speed 4G acceleration with 12 to 60 kW for cutting mild steel up to 4 in. thick.

The machine, which handles a variety of heavy-duty applications, includes a Precitec cutting head that provides integrated gas mixing and focused compensation. The system also features the optional Machitech bevel head for creating complex bevel cuts, including V, X, Y, and K, with custom angles in a single operation.

Moment connection made for steel construction in wind-governed areas

Simpson Strong-Tie has expanded its line of lateral strengthening systems for steel construction with the introduction of the Strong-Link moment connection. Engineered for wind-governed and low-seismic structures, the fully bolted unit eliminates the need for field welding and helps speed frame installation, the company reports.

The moment connection can be adjusted in the field using standard steel erection practices. Oversized holes in the link flange help ensure optimal alignment at the connection interface between the beam and column.

A notched section enables it to function without interfering with the shear tabs. This notch allows the link to be used with standard-depth shear tabs, which helps simplify the connection design and maintain the overall strength and reliability of the structural system, says the company.

Low-profile magnetic drill fits in tight workspaces

Blair Equipment Co. has launched the BFD-KX+ right-angle magnetic drill, designed for accessibility in tight workspaces. It enables operators to drill in areas that are hard to reach without compromising on performance or durability, the company states.

At 6 15/32 in. tall, the drill is suitable for confined spaces where standard drills cannot operate. The high-torque motor helps ensure consistent drilling performance on tough materials. It uses Karbon X+ annular cutters and features a magnetic base for steady drilling operations. The simple, intuitive control panel allows for easy operation, the manufacturer reports.

Voltage-sensing wire feeder offers remote voltage, wire feed speed adjustment

ESAB offers the RobustFeed AVS Echo automatic voltage-sensing wire feeder. The company’s new Echo communication technology allows voltage and wire feed speed adjustment at the feeder without a communication/control cable to the power source. The feeder works with the Warrior 500i CC/CV multiprocess welding machine with the Echo technology upgrade option.

On the first weld, the system performs a measurement and compensates for any losses that occur in the welding circuit for all the subsequent welds. Compensating for voltage drop helps improve accuracy when using long cables.

The feeder can handle a maximum output of 500 amps and a duty cycle rating of 100% at 350 amps.

AI-powered software helps accelerate quoting of complex assemblies

Paperless Parts has announced the general availability of its new BOM Builder. The software is designed to help make the process of building out the bills of materials (BOMs) for complex assemblies fast and secure, even when RFQ packages only include 2D drawings.

With the new AI-powered program, estimators can automatically create, organize, and update multilevel assemblies using a flexible, spreadsheet-style interface.

Features include BOM table extraction from prints or spreadsheets and a flexible BOM structure that allows the BOM to be built in any order—by part number, assembly group, or customer documentation structure. Smart purchased part mapping helps users indicate which parts are purchased in bulk and quickly associate any hardware with purchased component records.

Welding line helps fabricators create structural beams from steel plate

The Bendmak BHW beam welding line allows structural fabricators to create their own beams to size from steel plate. This completely automated system provides easy alignment of the web and flange, the company reports.

Available with single, twin, or tandem welding heads, it includes motorized and hydraulic side supports to prevent distortion while accommodating beam sizes up to 79 by 40 in.
