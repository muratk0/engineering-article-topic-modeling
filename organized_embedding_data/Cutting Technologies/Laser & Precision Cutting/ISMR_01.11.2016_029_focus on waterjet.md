# FOCUS ON WATERJET

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

OMAX® Corporation showcased the flexibility and efficiency of its abrasive waterjets at the EuroBLECH exhibition this year in Hanover, Germany. The company ran live cutting demonstrations on its OMAX 60120 and MAXIEM® 1515 JetMachining® Centers.

"On the 60120 machine, a Tilt-A-Jet cutting head highlights how taper-free cutting can be achieved without slowing down cutting speed, ideal for work requiring tight tolerances and quick turn-around times. The MAXIEM 1515 was fitted with an A-Jet cutting head, proficient at cutting bevelled edges, angled sides and creating complex 3D shapes. The waterjets in both the OMAX and MAXIEM lines are capable of machining virtually any material with no heat-affected zone and no need for tool changes," said OMAX.

TECHNI Waterjet revealed its latest product, the Generation 4 Quantum NXT (Electric Servo Pump), at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition. The Quantum NXT will be available as a Quantum ESP 37Kw/55 (55,000 psi) and ESP 37Kw/66 (66,000 psi). It is designed to accommodate the company's Advanced Diagnostics preventative maintenance programme.

"The Advanced Diagnostics Package will ensure the Quantum (ESP) will perform at its capacity without unplanned downtime due to general maintenance. The package is designed to protect the Quantum Electric Servo Pump against damage due to poor maintenance issues and unforeseen utility failures. By simply changing the mindset of reactive maintenance to pro-active maintenance, it will ensure that business owners achieve the maximum return on their investment. This Advanced Diagnostics package will help an operator maximize the output of the ESP without causing unnecessary maintenance downtime by diagnosing potential maintenance issues early, and then advise the operator how to perform the maintenance," said the company.

WARDJet Inc. is a US manufacturer of over 30 standard size waterjet cutting machines. It designs and builds custom waterjets integrated into complex processes. Standard waterjets range from 2'x 2' to 40' x 17' with custom sizes available. It has built machines with fifteen cutting heads on one cross beam, and can install as many as nine 5-axis cutting heads on a single cross beam. Its patented WARD Pro is an abrasive recycling system.

It recently introduced the new WARDKit Series of waterjet cutting systems, a waterjet system that it currently sells in kit form. The WARDKit is virtually identical to its E-Series of waterjets but costs about 40% less.

It also launched the mid-sized E-2040 with a 6 ft x 6ft cutting envelope capable of handling a variety of common sheet sizes.

"Set-up improvements allow us to machine the E-2040 more efficiently than ever before, which means shorter lead times for our customers. Perhaps one of the biggest feature improvements we've made to the E-2040 has to do with the crossbeam. Due to the new design, cutting speeds can reach north of 750 inches per minute. On top of that, you can add almost any cutting head configuration to your E-2040 to tailor the waterjet to your specific business needs, including multiple 5-Axis or taper compensation cutting heads," said WardJet.

With over 30 years of experience in water cutting, Water Jet Sweden is one of Europe's largest manufacturers of waterjet cutting machines. A new innovation is the first abrasive water jet machine with eighteen different axes moving simultaneously, as well as four standard cutting heads and two 5-axis "Beveljet 60" heads, adding up to six individually-controlled cutting units. WJS also introduced a new product range with standard machines but at a lower price.

"We have identified an increased demand for our machines but with features and pricing for more fundamental cutting needs," explained Lennart Svensson, CEO, Water Jet Sweden. To do this, the company has been developing a new machine platform. The new machine systems can be purchased for less than €100,000 which is a new price level for Water Jet Sweden.

Water Jet Sweden also unveiled a compact height sensor to keep the correct distance between the water jet cutting head and the material at last year's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart. It has also broken new ground by introducing an extended machine warranty guaranteeing new machine performance over five years of usage. The extended warranty comprises all Water Jet Sweden's gantry machine models ordered after 1 May 2015 and covers the frame and moving parts of the mechanical system. It spans 5 years or 10,000 hours of operation.

Italian manufacturer, Waterjet Corporation Srl, recently showcased its EDGE 5 cutting system with integrated ITC. It has been building and supplying thousands machines since 1991 from its headquarters and manufacturing facilities in Monza, Italy. Its machines include cantilever and bridge designs and 5-axis flying bridge technology (Edge 5) which delivers significant productivity improvements. The company also offers combination plasma and waterjet machines as well as waterjet surface texturing technology.

Lantek recently reached a global agreement to supply its Lantek Expert CAD/CAM software with the Waterjet Corporation's range of waterjet cutting machines.

With a 4,000bar operating pressure yet low energy consumption, the new Woma EcoJet 70M 4000/4 is an ultrahigh pressure, direct-drive pump for waterjet cutting system operators. Its compact dimensions mean that the unit with its plunger pump is easily installed in new or existing systems.

Using a new flocculant, the new WTA Eco water treatment system enables waterjet cutting system operators to discharge their wastewater into the sewage for the first time in an eco-friendly way that complies, it says, with statutory regulations. The WTA Eco can be retrofitted into existing filtration systems.
