Cold saws deliver high-speed cutting

Universal Tube & Rollform Equipment Co. offers new high-speed cold saws from ERWTech and UCG for burr-free cutting of tubes, pipes, and shapes up to 16 in. OD. Laser offered with 8-kW high-power-density source

Salvagnini now offers its L5 laser with an 8-kW high-power-density source, which improves cutting acceleration for better performance on medium-thin material thicknesses. According to the manufacturer, the 8-kW source achieves greater cutting speeds than a 10-kW source on thicknesses up to 6 mm.

Reducing the power of the source while keeping cutting speeds high reduces energy and gas consumption for the same performance, the company reports.

For small batch sizes that need rapid automation or masked-time production changes, the machine can be fed from an LTWS store tower equipped with automated loading/unloading. The tower makes different materials and thicknesses available for JIT production, reducing waiting times for sheet metal feeding and operator dependency.

Salvagnini

www.salvagnini-america.com

Copper coating on welding wire helps reduce clogging, flaking

ESAB has introduced an enhanced version of its Purus copper-coated solid wire for high-volume and robotic MIG and pulsed MIG welding applications. According to the company, the wire has an optimized copper coating application that reduces contact tip bore wear, copper flaking, and clogged tips and liners.

The wire is available in diameters from 0.030 to 0.0625 in. and sold in 40-lb. spools, or 550- and 1,100-lb. Marathon Pac bulk wire systems. Purus 42 is classified as an AWS A5.18 ER70S-6/EN ISO 14341-A G3Si1 wire. Purus 46 is classified as an AWS A5.18 ER70S-6/EN ISO 14341-A G4Si1 solid wire; it has a slightly higher manganese and silicon content than Purus 42 to increase the weld metal strength.

System detects, corrects programming issues before coil is cut

LaserCoil Technologies LLC has introduced its Virtual Controller, which can run the company’s coil-fed laser blanking systems virtually using true production rates. It helps optimize nesting patterns and detects programming issues before the program is run with actual material.

With the company’s LaserCoilCAM software, even inexperienced operators can use the controller to create an optimized program. The operator selects material type and thickness, and critical cutting parameters are imported based on the material parameters. A 2D CAD file is imported, and the nesting pattern is created for a material width that optimizes material usage, or the operator can input a specific coil width. The operator then selects features to be cut with each laser cutting head.

www.lasercoil.com

Automatic upcut circular cold saw has large cutting capacity

Scotchman Industries has introduced the GAA-600-90 CNC automatic upcut circular cold saw. The large-capacity saw has a 24-in.-dia. carbide blade for cutting nonferrous materials at 90 degrees up to 8 in. square or round and 14- by 4-in. rectangle.

The CNC stores 10 programs, with each program containing up to 30 different cut lengths and quantities that can be created or adjusted on the machine at any time.

The saw features a shuttle feed design with a 27-in. ballscrew servomotor automatic feeder, and adjustable length selector from 0.200 to 20 in., unlimited stroke, and adjustable cutting and feeding speeds.

Gas analyzer made for service calls, continuous analysis

The new MFA 10.0 multigas analyzer from Witt can determine the composition of 15 different gas mixtures. The compact, lightweight device is suitable for onsite service purposes, but it also can be used for continuous analysis.

The unit analyzes various two-gas mixtures of argon, helium, carbon dioxide, methane, oxygen, nitrogen, and hydrogen. The reading is carried out in seconds using the thermal conductivity measurement principle.

The analyzer is operated via a 7-in. touchscreen with password protection.

Six-axis robot weighs 7 kg

Yaskawa Motoman offers the MotoMini, which the company says is the smallest, lightest-weight 6-axis robot in the industry. Its 7-kg weight facilitates portability and installation, making it suitable for table-top-, floor-, ceiling-, tilt-, or wall-mount installations.

The robot’s 0.5-kg payload supports a variety of tooling and sensors to fulfill diverse project needs. It can be used for applications such as assembly, dispensing, inspection, kitting, machine tending, material handling, packaging, parts feeding, sorting, and STEM education.

The compact, 191- by 124-mm footprint allows the robot to be mounted close to workpieces and other machinery in existing lines or cells.
