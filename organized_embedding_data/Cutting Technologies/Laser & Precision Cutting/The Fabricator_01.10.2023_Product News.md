Gas mixer maintains gas purity required for laser cutting

The Witt MG100-ME HD ERC+ gas mixer for oxygen/nitrogen mixtures is designed for high process reliability and ease of operation in laser cutting applications, says the company.

The electropneumatic valve-operated mixer can be operated locally via touchscreen or remotely via interface with optional connections and software.

Adjustable or preset mixing ranges from 0 to 5% up to 0 to 95% are available for precise mixing.

Three fiber laser cutting systems introduced

Bystronic offers the new ByCut series fiber laser cutting systems. It includes three all-new, high-precision models.

The ByCut Star 3015 delivers 30-kW laser power. It offers a new design, laser cutting with intelligence, stable cutting to the highest powers, and an expanded range of applications, according to the company.

The compact, automation-ready ByCut Smart with expanded 15-kW fiber source handles a variety of materials and thicknesses, while the ByCut Eco 3015 with 6-kW fiber source delivers performance and speed at an economical price point, the manufacturer reports.

Bystronic Inc.

CAD/CAM software updated for advanced toolmaking

Cimatron has released version 2024 of its CAD/CAM software for the mold and die industry. It simplifies and automates many tasks for tool designers and offers more options for digital connection, incorporating new technology through collaboration with Sandvik Coromant.

Workflow updates include multiview spacing, batch processing for drawing updates, tangent control for blending faces, and an option for capping internal islands. New 2D drafting features include importing PDFs as real geometry and text, minimum distance dimensioning, enhanced symbols, and improved GD&T.

New abrasives address specific cutting, finishing challenges

Saint-Gobain Abrasives has introduced Vortex Rapid Prep nonwoven flap discs for increased cutting rates and smearfree finishes, Vortex Rapid Blend coarse grit wheels and discs for reducing and blending welds in a single operation, and Norton for Aluminum thin wheels for right-angle cutting and grinding of aluminum and other nonferrous soft metals.

Laser cutting robot handles elaborate cutting paths

Stäubli Robotics offers the TX2- 160L HDP (high dynamic precision) robot for automotive, metal, and other manufacturing plants tasked with laser cutting of complex parts. It handles elaborate cutting paths, displaying high dynamic performance and path accuracy at various speeds and across all trajectories and orientations, according to the company.

A tool changer enables the robot to switch quickly from cutting to inspection. This level of flexibility makes it suitable for use in mass production as well as high-mix, low-volume production. Easy programming software and a user-friendly robotic teach pendent eliminate the need for operator programming expertise.

Bender delivers programmable 3D CNC bending of small tubes

The new BMZ 6 CNC tube bender from WAFIOS has been developed for very small parts, such as those used in medical technology for cannulas.

The compact machine can bend small parts from tube or wire up to an OD of 6 mm. The modular design enables the production of highly complex component geometries by combining different bending processes. For example, closed bends with 360 degrees can be wound around a mandrel, or large radii can be produced by free-form bending.
