# Fibre laser cutting

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

The TruLaser 2030 fiber combines the advantages of a compact machine with the performance of higher machine classes.

TRUMPF'S NEW LASER cutting machine, the TruLaser 2030 fiber, is equipped with up to four kilowatts of laser power. If required, it is also capable of fully automatic manufacturing.

"Only 7.8 x 5.9 metres of installation space is required for the TruLaser 2030 fiber, including pallet changer. Important components such as the switch cabinet, suction system and laser are built into the machine frame. The three-metre working area is easily accessible along its whole length and is designed for large formats. Large windows and LED illumination provide views inside the machine," TRUMPF told ISMR.

"Its ease of operation meets the needs of newcomers to laser cutting. If the operator of the TruLaser 2030 fiber wants to assign a job, the relevant questions for setting up and programming the machine appear on the control user interface. The user is guided by the interface through the various steps until the program starts. TRUMPF cutting parameters for all materials and sheet thicknesses are stored on the machine prior to delivery. Together with its intuitive operation, this significantly reduces the amount of requisite training. Cutting programs can be easily transferred to the TruLaser 2030 fiber using a network or USB drive. In practically no time, drawings can be created directly on the machine and converted into cutting programs.

TruLaser 2030 fiber is available with two, three or four kilowatts of laser power. It can process sheet thicknesses from one to twelve millimetres as well as thicker sheets. With the TruDisk solid-state laser, it can also cut highly reflective materials such as copper (even using nitrogen).

"All materials and sheet thicknesses can be processed with the same cutting unit. This saves set-up time, as there is no need to change the lens and cutting head. Along with other parameters, the focal position of the laser also adjusts automatically to the respective material. The cutting head's collision protection and an automatic nozzle changer with space for eight nozzles reduce set-up times even further," added TRUMPF.

The nozzle changer with eight spaces replaces the nozzle in the cutting head on a fully automated basis. This reduces non-productive times.

Depending on the capacity utilisation and the degree of automation required, the machine is available with a pallet for manual loading and unloading, a pallet changer or LiftMaster Shuttle. See www.trumpf.com

The LiftMaster Shuttle transforms the TruLaser 2030 fiber into an automated production system.
