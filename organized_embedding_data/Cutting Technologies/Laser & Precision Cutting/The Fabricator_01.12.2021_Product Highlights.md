Plasma systems feature single-piece cartridge consumable

Hypertherm has announced three new air plasma systems called Powermax SYNC, featuring builtin intelligence and a single-piece, color-coded cartridge consumable. Technology embedded in the cartridge automatically sets the correct amperage, air pressure, and operating mode and lets operators know when a new cartridge is needed. Additionally, controls on the torch allow operators to adjust the amperage and change the cartridge without returning to the power supply.

According to the manufacturer, the single cartridge lasts up to twice as long and delivers cleaner cuts compared to traditional consumables. The cartridge also can track data, like starts and arc-on time, to identify trends and make a user’s operation more efficient.

System offers hybrid of fiber laser, forming, and tapping

Murata Machinery USA Inc. has introduced the LS3015HL hybrid fiber laser, which combines fiber laser cutting with the forming and tapping performance of the manufacturer’s punch press technology.

Its integrated flying-optics system for fiber laser cutting and upper and lower servo drives for forming help turn raw material into completed products in fewer steps. With repeat accuracy of ±0.001 in. and a 6,692.9-IPM traverse speed, the machine is suitable for prototypes, large-volume runs, short-run stamping, sample parts production, and varied geometries.

The all-in-one design helps maximize floor space, minimize WIP materials, eliminate the need for secondary processes, and increase throughput, the manufacturer reports.

Software brings automation to nesting and NC code generation

Jetcam has announced version 3.82 of its Orders Controller nesting automation software. Available in two versions—Premium and Premium Automation—the software works in conjunction with the company’s Expert nesting software to streamline and automate the CAD import and nesting process.

When ordering components, users now can specify a desired sheet size and material stock item on a per-component basis, which will override default settings. This gives users feeding orders from MRP a greater level of control.

Users can automatically set CAD files queued for import to be the active revision, where a revision is specified. Additional columns have been added to CSV import—order deliver, order priority, allow mirroring, and allow common cutting—shifting control to external systems to affect the nest with minimal user input.

Electric forklift offers environmentally friendly lifting to 5 tons

Combilift has launched the Combi-XLE electric, multidirectional forklift with up to 5-ton lifting capacity. It incorporates high ground clearance; large, cushioned front and rear tires; and a spacious cab.

The forklift features patented all-wheel traction to help reduce tire wear and load swing and enhance braking. Also included is a newly developed, patented Eco-Steer system for a small turning radius. The use of toughened, water-based paint also reduces the amount of VOCs during build.

Since the forklift is electric-powered, noise pollution and carbon emissions are eliminated. There is no need to check and top off engine fluids and lubricants, resulting in longer intervals between services, the company states.

Rotary tube bender processes complex tube geometries

The CNC 40 Rotary from Schwarze-Robitec bends tubes made of all common materials such as aluminum, titanium, copper, steel, and stainless steel with diameters from 0.25 to 1.5 in. This also applies to tubes with already preformed contours. The rotating bending head allows bending of tubes in left and right directions in a single operation.

The bender can be upgraded to a cell for fully automated production, as the rotating head processes complex tube geometries without collisions. All steps related to tube processing, from tube feeding to loading/unloading of the machine to postprocessing, are automated. Each bending cell is customized to the user’s production requirements.
