# AN EYE ON STRIPPIT INNOVATION

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

LVD unveiled a range of new laser cutting, bending and software solutions at this year’s Blechexpo

The LVD Group is a leading manufacturer of a range of sheet metalworking machines and software solutions. Its laser cutting systems, punching machines, press brakes, guillotine shears and automation systems are integrated with its in-house developed CADMAN®-software. The company has production facilities in Belgium, France, the US, Slovakia and China and sales and service offices in more than 45 countries worldwide.

At Blechexpo 2015, LVD featured some of its latest solutions from the bending technology of the ToolCell and the new electric Dyna-Press 24/12 Plus (making its debut at Blechexpo) to the Electra FL rapid fibre laser cutting system. Completing the line-up was LVD’s software solution, the CADMAN® suite V8, for the smooth and efficient flow of sheet metal parts. The company also confirmed the launch of a new Phoenix FL fibre laser system, completing its fibre laser line-up following the Electra and Lynx fibre laser systems.

“Innovation is at the core of our DNA – we continue to innovate every year with new products,” Matt Fowles, LVD’s marketing manager, told ISMR at its booth during Blechexpo. “We also feel it is important that customers know that we are in tune with what is happening in the industry today – small batch series production, short lead time and more complex part production. Our philosophy has always been to optimise the overall process flow.”

## The bending equation

LVD’s Dyna-Press is a compact, portable, high speed electric press brake designed for the cost-efficient bending of small parts. The series is now available with 12 and 24 tons of bending force and a working length of up to 1250mm. The electric press drive system delivers rapid bending speeds of 25mm/s. This results, Matt Fowles explained, in more parts per hour and a lower cost per part. At Blechexpo, LVD showcased the 24/12-Plus model featuring additional Z1 and Z2-axes and a 15” touch screen that is compatible with LVD’s offline bending CAM software, CADMAN-B®.

By reducing unproductive time through its automated tool-changing capabilities, LVD’s ToolCell 135/30 press brake, showcased at Blechexpo, maximizes bending productivity and features an integrated storage of tooling, housed within the machine. Tools are precisely and automatically placed, eliminating manual tooling adjustments. While the operator is preparing parts for the next job, the machine automatically unloads the previous tool setup and loads the next set-up.

“The ToolCell won a design award this year and has been very well received in European markets such as Germany, France, UK and the Netherlands, as well as further afield in Australia, Brazil, North America etc. ToolCell features our patented Easy-Form® Laser inprocess angle monitoring and correction technology, ensuring consistency from the first to the last part. It is offered with various configurable machine options, including stroke, open height front supports and sheet-following units,” explained Matt Fowles.

## Integrated software solutions

CADMAN® suite is LVD's answer to full process integration between software and bending, laser cutting and punching machines. It comprises an intelligent CADMAN core database and works on different levels, interacting with ERP, efficiently classifying jobs with CADMAN-JOB and offline work preparation with CADMAN-B, -L and -P. The touch controls, TOUCH-B and TOUCH-L for bending and laser cutting, can intuitively interact with the machines and the TOUCH-i4 tablet to quickly view and analyze realtime data.

At Blechexpo, the latest releases of CADMAN-B and L, for bending and laser cutting, highlighted the latest productivityenhancing software developments including the workshop management module, CADMAN-JOB, for more effective management and throughput of laser cutting and bending operations.

## Laser cutting in focus

LVD’s ultra-high speed fibre laser solution, the Electra (with integrated automatic nozzle changer) was also showcased at Blechexpo 2015. Powered by a solid-state fibre laser source, it is designed to offer rapid, accurate processing of traditional sheet metal materials such as mild steel, stainless steel and aluminum with the ability to process metals such as copper and brass.

Electra FL maximizes uptime with an integrated automatic shuttle table system that allows one table to be loaded while the machine is cutting on the other table. Electra FL is also automation-compatible and can be fitted with a Compact Tower (CT-L) automated material handling material warehousing system or high speed load/ unload system FA-L (Flexible Automation- Laser) with a load/unload time of 40 seconds. It features LVD’s latest touch screen control and user interface, TOUCH-L, to help make it easy to use and operate.

## Our philosophy has always been to optimise the overall process flow

During Blechexpo, LVD also confirmed the launch of its new Phoenix FL fibre laser system, completing its fibre laser line-up following the Electra and Lynx fibre laser systems.

“We aim to have a balanced product range and the Phoenix fits perfectly into our fibre laser product family. With 2-, 3- 4- and 6kw versions, it is compatible with LVD automation solutions. The capability of fibre lasers in general has now expanded to cover a wider variety of material thicknesses and types,” added Matt Fowles.

The Phoenix machine design includes a welded steel frame construction that minimizes deformation caused by high acceleration, thus improving overall machine accuracy. Powered by an efficient solid-state doped fibre laser source, it is designed to provide rapid, accurate processing of various sheet thicknesses and types. It also features a lightweight, high rigidity beam delivery system to allow for dynamic processing.

“The Phoenix FL maximizes uptime with an integrated automatic shuttle table system that allows one table to be loaded while the machine is cutting on the other table. Table change time is 30 seconds,” LVD told ISMR.

LVD’s touch-screen control and user interface, TOUCH-L, on the Phoenix employ a 19” touch-screen and icon-driven user interface to guide the user through all necessary manmachine interactions. It also incorporates a part programming and nesting feature allowing users to import drawings directly into the control, applying cutting technology and nesting sheets at the machine.

Modular automation options further increase the productivity and throughput of the Phoenix FL. The laser cutting system is offered with the option of a Compact Tower (CT-L) and the Flexible Automation for Lasers (FA-L) developed by LVD.

## Constant evolution

“We continuously invest in new machines in our factories. We also look to reinvest in our own manufacturing processes and methodologies (practicing what we preach) so that we can provide a competitive product to our customers and be as efficient as possible,” Matt Fowles told ISMR.

“The key is to keep innovating and to challenge convention. We aim to keep offering advice to customers and keep close to them, continuing to innovate and look for smarter ways of doing things.” ■
