For more than 60 years, Kjellberg Finsterwalde has been one of the market leaders in plasma cutting. Its portfolio includes products and technologies for plasma cutting, welding and customised special-purpose machines.

"We offer plasma power sources that can be used on 2D or 3D guiding systems i.e. cutting tables, pipe cutting machines or robots for precise, laser-like and rapid plasma cutting. At EuroBLECH 2024, we will be demonstrating our expertise in daily live demonstrations," it said.

"Our K 200 plasma cutting system provides optimal and reliable cutting quality with easy operation at the lowest costs. The plasma power source cuts with oxygen, air and nitrogen in the material thickness range from 1-60mm with a maximum cutting current of 200 A. It also has a marking function and integrated automatic gas control and direct-connection torch (plus hand torch)," it concluded.
