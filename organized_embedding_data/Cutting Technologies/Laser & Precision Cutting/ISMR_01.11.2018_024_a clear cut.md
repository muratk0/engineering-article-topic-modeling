# A CLEAR CUT

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

We highlight a selection of new and innovative waterjet technologies for sheet metal manufacturers.

Waterjet cutting is referred to as a "cold cutting" process because it requires no extra heat. In fact, as only high-pressure water and abrasives are used, waterjet cutting generates very little heat, even from friction, so there is no risk of burning, melting or affecting the material in any way (no heat-affected zone).

Minimising the effects of heat allows metals to be cut without harming or changing intrinsic properties. Sharp corners, bevels, pierce holes and shapes with minimal inner radii are all possible. Cold cutting also reduces overall production time because there is little or no need for clamping or bracing materials during set-up. Operators do not have to wait for materials to cool down before moving onto the next step in production.

Cold cutting also offers a high level of precision. As pressurised water is flowing through a very small area, users have greater control over the cuts being made. Cold cutting is often used for more detailed cuts, such as geometric shapes or designs. It can also be used to cut a wider variety of materials and more irregular surfaces.
