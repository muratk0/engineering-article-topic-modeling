Cutting machine equipped with 24 kW of laser power

TRUMPF has unveiled the upgraded TruLaser 5000 series laser cutting machine outfitted with a laser providing twice the power that was formerly standard. With an output of 24 kW, the TruDisk 24001 laser can process sheet metal parts up to three times more quickly, the company states.

The machine can cut mild steel up to ¾ in. thick using nitrogen as the cutting gas with barely any need for reworking. According to the manufacturer, the increased laser power improves the quality of cut parts, especially for medium and high sheet thicknesses.

Press brake includes automatic setup, tool change

Salvagnini offers the B3.AU-TO press brake, featuring automatic setup and tool change and combining features of electric and hydraulic systems.

The machine is suitable for smaller batches, kit productions, and batchones with very short lead times. The automatic tool change and the automatic tool adjuster, which adjusts the length of the stations, reduce the press brake retooling phase to reduce lead time and help increase productivity.

The press brake can be programmed on the machine, using the Face human-machine interface, or in the office with Stream-former software, which automatically creates the bending sequences, setups, and bending stations.

Four models are available, with a maximum bending length of 3 or 4 m and maximum force of 170 or 220 tons.

Salvagnini

www.salvagnini-america.com

Flap discs combine cutting power with fine finishing

Saint-Gobain Abrasives has introduced Norton Vortex Rapid Prep nonwoven flap discs, which the company says deliver increased cutting rates and smear-free finishes simultaneously.

Patented Vortex agglomerated aluminum oxide grain technology delivers coarse-grit cutting power while producing a finer finish. Suitable for light blending, stripping, edge breaking, and surface prep applications, the discs feature 3D nonwoven abrasive layers. This design helps ensure uniform finishing with a consistently low surface finish value.

End finishing machine performs flaring, trimming, beading

Tube-Line Technologies has introduced the TF2.0-M, a 2-in. tube end finishing machine for flaring, trimming, and beading. The 1-HP direct-drive spindle helps prevent motor stalls. The carriage moves on linear guideways for rigidity and precision, the company states.

Its pneumatic tube stop can be disabled with a knob turn for beading operations. In addition to the soft jaw close feature and safety covers, the machine comes standard with a lockout disconnect and emergency stop to keep operating personnel safe. It can use customers’ existing tooling.

Compact FMS automatically processes blank sheets into ready-bent components

Prima Power offers the PSBB compact flexible manufacturing system, which integrates punching, shearing, buffering, and bending processes in a single system. It includes the new Shear Brilliance punching/shearing system and the latest version of the EBe panel bender.

The line automatically processes blank sheets into ready-bent components, using servo-electric technology with automatic flexible material flow. It is suitable for producing square parts and panels for such sectors as HVAC, door, and elevator panel manufacturing.

Prima Power

www.primapower.com

Videoscope designed for wind power turbine gearbox inspection

The portable IPLEX G Lite-W video-scope from Evident enables users to visually inspect inside a wind turbine gearbox without disassembling it. The videoscope’s sealed tip keeps out lubricating oil from the wind turbine gearbox to make sure the lens stays clean and prevent blurry images.

The videoscope’s optics allow upclose viewing of bearings and gear teeth, while an optional LED guide tube offers ample illumination to spot defects in large, dark spaces.

SMAW/GTAW machine is battery-powered

ESAB has unveiled its Renegade VOLT ES 200i SMAW/GTAW system, developed in conjunction with Stanley Black & Decker, which is powered by four DE-WALT FLEXVOLT 12-Ah (amphour) batteries.

The machine offers maximum welding output of 150 amps on battery power. It also connects to 115-/230-VAC primary power and provides a maximum welding output of 210 amps on 230-V primary.

The battery box can detach for simplified transportation or connection to 115-/230-V power; it also enables users to hot swap the batteries so they can continue welding without interruption.

A four-port fast charger (included) charges the batteries from zero to full charge in about 100 min.
