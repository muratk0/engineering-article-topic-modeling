# Laser cutting large sheets

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

SWISS SHEET metalworking machine tool manufacturer, Bystronic, has extended its BySprint Fiber range of fibre laser cutting machines to include a larger model, designated 6520. It allows metal sheet measuring up to 6.5m x 2m to be processed, whereas the other two machines in the range have maximum table capacities of 4m x 2m and 3m x 1.5m, respectively.

“Material usage can be maximised when using larger sheet, as more parts can be nested with greater efficiency so there is less material wastage. Our BySoft 7 process software supports users with intelligent nesting procedures. More parts can be produced per cutting cycle before the next sheet is loaded, so productivity is raised. Additionally, the variety of applications is increased, as larger single components may be cut without having to interrupt the machine in-cycle to reposition the sheet," said Bystronic. As with the smaller BySprint Fiber machines, the larger 6520 model can be equipped with a 3-, 4- or 6kW fibre laser source. Maximum simultaneous positioning of the axes is the same at 140m/min. See www.bystronic.com

## Expansion for NEXTSENSE

THE AUSTRIAN optical sensor technology specialist, NEXTSENSE GmbH, is expanding globally and has received a multi-million euro grant from the SME section of the Horizon 2020 Programme. The company offers laser-based contactless profile measurement equipment and surface inspection systems in the rapidly-growing market of optical sensor technology. With its latest CALIPRI Smart generation of optical profile measuring devices, Nextsense intends to increase its head-count and triple its turnover.

"To launch our new CALIPRI Smart Range onto the market, we will open new subsidiaries in the USA and China while significantly developing our resources and infrastructure in Austria. As a result, we will create more than 90 new jobs from here to 2020," explained Clemens Gasser, managing director of NEXTSENSE GmbH.

At the core of all CALIPRI equipment is laser light sheet technology, which supports the contactless measuring of most types of profile by projecting laser lines.

"The hallmarks of the next generation of CALIPRI measurement devices are integration and miniaturisation. The now cable-free and ergonomically optimised system delivers ease of handling, as well as ease of use,"added the manufacturer. See www.nextsense.at

## Say it with water

JET EDGE has launched its latest generation of Mid Rail Gantry waterjet systems. Designed for heavy duty 24/7use in industrial environments, the Mid Rail Gantry waterjet is capable of operating up to four abrasive jet or water-only waterjet cutting heads for increased productivity. An optional mirroring package allows waterjet operators to cut large parts twice as quickly.

"Jet Edge Mid Rail Gantry precision waterjet systems hold +/- 0.001" linear positional accuracy (over 12") per axis and+/-0.001" repeatability (bi-directional). Moving elements are supported on THK linear ways, precision ball screws are directly coupled to servomotors and critical motion components are protected with metal covers, brushes, labyrinth passages, lip seals and high-volume, low-pressure, filtered air," the manufacturer told ISMR.

The Mid Rail Gantry separates the motion system from the waterjet cutting table which is designed to eliminate vibration and ensure high part quality. Motion is controlled with the Aquavision®Di industrial PC controller whose customised front-end guides the user through the process from job set-up to production. Single parts, mirroring, rotation, plate alignment and part arrays (optional) are at the operator's fingertips.See www.jetedge.com

## Pulsed welding

BY COMBINING new and enhanced components, ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has introduced a pulsed GMAW (MIG/MAG) system optimised for greater productivity and better quality when welding aluminium, stainless and carbon steel. The system includes the new, intuitive MA25 Pulse Panel, the enhanced Aristo Feed 3004 wire feeder and the upgraded Aristo MIG 4004i Pulse power source. ESAB offers the MA25 Pulse panel in two versions: an aluminium panel with 30 pre-programmed synergic lines for aluminium wire from 0.9mm – 1.6mm and a steel/stainless panel with 54 pre-programmed synergic lines for solid and cored wires from 0.8mm – 1.6mm.

“The MA25 control panel uses icons, push buttons and digital displays to simplify use and eliminate language barriers. The different welding programs are easy to find, set and fine-tune, which contributes to improved productivity," said Hans Örtengren, Product Manager, ESAB Welding & Cutting Products.

To begin pulsed welding, operators select the synergic line for the type of filler wire and gas being used, and the system then provides dynamic arc control. No additional process knowledge is required. The system can automatically adjust for variations in arc length (voltage) and wire feed speed, helping to maintain consistent penetration and weld bead appearance. These functions particularly help less experienced or moderately skilled welders to produce better results.

The MA25 Pulse panel connects directly to the Aristo Feed 3004 wire feeder. Now enhanced by a new motor drive, the system offers more torque for improved arc starts, more consistent speed and less noise. The Aristo Feed's re-designed drive rolls and feed mechanism provide a more positive feeding performance and reduced the risk of wire shavings. A function called start parameter R enables fine-tuning of the electrical arc during the start of the weld. At the termination of a weld, Short Circuit Termination (SCT) technology sharpens the wire to promote better arc starts and eliminate the need to clip the wire to remove the ball that would otherwise form.

ESAB has also upgraded its welding power source in the 400-amp category, the Aristo MIG 4004i Pulse. It now incorporates quicker responding hardware and new software controls that provide better arc starts, especially with aluminium and stainless steel. See www.esab.com
