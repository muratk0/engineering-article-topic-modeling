# Cutting a precision path

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

KERF DEVELOPMENTS WILL be using the MACH 2018 exhibition in the UK this April as the launchpad event for the latest additions to its extensive plasma, waterjet and oxy-fuel cutting product portfolio. In Hall 7, Stand 660, Kerf will be highlighting its new Plasmaster HD (High Definition) system. This will be fitted with the latest Lincoln Electric Flexcut plasma system.

"Complementing the Plasmaster HD will be the RUR2500p with UltraSharp plasma cutting technology. For manufacturers who need to cut even thicker materials, we will have a heavy-duty RUM4500g multi-head oxy-fuel cutting machine on the stand at MACH 2018. This cuts material of up to 150mm with its standard multiplehead configuration. With a UK-based team of specialist cutting engineers, we can configure its product line to cut 300mm thick steel with specialised versions of the RUM capable of cutting through mild steel billets up to 750mm thick," the company told ISMR.

For structural steel fabricators, Kerf will show a selection of parts cut on the PythonX™ machine. This robotic plasma structural steel fabrication system automates processing operations and is designed to increase productivity, minimise material handling and provide high cut quality. The team of specialists from both Kerf and Lincoln Electric will be on hand to offer advice and guidance on a range of cutting processes including oxy-fuel, plasma, UltraSharp and waterjet at MACH 2018.
