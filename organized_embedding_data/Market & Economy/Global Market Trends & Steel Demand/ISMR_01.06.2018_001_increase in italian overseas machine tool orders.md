# Increase in Italian overseas machine tool orders

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

IN THE FIRST quarter of 2018, the UCIMU index of machine tool orders went down, marking a 4.3% reduction compared to the same period in the previous year. However, the absolute value (base 2010 = 100) remained very high: 179.6. UCIMU is the Italian machine tools, robotics and automation systems manufacturer association.

The slowdown in orders recorded by Italian manufacturers in the domestic market was, however, counterbalanced by the positive trend of the orders collected overseas. For example, the index of overseas orders registered a 7.6% increase compared with the same period in 2017, for an absolute value of 180.8. Such a high level, said the association, had never been reached before.

On the contrary, on the domestic front, Italian manufacturers reported a 25.8% decrease versus the first quarter of 2017. The absolute value of the index was 175.3 i.e. 75 points above the average, confirming that the Italian market is still ready to invest, according to UCIMU.

Massimo Carboniero, President of UCIMU, commented recently on the latest Italian machine tool orders registered by UCIMU.

"We are not concerned about the slowdown in orders from the domestic market. We expected that. It is the rebound effect of the extraordinary situation at the end of 2017 when everyone accelerated the race to invest, fearing that Super- and Hyper-Depreciation incentive measures would not be confirmed. The result has also been compared with a really striking first quarter in 2017," he told ISMR.

"The uncertainty following the outcome of the political elections last March is not encouraging for entrepreneurs and especially for those who are deciding whether to make substantial investments. We need a Government plan aimed at competitively developing the manufacturing sector, taking into account the positive results obtained in terms of reforms and provisions over the last few years."

The association has organised various initiatives aimed at supporting the "Made in Italy" sector in overseas traditional and emerging markets. This includes markets in the ASEAN region, where the Italian machine tool manufacturing sector is the second largest supplier of machine tools (after Germany). See www.ucimu.it
