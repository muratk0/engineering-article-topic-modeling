# Global market for welding guns and torches

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

The global welding guns and torches' market is expected to reach a value of US$ 2.08 billion by 2028 from US$ 1.5 billion in 2022, growing at a CAGR of 5.6% according to new research on ResearchandMarkets.com.

"The global welding guns and torches' market boasts a highly competitive landscape with several key players competing for share and consumer preference. The market has a global presence, with multinational companies and regional players actively participating. Continuous innovation is a driving force in this industry. Companies that invest in research and development to create more efficient, precise and sustainable welding accessories, such as guns and torches, gain a competitive edge," said the report.

According to the report, the Asia-Pacific (APAC) region dominated the global welding guns and torches' market share in 2022 (responsible for over 37% of the total revenue). This substantial share can be attributed to the robust demand for welding guns and torches from India, China and Japan, it said, where manufacturing sectors are growing.

"Anticipated growth in the APAC industry remains promising, with Australia and Thailand predicted to show significant progress owing to the rise in robotics and automation. The construction of new factories, commercial structures and extensive infrastructure projects in the region has led to a substantial increase in demand for welding equipment," added the report.

The analysis highlighted advanced welding processes as "a significant phenomenon driving the growth of the welding guns and torches' market." The trend of automation in the welding industry, particularly with the emergence of welding cobots (collaborative robots) and welding drones, also holds considerable potential to drive the growth of the market, it added, as well as increasing efficiency and productivity. Welding cobots can work alongside human operators, assisting with tasks that require precision and repetitive motions.

Competition from alternative joining techniques such as adhesive bonding, fastening techniques and advanced materials (such as composites) was also underlined in the report.

"The global welding guns and torches' market is highly diverse and segmented, with various types of equipment tailored to different welding processes. These segments play a crucial role in meeting the specific needs of industries and applications ranging from heavy manufacturing to precision electronics. MIG is the dominating type, with a share of over 34%," concluded the report.
