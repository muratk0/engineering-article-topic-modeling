# Aluminium comes of age in North American auto market

[TARİH: 01.02.2015 ISMR]

ALUMINIUM BLANKS for making exposed body panels are in high demand as they finally begin appearing ‘en masse’ in passenger vehicles produced in North America. The most notable example is the 2015 Ford F-150 pickup truck which was voted Truck of the Year at the Detroit Motor show recently.

Global Trade Consulting’s new Market Research Report on ‘Aluminium Blanks for Automotives in North America’ is a survey of passenger cars and light trucks assembled in the US, Mexico and Canada that currently and in the future will be utilising aluminium blanks to produce exposed body panels such as doors, hoods and entire vehicle sides.

“Demand for aluminium blanks for exposed body panels for passenger vehicles in North America will be skyrocketing in the coming years,” commented Global Trade Consulting President, Ken O. Eldib.

The vehicle initially consuming the most aluminium blanks in North America will be the Ford F-150 pickup truck with an estimated consumption of over 500 million pounds in 2015. Exposed body panel parts to be made of aluminium, instead of steel, include doors; boots; bonnets; lids; lift-gates; entire vehicle sides; roofs; deck lids; fenders and quarterpanels. The aluminium-bodied Ford F-150 is reportedly 700 pounds lighter than its steel-bodied predecessor, which means its owners can carry a load that is much heavier than the steel version (with average savings in fuel consumption estimated at 3MPG).

“If the new aluminium Ford F-150 programme is deemed a success, every maker of full-sized pickup trucks will follow suit with their own full aluminium-bodied pickup truck. The Dodge Ram is expected to be the next major programme to convert to a full aluminium body and General Motors, Toyota and Nissan will not be far behind,” said the analyst.

In a separate initiative, Novelis has confirmed that it is the supplier of aluminium sheet for the recently launched Jaguar XE. This new addition to the Jaguar line-up, launched in Europe last Autumn and arriving in North America in 2016, is the first vehicle in the midsize segment to feature an aluminium-intensive body structure.
