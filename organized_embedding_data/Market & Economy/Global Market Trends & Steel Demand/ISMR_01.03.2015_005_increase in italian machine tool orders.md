# Increase in Italian machine tool orders

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

IN THE fourth quarter of 2014, the index of Italian machine tool orders, processed by the Studies Dept. of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, registered a 19.1% increase compared with the same period the previous year, for an absolute value of 138.4 (basis 2010=100). The global outcome was determined by the positive results from manufacturers in Italy, as well as abroad.

“The index of orders collected in markets outside Italy reported a 19.3% upturn against the October-December 2013 period, for an absolute value of 134.8,” reported the Italian machine tool and robotics association. “Domestic orders index recorded new growth of 18.8% in comparison with the same period the previous year, for an absolute value of 163.3.”

On an annual basis, the index showed an average increase amounting to 14.7% in comparison with 2013 (absolute value 118.5). The outcome was determined by the positive performances of Italian manufacturers in overseas markets (+10.1%), as well as by good results in the domestic market (+37.2%) which is due to investment in capital goods, supported by the introduction of the New Sabatini Law.

“This latest survey,” stated Luigi Galdabini, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE , “highlights that there were five consecutive quarters of growth registered by the index processed by our Studies Dept. This certainly confirms good expectations for the near future, taking into account that the collection of orders will materialize in production over the next six-eight months.”

Ten months before the EMO exhibition that is scheduled to take place in Milan from 5 to 10 October, 1,100 companies have already applied to exhibit, booking an exhibition surface area of over 100,000 square metres. ■
