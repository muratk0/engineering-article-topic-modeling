# MIDDLE EASTERN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

THE GROWTH in demand for welding and cutting equipment in the UAE and essentially the rest of Arabia is driven by the rapidly-expanding industrial sector, which has made this segment an essential component of the regional steel fabrication, metal working and metal manufacturing industry.

According to latest figures, the industrial sector in the UAE grew by 11 per cent in 2011. From US$ 35 billion in 2010, the manufacturing sector’s contribution to the UAE’s overall GDP swelled to an all-time high of US$ 39 billion in 2011.

In Qatar, one of the fastest growing economies in the world, the industrial sector is expected to post 18 per cent yearly growth between 2011 and 2012, with more than US$ 125 billion expected to be spent on projects between 2011 and 2016.

The manufacturing sector was Saudi Arabia’s fastest growing industry in the first half of 2011, rising by 22.5 per cent, driven by a 38 per cent increase in petroleum refining. Non-refining manufacturing expanded by 14.8 per cent. This exponential growth in the Arabian industrial sector is fuelling demand for a wide variety of welding and cutting equipment, highlighting the importance of the regional trade fair – SteelFab – which showcases not just welding machines but a complete range of welding and cutting equipment and devices.

“Every country has its own industrial characteristics. In Arabia, the welding and cutting segment essentially involves not just welding shops but fabrication yards and engineering workshops, fuelling demand for not just welding machines but a diverse and complete range of equipment and a substantial variety of brands,” said Mr Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, Director-General of Expo Centre Sharjah.

Mr Midfa was speaking during the launch of the forthcoming ninth edition of SteelFab, which will be held from 14-17 January 2013 at Expo Centre Sharjah (UAE) and will have a special focus area on welding and cutting. The welding & cutting segment at SteelFab has grown with the show. From around 1,200 square metres of exhibition space and 48 companies in its first edition in 2004, SteelFab expanded quickly to feature about 16,000 square metres of space and nearly 225 exhibitors and 600 brands for its show in 2012. SteelFab 2012 attracted about 7,000 trade visitors, registering nearly 22 per cent growth over its past show. ■
