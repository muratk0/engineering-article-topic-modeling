# THE SMART REVOLUTION

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

## We highlight some of the growth drivers for the global household appliances industry as well as smart home appliances.

ISMR SAYS: “China, Germany, Japan, South Korea and the United States represent the largest domestic appliance markets.”

Above: Electrolux connected steam ovens were recently on display at the CES exhibition

According to Research and Markets, the expansion of the global domestic appliance industry is forecast to reach 3.3% per annum in the coming years. Between 2007 and 2013, the market increased at an average annual growth of 7.1%. The global household appliances industry is expected to reach an estimated value of US$ 324.2 billion by 2019.

Currently, kitchen appliances account for 46.1% of global demand while the remaining market share is divided between laundry appliances (10.4%); heating appliances (6.8%); vacuum cleaners (6.8%); electric shavers and hair care equipment (5.5%); electric fans (4.1%); water heaters (4.0%); electro-thermic hand drying equipment (0.1%); parts for domestic appliances (6.0%) and other domestic appliances (10.3%).

China, Germany, Japan, South Korea and the United States represent the largest domestic appliance markets while the strongest annual growth is forecast to occur in Tanzania (22.2%); Bangladesh (12.4%); Bolivia (12.1%); Jordan (9.4%) and Sri Lanka (9.2%). Despite being the second most populated region after Asia Pacific, MEA (Middle East and Africa) accounts for a very marginal share in consumer appliances, both in volume and value. However, the region anticipates the second most dynamic growth in consumer appliances over 2014-2019, making it a strong hot spot for the industry. With very low possession rates and growing disposable incomes, opportunities co-exist for both the lower end and the premium segment to find growth.

## Global domestic appliance market revenues

Market analyst, GfK, released a report at the end of 2015 on the global major domestic appliance market for the first six months of 2015.

“Following the continuous growth over the past few years of the global market for major domestic appliances (North America not included), a different picture emerged during the first six months of 2015. While sales in units decreased by 1 per cent, the revenue driven by exchange rate effects based on the euro increased by 9 per cent. Western and Eastern Europe, China and India were the growth drivers. On the other hand, Brazil, and particularly Russia, lagged behind last year’s progress in the first half of 2015. The forecast for 2015 for the major domestic appliances market, including North America, accounts for revenue of 163 billion euros,” said the company.

Between 2007 and 2013, the global domestic appliances market increased with an average annual growth of 7.1%

“In terms of value, Western Europe is still considered among the most important markets for white goods. During the first six months of 2015, the market for major domestic appliances recorded an increase of 5 per cent in quantity and value. The larger countries’ markets yielded above average strong improvement. Germany boasts sales unit growth of 7 per cent and the UK of 6 per cent.”

Moreover, said GfK, markets recovered that had been strongly affected by the European financial crisis in previous years. In terms of units, the home appliance market in both Spain and Greece saw a higher demand than in 2014 during the first half of 2015. In fact, the market in Spain grew by 9 per cent and in Greece by 12 per cent. For 2015, GfK forecasted a sales value of 35 billion euros in Western Europe.

Above all, sales of heat-pump tumble-dryers rose significantly in almost every Western European country and, in part, achieved distinct double digit growth rates. In addition to the sustained trend toward efficient models, home appliances are becoming increasingly “smarter”. For example, appliances can be operated remotely by smartphone or can automatically dispense an optimal amount of detergent with the help of built-in sensors.
