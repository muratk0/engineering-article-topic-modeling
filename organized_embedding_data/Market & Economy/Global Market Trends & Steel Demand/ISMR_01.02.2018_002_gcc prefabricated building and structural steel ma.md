# GCC prefabricated building and structural steel market

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

THE GCC PREFABRICATED building and structural steel market reached a value of nearly US$ 3 billion in 2016 and is expected to reach a value of more than US$ 4 billion by 2022, growing at a CAGR of 7% during 2017-2022. The 'GCC Prefabricated Building and Structural Steel Market: Industry Trends, Share, Size, Growth, Opportunity and Forecast 2017-2022' report was recently added to ResearchAndMarkets.com's offering.

"GCC countries are focusing on economic diversification to reduce their dependence on the oil and gas sector. In line with this, they are planning long-term strategies for sustainable growth and are therefore opting for prefabricated buildings in the construction sector. In addition, increasing investments in the infrastructure sector by public and private stakeholders have further created growth prospects for the market. A surge in the tourism sector, coupled with forthcoming events in the GCC region, have led to a demand for quick and cheaper construction solutions," said the report.
