# Growth for metal can market

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO A report from ReportBuyer, the food and beverage metal cans market has grown rapidly in the last few years and is projected to reach 551.38 billion units and a value of US$ 63.01 billion by 2022.

"The primary factors driving the cans market are the recyclable and reusable properties of metal cans; nutritional contents and cheaper price of canned food and the hygiene offered by packaged food and beverage products. The major restraints are customers moving towards alternative packaging options; the high cost of metals and sluggish growth in developed markets," said the report.

Aluminium is the most widely used material to manufacture food and beverage metal cans. It is lightweight and has the highest recycling rates, compared to any other packaging material. Metal cans are used to package various beverage products, due to their design and tendency to chill quickly. Sports and energy drinks are the fastest-growing sub-application of the beverage metal can market. It is a part of the functional drinks market, with huge market potential.

Emerging economies such as BRIC (Brazil, Russia, India and China) and CIVETS (Colombia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Egypt, Turkey and South Africa) are poised to account for much of the global growth for metal cans in the upcoming years. Growth in these countries is mainly driven by favourable demographics, rising household incomes and the busy lifestyles of consumers, inclining them towards ready meals and canned foods in particular," concluded the report.

Food and beverage companies are pursuing aggressive growth strategies in emerging economies by diversifying their product offerings and strengthening their distribution bases.
