ISMR: How has the global economic downturn affected the sheet metal market and sales of CAD/CAM systems or nesting software, in your view? Has cutting cost become more of an issue for sheet metal users? How do you see the future?

CT: The economic climate has had an impact on the way manufacturers operate. With rising costs and ever-tightening budgets, companies have become much more conscientious about wasting material. Companies that haven’t ever used an automatic nesting engine in the past can save 10% or more on raw materials by implementing CAD/CAM software with a nesting engine. Even companies who have been using nesting software are now starting to explore different products with better nesting capabilities.

Many people are also making a more substantial effort to gather better performance out of the machines they have, rather than investing in newer faster machines. This means they are turning to their software, controllers etc. to gain cutting speed and increased productivity.

ISMR: Please give us some background history on SigmaTEK. How many people in the company and what are its main products and technologies?

CT: SigmaTEK Systems, LLC was founded in 1992 and has since provided nesting software solutions to enhance efficiency and profitability for sheet metal and heavy plate manufacturers. Through automatic CAD/CAM nesting and NC optimisation, SigmaNEST software programs profile cutting machines quickly, conveniently and effectively. SigmaTEK is headquartered in Ohio, USA and has an extensive global support network, with branches in Europe, Asia, India, Australia and South America.

We are committed to providing industry-leading capabilities and superior features in SigmaNEST. SigmaTEK’s technological leadership is maintained by a commitment to excellence and continued investment in Research & Development. Designed to maximise production productivity and fit seamlessly into a workflow system, SigmaNEST offers a comprehensive suite of production management and database integration tools. Our open architecture allows users to customize post processors and management reports. It provides a strong breadth and depth of functionality to ensure accurate part cutting, optimal material usage and reduced cutting time.

SigmaNEST is suitable for many cutting systems including plasma, punch, laser, waterjet, oxyfuel and router. SigmaTEK also collaborates with customers to integrate seamlessly with their production process and MRP systems.

SigmaNEST software is offered in modules that can be combined to best meet the needs of particular customers, from job shop environments to diverse corporations in aerospace, transportation, energy and more. Our major products are SigmaNEST and SolidNEST. We have also developed three more specified solutions that venture out of the profile cutting realm: SigmaNEST 3D and SigmaTUBE.

ISMR: You offer a SigmaBEND module for bending operations – what are the benefits for users and how does the software differ from others on the market? Any USPs?

CT: SigmaBEND runs the widest variety of machines in the market so, for manufacturers or job shops who utilise multiple machine brands, SigmaBEND is a single solution for all their machines. SigmaBEND allows great flexibility in importing from solid CAD products including SolidWorks, Inventor, NX and more, dramatically cutting down on part processing time.

Another key benefit is that SigmaBEND allows users to view a real-time simulation of the bending process with collision detection! That means no more trial and error on real material before the parts can be produced. A third USP is that SigmaBEND offers automated programming for batch processing of parts.

ISMR: How many customers do you have worldwide? Please outline SigmaTEK’s successes and achievements over the last two years.

CT: SigmaTEK Systems has over 7000 seats of software being used today. Our customer base has doubled in the past four years, despite the economic climate of recent years. We successfully launched SigmaTUBE and SigmaBEND in the last two years. We also launched SigmaNEST Version 9, with an entirely new graphical user interface.

We have successfully weathered the economic storm, which I believe is an exceptional achievement, and we have reached our 15-year milestone.

ISMR: Which other ancillary products have you introduced to help sheet metal users with workload planning, inventory control and other workshop operations? Please outline the benefits.

CT: Color Ofload – ease of managing parts and jobs after they’ve been cut
SimTrans – MRP integration module that helps to automate data transfers
Load Manage – machine bandwidth balancing; ensures that work is divided evenly between machines
SolidNEST – nesting add-in that runs inside SolidWorks
SigmaTUBE – tube/pipe cutting solution.

ISMR: Do you have plans to launch any new products over the next year? Please elaborate.

CT: One area that I’d like to see us grow is on the tube and pipe cutting side. SigmaTUBE software is about three years old and is absolutely ready for implementation on a large scale. I’d like to see that product really take off over the next few years. We have several customers successfully using the product now, and their positive responses have been a great encouragement.

We are also very excited about the launch of our new nesting engine. Unlike many other nesting solutions, SigmaTEK Systems developed its nesting engine in-house. It has been tweaked and improved over the past 15 years and we believe that, when the new nesting engine launches its capabilities, it will astound the market.

We are also working on a new punch tooling engine (an improvement on the one we have in the software currently) to further optimise tool load set-up for a production run. SigmaNEST’s current tooling engine analyses upcoming orders and optimises tooling set-ups for minimal tool changes throughout the series of production runs.
