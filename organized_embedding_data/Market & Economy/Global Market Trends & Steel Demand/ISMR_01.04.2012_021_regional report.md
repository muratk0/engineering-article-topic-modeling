## China In Focus

ISMR chronicles growth patterns in Chinese industry sectors and unveils new market forecasts for the future

ISMR SAYS: “The Government aims to transform the manufacturing industry from “Made in China” to “Created in China”.

China has been the fastest growing economy, expanding at 10.5 per cent annually driven by exports and investment. High priority is given to economic restructuring, from an export-driven to a consumption-driven economy during the 12th five-year plan period. The Government aims to transform the manufacturing industry from “Made in China” to “Created in China”. China is currently the largest manufacturer of office furniture in the world. In 2011, total production increased by 15% compared to 2010, according to CSIL Market Research.

Sheet metal forming is a large industry in China. The machinery building industry uses 60-70% sheet/plate, tube and section metal per year. Based on this, there are over 30,000 sheet metal forming companies in China. Most of China’s sheet metal forming companies mainly employ hand-operated production lines, which influences the productivity and production quality of parts produced, especially in the automotive industry.

“It is very important for car body parts-forming plants to install a multi-station press and automatic production lines,” CCMI’s sheet metal forming committee explained. “Many sheet metal forming plants are the workshops of OEMs. According to our investigations, there is very strong competition in stamping for electronics and home appliances. China’s sheet metal forming industry needs to increase its precision stampings’ production, as well as the number of automatic production lines and multi-station stamping press lines.”

## An eye on automotive

According to the CRIC, since 2009, China has become the largest country in automobile production and marketing globally. In 2011, production and sales volume of Chinese automobile were respectively 18.4189 million and 18.5051 million (new cars), and the growth rates were 0.84% and 2.45% respectively. The rapid increase of the production and sales volume of Chinese automobiles has driven the rapid development of the automobile distribution industry.

The market concentration rate of the Chinese automobile circulation industry is increasing rapidly. In 2010, the number of distributors in Top 100 enterprises reached 3,000, accounting for some listed automobile manufacturers, most of which are distributors who import luxury cars or middle and high-end joint brands.

The automobile market in large cities of China is nearly saturated, so automobile markets in small and medium-sized cities will inevitably see rapid development. For automobile distributors, it will be the key to the increase in the future automobile market. In December 2011, the Ministry of Commerce of the People’s Republic of China circulated Instructions on “Promoting the Development of Automobile Circulation Industry During ‘the 12th Five-year Planning”. It points to improving the concentration ratio of the Chinese automobile distribution industry; by the end of 2015, the proportion of Top 100 enterprises in the Chinese automobile retail industry will exceed 30% of gross values in the industry; there will be 30 regional automobile circulation companies with primary business revenues of over CNY 10 billion; the number of large-scale automobile circulation enterprises with primary business revenues of over CNY 100 billion will be three to five.

“The country’s low labour costs and supportive geographical location also make it one of the most attractive destinations for major market players. As per the estimates from our latest research report, the domestic production of completely built-up units in China will surge at a CAGR of around 19% during 2011-2014,” said RNCOS.

“According to the ‘China Automobile Sector Analysis’, government initiatives have provided a significant boost to the Chinese auto industry, placing it in forefront of the global automobile arena. During 2010, the demand for passenger cars and commercial vehicles increased radically in China, which has also been making every possible effort to become the world’s leading producer of the new energy vehicle. We found that the government has recently announced various incentives and subsidies to boost the new energy vehicle market and make China the world’s largest producer of electric cars. After the announcement, prominent global names like Toyota are planning to enter China’s promising green vehicle market.”

Changing lifestyles in Tier-II and III cities have pushed passenger car sales in China. With rising disposable income levels, sales of luxury cars have also been growing significantly in the country where BMW, Audi and Rolls Royce are prominent players in the segment. The country has also been posting considerable growth in the utility vehicle segment, particularly SUVs.

## Construction, steel and electronic markets

Following a slowdown in the second half of 2010, world steel output growth, led by production in China, accelerated to nearly 10% in the first quarter of 2011, year-on-year. In China, the moderate demand growth rate of 5% expected in 2011 and 2012 marked a turning point away from the high growth rates experienced during the last decade. Manufacturing will be the engine of demand growth in many countries, particularly in sectors such as automotive, machinery and metal goods. However, it may take some time for key steel end-using industries to fully recover from the recession particularly in countries outside Asia.

Construction is the largest market for steel and continues to be the weakest of all the demand sectors especially in advanced economies. Even in China, construction growth should slow as government stimulus ends and efforts are made to reduce energy use and cool down the real estate sector.

Driven by increased demand from downstream industries and growth in exports, the electronic components manufacturing industry in China is expected to experience average revenue growth of 11.2% per year over the next five years. As Chinese and global economies further recover from the global financial crisis, demand for a wide range of intermediate manufacturing inputs will increase. Industry revenue is forecast to reach $ 268.82 billion in 2016, according to IBISWorld, a large US publisher of industry research.

“With the rapid development of China’s Information Communications Technology (ICT) sector, China has become the world’s largest manufacturing centre for electronic products, including televisions, computers, handsets and DVD players. China’s output of some component categories, especially commonly used and medium- and low-end electronic components, is the highest in the world,” it said.

China’s demand for automotive electronics has grown at a fast pace in the past decade. In the next five years, both production and demand will continue to grow. Solving technology challenges and detailing upcoming opportunities in the semiconductor and emerging/adjacent markets in China was a key focus of SEMICON China 2012 in March at the Shanghai New International Exhibition Center (SNIEC). The most recent SEMI Consensus Forecast projects that final data for 2011 will show 26 per cent growth for semiconductor fab equipment spending in China. China’s current Five-Year Plan (2011-2015) targets the expansion of China’s microelectronics supply chain to meet the needs of regional and other global markets. Analysts project that investments of more than US$40 billion will fuel Chinese semiconductor industry growth during this five-year period.

However, increased foreign competition, high capacity and output levels, weaker future demand from downstream industries and continuing effects from the global financial crisis are expected to contribute to a more difficult operating environment for this industry in future years. Demand for electronic components, however, will remain high over the next five years. ■

ISMR flew to Turkey to witness the launch of Ermaksan's latest fiber laser cutter and hybrid press brake

ISMR SAYS: “Ermaksan has continuously invested in improving and adding to its portfolio of sheet metal products”

Turkish manufacturer Ermaksan has forged itself a solid profile in the sheet metal working industry. It manufactures 3000 machines annually with 700 qualified staff in a modernised and highly equipped 80,000 sq.m. factory in Bursa, not far from Istanbul. Today, it exports 80% of its production through agents under the ERMAK brand from Canada to New Zealand, in over 70 countries. Ermaksan also has a branch office in Chicago, USA for its marketing, sales and after-sales services for the North American market.

The company boasts a 45-year heritage ever since Erol Özkayan began his working life in 1965 with a small lathe workshop producing textile machines, hydraulic leather machines, generator groups, electric motors, furniture wood hot presses, oxygen cutting workbenches and automobile. Ermaksan has been focused on sheet metal working machines for the last 20 years and has continuously invested in improving and adding to its portfolio of products. The company currently manufactures guillotine shears, hydraulic/hybrid press brakes, laser cutting machines, punch presses, plasma cutters and assorted metalworking machinery.
