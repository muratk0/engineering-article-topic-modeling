# European machine-tool projections in focus

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

CECIMO Spring Meetings 2023, held in Antalya (Turkey), outlined emerging technologies and mega trends in manufacturing industries. Hosted by MiB (Turkish Machine Manufacturers), they were held from 6-9 May 2023. The four-day programme included strategic meetings focusing on the future roadmap of the machine-tool sector. Despite political and economic system uncertainties, CECIMO (the European Association of Manufacturing Technologies) confirmed a positive economic picture for European machine-tool producers.

"With easing supply-side pressures and strong order books, we expect positive output growth this year. The reduced risk of gas shortages and easing inflationary pressures are expected to have a positive impact on consumer and business confidence in Europe. However, it is important to recognize the negative impact of geopolitical tensions and rising interest rates on investment flows and global demand," said CECIMO.

"Looking back on the previous year, the European machine-tool industry experienced significant recovery despite all the challenges. Strong order momentum was maintained throughout the year, with capacity utilisation levels above the 2019 average. According to the latest figures, CECIMO's machine-tool production reached around €25 billion in 2022, maintaining a share of around 32% of global machine-tool production. On the consumption side, with a value of around €18.4 billion in 2022, CECIMO countries accounted for almost a quarter of the world's machine-tool consumption. These numbers reflect an increase in machine-tool production of around 11.9% and machine-tool consumption of 23.4%, compared to 2021 figures," it added.

CECIMO's total machine-tool exports increased by around 11.7% in 2022, while imports improved by around 28.8%. It confirmed that the USA and China remained the two main export destinations for European machine-tool producers in 2022, with the USA in first place. Japan, China and Taiwan remained the top three machine-tool supplying markets for CECIMO countries.

Chairman of the Economic Committee, Marcus Burton, announced the initial estimates of CECIMO machine-tool production growth in 2023: "Thanks to strong order backlogs and a further recovery in machine-tool consumption, especially in Europe, we see further growth of CECIMO machine-tool production by about 8% this year," he confirmed.

Commenting on the announcement, Filip Geerts, CECIMO Director General, said: "We are pleased to see the recovery of the European machine-tool industry in 2022, and we look forward to positive output growth in 2023. While there are challenges ahead, we believe that the industry is well-positioned to continue its growth trajectory in the years to come."

## 'Future of Industry' conference

While the CECIMO total orders index average for 2022 is around 11% higher than the 2021 average, the latest business climate indicators suggest a decline in orders in the first half of 2023. Later, however, order intake is expected to show a more stable and positive trend.

The 2023 CECIMO Spring Meetings hosted the "Future of Industry" conference. The conference brought together different industry experts who shared their knowledge and experience on the latest technological developments, the state of manufacturing, trends in the machine-tool industry and asset-based financing.

Steve Hope, New Business Development Manager – Europe, DLL Group discussed five key trends in the machine tool sector including e-mobility; additive manufacturing; sustainability and automation.

Memiş Kütükcü, Chairman of the Board, OSBÜK, outlined how to build a more resilient supply chain, which is critical in today's fast-paced and constantly changing business environment. The event was sponsored by Fanuc, Renishaw and DLL Group.

CECIMO serves as a platform for fostering cooperation, sharing knowledge and driving innovation in manufacturing technologies.
