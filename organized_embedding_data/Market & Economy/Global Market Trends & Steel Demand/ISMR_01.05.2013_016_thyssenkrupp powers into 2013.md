# ThyssenKrupp powers into 2013

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

THYSSENKRUPP expects the Group’s sales from continuing operations to remain at the prior-year level of around €40 billion in fiscal year 2012/2013, provided there are no major dislocations on the raw materials markets. Sales lost due to portfolio measures, in particular at Steel Europe and Components Technology, should be largely offset by organic growth in the capital goods businesses, it told ISMR.

ThyssenKrupp made further important progress with its strategic development programme in the first quarter of 2012/2013. The combination of Inoxum, the former Stainless Global business area, and the Finnish stainless steel manufacturer Outokumpu was successfully completed on 28 December 2012 and contributed to a reduction in net financial debt.

The sale of Steel Americas is proceeding. Since November 2012, a selection of potential buyers have had the opportunity to analyse the plants in Brazil and the USA in a due diligence process and submit binding purchase offers.

On the positive side, its Elevator Technology division achieved doubledigit growth rates in the first quarter 2012/2013, with order intake of €1.6 billion (up 10 per cent) and sales of €1.5 billion (up 14 per cent). Adjusted EBIT came to €169 million (prior year: €142 million). This improvement resulted from both increased sales and positive effects from the restructuring measures initiated in the last fiscal year. The business area continued its growth track with the closing of the takeover of AMCO Elevator, USA, on 31 December 2012.

The Plant Technology division performed particularly well in chemical plant construction. In the first three months, new orders were won with a total value of €1.8 billion, double the prior-year figure. Sales were 6 per cent higher than a year earlier at €1.0 billion. Marine Systems continued to profit from increased demand for frigates and submarines. Order intake in the reporting period came to €178 million, and sales to €305 million. At €30 million, adjusted EBIT was level with the prior-year quarter minus civil shipbuilding operations which were included a year ago and have since been sold.
