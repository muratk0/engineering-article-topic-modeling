# TRUMPF announces sales

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

IN THE 2012/13 fiscal year, ending in June, the TRUMPF Group generated sales of 2.35 billion euros. This corresponds to a small increase of 1% over its previous year’s sales of 2.33 billion euros. This annual sales figure is the highest in the German company’s 90-year history.

“The increase over the previous year is small but, considering the extremely difficult market conditions we faced, we can certainly be pleased with the result,” said TRUMPF President, Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller, when presenting the preliminary figures.

Orders received by sheet metal specialist TRUMPF during 2012/13 remained at the previous year’s figure of 2.33 billion euros (after 2.35 billion euros in the previous year).

“We are expecting a pre-tax result lower than that of the previous year,” explained Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller. In 2011/12, the company achieved a pre-tax result of 211 million euros.
