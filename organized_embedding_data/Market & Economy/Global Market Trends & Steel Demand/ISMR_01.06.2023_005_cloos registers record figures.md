# CLOOS registers record figures

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

The Cloos Group has closed the 2022 financial year with record figures. The welding and automation specialists from Haiger, Germany, increased turnover orders by nearly 30 per cent to more than 200-million euros compared to the previous year.

"We, too, are currently facing numerous challenges ranging from material and supply bottlenecks to the shortage of skilled workers and the effects of the energy crisis," said CEO, Stephan Pittner. "Despite these generally difficult conditions in the mechanical engineering sector, we can look back on a very successful business year."

CLOOS intends to continue on this growth course. The group expects order intake and turnover to increase at a disproportionately high rate in 2023. The potential for automated welding robot systems is enormous worldwide, said Pittner. Today, CLOOS has an export rate of more than 80 per cent. In addition to important markets in Germany and Europe, its number one growth market continues to be China with a further upward trend. However, CLOOS is also experiencing high demand for various automation solutions in North America.

Its budget for research and development, it told /SMR, is already above the industry average and is expected to increase further in 2023. Its focus is on the individual requirements of customers when it comes to new and further development of welding machines, welding processes and automation components.

Another development focus is its digital solutions. These will be combined in a complete platform in the future.

Since 1919, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH has been a specialist in welding technology. It has over 900 employees and offers welding power sources and robots, positioners and special purpose machines. It also develops and manufactures automated welding systems.■
