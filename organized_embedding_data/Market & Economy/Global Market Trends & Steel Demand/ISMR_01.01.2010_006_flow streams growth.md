# Flow streams growth

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

Flow International Corporation, a leading developer and manufacturer of industrial waterjet machines for cutting and cleaning applications, has reported results for its fiscal 2010 second quarter, ending 31 October 2009.

For the quarter, Flow reported consolidated revenues of US$ 42.0 million, an increase of 11% compared to US$ 37.8 million in the first quarter of fiscal year 2010. Revenue in the year-ago quarter was US$ 60.6 million. The company reported net income of US$ 0.7 million or US$ 0.02 per basic and diluted share, compared to net income of US$ 0.4 million or US$ 0.01 per basic and diluted share a year ago. Net income for the quarter just ended includes a US$ 0.6 million gain recognized on the sale of Flow’s building in Taiwan as part of the company’s consolidation of its manufacturing facilities.

“Sales from all sectors of our business, Standard systems, Advanced systems and consumable parts, all grew at double digit rates compared to the first quarter, the first time we have had a quarterly sequential increase in revenues since one year ago,” said Charley Brown, President and CEO of Flow.
