# TRUMPF reports strong order book and increased sales

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Sheet metal specialist, The TRUMPF Group, closed its 2021/22 fiscal year on 30 June 2022 with a significant increase in sales. According to preliminary calculations, sales amounted to 4.2 billion euros, up from 3.5 billion euros in FY 2020/21.

The Netherlands is now the company's best-performing market with sales of around 840 million euros. This is due to growth in the TRUMPF Group's EUV business with Dutch technology company, ASML. The company's second-biggest market is the U.S. with sales of around 655 million euros, followed by Germany with sales of approximately 590 million euros.

TRUMPF also entered the new fiscal year with a bulging order book. Order intake rose by 42 per cent to some 5.6 billion euros, up from 3.9 billion euros in FY 2020/21.

“The global disruption to supply chains clearly affected our sales," commented TRUMPF CEO, Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller. “Despite a high order backlog, we ended up making fewer machines and laser systems than we could have done due to the ongoing shortages in semiconductor and electronic components. But the strong economic momentum we're seeing in the U.S. and across Europe is encouraging, and we're now benefiting from growing demand for e-mobility solutions and for technology in other areas. We're entering the new fiscal year with robust order books, but the disruptions and uncertainty in global supply chains are likely to continue for some time."

The Group's headcount has now risen to around 16,500, including some 8,400 employees in Germany as of 30 June 2022, approximately 4,900 of whom work at the company's headquarters in Ditzingen, Germany.

The Group has also strengthened its software portfolio by acquiring the remaining 49 per cent stake in Indian software development joint venture, TRUMPF Metamation Private Limited. The Chennai-based software firm, which specialises in the development of CAD and CAM software applications and machine control solutions, is a key component of TRUMPF's global software strategy. The company currently employs around 40 software specialists.

TRUMPF is also set to invest around four million euros in ScaleNC. This start-up offers digital services on a cloud platform to sheet-metal manufacturers, for example to program their machines. TRUMPF intends to use this investment to fuel growth in German-speaking countries and branch out its services to customers in the USA.

ScaleNC offers digital services on a cloud platform to sheet metal manufacturers.

“ScaleNC is the answer to the skills shortage. Many of our customers struggle to fill vacancies. On top of that, ScaleNC provides cost benefits and helps our customers to strengthen their competitive stance," explained Tom Schneider, Managing Director, Research & Development, TRUMPF Werkzeugmaschinen SE + Co. KG.

TRUMPF offers manufacturing solutions in machine tools and laser technology. The company also drives digital connectivity in manufacturing through consulting, platform products and software. It is a technology and market specialist in machine tools for sheet-metal processing and industrial lasers. With over 70 subsidiaries, the TRUMPF Group is represented in nearly every European country as well as in North America, South America and Asia.
