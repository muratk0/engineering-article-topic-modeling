# Lantek reports record turnover and investment

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Lantek, a multinational company that develops and markets CAD-CAM/MES/ERP solutions and a pioneer in the digitisation of companies in the sheet metal sector, has again consolidated its market position after announcing its 2021 turnover. Despite the continued pandemic, the company has set a record turnover of 26.6 million euros, increasing sales to the machine tool manufacturer channel by 40%.

2020 was a record year for the company, despite the COVID-19 crisis. In 2021 the Spanish multinational went on to set a further all-time sales record, achieving total growth of 26% above 2020 levels. The number of active customers at the end of the year exceeded 29,000, with 3,400 new customers across 100 countries during 2021.

"Lantek continues to strengthen its growth in international markets with an increase in sales volume of 42% in Asia, 13% in America and 19% in the EMEA region, while growth in countries such as Germany with 33% and Italy, with 25.6%, are stand-out results. In Italy, we have further consolidated our presence with the construction of a new building. This new facility in Turin has a surface area of over 1,200m²." it said.

Lantek has already started a long-term R&D growth and investment plan that will run until 2025.

"In 2022, we're making the largest investment in the history of our company. In this first phase of our Strategic Expansion Plan, we have increased our commitment to research and development by 70%. Our workforce will grow by 50%, with a special emphasis on the R&D area and our international customer service team," concluded López de Biñaspre, the company's CEO.■
