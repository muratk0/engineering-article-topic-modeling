# AN EYE ON HVAC

HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning systems) are mainly used to reduce energy consumption, provide moisture consistency and improve thermal comfort and indoor air quality. They comprise both heating and cooling equipment. Nowadays, HVAC equipment is essentially installed at large industrial and commercial infrastructures to regulate temperature and humidity. It then provides fresh outdoor air and eliminates contamination.

According to Markets and Markets, the HVAC systems market was valued at US$ 170.11 billion in 2017 and is expected to reach US$ 251.60 billion by 2023, at a CAGR of 6.80% during the forecast period. Major factors driving the growth of the market include increasing demand for HVAC systems for reducing energy consumption, extreme weather conditions, government tax credit and rebate programmes, and the growing demand for HVAC systems to upgrade old systems. In separate analysis, the global HVAC market is also expected to grow in future due to more construction activities, increasing population levels, urbanisation and higher disposable incomes. The rising trend for smart buildings and demands for equipment replacement also underpin this growth.

The HVAC equipment market can be segmented by type such as heating (heat pumps, furnaces, unitary heaters and boilers); air conditioning (room air conditioners, unitary air conditioners, coolers and others) and ventilation (ventilation fans/air pumps and humidifiers/dehumidifiers). Residential HVAC equipment is further sub-classified into Ductless and Unitary. Commercial HVAC equipment is sub-classified into Ductless, Unitary and Applied.

## Industrial HVAC market 2018-2022

Technavio has published a new market research report on the global industrial HVAC market from 2018-2022. Its analysts forecast that the global industrial HVAC market will grow at a CAGR of around 5% during the forecast period, according to its latest market research report.

"The growing adoption of R32 refrigerant in air conditioners is one of the major trends witnessed in the global industrial HVAC market from 2018-2022. The global industrial HVAC market is moving towards the adoption of advanced refrigerants that have better efficiency and are more environmentally-friendly than traditional refrigerants. All air conditioning and refrigeration systems use some form of refrigerant to absorb the heat from the air to cool," commented the company.

According to Technavio analysts, one of the key factors contributing to the growth of the global industrial HVAC market is increasing demand for energy-efficient HVAC systems. The need for energy-efficient equipment for different industrial processes has risen as end-user industries have increased their production volume to cater to growing demand. On average, almost 9% of the cost of production in most industries is spent on electricity. However, in energy-intensive industries such as oil and gas, the cost spent on electricity is almost 40%.

"Of the two major end-users, the process industry segment held the largest market share in 2017, contributing to around 68% of the market. This end-user segment will dominate the global market throughout the forecast period. APAC held the largest share of the market in 2017, accounting for an almost 42% share. It was followed by EMEA and the Americas respectively. The APAC region is anticipated to dominate the global market throughout the forecast period," concluded the analyst.

Orbis Research confirmed that Asia Pacific has emerged as a major region for the HVAC market due to rising HVAC equipment and aftermarket and system sales by domestic companies and the high HVAC replacement rate in China, India, Australia and Korea. Factors which could hinder growth of the global HVAC market, it told /SMR, include raw materials prices; stringent regulations; data infrastructure system security; retail consolidations and economic and political events.

## Global commercial HVAC market

The global commercial HVAC market is expected to post a CAGR of close to 6% from 2018-2022, according to the latest market research report by Technavio. The data centre market is expected to boost growth of the global commercial HVAC market.

In this report, Technavio highlights the emerging smart and connected HVAC as one of the key emerging trends in the global commercial HVAC market.

"End-users can manage their HVAC systems better with the advantages of portable devices and monitoring systems. Air monitoring systems in HVAC equipment assist in detecting and removing allergens, harmful particulate matter, stale air and virus-causing contaminants. They also clean and regulate the moisture levels for optimal indoor air quality," said the analyst.

"Thermostats are one of the significant beneficiaries of smart technologies. Many vendors are introducing smart thermostats. These systems are automatically installed with advanced firmware and algorithms which, in addition to their learning ability, aid in boosting the energy efficiency of buildings while incurring low installation costs."

According to Technavio, the equipment segment held the largest market share in 2017, accounting for over 69% of the market. This application segment is expected to dominate the global market throughout the forecast period. APAC led the market in 2017 with more than 48% of the market share. It was followed by EMEA and the Americas respectively. The market share occupied by the APAC region is anticipated to increase by almost 2% by 2022.

## The market for HVAC services

According to Mordor Intelligence, the HVAC services market was valued at US$ 48.96 billion in 2017 and is projected to reach a market value of US$ 69.48 billion by 2023 at a CAGR of 6.1% from 2018-2023. The growing construction business in major emerging economies and the growing end-user markets (for data centres, for example) are the major drivers for the growth in the HVAC service market during the forecast period.

"Changing trends in urbanisation, industrialisation and migration are some of the key factors influencing the growth of the market, giving rise to the number of factories, manufacturing units and residential complexes which use HVAC systems. Additionally, factors such as climate change and technological innovations have increased the adoption of HVAC equipment. Global warming, which has led to changing and unpredictable weather conditions, has also increased the need for air conditioning equipment. Accordingly, the growing importance of maintenance and monitoring systems have increased the adoption of HVAC services," explained the analyst.

Government regulations on energy efficiency and the use of environmentally-friendly refrigerants are expected to increase sales of HVAC equipment in the developed region, particularly in Western Europe. Construction activity impacts both the HVAC equipment market and the HVAC service market. With increased construction activity in developed regions where infrastructure investments are high (to increase the standard of living), the installation and maintenance segment of the HVAC service market will also increase.

"Rapid industrialisation, steady economic growth and the need for enhancing energy efficiency in buildings have increased construction of modern building systems. Construction ventures in advanced economies..."

are likely to grow by 2% per year. The growth rate in developing markets will likely record 5.3% per year, during the same period. This growth is indicated with projects worth US$ 240 billion which are being announced by various countries in the Asia-Pacific region," concluded the analyst.
