# ASIAN PROMISE

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

MANUFACTURING INDONESIA 2009 Series Of Exhibitions  
MACHINE TOOL INDONESIA 2009

Held within the Manufacturing Indonesia series of exhibitions, Machine Tool Indonesia consistently attracts over 35,000 qualified trade visitors and more than 1,400 exhibitors.

Machine Tool Indonesia remains the main exhibition of metalworking technology serving the Indonesian market. According to the Indonesian Machine and Tool Industry Association (Asimpi), imports of machinery and components to Indonesia amounted to a total of US$ 8 billion and US$ 5 billion, respectively, in 2008.

The country’s economy, the largest in Southeast Asia, grew at an annual rate of 6.2 per cent in the second quarter of this year (an acceleration from 2009, when gross domestic product expanded 4.5 per cent).

“Indonesia’s vast natural resources will ensure that economic growth remains within the 5%-6% range over the next 10 years. Over our 10-year forecast, we envisage worth averaging an annual real rate of 5.5%, way above the previous decade’s 2.8% average expansion. In addition, Indonesia will continue to benefit from a young population, which will ensure a healthy consumer base over the next decade, alongside a rising working population. The new political system has improved long-term prospects for doing business in the country, as it has corresponded with an anti-corruption drive that is re-establishing trust in government institutions,” said FinanceIndonesia.org.
