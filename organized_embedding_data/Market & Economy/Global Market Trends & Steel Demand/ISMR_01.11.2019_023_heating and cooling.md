According to Grand View Research, the global HVAC systems market size was valued at US$ 118.7 billion in 2017 and is projected to expand at a CAGR of 5.7% from 2018 to 2025. Increasing urbanisation and the emergence of energy-efficient heating and ventilation systems are the driving forces for the growth of the market. Governments across the globe are promoting the use of energy-efficient Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC) units by offering incentives and rebate programmes to bolster growth.

"Technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), automated control systems and remote control access are transforming HVAC equipment. Automated control systems detect the surrounding climate and automatically control the temperature and fan speeds to offer optimum comfort. Remote control access allows users to operate connected systems through mobile applications through the Internet. IoT-enabled HVAC units can be connected to the Internet and provide real-time information about the condition of the systems to users and manufacturers, helping them to anticipate defects and reduce maintenance costs," explained the analyst.

## Drivers and trends

Factors such as evolving climatic conditions; growing population; demographic changes; rapid urbanisation and rising disposable income are driving the growth of the HVAC equipment market. An increasing focus on using environmentally-friendly refrigerants is also propelling demand for HVAC systems. The rising trend for smart buildings and demands for equipment replacement also underpin this growth.

"Based on end-user, the HVAC market has been categorised by commercial, industrial and residential. Among these, commercial was the leading end-user category, mainly propelled by the rising number of offices and buildings as well as the growing hospitality sector across the globe. Global events such as the ICC World Cup 2019 in the U.K., World Expo 2020 in U.A.E., FIFA World Cup 2022 in Qatar, FIFA World Cup 2026 in the U.S., Canada, and Mexico, are expected to drive the hospitality industry over the forecast period. The commercial category is further projected to remain as the largest revenue generator during the forecast period," explained analyst P&S.

"On the basis of region, APAC accounted for the highest revenue share in the HVAC market in 2018. This regional share can be attributed to increasing urban population coupled with rising disposable income. According to the World Bank, urban population in India has increased from 27.8 per cent of the population in 2011 to 34 per cent in 2017. Similarly, urban population in Thailand and Indonesia has grown from 45 per cent and 51 per cent in 2011 to 49 percent and 55 per cent, in 2017, respectively. Rising disposable incomes of the populace, predominantly in developing countries such as China and India, are also pushing market growth, as customers are now spending more on appliances such as air conditioning systems and refrigerators which are no longer a luxury, especially in India," continued the analyst.

The growing construction industry is also observed to be a major driver for the HVAC industry. In 2017, overall spending on the global construction sector reached about US$ 11- trillion and is further expected to cross US$ 13.5- trillion by the end of 2024, growing at a CAGR of 3 per cent from 2019-2024. India, China, U.S., U.A.E., Saudi Arabia and Qatar are key markets, accounting for the majority of spending. These countries are running several construction projects including major forthcoming events (such as World Expo and the FIFA World Cup), skyscrapers and the construction of new hotels and shopping complexes, owing to rising numbers of tourists in these countries.

"The integration of HVAC equipment with home automation technology is increasingly being recorded. With home automation, operation of HVAC equipment (such as temperature control and energy saving through smart HVAC systems) can be achieved. These systems also provide better temperature variability compared to conventional systems. For instance, with smart HVAC systems, you can pre-set the temperature when the house is unoccupied, which leads to energy savings for customers," added P&S.

## Global HVAC market

Around the world, residential, commercial and industrial buildings use some forms of heating, cooling or climate control technologies to obtain thermal comfort and satisfactory levels of indoor air quality. Increasing energy consumption in these buildings has been contributing to overall global warming, as the buildings directly use fossil fuels (heating oil, natural gas and coal) for obtaining space heating. Such heating and cooling applications in these buildings incur a significant cost. Therefore, the need for energy-efficient devices has led to the adoption of HVAC systems.

According to a new report announced in May 2019 by Markets&Markets, the global heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) market is expected to reach a size of US$ 136.5 billion by 2024, advancing at a CAGR of 3.9 per cent during the forecast period (2019-2024).
