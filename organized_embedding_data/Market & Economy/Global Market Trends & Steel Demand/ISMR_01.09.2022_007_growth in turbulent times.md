# Growth in turbulent times

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Reforms supported by investments in green and digital infrastructure and human capital can boost, and even double, the growth potential in some EU member states between now and 2030, according to a new World Bank report. The latest “European Union Regular Economic Report – Living Up to Potential in the Wake of Adverse Shocks" reviews how some EU member states can navigate turbulent economic times to foster long-term growth and inclusion following a pandemic, an ongoing conflict in the region and a cost-of-living crisis.

Many EU member state economies have recovered to pre-pandemic levels of growth, although at an uneven pace. The report finds that, in the long term, higher investment along with reforms (such as increasing labour force participation, integrating migrant workers, strengthening institutions and improving educational outcomes) can raise potential output.

The green transition, to put countries on a more sustainable path, was also highlighted.■
