# Forming the future in Germany

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

THE GLOBAL MARKET for forming technology had a total value of 18.1 billion euros in 2014, the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders’ Association) confirmed recently in Stuttgart. China, with a share of more than one third, is by far the largest market for this technology. Germany comes in second with 8% followed by the USA, Italy, South Korea and Mexico.

“In Germany, forming technology accounts for roughly 27% of overall machine tool production with a value of 10.8 billion euros. This number does not include parts, accessories or services. Their share has proven relatively stable over time. Accordingly, forming technology to a value of 2.9 billion euros was produced in 2014. This was 11% less than the previous year, after production output had previously risen sharply due to large orders. The largest group of machines involves presses, followed by sheet metal processing machines including bending, edging and straightening machines, as well as shears and punches,” said the VDW.

The largest buyer of forming technology is the automotive industry. The VDW expects a 2% rise in production output to roughly 2.9 billion euros for German forming technology in the current year. The industry sector exported roughly 60% of its production output last year. In 2014, exports declined by 4% and they dropped an additional 6% during the first half of 2015.

“During the first eight months of this year, forming technology sales increased by 3% and is thus making a positive contribution to turnover for the overall sector,” concluded the VDW.
