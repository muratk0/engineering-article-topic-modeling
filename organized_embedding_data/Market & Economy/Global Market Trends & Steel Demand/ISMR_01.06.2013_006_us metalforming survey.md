# US metalforming survey

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

ACCORDING to the April 2013 Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) business conditions report, metalforming companies expect a decline in business conditions during the next three months. Conducted monthly, the report is an economic indicator for manufacturing, sampling 136 metalforming companies in the United States and Canada.

The April report shows that only 28 per cent of participants predict that economic activity will improve during the following three months (down from 36 per cent in March), 56 per cent believe that activity will remain unchanged (down from 58 per cent the previous month) and 16 per cent anticipate that activity will decline (up from just six per cent in March).

Metalforming companies expect a dip in incoming orders during the following three months, with 37 per cent forecasting an increase in orders (down from 46 per cent in March), 42 per cent expecting no change (compared to 43 per cent last month) and 21 per cent predicting a decrease in orders (up from 11 per cent in March).

Average daily shipping levels also declined in April. Thirty-four per cent of participants report that shipping levels are above levels of three months previously (down from 42 per cent in March), 44 per cent report that shipping levels are the same as three months previously (compared to 42 per cent in March), and 22 per cent report a decrease in shipping levels (down from 16 per cent in March).

The percentage of metalforming companies with a portion of their workforce on short-time or layoff increased to 14 per cent in April, up from 11 per cent in March. The April figure is higher than it was one year ago when only nine per cent of metalformers reported workers on shorttime or layoff.

PMA is the trade association representing the US $113-billion metalforming industry of North America.
