# EUROPE IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

ISMR: What are your thoughts on current European markets for machine tool manufacturers, metal formers and fabricators, in the wake of the global economic downturn? How do you see European markets developing over the next six months? Will there be any specific regional pockets of growth?

FG: The European machine tool industry was very heavily hit by the economic downturn last year. Last year, CECIMO members were, in general, 50% down on order income and 2009 exports fell by almost 30% on a year-on-year basis. However, now there are signs of recovery. For the first quarter of 2009, we have seen a sharp rebound in order intake and this has followed through to the first half of 2010. We have now seen an almost 50% average increase in orders for CECIMO member countries on a year-on-year basis.

Looking forward, we think that the growth in sales shipments will continue but whether they will remain robust is still open to question. However, we are quite optimistic if future sales follow current investment patterns. For the next six months, I see a continuation of this increase in orders. However, I do not believe that we will reach 2008 sales levels again until at least 2014 – orders were down 50% in 2008 and it will take time for order levels to recover.

We are very export-oriented – around three quarters of our output is export (a lot is to outside Europe) - so we closely watch the situation in external markets. China is our largest external trade partner and is extremely important to CECIMO members. China increased its purchase of machine tools from Europe by one third in the last quarter, compared to the previous one. We expect that this year China will account for about 40% of the world’s machine tool consumption.

“CECIMO has become the voice of the European machine tool industry worldwide, representing around 97% of total machine tool production in Europe and over 40% of worldwide machine tool production”

One current trend in machine tool technology is to bring more intelligence and automation into the machine tool process. Our members produce a range of intelligent solutions to ensure the reliability of complex production solutions. The use of automation software in the process is becoming more and more important to optimise manufacturing processes.

People now talk more about integrated design, integrated manufacturing, reduction of time to market, reducing non-productive time (tool changeover, loading etc.), increasing operational speed and accuracy etc. Micro machining is another area of interest for high quality production and a lot of functionality. Machine tool energy efficiency is also a very hot topic, driven by customer requests and country legislation.
