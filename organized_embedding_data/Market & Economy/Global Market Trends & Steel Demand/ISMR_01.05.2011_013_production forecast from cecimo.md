# PRODUCTION FORECAST FROM CECIMO

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

CECIMO EXPECTS double-digit growth in production in 2011 as Europe’s machine tool market enters a ‘robust sustainable growth’ phase. CECIMO is the European Association of Machine Tool Industries, bringing together 15 national Associations of Machine Tool Builders, which represent approximately 1500 industrial enterprises in Europe, over 80 per cent of which are SMEs.

“The recent decline in machine tool production in Europe hit bottom in 2010. Strong growth in order intake indicates that the industry will continue its strong recovery over 2011,” it told ISMR.

In 2010, CECIMO production output reached 16.6 billion euros which is about 1 per cent lower than in 2009. External sales continued to be the driver; almost three quarters of last year’s production was exported, it said. Overall, CECIMO exports remained stable in 2010 at 12.3 billion euros.

“The apparent consumption of machine tools within Europe continued to decline for the second year. The size of the European market contracted last year by another 6 per cent against 2009 to 9.8 billion euros. Imports declined by 7 per cent to 5.4 billion euros,” it added.

“2010 clearly saw the rock-bottom of the decline in machine tool production, following the record high of 2008. 2011 will bring double digit growth in European machine tool production,” Frank Brinken, Chairman of CECIMO’s Economic Committee, predicts.

“Although we have been experiencing very strong growth in bookings over the previous three quarters, we are still a few years away from the production peak of 2008, before the bubble burst. It will take some time before this is reflected in the production numbers,” he added.

Frank Brinken is Chairman of CECIMO’s Economic Committee

“Europe’s machine tool production in 2010 amounted to one third of the world’s output, while a year earlier CECIMO members accounted for 43 per cent. This clearly and strongly demonstrates the dynamic development in Asian markets,” Frank Brinken stressed.

Europe’s lower market share in the machine tool market becomes apparent in the total export figures. For the first time, Europe’s share in the world’s exports dropped below 50 per cent compared with 62 per cent in 2009, said CECIMO. The major winners are manufacturers from highly developed Asian countries experiencing extremely sluggish domestic markets for the third year in a row.

“The global machine tool market is poised to grow and may reach a new peak in 2013, but the main growth is outside Europe, mainly in China and continental Asia. This brings a massive challenge, not only for our industry but also for Europe as a whole. We need to secure our share in this growth,” Brinken concluded.

“Our Asian competitors are not only massively increasing export sales at the expense of their profitability, but they are also enjoying low refinancing costs. This suggests a broad-based government endorsed approach towards securing market share in the surging regional markets. Europe absolutely needs to act before it is too late.”

CECIMO has called on the EU to sharpen the focus of its market access strategy on opening up markets in Asia and to fight anti-competitive practices. CECIMO covers more than 97 per cent of total machine tool production in Europe and more than one third worldwide. It accounts for almost 150,000 employees and a turnover of nearly 17 billion euros in 2010.
