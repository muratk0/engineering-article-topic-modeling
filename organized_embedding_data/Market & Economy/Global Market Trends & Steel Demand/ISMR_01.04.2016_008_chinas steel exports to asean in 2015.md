# CHINA’S STEEL exports to ASEAN in 2015

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

ACCORDING to the South East Asia Iron and Steel Institute (SEASI), China’s total global steel exports registered 112.4 million tonnes in 2015, an increase of 20% year-on-year. Around 30% of its total steel exports was to ASEAN-6 countries, an increase of 33% year-on-year.

“The third highest item of steel export from China to ASEAN in 2015 was hot rolled coil/sheets. The volume reached 4.2 million tonnes, an increase of 90% year-on-year. The majority of the exports were recorded as alloyed HRC. The biggest destination for (alloyed) HRC was Vietnam, at 3.2 million tonnes (80% of total export to ASEAN), and the volume represented an increase of 24% year-on-year,” said SEASI.

“Meanwhile, the export volume of HRC to other countries in ASEAN-6 was more moderate. Exports to Thailand registered 205,000 tonnes, a decline of 17% year-on-year. Exports to Singapore declined substantially from 68,000 tonnes in 2014 to 5,000 tonnes in 2015, and exports to The Philippines also dropped by 57% year-on-year to 156,000 tonnes. On the other hand, exports to Indonesia increased threefold to 155,800 tonnes. Exports to Malaysia also increased by 40% year-on-year to 177,000 tonnes.”

Exports of hot dipped galvanised sheet from China to the region registered 1.8 million tonnes in 2015, an increase of 36% year-on-year. A major destination in the region was Vietnam. Exports to Vietnam registered 786,000 tonnes, a two-fold increase compared to 2014. Exports to Thailand increased by 24% year-on-year to 464,000 tonnes, followed by exports to The Philippines at 342,000 tonnes representing a decline of 7% year-on-year. Meanwhile, exports to Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore registered smaller volumes of less than 100,000 tonnes each.
