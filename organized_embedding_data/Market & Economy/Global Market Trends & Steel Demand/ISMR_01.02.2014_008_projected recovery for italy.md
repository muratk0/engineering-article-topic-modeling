# PROJECTED RECOVERY for Italy

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

THE ITALIAN MACHINE TOOL, robotics and automation system manufacturing industry reached the end of 2013 in a stable condition. Compared with previous results, exports showed a slight downturn while an improvement seemed to register in the Italian domestic market.

As shown by preliminary data processed by the Studies Dept. of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the industry association, production in 2013 stood at 4,780 million euros with a 1% decrease compared with the previous year. Exports were once again the driving force of the sector. Stable at 3,615 million euros, -0.2% compared with 2012, they absorbed 75.6% of total Italian production.

According to the UCIMU study based on ISTAT data, during the first eight months of 2013, the main destination countries for Made In Italy products were: China (+9.5%) 294 million euros; United States (+9.8%) 254 million euros; Germany (-9.6%) 204 million euros; Russia (+6.7%) 125 million euros; France (-19.1%) 90 million euros; Brazil (-24.1%) 84 million euros; India (+3.5%) 78 million euros; Turkey (-33.1%) 67 million euros; Mexico (-35.6%) 50 million euros and Poland (-39.3%).

UCIMU believes that 2014 will show a definitive change in the Italian domestic market which, according to forecasts, should start to once again show signs of growth.

“In particular, in 2014 production will increase by 4.6%, reaching 5,000 million euros. With a 4.6% increase, exports will register a new record of 3,780 million euros. The export/production ratio will remain stable at 75.6%. After being down for three consecutive years, deliveries will show a positive trend: thanks to a 4.7% increase, they will reach 1,220 million euros, driven by the recovery in domestic consumption which will reach 2,145 million euros, 4.4% more than in 2013. Imports will also benefit, although at a lower level, from the increase in demand from Italian users, reaching 925 million euros, a +3.9% increase when compared with 2013,” outlined the association.
