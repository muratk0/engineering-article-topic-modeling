# BUILDING GROWTH

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: “Developing markets offer the best growth prospects for the construction sector”

ISMR highlights challenges and trends as well as growth opportunities in global construction markets

The global construction market is worth around US$ 7.5 trillion. Construction tends to track GDP growth but specific policies and events have an impact. Factors such as the euro crisis and the slowdown in the Chinese market have impacted global construction markets. However, by 2050, 75 per cent of the global population are expected to live in cities (from 50 per cent today) with the rise of the mega-city predicted.

Developing markets offer the best growth prospects for the construction sector, according to the HKL Group. In a conference presentation last year on global construction trends and forecasts, it confirmed that construction markets in developing Asia (worth around US$ 1250 billion) have registered growth of around six per cent and are focusing on infrastructure and energy and construction in countries such as China and India have experienced a slowdown.

“In Latin America, construction markets have been strong in countries such as Brazil (World Cup and the Olympics on the horizon), Chile, Panama and Peru. The Latin American construction market is worth around US$ 550 billion and offers good long-term prospects,” it told the audience.

“The European construction market is worth around US$ 1450 billion – markets such as Germany and Scandinavia are growing whereas indebted countries in Europe are still in crisis. In the US, construction is beginning to recover after a long recession but is still around 30 per cent below peak level. The private sector is driving growth whilst housing starts are still weak.”

It further confirmed that Middle Eastern construction markets are worth a total of US$ 450 billion and have grown by 5 per cent whilst the construction sector in CIS markets (dominated by Russia) are worth US$ 250 billion and have grown by four per cent.
