# INCREASED OUTPUT IN EUROPE

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

CECIMO, THE ASSOCIATION of the European Machine Tool Producers confirmed a strong upturn in this year’s output, stating that:

- The growth in Europe is forecast to be higher than that in the world
- Future trend is highly dependent on the soundness of public finances
- EMO Hannover 2011 confirmed Europe to be a leader in advanced manufacturing solutions.

“The European machine tool industry expects to increase production in 2011 by 20 per cent over 2010; up to 20 billion euros. The growth is driven mostly by exports, which are forecast to grow by almost a quarter to 15.7 billion euros,” said CECIMO.

“Recovery in European manufacturing also drives up machine tool imports and consumption in Europe. The latter will increase this year by 2 billion euros to 11.3 billion,” Michael Hauser, CECIMO President said.

Europe remains not only the biggest machine tool producer in the world, but is also increasing its share. “Our preliminary estimations show that we will increase our share from 33.3 per cent last year to 34.6 per cent in 2012,” Frank Brinken, Chairman of CECIMO Economic Committee estimated.

“The results for the next quarters are uncertain. European machine tool business, which is recovering from the crisis, is now confronted with another wave of uncertainty and deteriorating confidence in the markets, said CECIMO.

“There are clouds over the European and world economy. In Europe, they are reflected in hampered access to finance across the manufacturing supply chain. If the problems are not solved quickly in an appropriate manner, the situation can have severe impact on the machine tool industry in Europe. We are predominantly small and medium-sized companies thus we would be particularly affected if the negative scenario materialises,” Frank Brinken stressed.

“We need to ensure in Europe the efficient framework for research and innovation with the special focus on small and medium enterprises. This is the only way to ensure Europe’s success amid growing competition.”
