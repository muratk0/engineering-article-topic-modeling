# VIETNAM IN VIEW

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

ISMR previews the MTA Hanoi and MTA Vietnam exhibitions

“Vietnam is a promising market for sheet metal manufacturers”

Japan is one of the five biggest investors in Vietnam, totalling 11 per cent of foreign direct investment (FDI) in the country. Kyocera has announced its intention to build a factory near Hanoi to produce cell phone components, whilst Nikkiso will be expanding the company’s production of aircraft components in its Vietnamese factory in early 2013.

Global leading telecommunications and technology companies such as Nokia and Wintek are planning to set up factories in the northern provinces of Vietnam. Samsung reported in March 2011 that its mobile phone factory in Bac Giang province had achieved an export turnover of US$ 2 billion since official operation in 2009. These are in addition to multinationals such as Canon, Denso Manufacturing, Honda, Panasonic and Toyota which have already established manufacturing and assembly facilities in North Vietnam.

As a burgeoning centre for manufacturing growth, Vietnam plays host to two major manufacturing events this year, MTA Hanoi 2012 and MTA Vietnam.

## MTA Hanoi

According to a General Statistics Office report, the manufacturing sector in Vietnam experienced growth rates of about 10 per cent. Given the Vietnamese capital’s favourable geographic location, economic and political stability and strong potential for high-tech development, the manufacturing sector in Vietnam experienced growth rates of about 10 per cent.

MTA Hanoi 2012 will be held from 28-30 March 2012 in Hanoi. The companies at MTA Hanoi 2012 will showcase international technologies which include metal-cutting machine tools, metal-forming machine tools, test and measurement equipment and systems, cutting tools, tooling systems plus industrial tools and components.

Foreign investment in Hanoi recorded a three-fold increase for the first nine months of 2011, attracting US$ 999.6 million. Based on the national Index of Industrial Production (IIP), Hanoi grew 12.5 per cent between January to October 2011 compared with the same period the previous year. Hanoi lured US$ 875.1 million foreign direct investment (FDI) capital in the first half of 2011, up 6.7 times compared to the same period last year, according to the Hanoi Statistics Office (HSO), as VOV News reported.

MTA Hanoi is organised by Singapore Exhibition Services Pte Ltd (SES) and coorganised by VCCI Exhibition Service Co Ltd.

## EVENT PREVIEW

## MTA Vietnam

Since 2005, MTA Vietnam has been a link for providers of manufacturing technologies and metalworking services to meet Vietnam’s manufacturing market. This year, the 10th exhibition will be held from 3 to 6 July 2012 at the Saigon Exhibition and Convention Centre (SECC) in Ho Chi Minh City. According to VOV News, foreign direct investment (FDI) in Vietnam hit US$5 billion in first half of 2011. Ho Chi Minh City was the country’s biggest destination for foreign investors as it attracted US$1.47 billion of FDI-registered capital in the first six months. The southern province of Ba Ria-Vung Tau and Hanoi ranked second and third, respectively.

MTA Vietnam was first launched in Ho Chi Minh City in 2005 and the show has since grown to become Vietnam’s most comprehensive manufacturing solutions trade event, featuring at least 80 per cent overseas exhibitors. MTA Vietnam is organised by Singapore Exhibition Services Pte Ltd (SES) and co-organised by VCCI Exhibition Service Co Ltd.

MTA Vietnam 2011 attracted 9,974 attendees which included trade visitors, exhibiting staff, conference delegates and speakers, and members of the press. The total number of trade visitors totalled 8,243 – a 19 per cent increase on the previous show in 2010. Thousands of interested buyers scoured the halls over the four-day event to forge business relations with key industry players.

MTA Vietnam 2011 saw numerous business deals sealed at the show. Mazak, a leading machine tool builder, sold its latest model on the first day of the event. Other exhibitors, such as Amada, also displayed SOLD signs on their machines during the exhibition.

“There is a big market in Vietnam and we are dedicated to the manufacturing industry here as we would like to share our Japanese technologies with the Vietnamese industry. As for MTA Vietnam 2012 – we will definitely come back,” said Yukihiro Kikuchi, Group Leader of the Asia & ASEAN Department at Amada. ■
