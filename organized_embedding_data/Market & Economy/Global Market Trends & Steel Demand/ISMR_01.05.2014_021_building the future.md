# BUILDING THE FUTURE

[TARİH: 01.05.2014 ISMR]

We spotlight trends and areas of growth in global construction markets

Global construction, led by Brazil (the sixth largest economy in the world) and countries such as Argentina in Latin America, looks to be poised for positive growth. Brazil’s increased investment in infrastructure spending for the FIFA 2014 World Cup and the 2016 Olympics has boosted global construction revenues.

## Asian ‘tigers’ will fuel growth

Although the rapid construction market growth in China and the Pacific Rim has slowed down, their domestic construction market is still estimated in the US$ 2.1 trillion range. From 2014-2018, demand for construction machinery in China is estimated to increase and create many opportunities for manufacturers. China overtook the US to become the world’s largest construction market in 2010.

ISMR SAYS:

“Energy efficiency, permanent modular construction, changing design patterns and increasing urbanisation are having a significant effect on global construction markets”
