# An eye on Italy

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

IN THE first quarter of 2016, the index of machine tool orders, processed by the Studies Dept. of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian association of machine tools and robotics systems), registered a 14.3% increase compared to the same period in the previous year.

"The overall outcome was mainly due to the excellent performance achieved in the Italian market - a positive response also came from overseas markets. In particular, the index of orders from overseas markets recorded a 1.7% increase versus the period January-March 2015," said the association.

A much more significant upturn was reported for domestic orders, which increased by 31.8% compared to the same period in the previous year. Therefore, said UCIMU, the positive trend registered by manufacturers in the Italian market has now extended across eleven consecutive quarters.

Luigi Galdabini, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, stated: "In these first months of the year, demand for machine tools is getting stronger and growing considerably, after the slight slowdown experienced in the last period of 2015. All this proves that end-users, especially Italian ones, are willing to invest.

## Tata in focus

THE BOARD of Tata Steel Europe has announced that seven expressions of interest submitted for Tata Steel's UK business have been immediately taken forward to the next stage of the sale process. Tata Steel Europe is clarifying outstanding points with a number of other parties who have submitted an expression of interest. The remaining parties who submitted expressions of interest have been thanked for their participation in the process but informed that their expressions would not be taken forward currently.

"Besides the New Sabatini Law, the Super Depreciation provision is yielding benefits, even if below potential, which could be fully exploited with a small amendment to the provision. In particular, we believe that the fixed deadline of 31 December 2016 should relate to the collection of orders, accompanied by a down-payment bank transfer, and not to the delivery of machines, which should rather be postponed until 30 June 2017."

He also recommended the liberalisation of depreciation charges, where purchased machinery can be depreciated over a shorter time and the adoption of an incentive programme to encourage the voluntary replacement of obsolete machinery with new technologies, designed and built according to new requirements for productivity, energy saving and compliance with EU regulations regarding safety in the workplace.

“It is Tata Steel Europe's primary intention to assess expressions of interest for the whole of Tata Steel's UK business. The seven expressions of interest being immediately taken forward are on this basis. Expressions of interest for parts of the UK business are not being taken forward at this point," said the company.

This year's LAMIERA sheet metal trade show took place from 11-14 May 2016 in Bologna, Italy, which ISMR attended. Around 25,000 expected visitors saw new technology innovations from 386 exhibitors (30% of whom were from outside Italy). The show space was approximately 10% larger (at over 35,000 square metres) than the event in 2014. In May 2017, the show moves from Bologna to the Fieramilano Rho exhibition centre in Milan.

"In the first quarter of 2016, the index of machine tool orders collected in the domestic market registered a 31.8% growth," commented UCIMU President, Luigi Galdabini, during the inaugural ceremony at LAMIERA.

"Whilst the sales process continues, Tata Steel's business in the UK under the new leadership team continues to focus on the business performance," said Koushik Chatterjee, Group Executive Director (Finance and Corporate) of Tata Steel Ltd.

In the next phase of the sales process, the progressing interested parties will be given access to further business information and management team presentations to rapidly progress their interest to a binding stage.

In April 2016, Bimlendra Jha, an Executive Committee member of Tata Steel Europe, was appointed as the Chief Executive Officer of Tata Steel UK. Mr. Jha is currently Executive Chairman of Tata Steel Europe's Long Products Europe business and successfully led the divestment process that resulted in the signing of a Sale and Purchase Agreement with Greybull Capital on 11 April 2016. In this new position, he will report to Hans Fischer, Chief Executive Officer of Tata Steel Europe, and be supported by a team of executives drawn across functions including operations, procurement, finance, human resources and commercial.
