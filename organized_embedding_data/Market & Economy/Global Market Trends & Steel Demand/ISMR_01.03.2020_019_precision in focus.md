# PRECISION IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: The global market for medical devices is expected to grow at a CAGR of 5.33% to nearly US$ 521.64 billion by 2022.

"The market has the potential to grow by US$ 134.2 billion during 2020-2024, and the market's growth momentum will accelerate during the forecast period," analyst, Technavio, told ISMR.

"The medical devices industry is undergoing significant advancements in cardiology; neurology; orthopaedics; oncology and medical imaging. For instance, ECG stress devices have witnessed improvement in waveform analysis algorithms, connectivity with EMRs and wireless lead systems. Pacemakers have seen advancements related to battery longevity, implantation techniques and miniaturisation. The development of advanced brain monitoring devices is one of the key achievements of the last few years. These advancements have created a positive medical devices market outlook, and the market will continue to grow driven by further innovations," it added.

The growing penetration of medical devices in emerging countries is one of the key medical devices market trends. The operating margins for medical devices are significantly high in emerging countries. There is a large, growing and increasingly wealthy middle class in emerging countries who are willing to pay for high-quality medical devices that governments have not managed to deliver in the past. This factor offers a significant need-gap to medical device market vendors. Technavio expects the medical devices market to grow at a CAGR of over 5% during 2020-2024.

## Market growth

According to the 'Medical Devices Global Market Opportunities And Strategies To 2022" report, from ResearchandMarkets.com, the global medical devices market reached a value nearly US$ 423.8 billion in 2018, having grown at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 5.28% since 2014, and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 5.33% to nearly US$ 521.64 billion by 2022.

Historic growth has been attributed to rapid growth in the elderly population; strong economic growth and increasing access to healthcare facilities in emerging countries; the adoption of advanced technologies in the manufacture of medical devices and rising healthcare expenditure globally. Factors that negatively affected growth in the historic period were the lack of awareness about chronic diseases and decreased healthcare reimbursements.

"Going forward, faster global economic growth, increasing prevalence of chronic diseases globally and technological advances in the industry will drive the growth. Factors that could hinder the growth of the medical devices market in the future include regulatory changes that can potentially increase production costs for medical devices manufacturers, data security issues in connected medical devices, high costs and long payback periods for investments in new technologies and the expected rise in interest rates," said the report.

The medical devices market is segmented by type of equipment into in-vitro diagnostics; dental equipment and supplies; ophthalmic devices; diagnostic imaging equipment; cardiovascular devices; hospital supplies; surgical equipment; orthopaedic devices; patient monitoring devices; diabetes care devices; nephrology and urology devices; ENT devices; anaesthesia and respiratory devices; neurology devices and wound care devices.

According to the report, the cardiovascular devices market was the largest segment of the medical devices market, accounting for US$ 64.5 billion or 15.2% of the total in 2018. It was followed by in-vitro diagnostics, diagnostic imaging equipment and other segments. Going forward, the fastest-growing segments in the medical devices market will be the patient monitoring devices and diabetes care devices segments, where growth will be at CAGRs of 8.2% and 7.6% respectively.

## Regions and segments

"The USA was the largest country in terms of private expenditure on medical devices, accounting for US$ 88.98 billion in 2018. Going forward, the fastest-growing countries with private expenditure on medical devices will be India and the UK, where growth in expenditures will be at CAGRs of 11.39% and 11.03% respectively. The USA was also the largest country in terms of public expenditure on medical devices, accounting for US$ 76.92 billion in 2018. Going forward, the fastest-growing countries with public expenditure on medical devices will be China and India, where growth in expenditures will be at CAGRs of 12.12% and 8.85% respectively," said the report.

Hospitals and clinics were the largest segments of the medical devices market by type of end-user, accounting for US$ 372.59 billion in 2018, according to the report. North America was the largest region in the global medical devices market, accounting for 42.6% of the total in 2018. It was followed

by Western Europe, Asia-Pacific and then the other regions. Going forward, the fastest growing regions in the medical devices market will be Asia-Pacific and South America, where growth will be at CAGRs of 7.07% and 6.53% respectively. These will be followed by Africa and the Middle East where the markets are expected to grow at CAGRs of 6.47% and 6.43% respectively.

"The medical devices market is fairly concentrated, with a small number of large players constituting more than one-third of the market. The top ten competitors in the market made up to 35.8% in 2018. Major players in the market include Medtronic Inc., Johnson & Johnson, Abbott Laboratories, Becton, Dickinson and Company and Siemens Healthineers AG," commented the analyst.

"The top opportunities in the global healthcare market will arise in the cardiovascular devices segment which will gain US$ 16.17 billion of global annual sales by 2022. The medical devices market size will gain the most in the USA at US$ 35.33 billion. Market trend-based strategies for the medical devices market include investing in the research and development of self-diagnosis devices, developing and deploying technologies to capture and analyse data from medical devices, and collaborating with technology companies to develop and market devices to monitor and track the health of individuals. Player-adopted strategies in the medical devices industry include innovating product portfolios through collaborations and partnerships and expanding operations geographically through mergers and acquisitions," it added.

To take advantage of the opportunity, the analyst recommended that medical devices companies focus on the manufacturing of portable medical devices, ensure the reliability of connected medical devices, increase product portfolios through collaborations and expand in emerging markets, among other strategies.
