# TRUMPF UK announces new innovations

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

"THE LAST 16 months have been a period of extreme change in both international and national politics and economics, as well as inside TRUMPF UK. Most economic indicators are good (the stock markets in the most important markets are up) but the first signs of weakening are appearing. In the UK, we are facing a significantly reduced pound sterling value, leading to higher than originally expected inflation. Lack of salary adjustment of those in lower income segments will lead to reduced consumer spending. The low pound has also had an impact on the UK machine tool industry as many state-of-the-art machines are imported," explained TRUMPF UK managing director, Annette Doyle, at its recent Open Day.

"We do not know what is to come, with the Brexit negotiations entering a phase during which more tangible, possible outcomes will become apparent. We do not know which policies supporting manufacturing will be a result of Brexit. However, I do feel that everybody has become used to the uncertainty that now prevails in our private and business lives. That uncertainty, in itself, has become the new norm for everybody in which to operate and make decisions..."

She highlighted three major changes at TRUMPF UK, in response, including new product introductions in the UK market, procedural and structural changes in its UK customer service and TRUMPF UK's overall sales performance. The first TruMatic 1000 has now been installed in the UK (Bedfordshire), along with the first panel bender, the TruBend Center 5030 (in Shropshire). A TruPrint 1000 has also now been installed at LPW in Cheshire (UK).

"The first 16kW TruDisk Laser is now running at the AMRC - this is the highest power disk laser in the UK. In two months' time, we will be implementing the High Speed Eco cutting process, a development that complements our established High Speed cutting process (both showing vastly improved cutting speeds on existing machines). Our customers also got a first glimpse of our TruLaser Centre, the fully automated Laser Cutting Centre, at EuroBLECH and in our showroom in Ditzingen," she explained.

Rather than having a remote, Luton-based service management team in the UK, TRUMPF service now has regional managers. They are former service engineers who are based in the region which they are supervising. They are backed up by an internal support team and central management. The number of service engineers in the field and in-house has been increased to provide faster response times to customers, on the phone and on-site. A fully staffed technical call centre is now available until midnight so that engineers can troubleshoot, dial into machines and solve technical problems. Customer service has been TRUMPF UK's top management focus for the last 16 months, confirmed Doyle.

"I am especially happy to announce that TRUMPF UK is in the final months of what will be a record year for orders. We are confident of reaching an order intake of £55 million in FY 2016/17. We recognise that this is an achievement, for which both our sales team and our service team are to be recognized, and we thank our customers for the trust that they place in us. As a company, we will continue to adjust and improve our customer service performance and are looking forward to another Open House and a fiscal year!" she concluded.

## PEOPLE WATCH

Inspiring the engineers of the future

KMF MANAGING DIRECTOR, Gareth Higgins, has been awarded an MBE in the Queen's Birthday Honours list. Gareth, who has helped to steer KMF through 25 years of growth, is honoured for services to apprenticeships.

UK-based KMF has gained a national reputation as a leader in apprenticeship and training, supporting young people into a successful career in engineering. KMF currently has 225,000 sq. ft. of manufacturing facilities and employs over 500 staff, establishing it as the UK's largest precision sheet metal fabrication company.

The honour comes just a few months after KMF won the prestigious SEMTA skills innovation award, fighting off competition from major manufacturing companies including Jaguar Land Rover and BMW.

KMF invested £600,000 to create a purposebuilt training centre in 2009 and has trained more than 60 apprentices, providing skilled employees for the company and a chance for school leavers to gain high level qualifications and a long-term career in the engineering sector. This year, 16 young people will start their apprenticeship with the KMF Group.

KMF has gained many accolades for its apprenticeship programme and its award-winning "Young Engineer of the Year" programme which works with schools, colleges and businesses to inspire interest in engineering as a career. The company, based at Newcastle-under-Lyme, has successfully promoted engineering as a career for male and female school leavers and 19-year-old Chloe Quaile was recently crowned Apprentice of the Year in the 2017 Sentinel Business Awards. ## SMDI announces 2017 Lifetime Achievement award winners

THE STEEL MARKET DEVELOPMENT INSTITUTE (SMDI), a business unit of the American Iron and Steel Institute, presented Michael F. Engestrom (retired, Nucor-Yamato Steel) and Ronald P. Krupitzer (retired, SMDI) with SMDI Lifetime Achievement awards for their lasting contributions to the steel construction and automotive markets.

The awards were presented by AISI Chairman John Ferriola, Chairman, President and CEO of Nucor Corporation, during the Institute's General Meeting in Washington, D.C. The SMDI Lifetime Achievement award recognises individuals who, over the course of their careers, have had significant impact on advancing the competitive use of steel in the construction, automotive and packaging markets.

"We are pleased to recognise Mike and Ron, whose dedication, leadership and enthusiasm for the steel industry made transformative changes to the construction and automotive markets. They were both involved in introducing new tools and technologies to the marketplace and in implementing technology transfer practices to benefit those industries, while advancing the competitive use of steel. We commend them for their achievements and this much-deserved award," said Ferriola.

## Senior appointment to shape steel sector R&D

WITH THE APPOINTMENT of Tony Parkinson as strategic account manager, the Materials Processing Institute has strengthened its ties within the steel sector. He has joined to work with new and existing steel industry clients to manage ongoing client-focused research programmes and plan future research programmes. This includes promoting speciality metals and demonstrating how steel research expertise supports process improvements to enhance operating efficiencies.

Tony, 51, has more than 27 years' industry experience, having worked as a technical manager for the ironmaking and steelmaking sectors on integrated sites throughout the UK, and for various steel companies. He has implemented many innovations for product diversification, process control and optimisation, as well as quality improvements, throughout his career. While working in the ironmaking field, Tony helped his plant win a National Quality Award and has also worked on implementing recycling and environmental programmes.

## Lincoln Electric promotes Steven B. Hedlund

LINCOLN ELECTRIC HOLDINGS, Inc. has promoted Steven B. Hedlund to Executive Vice President and President of the International Welding segment. In this role, Hedlund will lead the Europe, Middle East, Africa and Russia plus the Asia Pacific regions' day-to-day operations and oversee the implementation of all strategic, operational and commercial initiatives. He will continue to serve as a member of the management committee. Hedlund succeeds Mathias Hallmann, who has decided to leave the company to pursue other interests.

Hedlund most recently served as Senior Vice President and President of Global Automation. He joined the company in 2008 as Vice President, Strategy and Business Development where he led strategic planning and acquisitions. Previously, he was with Fortune Brands, Inc. and at Booz Allen & Hamilton. He earned a Bachelor's Degree and an MBA from Dartmouth College.

"I am pleased to announce Steve's promotion to this role," said Christopher L. Mapes Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. "His extensive experience, process-driven approach and achievements in advancing our automation strategy will help drive the International Welding segment's next phase of growth and development with Air Liquide Welding."
