# Electroplating market forecast

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO THE "Electroplating Market - Forecasts from 2017 to 2022" report, the electroplating market is estimated to record a CAGR of 2.98% over the forecast period to reach US$ 14.217 billion in 2022, increasing from US$12.276 billion in 2017.

"The growth in the manufacturing industry, rising demand for consumer goods with good metal finishing, expanding demand for automobiles and aircraft and the surging electronics industry are some of the key market drivers. However, various governments are formulating regulations for the electroplating industry to keep a check on the harmful gases and chemicals released into the environment during the electroplating process, thereby restraining market growth," explained Research and Markets.

"The rising popularity of electronics products, owing to ongoing product innovation coupled with technology adoption and demand for electrical devices, is driving electroplating market growth. The manufacturing of electrical devices, components and electronic products employs electroplating for coating metal surfaces. Thus, as an industry vertical, the electronics and electrical segment is expected to grow at a high CAGR. Likewise, the automotive industry segment is also expected to witness high growth. The growing adoption of electroplating by vendors to coat vehicle bodies with rust-proof and non-corrosive material is driving automotive segment growth."
