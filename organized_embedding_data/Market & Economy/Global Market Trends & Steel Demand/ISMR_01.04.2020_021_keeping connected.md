## Global home appliances markets

According to the Freedonia Group, global sales of major household appliances are forecast to expand 2.7% annually from 2023 to 571.7 billion units. This will be driven by increasing home construction, as appliances are often purchased to furnish new homes, especially in industrialised nations.

“Throughout developed parts of the world, major appliances are considered to be essential for a number of household tasks, so most sales in high-income markets are often to replace existing appliances. The convenience of appliances, especially those that are considered more basic – such as refrigerators and large cooking appliances – is causing market penetration to grow in low- and middle-income countries as first-time purchases rise along with household incomes," said the analyst.

"China is, by far, the world's largest appliance market, accounting for 26% of global sales in 2018, followed by the U.S. with 10%. However, ownership of all types of appliances is already high in the U.S., and the market in China is beginning to mature, particularly for appliances that are considered to be more essential. Still, the two countries will together account for 25% of new sales through to 2023," it added.

However, lower- and middle-income countries in Asia and the Africa/Mideast region are expected to post faster advances as developing countries (such as India, Indonesia, Egypt and Nigeria) see gains from:

Middle class expansion that allow greater portions of the population to better afford appliance ownership.

Rising urbanisation and improving urban infrastructure with more reliable access to electricity and running water.

Rapid gains in the construction of new housing units.

"Innovations that improve energy efficiency are also very important to consumers in high-income countries. Additionally, product improvements which solve a particular problem experienced during the routine use of appliances are more likely to cause consumers to upgrade," explained the analyst.

## The smart revolution

"The smart home appliances market size has the potential to grow by US$ 31.66 billion during 2020-2024, and the market's growth momentum will decelerate during the forecast period," said analyst, Technavio.

"The market is fragmented. Companies are spending a significant amount on R&D to develop innovative and improved quality products and stay abreast of emerging technologies that have a bearing on their product lines. Panasonic Corp., Robert Bosch GmbH, Samsung Electronics Co. Ltd., Sharp Corp., and Whirlpool Corp. are some of the major market participants. Although the growing adoption of wireless connecting devices and IoT will offer immense growth opportunities, the high cost of smart home appliances will challenge the growth of market participants. To make the most of the opportunities, market vendors should focus more on growth prospects in the fast-growing segments, while maintaining their positions in the slow-growing segments," it added.

APAC region was the largest smart home appliances market in 2019, and the region will offer several growth opportunities to market vendors during the forecast period. The increasing dependence on appliances over manual labour for household work, increasing disposable income, expanding middle-class population, rising standards of living and the growing rate of urbanisation and new household construction will significantly influence smart home appliances' market growth in this region.
