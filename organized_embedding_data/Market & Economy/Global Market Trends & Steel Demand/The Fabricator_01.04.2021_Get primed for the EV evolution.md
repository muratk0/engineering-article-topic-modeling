Stamping In An EV World

As EV battery costs (and subsequently vehicle prices) continue to fall, more consumers than ever are making plans to buy EVs. Are you ready for it? Find out how to prepare for EV adoption and more at the Stamping in an EV World Conference.

This one-day conference, co-located with ALAW

®

, will explore how the transition from ICE vehicles to EVs and other autonomous, connected, electric, and shared (ACES) vehicles will impact metal stamping manufacturers.

Stay ahead of the EV curve with industry-exclusive insights into:

How the transition to EVs will affect stamping manufacturing

How soon electric vehicles will become mainstream

Which stamped automotive components are affected

Who are the major players to watch for

Hear from speakers Ramesh Paramaswaren of Ford, Paul Belanger of Gestamp, and Daron Gifford of Plante Moran.
