# China's sheet metal working sector

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

THE LATEST Brooks report on China’s sheet metal working sector is now out, listing current and forthcoming investment opportunities in the world's largest economy.

‘China's Sheet Metalworking Sector – A Market Overview’ examines activities in the sector and covers topics such as the economy; infrastructure, machine tools and metalworking; automotive sector; domestic appliances; aerospace; shipbuilding; railways and construction equipment.

“The report examines activities in the sector within the broader context of the country’s economy, now the world’s largest, and the challenges it faces as growth slows. It covers the principal sheet metal and sheet metal equipment user industries and identifies companies, trade fairs and organisations associated with sheet metal processing,” said Brooks Reports.

The company has also released an overview of Turkey's sheet metalworking sector. See www.brooksreports.com
