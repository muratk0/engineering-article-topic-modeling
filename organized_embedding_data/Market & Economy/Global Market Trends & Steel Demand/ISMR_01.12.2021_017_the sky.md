## We highlight trends, drivers, opportunities and challenges in global aerospace markets.

The aerospace market consists of sales of aerospace equipment (including aircraft) and related services by entities (organisations, sole traders and partnerships) that produce commercial aircraft; undertake aircraft maintenance, repair and overhauling services; and produce support and auxiliary equipment (such as radar, air traffic control towers and satellites for civilian purposes).

“The global aerospace market reached a value of nearly US$ 342.4 billion in 2019, having increased at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 3.1% since 2015. The market is expected to decrease from US$ 342.4 billion in 2019 to US$ 296.1 billion in 2020 at a rate of -14%. The decline is mainly due to lockdown and social distancing norms imposed by various countries and economic slowdown across countries owing to the COVID-19 outbreak and the measures to contain it. The market is then expected to recover and grow at a CAGR of 6.5% from 2021 and reach US$ 358.8 billion in 2023,” said analyst, The Business Research Company.

The global aerospace parts manufacturing market size was estimated at US$ 907.2 billion in 2019.

“Growth in the historic period resulted from increased demand for air travel technological advances, emerging economies, changes in social behaviour and low interest rates. Factors that negatively affected growth in the historic period were high exchange rate fluctuations, political uncertainties, volatile raw material prices and the grounding of planes.”

## Market trends and drivers

Going forward, the analyst could see growing demand for the commercial use of drones and rapid advances in technology. However, it also outlined factors that could hinder the growth of the aerospace market in the future such as budget airlines’ bankruptcy; order cancellations; increased cyber-attacks; global warming; poor aviation infrastructure; geo-political tensions; global recession and the coronavirus pandemic.

Major trends influencing the aerospace market include the use of cloud platforms for developing aircraft designs.

“All the major players in the market (including Airbus, Boeing, Gulfstream and Lockheed Martin) have started implementing cloud-based platforms to develop a new aircraft, engine, system or component design. The cloud platform enables simplification in the design process by bringing the suppliers, systems integrators, software developers and other stakeholders within the design and manufacturing ecosystem together over a virtual platform,” explained The Business Research Company.

Aircraft manufacturers are using machine learning techniques, such as artificial intelligence (AI), to improve aircraft safety and improve manufacturing productivity. Machine learning algorithms collect data from machine-to-machine and machine-to-human interfaces and use data analytics to drive effective decision-making. These technologies optimise manufacturing operations and lower costs. For example, GE Aviation uses machine learning and data analytics to identify faults in engines, which increases components’ lives and reduces maintenance costs. Boeing has successfully built machine-learning algorithms to design aircraft and automate factory operations.

## Opportunities and recommendations

“Top opportunities in the aerospace market, segmented by type, will arise in the aircraft Maintenance, Repair and Overhauling (MRO) services market segment, which will gain US$ 8.8 billion of global annual sales by 2023. The top opportunities in the commercial aircraft market, segmented by type, will arise in the commercial gliders and drones market segment, which will gain US$ 5.3 billion of global annual sales by 2023. Main opportunities in the aircraft maintenance, repair and overhauling services market (segmented by type) will arise in the commercial aircraft MRO services market segment, which will register US$ 3 billion of global annual sales by 2023,” added the Business Research Company.

It also highlighted opportunities in the commercial radar market segment, which it expects to register US$ 1.7 billion of global annual sales by 2023, as well as in the aircraft narrow-body segment, which will reach US$ 9.3 billion of global annual sales, it said, by 2023. By region, it expects the aerospace market size to gain the most in China at US$ 1.69 billion.

“To take advantage of these opportunities, we recommend that aerospace companies focus on the use of robots; the adoption of new technologies; automation to achieve standardisation; mergers and acquisitions; expansion in emerging markets; competitive pricing; the increasing adoption of internet and social media; and participation in trade shows and events,” it explained.

## Market segmentation and growth

The aerospace market is segmented by type, size, end-user, operation and geography. Types of commercial aircraft include passenger aircraft; aircraft engines; commercial helicopters; aircraft turbines; commercial gliders and drones; and rocket engines. According to the Business Research Company, the passenger aircraft market was the largest segment of the commercial aircraft market, accounting for 53.5% of the total in 2019. Going forward, it expects the commercial gliders and drones’ segment to be the fastest growing segment in the commercial aircraft market, at a CAGR of 18.9%.

“The commercial aircraft MRO services market was the largest segment of the aircraft maintenance, repair and overhauling services market, accounting for 63.6% of the total in 2019. Going forward, commercial gliders and drones MRO services is expected to be the fastest growing segment in the aircraft maintenance, repair and overhauling services market, at a CAGR of 52.3%,” added the analyst.

“The satellites market was the largest segment of the aerospace support and auxiliary equipment market, accounting for 69.1% of the total in 2019. Going forward, the commercial radars segment is expected to be the fastest growing segment at a CAGR of 4.5%. The commercial aircraft market was the largest segment of the aerospace market segmented by type, accounting for 71.1% of the total in 2019. The aircraft maintenance, repair and overhauling services segment is expected to be the fastest growing segment in the aerospace market, at a CAGR of 3.0%.”

It also pointed out that the narrow-body segment was the largest segment of the aerospace market segmented by size, accounting for 55.5% of the total in 2019. The private sector segment was the largest segment of the aerospace market segmented by end-user, accounting for 92.1% of the total in 2019 and is expected by the analyst to be the fastest growing segment in the aerospace market, at a CAGR of 1.2%.

“North America was the largest region in the global aerospace market, accounting for 49.7% of the total in 2019. It was followed by Asia-Pacific, Western Europe and then the other regions. The fastest-growing regions in the aerospace market will be Asia-Pacific and Western Europe, where growth will be at CAGRs of 3.7% and 1.5% respectively. These will be followed by the Middle East and North America, where the markets are expected to grow at CAGRs of 1.1% and 0.1% respectively,” added the analyst.

## Aerospace parts market

According to Grand View Research, the global aerospace parts manufacturing market size was estimated at US$ 907.2 billion in 2019 and was, in February 2020 (before the pandemic hit), expected to expand at a CAGR of 4.1% from 2020 to 2027. Rising demand for lightweight, new-generation and fuel-efficient aircraft, owing to the emphasis on reducing greenhouse emissions, is predicted to boost growth.

“The replacement of in-service aging aircraft with fuel-efficient aircraft is impelled by rising energy costs, which accounts for a significant share in the operation of commercial airlines. In addition, increasing emphasis to reduce total aircraft weight by using lightweight parts, attempting to improve its energy efficiency, is likely to have a positive impact on market growth,” it told ISMR.

The U.S., it added, is the largest market for aircraft parts manufacturing, owing to the presence of established aircraft manufacturing base, coupled with huge potential for aerospace Maintenance, Repair and Overhaul (MRO) activities.

“Tier One suppliers, such as systems and major structure manufacturers, engaged in the manufacturing of aero engines, turbines, jet and engine propulsion systems, experience severe pressure from Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs). As a result, the industry demonstrates increasing consolidation through Tier One of the supply chain, owing to rising mergers and acquisitions to reduce supply chain complexity,” explained the analyst.

“The market has witnessed a move towards the incorporation of lightweight materials for aerospace part manufacturing, on account of improvements in performance and cost-efficiency of the end-use structure. Market participants are exhibiting a preference for lightweight aluminium alloys because of its superior corrosion resistance and ability to retain strength at high temperatures.”

The players operating in the aerospace parts manufacturing market face competition in terms of product quality and design. They are targeting customers with customised requests to sustain in the market.

“The major players are likely to adopt emerging technologies, such as additive manufacturing, to replace the traditional manufacturing process of smaller aircraft components with complex structures,” concluded the analyst.
