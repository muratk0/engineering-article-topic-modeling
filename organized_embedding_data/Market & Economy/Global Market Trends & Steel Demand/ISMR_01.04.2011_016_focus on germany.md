# FOCUS ON GERMANY

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS

"The German machine tool industry is forecasting a 30 per cent increase in production output for 2011"

In the fourth quarter of 2010, order bookings in the German machine tool industry doubled once again. Compared to the previous year’s equivalent period, order bookings from October to December rose by 99 per cent.

The principal driver is still demand from outside Germany, which was up by 105 per cent. Domestic orders increased by 87 per cent. Overall, demand in 2010 was up by 85 per cent on the previous year. Domestic customers ordered 75 per cent more, while export orders came to 90 per cent more than in the preceding year’s equivalent period.

## FORMING FUELS UPTURN

“Recently, it’s been forming technology projects, in particular, that have been fuelling the upturn,” reported Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, Executive Director of the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders’ Association) in Frankfurt am Main.

Metal forming accounts for about 30 per cent of total machine tool business in Germany. Particularly prominent in this context is sheet metalworking which, in contrast to pressing technology with its heavy emphasis on project jobs, is able to run comparatively shorter lead times.

The high level of order bookings in the metal forming technology sector in Germany has also boosted production output: during 2010 as a whole, it was a mere 3 per cent down on the preceding year’s figure.
