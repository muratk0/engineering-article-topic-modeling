# Aluminium sheet forecast

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

THE GLOBAL ALUMINIUM sheet and plate market is set to grow at a CAGR of 7.04 per cent over the period 2015-2019, according to a report released at the end of January 2015. The “Global Aluminium Sheet and Plate Market 2015-2019” report notes that one key emerging trend in this market is the development of all-aluminium automobiles. Reduction in weight would significantly reduce carbon dioxide emissions and increase fuel efficiency of the vehicles.

Aluminium sheet and plate are the flatrolled product types that are used in the packaging, transportation and construction industries because of their advantages, such as being relatively light-weight and corrosionresistant. According to the report, the recycling property of aluminium is a key driver of the global aluminium sheet and plate market. Cost-effectiveness and efficiency in recycling aluminium, along with the continuous increase in the use of aluminium for the automotive and aerospace industries, will result in growth of the market.

The report also confirms that the main challenge faced by the market is the continuous reduction in the price of aluminium. Increased demand for aluminium over the past decades has resulted in the inventorying of aluminium globally in large numbers. The market can be broadly segmented into Alloy Aluminium and Pure Aluminium.
