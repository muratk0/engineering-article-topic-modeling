# Growth for global major domestic appliances market

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

FOR THE GLOBAL Major Domestic Appliances (MDA) market, research analyst GfK expected to see total revenues of €166 billion in 2016 and a moderate growth of one per cent. Built-in appliances in particular, it said, are enjoying record sales in Europe.

"In Western Europe, the MDA market increased by 5 per cent both in volume (units sold) and value during the first six months of 2016. This was mainly driven by positive sales trends in the Netherlands (plus 8 per cent), France and Sweden (each plus 6 per cent)," it said.

"Eastern Europe continues its strong development of the last three years, with an expected growth in the MDA market of 7.5 per cent. The strongest increase is in Romania, which is up 28 percent, driven by economic stability, higher salaries and increasing gross domestic product. However, Russia's market development for 2016 is very uncertain as it still faces low oil prices, high inflation and the prolongation of sanctions. After the slump in 2015, the sales figures have at least stabilised in the first half of 2016, rising 2 per cent."

In China, the MDA market, which is the biggest in the world, is expected to pick up during 2017. MDA markets in Latin America are in a deep recession. For the first time in Europe the built-in appliances market has reached 40 per cent of the total MDA, corresponding to an annual revenue of nearly €13 billion. See www.gfk.com
