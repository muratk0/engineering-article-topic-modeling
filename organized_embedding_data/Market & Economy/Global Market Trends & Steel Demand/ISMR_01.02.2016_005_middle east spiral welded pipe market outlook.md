# Middle East spiral welded pipe market outlook

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

THE HSAW/SPIRAL pipes market in the Middle East region is anticipated to flourish at a healthy rate from 2015-2021 backed up by the increasing infrastructural developments in most of the countries such as Qatar, Saudi Arabia and U.A.E. Announcements of major forthcoming pipeline projects in the Middle East would further boost the HSAW pipe market in the region.

Saudi Arabia is anticipated to have the largest market share of HSAW pipes, followed by the U.A.E. According to ERC, the Saudi Arabian spiral welded pipe market (by volume) will witness the highest CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) of 7.3%.

Citing the fact that there is more room for the utilization of existing desalination capacity in future and fewer requirements for the expansion of desalination plants, the market (by volume) of HSAW pipes in the U.A.E. is anticipated to grow at a modest CAGR of 5.5% during the forecast period.

HSAW pipe production in Iran declined drastically in the past few years owing to international sanctions. However, after the removal of sanctions in 2016-2017, Iran is anticipated to see a boom in the HSAW pipes market. Qatar would also emerge as one of the major attractive markets of HSAW pipes in the Middle East, contributing to some of the major infrastructure projects such as the Doha Metro and FIFA World Cup 2022.

A major challenge for the HSAW pipe market in the Middle East is declining oil prices in the international market, which has withdrawn most of the major industry players from investing in exploration and production activities in the oil and gas sector. This has led to the postponement and cancellation of major ongoing and forthcoming pipeline projects in the region.
