## The global market for home appliances

According to analyst Precedence Market Research, the global household appliances market size was evaluated at US$ 664.34 billion in 2022 and is projected to hit around US$ 1096.54 billion by 2030, with a registered CAGR of 6.46% from 2022 to 2030.

## Growth factors

“The global household appliances market is anticipated to witness a significant growth rate owing to the changing consumer lifestyle and evolving and growing economic pressure. The rising adoption of technologically advanced appliances is boosting the growth of the market. The proliferation of digital technologies has led to the development of energy-efficient and advanced appliances that offers enhanced performance at lower energy consumption," commented the analyst.

“Rising disposable income, improving living standards and the need for comfort are major factors driving the growth of the household appliances market. The demand for various advanced household appliances is rising owing to rising consumer expenditure on home improvement products. The easy availability of numerous brands and products at supermarkets and other retail formats has significantly contributed to the growth of the market," it added.

Samsung Electronics UK Ltd. launched its new single door Built-In Fridges and Built-In Freezers in March 2023. Users can track their energy usage with SmartThings Energy and access energy tips on their smartphones.

## Segments and products

Based on product, the global household appliances market was dominated by the 'Major Appliances' segment in 2021. The major appliances segment is further categorised into freezers; refrigerators; washing machines; dishwashers; air conditioners; ovens and others.

“The refrigerators segment was the dominating segment in the global household appliances market. The easy availability of refrigerators, rising disposable income, increasing affordability due to easy financing options and the growing number of nuclear families across the globe are some of the major factors that have led to the growth of the refrigerators' segment. The easy availability of energy-efficient and a wider variety of refrigerators (such as single-door, double-door and French-door refrigerators) are contributing to the growth of the refrigerator segment significantly. Rising investments in research and development by major market players to develop eco-friendly refrigerators are expected to offer numerous growth opportunities in the near future," explained Persistence Market Research.

On the other hand, small appliances are expected to be the fastestgrowing segment during the forecast period. The small appliances segment is sub-categorised into food processors; grills and roasters; tea/coffee makers; vacuum cleaners and others. The growing demand for small appliances for various daily routine tasks is a major factor that drives the growth of this segment. The easy availability of small appliances at affordable costs and rising standards of living are some of the major drivers of the small appliances' segment.

## Distribution channels

"Based on the distribution channel, the specialty store segment dominated the global household appliances market in 2021. The huge presence of various multi-branded and mono-branded consumer electronic stores across developed and developing regions and the higher popularity of these stores among consumers has led to the dominance of this segment in the market. The growth of this segment has therefore propelled significantly in the past few decades," outlined the analyst.

On the other hand, e-commerce is expected to be the most opportunistic segment during the forecast period. The most prominent factors driving the growth of the e-commerce segment in the global household appliances market are:

■The rising adoption of smartphones.

■The growing penetration of the internet.

■Rising investments by manufacturers in digital distribution channels.

■Huge discounts, easy return and replacement policies of e-commerce platforms.

■Rapid home delivery.

## Regional insights

"Asia Pacific was the leading household appliances market in 2021. The region has a huge consumer base due to the presence of a huge population. Strong economic growth; a growing urban population; rising government investments in the electrification of rural areas; the greater number of nuclear families and rising standards of living are the prominent drivers of the Asia Pacific household appliances market. Rising consumer awareness on the availability of technologically advanced appliances and growing demand for energy-efficient appliances is expected to further fuel the growth of the market. Asia Pacific is home to the world's leading producers of household appliances and ....facilitates the production of cheap and efficient household appliances in the region," explained Persistence Market Research.

North America is a mature market for household appliances. High disposable incomes; a strong digital and electrical infrastructure; strong living standards and higher demand for technologically advanced appliances in the region have fostered the growth of the household appliances' market in North America. The busy and hectic lifestyle of consumers and the presence of a huge number of nuclear families is augmenting the demand for innovative and advanced household appliances.

## Global market challenges

In its latest Annual Report, Electrolux set out a comprehensive list of challenges experienced by the global household appliance market in 2022. This included supply chain constraints; high general inflation; raised interest rates; soaring energy prices and increased geopolitical tensions.

Although industry supply shortages for semiconductor and electrical products industries remain far above their long-run average, these shortages have showed some signs of easing in recent months. Global electronics manufacturers faced intense cost pressures during the first half of 2022, due to rising international raw material prices.

COVID-19 lockdowns led to the closure of various plants and factories in leading regions such as North America, Europe, Asia Pacific, South America and the Middle East and Africa. This had a negative impact on the global supply chain and hampered manufacturing, delivery schedules and sales of various goods.

“The outbreak of the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020 had a negative impact on the consumer electronics' market. Lockdown measures and travelling restrictions imposed by government resulted in supply chain disruptions; lack of labour and the closure of production facilities. However, certain segments (such as dishwashers and laptops), witnessed positive growth owing to increased expenditure on home improvement products and rising work-from-home trends. E-commerce platforms also played an exceptional role in the sales of consumer electronics during the pandemic across the globe," explained analyst, Precedence Research.

## Kitchen appliances

Kitchen appliance manufacturers continuously develop, innovate and integrate IoT with kitchen appliances. Companies are focusing on reducing energy consumption and promoting sustainability as well as convenience. Appliances include devices such as ovens; refrigerators; stoves; blenders and dishwashers that help the user to save time, money and energy.

Data Bridge Market Research valued the global kitchen appliances market at US$ 241.30 billion in 2021 and expects it to reach a value of US$ 351.11 billion by 2029, at a CAGR of 4.80% during the forecast period.

“The rising introduction of advanced products is a vital factor responsible for market growth, as is the growing popularity of cooking reality shows and increasing disposable income. The growing number of restaurants and eateries will create new opportunities for the kitchen appliances market during the forecast period," explained the analyst.

"Furthermore, the introduction of novel shapes and sizes and new features for improved usability are expected to boost the market. The growing number of hotels and restaurants and government and association regulations governing cleanliness and hygiene are major factors driving the kitchen appliances' market. Consumers' growing preference for e-commerce is fuelling the growth of kitchen appliances. A growing desire to improve the visual appearance of various food dishes is also expected to drive market demand in the coming years. The introduction of modular and energy-efficient kitchen equipment (such as induction cooktops, speed cooking ovens and warming drawers) is also fuelling market growth," it added.

However, it pointed out, high initial capital and maintenance costs are expected to have a negative impact on consumer interest in new device developments. Uneven economic development in rural China, India and Africa is causing power outages. Approximately 50% of the population in 38 of 49 Sub-Saharan African countries live without electricity, which will have a negative impact on the size of the kitchen appliances' market.

## The smart home

Any collection of gadgets, systems or appliances that are connected to a single network and may operate independently and from a distance are referred to as smart home automation. A connected home is a more general term for a home's technology when it functions as one system. As an illustration, the home's thermostat; lighting; audio speakers; security cameras; TVs; locks; appliances and more are all interconnected into a single system that can be managed from a smartphone or via a mobile touchscreen device.

One of the major benefits of employing a smart home automation system is that it allows the user to remotely monitor their entire house. The rising focus on energy conservation and reduced carbon emissions are anticipated to drive market growth. Improved regulatory initiatives by governments for deploying smart home devices to save energy are encouraging people to adopt these products, thereby significantly impacting market growth.

## Italian machine tools

After an extremely positive 2021, the Italian manufacturing sector for machine tools, robots and automation systems closed 2022 with double-digit increases for almost all main economic indicators. A favourable 2023 is expected, although with lower growth rates. This was reported by Barbara Colombo, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association), during the association's year-end press conference.

According to data processed by the association, production reached 7,255 million euros (i.e. 2.5% more than in the previous year).

“Italian demand for machine tools, robots and automation systems was very dynamic in 2022, as it had been in the previous year. With a 31.3% rise, domestic consumption went up to 6,575 million euros, driving not only the deliveries of Italian manufacturers but also imports (2,595 million euros, or +38.5%)," commented the association.

The import/consumption ratio gained about two percentage points, standing at 39.5%. Based on UCIMU processing of data from the Italian National Statistical Institute (ISTAT), from January-August 2022 (latest available data), major destination markets for Italian products in the sector were the United States (281 million euros, +24.7%); Germany (199 million euros, -15.6%); China (122 million euros, -3.5%); Poland (111 million euros, +4.7%) and France (105 million euros, +9.1%). The export/production ratio decreased by about five percentage points, accounting for 45.1%.

"Despite the uncertainty factors, this positive trend should also continue in 2023," forecast the association. It believes that, in 2023, "production should rise to 7,565 million euros (+4.3% versus 2022), thanks to the upturn registered by exports (which should reach 3,375 million euros or +3.1%) and to manufacturer deliveries to the domestic market (expected to increase by 5.3% to 4,190 million euros)."

Consumption is also expected to continue growing, reaching 6,820 million euros (3.7% more than in 2022).

"Imports should also benefit from the dynamism of domestic demand, marking a 1.3% increase, which should bring their value to 2,630 million euros. The export/production ratio should drop again, standing at 44.6%," added the association. “Italian manufacturers' order portfolio in the first nine months of the year (latest available data), shows 8.1 months of production guaranteed by orders, the highest value ever registered over the last 30 years."

Barbara Colombo, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, summed up: “Despite problems such as high energy costs as well as difficulties in finding available raw materials, electric and electronic components, Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots and automation systems achieved new record results for most economic indicators in 2022."

"In particular," she continued, "there is evident dynamism of Italian demand, supported by the [Industry] 4.0 government incentives which, in 2022, are still offering extremely high rates (tax credit accounting for 40% of investment value). These rates will be reduced to 20% from 2023, unless amendments are made."

She appealed for incentive measures to become structural for the replacement of obsolete machinery and the digitalisation of production plants “because digital and non-digital innovation is a continuous process."

"With regard to immediate actions, we stress again that, for 2023, currently existing rates for the tax credit regarding investments in new production technologies have to be confirmed and the resources provided for by the Sabatini Law have to be increased," she clarified.

"We are aware that wide-ranging measures should be prioritised, to face problems that are particularly complex and of general impact on the community (such as the energy crisis). Nevertheless, the manufacturing industry is a pillar of the economy in our country and must be supported by provisions capable of enhancing the competitiveness of companies and ensuring their future, as well as stable employment."

She welcomed the idea proposed by Italian government authorities to ask in Europe for the option to employ part of the unused resources (amounting to 3.8 billion euros) allocated for Transition 4.0 in the PNRR (Italy's Recovery and Resilience Plan) to finance the tax credit measures "with the rates that are currently in force."

She also highlighted the strong record of Italian manufacturers in countries such as the United States, France and Poland. Less positive results for Italian manufacturers in Germany and China has prompted her to request increased funds to support the globalisation activities of Italian manufacturers. This includes resources to guarantee the attendance of overseas operators to international trade fairs in Italy and for initiatives organised with the ICE-Italian Trade Agency and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and of International Cooperation. She also mentioned the incentives reserved by SIMEST for the participation of Italian companies in exhibitions "of international relevance" held in Italy and overseas.

"We ask for resources not only for SMEs but also for Mid Cap companies which are the drivers of the production chains. These resources should be allocated for nonrepayable and soft financing of globalisation activities, such as the opening of subsidiary offices and branches and the creation of company networks overseas, as well as development projects for ecological and digital transitions," she concluded.

In the fourth quarter 2022, the index of machine tool orders processed by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE marked a 3.5% increase compared to the period October-December 2021.
