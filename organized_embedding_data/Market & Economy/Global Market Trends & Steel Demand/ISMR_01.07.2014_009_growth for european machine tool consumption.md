# Growth for European machine tool consumption

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

DURING the General Assembly that took place in Prague on 24 June 2014, CECIMO’s Economic Committee confirmed that:

• European machine tool production increased to 22.8 billion euros in 2013.

• The growth supported by a wider set of countries in Europe increased machine tool consumption by 2 per cent last year, making CECIMO modestly optimistic for this year.

• Policy areas related to improving access to finance and creating transparent trade conditions are still very important.

CECIMO is the European Association of Machine Tool Industries, bringing together 15 national associations of machine tool builders which represent approximately 1500 industrial enterprises in Europe, over 80 per cent of which are SMEs. CECIMO covers 98 per cent of the total machine tool production in Europe and about one third worldwide.

“In 2013, European machine tool production showed little growth and reached 22.8 billion euros from 22.7 billion euros last year, explained by the recent return to economic growth in Europe and improved business confidence,” CECIMO told ISMR. “However, growth prospects stay modest in Europe and so the outlook for considerable gains in machine tool production remain low in 2014.”

Transforming emerging markets have brought down CECIMO exports by 3 per cent to 18.2 billion euros in 2013. Recent changes in government policies in these emerging countries have cut the growth of investment in capital goods in the short term. In the medium term, the growing middle class will create an expansion in machine tool demand because of higher consumption in the form of rising per capita spending on manufactured goods and services.

European machine tool consumption increased 2 per cent in 2013 after contracting in 2012. After four successive quarters of economic growth, companies are finally upgrading their production facilities. Slow but wider-based growth and other economic indicators support the ongoing trend and CECIMO’s estimation of a 2 per cent increase in 2014.

“Domestic orders increased at the end of 2013, the index gained 6 per cent and 1 per cent in our quarter-to-quarter comparison and on a yearly basis respectively. CECIMO’s imports declined 5 per cent to 8 billion euro last year. Hence we can conclude that the companies investing in machine tools mostly used their domestic suppliers. This is further confirmed by the machine tool order intake,” added CECIMO.

“Demanding European buyers push local machine tool builders to constantly improve and innovate. Close cooperation with those clients ensures the competitiveness of the European machine tool industry globally. The trend to take labour costs to a large extent out of the calculation when investing in highly efficient production equipment is unbroken. Therefore, the increase in domestic investment activity is very good news for us,” explained Dr Frank Brinken, the Chairman of CECIMO Economic Committee and a member of the Board of Directors, Starrag Holding Group.

## Filip Geert, Director General, CECIMO

CECIMO is calling for coordinated action by the main players on the market, policy makers, the manufacturing sector and financial institutions, to improve the uptake of new production systems and ensure the competitive position of European manufacturing. It is also urging the European Commission to continue to support free trade based on transparent rules and to address distortionary actions decisively.

“Non-tariff barriers like certification requirements hinder trading. Bearing in mind that the European machine tool industry is dominated by SMEs, who lack access to deep legal expertise abroad, transparent rules are essential,” concluded Filip Geerts, Director General, CECIMO. ■

## Sheet metal champions

FOR the first time, the EuroBLECH exhibition (held in Hanover from 21-25 October 2015) will be presenting ‘Champions in Sheet Metal Working’, an online competition to recognise outstanding examples of high standards, best practice, innovation and excellence in sheet metal working. The competition will have five winners in total, one winner for each of the five main categories: 
1. Production in record time.   
2. Solution to a complex problem.   
3. Outstanding customer service.   
4. Production of an unusual product made of sheet metal.   
5. Excellent teamwork.

“If you think your organisation has performed exceptionally well in one or more of the categories, enter the competition and your entry will be published on the EuroBLECH website (www.euroblech.com). The deadline for entries is 29 August 2014. The competition is open to organisations of all sizes in any country in the sheet metal working industry. Winners will be formally presented with a prestigious award and a prize during a ceremony at EuroBLECH 2014,” the EuroBLECH organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR. See www.euroblech.com/english/competition/ ■
