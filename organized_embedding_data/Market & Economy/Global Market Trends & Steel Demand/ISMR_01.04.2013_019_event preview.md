## Major growth platform

The automotive, general engineering, IT, telecoms, construction and consumer goods markets in China are well-known to offer significant business growth opportunities. Whilst in recent months the global economic slowdown is showing an impact, economists predict a controlled and minor slowdown, revising China’s overall full year growth figures down from 8.6 per cent to 8.0 per cent in 2012 followed by higher growth through 2013. Investment in manufacturing continues at a very high level across all end-user sectors for sheet metal technologies and products.

BLECH China covers all sectors of the sheet metal manufacturing process, including metalforming and bending solutions; stamping; pressing; automation; flexible manufacturing systems; metal supply; cutting; punching; joining/welding; tooling; laser technology; CAD/CAM; finishing/coating; quality assurance and material handling.

Mack Brooks Exhibitions plans a series of seminars for the BLECH China exhibition – full details of the programme will be posted on the website (www.blechchina.com) nearer the date of the show. Its Meet the Buyer programme and technology seminars will invite key national and international buyers and players to discuss the hot topics within the industry throughout the 3-day show. The full programme will be announced in April 2013. If you require more information on this or are interested in participating in this existing programme, email: info@blechchina.com

“BLECH China 2013 is the dedicated sheet metal business-to-business exhibition for buyers and suppliers in the sheet metal and allied manufacturing industries to meet and do business,” the organiser explained. “Visitors will be drawn from the booming Yangtze River Delta and from right across China to see and experience the best sheet metal equipment, services, technologies and solutions for their business.

“China's manufacturing base is facing huge growth potential and more competition than ever. Keeping at the forefront of productivity and quality are key to sustained growth. BLECH China 2013 provides the ideal showcase of the leading metal forming and fabrication technologies, with visitors drawn from all the key end-user markets – from the automotive sector to micro-electronics.”

BLECH China 2013 focuses on the entire sheet metal buying chain in the Shanghai/Suzhou regions and across China. In a booming industrial region and with excellent links to all parts of China, the exhibition will attract key buyers to see domestic machinery and technology alongside the world's leading suppliers from Europe, the US, Asia and across the globe.

As the second largest industrial city in the country, a number of Chinese industrial clusters are based in Suzhou including electronics (31.2 per cent of total industrial output) and processing of ferrous metals (9.8 per cent). There is a development trend towards the hightech and IT industries, reflected in the concentration of globally renowned companies based in the area, including Acer, Philips and Siemens. More than 100 Fortune 500 companies have set up over 200 projects in the district.

Suzhou is often called the "Venice of the East" or "Venice of China" and boasts a deep cultural heritage. Originally founded in 514 BCE, Suzhou has over 2,500 years of rich history and relics of the past are abundant to this day. The city's canals, stone bridges, pagodas and meticulously designed gardens have contributed to its status as one of the top tourist attractions in China. ■
