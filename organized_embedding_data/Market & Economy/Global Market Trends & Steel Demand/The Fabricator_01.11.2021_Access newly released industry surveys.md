# Access newly released industry surveys

[TARİH: 01.11.2021 The Fabricator]

Get exclusive industry insights by analyzing robust data from the newly released Salary / Wage & Benefit and Financial Ratios & Operational Benchmarking survey results.

The 2021 Salary / Wage & Benefit Survey collects and reports salary, wage, and benefits data exclusively for the metal fabricating, processing, and forming industry. The survey results offer a detailed look at compensation for 78 production and administrative positions.

The 2021 Financial Ratios & Operational Benchmarking Survey provides comprehensive business performance data with key financial and operational details such as liquidity, profitability, asset management, operational benchmarking, value proposition, and marketing effectiveness.

Both surveys include a new COVID-19 Advisory section to help document unique challenges and innovations due to the pandemic that occurred during the 2020 calendar year. Each report is available as digital content listed at $395 for nonmembers and $175 for members.
