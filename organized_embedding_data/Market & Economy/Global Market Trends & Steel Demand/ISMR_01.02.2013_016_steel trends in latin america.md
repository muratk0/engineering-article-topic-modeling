# Steel trends in Latin America

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO ALACERO, the Latin American steel association, apparent steel use in Latin America and the Caribbean reached a volume of 54.9 million tons during January-October 2012, 5 per cent more than in the same period of 2011. Growth of regional consumption was mainly driven by Chile (+23 per cent), Mexico (+15 per cent) and Peru (+15 per cent). Production of finished steel increased two per cent compared to the same period in 2011 and extended to 47.5 million tons.

“In October 2012, the region´s finished steel use was 5.5 million tons, five per cent more than in same period of 2011. Production of finished steel reached a volume of 52.5 million tons during January-November 2012. Brazil represented 46 per cent (24.2 million tons). Mexico was the second largest producer with 15 million tons (29 per cent),” the association told ISMR.

“In November 2012, finished steel production reached 4.9 million tons in Latin America, ten per cent more than the same month in 2011. With 2.2 million tons and 1.5 million tons respectively, Brazil and Mexico stimulated this growth. Finished steel production in November increased one per cent compared to October 2012.”

Latin American crude steel production reached a volume of 61.3 million tons during January – November 2012, nine per cent less than the cumulated volume over the same period in 2011. Nevertheless, in November 2012 the crude steel production total (5.7 million tons) was nine per cent higher than in the same month in 2011. With 2.8 million tons, Brazil was the country with the highest crude steel production in the region in November 2012, two per cent more than in November 2011. Crude steel production in Argentina fell by 18 per cent compared to November 2011.
