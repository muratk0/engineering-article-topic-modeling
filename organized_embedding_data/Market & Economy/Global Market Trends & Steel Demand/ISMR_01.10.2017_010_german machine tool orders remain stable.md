# German machine tool orders remain stable

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

Order bookings in the German machine tool industry

VDW

Note: Index basis shipments 2010=100, data until June 2017, Sources: VDMA, VDW

ACCORDING TO THE VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association), in the second quarter of 2017, order bookings in the German machine tool industry fell by seven per cent compared to the preceding year's equivalent period. Domestic orders were down by 27 per cent, while export orders rose by four per cent. In the first half of 2017, total order bookings shrank by one per cent, with domestic orders decreasing by 15 per cent. Order bookings from abroad were up by six per cent. Orders for forming technology are performing better than those for metal-cutting machinery, said the association.

"With the mid-year figures, we're well on course for our expectations," commented Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, Executive Director, VDW. Export orders continued their upwards trend, with the Eurozone nations still constituting the principal drivers. Their orders rose twice as steeply as orders from the rest of the world. Following substantial growth last year, driven primarily by project business with the international automotive industry, the high level achieved is predicted to continue through 2017.

"Domestic demand, however, was a disappointment in the year's first half," said Dr. Wilfried Schäfer. This remained weak, due not least to a base effect from the high growth achieved during the first half of 2016. This effect, however, is now coming to an end. For the second half of 2017, significantly better figures are anticipated. This reflects the optimistic mood in the business community, rising cyclical indicators for Germany and macro-economic forecasts, which pundits have only recently revised upwards.

"Moreover, we're anticipating a huge boost from EMO Hannover 2017," added Schäfer. The trade fair for the metalworking sector, he explained, was very well booked and showcased numerous innovations in all technical categories. He expected it to provide a major boost to capital investment. See www.vdw.de
