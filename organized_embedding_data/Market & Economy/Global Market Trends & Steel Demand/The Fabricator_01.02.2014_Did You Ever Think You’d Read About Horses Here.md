By now your favorite Chinese restaurant has reminded you that this is the Year of the Horse, and if you looked up the characteristics of this year in the Chinese zodiac, you would have learned that this is a propitious year and that Horse is second only to Dragon when it comes to good fortune in the business category. It also seems appropriate for manufacturers to be looking at a good start to a year named after the most reliable of working animals. What about this year can we expect to be horse-like?

The character traits associated with the horse are strength, intelligence, ability to multitask, and commitment to follow through. This sounds like a pretty apt description of today’s manufacturer and the traits that are needed to succeed. In 2014 there will be a very obvious need to handle a plethora of demands all at the same time. This is the year that health care takes center stage, but it is also the year that consumers start to get engaged and foreign competition heats up. It may well be the year that inflation starts to show itself in the higher costs of commodities. It will be good to be a multitasker.

The issue of commitment will be paramount as well. The polls show that many people have become increasingly annoyed at poor service and lack of quality. Now that the economy is showing signs of life, people will be less attracted to lower prices if this means accepting poor performance and lack of commitment from those they buy from.

The strength and intelligence of the horse manifest in the manufacturer as well — especially after the last several years. The survivors of the recession are poised to grow and take advantage of the market opportunities they see now. The challenge will be to bite off only what can be chewed, even as the temptation to make big moves becomes too much to resist. Many of the companies that went by the wayside in the aftermath of the recession were those that took risks they couldn’t cover. The business owners active today know they can’t stand pat, but they also know they have to be smart about expansion and growth.
