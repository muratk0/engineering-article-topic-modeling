# INDUSTRY REPORT

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

## India and Turkey

Netscribes (India) Pvt. Ltd., a knowledge consulting solutions company, recently announced the launch of its report ‘The Domestic Refrigerator Market in India 2012’.

“The market has seen huge transformation over the years starting from traditional direct cool refrigerators to the evolved frost-free refrigerators. Due to rising income levels and easy financing schemes, demand for such products is witnessing steady growth.

“Growth in the retail sector and improved social indicators such as an increase in discretionary spending also promotes the growth of consumer goods. Government initiatives aiming at enhancing international trade coupled with relaxation of FDI policies provides many lucrative opportunities for existing players to grow and expand.

“New financing strategies and availability of credit have helped consumers to easily afford high priced consumer products. High input costs and import duties are the major constraints faced by the manufacturers in this market. Environmental hazards and poor response from rural areas have also restricted the growth of the refrigerator market,” said Netscribes.

The domestic refrigerator market in India was estimated to be worth INR 97.69 bn in 2010. According to a Deloitte report ‘Turkish Home Appliances and Electronics Industry’, Turkey has risen to become one of Europe’s leading home appliances manufacturers. The home appliances sector in Turkey earned revenues of USD 8 billion in 2008. Approximately 16 million units were produced of which approximately 12 million were exported, both in 2008 and 2009.

“The expected growth is a combined result of the growing population and disposable income, together with a trend towards smaller households which may increase spending on home appliances. The relatively young population in Turkey is the main force behind the increase in PC penetration and audio-video equipment,” says the report. Arcelik, Vestel Beyaz Esya, BSH Ev Aletleri, Indesit and Kumtel are the main home appliance producers in Turkey. The manufacture of white goods takes place predominantly in the Marmara, Aegean and Central Anatolia regions.

According to the Economist Intelligence Unit, the market demand for home appliances and electronics is expected to continue its growth in Turkey between 2009 and 2013. The expected CAGR for electrical appliances and white goods was 10 per cent whereas the highest growth was expected in household audio & video equipment with a CAGR of 12 per cent. ■
