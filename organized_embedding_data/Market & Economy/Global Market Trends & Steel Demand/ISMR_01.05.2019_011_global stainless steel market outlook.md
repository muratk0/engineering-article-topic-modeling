# Global stainless steel market outlook

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

THE GLOBAL STAINLESS steel market size is projected to reach US$ 133.84 billion by 2025, according to a new report. It is projected to expand at a CAGR of 5.2% during the forecast period. Increasing production of automotive and growth of construction sector are key factors driving the growth.

The "Stainless Steel Market Size, Share & Trends Analysis Report By Grade (200 Series, 300 Series, 400 Series, Duplex Series), By Product (Long, Flat), By Application and Segment Forecasts, 2019 - 2025" report has been added to ResearchAndMarkets.com's offering.

"Because of its high resistance to corrosion, stainless steel can be easily fabricated in building structures. As well as aesthetic purposes, it provides strength and support to various building structures. It is used in roofing, structures, fixings, drainage and water systems, handrails and wall support products. Rising investment in this sector and a number of construction activities are creating a high demand for stainless steel products," said the report.

"For instance, as per the 13th five-year plan of the Civil Aviation Administration of China (CAAC), China is aiming to build around 74 more airports by 2020. It is also planning to construct 30,000km of highways and one million kilometres of rural roads. This plan was also created to achieve cumulative sales and production of five million electric vehicles by 2020."

The report also pointed to increasing demand for stainless steel in automotive products such as frames; exhaust systems; wheel rims; engine cradles; floor panels; gaskets and suspension systems. However, it also highlighted the increasing use of aluminium and carbon fibre in automotive components citing benefits such as fuel efficiency, recyclability, durability and environmental safety.

"In 2017, global production of vehicles increased by 2.3%, compared to 2016, and reached 97.3 million units of production. This is also projected to assist the growth of the stainless steel market," it added.

Further key findings from the report suggest that 300 series grade steel is anticipated to reach US$ 71.9 billion by 2025, at an estimated CAGR of 5.1%. In terms of revenue, the long products segment is projected to expand at a CAGR of 4.7% over the forecast period. Automotive applications took the leading market share of 27.3% in 2018.

"The European stainless steel market is anticipated to reach 7,233.5 kilotons by 2025. In terms of revenue, Asia Pacific is projected to witness a significant CAGR of 4.7% over the forecast period, owing to a rise in infrastructure investments and demand for vehicles," concluded the report.
