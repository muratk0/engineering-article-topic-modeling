# MANIFESTO FOR COPPER IN EUROPE

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

The European copper industry has launched its “Manifesto for a Competitive European Copper Industry”. This paper declares the principles that can secure a competitive European copper industry and were the focus of a policy debate hosted recently at the European Parliament. Speakers from the copper industry, the European Commission and the Energy and Climate sector debated how to achieve an appropriate balance between EU policies that focus on sustainability and resource efficiency and those necessary to maintain a competitive business environment in Europe.

John Schonenberger, Chief Executive of the European Copper Institute, said: “Copper plays an essential role in our everyday lives. Copper’s superior electrical conductivity improves energy efficiency and facilitates renewables, both of which reduce CO2 emissions. Europe has the cleanest and most energy-efficient copper smelters in the world. 40% of the European copper demand is met today through the recycling of end-of-life products and offcuts from the downstream value.”

“But our industry is facing challenges in a globalised world such as, for instance, tough competition from countries with lower environmental and social standards. If we want to maintain our competitiveness and to fund continued EU innovation, European regulatory environment should allow us to remain competitive on a global market.”

The debate was followed by the opening of the exhibition ‘Copper – Essential for Everyone’ attended by high level representatives of the European Institutions and the copper industry.
