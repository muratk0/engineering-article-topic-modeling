## Airbus exceeds targets in 2015

AIRBUS HAS exceeded its targets for 2015, achieving a new record of 635 aircraft deliveries for 85 customers (of which ten are new). These deliveries comprise 491 A320 Family aircraft; 103 A330s; 27 A380s and 14 A350 XWBs. This production achievement means that Airbus’ aircraft deliveries in 2015 were up for the thirteenth year in a row, surpassing the previous year-end delivery record of 629 aircraft set in 2014.

Airbus also achieved 1,036 net orders from 53 customers (of which eight are new), comprising 897 single-aisle aircraft and 139 wide-bodies. At the 2015 year-end, the overall backlog had climbed to a new industry record of 6,787 aircraft valued at US$ 996.3 billion at list prices.

Overall, 2015 has been a year of solid and wide-ranging Airbus accomplishments. For example, the A320neo was certified by the aviation authorities on both sides of the Atlantic five years after its launch. Airbus also delivered 14 A350s. Important progress was made on the A350 programme’s next variant, the A350-1000 – whose major components and structures are now taking shape across various production sites. Likewise, parts are now in production for the first A330neo – with the machining of its first engine pylon and centre wing-box components

“Another notable highlight was September’s official opening of the first Airbus factory in the US, at Mobile on Alabama’s Gulf Coast, where between 40 and 50 A320 Family aircraft will be produced annually by 2018. On the other side of the globe in China, additional orders for the A330 not only complements our plans for a new A330 completion and delivery centre in Tianjin, but also helps to smooth our transition towards the A330neo. Furthermore, in 2015, we launched three new incremental aircraft developments which include the Long-Range version of the A321neo; the Regional version of the A330 which is optimised to seat up to 400 passengers on missions of up to 3,000nm and the Ultra-Long-Range version of the A350- 900, capable of 19-hour flights,” Airbus told ISMR.

## SteelFab opens its doors

THE 12TH STEELFAB 2016 was inaugurated by H.E. Sheikh Khalid Bin Abdullah Bin Sultan Al Qassimi, Chairman of Sharjah Sea Ports and Customs, at Expo Centre Sharjah on 17 January 2016. The four-day exhibition in Sharjah, at which ISMR was distributed and the team had a booth, provided the regional steel fabrication and metal working industry with machinery and equipment in metal forming; surface preparation and finishing; grinding and cutting; machining and other allied engineering disciplines. A fastenings exhibition was also held alongside the event.

The 12th edition of the show featured 370 exhibitors representing almost 1,000 brands from across the world, covering new vertical special segments, specific focus areas, live displays and technical presentations and seminars.

SteelFab 2016 is organized and hosted by Expo Centre Sharjah with the support of the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry. See www.steelfabme.com
