# Turkey’s sheet metalworking sector

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

BROOKS REPORTS has launched its first report on Turkey’s sheet metalworking sector (a market overview).

“With GDP now at around €700 billion, Turkey is rated as the world’s 16th, and Europe’s 6th, largest economy. This report highlights the broader context of sheet metal processing as well as the economy and infrastructure and also provides insight into topics such as the economy, infrastructure (transport, energy and telecommunications), machine tools and metalworking plus various industry sectors,” it told ISMR.
