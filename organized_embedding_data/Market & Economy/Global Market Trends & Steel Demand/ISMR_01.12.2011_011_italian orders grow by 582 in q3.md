# ITALIAN ORDERS GROW BY 58.2% IN Q3

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

THE ITALIAN machine tools index of orders has grown significantly with an increase of 58.2 per cent in the third quarter 2011 compared to the period July – September 2010, for an absolute index value of 73.9 (base 2005=100). This has emerged from a survey drawn up by the Studies Dept. of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the association of Italian machine tools, robot and automation manufacturers.

“The excellent performance of overseas orders continues but interesting data, emerging from our quarterly survey, is the awakening of Italian domestic demand. This reversal in trend, at such an uncertain point in time, gives positive hope for the immediate future and is an excellent base from which to start off after the halt in capital goods investment in 2009,” said Giancarlo Losma, UCIMU President.

For the first time, after four consecutive quarters of decreases, the index of orders for the Italian domestic market has taken off again marking an increase of 96.8 per cent. Nonetheless, the absolute value is still low reaching a quota of 30.3.

The performance of orders placed in overseas markets was excellent, UCIMU told ISMR, marking an increase of 32.3 per cent compared to the same period in the previous year, for an absolute value of 114.3.

“Despite the uncertain context, the Italian sector has recovered a good share of the ground lost during the 2009 crisis by strongly increasing its activity in foreign markets,” added Losma.

During the first six months of the year, Italian machine tool exports increased by 27.8 per cent compared to the same period in 2010. Deliveries grew in Germany (+67.3%), which regains its position as the main outlet market for Italian products in this sector.

Sales in China slightly decreased (-1.3%). Positive results were mooted for deliveries to the United States (+100.9%), France (+6.3%), Brazil (+63.4%), India (+40%), Russia (+8.7%) and Turkey (+113.1%).

Lamiera and BI-MU, key manufacturing exhibitions for the Italian market, will take place in 2012 and could give a further boost to the recovery of Italian domestic consumption.

## EUROPE GAINS MARKET SHARE IN WORLD’S MACHINE TOOL PRODUCTION

CECIMO, THE Association of the European Machine Tool Producers, confirmed a strong upturn in this year’s output. The European machine tool industry expects to increase production in 2011 by 20 per cent over 2010; up to 20 billion Euros. The growth is driven mostly by exports which are forecast to grow by almost a quarter to 15.7 billion Euros, CECIMO told ISMR.

“Recovery in European manufacturing also drives up machine tool imports and consumption in Europe. The latter will increase this year by 2 billion Euros to 11.3 billion,” Michael Hauser, CECIMO President, explained. Compared to the world, Europe remains not only the biggest machine tool producer in the world but is also increasing its share.

“Our preliminary estimations show that we will increase our share from 33.3 per cent last year to 34.6 per cent in 2012,” Frank Brinken, Chairman of CECIMO’s Economic Committee, estimated.

“There are clouds over the European and world economy. In Europe, they are reflected in hampered access to finance across the manufacturing supply chain. If the problems are not solved quickly in an appropriate manner, the situation can have a severe impact on the machine tool industry in Europe.

We are predominantly small- and medium-sized companies, thus we would be particularly affected if the negative scenario materialises,” Frank Brinken stressed.

“Innovation paves the way for the further growth in the European machine tool industry, it is our lifeblood. In Europe, we need to ensure an efficient framework for research and innovation with a special focus on small and medium enterprises. This is the only way to ensure Europe’s success amid growing competition.”
