# Metal roofing forecasts

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

ORBISRESEARCH.COM has added a new research report on the global roofing materials market, with a forecast to 2025, to its database.

The global roofing material market is anticipated to be worth US$142.88 billion by 2025, according to this new report by Grand View Research, Inc. Rapidly growing infrastructure demand, owing to the migration of people from rural to urban areas and improved standards of living (resulting in the need for building renovation projects etc.) are some of the key factors expected to support the demand for these materials.

"Asia Pacific is expected to emerge as the largest as well as the fastest growing region. North America is slated to register the second highest growth rate. The residential application segment is likely to emerge as the largest as well as the fastest growing application segment. Positive growth rates recorded by the residential construction industry in developed regions is anticipated to support the new residential roofing market, as well as re-roofing activities." commented the analyst.

"High brand loyalty in addition to major players focusing on establishing long-term relationships with customers is a primary characteristic of this industry. Market participants with a focus on achieving competitive advantage provide a comprehensive product portfolio of roofing materials."

Further key findings from the study suggest that:

The metal roof is expected to emerge as the fastest growing product during the forecast period and is expected to grow at an estimated CAGR of 3.3%.

Metal roofs are slated to gain ground with the development of pre-engineered steel buildings for non-residential applications.

The tile roof segment is anticipated to emerge as the largest product segment but is likely to register low growth rates compared to asphalt shingles.

Shingles, which are widely used in the North American market, have witnessed robust growth in developing regions.

According to the report, the residential application segment is expected to emerge as the fastest as well as the largest application segment. This segment accounted for over 55% of demand in 2015. North America is anticipated to grow at an estimated CAGR of 2.9% during the forecast period.

According to the Metal Roofing Alliance (www.MetalRoofing.com), the residential metal roofing market has more than tripled in recent years, growing from a 3% market share in 1998 to over 11% in 2016 with more growth expected. According to a recent independent study, conducted by Dodge Data & Analytics, the top reasons that homeowners choose a metal roof include longevity (26%), strength/protection (22%), attractiveness (15%) and good investment/ adds value (15%).

"Unlike most other home improvement purchases, a quality metal roof will last decades, providing homeowners with investment grade performance. In addition, many residential metal roofs now utilise reflective pigment technology, which results in overall home energy efficiency and lower utility bills," it said.

"The US demand for residential roofing products is forecast to advance 4 per cent per year to 147 million squares in 2020, valued at US$ 10.4 billion. Growth will be boosted by an increase in housing starts, which will fuel demand for roofing as well as other building products. Value gains for roofing will benefit from homeowner interest in choosing products that offer enhanced aesthetics and long-term protection against leaks and impact damage."

The re-roofing segment accounts for the vast majority of residential roofing demand. While weather-related activity plays a key role in generating replacement demand, the US's large stock of older homes is the main source of re-roofing demand.

## Indian market penetration

LIBELLULA SRL AND GVK Metalforms Private Ltd. have announced their partnership for the installation of Libellula software in machines and systems for sheet metal cutting by GVK Metalforms and for the provision of related technical support to customers in the Indian market. Through this partnership, GVK Metalforms receives official recognition as a LAC (Libellula Authorised Centre).

"Our LAC affiliation programme is really an innovation in the field," explained Umberto Cammardella, CEO of Libellula. "It is not an ordinary commercial agreement but a real partnership based, first of all, on shared corporate values and a common vision of how we do business. Our LACs are really a local Libellula extension. With our partners, we develop a common growth programme based on continuous training of local staff, constant technical assistance, the definition of agreed plans and marketing actions and on joint participation in exhibitions and local events."

The new partnership between Libellula and GVK Metalforms will be demonstrated through their joint participation at BLECH India 2017, the international sheet metal working exhibition held in Mumbai from 27-29 April 2017

In a separate development, Step Automation and GVK Metalforms Private Ltd. have also announced their collaboration for promoting sales of CNC controls manufactured by Step Automation (for CNC press brakes and shears). GVK Metalforms will promote the sales of CNC controllers and other allied equipment in the Indian market and will also support customers technically for applications and trouble-shooting.
