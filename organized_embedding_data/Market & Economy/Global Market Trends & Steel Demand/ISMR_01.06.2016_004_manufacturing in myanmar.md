# Manufacturing in Myanmar

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

MYANMAR HAS set its Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) target for the fiscal year 2015- 2016 at US$ 6 billion, with the Myanmar Investment Commission expecting to exceed the amount this year. Top sectors identified by the commission's secretary, U Aung Naing Oo, include infrastructure development, offshore natural gas and manufacturing. The country's economy is also projected to grow 8.4 per cent in 2016 due to an increase in foreign investment and a recovery in its agricultural sector.

With this optimistic outlook, prospects for Myanmar's manufacturing sector are bright. The third edition of Manufacturing Myanmar 2016, the country's only dedicated manufacturing trade exhibition, will return from 22 -24 September 2016 at the Myanmar Event Park in Yangon.

"The change in government and ongoing reforms has led to increased interest from international investors, as many are looking for business opportunities in the country. The first phase of the Thilawa special economic zone was launched in September 2015, with 70 per cent of the area already contracted to foreign and local companies. Most of the overseas companies are from Japan, but there are also companies from China and South Korea," explained the show organiser, SES.

About 70 exhibitors from 13 countries and regions including China; Germany; India; Italy; Japan; Korea; Malaysia; Myanmar; Singapore; Taiwan; Thailand; United Kingdom and the USA will be highlighting home-grown technologies and solutions. See http://manufacturingmyanmar.com
