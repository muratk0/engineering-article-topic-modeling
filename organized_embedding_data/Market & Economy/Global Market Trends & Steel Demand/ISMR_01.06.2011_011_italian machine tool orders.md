# ITALIAN MACHINE TOOL ORDERS

[TARİH: 01.06.2011 ISMR]

There has been a double figure increase in the index of machine tool orders that, in the first quarter of 2011, has grown by 19.1 per cent compared to the same period in the previous year says the studies Dept. of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation manufacturers’ association).

“notwithstanding this increase, the gap between internal market performance, which is still stagnant, and the overseas market, which has brilliantly recovered most of the ground lost in 2009, remains quite profound,” said the association.

“The index of orders from the domestic market suffered a 1.3 per cent drop over the same period in 2010. however, the feedback received by Italian manufacturers in overseas markets is satisfying, as shown by the index of orders collected abroad that, following a growth of 32.7 per cent compared to the same period in 2010, reached an absolute value of 141.9, nearing once more the record levels of 2007.”

In 2010, China was the top destination for the Made-in-Italy sector, gaining 14.2 per cent of total exports by the Italian industry, followed by the markets of Germany (10.5%), United States (5.8%), India (5.6%), France (5.6%), Russia (4.8%) and Brazil (4.7%). The first seven positions are all occupied by BRIC countries.
