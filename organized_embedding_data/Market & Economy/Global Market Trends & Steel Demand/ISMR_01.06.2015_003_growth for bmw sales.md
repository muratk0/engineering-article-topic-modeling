# Growth for BMW sales

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

APRIL SAW the steady growth in sales of BMW Group vehicles continue with BMW, MINI and BMW Motorrad all achieving new records. In April, a total of 175,972 BMW Group vehicles were delivered to customers around the world, an increase of 8.4 per cent on the same month last year. This continuing solid growth is reflected in the year-to-date figures, with a total of 702,643 BMW Group vehicles sold in the first four months of 2015, 8.2 per cent more than in the same period last year.

It was the best April ever for the BMW brand with a total of 148,896 vehicles delivered to customers in April 2015, 5.6 per cent more than in the same month last year. The year-to-date figures also set a new record for the brand with sales in the first four months totalling 600,473, an increase of 5.5 per cent compared to the same period last year.

MINI also achieved record sales in April with customer deliveries totalling 26,766, an increase of 27.8 per cent on the same month last year. It is the first time MINI has sold more than 100,000 cars in the first four months of the year. A total of 9,578 customers took delivery of a MINI 3-door in April (+30.3 per cent ) while sales of the new MINI 5 door reached 7,814 in the month.

All BMW Group sales regions saw deliveries increase both in April and year-to-date, in line with the company’s stated policy to strive for globally balanced growth. Sales of BMW and MINI vehicles in Europe increased by 8.7 per cent in April with 75,928 deliveries made to customers. The first four months saw sales grow 9.4 per cent to total 310,588. Solid growth was achieved in many European markets in April.

“April was also a successful month in the Americas, where customer deliveries of BMW and MINI vehicles increased 12.1 per cent to a total of 40,609. Year-to-date sales climbed 10.5 per cent with a total of 150,119 units delivered. North America is the region’s biggest growth driver with April deliveries totalling 32,428 in the USA (+9.6 per cent) and Canada seeing sales jump 39.7 per cent compared to the same month last year, with a total of 4,177 new BMW and MINIs delivered to customers,” said the carmaker.

“Deliveries of BMW and MINI vehicles in Asia increased by 5.9 per cent to total 54,469. In the first four months of the year, 220,799 vehicles were delivered to customers in the region, an increase of 5.4 per cent compared with the same period last year. April sales in Mainland China achieved the same high level as last year with a total of 37,976 vehicles delivered to customers (+0.6 per cent) while sales in South Korea rose 23.6 per cent to total 4,340.”

Following a record first quarter, BMW Motorrad continues to grow its sales. Around the world, a record 16,554 motorcycles and maxi-scooters were delivered to customers in April, an increase of 1.3 per cent on last year. That makes it the most successful month in BMW Motorrad’s history. In the first four months of the year, sales rose 6.3 per cent to a total of 47,924 units. The sales growth is being driven by high demand for the new models launched in 2014 and 2015.
