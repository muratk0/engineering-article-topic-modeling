# Steel market report 2023-2033

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Visiongain has published its 'Steel Market Report 2023-2033' forecast, valuing the global steel market at US$ 1,600.1 billion in 2022. It is projected to grow at a CAGR of 3.9% from 2023- 2033.

"Steel is used in the construction of thermal power plants, nuclear reactors, renewable energy structures (such as wind turbines and solar panels) and oil and gas extraction platforms. As the world transitions towards sustainable energy sources, the demand for steel in the energy sector is likely to increase, reinforcing steel's crucial role in powering the future," commented the analyst.

"Steel is also an essential material in various manufacturing industries for machinery, equipment and tools due to its durability and reliability. As industrialisation progresses, the demand for steel in manufacturing sectors (such as aerospace, machinery and consumer goods) continues to rise. Steel's recyclability and adaptability further enhance its appeal in industrial applications, supporting the growth of the steel market in the manufacturing sector," it continued.

Challenges for steel markets and manufacturers globally include the COVID-19 pandemic; disrupted supply chains; workforce disruption; logistics challenges and limited access to raw materials, affecting production capacity and causing delays in deliveries.

"Overall economic uncertainty during the pandemic led to cautious spending and investment decisions, affecting steel consumption across various industries. As a result of these combined factors, the steel market witnessed a decline in demand and prices, making it one of the industries hardest hit by the COVID-19 pandemic. However, with vaccination efforts and gradual economic recovery, the steel market is expected to rebound, although it may take time to fully recover from the negative effects of the pandemic," added Visiongain.■
