# INDUSTRY NEWS

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

THE WELDING equipment and consumables market in the global power industry witnessed a decline of 21.5% during the global financial crisis in 2009. However, the market has continued to grow since 2010 with an increasing number of investments into renewable power sources, which have fuelled the need for new projects.

New analysis from Frost & Sullivan, ‘Analysis of the Global Welding Equipment and Consumables Market for the Power Industry’, finds that the market earned revenues of US$ 445.5 million in 2011 and estimates this to reach US$ 578.7 million in 2017, with a compound annual growth rate of 3.8%.

Asia Pacific is expected to have the largest growth prospective over the forecast period, owing to the vast number of ongoing and proposed projects in China and India. SAW equipment and SAW wires and fluxes are expected to attract the largest demand – particularly in the wind sector – while large numbers of thermal projects are expected to propel the growth of the GTAW and SMAW equipment, along with stick electrodes and solid wires.

“The market has continued to improve in 2010, particularly from the growing economies,” commented Ruth Shilpa Sudhakar, Research Analyst at Frost & Sullivan. “However, emerging economies like China and India witnessed the lowest adoption of advanced technologies such as SAW and GTAW. As the cost of SAW and GTAW is higher than the largely used SMAW in the region, this was a growth limiting factor and is expected to continue driving this trend over the forecast period.”

In addition, Government investments in the power industries and foreign direct investments (FDI) in the wind and thermal sectors - particularly in Africa, China and India – are expected to drive the welding equipment and consumables market by 2017.

Growing demand has also led to a rise in emerging technologies. Product quality, suitability and customer support will be key decision-making factors for the global welding equipment and consumables market in the power industry.

## Mass production of AHSS

POSCO has announced itself as ‘the first in the world to prepare a mass production system to provide 490MPa class tensile strength advanced high strength steel (AHSS) for the outside panels of automobiles'. AHSS was recently provided for a new SUV model of an unidentified automobile company, serving as the foundation to spread the material to all domestic automobile brands. POSCO has continued to develop the market through EVI (Early Vendor Involvement) since starting commercial production of this steel type in 2007.

As a result, it has been applied to the new SUV model of automobile company ‘H’ being produced in Korea's Ulsan factory, Beijing China, Georgia USA since April, securing mass production and supplying 490MPa class automobile outer panels. AHSS is generally thinner than 270~340MPa class automobile outer panels, enabling lighter vehicles and is expected to grow in demand in the automobile outer panel market.

“Currently, approximately 60% of the outer panel materials applied to domestic and international automobiles are 270MPa class while 40% are 340MPa class. With the set-up of a mass production supply system, client companies can now apply 490MPa class steel materials to doors where 340MPa class steel materials were used. Moulding automobile outer panels, where surface appearance is important, is more difficult as tensile strength is stronger, but POSCO overcame this with technology, succeeding in mass producing and supplying 490MPa class steel materials as outer panels. Currently, steel companies worldwide are only providing 340MPa class steel materials to automobile companies due to processing limitations,” POSCO explained.

With AHSS being applied as outer panels, automotive companies are expecting to increase durability and fuel efficiency due to lighter bodies, thus increasing customer satisfaction. AHSS can withstand up to approximately 1.5 times the load of 340MPa class material and has excellent strength and dent resistance, making supplementary materials unnecessary. For these reasons, it leads to great cost reduction effects for automobile companies.

Based on this success, POSCO plans to expand the outer panel for doors to the automobile body, while also developing supply to overseas automobile OEMs.

POSCO is also aggressively seeking to develop new material markets in collaboration with Samsung Electronics. On 13 July 2012, POSCO signed an MOU with Samsung Electronics on joint development of new materials and product applications including exterior materials of electronic goods. Under the agreement, the two companies will cooperate to develop materials in the steel, non-ferrous metals and new material areas. ■
