# Duplex stainless steel in focus

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

According to new research on ResearchandMarkets.com, the "Global Duplex Stainless Steel Market Outlook 2023-2036", the market is set to gather revenue of almost US$ 9 billion by the end of 2036 from a revenue of around US$ 4 billion in 2023. The global duplex stainless steel market is set to grow at a CAGR of 6.4% over the forecast period (2024-2036).

"This growth is poised to be influenced by rising demand for oil & gas along with surging construction projects. The growing employment of duplex stainless steel in the paper & pump industry, along with rising maritime activities, is also projected to dominate market expansion," said the report.

“The Asia-Pacific market for duplex stainless steel is poised to generate the highest revenue of close to US$ 5 billion by the end of 2036, up from a revenue of more than US$ 2 billion by the end of 2023," said the report.
