# Galvanized steel market 2021-2025

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

The global galvanized steel market is poised to grow by 57.74 million tons from 2021-2025, progressing at a CAGR of about 3% during the forecast period.

The new 'Global Galvanized Steel Market 2021-2025' report from Technavio provides a holistic analysis, market size and forecast, trends, growth drivers and challenges, as well as vendor analysis.

“The market is driven by the rising demand for galvanized steel in industrial applications and the increase in demand for pre-engineered buildings. The study identifies economic growth in China and India as one of the prime drivers for the growth of the galvanized steel market during the next few years," it said.

The market is segmented by product (sheet and strip, structures, pipes and tubes plus others) and by geographic region. Leading galvanized steel market vendors include ArcelorMittal SA; Baosteel Group Corp; JSW STEEL Ltd; Nucor Corp; POSCO; Tata Steel Ltd and Thyssenkrupp AG.
