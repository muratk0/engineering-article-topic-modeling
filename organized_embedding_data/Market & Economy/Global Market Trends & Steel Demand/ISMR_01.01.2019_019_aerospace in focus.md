## Global aerospace and defence materials market

The global aerospace and defence materials market size was estimated at US$ 19.70 billion in 2017, according to Grand View Research. It is poised to progress at a CAGR of 4.3% over the forecast period. An increasing emphasis on the replacement of ageing fleets is anticipated to increase aircraft production, thereby boosting demand for the product over the forecast period.

"Increasing technological advancements in additive manufacturing and powder metals in aircraft production are one of the key trends supporting market growth. Furthermore, players in the market are focusing on development of high-performance composites to increase their strength, primarily due to growing demand for lightweight materials," said the analyst.

"Material buyers are involved in the value chain from earlier stages of design, which enables them to control quality, reduce cost and ensure inventory. In addition, these buyers work closely with other value chain participants which allow them to anticipate availability and pricing changes. Furthermore, material buyers usually opt for reliable suppliers with strong goodwill through past product procurement, thereby increasing entry barriers for new entrants."

The market is subject to internal substitution, wherein aluminium is being replaced by lightweight alloys and composites. Composites are increasingly being used to manufacture the fuselage and wings, whereas steel is likely to be replaced by carbon in braking systems.

The market is also capital-intensive and is characterised by the presence of established players with strong financial bases. The reduction in defence spending is a major restraint for the market. Several governments are in the process of diverting their expenditure towards armed personnel welfare schemes, which may impact military aircraft production. Decline in aircraft production is expected to reduce the demand, thereby acting as a stumbling block for aerospace and defence material manufacturers, added the analyst.

Durability and longevity of aircraft components are projected to be the key factor determining selection of materials. High-temperature resistance and weight reduction are factors influencing the development of aerospace and defence materials. Aluminium and titanium are most widely used in aircraft manufacturing, owing to their lightweight and high durability characteristics.

According to the analyst, aluminium is primarily used for manufacturing aerostructure components owing to its excellent characteristics, such as high corrosion and heat resistance. The segment is poised to register a CAGR of 4.8% in terms of revenue over the forecast period, thanks to the growing preference for aluminium alloys to pure aluminium.

"Titanium exhibits exceptional strength-to-weight ratio as it has lower density than that of steel and superalloys, making it an ideal aircraft component material. It is commonly used in major structural components such as fasteners, landing gear and actuators that need high strength and light weight. Furthermore, it exhibits excellent compatibility with composites owing to its superior corrosion resistance, thus promoting its use over the forecast period," explained Grand View Research.

Aerostructure was valued at US$ 9.72 billion in 2017 and is likely to exhibit substantial growth over the forecast period. Aluminium and composites are widely used in the structure owing to lightweight characteristics as well as improved rigidity. Properties such as superior corrosion and heat resistance reduce the overall maintenance cost of an aircraft.

Aircraft components are expected to post a CAGR of 4.1% over the forecast period, owing to their extensive use in aircraft manufacturing. These components include bearings, fasteners, bushings, and fuelling & refuelling systems, which contribute to the core functions of an aircraft.

The global commercial passenger & cargo fleet segment is expected to register strong growth during the forecast period owing to the rising number of passengers per annum, which is slated to double over the next two decades. Increasing efforts by manufacturers to reduce carbon emissions by improving fuel efficiency are estimated to create an upswing in demand for aerospace and defence materials.

The business and general aviation aircraft segment is projected to witness a CAGR of 4.3% over the forecast period, which can be attributed to higher corporate profits. North America accounts for half of the demand for business aircraft, followed by Europe. However, Asia Pacific is poised to experience substantial growth owing to widening base of billionaires in the region.

The military aircraft segment represented a sizeable share in the market in 2017 owing to increasing use of composites and superalloys in aircraft construction. However, demand for military aircraft is anticipated to decline due to reduced defence expenditure in developed economies, thus negatively impacting market growth.

North America was at the forefront of the arena, commanding more than half of the total volume in 2017. The strong presence of a raft of aircraft manufacturers is one of the primary growth stimulants of the regional market. The region has a large number of component manufacturers supplying parts to the aerospace & defence industry, thereby promoting growth.

The aerospace & defence industry in Europe experienced moderate growth against the economic slowdown and uncertainties in countries such as the U.K. and Italy. Furthermore, rising security threats in Europe are estimated to fuel the demand for military aircraft over the forecast period, leading to an increase in the demand for the product.

Asia Pacific is projected to be the fastest growing regional market, progressing at a CAGR of 4.5% in terms of revenue over the forecast period. Japan is the most prominent aircraft component manufacturing hub in the region, owing to ease of availability of raw materials and skilled labour. Increasing R&D investments by companies in researching new and improved materials are poised to drive the regional market.

Significant investments by the Government of China to increase domestic aircraft manufacturing capabilities and overall exports to the U.S. are anticipated to benefit the market in APAC. In addition, large scale production of China’s indigenously produced jet COMAC from 2021 onwards is likely to augment the market in the region.

"The market is highly concentrated in North America and Europe, with a large number of manufacturers and component suppliers in the regions. The industry is characterised by high investments into R&D by manufacturers to develop novel materials with superior properties. The market is witnessing a trend of mergers and acquisitions, with established players focusing on inorganic growth to enhance their technological capabilities," concluded the analyst.

## Titanium in the global aerospace industry

Titanium in the global aerospace industry is expected to reach an estimated US$ 5.4 billion by 2030 with a CAGR of 3.6% from 2018 to 2030, according to ResearchandMarkets.com

"The future of titanium in the global aerospace market looks promising with opportunities in the commercial aircraft, military aircraft, helicopter, general aviation and regional aircraft sectors. The major growth drivers for this market are increasing deliveries of aircraft and the demand for lightweight titanium materials," said the report.

Aircraft structure will remain the largest application over the forecast period. Major parts fabricated from titanium include seat tracks, fuselage chords, door frame, wing spar and crown bulkhead chord. Increased focus on weight reduction and fuel efficiency by aircraft manufacturers has augmented the use of titanium alloys in aircraft structure.

Commercial aircraft is expected to remain the largest segment and witness the highest growth by aircraft type due to an increase in aircraft deliveries and the increasing penetration of titanium alloys in commercial aircraft. North America is expected to remain the largest region and witness the highest growth over the forecast period due to a significant increase in aircraft deliveries and increasing demand for lightweight materials.

## Global aerospace fastener market

The global aerospace fasteners’ market is expected to register a CAGR of over 6% during the period 2018-2022, according to the latest market research report by Technavio. A key factor driving the market’s growth is the growing popularity of titanium alloy aerospace fasteners. Aerospace fasteners need to be secure, strong and lightweight. As a result, many fasteners are made from lightweight materials such as titanium and have extra security features to stay intact, even after the loss of torque. The low weight of titanium alloy hardware is its prime advantage over other hardware.

In the report, Technavio highlights the introduction of additive manufacturing in the aerospace industry as one of the key emerging trends in the global aerospace fasteners' market. Additive manufacturing is often referred to as 3D printing, as it works in a similar way to a laser printer. 3D printing, however, is used in both rapid prototyping and AM. The technique builds a solid object from a series of layers, each printed directly on top of the previous layer. In AM, objects of any shape or geometry can be produced using digital model data from a 3D model or electronic data source such as an AM file.

"AM is economical and allows for quick design and rapid changes of the internal features of the aircraft, overcoming the shortcomings of traditional manufacturing methods. A large aircraft requires almost one million durable fasteners with high flexibility, low weight and other aerodynamic requirements to withstand extreme forces," said a senior analyst at Technavio.

The commercial and business aviation segment held the largest market share in 2017, accounting for nearly 68% of the market. This application segment is expected to dominate the global market throughout the forecast period. The Americas held the highest share of the global aerospace fasteners' market in 2017, accounting for a market share of more than 42%. This region is anticipated to dominate the global market through 2022.
