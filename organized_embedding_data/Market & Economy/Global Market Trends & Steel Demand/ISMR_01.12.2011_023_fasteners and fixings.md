# FASTENERS AND FIXINGS

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

China’s high strength fastener industry has, for five consecutive years, maintained the stability of two-digit growth

“As to fasteners produced in China, many Japanese visitors noted that the price of China’s fasteners is very competitive and the development of China’s fastener industry has been extremely rapid,” continues the report. “With more and more products exported to Europe and America, many Chinese companies are beginning to build their brands. Therefore, many Japanese purchasers have great interest in fasteners imported from China. Some Japanese purchasers also hoped that Chinese manufacturers can go on to improve their products’ quality. They also said that convenient transportation was another reason that they chose to purchase from China.

“According to the Japanese government, Japan’s supply chain has been recovering since late April 2011. It predicts that the recovery will be strengthened as the plants’ production and mining industry revive, and individual consumption, public investment and housing investment are on track. We also found that the impact of the earthquake on Japanese companies is small now but the negative influence of Europe’s debt crisis is great, because most of Japan’s fasteners are exported to European and American countries. It was estimated that Japan fastener exports during the first half of 2011 decreased with export volume down 3.5 per cent to 150,791 tons and value down 4.19 per cent to 119.6 billion yen.”

Fastener exports to China from Japan during the first half of 2011 also declined with export volume down 6.32 per cent to 37,089 tons, taking up 24.6 per cent of the total amount, and export value down 6.87 per cent to 29.1 billion yen.

## LATIN AMERICAN PROSPECTS

Brazil is a growing and well established market that offers strong opportunities for suppliers in the fastener and fixing technologies industries. Fastener Fair Brasil 2011, held recently, is a highly focused exhibition, exclusively dedicated to fastener and fixing technologies. Brazil’s economy is expanding rapidly due to rapid development and large scale investments in infrastructure as well as a marked increase in domestic demand. The automotive, construction, domestic appliances, electronics, telecoms, petro chemical and energy sectors are expanding as quickly as the domestic market.

As the host for the World Cup in 2014 and the Olympic Games in 2016, Brazil is making massive infrastructure investments in preparation for these two worldwide events. The Brazilian government has several ongoing construction projects related to these events. ■
