# European machine tool production expected to grow 3% in 2015

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

DURING THE General Assembly that took place in Bordeaux on 6 July 2015, CECIMO’s Economic Committee confirmed that the value of European machine tool production increased to 22.8 billion euros in 2014. European machine tool consumption is expected to continue to grow this year by 3%. CECIMO (www.cecimo.eu) is the European association of machine tool industries, combining 15 national associations of machine tool builders.

“After two years of contraction and flat trends, the European economy has again showed positive economic growth in 2014. The improving economic climate has an impact on the European machine tool industry’s results. European machine tool production increased from 22.7 billion euros in 2013 to 22.9 billion euros in 2014. We estimate that the cautiously positive mood will continue this year and European machine tool production will increase by another 3%,” CECIMO explained.

CECIMO countries exported machine tools in 2014 to a value of 18.2 billion euros, which is marginally less than in 2013. Increasing business confidence, says CECIMO, supports an investment recovery in the main machine tool consumer sectors. European machine tool consumption grew 7% to 13.5 billion euros in 2014.

European machine tool imports are mainly driven by European machine tool consumption. After a decline in 2013, machine tool imports to CECIMO countries increased to 8.9 billion euros in 2014. The weak euro, making imported goods relatively more expensive, will also influence those flows this year.

“On one hand, we see growth slowing down in emerging markets, the risks relating to a possible Greek exit from the euro zone and the effects of EU sanctions on CECIMO export sales to Russia. On the other hand, we see a solid demand in advanced countries. The weak euro will certainly have a positive effect on machine tool exports. Therefore, our expectations for 2015 are still on the positive side,” explained Dr. Frank Brinken, the Chairman of CECIMO’s Economic Committee and a member of the Board of Directors, Starrag Holding Group. n

## Sheet metal bending

GREENFIELD ENGINEERING Sheet Metal Limited (Devon, UK) has just placed an order for its second Amada HD ATC hybrid drive press brake, with automatic tool changer, within ten months.

“For OEMs and sub-contractors, increasing productivity and through-put is the main target for improving profitability. Smaller batch sizes are causing more time lost due to an ever-increasing number of machine set-ups. Our HD1003 ATC is the solution, allowing fast, efficient set-up when dealing with smaller batch sizes,” commented Amada.

“The decision was very easy – the time savings and flexibility of switching between jobs we have made with the first HD-ATC far outweigh the initial larger investment. We are experiencing an increased uptime on the first machine of up to 60% in a single shift. The bending cell team can’t wait to get the next machine up and running as soon as possible and working like the first,” commented Gary Burnard, Greenfield Engineering’s operations director.
