# Lantek on track for growth

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

LANTEK, A SPECIALIST in digital transformation and advanced manufacturing for the sheet metal industry, has recorded an increase in its turnover of over 11%, reaching a milestone in its number of customers. In 2017, its turnover reached 17.6 million euros and its customer base grew by more than 2,000 new customers, exceeding the level of 20,000 active customers in more than 100 countries on five continents.

The company presented its results during its 2018 International Meeting. These highlighted the strength of the company's international business, which accounts for 86% of total revenue. Progress was recorded in markets with strong potential such as China (+67%) and the USA (+47%). In Europe, companies in countries such as Spain, France, Italy and Poland are taking advantage of the grants that governments have made available to businesses that opt for digitalisation, according to Lantek.

By business areas, growth was mainly driven by projects undertaken in Digital Transformation, with an increase in sales of 25% compared to 2016. This, said the company, showed that its commitment in recent years to the development of solutions for the sheet metal and fabrication sector oriented to Industry 4.0 is bearing fruit. As a result, Lantek is about to offer a new advanced manufacturing service using MES+ software and Lantek Analytics, which is designed to enable better performance of factories through data analysis.

Over the past year, the company allocated 1.8 million euros to R&D, implementation teams and project development. It also opened a new technological centre in Bilbao with the incorporation of 10 engineers. During 2017, Lantek hired about 30 professionals in all its departments, which increased its staff to over 200 people working at 19 offices all over the world.
