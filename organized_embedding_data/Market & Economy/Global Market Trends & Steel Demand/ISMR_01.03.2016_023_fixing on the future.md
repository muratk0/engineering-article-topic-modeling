# FIXING ON THE FUTURE

[TARİH: 01.03.2016 ISMR]

We examine growth patterns, new market analysis and key drivers in global industrial fastener markets

Industrial fasteners are used in various industries such as aerospace, automotive, construction, oilfield, turbine and power generation, chemical refining and marine. Commonly used fasteners include nails, screws, nut, bolts, clips, rivets, pins and washers. The basic requirements for fasteners are that they must be strong enough to handle loads and pressure, must be costeffective, easy to use and resistant to corrosion and aesthetic.

Zion Research has just published a new report which valued the global industrial fasteners market at around US$ 76.50 billion in 2014. It is expected to reach US$ 105 billion in 2020, growing at a CAGR of around 5.4% between 2015 and 2020. The externally threaded fastener segment share. Aerospace grade and standard fasteners segments are also expected to exhibit significant growth during the forecast period.

“The rapidly expanding construction industry is driving demand for industrial fasteners. Improvisation and restructuring activities in mature markets in Europe and North America are anticipated to augment demand for industrial fasteners. Due to the rising disposable income of consumers in emerging economies, fastener demand from automotive OEM is increasing. However, high anti-dumping duties are expected to be major restraining factor for the industrial fasteners’ market growth,” explained the analyst.

## Drivers for growth

“The industrial fasteners market was dominated by Asia Pacific with around a 32% share of the total market in 2014 due to rapid industrialisation in the region. Asia Pacific is followed by Europe. The Middle East and Africa is expected to be one of the most lucrative regional markets in coming years, due to increasing construction activities,” added Zion Research.

According to new research by Global Industry Analysts, the global market for industrial fasteners is forecast to reach US$ 81 billion by the year 2018. Key factors fuelling market growth include the revival in motor vehicle production, increased demand from developing regions and potential growth in construction, electrical and electronic, and aerospace sectors. Fasteners are also in high demand in the manufacture of electrically-oriented products, such as wiring devices, home appliances and construction equipment.

“The global industrial fastener market staged a dramatic comeback after the dismal market conditions prevalent during the global economic crisis. Burgeoning demand for durable goods and a strong rebound in motor vehicle output are anticipated to fuel growth at a fast pace in the global industrial fasteners’ market. Significantly, a major portion of this sales growth would be surfacing out of emerging markets in the Asia-Pacific region, especially China. Continuous increases in motor vehicle production provided industrial fastener manufacturers with new sales opportunities in countries such as China, which emerged as the largest manufacturer of motor vehicles in 2009,” said the analyst.

“Fastener production in China is projected to surpass domestic demand in the next couple of years. However, manufacturers based in developed regions, including Japan, Western Europe and the US, would continue lead the production scenario for superior and highlyengineered fasteners. Nonetheless, Chinese manufacturers, in an attempt to remain competitive, are narrowing the technological gap by acquiring technological and manufacturing expertise, and improving their accessibility to capital. Growth in demand for industrial fasteners in developed regions such as Japan, Western Europe and the US has been considerably slower compared to advances in the developing nations.

Strong recuperation in motor vehicle and durable goods demand and output would sustain growth in the developed nations.”

Heightened fixed investments and a rise in numbers of manufacturing bases have boosted fastener demand from emerging economies compared to the more developed markets. Subsequently, rapid sales growth in Asia/Pacific, Latin America, Middle East/Africa and Eastern Europe is expected to outpace demand in China, Japan, Europe and the US. Irrespective of the fact that emerging economies would grow at a fast pace, developed economies such as Western Europe, the US, Japan and Canada would continue to lead the market in terms of number of intensive users for fastening products, revealing the technological and industrially advanced nature of these countries.

According to Freedonia’s 2015 report on the world industrial fasteners’ market, worldwide demand for industrial fasteners is forecast to climb 5.4 per cent annually to US$ 93.8 billion through to 2018. Increases in motor vehicle, machinery and other durable goods production will all support growth in fastener markets as the global economy expands and fixed investment expenditures increase.

## Growth trends across sectors

The global market for aerospace fasteners alone is projected to reach US$ 8 billion by 2020, according to a separate 2016 report by Global Industry Analysts. This has been driven by the favourable industry outlook for the global commercial aviation industry.

“Although fasteners account for just 3% of the total aircraft manufacturing cost, they represent important components that influence the overall safety and efficiency of an aircraft,” commented Global Industry Analysts. “The aerospace sector uses a variety of fasteners such as hi-locks, bolts, nuts, washers, rivets, collars, screws, spacers and pins. Innovations in fastening technology are set to drive growth in the market as companies race to offer sophisticated, high performance fasteners.”

The rapidly expanding construction industry is driving demand for industrial fasteners.

According to the report, the US represents the largest market worldwide. Asia-Pacific ranks as the fastest growing market with a CAGR of 7.3% over the analysis period. The growth in the region is driven by the anticipated rise in the number of aircraft in service to over 11,000 by 2028.

Increasing demand for automobiles, particularly in countries such as China, Brazil and India has contributed to the growth of the fasteners’ market. However, the introduction and implementation of high anti-dumping duties by European Union is expected to hamper the global demand for fasteners. The development of tailor-made fasteners to suit rapidly growing niche application sectors such as railways and solar equipment is also opening new opportunities for the growth of the market.

High performance fasteners are likely to find demand from new car and automotive applications. The aerospace and automotive sectors continue to generate the bulk of industrial fastener demand. The highly fragmented industrial fasteners market is characterized by aggressive competition with several large and small players competing to maintain their respective market positions.

Technologically sound, high-performance specialty aerospace fasteners and application-specific standard fasteners are poised to achieve robust growth over the coming years researchers found. The resurgence of aircraft manufacturing and improved military expenditure is expected to boost market prospects. “These fasteners are also finding increasing uses in new markets including non-military applications and nanoaerospace,” commented Global Industry Analysts.

There is also a significant demand for high-performance fasteners from the wind energy industry, where large diameter and high tensile fasteners are required for wind turbines. Further governmental initiatives in the form of increased investments in non-building construction military and commercial aircrafts and satellites are fuelling demand for aerospace grade fasteners.
