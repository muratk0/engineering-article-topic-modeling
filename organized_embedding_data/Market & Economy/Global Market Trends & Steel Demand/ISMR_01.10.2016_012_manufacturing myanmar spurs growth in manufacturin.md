# Manufacturing Myanmar spurs growth in manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

FOREIGN INVESTMENT has reached more than US$ 900 million since the new government in Myanmar took office, with the manufacturing sector being one of the key industries for these investors. The recent announcement by the United States on the lifting of sanctions on Myanmar will also see more investment flowing into the country.

International exhibitors congregated at Manufacturing Myanmar 2016, now into its third edition, to present the latest range of machines, tools and equipment that are most suited for Myanmar's manufacturing sector. 48 companies from 14 countries and regions, including two group pavilions from Singapore and Taiwan, exhibited at the event in Yangon which finished on 24 September 2016.

"As the manufacturing sector moves up the value chain, it is time for local players to step up and be the agents for progress. This can be achieved by companies investing in new technologies and equipment plus industry professionals arming themselves with technical knowledge and skills, to meet industry needs. Manufacturing Myanmar is a major trade exhibition for the sector. We are pleased to support this trade event and we believe it will create even more opportunities for local players seeking business and growth," said U Aung Myint, President, Myanmar Engineering Society.

Manufacturing Myanmar 2016 also featured two group pavilions from Singapore and Taiwan. Coordinated by the Singapore Precision Engineering and Technology Association and the Taiwan Machine Tool & Accessory Builders' Association respectively, companies in the two pavilions highlighted a variety of multinational and national brands. The Singapore Pavilion showcased a range of technology and equipment from Japan, Germany and USA.

The 2016 edition of Manufacturing Myanmar also incorporated Tools & Hardware Myanmar 2016, featuring international makers and suppliers of machinery and various supporting tools and technologies for the industry.
