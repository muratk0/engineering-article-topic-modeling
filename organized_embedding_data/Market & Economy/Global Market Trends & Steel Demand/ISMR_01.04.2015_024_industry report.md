## Global household appliances

According to DataMonitor, the global household appliances market had total revenues of US$ 295.5bn in 2013, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 4.7% between 2009 and 2013. Electricals and electronics retailers accounted for the largest proportion of sales in the global household appliances market in 2013 – sales through this channel generated US$ 151.6bn, equivalent to 51.3% of the market's overall value. The performance of the market is forecast to accelerate, with an anticipated CAGR of 7.5% for the five-year period 2013 – 2018, which is expected to drive the market to a value of US$ 423.3bn by the end of 2018.

“On the basis of comprehensive research, our author report forecasts that the majority of the segments for household appliances will experience moderate growth during 2014-2019. Of all the segments in the industry, the home comfort segment is likely to grow at the highest pace. New product development, increasing urbanisation and rising working class population are the driving forces of the global household appliances industry. Strong economic growth in developing nations, such as China, India and the Middle East countries, is anticipated to boost the industry. Continuous innovation of products is very important for companies to sustain their successful position in the market,” explained Research and Markets.

The introduction of new features in washing machines such as large capacity, fast spin speeds, high energy consumption and digital displays provides comfort and gives a better user experience. The application of such new features in laundry appliances further helps to boost the global domestic laundry appliances market. The growth in garment purchasing is one of the key factors associated with market growth of domestic laundry appliances, says a Persistence market report on the global domestic laundry appliances market.

“Asia Pacific is expected to witness the fastest growth rates in the domestic laundry appliances market during the forecasted period 2014- 2020. China and India together hold the largest market share of domestic laundry appliances market in the Asia Pacific region. Asia Pacific holds the largest market share in the domestic laundry appliances market. Rising economy levels and a large population base offers huge market potential for the growth of the domestic laundry appliances market in the Asia Pacific region,” it explained.

Countries such as Bolivia, Japan, Tanzania and Vietnam are considered the highest potential markets and are expected to witness the fastest growth rates in the global domestic laundry appliances market during the forecasted period 2014-2020. Europe, Germany, Italy, Russia, Turkey and the United Kingdom account for the major market share of domestic laundry appliances. The global domestic laundry appliances market is expected to grow at a single digit growth rate during the forecasted period 2014- 2020.

## Market consolidation

Key players in the global home appliances market are looking to increase their market share via acquisitions and other moves to take advantage of ongoing strong expansion in the trade, according to a report from IHS Technology.

“Recent acquisitions within the home appliance market show that the world’s leading and largest home appliance producers hope to become even bigger, enlarging their footprint by moving into new territories and market segments,” said Dinesh Kithany, senior analyst for home appliances at IHS. “Production is also re-locating to increase efficiency and to cut down costs, while being closer to their addressable regional markets.”

Examples include U.S.- based Whirlpool Corporation’s plans to acquire a majority stake in Indesit Company S.p.A. of Italy, as well as its earlier acquisition of a 51 per cent stake in Hefei Rongshida Sanyo Electric in the Asian market. The acquisition by Spain’s CNA Group of appliance maker Fagor Electrodomesticos – which owns well-known brands Fagor, Edesa, Aspes and Spendid – strengthens the former’s position in the appliance market across several segments, including laundry; cold and cooking appliances; small electric houseware and comfort conditioning appliances.

The home appliance market will continue to see more such acquisitions and tie-ups. Synergies are expected not only within appliance companies but also across companies providing technological expertise and support to the home automation segment. ■
