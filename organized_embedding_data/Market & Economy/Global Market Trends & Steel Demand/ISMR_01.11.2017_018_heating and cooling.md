# HEATING AND COOLING

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:  
The global HVAC systems market is poised to grow at a CAGR of around 6.4% over the next decade to reach approximately US$ 210.5 billion by 2025.

The growing trend of smart homes, the development of energy-efficient systems and higher market penetration, supported by green technologies, are some of the factors contributing towards the growth of the HVAC market in the coming years.

HVAC (Heating, Ventilation and Air-Conditioning) systems are used in buildings to help control the climate indoors and improve indoor air quality. They heat, cool and control moisture levels in homes and buildings. According to Research and Markets, the global HVAC systems' market is poised to grow at a CAGR of around 6.4% over the next decade to reach approximately US$ 210.5 billion by 2025.

Most HVAC systems are typically centralised heat and air and forced or displacement ventilation. It is common for these systems to also feature a heat pump or furnace to provide hot water, steam or hot air, which is then distributed through duct work or pipes. Using a forced or displacement ventilation system, humidity and odours can be controlled. These have heat recovery ventilators with heat exchangers that cool the air in rooms, using the air from outside, or displacement ventilation which moves low velocities of air into the room.

Manufacturers are now investing in ways to make their units more efficient such as by using geothermal technology. The global HVAC market is highly diversified due to the presence of established international and regional players. With an increase in product extensions, technological innovation and strategic M&As, the competitive environment in the market is likely to intensify over the next four years.

## Drivers for growth

Technavio's market research analysts predict the global HVAC market to grow steadily at a CAGR of above 5% by 2021. One of the primary drivers for this market is the rise in preference for condensing boilers. Countries with extreme climatic conditions utilise HVAC systems for cooling and heating purposes. The two main systems for heating purposes are the furnace-based and boiler-based system.

"Furnace-based technology is prominent in the North American and APAC markets, whereas boiler-based systems dominate the European market. The major advantage of boiler-based systems is that they are based on radiant or convection heat, which provides steady heat with good temperature consistency. Moreover, advanced controls in terms of technology, easy maintenance and longer durability of the condensing boilers are other advantages that are expected to propel growth in the global HVAC market during the forecast period," explained Technavio.

"In terms of geography, APAC accounted for the maximum market share during 2016 and will continue to dominate the market for the next few years. APAC is the largest market for HVAC systems due to the growing population, climatic conditions, increasing urbanisation and demographic changes in the region. Also, the exponential expansion of the construction sector in countries like India and China is contributing significantly to the growth of the HVAC market in the region. Regulation and efficiency norms in these countries are expected to augment growth in the HVAC market in the coming years."

In this market study, analysts have estimated that the non-residential, end-user segment will dominate the HVAC market during the forecast period. The increase in construction, coupled with the capability to significantly reduce energy consumption, is driving the growth of this segment in the global HVAC market. Also, the rise in the number of net-zero buildings is a forthcoming trend in the construction business. The aim is to reduce consumption of non-renewable energy in these buildings. This rise in the popularity of net-zero buildings will also support the growth of the market in the non-residential segment in the coming years.

## Commercial and residential sectors

By equipment type, the HVAC market can be categorised as heating equipment, ventilation equipment and air conditioning equipment. Heating equipment includes furnaces, boilers, heat pumps and unitary heaters. Ventilation equipment includes humidifiers or dehumidifiers and ventilation pumps or air pumps. Air conditioning equipment includes unitary air conditioners, room air conditioners and coolers.

"The Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC) market is growing at a considerable rate due to technological developments for meeting consumer expectations, climate shift from historical records and stringent government regulations. The growing trend of smart homes, the development of energy-efficient systems and higher market penetration supported by green technologies are some of the factors contributing towards the growth of the HVAC market in the coming years," commented P&S Market Research.

"The integration of HVAC controls and building systems, such as lighting and access controls, is providing opportunities for the growth of the HVAC market. By integrating HVAC controls within the building systems, building managers could easily manage the building at the touch of single button. Integration also helps in saving energy and electricity bills by managing the power of HVAC systems as per the number of people in an office or a factory. Smart thermostats were launched to control the temperature in multiple rooms through a single interface."

The HVAC market is expected to grow in both the commercial and residential categories in the coming years. The central functions of ventilation, heating and air conditioning are interconnected, particularly with the need to provide acceptable indoor air quality and thermal comfort with affordable operation, installation and maintenance costs. The HVAC market can also be categorised as industrial, residential and commercial. It is expected to grow fastest in the industrial sector in the coming years due to the increasing use of HVAC equipment in this sector.

"The increasing commercial applications of HVAC and the expansion of the HVAC market in the Asia-Pacific region is driving the escalation rate of the global HVAC market. Some of the other factors driving the growth of the HVAC market are, for example, increasing disposable incomes of consumers, the rise in the number of new households, the need for product upgrades and the rise in demand for environmentally-friendly and energy-efficient products. Accordingly, some HVAC equipment manufacturers have started producing energy-efficient HVAC products," added the analyst.
