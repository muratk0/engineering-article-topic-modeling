# 5.18% CAGR growth to 2020 for galvanised steel market

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

SANDLER RESEARCH forecasts the global galvanised steel market to grow at a CAGR of 5.18% from 2016-2020. According to its galvanised steel market report, one of the primary drivers in the market is the upgrade of existing transmission and distribution networks. Global investments in transmission and distribution infrastructure will amount to approximately US$8.4 trillion by 2040, it told /SMR. Galvanised steel has wide applications such as equipment in buildings, platforms, stairs and handrails. Piping and its fittings consume massive amounts of galvanised steel in water reticulation, cooling water and fire protection systems. One of the latest trends in the market is the increased use of GFRP rebars.

"APAC is the fastest-growing region in the galvanised steel market and is anticipated to occupy around 68% of the overall market revenue by 2020. The primary reason for this region's growth is the high demand for galvanised steel flat-rolled products from the automotive and construction sectors. Moreover, with China being the largest producer in the galvanised steel market, metal-coated steel sheets (in particular) will accelerate steel production in the region in the coming years," said the analyst.

Galvanised sheet and strip dominated the galvanised steel market and accounted for around 61% of total market share in 2015. Much of the segment's growth can be attributed to the growing demand from the construction and infrastructure industry, which accounts for around 38% of overall market share. Galvanised sheet and strip are used for construction applications such as doors; sashes; shutters; walls; fences; beams; columns; partitions; carports; verandas and storerooms.
