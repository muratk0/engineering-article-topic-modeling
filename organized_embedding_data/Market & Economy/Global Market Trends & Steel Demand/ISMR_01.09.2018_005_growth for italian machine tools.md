# Growth for Italian machine tools

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

POSITIVE 2017 RESULTS were registered for Italian industrial machine tool, robots and automation systems, reaching a turnover of over nine billion euros and highlighting an upturn for all main economic indicators, according to the UCIMU association.

Ranking fourth amongst manufacturers, Italy took third position among exporters. In addition, it stabilised its fifth position in the consumption rankings, as a testimony to the lively domestic demand generated from the provision of incentives for competitiveness.

Massimo Carboniero, President of the Italian machine tool, robotics and automation association (UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE), stated: "With particular reference to the domestic market, the order index in the second quarter of 2018 registered positive indicators after decreases in the first quarter. It confirmed the dynamism of demand from Italian users, who have started to invest again after the extension of incentive measures into 2018. Obviously, we can no longer expect the increases registered last year but the growth, even if moderate, shows that the market is still increasing, stabilising at record levels."

UCIMU forecasts for 2018 confirm that this positive trend will continue for the entire year. Production and exports will grow, but, as in 2017, deliveries to domestic markets and imports will register stronger increases, all with double-digit rises, underpinned by the Super- and Hyper-Depreciation measures implemented in the Enterprise Plan 4.0. Italian production in 2018 is expected by UCIMU to increase by 9.3% and consumption by 13.6%. Exports are expected to rise by 4.6%.
