## Industrial HVAC market 2018-2022

Technavio has published a market research report on the global industrial HVAC market from 2018-2022. Its analysts forecast that the global industrial HVAC market will grow at a CAGR of around 5 per cent during the forecast period, according to the latest market research report.

"The growing adoption of R32 refrigerant in air conditioners is one of the major trends being witnessed in the global industrial HVAC market from 2018-2022. The global industrial HVAC market is moving toward the adoption of newer, advanced refrigerants that have better efficiency and are more environmentally-friendly than traditional refrigerants. All air conditioning and refrigeration systems use some form of refrigerant to absorb the heat from the air to cool," commented the company.

The integration of HVAC equipment with home automation technology is increasing being recorded.

According to Technavio analysts, one of the key factors contributing to the growth of the global industrial HVAC market is increasing demand for energy-efficient HVAC systems. The need for energy-efficient equipment for different industrial processes has increased as end-user industries have been increasing their production volume to cater to growing demand. On average, almost 9 per cent of the cost of production in most industries is spent on electricity. However, in energy-intensive industries such as oil and gas, the cost spent on electricity is almost 40 per cent.

"Of the two major end-users, the process industry segment held the largest market share in 2017, contributing to around 68% of the market. This end-user segment will dominate the global market throughout the forecast period. APAC held the largest share of the market in 2017, accounting for almost 42 per cent share. It was followed by EMEA and the Americas respectively. The APAC region is anticipated to dominate the global market throughout the forecast period," concluded the analyst.

Orbis Research confirmed that Asia Pacific has emerged as a major region for the HVAC market due to rising HVAC equipment and aftermarket & system sales by domestic companies and the high HVAC replacement rate in China, India, Australia and Korea. Factors which could hinder growth of the global HVAC market, it told /SMR, include raw materials prices; stringent regulations; data infrastructure system security; retail consolidations and economic and political events.

## Global commercial HVAC market

The global commercial HVAC market is expected to post a CAGR of close to 6 percent from 2018-2022, according to the latest market research report by Technavio. The data centre market is expected to boost growth of the global commercial HVAC market.

In this report, Technavio highlights the emerging smart and connected HVAC as one of the key emerging trends in the global commercial HVAC market.

"End-users can manage their HVAC systems better with the advantages of portable devices and monitoring systems. Air monitoring systems in HVAC equipment assist in detecting and removing allergens, harmful particulate matter, stale air and virus-causing contaminants. They also clean and regulate the moisture levels for optimal indoor air quality," said the analyst.

"Thermostats are one of the significant beneficiaries of smart technologies. Many vendors are introducing smart thermostats. These systems are automatically installed with advanced firmware and algorithms which, in addition to their learning ability, aid in boosting the energy efficiency of buildings while incurring low installation costs."

According to Technavio, the equipment segment held the largest market share in 2017, accounting for over 69 per cent of the market. This application segment is expected to dominate the global market throughout the forecast period. APAC led the market in 2017 with more than 48 per cent of the market share. It was followed by EMEA and the Americas respectively. The market share occupied by the APAC region is anticipated to increase by almost 2 per cent by 2022.
