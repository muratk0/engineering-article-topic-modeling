# Demand for German machine tools boosted by China

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

IN THE first quarter of 2016, order bookings in the German machine tool industry rose by an impressive eight per cent compared to the first quarter of 2015. While domestic order bookings stagnated, orders from abroad showed a steep rise of eleven per cent.

"In comparison to the cautious first quarter of 2015, demand for machine tools is currently showing a healthy increase," commented Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, Executive Director, VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association) in Frankfurt am Main.

The growth in demand results primarily from a substantial rise during the month of March, and in particular from a double-digit increase in orders from non-European countries outside Germany. The crucial factor was strong project business in China, where machining centres, particularly, were responsible for positive one-off results.

"The Chinese market is and remains important for us. This is very clearly evidenced by the latest figures. The transformation process in China's industrial sector, driven by technological upgrading, is far from complete and can be expected to offer potential in terms of order bookings for German manufacturers," continued Wilfried Schäfer.

While last year the Eurozone was the driving force behind demand for German machine tools, in the first quarter of 2016 the region recorded a sizeable drop of 14 per cent, said the VDW. This comparatively substantial decrease can be explained not least by marked baseline effects from the preceding year's strong figures.

"Europe's industrial sector invested substantially during 2015 and now, following a boom phase, is taking a breather," explained Wilfried Schäfer.

Meanwhile, German machine tool manufacturers reported an impressive increase of 18 per cent from non-Eurozone countries in the first quarter of 2016. Domestic business fell to 17 per cent in March so the German machine tool industry finished the first quarter of 2016 on a break-even figure for domestic order bookings.
