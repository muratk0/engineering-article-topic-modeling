# Global advanced high-strength steel market outlook

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

THE "ADVANCED HIGH-STRENGTH Steel - Global Market Outlook (2017- 2026)" report was recently added to ResearchAndMarkets.com's offering. According to the publisher, the global advanced high-strength steel market accounted for US$ 13,650.53 billion in 2017 and is expected to reach US$ 420,07.65 billion by 2026, growing at a CAGR of 13.3%.

The rise in penetration of lightweight components; the increasing need to curb CO, emissions and the development of the automotive industry are some of the key factors fuelling market growth. However, according to the report, the availability of substitutes in the market and the high production costs of highstrength steels, due to increased prices of raw materials, are restricting the market.

"Advanced high-strength steels are complex sophisticated materials containing multiphase microstructures and chemical compositions resulting from precisely controlled cooling and heating processes. These steels possess yield strength of 550 MPa and tensile strength of 780 MPa. They offer high strength and optimised formability, which enable weight reduction and design freedom for various types of crash components and structural reinforcements, added the report.

"Amongst type, the high-strength, low alloy segment held considerable market share during the predicted period. These low levels of alloying elements allow easy tempering and quenching of high strength low alloy steels so that high levels of impact toughness and strength can be obtained."
