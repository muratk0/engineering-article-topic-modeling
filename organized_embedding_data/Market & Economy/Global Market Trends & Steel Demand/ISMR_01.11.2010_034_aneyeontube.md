Tekno Tube Arabia 2011, the 10th International Trade Fair for Industrial Machinery, Metalworking, Machine Tools, Tubes/Pipes Industry, will take place from 8-11 January at the Dubai International Exhibition Centre, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

“The Middle East has the largest concentration of energy resources in the world. Advanced and modern logistics infrastructure coupled with a strategic location between five continents form a winning formula for base metals. From aluminium and steel to special and precious metals, Dubai is the place to provide the entire GCC and Middle East region with base metal products and articles,” said the show organiser.

## MIDDLE EASTERN PROMISE

Despite the economic pressure caused by the worldwide recession, and the knock-on effect on markets such as construction in the region, the Middle East is still seen as a key market for industries such as defence and aerospace. The Middle East - and the GCC in particular - has seen a growth in air transport, while elsewhere there have been falling passenger numbers. The Gulf has also seen launch customer deliveries of new business jets and Middle Eastern carriers have introduced new cabin and in-flight communication technologies. The region has also seen substantial investment in ground infrastructure, such as airports and air traffic management systems.

GCC states are presently on a drive to move away from their oil-based economy and diversify into the manufacturing sectors to sustain economic growth. Huge amounts of investment are pouring into the industrial sector to boost the countries’ industrial manufacturing base. Many new industrial units are being built while the existing ones are upgraded and expanded. The demand for metal-cutting machine tools is set to increase exponentially to cater to the special demands of the manufacturing sector.

Infrastructure, oil & gas and industrial projects are triggering demand for machinery and tools used in bending and cutting pipes and tubes. The presence of one of the largest aluminium smelters in the region (DUBAL) constitutes a solid opportunity for the downstream aluminium industry. Upstream and downstream opportunities for steel were abundant and lucrative, before the economic downturn, due to the rapid growth of the construction and real estate sector.
