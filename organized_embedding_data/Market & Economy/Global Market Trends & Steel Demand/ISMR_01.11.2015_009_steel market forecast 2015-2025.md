# Steel market forecast 2015-2025

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO analyst, Visiongain, the world market for steel will reach US$ 1.3 trillion in 2015 with production levels reaching 1,694.73 million tonnes whilst consumption will reach 1,545.50 million tonnes. “The market has been declining over the past few years as a result of the large oversupply of steel that pressured prices downwards. The global recession that hit the commodities sector hard also contributed to the shrinking growth. As a result, the steel market is expected to grow at a slow rate in the first half of the forecast period (2015-2020) before accelerating at a higher rate in the second half of the forecast period (2020-2025),” said the analyst.

Visiongain predicts that an oversupply will remain over the next decade. This, it said, will be mainly attributed to the resistance of China to cut production levels as well as declining demand across the world.

“The steel market has been dominated by China, accounting for half of the global market. It is the largest and fastest growing producer and consumer of steel and it will retain its leading position throughout the forecast period. India, Taiwan, Iran, Japan, Mexico and South Korea are other countries exhibiting strong growth in terms of steel production and consumption. Global steel demand over the next decade will mainly depend upon the emerging economies. However, economic conditions for the global steel industry remain uncertain and challenging,” said the analyst.

“The main factors that led to a previously significant increase in demand for steel are new infrastructure developments and the growing needs of the increasing middle class in developing countries. The construction, automobile and white goods industries will attract a high demand for steel over the next decade. The construction sector will be the key consumer of steel over the forecast period (2015-2025) as we predict growth above the global trend.” See www.visiongainglobal.com
