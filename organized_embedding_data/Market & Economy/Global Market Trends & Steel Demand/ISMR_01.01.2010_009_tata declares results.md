# Tata declares results

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

Tata Steel Limited has declared Consolidated Group Financial Results for the first half (H1) and second quarter (Q2) ending 30 September 2009.

Improving market conditions in the second quarter were demonstrated by total Group steel deliveries rising 17% to 6.22 million tonnes from 5.34 million tonnes in Q1 FY’10 and by a 9% rise in Group consolidated turnover (net sales plus other operating income) to Rs. 25,395 crores (US$ 5,280 million).

The worst of the downturn is in the past, the company said, and Tata Steel Europe (TSE) moved into positive EBITDA at the start of the third quarter. The cost-saving benefits achieved in the first half from the ‘Weathering The Storm’ and ‘Fit For The Future’ programmes at TSE totalled around Rs. 3,750 crores (US$ 780 million) and the target of realising savings of more than GBP 1 billion this financial year is on track, it says.

Despite the worst trading period for generations, the Group recorded profits on a consolidated EBITDA basis throughout the first half, doubling profits in Q2 FY’10 to Rs. 402 crores (US$ 84 million) from Rs. 204 crores (US$ 42 million) in Q1 FY’10. The Group’s net debt at the end of September 2009 stood at Rs. 50,745 crores (US$ 10,550 million).

Corus, owned by Tata Steel and Europe’s second largest steel producer, recently announced the appointment of Dr Karl-Ulrich Köhler as its new Chief Operating Officer with effect from 1 February 2010.

Dr Köhler succeeds Rauke Henstra, who held the position until his retirement last year. He will report to Kirby Adams, Managing Director and CEO of Tata Steel Europe. Dr Köhler, 53, will be based at IJmuiden in the Netherlands. Dr Köhler, the new COO of Corus, is a former member of the executive committee of the World Steel Association and Vice Chairman of VDEh, the German Iron and Steel Institute.
