# Global flat steels market will pass US 640 billion

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO A new Market Research Future (MRT) report, the global steel market size was valued at around US$ 380 billion in 2015 and is expected to grow at CAGR of approximately 6% by 2022. Flat steel refers to the sheet, coil or other flat-rolled steel product.

"Rapid growth in the construction & infrastructure sector is the key factor for the flat steel market. It not only reduced the cost of construction but also reduced the maintenance cost of buildings. The transportation sector and renewable energy sectors are also growing significantly, which is another key driver for the flat steel market," commented MRT.

"The Asia-Pacific (APAC) region is the largest market for flat steel, followed by Europe. In developing countries such as China, India and Japan, the construction & infrastructure sectors are growing rapidly which promotes the flat steel market. This region is expected to grow at the highest rate during the forecasted period. In the APAC region, vehicle manufacturing construction and infrastructure sectors are growing rapidly because of rapid urbanisation, which is ultimately driving the flat steel market."
