# Manufacturing marketers optimistic on economy

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

Published in December 2009, The Chartered Institute of Marketing’s latest Marketing Trends Survey (Autumn 2009) reveals that nearly half (47%) of marketers in the manufacturing and engineering sectors now expect the economy to improve over the next year - only one in ten (11%) marketers from these sectors expected the economy to improve at the same time in 2008.

The views emerging from this ninth wave of The Chartered Institute of Marketing’s Marketing Trends Survey, conducted for the Institute by Ipsos MORI, show that marketers in the manufacturing and engineering sectors have a more cautious view about their own business prospects with nearly half (48%) now believing that business for their own organisation will improve in the next 12 months. This is a 12-point increase from six months ago. There has also been a drop in those who are pessimistic about their business - from over a quarter (27%) in Spring 2009 to around one in six (17%) for this wave of the survey.

However, looking at expected sales figures for marketers working in the manufacturing and engineering sectors, the picture is less positive. Anticipated sales figures for this financial year actually show an average predicted figure of - 0.3%, which is a decline from Spring 2009 (+0.1%) and a significant drop from the expected growth of +2.5% twelve months ago. Views about sales plans have remained unchanged. Three in five (59%) consider their sales plan to be “challenging” (similar to Spring 2009) and just one tenth say that their sales plan will be “overachieved”.
