# THE ‘SMART’ PHILOSOPHY

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

ISMR analyses the global market potential of domestic/consumer market appliances and examines the ‘smart’ phenomenon.

## THE MARKET IN CHINA

In early 2009, with the issuance and implementation of China’s policies for promotion of home appliances in rural areas, home appliance replacement, energy-saving products and projects that benefit people, the sales volume of home appliances (including colour TV sets, refrigerators and washing machines) began to rebound in China.

The policy of promoting home appliances in rural areas stimulated rural consumption, which greatly boosted the growth of the industry. In 2009, the home appliance industry in China achieved total profit of RMB 29.5 billion in 2009, up 50 per cent from a year earlier; the profit margin rose from 3.0 per cent to 4.7 per cent.

In Q1 2010, the home appliance industry continued the momentum of rapid recovery since Q4 2009. The sales revenue grew by 31.3 per cent against the same period in the previous year, the total output value of new products rose by 35.3 per cent year-on-year, the output/sale ratio was 95.8 per cent and the total export value increased by 15.16 per cent year-on-year. The output of refrigerators reached 15.963 million sets, a 27.40 per cent YoY rise; 3.693 million home freezers were produced, up 40.4 per cent year-on-year; 23.757 million air conditioners were made, up 30 per cent year-on-year; 14.386 million sets of household washing machine were produced, up 40.7 per cent compared to the same period in the previous year.

In Q1 2010, 16.033 million sets of home appliances were sold in China, as a part of the home appliance promotion plan in rural areas, up 4.9 times year-on-year, bringing a revenue of RMB31.67 billion. By home appliance sold in rural areas in the first quarter of 2010, RMB11.3 billion came from colour TV sets, RMB9 billion from refrigerators, RMB4.2 billion from washing machines, with quarter-on-quarter growth rates of 33 per cent, 35 per cent and 29 per cent respectively.

Owing to the home appliance replacement policy, the sales volume and revenue of new home appliances grew by 60.9 per cent and 61.6 per cent respectively over Q4; in March 2009, 57,000 sets of new home appliances sold out every day.

In 2009, 99.66 million colour TV sets were produced in China, up 9.3 per cent from a year earlier; wherein, the output of LCD TV sets grew 85.2 per cent.
