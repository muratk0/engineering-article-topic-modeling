# Growth projected for global three-wheeler market

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

DEMAND FOR load carrier three-wheelers is anticipated to boost volume sales in the global three-wheeler market through to 2020. Penetration of passenger carrier three-wheelers is expected to increase in India, China, Nigeria and Egypt, due to expanding population bases and increasing numbers of three-wheeler registrations with radio taxi companies.

Three-wheelers are an important mode of passenger and load transportation, especially in inter-city transportation, across various developing countries. Passenger carrier three-wheelers are considered as the most economical and efficient mode of intra-city transportation, especially in rural and semi-urban regions of various developing countries across Asia, Africa and Latin America.

Over the last five years, robust growth in Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in developing nations has been fuelling growth in the global load carrier three-wheelers’ market. Globally, demand for three-wheelers is further being boosted by increasing penetration of ‘hub-n-spoke’ models in the supply chain network of various enterprises.
