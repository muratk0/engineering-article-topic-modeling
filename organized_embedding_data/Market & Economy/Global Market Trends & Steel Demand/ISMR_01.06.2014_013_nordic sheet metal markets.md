# Nordic sheet metal markets

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

“DESCRIBED as ‘probably the best-governed countries in the world’, Sweden, Finland and Denmark appear on the top 27 list of countries producing the most machine tools globally in 2013. A new Brooks report provides an overview of the sheet metalworking sector within the broader context of the economy and infrastructure in the Nordic countries consisting not only of Sweden, Norway, Finland and Denmark, but also of Iceland,” Brooks Reports told ISMR.

Aspects such as ‘Politics and the Economy’, ‘Infrastructure – Transport, Energy and Telecommunications’, ‘Useful Links’, ‘Machine Tools and Metalworking’, ‘Materials Supply’, ‘Automotive Sector’, ‘Shipbuilding’ and ‘General Engineering are covered in the report. See www.brooksreports.com/index.php?archive=&lang=en&view report=127 ■
