# INDUSTRY REPORT

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

“In Eastern Europe, sales in units of home appliances became noticeably stronger by 8 per cent than in Western Europe. For 2015, the forecast amounts to a sales volume of 15 million appliances. Positive economic development paired with low inflation created a solid foundation in almost every country in the region. The strongest growth of sales units among the larger countries of 14 per cent was reported by Hungary, whose government had issued a subsidy programme for efficient refrigerators and freezers. Poland and the Czech Republic, however, also reported clear growth,” added GfK.

Contrary to some Western European markets, average revenue also rose in most of the Eastern European countries. This is driven by the high quality features offered by appliances. For refrigerators, for example, fridge-freezers with NoFrost technology as well as side-by-side refrigerators gained popularity. For built-in hobs, consumer demand for flexible induction was stronger.

“Sales in units of major domestic appliances in China have risen by 5 per cent in the first six months of 2015. The forecast for revenue in 2015 amounts to 32 billion euros. Although brick-and-mortar business has come under pressure, online business has fared particularly well. The trend towards higher quality models continued. Thus front-loader washing machines, which are common in Europe, gained importance in sales. For refrigerators, side-by-side and multi-door models have become more popular,” explained GfK.

The analyst expected the Indian economy is expected to increase by about 7 per cent in 2015, and it therefore boasts the highest dynamics among large national economies beside China. The market for white goods developed in India even more positively. For 2015, 5 billion euros in revenue was forecasted by GfK. The rising middle class is increasingly able to afford major domestic appliances. For this reason, washing machines and refrigerators reported double-digit growth rates. The market for refrigerators was still dominated by single-door models. However, higher quality two-door appliances are becoming more important to consumers.

“Since the Brazilian economy has been growing steadily in the past years, forecasts for 2016 predict a downturn. There is a similar situation in the major domestic appliances market. Although increasingly broader sections of the population have recently been able to afford major domestic appliances, the market decreased during the first half of 2015. Only online business continued to become more prestigious in the market and can definitely make gains in terms of quantity. Brazil, with its market for white goods that is already important and its growing, young population, still offers attractive prospects,” concluded GfK.

According to Euromonitor, unlike in emerging markets, growth opportunities for small appliances in developed markets lie in factors such as luxury, convenience, healthconsciousness and “cocooning”, rather than rising disposable incomes. Since the growth in small appliances in these markets is driven by product replacement, rather than first-time purchases, continuous product development and advertising campaigns are significant factors in these products’ success.

## Smart connectivity and IoT

Smart home appliances are next-generation home appliances that can receive, process and transmit information and communicate with devices such as smartphones, tablets and laptops to give users real-time information on the appliance, along with remote access and control.

“According to the U.S. Energy Information Administration, global energy demand would increase by nearly 37% by 2035. The energy requirement per household has continually increased due to the aggrandised use of home appliances, creating a demand-supply gap for power companies. In this scenario, smart home appliances would facilitate real-time communication, enabling the user to operate the appliance at lower frequencies or shift the operating time to off-peak period. Thus, smart home appliances offer a revolutionary feature to consumers such as convenience, enhanced security and improved energy efficiency,” commented Custom Market Insights.

“The smart home appliances market is expected to witness significant growth, owing to the technological advancement in electronics and communication sectors and the growing need for energy efficiency. Additionally, increasing purchasing power and improving lifestyle would also foster the demand for smart home appliances, globally. Based on the product type, the market is segmented into washing machine, refrigerators, dishwashers, air conditioners and others (small electrical appliances such as coffee machines and vacuum cleaners). Among the types of smart home appliances, smart washing machines generate the highest revenue in the market.”

Smart home appliances are operated using various technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Radio Frequency Identification (RFID), ZigBee, cellular technology, Bluetooth and others. Wi-Fi, ZigBee and Bluetooth are the most widely used technologies in the market and this trend is expected to continue.

“The North American market is presently dominating the global market, owing to favourable market conditions and adequate awareness among customers regarding smart home appliances. However, the Asia-Pacific region is anticipated to emerge as a market leader post 2017, owing to rising demand for these appliances in China, India and Japan,” said the analyst.

For example, white goods manufacturer, Electrolux, has joined Google’s Early Access Program for its Brillo operating system and Weave communications protocol for the Internet of things aimed at developing connected appliances for smart homes. At CES 2016, Electrolux demonstrated connected ovens running on Google Weave/Brillo in Google’s displays. Electrolux also showed connected ovens running the AllJoyn protocol in the public Allseen Alliance booth and in Qualcomm’s booth at the Springhill Suites.

The Electrolux connected steam ovens on display at CES were equipped with the CookView® camera; a camera that provides a live feed directly from the oven to a mobile device (iOS or Android). Through the mobile application and WiFi access, the camera gives consumers an overview of the food in the oven.

With the accompanying app, consumers can start the cooking process through a mobile device and also respond instantly, changing temperature and humidity level or switching function, with a simple touch or swipe on the smartphone or tablet. Users can choose from hundreds of professional recipes in the app database. Notifications alert consumers as to when to get actively involved in the cooking process. Within the social feed section of the app, consumers have access to food inspiration from social channels and can also share cooking results with friends, captured live from the camera in the oven. ■
