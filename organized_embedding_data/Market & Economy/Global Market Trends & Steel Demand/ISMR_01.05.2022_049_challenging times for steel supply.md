# Challenging times for steel supply

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

The current crisis in Ukraine is having a drastic effect on the steel market. The acute shortage of raw materials has caused great uncertainty and an extreme rise in prices. In times like these, it is especially important to have a large warehouse and high degree of in-house production on which to fall back. Meusburger has long relied on these two factors and has positioned itself to remain a reliable supplier, even in challenging times.

“The current crisis has led to a noticeable shortage of steel in Europe. This has led to extreme price hikes in both steel and energy/ transport prices, as well as increased demand for well-known steelworks. Meusburger does not purchase any goods from Russia or Ukraine. Thanks to long-standing partnerships and the largest raw material and standard parts warehouse, the company is positioned to guarantee high supply security in these uncertain times. To continue to guarantee this service, we can't avoid buying steel and must accept the increased costs," it explained.

Guntram Meusburger added: "We are going through turbulent times, the likes of which I have not experienced in almost 25 years at Meusburger. Due to the unforeseen situation on the market, many steel producers are now invoking force majeure and withdrawing from their contracts. Despite the steel shortage, we are doing our best to ensure product availability for our customers".

At Meusburger, 100% of steel sheet/ plate are produced in-house and standard plate can be sent directly from stock. Various raw materials are additionally available for further processing in the company's outdoor storage area of around 24,000m². Currently, despite the difficult situation, Meusburger guarantees continuous availability.

"Our highly automated production lines and highly trained personnel guarantee quality in the μ range, and even the best raw materials are heat-treated for stress relief at the Meusburger facility. This process uses a lot of energy in the form of gas but creates optimal conditions for low-deformation processing of the parts," concluded the manufacturer.

## Pivatic DTP Double Tool Punch

The servo-electric PivaPunch punching station is equipped with Thick Turret tools that are set up in tool cassettes, configured for a product family, a large known batch, or flexible production, depending on the needs. Two identical cassettes are used at a time in a double punch process – one on each side of the material. This allows punching two holes simultaneously for double efficiency and low energy consumption. The integrated servo-electric shear takes care of Cut-to-Length (CTL) operation.

The tools used can be standard stationary or rotatable indexing tooling of size A to F or special shape tooling that would be used to punch a cluster of holes or do embossing. There can be several pre-set tool cassettes with different configurations, and for more tooling capacity, the large tool stations can be equipped with Multitool Adapters to achieve over 100 tools in a single two-sided punching centre.
