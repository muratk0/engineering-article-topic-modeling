# A FIRM FIX

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“ Worldwide demand for industrial fasteners is forecast to climb 5.4 per cent annually to US$ 93.8 billion through to 2018 ”

We examine global forecasts, growth patterns and trends in worldwide industrial fastener markets

Fasteners are devices which are used to mechanically join two or more

components, devices or surfaces together. Commonly used fasteners include nails, screws, nuts, bolts, clips, rivets, pins and washers. The basic requirements for fasteners are that they must be strong enough to handle loads and pressure, must be cost-effective, easy to use and resistant to corrosion and aesthetic. Applications span a wide range of sectors such as automotive, construction, electronics, machineries, industries and other everyday applications.

According to Freedonia’s 2015 report on the world industrial fasteners market, worldwide demand for industrial fasteners is forecast to climb 5.4 per cent annually to US$ 93.8 billion through to 2018. Increases in motor vehicle, machinery and other durable goods production will all support growth in fastener markets as the global economy expands and fixed investment expenditures increase. In addition, says the report, product prices will climb at a faster pace than during the 2008-2013 period, boosting demand in value terms.

The global market for industrial fasteners is quite mature. In 2011, this market had reached a value of US$ 65.50 billion. Growth in the enduse segments of industrial fasteners, such as the automotive sector, is further fuelling the industrial fasteners market. Key factors generally propelling market growth include a revival in motor vehicle production, increased demand from developing regions and potential growth in construction, electrical and electronic, and aerospace sectors. Fasteners are also in high demand in the manufacture of electrical-oriented products, such as lightening and wiring devices, home appliances and construction equipment.

## Regional growth trends

The global market for aerospace fasteners alone is projected to reach US$ 6.8 billion by 2018, according to a separate 2015 report by Global Industry Analysts. This has been encouraged by increased shipments of aerospace equipment and the growing trend towards large-sized aircraft.

Increasing demand for automobiles, particularly in countries such as China, Brazil and India, has contributed to the growth of the fasteners’ market over the forecast period. However, the introduction and implementation of high anti-dumping duties by the European Union is expected to hamper the global demand for fasteners. The development of tailor-made fasteners to suit rapidly growing niche application sectors, such as railways and solar equipment, is also opening new opportunities for the growth of the market.

“The global industrial fastener market staged a dramatic comeback post the dismal market conditions prevalent during the global economic crisis. Burgeoning demand for durable goods and a strong rebound in motor vehicle output are anticipated to fuel growth at a fast pace in the global industrial fasteners market. Significantly, a major portion of this sales growth would be surfacing out of emerging markets located in the Asia-Pacific region, especially China. A continuous increase in motor vehicle production provided industrial fastener manufacturers with new sales opportunities in countries such as China, which emerged as the largest manufacturer of motor vehicles in 2009,” said a 2013 report by Global Industry Analysts on the global industrial fasteners market.

According to the 2015 Freedonia report, gains will be strong worldwide for the US$ 72 billion world industrial fastener industry but developing areas will outpace demand in developed countries. The key motor vehicle OEM market will post the largest value gains, said the report, while the smaller construction market will grow the fastest.

High-performance fasteners are likely to find demand from new car and automotive applications. The aerospace and automotive sectors continue to generate the bulk of industrial fastener demand. The highly fragmented industrial fasteners market is characterized by aggressive competition with several large and small players competing to maintain their respective market positions.

Technologically sound, high-performance specialty aerospace fasteners and applicationspecific standard fasteners are poised to achieve robust growth over the coming years, researchers found. The resurgence of aircraft manufacturing and improved military expenditure is expected to boost market prospects. "These fasteners are also finding increasing uses in new markets including nonmilitary applications and nanoaerospace," commented Global Industry Analysts.

There is also a significant demand for high performance fasteners from the wind energy industry, where large diameter and high tensile fasteners are required for wind turbines. Further governmental initiatives, in the form of increased investments in non-building construction, military and commercial aircrafts and satellites, are fuelling demand for aerospace-grade fasteners.

## Asian fastener market

Asia’s industrial fastener market is expected to reach US$ 44.12 billion by 2020, according to May 2015 research by Radiant Insights, Inc.

“Growing automotive demand in light of the increasing disposable income of consumers is expected to propel demand for industrial fasteners over the next five years,” says the report. “China dominated the Asia fasteners market in 2013 and the trend is anticipated to continue over the forecast period on account of the rapid expansion of the domestic manufacturing base.

“India is expected to witness the fastest growth over the projected period as a result of increasing automotive production. In addition, infrastructure improvement in emerging economies is likely to propel demand for screws, bolts and rivets. Economic growth and rapid industrialisation in China, India, Malaysia, Vietnam, Malaysia and Thailand is anticipated to stimulate investments for construction which, in turn, is likely to augment the industrial fastener market over the projected period.”

The increasing use of fasteners in the manufacturing of automotive parts, including engine, body, moulding, suspension system, frame, body assembly and wheels, is expected to drive market growth. The growing production of aircraft parts in China on account of “The 2011 State Council Policy” for the aerospace sector is expected to spur market growth.

“The increasing number of regional manufacturers in China, including Shanghai, Chengdu, Xi’an, Jiangxi and Shenyang, is further anticipated to boost industrial fastener market over the forecast period. However, the rising market share of substitutes such as PU adhesives is anticipated to restrain the industrial faster market in the automotive and construction sector over the forecast period,” continues the study.
