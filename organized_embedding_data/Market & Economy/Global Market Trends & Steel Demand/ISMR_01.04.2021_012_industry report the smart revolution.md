GLOBAL HOUSEHOLD APPLIANCE MARKET 2020-2024

We highlight trends, growth drivers and challenges for the global household appliances industry.

According to analyst Technavio, the global household appliance market is poised to grow by US$ 21.76 billion during 2020-2024, progressing at a CAGR of almost 1% during the forecast period. Its research study identifies the increased adoption of integrated smart home technology as one of the prime reasons driving household appliance market growth over the next few years.

The smart kitchen appliance market size, said the analyst, also has the potential to grow by US$ 6.16 billion during 2020-2024, and its growth momentum will accelerate during the forecast period because of the steady increase in year-over-year growth.

According to Data Bridge Market Research, the global household appliances market is expected to see a growth rate of 4.3% from 2021 to 2028.

## Home appliances in focus

Home appliances refer to an array of devices, generally operated on electricity and used in the home for chores such as cooking, cleaning or preserving cold food. Also termed "domestic" appliances, these mainly involve white goods such as refrigerators; air conditioners; dishwashers; clothes dryers; drying cabinets; freezers; kitchen stoves; water heaters; washing machines etc.

Major appliances are characterised as large machines capable of completing a routine household job, the purposes of which include cooking or refrigeration in home, commercial, industrial or institutional locations. They are generally in a set position and are considered part of the home in the real estate market as they are typically included in the property when other furnishings are not. They frequently require a generous amount of electricity to function properly. The most prominent products in the major appliance industry are ovens, ranges, refrigerators, freezers, washers and dryers. Dishwashers and air conditioning units are also manufactured.

This multi-billion-dollar industry is run by some huge brands. Increasing urbanisation, a rising working-class population and new product development are just some of the factors driving growth in this market. One major driver of this industry is the rise in per capita income in developing countries, which has greatly spurred consumer spending.

## Connected home appliances

However, growing smartphone penetration and increasing internet speed is driving the global smart home appliances market. The market is segmented into smart washing machines and dryers; smart air conditioners; smart refrigerators; smart microwave ovens and smart dishwashers. Connected home appliances are smart electronic appliances with internet connectivity, accessible from any remote location using mobile devices such as smartphones, tablets and laptops.

The global increase in the number of smart homes; the increased adoption of smart grids; improved R&D infrastructure of existing players in the segment and increased product availability contribute to existing demand for smart washing machines and dryers. These are also considered key drivers to facilitate the growth of the segment.

## Growing smartphone penetration and increasing internet speed is driving the global smart home appliances market

"Smart home appliances enable users to monitor and control household activities from remote locations. The growth of connected home appliances depends upon various factors such as internet infrastructure availability and the smartphone penetration rate of the targeted region. If the internet infrastructure of any country is robust and efficient, there would be more demand for connected home appliances. As a more energy-efficient solution compared to their traditional counterparts, connected home appliances have substantially high adoption rates across the globe," commented Future Market Insights in its 'Market Overview.

Personal and family security is the primary factor driving the adoption of connected home appliances by consumers across the globe, said the analyst. Over the last decade, the adoption of connected home appliances has grown quickly with the evolution of consumers who are more enthusiastic for technology.

"With the increasing adoption of smartphones, consumers are becoming more familiar with touch controls and the world of apps which, in turn, drive their attention towards the adoption of smart appliances. As connected home appliances generate more profit for the appliance makers, compared to traditional ones, manufacturers are diverting their focus from the simple appliance manufacturing business to connected appliance manufacturing to increase their profitability margins. However, in various under-developed or some developing regions, the lack of internet infrastructure and affordability issue are factors hindering the growth of the global connected home appliances market," added Future Market Insights.

The analyst segments the global connected home appliances market into Wi-Fi, NFC and Bluetooth. Among these technology segments, Wi-Fi technology is the most popular and is expected to remain the dominant technology segment in the near term.

"Asia Pacific is the highest contributor in terms of revenue in the global connected home appliances market, followed by Europe and North America In Asia Pacific, China is projected to be the leading market of connected home appliances in the near future," added the analyst.

## Connected cooking

According to Grand View Research, the global household cooking appliance market was valued at US$ 80.74 billion in 2018 and was poised to progress at a CAGR of 6.5% from 2019 to 2025. A growing number of nuclear families, improved standards of living in developing countries and the availability of energy-efficient equipment are some of the crucial factors expected to drive the industry during the forecast period. The increased focus of leading players on innovation as a way of being more competitive has led to the introduction of smart kitchen gadgets, connected cooking facilities and cordless cooking devices. A smart kitchen comprises tech-enabled kitchen equipment designed to provide control, efficiency and convenience.

Another determining factor for the market is the price and lifespan of the product. The lifespan of cooktops depends upon usage and quality, which directly impacts sales. The quality has a bearing on the pricing of such equipment, which varies as per the budget of a buyer. Single students, bachelors or lower-middle-class families often prefer less expensive or economical products that can last up to two to three years. Expensive and premier cooking appliances, on the other hand, are preferred by upper-middle class and upper-class families.

"Apart from leading to technological advancements and innovations, changing consumer preferences and trends have also compelled manufacturers to experiment with product design, allowing them to expand their portfolios and more effectively cater to the demands of their customers. The product expansion strategy allows them to acquire a larger customer base and increase their market share. For instance, Klove, an IoT-enabled stove knob, can be replaced by the knob of a common gas oven or stove. It is connected to the internet and has sensors to monitor what is cooking. Klove's Artificial Intelligence (AI) powered app can parse through recipes and can also use Amazon's Alexa for voice interaction with users." added Grand View Research.

"The Internet of Things (IoT), facilitates cooking with minimal intervention by controlling these appliances remotely. For instance, the Bailey Weber Kitchens thermometer uses Bluetooth connectivity to keep a check on the set temperature and alerts when the dish reaches its ideal temperature. June Life Inc.'s intelligent oven can detect the type of dishes inside the oven using its built-in camera, automatically controlling and setting up the temperature and time required for cooking," explained the analyst.
