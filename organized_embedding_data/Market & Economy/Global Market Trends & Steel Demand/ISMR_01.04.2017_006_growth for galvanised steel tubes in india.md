# Growth for galvanised steel tubes in India

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO A new Technavio market study, covering the present scenario and growth prospects of the galvanised steel tube market in India for 2017-2021, the irrigation, process, plumbing and construction industries are key end-user segments of galvanised steel tubes. The irrigation and process industries together accounted for more than 61% of market share in 2016.

According to Ajay Adikhari, a lead analyst at Technavio for metals and minerals research, "The galvanised steel tubes market in India is witnessing considerable growth in the process industries. The increase in the number of investments is boosting the industrial growth of the country, which is likely to drive the galvanised steel tubes market."

The rapid growth in industrialisation and development in India is the key demand driver for galvanised steel tube products. The increasing and expanding process industries, such as food and beverage, paper and pulp, and chemicals, are also propelling the growth of the galvanised steel tube market in India. The consumption per capita is likely to increase due to rising infrastructure and construction projects in roads, highways and railways coupled with swift growth in several industrial sectors.

"The continuously growing economy of India and industrial growth are expected to drive the galvanised steel tube market during the forecast period," said Ajay.

In 2015, the Government of India launched the 'Housing for All by 2022' mission.

The Urban Housing Mission will be engaged in all 4,041 cities and towns across India. With rapid economic development, the purchasing power of the population has risen considerably over the years. This rise in purchasing power, coupled with the rising population, has increased the demand for residential projects across the country. As galvanised steel tubes are extensively consumed in residential projects, the rise in demand for residential projects will drive the Indian galvanised steel tube industry, said the report.

"Galvanisation provides resistance to mechanical damage, which is why galvanised steel tubes are used in many industrial applications to transport liquid, air and gas. Galvanised steel tube products offer high strength, formability and recyclability. For instance, in the automobile industry, galvanised high-strength steel tubes are used in the manufacturing of axle shafts, steering components, construction parts of chassis and seat construction. They also improve the safety performance of the vehicle, which is one of the primary requirements of modern cars. Thus, the rising demand for galvanised steel pipes in industrial applications is likely to drive the growth of the galvanised steel tubes market in India," concluded the report.
