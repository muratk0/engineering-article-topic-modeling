# Internet TV, 3D TV and LED TV will be the highlights in the market

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

The sales volume of colour TV sets in 2009 reached 39.50 million in Chinese market, up 10.5 per cent from a year earlier; of which 25.5 million LCD TV sets were sold out, accounting for a rising share of 67.02 per cent in the overall TV sales volume from 32.84 per cent in 2008. A new round of replacement has begun, and the future TV market still has great potential. Internet TV, 3D TV and LED TV will be the highlights in the market.

In 2009, 49.4 million sets of washing machine were produced in China, up 13 per cent from a year earlier; 26.8 million sets were sold domestically in China, up 12 per cent year-on-year. In the washing machine industry, brand concentration degree is quite high. The washing machine export market is still dominated by foreign capital.

In cities, the change of product mix was the biggest highlight in 2009. The consumption of medium and high-end refrigerators rose rapidly. The markets in first-tier and second-tier cities showed strong recovery. In rural areas, the promotion of home appliances also made remarkable achievements.

In 2009, 81.53 million air-conditioners were produced in China, down 4.1 per cent from a year earlier; and sales reached 80.70 million ones, down 5.2 per cent year-on-year. In H2 2009, the government granted subsidies of RMB300-800 for the first and secondary energy-efficient air-conditioners. Since then, high energy-efficient air-conditioners have been rapidly produced on a large scale, becoming the mainstream products in the market. Variable frequency air-conditioners also emerged and major manufacturers quickly launched these products.

In 2009, 60.38 million microwave ovens were produced in China, down 4.7 per cent from a year earlier; the export volume amounted to 44.9 million units, decreasing by 7.6 per cent year-on-year. Europe, the United States and other developed countries are major consumers of microwave ovens, while China is a potential consumer. ■
