# ECO-FRIENDLY CONSTRUCTION

[TARİH: 01.05.2012 ISMR]

ISMR examines trends and directions in global construction markets

Construction today is a US$ 7.5 trillion market accounting for 13.4% of world output. Forecasts show the most dynamic growth for construction over the next decade will come from emerging markets in India, China, Asia Pacific, South and Central America, Middle East and Africa and parts of East Europe.

The management of waste generated by the construction industry has become a cause of major concern. The use of recycled materials and chemicals instead of virgin materials is seen as supporting sustainable construction, while yielding a range of environmental and economic benefits.

"Increasing awareness about green trends is highlighting the importance of energy-saving technological solutions," notes Frost & Sullivan industry analyst, Deepan Kannan. "Manufacturers who serve the twin needs of consumers - that of providing products which are both performanceand price-oriented – stand to make major gains."

## US construction forecasts

The McGraw Hill Construction forecast for 2012 predicts that construction starts in the US will be near US$ 412 billion, 4% less than the forecasted level for 2011. Based on significant research and in-depth analysis of macro-trends, the 2012 Dodge Construction Outlook details the forecasts for each construction sector in the US, as follows:-

Single family housing in 2012 will improve 10% in dollars, corresponding to a 7% increase in the number of units to 435,000. This is still a low amount, as the excess supply of homes due to foreclosures continues to depress the market.

Multi-family housing will rise 18% in dollars and 17% in units, continuing its moderate, upward trend.

Commercial building will grow 8%. Warehouses and hotels will see the largest percentage increases, but improvement for offices and stores will be modest.

## INDUSTRY REPORT

The institutional building market will slip an additional 2% in 2012, after falling 15% in 2011. The tough fiscal environment for states and localities will continue to dampen school construction, and the uncertain economic environment will limit growth in healthcare facilities.

Manufacturing buildings will increase 4%, following the 35% gain in 2011, as the low value of the US dollar continues to support export growth.

Public works construction will drop a further 5%, after a 16% decline in 2011, due to spending cuts and the absence of a multiyear federal transportation bill for highway and bridge construction.

Electric utilities will retreat 24%, following a 48% jump in 2011.

The AIA Consensus construction forecast for 2012 predicts that non-residential and commercial construction will continue to dip due to an uneven recovery and the absence of strong economic indicators on these kinds of projects. The AIA forecast based its opinion on the problems faced when looking at finance options for construction projects, weak governmental finance and the sudden rise of construction costs.

The non-residential factor is expected to suffer a 6.5 percent decline, while manufacturing facilities will experience a 16 per cent reduction. On the other hand, institutional projects will decline 3 per cent, the least amount expected among general facilities.

During 2012, the top markets referred as key cities on this forecast will be:

Washington, D.C. – The DC area has a diversified economy, including communication, transportation and biomedical institutions that will see large investments primarily as improvements to existing facilities. The home market will pick up ahead of other cities, as predicted on the 2012 Construction forecast.

Austin – Driven by a large college campus, this city provides high paying jobs, all yearlong economic activities and still needs medical, educational and transportation infrastructure to deal with its high population.

San Francisco – While the luxury housing market is still struggling to get back in business, the office and retail market will benefit from improvements as old buildings and low-efficiency buildings receive a cash boost to improve their efficiency and accommodate new tenants. The hotel market can also expect an increase of business and retrofitting opportunities.

New York City – A new era of residential properties and market stability will lead the way in Manhattan, while hotel improvements are also scheduled.

Boston – One of the best housing markets during 2012 although office and retail projects will likely tend to reduce their activity. Housing prices will increase again after suffering modest declines in the downturn.

Other cities that should be monitored closely include Houston (the oil industry will continue to generate profits and new investments), Los Angeles (will likely be the US's number two residential and industrial market) and San Diego (the medical industry and retirees will attract investment to the area).

## Sustainable housing design

Traditional building methods must make room for cost effective, sustainable designs if housing crises are to be alleviated, according to an independent RICS research report.

"By making use of off-site construction methods, recycled materials and innovative structural design, homes can be constructed swiftly and cheaply with less impact on the environment. Many of the designs can be easily segmented and extended, eliminating the need for costly renovation work, and enabling them to evolve as the needs of the occupiers change," says the report.

"More innovative, modern off-site and modular designs are not only extremely cost effective, but can be constructed with ease in a very short period of time. Furthermore, many major mortgage providers are already willing to lend against these structures, which has been a problem in the past, meaning that first-time buyers could find them a highly practical way of getting onto the property ladder."

Emerging Asia leads the world in the implementation of sustainable real estate solutions, finds a RICS sustainability survey. The region ranked first across many property sectors and occupier types in the survey, with agents reporting the highest rises in demand for sustainable real estate solutions in the government, bank and commodity sectors. ■
