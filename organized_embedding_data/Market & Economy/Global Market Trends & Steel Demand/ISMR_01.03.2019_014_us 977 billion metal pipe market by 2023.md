# US$ 97.7 billion metal pipe market by 2023

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO THE "Metal Pipe Market Report: Trends, Forecast and Competitive Analysis", the global metal pipe market is expected to reach an estimated US $97.7 billion by 2023, with a CAGR of 3.9% from 2018 to 2023. The major drivers for this market are increasing construction of new pipelines for oil and gas, water and wastewater, replacement of ageing pipelines and infrastructure development.

Emerging trends which have a direct impact on the dynamics of the metal pipe industry include the increasing use of premium and technically advanced pipe and the development of multilayer pipe for strength and durability.

The researcher forecasts that steel pipe is expected to remain the largest segment and witness the highest growth over the forecast period due to increasing demand in oil and gas, power generation and automotive industries. Within the global metal pipe market, oil and gas will remain the largest end use industry during the forecast period, due to increases in oil and gas exploration and production activities.

North America is expected to remain the largest region because of increased oil and gas exploration activities. APAC is expected to witness the highest growth over the forecast period due to increasing infrastructure development, urbanisation and government economic stimulus measures in this region.
