# DRIVERS FOR GROWTH

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

The global household appliances industry is expected to reach an estimated value of US$ 324.2 billion by 2019, according to Research and Markets.

The major drivers of the household appliances industry are the increase in per capita income, consumer spending, housing activities and increasing urbanisation. The home comfort segment depicted the highest growth rate among all five segments in 2013. The implementation of technological changes in household appliances will possibly further drive this industry.
