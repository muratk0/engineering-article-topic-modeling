# Ternium invests in new lines in Mexico

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

TERNIUM S.A. HAS announced plans to build a hot-dip galvanising line and a pre-painting line at its facility in Pesquería, Mexico. The new lines will target Mexico's industrial markets, complementing the company's import substitution strategy.

Ternium's new hot-dip galvanising and pre-painting lines will have annual production capacity of 300,000 and 120,000 metric tons, respectively. With a total investment of approximately US$ 260 million, the new lines are expected to be operational by 2020 and will expand Ternium's current hot-dip galvanising and pre-painting production capacity in Mexico by 17% and 20%, respectively

"This new high-value-added processing capacity will support our position as a leading steel supplier to the growing household appliances, lightning and metal-mechanic industries in Mexico, and will broaden our product range," said the company.

Ternium is a leading steel producer in Latin America, with an annual production capacity of approximately 11 million tons of finished steel products. With production facilities in Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, the southern United States and Guatemala, it serves markets in the Americas. In addition, Ternium participates in the control group of Usiminas, a Brazilian steel company. See www.ternium.com
