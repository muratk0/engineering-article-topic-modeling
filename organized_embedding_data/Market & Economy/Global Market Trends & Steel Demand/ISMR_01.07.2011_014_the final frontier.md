# THE FINAL FRONTIER

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

ISMR analyses global defence markets and highlights areas of growth for manufacturers

“There are growing sub-sectors within the military aerospace industry, such as UAVs (unmanned aerial vehicles). According to Market Research Media’s revised and updated April 2011 Military US UAV Report, the US military Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAV) market has witnessed a meteoric growth over the past decade. UAS development went through a quantum leap, from lab concept to battle-tested proven technology. Unmanned Aerial Systems (UAS) are considered today to be a key component of US defence transformation and an integral part of US military doctrine.

“New budget and political realities facing the US government are calling for smaller, more effective and less risky solutions to win military and paramilitary engagements in the years ahead. Despite the general downturn trend in the defence market, the UAV segment is forecast to survive the recession unscathed. UAV technology is the answer for both a smaller defence department heavily reliant on solid intelligence gathering, and for an increasingly militarised CIA overseeing the counterinsurgency drone strikes in Afghanistan, Pakistan and now Libya,” said the company.

The US military UAV market is projected to grow at a CAGR of 10 per cent between 2010 and 2015, says “US Military Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAV) Market Forecast 2010-2015”, a new research report from Market Research Media. The report finds that the US military UAV market will generate US$ 62 billion revenues over the period 2010 – 2015.

Market Research Media analysts have found that there is a widening gap between growing UAV fleet and UAV infrastructure development, especially in sectors such as training; service; support and maintenance; and data management. This gap creates a number of market opportunities for UAV vendors, both large defence contractors and small technology companies.

## SUBMARINE MARKETS

Forecast International’s “The Market for Submarines” analysis projects that 111 submarines worth US$ 106.7 billion will be produced from 2011-2020. The average value of these submarines will be US$ 960 million, an indicator of the growing complexity of the modern submarine and the increasing use of air-independent propulsion, both of which add substantially to the cost of diesel-electric boats.

"Over the long term, constant shifts in the structure of construction costs and the steadily growing number of countries that are interested in nuclear-powered submarines mean that the average cost of submarines will continue to increase on an annual basis," said warships analyst Stuart Slade, author of the report. According to a new report, “The Global Submarine Market 2011-2021”, the global submarine market is expected to increase by a CAGR of 1.22 per cent during the forecast period. Worldwide, 41 countries possess submarine capability and together operate 450 submarines. Most of these nations are modernising their fleets or increasing them as a result of changing security situations. A total of 154 submarines are to be procured over the forecast period, costing US$ 186.3 billion.

## In 2011, the global market for warships and naval vessels will amount to US$ 75.4 billion.

The market, which currently stands at US$ 16.4 billion, is set to rise by a CAGR of 1.02 per cent to US$ 18.2 billion by 2021. North America is expected to constitute 47 per cent of the market during the forecast period, a total of US$ 87.3 billion. The US, as the largest defence spender in North America, accounts for almost the entire amount. European nations are expected to cumulatively spend US$ 46.3 billion across the forecast period and constitute 25 per cent of the total submarine market. Regional hostility prevailing among Asian states is driving the submarine market, which is expected to cumulatively be worth US$ 44 billion across the forecast period, 23.6 per cent of the total market. Brazil and Argentina are the main spenders in the submarine sector in Latin America, totaling US$ 8.6 billion, 4.61 per cent of the total market.
