## Global semiconductor sales

Global semiconductor sales, a significant component of worldwide electronic component distribution, generate US$ 300 billion annually, according to the Semiconductor Industry Association. Asia/Pacific represents 54 per cent of the global semiconductor market, while the Americas comprise 18 per cent.

The Semiconductor Industry Association (SIA) announced in July 2012 that worldwide sales of semiconductors reached US$ 24.4 billion for the month of May 2012, a 1.4 per cent increase over the previous month when sales were US$ 24.1 billion. Combined global sales for the March-April-May period increased over December-January-February by the highest rate (6.4 per cent) since June 2010. Additionally, May marked the third consecutive month that sales have grown over the previous month - the longest streak of sequential monthly growth since September 2010. However, sales from May 2012 were 3.4 per cent lower than the May 2011 total of US$ 25.2 billion, and 2012 year-to-date sales were lower across all regions than at this time last year.

“The upward trend of global semiconductor sales is encouraging,” said Brian Toohey, President & CEO, Semiconductor Industry Association. “Recent sales totals are in line with industry projections of modest growth for the remainder of 2012, but a sluggish global economy continues to provide substantial headwinds, limiting more robust growth.”

Regionally, semiconductor sales increased in Asia Pacific (10.5 per cent), Europe (4.4 per cent) and the Americas (1.6 per cent) over a three-month moving average, but fell slightly in Japan (-0.7 per cent). However, Japan achieved an increase in sales from May 2011 to May 2012 (0.4 per cent), while Asia Pacific (-1.9 per cent), the Americas (-3.2 per cent) and Europe (-13.6 per cent) all saw year-to-year decreases.

## Markets and sectors

Electronics contract manufacturing services can be classified by the following product segments: Consumer Electronics, Computers & Peripherals, Telecommunications, Industrial Electronics and Others.

According to MarketLine, the global office electronics market showed total revenues of US$ 98.6 billion in 2010, representing a compound annual rate of change (CARC) of -3.5% for the period spanning 2006-2010. Copier sales proved the most lucrative for the global office electronics market in 2010, with total revenues of US$ 48 billion, equivalent to 48.7% of the market’s overall value.

The performance of the market is forecast to accelerate, says MarketLine, with an anticipated CAGR of 2.9% for the five-year period 2010 - 2015, which is expected to drive the market to a value of US$ 114 billion by the end of 2015.

## Electronics manufacturing

Earlier this month, in India, the Union Cabinet approved a scheme for the development of Electronics Manufacturing Clusters (EMCs). The proposed scheme would support set-up of greenfield and brownfield clusters to help grow the Electronics Systems Design and Manufacturing (ESDM) sector.

Computer and mobile handsets manufacturing are expected to benefit from this scheme because this will make components available locally. These companies are currently importing components from countries such as China and Taiwan.

Dr. Satya Gupta, the new Chairman of India’s Semiconductor Association (ISA) and Founder and CEO, Concept-to-Silicon Systems Pvt. Ltd., will take on the responsibility of steering ISA towards nurturing the US$ 400 billion Electronics Systems Design and Manufacturing (ESDM) market in India by 2020, by promoting domestic design and manufacturing of electronics systems and semiconductor products.

“I am honoured to have an opportunity to lead ISA along with the newly elected EC at an exciting juncture, when the Indian ESDM market is poised for a rapid growth to reach US$ 400 billion by 2020,” he said. “Our goal at ISA is to help develop a complete eco-system for design and manufacturing of electronics systems and semiconductor products. Working jointly with government, industry, academia and the venture capital community, we plan to create various foundational blocks and programmes with the support of government to achieve the goal. Our task is to fulfil this market demand by making India the most favourable destination for electronics system design and manufacturing.”

## Advanced electronic materials

According to BBC Research, the total market for advanced electronic materials is projected to increase from US$ 27.6 million in 2010 and US$ 30.7 million in 2011 to more than US$ 5.5 billion in 2016 and almost US$ 26 billion in 2021 at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 36.3% for a period of five years i.e. 2016 to 2021.

Tata Steel has completed an important strategic project that makes it only the second vertically integrated producer of grain-oriented electrical steels in the European Union. As a result of collaboration between colleagues in the Netherlands and south Wales, grainoriented (GO) electrical sheet produced by Tata Steel subsidiary Cogent Power is now being manufactured using feedstock sourced from the company’s IJmuiden works.

“This is a big step forward, enabling us to expand our electrical steels portfolio, for example by producing significantly more highquality HiB grades. We will also be able to realise the greater flexibility and control in our manufacturing process that vertical integration brings," it said. ■
