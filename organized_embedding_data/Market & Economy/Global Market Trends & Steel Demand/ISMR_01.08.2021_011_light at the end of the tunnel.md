# Light at the end of the tunnel

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 ISMR]

During its online General Assembly, CECIMO outlined that the optimistic forecasts for the recovery of machine tool demand, expected investment growth and the EU recovery fund are generating 'a positive outlook' for the industry in the coming period. On the policy side, the General Assembly also debated the future of European data infrastructure. The discussion focused on manufacturing data spaces.

"The COVID-19 pandemic has profoundly affected numerous industries and the machine tool industry was no exception. There is no denying that a return to pre-crisis levels will probably take several years, if not more. However, after a very challenging year, the European machine tool industry sees light at the end of the tunnel, as economic statistics suggest a steady recovery. The machine tool market is set for solid growth in 2021 across all major geographies," commented CECIMO.

As CECIMO President, Dr. Hans-Martin Schneeberger, pointed out during his introductory speech, the resilience and recovery of the sector are central topics of this year's CECIMO General Assembly. In his opening speech, he stated that “CECIMO looks to the future with positivity and hopes that our industry will come out of this crisis more resilient than ever."

"Economic data gathered by our teams and discussed in detail during this years' Economic Committee Meeting, reinforces the idea of a return to pre-pandemic levels. The CECIMO Business Climate Barometer and responses from CECIMO companies highlight a positive business climate, with operating rates among many CECIMO companies going above 70% (April/May 2021). With the growth of 4% in both regions, the EU and EZ in Q4 2020, and 1% in Q1 2021, the Industrial Production Index reached pre-pandemic levels that we saw in the final quarter of 2019," explained CECIMO.

"Based on internal figures, we did see a sharp decline in terms of production in the first half of 2020. CECIMO's total production in 2020 decreased by 26.7% compared to 2019 levels. But, with an output of €19.9 billion, CECIMO retains the leading position in the global machine tool market, with a 33% share at the end of 2020. The provisional figures for the first quarter of 2021 give us reasons for optimism, as orders for machine tools increased by 5%, compared to the previous quarter, and are 7% higher than in the same period in 2019."
