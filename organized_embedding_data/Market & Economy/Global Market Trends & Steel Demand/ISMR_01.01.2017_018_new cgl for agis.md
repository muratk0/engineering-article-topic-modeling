# New CGL for AGIS

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

NIPPON STEEL & Sumitomo Metal Corporation (NSSMC) has a twenty per cent stake in Al Ghurair Iron & Steel LLC (AGIS), which is a galvanizing mill in the United Arab Emirates (UAE). In October 2016, AGIS began commercial production of its second Continuous Galvanizing Line (CGL) in Dubai, UAE.

"AGIS is the first company to install a cold rolling and galvanizing line in the UAE, and it has built a strong customer base in the flat steel market for building and construction in the Middle East, North Africa and West Asia. As a result, AGIS has kept a high level of sales of more than 200,000 tons per annum since commencing operation in 2009," said NSSMC.

After starting the second CGL, AGIS will scale up its annual production capacity from 200,000 tons to 400,000 tons. NSSMC will make a contribution to the further growth of AGIS and the UAE economy through the stable supply of Hot Rolled Coil (the substrate for hot-dip galvanized steel sheets), which is expected to increase in demand.■
