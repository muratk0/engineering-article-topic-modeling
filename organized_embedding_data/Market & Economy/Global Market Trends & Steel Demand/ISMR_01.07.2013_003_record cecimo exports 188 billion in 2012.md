# RECORD CECIMO EXPORTS – €18.8 billion in 2012

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

DURING its General Assembly in Villasimius on 4 June 2013, CECIMO’s Economic Committee confirmed that:

In 2013 the production of machine tools is forecasted to reach a level similar to that of 2012, when the European machine tool sector produced machines worth €22.2 billion.

European machine tool producers are becoming increasingly dependent on exports in 2013. CECIMO estimates the demand to be stable for machine tools in Europe for 2013.

Restoring the growth of domestic consumption will be vital for securing that future innovations continue to come out of Europe.

“European machine tool production grew 6 per cent in 2012, increasing to €22.2 billion. Taking into account global economic developments, production growth is estimated to stabilise and is forecasted to reach €22.5 billion in 2013. This outlook is supported by the 15 per cent increase in order intake in the last quarter of 2012 compared to the third quarter of 2012, which resulted in a modestly positive beginning for the current year,” said CECIMO, the European Association of Machine Tool Industries (which combines 15 national associations of machine tool builders).

CECIMO’s exports reached €18.8 billion in 2012, which is 9 per cent up from the previous record established in 2008. However, deteriorating business confidence in Europe has left its mark on machine tool consumption. European machine tool consumption contracted by about 2 per cent in 2012 compared to the previous year.

“CECIMO welcomes the strong focus placed on the production equipment industry by the European Commission in its latest Communication on Industrial policy. The Commission has identified ‘advanced manufacturing technologies for clean production’ as one of the six priority areas which will drive growth in Europe. We are fully supportive of mobilising EU funding instruments and European Investment Bank loans to help customers upgrade their production lines to the latest technology levels. This kind of modernisation will help sky-rocket productivity and resource-efficiency in user sectors whilst stimulating innovation in the machine tool industry,” concluded CECIMO. See www.cecimo.eu
