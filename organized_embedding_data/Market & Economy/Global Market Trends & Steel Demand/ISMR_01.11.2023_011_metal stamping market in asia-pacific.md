# Metal stamping market in Asia-Pacific

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

According to new analysis on ResearchandMarkets.com, the Asia-Pacific (APAC) metal stamping market is projected to be valued at US$ 110.86 billion by 2028 from around US$ 83.57 billion in 2022, growing at a CAGR of 4.8%. This report provides information on metal stamping in the APAC region.

“The demand for metal components across several end-user industries drives the APAC region's metal stamping market. Furthermore, major players are focusing on strategic agreements, acquisitions and collaboration with emerging players to enter the APAC metal stamping market to gain access to commercially launched products," said the report.

“Escalating demand for automobiles and consumer electronics has positioned the APAC region as one of the foremost markets on a global scale. The growth is driven by developing countries such as China and India, where the rising demand for consumer electronics is anticipated to boost market growth during the forecast period," it continued.

The escalating process of industrialisation, coupled with infrastructure expansion and advances in the defence sector, is anticipated to exert a favourable influence on the demand for machinery and equipment across the APAC region. Other industries, such as electronics, are also experiencing strong growth in the APAC region.

"The APAC region dominates the automotive metal stamping market. The growth is supported by the rise in vehicle production across the region. In 2022, China's automotive industry produced nearly 24 million passenger cars. Therefore, the increase in vehicle production and a rise in demand for passenger vehicles are anticipated to fuel demand for metal stamping in the automotive industry," added the report.

"China, Japan, India, South Korea, Europe and North America are the largest automotive production markets in the world, accounting for nearly 90% of vehicles produced globally. China held around a 32% share of global production, leading all markets in 2021. The domestic demand for vehicles in China, India and certain markets outside North America and Western Europe has increased," it concluded.
