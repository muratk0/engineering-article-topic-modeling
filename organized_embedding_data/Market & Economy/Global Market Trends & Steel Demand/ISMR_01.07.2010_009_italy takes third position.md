# ITALY TAKES THIRD POSITION

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

UCIMU SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools and robotics association) has reported that Italian production of metal forming machine tools fell by 31.6% to 1,830 million euros in 2009 as a result of the global economic slowdown.

“Despite this, however, Italy took third place in the international producer rankings, behind China and Germany but ahead of Japan, Korea and the USA,” it told ISMR.

Italian exports, while dropping by 31.2% compared to 2008, came to 57.1% of total production in the sector. The most exported machines were bending machines, followed by presses. The quota on total exports (machine tools and production systems) was 43.5%.

## TATA STEEL RESULTS

Tata Steel Limited has declared Audited Consolidated Group Financial Results for the financial year ending 31 March 2010. Tata Steel Group reported a major improvement in profitability in the second half (H2 FY’10), with consolidated EBITDA of Rs. 8,734 crores (US$ 1,944 million) almost 15 times higher than the Rs. 606 crores (US$ 135 million) reported in H1 FY’10. Group EBITDA in the fourth quarter (Q4 FY’10) at Rs. 5,333 crores (US$ 1,187 million) rose 57% over Q3 FY’10 (Rs. 3,401 crores, US$ 757 million).

The Group recorded Profit After Tax (after minority interest and share of profit of associates) of Rs. 2,907 crores (US$ 647 million) in H2 FY’10 compared to a loss of Rs. 4,916 crores (US$ 1,095 million) in H1 FY’10. Group EBITDA was Rs. 9,340 crores (US$ 2,079 million) in FY’10 compared to Rs. 18,497 crores (US$ 4,118 million) in FY’09. In FY’10 the Group reported a Loss After Tax (after minority interest and share of profit of associates) of Rs. 2,009 crores (US$ 447 million) compared to a Profit After Tax (after minority interest and share of associates) of Rs. 4,950 crores (US$ 1,102 million) in FY’09.

Tata Steel India reported an EBITDA margin of 44% in H2 FY’10, up from 33% in H1 FY’10 on the strength of robust demand, which led to higher prices, and the Jamshedpur plant operating at higher than rated capacity.

The Group’s results were enhanced by the dramatic turnaround in the performance of Tata Steel Europe, primarily on account of a stronger order book leading to higher capacity utilisation and lower costs in the second half of FY’10.

Consolidated Turnover for the Group at Rs. 102,393 crores (US$ 22,796 million) in FY’10 was 31% lower than in FY’09 (Rs 147,329 crores, US$ 32,800 million) because of reduced capacity utilisation in Europe due to the impact of the financial crisis in the first half compared to record high volumes and prices in H1 FY’09. Turnover in H2 FY’10 rose 10% to Rs. 53,706 crores (US$ 11,957 million) compared to Rs. 48,687 crores (US$ 10,840 million) in H1 FY’10.
