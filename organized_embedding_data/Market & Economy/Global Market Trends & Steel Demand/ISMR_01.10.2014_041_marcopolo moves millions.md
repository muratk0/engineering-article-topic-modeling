## Who:

Marcopolo S.A., headquartered in Caxias do Sul, Brazil. Founded in 1949, it has 20,000 employees. See www.marcopolo.com.br

## What:

The company manufactures bus bodies and makes use of modern development and production processes.

## How:

TruLaser Tube 5000, 4 punching machines and 12 laser cutting machines from TRUMPF, including 7 x TruLaser 5030 fiber, 4 x TruStore 3030

## Note:

Brazil is one of the BRICS nations – the world’s five most important emerging economies. Its 27 federal states are home to about 203 million people.The transportation and logistics sector accounts for a 5.3 per cent share in GDP. About 55 per cent of GDP is generated in southeastern Brazil – in the four states of São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Minas Gerais and Espírito Santo.
