# CECIMO forecasts growth to continue in 2015

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

Dr. Frank Brinken, chairman of CECIMO’s Economic Committee

“TOGETHER WITH THE EUROPEAN ECONOMY, European machine tool (MT) production intensified in 2014, growing by 1.3% to reach 23 billion euros. As the European MT industry exports worldwide, it is affected by two opposing trends. First, a slowdown in emerging markets makes these reduce their investment in industrial machinery. Secondly, growth is relatively stable in developed markets like Europe and North America, which implies more investment in industrial machinery. Both trends considered, European machine tool production is estimated to increase to 23.6 billion euros in 2015 (+3%),” commented CECIMO (the European Association of the Machine Tool Industries) at EMO Milano 2015.

Despite the slowdown in global economic growth, European MT’s exports still perform well. Machine tools with a total value of 18.2 billion euros were shipped abroad in 2014. CECIMO expects the exports to grow by 3% to 18.7 billion euros in 2015, which would be the second best result of all time.

“Rising wages in emerging economies put pressure on production costs. The European machine tool industry focuses on productivity and high efficiency that gives us our competitive edge. Our highly innovative machines are in demand all over the world because they offer intelligent and cost-efficient manufacturing solutions,” explained Dr. Frank Brinken, chairman of CECIMO’s Economic Committee and a member of the Board of Directors of several machine tool companies.

CECIMO imports rose to 8.8 billion euros in 2014. The weak euro will slow imports’ growth down but the association estimates that the European industry’s needs will push MT imports to 9.2 billion euros this year (+3%).

“After two years of stagnation, European businesses’ investments picked up in 2014. European MT consumption expanded by 10% in 2014 to 13.7 billion euros. The consumption is expected to go on increasing and record 14.2 billion euros in 2015. Improving demand in European markets is confirmed by the strong order intake. In the second quarter of 2015, the CECIMO domestic orders’ index increased by 6% and 14% in comparison with the previous quarter and the same quarter last year respectively. We expect European MT consumption to grow for the next four years,” it concluded.
