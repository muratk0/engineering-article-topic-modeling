# Tube bending solutions for infrastructure projects

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

The infrastructure sector has immense importance in driving the growth of an economy. It involves the construction of various essential structures such as power plants, bridges, dams and roads, as well as urban infrastructure. It also acts as a significant support system for other service sectors, making it a vital component for overall economic development.

"The European transportation infrastructure construction market is projected to witness substantial growth in the coming years. With an estimated market size of US$ 219.56 billion in 2024, it is anticipated to reach US$ 279.58 billion by 2029, reflecting a CAGR of 4.14% during the forecast period (2024-2029). The driving force behind this market expansion lies in the development of sustainable and energy-efficient transportation infrastructure. Moreover, the market is being fuelled by the increasing demand for substantial infrastructure investments to support the widespread adoption of electric vehicles," bending specialist, FACCIN Group, told ISMR.

It also highlighted various ongoing European rail and airport projects as examples of key infrastructure construction.

"The Suburban Rail System is set to experience a significant boost in capacity. Ten proposals have been put forward to modernise or upgrade the existing infrastructure, while eight plans aim to electrify specific rail sections. Additionally, countries such as Austria, Estonia, Italy and Spain have expressed their intentions to build new rail connections, primarily along the trans-European transport corridor known as TEN-T (some of which will be highspeed). Spain, France, Italy and Hungary have strategic plans in place to enhance their regional and local transportation infrastructure, while Croatia and Hungary are actively focused on optimising their networks by removing any existing obstacles. Additionally, many new airport projects moved forward, with various new initiatives emerging and undergoing essential maintenance work," added FACCIN Group.

## On a roll

In this ever-growing world of infrastructure projects, the demand for high-quality, rolled-metal structural elements is on the rise. Tubes, pipes, beams and channels are essential components that are needed to meet architectural design requirements.

With a track record spanning over sixty years, the FACCIN Group has firmly established itself in the European market. The Group provides tube bending solutions for large-diameter metal tubes. Its Roundo® R and B angle rolls, along with the Faccin® RCMI series, are suitable for the infrastructure sector.

"Our solutions are designed for precision and to boost productivity and lower energy consumption as well as to provide consistent repeatability and useability for operators of any skill level," FACCIN Group explained.

The Roundo® R is a part of the Group's premium line of angle rolls. Boasting capacities ranging from 14 to 20,000cm³, these angle rolls have been designed to boost productivity.

"The R series features a heavy-proportioned main frame; large shaft diameters and bearing sizes; high drive torque and rotation speed; great bending roll force and powerful guide rolls. Additionally, the low working height eliminates the need for costly pits, providing operators with even greater ergonomic benefits. A user-friendly control concept, the wCNC4, is provided as standard," commented FACCIN Group.

The Roundo® B series is designed for the seamless bending of both steel and aluminium profiles. With its design incorporating variable geometry and three motorised rolls, this machine bends small profiles as well as large and high-resistance modulus profiles. With a capacity of up to 19,000cm³, the B series machines are designed for heavy metal fabrication work.

"Our RCMI series offers precision and reliability in profile bending. It features fast roll configuration; six-directional, fully hydraulic guide rolls; infinitely variable speed (HTRP); automatic roll speed compensation and maximum bending torque. The RCMI can handle all standard profiles, including beams and curved ducts in the direction of greater inertia. It can also extend to bending both round and square tubes, which is why the series comes complete with custom-designed shaped rolls, tailored to accommodate tubes of any size," added FACCIN Group.

The company also offers solutions to mitigate tube deformation while bending with smaller radii. The RCMI can be equipped with a mandrel that maintains the integrity of the tubes throughout the bending process. This finds extensive applications in special bending requirements, such as roller coasters that require 3D bending. The Roundo®R and the Faccin® RCMI series can also be used for spiral bending.
