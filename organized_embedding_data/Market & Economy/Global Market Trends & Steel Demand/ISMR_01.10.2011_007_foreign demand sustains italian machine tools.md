# FOREIGN DEMAND SUSTAINS ITALIAN MACHINE TOOLS

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

THE POSITIVE TREND in orders placed for Italian machine tools continues: in the second quarter of 2011, an increase of 13 per cent over the same period of the previous year was recorded. Much of this is down to demand outside Italy, says UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the association of Italian machine tools, robots and automation manufacturers). The Italian domestic market, on the other hand, has not shown the same levels of growth.

According to data compiled by the association, the index of orders placed in foreign markets has increased by 44.5 per cent compared to the period April-June 2010, for an absolute index value of 102. The index of orders for the Italian domestic market marked a fall of 33.8 per cent over the same period in the previous year, for an absolute index value of 59.2 per cent.

“On a six-monthly base, the increase of order intake amounted to 16.3 per cent (for an absolute value of 101.5) compared to the period January to June 2010, thanks to the increase in foreign orders which rose by 37.4 per cent, balancing out the decrease of internal orders which fell by 19.5 per cent,” UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE told ISMR.

“The quarterly survey which extends the positive streak recorded from the start of 2010,” said Giancarlo Losma, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, “is certainly comforting. Our factories have now begun to return to productivity levels close to those reached before the crisis.”
