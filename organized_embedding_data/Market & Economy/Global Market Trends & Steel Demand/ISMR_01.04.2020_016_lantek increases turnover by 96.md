# Lantek increases turnover by 9.6%

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

LANTEK HAS disclosed its economic results from last year, reflecting the steadiness of its rate of growth. In 2019, its turnover reached 21.1 million euros, implying an increase of 9.6% compared to the previous year.

Lantek is a Spanish multinational, specialising in the development of software and advanced manufacturing solutions for the sheet metal sector. It employs over 200 people worldwide. Its number of active clients increased notably in 2019, bringing in 1,900 new users, doubling the normal growth rate and reaching a total of 23,700 distributed over more than one hundred countries.

“Our international business continues to grow, now constituting 86.3% of turnover. The countries with greatest significance for us are: Germany, Italy, the USA, France and China which, together with growing local business, are becoming strategic markets. The United Kingdom also achieved double-digit growth, despite the uncertainties surrounding Brexit," it told ISMR.

For South Korea and France, an increase of 30% was recorded in each territory. Germany posted an increase of 26%. Mexico, Turkey and Poland also increased their turnover, exceeding double digits.

"For yet another year, we have registered close to double-digit growth which has enabled us to achieve record sales figures. This demonstrates the stability of our business, thanks to our international market penetration, the tremendous growth of our installed client base and our strong portfolio of products and solutions. This stability allows Lantek's business to develop sustainably while maintaining a solid financial position, which means that we are able to face the growth in demand implicit in the new challenges of Industry 4.0", confirmed Alberto López de Biñaspre, Lantek's CEO.

The company presented its performance at IM2020, the international meeting that, every year, gathers 170 of Lantek's employees from Spain and its international offices to take stock of the year. The International Meeting is also the backdrop for defining business objectives that will reinforce the organisation's strategy and road map for the years to come.

In terms of business areas, the company's commitment to digitalisation has been reflected with an increase of 24.7% in the Solutions division. The division's mission is to achieve the Smart & Digital Factory, implementing an integral management model for manufacturing and the company as a whole. Growth in the subscription area for updates and technical also recorded growth of 16%.

Last year, the company dedicated around 2.96 million euros, which constituted an increase of 15% compared to the previous year, to Research, Development and Innovation. The aim of this investment was to launch innovative solutions in advanced manufacturing, specifically for the sheet metal sector. The company is finalising the launch of an advanced MES which bases decision-making on data analysis.

"In 2020, our strategy will focus on our clients and on the exponential growth of our installed international base, making the digital transformation of our sector a reality," concluded Alberto López de Biñaspre. See www.lanteksms.com/us ■
