# Smart Innovations

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Trends, drivers, innovations and market forecasts for global consumer electronics markets come under the spotlight.

ISMR SAYS:

“The consumer electronics market is characterised by fast-moving technology development and integration, with smart technology and AI now playing a key role in connected devices for consumers or for their day-to-day use. These include smartphones; household appliances; gaming equipment; cameras; wearables etc. The market is characterised by fast-moving technology development and integration, with smart technology and AI now playing a key role in connected devices for consumers. Increasing disposable incomes, especially in emerging markets, spur higher spending on consumer electronics and tech gadgets.

The consumer electronics industry is highly competitive and fragmented with prominent players such as Apple Inc; Bose Corporation; Samsung Electronics etc. Manufacturers are adopting strategic steps (such as mergers, acquisitions, partnerships and collaborations) to meet growing consumer demand and consolidate their positions in the market. The market is also dynamic and multifaceted: it is shaped by innovation, brand reputation and consumer preferences.

"Mobiles and smartphones lead the consumer electronics market due to high demand, rapid technological advancements and widespread adoption globally. Smart electronics offer increased connectivity, automation and integration with IoT, with enhanced functionality and convenience. The residential end-use segment is driven by the increasing adoption of smart home devices and personal gadgets," commented analyst, Transparency Market Research, in its latest report on the consumer electronics market.

## Market innovations

According to analyst Global Market Insights, the consumer electronics market was valued at US$ 1.3 trillion in 2023 and is anticipated to register a CAGR of 13.2 % between 2024 and 2032.

"The market is growing due to rapid technological evolution. Manufacturers are heavily investing in research and development, resulting in the production of innovative new products across various categories. This innovation extends to advances in crucial components of electronic appliances, such as display technologies like OLED, and the introduction of higher-resolution screens such as 4K and 8K, which are driving the production of smartphones and TVs," said the analyst.

“Additionally, the consumer electronics industry is driven by the increased use of smartphones. Manufactures are launching various models of smartphones with new features and technologies (such as advanced hardware components) which is further driving innovation in the market," it added.

Analyst Frost and Sullivan expects the consumer electronics industry to grow significantly because of advances in digital and electronics technologies which, it said, will focus on sustainability issues and improve functionality; user experiences; connectivity; integration; miniaturisation and advanced sensors.

Transparency Market Research estimates the global consumer electronics market to flourish at a CAGR of 8.5% from 2021 to 2031. It projects that the overall sales revenue for consumer electronics will reach US$ one trillion by the end of 2031.

## Trends, drivers and challenges

"Based on product type, the market is segmented into audio & video equipment, major household appliances, small household appliances and digital photo equipment. Audio and video equipment dominated the market in 2023, generating a revenue exceeding US$ 920 billion, and is expected to reach US$ three trillion by 2032," commented Global Market Insights.

“Manufacturers are heavily investing in enhancing consumer experiences through innovations such as active noise cancellation, surround sound systems and highresolution displays. These advances not only improve audio listening quality and visual clarity but also cater to the growing demand for entertainment experiences in home environments and commercial spaces," it added.

The integration of smart features, including voice assistants and connectivity options such as Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, further enhance the appeal and functionality of audio and video equipment. These smart capabilities allow for seamless integration with other smart home devices and services, contributing to overall convenience.

Consumer preference shifts towards eco-friendly and sustainable electronics are driving manufacturers to adopt greener practices and materials in production.

“The development of energy-efficient equipment and use of recycled materials in manufacturing will be fuelled by consumer demand for sustainable practices. Longer-lasting devices are in style, which will cut down on eWaste and promote ethical use," added analyst, Frost and Sullivan.

"Consumer preference shifts towards eco-friendly and sustainable electronics are driving manufacturers to adopt greener practices and materials in production," added Transparency Market Research.

The wearables market for consumer electronics is also growing.

“The integration of health and wellness features into consumer electronics is gaining traction. Wearable devices equipped with health monitoring sensors and software caters to consumers' increasing focus on fitness and wellbeing," commented Transparency Market Research.

"Consumers seek personalised experiences from their electronics. Companies offering customisable options for devices such as smartphones and laptops are gaining favour, allowing users to tailor their gadgets to specific preferences and needs. Collaborations between consumer electronics companies and other industries (such as healthcare, automotive and entertainment) are shaping product innovation. Partnerships enable the integration of electronics into various sectors, expanding market reach and diversifying product offerings," it continued.

The analyst also highlighted the incorporation of accessibility features that is becoming more prevalent in consumer electronics. Products designed with features such as voice command, larger text options and screen readers enhance usability for individuals with disabilities, fostering inclusivity and expanding consumer demographics.

"With increasing concerns over data privacy and security, consumer electronics companies are prioritising robust data protection measures. Investments in encryption technologies, secure authentication methods and privacy-focused policies aim to build consumer trust and safeguard sensitive information," concluded Transparency Market Research.

A major challenge for the consumer electronics market is counterfeit products. Counterfeit products are cheap alternatives available in the market which replicate original products, using cheap materials and components.

The global consumer electronics market is also highly competitive.

"Numerous brands and manufacturers operate in the market, offering the same types of appliances and products. There is a constant need for research and development to keep products updated with changing technology. There is pressure on manufactures to keep themselves ahead of their competitors by evolving with these technological changes. This leads to shorter product life span and low profit margins," added analyst, Global Market Insights.

## The rise of AI

The consumer electronics industry is experiencing the rise of smart electronics integrated with AI. This trend is driven by continuous technological innovations which enhance connectivity and introduce advanced features. Continuous advances in AI, IoT and 5G drive innovation, enhancing functionality and connectivity in consumer electronics products. Increasing consumer demand for smart home devices, wearables and IoT-enabled gadgets is also fuelling market growth.

“Manufacturers are introducing electronics by integrating voice assistant controls, Bluetooth connectivity and other smart capabilities. These advances aim to enhance the consumer experience by offering convenience, interactivity and efficiency across various electronic products. As a result, smart electronics are reshaping how consumers interact with devices further providing seamless integration into everyday life and driving market growth," outlined analyst, Global Market Insights.

Artificial intelligence (AI), said analyst Frost and Sullivan, will power features such as voice assistants with natural language processing, smarter recommendations and personalised user experiences.

“Generative AI (GenAI) technology will enable capabilities such as creating realistic images or music, editing photos with advanced tools and experiencing immersive augmented reality (AR) that seamlessly blends the physical and digital world. A growing network of connected devices will create smart homes, offices and even cities. Imagine seamlessly, wearables that communicate with smartphones or environmental sensors that adjust lighting and temperature automatically. Features such as real-time data processing for AR/VR experiences, smooth cloud gaming and quicker downloads for updates and content will all be made possible by faster and more dependable internet connectivity," pinpointed Frost and Sullivan.

Continuous advances in AI, IoT and 5G drive innovation, enhancing functionality and connectivity in consumer electronics products.

Environmental sensing for smart homes, enhanced camera capabilities in smartphones and health monitoring through wearables will all be made possible by smaller and more advanced sensors. Smaller components will also enable sleeker gadget designs and the integration of additional functionality into smaller form factors as technology develops.

"With rapid advances in AI, IoT and 5G, the competitive arena intensifies, driving companies to differentiate through seamless integration, design aesthetics and personalised user experiences," added Transparency Market Research.

## Regional segmentation

As confirmed by several market research reports, the consumer electronics market is dominated by the Asia-Pacific region.

"The Asia-Pacific region has emerged as the dominant force in the consumer electronics market, generating substantial revenue of US$ 560 billion and expected to reach US$ 1.3 trillion by 2032. This dominance is driven by large consumer bases in countries such as China and India. Additionally, the region is known as a technological hub with South Korea leading in the development of advanced components and technologies, including high-end resolution displays, AI assistants and Bluetooth connectivity. These innovations not only drive regional growth but also influence global consumer electronics trends, clarifying Asia-Pacific's leading position in the market," highlighted analyst, Global Market Insights.

The analyst confirmed that China dominated the Asia-Pacific consumer electronics industry with revenue of around US$ 210 billion in 2023 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 13.8% during the forecast period. China is a manufacturing hub for electronics which helps to lower production costs.

In North America, the U.S. dominates the consumer electronics market with an expected revenue of around US$ 550 billion by 2032 (according to the analyst). U.S. manufacturers are continuously developing technologies (such as AI-powered devices) to attract consumers who look for advanced technologies in smartphones, home entertainment and other consumer electronics products.

"With a tech-savvy consumer base, North America boasts high adoption rates for smartphones, smart home devices and wearable technologies," outlined Transparency Market Research.

European countries showcase robust demand for consumer electronics, driven by affluent populations and a penchant for cutting-edge gadgets. According to Transparency Market Research, European consumers prioritise design, sustainability and data privacy, shaping product offerings and market strategies for companies like Samsung and Philips.

"Germany led the European market with a revenue of around US$ 82.8 billion in 2023, with revenue expected to reach around US$ 200 billion by 2032 (growing at a CAGR of 13.9%). German consumers exhibit strong demand for luxury and premium technology products. They are 'tech-savvy' and display a strong interest in consuming the latest technological electronics products," concluded Global Market Insights.
