# analyses global HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning) markets and trends for sheet metal manufacturers

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Global demand for HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning) equipment is forecast to rise 5.7 per cent annually to US$ 120 billion in 2018. The HVAC equipment market was estimated to be over US$ 22 billion in 2013. The Asia/Pacific region will remain the dominant geographic market, while North America grows the fastest. Heat pumps will lead gains based on lower penetration rates and multiple abilities to heat and cool air, and heat water.

The key factors driving growth in the market include recovery in new construction, recovering economy, pent-up demand, energy efficiency, cost saving and increasing concern regarding indoor air quality. The market for cooling equipment is led by the unitary segment with ducted split and rooftop units covering the major market share. Heating equipment is led by air-source heat pumps and gas furnaces. Equipment that has the ability to provide both heating as well as cooling generally shows strong growth.

Sheet metal manufacturers in this market are generally focused upon the provision of equipment such as heat pumps; furnaces; boilers etc. for heating; air conditioners, ducted/ductless splits, rooftop units, chillers, air handling units, fan coil units and cooling towers for cooling and industrial/commercial blowers, fans and humidifiers/dehumidifiers for ventilation.

HVAC systems are primarily used in buildings to provide good indoor air quality through adequate ventilation and air filtration and to provide thermal comfort. They use equipment such as heat exchangers, pumps and fans to control and regulate a room's humidity and temperature. The systems are widely used in various industries/sectors such as the construction and infrastructure industry, the data centre industry and the healthcare sector.

## Regional market forecasts

Research and Markets analysts forecast that the HVAC equipment market in Europe will grow at a CAGR of 2.88 per cent over the period 2013-2018. The latest in-depth air conditioning reports from BSRIA are forecasting a value growth of 7% in the European air conditioning market in 2015, following a contraction of 3% in 2014. The total value of the European market in 2015 is forecast to be €8.7bn. BSRIA is then forecasting an accelerated growth that will see the market valued at over €9.5bn by the end of 2017.

“Whilst most countries are forecast to grow in 2014, both Russia and Italy are showing significant reductions in the value of their markets with falls of 24% and 14% respectively. Conversely, the UK market is showing good growth in 2014 with a growth of 18% forecast, and growth forecast in all product areas,” BSRIA told ISMR.

BSRIA is forecasting that Spain, which has been badly affected by the economic downturn, will return to growth in 2015 and reached a point of no further reduction in the market in 2014.

BSRIA’s new World Heating market study 2014 covers Europe, Asia/Pacific, ME & North Africa and North and South American regions. Altogether, the study includes 90 reports analysing Domestic Boiler / Commercial Boiler / Water Heating (including solar thermals) / Radiators and Underfloor Heating world markets.

“In 2013, the world heating market posted a healthy growth of 11% in volume and 13% in value although not all world regions were showing equally good results. The growth of the world heating market owes much to positive developments in the sales of domestic boilers and water heaters in the Asia Pacific, Middle East and North Africa regions while both European and American markets have seen a small decline of 1 to 2% in the total traditional heating markets,” commented BSRIA.

“In terms of volume, Asia Pacific accounted for the largest share of sales of all heating products while, in terms of value, Europe is holding the largest part of the market. The European heating market also posted a slight growth in value due to the accelerating shift towards more efficient products, to be noted mainly in the boiler market segment where condensing boiler sales keep increasing. In the years to come, the European Union area is to see an important change in the boiler market structure following the adoption of the Ecodesign Directive that is setting strict requirements towards the efficiency of heat generators which will become effective from September 2015. It will have a negative effect on the sales of non-condensing boilers, resulting in a very significant decrease in their market share by the end of the decade.”

BSRIA's specialist consultancy, Worldwide Market Intelligence (WMI), estimated the World Refrigeration market at over US$ 31 billion in 2012 and expected to rise to US$ 40 billion by 2016. Leading countries are driven by the need to improve energy performance and to replace refrigerants, together with tackling issues such as noise and recyclability. China has by far the largest share of the world market which is estimated at US$ 10.94 billion. India has the highest CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) at 15%, followed by Brazil and Russia on 9%.

## HVAC in India

HVAC systems are becoming one of the key building blocks in modern infrastructure. These systems are found in almost all upcoming commercial as well as residential buildings. The rise in infrastructure, rapid urbanisation and growth in commercial properties are some of the key factors fuelling the market for HVAC systems in India. With healthy growth anticipated in the real estate sector, the country is expected to witness strong infrastructure development, which would boost the market for HVAC systems over the next five years.

According to the recently published report by TechSci Research, "India HVAC Market Forecast & Opportunities, 2019", the HVAC market in India is forecast to reach US$ 3.97 billion by 2019. Growth in retail, hospitality and commercial sectors is significantly boosting the demand for such systems in the country, as these sectors involve large-scale application of HVAC systems in organised retail outlets, shopping complexes, hotels etc. Moreover, with anticipated growth in FDI (Foreign Direct Investment), several international players are expected to enter and start operations in the Indian retail market. Driven by strong FDI inflow from multi-national food processing companies, the retail market in India is projected to reach US$ 726.62 billion by 2019, which is expected to further fuel the country's HVAC market.

The room air conditioning segment captured majority revenue share in India HVAC market in 2013, and is expected to retain its dominance by 2019. This is largely due to portability; ease of installation and less space requirement of room air conditioning systems. Centralised air conditioners, in contrast, are comparatively costlier and are usually preferred for large commercial or office spaces, where more tons of refrigeration is required for effective cooling. Consequently, segment share of room air conditioners in the India HVAC market is expected to increase over the next five years on account of several residential projects coming up in the forthcoming years.

"The Northern region has been leading the HVAC market in India, with high revenue contribution from Delhi-NCR. In 2013, the real estate market in Delhi-NCR grew by 22.5%, leading to high growth in the HVAC market, in volume as well as value terms. Rapid infrastructure development activities have been witnessed in cities such as Gurgaon, Noida, Greater Noida and Chandigarh in recent year. Further growth in real estate sector in these cities is expected to spur the market for HVAC systems in Northern India", said Karan Chechi, Research Director with TechSci Research, a research-based global management consulting firm.

## An eye on sustainability

The real challenge for the future of HVAC systems is sustainability. Energy efficiency represents a major challenge across all HVAC systems. Heating, cooling and ventilation systems account for almost 66% of commercial and residential energy usage.

Improving these figures is a global challenge that will aid the development of the HVAC industry as well as the planet's environment. Studies have shown that reducing building energy consumption by up to 20% is a feasible goal, and the HVAC industry is expected to play a significant part in making this a reality.

Innovative Bosch management technology is already in use at over 1,500 industrial, commercial, retail and public facilities.

"When it comes to HVAC systems, there is a huge amount of unused energy-saving potential," commented Michael Blichmann, managing director of Bosch Energy and Building Solutions GmbH. "By improving HVAC system management, we not only enhance comfort, we can also achieve energy savings of 30 per cent on average. The investments often pay off within two to three years." ■
