# An eye on machine vision

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

THE GLOBAL MACHINE vision market is expected to grow from US$ 9.6 billion in 2020 to US$ 13.0 billion by 2025, at a CAGR of 6.1% during the forecast period.

A new report entitled ‘Machine Vision Market with COVID-19 impact Analysis by Deployment (General & Robotic cell), Component (Hardware and Software), Product (PC-based and Smart camera-based), Application, End-user Industry & Region - Forecast to 2025’ has been added to MarketsandMarkets’ portfolio.

“The major driving factors in the machine vision market is the increasing need for quality inspection and automation, growing demand for vision-guided robotic systems, increasing adoption of vision-guided robotic systems and many more. The major restraints for the market are varying end-user requirements and the lack of skilled professionals in the manufacturing factories. A current challenge for the market also includes the slowdown in production activities and further adoption of machine vision technologies and products in the manufacturing industries amid the COVID-19 outbreak,” said the report.

Position & guidance is expected to grow at the fastest rate in the machine vision market during the forecast period. Position & guidance applications enable manufacturers to build multiple products on the same production line and reduces the need for expensive hard tooling to maintain part position during inspection. This application also helps to improve the efficiency of production processes by instantaneously detecting the positional relationship between the target and machine tool or table and helps to carefully control the system.

The automotive industry is anticipated to hold a major market share in the machine vision market during the forecast period.

“The demand for machine vision systems is increasing in the automotive sector as it provides improved accuracy in critical activities such as bin picking and positioning of parts, such as doors and panels, for assembly. The demand for machine vision systems used in automobiles is significantly impacted by the outbreak of COVID-19 since the automotive sector is deeply affected by this pandemic. The demand for automobiles remains critical owing to the confidence and income sentiments of customers,” added the report.

The machine vision market in APAC is expected to hold a major market share during the forecast period. Countries in APAC (such as China, Japan, India and South Korea) have some of the largest manufacturing facilities where automation of manufacturing processes has been taken as the highest priority. Strong competition among consumer electronics companies in APAC is likely to boost the adoption of machine vision systems in the region, said the report. However, during the COVID-19 outbreak, shutdowns and decline in demand have affected the growth of the machine vision market in APAC.
