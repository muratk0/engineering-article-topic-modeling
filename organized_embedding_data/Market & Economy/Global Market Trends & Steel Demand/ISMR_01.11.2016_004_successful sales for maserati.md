# Successful sales for Maserati

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

MASERATI'S NEW LEVANTE SUV has helped Maserati North America increase sales by 10.8 per cent in October 2016, compared to September 2016, the company has confirmed. The new Levante is produced at the Mirafiori plant in Turin, Italy.

Total sales in North America for October 2016 were 1,377 vehicles, compared to 1,243 vehicles in September of 2016, an increase of 10.8 per cent. Sales were up from October 2015, year-over-year for the same month, 13.2 percent. In the United States, total October 2016 sales were 1,304 vehicles. Italy is once again the company's largest European market, with 97 new registrations in September 2016, representing a 273% increase compared to August 2015. This brings the total of Maserati vehicles registered in the first eight months of the current year to 1,050, +9% compared to the same period in 2015.

“The brand enjoyed continuous growth in all the main European markets: the United Kingdom, Germany, Benelux, France, Switzerland and Spain all made major contributions to this very strong performance. 4,180 cars have been registered since the start of 2016, with an average rise of 6% compared to the first eight months of 2015," Maserati told ISMR.

"The performance of the Maserati saloons - Quattroporte and Ghibli - was particularly striking in the United Kingdom, with a 52% increase in sales compared to the same month of 2015. Sales of the Maserati SUV are scheduled to start in this market in the last quarter of 2016."

## An eye on materials

THE GLOBAL LIGHTWEIGHT materials market is projected to reach US$ 225.35 billion by 2024. According to Research and Markets, a positive automotive outlook, coupled with increased numbers of wind project installations, is expected to boost demand for these materials.

"Lightweight materials are gaining popularity in various applications, instead of iron and steel, on account of improved performance. The fuel-efficiency advantage facilitates their use in application segments including automotive and aviation. The reduction in CO₂ emissions, on account of enhanced fuel efficiency in passenger vehicles, will augment the demand for these materials," said the analyst.

The US and China contribute to the majority of the product demand, owing to the presence of a large number of automotive and aviation component manufacturers. Rising innovation in the aviation sector and an increasing number of wind project installations globally is expected to have a positive impact on demand.

Positive results for EuroBLECH 2016

THE 24TH INTERNATIONAL Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, EuroBLECH 2016, ended on 29 October 2016 after five successful show days. A total of 60,636 visitors from 100 countries came to Hanover to see the latest technical developments in sheet metal processing and invest in new production equipment. A total of 1,503 companies from 41 countries exhibited at this year's show.

“The trend for digitised manufacturing is boosting innovation and business activity in the sheet metal working industry and its leading industry exhibition," said Nicola Hamann, managing director of the show organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions. “With an increase of 2% in visitor numbers compared to the previous show, this year's EuroBLECH clearly surpassed the 60,000 visitor mark. Not even the strike of two German airlines on the third exhibition day had an impact on the buoyant mood of the participants and the positive development in visitor numbers. The results of this year's EuroBLECH, a barometer of the industry sector, show a record floor space of some 88,000 square metres net and a sustainable increase in visitor numbers; this demonstrates a healthy economic outlook for the industry sector with its global business activity."
