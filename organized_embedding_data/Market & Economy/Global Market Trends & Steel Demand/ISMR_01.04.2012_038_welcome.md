# WELCOME

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

THE International Monetary Fund projects global growth to slow to 3¼ per cent in 2012, marked down from 4 per cent in the September 2011 WEO. The euro area is expected to enter a mild recession in 2012, while other major advanced economies face the prospect of weak and bumpy growth. In the US, it says, recovery is likely to remain tepid, even though growth accelerated in Q4 2011 and the recent flow of data has been positive. Growth will also moderate in emerging economies.

More and more sheet metal manufacturers recognize that price competition alone cannot build core competencies, nor a route to long-term profit, so efforts to explore new paths of development continue. Technical input to the development of new products is increasing as product differentiation helps to drive long-term planning and growth in the quest to find and fill new market demands and create growth for sustainable development. An eye on innovation, with firm pressure on cost and overhead, are strong priorities for manufacturers this year.
