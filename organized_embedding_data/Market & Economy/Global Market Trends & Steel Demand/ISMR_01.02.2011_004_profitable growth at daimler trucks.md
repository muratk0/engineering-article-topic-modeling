# PROFITABLE GROWTH AT DAIMLER TRUCKS

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

THE POSITIVE TREND in the commercial vehicle sector, which previously had to cope with an average sales decline of 50 per cent, was confirmed in the figures from Daimler Trucks for the third quarter of 2010. Compared to the result posted for the same quarter in 2009, Daimler Trucks’ sales increased by 44 per cent to 94,800 units. By the end of September 2010, total sales at Daimler Trucks were up by 34 per cent to 249,000 units, compared to the total sales of 185,600 units in the same period of 2009. Daimler Trucks also expected the global truck markets to see further recovery by the end of 2010. It forecasts market growth of 5 per cent in Europe, 10 to 15 per cent in the NAFTA region, 50 per cent in Brazil, and between 20 and 30 per cent in Japan. “The forecasts indicate that the worldwide markets for medium-duty and heavy-duty trucks will grow by 8 per cent to 2.6 million units in the period between 2010 and 2013. At Daimler Trucks, it looks like we will succeed in boosting sales by more than 40 per cent to over 500,000 units during the same period. Given this outlook, a return on sales of 8 per cent is within reach for Daimler Trucks across the entire business cycle,” explained Andreas Renschler, Board of Management member.

Daimler Trucks recently presented its concept for an electric truck at IAA Commercial Vehicles; the Fuso Canter E-CELL with battery-electric drive was presented in Hanover, Germany in September 2010. The nearly silent running and emission-free Canter E-CELL has a range of about 120 kilometres. It is based on a Fuso Canter 3S13 with a 3.5 ton gross vehicle weight – the batteries are positioned in the chassis frame. In addition to the new electric concept, the best-selling truck is also produced as a hybrid version and with a conventional diesel engine. Nearly 1,000 of the Fuso Canter Eco Hybrids are already on the road in Japan, Australia, Hong Kong, Ireland, and in a large scale testing programme in London.
