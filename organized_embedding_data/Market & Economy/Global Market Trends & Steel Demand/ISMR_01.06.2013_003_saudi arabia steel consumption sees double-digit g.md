# SAUDI ARABIA steel consumption sees double-digit growth

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

STEEL consumption in Saudi Arabia has been witnessing tremendous growth on the back of a rapidly-growing real estate and construction industry. These sectors have boosted the steel demand in the country, leading to double-digit growth in steel consumption.

However, the country imports almost half of its steel demand due to low steel production capacity and the consumption of steel is anticipated to gain uninterrupted momentum from the government, backed by massive infrastructure projects. With huge spending on the construction of real estate and railways, apparent steel consumption in Saudi Arabia was anticipated to grow at an impressive 19.5 per cent during 2012-2015.

According to KSA’s report, “KSA Steel Industry Forecast 2015”, Saudi Arabia has become one of the largest steel consumers in the GCC region due to increasing steel demand for infrastructure and construction projects. Rebar is among the most attractive steel products in the Saudi Arabia steel industry and accounts for the bulk of steel consumption due to a large number of construction projects. In addition, rising rebar demand has made the country a major rebar importer. Long products account for the major share in production as well as consumption in Saudi Arabia.

The UAE construction industry has also witnessed phenomenal growth over the past few years. A series of investments has resulted in infrastructure development to emerge as the third largest sector of the UAE economy after oil and trade. Now, the UAE infrastructure industry accounts for the highest investment in the Middle East region.
