# SteelFab will be held at Expo Centre Sharjah from 20-23 January 2014

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

## Market forecasts

“The buzz surrounding regional economic growth is having a ripple effect on the core sector that drives the steel fabrication and metal working sector. Construction projects worth billions of dollars are active in the region, with the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Qatar being lead markets,” explained the event organiser.

“The flourishing industrial and manufacturing sectors and the oil and gas industry are also fuelling the steel fabrication sector which will need to look at acquiring new machinery and technology to enhance production to meet rising requirements. Another sector driving demand is the region’s pipeline industry which is planning to invest billions of dollars to boost its capacities, as oil prices continue to remain bullish. Demand has also been consistent from several other sectors, including ship-building, food, construction and infrastructure.”

A study earlier this year forecasted that the regional steel industry in the Middle East is expected to continue growth in 2013, bolstered by increasing infrastructure spend, greater industrialisation and a higher demand for homes fuelled by a growing population and falling family sizes.

“The growth is facilitated by construction and infrastructure projects initiated by regional economies that have benefited from high oil prices over the past three years. The regional steel fabrication and metal working industry is a direct beneficiary of this growth which was amply demonstrated at the SteelFab 2013 exhibition through better than expected visitor turnout and sales leads,” said Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, Director-General, Expo Centre Sharjah.

There are about ten major steel mills in the region producing more than 3 mtpa. Around 25 medium scale mills producing between 1 and 3 mtpa and roughly more than 150 smaller size mills producing less than 1 mtpa.

“The Middle East is set to buck the global trend of a drop in steel consumption in 2012 and see robust growth in 2013 and beyond as against a projected marginal improvement in world steel demand. The long-term outlook for the steel industry in the MENA region, particularly in the GCC, has been termed as very promising due to ambitious economic development plans by the governments, with demand in MENA projected to reach a new height of 70 million metric tonnes by 2020. Middle East steel demand was 27.3 million MT in 2010, production was 20 million MT and the balance was met by imports,” continued the event organiser.

“Major projects planned in the railway, economic and industrial cities, petrochemicals, real estate etc. are set to drive the steel industry’s growth, according to a study. SteelFab has always been one of the best platforms for steel suppliers to effectively display and market their products to the region which has prompted the launch of a new section at SteelFab 2014.”

## New for 2014

New developments for SteelFab 2014 include a recently constructed additional hall adding 8000 sq.m. to the show. New segments have also been added to the exhibit profile of SteelFab 2014 to make it an all-inclusive “steel industry” show, such as:-

STEEL-TECH: Steel-making technology

FABRICATORS’ PAVILION: Showcase for fabricators

STEEL SHOWCASE: Steel manufacturers and traders.

Several new exhibitors have joined SteelFab 2014 including Amada; Bystronic; Eckert; ESP Machinery; Fagor Automation; Faspar; Galagar Welding; Hyundai Machine Tools; Kuhtreiber; OMSG; Promotech; Python-X; Salvagnini; Sarcoil; Techniweld and others.

The show size is set to increase by 50% to 24,000 sq. m. and exhibitors are expected to increase by about 25% to about 300, according to the organiser. Pavilions are confirmed for Germany and Taiwan, whilst Italy and Turkey have also confirmed participation.

“SteelFab has always enjoyed the participation of steel fabricators. SteelFab 2014 will feature greater participation from steel fabricators, steel contractors and manufacturers. Exhibitors in this segment can expect to meet visitors such as main contractors, construction companies, oil and gas industry, shipbuilding industry and heavy manufacturing Industries,” added the organiser.

“The core sector is flourishing in the region owing to high oil prices and a growing economy. Multi-billion dollar mega projects that are under way in the country and the region will ensure that the representatives from the core sectors such as construction, infrastructure, oil and gas, railways and heavy industries will be visiting the show.

“SteelFab has continuously showcased the largest welding and cutting segment to be found anywhere in the Middle East and North Africa and has been identified by the regional industry as the sourcing point for all welding requirements as well.”

With expanding manufacturing and production facilities, demand for machine tools is growing in the region. Sensing this requirement, SteelFab has introduced a special focus on CNC machine tools and metal-cutting machinery. SteelFab 2014 will have a special focus area for CNC machine tools and metalcutting machinery. It will feature lathes; milling; drilling; grinding; EDMs; work centres; tool room machinery; high-precision machine tools; machining centres and affiliated machine tools.

The 2014 exhibition will also feature a special area for machinery and tools used in pipe and tube fabrication as infrastructure, oil & gas and industrial projects are triggering demand for machinery and tools used in bending and cutting pipes and tubes. Machinery and tools for pipe & tube construction; pipe forming; pipe cutting; roll bending; tube forming; tube bending; orbital welding; pipe & tube straightening; clamps and accessories and other pipe & tube solutions will be on display.

SteelFab 2014 will also continue its focus on material handling in steel fabrication. Manufacturing in the Middle East region has largely been labour-intensive; however, developments in the past few years indicate a gradual shift to automation in process, with material handling being at the forefront of this change. The “Material Handling” special focus at SteelFab 2014 aims to provide the tools to accelerate this change and will feature a full range of equipment from overhead cranes; conveyor systems; rotators; positioners; column & boom; robots; magnetic lifting; vacuum lifting etc.

## Knowledge exchange

SteelFab will also be a knowledge-sharing platform through the technical seminars it runs alongside the exhibition. These informative short sessions will consist of a technical presentation followed by questions and discussions on technological innovations and the latest product offerings for the industry. The seminars at SteelFab 2014 will cover two days on 21st and 22nd January.

A two-day conference will also be held on various topics including technology; steel mills; traders; steel fabrication; material handling; welding and cutting. A separate conference on steel-making technology and steel mills will also be held to supplement “STEEL-TECH” at SteelFab 2014.

Strategically located near the business centres of Sharjah and Dubai, Expo Centre Sharjah has a full spectrum of exhibition facilities and services.
