# Metal can market forecast

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO ResearchAndMarkets.com, the global food and beverage metal cans market is estimated to be worth US$ 27.6 billion in 2020 and is projected to reach US$ 37.0 billion by 2025, recording a CAGR of 6.1%. Factors such as the growing demand for ready-to-eat snacks and canned food and beverages across the globe are associated with the growth of the metal cans market in this industry.

“The aluminium segment accounted for a major share in the global food and beverage metal cans market in 2018. Aluminium is majorly used as a raw material in the manufacturing of metal cans. It can customise the packaging with colours, 3D prints and embossing. Its properties (including its light weight, impermeability and high flexibility) recommend it as a material for packaging,” added the analyst.

The food & beverage metal cans market is segmented into pressurised cans and vacuum cans. Pressurised cans are estimated to account for the larger share because of their high usage in the beverage industry. They are light in weight and reduce the cost of logistics, therefore driving the market for beverage cans. These cans are also suitable for the packaging of carbonated and non-carbonated beverages such as beer, wine, sports drinks and fruit juices.

“North America accounted for the largest share in the food and beverage metal cans market, due to higher acceptability and growing awareness of environmental sustainability issues. North America is among the largest consumers of aluminium cans and is mainly dominated by a large number of consumers for packaged food and beverages in the world. Strong investment in R&D and technological advancements are expected to have a positive impact on the domestic production of food and beverage metal cans in North America,” added the analyst.
