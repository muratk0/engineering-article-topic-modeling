# KOREA STANDS TALL

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Over 500 million tons of steel, which is enough to produce 400 million mid-size sedans, is in reserve for the local steel industry, Korea Iron & Steel Association said in a recent report.

According to the report, the industry had 522.21 million tons of steel in reserve at the end of 2009, a 3.6 per cent increase from a year earlier.

The figure increased by 18.2 million tons from 504.1 million tons in 2008. The amount of steel accumulated in the country surpassed 200 million tons in 1995, 300 million tons in 2000, 400 million tons in 2004 and 500 million tons in 2008.

The amount of accumulated steel is an important barometer to predict changes in scrap demand – short term and long term – and the level of future self-sufficiency in scrap, as steel products, once re-collected in the form of steel scrap, are used to produce steel.

The country’s level of self-sufficiency was 71.2 per cent in 2009. The deficit has been imported from Japan and the United States, among others. In 2007, the Korea Institute for Industrial Economics & Trade predicted the point of self-sufficiency for the local steel industry to come in 2022. According to the KISA, however, the timing could be somewhat delayed, as the hike in demand for high quality steel scrap leads the industry to depend more on imports.
