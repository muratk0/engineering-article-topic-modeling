# Global arc-welding equipment market

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

According to new market analysis on Research and Markets.com, the global market for arc-welding equipment (estimated at US$ 4.3 billion in 2020) is projected to reach a revised size of US$ 6 billion by 2027 growing at a CAGR of 4.9% over the analysis period 2020-2027.

'Energy & Utilities', one of the segments analysed in the report, is projected to record a 5.9% CAGR and reach US$1.8 billion by the end of the analysis period. Growth in the automotive segment is readjusted to a revised 5% CAGR for the next seven-year period.

The arc-welding equipment market in the U.S. is estimated at US$ 1.2 billion in the year 2020. China, the world's second largest economy, is forecast to reach a projected market size of US$ 1.2 billion by the year 2027, trailing a CAGR of 7.6% over the analysis period 2020 to 2027. Among other noteworthy geographic markets are Japan and Canada, each forecast to grow at 2.6% and 4.4% respectively over the 2020-2027 period. Within Europe, Germany is forecast to grow at approximately 3% CAGR. Led by countries such as Australia, India and South Korea, the market in Asia-Pacific is forecast to reach US$ 801.4 million by the year 2027.

"In the global construction segment, the USA, Canada, Japan, China and Europe will drive the 4.1% CAGR estimated for this segment. These regional markets, accounting for a combined market size of US$ 580.8 million in the year 2020, will reach a projected size of US$ 750.1 million by the end of the analysis period. China will remain among the fastest growing in this cluster of regional markets. Latin America will expand at a 5.1% CAGR through the analysis period," concluded the report.
