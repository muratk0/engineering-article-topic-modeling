# Positive trend for Italian machine tool market

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

2015 CLOSED POSITIVELY for the Italian machine tool industry, which registered doubledigit increases for almost all main economic indicators according to UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tool and robotic systems’ association). Its expectations for 2016 are also optimistic, as the favourable trend is expected to continue.

On the basis of preliminary figures processed by the Studies Dept. of UCIMU SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, production grew to 5043 million euros in 2015, registering a 12.2% rise against the previous year. This outcome was due to the positive trend of exports and deliveries in the Italian market. Strong growth was also reported in Italian machine tool consumption, reaching 3595 million euros, marking a 31.3% increase compared to the previous year. This reawakening in the demand for production systems was evident in manufacturer deliveries, which increased to 1920 million euros (i.e. 21% more in comparison to the previous year) and imports which increased by 45.5%, to 1675 million.

“After the setback suffered last year, exports in 2015 started to grow again strongly, recording a 7.9% upturn at 3510 million euros,” explained the association.

According to UCIMU data, in the first eight months of the year, the main customer countries for Italian machinery were the United States at 248 million (+10.8%); China at 232 million (+0.5%); Germany at 227 million (+7.1%); Russia at 133 million (+38.1%); France at 114 million (+23.5%); Poland at 77 million (+21.3%); the United Kingdom at 77 million (+88.2%); Turkey at 63 million (-15%); Spain at 61 million (+12.1%) and India at 57 million (-0.3%).

Production is expected to reach 5820 million euros (+7.2%) in 2016, approaching the "record" value of 6 billion euros registered in 2008. Exports are expected to grow by 6.6%, to 3740 million. Imports are expected to reach 1830 million (+9.3%).
