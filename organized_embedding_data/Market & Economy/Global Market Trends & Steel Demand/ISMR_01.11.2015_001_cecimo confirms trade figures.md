# CECIMO confirms trade figures

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

IN THE SECOND quarter of 2015, CECIMO exports increased 1% to 4.5 billion euros, supported by improving economic growth in developed countries. Exports to Europe and the Americas recorded a solid growth of 15% and 16% respectively. CECIMO is the European Association of Machine Tool Industries.

“Europe is the most important market for European machine tool builders and, because business confidence is gradually recovering, this importance is increasing even further. The share of machine tools shipped to Europe grew from 45% to 51% while machine tool exports to Asia and the CIS declined 19% and 37%. The most important single export markets for CECIMO remain the same: China, the United States and Russia,” the association explained.

The Asian region is strongly affected by the economic transition in China: CECIMO’s exports to China decreased 27% to 709 million euros in the second quarter of 2015. European machine tool builders exported 19% more machines (a value of 467 million euros) to the United States. Despite some recent appreciation, the euro’s exchange rate remains at low levels.

Firm European consumption drove CECIMO imports to increase by 14% to 2.4 billion euros in the second quarter of 2015. CECIMO imports originate from Europe or Asia with a proportion of 93%. CECIMO imports from Europe reached 1.6 billion euros, gaining 13%.

Imports from Asia rose 16% to 0.7 billion euros. The main suppliers to European industry outside the EU are Japan, Taiwan and China.

CECIMO recently launched the 3DPrism initiative, funded by the EU, to improve the quality of VET (Vocational and Educational Training) by linking training provision with the needs of industry. The initiative aims at tackling this challenge by enhancing VET provision to equip technicians and operators with sought-after skills, with outputs that emphasise the applied aspects of 3D printing.
