# TRUMPF sales exceed 2.5 billion

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

IN THE PAST financial year ending 30 June 2014, the TRUMPF Group achieved sales of 2.584 billion euros. In relation to the previous year’s sales of 2.343 billion euros, this is equivalent to an increase of 10.3 per cent (preliminary figures). This is the highest ever annual sales figure in the over 90-year history of the Ditzingen-based, machine-tool and laser manufacturer.

In the past fiscal year, TRUMPF made a series of acquisitions including the purchase of Chinese machine tool manufacturer, JFY. Even after adjustment for these effects, TRUMPF still achieved record sales of 2.507 billion euros, reflecting an increase of 7 per cent.

"We’ve seen very strong growth, especially in Asia and Western Europe," said TRUMPF President, Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller. "Both regions were even weaker during the previous year, and that makes us all the happier that we’ve been especially successful there with our tailor-made products."

The order intake was higher, too. After last year’s figure of 2.33 billion, it rose by 15.9 per cent to 2.7 billion euros. TRUMPF anticipates a considerable increase in earnings as well. In 2012/13, the company achieved pre-tax earnings of 154 million euros.

"We’re expecting a result far in excess of the previous year’s figure," said Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller. ■
