## HVAC equipment market

The HVAC equipment market is fragmented with the presence of major players such as Trane Technologies PLC, Daikin Industries Ltd and Comfort SRL. Some players in the market are adopting strategies, such as partnerships and acquisitions, to enhance their product offerings and gain sustainable competitive advantage.

According to analyst, Mordor Intelligence, the size of the HVAC equipment market is estimated at US$ 141.36 billion in 2023. It is expected to reach US$ 195.96 billion by 2028, growing at a CAGR of 6.75% during the forecast period (2023-2028).

"The rapid rise in industrialisation and urbanisation worldwide is one of the primary factors driving the market's growth. The significant increase in the construction of different commercial and residential buildings worldwide is creating considerable demand for HVAC equipment," commented Mordor Intelligence.

Rising energy costs have also spurred demand for more energy-efficient HVAC systems. The real-estate market and regulatory environment are evolving, with sustainability making a considerable impact on the value, operating costs and marketability of properties. Governments worldwide have implemented stringent regulations and standards to encourage the installation of energy-efficient systems, as well as for reducing greenhouse gas emissions. Companies must therefore develop products that meet these guidelines to remain compliant. Buildings that are environmentally friendly and meet requirements for health, safety and comfort are becoming increasingly popular.

"Heating, ventilating and air-conditioning in a commercial building usually consume more energy than any other activity in the building. According to the U.S. Department of Energy's studies of commercial buildings, HVAC equipment usually accounts for over 40% of a building's energy usage. Owing to the huge amount of energy used by HVAC systems, improvements in equipment efficiency translate to significant reductions in building operating costs," explained Mordor Intelligence.

## Regional market growth

Mordor Intelligence predicts that the Asia-Pacific HVAC systems market will rise steadily due to commercial and residential construction activity in India and China as well as rising consumer expenditure on luxury products. Low ownership rates and increased disposable income in Asia are likely to boost the market's growth.

“Due to rising demand from India and China, the residential sector accounted for a significant portion of the Asia-Pacific air conditioning systems' market. Increased competition from Chinese producers offering products at lower prices than globally recognized names is projected to sustain sales growth. A large installed base in established regions, such as Western Europe and the United States, is expected to create development prospects in the comparatively untapped Asia-Pacific market," outlined the analyst.

"In the Asia-Pacific market, the energy and power sectors will be essential end users of heating equipment. Rising power plant capacity will be a primary driver of market expansion. According to the International Energy Agency's Global Energy Investment Forecast, between 2014 and 2035, over US$ 9.5 trillion will be spent on constructing new power plants and rehabilitating existing ones worldwide. Over one-third of this will probably be used for fossil-fuel-based thermal power plants. Increasing generation capacity is planned to be achieved primarily through fossil-fuelled thermal power plants, which are the primary source of electricity generation in these countries," it continued.

Supportive government incentives in some countries present consumers with a compelling opportunity to replace any ageing, lower-efficiency HVAC systems with advanced, high-efficiency technology at a fraction of the cost.

"At a global level, the HVAC industry is thriving in four different markets; the U.S.; Europe; UAE and APAC," confirmed Mordor Intelligence.

The U.S. market is poised for growth, it believes, as a result of requirements to improve Indoor Air Quality (IAQ). The ventilation fans category, it added, will see increased interest especially for maintenance services due to the need for fresh air in hotels, shopping malls and airports. There is also a focus on building 'green' buildings, leading to higher demand for both HVAC systems and maintenance services.

"Europe's HVAC industry is growing due to rapid urbanisation; an increase in disposable income; government regulations and increased spending in sustainable construction. Europe stands out for its push to reduce CO₂ emissions. This has led economies like Germany and Finland to promote the use of new technologies in the construction and HVAC industries," explained the analyst.

Increased construction in commercial and residential sectors in the UAE market, as well as the region's desert climate, has led to increased demand for HVAC solutions. Consumer trends such as automatic temperature regulation; monitoring of humidity levels; alerts for servicing and system access through mobile devices are creating more avenues for businesses to increase their revenues.

"As multinationals set up in the APAC region, they're driving up the demand for real estate space. With a construction boom in China, Japan and India, and an increase in average temperatures in the region, the demand for AC systems is set to go up. As India boosts its manufacturing capacity, local players are ramping up production even as the country experiences an inflow of FDI," added the analyst.

## Trends and challenges

With all the opportunities available for HVAC businesses, some challenges could stand in the way of growth. Mordor Intelligence lists several key challenges overleaf.

1. Seasonality. Business in the HVAC industry is seasonal. While summer comes with high demand for AC units, winter drives the sale of heating solutions. Providers of maintenance services experience similar fluctuations since the number of customer calls varies with seasons. This is making business growth a significant challenge, especially for smaller players.

2. Increased competition. The industry's growth is attracting new players and the competition is becoming stiff. Service providers are the worst affected since most of them rely on brand loyalty for growth. Whereas some businesses are getting stuck with low prices, others are differentiating themselves by providing exceptional customer experience.

3. Skills shortage. Baby boomers are retiring and creating job opportunities. The younger workforce is, however, reluctant to fill those positions resulting in questions about the industry's future. The talent crunch, said Mordor Intelligence, has also led to some players settling for improvements to current systems rather than disruptive solutions.

However, the HVAC industry is also witnessing change. The market has seen increasing levels of mergers and acquisitions.

"Lower-Middle-Market (LMM) companies are struggling and more profitable ones are buying them out to expand their customer base and increase market share. Some big players are also joining forces to establish more significant dominance in the market and to increase profitability," explained Mordor Intelligence.

"Smart HVAC systems are also coming to the fore as convenience

and the cost of energy drive the uptake of smart systems. The top smart solutions being incorporated into HVAC systems include learning thermostats; voice-controlled systems; motion-activated ACs; remote temperature adjustment; sensors to monitor system performance; alerts for systems maintenance; system access/control through mobile apps and automatic submission of work orders," it continued.

To solve problems with poor IAQ, manufacturers are also fitting UV Germicidal light into their systems. While some HVAC manufacturers are providing this as an additional service, there are also DIY solutions available from UV-C light bulb manufacturers.

The industry is also using customer experience as a key differentiator. HVAC consumers now have high expectations, and these influence their purchase decisions. Businesses are adapting by embracing CRM systems and analytics to understand and cater to consumer needs. Businesses are also increasingly undertaking market research to gain insight into consumer preferences and make better business decisions.
