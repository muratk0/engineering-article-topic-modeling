# REGIONAL REPORT

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

Due to its central location on the European continent, Poland has very favourable road, rail and aviation links with all the EU countries and is well placed to trade with the EU neighbours in the East. Poland’s three main sea ports, to the North of the country (on the southern shore of the Baltic Sea): Szczecin-Świnoujście, Gdynia and Gdańsk are connected to the world’s most important ports. All this makes Poland a strong location for firms that are active in Central and Eastern European markets.

Poland’s automotive sector is largely oriented towards exports, with key players selling into Western Europe (e.g. Fiat, Ford, VW and Opel), using Poland as a low-cost base for manufacturing. The presence of many major parts makers and suppliers (e.g. TRW, Faurecia, Delphi, Goodyear, Michelin and Valeo) has resulted in the formation of a highly integrated segment of suppliers. Poland is also an important bus producer and this sector is also driven mainly by export demand.

The aviation industry in Poland is highly concentrated; almost 90 per cent of the total production is concentrated in the south-eastern part of Poland, in the region of the Aviation Valley Cluster (Dolina Lotnicza). The cluster is not only a concentration of production plants but also consists of numerous scientific research centres, as well as educational and training facilities.

Special economic zones were created in Poland to accelerate the economic development of regions, manage post-industrial property and infrastructure, create new jobs and attract foreign investors to Poland. These offer major facilitations for investors (exemptions from income tax, property tax, R&D and training grants).

The ITM Poland (Innovations Technologies Machine Tool exhibition) in Poznań, Poland (9-12 June 2015), which includes the Mach-Tool exhibition, is a significant industrial event in the European machine tool calendar and the only such large-scale industrial event in Poland. It brings together all the sectors associated with the metal industry.

“In 2014, hundreds of companies from 30 countries presented their products in Poznań and the exhibition was visited by 16,728 guests from Poland and overseas. This year’s event promises to be even more interesting, so it is worth finding out why you should visit Poznań in June this year,” the show organiser, MTP, told ISMR.

As part of this year’s exhibition, at the MTP Poznań (Poznań International Fair), there will be nine halls and seven theme exhibitions (Mach-Tool, Metalforum, Surfex, Welding, Hape, Science for the Economy, Industrial Health and Safety). Visitors will have the opportunity to interact with industry players in the machine tool; automotive; welding; varnishing; munitions; energy and railway sectors. In the halls at Poznań, machinery and equipment for metalworking, welding or paint coatings; hydraulics; pneumatics and metallurgy will all be showcased. Industrial robots and manipulators will be also displayed. ■
