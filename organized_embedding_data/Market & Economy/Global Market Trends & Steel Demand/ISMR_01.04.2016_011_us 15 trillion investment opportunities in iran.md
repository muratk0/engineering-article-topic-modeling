# US$ 1.5 trillion investment opportunities in Iran

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

Several initiatives are being ushered in to achieve Iran’s 2025 strategic growth objective and chief among them is to discourage the dependency on oil and focus on enhancing manufacturing in non-oil sectors.

According to industry analyst, Frost and Sullivan, the Iranian Government is planning on progressively increasing its net investment in manufacturing to US$ 25.5 billion by early 2018, US$ 35 billion by 2020 and US$ 62 billion by 2025. It is keen on attracting foreign investment through BOO, BOT and/or PPP modes primarily in sectors of infrastructure, downstream segments of oil and gas, mining and petrochemical projects and is encouraging joint ventures with local partners in these sectors.

The 2025 strategic development plan identifies core industries that the Iranian Government shall focus on for development. These are Petrochemical Products; Mining and Minerals along the industry value chain including Plastics, Metals and Minerals; Food and Beverages; Pharmaceuticals; Industrial Machinery and Equipment; Home Appliances; Textiles and Apparel; Ground Vehicle; Rail and Maritime; Rubber and Power Generation and Transmission.

## Innovation spells success for Radan BV

A Dutch subsidiary of the international sheet metal software company, Radan, has been presented with a business success award for its innovative approach and technology trend-setting. Radan BV, one of Radan’s longstanding subsidiaries, has been named the 2016 industry winner in the CAD/CAM solution business section by the National Business Success Institute in the Netherlands.

Robert Zwaan, head of the Nomination Committee, said: “Radan BV is generally seen as one of the emerging organisations in the field of CAD/CAM solutions. They combine their vast expertise with excellent accessibility and an innovative approach, which gives them a very high score on customer satisfaction.”

Formed in 1976, Radan provides automation solutions for sheet metal fabricators. Radan CAD/CAM software includes web portals for quotation calculation as a cloud-based service, and a fully automated 2D/3D CAM system to control cutting and punching machines as well as press brakes. This year, Radan is celebrating its 40th anniversary. See www.radan.nl
