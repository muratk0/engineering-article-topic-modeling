# Mexico’s Auto Industry Success Highlighted at 5th Annual The FABRICATOR® en Español Leadership Summit

[TARİH: 01.07.2014 The Fabricator]

Monterrey, Mexico

Leading executives in the metal processing, fabricating, and stamping industry will convene Sept. 2-4 at the Camino Real Monterrey for

The FABRICATOR en Español

Leadership Summit to discuss the unique advantages and challenges of manufacturing in Mexico.

Market analyst Bernard Swiecki from the Center for Automotive Research will present findings on Mexico’s emergence as the new powerhouse in automotive exports.

Attendees will learn why automakers and other international manufacturers are expanding production throughout the country, and interactive discussions will explore the significance of foreign investment. Presentations will focus on issues such as tax reform, the steel market, and technological advancements in metals manufacturing. World-class speakers will present growth opportunities for Mexican manufacturers and share strategies for competing on a global stage.

Conference participants will meet and connect with key players in the Mexican metals industry at exciting optional events, including three networking receptions, a plant tour of John Deere Mexico, dinner and tour at the Horno

3

steel museum in Parque Fundidora, and a golf outing at the exclusive La Herradura Club de Golf. (Additional fees may apply for the optional events.)

In addition to the high-level discourse at the Leadership Summit, attendees will enjoy the technical education portion of the event held on Tuesday, Sept. 2, from 12-5 p.m. The preconference educational sessions will address a range of coil processing, fabricating, stamping, and welding topics. The preconference technical program will be available at no charge for registered Summit attendees or as a one-day-only registration for $ 295 USD.

Leadership Summit registration fees are $695 USD or $495 USD for FMA/TPA members. More details on the programs, registration, and sponsorship and exhibit opportunitirs can be found at fmanet.org/flsmexico or by calling 888-394-4362.
