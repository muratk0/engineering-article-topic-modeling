## Unmanned aerial systems

Israeli original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) of unmanned aerial systems (UAS) have aggressively marketed these platforms, helping them to become the top exporter of UAS platforms globally.

A recent market insight published by Frost & Sullivan, on Israeli unmanned aerial systems, finds that Israel's UAS export revenue totalled US$ 4.62 billion from 2005 to 2012. The top purchaser of these exports is the UK, although Israeli OEMs also have strong UAS trading relationships with Indian and Brazilian markets.

“Israeli defence firms rely on exports for the majority of their revenue,” explained Frost & Sullivan aerospace and defence senior industry analyst, Michael Blades. “UAS exports have accounted for nearly 10 per cent of the country's total defence export industry, and this is expected to increase.”

As worldwide demand for UASs increases, growth in Israeli UAS export and licensing agreement values are expected to grow since they are not subjected to the same “red tape” process that currently hinders the US State Department's Foreign Military Sales (FMS) processes.

“Total defence exports will probably increase steadily, as Israeli companies form strategic partnerships and continue aggressive marketing campaigns with countries in growing UAS markets such as Africa, APAC and South America,” added Blades.

Eran Flumin, Frost & Sullivan director of Israel Operations and Consulting, added that the success of the UAS industry in Israel, like other defence industries, is driven by innovation, early adoption and entrepreneurship. ■
