# An eye on the future

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

In September, Yutong produced about 2,000 new energy buses. From January to September this year, the company rolled out over 10,000 eco-friendly buses. The company estimates that its overall production volume of new energy buses will reach 13,600 units over the whole year. Yutong boasts a wide spectrum of new energy buses, ranging from 10-18m plug-in hybrid buses to 6-12m full electric buses.

In China's plug-in hybrid bus market, Yutong now holds 40% of the plug-in hybrid bus market while, in China's fully electric bus market, the brand's market share has exceeded 20%. Along with the technological advances in vehicle batteries and vehicle weight reductions, the majority of Yutong fully electric buses are capable of driving over 200km continuously.

“The current operating mode of transport is still largely based on fossil fuels, and is no longer sustainable in the short term. Climate change, depletion and exhaustion of fossil energy and public health issues - all these need to find new solutions very soon to ensure that future travel patterns are more 'soft' and respect our environment. Energy transition is the key to our common future - it is now a global issue that goes beyond the specific interests of companies, nations and states. This is simply an issue of survival of humans as we know it today," commented Yutong.

In August this year, Yutong sold 8,032 units plug-in hybrid buses. So far, the company has delivered 16,535 units energy-saving and eco-friendly buses in 116 cities across China, accounting for 26.76% of the new energy bus market in China. With its market share in the plug-in hybrid bus market and full electric bus market ranking as first, Yutong now boasts a solid No.1 position in China's bus industry.

As the Chinese saying goes, “Sharpen a sword for ten years". Through a long-term strategic arrangement and cooperation with powerful European partners, a number of Yutong buses have passed WVTA certification which covers city buses, tourism coaches etc. Yutong buses have been exported to 17 European countries and surrounding countries such as France, UK, Russia, Slovakia, Norway, Iceland and Israel. The sales volume, Yutong confirmed, is increasing year by year.
