## Market trends and movements

As a European solutions provider, RSA has been affected by the ongoing uncertainties around the euro in financial markets.

“We are based in Europe so we are, of course, aware of the ongoing economic issues around the euro,” said Berg. “This affects market confidence, especially when it comes to large machine tool investments. So, how have we reacted to that? As well as consolidating our base in Europe, we are looking further afield to regions such as South America, Asia, Russia etc. and focusing on a broader platform to develop new relationships and a presence in these markets so that we are not just focused on the European market.

“We have a broad customer base in Europe – in fact, many of the tube cutting companies in Europe are our customers. However, South America, Asia and Russia have experienced explosive growth rates, mainly driven by the automotive industry and domestic demand. In China, there is a growing demand for high quality production equipment (for industries such as automotive) to meet the quality standards needed by European markets. Our challenge and opportunity is to provide high quality equipment to automotive OEM suppliers in these markets (e.g. deburring systems, sawing centres, industrial brushes etc.) acting, in effect, as a systems provider. We can also offer very strong after-sales service to our customers.”

With this in mind, RSA has already set up new sales networks in regions such as China, northern Europe and South America.

RSA was one of the first manufacturers to provide professional deburring systems for industrial use. Aluminium extrusions, for example, require a special deburring technique which it provides with its RASAMAT machine. In the sheet metal sector, mass production with large machines has been more and more influenced over the last few years by waterjet and laser cutting which ensure high quality cutting levels but have, of course, had an impact on subsequent deburring operations.

As Berg explained, “We are now increasingly looking at mid-sized and smaller batch manufacturers who do not need large machines but require more customised, automated systems. We will demonstrate our machines on the customers’ premises, bring our mobile demonstrations service to their manufacturing sites and even provide test production services with sample parts at our technical centre in Schwerte for them.

"Right now we feel that companies are investing to secure further competitive advantages for the future. We will continue to expand and develop our existing product portfolio in all areas. RSA has always been a technology leader in the sawing and deburring technology sectors. This will be the same in the future. We want to offer our customers more advantages and options for developing even more markets. We have more than enough ideas." ■
