# LAMIERA in focus

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association) and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU, the LAMIERA sheetmetal trade show will take place in Milan, Italy, at fieramilano Rho from 10-13 May 2023.

"In the first six months of 2022, after an extremely positive 2021, Italian industry in this sector confirmed growth trends, registering double-digit increases in global trade data [for] sheet-metal forming and cutting machines," commented UCIMU.

"From January-June 2022, its global exports of sheet-metal forming and cutting machine tools (laser, waterjet, plasma etc.) registered a 12% upturn compared to the same period in 2021 (totalling more than eight billion euros). Worldwide imports also grew by 16%, exceeding six billion euros," it added.

Italian government tax incentives on capital equipment purchases (including Industry 4.0 equipment) will also continue next year for Italian manufacturers.

The exhibition will showcase, amongst others, machines/systems and equipment for sheet-metal processing and cutting; tubes; sections; presses; dies and moulds; welding; surface treatment and finishing; components; tooling and fastener materials.

Six technological themes at the show, with an increasingly crucial impact on the sector, will be:

■ Factory of the Future, dedicated to software and technologies for connectivity; data management; automation; robotics and digital systems applied to sheet-metal processing.

■ Saldatech, offering an overview of the wide and diversified world of welding.

■ Fastener Industry, presenting an overview of screws, bolts, fastening and clamping systems.

■ World of Heat and Surface Treatments, hosting machines, systems, accessories and consumables centred on pre- and post-production treatments for ferrous and non-ferrous materials.

■ Metrology & Testing, focused on testing, measurement and quality-control systems and machines.

■ BLECH Italy Service for technical subcontracting and services.

LAMIERA will also include a full programme of in-depth cultural, themed meetings arranged by the organisers and exhibitors. The meetings will be hosted at LAMIALAMIERA, an arena that will be set up in one of the exhibition halls.

The trade show will take place alongside 'Made in Steel', the exhibition dedicated to the production chain of steel, which will be held in the halls next to those occupied by LAMIERA.
