# SUCCESS IN SHEET METAL

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

Dr.Ing. Mathias Kammüller, Executive Vice President of TRUMPF GmbH + Co. KG and Head of TRUMPF's Machine Tool division, speaks to ISMR

ISMR SAYS: “TRUMPF is storming ahead with new innovations at EuroBLECH in Hanover against the backdrop of its highest ever sales figures achieved”

It has been a successful year for TRUMPF, with 15 per cent increases in orders received and a host of new sheet metal innovations on display at its booth at EuroBLECH. ISMR caught up with Dr.Ing. Mathias Kammüller, Executive Vice President of TRUMPF GmbH + Co. KG and Head of TRUMP’s Machine Tool division, to gauge his view of the future.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements with the reasons for this?

MK: The 2011/2012 business year was very successful for TRUMPF. We managed to achieve the highest sales figures of 2.328 billion Euros in our history. In relation to the previous year’s sales of 2.024 billion Euros, this corresponds to an increase of 15 per cent.

We also strengthened our position in all important markets. Growth in the US was especially dynamic. We added new production locations in Asia, to be more precise in Singapore and Yokohama. We also expanded existing ones like, for example, our production site in Taicang, China, where we doubled our production area. In Europe, we are also investing in our future growth. Current expansion projects relate to several locations in Germany, in Switzerland, the Netherlands and France.

On a completely different note: at the end of August 2012 our plant in Grüsch, Switzerland, produced its 10,000th TruLaser machine. Worldwide, we have already produced more than 15,000 2D laser machines. These numbers prove that we produce good quality machines. Otherwise, our customers would not place their trust in us over and over again.

Besides this, we successfully introduced several new products to the market and thereby strengthened our position as a market and innovation leader.

FACE TO FACE  
ISMR: What are your views on the current business climate? What do you foresee for the end of the year?

MK: The economy seems to be in a consolidation phase which is not surprising considering the very strong growth rates of the past few years. We are expecting stabilisation at a high level.

However, the financial situation in many markets and the current Euro crisis make future predictions somewhat uncertain.

ISMR: In the 2011/2012 fiscal year, the TRUMPF Group generated sales of 2.325 billion Euros, an increase of 15 per cent over the previous year. Can you outline which factors, in your opinion, account for this growth?

MK: All of our business divisions contributed to this success as well as all of the regions in which TRUMPF is active.

We have a very comprehensive product portfolio, especially in our big business fields - machine tools and laser technology – and the products complement each other very well.

I’d like to give you just one example of this: we have continued to equip our 2D laser machines with solid-state lasers, in addition to our proven CO2 laser models. We want to give our customers the opportunity to choose the right machine for their range of applications.

As we have all the different laser types in the portfolio of our laser technology business field, we can offer our customers a true variety. We are working very hard to provide the solutions our customers need. And I think this business year was proof of the success of this strategy.

## Which market regions do you for growth for your products and why?

MK: TRUMPF is present all over the world, and there are interesting markets in each continent. Sheet metal is used and needed everywhere - in highly developed markets as well as in emerging markets where we see large investments in the infrastructure development.

European as well as North American markets remain important to us and the US especially spurs high growth rates. But the BRIC states and other emerging markets are growing dynamically. We want to participate in this growth, although these markets each have distinct properties and have to be treated very differently. TRUMPF has to be and is active in these markets as well.

China has quickly grown to be TRUMPF’s third biggest market, after Germany and the USA. And markets such as India or Brazil grow in significance. Brazil, for instance, shows great promise. With the upcoming Olympics and the soccer world championships, this is certainly a market to watch. »

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus/vision over the next few years? Are there any aims, objectives or initiatives that you can discuss?

MK: In the future, the importance of software in production processes will grow. Therefore, we pay attention to new trends and developments in that area and analyse which might be useful for our machines.

I envision networked production in which software solutions regulate and coordinate the machines. Some developments already lead in that direction. For instance, in the LaserNetwork where several machine share the same laser source, all regulated by software. TruTops Fab software also controls processing sequences. We will see where future developments lead us.

At the same time, automation will grow more and more important. To create truly networked production, we not only need intelligent software but also intelligent automation solutions to link the individual machines in the production chain.

## markets and where do you see the greatest opportunities for your products?

MK: The expectations and needs of our customers grow more and more diverse – this refers not only to the different segments in which our customers are active, but also the different regions or market types. Therefore, we need to be able to provide solutions for all requirements. A wide product portfolio is necessary as well as individual automation solutions.

TRUMPF offers a wide range of solutions and keeps on developing more. We are, therefore, well prepared for future demands.

## Please give us an overview of benefits and capabilities? Are there any new product launches for EuroBLECH that you can discuss?

MK: Over the past year, we introduced several new machines to the market. For instance, the TruLaser 1030 fiber which is a solid state 2D laser cutting machine well suited for companies with little experience in laser cutting. Our new laser tube cutting machine, TruLaser Tube 7000, opens up entirely new manufacturing options with its pivoting laser cutting head. It can produce bevel cuts slanted by up to 45 degrees. For press brakes, we have new automated solutions such as the TruBend Cell 5000 and 7000 that make bending more productive than ever.

Of course, we will also show new products at EuroBLECH such as the SheetMaster Compact, a new place-saving automation solution for the TruPunch 3000. We are also offering new laser power capability for our TruLaser Series 5000. With a TruFlow 8000 and its 8-kilowatt laser power, these machines cut stainless steel up to 50mm thick. There will be another special innovation as well so you should come and visit us at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hall 11, Booth B30 / B61.

Among several world premieres, TRUMPF will show a wide range of machines and automation at EuroBLECH 2012 in Hannover. We will show how the TruTops Fab software controls the processing sequence and visitors can explore our complete TruServices consulting range. Hence it is worth a visit and we are looking forward to meeting new and old customers.

ISMR: Who are your customers? Can you segment them by activity or sector?

MK: Basically all companies in industries that use sheet metal are our potential customers. These industries could be appliances, construction, the automotive industry or furniture, to take just a few as an example. Therefore, our customers are very diverse. They range from small job shops with one machine and a few employees to large companies with their own brands and products.

Generally, we can segment our customers into two categories although there are companies that fit both. The first category, job shops, have to face quite unique challenges - they have to be very flexible since the jobs they receive can change every day. The second category is those companies that have their own products.
