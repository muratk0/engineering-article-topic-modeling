# Growth for global steel tube market

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

ACCORDING TO THE LATEST MARKET STUDY released by Technavio, the global steel tube market is expected to grow at a CAGR of more than 6% from 2016-2020.

Increase in demand for galvanised pipes and tubes is one of the main drivers for market growth. By volume, the global galvanised steel market was 326.2 million metric tons in 2015 and Technavio analysts expect the market to reach 420 million metric tons by 2020, growing at a CAGR of 5.18%. Galvanised steel is used for construction purposes including door; sash; wall; shutter; fence; beam; column; partition; under-ceiling; car port; veranda and store-room. The infrastructure and construction sector accounts for 38%-42% of the global galvanised steel demand.

“North America will spend about US$ 22 billion on the construction of pipelines by 2022. Mexico is planning to invest about US$ 8 billion in a natural gas pipeline system. Steel has high strength to weight ratio, thus it can easily be handled, installed and transported with less damage," said Chandrakumar Badala Jaganathan, a lead analyst at Technavio, specialising in research on metals and minerals. Oil and gas markets are key sectors for steel tube.

By volume, the global steel tube market for water or sewage was 24.25 million metric tons and is likely to reach 35 million metric tons by 2020, growing at a CAGR of 7.61%. Steel pipes are considered durable and strong for water supply. As a result, they are widely used for municipal and industrial water and sewage applications. They can be easily welded and can withstand water pressure. The global water market was US$ 365.22 billion in 2015 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 6% over the forecast period, triggering the demand for steel tubes.

By volume, the global steel tube market for infrastructure and construction was 14 million metric tons and is likely to reach 20 million metric tons by 2020, growing at a CAGR of 7.39%.■
