# FACE TO FACE

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

BLECH Russia and BLECH India are successful national sheet metal engineering events. They work extremely well for the domestic markets of Russia and India, which are large, buoyant national markets in their own right, and can support the vertical niche of sheet metal engineering.

However, EuroBLECH, and now AsiaBLECH, occupy a different position and function.

In recent years, China's economy around the Yangtze has become the engine of China's flourishing economy, with Suzhou at its heart

ISMR: Why is the timing right in China now?

Dan Wu: Nowadays, China has become a much more sophisticated engineering market domestically and, at the same time, it has also gained a regional and global reach beyond the borders of China. The parallel between EuroBLECH and AsiaBLECH is therefore very strong.

Importantly, AsiaBLECH will retain the same focus of technology hand-in-hand with that broader geographical reach. It will not be another broad-based engineering, or general metalworking event.

It will encompass the sheet metal working technology chain only. Any current exhibitor or visitor at EuroBLECH will be familiar with the show profile and our 100 per cent commitment is that the show will maintain this powerful focus. Now, we have the exciting opportunity to broaden the visitor base geographically, while maintaining our strict focus on sheet metal technology and solutions, under the AsiaBLECH brand.

ISMR: So why have you chosen Suzhou as a location for the show?

Dan Wu: Suzhou is a perfect location, with all the advantages of a strong manufacturing base and proximity to the hub of Shanghai, without many of the drawbacks of being in Shanghai!

It is a major city and a very pleasant city to visit, with great historical roots and a flourishing industrial present, due to its location on the lower Yangtze river delta. In recent years, China's economy around the Yangtze has become the engine of China's flourishing economy, with Suzhou at its heart.

Many of the best known suppliers of sheet metal equipment, such as TRUMPF, Bruderer and many others, have located their Chinese operations in Suzhou with good reason. The region generates 26 per cent of the country's GDP and, because it is the economic centre of China’s National Development Programme, it is an important international gateway to the Asia-Pacific region and beyond.

David Tellett adds: China has now become such an accessible destination for visitors throughout Asia. Suzhou is considerably less expensive than Shanghai, and therefore more cost-effective for attendees but, crucially, it also benefits from being alongside Shanghai’s international and domestic airports as well as all of Shanghai’s road and rail infrastructure. It is extremely accessible and therefore represents a better choice for exhibitors and visitors.

Being on the doorstep of China’s industrial heartland, allied to the pulling power of AsiaBLECH, means that the whole Chinese sheet metal family will find it possible to attend. Beyond China, specialist engineers from throughout the region are also learning that AsiaBLECH is a natural meeting point for those who need to stay abreast of the latest technologies and expertise/philosophy in the sheet metal sector.

Dan Wu concludes: Yes, we are very excited to welcome both exhibitors and visitors to the sheet metal sector’s new base in China in 2016. Many established EuroBLECH exhibitors, such as Bystronic, Bruderer, Schuler, Zani, AP&T, Messer Cutting Systems, Gasparini and ARKU, for example, have already recognised the value of applying the EuroBLECH approach in China, and have supported BLECH China in the first two editions. The launch of AsiaBLECH is bound to accelerate that trend. For companies who value EuroBLECH, it is time to take AsiaBLECH seriously.

ISMR: Best of luck with the growth of AsiaBLECH. Thank you all for your time. ■
