# Italian machine tools order rise by over 5%

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

Massimo Carboniero, President, UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE

NATIONAL PLAN INDUSTRY 4.0, implemented by the Italian government, has produced strong results. This is highlighted in the quarterly data reported by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian association for machine tools, robots, automation systems and ancillary products) on machine tool orders. In the first quarter of 2017, it registered a 5.1% increase compared to the same period in the previous year. The absolute value of the index amounted to 187.7, considerably over the average 100 established in 2010.

"In particular, the overall outcome of the index was due to excellent order performance in the domestic market, which has grown by 22.2% compared to the period in January-March 2016. The absolute value of the index was 236.3. Abroad, the index remained stationary, recording a 0.3% rise. The absolute index was 168.1," reported the association.

"After the decrease experienced in the last quarter of 2016, as we waited for the National Plan Industry 4.0 (announced by the Minister of Economic Development, Carlo Calenda), Italian demand for manufacturing systems has started to move again and in a significant way. This proof of the positive situation in the domestic market has benefited from important tax incentives, such as Super-Depreciation and Hyper-Depreciation."

Massimo Carboniero, President, UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, confirmed: "The order index in the first quarter of 2017, for the domestic market, is clear evidence that the programme implemented by the government authorities is showing good results. The propensity of the Italian manufacturing industry to invest in industrial technology and connectivity systems needed a boost, which has been given by the National Plan Industry 4.0."
