## Global home appliance market

The global home appliances market was valued at US$ 174.07 billion in 2017 by Orbis Research and is projected to reach a value of US$ 203.37 billion by the end of 2023, at a CAGR of 2.6% over the forecast period 2018-2023.

"The global home appliances market is experiencing steady growth," said the analyst.

The use of connectivity is a differentiating feature for manufacturers. Wireless technologies, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and ZigBee, can be used in or accessed by smartphones and tablets. But the inclusion of these technologies in home appliances (such as air conditioners) is a means of product differentiation by manufacturers that is enticing tech-savvy consumers. The improvement in network infrastructure has translated into better broadband and internet penetration. Therefore, consumers are increasingly opting for wireless and technologically advanced products, mostly for the convenience offered.

"Only a few vendors, such as LG Electronics and Samsung Electronics, have paved their way into the smart connected refrigerator space, with product offerings largely targeted at developed markets. The provision of a smart connection in a refrigerator is more of an addition, in terms of features, and not a breakthrough technology. In developed countries, the demand for smart connected kitchen appliances are gaining traction owing to early technology adopters. However, these are still in the introduction phase, and we expect that it will take three to four years for them to reach a log phase. However, the rise in consumer spending coupled with the introduction of technologically advanced models within the category are influencing the market," added the analyst.

The smart appliances market in Asia-Pacific is expected to grow at a rapid rate, according to the report. Developed countries such as Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong and Indonesia are expected to register an increase in the sales of smart appliances, mostly owing to rising energy and labour costs, the strong purchasing power of consumers and greater awareness of smart cities and smart integrated appliances.

"China has the highest demand for conventional air conditioners, by volume, across the globe, owing to government policies that favour energy-saving appliances, a growing property market and hot weather. The merger of Haier and GE appliances has given Haier an access to the latter's smart appliance technology expertise, which will make it a strong player in China," explained the analyst.

In January 2018, according to the report, Whirlpool Corporation announced its collaboration with Honeywell to expand its smart home appliance ecosystem. That same month, Electrolux announced its intention to acquire Schneidereit GmbH, a supplier of laundry rental solutions for professional customers in Germany and Austria, added the report.

According to analyst Technavio, about 51% of the incremental growth in the global household appliance market will be driven by APAC, with EMEA and the Americas making similar contributions of around one-fourth of the market size. In addition, APAC dominated the global household appliances market followed by EMEA and the Americas. However, it explained, the Americas will display a faster rate of growth than EMEA by the end of 2023, while APAC will continue being the dominant region in the global household appliances market.

"Some of the key home appliances manufacturers are focused on engaging in R&D and innovation to stay competitive in the global household appliance market.

Many leading home appliance companies are continuously widening their product portfolio to meet varying customer requirements and demands. The leading home appliance manufacturers are vying for their share of the global household beauty appliance market, and all the key players must differentiate themselves to gain vital traction over their peers," concluded Technavio.

## Kitchen appliance market

According to Research and Markets, the global kitchen appliance market was valued at US$ 163.8 billion in 2017 and is expected to reach a value of US$ 240.49 billion by 2023, witnessing a CAGR of 6.54% over the forecast period (2018-2023). Energy efficiency, technology advancements, cost and product quality are major factors that impact consumers' purchasing preference.

"The advent of the e-commerce industry and investment in expanding the range and coverage of distribution channels has allowed major industry participants to reach consumers with ease. Increasing demand for product upgrades, changing consumer lifestyles and increasing income levels are also expected to drive industry growth over the forecast period," commented the analyst.

Key highlights from the report include:

- Increasing disposable incomes are expected to drive the growth of kitchen appliances.
- Refrigerators are expected to be in demand amongst kitchen appliances.
- India has emerged as one of the primary hotspots for the growth of kitchen appliances.

## Smart home appliances

Smart home appliances are the class of next-generation connected equipment that are designed to be controlled with the help of commands from a central system or to communicate with other appliances in a network and make certain decisions for the owner. The concept of connected devices, which allow the user to manage everyday home appliances such as ovens, refrigerators, stoves, air conditioners, vacuum cleaners, washing machines, etc. from any corner of the house, with the use of a mobile computing device, a simple app, and some kind of network that connects the user with the devices, is fascinating.

The promise of added convenience when using these devices, the amount of time saved compared to manually operating them and the reduction in power and energy achieved are some of the major factors leading to the increased popularity of smart home appliances on a global scale.

Some of the major product types in the global smart home appliances market that currently have smart connectivity features are washing machines; vacuum cleaners; refrigerators; microwave ovens; air conditioners; coffee makers; lighting systems; television sets; sound systems; garage doors; hot water systems and gates. The major connectivity platforms used in the market include Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, ZigBee and Z-Wave.

"The global market for smart appliances has been expanding at an impressive pace in the past few years and is likely to continue on a bullish run in the next few years as well, rewarding innovators and first-movers. The increasingly popular trend of home automation is giving the smart appliances market a head start. Other factors that are helping bring about a remarkable shift in the way consumer appliances are designed and manufactured include the need for more energy-efficient appliances, a massive rise in the use of smartphones and an increasing number of technology-savvy consumers," commented Transparency Research.

The growth of the smart appliances market can be chiefly credited to factors such as developments in IoT, the growing demand for convenience and a need for safety and security. The rising need to save energy and make use of green or low carbon emission-oriented appliances at home are also expected to fuel the demand for smart appliances in the next few years. Leading players in the smart appliances market are thus expanding their product portfolio to include appliances that offer optimal energy management.

Smart home appliances enable consumers to focus their energy consumption to off-peak periods and optimise the use of energy. The growing prices of energy have compelled consumers to adopt energy-efficient appliances. In addition to this, various government regulations across the globe necessitate energy saving, which will further drive the global smart appliances market.

The smart appliances market in Asia-Pacific is expected to grow at a rapid rate.

The promise of added convenience when using these appliances, the amount of time saved compared to operating them manually, and the reduction in power and energy consumed are factors propelling the popularity of smart home appliances on a global scale. However, the high prices of smart home appliances as opposed to conventional non-smart home appliances could hamper their adoption to a certain degree. This is especially true for cost-sensitive countries.

Geographically, the Asia Pacific smart appliances market is most likely to witness significant growth in the coming years owing to a surge in infrastructure activities and new residential projects. The region also registers high demand for smart appliances from emerging economies such as South Korea, China and India. The demand for various home appliances such as lighting systems, household robots, smart refrigerators, vacuum cleaners, smart HVAC, microwave ovens, smart TV, air conditioners, hot water systems and smart washing machines are likely to be high in the region in the near future as increased disposable incomes in these countries have made these products easily affordable. The expanding network of high-speed internet and advancements in sensors, microchips, hardware products and connectivity mediums are aiding the growth of the global smart home appliances market, concluded Transparency Market Research.
