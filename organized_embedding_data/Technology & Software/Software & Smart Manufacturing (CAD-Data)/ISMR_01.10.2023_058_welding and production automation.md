# Welding and production automation

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Pemamek Ltd., a welding and production automation provider, exhibited at the recent Schweissen & Schneiden trade show in Essen, Germany (11-15 September 2023).

"Our presence at Schweissen & Schneiden reflects our commitment to innovation and excellence in welding automation," said Emilia Vuorela, Head of Marketing, Pemamek. “We're thrilled to return to Essen to unveil our latest automation developments and launch three new groundbreaking software systems. These technologies not only benefit our customers but also revolutionize the manufacturing landscape."

At the exhibition, Pemamek showcased its PEMA Skytrack robot station and introduced three new additions to the PEMA Core software family. These were:

- AutoScan: Sophisticated software for high-level adaptive welding that requires minimal user input.
- Visio 3D: An advanced solution for shipbuilding, designed for precise and efficient workpiece scanning.
- PathPlanner: Intelligent software for automatic, collision-free path planning.

Pemamek hosted live demonstrations of its PEMA Skytrack robot station daily at the exhibition, with product specialists on hand to answer questions from show visitors.

The manufacturer's booth also featured a weld gallery showcasing reference pieces that highlighted the extensive capabilities of laser-hybrid welding, SAW welding for thick materials, nozzle welding and more.

“Throughout the event, we also discussed a range of solutions and services designed to tackle industry challenges including the welder shortage, the demand for automation and the transition to greener solutions," Pemamek told ISMR.

Pemamek Ltd, founded in 1970, is a global welding and production automation specialist. With extensive experience in welding and production automation, it is dedicated to helping heavy fabrication industries (such as shipbuilding, wind energy and power generation) to raise their productivity levels.

The family-owned limited company employs 350 personnel at its headquarters and factory in Finland. It has daughter companies in the USA, Spain, Italy and Germany as well as local sales offices in Brazil and Poland. In June 2023, it invested 15 million euros into factory expansion and R&D. The investment decision was supported by orders from the wind energy industry during Spring 2022, but also the growing demand for heavy industry robot solutions.
