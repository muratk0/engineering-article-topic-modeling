# Safe production of stamped parts

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

MEUSBURGER has introduced different fork light barriers which guarantee the precise monitoring of the stamping process and the safe production of stamped parts, it told ISMR.

New in its range of products for die making are fork light barriers for feed and position control. These feature dirt-resistant and scratch-proof optical elements. The sensors are protected in a robust metal housing for precise monitoring of feed and position of the strip. Three different types are available (E 6532, E 6536 and E 6530) for infrared and red light types. They can be screwed in from above or from the sides.

“Also new in the range are the E 6542 Fork light barrier and the E 6592 Setting unit for double sheet detection and tilt control. These monitor the wiping-off of waste. An optical filter guarantees insensitivity to extraneous light and the setting unit ensures easy height adjustment. A light or dark mode is selectable for all fork light barriers,” the manufacturer explained.

Meusburger is a leading manufacturer of high precision standard parts. Offering an extensive range of standard parts, combined with high-grade workshop equipment products, it is also a global partner for making dies, moulds, jigs and fixtures. See www.meusburger.com ■
