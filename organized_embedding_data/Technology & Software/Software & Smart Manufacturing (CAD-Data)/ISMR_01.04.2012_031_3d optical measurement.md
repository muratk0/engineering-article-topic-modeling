# 3D OPTICAL MEASUREMENT

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

2012 IS a key and festive year for Creaform, a leader in 3D portable optical measurement technologies and 3D engineering services, as the Canada-based company reaches 10 years of innovation, growth and success. It offers the entire range of 3D applications, including 3D scanning, reverse engineering, inspection, design & engineering and FEA.

Creaform has developed and sells the Handyscan 3D line of portable, self-positioning handheld 3D scanners, along with the HandyPROBE portable arm-free CMM and the MetraSCAN optical CMM 3D scanner – two systems designed for inspection applications. The company recently launched the MaxSHOT 3D optical coordinate measuring system that brings photogrammetry to all these technologies. It also produces the Creaform 3D Body Digitizers line-up. www.creaform3d.com
