# An eye on inspection

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

GOM IS NOW offering new and additional application-specific features in its GOM Correlate Professional software.

"Interested users can familiarise themselves with the advantages of the Professional version free of charge for 30 days without any contractual obligations," it told ISMR.

With GOM's optical 3D ARAMIS system, material properties and behaviour throughout the design and manufacturing workflow can be understood. This contributes to improved product quality, reduced costs and time for manufacturers.

GOM Correlate software is used for the inspection and evaluation of 2D or 3D data from the ARAMIS sensor or other metrology systems.

"One application-specific feature is vibration analysis that can be used in the automobile and aerospace industries. For example, the fan blades of modern turbines for the aerospace industry must be continuously checked, inspected and, if necessary, maintained to withstand extreme operating conditions during their lifespan. Our 3D testing system, ARAMIS, perfectly supplements our ATOS system, which measures the surface defects of fan blades," explained GOM.

One new feature from GOM Correlate Professional, of particular use in the automotive industry, enables contour detection and analysis of airbag deployment tests.

Crack tip detection makes the tracing and evaluation of the trajectory of crack points possible. It can be used for a wide range of applications in materials research such as metals, plastics and composites and in many industries with high security requirements.

Measured data from typical material tests, such as Nakajima, bulge, tensile, bending, shear and hole expansion tests, is evaluated in the software to determine the material characteristics. This data is used as input parameters for the numerical simulation, enabling a more precise material model and more accurate prediction of material behaviour.

"Project templates can be carried out to repeat evaluations rapidly and easily. In a project template, among others, inspection elements, project keywords and reports are saved. Users do not need to set up the project again when carrying out another evaluation of the same type. To increase workflow efficiency, the GOM Training Centre offers classroom training, eLearning and sample data sets for the software at training.gom.com" added GOM.
