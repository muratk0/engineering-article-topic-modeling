# Part validation

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

HEXAGON MANUFACTURING INTELLIGENCE produces a series of shop floor coordinate measurement machines, which enable manufacturers to validate parts at the point of production without having to move them away from the line to a quality room.

The new Hexagon Manufacturing Intelligence GLOBAL EVO CMM was on show at this year’s MACH exhibition where visitors were able to learn more about new technologies such as Compass, a combined hardware/firmware vibration reduction function which acts like a suspension system.

“The machine can effectively compensate for vibrations caused by its own movements, enabling higher-speed scanning with no loss of accuracy. The GLOBAL EVO also incorporates Fly2 Mode, the next generation of our trajectory-optimising technology. It ensures smooth movements and reduces program execution times by automatically generating and implementing the most efficient path between points,” said the manufacturer.

“Created in collaboration with the industrial design house, Pininfarina, GLOBAL EVO augments the GLOBAL CMM platform with new ideas to push the boundaries of performance.”
