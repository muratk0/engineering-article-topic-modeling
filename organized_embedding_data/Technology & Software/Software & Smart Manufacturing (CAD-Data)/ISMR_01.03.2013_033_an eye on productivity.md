# An eye on PRODUCTIVITY

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

SEIKI SYSTEMS, a specialist provider of real-time production management solutions for over 20 years, has just released a completely re-developed OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) module as part of the latest version of Seiki NMS, the company’s Networked Manufacturing System.

“The OEE module has been redeveloped to meet the needs of businesses that increasingly recognise the shop floor not just as a production area but an internal profit centre. Having the ability to interrogate reliable, actionable, realtime data collected directly from the shop floor is central to this, yet many companies either cannot collect this in real-time or only collect part of the overall data available to them,” Seiki Systems told ISMR.

“The Seiki OEE module as part of the Seiki NMS bases its calculations on access to logged records taken directly, in real-time, from each machine on the production floor. By accurately including the often missed small events and avoiding misrecorded data, a business can base decisions to optimise the use of its assets whilst minimising waste on factual and complete historical information.”

Given the sheer volume of data that a shop floor generates, the Seiki OEE module as part of the Seiki NMS provides a powerful and intuitive series of analysis tools to enable informed and intelligent decisionmaking. Knowledge about the business can be accessed with manufacturing performance data viewed, summarised and analysed via a dynamic dashboard as well as customisable and detailed reports. Areas where losses can be reduced or even eliminated can be readily identified using the three metrics that comprise a company’s OEE.

“In some businesses, the Availability metric will identify that downtime is the largest area on which to focus attention. Alternatively, other companies find that their performance against standard times is poor, identified via the Performance metric leading to a review of standard times and the reasons for the deviations. With analysis of the Quality metric, a company can identify the time lost manufacturing non-conforming parts.

"In total, the Seiki Systems OEE module allows fine tuning of measures to improve overall performance on the shop floor as well as identifying areas which will provide the greatest ROI (Return on Investment). This business intelligence can help inform strategic business level decisions such as capital and plant investment,” the manufacturer added.

## New fiber laser from Hypertherm

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of advanced cutting systems, has confirmed the official release of a new 3kW fiber laser system (pre-launched at EuroBLECH 2012) – the HyIntensity® Fiber Laser HFL030 with a recommended cut capacity of 20mm for mild steel, 12mm for stainless steel and 10mm for aluminium.

Like all Hypertherm HyIntensity Fiber Laser systems, the HFL030 is specifically optimised for cutting applications. It comes complete with pre-developed cutting processes and all the required components for significantly simplified table integration and operation. As a result, the system can rapidly cut thicker materials.

“Hypertherm’s 3kW system has the cut capacity of other 4kW fiber laser systems without sacrificing cut quality,” the manufacturer told ISMR. “HyIntensity fiber laser systems can be easily integrated into a wide range of cutting machine types, allowing service centres and fabricators to expand their business by adding high precision fine feature cutting to their operations,” said Doug Shuda, product marketing manager for Hypertherm’s fiber laser products.

“All of the HyIntensity systems were developed and manufactured by Hypertherm, making Hypertherm the only company to offer laser, plasma as well as waterjet cutting expertise,” added the manufacturer.

Hypertherm’s product line includes hand-held and mechanised plasma systems and consumables, as well as fiber laser and now waterjet products, in addition to CNC motion and height controls and CAM cutting software.

SENSOR FUSION, a new 3U data acquisition device from Aerotech (www.aerotech.com), provides precise time alignment of motion and data acquisition functions in electronics and other industries. Integrating data acquisition into the motion control platform is a costeffective data acquisition solution providing quick and easy configuration and set-up for shorter development time.

“The Sensor Fusion is applicable in any test, measurement or inspection situation where sensor data must be precisely aligned with position. Major application areas include semiconductor, display, automotive and medical device manufacturing. It is available in desktop, rack-mounted and panel-mounted configurations,” confirmed Aerotech.

Coupled with Aerotech’s Automation 3200 (A3200) motion control software, the device accepts input from up to four different cards. Collection is synchronised in real-time so there is no need for an additional alignment process. Cards are available for digital input and output, analogue input and output, encoder input and PSO output. Coupling data playback with Aerotech’s traditional PSO functionality allows precision control of multiple devices – up to 96 digital outputs or 48 analogue outputs – from one positioning system.
