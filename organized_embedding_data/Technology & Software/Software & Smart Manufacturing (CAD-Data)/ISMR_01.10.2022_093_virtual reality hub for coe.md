# Virtual Reality hub for COE

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

At this November's FABTECH in Atlanta, COE Press Equipment will feature a group of technology hubs that together create the COE EXPERIENCE. Visitors can take part in a Virtual Reality (VR) demonstration, visit the Smart Manufacturing Decisions station or participate in other demonstrations that illustrate COE's equipment and service differentiators.

The “COE EXPERIENCE" is intended to help manufacturers make the best coil processing equipment decision for their needs, the coil processing specialist told ISMR.
