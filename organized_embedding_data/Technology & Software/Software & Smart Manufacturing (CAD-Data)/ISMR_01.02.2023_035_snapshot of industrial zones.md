## Value-add events

Two major initiatives are being held in the runup to the event, GI 360 and GI Connections, to encourage further participation.

GI 360 is a bi-monthly panoramic industry news programme, with a virtual tour of the factory of the future, an overview of business conditions with France Industrie and a big debate on topical issues. GI Connections offers premium business and 100% physical events held throughout the year, all over France, on various industrial themes.

During the exhibition itself, five major events will also be held for visitors to enjoy.

## 1. Golden Tech 2023: The aim of this

challenge is to evaluate the skill, technical knowledge, expertise and creativity of candidates for fifteen selected professions. It combines novel features such as individual technical tests and collective creative tests. All candidates will be part of a team, led by a team leader. The theme for 2023 is 'Hydrogen Cars' and two concept cars will be on display; the NamX French hydrogen concept SUV and the H2 24h Le Mans hydrogen concept car.

## 2. CIM 2023 Metrology Conference: A

four-day programme dedicated to the best industrial practices and advances in R&D applied to measurement, analysis and testing processes. It will include 200 oral and poster presentations; six roundtables on hot topics; one Plenary Session "Limitless Metrology at your Fingertips" on three key applications such as Industry 4.0, Environment and Health; one Climate Workshop – MetClimVOC; ten pitch presentations and various networking activities.

3. GI Avenir: An area entirely dedicated to employment, training and job opportunities where companies can post their job adverts and meet candidates. It included an online recruitment portal; employment wall; guided tours; job appointments and express coaching sessions. In partnership with the Aerospace Jobs and Qualifications Campus of the University of Evry, GI Avenir is also launching its Industry 4.0 Hackathon dedicated to data science.

4. GI Awards: Launched in 2018, the Global Industrie Awards (GIA) provide recognition for exhibitors at the show who have developed an innovative product, service or expertise. The categories for 2023 are Production Technology; Peripheral Technology; Digital Transition; Environmental Responsibility; Exemplary Partnership and Young Start-Up.

## 5. GI Conferences: More than 160 experts and manufacturers are expected to attend a series of events during the show around the theme 'The industry is mobilising'. These will include masterclasses by captains of industry, Keynotes by factory directors and technical managers as well as roundtables in which up to five speakers will discuss major industrial, technological or strategic issues. Some of these issues include:

• Reindustrialisation and sovereignty.

• Energy.

• Green growth.

• Technologies.

• Skills.

Industry 4.0 international business meetings will also be held onsite on Wednesday 8th and Thursday 9th March, by the regional economic agency Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Entreprises and its partners in the framework of the Enterprise Europe Network. These meetings attracted more than 350 participants from about 20 different countries during previous shows.
