# Mobile robot milestone

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Kivnon, a global producer of industrial mobile robots, has manufactured and installed its 5000th robot, representing a significant milestone in its history. The Spanish company designs, manufactures and implements mobile robots that are designed to enhance material-handling efficiency across various markets including automotive, food, logistics distribution and retail applications.

“We started in 2009 with a vision for new ways to improve intralogistics operations in production centres, warehouses and distribution centres along with a team of talented experts in material handling and robotic engineering. We are proud and excited to announce that we have now manufactured our 5000th mobile robot," said Juan Prieto, CEO of Kivnon.

Kivnon's product portfolio includes small vehicles that tow carts or carry the load directly, tractors and autonomous forklifts.

“The vehicles move materials and goods in factories, warehouses and distribution centres. Navigation systems allow them to drive safely and efficiently in work areas without the need for an operator," explained the manufacturer.

In late 2009, Juan Prieto and Luis Gómez, co-founders of Kivnon, registered the brand and designed their first mobile robot, the K10 model. Growth continued as global economic tensions eased. In 2017, the company began its international expansion, entering new sectors such as logistics and pharmaceuticals.

"Creating Kivnon was a visionary gamble that has paid off. Automating material logistics flows is making a visible difference in all sectors. For example, in one of our applications in the automotive sector, it helped transform assembly plants into open and organised lines," said Prieto.

Today, as digital transformation and the advance of Industry 4.0 increasingly bring warehouse and distribution centre functions to automation, Kivnon manufactures and deploys around 1000 mobile robots per year, reaching a total of 5000 units today. Growth has been driven by the success of its K05 Twister model, which automates the transport of medium loads in confined spaces as well as its expansion of distribution channels and commitment to simplifying logistical processes.

"We are constantly attentive to automation trends and the future. We develop new products based on the improvement of our technology to offer even better solutions to our customers, including leveraging the full value of Industry 4.0, AI and Machine Learning for logistics," concluded Luis Gómez. ■
