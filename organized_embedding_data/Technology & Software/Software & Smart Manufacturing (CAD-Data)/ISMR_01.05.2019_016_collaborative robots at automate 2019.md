# Collaborative robots at Automate 2019

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

SMC CORPORATION OF America, a leading global pneumatics and industrial automation components manufacturer, has joined Universal Robots' UR+ programme, launching the new UR+ certified MHM series magnetic gripper. It was demonstrated recently at the Automate Show 2019 in Chicago, USA.

"The gripper is bundled into an easy plug and play kit that integrates to a solution for picking, transferring and placing ferromagnetic workpieces of uneven surfaces, porous or that are susceptible when traditional methods of vacuum systems or mechanical grippers are not viable," it told ISMR.

"The UR+ platform is an opportunity for SMC to provide a plug and play solution for UR cobots that helps lower the barriers to automation for customers of all sizes," said Amanda Wease, Product Manager, SMC Corporation of America. "We reached out to Universal Robots to become a UR+ developer as it felt like a natural fit to join the global leader in collaborative robots. This is just the starting point of our relationship. We look forward to expand with the UR+ programme, developing certified offerings from our vast catalogue of over ten thousand standard products."

The UR+ programme debuted in 2016 and has since grown rapidly to include more than 140 certified UR+ products with more than 400 companies joining the UR+ developer programme. A charter partner with the UR+ programme is Robotiq, which launched three new UR+ certified products at Automate 2019; AirPick and EPick, Robotiq's new vacuum grippers and the Sanding Kit. These new UR+ product offerings are in step with the growth in new cobot applications.

"Processing tasks, such as sanding and polishing, are not what cobots have been traditionally known for... But by being able to easily add new advanced capabilities, such as a UR+ certified sanding kit, this helps open up a new frontier for us," it said.

Universal Robots was founded in 2005 and launched its first collaborative robot (cobot) in 2008. The company, which is part of Teradyne Inc., is headquartered in Odense, Denmark. See www.universal-robots.com ■
