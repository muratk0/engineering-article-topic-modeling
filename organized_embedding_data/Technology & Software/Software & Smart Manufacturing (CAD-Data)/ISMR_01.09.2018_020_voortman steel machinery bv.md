NEW INNOVATIONS will be shown at the interactive Voortman booth at EuroBLECH 2018. As a supplier to automatic steel fabrication and manufacturing markets, Voortman will present its newly released products, including new technology for heavy-plate processing and beams.

"Voortman customers, whether a stand-alone workshop or a multi-facility business, value the reduction of manual labour and increased productivity of their factory operations. The high level of autonomy and quality of our systems speed up part production, reduce machine downtime and enable 24/7 manufacturing. With high-quality components and an excellent support and service team on all continents, Voortman machines keep going for decades," commented Voortman. The company's booth at EuroBLECH will feature an interactive 3D experience into its machinery so that visitors can visualise actual customer production and optimise production lines for low costs.

Euroblech, Hannover | October 23th - 26th 2018 | hall 12 booth H93
