Visual inspection app includes AI-based object detection, classification

Pleora Technologies’ Vaira system integrates machine vision cameras, lighting, edge processing, manufacturing system connectivity, and a low-code development platform with a suite of customizable inspection and traceability apps for the shop floor. The system’s visual inspection app now includes AI-based object detection and classification that can be integrated with assembly checklist and work instructions. With the combined inspection and checklist features, the operator can follow digitized instructions that guide product assembly step-by-step, while AI and machine vison automatically verify quality.

Fiber laser system cuts variety of materials with 3- to 15-kW power

Bystronic’s new ByCut Smart 3015 cuts thin to medium-thickness steel, aluminum, stainless steel, brass, and copper with high laser power from 3 to 15 kW.

The Nozzle Control Tool feature provides automatic nozzle centering at the beginning of a cutting plan and after collisions, eliminating the need for operator interaction. The KerfScan feature also functions as a monitor to help increase process reliability. They help eliminate incorrect settings and poor material quality to reduce scrap.

The Parameter Wizard feature determines the correct parameters for the user with nitrogen and MixGas for steel with thicknesses of 4 to 15 mm. MixGas combines oxygen and nitrogen for cutting thicker steel at increased cutting speeds.

Plasma systems can be connected to double cutting output

Thermal Dynamics Automation’s new UC series high-precision automated plasma power supplies offer cutting power from 130 to 800 amps for piercing mild steel up to 2 in. (3.5-in. edge start) and stainless steel up to 4 in. (6.25-in. edge start).

Two UC 201, UC 301, or UC 401 systems can be connected to double cutting output to 400, 600, or 800 amps, respectively, or they can operate independently. Target applications for the paralleling capability include fabricators and steel services centers that use two torches on the same gantry and want to increase cutting speeds on steel plates up to 2 in. thick. It also lets fabricators cut stainless steel and aluminum sections up to 6.25 in.
