# Robotic plasma cutting system

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

Hypertherm Associates and its partner, Daihen Corporation, took part in the International Robot Exhibition 2023 in Japan recently, one of the world's largest robot exhibitions. The theme of the exhibition was "Sustainable Societies Through Robotics" featuring solutions that support sustainable development goals through the coexistence and cooperation of humans and robots. During the event, the latest robots and robot-related technology (such as AI, ICT and component technology) was on display.

Daihen Corporation exhibited its robotic plasma cutting solution at the exhibition. The new Daihen system is equipped with dedicated commands that set cutting parameters and conduct piercing sequences automatically. These are designed to make the system easy to use and maximize consumable life.

"Because of the height-sensor capability that maintains height between torch and workpiece, the system produces high-quality cuts," confirmed Hypertherm Associates.

Hypertherm Associates is a U.S.- based manufacturer of industrial cutting products and software. Its products include Hypertherm plasma and OMAX waterjet systems. In addition to cutting systems, the company creates CNCs and software. Founded in 1968, Hypertherm Associates is a 100 per cent Associate-owned company, employing approximately 2,000 Associates, with operations and partner representation worldwide.■
