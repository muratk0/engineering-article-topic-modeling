# Identify and trace

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

MARKING, IDENTIFICATION AND traceability specialist, Pryor Marking Technology, will be displaying its latest range of identification and traceability solutions at the Southern Manufacturing Show in the UK from 5–7 February 2019.

Visitors to the show will be able to view three of the company's latest marking products as well as discuss marking solutions, traceability and tracking software, whether for integration within existing operations, portable equipment or bespoke designs, on Pryor's booth number E205.

"We will be demonstrating our 'on-thefly' high-speed laser marking product which enables permanent laser marking on a highspeed conveyor in 0.5 seconds. The marks meet industrial standards and are as high quality as a stationery mark. Keeping the production line flowing has a massive impact on time saving, which is a huge benefit to customers," explained the company.

Portability is a key factor for a lot of businesses, particularly in the aerospace and automotive sector as they often have large heavy items that require marking. Pryor's portable laser is mobile and allows easy marking of large, immobile and heavy items. It can mark onto a wide range of materials.

Pryor will also be showing its Portadot, which provides a fast and mobile method of dot-peen marking, at Southern Manufacturing. It is suitable for marking permanent alphanumeric, logo or 2D barcode information onto immobile, large or heavy components. See www.pryormarking.com
