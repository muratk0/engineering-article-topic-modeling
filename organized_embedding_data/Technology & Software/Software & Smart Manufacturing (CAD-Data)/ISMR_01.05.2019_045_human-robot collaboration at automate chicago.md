# Human-robot collaboration at Automate Chicago

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

KUKA DISPLAYED SOME of its collaborative robot capabilities with experts on hand at Chicago's Automate (automation technologies exhibition) from 8-11 April 2019.

Held every two years, Automate demonstrates the range of automation technologies and solutions for the automation and manufacturing industries in North America. At the event, KUKA executives revealed its latest innovations in robotics, motion and control automation technologies, and discussed the importance of incorporating collaborative robots in the workplace in three speaking sessions:

KUKA also showcased some of its solutions offerings with experts on hand to discuss all six stages of HRC. These six stages demonstrate the different ways manufacturers can use collaborative robots to save costs, enhance productivity and address business challenges to see results on their bottom lines. The demonstrations on display included:

AGILUS 3D Bin Picking: This demo featured the KR 5 R900 AGILUS robot and showcased its solution for 3D bin-picking.

Mobility: Featuring the KMP 1500 (static) and KMR iiwa robots, this demo interacted with the AGILUS 3D Bin Picking demo, communicating through TCP/IP technology. The KMR iiwa robot restocked bins for the demo and was fully collaborative for attendees to interact with through safety scanners.

Welding: KUKA's welding demo included two KR CYBERTECH Nanos; KR 6 R1820 arc HW and KR 10 R1420. Using KUKA Roboteam software, the robots cooperated to execute coupled motions to dry weld a part.

AGILUS Fastening: During the demo, the KR 3 AGILUS showcased its fastening capabilities by taking a micro screw from the feeder and fastening it into a large plate – this technique is particularly useful for the production of small electronics devices.

LBR iiwa Hand-Guide: Using the LBR iiwa robot, this demo allowed attendees to trace a pattern to teach the robot a particular movement. The robot then performed the movement on its own, unassisted, until another attendee traced a new path for the robot to follow.

"These collaborative robots have played an essential role in the success of the manufacturing and automation industry, and their use will only grow from here," said Simon Whitton, SVP of Sales & Marketing, Americas, KUKA. "By representing select stages of HRC, we aimed to show Automate attendees the seamless production that these solutions can provide to users."
