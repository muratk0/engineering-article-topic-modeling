# Metal processing machine market in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

The global metal processing machine market is expected to witness substantial growth, projected to reach US$ 35.2 billion by 2030, with a CAGR of 4.8% from 2023 to 2030.

"The growth of this market is driven by investments in defence/aerospace sectors and increased demand for consumer electronics due to rising disposable incomes," said a new report on ResearchandMarkets.com

"Demand for the latest technologies as well as advances in manufacturing processes is expected to boost the growth of metal processing machine manufacturers. Innovations in additive manufacturing, smart robotics and computerised monitoring and control systems are enhancing the efficiency and precision of metal processing machines," it added.

The aerospace industry's focus on lightweight materials and advanced manufacturing techniques to improve fuel efficiency and performance is driving the need for metal processing machines. The use of robotics in metal processing operations has also increased. Robots offer precision and repeatability, leading to higher product quality and consistency. They can handle various tasks, improving productivity and reducing lead times.

"Europe holds a significant share of the global metal processing machines market, driven by well-established manufacturing industries. Germany, known for its advanced engineering and manufacturing capabilities, is a key market within Europe," highlighted the report.

Market restraints cited in the report include the volatility of raw material prices whilst market opportunities and trends flagged were automated fabrication processes and robotics, as well as user-friendly interfaces.■
