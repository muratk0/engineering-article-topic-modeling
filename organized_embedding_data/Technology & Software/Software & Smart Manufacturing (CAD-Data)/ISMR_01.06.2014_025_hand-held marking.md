# Hand-held marking

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

THE PRODUCT RANGE of MARKATOR® Manfred Borries GmbH encompasses several solutions for marking different types of workpieces with durable and tamper-proof marking. Various materials can be marked from plastics, aluminium and construction steel up to hardened steel (hardness of up to 63HRC). A product can leave a factory marked with a character, number string or two-dimensional Data Matrix Code. The product can also be labelled with a company logo or a test mark used for quality management.

Plagiarism costs the machine tool manufacturing industry several billion dollars every year from lost sales revenues and quality defects from product copies. Durable and tamper-proof marking enables users to identify the manufacturer and track batch number, production date, production plant etc.

The Flymarker® PRO hand-held dot peen marking system is a portable, cordless and battery-operated system for durable and tamperproof marking. It has a high marking speed, is small and weighs just 10lb. Equipped with a strong magnet and a powerful battery, the portable power package creates deep and durable markings.
