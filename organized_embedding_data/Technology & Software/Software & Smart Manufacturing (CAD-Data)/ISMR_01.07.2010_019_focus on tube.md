# FOCUS ON TUBE

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

To be able to manufacture profitably in spite of lower customer batch requirements, tube processing companies have had to become more flexible and shorten the entire production cycle. ISMR chronicles recent developments in tube machinery to help them do just that….

ISMR SAYS:

“Tube users today are looking to increase the degree of automation of processes, cut costs and hire from a wider pool of labour”
