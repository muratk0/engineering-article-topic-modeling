ISMR: Please give a brief background to Chin Fong, including its corporate philosophy?

Our origins go back to a small ironworks factory, now an international conglomerate. Enduring economic storms, two zero-to-hero start-up journeys and maturing over decades, we have become Taiwan's largest forging equipment manufacturer.

Chin Fong's operating philosophy is Innovation + Service + Feedback. On customer service, we uphold a partner-oriented approach. This embodies our three-fold philosophy of 'Innovation' for seeking change and value, 'Service' with a customer-centric focus and 'Feedback' in returning benefits. We aim to provide customers with superior levels of service.

We understand that innovation, service and feedback are three indispensable elements that intertwine and complement each other, forming the cornerstone of our operations. We will continuously advance innovation, elevate service quality and contribute to society through various forms of feedback, creating more value for customers, employees and the community.

ISMR: What, in your view, are important industry trends and challenges? How are you responding to these?

ST: The rapid development of the new energy vehicle (NEV) industry presents both significant trends and challenges for the metalforming sector. In this context, the upgrade and transformation of metalforming technologies have become crucial.

Firstly, the widespread adoption of NEVs is driving transformation and upgrading within the automotive manufacturing industry, particularly in metalforming. Taking pure electric vehicles as an example, their body design tends towards lightweight construction which imposes higher demands on metalforming processes. It requires precise control over the shape and thickness of each component to achieve weight reduction.

Secondly, the NEV industry's challenges (such as immature business models and weak profitability) also impact the metalforming sector. As a capital-intensive and technology-intensive industry, metalforming requires substantial investment and technological research and development, putting immense pressure on small and medium-sized enterprises.

Chin Fong Group provides comprehensive metalforming solutions to meet development trends in the NEV industry. This includes lightweight vehicle bodies, battery case production lines and motor production lines.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers and how are you addressing these issues for them?

ST: For Chin Fong's customers, quality consistency is the most crucial concern. Our stamping equipment is designed and developed using computer 3D models to verify the equipment's status. Finite element analysis is then used to confirm the related strength. Before production begins, machining and assembly review meetings are held to incorporate strict process control and subsequent maintenance requirements into the product design for optimisation.

Quality assurance uses Big Data analysis to list inspection points and requirements based on past machine anomalies and customer complaints, planning the product inspection engineering accordingly. After the machine is completed, design verification tests are conducted to ensure that the design planning and actual product output meet the original design requirements.

ISMR: Are there any products/technologies/ services that you have released or are releasing this year (including for EuroBLECH) that you would like to discuss?

ST: Our HST series of straightside high-speed precision presses is a new product launched this year. It is mainly used in multi-station, precision production lines for new energy vehicle motor chips. The HST series can integrate a stacker, straightener, feeder and automatic riveting stacking devices into a complete automated production line.

With a maximum capacity of 400 tons and a maximum table width of 3700mm, it meets the current international specifications for stamping equipment required for new energy vehicle motor chip production. The equipment precision of JIS Super Grade 1/2 satisfies the stamping trend of motor chip materials with thicknesses of 0.15mm and 0.2mm, reducing motor eddy current losses, weight and volume, thereby improving motor efficiency.

The Chin Fong iForming intelligent production management system enables real-time equipment and production line information. Through a visual management dashboard, timely equipment maintenance and production scheduling management can be performed, providing real-time production status monitoring. This ensures efficient process management and increases equipment unit productivity.
