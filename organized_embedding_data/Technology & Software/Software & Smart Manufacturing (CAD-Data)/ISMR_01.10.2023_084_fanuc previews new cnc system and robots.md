# FANUC previews new CNC system and robots

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

The focal point of FANUC's 1,444m² stand at EMO Hannover 2023 (18-23 September 2023) was a new CNC which is currently in development. A new HMI simplifies use for experienced and new FANUC operators. For every task, all interactions can be handled on one screen so that there is no need to jump between several of them.

"The PMC (programmable machine control) can be programmed with Structured Text and the CNC supports any machine kinematics. Although the new CNC is still under development, some of its many features are already available. The enhanced versions of our existing advanced FS30i/31i/32i-B Plus and standard FS0i-F Plus CNC solutions already include functions for energy saving and carbon neutrality, as well as digital twin technology, which will also feature in the FS500i-A for direct end-user benefits," commented Fanuc.

The new CPU, combined with newly introduced features and capabilities, is designed to improve basic performance and drive cycle time reductions.

Aside from various robots in the ROBOMACHINE area, FANUC showed a dedicated area featuring multiple robots of different sizes and capacities. All were suitable for beginners and experienced programmers, either using the traditional iPendant or a tablet TP. Among new FANUC robots making their debut appearance at EMO was the CRX-25iA collaborative robot in 30kg payload option, the FANUC M-710iD/50M with its curved arm and the CR-35iB collaborative robot in heavy-duty, 50kg option.

FANUC's IoT highlight at EMO was the introduction of a new 'FIELD system' version. FIELD system Basic Package is an on-premise platform (no Internet connection required) that allows manufacturers to collect, analyse and utilise data from connected machines and devices, offering compatibility with FANUC products up to 20 years old and extending its support to third-party products.

"It offers rapid set-up, easy-to-use templates and regular update capability which enables users to realise continuous production improvements in line with Kaizen strategies," explained Fanuc.

EMO visitors could also discover more about the company's latest service offer enhancements, including new features of the FANUC Assisted Reality (FAR) remote support tool. For instance, the company showcased tablet-sharing on a CRX collaborative robot with direct call capability to FANUC. FANUC also highlighted its service portfolio, FANUC Academy training courses and FANUC Care, a new full-service package that is similar in effect to an extended warranty.■

Lorch Schweißtechnik GmbH is a manufacturer of electric arc welding systems for industrial applications, metalworking, partially automated solutions and robot automation systems. Presenting a range of integrated innovations, the company showcased the future of industrial welding at the SCHWEISSEN AND SCHNEIDEN exhibition in Essen, Germany, from 11-15 September 2023. It launched its latest innovation in MIG-MAG welding at the show, the iQS inverter platform.

"The Smart Process Control Engine (SPC) is raising development and deployment of new welding processes to an entirely new level. Comprehensive networking allows faster service, and the Smart Cockpit gives customers further significantly simplified and intuitive operator guidance. A torch generation aligned with the new technology complements the various innovations while turning the iQS into an inverter platform with unprecedented capacities," commented Lorch.

Lorch's new MF-09 wire feeder boasts a modular build, bulk pack feed version, flexible change-over from water to gas and many other features. It supplements the newly developed iQS inverter platform.

Lorch also presented another generation of its LMS Performance torches to clearly simplify manual welding at the exhibition in Essen.

"Equipped with a high-quality colour display, an additional workplace LED and combined with a new and innovative torch connection to the power source, they offer control, comfort and performance when welding," said Lorch.

The new LMR 2 torches by Lorch are designed to unlock new performance dimensions in robot welding.

"Robots are now able to weld precisely with these torches developed specifically for automated welding. Their optimal contact tip cooling and gas coverage make them particularly economical during use. With new torches, the corresponding hose packages, the wire feed and the power source, we offer a precisely coordinated overall solution to warrant maximum efficiency for all robot systems," explained Lorch.

Lorch Schweißtechnik has also increased the degree of Cobot automation with smart sensor technology for seam tracking. The combination of the TouchSense weld seam search with SeamTracking weld correction is designed to ensure high-welding quality, even with tolerances and distortions, and without requiring any complex programming. The new TouchSense feature allows the torch to find the weld seam position for fillet welds automatically. SeamTracking weld correction automatically keeps the Cobot on the course of the weld seam. The SeamPilot feature ensures that, once the starting point has been specified, the Cobot will follow the weld seam to its end automatically, producing straight fillet welds as well as complex free forms.
