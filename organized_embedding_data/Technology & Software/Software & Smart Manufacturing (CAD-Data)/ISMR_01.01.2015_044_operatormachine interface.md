# Operator/machine interface

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

INDUSTRY 4.0, with automation increasingly defining work practices in mechanical engineering, is a hot topic in manufacturing. At FABTECH (11 to 13 November 2014), ARKU Coil Systems Inc. demonstrated how its Arvis 4.0´s visualization of process steps helps to network systems and integrate the operator into the processes. Visitors to ARKU´s booth performed their own tests using the HiCap® high-performance leveller animation, experiencing how functions can be simply and intuitively controlled via a central interface.

“Different functions of the HiCap® highperformance leveller can be selected and controlled via an interactive screen. The results of these actions are clearly displayed on a large monitor. It is easy to see the principal advantages of the new visualization solution for the HiCap® leveller. The machine is particularly suitable for levelling complex material defects and high tensile materials,” ARKU told ISMR.

“It significantly improves operation of the HiCap® high-performance leveller. All stations and external systems involved in the process are seamlessly networked. Operators not only enter their settings faster, they also receive detailed feedback from the system. In the event of an error, the Arvis 4.0 system provides accurate information and suggests ways of resolving the problem. Production can be quickly resumed; systematic error analysis enables rapid reaction times.”
