# PMF announces expansion

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

CONTINUING a longstanding tradition of expansion and advancement, PMF Industries has announced the recent addition of an 8,000-square foot warehouse to its Williamsport, Pennsylvania facility in the US. PMF has added over 45,000 square feet in the past six years, with the previous and largest addition of 37,000 square feet completed in 2006.

The recent addition serves as an environmentally-controlled storage centre for PMF’s custom tool-making department. PMF develops tooling for its own metal forming processes, including flowforming, deep drawing, hydroforming, heat treating and welding.

An increase in the current warehousing space sets the stage for future growth. Plans are in motion to increase inhouse manufacturing capabilities by adding a new flowforming machine, heat-treating furnace and a 1000-ton press.
