# The umati initiative showcased live demonstrations of networked machine data flows and the seamless communication of manufacturing data at EMO Hannover 2023.

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Those interested in experiencing the Future of Connectivity (live and in person) saw demonstrations of the umati (universal machine technology interface) at EMO Hannover 2023 (18-23 September 2023). The joint initiative from the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association) and VDMA (German Engineering Federation), together with a network of numerous industry and research-based partners, demonstrated how machines all over the world can communicate with each other simply, seamlessly and securely.

Umati celebrated its first major appearance at EMO Hannover in 2019 with a live demonstration.

The global network of partners has now grown to over 320 members and counting. Numerous OPC UA specifications, which are vital for the open and standardised exchange of data, have been added since then. The most recent example was the specifications for plastics processing machines and additive manufacturing systems.

Umati is the global initiative for open communication interfaces for the machinebuilding industry and its customers. It enables the exchange of data between machines, components and systems, and their integration into customer and user-specific IT ecosystems, simply, seamlessly and securely. Machinemakers, software manufacturers, component suppliers and users have joined forces to create a strong community aimed at promoting the use of open, standardised interfaces based on OPC UA Companion Specifications.

Left: Dr. Alexander Broos, Head of Research and Technology, VDW, and project manager for umati (image: Deutsche Messe.)

## A powerful triumvirate

At the umati stand in Hall 9 at EMO Hannover 2023, various 'Meet-the-Expert' rounds were held twice each day to offer first-hand information from the umati team and representatives of umati partner companies on the background, technology and benefits of open, standardised data interfaces for mechanical engineering. Various exhibits enabled visitors to experience how sophisticated the technical solution has become.

Stands belonging to the OPC Foundation and the Machine Information Interoperability (MII) department of the VDMA were also near umati's stand. Together, they represent a powerful triumvirate. OPC UA is the underlying technology, the OPC UA Companion Specifications is the global production language for numerous technologies in the machinery and plant engineering industry (driven by the VDMA) and umati is responsible for implementation and marketing.

Dr. Alexander Broos, Head of Research and Technology at the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association), highlighted the closeness of the three entities.

"For us, networking involves connecting not only machines but also people. After all, the intelligent linking of machines is an ongoing process that can only succeed if it is supported by a strong community working together," he explained.

## Following the data flow

Numerous partner companies (with their machines, components and software solutions) formed the framework of the live demonstration. The sectors featured at EMO Hannover included manufacturers of machine tools but also suppliers of geometric metrology, additive manufacturing, robotics, image processing and, of course, software.

Nils Tersteegen, Marketing Manager, Fanuc Germany, Neuhausen auf den Fildern, outlined why connectivity is an industry-wide topic.

"Connectivity is an essential tool to make factories more efficient and production safer. Fanuc has been a partner of the umati initiative from the beginning, as we believe that an independent interface will allow machines, robots and peripherals to grow together and provide added value to all. With umati, Fanuc controllers, machines and robots can be quickly and efficiently integrated into our customers' IT environment," he said.

Show visitors could follow what was happening via a QR code attached to each device connected to the umati demonstrator. The display showed the data flowing directly into the umati.app, where it is represented with data from other devices, making it available to users in a uniform manner.

Software providers could also patch themselves into this data flow and demonstrate the benefits of their products in real time based on actual data from the EMO Hannover 2023 machine pool.

"In an evolving technological landscape, our concept of Industry 5.0 represents a pivotal juncture in the digitalisation software industry. umati keeps us updated on OPC UA Standard evolution while putting us in contact with machine builders and increases our international visibility through effective communication and trade show exposure. Together, we fuel the change to Industry 5.0 and help SMEs around the world manage digital transformation quickly and effortlessly," commented Retuner's managing director, Guido Colombo. The software provider is also an umati partner.

## Three new developments

Umati also presented three further new developments at EMO: umati and MT Connect, the U.S. standardisation initiative, have resolved to join forces.

In addition, the seamless communication of manufacturing data from machines via OPC UA to the Asset Administration Shell (AAS) was demonstrated with the Industrial Digital Twin Association (IDTA) and the Institute for Control Technology of Machine Tools and Manufacturing Systems (ISW) at the University of Stuttgart.

Finally, the VDMA, VDW, Deutsche Telekom and various other partners demonstrated a federated data exchange space for monitoring the service life of cutting tools, as a reference prototype for the German government's Manufacturing-X initiative (which is now gaining momentum).

"Umati therefore proves that functioning solutions can be developed within a short space of time together with the relevant partners in a standardised, open and neutral manner," summarised Dr. Alexander Broos.

## About umati

Umati is supported by VDW and VDMA and is based on OPC UA, a framework for communication among shop-floor devices. Standardised data models defined in OPC UA Companion Specifications can be extended to include customer or vendor-specific data. Around 25 Companion Specifications have already been published for various engineering technologies such as robotics, measuring systems, plastics and rubber machines, woodworking, machine tools etc. Thirty more are currently in the pipeline. In addition, the central “OPC UA for Machinery” Companion Specification contains basic building blocks (such as identification, job control and energy monitoring) that are crucial for the entire machinery and plant engineering industry.
