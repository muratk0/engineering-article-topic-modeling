# Customised HMI options

Human machine interface (HMI) products for applications in agriculture; marine, military and aerospace sectors are available from APEM Components.

Products include toggle; rocker; slide and push-button switches. Its keyboard manufacturing facilities can provide bespoke control panels. Its IDEC range for machine safety applications includes light guards; laser scanning; machine interlocks; power supplies and emergency stop switches.

The company manufactures a wide range of joysticks (from thumb stick to hand grip). Hall-effect; CAN; USB or simple digital outputs are all supported and a team of engineers is available to provide customisation support.

The range of LED indicators covers single; bi-colour; tri-colour and RGB. A wide selection of panel cut-outs is also offered.
