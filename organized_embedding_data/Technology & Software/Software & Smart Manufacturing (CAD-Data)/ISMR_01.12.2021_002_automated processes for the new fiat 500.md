# Automated processes for the new Fiat 500

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

Comau has contributed to the production of the New Fiat 500 by providing FCA and the Fiat brand with advanced automation solutions and help in the development of innovative industrial processes to produce FCA’s first fully electric vehicle, a ‘Made in Turin’ product.

“The path started symbolically in July 2019 with the installation of the first Comau robot, which initiated the production line of the new fully-electric model at FCA’s historic Mirafiori plant. A total of 187 Comau robots were then integrated into the different phases of the car manufacturing process by a team of Comau specialists working side-by-side with the automaker’s designers to optimise production lines in an excellent example of Smart Manufacturing. The team also needed to adapt the systems to the new body design of an iconic car, while meeting the technical requirements of a fully-electric vehicle,” Comau told ISMR.

“We developed and equipped the body assembly lines dedicated to processing the structural parts of the New 500, including the front and rear floors, the bodysides and the frame, which are assembled and welded to give shape to the complete body of the car,” it added.

Each robot has been fully equipped by Comau to carry out all the special processes required by an electric car from custom-designed handling systems and grippers to a special 3D-printed end-effector for moving body components.

Comau has also integrated the lines with advanced vision systems to control the quality of the adhesive dispensing system, both during processing and at the end of the cycle. To optimise and speed up in-line tool changes, an articulated magazine system was designed to provide the robots with the different types of tools required to perform each application rapidly and easily.

In addition, Comau provided part of the design of the robotised line for the final assembly. After the bodyshop (forming the body of the New 500) and the paint and surface treatment phases, all the internal parts can be assembled and single components of the car are optimised.
