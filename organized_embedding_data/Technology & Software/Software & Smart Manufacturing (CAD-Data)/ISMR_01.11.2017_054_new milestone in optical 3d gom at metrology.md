# New milestone in optical 3D GOM AT metrology

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

IN RESPONSE TO growing quality requirements and the need for faster measurements, companies are now opting for automated measuring and inspection systems GOM showcased its optical 3D measuring machine, ATOS ScanBox, at this year's Blechexpo in Stuttgart, Germany. It also displayed mobile measurement systems that are portable and can be operated close to production. These included PONTOS Live for online measurement and part positioning, ATOS Compact Scan for full-field measurement on the shop floor and TRITOP, a mobile coordinate measurement machine for large-volume objects and tools.

With the ARAMIS 3D camera, GOM presented a new sensor for materials and components' testing. The ARAMIS system combines point-based and fullfield deformation measurement in one single system. This makes the 3D motion and deformation sensor an industrial standard measuring system when it comes to 3D testing. The system provides 3D displacements, 3D strains, velocities, accelerations as well as 6DoF evaluations of samples and components under load.

In the ATOS ScanBox standardised measuring cell, the entire measuring and no prior knowledge of metrology are able to operate the system. The nine different models of the series are suitable for different part sizes.

"The core of all measuring cells is a robot-driven 3D scanner in the ATOS series. which is used for non-contact and threedimensional measuring of parts. The standard software solution, Virtual Measuring Room (VMR), simulates the real measurement cell environment, making it possible to display movements during the measurement process in virtual simulations. The robot can be controlled via drag & drop commands without a control panel. The measurement and inspection process can be prepared based on CAD data without the need for a real part. The ATOS ScanBox models have been developed specifically for production-related inspection and quality assurance processes, and are already used by numerous companies including Daimler, VW, Rolls-Royce, Bosch, Honeywell, Samsung, Automotive Lighting and ZF," said GOM.

ATOS Compact Scan fits into one suitcase and can be easily transported. Due to its changeable measuring areas, the sensor enables digitisation and inspection of small parts up to large tools. GOM's TRITOP system, a coordinate measuringmachine that captures single 3D coordinates of large-volume objects, can be hand-held or used wirelessly. See www.gom.com
