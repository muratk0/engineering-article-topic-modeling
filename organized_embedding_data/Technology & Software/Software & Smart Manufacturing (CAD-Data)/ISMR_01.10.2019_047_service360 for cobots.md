# Service360 for cobots

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

UNIVERSAL ROBOTS (UR), the collaborative robot (cobot) manufacturer, has launched Service360, a new support offering that enhances performance, increases uptime and mitigates risk through seamless collaboration.

"Our customers are at the centre of everything. Now, we're empowering them to proactively monitor, optimise and safeguard cobot performance with enhanced support and preventative maintenance," said Chris Wilson, Vice President of Service, Universal Robots. "Service360 protects production lines, maximises uptime, reduces risk and enables success by ensuring that manufacturers are prepared for the unexpected."

Service360 was launched in North America at Universal Robots' Pack Expo booth 7279 in Las Vegas (USA) from 23-25 September 2019. Complementing the standard product warranty, and available in two different packages, Service360 equips UR customers to:

- Mitigate risk and take control of unexpected expenses by eliminating unplanned service costs and reducing downtime and delays.
- Maximise cobot ROI through remote and onsite performance and programming checks, environment assessments and access to UR's certified engineers, automation specialists and 24/7 customer support portal.
- Keep production running with preventative services and rapid-response visits.
- Recover rapidly in the event of the unexpected with access to service, parts and replacements through UR's Global Distributor Network.

Service360 is also designed to enable UR's partners to create stronger customer relationships and improve retention through more structured communication, tailored services contracts, improved support options and ongoing performance optimisation.

“Our partners will add even more value by equipping customers with everything they need to increase productivity, availability and ROI for their customers," said Chris Wilson. "We expect this new offering to strengthen relationships and create shared value across UR partners and customers."

AT THIS YEAR'S BLECHEXPO, Bihler will present an innovative automation solution for the production of shielding sleeves in electric car charging plugs – in the real world and in the virtual world.

"Shielding sleeves protect conductive or surrounding components from electric and magnetic fields. The GRM-NC servo stamping and forming machine with modular LEANTOOL progressive system is a suitable platform for the efficient production of these safety components: with very short time-to-market, excellent process stability and extremely high productivity. The powerful servo machine at our stand produces 170 shielding sleeves per minute. Variant changes can be carried out in minutes and 100 per cent reproduced by calling up the stored data in the VC 1 control system," explained the company.

"Our tool technology features a straightforward design and a high degree of standardisation. The LEANTOOL progressive system on the exhibition machine consists of three standardised modules with 250mm each. Sixty per cent of the lean bending tool are reusable standard parts and 40 per cent are individual tool parts. This significantly shortens implementation times and makes the tool particularly cost-effective," it added.

“In contrast to production in presses, the 60mm wide and 0.3mm thick brass strip does not have to be lifted out of the bending tool. The lean tool is clearly structured and easily accessible. Tool movements take place from three sides through the machine. Trade show visitors will also find a new feature in the 400kN servo press of the GRM-NC: a standardised stamping and forming frame with Meusburger standard parts of the 'spring-loaded guide plate' type. The standardised stamping and forming frame is suitable for use with precise parts and large batches.

“Visitors to our stand can also discover the strengths of the GRM-NC and the flexible LEANTOOL application possibilities in the virtual world. The highlight of the presentation is the simple one-to-one porting of the LEANTOOL progressive system to other production systems such as the BIMERIC platform or a BZ high-speed system," commented the company.

"After the LEANTOOL bending tool, further assembly processes can be integrated on the BIMERIC to produce ready-to-install assemblies. The BZ machine with extremely high cycle rates of up to 500 1/min is ideal for capacity expansion and mass production. The GRM-NC can be quickly converted to the radial LEANTOOL version for other tasks using the automatic alignment of the NC units and quick-action tool clamping systems."

In addition to shielding sleeves, Bihler servo technology is also used to produce battery cell connectors, ZSB circuits, busbars and hairpins for electric cars. These hairpin-like components are responsible for the safe and efficient power transmission in electric car motors. They are manufactured on a BIMERIC production system with standardised machining processes – directly from coils of both round wire and flat wire. The system combines all process steps such as highly dynamic feeding of the wire, cutting, NC-controlled mechanical stripping of the coating, simultaneous chamfering of the pin ends, pre-bending and 3D die bending as well as the final measurement of the component geometry and inline readjustment. Variant changes take place “on the fly" i.e. simply by calling up the stored data. Depending upon the variant, the output is between 60 and 120 precision hairpins per minute.
