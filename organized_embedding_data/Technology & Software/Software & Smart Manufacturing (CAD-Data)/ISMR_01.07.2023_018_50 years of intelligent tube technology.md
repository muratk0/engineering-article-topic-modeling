# 50 years of intelligent tube technology

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Unison Ltd, the UK-based specialist in allelectric tube manipulation, celebrated its 50th anniversary at its Scarborough headquarters on 24 May 2023.

"Just short of 100 customers, colleagues, friends and members of the 'Made In Yorkshire' trade group attended the event, which began with a hearty brunch and a warm welcome by our joint managing director, Alan Pickering," the company told ISMR.

Technology talks and machine-tool presentations followed. These included demonstrations of Unison's all-electric Breeze range of tube bending machines, as well as fibre laser cutting machines from Unison's sister company, Nukon Lasers UK. Unison's newly released Opt2Sim Scan hand-held 3D tube scanning system, the latest addition to the company's advanced Opt2Sim Suite of tube simulation software, was also showcased. The event concluded with a buffet lunch, followed by ice creams.

Established in 1973, Unison Ltd is a leading UK manufacturer of tube and pipe bending machines and continuously innovates in the tube and pipe bending marketplace.

"We manufactured the world's first allelectric tube bender in 1994, followed by the world's first all-electric multi-stack tube bender, then the world's largest all-electric tube bender for the shipbuilding industry. Available in single-stack, multi-stack and right/left varieties, our machines are delivered to more than 20 countries. Our tube bending software is a very user-friendly control system for tube bending machines," concluded Unison.
