# Schuler Automation receives its largest order to date – three lines for the blanking of outer body panels for FAW-VW (China)

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Manufacturing more than 3000 vehicles bearing the VW and Audi brands everyday, FAW-VW is one of China's largest car producers”

It is the biggest order in the history of Schuler Automation, a subsidiary of the Schuler Group: FAW-VW Automobile Co., Ltd. - a joint venture which includes Volkswagen AG in China – has ordered three lines for the blanking of outer body panels. The lines must meet stringent requirements for output performance and quality.

Design and manufacturing of the lines is currently in full swing at the company’s facility in Hessdorf, Germany. The first line is expected to be ready for operation in Changchun as early as November 2012, while the other two lines for facilities in Chengdu and Foshan will follow in January and February 2013.

## An accent on lightweight materials

As weight reduction is becoming increasingly important for car manufacturers, many production facilities are currently being technically upgraded so they can process aluminium, for example.

“The Chinese market offers tremendous potential and is thus particularly important for us,” stress Bernhard Kraus and Stephan Mergner, the general managers of Schuler Automation Hessdorf.

FAW-VW Automobile Co., Ltd. is a joint venture of the FAW Group Corporation, Volkswagen AG, Audi AG and the Chinese Volkswagen Automobile Investment Co., Ltd., which was founded in 1991. Manufacturing more than 3000 vehicles bearing the VW and Audi brands every day, FAW-VW is one of China’s largest car producers. The company also has additional capacities for exporting vehicles, components and drive systems.

“In order to meet our deadline, we’re building the three lines ordered by FAW-VW almost in parallel,” explains Martin Liebel, head of the blanking line division at Schuler Automation in Hessdorf. The current order comprises three blanking lines each equipped with an 800-metric-ton monoblock servo press. The blanks will later be used to form doors, roofs or engine hoods.

In addition to steel, two of the lines can also process such lightweight materials as highstrength steels and aluminium, while the other is already prepared for it. Thanks to their higher output levels, however, all three will be much more productive than their predecessors.

“The coil speed will increase from 90 metres per minute to as much as 110 metres per minute,” says Martin Liebel.

## An eye on quality

Numerous details in the blanking line are designed to ensure this level of quality – such as the use of optimised acceleration profiles for feeding the material; Schuler’s ServoDirect Technology (SDT) to ensure that the press adjusts flexibly to the respective material strength and die requirements and the Stop2Drop process which guarantees gentle stacking of the finished blanks.

The high quality standard is all the more important as the blanking process is at the very beginning of the production process – and only flawless blanks without any scratches or shadows are considered for further processing.

“After all, FAW-VW produces models such as the Golf or Audi A4” states Liebel. The company therefore uses the same production processes around the world and chose the same blanking lines as Schuler had already installed at many of its German facilities. www.schulergroup.com
