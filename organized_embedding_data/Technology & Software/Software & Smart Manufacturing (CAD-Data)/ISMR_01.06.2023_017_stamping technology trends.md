# Stamping technology trends

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

The latest trends in stamping technology and networking in the industry were the focus of the 13th Stamping Technology Congress in Dortmund, Germany, organised by KIST e.V. and the utg. Representatives from industry and research met for lectures, discussions and exchanges on current topics in stamping technology from 17-18 April 2023. Two well received contributions provided particular impetus.

Lectures at the conference focused on new stamping research and work; electromobility (manufacturing new components for the automotive industry); smart tooling; human resources and networked processes (planning, simulation and monitoring). Innovations, application examples and scientific findings were presented in 13 specialist lectures. The panel discussion topic was “New Work- New Working Worlds."

“Focal points of the discussion among representatives of industry and associations included recruiting employees when there is a shortage of skilled workers, future-oriented working models and employee motivation in every phase of life," said the conference organiser.

The event also included a trade exhibition in the stadium of the Bundesliga team 'Borussia' in Dortmund. Around 250 participants attended the event and preparations will soon be underway for the 14th Stanztechnik Congress in 2024.
