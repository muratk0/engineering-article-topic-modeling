# Augmented Reality demonstration

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

FANUC AMERICA, a leading supplier of robotics, CNCs and ROBOMACHINEs, demonstrated its award-winning Zero Down Time (ZDT) Industrial IoT solution at Automate 2019 in Chicago recently.

It welcomed visitors to a new Augmented Reality (AR) experience featuring a variety of manufacturing situations where ZDT can help. The experience included six common manufacturing challenges printed on two graphic display panels that simulate manufacturing facilities. Each story featured a 3D image with markers that connect to a mobile APP.

"The value of ZDT applies to every manufacturer," said Joe Gazzarato, Director of Engineering, ZDT Product Development, FANUC America. "No matter what industry you're in, the prospect of maintaining consistent production uptime and reducing costs equates to a competitive advantage - which is what ZDT offers our customers."

"ZDT's predictive analytics have already saved millions of dollars by preventing unexpected down time in the automotive industry. In addition to reducing unexpected down time, ZDT allows users to maximize production throughput, optimise maintenance costs, increase the life of their robots and access data from anywhere via the ZDT web portal," added the company.

"Since its introduction in 2015, ZDT analytics have successfully identified potential equipment issues, and notified customers to take corrective actions and avoid unexpected down time in over 600 cases, saving companies more than US$ 75 million."

Users can access FANUC's ZDT Augmented Reality experience by downloading the ZDT Experience brochure to a PC or laptop, texting ZDT28 to 43506 to download and install the ZDT Augmented Reality APP or scanning each interactive element to learn how ZDT can help to maximize efficiency.
