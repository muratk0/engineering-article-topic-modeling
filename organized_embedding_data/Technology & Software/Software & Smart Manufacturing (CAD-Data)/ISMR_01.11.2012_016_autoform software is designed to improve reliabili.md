# AutoForm software is designed to improve reliability in planning, reducing the number of die tryouts and tryout time, so that higher quality part and tool designs can be produced with confidence.

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Product designers, formability engineers, draw development experts, process layout specialists as well as tooling, stamping and manufacturing engineers in sheet metal markets, in major industries such as automotive, all use AutoForm software”

AutoForm Engineering GmbH is a leading supplier of software solutions for the diemaking and sheet metal forming industry along the entire process chain. With around 250 specialists around the world, it supplies all of the Top 20 automotive OEMs and most of their suppliers. Besides its headquarters in Switzerland where most of its software is developed, AutoForm has subsidiaries in Germany, The Netherlands, France, Spain, Italy, USA, Mexico, Brazil, India, China, Japan and Korea. It is also present through its agents in more than 15 other countries.

## Left: Dr. Markus Thomma,

Corporate Marketing Director, AutoForm Engineering Right: AutoFormplus R4 development was driven by the vision of a Comprehensive Digital Process Planning system. It allows users to quantify and evaluate all planning aspects of stamped sheet metal parts, such as function, quality, lead time and cost.

The company has kept its sights and focus firmly set on meeting its customers’ requirements in the sheet metal forming industry. Its software is designed to improve reliability in planning, reducing the number of die tryouts and tryout time, so that higher quality part and tool designs can be produced with confidence. An important by-product is the reduction of press downtimes and production reject rates.

## Sheet metal forming in focus

AutoForm Engineering has continuously evolved its portfolio of software products. An important issue in the industry, it says, is the gradual loss of skills and expertise from an ageing and retiring workforce and the lack of sufficient trained personnel entering the sheet metal industry to replace that knowledge. Software systems are increasingly stepping into the breach to provide user-friendly interfaces and the promise of seamless operational benefits even for less experienced personnel.

The automotive world faces major challenges such as CO2 emissions reduction and fuel savings, weight reduction, material consumption optimisation of the body-in-white and ever-increasing safety regulations.
