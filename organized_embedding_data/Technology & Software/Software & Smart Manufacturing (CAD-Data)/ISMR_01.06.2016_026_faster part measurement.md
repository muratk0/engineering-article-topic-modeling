# Faster part measurement

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

CREAFORM, a specialist in portable 3D measurement solutions and engineering services, has just announced the latest release of VXinspect, its intuitive dimensional inspection software for manufacturing companies that conduct first article inspection and quality control on the production line or shop floor.

Launched in parallel with the new, re-designed MetraSCAN 3D optical CMM scanner and HandyPROBE Next arm-free portable CMM, VXinspect's 3D inspection software upgrade ensures tighter integration with Creaform's hardware for a complete, end-to-end inspection solution. HandySCAN 3D also seamlessly integrates with VXinspect, the company told ISMR.

Some of VXinspect's latest new features include smart control; optical automatic alignment to facilitate the setup and positioning of a part with Creaform's C-Track optical tracker; easy colour mapping and boundary inspection improvements. VXinspect now also supports sheet metal workflows. VXinspect users can also benefit from new alignment methods and other workflow enhancements.

## Compact notcher

AT THE Coil Winding Expo in Berlin (CWIEME), Schuler unveiled its newly developed notcher. The Performer S is capable of producing laminations for electric motors and generators in a wide variety of sizes – it can notch blanks with diameters of 80 to 1800mm.

"The large tooling area makes it easy to use a controlled die, while the notcher's modular construction ensures short set-up times. The blankholder can be supplied as a retractable centring unit or blank ejector. With up to 1200 strokes per minute, the 200-kilonewton notcher is also up to 20% faster than our previous model. With a notch accuracy of one hundredth of a millimetre, it guarantees the necessary precision – especially for thin sheets with low blanking clearance. This is helped by the machine's rigid construction and the play-free, pre-stressed roller guidance of the slide," explained Schuler.

"The machine has virtually no hydraulics and is operated with oil-free air. The use of wear-free seals ensures low maintenance costs and a high level of availability. Thanks to a counterbalance moving in the opposite direction, the foundation forces are very low and the compact notcher can be placed above a cellar."
