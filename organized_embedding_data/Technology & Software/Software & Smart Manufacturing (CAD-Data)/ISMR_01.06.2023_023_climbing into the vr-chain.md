# Climbing into the VR-Chain

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

The German Federal Ministry of Education and Research (BMBF) is subsidising the VR-Chain project, to implement and improve collaboration and training scenarios in virtual space. AERO-LIFT Vakuumtechnik GmbH is involved, as an application company, along with VETTER Krantechnik GmbH.

Using virtual reality, the research team will develop an interactive experience space that is systematically linked to upstream and downstream work processes and practices within a value chain. This can establish the flow of knowledge along the value chain and enable the participating companies to react flexibly and 'situationally' to market, production and procurement changes.

“Our vacuum lifting devices are often combined with crane technology from other manufacturers to create a complete solution for the customer. For the development of such a complete solution, consideration of all combination and connection possibilities, as well as an exact interface definition, is essential," commented AERO-LIFT Vakuumtechnik GmbH.

“The VR Chain project aims to optimise the high level of coordination and time required between the departments involved (sales; project planning; design; purchasing; production; service) within the company (as well as across companies) in the direction of the supplier and customer. A seamless flow of information is the basis for success and should be enabled by the VR-Chain solution," it added.

Next, the technical team will develop a technical and content-related concept on the basis of which a VR and scanning solution can be jointly developed and tested in an iterative and demand-oriented manner.
