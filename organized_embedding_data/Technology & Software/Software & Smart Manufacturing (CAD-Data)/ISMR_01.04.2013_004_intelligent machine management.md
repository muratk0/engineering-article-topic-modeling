# Intelligent machine management

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

MECHA-TRONIKA is a new exhibition dedicated to the intelligent management of machines, processes and equipment in the manufacturing sector. It will take place from 23-26 October 2013 at the Fieramilano exhibition centre in Italy.

The event, organised by EFIM-Ente Fiere Italiane Macchine and Fiera Milano, is promoted and sponsored by the official representatives of the sectors on exhibition: AIdAM, the Italian association of mechatronic automation; ASSODEL, the national association of electronics suppliers; GISI, the association of Italian instrumentation companies; IMVG, the Italian machine vision group; SIRI, the Italian association of robotics and automation and UCIMU-Sistemi Per Produrre, the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots, automation systems and auxiliary products.

"MECHA-TRONIKA takes on the heritage of AmmTech and Bimec, two events that, following the development of the manufacturing sector and the production needs of companies, have now been joined into one single exhibition. This will present a much wider range of opportunities than the two individual events could separately," explained Alessandro Torsoli, President of AIdam – the Italian association of mechatronic automation.

"The Italian industrial automation sector has finally got an international showcase in its own territory, to promote and present its production range to a qualified public. In 2013 MECHA-TRONIKA will be the only specialised event in Europe that will involve the member companies of AIdAM and IMVG.”

"On a daily basis companies must be able to meet more and more stringent manufacturing, flexibility and product quality requirements. With the mechatronic approach, now implemented in all modern production sectors, mechanical, electrical and information technology elements are considered jointly,” added Luigi Galdabini, President of UCIMU-Sistemi Per Produrre, the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots and automation systems.

“This obviously means that an integration of the competencies of the various application sectors is necessary, with a systemic vision of the design process, to produce machines that are more functional, efficient, compact and reliable, as well as cheaper. Mechatronics is the innovative element for the production of machine tools with better performance. It represents the way forward in the optimisation of the way our plants are managed, and this is why I think the spotlight should be on this sector. MECHA-TRONIKA is the first step in this direction.” ■
