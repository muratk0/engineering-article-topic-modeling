# Autonomous bending aids productivity

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

HAEUSLER AG's autonomous bending process is designed to offer companies in the metal processing sector the opportunity to increase their productivity and competitiveness whilst reducing costs.

"We will be introducing a groundbreaking, metalforming innovation for our EVO machine series. For the first time, the EVO machines will enable accurate and repeatable bending results without scrap (regardless of material, dimension and shape) thanks to a sophisticated measuring system, precise hardware and intelligent software," the bending specialist told ISMR.

"Our EVO machines offer numerous advantages that make the everyday work of companies and operators more efficient. In an era of increased production costs due to raw material shortages, as well as increased energy prices, the EVO machine series can make a valuable contribution to reducing production costs," added the manufacturer.

"The intelligent technology makes it possible to compensate for material fluctuations and therefore increase independence in supplier selection. Production can be quickly adapted to new product requirements, which further increases flexibility."

At the heart of the EVO machine series is the BENDtronic® control system, which enables an autonomous bending process using intelligent technology. The control system acts as an “inside operator" so that machine operators can handle several machines or concentrate on other important tasks.

"Thanks to their user-friendly design, programming and operating EVO machines is also easier which reduces the amount of training and familiarisation time. Precision of the autonomously bent sheet reduces processing times in subsequent operations (such as welding). By eliminating manual straightening, welding can be done directly with the robot," explained HAEUSLER.

Designers can develop products with extended design freedom on EVO machines.

"Complex shapes can be bent from any radius without edges. This makes it possible to increase the efficiency of the product, for example by reducing tolerances or optimising shapes. Overall, autonomous bending technology enables companies to push their products to the physical limit and achieve optimal results while minimising the use of resources," concluded the company.
