Open platform controls numerous robot brands

Ready Robotics has launched Forge/OS 5, an open, cross-brand operating system for industrial automation. With support for FANUC, Yaskawa, ABB, Staubli, Epson, and UR robots, the platform offers a single programming interface for hundreds of models of collaborative and industrial robots, as well as the peripherals required for automation.

The platform lets companies manage their automation without needing deep expertise in robot programming and reduces complexity for those with multiple brands of robots and peripherals on the production floor. Grippers and other peripheral hardware are straightforward to integrate, and any robot powered by the platform can be programmed by operators using Task Canvas, Ready’s no-code programming app.

Plug-and-play platform brings Industry 4.0 connectivity to midsized manufacturers

Magic Software Enterprises Ltd. offers FactoryEye, powered by a plug-andplay IIoT platform. The system collects real-time data from existing machinery and operational systems and transforms it into actionable intelligence for immediate results and continuous improvement in the manufacturing process. It brings Industry 4.0 connectivity to midsized manufacturers in the metal processing, automotive, medical device, and specialty manufacturing markets.

The system collects data from automated and semiautomated machines and incorporates advanced analytics and AI into decision support. Customer KPIs are used to measure and qualify results.

Magic Software

www.magicsoftware.com

Web-based dashboard analyzes quality data to highlight trends in production, supplier quality

A new web-based dashboard enabling the rapid analysis of global quality data has been launched by Hexagon’s Manufacturing Intelligence division to help quality professionals identify trends in declining quality for parts and assemblies and investigate the issues relating to specific components, suppliers, projects, and locations. With the Q-DAS eMMA Q-Board, quality professionals can analyze aggregate data at a glance from any web browser and identify cases that require urgent intervention.

Manufacturers that build complex assemblies must ensure that final products can be seamlessly constructed from thousands of parts from suppliers and in-house production facilities around the world. The new dashboard presents an overview of a site and project from which the user can drill down into hierarchical layers of detail about a project’s components. The data aggregation capabilities simplify the comparison of design data with inspection data across several locations.

Software suite targets shop connectivity

Cambrio has released Version 21 of the Sigma-Nest suite for fabricators. Sigma-Suite aims to close the loop on manufacturing connectivity targeting "The Connected Shop"—scheduling, quoting, and logistics informed by nesting intelligence. This focus, along with CAD/CAM enhancements, strengthens the software’s capabilities, the company states.

Shop connectivity bridges CRM, ordering, inventory, production, delivery, and accounting. New software products and enhancements to the business systems and shop floor products have expanded this integration for the software’s users.

The debut of SigmaSchedule brings dynamic scheduling to fabricators, allowing them to plan projects with nested programs, primary cutting operations, and secondary operations such as bending. The software can be configured for specific business needs but has the flexibility to adjust the work flow plan instantly. Users can schedule forward for the quickest timeline (as soon as possible) or backward from a fixed due date (JIT).

Three-roll plate roller produces pipe that meets API standards

Faccin has developed an electrowelded three-roll plate rolling machine that includes variableaxis configuration, antideflection systems for the top roll and bottom rolls, and an automatic pipe ejection system. It handles pipe lengths up to 40 ft.

This CNC bending roll supports the production of pipes, reaching specific technical and roundness tolerances while offering a finished product that meets API standards, states the manufacturer.

A postbending machine that prepares the pipe for welding can be added, as well as a series of outboard motorized guide rollers that control the movement of the pipe from the plate rolling machine to the postbending machine and successive workstations.
