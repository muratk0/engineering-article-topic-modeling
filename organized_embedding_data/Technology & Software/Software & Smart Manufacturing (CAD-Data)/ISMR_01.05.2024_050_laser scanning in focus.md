# Laser scanning in focus

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

SCANLAB's XL SCAN, which was jointly developed with ACS Motion Control, processes workpieces of "practically unlimited size" by synchronously combining a 2D scan head with an XY positioning stage (where the scanner and stages are continuously moving during laser processing).

Its latest integration partner, based in Silicon Valley, is Turner Laser Systems which provides turnkey customisable machines and process development services in the United States.

"The XL SCAN solution can improve feature resolution and throughput of micromachining of larger-sized parts by synchronously controlling a 2D scan head and two-axis XY stage motion. It provides precision within a virtually limitless working area, which is a key feature for various applications in electronics manufacturing," highlighted SCANLAB.

All partners base their service offer on the technology partnership with ACS Motion Control and the software provider, Direct Machining Control (DMC).

SCANLAB GmbH is an independent OEM manufacturer of scanning solutions for deflecting and positioning laser beams in three dimensions. At the company's headquarters in Puchheim (near Munich, Germany), around 500 employees work on continuous innovation and a production capacity of over 40,000 systems per year. Its main R&D and manufacturing site is also based in Puchheim.
