# ERP software advances

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

MIE SOLUTIONS, a manufacturing ERP software provider, has announced the release of MIE File Reader, an advanced file conversion capability for MIE Trak Pro.

“MIE Trak Pro software now has the ability to take PDF purchase order files from a customer and automatically convert them into sales orders. The ability to parse a customer purchase order into its relevant pieces of information and automatically create a sales order is a massive time-saver. Mistakes are also eliminated because no typing is required. Just simply drop the PDF file into a folder on your network and the order can be imported effortlessly into MIE Trak Pro,” explained the company.

The ability to process files is not limited to PDFs as users can now drop any consistent file from a customer, including PDF, Excel, CSV or a standard text file, onto their networks and they can be imported directly into MIE Trak Pro.

Another possible use of this functionality is to obtain a list of jobs that are to be shipped that day and simply drop those jobs into a folder on the network. Packing slips will be automatically created without any data entry.

## Filtration for waterjet cutting

THE NEW WOMA WTA Eco filters out fine residual suspended matter. Using a new flocculant, the WTA Eco water treatment system enables waterjet cutting system operators to discharge their wastewater into the sewage in an eco-friendly way that complies with statutory regulations, the company confirmed. The WTA Eco can be retrofitted into existing filtration system at any time.

“In waterjet cutting of metal, stone or plastic, solids and suspended matter occur in the wastewater, along with oil added for the cutting process. Solids can be removed easily by means of an extractor or other techniques. That has not previously been the case with oil and suspended matter dissolved in the water. They could not be filtered out and the wastewater failed, in many cases, to comply with the threshold values set by Germany’s Water Resources Act. With the aid of the Woma WTA Eco water treatment system, even fine dirt and suspended matter can be removed before the wastewater is discharged into the sewage,” said Woma.

The system is fitted after the extractor and the sedimentation tank, both of which only filter solids out of the water. The Woma WTA Eco adds a flocculant that binds residual suspended matter and small droplets of oil. They are then caught in the system’s upstream sand filter.

## Single-point, multi-slide motion mechanical press

INGYU PRECISION Industries Co., Ltd. is a Taiwanese high speed press manufacturer, established for over 40 years. It provides high precision machines for can-making, lamination and complex forming lines.

“We are proud to introduce our single-point, multi-slide motion stamping presses, in capacities from 45 to 300 tons. The new Saga has been designed to meet customers’ requirements for running dies with the closest tolerances,” explained INGYU.

“With a one-piece frame and rugged plunger and post guiding system, the press’s vertical accuracy is ≤ 0.01mm for extended die life and improved part quality. The new drive technology enables lower power consumption and provides higher torque for press applications. Slide velocity decreases towards BDC to reduce the impact of stamping, noise and vibration. There are three different slide motion modes available for various product applications.”

In forming mode, pressing time is longer, compared to crank presses, for optimum material flattening results. In bending mode, the slide velocity decreases while bending the material and speeds up again to gain quick cycles. In drawing mode, consistent rapid pressing avoids uneven part surfaces.
