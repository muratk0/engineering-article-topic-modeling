# Chin Fong Machine Industrial Co., Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

https://chinfong.com/en/

Taiwanese press specialist, Chin Fong, has placed smart machinery at the heart of its technological innovations. From a single-press smart solution, the company has moved on to multi-press connections and cloud computing, linking ERP management and even AI computing systems to analyse Big Data.

It offers machine selection consulting services, combined with its "iForming PMS - Intelligent Forming Productivity Management System" solution. In response to ever-expanding customer requirements, the company has also implemented scanning code and fingerprint authentication to meet workplace safety requirements, as well as facial recognition and an iris recognition system.

“iForming is an intelligent forming solution which enables customers to quickly and cost-effectively introduce intelligent applications. Whether it is a generic stamping press, transfer press, forging press or servo press, all can be integrated into one platform, enabling Chin Fong presses to provide customers with a smart manufacturing solution for forming," explained Chin Fong.

## Biele Group

https://biele.com/

Biele Group develops turnkey stacking solutions for press-, shear- and laser-blanking lines. These solutions include magnetic, vacuum and robot stackers as well as handling and packaging systems for the steel and aluminium sectors.

"Biele Metal is the division focused on the metalautomotive sector for sheet-metal processing, handling and packaging, either of cold-rolled or hot-rolled steel, stainless steel or aluminium. These automation solutions include highproductivity lines for the stacking and packaging of metal components relating to cutting and pressing processes in the automotive, metal service centre and OEM sectors," it explained.

The Biele Automotive division designs and manufactures lines for vehicle interior component production in the automotive sector. Its lines range from solutions-based on one single press or several presses for thermoforming, coating and cutting workpieces to the supply of fully automated lines.

"We always work in close co-operation with our customers, enabling us to produce projects suited to their needs," it added.

## Cisam Impianti Press Feeding Equipment

https://cisamimpianti.com

Italian manufacturer Cisam Impianti offers a complete range of machines for coil processing. Its product range includes compact coilfeeding and long feeding lines as well as lines processing high-strength steel and high-thickness materials. It also manufactures lines using the zig-zag system; fine blanking lines; cut-tolength lines; laser- and TIG-welding machines as well as microfeeders, transfer systems and destackers. Equipment can be customised to user preferences.

Its main products are coil-feeding lines for various presses (hydraulic, mechanical and servo). Its lines for high-resistance materials for the automotive sector increasingly process highstrength steels (even over 1200N/mm²). Straightening rollers eliminate the internal tensions of the material through plastic deformation. The lines can be equipped with a cassettechange system with different banks of rollers.

Its TIG- or laser-welding machines are designed for head and tail strip welding and are designed to reduce the amount of material waste.
