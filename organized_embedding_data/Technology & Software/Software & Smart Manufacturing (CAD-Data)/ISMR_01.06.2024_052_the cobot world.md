# The cobot world

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Since 1994, Cobots Online have been developing industrial cobots to automate and streamline repetitive industrial processes.

"We understand the challenges and opportunities facing organisations from our own experience as a family-owned manufacturing company. Our team of experts work closely with manufacturers to configure and deploy cobots, accelerating their automation capability and increasing their operational flexibility and scalability," it explained.

Developed with high-mix, low-volume production processes in mind, cobots have revolutionised the world of automation for SMEs. Their versatility provides cost-effective, flexible, user-friendly solutions that companies of any size and technical experience can utilise. Cobots work with manufacturers to streamline processes, increase productivity, improve quality and proactively respond to changing customer demands.

As a Certified System Integrator, Cobots Online has been certified to offer automation solutions utilising Universal Robots' collaborative robot arms, providing manufacturers with support from concept through to deployment.

## CoboTend

"The CoboTend is an easy-to-programme and simple-to-customise, self-contained mobile cobot unit, enabling the user to lift and relocate where and when required. CoboTend features a single button press to remove and relocate different end effectors or grippers. Measure downtime between other jobs in single minutes, and not hours. CoboTend automates and streamlines repetitive industrial processes, increasing safety for workers and freeing them up to work on high-value, more profitable tasks," outlined Cobots Online.

## CoboWeld

"When precision and speed are key to success, CoboWeld adds to a production line's operating efficiency, increasing throughput and reducing quality issues. Linear, pipe, multi-pass and weaving patterns can all be quickly and easily be achieved through hand-guidance and pre-programmed weld. CoboWeld delivers high-quality weld outputs to help businesses remain competitive and agile to changing markets and customer demands," explained Cobots Online.

CoboWeld Mobile is designed to give manufacturers the flexibility to quickly and easily reposition the unit in different weld cells or areas around the shop floor. Built into a 1200mm x 1200mm square welding table, CoboWeld Mini provides a high degree of flexibility.

Designed for small-batch production, CoboWeld Midi is built into a 2000mm x 1000mm welding table, pre-drilled with 16mm-diameter holes to accommodate a range of welding tools and clamps. Providing scope for large fabrications, or multiple smaller fabrications, to be jigged up and welded in one programme, CoboWeld Maxi is built into a 2000mm x 2000mm welding table.

## Cobo Tower and CoboDoor

Designed with a small footprint to save valuable space on the shop floor, CoboTower is primed to take on highly repetitive tasks. Designed to work alongside humans, CoboTower can be integrated anywhere and retrofitted without the need to reconfigure the production layout. Quick-change, tool-mount technology allows standard or bespoke end-of-arm tooling to be changed within seconds to reduce downtime.

The repetitive motion of opening and closing machine doors can lead to increased injuries for the operators. CoboDoor can be operated at the push of a button to take the physical strain on this manual task.

"CoboDoor can be connected to almost any brand of CNC machine. It works with single and double doors up to 400kg (1.5m, 2m, 2.5m and 3m lengths are available as standard). However, bespoke sizes can be created to suit any door as required. Automatically opening at the end of the machine cycle, CoboDoor provides a visual notification that the CNC cycle is complete, leading to improved efficiency," concluded Cobots Online.
