# Industry 4.0 and ICT in Maintenance spotlight

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

THE INTERNATIONAL INDUSTRIAL Maintenance Fair will be held at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre (BEC) in Bilbao (Spain) from 4-6 June 2019. Industry 4.0 will play an important role at MAINTENANCE, the International Industrial Maintenance Fair where attendees can discover the latest developments in ICT and smart tools applied to company inspection and maintenance. The event will cover a full range of maintenance services from inspection and monitoring to preventive and corrective maintenance.

The Maintenance conferences will focus on aspects such as Electronic Maintenance, which will address the 4.0 strategy, Big-Data, predictive analysis and smart maintenance services. There will also be a discussion on how work is done and maintenance service as part of Asset Management. Finally, in Condition Monitoring Technologies, the talk will revolve around smart sensors and data acquisition, as well as smart tools, drones and robotics applied to the sector. The lloT (Industrial Internet of Things) and Internet Condition Monitoring will also be addressed, a process to identify a significant change in the machinery which could indicate that it has a fault.

The conferences will be held in the Conference Theatre by Spri during the morning and afternoon of 5 June 2019. The Basque Business Development Agency will also offer a Basque Industry 4.0 by Spri cocktail party on 4 June 2019. Around 80% of the exhibition area has already been reserved. Companies can present their latest developments and case studies at the Speaker's Corner in the centre of the exhibition area, which is open to all trade show visitors. A technical visit to a leading company in the Basque Autonomous Community will be organised on 5 June 2019.

A new addition to this year's Maintenance fair will be the Open Meetings, in which exhibitors and purchasers will be able to make contact and establish the links they believe may be suitable for future business between them. Maintenance is sponsored by Ondoan and Fieldeas, and is one of the seven fairs that form part of the +Industry platform, which will turn the Bilbao Exhibition Centre into a significant meeting point for Industry from 4–6 June 2019.
