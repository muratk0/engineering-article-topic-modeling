# Storage and cutting

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

Serving the British and Irish markets, KASTO Ltd is a subsidiary of KASTO Maschinenbau GmbH, Germany. Founded in 1844, the company is now in its sixth generation of family ownership. One of its activities is the manufacture of bandsaws, circular saws and hacksaws, either manually operated, semiautomatic or fully automated. Its solutions are suitable for a range of applications from job shop to mass production with robotic sorting.

The company also builds industrial warehouses for computer-controlled storage and retrieval of sheet, tube, bar, profile and other materials to provide very high storage capacity in a very compact footprint. Many stockholders and manufacturers use such systems to reduce labour costs, maximise machining up-time and ensure short leadtimes through efficient store management and retrieval of stock.

"A particular strength is our holistic service which includes planning, design, software development, project management, execution, service and support, all from a single source. Storage systems can be either stand-alone or interlinked with saws, other machine tools, automation, MRP and ERP systems and more. If a company is running out of space in the warehouse or factory, we can provide a solution," KASTO told ISMR.■
