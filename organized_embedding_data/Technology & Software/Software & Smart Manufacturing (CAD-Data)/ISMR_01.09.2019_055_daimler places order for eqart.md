# Daimler places order for eQart®

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

THE EQART® IS the first product to emerge from the "FlexQube 4.0" project. It is an autonomous and remote-controlled cart that can be configured 'in almost any shape', as it is built with FlexQube building blocks featuring digital modules such as motors, battery, control unit and sensors

Daimler AG in Germany has now placed an order for it and planned delivery is the middle of September 2019, if the current certification process is approved and finalised by the end of August 2019.

"Our goal with the eQart® is to offer the most user-friendly automation solution for intralogistics operations. In addition, customers can use the full potential of the FlexQube concept to build a wide variety of sizes and customised top structures," said FlexQube.

"We have invested a lot of time and capital into this project and it feels very positive to receive a formal order from a multinational automotive manufacturer like Daimler, before we have even introduced the product to the broad market. The eQart® will be tested at a Mercedes-Benz service centre and there is potential for full roll-out to all the service centres worldwide at a later date."

FlexQube is in the final stages of the development process and is now mainly focused on getting the supply chain in place; preparing for assembly and distribution; obtaining all necessary product certifications for the EU, USA and Mexico; training the sales organisation and preparing the marketing in connection with a sales start.
