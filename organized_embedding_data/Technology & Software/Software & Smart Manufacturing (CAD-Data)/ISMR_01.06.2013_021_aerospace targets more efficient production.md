# Aerospace targets more efficient production

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

For the second time now, the Institute of Production Engineering and Machine Tools (IFW) at the Leibniz Universität Hannover, Germany, is joining forces with the Machining Innovation Network to host an international event, called “New Manufacturing Technologies in the Aerospace Industry” at EMO Hannover. On 18 and 19 September 2013, international experts from the industrial sector and the academic community will be discussing how the stringent requirements of the aerospace industry can be met even more effectively with new machinery concepts, automation solutions and the use of the CAD-CAM chain.

“The aerospace industry is one of Europe’s most successful sectors, but also one of the most demanding”, said Cord Siefken, chairperson of the Machining Innovation Network and plant manager at Premium Aerotec in Nordenham, Germany.

There is a particular demand at present, he added, for resource-efficiency coupled with high levels of quality and productivity, both in the products concerned and the relevant production processes.

The aviation conference at EMO Hannover 2013 is themed around the challenges and solutions involved. In four thematic categories, ongoing trends will be addressed in the relevant production and machine technologies, and on planning and organisation. In addition, for the first time, academic presentations will complement these three areas in a dedicated session.

“With its keynote theme of ‘Intelligence in Production’, EMO Hannover provides an illuminating framework for our conference,” explained Professor Berend Denkena, a director of the IFW and a member of the Machining Innovation Network’s Executive Committee. “Direct networking between the production technology offered and ideas from the research community and the aviation sector shows where the challenges are and which areas need intensive work.”
