# ALMA GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

www.almaasco.de

ALMA and ASCO DATA, two European players in sheet metal CAD/CAM software with a head office in Grenoble (France) and subsidiaries in Blaubeuren and Bremen (Germany), have been a leading CAD/CAM software group since 2017.

“Ever since the company was created, we have been designing innovative software solutions to optimise consumption of material and the potential of machines and robots. Our main areas of expertise: optimisation of industrial, algorithmic processes, nesting and CAM applied to cutting; sheet metal CAD/CAM; robotics (trajectory computation, obstacle avoidance); development of web applications; SaaS and cloud environments,” it told ISMR.

## ATHADER, S.L. https://athader.com

ATHADER is part of the Bradbury Group and a leading worldwide supplier of coil processing since 1992. The company has established itself as a globally recognized manufacturer of custom-designed coil processing equipment including cut-to-length lines; slitting lines; automatic packaging lines for coil or sheet; recoiling lines; inspection lines and retrofits of existing coil processing lines. Athader supplies cut-to-length lines which integrate different levelling technologies: mechanical roll levellers; hydraulic e-Drive levellers and stretch levellers by LEVELTEK INTERNATIONAL. With its partner, SAMUEL NELSON TECHNOLOGY, it supplies new push pull pickling lines, revamps and consultancy services.
