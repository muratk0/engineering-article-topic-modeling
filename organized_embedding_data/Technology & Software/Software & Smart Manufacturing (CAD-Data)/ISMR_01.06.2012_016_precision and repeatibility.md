Robotics specialist ABB highlights ten reasons for manufacturers to robotise their operations

Over the past 30 years, industrial robots have advanced beyond all recognition. From the first all-electric microprocessorcontrolled robot in 1974, robotic suppliers have continued with pioneering developments to transform the manufacturing industry. Over the years, many features and capabilities of robotics have dramatically improved while reliability has increased, helping to deliver a host of benefits in a wide variety of applications. In fact, manufacturers introducing robots to their production processes have typically seen a significant transformation in their overall productivity and efficiency.

Many of the problems that have delayed the uptake of robotic technology have now been addressed, ensuring that all industries and businesses can unlock new levels of productivity, performance and profitability through faster, flexible and more accurate production. ABB’s own ‘top 10 reasons to invest in robots’ campaign highlights some of the key reasons to invest in robotics including reduced operating costs, improved product quality and consistency, increased production output rates and yield, product manufacturing flexibility and the reduction of material waste.

## New technology showcase

Some of these benefits were the key drivers for robotics appearance at the recent MACH exhibition (16-20 April at the NEC in the UK) where ABB showcased a variety of technologies, including ABB’s Flex Finishing package and the software and electronicsbased safety solution, SafeMove.
