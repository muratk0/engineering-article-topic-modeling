# KEEPING CONTROL

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

## U.S. sheet metal contractor, TCI, used new CAD/CAM software to reduce the nest creation process for its laser and waterjet cutters from several hours to ‘a couple of minutes’.

Before the software, creating manual nests took a sheet metal fabricator several hours, meaning shop floor staff moved onto other tasks while nests were being generated. However, a day's worth of nests can now be produced in "just a couple of minutes".

"This makes a huge difference to us," said Tindle Construction Inc.'s (TCI's) engineering manager, Jesse McGinnis. "We have an employee who only programs for half the day, freeing the rest of his time on engineering projects. Because of what we can do with our new RADAN CAD/CAM software, it's no longer necessary for someone to generate nests full-time."

TCI provides its customers with an array of options to complete their projects with precision. This includes design drafting; material selection; waterjet/laser cutting and 'start to finish' collaboration.

## Efficiency in focus

Continuous improvement has always been the goal at Tindle Construction, Inc. (TCI) where industry expertise and the implementation of efficiency-boosting technologies has helped the company to create high-quality products since 1994.

Headquartered in Fredonia, Kansas (USA), TCI is a family-owned, multi-divisional metal fabricator and specialised industrial services company working across the USA. Operating across two divisions, Industrial Services and Fabrication, the company offers products, design and planning services to a wide variety of customers.

Jesse McGinnis confirmed that some of the aspects separating TCI from its competitors include AWS-certified and R-stamp welds; custom engineering on architectural parts and custom high-end finishes on copper, brass, stainless and carbon display products.

Its diverse range of fabrication capabilities include ornamental and architectural metal products; signage and artwork as well as sheet metal products such as wall panels, corner trims and ceiling inserts. Sheet metal parts; weldments; structural steel; pressure vessels and unique metal finishes are typical projects that make their way across TCI's shop floor. Aluminium, carbon steel, stainless steel, brass and copper are the most commonly used materials.

## Flexible benefits

"We have a lot of different machines with a lot of different capabilities, and combining these is like playing chess," explained McGinnis. "It's interesting to see what we can come up with to get the job done, and that's definitely a challenge that I enjoy."

TCI has a broad range of equipment including a laser cutter; waterjet cutter; press brake; mandrel bender; CNC lathe; CNC mills and manual lathes and mills. Part of Jesse McGinnis's job is to decide which combinations of machinery will be the most efficient and best suited to each job.

He was hired by TCI specifically for the fabrication side of the business and was tasked with hand-programming the company's latest laser cutting system. TCI eventually invested in a CNC programming solution for the laser but, over time, found that the system was significantly limited in efficiency and flexibility. After investigating all options for implementing a new sheetmetal solution, TCI made the switch to RADAN software.

"I felt like RADAN was a better fit for us," affirmed McGinnis, who also uses RADAN to program TCl's waterjet cutting system. One of the benefits, he added, is the ability to edit automatically-generated nests ensuring that programming control remains with TCL.

"The ability to edit nests was something that our old software couldn't give us, but with RADAN software we can edit the lead in, lead out, tooling or just edit for cosmetic reasons. We wouldn't be as efficient as we are now without RADAN's nesting software and the system's ability to do what we need."
