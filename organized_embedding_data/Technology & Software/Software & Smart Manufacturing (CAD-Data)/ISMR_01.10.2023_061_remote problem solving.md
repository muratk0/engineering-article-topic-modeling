# Remote problem solving

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Swedish stamping press specialist, AP&T, is introducing a new Android-based tool that simplifies remote problem solving and reduces the risk of misunderstandings and downtime. With the aid of AP&T Remote Guidance Support, a customer's technician can connect to AP&T's technical support, visually show a problem and receive professional guidance along the way.

"In our latest service, Remote Guidance Support, we include an Android-based headset with a camera, microphone, monitor and headphones that the customer's operator or technician can use to show us where they need help. When both parties see the same thing, it's easier to clear up issues, make a diagnosis and determine what needs to be done. Simple issues can usually be managed by the customer's personnel with our help, saving time and money," said Mats Kinnby, Head of Global Technical Support, AP&T.

The headset works much like a smartphone. It communicates through Wi-Fi or the mobile network and the user connects to AP&T's support through platforms such as Microsoft Teams.

"The headset allows the user to keep both hands free which is good if you need to move things out of the way or point out a particular spot. It is also equipped with an anti-vibration stabiliser so we don't need to look at shaky camera images. Thanks to the monitor (which is included in the headset) we can show drawings, images and written instructions to the technician who is standing beside the machine," added Kinnby.

AP&T Remote Guidance Support is a selectable additional service for customers with AP&T technical support availability agreements. See https://aptgroup.com/solutions/aftermarket-services
