# Analysis of the data journey

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

LANTEK, FOCUSED on the development and sale of CAD/CAM/MES/ERP software and a specialist in the digital transformation of companies in the metalworking industry, was at the Blechexpo 2019 trade show in Stuttgart with a presentation on the value of data in the sheet metal cutting industry.

With a presentation entitled "Data's Journey in the Sheet Metal Industry," Javier Román (Product Manager, Lantek) explored the importance of data in this industry, analysing different data sources, ways to process data and how data can be used in decision-making and industrial automation processes.

"The importance of measuring the quality of the data, their validity, consistency, representativeness, uniqueness and complexity, with a specific Data Quality Report, is fundamental in data governance and essential for companies that want to digitally transform and optimise processes based upon data intelligence. The human factor also plays a fundamental role when guaranteeing data quality. In fact, a new figure is emerging: the Chief Data Officer (CDO), who is responsible for all things related to this kind of data," he explained.

Lantek also highlighted its latest developments included in its advanced manufacturing cloud platform, Lantek 360. It placed special emphasis on its Lantek Analytics, Control Panel, iQuoting and MetalShop solutions. For more information, visit www.lanteksms.com/us. ■
