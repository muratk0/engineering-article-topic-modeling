[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

The EinScan HX blue laser handheld scanner from SHINING 3D is the result of "years of measurement and 3D scanning experience." It is designed to deliver performance and versatility with blue LED and blue laser technology.

Blue light source technology is the gold standard for handheld 3D scanners. This technology has a narrower wavelength and can capture a narrower spectrum of light than white light, providing sharper results with less noise and the capacity to operate in brighter rooms and real-world scenarios.

“EinScan HX combines speed and accuracy with rapid scan and laser mode. Users can quickly capture data for most objects within minutes for CAD/CAM, reverse engineering and other applications using image projection technology. It has a built-in full-colour camera to capture colour models and track by texture," the manufacturer explained.

"We believe that 0.05mm minimum point distance and 0.04mm accuracy with the laser scan meets the needs of most industrial reverse engineering and measurement applications. In rapid scan mode, HX has a processing speed of up to 1,200,000 points/second for scanning most objects in minutes."

Laser mode captures more precise data, using intersecting lasers and registration targets to capture the movement of the scanner or object when using a turntable. The two methods complement each other, allowing the user to combine laser scan and registration targets and offering hybrid solutions for different challenges.

EinScan HX has a simple plug and play set-up. The device displays a colour light to keep users within the ideal range of an object. After collecting data, EinScan software provides a range of tools and methods for cleaning up scan data by removing and manipulating parts that are not needed.
