ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in coil processing markets?

FK: We see a definite trend towards advanced high-strength and thinner materials. Handling is a challenge for these thinner materials. The rise in automation is also a trend (one reason why we acquired Delta Technik).

There are three main materials that will be in aggressive demand – copper, aluminium and high-strength steel. Copper will be needed in the electric car industry, for example. A lot of our customers are trying to get into this industry, for example. We expect to see more demand for copper processing lines. We also see an increased demand for aluminium lines. However, we are also seeing a revolution in high-strength and high-tensile steels. Customers use both types of materials in the automotive sector.

ISMR: Have you launched or are you launching any new products/lines this year?

FK: Schnutz recently delivered a coil feeding system for high-tensile materials to a U.S. automotive supplier (supplied to a German facility). The customer needed to build three lines.

Schnutz is going to get more into coil feeding or press feeding lines. The leveller will be the key for this success, handling advanced materials. Schnutz will therefore investigate more systems for these special requirements.
