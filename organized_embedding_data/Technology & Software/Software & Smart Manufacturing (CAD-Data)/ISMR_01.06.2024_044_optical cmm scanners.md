# Optical CMM scanners

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Creaform, a business unit of AMETEK, Inc. (a worldwide provider of automated and portable 3D measurement solutions) has announced the release of its MetraSCAN BLACK+™, MetraSCAN BLACK+ Elite™ and HandyPROBE Next+. These are the latest versions of its suite of optical CMM 3D scanners and probes.

Leveraging new ISO 10360 certifications and set-up assistance tools, this latest generation of the MetraSCAN 3D line-up aims to provide professionals with sophisticated tools to optimise their operations. Enabling the simple and precise measurement of large parts, the expansion of this line-up marks a strategic move by Creaform to meet the evolving needs of quality-control experts.

“Engineered to optimise workflow and enhance outcomes in any given condition and for any users (experts and non-experts alike), these new solutions introduce other standout features such as rigorous acceptance tests, a new automatic volume extension specification and 360° magnetic targets. A range of visual guidance, control and troubleshooting tools are also available," outlined the manufacturer.

The troubleshooting tools offer advanced set-up diagnostics, making it possible to validate jig or part set-up deformations from any cause (thermal, impacts etc.) in a single click.

Boasting two decades of experience in dimensional metrology, Creaform helps its customers to achieve their measurement ambitions with its 3D solutions. Headquartered in Lévis, Québec, Creaform is present in more than 85 countries through its 26 locations all around the world and a network of over 100 distributors.
