Module adds production management to CAD/CAM nesting software

Hypertherm has released the Production Manager optional module for its ProNest CAD/ CAM nesting software. The web-based module integrates with the company’s Edge Connect CNC to capture machine data automatically, without the need for operator intervention. In addition, it displays real-time production data so team members across an organization can track the status of job orders, the production schedule, and pending inventory requirements.

Production stats and trends are provided in one view, with color-coded status alerts and an interactive display. Users can cancel or hold orders to add or remove parts based on last-minute changes and view machine backlogs.

Panel bender offers high box height

TRUMPF has developed a new, fully automatic panel bending machine. Designed to process complex parts quickly, the TruBend Center 7020 can bend parts with side lengths up to 350 mm and material thicknesses up to 4 mm extending over the entire bending length.

According to the manufacturer, the machine works up to 80% faster than a manual press brake, depending on the specific part and bending operations involved. It is suitable for fabricating complex parts with radius bends, short side lengths, and narrow profiles.

The machine’s rotary part manipulator can move the part even closer to the bending line, often reducing cycle time by eliminating the need to reposition the grippers during processing. The open design allows workers to load/unload the bender from the side without having to lean into the interior.

Wire prevents cracking in multipass stainless SAW

ESAB has introduced Exaton 19.9.Nb HF, classified as an AWS A5.9: ER347 as well as an EN ISO 14343-A: S 19 9 Nb. This 300 series austenitic steel SAW wire is made with niobium and high ferrite to help prevent weld cracking. It is suitable for stainless steel applications on pressure vessels and other thick-plate applications requiring multiple passes.

The wire has a FN DeLong of 13%, a FN as per WRC-92 of 12, and a nominal niobium composition of 0.7%. It is available in 2.4- and 3.2-mm dia., in 25-kg spools and 280-kg drums. Its recommended flux is Exaton 15W, although it can be used in combination with other fluxes.

Hand-held resistance weld checker aids troubleshooting

Amada Weld Tech has introduced the MM-410A hand-held resistance weld checker. The compact unit supports a variety of resistance welding technologies, including AC, DC inverter, AC inverter, transistor, and capacitive discharge.

Featuring a color touch panel display, the unit provides information on current, voltage, weld time, and force. It is suitable for use in process troubleshooting, where it can be used to correlate waveform and numeric data with process results and provide detailed weld data for process optimization and validation. If used continuously in a production environment, it helps reduce scrap by detecting drifts in the weld process and alerting operators before process failure.

Tool changer adds speed, precision to press brake bending

Bystronic offers the Modular Tool Changer to help reduce setup time on its Xpert Pro press brake. According to the manufacturer, the unit can help increase available bending time up to 30%.

The robot can lift tools that weigh up to 55 lbs. Bottom tools of up to 4.72 in. wide and top tools up to 13.77 in. tall can be used, and the robot can rotate the top tool 180 degrees. Adding new tools is automatic: The intelligent sensors automatically recognize new tools placed into the ram and table and independently integrate them into the magazine with their positions stored in the rack.

Cleaning the bending table is unnecessary. A compressed-air cleaning nozzle ensures that contaminants do not prevent the precise positioning of tools or damage bent parts.

Portable magnetic drill offers capacity to 1½ in. dia.

Hougen Mfg. has released the new HMD900 portable magnetic drill for general fabrication. It includes a hidden motor cord design that incorporates the cord into the drill’s motor casing and housing for safety and reduced maintenance.

The arbor system uses a ⅝-in. slot drive for rigidity, hole accuracy, spring slug ejection, and quicker use of accessories such as drill chucks and countersinks. A safety switch built into the base of the magnet shuts the motor off if the magnet disengages from the work surface.

The 27.7-lb. drill measures 15⅛ by 6¾ by 11⅜ in. and provides drilling capacities from 7/16 to 1½ in. dia. and 2-in. depth of cut.
