# 3D METROLOGY

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

FARO will be presenting a broad range of portable measuring machines at CONTROL 2010, Stuttgart, Germany (4 – 7 May). This will include live demonstrations of the FaroArm product line - Quantum, Platinum and Fusion portable systems for 3D measurement. The FaroArm products measure, digitize or create a CAD component analysis in production or during assembly.

Laser Tracker lON features longer measurement range, lighter weight and a sophisticated distance measuring system: Agile Absolute Distance Meter (aADM). FARO Gage is the smallest and most precise FARO measuring arm. With a measurement volume of 1.2m, it is designed for adjusting, aligning, measuring and verifying parts.

The FARO Laser Scanner Photon 120/20, when combined with new FARO Scene 4.6 software, offers users a rapid long range phase shift laser scanning solution. The new Photon models capture and analyse detailed 3D images of large objects by reducing scan time and post processing work.
