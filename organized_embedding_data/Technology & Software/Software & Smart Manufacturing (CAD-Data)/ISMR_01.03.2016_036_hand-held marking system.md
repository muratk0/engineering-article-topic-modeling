# Hand-held marking system

[TARİH: 01.03.2016 ISMR]

THE BATTERY-OPERATED, hand-held marking system, FlyMarker® mini, is a lightweight dot peen marker. Weighing just 2.7kg and boasting a compact design, this marking unit is mainly suitable for durable markings of large and unmovable workpieces directly on-site.

“With the new, optional available column frame, it is also very easy to mark small work-pieces or type plates. The mobile marking unit can easily be put into the column frame and fixed with a clamping lever. Smooth-running linear guides enable quick adjustment of the marking pin distance and marking can be started directly. Thanks to the T-slot base plate of the column frame, it is possible to mount the optional available MARKATOR® type plate as well as individual plates which are produced by the customer to position the work-pieces which need to be marked,” Markator explained.

“The portable marking system can mark several materials with a hardness of up to 63 HRC. Two Lithium-Ion-Batteries are integrated in the standard scope of supply so that it is possible to mark nonstop. The housing consists of glass fibre reinforced plastic which is break-resistant. Next to characters and numbers, it is also possible to mark logos, test symbols and Data Matrix Codes.”

With the new version of the FlyMarker® mini with an integrated barcode scanner, it is possible to scan 1D and 2D codes (e.g. barcodes, QR codes etc.) without any external accessories. The scanner is integrated directly on the front face of the marking unit. With this, barcodes can be scanned quickly and the content can then be marked in characters directly on a work-piece.

For transportation to another construction site or a different hall, the practical carrying case can be used. This carrying case contains all components of the scope of supply. Depending upon the material and the customer requirements, the marking force and marking depth can be adjusted individually.

Clearly designed software is very easy to understand and enables intuitive operation. Using the latest processor technologies, rapid navigation and quick access times to the marking files is possible. Several hundreds of marking files, fonts and logos can be stored and administered in the internal memory of the marking unit. Various language versions are available.

“The FlyMarker® mini is relevant for any sector producing large or small metal parts – metal sheet, plasma cutting, laser cutting etc. It can also be used as an emergency or as a backup system if a production line fails. This is the fourth generation of the system since 2004 and we have taken into account plenty of customer feedback on issues such as design and portability. The unit can be operated two-handed and the keyboard is user-friendly. It uses our own software and can also integrate items such as barcode scanners and other functionality for counting etc. into the system,” the company told ISMR at Blechexpo.

“In today’s manufacturing environments, there is a need for smaller and lighter units which are effective, simple to use and reasonably priced. In general, marking units have become smaller over the years and users want more durable systems. Component marking times are important (speed) as is the quality and depth of marking. Low weight is vital as is the ability to mark a wide range of materials such as plastics, aluminium etc.”
