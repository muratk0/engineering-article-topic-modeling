# Innovation accolade for Bobcat Company

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Bobcat Company (a global equipment, innovation and worksite solutions brand) has been recognized as one of Fast Company's '2024 Most Innovative Companies' as part of its manufacturing awards.

"Since 1958, Bobcat has been looking forward in all aspects of its business to transform how the world works, build cities and support communities for a more sustainable future. We have advanced our manufacturing facilities, both in terms of our footprint and the technology at our global locations," commented Bobcat.

Recent updates to its facilities in the United States have included a US$ 9.3-million automated press system at its Gwinner, N.D., manufacturing facility; technology at its Statesville, N.C., facility that streamlines component inspection and a new robotic forming press at its Johnson Creek, Wis., factory. These have led to improved efficiencies and more ergonomic worksites for its employees.

With a focus on innovation, Bobcat is paving the way for electrification and autonomy in equipment design and development.

Since 2022, the brand has launched several machines, including the all-electric T7X compact track loader, the all-electric concept S7X skid-steer loader and the fully electric and autonomous concept pair of loaders, the RogueX and the RogueX2.

Backed by the support of a global dealership network, Bobcat offers an extensive line of worksite solutions including loaders; excavators; tractors; utility vehicles; telehandlers; mowers; turf renovation equipment; light compaction; portable power; industrial air; forklifts and attachments. The Bobcat brand is owned by Doosan Bobcat, Inc., a company in The Doosan Group.
