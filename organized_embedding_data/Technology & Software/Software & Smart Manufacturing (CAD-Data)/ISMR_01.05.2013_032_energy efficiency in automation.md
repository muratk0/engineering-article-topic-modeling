# Energy efficiency in automation

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

FESTO, a global manufacturer of automation technologies, has published a new report which evaluates energy efficiency measures in automation and positioning technology fields. The report helps manufacturers to better understand and identify methods, tools and the alternative advantages of pneumatic and electric drive technologies for improving energy efficiency in automation processes.

It is free to download from www.festo.co.uk/energyreport and provides a simple introduction to the subject of efficiency with pneumatic and electric drives. The paper has been produced as part of a joint ‘EnEffAH’ project, part of the German Government’s energy research programme, and clearly outlines a broad range of technical and organisational topics. It explores the basic principles and measures for increasing energy efficiency and shows that the correct selection of technology (effectiveness) and the correct operation (efficiency) are critical.

“There are no quick-fix solutions for increasing energy efficiency, as it must be viewed in an overall context. Trying to save money at a component level without considering the overall system is ineffective and parameters must be looked at in detail to provide lasting efficiencies and savings,” said Steve Sands, product manager at Festo. "Selecting the right measures and using drive technology correctly means that notable energy and cost savings can and must be reached.”

UK vision system manufacturer, Meta Vision Systems, has received an order from a large Chinese windtower manufacturer for fourteen of its DLS digital laser scanners. They will be used to automate welding operations during the production of monopiles for offshore wind installations.

Meta’s DLS uses the scanning spot principle rather than the more usual laser stripe method. The scanning spot system has significant advantages when welding the deep joints that are common when manufacturing monopoles, where wall thickness can be over 100mm.

Dr Jing Zhao, Meta’s country manager for China, commented: “The performance of the DLS in generating accurate profiles of deep, narrow weld joints is a significant benefit for our customers. The large depth of the joints results in extended welding times so continuous, consistent performance over long periods is vital. Monopile welding has to be done quickly and efficiently to meet production targets but, at the same time, high quality standards are demanded.”

The windtower industry worldwide has become extremely competitive with the evolution of designs that place increasing requirements on fabrication and welding. To be competitive, windtower manufacturers must combine high productivity with excellent quality.

HYPERTHERM’S Centricut® brand now features new consumables for PerCut 210M and 450M torches found on all new and upgraded Kjellberg® HiFocus® systems.

“The Centricut brand consumables will match, and in many cases surpass, the performance of Kjellberg consumables. At the same time, they cost considerably less to purchase reducing the cost of the end user’s cutting operation. At 280 Amps, the Centricut parts delivered better cut quality through the life of the consumables, thanks in part to our patented SilverLine® electrode and CoolFlow™ nozzle technologies,” Hypertherm told ISMR.

SilverLine technology features a hybrid copper/silver weld joint that delivers performance equal to a solid silver electrode. Advanced cooling features reduce the operating temperature of the electrode. CoolFlow technology features improved cooling to help maintain the size and shape of the nozzle orifice for longer life. Centricut consumables for the Kjellberg PerCut 210M and 450M require no changes in system set-up and are designed with features that allow them to work with all versions of these new Kjellberg torches.

## Multi-touch monitor

FOR GESTURE AND MULTI-TOUCH operation, Siemens Industry Automation Division has launched an industrial monitor with projected capacitive touch technology (PCT). Simatic IFP1900 MT is the first device of this type that the company has marketed and expands its range of rugged HMI (Human Machine Interface) monitors with widescreen format.

The device for built-in installation features a scratchproof 100 per cent glass front with high resistance to chemicals and is offered with a 19-inch display diagonal. The highlights of the new industrial flat panels are the special industrial anti-glare surface and automatic detection of spurious touching and operator actions, for example, touching with the ball of the thumb or soiling.

Simatic IFP1900 MT is used as an HMI on or around the machine and at distances of up to 30 metres in distributed solutions with connection to industrial PCs. It can also be used for existing applications with single-touch operation. The PCT technology in Simatic IFP1900 MT enables efficient and rapid operation, even when wearing thin protective gloves. The device responds to gestures and operator actions with up to five fingers simultaneously. For example, image contents can be panned, zoomed, or hidden components shown both intuitively and quickly.
