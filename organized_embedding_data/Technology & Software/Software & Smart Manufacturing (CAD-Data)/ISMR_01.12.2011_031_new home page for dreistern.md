# NEW HOME PAGE FOR DREISTERN

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

DREISTERN, a specialist in roll forming technology, has added additional content to its website with ‘information that is much more application-related’. The site not only includes the systems marketed worldwide by the manufacturer from Schopfheim in the south of the Black Forest, Germany, but also its applications e.g. in the production of lightweight parts for side impact protection in the automotive sector. Videos on the homepage and on the video portal “YouTube” with machines live in action also form part, along with brochures for download, of the content. Dreistern has promised that there will be ‘something new every four weeks’ on the home page.

For more information, please see www.dreistern.com
