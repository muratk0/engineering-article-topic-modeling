# ROLLFORMING THE FUTURE

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

DATA M has launched new rollforming software design versions and is gearing up for Industry 4.0

Software specialist, DATA M, was present at Blechexpo with a re-worked product portfolio and new versions of its COPRA® RF design and COPRA® FEA RF simulation software.

“We are working on new developments for Industry 4.0, connecting individual steps of the process chain, and have also added extra products to our portfolio,” confirmed Stefan Freitag, Managing Director, DATA M, to ISMR in Stuttgart.

“We completed a larger version of our COPRA® ProfileScan Desktop 200 last year which allows users to measure larger rollformed sections more rapidly. This year, we also completed a new version of our Rollscanner (updated with improved features such as a new control and improvements in the repeatability of scanning results). We are still working on the engineering and development of 3D rollforming lines and will shortly announce more news on this.”

## Integrated rollforming solutions

Rollformed profiles are used for many different applications in various industry fields, while the requirements for each line of business vary greatly. Different specifications call for verified manufacturing strategies tailor-made for respective job definitions. With its product portfolio data, DATA M offers an integrated four-part rollforming solution concept for the design and simulation of the entire rollforming process as well as devices for quality control of roll tools and profiles.

While COPRA® RF covers the complete engineering process with flower design, calculation and profile design among others, COPRA® FEA RF provides simulation using non-linear elastoplastic calculation for a variety of possible solutions to the challenges faced by profile manufacturers. COPRA® RollScanner and COPRA® ProfileScan Desktop round off the package by offering optical measurement of roll tools as well as profiles with full integration into the COPRA® workflow.

One of the highlights of the COPRA® RF version 2015 is the new “SmartRoll” design feature. Not only does it enable very quick roll design, this new technology offers many options for easy adaptation of roll diameter, roll width, roll gap and relief angles. This powerful feature can reduce roll design times by up to 50%, DATA M told ISMR, as it is no longer necessary to use AutoCAD features such as construction lines or the LINE and OFFSET command. The roll design can be made using only the SmartRolls environment. The software is compatible with AutoCAD 2016, AutoCAD Mechanical 2016 and Inventor® 2016.

“We sell our software worldwide and have partners in 25 countries such as China and India. We try to maintain these contacts and increase their expertise in these markets. We are focused on extending our installed base and have gained some new partners this year in Australia, for example,” added Freitag. “One of our targets is to open new markets – rollforming can scan across markets. You have to be innovative all the time and keep creating new solutions to be successful.

“Finite element simulation (FEA) has become more important. When customers need to produce six weeks after receiving an order, FEA can reduce machine set-up times. As manufacturers use FEA more and more, they have specific requirements (e.g. features such as in-line punching, in-line welding or cutting etc.). As a software developer, we can offer our customers new versions with new features.”

To guarantee constant high quality of tubes and profiles, products need to be constantly measured. The COPRA® ProfileScan system offers this as well as a full external view of the product cross-section with its patented 360° measuring method. It is, says DATA M, the first optical measuring device that can measure the whole visible surface with only one sensor module and a turn-table.

## Roll lifecycle management

DATA M has also developed a new roll management system solution for rollformers with a lot of tools in stock – the software is designed to give them better access to existing rolls in stock and help them to find the right rolls to fit a line. Its Roll Lifecycle Management (RLM) software was released in November. It has demonstrated savings of up to 20% - reducing cost, time and reusable value from stock.

“Users can keep track of large stocks using the intelligent roll management features of COPRA® RLM and save time and expense through the flexible re-use of discarded rolls.

The new SmartSearch feature enables a full automatic real-time search during the design process. In the end our aim is to reduce the amount of time spent by the designer and make his life easier,” Freitag explained.

Nowadays rollformers face the problem of having to stock and manage large amounts of roll tool sets. As many rolls are needed for the production of new profiles manufacturers frequently encounter delays due to time consuming manual search of the required rolls. With intelligent stock management bottlenecks can be avoided and suitable roll sets can be proposed by the database.

COPRA® RLM allows search of the inventory using definable categories to find the required rolls quickly. The software is based on Microsoft SQL Server. Rolls can be returned and further processed to design through full integration into the COPRA® workflow.

The COPRA® RollScanner is a high precision measuring device for the quality control of roll tools as well as an entry point towards a fully integrated Roll Management Solution. It is not just limited to the quality control of roll sets in fusion joining or roll forming arrangements. With the Roll Management Solution users can keep track of large numbers of rolls. All scanned tools are stored in the COPRA® database so that users can keep an eye on their roll sets even with frequent reworking.
