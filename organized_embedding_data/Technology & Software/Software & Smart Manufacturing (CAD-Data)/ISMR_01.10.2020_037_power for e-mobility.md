# Power for e-mobility

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

THE HUGE INCREASE in demand for electric and hybrid vehicles in the future will require new precision parts for drives, batteries and power supplies. Bihler already offers the platforms and technologies needed for the industrial and automated manufacture of core components such as hairpins, busbars, high-voltage connectors, cell and module connectors and power distributors for stators.

The growth of e-mobility offers enormous business potential, believes the company. The advent of e-mobility is inevitable, it told ISMR, and ‘the demand for corresponding vehicle parts will very soon skyrocket. It believes it is crucial for OEMs and suppliers to invest in suitable manufacturing systems for e-mobility components ‘right now’.

To meet these requirements, Bihler is offering automation solutions in the form of its BIMERIC servo production and assembly system and GRM-NC servo stamping and forming machine for production of the new components needed for e-mobility.

“The BIMERIC concept is designed as a modular system in which all the process steps can be seamlessly integrated,” said the company. “It means that we can respond flexibly to all inquiries relating to the e-mobility field and configure systems designed to meet specific customer requirements.”

Customer enquiries can relate to different areas of e-mobility, such as components of the drive, the power supply and the battery. In the drive field, focus is placed on the manufacture of hairpins. In a BIMERIC, these can be manufactured in part numbers of up to 120 units per minute. The BIMERIC also offers manufacture of busbars – fully automated with high throughput rates of up to 60 parts per minute.

“The BIMERIC is also an optimum solution for hybrid components made from metal and plastic. So, for example, power distributors for stators, interconnections between assemblies, cell connectors and JV plugs can be manufactured in a single production process. Production of the metal parts, insertion of the plastic parts and final assembly are all performed at above-average speed. In contrast, the GRM-NC provides the perfect manufacturing capabilities for individual stamped and bent parts. It can be used for the high-precision, rapid and economical production, for example, of shielding sleeves and smaller busbars,” added Bihler.

“Standardised components and tools with a high percentage of standard parts are used in both the BIMERIC and the GRM-NC. While keeping engineering and tool costs down, these also guarantee very short set-up times and correspondingly rapid times-to-market.” See www.bihler.de
