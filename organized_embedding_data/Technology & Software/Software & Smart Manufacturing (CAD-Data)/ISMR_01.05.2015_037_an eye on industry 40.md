# An eye on Industry 4.0

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

AT THE HANNOVER MESSE INDUSTRIE (HMI) 2015 trade fair, SICK AG showcased itself as a technology driver for Industry 4.0 and presented its entire range of sensors and sensor solutions for industrial applications. Taking four examples from the areas of flexible automation, safety, track and trace, and quality control, SICK demonstrated its solutions expertise for Industry 4.0.

Flexible automation requires variable framework conditions. Manufacturing plants must be flexible and must adapt to individual customer requirements. With high product variance but ever-smaller quantities, intelligent components (smart sensors) must be able to adapt and control themselves, explained the manufacturer.

“The key driving force in safety is the interaction between humans and machines, taking into account ergonomic and safety considerations. Central issues include the role of humans in the production systems of the future and how sensor technology can support and protect them,” it added.

“Vertical integration is the keyword for track and trace. The traceability of products during complex manufacturing and logistics processes stands at the forefront of this field. Production and transport logistics merge together through to the customer and necessitate transparent commodity flows, so that decisions can be made more quickly.

“Increasing quality standards and the need for efficient use of resources require autonomous error detection by means of extensive product and production data. For quality control, reliable and unambiguous identification of goods in the production process and supply chain is a vital prerequisite for efficient, autonomous control,” it concluded.
