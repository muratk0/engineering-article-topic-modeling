CMS is used for all levels of material handling equipment, from heavy duty applications lifting hundreds of tonnes, to smaller hoists with a higher work rate. All CMS activities are non-invasive and Street CraneXpress engineers can obtain the data they need to make an accurate diagnosis in minutes. That is a huge benefit in minimising downtime within busy manufacturing units, and can ensure corrective action is taken before wear and tear leads to expensive machine breakdown, or worse, injury to personnel.

The integration of condition monitoring, planned preventative maintenance and original equipment manufacturing has been proven to reduce long term operational costs and improve safety in production facilities.

Banner Engineering has introduced remote display versions to extend the capabilities of its iVu TG Image Sensor and iVu BCR Bar Code Reader. Designed for applications where the sensor must be placed in a difficult-to-reach location, the new units allow set-up and inspection monitoring to be done at a remote control position.

The iVu TG image sensor monitors parts for type, size, orientation, shape and location. It includes a match sensor to determine whether a pattern on the item being inspected matches a reference, an area sensor to detect presence or absence of a particular feature, and an area sensor that adjusts for motion.

The iVu BCR reads all common linear and DataMatrix (ECC200) codes and includes the ability to read multiple codes of different types in the same image.
