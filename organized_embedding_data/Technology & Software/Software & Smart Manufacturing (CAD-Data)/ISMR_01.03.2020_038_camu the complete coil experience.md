PRIMA ADDITIVE participated in the 14th edition of A&T Automation and Testing, the trade fair dedicated to innovation, technologies and 4.0 skills, that took place in Turin, Italy, from 12-14 February 2020.

The slogan “Add Something New" on the Prima Additive stand was an invitation to customers to unleash the potential of Additive Manufacturing (AM) solutions for their business. Prima Additive products are designed to allow customers to exploit the competitive advantages of AM.

At the exhibition, it showcased the Print-Sharp 250, a medium-volume machine for Powder Bed Fusion applications. Print Sharp-250 includes an intelligent control software and a user-friendly interface as well as an integrated glovebox and a plug-in for the vacuum cleaner, to allow the user to operate on the machine without being in contact with metal powder.

"With a building volume of 250 x 250 x 300mm, Print Sharp 250 ensures highquality components, in terms of surface and mechanical characteristics, while it can offer post-processing solutions able to meet the most demanding targets," explained Prima Additive.

The Advanced Head is a multi-purpose DED laser head with a modular design that can be mounted in different configurations to easily adapt to multiple laser processes. Some of the strengths of the new head are fusion metal pool monitoring, a pressurised optical path that avoids dust infiltration and a water-cooled nozzle for continuous deposition, which reduces the risk of powder adhesion during the process. The additive nozzle itself is manufactured using additive manufacturing processes.

The new Convergent CS450 laser source, developed by Prima Electro, was also displayed on the Prima Additive stand. This is a compact fibre laser for Additive Manufacturing. See www.primaadditive.com
