# Alberto Martínez, Bystronic's chief digital officer, explains why and how the sheetmetal industry needs to address digitalisation. Reliable data is crucial on this journey.

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Digitalisation is not just for large companies. Small and medium-sized enterprises must also embrace this essential change.”

Bystronic is a leader in sheet-metal processing. Its focus is on automation of the complete material and data flow of the cutting and bending process chain. It believes that the intelligent connectivity of laser cutting systems and press brakes with innovative automation, software and service solutions is the key to comprehensive digitalisation in the sheet metal industry.

Software and automation solutions are a key pillar of Bystronic's growth strategy. In his role as Bystronic's chief digital officer, Alberto Martínez has successfully led the Competence Centre Software Services since September 2018. He has been instrumental in driving forward the development of its product portfolio for smart factory solutions, and the Smart Factory Software Suite.

In this interview, he discusses why and how the sheet metal industry needs to address digitalisation. The industrial sector, especially small and medium-sized companies, need accessible solutions to help transform themselves into smart factories, regardless of their size.

ISMR: Why is it necessary to digitise the sheet-metal industry?

AM: The world is constantly changing and companies in the sheet metal industry are also immersed in this uncertain and complex environment, where supply chains are affected by unexpected events.

In these environments, it is increasingly important to be able to adapt and become flexible, controlling uncertainty and risk as much as possible to ensure business feasibility. Digitalisation can help companies in this continuous adaptation process by supplying them with accurate information which they can analyse and then use to power data-driven decisions that can improve their performance.

We believe that digitisation should adapt to companies, and not the other way around. We want our customers to overcome the fear of becoming clones. For us, digitalisation must be able to maintain the unique character of each company allowing them, in each case, to make the most of that competitive advantage. That is why we have an offer for every need, taking into account the digital maturity level of a company and its real needs.

It would be pointless to implement software to turn a company into a smart factory if it is neither prepared for this nor needs these tools. We want our customers to trust us to accompany them on their digitalisation journeys. It is essential that they perceive that we understand their needs and adapt our solutions to each case. This is the only way to achieve success in the digitisation process.

ISMR: What are the main barriers for sheet metal companies, especially small and medium ones, to start the digitalisation process?

AM: Our experience has shown us that there are small and medium-sized companies in the sector looking, or who would like, to digitalise. But they feel that, because of their size and make-up, it is not within their reach. The reasons for this are varied.

In some cases, companies have already started a digitisation process in the past but failed because they could not find tools that met their specific needs or because they did not find someone who understood those needs and tried to fit them into a standard. They may also believe that these are processes with huge costs which require lots of resources.

Bystronic can respond in all cases. We have tools specifically developed for sheet metal, and our experienced software experts understand these needs and can advise customers. I like to say that we develop solutions from sheet metal for sheet metal.

ISMR: Why has Bystronic decided to create a software suite to address digitisation of the sheet-metal industry?

AM: Bystronic is a sheet-metal technology company committed to becoming our customers' trusted partner. We seek to provide them with the best tools to adapt to the future. Under this premise, we are constantly looking to add new products and services to complete our offering.

Digitalisation is an unstoppable process; we believe that it will contribute enormously to the development of the metalforming industry. That drove us to see how we could make our vision of the smart factory a reality. Today, I can proudly say that we are on the right track to move this sector in that direction without leaving anyone behind, adding all types of companies while maintaining those elements unique to each one.

BySoft Suite reflects our vision of the future of the metalforming industry. We are presenting a complete ecosystem that is born with the commitment to continue growing, expanding and adding the new requirements of our customers as they arise. This ecosystem can adapt to the continuous changes that occur in today's world and give our customers the flexibility required by the environments in which they operate.

ISMR: This ecosystem looks complex. How can companies in the sheet-metal sector access the solution that best suits their needs?

AM: A first glance at the ecosystem can be overwhelming; six product families are divided into a total of 18 products. However, it is precisely this modularity that is one of the virtues of BySoft Suite. The different processes that sheet-metal customers need to digitalise can be configured and adapted.

This enables users to digitalise their processes progressively. Organisations can take the steps that will enable them to make the most of the advances in digitalisation that they incorporate. Of course, our clients are not alone in this process. We have software experts with extensive experience in the sector who listen to the customer to configure the solution that best meets these needs. This way, the apparent complexity disappears, and our customers receive the software solution that fits their requirements.

ISMR: Who will benefit the most from this software solution?

AM: One of the fundamental challenges for a company like Bystronic is to be able to respond to all our customers. From this perspective, we considered how to meet the digitisation demands of companies as diverse as those that make up our customer universe. This is how we conceive this complete ecosystem, allowing us to shape a solution that best suits customers' needs at any time (depending upon their size and digitisation level).

This aim is what encouraged us to create BySoft Suite. It was the right time to develop a software solution that could contribute to 'universalising' the digitalisation of this sector. It is an affordable, powerful tool for small and medium-sized companies, which are the vast majority in this industry. The world of metalforming is highly fragmented. There is still a huge digital gap between the large companies with systems in place and those smaller companies with low or no digital implementation.

I am convinced of the enormous potential of BySoft Suite. Our goal, when we started to develop these systems, was to provide the industry with powerful tools at a cost that all types of companies could afford. The industrial

ISMR: How does Bystronic see the future of the sheet-metal industry?

AM: I believe that the future of sheet metal is bright. At Bystronic, we think that this sector has enormous growth potential, but we must prepare ourselves and face the challenges ahead. Digitalisation and sustainability are two crucial topics that remain unsolved in this sector and on which we are actively working to offer solutions that allow our customers to adapt and continue to grow and improve their business.

Regarding digitalisation, two concepts can make a difference within the sector.

One is a comprehensive solution that digitalises the entire business beyond production is one of them. The other is reliable data. From my point of view, there are two aspects to consider for reliable data: interoperability between systems and the ability of machines to be a trusted source of data.

In the future, I am convinced that companies in the sheet-metal industry will not only choose a device for its quality but also for its ability to provide reliable data. The software will therefore be a distinctive element in the choice of a machine. In this way, machines can be integrated into digital ecosystems contributing to data-driven decisions and even to the automation of certain processes.

## Sustainability in focus

In recent months Bystronic has invested heavily in the optimisation of production, products, supply chains and peripheral performance optimisers.
