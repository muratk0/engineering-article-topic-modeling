## PRODUCT REVIEWS

DANISH MANUFACTURER, FLADDER DANMARK A/S, has released its new FLADDER® 300/GYRO Highspeed to remove burrs, leave part edges with larger radii and remove oxides on part edges after laser cutting – in one operation.

"Our well-known GYRO model has been optimised for deburring of part edges with substantially greater rounding than seen before. The 'Highspeed' name indicates that the process is based upon using the abrasive tools at higher speeds. It is common knowledge that a grinding material has a certain cutting speed at which the material works best. The ideal cutting speed is often 30m/sec. or higher. Many deburring machines, equipped with abrasive brushes, normally run at a speed far lower than the ideal speed. The deburring result is then often limited in relation to burr size and hardness," the company told ISMR.

"However, there are many technological challenges when working with higher revolutions and it requires perfect interaction between tools, spindles and the machine's characteristics. New FLADDER® abrasive cylinders have been manufactured with a high density of abrasive material, but still with the flexible properties needed to create a uniform result on all part edges. The abrasive cylinders are mounted on our patented lock-it™ spindles, using longitudinal rubber elements which ensure a three-point fixation and optimal centring of the tools," it added.

This, explained the company, means elimination of harmful vibrations from the tools during the high-speed process. To ensure that items are held during the process, the machine is equipped with a powerful two-step vacuum system, with mechanical support. Sensors electronically monitor the machine's operating status and intervene if 'unacceptable vibrations' are detected. This reduces damage to the machine and its components.

“For manufacturers of mild steel, there is an added benefit to using the machine. In addition to rounding the edges, the oxide layer on the edges is also removed on parts up to 10mm thick," added Fladder Danmark A/S.

## A strong bond

AT SOUTHERN MANUFACTURING 2020 in the UK recently, Henkel demonstrated how its latest generation LOCTITE®, BONDERITE®, TECHNOMELT® and TEROSON® products can benefit an entire value chain.

"Whether the task is in system building, assembly or repair, these engineering adhesives, sealants and functional coatings improve performance and cut costs. Core technologies including threadlocking, sealing, gasketing, retaining, instant and structural bonding were demonstrated, using the latest product introductions, at the show. Regardless of which substrates are chosen and the conditions that prevail, if it's technically possible, we aim to provide the most appropriate solution," explained the bonding specialist.

Its range of adhesives incorporate five core technologies – epoxies, acrylics, polyurethanes, silane modified polymers and silicones. Continuous development across the range has now led to products that are more tolerant of contamination, able to support a wider range of operating temperatures and therefore be applied to a greater number of tasks.

"As a result of these ongoing developments, adhesive technology is being widely adopted across all engineering sectors to improve the performance and longevity of products, as well as allow dissimilar materials to be joined to reduce overall weight and energy consumption," added the company.

Henkel's Universal Structural Bonders, based on its Hybrid Technology, are designed to greatly extend the scope of application for structural adhesives in design and repair. They harness the strengths of different adhesive technologies to provide a combination of characteristics. Currently four different adhesives complete this range, each with its own distinguishing features and benefits.

Across the board, how products are applied significantly influences their overall performance and cost efficiency. Henkel also offers design and supply of dispensing equipment, from manual through to fully automatic and standard to bespoke. For more information, see www.henkel-adhesives.co.uk
