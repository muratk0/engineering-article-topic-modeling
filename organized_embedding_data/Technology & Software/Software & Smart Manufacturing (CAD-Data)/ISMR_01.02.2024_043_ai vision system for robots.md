# AI vision system for robots

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

MIRAI is an AI vision system from Micropsi Industries that makes it possible to automate tasks otherwise considered too complex to solve.

"Robots guided by the MIRAI system can deal with reflections, specular highlights and transparency. Thanks to MIRAI's real-time control, robots can track moving objects: no CAD files or simulations are required," explained the manufacturer. "MIRAI attaches to and augments industrial robots. It can perceive its workspace through cameras and correct its movements where needed as it performs a task," explained the manufacturer.

MIRAI consists of both software and hardware. Users attach the MIRAI system to their robot. The MIRAI kit can be tailored to various automation needs and includes a tablet/training app; a controller; camera; lens; ring light and camera mount.

“Users can familiarise the MIRAI system with the action they want it to perform and the variance it may come across. MIRAI then learns how to behave when it encounters variance while executing the desired action," added Micropsi Industries.

Common applications include insertion; end-of-line testing; rack hanging; picking and leak detection. Micropsi Industries and DEPRAG also offer an automated screwdriving solution that can handle variance in manufacturing workstations.

Founded in Berlin in 2014, Micropsi Industries now comprises more than 60 people including robotics engineers; software engineers; cognitive scientists; business developers and designers. It is headquartered in Berlin with its main U.S. office in San Francisco.
