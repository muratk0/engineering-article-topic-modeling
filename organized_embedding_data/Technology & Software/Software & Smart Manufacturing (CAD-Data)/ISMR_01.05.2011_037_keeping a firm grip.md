TECHNO, INC., a subsidiary of Designatronics, Inc., has launched its new gripping system and Automation division; Techno-Omil. This new division provides a wide array of 2-, 3- and 4-finger grippers, swivel units and hydraulic clamping blocks.

Techno-Omil products and services can now be found online at www.techno-omil.com. Here, customers can view product specifications, 2- and 3D CAD drawings, request the New H940 Techno-Omil Product Overview and receive custom application assistance or quotations.

## ABB ENHANCES ROBOTIC PALLETISING

The latest product introductions from ABB include two new palletising robots, three palletising grippers and easy-to-use programming software – all designed to make palletising simpler, faster and more efficient. ABB is further enhancing its current palletising market product line by introducing two new robots, three new grippers, and a comprehensive software program to complement its existing palletising product range.

Leading ABB’s new palletising offering is the compact, 110 kg capacity, IRB 460 which, says ABB, is ‘the fastest palletising robot in the world’.

“Capable of up to 2,190 cycles per hour, this 4-axis robot is suitable for high-speed end-of-line palletising and bag palletising. The IRB 460 has a reach of 2.4 metres, occupies 20 per cent less floor space and runs 15 per cent faster than its nearest rivals,” ABB told ISMR. For high-output full-layer palletising, ABB is introducing the IRB 760 robot. Boasting a payload capacity of 450 kilogrammes and a reach of 3.2 metres, this robot features high wrist inertia that enables it to rotate heavier and larger products faster. This speed makes the IRB 760 especially suited for palletising beverages, building materials and chemicals.

Three FlexGrippers round out the new hardware product offerings, including a clamp, which comes in two sizes, for handling cases; a heavy-duty claw for high-speed bag palletising and a vacuum gripper that can handle up to five products at once.

ABB’s new software, RobotStudio Palletizing PowerPac, allows users to configure, simulate and program ABB robots and grippers for palletising solutions, with little or no robot programming experience required.
