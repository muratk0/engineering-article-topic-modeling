# Intelligent warehouse technology solutions

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

Knapp, a global specialist in automation technology for distribution and production value chains, is presenting a range of its advanced software solutions at this year's IntraLogisteX exhibition (19-20 March 2024 at NEC Birmingham, UK). The company provides customised technology solutions for clients' interlinked business activities.

The MultiScan, Knapp's intuitive, customisable master data capture solution, will be amongst the technologies on display at IntraLogisteX. Available for rental or outright purchase, the system is designed for storage space optimisation, automating material flows and achieving high levels of efficiency through the digitalisation of work processes.

"MultiScan accurately records all data related to an item's dimensions, weight, fragility and stackability to ensure its storage is as safe and practical as possible. By maximising space utilisation (in totes, on shelves and in racking) and optimising the efficiency of packing operations by fitting more items into each carton or bag, MultiScan facilitates environmental benefits and lower transport costs for warehouse operators," said Knapp.

With manual labour shortages challenging operations throughout the warehouse, logistics and manufacturing sectors, redPILOT empowers business operators to optimise available personnel, space, energy and materials. Powered by AI, the award-winning software uses machine learning to increase workplace performance by detecting and eliminating issues such as bottlenecks and excess capacity. The system also utilises the 'teamAPP' smartphone app for flexible labour management.

"This simple-to-use initiative allows employees to enter their work availability dates and times, with employers able to structure shift patterns, accommodate holidays and overcome issues with absenteeism easily and effectively. Available as a cloud-based service, no additional hardware is required. redPILOT assists warehouse and factory managers in making data-driven decisions, boosting employee engagement and increasing overall workplace performance," added Knapp.

AutoPocket™ is also IntraLogisteX-bound. This innovation is designed and engineered by DFT, a Knapp Group company. A flexible solution for single-item fulfilment, it is the latest generation of DFT's pocket sorter automation. The system enables products to be deposited precisely and automatically, even at full operational speed.

"This seamless performance further increases production speed and efficiency, resulting in a more streamlined and profitable workplace operation," concluded Knapp.
