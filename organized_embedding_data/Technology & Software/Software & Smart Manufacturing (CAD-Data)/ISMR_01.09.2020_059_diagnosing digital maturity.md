# Diagnosing digital maturity

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

THE SPANISH ENGINEERING company, Kurago, which specialises in the development of industrial software, has launched a system that enables digital maturity level diagnosis of companies in the sheet metal industry. The diagnosis helps to visualise the steps to succeed in becoming a digital factory.

The audit system, called the Digital Maturity Model (DMM), allows Kurago’s engineers to assess the degree of maturity of a company through a comprehensive questionnaire without the need for laborious processes and systems audits. This level is the starting point for selecting and implementing the right production and managing software solutions. As Jesús Martínez, general manager of Kurago, points out, “To become a digital factory, a company must first know where it stands, its needs and objectives and its available resources.”

The system defines five digital maturity levels, based on the processes in place and applies data management, infrastructure and the qualification level of its human team, one of the most critical issues in this assessment.

A company can therefore be a starter, explorer, player, challenger or champion (depending upon its automation level, analysis capacity or process optimisation).

Kurago is a software engineering specialist in the sheet metal industry. Founded by two Basque entrepreneurs in October 2019, it is based in Bilbao (Spain) and employs more than 40 people. See http://kurago.software/
