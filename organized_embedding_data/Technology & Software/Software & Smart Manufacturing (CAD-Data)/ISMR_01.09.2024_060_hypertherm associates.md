Hypertherm Associates, a U.S.-based manufacturer of industrial cutting systems and software, will offer an immersive booth experience to visitors at EuroBLECH. Visitors can explore Hypertherm® plasma, OMAX® waterjet and the integration of sophisticated software all designed to enhance metalworking productivity and performance. Visitors will also be guided to the company's partner booths to see live cutting, ask questions and exchange ideas.

"Our booth is more than just a showcase; it is a dynamic digital experience designed to inform visitors and leave a lasting impression," said the manufacturer.

The new XPR460® plasma system, with X-Definition® technology, and the new Powermax45 SYNC® plasma series will be showcased. OptiMAX® and ProtoMAX® waterjet systems and ProNest® CAD/CAM software plus Robotmaster® robotic software will also be displayed.
