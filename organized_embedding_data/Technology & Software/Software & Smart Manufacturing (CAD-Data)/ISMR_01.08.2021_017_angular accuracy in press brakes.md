# Angular accuracy in press brakes

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 ISMR]

Vision Components provides OEM laser profilers for checking the angular accuracy in sheet metal bending pressbrakes. The VCnano3D-Z 3D line sensors reach measuring rates of up to 400Hz and a typical accuracy of <±0.1°. They can be directly connected to the machine's PLC for real-time control of the contact pressure to boost the productivity and efficiency of press brakes.

Delem, a leading manufacturer of controls for sheet metal machines, has already completed the integration of the laser profilers in its controllers.

"Our 3D laser triangulation systems operate autonomously, without an external computer. This provides customers with an extremely robust, low-maintenance and cost-effective solution. Featuring a compact IP67 housing and a large field of view, VCnano3D-Z sensors are easy to integrate and can be used with different dies without any conversion or adjustment," commented Vision Components.

The blue laser and Vision Components' ambient light suppression technology are designed for 'reliable detection on shiny and reflective surfaces', for example when bending stainless steel, copper, brass and foiled materials. The embedded vision system's 3D analysis is also designed to correctly detect perforated metal, chequered plate metal and brushed aluminium.

"A few profile points are sufficient for precise angle measurement, even with short sheet metal pieces," said Vision Components, which celebrates its 25th anniversary this year.
