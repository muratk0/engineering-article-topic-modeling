# A DIGITAL PATH

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

“Faccin Group is implementing new digital tools and sales channels to increase its interaction with customers.”

ISMR caught up with Faccin Group’s CEO, Andrea Ceretti, as the Group launches a new logo and website, as well as new interactive and personalised experiences for customers during the pandemic.

A specialist in the design, manufacturing and sale of plate bending rolls, profile bending machines, dished head machinery and special machines (including ship frame bending rolls and presses and plate straightening machines), Faccin Group has production facilities in Visano (Brescia province, Italy). Its complete facility covers an area of 60,000m2 (645,000ft²). Using the latest technology, the company follows through every stage of production, from receipt of an order up to design, construction, machining, assembly and shipping.

“Our Group is now a very powerful combination of well recognised brands in plate rolling, profile bending and dished-head manufacturing (Faccin, Boldrini and Roundo). These brands are all united under one roof; each brand specialises in a certain type of product and we aim to deliver the best quality and technology available in the market,” it told ISMR.

ISMR caught up with Faccin Group’s CEO, Andrea Ceretti, as the Group launched a new logo and website, as well as new interactive and personalised experiences for customers during the pandemic.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes/achievements and the reasons for these?

AC: Our greatest achievement this year has been guaranteeing complete customer satisfaction despite all the COVID-19 hurdles that we, like many companies, had to face. We completed the delivery and installation of all ordered machines on schedule. Some of these were large, heavy-duty machines (such as our 3-roll, 4000-ton HAV bending roll model for a customer in Saudi Arabia, completed in just four months during the worst of the pandemic) or several dished head lines for a customer in the United States, to mention a few. We have been able to achieve all this because of our vertically integrated production and the outstanding group of people working for the company.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals? What about the climate in 2021?

AC: What 2020 has shown is that today’s challenges will force many manufacturers to change their business models and to learn to operate in an uncertain environment while staying competitive and to prepare, not just for recovery, but for exceptional growth.

In 2021, sheet metal professionals, as in any other sector, will have to keep investing in digital tools to boost interactions with existing and new customers, and to protect their employees by providing the tools to increase remote working.

ISMR: How has the COVID-19 crisis affected your company’s operations and which steps have you taken in response?

AC: Even though the COVID-19 crisis affected our way of operating at certain levels, such as not being able to visit our customers as often as we used to, it was also an opportunity to accelerate some programmes that were already in place. Examples of these include digital communication channels and tools for our sales and service departments, remote service management of our machines and new marketing services which we were one of the first to implement in our industry, such as live presentations directly from our factory.

We are working in new ways to provide our customers with digital interactive tools to “be present” remotely and to enable them to experience our technology and our manufacturing processes.

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus for the company right now?

AC: Strategically, we will continue implementing new digital tools and sales channels to help our customers interact easily with us and quickly and easily obtain information about our products and services. Since customers find it difficult to visit us, due to current restrictions, then we are taking our factory to them through interactive presentations and virtual visits.

As regards our technical focus, we are working non-stop to develop new products and services that will be ready during the first quarter of 2021. They have been designed in accordance with today’s energy efficiency features, requested by the market.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers at the moment and how are you addressing these issues for them?

AC: The primary concern for our customers today is after-sales service (how the machines can be serviced when there are so many travelling restrictions). Our strategy has always been to provide the best service possible in the shortest of times. With this in mind, we developed new cloud-based Remote Service Management (RSM) that allows us to solve most of the issues on our machines remotely from our factory. This service was already available before the COVID-19 crisis, so our service department never stopped supporting our customers (even throughout the worst of the pandemic).

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal bending and pressing markets?

AC: Industry 4.0 has become increasingly relevant in the industry and is crucial for any manufacturer wishing to bring high-quality machines, advanced connectivity, analytics and people together to change day-to-day operations for a powerful business outcome.

As a group, we see Industry 4.0 as a way to capitalise on the technology that we already provide. Our machines are Industry 4.0-ready, featuring smart packages that offer, for example, system diagnosis; teleservice; management control; drawing import; rolling and production batch statistics and flexible network solutions. They are designed to help today’s manufacturers face tomorrow’s challenges.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

AC: One of the greatest challenges today for fabricators is the difficulty with finding experienced operators. This is also an obstacle to increasing production. This challenge is driving manufacturers to improve the quality of their metal forming equipment, particularly as regards precision and automation.

Our group offers machines designed to provide return of investment based on metalforming precision and automation as well as elimination of metal waste, combined with powerful technology that augments the productivity cycle.
