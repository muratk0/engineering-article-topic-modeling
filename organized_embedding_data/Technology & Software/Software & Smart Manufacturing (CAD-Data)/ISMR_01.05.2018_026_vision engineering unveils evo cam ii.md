# Vision Engineering unveils EVO Cam II

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

VISION ENGINEERING LTD unveiled the next generation of its EVO Cam digital microscope at the MACH Show in the UK recently. The new EVO Cam II is suitable for close inspection and the recording of high-resolution images and features customised user overlays, onboard measurement and Wi-Fi image transfer. All are supported by a 30:1 optical zoom and magnification of over 3,600x.

"EVO Cam II replaces the original EVO Cam which is already used for the inspection of electronics, automotive and aerospace components; medical devices; precision engineering; plastic moulding and more. High-definition images can be captured and stored directly to a USB memory stick. When connected to a PC, images and video can be easily captured, stored and shared. A Wi-Fi dongle even allows direct sharing with PCs, laptops, tablet computers and smartphones," explained the manufacturer.

Simple on-screen measurement can be performed using virtual callipers and scalable grids. Live images can also be compared to customised user overlays, increasing efficiency of use. A new graphical user interface and intuitive controls with 10 pre-sets enable rapid swapping-out of subjects under inspection, making EVO Cam II suitable for multiple users in high-speed production environments. A remote-control option is available.

EVO Cam II also features a powerful LED ring light and sub-stage lighting for viewing translucent samples. A UV ring light accessory is also available for electronic and other specific applications. High contrast subjects, such as reflective metals used in electronics and automotive manufacturing, can be viewed in more detail using EVO Cam II's Wide Dynamic Range (WDR) mode. EVO Cam II was also launched with a new range of objective lenses. See www.visioneng.com
