# New digital business segment for KUKA

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Automation group, KUKA, has founded a new business segment. With KUKA Digital, the long-established German company (known for its orange industrial robots and automation solutions) is expanding its software and digital business. The goal: the end-to-end digitalisation of a wide range of machines and systems in production, from the first 3D simulation to connectivity and data analysis through to the use of artificial intelligence.

Software is playing an increasingly important role in mechanical engineering. What used to be mostly solved mechanically is now done by electronics and the associated software. "With KUKA Digital, we are expanding our offering in order to position ourselves broadly for a market with strong growth potential. Our customers need products, solutions and consulting approaches to digitalise faster, more efficiently and more easily," highlighted Quirin Goerz, KUKA Chief Information Officer and CEO of the newly founded digital segment.

The open, collaborative cloud platform (mosaixx) will be added to the KUKA Digital portfolio this year.

KUKA is working on expanding the digital world beyond robot and automation solutions and driving forward the digitalisation of industry across the entire production lifecycle. Specifically, KUKA's digital segment is focusing on a software and digital portfolio that is not limited to KUKA robots.

“Regardless of machine type and manufacturer, customers should be able to use software solutions for their production machines and thus digitalise their production halls from end to end. This involves interfaces, data standards, cloud integration and consulting services. In line with an ecosystem approach, we also rely on a network of competent partners. We are moving beyond traditional automation solutions to become a digitalisation partner for the manufacturing industry," outlined KUKA.

“In addition to Device Insight and Visual Components, mosaixx will be expanding KUKA Digital's portfolio this year. An open, collaborative cloud platform for Industrial Software-as-a-Service (SaaS), mosaixx, forms the basis for the digital twin with a combination of tools (software), data management, cloud computing and process understanding," concluded KUKA.■

The Voortman V353 large format fiber laser cutting machine offers flexibility and autonomy for heavy processing. The machine is able to process large format sheets to maximize material yield and is equipped with 3D bevelling.

## MAXIMIZED FLEXIBILITY

Experience ultimate flexibility with the V353's extended cutting bed up to 36 meters / 120 ft. The system is designed for heavy plate processing up to 40 mm / 1.5” thick, in large format sheets which can go up to 3 meters / 10 ft wide. Being equipped with a 3D bevelling unit complete parts can be created.

## RUN UNMANNED FOR HOURS

The bed can be used to buffer multiple plates or remnants for running hours unmanned, also during the night. Several thicknesses can be loaded and are automatically measured for their position on the bed. The nozzle changer automatically changes to the right nozzle when processing a different plate thickness.

## IMPROVED LOGISTICS

The low bed allows for easy loading and unloading while the machine is processing another plate elsewhere on the bed. Several plates can be loaded and even be left on the bed to produce products just-in-time for the next processing step.
