Software helps managers control manufacturing operations

Lantek offers MES Wos software, designed to help sheet metal manufacturers get on the path to digitization. It helps managers maintain control of their manufacturing operations and allows them to use data from the workshop to make decisions about investments, customers, markets, pricing, and reorganization.

The software gathers real-time, key data from the production process, including machine status, labor, materials, and inventory. It provides information about every job that needs to be completed: nesting layout, material assigned, stock levels in the warehouse, processes, and the machines to be used. Feedback from the workshop comes from operator input at shop floor terminals and direct status monitoring on some models of machine tools.

Thermography system performs real-time body temperature measurement

The FeverTIR thermography system by VisionTIR uses a high-precision thermal imaging camera and visual camera to measure human body temperature without contact. It is designed to help companies screen employees before entering the establishment to help eliminate the risk of contagion.

The system alerts about people who exceed the established maximum body temperature, detecting possible cases of fever, one of the most obvious symptoms of COVID-19 infection and other contagious diseases. The included software provides an interface for the control and configuration of temperature, alarms and recording functions, as well as for the analysis of measured data.

Body heat sensor system helps ensure worker safety

Kenzen has launched a real-time worker heat monitoring system. The cloud-based software as a service system includes a wearable device worn by workers on their arm which alerts both the workers and their supervisor when core body temperature is too high. Real-time alerts allow for immediate intervention to help prevent heat injuries.

The unit monitors multiple physiological and environmental metrics, including heart rate, activity, skin, and ambient temperatures. Together, this sensor data allows for the real-time prediction of core body temperature, providing alerts to workers and supervisors when temperatures approach unsafe levels.

Wearable technology helps employees keep safe distance

The Social Distancer alert system from Social Distancer Technologies Inc. is designed to alert employees of close proximity risk during times of social distancing.

The wearable device helps employees stay 6 ft. away from each other while keeping focused on their work. Its patent-pending technology calculates the distance between employees 10 times per second and alerts employees to unsafe distance risks through a combination of sound, vibration, and LEDs.

Centrifugal disc finishers polish and deburr multiple materials

Bel Air Finishing Supply has developed centrifugal disc finishers for polishing and deburring materials including brass, copper, steel, stainless steel, aluminum, Kovar, and titanium. According to the company, the finishers eliminate 80% of handwork and produce more than five times the output of hand deburring operations.

The unit features a bowl-shaped finishing chamber comprising a fixed upper bowl and spinning bottom disc. The disc rotates both the media and parts within the chamber, forcing the mass to the stationary outside wall. This mass of parts and media then folds back to the center.

This process is repeated, creating a 3D toroidal action that produces 10 times the G force of a vibratory bowl finisher, according to the company. While this is happening, water and finishing compounds are added to the mix to keep the media and parts clean. Process effluent, which contains the broken-down parts of media, small amounts of part materials, and water mixed with compounds, flows through an adjustable north/south gap.

The unit comes in sizes from 4 to 60 l, and most models come with a mobile frame suitable for cellular and JIT production. Available features include integrated ultrasonic cleaners, on-board media storage, a direct compound feed system, and parts separation options.

CAD viewer allows simplified assembly highlighting

Hexagon has introduced WorkXplore 2021.0, a highspeed CAD viewer and analyzer. The software was originally created to import and analyze all file types and sizes at high speed. It often takes less than half the time to open a file compared to the original CAD application.

New functions include the ability to highlight individual parts in an assembly easily. The selected component is displayed in a vibrant color, while the rest of the assembly appears transparent in the background. This visualization shows the position of a certain part in an assembly.

In addition, the client viewer—a tool that enables users to communicate 3D parts and assemblies to subcontractors, customers, or colleagues using a stand-alone, lightweight application that can be transmitted easily through the internet—has been reviewed and enhanced.
