# JETCAM continues global expansion

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

AFTER the announcement of five new dealerships in 2013, JETCAM International s.a.r.l. has continued its expansion with the formation of JETCAM Pacific Pty. Based in Sydney, Australia, the company will offer sales and service for all existing and prospective companies in the sheet metal and composite manufacturing sectors.

“JETCAM already has a strong user base in Australia, driving virtually every CNC punch, laser, combination, waterjet, plasma, oxyfuel, router and knife cutter available today. JETCAM Expert is suitable for all companies – from small sheet metal shops up to multi-national companies. JETCAM’s CrossTrack manufacturing suite bridges the gap between the shop floor and ERP system by providing traceability and control,” the company told ISMR.

“Now that Australia’s post-recession roots are taking firm hold, we want to help manufacturers take their CNC efficiency to the next level. Last year, we announced several new features and, in 2014, will continue with our powerful Automatic Sheet Processing functionality, plus a new streamlined interface. We also released a new product, JOC Premium Automation, which allows processes such as nesting and CAD import to be performed remotely,” commented Martin Bailey, general manager of JETCAM International s.a.r.l.

## Transition for HYPERTHERM

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, has confirmed the transfer of all its common stock into an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP), making the company 100 per cent employee-owned under an S-Corporation structure.

Previously, Hypertherm’s ESOP, known internally as the Hypertherm Stock Ownership Plan or HSOP, held approximately one-third of the company’s stock. Controlling shareholders Dick and Barbara Couch created the plan in 2001, followed by a Global Stock Value Programme (GSVP) for international associates in 2004.

With the redemption and purchase of all remaining shares into the HSOP, Dick and Barbara Couch seek to ensure the continuation of Hypertherm as an independent company pursuing the long-term interests of its customers, associates and communities.

“We have witnessed the amazing power of associate ownership since we launched the HSOP and GSVP,” said Barbara Couch, Hypertherm’s vice president of corporate social responsibility. “We are delighted to transition ownership to our associates, who are best positioned to ensure that Hypertherm builds upon our strong culture, passion for technology and customer service, and commitment to our communities.” ■
