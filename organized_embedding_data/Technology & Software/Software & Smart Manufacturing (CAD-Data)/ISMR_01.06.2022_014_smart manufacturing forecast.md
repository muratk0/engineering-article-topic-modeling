# Smart manufacturing forecast

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

According to a new study on ResearchandMarkets, the smart manufacturing market by enabling technologies for digital twins is expected to grow at a CAGR of 68.9% from 2022 to 2027, reaching US$ 43,614.8 million by 2027 (from US$ 2,124.7 million in 2021). Digital twins are being increasingly used as a means of connecting information about a physical product and its behaviour in the real world with a 3D digital representation, which is commonly employed during engineering and other areas of business.

For instance, Dassault Systems (France) introduced a virtual twin concept that enables design and engineering teams to visualise and analyse products or systems virtually and provide insights similar to physical behaviours, including stress and vibration, as well as behaviours associated with software and control systems.

The automotive industry dominates the smart manufacturing market with the highest market share and is expected to grow at a rate of 29.1% during the forecast period, owing to "changing consumer preferences and the inefficiency of traditional processes."

"Changing consumer preferences, technological advances and the introduction of regulations associated with the automotive industry are responsible for the high complexity of automobile products," said the forecast.

“The latest machines and equipment replace the need for human operators for most of the crucial processes in the automotive industry, resulting in the adoption of smart manufacturing technologies," it added.

According to the report, major factors driving the growth of the smart manufacturing market in APAC (Asia Pacific region) are:

- The rising demand for smart tools due to increasing automation in industries.
- The growing adoption of technologies such as Industry 4.0, smart factory, IoT and IIoT.
- The increasing need to optimise productivity and reduce operational and maintenance costs.

"Government support in various APAC countries to drive industrialisation is one of the important factors that will boost the demand for smart manufacturing in the coming years. Various initiatives have been taken by the governments in the region," added the report.

Market drivers also include a growing emphasis on regulatory compliances and surging demand for software systems that reduce time and cost. Market restraints are the high costs involved in implementing smart manufacturing solutions; a lack of standardisation among equipment manufacturers and in connectivity protocols as well as maintenance requirements due to frequent software upgrades.

Opportunities will come from the increase in adoption of IIoT and Cloud technologies; the increased integration of different solutions to provide improved performance and the rapid industrial growth in emerging economies. Challenges include threats relating to cybersecurity; the complexity of implementing smart manufacturing technology systems and the lack of a skilled workforce, according to the report.
