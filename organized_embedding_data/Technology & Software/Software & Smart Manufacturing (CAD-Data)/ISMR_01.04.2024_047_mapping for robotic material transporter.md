# Mapping for robotic material transporter

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Kivnon, a global specialist in the production of Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) and Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs), has announced the integration of intelligent mapping navigation into its K05 Twister robot. This feature caters to users seeking enhanced flexibility and higher-density storage and retrieval capabilities, surpassing the limitations of traditional magnetic floor strips. By embracing mapping navigation, businesses can achieve higher storage density while eliminating the need for a physical guidance infrastructure, making workflow adjustments easier in response to changing business needs.

"In today's dynamic business environment, manufacturers face increased volatility, intricate product requirements, and rising floor-space costs. To address these challenges, we've introduced laser-guided mapping navigation to our popular K05 mobile system, empowering businesses to implement high-density storage strategies and adapt them almost instantly. It not only streamlines operations and reduces costs but also accelerates product delivery. It also enhances the aesthetics of the workspace, providing a more visually appealing environment," said Xavier Munells, Director of Product Management, Kivnon.

The K05 mapping relies on its location and navigation based on elements present in the environment. It is equipped with laser sensors that collect essential data about the physical environment, such as walls and landmarks, to generate a virtual map where paths and tasks for the unit are established. The ability to set routes and tasks on a virtual map eliminates the need for physical elements such as RFID tags, allowing for increased density of actions in a physical area and greatly facilitating modifications to the circuit of the units.

The K05 Twister AGV is designed to optimise the transport of medium loads in specific processes and small spaces. Its compact chassis measures 800mm x 800mm x 280mm. It can carry up to 450kg onboard and tow up to 1,000kg on a wheeled trolley, reaching speeds of up to 1m/s along cyclical or conditional circuits.

"The AGV is capable of precise stops and seamless interaction with other AGVs/AMRs, machines, systems and fleet management systems, including those based on VDA 5050 standards. The new model could come with a lifting table or retractable PINs options and boasts a 360º rotation on its axis. With rechargeable lithium batteries, as well as laser scanners and safety PLCs, the vehicle offers high safety standards," said Kivnon.
