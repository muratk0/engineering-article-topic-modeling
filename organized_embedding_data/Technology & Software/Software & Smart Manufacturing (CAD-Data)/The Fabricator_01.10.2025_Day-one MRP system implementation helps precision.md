# Day-one MRP system implementation helps precision manufacturer hit the ground running

[TARİH: 01.10.2025 The Fabricator]

Applications

Situation

After a German industrial organization successfully acquired their previous long-standing manufacturing enterprise in 2017, entrepreneurs Maurice and Paul Watson promptly decided to go at it again and co-founded MPW Precision in December 2020. With more than 60 years of combined experience in precision manufacturing, the father-and-son duo quickly set up their operation, specializing in high-precision, quality-engineered components for use in larger fabrications and power generation.

Today, with eight employees, the company produces steel pipe systems, metal joints, hoses, and other products using waterjet cutting and CNC machining manufacturing processes. Catering mostly to end users, its clientele comprises Formula One teams, British Steel, and other blue-chip companies.

With years of previous experience leading a manufacturing company, Managing Director Paul Watson knew that complex manufacturing needs a capable material requirements planning (MRP) system for efficient and well-organized operation. So from day one, he set out to find the right MRP software, and experience told him what to look for.

As he knew the company would be on a fast growth path, the first key requirement was scalability. Since all of MPW Precision’s clients require ISO 9001 and other quality assurance instructions, another important function was end-to-end traceability to simplify quality assurance processes. And with the company set up over three manufacturing sites and two home offices, it also was important that the software be web-based and able to run on off-the-shelf PCs and tablets. A final determining factor for the would-be MRP system was affordability.

Resolution

After testing a number of MRP systems, Watson demoed MRPeasy and found it ticked all the boxes and seemed easy for day-to-day use and implementation.

Having prior experience with implementing manufacturing software, he decided to go at this daunting task himself. "I had a target to install MRPeasy and become ISO 9001 approved within six months," he said. Using resources on the manufacturer’s website and inside the client, he got the system up and running in about four weeks.

"After six months of using [the MRP system], SGS came in to undertake the certification audit and we passed without a single nonconformance," he explained.

Commenting on the implementation phase, Watson explained that a key strategy for manufacturers that are just setting up their operations, or still adjusting their production workflow, is to rewrite the internal processes to match what is streamlined in the MRP system: "This way there is no need to shoehorn the software to fit your system, and implementation will be a breeze to get through," he added.

With the system, the owners can do things like taking an order and getting it to the shop floor in about 25% of the time it took with the MRP they used in their previous venture. "There was approval and other stages involved, and we had 40 employees instead of eight, but all-in-all, it’s around 70% more efficient," said Watson.

Today, the company uses the MRP system for almost everything, except for some ISO-specific quality control processes that it still handles via spreadsheets. The Watsons benefit from having business-critical information all in one place and accessible from the shop floor, office, or nearly any internet-enabled external location.

MPW Precision uses waterjet cutting and CNC machining processes to manufacture its products.

The system handles booking the goods and orders, the bar code system with printable and traceable labels, and everything in between. The owners have received comments from customers who praise the speed at which the system makes documentation available to them. Meanwhile, the seamless QuickBooks integration helps ensure the accounts are well taken care of.
