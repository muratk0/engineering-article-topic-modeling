## App for those 'on the go'

IN ITS LATEST UPDATE, business management software specialist OrderWise has launched an app to keep users connected to key data whilst out of the office. Built for the Android platform, the app works seamlessly with the main OrderWise system and integrates email, telephone and map facilities. It has offline capabilities and information is automatically synchronised and updated accurately in real-time when users are connected to the internet.

"For any businesses selling remotely via reps out in the field or at trade shows, having the tools needed to close a sale can be a constant struggle," said Jon Roberts, sales and marketing manager at OrderWise. "In many cases, staff will need to ring to check stock levels and pricing information before contacting their team at the warehouse so that the order can be processed, which can mean goods are oversold or that the sale falls through altogether. However, by using the OrderWise app, staff can make the most of sales opportunities when they are presented and significantly reduce the number of steps needed and time spent to close the deal."

The app allows users to check prices, raise sales orders, view customer details and keep track of recent activity at any time. OrderWise for Android™ is available on Google Play™. For more information, visit www.orderwise.co.uk/main-features/orderwise-for-android

Radan 2017 heralds start of major engineering functionality

THE 2017 RELEASE of the sheet metal CAD/CAM software, Radan, paves the way for exciting developments in the software's history. Product Manager, Olaf Körner, explained: “We are on the verge of reorganising how manufacturers will be using Radan in future, and important updates to Radmanager in the new edition are the first steps towards this." Developers are working on separating Radan into two distinct aspects; first, traditionally creating NC code to drive CNC machines, and second, what they call “engineering of parts“.

"But to achieve this, it relies on the parts being completely ready for nesting," said Olaf Körner. “And that means more than just having flat parts with the right geometry. We need to know as much as possible about the parts – whether they can be cut on any machine tool, and the cycle times for all of those solutions. For example, it may be feasible to cut a part on either a laser or water jet, but while it would take only minutes on the laser, it may take two hours on the water jet.

“In Radan 2017, Radmanager's ability to manage both 2D and 3D engineering of parts is the precursor to the new forthcoming engineering system for any type of parts, including 5-axis laser-cut sheet metal, tubes, flat and bent." The forthcoming Radquote, which is now available as a preview, will be demonstrated at exhibitions and pilot customer sites ahead of its official launch. Radquote will also rely on engineered parts.

"This 3D engineering of parts is becoming increasingly important. It will enable manufacturers to break assemblies down from CAD systems, into their constituent components, and fully understand everything about them.
