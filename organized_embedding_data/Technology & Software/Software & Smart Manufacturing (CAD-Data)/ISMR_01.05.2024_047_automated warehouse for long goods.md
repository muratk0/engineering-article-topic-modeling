# Automated warehouse for long goods

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

When large quantities of bars, tubes and profiles (with lengths of six metres or more) need to be stored in a space-saving manner and retrieved quickly, Remmert offers storage and handling solutions. At Logimat 2024 in Stuttgart, Remmert presented its optimised honeycomb storage technology and order picking solutions.

Remmert honeycomb warehouses are specially designed for long goods (such as bars, pipes or aluminium/plastic profiles). The bulky material is stored in cassettes, which are stored in honeycomb rack structures such as drawers of storage and retrieval machines.

"Cassette storage systems are particularly beneficial for long goods, as bulky material can be stored compactly and handled much more efficiently without risk of damage. Cassettes and racks are specially tailored to the material dimensions. This increases the storage density to a maximum and makes optimum use of the floor space. Energy-efficient storage and retrieval machines can be used for up to 40 storage and retrieval operations per hour," explained Remmert.

The storage and retrieval machines are designed so that they can pick up two cassettes alternately. This avoids empty runs and speeds up the movement of goods. Up to 40 storage and retrieval operations per hour are possible. The storage and retrieval machines can move material weighing up to 1.5 tons per cassette to a height of up to 25m.

Remmert's honeycomb warehouse, up to 25m high, can store a wide range of long goods. When the lifting beam is lowered, recuperation of the drives ensures that the electrical energy is recovered which contributes to low energy consumption.

When two stacker cranes are used in one aisle e.g. with very long racks or redundant operation, dynamic speed control ensures interaction between the two stacker cranes and rapid cycle times. Energy recovery of the drive units of the lifting traverse contributes to very low energy consumption, as does the path-optimised control system.

In addition to manual picking, Remmert integrates supporting systems such as special manipulators and transport trolleys with which orders can either be transported to the next processing stage or buffered directly back into the honeycomb warehouse. The integration of fully automatic pick & place robots or automatic saws is also possible.
