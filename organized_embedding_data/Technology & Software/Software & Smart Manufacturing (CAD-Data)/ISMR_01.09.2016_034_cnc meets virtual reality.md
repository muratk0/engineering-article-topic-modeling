# CNC meets Virtual Reality

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

ECKELMANN AG will present its new CNC user interface for cutting, as well as integrated computer vision, as its highlight at EuroBLECH 2016 (Hall 11, Booth G73). Its computer vision solutions include innovative features such as the precise measurement of contours and shapes or optical adjustment of the plate.

The automation specialist has also designed an interactive 3D machine simulator. Using a 21" E°Panel cutting operator station, exhibition visitors can enjoy hands-on experience of all the features of the cutting assistant. Eckelmann itself uses the machine simulator for development and training on its E°CUT cutting solution, based on the open E°EXC CNC controller architecture.
