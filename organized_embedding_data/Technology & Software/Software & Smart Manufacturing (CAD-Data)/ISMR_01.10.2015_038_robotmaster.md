# Robotmaster

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

Jabez Technologies, creators of software to simplify complex robotic programming, is joining Hypertherm to make it easier for companies to utilise robots for manufacturing applications. Both companies believe that combining Hypertherm’s cutting expertise with Jabez’s skilled engineering and support staff will make it easier for robotic programmers and integrators to achieve optimum results.

At the same time, Jabez can enhance its support for a wide variety of robotic programming applications, including those beyond cutting, by leveraging Hypertherm’s global infrastructure and strong engineering community.

Jabez Technologies specialises in simplifying complex robotic programming for 3D shapes, particularly in high-mix manufacturing environments. With the ability to program many different robots across multiple processes, and by providing tools for easy cell set-up and configuration, its Robotmaster software delivers the flexibility required to support a wide variety of robotic applications. Built-in simulation tools, on-the-fly calculation capabilities and optimisation and collision avoidance tools enable programmers to quickly prepare their jobs.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal and cutting markets?

TC: Automation is unstoppable. We see more automated operations and a move towards more practical and affordable operations. Customers are also placing increasing emphasis on a unique offering or product and are manufacturing in smaller batches – adding value to their customers.

As a consequence of the global crisis in 2009, people’s buying habits have changed. In the past, they would buy the bigger product and risk overkill but now buyers are opting for a ‘good enough’ approach focused on today’s needs.

There is also more interest in new production methodologies such as 3D printing. Issues such as higher productivity, greater efficiency, lower cost and more environmental focus are always on the mind of customers. Bevel cutting has increased rapidly, for example, which saves on secondary manufacturing operations.

ISMR: Please give details of any new products launched this year.

TC: In March, we launched the Powermax 30®AIR, a new portable air plasma system and internal compressor in one. With the ability to operate on 120 or 240 volt lines, the Powermax30 AIR is a highly versatile system that enables metal cutting just about anywhere. It only weighs 3.5 kg but provides 30 amps of output, giving it the highest power-to-weight ratio of any system in its class. This system has been a great success for us. We intend to launch more products in the coming 12-18 months.

ISMR: Do you intend to launch any other products this year or attend any international trade shows? Please give details.

TC: We will be launching several new products at FABTECH in Chicago this November. Our new products include a system designed to significantly reduce waterjet operating costs by delivering marked labour and consumable savings for companies operating waterjet machines. In addition, we will preview new technology designed to simplify CNC and robotic cutting through the automatic inclusion of process parameters to support a wide range of applications. This new technology will be showcased on a new line-up of Hypertherm CNCs, which will also be unveiled at this year’s show.

Two more products making their FABTECH debut are designed to support cutting and gouging in ‘out of the way’ locations. Our Freedom 38 PPA is a completely autonomous, all-in-one unit that combines a powerful 125-amp plasma system with a generator and air compressor for cutting in areas without access to electricity.

This year’s FABTECH, at Chicago’s McCormick Place Convention Centre, takes place from 9-12 November. We will exhibit from the centre’s South hall in Booth S3340, while our partners will conduct live demonstrations of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting from more than a dozen booth locations throughout the show. Hypertherm experts in plasma, waterjet, automation and CAM software will also give a number of presentations as part of FABTECH’s official educational programme.
