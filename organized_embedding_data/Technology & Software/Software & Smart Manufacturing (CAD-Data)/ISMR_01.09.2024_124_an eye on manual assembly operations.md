# An eye on manual assembly operations

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Founded in 2003, InspecVision is a pioneer of metrology solutions for the sheet- metal industry. Its commitment to continuous investment in research and development has provided innovative solutions for optimising efficiency and ensuring quality control throughout production processes.

InspecVision is now launching its latest innovation: the Guidance and Verification (GAV) system. Designed to enhance the efficiency and quality control of manual production tasks, this system leverages machine learning, artificial intelligence and augmented reality to guide manual assembly operations and ensure that they are performed correctly. Potential applications include welding, aircraft assembly and wiring looms.

“The GAV system can direct operators on where to place inserts and verify correct placement. It also provides instructions for assembling and welding parts while ensuring each step is accurately completed. With its ability to see in 3D, the system learns how to perform manual operations by observing an operator build a part correctly, step by step. It can also be trained using a CAD file and will flag any inconsistencies between the CAD file and the physical part," said the company.

Once trained, the system guides operators in building additional parts by projecting each step onto the workpiece and verifying the accuracy of the assembly. The 3D vision capability enables the system to highlight errors, such as by projecting a red light onto areas where mistakes occur. The system requires no computer interaction or CAD files for operation. It is designed to be userfriendly and suitable for welding bays.
