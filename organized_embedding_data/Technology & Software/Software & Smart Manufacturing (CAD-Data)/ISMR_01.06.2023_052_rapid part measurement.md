# Rapid part measurement

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Measurement targets, or reference points, are often placed on the component surface to be scanned before the 3D scan. The points are used to combine the individual scan images into an overall scan of the complete component and are often essential for accurate scan data.

The scanning of large component parts, such as engine hoods, is now becoming even quicker using a new net of measurement targets. The Target-Net by AESUB is laid over the object to be scanned and, instantly, reference points can be provided across its entire area.

“The 3D scan process can start immediately. The target net is therefore an efficient alternative to the adhesive dots used in the past, which had to be glued on individually and then laboriously removed again after the measurement process," explained AESUB.

The measurement targets are distributed uniformly at intervals of approximately 100mm across the entire Target-Net. The Target-Net itself measures 1500 × 2500mm and has approximately 300 measurement targets. Using high measurement target density, several scans can be performed very quickly from different perspectives.

“For metallic parts, a net with magnetic measurement targets is available; measurement targets hold securely to the surface. With non-metallic parts (e.g. made from stone or plastic), the Target-Net is stretched across them or, depending on the material or surface, fixed with optionally available suction pads. The reference points 'float' approximately 10mm above the surface. The individual mesh threads are either not detected by the scanner or can be cut out with a few clicks in post-processing using suitable software," added the manufacturer.

The Target-Net can be packed away quickly and is easy to transport. Since the net can be reused almost indefinitely, said AESUB, this significantly reduces the amount of consumables that are required. Special sizes can be manufactured on request.

"Currently, the Target-Net is equipped with 6mm retro-reflective reference points. In the future, there will also be a version with 3mm points. On request, Target-Nets with non-reflective points can also be produced," concluded AESUB.

AESUB presented the Target-Net at Control 2023.
