# THE RIGHT TOOL

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

UK-based Formit Ltd has seen a distinct upturn in orders for design and manufacture of tooling for complex rollformed profiles.

“Leading subcontract section manufacturers are increasingly turning to us for design and manufacture tooling for more intricate profiles,” Jim Bain, Formit’s managing director, told ISMR.

He attributes this success to Formit’s ability to simulate the rollforming sequence prior to manufacture of the rolls thereby eliminating costly re-machining and shortening delivery lead times.

AN EYE ON PRODUCTIVITY “As specialists in rollforming technology, we utilise the latest software with decades of experience to minimise downtime, increasing productivity and profits. We assist customers from initial concept, advising on section design to facilitate maximum efficiency of product and ease of manufacture, through to the finished production line,” Bain explained.

In recent months, Formit has supplied tooling and rollforming equipment for many industries including construction, automotive, heating and ventilation, aerospace and increasingly “green” projects in line with current UK government initiatives.

These include solar panels, double glazing and insulated cladding etc.

Current contracts include an innovative lock-seamed tube production line with automated inline powder infill, a suspended ceiling Tee grid section and a specialised rollformer producing three products on the same machine with circling facility.

Formit also provides a consultancy service to the rollforming industry, as well as coil processing and press feeding machinery, including service, repair or complete refurbishment of pre-owned equipment.

Installation and commissioning worldwide together with relocation of existing production lines is another Formit service. ■
