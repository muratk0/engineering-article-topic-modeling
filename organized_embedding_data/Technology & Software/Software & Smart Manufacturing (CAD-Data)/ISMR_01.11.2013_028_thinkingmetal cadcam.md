# THINKINGMETAL CAD/CAM

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

AT the Northern Manufacturing exhibition, Lantek recently displayed the 2013 version of its Lantek Expert CADCAM software which incorporates a new ‘ThinkingMetal Technology’ artificial intelligence algorithm. This produces significant improvements in nesting efficiency and cutting methodology resulting in savings in both material and production time. New functions include sheet turning, the ability to re-order cutting operations and the rectangular cutting of the skeleton of the metal sheet.

In addition, visitors saw the Lantek Flex3d software which has been redeveloped to enable it to share data with the majority of CAD and BIM software available in the market. The optimised user interface gives improved simulation and turret management as well as advances in 5-axis technology for inclined cutting in corners and speed control for water jet machines.

Plasma cutting using Lantek software

“Options to connect to a company’s management and CAD systems have been increased with the new version using XML, DWG/DXF, SAT, IGES and STEP. These make it possible to tailor the level of integration to suit the individual user and to phase its introduction to ensure that it is effective and productive. Other data exchange improvements allow the import of hole locations in tubes and drilled holes in standard section profiles such as I, U, L and T,” added Lantek.
