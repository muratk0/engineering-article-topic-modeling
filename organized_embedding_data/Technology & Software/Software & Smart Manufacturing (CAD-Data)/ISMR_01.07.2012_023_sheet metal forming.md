# SHEET METAL FORMING

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GmbH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has unveiled its latest software version, AutoFormplus R4.

"AutoFormplus R4 development was driven by the vision of a comprehensive digital process planning system. It allows users to quantify and evaluate all planning aspects of stamped sheet metal parts, such as function, quality, lead time and cost. The new and ergonomically designed AutoFormplus user interface integrates these aspects and allows users to reach a new level of competitiveness," the company told ISMR. AutoFormplus R4 is the fourth major release from the new AutoFormplus product line.

"AutoFormplus R4 allows a complete quantitative product line piece cost analysis as material, production and tooling costs can be determined at any stage of the planning process. Interpreting results can be carried out more quickly and easily. Semiautomatic issue identification facilitates and speeds up the identification of stamping issues, their types and locations. AutoFormplus R4 also features full AutoForm-Sigma integration, enabling engineers to systematically resolve forming problems and improve forming processes," added the company.

For projects engineered and simulated using AutoForm software, a report file can be generated and is now integrated into the AutoForm design file. Along with the new software version, AutoForm Engineering is also releasing AutoForm-ProcessDesignerforCATIA which represents a further integration of the AutoForm methodology into the CATIA environment and allows for the rapid creation of CAD quality die faces.

AutoFormplus R4 supports the usage and management of individual company standards, such as costing, dieface and simulation standards. It will be available on the market in Autumn 2012. See www.autoform.com

## An eye on welding

ITALIAN MANUFACTURER Cemsa S.p.A. recently released a new MRL-F series seam welding machine which facilitates the production of tubes for air, gas, exhaust smoke etc. The system is also designed for solutions such as the weldable assembly of water heaters for inner and outer bodies. The main materials it can process are galvanized steel, low carbon and stainless steel.

“The special double CNC push/pull system means high welding quality as well as the best possible aesthetic results. A further advanced solution has been ‘built-in’ to facilitate downstream processes like flanging, beading etc.,” explained Cemsa.

The machine pictured is the model for welding products with thicknesses ranging from 0.5 to 1.5mm, up to 1500mm in length, with diameters ranging from 100 to 900mm. Other versions are also available. See www.cemsa.it for more details.
