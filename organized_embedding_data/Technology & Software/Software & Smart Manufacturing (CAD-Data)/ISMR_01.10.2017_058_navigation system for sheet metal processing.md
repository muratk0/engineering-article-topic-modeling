# Navigation system for sheet metal processing

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

BYSTRONIC AND LANTEK are developing an MES software solution for sheet metal part production. The Bystronic MES interlinks all the manufacturing steps and guides sheet metal products on the ideal path through the production process, much like an intelligent navigation system. It will enable users to record, plan and evaluate every individual manufacturing step of their sheet metal products. Meet Bystronic on its booth 1712 in Hall 1 at Blechexpo in Stuttgart this November.

The intelligent software helps to manufacture and ship products with precisely defined costs and deadlines. An assistant creates transparency and describes the ideal path to guide sheet metal products through increasingly complex manufacturing environments. Nowadays, for sheet metal processing companies that want to produce on time and in a cost-optimised manner, all these considerations lead to a Manufacturing Execution System (MES). This planning system helps them to digitally record all the available resources within their production environment and to make ideal use of them.

## The path through complex production

"The Bystronic MES has been designed to create digital transparency across the entire added value chain of sheet metal products. All the steps from the incoming order right through to the shipping of the completed product are digitally recorded. This enables products to be manufactured according to defined costs and deadlines. The Bystronic MES is the brain for the entire production process. It never forgets an order, despite the hectic activity of day-to-day business. It is forward-thinking and maintains an overview of all the open production parts in the order pool," Bystronic told ISMR.

On the shop floor, it allocates the created cutting and bending plans to the resources of the available machines. Mix-ups of production parts with allocated cutting or bending plans are virtually ruled out. This prevents rejects. The MES also enables users to avoid bottlenecks at manufacturing stations as well as long idle times.

In the future, procurement and logistics will provide the required materials depending upon the order situation and supply them "just in time". This makes manufacturing processes leaner. Throughput times and tied-up capital are reduced and streamlined to achieve maximum efficiency in the face of fluctuating order situations.

"Ultimately, customers can always obtain precise information on the current manufacturing stage of their products and when they will be delivered. After completion of the order, the manufacturing data is available for evaluation," added Bystronic.

The Bystronic MES will enable the controlling department to precisely calculate whether the actual and target values relating to costs, times and material are within the defined limits. Questions on rejects and idle times accrued at the individual manufacturing stations can also be answered.

## Modular start and gradual growth

The Bystronic MES has a modular design. The entry-level version features an extensive basic package that includes the most important functions to allow the MES system to be integrated into the user's infrastructure. Initial upgrade modules will expand the functionality of the basic version, according to the customer's requirements. Bystronic and Lantek are already developing additional solutions. This enables users to build their customised Bystronic MES that is tailored precisely to their manufacturing environment. Once the basic version of the Bystronic MES has been implemented, users can digitally integrate additional manufacturing stations on a step-by-step basis. This enables the Bystronic MES to grow with the user's manufacturing environment.

The basic version of the MES initially enables the interactive integration of the laser cutting process step. The bending process step will follow next. Bystronic enables open data transfer with its BySoft 7 programming software and networking with Bystronic's own laser cutting systems. Amongst other things, the end-to-end integration facilitates feedback on the manufacturing status of the machines. Integrated Bystronic cutting systems provide fully automatic status messages about the start of cutting jobs, the remaining processing time and the completion of jobs. This information flows via the Bystronic MES back to the user's ERP system.

In the same way, the cutting plans from BySoft 7 are transmitted directly to the MES and from there they are distributed to the integrated Bystronic laser cutting systems. Conversely, production parts that already exist in virtual form as a cutting job in the Bystronic MES can be adapted in BySoft 7 at any time. From there, the updated data flows back to the Bystronic MES and then onto the allocated laser cutting system.

In addition to the interactive integration of the laser cutting process, the Bystronic MES also ties in manufacturing stations with third-party systems and manual workstations. These manufacturing stations return simple status messages about the beginning and completion of the processing steps to the Bystronic MES.

Bystronic is driving forward the digital integration of sheet metal part production at its own business location. The Bystronic MES is already being used successfully at Bystronic's Swiss headquarters in Niederönz. See www.bystronic.com

## Industry 4.0 welding portal

"With the new B2B portal, the Internet of Things is no longer just a bubble," emphasised Björn Kemper, Managing Director, Kemper GmbH. "The Internet-based cloud solution developed with our partner, Datacake, is a safe and cost-effective alternative for manufacturer-centred networks, specifically for medium-sized business."

KEMPER GMBH PRESENTED a new cross-manufacturer B2B portal at the recent Schweissen and Schneiden exhibition. This portal can network equipment and systems that are Internet of Things-enabled, beyond its own extraction technology – overheads can therefore be significantly reduced. As part of this, Kemper equips new extraction plants and room ventilation systems with a web-enabled control system.

The portal networks Kemper extraction technology as well as other IoT-enabled machines by other manufacturers. Thanks to the open standard, the user can integrate and monitor them on the www.gistry.io portal, as R&D manager Simon Kemper, who co-developed the platform, explained: "The user brings all equipment and systems together in a clear portal and manages and controls them centrally."

When selecting a device, the portal visualises current operating parameters and other properties location-independent on smartphone, tablet or PC. Measuring data, settings and other events are analysed automatically and this information is made available to other machines in the network. The system is therefore able to, for example, predict a maintenance requirement.

At Kemper's booth (as well as Kemppi's) at Schweissen and Schneiden, the company also displayed its AirWatch air monitoring systems. The portal transfers the data captured by AirWatch at the Kemppi stand to the Kemppi WeldEye software. Kemppi was therefore able to integrate the external Kemper sensor system. Kemper has also digitised its own portfolio as part of the introduction of the new network solution. New equipment and system generations equip the company by default with a newly developed control system. They communicate via the cellular network using special IoT SIM cards. See www.kemper.eu ■

## A smooth surface

AFTER THE SUCCESS of its Red Dot Award-winning Rotary Brush machine, Timesavers has developed a new deburring machine based on the same technology. The 32 RB series is a compact deburring and edgerounding machine with a smaller working width of 1050mm and four brushes instead of eight. This new Rotary Brush solution for edge rounding, deburring, smoothing and finishing for multiple materials is designed to be versatile – more innovative aggregates are available for the 32 series.

"The machine is easy to use due to the user-friendly pre-programmable Siemens HMI interface. The machine will feature a similar look as the Red Dot Award-winning 42 RB series design," explained the company.

At this year's Blechexpo in Stuttgart, the manufacturer will also be showcasing its 42 RB series WWRBW 1350mm for deburring and finishing, heavy slag removal, removal of the laser oxide skin and/or edge-rounding in one pass. The 42 RB series is using eight multi-directional brushes in combination with multiple grinding heads. Its new look 22 series will also be shown for the first time during the exhibition, featuring the same design as the award-winning 32- and 42 series. The company's 10 Series manual grinder will also be on display.■
