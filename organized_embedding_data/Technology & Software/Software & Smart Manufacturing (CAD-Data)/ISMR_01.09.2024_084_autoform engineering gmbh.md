# AutoForm Engineering GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand G52

www.autoform.com

AutoForm offers software solutions for sheet metal forming and BiW (body-in-white) processes. With over 400 employees dedicated to this field, AutoForm has become a leading provider of software for product manufacturability, tool and material cost calculation, die face design and virtual stamping as well as BiW assembly process optimisation.

"All of the Top 20 automotive OEMs, and most of their suppliers, have selected AutoForm as their software of choice," it explained.

Besides its headquarters in Switzerland, AutoForm also has offices in Germany; The Netherlands; France; Spain; Italy; Czech Republic; Sweden; USA; Mexico; Brazil; India; China; Japan and Korea. AutoForm is also present through its agents in more than 13 other countries.
