# 3D scanning of large parts

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Placing the reference points required for the 3D scanning of large-sized parts can be time-consuming and laborious. AESUB is therefore now offering continuous dots on a roll which can be quickly removed and placed on large areas. The over 50 metres-long, transparent adhesive strip contains retroreflective reference points measuring 6mm along its length. The dots are distributed across the adhesive strip at irregular intervals of 50-70mm.

"Because the adhesive tape is perforated at intervals of approximately 50cm, it can easily be adapted to the relevant part size. The transparent film is flexible and adapts to freeform surfaces, even without folding. In a few seconds, the continuous dots have been positioned which means that the scanning process can begin immediately," explained the manufacturer.

"Because the carrier film is transparent and very thin, it is only the dots that are recognised and scanning is possible through the carrier material. After scanning, the adhesive tape can be removed just as quickly and easily. With large component parts, the continuous dots save several hours of work spent affixing and removing the measurement points. In addition, handling is significantly more ergonomic compared to individual adhesive dots," it continued.

AESUB Infinity was launched at the Control exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany, from 23-26 April 2024.■
