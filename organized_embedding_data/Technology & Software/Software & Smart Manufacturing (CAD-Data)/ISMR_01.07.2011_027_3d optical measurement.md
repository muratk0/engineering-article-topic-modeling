# 3D OPTICAL MEASUREMENT

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

CREAFORM, A LEADER in portable 3D optical measurement technologies and 3D engineering services, has had its second best quarter in history, with US$ 8.2 million sales and a YOY growth of 56 per cent. It offers innovative solutions that cover the entire range of 3D applications, including 3D scanning, reverse engineering, inspection, design and engineering and FEA.

The company has just launched its latest product, the VXtrack dynamic tracking software module to be used with its portable HandyPROBE arm-free CMM and MetraSCAN optical CMM 3D scanner. Creaform has developed and sells the Handyscan 3D line of portable, self-positioning handheld 3D scanners that generate accurate 3D scanning data.

Creaform also manufactures and sells the HandyPROBE fully portable arm-free CMM and the MetraSCAN optical CMM 3D scanner, two systems designed for inspection applications. The company also offers Creaform 3D Body Digitizers.
