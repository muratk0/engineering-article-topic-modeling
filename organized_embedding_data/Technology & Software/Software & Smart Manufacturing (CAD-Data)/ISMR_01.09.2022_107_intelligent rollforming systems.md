# Intelligent rollforming systems

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

AMB Picot, a French rollforming specialist that develops intelligent three- and four-roll rolling machines, will present its latest innovations at the EuroBLECH trade show in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022). Show visitors can find the company on stand A26 in Hall 11.

"We will showcase our 100% electric rollbending machine, specialising in thin sheet-metal processing, at EuroBLECH. It will be the world premiere of this system which is optimised for energy consumption, rolling accuracy and simplicity of maintenance. It is equipped with a touch-screen industrial PC controller and the latest version (V4) of the Easyroll© rolling assistance software," it told SMR.

"The CNC rolling assistant, EasyRoll includes five major modules that enable the user to become very easily familiar with the Picot three-roll bending machine through an ergonomic interface. This means intuitive and efficient rolling that will accommodate both experienced and unexperienced users," it added.

Version 4 of this patented software, launched in June 2022, uses artificial intelligence to provide powerful assistance to the user by calculating any necessary corrections of the rolling program. This feature, based on the effectively measured rolled profile entered by the operator, automatically updates the program and fine-tunes the dedicated material database.
