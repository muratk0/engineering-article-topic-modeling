## Major efficiency gains come from having the right tool available when the job walks in the door.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers right now and how are you addressing these issues for them?

MR: Our customers are mostly focused on improving efficiency and productivity. We work with prospective and existing customers to identify opportunities to realize those gains with abrasive waterjet. Outsourcing and processes that use suboptimal machine tools or accessories are often the best candidates for improvement. Our software is also a tool that can help owners track utilisation and optimise settings for elements such as speed and cost per part.

ISMR: Please can you highlight any particular trends that you see developing in sheet metal waterjet cutting markets?

MR: Whether times are difficult economically or not, metal working professionals are always looking to be more efficient. It doesn’t really matter whether it’s 2020 or 1920, it’s still about how many parts you can get out of the door. Major efficiency gains come from having the right tool available when the job walks in the door.

The other trend emerging in the waterjet industry is micromachining. Since waterjets cut without heat-affected zones, and there is little side pressure (compared to traditional CNCs), the technology is beginning to be used to manufacture intricate telecommunication, aerospace and medical parts. Often these parts are made from a lightweight composite or speciality metal that doesn’t cut very well using laser or plasma cutting technology.

Along with the trends towards miniaturisation and personalisation that are creeping into the waterjet sector, the move towards automated and smart systems is another key trend.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

MR: For a lot of CNC-type work, the major challenge is finding a qualified workforce. At OMAX, we view this as an opportunity to make our products very easy to use. For example, our software is extremely easy to use - anyone with even a basic knowledge of CAD/CAM can be trained on OMAX software in less than a week. Our software controller, IntelliMAX, is extremely user-friendly; we’ve helped customers reduce the learning curve when onboarding new personnel to work on their machines.

We also offer extensive training options, onsite, at OMAX (as well as online) to help customers run their waterjets well.
