## AICON 3D SYSTEMS GMBH

D–Hall 11, Stand F38

AICON 3D Systems is one of the world’s leading providers of optical 3D measurement systems. At EuroBLECH, AICON will showcase TubeInspect – a tube and wire measurement system with optical gauge. TubeInspect is specially designed for the measurement of formed tubes and wires. The system can also calculate set-up and correctional data for bending machines in seconds (www.tubeinspect.de). AICON will also present its MoveInspect Technology - modular portable CMMs for a variety of measurement tasks. See www.moveinspect-technology.com

## ALMA GMBH CAM-SYSTEME

D–Hall 11, Stand G35 Website: www.almacam.de Alma will demonstrate its whole CAD/CAM software suite ‘act’ for 2D cutting (laser, plasma, oxy-cutting, waterjet); 3D cutting (5 axes and robots); tube cutting; bending on CNC press brakes and robotised welding. ## AutoForm Engineering Deutschland GmbH

D–Hall 11, Stand G29

AutoForm offers software solutions for the diemaking and sheet metal forming industries. The software improves reliability in planning, reduces the number of die tryouts and tryout time and results in higher quality part and tool designs. In addition, press downtime and reject rates in production are substantially reduced. AutoForm’s solutions form a complete, integrated system with highly specialised functions to analyse, review and optimise every phase of the process chain.

## BENDIRO PROFILE TECH AB

Website: www.bendiro.se

Bendiro is a leading Scandinavian manufacturer and supplier of advanced rollformed profiles. It manufactures laser welded, perforated tubes in ultra high strength steel and provides innovative rollforming with 2.5D and patented 3D rollforming technology. Rollforming of advanced profiles is done according to the principle ‘metal strip in – ready to use product out’. Integrated rollforming of profiles can be shaped as rings or ring-segments. 3D technology enables profiles of variable cross sections and curved shapes.

## CODATTO INTERNATIONAL SPA

Codatto International S.p.A. will introduce the latest version of its EVA automatic bending system for sheet metal. ## COLGAR SPA

Colgar SpA will showcase its synchronised hydraulic press brake model PIS 30091/121 TAC. Maximum working tonnage (adjustable) is 30,000kN and the distance between housings is 9100mm. Maximum bending length is 12,100mm. The hydraulic system is composed of two separate variable displacement pumps: the two pumps directly manage the two cylinders, calibrating oil flow during the bending cycle. The back gauge features three separate turrets of 3 X/Z/R axes each.

## FACCIN S.R.L.

I – Hall 11, Stand E14
Website: www.faccin.com

Faccin’s 4-roll plate bending machine 4HEL series with computerised numerical control software PGS-ULTRA (with graphic interface) combines accuracy and speed. The MCS multi-bearing system ensures that each roll is supported by four independent bearings which enable load distribution and alignment of the bending axis. “With the Faccin CNC, even an operator without experience, just by following the instructions on the screen, can bend cylinders, ovals and open shapes with variable radii,“ said the manufacturer.

## FLADDER DANMARK A/S

DK– Hall 11, Stand F30
Website: www.fladder.com

The FLADDER AUT-1000 VAC is an automatic machine for de-burring of even workpieces, cut workpieces and shaped workpieces. The flexible abrasive cylinders process the workpieces with four different movements so that they are de-burred several times from different directions. It is possible to de-burr inox without damaging the protection foil or de-burr galvanized workpieces without removing the zinc. Owing to the flexibility of the abrasive tools, workpieces with contours or grooves may be de-burred as deeply as 30mm, in some cases.
