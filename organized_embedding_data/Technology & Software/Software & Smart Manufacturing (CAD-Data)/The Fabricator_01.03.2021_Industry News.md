Miller Fabrication Solutions partners with Clarion University on robotics programming curriculum

A new partnership between heavy metal fabricator Miller Fabrication Solutions, Brookville, Pa., and Clarion University adds applied industry knowledge to an upcoming robotics programming concentration in the university’s computer science and information systems degrees.

Miller’s automation and robotics personnel provided curriculum recommendations on programming languages used in automation and on the manufacturing application of robots. Besides assisting in framing curriculum for the five classes that comprise the robotics programming concentration, Miller will look for other ways to support the university, located in Clarion, Pa., from engaging its own robot programmers in classroom instruction to arranging student tours of Miller plants or making equipment and facilities available for student use.

"In taking a new approach to education, I’m more of-ten reaching out to companies, asking them what knowledge and skills they’re looking for in new hires and then adding the relevant content in our courses," said Dr. Jeff Childs, chair of Clarion’s Department of Computer Information Science. "The partnership with Miller ensures our computer information science students that when they complete the robotics programming concentration and apply to Miller aer graduation, they’re prepared for the job."

IFS Coatings announces carbon offset program

Powder coatings manufacturer IFS Coatings Inc. has announced it is now purchasing monthly carbon offsets to further its work toward becoming more sustainable.

The carbon offset initiative builds on existing environmental responsibility programs and is part of a long-term strategy to improve the company’s environmental impact and ensure a more sustainable business for customers, employees, suppliers, and communities.

"We are constantly looking at ways in which we can reduce our environmental impact, and using the terrapass program to offset our carbon footprint is now a key part of that," said Glynn Mason, president.

Waterjet USA installs recordbreaking machine at Front Street Shipyard

Waterjet USA LLC, Geneva, Ill., has installed a 5-axis, 3D waterjet cutting machine at Front Street Shipyard, a custom boat builder, boatyard, marina, and service yard in Belfast, Maine. The Suprema DX 1340 is the largest ever built and installed in the U.S. by Waterjet USA, and it took about two weeks to assemble.

The machine has a 50-. table and lathe attachment and can cut stainless steel, titanium, and other materials with an accuracy of 0.002 in. The lathe attachment for the waterjet is designed for tubular indexing or turning applications.

While the shipyard will use the waterjet system for in-house work on commercial and recreational vessel retrofits, it plans to use the machine to cut custom specialty parts for manufacturers worldwide.
