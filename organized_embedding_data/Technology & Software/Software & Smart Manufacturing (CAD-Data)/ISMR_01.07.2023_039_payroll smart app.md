# Payroll smart app

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Hourly.io sells workers' comp insurance policies. Its cloud-based workers' comp and payroll platform connects real-time payroll data with workers' comp policy to ensure the accuracy of premiums.

“We’re the only company to combine workers' comp insurance, payroll and time-tracking in one software platform for small and medium businesses in the skilled trade industries (sheet metal included) and contractors. While other companies use payroll estimates to calculate workers' comp premiums, Hourly uses real payroll numbers to ensure that your business never overpays and won't be hit with a big audit bill. We recently saved a painting company from a surprise US$ 81K audit bill," it told ISMR.

“Hourly collects millions of data points to make insurance premiums accurate and to save people money. It automatically calculates premiums based on total monthly payroll. Payroll errors are slashed in half by collecting time data automatically without the hassle of manually calculating and importing time-card data," it added.

The platform tracks time, task and location data in real-time, making it easy to organize hours by client, project or location. Features include unlimited payroll runs; same-day direct deposit; next-day paper check delivery; automatic tax filing; 1099s and W2s; seamless workers' comp integration; fully managed garnishments; Quickbooks integration; unlimited phone support; employee document centre and precision time tracking. Other features include GPS tracking; geofencing; real-time alerts; overtime and double time; compliance logs; kiosk mode and job costing reports.

It is available for Apple and Android as an app and also on PCs as a web app. Two pricing plan options are offered (Gold and Platinum plans).

"Many of our customers are contractors in the sheet-metal industry. We were recently featured in the New York Post as ‘a smart app that can make your work life easier' and in Forbes. TechRadar even ranked us as one of the 'best payroll software for small businesses in 2023′," the company explained.
