# Seamless order tracking

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

WHAT HAPPENS WHEN production knowhow, industry experience, top quality and service are combined with customer-specific information? The result is a platform for more ideas, more transparency, more freedom of action and therefore also more value added.

Mbo Osswald is further extending its online portal with newly introduced services and is placing the focus on end-to-end, transparent processes, intuitive, efficient operation and resulting customer benefits beyond the hardware itself.

"End-to-end, forward-looking production planning can tolerate no gaps in available information. That is why, with immediate effect, we are offering seamless order tracking for all items. And not just from the moment that the ordered products are handed over to the parcel service, but right through from our raw materials planning to delivery to the customer's goods-in point. Each individual step benefits from clear, dedicated tracking – live and around-the-clock!," it told ISMR.

"Whether customers want to split an order, bring forward a delivery or check whether it is still possible to increase the ordered item quantity – in the future, customers will have all the information they need to make their own decisions."

What makes this solution so versatile? It makes no difference whether orders are placed via the mbo Osswald Shop or conventionally via e-mail or telephone. A tracking code is always issued so that customers can stay up-to-date at all times by clicking a mouse.

"Users don't need to search for the compatible accessories – they are there waiting for you. The product group links to 'Compatible Accessories' in the linking technology sector are intuitive. After choosing the perfect clevis, no-one wants to search endlessly in more extensive lists to find compatible bolts, washers and pins. Instead, visitors to the mbo Osswald platform can now directly access the underlying parts lists for assemblies and joints. All the relevant accessory parts are then displayed in a clearly structured way, together with quantity-dependent information on prices and delivery times. Users can click on them to add them easily and securely to an order," added the manufacturer.

mbo Osswald is a leading supplier of linking technology. At the Kuelsheim-Steinbach site, 70 employees manufacture more than 12,000 standard parts and are specialists in the production of individual drawn parts to meet specific customer requirements. Its portfolio includes standard parts; clevises; clevis joints; bolts; retainers; drawn parts; angle joints; ball sockets; ball studs; axial joints; rod ends; pivoting bearings; knuckle eyes; cardan joints and machine elements.
