## Bystronic and Kurago become innovation partners

TO CREATE PIONEERING process solutions that drive forward a smart factory environment, Bystronic is joining forces with the software specialist, Kurago, under the framework of an innovation partnership.

“The demand for automation and digital process solutions is increasing across the complete field of sheet metal processing. This trend is having a major impact on our customers' business models and strategies. The sheet metal processing world will face a fundamental change in the near future and software will play a key role in this transformation. Digital solutions will support users with the planning, interlinking, monitoring and optimisation of all their production processes. All this will boost the sheet metal processing industry to new levels of productivity and transparency,” Bystronic told ISMR.

“To develop pioneering process solutions that will drive forward the vision of a smart factory environment, we are now working with software specialist Kurago within the framework of an innovation partnership. By joining forces, we will be able to fast-track solutions to make a powerful vision come true very soon."

The first important step of this partnership, added Bystronic, will be the development of a new enterprise operational environment for the sheet metal processing sector, where all the processes relating to the day-to-day business of its customers will take place. Users, it added, would be able to achieve operational excellence and gain a competitive edge.

“Cloud technology will enable our customers to expand our solution as their company grows, reducing the total cost of ownership (TCO). In addition, this solution will allow tech companies of all sizes to develop and offer new products and applications designed specifically for the sheet metal processing sector," explained Bystronic.

Kurago is based in Spain and has offices in Bilbao and Vitoria-Gasteiz. Together with Bystronic, the company will conceive, create, promote and implement software designed to optimise the manufacturing of metal parts. Kurago will develop manufacturing systems aimed at making the smart factory vision a reality and guide and structure sheet metal processing throughout the entire value chain – with all types of machines from any manufacturer.

Alex Waser, CEO of the Bystronic Group, concluded:
“We are convinced that this partnership will allow Bystronic and Kurago to pool their expertise and energy to achieve a sustainable impact on future trends and demands in the sheet metal processing industry. Our shared goal is to offer our customers pioneering solutions that drive them forward on the path towards a digitally extended manufacturing environment." See www.bystronic.com ■
