# An eye on automation

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

TRUMPF presented a new automated bending cell for the mid-range price segment at this November's Blechexpo trade show in Stuttgart. The TruBend Cell 5000 Lean Edition can operate without human intervention for around eight hours, depending upon the geometry of the parts being produced.

"Automation is one of the biggest trends that we're seeing in bending going forward. For users on a limited budget, the TruBend Cell 5000 Lean Edition is an excellent way to get started with automated bending and make production more competitive," said Simon Hiebl, who heads product management for bending systems and premium solutions at TRUMPF.

To create the new TruBend Cell 5000 Lean Edition, the TRUMPF engineers drew on tried-and-tested hardware and software solutions, including its BendMaster bending robot and TruTops Boost programming software. TRUMPF offers the TruBend Cell 5000 Lean Edition in two variants, each of which can be supplied either with or without the small-parts package. With the basic variant, fabricators can process parts ranging in size from A4 to a Euro-pallet format. By adding the small-parts package, they can also bend small parts of a similar size to a business card.

The new machine is suitable for manufacturing small to mid-sized parts, such as electric charge points, control cabinets and PC housings. The bending cell is also simple to program using the intuitive interface of TRUMPF's TruTops Boost software. Users can accommodate two different grippers inside the bending cell, which makes switching the machine from one part to another fast, flexible and automatic. TRUMPF launched both variants of this new solution at Blechexpo.

## Automated panel bending

TRUMPF's stand at the Blechexpo exhibition also showcased a new robotic bending solution developed in collaboration with its automation specialist partner, Starmatik. The solution, developed for systems supplier Kuipers, features two robots from Starmatik which work simultaneously to load and unload TRUMPF's TruBend Center 7020 panel-bending machine.

“This reduces the cycle time for loading by up to 50 per cent. The machine itself includes new high-speed functionalities that can boost productivity by up to 30 per cent. Equipped with this automation solution from Starmatik, the TruBend Center 7020 is one of the world's fastest solutions for automated bending, offering efficient results for both volume production and prototypes," TRUMPF told ISMR.

Italian company, Starmatik, specialises in robotic automation systems for machines in the sheet-metal industry. The company has been working with TRUMPF on automated bending cells since September 2021. TRUMPF is increasingly incorporating solutions from partners into the strategy for its Machine Tools division.

## Automated punch-laser

TRUMPF also launched its TruMatic 5000 manufacturing cell with the new SheetMaster at Blechexpo in Stuttgart for fully automated laser-cutting, punching and forming capabilities. The new SheetMaster enables fully automated flow of materials within the manufacturing cell from loading and unloading the machine to removing finished parts.

“This solution is designed to be seamlessly integrated in a self-contained smart factory, giving users efficiency gains along the entire process chain," said TRUMPF product manager, Steffen Wagner.

TRUMPF's new machine is equipped with a 6kW fibre laser to maximize productivity. The system comes with a descending die that prevents parts from being scratched during the punching process. The TruMatic 5000 punching head also comes with a patented TRUMPF electric drive, known as the DeltaDrive, which reportedly uses around 30 per cent less energy.

TRUMPF has fully automated the material flow in the TruMatic 5000. This improvement was made possible by new SheetMaster technology, which uses grippers to handle parts in the manufacturing cell. The suction cups on the new SheetMaster's grippers are monitored by sensors and can be activated individually. This makes gripper operation more flexible, speeding up workpiece handling and saving valuable time especially for smaller batches.

The SheetMaster can be connected to an automated STOPA storage system which automatically supplies the manufacturing cell with raw material. The system also sorts parts without human intervention. The new SheetMaster can eject cut and punched parts from the manufacturing cell. Parts can then be transported directly to the next stage of the process such as bending, deburring or grinding. This is designed to reduce non-productive time and increase efficiency on the shop floor.

The TruMatic 5000's automation technology also includes the ToolMaster, a tool changer with up to 90 different tool cartridges that can automatically switch between multiple punching and forming tools. The ToolMaster enables users to produce small, high-mix batches of parts without having to manually retool the machine. Also included is an automatic nozzle changer with a nozzle inspection function. This prevents unscheduled stoppages during the night, for example due to a damaged nozzle. Typical areas of application include industrial kitchens, control-cabinet manufacturing and the furniture industry.■
