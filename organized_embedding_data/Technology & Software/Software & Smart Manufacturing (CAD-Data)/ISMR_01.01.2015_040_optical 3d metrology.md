# Optical 3D metrology

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

AT EUROMOLD 2014 in Germany, GOM presented its optical desktop scanner, ATOS ScanPort. The scanner has been developed for the measurement and inspection of small components. It has a 3+3 kinematic with three motorised and three manual axes. Recurring measurement tasks are simplified by automated rotation, tilt and linear axis.

GOM has also extended its series of industrial 3D scanners with its model ATOS Triple Scan 16M. The new 3D digitizer captures two times 16 million measuring points per scan and is suitable for quality control of medium-sized components with high resolution requirements. Applications include measurement and inspection of complex injection moulded parts or turbine blades, where fine leading and trailing edges play an important role.

With the current V8 release, GOM has introduced the new version of its measurement and evaluation software, GOM Inspect Professional. The software package includes functions, which help to speed up processes in tool- and mould-making. ■
