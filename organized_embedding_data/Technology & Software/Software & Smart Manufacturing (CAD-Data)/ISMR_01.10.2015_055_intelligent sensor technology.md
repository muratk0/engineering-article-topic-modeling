# Intelligent sensor technology

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

SAFETY TECHNOLOGY of the future and solutions for vision and identification tasks will be the focus of SICK AG's presentation at the SPS IPC Drives trade fair in Nuremberg which will take place from 24–26 November 2015. The manufacturer of intelligent sensors and sensor solutions will also be demonstrating its wide range of solutions for many industrial applications.

“In the future, SICK safety sensors will be even more closely connected to one another. The challenges of the future include rapid commissioning for significantly faster system effectiveness and continuous production for increased system efficiency. In the future, SICK's smart sensors in the context of Industry 4.0 will not only be used to ensure the safety of people, but also the implementation of everincreasing production specifications,” explained the company.

SICK will also be using the trade fair as an opportunity to show a wide range of solutions that do more than “just” see. These solutions are already prepared for the requirements of Industry 4.0 - they visualize process data and use it to create continuous process optimization using global data transparency, which facilitates industry-wide networked communication. See the company in Hall 7A, stand 340 at the SPS IPC Drives trade fair.

Quick-change systems with SAFE-LOCK

FIPA (www.fipa.com) has expanded its product portfolio to include quick-change systems featuring SAFE-LOCK. A snap-on mechanism, combined with a visual locking indication, further improves process safety.

“The SAFE-LOCK mechanism, with snap-on function, ensures a reliable connection between robot and gripper which can also withstand strong vibrations and rapid acceleration. Thanks to the visual locking indication (red or green), the operator can immediately tell if the quick-change system is securely closed and thus ready for operation. The pneumatic connectors are always correctly aligned which adds an extra safety element to the process. Quick change-systems with SAFE-LOCK are made from high strength, anodised aluminium alloy and are available in diameters of 50mm, 90mm and 150mm,” FIPA told ISMR.
