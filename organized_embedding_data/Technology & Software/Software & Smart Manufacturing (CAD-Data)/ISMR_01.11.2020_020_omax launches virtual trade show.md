# OMAX launches virtual trade show

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

OMAX’s new virtual trade show ‘Don’t Take Our Word For It’, on 1 October 2020, featured a range of customer insights into waterjet productivity. Attendees learned how and why abrasive waterjet technology has become an essential component of versatile productivity for manufacturers in numerous fields.

During the event, Eric Vossman, owner of Machine Repair & Design, hosted a virtual tour of his shop and shared his company’s experiences in using two OMAX JetMachining Centers to fabricate parts from concept to reality for aerospace, industrial, medical and consumer industries.

In addition to customer-driven insights into the use of abrasive waterjets for a wide variety of production runs, this virtual trade show event presented OMAX service and support and provided attendees with online training and maintenance resources to optimise their own processes. Presentations from OMAX Vice-President of Sales, Steve Ulmer, and Regional Sales Manager, Greg Smith, shared many ways that OMAX abrasive waterjets have heightened customers’ manufacturing uptime and responsiveness.

Vossman’s shop routinely uses abrasive waterjet technology to cut materials ranging from 2024 aluminium alloy to 12-inch-thick Inconel® 625, as well as steels, titanium, plastics, laminates and more. This is the fifth in a series of OMAX virtual trade shows, with a complete library of previous events available at www.omax.com/virtual-tradeshows
