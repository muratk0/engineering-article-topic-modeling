The new TUBOMAT pipe processing system

www.datam.de

BOOTH 4520

Rollformed profiles are used for many different applications in various industry fields, while the line requirements of each business vary greatly. Different specifications call for verified manufacturing strategies that are tailor-made for respective job definitions. With its product portfolio, data M offers an integrated rollforming solution concept, consisting of four parts, for the design and simulation of the entire rollforming process as well as devices for the quality control of roll tools and profiles.

“While COPRA® RF covers the complete engineering process with a flower design, calculation and a profile design (among others), COPRA® FEA RF provides simulation using non-linear elastoplastic calculation for a variety of possible problems that profile manufacturers today are facing. COPRA® RollScanner and COPRA® ProfileScan Desktop round off the package by offering accurate optical measurement of roll tools as well as profiles with full integration into the COPRA® workflow,” explained the company.

## TRACTO-TECHNIK GmbH & Co. KG

www.pipe-bending-systems.com

Over more than five decades, TRACTO-TECHNIK has acquired comprehensive experience in the mechanical working and machining of pipes. It now presents a complex system for processing media-bearing pipes: PIPE BENDING SYSTEMS stands for smart system solutions for the processing of pipes including systems for single-part and small-series production with great product variety.

“Fundamentally revised, completely modularly designed, functionally and ergonomically optimised – yet still as robust and reliable as ever – our new TUBOMAT generation is a pioneer in hydraulic pipe processing. The powerful bending machine can integrate the following pipe processing functions – sawing, deburring, flaring and cutting ring assembly. The modern control concept enables program operation as well as a 4-fold bending angle pre-selection mode and also offers the option of importing pipe bending data from the PIPEFAB software. Various detail improvements and optimisation of workflows also characterise the new TUBOMAT working centre,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

## DREISTERN GmbH & Co. KG

www.dreistern.com

“Thanks to their high production speed, rollforming systems are often not fully utilised with a single product. It is therefore appropriate to manufacture several different products on one system. Our production systems are designed for rapid tool change and frequent product change-overs,” Dreistern told ISMR.

Another technology from Dreistern, multifunctional rollforming technology, considerably enhances this capability. It is based on standardised processing modules for punching, bending or welding that can be quickly integrated at any point on the rollforming machine. With universal compatibility and the related flexible control unit, individual processing modules can also be used on different machines. The company will be showcasing flexible modules at its booth at Blechexpo.
