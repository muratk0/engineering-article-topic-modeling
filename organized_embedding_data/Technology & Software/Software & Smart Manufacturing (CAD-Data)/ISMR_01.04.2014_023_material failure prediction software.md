# Material failure prediction software

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

THE Altair Partner Alliance (APA) recently announced the addition of material failure prediction software offering DigitalClone, by Sentient Science, which will allow HyperWorks’ users to better control product lifecycles and predict failure probability.

Sentient Science develops technology that improves machinery design, testing, manufacturing and maintenance. Its proprietary software and sensor technology deploys patent-pending algorithms that can predict the future life of a component, based on its composition material.

The web-based software, DigitalClone Component™, determines the future life and performance of rotating components including bearings, gears and splines. Design and testing teams can interrogate DigitalClone material microstructure models to test the variance of a product’s reliability under trade studies such as different duty cycles, materials, lubricants and surface treatments.
