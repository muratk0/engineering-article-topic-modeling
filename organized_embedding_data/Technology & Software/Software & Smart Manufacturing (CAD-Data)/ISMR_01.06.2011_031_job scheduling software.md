Together with vital functions such as enterprise resource planning (ERP), estimating, quoting and job scheduling, job costing helps manufacturers to track and reduce their expenses significantly.

Although it uses some accounting data to determine profitability and to evaluate product manufacturing costs, unlike straightforward cost accounting, job costing does not entail processing debit-credit transactions. Instead, it acts as a stand-alone function that needs special attention and, whenever possible, integration with specialised automated modules i.e. ERP and scheduling software.
