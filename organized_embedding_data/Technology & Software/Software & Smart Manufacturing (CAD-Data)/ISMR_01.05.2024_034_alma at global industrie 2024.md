# ALMA at Global Industrie 2024

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

At Global Industrie 2024 in Paris, France, recently Alma presented its full range of CAD/CAM software applications for 2D/3D cutting, sheet metal work and robotics, integrated in the Almacam suite or available via the web. From quotation to workshop, these applications manage the entire digital sheet metal workflow and cover:

■ Nesting and CAM for 2D cutting (laser, plasma, oxycutting, waterjet), punching-nibbling and routing (Almacam Cut/ Punch/ Routing and the web application Nest&Cut).

■ 3D CAD for sheet metal and mechanical design (SpaceClaimSMOPlus).

■ CAD assembly import, unfolding of sheet metal parts and offline programming of press brakes (Assembly2cam, Unfold, Almacam Bend).

■ Production management (integration with ERP, job orders and stock management, workshop station, production planning etc.).

■ Sheet metal quoting (Almaquote, Webquote).

■ CAM for 3D cutting, tube and profile cutting (Almacam Tube and Almacam Space Cut).

■ Offline programming of welding robots (Almacam Weld).

The focus was on version 4.0 of Almacam and its different modules, with major upgrades to the Almaquote for faster and accurate quotations: management of multi-level BOMs with the associated operations; graphics tools to analyse key information relating to quotes; the option to make precise quotes on tubes and profiles and direct connection to the Assembly2cam CAD assembly importing module.

With Almaquote, Alma also provides Webquote, a web service that automates the preparation and processing of quotes and facilitates exchanges between subcontractors and ordering parties. In Webquote, users can now import and unfold 3D sheet metal parts and instantly calculate the cost of cutting and bending operations for reliable quotes online.

The Almacam Pass offer, Alma's subscription-based offer with no minimum period requirement, was also highlighted. This CAM software enables industrial companies to use Almacam in a simple and flexible way to program their cutting machines and draw up their quotes. It enables them to avoid making a major investment and benefit from an all-inclusive service.

“Offline programming of welding robots was also in the spotlight on the stand, with Almacam Weld's latest innovations enabling even greater progress to be made in reducing programming times, automation and improving the quality of robot programs. Particularly noteworthy was the automatic creation of relocation programs, which saves users precious time," added the company.

ISMR: Please explain the benefits of Software as a Service (SaaS) for sheetmetal manufacturers.

EM: More and more people are using software as a service (SaaS). Our Nest&Cut product is a good example. Simplicity, readiness-to-use, speed, ease of use and low cost are paramount, combined with powerful nesting. After quick online purchase, users can nest parts with no installation or training needed and can instantly start driving their machines. We combine basic CAM features with premium nesting. It also means we can supply more freelancers and smaller companies. It is not just offering 'CAM on the web' but a new, and more convenient, way for users to work with CAM.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers and how are you addressing these issues for them?

EM: Our sheet metal part manufacturer/ subcontractor customers are primarily concerned with material costs/consumption. Over the past few years, steel prices have been high and manufacturers therefore need to reduce their material consumption and waste levels. That means production optimisation. We have our own nesting algorithms to meet these end-user requirements.

For our machine-tool manufacturer, robot integrator and software editor customers, we have a product that will help them accelerate their time to market; the CAM software component (DLL) or a web service for software publishers. We also propose a nesting algorithm as a component and our recent new web service for 3D unfolding functionalities to produce sheet metal parts in 2D. These are new functionalities in the cloud that can be used, for example, for part cost calculation. The other type of product we have for these customers is Almacam software to drive machines/robots.

We are also pursuing more partnerships with machine-tool and robot manufacturers so that they sell their machines with our software to their customers. This is ready-to-use software and not a component.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

EM: We have seen several acquisitions in CAD/ CAM markets between machine builders and software publishers. This has meant that some machine-tool manufacturers end up using software from competing groups; that is not a very comfortable situation for them. Because we are an independent software company, we can offer machine builders neutral software solutions.

Almacam Pass is our 2022 CAM solution for manufacturers to flexibly adapt their production and evaluate the software. It is a no-commitment monthly subscription offer to allow customers to buy the software that they need and is a more flexible solution than a license. The subscription can be changed or stopped whenever the customer wishes. This new package offers complete software functionality to meet customer requirements now and in the future.

We also wish to increase our local presence in Spain and are increasing our investment in this market. It is a strategic market for us so we will be exhibiting at the forthcoming BIEMH exhibition in Bilbao. We are currently looking for more resellers in Spain and would like to reinforce our presence and strengthen our office team there.

ISMR: What is your approach to Industry 4.0/Big Data and technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality?

EM: For many years our CAD/CAM solutions have been contributing to the digitisation of sheet metalworking, as well as the automation and optimisation of industrial processes. Looking at the five main domains covered by Industry 4.0 (digitisation of production; Big Data; connected objects; robotics and additive manufacturing) our software packages naturally fit the first domain. But Almacam goes much further by communicating with information systems and controlled workshop machines.

We started integrating 3D into our software for robotic applications in the 1990s. In 2018, we started using Virtual Reality in our 3D CAM software for tube simulations. AI has a role in improving the nesting process in the sheet metal cutting industry as well as for part cost calculations, optimisation of material usage and for predictive methods. It improves the performance and efficiency of our algorithms and software, as well as the predictive capability of the technology. We already integrate AI into our algorithms and software as an ongoing process. As well as improving our software, it is also an opportunity to develop new functionalities and new products.

We have also integrated heightened data security functionality in our software as this is becoming more and more important for customers.
