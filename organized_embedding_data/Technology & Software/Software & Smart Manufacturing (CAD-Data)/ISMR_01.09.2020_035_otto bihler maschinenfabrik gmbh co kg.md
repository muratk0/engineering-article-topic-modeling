AS A leading system supplier in stamping, forming, welding and assembly technology, Bihler offers "Made in Germany" products. The family-owned company, with more than 1,000 employees, offers manufacturers of stamping and forming parts, as well as assembly producers, powerful machine systems, flexible process modules and intuitive control technology combined with automation solutions.

Since the company was founded in 1953, Bihler has successfully implemented more than 12,000 customerspecific solutions. In addition to machine and equipment engineering, Bihler is a provider of 3D technology software for the construction of stamping and forming tools.

## Timesavers International BV www.timesaversint.com

TIMESAVERS will display its complete rotation brush range at EuroBLECH 2021 for deburring, edge rounding and finishing of different metal parts and thicknesses. Rotation brush machines are offered for each working width and capacity requirement. The company will also showcase its new 22 series 600 WRBW compact rotary brushing machine at EuroBLECH. This compact 600mmwide machine offers uniform and even results, even wear of the tooling, high production capacity and Industry 4.0 functionality. It will also present its 42 series WWRBW and the 42 series HWRB rotary brush machines for deburring, edge rounding up to 2mm radius, finishing and laser oxide removal.
