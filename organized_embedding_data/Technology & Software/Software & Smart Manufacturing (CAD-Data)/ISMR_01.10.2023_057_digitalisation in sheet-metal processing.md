# Digitalisation in sheet-metal processing

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

TRUMPF spin-off Optimate is continuously developing its solution for AI-supported feasibility analysis and part optimisation. Its cloud-based software, which can be used both as an App and CAD plug-in, now has integrated cost-calculation functionality. Job shoppers and OEMs who produce and design sheet-metal-based products now only need one click to calculate the costs of their parts.

"With the Optimate App, product designers and sheet-metal processing companies can identify defective parts in advance and adjust them if necessary, even before they enter production. In addition to analysing individual parts, assemblies with up to 25 individual parts can also be checked for feasibility and optimisation potential," outlined Optimate.

Up to now, the App has given a percentage indication of the potential savings of the optimised design compared to the existing variant. This was a good indicator to determine changed production costs. For individual manufacturing costs, the indicator held potential for optimisation of the product.

"With the introduction of integrated cost calculation, this is now even easier and more precise," emphasised Jonas Steiling, CEO, Optimate. "Our users receive the specific price for their sheet-metal parts at the touch of a button."

The calculated sum is based on the contributing cost factors for material, scrap and the manufacturing process i.e. the complete sheet-metal process from laser cutting to bending and welding. In addition to the material costs, the costs for scrap, set-up and production times as well as machine hourly rates are also included in the sum. Based on the classification, companies in sheet-metal processing can then break down the costs for the detailed product processing steps. Once the cost drivers have been identified, the optimisation potential can be shown.

Users can also create their own profile in the Optimate App. The information stored with respect to machinery and tools is then also used for optimisation and individual cost calculation. These cost forecasts benefit not just the designers themselves, who can identify possible cost drivers already in the development process, but also other areas such as purchasing, controlling and cost calculation for sales.

“This makes it easy for buyers and jobshoppers to calculate the costs of sheetmetal parts online. Sales department can quickly create suitable offers for even the smallest batches or individual parts in a timely manner and without much effort," summarised Optimate.

"Benefits range from accelerated development processes and long-term planning as well as reliability in production to solid price and quotation calculation – and all in a matter of seconds," concluded Optimate CEO, Jonas Steiling.

Based at Borgo Ticino (Italy), a few kilometres from Malpensa International Airport in Milan, coil processing specialist Saronni celebrated its 70th anniversary in 2020. It manufactures press/coil feeding, cut-to-length (CTL) and embossing lines (including punching, cutting and stacking). It produced its first compact feeding line (without loop) in 1963. Lines can be customised to customer requirements or retrofitted.

## Customised coil feeding line

Founded in 1950, it has successfully developed and installed lines all over the world with hundreds of customers active mainly in the automotive and industrial household appliances sectors.

Saronni products include coil-handling equipment; compact feeding lines; traditional feeding lines (with loop); cut-to-length lines; embossing lines; coil-to-coil lines; straighteners; levellers; combi-straighteners and levellers with automatic straightening roll cassette-change; high-speed servo-feeders; zig zag servo feeders; coil welding units; automatic disk cutting lines and decoiling/ straightening software.

One of its customers, in the furniture hardware sector, asked Saronni to design, build and assemble two technically demanding and identical coil feeding lines. It was important that the lines offered higher levels of productivity which Saronni delivered through a higher degree of automation and process control. The resulting modern coil feeding line features a double decoiler; high-precision straightener with 21 straightening rolls in 'six-high' level configuration and intermediate spiral rolls for process optimisation.

Saronni also integrated an automatic, innovative system to clean the straightening rolls and avoid machine shut-down as well as operator intervention. This offered important safety benefits for its customer.
