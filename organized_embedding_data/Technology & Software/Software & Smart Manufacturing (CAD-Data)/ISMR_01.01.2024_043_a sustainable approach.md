# A sustainable approach

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

At Blechexpo in Stuttgart this November, Italian sheet metal specialist Salvagnini presented 'Impacts', its flexible tool for calculating the energy consumption of its own systems.

"We are committed to the environment and sustainability, choosing to build lean systems that limit energy usage to actual job needs. This is why we have chosen to adopt modern sustainability standards in our production sites. However, it is clear that the environmental impact of a machine tool goes beyond the boundaries of the site where it is manufactured. Our technologies also have a positive impact on the sustainability of the companies that use them, because they minimize the waste of time, resources, materials and energy," commented the manufacturer.

"Impacts has minimum hardware requirements: specific sensors are installed on the systems to measure consumption. The data collected are shared in real-time with various services, where they can be aggregated and viewed in different ways. With Impacts, it is possible to precisely measure the consumption of electricity, compressed air and assist gas. The future aim is to be able to calculate the equivalent volumes of CO₂ produced throughout the whole industrial process, with a granularity that can even record the equivalent volumes of CO₂ of each single manufactured component," it added.

Impacts aggregates data in the following ways:

1. Via FACE, the Salvagnini HMI interface for the entire product range. Installed on the machine, the data available in FACE relate to instant consumption, aggregated hourly consumption and aggregated daily consumption.

2. Via LINKS, the Salvagnini loT solution that monitors system performance. In a specific section of LINKS, data can be aggregated by shift, day, week and month, and can be downloaded.

## 3. Via an OPC UA interface.

'Impacts' sends the data to a data silo or third-party software. The data aggregation and viewing methods depend upon the technological solution chosen by the user.

Impacts is also a flexible tool that can customise data collection according to specific needs. It can measure the consumption of only electricity, electricity and compressed air, or electricity, compressed air and assist gas.

To demonstrate this functionality, Impacts measured consumption levels of all the systems on Salvagnini's stand at Blechexpo in Stuttgart. This included electricity consumption on the B3 press brake, electricity and compressed air consumption on the P2 panel bender and electricity as well as compressed air and assist gas consumption on the L5 laser.

"Monitoring and awareness of our own consumption are the first steps towards optimising and reducing [emissions]. In business terms, the initial benefit is the increase in profitability, because reducing consumption also means reducing running costs. The second is greater environmental sustainability and greater corporate social responsibility. The third is compliance with the latest environmental standards which, in many countries, have become or will soon become more stringent, setting CO₂ emissions limits and promoting energy efficiency. The fourth, but no less important, is the opportunity to benefit from the financial and tax incentives available to companies who invest in high energyefficiency machinery (often linked to the update of national regulatory frameworks)," concluded Salvagnini.
