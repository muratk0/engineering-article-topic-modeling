New details also simplify the operator’s work. For example, the mobile control unit, MobileControl, shortens walking distances, since the control system’s major functions can be available at up to three stations, right at the machine, with positioning to suit the user.

New image processing software, named VisionLine Mark and made by TRUMPF, makes marking simpler when using the lasers in the TruMark Series 3000, 5000 and 6000. The software has two new functions that make marking with a TRUMPF laser easier and faster: Autofocus and Traceability.

The Autofocus function automatically determines the right working distance for components with varying marking planes. The software determines the exact position on the surface of the part and, in response, the Z axis automatically moves to the marking position. This eliminates the need for elaborate set-up work and costly fixtures. Productivity rises at the same time and there are fewer rejects. The Traceability function supports users with part identification and tracing. ■

The TruLaser 2030 fiber, working with the TruDisklaser, offers the advantages of solidstate laser cutting. This machine is available with three or four kilowatts of power.
When dealing with very large and heavy components, TRUMPF offers, in addition to standard bending aids and support brackets, special heavy-duty versions that can carry up to 300kg parts on a press brake.

Crowning, under CNC control, ensures uniform angular precision along the entire length of the bend. The crowning curve can be adjusted at individual points, every 250mm.
