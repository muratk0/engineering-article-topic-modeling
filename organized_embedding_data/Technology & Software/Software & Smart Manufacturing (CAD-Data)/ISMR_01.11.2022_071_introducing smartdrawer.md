# Introducing SmartDrawer+

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Tooling Intelligence Ltd has introduced its latest inventory management system, the SmartDrawer+. The new system has evolved from the SmartDrawer and its intuitive touchscreen control, secure construction and user-friendly dispensing.

The new SmartDrawer+ has been developed to meet the expanding requirements of a broad range of industry sectors with weight-based vending, in-compartment charging, ticket printing, enhanced security and customised lighting.

Richard Swaffield from Tooling Intelligence commented: "The SmartDrawer system continues to be an extremely successful distribution point for engineers since its market introduction. We continuously record customer feedback and work to evolve the SmartDrawer to go beyond the demands of the industry."

It now includes an array of features for inventory management in industrial environments. Offering further enhancement to SupplyPro's serialised and calibrated asset management features, SmartDrawer+ includes in-compartment charging, with power sockets built-in to the locked compartments to allow the controlled recharging of assets such as laptops, tablets, batteries, power tools and testing equipment. Network-enabled pockets allow businesses to implement critical security updates or send important software and data to devices, whilst being securely stored.

The new SmartDrawer+ offers an extra level of control with its weight-based vending. This gives the customer 100% control over the movement of inventory in and out of the SmartDrawer+, whilst eliminating the need for users and administrators to spend time counting parts in and out of stock. The user simply takes what they need and SmartDrawer+ reports the activity instantly.

Complementing these enhancements are a further range of options including ticket printing, electronic cabinet locking and LED lighting display. The ticket printer can be customised to provide an instant transaction receipt, a pick-list history, collection instructions and more.

"With electronic cabinet locking, SmartDrawer+ delivers security where it is required, as the cabinet drawers are securely locked until a valid user login is presented. When the user logs out, SmartDrawer+ is automatically locked down for complete security. To further enhance SupplyPro's pick-to-light technology, SmartDrawer+ offers customisable integrated LED lighting," added the company.

"SmartDrawer+ is supported with proactive device monitoring, technical support and expert advice. It incorporates all of the latest Industry 4.0 technology that has been built into the user-friendly interface to make shopfloor utilisation a truly failproof operation," it concluded.
