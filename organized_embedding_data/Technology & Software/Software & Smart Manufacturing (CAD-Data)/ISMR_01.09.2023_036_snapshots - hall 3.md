## DREISTERN GmbH & Co. KG

## www.dreistern.com/en/

The roll-forming line specialist will present the full range of its expertise and technological innovations at Blechexpo 2023 in Stuttgart.

"We will offer a comprehensive insight into the entire rollforming process, under the motto 'From the initial idea to the finished profile'," Dreistern told ISMR.

"A particular highlight will be the introduction of our intelligent joint drive shaft and the intelligent straightening device. With this innovative solution, rollforming line operators can adjust their machines more precisely and make production processes more sustainable and efficient," it added.

Dreistern will also be presenting an interactive 3D model at the trade show that clearly illustrates the entire functionality and flexibility of a rollforming line. Visitors will be able to virtually walk through a rollforming line and experience each step of the process live, enabling them to develop a deep understanding of the technology and its potential.

## Prima Power GmbH

## www.primapower.com

Prima Power technologies include 2D and 3D laser cutting; welding and drilling; punching; bending; combined technologies and automation for sheet metal (with advanced servo-electric solutions for punching, bending and integrated processes).

Above: The PSBB system.

"Our range of sheet-metal processing machines and systems covers all steps of sheet metalworking: 2D and 3D laser cutting; punching; combined punching/shearing and punching/laser cutting, as well as automatic bending. Software also plays an important role; we can offer solutions to assist in all phases of production, from offline programming to production data collection and reporting," the Italian manufacturer explained.

Prima Power is the Laser and Sheet Metal Machinery Business Unit of the Prima Industrie Group. Its manufacturing facilities are in Italy, Finland, the USA and China and its sales and service network is active in over 80 countries, with a direct presence or through specialised dealers.

## STAM S.p.A.

## www.stam.it

Italian coil-processing specialist, STAM, is a manufacturer of standard and customised coil sheet. Its rollforming machines including Flexible and Variable rollformers and metal coil. It also manufactures processing lines such as slitting lines; cut-to-length (CTL) lines; blanking and multi-blanking lines.

"With production units in Spain and Italy, we have installed over 1000 machines around the world serving sectors such as metallurgy; construction; automotive; logistics; energy; infrastructure and household appliances," it told ISMR.

"Pre- and post-sales customer support is one of our key focuses. We listen to our customers' current needs and anticipate their future ones, and develop innovative solutions in partnership with them," it added.

Founded in 1963 in Ponzano Veneto (Treviso, Italy), its production cycle takes place in-house from design, steel structural work, mechanical machining, assembly and final testing.

STAND 3406

## HALL 3

## Voortman Steel Machinery

## www.voortman.net

Join Voortman Steel Machinery on its booth 3304 at Blechexpo in Hall 3 for live demonstrations of its V310 plate-processing machine.

"At the push of a button, it offers bevel cutting; drilling; milling; marking; thread-tapping and countersinking on a single machine. This results in faster production, flexibility and short lead times for high-quality finished parts," it said.

As a specialist in plate processing, Voortman focuses on interaction, automation and unmanned production. Show visitors will discover Voortman's latest software solutions, such as the Voortman Operator Dashboard which alerts operators of manual operations and plate- or job-loading requirements.

Combined with the Unmanned Mode feature to bypass manual intervention for extended periods, operators can add value elsewhere while the machine operates autonomously. The Dashboard indicates when operator intervention is required.
