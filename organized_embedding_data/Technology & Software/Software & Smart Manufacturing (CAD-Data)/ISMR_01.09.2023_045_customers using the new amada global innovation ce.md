# Customers using the new AMADA Global Innovation Centre (AGIC) in Japan can explore the future of manufacturing and create innovation.

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

## Form and function

AMADA is not just a manufacturer of machine tools; it proposes innovative solutions to boost the technological edge of its customers. It promotes an integrated concept of its work, offering bespoke solutions and creating customised responses for each individual requirement.

Accordingly, AGIC is equipped with a diverse range of environments and functions. This includes the Innovation LABO, a laboratory space specifically for customers with nine labs, and the Innovation SITE where AMADA's customers can interact with over 90 models of its latest machine and technology products. This also includes engineering expertise, utilising the experience accumulated by AMADA on the latest IoT (Internet of Things) systems.

There is no charge to AMADA's customers who use AGIC services. AMADA provides the majority of materials for tests, although customers can also bring their own components to test.

The 'Area for Creating the Future of Manufacturing' features the latest digital imaging equipment and information devices. Customers using the AGIC will work with AMADA to "explore the future of manufacturing and to create innovation."

Interactive displays and dashboards offer further support to AGIC visitors. Conference rooms and a 'Precision Sheet Metal Technology Fair' (showcasing sheet-metal projects), as well as communal areas, also supplement the visitor experience.

## Future processing technology

“Manufacturing sites in the metal-processing market are facing the global trends of 'action for a decarbonised society,' 'labour force and skilled worker shortages' and 'AI and digital transformation (DX) through the advancement of information technology' so addressing these issues is an urgent task. We established the new Innovation LABO at the AGIC as the first facility in the industry to contribute to the resolution of these issues and will be taking on the challenge of future processing technology by verifying customers' manufacturing," continued AMADA.

In nine customer-specific spaces that are equipped with the latest solutions and advanced inspection equipment, AMADA's customers will be able to address the various issues they face in their own company by working with AMADA technical staff to verify their processes (and issues such as quality requirements).

The "Innovation SITE" has product areas for sheet metal, cutting and structural, as well as presses, but it is not simply a conventional display of the machines. With the concept moving from "what is possible" to "why it is possible," the facility showcases AMADA's latest technologies (such as for mechanisms and structures).

"The aim is to deepen the understanding and satisfaction of customers by providing them with an experience centred on the technical reasons for why accuracy is higher than before and why processing is possible," explained AMADA.

## A sustainable project

AGIC was also completely renovated as an environmentally friendly building. Through collaboration with major general contractors and various manufacturers in Japan, the latest air conditioning equipment and lighting was installed, energy consumption has been reduced and renewable energy is utilised. This includes the large-scale installation of solar power generation.

As a result, CO₂ emissions have been halved compared to that generated by the previous Solution Centre. Idling functions on chillers and compressors in the facility also help to decrease CO₂ emissions. In total, CO₂ emissions at the AGIC are expected to be reduced by 700 tons per year.

As a machinery manufacturer in the global metal-processing market, AMADA intends AGIC to play a central role for its solution centres and technical centres in Japan and overseas. AMADA has also adopted sustainability in selected machine-tool designs, with features such as servo drives.

"We will also collaborate with our facilities around the world to create future-oriented manufacturing and innovation, and will disseminate this information worldwide," AMADA added.

## New product introductions

To further the concept of future-oriented manufacturing, AMADA is also using the AGIC to introduce new technologies, software and machine tools to its customers. Of the 90 machine models displayed at AGIC, around 85% are new products.

AMADA also held the "AMADA Group New Products WEEKS" from 10 May to 2 June 2023 at the new AGIC facility in Isehara.

"At the event, we introduced the new 'LIVLOTS' software designed to connect people, things and events to achieve manufacturing DX, and the new environmentally friendly sheet-metal processing machine that features new NC equipment (AMNC 4ie) that can be used by anybody anywhere," outlined AMADA.

Presentations were also given on various topics such as sustainability initiatives; the labour shortage caused by the declining birthrate and aging population; skills transference and action on AI and DX in accordance with advances in information technology. Suggestions were given on solving customer issues as the developers themselves explained the technological benefits of various new products. New products were also demonstrated in the "Innovation SITE" at the AGIC.

"Through the AGIC, the AMADA Group will continue to work with customers to explore ways of conducting future-oriented manufacturing and creating innovation," concluded AMADA.
