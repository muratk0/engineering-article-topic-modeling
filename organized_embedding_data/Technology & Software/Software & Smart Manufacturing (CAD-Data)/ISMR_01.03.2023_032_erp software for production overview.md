# ERP software for production overview

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

Priority Manufacturing ERP modules give businesses a holistic view of a production facility so they can optimise manufacturing performance, said Medatech UK. Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) is the process of integrating and delivering data and managing operations on a company-wide scale. In manufacturing, ERP is a dedicated software solution used to plan; manage; automate; operate; support and report on operational business and production processes.

To support the entire manufacturing process, a modern ERP seamlessly combines multiple operational business modules that manage and integrate a company's financials; supply chain; operations; commerce; reporting; manufacturing; human resources and more.

“They also enable leaner operations and a boost to analysis to keep up with the industry's changing rigours, strengthening customer loyalty and increasing the business' bottom line," it added.

The software focuses on the automation, personalisation and customisation of the system for each user (from hiding unused fields automatically to suggested fields for each user as they log in).

“Priority learns and understands work patterns and processes. It offers full visibility of product lines across the top floor and shop floor; improved agility/response times; seamless workflow synchronisation; realtime alerts at every stage and can simplify all complex processes," added the company.

Specifically designed for manufacturers, Priority delivers a complete, cloud-based manufacturing ERP software. It is designed to empower manufacturers with valuable business insights; a 360-degree real-time view of the production facility; performance tracking and enhancement capabilities using a wide range of reports and analytics to help optimise operations, improve visibility and remove inefficiencies.

“Users can benefit from greater system agility, with faster response times to shifting market conditions and order demands, and significantly lower costs by keeping tighter control of materials, machines and work hours," explained Priority Manufacturing.

There were live demonstrations of the latest version of the system at the recent Southern Manufacturing & Electronic exhibition at Farnborough, UK. The company also offers a comprehensive mobile ERP solution for use with any mobile device or tablet for real-time access and visibility of business operations anywhere and at any time.
