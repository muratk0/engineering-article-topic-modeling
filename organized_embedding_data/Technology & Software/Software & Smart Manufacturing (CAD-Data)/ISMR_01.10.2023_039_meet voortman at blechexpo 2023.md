# Meet Voortman at Blechexpo 2023

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Show visitors can meet Voortman on its stand 3304 in Hall 3 at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023). Live demonstrations of its V310 plate-processing machine will highlight functions such as bevel cutting; drilling; milling; marking; thread-tapping and countersinking on a single machine.

At the booth, Voortman will also focus on value chains, operator interaction and unmanned production. Its latest software solutions, such as the VACAM Operator Dashboard and the VACAM Scheduling Overview, will also be on display.

“Our hybrid milling and plate buffering capability, as well as new pipe-cutting functionality on our plate-processing machine, will be showcased. We will also present the full process of value chain automation and digitisation, from quoting to delivery, on the booth," outlined Thomas Krone, Business Development Manager, Voortman Steel Machinery.

Voortman will also be exhibiting at Tube and Wire (Germany) and SteelFab (UAE) in 2024.

## In conclusion

Voortman is continuously developing new solutions and machines and is eager to share its knowledge and experience with customers. It offers interactive webinars and technical support/expertise, covering a broad range of topics and designed to inspire users and outline new ways of working.

It has also placed a strong focus on providing software solutions that enables clients to provide insight into real-time process data, allowing them to make powerful decisions leading their business forward with confidence.

"Ultimately, we act as a partner to our customers and aim to make them more efficient and successful," summarised Thomas Krone.
