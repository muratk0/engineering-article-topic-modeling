Measuring tables come in 1-, 3-axis versions

Exact Metrology offers Raytech shop floor dimensional measuring systems for measuring parts for cutting, bending, punching, waterjet cutting, laser cutting, drilling, and tapping.

The single-axis table is suitable for checking flat sheets with straight edges and square corners. It helps reduce setup time, scrap rates, and part backlog, says the company. The table integrates with SPC software for quality tracking and reporting and reduces operator-to-operator variation. The squareness gauge allows users to check sheets for edge perpendicularity or camber, while diagonal blocks allow the user to check corner-to-corner squareness on sheets.

The 3-axis table is suitable for checking laser- and waterjet-cut sheet metal parts with many features that are bent and formed. It includes flexible metrology software, sealed Heidenhain encoders, low-maintenance linear bearings, and a Renishaw touch probe. The table can integrate with SPC software for quality tracking and reporting.

Arc welding system features cobot-assisted programming

TRUMPF has released its first automated arc welding system, the TruArc Weld 1000. It comes with a collaborative robot that assists in the creation of the program, rather than relying on values from an offline source or an operator entering positioning data into a control.

After the operator manually guides the cobot over a part, it automatically creates the weld program. This enables fabricators to use robotic welding, even with a lot size of one.

Equipped with an operating unit, the cobot allows users to store the weld path’s start, end, and intermediate points to create the program. Furthermore, the cobot control system includes templates for welding programs and parameters that cover scenarios such as different sheet thicknesses. Inside the welding system is a partition that can be moved up and down. This allows users to divide up the working area and choose between welding one large part or several smaller ones. In single-station operation, the cobot can weld parts measuring up to 80 by 24 by 24 in. In two-station operation, it can process parts measuring up to 24 by 24 by 24 in.

Four-roll plate bender handles material up to 60 mm thick

MG SRL offers the M 2060 H four-roll plate bending machine. It pinches the plate between the two central driven rolls, prebends the leading edge, and rolls the body of the part along with the trailing edge in one direction and in one pass. The plate is kept square, without slipping, during both prebending and rolling cycles because of the constant clamping of the bottom and top rolls.

With its 520-mm upper roll, the machine can bend a 60-mm-thick sheet over a working length of 2 m. Its Touch Command EVO helps the operator give the sheet the desired shape in series production, whether cylindrical, oval, squared, or with multiple diameters. Side roll parallelism is maintained by a torsion bar system that connects both ends of the roll, eliminating the weakness of extra encoders and hydraulic circuits. The two central rolls are driven by hydraulic motors and planetary gearboxes, coupled directly to the rolls.

Available with this machine is the Touch Command EVO CNC. Its new closed-loop system constantly monitors the rotation and side roll positions. In addition, the material library can be adjusted to work with the material being received. After the operator makes test bends and inputs the diameters achieved, the computer understands that material’s springback and can automatically generate programs for nearly any shape required.
