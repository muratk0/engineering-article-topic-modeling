Today’s manufacturers need to consider many factors that will affect their nesting if they are to be truly efficient and get the very best from it. Managers need to consider the effect that a nest would have downstream – for example, what tool changes do the machines need and, if they minimise that, are they saving more on the time the operator takes than they would be saving on materials with the nest that has the best utilisation? This is a very important factor, with labour costs rising all the time.

An authoritative new White Paper on modern nesting that addresses these issues has been published by Planit, the company that developed the powerful sheet metal CAD/CAM software, Radan. With the sea change in what nesting can now do for companies, manufacturers should ask themselves the following questions, revolving around where they want to be in five years time:

■ Do I want to increase my output?

■ Do I want to better utilise my staff?

■ Do I want to increase automation?

■ Do I want to make my resources go further?

If the answer to any of those questions is “Yes,” then the White Paper “Modern Nesting, Changing The Way You Think About Nesting” shows manufacturers how to select the nesting program that is absolutely right for their business.

It is available to download for free from www.radan.com/modernnesting

Nesting is moving away from its traditional roots – it has value as an integrated part of an overall ERP or process planning system, and managers need to consider the effect a nest has downstream. For example, what tool changes do the machines need, and does minimising that save more on the time the operator takes, than would be saved on materials if the nest produces the best utilisation?

The right type of nesting is capable of so much more than it is generally being used for – it can tie in with accurate quotations and capacity planning and it can readily adapt to produce both standard nests and nests for a Just In Time procedure, as well as a mix of parts for different customers.

Newly-launched Radan 2011 R2 software includes new nesting functions that give more control over specific parts and sheet sizes, to ensure that the final nest takes the user’s individual requirements for that particular job into account. There is also a new option of excluding certain sheet sizes from the nester, giving the user the ability to relate what he is doing offline to what is happening on the shop floor. Radan 2011 R2 also features a technology which allows the nester to use multi-part symbols to produce the correct number of parts. A new “Publish Remnants” function allows remnants to be published into a central folder. There is also improved tool optimisation for single nests and sequences of nests. Layer mapping in dxf and dwg files is now accessible from the import facility.

Nesting parts for today’s cost-conscious and time-conscious manufacturing industry has moved on from the original concept of nesting, which was simply the geometric problem of arranging parts on a sheet to minimise waste. Founded in 1992, SigmaTEK Systems, LLC provides solutions to enhance efficiency and profitability for flat material and tube manufacturers through CAD/CAM nesting solutions – SigmaNEST, SolidNEST and SigmaTUBE.

Automatic nesting software, like SigmaNEST, can analyse the geometry of each part that is nested, and create an NC tool path that optimises all four plasma cutting variables to ensure that every contour on every part is produced accurately and as precisely as possible. It takes geometric features, like holes and other cut-outs, bevels and external contours into consideration, and adjusts the four key variables while it generates the NC tool path.

SigmaTEK Systems has announced its partnership with Invera, the makers of STRATIX MRP software. Through this partnership, STRATIX/SigmaNEST-X will provide an integrated product that offers forward/backward integration of all data between nesting processes and MRP business management. The introduction of this new product allows the user to realise increased sales level inventory interaction, reduction in material costs, improved productivity of planning staff, increased order line profit and improved customer service capacity. It provides businesses with the ability to lower their overhead without lowering production levels and work more efficiently.

Remnants and other plate details and their diagrams are stored within STRATIX making them available to all members of an organisation. Inventory transactions for product items that meet “inventory extraction rules” are exported to SigmaNEST as required and the information is updated in STRATIX in real-time. Inventory for an entire warehouse can be updated on-demand as needed.

SigmaNEST Version 9.1 will soon be available. The new version contains a combination of improvements in intuition and efficiency-enhancing features and makes advanced programming tasks simple and more automated. This release introduces optimisation tactics that use brand new features like iPierce™, HDX™ precision plasma setting, improved import functions, NC sequencing and much more.

SigmaNEST HDX processes parts specifically for precision plasma cutting. It assigns special lead-ins and lead-outs to each part to eliminate divots in small holes. SigmaNEST adjusts gas pressures, plasma amperage and height controls while it generates the cutting program. SigmaNEST automatically calibrates the NC toolpath to achieve a balance between pierce holds, feedrates and advance-of-movements, resulting in cylindrical, taper-free holes.

In addition to improving processing speed and cut quality, SigmaNEST 9.1 also makes it easier to import geometry. The new import functions are an interactive dialogue that allows users to scale parts and gives them the ability to toggle and show errors within the part’s geometry before importing the parts. It can also import from vector-based PDF files. Work order graphs help determine the order in which to complete outstanding work orders.

SigmaTUBE is a complete tube-cutting software solution that runs seamlessly integrated inside SolidWorks. Designed for tube-cutting laser and plasma machines, SigmaTUBE offers linear nesting and NC Code generation for round pipe, rectangular tube and other cross sections. ■
