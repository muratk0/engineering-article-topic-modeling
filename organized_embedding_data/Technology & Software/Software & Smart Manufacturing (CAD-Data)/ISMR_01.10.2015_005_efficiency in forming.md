# Efficiency in forming

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

THE FRAUNHOFER INSTITUTE for Machine Tools and Forming Technology IWU will hold its 5th International Conference “Accuracy in Forming Technology” ICAFT 2015, in conjunction with the 22nd Saxon Conference on Forming Technologies SFU 2015 in Chemnitz, on 10-11 November 2015.

“German industry is on a path towards innovative modes of production. Driven by more and more individualized products and the options arising from digital data exchange and networks, efficient, flexible and adaptable process chains have to be created. As a result, manufacturing itself is becoming more and more complex. In this context, it is necessary to analyse and optimise value-added chains both in research and engineering of equipment configuration and process technologies, as well as in industrial practice,” it told ISMR.

With the motto ‘Efficiency in forming technology by an integrated approach to process chains’, the double conference (ICAFT 2015 and SFU 2015) will be a platform to discuss current research results and bestpractice examples from industry, as well as central future trends and innovation strategies. Experts from various fields will discuss topics encompassing the entire value-added chain, focusing on sheet and bulk metal forming, Industry 4.0, tools and dies, machine tools and joining, as well as semi-finished parts.

The conference programme will be complemented by an exhibition, as well as the traditional “Forming Live” event including demonstrations in the testing facilities of the Fraunhofer IWU. The participants can, among other things, see the institute’s new flexible press hardening line in action. This line is run via intelligent process monitoring and process guidance, and is equipped with a custom-developed furnace for contact heating to enable rapid and graded heating of blanks. Technologies and model process chains for sheet and bulk metal forming will also be presented in combination with innovative machine concepts.

The Saxon Forming Technology award (Sächsischer Preis für Umformtechnik) will be awarded during the networking dinner held at the Chemnitz festival hall Stadthalle on 10 November 2015. See www.iwu.fraunhofer.de/en/events-and-fairs/ICAFT-SFU_2015.html
