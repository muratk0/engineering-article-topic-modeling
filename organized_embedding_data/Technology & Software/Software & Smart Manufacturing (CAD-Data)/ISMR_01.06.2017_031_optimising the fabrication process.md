# Optimising the fabrication process

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

AT LAMIERA 2017 in Milan (Italy) recently, LVD showcased its latest fibre laser cutting machine, Phoenix FL 3015 6kW, and automated tool-changing press brake, ToolCell (equipped with Easy-Form® Laser adaptive bending technology for precision bending). Both machines feature LVD's most advanced TOUCH controllers and are connected to LVD's CADMAN® software to make real-time data exchange possible. The power of CADMAN will be demonstrated through the complete suite of CADMAN software products. A virtual Smart Factory was also on display – showcasing LVD's Industry 4.0 philosophy in action.

"LVD machinery, controls and software are Industry 4.0-ready. They make data collection possible and leverage the value of that data across the entire manufacturing operation to make the Smart Factory a reality. As the trend toward digitisation and smart manufacturing advances, LVD continues to drive the factory of the future with its integration philosophy and CADMAN® software. At LAMIERA, we are demonstrating how our Industry 4.0-ready products address today's small batch, increased part complexity and short lead-time culture by providing networked information that helps fabricators to make informed decisions," LVD told ISMR during the exhibition.

CADMAN looks at the big picture and helps to streamline the complete fabrication process – from production control, communication, planning and management to punching, bending and laser cutting – through to integrated, database-driven software that gives real-time insights from the shop-floor and instant control of production processes.
