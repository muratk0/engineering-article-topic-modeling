# Automated sheet metal folding

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

HANS SCHRÖDER MASCHINENBAU GmbH and SCHRÖDER-FASTI Technologie will showcase their machines for folding, cutting, rounding and swaging on booth 1709 in Hall 1 at this November's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart. A highlight will be Schröder's new, highly automated folding machine, EVO-CENTER.

"With a working length of 4,040mm, the EVO-CENTER bends up to 3mm thick steel sheet. The 3D-graphic control, POS 3000, which is operated via a 22" touchscreen, controls the 'almost automated' production. The operator starts the bending program/s. Once the workpiece is aligned with the reference gauge fingers, the suction cups fix the workpiece and the machine completes all further processing steps by itself," commented the manufacturer.

"The Up-and-Down folding beam and a rotating plate with suction cups then manages sheet bending with no intervention needed by the operator. With its fully automatic tool-changing portal, the machine is automatically able to rapidly change up to 400mm-high tools."
