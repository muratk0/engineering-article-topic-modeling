# Portable 3D measurement

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

Creaform, a specialist in portable 3D measurement solutions and 3D engineering services, will showcase its latest developments in 3D metrology solutions to the metal processing and manufacturing industry at the EMO exhibition this year.

3D measurement systems are used in the metal sector for a wide range of applications. These include the 3D inspection of stamping moulds; sheet metal parts; sub-units and body-in-white geometry conformity checks; part-to-CAD comparison; conformity checks after assembly and soldering processes; reverse engineering; soldering jigs control and adjustment.

“Our measurement equipment is portable and can be brought to the part which needs to be measured. Integrated TRUaccuracy technology ensures accurate measurements, regardless of the measurement environment,” the company explained.

Depending upon the application, Creaform offers tailored customer solutions: the HandyPROBE mobile CMM for tactile measurements and the MetraSCAN 3D, Handyscan 3D and Go!SCAN 3D portable 3D scanners for scanning tasks. For automated 3D inspection of parts on the production line, Creaform now also offers the MetraSCAN-R robot-mounted optical CMM 3D scanner.
