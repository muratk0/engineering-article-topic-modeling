# Ficep transforms with BigChange

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

FICEP, a supplier of structural steel and plate fabrication equipment, has deployed the latest mobile technology as part of a real-time management system that has transformed customer service. Supplied by UK-based BigChange, the cloud-based mobile resource management system means that Ficep field services engineers use tablets that synchronise in real-time with central systems as part of a completely paperless system.

With its UK headquarters at Europort Wakefield in West Yorkshire, a team of Ficep engineers install and maintain machinery across the UK and Scandinavia.

“Our steel fabricating machinery plays a key part in preparing steel for construction.

Keeping the equipment in optimum working condition with minimal downtime is crucial. We have been improving our engineering support services to support an increasing number of installations and the introduction of BigChange has completely transformed our customer service,” explained Richard Clark, UK Service Manager, Ficep UK Ltd.

Previously Ficep engineers filled out paper services sheets and other forms by hand which were then sent to the office for processing and actioning, such as the ordering of parts, the production of quotes and issuing of invoices.

“It used to take two or three weeks to get a quote out to a customer; now we achieve that within 24 hours. It’s the same for invoices and with electronic sign-offs from customers onsite who we can invoice almost immediately. As a result, there has been a much better and quicker conversion rate of quote to order and queries on invoices have virtually disappeared,” added Clark.

BigChange gives Ficep customer service teams and management up-to-the minute visibility of every service job. Ficep’s engineers receive their jobs on their tablets which provide navigation to site and allow access to all documentation, with workflows ensuring proper procedures are followed and information reported from site. The devices are used to take photographs which are, together with time and GPS location, matched to service sheets for the job. This provides an accurate and indisputable record of work done.

“We now have service sheets customised for each machine that identifies the correct parts and even gives the engineer a complete history of previous service work and parts used. It really helps them to do a good job every time and helps to ensure that machine downtime is minimised. Customers have been markedly impressed with the system and the information that it provides," said Clark.

As well as the benefits to the field engineering services, Ficep has reported significant time savings back at the office.

“By eliminating paperwork, we are saving about 20 hours per week in administration time and, as managers, we spend much less time sorting out customer and service issues as we have all the information we need at our fingertips 24/7. We no longer need to track back into paper records or call an engineer to ask about a job,” added Clark. See www.bigchange.com
