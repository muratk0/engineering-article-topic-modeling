# Comau showcases Industry 4.0 solutions

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

FOR THE FIRST time at MACH 2018, Comau UK walked visitors through real-life factories of the future by creating a 360º virtual tour of the digital production and collaborative robotic applications that the industrial automation solutions provider has deployed across Europe.

Comau defines HUMANufacturing as its new approach to manufacturing and its own way to Industry 4.0 to meet the challenges and needs of a changing market. In the "factory of the future", industrial robots are no longer confined within barriers, but can work closely and in total security with man, which plays a central role in the production process. The interaction between operators and machines is made efficient and secure by collaborative robots and new digital technologies. Industrial machines become 'smart', more and more flexible, and easy to use in an integrated and connected manufacturing system.

During the MACH exhibition in the UK, Comau UK let visitors "see" products developed following the HUMANufacturing approach, such as advanced logistics solutions including the new Agile1500 Autonomous Mobile Robot and e.DO buildit-yourself robot kit. Comau's future-focused technologies are designed to help companies improve their efficiency, while streamlining processes and increasing production quality.

Headquartered in Turin, Italy, Comau has over 45 years of experience and an international network of 36 locations, 15 manufacturing plants and five innovation centres that span 17 countries and employ more than 9,000 people. See www.comau.com ■
