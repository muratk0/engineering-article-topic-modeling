# The Salvagnini Group

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.salvagninigroup.com

Salvagnini has been operating for over fifty years in flexible automation and industrial machinery for sheet-metal processing. The specialist in panel bending technology, with headquarters in northern Italy and the largest panel bender production plant in the world (in Austria), has four production facilities and twenty-three companies dedicated to sales and customer support worldwide.

The Salvagnini Group designs, builds and sells flexible systems and machines for processing sheet metal: punching machines; panel benders; press brakes; fibre laser cutting machines; FMS lines; automatic store-towers and software.

It integrates the Industry 4.0 concept into its solutions: its automatic job shop (AJS) solution indicates a set of Salvagnini systems that are orchestrated by OPS process software for intelligent integration between machines and a customer's ERP system.
