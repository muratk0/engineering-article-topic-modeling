# InspecVision Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand B119

www.inspecvision.com

InspecVision specialises in automated 2D and 3D measurement systems for inspecting and reverse engineering sheet metal parts at every stage of production (including flat, folded, welded or assembled parts). Manufacturers worldwide use InspecVision's technology for quality control, improving efficiency and reducing waste.

"Our systems are high speed, low maintenance, can be supported remotely and are available in a wide range of sizes. Products include Planar 2D, the world's fastest measurement tool for 2D parts, and Opti-Scan 3D, the world's first 3D inspection system that can measure surfaces and edges in 3D," said the UK manufacturer.

At EuroBLECH 2024, InspecVision will showcase its latest innovation: the Guidance and Verification system (GAV). This production technology trains operators on how to complete assemblies and verifies if they are assembled correctly, highlighting any errors in real-time.

"Our systems save hours of inspection time, improve part accuracy, increase the percentage of parts inspected and reduce waste," it said.

## Pivatic Oy

Stand 11-E136 for sheet metal, 11-G15 for flat bar

https://pivatic.com/en/

Pivatic and Vaski are co-participating with two stands at EuroBLECH: 11-E136 for sheet metal and 11-G15 for flat bar.

"On the sheet metal stand, we will demonstrate a new PivaPunch line solution that integrates the fastest multi-coil system in the world into a standard coil punching and cutting line. A fully automatic coil change system is paired with a fluid ongoing punching and shearing process. PivaPunch punching machines utilise direct material flow to maximize efficiency and are suitable for product families and symmetrical parts. The MultiCOIL vastly increases flexibility in kit production from different material types," said Pivatic Oy.

On the flat bar stand, the companies will showcase the automation of flat bar production with modern technology. On display will be the VaskiPUNCH Legacy, a fully electric punching machine for copper, steel and aluminum flat bars. Vaski is also introducing the new VaskiBEND, with automatic bending tool-changer, designed for bending flat bars.
