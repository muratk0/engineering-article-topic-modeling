# MAKING ITS MARK

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“MARKATOR launched several new product innovations at Blechexpo in Stuttgart"

ISMR discussed trends in marking markets, new product innovations and strategic goals with MARKATOR.

MARKATOR, a manufacturer of dot peen and scribe marking systems for durable markings on metals and plastics, was at this year's Blechexpo in Stuttgart with a wide range of products. The company offers hand-held and bench-top marking systems as well as systems to integrate into automatic production lines.

Dirk Reinemuth, MARKATOR's managing director, discussed trends in marking markets, new product innovations and the company's strategic goals with ISMR at the exhibition.

## Trends in marking markets

"Marking is becoming more important. The use of data matrix codes, 2D codes that can be read by a camera, is increasing. Marking times are also decreasing so this unlocks more applications for our marking systems," Reinemuth told ISMR.

"Our customers range from small milling and drilling companies to large automotive or shipping companies as well as a wide range of sheet metal shops, OEMs etc. In short, anyone who needs to mark small or large parts. We sell about 50% of our machines to the automotive industry and its suppliers. Customers want highspeed, reliable machines that can mark lots of parts. Deeper marking depths are also needed as is flexibility (i.e. battery-operated units when you mark big parts)."

The demand for durable and economic marking on industrial parts is strong, he told ISMR, adding that nearly every industrial product needs to be marked with a part or serial number, VIN number, company logo or Data Matrix code for traceability reasons.

## An eye on innovation

MARKATOR placed a strong focus on its FlyMarker dot peen, handheld, batteryoperated marking unit at this year's Blechexpo, as it demonstrated to ISMR.

Nearly every industrial product needs to be marked with a part or serial number, VIN number, company logo or Data Matrix code for traceability reasons.

"The FlyMarker features an integrated display and keyboard so that it is portable and can mark especially big parts. We have developed a new column/clamp for the FlyMarker® mini so that you can also use the hand-held version as a table version. It has therefore become a combination version, which is a great benefit for users. They can use the table machine version for marking small parts and the portable, hand-held version for marking larger parts. We are constantly improving and innovating on this product, as we want to make it more useful for operators," explained Reinemuth.

The FlyMarker® mini is now optionally available with a powerful 5.2 Ah rechargeable battery which has increased its uptime threefold. The battery-operated, hand-held FlyMarker® mini is mainly suitable for durable markings on large and unmovable workpieces, directly on-site. The portable marking system can mark materials with a hardness of up to 63 HRC. As well as characters and numbers, it is also possible to mark logos, test symbols and Data Matrix Codes.

"Depending upon the material and the customer requirements, the marking force and marking depth can be adjusted individually. Several hundred marking files, fonts and logos can be stored and administered in the internal memory of the marking unit. There are also many language versions available," added Reinemuth.

He demonstrated the ease of conversion to the new table version of the system. Using the column frame, he showed ISMR how small workpieces or type plates could be marked. The mobile marking unit slides into the column frame and is fixed with a clamping lever. Linear guides enable quick adjustment of the marking pin distance so that marking can begin. Using the T-slot base plate of the column frame, it is possible to mount the MARKATOR® positioning plate for identification tags, as well as individual plates produced by the customer, to position the workpieces which need to be marked.

The FlyMarker can be used on most materials, using either special pins or higher power, and a wide range of materials such as steel, aluminium, titanium, fibre glass, plastics etc.

"We have also just launched a very new product – the MV5 U65/30 INTEGRAL integration unit. This innovative all-in-one marking unit, which is electrically-driven, can be integrated into production lines e.g. plasma cutting lines or testing machines. It can be used in any position for precise and warp-free marking," Reinemuth added.

It has been a good year for us - we increased our turnover by more than 20%.

The MV5 U65/30 INTEGRAL 3kg unit is a compact dot peen marker with integrated control unit. It features an electromagnetic-driven marking pin so that no compressed air supply is necessary. It can process a large marking area of 65 x 30mm in low volume. A user can check rapid function via six status LEDs on the marking head and the system includes double guided linear guides.

## Targets and goals

"It has been a good year for us – we increased our turnover by more than 20%. We predict another good year but our goal is to consolidate behind this rapid growth, so that we can provide a stable base to support it. We are very proud of our new products – we can deliver many of them from stock so it only takes one week.

Our strategic focus is to develop our exports business, add a lot of new functions to the marking systems and widen our range of marking systems. Next to new developments, our target is to keep up our high quality standards," explained Dirk Reinemuth.

"Most of our customers are in Europe but we have had great success in the USA over the last few years, especially for our hand-held marking units. We are also at FABTECH in Chicago this year. Our products are generally sold in over 40 countries."

MARKATOR has a strict development target to launch more products in the marking arena over the next three or four years. It specialises in dot peen and scribe marking units and intends to stay firmly in this field as, according to Reinemuth, 'there is still a lot to develop'.

"Our strategic focus is to develop our exports business, add a lot of new functions to the marking systems and widen our range of marking systems. We want to find new markets and sell more to existing markets. Next year, we are focusing on the USA, Japan and parts of Asia. We are also actively looking for more agents in certain Asian countries," he told ISMR.
