# Automation skills in focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

FOLLOWING THE SUCCESS of its first five courses, Comau is now accepting applications for the new term of the 'Second Level Masters in Industrial Automation' course from the Politecnico di Torino (Turin Polytechnic) in Italy. The course is organised by Comau with funding from Regione Piemonte. The selection period for the sixth edition of the course is open until 11 November 2016.

The Masters in Industrial Automation is a training and work programme that aims to attract and select the best engineering graduates from Italian and international universities, to specialise in industrial automation. Comau hires these students, with an apprenticeship contract, from the first day. The training course, taught entirely in English, consists of 540 hours of lessons in the first year and 660 hours in the second year, devoted to project work within the company. Classes are taught by Comau's managers and professors from Turin Polytechnic. The Masters is free of charge for selected students.

To date, more than 90 engineers who attended the Master's programme have joined Comau, a global specialist in the automation industry and a company that focuses on people and their skills as a core part of its business strategy.

"The close link between educational excellence and industrial reality is the winning choice for both our organisations, who were asked to address the growing challenges of the global market. The students themselves have to build a wealth of solid skills to move forward as leaders in this highly competitive and constantly evolving market," explained Comau.

For more information, visit https://didattica.polito.it/master/industrialautomation/2017/introduction and www.comau.com/en/comau-academy/young-talents/master-industrial-automation
