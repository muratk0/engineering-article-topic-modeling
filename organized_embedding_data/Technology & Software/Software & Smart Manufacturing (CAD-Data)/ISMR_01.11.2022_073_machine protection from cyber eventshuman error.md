# Machine protection from cyber events/human error

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Bystronic, a global specialist in sheet-metal processing systems, has announced the launch of a new machine-level cybersecurity service to ensure the operational integrity of sheet-metal processing systems against cyber events and human errors. This cyber protection service has been co-developed with NanoLock Security specifically for Bystronic systems. The service will be available to Bystronic customers with current laser and bending machine models, as of early 2023, as an option within the service packages of ByCare.

"The new protection service safeguards the operational integrity of Bystronic machines by preventing unauthorised modifications to critical content, without impacting their performance and functionality. The solution prevents cyber events from external sources as well as trusted sources, such as the negligence of employees and human errors. Furthermore, we plan to offer periodic or on-request services for online monitoring and analysis," commented Bystronic.

Bystronic and NanoLock Security are also further collaborating to develop the protection service for Bystronic automation and legacy machines. NanoLock is an experienced provider of device-level cybersecurity solutions for industrial and critical infrastructure. The new protection service was demonstrated during EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover from 25-28 October 2022.

Eran Fine, Co-Founder and CEO of NanoLock, added: “Insider breaches on industrial machines- cyber events originating from users with access or employee human error or negligence- are on the rise. While we can't predict when and where these events will take place, we can prevent them with a cyber solution that blocks device tampering, even from trusted sources. We are proud that Bystronic has chosen NanoLock as a partner to co-develop machine-level solutions to maintain the operational integrity of its smart machines, thereby ensuring business continuity for its customers and bringing new levels of security to the factory of the future."

Bystronic's operational headquarters are at Niederönz, Switzerland. Additional development and production sites are at Sulgen (Switzerland); Gotha (Germany); Cazzago San Martino and San Giuliano Milanese (Italy); Tianjin and Shenzhen (China); and Hoffman Estates (USA). It is active with its own sales and service companies in more than 30 countries and represented by agents in numerous other countries.
