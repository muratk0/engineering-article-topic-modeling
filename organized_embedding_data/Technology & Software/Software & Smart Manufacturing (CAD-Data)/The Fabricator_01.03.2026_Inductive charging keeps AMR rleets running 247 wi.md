# Inductive charging keeps AMR rleets running 24/7 with no brakes

[TARİH: 01.03.2026 The Fabricator]

Applications

SITUATION

In a dynamic and highly competitive industry such as logistics, efficient and uninterrupted material flows are crucial. To ensure maximum uptime for its autonomous transport systems, Bluepath Robotics needed a reliable and powerful power supply.

The company, which began as an internal innovation project, spun off independently from Ford Otosan at the end of 2023. Based in Istanbul, Turkey, it develops and produces autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and automated guided vehicles (AGVs) for a variety of intralogistics applications. By automating processes, the robots help to reduce human error, increase efficiency, and improve safety in the workplace.

The portfolio currently comprises more than 10 different AGV and AMR models categorized into three main types—tow, underdrive lift, and forklift— that can be customized for use in material handling, warehouse automation, and production line optimization. System payloads range from 100 to 18,500 kg.

The performance of autonomous transport systems depends crucially on their energy supply. This is a key challenge, particularly for the underride robots that Bluepath Robotics prefers to use in intensive applications with high operating time requirements. In environments in which AMRs run continuously and must always be ready for use, longer interruptions to operation for charging have economic consequences.

Conventional charging methods with physical contact points such as plug connections or sliding contacts were out of the question for the company, as these lead to undesirable downtimes and increased maintenance costs due to mechanical wear. They also hinder the flexible use of the robots by setting up separate loading zones, thereby limiting the overall productivity and availability of the fleet.

"Our robots are often in use around the clock and need to be continuously supplied with energy. That’s why we needed a solution that ensures maximum availability, minimizes human intervention, and is easy to implement and maintain at the same time," explained Fehim Duzgun, CCO.

RESOLUTION

After intensive testing of various systems, the company opted for the etaLink 3000 inductive charging system from Wiferion - a Puls brand. This technology enables contactless intermediate charging during short stops throughout operation, known as in-process charging. The result is continuous 24/7 operation of the AMRs without charging breaks.

"The Wiferion technology is not only fully compatible with our AMRs, but also with existing warehouse and logistics systems, as well as the existing IT infrastructure. The reduction in human intervention in battery management and the significantly increased availability of the robot fleets have been very well received by our customers," said Duzgun.

Wireless energy transmission eliminates the need for mechanical contacts, which reduces maintenance costs while increasing reliability. The charging pads can be approached from all directions, enabling flexible and fast energy absorption without restricting vehicle navigation or requiring additional positioning sensors.

Their 93% efficiency in energy transmission reduces energy consumption, thus lowering the operating costs of the entire AMR fleet.

Implementation at Bluepath’s customers also is simple and flexible. Wiferion’s inductive charging pads can be integrated into existing production or logistics environments at strategically sensible points: on walls, on the floor, along routes, or at loading/unloading stations. No extensive structural measures are required. In addition, the charging points only work when they are really needed, which helps save energy and increase safety.

The Wiferion technology is integrated into Bluepath Robotics’ fleet management system, allowing companies to control and monitor their entire AMR fleet centrally, from navigation and order management to energy supply.
