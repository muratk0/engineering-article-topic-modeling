# Autonomous mobile robot demonstrations

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Locus Robotics showcased its worker-friendly Locus Vector and Origin AMRs (autonomous mobile robots) at the IntraLogistex trade show (19-20 March 2024) at the NEC Birmingham, UK, on stand 600. The manufacturer is a specialist in AMRs for use in warehouses and distribution centres.

"The collaborative robots were fully operational and provided live demonstrations of their flexibility, high productivity, ease-of-use and worker safety in a live, simulated warehouse environment," said Locus Robotics.

Martin Donohoe, regional marketing manager at Locus Robotics, added: "IntraLogisteX 2024 was the public's first opportunity to see how two highly flexible form factors work together to optimise order fulfilment speed and efficiency. Visitors were able to experience, first-hand, their powerful capability in terms of optimising order picking rates and put away, while emphasising safety and ease of use for the worker. It's an exciting, hands-on experience and one that we enjoyed sharing at IntraLogisteX this year."

Locus robots are already deployed at commercial sites throughout the UK and in 300 sites across the world. The multi-bot picking solutions eliminate unproductive walking time, lifting and pushing heavy carts to improve the overall quality of the workplace. This, said the manufacturer, has additional impact on helping to improve staff recruitment and retention rates by facilitating a safer, less stressful working environment.

"Locus Origin is an intelligent autonomous mobile robot (AMR) designed specifically for collaborative, high-volume order fulfilment. By eliminating unproductive walking time and ensuring near-100% order accuracy, Locus Origin doubles fulfilment productivity and efficiency. Locus Vector is a highly flexible, autonomous material handling and logistics robot that delivers a high payload capacity of up to 272kg (600lb) that is ideal for case-picking, shelf-rack moving, discrete order picking and point-to-point material handling," explained Locus Robotics.

Developing warehouse automation that works collaboratively with employees is key to Locus Robotics' business model, designed to enable employers to meet the challenges of an increasing product inventory, volume and fulfilment speeds.
