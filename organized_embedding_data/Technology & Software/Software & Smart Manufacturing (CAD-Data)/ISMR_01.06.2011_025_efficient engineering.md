# EFFICIENT ENGINEERING

[TARİH: 01.06.2011 ISMR]

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GmbH, a leading supplier of sheet metal software solutions, has unveiled its latest software version AutoFormplus R3. It contains new functionalities for hot forming and heating and helps users prevent springback in a systematic way.

Its main feature offers pre-defined control parameter settings for the different stages in an engineering workflow. The three settings – Concept Evaluation (CE), Advanced Concept Evaluation (CE+) and Final Validation (FV) – are aligned to minimise differences when switching from one setting to the next.

“This new concept prevents over-engineering and gives the user the right accuracy at the right time,” AutoForm told ISMR. “As a result, the efficiency of the engineering process can be significantly increased.”

The shared memory multiprocessing version (SMP) solver version is available for 2, 3, 4 and 8 cores – the number of licenses needed to perform parallel execution has been reduced so that multiprocessing is offered at a lower cost. FOR MORE INFORMATION, SEE www.autoform.com

## ATKIN AUTOMATION APPOINTS NEW ASIAN AGENT

ATKIN AUTOMATION has announced the appointment of Baron international services (UK) Ltd to represent sales of its extensive range of steel coil processing equipment in the south east Asian countries of Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia and Vietnam. Atkin Automation has an international reputation for its range of specialist coil processing equipment and automated production lines and currently has equipment working in factories in Europe, North America, South Africa, Russia, China and the UK.

Atkin Automation Managing Director, Chris Ward, is confident that there is an opportunity for Atkin equipment in south east Asia. He said: “Although it is a price sensitive market, there are opportunities for fully automated equipment that can make consistent product to fine tolerances. I am delighted that this vision is shared by Baron international and we look forward to working closely with them, starting on stand T34 at intermach 2011 in Bangkok.” FOR MORE INFORMATION, SEE www.atkinautomation.com

## POWER WAVE

LINCOLN ELECTRIC has launched the PowerWave R350, a robotic welding power source with built-in robotic wire feeder control using the latest inverter power technology to deliver extremely rapid arc response. The multi-process R350 can handle GMAW (MIG), pulsed MIG and flux-cored (FCAW) processes, as well as GTAW DC TIG and pulsed DC TIG. Its Waveform Control Technology advanced process capabilities include standard pulse, as well as Pulse-on-Pulse, Power Mode and RapidArc.

Modular component design allows users to add a Surface Tension Transfer (STT) module for extra arc control on thin sheet metal or critical pipe welding applications. Lincoln Electric’s patent-pending PowerConnect technology enables the PowerWave R350 to automatically adjust to available single or three-phase 50 or 60 Hz input power, whilst maintaining a constant welding output of five to 350 Amps throughout the entire input voltage range (200-600 volts). FOR MORE INFORMATION, SEE www.lincolnelectric.com

THE GLOBAL FINNISH welding solutions provider, Kemppi Oy, and the Danish supplier of automation solutions, inrotech, have joined forces to improve welding efficiency at shipyards. This co-operation has resulted in a new welding automation system that allows the use of robotic welding in confined ship block manufacturing environments.

“Normally, welding automation can only be carried out in the early stage of ship block construction, when structures are relatively simple and easily accessible. The possibilities of using welding automation in the rambling structures of the latter shipbuilding production stage have always been limited, even though a big ship block contains a large number of similar, repeatable welds. They would be ideal for welding automation, but the lack of space has prevented the implementation of robotic welding to date,“ Kemppi explained.

To overcome this obstacle, both companies launched an innovative new solution, the Mobile Automatic Arc. This integrated solution features inrotech’s mobile shipyard robot equipped with Kemppi’s flexible arc welding solution. The new concept is specially designed to be used and operated inside structures where lack of space is an issue.
