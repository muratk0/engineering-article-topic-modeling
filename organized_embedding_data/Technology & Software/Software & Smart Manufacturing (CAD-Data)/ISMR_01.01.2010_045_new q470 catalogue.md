## RENT IT RE-BRAND

123mrp.NET, the award-winning production management system, is to be rebranded to 123insight, with Rent IT Systems UK Ltd. becoming 123 Insight Ltd.

The move is the result of the product’s expansion and the development of its CRM module and tight integration into accounting software, providing capabilities that cross the boundaries of traditional MRP and ERP systems.

123insight provides an end-to-end solution that is suitable for almost any manufacturing environment, including aerospace, marine, automotive, medical, food, textiles, electronics and oil. It has options for CRM, e-commerce and integration/interfaces into all major accounting systems. E-mail: info@123insight.com
