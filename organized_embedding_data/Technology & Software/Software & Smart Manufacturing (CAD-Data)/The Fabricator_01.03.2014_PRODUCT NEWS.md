CAD/CAM software

Vero Software Ltd. has introduced Radan 2014 CAD/CAM software. According to the manufacturer, simulation and time calculation have improved by moving away from NC verification to the area where sequences are defined. Enhancements to 3-D Workflow have reduced the time required to get parts to the nest.

Cluster tools now can be added to the master tool file, allowing the software to ensure the optimum arrangements of cluster tools to punch a specific pattern.

The software adopts the same common cutting strategy for punch presses as that used for profiling machines. It allows for common cut nests to be created automatically, meaning the user can choose, for example, how many parts form a common cut cluster of parts, reducing the sheet’s instability. Tagging for common cut parts also has been improved.

Feed system for circular cold saws

Scotchman® Industries offers its Advanced Feed System to turn semiautomatic circular cold saws into fully automatic production machines.

The operator enters cut lists into the controller, secures the material in the indexing clamp, and hits start. The system moves and clamps the material into position automatically, then cycles the machine.

Operators can enter dimensions manually into the controller, pull a cut list directly from the controller, or download cut list information from a PC. Powered by TigerStop, the programmable controller stores up to 99 programs. For one-off cuts, the operator keys in the desired cut length in inches, fractions, or metric and pushes start.

The system can be used as a programmable stop system or a fully automatic, programmable push-feed system with the optional material clamp. With optional optimizing software, the user can pull out any piece of material, even scrap, and enter the clear stock length into the controller.

Fume extraction system

Miller Electric Mfg. Co. has intro du ce d the FILTAIR® Capture 5 fume extraction system, designed to capture welding fumes up to 5 ft. away from the source. According to the company, this is more than three times farther than conventional technologies.

The system is suitable for applications in heavy equipment manufacturing and general fabrication, as well as in maintenance and repair operations. It can accommodate fume from SMAW, FCAW, GMAW, and GTAW processes.

The fume extraction system features the company’s ZoneFlow™ technology, which creates a capture area up to 4 ft. wide and 5 ft. deep. Air enters the arm at a standard fume-capture rate of around 900 CFM, while the technology also moves clean, filtered air out of the arm at about a 90-degree angle. This action creates a negative-pressure zone, which funnels the fume particulates toward the center of the arm to help maximize the fume capture.
