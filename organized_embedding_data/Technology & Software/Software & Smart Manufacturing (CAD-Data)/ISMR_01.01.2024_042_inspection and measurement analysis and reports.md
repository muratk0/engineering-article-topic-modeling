# Inspection and measurement analysis and reports

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

“Whether you want to analyse surfaces, volume data, motion sequences or meet specific challenges in aerospace, ZEISS INSPECT has the right software variant for your need," the manufacturer explained.

ZEISS INSPECT Optical 3D, for 3D inspection and evaluation, can be used for full-field data acquisition and mesh processing to trend analyses and digital assembly. It provides a consistent workflow and user-centric operation, said ZEISS.

"ZEISS INSPECT X-Ray is designed to analyse volume data efficiently. Users can visualize and analyse parts down to the inner core using CT data. ZEISS INSPECT software will help users to visualize and analyse the data. They can evaluate defects, structures and assembly situations, and bundle results in easy-to-understand reports (even with video)," outlined ZEISS.

ZEISS INSPECT Airfoil is inspection software for blade geometries. Whether in power supply, aircraft and shipbuilding, electrical appliances or the automotive industry, the software analyses optical and tactile measurement data from coordinate measuring machines (CMMs) and is compatible with all common data formats.

ZEISS INSPECT Correlate analyses movements, displacements and strains three-dimensionally. Videos can be turned into meaningful measurement data. Users can analyse dynamic processes such as displacements, rotations or angle changes. The software supports users in 3D testing applications.

Digital image correlation and point tracking algorithms can be used for sub-pixel measurement of 3D coordinates and tracked over time.
