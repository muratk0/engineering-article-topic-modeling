# New investment for Transfluid

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

ADVANCED IDEAS REQUIRE development space to optimise processes through the introduction of suitable solutions. This applies to prototyping and fully automated complex production systems. This is why tube processing specialist, Transfluid Maschinenbau GmbH, has further extended its production area by an additional 1000 square metres, to a new total floor area of 7000 square metres.

"This investment was an obvious one to make, since it enables us to improve further our customer-facing development and high-tech production facilities," explained Stefanie Flaeper, Transfluid CEO. "Particularly for our 't' motion automation systems' product sector, we now have much more resource available in terms of physical space, not just in relation to assembly work but also for test runs and commissioning runs. There is now also scope for implementing pre-series for our customers to further guarantee and evaluate our production process before the equipment is shipped." The additional space and latitude afforded by this new-build also stands to benefit the company's in-house prototyping department.

"To protect changed processes, we have been offering our customers intensive prototyping for many years. This also applies to projects where, in the past, tube connections need to be welded or soldered, and where these parts were to be replaced by geometric end forming operations," added Flaeper. "In such cases, components frequently need to be tested and installed prior to any final changeover. On this, we work very closely with our customers."

This building modification has equipped the prototyping department with three times as much floor space as before. Here, machines are available to tackle the range of reshaping processes, for diameters of 4 to 70mm. A chipless orbital cutting system and CNC bending technology are also on the shop floor.

During the extension work, a high-bay store for spare parts was installed. These new facilities have provided Transfluid with the space now, and in the future, for new and advanced solutions. The company presented these at the Tube 2018 trade fair, in Hall 5 on Stand G34. See www.transfluid.net

## GKN appoints CEO

THE GKN BOARD announced in January 2018 that Anne Stevens, currently Interim Chief Executive, has agreed to become the Group's new Chief Executive. Since her appointment, Anne has taken leadership of an ongoing and wide-ranging internal review of all GKN's businesses which has culminated in the development of a transformation plan to improve significantly GKN's performance.

GKN Chairman, Mike Turner, said: "The Board believes that Anne Stevens has the track record to transform GKN. After a successful turnaround of The Ford Motor Company's businesses in Mexico, Canada and South America, she was appointed as Chief Operating Officer for the Americas where she developed the transformation plan for Ford's U.S. business. Subsequently, Anne became Chairman, CEO and President of Carpenter Technology. She was a non-executive director of Lockheed Martin from 2002 until she stepped down at the end of 2017 to take up her executive role at GKN. Her operational and strategic skills are ideally suited to GKN and the Board is very impressed with the contribution she has made so far in setting out plans for a significant improvement in the Group's performance."

Anne Stevens commented: "GKN has a great executive team leading a dedicated and focused workforce with strong market positions and outstanding technologies. I am committed to, and I am confident that we can, deliver more for our shareholders while improving what we do for our customers and supporting our employees."

## New CEO for Tenova Metals

ROBERTO PANCALDI, current Tenova Metals Chief Operating Officer, has become the new CEO of Tenova Metals. Effective 1 January 2018, Roberto Pancaldi became the new Tenova Metals' CEO, assuming the role from Andrea Rocca who, after five years at Tenova, has taken new responsibilities at Tecpetrol, another company in the Techint Group.

Pancaldi, 55 years old, joined the Techint Group as a process engineer in 1988 and has spent his entire career at Techint and then at Tenova. He held various leadership positions in different business units in the Metals Division, contributing actively to the development and expansion of the company.

"His deep awareness of our technologies and products will guarantee the continuity of projects and will support and stimulate the innovation drive which characterises Tenova's approach to business," the company told ISMR.

Leveraging a workforce of over three thousand professionals in 22 countries across five continents, Tenova designs technologies and develops services that help companies reduce costs, save energy, limit environmental impact and improve working conditions.

DURING ITS MEETING on 22 November 2017 in Brussels, Belgium, the CECIMO General Assembly elected Dr. Roland Feichtl as President of CECIMO for a period of two years. Succeeding Luigi Galdabini, Managing Director, CESARE GALDABINI S.p.A., in this role, Dr. Feichtl has been entrusted with the responsibility of leading the European Association that represents more than a third of global machine tool (MT) production.

Dr. Feichtl affirmed that, in line with previous presidencies, he "will continue to promote the strategic importance of our industry towards European authorities and citizens. I will make sure that manufacturing and re-industrialisation remain at the top of EU policy priorities". He underlined how, in such a complex environment, businesses are faced with common grand challenges such as globalisation, climate change, resource efficiency, regulatory pressures and the increasing weight of emerging economies "A few decades ago, maybe it was possible for companies and countries or regions to solve all their problems on their own," he explained. "This is not the case any more. Machines, factories, companies and, yes, also countries are linked more than ever. CECIMO should help its members to tackle their challenges together."

During his presidency, he will closely follow the initiatives of the European Commission in the Digital Single Market which aims to achieve considerable benefits for the digital economy. Under his mandate, CECIMO will "develop industry information and expertise in new emerging areas of importance e.g. Digitisation, Digital Platforms, industrial security and trust, as well as monitor new customer technologies that change the workpieces to be machined such as new drive trains. CECIMO will look for the support of European authorities to sustain the efforts of its member companies in providing machine tool solutions that improve the energy-efficiency of combustion engines.

Dr Feichtl will "use the success of the Italian campaign for tax and other incentives to promote the modernisation of machine tool infrastructure in other CECIMO countries" and will push forward the discussion on the circular economy to achieve sustainability objectives. He will also continue to "explain to European authorities and citizens the strategic role our industry plays in boosting jobs, growth and investment across Europe, as well as the necessity to improve the business environment to ensure the development of Europe's manufacturing base within its borders, maintain its competitiveness and ensure a level playing field for our industry".■
