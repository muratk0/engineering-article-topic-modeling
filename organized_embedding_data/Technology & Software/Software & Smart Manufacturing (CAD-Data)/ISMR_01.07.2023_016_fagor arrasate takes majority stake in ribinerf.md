# Fagor Arrasate takes majority stake in Ribinerf

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Fagor Arrasate, part of the MONDRAGON corporation specialising in the development of capital goods for the forming and processing of metallic materials and composites, has acquired 70% of Ribinerf's shares. Ribinerf specialises in industrial robotics, 3D vision and artificial intelligence to automate part quality control, sorting, picking and racking processes. It is a Girona-based company established in 1986 that is dedicated to the development of technologically advanced solutions for the automation of industrial processes. It also provides automated solutions for stamping and foundry processes.

"This is an operation that is fully aligned with our innovation strategy, which is committed to offering increasingly intelligent, flexible and productive automation solutions," said Unai López Moñux, CEO of Fagor Arrasate. "This acquisition provides Fagor Arrasate with market-leading technology for the automation of quality control, picking and racking processes, which are critical for our customers in the automotive, household appliance and steel sectors."

Fagor Arrasate will be able to count on Ribinerf's capabilities and expertise, which are focused on manufacturing standardised solutions that solve non-deterministic scenarios, impossible to automate until now.

“Ribinerf machines do not simply perform the task but, thanks to AI, are able to make decisions and resolve on their own the incidents that occur in variable production, minimizing line downtime and working with maximum performance, allowing customers to optimise and improve the competitiveness of their processes," explained Fagor Arrasate.

“Automating low value-added processes that until now could only be carried out by people means offering solutions to problems that our customers face on a daily basis. Thanks to the application of robotics, artificial intelligence and 3D vision, these solutions also mean substantial savings in the costs associated with non-quality due to the reduction of defective parts and because batches of parts are not returned by customers. Ribinerf provides us with the capacity to incorporate this type of solution into our products," added Unai López Moñux.
