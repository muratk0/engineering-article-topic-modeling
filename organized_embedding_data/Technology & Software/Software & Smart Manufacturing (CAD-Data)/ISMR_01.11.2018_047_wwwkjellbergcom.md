As a member of the industry association, Business Network 4.0 e.V., Kjellberg Finsterwalde, in addition to developing new products, also works on solutions for existing machines to make the benefits of digitalisation, networking and operation available for existing power sources. At EuroBLECH, individual projects were presented, such as a new user interface for the monitoring and diagnosis of existing power sources or networking between the power source and the suction unit to enable automatic valve control which contributes to reducing energy costs.

"In cutting, we had got to a technology readiness level where we saw that our cutting quality and quantity was hard to improve. We believed it was important to have new functions on the machinery, especially if you think about entire production systems (not just cutting but handling and storage etc. as all these processes will be linked in the future). To optimise the entire production process, all the machines need to be networked, understood and predicted," outlined Dr. Schnick.

Volker Krink, Head of Development, Kjellberg Finsterwalde, agreed with this assessment.

"Our system has to work in collaboration with other systems, sharing and displaying all types of data. Our customers need all the information at their fingertips. We want to make it as easy as possible for them to work with our systems/machinery, so that they are as predictable and reliable as possible (which also helps production planning). This is the age of digitilisation and we are ready for it. We are working with partners on this, as a collaborative project, and are developing software that will integrate different systems. This will apply overarchingly across all our systems," he told /SMR.

He pointed out that it was already possible for the company to monitor the status and analytics from its cutting systems on different partner booths at EuroBLECH, in accordance with Industry 4.0 principles.

"We are also implementing our Plasma 4.0 solution into a bigger framework to turn it into an industry solution. Our customers have so many machines on their shop floor, with so many different dashboards – it all gets a bit complicated. We have therefore come up with the idea of one single dashboard where all our machine data can be seen, working smartly and interoperably. This can save the customer a great deal of money, particularly when it comes to saving energy costs," concluded Krink.

## Towards the future

It has been a busy year for Kjellberg Finsterwalde and it has seen tremendous growth in sales and personnel.

"Our product sales are doing very well this year – we are extremely busy trying to meet delivery times. This year, we invested millions of euros into increasing our production capacity. We are continuously optimising our assembly processes and have employed another 50 people this year. That is a huge increase for us," Dr. Schnick told /SMR.

"We are on track for growth of 50% this year on our SmartFocus machinery sales, especially in China. That market is growing hugely. Our main markets are in Germany and Europe, followed by China and the USA."

With its new technology launches, and its clear embrace of Industry 4.0 principles in its product development, the future looks bright for this German cutting specialist.
