“As a technology specialist in sensors, measuring, marking and identification systems (as well as microscopes and image processing systems), we are a key player in automation and quality assurance. We also offer a comprehensive range of services to support customers [in their quest] to increase process stability and product quality. With our direct sales concept, we maximise on-site support and offer a quick and optimal solution for every complex application problem," explained Keyence Deutschland GmbH.

Its IM-8000 image dimension measurement system offers precision measurements at the touch of a button, and is designed for rapid, precise testing of small and medium-sized components. These devices are suitable for measuring a wide range of geometries and facilitate quality control through automatic, non-contact measurement of multiple components simultaneously. With a measuring range of 300 x 200mm, the IM-8000 offers solutions for a wide range of manufacturing and quality assurance applications.
