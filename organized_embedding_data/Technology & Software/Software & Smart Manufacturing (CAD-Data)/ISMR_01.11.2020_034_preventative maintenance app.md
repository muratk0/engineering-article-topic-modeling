# Preventative maintenance app

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

Lincoln Electric® has introduced a new app that takes the guesswork out of preventative maintenance and aims to ensure strong performance from its Ranger® welder/ generator. The NEXTime App connects to most Lincoln Electric Ranger welder generators from up to 15 feet away or more to provide realtime data and alerts for a machine.

“This eliminates guesswork and confusion about equipment maintenance, and helps keep users prepared for the job, especially in the field,” explained the manufacturer.

The NEXTime App performs several valuable functions such as:

- Monitoring battery and fuel status.
- Providing maintenance alerts.
- Creating maintenance logs.
- Supplying maintenance instructions and locating service and distribution facilities.
- Managing a fleet of machines (unlimited number).

The app can be downloaded from iTunes or Google Play, and requires installation of the NEXTime Wireless Module inside an engine drive – a simple process that, says Lincoln Electric, takes minutes.

The NEXTime App is currently compatible with Lincoln Electric’s Ranger 225, 250 and 305 models. The 260MPX and 330MPX models are currently designed with maintenance screens as part of the user interface and will be compatible with the NEXTime App at a later date.

Lincoln Electric is a specialist in the design, development and manufacture of arc welding products; robotic arc welding systems; plasma and oxyfuel cutting equipment.
