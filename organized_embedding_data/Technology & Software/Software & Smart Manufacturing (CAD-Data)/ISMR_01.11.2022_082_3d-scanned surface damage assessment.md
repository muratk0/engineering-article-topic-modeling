# 3D-scanned surface damage assessment

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Creaform, a business unit of AMETEK, Inc. and a specialist in portable 3D measurement solutions and engineering services, has released VXintegrity, a 3D-scanning solution for surface damage assessment. Designed specifically to respond to the challenges facing industrial assets owners and NDT service companies, this new software platform contains four different modules, intended to tackle any surface analyses as well as simple metrology applications.

"Making it possible to measure up to 80 times faster than manual measuring tools, the new VXintegrity software platform, combined with our 3D scanners, represents the optimal solution to the current shortage of experienced technicians and aging and/or damaged infrastructures. This user-independent technology requires little training and few certifications, which means that technicians can be up and running faster than with other techniques," it told ISMR.

"This complete NDT solution also enables industrial asset owners to make the best maintenance decisions with the best available data, as it can accurately measure thickness loss on corroded, industrial and complex geometry such as elbows; nozzles; pressure vessel heads; tank floors and valves. A complete analysis of all types of surfaces is now possible," it added.

The VXintegrity software platform regroups four different application software modules dedicated to specific industry challenges:

- Pipeline module (also known as Pipecheck): This provides corrosion, denting (mechanical damage) and wrinkle analyses for pipeline integrity assessment.

■Aerospace module (also known as SmartDENT 3D): Suitable for the aerospace industry and MRO companies, this 3D scanning solution is used for hailstorm damage inspection; flap and spoiler maintenance; aircraft incident analysis and regulatory scheduled maintenance.

■New surface damage module: Specifically created with power generation and heavy industry owners in mind, this module assesses surface damage on complex geometries and a variety of shapes, sizes and materials.

■New monitoring module: This solution is a noiseless NDT method used to compare damage progression through time, deformation analyses and other simple metrology applications.

"The metrology-grade 3D data generated through this software platform can precisely identify small thickness variations between inspections to compare damage progression through time," said François Lachance, Product Manager, Creaform. "These four modules are sure to open up new business opportunities for NDT service providers."

2022 marks the 20th anniversary of Creaform. With headquarters and manufacturing operations in Lévis, Québec, it is present in more than 85 countries, through its 14 local offices all around the world and a network of over 125 distributors.■
