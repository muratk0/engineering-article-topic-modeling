# RAPID AND COMPLEX

[TARİH: 01.01.2020 ISMR]

Additive manufacturing (AM) is a method of making production parts and products directly from design data, building accurate components by adding layers of material to obtain the final shape with minimal waste and no expensive dedicated tooling. It enables radical product re-design and creates new material properties.

AM enables the manufacturing of complex designs that are too difficult or expensive to produce using traditional machining, milling, moulds and dies. It also excels at rapid prototyping and offers a dynamic and design-driven process. AM is ideal for making prototypes quickly using 3D CAD and eliminates the extensive process and associated high costs.

“The sphere of AM solutions providers and researchers has continued to expand, with leading providers now able to market a variety of truly manufacturing-oriented solutions alongside a deep roster of partners to help address key challenges during the processing chain,” commented industry analyst, SmarTech Analysis, in its ‘2020 Additive Manufacturing Market Outlook and Summary of Opportunities’ report.

## Market outlook

Bound metal additive manufacturing technologies have gained significant interest and investment dollars over the last three years and are being developed by the likes of HP; Desktop Metal; GE Additive; ExOne and more. They are now expected to achieve close to twice the rate of growth compared to the overall metal additive manufacturing market over the next ten years, according to the latest from SmarTech Analysis.

Aluminium AM solutions are in high demand cross technology segments, and players in bound metal technologies stand to gain significant opportunity if reliable aluminium printing and sintering solutions can be brought to market to compete with metal powder bed fusion,” added SmarTech Analysis.
