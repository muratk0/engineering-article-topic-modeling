Report says gaps in skills, data are slowing AI’s impact for small business owners

ECI Software Solutions, provider of AI-powered, cloud-based business management software and services, has released its "AI Readiness Report: Data, Trends, and Real-World Challenges." Based on a survey of more than 550 leaders of small to medium-size businesses across the U.S., Canada, and Australia, the report details where AI is being applied first and what is blocking progress.

The research shows more than 70% of respondents hold a positive view of AI, but many are not yet prepared to turn it into measurable outcomes. With labor shortages and operational complexity rising across manufacturing, distribution, and field service, they are looking to AI for relief, but they cite lack of in-house expertise, data readiness, and clarity on where to begin as the top barriers to adoption.

Among those using or planning to adopt AI, 60% identified data analysis and reporting as a top focus, followed by content creation and marketing at an average of 49%, customer service at 42%, and inventory management at approximately 34%.

Nearly 40% said they have not yet seen measurable results from AI initiatives.

ASMAG Group acquires Mill Masters

ASMAG Group, an Austria-based manufacturer of machinery and equipment for the tube, pipe, and profile industry, is acquiring Mill Masters, located in Jackson, Tenn. This strategic move strengthens ASMAG’s presence in North America while expanding its technological capabilities in the field of tube welding mills.

Mill Masters specializes in tube mills for smaller tube dimensions, complementing ASMAG Group’s existing portfolio, which focuses on mills for larger dimensions.

Mill Masters will continue to operate under its established name and will remain at its current location. While it will be integrated into the ASMAG Group organizationally, customers and partners can expect continuity in relationships, expertise, and service.

ASMAG Group already operates a sales office in Chicago. With Mill Masters, the company now establishes its first full operational production and service site in the U.S.

All Metals Fabricating Targets Data Centers

All Metals Fabricating, a provider of precision sheet metal fabrication, machining, finishing, and related production capabilities, has joined a group of investors in acquiring Power Electric Supply Co. to strengthen its capabilities in the data center and power generation sectors.

Power Electric Supply Co., formerly known as Meister Supply Co., focuses on procurement, warehousing, and distribution of electrical and telecommunications equipment. The company operates across Texas with locations in San Antonio, Dallas, and Houston.

Through the partnership, Allen, Texas-based All Metals Fabricating expects to serve as a one-stop shop by offering additional sourcing options alongside its existing manufacturing services. The collaboration is expected to help the company secure more competitive pricing for copper bus bar and related components, while Power Electric Supply customers will gain access to All Metals Fabricating’s manufacturing capabilities.
