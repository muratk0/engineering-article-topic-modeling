Add-on for CAD/CAM software quickly generates per-part cost

Striker Systems has announced the availability of a new software application that allows users of its CAD/CAM and nesting software to quickly generate a per-part cost of CNC cutting operations.

The Striker Costing Assistant add-on calculates a per-part machine operation cost for SS-Profile cut parts to assist with cost allocation and quoting. It considers all machine consumables, utilities, facilities, labor, maintenance, and other costs as configured by the user.

User-configurable to adapt to specific manufacturing operations, the addon allows for multiple styles to be created to handle various materials and cutting operations. When integrated with SS-Nest, it provides an individual part-cutting cost and provides the machine cutting cost for each part across an entire nested job containing multiple layouts.

Hand-held laser welding system penetrates thick materials

IPG Photonics has launched the LightWeld XR hand-held laser welding and cleaning system with an expended variety of capabilities to address more materials and thicknesses than previous models in the line.

Filter removes vapors, contaminants at point of use

Walmec North America has introduced the .01 Micron SuperStar Filter, designed to remove of any vapors and contaminants down to .01 micron and provide clean, dry, compressed air.

The four-stage filtration system has a .01-micron rating, with flow of 50, 75, or 100 SCFM and pressure rating up to 250 PSI. The filter works best when installed at or near the point of use.

The first- and second-stage filters remove moisture, liquids, dust, rust, scale, and other contaminants to 5 microns. An automatic float drain under the second-stage filter opens and expels all collected liquids whenever an ounce or more is present. The third and fourth stages remove remaining particles down to .01 micron.

Voltage-sensing wire feeder designed to be weatherproof

The weatherproof feeder has an IP44 protection rating and features a completely sealed wire feed compartment that protects the wire and internal components from water splashes in all directions.
