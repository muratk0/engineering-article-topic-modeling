Temperature controllers help aerospace component processor earn Nadcap certification

Situation

Active Magnetic Inspection Inc. (AMI), Sun Valley, Calif., performs plating, stripping, stress relieving, and magnetic particle inspection processes for fastener manufacturers in the aerospace industry. In an industry that is highly regulated because of the extreme need for safety, AMI must use a precise level of process control and accurate recordkeeping to meet quality control requirements.

AMI decided to raise the bar and establish a process system that would earn accreditation from Nadcap, thus enabling the company to strengthen its position in the aerospace fastener business.

Resolution

AMI worked with Titan Industrial Heating Systems, a designer and manufacturer of temperature-controlled manufacturing processes, to identify the many points of control required, including two stress relief ovens that each needed monitoring of 60 product containers. To achieve Nadcap accreditation, the company also needed to establish a thorough quality system, including well-documented process instructions and complete records for all production lots, including time and temperature data, to meet the needs of the annual audit.

With AMI having to provide 100 percent traceability on its output, Titan recommended temperature controllers from West Control Solutions.

The CAL 9500P process controller was chosen as the front end for its flexibility and profiling capability. To satisfy Nadcap process data requirements, all of the instruments are tied to a central PC that runs CALGrafix process monitoring software. The result is that every point is monitored and stored on a minute-by-minute basis.

AMI earned its Nadcap certification, and there have been no flaws in the system of two ovens, one with 36 controls and one with 34, both running 24/7.

Custom wheel manufacturer uses carts to move products safely through production

Situation

HRE Performance Wheels, Vista, Calif., makes built-to-order wheel sets for racing, performance, and luxury cars, as well as SUVs. The wheels, offered in a customized choice of offsets, widths, and finishes, are sold through high-end car dealerships, specialty retailers, and performance companies worldwide.

The one- and three-piece forged aluminum wheels travel during production through numerous manufacturing and finishing operations. Safe handling throughout the production process is a must because even the slightest damage could result in a rejected product—a significant cost with wheels that average $8,000 per set.

Resolution

HRE selected the Creform system of plastic-coated steel pipe and joints to create a fleet of custom-designed pushcarts to move the wheels safely through the manufacturing facility. The steel pipe and metal joints provide the strength to cradle the wheels by the OD of the rims, while the plastic coating protects the wheels from damage during handling and transit.

Associates move the carts from station to station, removing the wheels for processing and placing them back on the carts for continued processing. Each wheel fits into a designated space to prevent nesting and the possibility of contact with another wheel. This designated space also allows for quick identification and simplified loading and unloading. The outside upright supports on either end of the carts also act as push/pull handles while helping maintain a high degree of stability.

The carts have two height levels and six 5-in.-dia. urethane casters. Four casters feature brakes that can be engaged for secure positioning, and two provide swivel support. Each cart measures 20 in. wide by 70 in. long by 65 in. high, with a weight limit of 500 lbs. to hold eight wheels.
