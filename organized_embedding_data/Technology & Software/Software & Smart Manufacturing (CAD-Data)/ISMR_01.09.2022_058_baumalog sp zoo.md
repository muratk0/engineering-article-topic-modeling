# Baumalog Sp. z.o.o.

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.baumalog.eu

Baumalog is a Polish engineering company which manufactures automated storage and transport systems used by many industries. It designs and manufactures solutions that enable the automation processing of goods (such as sheet, profiles, bars or other materials with large masses and dimensions). They are also designed to increase the efficiency of processes connecting with internal logistics, optimising the flow of materials in the production process and at the picking and shipment stages.

"Supporting our clients, we deal the entire process i.e. from reception of material to the warehouse, through to warehousing, effective material flow between individual processes and up to picking," it explained.

Its portfolio includes Modula vertical lift modules as well as automation options such as the Transfer Tower; TwinTower; SmartFlow system; Loader; Multi-Tower and MonoTower.

## Eagle Lasers

www.eaglelasers.com

Eagle is a manufacturer of fibre laser cutting systems which are designed, programmed and manufactured in accordance with Industry-4.0 via EAGLE eRs (EAGLE Reporting System) software.

"The combination of high powers; dynamic motion; the latest cutting technology and automated material handling systems provide our customers with the highest efficiency," it told ISMR. “We believe in optimising processes through effective innovations that lead our customers towards industrial transformation."

Eagle components are designed to boost productivity. The rapid nine-second pallet exchange adds extra working hours per week for users. Eagle's patented eVa cutting head is designed to reduce contamination by up to four times and minimise downtime due to lens replacement.

"Up to 30kW of power and 6G acceleration increase cutting speed and productivity while automated systems enhance repeatability, allowing for 24/7 production. Come and see our rapid fibre laser cutting systems on our booth at EuroBLECH 2022," it added.
