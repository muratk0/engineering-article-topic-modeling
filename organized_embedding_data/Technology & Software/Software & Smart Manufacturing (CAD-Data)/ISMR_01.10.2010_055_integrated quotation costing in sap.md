# INTEGRATED QUOTATION COSTING IN SAP

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

For most suppliers in the industry, an efficient quotation costing is a substantial competitive edge. The placement of an order often requires numerous quotations within a short period of time. Therefore, high availability and reliability of the underlying costing data is crucial to reach target prices and minimum margins.

With the OSCo Quotation Costing, the quotations are calculated based on rules, as only the particularities of the inquiries have to be entered on prepared screens. Thus, complex quotations can also be calculated quickly and accurately. It is now possible to build several assemblylike costing alternatives for different production technologies or lot sizes (scale quantities) without existing master data structures.

For subsequent orders, users are able to generate rule-based article masters, routings and bills of material as well as customary data, all of which are necessary for a repetitive manufacturing in the ERP system. The procedures of the SAP Variant Configuration and SAP Document Management can be integrated with all functions.

The solution is completely integrated in the SAP ERP system without any interfaces whereby the essential logistics functions of SAP are appropriately consolidated. The high degree of integration of control in the SAP system is carried out methodically to ensure the transparency and reliability of the product cost data at any time. In the last six months, the software has been implemented successfully in three European production companies.
