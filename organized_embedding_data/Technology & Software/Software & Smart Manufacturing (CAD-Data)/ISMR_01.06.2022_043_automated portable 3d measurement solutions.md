# Automated, portable 3D measurement solutions

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

Creaform, a worldwide specialist in portable and automated metrology solutions, exhibited at Hannover Messe (Hall 6, booth G30) recently. Its latest 3D measurement technologies were presented at the show.

“The future of quality control in smart factories begins with new approaches to quality control, including the use of portable or automated dimensional inspection systems directly on the production line. Many manufacturers are turning to online and offline metrology solutions, including 3D scanners, to automate their inspections and quality control," it explained.

"With a 3D optical measurement solution, manufacturers have a fast, portable, accurate and versatile instrument for measuring, testing and validating parts and components during iterative product development processes, as well as during quality control and maintenance. Additionally, a colour palette can be used to quickly identify discrepancies between a part and its CAD file. The software displays the results in a clear and intuitive way, which facilitates the analysis. 3D scanning displays an overview of the inspected part and measures surface profiles, not just individual points," it added.

Creaform's portable 3D scanners Go!SCAN 3D, HandySCAN 3D and MetraSCAN 3D and its 3D measurement software, VXelements, help manufacturers to perform precise measurements directly in the production environment. Solutions such as Creaform's MetraSCAN 3D-R, robot-mounted, optical 3D scanner and CUBE-R, a customisable, turnkey, automated 3D scanning CMM, enable manufacturers to perform quality control fully automated.

With headquarters and manufacturing operations in Lévis, Québec, Creaform operates innovation centres in Lévis, Grenoble (France) and Texas, with direct sales operations in Canada; USA; Mexico; Brazil; France; Germany; Italy; Spain; China; Japan; Korea; Thailand and Singapore. Creaform is part of Ultra Precision Technologies, a division of AMETEK Inc.■

Automated 3D measurement solutions are used for a wide variety of different applications, including in-process inspections of parts and tools; online and offline ISPs; inspections of supplier parts before they go into production and the digitisation of quality control records for archiving, traceability and updating old plans.
