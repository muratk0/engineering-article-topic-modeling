# Surface inspection solutions

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 ISMR]

AMETEK Surface Vision, a provider of surface inspection solutions, exhibited its products for the metals market at AISTech's 2021 Iron & Steel Technology conference and exhibition in June (Nashville, USA). Among the modular, scalable products that the company's team introduced are the SmartView® surface inspection and SmartAdvisor® process video monitoring systems.

"Our surface inspection systems are relied upon by almost every major strip metal producer around the world, with inspection solutions for every key stage of production. Many key processes in the steel industry depend on these systems for quality control. We also provide solutions that meet the challenges of the recently expanded electric vehicles (EV) market, with defect detection and quality assurance for silicon steel used for motors and transformers, and lightweight or other steel material for EV body structures," it told ISMR.

AMETEK Surface Vision jointly exhibited with sister company, AMETEK Land, at the event, allowing attendees to explore temperature measurement systems for their steelmaking processes.

AMETEK Surface Vision (formerly Cognex Surface Inspection Systems Division) is a specialist in automated online surface inspection solutions with a broad product portfolio optimised for web and surface inspection as well as monitoring and process surveillance applications. It is a unit of AMETEK Process and Analytical Instruments, a division of AMETEK, Inc., a global manufacturer of electronic instruments and electromechanical devices.

Its product portfolio includes three distinct product lines: SmartView® systems, SmartAdvisor® systems and Slit Inspection. Each product line enables customers to inspect the surfaces of materials processed in a continuous fashion across the metals, paper, plastics, non-wovens and glass industries.
