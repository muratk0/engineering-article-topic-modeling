# 3D measurement scans of large and complex parts

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Creaform, part of AMETEK, Inc. and a worldwide provider of automated and portable 3D measurement solutions, has announced the latest advances to its HandySCAN 3D|MAX Series. These include a significant enhancement of the volumetric accuracy for large parts, additional measurement feedback to guide users to "capture the right data on the first attempt" as well as other software upgrades designed to enhance hardware performance.

"The HandySCAN 3D|MAX Series integrates the core benefits of the HandySCAN 3D™ lineup, optimised to obtain metrology-grade measurements in minutes for large and complex parts. This results in accurate 3D scans without requiring surface preparation, ideal for professionals in diverse industries such as mining, transport, energy and heavy industries," explained the manufacturer.

"With the introduction of new algorithms the upgraded HandySCAN 3D|MAX Series now achieves an accredited volumetric accuracy of up to 0.075mm + 0.010mm/m. This represents, for example, an over 30% increase in accuracy on a 5m part. A new target acquisition mode, enhanced target quality indicators and a target deviation table have also been integrated to provide comprehensive measurement feedback, ensuring the quality of the 3D model, as well as an increase in speed, portability and simplicity," it continued.

The HandySCAN 3D™|MAX Series has been designed to reduce the time and resources required for product development and quality assurance by offering faster and accurate measurements.

"With accuracy of up to 0.075mm + 0.010mm/m, a first on the market for a handheld device, it measures features with smaller tolerances, ensuring precision. It can measure and analyse any part (regardless of size, shape, complexity and finish) and offers an extra-large scanning area (up to 2.0m x 2.4m or 6.6ft × 7.9ft. Part size ranges from 1m to 15m (3.3ft to 49.2ft). A scale bar mode enables large depth of field for seamless target acquisition from 0.3m to 2.5m. Powerful and intuitive software provides optimal user experience, even for non-experts," added Creaform.

Headquartered in Lévis, Québec (Canada), Creaform is present in over 85 countries through its 26 global locations and a network of over 100 distributors.
