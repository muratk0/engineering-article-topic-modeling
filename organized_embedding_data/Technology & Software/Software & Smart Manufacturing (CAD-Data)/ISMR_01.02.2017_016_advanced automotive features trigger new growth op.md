# Advanced automotive features trigger new growth opportunities

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

INTENSIFYING COMPETITION, DYNAMIC consumer requirements, and volatile markets are compelling automotive Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs) to differentiate themselves through novel features in safety and Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS). The market is gradually altering with participants' keener focus on comfort, convenience, handling, sustainability, smart mobility, as well as vehicle versatility and utility.

"Multiple airbags and driving aids, such as parking sensors and brake assist, are expected to be standard features across all cars sold in Latin America (LATAM) over the next three to five years," said Frost & Sullivan mobility industry analyst, Thales Jurado. "Emphasis on these new technologies, services and features will provide OEMs with greater opportunities to capture market share and directly impact future product roadmaps and vehicle developments."

Some of the companies that already offer holistic, next-generation vehicle features include Ford, BMW, Audi, Daimler, VW and General Motors.

"The three key strategies for OEMs with regard to feature selection and offering are 'feature cascade', 'flagship model' and 'customer customisation'," noted Frost & Sullivan mobility research manager, Yeswant Abhimanyu. "As 'customer delight' is becoming an integral part of an OEMs customer acquisition strategy, offering high levels of customisation will lead to winning mind and market share."

Features such as ambient vehicle lighting and heated/ventilated seats are currently optional, but these convenience features in seating, lighting and sensing will drive growth for OEMs. Meanwhile, transitional features, such as smart keys and connected infotainment, are fast becoming must-have features.

## AWL-Techniek continues to expand

DUTCH ROBOTIC LASER welding specialist, AWL-Techniek, continued its expansion in 2016. Not only did the company reach a record high of 100,000,000 euros turnover, it also strengthened its relationship with its customers. With branches now fully operational in the Netherlands, Czech Republic and China, the company now has further ambitions.

At the end of 2016, AWL established a new business in Mexico and the first employees started working there. For 2017, its plan is to build a third hall at the Czech facility to keep up with growth. In addition, AWL is investigating opportunities to develop its own facility in the USA.

"These steps ensure that we can truly be a global partner for our customers, offering local solutions and service around the world," said Van 't Hof, CEO of AWL. The continuous R&D push is helping the company to serve the demanding automotive industry, now and in the future. Modularisation is one of the key pillars in its R&D programme as customers are able to define their own personal standard, based on standard building blocks. In addition, company R&D is focusing on future developments such as Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, applications for cobots (collaborative robots), vision and extensive automation. All these developments are expected to contribute to AWL's further growth. See www.awl.nl
