# SCAN, INSPECT AND MEASURE

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]
Optivision has a range of solutions for 2D and 3D part measurement and inspection.

InspecVision was formed by sheet metal manufacturers in 2004 to create a complete sheet metal inspection and reverse engineering solution. The system it created can automatically inspect or reverse engineer sheet metal parts at every stage of production – flat, folded, welded or assembled.

“Most of our customers are sheet metal fabricators and generally any manufacturers who own/operate X/Y CNC, laser, plasma, punch, waterjet cutting and forming machines. We have installations in a wide range of companies, from large multinational firms to small job shops in over 30 countries,” the company explained.

## 2D inspection

The company has two main product lines, the Planar for 2D inspection and the OptiScan for 3D inspection. At EuroBLECH, the company introduced its new Planar P70.20 sheet metal inspection model as a lower cost alternative for some customers.

“Our award winning Planar is the world’s fastest 2D measurement system. There are no moving components and the measurements are taken using a very high resolution camera, which scans parts placed on a backlit glass surface. It can be used for inspection and reverse engineering of flat opaque and semi-transparent parts. By adding our additional Opti-Probe and Opti-Scan 3D modules, the measurement capability can be extended to full 3D using both touch probe and white light scanning technology,” explained Inspecvision.

“Using this complete system, parts can be checked during every stage of the production process. Flat profiles can be inspected with Planar then, after bending/forming, the final part can be inspected with one or both 3D measurement modules. We have a large selection of machine sizes from small to large and users can measure parts of up to 3000mm x 1220mm (118” x 48”) or 2355mm x 1570mm (93” x 62”) at accuracies of between +/- 12 microns and +/-100 microns (+/- 0.00047” to +/- 0.0039”).”

One special feature of the Planar system is its ability to rapidly reverse engineer flat parts. A profile can be placed onto the machine and reverse engineered in seconds. The machine will output DXF or DWG CAD files which can then be used on a cutting system to make duplicates of the part, or simply to document a part for which no drawing exists. Familiar CAD functionality allows the user to edit and clean the data to ensure standardisation of hole sizes etc. and clean edges, for example, before saving the file.

“Flat parts are measured with our Planar machine which takes up to 40 million measurements in 0.1 seconds. The measurements are then automatically compared to a CAD file to create the inspection report. Flat parts with forms, embosses, louvres or inserts can also be checked with the new SurfScan option. Surfscan is much lower cost than the full 3D option and much simpler to use,” the company told ISMR.

Depending upon the model, the Planar 2D model can be retrofitted with the OptiScan 3D or Surface scan. One option is 2D repositioning so that users can easily measure parts that are over the table size. A bar code scanner option is available for any model so that users can inspect using a part's barcode.

## 3D inspection

Planar has the additional option of adding 3D measurement capability by installing the Opti-Probe module. The Opti-Probe is a wireless touch probe device fitted with special optical markers that are tracked by a camera. Measurement can be taken in 3D, which can then be used for inspection or reverse engineering applications. This device can be retrofitted to the Planar system and is also available as a stand-alone product.

For folded and assembled parts, InspecVision has also developed the OptiScan.

“It is the only 3D scanner in the world which can measure surfaces and edges in 3D. 3D edges are critical when measuring sheet metal parts as their geometry is mostly defined by their edges, not by their surfaces,” said Dr Jan Antonis, Managing Director, InspecVision.

Opti-Scan 3D is a retrofittable additional module that can be added to existing Planar measurement systems. Using white light scanning/fringe interferometry, it can measure parts in seconds. InspecVision is a specialist in edge measurement and holds the patent for edge measurement using a backlight. With the Opti-Scan, it is possible to measure both surfaces and edges of sheet metal components in 3D.

With the addition of the Opti-Probe handheld wireless device, hidden features can be quickly measured by touch probing. The OptiScan camera is then used to calculate the position of the Opti-Probe in 3D by tracking retro-reflective markers mounted onto the probe hardware.

Available in a range of flexible configurations, the Planar and OptiScan integrate to create a complete sheet metal inspection solution.
