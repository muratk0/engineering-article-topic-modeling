# Optimate GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

"By using our services, designing companies can optimise their sheet metal part designs during the development process. Manual feasibility check is a thing of the past. With just one click, sheet metal processing companies can analyse and adjust their customers' parts. Job shoppers can order sheet metal parts online and check feasibility at the same time. The integration of our services via an API interface makes this possible," it added.

Optimate GmbH is an independent spin-off from high-tech company, TRUMPF, based in Stuttgart (Germany). Founded in 2020, the company specialises in the digital analysis and optimisation of sheet metal parts. Optimate aims to enable the sheet metal fabrication market to design more sustainably, reduce costs and manufacture more efficiently.

"Just one click; analyse and optimise your sheet metal part design at the push of a button. Your benefits: reduce production steps, optimise quality and ensure process reliability to save costs," said the company.

## Saronni Srl

Stand E142

www.saronni.it or https://saronni.com/en/

Saronni will showcase its flexible press feeding line at EuroBLECH 2024.

“Featuring a double decoiler; automatic coil containment; precision straightener with automatic roll pack change and electronic feeder, it is a highly flexible solution. A doublespindle decoiler with automatic rotation, controlled by a servo motor, reduces changeover times. The DSG Direct Servo Gear system reduces mechanical backlash and maintenance, ensuring precise movement," it said.

"Automatic coil containment is managed by lateral conic rolls and pressure arms. SYNCRONAMICS software synchronises the decoiler and straightener servo motors for precise strip tension control. The precision multi-roll straightener includes a backlit cabin with integrated HMI, Automatic Straightening Adjustment (ASA) and an automatic roll pack change device for high flatness performance. Integrated brushing and suction devices ensure strip and roll cleaning. The Siemens Drive System enhances energy efficiency by recovering energy during motor braking. The electronic microfeeder optimises strip end processing to prevent waste," it added.
