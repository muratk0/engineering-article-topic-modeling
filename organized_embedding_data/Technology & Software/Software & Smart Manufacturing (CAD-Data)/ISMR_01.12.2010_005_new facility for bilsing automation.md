# NEW FACILITY FOR BILSING AUTOMATION

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

Bilsing Automation (Attendorn, Germany) recently opened a new manufacturing facility near its existing headquarters in Attendorn industrial park. The new 750 m² (8,073 ft²) plant offers additional space for prototyping, pre-assemblies and inventory for Bilsing customers. Additionally, it brings more capability to the company’s research and development efforts spearheaded by its engineering department.

The company is in the process of adding another building at the same location, which will be used to house new administrative offices and training facilities. With a heritage of over 30 years, Bilsing Automation

GmbH is a leader in the development and manufacturing of modular tooling systems for the pressroom, body shop and plastic moulding applications in the automotive, food, packaging and glass industries. Bilsing Automation also offers 3D digital simulation and on-site training as part of its service offerings.
