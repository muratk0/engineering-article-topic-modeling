# Yaskawa Europe and Alma agree partnership

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Yaskawa Europe, an industrial robotics company, and Alma, a specialist in offline programming (OLP) for welding robots, have announced the signature of a partnership agreement. Within the framework of this agreement, in line with the historical collaboration between the two organisations, Alma's Almacam Weld software becomes a reference OLP solution in Yaskawa Europe's catalogue and will be promoted by the manufacturer to its customers with Alma's support.

“Offline programming, which allows a robot to be graphically programmed from a virtual scene and its movements to be simulated, is more than an alternative to traditional programming by learning. It is an approach that is becoming more and more common among manufacturers and is all the more interesting when the products manufactured are varied, modular or subject to frequent adaptations, and when the batches to produce are small," Alma told ISMR.

"In Europe, dozens of companies of all sizes use Almacam Weld OLP solution to program their Motoman welding robots from Yaskawa. The agreement formalises our longstanding cooperation in the field, with Alma and Yaskawa Europe teams or their local resellers (Spain, Portugal, Netherlands...) already working together to provide their customers with a highperformance robotic solution. The capabilities of the Almacam Weld software, particularly in terms of automation drastically reducing part programming times, were particularly appreciated," it added.

"Thanks to Alma, we will be able to increase the added value of our solutions and provide a turnkey solution to our customers," confirmed Richard Tontsch, Manager Marketing, Yaskawa Europe. "Yaskawa Europe will be able to rely on Alma's presence in different European countries and on its local teams of experts for localisation of the service to the end customers," he added.

Yaskawa Europe and its resellers will receive tailor-made support, with technical and commercial teams trained on Alma's software and accompanied in the deployment of OLP projects. The long-term objective for the manufacturer is to gain autonomy and directly ensure the implementation and support of the software for its customers.

Based in Eschborn near Frankfurt in Germany, Yaskawa Europe GmbH comprises the Drives Motion Controls (automation, drive and control technology), Robotics (industrial robots, systems) and Environmental Energy (environmental engineering) business units.

Founded by researchers at the University of Grenoble in 1979, Alma is a CAD/CAM software publisher for cutting, sheet metal and robotics. Its software solutions and web applications are designed to optimise the potential of machines and robots.
