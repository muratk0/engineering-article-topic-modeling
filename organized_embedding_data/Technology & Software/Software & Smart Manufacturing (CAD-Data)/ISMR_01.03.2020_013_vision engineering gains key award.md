# Vision Engineering gains key award

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

VISION ENGINEERING has been recognised as the 'Optical Instrumentation Company of the Year' by Business and Industry Today magazine for ‘its ongoing commitment to providing cutting-edge and ergonomically optimised inspection equipment'.

Vision Engineering, a leading manufacturer of visual inspection and measurement technology, has established a reputation for developing and manufacturing 'revolutionary' optical equipment. Last year, its best-selling product, the Mantis microscope, saw record success with 20,000 sales over five years. In 2019, Vision Engineering released its flagship product, the DRV-Z1 (Deep Reality Viewer).

"The DRV-Z1 uses a world-first technology which produces high-resolution, three-dimensional stereo images without the need for a monitor, headset or glasses. Essentially, images 'float' in front of a viewing mirror for the user," the company told ISMR. As a result, Vision Engineering won the 2020 Inspect magazine award in the 'Vision' category for its Deep Reality Viewer technology. It was also awarded the Palmares Technologique award for Innovation by French publication, Mesures, and named 'Best Stereo Microscope & Measuring Systems Manufacturer 2019' by Global Health and Pharma Magazine in March 2020, as well as being awarded 'Optical Instrumentation Company of the Year' by Industrial Process News.

Vision Engineering Ltd. is a global manufacturer of patented ergonomic stereo microscopes, digital microscopes and non-contact measuring systems. Twenty-five years ago, it introduced the world's first 'eyepiece-less' stereo microscope, the Mantis. Its headquarters are based in Send, Woking, UK, with manufacturing facilities in the UK and U.S. Regional offices are located throughout North America, Europe and Asia.
