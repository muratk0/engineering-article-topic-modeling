# Sheet storage solutions

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

Remmert BASIC Tower sheet

AT THIS YEAR’S EuroBLECH trade fair, logistics expert Remmert will focus on low storage volumes on its stand D42 in Hall 12.

“In this sector in particular, the demand for storage systems that facilitate effective handling and smooth production is rising. With our BASIC towers for sheet metal and long goods, the systems are compact, fast and efficient. They can also adapt flexibly to individual customer requirements,” said Remmert.

The automatic stores are available with a variable number of storage levels. Users can stockpile up to 120 tonnes of material over an area of 25m2 depending upon the construction. The storage towers are therefore suitable for integration into the direct product environment.

The BASIC Tower for long goods is also designed in such a way that flat goods, such as pallets and blanks, can be stored alongside long goods. Specially developed drive and lifting technology reduces access times to the materials. Improved handling also increases the productive time of the indirectly-connected machinery. The towers can be operated via an intuitive touch terminal. See www.remmert.de ■
