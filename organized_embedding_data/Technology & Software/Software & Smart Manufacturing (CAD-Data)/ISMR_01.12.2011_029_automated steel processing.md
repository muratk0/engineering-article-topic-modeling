# AutomAtEd StEEl ProCESSIng

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

PARKER STEEL has made a massive investment in a completely automated production line from FICEP, with a value of 6 million Euros, to deliver a ‘direct from dock’ structural steel processing system.

The company has invested in a fully automated production system from FICEP, combining a full software management solution with completely automatic and integrated drilling and sawing, drilling and coping, shotblasting and painting line. This production facility will automatically transfer material direct from the dock side through each of the desired FICEP CNC processes. The material can be sent for a combination of shotblasting and painting, cutting to length,

drilling, countersinking, tapping, milling or scribing, notching or coping and then be loaded onto transport in a pre-planned delivery sequence with minimum numbers of personnel.

The system that FICEP has designed will have the capacity to produce up to 100,000 tons of steel per year from the Shoreham site and is capable of operating 24 hours per day, 7 days per week.
