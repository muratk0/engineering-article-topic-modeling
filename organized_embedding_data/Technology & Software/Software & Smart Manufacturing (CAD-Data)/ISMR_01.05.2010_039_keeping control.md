# KEEPING CONTROL

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

At MACH 2010 (Hall 5, Stand 5470), FANUC CNC UK will exhibit the latest control products to obtain high productivity solutions. This will include new functions for CNCs, as well as service and support products. Also, FANUC Robotics (UK) will showcase a variety of automation concepts displaying integration of intelligent robots with machine tools.

FANUC CNC will present many new features of its Series 30i/31i/32i CNCs for higher productivity. These CNCs are especially suited for complex machines. Visitors will discover enhancements such as the expansion of 3D Interference Check, which safeguards collision-risk areas.

New features for the CNC Series 0i - Model D will also be shown. These CNCs offer nano-interpolation, a function which satisfies demanding finish requirements at high feed rates and speeds. Dual Check Safety is a valuable new feature on the Series Oi, which uses the CNC's multiple processors to cross-check data to monitor safety critical parts of the machine; this enables compliance of the machine with international safety standards without the need for additional safety units.

New FANUC robot solutions can also be seen operating on the FANUC stand, offering increased productivity in manufacturing through intelligent integration with machine tools.
