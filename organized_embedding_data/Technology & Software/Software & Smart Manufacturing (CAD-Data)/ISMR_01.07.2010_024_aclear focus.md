# ACLEAR FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

Manufacturers in the metal fabrication industry face many challenges. They must maintain flexibility to change capacity requirements while operating continuous replenishment systems. They must determine a productive way to collaborate with customers on designs and manage resources globally to fulfill design elements. To align their own production plans with customer demand, metal fabricators must integrate seamlessly into the customer’s own processes. As a result, integrated systems are essential, bringing design, product lifecycle management and service together in ways that predict, act and rapidly change in accordance with the customer’s needs.

ERP is an integrated computer-based system used to manage internal and external resources including tangible assets, financial resources, materials and human resources. It is a software architecture whose purpose is to facilitate the flow of information between all business functions inside the boundaries of the organization and manage the connections to outside stakeholders. Built on a centralised database and normally utilising a common computing platform, ERP systems consolidate all business operations into a uniform and enterprise-wide system environment.

## ERP BENEFITS

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) systems, that control a multitude of functions, integrate data and processes to give you better visibility into customer needs, production operations and extended supply chains. They enable you to respond rapidly to changing customer product mixes and shorter lead times. They can also save you time and money in the sourcing process, enabling you to purchase raw materials when you need them at the best time possible, then help you make use of factory equipment and inventory as efficiently as possible.

123insight provides an end-to-end solution that is suitable for most manufacturing environments. It has options for CRM, e-commerce and integration/interfaces into all major accounting systems. PB Design halved its lead times and grew 30% using 123insight.

Faster, more accurate flow of information helps your design and engineering teams to develop new and innovative products. Built-in industry functionality supports a wide range of fabrication and machining processes right out of the box. You can also carry only the raw materials and work-in-process you need, with cost savings that pass directly to your bottom line. Automated lot control assigns and tracks orders with specific materials, including lot-based expiration dates to help you avoid unusable or obsolete inventory. Multi-dimensional inventory management maximizes the use of available materials.

Automated financial monitoring of preparation, machining, fabricating and finishing processes, including the availability and disposal of raw materials, production chemicals and tailings, enables better quality through more efficient use of materials. In addition, the documentation required for regulatory compliance is greatly simplified.

To ensure your customers receive the highest quality products, your company must expertly manage orders from quoting through production and shipping. You need to accurately track materials and inventory, communicate effectively with suppliers, schedule for increased profitability, and deliver products on-time to customer specifications. Your operations demand precision and attention to detail. ERP software can help maintain this precision and improve front office, back office, manufacturing and information management needs.

## CHOOSING AN ERP SYSTEM

When choosing to purchase an ERP system, there are multiple solutions and choosing the right solution for your company will require a lot of research. Implementing ERP software will make a large impact on your operations - they have been proven to benefit a company’s profitability, customer relations and speed of production.

Software that is agile and allows you to customize the infrastructure to your needs is a major benefit. Choosing a custom tailored system, that is easily upgradeable, enables you to continue to grow with your organization. ERP software will ideally coexist with and enhance other systems such as Six Sigma, Green Sigma, Lean Manufacturing etc. In its White Paper on Selecting an ERP System, Infor sets out several important steps to help manufacturers choose the right system for their requirements.
