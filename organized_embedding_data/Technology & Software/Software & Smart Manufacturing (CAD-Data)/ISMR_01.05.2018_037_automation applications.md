# Automation applications

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

RARUK AUTOMATION APPEARED at the MACH exhibition recently for the first time with a wide choice of automation products to show visitors. New additions at the show included Toshiba Machine industrial robots. As the recently appointed sole UK distributor of TM Robotics' SCARA and Cartesian robots, RARUK Automation is now able to provide solutions using these caged systems as well as its 6-axis, collaborative Universal Robots (UR).

At MACH, one of the TM Robotics' SCARA systems was demonstrated with FlexiBowl, another new product from RARUK Automation for feeding a wide variety of parts.

"Regardless of whether parts are smooth, sticky, tangled or burred, FlexiBowl will feed the parts uniformly, effectively and, if required, continuously," RARUK Automation told ISMR.

RARUK Automation is a preferred Universal Robots supplier in the UK and its cobots featured heavily on its stand.

"The UR systems give companies access to all the benefits of advanced robotic automation with none of the extra costs associated with traditional robot programming and set-up. They make robotic automation affordable for SMEs and small batch production runs," added the company. "Universal Robots are designed to operate alongside employees, assisting with work that may be too dangerous, strenuous or tedious for them to accomplish on their own.

Three Universal Robots were demonstrated on the RARUK Automation stand, performing a variety of applications and all complete with Robotiq grippers, vision system and force torque sensors.

Only recently introduced to the RARUK Automation range, the MiR200 customisable mobile robot (for transport tasks) transports a load of up to 200kg and can be customised with top modules such as bins, racks, lifts, conveyors or even a Universal Robot.
