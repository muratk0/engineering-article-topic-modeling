# Portable 3D scanner for large parts

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Creaform, a business unit of AMETEK, Inc. and a specialist in automated and portable 3D measurement solutions, has announced the latest addition to its flagship HandySCAN 3D line-up, the MAX Series.

The handheld MAX Series line of industrial 3D scanners, designed and manufactured in Canada, offers a 3D scanning area of approximately 1m x 1m and was developed to acquire 3D measurements of any large and complex surfaces. Featuring multiple scanning mode that allows users to choose between the fastest scanning time or maximum resolution, the new portable 3D scanners enable professionals to measure any large parts and assemblies (up to 15m) in sectors such as aerospace, transportation, energy, mining and heavy industries amongst others.

"With volumetric accuracy of 0.100mm + 0.015mm/m, ISO 17025 accreditation and compliance with the VDI/VDE 2634 Part 3 standard, the MAX series provides metrology-grade results making it ideal for quality control, a precision link between parts with tight tolerances and demanding reverse engineering of large parts. The new scanners still feature our powerful dynamic referencing algorithms for accurate and rigorous 3D scans of any object whether in the lab, on the shop floor or in the field," explained Creaform.

“The system is plug-and-play and quick to set up. The 3D scans can be easily and seamlessly integrated into any CAD software, speeding up and simplifying product development, 3D inspection and reverse engineering processes of large parts and assemblies. The MAX Series has all the attributes of the HandySCAN 3D line-up: true portability; blue laser technology; integrated photogrammetry; live meshing and more," it continued.

New key features, designed to simplify the scanning process, include Flex Volume (users can scan at a short standoff distance and measure large parts at very high speed from a longer standoff distance). Another new feature is the Smart surface algorithm for sophisticated image processing, combined with artificial intelligence, to optimise surface measurements for tracking, better performance, better readings of difficult, contrasted finishes and a simpler scanning process. Real-time calibration enables the MAX Series to integrate the calibration step directly into the scanning workflow, performing it automatically and seamlessly.

Creaform develops, manufactures and markets 3D automated and portable measurement technologies that provide solutions for applications such as 3D scanning; reverse engineering; quality control; non-destructive testing; product development and MRO. Headquartered in Lévis, Québec, Creaform is present in more than 85 countries through its 15 local offices and a network of over 125 distributors.■
