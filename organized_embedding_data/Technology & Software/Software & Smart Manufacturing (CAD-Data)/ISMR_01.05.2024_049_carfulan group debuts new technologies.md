# Carfulan Group debuts new technologies

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Carfulan Group, which has seen sales soar to £20m this year, showcased more than £1m of new technology at MACH 2024 (15-19 April 2024) including the unveiling of multi-sensor measurement solutions.

OGP UK, the Carfulan Group's multi-sensor measurement system specialist, launched three new multi-sensor measurement systems: the Fusion 400, the Shapegrabber Ai620 and the E-Series E7, for precise industrial quality control measurements.

It was joined on Stand 320 by ZOLLER UK, adding to its tool presetting range by taking the wraps off a new advanced heat-shrinking solution: the powerShrink. This innovation is designed to provide high-level rotational accuracy, long tool life and reduced tooling costs, preparing tools for production in under a minute.

The Carfulan Group's 3D printing company, SYS Systems, showcased a range of its 3D printing technologies, including the Stratasys F900 3D printer.

"Alongside four new machines, our new dedicated software company, Prolink UK, also made its debut at MACH. It demonstrated how real-time data monitoring, automated reporting and shopfloor integration can empower manufacturers to increase productivity," confirmed the Carlufan Group.

VICIVISION UK, which produces noncontact measurement systems, featured the Techno M614 designed for precision inspection of complex components as well as the PRIMA 306, an optical measurement system for turned parts. It also unveiled its shopfloor measurement devices: the Metrios Cross and Metrios Cross HD. These machines are built for rapid inspections directly on the shopfloor.
