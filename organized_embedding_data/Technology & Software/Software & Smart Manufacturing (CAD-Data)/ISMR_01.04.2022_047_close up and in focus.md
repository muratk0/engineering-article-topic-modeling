# Close up and in focus

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Vision Engineering showcased its new digital microscopes recently at the Southern Manufacturing exhibition in the UK. Its new Makrolite 4K ultra high-definition model for 4K video inspection and VE CAM (compact digital inspection system) were highlighted on its booth at the show.

Amongst other innovations on show were new digital inspection systems; eyepiece-less microscopes (including its flagship Mantis) and the Lynx EVO (winner of the Queen's Award for Innovation); the DRV-71 3D digital microscope and a selection of contact and non-contact metrology systems.

The Makrolite 4K digital microscope is designed to simplify optical inspection, image capture and measurement tasks with ultrahigh-definition image resolution and a 20:1 optical zoom. It provides live, sharp, high-definition video images in Ultra HD and up to 330x magnification for real-time viewing or data sharing. Users can stream or record live 4K video via computer to share information or as a teaching/training tool.

Manual or autofocus and four user-programmable pre-sets are designed for quick and efficient operation and enable consistency between operators and across multiple systems. It can either be a standalone system operated using a console or used as a PC system with software camera control and annotation/measurement capability. For precision engineering, it finds application in a wide range of industries including automotive, aviation and micromachining and is used for quality control, surface finishing, contamination control and deburring.

“Makrolite 4K's wide dynamic range is ideally suited for inspection of subjects that are highly reflective, such as electro-polished finishes and solder joints, and subjects where details are often lost in areas of shadow, highlights or poor contrast," said the manufacturer.

Vision Engineering's VE CAM is a compact full HD digital microscope system, with custom-made optics and digital features, for visual inspection or manufacturing quality control. It has wireless connections to smart devices and displays with screen mirroring. User pre-sets can be changed to allow different operators to work efficiently. Ten user programmable pre-sets enable faster switch-over times between inspections of different samples and product lines. Six hotkeys give instant access to the most commonly used pre-sets.

“There is no need for a PC, keyboard or mouse and, if you are exceptionally tight on space, you can simply mount a screen onto the unit. With a compact mini monitor, set-up has a footprint of just 25cm x 34cm," said Vision Engineering.

VE CAM is suitable for applications from goods-in quality inspection to shop floor inspection of parts.
