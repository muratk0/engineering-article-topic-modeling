# Universal 3D printing software additions

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

German/French software manufacturer, CoreTechnologie (CT), presented the new version 1.5 of its 4D_Additive software at the Formnext trade fair in Frankfurt, Germany (7-10 November 2023). The universal 3D printing tool for additive manufacturing offers new functionality and improvements as well as new direct connections to 3D printers based on standard file formats.

"Optimised functions and new direct interfaces make this universal 3D printing software for all 3D printers. At the trade fair, we presented a new product based on the nesting functions of 4D_Additive, which allows the integration of fully automated nesting functions in MES systems in batch mode through a web service. With the system's script language, the automated nesting processes can be individually adapted to customer requirements," said CT.

With the further developed 3D nesting module, large packing densities are generated and heat distribution without heat pockets is possible.

“The revised Lattice functions create complex lightweight components or individual utility objects directly from CAD models. Just select the desired surface areas of the CAD model and define the edge thickness, desired structure and cell size. The software enables the creation of complex models with just a few clicks. A total of 22 different lattice structures are available," added the company.

With easy-to-use texture capabilities to create surface designs and connectivity to Adobe Substance Community Assets, thousands of textures are now available for free download to design 3D-printed parts.

The enhanced slicing module of the new 4D_Additive software version 1.5 has a build manager for SLS and SLM printers and specific image generation processes for inkjet printers. For SLS and SLM machines, the laser paths and the laser parameters are freely defined and can be stored in the new material database. The slices for inkjet printers are defined by hollowing out the geometry with any wall thickness with different densities for the edge and fill areas. The slices are output as vector graphics or as a bitmap with full HD resolution.

Support structures for metal printing, DLP and SLA printing processes are further highlights.

## A firm grip

“Metalworkers are faced with several challenges: high variance of workpieces with small batch sizes, cost pressures and a lack of personnel due to strenuous and monotonous activities in sheet-metal processing. With our ivOS Sheet Metal Solution Kit, companies can automate machine loading simply, flexibly and economically," Schmalz told ISMR.

The vacuum specialists from Germany's Black Forest automate the handling of sheet metal parts. Its Solution Kit consists of the latest 3D camera technology, the ivOS vision operating system and the flexible FMG matrix gripper. This gripper features seven modules, each with twelve suction cups which are linked to form a flexible area gripper. The 84 individually controllable suction points adapt automatically to changing workpiece geometries. This offers users flexibility when handling different sheets and gripper changes can be reduced to a minimum.

"Sheet-metal processors and contract manufacturers want to produce efficiently to be profitable and ensure their competitiveness. The challenge: high part variance with fluctuating batch sizes. Labour shortages and rising wage costs exacerbate the situation," highlighted Schmalz, outlining the versatility of its solution kits.

Schmalz products are used in logistics applications as well as in the automotive industry, the electronics sector and furniture production. Schmalz is represented with its own locations and trading partners in around 70 countries. The family-owned company employs around 1,800 people at its German headquarters (Glatten, Black Forest) and in 29 other companies worldwide. In September, the Schmalz Group established its 30th location in Slovenia. The sales company, Schmalz d.o.o., is based in Vrhnika and focuses on south-eastern Europe.
