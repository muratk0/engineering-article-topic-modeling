# Quality control with cobots

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Creaform, a business unit of AMETEK, Inc. and a specialist in portable 3D measurement solutions and engineering services, has released its new VXscan-R software module for the VXelements 10 platform. This latest version now offers advanced compatibility with a wider range of robots, including collaborative ones, and is designed to increase quality control productivity.

The VXscan-R software is an integral part of the R-SeriesTM. It is an automated 3D scanning solution for at-line applications, turnkey solutions or customisable layouts.

“VXscan-R offers a reliable and accurate digital twin environment for program preparation, scan simulations and execution. Used with the MetraSCAN 3D-RTM, a robot-mounted optical CMM scanner, the R-Series is designed for companies that want to find part defects earlier and ensure that all parts are measured correctly," Creaform told ISMR.

“The new version of VXscan-R is now compatible with collaborative robots (Fanuc CRX), as well as a variety of new industrial robots, which enables it to adapt to any workflow, from large production lines to small and medium enterprises. With its lightweight and compact design, collaborative robots in the R-Series can be deployed easily and rapidly regardless of the users' levels of expertise or experience," it added.

The MetraSCAN 3D-R offers high-performance optics, blue laser technology and 360° target coverage for quality control tasks. The R-Series solutions are integrated in VXscan-R software and are designed to “seamlessly integrate into any automated quality control processes."

The collaborative robot is expected to work with and around workers. Safety features, such as automatically stopping when meeting resistance, are therefore paramount to maintaining a safe working environment.

“By adding the collaborative robots to the R-Series offer, and by supporting more industrial robots, we can guarantee that this scanning technology (powered by offline programming software) is accessible to businesses of all sizes," explained Jérôme-Alexandre Lavoie, Product Manager, Creaform. “Harnessing the power of automation has never been easier."

Creaform celebrates its 20th anniversary this year.
