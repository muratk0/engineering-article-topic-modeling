Electronic surface thermometer reads temps without constant calibration

LA-CO Industries Inc. has introduced the Tempil Estik

®

electronic surface thermometer for temperature-monitoring applications. It eliminates the need to carry multiple temperature indicators.

The unit directly touches the surface of the materials being inspected. The digital readout instantly displays surface temperatures with an accuracy of ±2 percent. Measuring surface temperature from 32 to 999 degrees F, the thermometer automatically reads all standard preheating, interpass, and cool-down temperatures and can be used on polished or nonpolished surfaces.

The unit is not subject to variables like calibration or distance from the surface, and it is not necessary to know what the emissivity of the surface material is to obtain an accurate reading.

Maintenance-free casters keep grease in, contaminants out

Hamilton Caster has introduced the Spinfinity. line of heavy-duty, maintenance-free casters. The casters’ swivel, kingpinless construction features a completely enclosed, CNC-machined, large internal seal made from nitrile butadiene rubber (NBR) inside the swivel assembly. With no outside exposure, the swivel assembly is well-suited to keep grease in and contaminants out.

The casters are made with extra-thick forgings. The top plate and inner raceway are one forged steel piece, not a weldment; the raceways are CNCmachined and then hardened to a uniform depth.

The casters are offered in three series. The ZFWH series, which features 2-in.-wide wheels and a 4-in. by 5-in. mounting plate, is rated up to 2,500 lbs. per caster. The ZFHS series also has 2-in.-wide wheels, but with a 41.2.in. by 61.4-in. mounting plate, and it carries up to 3,000 lbs. per caster. The ZFFM series has 3-in.-wide wheels with the same mounting plate as the ZFHS. It is rated up to 3,200 lbs. per caster. All are finished in red crimson and have double-sealed, precision ball bearings.

Portable, hand-held scribe marking unit is industry first

The Borries model 317 is the first portable, hand-held marker capable of scribing on sheet metal and other hard surfaces, reports the manufacturer. The unit produces continuous-line markings, operating at 74 dBA or less.

Designed for low-volume production, backup applications, or repair in highvolume production environments, the unit meets international automotive industry standards for marking depth and font norm. It is suitable for online or offline repair VIN marking, with errorproofing features such as format check on VINs depending on vehicle type and safeguards against double-marking the same VIN.

Weighing 12 lbs., the marker can be taken directly to the workpiece. It comes with a selection of interchangeable fixtures for stability when marking large parts, such as structural steel components.

The high-strength, low-wear scribe stylus is made of synthetic diamond or carbide. The marker operates as a stand-alone unit or via PC or notebook software in a variety of languages. Its marking controller features an EK-Box with integrated text display and foil keyboard, protection IP53 interfaces for an external keyboard, a bar code reader, Ethernet, and optional onboard field bus communication.

Structural acrylic adhesives simplify production of large assemblies

3M™ offers Scotch-Weld™ acrylic adhesive DP8425NS and low-odor acrylic adhesive DP8825NS. The products reach handling strength in 45 min. and structural strength (1,000 PSI) in 50 min.

According to the manufacturer, the adhesives help simplify the production of large assemblies because workers do not have to switch out mixing nozzles or clean up cured adhesive.

In addition to expanding process work time up to 25 min., the adhesives offer an 18-month shelf life and a high impact of resistance, the company states. They are available in low-odor and nonflammable formulas.

Intuitive analysis software offers weld seam inspection

Vitronic will introduce a new release of its VIRO

wsi

weld seam inspection system at FABTECH. The analysis software has a new design to help ensure intuitiveness and usability.

At the heart of the optical system is a user interface for setting the parameters of machine vision components and for configuring new seams, as well as image evaluation, logging, and data archiving.

The configuration of new seams requires the creation of inspection programs, or recipes, that define parameters such as inspection sequence, inspection limits, and inspection criteria in accordance with production requirements. If changes are needed to an existing inspection process, they can be made while an inspection is in progress. Once confirmed by the user, they are applied to the inspection of the next part.

The graphic design of the user interface and the system’s intuitive navigation enable simplified teach-in of inspection sequences and inspection limits. Users can select from a variety of configuration functions. They can access predefined templates of standard seam types or have scan frequency and ranges calculated automatically.

Inspection results are presented in a clear, structured format in different views. Defects such as pores and holes are displayed in high-precision 3-D. The software classifies the defects and shows detailed information about the position and type of defect in each case. Inspection results for individual seams are shown in real time on a photo of the component.

All information is immediately calculated into a view of real-time statistics. In parallel, it is stored in an integrated database, where it is linked with component and seam IDs for subsequent tracking, evaluation, and statistics. With the help of the database-supported evaluation tool and the real-time statistics function, the user can detect process deviations early on.

Three-spindle drilling line compatible with all types of tooling

The ball screw feed, 5,000-RPM Vanguard drill lines from Ficep are compatible with all types of tooling.

The direct-drive spindle prevents power loss at the tool. The ball screw feed permits tapping, countersinking, and counterboring. Other features include rack-and-pinion positioning on all axes; scribing (directly downloaded from DSTV files) available on up to four sides; six-position automatic tool changers available for each spindle; and capability for tandem placement with one of the company’s band saws or plasma robots.
