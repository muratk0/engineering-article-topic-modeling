# Bringing autonomous operations to life

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Rockwell Automation, Inc. has announced that it is integrating NVIDIA Omniverse application programming interfaces into its Emulate3D™ digital twin software. The aim is to enhance factory operations through artificial intelligence and physics-based simulation technology.

Digital twins enhance equipment development and control testing through simulation models and emulation, reducing start-up time and risk. As equipment is connected into lines, models scale and challenges arise from siloed expertise and integration issues between separately engineered components.

"A system-level perspective, including interoperability across machines, can solve these issues but requires collaboration for system-level testing. As lines scale, larger digital twins require more computational power, risking bottlenecks. Automation leaders need scalable solutions to achieve full factory-scale models, building on digital twin successes," explained the automation specialist.

## Real-time collaboration

Rockwell Automation's Emulate3D software uses the latest NVIDIA Omniverse APIs to create factory-scale, dynamic digital twins based on OpenUSD interoperability and NVIDIA RTX rendering technologies.

“While visualisation was previously possible, this enhancement enables true emulation and dynamic testing of multiple machines within a system. This integration, planned for early 2025, will enable improved visualisation and simulation capabilities for manufacturing environments," the company told ISMR.

"Our integration of Emulate3D with NVIDIA Omniverse marks a significant leap forward in bringing autonomous operations to life," added Blake Moret, Chairman and CEO of Rockwell Automation. "By combining our deep industrial expertise with NVIDIA's cutting-edge technology, we're helping our customers to achieve new levels of efficiency, innovation and collaboration in their manufacturing processes."

By using NVIDIA Omniverse, Emulate3D will allow multiple dynamic digital twins to be combined and visualised as a complete factory through a web app. This vendor-agnostic, scalable approach addresses the growing need for factory-scale digital twins created by engineers collaborating in real-time across various teams.

## Counting the benefits

“AI-enabled digital twins are driving the next wave of digital manufacturing and automation," said Rev Lebaredian, Vice President of Omniverse and Simulation Technology, NVIDIA. "Rockwell Automation's Emulate3D software allows manufacturers to collaboratively visualise, simulate and optimise their operations in real time with NVIDIA Omniverse."

Benefits include:

■ Hyperscale capabilities through Emulate3D's multi-model technology.

■ Cloud-based deployment options for flexibility.

■ Vendor-agnostic connectivity to a wide range of 3D applications.

■ A unified web app for stakeholder visualisation.

“Emulate3D™ digital twin software helps users to pre-emptively identify potential control issues, saving valuable time and resources during implementation. Plant personnel receive additional support by having a virtual space to train on new systems, predict future performance and simulate line changes without real-world consequences. Emulate3D digital twin software provides a comprehensive and dynamic approach to enhancing efficiency and precision in industrial operations," concluded Rockwell Automation.

NVIDIA Omniverse lets developers integrate various factory layers into a comprehensive model, combining architectural software with industrial digital twins. This enables greater coordination across industrial design and operation. Built for scalability, Omniverse's Universal Scene Description (OpenUSD) foundations and cloud deployments grow alongside projects.■
