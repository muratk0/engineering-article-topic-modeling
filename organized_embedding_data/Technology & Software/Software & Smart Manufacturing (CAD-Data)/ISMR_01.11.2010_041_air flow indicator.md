# AIR FLOW INDICATOR

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

Flextraction Ltd, supplier and manufacturer of Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) products, has launched a new, small Electronic Air Flow Indicator for use on all LEV hoods or at any LEV point on the extraction hose to ensure that the airflow is adequate to control operator exposure to industrial dust and fumes, including hazardous and corrosive fumes at the point of source.

These electronic airflow indicators are designed for use in a multitude of applications including surface grinding, welding, product tipping and drum filling as well as food processing where fumes are emitted. Not only do they visually show the operator if the airflow is correct by a green LED or if it is incorrect by a red LED, but an audible alarm gives the operator an extra indication if there is a fall in the air flow velocity.

Features include an indication of system performance from a single LED, constant monitoring allows the operator to be protected at all times, no external buttons or switches and simple calibration/setup using internal switches can be carried out by Flextraction engineers. It is powered by 4 x AA batteries which give up to two years’ continuous operation.
