# ifm and Schuler cooperate on forming

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

To monitor and improve quality in press shops through digitalisation, ifm and the Schuler Group have now concluded a cooperation agreement. Their aim is to offer customers more transparency and increase efficiency in sheet metal forming.

Sheet metal forming presses are among the most important machine tools in sheet metal processing. The Schuler Group has 180 years of experience of forming technology. Using digital solutions such as Track & Trace, Visual Die Protection or DigiSim®, the company offers numerous tools that support digitalisation in press shops. Now, ifm SmartStamp expands this offering with a software tool that detects tilting, eccentricity and the resulting tilting moment on forming presses, reportedly within milliseconds.

"If the ram is tilted too far, the press guides are subjected to excessive strain and will be damaged in the long term," said Christoph Schneider, Vice President Product Management Applications, ifm.

The tool protects the press from excessive wear and damage as well as helping to improve availability. SmartStamp is seamlessly integrated into the moneo IloT platform and now also into the Metris platform from ANDRITZ, Schuler's parent company.

"Thanks to combination with the data from our cloud-based Digital Solutions, customers benefit from interoperability and consequently even higher transparency in the press shop," emphasised Rohitashwa Pant, Chief Digital Officer, Schuler Group.

The signed cooperation agreement provides for collaboration in sales. Customers will be able to use ifm's SmartStamp and Schuler's Digital Solutions. It is immaterial, said Schuler, which system customers use when they start to digitalise as they can expand their digitalisation solution with the other system, at any time.

"In this way, we can offer all of our customers the optimum solution for press shop digitalisation," concluded Florian Siebert, Senior Manager Systems and IloT, ifm.
