Automated measurement workcell delivers four pillars of automation in one system

Hexagon Mfg. Intelligence has introduced the 360° Flexible Measurement Cell. According to the company, this is the first automated measurement workcell to deliver the four pillars of automation—flexibility, footprint, throughput, and performance—seamlessly in one system.

The workcell is suitable for automotive and aero structure applications, including powertrain and closure panel fabrication. It is built on a commercial, off-the-shelf workcell system integrated with the company’s measurement systems. Intelligence measurement systems rapidly return operational data to fabricators to help make the manufacturing process data-driven.

Offered in three configurations for inline, near-line, or offline applications, it can be adapted to any production space.

Waterjet system cuts 3-D parts, bevels to 50 degrees

Jet Edge’s EDGE X-5

®

The film serves as an alternative to oils and desiccants used to protect packaged metal parts, eliminating the need to remove greasy coatings. Metal parts protected in the film are ready to use immediately after unpacking. The nontoxic film is recyclable.

According to the manufacturer, parts packaged in the film or bags can be protected from rust, tarnish, stains, white rust, and oxidation for up to five years, depending on film construction thickness and application.

Metal parts packaged in the film are continuously protected from the corrosive effects of salt, excessive humidity, condensation, moisture, aggressive industrial atmospheres, and dissimilar metal corrosion. Vapor-phase corrosion inhibitors (VpCIs) in the film vaporize and condense on metal surfaces in the enclosed package, reaching every area of the part to protect exterior as well as hard-to-reach interior surfaces.

The heat-sealable film can protect such metals as aluminum, galvanized steel, carbon steel, silicon steel, stainless steel, silver, copper, solder, and brass.

Transfer press simulation software allows troubleshooting of stamping runs

3-D Solutions Design Service LLC offers T-Sim transfer press simulation software. With the software, stamping manufacturers, die builders, and designers can troubleshoot and perfect their transfer die stamping runs before going to production.

The software uses kinematics to check for crashes and close calls between the dies, transfer system, and press. The entire die is put into motion, including all pads, lifters, and cams, so full functionality can be verified. A corrective action report (CAR) is generated to communicate where changes are needed.

For new tooling, upper and lower interference curves are generated for export to the design source, and full-motion videos are included to communicate any issues found in simulation.
