# Scanning complex surface structures

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

THE STEREOSCAN NEO white light scanner, from AICON 3D Systems GmbH (now part of Hexagon), is designed for the rapid and precise capturing of complex surface structures.

"The StereoScan neo is a consistent further development of the classic StereoScan system series. Previously, the fringe projection was highly dependent upon the surface properties of the scanning object. The colour and reflection properties of the surface significantly affected the quality of the measurement results. This is where the digital adaptive full colour projection technique of the StereoScan neo provides the necessary rectification by using colour and intensity control to optimally adjust to surface properties," AICON told ISMR.

"For the first time, it is now possible to visualise the results of a measurement directly on the object. The adaptive full colour projection technique enables not only projection of the colour patterns, which are necessary for the scanning process onto the object, but also of the measurement results. This allows for the deviations from the CAD to be visualised in colour immediately following the measurement on the object surface."

This procedure, added the manufacturer, provides the user with new possibilities during workflow. In the tooling and moulding industry or in the context of model-making, deviations of the component surface can be measured and visualised quickly and precisely using the SWYM technology, explained AICON. Based on the projected deviation images, corrections on the object can be made promptly. Deviations in the context of inspection tasks can also be made visible and rectified immediately.
