# The final cut

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

THE SGX PLASMA and oxy-fuel automated cutting machine from ESAB is designed to be compact, rugged and easy to use. Its compact size optimises valuable floor space for standard definition or high precision plasma, as well as CNC oxy-fuel cutting. One-metre rail increments maximise production space, while a low rail design allows loading and unloading of material by fork truck – a benefit for shops with no overhead crane.

“Designed for performance, speed and accuracy, the SGX is also easy to operate. Our intuitive Vision® T5 controller and Operating Wizard simplify set-up and make it easy for operators to quickly advance to automated operation using the standard, high performance features of ESAB cutting automation systems,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

“The SGX is ideal for a range of applications – for small fab shops or manufacturers, steel service centres and general fabrication – any fabrication operation that values cutting technology that is simple and easy to use, yet brings a higher level of accuracy and capability to operations in a compact, automated system,” explained Doug Shuda, Director of Marketing - Global Cutting Technologies for ESAB.

The SGX makes a number of automated functions available. Built-in EasyNest™ automatic true-shape nesting ensures high plate utilisation, while SmartCycle™ Technology automatically optimises cut-to-cut cycle times on nested parts. Enhanced Hole Technology™ produces high quality cut holes down to a 1:1 diameter-to-thickness ratio on mild steel of up to 25mm (1 in.) thick, and the Smart Voltage Height Control promotes longer consumable life and more consistent cut quality.

The SGX is offered in cutting widths from 1.5 to 2.5m (4 to 8ft.) and 2.5m (8ft.) to 14.5m (48ft.) cut lengths (enables cutting of two 6m/20ft. long plates), and there are tooling options for one plasma torch and one or two oxy-fuel torches.

The SGX equipped with ESAB’s Precision Plasmarc™ m3 Plasma® technology enables precision plasma cutting for a wide range of material types and thicknesses. The m3 system performs high speed plasma cutting, high precision cutting, plasma marking, thick-plate cutting, dry (multi-gas) cutting and water injection cutting. To produce the best cut quality, the operator simply selects the material type and thickness, and the Vision T5 CNC automatically sets the start, cut and shield gas pressures and flow rates. Options for 200-, 360-, 450- or 600-amp systems are available.

Also available is ESAB’s new Smart Plasmarc™ m2 Plasma technology which is designed to deliver high cutting speeds, consistent cut quality and long consumable life with air and oxygen plasma gas for low operating costs. Optimised cutting parameters are integrated into the system for easy process set-up and operation.
