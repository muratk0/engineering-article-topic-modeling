# Portable 3D metrology

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

The portable T-SCAN hawk 2 from GOM, a next-generation lightweight 3D laser scanner, comes with metrology-grade precision and ease of use.

"Control your working distance with a new projection mode; a red laser marker helps you to easily adjust for perfect scanning results. This solution adapts to workflows as T-SCAN hawk 2 is intuitive to operate and adapts easily to the movement of a hand. It is also the first portable laser scanner with the new satellite mode to scan up to multiple metres. There is no need for classical built-in photogrammetry with coded markers and no compromise on accuracy," GOM told ISMR.

The T-SCAN hawk 2 scanner comes with GOM Inspect all-in-one software for 3D inspection, which is part of the ZEISS Quality Suite. Users can enjoy a free trial of GOM Inspect Pro for 14 days. The scanner also offers seamless adjustments for resolution and field of view for small parts, fine details, larger objects or deep pockets, confined spaces or hard-to-reach areas.

T-SCAN hawk 2 supports scanning on a wide range of materials and surfaces, delivering 3D measurement data with the highest precision. Maintenance applications include 3D inspection of dents, corrosion and damage and 3D scanning and remanufacturing of legacy parts. It can be used indoors and outdoors as well as in rugged and harsh environments," concluded GOM.■
