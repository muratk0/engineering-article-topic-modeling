# GABELLA MACCHINE

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand J81

https://gabella.it/en/

The Italian company from Crevacuore remains faithful to the concept of customising systems for each individual customer (large and small manufacturers). Its Compact Multi-Blanking Line is designed for a user who needs to produce small batches of sheets of different sizes and lengths. The line features a slitter with automatically positioned heads.

"This basic model can be developed according to specific needs, adding precision straighteners, levellers, accessories for loading and exchanging coils etc. These can be applied from the beginning or even after production is well established," explained the manufacturer.

The line on display at EuroBLECH 2024 will be equipped with the latest developed control and MES software which manages the identification, labelling and marking of the cut pieces, along with the different working steps. It is web-based, which means it allows data exchange with PCs, notebooks and tablets in all operating systems and through a simple Internet connection," it concluded.

## Jorns AG

Stand K128

www.jorns.swiss/en/

Krasser GmbH, a manufacturer of sheet metal cutting and coil processing systems, Jorns AG (a manufacturer of bending machines) and Stolarczyk (a coil-processing machine manufacturer) will be exhibiting together at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover on stand K128.

The three machine manufacturers highlight the advantages of exhibiting together as their packaged solution can be offered to sales agencies and customers on the trade fair stand to meet the increasing demand for digitally networked sheet metal processing machines and increasing capacities.

Beside this cooperation, Jorns will also present the latest geometry on the clamping and bending tools of its JDB (Jorns double bending machine). Information will also be available on the latest functions of the automated Jorns Profile Manipulator as well as the latest options and functionality of the new JC100 control system for Jorns JB bending machines and Jorns JDB Double Bending machines. Jorns celebrated its 50-year anniversary in 2023.
