# Two measurement systems in one

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

"SWIFT PRO DUO is the only video measuring system to incorporate an ergonomic measuring microscope," Vision Engineering told /SMR. "Previously difficultto-view samples can now be measured and inspected on the same system, in the same routine, by the same operator. Swift PRO Duo provides rapid and accurate measurement of both routine and complex precision parts."

With a rich suite of measurement and reporting features, it is a measurement tool for a broad range of applications, from electronics and aerospace to automotive, plastics and medical device manufacturing. Video measurement is suitable for routine components where edges of features can easily be identified. The smart video measurement tools are designed to allow the operator to quickly detect and measure features accurately and repeatedly.

"The Dynascope technology allows operators to take accurate measurements whilst wearing prescriptive glasses, safety goggles and contact lenses. Other system benefits include a large field of view and peripheral vision," added the company.

Swift PRO Duo includes Vision Engineering's 6" x 4" (150mm x 100mm) precision measuring stage (8" x 4", 200mm x 100mm option also available). The stage comes complete with factory-set Non-Linear Error Correction (NLEC) calibration, designed to ensure accuracy and traceable to international standards for the purposes of ISO9000.

Swift PRO Duo comes with a choice of M-series metrology software or robust QC 3000 microprocessor.

"Designed especially for toolmakers and shop floor operators, it takes accurate measurements in seconds, with no need for pre-programming. There is a Z-axis variant for height measurement. The system is ready for use on the shop floor and in the QC lab, added Vision Engineering.
