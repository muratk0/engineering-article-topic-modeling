# Interactive VR experiences

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

Group Rhodes, a leading UK designer and manufacturer of metalforming, composite and bespoke machinery, will be showcasing several new innovations at the MACH 2022 exhibition (alongside group companies Rhodes Interform and Atkin Automation). Its stand 616 will feature as part of the MMMA Metalworking Village in Hall 6 at the NEC, Birmingham (UK), from 4-8 April 2022.

Rhodes Interform will be demonstrating its latest machine designs through Virtual Reality (VR), enabling visitors to experience the company's new game engine architecture and take an interactive journey into its latest metalforming and composite innovations. Group Rhodes recently introduced VR throughout its group companies to visualise project technology and assess practical aspects of an installation such as machine access, serviceability and equipment positioning.

Sister company Atkin Automation will be promoting its large portfolio of coil processing machinery, which includes a comprehensive range of servo roll feeds.

All Atkin feed units feature torque shaft upper roll pivots with a Regen grip system and backlash free servo gearbox transmission.

At MACH22, Atkin will be demonstrating its RF70-300 servo rollfeed (70mm dia. rolls x 300mm wide). It offers a manual heightadjustable press mounting bracket, pneumatic pilot/strip release system and electronic cambox. Entry rolls and material side guides come as standard, with options of motorised height adjustment and bespoke press or floor standing brackets. Soft/surface-sensitive materials can also be accommodated with either chrome or Febrofen coated rolls.■
