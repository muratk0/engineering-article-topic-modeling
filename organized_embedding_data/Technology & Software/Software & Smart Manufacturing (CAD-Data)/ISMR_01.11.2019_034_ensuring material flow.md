# ENSURING MATERIAL FLOW

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

Matthias Remmert, joint CEO of Remmert, outlines his view of the future to ISMR for automation systems services for agile material flows.

At Blechexpo 2019 in Stuttgart (Germany), material flow expert Remmert and software manufacturer WiCAM sealed a strategic cooperative agreement to intensify the development of integrated solutions for agile material logistics and fully automatic sorting of finished parts for sheet metal processing production.

The aim is to offer joint customers a standardisation and simplification of interfaces as well as the standardisation of information flow from ERP/PPS via CAD/CAM/Nesting to raw material and logistics of finished parts.

This cooperation is based on customer solutions that have been successfully implemented, such as the Remmert LaserFLEX using WiCAM PN4000 which form a basis for collaborative projects in 2020. Users, said Remmert joint CEO Matthias Remmert, benefit from high process reliability; achieve measurable increases in productivity and have the option to grow their system modularly with increasing hardware and software requirements.

ISMR spoke with Matthias Remmert at Blechexpo in Stuttgart to discuss his view of a connected and integrated future in manufacturing.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes, achievements or awards?

MR: We have put a lot of effort into staff issues; finding the right people and looking after them properly. This includes offering a range of training programmes for our workforce. I am proud to say that we won a 'Top Job' German award in 2017 for staff development (one of 100 companies in Germany to get this award). We believe that our people are important. Everything is a team effort at Remmert. We are nothing without our team.

We have also put a lot of effort into our products. Our smart control system won a design award. We have two main product lines – one is for the storage and handling of long bar material as well as storage, automation and material handling of sheet materials. For our sheet handling product, we have taken into account our customers' visions for the next five years. Many of our customers like to start 'small and smart' and grow with our product. So, we make the material grow, but we also make the material flow.... Starting with different modules, we use these to build up a customised solution to fit our customers' needs. We have made a significant investment into this approach over the last five years.

Right now, we have a toolbox of 25 different modules that we can put together to build up a perfect solution for our customers. We see ourselves as an independent supplier of intralogistics in the sheet material process, with the capability to interface to different machines and processes from third parties.

ISMR: What are you showcasing here at Blechexpo in Stuttgart?

MR: Our slogan is 'We boost your productivity'. By this, we mean that our modules and solutions can make our customers' material flow. This means customised but also standard components. Modularity is essential. We create modules, not processes, that fit into our overall concept and this simplifies things for our customers.

Technically, we offer flexibility and you can see this on the booth here at Blechexpo where we are highlighting technology for sorting small parts. This is something we announced last year at EuroBLECH – we have significantly improved the sorting and part-picking process.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals?

MR: 2017, 2018 and 2019 have been great years for us. However, we see clouds on the horizon for next year – a reduction of around 10-15% of business is expected. 2020 is about 75% blocked now by our forward order book but we expect clouds as the global economy is struggling a bit. However, we are well prepared and have seen similar situations in the past.

We doubled our turnover two years ago and we see about 5-10% growth each year. 2020 will be a slower year but we still see many opportunities. Europe is still our largest market (60%). The U.S. market accounts for 20% of our business and Asia takes 20%.

ISMR: Please explain your strategic and technical objectives?

MR: The main strategic focus for us is to be present in key markets, to be well known as an independent supplier, to be the best intralogistics partner to our customers and to grow our business globally.

The next strategic and technical step for us is Industry 4.0. However, our vision for this is to implement data from other sources and bring it together in a visual monitoring process for customers, in tandem with third parties. We develop our own software.

Our target for 2025 is to be represented globally in about 35 different countries. We sell in different countries with partners, and we also have our own engineering teams (mechanically, PLC, IT and comms) – within that is software development. We have our own fabrication manufacturing and service (we guarantee 24/7 worldwide). Whenever customers call us, they can call our specialists directly – they are not put through to a call centre. We also have a crew on the road providing service around the world.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal handling markets?

MR: Flexibility is key for us and our customers. Moving from large batch production to one-offs, for example, requires flexible systems. We have seen this trend develop over the years. Easy operation for complex systems is also another trend, which also adds flexibility.

Our smart control system, which won a design award, is as simple and as smart to use as an iPhone. Customers need minimal training to operate it (two hours) as it is largely intuitive and self-explanatory – very simple to operate. It can be operated via an app on smartphone or tablet, which also feeds into Industry 4.0 requirements.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

MR: For us, as a relatively small company (approx. 140 people), the challenge is to foresee new trends and keep being adaptable and flexible. We also need to ensure that we keep our focus in the right direction as we cannot cover every market aspect globally. These challenges, however, are also opportunities.
