# ARKU InfoDays 2018

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

ARKU, A SPECIALIST

provider of levelling and deburring technology, held its Levelling + Deburring InfoDays from 11-15 June 2018 at its headquarters at Baden Baden, demonstrating how robotics and levelling systems can harmonise.

This year, the event focused on a new solution for optimising sheet metal working. In live demonstrations, ARKU showed the FlatMaster® 88 200 precision leveller with the FlatJack® flatness testing system, with an upstream pick-and-place robot. This move towards automated production represents a logical step for ARKU: this year, the company celebrates its 90th anniversary.

Utilising modern EasyEye® camera technology, the selflearning robot "recognises" parts with different thicknesses and materials. It lifts panels weighing up to 100kg onto the leveller's supply roller conveyor. The robot is suitable for removing the parts. This also reduces physical demands on the operators. Deburring machines can also be equipped with the robot. The robot handles the loading procedure, reducing the physical demands on the operator who can focus on operating the system.

ARKU presented a new variant of the FlatJack® in combination with the FlatMaster®. The flatness testing system is now also available for levellers with an opening width of 2000mm. The FlatJack® control system has been integrated into the control system of the FlatMaster® 88 200 so that both machines can be controlled via a single operator panel. This eliminates the need for operators to move backwards and forwards between control panels.

In addition to the live demonstrations, ARKU also presented individual machines such as the EdgeBreaker® 2000 and 4000 deburring and rounding machines for thicker panels, the FlatMaster®55 precision leveler and the EcoMaster® part levellers in action. Visitors also had the opportunity to level and deburr their own parts on the machines. See www.arku.com.

Magnets on the gripper arm ensure that parts made of different materials and with different thicknesses are loaded onto the infeed conveyor

The FlatJack® flatness control system measures the levelled parts with precision to a range of tenths of a centimetre
