# Automated CMM inspection

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

WMH Robotics, a provider of flexible cobot automation, has announced a partnership with Aberlink, a large UK-owned manufacturer of CMMs and metrology solutions. By debuting the partnership at the MACH Exhibition 2024 (15-19 April 2024 at the NEC in Birmingham, UK), WMH Robotics and Aberlink will unveil their integrated solution, combining CMM metrology and collaborative robot automation.

"This collaboration is set to create a new precedent for manufacturers looking to transform their quality assurance processes by enabling unmanned CMM inspection. It offers our customers the ability to transform their current metrology applications by providing an integration of quality control with enhanced efficiency. This ensures that businesses stay at the forefront of technological advancements by offering an autonomous, fast, easy and flexible automation solution for their current manufacturing operations," explained Aberlink.

AT MACH 2024, visitors can see WMH Robotics on stand 18-538 and Aberlink on stand 19-729.■
