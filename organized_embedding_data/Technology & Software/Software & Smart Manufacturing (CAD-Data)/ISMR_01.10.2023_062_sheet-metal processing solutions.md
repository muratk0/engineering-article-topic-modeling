# Sheet-metal processing solutions

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Durmazlar (Durma) is a global manufacturer of sheet-metal processing machinery, established in 1956, with headquarters in Turkey. Its wide product portfolio includes laser cutting systems; press brakes; punch presses; bending centres; Smart Line solutions; plasma-cutting machines; guillotine shears; plate rollers and profile bending machines.

The company also offers special sheetmetal processing solutions, autonomous and robotic technology-based smart factory configurations and various Industry 4.0 applications. As a founder member of Turkey's Industry-4.0 platform, Durma designs machines that can communicate with each other through customised cloud technology, designed in-house.

At this year's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023), visitors can meet Durma on its stand 1807 in Hall 1. The manufacturer will be showcasing its range of systems and smart factory projects, including automation technologies, at the show. It will highlight its 4kW and 30kW fibre laser cutting systems; 8m laser tube and systems; robot automation AD-SERVO 30220 press brake; energy-efficient and cobot automation AD-ES 2040 pressbrake and its PB4 Bending Centres.

“The modern robot technologies used in our machines, which we will also showcase at Blechexpo, are successfully contributing to higher quality products and shorter return on investment periods across various industries. We offer cloud computing, artificial intelligence and other advanced technologies that enable communication between the machines that we manufacture. Visitors to our stand at Blechexpo will see how these solutions can be adapted to address sustainability goals; productivity enhancements; supply-chain challenges; workforce shortages and more," outlined Durma.■
