# Tooling webshop

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

WILA has launched an intuitive web-shop for its tools with a comprehensive overview of standard top and bottom tools.

"The webshop can also be used to directly request quotes, check the latest prices, find delivery times and also place orders. It also features relevant product information about tool holders, components and accessories. There will be regular updates on product information and new features will be added to improve the functionality of the webshop,' explained WILA.

Customers have to complete a one-off registration process on the webshop to request a quote or place an order. The 'Authorised Service Partners' list selected by WILA will be displayed during the registration process.

The launch of the webshop is compatible with Smart Industry developments, WILA told ISMR. Its e-commerce model 'ensures that the whole supply chain, from production process to end user, is fully digital and paperfree', according to the company.

See webshop.wila.nl.
