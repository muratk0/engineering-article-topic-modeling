# Laser marking at IMTS

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

FOBA attended the International Manufacturing Technology Show IMTS in Chicago this September with three laser-marking systems on-site and two sample marking giveaways.

The FOBA M-series laser-marking workstation M1000, equipped with a rotary unit, was available for laser-marking demonstrations at the IMTS trade show. Two M-series marking systems, FOBA M1000 and M2000, and FOBA Titus, a laser-marking head for line integration particularly in restricted spaces, were also available for laser-marking live demonstrations. With various functionalities and integrated camera, each laser system can be selected according to the application requirements and the specific metal or plastic substrate characteristics.

Titus™ marking system.

The exhibited M2000 laser-marking workstation was equipped with FOBA Mosaic to demonstrate automated optical mark alignment on parts that are placed randomly in the marking field. Another M-series system at IMTS, a compact tabletop M1000 workstation, was used with a rotary unit for circumferential marking on metal drinking straws.

Anodized coloured aluminium plates were used as marking demonstrators for the Titus™ marking system.

“Titus™ is the smallest marking head in the market and enables high-speed inline processing in manufacturing lines that require much flexibility of integration. Apart from the minimum size of the marking head, it is the simplicity of mounting and a three- or ten-metre umbilical that contribute to the flexibility of Titus™," explained FOBA.

FOBA develops and manufactures marking lasers for integration as well as laser-marking workstations with vision-assisted marking workflows. With its worldwide sales and service branches and headquarters near Lübeck/Hamburg (Germany) ALLTEC/FOBA is part of the Danaher Corporation.■
