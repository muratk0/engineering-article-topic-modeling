Compact spark spectrometer performs material analysis in metal production

Hitachi High-Tech Analytical Science Corp. has introduced the FM Expert optical emission spectrometer for metals analysis in foundries and metal production. The compact unit with optional floor stand has a large wavelength range suitable for analyzing all relevant elements in metals quality assurance and production process control. Its compact CCD optics system also allows the analysis of nitrogen at low levels.

The spectrometer combines patented smart optics with an improved plasma view to help ensure excellent analytical performance with low detection limits over the entire wavelength range, states the manufacturer. The argon-purged optics help lengthen maintenance intervals. The unit’s sealed argon system, optimized optical plasma view, and concentric electrode shielding argon flow help ensure reliable nitrogen analysis down to a detection limit of 30 PPM.

60-ton press brake features compact base dimensions

Cincinnati Incorporated has introduced a newly enhanced, 60-ton Autoform hydraulic press brake. The compact hydraulic machine has a 6-ft.-long bed and base dimensions of 83 in. wide by 48 in. deep.

The machine includes a swinging pendant control that can be positioned in front or at the side. Other features include ±0.0004-in. ram repeatability, 11-in. stroke length, and forming speeds programmable from 1 to 120 IPM. The available 6-axis backgauge allows the machine to excel at fabricating small, complex parts with greater throughput than a larger machine, the company states.

The Angle Measuring feature automates the measurement and adjustment of bend angles. During production, a high-speed digital camera captures images of the bend. The images are analyzed, and the press is commanded to continue bending until the part is within the desired tolerance.

Automatic sawing system with autoload cuts multiple lengths of tube up to 3 in.

Scotchman Industries has introduced a new autoloading circular cold saw. The CPO 350 Auto Loader is a fully automatic sawing system designed to help save time, reduce scrap, and eliminate mistakes, the company reports.

Comprising a semiautomatic CPO 350 PKPD saw and an autoloading system, the sawing system can cut multiple lengths of tube up to 3 in. dia. It comes standard with a Windows

®

-based touchscreen control; 5-HP, variable-RPM motor; and a 12.4-in.-dia. blade.

The user can run a complete cut list or run of cut lists at 90 degrees on an automatic cycle or switch to operate the saw in a semiautomatic cycle to miter cut. Quick setup, automatic part length change, and fast reloading of material help add efficiency. The system provides accurate cut lengths and is within ±0.002 per dia. in. in squareness.

Remote maintenance assistance offered for sawing machines

Kasto has launched a new process for maintaining its sawing machines and systems remotely. By means of a tablet, smartphone, or smart glasses, users can send live videos to the manufacturer’s service technicians and receive visual assistance and information in real time in the event of a fault or maintenance work. Service engineers can connect to the systems online to rectify faults or optimize processes.

The heart of the VisualAssistance system is an interactive app that customers can use to connect to service staffusing video and audio streams. Users and engineers share the same field of view in real time to quickly identify individual system components and possible faults.

The app also enables service staffto provide visual assistance and to superimpose markings, for example, on the live video. The customer receives all necessary information directly on his display by means of augmented reality while he is carrying out maintenance or repair work on the saw or storage system on-site. If he is using smart glasses, his hands also are free.

Industrial ceiling fan employs direct-drive motor for light weight

Hunter Industrial’s high-volume, low-speed Titan industrial ceiling fans are designed with fewer blades, parts, and hardware, along with an optimized, direct-drive motor and blade pitch, to require less energy to operate.

At 24 ft. dia., the fan is up to 2.5 times lighter than competitive fans with gearboxes, states the company. It offers silent operation, single-person installation, and a maintenance-free platform.

Personal CNC plasma table brings industrial cutting capability to the home fabricator

Langmuir Systems LLC has launched the CrossFire™ personal CNC plasma table. By distilling the core components of much larger industrial machines, the company has created a fabrication tool with full capability that is compact enough to operate in a home garage.

The system can be rolled to the work area, cut intricate digital designs quickly and accurately, and be stored away when not in use. The open cantilever table design can cut material that typically would require a much larger machine.

Users can select from an online library of designs or create one with Autodesk

®

’s Fusion 360™ software. It also integrates with the work flows and file formats already in use.
