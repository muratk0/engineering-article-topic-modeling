Made-to-order blanks ready for lights-out operation

TCI Precision Metals has introduced close-tolerance, Machine-Ready for Production (MRP) materials. The made-to-order blanks eliminate the need for in-house sawing, grinding, flattening, and squaring operations and outside processing.

Each blank arrives deburred, clean, and to the customer’s net specifications as close as +/-0.0005 in. dimensionally and 0.002-in. flatness, squareness, and parallelism. Four types are available: flat, block, dovetail, and feature-added.

Software add-on allows video streaming of weld processes

Xiris Automation Inc. has released the Real Time Streaming Protocol (RTSP) to stream captured video of weld processes over shared networks. This add-on to Xiris WeldStudio software allows many users to access the same live video stream using a tablet, smartphone, smart TV, or PC with any compatible streaming software such as VLC media player for Windows, Linux, Apple, or Android.

Using the add-on, operators can access their installed weld camera stream remotely to check on how their build is progressing. Educators can monitor multiple student welding booths from a central office without the need to be present in each booth. Streaming to cloud services such as Vimeo is available for unlimited storage, hosting, and controlled redistribution to secure users.

All-electric press brake designed for precision bending

The EG 4010 from Amada is a compact, all-electric press brake for producing small sheet metal components that demand a high degree of precision. A key feature is its high-speed dual servo power drive system. Two AC servomotors allow the machine to reach quick approach, bending, and return speeds that yield fast part cycle times, the company states.

The press brake also is equipped with a thickness detection system to maintain bending accuracy while processing materials that vary in type, thickness, and grain direction. It reads variance of material thickness and compensates depth position automatically for improved precision.

Tube cutter brings cutting versatility to the job site

RIDGID, part of Emerson’s professional tools portfolio, has introduced the PTC-400 Power Tubing Cutter, a portable machine designed to cut stainless steel, carbon steel, copper, aluminum, and plastic tubing from ½ to 4 in. Its quick-acting engagement feature and easy-touse feed screw help ensure high-speed cutting, the company states.

The 32-lb. machine features two rotating speeds and an optimal cutter wheel profile for efficient cold cutting. The cutter wheel cartridge can be replaced without tools.

Quoting tool saves laser job shops time

Tempus Tools Inc. has introduced a new laser cutting and fabrication quoting tool suite. The ToolBox digital tool turns a CAD drawing into an accurate quote in seconds to help laser job shops save time, the company states. The tool also performs 3D unfolding: It can be fed full 3D schematics of a product and will select all the 2D sheet metal surfaces required to be cut by a laser shop. The quoting tool includes a Quick Part parametric parts library with a variety of customizable parts, including brackets, flanges, gussets, and plates that can be adjusted to the specific needs of the user.
