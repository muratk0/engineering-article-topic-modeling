# RedDot Award 2019 for the FACE project

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

## reddot award 2019 winner interface design

SALVAGNINI, a specialist in the production of flexible machinery and systems for sheet metal machining, and NiEW, a business design and customer experience company specialising in the industrial sector, have received a RedDot Award in the Brands & Communication Design 2019 category for the FACE project. The project is dedicated to the design and development of the new human-machine interface with the same name.

The jury of this global competition, now in its 65th edition, recognised the high levels of design, innovation and creativity of this Italian-made product, after carefully evaluating over 8,000 international projects submitted in different categories.

From September 2019, every new Salvagnini system is controlled and managed with FACE, the new interface created from the partnership with NiEW. In today's industrial scenario, the human-machine interface plays a significant role in the commercial success of any system, as it is one of the main points of contact between the manufacturer and the user of every technology and, as such, is able to consolidate the perceived value of the product.

The Red Dot Awards 2019 ceremony was held on 1 November 2019 during a gala event at the Konzerthaus in Berlin, before an audience of around 1400 international guests. The prize-winning projects will be described in the International Yearbook Brands & Communication Design 2019/2020, on sale worldwide from 14 November 2019.

## TRUMPF reports higher sales, but order decline

Dr. phil. Nicola Leibinger-Kammueller, TRUMPF President and Chair of the Managing Board

SHEET METAL SPECIALIST, the TRUMPF Group, recorded a renewed increase in sales in the 2018/19 fiscal year that ended on 30 June 2019, while orders received and profits declined. Sales rose by 6.1 per cent to 3.78 billion euros (3.57 billion euros in the 2017/18 fiscal year). Orders received decreased to 3.68 billion euros (3.80 billion euros in the 2017/18 fiscal year). This equates to a reduction of 3.1 per cent. Earnings before interest and taxes (EBIT) amounted to 349.3 million euros, which was 34.7 per cent below the prior year figure (fiscal year 2017/18: 534.7 million euros). The EBIT margin was 9.2 per cent (fiscal year 2017/18: 15 per cent).

Drivers and challenges "In addition to the high backlog of orders from the previous year, the expansion of the EUV business field was a key driver of the TRUMPF Group's growth in sales revenue. TRUMPF supplies special lasers to ASML, a customer in the Netherlands. These lasers are integrated into systems that use extreme ultraviolet radiation to expose chip surfaces for the computer industry. Sales revenue from this business climbed from 0.26 billion euros in the previous year to 0.39 billion euros in the year under review, an increase of 48 per cent. EUV consequently accounted for a significant share of Group revenues," TRUMPF told ISMR.

Revenues for the Machine Tools division rose by a slight 1.2 per cent to 2.39 billion euros (previous year: 2.36 billion euros). The Laser Technology division posted revenues of 1.38 billion euros, marginally (-2.1 per cent) below the prior-year level of 1.41 billion euros. This decline was attributable to the slowing market in Asia (in particular, in China and South Korea) as well as to the automotive industry's 'reluctance to invest'.

Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller, TRUMPF President and Chairwoman of the Managing Board, explained: "As a company operating in the investment goods sector, we are particularly exposed to the impact of cyclical highs and lows. That is currently the case. Given the uncertainty due to the U.S.-China trade conflict and structural change in the automotive industry, many customers have become more cautious and are postponing investments."

As in previous years, Germany was the largest individual market for TRUMPF with sales of 721 million euros, up 0.2 per cent compared to the previous year (719 million euros). Sales revenue in the United States, the company's second-largest market, grew by 23.2 per cent

to 547 million euros (previous year: 444 million euros). At around 460 million euros, the Netherlands is the third-largest individual market due to the good development of EUV business, followed by China at 415 million euros (previous year: 457 million euros).

Group-wide, the TRUMPF workforce grew by 8 per cent from 13,420 to 14,490 employees in 2018/19. There were 7,427 people employed in Germany as of 30 June 2019 (previous year: 6,778), with around 4,400 working at the Ditzingen headquarters. The number of employees outside Germany rose by 6.3 per cent to 7,063 (previous year: 6,642).

In the year under review, 485 young people completed a training course or co-op work-study programme (previous year: 450). The Group's training rate was 3.4 per cent (previous year: 3.5 per cent). There were two reasons for this development: first, continuing growth in employee numbers at TRUMPF; second, the incipient shortage of qualified staff in the market, especially in the STEM professions.

## Investments, acquisitions and a focus on ‘green'

"After a strong rise in the previous year, investments increased yet again – by 33.3 per cent to 288 million euros (previous year: 216 million euros) – in line with the company's growth strategy. More than half of this expenditure concerned projects in Germany. Construction projects, the majority of which are being carried out at our headquarters in Ditzingen, accounted for over 70 million euros. Completed projects included a lightweight hall, which is being used for the EUV business field, and a daycare centre for employees' children," added TRUMPF.

TRUMPF further pursued its strategy to enhance its technological expertise with new acquisitions. Effective from 1 April 2019, TRUMPF completed the acquisition of Photonics GmbH from Philips, which it had announced in December 2018. As of the same date, the company set up a new business field, TRUMPF Photonic Components. In May 2019, TRUMPF acquired the remaining shares in the Chinese subsidiary, JFY, and now owns the company outright. To reflect this change, TRUMPF introduced a new organizational structure with a CEO China.

## TRUMPF Group Management Board

TRUMPF aims to achieve a CO₂-neutral energy balance at its production sites worldwide by the end of 2020 and therefore intends to increase investments in measures to protect the climate. All TRUMPF sites in Germany already cover 100 percent of their electricity needs through green power contracts. Worldwide, 60 per cent of TRUMPF's electricity needs are covered by green power contracts. Between now and June 2021, in addition to efforts to close this gap and becoming 100% green, TRUMPF intends to invest some 6.4 million euros in improving energy efficiency, for example by using its own cogeneration or photovoltaic plants to generate electricity. Other areas of improvement include process optimisation, generation and distribution of cooling energy, lighting systems and systems for the generation, recovery and distribution of heat. In the mediumterm, TRUMPF aims to significantly reduce its use of emissions trading certificates.

## Record sales in UK

In the UK, TRUMPF sales performance in the year 2018/19 recorded an order intake of £65 million, and sales of £60 million – a 29% increase on the previous year. Introduction of the new modular format laser machines has been successful for TRUMPF along with a wider portfolio of automated bending solutions which supplied the growing demand from the market.

"We continue to introduce innovative solutions in the laser market, such as Highspeed Eco which reduces gas consumption while increasing processing speeds for the same laser powers. Another notable addition is Active Speed Control which automatically adjusts laser parameters based on material conditions to maximise process stability and quality," said the company.

"We continued, for a second year, to see a high number of new customers and existing customers purchasing more complex solutions. There was also very strong growth in bending, especially automated bending." explained TRUMPF UK managing director, Lee Moakes.
