## Fruit weighing stations

BIZ regularly manufactures complex sheet and tubular fabrications. One recent project involved fabricating a series of fruit weighing stations for a packaging conveyor line in a high-hygiene environment. This project involved sheet and tube laser cutting; tube bending; CNC forming; TIG welding and glass bead blasting.
