## Listing the benefits

Since installing the Planar machine, Mr. Nemi stated that "Viscor has saved between five and 10 minutes per part on over thousands of different parts" and confirmed that Viscor can now inspect 300 to 500 parts per week.

"Before we started using the InspecVision machine, a designated full-time professional would have been required to inspect the parts thoroughly. With the InspecVision system, operators can check their own parts swiftly without compromising quality," Nemi told ISMR.

The company's skilled CNC turret and laser machine operators use the Planar machine on a regular basis, finding the machine to be 'very user-friendly, which has made it easy to train all the operators'.

Mr. Nemi also commented that "the ease of use means that we have been able to take on a higher percentage of thorough checks. The InspecVision system also allows us to continue WIP inspections as prescribed."

"Viscor had no difficulty justifying the investment in the Planar due to its instant time-saving benefits and the reduction in operator error. In fact, the payback period on its investment was less than one year. In addition, improving its accuracy control methods has allowed Viscor to take on more work and create consistent quality that is recognised by its partners and customers," Inspecvision told ISMR.

Mr. Nemi added that "the direct benefits include more time to run products without corrections, a reduction in scrap metal waste and quick turnaround times." Key benefits of the Planar machine, he added, include its suitability for the factory floor and the fact that 'minimal maintenance is required'.

"The machine has held up very well against a few bumps and bangs. No special environment or workspace was needed or created. Because the Planar machine has no moving parts, we have seen fewer problems. In the past twelve years, we've only required support on two occasions and our requests were addressed quickly. The service people were great," he added.

Viscor regularly hosts factory tours with industry partners, suppliers and customers and always includes a demonstration of the Planar machine. The company has over a dozen flat sheet turrets, lasers and coil-fed punching machines and processes a lot of short (JIT) runs across three shifts.

As each operator is responsible for checking First and Last Offs plus intermediate checks on longer runs, Viscor confirms that the main benefits it has experienced since installing the Planar are the reduction in downtime measuring and checking against templates as well as less human error.
