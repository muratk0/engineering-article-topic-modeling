# Pipecheck software

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

CREAFORM, a specialist in portable 3D measurement solutions and engineering services, has announced the release of Pipecheck 5.0, which features major enhancements enabling integrity assessment for full pipe joints. Since its introduction in 2011, Pipecheck has provided NDT surface damage diagnostics on both the inner and outer linings of pipes, using both ultrasonic testing and 3D scanning, whether for corrosion, dents or gouges in the metal.

"Improvements include enhanced weld to weld inspections; full-pipe joint assessment of up to 18m pipes; surface inspection of industrystandard pipe joints of up to 56 inches in diameter; faster analysis and reporting; enhanced workflow and multiscanner capability. Other key competitive advantages of the Pipecheck solution include repeatable measurements that are independent of the operator’s or the technician's skills," said the company.

"The challenge wasn't only to increase Pipecheck’s capability as far as pipe lengths, but also to improve the speed of analysis and reporting, regardless of a pipe's size," said Steeves Roy, Creaform product manager. "With no compromise on the speed of data processing, analytics and reporting, Pipecheck 5.0 is a trusted full-pipe joint assessment solution. All Pipecheck license owners already in the field and covered by the Creaform Customer Care Program will benefit from this major software upgrade."

"What is key for our customers is the accuracy and repeatability of 3D mapping compared with manual measurements, as customers need to make the safest decision regarding a pipeline's integrity," added Mathieu Magnan, Business Development Manager, Creaform. "Inspections that used to take days can now be performed in only a few hours with direct on-site reporting using Creaform technologies. With Pipecheck 5.0, it is now possible to scan, analyse and report data from a full pipe joint in less than a day. This is a major performance leap forward compared with previous versions of the software." See creaform3d.com
