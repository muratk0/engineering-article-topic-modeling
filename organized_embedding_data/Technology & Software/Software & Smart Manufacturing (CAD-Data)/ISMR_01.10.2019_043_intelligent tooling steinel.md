# INTELLIGENT TOOLING STEINEL

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

STEINEL is gearing up for Industry 4.O by introducing smart, intelligent tooling systems such as its smart nitrogen cylinder.

STEINEL is a well-positioned company which specialises in tooling. It manufactures its products in two plants in Germany with around 130 employees. The company is now a well-known brand in the punching and bending industry and a solutions provider for the machine building industry sector.

STEINEL itself performs almost all the in-house operations required to manufacture its tooling at its factories at Villingen-Schwenningen and Buching im Allgäu in Germany. Its over-riding mantra is "Everything from a single source".

"We are a standard parts supplier as well as a system solutions partner. We deliver and manufacture nitrogen systems, small tools etc. We have expanded our product range in discussions with our customer as a trusted partner and supplier. We offer a comprehensive range of tooling products necessary for toolmakers in stamping and pressing industries starting from guiding units up to high-end tooling for high-speed presses. We also offer punching and bending tools, thread forming units, nitrogen cylinders, manifold plates and die sets," explained Christian Pape, Head of Sales/ Marketing, STEINEL.

## Preparing for Blechexpo

At this November's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, STEINEL will introduce its smart nitrogen cylinder (booth 6307 in Hall 6). It has been developed as an additional feature for its standard nitrogen cylinders to add value for its customers. Three patents have already been granted for this system. The new intelligent nitrogen cylinders can measure the pressure and the temperature inside the cylinder.

"To give you a brief idea of how the new product works, the integrated sensor system in the smart nitrogen cylinder with remaining lifetime calculation measures the pressure and temperature inside the cylinder and transmits the data by wireless connection or cable to the machine controller, a PC or custom applications," Christian Pape, Head of Sales/ Marketing, STEINEL, told ISMR.

"The positioning of the sensor system in the base of the nitrogen cylinder offers two key advantages over external sensors; measured data is more precise and the installation in the tool saves space. This data not only allows continuous limit monitoring but also displays the remaining lifetime. Knowing how many cycles remain with the current stroke speed and stroke length reduces standstill times and spare part costs," he added.

The company is very strong in the European market (especially Germany) and has planned to substantially increase its activities in the USA over the next few years, as well as in China (a lot of German manufacturers also produce in China).

## Trends and directions

Neudörffer's strategic focus over the next few years is to further develop Steinel's products to support its customers in the digitalisation of their processes as well as to increase activities in new markets, such as electromobility and renewable energy. He has also identified a number of trends in global tooling markets.

"A key trend is advancing standardisation and digitalisation in tooling systems. Because of the indispensable development of predictive maintenance, we expect to see more and more intelligent systems in the future. We have accordingly already developed our modular tool system, the electronic thread forming unit (S-Former E) and the smart nitrogen cylinder, in line with this trend," he told ISMR.

However, he also sees both challenges and opportunities in the continuing globalisation of the market.

"The globalisation and internationalisation of the industry will lead to more price competition in the markets if the quality of products is comparable. On the other hand, special solutions will always be important when it comes to generating a real competitive advantage. For us, as a manufacturer of high-quality and high-precision products, this situation will also be an opportunity. On the one hand, we offer our customers intelligent and reliable systems that significantly increase production efficiency. On the other hand, we develop individual solutions that are optimally tailored to our customers' production processes. I am sure that in the future we will be able to offer our products in new markets where we are not yet present," he explained.

## A system overview

STEINEL's new modular systems, miniaturised high-speed manifold plates as well as guide bushes with adhesive grooves also made their first appearance at previous editions of the Blechexpo trade fair in Stuttgart. Its system components, such as tool-specific nitrogen systems, customer-specific guides and tapping systems, have also been showcased at the exhibition, along with its Modular Tooling System and nitrogen spring systems.

STEINEL's Modular Tooling System (MTS), for progressive press tooling, combines the advantages of a standardised tool with the necessary flexibility for parts production. The modular tool system has a three-stage design. It consists of a machine-specific basic structure, a tool-specific adapter plate and standardised quick-change tool modules mounted on it, designed so that production remains flexible and retrofitting is quick and cost-effective.

"With our modular tooling system, we are tapping enormous efficiency potential," said Christian Pape. "Because, in addition to saving a great deal of time when retooling the machines, there are also considerable simplifications in toolmaking and design."

The "Just-in-Time philosophy", which requires speed and flexibility as well as cost reductions, and the "flying tool change" can become a decisive competitive advantage, said STEINEL. Better machine utilisation and smaller batch sizes with lower stock and capital risks help to increase returns and open up new business niches.

"Whether intelligent nitrogen cylinders, electronic tapping units or modular systems – STEINEL has a lot of experience in developing them, as well as high-quality components," Pape told ISMR, referring to the pedigree and experience of the company from Villingen-Schwenningen, founded in 1925.

"In all our developments, it is our declared goal to combine the advantages of standardisation and digitalisation with STEINEL precision and high-quality processing. I think that is what we have achieved."
