# Spain eyes new manufacturing wave

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

ADDIMAT, the Spanish Association of 3D and Additive Manufacturing Technologies, was created in San Sebastian on 17 December 2014 to group together enterprises related to the manufacture and marketing of equipment and materials for additive manufacturing and 3D printing.

ADDIMAT has emerged as a spin-off of AFM in response to the specific demand to provide a context for 3D and additive manufacturing in the world of manufacturing. AFM, Advanced Manufacturing Technologies, is the Machine Tool, Accessories, Component Parts and Tools Manufacturers’ Association of Spain, and includes 90% of Spanish companies involved with advanced manufacturing technologies and the machine tool sector.

Danobat Group; Egile Group; Fagor Automation; Goratu; Kondia; Ibarmia; Nicolás Correa; Ona Electroerosión; Tumaker; the IK4 and Tecnalia technology centres; the University of the Basque Country; IMH-Machine Tool Institute; the TKNIKA innovation centre and the Bilbao Exhibition Centre are just some of its founding members, together with AFM. Any Spanish company involved in 3D and additive manufacturing technologies can request membership of ADDIMAT.

AFM’s team of professionals provide the support to manage ADDIMAT. Its headquarters are at its facilities in the Technology and Science Park of San Sebastian. Email maria.rlopetedi@afm.es or see www.afm.es
