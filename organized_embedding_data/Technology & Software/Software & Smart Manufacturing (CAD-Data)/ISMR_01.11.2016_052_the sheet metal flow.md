# The sheet metal flow

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

USING automation and digital connectivity, Bystronic cut, transported, bent and sorted sheet metal parts were showcased in a fully automatic Production Cell at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover.

"The Production Cell is a networked manufacturing solution for the cutting and bending of sheet metal products. We are therefore taking the next logical step towards full automation and cross-process solutions. We interlinked systems on our booth to form a fully automatic production cycle," explained Bystronic.

The Production Cell is capable of processing sheet metal products in large and small series. The cell adapts flexibly to varying order situations. The idea behind this automation solution is to combine versatility and productivity. Interlinking is enabled by the modular design of the integrated systems and the software.

Networked sheet metal processing starts on the screen. There is a wall with numerous screens on the exterior of the Production Cell - the ByCockpit. Here, all the information relating to production processes is bundled. On Bystronic's EuroBLECH exhibition booth, visitors could monitor all the process steps within the Production Cell on the screens.

The information on the screens is based on real-time data that is transmitted by the sensors and video systems on the individual processing stations. With the ByCockpit, Bystronic has implemented an important aspect of Industry 4.0: the meaningful utilisation of the ever-increasing availability of process data. One of the ByCockpit's screens showed a live stream so that visitors could see the laser dancing over the metal sheet, cutting part after part. In future, Bystronic will extend this stationary version of the ByCockpit with a mobile version. This will enable users to launch the ByCockpit as a web interface on mobile devices.

The heart of the Production Cell is the ByStar Fiber 3015 laser cutting system. Inside the ByStar Fiber lies a newly developed cutting head that Bystronic presented for the first time at the EuroBLECH exhibition. Its Spot Control function can automatically adapt the focal point of the laser beam with high precision to the sheet. ByVision Cutting control software is also offered.

The ByStar Fiber is directly linked to another Bystronic innovation, the new ByTrans Cross loading and unloading system. Within the Production Cell, it handles the entire material flow associated with laser cutting. Within the Production Cell, the ByTrans Cross system can be upgraded with a new sorting module. This further increases the automation level of the loading and unloading unit.

After cutting and unloading, the cut parts are bent in the bending station. Bystronic has integrated this process step into the Production Cell. An Xpert 150 press brake waits in the form of a fully automatic bending station. The cut parts flow from the laser cutting system via the storage facility and then onto a material deposit for the bending machine. A robot picks up the individual parts, turns them to the correct position and feeds them to the Xpert for bending. To achieve this, the robot is equipped with a 7-axis arm and can handle parts of up to 270kg.

The Xpert press brake is the heart of the bending station in the Production Cell

After the bending process, the robot sorts the finished parts and places them in separate storage areas. Depending upon the configuration, the bending station offers room for up to 15 of these areas, where the finished parts can be sorted, for example, according to jobs. All that the user has to do now is to pack, label and ship.

At the EuroBLECH exhibition, in connection with the Production Cell, Bystronic presented the extended version of the Plant Manager. In addition to cutting processes, the software module now also covers bending.

The increasingly complex demands that are placed on the manufacturing of sheet metal products means that the relevance of such software is constantly growing. When all the parts have been cut and bent, and are ready for delivery, the Plant Manager sends a message to one of the screens in the ByCockpit or to the user's ERP system. The shipping department then knows that the job has been completed and the parts are ready for shipping. See www.bystronic.com
