# Saving valuable space on the shop floor

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

With thousands of installations all over the world, Modula Lift is an advanced automated vertical warehouse. Featuring vertical trays, it has been designed to reduce floor space by 90%, according to its Spanish manufacturer, Modula Logistica Y Almacenaje SL.

“The warehouse is very versatile, hence its usage in companies of all sizes to store shapes, weights and items of different sizes, in any industry sector. You can find Modula Lift automated vertical carousel systems in shops, ironmongers', warehouses and manufacturing, as a production buffer or as storage space. It can store industrial and semi-finished products, spare parts and any kind of product in any industrial environment and every department," said the company.

Different configurations are available, depending upon space. Modula Lift offers the option for operators to select the dual-delivery level and work on two trays simultaneously (while one is in the bay, the other is preparing the next picking) or with an external bay to facilitate the operator's work if they are using mechanical aids such as forklifts, zero-weight cranes or overhead hoists.

Simultaneous picking can be done on both sides of the machine with opposite bays, or from different levels of the same structure, with the multi-level bay.
