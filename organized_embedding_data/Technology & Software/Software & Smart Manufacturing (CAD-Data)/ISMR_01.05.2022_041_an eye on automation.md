# An eye on automation

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Comau is expanding its product portfolio with the new N-220-2.7 (N-220), a 220kg payload robot featuring a modular set up, direct kinematics, absolute calibration and enhanced algorithms.

“These characteristics deliver significant ease of use, straightforward integration and faster post-installation maintenance while ensuring precision, flexibility, repeatability and minimum backlash,"said the Italian automation specialist.

It will, hopes Comau, consolidate the company's position in the automotive sector while enabling its expansion into high-growth industrial segments including metal, food & beverages, fulfilment and plastic & chemicals.

"Based on the 2021 IFR (International Federation of Robotics) Report, we expect 160-220kg payload articulated robots to represent an estimated 30% of the total installed market, composed of 250,000-300,000 units in 2022, with a potential Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) '22-'26 of 2%"said Nicole Clement, Chief Marketing Officer, Comau.

The new N-220 industrial robot features an innovative architectural approach based on progressive modularity, component reduction, advanced motors and APC drivers. With its wide working area and Performance Level D cartesian safety features, the high payload robot is suitable for a range of industries. It has a hollow forearm and an integrated application dressing. The N-220 robot also benefits from its use of direct kinematics, absolute calibration and evolved motion algorithms. It comes fitted with integrated forklift adapters and optional high-torque washers for automatic anchoring base fixing, easier relocation and faster installation.

The first of Comau's new generation of 6-axis articulated robots, N-220 addresses the specific market need for cost-effective and easy-to-integrate robotics that also deliver high-speed industrial performance. The holistic design and modular architecture of the N-220 also make it easier to install, use and maintain. Manufactured at Comau headquarters in Grugliasco (Turin), Italy, using a robotic assembly line, it automates assembly, spot welding, packaging and material handling operations in addition to continuous process applications such as 3D printing and gluing.

“Our e-Motion software algorithm improves cycle time, motion quality and path control. Thanks to the use of a nitrogen balancing system on axis 2, and larger stroke-ends on axis 3, the N-220 enjoys increased agility and a wide backwards working area that is ideal for bending

and handling applications. And because the N-220 uses only two motor sizes and a single oil type, replacing a faulty motor becomes quick and easy. An additional benefit of the simplified, modular architecture is easier overall maintenance and improved Mean Time Between Failure (MTBF) due to a 50% reduction of robot componentry," added the manufacturer.

Recognizing that the integration speed has a direct impact on production potential, the N-220 implements Nodal speech-based coding language for open, easy programming.■
