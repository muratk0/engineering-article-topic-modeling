# Robotic automation, drive and control technology

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

For three days, the Nuremberg Exhibition Centre in Germany opened its doors to the world of international automation. "Best in Class Products and Solutions" was the motto of the Yaskawa stand at SPS 2023, providing a platform for several highlights.

Yaskawa made use of a special area to present the i³ Mechatronics industry sector, including the concept and benefits of Industry-4.0 production. "i3" stands for integrated, intelligent and innovative. This integrated approach comprises existing and newly developed products and solutions for drive and control technology as well as in-house software solutions and Motoman robots.

Yaskawa also focused on its iCube CONTROL automation platform at SPS. The system forms a technically coordinated total solution for industry-specific controllers including everything from engineering software and controller hardware to integrated chip technology. It has the potential to do much more. After this new platform was presented for the first time at the last SPS trade fair, the complete portfolio of the iC922x series of control systems followed this year.

The Yaskawa portfolio of HMls and panel PCs also offered hardware and software innovations. The new generation can be programmed with new visualisation software. This is integrated into iCube Engineer, the software environment of the iCube CONTROL platform.

Yaskawa also provided an exclusive preview of its forthcoming LA700 series: elevator-specific frequency inverters. Another feature presented at the stand was the 300S+ high-speed control system, which Yaskawa is using to respond to current changes in the automation industry. Compatible and available from stock, it ensures that the existing control environment is well equipped for the future "without any issues," said Yaskawa.

Yaskawa robots were also on display at SPS. Some of the stand highlights at SPS included the current Cobot ecosystem; Motoman industrial robots for handling, welding, palletising and many other applications; as well as “Made in Europe” robots.
