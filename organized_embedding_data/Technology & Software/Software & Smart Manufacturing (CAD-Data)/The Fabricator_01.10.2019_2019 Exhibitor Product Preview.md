Welding process uses reversing wire for process stability

MoTion Control Weld from Cloos is suitable for fineplate applications and applications in the lowest capacity ranges, as well as those with special demands for weld surface and appearance.

The process combines the manufacturer’s Control Weld process and reversing wire. In the process, the wire is drawn back and forward at a frequency of up to 180 Hz, which results in an extremely high process stability to the lowest capacity range. Formation of spatters and powder residues is minimized to help prevent extensive rework, making the process suitable for applications in additive production. Precise heat control helps produce excellent weld quality, and the 40 percent higher welding speed improves production efficiency, the manufacturer reports.

The process can be used in all welding positions on steel, chrome-nickel, and coated plates for corner and flange welding, GMAW brazing, surfacing, thinplate welding (up to 3 mm thick), and additive production.

System simplifies mechanized loading of products into containers

The Combilift Container Slip-Sheet (Combi-CSS) has been designed for quick and easy mechanized loading of products (particularly long ones) into containers. The low, freestanding platform has a dual-directional, motordriven pulling mechanism on which a Hardox 500 steel sheet rests. With a capacity of 66,000 lbs., it allows a full load of product to be prepared behind the container on the sheet and loaded onto a 20-ft. container in just three minutes (or a 40-ft. container in six minutes).

Once loaded, the sheet is guided into the container by the mechanism underneath, which comprises four hydraulic cylinders moving in sequence. Hydraulic pins on the ends of the cylinders move up, fitting into holes cut into the sheet to secure it, while the cylinders pull the sheet forward to help ensure safe loading without product damage.

When the goods are fully loaded within the container, a hydraulic rear barrier gate swings across the container opening.
