# Dynamic French software house, Alma, launched its almaCAM platform at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Generating a consolidated annual turnover of 10.8 million euros, Alma is a profitable software house and a market leader in France. At this year’s EuroBLECH, it announced the worldwide launch of almaCAM, its new CAD/CAM software for sheet-metal working.

In a consistent environment housing Alma’s CAM software modules (beginning with 2D cutting by following contour and punching applications), almaCAM provides efficient data management and renews the process for machine programming, which combines agility and security. As an open platform, almaCAM enables the plug-in of external applications and the development of new ones (quote creation for sheet metal, production scheduler etc.). And, said the company, with a completely re-designed user interface, almaCAM ensures usability.

“Our software can be used to program any kind of cutting machine (laser, plasma, punching, waterjet), punching-nibbling, routing, 3D cutting, tubes/profile cutting and robot welding. EuroBLECH is the right platform for us to launch our new almaCAM software, an environment that embeds all our CAM applications and integrates all CAM features, data and process management,” Sylvain Cathebras, Alma’s marketing manager told ISMR on its EuroBLECH booth.

“AlmaCAM is an open platform that can communicate with any production management software (ERP) and can incorporate additional software modules either from Alma or from partners. For example, we have already developed a quotation module (almaQuote) which we can add to the platform. It makes the programming process much easier and more flexible.”

It has taken four years for Alma to develop this very ambitious product – it introduced the first prototype last year in France. The company expects to get its first customers for the new software at the end of this year (mainly in France) and will then start European deployment in early 2015.

## Growth through partnership

“We have been working in Europe, mostly through our subsidiaries in France, Germany and Italy. We also have offices in Brazil, China and the US with distributors in other key European, South American and Asian countries. Alma is a cooperative, 100% owned by its employees, so we are a motivated company, with lots of ambition. We are open to believe that this is the way to growth,” explained Cathebras.

Alma, which has long been selling its CAD/CAM software for cutting and sheet metal working applications in Germany, also recently announced a partnership with AdeQuate Solutions GmbH, a leading reseller of TopSolid's CAM software. The objective behind the two companies' cooperation is to enhance their presence in Germany, Austria and in German-speaking Switzerland, and to offer their clients a larger panel of products.

The collaboration with AdeQuate Solutions is a confirmation of Alma's strategic pursuit of the German market. On 1 September 2014, the CEO of AdeQuate Solutions, Jørgen Lorenzen, was appointed Director of Alma GmbH. The two companies will test their capacity to work together over the coming year. If the result is positive, they will ultimately merge.

“One of our targets this year is to develop the German market. Adequate Solutions, a software company involved in CAM for machining, wanted to enlarge their portfolio and are interested in sheet metal and robotics software – our partnership with them will also strengthen our German subsidiary,” Cathebras told ISMR.

“As an independent software developer, we have to be very good at understanding current machine tool technologies under development so that we can properly integrate our software into these machine tools. It is important that we maintain a close relationship with the machine tool builders. We need to keep a close eye on technology developments and try to anticipate what might be coming…. We are involved in enduser software products but we also sell our sheet metal software library (like automatic nesting) to other CAM developers or machine tool builders. We also develop relationships with resellers.

“We are looking for new machine tool builders OEMS to integrate our software as well as distributors in some key countries. Currently, for our sheet metal/CAM software activity, we are +15% on turnover this year so the future looks promising.”

## Robot welding and nesting

Sheet metal CAD software, said Cathebras, needs to integrate with production management systems and other workshop programs. Software can no longer be isolated in the workshop – connectivity is key.

His other main focus is software for robot welding – he sees great potential for that and Alma is investing a lot of resources into automating and renewing this product ready for the launch of a new version next year.

“Robot welding is a niche market and we can prove that offline programming is reliable, efficient and profitable. Germany is a big market for robot welding,” he told ISMR.

Alma is the only CAD/CAM software developer for cutting and sheet metal working applications to be involved simultaneously in applicative software and software components. Alma’s components deal specifically with automatic nesting for cutting optimisation. To make those components more visible to software developers, Alma has released a specialised website in English.

Alma’s nesting software components can be integrated into third-party applications developed by CAD/CAM or ERP software developers/publishers working on specific market segments or manufacturers developing their own software. Alma can also customise components to meet the different requirements and specifications of targeted industries.

Distinct from Alma’s generic “CAD/CAM software” website, largely committed to promoting applicative software for end-users (2D cutting, 3D cutting, robot welding, etc.), the new nesting website is specific to Alma’s nesting software components. This segmentation will enable visitors to access more easily the information they need. The new nesting website also points to the automatic nesting demonstrator, a cloud-based application that enables users to try Alma’s product with their parts.

For further information, see www.almacam.com. ■

Machine for edge rounding and finishing of parts in a single pass.

The oscillating system is equipped with 12 brushes. The 6 top and 6 bottom, left and right rotating grinding flap brushes process the parts on both sides simultaneously.

## Multi rotating brush machine

Machine for uniformly deburring, rounding edges and finishing of parts. For laser, punched and machined parts, or parts with pockets/protrusions in all directions. The rotating carrousel, equipped with 8 left and right rotating abrasive flap brushes, processes the parts in all directions, no matter how fine the edges of the work pieces are.
