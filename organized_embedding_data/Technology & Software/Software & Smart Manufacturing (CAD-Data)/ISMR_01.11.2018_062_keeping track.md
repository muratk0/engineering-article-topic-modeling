# KEEPING TRACK

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

MARKATOR® presented the latest model of its FlyMarker®mini - The FlyMarker® mini 120/45, at EuroBLECH 2018.

MARKATOR®, the German manufacturer of dot peen and scribe marking systems for durable markings on metals and plastics, was at this October's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover with a wide range of products. The company offers hand-held and bench-top marking systems as well as systems to integrate into automatic production lines.

At the exhibition, MARKATOR® presented the latest model of its FlyMarker® mini – The FlyMarker® mini 120/45. The new hand-held marking system builds on the strengths of previous models and offers users a larger marking area and high marking power.

Nearly every industrial product needs to be marked with a serial number, VIN number, company logo or Data Matrix code for traceability.

It is possible to mark characters and numbers on nearly all materials – from plastics up to hardened steel.

## A new launch

"We are launching the new version of the battery-driven Flymarker Mini 120/45 here at EuroBLECH," Rahn told ISMR. "It has a large marking area (120 x 45mm) and more functionality than previous models. Users can mark deeper and more solidly, with higher marking power. It also features an optionally available extra strong magnet for very deep markings. The FlyMarker® mini 120/45 has a lightweight, compact design and integrated keyboard. The powerful lithium ion battery enables completely mobile work, which is very helpful when big, unmovable workpieces need to be marked on-site."

MARKATOR's long-term experience in the development of mobile hand-held marking systems enabled it to twin, on the new model, a very large marking area of 120 x 45mm with a low weight of approx. 3.2kg incl. battery. The new system can even mark round workpieces (radial and axial). Programming of marking files is simplified through selfexplanatory and intuitive software. Marking files can be created easily and quickly, the company told ISMR, and a high-resolution LC colour display provides a practical preview function to picture the marking file before actual marking.

## The FlyMarker®mini 120/45

"It is possible to mark characters and numbers on nearly all materials – from plastics up to hardened steel. Even after subsequent processing, such as coating, the marking remains visible, in most cases," explained Andreas Rahn.

"It is able to connect to a scanner and even mark parts that are not flat but curved, round or uneven (such as tube, for example). We have also developed new software so that customers can create logos themselves on a PC," continued Rahn.

MARKATOR® is a specialist in the durable and economic marking of industrial parts – the German, family-owned company was established in 1984, selling conventional marking systems. In 1988, it started selling pin stamping machines and, in 1997, launched its own product for pin stamping and scribemarking systems at the EMO exhibition in Hanover.

We decided to focus solely on developing pin stamping and scribe marking systems.

"Our competitors have developed lasermarking systems but we decided to focus solely on developing pin stamping and scribe marking systems. We were the first to launch this type of battery-driven handheld marking system - the FlyMarker mini is the fifth generation of the product group FlyMarker, first launched in 2004. We have our headquarters including our own R&D department in Germany but work with agents around the world to sell the systems. We have a large mix of customers from a range of sectors (not always industrial), which keep us busy," smiled Rahn.

Its customers range from small milling and drilling companies to large automotive or shipping industries as well as a wide range of sheet metal shops, OEMs etc. In short anyone who needs to mark small or large parts. It sells about 50% of its machines to the automotive industry and its suppliers.

## The future in focus

Rahn sees demand for durable and economic markings on industrial parts continuing to grow.

"We maintain a constant dialogue with our customers – it is their requirements that direct our R&D projects. We often visit them to demonstrate systems and their feedback is invaluable. We create and launch products on the back of what our customers tell us they need," Rahn told ISMR.

"Our strategic focus is to develop our exports business, add a lot of new functions to the marking systems and widen our range of marking systems. We want to keep growing, but in a sustainable way. That means continuing to build products that our customers need and staying up to date with the latest trends and developments in scribing and marking technologies."
