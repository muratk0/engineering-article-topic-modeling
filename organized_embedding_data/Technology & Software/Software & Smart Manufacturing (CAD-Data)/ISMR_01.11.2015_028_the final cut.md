# THE FINAL CUT

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

Yutong, the world’s largest bus manufacturer, chooses Lantek software for its laser, plasma and shearing machines

ISMR SAYS:

“ At Yutong, an advanced nesting algorithm has been implemented to optimise its shearing process”

Each year, more than 60,000 buses roll off the line of Yutong's factory in the city of Zhengzhou, China. This figure makes the Chinese company the largest bus manufacturer in the world, with buses operating in over 120 countries and a market share of more than 30% of the sector in China.

Yutong, which allocates around 4% of its annual budget to innovation, has chosen Lantek CAD/CAM software to make its production system more efficient, incorporating Lantek Expert for its laser, plasma, and shearing machines.

## Nesting advances

Yutong has set up the only experimental centre in the world for the study of buses and coaches. It also boasts leading cathodic electro-coating lines for entire vehicles as well as an advanced chassis workshop. These have comprehensively improved the design level and manufacturing capability of Yutong’s bus products. The group has also introduced ERP, PDM, KOA, CRM, SRM and other systems successively, and integrated production, supply, sales, technology, finance, HR etc.

Yutong manufactures 310 buses daily and 145 different models

“At Yutong, an advanced nesting algorithm has been implemented to optimise its shearing process. It maximizes the efficiency of the machine process and the use of material. To achieve this improvement, complex preliminary calculations were done before the nesting to establish the optimal sheet size and the best part placement strategy to reduce changes in the orientation of the sheet,” explained Mr. Liu, head of Lantek China in Shanghai.

Through this partnership, Lantek, an international specialist in the development and sale of CAD/CAM/MES/ERP software solutions for the metal industry and the machine tool sector, has become one of the suppliers of this Chinese multinational, which manufactures 310 buses daily and 145 different models.

Lantek provides CAD/CAM/MES/ERP software solutions for companies fabricating sheet metal, tubes and beams with any cutting (laser, plasma, oxy-cut, water jet and shear) and punching technology. This includes advanced nesting software and manufacturing management solutions.

This year, it expects its business is expected to grow by 15% in line with the trend that the multinational has seen in recent years, despite the global economic crisis. The international market represents 88% of Lantek's results.

According to Joseba Pagaldai, Lantek's commercial director, "Yutong's trust is an important milestone for our company and solidifies its international development in a sector as strategic as automotive in one of the world's largest economies. This sale also reflects one of our main competitive advantages, the customised adaptation of our services to the needs of each client."

“Prominent manufacturers of commercial vehicles stand out mainly because of their customised vehicle concepts. The transport tasks of their clients vary and have to satisfy strict requirements in terms of safety, flexibility and volume. Our software supports them, using the most up-to-date technology for the manufacture of trucks, tankers and buses, both standard models and custom products, for the widest variety of purposes and transported materials. Our CAD/CAM, MES and ERP software solutions optimise all processes from quote generation to computer-assisted design, intelligent materials usage and synchronization of production steps, to on-time completion and inventory management,” the company told ISMR. ■
