# Surface inspection technology

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

LAST YEAR, CSC’s (China Steel Corporation) continuous annealing line no. 2 in Kaohsiung, Taiwan, appeared in need of repair and eventual replacement. Because very light longitudinal defects can occur on the surface, CSC made rigorous demands on the new annealing line and its performance. For quality assurance and process optimisation, it requested features such as a side light sensor and a separate hole detector.

“In the end, CSC decided in favour of technology from ISRA VISION Parsytec. The advantages of the new system include reliable defect detection, as well as detection of the smallest longitudinal defects using high-resolution cameras and the combination of two lighting technologies. By substituting its surface inspection system with the latest system from ISRA VISION Parsytec, CSC has been able to improve the quality and yield of its annealing process line to a considerable extent,” ISRA VISION Parsytec told ISMR.
