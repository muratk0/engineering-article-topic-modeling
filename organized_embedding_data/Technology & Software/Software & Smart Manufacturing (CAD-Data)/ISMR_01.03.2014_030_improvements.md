ABB will be showcasing its latest technologies at MACH 2014 at the Birmingham NEC from 7 to 11 April 2014. Stand 5771 in Hall 5 will showcase ABB’s latest developments in robotic automation, with an integrated demonstration cell featuring IRB 6700, IRB 2600 and IRB 140 robots.

A highlight of the stand will be the new IRB6700 seventh generation large ABB robot featuring a multitude of improvements. These include increased accuracy and speed, as well as the ability to handle heavier payloads of up to 235kg which will be further increased to 300kg as new variants are added over the next 18 months. It also delivers improved energy efficiency, with a 15 per cent reduction in power consumption.

Another new feature is the inclusion of ‘Lean ID’, a new Integrated Dressing (ID) solution which integrates the most exposed parts of the dress pack into the robot. A dual act between ABB’s IRB 2600 robot and IRB 140 will be featuring ABB’s MultiMove which can control up to four robots from one IRC5 controller, with a compact drive module added for each additional robot.

The stand will also feature a welding cell demonstration incorporating the IRB 1600 robot with positioner. The built-in second generation of TrueMoveTM and QuickMoveTM substantially improves the path accuracy and cycle time of the IRB 1600 robot. For arc welding applications, this robot also has the option of an integrated arc welding hose package. See www.abb.com/robotics ■

## Rapid set-up

MATE has introduced new TRUMPF-Style Forming Tool and Special Shapes programming files for easier and faster press set-up, even before the tooling arrives. Now, buyers of Mate’s TRUMPF-Style forming tools and special shapes receive programming files via email for setting up and programming the new tools in their press even before tools are delivered. Previously, the set-up process was done one of two ways: manually from set-up drawings or with a USB stick supplied with the new tool data from the original equipment manufacturer.

“Mate emails the files to the customer for import,” reported John Galich, Mate marketing manager. “This is a faster, more efficient method because users can start programming and setting up their machine while it’s still being manufactured. By being able to do all of the prep work ahead of time, our customers can essentially start the job once the tool arrives. Mate’s express process eliminates easy-to-lose USB sticks and allows for direct importing and storing the information correctly with other programs.”

Mate’s TRUMPF-Style tooling includes a full range of options for every application. These include Next™ high performance tooling, QuickLock™ convenience tooling, standard style economy tooling and LongLife™ slitting tools. See www.mate.com ■

## On the surface

THE ELLESCO exhibition booth at MACH 2014 will mark the re-branding of the company and also the launch-pad for the company to promote a variety of innovative new surface finishing and deburring solutions. On Stand 4510, Ellesco will be keen to emphasise the importance of high quality surface finishing and efficient deburring solutions and the added value it offers to manufacturers.

With seven fully operational machines on its stand, Ellesco is inviting show visitors to bring their components to the show, so that the company can demonstrate the efficiency, speed, reliability, cost effectiveness and value that high quality surface finishing can provide. For high-end applications, Ellesco will be showing Sugino’s line of Jet Cleaning Centres at MACH. The Sugino range of high pressure waterjet deburring machines are primarily aimed at the machined component and cast part sectors.

Alongside the Sugino machine will be the Autopulit line of polishing machines. At MACH, the Autopulit machine will incorporate a robotic display to demonstrate its ability to deliver high quality with consistency through high levels of automation. Complementing the Autopulit machine will be the Kuhlmeyer line of long belt abrasive systems for surface finishing, weld dressing and edge bevelling via manual or automated operations. At MACH, this will be demonstrated with the ZBS2, 3m-bed, long belt grinding machine. The ZBS2 operates with two belts running side by side on a controlled table that can be utilised with two different finishing belts.

“Our leading supplier, Timesaver / Grindingmaster, will be launching a new range of machines – the Series 1000 T&B. Timesavers’ first machine to process both sides in one pass has been launched only after proving it can match the feed speed of all Timesavers machines with efficient and consumable cost management. The machine will be the first in the UK and MACH 2014 is the company’s chosen launch show,” explained Ellesco.

With three Timesaver machines being showcased at MACH, Ellesco will also highlight the rotary bush machine which can radius all edges on components from 10mm by 10mm, through to parts of 1350mm wide.

Supporting the deburring and surface finishing product lines, Ellesco also works closely with Carter Thorne, a UK manufacturer of specialist extraction products with solutions that work in harmony with the Ellesco range of deburring and surface finishing machines. At MACH, the Ellesco stand will be highlighting the benefits of the W120/4kW wet extraction unit. ■

## Mobile welding

## A PORTABLE WELDING

SYSTEM from Fronius enables welders to have direct access to mains electricity, giving them unprecedented freedom of movement. Lightweight at just 11kg, portable and offgrid, the Accupocket 150/400 from Fronius is an MMA welding system with a lithium-ion rechargeable battery for manual electrode and TIG welding. The Fronius Accupocket is supplied with a charging device that is specially tailored to the requirements of the welding unit. In fast charging mode, it only takes 30 minutes to recharge the battery to more than 90% of its total capacity of 400Wh. A fully charged battery delivers sufficient power to weld up to six 3.25mm diameter electrodes or 18 electrodes of 2.5mm in diameter.

“Even when attached to the ActiveCharger, the Accupocket can still be used to carry on working in hybrid mode. During run-times, the energy from the battery is used to ensure a constant arc under all operating conditions (e.g. mains-voltage fluctuations),” the company told ISMR.

See www.fronius.co.uk ■
