# Bending software and calculation tool

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Lantek, a digital transformation specialist for the sheet-metal sector, presented a new software update and global release at EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover this October. It presented two innovative projects and the new features of its Global Release 2022 software update.

From its EuroBLECH stand, Lantek showcased its entire portfolio for sheet-metalworking at the international technology exhibition.

Six different stations presented the individual modules of the comprehensive software suite illustrated by short films. The 2022 software update that Lantek at EuroBLECH 2022 was themed “flexibility, efficiency and automation".

"The software puts the user at the centre to improve usability and automate processes for greater efficiency. The goal is to speed up work, reduce manual steps and enhance the user experience," Lantek told ISMR.

The Lantek Bend innovation, which Lantek presented to the public for the first time at EuroBLECH, is designed to quickly train any operator who needs to bend workpieces. Lantek Bend is a fully functional module and represents an important step in Lantek's multi-vendor strategy of continuously incorporating new machine models from different manufacturers.

“EuroBLECH also saw the launch of the commercial version of Lantek iQuoting: a simpler, faster and accurate budgeting solution based on the latest cloud technology, data modelling and process automation. With Lantek iQuoting, any sales team can calculate quotes in sheet-metal fabrication, even without technical training. The cloud application covers all the needs of the industry and generates a reliable calculation based on real data in just a few minutes," added Lantek.

Lantek's patented smart manufacturing software enables factories to be connected, turning them into Smart Factories. It rounds off its range of services with CAD, CAM, MES and ERP solutions for companies that manufacture metal parts from sheet metal, tubes and profiles using any cutting technology (laser, plasma, oxycut, waterjet, shearing and punching).
