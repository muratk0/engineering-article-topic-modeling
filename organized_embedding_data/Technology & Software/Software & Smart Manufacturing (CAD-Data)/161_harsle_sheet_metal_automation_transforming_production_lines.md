## Key Technologies Driving the Change

- **Automated Press Brakes and Shearing Machines:** CNC-controlled machines can automatically adjust bending angles, back gauges, and cutting parameters to match digital designs, reducing setup time and human intervention.
- **Robotic Handling Systems:** Integrated robots load and unload sheets, position them accurately, and even stack finished parts, improving safety and throughput.
- **Smart Software Integration:** CAD/CAM systems connect directly to machines, enabling offline programming, nesting optimization, and real-time production monitoring.
- **Laser Cutting Automation:** High-speed fiber lasers paired with automatic loading systems can run unattended shifts and produce complex profiles with minimal waste.

## Smart Low-Voltage Engineering: Empowering the Production Line
In sheet metal automation production lines, electricity is an indispensable element. Smart low-voltage engineering is responsible for managing and monitoring the power supply system. It ensures stable power supply and communication for equipment and systems, injecting a continuous stream of energy into the production line. In this process, the management of power and signals is intelligently integrated, allowing robots, sensors, and sheet metal automation control systems to operate continuously and stably. Additionally, low-voltage engineering includes cable wiring and signal transmission, enabling seamless data transfer between devices and ensuring the stable operation of the production line.
