At EuroBLECH 2022, ARKU (Baden-Baden, Germany) will be demonstrating its deburring/levelling technology on stand A94.

"Drastic time savings are possible thanks to new and intelligent software called the ARKUWizard. It can be seen live in action for the first time at the trade show in Hanover," it told ISMR.

"With the Wizard, users only have to input basic data about the parts (thickness, material type etc.). The software then calculates the optimum tools and processing settings. This means that even novice employees can operate the deburring machines. As skilled workers are still hard to find, this is an immense help for sheet metal processing companies," it added.

During live demonstrations, the Wizard will be in operation on an EdgeBreaker® 6000 deburring machine, the latest addition to ARKU's deburring portfolio. The EdgeBreaker® 6000 has been designed specifically to meet the needs of laser job shops and is extremely flexible for uniform edge rounding, even up to 2mm radius.
