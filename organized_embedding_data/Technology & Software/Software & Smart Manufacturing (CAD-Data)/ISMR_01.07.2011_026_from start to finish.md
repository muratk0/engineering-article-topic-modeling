## MEASUREMENT AND SCANNING

The Werth ScopeCheck, with full 3D capability, offers precise quality control in the workshop environment and is suitable for applications such as sheet metal punching. Using an additional integrated measurement probe, surfaces which cannot be accessed by the optics, such as undercuts, can be probed and scanned. This allows for measurement uncertainties of as little as several micrometres.

The elements determining accuracy are made of materials with a low expansion coefficient, minimising thermal deformation. Sensitive components are covered and the use of a customised compact control in separate housing minimises heat build-up. This machine class is equipped with an image processing sensor featuring zoom optics and basic modes of illumination. It can be further equipped with a variety of sensors and probes for different measurement tasks.

The ScopeCheck, like other Werth CMMs, includes an image processing sensor system, designed for fully automatic measurement of complex workpieces even in poor contrast conditions. WinWerth measuring software allows efficient operation of the machines and measurement programs to be created easily – these are graphically displayed as a CAD drawing, enabling simple inspection.
