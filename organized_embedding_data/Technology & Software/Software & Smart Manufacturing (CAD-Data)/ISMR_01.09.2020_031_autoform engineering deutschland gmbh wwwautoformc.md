# AutoForm Engineering Deutschland GmbH www.autoform.com

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

AUTOFORM develops and distributes software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry.

“With the introduction of rapid process simulation and specialised tool modelling, we have revolutionised the workflow in tool making. Our software offers strong business benefits in the development and production process and is also characterised by easy implementation and customised operating procedures,” said the software specialist.

Over 3,500 users in more than 1,000 companies and 50 countries rely on AutoForm software for the sheet metal forming industry. The company is headquartered in Switzerland with subsidiaries in 14 countries.
