# AutoForm in the Cloud

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GMBH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has announced a new service, AutoForm in the Cloud.

"Existing, as well as new, customers now have flexible, browser-based access to AutoForm technology. We have expanded our range of services to better meet the needs of our customers. From now on, both software and hardware can be used directly from the browser, making on-site installation, maintenance and updates unnecessary. Users can now gain access to AutoForm software products and hardware which they would not have been able to use before," the software specialist told ISMR.

This new service addresses three different use cases. Firstly, for users who are interested in the full suite of AutoForm Forming R8 products but have either never or hardly ever used AutoForm products or have only periodic peak loads. This new service allows access to AutoForm software products and hardware as well as parallel computing on eight cores. Secondly, AutoForm in the Cloud makes robustness studies available to all existing users. Thirdly, AutoForm in the Cloud serves the needs of existing users who require faster turn-around times and, in particular, more sophisticated computing for complex parts. The service is designed to enable users to successfully complete their tasks through parallel computing on eight cores.

"With AutoForm in the Cloud, AutoForm software can be used in combination with the optimum hardware architecture, in terms of processor type and core memory, storage as well as parallel processing. Users now have access to AutoForm software products and hardware based on their specific needs and can carry out their work effectively and without delays," concluded the company.
