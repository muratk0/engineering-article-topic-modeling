# INDUSTRY NEWS

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

“At EMO Hannover 2013, we shall be seeing plenty of interesting tool and technology solutions incorporating concepts of this kind with sensors and actuators”, commented GFE’s Executive Director, Prof. Dr.-Ing. Frank Barthelmä.

Manufacturer Komet has already come up with a method for transferring a tool’s presetting data into the tool management system by simple scanning. But the company aims to achieve a lot more: the goal is a cloud with a large quantity of process and tool data, serving as a foundation for a huge knowledge database. It will then be able to provide dependable answers to questions e.g. on tool behaviour during operation, or the nature of any machine malfunctions. But, says Komet, there are even more options since, as larger quantities of data are acquired, the error rate will also fall. Some initial concepts/mock-ups will be on show at EMO Hannover 2013.

EMO Hannover 2013 is a key trade fair for the metalworking sector and will be held from 16 to 21 September 2013. International manufacturers of production technology will be spotlighting “Intelligence in Production” at EMO Hannover 2013. The principal focus is on metal-cutting and forming machine tools, production systems, high-precision tools, automated material flows, computer technology, industrial electronics and accessories. In 2011, the fair attracted more than 2,000 exhibitors and around 140,000 trade visitors from over 100 countries.
