# Customised stamping lines

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Founded in 1914, Bulgarian manufacturer Vaptech has over 100 years of experience in the machinery and manufacturing sector. Today, it supplies products, services and solutions to the metalforming and hydro power industries. Its portfolio includes mechanical and hydraulic metalforming presses, ranging from 300-30,000kN nominal force. It can also engineer complete, customised production line solutions for metal processing, including transfer systems; decoilers; feeders; straighteners and other auxiliary equipment.

"Our metalforming solutions are equipped with proprietary algorithms and automation designed to provide maximum interaction and synchronisation. They are Industry-4.0 ready. We have developed two tailored solutions for Industry 4.0 (remote monitoring/ troubleshooting and preventive/predictive maintenance)," it explained.

The software also helps to improve profitability by providing managers with realtime information about production levels and energy consumption. Other aftersales services include on-site repairs, a dedicated service hotline and machine warranty periods of up to 36 months.

Headquartered in Sofia, Bulgaria, with its main production facility in Pleven, Vaptech has over 250 employees and more than 40,000m² of workspace. It has completed projects in over 40 countries on six continents.
