# Taking new measures

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

RPI has launched a new metrology-grade rotary axis table for portable coordinatemeasuring machines (CMMs) which, it says, "improves productivity by up to 40%." OctoScan is designed to enhance the capability of portable measurement arms. It has a radial and axial runout of 1 micrometre and coning of axis +/- 0.5 arcseconds.

"It enables objects that are larger than the standard reach of the portable arm to be easily and accurately recorded. It also provides additional flexibility to the measurement process, minimising errors and increasing accuracy for otherwise difficult-to-reach areas with an overall increase in measurement volume," it told ISMR.

OctoScan offers wireless functionality and is portable around a production line. It can be integrated with any measurement arm or portable CMM. It has been designed so that parts can be fixed into place in different ways. Built-in magnets and multiple threaded fixing holes allow for an element of bespoke fixing.■
