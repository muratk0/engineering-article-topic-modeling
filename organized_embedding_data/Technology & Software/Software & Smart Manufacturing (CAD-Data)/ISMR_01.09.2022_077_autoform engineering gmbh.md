# AutoForm Engineering GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

AutoForm offers software solutions for sheet-metalforming and Body in White (BiW) assembly processes. With over 400 employees dedicated to this field, AutoForm is a leading provider of software for product manufacturability; tool and material cost calculation; die face design and virtual stamping as well as BiW assembly process optimisation.

"All of the Top 20 automotive OEMs and most of their suppliers have selected AutoForm as their software of choice. Our technology covers the process chain and brings together all the important aspects of sheet metal forming. It also covers the BiW assembly process chain, combining all of the critical aspects of assembling parts. Our software products enable users to expand digitalisation and address complex issues before they arise in production," it said.

Besides its headquarters in Switzerland, AutoForm has offices in Germany; The Netherlands; France; Spain; Italy; Czech Republic; Sweden; USA; Mexico; Brazil; India; China; Japan and Korea.
