# TRACKING & NESTING

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

JETCAM International will be displaying a range of new and updated products at this year’s FABTECH International trade fair (Las Vegas, 11-13 November 2012).

To complement CrossTrack for composites, JETCAM will also release a sheet metal-specific version, providing complete visibility of orders/material stock/nests/machine workloads and full traceability, delivering reduction of errors and time savings. All information is updated in real time across the network, so all staff work from the same live data. CrossTrack works in conjunction with JETCAM Expert nesting software to provide dynamic high performance nesting.

“A completely re-written version of JETCAM Orders Controller Lite (JOC Lite) will also be demonstrated. Available for all JETCAM Expert customers using automatic nesting, JOC Lite provides a quick and clean method of remotely importing orders for parts or complete assemblies for nesting, drawing its new interface design from CrossTrack,” Jetcam told ISMR.

The latest version of JETCAM Expert has new material management features, upgraded remnant sheet management, support for high end part unloading systems and new automatic destruction algorithms, along with other minor enhancements and additions. www.jetcam.com
