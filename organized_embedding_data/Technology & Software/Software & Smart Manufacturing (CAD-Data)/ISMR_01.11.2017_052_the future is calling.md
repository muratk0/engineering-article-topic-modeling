# The future is calling

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

THE BLM GROUP recently participated at Blechexpo (7-10 November 2017 in Stuttgart, Germany). The systems it displayed showcased some key concepts which, it told ISMR, will characterise the manufacturing world of the future and herald the fourth industrial revolution, in the eyes of the BLM Group.

Under its strap line, "The Future Is Calling" recently used during its INTUBE XXVII Open House, the BLM Group interprets Industry 4.0 as the change that will affect the entire manufacturing world, characterised by innovative concepts and, above all, practicality. On display at Blechexpo was its LC5 combined tube and sheet system (laser processing automation with a 6kW fibre laser source); the LT8.10 Fibre (lasertube); the E-TURN 40 tube bender and the LT-FREE 5-axis laser system for processing sheet, boxed sections, drawn materials and tube.

E-TURN is the family of all-electric tube bending systems for making complex shaped parts. The family of machines comes in four models, for tube of up to 30mm (1.125"), 35mm (1.375"), 40mm (1.50") and 52mm (2.00") in diameter and in-process right-hand and left-hand bending. The systems have fixed and variable multi-radius capabilities and integrated loading and unloading systems.

Multiple versions of the LS5 and LC5 for sheet cutting, from 1500 x 3000mm to 2000 x 6000mm, are available with the option of fitting longitudinal or transversal pallet changer systems. A complete range of automation systems, including loading/unloading systems and/or tower pallet storage systems, are available for all. The LS5 and the LC5 are available with CO2 laser sources of power to 4.5kW or fibre laser sources of power up to 6kW.

LT-FREE is the 5-axis laser cutting system that BLM Group developed for operating flexibility and ease of use in the laser cutting of bent tubes, flat and moulded sheet metal, hydroformed components and welded assemblies. A few technical innovations make the system more powerful and efficient, including new 3D graphic programming software, ArtCut, which enables offline programming and simulation of the LT-FREE work cycle, reducing production time and cost and allowing the system to work 24/7. Using a fibre laser source of 1kW and 3kW, LT-FREE can cut a wide range of materials. See www.blmgroup.com
