At EuroBLECH 2024, ARKU will be exhibiting its deburring/leveling technology.

"Drastic time savings are possible, thanks to our ARKU EdgeBreaker® 6000 deburring and edge-rounding machine. Wizard intelligent software. Users input basic data about parts (thickness, material type etc.). The software calculates the optimal processing settings and recommends the correct tools. This means that even novice employees can operate ARKU deburring machines and levellers. During live demonstrations at EuroBLECH, the Wizard will be in operation on an EdgeBreaker®6000 deburring machine. This machine has been designed to meet the needs of laser job shops and is extremely flexible for uniform edge rounding, even up to a 2mm radius," said ARKU.

"Visitors will also see the next generation of ARKU deburring machines for laser-cut and punched parts, saving up to 40% of energy with a sophisticated drive concept. In addition, the ARKU booth will feature the FlatMaster® leveller for stress-relieved, even sheet metal parts," it added.
