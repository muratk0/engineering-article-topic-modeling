# LEVELING TECHNOLOGY IN EIGHT LANGUAGES

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

THE NEW ARKU MASCHINENBAU GMBH website went online recently in eight languages. The website from the German leveling specialist has been completely redesigned. The home page provides a clear overview of the company's two business areas: parts leveling and coil lines.

“The intuitive layout allows visitors to view news, exhibition dates and specialist articles at a single glance. A direct contact option for ARKU service along with an overview of all of the worldwide locations and branches is also available,” ARKU explained.

The "Second Hand Machines" section displays the second hand machine of the month as well as leveler and feeding line components. In addition, the newly designed “References” section features customer statements and requirements together with the solution provided by ARKU.

The website’s "Why Roller Leveling?" section provides visitors with detailed information about the leveling process as well as answers to frequently asked questions concerning leveling technology.

Following the completion of its new 1,600 m² production building in Baden-Baden, ARKU moved into its new leveling center in February. The increased amount of contract leveling it is receiving also increased the need for space for machines and material, it told ISMR. ARKU's new 400m2 workshop for apprentices in Baden-Baden, Germany, houses 12 trainees and is twice the size of the former location.
