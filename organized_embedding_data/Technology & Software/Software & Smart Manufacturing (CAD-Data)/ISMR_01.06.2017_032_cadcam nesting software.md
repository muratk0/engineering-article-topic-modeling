# CAD/CAM nesting software

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, A U.S.-based manufacturer of plasma, fibre laser and waterjet cutting systems, has announced a version update to its ProNest® advanced CAD/CAM nesting software for automated cutting. ProNest 2017 v12.1 contains numerous enhancements designed to enable customers to achieve more. New features include:

Dynamic Align. This allows users to create a connected column or row of rectangular parts, aligned on one side. Aligned parts can be connected with a common line or bridge, then quickly bumped together manually on the nest into dynamic align columns or rows. Dynamic align parts can also be created during automatic nesting.

3D CAD Part Bevel Detection. This identifies bevelled edges on SOLIDWORKS® and Inventor® parts and automatically assigns ProNest bevelling to them during import. Single and multi-pass bevelling is supported.

Output for Multiple Machines. This creates NC output files for one or more additional machines at the same time as output for the current ProNest machine.

"This new version builds upon the capability of ProNest 2017, with the addition of more than a dozen new features and enhancements covering everything from common line cut sequencing to ERP integration and machine interface modules (many of which are suggestions provided by current users)," explained Hypertherm.

"Users with an active software subscription can upgrade to this new version at no additional charge and continue to receive unlimited technical support, and other benefits. Others interested in trying ProNest can obtain more information and request a trial at www.hypertherm.com/CAM."

## Steel Navigator

VISITORS TO SUBCON 2017, the UK's subcontract manufacturing supply chain show dedicated to outsourced engineering services, took part in special interactive demonstrations of Ovako's Steel Navigator. The powerful digital platform allows customers to search and find the most suitable steel for their applications.

"By visiting steelnavigator.ovako.com, users can search from a growing online database of more than 200 steels by standard, mechanical properties, weldability and hardenability, chemical composition and more. Steel Navigator not only lists Ovako grades, such as the attribute brands BQ-Steel®, IQ-Steel®, M-Steel®, WR-Steel® and SZ-Steel®, but also steels supplied by other manufacturers," said the company.

Ovako also gave a seminar entitled 'Green Clean Steel' during day one of Subcon (6 June 2017) at the NEC in Birmingham (UK).

Ovako also showcased its attribute brands including BQ-Steel® for bearing quality, designed to deliver increased fatigue strength, and IQ-Steel® (Isotropic Quality) ultra-clean steels which are designed to offer up to three times the fatigue performance of conventional steels.

M-Steel®, which stands for machinability and is designed to facilitate higher cutting speeds and, conversely, superior tool life, was also on display.
