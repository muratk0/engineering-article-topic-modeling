# Small part inspection

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

OR3D (Stand 19-635), a specialist provider of CAD (computer-aided design) models or inspection reports from laser scan data, is displaying and demonstrating its latest CT scanner at the MACH 2022 exhibition in the UK. The scanner offers critical inspection of small parts.

Computer Tomography (CT) is a powerful technology now being utilised for inspection purposes. It involves the collection of 2D X-Ray slices that are combined to provide a complete 3D dataset. The CT scanner enables the collection of accurate and high-resolution (0.25 micron) scan data. This enables the inspection and reverse engineering of very small and highly detailed components or components where surface scanning technologies cannot capture geometry.

This technique uses X-Ray technology enabling NDT (Non-Destructive Testing) of part assemblies or multi-material components. Additional analysis, such as porosity checks within castings or plastic injected mouldings, offer additional insight.

“CT scanning has many other applications and is not limited to finding defects within a part. The extremely high levels of accuracy mean scanning parts for typical inspections can be completed more accurately and reliably, especially on a small scale," added OR3D.

OR3D, which is partnered with RX Solutions, works with companies worldwide to provide a consultancy service to match requirements to actual needs.
