# Indoor air monitoring

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

THE LATEST ENVIRONMENTAL control solution from Extractability, the ProtectoScan EDI, is an Environment Detection Instrument (EDI) that provides intelligent, data-driven protection in the industrial workplace. An indoor air monitoring instrument that measures and records real-time data for dust particulate, noise, temperature and humidity, EDI has an LED traffic light display. This alerts users to any notable issues, giving users instant feedback and a visual representation of what is going on in their workshop.

"Users will also benefit from EDI email alerts when exposure limits are surpassed. All live data sits at the users' fingertips via WiFi on the dedicated WebApp, turning the EDI into a cloud-based solution, monitoring and recording exposure levels for every second of the day, 365 days per year," the manufacturer told ISMR.

Extractability, a division of Weldability-Sif, can also provide a full report and calibration certificate for formal risk assessment. Extractability will process the data gathered and provide a table and graph, along with a report following an annual service. Leasing options are available for those looking to hire the equipment on a more flexible basis.
