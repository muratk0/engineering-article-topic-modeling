CAD/CAM nesting software contains numerous updates

Hypertherm has released the ProNest 2021 update to its CAD/CAM nesting software for automated cutting. It includes a redesigned 2D CAD package with improved font support and the ability to shape text. Also, users now can set a default CAD program for edits to parts in the ProNest part list.

Automated tube/bar straightener learns part types without operator intervention

Coldwater Machine, a Lincoln Electric Company, has announced the availability of its automated, servo-controlled tube and bar straightening system for carbon steel, stainless, aluminum, copper, and brass. It incorporates artificial intelligence that automatically adapts by learning different part types without requiring operator intervention.

Bundles of tubes are loaded onto a staging deck and then automatically fed to the straightening equipment. In the straightening process, the tube is rotated 360 degrees while sensors measure it at multiple data points along its length to create a profile. This data is sent to the programmable logic controller, where it is compared to the straightness specification to determine where it needs to be corrected.

A servo press, mounted to a servo-controlled rail, is positioned to the indicated location determined by the sensors and then bends the tube repeatedly over its length to bring it within tolerance. This process continues until the straightness requirements are achieved.

Fiber laser features dual beam output

The new Coherent HighLight FL4000CSM-ARM fiber laser enables more advanced welding capabilities for high-thermal-conductivity metals and aligns to welding requirements for emobility, energy storage, and general electrical interconnects comprising dissimilar materials, such as copper and aluminum, and foil stacks that require precise control.

The dual beam output (center beam surrounded by a ring beam) allows welding of thin, conductive materials. The center beam has extremely high brightness (nominal BPP 0.6), allowing a small spot size that provides very high laser intensity and minimizes material heating. The ring beam offers a preheating effect. Together, they produce a melt pool with improved stability, which increases weld consistency.

Strip evaluation system helps user choose adjustments to improve flatness

The FlatTrak CL strip evaluation system from Bradbury uses artificial intelligence to analyze strip condition and provide suggested adjustments to a leveler for processing flat material.

The machine continuously scans the strip surface for shape defects. The video monitor depicts actual shape conditions in real time, allowing the operator to make an informed decision for adjustment to improve flatness. The operator can adjust the leveler and see in real time how the changes affect material shape.

When installed with the e•Drive leveler, the system can adjust the leveler automatically to maintain a flat strip, allowing the operator to focus on other production line tasks.
