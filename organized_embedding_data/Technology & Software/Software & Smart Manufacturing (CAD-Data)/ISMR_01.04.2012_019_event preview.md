“Italian companies exploit to the full the limited size of their companies, successfully penetrating the markets worldwide with their flexibility and quick response. It is no coincidence that one of the strengths of the Italian machine-tool industry is supplying custom-made machines and systems – in other words, machines and systems produced to meet the specific requirements of individual users. As to the quality of products, it is significant that almost 70% of the machine-tools produced in Italy are electronically controlled, and 20% of these form part of integrated systems (cells and systems made up of more than three machines).”

## Forming in focus

Lamiera features its Lambda forming showcase every year - this year is the third version of LAMBDA RTDI-IN-FORMING since the event combines the themes of sustainability (economic, environmental and social) with research and innovation. Its goal is to strengthen the cooperation between universities/research centres and industry.

Key international players in the world of research/innovation, as well as of European research projects, will showcase innovations related to sustainability and demonstrate, with their industrial partners, that SMEs can also be involved in these activities.

At Lamiera, the LAMBDA RTDI-IN-FORMING arena will be arranged with information booths to host the showcase. The following exhibitors will be present:–

■ DII – Department of Industrial Engineering, University of Padua

■ DMTech – University of Bologna

■ EMC2 Factory (European project – 7th Framework Programme)

■ Forum ASAP – Politecnico of Milan

■ Innovanet (innovation network for SMEs – CNA Bologna)

■ Integmicro (European project – 7th Framework Programme)

■ InTime (European project – 7th Framework Programme)

■ Fraunhofer IWU

■ MUSP
