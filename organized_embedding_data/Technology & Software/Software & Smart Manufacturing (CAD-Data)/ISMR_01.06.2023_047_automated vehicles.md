# Automated vehicles

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

At this May's Automate show in Detroit, Michigan (USA), AGV/AMR navigation specialist, BlueBotics, highlighted how its ANT server management software enables the creation of multi-brand fleets. Its team also joined forces with partner, Oceaneering Mobile Robotics, to host a seminar dedicated to building the business case for automated vehicle technology.

The seminar spanned manufacturing and intralogistics, featuring two detailed case studies from the automotive sector.

"As the AGV market grows and continues to mature, more businesses are realizing the need for scalable custom fleets which can incorporate different types and even brands of vehicle," said BlueBotics CEO, Nicolas Tomatis. "Our team was happy to explain to Automate's visitors how the ANT navigation platform (in particular, our ANT server software) can enable that.

"Today the mobile robotics market is thriving due to hiring and retention challenges, and the need to build more productive, competitive and resilient businesses," added Dr. Tomatis.
