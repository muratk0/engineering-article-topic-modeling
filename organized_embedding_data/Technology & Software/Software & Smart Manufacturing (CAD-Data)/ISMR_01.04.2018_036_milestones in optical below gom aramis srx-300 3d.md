WITH THE NEW ARAMIS SRX system, GOM has extended its range of 3D sensors for the dynamic measurement of 3D coordinates, 3D displacements and surface strain. The ARAMIS systems are non-contact and material-independent measuring systems based on Digital Image Correlation (DIC). They provide information on the properties of materials used and behaviour of the resulting parts under load.

"GOM Inspect software enables the import and inspection of CT data. 3D volume models from computer tomographs provide comprehensive data about the inner and outer characteristics of a part. Using GOM Inspect software, you can easily import volume data in all common formats, such as *.vgi, *.vgl, *.pcr, *.exv, *.rek etc., into an established software format and inspect surfaces as usual,” GOM told ISMR.

GOM Inspect is used as evaluation software for 3D measuring data, including 3D inspection and STL mesh processing, for an independent evaluation of measurement results.

In adaptive machining, 3D measurement data from GOM’s ATOS system is already used for automated adjustment of single manufacturing and repair steps. Based on the actual states, CNC programs and travelling paths are calculated so that parts are manufactured according to nominal dimensions. In the long-term, the whole process control could be based on 3D data. A prerequisite is the establishment of standardised interfaces for software and hardware.

In mobile optical 3D measuring systems, GOM offers PONTOS Live as a tracking system for the live positioning of parts designed particularly for production environments. The system, equipped with a GOM Touch Probe, enables the combination of optical with tactile measuring techniques. With PONTOS Live, GOM offers a measuring system for another important step in the manufacturing process; assembly and development of tooling.

“Using 3D metrology during the development phase of the fixture shortens development times, improves production workflows and enhances the profitability of the product,” concluded GOM.

THE IRISH SPECIALIST forklift manufacturer, Combilift, will be exhibiting once again at MACH (Stand H6 252) showing a selection of its range, designed for 'the safe, space-saving and productive handling of the long and bulky, pallets and oversized products'.

"Exhibits will include the relatively new pedestrian trucks which offer an enhanced level of safety for both the operator and other personnel. Available as reach stackers and counterbalance stackers (also with multi-directional capability), they incorporate a key feature: the multi-position tiller arm, developed and patented by Combilift, which enables maximum operator visibility and narrow aisle performance," the manufacturer told ISMR.

Combilift is a large global manufacturer of articulated forklifts and its Aisle Master VNA trucks replace a combination of reach and counterbalance trucks and work in aisles as narrow as 1.6m, lifting to heights of 15m to optimise every inch of storage space in the warehouse. Suitable for indoor and outdoor operation, they load and offload HGVs and direct trailer to racking transportation productivity by reducing timeconsuming double handling. Aisle Masters are available with lift capacities of up to 3t, with LP Gas, diesel or electric power options.

"A model from our original multi-directional C-Series will also be on the stand. Designed for the safe, space-saving and productive handling and storage of long and awkward loads, these work as 'three machines in one' – counterbalance, sideloader and narrow aisle trucks and are tough workhorses for indoor and outdoor operation in all weather conditions," concluded Combilift.
