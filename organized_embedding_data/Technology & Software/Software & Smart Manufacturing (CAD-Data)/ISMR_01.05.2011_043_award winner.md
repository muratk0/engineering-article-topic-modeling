# AWARD WINNER

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM DESIGNS and manufactures advanced cutting systems for use in a variety of industries such as shipbuilding, manufacturing, and automotive repair. Its product line includes handheld and mechanised plasma and laser systems, consumables, as well as CNC motion and height controls and cutting software. The company was recently nominated by Integron BV for its Customer Experience award in the category ‘Most Recommended by Customers’ and won the category.

Integron is an internationally recognised research firm specialising in customer satisfaction, employee satisfaction and user satisfaction research. Every two years, Hypertherm executes a customer satisfaction survey in cooperation with Integron to gain a better understanding of where the company is improving in the eyes of its customers and where it needs more focus. Integron executes hundreds of surveys each year and, for the past two years, has organised the Experience Awards to recognise exceptional results.

“We were already very proud to have been nominated out of hundreds of other companies, but it is of course a truly great feeling to win!” said Theo Cornielje, Hypertherm’s European Director. “Focus on the customer is one of our core values so it is particularly gratifying that we were nominated and won in this category.”
