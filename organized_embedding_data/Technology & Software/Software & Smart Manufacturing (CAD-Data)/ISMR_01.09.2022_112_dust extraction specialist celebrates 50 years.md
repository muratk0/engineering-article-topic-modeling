# Dust extraction specialist celebrates 50 years

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Dustcontrol UK will exhibit a range of its extraction equipment at the Processing and Packaging Machinery Association Show (PPMA) from 27-29 September 2022 (Birmingham, UK), as the company continues to mark 50 years of extraction solutions for the production industry. The Dustcontrol team will be showcasing the firm's range of fixed and mobile cyclone-based dust extractors, air cleaners and a selection of mobile machines on Stand E30.

"The DC11-Module for example, which comes in several models, is a standalone unit for source extraction and industrial cleaning. It has been designed to service up to six normal extraction points or several cleaning outlets at a time and is modularly built, so that it can be tailor-made to suit any manufacturing and production environment. As with all our equipment, the DC11-Module can be fitted with HEPA-13 filters allowing exhaust air to be safely returned to the work environment," it explained.

The company, based in Milton Keynes, develops dust extraction solutions and centralised vacuum systems to fit client requirements in the production industry. Its evolved GFF (Good For Food) range of food and pharma-grade dust extraction accessories and equipment will also be on display. The GFF range is designed to offer dust extraction and cleaning of food contact areas that previously may not have been allowed, while also offering the possibility of recycling.

The Dustcontrol team will also be showcasing its DC AirCube 500; DC AirCube 2000; DC Tromb 240V and DC 1800 EX Stainless Steel. The DC AirCube 2000 is the company's most powerful singlephase air cleaner with a capacity of 1800m³/ hr. It features an energy-saving ECO-fan; a pre-filter; a HEPA H13 microfilter of 10m²; a filter indication lamp and variable speed transmission to save energy consumption. It can also clean air from hazardous particles as small as 0.3 micrometres, including both silica dusts and airborne viruses.

James Miller, Director, Dustcontrol UK, commented: "We supply a complete range of dust collector equipment and accessories for large and small companies across many industries – from aviation and automotive, to metalworking, printing, food and pharmaceuticals."
