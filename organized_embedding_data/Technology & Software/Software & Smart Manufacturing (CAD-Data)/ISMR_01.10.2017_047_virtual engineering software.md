# Virtual engineering software

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

Right: ESI SYSWELD simulates the entire assembly and welding process chain in the body shop, step by step

ESI GROUP, A LEADING innovator in Virtual Prototyping software and services for manufacturing industries, has announced the release of SYSWELD, ESI's software solution for assembly, welding and heat treatment. ESI SYSWELD is Finite Element Analysis (FEA) multiphysics software which simulates thermal joining (arc, electron beam laser, friction stir and spot welding) and heat treatment (carburising, carbonitriding and quenching)

"SYSWELD accurately predicts material characteristics, residual stresses and distortions of structures. By accounting for all relevant manufacturing effects and enabling the transport of simulation results from one manufacturing step to the next, it delivers a truly predictive end-to-end solution for the manufacturing of welded and assembled industrial parts. These capabilities enable manufacturers to reduce product development costs and time-to-market and ensure process automation and optimisation," ESI told /SMR.

With this latest release, ESI has enhanced the welding and heat treatment simulation capabilities of SYSWELD 2017 to secure process feasibility and security, control material characteristics and residual stresses, keep distortion within specified tolerances and improve the performance of the product. The release includes new specific meshing capabilities designed to greatly reduce the time needed to create dedicated meshes for weld and heat treatment

"SYSWELD 2017 also brings new enhancements to simulate assembly in body shops, enabling the modelling of the full stamp-welding-assembly simulation chain for fast distortion engineering in the context of car body manufacturing. Design engineers can now control the dimensional inaccuracies of hot- and cold-joined assemblies by accounting for the mechanical load effects during successive assembly processes, and heat effects induced by welding. This way, engineers can virtually manufacture, assemble and test physically realistic virtual components, long before their hardware prototypes are manufactured. Automotive manufacturers and their suppliers can consequently reduce the cost and delays caused by manufacturing planning, try-out and process validation," added the manufacturer.
