# The transparent press plant

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

WITH ITS MACHINE Monitoring System (MMS), Schuler presented a comprehensive model for system monitoring at the EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover this October. Using comprehensive system monitoring, availability can be increased, production and part quality can be improved and energy consumption can be lowered. The MMS is part of the Smart Press Shop, a collection of Schuler solutions for networking in forming technology. Schuler's model for system monitoring combines a variety of existing solutions. An integral part of the MMS is intelligent diagnostics, which automatically archives and evaluates physical variables and control states when specific events occur. This enables rapid fault analysis.

The state monitoring function monitors the system at regular intervals for damage and wear, using torque curves and structure-borne noise analyses, for example. Components only therefore need to be replaced when they have actually reached the end of their life, and not just because they have been in operation for a specific amount of time.

"When it comes to process monitoring, the focus is on machine protection. Permanent logging of parameters such as press force or vibration progression enables a cycle-accurate response in real-time, where necessary. Process reliability is thereby also improved. Energy monitoring involves recording and evaluating all measured variables which are relevant for energy-efficiency and network quality, such as power consumption, voltage dips or harmonics. This means that not only can system operators save energy costs, in some circumstances they can also obtain investment grants and favourable loans," said Schuler.

The production data acquired includes all planned and unplanned downtimes including their causes, fault messages, target/actual production and the quality of the parts. In this way, system operators gain an overview of the production status and a basis for calculating the Overall Equipment Efficiency (OEE). All quality-relevant data is recorded and archived for each part produced. This enables the manufacturer to provide the necessary proof for items such as safety parts.

With its new operator ID concept, the Press Card, the Schuler Forming Academy has also launched a training programme which provides the skills needed for the costeffective production of high-quality parts.

The training programme consists of three qualification modules, each one lasts two days and concludes with a test. Participants that successfully pass all three modules receive a visible qualification award: The Schuler Press Card.

The Schuler service app

Expert assistance is now available right at the machine – thanks to the new, free service app from Schuler. Customers can quickly obtain answers to their questions and help in solving a problem via their smartphones, as experts at Schuler will assess images and videos, for example, sent to them via the app. The software is part of the Smart Press Shop concept which Schuler used to present solutions for networking in forming technology at the EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover. The service app from Schuler is available now, free of charge, as a download for Android phones and iPhones.

## PRODUCT REVIEWS

## TRUMPF launched a range of new systems at EuroBLECH 2016

The synchronous interaction of the individual processes is the result of near-fully-automatic programming via push-button

## Cutting

At EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover this October, TRUMPF presented a new, freshly developed, fully automated and compact machine concept. The TruLaserCenter 7030, for the manufacture of laser-cut parts, works with a hybrid drive system to meet the ambitious development goal of eliminating obstacles to smooth processing in laser cutting today. These include shutdowns due to collisions involving parts tilting over, refinishing work on microjoints, spatter on the undersides of components and excessive programming work.

With the TruLaser Center 7030, TRUMPF is addressing sheet metal fabricators from all sectors who cut sheets (from 1-12mm thick) at high capacity. The TruLaser Center 7030 features a TruDisk solid-state laser, with six kilowatts of laser power.

"The TruLaser Center 7030 offers extremely efficient, automated and synchronous processes, from its programming to sorted and stacked workpieces. It moves the sheet and the cutting head simultaneously, and the slats have now been replaced by brush tables. The machine can eject small parts safely and sort them into containers. It also disposes of residue and slag; sorts and stacks larger parts during machining; can load itself with blank sheets on a very tight space and stacks scrap skeletons. The programming is also largely automatic," TRUMPF told SMR.

TRUMPF developers also invented the SmartGate: two slides which move synchronously with the cutting head. The sheet is therefore securely supported during the cutting process and small parts measuring up to 160mm x 160mm can be simultaneously ejected downwards. For automatic removal of the remaining parts from the scrap skeleton, TRUMPF experts developed SmartLift and SortMaster Speed.

"The Performance Cockpit gives an overview of relevant production data. The Dot Matrix Code supports connection to various processing technologies, and creates transparency during part tracking. The MobileControl app reduces the already low attendance of the operator to a minimum via remote control," said TRUMPF.

## Punching and cutting

The new TRUMPF TruPunch 1000 is a punching machine for the entry-level segment that can gradually be expanded into a fully-fledged combination machine. The result is the TruMatic 1000 fiber, a laser machine equipped with a host of innovative features that punches holes, bends flanges and forms threads.

"With its expandable functionality, the TruPunch 1000 can handle sheets of up to 6.4mm thick at rates of up to 600 strokes per minute, yet is remarkably compact. With a footprint of just 6.5 x 4.9m, the TruPunch 1000 stand-alone machine is around 15 percent smaller than its predecessor. Thanks to its novel modular design, the TruPunch 1000 can be retrofitted with a laser cutting system, a laser evacuation unit and a beam guard system," explained TRUMPF.

A 3kW TruDisk solid-state laser can be connected up to convert the TruPunch 1000 punching machine into a punch laser machine. This is the first time that TRUMPF has offered a combination machine in the entry-level segment, a move that makes it easier for customers to make the switch from purely 2D laser processing to punch laser technology.

The patented "Delta Drive" eliminates the need to move the sheet and work table in the y-axis - normally an integral requirement of sheet metal processing. It achieves this by making the punching head quickly manoeuvre in that direction.

## Laser welding

At EuroBLECH 2016, TRUMPF also showcased the TruLaser Weld 5000, a new laser welding cell designed to simplify the laser welding process. Its FusionLine option is a laser welding process using welding wire supply that allows for the welding of components with gaps.

"FusionLine makes it possible to compensate for component imperfections in the welding process and even to close gaps of up to 1mm in breadth. It is now possible to laser weld an array of parts developed for conventional welding without having to modify them specifically for the purpose. This enables users to manufacture parts true to the drawing, and also more types of parts than before," TRUMPF told SMR.

Users are free to switch between FusionLine and the conventional laser welding techniques of heat conduction and deep welding, with no need to reset the machine. The new TruLaser Weld 5000 also features a movable nozzle that rotates around the optics. The result is faster welding times, since the robot does not have to reorient itself as much. This also simplifies programming and fixture design.

## TruConnect and AXOOM

With TruConnect, at EuroBLECH 2016, TRUMPF presented innovative solutions on the path to Industry 4.0 in sheet metal processing. The concept for networked production links machines, people and information - for example, via its new data output interface, Central Link, which provides information about the production status of machines. Customers can use it to integrate web applications, platforms and local systems as well.

It is also the basis for more transparency in production control and plant planning. It transmits production status information to local ERP systems and also, with high data security, to cloud-based solutions - for example, to the business platform AXOOM from the TRUMPF

Group. Central Link is a key component of TruConnect. TruConnect services are also available to other manufacturing companies via the AXOOM business platform.

As a third pillar of its Industry 4.0 strategy, TRUMPF is leveraging digital connectivity in its own value chains, creating more flagship companies in which customers can experience connected production – for example, by taking a Smart Factory Experience tour. At EuroBLECH, AXOOM introduced several new apps.

## Panel bending

Panel bending with high part flexibility is now even faster with the TruBend Center 7030, TRUMPF's new and fully automatic bending cell. The machine turns the parts automatically freeing up the operator and increasing the productivity of the bending process. A laser-based ACB angle measurement system enables operators to work with greater precision and, in conjunction with TRUMPF's TecZone Fold offline programming tool, more efficiently.

Using the new rotary part manipulator, the TruBend Center 7030 can bend parts fully automatically. With its TruBend Center 7030, TRUMPF offers a fully automatic panel bending machine with an additional vertical axis for the 2-axis part manipulator. This makes for real flexibility, since the gripper modules can fasten onto parts in a variety of positions, making it possible, for example, to secure narrow profiles on their edge or grip them with clamps and bend them.

The TruBend Center 7030 is designed to process a wide range of shapes and formats, including sheets of aluminium of up to 4mm thick, mild steel of up to 3mm thick or stainless steel of up to 2.2mm thick. The machine handles extremely short side lengths, boxes of up to 220mm deep, small profiles, narrow or negative bends, and diverse radii.

## 3D cutting

TRUMPF has completed a major revamp of the second generation of its TruLaser Cell 8030 hot-forming machine. At EuroBLECH, the company demonstrated new functions that can increase the productivity of the machine by up to 20 per cent. The focus was also on x-blast technology, which greatly reduces machine downtimes and collisions between cutting nozzle and workpiece. The main field of application for the re-engineered TruLaser Cell 8030 continues to be the laser cutting of hot-formed 3D components, a technology that is primarily used in the automotive industry.

TRUMPF has also expanded its optical slug detection feature for quality control. ObserveLine Comfort detects cutting slugs, increasing production dependability.

Another new feature is SmartApproach, which quickly calculates the actual height of the component edge; the laser ignites the beam even before it is positioned over the workpiece and then swiftly executes the cutting contour. SmartApproach increases the productivity of the machine by up to nine per cent and the additional dynamism of the drives yields a further eleven per cent, according to TRUMPF.
