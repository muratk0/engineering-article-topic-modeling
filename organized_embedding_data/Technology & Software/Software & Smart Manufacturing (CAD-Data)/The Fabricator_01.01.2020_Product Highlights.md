Subscription pricing model introduced for nesting software

Hypertherm has announced subscription pricing for its ProNest

®

LT CAD/CAM nesting software for light industrial cutting applications. Rather than purchasing the software outright, as is typically required, smaller companies and individuals who have air or conventional plasma and oxyfuel cutting machines can subscribe to the software on a monthly, annual, or three-year basis.

Subscribers get access to one-on-one live support, as well as online resources such as video tutorials. Cutting parameters based on the specific material type and thickness are included with the software setup, providing optimal part separations, leads, and kerf compensation for the job, says the company. Advanced automatic nesting also is available to help ensure good material yield for larger jobs.

Subscribers can start a subscription, add or remove users or features when needed, and cancel anytime. A free 7-day trial subscription also is available.

Angle grinder interface speeds accessory changes

Bosch has released the X-Lock interface for angle grinders. It offers a tool-free grinder wheel change that eliminates the need for spanner wrenches or flange nuts. An audible snap indicates a secure, proper connection when an X-Lock wheel is mounted on an X-Lock grinder. The wheel is released from the grinder with the pull of a lever, which the company says is up to five times faster than a conventional interface.

Available accessories include bonded wheels, diamond wheels, flaps discs, wire wheels and cups, backing pads, and fiber discs. Most are backward-compatible with standard ⅞-in. mounts.

Abrasive grain triangles penetrate workpiece using little energy

VICTOGRAIN

®

, offered by PFERD, is an abrasive grain with precision-formed abrasive grain triangles that are identical in shape and size. Their cutting edges are applied to the workpiece at the optimum angle so that each grain needs very little energy to penetrate the workpiece, the company states.

Each abrasive grain triangle is firmly fixed on one of its sides to the backing material. The very fine crystalline structure helps ensure optimal wear as sharp cutting edges are always exposed, with the minimum amount of abrasive breaking off from the triangle.

Copper-plated magnetic welding squares resist spatter

Industrial Magnetics Inc. has introduced a new line of copper-plated magnetic welding squares. Because of the highly conductive properties of copper, the heat from small particles of weld spatter that hit the face of the welding square is quickly conducted away, preventing bonding of the materials. This keeps the sides and edges of the square clean, true, and free from spatter buildup, protecting the accuracy of its alignment.

The squares are available in four models for holding sheet stock or tubing at 30, 45, 60, or 90 degrees. Mounting holes are provided for fixturing the squares into jigs, and a large finger hole is provided for setup and removal of the square from a metal surface.

Digitally controlled plasma cutting unit ready for Industry 4.0

Kjellberg Finsterwalde has introduced its first Industry 4.0-capable series of plasma cutting systems. With the newly developed inverter power source for the Q, the cutting process is controlled digitally through real-time communication between the components and monitored by a variety of sensors on the basis of process and status indicators.

A browser-based user interface, the Q-Desk, displays the machine, process, and consumption information to the user and provides forecasts and recommendations for action. The interface also provides the user with additional information independent of device and location, such as FAQs and video tutorials. The process information can be made available to a larger machine network to ensure interoperability with other systems such as extraction systems or to provide comprehensive cost monitoring.
