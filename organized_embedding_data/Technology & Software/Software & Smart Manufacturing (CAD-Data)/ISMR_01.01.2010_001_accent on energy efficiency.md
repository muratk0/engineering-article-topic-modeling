# ACCENT ON ENERGY EFFICIENCY

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

At METAV 2010 (23 - 27 February 2010 in Düsseldorf), 15 machine tool manufacturing companies and their cooperation partners will be presenting solutions for improving the energy-efficiency of machine tools, as part of the “Blue Competence® – Taking the initiative on energy and environment” campaign.

The background to this is the decision taken by the European Commission to put machine tools on the list of products requiring examination under the EuP Directive (2009/125/EG). Secondly, the aim is to develop awareness for responsible husbandry of natural resources, not least among the users of production technology, and to join forces in formulating new approaches and achieving continual improvements.

The exhibitors involved will be presenting not only individual aspects on the component level, such as energy recovery and efficient drive technology, but also energy-efficient process developments, and design-enhanced machinery concepts. The joint booth is to be found in Hall 16, Booth B9 at METAV.

The symposium aims to provide fruitful insights into energy saving options for users and manufacturers of production machinery. To complement the joint booth, the Institute for Production Management, Technology and Machine Tools (PTW) in Darmstadt has joined forces with the VDW to organise a symposium on 24 February 2010 entitled “Energy-efficient machine tools - requirements and solutions for effective production technology”.
