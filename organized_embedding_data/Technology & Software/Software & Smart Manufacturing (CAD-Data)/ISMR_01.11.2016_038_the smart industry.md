# THE SMART INDUSTRY

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

With new technology, Dutch bending specialist SafanDarley is paving the way for smarter manufacturing in the ‘smart industry'.

With new technology, Dutch bending specialist, SafanDarley, is paving the way for smarter manufacturing in the 'smart industry'. The smart industry, it told /SMR, is a way of working that enables industrial companies to maximise their use of the latest information and technological developments. This can be achieved by far-reaching digitisation and the linking of devices, production resources and organisations to create a new, integrated method of manufacturing.

“For us, this means manufacturing more efficiently, flexibly, to a higher quality and tailor-made. In concrete terms, 'smart manufacturing'means improvement of your OEE, or Overall Equipment Effectiveness," explained the company.

With this in mind, SafanDarley presented four significant new product innovations in sheet metal forming at this October's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover.

## New innovations

In concrete terms, ‘smart manufacturing' means improvement of your OEE, or Overall Equipment Effectiveness.

The first SafanDarley global premiere was the E-Brake 200T 4100 ATC. Equipped with an automatic tool changer, it is designed to be a flexible solution for small batch production. The servo-electric press brake features a 200-ton pressing force and a working length of 4100mm. The tool changer is integrated into the machine frame, resulting in a footprint that is no larger than the standard E-Brake. The machine and tool changer can be programmed in the SafanDarley E control or via external software systems, such as Autopol and Radan.

"Even when a machine has ATC (Automatic Tool Change), it is possible to move loaded tools manually. If the WILA TIPS option is chosen, the tool-change robot can also recognise tools which have been moved manually and put those tools back into the magazine of the machine. This makes the SafanDarley tool changer unique and results in significant increases in efficiency," Wim van Kranen, SafanDarley's sales and marketing director, told ISMR.

"We developed this machine with the tool changer (ATC) in less than one year. The tool changer helps customers who struggle with high mix, low volume products in their factories. The tooling communicates with the robot and the control. The tools are equipped with an RFID chip so that the robot always knows where it has placed the tools. The chips can be read in the clamping system so that the machine always knows."

The tool magazine can, as standard, store two full working lengths of upper tools and three full working lengths of lower tools. The tools are placed in a secure place, preventing any damage or contamination. As an option, storage capacity can be expanded. At EuroBLECH 2016, SafanDarley also launched a new generation of its E-Brake, called Ultra. It sold the first few machines to some Dutch customers at the EuroBLECH exhibition.

"The Ultra series has a new design and is equipped with CNC crowning. This new series is available in several sizes – 50 tons x 2 metres, 80 tons x 2.5 metres, 100 tons x 3 metres and 130 tons with a working length of either 3 metres or 4 metres," van Kranen told ISMR. "The Ultra has, as standard, a dualscreen monitor and is therefore able to create a paperless working environment. Optionally, the E-Brake Ultra can be equipped with a 3D back gauge and the E-Bend L Blue laser angle- measurement system."

The third premiere for the Dutch manufacturer at EuroBLECH was its new energy-saving H-Series Hybrid press brake. These H-Brakes are equipped with an innovative hybrid drive (a combination of hydraulics and electronics). The system is designed to deliver huge energy savings, with very low noise levels, and uses only a fraction of the usual amount of oil.

"The new hybrid drive combines economy with high precision and performance. The hybrid drive is only operational when actively bending. Because of its highly dynamic Y-axis system, with approach speed and return speed of 230mm/sec., cycle times are typically 30% less than traditional hydraulic press brakes. This hybrid drive principle also ensures lower operating costs, because the oil lasts longer in the hybrid system and oil change and routine service intervals are typically 7000 hours," said van Kranen.

The H-Brake Hybrid series includes 110 tons x 3 metres and 170 tons x 3 metres (3.7 metres and 4 metres). The machines are equipped with a large Q-dimension of 655mm and a stroke of 280mm. The Hybrid has a standard gap of 510mm.

Other machines from the company's standard range, which were also on display at its EuroBLECH booth, included the E-Brake 40T-1600 Ergonomic; E-Brake B100-3100 edition; H-Brake 320T-420 with E-Bend L Blue and M-Shear 310-6 with 'return to sender' functionality.

## Smart control

The fourth premiere for SafanDarley at EuroBLECH was its E-control 40 Smart software innovation.

"In addition to EC20 functionality, the EC40 Smart has the ability to read-in 3D drawings, unfold and carry out a bending simulation. Through 'docking', the system can be synchronised with the machine control allowing data and the program to be accessed in the EC20 and produced on the machine. This enables programming in 3D at the machine or at any other location," explained van Kranen.

This limits the time during which the machine is at a standstill, thus maximising productivity. The software will automatically determine the correct results, best bending sequence, stop positions and tool set-up with as few manipulations as possible based on an imported 3D model. Users also have the option of adjusting the bending sequence and stop positions manually.

"With Industry 4.0, we are entering the digital world. Our controls are already Industry 4.0-ready, transmitting data and connecting to ERP systems, shop floor controls etc. We develop our own controls for the machines. In our opinion, the provision of integrated manufacturing solutions will play an increasingly important role. This will mainly concern the integration of machine controls, automation and communication," explained van Kranen.

At EuroBLECH, SafanDarley also demonstrated its SafanDarley Eye system in combination with the Microsoft Hololens. By using augmented reality, said the manufacturer, productivity can be increased. The SafanDarley Eye shows additional information from the press brake to the operator, via the Hololens, and fits snugly within the Industry 4.0 digital remit.

"SafanDarley Eye is frankly amazing," van Kranen told ISMR. "We created software for augmented reality, which means that we add extra images onto the glasses that the operator wears. On starting the bending process, the operator can already see which steps he has to make, as well as the bent parts, back gauge and any other relevant imagery - we add all these images to his glasses. He can also see several products in his glasses, for example, and select one using voice control so that the program for that part is automatically loaded.

"When he starts bending, the bent product and the bending steps will appear literally in front of him. We started this development four years ago but could not find the right hardware device to display this information. We have now chosen the Microsoft Hololens as the right device for this application. It's a very advanced new system and perfect for Industry 4.0."
