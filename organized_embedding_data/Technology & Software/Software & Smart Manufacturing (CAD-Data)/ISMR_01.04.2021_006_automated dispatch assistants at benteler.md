# Automated dispatch assistants at BENTELER

[TARİH: 01.04.2021 ISMR]

BENTELER is increasingly driving the digitalisation of production sites as part of its own Industry 4.0 strategy. In the dispatch centre at its Schloss Neuhaus site in Germany, intra-logistic operational processes are automated and made even more productive with automatic guided vehicles (AGVs). This automation also reduces transport damage and increases occupational safety.

"The automatic guided vehicles are completely automatically controlled forklifts. Without the intervention of an employee they take over the transport of dispatch units and the lifting of loads into our rack storage," explained Thomas Michels, head of the Schloss Neuhaus and Paderborn Plant Network. "This saves waiting times at the machines, increases productivity and improves the safety of our employees. With the automated dispatch assistants, BENTELER is taking a big step towards a digital future."

Following a successful test phase in three-shift operation, the system has proved itself in everyday usage. The scope of application of the AGVs has been extended with new functions; production scrap is now disposed of fully automatically. At peak periods, up to 250 trips are made per day and 100 tons are moved. The vehicles can lift loads of two tons at heights of up to five metres automatically.

"We have continuously improved the system over the past months. At locations susceptible to failure, the AGV's track guidance is now provided by magnetic points," explained Felix Schmidt, project manager for the introduction of the AGVs. "This type of navigation is new and much less sensitive than the optical version."

"The new equipment consists of a dual cone uncoiler; rotating brush dust collector system; thread system; 40' (12,000mm) stretcher leveller; servo roll feed; variable rake shear with quick change blades and a heavy gauge drop stacker with new high-capacity floor conveyors," Red Bud Industries told ISMR. Mississippi Steel Processing (MSP) handles a wide range of hot rolled steel on cut-to-length levelling and slitting lines. Its facility in Columbus is 300,000 ft², including 84,000 ft² master coil storage, 134,000 ft² finished goods storage and 7200 ft² of office space. A major expansion in 2014 expanded the original 2010 facility by 67% and added significant new processing capacity to shorten lead times and handle heavier gauge material. Liberty Steel Products, Inc., located in North Jackson, Ohio, is the controlling partner of MSP.

"Our intelligent automation makes inventory, job status, QC and shipment information immediately available to our customers," added MSP.
