# K55 pallet stacker AGV

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

At this year's LogiMAT exhibition in Stuttgart (25-27 April 2023), Kivnon will present and demonstrate its K55 Pallet Stacker AGV on booth 6C05 in Hall 6.

"The vehicle is an ideal solution for end-of-line palletising, food and beverage, and retail applications. It is a real workhorse that will carry out mundane manual tasks efficiently and reliably. Our range of AGVs is designed for robustness, safety and accuracy, and this latest addition to the portfolio provides customers with a vehicle that can automatically transport stack pallets," said Kivnon.

Equipped with lifting forks, the K55 Pallet Stacker will automatically transport palletised loads of up to 1200kg and can lift to a height of up to 1500mm. The system can also be customised to meet specific application goals.

The K55 uses mapping navigation to perform cyclic or conditioned routes and can interact with other site vehicles, machines and systems for a coordinated, site-wide logistics solution.

"Security scanners ensure safe operation in shared spaces and an online battery charging system means units can operate 24/7 without interruption," explained Kivnon.

Kivnon offers a wide range of autonomous vehicles (AGVs/ AMRs) and accessories for transporting goods, using magnetic navigation or mapping technologies that adapt to various environments and sectors. The company offers an integrated solution with a wide range of mobile robotics solutions automating different applications in the automotive; food and beverage; logistics and warehousing; manufacturing and aeronautics industries.
