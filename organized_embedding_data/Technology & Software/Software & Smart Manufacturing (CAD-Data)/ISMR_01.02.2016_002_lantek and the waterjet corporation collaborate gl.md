# Lantek and the Waterjet Corporation collaborate globally

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

LANTEK HAS reached a global agreement to supply its Lantek Expert CAD/CAM software with the Waterjet Corporation’s range of waterjet cutting machines.

Waterjet Corporation has been building and supplying thousands of machines since 1991 from its headquarters and manufacturing facilities in Monza, Italy. Since then, it has developed an extensive range of standard and high-tech customised waterjet machines offered through regional offices and a dealer network and global branches in the Far East, North America, Northern Europe and the Middle East. Its machines include cantilever and bridge designs and an exclusive 5-axis flying bridge technology (Edge 5) which delivers significant productivity improvements. The company also offers combination plasma and waterjet machines and waterjet surface texturing technology.

“We have managed to satisfy the technological needs of Waterjet Corporation to obtain high quality cutting and use the same software for cutting tubes and versatility in 5-axis cutting,” commented Lantek.

“By working with us across all our global offices, the Waterjet Corporation will be able to benefit from a CAD/CAM system that has been tailored to suit each machine in its range and which will maximise the productivity possible on those machines. We have been developing CAD/CAM systems for the sheet metal industry since 1986 and now have more than 16,000 customers worldwide, offices in 15 countries and a global distribution network,” Lantek explained.
