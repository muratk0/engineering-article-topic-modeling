MES software designed for plant product management

BLM GROUP USA has revamped its ProTube Enterprise manufacturing execution system (MES) software and rebranded it as Prometheus. It now extends beyond the company’s laser tube cutting machines by adding compatibility with its laser cutting systems for sheet metal.

The software allows these laser cutting machines to be integrated seamlessly with all machines on the floor. It also is compatible with any customer software and ERP systems the machine may be using, according to the manufacturer. Job management for all machines on the floor is streamlined into a single planning system with a single monitoring dashboard.

The inclusion of a live efficiency analysis system allows estimates based on real statistical data to be provided for new job orders, helping to ensure accurate timeframes and cost estimates for production.

Electronic press brakes offer customizable energy efficiency

As the inventor of the electronic press brake, Safan Darley offers a variety of E-Brake systems in tonnages from 39 to 330. According to the company, its machines offer energy savings up to 70%, shorter cycle times with up to 30% more productivity, and high machine uptime.

The press brakes can be configured with options such as measuring systems and bending aids to customize bending capabilities for the user’s specifications and requirements.

The modular electronic press brakes also can be expanded with the company’s Robo Mate to create a lights-out production environment.

Plasma cutting system increases cutting speed in 2-in. mild steel

Hypertherm Associates has released the XPR460 plasma cutting system for mild steel, stainless steel, and aluminum. The system performs multiple applications with SureCut technology for boltready holes and smooth 45-degree bevels, the company reports.

Suitable for XY and robotic cutting applications, the system increases cutting speed by an average of 12% through 2-in. mild steel. It also pierces up to 2.5-in. mild steel.

Ramp-down error protection helps increase realized consumable life, and torch protection helps reduce the impact of catastrophic electrode blowouts, which can damage the torch at high current levels.

Storage rack handles round stock in a compact space

Lean Manufacturing Products offers the Lean Round Stock Racking System, a cartridge-style system designed to accommodate a variety of round stock of different sizes and volumes. Its flexible design allows for the use of interchangeable adapters on the cartridges, which can be customized based on the materials being stored.

The cartridge-style design minimizes the footprint required for storage, freeing up floor space in a facility.

CAD/CAM software updated

Jetcam Intl. has announced the release of version 24.05.01 of its Jetcam Expert CAD/CAM software for sheet metal fabrication. It is available in both 32-bit and 64-bit versions.

According to the company, the software provides up to 80% performance enhancement when generating NC code after nesting and delivers other performance gains when working with extremely complex/dense nests.

This release offers skeleton parting tooling functionality for punch and punch/laser machines; automatic remnant cutoff capability for laser, punch, and combination machines; improved nesting algorithms and automation features; and enhanced installation options suitable for enterprise security IT requirements.
