# Sheet metal innovations

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

During EuroBLECH, SafanDarley will demonstrate the new SafanDarley Eye system in combination with the Microsoft Hololens. Using Augmented Reality, productivity can be increased.

AT EUROBLECH 2016 in Hanover, from 25-29 October 2016, SafanDarley will present four product innovations for sheet metal forming on its stand H14 in hall 14.

The E-Brake 200T 4100 ATC press brake, for bending sheet metal, is equipped with an automatic tool changer and is designed as a flexible solution for small batches. A 200-ton press force and a working length of 4100mm are the basis of this servo-electric press brake. The tool changer is integrated into the machine frame, resulting in a footprint no bigger than the standard E-Brake. The machine and tool changer can be programmed in the SafanDarley E control or via external systems, such as Autopol and Radan.

"Press brake tooling can also be loaded manually, if required. If the WILA TIPS option is chosen, the tool-change robot can also recognise tools which have been placed manually and put those tools back into the machine's magazine. This makes the SafanDarley tool changer unique and results in significant increases in efficiency. This model joins the E-Brake 150T-3100 ATC, launched earlier this year, to complete the range," the manufacturer explained.

SafanDarley will also showcase a new generation of the E-Brake, called Ultra. The Ultra series boasts a new design as well as CNC crowning. This new series is available in several sizes and features a dual-screen monitor, helping to create a paper-less environment. Optionally, the E-Brake Ultra can also be equipped with a 3D back gauge and the E-Bend L Blue laser angle measurement system.

During EuroBLECH, SafanDarley will also introduce its new energy-saving press brake, the H-Brake Hybrid. H-brakes are equipped with an innovative hybrid drive, a combination of hydraulics and electronics. The hybrid drive is only operational when actively bending.

"Because of its highly dynamic Y-axis system, with approach speed and return speed of 230mm/second, cycle times are typically 30% less than traditional hydraulic press brakes. The hybrid drive principle also ensures lower operating costs, because the oil lasts longer in the hybrid system. Oil change and routine service intervals are typically 7000 hours. The system delivers huge energy savings, has very low-noise levels and uses only a fraction of the usual amount of oil," SafanDarley told ISMR.

The new E-control 40 Smart, the latest software innovation from SafanDarley, will also be on display at EuroBLECH 2016. In addition to EC20 functionality, the EC40 Smart has the ability to draw on a mobile device 3D models (Designer), to read-in 3D drawings and to unfold as well as carry out a bending simulation. The system can be synchronised with the machine control, allowing data and the programme to be accessed in the EC20 and produced on the machine. This enables programming in 3D at the machine or at any other location. See www.safandarley.com
