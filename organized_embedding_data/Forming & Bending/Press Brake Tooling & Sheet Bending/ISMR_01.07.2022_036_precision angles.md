The precision bending of sheet metals, which are then treated and used in a variety of industries. Press brakes form pre-programmed bends between the die and the punch. The press brake's bending force, known as tonnage, determines how much pressure can be applied by the punch during a bend. Higher tonnage allows for bending thicker and harder metals, and lower tonnage is most appropriate for thinner metals.

There are several types of press brake (such as pneumatic, servo electric, hydraulic and mechanical). The workpiece is clamped between matching punch and die to form a pre-set bend. The characterisation of capacity of a press brake machine is based on parameters such as working length; working height; amplitude; stroke; tonnage and distance between side housings or frame uprights.

According to analyst Technavio, press brakes global market share is expected to increase to US$ 474.76 million from 2021 to 2026, and the market's growth momentum will accelerate at a CAGR of 5.11%. Increasing demand for fabricated metal products is one of the key press brake market trends that is expected to positively impact the industry over the forecast period.

“Around 39% of the market's growth will originate from the APAC region during the forecast period. China, Japan and India are key markets for press brakes in APAC. Market growth in this region will be faster than the growth of the market in North America. Press brake market share growth by the hydraulic segment will be significant during the forecast period. These [types of] press brakes are driven by two synchronised hydraulic cylinders and offer benefits such as quick retraction; variable speed control as well as the capability to reverse the stroke at any time," commented the analyst.
