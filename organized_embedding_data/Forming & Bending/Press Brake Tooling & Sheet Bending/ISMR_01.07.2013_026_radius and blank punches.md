# RADIUS AND BLANK PUNCHES

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

Urethane Tooling & Engineering Corp. has launched a new product line of aluminium and urethane radius and blank punches for forming development and tryout. The products, designed to work with the Tool-A-Thane Urethane press brake die retainers and springback development tools, are available in radii of 1/64” to 12-7/8”. Punches and blanks can be bought individually or in pre-determined sets. Custom sizes are quoted upon request.

“This new product line is specifically designed and made to work with our springback development tools, retainers and forming pads, to allow our customers to have economical, long-lasting and accurate tools for use in their press brakes,” said Knight Farwell, UTEC President.
