# Press brake award

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

JEAN PERROT FRANCE has developed its high speed and high precision Maneo Premium LCS press brake, featuring the award-winning Laser Correction System (LCS). The system received the innovation award at Tolexpo 2015 (Paris).

Developed by Pinette Emidecau Industries, the parent company of Jean Perrot, the patented LCS system is able to handle bending angles from 80° to 160°. The bend angle is measured and corrected at ultra-high speed, without contact, giving, according to the manufacturer, a guaranteed correct first-time bend. This equipment has been developed primarily for use with robotised bending cells but is also suitable for more conventional CNC press brakes, especially when high precision productivity is required. The system has also been successfully installed on tandem press brakes.

Jean Perrot France has been designing and building heavy duty press brakes since 1962. Many of these custom-built machines are designed to the specific needs of its customers. It operates in a dynamic ISO 9001 environment (version 2008) with a staff of 120 employees, including 90 experienced engineers and technicians. TRUMPF will be presenting an array of new laser systems and products at the LASYS trade fair in Stuttgart, starting on 31 May 2016. The company will be focusing on technologies and solutions for the additive manufacturing of metal parts, also known as 3D printing.

“TRUMPF is the only manufacturer in the world to offer both key technologies in this field: Laser Metal Fusion (LMF) and Laser Metal Deposition (LMD). We carefully consider the specific part and application in each case to decide which is the most suitable method for each customer,” said TRUMPF Laser- und Systemtechnik GmbH. “Our new TruPrint 1000 LMF system allows us to create high quality parts with delicate and geometrically complex structures for lightweight design and other uses. And if a customer has a hybrid application and wants to apply new structures and shapes to existing components, we can come up with a creative solution using our versatile TruLaser Cell 3000 machine and the new LMD package.”

TRUMPF has also developed new solutions for welding copper. As an excellent conductor of both heat and electricity, this versatile metal is a hugely important raw material, especially for the electronics industry.

“More and more companies are using infrared lasers to weld copper, but the problem is that copper is highly reflective at a wavelength of 1,000 nanometers. That leads to spatter which can damage the part and – in the worst-case scenario – cause short-circuits on the board.”

This problem is solved by the TruDisk Pulse 421. The laser works in pulsed mode, emitting laser light in the green spectrum at 515 nanometres. This results in far less spattering during the welding process – regardless of the nature of the surface, said TRUMPF.
