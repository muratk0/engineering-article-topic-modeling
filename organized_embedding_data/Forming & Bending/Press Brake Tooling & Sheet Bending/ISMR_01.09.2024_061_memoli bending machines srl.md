Italian manufacturer, MEMOLI Bending Machines Srl, has been manufacturing tube bending machines for more than 55 years.

“Each Memoli machine is a unique and customised product, specifically designed to fulfil every single customer's needs and resulting from the multiple possible combinations of models, sizes and equipment. We also offer a consultancy service through our internal technical department, which is always available to propose the best possible solution. Our goal is actually our customer's complete satisfaction. 'Made in Italy' products are a guarantee: high quality and security standards," it said.

"Our ETM-H range of semi-automatic hybrid tube benders is ideal for the machining of thin tubes without deformation of the processed tube: a compact, high-performance and economical solution. In semi-automatic bending machines in the ETM-H series, the bending function is performed automatically while the feeding and rotation of the tube between the bends must be done manually," it added.
