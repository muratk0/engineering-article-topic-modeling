# Folding with the FORMA Z

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Various techniques such as press brakes, manual Up-folding and fully automatic bending lines offer different benefits. The semi-automatic Up/Down-folding capabilities of CIDAN's FORMA Z have been designed with operational efficiency and ergonomic design in mind to enhance productivity and operator comfort.

"The cost-effectiveness of a sheet metal forming machine hinges on several crucial parameters: operational and manual handling costs, machine price and production capacity. The CIDAN FORMA Z excels in reducing labour costs because it requires minimal handling and operators mainly reposition and turn sheets without pausing the machine. This streamlined process not only speeds up production but also reduces fatigue, allowing a single operator to efficiently manage the workflow from blank to finished part," CIDAN Machinery told ISMR.

Strategic design features, such as the front gauging function of the folding beam and an accessible back-gauge equipped with vacuum grippers, simplify programming and set-up thereby minimising tool changes. This is particularly beneficial, explained CIDAN, for short-batch production.

"The FORMA Z's rapid operation and multiple simultaneous movements ensure a very low operational cost per part, making it an ideal choice for both low and high-volume production needs," continued CIDAN.

## An eye on the operator

The FORMA Z is designed with the operator's ease in mind. Standard front gauging aligns with familiar press brake operations, reducing the learning curve. The movement of the folding beam directly defines the bend angle, simplifying the programming process. The machine's software, developed by CIDAN Machinery, has a user-friendly interface and is suitable for upgrades, enhancing long-term operational efficiency.

"Operating the FORMA Z does not involve strenuous physical effort. The machine's design focuses on ergonomic principles such as optimal height positioning and Up/ Down folding that eliminates the need to flip heavy sheets. Features like a deep and wide cut-out in the back-gauge and compact placement of vacuum grippers ensure easy access and minimise the risk of sheet jamming or operator injury," outlined CIDAN.

## A flexible focus

No tool changes are required when adjusting for different material thicknesses, thanks to automatic adjustment of the folding centre. The gentle folding process significantly reduces material surface scratches, extending tool life.

An automatic crowning system in the folding beam is designed to ensure precise bends based on material specifications. Its flexibility allows for operation from different sides, accommodating complex profiles and large parts.

"Enhanced safety features around the machine safeguard the operator from potential injuries. Its mechanical and electrical design [requires] less maintenance and uses less energy compared to traditional press brakes," concluded CIDAN.

The CIDAN Machinery Group is a fullrange supplier of sheet-metal machinery and control solutions for multiple industries. Its headquarters are in Götene, Sweden. It operates manufacturing locations in Sweden, Switzerland and Austria, with office locations in several European countries, the United States and China.

Its acquisition in March 2022 of Swiss folding specialist, Thalmann Maschinenbau AG, has added another company to the Group that includes CIDAN Machinery Austria GmbH (formerly coil-processing line specialist, Forstner Maschinenbau GmbH) and nulT GmbH (which manufactures control software). CIDAN itself (formerly Göteneds) specialises in sheet metal folders, shearing systems and rollformers.
