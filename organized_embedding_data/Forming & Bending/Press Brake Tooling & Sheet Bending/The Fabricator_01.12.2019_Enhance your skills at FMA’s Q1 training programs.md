Precision Press Brake Certificate Course Feb. 18-19, 2020 | Elgin, Ill.

Gain a Deep Understanding of the Principles of Precision Sheet Metal Bending

The two-day Precision Press Brake Certificate Course teaches the principles behind quality sheet metal bending and how to calculate accurate bend deductions, select the best tooling for the job, and determine the correct V-die opening to avoid part distortion. The course includes a brief final exam, enabling you to earn an industry-recognized FMA certificate. Learn more and register at

fmanet.org/precision-press-brake-certificate

Mexico Technology Conference Feb. 25-27, 2020 | Queretaro, Mexico

Improve Your Stamping and Fabrication Knowledge

The Mexico Technology Conference 2020 will bring over 100 attendees, industry experts, and suppliers to Queretaro on February 25-27, 2020, at the Fiesta Americana. Expand your knowledge, advance skills, and find solutions in the areas of stamping, tool and die design, bending, laser and plasma cutting, welding, and disruptive technologies.
