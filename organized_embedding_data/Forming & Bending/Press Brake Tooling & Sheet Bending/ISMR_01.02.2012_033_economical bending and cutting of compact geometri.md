# Economical bending and cutting of compact geometries

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

IN SHORT and compact tube geometries, material loss can be high if tubes are cut after bending. A new system from Transfluid Maschinenbau GmbH addresses this issue by using a combination bending and cutting process. It will be on display during TUBE Düsseldorf (26-30/3/2012, hall 05 / stand 5G34).

The machine features three bending levels, with 6m usable length and ten servo-electrical driven axes. The tube is then accurately cut with no further processing needed, says the company. The machine can bend and manufacture tubes of up to 140 x 2 D. Because the loading of short pre-cut parts is not necessary, non-productive times are reduced and savings can be made.

“Per geometry, this leads to an average saving of material up to two times the tube diameter,” adds the company. “An interesting application for this type of machine is, for example, the manufacture of exhaust systems.” See www.transfluid.de

## Packing a punch

THE NEW battery-powered Hougen-Ogura Model 76000 Punch-Pro electro-hydraulic hole puncher is designed for on-site hole punching where power is not available or hard to access. Hougen Manufacturing (MI, USA) demonstrated the system at FABTECH 2011.

“Although it is battery-powered, it packs enough punch to generate 8.5 tons punching force and can punch round or oblong holes up to 19/32” diameter in 1/4” mild steel (65,000 psi tensile strength) and 19/32” in 5/32” stainless steel (89,000 psi tensile strength) in four seconds,” said the company.

Weighing 16.5lbs, the 76000 model is suitable for applications typically done with electric hand-held twist drills. The operator can easily position the tool when working in narrow or awkward positions by rotating the motor 360 degrees in either direction during operation. The punch can be jogged down to the centre mark to confirm hole location accuracy. An auto-return mechanism returns the punch to the starting position. It can punch approximately 150 holes through 1/4” mild steel on a single battery charge. See www.hougen.com
