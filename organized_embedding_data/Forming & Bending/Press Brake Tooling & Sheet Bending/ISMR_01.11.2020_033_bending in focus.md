# Bending in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

Bystronic’s Xpert Pro press brake has now been equipped with the LAMS 4.1 angle measurement system’s springback compensation for bending sheet metal.

“High-tensile strength materials are used, for example, for automation projects in the agricultural sector, and in the trailers and automotive industries. During processing, these materials can spring back by up to 20° and more. The Xpert Pro’s springback compensation prevents this and can guarantee optimal results from the very first bend,” Bystronic told ISMR.

The LAMS angle measurement system compensates for any deviations in sheet thickness. In addition to sheet thickness fluctuations, the system also automatically compensates for springback and rolling direction variations. Corrections relating to the sheet metal that is being processed can be stored and transferred to other machines – even those that are not equipped with LAMS. This means that all other Bystronic press brakes in operation within a company also benefit from this function.

“The Xpert Pro reduces the need for tedious manual corrections as well as the operator workload during the programming of bending processes. Our ByVision Bending software solution determines the ideal bending process for every material thickness and bending angle. Convenient offline programming enables data to be imported without interrupting running production processes,” it added.

Bystronic’s Xpert 40 press brake also now features new optical tool detection. Cameras recognize the data matrix codes on the tools and sound an alarm if any deviances are detected. This results, says Bystronic, in reduced set-up time and improved process quality because ‘the right tool is always in the right position’. The Xpert 40 is a compact press brake designed for cost-effective small parts production and simple operation.

“Plug and bend means that you simply set up the machine and start bending. Its compact dimensions are ideal, wherever space is at a premium and capacities fluctuate. The press brake can be set up and put into operation in less than five minutes,” concluded Bystronic.
