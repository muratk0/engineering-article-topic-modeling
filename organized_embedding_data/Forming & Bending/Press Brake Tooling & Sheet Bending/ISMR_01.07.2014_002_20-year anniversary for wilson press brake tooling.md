# 20-year anniversary for Wilson press brake tooling

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

Chris Lawless, Wilson Tool President

WILSON TOOL International, the worlds largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for the metal fabrication industry, is celebrating the 20th anniversary of its press brake tooling division in 2014.

Launched in 1994, Wilson Tool’s press brake division began by manufacturing a single line of tooling for the press brake. Today, the company offers a comprehensive selection of press brake tooling solutions. From standard tooling solutions for European style, American Precision®, WT Style Precision™ and Exacta conventional press brake tooling to special customised tooling for the most unique bending applications, plus an array of clamping solutions designed to speed set-ups, Wilson Tool offers a broad selection of tooling options to press brake fabricators around the world.

“The growth of our press brake division during the past two decades has been remarkable,” said Chris Lawless, Wilson Tool President. “Much of that success is owed to our highly experienced staff that are passionate about delivering world class solutions. We are proud to continue that tradition and look forward to providing press brake fabricators with tooling solutions that are designed to improve productivity and achieve even greater success for many years to come.”

Wilson Tool’s latest press brake tooling achievements include the introduction of ExpressRail hydraulic press brake clamps (2010); the purchase of EXACTA Fabtool of Canada and introduction of conventional tooling with induction hardening (2010); the purchase of Toolspress of Italy (2012) and the introduction of V-Series Black dies (2014).
