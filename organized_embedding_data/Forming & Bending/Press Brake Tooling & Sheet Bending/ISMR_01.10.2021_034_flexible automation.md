# Flexible automation

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Salvagnini's P2 panel bender is now more flexible and productive, with a new MAC3.0 function. This, said the Italian metal forming specialist, gives it 'the flexibility, productivity, adaptability, versatility and ability to perform positive and negative, flattened and radius bends.'

With a footprint of less than 35m2, and all the features of the P4, Salvagnini's P2 is a compact panel bender designed to reduce re-tooling times when passing from one production item to the next and minimize, even eliminate, waste. The company continues to perfect the system, introducing new hardware and software solutions to further extend its field of application.

This is the case, for example, with the CUT option. Already popular on Salvagnini's P4 panel benders and, since last year, now also available on the P2, the CUT tool enables the machine to complete up to 17 bends per minute and cut profiles. This was followed by MAC3.0: a set of integrated technologies (sensors, formulas and algorithms) to make the P2 even more intelligent.

MAC3.0 is the evolution of MAC2.0, a standard feature on Salvagnini panel benders since 2014, which allows the P2 to adapt to the material being machined by measuring the tensile strength in real-time and comparing it to the reference values of some of the most common materials on the market. If the mechanical characteristics of the material being machined are within ±25% of the reference value, compensation is fully automatic: the panel bender recalculates the force required to obtain the right bends, limiting the risk of over- or underbending. In this scenario, MAC3.0 drastically reduces the risk of obtaining faulty parts and eliminates reworking and material waste.

MAC3.0 also extends the range of machinable materials and allows new ones to be defined simply and intuitively if necessary. These newly defined materials can also be used as the starting point for new adjustments and new materials. All Salvagnini panel benders manufactured since March 2021 have the MAC3.0 function.

"MAC3.0 can correctly bend materials with tensile strength up to double that of the related reference material. This solution is particularly relevant in today's raw material supply chain where sourcing difficulties, along with the dynamism of the market and the need to comply with set delivery times, force companies to accept compromises on the quality of the sheet metal, risking significant increases in waste," added Salvagnini.

"S1 optimises the production process, reducing waste by concentrating various activities in a single work phase, reducing intermediate handling and operator intervention. The multi-press head with evolved hybrid technology on the S1 is a Salvagnini patent: the tools are always available, housed in the solid die structure. The S1 multi-press head eliminates setups, guaranteeing reduced cycle times and high-quality jobs through the high-precision, punch-die alignment and extending the tool life cycle," explained Salvagnini.

The manipulator with two independent carriages, and pincers that move independently within the same carriage, facilitates maximum sheet reachability and greater working flexibility. It also improves process efficiency by eliminating downtimes. The S1 is equipped with compact loading/unloading automation, which does not increase the footprint of the system and, during the unloading phase, uses the Cartesian manipulator with two gripping devices. A wide range of Salvagnini automation devices can be used to customise its systems to improve efficiency.
