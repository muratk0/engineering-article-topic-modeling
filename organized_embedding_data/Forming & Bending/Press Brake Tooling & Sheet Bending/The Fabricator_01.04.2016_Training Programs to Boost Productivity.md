May 4-5, 2016

Perfection Machinery Sales Inc.

Elk Grove Village (Chicago), Ill.

Practice the techniques of precision sheet metal bending with this hands-on workshop

This combined classroom and hands-on training event will review the critical elements of sheet metal bending processes and best practices. Apply knowledge immediately as you use a press brake to create a formed piece. With individualized attention and expert coaching, you will follow the steps from flat layout to finished part. This program also includes a tour of Perfection Global.

Boost your press brake knowledge and skills and hone your craft with this unique training. This program is also available in Spanish on May 3-4, 2016.

Course Overview:

Bend Functions and Sequence

Blueprints and Layouts

Tooling Types and Selection

Metallurgy Overview

Tonnage Calculations

Methods of Press Brake and Precision Hand Tools

June 14, 2016

Harper College

Palatine (Chicago), Ill.

Putting sustainability to work

This course is designed to introduce and familiarize attendees with the key concepts and strategies related to sustainable manufacturing. Businesses are becoming more focused on their environmental and social impacts and for good reason: Identifying opportunities for improved efficiency and reducing waste benefit operations and increase competitiveness. If you are leading the environmental or sustainability efforts in your organization or are interested in learning more, this course can help you advance your career and sustainable stewardship in your company.

Course Overview:

Introduction to the Concepts of Sustainability

Measuring Environmental and Social Impacts

Identifying Business Opportunities

Creating a Sustainable Business Case for Management
