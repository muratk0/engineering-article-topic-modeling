# FLEXIBLE FOLDING

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

Thalmann put the focus on folding at EuroBLECH

Swiss manufacturer Thalmann Maschinenbau AG presented a range of folding innovations at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition, including its double folder TD Professional Plus series for roofing, facades and industrial applications.

With a working length of 8.2m and 2mm bending capacity, the TD 200 double folding machine model automatically processes sheet metal bending with precision up as well as down, reducing material handling to the minimum. A world premiere was the newly developed high performance hydraulics which, in combination with the recently introduced DFT system, provide a high degree of bending dynamics and productivity. The combination of both innovations results in a significant rise of bending speed which has increased the machine output significantly, said Marco Cappello, managing director of Thalmann.

## Adaptable and versatile

Thalmann’s Professional Plus Series models offer great flexibility in the design of sophisticated sheet metal parts. Thanks to a new tool concept, equipped with interchangeable bending beam utensils, users can adapt the tool mounted onto the carrier-bending beam, depending upon the situation and requirements. The working range of the machine therefore significantly expands. There is also the option to retrofit standard tools to Hardox – this will increase the design scope of products. A further advantage over previous solutions includes service issues – the tool, designed as multiple parts, can be replaced singularly, quickly and without long interruptions in production. Downtime is therefore reduced to a minimum.

“For the precise positioning of the blank, the TD model uses Hardox fitted Grippers. These hold the part firmly throughout the bending sequence and position the sheet metal automatically for each bend. Thanks to its large working range of up to 1300mm insertion depth, the TD model enables easy processing from the coil, although the gripper can clamp even the smallest dimension of 14mm. To further optimise the working process, a multi-zone gripper system enables the independent processing of several parts at the same time, with just one operator, considerably reducing labour costs,” explained Marco Cappello.

Simple and intuitive operation were the primary goals in developing the latest Thalmann machine control software. It is compatible with the DXF-format as well as the BENDEX software, so that the machine can communicate and exchange data with the most common CAD programs. All Thalmann machines are equipped with dynamic control shaft technology. The kinetic drive system distributes the power of all machines evenly on the clamping and bending beam. This is comparable to the mechanical flap control used in aircraft construction.

“The latest generation of Thalmann double folding machines are available in lengths of 3 – 12m. With different insertion depths, capacity of up to 3mm sheet metal (400 N/mm²) and a number of smart accessories, the machines are innovative and flexible at the same time. We therefore offer a clear competitive advantage to the user and measurable added value for the customer,” concluded Cappello.
