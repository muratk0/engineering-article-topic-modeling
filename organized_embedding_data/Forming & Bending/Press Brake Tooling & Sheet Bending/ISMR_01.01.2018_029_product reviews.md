## The bending principle

AMADA HAS UNVEILED its new compact press brake. The HFE-5012M2 is designed with user ergonomics and high productivity in mind, and was previewed for the first time in the UK at AMADA's Christmas exhibition between 5-7 December 2017.

The new HFE-5012M2 has been developed to offer rapid and comfortable bending of small parts with medium complexity. The machine provides 50 tonnes of bending force and a working width of 1270mm.

"Thanks to the performance of the upper beam, combined with the velocity and flexibility of the new back gauge, high efficiency is a primary attribute of the HFE-5012M2. The open height of the frame has been increased, resulting in an optimised solution for all tooling types and allowing for deeper box bending. The new, narrower lower beam increases process flexibility and combines ergonomics with crowning precision, while the back gauge with active security provides better productivity and safety with its high-speed motion, yet low impact force," the Japanese manufacturer told SMR.

The HFE-5012M2 has been equipped with several features designed specifically to support the comfort of the operator, including a reclining and adjustable table; an ergonomic chair with adjustable height and backrest inclination; a retractable foot rest; six drawers equipped with tooling and inserts, and a workplace lighting kit. The AMADA Bending Pad NC is an intuitive touchscreen HMI designed to provide a user-friendly operating experience.

"The HFE-5012M2 is the first in a series of bending machines developed to more carefully consider actual working environments, aiding the operator's daily activity with the benefits of comfort and enhanced productivity," said Alessandro Perdoni, European Bending Product Manager, AMADA Europe. See www.amada.co.uk

## Digital magnifier for portable inspection

VISION ENGINEERING HAS launched Camß (CamBeta), a new digital handheld magnifier for portable inspection and documentation. The new inspection device is designed for electronics; mechanical inspection; automotive manufacture; watchmaking; plastics engineering; medical inspection and whenever rapid visual, digital documentation is needed. Camß provides magnification of up to 20x, stores up to 20,000 images and uses grids and cursors for X & Y dimensioning, allowing objects to be inspected in situ and information to be recorded and shared.

"With a high-resolution colour display, easy button operation and image capture and download capability, Camß is ideal for roving inspection tasks, documenting faults and inspecting large or immobile subjects. The inspection device is equipped with dual LED illumination with four settings; high-contrast imaging and 30-frame video capture. A live-view video output via micro HDMI allows for images to be displayed on a larger screen, making Camß suitable for training purposes," said the manufacturer.

The Camß is suitable for production environments where company policy disallows the use of mobile phones. Its lightweight and ergonomic design enables the Camß to be easily manipulated with one hand and operated by right- or left-handed users.

“Camß offers a convenient way of meeting critical inspection and documentation requirements. The simple design helps to optimise the inspection process, as it requires minimal training. When time is of the essence, the portable design of the Camß also helps to speed up the process of inspection and decision-making for operators," concluded the company. See www.visioneng.com
