# The bending equation

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

From 4-7 June 2013, over 300 visitors from 35 countries attended the Prima Power Technology Days, organised at the Group unit in Finland. During the event, a new compact and economical fabrication solution for pre-cut materials was on display for the first time. PunchBend processes pre-cut material into ready-bent components. Both punching and bending technology are servo-electric, making the new product part of the company’s Green Means® offering.

The same high-end Prima Power punching technology that is featured in the punch laser cell is included. As the punching stroke is NC-controlled, in addition to high-performance punching, accurate forming capacity is available. High repeatability facilitates forming, roll forming, marking etc. and shortens set-up times. The PunchBend process is fully automatic.

Prima Power’s wide range of press brakes has also been extended with a new model, eP1-0212. Its working length is 1200mm with a bending force of 25 tons. The Prima Power eP1 series press brake is a compact, rapid, non-hydraulic bending solution. The ram is driven by a combination of heavy-duty servo motor and a high performance ball screw. Further machine features include side frames which are outside the maximum bending length, a Lazer Safe 005 “Block Laser” safety system and 2D graphical NC control.
