# Key anniversary celebrations

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

On 23 September 2023, PINETTE PEI celebrated 160 years as an independent industrial engineering company, and 60 years of its JEAN PERROT brand.

Today, PINETTE PEI is a specialist in the engineering and manufacturing of machines for the processing of composite material. JEAN PERROT was founded in Dijon, France, and named after its founder. At the time, shear production was predominant. In the mid-1970s, JEAN PERROT launched its first synchronised press brake. In 1993, the company was taken over by PINETTE. In 2014, JEAN PERROT pioneered robotic bending with its first automated bending line. Since then, it has become a specialist in automated bending and XXL (high thickness and long length) bending.

This year, to mark this double anniversary, JEAN PERROT and all its partners in the Metal Village (LUCAS; SPALECK; Vernet-Behringer; Hexagon/ Radan; GYS; System 3; Dimeco; Rolleri; the Compagnons du Devoir and Tour de France) opened their doors for a special TECHNIVERSAIRE day. Visitors were shown new innovations in robotic bending lines, large presses and 4.0 tools. They could share projects, development ideas or questions about particular production processes.

PINETTE PEI is an independent industrial engineering European SME with three industrial facilities in France, Germany and Italy. It provides standard or customised machines – forming presses, press brakes under the JEAN PERROT and GADE brands, assembly stations, and test benches – within production lines or turnkey plants.
