Press brake’s bending control provides comprehensive database

Bystronic offers the Xpert 150 press brake, equipped with the ByVision Bending control. The control uses a 22-in. HD touchscreen to display 3-D part animations and software-driven menu selections.

Bending profiles can be imported or drawn directly on the touchscreen and then automatically converted to a bending program with proper bending sequences and tool selections. The control offers a comprehensive database of material bend angles and bend allowances.

The high-speed ram positioning system can reduce bend cycle times by 15 to 35 percent with approach speeds of up to 600 IPM and bending speeds up to 47 IPM. The servo-hydraulic machine with dynamic press brake technology introduces no angle errors itself, with a ram positioning accuracy of 0.00015 in., the company reports.

Fiber laser marks in color on stainless steel

Cajo Technologies has developed new technology to produce consistent, repeatable laser-marked colors on stainless steel using a pulsed fiber laser.

The laser can produce deep black markings to aid readability of markings on darker surfaces. The dense, rich white also is extremely readable on a darker surface.

The fiber laser also can mark readable QR codes and bar codes on very small surfaces, such as electronic components.

Flap discs designed for fillet weld applications

POLIFAN

®

-Curve flap discs from Pferd Inc., designed for fillet weld applications, go from rough grinding to smooth finishing in a single step. This single tool allows weld seams and slag to be removed, leaving a finish smooth enough for identification of cracks, inclusions, and porosity in the fillet during weld inspections.

The disc has three usable working surfaces— top face, bottom face, and radial edge—rather than a single surface. It is available in diameters of 4.5, 5, 6, and 7 in. and in zirconia, A/O, and ceramicoxide abrasives.

Consumables designed to bend the plasma arc

Hypertherm offers FlushCut™ consumables for its Powermax plasma cutting systems. The patented consumable design features an off-center nozzle bore that delivers the plasma arc at a 45-degree angle, which allows system users to cut closer or more flush to the base to reduce grinding and increase the opportunity to reuse pad eyes, attachments, and other temporary weld supports.
