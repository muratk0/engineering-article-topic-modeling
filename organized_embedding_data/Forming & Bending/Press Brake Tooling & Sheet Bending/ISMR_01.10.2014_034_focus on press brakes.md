Bystronic has a surprise up its sleeve on bending at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition: a speedy machine with a compact design. The Xpert 40 provides a first glimpse of Bystronic’s vision for the small format bending experience of the future. The Xpert 40 will be launched in Spring 2015 and will round off the range of press brakes for the small machine segment.

The attraction of the new press brake is primarily its speed. The Xpert 40’s innovative, lightweight design delivers high levels of dynamics at bending speeds of up to 25 millimetres per second. This means, says Bystronic, that it can bend small parts three times faster than larger press brake models. On bending lengths exceeding one metre, the Xpert 40 generates a press capacity of 40 tons. Bystronic’s newly developed ByMotion drive control system ensures a finely tuned interplay between speed, precision and power. By coordinating the precise acceleration of the upper beam and the back gauges, it delivers bending results with high degrees of repetition accuracy. »

Ermaksan, who specialises in the production of sheet metal working machineries, has won three awards in two different international design competitions for both its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine in their own categories. Its EVO-II the hybrid press brake with its green energy, silence, low oil usage, high repeatability precision and maintenance-free features compared to ordinary hydraulic press brakes picked up a best design award from the GOOD DESIGN institution.

In conventional press brakes, energy is consumed even in standby mode but the Evolution II press brake only uses energy whilst it is bending. It can bend at a rate of 20 mm/sec. using servo motors and an efficient pump. It is also quiet – at 63dB noise-less and has high bending accuracy of between +/- 0.01mm. These new generation press brakes are developed to require fewer oil changes (every five years).

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the SPEED-BEND (synchronised hydraulic press brake), the POWER-BEND PRO 3-axis CNC press brake and the ECO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake. A 4V bottom die on the ECO-BEND allows users to make variety of bends on different material thicknesses. A movable control unit allows the operator to choose his working position.

The company’s MICRO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake is a small, productive hydraulic press brake with the capacity to bend 5mm mild steel with 1m x 40 tonnes of press force and one-axis back gauge. Ermaksan’s HEAVY DUTY press brake features a dynamic crowning system and 6-axis servo motor back gauge system. It also boasts special top and bottom tools and a 610mm throat depth.

Italian manufacturer Gasparini offers a press brake range that includes the X-BEND (press force from 50 to 170 tons) and the X-PRESS ECO. The X-PRESS is available in a wide range of models and powers, with a tonnage that varies between 25 and 1500 tons and lengths of between 1250 and 16000mm. The company offers stand-alone, tandem and tridem configurations, with a wide range of accessories (back gauges, front supports, clamping systems, sheet followers, controls etc.) and a tailor-made approach.

Gasparini’s new Dener hydraulic press brakes were designed to meet a wide variety of needs, accommodating weights ranging from 40 to 2,000 tons and offering from 3 to more than 11- axis capabilities. Its new electric MC press brake is leaner, greener and more reliable delivering accuracy, quiet operation and faster cycle times.

The Diamond BB Series features a compact, ball screw design driven by an AC servo motor with 40 metric tons of force and one-micron repeatability. It utilises a dual-drive system with a high-speed, high-accuracy back gauge, larger stroke and hydraulic power.

In May, LVD Company nv announced the next generation of its Easy-Form® and PPEB Series of high performance press brakes featuring a new, modern design that is more energy-efficient, easier to use and incorporates added safety features. Aesthetic and ergonomic, the new generation press brakes also feature advanced control technology, servo drives and scanning sensors, ensuring more responsive operation and higher productivity.

LVD’s latest bending technology includes the Dyna-Press, its compact press brake for the bending of small and simple parts. The new generation Easy-Form with advanced in-process adaptive bending technology and an advanced touch screen control ensures more responsive operation and higher productivity.

The ToolCell, LVD’s press brake with an integrated tool-changing system, significantly reduces press brake set-up time. LVD is now offering a 220-ton, 3-metre model of the ToolCell. A large selection of top and bottom tooling is stored in a tooling stadium/warehouse under the back gauge. An innovative gripper design built into the machine’s back gauge finger serves as the tool changer mechanism. When a job is complete, the press brake automatically changes the top and bottom tooling as required for the next job. All tool stations are precisely positioned, eliminating the need for manual adjustments.

PunchBend is Prima Power’s new, compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials, which processes pre-cut material automatically into ready-bent, high quality components. The essential characteristic of the concept is tight integration of all the elements needed: software, punching and bending technology as well as automation. Both punching and bending technology are servo-electric, making the new product part of the Prima Power Green Means® offering. The high-end Prima Power servoelectric is included.

SafanDarley will present its latest product innovations and developments in sheet metal forming, including its new generation of servo-electric press brakes, at EuroBLECH 2014. The drive-based pulley principle has evolved into a new machine concept with a C-frame, a narrower press beam and higher pressure load per metre. Other benefits include rapid direct positioning with higher acceleration and accuracy.

“The SafanDarley Eye new generation press brake reduces downtime and increases productivity, using Augmented Reality. Glasses shows the operator additional information that he cannot perceive directly, such as location data for the positioning of products. Even complex components can be produced with comparatively little effort,” explained SafanDarley.

The company will also display its new, E-Bend L angle-measurement system for press brakes at EuroBLECH.

Salvagnini designs, builds, sells and services flexible machines and systems for processing sheet metal. Its bending portfolio comprises the P4Xe and P2Xe hybrid panel benders; E2, B2 and B3 KinEtic press brakes, ROBOformER® robotic bending cells; automatic pack or tray store-towers and integrated systems for factory logistics.

The company’s S4+P4 line, in a new compact version, is designed for effective manufacturing flow of bent and punched parts.

It aims to increase flexibility and production. Its new smart FlexCell solution, stand-alone machines controlled by proprietary software, is designed to guarantee efficient cell management and increase production effectiveness and ease of use.

For manual bending, Salvagnini has launched its ATA device (Automatic Tool Adjuster), the automatic tool changer mounted on a B3 press-brake. Tool length can be adjusted automatically in a few seconds enabling parametric parts, single batches or kit to be produced. The company’s new solution for bending metal parts, the P2 lean, has been optimised to widen the bending capacity to a variety of sheet metal parts, including small and narrow profiles. It boasts an average consumption of around 5kW during operation, as the direct electric drive has made it possible to eliminate the hydraulic power pack and all circuits.

“The Salvagnini P1 panel bender has twice the productivity of press brakes and is indispensible for the production of thinner parts, less than 49 inches in length. With manual blankholder set-up (MLA), universal tooling, the briefest of cycle times and sophisticated, intuitive software, if you are capable of loading a blank and unloading a finished part, you can produce a high quality part on a P1 Performer,” the company told ISMR.

Salvagnini’s B3 series of press brakes combines the company’s knowledge of panel bending and press brake forming technology. This press brake solution can reach speed and power levels similar to those of a hydraulic press brake whilst delivering the accuracy and reactivity of an electric press brake and maintaining the consumption of a hybrid system, all managed by the proprietary Salvagnini SiX controller.

On display at TRUMPF’s booth at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition will be the new generation in the TruBend Series 5000 and – with the semi-automated TruBend Center – a completely new member of the TRUMPF press brake line. Also celebrating its premiere will be the TruPunch 5000, which has raised productivity and reliability standards in punching.

The TruBend Series 3000, made by TRUMPF, combines high joint speeds with simplified controls and an attractive cost-to-benefit ratio. This makes for profitable manufacturing even at low machine utilisation rate. The multi-touch-control and the space-saving design are the predominant features. The back gauges can move across the entire bending length, which enables its unrestricted use when bending. Automatic crowning makes for constant angles. In addition, high joint speeds make this machine the fastest in its class.

The TruBend 3100 has a press force of 1,000 kilonewtons and three metres of bending length. Standard equipment includes a two-axis back gauge; it can, as an option, be expanded to a four- or five-axis back gauge. This makes it possible to position sheet metal securely, even where complex part geometries are involved. ■
