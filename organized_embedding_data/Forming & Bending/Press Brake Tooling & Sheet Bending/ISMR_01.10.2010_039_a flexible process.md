# A FLEXIBLE PROCESS

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Multiple operations on specialised lines, including punching, are IRON’s speciality. It recently developed a new concept line for flexible punching and bending of stainless steel cabinets from coil (FPB) for new customers.

It is a flexible modular line that incorporates a punch press (with thick turret tooling), a part-turning device (after punching), positive and negative bends on the punched parts and can handle part dimensions ranging from 650mm width x 1500mm length to 1500mm width x 3600mm in length.

## PRODUCTIVITY IN FOCUS

“Productivity features designed into the line translate to the end-user processing more tons per hour. All along the line, rapid changeover is achieved through a powered punch press and bending system for quick online set-up, push-button lockup and start, interchangeable punch head, quick-change tooling bars and automatic transmissions on all DC motors for the bending stations. The electrical controls system is key to increasing yield by automating functions that traditionally have been operator-adjusted.”

Other design elements promote high yield output such as tolerance quality and accuracy in both operations - punching and bending - as well as the ability to hold tight strip tolerances, which can potentially increase production by 100 per cent, says IRON.

“The machine has been designed to handle 0.5 – 1.5mm stainless steel (mild steel of up to 2.5mm),” Dalla Pace told ISMR. “The strip holder and bending process is completely automated – using servo drives. We have developed a CAD system that works directly from a customer’s drawing. The line itself is very complex, with different stations – coil handling; a punching unit; a shear; a buffer; a tilting device and two bending stations – and is designed for high production volumes.
