PBT’s ARM System

Much of PBT profile bending machines’ work is for custom aluminum extrusions. The assumption is that aluminum is an easy metal to bend. It can be trickier than it seems. As aluminum ages, its material characteristics change, which means that its elasticity or "spring-back" changes over time. PBT’s ARM system enables the machine to correct for these varying characteristics. The ARM system continuously measures and corrects multiple radii on a single workpiece—even on complex forms with variable material properties, eliminating time-consuming test runs and trial rejects, and achieving accurate part batches without deviations.

Picot’s EasyRoll© CNC Software

EasyRoll takes a different approach – bordering on artificial intelligence. EasyRoll puts 150 years of plate roll experience to work on each project with an extensive materials database that can be operated by users of any experience. With the material, thickness and prescribed diameter of any part, EasyRoll does the rest. It also keeps learning—gaining deeper experience and control based on the inputs from the operation’s projects. EasyRoll allows the plate roll bending machine to take into account both spring-back and bending resistance to deliver the correct diameter on the first try.
