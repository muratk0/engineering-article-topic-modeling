# Sheet metal bending

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

SafanDarley will present a wide range of innovative solutions at the LAMIERA sheet metal exhibition in Milan, Italy (18-21 May 2022). Visit the company on its stand E10 in Hall 15 at the trade show. Besides press brake innovations, SafanDarley will focus on its latest developments in offline bending software.

The Dutch bending specialist has added the servo-electric E-Brake C 200T Ultra (bending length of 4300mm) with Tool Mate to its press brake range. The Tool Mate is an external automatic tool changer, designed to offer substantial time savings when producing small batches. The correct tool settings can be loaded and used directly on the machine from AutoPOL off-line software (or other independent offline bending simulation software). The tool storage magazine has a capacity of 32-, 52- or 60- metres of tools.

## SafanDarley will also showcase a new model of Hybrid press brake, the H-Brake Hybrid+ 230T Ultra, with higher tonnages.

The H-Brake 230T + will be equipped with an E-Bend L blue angle measurement system. The blue light spectrum of the E-Bend L Blue is much easier to distinguish from "natural" light, so reflections and sunlight do not interfere with the angle measurement.

“At its core, there is an advanced dual hybrid drive with high return speed, high productivity and low energy consumption. As a result, costs are reduced. One of the most significant benefits of this system is the dramatic reduction in power consumption," the manufacturer told /SMR.

SafanDarley will also present its 100% electronic E-Brake 35T Premium with Ergonomic package at LAMIERA. The operator is surrounded by a support table, which is useful when bending large quantities. The seating position can be fully adjusted, as desired.

Another highlight at LAMIERA will be its AutoPOL off-line bending simulation software, a 3D system for off-line press brake programming and unfolding of 3D CAD files. Additional 'Batch & Remote' functionality enables automatic processing of multiple parts.

"All tasks are performed without user input, results are reported back and production data created. It dramatically reduces programming costs. Smart Bending with the innovative 3D off-line programming guarantees full and reliable process control, it concluded.■
