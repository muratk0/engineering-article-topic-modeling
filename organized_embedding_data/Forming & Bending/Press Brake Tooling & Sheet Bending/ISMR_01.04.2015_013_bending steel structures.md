# Bending steel structures

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

ANOTHER LARGE capacity DAVI angle roll has been sold in the Far East, to bend sections and heavy beams, for the construction of bridges, steel structures and airport buildings.

“Our angle roll,” explained Promau S.r.l. president, Orazio Davi,”can bend up to IPE 1000 and will be installed in early 2015, becoming the largest angle roll in terms of capacity installed in Indonesia. The machine is equipped with a universal set of rolls and guides that allows operators to easily bend all sections while reducing undesired deformation of the beams.” See www.promausrl.com ■
