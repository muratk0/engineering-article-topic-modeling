# Digitised sheet metal processing

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

At Blechexpo 2023 in Stuttgart this November, Tommaso Bonuzzi, Sales Manager, Salvagnini Group, presented its latest starter kit for sheet metal processing. Its L5 laser cutting system offers 5G axis acceleration and over 20% energy savings. As a world premiere, the fourth generation of the P2 panel bender and the P3Auto, a fully automated press brake with automatic toolchanger, were on display. These technologies are seamlessly integrated into unified programming and management software for efficiency in production. NEXUS is new Salvagnini software for MCU automatic sorting devices.

## Panel bending

The P2-2120 panel bender that Salvagnini presented in Stuttgart combines productivity (automatic bending and handling cycles with an average of 17 bends per minute) and flexibility (with its universal bending tools).

"No re-tooling is required because the upper and lower blades, counterblade and blankholder are universal tools able to process the whole range of thicknesses and machinable materials. The ABA automatic blankholder adapts the tool length according to the size of the part being produced in-cycle, without machine downtimes or manual re-tooling. P2 is suitable for kit or single-batch production," Salvagnini told ISMR.

The Salvagnini P2 panel bender automatically adapts to variations in the mechanical and geometrical characteristics of the sheet metal being machined, as well as to the external environment. Its sensors measure the actual thickness and effective size of the blank, detecting any deformations caused by variations in temperature. MAC3.0 allows it to adapt to the material being machined by measuring the tensile strength in real-time. The strength of the sheet metal measured can be saved as a reference value for a new material: the panel bender adapts the bending force as necessary to keep working within the parameters.

In Stuttgart, Salvagnini presented the AQC option in response to demand for a simple tool to speed up quality controls. AQC automatically measures, ensures and, if necessary, certifies the bending angle precision in a report. The P2-2120 on display in Stuttgart was just one of the 13 panel benders models in the Salvagnini portfolio, all differing in maximum bending length, maximum bending height and automation.

## Press brake bending

Automatic set-up and tool change are two features of the B3.AU-TO press brake. It is available in four models, with bending length of up to three or four metres and maximum force of 170 or 220 tons. The AU-TO automatic tool change device and the ATA automatic tool adjuster, which adjusts the length of the stations, significantly reduce press brake re-tooling, increasing autonomy and productivity while reducing lead-time.

Distinctive features include the Salvagninipatented upper and lower automatic toolchange unit. The tool store is covered and located in the rear part of the B3. It can contain up to 24m of tools of up to 1000mm in length without segmentation. The press brake allows mixed tooling.

All B3 press brakes are equipped with MAC3.0 integrated adaptive technologies; S-Crowning mechanical crowning system; TFC2.0 or Total Frame Control to prevent deviations in the bending angle and AMS laser angle measurement system.

## L5 laser cutter

A patented design, a wide automation offer and a range of advanced process control and efficiency solutions are the cornerstones on which Salvagnini has built its L5 laser. At Blechexpo 2023, Salvagnini presented a global preview of a new 8kW high-power density source "that guarantees 50% higher performance," it told ISMR.

"Reducing the power of the source while keeping the cutting speeds high means reducing energy and gas consumption for the same performance. In addition to lower consumption linked directly to the source, it also enables users to reduce the chiller size. Consumption is therefore very similar to that of a 6kW source," explained Salvagnini.

In the configuration presented at Stuttgart, the L5 is fed by ADC, compact automation for sheet metal loading and unloading. ADC is also equipped with the new AVC adaptive vacuum control technology which constantly monitors the vacuum degree in the gripping suction cups. Vacuum generation is activated only when required, resulting in a reduction in compressed air consumption of around 90%. The L5 is equipped with the APC2 adaptive process sensor which monitors piercing in real-time for greater speed and higher quality.

## Salvagnini services

Rethinks is a service exclusively for Salvagnini customers to help them exploit the potential of their systems for an even higher competitive position in the market. Rethinks connects customers with Salvagnini specialists to optimise production processes and improve quality and efficiency, reducing the number of work steps, material usage and costs.

At Blechexpo, Salvagnini also presented Impacts, a flexible tool for calculating consumption of its own systems. It is possible to precisely measure the consumption of electricity, compressed air and assist gas. The future aim is to calculate volumes of CO₂ equivalent produced throughout the whole industrial process "with a granularity that can record the volumes of CO₂ equivalent of each single manufactured component."
