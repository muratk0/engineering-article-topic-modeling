The RAS MiniBendCenter is an automatic folding centre for small parts. Blanks are automatically fed, squared and measured. Tool set-up is all automatic. A manipulator moves and rotates the blank automatically to the exact position. The folding cell folds the part up and down.

For the automated bending of long profiles, the RAS XXL long folding centre automatically inserts, squares, positions to the bend line and folds the flanges up or down. This, says RAS, eliminates rotation, complicated handling and the need for a second or third operator.

Straight bends are essential for perfectly folded parts. The RAS Gigantic is a sturdy and solid machine with an intelligent crowning system and 120 tons of clamping pressure to fold parts of 6mm mild steel or 4mm stainless steel.

RAS software is designed and optimised for each machine and allows for 2D and 3D models and images for the parts that operators are looking to create. It also offers the Bendex Professional.shop and Bendex Professional. production software to optimise workflow and create clear production documents.

SchechtI (Edling, Germany) began as a blacksmith shop, run by Josef Schechtl, in 1910. 22 years later, it produced its first folding machine. Today, it manufactures shears and folders for sheet metalworking with various distributors throughout the world.

Its folder range includes the MAX; MAX-F; MAB; MAZ; MAE and MBM series. The Model MAX is a motor-driven folder with a capacity of max. 1.5mm (3100mm working length).

“The speed of the bending beam at 80°/ second and the opening of the upper beam at 50mm/sec are impressive. The upper beam opens while the bending beam moves down so the part can be positioned for the next step simultaneously. The combination of the Schechtl CNC touch control, the motor-operated back gauge and the movable foot switch provides a bending system for one-man-operation even for sheets of up to 3100mm," it explained.

The Schröder Group will present its broad portfolio of sheet-metalworking machines at EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022). Highlights will include the fully automatic folding beam tool-changer on its EVO Center motorised sheet-metal folder and presentation of the PowerBend Multi. The EVO Center has been developed for industrial series production with high repeatability and offers several automation options.

A completely redesigned version of the PowerBend Professional folder will also be on display, with the option of a rotating clamping beam. This machine has an increased clamping beam stroke of 500mm and now offers more space for bulky workpieces.

"At EuroBLECH, we will be focusing on the flexibility of the machine to efficiently produce single pieces and small batches. Whereas rapid automatic changes of up to 400mm-high tools on the clamping beam were already possible via two asynchronously movable gripper units, there is now another special feature to showcase. The EVO Center will be equipped with an automatic tool changer for the folding beam. Recesses (e.g. for loops that are not to be folded) can now be set, as required, by the program control," the bending specialist outlined.

It offers a wide portfolio of folding machines, including the MAKU; MAK 4 Evolution UD; Bending Centre; the automated EVO-Centre; the SPB Evolution UD; PowerBend Industrial/ Professional/Universal; the MPB (for thin sheetmetal processing) and a variety of smaller models.

Sucorema is a Portuguese company dedicated to the manufacture of machines and systems for the sheet-metal industry, under its own brand Stefa. With more than two decades of experience in designing and producing its own machines, Sucorema specialises in long folding machines, rollformers and cut-to-length/ slitting lines.

It will display its Stefa VH folder at EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, Germany, with the latest innovations. The EliteBender CNC control comes with new functions such as remote programming. A new hydraulic circuit has been developed, with proportional variation for greater efficiency.

Australian manufacturer, SWI Machinery, offers a range of folders.

“Our Simplex folder is fast, simple and flexible enough to be used by the most inexperienced, as well as the most experienced, operators," it told ISMR. “Barcode scanning and integration with the ShopLink & eBend app is available and the machine boasts a recessed fold apron geometry with 23" touch-screen controller. A sleek clamp and apron design eliminates catch points when folding flashings and slitter light curtains provide positive finger detection during slitter operations," it explained.

SWI's range includes Simplex and Duplex folders; Marxman slitters; integrated cut-to-length lines; uncoilers; recoilers; multistation uncoilers; rollformers and complete customised process lines. SWI's latest model Duplex folder incorporates dozens of advances in control and mechanical design.
