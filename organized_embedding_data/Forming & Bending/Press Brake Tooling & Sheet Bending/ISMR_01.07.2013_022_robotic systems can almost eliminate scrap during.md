# Robotic systems can almost eliminate scrap during production

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

Haco has developed bending software for a robotic press brake where the bending program is suited to the product the client wants to manipulate – it is, in effect, an operating system for the entire bending cell. The programming of all parts to be produced can be done within minutes. The cell builder allows the operator to define the fastest processing flow. The bending process can be simulated and the integrated collision detection feature avoids collisions in the bending cell.

Amada’s Astro 100NT Cell is an automatic robotic bending system integrated with a 110- ton hydraulic press brake. It is designed for high-precision part productivity through reduced cycle times. It is a totally unmanned robotic bending system, which increases productivity and profitability without increasing costly labour. The system incorporates a larger press brake with larger capacity robots. This enables process of bigger and/or heavier parts. The robot utilises the entire length of ram allowing flexible multiple stage bending.

“Our Amada Astros are automatic robotic bending systems integrated with 110-ton hydraulic press brakes. They are completely unmanned robotic bending systems, and are designed for exceptional repeatability and highprecision part productivity through reduced cycle times. The robots utilise the entire length of ram allowing flexible multiple stage bending,” Latitude Precision Metal Fabrication, in the US, told ISMR.

Bystronic has robotised its press brake systems and added new upgrades to its bending portfolio to further improve productivity. These include the new safety system Fast Bend+ which not only improves the machine safety but also increases parts output by some 20 per cent, it told ISMR. With the aid of a barcode scanner the change between jobs can be achieved even more quickly. Reduced energy consumption and less machine noise characterize the Energy Saver. New bending aids, with for example rollers for heavy parts and brushes for the careful handling of stainless steel, ensure even greater flexibility in material handling particularly for small batches.

LVD’s RoboCell combines an LVD press brake, multi-axis Kuka robot and LVD CADMAN software to provide an integrated automated bending solution. RoboCell increases machine throughput by automatically loading, forming and stacking finished parts in an unmanned process, providing high-precision parts, shorter cycle times and lower production costs.

Prima Power’s new automatic bending cell EBe automates the bending process of highquality sheet metal components. The new construction features actuation of the bending blade movements (vertical and horizontal) by servo NC-axes instead of hydraulic cylinders. The upper tool movements are also made by another NC servo-axis.

For optimum product quality, a new bending principle is now available. When the “rolling mode” is used, there is wider contact surface between the blade and the sheet but no relative friction. Alternatively, when using a standard “circular mode”, the contact point remains constant whereas the contacting point of the blade changes during the bending movement.

Prima Power’s BCe series bending centres provide capacity for single piece and small batches as well as serial manufacturing. They extend bending capability to applications that were not possible before, allowing production of components which are fully perforated, have large or high forms or need large openings.

Safan developed a new breed of machines for automated sheet metal processing, dubbed the I-Brake (an intelligent press brake with two robotic arms). Because the robotic arms are mounted on the frame horizontally to the left and the right, the floor in front of the press brake is free for the pallets containing the sheets to be bent. The use of two robots results in great flexibility. The robotic arms can work simultaneously for handling of one long sheet, but can also bend two products at the same time in a single cycle.

Safan’s R-brake concept has all the advantages of the servo electronic concept Safan has on the E-Brake series, such as less energy consumption, low noise, no oil, high accuracy, no crowning needed, fast cycle-times and low maintenance costs. For the off-line programming, Safan is offering the RoboWave programming software which enables the user to make programmes for the bending cell and make an accurate simulation. Set-up times are reduced using this software. Most important is the man-machine interface.

## An eye on robotics

The North American robotics market recorded its strongest year ever in 2012, according to statistics from Robotic Industries Association (RIA), the industry’s trade group. A total of 22,598 robots valued at US$ 1.48 billion were sold to companies in North America in 2012, beating the previous record of 19,337 robots sold in 2011.

Sales were also up in metalworking industries (+12%) and life sciences/ pharmaceuticals (+3%). In terms of applications, increases were seen in assembly (+40%), spot welding (+37%), arc welding (+24%), coating & dispensing (+13%), and material handling (+3%).

“2012 was quite satisfying for the global robot industry and robot sales reached the second highest level ever,” stated Dr. Shinsuke Sakakibara, President of the International Federation for Robotics, on 1 July 2013 at CIROS in Shanghai. Between 2008 and 2012, robot sales increased by nine per cent on average per year.
