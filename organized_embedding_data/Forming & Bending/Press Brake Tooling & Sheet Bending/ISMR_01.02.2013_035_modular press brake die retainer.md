# Modular press brake die retainer

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

URETHANE TOOLING & ENGINEERING CORP has launched a new line of modular Tool-A-Thane Urethane press brake die retainers used for most metal forming applications up to 1/8” material or more. These press brake die retainers enable accurate bending without marring or scratching ferrous or non-ferrous, polished, pre-finished or other materials. The retainers are available in five sizes ranging from 1” wide x 1/2” thick to 12” wide x 2” thick forming pads.

“One major benefit is that you can buy a retainer for a specific job length today; when you get a longer job, you can buy additional length modular pieces to add to the original retainer,” said Knight Farwell, company president. “This saves money in the long run as you don’t need to keep buying a new full length retainer for every larger job.”

See www.urethanetooling.com for more information on die retainers, forming pads and spring gauges.
