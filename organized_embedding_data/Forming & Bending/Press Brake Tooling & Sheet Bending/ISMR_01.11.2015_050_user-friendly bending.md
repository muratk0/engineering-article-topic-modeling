# USER-FRIENDLY BENDING

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

Schiavi demonstrated its innovative crowning-free LineAr 130.30 press brake, with touch-screen control on the frame of the press brake, to ISMR at Blechexpo

ISMR SAYS:

Since 1958, Schiavi Macchine International Srl’s watchwords have been enduring reliability and quality, the result of continuous research and development and its accent on customer service.

“Continuous training, prompt pre- and aftersales responsiveness plus technological and design expertise has allowed us to uphold the tradition of ‘Made in Italy’ excellence around the world,” Nicolò Zinetti, the company’s marketing and communications manager, told ISMR at Blechexpo in Stuttgart. “The key feature of our press brakes is the production of simple shapes or complex geometrical structures with the same degree of accuracy. Every model has a suitable system for parallel plate bending to guarantee a constant bending angle without the need for any additional external crowning system.”

Its capabilities in designing and adapting its bending products to the specific needs of the market, either through integrated or customized solutions, are the keys to providing its customers not only with the performance they seek but also a competitive edge that they can “deploy” in their target market.

## New launch at Blechexpo

Schiavi arrived at Blechexpo in Stuttgart with a real innovation – its new press brake, the LineAr (model 130.30 with 3m length was showcased on its booth). This press brake model features the patented Schiavi “Crowning Free System” which guarantees a constant angle along the bend and horizontal edge along the line of bend (for a perfectly straight bend). LineAr has three beams for the lower table and three for the upper and the bending force is distributed on the external beams so that the tool holding beams remain straight during the bending process.

Another highlight of its booth presence was its new CNC control, Atlas, integrated with the Moses system, which project all the information that the operator needs directly onto the frame of the press brake. This system is touch-screen so that the user can directly work on the press brake frame instead of walking backwards and forwards to the CNC next to the press brake.

“2015 has been a revolutionary year for Schiavi so far, especially when it comes to overseas markets which are our main target,” said Elia Zinetti, R&D Production Manager, Schiavi. “The biggest event for us this year is Blechexpo in Stuttgart, where we are launching this new generation of press brake and CNC control. Our new LineAr 130.30 adopts the Schiavi international triple beam patent, with the middle beam remaining horizontal and unloading the force onto the other two beams. The two beams holding the tools are therefore parallel along the entire machine length. In addition, a more sophisticated hydraulic system reduces energy consumption to a minimum. Our new Atlas CNC control brings several user interface improvements which simplify and optimize communication between the operator and the machine.”

It is definitely an impressive innovation – the projection of graphical information onto the frame of the press brake (rather than to the side of the machine) is both user-friendly and time-saving. Operators do not have to walk between the control and the work-piece to check processes, just simply glance upwards to check the finer details of a job. Schiavi fully demonstrated the concept to ISMR at its booth in Stuttgart.

“The main advantages of the application are that the tool holder beams (upper and lower), under a uniform load, remain straight. No crowning, or any other systems to compensate for the deformations of the tool holder beams, are needed. The effects on the quality of the bending work are immediate - the bending line is straight. There is no need to correct the theoretical crowning compensation for each different material reaction. There is no need to calculate the required crowning compensation before each different bend. There is also no curvature in the line of bend, which is a typical effect of traditional crowning systems,” continued Elia Zinetti.

The press brake has been built with no welds; the entire structure is bolted together. Schiavi offers the press brakes in three sizes. Small press brakes, up to 400 tons, feature 3- 4m bending length. The height of the lower tooling from the ground is reduced. Mediumsized, (up 600-ton) press brakes have a 4-6m bending length. The foundation for the lower table is not necessary. Large (up to 1200-ton) press brakes feature a 6-12m bending length. The foundation for the lower table is smaller than ‘traditional’ ones.

“The LineAr system, with the upper and lower tool-holding beams that remain straight during the bend, allow for components with the correct angle along the component and perfectly straight components along the line of bend, even on very large press brakes,” added Nicolò Zinetti. “The LineAr 130.30 model improves upon the current LineAr specifications and features tables with smaller dimensions and smaller thicknesses, increased daylight opening to easily manufacture large and complicated components and a working plane for the operator which is closer to the ground.”

The LineAr 130.30 is also equipped with an inverter that speeds up the bending cycle. A specially designed hydraulic system, with Start & Stop function, allows LineAr 130.30 to significantly improve efficiency by using maximum bending force only when in contact with the material, thus saving energy costs for the customer. The CNC control can also set a time after which the press brake will stop consuming electricity, whilst keeping all available data in memory.

## Bending accessories

Schiavi has also patented and produced an electric-pneumatic angle measurement system to reduce bending times and obstructions, cut out springback issues and lower operational costs. All Schiavi press brakes are equipped with its LASER BEAM safety device solution which has been designed to ensure the safe conduct of the cycle without losing manufacturing time.

Schiavi produces every style of tooling: Wila, TRUMPF and European. It also has a dedicated office to assist the customer in buying the correct tools for his products and in making the special tools required for his production. The company has a wide range of punches and dies which constitute the focal point of the press brake during the bending process. Its tools are built using special steels and carbon C50, and hardened through induction to increase resistance up to 55-60 HRC.

Schiavi following arms are designed to give the operator full support when it comes to bending large metal sheets. They support the material and can be used by a single operator, saving labour costs.

“The following arms eliminate the V-fold in thin metal sheets and the fold can be corrected when bending large sheets. They reduce risks for the operator, as the bend programming can be done at a safe distance and, as the arms function autonomously, they can also be used one at a time,” explained Nicolò Zinetti.

Schiavi has also developed, together with COMAU Robotics, a series of solutions for automated bending cells in response to market demands for high productivity and workplace safety. It offers two bending options - the Cartesian Robot Multi-Axis, which operates according to precise movement axes, and orthogonal/anthropomorphic rotation (six degrees of freedom - 12-370kg).

## Bending and control system software

The CNC control system of the LineAr 130.30 is produced by Task84, a division of Schiavi Macchine International. Task84 is committed to the development of simple and efficient CNC controls using Schiavi customer feedback.

“Simplicity and usability are the keys to our software success,” Nicolò Zinetti told ISMR.

TITANO is a graphic multi-axis numeric control in an ergonomic cabinet, with a 'Made in Italy' design. It is the combined result of 20 years of hardware and software experience accumulated by TASK84 in the bending press control field. Its simple and friendly GUI (Graphical User Interface) is complemented by a High Resolution LCD, with touch-screen. Another important feature is the environment dedicated to production management, a true innovation in numeric control for press brake systems. With clear graphics and advanced 3D features, TITANO is an onboard CAM. The graphic creation of tool and 2D/3D parts, import of 3D parts from CAD-CAM, simulation and 2D/3D reproduction, in real-time, of all of the bend phases are some of the basic features. The presence of a Tool-Navigator helps the operator in the machine's tool set-up operation, while special software functions ensure Automatic Search of the best bend sequence.

Schiavi’s Bending System CAM is a complete suite that solves most bending management challenges. Bending System selects the equipment and tools for a job based upon part characteristics and tools available. It offers a simple three-dimensional part editing environment with import functionality from the most popular CAD standards (DXF, IGES and STEP).

“It is a powerful three-dimensional simulation environment that allows manual and automatic bending sequence search. It detects collisions and estimates part completion times. Robot gripper management is also included,” concluded Elia Zinetti.
