# SAY IT WITH TUBE

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

Tubes are measured by their outer diameter and are usually smaller and less rigid than pipes. Bending involves metal forming processes used to permanently form pipe or tubing. The objective is to obtain a smooth bend without flattening the tube or pipe. Bending processes include form-bound and freeform, as well as heat-supported and cold forming. Factors such as material wall thickness, tooling and lubricants used all affect the bending process. Generally, round stock is used in tube bending. However, square and rectangular tubes and pipes may also be bent to meet job specifications.

Form-bound bending, such as press bending or rotary draw bending, are used to form the workpiece into the shape of a die. Straight tube stock can be formed using a bending machine to create a variety of single or multiple bends and to shape the piece into the desired form. These processes can be used to form complex shapes out of different types of ductile metal tubing. Freeform-bending processes, such as three-roll-push bending, shape the workpiece kinematically so that the bending contour is not dependent upon the tool geometry.

Tube end forming machines use hydraulic or mechanical presses. Often these presses use a repeated hammering motion to shape the tube. Because tube end forming is a shaping process and requires no cutting, there is no wasted metal that needs to be thrown out at the end of the process. In addition, the tube end forming process can provide improved tube strength, better connectivity between component, and improved sealing capabilities.

## The need for flexibility

At EuroBLECH 2018, Czech manufacturer, 4ISP s.r.o., presented a professional HSG fibre laser for cutting metal tubes, pipes and profiles up to 210mm in diameter and 6500mm in length. The machine is equipped with fully automatic feeding of the processed material as well as pneumatic chuck, automatic zoom and electromagnetic clutch with impact protection.

The AICON BendingStudio software platform now allows for the integration of a ROMER Absolute Arm with Integrated Scanner to determine complex tube and wire geometries using 3D laser scanning technology at almost any point in the production process. The latest BendingStudio 5.0 offers a new evaluation strategy for the determination of the bending model. It provides benefits especially for the measurement of components with flat bending angle (< 3°), bend-in-bend-geometries and bends larger than 180°. The latest version is compatible with all TubeInspect models and the ROMER Absolute Arm.

In May 2018, BAE Systems – one of the largest defence contractors in the world – chose AMOB as the supplier of its new tube bending cell. The eMOB-100 fully-electric CNC pipe bending machine was installed at the company's pipe shop in Scotstoun, Glasgow, to bend all the piping on the Type 26 frigate – which is used to hunt submarines. With the system including 49 different tool sets, the installation and training team were with BAE for just under two weeks

The eMOB-100 covers a range of sizes from 21.3mm x 1.6mm to 88.9mm x 7.1mm. For BAE, the material ranged from extra heavy-duty carbon steel to stainless steel through to 90/10 copper nickel. The multistack capability of the machine allowed it to produce 2D and 3D bends in a single length of tube – something it did not have the capacity to do before. The eMOB-100 also enabled a new processing capability in variable radius roll bending - something it has already been utilising and incorporating into the design of the Type 26.

BLM Group, a specialist in the metal tube processing sector, showcased its LS5 (sheet metal cutting) and LC5 (tube cutting) systems at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (October 2018). Both systems offer material shuttling modules for loading and unloading and automated vertical storage lift systems for flat sheet and tube.

"An automatic tube processing line can be integrated to the LS5 system to create LC5, a powerful combined system for tube and sheet metal laser cutting," BLM Group told ISMR. "LS5 and LC5 systems are compact to reduce floor space requirements and minimise installation times. They offer a wide-access work area (for ease of use and maintenance) and are designed to be modular and versatile, with adaptable configurations, interconnected, with the ability to connect to other systems and communicate to process management systems in an Industry 4.0 environment."

The LC5 tube processing system is an automatic system that cuts tubes in diameters of up to 120mm. The tube processing module is autonomous from the sheet metal processing module, only sharing the laser cutting head. It uses an independent operational panel to control the entire facility during tube processing. LS5 and LC5 are offered with a fibre laser source of up to 8kW.

At EuroBLECH 2018, BLM also showcased its LT-FREE, a 5-axis laser cutting system for the processing of curved tubes and three-dimensional deep-drawn components as another way of portraying the conjunction between sheet and tube. As the latest member of the Lasertube family, it also displayed the advanced LT7 system. Another highlight at the exhibition was its flexible bending system, E-TURN. The B_RIGHT and B_TOOLS systems communicate with each other. It also demonstrated applications for its software-suite, BLMelements.

A gigantic fully electric multiple-radius tube bending machine, featuring transport boost technology, was showcased at FABTECH 2018 in Atlanta (USA) and delivered to an automotive customer in the U.S. at the end of last year. A vertical height-adjustable pressure die moves to the required bending level in each individual case. This allows the requisite bending forces to be gauged to consistently apply the correct contact pressure to the tubes and pipes to be bent.
