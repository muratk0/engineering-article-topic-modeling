# Compact metal bending operations

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Sheet processing manufacturers often struggle with the same problem; limited space for their production. The ByBend Star 120, the latest member of Bystronic's family of press brakes, offers a solution as it has a minimal space requirement. The Swiss sheet-metal specialist offers users bending technology for high-level requirements on process speed, flexibility and accuracy.

"Bending small-to-medium-sized parts in a variety of sheet thicknesses and materials in higher-tensile strength classes are a breeze for users. The ByBend Star 120 is the compact but rapid bending machine for [the most demanding requirements] because it bends everything from small to medium-sized parts in a variety of sheet thicknesses, up to a length of 6.73 ft.," outlined Bystronic.

“The ByBend Star 120's dimensions are modest. With a length of just over 8.53 ft, a width of approximately 6.23 ft and a height of 9.35 ft, it can fit in any production hall. In contrast, the bending power is an impressive 132,28 tn. sh.," continued Bystronic.

The ByBend Star 120 works rapidly and, due to very short cycle times, is designed to minimise the costs of bending (particularly for small and medium-sized parts). Operators can spare themselves elaborate bending angle measurements by using the machine's "LAMS" (Laser Angle Measuring System) functionality.

The “FastBend Plus" function offers personal protection with high productivity and reduced set-up time. Users can get started with bending right away. As a result, bending small-to-medium-sized parts in a variety of sheet thicknesses and materials in higher tensile strength classes is easy, according to Bystronic.

"The new ByBend Star 120 is fast, flexible and sustainably efficient. Thanks to energy-efficient servo hydraulics, up to 30 per cent can be saved in energy with 20 per cent more speed in return. The entire bending process is controlled with just a few swipes of the finger on a 21.5-inch touch screen. The user interface, BySoft Cell Control Bend, relieves operators of much of the work. Taking into account material thickness and bending angle, BySoft Cell Control Bend determines the ideal bending process and suggests suitable tools," concluded Bystronic.
