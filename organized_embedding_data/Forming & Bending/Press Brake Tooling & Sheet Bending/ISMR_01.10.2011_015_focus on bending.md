# FOCUS ON BENDING

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

Safan developed a new breed of machines for automated sheet metal processing, dubbed the I-Brake (an intelligent press brake with two robotic arms). Because the robotic arms are mounted on the frame horizontally to the left and the right, the floor in front of the press brake is free for the pallets containing the sheets to be bent. The use of two robots results in great flexibility. The robotic arms can work simultaneously for handling of one long sheet, but can also bend two products at the same time in a single cycle. For the Safan I-Brake, the SafanTouch Screen has been refined even further. With the integrated TS controls, all products can be bent using graphic programming.

Its R-brake concept has all the advantages of the servo electronic concept Safan has on the E-Brake series, such as less energy consumption, low noise, no oil, high accuracy, no crowning needed, fast cycle-times and low maintenance costs. For the off-line programming, Safan is offering RoboWave programming software which enables the user to make programmes for the bending cell for accurate simulation. Set-up times are reduced using this software. Most important is the man-machine interface.

The press beam of the TruBend 7036 Cell Edition can be divided into two 510-millimetre sections so that the gripper can reach into the machine and can also place parts from behind.

Italian innovator Starmatik has showcased its bending Minicell BHS (for high speed bending). The use of the modern high-speed electrical press brake together with the high performance robot enables reduced bending times, lowering operational costs and saving on energy.

ISMR watched the Minicell demonstration at the Group’s factory near Treviso. The robot controller handles both robot and press brake movements. The programming system reduces overall start-up and job-to-job set-up time to allow extremely small batch sizes. Such a concept overturns the traditional view that robotising press brakes is only cost effective for large batch runs.

The robotised system made by Starmatik allows the metal sheet to be followed during the entire bending cycle. The cell is composed of the following elements: an industrial robot equipped with a suction or magnetic gripper, two software solutions i.e. Easybend and Simuleasy which are used to create off-line bending programs for the robot and the press brake, a sheet loader with sheet thickness control, a line tracking system, an aligner, an overturner unit, an optical guard or interlocked gates for safety and pallets for offloading of folded parts.

Starmatik has a unique in-cycle, blank-loading system with a thickness control unit which reduces by 10 – 15 seconds the cycle time per piece. All components are standard and designed to suit any press brake brand. Set-up time is minimal and the modular design ensures compatibility with almost any CNC control press brake.

Techno Macchine’s Twister robot is designed for the automatic bending of small pieces, a compact and rapid robot designed specifically for this application. Its short axis run means a considerable reduction in production time. The bending robot comes standard with dedicated software that enables rapid development and simplifies programming, eliminating the learning curve for operators and making it easy to use.

The Twister moves along a linear axis of 4m, with parallel coupling to the press brake table, ensuring maximum flexibility for positioning in front of the bending stations and the maximum discharge capacity, and positioning away from the working space during use in manual mode. The other linear axis is the vertical axis. The robot moves along this axis to allow the maximum area of vertical discharge and maximum positioning flexibility during the bending cycle. The four interpolated axes grab any piece in any position during the bending process. The total workspace of the Twister covers 4000 x 1600 x 1300mm.

“Our TruBend Cell 7000 is the fastest system in the world for the automatic bending of small pieces. With a cycle time from an average of only 4 seconds per tilt, it delivers up to twice as high part throughput as conventional bending cells and could replace three manual bending units,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

This bending cell comprises essentially one TruBend 7036 Cell Edition press brake, a BendMaster robot and a LoadMaster Bend loading unit. All three systems operate under the command of a single central controller, without the need for interfaces. It is this sophisticated coordination that makes the short cycle time of four seconds per bend possible. Changing out the bending dies can also be automated with the ToolMaster Bend, from which the robot selects the dies, all fitted with chips for identification purposes. The components, including the system pallet and tracks, are fully encapsulated and require just 21 square metres of floor space.
