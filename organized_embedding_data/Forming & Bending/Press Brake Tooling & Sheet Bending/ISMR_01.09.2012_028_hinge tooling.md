# Hinge Tooling

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

Hinge tooling operation in the press is simple. Knuckles are formed from a pre-punched flat sheet containing the tabs for the forming operations. Two tools are used in three hits as follows:

“Using Mate Hinge Tooling, hinges can be formed in mild steel, aluminium and stainless steel in thicknesses ranging from 0.030 inch (0.80mm) to 0.060 inch (1.50mm) incorporating pin diameters from 0.062 inch (1.60mm) to 0.188 inch (4.77mm),” the manufacturer told ISMR.

FABRICATORS CAN form many different types, lengths and diameters of hinges for a variety of applications using Mate hinge tooling. By incorporating hinges into products such as enclosures or other metal cabinetry, the need for separate hinge components, fasteners, spot welds or assembly is eliminated.

• Hit one with Tool 1 produces the radius at the end of the knuckle 
• Hit two with Tool 1 produces the radius at the beginning of the knuckle 
• Hit three with Tool 2 curls the form to complete the knuckle.

“Forming hinges in a punch press using hinge tooling adds more value to the punching process,” added John Galich, Mate marketing manager. “It can eliminate the cost of specialised press brake or stamping tooling necessary to create hinge forms. Also, forms created in a punch press ensure greater accuracy compared to more manual secondary operations.” Email: marketing@mate.com
