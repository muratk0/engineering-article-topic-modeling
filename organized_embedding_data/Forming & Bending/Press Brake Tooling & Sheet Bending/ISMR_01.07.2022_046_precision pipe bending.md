# Precision pipe bending

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Unison Tube LLC of Danville, VA, USA, has assisted Morse Hydraulics, a U.S.-based provider of high-pressure hydraulic hoses and fittings, in removing its reliance on external suppliers for the bending of specialist pipe fittings and, in doing so, reducing the volume of bent components that it holds in stock.

Morse Hydraulics required a pipe bending machine that would offer "uncompromising levels of accuracy and repeatability and would also be well suited to a manufacturing environment specialising in both one-off and small batch runs."

"Our goal was two-fold," said Richie Damian, owner of Morse Hydraulics. "First and foremost, we wanted to carry out pipe bending in-house, to enable us to respond even more quickly to the varied needs of our customers. Secondly, as a knock-on benefit, we also wanted to reduce the significant levels of bent pipe fittings that outsourced production meant we were having to hold in stock."

Listening to Morse Hydraulics' pipe bending requirements prompted Stuart Singleton, VP of Unison Tube LLC, to recommend one of Unison's EvBend 2000 power-assisted CNC machines. Recently introduced by Unison to assist precision manufacturing companies that require low-volume tube and pipe production and prototyping, EvBend machines are CNC-controlled, manually operated three-axis models designed for precise multi-plane bending.

"Since the Unison EvBend 2000 was installed at our facility in Portland, Oregon, we have completely removed our reliance on outsourced pipe bending and have been able to significantly reduce our stockholding," added Richie Damian. "With bending on-site, we are better placed to deliver the high-quality hydraulic tube fittings that our customers demand, as well as custom bend even the most hard-to-find fittings and connectors. Unison's training ensured that we were producing saleable bent components from day one, and they are never more than a phone call away should we need them for guidance."

Unison EvBend machines are widely used across aerospace, Formula One and the oil & gas industry. The EvBend 1000 machine will precision-bend tube and pipe of up to 16mm OD, while the bigger EvBend 2000 machine, purchased by Morse Hydraulics, will precision-bend tube and pipe of up to 50mm OD.

Unison Tube LLC is the North American division of Unison Ltd, a UK manufacturer of tube and pipe bending machines offering a large range of all-electric benders for diameters from 4mm (5/32") to 275mm (10" schedule pipe). Available in single-stack multi-stack and right/left varieties, Unison machines are delivered to more than 20 countries globally.
