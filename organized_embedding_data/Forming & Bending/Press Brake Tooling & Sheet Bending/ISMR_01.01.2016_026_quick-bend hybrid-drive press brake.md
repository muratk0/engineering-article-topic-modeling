# Quick-bend, hybrid-drive press brake

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

BOSCHERT GMBH has launched a dynamic hybrid-drive press brake, the Quick-Bend, with energy savings of up to 80%. Quick and nearly silent, the Quick-Bend only uses electrical energy during the bending process. Based on the successful PROFI 28 CNC and PROFI 56 CNC press brakes series, with bending lengths ranging from 1000 to 2200mm, the designers developed a press brake with an innovative electric-hydraulic hybrid drive, characterized by a combined E-motor and pump unit.

“The pump builds up the required pressure immediately at the start, the upper beam moves rapidly down (250mm/s) in the working position (Y-axis/stroke = 200mm), then sets the pressing force for the bending process and proceeds with a high retraction speed (200mm/s) back to the starting position. The direct-drive pump motor runs only when the foot pedal is depressed, and only until the return stroke has been completed or the foot pedal is no longer pressed,” explained the manufacturer. “This can save up to 80% of electrical energy compared to conventional hydraulic presses.”

Secondly, the direct coupling of the electric motor and pump provides an immediate pressure build-up for a significantly higher dynamic of motion, which minimises non-productive time. The high energy efficiency, despite very powerful hybrid drive-generated press forces of 280kN or 560kN, brings the Quick Bend press brakes to the same level as their hydraulicallydriven PROFI 28/1000 NC and CNC PROFI 28/1000 sisters.”

The bending speed range is 8 to 10mm/s and repeatability is +/- 0.02mm. The linear-guided back gauge (X axis) and height-adjustable finger stops (R-axis), along with the ROL1 toolchange system, are included in the standard equipment. ■
