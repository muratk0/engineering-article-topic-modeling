# Compact bending with ByBend Star 120

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Where applications are too large for a "small" machine and too small for a "large" machine, a special solution is needed. Bystronic offers this with its new and compact ByBend Star 120, the latest member of its family of press brakes. With a minimal space requirement, it offers users metal bending technology for exacting demands on process speed, flexibility and accuracy.

“Bending small to medium-sized parts in a variety of sheet thicknesses and materials in higher- tensile strength classes are possible. ByBend Star 120 bends everything—from small to medium-sized parts in a variety of sheet thicknesses, up to a length of 2.05m. Its dimensions are modest; a length of just over 2.6m, a width of approximately 1.9m and a height of 2.85m, it is guaranteed to fit into any production hall. In contrast, the bending power is an impressive 120 tons," Bystronic told ISMR.

"The ByBend Star 120 works very quickly and, due to very short cycle times, minimises the costs of bending (particularly for small and medium-sized parts). Operators can spare themselves elaborate bending angle measurements as the 'LAMS' function (Laser Angle Measuring System) takes care of these precisely and automatically. The perfect bending result can therefore be achieved from the first part," it added.

The 'FastBend Plus' function offers personal protection with productivity and also reduces set-up time. Users can get started with bending right away. The new ByBend Star 120 is also sustainably efficient, featuring energy-efficient servo hydraulics so that up to a reported 30 per cent of energy can be saved (with 20 per cent more speed in return) according to the Swiss sheet metal specialist.

The entire bending process is controlled with a few swipes of the finger on a 21.5-inch touch screen. The user interface, BySoft Cell Control Bend, relieves operators of much of the work. Taking account of the material thickness and bending angle, BySoft Cell Control Bend determines the ideal bending process and suggests suitable tools.

In a separate recent development, Bystronic's ByCut Smart laser cutting system is now available in 4020 size (for sheets measuring 4 x 2m). It can be equipped with laser powers ranging from 3-15kW and is “ready for automation."
