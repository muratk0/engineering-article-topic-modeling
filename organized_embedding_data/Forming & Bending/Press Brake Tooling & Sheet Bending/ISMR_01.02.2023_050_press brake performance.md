# Press brake performance

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

IRIS Plus with BendVision technology.

Lazer Safe Pty Ltd is a global specialist in press brake safety and performance technology, delivering solutions and products to press brake manufacturers and users around the world.

"We partner with press brake manufacturers to embed systems and technology into machine designs to achieve the highest possible level of functionality, safety and performance. We also offer advanced guarding systems for the pressbrake retrofit market," it explained.

Its press brake safety and performance technology includes autonomous guarding systems, wireless foot pedals and BendVision high-speed angle control.

“IRIS Plus delivers advanced optical guarding combined with BendVision image processing. BendVision processes images of the press brake tools and material during the bending process and transfers real-time data to the CNC during bending. The press brake manufacturer can use BendVision data to enable a wide range of customised functions and processes such as real-time angle measurement, angle control, tool recognition and more," it said.■
