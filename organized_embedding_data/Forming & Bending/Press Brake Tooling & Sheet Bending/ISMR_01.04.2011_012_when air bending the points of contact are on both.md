## BENDING CELLS

TRUMPF TruBend Cells, its family of automated bending systems, now has a new addition with the TruBend Cell 7000 – described as ‘the world’s fastest bending system for small components’. With an average cycle time of just 4 seconds per bend, this new system delivers a parts flow rate that is twice as fast as conventional bending cells. Thanks to this productivity, it can effectively take the place of two or three manual bending machines.

The elements that make up the TruBend Cell 7000 account for this speed and performance: a TruBend 7036 press brake (cell edition) which is optimised for automated use – the BendMaster robot, the LoadMaster Bend loading unit, a pallet system and tracks. The complete, enclosed system requires 21 square metres of shop floor space.

The TruBend 7036 cell edition has a press force of 36 tonnes and uses high torque motors to drive the press beam; maximum working speed is an impressive 50mm/s. A lightweight, high speed back gauge, with intelligent sensors, assists in the quick positioning of parts for bending.

To accommodate the robotic gripper, the press beam is divided at the centre to create the necessary open space. This makes it possible for the BendMaster grippers to move right or left of each clamp, reach into the machine and also place parts from behind the beam.
