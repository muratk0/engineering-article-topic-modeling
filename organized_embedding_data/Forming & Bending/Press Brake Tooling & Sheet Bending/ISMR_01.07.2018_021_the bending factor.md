# THE BENDING FACTOR

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Safety, environmental factors, increased accuracy, ease of operation and the move to Industry 4.0 have all impacted bending system design”

Sheet metal is bent when it is forced between two tools by a press brake; an upper tool (known as a punch) and a bottom tool (known as a die). The press brake controls the movement of either the punch or the die and provides the press force using hydraulic rams or electrical servo motors. The bend angle is predominantly determined by the depth of penetration of the punch within the die.

In contrast, sheet metal folders support the part to be bent on the back-gauge table, instead of being supported by the operator (as on press brakes). They are also capable of bending different angles and material thicknesses with the same set of tooling, therefore eliminating some set-up time. Panel benders can process different types of multiple folds as well as complex radii bending. They require very little set-up time and automatically bend metal at much faster rates than a manual sheet metal bender.

Safety, environmental factors (including energy efficiency and noise reduction), increased accuracy and ease of operation requirements, as well as the move towards Industry 4.0, have affected machine tool design. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake, folder and panelbending technology developments in the global market.

## The bending factor

Portugese manufacturer, ADIRA, has developed the HD Greenbender, which it dubs the first "dematerialised" heavy duty press brake in the world, designed with a topology optimisation technique.

"The patented multi-frame concept involves a very deep throat and avoids the need for pit installation in long machines, for a fraction of the steel needed when compared to conventional press brakes," the manufacturer told ISMR.

BB3515, the latest member of ADIRA's electric press brake Bluebender family, has a bending force of 35 tons (1.5 metre length) and features rapid cycle times with very low energy consumption.

Japanese manufacturer AMADA presented its latest HG1003ATC bending system for its V-factory digital sheet metal at the SIMM2018 exhibition. The servo/ hydraulic hybrid drive system features an Automatic Tool Changer (ATC) and an automatic bend indicator sensor plus a new AMNC 3i control and ID scanner.

The manufacturer also offers fully automated bending solutions and fibre laser technology, welding machines, press brakes and VPSS 3i software, which can simulate all processing operations at once. The company’s comprehensive range of press brakes is designed for flexibility, productivity and energy-saving (inverter-controlled stop/ start technology). Its HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new 'Smartphone-style' 3D control unit. New Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition, ready for manufacture.

Its HFE 3i, HG ATC and HG ARs press brake series encompass most bending scenarios from a manually-based scenario to a fully automated, robot-supported production cell.

AMADA UK unveiled its new compact press brake in December 2017. The new HFE-5012M2 has been developed to offer fast and comfortable bending of small parts with medium complexity. The machine provides 50 tonnes of bending force and a working width of 1270mm. The open height of the frame has been increased, enabling deeper box bending.

## Above; AMADA’s HG1003ATC bending system

ASCO GmbH provides technical solutions from long folding machines up to fully automated bending centres. Its Variobend long length folding machines and up and down bending machines are available (from 4 - 12 metres) to process steel of up to 2mm.

Turkish manufacturer Baykal’s APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. It has pioneered development of a fully automatic pole cutting and bending machine and boasts one of Europe’s largest facilities for sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.

Boschert GmbH has produced a dynamic hybrid-drive press brake, the Quick-Bend, with purported energy savings of up to 80%. Quick and nearly silent, the Quick-Bend only uses electrical energy during the bending process. Based on the PROFI 28 CNC and PROFI 56 CNC press brake series, with bending lengths ranging from 1000 – 2200mm, the designers developed a press brake with an innovative electric-hydraulic hybrid drive, characterised by a combined E-motor and pump unit. The bending speed range is 8 – 10mm/s and repeatability is +/- 0.02mm.

Below; ASCO folder  
Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, has enjoyed success with its Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre 'plug and play' press brake with rapid bending speeds. This small press brake has already won a German design award and is designed for rapid, efficient parts production. A new Mobile Bending Robot is available for feeding the Xpert 40, turning it into a compact, automated bending cell for producing 3D sheet metal components. The automation module is wheeled in front of the press brake and simply interfaced to it in a plug-and-play arrangement. In minutes, users can set up the robotics and load sheet metal blanks for bending. Programming is carried out offline and ByVision Bending software allows data to be imported without interrupting the job currently in progress.

Above; Bystronic Xpert 250

Bystronic has introduced a new app that monitors, in real-time, all processes taking place within its laser cutting machines, pressbrakes and water jet cutting machines. Called ByCockpit, the software fits neatly with the digital networking requirements of Industry 4.0. It evaluates the collected data and generates status reports, allowing factory output to be optimised.

Bystronic and Lantek are also developing an MES software solution for sheet metal part production. The Bystronic MES interlinks all the manufacturing steps and guides sheet metal products on the ideal path through the production process, much like an intelligent navigation system. Bystronic is also acquiring 70 per cent of the shares of Antil S.p.A. This majority shareholding in the Italian specialist for automated sheet metal processing will enable Bystronic to expand its existing range of technologies and services in automation.

Group Rhodes, through its Rhodes Interform business, has announced a new working partnership with the German manufacturer, Dr. Hochstrate Maschinenbau GmbH. The partnership will involve Rhodes Interform acting as distributor for Dr. Hochstrate Maschinenbau GmbH in the UK and Ireland for its range of hydraulic folders and guillotines. Dr. Hochstrate Maschinenbau, based in the Ennepe-Rhur area of Germany (near Dortmund), has over the past 40 years become well known for manufacturing folders for sheet metal processing.

CIDAN Machinery Sweden AB announced the acquisition of 100% of shares in Austrian Forstner Maschinenbau GmbH, effective at the end of December 2017. Its redesigned PRO Z Up/Down folder is for folding complex parts with reduced handling. The patented OED is designed to ensure maximum pressure and capacity, regardless of tool height. Automatic CNC sheet thickness and folding centre adjustment, without requiring crowning, is a key feature of the machine.

Its PIVOTAL folding machine is now able to bend 360° – it showcased a concept folder that can produce a four-sided part to reduce welding at EuroBLECH 2016. Also on display was its 3100 x 3.0mm FORMA folding machine with new clamping beam drive technology and redesigned side frames as well as its new CTL-line COMPACT-12.

At LAMIERA 2017 in Milan, Colgar highlighted one of its most recent installations for tandem press brakes installed by FIDA G, what it dubbed 'the longest tandem system in Europe mainly dedicated to the production of telescopic arms'. The total bending length is 21 metres using a tandem press brake (one with 14m length and one with 7m length) and a total installed power of 3,600 tons and concentrated charge of 500 tons/metre.

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. At EuroBLECH 2016, it launched three new press brakes – its green FX Servo press brake uses electric technology for low energy consumption (approximately 50% lower, compared to hydraulic press brakes). With a 4-axis back gauge, full-length bends can be made between side columns, with no obstructions, and the machine is almost silent in operation. Sliding front support arms assist the operator for easier material handling. This model was also on display at its booth at Blechexpo 2017.

Above; Ermaksan’s green press brake FX Servo 1003

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the Evolution II (hybrid), Speed Bend Pro, the Power Bend Pro, the Power-Bend Falcon, the Micro-Bend, the Eco-Bend Expert and the Heavy-Duty press brake (featuring a hydrodynamic hybrid crowning system, 6-axis servo motor back-gauge system and special top and bottom tools)

Italian company F.lli Vimercati has developed e.TERNA, a hybrid bending system. Energy savings of 30-40% and 30% decreases in machine cycle times, it says, are key results. This system can be applied to all the bending models in its range, without limits in stroke, power or dimensions.

Fiessler Elektronik GmbH & Co. KG has cited its AKAS 3P as 'the first co-moving pressbrake safety system worldwide which provides high-speed operation up to the clamp point'. Using the new press brake safety system, it is now possible to operate and close a pressbrake at high-speed to the clamp point.

Fintek Industry Company Limited provides sheet metal machines, such as hydraulic press brakes, shearing, folding, rolling machines etc. With over 30 years of experience in the manufacturing of machines, it has become one of the largest companies in the machine tool industry in Taiwan.

Italian specialist Gasparini offers a press brake range that includes the entry-level X-Press Easy and the X-press ECO energysaving system that increases speed and lowers energy consumption using an inverter motor drive. It is available as an option for the X-Press product line. The ECO inverter motor drive is leaner and greener, delivering quiet operation and faster cycle times. Press brake tonnage starts from 25 tons up to 3000 tons and from 4 to more than 11 axes.
