Mandrel tube bending machines integrate with automatic bending cells

Schwarze-Robitec offers the MR series of mandrel tube bending machines. The freely programmable machines can be integrated into existing production processes in automatic bending cells.

The cold bending process helps ensure fast, energy-efficient machining while maintaining the microstructure of the material. The use of a bending mandrel permits the production of very small radii, even thin-walled tubes.

The machines are designed for the efficient production of tubes with different radii and bend-in-bend geometries. Fitted with stacked bending tools, they help ensure minimal setup times, the company reports. For each machine, the company offers a modular, configurable accessory program that can adapt to specific project demands and user requirements.

Bending tool’s rollers rotate under force to create clean bend

Mate Precision Tooling has expanded its press brake tooling product line with the addition of CleanBend™ forming technology. The tool is available in both European precision-style and Wila TRUMPF

®

-style tooling and can be adapted to American precision-style tooling.

It is designed with two opposing spring-loaded, half-moon rollers that rotate as force is applied in the middle of the die, resulting in a clean bend with little to no marking of the sheet metal, the company reports.

Primary applications include bending short flange, flange to a diagonal sharp, across pre-existing holes, and across a notch or gap. The tool is suitable for use on sensitive or delicate materials, highly polished materials, painted or coated surfaces, materials with foil or plastic coverings, and tread plate.

Magnetic drills redesigned with pressure indicator, material and thickness detector

Walter Surface Technologies has introduced the newly redesigned Icecut 100 and Icecut 200 magnetic drills. They include the addition of the Accudrill pressure indicator, as well as a material and thickness detector.

Redesigned for heavy-duty applications, the safety amp meter, located in the central control panel, prevents the motor from overloading and overheating. The material and thickness detector will not allow the drill to start when the magnetic holding power is insufficient to secure the unit to the workpiece and operate safely.

The 100 model is the most compact, portable unit available, the manufacturer states. Designed for off-site drilling, it weighs 22 lbs. and offers 850 W of power and 600 RPM. The 200 model is a two-speed unit engineered for heavyduty applications. Weighing 27 lbs., it operates at 300 and 600 RPM, allowing users to cut smaller and larger holes with precision, the manufacturer reports.

Bridge decking roll forming line changes over quickly from one profile to another

Samco Machinery has introduced a continuous bridge deck roll forming line with a postcut operation. Four profiles—2, 2.5, 3, and 4 in.—are roll formed, end formed in the press, and cut to length. Rafted tops (cassettes) with quickcoupling disconnects provide quick changeovers from one profile to another.

The line features a 20K single-ended uncoiler with coil car, hydraulically powered, driven with mandrel expansion. The hydraulically powered up/down threading table assists in threading raw materials into the system. The length control system helps operators set the length and quantity.

The 28-pass rafted roll former features a gearbox drive at every station. The stationary hydraulic cutoff press is suitable for stop-and-go cut and form operations. The 20-ft., nonpowered runout roller exit conveyor pushes completed parts out.

Compact material handling system loads/unloads laser cutting machine

Bystronic has introduced the larger format 4020 (6- by 12-ft. material size) of its ByTrans Extended compact material handling system. Loading and unloading of materials to the laser cutting machine takes 75 sec. so that the system is faster than the shortest cutting plan.

The short cycle time, which is independent of the sheet metal thickness, helps maximize overall throughput, the manufacturer states. With a small, inline footprint, the system handles all sheet thicknesses up to 0.75-in. plate. It offers two raw material locations and one unload location. The second raw material location also can serve as the location for the unloading of automatically sorted parts.
