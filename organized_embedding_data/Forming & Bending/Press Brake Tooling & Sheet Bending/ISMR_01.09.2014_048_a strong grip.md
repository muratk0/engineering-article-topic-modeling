# A strong grip

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

SAMCO MACHINERY manufactures roll forming and stamping equipment for several industries. Among its latest partners is Nucap, a global leader in cold form stamping for vehicle brake system components. Nucap has developed a product called GRIP Metal (bonding material configured to requested specifications and available in a range of thicknesses and hook depths in steel, aluminium, copper, brass and other metals).

Nucap approached approximately 20 roll forming manufacturers to enquire about the possibility of roll forming this innovative material. The only positive response, it said, came from Samco, a Toronto neighbour. Samco has successfully formed GRIP Metal and expects to be manufacturing forming machines for Nucap shortly.

“We look to partner with companies willing to take on a challenge,” said Rahul Pereira, marketing manager of Nucap. “Samco was willing to take on the challenge of forming GRIP Metal. We’re in the process of testing its use with various substrates and expect to begin production of GRIP Metal components for various practical applications, stamped and formed by Samco equipment, by the end of the year.”

Maintaining ISO 9001 certification, Samco manufactures rollforming machines, decoilers, roll form dies and presses and supplies a multitude of customers worldwide in various industries. See www.samco-machinery.com ■
