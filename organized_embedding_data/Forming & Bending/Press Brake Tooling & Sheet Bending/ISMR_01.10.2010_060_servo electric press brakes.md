# SERVO ELECTRIC PRESS BRAKES

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

During the coming EuroBLECH exhibition, SAFAN will focus on the expansion of the E-Brake series at the top and lower ends of the range. At the top end, SAFAN will premiere the SAFAN E-Brake Dual Drive 300T 4100. This press brake features servo electric bending. At the lower end of the series, a small, new E-Brake will be introduced. It is a 20-ton servo-electronic press brake with a bending length of 850mm.

“The unique feature of the new SAFAN E-Brake Dual Drive 300T 4100 is that the single pulley drive used on machines of up to 200 tons is extended to a double-pulley drive which creates a very innovative and compact drive system. This new system is patented,” Safan told ISMR.

Another innovation is the specially developed lower beam which compensates for any minimal distortion during bending. This means that, even for a 300-ton 4-metre press brake, crowning is not necessary.

“Because there are no hydraulic valves, extremely quick cycle times are possible. They are on average 30% lower compared with conventional hydraulic press brakes. The servo-electronic drive system uses up to 50% less energy because the machine only uses energy when the pedal is pushed,” said Safan.

Options for the SAFAN E-Brake Dual Drive 300T 4100 include the SAFAN E-Bend M angle measuring system and the SAFAN E-Bend S sheet thickness measuring system. A popular option is the bending aid system called SAFAN E-Mate. For larger and heavier sheet sizes, it is a great help in maintaining accurate and ergonomic bending.

All SAFAN E-Brake press brakes at EuroBLECH are equipped with the new SAFAN E-Control press brake control. At EuroBLECH, Safan will also demonstrate the SAFAN R-Brake flexible bending cell in combination with ROBOWAVE of-line programming software. See www.safan.nl for more details.
