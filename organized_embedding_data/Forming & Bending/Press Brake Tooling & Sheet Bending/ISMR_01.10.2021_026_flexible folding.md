# Flexible folding

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Bending sheet steel with a thickness of up to 16mm over a working length of 3240mm is now possible with the Schröder EVO Heavy Duty swivel-folding process. The EVO Heavy Duty, equipped with hydraulic compact drives, takes over tasks that previously required press brakes. For flexible use, the machine features new control software, including the option of offline programming, to maximize machine availability and productivity.

"Swivel folding increases productivity due to easier workpiece handling and enables greater accuracies. Its innovative hydraulic compact drives offer a clamping force of 200 t. The main drive has an output of 37kW," explained the German bending specialist.

"The clamping and folding beam drives on both sides ensure rapid closing of the clamping beam and swivelling of the folding beam. The clamping beam stroke of 500mm offers plenty of space for workpieces- 16mm-thick sheet steel can be formed up to a bending angle of 180°, which means that pre-bent sheets can also be closed," added the manufacturer.

The EVO Heavy Duty is operated with the POS2000 Professional or optional POS3000 Professional control. The folding programs can also be prepared offline in the Schröder Unfold CAD/CAM solution. It was showcased at FABTECH in Chicago, USA, this September. The successful integration of the Fasti Company in 2006 and its worldwide presence makes the Schröder Group a leading provider of machines for bending, cutting, beading, flanging and circular bending all types of sheet metal. In 2021, the Schröder Group was expanded by addition of the tool manufacturer, SMU GmbH. Overall, the Schröder Group currently employs more than 300 people at various locations.
