POWERFUL CNC BENDING

The Nargesa MP1500CNC and MP3003CNC Press Brakes are designed for industrial-grade precision and robust performance. Equipped with CNC controls, these machines offer ease of use with high accuracy, making them ideal for bending complex parts with consistency. The MP1500CNC is tailored for medium-duty work with a maximum bending length of 1500 mm, while the MP3003CNC is built for bending lengths up to 3000 mm. Both models ensure quality and efficiency, suited for a wide range of metalworking tasks.

THE NARGESA CNC CONTROL

The 10″ high-resolution touchscreen enables intuitive programming, tool management, simulations, and diagnostics with 20+ language options. Connectivity features include an Ethernet port for remote management, ensuring seamless network integration.

ENHANCED FEATURES

1 LED LIGHTING:

The LED directional light strip for lighting the bending zone permits the operator to work in optimum visibility conditions.

2 SIDE DOORS:

The side doors offer a large opening angle which makes it easier to change punches and dies. Their methacrylate viewing window allows precision bending operations to be inspected safely.

3 SLIDING ARMS:

The front arms which slide by means of recirculating ball screw guides enable the sheet metal to be held in position securely and can be fully adjusted both horizontally and vertically by hand.

4 LASER SENSOR:

The Laser has a metric guide to facilitate vertical adjustment and can easily be lifted up and removed when work tools (punches and dies) need changing.

5 S-BEND:

Is included and enables complex pieces to be produced, facilitating bending at both ends of the machine.

PRECISION BENDING:

For maximum bending precision a GIVI MISURE high resolution (0.00020″ / 0.005mm) optical encoder is used, which controls the vertical position of the bending axis (Y axis) for excellent reliability in each operation. Combined with control of lowering speed, this ensures punch holder plate vertical axis precision for bending operations and gives positioning error of less than 0.00040″.

BACK GAUGE:

The back gauge is fitted with ESA servo motors and precision BOSCH REXROTH recirculating ball screw guides which permit automatic positioning of X and R axes with 0.05 mm precision. It also has a manual adjustment system for the Z axis which enables the gauge calipers to be slid easily.
