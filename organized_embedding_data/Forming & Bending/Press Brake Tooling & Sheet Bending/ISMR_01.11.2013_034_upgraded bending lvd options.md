# UPGRADED BENDING LVD OPTIONS

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

LVD Company NV has upgraded and expanded its PPS Series of precision hydraulic press brakes for increased performance to offer a wider range of models of up to 320 tons in capacity. New, higher specification PPS models offer greater flexibility and performance with increased open height, stroke and operating speeds.

PPS series machines are available with LVD, Wila, Universal or US-style tooling systems.

“The latest models boast new style back gauges: from the standard Xaxis back gauge for better, more accurate gauging and a hand-wheel Raxis adjustment to the optional X-R back gauge equipped with a programmable R-axis,” explained LVD.

Available in 3- or 4-axis configurations, PPS press brake models include LVD’s touch screen control unit equipped with LVD’s graphic user interface, enabling quick programming of simple parts. Three new table sizes, 140mm for Multi-V 70mm square, 180mm for Multi-V 90mm square and 250mm for Multi-V 130mm are available, depending upon the model. See www.lvdgroup.com

## Laser cladding in focus

SULZER METCO is a global leader in the development and production of coating solutions-based materials and devices. Solutions to optimise the performance, efficiency and reliability of industrial components are covered by a wide range of surface technologies such as thermal spraying, laser cladding and other methods. Its new portfolio will be extended to materials and services for laser cladding, with systems for laser cladding.

Its Clad™ systems provide complete laser cladding systems, materials and services from a single source. The robot-based laser cladding systems with coordinated materials are for applying functional coatings against wear and corrosion in new and used parts, as well as repair of components. In addition, near net shape, three-dimensional processing steps can be carried out using a CAD model and CAM software. Clad laser cladding materials provide high quality, consistently reproducible coating results and accelerate the cladding process. See www.sulzer.com
