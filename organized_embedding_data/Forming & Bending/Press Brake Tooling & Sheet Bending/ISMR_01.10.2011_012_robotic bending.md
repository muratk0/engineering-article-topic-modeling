# ROBOTIC BENDING

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

ISMR examines the latest upgrades and developments in robotic sheet bending technologies.

Robotic bending is no longer the enclave of large batch throughput – smaller batches can now be economically processed using this method. Manufacturers are starting to realise the benefits offered from increased time savings, better efficiency levels (more parts per day) and the lack of limits on the speed of the press brake for a robot.

## NEW SYSTEMS AND UPGRADES

Robots can work 24/7 with no breaks – adding one to a line can free up an operator who can then be deployed elsewhere more profitably. As long as consistent part quality is maintained, repeatability is assured. The introduction of offline programming software for robotic bending systems further eliminates machine downtime.

When Mid-West Machine Products began considering upgrading their robotic bending technology, it researched several options to determine which equipment would best fit their needs. It decided to purchase an Accurpress Accell 9-axis press brake and integrate it with a MotoMan robot.
