# Intelligent straightening device

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Roll-forming specialist, DREISTERN GmbH & Co. KG, has launched a new RF4.0 intelligent straightening device, designed to eliminate errors in a profile’s longitudinal geometry. Part of its ‘Roll Forming 4.0’ portfolio, the device is built to:

- Immediately identify changes in the roll-forming process.
- Alert operators of the need for action.
- Provide information about the cause of the problem and the most effective countermeasure.
- Prevent material waste to increase efficiency and save resources.

'Sensors, used in the axes of the straightening device, detect the acting horizontal and vertical loads. If the longitudinal geometry changes, these forces also change. Changing geometries can therefore be detected early. When that happens, the force applied by the straightening device can be readjusted to guarantee profile straightness," explained the German roll-forming specialist.

Sensor values are presented in a specially developed app. Apart from the integrated target range adjustment, the app supports the operator by evaluating the sensor signals so that they are transferred into three characteristic signals. These three measured values are connected to the three basic defect types: bow, camber and twist. The information on the app gives the operator direct hints on the causes of the current defect and guides him/her through the process optimisation.

Straightening devices have been established in roll-forming systems for a long time. Their basic task is to compensate for any deviations in the longitudinal direction (especially camber, bow and twist).

"The cause of these geometry defects are strains in the longitudinal profile direction being distributed irregularly across the cross-section, which 'relax' again after the forming process. To correct these defects, one or more straightening devices are used in roll-forming systems, usually at the end of the roll-forming line. These stress the profile so that inverse longitudinal strains are applied, which can compensate for the deviations caused in the roll-forming process," said DREISTERN.

Founded in 1949, DREISTERN GmbH & Co. KG is a specialist in roll-forming technology and complete process integration. More than 2,000 of its units are in use, producing more than 8,000 different profiles. It has applied for over 40 patents.
