# Dutch subcontractor Tosec BV has invested in a new and customised H-Brake 320T Ultra for a wider range and variety of sheet-metal bending options.

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

Tosec BV is a Dutch subcontractor based in Zwolle that specialises entirely in sheet-metal working. The company performs all its operations at one location: cutting, machining, bending and welding. It has several press brakes, including a swivel bending machine, and recently purchased a new H-Brake 320T Ultra from Dutch bending specialist, SafanDarley.

“We are known for being able to deliver high-quality products quickly," explained company manager, Ruben Nieboer. “These are generally products in small batches or single pieces. Often, we don't know today what we are going to make next month. That's why SafanDarley's new H-Brake 320T Ultra suits us perfectly. The custom-made press brake is widely applicable."

Tosec has over 35 years of experience in metal edging and has a range of press brakes of different sizes and capabilities. Press-brake tool clamping at Tosec is standardised, making the tools interchangeable. Due to its wide variety of tooling, Tosec can be very flexible when it comes to different angles, dimensions and options for bending sheet metal. It has even developed its own tool-change system and likes to rely on its own expertise, rather than third parties, to overcome sheet-metal bending challenges.

## A proud history

Tosec's history goes back more than 100 years. Some 25 years ago, it was taken over by Tollenaar Industries, currently run by brothers Frans and Roland Tollenaar.

"We are a real family business with a very flat organisation and short lines of communication. That's definitely not marketing talk ..." laughed Ruben Nieboer.

The parent company has two more branches in Zwolle focused on engineering and automation as well as production sites in Germany and South Africa.

"For the Netherlands, Tosec is the manufacturing company, [with all operations at] Zwolle with some 140 colleagues. Sister company Teqram, for example, focuses on robotisation. The machines that it makes are in production with us and now also with many other customers," added Ruben Nieboer.

## Company ethos

Variety is important for Tosec.

"We consciously choose to produce small and medium-sized batches and single pieces, because this ensures variation in the work. [This means] setting up the machine every time, looking at drawings and checking products. Parts can be big, small, thick or thin. We also work with different types of steel, such as very hard steel, Hardox, which is difficult to bend, or high-strength steel, which is very tough. This constantly requires a different approach," explained Ruben Nieboer.

“That is why no day is ever the same for the people in the office, our setters and our colleagues in the dispatch department. We make a wide variety of products for machine builders, the trailer and crane industry, the agricultural sector and offshore companies, among others. In total, we have over 500 customers throughout the Netherlands," he continued.

Tosec's relationship with SafanDarley goes back a long way.

“Before the companies Safan and Darley merged, we already had Darley press brakes. So, we have known the company for decades," explained Nieboer.

However, the company did not opt overnight for its most recent investment. Several competing brands were assessed.

“In the end, the decisive factor was the opinion of our people on the shop floor. Based on their experiences with the machines and SafanDarley's service, the management chose the new model," Nieboer explained.

## An eye on customisation

The H-Brake 320T Ultra supplied to Tosec is a special version with an enlarged Q-size.

“The machine was purchased as an expansion investment that needs to be widely applicable [versatile]. That means lots of power, wide, deep and high, so we can make almost anything on it. Moreover, it features an E-bend L angle measurement system. That saves a lot of time on the machine, because we are also constantly looking for staff. Another benefit for the future is the possibility of offline programming. We don't do that yet, but we will definitely use this option in the future," explained Ruben Nieboer.

SafanDarley's E-Bend L Blue angle measurement system uses two blue laser sensors one each side of the table. The new blue laser sensors offer an important benefit compared to the usual red sensors. Red laser light has an almost identical wavelength to natural light, which means the measurement is often affected by local conditions. The blue light spectrum of the E-Bend L Blue is much easier to distinguish from “natural" light so reflections and sunlight do not interfere with the measurement. This, SafanDarley told ISMR, guarantees a final product with high angle precision.

"All in all, SafanDarley was very thoughtful and looked at what was important to us. So, just like us, it also provides customised solutions," concluded Ruben Nieboer.
