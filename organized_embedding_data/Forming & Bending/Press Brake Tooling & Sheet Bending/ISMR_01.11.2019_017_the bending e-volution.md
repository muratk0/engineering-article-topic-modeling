# THE BENDING E-VOLUTION

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

Dutch bending specialist, SafanDarley, sees key opportunities for open software solutions in combination with press brakes and automation.

ISMR SAYS:

"SafanDarley has plans to launch an extensive range of software developments in 2020, focused around Industry 4.0 developments and the need for open access software in production lines"

Marco van Drie, Manager, Direct Sales & Products (left) and Marijn Laurensse, Director Development SafanDarley at Blechexpo in Stuttgart

Industry 4.0 continues to progress towards a factory that is fully controlled and managed digitally to create a process that is as efficient as possible. During Blechexpo 2019 in Stuttgart, Dutch bending specialist SafanDarley presented a complete programme of solutions for sheet metal working.

On its stand, the press brakes were connected to a workstation. All relevant information was available in a single overview on a dashboard. Its press brake of the future and the latest developments in its programming software were also showcased at EuroBLECH. SafanDarley also showcased its very first E-Brake from 1995, showing stand visitors at Blechexpo how the E-Brake has helped to create an efficient working environment.

## The bending factor

In 2018, SafanDarley introduced the H-Brake Hybrid 110T-1600. This has an extremely high tonnage range over a small working length. This compact, rapid machine is suitable for setting small, complex thicker sheet metal parts and is a good solution for small spaces. Development of the machine has been ongoing and, as of now, is also available in an Ergonomic variant, which is particularly useful for bending large batches.

During Blechexpo in Stuttgart, SafanDarley showcased a new model of Hybrid press brake, the H-Brake Hybrid+ 230T Ultra. The Hybrid+ series is an expansion of the existing Hybrid series and offers higher tonnages but also further benefits. At its core, there is an advanced dual hybrid drive with high return speed, high productivity and low energy consumption. As a result, says SafanDarley, costs are reduced and CO₂ emissions decreased. This machine, the manufacturer told ISMR, is an example of sustainable thinking in the Smart Industry.

"The new machine on our booth is the hybrid press brake. We are also highlighting energy savings on all our machines (energy and CO₂ savings) here. We started at 110 tons, then 170 tons and now we have a 230-ton machine. This is another advantage of the hybrid principle because the machine can adapt totally to the customer's wishes (i.e. in terms of stroke, opening, speed/acceleration of upper beam etc.). On other words, it offers higher specifications, can be customised and is characterised by low energy consumption," Marco van Drie, Manager, Direct Sales & Products, SafanDarley told ISMR.

"We are proud to be expanding our hybrid drive electrical press brakes aimed at saving energy. Our hydraulic machines also offer options such as start/stop systems to save energy. We are now extending our hybrid range of models up to 230 tonnes as well as our fully electric press brake line. We launched our first electric press brake twenty-four years ago, so next year is our anniversary."

At Blechexpo, SafanDarley also demonstrated its high-end E-Brake Ultra series. The E-Brake 50T-2050 Ultra is fitted, as standard, with a CNC crowning system and has a double screen to facilitate a paperfree working environment. The E-Brake Ultra can also be fitted with a 3D backgauge and patented E-Bend L Blue angle measurement system. On each machine, an animation was shown of the new intelligent bending aid. This bending aid is still under construction and will be shown live at the Techni-show Utrecht next year.

During Blechexpo, the Dutch manufacturer presented an E-Brake 40T with an extremely large Q-size. The standard E-Brake 40T is equipped with a Q-size of 590mm; the machine demonstrated at BlechExpo had a Q-size of 1090mm. This enlarged Q dimension makes it possible to bend products with very tall upstanding sides.

SafanDarley also demonstrated machines to ISMR from its standard supply range at Blechexpo, such as the E-Brake 35T Premium and the M-Shear 310-6 with 'return-to-sender' function.

## Towards Industry 4.0

One of the highlights on SafanDarley's booth was its Autopol offline bending simulation software, a modern 3D system for programming press brakes offline to unfold 3D CAD models. The optional Batch & Remote functionality enables the automatic processing of multiple components. It significantly reduces programming costs and reports the result of every step in the process.

All machines on the stand are linked to a workstation. The entire process, from offline programming, through to uploading programs to the machine and then reading information on the machine's productivity, is displayed on a dashboard with clear graphics.

"What we are showing here with Autopol software is its ability to check the status of different machines on the stand here at Blechexpo. It has a user-friendly interface and aims to save cost and time for our customers," explained Marijn Laurensse, Director Development, SafanDarley (responsible for sales, service and R&D).

However, SafanDarley has plans to launch an extensive range of software developments next year, focused around Industry 4.0 developments and the need for open access software in production lines.

"Next year will be a year of change for us, with a range of new developments. We will focus more on software solutions next year. Our goal for 2020 is to ensure that our software can communicate with other software and connect to other systems in an Industry 4.0 environment. This is a key consideration for us. It is important that we offer open software that is able to connect to other systems. We will be launching new versions of this software in 2020," added Marco van Drie.

The key point here is that it is not about just the machine, but the machine and the software integration as part of a complete production line, which may also include other processes and machines (such as laser-cutting or welding systems). SafanDarley's software will be designed to interface with these different operations and environments. The aim is to offer customers a total solution, as Marijn Laurensse explained.

"The key is to keep our technology/software simple for users and ensure connectedness to other systems. Next year, we will really highlight this capability and its Industry 4.0 applications. We will also incorporate our customers' requirements into the software development so that it stays relevant. Our ultimate aim is to be able to use the same single-source, open software across a multitude of machine types. This makes life simpler for customers. The software is easier to update and service, add new features etc. Customers will use our equipment as part of an integrated production line," underlined Laurensse.

"It is important that we share data in the machines with machine users – it really needs to be accessible to operators. We are therefore also looking at technologies such as cloud solutions, AI etc. to enable this. If we offer open solutions, this also helps users when it comes to predictive maintenance. They can analyse the machine data, which enables them to make better production decisions, save time/cost and establish better ways of working."

SafanDarley is working with other partners on further software development, including the human-machine interface (HMI) and communication between machines.

## Forecasts and trends

As part of its commitment to the U.S. market and following successful growth in the Midwest and south east, SafanDarley North America, LLC has relocated its U.S. facility to Waukesha, Wisconsin. 4 FTE has been hired to support growth in the U.S. market.

The move took place in September 2019. The new facility will provide Service, Help Desk Support, Parts, Applications Engineering, Robotics Support, Technical Sales and Administrative support for the company's distributors and customers in the USA, Canada and Mexico. The facility is within 25 miles of Milwaukee's Mitchell Airport and 90 miles from Chicago O'Hare Airport.

"2019 has been a good year for SafanDarley. There have, of course, been regional fluctuations. The German market has seen a 30 per cent drop but our sales in this region have been unaffected so far. Sales revenues are around the same as the year before. Sales in the U.S. and Benelux regions have also been strong this year. Overall sales and turnover look to be similar to last year (which was an excellent year for SafanDarley). Our turnover in 2018 was 45 million euros," continued Marco van Drie.

"Next year, however, may not be as strong. However, in years where the economy dips, we see greater opportunity for automation/special requests and more opportunities to help our customers with their production line efficiencies. Users look more critically at their overall production and come up with ideas to improve productivity and make further efficiencies – we can help them with that. This is a key strategic and technical objective for us. Our largest market is Europe, followed by U.S. We feel that the U.S. market still offers further potential for us but we expect issues next year because of the U.S.-China steel tariff situation."

## Robotics and automation

Robotics/automation is also part of this process and is becoming more attractive to help customers reach these productivity objectives. SafanDarley already offers robots with press brakes in its portfolio. Its R-Brake 130T integrates the press brake and robot into one system. It features fully automated tool and gripper change that is connected to the machine. Together with the product, they can be programmed offline using SafanDarley's RoboBend offline simulation software.

SafanDarley's E-Brake 35T Mini Cell is suitable for bending smaller-sized parts. The optional automatic tool and gripper change function makes it possible to quickly switch between different products. The tool-changer is built into the existing machine frame, resulting in a machine that takes up no more space than the standard E-Brake.

"Large batch production is largely behind us now – it's all 'fit to order' and small batches. When you want to make a success from an automated solution, you need to cut the time (particularly for small batch production). You also need to look at modular solutions and move solutions around to fit customer requirements. We work with FANUC as a robot supplier but have also developed a special robot interface which can be used with any robot manufacturer. Open access software and automation is key for this," explained Marco van Drie.

Both men also see greater customer demand for tandem press brakes (1000 tons, 500 tons etc.).

"10/12m machines are 5 per cent of the total production of customers. If 90 per cent are less than 8m, this makes tandem production more efficient (using two machines instead to combine, synchronise and scale-up production). We receive these requests from European customers in Italy and the Benelux countries," added van Drie.

## An eye on simplicity

However, it is the company's immediate focus on simplifying the operator's role on its pressbrakes that stands out during the conversation.

"All our press brakes have a split screen – one to display information for the operator and one for the software program that is running. If you look at developments in press brake tooling, you now have LED lights to show you where to put your tools and to highlight your back gauge fingers. The issue is always to make things visible for operators. We believe that data can always be shown in a better way, especially with smart tooling and automatic tool-change systems. It is all about making it simple for users with the combination of press brake, tooling and software," explained Marijn Laurensse.

"The fastest way of changing tools is not to change them. Automatic tool-changing systems can be expensive. Our software can generate a report to recommend as few tool changes as possible (saving time and money for customers). This feature is called the Tool Set-Up Optimiser."

With a keen eye on its customers' requirements, and the drive to implement new technological developments, SafanDarley is truly gearing up for the future.
