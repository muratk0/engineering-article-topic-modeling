# THE BENDING EQUATION

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

ISMR examines new bending innovations, systems and processes.

Bending is a flexible process through which many different shapes can be produced. Standard die sets are used to produce a wide variety of shapes. The material is placed on the die and positioned in place with stops and/or gauges. The upper part of the press, the ram with the appropriately shaped punch, descends and forms the V-shaped bend.

When air bending, the points of contact are on both sides of the material. The angle is determined by the depth of entry of the tool into the die plate.

With 3-point bending, the points of contact are all on the same side of the material. The angle is determined by the height adjustment in the bottom tool. Bottoming or coining is the bending process where the punch and the workpiece bottom on the die. This makes for a controlled angle with very little spring-back. Greater tonnage is required on this type of press than for air bending.

## NEW BENDING INNOVATIONS

Bystronic has introduced a new bending safety system, Fast Bend+, which not only improves the machine safety but also increases part output by some 20 per cent. Using a barcode scanner, the change between jobs can be made even more quickly. Reduced energy consumption and less machine noise characterise the Energy Saver. After a bend, as soon as the upper beam reaches the upper dead centre, the main motor of the Bystronic Xpert press brake switches off automatically until the next bending process is started.
