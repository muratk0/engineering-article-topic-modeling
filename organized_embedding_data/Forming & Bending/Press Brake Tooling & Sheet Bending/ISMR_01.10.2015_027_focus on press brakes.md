# FOCUS ON PRESS BRAKES

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

Haco is a specialist in sheet metal fabrication and routinely produces press brakes for a diverse group of industries. Its current portfolio includes press brakes with capacities from 40 to 2,000 tons and working lengths from 1.6 to 10m.

LVD Company nv has expanded its Dyna-Press Series of compact, portable, high speed electric press brakes with two 24-ton models that offer higher capacity and a longer working length.

“The Dyna-Press series is ideal for the costefficient bending of small parts. Rapid acceleration and deceleration of the electrical servo-driven ram deliver bending speeds of up to 25 mm/s. The new models can handle bend lengths of up to 1250mm and have 24 tons of bending force. The coupling of the ram and servomotors is done through two heavy duty ball screws to distribute force and tonnage evenly across the working length,” it told ISMR.

Compact and portable with a footprint of 1140 x 1760mm, the new Dyna-Press models save on valuable floor space and can still be conveniently relocated using a standard forklift.

The next generation of LVD’s Easy-Form® and PPEB Series of high performance press brakes feature a new, modern design that is more energy-efficient, easier to use and incorporates added safety features. The ToolCell, LVD’s press brake with an integrated tool-changing system, significantly reduces press brake set-up time.

LVD is now offering a 220-ton, 3-metre model with a large selection of top and bottom tooling stored in a tooling stadium/warehouse under the back gauge. An innovative gripper design is built into the machine’s back gauge finger and serves as the tool-changer mechanism. When a job is complete, the press brake automatically changes the top and bottom tooling as required for the next job. All tool stations are precisely positioned, eliminating the need for manual adjustments.

Mate Precision Tooling will be showcasing various press brake tooling solutions at the forthcoming FABTECH 2015 exhibition in Chicago from 9-12 November 2015 (booth S1944). These will include CleanBend™ forming technology and “Bevel Tang Style” press brake tooling.

“The new “Bevel Tang Style” press brake tooling is for use in newer Amada style press brakes. Enabling faster and safer tool changes, the press brake tooling features a punch tang geometry that is compatible with punch clamping systems requiring a bevelled receiving surface,” the manufacturer explained.

“Our new CleanBend forming technology for press brakes enables high quality sheet metal forming. CleanBend is designed for a wide range of applications including the bending of short flanges; bending a flange to a diagonal sharp or across existing holes and bending delicate or polished materials, including plastic coverings and more. Using rotating supports, the process mimics a folding operation with minimal to no marking of the sheet metal. CleanBend is available for European Precision, Wila, TRUMPF and American-style press brakes in a range of sizes from 8mm (0.315”) to 30mm (1.181”).”

Mate’s press brake tooling also includes a full array of European Precision and Wila TRUMPF-style punches, dies, standard specials, specials and accessories, available in segmented, full-length and halflength sizes.

PunchBend is Prima Power’s new, compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials, which processes pre-cut material automatically into ready-bent, high quality components. The essential characteristic of the concept is tight integration of all the elements needed – software, punching and bending technology as well as automation. Both punching and bending technology are servoelectric, making the new product part of the Prima Power Green Means® offering. The highend Prima Power servo-electric is included.

At EuroBLECH 2014, Prima Power also displayed, inside its FMS line, an updated version of its electric panel bender EBe5 with 2650mm of bending length and new NC control. Close to the panel bender was its 50-ton, 2m electrical press brake. All its machines are electrically-driven (no hydraulics) to reduce electricity consumption and maintenance as well as to be more environmentally sustainable.

Rolla-V is celebrating the release of its latest press brake tooling catalogue. With over 80 years of experience, it supplies a comprehensive range of press brake tools as well as the patented Rolla-V.

One of the highlights of Dutch press brake specialist, SafanDarley, at its EuroBLECH booth was the presentation of its new generation of servo-electric press brakes. Its well-known drive-based pulley principle has evolved to a new dual-drive machine concept offering further advantages than its E-brake concept. The new press brake boasts a C-frame and narrower press beam, with higher pressure load per metre. Other benefits of this new principle are rapid and direct positioning with higher acceleration and accuracy.

Another SafanDarley highlight at EuroBLECH was its new, E-Bend L laser angle measurement system to measure precise bends. It also showcased its augmented reality concept, the SafanDarley Eye. The software can be used for tool change on the press brake, for example, to give additional machine tool information (such as location data for the positioning of the products) to the operator through the glasses that he/she wears when using the machine. An innovative gripper design is built into the machine’s back gauge finger and serves as the tool-changer mechanism. It is a very innovative development.

SafanDarley made some recent significant investments last year in both its factories in the Netherlands. Last year, it invested over 2 million euros into the SafanDarley factory in Eijsden to make it more efficient for larger equipment (it now manufactures press brakes of up to 1250 tonnes).

The issue of bending and improving efficiency downstream from the cutting process is high on Italian manufacturer Salvagnini’s agenda. Its B3 ATA press brake is its benchmark for efficient sheet metal bending.

“The B3 is the only solution of its kind on the market and is the product of proprietary expertise in panel bending and press brake bending, combining the best of both worlds. Employing an original solution that teams design innovations with the latest technologies on the market, this press brake combines the power of a hydraulic press brake with the responsiveness and accuracy of an electric press brake, whilst maintaining the consumption levels associated with a hybrid system,” it told ISMR.

The B3 press brake in its ATA version employs automatic bending tool set-up to make the bending process even more efficient. Other Salvagnini bending solutions comprise panel benders such as the P4, P2 and P1 automatic panel benders in a range of 14 models and the FMS S4+P4 automatic panel production line. It will showcase the new P4lean-2520 panel bender at Blechexpo.

Salvagnini is also presenting SaM, its new proprietary app which provides real-time information on Salvagnini machine status on smartphone, tablet or PC using remote monitoring. By implementing the philosophy of Industry 4.0, this new tool allows the user to keep up-to-date with the status of the system and to receive messages and statistics on current production.

With its new TruBend Series 8000, TRUMPF is offering press brakes to handle especially big jobs. They process large-format and thick components with precise results. Both large open heights and bending lengths, along with press power of up to 1,000 tons, make the machines in the new TruBend Series 8000 true powerhouses. Featuring lengths of up to eight metres and bending depths of up to 500mm, these machines are designed for machining larger parts.

“In addition to their use on long profiles, the immense press power can also be concentrated on smaller parts with short bending sides. High strength and, at the same time, very thick sheets can be bent with no difficulty, thanks to high press power levels. The bending length also has advantages when bending smaller workpieces at multiple bending stations. Typical applications will be found in the working vehicle production industry and in agricultural vehicles and shipbuilding. Components for façades, roofs and gates, as well as door frames and commercial kitchens, can be produced with these machines,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

A mechanical wedge crowning facility features a precisely adjustable crowning curve. The adjustment is made automatically via a drive integrated into the machine table. Crowning, under CNC control, ensures uniform angular precision along the entire length of the bend. It is available in two load versions: up to 3,000 or up to 6,000 kilonewtons per metre. The crowning curve can be adjusted at individual points, every 250mm, to compensate for tolerances resulting from tool wear or special demands on the bent components. The TruBend Series 8000 is fitted with the LCB (Laser Controlled Bending) angle measurement system.

The latest addition to the TruBend 3000 range is the TruBend 3066 with a press force of 600kN and 2m bending length. It is supplied as standard with a two-axis back gauge but can also be specified with a four- or five-axis back gauge. This makes it possible to position sheet metal securely, even where complex part geometries are involved.

TRUMPF’s optoelectronic safety system, BendGuard, uses a light field to monitor the area beneath the upper tool. If there is an interruption, a hand breaking the light field for example, the machine automatically stops. The operator can therefore make full use of the machine’s full speed, without risk.

WEMO has developed a combined rollbending and press brake machine for the production of roller shutter boxes. These boxes are made from pre-coated aluminium with a thickness of between 0.5mm and 1mm.

The complete line consists of a reel, a straightening machine, a profiling line, a sawing machine and a combined rollbending/press brake machine. By combining these processes into a single machine, manual handling and intermediate stock is eliminated.

“With this WEMO machine, you can create standard right-angled, quarter-round and half-round boxes. It is also possible to produce products with a variable slanted edge,” WEMO explained.

Two of WILA's latest innovations, to be demonstrated at BlechExpo (Hall 1, stand 1409), are TIPS (Tooling Identification and Positioning System) and the New Standard pneumatic tool holders. TIPS is an integrated part of the press brake control system and supports tooling identification and continuous monitoring of tooling position.

“Errors and test bends can be avoided, resulting in a substantial increase in productivity,” said WILA. “The New Standard pneumatic tool holders are based upon a unique, patented, self-locking clamping mechanism. Tools are clamped using air pressure. The main advantage of this approach is that tools can be clamped and unclamped extremely quickly. This system further increases the speed of tool changes and results in significant increases in productivity.”

WILA B.V. has been specialising in tool holders (clamping, crowning, bottom tool holders), tooling and accessories for more than 80 years now, with the goal of reducing the adjustment time of the press brake and to further increase accuracy. Three of WILA's innovations – E2M® (Easy to Move), Smart Tool Locator® and V-Lock – were showcased at its booth at EuroBLECH 2014 in Hanover.

Wilson Tool International, the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, recently introduced the company’s new, complete line of press brake tooling called LVD Style.

“Available to the North American market, the LVD Style press brake tooling is made in the USA, which means that we can provide shorter lead-times and rapid delivery on this type of tooling to the North American market. Manufacturers can additionally benefit from fast issue resolution and quick tooling replacements by ordering directly from Wilson Tool’s Minnesota plant, located in White Bear Lake,” said the tooling manufacturer.

LVD Style tooling comes standard with features such as laser-hardened punches and dies (for increased life and durability). Dies are Nitrex® – treated for optimal performance. Die heights are designed to work with LVD’s Easy-Form system and LVD’s patented STONE® radius die design (designed to ensure accurate bend angles along the full length of the bend and reduce friction between material and die).

The press brake tooling features a punch tang geometry that is compatible with punch clamping systems requiring a bevelled receiving surface.

The WILA patented Tooling Identification and Positioning System (TIPS) continuously detects and identifies tooling in the press brake as well as in the storage system. With this detailed information set-up failures and test bends can be avoided. Moreover TIPS enables trouble free automatic tool change in bending cells. TIPS: Always the right tool at the right spot for the right job.
