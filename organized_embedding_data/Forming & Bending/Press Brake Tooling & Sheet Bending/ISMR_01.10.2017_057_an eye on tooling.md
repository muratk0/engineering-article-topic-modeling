WILA's Tooling Identification and Positioning System (TIPS)

AS WELL AS its range of tooling, crowning systems and clamping systems, WILA will showcase several other innovations at Blechexpo (7-10 November 2017, Stuttgart, Germany) that are aligned with the Industry 4.0 initiative. The company can be found in Hall 1 on booth 1408.

WILA's Tooling Identification and Positioning System (TIPS) is an intelligent system that can be incorporated in the clamping system of press brakes. Built-in electronics in the clamping system communicates with tools via an ID tag. The ID tag contains all the key specifications of each particular tool. Users can therefore easily locate and determine if the correct tools have been selected for the bending program and whether they are in the correct position in the press brake.

"This new technology will enable users to maximise the productivity of their press brakes and also makes programming for automation much simpler. We will be giving an interactive demonstration of this system during the exhibition," said the Dutch manufacturer
