Amada’s Astro 100NT Cell is an automatic robotic bending system integrated with a 110-ton hydraulic press brake. It is designed for high-precision part productivity through reduced cycle times. It is a totally unmanned robotic bending system, which increases productivity and profitability without increasing costly labour. The system incorporates a larger press brake with larger capacity robots. This enables process of bigger and/or heavier parts. The robot utilises the entire length of ram allowing flexible multiple-stage bending.

“Our Amada Astros are automatic robotic bending systems integrated with 110-ton hydraulic press brakes. Bystronic has robotised its press brake systems and added new upgrades to its bending portfolio to further improve productivity. These include the new safety system Fast Bend+ which not only improves the machine safety but also increases parts output by some 20 per cent, it told ISMR. With the aid of a barcode scanner the change between jobs can be achieved even more quickly. Reduced energy consumption and less machine noise characterise the Energy Saver. After a bend, as soon as the upper beam reaches the upper dead centre, the main motor of the Xpert switches off automatically until the next bending process is started.

New bending aids, with for example rollers for heavy parts and brushes for the careful handling of stainless steel, ensure even greater flexibility in material handling particularly for small batches.

LVD’s RoboCell combines an LVD press brake, multi-axis Kuka robot and LVD CADMAN software to provide an integrated automated bending solution for a modest investment. RoboCell increases machine throughput by automatically loading, forming and stacking finished parts in an unmanned process, providing high-precision parts, shorter cycle times and lower production costs. Available in models from 80–110 tons with 2.5–3 metre bending configurations, with PPEB or Easy-Form Series press brakes, RoboCell is suitable for forming small-to-medium-sized components. This robotic bending cell uses LVD’s new CADMAN Touch CNC control with intuitive user interface, which minimises input to the control so you move from drawing to part in fewer steps.

All press brake and robot programs are generated offline by LVD’s integrated CADMAN–B 3D programming software and Kuka Sim software. RoboCell allows both automatic and manual operation of the press brake with a full two metres of space between the press brake and the robot for greater bending flexibility.

Prima Power’s new automatic bending cell EBe automates the bending process of high-quality sheet metal components. The new construction features actuation of the bending blade movements (vertical and horizontal) by servo NC-axes instead of hydraulic cylinders. The upper tool movements are also made by another NC servo-axis. For optimum product quality, a new bending principle is now available. When the “rolling mode” is used, there is wider contact surface between the blade and the sheet but no relative friction. Alternatively, when using a standard “circular mode”, the contact point remains constant whereas the contacting point of the blade changes during the bending movement.

Prima Power’s BCe series bending centres provide capacity for single piece and small batches as well as serial manufacturing. They extend bending capability to applications that were not possible before, allowing production of components which are fully perforated, have large or high forms or need large openings.
