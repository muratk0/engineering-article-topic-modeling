# A clamp in Time

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

AS A LOW-COST alternative to magnetic clamping plates, Hilma-Römheld is introducing a new compact mechanical clamping claw. It is suitable for clamping dies and workpieces into place on machine tools precisely and accurately and is equipped with a movable self-locking lever. The self-locking feature prevents the device from changing its position, which is especially advantageous in the case of a vertical arrangement.

The compact clamping element is easy to handle and requires little force. It is available in two versions, with a clamping force of 15 kN or 25 kN. The higher clamping range is infinitely variable and can even be extended using a height adapter, while the clamping force remains unchanged across the complete adjusting range. To change the workpiece unimpeded, the clamping lever can be completely retracted into the housing.

The base body and the clamping lever of the new compact clamping claw are made from tempered steel, which have a coating to protect them against corrosion. FOR MORE INFORMATION, PLEASE SEE www.hilma.de
