# Double bending machine

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Swiss company Jorns AG celebrated its 50-year milestone anniversary in 2023. The company is a specialist manufacturer of semi-automatic and fully automatic bending machines.

The company will be exhibiting at Dach & Holz (5-8 March 2024) in Stuttgart, Germany on booth 407 (Hall 7) with manufacturer Krasser GmbH as well as its local sales and service partner, Maschinen Stockert.

Jorns will present the Jorns JDB-150G-HM-DP-SE-1250-JC100-6.4-dsp double bending machine, equipped with automated side-infeed system as well as the new JC100 control which offers a direct interface to the “Bendex Enterprise Suite" from MicroSea. In addition, Jorns and Krasser offer a direct interface whereby the required pre-cut blanks can be exported to the cutting and slitting line from Krasser.

The fully automatic Jorns JDB double bending machine can be configured in lengths of 3.2 to 12.2 metres, with a bending capacity for sheet metal of up to 3mm. The simultaneous bending of complex profiles is possible. The area behind the clamping tool with a retraction of over 290mm for the bending tools offers above-average open space. Users can then produce bending profiles without turning them around or over, with an array of possible profile variations.

Several parallel, tapered or overlapped profiles can be bent simultaneously.

"The use of servo-technology, the automatic loading and unloading table as well as the individually driven clamping fingers allow maximum precision, flexibility and productivity. Thanks to two bending tools, material handling has also been drastically reduced. The consistent calculation of processing and stop positions according to the 'Shorten bending according to DIN 6935' method, intuitive graphic programming, angular accuracy and uniformly closed water folds are all an integral part of the JDB," concluded Jorns.■
