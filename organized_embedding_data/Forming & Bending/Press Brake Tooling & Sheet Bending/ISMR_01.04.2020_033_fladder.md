## PRESS BRAKES IN FOCUS

## We highlight a selection of the latest new press brake bending technologies in the global market.

Sheet metal is tested between two tools by a press brake; an upper tool (known as a punch) and a bottom tool (known as a die). The press brake controls the movement of either the punch or the die and provides the press force using hydraulic rams or electrical servo motors. The press brake's bending force, known as tonnage, determines how much pressure can be applied by the punch during a bend. Higher tonnage allows for bending thicker and harder metals, and lower tonnage is most appropriate for thinner metals.

The press brake market is poised to grow by US$ 379.37 million during 2020- 2024, progressing at a CAGR of over 4% during the forecast period, according to a new report by Technavio. In addition, the incorporation of press brakes with IoT is anticipated to boost the growth of the press brake market. Press brake technology ranges from hydraulic and mechanical to pneumatic and servo-electric, with robotised bending increasingly being used.

Safety, environmental factors, increased accuracy and ease of operational requirements, as well as the move towards Industry 4.0, have affected press brake design. The penetration of Industry 4.0 is one of the major trends being witnessed in the global press brake market, with digital management of product assembly, quality check, inventory management, data and resources management and integrated communications with suppliers and vendors. The market has also seen an increase in automated and robotised bending operations.

In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake technology developments in the global market.
