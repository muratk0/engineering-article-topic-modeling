# FOCUS ON BENDING

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

All press brakes are hydraulically-driven and range from 45T up to 1,200T. Haco Atlantic has partnered with Robosoft to provide an automated production cell for its press brake customers. It can now produce a complete part in speeds of less than one minute.

LVD Company nv has upgraded and expanded its PPS Series of precision hydraulic press brakes for increased performance to offer a wider range of models of up to 320 tons in capacity. New, higher specification PPS models offer greater flexibility and performance with increased open height, stroke and operating speeds. It launched the new electrically-driven Dynapress at FABTECH last year. LVD also introduced new generation PPEB and Easy Form press brakes and the Strippit-PX new generation single-head Pullmax-style punch press which can also tap, bend and form. Pivatic’s bending solutions overcome the traditional challenges of part processing by eliminating separate bottlenecking process stages, material losses and surface damage. With PivaBend, the parts are bent efficiently and can also be fully machined into the desired product on a single line. Short part cycle times are achieved by simultaneously bending all sides of the work piece. Different bending angles, both upwards and downwards, are performed by the same unit. The programming system makes flexible production possible, whether just for a single part or for batches of thousands of parts.

PivaBend integrated with PivaPunch provides even greater flexibility for maximum uninterrupted part production efficiency. The PivaBend Bending Centre for sections features a cycle time of 24 seconds for a section and a de-stacker for unloading of punched blanks from a lifting table. Capacity depends on part geometry and varies from two to four parts per minute.

PunchBend is Prima Power’s new, compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials, which processes pre-cut material automatically into ready-bent, high quality components. The essential characteristic of the concept is tight integration of all the elements needed: software, punching and bending technology as well as automation. Both punching and bending technology are servo-electric, making the new product part of the Prima Power Green Means® offering. The high-end Prima Power servo-electric is included.

As the punching stroke is NC-controlled, in addition to high-performance punching, accurate forming capacity is available. High repeatability facilitates forming, roll forming, marking etc. and shortens set-up times. The servo electric drive offers low energy consumption, low maintenance costs and rapid operation.

SafanDarley offers a very comprehensive range of electronic press brakes. As the inventor of the E-Brake, it has continuously developed its machine concept further into a number of ranges: with a pressure force of 20 to 300 tons and working lengths of 850 to 4100mm. The servo-electronic drive based on the pulley principle ensures an even distribution of forces so that, in 95% of all cases, no crowning table is needed. Cycle times are shortened by up to 30% compared to traditional hydraulic press brakes, said the manufacturer.

“The absence of hydraulics makes each SafanDarley E-Brake press brake as environmentally friendly as possible. There is no risk of polluting oil and lower energy consumption means lower CO2 emissions. The machine only consumes energy when the upper beam is actually moving,” the company told ISMR.

The servo-hydraulic SafanDarley H-Brake ranges from 175 tons to 1250 tons in pressing force and varies in bending lengths from 3100mm to 6220mm.

Salvagnini designs, builds, sells and services flexible machines and systems for processing sheet metal. Its bending portfolio comprises the P4Xe and P2Xe hybrid panel benders; S4+P4 complete panel production FMS lines; E2, B2 and B3 KinEtic press brakes and ROBOformER® robotic bending cells.

“The new Salvagnini P1 panel bender has twice the productivity of press brakes and is indispensible for the production of thinner parts, less than 49 inches in length. With manual blankholder set-up (MLA), universal tooling, the briefest of cycle time, and sophisticated, intuitive software, if you are capable of loading a blank and unloading a finished part, you can produce a high quality part on a P1 Performer,” the company told ISMR.

Setting up TRUMPF's TruBend 3100 for operation is made simple by the capability to insert the tools vertically.

Salvagnini’s B3 series of press brakes combines the company's knowledge of panel bending and brake forming technology. This press brake solution can reach speed and power levels similar to those of a hydraulic press brake whilst delivering the accuracy and reactivity of an electric press brake and maintaining the consumption of a hybrid system, all managed by the proprietary Salvagnini SiX controller.

The structure is unique in that both the top and the bottom of the press are completely closed. This makes it extremely stable and keeps the positions of the references constant throughout the entire bending phase, generating a remarkably high degree of precision. The up and down movements of the top-beam can reach a speed of 220 mm/s.

The new generation of the TruBend Series 3000, made by TRUMPF, combines high joint speeds with simplified controls and an attractive cost-to-benefit ratio. This makes for profitable manufacturing even at low machine utilisation rate. The multi-touch-control and the space-saving design are the predominant features. The back gauges can move across the entire bending length, which enables its unrestricted use when bending. Automatic crowning makes for constant angles. In addition, high joint speeds make this machine the fastest in its class.

The BendGuard safety system on the TruBend 3100 protects the machine operator.

The TruBend 3100 has a press force of 1,000 kilonewtons and three metres of bending length. The TruBend Series 5000 is a versatile bending machine with up to 6 CNC-controlled backstop axes. With its adjustable arm and foot rests, a pivoting control panel and seated operation, the TruBend Series 7000 series provides easy working conditions for any operator.

Codatto International S.p.a., which specialises in developing and manufacturing panel bending machines, recently became a member of the TRUMPF corporate group. The purchase agreement was signed in July 2013, the transaction has now been completed and ownership has passed to TRUMPF.

At FABTECH, Ursviken showcased its OptiFlex press brake (press power 200 tons, length 3.1 metres). The OptiFlex press brake comes standard with extended tool space, unlimited gap frame and advanced technology for automatic manufacturing. An angle and springback control device ensures that every bend is kept within a tolerance of +/- 0.3 degrees. The angle measurer and the CNC-controlled dies allow for more versatile and rapid production when it comes to more demanding projects and shorter runs.

The company also offers a robot-assisted press brake (press power 200 tons, bending length 5.1 m). Equipped with a tool exchanger for upper and lower tools, it is designed for very thin material e.g. 0.8 mm aluminium.

Optima is Ursviken’s series of large press brakes made for highly specialised needs and specifications. With a complete range of automatic material handling components, Optima offers solutions for the profitable manufacturing of large products. Press power ranges from 400 to 10,000 tons and bending lengths from 3.1 to more than 20 metres.
