# STEINEL keeps packing a punch

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

STEINEL, a family-owned business with production facilities in Villingen-Schwenningen and Halblech/Buching (Germany), offers punching and bending technology as well as mechanical engineering solutions. Its services range from the production of standard parts, active elements and tool systems to the implementation of complete solutions. It also develops and produces custom and precision guide units for customers in the mechanical engineering sector.

“Professionals know that the tools used for sheet metal processing must be even more precise than the products they manufacture. And, since time is money in punching and bending technology, it's all about speed and durability. Guide units, along with active elements, endure some of the highest stress in punching tools. They must absorb large forces, minimise friction and function with precision. Backed by 100 years of mechanical and tool engineering experience, we provide precise and durable roller and sliding guide units designed for high production speeds and low wear," outlined STEINEL.

"Our load-bearing and free-running profile rollers or balls are precise and of an identical sorting. As a result, the ball and roller guide units are free of clearance, run smoothly, have low maintenance and offer service lives of several million strokes without quality loss. Ball guide units can be installed in any position, supporting both linear and rotational movements, making them ideal for high cycle rates. Roller guides, specifically designed for linear movements, excel under high lateral forces from any direction," it continued.

STEINEL's product range includes many guide pillars with their matching sliding, ball and roller guides made from various materials. The standardised products cover lengths from 30-560mm and diameters of between 3-160mm. For dimensions outside this range, STEINEL provides custom and application-specific guide units. These can be coated; dirt-protected; equipped with integrated seals; high-strength; rustfree and acid-resistant (depending upon requirements).

“The guide units are produced at intelligent manufacturing centres at our plant in Germany. Since all production stages take place under one roof, customer-specific orders can be implemented in the shortest time possible. Beyond rapid production, our team brings expertise in managing complex applications and special requirements, supporting customers from initial concept through to design and development. Using modern computer-aided tools (such as CAE, CAD and FEM), we can create optimised prototypes and provide accurate service life predictions to ensure that each product meets its intended purpose," concluded STEINEL.■
