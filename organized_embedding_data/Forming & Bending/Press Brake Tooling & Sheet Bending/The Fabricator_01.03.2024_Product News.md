Automated press brake cell processes parts up to 24 by 32 in.

BLM GROUP offers the new ProBend automated press brake cell. With a compact size of 281 in. by 160 ft. by 122 in., it features the ProBend E-Series 205-60 and a 30-kg robot. It has the flexibility to process a variety of part sizes, from 4 by 3 in. to 24 by 32 in. and 33 lbs. The E-Series 205-60 press brake has a bending force of 66 tons and a bending length of 81 in.

The system has two load/unload locations with the ability to integrate the unload pallets with an AGV as desired. The preload section is configurable for adaptation to part geometry and can be removed to accommodate larger parts. The 3-axis Cartesian pick-and-place system with pivoting head allows the robot to continue processing parts while staging the next piece of material.

Bell-type suction cups hold fast to sheet metal

Coval offers CTC bell-type suction cups for sheet metal handling. This design combines the strength and antislip qualities of flat suction cups with the ability of bellows suction cups to grip complex or angular shapes.

The new cups have a large deflection capability, able to adapt to gripping flat, convex, or angular sheet metal. Antislip cleats grip heavy, oily sheets, while the central stops help ensure thin sheets are gripping tightly without deformation.

Available in diameters from 4 to 100 mm, the thermoplastic polyurethane cups resist wear and oil. They are suitable for handling materials to feed machines, transfer parts between machines, and move parts to storage.

Unit traps sparks to prevent fire in dust collectors

Nordfab Ducting has released the Inline Spark Trap 2.0, an updated version that uses flow design analysis technology to quench sparks generated by welding and grinding.

When installed in a dust collection system, the unit helps reduce the possibility of fire in cyclones and collectors by decreasing the number of sparks that reach these units through the ductwork. Part of the company’s Quick-Fit clamp-together ducting product line, the trap requires no rivets, screws, or welding. It has no moving parts, and pressure loss is minimal.

Miniature hand-held videoscope supports NDT, remote visual inspection

Titan Tool Supply Inc. has launched the TVGM series of miniature, high-definition, hand-held, industrial-grade videoscopes for NDT and remote visual inspection tasks.

Offered in four models with a choice of a 3.9- or 6-mm camera and a 1.5- or 3-m probe length, the portable videoscope features dual-key operation, modular design, and one-handed operation. The probe lengths can be swapped out for different applications.

A 4.3-in. high-definition LCD touchscreen with high-intensity LED illumination allows for clear viewing of the magnified images and videos captured remotely by the high-resolution videoscope camera.

Band saw upgraded with features for cutting ferrous metal

Laguna Tools Inc., in collaboration with its metalworking division Dake, has introduced the 14CX band saw with upgraded features for cutting steel, iron, and stainless steel.

The foot brake system has a larger brake disc for enhanced stopping power and operator safety. The digital readout offers a quick reference for blade speeds and allows the speed to be set based on a specific blade and material.

The two-speed, high- and lowrange pulley system keeps the band saw in the correct torque range for the blade speed and materials being cut. The speed control knob located on the control panel allows speed adjustment on the fly, and the pulse width modulation system provides electronic speed control for quick stops of the DC motor and wheels when shut off.

Kitting cart provides horizontal and vertical storage

Creform Corp. has designed and built a kitting cart for an aerospace manufacturer. It features four custom level shelf sections in the front and a vertical storage section in the back. Each shelf level has three storage positions to store ready-to-assemble kits. The vertical storage section has lightweight plastic walls for containment and provides space for larger parts. The unit features six 5-in.-dia. casters for portability, and the open design lets the user quickly see and retrieve desired parts. The cart can hold up to 1,000 lbs.
