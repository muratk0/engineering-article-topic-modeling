# THE BEND FACTOR

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

Many of the latest bending innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending

Featuring a range of innovations from ecohybrid drive systems to automatic angle measurement, quicker tool change/set-up and more sophisticated sensing technologies, today's press brakes can also handle smaller batches and, by integrating robotic and automation technologies, deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations.

Many of the latest bending innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending.

In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake, folder and panel bending technology developments in the global market.

Salvagnini's new P4lean-2520 is an energy-efficient machine with a wide-ranging field of application. The minimum panel size that can be produced with this panel bender has been reduced by 10, dropping from 400 to 370mm. The P4lean-2520's new geometrical dimensions help to boost machine performance, for the same overall system layout, by an average 15% - the maximum diagonal has also gone from 2,800 to 3,200mm to allow feeding of panels of up to 3,050mm wide. The P4lean-2520 gives users the option of processing panels with a maximum bend height of 203.5mm.

Salvagnini has also launched SaM, its new proprietary app which provides realtime information on Salvagnini machine status on smartphone, tablet or PC using remote monitoring.

Schiavi arrived at Blechexpo in Stuttgart last November with a real innovation - its new press brake, the LineAr (model 130.30 with 3m length). This press brake model features the patented Schiavi "Crowning Free System" which guarantees a constant angle along the bend and horizontal edge along the line of bend (for a perfectly straight bend). LineAr has three beams for the lower table and three for the upper and the bending force is distributed on the external beams so that the tool holding beams remain straight during the bending process.

Another highlight of its booth presence was its new CNC control, Atlas, integrated with the Moses system, which projects all the information that the operator needs directly onto the frame of the press brake. This system is touch-screen so that the user can work directly work on the press brake frame instead of walking backwards and forwards to the CNC next to the press brake. Schiavi offers the press brakes in three sizes.

Schiavi has also patented and produced an electric-pneumatic angle measurement system to reduce bending times and obstructions, cut out spring-back issues and lower operational costs. All Schiavi press brakes are equipped with its LASER BEAM safety device solution.

The PowerBend Professional, one of the most successful folding machines from Schröder Machinery, is able to bend 3mm thick sheets at a working length of 3200mm. The Up-and-down-folding beam allows sheet to be bent from the top to the bottom and from the bottom to the top. There is therefore no need to turn over sheet, which saves a lot of time and effort.

Schröder Group also offers a fully automatic bending centre based on folding machines from the Evolution-Series. The Power-Bend-Center is designed to be smoothly integrated into production plants for electronically controlled machining sequences.

There is also new material handling functionality that can grab and position small parts down to widths of 14mm (5mm, with stop finger)."
