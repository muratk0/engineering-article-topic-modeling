# Customised bending

[TARİH: 01.04.2021 ISMR]

ACCURL CNC Machine Tools (Anhui) Co., LTD, produces CNC machine tools and sheet metal processing equipment. It offers a wide range of precision machines such as press brakes; laser cutting machines; shears and CNC punch presses.

"Our machine tool quality is a result of strong materials, thoughtful design and intuitive controls, along with additional options and add-ons that can be used to modify machines to fit the needs of customers," the Chinese sheet metal specialist told /SMR.

Its GENIUS press brake range is fully configurable to customer requirements. The standard press brake range features models with capacities from 40 to 2000 tons. ACCURL also builds bending machinery with different configurations, upon customer request. It described one such example to /SMR.

"Early in 2020, we received an enquiry from a Czech customer. He had been looking for one qualified and premium manufacturer to meet his bending requirement for a large box up to 600mm deep. He had watched a few ACCURL press brake videos on YouTube and then contacted us through our website. We explained that our high-end CNC metal fabrication machines are mainly exported to Europe, the United States, Australia etc. and are built according to European CE safety standards and American standards," Elvis Dong, ACCURL's deputy general manager, explained.

"After receiving the customer's workpiece drawings, our engineers carried out pressure analysis, deformation mechanics analysis and simulations using SolidWorks software to validate the bending effect on the workpiece. In the end, we designed a customised pressbrake for this Czech customer."

The customised version was based on the standard configuration of the GENIUS pressbrake range, which features a Delem DA66T 3D CNC control unit with Profile-T3D offline software; 4-axis model (Y1, Y2, X, R axes); CNC crowning compensation; ECO POWER technology; a CNC front support follower (2 × 180kg); CE Norms: Lazersafe Systems PCSS A1 + IRIS and an ACCURL-designed doublesided clamp-holder. ACCURL's Genuis B25110 was specially configured for deep box bending for this customer (960mm daylight, 450mm throat and 350mm stroke).

"Due to the COVID-19 epidemic, the customer could not personally inspect the machine. We therefore took photos and videos in accordance with the details agreed with the customer. Online videos of the entire bending process (import of drawings, parameter settings and unfolded bending process) were all shown to the customer. The customer professed himself very satisfied and accepted the customised press brake," added Elvis Dong.

The Hybrid Eco Function on this press brake is a hybrid servo-drive system. Depending upon cycle characteristics and rating, servodrives can offer substantial energy savings to manufacturers.

"We aim to deliver continuous product innovation to help our customers achieve high performance, with reduced production costs and scrap. Our press brakes, configured to customer requirements, are fast, productive and reliable," concluded ACCURL.
