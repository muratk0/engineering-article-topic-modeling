POWERFUL CNC BENDING

The Nargesa MP1500CNC and MP3160CNC Press Brakes are designed for industrial-grade precision and robust performance. Equipped with CNC controls, these machines combine user-friendly operation with high precision, ideal for bending complex parts with repeatable accuracy. The MP1500CNC is tailored for medium-duty work with a maximum bending length of 1,500mm, while the MP3160CNC is built for bending lengths up to 3, 160mm. Both models ensure quality and efficiency, suited for a wide range of metalworking tasks.

LED LIGHTING

The LED directional light strip illuminates the bending zone, providing the operator with optimal visibility for precise, safe operation.

BACK GAUGE

The back gauge is equipped with ESA servo motors and BOSCH REXROTH precision recirculating ball screw guides, allowing automatic positioning of the × and R axes with high accuracy. It also features a manual adjustment system for the Z axis, enabling smooth sliding of the calipers for quick setup changes.

SLIDING ARMS

The front support arms, which slide on recirculating ball screw guides, securely hold sheet metal in place. They can be manually adjusted both horizontally and vertically for precise positioning.

LASER SENSOR

The laser includes a built-in metric guide for vertical adjustment and can be easily lifted and removed when changing punches and dies.

S-BEND

The included 5-Bend attachment allows for the production of complex parts, enabling precise bending operations at both ends of the machine.

PRECISION BENDING

For maximum bending precision, a GIVI MISURE high-resolution optical encoder (0.00020″) controls the vertical position of the bending axis (Y-axis), ensuring excellent reliability in every operation. Combined with controlled lowering speed, this system maintains precise vertical positioning of the punch holder, achieving a positioning error of less than 0.00040″.

AUTOMATIC CROWNING TABLE

The Automatic Crowning Table on the MP3160CNC Press Brake is essential for achieving precise, consistent bends. Included as a standard feature, it automatically compensates for deflection across varying part lengths, ensuring uniform punch penetration into the die. Using advanced sensors and control software, it maintains accurate bend angles throughout the entire folding process.
