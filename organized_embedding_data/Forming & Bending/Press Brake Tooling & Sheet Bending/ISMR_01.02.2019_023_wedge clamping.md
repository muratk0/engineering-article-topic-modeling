# Wedge clamping

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

THE ROEMHELD RANGE of die clamping systems for presses, systems and injectionmoulding systems has been supplemented with a new series of robust wedge clamping elements. The group of companies has developed a hydraulic model specifically for the fixing of upper dies. An additional locking cylinder hold the wedge clamping bolt in position and prevents any inadvertent retraction. Release of the clamping device is only possible once the locking mechanism has been opened. This will ensure that the upper die is retained in position, even in the event of a pressure drop.

The wedge clamping element itself features a block cylinder with housing-guided clamping bolt. Its angled contact surface creates a frictional connection in conjunction with angled die clamping edges. A position control is integrated as a space-saving measure to monitor the clamping and release position, as well as the status of the locking mechanism. A notification is also issued wherever there is no die available.

"Our wedge clamps can be flexibly adjusted in accordance with respective customer requirements. The standard range already includes seven designs with clamping forces of 25 to 630 kN and cylinder dimensions of between 25 and 125mm. Clamping strokes of 15 to 36mm are available here, and the maximum operating pressure is 350 bar respectively. If the elements are to be used for processes with high heat requirements, special designs are also available which are temperature-resistant up to 300 °C. There are also versions for straight clamping edges, as well as models with clamping forces of up to 1250kN and even higher retention forces. Further options include a special permanent lubrication and multi-layer, high-tech coatings for the clamping bolt and the housing which have a positive influence on the wear properties," said Roemheld.

As existing dies can often be retooled for wedge clamping with the introduction of wedge bars, the elements are also suitable for retrofitting.

With its extensive range of quick dieclamping technology, Roemheld's set-up time optimiser can complete most clamping technology tasks in sheet forming, plastic and rubber processing. The magnetic, hydraulic and electromechanical clamping systems help to make the processes of single and series production more efficient and economical. Products for changing dies, including diechanging carts, roller bars and powered carrying consoles, complete the range.
