# Folding machine for Chinese market

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

SCHRÖDER MASCHINENBAU displayed its versatile folding machine, PowerBend Professional, at the International Industry Fair in China recently. With the torsion-resistant, high precision PowerBend Professional, it wants to reach customers that put special emphasis on repeat accuracy and careful handling of the material surface.

“Schröder’s Up-and-Down technique enables users to bend up and down without needing to turn over the sheet metal as the folding beam is able to bypass the work piece. This procedure reduces time and effort in terms of handling the sheet and thus increases productivity and minimizes the risk of harming the material surface,” explained the company.

Hans Schröder Maschinenbau specialises in the development of modern machine concepts for folding and cutting sheet metal. Thanks to the 2006 integration of the Fasti Company and its workshops and a global presence, the Schröder Group is now a leading provider of machines for folding, cutting, crimping, beading and circular folding of sheets of all kinds.

At EuroBLECH, its product highlights included the new hydraulic shear, PowerShear Professional, that is able to cut up to 6mm thick steel sheet at a working length of 3000mm as well as the newly developed tool changer for its motorised folding machine, SPB Evolution UD.

## Improved efficiency in arc welding

AT the heart of EWM's presence at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover was the enhanced "Multimatrix" system for manual and automated welding, for more efficient production. EWM’s "drive 4X" was developed in-house as a universal wire feeder for all "Multimatrix" MIG/MAG welding machines. The company’s advanced "ewm Xnet" quality management software networks not only "Multimatrix" inverter welding machines but also a large number of EWM TIG, MIG/MAG and plasma power sources, and facilitates precise logging of the use of gas, energy and consumables as well as production and auxiliary process times. Work can be improved and costs precisely calculated by logging every single weld.

EWM’s "Expert 2.0" control system, a method of maximising customer benefit, was also an important feature of its stand in Hanover. It makes direct access to expert knowledge and selfexplanatory operation of the power source possible. Every welding task can now be configured and read even more easily on the plain text LCD. EWM has now introduced the "xButton" to identify the welder and provide the individual displays and welding parameters required. This drastically reduces operating errors.

The power sources themselves play a major role in enhancing efficiency. Among EWM's exhibits at EuroBLECH was the MIG/MAG "alpha Q", "Phoenix" and "Taurus Synergic" welding machines. EWM’s "Tetrix 200 puls" and "Tetrix 300 puls" TIG welding machines all made their premieres at EuroBLECH. A newly-developed power unit combines a higher open circuit voltage and longer duty cycle with optimised ignition and welding characteristics. The user can also carry out MMA pulse welding with both the new "Tetrix" machines, which are suitable for root welding and produce a fine-flaked weld surface with a TIG look on final passes. Other highlights are minimal finishing work, because there is less spatter and reduced distortion, due to controlled heat input.

EWM also launched "frontDrive" a new robot welding torch for automated MIG/MAG applications. Its pure pull concept avoids the down times and synchronisation problems associated with push/pull. The "tigSpeed Rob" for automated TIG welding is also new.

Finally, EWM’s "ewm maXsolution" is a new comprehensive innovation and technological consultancy service. The customer has a choice of three packages, extending from analysis with suggestions for improvements through to additional consideration of subassemblies and a tailor-made concept to a full analysis of the entire production flow on this basis, offering optimisation in individual areas. ## Coil in focus

ATHADER has been growing its business in markets such as Russia, US, India and Saudi Arabia over the last few years. It is part of its strategy to establish and strengthen customer relationships in these regions.
