# SPEED, PRECISION AND POWER

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

As press brake manufacturers continue to bring more innovation to market, we highlight a selection of the latest new bending developments

The science of bending metal has become an art as machine tool manufacturers strive to introduce new and innovative ways of processing materials. Many of these innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending.

With an eye on some of the latest innovations to be presented at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition, we highlight a selection of some of the latest press brake developments on the market.

Adira is one of the leading manufacturers and global suppliers of sheet metal working machinery, specialised in the production of laser cutting machines, hydraulic press brakes, shears, robotised bending cells and automatic sheet metal transforming systems. It has a large portfolio of press brakes, targeting all common bending needs as well as specific custom-tailored bending projects. It can also supply robotised bending cells and fully customised solutions to suit bending needs.

Sheet metal machinery specialist, Amada, will be showcasing a variety of new and recent technology introductions at the EuroBLECH 2014 exhibition designed to offer customers significant competitive gain in all aspects of the component fabrication process. It will focus on solutions that do not consider systems in isolation but in relationship to the entire production process. Here, lasers, robots and software all have an equally important role to play.

It has launched a new EG-6013AR fully automatic bending system. The EG-AR series is the latest addition to Amada’s range of manual and automated press brakes. Designed specifically for high speed bending of small complex parts, the entire system is designed for unmanned production of any volume size or part mix. The robot is specifically designed for bending complex parts and the system boasts a BiS automatic angle measuring system. Offline programming is done through dedicated CAM software.

ISMR SAYS:

“Many new press brake innovations are centred upon more user friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending”

“Equipped with the world’s first twin-servo electric drive mechanism, the EG-AR offers high speed precision bending, with lower power usage and a compact footprint. Features such as automatic angle measuring, offline programming, multiple part load/unload stations and an automatic tool changer all add to the system’s list of production capabilities,” Amada told ISMR.
