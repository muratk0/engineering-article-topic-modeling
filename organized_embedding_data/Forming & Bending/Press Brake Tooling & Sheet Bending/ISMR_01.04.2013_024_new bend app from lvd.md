# New bend app from LVD

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

LVD Company’s new Bend app for smartphones and tablets provides quick access to helpful bending calculations and tooling information to aid press brake set-up and part production, and offers users LVD news and product updates.

“Users have key bending calculations at their fingertips, including bending force estimation and bend allowance and the ability to select key variables such as vee opening, minimum flange length and internal bend radius in metric or inch mode settings. Tooling brochures can be downloaded from the app to help identify and select the correct tooling style and form for the job. Available content also includes LVD latest product news and updates, customer case studies, technical articles and LVD’s Discovery magazine,” LVD told ISMR.

The LVD Bend app is offered as a free download from Apple's iTunes® store and the Android market place. The LVD Group is a leading manufacturer of a comprehensive range of sheet metal/plate working machine tools and software solutions, including laser cutting systems, punch presses, press brakes, guillotine shears and mid-level automation systems, integrated and supported by its CADMAN® PCbased Windows® compatible software. For more information, see www.lvdgroup.com ■

## Intelligent assembly

FANUC ROBOTICS demonstrated intelligent assembly using its new LR Mate 200iD robot during the Automate 2013 exhibition this January in Chicago, USA. The new six-axis robot is extremely lightweight and compact, specifically designed for high speed and precision applications such as machine tending, material handling, assembly, picking, packing and material removal.

The intelligent mini-robot features a 7kg payload, a slim arm with integrated power, signal and air, and flexible mounting (upright, invert and angle) for easy integration. At the show, a ceiling-mounted LR Mate 200iD robot used iRVision® to pick socket parts from a conveyor and assemble them at high speeds on a fluorescent light frame. This precise assembly operation demonstrates the new robot’s flexible mounting and bottom cable exit feature.

“The new LR Mate 200iD robot is a versatile solution for a wide range of manufacturing operations that require access into small spaces,” said Nishant Jhaveri, senior engineer, FANUC Robotics.

“The robot’s slim arm is about the same size as a human arm, and offers a bottom cable exit option to minimise interference with peripheral devices.”

FANUC Robotics America designs, engineers and manufactures industrial robots and robotic systems for a wide range of applications including arc and spot welding, material handling (machine tending, picking, packing, palletising), material removal, assembly, paint finishing and dispensing. The company also provides application-specific software, controls, vision products and complete support services. For more information, see www.fanucrobotics.com ■
