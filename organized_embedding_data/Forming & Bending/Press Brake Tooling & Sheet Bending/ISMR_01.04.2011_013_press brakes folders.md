The BendMaster precisely positions components measuring up to 500 x 400mm and sheets of up to 8mm thick. The LoadMaster Bend has 4-axis movement and features a multi-vacuum gripper.

Process safety is enhanced by weight sensors that recognise if two sheets have been picked up instead of one; separation of the two sheets is also automatic. Optical sensors ensure sheets are transferred to the BendMaster robot in the correct position, even if they are not accurately stacked.

All machine elements operate through a central controller without interfaces, another factor that contributes to the exceptional cycle time.

Standard on the TruBend Cell 7000 is a pallet system in which up to 24 different blanks or 4,800 parts can be supplied at a thickness of up to 1mm. The compartments can easily be retrofitted, are 200 x 200mm or 600 x 400mm in size and can accommodate removal of sheets from a stack up to 200mm high. This makes the machine highly flexible both in terms of the materials used and the geometries processed.

## TOOLING SYSTEMS

As an option, the process of changing bending tools on the TruBend Cell 7000 can also be automated using the new tool changer, the ToolMaster Bend. The BendMaster uses the ToolMaster Bend to select the appropriate tools without having to change.

System identifies them automatically, any tool arrangement can be selected. ID chips are placed in the tools that communicate with the controller via data channels in the tool clamp and in the support tracks of the ToolMaster Bend. ToolMaster Bend is particularly advantageous when processing small batches because the cell can process different orders without operator supervision.

Because not every bending application requires the latest equipment, press brake tooling specialist Wila has developed New Standard Pro. This new range of tools is aimed at bending applications that do not impose extreme requirements, but where customisation and productivity do have priority. New Standard Pro is suitable for very diverse applications.

At EuroBLECH last year, Wila launched the innovative TIPS (Tooling Identification and Positioning System). In Wila’s Universal Press Brake Concept, the machine builder supplies the press brake with upper and lower beams that are flat, equipped solely with screw holes with which the clamping system can be attached to the beam. Over the entire length of the machine, the clamping can be aligned in relation to the x axis (Tx), meaning in relation to the back gauge, using adjustment screws all along the upper beam. Exceptionally stringent requirements therefore no longer need to be applied to the straightness of the upper beam.

Based on the (New Standard) crowning system on the lower beam, it is also possible to correct not only for machine deflection, but also for local deviations to the y axis (Ty) all along the machine length. Inaccuracies occurring in the course of time due to machine wear can also be corrected in this manner. Production losses and the costs for extensive re-machining of crucial machine parts are therefore avoided. At the same time, press brake life expectancy can be extended.

The Wilson Tool range of European style press brake tooling is manufactured to extremely high tolerances. Every standard press brake die is treated with its patented Nitrex surface enhancement. Nitrex is a high endurance treatment (HRC 60) which extends the life of tools and reduces sheet marking. ■
