# Rapid panel bending

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

"OUR NEW TRUBEND Center 7030 bends parts up to 30 per cent faster than its predecessor – and its tool change cycles are up to 70 per cent quicker. For the first time, we have also included the option of equipping the panel bender with a loading and unloading station. Panel bending machines are particularly adept at fabricating complex parts with radius bends, short side lengths and narrow profiles. When it comes to producing parts with these kinds of specifications, this alternative bending technology is quicker than die bending machines," sheet metal specialist TRUMPF, told ISMR.

It has focused on making the TruBend Center 7030 faster than ever. The engineers achieved this by splitting the machine's drive unit into two parts. Instead of equipping the machine with just one central hydraulic cylinder, the new design features one cylinder on the left, and one on the right. This has increased axis velocity and boosted the machine's productivity. The new on-demand servo drive also saves energy by only running the motor when the machine actually needs it.

The operator can stack various piles of metal blanks on the righthand side of the machine. A suction cup initiates the automated loading process by picking up a sheet whilst checking that it has not taken two by mistake. This gripper device deposits the blank on a brush table, with a positioning bar ensuring that it is aligned correctly. A loading carriage then moves the blank into the machining area, simultaneously removing the most recently bent part from the machining area and transporting it to a conveyor belt. During this loading and unloading process, the TruBend Center 7030 continues to operate.

Changing the bending tools is handled by the ToolMaster. TRUMPF engineers have added a feature that allows it to change multiple small tools at the same time. These improvements reduce machine set-up time by up to 70 per cent, according to the manufacturer. The new TruBend Center 7030 also features the offline TecZone Fold programming solution, enabling operators to program several parts with a few clicks of the mouse. TecZone Fold generates the bending program and 3D simulation of the parts, including collision monitoring. See www.trumpf.com

## Corrosion protection

VPCI®-329 D ES is a ready-to-use spray that is designed to provide strong rust prevention for metals in both indoor and outdoor sheltered conditions. "It can be easily applied/sprayed from a handheld trigger spray bottle and offers a convenient way to protect gears, spindles, coils or any other metal parts that require a light coat of oil for further processing and shipment. VpCI®-329 D ES contains innovative Vapour phase Corrosion Inhibitors (VpCI®) technology and its carrier is a combination of bio-based oil and mineral spirit. By spraying VpCI® 329 D ES, the corrosion is attacked on two fronts. Its oilsoluble corrosion inhibitors first create a tenacious film that clings to metal surfaces, providing excellent contact corrosion protection," explained its manufacturer, Cortec.

VpCIs release into the air and attach electrochemically to the metal surface. The coating provides universal corrosion protection to ferrous and non-ferrous metals and performs, said Cortec, effectively even under the adverse conditions of 100% relative humidity as well as in the presence of corrosive species such as chlorides, hydrogen sulfide or other sulfur compounds.

"The product does not contain any hazardous chromates, nitrites or phosphate inhibitors. VpCI®-329 will protect against corrosion for up to four years. It is thermally stable, providing long-lasting contact and vapour phase corrosion protection for a wider variety of applications, concluded Cortec.

See www.cortecvci.com
