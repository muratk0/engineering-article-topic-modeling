Opportunity for Press Brake Operators and Safety Personnel to Earn Certificates

FMA offers individuals and companies the ability to gain knowledge and hone skills through its many professional development programs. In-person certificate courses give you or your employees the chance to gain an industry-recognized credential following in-depth classroom instruction.

The following courses will be held in September at the TRUMPF Training Center in Farmington, Conn.

Precision Press Brake, Sept. 6-7

This two-day seminar is dedicated to mastering the fundamentals of precision sheet metal bending. Attendees will gain knowledge on the theory, principles, and math fundamentals behind the machine. The instructor is Steve Benson, a nationally known expert and author of

Bending Basics

.

Cost:

$495 FMA member $695 General

Press Brake Safeguarding, Sept. 7

This one-day seminar focuses on current best practices in press brake safeguarding. The curriculum, based on real-world shop applications, studies the risk assessment process, OSHA requirements and ANSI standards, and application-specific safeguarding system selection. The instructor is Doug Raff, currently vice president and a principal of Paragon Industrial Controls Inc. and a member of FMA’s Safety Council.

Cost:

$295 FMA member $495 General
