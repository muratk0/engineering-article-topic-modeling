# Sheet-metal bending

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

At this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022), Schiavi Macchine will display a 170-ton HFBx hybrid press brake (3m, eight axes, with 800mm large opening and 500mm stroke).

“It is ideal for bending complex profiles and can reach speeds of up to 300mm/s," the Italian bending specialist told ISMR.

The second press brake on its stand will be a small 50-ton Hybrid BSTs (1.2m, six axes) for the rapid bending of small components. This press brake consumes 0.6-0.7kW/h of energy and offers speeds of up to 250mm/s.

“Our Atlas CNC control will feature on both press brakes. It automatically generates the entire bending program, including tooling set-up," added the manufacturer. “We will also present our laser automation line by introducing the Compact Tower, which enables a laser-cutting system to run without operators."

Meet Schiavi Macchine International on stand F86 in Hall 12 at EuroBLECH 2022.
