PP200CNC: 20 TON HORIZONTAL PRESS BRAKE

PP200CNC: OPTIMAL CNC PERFORMANCE

Built for high-performance shops, the 20-ton PP200CNC horizontal press brake delivers precise bending, folding, cutting, stretching, and shaping of cold materials, all in one rugged machine. Its 60 mm-thick welded steel monoblock frame ensures rock-solid rigidity and smooth, consistent operation under load. Fabricators can bend solid rods to produce parts like hooks, chain links, and balusters from square or round bar stock with ease. It’s equally effective at shaping tubes, cold-forging components, and fabricating structural elements such as railings, fences, gates, and doors. Engineered for adaptability, the PP200CNC is a go-to solution for metalworking professionals who demand flexibility, repeatability, and real-world reliability in demanding environments.

HYDRAULIC SYSTEM

Equipped with a hydraulic power unit and integrated positioning and pressure control valves, the system allows precise regulation of piston force to match application requirements.

GUIDED HYDRAULIC CYLINDER

The hydraulic cylinder is mounted directly to the workbench, transferring full force straight to the tooling for maximum efficiency. A fully guided hydraulic piston prevents deflection during folding operations, ensuring precision and durability.

FASTENING BOLTS

Fastening bolts stabilize parts and tooling during operation, keeping attachments firmly in place. This reduces vibration and enhances both precision and safety during every cycle.

REPLACEABLE DIES

Crafted from high-quality steel, these dies deliver long-lasting durability and reliable performance under load. They allow the PP200CNC to bend and shape a wide range of metals with efficiency.

WORK SURFACE

The frameless workbench allows unobstructed movement of parts during machining, with no structural elements interfering with the workflow.
