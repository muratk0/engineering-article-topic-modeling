# Press brake tooling

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

AS THE ONLY piece of equipment that touches the workpiece during the bending process, press brake tooling is an integral component of any successful bending operation. WILA develops and manufactures durable precision tooling with award-winning innovations designed to make the toolchanging process faster, safer and accurate.

“WILA New Standard Premium tools are press brake tools of the very highest quality which have been developed with great care to comply with extreme tolerances and the most complex requirements under all conditions,” the Dutch tooling specialist told ISMR.

WILA New Standard Premium Tooling is CNC-Deephardened® to 56-60 HRC, up to 4mm on all working surfaces. This, said the company, provides maximum wear resistance, durability and long-term accuracy. All tools are produced to a tolerance of up to +/- 0.01mm and undergo strict quality assurance checks at every step in the production process.

“This guarantees an extreme level of precision which makes each type of WILA New Standard Tools interchangeable. Because of this interchangeability, it is not necessary to order the complete machine length at the same time,” it added.

“Tool segments, new and existing, are guaranteed to match up. Segmented and reversible tooling of the optimum height allows the forming of a wide variety of part configurations. This leads to easy handling and a high level of flexibility.”

To further support rapid and safe tool changes, WILA’s Safety-Click® and Easy-to-Move® (E2M) technologies are designed maximize speed and safety when changing top and bottom tools vertically or horizontally. With a scannable DM-code on every New Standard, all New Standard Premium tools can be ordered online via the WILA webshop. Many standard tools can be delivered from stock to reduce delivery time.
