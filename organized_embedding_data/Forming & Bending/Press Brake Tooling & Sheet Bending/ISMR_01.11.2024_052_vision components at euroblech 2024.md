# Vision Components at EuroBLECH 2024

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Vision Components presented its sensor for angle measurement in metal processing at EuroBLECH (22-25 October 2024 in Hanover, Germany). It also introduced a new, compact OEM module that enables companies to quickly develop universal sensors for applications with laser triangulation (e.g. for glue bead and weld seam inspection directly on the tool head).

## Angle measurement sensor

The VC nano 3D-Z angle measurement sensor, optimised for sheet metal bending applications, works autonomously and without an external computing unit.

“It can be quickly and easily integrated into new and existing systems by bending machine manufacturers and distribution partners, enabling the bending process to be adjusted in real-time. Leading press-brake manufacturers (such as Deratech, Doray and Placke) already offer the sensor with their machines. It is also integrated into control systems from Cybelec, Delem, ESA, Laimore and Step Automation, among others, and only needs to be activated," said Vision Components.

“Calibration is not necessary. The sensor unit for angle measurement in sheet metal bending has a large detection range and can cover dies from V6 to V200 with a fixed mounting position, with a typical measurement resolution of better than +/- 0.05°," it continued.

## Compact OEM laser sensor

At EuroBLECH 2024, Vision Components presented an OEM module for the development of individual sensors that combines the technology of the VC nano 3D-Z triangulation sensor with a new arrangement of the mirror elements for the camera and laser. This, said the company, enables the smallest possible format of around 69mm x 45mm x 56mm.

The module with onboard data processing serves as the basis for the rapid development of individual sensors by OEMs and suppliers e.g. for applications such as adhesive bead and weld seam inspection directly on the tool head.

Vision Components is a manufacturer of embedded vision systems with over 25 years of experience. Its product range extends from MIPI camera modules to freely programmable cameras with ARM/Linux and OEM systems for 2D and 3D image processing.
