# High speed panel bending

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

THE WEBEND PRO high speed panel bender from Dutch manufacturer, Wemo, has been designed as a plug and play system for rapid part output and a quick Return on Investment (RoI). With a 10-year warranty on all solid parts, the panel bender comprises a head-bending station, a take-off station, a feeding station, longitudinal bending and hydraulic control.

The system can accommodate sheet metal blanks – Ferritic steel or (pre-painted) Steel DC01 – from 0.5mm to 1mm thick. Blank widths range from 245mm – 900mm, with lengths ranging from 415mm – 2275mm.

“Product tolerance is ± 0.2mm (depending upon your pre-punched blanks),” said Wemo. “It is easy to extend the line with extra features and we believe the line’s output is 2.5 times faster than our competitors’ product lines.”
