Pneumatic clamping systems use self-locking mechanism

Wila USA has introduced a line of upper and lower New Standard

®

Premium pneumatic clamping systems with patented self-locking mechanism for press brakes. Designed for use with the company’s New Standard punches and dies, the clamping systems use shop air pressure.

Tool segments are clamped and positioned individually using clamping pins, which are controlled via self-adjusting wedges. When the wedges are engaged, they become an integral part of the tool and ensure that the tools remain clamped even if there is a loss of air pressure. Clamping also is released pneumatically, so no hydraulic oil is needed.

The systems clamp and unclamp tools quickly. They are suited for electric press brakes and press brakes with automatic tool change.

Cordless grinders fit in compact spaces

Suhner Industrial Products has introduced four cordless, compact grinders. Each grinder features an electric motor and an 18-V battery pack with a total capacity of 4 Ah. A detachable particle guard protects the motor from coarse dust.

The ASC 9 straight grinder is suitable for work with nonwoven brushes and flap wheels. Typical work includes the removal of tarnishing and blending on matte surfaces. The ABC 7 cordless belt grinder is designed for use in tight spaces, such as fillet welds or miters. The machine offers extremely fast belt changes for grinding or polishing applications, says the company.

The AKC 3 battery fillet weld grinder is made for metalworking, with reduction gears that make it very powerful. It has a lightweight design and long neck for maneuverability and use in tight places.

The ATC 7 tube polisher is designed for tubes up to 1.75 in. dia. It offers a wraparound angle of at least 180 degrees that is not maintained by the contact pressure.

Parting tool offers long grind life

Wilson Tool International

®

offers the Dura-Die parting tool. Its replaceable die inserts are made from the company’s Ultima tool steel, which offers high wear resistance for long grind life. The die base is made from shock-resistant steel, allowing it to withstand high punching force.

Extension die grinders extend into previously inaccessible areas

Dynabrade Inc. has introduced a new line of 0.4-HP extension die grinders. The air tools allow for work within long pipes, castings, and other places where an extended reach is needed.

The straight-line air tools are available in either a front- or rear-exhaust air motor and run at 25,000 RPM. They are available in 3-in. and 6-in. extension models.

Each grinder features a safety-lock lever to prevent accidental tool startup. Running as low as 72 dBA, the grinder powers optional carbide burrs and mounted points for deburring, grinding, etching, and cleaning.

Sensor measures profile angle within die cavity

Gasparini offers the GPS4, a sensor embedded into the press brake die that measures the profile angle within the die cavity mechanically. It is designed to combat problems with springback.

The sensor allows the bending of complex shapes with Zbends or with narrow flanges. Surface finish has no influence on the sensor’s effectiveness. The operator does not need to know thickness, length, material, or bending force.

Machines perform deburring, edge-rounding of punched, cut parts

Arku Coil Systems Inc. has introduced deburring and edge-rounding machines for use on metal parts that have been punched or cut by laser, plasma, or flame.

The EdgeBreaker

®

can remove thick slag and heavy burrs up to 0.12 in. on parts up to 3.15 in. thick and 51.18 in. wide. The machine can process parts of steel, copper, various alloys, aluminum, and titanium. Its oscillating roller self-cleans residue from the grinding belt, while sanding blocks round the edges on both sides.

The EdgeRacer

®

is made for thinner parts up to 1.97 in. thick. Made by Germany-based Wöhler and sold exclusively by Arku, the machine can remove oxide layers and handle 3-D parts or those with polished, brushed, or coated surfaces.
