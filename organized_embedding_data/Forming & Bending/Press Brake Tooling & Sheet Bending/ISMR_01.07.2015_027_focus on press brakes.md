# FOCUS ON PRESS BRAKES

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

Compact and portable with a footprint of 1140 x 1760mm, the new Dyna-Press models save on valuable floor space and can be conveniently relocated using a standard forklift.

US tooling specialist, Mate, is a little more than ten months into the launch of its press brake tooling line and is expanding its product line with additional products and services.

“We knew that entering the press brake tooling market would add a significant dimension to our punch press tooling and laser consumables businesses, which customers have been asking us about. But we haven’t stopped there. We are expanding our product lines with additional products and services, and see a lot of opportunities ahead,” it told ISMR.

PunchBend is Prima Power’s new, compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials, which processes pre-cut material automatically into ready-bent, high quality components. The essential characteristic of the concept is tight integration of all the elements needed: software, punching and bending technology as well as automation. Both punching and bending technology are servo-electric, making the new product part of the Prima Power Green Means® offering. The high-end Prima Power servoelectric is included.

One of the highlights of Dutch press brake specialist, SafanDarley, at its EuroBLECH booth was the presentation of its new generation of servo-electric press brakes. Its drive-based pulley principle has evolved to a new dual-drive machine concept offering further advantages than its E-brake concept. The new press brake boasts a C-frame and narrower press beam, with higher pressure load per metre. Other benefits of this new principle are rapid and direct positioning with higher acceleration and accuracy.

Another SafanDarley highlight at EuroBLECH was its new, E-Bend L laser angle measurement system to measure precise bends. It also showcased its augmented reality concept, the SafanDarley Eye. The software can be used for tool change on the press brake, for example, to give additional machine tool information (such as location data for the positioning of the products) to the operator through the glasses that he/she wears when using the machine. It is a very innovative development.

SafanDarley made some recent significant investments last year in both its factories in the Netherlands. Last year, it invested over 2 million euros into the SafanDarley factory in Eijsden to make it more efficient for larger equipment (it now manufactures press brakes of up to 1250 tonnes).

The issue of bending and improving efficiency downstream from the cutting process is high on Italian manufacturer Salvagnini’s agenda. Its B3 ATA press brake is its benchmark for efficient sheet metal bending.

“The B3 is the only solution of its kind on the market and is the product of proprietary expertise in panel bending and press brake bending, combining the best of both worlds. Employing an original solution that teams design innovations with the latest technologies on the market, this press brake combines the power of a hydraulic press brake with the responsiveness and accuracy of an electric press brake, whilst maintaining the consumption levels associated with a hybrid system,” it told ISMR.

The B3 press brake in its ATA version employs automatic bending tool set-up to make the bending process even more efficient. Other Salvagnini bending solutions comprise panel benders such as the P4 P2 and P1 automatic panel benders in a range of 14 models and the FMS S4+P4 automatic panel production line.

The TruBend 3000 Series press brakes from TRUMPF enables, says the company, costeffective bending even with low throughput.

“High productivity through easy set-up, rapid axis-speeds and fast tool change, with automatic crowning for consistent angles, helps with this. High application flexibility and simple operation makes these press brakes suitable for those introducing automated bending into their processes for the first time or established users wishing to expand their capability,” explained TRUMPF.

The latest addition to the range is the TruBend 3066 with a press force of 600kN and 2m bending length. It is supplied as standard with a two-axis back gauge but can also be specified with a four- or five-axis back gauge. This makes it possible to position sheet metal securely, even where complex part geometries are involved.

TRUMPF’s optoelectronic safety system, BendGuard, uses a light field to monitor the area beneath the upper tool. If there is an interruption, a hand breaking the light field for example, the machine automatically stops.

TRUMPF UK recently received its biggest ever order for CNC press brakes. Nine TruBend Series 5000 press brakes were ordered by KMF, a leading UK sheet metal subcontractor based at Newcastle-under-Lyme in Staffordshire. KMF ordered seven 2.5 metre bed TruBend models (85 tonnes capacity) and two 3-metre bed models (130 tonnes capacity). All nine machines feature an extended open height for bending components, such as deep boxes, and all offer tooling compatibility. One machine comes with TRUMPF’s bending aid, which reduces a two-man operation to a one-man function when handling large panels.

Dutch tooling specialist, Wila B.V., has been specialising in tool holders (clamping and crowning), tooling and accessories for more than E2M® (Easy to Move), Smart Tool Locator® and V-Lock – were showcased at its booth at EuroBLECH in Hanover last year.

KMF ordered all of the tools for the nine recently installed TRUMPF press brakes from Wilson Tool, making this the largest European press brake tooling order it has ever received. The tooling company offers a comprehensive selection of press brake tooling solutions from standard tooling solutions for European style, American Precision®, WILA/TRUMPF Style Precision™ and Exacta conventional press brake tooling to special customised tooling for unique bending applications, and an array of clamping solutions designed to speed set-ups.
