# Bending ‘On The Go’

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

A NEW app can calculate major bending parameters simply and quickly, anywhere, any time. The new BendGuide mobile application by TRUMPF makes the major parameters available to job schedulers, designers and operators - on the smartphone or tablet PC – whenever needed. These include the press power, die width, minimum leg length, installation height monitoring, inside bending radius, and the weight of the sheet metal and its tooling. Each of these factors can quickly be calculated.

“Users also receive current news and brochures on the topic of bending. The operating interface for this mobile app is available in German, English, Spanish, Italian, French or Czech. BendGuide is available for the iOS and Android operating systems and as a web app for every standard PC,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

See www.trumpf-machines.com/bendguide

## Helping Volvo to Maximise Uptime

WITH THE many challenges facing the automotive industry, such as shortened development times, Volvo is increasingly moving its Noise, Vibration and Harshness (NVH) development into computer simulation and modelling, with less traditional prototyping.

“An established customer of Brüel & Kjær, Volvo benefits from the value added to its systems in terms of the minimised downtime, optimised functionality and assistance with new applications – thanks to its PULSE software maintenance and support agreement,” B&K told ISMR.

According to the Director of Volvo’s NVH Centre, “The market is moving faster than ever now and all manufacturers have to keep up, to stay on top. We also have more things to measure and verify than ever before”.

For the Director of Volvo’s NVH Centre, the software maintenance and service agreement (M1) adds vital value to the different Brüel & Kjær solutions his department uses – totalling over 25 separate PULSE analysis systems.

As the NVH Centre Director says: “Firstly, it ensures that all of our hardware and software works properly, with full solution functionality. Secondly, we get local support and support from headquarters when we need it, such as for new applications, as well as for training and system configuration. There is also continuous dialogue with Brüel & Kjær to optimise our set-up by shortening the time needed, increasing test efficiency and maximising data quality.” See www.bksv.com

## INVERTER WELDER

GYS, a French manufacturer of welding machines and battery support equipment, has launched its flagship Gysmi 125 MMA/Arc inverter welder.

The Gysmi 125 is capable of welding Ø2.5mm electrodes. It measures just 24x18x10cm and weighs 2.5kg. Electronic advancement means assistance for the welder with Hot Start, Anti-Sticking and Arc Force control negating the frustration of sticking electrodes and inconsistent welds.
