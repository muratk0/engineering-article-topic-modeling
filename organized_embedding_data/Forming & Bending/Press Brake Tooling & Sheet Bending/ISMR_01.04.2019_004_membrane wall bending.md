# Membrane wall bending

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

Above: MARTIN Caldeiras in Portugal is a subsidiary of Munich-based MARTIN GmbH and specialises in the manufacture of boiler spare parts and robot cladding

THERMAL WASTE TREATMENT is a complex process. At the centre of the process is the boiler consisting of several tubes welded together to what is known as a membrane tube wall. Before those walls can be used in boiler construction, they first need to be bent. In early 2019, Schwarze-Robitec delivered an FL 2000 membrane wall bending machine to Portugal to perform this task. The machine enables MARTIN Caldeiras, a manufacturer of boiler spare parts and a subsidiary of German company MARTIN GmbH, to bend entire membrane tube walls up to 2,000mm wide in a single step.

To ensure a perfect fit with the boiler design and adjacent supply line segments in modern power plant construction, the membrane tube walls first need to be bent with millimetre precision. MARTIN GmbH designs and constructs waste-to-energy plants, while its Portuguese subsidiary specialises in the manufacture of boiler spare parts and in robot cladding.

"We wanted to optimise our processes and bend the membrane tube walls directly on-site at our own facility," said Peter Alpiger, Managing Director, MARTIN Caldeiras.

Looking for a manufacturer of tube bending machines, MARTIN GmbH turned to Schwarze-Robitec. The machines in its Boiler & Power series are suitable for boiler and power plants.

Above: Entire membrane tube walls of up to 2,000mm wide can be bent with the Schwarze-Robitec FL 2000

Its FL 2000 27-ton membrane wall bending machine is used by MARTIN Caldeiras to process walls consisting of tubes with a diameter of up to 70mm and a wall thickness of up to 5mm.

"Continuous and safe operation with zero downtime is of major importance to us," underlined Alpiger.

The Schwarze-Robitec product range includes, in addition to tube bending machines and bending tools, tube perforating machines, measuring stations and solutions in special machinery construction. See www.schwarze-robitec.com
