# High speed in a small space

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

BYSTRONIC’S NEW XPERT 40 COMPACT press brake can be assembled to be portable and can be integrated into almost any production environment. It is suitable for flexible job order production with fluctuating batch sizes and highly variable bent parts, but also for extensive series production with recurrent parts.

“Drawers offer sufficient storage space for equipment. All-important tools are always close at hand. An automatic clamping system simplifies equipping the machine – connect the tools and you are ready to go. A height-adjustable folding table on the front of the machine can be used as a shelf. Bending on the Xpert 40 is comfortable and efficient,” Bystronic explained.

“Users can bend three times as many parts as on a large press brake. A compact chassis and ByMotion are what make the Xpert 40 so fast and precise. The chassis makes the Xpert 40 highly dynamic with bending speeds of up to 25mm per second. Simultaneously, the machine maintains sufficient rigidity. ByMotion ensures precise acceleration of the upper flank and back gauge. The newly developed drive control regulates the coordination between speed, precision and power. This ensures bending results with the highest possible repetition accuracy.”

The Xpert 40’s Dynamic Drive System (DDS) enables dynamic bending without loss of power. This increases not only the number of parts produced on the machine, but also the energy efficiency of the entire bending process. With intelligent functions such as the Energy Saver and automatic start-stop, the Xpert 40 is designed to operate economically, while still bending rapidly.
