# Multi Cell automated bending at EuroBLECH 2024

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

This October, SafanDarley presented its latest innovation at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, Germany. The Dutch bending specialist introduced a versatile addition to its automated bending solutions with the new Multi Cell, for all midsized products of up to 2000mm x 500mm, and featuring 40-125 tons of pressing force.

“This innovative solution enables automated bending of a wide variety of products in a very compact space. The Multi Cell offers flexibility in the selection of an electronic or hydraulic press brake, of different tonnages, based upon the sheet thickness and product length with the option to switch machines over time," outlined SafanDarley.

“The machine is modular and can be configured per item and according to customer requirements. The system allows easy transition between manual and automated bending. The integrated ASC Control and RoboBend package provides a single point of contact for complete automation needs, eliminating dependency on third parties," it added.

Its control enables less experienced operators to effectively manage automated machines, automatically programming and adjusting tools and grippers for different jobs and minimising changeover times in robotic bending processes.

“The demand for automated bending solutions is rising as companies aim to boost competitiveness. Automation offers scalability, improved quality and higher efficiency. It reduces labour costs, ensures consistent output and supports 24/7 production, even with staff shortages. Automation also allows employees to focus on complex tasks, while new staff quickly adapt to the technology. In short, automation is key to cost-effective, efficient production and competitiveness," summarised SafanDarley.

The Dutch manufacturer produces electric, hybrid and hydraulic press brakes, as well as guillotine shears, for both thin and thick sheetmetal segments.
