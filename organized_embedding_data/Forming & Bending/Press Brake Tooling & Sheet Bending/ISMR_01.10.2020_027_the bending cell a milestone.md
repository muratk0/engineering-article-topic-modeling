# The Bending Cell – a milestone

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

The Bending Cell was launched in 2018 and is already in operation in Canada, Hungary, France, Italy and the United States. The commissioning of the first Bending Cell in Switzerland marks a milestone for Bystronic: “The geographical proximity of Stoppani to our headquarters in Niederönz means that we can show customers the system in live action – and not only in our Experience Centre,” says Benedikt Kreisel, Bending Automation Product Manager, Bystronic.

The Bending Cell is available for the Xpert Pro 100, 150, 200, 250 and 320 press brakes. The maximum press capacity is 320 metric tons and bending applications up to 3 metres are possible. Five robot types are available with lifting capacities ranging from 90 to 270 kilogrammes.

“This wide range of configurations enables Bystronic to offer its customers a set-up that is tailored to their specific needs. The Bending Cell is also characterised by its flexibility in terms of applications,” concludes Benedikt Kreisel.

## LVD outlines major new initiatives to support its customers in challenging times, including new digital events, four new product launches and joint programmes.

LVD IS A global solutions manufacturer of sheet metal/plate working equipment including laser cutting systems; punch presses; press brakes; guillotine shears and automation systems, integrated to CADMAN® and supported by its CADMAN® software suite.

The LVD Group is active in 47 countries, with 19 subsidiaries. Its main factory and headquarters is in Wevelgem, Belgium, with a central European factory in Slovakia (for European production), an automation factory in Italy, as well as a factory in the U.S. and factories in China for its two Chinese Joint Ventures.

ISMR caught up with Matt Fowles, its group marketing manager, to find out how it is supporting its customers through challenging times, and to uncover its latest new initiatives and product launches.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes or achievements this year.

MF: I can confirm that we have requalified again this year with Deloitte as one of Belgium’s best managed companies. I feel that this is a real testament to LVD’s ongoing governance; agility; certification, talent development etc.

We have also been expanding our global subsidiary base and opened a new sales and service subsidiary in Vietnam in April 2020. This is our nineteenth office worldwide. Vietnam is an interesting market – customers are quite hungry for the higher spec, higher technology machines. Some Japanese and Korean companies have already invested in manufacturing infrastructure in Vietnam.

ISMR: Which products and technologies are you releasing this year? Please give details.

MF: I am delighted to say that we are introducing several new products this year including two really groundbreaking new machines – the first is an XXL format (6-, 8- and 10kW) fibre laser cutting machine called the Taurus. Standard format is a 12 x 3m, which can be extended in 2m intervals up to 40m. The large format allows a diverse range of parts to be cut and efficiently nested, increasing productivity and optimising material usage. This goes hand-in-hand with our capabilities for XXL large bending. This machine will also be launched at our INSIGHTS event, which runs from September to December.

The second new groundbreaking machine is the Ulti-Form bending system. It is an automatic tool-changing press brake, with a robot. It takes the same approach as the Dyna-Cell with ultra-fast art to part. It has an adaptable gripper (patent-pending) which enables us to process much larger parts (min. part size 50 x 100mm to a max of 1200 x 800mm and up to 15kg). The Ulti-Form uses the same automatic tool-changing capability, and our Easy-Form® Laser adaptive bending system, as our popular ToolCell press brake. It is suitable for small batch work with high throughput. So, we have brought automation to larger, more complex parts with high throughput in small volumes using ultra-fast programming technology.

We are also introducing a new laser, the YSD LaserONE (a brand from one of our Joint Ventures). It is offered with a 2- or 4kW laser power source in a 3000 x 1500mm table size and with optional Load-Assist automation. It is a ‘no frills’, low cost, entrylevel approach to fibre laser cutting to meet the price/performance balance for some customers. This enables customers to buy from an established brand name like ours, but at a price point that enables them to get on the first rung of the fibre laser ladder. It also enables us to reach new customers that might not have been within our reach ordinarily.

We also had intended to launch our new punch-laser combination machine at EuroBLECH this year. Our daughter company, Strippit, was the first company to develop a punch-laser combination back in 1978. We are the only manufacturer who can provide users with a turret-type machine or a single head-type machine. We therefore launched the new Strippit PL punch-laser series and offered users the choice – either a turrettype or single-head version. So, we are back in the combination machine market and will showcase this new machine at one of our INSIGHTS events later this year.
