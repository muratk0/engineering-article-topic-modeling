# Roll forming and plate bending

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

ISITAN MAKINA'S 3-roll plate bending machine with variable geometry is particularly suitable for bending heavy plates. The machine can change the position of the bottom rolls horizontally and the vertical adjustment of the upper roll position can be determined by pressing the bending edge of the plate being processed.

"On this machine you can change the geometrical position of the rolls and, in this way, the machine can change into a pyramidal, asymmetric or symmetric machine according to the operator's wish. It is then possible to considerably vary the bottom rolls' centre-distance. The ability to increase centredistance between rolls enables bending capacities that are a lot higher than prebending capacities," the company explained.

"The variable geometry-type machine can be considered in all respects a heavy-duty machine, with three movable rolls on linear slides. It is a very strong machine, without pivoting points and therefore free of torsional stresses and load-bearing with wear points." Benefits include:-

■ Pre-bend both edges in two operations without removing plate and with plate in horizontal position.

■ Reduces flat edge to about 1.7 x plate thickness

■ Low working height, if compared with other 3-roll models

■ Low installed power, if compared with other 3-roll models

■ Smaller foundation required

■ Facility to increase bottom roll distance

■ More compact, versatile machine.

Rectilinear guides provide longer machine life and the system allows better roll parallelism over the life of the machine.

The company's 4-roll plate bending machine features rectilinear guides to provide longer machine life and the system allows better roll parallelism over the life of the machine. Rectilinear guides also produce the smallest flat part on the ends of the bent plates. The forming rolls transfer the working forces to the machine frame and/or the foundation. The distance of the forming roll from the central roll is minimal which produces the minimum edge flat part.

"We believe that purchasers and users of these machines must be able to enjoy the following benefits to stay competitive in the market," added the manufacturer.

■ Clarity on the machine capacity, particularly on pre-bending and rolling thickness according to the minimum bending diameter, plate material and machine useful length

■ Clarity on the roll dimensions, machine dimensions and installed power

■ Machine sturdiness and weight, compared with similar capacity machines

■ Larger installed motor-reducer torque, hydraulic cylinder force and hydraulic plant power, compared with similar capacity machines.

■ Best ratio "machine price/installed power" and "machine price/machine weight"
