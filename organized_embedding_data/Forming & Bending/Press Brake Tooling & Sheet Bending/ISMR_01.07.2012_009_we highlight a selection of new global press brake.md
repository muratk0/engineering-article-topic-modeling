# We highlight a selection of new global press brake developments and bending technologies

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

A culture of continuous innovation has characterised bending markets - new systems, technologies and processes are regularly introduced as manufacturers strive to improve and refine the art of bending. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new global press brake developments, isolating the upgrades and features that provide extra benefit for manufacturers.

The end of 2011 saw the market launch of Bystronic’s optical bend guiding system. This system guides the operator of a press brake through all bending applications. LEDs indicate the correct position of the tool stations and hence reduce set-up times. The bending process is also accelerated, since the system shows the position of the backstop finger and always marks the next bending position. Additionally, the LEDs indicate when the clamping is open and issue a warning if an incorrect operating procedure is attempted. The end of the order is indicated optically for the operator.

Bystronic’s new Xcel 50 press brake is small and compact. The crowning system developed in Switzerland ensures consistent quality over the complete length of the bend. With a press capacity of 50 tons over a bending length of 1,600mm, the Xcel 50 is suitable for thin and small part bending as well as medium batch sizes.

Turkish manufacturer Ermaksan recently launched a new hybrid press brake at its new plant in Bursa, attended by ISMR. The Evo II hybrid press brake, the result of two years of R&D, reportedly provides 60 per cent lower energy consumption and 80 per cent lower oil usage than other press brakes. The machine has a closed-circuit servohydraulic system that requires no maintenance and maintains precision at high speeds.

LVD has introduced a number of bending developments to the market such as Smart Crowning, a programmable crowning V-axis system, for its line of PPEB-H Series and Easy-Form Series press brakes. Smart Crowning provides an additional independent crowning axis on top of a conventional V-axis to automatically compensate for asymmetric variations in material thickness and material properties and ensure bending accuracy over the entire length of a work piece.

A press brake’s crowning system ensures the ram and table are parallel during the bending process. Traditional crowning tables compensate for the symmetric deflection of the press via a single V-axis and are suitable when material properties are the same over the length of the part. When properties are inconsistent (particularly with heavier or longer work pieces), Smart Crowning provides an additional axis that allows for additional independent asymmetric compensation.

In automatic mode, Smart Crowning in conjunction with Easy-Form Laser, measures angles at several points along the length of the sheet under pressure, up to a maximum of 16 points. Material spring-back is then measured in the centre of the press brake and values for individual Smart Crowning actuators are identified. The Smart Crowning system then adjusts to provide an accurate bend angle over the complete work piece length.

Co-manufactured press brakes are engineered and manufactured by LVD and its joint venture company LVD-HD for simple to complex heavy bending applications. LVD has also expanded its PPEC series of mid-range precision hydraulic press brakes to offer machines of up to 640 tons. The higher tonnage models provide heavier forming capacity to suit a broad range of applications. All PPEC press brakes are equipped with LVD’s CADMAN Lite control.

The new eP-1030 press brake from Prima Power has been designed to be highly productive and energy-efficient; maximum power requirement of a 105-ton press brake is 6 kW. Energy is used solely during actual bending and only as much as required.

“Power transmission by a pulley mechanism distributes force evenly over the whole bending length and eliminates deflection of the upper beam during loading. Control or servo drives and the O-frame design contribute to highly accurate operation; the yawning typical of C-frames does not occur. The linear sensors used for monitoring the position of the upper beam have been mounted on separate auxiliary frames: positioning accuracy is ± 0.005 mm,” the company told ISMR.

The new Block Laser version by Lazer Safe enables the operator to hold the workpiece close to the bending line with no effect on the rapid approach movement of the ram. Prima also produces an automated bending cell for thin sheet components in large batches. The sheet is loaded using a robot, rotated and manipulated during the bending sequence, bent and unloaded by the robot.

Prima Power’s BCe series bending centres provide productive capacity for single piece and small batches as well as serial manufacturing. They enlarge bending capability to applications that were not possible before, allowing production of components which are fully perforated, have large or high forms or need large openings. Loading is performed manually.

Safan and Darley recently exhibited at the Techni-Show 2012. The award-winning Safan Eye, Augmented Reality attracted attention at the event. On the glasses that the operator wears, additional information is displayed that only the operator can perceive. For example, the operator can see where the backgauge stop is located whilst, with the naked eye, only the tools are visible. Other relevant data, such as the position where the tools should be placed, are projected in the glasses. It is also possible to activate functions in the control by virtual pointing.

The new Safan E-Brake 130T 4100 was also on display. This model and the 3100 130T Safan press brake has applications in the cladding market. Darley showed the EHP 400T 6200 with specific bending aids. The machine was delivered directly after the show to Kiremko Food Processing Equipment.

Safan’s new E-App, now available in the Apple store, provides information for cutting and bending sheet metal and calculates the tonnage for bending plate on a press brake as well as calculating the maximum sheet thickness to be cut in a guillotine shear.

Safan offers a complete range of electronic press brakes. As the inventor of the E-Brake, it has developed the machine concept further into a number of ranges: with a pressure force of 20 to 300 tons and working lengths of 850 to 4100mm.

“The servo-electronic drive based on the pulley principle ensures a very even distribution of forces so that in 95% of all cases no crowning table is needed. Cycle times are shortened by up to 30% compared to traditional hydraulic press brakes,” Safan told ISMR. “The absence of hydraulics makes each Safan E-Brake press brake as environmentally friendly as possible. There is no risk of polluting oil and the lower energy consumption means lower CO emissions.”

The new generation of Safan E-Control touch screen controls include 2D and 3D graphic displays, 2D bending simulation and 3D graphic programming.

Salvagnini’s most recent bending developments include the B3 KinEtic series of press brakes, the fruit of the company’s knowledge of panel bending and brake forming technologies. This original solution combines a number of innovative design solutions, creating a model that can reach speed and power levels similar to those of a hydraulic press brake yet still deliver the accuracy and reactivity of an electric press brake whilst maintaining the consumption of a hybrid system, all managed by the proprietary Salvagnini SiX controller, the company told ISMR.

“The structure is unique in that both the top and the bottom of the press are completely closed. This makes it extremely stable and keeps the positions of the references constant throughout the entire bending phase, generating a remarkably high degree of precision. The absence of proportional valves, the presence of direct-drive transmission and brushless motorisation and the utilisation of energy in proportion to the real intensity of use, mean that the B3 press brake offers extremely high performance yet keeps consumption low. The up and down movements of the topbeam can reach a speed of 220 mm/s,” it added.

PRESSTUDIO is the press brake programming software, based on a native 3D model, which is used for graphical and numerical programming and production management.
