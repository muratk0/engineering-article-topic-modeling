# FORM AND FACTOR

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

We highlight a selection of some of the latest global tube bending innovations.

Bending processes used to permanently form pipe or tubing. Bending processes include form-bound and freeform, as well as heat-supported and cold forming. Factors such as material wall thickness, tooling and lubricants used all affect the bending process. Generally, round stock is used in tube bending. However, square and rectangular tubes and pipes may also be bent to meet job specifications.

Form-bound bending, such as press bending or rotary draw bending, are used to form the work-piece into the shape of a die.

Straight tube stock can be formed using a bending machine to create a variety of single or multiple bends and to shape the piece into the desired form. These processes can be used to form complex shapes out of different types of ductile metal tubing. Freeform-bending processes, such as three-roll-push bending, shape the workpiece kinematically so that the bending contour is not dependent on the tool geometry.

Growing requirements from industries such as oil & gas; pipelines; refineries; power & water; food manufacturing; electrical; marine & offshore; furniture; construction & infrastructure and heavy industries & manufacturing have increased the need for bent tube stock. In this article, we highlight a selection of some of the latest global tube bending innovations.

## The need for flexibility

The BLM GROUP is a global partner for the tube fabrication industry with a worldwide presence and many successful installations in a wide variety of industries. Its LT8.10 is 'the first machine in its size range to provide 3D cutting capability using a fibre laser', it told /SMR.

It features a new Tube Cutter focusing head to enable cutting of tubes with diameters from 10 and 240mm with weights of up to 40kg/m. This expansion in capabilities meets the requirements for 3D machining which is particularly required for larger and thicker tubes which require bevels or weld preps. A new user interface and more options for the Artube3 CAD/CAM package (for tubes and profiles with open sections) are featured.

"The CETube Cutter head, with its tapered geometry and easy handling, has increased the capabilities for processing open profiles or asymmetrical sections with both quality and precision," said the manufacturer.

CEActiveScan measures the deviations of the actual section of the tube, with respect to the theoretical tube shape/size, and automatically adjusts to achieve high part accuracy as quickly as possible. Users can measure the position of the tube and centre the geometry to the actual tube location.

“The software features a parametric design interface that allows main tubes and side-wall cut-outs to be added from a list of pre-defined shapes.

BLM GROUP appeared at EuroBLECH 2016 with a range of equipment, including the ADIGE-SYS LC5 (a combined cutting system for metal sheet and tubes), the LT8.10 laser tube machine and its 'All-In-One' concept (two working systems i.e. at the end of an ELECT80 bending system, the 5-axis LT-FREE laser cutting system performs a laser process on an already bent piece). Its proprietary suite of software, BLMelements, is a set of tools for designing parts, estimating production times, creating part programs, planning production and, if need be, integrating with other BLM GROUP machines.

Hypertherm, a U.S. based manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, recently released its Rotary Tube Pro™ software that makes it easier to design and cut tube and pipe parts with no 3D CAD experience required.

"The software features a parametric design interface that allows main tubes and side-wall cut-outs to be added from a list of pre-defined shapes. Alternatively, users can add tubes and cut-outs directly from a CAD program. This flexibility means that fabricators and manufacturers can enjoy greater productivity by increasing programming speed, and improving cut quality for improved fit-up with fewer secondary operations. The software also includes an optional rotary bevel interface provided with bevel cut charts based on True Bevel™ technology, which greatly reduces bevel set-up time," it told /SMR.

Enhancements include a new 3D geometric modelling engine; the ability to import STEP file formats for the quick exchange of 3D objects from one platform to another and cut sequence enhancements that allow for the cutting of interior parts first and improved sequencing flexibility. Simplified bevel material selection and new set-ups/spreadsheets/ machine files (that enhance cut quality and accuracy on bevelled tubes) are also featured.

In November 2016, Nucor Corporation announced that it had closed on its acquisition of Independence Tube Corporation for approximately US$ 435 million. Independence Tube is a leading manufacturer of hollow structural section (HSS) steel tubing, which is used in a broad array of structural and mechanical applications including non-residential construction, infrastructure and agricultural and construction equipment end-use markets. It sells its products primarily through service centres, which are also an important channel to market for Nucor products.

RSA Cutting Systems GmbH, Schwerte and Sema Systemtechnik GmbH & Co. KG have developed and brought into operation an integrated high-performance system for sawing, deburring, punching, doming, flattening and unloading of tubes made of steel and square/rectangular tubes for PERI GmbH, Weißenhorn. PERI requires constant innovation in scaffolding technology and formwork and is one of the world's largest manufacturers in these areas.

The challenge was to be able to produce individual scaffolding parts as well as shuttering props with the sawing and machining centre, but much faster than before. Up until that point, there were individual processing stations for each process together with set-up times of up to 30 minutes. The aim of the new line was to connect all the processes into one system and to reduce the changeover time to less than five minutes, which is now being successfully implemented in daily operations at PERI.

Project Manager Günter Reif confirmed: "Our ambitious demands for very short cycle and set-up time have already been met. For this reason, we are able to economically handle both large series and rapid/individual parts that are less frequently used and required in lower quantities."

For example, the cycle time includes all single processes such as rod feeding from the magazine, sawing, deburring, doubleside punching of up to eight holes for a steel tube (48.3 x 3.25mm) in just six seconds. Alternatively, in the same low cycle time, a tube of 38 x 3.2mm can be punched and domed from one side.

The modular overall concept enables the integration of further modules for measuring, washing, drying and marking etc.

Canadian supplier, Deco Automotive, has replaced three existing older bending machines with three automatic CNC 100 E TB MR VA pipe bending lines by Schwarze-Robitec. Deco Automotive, headquartered in Toronto, Canada, belongs to the global Magna Group and manufactures various automotive components including vehicle frames and structures, and engine cradles.

Of the three CNC 100 E TB MR VA machines, two machines are right- and one is left-bending. In addition, the multi-stack bending machines are equipped with a pipe magazine, a weld seam finding device, an automatic loading and a removal device. The electrically operated systems process round and oval tubes that are 2.8m long and have a diameter of up to 76.2mm, including a wall thickness from 1.2 to 3mm. The process is fully automated.

The bending cell by Schwarze-Robitec

The pipes to be processed are taken randomly from the tube magazine and fed to the integrated weld seam finding device. This device aligns the pipes in accordance with their weld seam position. Following the alignment, the pipe is passed on to the pipe bending machine. To do this, a mandrel is used which supports the tube on tight radii from the inside. A fully automatic loading arm then removes the finished bent tube from the machine and places it on a conveyor belt. From there, the tube continues to the hydroforming equipment. Another feature of the solution is the integrated raised, vertical travel routes.

This enables pre-loading of the pipe bending machine while, alongside this function, a finished bent tube is removed at another location.

The new machines are equipped with the high-performance control system, NxG. One advantage of the new control system is significantly reduced non-productive times, as individual steps of the bending process were arranged synchronously. Using the new automatic bending cells, the automotive supplier was able to optimise its cycle times and increase its production output of higher complex shapes and materials.

## Pipe bending

Tracto-Technik PBS is a leading specialist in the pipe bending sector. Its product range currently covers all-electric and electrohydraulic CNC pipe bending machines, semi-automatic pipe bending machines, machines for pipe end processing and hydraulic joining technology, pipe measuring systems as well as special solutions and software solutions for pipe processing.

It has now introduced a new brand for efficient pipe fabrication called Pipe Bending Systems (PBS). PBS is aimed primarily at pipe processers with single-part or small batch production and large product variety.

The company's new TUBOMAT has been fundamentally revised and modularly designed. This pipe bender (up to 48.3mm pipe OD) also optionally integrates the sawing, deburring, flaring and cutting-ring assembly function. Various technical improvements have been made to all working functions, such as the tooling concept, handling and control techniques, workflow and energy consumption. The modern control concept provides program operation as well as a "4-fold bending angle pre-selection mode" and also enables bending data import from TT's own PIPEFAB software solution.

For pipe pre-fabrication, TT also offers its semi-automatic TUBOBEND pipe bender series and the fully automatic TUBOTRON CNC pipe bender series.

The new modular TUBOBEND machine prototypes are optionally available as right-hand or left-hand benders. The exposed bending head, which allows for large bending space, enables them to bend highly complex pipe shapes. The TUBOTRON 25 MR, an all-electric CNC pipe bending machine with multi-radii capability, rapidly bends pipes with an outside diameter of up to 25mm.

"At EuroBLECH 2016, as well as the PBS system, we presented the first two machines of a completely new TUBOBEND family of bending systems (the first up to 16mm and the second up to 42mm pipe OD). These were two prototypes for the show. We use the same machine design and integrate different functions into the design, based upon customer requirements, so that we can effectively customise the design for a user," the manufacturer told ISMR.

Transfluid has simplified complex tube processing with an automatic tool change system. Bending requirements for tubes with diameters of up to 127mm are becoming more demanding, said the company.

The specialist from North Rhine-Westphalian Schmallenberg has developed its tube bending machine to bend DB 40120-CNC-VE for this. It enables bending radii of 1 x D at very low wall thicknesses in such tubes.

Simple operation is a key focus of the machine. All parameters can be taken from the CAD and the corresponding form spanners can be called up automatically, as required. An automatic spanner change system is also a feature. The time frame for tool change is comparable to that of a radius change.

The new DB 40120-CNC-VE has two tool levels. It is able to change the spanners eight times, so that each tube can be bent even in highly complex geometries. This form of tool equipment is particularly efficient.

"In particular, because the diameters are relatively similar and radii are very narrow at 1 x D, only the course geometries really change in new products. This means that only new spanning elements need to be used," explained Stefanie Flaeper of Transfluid.

To implement the narrow radii at a relatively low wall thickness, the new pipe bending machine has an additional pusher device that can guide even the last bend at short remaining lengths.

At the 2016 FABTECH exhibition in Las Vegas (USA) last November, pipe specialist Unison exhibited the Breeze all-electric CNCcontrolled 25mm Left & Right machine, the Breeze all-electric CNC controlled 50mm multistack, multi-radius machine plus its manually controlled EB1000 machine.

Unison offers a range of all-electric tube bending machinery, which can process tubes from 10mm to 273mm. Its software tools provide an end-to-end tube bending service and feature 3D simulation, allowing users to analyse, optimise and validate programs without needing access to the physical machine.

A new Unison all-electric tube and profile bending machine is helping Crisp Clean Services - a UK manufacturer of mobile cleaning stations - to substantially boost productivity and make its manufacturing process much leaner. The 30mm bender was specially designed by Unison to improve Crisp Clean's production efficiency and flexibility. It achieves this by incorporating a number of advanced features including a built-in hole piercing system that eliminates a separate processing stage, multi-stack tooling for quick changeovers between tube sizes and rollform bending - a new capability that Crisp Clean will be exploiting in future designs.

## Above: Crisp Clean Services makes a wide variety of mobile trolley systems for commercial janitorial applications

One of the first applications for the new Unison bender illustrates the productivity gains achieved by Crisp Clean.

The machine is used to crush-form a frame which provides the structure for a custom trolley system. This bending process uses special tooling - provided as part of the package by Unison - to deliberately collapse part of the tubing as it bends. This technique provides great structural strength that serves to make Crisp Clean's trolleys extremely robust.

The key piece of this particular frame is a rectangular shape, made from a single square profile tube with four 90-degree bends plus 12 holes on all four sides for subsequent assembly. Crisp Clean used to make this part using two separate machines - bender and piercer - in seven separate bending and piercing stages. The new bender now makes the same part in one single automatic cycle, almost tripling the speed of production.

This flexibility is helping Crisp Clean to reshape its production process. One of the first gains has been a substantial reduction in batch sizes. This has major benefits for Crisp Clean: it reduces the need for storage and this, in turn, is helping the company to adopt a much leaner build-to-demand process.
