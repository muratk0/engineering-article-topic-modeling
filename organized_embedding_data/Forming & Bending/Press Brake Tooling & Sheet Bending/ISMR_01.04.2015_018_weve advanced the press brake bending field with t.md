# We’ve advanced the press brake bending field with the B3 ATA by introducing a number of advantages typically associated with panel bending

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

“The B3 is the only solution of its kind on the market and is the product of proprietary expertise in panel bending and press brake bending, combining the best of both worlds. Employing an original solution that teams design innovations with the latest technologies on the market, this press brake combines the power of a hydraulic press brake with the responsiveness and accuracy of an electric press brake, whilst maintaining the consumption levels associated with a hybrid system.

“Part of the credit for this result lies in the operating principle of P4 panel benders – the B3 has harnessed the P4’s ability to handle heavy loads highly efficiently, accurately and with high dynamics by drawing on this principle, adjusted and optimised to suit its own purposes,” Salvagnini explained.

The B3 press brake in its ATA version employs automatic bending tool set-up to make the bending process even more efficient. ATA (Automatic Tool Adjustment) allows users to change and adjust upper tool length automatically, saving time and, consequently, improving production efficiency when bending both “batch one” and parametric parts. While the operator turns to pick up a new sheet for bending, the ATA system automatically sets the tool length and the width of the variable V-shaped groove. ATA uses standard, commercially-available tools. It can also be disabled so that the press brake can be operated in traditional mode.

“Rather than think of ATA as a tool change system, we like to think of it as an actual system in its own right that changes our approach to press brake bending, reinterpreting its use, its potential and its fields of application,” explained Nicola Vanzo, project management director at Salvagnini Italia SpA.

“Without going into detail, these past 30 years have shown us that panel bending is the best system for bending sheet metal. Whenever press brakes have found themselves competing directly with panel benders, they’ve inevitably come off second best. Backed by our ongoing development of panel bending technology – which enables us to offer the widest product range in the world and present new solutions like the P2lean – we’ve advanced the press brake bending field with the B3 ATA by introducing a number of advantages typically associated with panel bending, such as production flexibility, while still retaining the technology’s underlying strengths, namely size of investment and versatility. As a result, by adopting the same philosophy as the panel bender, the B3 ATA gives the option of setting up bending tool compositions to suit the part to be made.”

Other Salvagnini bending solutions currently comprise panel benders such as the P4 P2 and P1 automatic panel benders in a range of 14 models and the FMS S4+P4 automatic panel production line.

Swiss company Stierli-Bieger AG recently developed a CNC control system with angle programming for bending and straightening machines that was presented at the EuroBLECH 2014 exhibition in Hannover. It has developed a solution for a new bending and straightening machine with CNC control system, touch-screen and integrated angle control.

“The control system has been developed in cooperation with existing customers. The operation is dialoguebased and enables operation easily and without requiring previous knowledge. Thanks to the clear operator interface, the desired functions can be easily entered as symbols and then saved. Angles are automatically calculated by the software via the bending stroke,” explained the company.

Bending with Thalmann folder  
“Thanks to the solid back gauge, multiple bends can be performed in one operation or sequence. The additional newly integrated retraction of back gauge offers the operator a further operational advantage for the manufacture of bent parts.”

The improved control system as well as the simple, rapid tool interchange, make it possible to easily and more rapidly manufacture single items as well as serial parts. The open design enables rapid bending of closed shapes, boxes, pipes and other similar parts.

Swiss manufacturer Thalmann Maschinenbau AG presented a range of folding innovations at last year’s EuroBLECH exhibition, including its double folder TD Professional Plus series for roofing, facades and industrial applications. With a working length of 8.2m and 2mm bending capacity, the TD 200 double folding machine model automatically processes sheet metal bending with precision up as well as down, reducing material handling to the minimum.

A world premiere was its newly developed high performance hydraulics which, in combination with the recently introduced DFT system, provide a high degree of bending dynamics and productivity. The combination of both innovations results in a significant rise of bending speed which has increased the machine output significantly, said Marco Cappello, managing director of Thalmann.
