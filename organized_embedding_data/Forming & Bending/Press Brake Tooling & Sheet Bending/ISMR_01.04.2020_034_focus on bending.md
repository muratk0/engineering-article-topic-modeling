# FOCUS ON BENDING

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

French manufacturer, Jean Perrot, has a new 'JEAN PERROT ROBOTICS by CLEIA' brand.

Following its Bending 4.0 Open Day and the inauguration of the E COBOT BEND (collaborative bending cell) in France, Jean Perrot's announcement encompasses a robotic bending solution to improve material handling, in-process material flows and machine loading/ unloading. Using vision systems and pattern recognition, the system is designed as an 'all-in-one' solution. Previous innovations from the French manufacturer include the Maneo Premium LCS press brake, the Tool Box and dynamic hydraulic crowning for the press brake. New versions of the E FOLLOW Tool and the Tool Box are now available, along with a new customised version of its HMI (Human Machine Interface) and a new barcode management system.

At Blechexpo in Stuttgart (November 2019) and FABTECH in Chicago, LVD Company NV spotlighted its high-speed robotised bending cell, Dyna-Cell. Blechexpo was the world premiere of the Dyna-Cell with LVD's patented Easy-Form® Laser adaptive bending technology.

"With Dyna-Cell design criteria, we stipulated that the overall cell cost should not be more than twice the cost of a stand-alone Dyna-Press press brake inside the cell. So, this does not load the direct part cost and users do not need large volumes to make the process cost-effective," explained Matt Fowles of LVD.

Prima Power's eP-1030 Genius with tool change storage

In some automated press brake operations, users sometimes needed to allow around one hour per bend to teach a robot what to do. So, in conjunction with KUKA, LVD has developed an intelligent automatic programming wizard which uses the 10:10 rule. This means ten minutes for part import, bending and robot program generation. Ten minutes is also set aside for tool set-up and bending of the first part.

"Basically, it is art to part in 20 minutes – the benefit is 'no robot teaching'. We consider this to be a complete-game changer. Dyna-Cell can also handle both manned and unmanned operations for further flexibility," added Matt Fowles.

Dyna-Cell includes a Dyna-Press 40/15 Pro high-speed, electric-drive press brake. The 40-ton press brake features a 1500mm working length and a precision five-axis back gauge. It can bend at speeds of up to 25mm per second to generate more parts per hour, at a lower cost per part. The press brake uses vertical removal tooling style for rapid tooling changeover, even of heavier tools.

LVD has also developed and patented a new universal gripper which enables users to handle different families of products, and part geometries, without having to change the gripper. It can bend on three sides of a part without regripping. Because one gripper fits all applications, production is continuous and uninterrupted.

LVD's Industry 4.0-ready ToolCell XT automated tool-changing press brake now has extended tool storage capacity. Its Easy-Form 80/25 press brake is equipped with the Easy-Form® Laser in-process angle monitoring and correction system. Synchro-Form is LVD's next leap in adaptive bending technology and a new solution for large-profile bending. The company also produces PPED Series hydraulic press brakes, featuring a multiaxis back gauge, CNC V-axis crowning system and a 15" version of LVD's TOUCH-B graphical icon-driven CNC controller.

Mate Precision Tooling's CleanBend™ forming technology for press brakes is designed for high-quality sheet metal forming. Using rotating supports, the process mimics a folding operation with minimal to no marking of the sheet metal. Mate's press brake tooling also includes a full array of European Precision and WILA TRUMPF-style punches, dies, standard specials, “Bevel Tang Style" pressbrake tooling, specials and accessories, all available in segmented, full-length and half-length sizes.

Murata Machinery (Muratec) was established in 1935. In 1989, Muratec merged with Wiedemann. It exhibited its BB6020 ball screw press brake with operator support system, “VIDERE", at the EuroBLECH 2016 exhibition.

Turkish manufacturer, MVD Makina Saniya AS, has been manufacturing machines since 1950 and sheet metal processing machine tools, such as press brakes, shears, punching, plasma and laser cutting systems, since 1971. In 1994, it told ISMR, it produced the first tandem press brake in Turkey.

The move to servo electric drives has characterised the company's press brake development. Its range of iBend hydraulic press brakes sport numerous features, such as large throat depth and stroke, a height-adjustable control panel and automatic height-adjusted front support system. The company started hybrid press brake manufacturing in 2015 and launched its first servo press brakes in 2018.

"We also receive enquiries for large tonnage press brakes, as well as tandem press brakes, but the main enquiries we receive from customers are for 4.0 connectivity. Speed and increased productivity and efficiency of production are the main issues for customers," it told ISMR.

Olma Srl's Allbend is a flexible bending centre with an innovative sheet metal bending method inspired by programming simplicity, ease of operation, reduced energy consumption and low environmental impact.

Parma Stamp Srl has manufactured special and standard tools for sheet metal bending since 1974. Tools for press brakes are designed according to customer specifications.

At Blechexpo 2019, Prima Power exhibited its new eP- Genius 1030 bending solution with tool change storage. It features a servoelectric press brake, eP-1030, with automatic tool change storage.

“The result is a more versatile, faster and more reliable bending system, which eliminates manual set-up of the machine and allows the operator to commit his time and skills to the preparation of bending batches. This solution is especially suitable for minimum batches, where it is possible to reliably estimate machine set-up, and short cycle times which have become imperative for greater market competitiveness," it told ISMR.

The eP Genius press brake has 15 axes and a maximum tool capacity up to 32 metres (105 tons with a bending length of 3060mm), automatic CNC crowning, 5-axis back-gauge and IRIS Plus angle control system on CNC-controlled motorised arms. The tool storage can accommodate a total of 32 metres of tooling to be placed on eight tool-holder supports that move on three axes. It can handle tools (Wila-style) with a minimum length of 20mm up to 515mm. A rotating turret rotates the tools taken from storage for multiple processing possibilities. The latest-gen user interface allows the programming of the piece in a simple and intuitive way, both on the machine and off-line, as well as automatic management of machine set-up.

Prima Power also showcased its servoelectric BCe Smart bending cell with two new technologies. API, Advanced Profile Inspection, consists of a camera device used to apply corrections to a bend to reach the target angle within the required tolerance, and DABA technology (Dynamic Adjustment of the Bending Angle).

ROLLERI SpA has produced a "ROL200" clamp for press brake tool punches. The patented system enables pneumatic or manual vertical clamping for punches. Its QuickLock-System, compatible with Rolleri tools Type R2 and its ROL200 compatible with Rolleri tools type R1, are the latest innovations developed and manufactured in its production facilities. The family-owned company (Francesco and Marco Rolleri) is one of the largest press brake tooling manufacturers in Europe.

Rolla-V supplies a comprehensive range of press brake tools, as well as the patented Rolla-V.

At Blechexpo in Stuttgart at the end of last year, SafanDarley showcased a new model of Hybrid press brake, the H-Brake Hybrid+ 230T Ultra. The Hybrid+ series is an expansion of the existing Hybrid series and offers higher tonnages but also further benefits. At its core, there is an advanced dual hybrid drive with high return speed, high productivity and low energy consumption. As a result, says SafanDarley, costs are reduced and CO2 emissions decreased.

"We are proud to be expanding our hybrid-drive electrical press brakes aimed at saving energy. Our hydraulic machines also offer options such as start/stop systems to save energy. We are now extending our hybrid range of models up to 230 tonnes as well as our fully electric press brake line. We launched our first electric press brake twenty-four years ago, so 2020 is our anniversary," Marco van Drie, Manager, Direct Sales & Products, SafanDarley told ISMR.

At Blechexpo, SafanDarley also demonstrated its high-end E-Brake Ultra series. The E-Brake 50T-2050 Ultra is fitted, as standard, with a CNC crowning system and has a double screen to facilitate a paper-free working environment. The E-Brake Ultra can also be fitted with a 3D back-gauge and patented E-Bend L Blue angle measurement system. On each machine, an animation was shown of the company's new intelligent bending aid.

During Blechexpo, the Dutch manufacturer also presented an E-Brake 40T with an extremely large Q-size. The standard E-Brake 40T is equipped with a Q-size of 590mm; the machine demonstrated at Blechexpo had a Q-size of 1090mm. This enlarged Q dimension makes it possible to bend products with very tall upstanding sides.

SafanDarley has plans to launch an extensive range of software developments this year, focused around Industry 4.0 developments and the need for open access software in production lines. The company already offers robots with press brakes in its portfolio; its R-Brake 130T integrates the press brake and robot into one system. SafanDarley's E-Brake 35T Mini Cell is suitable for bending smaller-sized parts. The optional automatic tool and gripper change function makes it possible to quickly switch between different products.

At Blechexpo in Stuttgart (November 2019), Italian sheet metal specialist, Salvagnini, presented its B3.AU-TO press brake, equipped with the AU-TO automatic tool changer. The press brake also has ATA and ATA.L devices for setting up the upper and lower tools, for kit and batch one production.

"First and foremost, we simplified the use of the machine," explained Giulio Farina, CEO of Salvagnini Robotica, "and we made sure it could communicate its own data to the other machines and a company's ERP. The aim is to make bending an operation that is less influenced by process variables and, as a result, provides greater certainties, for example, in terms of processing times, as well as costs and budgets. The MAC2.0 (Material Attitude Correction) guarantees greater process stability, simultaneously reducing cycle times. For example, with a single laser detection, the press brake adapts to any variations in the material which, when necessary, can be automatically compensated for..."

Salvagnini Robotica is the Salvagnini Group's division for the development and manufacture of press brakes and associated robotic applications.

STREAM is Salvagnini's programming software for managing all activities in the office and on the factory floor. It is, says Salvagnini, the only point of access for all technologies, from cutting to bending, meeting all planning, programming, production, management, control and optimisation needs throughout the production process.

Salvagnini and NiEW, a business design and customer experience company specialising in the industrial sector, received a RedDot Award in the Brands & Communication Design 2019 category for the FACE project, dedicated to the design and development of the new human-machine interface for Salvagnini machine tools.

Italian manufacturer Schiavi has a number of innovative products, including its LineAr press brake with patented Crowning Free System designed for accuracy and "canoe-free" bending. Its ATLAS Pro CNC controller with new functionality equips Schiavi press brakes for the “Internet of Things" (Industry 4.0) while its Moses projection and touch-panel user interface, for full integration with the ATLAS CNC controller, is designed for 'error-free' set-up.

With the LineAr Plus, the manufacturer told ISMR, Schiavi has improved the LineAr concept, ensuring even greater accuracy for the bend. The “Plus" package is an optional extra that is available on all LineAr machines. Schiavi's LineAr Plus 220-40 (220 tonnes x 4 metres) large press brake features this capability. With LineAr technology, Schiavi manufactures press brakes of up to 400 tons and up to 6 metres in length with no pit.

The smart tool shuttle ensure quick tool setup, with 2-axis to handle clamps, punches and die handling. The ToolExpress TXP guarantees quick set-up of the machine without program, alongside expandable tool storage (from 32 to 50 metres).

TEDA Srl Speedy compact press brakes are designed for small part bending and are available in two versions, manual and NC-driven, and in two dimensions (550 - 835mm, 16 and 18 tons respectively). The company also offers SPEED GRIP pneumatic and manual clamping systems for press brakes, available in different versions.

Turkish manufacturer TOSKAR MAKINA's EaSy Fab series of press brakes has been designed with two main goals in mind - easy operation and maximum productivity in the long run. They feature a sturdy steel frame (ST44 A1 steel) and a wide variety of standard features such as a motorised R axis, manual crowning and full-length tooling.

With its TruBend Series 8000, TRUMPF is offering press brakes to handle large-format and thick components. Large open heights and bending lengths, along with press power of up to 1,000 tons, make the machines in the new TruBend Series 8000 true powerhouses. The latest addition to the TruBend 3000 range is the TruBend 3066 with a press force of 600kN and 2m bending length.

TRUMPF's extensive portfolio of bending machines incorporates the TruBend 7000 Series for the high-speed bending of small parts as well as the TruBend 3000 Series and 5000 Series for processing medium and large-scale parts with a press force of up to 3200kN. The TruBend Center Series takes automated bending to the next level of productivity.

TRUMPF also presented its new Workmate software solution at Blechexpo in November 2019 in Stuttgart. Using a tablet, machine operators and assembly workers can browse information designed to make their daily work easier – from advice on setting up machines to recommendations on how parts should be packaged. Workmate also provides assistance in setting up laser, punching, punch-laser and bending machines.

WILA offers a wide range of tooling, crowning and clamping systems. Its Tooling Identification and Positioning System (TIPS) is an intelligent system that can be incorporated in the clamping system of press brakes. Built-in electronics in the clamping system communicates with tools via an ID tag. The ID tag contains all of the key specifications of each particular tool. Users can thereby easily locate and determine if the correct tools have been selected for the bending program and whether they are in the correct position in the press brake.

The WILA Tool Advisor, intelligent online software, also helps users to design 'the best possible tools for specific bending applications'. If a standard tool is not available to form the sheet part, users can immediately create a special tool and order it online. Its new smartphone app also offers tool management for press brake tools.

Wilson Tool International (www.wilsontool.com), the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, offers easier part removal in press brake operations with swing ear sets. It manufactures a full selection of European and WILA/TRUMPF-Style precision press brake tooling and clamping systems. New innovative tooling ideas include the Corner Former and its Express Crowning™ system. It has also introduced intelligent tooling systems.

Express Air is its press brake clamp for bending applications. It gives sheet metal fabricators an air-powered option that clamps and seats front and back tools simultaneously with the touch of a button. AFH is the company's line of fixed height tooling designed to be fully compatible with AMADA fixed height tooling and machine control. Wilson Tools' Express CROWNING deflection control system helps to control deflection, or "the canoe effect", in press brake applications over four feet long. Press brake operators can now crown the lower beam with manual or electric options and secure dies with manual or hydraulic power.

"A lot of sheet metal contractors have a laser and a press brake and are starting to realise that they miss their turret punch press. We are consequently seeing an uptick in turret punch press sales and operators who want to figure out how to punch and form in the press brake. We now have this great new Brake Partner™ product that allows them to form in the press brake. We are seeing much greater demand for this," the tooling specialist told /SMR.

Wilson's ExpressRail™ 2000 hydraulic clamping system reduces set-up time with a wireless pendant control that clamps and seats press brake tooling in seconds, giving fabricators more open height and extended clamp life. Wilson Tool International now has a new division: Wilson Tool Additive™.■
