# NEW PRESS BRAKE SERIES

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

SALVAGNINI ALSO premiered the B3 KinEtic press brake series, featuring direct brushless drive technology, the innovative high-dynamic KERS energy recovery system, the AMS angle measurement system, the Salvagnini SiX numerical controller and the PRESSTUDIO programming console.

“This solution shares our expertise on panel bending technology with the world of press brakes, teaming speed and power levels similar to those of a hydraulic press brake with the accuracy and reactivity of an electric press brake and the consumption of a hybrid system, all controlled by SiX, our proprietary controller,” Salvagnini told ISMR.

“Uniquely, in that both the top and the bottom of the press are completely closed, this makes it extremely stable and ensures that the positions of the references remain constant throughout bending, generating a remarkably high degree of precision,” it added.

With two independently managed brushless motors, direct drive transmission and a circuit that changes the transmission ratio as required, the “fast” movements of the top-beam (both up and down) can reach a speed of 220 mm/s. The absence of proportional valves, with the use of direct drive transmission, brushless motorisation and energy in proportion to the real intensity of use and utilisation means that the B3 press brake offers high performance yet keeps consumption and power levels low. The KERS technology recovers energy and allows it to be re-used later. ■
