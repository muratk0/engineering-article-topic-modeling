# Step Automation Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.stepautomation.it

Step Automation is an Italian company that specialises in numeric controls. Its core business is developing, manufacturing and selling products and services for industrial automation and, more specifically, CNCs, software and devices to manage press brakes.

Founded in 2012, it has become a supplier to various manufacturers of bending systems. Recently, it has developed specific projects for the automation of folding machines and traditional hydraulic presses with customised control and programming systems.

"Our Motion modular units are designed to interface with, and control, all types of press brakes. Our EASYCUT new generation of controls (for swing shears and rack angle shears) uses a touchscreen for intuitive and interactive programming," it told ISMR.

"Nowadays, modern sheet-metal production is inconceivable without high-performance software. Control algorithms optimise the machine cycle, reduce the time required for setup and ensure consistently high quality," it concluded.

## TECOI Corte SL

https://tecoi.com

Spanish company, TECOI, designs, manufactures, installs, develops and maintains metal processing machines with technologies such as laser, plasma, oxy-cutting, platemachining and edge preparation for welding.

"Currently, we export 85% of our production. We have also implemented Industry 4.0 technology in some of the leading shipyards in the sector," it explained.

Its TEKNOS machine is designed for processing largedimension material with processes such as plasma cutting; bevelling; oxy-fuel cutting; drilling; marking and pipe cutting. THOR is a versatile sheet-metal processing system offering plasma cutting with one or two heads; machining; bevelling; oxy-fuel cutting of sheet metal, tubes, profiles and billets; drilling; milling; countersinking; marking and scribbing.

Developed for fibre laser cutting of large formats and thicknesses, the LS-CF features laser power of up to 20kW and comes with TWIN DISK dual-head system and CAP automatic pallet changeover. Industry 4.0 systems include Solomon (a collaborative cell system), SCADA and Production Plan software.
