# Faster bending demands the machine operator’s full concentration

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

## AUTOMATE TO ACCUMULATE

“The RAS MiniBendCenter is the world’s first automatic folding centre for small parts. Blanks are automatically fed, squared and measured. Tool setup is all automatic. A manipulator moves and rotates the blank automatically to the exact position. The folding cell folds the part up and down. The MiniBendCenter leads to low costs per part and a quantum leap in productivity,” RAS Reinhardt told ISMR.

RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH now offers the patented intelligent crowning system on its line of FLEXIbend folding machines.

Integrated sensors measure the actual beam deflection during the first couple of degrees of the folding beam movement. Using this information, the CNC control activates the crowning system which automatically compensates for beam deflection during the folding beam movement. The CNC writes the adjustment value for each bend into the program so there is no need for additional measuring cycles for the remaining parts.

As a specialist in press brakes for the thinner sheet metal segment, Safan offers systems from the lightest E-Brake (35 tons and a working length of 1.25 metres) to the heaviest H-Brake (480 tons with a working length of 7.50 metres). Safan’s E-Brake was the first electronic press brake in the world, developed as a new concept in 1995. Its groundbreaking feature was its servo-electronic drive, leading to a very even distribution of pressure (making crowning unnecessary in the majority of cases).

Safan developed a new breed of machines for automated sheet metal processing, dubbed the I-Brake. The robots are integrated into the machine (horizontally, on the left and right).

Safan is part of the Nivora Group, which acquired Darley, a press brake specialist in the Netherlands, in January this year. Darley produces a range of specialised press brakes varying in bending length from 1550 up to 10,000mm and pressures from 50 up to 1,000 tons.

## BENDING CELLS

“Our TruBend Cell 7000 is the fastest system in the world for the automatic bending of small pieces. With a cycle time from an average of only 4 seconds per tilt, it delivers up to twice as high part throughput as conventional bending cells and could replace three manual bending units,“ TRUMPF told ISMR.

The cell basically consists of a bending machine TruBend Cell 7036 Edition, a bending robot BendMaster and a loading unit LoadMaster Bend. For all three there are no central control interfaces, which makes the short cycle time of 4 seconds per tilt possible. Replacement of the bending tools can be automated, with the tool changer ToolMaster. The range of components including system and roadway are in an enclosure and need 21 square feet of floor space.

The great strength of the small TruBend 7036 press brake is its speed which it owes to a new TRUMPF strategy for beams and back gauges: reduced mass combined with a high-performance drive. Back stops made of light carbon fiber (CFK) allow the back gauge to achieve an acceleration of up to 20 m/s2. The beam of the TruBend Series 7000 can maintain this speed due to its electrical torque engine, which operates up to three and a half times as fast as the beam on a conventional press brake.

Faster bending demands the machine operator’s full concentration which the TruBend 7000 Series supports with a novel ergonomic design. The LED lighting system also aids concentration by illuminating both the work area and the space behind the beam without emitting heat.
