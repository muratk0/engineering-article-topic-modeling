AT EUROBLECH 2021, WILA will introduce a complete range of its patented self-locking pneumatic tool holders consisting of clamping, crowning and bottom tool-holder devices.

“This extremely rapid self-locking clamping system clamps and locks the tools using self-adjusting wedges to secure each clamping pin. In the event of a loss of air pressure, the tools stay firmly locked. The system works on normal shop air pressure, which eliminates the need for hydraulic oil. This development focuses on the rapidly growing market for electrical press brakes where the use of oil and hydraulics is not required,” outlined WILA.
