# New panel bender

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

SALVAGNINI has chosen Blechexpo 2015 in Stuttgart, Germany (3-6 November 2015) for the world premiere of its new panel bender P4lean-2520, the result of thorough market analysis and feedback from more than 3000 panel benders installed at Salvagnini customers’ plants worldwide.

“The lean generation adopts electric actuators and sealed bending cylinders which are driven by brushless motors. This solution not only eliminates the hydraulic unit but reduces consumption significantly as well as the wear and stress of components. Each lean panel bender features MAC 2.0 proprietary technology which detects and automatically compensates for any deviations from the nominal values of thickness and material properties,” Salvagnini explained.

“P4lean-2520 also provides new and significant geometrical dimensions which boost performance on average by 15%, maintaining the overall layout of the system. The bending height rises from 165 to 203 mm with the advantage of greater versatility. The maximum diagonal rises from 2800 to 3200 mm, which makes feeding of large panels (of up to 3050 mm) possible.”

Also on show during Blechexpo at the Salvagnini stand 1608 in Hall 1 will be the latest L5 High dynamic fibre laser and B3 ATA press brake.

Salvagnini recently presented SaM, its new proprietary app which provides real-time information on Salvagnini machine status on smartphone, tablet or PC using remote monitoring. By implementing the philosophy of Industry 4.0 this new tool allows the user to keep up-to-date with the status of the system and to receive messages and statistics on current production.
