# Press Brake Leveling: Safety and Verification Principles

Proper leveling is part of press brake installation and should be completed before normal production use. The article describes checking level in both the left-to-right and front-to-back directions, then ensuring the machine is supported as intended at its base.

## Before Any Adjustment

Leveling and support adjustments involve a heavy machine and must follow the machine manual, the approved installation plan, and site requirements. The floor must be suitable for the machine and its intended support or anchoring arrangement.

Before work begins:

- Isolate energy sources as required by the machine manual and site safety procedures.
- Do not work beneath, support, lift, or reposition the machine using improvised methods.
- Use only approved lifting, support, adjustment, and fastening methods.
- Have qualified personnel assess the floor, machine supports, and anchoring requirements.
- Do not rely on generic shim thicknesses, adjustment-screw positions, or support arrangements; these are machine- and site-dependent.

## Checking Level

The article recommends using a level placed on a suitable reference surface associated with the lower die area. Any machine settings that affect the reference surface should be placed in the condition specified by the manufacturer before measurement.

Check level in two directions:

1. **Left to right:** Place the level across the machine in the transverse direction. A displaced bubble indicates that one side is higher than the other.
2. **Front to back:** Reorient the level in the longitudinal direction. A displaced bubble indicates a difference in elevation between the front and rear of the machine.

The objective is to bring the measuring instrument to its centered indication in both directions, within the tolerance specified for that machine. Recheck the first direction after adjusting the second, since changes at one support point can affect the overall condition.

## Adjustment and Support Principles

The article describes using the machine's base feet for leveling adjustments. Such work should be performed only with the approved adjustment method and tools specified by the machine manufacturer.

Adjustment points may be used to correct transverse and longitudinal level, but the sequence, allowable travel, load distribution, and final locking method must be confirmed from the manual. Do not assume that a particular foot pair, screw, or adjustment sequence applies to every press brake.

After the machine is level, its structural supports should not be left suspended or unevenly loaded. Where shims or support plates are required, their material, thickness, placement, and load-bearing suitability must be determined by qualified personnel in accordance with the installation documentation. The article mentions placing support material beneath machine uprights after leveling; this should be treated as a machine-specific installation requirement rather than a general instruction.

## Final Verification

After adjustments are complete:

- Verify level again in both directions.
- Confirm that all required supports are carrying load as intended.
- Confirm that adjustment devices, fasteners, and anchors are in their required final condition.
- Inspect for unintended gaps, instability, or uneven support.
- Perform any commissioning checks required by the machine manual before production operation.

If leveling cannot be achieved within the manufacturer’s limits, or if the floor, supports, or anchors appear unsuitable, stop work and obtain assessment from qualified installation or service personnel.
