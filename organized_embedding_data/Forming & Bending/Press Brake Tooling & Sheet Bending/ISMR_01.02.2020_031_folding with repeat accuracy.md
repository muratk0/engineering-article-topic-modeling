# Folding with repeat accuracy

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

HANS SCHRÖDER MASCHINENBAU was at SamuExpo 2020 (Pordenone, Italy, from 6-8 February 2020) to showcase its folders, together with its Italian partner EVOMACH. On two booths (hall 4, booth 10 and hall 2, booth 59) three manual folders, as well as one motorised folder, were displayed.

“The PowerBend Professional UD 3200/3.0 folder enables semi-automated operation and is very gentle on the material. The ASK 3 1250/1.5 segment folder is equipped with a patented eccentric quick clamping system that enables tool change of the clamping- and the folding beam with just one single manual action. Thanks to segmented tools on all beams, the ASK 3 is suitable for those who need more clearance for their complex workpieces,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

Hans Schröder Maschinenbau also presented its BAM 2000/0.75folder which has been optimised for construction sites. The folding beam with crowning device can be adjusted for a radius blade cutter and features a quick clamping system for better handling. Schröder also exhibited a Modular 2000/0.8 manual folder on booth 59, hall 2, which can be connected to one long folder in a few simple steps.

“On booth 10 in hall 4 of SamuExpo, EVOMACH presented the PowerBend Professional, one of the most popular Schröder machines. This machine offers the option of an up-and-down folding beam.

The ability to carry out counter-folds, without turning the sheet, makes handling of large sheets easier and reduces the risk of damage,” added the manufacturer.

“In combination with the suction gauge, all bends on one side can be automatically processed. Automation increases the machine's utility in series production. It is also extremely flexible for processing small batches and individual workpieces, using the rotating clamping beam with its second tool station.”

The Schröder Group is a leading provider of folding, cutting, crimping, beading and shearing machines of all kinds. Further information is available at www.schroedergroup.eu/en
