# Invest for success

[TARİH: 01.02.2021 ISMR]

Belia already has a range of sheet metal bending equipment on-site, including press brakes, folders and a semi-automatic panel bender (FAST-BEND Fbe5) from Prima Power. It continuously invests in the improvement and modernisation of its sheet metal bending machinery to provide 'any type of bent profile' to its customers.

"We have a large, spacious raw materials warehouse for the immediate availability of material to satisfy even the most demanding customers or for urgent laser cutting, welding or bending processes. It also contains over 100 metres of standard and special tools. We carefully manage delivery of materials, checking and preparing them for shipment in the most appropriate way to protect workpieces (whether one-offs or mass-produced parts)," explained Belia Srl.

Quality is a key consideration for the manufacturer, both in the prototyping and production phases.

The 80-ton, two-metre press brake is equipped with a two-axis back gauge X-R: with two straight and two shaped fingers for more stable support of workpieces.

"Our new 4,600 square metre headquarters meant the purchase of increasingly advanced machinery, aimed at innovation and the widest processing possibilities for various types of metals ranging from steel, copper and iron to bronze," concluded the Italian subcontractor. ■
