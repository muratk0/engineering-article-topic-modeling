# Complex tube and pipe geometries

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

SCHWARZE-ROBITEC PRESENTED its extensive product range of tube- and pipe bending machines and fully automatic bending cells at FABTECH in Chicago recently. Visitors saw a CNC 100 E TB MR tube- and pipe bending machine in action.

"Whether for the production of different radii or bend-in-bend systems, such as manifolds and exhaust pipes, with the fullelectric and multi-stack CNC tube bending machines, a particularly wide range of different tube and profile dimensions can be bent. In combination with individually adjustable tools, the multi-level technology ensures the problem-free and accurate shaping of tubes with very short lengths between the individual bends. This can be used to bend exhaust pipes with a bending radius of 1 × D, using the smallest straight intermediate lengths between two bends," the German tube specialist told ISMR.

The integrated rapid clamping system "Quick Tool Unlock" enables users to rapidly change bending tools and requires almost no tools at all. Set-up times are therefore significantly improved and machine downtime can be substantially minimised. The NxG control system also contributes to the rapid cycle speeds of the highperformance series – users can therefore reduce production time by up to 35%, depending upon the tube system to be bent, according to the manufacturer.

The company's U.S. branch office moved to new offices warehouse facilities in Grand Rapids (Michigan) in April 2017. The goal is to further expand its market in North America.
