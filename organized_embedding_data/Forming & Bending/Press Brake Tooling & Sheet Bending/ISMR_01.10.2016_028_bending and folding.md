# BENDING AND FOLDING

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

Intelligent press brake tooling, eco hybrid drive systems, small batch processors, automatic angle measurement and energy-saving systems are just some of the bending innovations under the spotlight in this article.

From touchscreen control and ecohybrid drive systems to automatic angle measurement, quicker tool change/ set-up and more sophisticated sensing technologies, today's press brakes can also handle smaller batches and, by integrating robotic and automation technologies, deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations.

Many of the latest bending innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending.

In this article, we highlight an alphabetical selection of the latest new press brake, folder and panel bending technology developments in the global market.

## Technology in focus

Portuguese manufacturer, ADIRA, has developed the HD Greenbender, which it dubs “the first dematerialised heavy duty press brake in the world", designed with a topology optimisation technique.

"The patented multi-frame concept involves a very deep throat and avoids the need for pit installation in long machines, for a fraction of the steel needed when compared to conventional press brakes," ADIRA told ISMR.

BB3515, the latest member of ADIRA's electric press brake Bluebender family, has a bending force of 35 tons (1.5 metre length) and features rapid cycle times with very low energy consumption.

With these latest technologies integrated around the "VPSS 3i" concept, AMADA aims to provide its customers with highly productive solutions. AMADA also provides automation and accessories for bending.

The company's range of press brakes is designed for flexibility, productivity and energy-saving (inverter-controlled stop/ start technology). Its HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new 'Smartphone-style' 3D control unit. New Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture.

ASco GmbH provides technical solutions from innovative long folding machines up to fully automated bending centres. Its Variobend long length folding machines and up and down bending machines are available (from 4 - 12 metres) to process steel of up to 2mm.
