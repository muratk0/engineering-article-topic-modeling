# A selection of the latest global press brake bending systems, tooling and technology innovations come under the spotlight in this update.

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

The bending length of a press brake is the maximum possible length of a metal sheet which can be bent. The press brake's bending force, known as tonnage, determines how much pressure can be applied by the punch during a bend. Higher tonnage allows for bending thicker and harder metals, and lower tonnage is most appropriate for thinner metals.

A press brake bends metal by lowering a punch onto sheet metal that has been positioned on top of a die. The workpiece is clamped between matching punch and die to form a pre-set bend. There are several types of press brake (such as pneumatic, servo electric, hydraulic and mechanical). The characterisation of capacity of a press brake is based on parameters such as working length; working height; amplitude; stroke length; tonnage and distance between side housings or frame uprights.

According to a new report by Persistence Market Research, the global press-brake market size reached US$ 569.1 million at the end of 2021. Sales of press brakes are projected to increase at a CAGR of 6.1% across the 2022-2032 forecast period to reach a market value of US$ 1.03 billion by the end of 2032.
