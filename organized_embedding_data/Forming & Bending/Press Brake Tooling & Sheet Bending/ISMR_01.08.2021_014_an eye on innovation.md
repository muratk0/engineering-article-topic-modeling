# AN EYE ON INNOVATION

We highlight a selection of the latest new press brake bending systems, tooling and technology innovations in the global market.

ISMR SAYS:

“Industry 4.0, automation, safety, environmental factors, increased accuracy and ease of operation are key elements in today's press brake designs."

A press brake bends sheet metal by lowering a punch onto sheet metal that has been positioned on top of a die. The metal may be bent several times by the press brake until the desired form has been achieved. There are several types of press brake (such as pneumatic, servo electric, hydraulic and mechanical).

The press brake controls the movement of either the punch (upper tool) or the die (bottom tool) and provides the press force using hydraulic rams or electrical servo motors. The press brake's bending force, known as tonnage, determines how much pressure can be applied by the punch during a bend. Higher tonnage allows for bending thicker and harder metals, and lower tonnage is most appropriate for thinner metals.

Market analyst, Technavio, estimates that press brake market size will grow up to US$ 413,87 million, at a CAGR of 4,96% during 2021-2025. It expects 39% of market growth to originate from the APAC region during the forecast period. To make the most of the opportunities and recover from post COVID-19 impact, it recommended that market vendors focus more on growth prospects in the fast-growing segments, while maintaining their positions in slow-growing segments.

## New bending innovations

Industry 4.0, automation, safety, environmental factors, increased accuracy and ease of operation are key elements in today's press brake designs. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake systems, tooling and technology developments in the global market.

ADIRA Metal Forming Solutions SA is headquartered in Portugal and operates the business under various segments such as press brakes, shears, software and AM. The company offers products such as PF Fast PH Heavy Duty, GreenBender, Bluebender, and PA Hexa C (hydraulic press brake). At EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover, ADIRA presented a hybrid press brake (PF016040) with a new generation of Green dynamics technology (based on the Hoerbiger hybrid ePrAX®control), a fully electric Bluebender press brake (BB003515) with a new generation of Bluedrive systems and, for the first time, an electric press brake (BB005020) with automatic tool changer (ATC) to reduce set-up time and maximise machine productivity.

Japanese manufacturer AMADA offers products such as HG Series, HG-1003ATC HD-NT Series and EG Series. Its HG1003ATC bending system, for its V-factory digital sheet metal processing solution, is a servo/ hydraulic hybrid drive system which features an Automatic Tool Changer (ATC) and an automatic bend indicator sensor plus an AMNC 3i control and ID scanner.

Its HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new 'Smartphone style' 3D control unit. New Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture. Its EG series electric press brake incorporates AMADA's patented dual servo technology which enables high-speed precision part manufacture with additional operator-focused benefits. Its HFE 3i, HG ATC and HG ARs press brake series encompass most bending scenarios from a manuallybased scenario to a fully automated, robotsupported production cell. Its HG ATC series with automatic tool changer, complex parts in batch sizes starting at quantities of one can be produced whereas with the HG ARs, even large batches of highly complex parts (in unmanned operation) can be produced.

Amada's compact HFE-5012M2 pressbrake has been developed to offer fast and comfortable bending of small parts with medium complexity. The machine provides 50 tonnes of bending force and a working width of 1270mm. The open height of the frame has been increased, enabling deeper box bending AMADA's FAST (Finger with Active Security Technology) stop fingers can be fitted onto most new AMADA bending machines and have an active safety device built-in that limits risk to operators if they come into contact with moving parts, even at full speed.

The manufacturer also offers fully automated bending solutions and VPSS 3i software, which can simulate all processing operations at once.

Boschert GmbH has produced a dynamic hybrid-drive press brake, the Quick-Bend, with purported energy savings of up to 80%. Quick and nearly silent, the Quick-Bend only uses electrical energy during the bending process. Based on the PROFI 28 CNC and PROFI 56 CNC press brake series, with bending lengths ranging from 1000 - 2200mm, the designers developed a press brake with an innovative electric-hydraulic hybrid drive, characterised by a combined E-motor and pump unit. The bending speed range is 8 - 10mm/s and repeatability is +/- 0.02mm.

Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, has enjoyed success with its Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre 'plug and play' press brake with rapid bending speeds. This small press brake has already won a German design award and is designed for rapid, efficient parts production. A new Mobile Bending Robot is available for feeding the Xpert 40, turning it into a compact, automated bending cell for producing 3D sheet metal components. The automation module is wheeled in front of the press brake and simply interfaced to it in a plug-and-play arrangement. In minutes, users can set up the robotics and load sheet metal blanks for bending. Programming is carried out offline and ByVision Bending software allows data to be imported without interrupting the job currently in progress.

Bystronic's Xpert Pro press brake has now been equipped with the LAMS 4.1 angle measurement system's springback compensation for bending sheet metal. The LAMS angle measurement system compensates for any deviations in sheet thickness. In addition to sheet thickness fluctuations, the system also automatically compensates for springback and rolling direction variations. Corrections relating to the sheet metal that is being processed can be stored and transferred to other machines - even those that are not equipped with LAMS This means that all other Bystronic press brakes in operation within a company also benefit from this function.

Bystronic's Xpert 40 press brake also now features new optical tool detection. Cameras recognize the data matrix codes on the tools and sound an alarm if any deviances are detected. This results, says Bystronic, in reduced set-up time and improved process quality because 'the right tool is always in the right position'. The Xpert 40 is a compact press brake designed for cost-effective small parts production and simple operation.

Bystronic has introduced a new app that monitors, in real-time, all processes taking place within its laser cutting machines, press brakes and water jet cutting machines. Called ByCockpit, the software fits neatly with the digital networking requirements of Industry 4.0. It evaluates the collected data and generates status reports, allowing factory output to be optimised. Bystronic and Lantek are also developing an MES software solution for sheet metal part production. The Bystronic MES interlinks all the manufacturing steps and guides sheet metal products on a suitable path through the production process, much like an intelligent navigation system.
