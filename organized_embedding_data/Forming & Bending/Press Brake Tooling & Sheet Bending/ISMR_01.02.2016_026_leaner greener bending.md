# LEANER, GREENER BENDING

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

Today’s press brakes can handle smaller batches and deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations

Greater flexibility, accuracy, safety and a focus on energy savings characterise many of the press brake models available today for leaner, greener bending operations. Featuring a raft of innovations from eco hybrid drive systems to automatic angle measurement, quicker tool change/set-up and more sophisticated sensing technologies, today's press brakes can also handle smaller batches and, by integrating robotic and automation technologies, deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations.

Many of the latest bending innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake developments in the global market.

## The latest innovations

Japanese manufacturer Amada’s comprehensive range of press brakes is designed for flexibility and productivity. Its energy-saving systems (inverter-controlled stop/start technology) reduce energy consumption when bending by 60% and, consequently, overall running costs.

Amada’s HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new ‘Smartphone-style’ 3D control unit. Its new Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture. Following impressive growth in Spain, Amada has announced the opening of a new Technical Centre in Barcelona.

Turkish manufacturer Baykal’s APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. It has been working on the project line to be used for the construction of the seven sports stadiums for the 2022 Qatar Fifa World Cup for almost a year and has pioneered development of a fully automatic pole cutting and bending machine.

For its manufacturing operations, Baykal uses three factories which together combine a production area of 60,000 square metres, making it one of Europe’s largest facilities for sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.

Swiss manufacturer Bystronic has had success with its Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre press brake with rapid bending speeds. It has been designed as a ‘plug and play’ system - the machine can be moved by a fork lift truck, partly for offloading and installation but, more importantly, can also be moved to suit changes in a factory layout.

“This small press brake has already won a German design award and aims for rapid, efficient parts’ production. When we developed Xpert 40, our customers took an active part in the development process. This was very helpful and useful – we will, no doubt, employ this principle again for future product development,” Johan Elster, Head of Markets, Bystronic told ISMR in November 2015.

At Blechexpo in November 2015, Bystronic also showcased its new automatic tool changer for a standard Xpert press brake (18m of bottom tooling and 9m of upper tooling stored at the back of the press brake) to free press brake operators for other tasks and eliminate errors.

It intends to launch a global performance line in North America and Asia, which was on display at FABTECH in Chicago the following week. This featured a mid-range fibre laser and press brake for mid-range performance

Bystronic also profiled its Xact Smart press brake at Blechexpo – it is available in several versions with press capacities of between 100 and 225 tons and with up to four axes on the back gauge. Depending upon the press capacity and the back gauge system, the Xact Smart enables a broad spectrum of simple angles through to complex bent parts to be processed. This creates a high level of flexibility for rapid job changes and use as a fixed bending station.

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. The company currently manufactures guillotine shears, hydraulic/hybrid press brakes, laser cutting machines, punch presses, plasma cutters and assorted metalworking machinery.

In 2014, it won two awards in the industria category of the GoodDesign awards for its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine. The Fibermak machine also won IF’s Best Design Award. In 2015, the family-run company celebrated 50 years in operation.

“In conventional press brakes, energy is consumed even in standby mode but the Evolution II press brake only uses energy whilst it is bending. It can bend at a rate of 20 mm/sec. using servo motors and a highly efficient pump. It is also quiet – at 63dB noise-less and has high bending accuracy of between +/-0.01mm. These new generation press brakes are developed to require fewer oil changes (every five years),” Ermaksan explained.

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the SPEED-BEND (synchronized hydraulic press brake), the POWER-BEND PRO 3-axis CNC press brake and the ECO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake. The company’s MICRO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake is a small, productive hydraulic press brake with the capacity to bend 5mm mild steel with 1m x 40 tonnes of press force and one-axis back gauge. Ermaksan’s HEAVY DUTY press brake features a dynamic crowning system and 6-axis servo motor back gauge system. It also boasts special top and bottom tools and a 610mm throat depth.

“Customers want flexibility and less downtime – there is an increasing need for automation. For press brakes, the trend for higher durability is increasing,” Ersin Yildiz, Ermaksan’s sales manager, told ISMR.

Italian specialist Gasparini offers a press brake range that includes the X-BEND (press force from 50 to 170 tons) and the X-PRESS ECO. Its new Dener hydraulic press brakes were designed to meet a wide variety of needs, accommodating weights ranging from 40 to 2000 tons and offering from 3 to more than 11-axis capabilities. Its new electric MC press brake is leaner, greener and more reliable delivering quiet operation and faster cycle times.

The Diamond BB Series features a compact, ball screw design driven by an AC servo motor with 40 metric tons of force and one-micron repeatability. It utilises a dualdrive system with a high speed back gauge, larger stroke and hydraulic power.

Haco is a specialist in sheet metal fabrication and routinely produces press brakes for a diverse group of industries. Its current portfolio includes press brakes with capacities from 40 to 2,000 tons and working lengths from 1.6 to 10m.

LVD Company nv recently announced a new generation of its PPEC series hydraulic press brakes. PPEC models of up to 350 tons now feature a new modern and ergonomic design with integrated status lighting. In addition, the touch control for all PPEC machines, ranging from 35 to 640 tons, has been upgraded to LVD’s latest graphical icondriven TOUCH-B controller.

Greater flexibility, accuracy, safety and a focus on energy savings characterise many of the press brake models available today.

“The new design, with built-in status lighting, gives the PPEC an elegant appearance in line with the Easy-Form® press brakes. The status LED lights indicate the machine’s operation status, enabling more effective shop management for higher throughput. An optional LED lighting system illuminates the back gauge and front work zone areas to provide better operator visibility,” LVD told ISMR.

The touch control has been upgraded from the CADMAN® Lite control to the full-featured, icon driven TOUCH-B control. The controller is compatible with LVD's offline bending software CADMAN®-B. Press brakes equipped with the TOUCH-B control also feature a jog button on the foot pedal, allowing the operator to ‘jog’ individual axes of the machine for fine adjustments.
