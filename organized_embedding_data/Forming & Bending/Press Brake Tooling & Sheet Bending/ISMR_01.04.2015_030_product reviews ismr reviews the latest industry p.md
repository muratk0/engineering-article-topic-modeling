## Flexible folding

HANS SCHRÖDER MASCHINENBAU GMBH has re-engineered its ASK folding machine. Segmented tools on all beams enable large clearances for complex work pieces. New ASK 3 options include better tool heights and improved machine handling.

“In addition to the ASK’s working lengths of 1000mm and 1500mm, the ASK 3 now offers a 1250mm version. Another innovation is the option to choose from three different working heights. As a result, greater clearances can be achieved – up to 180mm at the clamping beam, up to 12mm at the bottom beam and up to 142mm at the folding beam. The foot pedal for rapid opening and closing of the clamping beam has been re-worked and facilitates the repeated clamping of the sheet through the significantly larger opening of the clamping beam,” explained the manufacturer.

“The freely positionable sheet support and gauge arms are built so that they be moved and fixed. The position of the sheet support arms can easily be changed from the operator’s side. Unnecessary walking routes around the machine can therefore be avoided. Clamping pressure adjustment and folding beam have also been improved. They can now be adjusted with knurled screws without any additional tools. A very practical feature is the new, removable gauge for the bending angle. This means that the operator can keep an angle gauge adjustment but still, for example, hem a flange. The gauge can easily be moved to the side so that the folding beam is free.”

The manufacturer has also added a deposit station for tool segments that are not required for an operation in progress. See www.schroedergroup.eu ■

## Prima Power launches new fibre laser

BASED ON THE Platino® Fiber laser platform, which has clocked up over 1,500 installations worldwide, the new version of the Platino® Fiber laser is now available in models of up to 5kW.

“The new machine delivers piercing time reductions of up to 90%, near-zero lens set-up times and cutting speeds of up to 5m/min. on stainless steel, which is on average 20% faster than the 4kW machine. These advances result in ground-breaking levels of productivity and cost reduction,” Prima Power told ISMR.

The latest Platino® Fiber features nitrogen piercing, which uses a flush of nitrogen to suppress the plasma and reduces piercing times in mid- to high-thickness material by up to 90%. For 12mm mild steel, the new Platino® Fiber pierces in less than one second.

During trials, on a sample 12mm thick mild steel part with 120 holes, cycle time reductions were around 25%, said the manufacturer. Additional benefits for users of nitrogen piercing include less heating of the part during piercing and hence a better quality part, due to a smaller heat-affected zone and the cooling effect of the nitrogen.

The new Platino® Fiber also has an option for a Laser Piercing Monitor to give real-time feedback and check when piercing is completed. The re-designed fibre head uses a single universal lens for all materials, eliminating the need to change lenses resulting in near-zero set-up time. Other features of the new fibre head include 35mm stroke on the focal axis, a dedicated lens drawer with Prima Power OPC (Optical Precision Control) quick alignment technology and exchangeable protective glass, which enables easy inspection.

The new machine also includes Prima Power Fibre Plasma Monitoring. This system detects problems with the cutting and formation of plasma when cutting is lost. An automated cycle then resolves the problem. The machine is available with a wide range of nozzles for applications, with an option for automatic nozzle exchange, and comes with Prima Power SIPS which protects the head in the event of a collision. For higher efficiency on material of up to 5mm thick, the machine integrates a new piercing strategy which results in drastic cycle time reductions, especially on complex nests of parts. Prima Power’s SmartMoves technology is also integrated for intelligent management of cutting parameters, which allows shorter cycle times and increased reliability through smoother operation. ■
