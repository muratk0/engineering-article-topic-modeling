# New Pipe Clamp for Workbench

[TARİH: 01.04.2026 The Fabricator]

Pipe Clamp Universal, with swiveling arbor, enables flexible clamping at a + / - 42 degree angle. This Pipe Clamp combines the advantages of conventional 45° or 90° clamps. It can be used at an angle as well as straight, resulting in additional clamping options and a more variable clamping range.

The horizontal round tube with a length of 6″ is fully adjustable and creates, combined with the swiveling arbor, a variable clamping range. The length of the vertical pipe is 6″.

To integrate the vertical pipe into the system boreholes, an adjusting ring is used (Item No. US160653).

A long lifetime and efficiency is guaranteed by the high-quality material, as well as the option to order spare parts individually.
