# THE BEND FACTOR

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

The TruBend 7036

ISMR highlights the latest press brake innovations on the market today

Quality bending, right first time, is key for today’s press brake users. Accordingly, manufacturers are launching key innovations to ensure high levels of productivity, precision and safety on their press brake designs. ISMR spotlights some of the latest new innovations and models to be launched in the market.

## AN EYE ON SAFETY

Bystronic’s Fast Bend safety system increases the safety and productivity of a press brake.

“It enables travel at the rapid-advance speed right until the tool is almost on the metal sheet so that travel at working speed is virtually limited to just the actual bending process itself,” the Swiss manufacturer told ISMR. “This achieves a time saving of around 20 per cent or, put another way, instead of four conventional bends, it is now possible to make five bends over the same period of time.”

The new safety system is re-set automatically after every tool change and places the tool at the correct working height. If a tool is not recognised by the system, the machine stops. Fast Bend is delivered as standard on Bystronic’s Hammerle 3P press brakes and is available as an optional extra on its Xpert range.

Up to a third of the energy used by a conventional press brake can be saved

Lower energy consumption, reduced machine noise and less heat loss characterise Bystronic’s Energy Saver. As soon as the upper beam has reached the upper dead centre following a bending operation, the main motor of the press brake is automatically switched off until the next bending process is started. Up to a third of the energy used by a conventional press brake can be saved, says Bystronic.

The Swiss manufacturer has also produced a new bending aid that increases the flexibility of material handling, particularly for small batches incorporating different types and thicknesses of material.

For example, the user has at his disposal rollers for heavy parts and brushes for the careful handling of stainless steel material.

Italian manufacturer Codatto specialises in panel bending technology. It is already working on new machines and options to support its EVA 2516 panel bender.

“The EVA panel bender can fold sheet of up to 3.2mm thick and 2500mm along the side. The bending angle control option makes it possible to obtain usable panels from the very first piece, eliminating production scrap,” Codatto told ISMR.

Colgar SpA has over 60 years of manufacturing experience and now has a global user base. It operates from two sites close to Milan and Venice, Italy and produces a range of press brakes from 320kN to 30MN with bending lengths of up to 12 metres (including heavy tandem press brakes), multi-axis capability and a range of angle correction and robotic handling systems.

## SAVING ENERGY

Turkish manufacturer Ermaksan designs a range of press brakes. Its new generation Evolution series hybrid press brakes are fast, silent, precise, environmentally friendly and energy-saving. They are designed to bend small parts, even at different points, with high repetition and precision levels.

Hydraulic pistons driven by AC servo motors with no proportional direction valves are featured on Evolution series hybrid press brakes, meaning more precision and less maintenance. And there is no pressure loss. While the pump is working continuously all day on conventional press brakes, the only energy consumption used on the Evolution hybrid press brake is when the operator pushes the foot pedal and moves the top beam for bending. As there is no pressure loss, energy i.e. bending power is at its highest level. Oil level is also reduced by 80 per cent, Ermaksan told ISMR.
