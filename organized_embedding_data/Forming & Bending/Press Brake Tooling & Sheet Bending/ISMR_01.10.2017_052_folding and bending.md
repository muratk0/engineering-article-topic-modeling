# Folding and bending

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

AT THIS YEAR'S Blechexpo trade fair, Schröder Group will show a representative crosssection of its wide range of folding machines (Hall 1, stand 1709). A highlight on the booth will be its industrial folder, PowerBend Industrial, which can bend up to 6mm thick steel sheet. With its Up-and-Down-folding beam and a suction gauge, this machine offers semiautomated operation.

When working with a suction gauge, the sheet metal can be gauged against the folding beam and is fixed pneumatically. In combination with the Up-and-Down folding beam, the bending program executes all bends on one side with no further intervention needed from the operator.

The same features can be seen on its PowerBend Professional with a working length of 3200mm and 3mm sheet thickness. The Evolution-series industrial machine will be on display with a fully automatic tool changer. Schröder Group is also presenting two motorised shears in Stuttgart; MHSU 2000 x 2.5 and PDC Power-Duo-Cut NC 3200 x 3.0. The ASK 3 manual folder with segmented tools on all beams will also be shown on its booth as well as its FASTI type 417 swaging machine and Type 134 rounding machine.

YIH SHEN MACHINERY CO, Ltd. (YSM) now offers its YSM NC-26 strip and wire forming machine. This servo automatic forming machine has 11 axes (with a capacity of up to 16 axes).

"Each forming axis is operated by servo motors and is designed for long production times, replacing the traditional cam-driven style, to increase the efficiency of mould change and be suitable for small-scale production," the manufacturer told ISMR.
