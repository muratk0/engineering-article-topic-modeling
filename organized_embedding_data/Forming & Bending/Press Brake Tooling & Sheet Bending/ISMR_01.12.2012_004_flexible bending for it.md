# Flexible bending for I&T

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

FOUNDED IN 1948 and purchased by the Outwin family in 1965, Industrial & Tractor Ltd is now home to a latest-generation BLM ELECT 40 all-electric CNC tube bender.

The UK-based company specialises in rigid manipulated tubular assemblies and last year dealt with 2023 different part references, of which 1300 were for quantities of less than 100 pieces a year. As Michael Outwin, managing director, points out: “We have a lot of experience in terms of setting up and changing from one part to another, and we focus hard on standardising the production process to reduce the risk of a mistake.”

“The new machine’s precise calibration cycle enables it to be set right-first-time, meaning that a first-off part, and all subsequent parts, will be precisely to specification. Essentially ‘two machines in one’, the ELECT 40 can be switched quickly into a right-hand or left-hand bend configuration – providing a flexible production system and eliminating secondary bending operations,” BLM added.

Since joining the family business full-time in 1972 as sales director and taking over as MD in 1988, Outwin has implemented significant changes to the company’s production methods. “Progress can no longer be judged simply by the number of machines or people involved. In the early ‘70’s we were bending tube by hand whereas now we use CNC bending machines and robot weld the assemblies. Back then, for example, we had around 30 welders and brazers: today we have eight people producing far more and to a very high standard.”

The net result is that ISO 9002/QS 9000-accredited Industrial & Tractor continues to thrive, employing 137 people and with 80,000 sq. ft. under cover, including a purpose-built headquarters building. Turnover is around £9 million, with parts destined for industry sectors such as diesel engine manufacture, earthmoving equipment, transport, automotive, defence and medical, and varying in size from 10mm up to 5.5m long and weighing from a few grams up to 50kg. A third of sales by value go to North and South America, continental Europe, India, China and Japan, reflecting Michael Outwin’s belief that the UK should concentrate on the design and manufacture of high value-added products rather than competing directly in the high volume, low cost global marketplace.

The decision to invest in the BLM ELECT 40, which can bend tube up to 42mm diameter, was taken because of an urgent need to free up capacity on larger 75mm tube benders. In practice, the ELECT 40’s multi-stack tool mounting facility allows multi-radius and variable radius bending of the same tube to take place in a single setup. Bends with very little straight between them, even compound bends, can also be accommodated, along with tight bends that can have a radius less than the tube diameter.
