MC SERIES: PRECISE BENDING

Unlock high-performance bending with Nargesa Roll Benders, engineered for precision and efficiency in shaping pipes and profiles. These robust machines excel across a range of applications, from structural frameworks and automotive components to decorative and artistic metalwork. Featuring multi-directional bending capability and intuitive operation, they deliver superior control and versatility for demanding projects. Each machine comes equipped with universal standard dies, allowing the user to work with a variety of profiles, including square and rectangular tube, square bars, C-channel, T-channel, angle, flat bar, and edged flat bar.

Select MC Benders allow both vertical and horizontal bending, offering greater versatility for a wide range of applications.

The MC Series features bending regulation through the upper shaft, providing added support during operation.

The MC650NC and MCSSO feature intuitive enc controls, simplifying complex bending operations.

Angled correction rollers provide stable material support at both the entry and exit points of the bending process.
