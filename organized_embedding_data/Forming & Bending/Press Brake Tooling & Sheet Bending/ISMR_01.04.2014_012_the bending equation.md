# THE BENDING EQUATION

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

## As bending technologies continue to develop, we highlight a selection of the latest innovations on the market

Today’s machine tool manufacturers are constantly introducing innovative and smart ways of bending sheet metal. In this article, we highlight a selection of new developments available on the market.

Amada has launched a new EG-6013AR fully automatic bending system. The EG-AR series is the latest addition to Amada's range of manual and automated press brakes. Designed specifically for high speed bending of small complex parts, the entire system is designed for unmanned production of any volume size or part mix. The robot is specifically designed for bending complex parts and the system boasts a BiS automatic angle measuring system. Offline programming is done through dedicated CAM software.

“Equipped with the world's first twin-servo electric drive mechanism, the EG-AR offers high speed precision bending, with lower power usage and a compact footprint. Features such as automatic angle measuring, offline programming, multiple part load/unload stations and an automatic tool changer all add to the system’s list of production capabilities,” Amada told ISMR.

Amada demonstrated its new HG press brake at the Southern Manufacturing & Electronics Show 2014 in Farnborough this February. Featuring a patented hybrid drive system and a new ‘Smartphone style’ 3D control unit, the HG is the fastest and most precise press brake within the Amada range. Amada's new Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfold data and feature recognition ready for manufacture.

“The HG machine is the flagship model of the Amada press brake range. Utilising electric servo-driven hydraulics, the patented Hybrid Drive system has a positioning accuracy of 0.001mm, providing users with increased accuracy and repeatability along with reduced running costs using 50% less power and 60% less oil. Precision bending is achieved using an automatic material thickness detection system and a new active lower beam which automatically adjusts during part processing,” Amada explained.

With roots going back to the early 1950s, Turkish manufacturer Baykal is a leading manufacturer and global supplier of sheet metal working machinery specialising in the production of press brakes, shears, punching machines, laser cutting systems, plasma cutting machines and waterjet cutting machines. Its APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. For its manufacturing operations, Baykal uses three factories which together combine a production area of 60,000 square metres, making it one of Europe’s largest facilities for sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.

The Xcite electric press brake was the bending highlight from Swiss producer Bystronic at the EuroBLECH exhibition. Using Bystronic’s proprietary Force Dynamic Drive, it bends to within a tolerance of plus/minus 25 arc minutes and with repeatability of plus/minus 0.0025 millimetres.

“It is the fast and economical way to high-quality bent parts”, the company told ISMR. “Above all, fast. In concrete terms, some 2.5 times faster than a hydraulic press brake. Using the Fast Bend+ safety system, the tool can be positioned very close to the metal sheet without the operator being placed in any danger. Only then does the machine switch over to bending speed.”

Ermaksan, who specialises in the production of sheet metal working machineries, recently won three awards in two different international design competitions for both its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine in their own category. In conventional press brakes, energy is consumed even in standby mode but the Evolution II press brake only uses energy whilst it is bending. It can bend at a rate of 20 mm/sec. using servo motors and a highly efficient pump. It is also quiet – at 63DB noise-less – and has bending accuracy of between +/- 0.01mm. These new generation press brakes are developed to require fewer oil changes (every five years).

“The system offers an average 65% energy saving when used in comparison to standard press brakes. Oil is more durable than ever because the oil does not heat up thanks to the pumping system which does not run when the machine is idle. For ease of maintenance, the Y1 and Y2 axes have stand-alone oil tanks. The amount of oil used in the machine has been reduced by 95% in comparison to standard hydraulic press brakes of the same spec. This again helps to reduce manufacturing costs,” Ermaksan told ISMR.

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the SPEED-BEND (synchronized hydraulic press brake), the POWER-BEND PRO 3-axis CNC press brake and the ECO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake. A 4V bottom die on the ECO-BEND allows users to make variety of bends on different material thicknesses. A movable control unit allows the operator to choose his working position.

The company’s MICRO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake is a small, productive hydraulic press brake with the capacity to bend 5mm mild steel with 1 m X 40 tonnes of press force and one-axis back gauge. Ermaksan’s HEAVY DUTY press brake features a dynamic crowning system and 6-axis servo motor back gauge system. It also boasts special top and bottom tools and a 610mm throat depth.

Italian manufacturer Gasparini offers a press brake range that includes the X-BEND (press force from 50 to 170 tons) and the X-PRESS ECO. The X-PRESS is available in a wide range of models and powers, with tonnage that varies between 25 and 1500 tons and lengths of between 1250 and 16000mm. The company offers stand-alone, tandem and tridem configurations, with a wide range of accessories (back gauges, front supports, clamping systems, sheet followers, controls etc.) and a tailor-made approach.

Gasparini’s new Dener hydraulic press brakes were designed to meet a wide variety of needs, accommodating weights ranging from 40 to 2,000 tons and offering from 3- to more than 11-axis capabilities. Its new electric MC press brake is leaner, greener and more reliable.

The Diamond BB Series features a compact, ball screw design driven by an AC servo motor with 40 metric tons of force and one-micron repeatability. It utilises a dual-drive system with a high-speed, high-accuracy back gauge, larger stroke and hydraulic power.

HACO-ATLANTIC, Inc. is one of the world’s leading manufacturers of conventional hydraulic and CNC servo-driven press brakes. In addition to line of high-level CNC controls, it now focuses on 2D and 3D controllers for its press brake line. The new sturdy, heavy-duty designed X-R axis has an accuracy of 0.004”. Ram repeatability is 0.0004” and is designed for very accurate air bending.
