# Rapid slitting

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

AT THE ALUMINIUM 2018 show in Düsseldorf, GEORG debuted its new vacuum braking roll for slitting lines. The new roll was developed specifically for strip that needs to meet exacting surface quality requirements. The new energy-saving vacuum roll is designed to enable high strip speeds and improve recoiling of difficult-to-process strip.

"In slitting lines, braking units arranged ahead of the recoiler are often fitted with rollers, felt pads or belts which are pressed against the strip from below and above. However, especially when slitting strip for applications that demand high-quality surfaces, these methods risk leaving marks on the ‘good side’ of the strip. Moreover, the felt pads are subject to intensive wear and need to be replaced at short intervals. All this impairs the productivity of the line," explained GEORG.

At the trade show, GEORG presented its advanced vacuum braking unit, which achieves the braking effect by sucking the lower side of the strip against the braking roll. The upper strip side remains completely untouched, ruling out any risk of damage by mechanical components.

A slitting line with the new technology, which GEORG is currently manufacturing for a customer, has been designed for up to 2,150mm wide strip.

"One of the innovative features of the unit is the ability to set the angle of aperture around the circumference of the roll within wide ranges. Depending upon the wrap angle, the aperture may cover between 60 and 180 degrees of the roll circumference, allowing the strip tension to be flexibly adjusted to the specifics of the strip processed. Together with the high-precision regulation of the vacuum pressure, the roll can achieve optimum recoiling with varying strip types and under varying conditions," added GEORG.

Only those sectors of the roll length covered by strip will be activated. In this way, GEORG delivers much lower levels of energy consumption. It has developed the vacuum system specifically for strip thicknesses of between 0.08 and 0.8mm. It is suitable, said GEORG, for all customary widths of cold rolled aluminium strip. GEORG is currently manufacturing a slitting line with the new technology for an aluminium producer in Asia. The line will be designed for up to 2,150mm wide strip. See www.georg.com
