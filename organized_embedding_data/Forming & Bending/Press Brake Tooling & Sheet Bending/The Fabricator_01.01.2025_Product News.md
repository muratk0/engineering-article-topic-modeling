Slat cleaning tool removes slag in two-step process

TRUMPF has developed the TruTool TSC 200 slat cleaning tool that removes slag in a two-step roughing and finishing process. The tool is equipped with serrated cutting edges that break up the slag and continuous cutting edges that scrape it off. This process runs continuously, allowing cleaning to proceed at 13 to 32 FPM.

The cleaning tool is available in two versions, one for slat thicknesses of 0.080 to 0.120 in. and another for cleaning 0.120- to 0.150-in.-thick slats. Wear markings, designed as notches in two locations, function like the indicators in tire treads. If they are no longer visible, the tool is worn out and must be rotated 180 degrees.

Automated bending cell adapts to user requirements

Bystronic has enhanced the Xpert Pro press brake to create a fully automatic bending cell designed to offer flexibility to both small and large manufacturing companies.

The modular design allows the cell to be adapted to the user’s requirements. It is suitable both for processing long job lists and for changing orders, from small batch sizes through to large series. In combination with the press brake, the robot processes bending jobs fully automatically and without errors, the company reports.

The 7-axis robot autonomously changes the grippers and bending tools, and the intelligent software determines the optimal sequence of the bending tools for each job list.

Laser cutting machine offered in three modular designs

LVD Co. has introduced the Laser-Two modular-design laser cutting machine. Featuring a sheet size format of 3,050 by 1,525 mm, the system is offered with a 3-, 6-, or 12-kW laser source. Three configurations are available:

• A model with local covers and light guards includes a cutting zone cover that can be opened for maintenance, check-ups, part evacuation.

• A model with full covers features an integrated shuttle table and offers optional integration with the LA Load Automation system.

Fiber laser welding system includes patented rotating lens

AMADA offers the Fiber Laser Welder (FLW) ENSISe welding system. NC Beam Focus adjusts the lens to an optimum setting based on the welding conditions. The patented rotating lens creates beam weaving effect to help produce fast, accurate, and smooth results, the company reports.

With the rotating lens, the laser beam is no longer aligned with a specific point along the target path; instead, it circles within a small area as it is welding. The rotating lens makes it possible to bridge larger and uneven gap sizes while producing a clean, even weld. Optimized heat input into the material helps create precisely defined weld edges with virtually no unnecessary deposits of material.

Software update helps improve connectivity while offering real-time insights

Lantek has released the Lantek v44 update with new features across its CAD/CAM, MES, and ERP software.

Expert v44 to help optimize CAD/CAM 2D workflows features properties-based nesting, which allows users to group parts by attributes like delivery date, customer information, or other data. In Flex3D v44 for tube and profile cutting, the part import process and job management/saving have been reengineered, and new split parts techniques allows users to choose from straight, angle, or shape z splits.

MES v44 features intralogistics to help optimize the flow of raw materials from the warehouse to work centers. Integra v44 introduced a files drag-and-drop feature that lets users add geometry files or documents directly into quotes, sale orders, or tracking numbers.
