Adaptive bending system handles large profiles

LVD Strippit has introduced Synchro-Form adaptive bending technology, an integrated feature of the Synchro-Form series press brake. The technology automatically maintains angular consistency and the required geometric profile when handling, positioning, and bending large parts with multiple bends. The system overcomes the problems of accumulative error and trial-and-error bending when forming large profiles, states the company.

The system uses a laser scanner and synchro modules (X-, R-, and A-axis magnets) to manipulate, position, and measure each bend, relaying the digital information to the Touch-B control, which makes adjustments to part and ram position to achieve the correct profile. Variations are not accumulated, but instead compensated with each bend step to maintain accuracy even after multiple, consecutive bends.

Multitool holds four punching tools, four embossing tools

As OEM supplier of Prima Power, Pass Stanztechnik AG has designed and produced a new multitool to help increase the efficiency and capacity of the Prima Power Genius punching machine’s turret table.

The rotatable ram head on the punching machine comprises four punching tools to create a flexible-use multitool. The ps:multitool-MT4B

®

enables the use of four punching tools (Station B) as well as four embossing tools on one toolstation in the turret. Maximum diameter is 1.25 in. Tools for engraving, embossing, and countersinking are integrated into the tool and do not need additional turret places. Tool selection functions in combination with the rotatable ram head and the index station. The index station enables the rotation of the tools, which helps speed up tool selection and reduce production time.

Embossing tools are arranged between the punching tools. The embossing ram is placed 0.16 in. higher than the tool head to enable activation of the tool from the top.

Press brake delivers high-tonnage bending with automatic tool change

Amada offers the HG 2204 ATC press brake for variable lot sizes. The company’s patented Automatic Tool Changer (ATC) loads complex tool layouts within 3 min., allowing operators of varied experience levels to use the bending system.

This larger model incorporates all of the standard features of the HG 1003 ATC while delivering 240 tons of pressure, an extended 13-ft. bend length, and an increased 112.8-ft. ATC tool capacity.

A factory-installed sheet follower helps the operator bend large, heavy parts. Parts that typically require multiple operators can be handled by a single person. When not in use, the arms of the sheet follower slide to the right side of the press brake to allow for full machine accessibility.

Automated bending cell bends small parts at 4 sec. per bend

TRUMPF Inc. has released the TruBend Cell 7000, which the company says is the world’s fastest system for the automated bending of small parts at an average cycle time of 4 sec. per bend.

The cell comprises a TruBend 7036 Cell Edition press brake, the BendMaster bending robot, the LoadMaster Bend material handling unit, a pallet system, and tracks, all within an enclosure that requires 69 sq. ft. of floor space.

The press brake offers a press force of 40 tons and high-speed acceleration. The ram is divided into two 20-in. sections to provide clearance for the grippers on the bending robot. The material handling unit’s multivacuum gripper prepares the bending part parallel to the current bending operation. A highspeed vision system identifies the exact orientation of the blanks. The flexible loading arrangement allows for up to 24 different blanks or 4,800 parts to be delivered to the machine.

FMC allows for fully automated part production

Salvagnini offers the automatic FlexCell flexible manufacturing cell. It comprises an L5 laser cutting system, P2lean panel bender, and B3 ATA press brake, in line with the digital factory model. With all the necessary automated devices, the machines can set themselves up automatically, ready for processing, detection of variations, and real-time correction of those variations.

OPS-FlexCell integration software monitors and controls the entire process. The user can choose which product to produce, and the software, handling feedback from the laser on the positioning of parts required for a given kit, transmits the information to the downline machines, which set themselves autonomously. The system knows how the parts have been stacked and automatically decides the work sequence based on machine optimization or how the parts have been set on the stack.
