# E-volution in bending

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

## At EuroBLECH 2022 (25-28 October 2022, Hanover, Germany), Dutch sheetmetal bending specialist, SafanDarley will demonstrate the E-Brake Mini Cell and R-Brake in a separate area, dubbed the 'production environment of the future'.

“In this production environment, the E-Brake Mini Cell and the R-Brake will be supplied with material using Kumatech's new Pallet Jack. This AGV (automated ground vehicle) will also remove pallets loaded with completed products. The bending process on the R-Brake and the Mini Cell is 100% automated," it explained.

With the R-Brake, the press brake and robot are integrated into one modular system. The automatic tool and gripper change are linked to the machine and, with the product, are programmed offline using SafanDarley RoboBend offline simulation software. The robot can be traversed horizontally along a gantry of six metres above the press brake, leaving the floor in front of the machine free for supply and unloading stations. This saves a lot of space.

For the automatic production of small complex products, SafanDarley offers the E-Brake Mini Cell. The Mini Cell has a modular structure and can be fully configured according to customer requirements.

“At EuroBLECH, we are exhibiting a very sophisticated Mini Cell, equipped with a discharge belt for the scratch-free disposal of products outside the cell. The Mini Cell also has a pallet management system for the automatic supply and removal of pallets," added SafanDarley.

The manufacturer is also showing the E-Brake C 200T with Tool Mate, an external automatic and intelligent tool changer.

“This combination offers significant time savings for users producing small batches. The Tool Mate is very intuitive and does not require extensive training. The SafanDarley E-Control manages the tool change, ensuring that the tools are placed exactly in the right position. The intelligent software makes the necessary changes and tools from the previous tool set-up can be reused. This means considerably shorter changeover times," it told ISMR.

The latest model in the hybrid range, the H-Brake Hybrid+ 230T Ultra, will also be on display.

“At its core is an advanced dual hybrid drive with high return speed, increased productivity and low energy consumption. This reduces costs, with one of the main advantages of this system being a dramatic reduction in energy consumption," explained the Dutch sheetmetal bending specialist.

SafanDarley will also be showing machines from its standard product range, such as the H-Brake Hybrid 110-1600 and the E-Brake 130T Ultra equipped with an intelligent bending aid, the I-Mate.

Visit SafanDarley at EuroBLECH in Hanover on stand H14 in Hall 14 to view its intelligent lights-out factory solutions.
