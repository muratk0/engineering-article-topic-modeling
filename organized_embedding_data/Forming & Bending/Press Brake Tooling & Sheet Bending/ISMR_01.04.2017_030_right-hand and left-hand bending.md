# Right-hand and left-hand bending

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

E-TURN IS THE BLM GROUP family of all-electric tube bending systems for making complex shaped parts. The family of machines comes in four models, for tubes of up to 30-, 35-, 40- and 52 millimetres in diameter and in-process right-hand and left-hand bending. The systems are all-electric with fixed and variable multi-radius capabilities and integrated loading and unloading systems.

The E-TURN features 15 "all-electric" axes; integration to automatic loading and unloading systems; the VGP3D programming system and the BTools and BRight functions to 'get it right from the first part'. Each of the two bend heads for in-process right-hand and left-hand operation can mount up to four tools for creating various fixed and variable bending radii. In many cases, this means that a variety of jobs can be performed without the need to change the tools between each one.

"The bend head design is capable of applying up to 2000kg of pressure, making it possible to bend complex radii of less than 1D, on tubes ranging from 6- to 52 millimetres (0.250"- 2.0") in diameter. The ability to modulate the torque during bending, depending upon the clamping force, means creating unblemished parts with perfect quality," said the company.

"All machine movements, including the clamps, are controlled via CNC with electric axes to guarantee accuracy and repeatability. The tools are calibrated automatically using pre-stored parameters. This drastically saves set-up and job changeover times," it added.

The E-TURN's electrical consumption, said the company, is less than one tenth of a conventional hydraulic bender. It only draws electricity when the axes are actually in use. The elimination of the hydraulic system means that there is no need to dispose of waste oil and quiet operation is possible. See www.blmgroup.it
