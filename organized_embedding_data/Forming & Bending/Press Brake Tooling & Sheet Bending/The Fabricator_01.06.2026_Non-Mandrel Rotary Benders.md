CC SERIES: ZERO HASSLE BENDING

Forget the complex setups and expensive tooling of mandrel bending. The Nargesa CC Series utilizes an advanced Counter-Shape Die System to support the tube externally during the bend. This innovation delivers wrinkle-free, high-quality bends on materials ranging from thin-wall stainless steel to heavy schedule pipe, all with faster cycle times and simplified tooling changes.

ROBUSTNESS AND SPEED

Engineered for heavy-duty use, its sturdy construction ensures long-lasting reliability, while advanced design features enable rapid bending operations without sacrificing precision or efficiency.

CONTROL PEDAL

This feature provides precise control of the bending process while offering an immediate stopping mechanism to ensure operator safety and prevent accidents during operation.

TOOL POST

Supports a variety of bending dies and enables quick tool changes, enhancing both flexibility and efficiency during metal fabrication tasks.

OFFSET HEAD

The unique offset head design allows the machine to bend complex geometries and 11S11 shapes that would collide with the frame of a standard bender.

AUTOMATIC UNLOCKING

The CNC automatically calculates the material’s elasticity and over-bends slightly to ensure the final angle is perfect every time.
