# Roll bending innovations

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

AMB PICOT has been designing and manufacturing roll bending machines for more than 150 years and is constantly expanding its range to create value for its customers, whatever their technical constraints and sector of activity i.e. aerospace, transport, public works, industry architecture etc. The company's portfolio includes a wide range of plate roll bending, as well as roll-profile bending and special bending, machines.

Gérard Crozier, Technical Director, AMB Picot, explained: "Our portfolio includes two-, three- or four-roll bending, developed along the key principles of reliability, quality and ergonomics. Since the 1980s, the fourroll bending machines have become the standard for roll bending. Our standard at AMB Picot was the four-roll bending machine with 100% hydraulics, which offers a range of benefits."

The French roll forming specialist highlighted the following advantages of its technology:

- Double pre-bending plate bending machine.
- Four-roll-driven and synchronised rolls.
- Automatic and hydraulic synchronization of roll parallelism.
- Progressive speed.
- No wear components.
- Equipped with CNC.

"Pre-bending was easier but limited in its capacities," continued Gérard Crozier. "At AMB Picot, we listen to our customers and strive to improve so that we can meet their specific needs. This was why we launched our three-roll bending machine with 100% hydraulics and a range of further benefits.

- Perfect pre-bent edges.

No lamination.

The ability to roll any metal/any thickness/ any form (including Aluminium composite).

Ease of use with our new patented easyroll software.

## Intelligent software

Easyroll is intelligent rolling software based on springback prediction and material simulation, designed to reach the expected rolling diameter first time, with no corrections needed. AMB Picot launched its new easyroll 2.0 software at EuroBLECH in Hanover in October 2018. It is Artificial Intelligence software for roll bending machines.

"We use simulation, combined with algorithms, to predict and minimise deformation on a sheet. Each sheet is different, so the software needs to adapt. We continuously update the algorithms and offer updates every three months to customers. Incorporating a smart material database, it predicts material behaviour, according to material characteristics (e.g. thickness, springback, mechanical characteristics etc.) and continuously learns from experience. It is as efficient for bending special parts (in manual mode) as it is for large batches (assisted programming) and keeps customers on track for higher productivity," Grégory Conraud, managing director of AMB Picot, told ISMR.

"Innovation is in our DNA and simplicity is key. Our aim is to improve our technology to simplify our customers' business," added Etienne Camus, AMB Picot's development engineer. "In the last version of easyroll, we updated the 'rolling' algorithms to simplify the use of the numerical command; employ high elastic limit materials (Hardox); use composite materials such as 'alucobond'; increase productivity by more autonomous management of the laser scanning area and offer automatic restart of programs when leaving the zone."

AMB Picot is currently developing different versions of easyroll technology to suit its customers' environments e.g. for high-tensile strength materials or sandwich material (aluminium). It is able to fine-tune its technology to suit its customers' usage requirements.■
