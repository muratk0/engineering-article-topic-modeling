## **Hydraulic System Operation and Safety Guidelines for Bending Machines**
The clockwise rotation of the motor drives the axial piston pump to pass the filtered oil through the pipeline into the valve plate and the solenoid valve to return to the mailbox.

The No. 13 hydraulic control check valve closes the lower chamber of the No. 15 cylinder to make the slider stay at any position. At this time, the solenoid valves do not work, and the oil output from the oil pump returns to the mailbox.

When the No. 11 D4 electromagnet works, the slider descends rapidly, and the oil in the lower chamber of the No. 15 cylinder is returned to the mailbox through the No. 17 and No. 15 throttle valves, and the upper chamber of the cylinder is filled with oil.

When the slider hits the limit switch, the No. 5 D2 and No. 12 D3, No. 14 D5 solenoid valves work.

The No. 16 flush valve and the No. 17 cone valve are closed, and the oil output from the oil pump enters the cylinder, and the slider enters the working speed.

The No. 6 high-pressure relief valve is to ensure the rated pressure of the machine, and the 18th remote pressure regulating valve is adjusted to obtain the allowable pressure.

The working time of the No. 5 D2, No. 11 D3 and No. 14 D5 electromagnets is controlled by the foot switch, and the jog distance when the slider is lowered is realized, and the speed at which the slider speed is lowered is adjusted by the No. 9 throttle valve.

After stopping the slider lowering action, move the foot to step on the foot switch.

At the moment of the return trip, the No. 14 D5 electromagnet was first to reset for 2 seconds and then relieved.

Then D1, No. 5 D2/14 D5 electromagnet works on the 10th, and the slider returns. The return speed is constant.

The working pressure of the return stroke is 120kg/cm2, which is adjusted by the No. 10 relief valve.
