# THE BENDING EQUATION

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

We highlight a selection of the latest new innovations in panel bending, folder, press brake and bending tool technologies.

ISMR SAYS:

“Intelligent press brake tooling, eco-hybrid drive systems, automatic angle measurement and energy-saving systems are just some of the bending innovations under the spotlight in this article.”

Many of the latest bending innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending.

Featuring a range of innovations from ecohybrid drive systems to automatic angle measurement, quicker tool change/set-up and more sophisticated sensing technologies, today’s press brakes can also handle smaller batches and, by integrating robotic and automation technologies, deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations.

In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake, folder and panel bending technology developments in the global market.

## The latest innovations

Japanese manufacturer Amada’s comprehensive range of press brakes is designed for flexibility, productivity and energy-saving (inverter-controlled stop/start technology). Its HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new ‘Smartphone style’ 3D control unit. New Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture.

Turkish manufacturer Baykal’s APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. It has pioneered development of a fully automatic pole cutting and bending machine and boasts one of Europe’s largest facilities for sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.

Swiss manufacturer Bystronic has enjoyed success with its Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre press brake with rapid bending speeds. It has been designed as a ‘plug and play’ system – the machine can be moved by a fork lift truck, partly for offloading and installation or to suit changes in a factory layout. This small press brake has already won a German design award and is designed for rapid, efficient parts production.

At Blechexpo in November 2015, Bystronic also showcased a new automatic tool changer for a standard Xpert press brake (18m of bottom tooling and 9m of upper tooling stored at the back of the press brake) to free press brake operators for other tasks and eliminate errors. It also profiled its Xact Smart press brake at Blechexpo, available in several versions with press capacities of between 100 and 225 tons and with up to four axes on the back gauge.

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. In 2014, it won two awards in the industrial category of the GoodDesign awards for its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine. In 2015, the family-run company celebrated 50 years in operation.

“In conventional press brakes, energy is consumed even in standby mode but the Evolution II press brake only uses energy whilst it is bending. It can bend at a rate of 20mm/sec. using servo motors and a highly efficient pump. It is also quiet (at 63dB noise-less) and has high bending accuracy of between +/-0.01mm. These new generation press brakes are developed to require fewer oil changes (every five years),” Ermaksan explained.

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the SPEED-BEND, the POWER-BEND PRO, the ECO-BEND, the MICRO-BEND and the HEAVY DUTY press brake (featuring a dynamic crowning system, 6-axis servo motor back gauge system and special top and bottom tools, with a 610mm throat depth).

“Customers want flexibility and less downtime – there is an increasing need for automation. For press brakes, the trend for higher durability is increasing,” Ersin Yildiz, Ermaksan’s sales manager, told ISMR.

Italian specialist Gasparini offers a press brake range that includes the X-BEND (press force from 50 to 170 tons) and the X-PRESS ECO. Its new Dener hydraulic press brakes were designed to meet a wide variety of needs, accommodating weights ranging from 40 to 2000 tons and offering from 3- to more than 11-axis capabilities. Its new electric MC press brake is leaner and greener, delivering quiet operation and faster cycle times. The company has also developed its GPS4 angle measurement system for press brakes. Complex shapes, with Z-bends or with narrow flanges, can therefore be bent and set-up times reduced.

Above: Bystronic’s Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre press brake

The Jean Perrot Industries’ Maneo Premium LCS press brake features a new laser control system and won over the jury committee for the 2015 Tolexpo innovation award. Launched in January 2015, the press brake is equipped with a high speed bend angle and correction system.

“This modular and hydraulic press brake is designed for precise and repetitive bending. It is suitable for complex component, large quantity batch-work where speed and precision are vital to ensure productivity. It features electronic synchronisation (Y1, Y2) and an approach speed of up to 200mm/second. Tonnage capacities for this press brake range from 75 tonnes and 2.5m to 1600 tonnes and 11m,” commented Erick Rousseau, Jean Perrot’s line product manager.

LVD Company nv has announced a new generation of its PPEC series hydraulic press brakes. PPEC models of up to 350 tons now feature a new modern and ergonomic design with integrated status lighting. In addition, the touch control for all PPEC machines, ranging from 35 to 640 tons, has been upgraded to LVD’s latest graphical icon-driven TOUCH-B controller.

“The new design, with built-in status lighting, gives the PPEC an elegant appearance in line with the Easy-Form® press brakes. The status LED lights indicate the machine’s operational status, enabling more effective shop management for higher throughput. An optional LED lighting system illuminates the back gauge and front work zone areas to provide better operator visibility,” LVD told ISMR.

The next generation of LVD’s Easy-Form® and PPEB Series of high performance press brakes feature a new, modern design that is more energy-efficient, easier to use and incorporates added safety features.

The ToolCell, LVD’s press brake with an integrated tool-changing system, significantly reduces press brake set-up time. LVD is now offering a 220-ton, 3-metre model with a large selection of top and bottom tooling stored in a tooling stadium/warehouse under the back gauge. An innovative gripper design, built into the machine’s back gauge finger, serves as the tool-changer mechanism. When a job is complete, the press brake automatically changes the top and bottom tooling as required for the next job. All tool stations are precisely positioned, eliminating the need for manual adjustments.

LVD has also expanded its Dyna-Press Series of compact, portable, high speed electric press brakes with two 24-ton models that offer higher capacity and a longer working length. Compact and portable with a footprint of 1140 x 1760mm, the new Dyna-Press models save on valuable floor space and can still be conveniently relocated using a standard forklift.

Mate Precision Tooling showcased various press brake tooling solutions at the FABTECH 2015 exhibition in Chicago in November 2015. These included CleanBend™ forming technology and “Bevel Tang Style” press brake tooling.

“The new ‘Bevel Tang Style’ press brake tooling is for use in newer Amada style press brakes. Enabling faster and safer tool changes, the press brake tooling features a punch tang geometry that is compatible with punch clamping systems requiring a bevelled receiving surface,” the manufacturer explained.

Mate’s press brake tooling also includes a full array of European Precision and WILA TRUMPF-style punches, dies, standard specials, specials and accessories, available in segmented, full-length and half-length sizes.

Italian manufacturer, Prima Power, launched its eP2040 200-ton electric press brake at Blechexpo in November 2015. It is a rapid, non-hydraulic bending solution with a new servo-electric drive system and CNC crowing system.

“This new press brake features crowning on the lower beam and a bending angle control system (laser measurement system) that we developed ourselves. It is a rare example of an electric press brake that can cover higher tonnage,” Claudio Banchi, Prima Power’s Executive VP of Sales, told ISMR.

“We are also promoting an upgrade on our electric Fastbend panel bender. It has been re-designed and features a new system that can control the C-frame from both sides and is more accurate, which is better for bending delicate parts (3.2mm max. thickness),” he added.

PunchBend is Prima Power’s compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials, which processes pre-cut material automatically into ready-bent components.

Rolla-V is celebrating the release of its latest press brake tooling catalogue. With over 80 years of experience, it supplies a comprehensive range of press brake tools as well as the patented Rolla-V.

One of the new products on display at SafanDarley’s booth at Blechexpo in Stuttgart (November 2015) was the E-Brake 40 Ton 1600 Ergonomic, with a longer working length of 1600mm. The E-Brake 150 Ton 3100 was also showcased on the booth.

The E-Brake Ergonomic 40 Tons has an open height of 590mm standard and a stroke of 300mm standard. It is designed for bending small parts. Rapid acceleration and deceleration of the electrical servo-driven ram delivers approach speeds of up to 180mm/sec. The machine has a very compact footprint. The company also showcased an upgraded version of its laser angle measurement device with a new blue light as well as the latest developments in its Autopol offline software.

Italian manufacturer Salvagnini’s B3 ATA press brake employs an automatic bending tool set-up to make the bending process more efficient.

“The B3 is the only solution of its kind on the market and is the product of proprietary expertise in panel bending and press brake bending, combining the best of both worlds. This press brake combines the power of a hydraulic press brake with the responsiveness and accuracy of an electric press brake, whilst maintaining the consumption levels associated with a hybrid system,” it told ISMR.

Other Salvagnini bending solutions comprise panel benders such as the P4, P2 and P1 automatic panel benders in a range of 14 models and the FMS S4+P4 automatic panel production line. Salvagnini’s new P4lean-2520 is an energyefficient machine with a wide-ranging field of application. The minimum panel size that can be produced with this panel bender has been reduced by 10, dropping from 400 to 370mm. The P4lean-2520’s new geometrical dimensions help to boost machine performance, for the same overall system layout, by an average 15% – the maximum diagonal has also increased from 2,800mm to 3,200mm to allow feeding of panels of up to 3,050mm wide.

Salvagnini has also launched SaM, its new proprietary app which provides real-time information on Salvagnini machine status on smartphone, tablet or PC using remote monitoring.

Schiavi arrived at Blechexpo in Stuttgart last November with a real innovation – its new press brake, the LineAr (model 130.30 with 3m length). This press brake model features the patented Schiavi “Crowning Free System” which guarantees a constant angle along the bend and horizontal edge along the line of bend (for a perfectly straight bend). LineAr has three beams for the lower table and three for the upper and the bending force is distributed on the external beams so that the tool-holding beams remain straight during the bending process.

Another highlight of its booth presence was its new CNC control, Atlas, integrated with the Moses system, which project all the information that the operator needs directly onto the frame of the press brake. This system is touch-screen so that the user can work directly work on the press brake frame instead of walking backwards and forwards to the CNC next to the press brake. Schiavi offers the press brakes in three sizes.

“The LineAr 130.30 is also equipped with an inverter that speeds up the bending cycle. A specially designed hydraulic system, with Start & Stop function, allows LineAr 130.30 to significantly improve efficiency by using maximum bending force only when in contact with the material, thus saving energy costs for the customer. The CNC control can also set a time after which the press brake will stop consuming electricity, whilst keeping all available data in memory,” Niccolo Zinetti, the company’s marketing and communications manager, told ISMR.

Schiavi has also patented and produced an electric-pneumatic angle measurement system to reduce bending times and obstructions, cut out springback issues and lower operational costs.

Turkish machine dealer ERFAB presented its broad portfolio of Schröder folding machines with a PowerBend Professional (working length 3200mm, sheet thickness 3mm steel sheet) at the WIN EURASIA Metalworking show in Istanbul recently. ERFAB showcased a PowerBend Professional with an Up-and-Down-folding beam which is able to move around the workpiece, bending from below and above. For counter folds, the sheet therefore does not need to be turned over.

Swiss manufacturer, THALMANN Maschinenbau AG, presented a range of folding innovations at last November’s Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, including the launch of its newly upgraded double folder TD Professional Plus series for roofing, facades and industrial applications.

“This upgraded model offers a 10% speed gain and new folding tool geometry which allows us to bend shapes or parts that I believe no-one else can do right now,” explained its CEO, Marco Cappello. “The clamp beam tool has the flattest angle in market so it can form parts with proportion 3:4 (e.g. 5cm high x 7cm deep). We have also introduced a loading table for the machine that pops out automatically. This loading aid will simplify the material feed significantly. The device can handle parts of full machine length (3-12m) and thicknesses of up to 3mm mild steel. There is also new material handling functionality that can grab.”

An array of additional features makes TRUMPF’s TruBend Series 5000 more productive and user-friendly. Using a new control concept, the machines are easy to operate. Two innovative angle measurement systems are also offered. The operator is assisted by a range of ergonomic components such as the MagicShoe, a work shoe fitted with smart sensors. This makes it easy for the operator to move around and initiate the stroke wherever he/she is standing at the machine.

“These high-productivity, all-purpose TRUMPF machines come equipped with a new On-Demand Servo Drive that features 4-cylinder drive technology. It consumes energy only during the bending process,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

“The new Touchpoint TruBend control is revolutionary in its simplicity and intuitive to use. A realistic 3D visualisation incorporating collision monitoring is also available to assist the operator. The machine can be operated without a mouse, using a 21.5-inch monitor which, like a tablet, is equipped with the multitouch function and can even be used wearing work gloves. Operators are free to swivel the monitor into whatever position is convenient for them, and have quick access to direct help whenever they need it,” added TRUMPF.

To cut down on walking distances and increase productivity, operators can also use an additional mobile control unit, the new MobileControl Pro. Operators can position the unit anywhere along the press beam so that it is always within reach and saves them numerous trips to the fixed control panel. TCB (Thickness Controlled Bending) and ACB (Automatically Controlled Bending) angle measurement systems also increase bending accuracy.

With its new TruBend Series 8000, TRUMPF is offering press brakes to handle large-format and thick components. The latest addition to the TruBend 3000 range is the TruBend 3066 with a press force of 600kN and 2m bending length.

Two of WILA's latest innovations, demonstrated at Blechexpo, are TIPS (Tooling Identification and Positioning System) and its New Standard pneumatic tool holders. TIPS is an integrated part of the press brake control system and supports tooling identification and continuous monitoring of tooling position.

“Errors and test bends can be avoided, resulting in a substantial increase in productivity,” said WILA. “The New Standard pneumatic tool holders are based upon a unique, patented, self-locking clamping mechanism. Tools are clamped using air pressure. The main advantage of this approach is that tools can be clamped and unclamped extremely rapidly. This system further increases the speed of tool changes and results in significant increases in productivity.”

Wilson Tool International recently introduced the company’s new, complete line of press brake tooling called LVD Style. At Blechexpo in November 2015, the company also showcased its V-Series Black press brake tooling.

“The tool produces no distortion or marking on sheet metal. The press brake dies incorporate replaceable inserts that decrease friction and enable better performance. Unlike traditional V-dies that can warp and distort material, V-Series Black dies produce clean, crisp bends with no distortion, making them the best solution to produce small flanges, small bend radii and bends close to holes,” Klaus Neumann, Wilson Tool’s sales director for Europe, told ISMR.
