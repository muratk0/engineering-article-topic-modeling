# Durmazlar Makina San. ve. Tic. A.S. (DURMA)

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand G48

www.durmazlar.com.tr/en

Durmazlar (DURMA) is a global manufacturer of sheet metal processing machinery, established in 1956, with headquarters in Turkey. Its wide product portfolio includes lasercutting systems; press-brakes; punch presses; bending centres; Smart Line solutions; plasma cutting machines; guillotine shears; platerollers and profile bending machines. The company also offers special sheet metal processing solutions, autonomous and robotic technology-based smart factory configurations and various Industry-4.0 applications.

"We offer robotic bending solutions for press brakes with precision, speed and repeatability. A robotic press brake cell is a fully automated system designed to bend sheet metal products without requiring an operator. The robot uses a specially designed gripper to pick up the sheet metal from the pallet and then processes it all the way to the final product, similar to the way an operator would, but more efficiently and quickly," said DURMA.
