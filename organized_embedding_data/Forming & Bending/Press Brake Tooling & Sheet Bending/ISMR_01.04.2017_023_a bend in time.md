# A BEND IN TIME

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

Many of the latest bending innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and faster, accurate bending.

ISMR SAYS:

“Intelligent press brake tooling, ecohybrid drive systems, the move towards Industry 4.0, automatic angle measurement and energy-saving systems are just some of the bending innovations under the spotlight in this article.”

With touchscreen control and eco hybrid drive systems to automatic angle measurement, quicker tool change/set-up and more sophisticated sensing technologies, today's press brakes can also handle smaller batches and, by integrating robotic and automation technologies, deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations.

Safety, environmental factors (including energy efficiency and noise reduction), increased accuracy and ease of operation requirements, as well as the move towards Industry 4.0, have also affected press brake design. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake, folder and panel bending technology developments in the global market.

## Bending innovations

Portuguese manufacturer, ADIRA, has developed the HD Greenbender, what it dubs the first "dematerialised" heavy duty press brake in the world, designed with a topology optimisation technique.

"The patented multi-frame concept involves a very deep throat and avoids the need for pit installation in long machines, for a fraction of the steel needed when compared to conventional press brakes," the manufacturer told ISMR.

BB3515, the latest member of ADIRA's electric press brake Bluebender family, has a bending force of 35 tons (1.5 metre length) and features rapid cycle times with very low energy consumption.

Japanese manufacturer AMADA demonstrated a range of innovative solutions at its booth at EuroBLECH in October 2016. Fully automated bending solutions and fibre laser technology, welding machines, press brakes and new VPSS 3i software, which can simulate all processing operations at once, were on display. Its "V-factory" was introduced as a preview to AMADA's new Internet of Things (IoT) manufacturing. Visitors were shown live demonstrations of the VPSS 3i engineering system, AMADA's IoT solution covering the entire range of sheet metal processing.

The company's comprehensive range of press brakes is designed for flexibility, productivity and energy-saving (inverter-controlled stop/start technology). Its HG pressbrake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new 'Smartphone-style' 3D control unit. New Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture.

Its HFE 3i, HG ATC and HG ARs press brake series encompass most bending scenarios from a manually-based scenario to a fully automated, robot-supported production cell. With the HG ATC series with automatic tool-changer, complex parts in batch sizes starting at quantities of one can be produced whereas with the HG ARs even large batches of highly complex parts (in unmanned operation) can be produced.

AMADA's FAST (Finger with Active Security Technology) new stop fingers can be fitted onto most new AMADA bending machines and have an active safety device built in that limits risk to operators if they come into contact with moving parts, even at full speed. This safety feature and "productivity provider" was introduced at EuroBLECH 2016 on AMADA's HFE 3i 1003 machine equipped with the new generation of AFH tools and connected to the VPSS 3i BEND software.

ASco GmbH provides technical solutions from innovative long folding machines up to fully automated bending centres. Its Variobend long length folding machines and up and down bending machines are available (from 4-12 metres) to process steel of up to 2mm.

Turkish manufacturer Baykal’s APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. It has pioneered development of a fully automatic pole cutting and bending machine and boasts one of Europe's largest facilities for sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.

Boschert GmbH produces a dynamic hybriddrive press brake, the Quick-Bend, with purported energy savings of up to 80%. Quick

and nearly silent, the Quick-Bend only uses electrical energy during the bending process. Based on the PROFI 28 CNC and PROFI 56 CNC press brake series, with bending lengths ranging from 1000 - 2200mm, the designers developed a press brake with an innovative electric-hydraulic hybrid drive, characterised by a combined E-motor and pump unit. The bending speed range is 8 - 10mm/s and repeatability is +/- 0.02mm.

With features such as ByVision Bending Control, Voice Control, Part ID and Bystronic Tooling and Clamping Systems, the Bystronic Xpert 150 brake press includes a comprehensive database of material bend angles and bend allowances. The Xpert 150 brake press uses a full 2-inch HD touch-screen to display realistic 3D part animations and software-driven menu selections.

Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, has enjoyed success with its Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre 'plug and play' press brake with rapid bending speeds. This small press brake has already won a German design award and is designed for rapid, efficient parts production. A new Mobile Bending Robot is available for feeding the Xpert 40, turning it into a compact, automated bending cell for producing 3D sheet metal components. The automation module is wheeled in front of the press brake and simply interfaced to it in a plug-and-play arrangement. In minutes, users can set up the robotics and load sheet metal blanks for bending. Programming is carried out offline and ByVision Bending software allows data to be imported without interrupting the job currently in progress.

CIDAN Machinery launched several new innovations at last October's EuroBLECH exhibition. It showed ISMR its redesigned PRO Z Up/Down folder for folding complex parts with reduced handling. The patented OED is designed to ensure maximum pressure and capacity, regardless of tool height. Automatic CNC sheet thickness and folding centre adjustment, without requiring crowning, is a key feature of the machine.

It also launched its new PIVOTAL folding machine which is now able to bend 360° - it focused on a concept folder that can produce a four-sided part to reduce welding. Also on display was its new 3100 x 3.0mm FORMA folding machine with new clamping beam drive technology and redesigned side frames and its new CTLline COMPACT-12.

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. At EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover last October, it launched three new press brakes.

"Our green FX Servo press brake uses electric technology – there are no hydraulics. Users can dramatically improve their energy efficiency and make massive power savings with the press brake, which can be used with standard controllers and is very user-friendly for press brake operators. It has been designed for more uptime, less maintenance and a special focus on low energy consumption (approximately 50% lower compared to hydraulic press brakes). More accurate bending results can also be achieved due to equal bending force along the ram, Ermaksan told ISMR.

The FX servo green press brake utilises servo motors and has a number of interesting features, including a 4-axis back gauge. Full-length bends can be made between side columns, with no obstructions, and the machine is almost silent in operation. Sliding front support arms assist the operator for easier material handling.

Other existing Ermaksan press brake models include the Evolution II (hybrid), Speed Bend Pro, the Power Bend Pro, the Power-Bend Falcon, the Micro-Bend and the Heavy-Duty press brake (featuring a hydro-dynamic hybrid crowning system, 6-axis servo motor back gauge system and special top and bottom tools).

Italian company F.lli Vimercati has developed e.TERNA, a hybrid bending system. Energy savings of 30-40% and 30% decreases in machine cycle times, it says, are key results. This system can be applied to all the bending models in its range, without limits in stroke, power or dimensions.

By using the new press brake safety system, it is now possible to safely operate and close a press brake at high-speed to the clamp point.

## Wilson Tool International

(www.wilsontool.com), the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry offers easier part removal in press brake operations with swing ear sets.

"Suitable for bending boxes with four sides and return flanges, swing ears are available in most profiles for American, European and WT style punches. With the addition of swing ears, forcing the punch out of the box when making the final bend is no longer necessary, because the ears simply swing out of the way," said the manufacturer.

It also recently introduced its Express CROWNING deflection control system to help control deflection, or "the canoe effect", in press brake applications over four feet long. Part of the company's line of bending solutions, the new Express Crowning system gives fabricators the flexibility to fine-tune the deflection-compensating curve of the unit to overcome the nuances built into the machine. Press brake operators can now crown the lower beam with manual or electric options and secure dies with manual or hydraulic power.

Wilson's ExpressRail™M2000 hydraulic clamping system reduces set-up time with a wireless pendant control that clamps and seats press brake tooling in seconds, giving fabricators more open height and extended clamp life. It is compatible with existing WT and American style press brakes.
