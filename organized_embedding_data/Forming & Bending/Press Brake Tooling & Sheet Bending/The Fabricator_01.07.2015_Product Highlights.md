All-electric CNC tube benders designed for 24/7 production

Horn Machine Tools Inc. has introduced a line of all-electric CNC benders in capacities from 2 to 10 in. The machines are based on the design of the HMT hybrid benders, with the clamp and pressure die now equipped with large electric servomotors.

The available Quad Stack design allows the user to mount up to four sets of full-size bend tools at once. This gives the flexibility to switch between sizes rapidly or allows for bending of complex shapes. The machines can also be equipped with a shearing device and load/unload automation for continuous production.

Designed for 24/7 production, the machines can bend very tight-radius stainless steel on the rated diameter size. Draw bending and push rolling capabilities are standard.

Automatic plate-processing system punches, shears simultaneously

Trilogy Machinery Inc. has introduced the new Sunrise APS fully automatic plate-processing system. It can punch and shear simultaneously.

The fully enclosed system is driven by a rack-and-pinion infeed that can handle up to 40-ft. lengths of material at speeds up to 40 FPM. Standard features include pneumatic clamping, hydraulic plate hold-down, hydraulic stripper, touchscreen control, and triple punch.

The system allows hands-free operation for processing flat bar up to 20 in. wide. Models featuring the triple punch allow the operator to assign one of three punches to any particular hole, including spot marks.

A plate shear antitwist feature allows small plates to be sheared straight.

Pneumatic material guides, autolubrication system, and rolling slug bucket are standard.

Optics for fiber lasers offer extremely low absorption

Ophir Optics offers Fiberlens™, a series of collimating lenses, focusing lenses, and protective windows for use with high-power, 1-micron fiber laser systems. They are suitable for all applications from 1,030 to 1,080 nm, including fiber lasers, disk lasers, and YAG lasers.

The optics feature extremely low-absorption fused silica substrates with low-absorption, antireflective coatings on the lenses and windows that protect against laser damage. Absorption is less than 50 PPM to help prevent thermal lensing and focus shift, which can degrade cutting quality and speed. Focal length tolerance is ±0.5 percent, and the laser damage threshold is greater than 10 J.

The absorption rate of the lenses and windows is measured using a Primes optical quality monitor (OQM). The monitor, which measures the temperature rise of the optics when exposed to 1-micron laser radiation, can measure extremely low absorption levels accurately.

Off-the-shelf chamber designed for titanium welding

Filter cleaning technology eliminates leaks across filter gaskets

Imperial Systems Inc. has introduced Even-Lock Technology. This filter cleaning technology helps ensure even gasket compression on all cartridge filters in a CMAXX industrial dust collector and eliminates leaks across the filter gaskets.

The technology employs individual cams at each filter, from the front to the back, compressing the Deltamaxx filter gaskets at a 50-to-1 compression rate. Regardless of how heavy the filter is from loading, if the slide rail arm is locked forward, the gaskets are protected.
