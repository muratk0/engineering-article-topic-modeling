# FLEXIBLE BENDING

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

FACCIN's CEO, Andrea Ceretti, outlines where he sees future market potential to ISMR.

In the design and sale of plate bending rolls, profile bending machines, dished head machinery and special machines (including ship frame bending rolls and presses and plate straightening machines), FACCIN's production facilities are in Visano (Brescia province, Italy). Its complete facility covers over an area of 60,000m² (645,000ft²). Using the latest technology, the company follows through every stage of production, from receipt of an order up to design, construction, machining, assembly and shipping.

Using 3D CAD and 2D CAD systems and finite element methods (FEM) for structural design, its engineering department performs motion simulations, structural machinery and mechanical limit tests, BOM creation and complete management of data flow to production. It also uses specific programs for the development and implementation of hydraulic and electrical systems. ISMR spoke with its CEO, Andrea Ceretti, to discover where he sees future market potential.

## A platform for growth

"2018 has been a particularly good year for the FACCIN Group. After a short period of Group consolidation in 2017, we have been able to finalise some very important projects for high-performance, heavy-duty machines with tight tolerances. Our Group is now a very powerful combination of the best brands in plate rolling, profile bending and dished head manufacturing under one roof. 2019 and beyond looks very bright for the FACCIN Group," Ceretti told ISMR.

"Our strategic and technical vision is to keep growing as a group, using the synergies provided by each brand, technically and commercially, to offer customers an even wider range of options."

FACCIN's consolidation initiatives this year include the opening of a new office in Germany (FACCIN Deutschland GmbH), with a showroom and stock for its customers. Strategically, said Ceretti, this move was very important for the group's future expansion. The group is also about to open new offices, a showroom and warehouse in the United States to spearhead further growth in the North American market.

Trends and directions "There is an increasing demand for fabricated metal products and metalforming machines. The volatile steel market and the difficulty in finding experienced operators in the sheetmetal industry is driving manufacturers to improve the quality of their metalforming equipment, particularly as regards precision and automation," explained Ceretti, pointing out that current geopolitical volatility will also push metal fabricators to be 'as flexible and reactive as possible'.

"The metal industry will attempt to standardise as much as possible, with measures such as Industry 4.0, to maximise the production capacity of equipment, apply energy-saving measures and lobby governments for further tax reforms and incentives to stay competitive and improve workforce development," he added.

He believes that Asia-Pacific will drive demand in the metalforming market, with countries such as India and China being key players. Markets in Europe, North America and Middle East/Africa, he told /SMR, are evolving their infrastructures which is also driving the metalforming market.

CASE STUDY: Wind Tower Fabrication The wind tower market keeps growing at a steady pace and has become extremely competitive. SIAG, one of the leading suppliers to the energy industry with factories in the Czech Republic and Germany, manufactures massproduced rolled cans using FACCIN's Wind Tower Automation System.

Faccin's system combines its plate rolling machine with key components such as a strong side support with clamps that enable the correct alignment of the plate before tack-welding, a sturdy top support for conical sections covering the full width of the plate and a motorised feeding table with alignment device to prepare the plate for rolling. These are controlled by an advanced fullyprogrammable CNC designed to automate complex rolling projects.

Industry 4.0 has been a central pillar which FACCIN has embraced, quick to see its productivity potential for customers.

"Industry 4.0 has become more and more prevalent in the industry and is crucial for any manufacturer that wants to bring high-quality machines, advanced connectivity, analytics and people together to change day-to-day operations for a powerful business outcome. As a group, we see Industry 4.0 as a way to capitalise on the technology that we already provide. Our machines are Industry 4.0-ready featuring smart packages that offer, for example, system diagnosis; teleservice; management control; drawing import; rolling and production batch statistics and flexible network solutions. They are designed to help today's manufacturers face tomorrow's challenges. Indeed, we are already thinking about Industry 5.0," Ceretti told /SMR
