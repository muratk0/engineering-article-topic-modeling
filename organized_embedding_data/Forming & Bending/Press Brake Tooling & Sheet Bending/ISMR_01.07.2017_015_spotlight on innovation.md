# SPOTLIGHT ON INNOVATION

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“We highlight a selection of the latest new press brake, folder and panel bending technology developments in the global market”

## Today's bending technologies, by integrating a range of innovations as well as robotic and automation technologies, can deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations.

By forming the metal, the flat parts are re-shaped by bending the product between a set of tooling, so the robustness of the structure is enhanced. Sheet metal folders support the part to be bent on the back gauge table, instead of being supported by the operator (as on press brakes). They are also capable of bending different angles and material thicknesses with the same set of tooling, therefore eliminating some set-up time. Panel benders can process different types of multiple folds as well as complex radii bending.

With a range of innovations from touchscreen control and eco hybrid drive systems to automatic angle measurement, quicker tool change/set-up and more sophisticated sensing technologies, today's technologies, by integrating robotic and automation technologies, deliver huge gains in production efficiency for metal bending operations.

Safety, environmental factors (including energy efficiency and noise reduction), increased accuracy and ease of operation requirements, as well as the move towards Industry 4.0, have also affected machine tool design. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new pressbrake, folder and panel bending technology developments in the global market.

## Bending innovations

Portugese manufacturer, ADIRA, has developed the HD Greenbender, what it dubs the first "dematerialised" heavy-duty pressbrake in the world, designed with a topology optimisation technique.

"The patented multi-frame concept involves a very deep throat and avoids the need for pit installation in long machines, for a fraction of the steel needed when compared to conventional press brakes," the manufacturer told ISMR.

BB3515, the latest member of ADIRA's electric press brake Bluebender family, has a bending force of 35 tons (1.5 metre length) and features rapid cycle times with very low energy consumption.

Japanese manufacturer AMADA demonstrated a range of innovative solutions at its booth at EuroBLECH in October 2016. Fully automated bending solutions and fibre laser technology, welding machines, press brakes and new VPSS 3i software, which can simulate all processing operations at once, were on display. Its "V-factory" was introduced as a preview to AMADA's new Internet of Things (IoT) manufacturing. Visitors were shown live demonstrations of the VPSS 3i engineering system, AMADA's IoT solution covering the entire range of sheet metal processing.

The company's comprehensive range of press brakes is designed for flexibility, productivity and energy-saving (inverter-controlled stop/start technology). Its HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new 'Smartphone-style' 3D control unit. New Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture.

Its HFE 3i, HG ATC and HG ARs press brake series encompass most bending scenarios from a manually-based scenario to a fully automated, robot-supported production cell. With the HG ATC series with automatic tool changer, complex parts in batch sizes (starting at quantities of one) can be produced whereas, with the HG ARs, even large series of highly complex parts (in unmanned operation) can be produced.

AMADA's FAST (Finger with Active Security Technology) new stop fingers can be fitted onto most new AMADA bending machines and have an active safety device built in that limits risk to operators if they come into contact with moving parts, even at full speed. This safety feature and "productivity provider" was introduced at EuroBLECH 2016 on AMADA's HFE 3i 1003 machine equipped with the new generation of AFH tools and connected to the VPSS 3i BEND software.

ASco GmbH provides technical solutions from innovative long folding machines up to fully automated bending centres. Its Variobend long length folding machines and up and down bending machines are available (from 4–12 metres) to process steel of up to 2mm.

Turkish manufacturer Baykal's APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. It has pioneered development of a fully automatic pole cutting and bending machine and boasts one of Europe's largest facilities for sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.

Boschert GmbH produces a dynamic hybrid-drive press brake, the Quick-Bend, with purported energy savings of up to 80%. Quick and nearly silent, the Quick-Bend only uses electrical energy during the bending process. Based on the PROFI 28 CNC and PROFI 56 CNC press brake series, with bending lengths ranging from 1000 - 2200mm, the designers developed a press brake with an innovative electric-hydraulic hybrid drive, characterised by a combined E-motor and pump unit. The bending speed range is 8 - 10mm/s and repeatability is +/- 0.02mm.

With features such as ByVision Bending control, Voice Control, Part ID and Bystronic Tooling and Clamping Systems, the Bystronic Xpert 150 brake press includes a comprehensive database of material bendangles and bend allowances. The Xpert 150 brake press uses a full 2-inch HD touch screen to display realistic 3D part animations and software-driven menu selections.

Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, has enjoyed success with its Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre 'plug and play' press brake with rapid bending speeds. This small press brake has already won a German design award and is designed for rapid, efficient parts production. A new Mobile Bending Robot is available for feeding the Xpert 40, turning it into a compact, automated bending cell for producing 3D sheet-metal components. The automation module is wheeled in front of the press brake and simply interfaced to it in a plug-and-play arrangement. In minutes, users can set up the robotics and load sheet metal blanks for bending. Programming is carried out offline and ByVision Bending software allows data to be imported without interrupting the job currently in progress.

Bystronic has just introduced a new app that monitors, in real-time, all processes taking place within its laser cutting machines, pressbrakes and water jet cutting machines. Called ByCockpit, the software fits neatly with the digital networking requirements of Industry 4.0. It evaluates the collected data and generates status reports, allowing factory output to be optimised. For example, the ratio between the parts produced and the number of tool changes on a Bystronic Xpert press brake is an important parameter. If the number of tool changes is too high, overall production time increases, affecting productivity. All this information is provided in the performance window.

Bystronic and Lantek are also developing an MES software solution for sheet metal parts production. The Bystronic MES interlinks all the manufacturing steps and guides sheetmetal products on the ideal path through the production process, much like an intelligent navigation system.

CIDAN Machinery launched several new innovations at last October's EuroBLECH exhibition. It showed ISMR its redesigned PRO Z Up/Down folder for folding complex parts with reduced handling. The patented OED is designed to ensure maximum pressure and capacity, regardless of tool height. Automatic CNC sheet thickness and folding centre adjustment, without requiring crowning, is a key feature of the machine.

It also launched its new Pivotal folding machine which is now able to bend 360° - it focused on a concept folder that can produce a four-sided part to reduce welding. Also on display was its new 3100 x 3.0mm Forma folding machine with new clamping beam drive technology and redesigned side frames and its new CTL-line Compact-12.

At LAMIERA 2017 in Milan, Colgar highlighted one of its most recent installations for tandem press brakes installed by FIDA G, which it dubbed 'the longest tandem system in Europe mainly dedicated to the production of telescopic arms'. The total bending length is 21 metres using a tandem press brake (one with 14m length and one with 7m length) and a total installed power of 3,600 tons and concentrated charge of 500 tons/metre.
