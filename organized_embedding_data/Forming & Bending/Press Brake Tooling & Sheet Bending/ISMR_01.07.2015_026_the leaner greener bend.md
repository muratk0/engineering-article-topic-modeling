# THE LEANER, GREENER BEND

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: “We highlight a selection of the latest new press brake developments on the global market”

Many of the latest press brake innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and safer, quicker, accurate bending.

Press brakes today can bend metal parts with accuracy to a fraction of a millimetre. Many of the latest bending/press brake innovations are centred upon more user-friendly and simpler processes, more sophisticated sensing and tool-changing technologies, lower power consumption and safer, quicker, accurate bending.

The move to smaller batch sizes has sparked more technological innovation such as interference identification, quicker tool-change/set-up/selection and automatic angle measurement/correction systems. In the bending arena, the integration of robots and software solutions is delivering huge gains when it comes to production efficiency for bending operations.

In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new press brake developments on the global market.

## New bending innovations

Japanese manufacturer Amada demonstrated three fully automated bending cells at the EuroBLECH 2014 exhibition in Hanover. Amada also showcased several bending innovations at EuroBLECH, including a new range of press brakes based on its traditional down-stroking HFE range but in a 3i version (its latest 3D graphic control interface).

“This means that we can get all the bending information that the operator needs on one screen – the 3D modeller can plan, zoom and rotate and check the bending process before the products are placed on the machine. We have also introduced mechanical and laser bend angle measurement systems so that we can check correct spring back bend results and the operator does not need to pick the parts up twice to get the correct bend results – it is done automatically,” Amada told ISMR.

Its energy-saving systems (inverter-controlled stop/start technology) reduce energy consumption when bending by 60% and, consequently, overall running costs. Amada’s HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new ‘Smartphone style' 3D control unit. Its new Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture.

Following impressive growth in Spain, Amada recently announced the opening of a new Technical Centre in Barcelona.

Turkish manufacturer Baykal’s APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. It has been working on the project line to be used for the construction of the seven sports stadiums for the 2022 Qatar Fifa World Cup for almost a year and has pioneered development of a fully automatic pole cutting and bending machine. Baykal Machinery completed the transportation of the line to Qatar in December 2014.

For its manufacturing operations, Baykal uses three factories which together combine a production area of 60,000 square metres, making it one of Europe’s largest facilities for sheet metal working and fabricating machinery.

Boschert Gizelis presented its new production range of CNC press brakes and CNC shears at EuroBLECH 2014, the ‘i-bend‘ and ‘i-cut‘ respectively.

Bystronic’s Xpert 40 press brake is the smallest press brake currently manufactured by Bystronic.

“The innovative press brake design results in highly dynamic movements at bending speeds of up to 25mm per second, allowing small parts to be processed three times faster than on larger press brake models. On component lengths of up to one metre, Xpert 40 generates a bending capacity of 40 tonnes,” Bystronic told ISMR.

Bystronic’s latest ByMotion drive control system ensures finely tuned interplay between speed, precision and power. By coordinating the acceleration of the upper beam and the back gauges, it delivers bending results with a high degree of repeatability.

Ermaksan, a Turkish manufacturer specialising in the production of sheet metal working machineries, has won three awards in two different international design competitions for both its EVO II hybrid press brake and Fibermak laser cutting machine.

“In conventional press brakes, energy is consumed even in standby mode but the Evolution II press brake only uses energy whilst it is bending. It can bend at a rate of 20mm/sec. using servo motors and a highly efficient pump. It is also quiet – at 63dB noise-less and has high bending accuracy of between +/-0.01mm. These new generation press brakes are developed to require fewer oil changes (every five years),” Ermaksan explained.

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the SPEED-BEND (synchronized hydraulic press brake), the POWER-BEND PRO 3-axis CNC press brake and the ECO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake. The company’s MICRO-BEND CNC hydraulic press brake is a small, productive hydraulic press brake with the capacity to bend 5mm mild steel with 1m x 40 tonnes of press force and one-axis back gauge. Ermaksan’s HEAVY DUTY press brake features a dynamic crowning system and 6-axis servo motor back gauge system. It also boasts special top and bottom tools and a 610mm throat depth.

Italian specialist Gasparini offers a press brake range that includes the X-BEND (press force from 50 to 170 tons) and the X-PRESS ECO. Its new Dener hydraulic press brakes were designed to meet a wide variety of needs, accommodating weights ranging from 40 to 2000 tons and offering from 3 to more than 11-axis capabilities. Its new electric MC press brake is leaner, greener and more reliable delivering quiet operation and faster cycle times.

The Diamond BB Series features a compact, ball screw design driven by an AC servo motor with 40 metric tons of force and one-micron repeatability.

Belgian manufacturer LVD Company nv has expanded its Dyna-Press Series of compact, portable high-speed electric press brakes with two 24-ton models that offer higher capacity and a longer working length.

The Dyna-Press series is suitable for bending small parts. Rapid acceleration and deceleration of the electrical servo-driven ram delivers bending speeds of up to 25mm/s. The new models can handle bend lengths of up to 1250mm and have 24 tons of bending force. Coupling of the ram and servomotors is achieved through two heavy duty ball screws to distribute force and tonnage evenly across the working length.

“The Dyna-Press 24/12 standard model has a 12” touch-screen which allows operators to easily adjust individual parameters. The 24/12 Plus model offers TOUCH-B Lite control which enables the operator to create and simulate 2D-designs on a 15” touch screen. The TOUCH-B controller is compatible with our offline bending CAM software, CADMAN-B®,” LVD told ISMR.

The operator does not need to pick the parts up twice to get the correct bend results – it is done automatically

The next generation of LVD’s Easy-Form® and PPEB Series of high performance press brakes feature a new, modern design that is more energy-efficient, easier to use and incorporates added safety features. The ToolCell, LVD’s press brake with an integrated tool-changing system, significantly reduces press brake set-up time.

LVD is now offering a 220-ton, 3-metre model with a large selection of top and bottom tooling stored in a tooling stadium/warehouse under the back gauge. An innovative gripper design is built into the machine’s back gauge finger and serves as the tool changer mechanism. When a job is complete, the press brake automatically changes the top and bottom tooling as required for the next job. All tool stations are precisely positioned, eliminating the need for manual adjustments.
