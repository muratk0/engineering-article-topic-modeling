# New tooling solutions

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

MATE PRECISION Tooling will be exhibiting on booth S1908 in Chicago from 18-20 November at FABTECH 2013, North America’s largest fabricating, metal forming, finishing and welding event. Its new VariBend™ tooling performs 90° bends on a punch press – it is a flexible, multi-purpose tool that bends sheet metal tabs at any angle up to 90° and eliminates time-consuming secondary operations usually performed on a press brake.

“VariBend’s bending angle is adjustable in 1° increments and will handle the specific material types and thickness for which it was designed. VariBend will produce form heights of approximately 0.300 inch to 0.700 inch (7.60mm - 17.75mm). Programmed as a normal forming tool (regular forming or as an up-forming tool), VariBend operates with CNC punch press accuracy and with little or no sheet marking,” explained Mate.

Mate’s VariBend is suitable for punch presses with upforming capabilities and especially machines with stroke control. For thick turret machines without stroke control, Mate’s Ultraform® holder can be used for length adjustment. VariBend is available in many tooling styles and station sizes.

Mate’s Premia™ is an automated tool sharpening system with a touch-screen interface that simplifies all tool set-up and maintenance operations. Everything is menu-driven, with three standard programs for small, medium and large tools, plus two more that are customisable. If an operator prefers manual operation, Premia has a manual mode.

Mate’s UltraTEC® A and B punches are now available in optional M4PM steel for high performance punching applications. M4PM steel has at least 50 per cent or greater wear resistance according to independent metallurgical experts. For demanding punching projects, M4PM offers superior resistance to adhesive and abrasive-wear to maximise intervals between regrinds. The increased alloy content results in higher effective hardness for better wear resistance. A more uniform distribution of smaller carbides results in significantly reduced tool breakage and edge chipping. M4PM steel helps to ensure increased machine uptime, improved sheet metal products, lower tooling and production costs.

New for TRUMPF press users is Mate’s support for forming applications and special shape tooling. Mate now provides complete set-up and TruTops programming information for special shapes and forming applications for all its TRUMPF customers. This value-added product enhancement is automatic and provided at no added cost.

## DEEP HOLE DRILLING

CHIN MINN’S CNC twin-spindle, deep hole drilling machine is designed and manufactured for making master cylinders, wheel cylinders, clutch cylinders etc. This machine is designed with unit spindles upside down so that chips can be easily ejected. The twin-spindle machine can also make two pieces at a time.

After manufacturing 800 special purpose machines in its first 15 years, Chin Minn has leveraged the majority of its efforts into roll forming machines for steel ball slides and under-mount slides (including assembly machines and turnkey equipment) and special purpose machines for automotive parts. It has manufactured more than 50 production lines for brake cylinders and wheel cylinders. Chin Minn has also manufactured machine tools for high volume metal component production.

“We can provide either complete production lines or individual machines,” Chin Minn told ISMR. “In the automotive industry, component manufacturing is high-volume and special purpose machines offer production and cost benefits.” www.chinminn.com.tw

## FUTURE SHOCK

ENERGY- AND resource-efficient production is the objective of the Fraunhofer Institute for Machine Tools and Forming Technology (IWU) as it works on solutions for the production line of tomorrow. It focuses on the optimisation of cutting processes, intelligent adaptronic components and lightweight design potential in the design of machine tools, as well as in the process environment.

A newly developed mounting element isolates machines and equipment from external shock and vibration in an efficient and relatively inexpensive manner. Disruptive external influences can have a particularly negative impact on production quality when a high level of precision is required in production involving metal processing.

“Up to now, the only option available for reducing the effect of shocks and vibrations, which can be caused, for example, by presses in the production environment or by a busy street in the vicinity of the production hall, has been the introduction of expensive passive isolation measures. This has been true especially for large equipment. The new, active system can be implemented flexibly, is quick and easy to install and relatively inexpensive,” explained the IWU. www.fraunhofer.de

## LASER LENS SELECTION

OPHIR OPTRONICS Solutions Ltd., a global specialist in precision infrared optics, photonics instrumentation and 3D noncontact measurement equipment, has confirmed that TRUMPF, a world leader in industrial lasers and laser system technology, selected its CO Optics Division to provide replacement lenses for its latest 40mm lens with an embedded radio-frequency identification (RFID) chip. The chip, which is comparable in size to a single grain of rice, wirelessly transfers data over electromagnetic fields.

The RFID chip embedded in the new 40mm lens communicates with an integrated system that can monitor the condition of the lens. With alerts on detected contamination, laser operators can now clean or replace a lens before there is a cutting problem, thus averting unnecessary laser downtime and poor cutting quality. TRUMPF, a world leader in industrial lasers and laser system technology, provides CO lasers, solidstate lasers, marking lasers and laser systems. www.ophiropt.com

GOING WITH THE FLOW

PRO technology by KMT Waterjet Systems enables waterjet cutting at a working pressure of up to 6,200 bar. Cutting at this pressure range has numerous advantages and respective cutting machines are more than ever in demand. KMT’s STREAMLINE PRO-2 – the second and optimised generation of a high pressure pumps capable of generating the ultra-high pressure, features the KMT Hard Seal End Cap which is fastened by just 8 jack bolts.

The torque necessary to assemble and disassemble the end cap amounts to a maximum of 95 Nm – which corresponds to the fastening torque of an M10 screw (quality grade 12.9). This means that the maintenance effort and downtimes of the pump can be significantly reduced. For example, a trained employee can carry out a seal change in no more than 20 minutes.

“The operation of a waterjet cutting machine with a working pressure of up to 6,200 bar has now become more user-friendly and affordable. Especially if cutting thick or hard material, a particularly smooth cutting edge or the resourcing of spare capacity for covering peak work times are important. Many machine operators can now easily make a decision in favour of a 6,200 bar system,” KMT explained. www.kmt-waterjet.com
