We highlight a selection of the latest new press brake bending systems, tooling and technology innovations in the global market.

Folders and press brakes. The press brake controls the movement of either the punch (upper tool) or the die (bottom tool) and provides the press force using hydraulic rams or electrical servo motors. The press brake's bending force, known as tonnage, determines how much pressure can be applied by the punch during a bend. Higher tonnage allows for bending thicker and harder metals, and lower tonnage is most appropriate for thinner metals.

There are several types of press brake (such as pneumatic, servo electric, hydraulic and mechanical). The workpiece is clamped between matching punch and die to form a pre-set bend. The characterisation of capacity of a press brake machine is based on parameters such as working length; working height; amplitude; stroke; tonnage and distance between side housings or frame uprights.

Market analyst Technavio estimates that the global press brake market size will grow up to US$ 413.87 million, at a CAGR of 4.96% during 2021-2025. It expects 39% of market growth to originate from the APAC region during the forecast period. The global electric press brake market was valued at US$ 343.8 million in 2020 and is projected to register a CAGR of 3.9% by 2031, according to analyst MarketResearch.biz
