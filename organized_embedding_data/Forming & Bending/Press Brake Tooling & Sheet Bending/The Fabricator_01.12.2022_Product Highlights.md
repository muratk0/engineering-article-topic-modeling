Tube bending software offers insight before bending occurs

Unison Ltd. has developed Opt2Sim Tube, the latest addition to its Opt2Sim software suite. This tube bending simulation software uses machine and component data to provide virtual insight into the tube bending process before a single part is bent.

It is designed to simplify creation of virtual parts ready for bending data to be uploaded to the bending machine. Users can drag and drop STEP files into the software program and extract tube data instantly. Parts also can be drawn by entering XYZ or YBC coordinates and viewed in 3D. Bend data is quickly converted into printable reports, and individual tube data also can be extracted.

Products within the Opt2Sim software suite include a version in which both CAD modelling capability and control engine are used to provide not only accurate simulation, but also enhanced collision detection. Simulated bending takes place on an exact CAD representation of the Unison Breeze or Synergy machine that will be used.

Ultrahigh-pressure fan jet offers maximum surface coverage

Diamond Technology Innovations has introduced an ultrahigh-pressure fan jet for blasting and surface preparation. The fantomJET nozzle is equipped with a tetraCORE diamond orifice shaped to create a fan jet spray pattern that covers an average of five times more surface area than a single jet while maintaining maximum impact force over the entire jet stream spread, according to the manufacturer.

Rated up to 60,000 PSI, the nozzle performs with zero degradation of the fan jet inlet geometry over the product’s lifetime to prevent loss in system pressures during use. The fan jet enables the cleaning of softer material with less damage and improved product removal control, says the company.

All-in-one robotic plasma cutting machine handles tube, pipe, channel

HGG has introduced the RoboRail, a small, robotic plasma machine that cuts 12-in. channel, tube columns and angles up to 8 by 8 in., and 11/4- to 10-in. pipe. It takes care of miscellaneous work and supplements larger-capacity machines with added flexibility.

Represented exclusively in the U.S. by Automated Layout Technology, the machine incorporates a Hypertherm plasma system to make cuts, holes, and copes in handrail pipe, stringer channels, box tubes, and angles.

The system accepts files directly from SDS/2, TEKLA, STEP, and various other modeling software to automate the cutting process.

Foot pedal includes fingertip amperage control

The new, patented Steady-Grip foot pedal from CK Worldwide combines a fingertip amperage control and low-profile foot pedal in one device. Based on the design of the Steady-Grip fingertip amperage control, the new two-inone option incorporates this design into a quick-release foot pedal, giving users the ability to work either way.

The unit, with the option to remove the pistol-grip-style handle, is a linear slide control that provides a smooth adjustment of amperage, perfect for working out of position or when a foot pedal isn’t the best choice. The foot pedal comes with a quickrelease pad allowing for quick changes from remote fingertip use to foot pedal use.
