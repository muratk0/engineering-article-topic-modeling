Orbital pipe welding systems feature autoprogramming

Magnatech’s Pipemaster power source models are used for multipass orbital pipe welding. The 515 model has a 150-amp output, and the 516 model either 200- or 400-amp output. They control weld head mechanical functions for torch rotation, wire feed, torch oscillation, arc voltage control, and control of arc length. The power sources can operate 22 different models of the manufacturer’s weld heads, as well as certain competitor models.

A primary concern in the welding industry is the need to program the equipment for each pipe size and material and the time needed for welders to learn to program a CNC tool. The proprietary autoprogramming mode allows operation without programming training. An inexperienced user can enter the pipe’s OD, wall thickness, and material, and the control generates a weld program.

Edit and scaling modes are available for modifying the parameters. The edit mode allows changes at any point in the weld cycle; the scaling mode allows a percentage change for the entire program. The power source stores up to 100 weld programs. Programs can be transferred using a standard USB flash drive.

3-D marking stylus follows curve of cylindrical workpieces

Gravotech Marking has unveiled the Technifor high-amplitude 3-D stylus capable of following the relief of any part to be marked. The stylus is compatible with the company’s full line of pneumatic machines.

The stylus can perform a succession of markings regardless of the shape of each workpiece. The stylus tip extends and retracts automatically as it follows sloping planes, out-of-flat surfaces, and deformed surfaces.

With an amplitude of 0.63 in., the stylus can perform stepped markings on different levels of the same workpiece in a single cycle. With no need for an actuator, without moving the marking head, it can engrave in recesses, hollows, and difficult-to-reach places.

The stylus also can mark multiple lines following the curve of a cylindrical workpiece, without the need for a part rotation system.

On automated lines, the unit handles a succession of parts of varying heights. Complex programming and a Z axis on the marking head are eliminated. For manual or semiautomatic production operations, a single marking file can be used for a multitude of parts, simplifying adjustment tasks.

According to the manufacturer, the quality of Data Matrix™ codes is close to the results obtained with electromagnetic technology, with a homogeneity and alignment of points that simplify code reading.

Airflow amplifiers extract dust, fumes with no moving parts

Vortec airflow amplifiers extract smoke, fumes, and debris to help enhance safety and productivity in the workplace. The high-flow, bladeless blowers amplify compressed air up to 60-fold, in unducted applications, with no moving parts. They can be mounted, ducted, and moved easily, and airflow and output are adjustable.

With no electrical requirements, they can be mounted wherever there is compressed air, and their quiet operation meets OSHA noise requirements.

The airflow amplifiers are available in multiple sizes, in either aluminum or stainless steel, to deliver flow rates from 32 to 2,300 SCFM. They can adapt from extraction and ventilation of fumes and gases to blowoff applications such as drying of parts and cooling of moldings and castings.

Portable CMM offers increased accuracy for 3-D measurement

Hexagon Mfg. Intelligence has introduced the 77 series portable coordinate measuring machine (PCMM), which the company says is the most accurate Romer Absolute Arm to date. It is designed for high-end 3-D measurement applications.

The machine achieves nearly a 20 percent improvement in scanning accuracy and a 15 percent improvement in touch-probe measurement accuracy over the 75 series. It is available in five sizes from 8.2- to 14.8-ft. measuring volumes.

The PCMM can be switched on and used immediately without warmup or referencing. Probe changes can be made without calibration. Acoustic and haptic operator feedback facilitates usage in harsh shop floor environments.
