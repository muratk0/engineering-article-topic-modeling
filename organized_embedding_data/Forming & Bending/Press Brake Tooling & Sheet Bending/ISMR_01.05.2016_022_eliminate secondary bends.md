# Eliminate secondary bends

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

WILSON TOOL INTERNATIONAL www.wilsontool.com, the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, now offers Opti-Bend tooling for punch press operations.

“Opti-Bend tooling gives sheet metal fabricators the ability to create bends of up to 90 degrees on the punch press, which helps to eliminate the need for secondary operations at the press brake, saving time and cost. The Opti-Bend tooling includes a rotating cam in the die that rotates and bends the material during the punching stroke, which simulates the cycling of a press brake, with little or no sheet marking on soft or highly-polished materials.”

Opti-Bend tooling is available for use in Thick Turret and TRUMPF-style punch presses. You can watch Wilson Tool’s Opti-Bend tooling video and animation, and see the tooling in action on a punch press, on the video section of the ISMR website at www.ismr.co.uk/videos.html ■
