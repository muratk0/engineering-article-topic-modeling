## Rapid bending

Bystronic’s Fast Bend safety system increases the safety and productivity of a press brake.

“It enables travel at the rapid-advance speed right until the tool is almost on the metal sheet so that travel at working speed is virtually limited to just the actual bending process itself,” the Swiss manufacturer told ISMR.

“This achieves a time saving of around 20 per cent or, put another way, instead of four conventional bends, it is now possible to make five bends over the same period of time.”

Lower energy consumption, reduced machine noise and less heat loss characterise Bystronic’s Energy Saver. As soon as the upper beam has reached the upper dead centre following a bending operation, the main motor of the press brake is automatically switched off until the next bending process is started. Up to a third of the energy used by a conventional press brake can be saved, says Bystronic.

Italian manufacturer Codatto specializes in panel bending technology. It is already working on new machines and options to support its EVA 2516 panel bender.

Colgar SpA has over 60 years of manufacturing experience and now enjoys a global user base. It produces a range of press brakes run from 320kN to 30MN with bending lengths of up to 12 metres (including heavy tandem press brakes), multi-axis capability and a range of angle correction and robotic handling systems.

“Our TruBend Cell 7000 is the fastest system in the world for the automatic bending of small pieces. With a cycle time from an average of only 4 seconds per tilt it delivers up to twice as high part throughput as conventional bending cells and could replace three manual bending units,” TRUMPF told ISMR.

The cell basically consists of a bending machine TruBend Cell 7036 Edition, a bending robot BendMaster and a loading unit LoadMaster Bend. The great strength of the small TruBend 7036 press brake is its speed which it owes to a new TRUMPF strategy for beams and back gauges: reduced mass combined with a highperformance drive. Back stops made of light carbon fiber (CFK) allow the back gauge to achieve an acceleration of up to 20 m/s2. The beam of the TruBend Series 7000 can maintain this speed due to its electrical torque engine, which operates up to three and a half times as fast as the beam on a conventional press brake.

## Heavy duty bending

Baykal was the first company in Turkey to produce power guillotines and hydraulic press brakes. In February 2009, it completed a significant machine installation in the State of Pennsylvania, USA. The machine is a monoblock press brake with 12 metres bending length and 2500 tons bending capacity, which the buyer will use in manufacturing the steel construction parts of the new Freedom Tower, which is now being built in New York city in place of the old World Trade Center. This giant press brake has a weight of 320 tons.

Flexible, modular and lean, Salvagnini machines and systems for automatically bending sheet metal include two lines of products: the P4X & P2X Panel Benders and the Roboformer robotic press-brakes. At the end of 2011, Salvagnini Maschinenbau completed building work on its new production hall number 6, enlarging the complete area of the company to a total of 46,000 square metres.

LVD Company n.v. offers its co-manufactured heavy duty series of press brakes designed to address the need for cost-effective custom solutions for simple to complex heavy bending applications. Co-manufactured press brakes are engineered and manufactured by LVD and its joint venture company LVD-HD. Its Smart Crowning system, a line of PPEB-H Series and Easy-Form Series press brakes. Smart Crowning provides an additional independent crowning axis on top of a conventional V-axis to automatically compensate for asymmetric variations in material thickness and material properties and ensure bending accuracy over the entire length of a work piece.

## Press brake tooling

LVD also offers precision ground tooling with its patented STONE radius for all models of LVD press brakes. A special patented compound die radius, STONE tooling (“Shaped Tooling for Optimal beNding without wEar”) is produced with a progressive radius on both sides of the Vopening, allowing the material to flow into the die more evenly and with less drag. The design of the V-die reduces the friction between the material and the die encountered when bending by creating a rolling condition as the material enters the die. The STONE radius assures accurate bend angles along the entire bending length.

Italian manufacturer Rolleri provides customers with new bend tooling solutions using application software such as FEM analysis systems, bending simulators and CAD 3D to make feasibility studies. All tools are tested on a new CNC press brake machine.

Wila offers a comprehensive range of press brake tooling, including its New Standard Pro and New Standard premium series. It also offers a range of flattening and hemming tools, which can be customised. Wila’s new TIPS system is a digital control system for press brake tools.

With Wila’s Universal Press Brake Concept, the user can apply various tooling systems and optimise his choice according to factors such as the type of product and type of processing, the series size and the changeover frequency. It is well suited to the goal of increased press brake productivity.

Since its foundation over 40 years ago, Wilson Tool has grown to become one of the largest independent manufacturers of punch press tooling, press brake tooling and accessories in the world. Every standard press brake die from Wilson Tool is treated with its patented Nitrex surface enhancement. It is now offering hydraulic and manual clamping options, exhibiting the hydraulic clamping option at the Blech EXPO exhibition in Germany last year. New urethane products from Wilson Tool help press brake operators reduce or even eliminate sheet marking during bending operations. ■
