## The bending factor

Portuguese manufacturer, ADIRA, has developed the HD Greenbender, which it dubs the first "dematerialised" heavy duty press brake in the world, designed with a topology optimisation technique.

"The patented multi-frame concept involves a very deep throat and avoids the need for pit installation in long machines, for a fraction of the steel needed when compared to conventional press brakes," the manufacturer told ISMR.

BB3515, the latest member of ADIRA's electric press brake Bluebender family, has a bending force of 35 tons (1.5 metre length) and features rapid cycle times with very low energy consumption.

Japanese manufacturer AMADA presented its latest HG1003ATC bending system for its V-factory digital sheet metal processing solution at the SIMM2018 exhibition. The servo/hydraulic hybrid drive system features an Automatic Tool Changer (ATC) and an automatic bend indicator sensor plus a new AMNC 3i control and ID scanner.

The manufacturer also offers fully automated bending solutions and fibrelaser technology, welding machines, pressbrakes and new VPSS 3i software, which can simulate all processing operations at once. Its HG press brake features a patented hybrid drive system and a new 'Smartphone style' 3D control unit. New Product Designer software can unfold 3D sheet metal models and automatically apply unfolding data and feature recognition ready for manufacture.

Its HFE 3i, HG ATC and HG ARs press brake series encompass most bending scenarios from a manually-based scenario to a fully automated, robot-supported production cell. With the HG ATC series with automatic tool changer, complex parts in batch sizes starting at quantities of one can be produced whereas with the HG ARs even large batches of highly complex parts (in unmanned operation) can be produced.

ASCO GmbH provides technical solutions from innovative long folding machines up to fully automated bending centres. Its Variobend long length folding machines and up and down bending machines are available (from 4 - 12 metres) to process steel of up to 2mm.

Turkish manufacturer Baykal's APHS range of press brakes is highly versatile. It has pioneered development of a fully automatic pole cutting and bending machine and boasts one of Europe's largest facilities for sheetmetal working and fabricating machinery.

Boschert GmbH has produced a dynamic hybrid-drive press brake, the Quick-Bend, with purported energy savings of up to 80%. Quick and nearly silent, the Quick-Bend only uses electrical energy during the bending process. With bending lengths ranging from 1000 - 2200mm, the designers developed a press brake with an innovative electric-hydraulic hybrid drive, characterised by a combined E-motor and pump unit. The bending speed range is 8 - 10mm/s and repeatability is +/- 0.02mm.

Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, has enjoyed success with its Xpert 40-tonne, one-metre 'plug and play' press brake with rapid bending speeds. This small press brake has already won a German design award and is designed for rapid, efficient parts production.

A new Mobile Bending Robot is available for feeding the Xpert 40, turning it into a compact, automated bending cell for producing 3D sheet metal components. The automation module is wheeled in front of the press brake and interfaced to it in a plug-and-play arrangement. In minutes, users can set up the robotics and load sheet metal blanks for bending. Programming is carried out offline and ByVision Bending software allows data to be imported without interrupting the job currently in progress.

Bystronic has introduced a new ByCockpit app that monitors, in real-time, all processes taking place within its laser cutting machines, press brakes and water jet cutting machines.

Bystronic and Lantek are also developing an MES software solution for sheet metal part production.

CIDAN Machinery Sweden AB announced the acquisition of 100% of shares in Austrian Forstner Maschinenbau GmbH, effective at the end of December 2017. Its redesigned PRO Z Up/Down folder is for folding complex parts with reduced handling. The patented OED is designed to ensure maximum pressure and capacity, regardless of tool height. Automatic CNC sheet thickness and folding centre adjustment, without requiring crowning, is a key feature of the machine.

Cidan's PIVOTAL folding machine is now able to bend 360° - it showcased a concept folder that can produce a four-sided part to reduce welding at EuroBLECH 2016. Its 3100 x 3.0mm FORMA folder now has new clamping beam drive technology and redesigned side frames.

At LAMIERA 2017 in Milan, Colgar highlighted one of its most recent installations for tandem press brakes installed by FIDA G, what it dubbed 'the longest tandem system in Europe mainly dedicated to the production of telescopic arms'. The total bending length is 21 metres using a tandem press brake (one with 14m length and one with 7m length) and a total installed power of 3,600 tons and concentrated charge of 500 tons/metre.

Turkish manufacturer, Ermaksan, has been continuously improving and adding to its portfolio of products. Its green FX Servo press brake uses electric technology for low energy consumption (approximately 50% lower, compared to hydraulic press brakes). With a 4-axis back gauge, full-length bends can be made between side columns, with no obstructions, and the machine is almost silent in operation. Sliding front support arms assist the operator for easier material handling. This model was on display at its booth at Blechexpo 2017.

Other Ermaksan press brake models include the Evolution II (hybrid), Speed Bend Pro, the Power Bend Pro, the Power-Bend Falcon, the Micro-Bend, the Eco-Bend Expert and the Heavy-Duty press brake (featuring a hydro-dynamic hybrid crowning system, 6-axis servo motor back-gauge system and special top and bottom tools).

Italian company F.lli Vimercati has developed e.TERNA, a hybrid bending system. Energy savings of 30-40% and 30% decreases in machine cycle times, it says, are key results. This system can be applied to all the bending models in its range, without limits in stroke, power or dimensions.

Fintek Industry Company Limited provides sheet metal machines, such as hydraulic press brakes, shearing, folding, rolling machines etc. It has become one of the largest machine tool manufacturers in Taiwan.

Italian specialist Gasparini offers a pressbrake range that includes the entry-level X-Press Easy and the X-press ECO energysaving system that increases speed and lowers energy consumption using an inverter motor drive. It is available as an option for the X-Press product line. The ECO inverter motor drive is leaner and greener, delivering quiet operation and faster cycle times. Press brake tonnage starts from 25 tons up to 3000 tons and from 4 to more than 11 axes.

Gasparini has also brought out AIC AirSlide pneumatic tool adapters, a barcode reader, wireless pedal and the X-Press SuperCustom Edition, a new line dedicated to deep-box bending. The company has also developed its GPS4 angle measurement system for press brakes. Complex shapes, with Z-bends or with narrow flanges, can be bent and set-up times reduced.

HACO's new FastBEND-2D MT Premium was demonstrated at Metaltech Malaysia. The system enables rapid data processing and accurate calculation of the optimal bending programs for hydraulic CNC press brakes. HACO's bending portfolio includes press brakes with capacities ranging from 40 to 2,000 tons and working lengths from 1.6 to 10m.

On 10 October 2017, bending specialist Jean Perrot and its partners Radan, Mate Outillage France and CLEIA launched its first technical open day based upon the innovative Bending 4.0 concept.

"We introduced the E COBOT BEND, a collaborative robot linked to our Maneo Premium press brake. The Maneo Premium 1000T version featured dynamic hydraulic crowning and Tool Box XXL. We demonstrated 3D vision with the robot and highlighted the customised HMI (Human Machine Interface) on the Maneo Premium with special preventive maintenance pages," explained Erick Rousseau, Business Unit Manager, Jean Perrot and Project Department Manager, Pinette Group.

"We also showcased our new Maintenance Contract 4.0, with Augmented Reality (AR) technology, as well as a Special Tool Box to feed two press brakes, installed next to each other. Visitors on the day could also see our shear with a rear belt to recover any cut parts to the front of the machine."

The new 'JEAN PERROT ROBOTICS by CLEIA' brand has now officially been launched (see more details in ISMR Product Reviews).

Jorns AG is a Swiss manufacturer of folders, double folders and accessories used for processing sheet metal. In 2016, the company launched its own CP200 touchscreen control panel.

JORNS AG double bending is an efficient method of processing sheet metal in a slitter-folder. Its TwinMatic-Pro double bending folders operate using both upper and lower bending beams to process sheet metal profiles. The Norma-Line, Maxi-Line and Super-Line long-length slitter folders from Jorns AG boast a modular design which allows owners to configure the machine in the way that suits their own bending requirements. Optional features include variable lengths (4m, 6m, 8m, 10m and 12.2m), CNC controls, shearing systems, hydraulic material thickness adjustment, depth stop and top beams. Its J-Bend app, for tablets and mobile phones (Apple/Android), supports the management and production of sheet metal profiles.

LVD Company NV has expanded its line of ToolCell automated tool changing press brakes with the ToolCell 220/30 Plus and ToolCell 220/40 Plus. The new Plus models can handle bend lengths of 3m and 4m and have 220 tons of bending force. They feature an open height of 570mm to accommodate higher tools. The new tools - 231mm punches and 130mm dies - allow operators to bend parts with higher flanges. LVD's ToolCell hydraulic press brake features an integrated tool changer and tool storage system.

Synchro-Form is LVD's next leap in adaptive bending technology and a new solution for large-profile bending. The company also produces the Dyna-Press electric press brake, Easy-Form® series press brake and PPED Series hydraulic press brakes, featuring a multi-axis back gauge, CNC V-axis crowning system and a 15" version of LVD's TOUCH-B graphical icon-driven CNC controller.

Mate Precision Tooling's CleanBend™ forming technology for press brakes is designed for high-quality sheet metal forming. Using rotating supports, the process mimics a folding operation with minimal to no marking of the sheet metal. Mate's press brake tooling also includes a full array of European Precision and WILA TRUMPF-style punches, dies, standard specials, "Bevel Tang Style" press brake tooling, specials and accessories, all available in segmented, full-length and halflength sizes.

Murata Machinery (Muratec) was established in 1935. In 1989, Muratec merged with Wiedemann. It exhibited its BB6020 ball screw press brake with new operator support system, "VIDERE", at the EuroBLECH 2016 exhibition.

Turkish manufacturer, MVD Makina Saniya AS, has manufactured press brakes, shears, punch machines and laser machines since 1950. The company has showcased new features and options on its iBend press brakes. Its iBend C series of press brakes can configure up to a maximum of 12 axes, has large throat depth and stroke, a heightadjustable control panel and automatic heightadjusted front support system.

Olma Srl's Allbend is a flexible bending centre using a sheet metal bending method inspired by programming simplicity, ease of operation, reduced energy consumption and low environmental impact.

Parma Stamp Srl has manufactured special and standard tools for sheet metal bending since 1974. Tools for press brakes are designed according to customer specifications.

Prima Power's new BCe Smart servo electric panel bender is equipped with a range of options to adapt it to specific applications such as automatic tool change, additional short blades for symmetrical or asymmetrical positioning, the UBC to minimise bending profile width and an additional screen for self-learning mode. Bending force is 32 tons.

The eP1030 is the bestseller in its eP electric press brake family. All the eP-series press brakes use servo electric technology; feature an O-frame construction and open tooling concept; the Prima Power touch-screen numerical control and the new Lazer Safe IRIS for operator and tool safety.

An upgrade on Prima Power's electric Fastbend panel bender features a new system that can control the C-frame from both sides and is more accurate, which is better for bending delicate parts (3.2mm max. thickness). PunchBend is Prima Power's compact fabrication solution for cut-to-size materials, which processes pre-cut material automatically into ready-bent components.

RAS Reinhardt produces fully automatic double folders, MultiBend-Center and MiniBendCenter. The UpDownCenter RAS 78.43 features an automatic tool changer. The XXL-Center RAS 75.06-2 long folding centre was equipped with a roller shear at EuroBLECH 2016.

Rolleri SpA has produced a new "ROL200" clamp for press brake tool punches. The patented system enables pneumatic or manual vertical clamping for punches. Its new QuickLock-System, compatible with Rolleri tools Type R2, and its new ROL200 compatible with Rolleri tools type R1, are its latest innovations.

Rolla-V supplies a comprehensive range of press brake tools, as well as the patented Rolla-V.

Dutch bending specialist, SafanDarley, presented a range of product innovations for sheet metal forming at Blechexpo in November 2017. Its high-end generation press brake, the E-Brake Ultra series, boasted a new design and was equipped with CNC crowning. SafanDarley also showcased its H-Brake Hybrid series of press brakes with an innovative hybrid drive, a combination of hydraulics and electronics. Machines from its standard range, such as the M-Shear 310-6 with Return-to-Sender function, were also on display.

Options for the E-Brake Ultra include a 3D back gauge and the E-Bend L Blue laser anglemeasurement system. Another option for the E-Brake Ultra series is EC40 Smart software. In addition to EC20 functionality, the EC40 Smart has the ability to read-in 3D drawings on a mobile device, unfold and carry out a bending simulation.

At Blechexpo, the company also presented an E-Brake 35T with an extremely large Q-dimension of 1090mm.. The enlarged Q size allows operators to bend products with high upright sides. The company's AutoPOL simulation software, for offline programming of press brakes, was also on display. A new generation, called AutoPOL GT, has just been released.

Salvagnini Robotica, the Salvagnini Group's new division for the development and manufacture of press brakes and associated robotic applications, opened its doors on 19 June 2017 in Italy. Blechexpo 2017 was the global debut of its new, compact P2L-2120 panel bender, capable of bending material of up to 2,180mm in length and 203mm in height.

"The new P2lean-2120 is equipped with an ABA automatic blankholder and CLAN negative auxiliary blades that increase its versatility and allow production in kit and in sequence with masked time tool set-up (meaning no changeover time)," explained the Italian manufacturer.

"P2lean-2120, like all panel benders in the Salvagnini range, implements MAC2.0 adaptive technology, that makes it independent from the quality of material being processed; the panel bender detects in masked time any variations in the material being processed. If relevant, they are automatically compensated for by the adjustment of the blade movements, guaranteeing constant quality of the bend even with variations in the material inside the batch, and eliminating waste even in the presence of limited quantity production batches."

The global debut of its B3.ATA press brake, equipped with upper and lower automatic tool adjustment devices and MAC technology, was also celebrated at Blechexpo. B3.ATA is a native 4.0 hybrid press brake which can also be monitored remotely. B3 was equipped with the new iCON user interface. The ATA system eliminates the re-tooling of the press and changes and automatically adjusts the length of the tool in approximately five seconds, allowing for kit production even in press brakes.

Italian manufacturer Schiavi has a number of innovative products, including its LineAr pressbrake with patented Crowning Free System designed for accuracy and "canoe-free" bending. Its ATLAS CNC controller with new functionality equips Schiavi press brakes for Industry 4.0 while its Moses projection and touch-panel user interface, for full integration with the ATLAS CNC controller, is designed for 'error-free' set-up.

Salvagnini’s electric panel bender

With the LineAr Plus, the manufacturer told ISMR, Schiavi has improved the LineAR concept, ensuring even greater accuracy for the bend. The "Plus" package is an optional extra that is available on all LineAr machines. Schiavi's LineAr Plus 220-40 (220 tonnes x 4 metres) large press brake features this capability. At the LAMIERA 2017 exhibition in Milan, the Schröder Group showcased its new folder, the PowerBend Industrial UD. The folder featured an innovative back gauge system and suction cups. The Group also produces the SPB Evolution UD and MAK 4 Evolution UD with automatic tool changer. Both machines are able to bend up and down.

It has also developed unfolding software to calculate the blank size and program. Step-files are imported into Schröder Unfold, they are unfolded to create the flat pattern, calculating cut size, notch development and internal geometry placement. The last step is the creation of the flat profile that can be exported for use in the blank creation process.

Biegemaster long folders, from Sperr + Lechner GmbH & Co. KG Biegemaster, feature robust construction and simple handling through intuitive control. Biegemaster's newly developed software, the BMS Multi-Touch Control, is now a feature on all its CNC folders with automatic backgauge. The software boasts several interesting functions and a new, modern design.

"The BMS Multi-Touch Control features a multi-touch screen, rather like those you see on an iPad. Using one finger on the screen, you can make profiles larger or smaller and angles wider or shorter in preparation for the bending process. Users can open existing profiles with one click. The software is easy to use and operate, even for someone who has never used a folder before or bent a metal sheet," explained Mario Traub of Biegemaster.

Other time-saving features have been designed into the software, such as a tapered back gauge feature with a 'stick- together function. A new laser-pointing solution for taper bending (which highlights the point where the sheet should be positioned) is another innovation.

TEDA Srl Speedy compact press brakes are designed for small part bending and are available in two versions, manual and NC driven, and in two dimensions (550 - 835mm, 16 and 18 tons respectively). The company also offers SPEED GRIP pneumatic and manual clamping systems for press brakes, available in different versions.

Swiss manufacturer, THALMANN Maschinenbau AG produces its TD double folder and TZ single folder. The technology features kinetic control shaft technology for maximum angular accuracy and parallelism of parts, a new VFD construction principle (Vertical Force Drive) of the TZ model for maximum force, an energy-efficient DFT-drive system (Dynamic Folding Technology), a new tool geometry and intuitive CNCs.

"We have developed a next-generation model in the TD series that can process harder (higher-tensile strength) material as more and more stainless steel and other steel alloys are being processed. The customer can save money because the folders can process thinner, but stronger, sheet metal. We developed this as an open and flexible software platform, with Industry 4.0 principles in mind," the manufacturer told ISMR.

Turkish manufacturer TOSKAR MAKINA's Easy Fab series of press brakes has been designed with two main goals in mind - easy operation and maximum productivity in the long run. They feature a sturdy steel frame (ST44 A1 steel) and a wide variety of standard features such as a motorised R axis, manual crowning and full-length tooling.

Panel bending with high part flexibility is now even faster with the TruBend Center 7030, TRUMPF's new and fully automatic bending cell. The machine turns the parts automatically, freeing the operator and increasing the productivity of the bending process. A laser-based ACB angle measurement system enables operators to work with greater precision and, in conjunction with TRUMPF's TecZone Fold offline programming tool, more efficiently.

The TruBend Center 7030 is designed to process a wide range of shapes and formats, including sheets of aluminium of up to 4mm thick, mild steel of up to 3mm thick or stainless steel of up to 2.2mm thick.

With its new TruBend Series 8000, TRUMPF is offering press brakes to handle large-format and thick components. Large open heights and bending lengths, along with press power of up to 1,000 tons, make the machines in the new TruBend Series 8000 true powerhouses. A new addition to the TruBend 3000 range is the TruBend 3066 with a press force of 600kN and 2m bending length.

WILA's Tooling Identification and Positioning System (TIPS) is an intelligent system that can be incorporated in the clamping system of press brakes. Built-in electronics in the clamping system communicates with tools via an ID tag. Users can thereby easily locate and determine if the correct tools have been selected for the bending program and whether they are in the correct position in the press brake. The WILA Tool Advisor, intelligent online software, also helps users to design 'the best possible tools for specific bending applications'. If a standard tool is not available to form the sheet part, users can immediately create a special tool and order it online.

Wilson Tool International, the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, offers easier part removal in press brake operations with swing ear sets.

Express Air is its press brake clamp for bending applications. The air-powered option clamps and seats front and back tools simultaneously. AFH is the company's line of fixed height tooling designed to be fully compatible with Amada fixed height tooling and machine control. Wilson Tool has also introduced its Express CROWNING deflection control system. Press brake operators can now crown the lower beam with manual or electric options and secure dies with manual or hydraulic power.

Wilson's ExpressRail™ 2000 hydraulic clamping system reduces set-up time with a wireless pendant control that clamps and seats press brake tooling in seconds, giving fabricators more open height and extended clamp life. It is compatible with existing WT and American-style press brakes.
