# Surface Technology coating investments

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

SEVERAL KEY investments are underway at the Surface Technology plant in Coventry (UK) as the facility gears up to meet an influx of high volume work for black passivation and stainless steel zinc nickel finishing.

By investing £60k into the latest coating technology, the Surface Technology site in Coventry is reinforcing its reputation as a zinc nickel specialist as well as showcasing its capability into new markets. In addition, the company has implemented its first fixed weekend working shift across the group to meet the increased capacity, as well as demonstrating its approach to creating safer working conditions with OHSAS 18001 accreditation.

The new black passivation tank was introduced in response to a significant increase in demand for black coatings. Through a fully automated process, Surface Technology will see its black capacity increase to in excess of 5,000 parts per hour. As well as supporting its current core industry sectors of automotive and construction, the new work will see the site diversify into mining, agriculture and military industries. The provision of this new service will allow Surface Technology to offer a viable alternative to the highly competitive black paint market.

Investment has also gone into adding a new nickel strike facility to the plant for high volume work on hydraulic adapters where the plating is required for corrosion resistance on stainless steel. The Deedmore Road facility is currently in the final stages of achieving the Occupational Health and Safety Assessment Specification (OHSAS) 18001 certificate for the Health and Safety management systems. ## Hydraulic presses for cold forming

AS PART OF its ongoing expansion plan, Dawson Shanahan, a global specialist in precision engineering, has installed two new Locatelli Meccanica hydraulic CNC presses. This has enabled the Welshpool-based precision cold forming and machining specialist to enhance its cold forming capability and meet growing demand.

The two new presses, of which one is a 70-tonne version and the other is a more powerful 200-tonne version, are designed to remove common tooling restraints and are therefore suitable for manufacturing components for almost any application. Between them, the presses have boosted Dawson Shanahan’s component output as well as delivering high quality final product. Both presses feature a built-in, load-dependent lubrication system.

With over 70 years of experience, Dawson Shanahan Limited is a specialist in cold forming and machining of high precision, customer specified copper, aluminium, ferrous and assembled components.
