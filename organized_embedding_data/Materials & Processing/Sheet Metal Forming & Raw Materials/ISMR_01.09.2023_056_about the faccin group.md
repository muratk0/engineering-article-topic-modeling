# About The Faccin Group

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

For over six decades, the Faccin Group has positioned itself as a global specialist in the manufacture of metalforming machines. The group's three leading brands (FACCIN, BOLDRINI and ROUNDO) offer a range of plate rolls, angle rolls, dished end lines and integrated solutions for rolling, bending and forming metal sheets, profiles and heads.

The company's goal is to deliver modern industrial solutions in forming technology, with a strong emphasis on enhancing performance and reducing energy and production costs.
