# Six-sided sealed packaging of heavy steel plate

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

Pruszyński Sp. z o.o, a Polish manufacturer of steel roofing and facades, has installed a RoRo StretchPack® packaging line at its facility near Warsaw (Poland). The new line is for packaging pallets with stacks of heavy steel plates for roofing.

The pallet dimensions, including the stacked plates, are: 1250mm wide, 3000mm long and 220mm high. The process used to be manual, but Pruszyński wanted to automate the packaging line and therefore chose a RoRo StretchPack®solution developed by Tentoma.

"The solution includes an infeed conveyor with a moveable tongue which carries the heavy pallet through the RoRo StretchPack® machine. Pruszyński now benefits from a fully automated horizontal packaging process for six-sided sealed packaging, protecting its products against external elements," explained Tentoma A/S.

RoRo StretchPack®is sealed horizontal packaging using tubular stretch film. It is based on the stretch hood packaging technology for vertical hooding of, for example, pallets. Tentoma has refined the technology for horizontal packaging, which is designed for 100% sealed packaging in one operation.

“RoRo StretchPack® is ideal for the 100% waterproof packaging of rolls, building materials and similar larger products. The stretch film prevents excessive film use and when customers shift to RoRo StretchPack®, they typically see reductions in film consumption of between 25-60% compared to conventional packaging methods," added Tentoma.
