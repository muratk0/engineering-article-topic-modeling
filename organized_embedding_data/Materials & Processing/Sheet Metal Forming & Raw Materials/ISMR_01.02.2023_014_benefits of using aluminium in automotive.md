# Benefits of using aluminium in automotive

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

■Strength/weight ratio.

■Energy absorption for safety parts.

■Surface quality for trims.

■Conductivity for high-voltage battery cables.

■Recyclability.

Examples of car parts that can be made with aluminium extrusions include side sills; crush cans; front crash members; rear bumpers; subframes; battery boxes and exterior details such as roof rails and trims.

Hydro is committed to delivering a 30 percent reduction in its CO₂ emissions by 2030. For primary aluminium production, emission reductions will come from reducing emissions in its alumina production facilities in Alunorte, Brazil, and through further optimisation of its electrolysis and cast-house operations. Hydro is developing carbon capture solutions for its existing aluminium production facilities while spearheading a new, zero-carbon primary aluminium technology. It aims to have industrial scale pilots for such technologies by 2030.
