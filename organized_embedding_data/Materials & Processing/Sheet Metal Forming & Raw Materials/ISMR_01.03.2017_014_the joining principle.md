Fasteners hold components within equipment and prevent the structure from disassembling. They have now evolved into multi-purpose and highly engineered components. These products are expected to witness significant demand over the years to come as they increase the durability of joined parts, and can be used on different kinds of substrates such as metals and plastics.

Stainless steel, carbon steel and alloy steel are the major steel fasteners used in industries. Automotive fasteners can also be made from aluminium, brass, nickel or plastic.

Industrial fasteners are used in various industries such as aerospace, automotive, construction, oilfield, turbine and power generation, chemical refining and marine. Commonly used fasteners include nails, screws, nuts, bolts, clips, rivets, pins and washers.

Self-clinch fastener ranges include a variety of studs, nuts, stand-offs and panel fasteners. The basic requirements for fasteners are that they must be strong enough to handle loads.

The global market for industrial fasteners is projected to reach US$ 83.8 billion by 2020, according to a 2016 market report by GIA. This is reportedly being driven by 'the improvement in the global economy as well as reviving automotive and manufacturing sectors'. Other factors forecast to fuel growth in the industrial fasteners sector are 'growing demand from developing regions as well as growth in construction, automotive, electronic/ electrical and aerospace sectors'.

"The miniaturisation of devices has spurred the advent of the micro fastener, while there is also increasing use of innovative threaded and plastic fasteners. Demand for industrial fasteners is also set to be driven by other end-use sectors such as construction, domestic appliances and mechanical engineering," said the report's authors, Global Industry Analysts Inc. (GIA).

They include critical fasteners; machine screws; thread forming and screw & washer assembly. Aerospace grade fasteners are projected to witness the fastest growth over the forecast period, in the report, owing to their increasing use in aircraft engines; baggage racks; cabin doors; cargo containers; floor panels; bulkhead and cabin structures.

## An eye on aerospace

Aerospace fasteners are broadly used in commercial aircraft, military aircraft, ballistic missiles, rockets and satellites. Aerospace fasteners are made under strict regulations and standards so as to withstand high fatigue loading and drastic temperature changes. Lightweight and high-strength fasteners help to enhance the fuel efficiency of aircraft. The number of fasteners required in a typical aircraft could reach millions in the form of nuts, bolts, pins, rivets, washers etc.

According to FMI (Future Market Insights), on the basis of product type, aerospace fasteners can be segmented as pins, bolts, studs, screws and rivets. On the basis of material, they can be segmented as aluminium, steel, super alloys and titanium. On the basis of head type, aerospace fasteners can be segmented as 12 points (Double Hex); 6 lobe recess; Dee; Rectangular; Screw and others. Based on type of fixing, the market can be segmented as 'permanent fixing' or 'temporary fixing'.

In Market and Markets' latest analysis, the aerospace fasteners market is projected to grow from US$ 5.49 billion in 2016 to US$ 7.73 billion by 2021, at a CAGR of 7.08%. The base year considered for the report is 2015, and the forecast period is 2016 to 2021.

"The rivets segment is estimated to account for the largest market share during the forecast period. They are used extensively during the manufacturing and maintenance process as they have high tensile strength and the ability to affix critical structural components together. Considering the requirements for fuel-efficient and lightweight aircraft, there is a huge demand for precision-made, technologically sound, lightweight and high-performance fasteners. Innovations in research and development for lightweight and high performance aerospace fasteners will drive this market in the coming years," said Markets and Markets.

"The wide-body aircraft segment is anticipated to grow at the highest pace during the forecast period and dominate the market owing to the efficiency of wide body aircraft and the rise in passenger traffic. Increasing connectivity between important geographical locations of developing and developed regions has led to a rise in demand for wide body aircraft. The aerospace fasteners market is on the rise in the Asia-Pacific region due to the presence of aircraft manufacturers in China and Russia. The rise in number of component manufacturers in Japan, India and China is also one of the factors driving the growth of aerospace fasteners market in this region."
