# AWL-Techniek invests in 3D metal printing

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

AWL HAS INSTALLED what it calls 'an innovative 3D metal printing system' at its headquarters in Harderwijk (The Netherlands). The company is a worldwide partner for automated joining solutions.

Over the last few years, it has been gathering experience in metal and plastics printing. In addition to the two 3D printers that the company is already using, this new 3D metal printing system is an investment in the future.

"The printer, the Desktop Metal Studio System+, is the first of its kind in the Benelux and one of the first in Europe," it told ISMR.

Wouter Zweers, technology manager at AWL, said of the DM Studio: "The 3D metal printer provides unprecedented new possibilities. The freedom in design and the short period of time from idea to product are essential for AWL. We see a great future in the additive production of metal parts and this is an important reason for this investment. It allows us to provide our customers with quick reliable solutions. We see great opportunities in applications such as robot grippers and welding fixtures, as well as in support of our R&D activities."

Luuk Nollet, development engineer at AWL, added: "There is no need to handle dangerous metal powders and requirements for facilities are limited. At the same time, the system delivers high-quality parts and the technology offers a future promise of scalability to high production levels. Looking to the long-term future, we expect 3D printing technologies to become part of our machines and services to our customers. Developing our competence and experience in this field will help us to prepare."
