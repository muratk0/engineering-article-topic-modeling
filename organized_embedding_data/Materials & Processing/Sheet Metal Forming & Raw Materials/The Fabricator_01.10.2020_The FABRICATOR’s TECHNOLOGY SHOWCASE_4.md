RAS Systems LLC is the North American Sales and Service entity of RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH of Germany that has been producing fabricating machinery for over 77 years.

We are known for our world-class innovation in the sheet metal industry and offer many specialized and optimized machines for bending, cutting, and forming. Our fabricating machines and software are built to save you time and money, while delivering exceptional results every time. Choose from our diversified offering of CNC Metal Folders and Metal Panel Bending Systems that provide different application solutions.

The UpDownCenter is a semi-automated bending center for panels that is ideal for thin and medium material thickness.

The semi-automatic XLTbend is the perfect fit for those who want to form complex precision sheet metal parts like cassettes and panels profitably even in small batches.
