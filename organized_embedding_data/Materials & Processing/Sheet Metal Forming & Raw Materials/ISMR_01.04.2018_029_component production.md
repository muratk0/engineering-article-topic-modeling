# Component production

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

DAWSON SHANAHAN, A SPECIALIST in precision engineering, will be showcasing the company's total precision engineering solution – 'the future of component manufacture' - at Automechanika in the UK (June 2018). It will be championing total precision engineering at Automechanika.

"We will be demonstrating the future of component manufacture for the automotive sector by showing how to achieve faster turnaround times, ensure high component quality and minimise costs through a fully integrated, one-stop solution that covers the full automotive component lifecycle. Visitors to our stand will also be able to see a wide range of other precision engineering services, including a selection of machining techniques and an unrivalled cold forming process," it told ISMR.

Visitors to the exhibition will find Dawson Shanahan in the SMMT area, at Stand C02.

Further information on Dawson Shanahan is available at www.dawson-shanahan.co.uk
