## Cladding for Hackney sixth-form centre

BIZ Engineering was contracted to manufacture a bespoke cladding cassette system for the new Hackney Sixth Form Centre. This initially involved modelling the panel system in SolidWorks based on architectural drawings supplied by the client. Panels were punched and laser cut from 3mm thick aluminium, then welded and powder coated before final delivery to site.
