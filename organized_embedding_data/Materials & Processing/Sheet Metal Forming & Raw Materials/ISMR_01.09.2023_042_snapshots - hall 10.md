## ATHADER SL

## www.athader.com

Athader, a company in the Bradbury Group, is a Spanish manufacturer of coil-processing lines; slitting lines, cut-tolength levelling lines; recoiling lines and coil/sheet automated packaging systems.

"In 2022, we successfully supplied our first complete cut-to-length line with stretch leveller (Leveltek International) as well as our second retrofit of an existing roller leveller and cut-to-length line into a hybrid cut-to-length line with roller leveller and Leveltek stretch leveller. Both lines were installed in Poland. We also commissioned our first heavy-gauge slitting line in the United States," commented Athader.

"In 2023, we delivered a coil-to-coil levelling line featuring an Athader edge trimmer (up to 175mm per side) with scrap chopper; two Bradbury levellers with Flat Trak® CL; an Athader rotary parting shear and Lamiflex robotic ID wrapping. The line will provide continuous processing of aluminium coil for Texarkana Aluminium in Texas (USA)," it concluded.

## FIMIGroup

## www.fimimachinery.com

FIMIGroup offers sixty years of expertise and a tailored range of solutions designed to meet the most demanding requests. FIMI coil-processing lines can handle a wide range of materials from ferrous to non-ferrous including steel; high and ultra-high-strength steel; stainless steel; pre-painted steel; aluminium; copper and brass.

"The FIMIGroup is not just a simple manufacturer of metalprocessing equipment and equipment but a high value-added group of companies made up of three divisions, eight sites and 200 employees with over 1,500 lines installed all over the world," it explained.

Its core business is the design, construction and start-up of machines and equipment for the processing and treatment of coilrolled metals in different types of material.

"Our products evolve through synergy between us and our customers from the first planning phases to complete development of the equipment," it concluded.

## Marcegaglia

## www.marcegaglia.com

Marcegaglia is the largest independent global operator in the steel-processing industry with a product range that covers everything from carbon to stainless steel, from long to flat products and from commodity to specialty. Established in 1959 by Steno Marcegaglia in the town of Gazoldo degli Ippoliti (Mantua, Italy), today the Group is run by his children Antonio and Emma Marcegaglia. Marcegaglia Group has created a network of 37 production plants, with a logistics and distribution network.

In January 2023, the company acquired an electric furnace for special steel products in Sheffield, UK; the first primary production asset in the Group's history. Marcegaglia invested with a view to shortening and stabilising supply chains and developing increasingly sustainable and competitive products.

Marcegaglia Carbon Steel is a group company which manufactures flat products (coil, strip and sheet), carbon and pre-painted (PPGI) steel and carbon steel welded tubes.

## MARKATOR Manfred Borries GmbH

## www.markator.de

MARKATOR®, the German manufacturer of dot peen and scribe marking systems for durable markings on metals and plastics, offers hand-held and bench-top marking systems as well as systems to integrate into automatic production lines.

"Nearly every industrial product needs to be marked with a serial number, VIN number, company logo or Data Matrix code for traceability. MARKATOR® systems have been designed for ease of use in multiple applications," it commented.

One of its latest developments is the mobile dot peen marker, FlyMarker® mini 65/30 4.3" touch.

"With its new touch operating concept, customers are offered a modern upgrade and operation has been further simplified. It has a large 4.3" colour display and a touch user interface which, using intuitive software and a newly designed main level, makes programming easier and faster. A faster processor also ensures smooth menu navigation and increased boot time," it explained.
