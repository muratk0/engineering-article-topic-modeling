# The resistance challenge

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

Recently, the AGCO Corporation celebrated the production of its 100th Fendt Katana forage harvester, rounding out its line of agricultural machinery. Solvaro was responsible for developing an assembly to protect the cooling system. After extensive testing, the company was able to begin series production with a cone-shaped sieve box for reliable resistance to contaminants.

Continuous operation for tractors, combines, forage harvesters etc. means that agricultural machinery has to do its job reliably. Heat, dirt, dust and moisture must not be allowed to impact its performance in any way. Every failure costs money – and in many cases, the entire harvest. One of the challenges that forage harvesters, such as the Fendt Katana, face is dust. When large quantities of forage are processed in a short period of time, this generates a huge cloud of dust. Conventional contaminant grilles let the dust in; finer grilles are in danger of clogging.

The cone-shaped sieve box from Solvaro, the manufacturer of metal components based in Kirchheim, features a grille with an air passage of over 60% to cool the 653-horsepower engine. The perforation used, HV 2–2.5, also guarantees reliable filter function. At a diameter of 1380mm, the dimensions of the cone posed a manufacturing challenge. Solvaro prevented the ‘frog effect’ by using a material thickness of 1.2mm.

A cone of this size can no longer be made from one piece so the Solvaro technicians developed a joining technique for the perforated metal component specifically for this application. It does not reduce the throughput quantity and the brush on the rotating suction device is worn down evenly across the cone, even at the joints. For rust protection, the entire assembly is chamfered and KTL- and powder-coated.
