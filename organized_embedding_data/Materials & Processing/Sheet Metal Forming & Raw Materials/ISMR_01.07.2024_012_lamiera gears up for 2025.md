# LAMIERA gears up for 2025

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

From 6-9 May 2025, Fieramilano Rho in Milan (Italy) will host the twenty-third edition of LAMIERA, the international exhibition dedicated to sheet metal forming and cutting machine tools and innovative technologies related to the sector. Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association) and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU, LAMIERA is the only trade show in Italy exclusively dedicated to the metal forming sector.

Machinery; equipment for processing sheet metal, tubes, profiles, wires and structural metal fabrication; presses; moulds and dies; moulding; surface treatments and finishing; parts; components; accessories; tooling; fastener materials; technical and industrial subcontracting form the heart of the exhibition. LAMIERA will showcase the latest sheet metal technologies. The event will also highlight various technological themes relating to machine tools.

There will be six exhibition areas. FABBRICA FUTURA will be dedicated to software and technologies for connectivity, data management and automation and to digital systems and robotics applied to sheet metal processing. SALDATECH will offer an overview of the wide and diversified world of welding, one of the most important sheetmetal joining technologies. FASTENER INDUSTRY will showcase screws, bolts, fastening and clamping systems.

WORLD OF HEAT AND SURFACE TREATMENTS will host machines, systems, accessories and consumables focused on pre- and post-production treatments, for ferrous and non-ferrous materials. METROLOGY & TESTING will be centred on testing, measurement and quality-control systems and machines. BLECH ITALY SERVICE focuses on technical subcontracting and services for the sector's industry.

In addition, starting this year, LAMIERA will propose a Revamping & Retrofitting area focused on machines and equipment that have been adequately renewed and equipped with new functionalities to meet the needs of a market that is complementary to that of 'new machinery'. The LAMIALAMIERA area of the trade show will offer a comprehensive meeting programme covering relevant topics for the sector as well as educational initiatives.

"International and domestic market trends are expected to grow for the entire three-year period from 2025-2027, and the availability of Transition 5.0 and 4.0 tax incentives throughout 2025 will certainly favour all operators who decide to exhibit at LAMIERA, which is hosted in the world's third largest consumer market," commented Alfredo Mariotti, General Manager, UCIMU.

In 2023, over a total floor space of 40,000 square metres, LAMIERA attracted approximately 400 exhibiting companies (of which 27% were from outside Italy), representing 22 countries.
