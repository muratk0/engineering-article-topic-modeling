# Metal strip processing

[TARİH: 01.04.2021 ISMR]

After taking over UNGERER GmbH in 2017, REDEX Group has acquired strategic patents from BWG GmbH. As a result, it is now able to offer a large portfolio of metal strip processing solutions, from foil to heavy gauge coils.

Aiming to develop new technologies and support metal strip producers worldwide, REDEX Group has also strengthened its R&D by opening an engineering centre in Duisburg.

"Modern production and assembly facilities in France (Ferrieres) and Germany (Pforzheim), as well as service centres in China (Shanghai) and the USA (New Jersey) allows us to offer full service, from engineering to key equipment through to complete strip processing lines, and from automation or measurement and control systems to spare parts and training," it told ISMR.

The portfolio of metal strip processing solutions from REDEX

Group includes consulting; engineering and line modernisation; complete process lines (heat treatment, coating, cleaning etc.); finishing lines (tension levelling, slitting, cut-to-length); in-line flatness measurement and closed loop control systems, as well as key equipment (skin-pass mills, flying shears, stacking units, edge trimmers etc.) and tooling (20-high mill backing assemblies, leveller cassettes and working rolls).

REDEX Group is a 70 year-old family owned European group with facilities in Europe, USA, China and India. With more than 100 engineers and 450 employees worldwide and a portfolio of 40 patents, it enjoys a global footprint and offers expertise on key industrial processes.
