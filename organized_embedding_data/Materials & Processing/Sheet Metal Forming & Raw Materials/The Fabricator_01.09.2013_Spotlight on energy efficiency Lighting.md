Induction light delivers a broad footprint

The AGT HZ02 induction light fixture from American Green Technology delivers a broad and diffused, rather than pinpointed, footprint of crisp, white light at low operating temperature.

According to the company, the unit saves 50 to 70 percent of energy consumption and nearly eliminates maintenance. It is suitable for harsh, heavy industrial applications, such as shipyards, mines, mills, grain barns, and petroleum and chemical processing plants. The housing, which measures 16.5 in. high by 12.5 in. wide, is polyester powder-coated diecast aluminum.

American Green Technology, 269-340-9975,

www.agtus.org

LED high-bay ensures high light output in hot environments

Acuity Brands® Inc. has introduced the Phuzion™ LED high-bay from Holophane®. The luminaire is designed to achieve and maintain high light output at high ambient temperatures. Its prismatic glass optical system helps ensure high-quality volumetric light distribution that will not degrade over time, says the company.

The luminaire’s vented design helps maximize natural airflow over electrical components to produce thermal protection at ambient temperatures up to 149 degrees F. At the same time, the prismatic borosilicate glass optics helps improve vertical lighting levels while keeping horizontal lighting strong and uniform. The glass refractors resist chemicals, detergents, dust, and dirt. Designed as an alternative to 250- to 1,000-W high-intensity discharge fixtures, the luminaires are suitable for maximum spacing within a range of indoor applications, including heavy industrial or light manufacturing and warehouses. They can be used at mounting heights from 15 to 60 ft. The embedded Sensor Switch® controls can sense occupancy and daylight levels to reduce energy consumption during periods of inactivity or when natural light is present.

Acuity Brands Inc., 866-759-1577,

www.holophane.com

High-bay lighting offers 91+ lumens per watt

Cooper Lighting offers the Metalux LED high-bay series for industrial, manufacturing, and commercial applications. It features precision-designed optics that are available in multiple distributions, three lumen packages (9,000, 18,000 and 23,000), and two color temperatures (4,000 K and 5,000 K).

The LED is designed for multiple mounting heights—even some low-bay applications. According to the company, the proprietary low-power, low-brightness LED module assembly offers optical performance equivalent to or greater than fluorescent systems, with the benefits of LED lighting including energy savings, extended system life, and a reduced carbon footprint. The very high-efficiency LED offers 91+ lumens/W and provides savings as great as 43 percent when compared to traditional HID luminaires, claims the company. It features both general and aisle distributions and has a 55-degree-C ambient rating and 80+ CRI.

Options include an integral occupancy sensor; multiple mounting options (pendant, chain, toggle, hook, and loop), and multiple lens choices. A door frame and lens assembly option is available for demanding environments.

In addition, the company’s F-bay modular power supply option allows external access for safe and easy servicing, says the company. There is no need to remove lamps or reflectors to disconnect the fixture’s power. The LED is on the DesignLights Consortium®-qualified product list and qualifies for energy rebates. The fixture is designed to last 50,000 hours at 70 percent lumen maintenance.

Cooper Lighting, 770-486-4800,

www.cooperlighting.com

LED lighting fixture can replace 250- to 1,500-W HIDs

GE Lighting has introduced the Albeo™ ABHX series high-bay LED lighting fixture for warehouses, industrial spaces, and other commercial settings with high ceilings.

The energy-efficient fixture can replace a range of legacy high-bay lighting systems—250- to 1,500-W, high-intensity discharge and four- to eight-lamp T5/T8 high-intensity fluorescent lighting, which the company says burn out after 20,000 to 30,000 hours. The fixture’s life is rated for 100,000 hours at L70, diminishing maintenance needs in areas that are difficult to reach and where swapping bulbs often requires special equipment and trained personnel. It offers precise illumination, which often is needed to locate materials and pack boxes in storage and warehouse settings.

GE Lighting, 800-435-2677,

www.gelighting.com

Induction lighting lasts 100,000 hours

Future Energy Solutions offers the HB-1701 series of prismatic high-bay lighting. The 100,000-hour induction lighting is designed to reduce maintenance costs and offers high lumen output combined with excellent color and reduced wattage to provide high-quality light with energy savings, the company states.

The product features a die-cast aluminum housing with a ½-in. hub and gray finish; 22-in. ribbed prismatic polycarbonate refractor; Instant On™ flicker-free cold start and hot restart; high power factor, low THD replaceable ballast; concealed continuous gasket seals against harmful dust, dirt, moisture, and insects; advanced phosphors for high lumen maintenance and high lumen output; and as many as 20 years’ maintenance-free operation.The lighting is surface-, pendant-, or chain-mount.

Future Energy Solutions, 954-660-1552,

www.feslighting.com

LED lighting fixture provides 12,483 lumens using 135 W

Precision-Paragon [P2] offers the new QHC, a high-bay LED lighting fixture. The fixture uses the latest LED technology to provide 12,483 lumens using 135 W, less than 30 percent of the electricity required for an equivalent traditional metal halide fixture. The two-piece cast aluminum housing is smaller, lighter, and more compact for easier installation and serviceability, says the company.

The fixture is designed to maximize performance and reduce wattage in unconditioned areas where heat can be an issue. An integrated, vertical, heat-sink structure with flow-through cooling reduces LED junction temperatures for enhanced reliability.

Low wattage, combined with intelligent, integrated controls featuring instant restrike and occupancy sensors, can reduce energy usage.

Its modular design allows for multiple chassis configurations as well as a reported L70 of over 60,000 hours and calculated L70 of 153,000 hours per TM-21.

The fixture features configurable light distribution to accommodate a variety of industrial and commercial lighting applications. Its optics distribute light evenly with a low maximum/minimum ratio to meet Illuminating Engineering Society of North America (IESNA) light-level standards. Fixtures for wet and dry applications are available.

Precision-Paragon [P2],

714-386-5550,

www.p-2.com

Fluorescent lamps last up to 84,000 hours

Osram Sylvania offers OCTRON® 800 XP® XL (eXtended Life) SUPER-SAVER® ECOLOGIC®3 T8 fluorescent lamps. The lamps provide up to 84,000 hours on T8 ANSI-rated programmed rapid-start ballasts—up to 2.7 times longer life than standard T8 systems, according to the manufacturer.

Available in 28- and 25-W types and designed with lead-free glass, the lamps are suitable for a variety of industrial and commercial settings in recessed-troffer, well-washing, stairways, direct and indirect pendant, and linear-recessed applications.

Manufactured in the U.S., the lamps provide up to 96 percent mean lumens with a color rendering index of up to 85.

They are available in color temperatures from 3,000K to 5,000K. The lead-free lamps are TCLP and RoHS compliant.

For increased energy savings, the lamps can be coupled with the high-efficiency QUICKTRONIC® ballast and use POWERSENSE® dimming.

Osram Sylvania Inc., 978-777-1900,

www.sylvania.com

LED light is an alternative to metal halide fixtures

USAI offers the BevLED® 5.0 LED fixture suitable for high-ceiling applications. Delivering 4,800 lumens from 80 W (60 lumens/W) and 5,565 sourced lumens (3,000K) within a streamlined 6-in. aperture, it serves as an alternative to metal halide fixtures.

The fixture also offers 10- to 80-degree beam spreads and a 10-degree optic with a center beam candlepower (CBCP) of more than 40,000.

Tubular daylight device lets more light in

Solatube Intl. Inc. offers the SkyVault series M74 DS day-lighting system. This large tubular daylighting device has a 29-in.-dia. opening to the sky that lets more light to enter an area, and more daylight means higher daytime illumination and greater spacing between units with fewer roof penetrations.

The system offers maximum output while minimizing impact on the building envelope and reducing installation costs. It has temperature control with insulated and sealed, curb-mounted roof assemblies that minimize heat gain and heat loss through the roof. It is suitable for large-volume spaces with high ceilings.

LED fixtures rated for hazardous environments

Hubbell Lighting introduced two industrial LED fixtures classified for severe conditions: HBL LED Highbay and Kemlux III LED luminaire.

The hazardous-rated HBL high-bay lighting carries a Class I, Division 2 rating. It offers efficacies as high as 100 lumens per watt. Its cast construction and sealed design make it suitable for hazardous locations.

The Kemlux line includes an LED tank with high-output LED counts of 18, 24, and 36. The tank is available for installation as a new fixture or as a retrofit to existing Kemlux III fixtures.

High-bay luminaires designed to reduce energy use up to 90 percent

The Sensity Systems LED high-bay luminaire is designed to help reduce energy use by up to 90 percent compared to conventional lighting, the company reports, while improving light levels and safety in warehouses, factories, and other facilities.

The products, which achieve up to 94 lumens/W of luminaire efficacy, are available with 3 to 12 LED petals per luminaire. The LED petals are arranged in a circular fashion around the bottom of a driver box that holds the AC power supplies.

The luminaire is part of the Net-Sense Platform, which is the manufacturer’s implementation of the Light Sensory Network, a new class of digital sensor network that helps improve energy efficiencies.

LED system can make every light intelligent

Digital Lumens launched a strategic partner program to bring integrated intelligence to industrial lighting applications.

The company has extended its LightRules™ web-based software platform for managing and controlling LED lighting fixtures to a range of industrial applications. As the cornerstone of this new initiative, the company will integrate its patented Digital Light Agent (DLA) technology into other manufacturers’ LED fixtures.

DLA technology makes ordinary LED fixtures intelligent, controllable, and more energy efficient, the company states. A key part of this strategy involves the creation of an open, standards-based control interface.

Luminaire produces 9,649 lumens

LED Living Technology’s Helio high-bay LED luminaires are designed to be an energy-efficient alternative to halogen, mercury-vapor, high-pressure sodium, and metal-halide lighting technologies. The 80- and 120-W lighting produces 7,040 and 9,649 lumens, respectively, in a Type V aisle lighting optical distribution suitable for factory or warehouse aisle lighting applications.

With die-cast aluminum housings and LEDs mounted directly to the heat sink, the lighting is designed with thermal management to help maximize lifetime.

The fixtures are used in industrial applications with mounting heights more than 15 ft., lensed to distribute lumens where they are needed the most for aisle lighting. The systems feature intelligent sensor controllability for 0- to 10-V dimming and step dimming. The company’s products are Buy American compliant, making projects eligible for 179D tax incentives.
