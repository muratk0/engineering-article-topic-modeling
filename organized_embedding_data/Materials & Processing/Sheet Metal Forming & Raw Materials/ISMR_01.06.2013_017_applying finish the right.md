# APPLYING FINISH THE RIGHT

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

BASF outlines the latest trends, research and market developments for coil coated sheet metal products

ISMR SAYS:

“Coil coated sheet metal products are in high demand for sectors such as automotive, construction and household goods”

BASF is the world’s leading chemical company. With more than 110,000 employees, six Verbund sites and approximately 380 additional production sites worldwide, it posted sales of €72.1 billion and income before special items of approximately €6.6 billion in 2012. It combines ‘economic success, social responsibility and environmental protection’ to serve its customers.

Coatings for a wide range of applications are one of its key business sectors. The international Coatings Division of the BASF Group develops, produces and markets a high quality portfolio of innovative automotive OEM coatings, automotive refinishes and industrial coatings as well as decorative paints. In 2012, the Coatings Division achieved world sales of €3 billion.

## Coating in focus

The industrial coatings industry is one of the most diverse served by BASF Coatings. It requires a broad range of curing technologies, chemistries, properties and coating processes. Coatings need to be suitable for different substrates, including metal, plastic and composite, and are applied on wind blades, trains, pipe and protective coatings. Industrial coatings therefore need to offer a range of different properties including a balanced level of hardness and toughness, high chemical resistance, easy processing, low VOC and good appearance.

Amongst other features, coatings for automotive and transport applications need to be easy to apply, quick to dry, able to withstand the weather, be low in odour and resistant to scratches and scrapes. But manufacturers also want a wide variety of shades, effects and finishes to produce impressive and attractive car bodywork. Fortunately, BASF has the expertise and experience to be able to provide and further develop raw materials for automotive coating products that fulfil these requirements.

“Coil coated sheet metal is tough, easy to process and highly versatile in its applications. For this reason these materials are in high demand, particularly in the construction industry, for example for façade and roof elements made of steel or aluminium. Coil coated metal is also indispensable for the manufacture of household products such as home appliances, light fixtures and brown goods. Our coating products are not only used for the optimum surface protection they offer. The colours and special effects made possible by BASF Coatings products also enable architects to create new impacts repeatedly i.e. building colours matching the company logo and metallic effects etc.,” BASF told ISMR.

“Coil coating is not only an issue of corrosion protection but of aesthetic and other functionalities. Our main market in the coil coatings sector is still Europe. The customers are for example in the UK, France, Belgium, The Netherlands, Germany and Austria. Emerging markets, such as Russia or Belarus, are of increasing importance. As a result, we will boost our activities in these countries and offer innovations for our current core business as well as for new markets,” BASF added.

## An eye on the environment

“There is a worldwide trend in the global coating arena for a greater focus on sustainability issues. This is a very positive opportunity for us since we have been engaged in the sustainability arena for many years. To us, sustainability is a lot more than just a trend. It is a huge opportunity in many ways. Sustainability is a key factor for growth and value creation – this is why we made it one of the strategic principles in our ‘We create chemistry’ strategy,” BASF told ISMR.

"At BASF Coatings, we committed ourselves to the environmental sustainability of our products in the coil coatings sector early on and were the first company worldwide to introduce a chromate-free alternative to traditional primers. We have been the global market leader in chromate-free universal primers for a long time and have already developed the third generation of this product.

“The improved chromate-free universal primer offers better surface cover and efficiency, along with higher eco-efficiency, than its predecessors. At the same time, it effectively protects metal surfaces against corrosion. Since it can be used for nearly all galvanized substrates and topcoat technologies, it accelerates and streamlines the production process. This saves time and money and is good for the environment.”

## New product development

One of BASF’s main successes is its continuous product development over the last few years. In 2009, it launched its new coil coatings topcoat system, POLYCERAM® Plus, for example. With the POLYCERAM® Plus topcoat system for coil coating applications, BASF offers customers tailor-made products for coating aluminium and steel coils.

“The special properties of POLYCERAM Plus paints include UV resistance, flexibility, structure and hardness. In the new range of products offered, we focused particularly on the weather resistance, the favourable surface characteristics and the high robustness in the application. Four UV classes defined under European standard EN 10169 and the flexibility of the coating were applied as criteria for positioning the different products within the new topcoat system,” BASF explained.

Using precisely formulated pigment pastes, BASF experts can produce nearly any imaginable colour and gloss level – either at major BASF production sites or on-site with customers using local technical service centres, known as Pevicoat® plants. BASF Coatings Pevicoat plants can produce any colour nuances in exactly the amount needed in 24 hours, the company told ISMR.

“With Pevicoat, the paint factory comes directly to our customer’s coating line. This computer-assisted mixing system can mix any colour and gloss level in a very short time – and supply the exact quantity of precisely reproducible material. This allows coaters to respond quickly and flexibly to customer requests or technical necessities, whilst saving costs. Pevicoat speeds up production processes, optimises production planning and means that no warehousing is required. Minimum purchase quantities are also a thing of the past," BASF Coatings explained.

## Looking to the future

BASF is also investing in infrastructure, and not just in the coil coatings segment. BASF has now set up a new Coatings Technical Competence Centre ASEAN in Bangkok, Thailand. This new facility supports technical and laboratory activities mainly in motorcycle coatings including technology transfer, product development, performance testing, colour design and development, and houses a sales and marketing team as well as a technical service team of more than 20 professionals, all catering to motorcycle manufacturers in the ASEAN region.

Looking towards the future, service has now become more crucial and a key differentiator, BASF explained.

“We provide our customers with much more than high-quality paint products. As a true partner, we support them with a whole range of services related to coatings and paint technology. We are also happy to share our knowledge and expertise with our customers – in workshops, at conferences or in individual consultation.

“Collaboration with our customers is the central element of our corporate activities in BASF Coatings’ worldwide operations. This close partnership, our global presence and our expertise to develop innovative solutions are the pillars of our success,” the company concluded. ■
