# MAKING ITS MARK

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

THE NEW ME3000PC from UMS delivers fast, semi-automated electrochemical marking for high contrast quality marks on most metals (suitable for marking serial numbers, date/batch codes, datamatrix marks and even logos).

“Whether you need to mark the smallest character on the most awkwardly-shaped component or a large logo on a piece of sheet metal, the ME3000 series can do it all,” UMS explained.“Rapid marking speeds range from 0.25 seconds to 4 seconds for most sized marks and a wide range of long life or disposable stencils are available.”

The small, portable unit marks thin-walled section, from very small to large and awkwardly-shaped parts, onto flat, round, concave or convex surfaces including text on an arc. See www.ums.co.uk
