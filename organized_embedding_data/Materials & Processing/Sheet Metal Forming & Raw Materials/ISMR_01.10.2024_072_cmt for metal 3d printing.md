# CMT for metal 3D printing

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

"Metal 3D printing is on the rise in many industries: this process saves a lot of time and material, providing enormous flexibility when designing and adapting different components," commented Austrian welding specialist, Fronius.

With CMT Additive Pro, Fronius is presenting a 3D-optimised welding process with features such as an especially even layer structure as well as stability. With CMT (Cold Metal Transfer), Fronius has been offering a stable, controllable and comparatively cool welding process for decades. It has now optimised CMT specifically for metal 3D printing with Fronius CMT Additive Pro. The integrated arc-on rate stabiliser is designed to ensure a consistent wire speed, resulting in an even and predictable build-up of layers. This, it says, significantly improves the overall stability of the manufacturing process.

"The right welding path with the right welding parameters and processes is crucial for a good result in metal 3D printing," explains Philipp Roithinger, an expert in additive manufacturing at Fronius International GmbH. “However, this requires a high-performance welding system that precisely ensures the accuracy of the welding torch."

The new iWave Multiprocess Pro, combined with the Fronius CMT Additive Pro characteristic, is optimised for metal 3D printing. The complete solution is compatible with common robot systems for additive manufacturing. Adjustable heat input ensures consistency in the height and width ratio of the bead. Identical welds can be stacked on top of each other, layer after layer. A Pulsed Hot Start function simultaneously achieves sufficient penetration and a constant layer height by starting welding within the pulsed arc. The entire process is controlled automatically, so no extra settings are necessary.
