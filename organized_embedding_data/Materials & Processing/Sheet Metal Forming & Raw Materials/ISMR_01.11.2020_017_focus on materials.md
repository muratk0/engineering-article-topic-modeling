# FOCUS ON MATERIALS

“Tensil-Pro and Smart Line enable lower production costs using energy savings, as well as productivity increases, to obtain greater homogeneity of mechanical characteristics (particularly for the automotive sector) and to study quality and process trends as input material is varied,” said Marcegaglia.

“Among other process innovations, it is also important to mention our ‘Improvement of Flatness and Defect Reduction of Cold-Rolled Products through Neural Network Models’ project. This was developed in collaboration with Italian (CSM, Istituto S. Anna of Pisa) and European (BFI, CETIC) research centres and concerns the development of a special Industry 4.0 platform through machine learning models for the prediction of specific defects,” it added.

Innovative products at an advanced stage of development include the ‘Fully Processed (NGOES) Non-Grain Oriented Magnetic Steel’ R&D project.

“In Ravenna, a production cycle has also been defined with some significantly innovative elements that, from the first industrial tests, have improved magnetic properties compared to conventional cycles. Thanks to this R&D project, the first magnetic steel coils in Italy were produced (at an industrial level) after almost 20 years of total absence of production,” Marcegaglia told ISMR.

As far as cost reduction is concerned, Marcegaglia Carbon Steel cites its R&D projects (“recovery annealed” and “fully annealed”) for structural steel production. These projects have enabled the company to define manufacturing cycles for products with significantly higher quality, profitability and productivity, according to the company.

## Marcegaglia Digital Print

“Last but not least, our pre-painted processed products have seen spikes in growth thanks to new projects designed to implement special coating cycles with high added value, such as Marcegaglia Digital Print. This is a printing solution for any kind of project, a technology that enables us to offer a wide range of products aimed mainly at the design and interior fittings sector, with a focus on both residential and naval construction. Digital Print enables printing directly on steel, ensuring a high level of customisation and high detail definition, using in-line printing,” it explained.

Advantages of the new solution, maintains Marcegaglia, include longer image duration; scratch protection and UV protection (RUV) in addition to corrosion resistance (CR). Typical applications for Digital Print include facades; doors; gates; garages; walls; street furniture; cruise ship interiors; household appliances; exterior walls (cubis); suspended ceilings and interior design.

“With our deep knowledge of raw materials and manufacturing processes, we will continue to invest to customise our products to customers’ requirements, so that we remain a leader in carbon steel flat products,” concluded Marcegaglia.
