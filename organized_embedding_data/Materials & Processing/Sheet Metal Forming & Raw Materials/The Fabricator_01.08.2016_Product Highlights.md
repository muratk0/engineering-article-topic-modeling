Flatness control integrates directly onto leveler

Arku offers the FlatJack

®

, a new process for monitoring the flatness of sheet metal parts. The process rapidly checks the flatness of punched, laser-cut, and flame-cut parts with minimal effort before and after leveling.

The process can be integrated directly onto the inlet rollers of the FlatMaster

®

precision leveler. This helps minimize cycle time and costs through reduced nonproductive times in the leveling process, the company states.

The surface of the sheet metal part is tested by the laser integrated into the inlet rollers, regardless of the orientation and position. The measurement equipment traverses the part and measures its flatness. The results are transferred automatically to the leveler’s control system and can be accessed at any time to help ensure complete documentation and reproducibility.

Quick-change thick-turret punching system uses toolless design

Mate has introduced the Ultra

®

QCT™, a quick-change, toolless thick-turret punching system. It is compatible with the company’s Ultra line of guides and standard canisters.

The system features a toolless punch-retention mechanism. The user flips the latch to remove and snap the new insert into place. The punch is keyed at the perimeter of the tool, not the center, to provide angularity control. The shoulder of the punch remains securely guided by the guide when punching. Only one punch driver is needed for rounds or shapes.

The punch insert is made from MPM4™ tool steel. The punch driver is coated with SuperMax™ coating to help minimize wear and preserve fits.

Stationary beveling machine allows hands-free beveling of plate and bar stock

Saar-Hartmetall USA has introduced the MMB 500 stationary beveling machine, suitable for processing small, medium, and large aluminum, steel, and stainless steel plate and bar stock workpieces. Designed for hands-free operation, the machine provides bevel angles from 30 to 60 degrees.

The machine features separate, variable-speed motors for feed-rate adjustment and a milling head with eight four-sided, indexable inserts.

Swing ear sets help simplify removal of parts from press brakes

Wilson Tool International

®

offers swing ear sets for removing parts from press brakes. Suitable for bending boxes with four sides and return flanges, the swing ears are available in most profiles for American-, European-, and WT-style punches. With the addition of swing ears, the punch does not need to be forced out of the box during the final bend because the ears swing out of the way.

Standard swing ear sets are available in 6-in. lengths, and nonstandard profiles and custom lengths are available as made-to-order options for any punch profile. Each set includes a left- and right-hand swing ear section.

40-ton electric press brake offers 60-in. working length

LVD Strippit has introduced the Dyna-Press 40/15 Plus, a compact, high-speed, 40-ton electric press brake. The machine, which offers a working length of 60 in., features a precision 4-axis back-gauge and the Touch-B touchscreen CNC.

The press brake is designed to bend parts at 59 IPM. The ram and servomotors are coupled with two heavy-duty ball screws to distribute force and tonnage evenly across the working length. The electric ram offers smooth transition from approach to working speed and minimizes power consumption through the use of an optimal power-to-inertia motor ratio, says the company. The 15-in. touchscreen control helps minimize operator input while simplifying part programming. With minimal input, the operator can create in 2-D and simulate in 3-D on the touchscreen.
