# Steel tubes for e-methanol production

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

German manufacturer, BUTTING, recently received a piping package, ordered via a partner in the steel-stockholding trade, for an ambitious project in Scandinavia.

“It is the largest project currently under construction for an e-methanol production plant in Europe. The plant is expected to produce several thousand metric tons of e-methanol per year and will go into operation as early as 2025. For us, this means completing the project in a short space of time," outlined the manufacturer.

The scope of delivery includes pipes made of materials 1.4307, 1.4541 and 1.4571 in sizes ranging from DN 150 to DN 500. To be able to deliver on time, longitudinally welded stainless steel tubes are taken from BUTTING's widely diversified warehouse and processed according to the project specifications: Special markings and chamfers on the pipe ends are applied mechanically and the quality is confirmed by extended acceptance tests. Some pipe dimensions are customised by drawing from steel plate stock.

BUTTING's products include stainless-steel welded pipes; clad pipes; customised components; spools and plant construction; vessels, tanks and apparatus as well as assemblies.

E-methanol is a sustainable alternative to fossil fuels, which are currently still used to power large ships. E-methanol is a light fuel that is produced with the help of green electricity from wind and solar energy. The fuel enables the effective storage and transport of renewable energies and represents an important step towards the decarbonisation of shipping to reduce greenhouse gas emissions in the future.
