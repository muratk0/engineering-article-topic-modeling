POSCO flexes muscles

KOREAN STEELMAKER POSCO recently completed an annual 750,000-ton scale continuous galvanizing line at Pohang Steelworks. It can now manufacture various products including plated steel sheet with consistent quality and high corrosion resistance as well as high strength. The newly completed CGL also drastically reduces the use of environmental pollutant materials.

During the CGL construction process, 34 local companies in the Gyeongsangbuk-do Province took on the project. The CGL completion ceremony took place on 5 April 2012. POSCO completed and operated an annual 100,000-ton scale CGL at Pohang Steelworks in 1977 and moved the facilities to Shunde, China in 1997, rebuilding a new CGL at Pohang Steelworks.

The company completed the construction of an annual 150,000-ton scale stainless cold-rolled factory in the Nhon Trach Industrial Complex located near the city of Ho Chi Minh at POSCO VST on 7 March 2012. The factory was completed 13 months after starting construction in December 2010. With its completion, the annual production capacity of POSCO VST, the Vietnam subsidiary, increases to an annual 235,000 tons.

POSCO will install an additional rolling machine by 2015 to increase annual production to 285,000 tons. Once completed, POSCO VST may possibly become Southeast Asia's largest STS cold-rolled product manufacturer alongside Thailand's POSCO Thainox which officially launched in December last year. STS cold-rolled product demand in the Southeast Asia region is forecasted to increase at an average annual rate of 8% from 750,000 tons in 2012, reaching 950,000 tons in 2015. ■
