M.T.C. Bamieh, based in Italy, has specialised in sheetmetal working machines since 1992 and supplied over 100 production lines to Middle Eastern countries.

“We supply standard machines produced by our regular suppliers, customised according to a client's production needs, and special machines made to our own design to meet specific production requirements. This enables us to supply single lines or complete production facilities with minimum cost and the best return on investment to customers. Aftersales service is a priority for us," it said.

The company can supply slitting and cut-to-length lines; welded pipe lines; packaging machines; steel tape; coil preparation systems; serrated bar production in coil or bar and pole/ high mast production lines.
