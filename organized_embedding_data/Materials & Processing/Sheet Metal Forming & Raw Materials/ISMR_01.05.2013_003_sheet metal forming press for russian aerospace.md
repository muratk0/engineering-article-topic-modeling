# SHEET METAL forming press for Russian aerospace

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

Avure Technologies, a specialist in high pressure fluid cell press technology, has received an order from a Russian manufacturer of mid-sized aircraft for a 50,000- ton press-force Flexform™ sheet metal forming press. The agreement stipulates that the press will be delivered to the customer by mid-2014.

“Demand for mid-sized aircraft has increased steadily in recent years,” observes Sture Olsson, Avure’s global business unit director for sheet metal forming. “Avure’s advanced Flexform technology is well suited for the development of more efficient production methods for these types of aircraft, reducing component costs, increasing product quality and improving design flexibility.”

The order indicates that the Russian aerospace industry is determined to revitalise its domestic aircraft production, in line with growing airline interest worldwide in single-aisle aircraft, Avure told ISMR. In this market segment, the recently introduced Russian Superjet 100 and the MS-21 are competing with, for example, the Airbus A320-NEO and the Boeing 737- MAX. These new aircraft designs seek to boost fuel efficiency, enhance safety, increase passenger comfort and improve manufacturing efficiencies in the next generation of commercial aircraft.

In addition to high-pressure Flexform equipment, Avure also offers in-depth tool design and process expertise to help customers realize the full value of highpressure sheet metal forming technology, which is essential to maximise process utilisation. Based in the company’s manufacturing facilities in Västerås, Sweden, Avure is the largest provider of ultra-high pressure systems utilised to form sheet metal, process advanced materials and pasteurize foods without heat or chemicals. It has installed over 1,700 high pressure systems worldwide.
