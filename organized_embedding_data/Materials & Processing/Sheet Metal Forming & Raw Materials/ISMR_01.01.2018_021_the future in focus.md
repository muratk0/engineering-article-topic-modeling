# THE FUTURE IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: "Ermaksan has forged itself a solid profile in the sheet metal working industry"

Ermaksan's sales crew promotes 4G acceleration on the Fibermak Momentum series

With investment/experience and an innovative R&D department, Turkish company Ermaksan has become one of the leading manufacturers in the sheet metal fabrication machinery industry. Its products include fibre laser and plasma cutters, press brakes, punch presses, controllers, 3D printers and shears. With a 100,000m² modern production facility and a qualified team of 800 employees, it offers technical support to customers in 110 countries.

Ermaksan has forged itself a solid profile in the sheet metal working industry. The company has been producing sheet metal fabricating machines since 1980. It exports 80% of its production through agents, under the Ermaksan brand, and has a branch office in Chicago, USA for its marketing, sales and aftersales services for the North American market.

Ersin Yildiz, International Sales Manager, Ermaksan, introduced ISMR to the company's latest innovations.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements?

EY: We recently launched our own laser source, which is 100% designed, engineered and made in-house at our headquarters in Bursa. We have spent over 50 million euros on this investment and have been working on its development for the last six years. We also recently unveiled some new fibre laser cutting machines, with new designs.

We are therefore supplying alternative options for the fibre laser market, which I believe is very good for customers as well as for competitors. When someone does something new or more competitive, it triggers competitors to do more such as produce more advanced or more efficient machines and better solutions so that they can fight for their market share. Eventually, customers benefit from this. We feel that we are doing our share to help the sheet metal forming industry grow.

ISMR: What are your views on the current business climate for sheet metal professionals?

EY: Technology advancement is good because we see more and more energyefficient machines in the market with lower and lower price tags. This makes more professionals think of expanding their business with new, more advanced technology or even more machine tools. There are also a lot of business owners who are currently outsourcing their sheet metal jobs and are now thinking of buying their own equipment.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers and how are you addressing these issues for them?

EY: Machine uptime and service have always been, and should always be, the two most important customer stipulations. Ermaksan, and a lot of other companies, have been innovating in these areas to improve both machine uptime and service. The initial purpose of each innovation should be to improve the machine so that its uptime is better than before. Service should be required less often and delivered in a faster and easier fashion. I believe these will always be the two major elements required by customers.

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus/vision over the next few years?

EY: We have just unveiled some new products, after years of research and development. We are also aware that it is going to take time to leverage our expected market share. We are at the very early stages of development of the Ermaksan CNC controller, Ermaksan laser source and Ermaksan ENA 3D Titanium printer. We will be focusing strongly on these three new products over the next few years.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal markets?

EY: I see a strong trend towards more automated solutions. Producers and service providers are offering more automated solutions. More sheet metal professionals are also asking for failurefree solutions.

ISMR: What was the focus of your booth presence at Blechexpo and FABTECH in 2017? Did you launch any new products at these exhibitions?

EY: The focus of our booth at Blechexpo in Stuttgart, FABTECH in Chicago and MWCS in Shanghai was on our laser source. We also launched a lot of new products at these shows.

HAWK is the new fibre laser cutting machine that we have brought to the market. It can be equipped with 0.5kW or 1kW Ermaksan laser sources. It has no covers but still holds the CE mark – it is probably the first fibre laser cutting machine without covers with the CE mark in the world. It is our budget fibre laser cutting machine solution with a 3 x 1.5m bed size (without shuttle table). Its small footprint, rapid installation and easy-to-use interface make it ideal for job shops who cut thin material.

4G acceleration is now available for the Fibermak Momentum series.

Ermaksan's Fibertube was the second world premiere at Blechexpo. It is a fully automated pipe and profile cutting machine. It can cut material of up to 6m in length and 120mm in diameter.

Our flagship laser cutting system, Fibermak, was also showcased with a new 4G optional upgrade. This offers 4G acceleration to users and is now available for the Fibermak Momentum series.

Ertouch was another world premiere for us. This multi-touchscreen controller is the latest innovation from Ermaksan. At Blechexpo, we fitted it onto a small press brake and demonstrated it to visitors. This controller is 100% designed, engineered and manufactured by Ermaksan, just like our laser source.

We also displayed last year's innovation, the Green Press, at our booth at Blechexpo. We have already sold this press brake model to several customers.
