# Bronx launches new Belarus colour coating line

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

BRONX INTERNATIONAL has successfully completed the installation of a compact warehouse coil paint line in Minsk (Belarus). Commissioning took place during the Baltic summer months and the project was completed in early October 2015. Installation began in January 2015 and led to a successful start-up in October 2015.

A process line asset will help build and grow the Belarusian coated steel market in the many facets of building and construction; roofing and walling; facades; fencing; rain water accessories; sandwich panels; sign writing panels and other potential uses.

“The line has a maximum line process speed of 40m/min. and delivers an excellent quality paint finish on both top and reverse sides. The wet paint film is oven cured with the end product meeting worldwide standards in various formulations from Polyesters, Polyurethanes and Fluorocarbon paint systems,” Bronx explained.
