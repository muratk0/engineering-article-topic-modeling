Abrasive hand pads impart flexibility without extra bulk

Saint-Gobain Abrasives Inc., One New Bond St., Worcester, MA 01615, 508-795-5000,

www.nortonbeartex.com

Saint-Gobain Abrasives offers Norton Bear-Tex® Thin-Flex™ hand pads. According to the company, the pads provide increased cutting action through a web structure created by a needling process to allow more pressure to be applied per abrasive grain without extra effort from the user.

The pads are flexible for contoured and hard-to-reach areas without the extra bulk of thicker pads, with the webbing providing durability. They are suitable for a variety of applications in automotive, MRO, and industrial markets.

A specialized coating process helps ensure superior grain exposure for constant cutting action. The grain coating, in combination with the open web structure, minimizes loading and helps impart a consistent scratch pattern.

The pads are available in very fine aluminum oxide for light cleaning and primer preparation and ultrafine silicon carbide for blending, scuffing, and de-burring of primer, weld beads, rust, and oxidation.

Power sensor measures very high-power fiber lasers

Ophir Photonics, 3050 N. 300 W., North Logan, UT 84341, 435-753-3729,

www.ophiropt.com/photonics

Ophir Photonics, a Newport Corp. brand, has introduced the 100K-W laser power meter. The first commercial sensor for measuring very high-power 100-kW lasers, the meter is designed for fiber lasers used in industrial material processing, such as thermal ablation, heavy-section welding, and metal forming, as well as military directed-energy applications. The portable meter is designed to allow measurement of very high-power, near-infrared, Nd:YAG, and fiber lasers.

The meter measures laser power at four times the power of any other commercial laser meter, according to the company. It measures high-power beams at a distance past the focal spot of the laser or, alternatively, at a distance from the diverging fiber output where the beam has expanded to about 3.94 in. dia.

A reflective cone in the meter deflects the laser beam onto the inner circumference of the sensor. This increases the radiated area and reduces the power density to manageable levels. As the cone dissipates the beam, it captures more than 99 percent of the radiation to help minimize safety hazards. The response time is less than 1 min.

The compact, portable unit comprises a beam dump with overall dimensions of about 19.69 in. dia. by 19.69 in. deep and a water flow power measurement unit of 4.33 by 5.91 by 13.39 in. The meter connects to a PC via RS-232 and is provided with software that displays and graphs the power and water flow rate.

Push/pull welding torch delivers current up to 500 amps

TBi Industries GmbH, Ruhberg 14, 35463 Fernwald, Germany, 49-6404-9171-78,

www.tbi-industries.com

TBi Industries GmbH offers the new PP-TX push/pull welding torch with a current up to 500 amps and a maximum cable length of 39.37 ft. One gas-cooled and two water-cooled models are available.

The 511-CC model is a two-circuit-cooled torch. The intensified gas nozzle cooling provides the user with the comfort of a cold torch, even during a high-performance or pulsed welding process.

Aluminum wire can be fed up to 39.37 ft. without problems, even without having a straightened cable assembly, the company states. Performance up to 500 amps with 100 percent duty cycle is possible, giving the user flexibility for various applications.

The torches are supplied with a standard PCB that enables quick connection to the wire feeder. For crucial applications and procedures, some optimized connection varieties are available. The optional Syntronic, for example, supports the correlation between the wire feeder of the welding machine and the compact driver inside the torch.
