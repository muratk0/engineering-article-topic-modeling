# Colour coating line for Southern Steel Sheet Co. Ltd.

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

TENOVA AND SOUTHERN Steel Sheet Co. Ltd. (SSSC) – a Joint Venture of Vietnam Steel Corporation and partners Sumitomo Corporation (Japan) and FIW Steel Sdn. Bhd. (Malaysia) – recently signed a contract for the upgrade of a colour coating coil line. The line has been in operation since 2014 and the aim is to increase the annual production of the line and satisfy requirements for high-quality coated coils from the international and domestic market.

Over the last four years, in addition to the colour coating coil line, Tenova has also supplied a continuous galvanizing line to SSSC, followed by several orders for spare parts, technological assistance and supervision services. A total of ten contracts has been awarded by SSSC to Tenova and demonstrate the long-lasting cooperation between the two companies.

"The upgrade of the coil colour coating line consists of two bake ovens with rotary thermal oxidizer and four-roll coater, of which one is horizontal. The newly equipped colour coating line will produce up to 100,000 tons per year," said Tenova. See www.tenova.com
