# Georg has installed new slitting and cut-to-length lines for customers in Germany and Russia

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Georg designs its lines foreasyoperation, short tooling times andimprovedprocess-controlled workflows forgreaterefficiency”

The German steel service centre, Delta Stahl GmbH, located in Barsinghausen near Hanover, is a competent partner for the metal-working industry for surface-finished sheets all over Europe. Since 1 July 2007, Delta Stahl GmbH has been part of the independent KNAUF INTERFER Group with its headquarters in Essen, Germany.

With the commissioning of a new heavyduty slitting line, supplied by the Georg finishing lines’ division, production capacity can now be increased and it will be able to produce higher strength material more efficiently for sectors such as automotive. In addition to the cut-to-length line supplied by Georg in 2008, Delta Stahl is now operating with a further process-oriented finishing line, built by Georg.

“This new line has been designed for processing steel coils with material widths of up to 1800mm and thicknesses ranging from 0.25 to 4.18mm, whereby the master coils are separated into max. 50 strips and then recoiled with straight edges. The line is running at a speed of max. 400 m/min. at high quality standards for cutting result and surface quality. This is especially valid for material strength ranges of up to 1.400 N/mm as per today’s requirements,” Georg told ISMR.

With the new Georg high-duty slitting line, Delta Stahl is capable of cutting high strength strip material of the quality required for the automotive industry

To guarantee the flexibility and efficiency of the line, as required in steel service centres, it has been provided with very high automation levels and special mechanical components. These include, for example, the coil turnstiles in the line entry and exit which enable flexible loading and unloading of the line and the further processing of incompletely-treated coils.

The line is also equipped with automatic tool change on the slitting shear which can be completed in less than five minutes. Automatic CNC separation has also been integrated for the fully automatic positioning of all separating discs during the tool change process.

“For protection of the strip surfaces, the slitting line is equipped with a variety of brake systems which can be combined, depending on the specific material to be processed. Particular importance is attached to the belt brake which operates almost without relative movement between material and brake material. Thus, a gentle re-coiling of the strip can be achieved,” added Georg.

Another feature of the new line is the integrated Georg coil packaging line, which has been designed for a production of max. 90 rings per hour. Besides an electromechanically operating coil tilting table for taking slitted rings from the coil turnstile, this packaging line is equipped with a stacking bridge for the commissioning of rings, according to a customer’s requirements. The strapping of coils through the coil eye is done automatically.

Georg's finishing lines division offers complete line solutions, individual components and aggregates for slitting and cut-to-length lines, multi-blanking lines, edge trimming and inspection lines and packaging lines. It designs its lines for easy operation, short tooling times and improved process-controlled workflows for greater efficiency. The company is a whollyowned subsidiary of Heinrich Georg Maschienfabrik GmbH, a family-owned group of companies based in Germany. In 2008, it celebrated its 60th anniversary and approximately three hundred people work at the Kreuztal facility in Germany.

## Cutting lines for aluminium coils

At the end of 2011, Georg was assigned with the development and delivery of two cut-tolength lines and one slitting line, each with a packaging line, for the Russian aluminium producer Kamensk-Uralsky Metallurgical Works (KUMZ). Those lines are able to process aluminium coils with a strip width of up to 2800mm. This investment by KUMZ is focused on increased export demands and the enhanced technology requirements of the aviation and transport industries. The start of production is planned for the beginning of 2014.

“The scope of supply is entirely handled by Georg and includes, besides the mechanical element, the electrical control and automation. All components will be manufactured in our German workshop,” Antonio Garcia, managing director of Georg's finishing lines division and Member of the Board, said.

Each line, the two cut-to-length lines and the slitting line, are served with coils by the high bay warehouse system and are designed to produce coils with a total weight of up to 25 tons. The cut-to-length lines can process material thicknesses of 0.20 – 3mm and 3 - 10mm. They are equipped with hydraulic expandable double decoilers and rotating cut-to-length shears.

Both lines have edge trimming shears with automatic cutting and strip width adjustment. Additionally the approved Georg levelling machines in cassette change design and the electrostatic oiling machines are used to produce high quality plates. To protect the finished stacking bundles, they can be wrapped by the semi-automatic packaging line at the end of the process.

The thickness range of the slitting line is 0.20 – 5mm and is covered by two slitting shears with a shear exchange unit and a toolchanging device. The additional packaging line has a stacking device for commissioning of the produced baby coils, an automatic strapping device and a foil-stretching machine to wrap the stacked coil packages. ■
