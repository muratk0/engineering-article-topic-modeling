# Coil straighteners

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

COE Press Equipment has announced the suitability of its precision power straighteners for metal service centres, processing a variety of modern materials and alloys.

“Developed from a straightener design originally launched in 2016, our precision power straightener can process coil at widths from 1.0” to 78" and thicknesses of up to 0.650”, weights of up to 40,000lbs and yield strengths of up to 300KSI, while holding flatness tolerances of 0.003"TIR across 18. This makes these straighteners a good solution for filling the capability gap between old straightener technology and corrective levellers," said the manufacturer.

The straighteners effectively remove coil set across a variety of coil materials including CR, HR, galvanized, pre-painted, dual- and triple-phase steels; aluminium and copper. They also remove crossbow in many applications.

"In comparison to other straighteners, this machine provides increased roll force delivery, stronger gears and bearings and an improved straightener roller back-up mechanism for more robust rigidity. These straighteners can consistently achieve a level of flatness appropriate for highly precise applications, potentially saving money for customers while enabling them to handle more tighttolerance orders. This includes processing fine blanking and lasercut blanking applications," continued COE.

“Coil straightening is a primary metal service centre responsibility, correcting material memory to deliver high-quality product to customers," added President, Reid Coe. "Our straighteners are designed to be robust, precise and flexible, making them an effective and economical alternative solution for this task."

The straighteners can be purchased alone or as part of a complete press feeding solution or Cut-to-Length (CTL) line. COE CTL lines are configured from the company's feeds, straighteners and reels coupled with shear and stacking solutions that are customised to particular needs.■
