# New slitting line for Eisen + Stahl Service Centre

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

GEORG HAS RECEIVED an order to manufacture and supply a new slitting line for the German steel service centre, E + S Eisen + Stahl Service GmbH. With the new line, the Mannheim-based company will be adding the sale of slit strip out of its own facilities to its portfolio, taking another important step towards metal product processing in addition to trading.

Eisen + Stahl sells steel, stainless steel and aluminium sheet and plate, which the company cuts at its own facilities to the sizes required by its customers. To date, slit strip ordered by customers has been sourced from third parties. With the new slitting line supplied by GEORG, Eisen + Stahl will, in the future, be able to slit the strip at its own facilities in Mannheim. The company will therefore be able to cater to call-off orders much more flexibly. Additionally, the new line will reduce the company's dependency on third-party supplies.

"The new line will be designed to handle up to 1,650mm wide and up to 5mm thick steel strip, or up to 2mm thick strip in stainless grades. It will be able to produce up to 30 slit coils from one mother coil at speeds of up to 400m/min. The new line will be designed to optionally pay the strip off from above or below so slitting can be performed with the inside or outside coil surface up, as requested by the customer." GEORG told ISMR.

With a high degree of automation, the GEORG slitting line will have much shorter non-productive times than traditional lines. The scrap chopper features GEORG's "shimless tooling" technology and an automatic, patented CNC-based system for arranging the separators discs to save set-up times. GEORG will equip the machine with energy-efficient motors and drives. Certain components, such as the brake rollers, will be operated in generator mode, allowing them to feed excessive energy into a link. The new line is expected to start operation at E + S facilities in Mannheim in September 2018. See www.georg.com
