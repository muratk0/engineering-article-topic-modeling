# FOCUS ON FOLDERS

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

GMC MachineTools' electromechanical sheet metal folders have been designed to bend light-duty sheet metal. They are equipped with an Italian ESA 6-axis CNC controller and an 11" touch screen monitor. Most of its product line is manufactured in Taiwan. Other machines are designed in the U.S. and manufactured in Italy and China.

"Our sheet-metal folders are specifically designed for a wide range of industries and are suitable for light-duty applications such as box and pan, panels, flashing etc. We recommend them for metalworking fabricators in the HVAC, roofing and signmaking industries," it said.

Hershey's Metal Meister in Mattoon, Illinois (USA) is a family-owned business with a history of rollforming and machinery manufacturing. The company purchased the North-American patent rights to European folding manufacturer Variobend's products and is the U.S. manufacturer of Variobend equipment. Its portfolio includes single/double folders, a double bender and a single bender (see also Variobend entry in this article).

It builds Variobend machines in the U.S. with SAE hydraulic lines, hoses and other components. In fact, all hydraulic components are sourced in the USA. The company is also a distributor for the Slinet automatic slitter lines built by the same company who designed the Variobend machines. It also have a subfoundation insulation division known as Hitz Halter, a metal supply store – Permanent Building Solutions, and a roofing metal brand, Pemamag.

H.M.Transtech, s.r.o. is a privately owned Slovakian company with more than 25 years of experience in the production of sheetmetalworking machines. Its production programme offers standard machines as well as machines with modern graphic CNC control systems. The production range includes manual folders up to a maximum length of 4m; shears; plate bending machines; motorised and hydraulic shears; motorised folding machines; coil-processing/ sheet lines and other special machines.

"Our motorised folders are suitable for small-batch and mass production of simple open and closed profiles and boxed products. Motorised sheet metal folding machines are highly flexible in the transition to a different type of products. The capacity of the motorised folding machines ranges from 1-8mm of thickness and length is in the range of 1-5m," said the company.

Jorns AG is a Swiss manufacturer of folders, double folders and accessories used for processing sheet metal. It celebrated its 50-year anniversary in 2023. Its customers mainly work in roofing or architectural sheetmetal. Products range from simple NCcontrolled machines to sophisticated CNCcontrolled sheet metal bending systems and a J-Bend app, functional on tablets and mobile phones, to support the management and production of sheet metal profiles.

"Our specialty is manufacturing bending machines and double bending machines as well as accessories for processing sheetmetal. We have been developing and building these since 1973. Jorns manufactures 120 bending machines per year, of which 40 are double bending machines. These are sold, installed and serviced by our international partners, who are independent sales and service agencies. 5500 Jorns bending machines are currently in operation, creating new halls and roofs for tinsmiths, hall constructors and cladding constructors around the world," commented the folding specialist.

Jorns will be exhibiting with Krasser GmbH, a manufacturer of sheet metal cutting and coil processing systems, and Stolarczyk (a coil-processing machine manufacturer) at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, Germany (22-25 October 2024) on stand K128. The three machine manufacturers will highlight the advantages of exhibiting together as their packaged solution is designed to to meet the increasing demand for digitally networked sheet metal processing machines and increasing capacities.

At EuroBLECH, Jorns will also present the latest geometry on the clamping and bending tools of its JDB (Jorns double bending machine). Information will also be available on the latest functions of the automated Jorns Profile Manipulator as well as the latest options and functionality of the new JC100 control system for Jorns JB bending machines and Jorns JDB Double Bending machines.

“The area in front of and behind the clamping tool of the Jorns JB bending machine offers above-average open space, letting users manufacture bending profiles extremely efficiently. The use of servotechnology and the stop fingers that can be moved freely on the sides guarantee maximum precision and flexibility to bend parallel, tapered or overlapped profiles," it told ISMR.

The semi-automatic Jorns JB folder and fully automatic Jorns JDB double bending folder can be configured in lengths of 3.2 to 12.2 metres and boast bending capacities for sheet metal of up to 3mm.

“The simultaneous bending of complex profiles poses no problem for the Jorns JDB double bending machine because multitasking is ’a given'. The area behind the clamping tool, with a retraction of over 290mm for the bending tools, offers above-average open space. Users can then produce bending profiles without turning them around or over, with an array of possible profile variations. Several parallel, tapered or overlapped profiles can be bent simultaneously, an innovation that increases the speed of work," commented Jorns.

The use of servo technology, the automatic loading and unloading table as well as the individually driven clamping fingers are designed for precision and flexibility. Thanks to two bending tools, material handling has also been drastically reduced.

Jorns also offers dynamic positioning which allows the uniform closure of a hem over the entire length of the profile; the pre-tension is programmed into the control system and lets the clamping arms exert more pressure in the middle of the profile, up to the maximum clamping pressure if needed. Due to the modular design of the machines, Jorns bending machines can also be retrofitted with this function; dynamic positioning can be integrated as an option.

At Dach & Holz in March this year, Jorns presented the Jorns JDB-150GHM-DP-SE-1250-JC100-6.4-dsp double bending folder, equipped with automated side-infeed system as well as the new JC100 control which offers a direct interface to the “Bendex Enterprise Suite" from MicroSea. In addition, Jorns and Krasser offer a direct interface whereby the required pre-cut blanks can be exported to the cutting and slitting line from Krasser.

After celebrating 150 years of trading in 2022, UK-based Morgan Rushworth offers a range of manual, hydraulic and CNC folders. Its HBP hydraulic box and pan folder uses an adjustable manual angle-setting device. Clamping and bending is controlled by a joystick or foot pedal or combination of the two, allowing easy positioning of the material for folding. It also offers a manual BPC Box and Pan. It also offers powered and hydraulic section benders.

Its EXR range of CNC folders are manufactured with a rotating upper beam with one straight rail and one set of high divided tools that can both be utilised in the same folding program without any manual tool change. The back gauge is integral to the rear support table, which is suitable for the folding of large sheets as the weight of the material is supported, resulting in a traditionally two-operator job being able to be performed with just one. Three models are available.

The ESR range of CNC folders are designed to produce fast, accurate and consistent folded parts. Available from 1.5m to 4.2m bending lengths, all models are supplied with a 1m powered back gauge, with optional longer back gauges (including U- and L-shaped configurations). All ESR models are supplied with a straight rail with optional high divided tools for box and panel-type operations.

"Our EXL range of CNC folding machines are manufactured with a rotating upper beam with one straight rail and one set of high divided tools that can both be utilised in the same folding program without any manual tool change. This versatile design enables accuracy, high consistency and high productivity as there is only one initial tool setup to carry out. This is ideal for many folding operations including rectangular panel work with return edges to the four sides," explained the company.

PROfirst has been creating software for sheet metal processing since 1987. Its 3D STEP-UNFOLD (STEP and IGES unfolder) software imports 3D files and unfolds to DXF. It enables manufacturers to design sheet metal parts. Its PROfirst CAD software can free up cutting contours in customer drawings, find open contours and close them automatically or smooth contours with its "KEEP"function.

RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH manufactures semi-automated and fully automated folding machines for companies looking to maximise automation. Founded in 1930, it builds metal bending, cutting and forming equipment, as well as a range of software (such as Bendex fabrication software). RAS Systems LLC was founded in 1992 near Atlanta, Georgia (USA). RAS announced the opening of its new sales and service company, RAS France SAS, in June this year.

At this October's EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition in Germany, the company will be showcasing its XLTbend with tool support, MEGAbend with MetalLift for heavy parts and MinibendCenter 2.

The second-generation RAS MiniBendCenter has been designed to process small bent parts. As a fully automatic folding centre, it enables the seamless, automated processing of sheet metal from automatic tool set-up and feeding of the blanks to alignment and measurement. A sophisticated manipulator positions the sheet for the bending process, which can be done upwards and downwards. The integration of an intelligent robot processes the loading of punched or laser-cut blanks as well as the unloading of the finished bent parts, which it deposits or stacks unsorted as required. The newly implemented pallet-changing station optimises the material flow by providing a central point for feeding in new blanks and removing the processed parts.

“For those who want to form complex precision sheet metal parts such as cassettes, panels and boxes profitability (even in small batches), the XLTbend is a perfect fit. It is a very flexible UpDown folding machine. Bendex software for automatic programming of the bending sequence, front and rear operating options, ViN laser loading assistance, as well as the hybrid gauging system, are features of the XLT series," confirmed RAS.

"Our MEGAbend metal folding system proves the precision of up-down bending on thick and thin materials. Large-size parts, difficult and labour-intensive for press brakes, are ideal for the MEGAbend. The Bendex graphics software programs the bending sequences automatically. The stop system with fingers and suction bars allows automatic sequential bends. The ViN laser shows the operator the blank loading position. The tools offer plenty of free space for unique bending geometries. The system provides up-down folding of up to 6mm mild steel; a single person can even bend large and heavy components in an easy operation (part rests on the gauging system)," added the manufacturer.

It offers a wide range of almost thirty folding systems, including the Multibend-Center (and ECO version) as well as the MiniBendCenter (automatic folding centre for small parts), two UpDownCenter models and XXL/XL Centers for folding longer parts. Other models include the GIGAbend; XLTbend; FLEXIbend/FLEXI2bend; TURBO2plus and TURBObend (for wall and roof profile bending).

For flexible and automatic profile bends profiles (such as door frames, elevator parts or cable ducts in batch size one), it offers the ProfileCenter. The FlexGripper handling system moves, rotates and flips the blank automatically during the bending process.

SchechtI (Edling, Germany) began as a blacksmith shop, run by Josef Schechtl, in 1910. 22 years later, it produced its first folding machine. Today, it manufactures shears and folders for sheet metal, working with various distributors throughout the world.

The MAK 4 Evolution UD with robot module in the control system.

It offers seven motorised swivel bending machines and 11 manual swivel bending machines, in a wide variety of designs and control types, for folding sheet metal. It also manufactures segment and shingle bending systems along with shears and profilers.

The Schröder Group has teamed up with automation specialist, STARMATIK, to present a system at EuroBLECH 2024 this October that can be integrated into fully automated industrial production processes. A MAK 4 Evolution UD has been equipped with a Fanuc robot for this purpose. It loads the machine with up to 6mm thick sheets (3000 x 1500mm in size) and places the finished bent product on a palletising station.

Using the pneumatic stop in the Advanced Handling System and the up-and-down folding beam, the folding machine itself runs through the production process without any intervention from the robot. The entire automation solution is controlled via Schröder software. No knowledge of robotics is required, confirmed the manufacturer. The robot module in the Schröder POS 3000 control system will be available in future for all machines in the Evolution class, as well as the PowerBend folder series.

The Schröder Group, which is celebrating its 75th anniversary this year, has also redesigned its PowerBend Industrial UD folding machine. The machine (the bidirectional folding version at EuroBLECH processes thick steel sheets of up to 5mm thick to a length of 4000mm) has been equipped with improved servo motors throughout.

Another Schröder highlight at EuroBLECH will be the PowerBend Professional UD folding machine for up to 3mm thick sheet metal, designed to provide flexibility for plumbing companies (for example). It is equipped with an up-and-down folding beam, rotating clamping beam for two tool stations, radius step bending function and suction plate stop. This equipment enables semi-automated production and quick set-up for individual pieces.

At the Dach + Holz exhibition earlier this year, it showcased its motorised folding machines and shears in addition to classic manual sheet metal folders.

"Just like the other MAKU motorised folding machine on display, the PowerBend Multi is also capable of conical bending. Thanks to the electronically controlled two-axis back gauge, it can be used to produce conical and form-fit, plug-in profiles with a precision in the tenth of a millimetre range," explained the manufacturer.

Another innovation of this folding machine for processing thin sheet metal of up to 2.5mm sheet thickness is the clamping function. In combination with the option of the rotating clamping beam, it is possible to reset the clamping beam, clamp the sheet and make room for the folding beam to fold hems.

“Our demonstration of radius bending met with great interest. The PowerBend Multi handles radius step bending, which involves pressing the sheet metal with the clamping beam against an angle formed by the bottom beam and the folding beam. This creates a slight curve. This is now repeated in fine steps as often as required. The result is a rounded sheet that shows no signs of its gradual reshaping," added The Schröder Group.

Schröder will be showcasing the revised PowerBend Industrial UD folding machine at EuroBLECH 2024.

With the EVO DuoBend, the Schröder Group has increased the flexibility of its up-and-down technology. The new folding machine for up to 5mm thick sheets has two independently operating folding beams. This unlocks new possibilities for manufacturing large-format products with opposing bends. EVO DuoBend was presented at Blechexpo last November in Germany.

“In special applications, the simplified handling results in enormous time savings and increased efficiency. Large trapezoidal sheets can be produced with two folding beams in one tenth of the time required with a single up-and-down folding beam," said the manufacturer.

The EVO DuoBend is available in working lengths of 4040mm and 3200mm. As standard, this machine is equipped with a fully automatic tool changer on the clamping beam, which can be equipped with up to 500mm high tools. The machine is programmed with Schröder's own POS 3000 control system.

The Schröder Group consists of Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH in Wessobrunn-Forst (Germany), SCHRÖDERFASTI Technologie GmbH (Wermelskirchen, Germany) and SMU GmbH (Leinburg-Weißenbrunn, Germany).

Sucorema is a Portuguese company dedicated to the manufacture of machines and systems for the sheet metal industry under its own brand, Stefa. With over two decades of experience in designing and producing its own machines, it specialises in long folding machines, rollformers and cut-to-length/slitting lines. Its Stefa VH long folder features rounded “C" arms to ensure enough pressure for hemming.

Australian manufacturer, SWI Machinery, offers a range that includes Simplex & Duplex folders; Marxman slitters; integrated cut-to-length lines; uncoilers; recoilers; multistation uncoilers; rollformers and complete customised process lines. SWI's latest model Duplex folder incorporates dozens of advances in control and mechanical design. The folder is used by roofing fabricators worldwide.

"Our Duplex folders are built for speed and durability. When designing the Duplex, we listened to what our customers wanted from an up & down folder," it explained.

"Our Simplex folder is fast, simple and flexible enough to be used even by the most inexperienced operators."

Variobend-ASCO GmbH has set itself the task of developing innovative long-length bending machines. It was able to develop into one of the worldwide leading manufacturers of long-length bending machines (see also Hershey's Metal Meister entry in this article). It created its patented VARIOBEND bending system.

"The new Variobend V 2.5 is 100% electric. Thanks to the new bending geometry with 300 degrees of free space, profiles with an aspect ratio of 1:2 can also be produced without any problems. The closed frame profile ensures that the V2.5 no longer has to be anchored to the workshop floor. The machine can be adjusted perfectly to uneven floors using narrowly positioned adjustable feet," said the manufacturer.

“The bending of narrow counter edges of 12mm is also standard on the new V 2.5. A maximum bending angle of 150 degrees and the typical Variobend inclined bending beams for even more space round off the innovative overall package and enable the production of even more demanding profiles for the facades of the future," it added.

A newly introduced DP system (Dynamic Pressure Regulation) allows the clamping pressure to be individually adjusted on each stand. Other Variobend models include the DB Up/Down folder and the EB long-length folder.■
