# New air-tightness class for ductwork

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

AT THE ISH Aircontec fair this March in Frankfurt, MEZ-TECHNIK is aiming to present its AEROSEAL ductwork sealing process, patented technology for the sealing of previously installed ductwork from the inside out.

“Very quickly, ductwork leakage is reduced by an average of over 90%. On the North American continent, the patented process has been proved and tested since the end of the 1990s. From 2015, the technology will be introduced to the European HVAC industry under the name MEZ-AEROSEAL. This technology makes a major contribution to achieving the EU’s climate and energy targets for 2020,” claimed MEZ-TECHNIK.

“By sealing ductwork systems with MEZ-AEROSEAL, tightness classes can be achieved that are usually only achieved with laboriously sealed or welded ductwork. It allows us to reach, or even to drop below, the requirements of tightness class D, according to EN 1507, EN 12237 or DW144 TM1. Unlike traditional subsequent sealing, when applying MEZ-AEROSEAL no ductwork components have to be uninstalled and no walls or ceilings have to be damaged.”

Thermal loss from the heating and cooling system, caused by leaking ductwork, is also reduced. This ensures equal distribution of warmth and cold across the entire building. The vinyl acetate polymer-based sealant is certified to VDI 6022. See www.mez-technik.de ■
