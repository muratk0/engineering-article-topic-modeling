24th Annual ALAW: Advanced Laser Applications Workshop

June 5-7, 2017

The Inn at St. John’s Conference Center Plymouth (Detroit), Mich.

The theme for this year’s ALAW

®

is Lasers: Lighting the Way for the Future of Transportation. As the use of lasers becomes more widespread, product designers and engineers in the transportation sector are using various metals to lightweight end products during the laser welding process. They must learn how to design components, assemblies, and systems for the successful implementation of these lightweight parts. These topics are addressed in-depth, along with practical examples and case studies to help build a foundation in laser design and implementation.

This annual workshop is designed for metal fabrication job shops, manufacturers, suppliers, automotive, construction, agriculture, and aerospace engineers and anyone interested in learning more about laser material processing systems to reduce costs, improve quality, save material, and reduce weight and provide flexible laser manufacturing.

In addition to keynote presentations on industry forecasts and lightweighting solutions, there will be over 20 presentations on the latest topics in laser welding of multimaterials, remote laser welding, lightweighting, green lasers, hybrid manufacturing, and laser additive manufacturing, to name a few.

The ALAW tradition continues with two world-class open houses featuring live welding and cutting demos at Fraunhofer USA-Center for Laser Applications and TRUMPF’s Laser Technology Center, both in Plymouth. Registration for both open houses is free to ALAW attendees.

For the second year, technology demonstrations will be given at Laser Mechanisms Inc. in Novi and Precitec in Wixom. Both tours on Monday are sponsored by nLIGHT. Registration for the tours is free to ALAW attendees and students. This will be the third year for the popular Lasers 101 certificate course, which will be held on Monday, June 5, at Precitec with new, hands-on learning demos.

Attendees will also have the opportunity to network during the receptions and visit tabletop exhibitors.

ALAW Remembers…

FRANK A. DiPIETRO,

the founder of ALAW-Advanced Laser Applications Workshop, died on December 12, two weeks after turning 90. When he was 8, his immigrant father died, leaving his family penniless. It was the first of many challenges he faced with courage, intelligence, and grit. He worked his way through Kettering University (formerly General Motors Institute) and rose through GM’s ranks, eventually running a division with 2,000 people, a budget exceeding $2 billion, and responsibility for purchasing and installing tooling at GM’s North American Assembly Plants. "I met Frank 24 years ago in Ann Arbor, Michigan. He was a great evangelist for laser welding car bodies and components and is the reason ALAW has run nearly every year since he started the event with the University of Michigan in 2003," said FMA Executive Director Jim Warren. "His enthusiasm was infectious and his motto ‘The Best Is Yet To Come’ was prophetic as laser welding applications took off in the North American auto industry in recent years. Frank will be missed, but his ALAW legacy and great memories of him go on," Warren said.

Frank is survived by his wife of 64 years, Beverly; daughter, Della; son, Trey; grandsons, Frankie and Domenic; and sister, Marie. An ALAW Award will be given annually for the person most exemplifying the vision, spirit, and determination Frank demonstrated in bringing laser and robotics technology to transportation manufacturing.
