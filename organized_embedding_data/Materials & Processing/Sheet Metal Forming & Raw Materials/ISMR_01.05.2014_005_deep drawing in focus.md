# Deep drawing in focus

[TARİH: 01.05.2014 ISMR]

The IDDRG 2014 conference, entitled “Innovations for the Sheet Metal Industry”, will be held in Paris (France) from 1-5 June 2014.

“Different industrial sectors using sheet metal parts are facing increasing challenges. These challenges require not just further development of existing technologies but also scientific and technical innovations in sheet and tool materials, forming processes etc.,” explained the International Deep Drawing Research Group (IDDRG). The conference will cover topics such as materials, processes, presses/press tools and modelling /simulation.

The International Deep Drawing Research Group was started in the late 1950s as an organisation of national groups devoted to the study of sheet metal forming including forming processes; materials; formability issues; tooling; tribology and other interesting aspects of sheet metal forming research and industrial practices. For more details of the conference, see www.iddrg2014.fr/en/ ■
