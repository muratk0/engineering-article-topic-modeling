# Ornamental Machinery

NARGESA ORNAMENTAL SOLUTIONS

Nargesa’s ornamental machinery is engineered to shape metal with precision, enabling the creation of intricate designs with speed and consistency. Purpose-built for the ornamental metalworking industry, these machines boost production efficiency while upholding superior craftsmanship and long-term durability. Quantum offers a comprehensive range of Nargesa ornamental equipment, including bar twisting and scrolling machines, broaching machines, cold iron embossing machines, end wrought iron machines, gas forges, and power hammers, making it easy for fabricators to achieve better results.

EMBOSSING MACHINES

Nargesa embossing machines use a cold-forming process to create detailed decorative designs without compromising material strength. Ideal for ornamental metalwork, ensuring consistent, repeatable patterns across a wide variety of applications.

MT SERIES MACHINERY

The MT Series is purpose-built for manufacturing iron and steel components such as chairs, handrails, and decorative metal elements. Ideal for ornamental fabrication, it combines reliability with production efficiency.

BROACHING MACHINES

The BM2S Broaching Machine produces accurate internal keyways, splines, and profiles with smooth, controlled cutting. Built for repeatability and precision, it ensures efficient production while maintaining tight tolerances across mechanical components.

H SERIES GAS FORGES

Engineered for even heat distribution and fuel-efficient performance, the H Series supports precise steel forming with adjustable auxiliary supports for stability and operator safety.

HOT FORGING PRESS

The PV21 delivers 23 tons of controlled forging power with exceptional precision, making it ideal for blacksmiths and metalworkers who demand accuracy and high performance.
