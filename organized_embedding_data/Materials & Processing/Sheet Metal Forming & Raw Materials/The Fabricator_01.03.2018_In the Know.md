Summer Manufacturing Camps Prepare for Liftoff

This summer Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT) will support a record number of 85 camps, a substantial increase from last year’s high of 64. One of the long-term goals of NBT is to support 100 camps and bring at least one program to every state.

The aim of the camp grant program is simple: introduce young people to the excitement, challenge, and pride of building something with their own hands and using advanced technology to inspire them to pursue careers in manufacturing.

You can help the next-generation workforce to discover manufacturing by supporting a camp in your area. Offer to host a plant tour of your manufacturing facility, volunteer as a guest speaker or camp mentor, or give financial support as a camp sponsor.

To find a 2018 NBT camp location near you, visit

nbtfoundation.org/camps

Now Open — 2018 Financial Ratios & Operational Benchmarking Survey

Participate in the 2018 Financial Ratios & Operational Benchmarking Survey — to discover your company’s strengths and identify areas for improvement!

This tool provides comprehensive business performance data for company owners and managers. The survey collects key historical financial and operational data from 2017 on 32 metrics in the areas of liquidity, profitability, asset management, operational benchmarking, value proposition, and marketing effectiveness. The responses will be compiled and then reported anonymously using five different demographics. Participants will be able to compare their data to that of other companies based on the number of employees, company type, annual sales volume, geographic region, and plant size.

More participation means broader data and more valuable results about the industry!

Sources that will help you complete the survey:

Detailed 2017 Income Statements: Month and Year End

2017 Balance Sheets: Month and Year End

Quotation logs, Bookings logs, Invoice register

Payroll and Headcount logs
