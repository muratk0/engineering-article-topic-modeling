ISMR:Which trends do you see developing in global sheet metal/stamping markets?

WB: Servo technology and the ability to control motion profiles for superior part quality and production is key. We are experiencing this need for both presses and automation solutions.

ISMR:Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

WB: I see the continuous development of the global market for alternative drive solutions in the automotive industry (EV/fuel cells/ hydrogen) and its impact on stamping, both positive and negative. This is a very dynamic and fast-moving development with changing parts, materials, volumes and much more. It is crucial that we are able to respond to the demands of the market with efficient and reliable solutions.

ISMR:What is your approach to Industry 4.0 and enabling technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR)?

WB: All our control systems are already equipped with the Industry-4.0 interface. This enables us to provide our customers with real-time system data from the control system to monitor the efficiency of the system and therefore ensure a continuous increase in performance.

ISMR: Are there any products/technologies that you have released or are releasing this year that you would like to discuss?

WB:We introduced in-line cutting and welding technology to improve line productivity and reduce material waste. This technology reduces manual processes and reliance, while improving part quality.

ISMR: How have you embraced sustainability/energy efficiency in your products, processes and operations?

WB: With our facility expansion, we are heavily focused on being carbon-neutral. We are investing to reduce our footprint as much as possible. We have significantly invested in solar power to almost completely eliminate our reliance on the local grid and are also implementing other processes through construction (such as repurposed building rubble for new construction).
