# Future steel vehicle technologies

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

THE STEEL MARKET DEVELOPMENT Institute’s automotive senior director, Dave Anderson, recently gave a presentation at the FABTECH Expo at McCormick Place in Chicago. His presentation on “Current Applications of FutureSteelVehicle (FSV) Technologies” reviewed the technologies introduced in the FSV project, highlighted examples of vehicles on the road today implementing these advanced steel technologies and demonstrated how a vehicle is able to successfully lightweight with steel.

The FutureSteelVehicle (FSV) features steel body structure designs that reduce mass by more than 35 per cent over a benchmark vehicle and reduce total life cycle emissions by nearly 70 per cent. The FSV programme developed optimised Advanced High-Strength Steel (AHSS) body structures for four proposed 2015-2020 model-year vehicles - Battery Electric (BEV) and Plug-in Hybrid Electric (PHEV) A-/ B-Class vehicles; and PHEV and Fuel Cell (FCEV) C-/D-Class vehicles.

The FSV programme brings more advanced steel and steel technologies to its portfolio, including more than 20 new AHSS grades, representing materials expected to be commercially available in the 2015 – 2020 technology horizon. The FSV material portfolio includes Dual Phase (DP), Transformation-Induced Plasticity (TRIP), Twinning-Induced Plasticity (TWIP), Complex Phase (CP) and Hot Formed (HF) steels, which reach into GigaPascal-strength levels and are the latest in steel technology offered by the global industry.
