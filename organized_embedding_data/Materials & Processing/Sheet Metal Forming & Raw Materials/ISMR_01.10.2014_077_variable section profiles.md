# Variable section profiles

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

IN 2013, STAM reached a new technological frontier. It designed and built ‘the first machine in the world capable of roll forming variable section profiles for beams of thicknesses of up to 7mm’. The material used was HSLA steel with a 350 to 500 MPa yield strength. The success of the project, followed by the positive testing of the line, was a rewarding way to celebrate 50 years of STAM activities.

After seeing the success of the machine producing U-profiles for light trucks, automotive customers began to request a machine capable of producing U or other large thickness sections (up to 7mm) for heavy vehicles. The variable section beams help manufacturers to improve the structure of the vehicle’s chassis by lowering the height of the web at one or both ends of the beam.

“Most of these products are manufactured today using large presses or welding centres which bend or weld pieces together to produce the beam of the desired section. In addition to the enormous manpower and space needed for production, the work pieces obtained from these pressing and welding operations show deformations that need to be corrected in subsequent straightening operations,” STAM told ISMR.

“Producing these special beams, in lengths of up to 15m, using the automatic and continuous roll forming method is therefore an enormous advantage in terms of flexibility and number of personnel employed, and consequently in quality and cost of the final product,” it added.

The STAM machine provides roll forming stands which can withstand not just the stresses from the bending action of the strip but also the tension and the loads generated by the displacement of the roll forming stands during roll forming, when the material is between the forming rolls. The system is able to accommodate the shape of the incoming strip and reach the required roll forming speed. The faster the speed of roll forming operations, the quicker are the transversal and rotary movements of the roll forming stands to follow the curved shape of the flanges being formed. The machine has more than 100 dynamic axes, which are constantly changing position during the roll forming operation, as well as over 40 fixed axes which are positioned according to the profile to be produced and will constantly be maintained in position during the process. Each axis is driven by servo-motor, with positional control in a closed loop, with each axis moving in a different manner to the other axes depending upon its physical position along the machine.

The choice of electronic components is important and control software was developed that could control the positioning of all the dynamic axes, following the progress of the strip inside the machine with precision, reliability and repeatability. Movement error of just one single element, during the roll forming of a 7mm thick beam, could cause serious damage. For this reason, a continuous monitoring system was developed which immediately stops all operation as soon as any anomaly is detected. The machine is able to roll form pieces of different shapes and thicknesses and is not linked to a fixed vehicle design. Vehicle manufacturers have various production models and various chassis and must be able to move quickly and safely from one product to another through easy programming. To do this, STAM developed a CAD/CAM system which, starting from a CAD drawing in dwg or dxf format from the customer, converts the drawing into a machine programming file. The software automatically calculates the positions of the various roll forming stands and the positions of the guides while the profile is being processed in the roll forming machine.

## Pressing for action

FOUNDED IN 1962, SEYI has established a strong position in the metal forming industry over the past 50 years. It offers mechanical presses, ranging in size from 25 to 4000 tons, at facilities located in Taiwan and mainland China. It is expanding its Total Solution Service emphasising peripherals such as feed mechanisms, transfer equipment and other ancillaries to maximize SEYI press productivity. It recently reported an 8 per cent increase in net sales and a 22 per cent increase in net profits for the six months ending 30 June 2014.

SEYI Presses Europe is growing its business in Europe and, in August 2014, moved into new and larger facilities in Hanau, Germany (close to Frankfurt Am Main) to better support its European customer base. The facilities are over 700 sq.m. and not only include spare parts stock for European customers but also a showroom facility where a wide variety of SEYI presses can be seen and tested daily. ## Press brake scales

ITALIAN company GIVI MISURE has been producing optical scales, rotary encoders, magnetic systems, digital readouts and position controllers since 1979. Its continuous investment in R&D, with constant monitoring of market needs, have resulted in the recent introduction of its GVS self-aligned scales for press brakes.

“The press brake scales are designed to offer high performances and stability over time, even under heavy usage conditions. With resolutions up to 0.1µm, accuracy grades up to ±1µm and backlash error below 0.2µm, each scale undergoes stringent tests and is supplied with a metrological certificate as a further proof of its quality,” said the manufacturer.

The new scale is available in four versions. Optical or magnetic, with incremental or absolute readings, GVS scales are symmetrical and interchangeable.

## Tool holders

WILA B.V. has been specialising in tool holders (clamping and crowning), tooling and accessories for more than 80 years. It has been able to reduce press brake adjustment times and further increase accuracy. Three of Wila’s latest innovations, to be demonstrated at the EuroBLECH exhibition, are the E2M® (Easy to Move), Smart Tool Locator® and V-Lock.

E2M® has been developed so that (heavier) New Standard tools can be replaced in a convenient, safe and efficient manner. Besides fast and safe tool changes, it also offers major improvements in productivity. The Smart Tool Locator® now makes it instantly clear where the tooling should be positioned in the clamp, thanks to this smart ruler with LED light. The ease and speed at which tools can be replaced also increase with this innovation.

“The V-Lock makes it possible to further improve bottom-tool positioning. A groove at the back of the tool is used to automatically align bottom tools in X and Y directions, and to firmly clamp them in place. This, for instance, prevents tools from sliding or being pulled upwards during the bending process. The V-Lock helps to significantly improve accuracy and safety in the bending process,” Wila told ISMR.
