# 3D printing solutions

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

HENKEL IS A specialist in adhesive technology and has consistently introduced new developments for industrial applications at successive Southern Manufacturing exhibitions in the UK. Henkel brands LOCTITE®, TEROSON®, BONDERITE® and TECHNOMELT® include structural and general bonding, threadlocking and sealing, gasketing, retaining and functional coating.

At the 2019 Southern Manufacturing exhibition (5-7 February 2019, Farnborough, UK), Henkel will introduce visitors to its 3D printing material solutions for industrial manufacturing. Visitors can find the company on booth H165.

"We are applying our expertise in highperformance materials, such as light cure acrylic, silicone, epoxy and polyurethane adhesives, to the rapidly growing market for additive manufacturing - from prototyping to final parts production," the company told SMR. "We are also developing tailor-made products and services to provide high-impact solutions for various industrial sectors and expanding our strategic partnerships with other technology leaders such as HP, for which we are the first global reseller of its 3D printers' portfolio."

The company's 3D capability will be demonstrated on the stand by experts who will combine the correct 'building' materials with the appropriate printing process and software to enable customers to realise the full potential of additive manufacturing.

Henkel's LOCTITE 518 gasket sealant will also be on display at Southern Manufacturing. This product's scope of application has been increased by its newfound capability to cure through light surface contamination and to work well on stainless steel and aluminium without an activator.

First introduced to overcome problems associated with conventional pre-cut compression gaskets, it has now also been made available in pen form.

"This allows LOCTITE 518 to deliver its leak-fighting properties via a roller, making application easier with no waste and less mess. It fills all voids, resists high pressure and, when fully cured, maintains clamp load and prevents corrosion," explained Henkel.

"LOCTITE Universal Structural Bonders have transformed the design and assembly of many products throughout the UK. The bond strength, speedy cure and durability that are characteristic of these adhesives are thanks to our hybrid technology. Another key differentiator for us is our ability to develop and supply the best equipment to dispense our adhesives. As we build our own systems, rather than work with a dispensing 'partner', we can supply a turnkey solution that is truly fit for purpose and for which the customer has a single point of contact."
