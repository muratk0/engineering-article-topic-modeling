Metal stamping, or pressing, is a manufacturing process used to convert flat metal sheets, or blanks, into specific shapes. It is a complex process that includes several metalforming techniques — blanking, punching, flanging, bending, embossing, coining and piercing, to name a few. The cold-forming process uses tools, dies and stamping presses to transform sheet metal into different shapes. Typically, stamping can be used for both sheet and coil forms of metal.

It uses a moving ram to transmit force, or an amount of tonnage, to specific tooling to achieve a product's final shape, often with little or no scrap and, whenever possible, with minimal operator intervention. Stamping dies are the tools that shape and cut sheet metal parts. Some of the most common types of metal stamping machines include mechanical, hydraulic and mechanical servo presses that are fitted with multiple dies to cut and shape the sheets of stainless steel and metals such as aluminium, zinc and copper. There are three major types of metal stamping techniques: progressive, fourslide (multi-slide) and deep draw. Greater speed and versatility, shorter cycle times and higher energy savings are just some of the hallmarks of the latest technologies. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing innovations.

## Metal stamping techniques

Progressive die stamping – This features several stations, each with a unique function – it is a strong solution for metal parts with complex geometry.

Fourslide, or multi-slide – This type of stamping involves horizontal alignment and four different slides (four tools are used simultaneously to shape the workpiece). This process allows for intricate cuts and complex bends to develop even the most complex parts.

Deep drawing – This involves pulling a sheet metal blank into the die via a punch and forming it into a shape. It is suitable for creating components that need several series of diameters. Short-run metal stamping requires minimal upfront tooling expenses and can be a solution for prototypes or small projects.
