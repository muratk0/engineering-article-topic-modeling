# Coil processing innovation

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Italian coil-processing specialist, Camu Srl, offers industrial cutting lines, slitting lines and cut-to-length lines. It has solid roots with a 44-year history of manufacturing in Italy. Based in Bressanvido, in the Vicenza region, the manufacturer follows a philosophy of continuous innovation to meet new technological challenges and changing requirements in global metalforming and fabrication markets.

CAMU Srl was set up in 1980 by two young brothers and entrepreneurs from Vicenza, Italy: Loris Basso and Fabio Basso. In over four decades, the company has grown from a workshop into a specialist in the construction of slitting and cut-tolength lines. It collaborates with major global manufacturers such as Marcegaglia; Efinox; Milesi; Tecnofondi; Eurotubi; Liberty Steel and APL Apollo.

CAMU will be exhibiting at the Tube exhibition in Düsseldorf, Germany, from 15-19 April 2024 to showcase its expertise in tailored cut-to-length lines with 4H-6H leveller; slitting lines; recoiling and inspection lines; coil-to-coil and combined lines; packaging lines; feeding lines and specialpurpose machines. CAMU lines are used in steel service centres to cut coils and various industry sectors (such as automotive, construction and appliances) as well as in modern tube, pipe and profiling centres for open profiles.

CAMU lines can process different types of material (steel; high-strength steel; stainless steel; galvanized; pre-painted; aluminium; brass; copper; tin plate; CRGO-CRNGO and titanium) in thicknesses ranging from 0.2- 25mm and widths of up to 2250mm.

CAMU Srl is present in five continents and 97 countries. It has supplied over 820 installed lines. For over 43 years, it has aimed to supply “the complete coil experience."
