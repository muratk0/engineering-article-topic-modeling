# Mobile roll forming

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

AT EUROBLECH 2016 in Hanover, Dreistern will demonstrate its mobile rollforming machine. Dreistern will present this concept, along with other smart solutions, at booth G60 in hall 27 at the exhibition.

Cost savings are always an issue in each new product. But it should not be at the product's expense. Why not save on logistics' costs? Our mobile roll forming production line offers full containers, because (from now on) just space-saving coils are delivered to the construction site The mobile roll forming machine is split into three easy-to-assemble containers which produce diverse parts (directly out of the container) on the spot at the installation site. There is no need to transport finished parts any more," explained the German manufacturer.

This practical concept has led to some smart solutions. For instance, Dreistern has managed to shorten the machine by 10m because the strip is cut directly from the coil to the respective length blank, without using an entry table (which would need to be the length of the largest part produced). This is bypassed by Dreistern's start-stop® operation, whereby the profile is stopped during the running/forming process to achieve a precise and clean separation cut from the coil.

"The mobile roll forming machine uses a simple cutting device for the flat strip – independent from the profile form - for minimal scrap. Contrary to cutting after rollforming, this concept reduces costs and set-up time as cutting tools need not be purchased or changed. Our quick-change cassettes and the flexible positioning of punching and forming units enables the production of varied profile shapes for diverse applications with minimal logistics costs to almost everywhere in the world,'" added Dreistern.
