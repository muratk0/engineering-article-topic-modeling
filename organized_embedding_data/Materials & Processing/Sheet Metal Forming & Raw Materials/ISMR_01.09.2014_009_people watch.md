Setting a new direction

SURFACE TECHNOLOGY, a specialist supplier of high performance surface treatments and metal finishing, has appointed Andrew Courtney to the position of Group Operations Director. He started his career as an apprentice engineer and gained experience in various industries before being appointed to the position of Regional Sales Engineer at Surface Technology in 1988.

With an HNC in Mechanical and Production Engineering, Andrew previously held the positions of Operations Director at Surface Technology’s Slough and Coventry facilities respectively and, most recently, was Group Sales and Marketing Director before being appointed to the Group Operations Director role.

Surface Technology employs a wide range of painting, coating, electroless and electro-plating surface finishing processes. See www.surfacetechnology.co.uk ■
