# An eye on coil processing

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Ekicontrol Coil Processing Lines is a family company based in the Basque Country, Spain, since 1986. It designs, manufactures and installs coil processing lines for carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium metal coils.

"We manufacture slitting; cut-to-length and multi blanking lines as well as combined cutting lines to cut and wind simultaneously; complete press feed lines and lines with special requirements (such as internal punching and profiling). We also supply peripherals such as decoilers; coil-loading cars; straighteners; rollers; static shears; flying shears; stacking tables; packaging etc.," it outlined to ISMR.

"We have been providing solutions to our clients around the world for more than 30 years and offer the highest European quality standards. We listen to our customers and work together to find the right solution for any requirement," it added.

Ekicontrol's TWINCUT20 Coil Multiblanking Line is designed and built for the simultaneous slitting and cut-to-length of material.

"The line offers high-quality straightening and cutting as well as repeatability. The use of coil over sheet material implies an average saving of 5% in raw material cost. We make the most of the coil length so that less material is wasted. The coils also take up much less storage space than sheet-metal blanks. A one-ton, 1mm-thick coil is 850 metres long; an Ekicontrol cutting line cuts 3m blanks in less than an hour. Incorporating circular blades in the straightener module enables us to adjust the width and make different cuts on the coil using the same machine," it concluded.
