# ‘Largest cold bent tubes in the world’ for Poland

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

AFTER RECENTLY celebrating its century anniversary, Sopot’s Forest Opera in Poland is currently undergoing a huge renovation. The £16.6 million (75 million zł) re-design contains the largest ever cold bent arches, curved by UK company Barnshaw Section Benders. The modernised, open-air amphitheater, which sits in the picturesque Sopot forest, will be covered by a bowstring structure shaped by two arches spanning 90m.

The project involved the bending of 16 steel tubes at 1300 cut diameter, which were bent to a radii of 49.785m. Supplied by Interfare Rohr Union, each tube was 70mm thick, with the largest section weighing 26 tonnes. Contractors acknowledge that previous options of producing structures such as these were limited to induction bending or producing the curve by a series of facets, which is not so appealing architecturally.
