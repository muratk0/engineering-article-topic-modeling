# Gorenje Orodjarna, d.o.o.

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.gorenje-orodjarna.si/en/

Gorenje Orodjarna d.o.o. is a major toolmaking company in Slovenia. A subsidiary of the Gorenje Group, Orodjarna's core business involves the development, manufacturing, marketing and maintenance of a variety of tools for sheet-metal processing; injection moulding; thermoforming and Styrofoam packaging as well as measuring systems for the control and testing of appliance functions and safety features.

It is a member of the Slovenian Tool and Die Development Centre and of the Automotive Cluster of Slovenia. The company is also involved in various national technology and development projects as well as international projects, such as EUREKA.

"We manufacture stamping tools for more complex sheet-metal parts for the automotive industry and household appliances' producers. We also make equipment for production lines and complete lines, as well as producing stamped sheet-metal parts for the automotive industry. We can manufacture individual tools for manual transfer or transfer line tools, as well as progressive tools," it added.
