# Industrial cleaning tasks

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

At MACH 2024 in Birmingham, UK (15-19 April 2024), Ecoclean will present its portfolio of cleaning products with high cleanliness requirements. An advanced EcoCwave machine will be shown live on its stand (Hall 6, Booth 829) to illustrate various features and process options.

Regardless of the industry sector, parts cleaning is one of the critical processes in manufacturing today. Due to increasing demands on component performance and reliability, new products, changed manufacturing, coating and bonding technologies as well as modified materials and the requirements for particulate and filmlike cleanliness are constantly rising.

"As a supplier of future-oriented, flexible and energy-efficient solutions for industrial component cleaning, Ecoclean and UCM (our Group's division specialising in precision and ultra-fine cleaning) cover the entire spectrum of wet-chemical processes using waterbased media, solvents and modified alcohols. Cleaning processes and systems, including the most suitable process technologies such as injection flood washing, spray cleaning, high-pressure, immersion, ultrasonic, megasonic and plasma cleaning, plus Ultrasonic Plus or Pulsated Pressure Cleaning (PPC) and drying techniques for both batch and individual part cleaning, can be very efficiently tailored to product- and companyspecific requirements," said Ecoclean.

Machine and process design is carried out in the company's own test centres, which include cleanrooms as well as adapted plant and measurement technology available for tasks in medical technology and for high-purity applications, as well as matching high-purity equipment packages. The latter will be demonstrated on the advanced EcoCwave for immersion and spraying processes with water-based cleaning agents.

"The system technology, media routing, media preparation and design of this full-vacuum, single-chamber system have been specially adapted for highend cleaning applications. It is capable of achieving consistent results that meet high requirements, even for geometrically complex components. Process options that can be combined in almost any way (such as high-pressure spray cleaning, ultrasonic, plasma cleaning as well as PPC) contribute to this," added Ecoclean.

Left: The EcoCwave for aqueous media can handle large product quantities for high-purity cleaning results.

Forward-looking service solutions will also be highlighted at Ecoclean's booth. These include an iOS- and Androidcompatible Service app for maintenance and repair requirements; tailored service and maintenance concepts; developments for the digitisation of cleaning processes; options for modernising and adapting systems as well as training programmes for customer employees.■
