# LONGER LIFE AND LOWER CUTTING COSTS

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, A MANUFACTURER of advanced metal cutting systems, is announcing the launch of Centricut brand consumables for owners of Kjellberg’s Fine Focus 800 Plus or HiFocus 280i, 360i or 440i systems.

Companies that own the above systems and cut mild steel using either a PB-S80 or PerCut 370.2 torch can now lower their overall cost of cutting by purchasing Centricut brand consumables.

“In addition to costing less and lasting longer, the new consumables provide exceptional cut quality and process reliability, while maintaining the fast cutting speeds customers demand and need,” said Martin Geheran, product marketing manager for Hypertherm’s Centricut brand.

Like all Hypertherm consumables, the new mild steel consumables are precision manufactured to tight tolerances.
