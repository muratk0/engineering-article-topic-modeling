# Wuppermann strengthens international growth

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

AT EUROBLECH IN HANOVER from 25-29 October 2016, the Wuppermann Group presented its newly restructured Flat & Tube Products Business Unit with new locations in Hungary and the USA.

Production is already in the start-up phase at the new Wuppermann factory for flat products in Hungary. The new building in the harbour of Győr-Gönyű, a production site for strip galvanizing, represents the largest investment in the 140-year history of the family business. The new production site has pickling and hot galvanizing facilities and the capacity for processing 500,000 tons of steel per year. The official opening of the site is expected in September 2017.

“We put all of our experience into the construction of this factory and particularly into the development of the line of pickled and galvanized hot-rolled strip," reported Carl Swoboda, head of sales for the Flat & Tube Products business unit.

“Production will start very soon. Around the same time that EuroBLECH opened at the end of October, we were selling our first pickled material from Hungary and, three weeks later, our first galvanized material. At the start of 2017, the production of our film-coated, hot-rolled strip (WProtect) will begin. Protected by a layer of zinc and an additional layer of high-performing film, WProtect defies practically all corrosive attacks such as moisture or mechanical abrasion."

With the set-up of Wuppermann Inc. and its office in Chicago, Illinois at the beginning of 2015, Wuppermann has also taken the next step in building up its business activities on the American continent.

“The American market offers us a lot of potential. We see great market potential for our specialties from the Flat & Tube Products business unit in particular, for example our WGalweld tubes for the sprinkler market," said Dr. C. L. Theodor Wuppermann, President of Wuppermann Inc.

The Flat & Tube Products business unit includes seven production sites. Wuppermann produces flat strip in the Netherlands, Austria and very soon in Hungary. Tubes and profiles are produced in Germany, Austria and Poland. The company also has sales sites for flat products in Germany, France, Austria, Romania, Sweden and the USA. The sales network is rounded out by partnerships, such as with Galva Metal in Turkey.
