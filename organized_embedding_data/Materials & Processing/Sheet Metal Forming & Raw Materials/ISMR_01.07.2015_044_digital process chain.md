# Digital process chain

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GMBH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has unveiled its latest software version, AutoForm plus R6. This release offers a new set of powerful functionalities to support users along the entire digital sheet metal process chain.

“Several new options included in this release, such as for drawbead modelling, blank definition, enhanced springback compensation, systematic process improvement as well as the fully integrated hemming solution, further demonstrate and emphasize our continuous efforts to streamline and accelerate the entire engineering process of sheet metal forming,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

One of the major innovations in AutoForm plus R6 is the drawbead reduction strategy used in combination with the adaptive line bead. The main idea behind this innovation is to support users with an easy-to-use and realistic model when determining and optimising the drawbeads. The reduction strategy defines the order, the measures and the ranges in which a drawbead shape is modified, such as bead height, bead radius or groove radius.

“In this model, all of the defined measures are combined and made available to the user through one single parameter. Instead of dealing with all kinds of measures and geometries, the user can focus on this single parameter only, which allows for efficient process engineering even when complex parts are analysed. With this model, drawbead handling in engineering is aligned with the real tryout and is very easy to use,” explained AutoForm.

Another important novelty in this version are additional options for the blank definition. AutoForm plus R6 offers complex blank types and combinations of patchwork, tailor-welded and tailor-rolled blanks, which result in optimised material usage and cost performance. Chevron blank shapes for embedding provide additional options for material cost optimisation. In addition, AutoForm plus R6 provides strong nesting layout while taking into account the variation in costs of different coil widths. The user can calculate the optimal layout of the blank on the coil based on the minimisation of material costs and material usage. Users of hemming processes will also benefit from a fully integrated hemming solution.

AutoForm offers software solutions for the die-making and sheet metal forming industries along the entire process chain. See www.autoform.com
