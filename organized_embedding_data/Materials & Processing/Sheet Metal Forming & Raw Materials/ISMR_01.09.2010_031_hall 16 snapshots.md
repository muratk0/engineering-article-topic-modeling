## ARINOX S.P.A.

## I – Hall 16, Stand E24

## Website: www.arvedi.it

Arinox SPA is a manufacturer of stainless steel precision strip and is part of the Arvedi-Group in Italy. With new production lines, it can produce strip thicknesses from 0.03 – 1.00mm and widths from 2.5 – 1270mm, in temper rolled or bright annealed condition or with a SUT-Treated surface. The strip can be supplied in coils, cut-to-length sheet or in welded spools up to 1500kg. Arinox employs 220 people covering 21 shifts and has a capacity of 50,000 tons per year.
