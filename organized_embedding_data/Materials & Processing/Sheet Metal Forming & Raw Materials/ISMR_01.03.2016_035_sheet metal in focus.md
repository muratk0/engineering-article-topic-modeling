# Sheet metal in focus

[TARİH: 01.03.2016 ISMR]

THE UK MACHINERY GROUP has some fascinating new products to launch at MACH 2016 in April (NEC, Birmingham, UK) on its stands 4610 and 4712 in Hall 4.

“MACH visitors will see us unveil a highly flexible robotic bending solution, which can be fitted to new or existing press brakes, as well as an exciting sheet metal folding innovation from Morgan Rushworth that will significantly drive productivity. Our focus is usability, productivity and innovation – these products will be performing live on stands 4610 and 4712 in Hall 4 to practically demonstrate these benefits,” it told ISMR.

“With over 300 square metres of stand space in total, we will also be showcasing a selection of our plasma, waterjet and laser profiling machines along with a wide representation of our popular brands. Group companies Bison Machinery, RS Machinery and Selmach Machinery will all be present on the stand. Please get in touch to register for pre-show updates – you can reach your local branch by contacting info@ukmachinerygroup.com or visiting www.ukmachinerygroup.com.” ■
