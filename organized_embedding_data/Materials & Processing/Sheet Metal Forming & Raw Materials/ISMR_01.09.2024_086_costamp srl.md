www.costampsrl.it

Costamp produces dies for metal stamping components. It develops and builds manual, transfer and progressive dies according to customer requests. It also provides manual and automatic jigs for assembly parts. The manufacturer has been in the sheet metalworking business since 1987.

"We stay with the customer from design to construction of equipment prototypes to the final testing of the moulds using our automatic presses or testing them directly at the customer's premises. Sheet metal moulds (for drawing, transfer, progressive presses etc.) can be designed and manufactured in the shortest possible time. For over thirty years, we have been involved in cold sheet metalworking; designing, building and revising dies of all kinds," the Italian stamping specialist told /SMR.
