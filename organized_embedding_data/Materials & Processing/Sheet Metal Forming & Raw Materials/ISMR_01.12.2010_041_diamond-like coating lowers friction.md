# DIAMOND-LIKE COATING LOWERS FRICTION

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

DIARC-Technology has developed its next generation coating system which enables ta-C (tetrahedral amorphous carbon) structured diamond-like coatings to be produced in volume quantities for industrial applications. The company’s improved process is aimed at companies who need a hardwearing protective coating that can deliver increased efficiency and reliability of components operating in challenging and critical wear environments in mechanical systems.

The latest generation of coatings from DIARC, also offers extremely low friction in the presence of lubricants thus supporting reduced energy consumption. The super-lubricant coating has broad industrial applications for all types of wear parts in engines, hydraulic systems and power transmissions as well as precision tool applications.

DIARC is a pioneer in a low temperature vacuum plasma deposition process which can lay down a thin tough layer of diamond like carbon onto a range of materials from metals to plastics. See: www.diarc.fi
