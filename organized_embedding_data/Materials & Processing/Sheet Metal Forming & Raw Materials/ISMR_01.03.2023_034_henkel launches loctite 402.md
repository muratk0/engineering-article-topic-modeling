# Henkel launches Loctite 402

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

Among the highlights on the Henkel stand at the recent Southern Manufacturing & Electronics exhibition in Farnborough (UK) was the new Loctite 402 adhesive. It is designed for the assembly of small components and quick maintenance repairs.

“The cyanoacrylate instant adhesive develops handling strength twice as fast on most substrates in comparison to other assembly solutions (three to 10 seconds)," said the company. “This single component product remains three times stronger on most metals at temperatures of up to 150°C and offers sustained hot strength performance on selected metals at 135°C," it continued, adding that this is a key benefit for products whose power supply and connectivity generate a lot of heat.

The patented formula contains more globally accepted raw materials to enhance worker and workplace safety. A novel stabiliser package removes the need for ingredients that have recently come under greater regulatory scrutiny.

The company's continuous development of adhesive formulation has resulted in notable improvements such as higher service temperatures, the ability to cure through light surface contamination and improved curing on inactive metals without the need for a primer.

Henkel also showcased its ability to provide a complete turnkey package, with standard and bespoke dispensing systems. A cross-section of both manual and automated systems was also featured on its stand.

Henkel Adhesive Technologies provides adhesives, sealants and functional coatings. Its industrial product portfolio is organised into five Technology Cluster Brands – Loctite; Technomelt; Bonderite; Teroson and Aquence. Its Adhesive Technologies business comprises four areas: Automotive and Metals; Electronics and Industrials; Packaging and Consumer Goods and Craftsmen, Construction and Professional.
