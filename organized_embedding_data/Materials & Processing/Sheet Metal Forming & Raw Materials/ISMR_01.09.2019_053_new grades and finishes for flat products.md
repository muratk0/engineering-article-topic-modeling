# New grades and finishes for flat products

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

MARCEGAGLIA CARBON STEEL has enriched its offer of carbon steel flat products with new grades and finishes. These include:

* Hot rolled micro-alloys sheet and strip to EN 10149-2, with high yield strength for cold forming in grades from S280MC up to S700MC, with the option to provide tolerances of very narrow thicknesses compared to EN 10051.
* Enamelling steel by vitrification, according to EN 10209, in EK1 and EK4 grades.
* A semi-finished range of steels for magnetic applications' development, responding to the requirements of a market that is increasingly concerned about energy efficiency.
* New grades of hot-galvanized strips for structural uses, currently available from S220GD to S550GD, with zinc coatings of up to Z800.
* Pickled sheets S235JR/DD11 (thicknesses from 1.5 to 15mm – widths up to 2,000mm), new grades S355J0WP (thicknesses from 2 to 10mm – width of 1,500mm) and S355K2 + N (thicknesses from 3 to 15mm – widths up to 2,000mm), available in stock.
* LASER SHARP®, a range of structural sheets for laser applications that are suitable for special finishes (thicknesses from 1.5 to 15mm – widths up to 2,000mm).
