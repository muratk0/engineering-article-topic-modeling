New consumables available for air plasma systems

Hypertherm has introduced the availability of FlushCut consumables for more Powermax air plasma systems. The consumables are designed to work at 45 amps on Powermax45 XP, 65, 85, and 105 systems.

The consumables feature an angled nozzle bore that delivers the plasma arc at a 45-degree angle. Instead of locating the nozzle opening at the tip, the nozzle orifice is located on the side. This bends the plasma arc, causing it to exit the torch at an angle nearly parallel to the workpiece. This allows the operator to cut more flush to the base metal, reducing the amount of material left behind and reducing grinding time.

Collaborative robot GMAW system offers simplified programming

Universal Robots and Arc Specialties Inc. have developed the SnapWeld collaborative robot welding package. The robot-assisted, interactive GMAW system can be deployed easily and flexibly in existing manual welding booths.

Measurement software upgraded with universal CMM support

Verisurf Software Inc. has released Verisurf

®

2018 measurement software for automated quality inspection, reporting, scanning, reverse engineering, tool building, and assembly guidance.

The company reports that all CMMs, including previously closed legacy systems, are now open to Verisurf users. Expanded CMM support does not require controller upgrades or added software; the legacy CMM data is translated, allowing the operator to take advantage of the user interface, data management, and reporting functionality.

New functions include automatic feature recognition, which allows any CAD feature to be selected and immediately added to an inspection plan; quick measurement of torus shapes for parts such as O-rings; ability to use the probe shaft for measuring thin edges, such as sheet metal; and improved mesh editing and added mesh Boolean controls for combining and dissecting meshes with intersecting geometry.
