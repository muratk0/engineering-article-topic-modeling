Six-roller machine bends profiles in two opposite directions

MG srl has introduced the six-roller SZ series section bending machine, which bends the profile in opposite directions.

The profile passes through two pairs of dragging rollers. Two of the rollers are rubberized to increase the grip on the profile, and the last two are made of steel or nylon, depending on the type of material to be processed.

The two bending rollers move on a vertical axis and are retractable to allow insertion of the profile. They also move on a horizontal linear guide system with the possibility of working in an incline to minimize the straight part between one change of direction and the other.

Wedge clamp offers patented safety feature

Hilma has introduced a new version of its wedge clamp that features a patented safety notch added to the tapered clamping bolt. The notch supports the upper die in the event of a pressure loss.

The wedge clamp comprises a hydraulic cylinder block and a bolt guided within the housing. As it clamps and the bolt advances, it mechanically locks the die in place with its 20-degree tapered clamp surface. If a pressure loss occurs, this safety feature will engage with a mating notch in the tapered clamping plate on the die set, securely holding the upper die. During unclamping, the extended bolt retracts fully into the housing for easy die removal.

The wedge clamping elements, with single or dual action, exert forces of 3 to 69 U.S. tons and have a maximum temperature rating of 570 degrees F. The clamps can be actuated via hydraulic, electric, or springs.

The conductor tube is made from a single piece of copper with machined threads to hold the contact tip and nozzle in place. An O-ring prevents gas leakage in the event of a downstream obstruction, and a silicone sleeve over the copper tube minimizes radiant heat and improves impact resistance.
