# Bending from coil with mandrel

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

WHEN the bending technological parameter requirements (average radius, diameter and thickness) are stringent and finished part appearance is important, the coupling of a BLM GROUP 3-RUNNER and a SMART creates a fully integrated mandrel bending process that runs from coil.

At Tube 2016, BLM GROUP displayed an integrated process for the manufacture of copper HVAC (automotive or residential) components. The system involves three metal tube processing technologies (two of which were making their world debut) into one automated device.

A copper tube from a coil is straightened, end-formed, cut to size, bent and formed on the other end. The process demonstrated BLM GROUP's All In One concept, its in-depth knowledge of metal tube processing technologies and the capacity to integrate them into a single efficient process which focuses on the finished part, not the single job.

The 3-RUNNER, BLM's new system which made its world debut at Tube 2016, feeds the tube from a coil, straightens it, shapes one end, cuts it to length and automatically feeds it to the tube bender loader. The SMART multi-radius, right-hand / left-hand tube bending machine mandrel bends the part and automatically unloads it onto a conveyor belt which feeds the E-SHAPE. The E-SHAPE, also a new model, shapes the opposite end of the tube.

“By carrying out some operations in the background, this solution is extremely efficient in terms of cycle time and part cost. Furthermore, by starting from a coil, material utilisation is optimised because all the jobs are integrated in a single process. This simplifies logistics by eliminating all intermediate steps, the need for secondary operations on other devices and work in process inventory for maximum repeatability and production quality,” commented BLM.
