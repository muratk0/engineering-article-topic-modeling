# We outline the benefits of industrial adhesives and sealants for a wide range of metallic bonding and sealing applications.

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

ISRSAYS:

Today's adhesives and sealants are based on a wide variety of natural and synthetic technologies. These numerous formulating options allow products to be tailored that match the requirements of a huge range of disparate substrates and applications.

An adhesive is a compound that adheres or bonds two or more substrates together. Adhesives may come from either natural or synthetic sources. Adhesion may occur either by mechanical means, in which the adhesive works its way into small pores of the substrate, or by one of several other chemical mechanisms. In some cases, an actual chemical bond occurs between adhesive and substrate. In others, intermolecular forces hold the substances together. A third involves the moisture-aided diffusion of the glue into the substrate, followed by hardening. Good wetting of the surface is a pre-requisite for this kind of adhesion.

A sealant is a soft, pliable material that is used to seal cracks or joints where structural strength is not required. The sealant, initially a fluid or semi-fluid (or alternatively hot applied) placed between two opposing solid materials, becomes solid itself (by solvent evaporation, chemical reaction or both), and bonds to the surfaces to which it is applied.

It therefore accommodates joint movement without adhesion loss. The sealant's purpose is to prevent excessive absorption of water, penetration of other liquids, gaseous substances or airborne particulates. A sealant has the adhesive and cohesive properties to form a permanent seal.

“Adhesives and sealants share similar characteristics and perform similar roles. The main difference between the two is that sealants typically have lower strength and higher elongation than adhesives," explained FEICA (the Association of the European Adhesive and Sealant Industry).

## Adhesive and sealant market

According to a new market study from FEICA, "The European Adhesives and Sealants Market 2023-2028, adhesives based on polymer dispersions and emulsions are the largest product group used in Europe (over 45% share of volume demand in 2022). Reactive adhesives are forecast to exhibit the fastest growth rate in the next five years up to 2028.

"This growth comes from increased demand from high-performance applications, such as adhesives used to replace mechanical fastenings in assembly operations. Adhesives based on natural polymers are also forecast to see strong growth to 2028 due to an increased demand for sustainable materials," commented FEICA.

Kristel Ons, FEICA Secretary General, added: "There is increasing demand from consumers and brand owners aiming to improve their environmental credentials for greater use of sustainable materials in the products that they buy or make. Adhesives and sealants are no exception and are, in fact, enablers of the circular economy."

## Adhesive bonding benefits

"Metal is relatively well suited for bonding. Nevertheless, it is important to find the right product for the corresponding application. The choice of adhesive, a load analysis as well as potential surface treatment are decisive factors that lead to a good bond. Then bonding can increasingly replace welding, screwing or riveting," commented manufacturer, 3M.

"There is no such thing as one adhesive for metals. The properties of the adhesive, such as composition, viscosity or temperature resistance, must be matched to the surface to be bonded. For example, precisely fitting parts where small surfaces are joined together place different demands on the adhesive than in the case of surface laminations," it continued.

Adhesive bonds are durable and seal when applied properly. They also offer several benefits over thermal bonding processes, such as:

■ Enabling the use of dissimilar metals. The adhesive isolates different metals against galvanic corrosion.

■ Reworking the bond. The bond is easier to disassemble, rework and repair.

■ Reducing labour costs. Rapid and easy application that contributes to reducing the total cost.

■ Improving aesthetics. The bonding process does not distort or discolour the substrates.

■ Enabling the use of thinner substrates. Because the metal is not distorted, a thinner substrate can be just as strong.

■ Lower energy costs. Less energy-intensive than thermal bonding.

■ Improving worker comfort and safety. Most adhesives can be applied at room temperature without the need for intense heat.

■ Faster finishing. No risk of metal oxidisation or deformation, so finishing is quicker and easier.

Focus on sustainability

Environmental and energy-saving issues are of particular importance for today's manufacturers. Wind and solar energy are sustainable and renewable energy sources that have a much smaller impact on the environment compared to fossil fuels. Adhesives and sealants allow long lifetimes for solar power systems and windmills by meeting the demand of weathering resistance for long-term performance. They also adhere solar panels to all types of vehicles and buildings with the same weather-resistant, cost-effective and long-term performance properties.

"Adhesives and sealants reduce the weight of all modes of transportation making them more energy-efficient. For electric vehicles (EVs), this also significantly increases range. As demand for hydrogen-based fuel cells increases, adhesives and sealants play a crucial role in providing reliable sealing that facilitates automated production. Our industry is also increasingly installing wind turbines and photovoltaic panels at plants and factories to cover the electricity needs of production lines through renewables," outlined FEICA.

Sealants can also help in the journey to lower-carbon (and eventually net-zero/carbon-negative) buildings and provide long-term structural capability. For instance, they can weatherproof roofs and can be designed to tolerate extreme temperatures, water and chemicals; as well as to withstand significant stress for more energy-efficient buildings. Adhesives and sealants also facilitate insulation systems that are both durable and airtight (as well as carbon-neutral), ensuring less energy consumption.

"The [adhesives and sealants industry] is continuously innovating with debonding technologies to repair, reuse, repurpose and/or recycle to create a circular system. Innovations within our industry extend even further, with reusable silicone cartridges for example, and by using renewable energy to create the products," added FEICA.

Sustainability was a key focus of this September's European Adhesive & Sealant Conference and Expo in Tarragona (Spain). The current energy crisis; post-COVID recovery; shrinking market share of EU producers; increasing regulatory pressure and megatrends such as global competition are all converging upon the business sector simultaneously. In addition, the circular economy has become key to driving value for everyone.

## Choosing the right adhesive

The lower the viscosity of the adhesive, the easier it is to wet the workpiece surface. Most adhesives for metal are therefore two-component adhesives. Acrylic, epoxy and polyurethane adhesives are predominantly used.
