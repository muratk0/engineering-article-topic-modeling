# High-strength material pipes

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

STEP-BY-STEP shaping of a tube end or a tube has been made possible by a newly developed procedure for processing high-strength and ultra-high-strength materials. This revolutionises manufacturing, particularly in industrial applications where the requirements are set ever higher. Straight tubes can also be formed into increasingly extreme shapes.

Incremental tube shaping can be used for weight reduction purposes in the automotive industry, for example. A wide variety of products, manufactured with this new type of process, can be exposed to heavier loads and are lighter. The new development also scores with respect to resource efficiency as significant quantities of material are saved.

The worldwide innovation of incremental tube forming has arisen from intensive cooperation between transfluid Maschinenbau GmbH and the institute for forming technologies and

lightweight construction at Dortmund’s technical university. At the Dortmund seminar for tube and profile bending (DORP) on 1 October 2014, Gerd Nöker, managing director of transfluid, will be giving an expert lecture to industry professionals and the scientific community on the topic of “Bending and Forming: The Sequence Makes the Difference”.
