# What lies ahead for coil coating? We analyse the latest market analysis, technology updates and new initiatives in global coil-coating markets.

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Coil coating is a continuous or automated process of coating coils of metal with a polyester resin that includes cleaning, pre-treating and coating flat coils of steel or aluminium sheet. The process includes cleaning; exchange treatment; drying; primer coating; top coating and foil lamination. The most common formulations are polyester, polyflourocarbons, alkyds, acrylics and plastisol. The process is used in construction, automotive, industrial and domestic appliances, as well as other industries.

“Factors such as rapid industrialisation; the growing construction industry and burgeoning automotive industry in countries such as China, India; Malaysia; Indonesia; Vietnam; Japan and South Korea will facilitate the coatings' raw material market growth in APAC. Around 51% of the market growth will originate from the APAC (Asia Pacific) region up to 2025, explained analyst, Technavio.

Based on material type, the polyester segment is anticipated to dominate the global coil-coatings market in forthcoming years. Polyester coil paint is increasingly being used in both the exteriors and interiors of buildings as it can withstand harsh weather conditions, has good formability and excellent chemical resistance properties. It can also provide strong flow characteristics to multiple temperatures, thereby finding applications in pre-engineered buildings.

The coil-coating process, which is used to produce pre-painted metal, is continuously evolving to produce better products at a higher quality, faster and with lower environmental impact.

## Coil coating in focus

Coil coating is an automated and continuous industrial process that coats metal coils effectively. The coatings work on a simple, but successful, concept of cleaning, pretreating and coating flat coil, steel sheet or aluminium sheet.

According to analyst Future Market Insights, the steel segment is projected to hold the lion's share of the global coil-coatings market (by application). The coil paint process is considered to be an environmentally responsible way to apply paint to steel.

“It is widely known to be the most effective and affordable way to pre-paint, pre-clean, pre-prime and pre-treat steel. Pre-painted steel is weatherproof, waterproof and highly durable. Compared to post-painted surfaces, pre-painted cut-edge can hold up better over time owing to its superior surface," explained the analyst.

“The rapid expansion of the construction industry as a result of greater disposable income and increased government investment in public infrastructure is expected to drive sales. The increasing use of coil-coating paint in pre-coated items or metals used in construction wall cladding and roofing will continue to drive the market," reported Zion Market Research in its latest coil-coating market report released in May 2022.

"Additionally, rising disposable income and a higher standard of living fuel demand for modern furniture, which is predicted to drive up demand for functional coil-coated metals.

The automobile industry is also expected to drive global expansion. Colourcoated coils are commonly used by automakers because they give metals a consistent and high-quality finish at a low cost. The use of these metals improves the design and aesthetics of home appliances and allows producers to customise the texture," it added.

It estimates an annual growth rate (CAGR) in the coil-coatings market of approximately 4.3 per cent over the forecast period (up to 2028). However, it cautioned, the high cost of these processes could hamper market growth in the future.

"The high cost of processes involved in the manufacture of functional coil coatings is a factor impeding the growth of this market.

The coil-coating line (which includes various cleaning, heating and coating tools and equipment) is used in the manufacturing process. The higher the expense of these machines' upkeep, the higher the cost of the process and the higher the cost of functional coil coatings," it warned.

“Furthermore, due to the higher cost of precoated metal, consumers choose household appliances that require the production of fibre or plastic. This is due to the inexpensive pricing of these materials with the same performance as functional coil coatings. The functional coilcoating process necessitates the employment of various coating machinery and tools. The usage of these tools for an extended period of time causes wear and tear, restricting the adoption of functional coil coatings," it added.

The ever-increasing demand for oil and gas resources around the world is a major factor that is expected to drive the coil-coatings market in coming years.

## Market forecasts and insights

According to analyst The Business Research Company, the global coil-coatings market size is expected to grow from US\$ 4.67 billion in 2021 to US\$ 5.13 billion in 2022 at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 9.8%. It is expected to reach US\$ 6.55 billion in 2026 at a CAGR of 6.3%.

In its 'Coil Coatings Market Outlook – 2022-2029' from analyst, Future Market Insights, sales in the global coil-coatings market are slated to top US$ 5 billion in 2022. Expanding at a healthy 4.7% CAGR, the market size is projected to reach US$ 6.8 billion by 2029.

"Rapid expansion of the construction industry, owing to rising disposable incomes and increasing government investment in public infrastructure, are set to propel sales. The increasing usage of coil-coating paint in pre-coated products or metals used in wall cladding and the roofing of buildings will continue driving the market," it told ISMR.

The automotive industry is also anticipated to accelerate growth in the market. Colour-coated coils are extensively used by automakers as they provide a uniform and superior quality finish to metals within an affordable range.

"The global demand for coil coatings increased by a magnitude of 4.3% over the first half of 2021. Demand is estimated to witness a growth rate of 4.6% during the first half of 2022, a decrease of 10bps compared to earlier projections for the same period," added the analyst.
