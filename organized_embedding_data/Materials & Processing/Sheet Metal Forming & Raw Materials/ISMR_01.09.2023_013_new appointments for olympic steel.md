# New appointments for Olympic Steel

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Olympic Steel Inc., a metals service centre in the United States, has announced several recent leadership changes. Founded in 1954, Olympic Steel is focused on the direct sale of processed carbon; coated and stainless flat-rolled sheet, coil and plate steel; aluminium; tin plate and metal-intensive branded products. The company's CTI subsidiary is a leading distributor of steel tubing; bar; pipe; valves and fittings; and a fabricator of value-added parts and components. Headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio (USA), Olympic Steel operates from more than 44 facilities in North America.

Michael Heenan has been named to the role of general manager for Olympic Steel's Gary, Indiana, temper mill processing and distribution facility. Gerald "Jerry" Gideon has been named commercial director for Olympic Steel's Specialty Metals business segment. Richard Ranells is the new general manager for the company's processing facility in Latrobe, Pennsylvania. Scott Silverman has been named general manager for the company's processing facility in Streetsboro, Ohio.

All of the individuals moving into new roles will report directly to Andrew Wolfort, Olympic Steel's Vice President – Specialty Metals.■
