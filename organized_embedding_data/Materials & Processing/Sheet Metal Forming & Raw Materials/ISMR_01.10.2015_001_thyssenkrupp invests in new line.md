# ThyssenKrupp invests in new line

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

A TWO-YEAR £4.0 MILLION investment programme is entering a critical phase at Vetchberry Steels, the coil processing division of ThyssenKrupp Materials (UK) Ltd., a subsidiary of ThyssenKrupp AG.

Having completed the ground work, the custom-built components for the new flagship 1850mm-wide coil slitting line are currently undergoing installation in Birmingham (UK) by Heinrich Georg GmbH Maschinenfabrik, a third-generation, family-owned German company.

Under the close supervision of Adrian Palmer, Operations Manager, ThyssenKrupp expects it to be ‘the most efficient slitting line in the UK for stainless steel and aluminium coil production’.

“It took 11 full size trucks just to ship in the components from factories in Germany and the UK, which gives you some idea of the scale of the equipment. Installation is, of necessity, a painstaking business, and I can’t wait to see it up and running in full production,” said Adrian Palmer.

Billy Kingsbury, director in charge of the business, added: “This is an exciting time for us. ThyssenKrupp Materials has a strong reputation for delivering projects such as this one, on time and within budget. Clearly, the new line will yield significant benefits for ThyssenKrupp Materials‘ existing customers, and also enable us to develop new markets, securing Vetchberry’s position as the UK’s premier service centre for stainless steel and aluminium.“

Commissioning was expected to complete in September, with full production from October 2015. See www.vetchberrysteels.co.uk
