# Wrapping it and packing it

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Kingfisher Packaging will be showcasing a range of its packaging solutions at UK Metals Expo from 11-12 September 2024 at the NEC in Birmingham, UK (stand L50).

The company specialises in delivering innovative, sustainable packaging solutions that protect products during transport and storage as well as streamlining operations to boost profitability. Its portfolio, which includes Omegatech®, Wrapsolut and Dynawrap wrapping machines, is designed to meet the rigorous demands of manufacturers, engineers and fabricators. It also offers a free packaging consultancy programme.

"These machines, offering both semiautomatic and fully automatic options, are suitable for wrapping sheet materials; profiles; extrusions; rods; pipes and coils for a reliable and efficient packaging process. Our comprehensive range of consumables includes recyclable and biodegradable products, and the innovative Autoknot® stringtying machines as an eco-friendly alternative to plastic strapping," said the company.■
