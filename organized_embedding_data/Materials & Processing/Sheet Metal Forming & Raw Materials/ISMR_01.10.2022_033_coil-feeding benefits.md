# Coil-feeding benefits

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Coil feeding, as a convenient solution, offers various advantages for manufacturers. These include:

■ Material optimisation. Users can purchase raw material at lower costs and will handle less waste material thanks to nesting. This means reduced production costs.

■ A complete and automated line means that users do not need to keep loading and unloading the machine with metal sheet (making production much faster).

Coil processing enables users to reduce waste material and purchase costs, while increasing productivity in terms of number of pieces produced. There are also sustainable benefits as less raw material is used, hazardous emissions can be reduced and direct-drive engines mean lower frequency of changed parts.

ISMR: Are there any products/technologies that you have released this year, or are releasing at EuroBLECH in Hanover, that you would like to discuss?

FM: Our flagship product, recently presented to the market, is the coil-fed, combination "EffiCOIL" machine. Compact and able to offer high levels of production flexibility, speed and machining precision, EffiCOIL is a combined punching/laser machine. It incorporates a lasercutting system with a smooth sheet-metal support surface, with no fixed slat bed ("bed of nails" or “flatbed"). This feature improves cutting quality and avoids the frequent replacements and levels of maintenance required by using slats.

The laser-cutting system consists of two mobile units with reduced dimensions (cuttinghead and fume/metal scrap extraction unit) which operate synchronously. The punching system has no bulky metal structures and offers reduced dimensions; extreme production flexibility (thanks to indexed tools); high speeds (reduced weight) and precision. The two production systems interact (punching/forming and laser cutting) on a single coil-fed machine. This means the ability to process and produce workpieces of any shape, with virtually unlimited lengths. The punching and laser-cutting units can operate alternately over the same work area on the same workpiece.

This solution allows users to create components with complete and complex machining on a single machine automatically, with no need for processing on different machines and avoiding logistics, warehouse, tooling and labour costs.
