# Coil line productivity

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

BTH Import Steel in Poland boasts a coil-to-coil line that features two Timesavers' 71-series finishing machines. These heavy-duty machines can process 1.5km of stainless steel coil without interruption or manual intervention.

"Processing coil material is much more efficient than sheet-to-sheet both in terms of throughput and production costs," commented Tomasz Kotarba, a Board Member of the family-owned business. For the past 18 years, BTH Import Steel has worked with Timesavers on a variety of projects.

Timesavers provides two machines for coil finishing. The wet working 71 series and the dry working 72 series. Depending upon the size of the sheet metal rolls, users can opt for working widths of 1350mm, 1600mm or 2100mm. The machines can be equipped with two abrasive belts, two brushes or a combination of both.

"Each machine head can be operated separately, allowing for a variety of surface finishes. We value ease of use. Therefore, a grinding belt can easily be changed to another grit size. Users can choose the right grit of belts to achieve the required surface roughness and finish on the coil. Coils are efficiently finished at speeds of up to 30m/minute," added Timesavers International, a Dutch surface finishing specialist.

Designed for continuous operation, these grinding machines can be seamlessly integrated into fully automated production lines. They are built to withstand heavy-duty use in industrial environments. "If required, the machine can be purchased as a turnkey project. Everything can then be taken care of from dust extractors or water filter units to roller tables, a protective film coating machine, a decoiler, levelling machine and other accessories," concluded Timesavers.
