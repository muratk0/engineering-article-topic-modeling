# Roll grinding machine for U.S. aluminium market

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

TENOVA, A COMPANY in the Techint Group, has been contracted by JW Aluminum to supply a roll grinding machine for work rolls and back-up rolls, a chock changer for work rolls and back-up rolls and a chock tilter for its expansion plant at Goose Creek, South Carolina (USA).

Aiming to increase production in its South Carolina plant with the installation of equipment utilising green technology JW Aluminum chose Pomini - the Tenova brand in roll grinders – for its aluminium applications worldwide and in the USA.

Pomini Tenova equipment will be installed by the end of 2019 to support the launch of the new equipment, foreseen in early 2020.

The Pomini Tenova roll grinder will also be used on rolls from other, existing rolling mills on the same site.

JW Aluminum manufactures specialty flat-rolled aluminium products for building and construction markets; fin stock used by the heating and cooling industry; light gauge converter foil for the flexible packaging industry and honeycomb foil for the aerospace industry.
