# A smooth finish

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

FOR JUST OVER a year now, an SBM-L 1500 G1S2 Alu Mix grinding machine, made by LISSMAC Machinenbau GmbH in Bad Wurzach, has been used for deburring parts and rounding edges at Baumgarten GmbH Co.KG. The engineering firm designs, amongst other things, equipment for the concentrated animal feed industry, human food and pharmaceutical industries, medical engineering, breweries, coffee plants as well as rotary valves. Its laser cutting activities, however, leave sharp edges and burrs, especially on high-strength steel, so it uses one machine for both deburring and edgerounding of various sheet materials.

At LISSMAC Maschinenbau GmbH’s Beckum branch, I ran into an old friend from school who offered me, just like all of their customers, support in choosing the right sheetmetal processing machine. We were offered on-site deburring and edge rounding of our customers‘ metal parts. We were very pleased with the processing quality, and especially with the fact that the LISSMAC machine processes both sides of the parts in a single pass which dovetails nicely with our desire to deliver faster than our competitors. Compared to manual labour, roughly, the machine is about five times faster. Above all, the machine produces uniformly high quality. Immediately following the first few orders that were filled using the new machine, we were able to not only strengthen, but also significantly expand, our customers‘ loyalty,” commented Thomas Röttger, Technical Sales Manager, Baumgarten GmbH.

Deeming it suitable for the size of its parts, Baumgarten bought an SBM-L 1500 G1S2 grinding machine. With a working width of max. 1500mm and material thickness setting capabilities from 0.5 to 50mm, all cut parts can be deburred and edge-rounded. The SBM-L 1500 G1S2 machine is equipped with six grinding units, which process all inside and outside edges of the metal parts. Pressure rollers guide the parts through the machine.

First a set of grinding belts, one on the top and one on the bottom, remove laser cutting burrs. After the parts are free of burrs, the subsequent sanding block units round their edges. Two aggregates on the top and two at the bottom power the sanding blocks, which run in opposite directions to each other. These belts are equipped with sanding blocks featuring a combination of abrasive fleece and grinding flaps, which abrade the parts on all inside and outside edges in one single pass.

The SBM-L 1500 G1S2 can process steel, stainless steel, aluminium and, if required, high-strength steel, copper and brass parts. For further details, see www.lissmac.com
