# Smoothing the edges

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

NUMEROUS VISITORS found their way to the two booths belonging to ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH during Blechexpo. Its new deburring machines - the EdgeBreaker® 2000, EdgeBreaker® 4000 and the EdgeRacer® - were on display at the booth.

ARKU has been active in the deburring sector since May 2015, following the successful development of new deburring and edge rounding machines. Thanks to a cooperative agreement with Wöhler Brush Tech GmbH, ARKU is also able to offer the complete EdgeRacer® machine series. These are designed for processing various different materials such as aluminium, steel, stainless steel, titanium and more.

“EdgeBreaker® is the latest machine series developed specifically for processing thicker parts. Fitted with an oscillating roll, the EdgeBreaker® effectively removes, burrs and grinds thick slags from laser-cut, plasma-cut and flame-cut parts, while the transverse rounding units with their grinding heads round the edges from both sides. The EdgeBreaker® series is now available to customers for test and contract work in the ARKU levelling and deburring centre in Baden-Baden,” ARKU told ISMR.

Thinner stamped and laser-cut parts are processed by ARKU on the EdgeRacer®. Fitted with special disk brushes, these machines deburr and round sheet parts of up to 2,000mm in width. See www.arku.com ■
