# Industrial grinding solutions on display

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Weiler Abrasives, a provider of abrasives and power brushes for surface conditioning, attended GrindingHub 2024 in Stuttgart, Germany (14-17 May 2024), to showcase its portfolio of industrial grinding solutions. This included engineered solutions for spring grinding, gear grinding, roll grinding and metal conditioning to help manufacturers increase their productivity.

“The Weiler Abrasives approach is built on the foundational pillars of partnership, technology and service, with the ultimate aim of providing innovative, sustainable and scalable industrial grinding solutions that are tailored to the specific needs of the customer and their industry," said the company.

“The Weiler Process Solutions (WPS) programme helps to enhance and significantly boost a company's efficiency. The programme delivers customised solutions based on specific application needs, leveraging the latest technology and on-site technical expertise to optimise production processes for customers. As a result, it helps to reduce cycle times; eliminate production bottlenecks; reduce total cost per part; minimize rework; reduce waste; improve energy efficiency and sustainability; and improve part quality and consistency," it continued.

As a global manufacturer of surface conditioning solutions, Weiler Abrasives Group is dedicated to forging collaborative relationships with customers in metal fabrication and industrial production markets to tackle tough cleaning, grinding, cutting, deburring and finishing challenges.■
