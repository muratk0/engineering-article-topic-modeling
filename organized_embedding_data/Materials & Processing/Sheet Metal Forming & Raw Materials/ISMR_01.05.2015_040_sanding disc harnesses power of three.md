# Sanding disc harnesses power of three

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

Lasts Longer! Disc Life Test

3M has integrated its Precision Shaped Grain (PSG) technology into a new sanding disc which can last up to six times longer than conventional A/0 80 Grit, according to its Abrasive Systems Division.

“In six-minute removal tests, the new Cubitron II Sanding Disc has also been shown to remove twice as much material as conventional A/0 80 Grit,” commented the company (see opposite table).

The new Cubitron II brand Hookit Clean Sanding film disc 775L is an addition to the Hookit range of abrasive discs by 3M. Their grades range from 80+ to 120+ and can be used on a variety of surfaces including wood, paint, metal and composites. PSG is an innovative technology that is designed to cut faster and last longer than traditional abrasives. As the triangular-shaped grain abrades, it continually fractures to form sharp points that slice through the substrate and wear evenly.
