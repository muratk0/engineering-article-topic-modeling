# BURGHARDT + SCHMIDT GmbH www.b-s-germany.de and www.schnutz.com

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

THE BURGHARDT+Schmidt Group will present a new high-efficiency leveller for perforated sheet at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition.

"The leveller features an integrated pinch roll and high-precision adjustment mechanisms, which are also used in Schnutz levellers for high-tensile materials. The sturdy leveller is also equipped with vertically adjustable back-up elements, as well as 23 levelling rolls, and is therefore suitable for addressing all the levelling issues that are typical for perforated sheets. In particular, combinations of perforated and non-perforated areas can be levelled. Automation of the machine allows stand-alone operation as well as integration into a line," commented the manufacturer.

"Another exhibit will be our EP300 burr quality measurement system for contact-less measurement of slitting samples. With the system, you can determine accurate, reliable and fast burr, edge drop and cutting curvature of your stripes. Your individual slitting samples can be measured live at our booth. We will also show a column of an edge trimming shear, which is suitable for trimming as well as for the mid-section of strips of aluminium, copper and stainless steel (with material thicknesses of 0.25 to 1.2mm)."
