# The grinding principle

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

NOWADAYS, sheet metal working with deburring and surface grinding is an essential part of the modern production process. HANS WEBER offers a complete range of grinding machines with different technologies for different requirements.

Its main focus is on deburring and rounding of thermally separated parts cut on laser, plasma or gas cutting machines. When processing stainless steel sheets, a surface finish is also often required. WEBER machines solve this challenge through a combination of different grinding techniques in a single machine. In this manner, the components for diverse requirements can be manufactured economically.

“Steel components often require a defined radius and stainless steel components frequently also demand a specified finish. These parts are then often used where they are installed with completely pre-ground sheets. WEBER grinding machines use a through-feed method and can be designed for sheet sizes of up to 2m in width. This means that grinding of all customary sheet dimensions is possible,” WEBER explained. See www.hansweber.de/ ■
