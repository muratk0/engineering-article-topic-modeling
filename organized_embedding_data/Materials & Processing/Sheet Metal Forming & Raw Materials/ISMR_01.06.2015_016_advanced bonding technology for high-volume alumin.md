# Advanced bonding technology for high-volume aluminium vehicles

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

NOVELIS, A SPECIALIST in automotive aluminium sheet and Henkel Adhesive Technologies have signed a long-term agreement to collaborate on the development of advanced bonding technologies for the use of aluminium in high-volume vehicles.

“Our goal is for these next-generation solutions to be adopted broadly by the aluminium supply base, free from licensing, because it’s the right way to ensure long-term success for our industry and our OEM partners,” commented Jack Clark, Senior Vice President and Chief Technical Officer, Novelis.

The first product to launch, as a result of this partnership, is BONDERITE® M-NT 8453, an aluminium surface pre-treatment and adhesive bonding system for demanding vehicle requirements.

Referring to the difficult economic environment in fiscal year 2014, and Henkel’s figures, Kasper Rorsted (Henkel CEO) said: “We are very satisfied with our performance. We reached our financial targets for 2014… We expect to achieve organic sales growth of 3 to 5 per cent in the full fiscal year 2015. We expect adjusted return on sales to be approximately 16 per cent and anticipate an increase in adjusted earnings per preferred share of approximately 10 per cent.”
