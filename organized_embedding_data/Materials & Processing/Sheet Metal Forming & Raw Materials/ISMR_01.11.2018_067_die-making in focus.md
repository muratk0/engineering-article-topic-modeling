# Die-making in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

THE MEUSBURGER GROUP was present at the international sheet metal working technology trade fair, EuroBLECH 2018, from 23–26 October 2018 in Hanover, Germany. A range of visitors were at their booth at the show.

In addition to products, such as its patented bending unit Tribmate® Easy Rolla for bending operations and the practical slide-in aid for modules, an extensive product range of standard parts for transfer and large die-making was on display. With this product range, for the first time, Meusburger is able to supply this specific market with quickly available standard parts. Another highlight at the Meusburger stand was its H 3000 Clamping system which, since last year, has also been available for diemaking. With this new product, standardised die plates can be machined quickly, precisely and with repeatable accuracy, said the company. "The ongoing trend towards standardisation, which is becoming more and more prevalent in die-making, also provided many topics of conversation at the show. A logical development, since with progressing globalisation, ever shorter project lead times and efficiency improvements are required to remain competitive in the international market. More and more customers are discovering the advantages of standard die sets that can be delivered at short notice as opposed to individual custom-made products, which is a trend that particularly pleases us," the company told ISMR.
