# Grillo-Werke commissioned a precision slitting line for highquality special zinc strip from Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH.

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

Grillo-Werke AG in Germany manufactures a range of products, including customised special strip. The strip is used, for example, for organ pipes; fuses for special vehicles; in textiles and fashion; in construction and in the automotive sector. A new precision slitting line from Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH plays a central role in the production of high-precision zinc-based strip.

Grillo-Werke's metal division covers the entire value chain from zinc production to the production of finished and semi-finished products made of zinc and its alloys. The company operates a foundry and a rolling mill to produce customised zinc alloys.

Wilhelm Grillo was one of the founding fathers of industry in Germany's Ruhr region. A man of enormous energy, visionary thinking and social involvement, he started an impressive business development programme in 1842 when he was just 23. A hardware store in Mülheim an der Ruhr was followed by a zinc rolling mill in Duisburg-Neumühl in 1849. A few years later – in 1855 – he founded the first production plants for zinc oxide in Oberhausen, alongside two rolling mills and a gas production plant.

From 1881, the crude zinc needed by the plants in

Oberhausen was produced in a proprietary smelting plant in Duisburg-Hamborn (Germany). Liquid sulphur dioxide and sulphuric acid were by-products of this process. Today, Grillo-Werke's headquarters are in Duisburg-Hamborn and its CEO is Ulrich Grillo.

## Precision strip production

"Generally, the customer does not prescribe the alloy but specifies parameters such as strength, deep-drawing capabilities and other deformation properties. We then check which zinc alloy best meets the requirements and produce it ourselves in our factory," explained Dr. Peter Staubwasser, production manager in the Metal Division of Grillo-Werke AG.

In the metal strip sector, the focus is on order-specific strip, sometimes in small batches. The company manufactures its own coils for production.

Bernd Kasprowicz, responsible for the distribution of zinc strip, explained: "For us, 50kg batches are common; sometimes batch sizes of 500kg or 1000kg are ordered. We therefore need a flexible machine park that covers a large portfolio with many small set-ups."
