# Athader SL

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

## Athader produces

productivity-enhancing, metal-processing equipment for customers around the world. In the market since 1992, it is a leading supplier of steel and aluminium cutting lines. In 2012, it became a part of The Bradbury Group.

“Based in Spain, we specialise in the design and manufacture of slitting lines, cut-to-length levelling lines; recoiling lines and coil/sheet automated packaging systems. Over the last two years, with our partner Leveltek International, we have successfully supplied and installed five stretch-levelling lines, one in Australia and four in Europe," it explained.

"Due to sustained increases in our order book, we made two strategic decisions in 2022. We increased our manufacturing facilities in the Basque Country with the acquisition of three additional workshops. We also opened a new branch office in North America to assist the customer service and sales support team in the area, due to the significant number of installations on the continent," it continued.
