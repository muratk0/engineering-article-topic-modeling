# Horyong picks Strenx steel

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

SOUTH KOREAN-BASED Horyong Ltd. boasts 28 years of expertise in the production of truck-mounted aerial lifts, used in construction and other work sites. The company almost exclusively uses SSAB's Strenx performance steel in the special purpose vehicles which it manufactures.

As global urbanisation continues to grow, and the construction of more housing and office buildings intensify the demand on industrial equipment, demand has risen for safe and durable vehicles Horyong aims to meet this need by designing aerial lift cranes.

"I think that safety is the most important concern for our customers and our equipment is known to be strong," commented Soo-jeong Lee, Senior Researcher, Horyong. "Most of the steel that we use in our vehicles is SSAB's high-strength steel. When we design our truck-mounted aerial lifts and ladder cars for operation in high places, we use 90 to 95 per cent Strenx steel in the equipment. When operating in such high places, shaking and bending can be dangerous."

From its 68,359m² factory in Gimje, Horyong sends aerial lift trucks, cargo cranes and eco-friendly electric self-propelled cranes all over the country as well as abroad. Truck-mounted aerial lifts are mainly used in construction and other work-sites in high places, such as building maintenance. The vehicles improve logistics to high buildings, lift heavy material and move heavy objects.

"We produce the tallest ladder and aerial lift vehicles in South Korea – the highest of these reach up to 75 metres. To make the trucks more efficient by lifting more material and reaching higher up, the structure needs to be as light as possible. Strenx steel improves the quality of our products," added the researcher.
