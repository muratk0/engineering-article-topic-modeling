# ELEMENT plate processing

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

The ELEMENT cutting machine is a flexible all-rounder from Messer Cutting Systems.

“It is safe, efficient, can be automated, is versatile and can be expanded with hardware and software from a single source. The ELEMENT is suitable for all common cutting and additional processes, for series tasks with continuous use as well as special tasks where the focus is on perfect cuts," explained the cutting specialist.

“Whether cutting complex contours, holes or bevels for weld seam preparation: specially developed for use with the ELEMENT platform, the Bevel-S plasma bevel unit can be used very flexibly. It enables precise bevel cuts in the angle range from +45º to -45°. With rotation of ±540° and high acceleration, the machine can be used efficiently. The torch height is automatically and precisely controlled by the arc voltage during the entire cut," it added.

The ALFA oxyfuel torch allows nozzle change without tools, which eliminates the need to align the torch and drastically reduces set-up times. The cap unscrews easily, exposing the nozzle for easy inspection or replacement. Material is utilised more efficiently as the ALFA effectively cuts to, and along, the edge of the plate.

“The OmniWin 2023 CAD/CAM software from MesserSoft is a simple, clear and fast design. The nesting software adapts intelligently to the machines and requirements. It takes care of all the cutting tasks involved, tailored to order-based production with CNC-controlled, flame-cutting machines," concluded Messer Cutting Systems.

## Productivity savings

Efficient project management by Messer Cutting Systems Benelux, in combination with smooth installation of the machine, ensured a quick start to production at J&B Grijpers.

"The visit to Groß-Umstadt impressed us very much. The professional planning and preparation of the installation further confirmed our positive opinion of Messer Cutting Systems. But the biggest milestone for us was the first acceptance part that was successfully cut on-site. The first bevel cut met our expectations 100 per cent," confirmed Theo Vermeulen, Production Engineer, J&B Grijpers.

"We are especially excited now that the parts are 100 per cent prepared and ready within 55 minutes, compared to the three hours it used to take us for the same cutting task," he added.

The new machine not only saves a lot of time but, said Messer Cutting Systems, also achieves "excellent cutting quality." But welding preparation and work preparation are also much faster with OmniWin and the ELEMENT. The lower noise level is perceived by the operators as a significant improvement in their working conditions.

J&B Grijpers' production manager, Armand Cretz, is convinced: "The stability of the machine and the bevel function have already helped us a lot. We are now much more flexible and can process more orders in a shorter time. Time is money and this machine will save us a lot of time. We can now put that into building our grabs instead."

## Next steps

J&B Grijpers is already planning its next steps, especially towards digitalisation.

"We would definitely opt for this solution again. With the ELEMENT we have a good, stable machine with lots of possibilities and good support from the guys at Messer," summed up Edgar Joustra.

Theo Vermeulen added: "We are still working on the integration of the 3D software with OmniWin. In the next step, we will couple the ELEMENT with our ERP system for material management and scheduling. When we are familiar with the machine and all its possibilities, OmniFab implementation will be on the agenda."

The OmniFab Suite supports the creation of quotations and is used for production planning, control and monitoring. A demonstration of the modules has already taken place. At the end of September 2023, the Industry-4.0 software solution was discussed in detail with J&B Grijpers.

"We need more and more flexibility in our production. Machines must be able to cover more complex cutting tasks and save us working time. This is a trend that is becoming increasingly important from our point of view, especially in view of the dwindling number of qualified workers," Armand Cretz outlined.

He has the following advice for companies who also wish to move towards Industry 4.0 and production digitalisation:

"Take your time, select the right partner without rushing and look at what other companies are doing. Carefully consider all the necessary changes and technical requirements in your production hall such as flooring, electrical and gas supply," he concluded.

## About Messer Cutting Systems

Messer Cutting Systems offers products and services for the metalworking industry and focuses on the digitalisation of processes and products. It is represented with nearly 1,000 employees in five main locations with production facilities and is active in over 50 countries.

Its product range includes oxyfuel, plasma and laser cutting systems from hand-guided machines up to special machines for shipbuilding as well as machines and equipment for oxyfuel welding, cutting, brazing, soldering and heating. Spare parts, repairs, modernisation, maintenance and service, as well as environmental technology, round out its product portfolio. In-house software solutions optimise production and business processes. The product range is extended by solutions from technology partners, for example in automation.
