# Additive/3D print manufacturing and laser marking

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

Laser Lines exhibited a large range of laser marking and additive manufacturing at Southern Manufacturing from 6-8 February 2024 at FIVE, Farnborough (UK).

Its 3D printers included the XM200C metal powder-bed fusion 3D printer from Xact Metal which is suitable for universities, prototyping laboratories and small to medium enterprises that require multiple machines. Also on show were two printers from Stratasys; the F370CR and the J35 Pro.

"The Stratasys F370CR is the ideal additive manufacturing machine for more demanding projects that require stronger, stiffer parts with greater impact resistance. With a large build volume, the F370CR takes everything that the F370 offers and hardens the platform, enabling it to process high-performance, composite materials," explained Laser Lines.

"The Stratasys J35 Pro (Polyjet multimaterial 3D printer) is suitable for an office environment, matching the expectations of engineers and designers wanting to produce models intended as either concept or functional designs. With layer thicknesses down to 18μm, the J35 Pro prints quality models, including complex geometries and fine features, without compromising accuracy or detail," it added.

Laser Lines' industrial laser solutions on display at Southern Manufacturing included the Sisma LMD laser welder and the Datalogic Ulxye laser marker as well as other lasers in its portfolio including those from Novanta (to improve performance for laser-based diagnostic, analytical, micromachining and fine material processing equipment).

Laser Lines is one of the UK's leading suppliers of lasers and accessories, 3D printers and additive manufacturing systems. As a Platinum Partner of 3D printer manufacturer Stratasys, its portfolio encompasses FDM, polyjet, Saturated Absorption Fabrication (SAF), stereolithography and P3 polymerisation technologies.■
