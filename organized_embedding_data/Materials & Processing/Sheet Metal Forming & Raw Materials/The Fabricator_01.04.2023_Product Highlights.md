Ceramic cutoff wheel offers speed, durability

Walter Surface Technologies has introduced the ZIP XX cutoff wheel. The slightly thick, 1/16-in. ceramic wheel has reinforcements that help make it safe and durable. According to the company, it outlasts other ceramic wheels by at least 30%.

Formulated with 100% self-sharpening ceramic grain, the wheel cuts up to 10% faster than aluminum oxide wheels, the manufacturer states. The proprietary SMART RESIN technology allows the wheel to expose the fresh grains at the optimal moment. It is compatible with steel, stainless steel, and hard alloys and available in diameters of 4.5, 5, 6, and 7 in.

Weld fume extractor provides ductless installation

Donaldson Co. Inc. has released the Torit Downflo Ambient weld fume extractor, which gives fabricators the ability to weld in a variety of different workstation configurations.

The collector provides weld fume extraction without duct work or hoods. It comes prewired with an integrated control panel for simple installation, and the dust disposal drawer and hinged access doors allow for turnkey filter changes.

Designed to collect welding fumes as they rise and recirculate clean air, the unit uses the company’s proprietary Ultra-Web media technology.

AI tool sends alarm when it detects welding defects

Xiris Automation has released the Audio AI artificial intelligence tool to process welding audio signals. The fabricator teaches it an acceptable range of audio signal for a welding process, and the tool then sends an alarm in real time when it finds anomalies that could indicate welding defects, like burn-through and wire feed skips.

When a weld camera is added to the system, all welding audio, video, and AI tool results can be recorded for further evaluation for quality assurance.

The tool is included with the company’s WeldStudio 3 Pro software, which provides camera controls, graphical overlays, video recording and playback, and image and audio processing.

Platform automates robotic deployment with zero programming

OnRobot has launched D:PLOY, an automated platform for building, running, monitoring, and redeploying collaborative applications. By automating the process of getting a robotic application up and running, the platform allows complete applications to be deployed and redeployed directly on the manufacturing floor in a few steps with zero programming, all within a few hours, the company states.

The system automatically discovers most of the installed hardware and generates the robot motion based on the obstacles and cell boundaries defined in the workspace. The program logic, signals exchange, event handling, and robot movement are automatically created for the entire application based on a few inputs such as workpiece attributes and pick position.

The platform, which works with many robot brands, supports CNC machine tending, palletizing, packaging, and transferring (pick-and-place) applications.

Plasma cutter includes operator-friendly controls

Thermal Dynamics, an ESAB brand, has introduced the Cutmaster 30+ portable manual plasma cutting system. It can cut and pierce ⅜-in. metal, sever metal up to ⅝ in., and has a rated cutting output of 30 amps at 40% duty cycle. The system weighs 24.25 lbs. and uses 110- to 240-V 1ph primary power.

The new 4.3-in. TFT LCD is paired with a glove-friendly knob for selecting and setting parameters. Features include normal and grate-cutting mode, automatic inlet/outlet air pressure detection, automatic postcut air purge, and an automatic voltage detection function that sets maximum output amperage based on primary power.

Tools bends large profiles into complex 3D shapes

Boschert USA offers PBT AG’s upgraded 3D Bending Device for operators bending unique profiles into complex 3D shapes. The tool’s capacity has been increased, allowing for profiles up to 10 by 6 in.

The device now can be added to the PBT 35 and PBT Helix models, offering up to 75 tons of capacity for heavy-duty bending jobs. It delivers a 25% increase in range of motion over the previous-generation device.
