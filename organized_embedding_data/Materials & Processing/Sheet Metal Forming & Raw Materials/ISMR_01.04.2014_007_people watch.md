## On a roll

US rollforming specialist, Samco, has announced the promotion of Jorgo Ruco to international sales manager.

“Through his years of experience, Jorgo has proved to be a passionate team player, is driven for sales results and focused on servicing customers’ requirements. Jorgo will manage international prospects and customers and work directly with Samco’s agent base around the globe,” the company told ISMR.

Samco Machinery creates customised roll forming machines and coil processing equipment, down to the specific profile required by customers. Its innovations are aimed at improving line efficiency and end product quality for its customers.

“We take the time to understand the end product our client intends to manufacture so that we can recommend the best roll forming equipment for the job,” it explained. ■

## New Chair of Steelmaking Technologies

DR. RONALD J. O’MALLEY, the former chief metallurgist at Nucor Steel Decatur in Trinity, Alabama (USA), has joined Missouri University of Science and Technology as the new F. Kenneth Iverson Endowed Chair of Steelmaking Technologies. The Iverson Chair was established in 2006 through a US$ 2 million endowment from Nucor Corp., the United States’ largest steel company, and named in honour of Nucor’s founding chair. O’Malley also serves as director of the Kent D. Peaslee Steel Manufacturing Research Center.

“We are very proud to have Ron join our faculty,” said Dr. Wayne Huebner, Chair of Materials Science and Engineering at Missouri S&T. “He is a world-renowned leader in steelmaking practices and research who brings a wealth of experience from industry to our metallurgical engineering degree programme. His strong connections with the steel manufacturing industry and the metallurgical engineering profession also make him a perfect fit for this new role. I look forward to Ron’s contributions as a teacher and researcher.”

O’Malley served as chief metallurgist for Nucor Steel Decatur from 2002 until joining Missouri S&T this January. At Nucor, he was responsible for quality and metallurgical development at all Decatur operating units – from steelmaking to casting, hot rolling, cold rolling and galvanizing. He also served as a consultant to all Nucor facilities in steelmaking and casting. ■

## New CEO for POSCO

CURRENT POSCO PRESIDENT, Kwon Oh-joon (Chief Technical Officer) has been selected as the next POSCO CEO. POSCO’s 8th Chair, Oh-joon Kwon, was also officially inaugurated on 14 March 2014.

“The entire steel industry, including POSCO, is facing an environment wherein margins are squeezed due to steel oversupply and intensification of oligopoly in the raw materials market,” said Young-sun Lee, POSCO Board of Directors’ Chair. “We need someone to reinforce the POSCO Group's financial stability by promoting a strong reorganisation of the group’s business structure, and to ultimately enhance the POSCO Group value in the long run.

“We selected President Kwon as CEO candidate because we consider him to be the right person to take the initiative in re-engineering the POSCO Group by enhancing original steel competitiveness through technology and marketing convergence, and fostering long-term company mega-growth opportunities through the development of unique new growth technologies.”

Born in 1950, Kwon graduated from Seoul National University, where he specialised in Metallurgical Engineering, and earned his master’s degree in the same department at the University of Windsor and a doctorate at the University of Pittsburgh. He joined POSCO in 1986, and served as a member of RIST; assistant director of the POSCO Technology Research Institute; Head of the European office; Head of the Technology Research Institute and Head of RIST. He served as the Chief Technical Officer, starting in March 2011, and was promoted to become POSCO’s president in March 2012. ■

## SPI Lasers appoints new CEO

SPI LASERS, a leading designer and manufacturer of fiber lasers, has confirmed the appointment of Dr. Christian Schmitz as its new CEO.

To build on the success of SPI, the TRUMPF group is investing in further resources available to the company, particularly for research and development. As part of these changes, the CTO of the TRUMPF laser division, Dr. Christian Schmitz, will take up the new role as CEO of SPI Lasers assuming full responsibility in guiding SPI forward in the company’s growth and development.

John Tinson, Vice President, Sales and Marketing (SPI), said: “Over the last year, SPI has seen significant changes in fiber laser markets. One outcome from these changes has been a rapid expansion in market size and opportunity, and it is vital that SPI takes maximum advantage of this. It is an exciting time for SPI and we welcome Christian to the team.”

SPI launched new products in both pulsed and medium-powered product ranges at the LASER World of Photonics China exhibition in Shanghai in March 2014. See www.spilasers.com ■

## Bluescope appoints new non-executive director

BLUESCOPE Steel Chairman, Graham Kraehe, has announced the appointment of John Bevan as a non-executive director, effective immediately.

Mr Bevan, who recently retired as CEO of Alumina, spent 29 years of his career in a variety of senior management roles with BOC Group, including as a director on The BOC Group plc Board, Chief Executive Process Gas Solutions with responsibility for the bulk and tonnage business for the entire BOC group, Chief Executive Asia and country lead roles in the United Kingdom, Thailand and Korea. Mr Bevan is also currently a non-executive director of Ansell Limited.

Welcoming Mr Bevan, Mr Kraehe said, “We are very pleased to welcome John to the Board. His significant experience in heavy industrial operations and experience as a supplier to the steel industry globally will be a great asset to the BlueScope Board.” ■
