# CML ALLOYS SPECIFIES WARDJET

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

CML Alloys, a leading international supplier and processor of nickel alloys, titanium and speciality materials, has invested in a new WARDJet 100 hp waterjet cutting machine. This will be supplied by Wightman Stewart, WARDJet’s UK agent.

Based in Kingswinford, West Midlands in the UK, CML Alloys provides products in all wrought forms for corrosion, heat resisting and other applications where the material performance and properties are essential to the application. Services include the provision of fully machined parts to exacting customer specifications. CML now has a production line for the machining of sheet and plate titanium in response to demand from customers from areas as diverse as the oil, gas and pharmaceutical industries.

Rather than relying upon sub-contract waterjet cutting, CML Alloys decided to invest in its own waterjet facility. CML Alloys is extending its business into the supply of finished machine products and the WARDJet will cut materials such as titanium and nickel alloys. It will also provide the capacity for further expansion of the company’s waterjet cutting facilities. Installation was scheduled for November 2010.
