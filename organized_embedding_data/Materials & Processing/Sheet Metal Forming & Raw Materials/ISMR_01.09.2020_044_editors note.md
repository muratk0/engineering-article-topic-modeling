METAL STAMPING, or pressing, is a manufacturing process used to convert flat metal sheets, or blanks, into specific shapes. It is a complex process that includes several metalforming techniques — blanking, punching, flanging, bending, embossing, coining and piercing, to name a few. The cold-forming process uses tools, dies and stamping presses to transform sheet metal into different shapes. Stamping presses utilise the force of a moving ram to transmit force, or an amount of tonnage, to specific tooling to achieve a product’s final shape, often with little or no scrap and, whenever possible, with minimal operator intervention.

There are three major types of metal stamping techniques: progressive, fourslide (multi-slide) and deep draw. Progressive die stamping features several stations, each with a unique function – a strong solution for metal parts with complex geometry. Fourslide, or multi-slide, stamping involves horizontal

alignment and four different slides; in other words, four tools are used simultaneously to shape the workpiece. This process allows for intricate cuts and complex bends to develop even the most complex parts. Deep drawing involves pulling a sheet metal blank into the die via a punch, forming it into a shape. This type of forming is suitable for creating components that need several series of diameters. Short run metal stamping requires minimal upfront tooling expenses and can be a solution for prototypes or small projects.

Greater flexibility, shorter cycle times and a focus on energy conservation are just some of the hallmarks of the latest technologies. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing innovations.

## New processes, products and technologies

AIDA is a global specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metal forming automation equipment. Its servo and mechanical stamping presses range in capacity from 30 to 4,000 tons, from 1 to 1,500 strokes per minute, from gap frame to straight-side, high speed to cold forging, transfer and progressive die, material handling and coil-feeding equipment.

Its AiCARE (AIDA Information Care System) enables the collection, analysis and management of press information (such as forming information, operational data and maintenance information including machine learning-based, high-level analytics) by gathering data in real-time. It can be integrated with all types of AIDA presses as well as on installed presses (also from other manufacturers).

At the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (October 2018), it showcased its MSP highspeed stamping presses for the production of motors such as EV motors, HEV motors and energy-efficient home appliances motors.
