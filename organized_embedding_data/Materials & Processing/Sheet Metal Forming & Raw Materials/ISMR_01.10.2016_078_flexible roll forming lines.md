# Flexible roll forming lines

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

STAM SPA, based in Ponzano Veneto (Italy), is a specialist in flexible roll forming lines.

"Our roll forming lines are extraordinarily flexible, able to change in just a few minutes from the production of one type of profile (with a specific section and dimensions) to another, without the need for manual intervention, enabling their use for large, as well as small, production batches," Stam told ISMR.

One of these applications is a flexible roll forming line for U, C, Z and Sigma profiles. This can process profiles with thicknesses of up to 4mm, and variable width of up to 400mm. Stam has developed a complete production line which can punch, roll form, cut and stack these profiles in automatic and flexible mode.

The roll forming machine can quickly change from one profile to another, within the range indicated, with no manual tool change needed. For instance, the passage from a Cprofile 100/400/100, of 4mm thickness, to a Zprofile 50/200/50, 2mm thick, can be done in approximately six minutes. The pre-punching unit of the strip is automatic and flexible, and is equipped with all the punches necessary for the different products.

"The cutting process can be done either before roll forming, to avoid the need for interchangeable cutting tools for the different profiles, or after roll forming, by flying cutting systems. The traditional limit of flare of the profiles has been solved by using a set of forming tools, which remove tension, flare and torsion effects, for production of highquality profiles across the entire thickness range," explained the manufacturer.

"For the construction industry, the flexible roll forming lines for U,C,Z and Sigma profiles are complete production lines that can punch, roll form, make the middle and the end ‘Tab' supports, cut to length and stack the various profiles necessary to construct a building, in a completely automatic and flexible mode. All the adjustments are made automatically, with no operator intervention."

Stam also produces machines and equipment for cut-to-length and slitting lines for coils and lines for flexible manufacturing systems.
