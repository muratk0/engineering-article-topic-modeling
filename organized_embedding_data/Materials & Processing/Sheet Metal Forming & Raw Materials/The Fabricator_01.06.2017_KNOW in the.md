Plymouth Tube Receives the 2017 Rusty Demeules Award

Plymouth Tube of Streator, III., has been named the winner of the 2017 Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence. This is the second year in a row for Plymouth Tube to receive the award, which is FMA’s highest safety honor. The award, presented by FMA and sponsored by CNA, was created by FMA’s Safety Council to recognize a company with a demonstrated safety culture. The award is named in honor of Rusty Demeules, a significant volunteer contributor to FMA and president of the FMA Safety Committee from its inception in 1989 until 1996.

Plymouth Tube’s commitment to safety includes its Safety Engagement Program that focuses on safety tours, meetings, observations, and safety suggestions from employees and helps to generate improvements throughout the facility. Employees are also required to participate in their daily Stretch Program to help ensure their minds and bodies are ready for a safe day at work. Congratulations to Plymouth Tube for meeting this standard of excellence for safety.

"We are proud to sponsor the Rusty Demeules Award and to recognize Plymouth Tube as the 2017 recipient for their conscious work to create a culture of safety," said Roger Wilson, assistant vice president and industry leader, manufacturing, CNA.

FABTECH Mexico 2017 Brought Best Innovation in Manufacturing to Monterrey, Mexico

FABTECH Mexico 2017 Brought Best Innovation in Manufacturing to Monterrey, Mexico

FABTECH

®

Mexico, the leading manufacturing event in Latin America for metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing industries, brought the latest manufacturing innovations to Monterrey, Mexico, May 2-4, with close to 11,000 attendees and over 500 exhibitors throughout the three-day show.

Spanning 140,000 net square feet of exhibit space, the ninth edition of FABTECH Mexico provided attendees with the opportunity to see live demonstrations, explore the latest manufacturing and welding technologies, meet with industry experts, and attend educational sessions. Educational sessions covered technical topics such as automation, cutting, forming and fabricating, lasers, lean management, software, finishing, stamping, and welding.

"Corporate executives and owners made up over 28 percent of the attendance and bought machinery and technology directly off the show floor," said Mark Hoper, vice president of expositions and media, FMA, co-sponsor of FABTECH. "Giving businesses and customers the opportunity to come together at one place to learn, network, explore new products and technology, and make lasting connections is what has made this show the largest of its kind in Latin America."

The annual show comprises four co-located shows: FABTECH Mexico encompasses metal forming and fabricating as well as tube and pipe technology; AWS Weldmex features welding technology; METALFORM Mexico highlights metal forming and tool and die technology; and COATech focuses on finishing technology. The event is sponsored by the American Welding Society (AWS), the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association (FMA), SME, the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA), and the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI).

FABTECH is known throughout North America as the leading metal fabricating event with shows in the U.S., Canada, and Mexico. The U.S. shows rotate each year among Chicago, Atlanta, and Las Vegas. FABTECH Mexico rotates each year between Monterrey and Mexico City, and FABTECH Canada is held every two years in Toronto. The next FABTECH event will be held Nov. 6-9, 2017, in Chicago.
