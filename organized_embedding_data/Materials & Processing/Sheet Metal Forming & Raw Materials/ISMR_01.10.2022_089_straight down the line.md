# Straight down the line

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Italian coil-processing specialist, Saronni Srl, has expanded its range of straightener machines.

“We developed our straightening portfolio to address the difficulties of processing high-strength materials with wide thickness ranges and challenging flatness tolerances. Our solution, with multiple straightening rolls (9-21); counter-rolls at different levels (4-hi or 6-hi levellers) and automation, enables our customers to meet their production goals," it told ISMR.

“This expanded range of equipment offers significant performance increases and cost reductions. It also means that the most suitable solution, to fit the specific requirements of each customer, can be chosen so that no 'oversized' or 'over-specified' machines are supplied. This benefit is particularly clear on smaller production lines (e.g. for processing magnetic steel)," it added.

The manufacturer was keen to add that all the existing features (e.g. the ASA automatic straightening adjustment proprietary software) and options on the Saronni lines are still available and, over the last few years, have been upgraded with the latest available technologies.

The company, which celebrated its 70th anniversary in 2020, produces feeding and cut-to-length lines. With lines installed all over the world and hundreds of customers active (mainly in the automotive and household-appliance sectors), Saronni supplies customers looking for automated systems with high technological content. Its main products include coil-feeding lines for presses (short and long versions) which incorporate decoilers; straighteners; levellers; feeders etc. It also offers blanking, cut-to-length, embossing and disc-cutting lines.
