# Blanking line at Thyssenkrupp facility in Mexico

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

THE AUTOMOTIVE INDUSTRY in Mexico has been growing steadily in recent years. To meet this increased demand, Thyssenkrupp Materials de Mexico S.A. de C.V. expanded its operations in Silao, Guanajuato, by installing a Schuler blanking line featuring ServoDirect Technology. It produces up to 45,000 aluminium and steel parts per day and has increased the site's annual processing capacity by 70 per cent (up to 270,000 metric tons).

"We are proud to be the first supplier in Mexico to offer customers a combined blanking line for carbon steel and aluminium blank processing," said Olaf Voss, President, Thyssenkrupp Materials de Mexico.

Since early 2016, the line has been processing aluminium and carbon steel for exposed and unexposed automotive applications, including high-strength steel of up to 1,200 N/mm² of tensile strength. This allows Thyssenkrupp Materials de Mexico to meet the automotive industry's needs for lightweight construction. From the coil line to the stacker, Schuler supplied all the components, which are coordinated and operated by a single control and safety system.

"At the heart of the line is an 800 metric ton servo press with energy storage and an output rate of up to 75 strokes per minute. Depending upon the type and size of the blank, productivity is increased by up to 150 per cent, compared to a blanking line equipped with a conventional mechanical press using the programmable slide motion. Our ServoDirect Technology not only ensures maximum flexibility and productivity, but also keeps the wear on dies and machinery to a minimum. Lines using this technology are equipped to process the widest variety of material, such as aluminium or high-strength steels. Even surface-sensitive material can be handled safely on the same line," Schuler told ISMR.

The coil line features a maximum coil speed of 120 metres per minute, and the roll feed with servo direct drive has an acceleration of up to 13m/s². Belt cleaning devices and wipers all along the blanking line reduce cleaning intervals, with separate pairs of rolls for steel and aluminium, including automatic cleaning units with a quick-change system.

## Kemppi appoints new CEO

VILLE VUORI (44) has been appointed as the new CEO of Finnish welding specialist, Kemppi Oy. Currently, Vuori acts as the CEO of Incap Oyj. According to current plans, Vuori will begin work at Kemppi by the end of this year.

Before Incap Oyj, Vuori was the CEO of Kumera Drives and Finavia's SkyHow Ltd. He also has extensive experience in global management positions whilst at ABB.

"We are very pleased to welcome Ville to the Kemppi team. He has extensive experience in leadership and business, with strong experience of managing companies successfully. Ville is familiar with the characteristics of both large and small companies as well as public companies and family-owned businesses. He is used to operating in an international environment and has experience in working with many of the customer segments important to us. He worked as a plater-welder before starting his studies, which means he has also seen the world of welding on a more practical level," commented Teresa Kemppi-Vasama, Executive Chairman, Kemppi Oy.

Headquartered in Lahti, Finland, Kemppi employs over 600 welding experts in 17 countries. Its offering includes welding solutions - intelligent equipment, welding management software and expert services - for industrial applications and ready-to-weld needs. See www.kemppi.com

## Rocky Pinheiro joins PennEngineering®

ROCKY PINHEIRO HAS joined fastener specialist, PennEngineering®, as global vice president of quality. Pinheiro will be responsible for driving enterprise-wide quality systems improvement, excellence and evolution and will function as a corporate subject matter expert with PennEngineering's established quality teams in each plant and selling organisation.

Pinheiro will also serve as a resource for the various implementations of third-party quality registrations, most importantly efforts to implement IATF16949, one of the automotive industry's most widely used international standards for quality management. He also will become involved with all forms of third-party compliance requests and will lead the company's quality metrics and measurement and global sustainability initiative.

Pinheiro has significant experience and expertise in the quality arena.

He most recently served as vice president of quality at Acument Global Technologies, a multilocation global manufacturer of automotive fasteners. Before that, he led Lean Implementation for the six-site Detroit Medical Center and has held relevant roles over the years with Johnson Controls and Fiat Chrysler North America.

Pinheiro holds a Ph.D. in Organizational Management from Capella University, an M.B.A. from the University of Detroit Mercy and a B.A. degree from Cal State Northridge.

## New GM for Kentucky Electric Steel

OPTIMA SPECIALTY STEEL Inc., one of North America's leading independent producers of specialty steel products, has announced the appointment of Ron Witzig as general manager of Kentucky Electric Steel ("KES"). KES is a value-added producer of SBQ and MBQ products serving a diversified customer base across end markets including transportation, industrial equipment, yellow goods, agriculture and construction.

"I am excited about the opportunity to join the team at KES and create value for all of our stakeholders," said Ron Witzig, adding, "While the steel industry has recently faced challenging market conditions, my goal is to position KES for future success and I firmly believe that success begins with a safe workplace and superior customer service."

Witzig has a wide range of experience in areas such as engineering, maintenance, steel-making and hot rolling in long products and plate. He has served in various management roles including operations manager, superintendent, general manager, plant manager and VP. He holds a B.S. in Mathematics from Illinois State and a degree in Electronic Engineering Technology from Illinois Central.

Headquartered in Miami, Florida, Optima Specialty Steel, Inc. is one of North America's leading independent manufacturers of specialty steel products. It produces highly engineered products, including seamless cold drawn pipe and tube, cold finished steel bars and flats and various SBQ and MBQ products.

## Steel and Tube initiative

FROM 3 JULY 2017, general manager of S&T (Steel and Tube Stainless), Steve Kubala, will add the leadership of Steel & Tube's roofing, coil and purlins businesses to his current portfolio. Steve was appointed to his current position in December 2014, and has held a number of senior management roles since rejoining the company in 2007.

New Zealand-based S&T is a leading provider of steel solutions with a national network of branches and distribution centres. It engages in markets and projects across infrastructure; construction (commercial and residential); heavy and light engineering; energy; manufacturing; viticulture and rural sectors. The company distributes and manufactures steel products - from nuts, bolts, nails, piping, roofing and farm fencing, to the largest structural steel products used in commercial construction, such as purlins, girts, joists, universal beams and seismic mesh.

"The roll forming businesses have had significant investment in Auckland and the Christchurch businesses are currently undergoing similar investment. Steve's appointment reflects the company's desire for further growth within this area," explained Dave Taylor, CEO, S&T.
