# Swedish subcontractor, Norrsken Metall, is targeting growth with a new waterjet.

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Norrsken Metall is a manufacturing company that has been based in the Skelleftea region of Sweden for many years. Its specialty is processing materials in different dimensions (mainly iron, aluminium and stainless steel as well as other materials such as copper and plastic).

Cutting; machining; welding; bending; surface treatment; assembly and delivery all form part of its service. The manufacturer also undertakes various development projects such as drawing, designing and producing customer ideas from prototypes to full industrial scaling.

Norrsken Metall has refined its waterjet cutting experience since 2000. Among the metals that it cuts by waterjet are stainless steel, aluminium, brass and copper. It can cut, for example, stainless steel in thicknesses of up to 100mm. It can also plasma cut materials of up to 40mm thick.

## Greater cutting capacity

The company's existing waterjet cutting machine was experiencing recurrent operational problems, so it decided to opt for a new solution. Patrik Flodin and Niklas Fällman, owners of Norrsken Metall, contacted Christian Svensson, regional sales manager at Water Jet Sweden (based in Ronneby, Sweden). Svensson has more than 25 years of experience in waterjet cutting solutions.

"We developed a machine solution with Norrsken Metall that provides both increased capacity and better reliability," explained Christian Svensson."The new WJS Grand model waterjet cutting system, chosen by Norrsken Metall, is a robust and powerful machine system for machining all types of materials, and can also handle large or odd dimensions.

"Thanks to the WJS patent, the machine has the same level of precision, regardless of machine size. The 3.2 x 7.7m working area has been divided into two working zones to allow loading and unloading while the machine is running," he added.

## Equipped for growth

There are many new improvements, explained Christian Svensson. The systems for abrasive supply and sludge handling have been designed to handle cutting without interruption, and the machine is equipped with a more powerful pump for faster cutting.

"Thanks to its new investment, Norrsken Metall has both the capacity and reliability to meet growing demand for waterjet cutting in the region. It has already started to notice the growth as it has, among others, received several assignments from the new battery factory that is being built in its neighbourhood," outlined Christian Svensson.

Made to be versatile with a wide range of design options, the Grand offers large tables sizes with the capacity to manage large and odd-size materials. It can be found in specialised job shops and as a workstation in industry production lines. Each machine is tailored to fit customer process requirements and can be adapted for multi-job processing and unmanned operations.

"In 2022, the Swedish government estimated a total of 40,000 new jobs in the Norrbotten and Västerbotten region, in Sweden's northern territory. The growth is a result of large industrial set-ups in the area. The City of Skelleftea Municipality predicts that 'perhaps it will be precisely here that growth and population growth will be the greatest in Sweden until 2030'. This growth also creates new business opportunities for existing industry," concluded Waterjet Sweden.

The machine safety solution is based on light beams. As soon as a circuit is broken, the machine stops.
