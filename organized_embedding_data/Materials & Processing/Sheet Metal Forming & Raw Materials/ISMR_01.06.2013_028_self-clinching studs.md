# Self-clinching STUDS

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

PEM® TYPE FHP™ self-clinching flush-head studs install permanently into thin stainless steel sheets to provide solutions for attachment applications requiring superior corrosion resistance.

“They will install more easily and at a lower cost than weld studs and additionally can contribute to thinner and lighter designs for component assemblies in the medical, food service and marine industries, amongst many others,” the manufacturer, Penn Engineering and Manufacturing, explained.

PEM Type FHP flush-head studs mount flush and permanently in stainless steel sheets as thin as 0.040”/1mm (and greater) and become integral parts of an assembly with just a mating nut required for final component attachment. Their manufacture from A286 stainless steel imparts high hardness and the studs can be specified for use in stainless sheets with hardnesses of 92 or less on the Rockwell “B” scale.

PEM Type FHP flush-head clinch studs can be supplied in thread sizes of #4-40 through #10-32 and M3 through M5 and in a range of 10 standard lengths.
