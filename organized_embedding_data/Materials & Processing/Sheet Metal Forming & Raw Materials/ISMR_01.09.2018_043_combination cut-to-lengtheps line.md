# Combination cut-to-length/EPS line

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

THE MATERIAL WORKS, Ltd. (TMW) has unveiled a new application of EPS technology to produce 'stay flat' pickled dry plate up to 1" (25mm) thick. This is done by a specialised cut-to-length line utilising the EPS Version 6 'mini-cell' in combination with a heavy gauge stretcher leveller or temper mill. The EPS process is an environmentally-friendly replacement for acid pickling of hot rolled steel strip developed by The Material Works, Ltd. (TMW) of Red Bud, Illinois, USA.

"ConAg equipment, rail car, tank/vessel manufacturers and other users of large plate require a dry, scale-free product because it simplifies paint preparation, keeps their shops cleaner and eliminates blasting operations. However, it has proved to be a difficult product to source; there are few acid pickling lines that run material over 1/2" (12.5mm) thick and a few CTL processors that can transform coils of 1/2" - 1" (12.5mm - 25mm) material into sheets," explained the company.

TMW President, Kevin Voges, then added: "Rather than perform two separate operations on plate, we thought: 'Why not combine the pickling and CTL operations into a single processing line?' Integrating a smaller EPS cell into a heavy gauge stretcher levelling or temper pass CTL line creates an efficient, compact CTL line that can produce 'stay flat', dry, rust-resistance plate up to 1" thick."

A patent on this application of EPS technology has been applied for and the technology will soon be offered for sale to steel producers and processors.

See www.thematerialworks.com or www.epsprocess.com for further details.
