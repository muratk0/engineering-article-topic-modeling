# Coil-fed laser blanking lines

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 ISMR]

LANTEK SHEET METAL Solutions has signed a global collaboration agreement with the sheet metal (cutting, punching, and bending) division of DANOBATGROUP to ship Lantek software with its coil-fed laser blanking lines. DANOBAT's coil-fed laser blanking lines will now feature Lantek software.

"Under this collaborative agreement, DANOBAT sheet metal customers will have Lantek software on their coil-fed laser blanking lines and all their machines. They will also benefit from our extensive global network of offices, technical service and distribution channels. Our experience in the metal industry will allow end customers to optimise processes and get the greatest return on machinery and software investments," the Spanish sheet metal software specialist, Lantek, told ISMR.

Headquartered in Gipuzkoa, Spain, DANOBAT has over 50 years of experience. From raw material to finished product, DANOBAT's machines encompass all metalworking processes. This includes the focus of the agreement is software provided by Lantek. Lantek is a global specialist in CAD/CAM/MES/ERP software solutions for companies that manufacture metal parts from sheet, tubes and profiles with any cutting technology. It also integrates software for Smart Factories. Lantek is globally headquartered in the Basque Country of Spain.
