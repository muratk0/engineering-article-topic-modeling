# 5 Quick Tips Regarding Spiral Duct Machine

## What is Spiral Duct Machine

A spiral duct machine is a machine for producing spiral ducts. Spiral air duct, also known as spiral bite seam thin-walled pipe, was first used in the military industry of western countries, such as the exhaust system of warships and ships, and later used in civil facilities such as trains, subways, and mines. By 2000, according to statistics, the use of spiral air ducts in office buildings, shopping malls, and subways in the United States reached 95.6%, and the application of spiral air ducts in central air-conditioning in residential buildings reached 72.5%.

**1. Use:**

The spiral duct machine is a thin-walled pipe made of a spiral bite made of metal strips. It is round, without welding, without air leakage, and without water leakage. It is mostly used for air supply and bulk material transportation, instead of traditional white iron pipes. It is a kind of air duct, the straight pipe of the whole mechanism does not require a manual beating. Its main uses are as follows:

● Air supply, including ventilation, such as fresh air supply and exhaust air, has a wide range, such as factory workshops and production site air. Due to the generation of harmful gases, it needs to be discharged outdoors, and outdoor air needs to be transported indoors. At this time, it is necessary to use the air conveying pipe with a large flow and low pressure. The spiral air pipe is the most suitable. Generally, the galvanized iron pipe is used, and stainless steel pipe is used in corrosive and particularly humid places.

● Send cold air. The most common is the pipeline of the central air conditioner, which requires insulation materials. The spiral air duct can be pasted with thermal insulation material, and the appearance looks beautiful.

● Exhaust fumes. The kitchens of restaurants, restaurants, and guesthouses produce a lot of oil fumes, which need to be discharged. The use of circular air ducts is the oil chimney. Here, the spiral duct should be called a fume duct.

● Dust off. In some factories, there is a lot of dust in the production workshop, and special dust removal devices are required. For pipes with large air flow, spiral air ducts can be used.

● Bulk material conveying. In the production process of some factories, it is necessary to transport loose particles, especially the use of spiral air ducts with low specific gravity such as foamed plastic particles, which has low cost and good effect.

● Good product compatibility and a high degree of standardization;

● Strong airtightness;

● The ventilation loss is small; the ventilation noise is small, and the round pipe is better than the square pipe.

● Factory inspection is convenient;

● Anti-equilibrium external pressure (negative pressure) procedure;

● The overall layout of the installation is beautiful and the grade is high.

**4. Connection Method:**

● Hoop-type connection of spiral air duct Hoop-type connection The two ends of each pipe section are rolled into drum bars, and one end is reduced into a small opening. When installing, insert the small opening into the large opening according to the airflow direction, and use a steel tow hoop on the outside to connect the two The drum rib at the end of the pipe is tightly connected and finally bolted through the earrings to fix and tighten.

● Spiral air duct plug-in connection The plug-in connection is mainly used for rectangular or circular air duct connections. First, make the connecting pipe, then insert the air ducts on both sides, and then use self-tapping screws or rivets to fix it tightly.

● Spiral duct insert type connection Insert strip type connection to rectangular air duct connection Insert different forms of inserts into both ends of the air duct, and then compact.

● The hose connection of the spiral air duct is mainly used for connecting the spiral air duct to the components (such as the air supply port on the side of the static pressure box of the diffuser, etc.). Clamp the hose tightly.
