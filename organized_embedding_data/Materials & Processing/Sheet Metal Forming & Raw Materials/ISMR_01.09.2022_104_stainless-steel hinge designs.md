# Stainless-steel hinge designs

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

WDS Components Ltd has expanded its range of leaf hinges, adding robust anticorrosion designs. The UK manufacturer and supplier of standard parts and components has also released new lift-off detachable hinges, enabling rapid and simple door removal. Both the new hinge types are manufactured from 316 stainless steel.

“Our 316 stainless-steel leaf hinge design (part number WDS 8605) has been developed for use on doors in hygienic applications, as well as marine environments, providing strong resistance to salt-water corrosion. The durable hinges are also well suited to machinery and industrial cabinets, and their construction also means they can be used long-term in any outdoor environment," said the manufacturer.

The hinges can be used in environments subject to regular washdown with aggressive detergents. The design provides long-term resistance to corrosion, and rounded edges prevent accumulation of bacteria for easier and more effective wash-down.

The new hinges are also available in a stud fitting that can be inserted directly into a door or panel and secured with a nut. This metricsize stud design removes the need for fitting with screws and doesn't leave the hinge exposed. This creates smooth edges without any crevices, adding to its hygienic credentials. This design also protects the hinge from removal, making it “tamper-proof and suitable for protecting factory machinery or outdoor cabinets." Traditional countersunk designs that can accommodate screws or bolts are also now available in 316 stainless steel. The new leaf hinges are part of WDS' stock guarantee programme that guarantees the availability of a specified order quantity, in return for a 10% discount on any unavailable units.

"The hinges feature a high-quality, polished mirror finish, ideal for luxury and premium environments, as well as a matt style, which is more forgiving and suitable for concealing blemishes and hand-prints. Hinge lengths are available in 30mm and 48mm, and the new range joins our existing line-up of hinges that are available in a variety of materials, designs and sizes," added WDS Components.

WDS' new lift-off, detachable hinges (WDS 8620), also made from 316 stainless steel, are designed for fast, simple access when door removal is required (for example, to aid installation or for maintenance procedures).

The design features two different leaves that can be removed with a simple lift-off action, while firmly securing together when the top plate is positioned over the hinge pin. As the hinges include countersunk holes on both sides, it is a reversable design that can be used on left- or right-hand hinges. This saves time in design and adds ease and flexibility in ordering and on-site installation.

"The detachable hinges have a high-quality mirror finish and are durable for long-life use. They're also rust-free and can be used in aggressive environments. The hinges are 50mm long and are available in a wide range of countersunk hole position layouts. They can support a maximum weight of 50kg," added WDS.
