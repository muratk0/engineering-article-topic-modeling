## Automatic hardness testing

Keighley Laboratories has added a Zwick Roell ZHV-10 automatic micro and macro hardness tester to its Test House resources. Featuring fully automatic Vickers indentation measurement and dedicated hardness testing software, the new equipment will bring about measurable improvements in accuracy, repeatability and productivity, to the benefit of the company’s internal Heat Treatment division and clients of its Technical Services department.

Used for the precise measurement of surface hardness and case depth of metallic materials, with test loads of between 100g and 10kg, it will introduce considerable time savings over previously manual routines, freeing up highly skilled operators for other analytical duties.

The hardness of a material is determined by its resistance to indentation by a hardened ball or diamond indenter, then measuring the size of the permanent impression produced. See www.keighleylabs.co.uk

## Rapid analysis

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GmbH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has enhanced its hydroforming products. AutoForm Hydroforming contains innovative functionalities for the rapid analysis and simulation of the entire hydroforming process.

## Cut-to-length lines

STAM BUILDS a wide range of machines for cut-to-length, blanking and multi-blanking lines for different materials: cold rolled steel, hot rolled steel, zinc-coated materials, pre-painted materials, stainless steel, aluminium, zinc etc. Tailor-made lines are designed, manufactured and assembled completely in-house.

STAM cut-to-length lines are suitable for processing materials of up to 2200mm wide and thicknesses of between 0.2 and 20mm. Special attention is given to the levellers, when delicate material is processed, especially stainless steel, prepainted steel, aluminium, zinc etc. so that no traces of oil or grease appear on the strip. The STAM generation of levellers comprises a range of machines from 300 to 2200mm working width and thicknesses from 0.2 to 20mm.

The cut-to-length lines can be equipped with high performance cropping shears, hydraulically or mechanically operated, fixed or swinging for trapezoidal and parallelogram cutting. STAM also supplies rotary shears and supply stackers, single or multi-station. STAM software can be linked to the customer’s operating systems via an interface for fully integrated control. STAM also produces machines and equipment for rollforming lines, slitting lines for coils and flexible manufacturing systems. See www.stam.it

## Mate introduces RAPIDSET

TOOLING SPECIALIST Mate has introduced RAPIDSET adjustable thick turret canisters for use with Mate Original Style and AMX punch press tooling. RAPIDSET is also compatible with Amada Standard, Amada ABS and Wilson Standard tooling styles.

Designed to provide quick tool changes and make punch length adjustment faster with existing tooling inventories, RAPIDSET is the latest innovation from Mate. Available in A and B station canisters, RAPIDSET works with keyed or non-keyed punches and features a non-rotating clamping head with easily adjustable clamping screw for setting punch length. A pre-loaded, self-contained spring pack provides consistent stripping pressure, consistent die penetration and reduced slug-pulling.

It punches material thickness of up to 0.250 inch (6.35mm). Competitive canisters are restricted to 0.150 inch (3.81mm) material. www.mate.com
