# Printing on steel

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

AS THE WORLD evolves towards product personalisation, Italian materials specialist Marcegaglia has launched Marcegaglia Digital Print.

“Using in-line printing, Marcegaglia Digital Print is an innovative solution which enables printing directly on steel, even in small quantities, to ensure maximum product customisation,” it told ISMR.

“The advantages of the process include increased image durability; scratchproof protection; UV protection (RUV) and corrosion resistance (CR). Marcegaglia Digital Print is suitable for any kind of project,” it added.

The technology enables Marcegaglia to offer a wide range of products aimed mainly at the design and interior fittings sector, with a focus on the residential and naval construction sectors. In the construction sector, it can also be applied to insulated panels and panels for sectional doors.
