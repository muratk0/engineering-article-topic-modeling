# Congratulations to the 2019 Safety Award Winners

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 The Fabricator]

The FMA/CNA Safety Award winners were honored at a reception and dinner where company members received awards for achieving safety records better than the OSHA recordable rate for their NAICS codes.

The 2019 Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence was presented to Tempel Steel of Chicago, Illinois, for its commitment to ensuring a safe culture in its facility.
