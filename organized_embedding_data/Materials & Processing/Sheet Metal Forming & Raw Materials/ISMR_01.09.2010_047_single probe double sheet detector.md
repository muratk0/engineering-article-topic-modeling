# SINGLE PROBE DOUBLE SHEET DETECTOR

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Prime Controls, Inc. has introduced a single probe double sheet detector that can operate in locations where space limits the use of dual probe detectors. The new Pulser Double Sheet Detector features a newly designed single probe with a pulsed electromagnet that quickly samples ferrous metal sheets without holding on to them like previous single-probe detectors that used permanent magnets.

In addition, the Pulser is able to detect sheets that are 30% thicker than comparable double sheet detectors, which makes it suitable for more types of steel sheet feeding applications. It consists of a sensor probe and a control unit. Four models of Pulser probes are available with different diameters and magnet strengths to detect metal thickness from as little as 0.25mm (0.01”) up to a maximum of 6.4mm (0.250”).

The sensor probes are ruggedly constructed of stainless steel and have a built-in metal proximity detector, which allows the detector to turn-off after thickness measurement, count sheets and/or detect that the last sheet has advanced.

The DS200 control unit features simple one-button calibration that automatically measures the thickness of a sample sheet, calculates the double thickness value and sets the reject threshold. An optional two sample calibration process allows the control unit to detect and respond to variations in metal thickness as small as 5%.
