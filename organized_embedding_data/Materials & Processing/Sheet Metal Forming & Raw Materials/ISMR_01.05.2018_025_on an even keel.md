# ON AN EVEN KEEL

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

"Swiss specialist Cendres+Métaux SA is using KOHLER part levellers for parts made from gold, silver, platinum and titanium"

Levelling parts is a necessity for many areas of the strip and sheet metal working industry. Often, levelling parts is associated with large and thick components. However, part levelling machines from KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH have also been tasked with more delicate work, such as the work done for Cendres+Métaux SA (C+M) in Biel/Bienne, Switzerland.

For more than 100 years, Cendres+Métaux SA has worked with the noblest and most precious of metals; gold, silver, platinum and titanium. Levelling technology is essential for the broad selection of small and tiny components, as both semi-finished components and finished products, for dental and medical technology applications, as well as for the jewellery and watchmaking industries. The company also recycles and processes precious metals worldwide.

Precious metals (gold, silver and platinum) are all part of everyday business at Cendres+Métaux SA (from the French cendre = ash and métaux = metals). For more than 100 years, the company has been combining precision with high levels of purity in the development and production of semi-finished components and finished products made from precious metals. In addition to its European subsidiaries in France, Italy, United Kingdom and its Asian subsidiary in South Korea, Cendres+Métaux now has its own affiliate in Shanghai, China.

## High quality parts

Cendres+Métaux SA (C+M) was founded in 1885 by Louis Aufranc as a precious metal smelting company in Biel/Bienne, right in the heart of the Swiss watchmaking industry. It has around 450 employees.

"We see our core competencies, in particular, as the development and casting of homogeneous precious metal alloys with precise compositions," explained Raphael Volery, head of foundry/forming at C+M.

Semi-finished components and precision profiles, with specified and proven mechanical properties, are produced for the company's proprietary and custom product ranges. C+M has an extensive production facility for the production and processing of its precious metal alloys, with smelting and casting equipment and a suite of machine tools for further cutting and forming.

"One of the safeguards guaranteeing the technical production quality requirements is a new part levelling machine from the Peak Performer 50P range by KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH, based in Lahr (Black Forest, Germany)," added Raphael Volery. KOHLER has been manufacturing levelling machines and systems for the sheet metal working industry for decades. At C+M, the new part levelling machine replaced an older unit in May 2017.

"The old unit was replaced primarily because 100% replacement part deliveries could no longer be guaranteed. As the need for the investment became clearer, a secondary consideration was that a new unit should also be able to level thicker materials than before," added Volery. "That's why we decided to invest in a KOHLER machine after thorough research."

## Meeting the specifications

One of the criteria for investment in the KOHLER Peak Performer 50P.600 was the geographical proximity of Biel/Bienne to Lahr in Baden-Württemberg.

Mostly the work involves cast bars of rolled and interstage-annealed strips of gold, silver or platinum alloys. The requirements specification for the KOHLER Peak Performer 50P required sheet thicknesses of between 0.5 and 12mm, which is within the operating range of the Peak Performer (the maximum thickness of which is 15mm). The required strip width was specified as 450mm.

The decision-makers were particularly keen to have a simple, fast, and neat cleaning procedure for the levelling rollers. To achieve this, KOHLER has equipped the Peak Performer with a motorised cleaning and quick-change device. At the push of a button, the user can extend the upper and lower levelling roller frames separately out of the unit and thoroughly clean levelling and supporting rollers, saving time and money.

"This is a decisive requirement for us," said Volery. "As levelling can create dirt, if we did not eliminate this after every change of material, we would have a mixture of hundreds of alloys. We cannot and do not wish to allow that."

The Peak Performer 50P.600 is also equipped with intermediate rollers – C+M selected this optional extra. Implemented in the form of spirally grooved rollers, they are installed between supporting rollers and levelling rollers and prevent pressure points or marks on the delicate materials to be levelled.

With an emphasis on relatively long and narrow strips, a camber levelling unit is required which has been carried across from the previous unit at C+M and adapted and integrated by KOHLER into the Peak Performer, below the inlet table. The camber levelling unit prevents the appearance of what are known as camber defects, a uniaxial curvature in the longitudinal running direction of a strip or a profile.

"We use the camber leveller as required, but mainly for levelling long profiles where we connect it mechanically," revealed Raphael Volery.

"The Expert Calculation System that we developed simplifies the setting of the component-specific levelling parameters. The control unit calculates the optimum levelling parameters from material data and component dimensions. We already stored the alloy data based on customer specifications for products repeated regularly at C+M. This makes it easier for machine operators to carry out product changes quickly and safely. Machine operators are additionally supported by a control unit with a convenient multi-touch interface that we re-designed," added KOHLER.

There are various semi-finished products, made of precious metals, which can be further processed. These include watchcases; watch faces; sheet metal for brand logos; watch straps and clasps.

In response to the requirements of leading precious metal specialist Cendres+Métaux SA, and with the delivery of a Peak Performer 50P with optional features, KOHLER Maschinenbau GmbH has proved that levelling is not just a task for coarse applications.

## Arpal One, a unique watch

It's neither a Laurent Ferrier watch nor an URWERK, yet the signatures of both firms appear on its back. Laurent Ferrier and URWERK have created a piece of work that is unique, for only one-of-a-kind watches qualify to be sold at the Only Watch auction in aid of the battle against Duchenne muscular dystrophy.

"It's a cleanly cut watch. My friends know my love of cars, a predilection expressed in this watch. This new creation is like streamlined coachwork, fluid and without any affront to the eye," explained Laurent Ferrier. "Such a design required special material. For that we turned to the Cendres+Métaux company in Biel."

The case was shaped from an exclusive alloy called ARPAL+, specifically developed for this unique watch. ARPAL+ is an alloy which contains precious metals and resists wear and scratches better than white gold or steel. Its colour never fades, nor does it oxidise. This alloy was first developed for the electronics industry, where it is much appreciated for its strength and because it is easily worked.
