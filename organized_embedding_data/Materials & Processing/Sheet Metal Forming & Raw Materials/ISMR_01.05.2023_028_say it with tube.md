[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

## In Part Two of this series, we highlight a selection of some of the latest global tube and pipe forming innovations (including cutting and bending)

ISMR SAYS:

“Tube and pipe processing can often be a complementary activity to sheet-metal forming in job shops."

Transformation often take place in sheet-metal job shops. Tube and pipe manufacturers face growing production challenges from increased competition, rising material costs and thinner profit margins. Demand continues to rise for low weight and durable tubes and pipes. Seamless tubes and pipes with multiple bends are also needed in sectors such as energy, healthcare and transportation.

Tubes are measured by their outer diameter and are usually smaller and less rigid than pipes. Bending involves metal forming processes used to permanently form pipe or tubing. The objective is to obtain a smooth bend without flattening the tube or pipe. Bending processes include form-bound and freeform, as well as heat-supported and cold forming. Factors such as material wall thickness, tooling and lubricants used all affect the bending process. Generally, round stock is used in tube bending. However, square and rectangular tubes and pipes may also be bent to meet job specifications.

Form-bound bending, such as press bending or rotary draw bending, are used to form the workpiece into the shape of a die. Straight tube stock can be formed using a bending machine to create a variety of single or multiple bends and to shape the piece into the desired form. These processes can be used to form complex shapes out of different types of ductile metal tubing. Freeform-bending processes, such as three-roll-push bending, shape the workpiece kinematically so that the bending contour is not dependent upon the tool geometry.

Tube end forming machines use hydraulic or mechanical presses. Often these presses use a repeated hammering motion to shape the tube. Because tube end forming is a shaping process and requires no cutting, there is no wasted metal at the end of the process. In addition, the tube end forming process can provide improved tube strength, better connectivity between components and improved sealing capabilities.

## Tube and pipe processing

Parmigiani Macchine SAS has a universal pipe and section bending machine with CNC control in its portfolio.

Prada Nargesa SL, from Spain, has over 50 years of experience in manufacturing industrial machinery. Its new generation CC60 and CC90 CNC non-mandrel tube and pipe benders, featuring touch-screen control, can bend up to 180° curves on hollow profiles. Materials can be round, oval, square, rectangular and made of a range of different metals such as steel, aluminium, stainless steel, copper etc.

Its new MC650 Nargesa section and pipe bending machine is designed to carry out mass production through the control, which saves the position of the rollers in each curve to repeat the same operations later without longitudinal positioning control. It is suitable for manufacturing flanges, greenhouses, railings and large structures for construction and has a maximum capacity for bending round pipe of 4" inches or 101.6mm.

Preco's RT1000 is designed for precision laser cutting small diameter tubes for medical devices such as surgical instruments, stents and other implantable devices. It incorporates a granite base, precision servo drives, rigid work holding, fibre-delivered laser sources and Preco's fine cutting head.

PLATINO®is the general purpose 2D laser cutting machine by Prima Power which cuts a broad range of materials and thicknesses. Change-over to tube processing can be achieved with zero set-up time, said the Italian manufacturer. It is available in two sizes (1530 and 2040), and with laser powers ranging from 2500W to 5000W.

Platino Fiber Evo is now available with high brilliance, energy-efficient fibre lasers with 2-, 3-, 4- or 6kW power. The new fibre laser cutting head with adaptive collimator, designed and manufactured by Prima Power, is equipped with a single focus lens.

3D laser cutting machines from Prima Power include the Laser Next 2141 and Rapido multipurpose machines. Laser Next 1530 and Laser Next 2130 models are suitable solutions for massive or specialised automotive production. Its LASERDYNE multi-axis laser processing systems can also be used for 3D cutting, welding and drilling.
