# OLAF GEHRELS OVERSEES FANUC OPERATIONS

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

A FRESH COAT

SPECIALIST COATINGS company Cooper Coated Coil (CCC) has taken on a new marketing officer to assist with the company’s new branding and future growth. Heather Clapton joined CCC in August with eight years of experience in marketing and PR. As part of her initial induction to CCC, Heather will lead a complete rebrand encompassing a new logo, signage, web and collateral, as well as pushing the brand further into the engineering and bakeware consumer fields.

CCC applies specialist coatings onto aluminium and steel coils for use in the manufacture of housewares, bakeware, automotive and white goods. Metalrax, CCC’s parent company, has seen a rise in its profits over the last year. FOR MORE INFORMATION, SEE www.coopercoated.co.uk

FANUC’S RESTRUCTURING of its European operations is aimed at improving its position on European markets. All former operations of FANUC divisions active on European-wide scales are being merged into the new FANUC Luxembourg Corporation (FLC), headquartered in Echternach, Luxembourg. Olaf Gehrels, for many years European president of FANUC Robotics, has been appointed FLC’s president.

The move merges all FANUC’s European divisions i.e. FANUC Robotics (industrial robots), FANUC FA (factory automation, CNC systems and drives) and FANUC Robomachine Europe (milling machines, electric-powered injection-moulding presses and wire-erosion machinery) under the aegis of a single parent corporation. FANUC’s European-wide operations have nearly 800 employees and consolidated annual revenues of 600 million Euros.

The regional organisational structures of its various subsidiaries, such as FANUC Robotics Deutschland GmbH, FANUC FA Deutschland GmbH and FANUC Robomachine Europe GmbH, will be retained. Wherever sensible, they will be supported by teams formed at the European level.

Olaf Gehrels regards the move as giving FANUC a leaner organisation: “Our growth targets demand a clear-cut organisational structure. Although we must remain highly mobile on a regional scale, we have to be able to project a strong corporate image European-wide. The key to our success is the trusting relationships we have with our customers.”
