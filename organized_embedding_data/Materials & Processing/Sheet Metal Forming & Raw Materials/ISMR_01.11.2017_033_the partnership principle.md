# THE PARTNERSHIP PRINCIPLE

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

Italian coil processing specialist, Viganò International, is keen to deepen relationships with existing customers as well as develop new customer bases globally.

Italian coil processing specialist, Viganò International, designs and manufactures single machines such as multi-roll levellers; flying shears; rotary shears; circular blade shears; decoilers and recoilers; straighteners; side trimmers and coil cars as well as a range of coil processing lines and systems. Its lines are designed to process high-strength and heavy gauge materials. These include cut-to-length (CTL), slitting, blanking and pickling lines. It has also developed a tension-levelling system for 'hard-to-process' materials as well as brushing machines introduced into hot roll processing lines to preserve the quality of the material.

The company has also developed a design of "movable lines", a more effective version of standard lines, which can be easily installed and uninstalled as well as re-located depending upon customer requirements.

"Coil processing customers are asking for higher levels of automation, Industry 4.0 compliance and continuous equipment upgrades."

ISMR SAYS:

Products made from stainless steel, hot/cold rolled steel, annealed, galvanized and carbon steel, aluminium and other materials, can be processed on Viganò coil processing lines. Viganò International is now part of Galbiati Group Srl, which specialises in precision machining, the assembly of large machines and the manufacture of special equipment under the Galbiati brand.

## Customised projects and lines

"We are growing every year, with new orders in the pipeline and higher turnover anticipated this year. 150 employees work in our manufacturing facilities (just outside Milan). The majority of our customers are in Europe (particularly Italy) as well as in countries such as Mexico, China, South Africa, Brazil, South Korea, North Africa and India. We work through agents in specific countries as well as with some of the best automation and software companies in Europe to develop our lines," Simone Fumagalli, Viganò International's sales manager, told ISMR.

"We never sit still – we always analyse market developments and try to anticipate future customer requirements. We are constantly looking ahead…"

Viganò International has worked on a few customised projects this year, including a brushing machine for the surface cleaning of hot-rolled materials. It has also revamped coil processing lines for some customers in Europe. CTL lines can feature plastic film or paper to protect material surfaces as well as brushing, edge trimming, scrap cutting or winding functionality.

"We were also chosen for a new project, manufacturing and installing a new cutting line for an Italian customer to process aluminium across a range of thicknesses, from 0.15mm up to 6mm material, for high-quality automotive products. We just received the order and will deliver the line mid-next year. We are also proud of a delivery we made two years ago to a Polish customer, the first line of its kind in Europe, a push-pull pickling line (with slitting line) used across various industry sectors. It was designed as a very eco-friendly line to limit pollution. We worked very closely with the customer on this," added Fumagalli.

## Market trends

Fumagalli reported that Viganò International's customers are asking for more automation on different parts of the line. Industry 4.0 is a key consideration and data interchange is crucial – aspects such as remote networking and remote assistance/diagnostics become much more important.

"Customers want to upgrade and improve their lines because the market constantly requires higher and higher quality parts. If you are using 30-year-old machinery, you will not get this level of quality. Many of our customers are involved in the automotive industry, which is continuously growing. Aluminium is also becoming a more popular material to process – many of our customers are now processing aluminium and higher strength materials now," he commented.

"Nowadays, it is possible to get the same performance on thinner materials than was possible on thicker materials in the past. Thinner material processing now is far better than thicker material processing ten years ago and we see process improvements all the time."

## Aims and objectives

Fumagalli is keen to deepen relationships with existing customers as well as develop new customer bases globally.

"For me, the greatest satisfaction comes from working with the same customers, year after year. This type of partnership, based on mutual trust, is wonderful – it is not just about price. We cherish these strong relationships and wish to develop them further," he explained.

"We also want to develop new customer partnerships in countries where we are not currently present, such as India. We are constantly looking to develop our geographic customer base."

Quality is a key issue for the Italian coil processing specialist – it chose to keep its manufacturing operations in Italy, keeping its competence centre firmly at its home base.

"It is crucial for us to continue with the quality levels we have today. We chose to manufacture an Italian product. We do not want to manufacture more cheaply in China, for example. We want to continuously maintain and improve quality levels. Our technical department is always looking for new solutions, innovations and improvements – these are important R&D investments for us," Fumagalli told ISMR.
