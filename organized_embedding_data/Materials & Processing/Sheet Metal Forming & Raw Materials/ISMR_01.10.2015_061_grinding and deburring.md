# Grinding and deburring

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

WEBER manufactures grinding and deburring machinery for metal sheet and sheet metal parts. This November’s Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart will see the company showcase its new WEBER PT series and the NLCA series.

“Launched in their new design at the last EuroBLECH exhibition, the PT machines will replace the WEBER TT series which has enjoyed many years of success. In terms of technology and ergonomy, the series meets all requirements for deburring, rounding, descaling and surface grinding metal sheets and sheet metal parts of all thicknesses,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

“Special grinding processes are available for the relevant requirements. One of the distinguishing features of the various machining stations is their compact design. All machining stations can be accessed from the side for tool changes. SIEMENS touch panels are attached to the machine for ergonomic operation. In line with proven WEBER philosophy, settings on the grinding stations are made using the touch panel or by means of the "i-Touch" rotary knob that enables a user to navigate smoothly over the surface of the touch panel.”

For rounding and descaling, WEBER has the patented DR planetary head station and the MRB multi-rotation brush system. For larger working widths, both of these have several machining heads arranged side by side to ensure that the grinding result is the same at all points. The WEBER MRB has rotating machining heads which each carry roller brushes. When both methods are combined, radii of 2mm can be achieved.

The WEBER NLCA series works wet and uses the same grinding method. The series was developed specifically for mass-produced punched parts. See WEBER in Hall 1, Stand 1704 at Blechexpo or visit www.hansweber.de
