Square offers functions of multiple squares in one unit

Omni Tool Works offers the OmniSquare multipurpose square. It folds compactly for easy portability. The blade flips out and clicks in at 45, 90, and 135 degrees.

Like a combination square, the blade slides to any depth setting while maintaining 90 degrees. Like a T-bevel square, it can copy or be set at any angle and provides the degree readout of the angle.

In its closed position, the square acts as a depth gauge for many uses. Like a compass, it can scribe up to a 12-in.-dia. circle for rough cutouts.

The square is made of anodized aluminum with stainless steel hardware. Inch markings are permanently die cut, and white powdered paint is baked into the resulting impressions for high visibility.

Oscillating laser beam helps boost cutting accuracy

Scanlab GmbH has developed the Weldyna scan head for cutting and welding with a high-frequency oscillating laser beam.

The oscillating beam cuts through thick metal sheets and fiber-reinforced plastics. With its high wobble motion frequencies of several kilohertz and the availability of freely definable scan patterns, the beam offers fast cutting speeds and improved cut quality, the company reports.

Overlapping laser beam motions relative to the seam geometry enable tearresistant welding of diverse materials, even those with poor weldability. Splatter formation is reduced, making weld seams and cut edges smooth while slowing down optics wear.

The scan head is designed for multikilohertz lasers of high beam quality and features digital servo control, an integrated sensor system for real-time monitoring, and water and air cooling. It can be easily integrated or installed with collimation and focusing modules of commercial fixed optics.

Gripper kits show automation no longer stops at the wrist

Rethink Robotics has introduced the ClickSmart™ series of end-of-arm tooling that incorporates smart sensing and rapid swapping capabilities. It includes the ClickSmart Plate, a toolplate that lets users swap end effectors in seconds without any tools. According to the company, embedded sensors in the five new gripper kits enable intelligent grasping and smarter part handling on its Sawyer™ robot powered by the Intera™ 5 software platform.

With a click, manufacturers can switch out end effectors on the robot in seconds. The plate has an embedded memory module, which stores the configuration of the attached end effector. When the plate is attached to any Sawyer robot, the robot immediately recognizes the type of end effector and how to control it, with no programming needed.

The gripper kits are available in large pneumatic, small pneumatic, large vacuum, small vacuum, and foam vacuum options.

Tube bending, cutting incorporated in one tool

Schwarze-Robitec has expanded its cutting tool, integrating it into a bending tool for large diameters of up to 3 in. According to the company, this one-step process decreases scrap material up to 90 percent and reduces production times.

The integrated tool is suitable for producing parts in high quantities. Machining of longer pipe units instead of cut-off pipe sections is possible, which reduces the time required for machine loading and unloading.

With the new cutting technology, all components receive a clean cutting edge suitable for further processing. This makes the technology suitable for series production, such as in the automotive sector or supplier industry. The controlled chip removal process keeps most of the chips produced during the cutting process away from the machine.
