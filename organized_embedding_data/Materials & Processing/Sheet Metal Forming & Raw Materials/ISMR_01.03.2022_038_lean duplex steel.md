# Lean Duplex steel

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

Rising prices, supply problems and a shortage of raw materials are currently causing tension in business climates in Germany and further afield.

Wilhelm Modersohn GmbH & Co. KG, a manufacturer of industrial components and fastening systems for concrete and masonry, is focusing its attention on the price development of stainless, acid and heat-resistant steels and the corresponding supply of raw materials. The company is based in Spenge, North Rhine-Westphalia (Germany).

After more than 50 years of experience and expertise in the processing of stainless steel and Lean Duplex steels, the company has also made a name for itself with the manufacture and sale of series and special products for industry as well as construction fastenings. As a DEKRA-certified welding company with manufacturer's qualification according to DIN EN 1090 EXC3, the stainless-steel specialist also covers special customer requirements in custommade products for heavy-duty construction e.g. tunnel and bridge construction and the renovation of old buildings and monuments.

## Prices in focus

"The relevant stainless-steel prices for materials such as 1.4301(austenitic steel grade, also known as V2A) and 1.4016 (ferritic steel grade) have doubled since January 2020. Raw materials such as chromium, nickel and molybdenum are important components of stainless steels and peaked in terms of prices for alloy surcharges in the fourth quarter of 2021," it said.

For the first quarter of 2022, stainlesssteel experts do not reportedly foresee any major price reductions, which does not come as a surprise to Wilhelm Modersohn, managing partner and owner of Wilhelm Modersohn GmbH & Co. KG.

"We have been experiencing fluctuating raw material prices in the procurement markets for stainless steel for quite some time. It is precisely these price volatilities that reflect the high level of supply uncertainty in the raw material markets, which is then confirmed by the real supply bottlenecks," explained Wilhelm Modersohn, who is also a Member of the Board of Informationsstelle Edelstahl Rostfrei.

He continued: "This enormously restricts planning reliability in the processing industry and for construction companies. To offer our customers supply security, we have taken strong precautions with our material purchases last year and this year. We should not experience a supply shortage due to material shortages in 2022."

High stock levels, rapid production and delivery capabilities are part of the company's 2022 corporate strategy.

## Lean Duplex steel

For some time now, Modersohn has been focusing on Lean Duplex steels which are less volatile in terms of price.

"These steels are also currently cheaper and, due to their basic strength which is at least twice as high as standard austenites, significantly more economical than austenites, which are comparable in terms of corrosion technology. The explanation lies in the lower alloying proportions of the expensive components, such as nickel and molybdenum, and in the increased proportion of chromium and nitrogen. This makes Lean Duplex steel a clear alternative to the standard A4 and A2 austenites in almost all common areas of application," explained the materials specialist.

Other benefits cited over the standard austenites include:

■ Higher fatigue strength.
■ No chloride- and hydrogen-induced stress corrosion cracking up to 50°C.
■ Less risk of cold welding when bolting under load.
■ Good resistance at higher temperatures i.e. not the typical chi or sigma phase embrittlement).
■ Lower coefficient of thermal expansion with the same thermal transmittance of Lambda 15.

For many years, Modersohn has kept an extensive stock of Lean Duplex steels, especially for sheet.

"At the beginning of this year, the standard for 'Mechanische Verbindungselemente' (Mechanical Fasteners) DIN EN ISO 3506 was published in a revised version. With this internationally valid manufacturing and application basis for screws, nuts, washers, threaded rods etc., more and more standard parts are coming onto the market as stock items. This means that strength class 100 is then often also available, which our engineers can once again optimise to the customer's product due to better design possibilities," said Modersohn.
