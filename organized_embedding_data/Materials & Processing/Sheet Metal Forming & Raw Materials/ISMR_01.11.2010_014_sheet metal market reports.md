INTERNATIONAL SHEET METAL REVIEW • NOVEMBER 2010

PRESS BRAKE PRODUCTION

In September 2010, Salvagnini moved production of its E2 and B2 press brakes from its historical facility, Mecos in Piacenza (acquired by the Salvagnini group in 2009), to its facility in Sarego, Italy. The decision was made as an important investment into the future development of press brakes by the Salvagnini Group as its Sarego facility integrates R&D expertise with technology development for the manufacturer of sheet processing machines.
