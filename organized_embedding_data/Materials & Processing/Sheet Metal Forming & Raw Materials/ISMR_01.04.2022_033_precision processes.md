# PRECISION PROCESSES

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

BSH Hausgeräte's new b+s slitting line, with fully automatic shear, cuts up to seven slit strips from one coil with minimum material thickness of 0.3mm.

ISMIR SAYS: "The household appliance manufacturer has drastically reduced line set-up times and production costs."

The Traunreuter plant, initially owned by Siemens AG and incorporated into BSH Hausgeräte GmbH in 1967, has developed and produced cookers, ovens and hobs for the international market for more than 70 years. The world's first built-in cooker was reportedly developed and produced in this factory.

The household appliance manufacturer produces almost all the sheet metal it needs itself (e.g. for housing or ventilation parts, as well as heating elements). However, because its previous coil slitting line in Traunreut was getting older, BSH invested in a new slitting line for C steels from the b+s group.

The line's specially developed, fully automatic longitudinal slitting shear can be easily and flexibly adjusted to new coil widths and cuts up to seven slit strips from one coil. In addition, the new line can process larger coils of up to 18t, so that set-up times can be reduced and production process efficiency is increased.

The lower minimum material thickness of just 0.3mm (compared to the old slitting line) is less than that of the sheet metal strip usually processed, so the household appliance manufacturer is already preparing itself to meet new market requirements and future trends.

In terms of increasing efficiency and improving occupational safety in sheet metal production, BSH set itself the goal of optimising set-up times and reducing production costs with the new, highly automated system. Since the purchase of special sheet widths in special dimensions is very cost-intensive, the slitting shear also had to be able to cut any coil widths required for the cooker and hob production.

After b+s delivered the system in the summer of 2020 and made specific adjustments on-site, the line was finally incorporated into ongoing production in Traunreut in the second quarter of 2021.

## Automated processes increase production

One of the central aspects that were particularly important to BSH for the new coil slitting line is the specially developed, fully automatic slitting shear.

"Using the automatic system, the knives can firstly be adjusted to new coil widths of between 400 and 1620mm in a completely uncomplicated way,“ explained Radloff. "Secondly, the shear cuts up to seven slit strips – the industry average is only two to three coil strips."

This is a striking technological innovation. To ensure strong system performance, b+s made improvements to the automated adjustment system of the knives on-site.

"The adjustments were successful, so the slitting line now meets our exacting requirements on cut quality to our complete satisfaction," confirmed Radloff. The new machine can also process material thicknesses of between 0.3 and 1.5mm. The low minimum thickness is particularly important, as these coils are very sensitive and require great care within the plant processes.

A large number of 0.4mm sheets are already needed, which the old coil slitting line in Traunreut was not designed to produce. It can be assumed that future market trends will require the processing of even thinner sheet, for which the plant is now prepared thanks to the new line from b+s.

As BSH attaches great importance to high efficiency, set-up times were to be drastically reduced with the new coil slitting line. In addition to the fully automatic adjustment of the slitting shear, the line is designed for larger carbon steel coils of up to 18t compared to the old system. The mobile coil storage system has two coil positions so that changing the coils takes less time. Loading the system with a new coil is now highly automated.

“Up to now, several manual steps were still necessary for this," explained Achim Kunz, managing director of b+s. “Thanks to our selfdeveloped plant processes, we now achieve a higher level of automation and the new coil is ready for series production at the touch of a button."

The system also runs significantly faster than its predecessor, with a speed of up to 200m/minute. Due to the increase in throughput as a result, the Traunreut plant can now not only supply its own production of cookers and hobs with matching sheet metal parts, but also supply other production sites belonging to the BSH Group.

## Customisation and safety

Another requirement that the BSH Group placed on the new slitting line concerned the safety technology and this was not just to bring the outdated standards of the older machine up to date.

“After setting up the system here at the plant, we realised that our safety philosophy exceeds the level generally used in the industry," explained Radloff.

To meet internal processes in Traunreut, b+s adapted the line's system control. For example, it was originally assumed that the slitting line would only be operated by one person. It was therefore possible to move individual machine parts even when the safety gate was open. At the Traunreut plant, however, it is common practice for up to two employees to be working on the system at the same time, which is why the standard specification would have suffered safety-related weaknesses in day-to-day production there.

"Each production facility has its own operating procedures and correspondingly different needs in terms of its machinery, which sometimes only emerge during implementation," added Kunz.
