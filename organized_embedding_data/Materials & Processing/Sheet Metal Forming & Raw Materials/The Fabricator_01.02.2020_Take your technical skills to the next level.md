Back by popular demand, the third annual Mexico Technology Conference will be held February 25-27 at the Fiesta Americana in Querétaro, Mexico. Over the course of three days, attendees will receive valuable updates on the latest advancements and technical training in stamping, tool and die design, stamping press operation and maintenance, press brakes, laser cutting, coil feeding and processing, welding, software, Industry 4.0, and much more.

Award-winning stamping guru Art Hedrick, Dieology LLC, will be joined by stamping experts Robert Braswell, Consultamex, and Alvaro Garcia, MexiMH.
