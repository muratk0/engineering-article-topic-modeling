# Russian grain industry enjoys Magnelis® protection

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

THE SKESS CORPORATION is one of Russia's leading suppliers of grain silos and grain processing equipment. The company recently started to use Magnelis® coated steel from ArcelorMittal Europe - Flat Products for elements of its silos and related products. Magnelis® is a corrosion protection solution for grain silos, as it is compatible with food applications and, said the manufacturer, very easy to process.

Director General of SKESS, Alexander V. Dormostuk, explained: "Magnelis makes our silos and equipment much more competitive, as we benefit from ArcelorMittal's 20-year guarantee. Other corrosion protection solutions, such as pre- and post-galvanisation do not come with any guarantees in Russia."

"Magnelis is the only metallic coated product which is suitable for use in C5 environments such as marine, industrial and farm locations. Cut edges are also protected thanks to its self-healing properties. This ensures the integrity of the silos and equipment over the long-term and is just one of the reasons that we can offer Magnelis with a 20-year guarantee," ArcelorMittal told ISMR.

ArcelorMittal supplies SKESS with S350 high-strength steel in coils, slit coils and sheet. The steel coils are manufactured and coated with Magnelis ZM310 at ArcelorMittal's mills in Bremen (Germany) and Liège (Belgium).

Some coils are also sent to ArcelorMittal ESP (Belgium) where they are slit and cut into sheet for various parts of the silos. Additional coil slitting is carried out by ArcelorMittal Desvres (France).

Magnelis ZM310 is a zinc-aluminium-magnesium coating which is suitable for construction and civil engineering applications. The coated steel is available in a wide range of thicknesses and can be used to form the walls, roofs and hoppers of the silos. Magnelis ZM310 is applied to both sides of the steel at a thickness of 2 μm per side. It is compatible with all conventional processing operations including bending, drawing, clinching, profiling, stamping and welding.
