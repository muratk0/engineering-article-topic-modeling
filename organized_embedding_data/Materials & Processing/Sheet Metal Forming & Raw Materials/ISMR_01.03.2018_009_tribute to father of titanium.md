# Tribute to 'Father of Titanium'

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

STANLEY (STAN) ABKOWITZ died late last year at the age of 90. Known as the 'Father of Titanium,' he invented the most commercially successful titanium alloy, Ti-6Al-4V, at a US Army laboratory in 1951. Ti-6Al-4V is still the most widely used titanium alloy today, and the large-scale production of this alloy, used for the U2 high-altitude reconnaissance plane in the 1950s, jump started the titanium industry.

In 1972, Abkowitz founded Dynamet Technology (now Arconic Titanium & Engineered Products) to focus on titanium PM development. A graduate of MIT in Chemical Engineering, he was a pioneer in the development of titanium metals and alloys and held 24 U.S. and international patents. Abkowitz provided research that helped conquer strength, corrosion and weight issues in the aerospace, biomechanical and energy industries, among others. This research included advanced materials for energy, defence, medical and aerospace applications such as missile and aircraft components, as well as composite materials for medical implants.

Abkowitz was a recipient of numerous US Army, Department of Commerce, powder metallurgy and titanium industry awards. A Fellow of ASM International, he received ASM's 1993 William Hunt Eisenman Award and the Distinguished Service to Powder Metallurgy Award from the Metal Powder Industries Federation (MPIF) in 1999.

The author of 'Titanium in Industry' in 1955, the first book published on titanium, Abkowitz also wrote a monograph in 1999, 'The Emergence of the Titanium Industry'. Later, he earned the inaugural achievement award from the International Titanium Association and the lifetime achievement award from the American Society for Metals International.

## New President for ALMAG Aluminum

BOB PEACOCK, CEO of ALMAG Aluminum, announced the promotion of Joe Jackman, currently General Manager and Executive Vice President, to the role of President at the company's annual meeting.

"Joe has been a key contributor to our growth and success, particularly over the past eight years as part of our Leadership Team," said Peacock, who will remain active in the business as CEO. "Joe's drive, energy and passion for providing solutions, not just products, to customers has strengthened our position as an industry leader."

Jackman joined ALMAG in 2000 as territory sales manager and was promoted to VP, Sales and Marketing, in 2010 and General Manager in 2016. In these roles, he demonstrated the successful promotion and development of ALMAG's core competencies

"Joe's strong leadership and strategic ability will carry on the company's 65-year long passion for innovation in both extrusion and value-added products. He will continue to build a strong team rooted in the four core values of respect and fairness; humility; passion for progress and ownership," added the company.

With over 300 employees, operating from plants in Brampton ON and Ardmore AL (USA), ALMAG Aluminum has built a reputation as a specialist in high-visual, tight tolerance, thin-walled and complex aluminium extrusions.
