# Canmaking today and tomorrow

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

TATA STEEL WILL present its full range of packaging steels, plus a range of new product developments, at Metpack this year, leading with Protact® laminated steel for three-piece cans.

Already a proven material solution across a number of canmaking applications, including two-piece cans, Protact has now been developed for three-piece canmakers. Its multi-layered polymer coating system is designed to free capacity and simplify three-piece canmaking operations. At Metpack, Protact will be presented in a range of colours including clear, black, gold and white, providing greater creative scope when using metal for food packaging.

High-strength steel provides opportunities for downgauging for easy-open ends. A Metpack stand highlight will be Tata Steel's latest range of nitrogenised steel-based products developed to provide greater strength, whilst ensuring the easy-open functionality of can ends.

"Meeting the latest packaging materials legislation, we have also developed an ECCS replacement product resulting in TCCT™, a coated steel which, presented for the first time at Metpack, delivers the stringent requirements of REACH legislation," added Tata Steel. See www.tatasteel.com
