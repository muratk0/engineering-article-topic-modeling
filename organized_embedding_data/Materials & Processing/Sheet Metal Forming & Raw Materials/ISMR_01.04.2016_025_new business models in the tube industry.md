# New business models in the tube industry

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

DREISTERN GMBH & CO. KG exhibited at the recent Tube & Wire show in Düsseldorf. The centrepiece of its exhibit was a multifunctional rollforming system designed for flexible and rapid product changes. Dreistern was also on booth 6D42 with Tube Innovation Days where visitors learned about the entire tube processing chain encompassing solutions for all aspects of tube processing today and in the future.

“The multifunctional rollforming system developed from an idea to evolve a rollforming machine into a sheet metal machining centre that would produce not just tubes but all products combining rollforming, punching, bending and welding.

“With this technology, instead of exclusively offering tubes, a tube manufacturer is now in a position to serve its clients with a greatly expanded range of products up to pre-finished complete assemblies,” said the manufacturer.

“With this technology, risks with the development of new products will be drastically reduced, it added. “During the transitional period, the system can supply the previous products at no extra cost and even contribute to finance the investments for future products. Such unprecedented flexibility opens up new business opportunities along with complementary new business models. A tube manufacturer can do so concurrently and without risky investments to become a supplier of pre-finished tube and open profile products. Conversely, a profile manufacturer can produce tubes to offer a complete product range to its customers,” it explained.

Work modules, with standardised mechanical and electrical interfaces, can be integrated within minutes depending upon individual product requirements. Each work module has its own mobile control unit and modules can be interchanged easily between different systems of the same size. The modules can be utilised in continuous or flying operation as well as in start/stop mode configurations.

“Depending upon the size, tool weights of up to 1,500kg and production speeds up to 60m/min. are possible. In addition to the punch modules, laser welding modules are also available and, depending upon the requirements of each product, the welding module or punching modules can be placed in the optimal position on the multifunctional system allowing additional reduction of production costs. This technology enables the actual tube welding system and rollforming system to become a universal sheet metal machining centre,” concluded Dreistern.
