Profile Precision Extrusions moves to new facility in Arizona

Profile Precision Extrusions, a provider of small, complex aluminum extrusions and extruded aluminum tubing, has moved into a 100,000-sq.-ft. manufacturing facility. Located in Phoenix, the new building is more than twice the size of the company’s former facility.

The manufacturer plans to make additional hires in 2021 and will be expanding the types of aluminum extrusions and value-added services it offers.

Russel Metals to acquire Sanborn Tube Sales

Canada-based Russel Metals Inc., a distributor of tube, pipe, and other metal products, has entered into an agreement to acquire Sanborn Tube Sales of Wisconsin Inc., a metals service center with a heavy emphasis in value-added processing.

The Sanborn operation, based in Pewaukee, Wis., is situated close to Russel’s three Wisconsin service centers and offers the opportunity for the combined business to expand the metal distributor’s product offering.

Phoenix Stamping Group acquires Stampsource

Metal stamper and fabricator Phoenix Stamping Group, a division of PHX Holdings, has purchased Stampsource, a Charlotte, N.C.-based metal stamping and fabrication manufacturer.

Located in Atlanta, Phoenix Stamping offers sheet metal and rod products serving primarily the material handling, towable trailer, truck after-market, and agricultural equipment markets. The company currently has more than 200 employees.

This acquisition adds 50 employees, as well as capacity and redundancy in metal stampings, fabrication, tool and die design, and kitting and assembly. Stampsource will be integrated as Plant 04 of Phoenix Stamping Group with Michael Pinkerton continuing to serve as general manager of the facility.

Coherent and II-VI to supply turnkey automotive and electrification welding systems

Coherent and II-VI Inc. have entered into a supply agreement creating a collaboration to enable faster process development and streamlined production services for automotive laser welding applications. These include high-strength alloy welding for automotive body-in-white applications and mass-production copper welding for electrification applications.

Specifically, the combination of IIVI’s RLSK and HIGH-motion 2D remote laser processing heads and the Coherent Adjustable Ring Mode fiber laser technology delivers power adjustment and beam positioning on-the-fly to produce high-quality welds at high speed over large working areas, the companies report.

Etnyre Intl. acquires SMF Inc.

Oregon, Ill.-based Etnyre Intl., a manufacturer of equipment for the asphalt roadbuilding industry, has acquired SMF Inc., Minonk, Ill.

SMF provides medium- to heavygauge metal fabrications for OEMs in the heavy industrial engine and power generator, wind turbine, and mining and construction equipment markets. The company offers laser cutting, forming, welding, machining, and finishing services.

PEMA to deliver three shipbuilding automation lines to Babcock

PEMA has received an order from aerospace and defense company Babcock Intl. Group for three lines to be used for shipbuilding at Babcock’s Rosyth, Scotland, facility.

The order includes a thin-plate panel line, equipped with plate edge milling and robotic welding; a T-beam fabrication line designed to make straight T-beams without additional straightening processes; and a micro panel line that assembles profiles before they are welded by a compact Vision robotics system.

The lines initially will be used for the Type 31 general-purpose frigate program being built and assembled at the Rosyth facility.

Trace-A-Matic increases CNC turning capacity

Trace-A-Matic, a producer of precision CNC machined parts and fabrications, has installed a new HNK VTC-28/30 vertical turning center at its Brookfield, Wis., location.

The machine features a 60-HP, 130-RPM table with a 44,000-lb. load capacity and a 25-HP live spindle to support tapping and other machining operations. The installation increases the company’s turning capability to a cutting diameter of 118.1 in. and height of 78.67 in.

The new turning center will process large industrial rings, forgings, and castings for the military, water, oil and gas, and space industries.

Ohio Laser adds 12-kW fiber laser cutting system

Ohio Laser has installed a third TRUMPF 12-kW L5030 flat sheet laser cutting system with a 3030 automated storage and retrieval system at its facility in Plain City, Ohio.

These machines offer nitrogen cutting of steel to ½ in. thick, aluminum to 1 in., and stainless steel to 1½ in., as well as oxygen cutting of steel to 1½ in. and copper to ⅜ in. thick. They are equipped with Active Speed Control, which adjusts the cutting parameters in real time to help maximize cutting speed and edge quality as material quality changes.

Bystronic hosts Competence Days 2020

Bystronic Inc., the U.S. subsidiary of Bystronic Laser AG in Switzerland, held its Competence Days for the Americas 2020 event over 10 days in November for guests to view demonstrations of the company’s latest product releases and to see its new Experience Center in Hoffman Estates, Ill.

The event, held Nov. 9 to 20, offered morning and afternoon sessions each day that allowed the annual open house tradition to continue while adhering to local and state COVID-19 guidelines. Presentations, demonstrations, lunch, and guided tours were all part of the agenda in each session, and the company’s applications team was on hand to demonstrate each machine and its capabilities.

Guests viewed the company’s laser cutting and bending products, including the new ByStar Fiber 15-kW laser cutting machine with the By-Trans Cross automation component for loading/unloading, as well as the Next Generation BySmart Fiber, now available with up to a 10-kW fiber laser source.
