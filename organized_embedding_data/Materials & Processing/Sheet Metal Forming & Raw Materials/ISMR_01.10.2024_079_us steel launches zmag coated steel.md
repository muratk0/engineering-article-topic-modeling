# U.S. Steel launches ZMAG™ coated steel

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

United States Steel Corporation (U.S. Steel) has announced the roll-out of ZMAG™ coated steel, a new carbon flat-rolled, coated steel product.

“Engineered to be stronger, more resilient and more sustainable than existing products on the market today, ZMAG™ steel rises to the challenge of the world's harshest conditions and introduces a new level of reliability and durability for the solar, automotive and construction industries," the company told ISMR.

ZMAG™ steel features a zinc-aluminium-magnesium coating that, says U.S. Steel, offers “up to five times the corrosion resistance of conventional galvanized steel." ZMAG™ coated steel is designed to lower maintenance costs and increase efficiency for products that must withstand long-term exposure to the elements (such as solar frames and racking systems). Melted and manufactured in the U.S. and coated at PRO-TEC in Ohio, ZMAG™ coated steel is produced in the USA.

"In today's industrial landscape, the need for materials that can withstand harsh and corrosive environments is more critical than ever," commented James Bruno, Senior Vice President – Business Development and President – U.S. Steel Kosice.

"Whether it's rain, sleet or snow, ZMAG™ coated steel has the toughness to ensure that projects are built to last. With a production process that reduces waste, along with unmatched longevity, ZMAG™ steel also represents U. S. Steel's commitment to reducing its environmental impact," he continued.

U.S. Steel is also offering a 25-year warranty on ZMAG™ coated steel.
