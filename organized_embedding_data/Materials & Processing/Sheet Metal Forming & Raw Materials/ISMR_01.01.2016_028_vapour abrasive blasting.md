# Vapour abrasive blasting

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

“OUR ECOQUIP™ vapour abrasive blasting equipment is a clean and easy method of blasting that eliminates up to 92% of airborne particles while achieving removal rates comparable to traditional dry blasting,” Graco told ISMR.

In vapour abrasive blasting, water is added simultaneously with the abrasive media in a pressure pot. The abrasive becomes suspended in water, and the mixture is then projected at high velocity by a forceful flow of compressed air. Graco’s new developments in wet and vapour abrasive blast technologies include increasing production rates to decrease water consumption, optimising the air, water and media ratio for strong performance and achieving the same profile compared to traditional blasting methods.

“Conventional blasters only have the weight of the media. Therefore, they try to blast with larger particles in order to blast more aggressively. With vapour abrasive blasting, abrasive media and water are combined in a blasting pot which is pressurized only by water. By encapsulating each blast particle with water, extra weight is added to the media which enlarges the impact of the blast media on the substrate. In addition, the removal and feathering of the material is smoother and less media is used. The water reduces splash-back and traps the dust being produced,” explained Graco.

“EcoQuip, our vapour abrasive blast equipment, uses up to 75% less blast media as well as far less water than wet blasting, and creates little run-off. There is also less waste and no large slurry mess to clean up. It removes coatings without damaging sub-surfaces. The bottom line is that it can reduce project costs and increase the speed, efficiency and productivity of blasting.”

The equipment is suitable for a range of applications including paint layer removal; marine coating removal; rust and corrosion removal; industrial coating and lining removal and general metal cleaning. It is supplied as standard in a stainless steel frame and roll cage, with a galvanized blast pot, integrated water tank and control panel.
