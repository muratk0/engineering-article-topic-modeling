# INNOVATION, AUTOMATION AND COOPERATION

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

Lucio Treu, FASPAR's area manager, at Blechexpo

ISMR SAYS:

"FASPAR aims to increase line productivity, flexibility and part quality"

ISMR caught up with Lucio Treu, FASPAR's area manager, at this year's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, to find out about its latest new coil processing line projects and initiatives.

FASPAR S.p.A. is a leading specialist in the design and manufacture of turnkey coil processing lines for ferrous and non-ferrous metals, with facilities near Milan (Italy), and with 80% of its turnover dedicated to export markets. These include customised surface-finishing lines; slitting lines; levelling and cut-to-length lines (feeder, flying and rotary shear); feeding and pressing/punching lines; coil coating lines; tension leveller lines and sandwich panel lines. With customers in 27 countries, the company acquired the CIMSA brand, and its coil-punching and bending line expertise, in 2015.

Over 300 FASPAR lines have been installed in Italy; Europe; Russia; the USA and Africa. Its products serve steel service centres in automotive, construction and white goods industry sectors. Its feeding, pressing and punching lines are used to produce various parts such as washing machine parts, cookerhoods, electric box panels, automotive parts and shelves. FASPAR is now focused on launching robot systems for automatic handling of cutters and spacers on its own and customers' slitting lines, using an exclusive licence agreement with Scandinavian Robotics signed in 2015.

Partners in production Faspar acquired the CIMSA brand in 2015 and relaunched the brand into the market.

"The decision to acquire the Cimsa brand, and its associated expertise, was driven by several factors. Certainly, the physical proximity of the company, which was only 500m from our facility, played a part. We also had several local common acquaintances and friends and had, in the past, formed partnerships for the exchange of machines. We had always respected Cimsa's technologies and products and we felt that adding Cimsa's product lines to our portfolio would help us to grow further," Lucio Treu told ISMR.

The idea of acquiring and integrating Cimsa into FASPAR's operations was based on differentiation of the lines and the option to offer the lines both as an intermediate 'product in transformation' and as 'a finished' product.

"We consider cooperation between companies a fundamental drive for 'splitting' the burden of work and sharing success together, but also for growing and learning from each other."

FASPAR has always encouraged strong partnerships in its business, both in Italy and overseas.

The partnership between Servopresse and FASPAR-CIMSA, for example, has led to the creation of a highly automated coil blanking line, now installed at an Italian company in Tuscany for the production of customised pressure-bent profiles. This new coil blanking line was recently installed at INPLAF's factory, an Italian company that has been engaged in the manufacture of pressure-bent profiles, for a wide variety of applications for about 35 years.

"We consider cooperation between companies a fundamental drive for 'splitting the burden of work and sharing success together, but also for growing and learning from each other. We do not consider them as 'companies who work with us' but as very close partners with whom we plan missions abroad and commercial strategies. These are very important collaborations for us and we are developing joint projects with great results with several partner companies: Olimpia80, Costa Levigatrici and Servopresse," explained Maddalena Aime, Sales Director, FASPAR S.p.A.

## Robotic handling for slitting lines

In 2015, FASPAR signed an exclusive licence agreement on production and sales with Scandinavian Robotics for robot systems for automatic handling of cutters and spacers for its own and customers' slitting lines. With this solution, the line can run 24/7, maximising profitability for customers.

"The robot is the most recent and most successful technology we can propose for the slitting lines," Lucio Treu told ISMR. "Technology that, together with automatic tool changer units, are the most common quick-change solution for our Hi Performance class of slitting lines. Our lines also comes with several options designed to reduce downtime during all cycle operations, increase modularity and layout flexibility and increase part quality."

FASPAR offers the robot, produced under licence to Scandinavian Robotics, to improve slitting line efficiency by eliminating manual set-up. In fact, preparation of the tool kit requires a good percentage of the total setup time of the line and requires accurate execution.

The robot is also proposed for jobs that are heavy and monotonous, or that may be hazardous to operators who may handle several tons of slitting rolls during a working day.

Designed according to actual and future customer requirements, the robot comes complete with a special interim station, where the robot holds the tool during preparation, leaving the turnstile of the line free to operate as usual. The robot engages with the turnstile of the line only for the very short "transfer time" of the entire tool setup, avoiding long interruptions for partial transfer.

"Recently a newly developed robot, the SRC type, was launched into the market, with special storage tower configuration to use less space in the plant. Several options are available, such as a washing machine for tools; a camera device to automatically check blade wear and status and a twinhead to decrease cycle time. The new robot system offers several flexible configurations and performance levels to meet the ever increasing needs of our customers. The robot can be integrated into every type of slitting line, new or existing," added Lucio Treu.

## An eye on the future

"2017 has been a good year for us and we have a great 2018 in front of us. We recently designed, manufactured and installed lines in Europe, Asia, North Africa and overseas," explained Lucio Treu.

"We always advise our customers to diversify their activities and the materials that they process on the same line so as to spread and increase the quantities. Some of our lines have been designed to process up to seven materials on one line. We also see the increased use of automation on the lines and we advise customers on the safety, speed and productivity benefits of using robots and automation on their lines."

With an eye on innovation, automation and cooperation, FASPAR S.p.A. is moving confidently into the future.
