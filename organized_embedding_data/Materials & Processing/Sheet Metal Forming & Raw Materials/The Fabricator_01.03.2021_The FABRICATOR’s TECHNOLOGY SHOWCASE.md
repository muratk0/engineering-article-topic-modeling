FACCIN, plate roll solutions for all kind of bending needs!

FACCIN GROUP is nowadays a top global metal forming machine manufacturer that offers three leading brands—FACCIN, BOLDRINI & ROUNDO—and can proudly claim to have more than 30,000 units successfully installed all over the world.

The Group is focused on the production of plate rolling machines, angle rolls, dished head lines and special solutions that serve several applications such as Boiler and Pressure Vessels, Road Tankers, Earth Moving Equipment, Storage Tank & Silos, Tube and Pipelines, HVAC, Wind Tower and Off-shore Foundations, Steel Structures, Tank Heads, Shipbuilding and the Off-shore Industry.

Faccin Group is internationally present today with its corporate headquarters in Italy, branches in the USA and Germany and Sales O‘ces in Spain, China and Asia Pacific which provide prompt response and assistance to all its customers around the world.

The FACCIN brand is recognized as the worldwide largest line of plate bending rolls with the widest range of products and geometries available in today’s market.

The 2-Rolls model HCU is the ideal solution for rolling medium and large batches of tubes with thickness up to 3/16″ and width up to 6½′.

Productivity, speed and precision: HCU is an innovative 2-roll bending roll model, made with a special polyurethane roll and built to roll thin plates with high productivity (180 pieces per hour, reaching a peak of over 300 pieces per hour in its special configuration)

The 3Rolls model HAV is an unbeatable combination of rolling power and pre-bending precision. The top roll moves vertically while the two lower rolls move horizontally, each one independent from the other.

This special design ensures the simple adjustment of the distance between the bending points according to production needs, the same principle as a press brake with variable V dies.

Ideal for medium and heavy-duty rolling requirements (from 1.6′′ to over 12′′ thickness) the HAV plate rolls are undoubtedly the most technologically advanced three-roll bending machines available worldwide.

It’s the preferred solution for applications such as Pressure Vessels, Off-shore Foundations for the Wind Industry, Off-shore Platforms, Reactors and Heat Exchangers.

The 4Rolls system in its version with Linear Guides (model 4HEL) or with Swing Arms (model 4HEP) is the most precise and easiest to use plate bending rolls with total pre-bending. Highly technological and innovative, it assures high productivity compared to any other alternative and thanks to its advanced design, can be effectively controlled through numerical control systems. It is the best solution to bend plates from less than 3′ and up to 33′ with thickness from 3/16″ to 6″

It’s the ideal solution for Tanks, Pressure Vessels and Wind Tower production.
