# Automotive steel factory completed in China

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

KOREAN steel specialist POSCO has completed construction of a continuous galvanising line (CGL) in Guangdong, China, establishing an optimised client service system in China offering one-stop services from production to processing and sales of high quality automobile steel plates.

At the completion ceremony of the annual 450,000-ton production scale CGL held in Foshan City of Guangdong Province, China, on 15 April, CEO Chung Joonyang, Gwangdong Province Guangzhou Consul General Yang Changsu, Guangdong Province Vice Governor Zhao Yufang and 300 other local client companies and personnel were present.

Galvanised steel (GI) and galvannealed steel (GA) that are used for automobile steel plates will be produced at this factory and then sent to eleven specialised processing centres and sold to global automobile companies with locations in China including Toyota, Honda, Nissan, GM, Volkswagen and Hyundai Kia Motors, as well as local Chinese automobile companies.

With the completion of the Guangdong CGL, POSCO can now offer integrated operations from production to processing and sales through POSCO China and eleven processing centres.

POSCO expects to expand automobile steel plate sales within China, projecting annual production of China's automobile industry to exceed 380 million by 2020, with Chinese automobile companies' localisation and the expansion of policies on local procurement of global automobile companies. POSCO has secured automobile steel plate production bases in areas close to large-scale automobile industries in China, India and Mexico, securing its position as a global steel company.
