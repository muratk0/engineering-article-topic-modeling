# Stocking and processing

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

Quest 4 Alloys Ltd was established in 1991 as an independent UK stockholder and processor supplying specialist high-temperature and corrosion-resistant materials worldwide, to end-users and other stockholders. This includes sheets and plates from 0.4mm thick up to 500mm thick.

"Operating from our purpose-built service centre in the UK and offices in France, we can despatch orders by road, sea or air to meet our customers' exact delivery requirements. We can deliver coil; sheet; plate; bar; tube; pipe and fittings. We stock and supply a wide range of grades of stainless steels; nickel alloys; duplex; super duplex and titanium to customers across the globe. We offer a wide and varied range of both materials and products, supported by our extensive stock range and global supply chain, to meet nickel alloy, stainless steel and titanium requirements," it explained.

"From extensive stocks or special production, if it is a special nickel alloy grade or an odd-sized pipe fitting, we can find it. Through our extensive worldwide approved supplier database, we have the experience and expertise to locate the most difficult of items. We supply special materials in all product forms from foil in strip to large bespoke forgings," it added.

Quest 4 Alloys started with two small slitting machines enabling it to offer strip in coil, over the years. Investment in modern equipment and experienced personnel has seen continued growth in the strip/coil part of the business. It supplies from 0.001" foil up to 5mm thick coils in widths from 3mm to 1500mm, in weights to suit individual project requirements.

The stockholder also supplies pipes, tubes and fittings in stainless steel and nickel alloys (standard lengths or cut to a required length to minimise waste). Stainless steels come in five main different groups (Austenitic, Ferritic, Martensitic, Duplex and Precipitating Hardening). Processing capabilities offered include slitting; round edge/safe edge/deburred edge coils; cut-to-length coil; waterjet-/laser cutting of plate; cut-to-length bar blanks; turning and boring as well as third party testing and inspection.
