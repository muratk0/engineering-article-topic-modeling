Workstation provides light-guided picking for assembly

Creform Corp. offers a workstation suitable for assembly operations that require lightguided picking to control process flow and to help reduce errors. The workstation also incorporates an ergo lift. Once actuated, the workstation travels up and down and can carry a 1,000-lb. capacity with a 12-in. stroke. An electrohydraulic system with push-button switch provides the activation.

Parts for an assembly operation can be presented on shelves or gravity flow lanes above and below the work surface. Each part location features light for guided picking. Each light has a three-digit display for quantity information, as well as a push-button or infrared sensor for pick acknowledgment.

The pick-to-light system is selfcontained in the workstation and is powered by a basic PC and a controller mounted to the side of the workstation.

System combines bending robot, press brake, tool changer

Amada offers the HG ARs, which integrates a bending robot with an HG series press brake and patented Automatic Tool Changer (ATC), for bending a variety of parts.

A 6-axis robot with a range of motion capabilities performs all part loading, bending, and unloading operations. During bending, a potentiometer backgauge system with additional side gauge compensates for any deviation when the workpiece is placed on the die.

Roller hemming tool hems complex joints in lightweight vehicles

Comau has introduced a new tool for robotic roller hemming, called the Smart Hemmer. The compact, symmetrical roller hemmer can handle any complex joint. It is suitable for use on electric, hybrid, and traditional lightweight vehicles as it is designed to allow automakers to join dissimilar materials using a cold process that grants complete control throughout the process.

The tool’s design features two flexible rollers in which the linear roller can automatically disengage when encountering obstacles or a curved path. This allows it to perform curved-path hemming and manage complex angles as part of a single, continuous process. It maintains a constant hemming thickness even when encountering multiple layers of dissimilar materials.

Wrapper’s powered conveyor option aids load handling safety

TAB Industries LLC has introduced powered conveyors as an option on its fully automated TAB Wrapper Tornado Perfect Storm wrapping systems. Offered in a choice of roller or chain conveyors, they automatically advance the pallet load into and through the wrapping ring smoothly and hands-free to support worker safety and help speed the wrapping process.

The powered conveyors grip the palletized load and gently advance it as the machine wraps the load to the pallet with multiple layers of plastic film. When complete, the wrapped pallet load is discharged from the wrapping ring, ready for transport or storage.

The system is offered in 100-, 80-, and 40-in. wrapping rings and in custom sizes for wrapping oversized products.
