# Cost estimation, process planning and metal forming simulation

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

FORMING TECHNOLOGIES INC. (FTI), a leading developer of sheet metal forming solutions, has announced the worldwide release of FormingSuite Sculptured Die Face (SDF) to address the needs of cost estimation, process planning and metal forming simulation.

“Our patent-pending Sculptured Die Face is a revolutionary, free-form approach to creating and morphing addendum geometry resulting in a fast, flexible and visual approach to die face design,” said Michael Gallagher, FTI’s vice president of forming technologies. Typical applications include:

Improving the accuracy of Material Cost Estimates by accurately considering the Process geometry (including addendum) rather than just the part shape.

■ Providing more detailed information to process planners responsible for identifying die size, forming and trimming operations, press tonnage and tooling costs.

■ Providing the necessary geometry to run accurate virtual die tryout.

FTI’s approach gives users the ability to develop full draw developments, die faces and first form geometry in minutes, without the limitations of section lines and the difficulties associated with creating complex transition geometry in CAD systems. Fully interactive editing tools enable users to adjust and refine addendum geometry.

SDF simplifies the work involved in positioning and attaching double- (and multi-) attached parts, creating, editing and evaluating fully developable curved binder geometry, controlling the depth of draw, defining addendum shape and creating fully surfaced draw developments. Additional capabilities of Sculptured Die Face include 3D CAD data import, geometry editing tools and detailed evaluation and visualization of undercut, draw depth, draft angle and trimming conditions.
