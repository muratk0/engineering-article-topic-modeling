# Finkl Steel invests in production line for semi-finished products

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

SUPPORTING AUTOMOTIVE SUPPLIERS to improve their level of competitiveness was the motivation for Finkl Steel to invest in a new production line at its factory in Québec, Canada. The investment totalled almost 2.7m Canadian dollars (approx. US$ 1.85m). The aim of this subsidiary of the SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH Group in the future is to also offer fine machining of tool and die steel as well as secondary heat treatment. Its customers receive semi-finished products for further direct processing, making time-to-market of their products faster. The new facility is slated to start operations in Spring 2017.

“To be able to meet the requirements of tool and die manufacturers, one of our key customer groups, we are installing a new, lean production line in Québec," explained Richard Lahaye, President and CEO at Finkl Steel-Sorel. “With this new production line, Finkl Steel will continue to differentiate its product portfolio and extend its range of services in fine and precision machining."

As of 2017, customers will be offered three different finishing processes from a single source at Finkl Steel – drilling, milling and heat treatment. Technical components of the new production line include an overhead crane with a capacity of up to 30 metric tons, a horizontal CNCcontrolled boring mill and a new furnace for heat treatment.■
