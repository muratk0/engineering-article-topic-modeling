# Rapid sheet metal quotes

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

IN THE SHEET metal industry, financial planning is one of the most important activities to ensure the smooth running of a business. Preparing quotes is one of the most critical activities. To be managed effectively, quotations must be as close as possible to the reality of production. With so many variables involved, most of the time it is necessary to carry out a complete simulation of the process including CAM, nesting, method selection, process capacity control, material selection, geometries etc.

Lantek’s modular and scalable software is designed to enable manufacturers to analyse, plan and quote quickly and accurately. Its quoting tools are designed to be intuitive, easy to use and rapid.

“They eliminate the need for expert technical personnel as it is no longer necessary for users to have advanced technical knowledge of design or nesting by making this process more accessible, simple and standardised so that anyone can carry it out,” explained Rob Powell, Commercial Director, Lantek.

Two advanced solutions are Lantek iQuoting and Lantek MetalShop. Lantek iQuoting is a cloud application which connects to Lantek Expert CAD/CAM, for fast and consistent quotes, which can be prepared from anywhere, with instant access to the information needed for the job.

“With everything connected, it is possible to collect key information on the parameters to be considered (such as part geometry, hourly wages, shipping time, type of material, margin etc.), eliminating production stoppages and the long waiting times that can occur from the time a quote is requested until it is costed and then accepted by the client,” said Lantek.

Lantek MetalShop further streamlines the process with an online store which is available 24/7. The operation of requesting and confirming a quote is totally digital. This e-commerce system also helps customers to track their orders, providing total visibility and transparency of the processes.

Analytics help manufacturers to keep up to date with business performance. Lantek’s Customer Analytics software delivers KPIs which are used as feedback, further optimising the accuracy of quotations.

“Many of our customers using this technology report that there is a definite correlation between the speed in returning an accurate quotation and the chances of gaining the order,” concluded Lantek.
