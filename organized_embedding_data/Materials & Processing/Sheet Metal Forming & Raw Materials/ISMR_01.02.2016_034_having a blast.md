# Having a blast

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

NO. 885 IS A 1400°F electric, inert atmosphere tempering furnace from Grieve, currently used for heat treating weldments at a customer’s facility. Workspace dimensions of this unit are 24”W x 36”D x 24”H.

“42kW in nickel chrome wire coils, supported by a stainless steel frame, heat the workspace while a heat-resistant alloy recirculating blower, powered by a 5HP motor, provides vertical downward airflow to the workload,” Grieve told ISMR.

“This tempering furnace features 7” thick insulated walls, comprising 1” of 2300°F ceramic fibre blanket, 2” of 1700°F ceramic fibre blanket, 2” of block insulation and 2” of rockwool insulation. The 6-1/2” thick furnace floor comprises 4-1/2” of 2300°F firebrick and 2” of block insulation. Stainless steel interior lagging and a stainless steel subway grating hearth are also included.”

No. 885 has an electrically-operated vertical lift door with an automatic door switch to shut off the heaters when the door is opened. Grieve’s inert atmosphere construction includes a continuously welded outer shell, a high-temperature door gasket, sealed heater terminal boxes, an inert atmosphere inlet and inert atmosphere outlet, plus a recirculating blower shaft seal. The furnace also has an inert atmosphere flowmeter and digital programming temperature controller onboard.
