# CAMU Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

https://camu.it/

CAMU exports about 75% of its production in Europe and all over the world (Middle East; South/North America; Russia; India, Africa and China).

A manufacturer of coil processing lines and sheet-metal coil levelling since 1980, it is based in Bressanvido (VI, Italy) with production and assembly at two facilities (total 4200m²) with 48 personnel.

"We design and build innovative machines 100% made in Italy, such as cut-to-length lines with 4H-6H leveller; slitting lines; decoiling and inspection lines; coil-to-coil; combined lines; packaging lines; feeding lines as well as special purpose machines to satisfy the particular needs of each customer," it highlighted.

CAMU lines are used in steel service centres to cut coil in industries such as automotive and appliance construction as well as in modern tube/pipe and profiling centres for open profiles. The lines can process different types of material and thicknesses (0.2 to 25mm with widths of up to 2250mm).
