# Smoothing the edges

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

WITH ITS NEW SMD 3 series edge rounder, LISSMAC presents a machine series that is designed to be flexible and efficient, with an eye on ‘excellent’ processing results. The newly developed series features seven singlesided working models for maximum versatility in thin and thick sheet metal processing.

“Depending upon the application, dry working models with three or four processing units are available. This wide range of variants, as well as useful options, features and accessories, ensure the necessary flexibility to meet diverse customer requirements. From deburring to strong edge rounding up to a radius of 2mm, slag removal, oxide removal or high-quality surface finishing - the SMD 3 series offers a solution for every application,” said the company.

LISSMAC SMD 3 series aims to deliver high-quality processing results on all parts from 50 x 50mm to large format sheets. For further information, see https://www.lissmac.com/en/metal-processing/products/
