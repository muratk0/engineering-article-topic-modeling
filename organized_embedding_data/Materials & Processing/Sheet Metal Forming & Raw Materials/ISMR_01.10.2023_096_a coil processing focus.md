# A coil processing focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Athader, a Bradbury Group company, is a leading manufacturer of coil processing lines. Based in San Sebastian, Spain, it has been manufacturing slitting; cut-to-length levelling and recoiling lines as well as coil/sheet automated packaging systems for 30 years.

In the last three years, Athader and Leveltek have completed five projects for cut-tolength lines with stretch levelling machines incorporated into the lines: three of them in Europe and two in Australia. Athader and Leveltek can provide new cut-to-length lines and retrofits of cut-to-length lines. The stretch leveller and flying shear fit into new or existing cut-to-length lines.

"Our success in the North American market continues with several slitting lines, a coil packaging line and a coil-to-coil edge trimming and levelling line for aluminium products supplied in 2022 and 2023. We are currently manufacturing several heavy gauge lines for the USA, Mexico and Canada," it told ISMR.

To better serve the North American market, Athader has positioned service technicians and spare parts at the Bradbury Group head office in Kansas (USA).

"Our stretch leveller eliminates visible shape defects such as edge waves, centre buckles, quarter buckles or oil can and mandrel breaks. The Leveltek Process also eliminates 'Memory', the distortion that can occur in cut parts after laser or plasma cutting of visibly flat sheets. The only measurable effect on the coil is a slight reduction in material width, and a slight increase in length," commented Athader.

The stretch levelling involves the coil being uncoiled, stretch-levelled and either recoiled or cut-to-length. Through a Leveltek proprietary, non-marking process that grips and stretches the unrolled coil, Athader's equipment is designed to eliminate shape defects and not mark or scratch the surface being stretched.

Leveltek's stretch leveller stretches the strip with the required force to exceed the yield strength and permanently elongate it. Even with materials that can be flattened using other methods, its process provides a high standard of flatness for critical or special surfaces that are bright annealed; polished; painted; patterned; plated or coated. Leveltek can process various coil materials in a wide range of thicknesses and widths.
