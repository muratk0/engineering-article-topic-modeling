# Sheet joining screws

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

ARNOLD UMFORMTECHNIK, a manufacturer of intelligent joining solutions, developed Flowform screws in response to the increasingly complex requirements for sheet metal joining. These trilobal (triangular) screws utilise the technology of flow-hole forming in the fabrication process.

“Flowform is a cost optimised solution that offers higher process reliability for threaded fastener joints and considerable advantages over alternative sheet metal joining methods: ease of loosening, accessibility, positioning, reassembly with threaded fasteners, freedom from fabrication residues etc.,” said the manufacturer.

“The market trend is to constantly reduce the thickness of sheet metal components while at the same time requiring their joints to be stronger.”

The screw heats the sheet metal through a combination of axial force and relatively high driver speed. After the screw penetrates the material, the trilobal shape causes a metric thread to be formed in the host material, which can accept a standard metric screw if repairs are necessary. After controlled-torque assembly, the formed hole adapts precisely to the shape of the screw. This process eliminates the need for additional securing components or component preparation steps such as drilling or punching. See www.arnold-umformtechnik.de

## Profile dampers

FOR OVER 60 years now, Dutch company Schut Geometrische Meettechniek BV has been supplying solutions for innovative metrology. In addition to purely video measurement machines and multi-sensor systems, Schut has designed specialist probing machines such as those with air bearing guides, and built a movable bridge construction (DeMeet-A7). The DeMeet-A7 is equipped with a special clamping system that securely fixes the object to be measured. The measuring range of 715mm x 500mm x 400mm is CNC-controlled.

Economist Dr. Bronno Schut, marketing manager, said: “So that we could make our DeMeet-A7 measuring machine even safer, we were looking for shock absorbers and low cost alternatives. Although ACE is the leading company for industrial shock absorbers, we quickly decided upon their ACE TUBUS profile damper. Their engineers provided consultancy but readily undertook the entire design work in this case.”

The innovative damping parts, adapted to DeMeet-A7 requirements and made of co-polyester elastomer, were theoretically able to compensate for the failure of the control parts one million times over. A special batch of the TR series is used to comply with braking mass requirements for a maximum speed of 400 mm/sec. Designed for a temperature range of -30° C to 90° C, the easy to install, either axially or radially damping parts can be used in almost all machines and operating conditions. See www.ace-ace.de or www.schut.com

One of six TUBUS families: radially absorbing types for long, soft braking with progressive energy reduction at the end of travel

## No primer required

HMG PAINTS, an independent industrial coatings producer, has introduced a single stage, 2k polyurethane topcoat, Monothane, which is formulated to be applied directly onto a variety of substrates without the need for a primer. A single coat, gloss finish, it is suitable for mild steel, aluminium, galvanised steel, castings and difficult-to-paint substrates such as uPVC, polystyrene, GRP and sheet-moulded composites (SMC) plastic, giving mechanical properties similar to powder coatings.

“Monothane’s superior pigment quality and high film build enable most paint jobs to be carried out in a single stage, reducing process times and product usage, as well as drying times down to 20 minutes which further increases paintshop productivity. It is suitable for agricultural and construction plant, office hardware and shelving, trailer frames and chassis, garage doors and metal frames, uPVC fascia panels, doors and windows, PVCu fencing, bicycles and many other applications, either as a direct-to-substrate OEM coating or as a high performance touch-up paint for use by distributors and end users,” said HMG Paints. Monothane can be applied by airless, air-assisted, conventional gravity and HVLR spray guns, or even brush applied, giving coverage of between 18-24sqm/litre at 25 microns film thickness, depending on colour and finish. Surface preparation is as simple as a solvent wipe or shotblasting and, as no primer is required, this helps to cut application costs and speed throughput. See www.hmgpaint.com

## A solid drill

CUTTING TOOL and tooling systems specialist, Sandvik Coromant, has launched what it terms ‘the fastest solid carbide drill available on the market, CoroDrill 860’.

“Available in lengths 3-8 x D as standard, this high performance drill can be applied to a wide range of hole-making tasks for maximised cost-per-hole reductions in steel workpieces. For instance, aside from conventional drilling and stack drilling, CoroDrill 860 produces chamfered holes and cross-holes, as well as holes through inclined or convex/concave surfaces. Excellent chip evacuation control is demonstrated when using high cutting data in long and short chipping materials, enabling the drill to provide consistently problem-free drilling at high penetration rates to boost hole quality and tool life,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

The secret behind this drilling concept is new geometry combined with an innovative flute shape optimised for effective chip clearance, particularly at increased rates of penetration. The lower cutting forces offered by this drill leads to fewer problems when encountering issues such as weak fixtures or thinwalled components. See sandvik.coromant.com

## Fume and dust extraction

FLEXTRACTION LTD, a supplier and manufacturer of Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) products for dust and fume extraction, is exhibiting its latest self-supporting Hood Positioning Device (HPD) along with TEKA’s dust and fume extraction systems in the AWD Welding World Village at MACH 2012 (16 – 20 April 2012).

HPDs being demonstrated will include the Flextractor HPD, Extended HPD, Mini HPD and Flextender HPD. HPDs are designed to extract industrial dust and fumes at the point of source for surface grinding, welding or other manufacturing applications where dust and fumes are emitted. They are also ATEX-compliant for use in hazardous area zones. HPDs are supplied complete with mounting brackets, hoods and hoses.

Other products on show will include the TEKA ‘filtoo’, a BIA-approved 1.3kW, 230V or 110V/50Hz, mobile suction and filtering unit for the local extraction of fumes, dust and gases found in welding, bonding, gluing, brazing, soldering and laser welding. It is a complete plug-and-go system that takes polluted air in through the suction hood and hose into four different filters. E-mail: lkd@flextraction.co.uk
