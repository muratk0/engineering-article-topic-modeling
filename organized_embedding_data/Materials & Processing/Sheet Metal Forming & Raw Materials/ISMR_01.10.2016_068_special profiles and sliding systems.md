# Special profiles and sliding systems

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

SCHOCK METALLWERK GmbH, a specialist in roll form technology from southern Germany, is a development partner for its OEMcustomers and their suppliers for solutions based upon roll formed precision profiles and sliding systems.

Recent examples of Schock special profiles can be found, for example, in car interiors. Extremely tight tolerances combined with creative tool solutions allow them to be used as structural elements or functional components. For design-oriented applications in the cargo area, the company supplies stiffener profiles based on pre-painted steel plates, which are usedin the visible part of the trunk floor. This technology is designed to be an economic alternative to powder coating or cataphoretic painting. Without any further processing, the parts are packaged directly on the roll forming line as single-process constructional elements which are ready for installation.

Schock Metall also manufactures ball-bearing telescopic slides and sliding systems. These are used for adjustment systems or pull-out storage compartments. The company will be presenting its storage solutions for car interiors at the IZB trade fair in Wolfsburg under the motto “Moving Spaces”.
