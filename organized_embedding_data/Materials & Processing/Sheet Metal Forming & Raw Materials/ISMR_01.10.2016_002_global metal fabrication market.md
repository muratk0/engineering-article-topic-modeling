# Global metal fabrication market

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

RESEARCH AND MARKETS has announced the addition of the "Global Metal Fabrication Equipment Market 2016-2020" report to its offering. According to the report, the global metal fabrication equipment market is expected to grow at a CAGR of 6.57% from 2016-2020.

The report covers the present scenario and the growth prospects of the global metal fabrication equipment market for 2016- 2020. To calculate the market size, it takes into account the individual processes that constitute metal fabrication - metal cutting, metal forming and metal assembly. The report also includes a discussion of the key vendors operating in this market.

"One of the key trends for market growth will be the emergence of friction stir welding technology in the automotive sector. Friction stir welding is an innovative and novel technique that makes use of frictional heat combined with precisely controlled forging pressure to join two metals. This technique adds high integrity to the final product, by eliminating the possibility of mechanical distortion as the entire operation is carried out at low operating temperature," said the report.

According to the report, introduction of 3D metrology equipment will also be a key driver for market growth. With customers becoming more demanding and as the global economic outlook constantly faces new challenges, metal fabricators must deal with the everincreasing need for greater optical precision and measurement objectives. In the present era of additive manufacturing, 3D metrology equipment (such as advanced laser scanners) are being introduced into the market.
