# Gorenje Orodjarna, d.o.0.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.gorenje-orodjarna.si/en/

Gorenje Orodjarna is a producer of stamping tools for more complex sheet metal parts for the automotive industry and household appliances producers. It also produces equipment for production lines and complete lines as well as stamping sheet metal parts for the automotive industry.

"Our main products are tools for sheet metal processing, including transfer and progressive tools. The production of tools starts with computer simulation and ends with tool-buy off in the customer's press. This method of production and testing ensures highquality tools and their immediate use in production," it confirmed.

Its tools for the automotive industry are designed to produce stamped parts of all types, including those made from high-strength materials, aluminium and stainless steel (e.g. tailored blanks).
