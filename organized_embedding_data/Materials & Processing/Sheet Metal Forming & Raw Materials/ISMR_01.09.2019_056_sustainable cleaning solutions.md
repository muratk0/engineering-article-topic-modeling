# Sustainable cleaning solutions

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

MICROCARE CORPORATION, A manufacturer of cleaning, coating and lubrication products, will put the spotlight on sustainable cleaning and finishing solutions when it exhibits at FABTECH in Chicago (11-14 November 2019).

"Sustainability should be a key driver when it comes to choosing cleaning solutions. It is important to understand the value of a simple, consistent and sustainable process. It must be flexible in that it can adapt to any future changes, whether it be regulatory, environmental or with specific cleaning requirements," it told ISMR.

With this in mind, MicroCare will present its portfolio of cleaning and finishing solutions during the three-day event in Chicago, with the company's most recent addition Tergo™ Metal Cleaning Fluid (MCF) taking centre stage.

"Tergo™ MCF is one of the toughest vapour degreasing fluids on the market and is an effective metal degreaser and parts debinder. We are looking forward to demonstrating the benefits of this cleaning fluid, together with the rest of the MicroCare portfolio, at FABTECH," said Rob Lee, Senior Market and Technical Manager, MicroCare.

Working on highly advanced processes, such as metal injection moulding and additive manufacturing, Tergo™ MCF helps in the critical step of selectively removing the correct amount of the binder so as not to cause any damage, but enough for the final sintering process. It has been formulated to replace traditional solvents such as nPB, TCE, methylene chloride and PERC. The range of Tergo™ products have a low Global Warming Potential and are REACH/ROHS-compliant.

"Compatible with existing vapour degreasing equipment, Tergo™ MCF is compliant with all current and proposed environmental, health and safety regulations, giving it long-term sustainability credentials," concluded the company. See www.microcare.com
