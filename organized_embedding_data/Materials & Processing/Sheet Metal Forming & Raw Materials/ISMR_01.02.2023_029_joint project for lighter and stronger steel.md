# Joint project for lighter and stronger steel

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

Together with SSAB, Gestamp developed a new steel with high strength levels and good formability, that is lighter and environmentally friendly, to manufacture a chassis part for the Toyota Yaris.

"Docol1000CP steel, which has a significantly higher strength (1000 Mpa) than conventionally used grades, maintains the quality, performance and geometry of the parts,” outlined Gestamp.

The characteristics of Docol 1000CP, an advanced high-strength steel, also allow lighter parts to be produced and is effective against fatigue, an important property for any part of the steering and suspension of a car such as the FLCA. In addition, since lower weight equals lower fuel consumption and therefore reduced emissions, this steel contributes to protecting the environment.

Both companies worked closely throughout the process, from composition of the material to the characterisation and, finally, the homologation of the steel to be used in parts as sensitive to vehicle stability as a front lower control arm (FLCA).

Edscha also develops customised sliding door solutions for passenger cars and commercial vehicles. In addition to the lower, middle and upper roller, it has developed a multi-part rail in which both aluminium and plastic components can be used. The benefits of this new concept include less weight, lower costs, lower tolerances and faster processes.
