# Single and multiple decoiling lines

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

Forstner Machinery (hall 13, stand B50), will be showcasing its decoiling lines at this October's EuroBLECH exhibition. Three different decoiling lines will be on display in Hanover. Forstner will be showing individual solutions for fully automatic decoiling, sheet changing, straightening, slitting and cutting-to-length of various kinds of sheet metal. By using a nesting software and a programmable slitter from Forstner, the production of blanks from coil can be more efficient.

Forstner produces multiple decoiling lines with coil selectors for the quick-change of coils. Four powered decoilers for thick coils (to a maximum of 1250mm wide and 0.40 - 1mm thick coils) can be stored. The four coils are fed via sheet guide-lanes into the vertically adjustable coil selector for a maximum of nine coils. The straightener adjusts automatically to the number of coils selected. The programmable slitter can produce a maximum of 11 strips at the same time. An electric guillotine and graphic touchscreen panel complete the line. When using the Forstner multiple decoiling line, up to a total of eight different material types are available "just in time". It is therefore possible to have the right mixture of coil width, sheet thickness and material types for blank production.

A single decoiling line with hydraulic decoiler, programmable straightener (with sensitive material mode), manual slitter and electric guillotine will also be on display in Hanover. The straightener is designed to prevent marking and damaging sensitive and expensive material.

Forstner's smallest line for slitting and cutting to length is the MST-AL, equipped with the new Forstner SPS-S1 control unit. The control is a retrofit control for used lines as well as for new standard lines.
