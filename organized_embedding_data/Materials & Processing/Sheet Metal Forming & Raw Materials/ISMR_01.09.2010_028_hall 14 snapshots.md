## ACTAS GMBH

## D – Hall 14, Stand G01

## Website: www.actas-gmbh.de

Actas supplies CATIA add-on applications for sheet metal method planning and tool & die engineering. It has developed a range of advanced CATIA modules in close cooperation with German car manufacturers and their supply industry. TDE Method Planning Assistant (MPA) computes and analyses draw directions, undercut, cut directions and cut angles; analysing punch directions and defining minimum die-size and die-opening dimensions. TDE Tool Design Assistant (TDA) maps complex contours; performs trimline development of complex sheet metal components; drawn blank size and product surface modification for spring-back and over-crowning. ●

## FORMING TECHNOLOGIES

## Website: www.forming.com

FTI provides software for the design, validation and costing of sheet metal components. Since 1989, FTI has provided OEMs and suppliers in the automotive, aerospace and appliance industries with innovative, modular software solutions to reduce development time and material costs. With FormingSuite, FTI offers the first regenerative and associative Graphical User Interface including the recent innovation of parameterised models for Multistage Simulation. FormingSuite supports FTI’s inverse, implicit and explicit solver solutions.

## GASPARINI INDUSTRIES S.R.L.

## I– Hall 14, Stand G32 Website: www.gasparini.com

Gasparini S.p.A. is located in Mirano, in north-east Italy, 20km from Venice. It is a leading European company producing high tech machinery for the sheet metal processing industry. Its services have been used by more than 1,200 manufacturing companies around the world since 1952. Gasparini rollforming lines are tailor-made machines, with integrated processes such as punching, drilling, folding, welding, cutting, handling and packing, depending upon customer requirements.

## TASK 84

## I – Hall 14, Stand G03

## Website: www.task84.it

Task84 has been planning and producing electronic systems for the automation of manufacturing machines since 1984. Task84’s presence at EuroBLECH 2010 has one objective: to present its complete range of automation for press brakes (numerical controls, drives, bending cam, simulators for robots). On display will be Bending System CAM (software that locates and resolves problems with bending management) and SimRobot (3D graphics simulation software) developed for the automation robot sector. Tk6, Tk10 and Tk15 are three numerical controls that complete the range of bending products.

## VISION ENGINEERING LTD.

## D– Hall 14, Stand F75

## Website: www.visioneng.de

Vision Engineering Ltd is a manufacturer of ergonomic stereo microscopes and non-contact measuring systems. Since its formation in 1958, it has become one of the most innovative and dynamic microscope manufacturers, with offices across Europe, Asia and North America. Engineers and scientists use its systems for a wide range of general magnification, inspection and measurement applications. More than 300,000 units have been installed worldwide to date. New at EuroBLECH will be the Falcon video measuring solution and its range of award-winning stereo inspection microscopes (such as the Mantis), and noncontact optical and video metrology solutions.
