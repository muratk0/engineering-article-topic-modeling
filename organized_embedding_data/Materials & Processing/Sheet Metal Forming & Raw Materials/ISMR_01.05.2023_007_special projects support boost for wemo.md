# Special Projects support boost for WEMO

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

North American Market, WEMO.

Danyel Benoit has joined the sales team for WEMO Special Projects. He has 30+ years of experience in high-end sheet metal fabrication solutions and will be responsible for further business development and expansion in Canada.

“We are very pleased with the arrival of our new colleague whose vast knowledge, experience and drive to achieve the highest possible performance in the industry perfectly fit our strategy to strengthen our presence in the Canadian market," commented WEMO, a line automation specialist.

"Just like the European market, the Canadian market is also undergoing strong development in the automation of sheet metal production. This process is further strengthened by labour market shortages, the desire for short delivery times, production per order and flexibility in the production process," continued WEMO.

Danyel's extensive experience in developing new and existing markets at various companies such as the BLM Group, Danobat and AMADA equip him well as area manager, Canada, for WEMO, added the company.

"We are beginning a new chapter to further our development of the North American market; it is a market of great importance to WEMO and fits our planned expansion in coming decades, with emphasis on growing our sales and services. I look forward to working closely with Danyel and bringing great solutions to our customers," added René van Zon, Vice President Business Development,

WEMO's solutions include elevator panels; shelving; electrical enclosures; wall and ceiling panels and steel doors with further automotive and HVAC applications.

## An eye on India

WEMO has also expanded its strategic market presence by assigning Amit Upreti as VP Business Development for the Indian market. As of 1 February 2023, he joined the WEMO sales team for WEMO Special Projects.

“Amit has 10+ years of experience in high-end sheet metal fabrication solutions and will be responsible for further business development and expansion in India. We are very pleased with the arrival of our new colleague, whose knowledge, experience and energy is exactly what we need to expand our presence in the Indian growth market," commented the company.

Amit Upreti was born and raised in India and has successfully worked for the past ten years for a manufacturer of stand-alone bending solutions as national sales manager.

WEMO has identified huge potential in the Indian market.

“The country is experiencing economic and demographic growth, which means there will be more demand for a variety of steel products much more. Many WEMO customers also established manufacturing facilities in India 5-10 years ago and now want to upgrade their manufacturing facilities by moving away from stand-alone machines and meet demand with automated sheet-metal bending and punching lines from WEMO," it told ISMR.

WEMO Special Projects is a family business in the Netherlands with 60 years of experience in sheet-metal processing and machine building. It develops, build and installs fully automated metalworking machines for punching, bending and joining sheet metal and coil material into a semi-finished or finished product.
