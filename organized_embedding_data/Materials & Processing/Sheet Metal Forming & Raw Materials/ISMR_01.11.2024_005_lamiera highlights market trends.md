# LAMIERA highlights market trends

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

LAMIERA, the international exhibition dedicated to sheet metal forming and cutting machine tools and technologies, will take place from 6-9 May 2025 in Milan, Italy.

LAMIERA is organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU and promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robotics and automation system manufacturer's association).

## Italian metal forming and cutting trends

At a meeting during the EuroBLECH exhibition in Germany this October, chaired by Filippo Gasparini (VP of UCIMU), Riccardo Gaslini (UCIMU's exhibition manager) outlined Italian metal forming market trends as well as the latest LAMIERA news.

"In the world of sheet metalforming and cutting, Italy takes second place in the world ranking of production and exports, and third place in consumption (2023 data). With a production of 3.4 billion euros, up 8% on 2023, the Italian metal forming machinery and technology industry consolidated its second position in the world ranking of manufacturers. It came second, after China, but ahead of Germany," Gaslini explained.

## LAMIERA in focus

"LAMIERA is the only trade fair in Italy exclusively dedicated to the metal forming sector. It is chosen by more and more Italian and overseas companies to present their projects and innovative solutions. Its slogan is 'The Bright Side of Metal Forming' and there will be a special focus on technological themes related to machine tools," Gaslini said.

"FABBRICA FUTURA will be dedicated to software and technologies for connectivity, data management and automation as well as to digital systems and robotics applied to sheet metal processing. SALDATECH will offer an overview of the wide and diversified world of welding. FASTENER INDUSTRY will showcase screws, bolts, fastening and clamping systems. WORLD OF HEAT AND SURFACE TREATMENTS will host machines, systems, accessories and consumables focused on pre- and post-production treatments, for ferrous and non-ferrous materials. METROLOGY & TESTING will centre on testing, measurement and quality control systems and machines. BLECH ITALY

“Italy did even better in terms of exports. Thanks to its excellent performance in the overseas market (with an increase of almost 30% in exports to a value of 1.8 billion euros) in 2023, Italy again took second place in the international rankings after China and before Germany," he added.

According to Gaslini, Italy came third in the international consumption rankings (-7.6%, 1.9 billion euros) in 2023. Despite this fall, Italy is one of the most important consumer markets worldwide, after China and the United States.

Filippo Gasparini added: "We expected weak performance, starting in 2023 and confirmed in 2024 which, according to our new forecasts, will stop as early as 2025 thanks to the availability of the new Transition 5.0 law in Italy."

According to new forecasts by Oxford Economics for the entire machine-tool industry sector (metal cutting and metalforming machines), 2024 is expected to be a complicated year for the world sector industry, but “recovery is expected in 2025 and confirmed for 2026, said UCIMU.

"In this recovery context, also confirmed for 2026, Italy will show strong resilience. After the downturn in 2024, and already in 2025, we will see a significant rebound in demand for investment in new production technologies by Italian users (accounting for 10.5%, to reach 3.8 billion euros). This positive trend should continue even in 2026, although at a slower pace (+ 4.2%, 3.9 billion euros)," outlined UCIMU.
