# FRONTIERS OF ENGINEERING

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Diana Lados, assistant professor of mechanical engineering at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI) and director of the university’s Integrative Materials Design Centre (iMdc), is among 87 young engineers selected to take part in the National Academy of Engineering’s (NAE) 16th annual US Frontiers of Engineering symposium. Lados is the first WPI professor chosen to participate in this prestigious programme.

Earlier this year, she received the 2010 Sigma Xi Graduate Research Scientific Award for the best PhD thesis, the American Foundry Society’s Aluminum Division Scholarship Award and the ASM Worcester Chapter Chester M. Inman Award.

In 2008, she received the Orr Early Career Award and the Orr Best Paper Award from the Materials Division of the American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME).

Lados has published more than 50 articles and one book chapter and delivered more than 100 conference and industrial presentations. In addition to her early career awards, she has received the Axel Madsen Award from the Center for Powder Metallurgy Technology, the Sigma Xi Outstanding Junior Faculty Researcher Award from WPI and the Robert Lansing Hardy Award, which honours young metallurgists who demonstrate “exceptional promise of a successful career in the broad field of metallurgy and materials science“ from The Minerals, Metals & Materials Society (TMS).

She is also a founding member of the Women in Materials Science and Engineering Committee of TMS.
