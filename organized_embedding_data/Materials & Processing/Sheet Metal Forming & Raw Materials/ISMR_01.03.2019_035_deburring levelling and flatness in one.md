# Deburring, levelling and flatness in one

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

ARKU PRESENTED ITS triple linked production line for the first time at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany (October 2018).

"This line covers all the crucial processing steps such as deburring, levelling and measuring flatness. Its core components consist of an EdgeBreaker® 2000 Plus, which deburrs and rounds both sides of the panels, and a FlatMaster® 55 80 precision leveller which transforms them into flat panels almost entirely free of internal stresses. At the end of the chain, the integrated FlatJack® control system checks the flatness of the cut pieces. With this linked system, we cover all central material preparation processing steps with one single line," the manufacturer told ISMR.
