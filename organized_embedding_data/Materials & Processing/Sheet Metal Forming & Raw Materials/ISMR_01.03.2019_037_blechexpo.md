## PRODUCT REVIEWS

New servo press launch

SIMPAC LAUNCHED A new servo press generation at the EuroBLECH exhibition in October 2018. Since the foundation of Simpac Europe GmbH at the beginning of 2017, the focus of the international team has been on research and development, sales and marketing. The MX series (3,000 – 10,000kN) and the SX series (8,000 – 30,000kN) are the first results of this new, German-Korean cooperative venture.

"The presses have been further developed; the entire drive concept has been revised with an eye on efficiency and dynamics. The very solid, low-stress annealed press bodies are FEM-calculated and improved by individual 'hot-spot analysis' in area which are subject to higher loads. Motor, brake, lubrication and power electronics have been replaced by German brands. These offer the highest dynamic properties, a long service life and a reliable supply of spare parts," commented the company.

The automatic punching and forming presses in the MX series are particularly suitable for processing small and medium-sized components. The standardised press concept enables application as a stand-alone press in progressive or transfer operation as well as installation as an interlinked line. The SX series covers all larger components and features motors in three different performance classes, which are individually adapted to the customer's requirements. See www.simpac-europe.com

## Print Sharp, 250

PRIMA ADDITIVE presented Print Sharp 250, its new machine for Powder Bed Fusion applications, at the A&T Automation and Testing trade fair (dedicated to Industry 4.0; test and measurement; innovative technologies and robotics) in Turin, Italy from 13-15 February 2019.

The Print Sharp 250 is its medium-volume machine for powder bed fusion applications. Designed to facilitate efficient operation, Print Sharp 250 includes an intelligent control software and user-friendly interface designed to enable easy set-up and operation.

"Simple operating and maintenance procedures can increase the machine's utilisation rate and performance. With a building volume of 250 x 250 x 300mm, Print Sharp 250 assures high-quality components in terms of surface and mechanical characteristics and can offer post-processing solutions for demanding targets," Prima Additive told ISMR.

Visitors at the A&T exhibition could handle workpieces produced by the Print Sharp 250 and examine Prima Additive's latest additive manufacturing solutions. The company's stand also showcased the Convergent CS450 fibre laser source, developed by Prima Electro for additive manufacturing (using laser expertise from the Prima Industrie Group).

Prima Additive's stand also hosted the Fondazione Amaldi, an important Italian research foundation. Its main goal is to promote and support scientific research, mainly in the aerospace sector. This follows the recent agreement between Prima Additive and the foundation to support the development of additive manufacturing applications for the aerospace sector. See www.primaadditive.com
