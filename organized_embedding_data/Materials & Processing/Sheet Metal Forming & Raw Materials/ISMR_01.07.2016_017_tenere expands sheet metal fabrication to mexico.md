# Tenere expands sheet metal fabrication to Mexico

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

TENERE INC., a full service provider of complex metal and plastic components and assemblies, has announced the expansion of its sheet metal capabilities with the addition of a facility in Monterrey, Mexico. The new Tenere Monterrey sheet metal operation is the company's first wholly-owned international site.

The Tenere Monterrey facility will complement Tenere's sheet metal fabrication capabilities by adding capacity and strengthening its core competencies of engineering support, production, quality and assembly. The new facility provides soft tool metal fabrication and assembly services. Capabilities include laser cutting, punching, bending, pressed hardware and welding, with expansion plans to include powder coating. The facility has approximately 103,000 square feet of manufacturing space.

"Our Tenere Monterrey operation is well positioned to provide an expanded offering of services and solutions to our customers in the region," stated Francisco Carreon, General Manager, Tenere Monterrey. "Critical Monterrey functional positions were trained out of Tenere's Wisconsin sheet metal facilities to assure our customers that the Tenere Monterrey facility and its employees have a deep understanding of Tenere brand quality. We are ready to help our customers succeed."

KEMPPI INDIA and Amaljyothi College of Engineering have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to set up the Kemppi Amaljyothi Centre of Welding at Kanjirappali in the state of Kerala, India. Amaljyothi College is a well known educational college and numbers several institutions under its brand name.

The MoU was signed by Rashmi Ranjan Mohapatra, managing director of Kemppi India, and Rev. Dr. Fr Jose Kannampuzha, Principal, Amaljyothi College of Engineering, in the presence of Dr. Samuel, Dr. Shaju Albert and Mr. Radhakrishnan Unni. The centre will undertake to train welders from across India, with a special focus on female welders.

The college's metallurgy department will also carry out welding R&D activities, in collaboration with Kemppi India.
