## Regional initiatives

Rising investments by the Chinese government in the development of innovative infrastructure will augur well for the coil-coatings market.

"In February 2022, for instance, local governments across China announced 16,079 key investment projects for 2022 after the Spring Festival holiday, with a total investment of more than US$ 3.95 trillion. Therefore, similar investment plans by government bodies will continue pushing sales in the China market," outlined Future Market Insights.

The presence of large numbers of coil-coating product manufacturers in China, along with increasing import and export activities, are set to drive the market. The Asia Pacific excluding Japan (APEJ) coil-coatings market is expected to account for about 39.3% of the share in 2022, confirmed FMI.

The presence of a well-established automotive industry in Germany is also expected to augur well for the market (it accounts for around 25% of all passenger cars and 20% of all new registrations in Europe).

"The Western Europe coil-coatings market is set to hold around 21% of the share in 2022," estimated FMI.

Increasing spending on non-residential construction in the U.S. is likely to bolster the market in the forthcoming years. Analyst FMI believes that North America is anticipated to account for approximately 18.9% of total market share in 2022.

## New coating investments

The new 120 million-euro coating line 13 for packaging steel, at thyssenkrupp Rasselstein GmbH, has now been completed after three years of construction. It will produce chrome-plated packaging steel used for the safe and durable packaging of food in cans, lids and crown corks. Commissioning of the plant was celebrated during a party for employees at Andernach on 17 September 2022 with the Minister of Labour of the State of Rhineland-Palatinate. The new equipment enables chrome-plating of even thinner and wider metal sheet.

“The new plant uses chromium(III) in the manufacturing process instead of the chromium(VI) previously used. The new manufacturing process therefore fully complies with the requirements of the EU REACH Regulation. The new production plant is the largest single investment at the Andernach site in around 15 years," commented the manufacturer.

Bernhard Osburg, Chairman of the Executive Board of thyssenkrupp Steel Europe AG, added: "This major investment marks an important and very special milestone in the history of thyssenkrupp Steel. We are converting to the highest sustainability standards here at the world's largest production site for packaging steel."

More efficient and energy-saving processes can be achieved with a digitalised workflow. Chrome-plated material is used for lids and bases for pet food cans, valve disks on aerosol cans, crown corks or printing ink cans.

BASF Coatings has inaugurated a new research centre for electrophoretic dip coating at its headquarters in Münster-Hiltrup, Germany. The electrophoretic or cathodic dip coat (e-coat) protects the surfaces, edges and cavities of a car body against corrosion, and evens out the roughness of the pre-treated metal surfaces, providing a strong basis for application of the subsequent paint layers.

"We invested 15 million euros in the new building at the Münster site," said Mathias Schöttke, Managing Director, BASF Coatings GmbH. "The health and safety of our colleagues is particularly important to us at BASF. This is why, during the planning stage, we have not only given high priority to the quality and efficiency of the coating process, but also to ergonomics and occupational safety right from the start."

Frank Naber, head of the Automotive OEM Coatings Solutions EMEA business unit at BASF Coatings, emphasised: "In our new e-coat research centre, we can simulate our customers' processes and paint systems. We can coat pre-treated original parts from our customers under the conditions of their individual paint lines in a 2,000-litre dip tank and bake them in a chamber oven. These parts can be doors, fenders or bonnets."

In addition, the research centre has two ultra-filtration and filtration stands with which the quality and stability of new e-coats can be tested under the conditions at customer sites before the tank is filled for the first time.

"In this way, we can ensure that the coating quality is at a very high level immediately after changing over to a new e-coat material, and that it meets the customer's specification," explained Naber.

The centre will mainly be used for the CathoGuard®800 e-coat technology, which has been applied to more than 100 million vehicles worldwide.

## Sustainability in focus

“We are an innovation leader in arc welding and robotic welding, focusing specifically on industry sectors such as automotive industry, heavy machinery, general industry and commercial transportation. The Welding Automation division creates custom automated complete welding solutions for container construction and offshore cladding, for example. We work very closely with our customers to find a custom solution for any welding challenge. People and their needs are at the heart of what we do," explained Harald Scherleitner, head of the Fronius Perfect Welding Business Unit.

The unit also aims to help shape future trends in welding technology. The Cold Metal Transfer process, for example, enables the thermal joining of galvanised steel with aluminium sheet (down to a thickness of 0.3mm). It also seeks to be the innovation leader in sustainability.

"We place a special focus on quality, repairability, recycling and long service life as early as the development stage of our products to ensure a sustainable resource use. Our welding instruments are characterised, in particular, by high levels of energy efficiency. We were the first manufacturer to integrate transistor-based inverter technology, for example. This translates to around one third lower electricity consumption and about 80- per cent savings in terms of raw materials. Sustainability is anchored in our company DNA. Our pillars of sustainable management, where we measure our achievements, are Planet, People and Profit," continued Scherleitner.

Fronius was awarded with a prestigious Silver Status sustainability rating from EcoVadis in 2022, and 81 % in the S-Rating Sustainability Test from SAQ (an evidencebased supplier sustainability assessment on the NQC platform).

"We don't make success dependent on financial profit alone, but always take a holistic view of our business activities. What impact do they have on people and the environment? How can we manage risks on a long-term basis? And how do we make an effective contribution to a sustainable world worth living in? These are the considerations that make us more crisis-resistant and create added value for our customers. When it comes to environmental and climate protection, we have set ourselves some ambitious targets. For instance, we want to stop using fossil fuels at all Fronius sites in Austria and switch to 100% renewable energy sources," added Scherleitner.

In production, cleanliness is a key criterion: impurities on the component must be removed in advance so that various process steps can be carried out correctly. This is important for processes such as adhesion and sealing, painting and printing, as well as brazing and welding. The Acerios system from Fronius is designed to enable environmentally friendly surface cleaning using plasma technology.
