# Hot strip galvanizing line with "heat-to-coat" technology

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

THE SMS GROUP (www.sms-group.com) has been awarded an order to supply a "heat-to-coat" line with an annual capacity of 500,000 tons for Nucor Steel Gallatin in Ghent, Kentucky, USA. "Heat-to-coat" technology is characterised by a compact U-shape design, a turbulence pickling system, a high-power inductive heating system, FOEN galvanising equipment and a Drever after-pot cooling system.

Above: The new SMS group "heat-to-coat" pickling and galvanizing line for Nucor Steel Gallatin will be capable of producing 500,000 tons of galvanised hot strip per year

"With a high production rate of 180 tons per hour and a large strip cross-section (up to 6.35mm thickness and up to 1,854mm width), this line sets a new global standard for hot strip galvanizing. Start-up is anticipated for 2019," explained the SMS Group.

"The 'heat-to-coat' process, based on SMS patented technology, is designed for high profitability, as all processes take place in a single facility. The process enables the production of galvanized steel strip with durable corrosion protection, an appealing visual appearance as well as an increased mechanical load-capacity while still maintaining low production and investment costs. With the integrated inductive galvannealing furnace, the line is also able to produce galvannealed strips."

There is a broad area of applications for galvanized hot strip, especially in construction, transportation and the automotive industry. It is also possible to substitute galvanized cold strip with hot strip.

"The U-shape design allows quick and easy bypass of the coating section to use the line as a continuous pickling line and produce just pickled and oiled material. Since entry and exit are located side by side, fewer staff are required. The 'heat-to-coat' process stands for environmentally-friendly production of galvanized material since the entire process takes place on one line and uses efficient technologies. Another advantage is the inductive furnace which operates on electrical energy and is therefore emissionfree. A special fume exhaust system will also be integrated to ensure low emission rates over the entire process," added the SMS Group.

For post-treatment, a skin pass mill, tension leveller and horizontal roll coater system have been integrated.
