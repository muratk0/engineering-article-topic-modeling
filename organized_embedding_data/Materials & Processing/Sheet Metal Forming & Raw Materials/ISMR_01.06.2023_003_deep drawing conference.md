# Deep drawing conference

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

The 42nd Conference of the International Deep Drawing Research Group, IDDRG 2023, will be held from 19-22 June 2023 in Luleå, Sweden.

Conference topics will include formability; fossil-free steel; sustainable materials and material forming. The programme will also cover advanced/smart materials; bipolar plates and multi-material concepts/solutions. Lightweight structures; digital transformation; digitalisation and smart factories/press shops will also come under the spotlight.

Subjects such as cold and hot forming and trimming (including single- and multistep forming); tribology; simulations; testing; springback; surface quality; tooling and additive manufacturing will also be highlighted at the conference.

A range of Plenary speakers have been invited, including Per-Emil Back (R&D Director, Gestamp R&D, Sweden); Bart Carleer (AutoForm Engineering, Switzerland); Vincent Grolleau (Senior Scientist at the Chair of Artificial Intelligence in Mechanics and Manufacturing at ETH's Department of Mechanical and Process Engineering) and Mathias Liewald (Head of the Institute for Forming Technology at the University of Stuttgart, Germany).

Martin Pei, Executive Vice President & CTO, SSAB AB (Swedish Steel) and Mats Sigvant (Technical Leader in Sheet Metal Forming Simulations, Volvo Cars, Sweden) will also be speaking at the conference.■
