# Cold roll forming

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

SEN FUNG started in 1963 as a professional manufacturer of cold roll forming machine tools. It has amassed over 50 years of expertise and continues its efforts in research, development and product improvements.

“We are a major supplier and leading exporter of this equipment in Taiwan. We manufacture different kinds of metal sheet cold roll forming machines for producing products such as carbon steel pipe; stainless steel pipe; light gauge steel; door frames; roofing corrugated sheet; roofing tiles; wall panels; ceiling T-bars; steel decks; partition beams and other sheet metal building materials,” the company told ISMR.

Its C&Z pre-cut purlin features web sizes from 100 to 300mm and flanges from 50 to 100mm. Thickness is self-regulated. C or Z profiles can be automatically changed over in less than one minute by pushing a button on the control cabinet. With pre-cut systems, no cut-off dies are required. The purlin features a flexible in-line punching system, automatic C and Z profile changeovers and a straightening unit to rectify any material distortions. Forming speed is 10-20m/min.
