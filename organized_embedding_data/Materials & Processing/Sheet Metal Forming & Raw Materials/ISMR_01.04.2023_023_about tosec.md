# About Tosec

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

From machine building to offshore and from heavy lifting to earthmoving, Tosec produces customer-specific sheet metal and construction work. This includes XXL sheet-metal working to construction assemblies of up to 40m long. It has a versatile range of machine tools and aims for rapid, reliable delivery. Tosec is ISO 9001, ISO 3834-2 and EN 1090-2 certified and has re-marking authority.

Long bending and long profiles are used in the on-/offshore sector, trailer construction and in conveyor belts as well as shipbuilding, civil engineering and hydraulic engineering. Due to the large pressing force of Tosec's machines, it also provides the option of bending highstrength steel and wear-resistant steel.

Tosec manufactures a range of parts for civil engineering construction, for example, including roof racks, anchor seats; pontoons; parts for bridges and complete steel constructions. In addition to sheet-metal work, operations such as breaking edges, pre-processing welding edges or sawing profiles are also part of its daily work. It also bends a wide range of profiles in steel sheet.

## About SafanDarley

SafanDarley is a global specialist in developing and building press brakes and shears for thin and thick sheet metal. It also provides automated, modular and software solutions for bending sheet metal in future production environments, contributing to lower energy consumption, higher productivity and cost savings.

The company has two production plants, a head office in Lochem and a branch in Eijsden (the Netherlands). It employs approximately 200 members of staff.

On its stand at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (October 2022), it set up a special area to represent the production environment of the future. The automatic Mini Cell and R-Brake bending cell were installed there and, during the exhibition, the R-Brake 130T produced fork-lift parts. The company also showcased its I-Mate intelligent CNC bending aid, installed on the E-Brake 130T Ultra press brake, and its new Hybrid press brake model, the H-Brake Hybrid+ 230T Ultra.
