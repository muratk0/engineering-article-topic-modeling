# Coil punching and shearing solution

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Pivatic Oy designs, manufactures and markets punching, cutting and bending equipment and lines for the thin sheet-metal manufacturing industry. The Finnish specialist tailors its lines to produce parts for OEMs in various industry sectors from metal furniture; white goods and electrical enclosures to HVAC equipment and industrial construction. The lines include automated punch and bend from coil or blank stock production systems for highvolume producers of precision sheet-metal components.

Founded in 1975, with production facilities and headquarters in Hyvinkää, Finland, it has a wide sales and partner network around the globe of more than 30 representatives. The customisation of hundreds of lines installed worldwide has been a key pillar of Pivatic's success. It is a member of The Ursviken Group, a global provider of high-end solutions for sheet-metal manufacturers which provides a worldwide business network for production, sales and services.

Pivatic is now offering a new coil punching and shearing solution to metal manufacturers.

"Pivatic systems give users multiple options to run your parts. Using advanced nesting, operators may choose the part type, amount and the order in which they want the parts to be produced. All parts of the same material can be produced from the same coil width, nested side-by-side. After the Pivatic double punching process, material is cut to length (CTL) from the coil by a shear," it explained.

To nest parts side-by-side on a strip, Pivatic uses a CTW (Cut-to-Width) station to shear and separate the nested parts. All parts are stacked or buffered automatically in piles on top of pallets for easy transportation or transferred directly to follow-up processes.

Waste is minimised (in the X and Y axes) by filling the coil with nested parts. By separating each process step, it aims to deliver significant leaps in productivity.

## On the surface

Fladder Danmark offers a wide range of manual, automatic and special machines for processing thin and thick sheet metal parts. These are used for deburring; oxide/dross removal; surface finishing; denibbing and intermediate sanding.

The Fladder® 300/GYRO HYBRID system for grinding, deburring and edge rounding removes laser spatter and burrs from material after laser cutting and punching. Edges are rounded in the same pass. The machine has a modular structure with a grinding belt and the oscillating FLADDER® Gear head. Workpieces are transported through the two grinding processes on a vacuum belt.

“To achieve optimal cycle times for the two very different grinding processes and to avoid unwanted heating of the workpiece, calibration grinding is carried out using a cross grinding belt. The grinding belt is pressed towards the workpiece by an oscillating contact wheel. The short contact time protects against impermissible heating and ensures optimal utilisation of the abrasive belt. Flexible FLADDER® Abrasive Cylinders are used to create perfect rounding and radius on the edges as the last step in the process," explained Fladder.

## NSX mechanical press

The press specialist will debut its NSX mechanical stamping press at the show. This newly designed; two-point; 110-metric ton; unitised frame press is capable of speeds ranging from 120-300 strokes per minute. It features a zero clearance; lubrication-free; eight-point roller slide guide system and dual counter-rotating eccentric shafts. Press and die integrity are protected by AIDA's metal seal type Hydraulic Overload Protection (HOLP) at 10 milliseconds to zero pressure.

Locally engineered at AIDA-America in Dayton, OH, the NSX includes many standard features such as a low deflection; rigid; unitised steel frame and a cast iron slide with detachable face. Other features include a 65mm stroke length; 50-inch L-R bed size; floor-mounted design for quick, pitfree installation and standard Allen-Bradley control (with optional Link and Wintriss controls).

The NSX is designed to meet the demands of high-volume stamping operations. It will be running at its top speed of 300spm on AIDA-America's booth at FABTECH 2023.
