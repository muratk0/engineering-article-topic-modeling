# U.S. Steel Košice places major order for ACL line

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

THE SLOVAKIAN COMPANY, U.S. Steel Košice – one of the largest integrated steel producers in Central Europe – has placed a major order for the supply of an Annealing and Coating Line (ACL) for dynamo steel strips to Tenova LOI Thermprocess, a specialist in heat treatment lines and furnaces based in Essen, Germany.

"The plant bears the internal name Dynamo-Line no. 4 and will meet stringent requirements for the production of non-grainoriented electrical steel. The contract scope includes the engineering, to a large extent the turnkey delivery of all equipment as well as the supervision of the assembly and commissioning, including training," Tenova told ISMR.

The entire line will be designed and delivered by Tenova, part of the Techint Group and a specialist in solutions for the metals and mining industries. Tenova, with its companies Tenova LOI Thermprocess and Tenova Italimpianti, will supply the complete Annealing and Coating Line (ACL). This includes the heat treatment section (furnace) of the plant, the entire strip handling and chemical processing with drying oven after coil coating and the associated electrical, instrumentation and control technology (including automation system).

U.S. Steel Košice is a wholly owned subsidiary of U. S. Steel Group, headquartered in the United States. It is a fully integrated metallurgical plant and one of the largest industrial companies in the Slovak Republic. In addition to the production of thin sheet, ultra-fine sheet and galvanized strip, nongrain-oriented electrical steel sheets have been produced for more than 30 years. The company is making extensive modernisation measures to improve product quality. One of the measures is the dismantling of two outdated annealing and coating lines as well as the construction of the new modern annealing and coating plant.

From 2000-2019, Tenova received a total of 60 orders for heat treatment systems for electrical steel, of which 34 plants are completely new installations. For more information, visit www.tenova.com■
