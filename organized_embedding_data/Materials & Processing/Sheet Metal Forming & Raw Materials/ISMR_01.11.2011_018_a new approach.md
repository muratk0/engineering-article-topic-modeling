# A NEW APPROACH

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

## IRON SpA has announced a historic agreement with Chicago Slitter to expand its business in the US market.

Massimo dalla Pace (centre) of IRON flanked by Chicago Slitter management

IRON SpA, the Italian specialist coil processing manufacturer, has announced a historic public relations agreement with Chicago Slitter (part of the RDI group) to expand its business in the US market. For more than 30 years, Chicago Slitter has been a leading designer and builder of high performance heavy duty coil slitting lines, blanking lines, cut-to-length lines and slit coil packaging lines with more than 1200 successful installations worldwide.

"IRON SpA and Chicago Slitter will share product and market coil processing expertise for the US market"

“Chicago Slitter is part of The RDI group – the group also features a roofing division (steel coil). It started operations in 1970, whereas IRON started operations in 1969 and our two companies share similar philosophies, design styles and a strong focus on coil processing and high-end slitting operations,” Massimo dalla Pace, export sales manager of IRON SpA told ISMR.

“We have been looking at the US market for a long time but have never been able to find the right partner for this market, up until now. After visiting Chicago Slitter, we decided that they were the right partner for us in this public relation agreement. We signed the agreement and they will now manufacture and sell our coil processing products in the US (apart from our slitting lines) and share product and market expertise as well as development and marketing costs.”

IRON’s four main products – its cut to length lines, coil feeding lines for mechanical presses, punching and bending line from coil and laser cutting from coil line (dubbed TEMNOS) will be sold through the Chicago Slitter portfolio in the US.

“By working this way, we can give better technical service support around the clock and share our product design knowledge to produce even more customised products for this market,” dalla Pace continued.

## RENEWED DEVELOPMENT FOCUS

IRON SpA continues to develop its TEMNOS laser cutting from coil line as well as its punching and bending line from coil with a view to injecting more flexibility and efficiency into manufacturing operations. The punching and bending line is a flexible modular line that incorporates a punch press (with thick turret tooling), a part-turning device (after punching), positive and negative bends on the punched parts and can handle part dimensions ranging from 650mm width x 1500 mm length to 1500mm width x 3600mm in length.

Other design elements promote high yield output such as tolerance quality and accuracy in both operations – punching and bending – as well as the ability to hold tight strip tolerances, which can potentially increase production by 100 per cent, says IRON.

“We are continually looking at new product and machine tool development to complement our slitting and cut-to-length lines,” dalla Pace explained. “The main advantages of using coil over sheet is that you don’t need to cut to length, there is no need to nest (hence less scrap) and no downtime as you do not have to wait for blanks to be loaded onto the machine. There are substantial time and cost advantages to using coil over blanks with less material used overall. It is a more efficient process and certainly less costly.” ■
