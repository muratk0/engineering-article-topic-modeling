Plate processing machine creates complex bevels in single cut

Tecoi North America has introduced the TRF plate processing machine for heavy welding edge preparation and drilling in such industries as wind tower fabrication, civil construction, shipbuilding, large steel structures, large vessels, and petrochemical infrastructure.

The machine incorporates CBM Cold Bevel Milling technology to create complex bevels in a single, high-speed cut. According to the company, this results in improved quality and dimensional tolerance without affecting the material properties of the metal. The edges do not need to be cleaned after the bevel is cut, and bevels for narrow-gap welding can be milled in one pass. Customized milling tools with specific geometries are available.

The machine features an automatic multihead system that duplicates productivity in the same processing station. A built-in automatic tool storage selection system also is available, capable of storing and configuring five tools for each job. A marking system can be provided for laser, plasma, scribing, micropercussion, and ink, with options available for text, numbers, bar codes, and auxiliary lines.

Compact unit assists manual change of robotic end-of-arm tooling

ATI Industrial Automation has introduced the MC-50 manual tool changer for changing robotic end-of-arm tooling by hand. The compact unit is designed for applications on collaborative robots that support payloads up to 25 kg and small industrial robots supporting payloads up to 10 kg.

Featuring an ISO 50-mm mounting interface on the master side and tool side, the low-profile unit mounts directly to most cobots and integrates with many common cobot marketplace grippers and end effectors. A safety latch button on the lever provides secondary locking for increased safety and prevention of involuntary uncoupling.

Headset offers hearing protection with clear, hands-free communication

Savox Communications has introduced the Savox NC – 520XP hearing protection headset. Part of the Noise-Com 500 series, the headset is designed for reliable use in demanding and noisy environments.

Along with acoustic protection, the headset offers Savox Dual Bluetooth, which connects two Bluetooth devices simultaneously, in most cases a radio and a mobile phone. This enables hands-free communication and allows users to listen to radio transmissions and still be reachable. The noise-canceling push-to-talk boom microphone helps ensure clear speech in loud environments.

The headset also has an answer button and rotary switch for control of ambient sound volume and incoming communication.
