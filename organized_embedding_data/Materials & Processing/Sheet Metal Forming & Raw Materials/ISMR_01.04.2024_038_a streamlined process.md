# A STREAMLINED PROCESS

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Metal door fabricator, Fireblock, has increased its productivity five-fold by integrating an automated bending centre into its production.

Above: The production layout for the bending centre at Fireblock Ireland.

ISMR SAYS:

“Fireblock, a manufacturer of fire-resistant doors and windows, has massively increased the efficiency and precision of its manufacturing process at its factory in Ballymakeera, Ireland.”

In the heart of Ireland, Fireblock, a manufacturer of fire and explosion-resistant doors and windows, has achieved a remarkable breakthrough in metal fabrication. By implementing advanced technologies, the company has managed to increase its productivity five-fold while establishing new quality standards.

General Manager, Pat Collins, explained: "Our mission was not only to increase efficiency, but also to improve the precision and ergonomic efficiency of the process." To achieve this, Fireblock integrated a fully automatic RAS Multibend-Center bending centre into its production line to bend its door panels.

The machine has a bending length of 2560mm and is completed with a two-place gantry loading system, which alternately feeds a sheet metal blank for the cover and the box of the door leaf to the bending cell. The second machine, the RAS ProfileCenter, automatically bends the elements of the door frames.

## Automate to accumulate

Fireblock designs, manufactures, delivers and installs a wide range of multipurpose steel doors and windows for various types of commercial and industrial applications including the food industry; clean rooms; electronics; engineering; medical; petrochemical; manufacturing; telecommunications; construction, aerospace and defence.

The RAS machines are designed for speed, precision and repeat accuracy.

“Every element that we produce corresponds exactly to the given specifications. With fire protection products, this level of accuracy is essential," emphasised Collins. This precision has enabled Fireblock to virtually eliminate rejects and rework as well as achieve a significant increase in quality.

The automatic two-place gantry loading system has revolutionised the production processes at Fireblock. By automating the entire loading process for the heavy and unwieldy sheet metal blanks, the Multibend-Center has raised the throughput speed and repeat accuracy in the production of door panels at the manufacturer's facility.

A laser scanner in the suction frame of the gantry loading system reads all production-related information via a QR code attached to the blank. The Multibend Center then automatically loads the appropriate bending program and feeds the sheet metal blank to the bending centre. The exact position of the blank is measured on the material transfer table and then transferred to a precision manipulator, which guides the bent part through the bending process. If a sheet metal blank needs to be fed into the process by hand at short notice, the operator can do this by handing it over to the transfer table. This creates flexibility without any loss of speed.

## Speed meets ergonomics

"The RAS Multibend-Center is characterised by its high speed and efficiency. Not only are the pure bending processes carried out in record time, but the tool change is also lightning fast and automatic. This is the task of the tool changer, which positions the tool segments with precision. Due to the complex bending geometries, it is even common for a tool change to take place during the bending process on a door leaf. Many bending geometries would therefore be inconceivable without automatic tool changes. This enables Fireblock to produce sheet metal components in record time without having to compromise on quality," commented RAS.

It used to take two employees and ten minutes to produce a door panel on pressbrakes. The machine operators had to be well attuned to each other and lift and guide the large and heavy blank for each bend. Today, a single machine operator looks after the bending centre without actively intervening in the bending process. This means that a bending cycle can be completed precisely and repeatably in two minutes without any physically strenuous work.

"This not only brings considerable cost benefits, but also protects the health of the employees. This ergonomic improvement has significantly added to the manufacturing efficiencies at Fireblock," added RAS.

## An eye on efficiency

Through the combined use of the RAS ProfileCenter, Fireblock has also achieved significant speed improvements in the production of door frames. Where it used to take several employees 15 minutes to produce a metal door frame, this can now be done in four minutes with just one employee.

Above and below: The RAS ProfileCenter automatically bends door frames at Fireblock Ireland.

Three steel single doors with lock latch, levers and thumbturn locks were installed at Applegreen service station (Lusk) by Fireblock in 2017, as well as one steel double door with panic bar and external access/access control key pad.

"In addition to the increase in efficiency, the automatic bending of the frames also brought a leap in quality, particularly for further processing of the frame elements. The side sections are inserted with the top section in pre-fabricated templates and can be welded together to form a complete door frame without any preparatory work," commented RAS.

The Multibend-Center is not only used for door panels. It also bends sheet metal parts from the Fireblock range and associated companies. This production synergy has increased the capacity utilisation of the bending centre, which matches the requirements of Fireblock product range components. With automatic loading via two loading stations, a further increase in productivity is possible with automation on the out-feed side of the finished bent parts.

## Looking to the future

To this end, Fireblock is planning further optimisations on the Multibend-Center, particularly for parts dispensing. The use of an unloading robot and a pallet station promise even greater efficiency. RAS bending specialists will support these forward-looking developments.

The implementation of RAS technologies as well as the combination of increased productivity, improved quality and ergonomic working conditions have not only strengthened Fireblock's competitiveness, but also cemented the company's role as a pioneer in the industry.
