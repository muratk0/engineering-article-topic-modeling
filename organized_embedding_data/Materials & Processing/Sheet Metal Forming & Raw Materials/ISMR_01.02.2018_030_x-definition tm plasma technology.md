# X-Definition™ TM plasma technology

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, A U.S.-BASED manufacturer of industrial cutting systems and software, showcased its latest plasma technology at IMTEX Forming 2018 (25–30 January 2018 in India).

"Visitors witnessed the capabilities and cut quality of our X-Definition plasma during live cutting demonstrations at the Hypertherm stand in Hall 4," the company told ISMR. Hypertherm's latest class of plasma, X-Definition™, is enabled by new technologies such as Vented Water Injection™ (VWI), plasma dampening and Vent-to-Shield technologies.

"The result is squarer-cut edges, markedly less angularity and excellent surface-finish on non-ferrous metals like aluminium and stainless steel. The technology enables the XPR300 to cut faster, providing users with greater productivity and lower operating costs when compared to earlier Hypertherm systems," added the company.

The XPR300 is easily operated. Sensors in the power supply deliver refined diagnostic codes and enhanced system monitoring information. This reduces troubleshooting time and provides proactive data to boost overall system optimisation and uptime. The system is designed with fewer consoles and connections so that operators can reduce time wasted on set-up and maximise cutting time. Other new design features include the EasyConnect™, QuickLock™ and the Quickchange torch for added improved usability.

Hypertherm also featured its Powermax125 plasma system at the show, and visitors viewed hourly live demonstrations of the Powermax125 and FlushCut consumables in action. FlushCut consumables provide users with the ability to cut closer to base materials making them suitable for challenging removal applications, substantially reducing grinding work, and increasing the opportunity to re-use lugs and attachments.
