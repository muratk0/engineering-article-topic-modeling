# Bringing plate rolling in-house cuts fabricator’s lead times, transforms production

[TARİH: 01.07.2025 The Fabricator]

Applications

The Faccin HAV-3145 plate roll enables JWT to fabricate a broader range of cylinders and thick-walled cones.

Situation

As a manufacturer of pressure vessels, tanks, piping systems, and general fabrications, Joe White Tank Co. Inc. in Fort Worth, Texas, was outsourcing its rolled cylinders. But remaining competitive with lead times became challenging. In the heart of oil and gas country, when the industry is booming, rolled cylinder lead times can extend to several weeks. Because cylinder sizes vary, stocking them is impractical.

"We found ourselves competing with the rest of the market for larger-scale refrigeration jobs, as we couldn’t expedite purchased cylinder deliveries with long lead times during oil booms," said Jeff Yurtin, vice president.

As customer demand grew, the company decided to gain greater control over production and lead times and bring cylinder fabrication in-house.

Resolution

JWT decided to integrate a plate rolling machine from Faccin into its production line, both enhancing its manufacturing capabilities and reducing lead times.

"Though I was not involved in the purchase of the plate roll, I come from the oil and gas fabrication industry, specifically the manufacturing of rolled cylinders," Yurtin explained. "Upon my arrival at JWT, I was relieved to see that our ownership had selected a Faccin plate roll, as they had the ideal machine for our requirements."

The implementation of the HAV-3145 series three-roll plate rolling machine marked a shift in JWT’s manufacturing process. The machine’s ability to handle plate widths up to 120 in. allowed the company to fabricate a wider range of cylinders and thick-walled cones with precision and consistency.

The plate roller includes an electronic parallelism system to help increase precision, a centralized lubrication system for reduced maintenance, and a braking system for optimal prebending, all managed by a Siemens numeric control unit.

The machine also features a minimal offset between the top and lower rolls to help improve prebending, a maximum opening of the lower rolls for heavy-thickness rolling, and a minimal side roll distance for optimal calibration after welding. It can also prebend both edges in two operations without removing the plate.

One of the most immediate impacts of the installation was the reduction in delivery times, as the machine allows JWT to roll cylinders as soon as its steel distributors deliver raw material. This has enabled the company to accelerate production schedules and fulfil customer orders more quickly.

"The payback for the machine can be measured in months, not years, thanks to the many intangibles not normally considered in an ROI calculation," Yurtin said. "So much more goes into calculating payback than just the amount of steel processed through the machine. Many of our break-in rush jobs come with premium pricing—opportunities we wouldn’t be able to accommodate without our own rolling capabilities. We’ve also found returns in using drop material more efficiently and in rolling our own backing bars and formed components."
