# Zorrotz Legazpi, SLL

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.zorrotz.com

Founded in 1953, Zorrotz is a manufacturer of slitter and shear knives. At EuroBLECH, it will present a new company brand image and new products to increase productivity and improve the working environment.

"We manufacture all kinds of knives and blade for the metal industry. We have decades of experience in various metalshearing and slitting applications. We source raw materials from the main European suppliers of tool steel," the Spanish company told ISMR.

“We manufacture complete slitter tooling sets (slitting knives; metal spacers; rubber-bonded spacers and all kinds of shear knives) as well as guillotine knives; flying shear knives; notchers and die knives. We can suggest the most appropriate solutions in terms of the right steel and hardness selections to ensure the best performance/price. We provide knives to different businesses including steel service centres, steel mills, tube and profile producers. We ship our products around the world," it continued.
