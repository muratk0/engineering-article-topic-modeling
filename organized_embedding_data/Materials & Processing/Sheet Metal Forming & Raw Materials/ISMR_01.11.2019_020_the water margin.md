# THE WATER MARGIN

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

We outline a selection of new and innovative waterjet cutting technologies for sheet metal manufacturers

With low water consumption and total flexibility, the waterjet offers an environmentally-friendly solution for profiling material. There are no chemically contaminated pieces to dispose of and it generates no noxious fumes during the cutting process and requires no cooling or lubricating oils. The introduction of 5-axis cutting heads has triggered massive growth in waterjet cutting. No longer confined to cutting out flat objects, waterjets are now able to produce continuous bevels, revolve around the workpiece and cut at angles from 0-90 degrees.

"Waterjet technology is currently one of the most advanced cutting methods in the market, thanks to its ability to cut practically any material. This ranges from soft substances (such as gel, food and sponge) to some of the hardest materials to cut in the industry (stainless steel, titanium, granite, marble and ceramic at up to 200mm thicknesses) which is almost impossible for many alternative cutting technologies on the market," said Bradley McBain, MD, MBA Engineering.

"More often than not, a waterjet machine will create a precise cut that requires no machining or additional finishing treatments to complete the job. In addition, there is no cutting dust to be cleaned or 'burnt' edges to polish. The technology has minimal kerfs – the amount of material along the vector line that is removed during the cutting process, which is usually 0.030" to 0.040" (0.762 to 1.016mm), resulting in less waste of product and materials. This is much more efficient as cutting guides can be closer together and more products can be produced per piece of material."

Below is a selection of the latest new and innovative waterjet technologies for sheet metal manufacturers.

## New technology developments

In thermal/mechanical separation technologies, ANT Applied New Technologies picked up a 2017 Blechexpo Award (presented by Blechnet and MM Maschinenmarkt) for its "Consus", an abrasive remixer that enables faster shaving and savings of the abrasive. To produce an air-free jet, the suspension is pressurised in a high-pressure vessel and forced out of the nozzle via a hose.

A new smaller-format waterjet cutting machine has been introduced by AXYZ Automation Group. Called the WARDJet A-0612, it forms part of the A-Series of machines and shares all the same design and performance characteristics of the larger X-Series, but at a lower cost.

"A key benefit of the A-6012 is that, whilst similarly configured small-format waterjet machines operate at pressures ranging from 30,000 to 45,000psi, the A-6012 will operate at 60,000psi. The ability to operate at this much higher pressure equates to the cutting power of full-size machines and radically reduces the amount of abrasive required for the cutting process, contributing to the machine's cost effectiveness," AXYZ told ISMR.

The company launched two new waterjet cutting systems in the UK last October. It followed the acquisition by the parent company in Canada of WARDJet, a leading US-based manufacturer of waterjet cutting systems. The new large-format X-Series and the smaller-format A-Series waterjets are suitable for a wide range of industries.

The X-Series incorporates a re-engineered lightweight and rigidly constructed modular gantry to hold multiple cutting heads and components and an optional integrated variable water level control system. This enables materials to be processed under water, thereby eliminating splashback and consequent loss of water and drastically reducing the level of noise during the cutting process.

Since then, the technique has been applied to specially developed precision machinery designed to manufacture micro-components. Water Jet Sweden has now broadened the possibilities and launched The FAWJ Micro Head Package for its ordinary machine models. It is designed for customers who wish to cut fine parts with narrow incisions and high precision, without the need for the most extreme micro part tolerances. Its "Micro" (NCM 10) is specially developed for the FAWJ-process and is used for the manufacturing of micro components. Micro waterjet cutting machine is a popular alternative to Micro-EDM and Laser.
