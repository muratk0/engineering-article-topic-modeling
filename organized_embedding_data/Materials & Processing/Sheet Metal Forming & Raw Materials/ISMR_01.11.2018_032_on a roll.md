# ON A ROLL

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH, established in 1945, founded the b+s group through the acquisition of well-known and firmly established companies, Schnutz GmbH and Delta Technik GmbH. Its entire product portfolio is based around slitting lines; stretch-bend-levelling lines; cut-to-length lines; Schnutz levelling machines; packaging lines; traverse winders; strip feeding systems; retrofit and modernisation of used and third-party lines plus Delta automation technology.

From the beginning, Burghardt + Schmidt (b+s) has focused on slitting lines and, over the years, has expanded its portfolio to slitting and levelling equipment for very thin and delicate metal strip (0.02 up to 2.0mm for stretch-bend-levelling and 0.02 up to 6.0mm for slitting and cut-to-length lines).

With 110 employees in the group and all products made in Germany (at three production sites), b+s was present at this year's Stanztec, Aluminium 2018, EuroBLECH and FABTECH exhibitions. ISMR spoke with Fabian Kunz, b+s's sales and marketing manager, to discuss the group's strategic and technical focus going forward...

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements?

FK: First of all, I am one of the second-generation family owners of the b+s group of companies. I am proud to confirm that we will be celebrating our 75th anniversary in 2020.

One of our successes this year has been to acquire a new company, DAA Delta Technik GmbH, based in Duisburg (Germany). We have been working with this automation company for over 22 years, but it has now become an in-house department as it was always one of our goals to have our own automation department to comply with Industry 4.0 manufacturing requirements. DAA DELTA Technik GmbH has been supplying electrical equipment for the b+s group's lines for over 22 years. DAA Delta Technik also works with our competitors and companies in other industries to provide all kinds of automation, so we can cover a very wide range of products now in the b+s group.

In 2021, Schnutz will celebrate its 175th anniversary. We integrated Schnutz into our company four years ago - it is Schnutz's fifth anniversary, as part of the b+s group, next year. We have also expanded our product portfolio with Schnutz products (for example sheet levellers, perforated levellers and strip feeding lines), Schnutz GmbH is the oldest leveller producer worldwide and supplied its first leveller in 1882, which is extraordinary. All three companies - Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH, Delta Technik GmbH and Schnutz GmbH, will keep their own branding, names and products under the b+s umbrella.

I am also proud to announce that we recently updated our vacuum tension stand for surface-critical materials (in slitting lines) so that the upper surface of the material is not damaged. We have just supplied the first ever, to our knowledge, vacuum tension stand to a customer in the United States. The equipment will be installed very shortly.

ISMR: What is your approach to Industry 4.0 as a company?

FK: We integrated Industry 4.0 systems with our ERP systems for our own production cycles around ten years ago. We are continuously improving and updating this to eliminate worker error and ensure more process monitoring on the shop floor, including assembly.

We are also currently investigating a new levelling system with Schnutz. In fact, it is currently being tested, and incorporates a new Industry 4.0 innovative feature. The line features a wide range of services for our customers. The market will be surprised by the innovative features, which we will announce very shortly.

ISMR: What are your views on the current business climate for sheet metal professionals?

FK: It has been an extraordinary year for us. We have received a lot of orders – new orders and new clients – as well as new approaches from existing customers. The industry climate has been improving for the last two years in Europe, North America and Asia. However, we do believe that this growth will calmly slow down. Our turnover has grown substantially this year and I expect this growth momentum to continue over the next few years. It has put pressure on us to deliver and we are busy right now.

For next year I see almost the same levels of high growth, but believe that we are going to face a calmer market. Nevertheless, we have projects in hand that will still be in operation by the end of next year.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers and how are you addressing these issues for them?

FK: Customers are focusing on high tensile materials, especially in the steel and aluminium industry, and advanced materials (particularly in the automotive industry). For levelling equipment, this is a challenge. We have done various tests with customers, and they tell us that only Schnutz levellers, amongst those tested, have successfully processed these materials, which is a great success for us. Customers cannot always process new materials on old equipment lines – there really is a need to invest in new equipment to get the best outcomes they need with these advanced materials.
