# Italian sheet metal subcontractor, Belia Srl, has taken delivery of a fourth X-Press Next 80/2000 4.0 press brake from Gasparini Srl.

[TARİH: 01.02.2021 ISMR]

For over 50 years, Italian sheet metal subcontractor, Belia Srl, has been offering solutions for laser cutting sheet metal and tube; bending; welding and other forms of metal processing. It also manufactures mobile tool-carrying workstations for industrial use. Using a network of external collaborators, Belia Srl offers mechanical processing and other ancillary processing, as well as metal treatment and assembly.

Belia's story began in 1967 with Giuseppe, a skilled Paduan craftsman with a passion for working iron. As a simple village blacksmith, he wanted to grow his company by offering its own agricultural products, producing wood saws with circular blades and log saws, as well as working on third-party orders for the production of various detailed designs.

When his sons Michele and Stefano joined the business, Belia began investing in plasma cutting technology, moving onto laser cutting and CNC bending technology. A culture of continuous investment marks Belia as a company which has seized the opportunities offered by Industry 4.0 in its own manufacturing, now firmly embedded in its operations. This Paduan manufacturer has been able to evolve, follow and anticipate changes in the sectors which it supplies and aims to ensure that its customers 'receive precise, punctual service, with a high standard of quality at a competitive price'.

Despite the uncertainties from the COVID-19 emergency, Belia continues to invest in new machinery to meet its growing needs.

Italian bending specialist, Gasparini Srl, started its relationship with Belia in 1986 when it supplied Belia's first 40 t/2000 press brake, followed by an X-Press 100/3000 press brake in 2016 and a new 165/3000 model in January 2020.

Gasparini itself has 45 years of experience in shearing and bending, with over 8,000 shears and press brakes installed worldwide. It bases its overall strategy on three pillars of performance, quality and service. Investing 10% of its turnover on innovation/R&D, it designs its products to meet specific customer challenges. It also cooperates with universities and research centres in the quest to improve customer productivity and efficiency.

"Despite the uncertainties from the COVID-19 emergency, Belia continues to invest in new machinery to meet its growing needs. For this reason, we were entrusted with a fourth X-Press Next 80/2000 4.0 press brake to deliver to the company," Gasparini told ISMR.

The 80-ton, two-metre press brake is equipped with a two-axis back gauge (X-R) with two straight and two shaped fingers for more stable support of the workpieces. The stops can be repositioned along the Z axis using the F function; the stops are pneumatically unlocked and moved over the bending line for easy manual movement. The 700mm open height and 400mm stroke enables even medium-sized boxes to be bent.

"The Delem DA-66T CNC enables office programming with Delem Profile Lite TL 2D offline software. The Tele-Link package also enables our technicians to monitor the machine's health and help with configuration.

On board the CNC is a suite of software tools, developed by us, which enable the interconnection and exchange of production data with the factory information system. A network of sensors detects and corrects process drifts, and then sends the information to the management system together with the times and jobs in progress," explained Gasparini.

"To manage even small batches efficiently, it is essential to reduce non-productive times. For this reason, we provided the Gasparini AirSlide top pneumatic clamping system without air hoses. AirSlide allows users to reposition tool holders without having to reconnect anything, simply by moving them with one finger. With the Syner-G remote control you can open and close the clamps directly from the workstation," it added.

On the front side of the press brake are two sliding front supports with tool drawer. Inside the machine is an ECO energy saving package: a vector inverter connected to the main motor which continuously regulates voltage and frequency to regulate the correct speed and force for work in progress at all times. The result is a reduction in energy consumption and an increase in performance, with less oil stress and a quieter machine.

Gasparini supplied the new 80-ton, two-metre press brake to Belia Srl in September 2020.
