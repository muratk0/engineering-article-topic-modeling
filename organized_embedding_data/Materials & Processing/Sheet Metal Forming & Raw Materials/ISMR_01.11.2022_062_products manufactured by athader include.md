2022 has been a very busy year for ATHADER. It has exhibited at several key exhibitions in 2022, including Expo Acero in Mexico and EuroBLECH in Germany. ATHADER and The Bradbury Group team for coil processing, as well as Leveltek (with its stretch levelling system) and Samuel Nelson Technology with its push-pull pickling lines, presented their latest developments at EuroBLECH in Hanover (Germany) this October.

## A strong vision

ATHADER has established itself as a globally recognized manufacturer of custom-designed coil processing equipment. It has supplied more than 300 new coil-processing lines worldwide. Since March 2020, when the COVID-19 pandemic started, it remotely installed and commissioned eight coil-processing lines and projects worldwide. These included lines in Canada; Ecuador; Argentina; Chile; the United Kingdom and Australia.

"Our vision over the next few years is to continue growing with our customers. We are focusing our efforts on improvements relating to equipment process automation and the reduction of downtime and production costs. We are developing virtual vision models and software tools to increase and improve the productivity of our lines, in addition to the remote assistance functionality which we implemented years ago on all our lines," explained Harkaitz Luengo.

"With artificial intelligence (AI) research; remote diagnosis; data collection and implementation of our expertise, we intend to improve the maintenance and process of our installed lines worldwide by updating the software and related components," he continued.

Focusing on the cultural transformation of the company, and in a continuous process of change and innovation, ATHADER faces the challenge of technological advancement with the "virtualisation" of services, digitisation and growth in its products and services. Luengo is keen to point out that this must all be done within the framework of good practice regarding the environment, sustainability and "the values that define our company."
