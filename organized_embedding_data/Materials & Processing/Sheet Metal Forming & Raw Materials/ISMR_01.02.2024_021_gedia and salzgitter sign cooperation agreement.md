# GEDIA and Salzgitter sign cooperation agreement

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Automotive supplier GEDIA Automotive Group and Salzgitter Flachstahl GmbH recently signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU). GEDIA will be using CO₂-reduced steel from the SALCOS® route in manufacturing its products to make the production of body press parts and welding components more sustainable in the future.

Salzgitter AG is one of Germany's leading steel and technology groups. The Salzgitter Group is gradually converting its steel production to hydrogen-based processes by way of its SALCOS® - Salzgitter Low CO₂ Steelmaking transformation programme. The aim is to achieve virtually carbon-free production from 2033. To this end, the conventional blast furnace route is being replaced by production processes harnessing direct reduction and electric arc furnaces. Salzgitter Flachstahl is ready to supply its customers with CO₂-reduced steel through what is termed the Peine route, which entails crude steel being produced in electric arc furnaces and cast into slabs.

GEDIA is a longstanding partner of Salzgitter Flachstahl (SZFG). With its nine locations across the globe, the company is supplied by SZFG mainly with cold-rolled strip and galvanized steels for bodywork lightweight construction and chassis components.

25-year milestone for ISMR

2024 is a very special year for ISMR as it celebrates its 25th anniversary. It is such a pleasure and privilege to be part of such a vibrant and innovative industry, supporting and promoting its remarkable developments over the years. Acknowledged as the world's number one international sheet metal forming and fabrication journal, International Sheet Metal Review offers truly global and independent coverage.

International Sheet Metal Review magazine (ISMR) was launched in February 1999 by the Mack-Brooks Group, owner and organiser of the leading sheet metal technology show in the world, EuroBLECH. The aim for the magazine was clear: to produce the best-written, best-designed and most in-depth sheet metal magazine in the market, that was truly international. Just like the sheet metal business as well as fabrication and forming technologies, it would not be bound by national borders.

Published today by In2 Publishing Ltd, on behalf of Mack-Brooks Exhibitions (now RX), ISMR now reaches over 60,000 qualified registered readers worldwide through its print and digital issues. Over the years, it has generated various supporting titles such as the UK Sheet Metal Forming and Fabrication magazine and ISMR's Asian Supplement (published in Chinese). It has also released yearly 'Global Reports', chronicling its individual industry sector reports and updates over the year.
