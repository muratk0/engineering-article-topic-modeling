C11: THE BASICS OF ELECTROCOATING

B

Understanding Electrocoat Materials

Electrocoat is the process of using an electrical field to migrate charged colloidal particles onto an oppositely charged conductive electrode. It is highly efficient, and it has the ability to give uniform film thickness and to coat complex objects. Electrocoat systems are easy to automate and control, and the systems have operational robustness. This presentation will review the two types of electrocoating processes, anodic and cathodic as well as both epoxy and acrylic based paints and their usage.
