# Sheet metal forming simulation

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

TRIBOFORM ENGINEERING HAS unveiled its latest software version, TriboForm 3.1. This new software version offers important new enhancements and allows for even more realistic consideration of tribological effects for sheet metal forming simulation.

Accuracy in sheet metal forming simulation is increased when the constant coefficient of friction, which is commonly used in this type of simulation, is replaced with realistic and accurate friction and lubrication data.

The latest industrial trends and needs indicate that the integration of the friction and lubrication data into the sheet metal forming simulation significantly contributes to higher simulation accuracy.

"With TriboForm R3.1, users are able to import and use their own experimental friction data as well as sheet surface and tool surface measurement data. In addition, the direction-dependent friction can now be taken into account. This is important since the selected sliding direction over the sheet surface has direct influence on frictional behaviour. With TriboForm 3.1, users can efficiently and accurately carry out the simulation, visualisation and evaluation of friction and lubrication conditions," said the company.

Dr. Jan Harmen Wiebenga, General Manager, TriboForm Engineering, added: "The main challenge in developing software solutions in this field has been how to appropriately represent complex tribological phenomena through easy-to-use software. With the latest enhancements, TriboForm R3.1 is ultimately the most innovative software to deal with this issue. A more realistic consideration of tribological effects within TriboForm R3.1, in particular customised friction models and direction dependency, provides a new dimension in sheet metal forming simulation."

The new TriboForm R3.1 software version can be integrated into the latest AutoForm R8 software version. TriboForm offers software solutions for the simulation of tribology, friction and lubrication in metal forming processes. Since 2016, TriboForm has been part of the AutoForm Group.
