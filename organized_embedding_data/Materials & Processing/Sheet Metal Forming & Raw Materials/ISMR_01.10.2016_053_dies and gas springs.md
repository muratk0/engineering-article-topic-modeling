# Dies and gas springs

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

MEUSBURGER GEORG GmbH & Co KG, the German diemaking and gas spring specialist, will be present with numerous innovations at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (25-29 October 2016) Its stand highlights will include products to try out hands-on, a demonstration table for Meusburger technical tip video clips and the new wizard for gas springs to test. New innovations for die making-mainly different active parts, carbide blocks for eroding, customised cutting elements and new guiding element items – will be presented at EuroBLECH 2016 on stand M113 in hall 27.

True to the motto of EuroBLECH 2016, "The new generation of sheet metal working", Meusburger will also offer a gas springs wizard with which a die maker can quickly and easily find the required spring for a project from the extensive range on offer. By entering the initial force, spring stroke, maximum outside diameter and installation height, the appropriate gas spring can be selected. Additional time is saved through a direct interface for all common CAD systems. The wizard by Meusburger is also available in the CD catalogue and online shop, and can be tried out directly on-site at EuroBLECH 2016.

At the product demonstration table, application engineers will be available for questions, requests and expert discussion on surface finishing equipment. See www.meusburger.com■

## Cold rolled sections

THE HADLEY GROUP is one of the world's leading manufacturers of cold rolled sections and products.

"Our continued investment and the development of new roll forming technology has kept us at the forefront of new innovations for the past 50 years. Resulting from over 20 years of extensive R&D investment by the Hadley Group, our internationally patented UltraSTEEL® process has twice won the Queen's Award for Enterprise Innovation (2006 and 2014). As a major technical innovation, we have revolutionised and developed the use of any cold rolled metal section," it told ISMR.

Meet the Hadley Group on stand E70 in hall 17 at EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover (25-29 October 2016) or see www.hadleygroup.com
