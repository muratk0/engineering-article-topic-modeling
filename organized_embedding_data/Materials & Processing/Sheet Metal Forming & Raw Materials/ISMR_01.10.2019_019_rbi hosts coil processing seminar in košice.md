# RBI hosts coil processing seminar in Košice

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

RED BUD INDUSTRIES hosted a coil processing seminar at the DoubleTree Hotel, Košice, Slovak Republic, on 11 September 2019. Attendees learned about blanking, multi-blanking, levelling, slitting and EPS® (Eco Pickled Surface), as well as how to apply new technology, improve tolerances, produce optimum flatness in material and maximize overall efficiency.

The programme included a tour of Valcovňa profilov a.s., a toll processor located close to Košice. Attendees were able to view Valcovňa's 8mm x 1829mm Red Bud Industries Precision Blanking Line.

“The line incorporates a T-Series Stretcher Leveller, which equalises the trapped internal stress in material. The result is exceptionally flat parts that stay flat during subsequent processing," Red Bud Industries told ISMR. See www.redbudindustries.com/
