# Safran acquires 3DMF

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Safran Nacelles has completed the acquisition of 3D Metal Forming (3DMF), a company specialising in high-energy hydroforming (HEHF) of metal parts. This manufacturing process reinforces Safran Nacelles' capacity to develop and produce parts for nacelles and engines, including nozzles and air inlet lips. HEHF is also an enabling technology for manufacturing Monolithic Integrated Structures from thick plate.

Based in the Netherlands, 3DMF manufactures prototype and series parts mainly for the aerospace industry. The manufacturing process developed by the company enables it to produce a finished product which can be large and of various thicknesses. 3D Metal Forming is now a wholly owned subsidiary of Safran Nacelles.

Safran is an international group operating in the aviation (propulsion, equipment and interiors), defence and space markets. Safran Nacelles designs, integrates and provides support and after-sales service for aircraft nacelles.
