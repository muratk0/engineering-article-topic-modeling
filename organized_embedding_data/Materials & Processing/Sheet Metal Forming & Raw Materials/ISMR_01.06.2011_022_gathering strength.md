# GATHERING STRENGTH

[TARİH: 01.06.2011 ISMR]

Recent Marcegaglia investments include a new manufacturing plant in China.

ISMR SAYS

"Marcegaglia has started new processing lines for stainless steel coil, sheet and tube"

On 19 April 2011, Marcegaglia opened its first manufacturing plant in China dedicated to the production of stainless and carbon welded precision tubes. The new plant is based in Yangzhou, 280 km from Shanghai.

The plant features the latest stainless steel tube mills for added-value applications (process, automotive, decoration), but also three other product lines: heavy wall, high tolerance ERW tubes for precision mechanical applications; cold-drawn (drawn over the mandrel) tubes for applications in automotive, hydraulic and mechanical sectors and refrigeration tubes for use in applications such as condensers and evaporators for heating and cooling systems and household appliances.

“This investment was first planned less than three years ago,” explained Antonio Marcegaglia, CEO of the group. “It was eventually decided upon two and a half years ago, then construction started about one and a half years ago. Everything has proceeded very well with contained costs, highly professional subcontractors (including Chinese companies) and with maximum support from the Government.”

The Yangzhou area is currently in its first development phase, including 153,000 square metres of covered production area equipped with European manufacturing systems, plus 4,200 square metres dedicated to office buildings. At full running capacity, the complex will employ approximately 500 people. The current value of the investment is estimated at 160 million euros.

## A ‘FLAT’ STRATEGY

Flat products remain at the core of Marcegaglia’s strategy. Aiming for the development of its own production value chain, Marcegaglia has started new processing lines for stainless steel coil and sheet at its headquarters in Gazoldo degli Ippoliti (Mantova, Italy). On two annealing and pickling lines and two cold rolling and skin pass lines, hot rolled steel (1D finish) from 2 to 6 mm thickness (500,000 tonnes annual capacity) and cold rolled steel (2B finish, 250,000 tonnes annual capacity) are being processed.

“This new product range strengthens Marcegaglia’s role as a global partner for manufacturers who engineer solutions in the building and construction sectors, in all process sectors including food and drink as well as in the automotive sector and for mechanical applications. In the stainless steel welded tube sector, Marcegaglia currently has a leading position and expects a global output of over 200,000 tonnes in 2011,” the company told ISMR.

Within the carbon steel flat sector, the Ravenna plant (Marcegaglia’s largest processing complex) has also recently been upgraded thus doubling the Group’s global capacities to 2.5 million tonnes per year of cold rolled products and 2 million tonnes per year of galvanised steel.

Pre-painted coil and sheet capacity was also doubled thanks to recent investments, and is now at 500,000 tonnes per year with more than 20 organic painting cycles and the capability for double-sided painting. The production is mostly dedicated to applications including building and construction, packaging and white goods. ■
