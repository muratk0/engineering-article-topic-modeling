# Material cost estimation

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GMBH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, now offers its EasyBlank software in the Cloud. This new EasyBlank Cloud Service is free of charge and is a pure web-based application which can be accessed directly from the browser, without having to install any special software. The results are provided in the browser and are available to the user in IGS CAD format, accompanied by a detailed report in Excel format.

"EasyBlank Cloud Service targets the needs of small and mediumsized enterprises in sheet metal stamping. The EasyBlank software is specially designed for sheet metal part designers, tool and die designers and material cost estimators. Using our simulation technology, the software rapidly determines the developed blank outline from CAD part geometry. EasyBlank includes several types of nesting layouts and enables users to identify the optimal one for their needs," explained the Austrian software specialist.

The material database, which includes all of the important parameters for the most commonly used materials in stamping, is already incorporated in EasyBlank. EasyBlank also enables users to assess product feasibility. Early feasibility assessment eliminates costly, late changes to part design and thereby enables the early optimisation of material costs. The analysis results are summarised in an Excel-based report which contains detailed information on blank outline, optimal nesting layout, material consumption and costs, thinning distribution of the part, feasibility plot and the associated FLD diagram. EasyBlank enables the automatic creation of such reports, which facilitate internal communication between different departments as well as between OEMs and suppliers.

"For even more accurate results regarding blank shape determination and material cost estimation, AutoForm-StampingAdviserplus takes into account forming conditions i.e. restraining forces and material properties in more detail, which leads to more reliable feasibility results. It also enables better addendum determination and offers more nesting options, which enables more accurate material cost estimation," added the manufacturer. See www.autoform.com ■
