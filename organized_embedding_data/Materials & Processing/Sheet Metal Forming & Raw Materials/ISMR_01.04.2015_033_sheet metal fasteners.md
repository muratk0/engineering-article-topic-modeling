# Sheet metal fasteners

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

TR FASTENINGS continues to strengthen its position as one of Europe’s leading suppliers in the sheet metal sector with the introduction of a range of high-strength studs for thin sheets. These new products have enlarged heads for strength, but can be used in materials as thin as 1.0mm. TR Fastenings has also expanded its range of hardened stainless steel standard flush head studs and they are now available in two thread diameters, M2.5 and M8.

These newly available sizes of studs have been introduced in response to customer demand and are used widely by sheet metal subcontractors for a variety of applications. Studs are available in a number of types, materials and finishes.

Across the whole self-clinch stud range sizes are ex-stock in diameters from M2-M10 and lengths from 5mm-50mm. These products form part of TR Fastenings’ extensive portfolio of sheet metal products, which include the genuine Hank rivet bush; blind rivets; blind rivet nuts; K-Series nuts; cage nuts; weld studs & nuts; swage nuts and the Euro rivet bush.
