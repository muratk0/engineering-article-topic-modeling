# Meusburger Georg GmbH & Co KG

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.meusburger.com

Meusburger is a market specialist in high-precision standard parts. It has over 55 years of experience in working with steel. Its product portfolio ranges from high-precision standard parts and selected products for workshop equipment to hot runner systems and hot control systems as well as systems in knowledge management and ERP. It has become a global partner to manufacturers. Its standard parts provide the basis for making dies, moulds, jigs and fixtures.

The company's roots go back to 1964 when Georg Meusburger established a one-man business specialising in tooling and mould-making.

Meusberger expanded its workshop equipment product range with innovations in almost all product groups in Autumn 2021 (particularly assembly equipment, consumables and EDM accessories).

"The metal sheet/plate that we use is heat-treated for stress relief and provides reduced plate warping during machining. Less allowance is needed for subsequent heat treatment and more precise components are the result," it explained.
