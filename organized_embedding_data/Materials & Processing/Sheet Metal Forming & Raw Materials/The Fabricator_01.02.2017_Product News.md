System offers speed change point of 0 in. on press brakes

Lazer Safe offers Iris, a combination guarding and image processing system developed for press brake applications. RapidBend Ultimate technology enables tools to close at high speed until the pinch point, regardless of the machine’s stopping performance. This reduces the speed change point to 0 in. to eliminate slow travel and help improve cycle times.

This speed change works with flat sheets and also box profile parts. When bending box shapes, the system compares the flange dimension in the CNC bend program and optically verifies the actual flange height during fast closing. The system then automatically blanks the forward sensing zone without the need for stopping and requires only a single pedal press per cycle.

When a new bend program is started, the CNC can automatically initiate the system to perform a tool scan. The optical system scans the punch to detect the profile and size of the tool, then automatically aligns the protective zone relative to the tool tip.

System combines communication, power functions for one-cable automation

With the newly introduced EtherCAT P technology, Beckhoff unites EtherCAT communication and power functions in a standard, four-wire Ethernet cable. Bending line laser, optical foot switch options offered for press brake

Bystronic now offers two new options for its compact, portable Xpert 40 press brake: an optional bending line laser that allows parts to be bent without a backgauge, and an optical foot switch.

It is not always possible to position bent parts on the press brake using the backgauges—for example, conical parts with shapes that do not provide a stop position. By projecting the optional bending line laser directly onto the surface of the part, the operator can position and subsequently bend the part without using the backgauge.

The optical foot switch allows operators to trigger the stroke of the press brake through a foot movement.
