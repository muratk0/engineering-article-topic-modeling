# Protection from steel corrosion

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

China Merchants Cruise Shipbuilding (CMCS) is one of the leading manufacturers of expedition ships, floating platforms and floating cranes. To date, the company had outsourced corrosion protection for the steel plates and beams required for its products. With the expansion of its business activities into passenger and cruise ships, the company had to abandon this outsourcing policy. The construction of a mid-sized passenger ship alone requires 25,000 metric tons of steel.

For this reason, the company looked for a partner on the design and installation of a preservation line. After extensive analysis of the market, the Chinese shipbuilder chose Rösler (whose headquarters are in Untermerzbach, Germany) as its supplier for this crucial investment.

“Fully automatic interlinked steel preservation offers competitive advantages The 180m-long preservation solution was specially engineered to meet the requirements of CMCS. It includes a plate section (the so-called main line) and a steel beam section, from which the shotblasted beams are fed into the main line. This preservation line features two roller conveyor shot-blast machines, equipped with Gamma 400G and Rutten 400 HD turbines, a straightening system for the beams and up to 12,000mm long plates. It also includes a fully automatic coating system including a paint supply unit and a thermal post-combustion system. All these line components are digitally connected and integrated into the production control system of the shipyard," explained Rösler.

The intelligent control system allows fully automatic processing of the steel components from the loading to the unloading zone at a speed of five metres per minute.■
