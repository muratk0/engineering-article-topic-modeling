# Emberson welcomed to the fold by Prince Manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Prince Manufacturing, a U.S. contract manufacturing company with sites in North Carolina, Indiana and Mexico, has announced the appointment of Bill Emberson as its VP of Sales and Marketing. He will spearhead strategic initiatives in his new role to drive growth, enhance market presence and strengthen client relationships. Before joining Prince Manufacturing, Emberson held key leadership positions in the chemical, environmental and textile sectors.

Founded in 1965, Prince Manufacturing is a contract manufacturer that aims to eliminate waste through fabrication; forming; assembly and finishing of metal products. Its expertise includes sheet metal forming; metal fabrication; metal stamping; powder coating, e-coating and more, serving diverse industries.■
