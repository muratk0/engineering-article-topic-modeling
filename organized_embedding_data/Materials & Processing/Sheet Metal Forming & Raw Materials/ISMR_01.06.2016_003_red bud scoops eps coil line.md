# Red Bud scoops EPS coil line

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

US MANUFACTURER Red Bud Industries recently received an order for an EPS® coil line from TYASA in Orizaba, Veracruz (Mexico). The new line can descale hot-rolled strip of up to 0.075" (1.9mm) thick and 72" (1829mm) wide and can handle coil weights of up to 73,000lbs.

EPS (Eco Pickled Surface) is an “acid-free" replacement for traditional acid pickling that is environmentally friendly and produces a scale-free, clean, rustresistant surface without the use of hazardous chemicals associated with pickling steel. EPS eliminates harmful chemicals that create safety and environmental risks.

"With EPS, the surface roughness of the strip is far more uniform when comparing it to acid pickled material. This surface is ideal for paint adhesion and appearance. Also, no protective oil coating is needed after EPS processing, making it perfect for post processing operations," explained Red Bud Industries.

TYASA has also invested in a new Castrip thin strip casting facility. Red Bud Industries configured and designed the EPS Line to process this thin strip.

"Once installed, this investment will make TYASA the first 100% Mexican steel producer to operate the EPS technology," added Red Bud Industries. “The line consists of a coil stage and load system; uncoiler; peeler breaker; entry crop shear; stitcher; two EPS cells; electrostatic oiler; exit crop shear; tension stand; recoiler and exit coil removal station."■
