## DALLAN SpA www.dallan.com

SINCE 1978, Dallan has designed and manufactured coil-fed production systems: punching, laser, rollforming and packaging. DALCOS is the Dallan division which specialises in punching and laser cutting systems.

"This year, at EuroBLECH, you will see the new DALCOS LXN in action (the fibre laser cutting system for coil and standard sheets). Its high resolution vision system boosts machine performance. The LXN can handle products of up to 14 metres in length," explained DALLAN.

## FLADDER DANMARK A/S www.fladder.com

THE DANISH deburring specialist will be highlighting the FLADDER® 200/GYRO, the next generation of the FLADDER® AUT, in Hanover this year.

"A new vacuum unit, which increases the vacuum effect by 30%, ensures safe fixation of workpieces. The central gearhead is equipped with conical spindles, as standard. This enables the use of the larger brush (dia. 400mm), which increases the oscillation of the gearhead for a 40% greater deburring effect. A simplified control panel, with touch-screen, makes operation of the machine easier," said FLADDER.

## II-VI INCORPORATED (HIGHYAG) www.ii-vi.com

THE II-VI Incorporated booth at EuroBLECH will house the business divisions II-VI HIGHYAG; II-VI Infrared; II-VI Photop; II-VI Laser Enterprise and II-VI SUWTECH to showcase a broad portfolio of products for laser material processing.

"One highlight will be the II-VI HIGHYAG laser cutting head, BIMO-FSC. This processing head can cut various sheet thicknesses, fully controlled by the cutting machine, without manual intervention. In combination with the BIMO-FSC's rapid focus movement on the workpiece, this guarantees efficient flat sheet cutting and high productivity in manufacturing," it explained.

The latest laser welding heads, laser light cables, laser modules and laser optics (including fused silica optics) will also be showcased. II-VI, with its global manufacturing footprint, can provide these optics in different configurations, including aspherical lenses, spherical lenses and debris shields.

## KASTO MASCHINENBAU GmbH & Co. KG www.kasto.com

AT EUROBLECH 2016, KASTO will be presenting its new KASTOecostore tower storage system. It is suitable for storing pallets and flat goods of various sizes. Benefits of the automated and standardised system include its low space requirement, high storage density, flexible clamping and fastening and short access times.

"The standard version of the KASTOecostore accommodates materials with widths of up to 1,524mm and lengths of up to 4,000mm. The system height, up to eight metres, can be chosen as required. Users can specify up to three different loading heights of between 50 and 950mm. The lifting gear drive is equipped with modern frequency converters and the motor is stationary to allow precise, dynamic loading and unloading. The KASTOecostore comes as standard with the KASTO SmartControl machine control system, which has an intuitive user interface with touch-screen for easy operation," said KASTO.

## KARL HEESEMANN MASCHINENFABRIK GmbH & Co. KG www.heesemann.com

KARL HEESEMANN Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG will present a new generation of deburring and edge-rounding machines at EuroBLECH 2016. For more than 80 years, Heesemann has been inventing, developing, producing and selling sanding machines all over the world.

"This new machine series offers a broad and specific range of technologies not yet found in any other machine types. The new Heesemann series is able to replace more than one machine at a time," the manufacturer told ISMR.

## PIVATIC OY

www.pivatic.com

PIVATIC'S PUNCHING and cutting centre for coil is a tool to produce punched blanks, in any size and quantity, straight from the same coil. Parts are produced, as required, in batches and as kits. Material utilisation can be maximised by nesting parts on wide strip.

"Since shears are used, both for cutting to length and cutting to width, the edge quality is better than when nibbled. The double tool punch feature of PivaPunch enables flexible punching with indexing tool stations on both sides of the strip for excellent cycle times. Single pick-up, double- and multidecoilers are available for coil handling. The punching station is equipped with a CNC coil punch and press tool capability, as required. Finished products are stacked automatically on pallets to multiple addresses," explained the Finnish manufacturer.

## SALVAGNINI ITALIA SpA www.salvagninigroup.com

SALVAGNINI DESIGNS, builds, sells and services flexible machines and systems for processing sheet metal. Its diversified range of machines for numerous different application sectors currently comprises: P1, P2Lean and P4 panel benders; S4 integrated punch-shearing systems; S4+P4 complete panel production FMS lines; L3 and L5 fibre laser cutting machines; SL4 integrated punching fibre laser cutting system; B3 press-brakes, ROBOformER robotic bending cells; automated pack or tray store-towers and integrated systems for factory logistics. Proprietary software packages, with user-friendly graphical interfaces to program, control and manage the systems, complete these solutions.

The Salvagnini P4 Panel Bender is designed to evolve and meet the demands of ever-changing manufacturing trends as well as easy integration with automatic handling devices and to be ready for Industry 4.0.

"An important feature of the latest generation of Salvagnini panel benders is the new adaptive MAC 2.0 technology, which enables the machine to detect any material variations in masked time, and, if values are outside the tolerance range, to automatically compensate for them by adjusting the blade movements. As a result, part quality remains consistent, even with variations in material, resulting in zero waste and optimised production times for maximum productivity," it explained.

## KMT WATERJET SYSTEMS www.kmt-waterjet.com

AT EUROBLECH 2016, KMT Waterjet Systems will be presenting its latest development in high-pressure pumps for waterjet cutting. With the new STREAMLINE PRO-III model series, the company makes waterjet cutting in the ultra-high-pressure range of up to 6200bar a great deal more efficient. KMT has achieved this by making significant improvements in the pressure intensifier, especially with its new SUPRALIFE high-pressure seal (for which it specifies a guaranteed service life of 500 hours).

With the 93kW pump (125hp) from the PRO-III model series, KMT can now offer an efficient combination of motor power and water pressure, for very high cutting speeds.

Flexibility and efficiency of its abrasive waterjets at the EuroBLECH exhibition this year in Hanover, Germany. The company will be running live cutting demonstrations on its OMAX 60120 and MAXIEM®1515 JetMachining® Centers. Visitors to the OMAX stand will have the opportunity to speak with abrasive waterjet experts to discuss table size offerings and accessories as well as how waterjet machining can enhance metalworking productivity.

"On the 60120 machine, a Tilt-A-Jet cutting head will highlight how taper-free cutting can be achieved without slowing down cutting speed, ideal for work requiring tight tolerances and quick turn-around times. The MAXIEM 1515 will be fitted with an A-Jet cutting head, proficient at cutting beveled edges, angled sides and creating complex 3D shapes. The waterjets in both the OMAX and MAXIEM lines are capable of machining virtually any material, including stainless steel; carbon steel; aluminium; titanium; alloys; precious metals; copper and even carbon fibre and glass, with no heat-affected zone and no need for tool changes," explained the manufacturer.

Energy-efficient direct drive pumps will power both machines at the show; a 50HP EnduroMAX pump on the 60120 and a 40HP pump on the 1515. The direct drive pump design delivers greater power to the nozzle than intensifier-type pumps of the same input motor size, resulting in faster cutting speeds.
