# In Part One of this article, we highlight a selection of some of the latest global tube and pipe forming innovations, including cutting and bending

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

ubes and pipes are some of the most versatile products and design elements in general. Whether in the energy sector; mechanical; power; automotive engineering; chemical; petrochemical; construction or other areas, tubes and tube designs in different shapes, sizes and weights can be found everywhere. Steel tubes and pipes cover a particularly broad spectrum, as these products are conquering new applications partly due to the innovative strength of manufacturers. However, thanks to new and further developments, they are also maintaining a strong position in traditional applications.

In the energy sector, pipes have become indispensable for the transportation of oil and gas, while also playing a major role as design elements. In fact, most structural foundations of offshore wind turbines consist of pipe elements. Pipes also fulfil important functions in the offshore production of oil and gas.

"Other growth areas that will be supported by research and development projects over the next few years will focus on the customised optimisation of pipe and tube properties. Take, for example, new developments for automotive engineering. In non-motor applications, tubes with variable wall thicknesses can be used to support load-optimised lightweight construction," commented Messe Düsseldorf, which ran the Wire & Tube exhibition in Germany last year.

## Forming operations

Tube and pipe manufacturers face growing production challenges from increased competition, rising material costs and thinner profit margins. Demand continues to rise for low weight and durable tubes and pipes. Seamless tubes and pipes with multiple bends are also needed in sectors such as energy, healthcare and transportation. Tube and pipe processing operations often take place in sheet-metal job shops.

Tubes are measured by their outer diameter and are usually smaller and less rigid than pipes. Bending involves metal forming processes used to permanently form pipe or tubing. The objective is to obtain a smooth bend without flattening the tube or pipe. Bending processes include form-bound and freeform, as well as heat-supported and cold forming. Factors such as material wall thickness, tooling and lubricants used all affect the bending process.

Generally, round stock is used in tube bending. However, square and rectangular tubes and pipes may also be bent to meet job specifications.

Form-bound bending, such as press bending or rotary draw bending, are used to form the workpiece into the shape of a die. Straight tube stock can be formed using a bending machine to create a variety of single or multiple bends and to shape the piece into the desired form. These processes can be used to form complex shapes out of different types of ductile metal tubing. Freeform-bending processes, such as three-roll-push bending, shape the workpiece kinematically so that the bending contour is not dependent upon the tool geometry.

Tube-end forming machines use hydraulic or mechanical presses. Often these presses use a repeated hammering motion to shape the tube. Because tube end forming is a shaping process and requires no cutting, there is no wasted metal at the end of the process. In addition, the tube end-forming process can provide improved tube strength, better connectivity between components and improved sealing capabilities.

## Tube and pipe processing

AICON 3D Systems, part of Hexagon since 2016, offers TubeInspect, an update of its turnkey tube and wire inspection solution which is compatible with Industry 4.0. Powered by the BendingStudio XT software platform, a TubeInspect system can become the central hub for controlling and analysing the entire tube production process.

The TubeInspect range is available in four system configurations, the industrial-sized P16.2 model and the smaller P8.2 (both of which are available in a high-end HRC – highresolution camera – version). The TubeInspect P8.2 models are suited for tubes of less than 1000 millimetres long from end-to-end and with a maximum diameter of 125mm. TubeInspect P16.2 models can measure tubes less than up to seven metres long and with a maximum diameter of 200mm.

The platform also delivers full automation capabilities, with high-resolution models ready for complete integration as the quality assurance step within a larger robotic tube production cell. There is also an option to measure end holders and fittings with CAD-Adaptors through image analysis, rather than with mechanical adaptors.

Alma's AlmaCAM 3D/Tube software is dedicated to the programming of all 3D cutting machines and robots. The software pilots all tube and profile cutting installations: 3-, 4- or 5-axis machines with one or more mandrels and robotised cells for tube and profile cutting. It covers all programming process steps: CAD import or modelling of tubes and profiles, management of manufacturing orders, automatic nesting of tubes or profiles plus creation and automatic scheduling of trajectories with generation of ISO codes.

Akyapak Machinery offers profile bending and tube-cutting systems, as well as drillingcutting machines. Its portfolio includes plate/ profile rolling and tube-bending machines. Its ABM range of tube and pipe bending machines includes the ABM-E25 all-electric system.

Portuguese manufacturer, AMOB, offers a comprehensive range of tube-bending machines. Its right and left fully electric CNC tube-bender eMOB series processes tube from 6mm up to 52mm OD and has multi-stack capability. Machines are divided into two different concepts; eMOB 2Bend series, which changes bending direction using a rotative head, and the eMOB LR Series which positions automatically and in cycle in a right and left bending configuration. The eMOB series fully electric tube bending machines can be integrated into fully automatic cells, with AMOB 3D software.

The AMOB team developed a fully automated cell based on the eMOB 100 model that bends tubes of up to Ø100mm with integrated high-precision magnetic welding detection, automatic tube feeding, robot unloading and even automatic palletising. The eMOB Super Heavy-Duty Series fully electric tube-bending machines can bend tubes of up to 225mm in diameter.

Autopulit showcased its "centre-less' machine for grinding and polishing round tube and bar at the EuroBLECH 2022 exhibition in Hanover, Germany. It has three working units as well as loading/unloading tables. Innovations included an HMI with touch-screen interface and gull wing doors for easy front and rear access.

At EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, Germany, BLM Group showcased its LT8.20 Lasertube system with a 3D laser-cutting head for standard and special section tubes from 12-240mm in diameter.

"With the LT8.20, productivity for large and small batches is guaranteed because production changeover is automatic and a new batch can be started in minutes," it told ISMR. “It can process a range of materials and thicknesses with closed or open profiles, for 2D or 3D laser cutting and regardless of the number and shape of geometries. It can also be equipped with special additional features called Active Tools for even better and faster results, regardless of operator experience."

Artube is BLM's CAD/CAM software for programming Lasertube systems to allow operators to draw, manipulate and design single parts or complex tubular assemblies of any section. Protube is the BLM Group production MES, a single interface for management of part programs for the main BLM Group machines.

BLM Group USA also recently announced its new automated tube storage and retrieval solution that fully integrates with the company's tube-laser systems, using special cassettes that can be loaded directly inside the machine.

BLM Group also offers its ELECT40 all-electric tube bender with an elongated, compact head "capable of executing shapes previously not possible with single-direction bending machines," it told ISMR. The new bending head is reduced in height by 35% and has a 31% longer bending head, increasing the distance between the bending tool and the machine body.

Other BLM models include the E-Turn for in-process right-handed and left-handed bending, with fixed or variable radius, in single or multiple radius mode for tube, pipe and profile bending. The E-Turn has different sizes for tubes of diameters of up to 50.8mm.

Bölük Makine's BPK hydraulic pipe and profile bending machine offers a range of different sizes and options.

Bystronic's new ByTube Star 130 offers laser power of up to 4 kilowatts and a fully automatic set-up with open profiles and ellipses for more flexibility and higher quality in production. Displayed at EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover, it has a wide range of applications for all metallic materials in sizes from 10 to 130mm and raw material lengths of up to 8.5m.

“The automated system reduces manual intervention to a minimum, making it particularly easy to get started in tube processing," explained the Swiss sheet-metal specialist.

Additional options include Laserscan, realtime compensation of pipe bending to improve cutting precision, and Quick Cut greater speed for better performance (thanks to an additional linear axis). Automatic weld seam detection enables the weld seam to be automatically oriented to the desired position. With the ByVision Tube user interface, Bystronic puts the control of all functions related to the laser cutting of tubes on a touchscreen.

Bystronic's FL 170 3D fibre laser cutting system has a wide range of applications for the cutting of steel, stainless steel, aluminium and non-ferrous metals. The cutting spectrum can be extended to open sections, such as beams, angles and channels. Its FL 300 CO₂ laser cutting system offers minimal scoring. The FL 300 laser tube cutting machine can be used for a wide range of diameters. Its ability to process raw tubes of up to 12.5m and cut parts of up to 12.2m makes it a versatile steel cutting tool. It has a linear loading capacity of 60kg/m, which makes it possible to efficiently cut thick materials.

CAMU Srl has been a specialist manufacturer of coil processing lines and sheetmetal coil levelling since 1980. CAMU lines are used both in the important steel-work service centres to cut coils and in sectors, such as automotive, for the construction of appliances as well as modern tube & pipe and profiling centres for open profiles.

The Digital Mini Ring Roller from CBC Srl performs changeable radii on small-sized metal pipes, sections and flat bars. The PCB controls ring rolling times and running directions. The machine can be used vertically and horizontally and is supplied with the three rollers to ring roll sections and flat bars. It is suitable for the hydraulic, lighting, shipbuilding, oilhydraulic, steel manufacture, ornamental and goldsmithing sectors.
