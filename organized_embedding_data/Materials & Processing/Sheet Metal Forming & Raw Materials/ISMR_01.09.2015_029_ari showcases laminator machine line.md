# ARI showcases laminator machine line

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

ARI METAL (www.arimetal.it), which has developed a modular "Aluminium Profile System®", will be exhibiting at Coiltech, the international exhibition for coil winding, in Pordenone (23-24 September 2015) on booth P9, Hall 7. Coiltech specialises in materials and machinery for the production and maintenance of electric motors; generators; transformers and power distribution; industrial transformers and windings in general.

At the exhibition, ARI Metal will present its new production line for machines producing magnetic laminations for stators and rotors. The new line includes automatic machines for cleating and welding stators of up to 1000mm in diameter and 1000mm in height; automatic machines to arrange rotor packs at the die-casting process; a sheet metal automatic dosing unit; a rotor stack height check system which can automatically correct punching machines and a laser welding plant.

The systems are designed and manufactured in accordance with a customer’s specific requirements, catering for different sizes and geometries. See www.arimetal.it
