“Are you looking for innovative and efficient roll forming, cut-to-length or slitting lines? If so, our sales team will be on stand D156 at EuroBLECH 2024 to welcome you," commented STAM SpA.

STAM is a manufacturer of processing machines for coil sheets and roll forming lines. Founded in 1963 in Ponzano Veneto (Treviso, Italy), the company has production units in Spain and Italy.

"Our lines are equipped with the latest technologies. These include 3D roll forming; flexible roll forming; cassette roll forming or traditional roll forming as well as punching, forming and bending; laser-plasma cutting and welding; stacking and packing. Our cutting lines include slitting; cut-to-length; blanking and multi-blanking as well as coil processing lines," it told ISMR.

"We believe that the keys to our success come from the integration of different technologies, process automation, close attention to customer specifications and, last but not least, our support service," it concluded.
