# ROLL FORMING FUNCTIONALITY

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

At the BIEMH 2010 exhibition in Spain, data M Sheet Metal Solutions presented the new ‘Remesh’ function in its COPRA FEA RF software - the 'Finite Element Simulation of the Roll Forming Process'.

"In recent years, it has become apparent that the use of Finite Element Simulations will be more and more important in the roll forming industry. Due to the growing popularity of lightweight construction, sheet thickness is constantly being reduced to compensate for the loss in stability; more and higher strength materials are being used. More and more profiles are also being rolled with pre-punched material, where the quality of the holes in the final section in terms of correct position and shape are also an important issue," data M told ISMR.

"The only way to avoid expensive and time wasting experiments is to virtually simulate the process with FEA."

The company has focused upon making the roll design faster whilst removing the modification of the FEA mesh when using pre-punched material, with COPRA FEA RF. The target is to significantly reduce the time required to prepare an FEA simulation. The mesh can be re-defined as needed during the simulation.
