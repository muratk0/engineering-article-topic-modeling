# Stalatube ramps up in Poland

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

STALATUBE'S NEW PRODUCTION facility, manufacturing stainless steel tubes in special dimensions and further processed stainless steel products, is moving into ramp-up phase. This investment in Lódź, Poland, with its new production line in Finland, aims to enhance the company's competitiveness through a capacity increase and significantly shortened delivery times as well as closer reach, especially to central European markets. The total investment sum is twenty million euros.

The investments increase Stalatube's production of tailored sized l-beams and further processed products, such as stainless steel hollow sections, both of which are intended for demanding structural end-uses. Production of pre-fabricated components for the growing transportation sector will also be expanded.

"Our strategy is to be closer to our clients and very flexible in meeting our customer needs. Tailoring products to meet the exact end-user requirements translates into savings in material and transportation costs and cuts down the time needed for further processing by the end client. This shows as savings on the bottom line," said Stalatube's CEO, Jukka Nummi.

"Stainless steel is practically maintenancefree. Its competitive lifecycle costs open up significant opportunities for our stainless steel product in areas such as transportation, oil and gas, construction and other demanding end uses," added Sami Packalén, SVP, Marketing and Sales.

Stalatube is, in its sector, the world's third largest manufacturer of stainless steel square and rectangular tubes and a leading manufacturer of ferritic and Duplex stainless steel square and rectangular tubes.
