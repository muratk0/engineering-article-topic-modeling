# First cut on Chinese CTL line

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

A NEW CUT-TO-LENGTH line (CTL) for aluminium, installed by Fagor Arrasate for one of the largest Chinese companies in the production of aluminium, has successfully cut its first coil. This is the second installation of this type supplied by Fagor Arrasate at one of the biggest primary aluminium and alumina (aluminium oxide) producers of the Asian giant. This new cut-to-length line for the aerospace sector opens a wide range of opportunities for the company in this important sector.

"At the milestone of the first cut, the new CTL line only required final adjustments to start production, which is destined for the automotive, aeronautical and shipbuilding industries. The CTL has a line width of 2650mm and roto-oscillating shears to make curved cuts," the company told /SMR This now makes five aluminium lines ordered from Fagor Arrasate in China. It has sold equipment widely in the buoyant aluminium market in China, where it has a manufacturing and assembly plant in Kunshan, on the outskirts of Shanghai, as well as offices in Shanghai and Beijing.

## BLECH India returns to Mumbai

THE SIXTH EDITION of BLECH India, a key exhibition for sheet metal working, will take place from 25-27 April 2019, at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai. "Building on the success of five previous editions, BLECH India provides a networking platform for industry experts to establish successful business relations with customers, agents and partners in India. The exhibition takes place in the newly built Hall 4 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre," the event organiser, Inter Ads-Brooks Exhibitions (India) Pvt. Ltd, told /SMR.

The fifth edition of BLECH India took place from 27-29 April 2017 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai. A highly qualified audience of 4,568 attended the show. This was an increase of over 20%, compared to the previous edition of BLECH India, held in 2015. Around 151 exhibitors from ten countries helped to make the event an international networking experience. Exhibitors were from Germany, Italy, the UK, Turkey, China and Spain.
