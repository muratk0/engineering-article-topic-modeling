## Security Door Production-Material selection

Punching: According to the positioning of the mold and the backing, the punching machine (as shown in Figure 3) can accurately complete the punching angle, punching the hand hole, punching the doorbell hole, punching the keyhole, punching the cat's eye hole, and ensuring the matching size of the product.

5. Bending: We can use the advanced press brake machine equipped with a bending tool (as shown in Figure 4), repeat the bending of the door panel and the door frame several times to meet the production requirements.

6. Electric welding: Our workers need to weld small pieces in the door panel (as shown in Figure 5). The gantry is required to weld the ribs, the concrete cover and the four frames of the door frame together.

7. Degreasing, descaling and phosphating: We usually use degreasing, descaling and phosphating lines without sewage to degrease, derust and phosphate (as shown in Figure 6).

8. Glue welding: The middle part of the front and rear door panels is filled with honeycomb paper or fireproof cotton for welding. The door panel is fixed by a hot press (as shown in Figure 7), so that the facade is flat and invariant.

9. Spray, transfer, water mill: the special treatment process for the door panel is to ensure the beauty of the facade color and wood grain. In the painting workshop, the facade should be painted, washed, dried, wood grain thermal transfer and oiled and dried.

10. Oven cover: In order to solve the problem of fading of the steel door by ultraviolet light, it is necessary to increase the anti-fading ability of the surface.
