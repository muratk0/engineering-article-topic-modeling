# Keeping a firm grip

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

THERE IS A WIDE variety of applications within sheet metal industries and various challenges arise when supplying into each of these. As a major supplier to the sheet metal, electronics and HVAC sectors, TR Fastenings aims to ensure that it has robust business processes and technical expertise in place for large OEMs and SMEs.

Its extensive sheet metal product portfolio includes a range of fasteners for pressing, rivetting and welding, including own-branded Hank® Self Clinch Fasteners and Hank® Rivet Bushes. It also includes blind rivets; blind rivet nuts; weld nuts; weld studs; K-Series nuts and various related products, from spring steel fasteners; masking caps and plugs; DIN & ISO standard bolts and screws to enclosure hardware.

“Selecting and installing the correct fasteners for sheet metal is one of many application challenges. Faced with increasing environmental responsibilities and unprecedented technological change, manufacturers and contractors are challenging for higher quality and smarter, application-based sheet metal fastening solutions. Flexibility in service and stock availability, including high volumes, are also key requirements,” Steve Wallis, sales office manager at TR Fastenings, told ISMR.

With a global network of manufacturing and distribution sites aimed at delivering flexible lead times and quick turnaround, TR produces approximately eight billion parts per annum across eight manufacturing sites, spanning two continents, and has over 100,000 stocked items available for prompt delivery.

Steve Wallis added: “Adapting to change in the current technologically fast-paced environment is essential and, with quality application engineers based at most TR sites, rapid communication enables faster advice and solutions. Our global technical team is able to respond to requirements for smaller, lighter and more flexible product solutions, assist in troubleshooting and in the specifications for bespoke orders.”
