# AUSTRALIAN SEMINAR FOR RED BUD

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

Red Bud Industries, Red Bud, Illinois, USA, hosted a coil processing seminar on 15 March 2011 at the Stamford Plaza Sydney Airport Hotel in Mascot, NSW, Australia. Attendees learned about blanking, multi-blanking, levelling, slitting, and a new process (EPS for hot roll black material) along with learning how to apply new technology, improve tolerances, produce optimum flatness in material and maximise overall efficiency.

Featured speakers included Dean Linders, Vice President of Marketing and Sales for Red Bud Industries, Tom Hazen, formerly of Herr-Voss and now a consultant to the metals processing industry, Rick Thomas, Director of EPS Projects at The Material Works, and Ray Fausz, Director of International Marketing and Sales for Red Bud Industries.

For more information, see www.redbudindustries.com
