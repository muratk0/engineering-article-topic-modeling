# COMPLETE COIL LINES

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

MILLUTENSIL EXHIBITED its coil lines during the recent LAMIERA exhibition in Bologna, Italy. Since 1955, it has manufactured complete coil lines (press feeding lines, cut-to-length lines and slitting lines). Products include single- and double head decoilers, straighteners, flatteners, shears, de-stackers and stacker units as well as lines for fine and thick blanking.

The company outlined an example of a coil line for a customer at Lamiera, underlining the importance of continuous working.

“For example, for a customer who works strip width max. 400mm, thickness min. 4mm – max. 7mm – resistance 700 N/mm2 the line would consist of a non-driven 6000kg decoiler with motor for strip feed-in and rewind; with translation on slides for strip-end alignment in respect to the press axis; a movable 6-ton coil car; outside diameter max. 1900mm; upper driven snub arm; lateral driven guide and lower non-driven snub arm,” it told ISMR.

“A pre- straightening unit helps the strip entering and the strip-head straightening before welding. A joining unit with hydraulic shear would be included for strip welding, with semiautomatic positioning. A straightener with 2 pinch rolls (180mm diameter) and 13 straightening rolls (80mm diameter) would also be included.” www.millutensil.com
