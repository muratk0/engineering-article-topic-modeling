# Non-destructive material testing

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

CRITICAL MATERIAL defects can be prevented by non-destructive material testing, for example by Computed Tomography (CT). VisiConsult, as a provider of X-ray solutions, has already installed several fully automated in-line CT systems at a leading automotive supplier. One typical inspection is necessary for short circuit rings.

VisiConsult's cooperative partner in electromobility is breuckmann eMobility GmbH, a subsidiary of Breuckmann GmbH & Co. KG. which offers casted rotors for electric motors. Within the rotor, a laminated core is positioned between the short circuit rings which are connected to each other by aluminium. While casting the short circuit rings, the greatest challenge is that the aluminium needs to be sufficiently fluid when arriving at the end side of the laminated core. If this does not happen, pores come up and, depending upon size, they are either tolerable or lead to discard of the rotors. While small pores are harmless discontinuities in the material, large pores reduce the strength of the short circuit ring. For it to be sorted out accordingly, an inspection by Computed Tomography and Automatic Defect Recognition (ADR) is needed.

VisiConsult has integrated many CT solutions into inline productions, so that customers benefit from 100% inspection without wasting time. Directly after the casting, the rotors can be moved to inspection because the conveyer drives through a cooling tunnel. With this, constant temperature condition during inspection are achieved. Subsequently, a robot positions two rotors in the X-ray cabin in which two X-ray tubes are located. As a result, one X-ray cone can detect in its centre two shorting rings that lie side by side. That way, four short circuit rings are scanned at once. VisiConsult was able to install the complete test cell as a black box. The operator doesn't need to have any knowledge of X-ray or the process.

"With loading and unloading, the inline solution is currently available in a cycle time of 42 seconds per rotor. In addition to the time advantage, the double X-ray source equipment also serves a more accurate scan and ensures greater system availability through redundancy," added the manufacturer.

"According to the customer's request, the subsequent analysis can be carried out according to the P202 standard by VW or other car manufacturers. With this system, it is possible to perform 3D analyses, which means that it can be inspected 100% nondestructively. If a pore size goes beyond that of a discontinuity, it is classified as a material defect and categorises a component as not OK (n.i.O.), which leads to the sorting out of it. The rotor cast, according to the Zero Porosity Rotor Standard, has no porosity even in the upper end ring and is thus okay."
