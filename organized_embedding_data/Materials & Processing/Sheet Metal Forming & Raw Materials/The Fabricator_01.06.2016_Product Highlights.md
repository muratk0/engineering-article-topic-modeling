Depressed-center grinding wheels introduced

Saint-Gobain Abrasives has introduced Norton

QUANTUM3

™ (NQ3) depressedcenter grinding wheels. They feature a proprietary grain, as well as a bond system that combines fillers and bonding agents, to help increase metal removal and wheel life.

According to the company, tests showed that the grinding wheel removed almost twice the amount of carbon steel in 5-min. intervals than competitive wheels.

Constructed using an iron-, sulfur-, and chlorine-free resin technology, the wheel offers uniform abrasive distribution. This bond is designed to retain the grains long enough during and after grain fractures.

Switchable magnetic ground clamps designed to stay cooler

Magswitch Technology has introduced a new line of magnetic ground clamps. The second generation of the product series features a new fin design with increased surface area to help lower temperature by about 27 percent. It offers 300, 600, and 800 amps for a strong hold in the shear or vertical position.

The operator places the clamp on a clean surface, turns it on, and instantly grounds on flat or round surfaces. The clamps can attach to surfaces where traditional clamps cannot, the company states, eliminating the need for welding tabs. Complete on/off technology allows the grounding clamp to stay clean.

Press brake tooling enables up to 40-degree bending with no marking

Mate offers CleanBend™ forming technology press brake tooling dies. Rotating supports within the tooling allow bending of delicate and standard materials up to 40 degrees with little or no marking.

The dies are designed for bending short flanges; bending a flange to a diagonal sharp or across existing holes; and bending delicate and highly polished materials, including painted, coated, and plastic-covered.

Motor-powered caster moves loads up to 6,000 lbs.

Caster Concepts Inc. has introduced what it says is the first motorized industrial caster. The Drive Caster™ integrates an electric motor with an industrial caster to create a single unit that can be added to carts and racks.

With a mere flip of a switch, operators can engage the ¼- or -HP motor to move loads up to 6,000 lbs. at up to 5 MPH. Adding a second motorized caster doubles the load capacity that can be moved and enables tank-style steering, with each caster pushing in opposite directions to turn.

Heavy-duty plate saws cut aluminum automatically

Behringer Saws Inc. has introduced the LPS series heavy-duty, electronically controlled, vertical production plate saws for automated cutting of aluminum, low-alloy, and high-tensile-strength solid materials.

The saws feature a main frame with a feeding mechanism that provides automatic positioning of plates or blocks via two parallel servo-driven units that feed the material to the blade. The feeding mechanism and roller conveyors are mounted on sectional inner and outer tables with hardened material supports at the cutting zone. Paired, adjustable hydraulic clamps provide secure workholding during sawing, and a laser measurement system on the saw frame detects workpiece ends for automated operation. The saws’ infinitely variable downfeed rate monitors cutting force, which is displayed on the operator control panel.
