# AN EYE ON EFFICIENCY

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

FACCIN Group's CEO, Andrea Ceretti, sat down with ISMR to highlight some of its latest new projects, achievements, product launches and plans.

ISMR SAYS: "This year, FACCIN is launching a new roller design and a new line of premium angle rolls."

Founded in the 1960s, the FACCIN Group is a global metal forming machine manufacturer, headquartered in Visano, Italy. It also has two branches (one in the U.S. and the other in Germany) as well as sales offices, dealers and business partners around the world. It is a specialist in the design, manufacture and sale of plate bending rolls, profile bending machines, dished head machinery and special machines (including ship frame bending rolls and presses and plate straightening machines).

The Group is a powerful combination of well-recognised brands in plate rolling, profile bending, dished-head and special machine manufacturing (Faccin, Boldrini and Roundo). These brands are all united under one roof; each brand specialises in a certain type of product, all united by a common feature—tailored and energy-efficient product-service systems. Its machine tools are used to manufacture a range of varied products, including pressure vessels, heat exchangers, road/railway tanks, gas pipelines and wind towers.

## Forging ahead

Despite the challenges and hurdles created by the COVID-19 pandemic, FACCIN has scored some important successes this year. "During the first half of this year, we have been able to finalise some very important projects, due to our longstanding relationship with our network of distributors. I am pleased to say that we have beaten our nearest competition in many markets, particularly in Asia, by delivering heavy-duty machines that meet requirements for high performance and tight tolerances," Andrea Ceretti, CEO of FACCIN Group, told ISMR.

"The determination and dedication of our people are the main reasons our group has become one of the most reliable metal forming machine providers, committed to the growth of each of our clients," he added.

Supply chain and delivery disruption has been a key feature of the pandemic, but Ceretti has insulated the FACCIN Group from these negative effects.

"Over the last few decades, many industries have organised their supplies through outsourcing. However, the pandemic has shown us that outsourcing can be risky in a volatile market and put a spotlight on local manufacturing. At FACCIN, we have vertical integration. This starts with plate profiling, fabrication and machining; we can perform turning, milling, boring and polishing operations in-house. We also have full control of quality inspection, pre-shipment commissioning, testing and crating for shipment. This has been crucial for our resilience throughout the pandemic. It also enabled us to keep working and acquire new orders, as we have better control over delivery and scheduling," he explained.

The FACCIN Group launched a new logo and website, as well as new interactive and personalised experiences for customers, during the pandemic. Ceretti believes that sheet metal professionals, as in any other sector, must keep investing in digital tools to boost interactions with existing and new customers, and to protect their employees by providing the tools to increase remote working.

## Growth in focus

Ceretti believes that global markets will continue to increase demand for metal formed products but, due to the current geopolitical situation, volatility will be high and will push metal fabricators to be as flexible and reactive as possible.

"We believe the U.S. and Asia-Pacific regions will drive demand for metal forming machines, with countries such as India becoming significant players as their infrastructure develops," he told ISMR.

"Our strategic and technical focus is to keep growing as a group, using the synergies provided by each brand, not only technically but also commercially, to offer customers an even wider range of high-quality options. We will keep working with our distribution network (companies with whom we have been collaborating for many years now) and consolidate our position in new regions through new powerful distributors with very professional technicians who can support our machines. High quality and great service are the keys to our success in markets like India," he explained.

One of its webinars in April this year, watched by hundreds of attendees, was focused on challenges in the wind energy industry and, particularly, understanding how industry will cope with its monumental growth and the hype created around this renewable source of energy for the future.

Ceretti also aims to continue implementing new digital tools and sales channels to help customers interact easily with FACCIN and obtain information on its products and services. In particular, FACCIN has invested in building closer relationships with its customers by launching the interactive "FACCIN Group World" project. This project takes the FACCIN factory directly to its customers through interactive presentations and virtual visits.

## Trends and challenges

"Fluctuations in the market and the struggle to find skilled workers are driving fabricators to replace their old equipment with high-quality equipment, principally looking for accuracy and automation to increase their production output, which is precisely what we offer," said Ceretti.

"As steel prices go up and margins grow smaller, accuracy is the answer. We design our machines to offer a return of investment centred on the accuracy of the forming process and avoidance of metal waste, always integrating powerful upfront technology that increases the output cycle and return on investment," he explained.

FACCIN has been ISO-9001 certified for more than ten years and implements the Lean philosophy in all production phases of its rolling machines.

Ceretti highlighted several ongoing trends in the machine tool industry such as enhanced automation using advanced measurement systems; connectivity and remote diagnostics; Industry 4.0; reduction of environmental impact and increased machinery safety.

"Our Group has always been at the vanguard, in terms of precision and automation, of the forming process. FACCIN was the first company to offer a CNC for plate rolling machines, back in 1984. We will keep focusing on providing metal forming companies with equipment of the maximum quality, powerful and modern but, above all, accurate and highly automatic," he told ISMR.

## New product launches

This year, the FACCIN Group is releasing a new roller design and a new line of premium angle rolls. At the FABTECH exhibition in Chicago (booth #A4912), EMO Milano (Hall 5, stands C28 and D13) and Blechexpo in Stuttgart (Hall 1, stand 1110), it planned to launch its 4HEP-E model, a new four-roll direct electric-drive roller. It features increased rolling speed of up to 10 m/minute, energy efficiency improvement of 35%, enhanced braking torque and silent operation via screw pumps. The new design implements a clean front profile as the drop-end system has been integrated and the pressure switches have been replaced by electronic sensors displayed on the pulpit.

"In addition, the Roundo R6X is a new line of premium angle rolls that has been re-designed internally based on strict criteria also used on the automotive world, where a reduction of part codes ensures simplified service to customers with shorter downtimes. The new Roundo line also includes the use of top brand components, oil-bathed gear based transmission and the advanced Roundo Wcnc4 as the Control CNC," Ceretti told ISMR.

FACCIN's Eye Bend System enables automatic verification and control of the radius of curvature. Recently, the company completed a sturdy electro-welded three-roll plate rolling machine with features such as variable axis configuration; maximum pipe length of 40 feet; anti-deflection systems for top and bottom rolls; three-roll drive and automatic pipe ejection.

## A networked future

Industry 4.0 has become more and more prevalent in the industry and is crucial for any manufacturer who wants to bring high-quality machines, advanced connectivity, analytics and people together to change day-to-day operations.

"As a group, we see Industry 4.0 as a way of capitalising on the technology that we already provide. Our machines are Industry 4.0-ready, featuring smart packages that offer, for example, system diagnosis; teleservice; management control; drawing import; rolling and production batch statistics and flexible network solutions. They are designed to help today's manufacturers face tomorrow's challenges," added Ceretti.

"We believe in deep collaboration with our customers and suppliers so we can keep innovating and stay up-to-date with the latest production needs. We believe in our people so that we can give great service and, last but not least, we believe the success of our clients is our success," concluded Ceretti.
