SIMUFACT LAUNCHED THREE product novelties at EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover. Besides the releases of Simufact Forming for forming simulation and Simufact Welding for welding simulation, Simufact also announced the launch of Simufact Additive, new software for the simulation of metal additive manufacturing processes.

With its solutions, Simufact covers a wide range of forming and joining processes in sheet metal forming. Forming's application module for the simulation of sheet metal forming processes enables a simulative description of drawing processes and covers further processes such as stretch forming; stamping; punching/bending; fine blanking; embossing; roll forming; draw bending; molding; pressing; flow forming; pulling and more.

"The simulation software helps the user to identify and avoid problems in the run-up to the manufacturing process. The software delivers, among others, reliable information on the wall thickness distribution and the edge curvature and gives users the opportunity to make predictions on potential crack regions. Simufact Forming supports manufacturing engineers to determine the required forming forces. The software also predicts elastic springback and distortion due to residual stresses during forming and trimming," explained the manufacturer.
