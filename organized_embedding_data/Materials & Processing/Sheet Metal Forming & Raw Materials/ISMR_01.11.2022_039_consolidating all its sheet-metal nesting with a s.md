# Consolidating all its sheet-metal nesting with a single software system has reaped continuous rewards for BIZ Engineering in the UK.

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

BIZ Engineering Ltd, based in Enfield (UK), has been a user of JETCAM Expert nesting software for almost thirty years. Today, the software currently drives its NUKON, TRUMPF and AXYZ CNC machines.

Originally founded in 1816, BIZ (Builders Iron & Zincwork) has evolved into three distinct businesses, all of which rely on punch, laser and routing capabilities to manufacture a diverse range of products (from conveyor systems to go-karts). It is part of the familyowned and operated BIZ Group Holdings, also comprising BIZ Karts and BIZ Power Tools.

BIZ has a comprehensive knowledge of the sheet-metal industry, with capabilities including design; CNC laser cutting; CNC punching; CNC bending; MIG, TIG and arc welding; CNC routing; bead blasting and inspection. Over 200 years, it has built up a wealth of experience in complex sheet-metal fabrications and welded assemblies with a wide range of applications from the construction sector through to finelimit electrical enclosures.

Single Component Automatic Processing (SCAP) also allows entire directories of CAD files to be automatically tooled.

## Free-form nesting

In 2017, BIZ decided to automate the nesting process, adding JETCAM's free-form automatic nesting (FFAN) to its licenses.

"The amount of time that free-form nesting saved us was crazy. One of my colleagues programs the nests for go-karts and it's literally halved his time spent on JETCAM and with better results," explained Peter Smith.

In 2019, the company purchased a NUKON REX 315 4kW fibre laser-cutting system. Although the software was supplied with a competing system, BIZ specified that it wanted to continue using JETCAM to drive all its machines, so it contacted JETCAM to develop a postprocessor.

Peter Smith noted: “The company gave us a demo of the other nesting software, and one of our engineers had used it in the past. However, we didn't want to operate two

systems in-house as JETCAM has always run any machine without issue. Also, we know how helpful the company has been in the past in getting new machines up and running, so we were confident that it could do the same with this new fibre laser."

A postprocessor was written, based on information and sample NC code, was supplied. It was installed and operational within a day.

“The new post worked immediately, and minor features were added as new requirements were discovered," confirmed Peter Smith.

In 2020, BIZ purchased a TRUMPF TruLaser 1030 L88 laser-cutting system, again contacting JETCAM for a postprocessor. A new post was developed the same month, with Peter Smith citing that there were no issues after installation. Additional time was spent to optimise the runtime estimation, which had knock-on benefits.

“More accurate runtime estimations mean that not only are our quotes much more accurate, but our lead times have also improved as we can schedule jobs much more effectively," explained BIZ.

At the start of the COVID-19 pandemic, the AXIS router was also used to manufacture PPE products using new acrylic materials. BIZ contacted JETCAM, who assisted in ensuring that the system was configured correctly for quality cuts.

## Looking to the future

Peter Smith professes himself very satisfied with JETCAM's levels of support, "Compared to, for example, our MRP system we get very few issues with JETCAM, and any that we do are resolved quickly, often instantly, via remote connection," he said.

Consolidating all nesting with a single system also constantly delivers benefits.

"The upgrade to free-form nesting probably saved us half a day per week, and adding a post as opposed to implementing and learning other systems is a massive time and cost saving. For training alone, we would have needed 4-5 days per user," continued Smith.

"While we have seen many benefits over nearly 30 years of using JETCAM, free-form nesting delivered a significant improvement and the support to get new machines going has been superb. Going forward, our next step is to link JETCAM to our MRP system using our JOC Lite licenses to import orders for nesting, and to take advantage of other features in the software to further automate our manufacturing processes," he added.
