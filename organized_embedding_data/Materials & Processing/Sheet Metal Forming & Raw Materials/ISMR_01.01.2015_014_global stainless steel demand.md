## R&D stamps way to sales growth

UK METAL PRESSWORKERS, H T Brigham, predicts that product development will lead the way in 2015 to sales growth and customer satisfaction. Barry Smith, managing director of the metal presswork manufacturer, is predicting that 2015 will be a year of opportunity, but only for those manufacturers who can successfully combine their technical production capabilities with the capacity to offer intelligent design and product development.

“Manufacturing is, of course, a repetitive process where standard parts are made in high volume, but manufacturers looking for growth need points of differentiation in their markets and that starts with research, design and development of products. Volume manufacturing alone is too often a road to prices being driven down by overseas competition, whereas thoughtful proven designs are harder to imitate and therefore rightly attract a premium for their quality and superior performance credentials,” he said.

Smith believes that the automotive and alternative energy sectors are the markets best placed to experience high demand next year, as well as predicting significant opportunities through the trend of re-shoring business back to the UK.

“We now invest in research and development as a priority. Our customer relationships are focused on making things better instead of just making things. Our design engineers spend time with customers to understand the function of the required component and this enables them to focus on engineering cost savings and functional improvements into the finished product. It’s a far cry from the volume sales approach to working with customers, but a much more intelligent and valuable one for our customers,” said Smith.

One project, in which H T Brigham’s design team participated, ended with the successful re-design of a troublesome and costly cast component into a metal pressing. This resulted in a 70% unit cost reduction, reduced component weight and enhanced strength and durability. This re-engineered component assembly is now scheduled to be incorporated into the automotive OEM’s entire vehicle platform. See www.htbrigham.co.uk ■
