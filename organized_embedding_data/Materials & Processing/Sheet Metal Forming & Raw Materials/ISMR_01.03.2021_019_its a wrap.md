# It's a wrap!

[TARİH: 01.03.2021 ISMR]

Eco Wrap®, dubbed 'the world's first compostable industrial strength machinegrade stretch film' by Cortec, is a new packaging option for manufacturers and warehousing industries. The latest formula uses a certified compostable resin, plus a tackifier additive, to make an industrial-strength compostable stretch wrap that can be used on most standard automated stretch wrap equipment (such as pallets of goods for storage, inventory) for waste reduction. Most applications requiring three wraps of standard film can use two wraps of Eco Wrap® without sacrificing strength or protection. Eco Wrap® is commercially compostable (according to ASTM D6400). Eco Wrap® allows users to avoid tariffs, fines and tip fees in areas where polyethylene is prohibited or restricted. It is also shelf- and curb-stable and will retain its integrity until disposed of properly," added its manufacturer, Cortec.

There are many ways to make use of it as a "greener" packaging option for applications where a conventional stretch film is needed. These include corralling of goods for storage and shipment; pallet wrapping; packaging construction materials and transporting furniture. It works on most existing automated machines and is applied by adjusting the tension (typically increasing since Eco Wrap® is very elastic).
