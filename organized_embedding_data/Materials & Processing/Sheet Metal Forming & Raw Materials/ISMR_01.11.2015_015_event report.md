# EVENT REPORT

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

## Sheet metal, pipes and profiles

Blechexpo’s focus was squarely on sheet metal processing machines; cutting and forming technology; pipe and profile processing; joining and fastening solutions as well as semi-finished products made of sheet metal, pipe and profiles. Schweisstec covered the complementary areas of thermal cutting and processing; thermal and mechanical joining; fastening technologies and processes; automation for welding and cutting technology; applications; accessories and operating equipment.

“Presentations covering the issues of cutting and forming technology, pipe and profile processing, as well as tooling and surface finishing, were met with great interest by experts from all levels who are involved with sheet metal, pipe and profile processing,” the organiser told ISMR.

“Many returning and new exhibitors reported above average numbers of orders taken directly at the event with existing and new customers, so that more than just a few can look forward to full order books at the end of the year and for the new year as well.”

## New and upgraded technologies

Announcements of many new product launched and upgrades were made during Blechexpo – we highlight a selection of these below, in alphabetical order (see page 00). ISMR also conducted interviews during the show to unveil new industry and technology developments and trends, which can be found throughout this issue.

Swedish press specialist, AP&T, advised ISMR of new press developments and recent European press line deliveries (see page 99). ARKU showcased its new deburring machines for two-sided deburring and rounding – EdgeBreaker and EdgeRacer (see our article on page 116). AS Scholer + Bolte GmbH presented its new DUOFIX batterypowered stud welding unit, with double welding gun, for the simultaneous welding of two studs at the show. New welding products were also launched by Bokli, Bolzenschweisstechnik BSK and Bernd Siegmund GmbH. Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH showcased a new EP300 burr height measuring system for slitting processes. Swiss manufacturer, Bystronic, launched its ByStar Fiber laser cutting system with a short cutting bridge, newly developed control and access to the cutting area over the long side of the machine (see our interview on page 26). Its new Xact Smart press brake is available in several versions with press capacities of between 50 and 300 tons and with up to four axes on the back gauge. C. Hilzinger-Thum announced a new T7/SLF disk brush for the uniform edge rounding and deburring of the inner and outer contours of sheet metal parts.

The focus of the CLOOS booth at Blechexpo was on an automated robot system equipped with an automated torch changing system. The new fully automated current tip and gas nozzle changer, SpareMatic, enables non-stop welding with operative components and can be integrated into new and existing systems. The manufacturer also presented its new QINEO QINTron inverter welding machine for manual welding and the QIROX Laser Cell, a new product line of compact cells for automated laser cutting and welding. The turnkey systems consist of a laser welding head; safety equipment; operating terminal; positioner; QIROX QRH-280 robot; flat screen with HD camera for visualisation and a pre-assembled media room. The laser cells are equipped with a diode laser.

CoastOne Oy showcased its new Cone Cseries electric press brake for small parts bending. Using ball screw technology, new features include a bigger opening and longer stroke. A high performance slitting line, with integrated deburring, was also unveiled by Cobra Bandstahl (in cooperation with Leicht Stanzautomation) at Blechexpo.

Coherent displayed its HighLight FL-Fiber lasers, OEM fibre lasers that deliver 1-4kW of continuous-wave fibre-coupled output at 1μm wavelength. The lasers consist of individual laser modules, which contain pump diodes and a proprietary non-photodarkening active fibre. The diodes, fibres and optics are designed and manufactured in house.

Coherent has extended its integrated laser machine tool offering with the META platform featuring faster cutting speeds and better positioning repeatability. META offers an industrial-type controller and new software with multilingual software interface. It will be equipped with Coherent Laser sources for cutting metal and non-metals.

The HighLight 10000D is a high power, direct-diode laser system that produces 10kW of output, with free space beam delivery. This preserves the inherent brightness of the diode laser source and enables the use of an optical system with a large (275mm) working distance. The HighLight 10000D outputs a line beam, with a wide range of length options, ranging from 6mm to 36mm.

Data M Sheet Metal Solutions was present with a re-worked product portfolio and new versions of its COPRA® RF design and COPRA® FEA RF simulation software. For further details, please see our interview on page 86.

At this year’s Blechexpo, Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik GmbH & Co. KG displayed its BIMERIC SP intelligent servo production press system.

“A large number of components can be assembled into hybrid modules," explained Bernd Haussmann, Head of Technical Sales/Engineering, Bihler. “Processes such as stamping, bending, cutting and moulding remain under the press. Processes such as forming, tapping, screw fitting, welding or assembling can easily be outsourced from the press to the BIMERIC platform.”

As a result, the overall tool length under the press is greatly reduced and enables the use of a smaller press. Different processes can be quickly and easily integrated and extended on the BIMERIC SP.

Prima Industrie, launched its Laser Genius 2D fibre laser cutting machine, with linear drives, at Blechexpo. It also introduced a new generation of servoelectric punching technology in the punching unit of the new Combi Genius punching-laser cutting cell, with flexible turret concept. It also widened its press brake line with a new product, the eP2040, a rapid, nonhydraulic bending solution with a new servo-electric drive system and the new generation of FastBend panel benders. New software developments were also on display (see our interview on page 62).

RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH demonstrated its RAS ProfileCenter which automatically bends door frames or elevator parts in batch sizes as low as one. Rohrmann Schweisstechnik presented new TIG welding innovations such as new clear quartz glass nozzles with better weld puddle visibility and high heat resistance and new MegaFlo glass nozzles with weld zone gas protection.

Italian manufacturer, Salvagnini, showcased its new P42520 panel bender, a highly energyefficient machine, at Blechexpo. The bend height has been increased to 203.5mm for greater versatility and a wider field of application. The minimum panel size that can be produced with this panel bender has been reduced by 10%, dropping from 400 to 370mm. The maximum diagonal has also increased from 2,800 to 3,200mm to enable the feeding of panels up to 3,050mm wide – interesting for door manufacturing, air handling and sheet metal building cladding applications.

Italian specialist, Prima Industrie, launched its Laser Genius 2D fibre laser cutting machine, with linear drives, at Blechexpo.

Salvagnini’s B3 ATA press brake was fitted with an automatic tool-change system (see page 00), made even more efficient than the new dual-composition version. The latest version of its L5 laser cutting system now features a new “compass” with advanced kinematics and a cutting head with Dry Cooling functionality for, said the manufacturer, improved cutting precision and better reliability with lower consumption. In line with Industry 4.0 and smart factory philosophies, it also introduced Values, new cost analysis software that can be applied across all Salvagnini product lines.

“This software has been redesigned, optimised and developed further, extending its computational functions to all our technologies. In fact, Values no longer takes the type of machine and technology as its reference point, instead focusing on the part to be produced. This means that if the part is being punched, for example, on the S4, then laser finished with an L5 and then bent on a P4 panel bender, we can calculate and provide an accurate cost estimate for the part as it goes through all these processing stages involving our machines. In Values, the customer can also create additional stages or processes, such as painting, by exploiting the data that Values can provide,” explained Pierandrea Bello, Salvagnini’s product manager.
