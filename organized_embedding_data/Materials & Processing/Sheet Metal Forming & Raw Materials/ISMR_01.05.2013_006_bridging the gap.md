# BRIDGING THE GAP

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

ACROW BRIDGE, a leading international bridge engineering and supply company, has announced an agreement with Structal-Bridges, Canada’s largest steel bridge manufacturer. Under the agreement, the two companies will jointly market their complementary solutions in temporary and permanent modular steel bridging.

“We are excited about increasing our presence in North America and in other parts of the world through our partnership with Acrow Bridge and, together, raising the bar in our industry to the benefit of our customers. With our combined ability to deliver steel bridges with spans that can exceed 400 feet (122 metres) long and four lanes wide, we can now offer more choices to customers around the world,” Bill Killeen, President and CEO of Acrow Bridge, explained.

“Both companies’ modular bridging solutions are designed to roll into place without the need for large cranes. This enables us to install bridges in locations where limited space or heavy equipment has been a major obstacle to bridge-building.”

Acrow Bridge and Structal-Bridges will also leverage each other’s extensive engineering and fabrication capabilities to enhance customer service and response time. Acrow Bridge’s main fabrication plant is located in the US. Structal-Bridges operates manufacturing facilities in the US and Canada. Both companies have engineering, sales and service staff located in company offices and at customer sites across the US, Canada and around the world.
