# STILFOLD secures U.S. patent protection

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

STILFOLD, an innovator in sustainable design and manufacturing technology, has announced that its foundational patent family has been granted by the United States Patent and Trademark Office (USPTO).

"This milestone represents a significant advance for our origami-inspired manufacturing method, which radically improves material efficiency and reduces the environmental impact of industrial production," it said.

"The newly granted U.S. patent, covering our proprietary curve folding technology, enables the creation of complex geometrical structures from flat sheet metal with unprecedented precision and strength. This method minimises material usage by parts integration and, as a result, decreases production costs and significantly lowers the carbon footprint of manufacturing processes. This makes it a game-changer for industries ranging from automotive and aerospace to renewable energy, construction and consumer products," continued the company.

"We are thrilled to receive this patent recognition in the U.S.," outlined Jonas Nyvang, CEO and Co-Founder of STILFOLD. “Our technology is not just about creating stronger and lighter products; it's about redefining how we approach manufacturing in a way that benefits both businesses and the planet. This patent will protect our innovative process as we continue to expand our presence in the U.S. market and collaborate with industry leaders to create more sustainable solutions.“

With the U.S. patent secured, STILFOLD is positioned to accelerate its strategic initiatives, including partnerships with key players in the automotive, manufacturing and aerospace sectors. The company's approach offers an alternative to traditional manufacturing methods, reducing the need for expensive tooling and enabling more flexible, efficient production systems.■
