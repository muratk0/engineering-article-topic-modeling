# A precision slitting line with minimal burring delivered by B+S to NIE•MET in Germany enables tension-free custom cutting for aluminium coil widths of up to 2,100mm.

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

"Manfred J.C. Niemann Zentrale KG (NIE•MET) has added a new Burghardt + Schmidt wide-material slitting line to its machine fleet"

Since autumn 2018, a new production hall at Manfred J.C. Niemann Zentrale KG (NIE•MET) has been manufacturing special wide aluminium strip for industries such as automotive and aerospace.

Requirements are even more demanding now because of the increased use of modern materials.

High-strength aluminium – highly resilient and extraordinarily flexible – is unlocking new opportunities in industries such as automotive and aerospace, but it is extremely difficult to process.

Precision and cut quality matter if you want to avoid failed batch runs, especially in sheet processing and assembly. However, not all machinery introduced over the last few years is able to cut modern materials with enough precision due to customer requirements for thickness and width in the geometrics of the slit strip.

Manfred J.C. Niemann Zentrale KG (NIE•MET) added a new Burghardt + Schmidt wide-material slitting line to its machine fleet in late 2018. The new line is designed to process particularly wide aluminium coils of 800 to 2,100mm. And, using high-precision shears and a circumferential setting mechanism on the coiler, it can also set an outer diameter of 1,850mm.

## Order and set-up

Headquartered in Bremen, Germany, NIE•MET was founded in 1976 by entrepreneurs Manfred J.C. Niemann and Ingrid Niemann. In the space of a single generation, it has become one of Europe's largest and most successful service and wholesale suppliers for semi-finished products in NF metals.

The company has a nationwide network of service partners in Germany as well as representatives in Europe and overseas. Its portfolio covers aluminium, copper, brass and bronze. Its 60,000m² central warehouse in Bremen stocks over 12,000 tons of products. Its extensive plant and machinery fleet includes seven modern slitting lines, two cut-to-length lines, eight plate saws, six band saws and eleven rod saws. NIE•MET has a team of 250 highly qualified employees and offers customers comprehensive product and service quality from a single source.

The new production hall at Manfred J.C. Niemann Zentrale KG (NIE•MET) in Bremen, Germany, has been producing aluminium strip since autumn 2018.

"Our aluminium slit strips are 0.5 to 4.0mm thick and a minimum of 80mm wide. As a result, they are suitable for a very wide range of applications," said managing director, Jörn Niemann. "Depending upon the thickness of the material, the yield strength is 20 to 580 N/mm² and the tensile strength is 65 to 600 N/mm², so it is especially demanding having to cut them to custom sizes."

The company therefore started looking for a machine designed to process wide coils. Based on decades of positive experience, it opted for a wide-material slitting line from Burghardt + Schmidt. Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH was founded in 1945 and manufactures tailored machines and equipment for cutting and levelling metal strip. The company has experience in the thin strip sector and specialises mainly in slitting lines, stretch-bend-levelling lines, packaging lines, traversing spoolers, levellers, cut-to-length lines and coil processing lines.

Having won the order, the Burghardt + Schmidt team made a site visit to establish the ideal position for the line in the production hall. The two companies have been working together for years, so Burghardt + Schmidt experts have a track record of trust with NIE•MET, built upon previous projects.

"Because the site is so close to the Weser river, we had to make sure the foundations were not too deep, otherwise groundwater would get in," said Thomas Baral, Managing Director, Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH. The team also met NIE•MET's processing range specifications for the new precision slitting set-up, with coil widths of 800 to 2,100mm. Burghardt + Schmidt delivered the turnkey line in time in mid-2018.

## Catering for large coil diameters

The heart of the new line is the TWIN CNC slitting shear, with a pre-tensioned precision roller bearing and 'zero backlash' linear guide designed for accurate and, above all, stable positioning.

"To meet the sophisticated processing requirements and cut strips of the required width with minimal burring, the shears have to guarantee tension-free cutting every time,' confirmed Baral. "So, when producing the line, we paid particular attention to the blade shaft in terms of precision and calibration. The installed blades are 400mm in diameter and are assembled with rubber-bonded spacers.

This means that, in one pass at a working speed of 200 m/min. (depending upon the material strength and thickness), the line makes no more than 22 cuts for 21 cuts.'

The slitting shears feature a push-off plate to guarantee rapid retooling with the four-arm tool change system outside the line.

The decoiler and coiler feature auxiliary control panels to ensure that operators can keep an eye on the whole line.

"Retooling is often complicated, but the tool-changer makes it much faster and simpler," explained Niemann. "Having it in our new line gives us semi-automated and, therefore, faster retooling."

This innovation speeds up the workflow, significantly improving not only availability but also safety, because workers no longer have to manually position the tools in the line.

## User-friendly operation

For a perfect cut, parallel blade shafts are needed. This is made possible through active repositioning during the cutting process. A specially developed transfer table transports the cut strips. A deflector with roller bearings separates the strips. The recoiler is fitted with a quick-change fastener for easy mandrel changes. A circumferential setting mechanism on the recoiler boosts line efficiency and improves operator ergonomics.

"Our employees really appreciate the occupational safety improvements in terms of handling," added Niemann.

"The central main control panel and the latest software, which is tailored to customer requirements, guarantees intuitive handling with straightforward data entry. It also has a clear, well-structured display that shows faults, line speed and other information. The decoiler and coiler feature auxiliary control panels to ensure that operators can keep an eye on the whole line. This means that they can adjust and monitor all the key quality and safety parameters from different positions, ensuring smooth production despite the challenges of the material," explained Thomas Baral.

To allow in-process quality monitoring, cameras continuously check the upper and lower surface. This means that faults are easier to identify without stopping the line and taking random samples for assessment.

"We are delighted to have helped such a long-standing business partner meet its demanding quality requirements," concluded Baral. "We value NIE●MET's confidence in our lines and look forward to working with them on future projects."
