# Slitting line for roofing panels

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

RED BUD INDUSTRIES recently shipped a new light gauge slitting line to Roofing Industries, LTD in North Harbour, New Zealand (a manufacturer of metal roofing panels). The line is rated for material of 1mm thick by 1524mm wide. It has a coil capacity of up to 6,350kg.

The new line will provide Roofing Industries with a low passline, small footprint slitting line that eliminates the need for costly foundation work. The line consists of an entry coil lift; traversing decoiler; support table with pinch rolls; PVC applicator; crop shear; slitter head; ball scrap winders; thread system; recoiler with coil pusher and exit coil lift.

DR. ING. H.C.F. Porsche AG has exceeded its 2015 sales record. The Stuttgart-based sports car manufacturer delivered a total of 237,778 vehicles around the world, some 6 per cent more than its previous record. The key drivers of growth were Europe, the USA and China. The Macan and the new 718 Boxster had a particularly positive effect on the result. Excellent response to the new Panamera also provided an additional boost, it told ISMR.

After the success of the Porsche Experience Centre in Atlanta, Porsche reinforced its presence in the key American market by opening a second site in Los Angeles in November 2016. China is once again its strongest individual market, with a total of 65,246 vehicles delivered – an increase of 12 per cent. Porsche is responding to the high level of demand by further expanding its dealerships and it plans to open the Porsche Experience Centre in Shanghai in 2017.

Next is the launch of the completely re-developed second generation of the Panamera in the USA and China. The shooting-brake version – the Panamera Sport Turismo – will celebrate its world premiere in March at the Geneva Motor Show.
