# NEXT GENERATION LINES

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

Harkaitz Luengo, Managing Director of ATHADER, outlines his view of coil processing markets and latest trends to ISMR.

ATHADER, part of The Bradbury Group, is a leading European supplier of coil processing lines. It is based in the city of Renteria in one of the most highly industrialised areas of Spain, the Basque Country. Its product range includes automatic slitting lines, levelling and cut-to-length lines, combined lines (slitting and cut-to length) and multi-blanking lines, stacking and strapping lines; inspection lines and special lines. Its main activity is the design, manufacturing and installation of coil processing lines for carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium.

ISMR sat down with its managing director, Harkaitz Luengo, to discuss the company's latest coil processing projects and his views on the industry.

ISMR: Please give a brief background to ATHADER, including any recent successes and achievements?

HL: ATHADER has been a supplier of process line equipment for over 28 years. With its beginnings in the Spanish market, it has grown to have a true global market presence. We now have over 300 slitting and CTL (cut-to-length) line installations globally.

In 2012, ATHADER and The Bradbury Group became joint venture partners which drove further growth for ATHADER in manufacturing, engineering, sales and service (in addition to the use of Bradbury's e-Drive® levelling technology for flatness control and energy conservation). Today, we provide a full range of services such as complete turnkey processing line installations, upgrades and customer service. The main markets for ATHADER include the Americas, Europe and Australasia. Our plan is to expand into these markets with Push-Pull Pickling Technology together with the company, SAMUEL NELSON TECHNOLOGY.

ATHADER supplies cut-to-length (CTL) lines with stretch levellers which are manufactured by our strategic partner, LEVELTEK. Unlike competitors, we utilise a flying shear, dual zone stackers and automatic packing line which enables high-speed processing of the coils, avoiding bottlenecks and ensuring greater productivity.

Currently, we are manufacturing a hands-free operation slitting line with tooling robot and a recoiling line with an integrated fully automated robotised packaging system for a customer. Automation of the lines is designed to meet Level 2 case requirements, interface with the Plant MES/ERP system and tracking system from entry warehouse management system, over the lines and stock warehouse management system.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals?

HL: Our customers see a certain slow-down in demand. Brexit, trade negotiations between China and the U.S. and the tariffs that the U.S. government is introducing to European steel imports result in uncertainty in the European market. New investments are being studied carefully and, in some cases, postponed until a clearer market situation is perceived.

The automotive industry is having a difficult time due to the reduction of sales and an uncertain future as a result of changes brought about by electric vehicle technology that will constitute a revolution over the next few years. These will be a game-changer in the automotive business model and will come with new material requirements as well as car sharing, with consequent reduction of new car production and unpredictable consequences for the steel industry.

ISMR: Please explain your strategic and technical objectives?

HL: ATHADER is focusing on the automation of coil processing line operations and Industry-4.0 industry developments, since these are the main requirements we are seeing from our customers. This also includes integration of quality control systems, such as thickness or width measuring systems, and condition monitoring of electric motors and gearboxes.

A key strategy for us is customer service. The high level of automation and integration of different technologies makes it even more necessary to increase our number of customer service technicians and ensure that more software engineers can connect online, at any time, to our customers' equipment for troubleshooting as well as software upgrades and improvement updates.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers? How are you addressing these issues for them?

The necessity to reduce weight in the automotive industry has resulted in the increasing demand for UHTS (Ultra High Tensile Strength) materials.

HL: There are an increasing number of requests for Industry 4.0 applications - typically predictive maintenance, condition monitoring with smart sensors, health-status of the line and remote support. We study, with our customer, process times to improve production.

Replacing several coil processing lines with just one line results in a substantial reduction in the cost of production. It also means a smaller stock of coils as well as lower maintenance and operator costs.

ISMR: What is your strategic focus/ vision over the next few years?

HL: We are focusing our strategy on the integration of high added value technologies in coil processing lines, which will provide our customers with improvement in the quality of their products and the services that they supply. We are also developing new tools such as business software, 4.0 applications and production management software.

We currently integrate descaling, stretch levelling, automatic strapping systems and robots into our lines. We also carry out complete functional tests of coil processing lines to reduce the time required for commissioning.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in coil processing markets?

HL: The necessity to reduce weight in the automotive industry has resulted in the increasing demand for UHTS (Ultra High Tensile Strength) materials. We are manufacturing several lines with these requirements and have already replaced slitting heads and levellers on existing coilprocessing lines with new equipment adapted for these products with higher tensile and yield strength values.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

HL: Providing rapid and efficient aftersales service to our customers is our greatest challenge.

We also see a move to increasing automation in the equipment that customers purchase with requests for complex machine maintenance by customer personnel.

The ATHADER after-sales service department has grown considerably, as a result, to improve our technical assistance to customers.

ISMR: Are there any products/ technologies that you have released or are releasing this year that you would like to discuss?

HL: Service centres are our main customers. They are dealing with tighter and more demanding requirements, especially for levelling quality and surface finishing. ATHADER is integrating BRADBURY Hydraulic e-drive levellers, LEVELTEK stretch levellers, brushing systems, pickling and embossing units into its lines, as a result.

ISMR: Which exhibitions will you be attending this year?

HL: ATHADER exhibited at FABTECH 2019 in Chicago, USA, recently with other BRADBURY GROUP companies.

In 2020, we will participate EUROBLECH 2020 in Hannover (booth H12 in Hall 27) and we will also be attending FABTECH 2020 in Las Vegas as well as ALACERO in Latin America and METALEXPO in Russia with the BRADBURY GROUP.
