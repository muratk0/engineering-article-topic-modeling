# Plansee invests in new cold rolling mill

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

PLANSEE USES modern rolling mills to produce the world’s largest molybdenum, tungsten, tantalum and niobium sheets. New investments mean that customers will immediately benefit from cost savings, which Plansee will also be passing on in its webshop, Plansee Express.

The company operates the world’s largest hot rolling mill for refractory metals. With a massive rolling force of 50,000 kilonewtons, this produces sheets of up to 2000mm wide and several metres long. To produce larger sizes of thin and medium-thickness sheets and to ensure increased capacity, Plansee recently invested in an ultra-modern cold rolling mill – the second largest single investment in the company’s history. It is used to produce molybdenum sheets of up to 1000mm in width.

This increased capacity means that Plansee is capable of manufacturing large quantities and formats very quickly. The improved quality characteristics and even greater availability of products are important benefits for Plansee’s customers, it told ISMR.

Plansee also sells rods, wire and ribbon made from molybdenum, tungsten, tantalum and niobium at shop.plansee.com. Online customers will also find TIG welding electrodes and spare parts for high-temperature furnaces.

Aside from semi-finished products, Plansee also develops and manufactures custom components from refractory metals. See www.plansee.com
