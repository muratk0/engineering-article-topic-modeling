# A STAMP IN TIME

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

The ability to cut the lead time of a product means being much more competitive on the market in terms of price, as Italian die designer Attrezzeria Universal explains…

ISMR SAYS: “Attrezzeria Universal’s speciality is the design and manufacture of dies for the cold stamping of all types of sheet metal”

Founded by Armando Caramaschi in 1972, Attrezzeria Universal has continued to build on its experience in designing and manufacturing sheet-metal stamping dies. Over the years, the Italian company has acquired machinery which has enabled it to extend its range of products and services. Today, the flagship products in its portfolio are its die-splitter machines sold to global metalstamping companies. Its customers include Miele, Indesit, Fagor, Valeo and Electrolux.

In 2006, the company moved into new headquarters, with triple the work space, equipped with the latest machines for the three-dimensional control of stamped work pieces. It has gradually increased its workforce and is constantly upgrading the design and manufacture of equipment and dies in shorter times.

“It has always been closely focused on the quality of its products which is reflected in our ongoing investment in the latest-generation machine tools which, together with CAD/CAM technologies, continuously reduce the production times of sheet metal dies,” Attrezzeria told ISMR.

We are already seeing a recovery in business – our order book is full for the next six months.

## Sheet metal dies

UNIVERSAL1972 is the new international guise of Attrezzeria – the company currently employs 40 people in a covered facility of over 4000 sq. m. and can machine parts of up to 6000 x 2500mm.

“Our strength is in providing customers with a ‘turnkey’ product. We design and make the dies ourselves and provide solutions to cut metal stamping costs. The dies are tested in our facility using a 1000-ton hydraulic press so that, when they reach the customer, they are immediately ready to go into production,” explained executive manager, Ing. Alessio Caramaschi.

“Our turnover in 2011 was 3.8 million euros. The trend is upwards – we think as much as + 15%. We are already seeing a recovery in business – our order book is full for the next six months. The current economic downturn has helped to spotlight sound, strong customers that we will move forward with and continue to support.”

Attrezzeria Universal’s speciality is the design and manufacture of dies for the coldstamping of all types of sheet metal, including:

■ dies for drawing and bending

■ dies for shearing and drilling

■ block dies

■ progressive dies

■ programmed progressive dies

■ transfer dies

■ progressive dies for primary operations and subsequent dies with integrated transfer.

The company manufactures small, medium and large dies, with maximum overall dimensions of 2500 x 1500 x 900mm (h). Its core business is the home appliance industry, but it can cater to requests of all types i.e. industrial motor vehicles, agricultural machinery and industries requiring sheet metal stamping.

“All the dies are tested on our presses (1000-ton hydraulic press and a mechanical press of up to 400 tons). On request, we can also supply pre-production parts and small stamped batches. Over forty years of experience, backed by the latest CAD/CAM tools, means that we can cater to any metal stamping requirement using the most modern technologies available,” it told ISMR.

The ability to directly digitize a customer’s prototype, interfacing with the most widelyused CAD systems, whilst making use of the latest materials and production technologies are an integral part of the company’s production philosophy. It can also provide customers with a die repair and maintenance service for existing dies.

## Moving into new markets

“We approached international markets decades ago, when it was not easy to do and few firms were willing to take the challenge. At the time, overseas market share was low compared to our domestic share but this is now changing. At the moment, Russian markets have been very profitable and we believe there are still a lot of opportunities there, above all in the household appliances market which is our main market. Not every company is equipped to approach such a market,” explained export manager, Mrs. Rossella Marangon.

“We also see strong opportunities in South America as it us undergoing a period of great expansion and looking for new industrial tools and equipment. We receive a lot of enquiries from that region and already have a significant customer base in Latin America.”

“We see that large manufacturers are tending to invest in safety and, obviously, cutting cost. With our die-splitter, we believe it is possible to cut up to 90% of overall production costs. The die-splitter enables the opening, turning and closing of dies of up to 100 tons by just one operator in less than two minutes using our permanent-magnet system,” added executive manager, Ing. Alessio Caramaschi.

“The die-splitter has been designed to open a die and overturn the top section in two minutes. In the time taken by traditional systems to open a die, our die-splitter has opened and closed it at least ten times. The ability to cut the lead time of a product means being much more competitive on the market in terms of price. The die-splitter reduces the number of operators needed for die handling – just one operator can move dies of any size and weight.”

Safety is paramount as no suspended loads are held above the operator, especially when the top section of the die is overturned. In comparison with traditional systems (such as chains, forklifts, bridge cranes etc.) the die-splitter offers many benefits, points out the company.
