## Longspan shelving

STOPA longspan shelving LG-B1.5, which came into operation in August 2013 and can handle five to six tons of material per shift, is mainly used by Schletter to stock extruded aluminium sections. 222 large cassettes, rated for a payload of up to 1500kg each and with a size of 830 x 600 x 6626mm (W x H x D), are used as load carriers. An overhead SRU inserts and retrieves the cassettes in the twelve aisles of the system.

The store, about 8.5m high and, when the service bridge is counted, 34.5m long and 7.3m wide, uses four output stations to supply the linked-up saws operated manually, automatically and semi-automatically by Schletter. The longspan shelving is also equipped with an insertion station.

Hackner unfolded a drawing. “Since the system is set up close to the incoming goods area and the saws, there are short distances to the machines. The main advantage of longspan shelving is, however, that the sawing unit has, due to the space-saving storage of the sections, considerably more available production area and we can provide our employees with a convenient material handling process.”

Requested sections are taken by the stations in automatic mode, monitored by scanners, directly to the saws. This saves the operator’s time which can then be used for machinery loading so that productivity increases. More time is won back by interim storage positions at the output stations, which are used by the system to minimise material supply cycle times.

The issue of return storage was also resolved by STOPA in the sawing unit. This is a crucial point because it is often necessary to put a half-opened coil back into storage. This is mainly because the extruded sections come from pressing in very large coils and cannot always be processed straight away in these quantities. During return storage, the weighing units of the stations make stock recording easier.

## Data exchange via SAP-certified interface

The sheet metal storage system control consists of an industrial PC with integrated slot-PLC, and, for longspan shelving, an integrated soft PLC. Both stores are linked to the operator’s SAP-supplied ERP system. Data exchanges on storage stocks and material requirements for work orders go through an SAP-certified iDoc interface. This is a proprietary transport record through which storage management is reproduced in the SAP system. The ERP system always keeps the master data and the complete stock records up-to-date. Production orders are planned by the company in the SAP system, which sends these orders as transport orders to the STOPA storage management systems (SMS).

The SMS of the sheet metal dynamically assigns the materials to the storage positions, manages the TARE weights of the pallets, processes manually-initiated insertion and output orders, controls/monitors movement orders and performs diagnostic functions.

Once the sections that were inside a longspan material cassette have been completely used up, the cassette goes to an empty cassette pool. From there, the operator requests a cassette for new material and books the incoming goods onto it. Management of the sections and standardised cassettes is handled by the SMS.
