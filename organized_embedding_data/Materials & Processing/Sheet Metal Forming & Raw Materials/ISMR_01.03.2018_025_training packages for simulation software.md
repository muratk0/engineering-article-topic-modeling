# Training packages for simulation software

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

BAVARIAN COMPANY, data M Sheet Metal Solutions, will be presenting its service and training packages for its software solution, COPRA® FEA RF, at the TUBE Fair 2018.

"The packages contain complete example models including video tutorials for the integration of pre- and post-processing operations with a focus on embossing and tube bending. The industry demands precisely fitting and complex products with small bending radius to wall thickness ratios. This makes manufacturing even more demanding. COPRA® FEA RF software visualises and verifies the production process within a virtual environment to prevent deviations during production. By taking into account previous roll forming procedures, pre- and post-processing steps can be optimally calculated to improve the forming process as a whole," explained the manufacturer.

With the software training packages, roll formers will be able to integrate additional processing steps into the forming process. An applicable package contains a ready-to-use simulation model as well as 3D models for any necessary tools. These can be used immediately with the 3D import functions of COPRA® FEA RF 2017. A manual for the creation of example models, including a video tutorial, is also added to the portfolio. Detailed instructions for the application of COPRA® FEA RF 2017 enable roll formers to create better models for future projects. Special training can also be conducted upon request.

The company also offers optical measuring systems (COPRA® ProfileScan Desktop and COPRA® RollScanner) for quality control of products and roll tools. See www.datam.de

EUROTECH WILL BE presenting some of its specialist products at the FENSTERBAU FRONTALE trade fair in Nuremberg (Germany) from 21-24 March 2018. Lifting devices from its eT-Hover series, tube lifters from the eT-Lift series and its new generation of suction boxes from the eT-Gripper series will be on display. Visitors to booth 261 in Hall 3 at the exhibition will be able to put devices to the test.

"Our trade fair exhibits will include the modular eT-Hover-allround, a compact, versatile tool whose base unit can be converted individually and flexibly to fulfil a wide range of handling tasks. It facilitates horizontal and vertical lifting, pivoting and rotating through 360° and any combination of these movements. Depending upon the vacuum components and vacuum generators used, these lifting devices can handle loads weighing up to 500kg," the manufacturer told ISMR.

The eT-Hover-modesty vacuum lifting device, which can rotate loads to 90°, is suitable for lifting and rotating vacuum-tight, flat loads such as glass panes, wooden boards, plastic panels and metal sheet. Depending upon the vacuum components and vacuum generators used, these lifting devices can handle loads weighing up to 250kg.

Eurotech will also be presenting its new generation of eT-Gripper devices, which are even lighter than previous versions. These grippers handle frequently varying surfaces and formats as well as smooth, rough-sawn, uneven and undulating structures. They can be part of a portal or robotic solution.

Tube lifters from the eT-Lift product family are easy to handle and equipped with an ergonomic, one-hand control system. Using a quick-swap system, changing tools takes a few simple steps. Graded carrying capacities range from 20kg up to 250kg. The eT-Lift-mini is a small, compact tube lifter with a pistol grip. The pick-and-place device for frequently recurring work processes can handle loads such as furniture parts, sheet material, cardboard boxes and other packaging weighing between 20 and 65kg.
