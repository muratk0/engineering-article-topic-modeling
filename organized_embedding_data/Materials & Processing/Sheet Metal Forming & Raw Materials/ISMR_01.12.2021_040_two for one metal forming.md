# ‘Two for one’ metal forming

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

FluidForming Americas, Inc. is promoting its FormBalancer as both a sheet metal and tube forming machine.

Using the FluidForming process, this universal sheet metal and tube forming machine offers ‘two for one’ metal forming capability.

“With a simple adaptation, any FormBalancer can be converted from precision fluid cell sheet metal forming to powerful, high-pressure tube forming. This gives increased manufacturing capabilities and flexibility to users, with tube forming pressures up to 60,000 psi. It also enables design innovation and creativity by adding material during forming. Force-contained, compact tube forming machines fit in almost any shop. Costly structural or foundational reinforcements are unnecessary. The machine also offers low maintenance and low overall cost of ownership,” said the company.

“The forming process is simple: just place the tube forming module and tube dies into the FormBalancer. Then, insert the tubing into the machine. Seal the tube ends and fill the cavity with water. As the highly pressurised water flows into the tube, the tube will expand to fit the custom mould. It is a highly precise and controllable 360-degree forming process,” it added.

The FormBalancer was designed to accommodate tube material that is forcefed into the die cavity as the tube is being formed, essentially “adding” material to the formed part. This feature maximises tube use and enables designers to develop complex parts with unusual shapes. As with metal forming, FluidForming’s approach to tube forming enables even greater design flexibility.

“Tube hydroforming — or tube FluidForming, in this case — is a costeffective way to form strong, lightweight tubular components. FluidForming tube forming is ideal for small production runs or for manufacturing custom shapes for highend lighting fixtures, designer appliances, intricate medical and laboratory equipment, or precision aerospace components,” concluded the manufacturer.
