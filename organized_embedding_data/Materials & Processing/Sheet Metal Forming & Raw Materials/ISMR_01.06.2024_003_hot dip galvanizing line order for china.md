# Hot dip galvanizing line order for China

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Angang Guangzhou Automotive Steel Co., Ltd. and Ansteel Engineering Technology Corporation Limited have signed a contract with SMS Group for the supply of a new hotdip galvanizing line (No. 2). The first readyfor-sale coil is scheduled for production in November 2025.

The new No. 2 hot dip galvanizing line will be installed at the premises of Angang Guangzhou Automotive Steel Co., Ltd. in Guangzhou, China, and feature an annual capacity of 400,000 tons. The production bay is to be placed close to the existing No. 1 hot dip galvanizing line, supplied by SMS in 2013.

The new No. 2 hot dip galvanizing line is intended to produce exposed automotive panels that require strong surface quality. All key technological components covering the mechanical, thermal, process and E&A equipment will be designed and supplied by entities operating within SMS.

“The technological centrepiece of the new No. 2 hot dip galvanizing line is the allradiant-tube annealing furnace from DREVER International with the air knife system from DUMA-BANDZINK, both companies in the SMS group. These systems ensure the right material properties within tight tolerances as well as the flawless surface condition that automotive end-users require for exposed qualities. The terminal and process equipment guarantees stable production and efficient maintenance. A high degree of modularisation is incorporated in the chosen design. The electrical and automation systems from SMS bring the individual units together to form one smart and homogeneous facility," confirmed SMS.

SMS's cleaning section removes surface contaminations using a combination of mechanical, hydrodynamic and chemical process steps. The cleanliness of the strip surface is decisive for the final achievable product quality. SMS's inline skin pass mill is designed to finish the galvanized surface with a controlled surface imprint. In addition, the unit improves the strip shape to allow for easy stamping at the end user's facilities in the automotive industry.

SMS put the first hot dip galvanizing line – No.1 – successfully into operation at Angang Iron & Steel Co. Ltd in 2013. Two continuous annealing lines were also jointly put into service at Angang's main site in Anshan.■
