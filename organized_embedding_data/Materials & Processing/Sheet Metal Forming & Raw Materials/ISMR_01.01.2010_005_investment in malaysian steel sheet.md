# Investment in Malaysian steel sheet

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

Nippon Steel and HANWA have reached an agreement with Tatt Giap Group to acquire 10% and 15%, respectively, of the voting shares in E-Galv Steel from its largest shareholder, Tatt Giap Steel Centre, a coil centre and a subsidiary of Tatt Giap Group, around the end of December 2009.

Malaysia is regarded as a large consumption area of electro-galvanized steel sheets, where many Japanese manufacturers of home appliances such as flat-screen TV and audio instrument have established their factories. Demand for electro-galvanized steel sheets is expected to grow steadily in Malaysia.

By investing in E-Galv Steel, Nippon Steel and HANWA aim to strengthen their relationships and expand its business with Tatt Giap Group. Nippon Steel and HANWA also intend to build a supply structure for electrogalvanized steel sheet that is capable of satisfying the quality requirements of Malaysian customers including Japanese home appliance manufacturers in Malaysia. They also wish to securely capture demand for steel, mainly for home appliances, in Malaysia by stable supply of coldrolled steel substrates to E-Galv Steel and through possible technical assistance to E-Galv Steel, as necessary.

E-Galv Steel is planning to change its current name to Nippon EGalv Steel Sdn. Bhd. after Nippon Steel and HANWA complete their investment.
