# Development of lighter high-strength steel

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

CARPENTER TECHNOLOGY Corporation has finalised the completion of a formal cooperation agreement with United States Steel Corporation in Pittsburgh, Pa., which calls for the development of a new “lightweighting” steel for automotive applications.

Carpenter’s patented alloy, TEMPER TOUGH™ specialised steel alloy, is designed for use in flat-roll automotive applications as well as other demanding applications.

Originally designed for the aerospace industry, the new alloy may help automotive manufacturers meet recently mandated Corporate Average Fuel Requirement (CAFÉ) standards by lightweighting vehicles through mass reduction, said the manufacturer. The specialised steel alloy also offers high-strength and ductility attributes that support increasing safety requirements to be designed into critical automotive support end-uses.

US Steel will develop the grade through its operational footprint. It is an integrated steel producer focused on high value-added flatrolled and tubular steel products with major production operations in the United States, Canada and Slovakia and an annual raw steelmaking capability of 29.3 million net tons.
