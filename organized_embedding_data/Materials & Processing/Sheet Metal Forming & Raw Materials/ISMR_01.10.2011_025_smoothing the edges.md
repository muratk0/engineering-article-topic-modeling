# SMOOTHING THE EDGES

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

To continue to be successful, surface finishing companies must be knowledgeable about the economic and technological trends facing the industry.

Surface finishing is the process of altering the surface on an object (e.g. plating, polishing, anodising, coating) for the purpose of enhancing its appearance or functional properties. Apart from its decorative uses on a wide range of consumer and industrial products, surface finishing provides essential functional properties such as:

Corrosion resistance
Abrasion resistance
Wear resistance
Improved lubricity
Non-toxicity and bio-compatability
Dimensional alteration
Light reflectivity / absorption
Electrical insulation / conductivity
Solderability
Thermal management
Magnetism
Catalytic electrodes and devices
Enhancement of paint adhesion.

“There is not a piece of metal in our homes, businesses, cars or in our pockets that has not passed through a surface finishing plant somewhere. Without this industry, the entire economy would grind to a halt.

“To continue to be successful, surface finishing companies must be knowledgeable about the economic and technology trends facing the industry,” said the US National Association for Surface Finishing (NASF).
