# PARTNERS IN PROGRESS

## COMPOSITES ALUCOBOND R

3A Composites GmbH has developed, manufactured and marketed high-quality aluminium composite panels, structural composite material, plastic sheets and lightweight foam boards for architecture, visual communication and transport & industry markets since 1969. The company's brands (ALUCOBOND®, DIBOND®, ALUCORE®, KAPA® and FOREX®) have established its reputation as a global company. 3A Composites GmbH is part of the international 3A Composites Group.

This year, 3A Composites is celebrating the 50-year anniversary of its main ARC product, ALUCOBOND®, which is sold on all five continents and over 100 countries. ALUCOBOND® is a composite panel consisting of two aluminium cover sheets and a fireretardant or non-combustible mineral-filled core.

“3A Composites has installed a rollbending model from AMB Picot in its new Technicum training and demonstration centre to show its customers how to bend flat ALUCOBOND® panels for building facades”

Guntram Eydner, Head of New Products & Product Management, 3A Composites GmbH

To celebrate this key 50-year anniversary, 3A Composites invited over 200 customers to a special innovation event at its facility in Singen, Germany, at the end of June 2019. At the event, it also celebrated and demonstrated the results of a fruitful new partnership with roll forming specialist, AMB Picot, which enables 3A Composites' customers to quickly and easily bend ALUCOBOND panels using Picot roll bending technology. AMB PICOT has been designing and manufacturing roll bending machines for over 150 years and is constantly expanding its range to create value. The company's portfolio includes a wide range of plate roll bending, as well as roll-profile bending and special bending machines.

## Intuitive operation

3A Composites bases its extensive manufacturing facilities, for the production of ALUCOBOND® aluminium composites, at Singen. It has also established its 'Technicum at Singen as an educational and training facility to help its customers transform ALUCOBOND® flat panels into building facade elements e.g. tray panels or in whatever form, format or colour is required. This also includes round-shaped elements.

"Since 2016, 3A Composites has been trying to facilitate the use of ALUCOBOND® in creating new cladding systems and new software, providing an intuitive way for nonexpert customers to make ALUCOBOND® claddings. We searched for a strong partnership with an innovative roll bending machine supplier on which to use ALUCOBOND®. We found AMB Picot to be the right partner for 3A Composites, thanks to its patented CNC easyroll software program," explained Vincent Spodar, Civil Engineer, 3A Composites GmbH.

The façade material, 3A Composites told ISMR, offers precise flatness, a variety of surfaces and colours as well as excellent formability. The rear-ventilated construction technique is suitable for creating façades on both new and existing buildings as well as roof constructions. Users can modify shapes, patterns and grids, adapt colour and create their desired building envelope. ALUCOBOND® Designmaker is 3A Composites' new tool for users to create individual surface designs in façade concepts. 3A Composites also offers a comprehensive technical service, which includes the training of architects, fabricators and installers on bending and fabricating its facade material.
