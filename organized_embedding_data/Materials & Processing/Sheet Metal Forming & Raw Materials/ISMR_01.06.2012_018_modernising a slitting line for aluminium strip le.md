# Modernising a slitting line for aluminium strip led to an 80% increase in productivity for a Swiss metal service centre

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

Metall Service Menziken AG (MSM) is a leading multi-metal service centre in Switzerland for customer-oriented procurement of aluminium, copper, brass, bronze and stainless steel. With a workforce of 55, MSM handles approximately 400 orders per day, increasing to 600 on peak days, with an average material turnover of 140 tonnes. The company maintains 2,500 stock items on a total storage area of 14,200 m2.

In 1989, Metall Service Menziken AG procured a machine for slitting aluminium strip which no longer complied with current market requirements in terms of strip precision, changeover times (particularly cutter configuration programmes) and operational safety, so a decision had to be made whether to replace or upgrade the equipment. For reasons of cost, shorter distances and the considerable advantage of direct communication, MSM decided to commission a Swiss company with the task of upgrading the machine.

NOBAG Nobs Engineering AG is a leading manufacturer of slitting lines for a wide range of materials. The company has decades of experience in processing steel, non-ferrous metals, aluminium and silicon steel. MSM opted for NOBAG as its partner for the machine upgrade.

## An eye on precision

Strip precision, changeover time and operational safety of the existing machine were no longer acceptable for modern requirements. In particular, the existing slitter cutter shaft dimension was inadequate, which meant that both cutting quality and capacity were significantly restricted.

At maximum cutting capacity, the theoretical deflection of the cutter shaft was greatly exceeded. It therefore became necessary to re-dimension and replace the slitter. By installing an automatic tool clamping and loading system, it was possible to reduce setup times significantly and improve the productivity of the machine – an important factor for MSM.

New requirements for operational safety were complied with by renewing the feeding section, changes in the electrical control and hydraulic system and by moving the position of the scrap container under the floor.

The time available for design, production of machine components and work on-site, including installation and commissioning, was seven months.
