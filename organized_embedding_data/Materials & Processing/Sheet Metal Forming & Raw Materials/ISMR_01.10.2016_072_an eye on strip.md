# An eye on strip

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

AT THIS YEAR'S EuroBLECH (25-29 October 2016), in hall 17 (stand G12), Tata Steel will demonstrate how customers operating in the automotive, yellow goods and consumer goods industries can benefit from the steel producer's increased focus on strip, following the sale of its Long Products Europe business earlier this year. The steel maker will showcase optimised strip steels with higher formability and strength along with value-adding technical services that can improve manufacturing rates and end-product performance.

One of the key developments that it will highlight at EuroBLECH is a new 25mm thick strip steel, which is currently in the trial phase. Following successful testing, the steel producer will offer the product in widths of up to two metres, as well as in different thicknesses. The resulting product will, it says, give customers a tough and strong alternative to discrete steel plate for applications such as yellow goods machinery, where thickness, toughness and strength is required.

Customers in the yellow goods industry will also be offered insights into Tata Steel's more established grades, including its HR HSLA Ympress® range. These products comprise tough and strong steel grades developed to operate in harsh environments, with weight-saving potential. Consumer goods industry customers will get an overview of the steel producer's series of pre-finished steels like Ymvit®, its enamelling steel, with coatings tailored to meet exacting functional and aesthetic performance for Large Domestic Appliances.

The new premium steel XPF800 gives more design freedom for demanding chassis applications, such as lower control arms, due to its improved combination of stretch flangeability and elongation

“Re-roller customers will receive information about our high-carbon grades as well as our work in midcarbon products, aimed at the tube market for end-use in the automotive sector. For the automotive industry, we have just launched XPF800, a new premium steel for highly demanding chassis applications, such as lower control arms, at an 800MPa strength level. Its improved combination of stretch flangeability and elongation creates a unique advantage on conventional chassis steels, enabling more design freedom for attractive lightweighting, without compromising manufacturing robustness and performance," said Tata Steel.
