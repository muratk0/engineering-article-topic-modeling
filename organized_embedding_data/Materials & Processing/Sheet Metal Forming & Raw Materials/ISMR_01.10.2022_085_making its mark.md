# Making its mark

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

AP&T will be participating in North America's largest metalforming event, FABTECH, in Atlanta (8-10 November 2022). On stand B5721, under the banner of 'One Responsible Partner®’, the Swedish manufacturer will be presenting its total offering to the sheetmetalforming industry.

"New products and business models provide our customers with the best conditions for long lifetime performance and sustainable profitability. We offer turnkey solutions for fibre and sheet-metal forming based on our deep knowledge of processes and machines as well as long-term dedication to our customers' business. Our global portfolio includes complete production lines with presses; automation; tools; heat-treatment systems and services such as maintenance; sparepart provision; optimisation and rebuilds," it explained.

"During FABTECH, visitors can also gain insight into the opportunities that we can offer as an employer," it added.

There is significant potential to shorten cycle times and increase production by optimising press-hardening lines. Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (Stellantis) has increased its productivity by allowing AP&T to optimise its press-hardening lines.

"Although the greatest benefits are seen in revenues, optimisation also enables lower costs per produced unit, such as through improved energy efficiency. The more units that can be manufactured within a certain time frame, the lower the energy consumption per unit, as idle time is minimised," explained Jan Larsson, press-hardening specialist at AP&T who is also responsible for developing AP&T's aftermarket business.

Above: AP&T will be at FABTECH in Atlanta, USA. Left: Jan Larsson, press-hardening specialist at AP&T.

"In terms of potential, it's not uncommon that cycle times can be reduced by about 20% or more," added the company.
