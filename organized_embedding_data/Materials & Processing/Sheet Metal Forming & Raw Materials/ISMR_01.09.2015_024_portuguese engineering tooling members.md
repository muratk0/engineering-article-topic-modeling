Exhibition hall 6 at the Frankfurt Trade Fair was built in 1963 by the architect, Martin Schoenmarker. The 35,500 square metre space is located in the middle of the Frankfurt trade fair grounds and has four levels which are spread over a hall ground size of approximately 160m x 70m.

When renovation was required due to technical fire legislation, the façade was clad with Novelis ff2® pre-painted aluminium.

## Metallic shades of grey

With its high-strength alloy and PVdF coating, Novelis ff2® pre-painted aluminium was the material chosen for this project. A key aspect was its non-flammable fire rating A1 (according to DIN EN 13501). Novelis ff2® is produced under strict environmental regulations and is 100 per cent recyclable.

“The pre-painted aluminium panels, with a permissible load of 96MPa according to DIN 4113, were formed and installed as cassettes with clip fixing. This construction method enables a cost-efficient substructure as well as easy installation with economic efficiency. The facade was installed by Radabau GmbH in Zwingenberg, Germany,” Novelis told ISMR.

For the façade cladding, about 6,000m² of Novelis ff2® and 500 m² Novelis ff3® were used in metallic silver and matt-grey. After the renovation, the exhibition hall sparkled through the use of these different shades of colour planned by “Kölling Architekten”.

About Novelis
Novelis Inc. is a specialist in aluminium rolled products and the world’s largest recycler of aluminium. The company operates in 11 countries, has approximately 11,500 employees and reported US$ 11.1 billion in revenue for its 2015 fiscal year. Novelis supplies aluminium sheet and foil products to transportation, packaging, construction, industrial and consumer electronics markets throughout North America, Europe, Asia and South America.
