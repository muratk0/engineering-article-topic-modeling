# IN-LINE PROCESSING

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“The market is moving more towards higher strength and thicker material processing”

Coil processing specialist, Athader SL, discusses new technologies and the rise of automation in the lines along Industry 4.0 principles.

Athader is a leading European supplier of coil processing lines. It is based in the city of Renteria in one of the most highly industrialised areas of Spain, the Basque Country. Its product range includes automatic slitting lines, levelling and cut-to-length lines, combined lines (slitting and cut-to length) and multi-blanking lines, stacking and strapping lines; inspection lines and special lines. Its main activity is the design, manufacturing and installation of coil processing lines for carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium.

"We have hundreds of lines in operation across five continents, and can also offer coil processing lines with flexible designs tailor-made designed machines, adapted to customer requirements, and turnkey projects. We invest in the continuous improvement of our products and processes and offer global after-sales service and spare parts supply," its general manager, Harkaitz Luengo, told ISMR.

As the company celebrates its 25th anniversary this year, ISMR sat down with Mr. Luengo to discuss the company's latest coil processing projects and its views on the industry.

## New line deliveries

"We have customers in North and South America, Europe, Middle East, Australia, India and North Africa. The majority of our customers are in Europe. We are growing a lot in North America, Middle East, India and Australia. Since 2013, we have grown about 10% every year. From last year to this year, we will grow another 5%. So turnover, employment, staff and facilities have all grown and we are very pleased with this. Another welcome effect this year is that the Spanish market is delivering much better returns for us," Mr Luengo told ISMR.

"In addition, we have been involved recently in Canadian and Australian markets, delivering three slitting lines. We are providing two fully automated slitting lines for the NOVA STEEL Group in Canada (Montreal and Hamilton) which can process coil material of up to 10mm - these are currently being commissioned at NOVA STEEL's two service centres. We have also delivered a new coil (16mm material thickness) slitting line to BLUESCOPE STEEL in Australia, that includes many of the most advanced technologies for operations (such as robotic packaging and production, vibration systems, monitoring systems etc.). It is a very sophisticated slitting line.

The lines will be processing steel coils of up to 30 tons (65,000 lbs), with material widths of 1550mm (61") and material thicknesses ranging from 1.5mm (0.059") up to 16mm (0.63") at maximum speeds of 100mts/min.

## A technical focus

Athader is currently manufacturing a cut-to-length line with an e-Drive Bradbury leveller, rotary shear and combined Air/Bomb Door stacker for critical materials such as stainless steel, aluminium and coated steel which, it told ISMR, can be stacked without scratches using a new integrated automated bundle evacuation system.

"We have been developing a new blank stacking system – it is a double stacker with combined systems designed to offer customers high levels of efficiency and productivity. It can process stainless steel, aluminium or hot-rolled steel with no scratches. We are manufacturing these now and will be installing these on an advanced cut-to-length packaging line for a customer in Barcelona," explained Harkaitz Luengo.

Athader also told ISMR that the market is moving more towards higher strength and thicker material processing. Customers are also trying to reduce the number of lines they employ i.e. by doing the work of three lines on one line.

"We also see lines processing laser-cut material (laser blanks, flat and stress-free material), so we are successfully integrating the hydraulic e-Drive Bradbury leveller which eliminates internal tensions in the material with ultra flat results, so that it can be laser cut with no secondary operations afterwards," added Harkaitz Luengo.

## Looking to the future

"One of the strategies for us going forward is to 100% automate our lines. We want to reduce or take out altogether the number of human operators on the line. Right now, our lines can be operated by three or four people – we want to reduce that to just one operator. That means more reliance on robot systems and automation. We already use automation but the level of automation on our line will substantially increase," he explained.

In line with this philosophy, Athader has been working on monitoring systems for its lines, in accordance with Industry 4.0 considerations. It wants its customers to have higher visibility of what is happening on the line so that they can substantially increase their productivity levels.

"In today's sheet metal shops, there are fewer skilled personnel and more automation is needed. If you can reduce the manpower on the lines and automate many of the procedures, then you can increase the efficiency of the entire coil processing operation as well as increase safety levels for the operators," said Luengo.

We will also be at EuroBLECH next October in Hanover," he concluded.
