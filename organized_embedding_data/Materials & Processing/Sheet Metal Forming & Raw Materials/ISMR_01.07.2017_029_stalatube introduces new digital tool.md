# Stalatube introduces new digital tool

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

STALATUBE, A SUPPLIER of stainless steel tubes, now offers its customers a digital Stalafit tool which guides the designer in choosing the best tube to fit requirements. The tube optimisation tool is part of Stalatube's Stalafit service concept. Traditionally, structural tubes have been produced in size intervals of 10mm but, with the new service concept, the designer does not need to compromise when looking for the best tube size. The outer dimensions of tubes can now be defined down to the millimetre.

"The most significant advantage of the Stalafit concept is its customisability: the dimensions and material of the tube can be selected according to the intended use. Cost savings can be seen when it comes to material purchases and logistics.

The application also helps the designer to choose the right material according to EN 1993-1-4, taking into account the applicable requirements. It also speeds up the design process as the details of the design, such as the bending resistance and cross-sectional properties of the tubes, are updated in real-time according to the choices made," explained the company.

The Stalafit application is available as a web and a mobile application. Stalatube is the world's third largest manufacturer of stainless square and rectangular tubes in its sector and a leading manufacturer of ferritic and Duplex stainless square and rectangular tubes. In addition to hollow sections, the company produces stainless I-beams, flat bars and components. See www.stalatube.com
