## A turnkey solution

The installation chosen by Steel Constructions consists of a deburring machine, a return table, a turnover unit, a second deburring machine and a powerful dust extractor. This arrangement was chosen for its ability to deburr and grind steel to the precise radius required, without overheating the material.

The turnover unit ensures that Hage and his colleagues turn a product as little as possible (steel rectangular tubes, that are machined on four sides, just need one turn). This, said Timesavers, is a huge benefit, considering that some parts are up to six metres long and therefore very heavy.

“For smaller material, we have another solution; anything less than 400 x 400mm cannot pass through the turnover unit. Those parts are transported back with a return table," added Ludo Vermeulen.

The operator takes off the small parts and puts them back on the transport table for deburring of the other side. Large parts of up to six metres are placed on the roller table at the beginning. Those pass through the first Timesavers 42RB. The turnover unit then turns the product over and the second Timesavers 42RB machine processes the other side. The belt then transports the product to the end of the line, where a forklift collects it.

## Counting the benefits

The set-up has increased productivity and ensured cleaner and safer working conditions at Steel Constructions. The Timesavers machines' dust extraction system significantly reduces the release of dust and particles, improving the work environment for operators.

"As a result, we have seen productivity increases with fewer people," explained Marco Hage. “However, just as importantly, production is cleaner for the employees. We can achieve a good radius for the customers and the product is super-clean before it goes to the welder. If the product has to be galvanised, the zinc layer will also adhere better," he continued.

He confirmed that he is very satisfied with machine maintenance.

"As a maintenance engineer, I have very little to do on the Timesavers' line. Once, someone accidentally bumped into a sensor which I had to realign. Other than that, I have had very little maintenance to perform on the line. I am happy about that, because I have plenty of other work to keep me busy," he smiled.

## About Timesavers International BV

Timesavers International BV is a manufacturer of industrial wide belt grinding machines, with customers all over the world. Starting in 1939 as Linden Machine factory in Rotterdam, with a focus on woodworking machines, it moved into metalworking machines in the late 1970s.

In 1965, the company moved to Goes in the province of Zeeland in the Netherlands and merged with Timesavers USA in 1996 to become Timesavers International. Today, the company is part of U.S. holding group; InCompass, Manufactured Growth Solutions. It specialises in deburring, edge rounding, finishing and precision grinding systems.
