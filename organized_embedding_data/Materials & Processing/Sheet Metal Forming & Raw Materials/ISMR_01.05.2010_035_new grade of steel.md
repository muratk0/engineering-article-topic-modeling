# NEW GRADE OF STEEL

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

Corus is first to market with an innovative, new grade of steel, Dual Phase (DP) 800HyPerform – an advanced high-strength steel designed specifically with the needs of the automotive industry in mind.

"DP800HyPerform combines the strength levels of a DP800 grade with the formability properties of a DP600 grade, without compromising on weldability. This grade was developed in close cooperation with a number of automotive OEMs to meet their exacting needs," Corus explained.

DP800HyPerform has been specifically developed for making lightweight automotive parts such as structural members and reinforcements that must be highly crash-resistant. The steels' minimum ultimate tensile strength is 780 MPa, similar to that of Corus's existing DP800 grade. The difference is that DP800HyPerform offers extra ductility, resulting in 'good formability in combination with good weldability' said the manufacturer. This makes it particularly suitable for complex shaped stamped parts. DP800HyPerform has a hot dipped galvanised zinc coating, which incurs a lower cost than electrolitically galvanised zinc coating that is required for TRIP steels, for example.

Maurice van Giezen, Manager, Corus Automotive Business Development, commented: "We are often faced with the apparent paradox of vehicle manufacturers wanting materials that have better crash resistance and offer improved press shop performance, but that at the same time can be supplied at lower cost. By combining the benefits of a number of different grades of steel into one product, DP800HyPerform achieves all three of these objectives."
