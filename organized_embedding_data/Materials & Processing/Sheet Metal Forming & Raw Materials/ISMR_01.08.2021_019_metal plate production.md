# Metal plate production

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 ISMR]

Meusburger is guaranteeing continuous delivery availability of metal plate, even during the raw material crisis. The current sharp increase of raw material prices is causing uncertainty for many companies. The dramatic rise in costs is due to increasing demand for steel, which has resulted in global steel shortages. This has caused not only supply bottlenecks but an increase in prices.

"We offer 100% in-house production of metal plates. The procurement difficulties and the significant rise in steel prices are also very big issues for our company. Regardless, our top priority is to continue to fill our warehouse to ensure continuous supply to our customers as they implement their projects. Our mission is to guarantee delivery availability through our raw material warehouse - now and always. That is why, despite the current challenge, we will not stop purchasing raw material," explains managing director, Guntram Meusburger.

"Thanks to our large stocks, we were able to stop this drastic price development affecting our customers over the last six months. However, as there is no relief in sight and steel prices are still rising, we are being forced to make price adjustments," he added.

With extensive stocks of standard parts and plates, Meusburger can ensure continuous availability to customers. The Austrian company's mission is to continue to guarantee reliable supply in the future. It is a market leader in high-precision standard parts, with over 55 years of experience working with steel.

Its product portfolio ranges from high-precision standard parts and selected products in workshop equipment to hot runner and control systems, the WBI Knowledge Management Method and solutions for efficient corporate management in the ERP/PPS software area. This makes Meusburger a global partner for making moulds, dies, jigs and fixtures.
