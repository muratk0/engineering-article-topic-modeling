# Stam SpA

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.stam.it/en/

Stam SpA is an Italian manufacturer of processing machines for coil, sheet and rollforming lines. The Italian manufacturer supplies rollforming lines for industry sectors such as logistics; construction; household appliances; energy; infrastructure and automotive.

"Our lines are equipped with the latest technologies: 3D; flexible; cassette or traditional rollforming; punching; forming and bending; laser-plasma cutting and welding; stacking and packing. Our cutting lines encompass slitting; cut-to-length; blanking and multi-blanking lines as well as coil-processing lines," it highlighted.

Its punching and rollforming line for uprights includes a coil car; decoiler; straightener; loop tables; mechanical press; flexible rollforming machine; straightening head; flying shear and unloading table. It is controlled by Stam customised software. The line can process high-strength, cold-rolled steel of between 1.75-4mm thick at production speeds of up to 25m/minute. Its slitting line processes hot-rolled steel (4-15.9mm thick) at coil widths of 1850mm and production speeds of 60m/minute.
