# Burnishing agent for steels

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Emil Otto GmbH has introduced burnishing agents for special steel alloys and high-alloy stainless steels.

Its new burnishing agent, Nigrit VA-Extra, has enabled the German metal chemistry specialist to further expand its portfolio. The new product is intended for cold burnishing and blackening special steel, alloys and high-alloy stainless steels.

Emil Otto offers a range of different burnishing agents to create a black colouration on metallic surfaces. It already offers burnishing agents in the Nigrit series for aluminium and aluminium alloys; iron and steel; copper; brass; bronze as well as titanium and titanium alloys. With the newly developed Nigrit VA-Extra, special alloys can now also be processed.

By soaking the workpieces in the solution, black mixed oxide layers are formed which protect the surface. The layers created by burnishing are resistant to bending and abrasion, which enables subsequent processing of the metal.

“Depending upon the material, blackening occurs relatively quickly, approx. 15-30 seconds after application. If the workpiece remains in the Nigrit VA-Extra longer, loose oxides accumulate on the surface, which have to be removed after the process," Markus Geßner, Marketing and Sales Manager, Emil Otto GmbH, explained.

After removing the loose oxides, which can be done with the Neutrasol product developed by Emil Otto, the workpiece must be dried and preserved. Emil Otto also offers a product for preservation.

"The after-treatment fluid is temporarily-effective, preservative metal protection that can be applied thinly after the Nigrit VA-Extra has been used. All burnishing solutions are intended for immediate use in metal processing," concluded the manufacturer.

As an owner-managed company, in existence since 1901, Emil Otto develops and manufactures fluxing agents. This includes fluxing agents for electronics production; strip tinning; cooler construction and for galvanizing.
