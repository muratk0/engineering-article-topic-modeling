# LDM Officina Meccanica Srl

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.ldmsrl.it

LDM has over 40 years of experience in designing, manufacturing, installing and start-up of coil processing machines: slitting and cut-to-length lines; coil rewinding lines as well as upgrading used lines with the latest technologies. It focuses on continuous quality increases of its equipment.

LDM levellers are used for levelling cold-rolled steel coil and sheet and can process any kind of steel and non-ferrous materials (material thicknesses from 0.3 to 16mm, max. material width 2100mm).

Its slitting lines handle hot and cold-rolled galvanized, pre-painted and stainless-steel coil, as well as non-ferrous materials (material thickness from 0.3 to 16mm; max. coil width 2100mm; max. coil weight 40 t.; max. line working speed 500m/min.).

LDM cut-to-length lines (CTL) include decoiling and levelling, producing cut-to-length sheet and stacking-ready products. Material thickness ranges from 0.3 to 16mm; max. coil width 2100mm; max. coil weight 40 t. and max. line working speed 60m/minute.

## https://lamiflex.com

“Ship your coils with confidence! Lamiflex provides packaging materials to protect from corrosion and transport damage. Our automation solutions provide high throughput at the packing station. We represent a variety of brands; MultiWrapper; PushWrapper; iTube; Lamiflex; Lamistretch; Lamishield and Lamiedge," said packaging specialist, Lamiflex.

Lamiflex provides automated packing solutions for steel and aluminium coils as well as other heavy goods. Automated wrapping increases the speed and quality of packing, improves ergonomics and saves manual labour.

“Packaging materials with VCI provides extra corrosion protection without the use of toxic substances and without leaving any residue on the metal surface," it added.

The MultiWrapper is an automated coil packaging solution; each coil is wrapped tightly with stretch-wrap, through the eye. The PushWrapper concept consists of a tailored plastic packaging material with a new machine for wrapping steel or aluminium coils. iTube is a solution for tube and bar packaging with stretch film.
