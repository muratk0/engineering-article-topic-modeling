# Keeping it green

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

TECHNOLOGICAL INNOVATIONS have enabled Cortec® Corporation to replace hazardous synthetic chemicals with “green" alternatives. Cortec® recently launched production of its bio-based and biodegradable coatings for corrosion protection in metalworking industries in Croatia.

“Thanks to committed research, study and experimentation, we produce ecologically safe corrosion control solutions whose performance has outgrown conventional rust preventatives. Not only are these bio-based, environmentally safe alternatives functionally superior to conventional petroleum-derived products, but they are a far more economical solution," said the manufacturer.

BioCorr® is a water-based, ready-to-use formulation intended for preservation of metals in storage and during transportation. Formulated with renewable raw materials, BioCorr® contains 64% bio-based content. It provides multi-metal corrosion protection by combining film-forming additives with Vapour phase Corrosion Inhibitors (VpCIs).

“BioCorr® provides protection for up to two years of indoor storage or during shipments. Unlike rust preventative oils, it leaves a dry film on the surface of the metal that is virtually undetectable. BioCorr® is used for the protection of machines and equipment; pipes; flanges; gears; cast iron; sheet and coil; and as a temporary coating for storage and shipment," said Cortec.

EcoLine® 3220 and EcoLine® 3690 are bio-based and biodegradable coatings developed to replace traditional oil-based rust preventatives, and designed to eliminate the expensive disposal costs associated with oils. The coatings conform to NACE Standard RP0487-2000. EcoLine® Cleaner/Degreaser is a soya-based product designed for tough cleaning jobs in industrial and commercial applications. It can be used on all engineered metals and alloys, said the company.

“All products in our bio-based system are VOC-, chromate- and nitrite-free and conform to the highest environmental standards. Their biodegradable formulation enables elimination of the expensive disposal costs associated with hazardous mineral oils and flammable solvents, making them far more affordable solutions for customers," concluded Cortec.
