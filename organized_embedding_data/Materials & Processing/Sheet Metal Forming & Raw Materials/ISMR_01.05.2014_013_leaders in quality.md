Amada recently unveiled its new interactive 9,500m² Northern European Technical Centre, as part of a complete €6 million refurbishment of its customer facilities based in Kidderminster, near Birmingham (UK). It invited ISMR to tour the facility and view its latest sheet metal machine tool technologies.

The Japanese specialist’s aim is to provide an even better experience for its customers and visitors with expanded showrooms, demonstration suites and meeting areas. The new Technical Centre will serve as a core base from which Amada carries out its activities in the UK, Spain and Scandinavia.

ISMR was also shown demonstrations of the latest new Amada technology, including the FLW fiber laser welding cell, the EG-AR robotic press brake cell and the European launch of its new LCG 3015 laser cutting machines. Additional new technologies included HFE3i and HG press brakes, an EM-3612M II turret punch press and various tooling and software solutions.
