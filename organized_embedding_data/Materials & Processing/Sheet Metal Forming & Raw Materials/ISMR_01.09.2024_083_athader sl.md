# Athader SL

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

https://athader.com/

“We are dedicated to producing high-quality, productivityenhancing metal processing equipment for customers around the world. We have been in the market since 1992 and, in 2012, became part of The Bradbury Group," coil processing line manufacturer, Athader, told /SMR.

"Based in Spain, we specialise in the design and manufacturing of slitting lines; cut-to-length levelling lines with roller levellers and stretch levellers, with our partner Leveltek International, recoiling lines and coil/sheet automated packaging systems," it continued.

2023 has seen the highest order intake in the history of the company with an important number of new lines and revamps especially for Mexico, Eastern Europe and the UK. In 2023 and 2024, North America (USA, Canada and Mexico) has been its number one market.

The Bradbury Group is one of the world's largest manufacturers of rollforming lines, coil processing facilities, flattening systems and automated production systems.
