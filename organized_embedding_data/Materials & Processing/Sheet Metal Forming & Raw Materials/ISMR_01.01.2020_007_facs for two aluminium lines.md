# FACs for two aluminium lines

[TARİH: 01.01.2020 ISMR]

ON 19 NOVEMBER 2019, Tenova and Chalco Ruimin Co., Ltd, a company belonging to Aluminium Corporation of China (Chinalco), one of the world's largest aluminium producers, officially signed the Final Acceptance Certificates (FACs) for a continuous annealing line and a chemical pre-treatment line for processing aluminium strip coil. The new plant is in the Fuzhou Economic Technical Development Area, on China's South Eastern Coast.

The two new lines process automotive and aerospace industry grade aluminium alloys (series 5XXX, 6XXX and 7XXX) with strip widths ranging from 1,000 up to 2,200mm and thickness ranging from 0.5 up to 4mm.

"The total production rate for the continuous annealing line is 100,000 tons per year with a process speed of 80 m/min, whereas the chemical line production rate is 120,000 tons per year, with a process speed of 60 m/min.," confirmed the companies.

Tenova commissioned and started both lines ahead of the planned schedule.

“In the next couple of years, several additional lines we are currently supplying will start their production, further strengthening our position," commented Nicola Cavero, Senior Vice President, Tenova Italimpianti
