PRODUCTS MACHINED from large solid blocks of aluminium often suffer with reduced strength properties, potentially leading to catastrophic failure during service. Alloy Heat Treatment (AHT), a subcontractor in heat-treating aluminium, restores optimum strength properties to proof machined products using re-heat treatment, inspection, testing and release to the required temper.

AHT has the capacity to heat-treat automotive and aircraft products up to 2.5m x 2m x 1.9m and 5 tonnes gross weight in its drop bottom facility, located at its 70,000 sq. ft. Dudley site. In addition to providing straightforward heat treatment

Aerospace component post-heat treatment

To manufacturers and machinists, AHT also offers hardening and softening heat treatments to material stockists and suppliers who have difficulty in sourcing material in the required temper.

A quench distortion correction function, termed as “setting”, endorsed by Nadcap, automotive OEMs and suppliers is an added value option. The setting enables freshly quenched products to be manipulated to customer-supplied checking aids or to specified machining tolerances. The setting process is further supplemented by a refrigeration process, allowing for storage of freshly quenched products at temperatures as low as -29oC.
