# About Salzgitter Mannesmann Stahlhandel GmbH

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

Salzgitter Mannesmann Stahlhandel GmbH, as a stockholding trading company with decentralised structures, maintains a presence throughout the entire market with warehouses and sales locations, in keeping with its motto 'Systems, Service, Steel.' In addition to a comprehensive range of steel products, rolled steel, tube products and tube accessories in all dimensions and grades, the company's technology in warehouses enables most forms of processing for final assembly.

The production site in Zeithain manufactures parts of various thicknesses and lengths. Most of these are up to 40mm thick, with dimensions of up to 300 x 500mm. This is fine for ARKU's machines: using its oscillating grinding drum, the EdgeBreaker® deburrs and rounds even thick sheets on both sides in a single pass. The machine also handles stubborn burrs and slag produced by flame and plasma cutters.

"When it comes to levelling, the FlatMaster® 140 roller leveller processes materials of up to 60mm thick and 2000mm wide which run through multiple rollers in the machine. This creates alternating bending movements that reshape uneven areas. The process levels flame-cut parts and eliminates internal stresses within seconds. Subsequent processes (such as welding, bending or chamfering) all benefit from the condition of the materials. This enables work to be carried out faster and with greater process reliability," ARKU told ISMR.

Salzgitter Mannesmann Stahlhandel ordered both machines. In Zeithain, it is now able to safeguard efficient and customer-oriented production. ARKU also assisted with the installation of the machines.

"ARKU ensured that we were thoroughly prepared for the expansion of our machine park," explained Götze. "The service technicians carefully inspected our existing infrastructure of blasting, plasma cutting and oxyfuel cutting systems and installation of the new machinery in advance. This allowed us to make the necessary preparations.

As a result, actual installation was completed quickly, enabling the company to successfully get to work with its new machines. The EdgeBreaker® 4000 processes around 100,000 parts per year for SMSD.

New features and markets

After the company had commissioned the new equipment, it soon went on to make further upgrades. The EasyBot is a handling robot that loads and unloads the EdgeBreaker® and enables autonomous deburring. A key feature of this robot is its extreme flexibility which also allows it to automatically move small and medium batches.

The robot moved into the Zeithain production hall at the end of 2019. The move was preceded by numerous analyses which were in favour of the robot - with convincing results.

"We compared the robot's performance with manual loading and the magnetic crane that moves our parts," Götze reported. "The robot works much faster than any worker and saves colleagues from this extremely fatiguing activity."

The metal panels are often heavy, which means back-breaking work for the employees. The analysis clearly showed that using a robot to handle the parts would help Salzgitter Mannesmann Stahlhandel significantly with deburring.

The levelling machine currently processes 25,000 panels per year. Although the system is still loaded manually, it then makes all the necessary adjustments automatically. Verifying the material flatness still plays an important part in the levelling process, which is why colleagues check the levelled sheets with a straight-edge. Despite all the automation, employees continue to play a central role at Salzgitter Mannesmann Stahlhandel. Overall, ARKU has significantly accelerated the levelling process at SMSD. "We used to level far fewer parts because all the steps were done by hand. Eventually, this manual work meant that we were no longer able to fully satisfy our customers' timing requirements. Now, we are working at a completely different pace," confirmed Götze.

SMSD has not only achieved a new level of quantity but also of quality.

"Our customers truly appreciate the deburred parts with their low internal stress," added Götze. "Our own demanding quality standards for our products are opening up new markets for us. Since we have begun working with the new levelling and deburring technology, our customers perceive us even more clearly as an important part of their value chain. It also translates to a reliable guarantee for the quality of their end products. Ultimately, this also gives them a competitive advantage, in addition to production reliability."

## An eye on the future

Automation has also created changes for SMSD employees, or "from muscle power to brain power" as Götze puts it. Instead of lifting sheets themselves, as they had in the past, now the employees just control the technological processes that do this. The EdgeBreaker® operator requests parts, configures the deburring machine and robot, and keeps an eye on both machines. The robot then handles the lifting work. Salzgitter is therefore implementing automation in its purest form and enjoying its many advantages.

The prefabrication specialist is also increasing the efficiency of its production whilst creating 'an interesting and changing working environment' for its employees. At the same time, this step also significantly reduces noise and dust emissions. The EdgeBreaker® now sees so much use in a single shift that Götze is even considering a two-shift operation. Because of the EasyBot®, SMSD employees can now focus on specific benefits.

"Employee A operates the deburring machine and employee B operates the cutting system. No-one has to shut down machines, walk around and load or remove parts. This increases the uptime of the various machines and we produce more, as result," confirmed Salzgitter.

Although the EasyBot, with its single gripper arm, has no human features at all, it still succeeded in immediately becoming a part of the team. Götze explained that staff, particularly the younger generation, were enthusiastic about working with the new technology. Training courses by the manufacturer paved the way.

"Before the robot arrived, our colleagues already knew how to work with it," explained the product manager for Preprocessing. "The training created the necessary acceptance for exceptional innovation."

Beyond the machines themselves, big changes are coming to Zeithain. There is a clear focus on networked industry and transparent processes.

## About ARKU

ARKU was founded in 1928 as a family-owned company and is a market specialist in roller levellers and press-feeding technology, with more than 50 years of experience. It offers an extensive range of high-capacity and precision levellers, as well as deburring and edge rounding machines. This extensive portfolio is completed with the addition of parts handling solutions for levelling and deburring machines. The product range features precision levellers, deburring and edge rounding machines for parts; automated parts handling via robots; in-line levellers; cut-to-length lines; press feeding lines and coil entry lines for roll formers. With headquarters in Baden-Baden, Germany, it maintains ISO-certified subsidiaries in Kunshan (China) and Cincinnati (USA), serving markets in almost 30 countries.

"Customers now expect more than short delivery times and flawless products. They want to be constantly updated on their suppliers' production and stock levels - via digital devices and in real-time," explained Götze. He is excited by digital changes, which represent the next major trend.

"We have already launched our next project. We are working intensively on a networked production solution that includes the ARKU machines," he added.

This will make it easy to quickly monitor energy consumption, capacity utilisation and productivity. It is good news for SMDS customers, but mainly for Salzgitter Mannesmann Stahlhandel which fully appreciates the importance of critical data nowadays, especially when it means a major leap forward on the path towards highly efficient manufacturing.
