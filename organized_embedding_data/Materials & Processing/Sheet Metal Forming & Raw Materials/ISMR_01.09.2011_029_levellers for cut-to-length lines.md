# LEVELLERS FOR CUT-TO-LENGTH LINES

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

## The leveller determines the essential quality features of products manufactured on cut-to-length lines. Becker-Stahl-Service chose Schnutz levellers for its line.

BECKER, LOCATED IN BÖNEN, Germany is one of Europe’s largest steel service centre companies. The Becker range of products includes broad strips, slit strips, metal sheets, cuttings, trapezia and circular blanks. With 400 employees, Becker processes approximately 1 million tons of steel flat products annually. Becker maintains a 230,000-ton capacity warehouse for raw and finished materials. It also operates four cut-to-length lines and five slitting lines among others in 64,000 m² of hall space.

The latest cut-to-length line was commissioned in 2009 at its factory in Bönen/North. This cut-to-length line is manufactured by Schuler and can process strips with a thickness of between 0.4mm and 4.0mm and a width of up to 2,000mm. Depending on the thickness of the sheet, it can process steels with a yield strength exceeding 500 N/mm².

Since Becker first chose a Schnutz leveller back in 1984, all Becker cut-to-length lines have been equipped with levellers from this manufacturer. As a specialist in levellers and strip feeding systems, Schnutz supplies its customers with tailored solutions. The levellers can be delivered as components in new systems or integrated into existing systems as part of a modernisation or system upgrade. The modern leveller is equipped with programmable controls which command different interfaces and enable a simple connection to the available system controls.

“System speeds of up to 80 m/min guarantees high productivity. The operator can enter all required settings using a touch screen or, alternatively, two joysticks. Optimal settings can be saved and recalled when needed so that individual adjustments are reduced to a minimum,” Schnutz told ISMR.

To influence the levelling results, the operator can set the inlet and outlet gap of the material being levelled into the levelling roll cassette from the beginning. Corresponding preparations ensure that the curvatures of the strip and the residual tensions are continuously and simultaneously reduced when running through the leveller.

The levelling rolls can be purposefully worked over their width to remove edge and middle waves from a strip. For this purpose, the levelling machines delivered to Becker possess new support rows which support the widths of the lower and upper levelling rolls. The operator is able to adjust the height of the individual lower support roll using electric motors. The automation of the levelling machine makes it easier for the operator and ensures that inadmissible settings are not carried out.

For the ability to adjust to a range of products, the levelling machine features the exchangeable cartridge system that Schnutz has been perfecting for over 20 years. The work rolls, which are in direct contact with the strip, the intermediate rolls and the support rolls are built into an exchangeable cassette, which also comprises the universal shafts that drive the levelling rolls.

The complete cassette can be pulled out of the levelling machine on the operator’s side. The levelling machine delivered to Becker is also equipped with a hydraulic tensioning system. The cassette can thus be changed quickly without loosening screw joints.

A roll-changing car with space for two cassettes is also included in the Becker scope of supply. Automatic cassette recognition allows the levelling machine to automatically recognise the diameter of the respective levelling roll currently inserted, making a manual intervention on the part of the operator unnecessary.

Should the levelling rolls become worn-out, Schnutz offers an overhaul of levelling roll cassettes as part of its service.

It has set up a database which records the history of all the machines and plant it has delivered. Only in this way, it says, can competent service be ensured after decades of operation.
