# An eye on coil

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Italian coil-processing and bending specialist, Gabella Macchine SpA, will be exhibiting in Hall 8 on stand 8417 at Blechexpo 2023 in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023).

"This exhibition will give us the opportunity to showcase the latest technological developments in our sheetmetalworking lines. In particular, visitors will be able to see a distinctive Gabella Macchine slitter with automatically positioned heads," the family-owned company told ISMR.

"We can customise systems to the requirements of each customer. This synergy with end users underpins the solutions that will be on display on our stand in Stuttgart," it continued.

The company recently installed two customised coil processing solutions to customers in Germany and Portugal. The lines can process thin to high-thickness sheet metal and have been developed with a special focus on slitting and recoiling precision as well as speed. They can handle coils of up to 25 tons.

"However, the European market also requires rapid solutions for loading and changing lighter coils. Here, the challenge is for high levels of flexibility in small-batch production using a wide range of materials. A variety of coil types can be on standby for this process," explained the manufacturer.

This has led the company, over the years, to develop sliding platforms and trolleys, multi-positional rotary decoilers and various types of customised coil-storage systems.
