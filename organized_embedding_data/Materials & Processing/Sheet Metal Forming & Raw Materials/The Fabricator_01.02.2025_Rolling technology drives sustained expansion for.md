# Rolling technology drives sustained expansion for plate fabricating company

[TARİH: 01.02.2025 The Fabricator]

Applications

Situation

Family-owned enterprise Winnebago Manufacturing Co. offers large-scale metal fabrication, plate and bar rolling, machining, welding, and finishing services to a diverse range of industries. Its facility in Blue Earth, Minn., houses two Faccin four-roll plate bending machines—a 4HEL-2122 model for thin-gauge materials and a 4HEL-3139 model with top and side supports for medium-thickness plates—as well as a Roundo threeroll angle roll R-62 model for profile and beam bending.

With customer demand increasing for rolling thicker steel plates with greater precision and efficiency, the company realized its existing equipment was limiting its ability to handle plates beyond specific thickness and size constraints.

Based on its positive track record with equipment from Faccin Group (which includes the Faccin, Roundo, and Boldrini brands), along with discussions involving engineers and operations managers, Winnebago decided to partner with Faccin again to find equipment to help the company meet evolving customer demands efficiently.

Resolution

After evaluating several options, Winnebago chose a Faccin three-roll plate bending machine that handles materials up to 10 ft. wide. The process from decision-making to installation took about six months, with Faccin’s technicians ensuring a smooth installation and providing comprehensive training for the Winnebago team. Within days, the machine was fully operational.

The three-roll plate bending machine is now a cornerstone of Winnebago Manufacturing’s operations. It allows the company to roll materials like high-strength steel grades up to 2.5 in. thick in larger diameters, opening new opportunities to take on more complex projects with stringent quality standards, such as in the construction and agriculture machinery industries.

With its advanced CNC, the machine helps ensure fast setup, as well as improved accuracy and efficiency. Faster setup times and reduced cycle times have improved throughput, allowing the company to complete projects quickly to meet tighter production schedules.

"The addition of Faccin’s three-roll plate bending machine has been a game-changer for our business. We’ve seen significant improvements in efficiency and quality, enabling us to meet customer demands like never before," said Travis Pomranke, sales manager at Winnebago Manufacturing. "This investment positions us for long-term growth and strengthens our competitive edge."
