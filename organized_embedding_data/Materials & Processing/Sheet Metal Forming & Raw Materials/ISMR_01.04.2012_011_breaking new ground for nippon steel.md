# BREAKING NEW GROUND for Nippon Steel

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

NIPPON STEEL Corporation established in June last year Nippon Steel Galvanizing (Thailand) Co., Ltd. (“NSGT”) for the manufacture and sale of hot-dip galvanized and galvannealed steel sheets. NSGT held a groundbreaking ceremony for the business on 27 February 2012. The ceremony took place at the construction site in the Hemaraj Eastern industrial estate, Rayong Province, Thailand.

NSGT plans to build facilities at this site adjoining SUS, thus structuring an integrated control setup downstream of cold rolling.

“We shall continue building manufacturing equipment and facilities, with the objective of precisely responding to customer needs for high-grade automotive steel sheets and others, in time for the scheduled start of commercial operations in October 2013,” the company told ISMR. ■
