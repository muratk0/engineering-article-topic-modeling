# Abrasive grinding wheels

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

3M has introduced a range of bonded abrasive grinding wheels into the European market which offer an innovative approach to the grinding process.

“Employing the advanced abrasive grain technology, precision shaped grain, introduced with the Cubitron II abrasive belts and discs, the new grinding wheels cut faster, last longer and require significantly less pressure than conventional grinding wheels. This results in increased productivity and reduced operator fatigue,” 3M explained.

Suitable for heavy weld removal as well as numerous other grinding applications, Cubitron II bonded abrasive grinding wheels deliver extremely high cut rates on a variety of metals. They can be used across a wide range of industries, including general metal fabrication, ship-building, structural steel, oil and gas, agriculture and construction machinery.

The irregularity of conventional ceramic grain results in it ‘ploughing’ through metal, causing a build-up of heat and resulting in a slower cutting rate; this directly affects product durability, potentially resulting in shorter life. In contrast, precision shaped grain technology features precisely shaped, uniformly sized and vertically oriented triangles of ceramic abrasive. These self-sharpening structures fracture as they wear, forming new, super-sharp points and edges which slice cleanly through metal, rather than gouging or ‘ploughing’, allowing the grinding wheel to stay cool and last longer. This has the potential to deliver significant advantages in productivity and consistency, as well as longer service life.

“Cubitron II products substantially reduce the amount of pressure an operator needs to apply compared with traditional grinding wheels made from aluminium oxide, zirconia and ceramic grain,” added 3M.
