# ECCA Premium® status allocation

[TARİH: 01.02.2015 ISMR]

THE EUROPEAN Coil Coating Association (ECCA) has granted its ECCA Premium® status to six ArcelorMittal products. ECCA’s Premium® labelling scheme, which it launched last year, assures users of pre-painted metal that they are getting quality and sustainable products.

Manufactured at the ArcelorMittal Flat Carbon Europe plant in Liège, Belgium, the ArcelorMittal products now carrying the ECCA Premium® label are: the Granite® Standard, Granite® HD, Granite® HDS, Granite® HDX, Granite® Deep Mat 35 Microns and Granite® Flex, a range of high performance external steel coatings from the company’s Nature collection.

“The ECCA Premium® Quality and Sustainability label represents value and peace of mind for users of pre-painted aluminium and steel products by assuring colour durability, optimum corrosion resistance and suitability for creative design applications. The scheme also guarantees that the metal used is sourced and manufactured in a safe and environmentally responsible way,” explained ECCA.

The label is granted only to coil coaters who have successfully passed inspections performed by duly certified, independent auditing bodies and demonstrates manufacturers’ dedication to quality and sustainability. The first two companies to which ECCA granted its ECCA Premium® Quality and Sustainability label were Hydro Aluminium Rolled Products and Novelis Italia. ECCA plans to inspect a further six ArcelorMittal sites over the next few weeks.

A list of ECCA Premium® specification, labelled products and inspectors is available on the ECCA Premium® website www.eccapremium.com

## TIG and MMA welding

PARWELD IS a specialist in the manufacture and development of welding equipment. The New Parweld 320A XTI 353 AC/DC Tig Inverter with MMA is the latest addition to its expanding range.

“The XTI 353 can be used in both AC and DC modes for TIG or MMA welding allowing the welding of virtually any metal. Square wave AC output and power pulsing maximises productivity whilst reducing heat input. The majority of steels can be quickly welded in DC mode using the pulse power function.

The AC mode allows the welding of aluminium and magnesium alloys. The square wave output with adjustable frequency maximises the arc energy, offering high welding speeds when coupled with the power pulsing function,” explained Parweld.

The XTI 353P AC/DC can cope with inputs which fluctuate by +/-15% and can be used with the majority of generators. It conforms to ISO/IEC60974-1 and ISO/IEC60974-6, as well as being RoHs-compliant. www.parweld.co.uk

## Smart marking

A NEW product from SCANLAB AG, the ‘little brother’ to the SCANcube, SCANLAB's new basiCube 10 scan head has been specifically refined for classic laser-marking requirements. Specifically optimised for basic marking applications, the new scan head is around 15% smaller than the SCANcube 10.

“Users gain a very high speed scan system perfectly tailored to their requirements, with a 10mm aperture and laser marking optimised tuning,” explained SCANLAB CEO, Georg Hofner.

The new scan head is available with silicon mirrors for three laser wavelengths.
