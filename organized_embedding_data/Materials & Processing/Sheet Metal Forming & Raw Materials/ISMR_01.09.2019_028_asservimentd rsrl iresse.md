## IMS MESSSYSTEME GmbH HALL 10 www.ims-gmbh.de/?lang=en

ESTABLISHED IN 1980, IMS develops and produces isotope, X-ray and optical measurement systems for industrial use in the steel, aluminium and metal industries. Its head office is in Heiligenhaus, Germany, and it currently has a workforce of approx. 450 employees on five continents.

"Since the successful introduction of more than 4,000 inline-measurement systems worldwide, our systems guarantee (especially in slitting lines) reduced leadtimes, scrap ratio and quality management in customer systems. Our new CAMERA CLUSTER-SYSTEMS (CCS) measure geometry (length, width, out of squareness, camber); thickness; strip slit width; flatness; surface defects; pinhole and edge defects. All measurement and process dimensions can be stored in a quality data management System," said the company.

"We have also developed a coil face measurement system to assess coils. This system uses a triangulation laser to detect the transverse contour. Once the coil has been positioned, it records the state of the winding profile vertically across the complete height of the coil. Our surface inspection system (Surcon X-3Dvision) inspects the tops and bottoms of blasted plates for defects. It offers automatic detection and classification of relevant defects, their position and geometric data."
