# Integrated solution concept for roll formers

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

BAVARIAN COMPANY, data M Sheet Metal Solutions, presented an integrated solution concept for roll forming at EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover. It showcased innovative soft- and hardware products for roll formers. Among other things, the fully integrated solution concept was demonstrated using the example of a roll formed bumper. All assembly steps, including stretch bending, can be processed with COPRA® products. In recent years, data M has further refined its product portfolio and particularly focused on the consistent interconnectedness of the various COPRA® solutions.

Company engineers demonstrated how the set-up of a new roll formed profile is possible with the COPRA® RF design software, the COPRA® RollScanner as well as the RLM roll tool database. With the new "AssemblyCheck" feature, the design will be instantly updated after modification of the forming stations with subsequent scans of the roll tools. The designer can get an overview on the state of the modified tools, in mounted condition, compared to the initial design. For this purpose, the designed as well as the scanned roll contour will automatically show overlaying in COPRA® RF.

"The portable COPRA® ProfileScan Desktop provides accurate measurement of the profile after each station. Consequently, the designers can verify if the profile matches the designed contour after each forming step. With this method, potential deviations can be located immediately and not only after the profile is finished – which involves a tiresome search for the source of error," said the manufacturer.

COPRA® FEA RF not only simulates the roll forming process, but also handles the complete production process for modern and complex profiles. Nowadays, pre- and postoperations, such as stamping, embossing or welding, are as important as the rollforming itself. An accurate simulation of the roll formed base profile is key for additional calculations such as stretch bending, since strains and stresses during roll forming may influence the results significantly.

The simulation module identifies and visualises possible defects such as strip edge waviness or unwanted collisions between the roll and strip edge during the (virtual) forming process. With the results, the new roll design can be instantly implemented and verified. The new COPRA® FEA RF 2017 version is equipped with Marc/Mentat 2015 and contains an integrated model browser. The company has also introduced new import functions. Besides IGES, new formats such as STEP and SAT or native formats from COPRA® Inventor® can be imported and edited.
