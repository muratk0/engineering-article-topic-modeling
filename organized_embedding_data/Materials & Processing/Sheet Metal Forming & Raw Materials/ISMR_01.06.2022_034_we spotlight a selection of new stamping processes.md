# We spotlight a selection of new stamping processes, technologies and press models.

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

According to a new report by analyst Technavio, the global metal stamping market will post incremental growth of US$ 25.62 billion from 2021-2025, progressing at a CAGR of almost 3.54% during the forecast period.

Stamping and pressing technology was built on the foundations of blacksmithing. Metal stamping, or pressing, today is a manufacturing process used to convert flat metal sheets, or blanks, into specific shapes. It is a complex process that includes several metal forming techniques — blanking, punching, flanging, bending, embossing, coining and piercing, for example. The process uses tools, dies and stamping presses to transform sheet metal into different shapes.

Stamping presses utilise the force of a moving ram to transmit force, or an amount of tonnage, to specific tooling to achieve a product's final shape, often with little or no scrap and, whenever possible, with minimal operator intervention. Stamping dies are the tools that shape and cut sheet metal parts.

Some of the most common types of metal stamping machines include mechanical, hydraulic and servo presses that are fitted with multiple dies to cut and shape the sheets of stainless steel and metals such as aluminium, zinc and copper. There are three major types of metal stamping techniques: progressive, fourslide (multi-slide) and deep draw. Greater speed and versatility, shorter cycle times and higher energy savings are just some of the hallmarks of the latest technologies.
