Repkon has spent many years refining the free flowforming process and intends to present it as a viable alternative forming option at the forthcoming EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover as well as at its Open House that follows. Gurhan Bilgin, Board Member and Business Development Director, believes that there is a myriad of applications and markets (such as the pipe industry and gas sector) for the technology.

“Our revolutionary “free” flowforming model has opened-up vast opportunities in metal forming to present a commercially viable alternative technique against the widely used chip-removing modus operandi,” said Bilgin.

“Free flowforming, along with patented follow-on developments, such as ‘free angle’ and ‘inside roller’, provides a great perspective for the future of the flowforming process, because the possibility of applications for new design work pieces is now attainable with high efficiency and top quality.”

“Flowforming has a great deal of new potential for new processing applications and new parts,” said Mr Köstermeier. ”You can make contours and the process is far more flexible. Our aim was to reduce the friction between the part and the mandrel to improve efficiency. The material does not heat up – a great advantage when forming parts.

“We found that we did not need to use the mandrel to support the part. Instead, we added an inside roller with differing contours and an adjustable axial position to the outside roller. This means that we can form parts with different contours without mandrels. In short, a much more flexible process using the inner roller. A CNC control enables you to make adjustments during the forming process to reduce the weight of parts etc. With flowforming, you usually produce the part you need with no need for additional secondary operations during or after the process.”

Founded in 1978, Repkon is a sheet metal forming machinery and turnkey solution provider, offering complete turnkey production plants for the manufacture of metal products, such as wheels, high/low pressure gas cylinders, panel radiators and steel drums. Its technological capabilities include flowforming; shear forming; hot spinning; hydraulic presses as well as tooling and high level automation for industries such as automotive, mining, energy and defence.

Repkon recently acquired the wheel butt welding division of the Swiss welding giant, Schlatter. It also recently entered into an alliance with Siderest in Italy to offer complete high pressure gas cylinder solutions (from pipe and plate material).

Traditionally, Repkon’s markets have been Europe (including Russia and the former Soviet Union), Middle East and Northern Africa. However, it has now decided to exploit other markets in the Far East such as China, Indonesia, India etc. The Far East particularly is a booming market and South America, Bilgin says, is also very attractive.

“We also see China as a perfect environment for us to explore new applications for our technology as it is so large and so diverse. The country is open to new ideas and willing to incorporate new technologies into its production so there is plenty of opportunity in that market,” Bilgin told ISMR.
