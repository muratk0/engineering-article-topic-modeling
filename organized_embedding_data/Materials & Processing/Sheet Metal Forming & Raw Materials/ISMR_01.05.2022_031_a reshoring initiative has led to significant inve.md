# A reshoring initiative has led to significant investment in tube processing technology at Pendle Engineering.

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Pendle Engineering started as a two-man business in 1977 and has grown steadily into a subcontract supplier of steelwork, with a focus on tube and flat sheet manipulation along with turning, milling, robotic and manual Mig/Tig welding as well as powder paint and design capabilities. Along the way, it has also branched out into manufacturing a range of bicycle racks, trailers and storage under the name of its sister company, Pendle Bike Racks.

All this capability under one roof allows Pendle Engineering to offer turnkey manufacturing solutions to its customers, who come from a wide selection of industries such as office furniture, energy, retail and defence. These customers range from SMEs to global blue-chip companies where Pendle's ability to deliver consistent, high-quality batch production has created strong relationships.

To ensure that any growth could be met with improved efficiency, the decision was taken to enhance its tube working capabilities, bringing in additional BLM ELECT 52 and E-TURN 32 tube benders alongside a BLM Laser Tube LT-FIBER EVO fibre optic, fully automated laser tube cutting system.

## Increasing capacity

"Business has been increasing, especially with increased orders for Pendle Bike Racks, so we knew we had to upgrade our existing tube processing capacity," said Chris Smith, Managing Director, Pendle Engineering.

"We had a history with BLM for tube bending over many years so when it came to upgrading our 15 year-old tube laser it made sense to talk to them and put a bending and laser package together. A major advantage in doing that was the ability of the controls on the BLM benders and the Laser Tube to communicate with each other. The ease of use of BLM's software and offline programming was also a significant factor. The less time a machine is idle, the better," he added.

The BLM Laser Tube LT-FIBER EVO is available with either a 2kW or 3kW fibre laser and the capacity to cut round tube of up to 152.4mm diameter or square tube up to 120 x 120mm section. Productivity is enhanced by the standard bundle loading facility which can handle up to 4000kg of material to a maximum length of 8.5 metres. BLM Group has also developed several patented technologies to maximise productivity.

“These include a series of 'Active-Tools', such as Active-Focus, which makes automatic adjustments to compensate for variations in material thicknesses. Active-Scan ensures accurate parts (even with tubes that are poorly dimensioned), Active-Speed adapts all the cutting data to achieve the highest quality cut at the highest output while Active-Pierce boosts piercing reliability and speed, especially on thicker materials," explained BLM.

"The BLM Laser Tube LT-FIBER EVO machine's cutting capabilities are enhanced by software systems from BLM Group. A range of systems enhance production, including the patented Artube 3D CAD/CAM system which is designed to take users from concept to finished part as easily as possible. ProTube Express and Enterprise are also tools for production control, estimating and general management functions. All BLM systems are compatible with Industry 4.0 requirements allowing them to be integrated not only with other BLM machines but also automation and other compatible technology," it added.

## Productivity in focus

"We already seeing the benefits of increased productivity, even though we are not running at 100 per cent yet with the BLM Laser Tube LT-FIBER EVO. It is faster, the nesting capability brings a reduction in waste and we can cut more complex components yet the operation of the machine and software is much simpler, which will allow us to introduce a third shift, as and when required," said Chris Smith. "We're looking forward to fully understanding the potential capability and capacity before driving growth."

The two new BLM tube benders completed the machine package and give additional capability to Pendle Engineering.

“Our existing E-TURN 40 gave us good left- and right-hand bending capacity, but the new E-TURN 32 gives us greater flexibility for smaller diameter tube of up to 30mm in diameter, while the ELECT 52 provides a costeffective solution for tube of up to 50mm in diameter," commented Smith.

“Bending is critical to what we do, so we need a reliable supplier for machines, service and support. In total, the investment in the BLM laser and tube benders was over £1- million and it was a big decision for us, but we are here for the long term. Having this capability ensures that we can catch any opportunities that come our way, especially as we talk to customers about reshoring and recovery from the pandemic. It was definitely a decision worth taking and we are already seeing business improving," he concluded.■
