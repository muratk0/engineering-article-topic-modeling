# Sheet metal processing

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

WITH A HISTORY dating back to the 1950s, Turkish manufacturer MVD Inan produces mechanical press brakes and guillotine shears. At EuroBLECH 2018, the sheet metal specialist will exhibit press brakes, fibre laser cutting systems, plasma cutting systems, guillotine shears and punching machines. Catch the company in Hall 14 (Stand H42).

"Quality and post-sales support are two important cohesive elements of our work (supporting a low-quality machine is not a solution, just as quality machines without support are of no use to the customer)," it said. " We are fully committed to our machines' quality and long-term functionality. Our factory service line and sales and service office in Istanbul offer customers ongoing post-sales support."
