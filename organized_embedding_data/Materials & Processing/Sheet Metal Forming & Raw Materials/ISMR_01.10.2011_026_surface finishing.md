## COATING AND SURFACE TREATMENT

“Surface finishers have many techniques at their disposal. The coating materials can be conveyed to the surface with the help of a liquid carrier, and the surface can be sprayed, spread or dipped. The materials can be applied passively, or can be joined to the surface with the aid of a chemical or an electrochemical reaction. Or the materials can be applied directly, with no carrier medium at all, in the form of a heated spray or an ionised plasma,” said the US National Metal Finishing Resource Center.

Electroplating is an electrochemical process which deposits a metallic coating on the base material of an object by immersing it in a properly designed solution and applying a suitable low voltage electric current flow which causes the metal ions to be attracted to and deposited upon the object being plated. Common electroplated coatings include brass, nickel, cadmium, copper, silver, tin and zinc.

“Natural resources are conserved both because surface coatings preserve products, as an irreplaceable weapon in the war against corrosion, and because rare and costly materials can be used only on surfaces to impart desired physical properties while a more abundant and less costly material can be used as the base,” said the NASF.

Edge finishing is a new and deeper focus on what used to be termed de-burring, edge honing, edge preparation, edge prepping, burring, chamfering or edge blending. De-burring and edge-finishing processes add many benefits to parts – they create highly desirable edge quality.

“De-burring and edge finishing are still areas that are under-served by modern technology. There is a lack of detailed data and models for burr removal and edge radiusing. Additional research is required to fill this need. Such research is relatively low cost and involves more application orientation than traditional research. The existence of such data and models will reduce the cost of burrs and increase flow through factories,” said LaRoux Gillespie in the description of his book ‘De-burring and Edge Finishing: Research and Other Needs to Make Industry More Profitable‘.

“In addition to research, more effective promotion of de-burring based on existing knowledge also is needed to allow more effective process decisions. The field of de-burring lacks case histories of successes and problems. De-burring lacks commercial software to facilitate process selection and use, and few equipment vendors have defined the capability of their equipment. Standardising terminology and providing international standards related to burrs will also accelerate better practices.

“Removing burrs from micro-sized parts is one of the biggest issues facing producers. For some parts, burr removal represents 30 per cent of the cost of fabrication. Because of part size and precision, only a few of the 122 de-burring processes are directly applied to micro-sized parts and precision tolerances.”

Vibro de-burring takes off the sharp edges, improves surface finish, polishes parts and makes them fit for assembly and plating.

## SURFACE ANALYSIS

The composition and structure of the outermost layers of a surface have a profound effect on the properties and performance of materials and systems in an increasingly wide range of technological and nano-technological applications. Analyses of the compositional, chemical and physical properties of these first few atomic layers have proved invaluable in the characterisation and understanding of the surface properties of materials. Surface analysis can identify potential issues or areas of application for customers, such as:

■ Looking at the surface issues that affect adhesion
■ Assessing the performance capability of catalyst
■ Analysis of failure linked to surface changes
■ Identification of diffusion and segregation at surfaces
■ The surface performance of semi-conductors
■ Tribological surface/additive interactions
■ Metal passivity and corrosion studies
■ Identification of surface contaminants
■ Chemical state and oxidation assessment
■ Investigating material biocompatibility and biosystems.
