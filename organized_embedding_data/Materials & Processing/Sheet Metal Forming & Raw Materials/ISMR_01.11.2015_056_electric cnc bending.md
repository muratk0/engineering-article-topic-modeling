# Electric CNC bending

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

MANUFACTURER, Inductaflex, launched its first ever fully electric CNC bending machine at FABTECH in Chicago recently, North America's largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event. The AL-1 is designed to provide automotive, aerospace, commercial vehicle and other customers with a multiple-bend solution.

“AL-1 can bend most materials and profiles, from complicated aluminium extrusions to polished stainless steel tubes. During development, the machine has already demonstrated its capabilities by successfully curving a 45mm wide x 65mm high extruded aluminium window section to a 165mm outside radius and 120mm inside radius - a task that has traditionally been considered to be beyond normal limits for such an extrusion. Inductaflex therefore claims that, even before its FABTECH launch, the AL-1 is opening up new possibilities in aluminium glazing section design,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

The new Inductaflex unit is also capable of automatically curving up to 10 radii per bar and, according to company managing director Craig Barnshaw, can operate with running costs reduced by 70% in comparison to an equivalent hydraulic machine. For more details, see www.inductaflex.com ■
