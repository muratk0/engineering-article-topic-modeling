To address the skilled-worker shortage, FMA has assembled some of the metal fabricating industry’s best experts in today’s essential metal manufacturing technologies to develop curriculum and criteria to verify and validate critical skills. These training and certificate programs can help employers and employees verify skills developed through training and validate skills and knowledge learned through on-the-job experience. FMA offers training and credentials for all levels of experience within the industry.

Basic

training, designed for entry-level operators, covers the primary concepts. These programs are best-suited for college and technical school students, as well as individuals new to metal forming.

The brand-new

Bending With Press Brakes

workshop provides both classroom instruction and hands-on experience in sheet metal bending practices. Given individualized attention and expert coaching, attendees follow the steps from flat layout to finished part. (This course also is offered in Spanish.)

The

Fundamentals of Metal Fabrication

certificate program has been developed for students enrolled in metal forming programs. Upon completion of their school’s program, students can take the certificate exam to validate the skills they learned through classroom and hands-on instruction.

Intermediate

offerings tend to cover a specific machine or process. These seminars are generally one to two days long and include certificate testing at the end of the course. They are recommended for individuals with at least six months of shop experience, but can be valuable for all levels of experience.

Coil Processing teaches the theory of operation behind the slitting, leveling, shearing, and blanking processes. Instruction includes a comprehensive overview of coil slitting and leveling with an in-depth look at techniques and practices.

The

Lasers 101

curriculum addresses how a laser works, the role of the resonator, types of lasers in use, an overview of what each type of laser can do, and the different applications in which lasers can be used.

The

Precision Press Brake

seminar teaches the theory and math fundamentals behind the machine to uncover the secrets of working with press brakes. The principles of quality sheet metal bending are covered through interactive instruction and sample work problems.

Press Brake Safeguarding

focuses on current best practices in press brake safeguarding. The curriculum, based on real-world shop applications, studies the risk assessment process, OSHA requirements and ANSI standards, and application-specific safeguarding system selection.

Roll Forming

covers the history, terminology, and processes involved in roll forming. Attendees learn the definition of roll forming and understand the basic principles every operator needs to be effective.

FMA’s

advanced-level

Precision Sheet Metal Operator (PSMO) Certification

, the metal fabricating industry’s only comprehensive exam, is designed to assess a candidate’s knowledge of fundamental precision sheet metal operations. It is highly recommended that test-takers have at least two years of fabrication experience prior to attempting this exam. Study resources and review materials are available to help candidates improve their chance of success.
