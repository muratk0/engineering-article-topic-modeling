# Interface for panel coating line

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

HOW CAN you automate a second-hand powder coating line? That was the question posed to AWL-Techniek by a Dutch customer in early 2013. AWL accepted the challenge and has now built the entire interface for the line.

The 600m powder coating line consists of a running Power & Free track, with 17m crossbeams attached. Hooks are secured to the crossbeams to suspend panels that vary from 600 to 2500mm in length. The P&F track takes the panels through the coating station, a process that takes a couple of hours. The first line owner used to feed and remove panels manually, but the new owner demanded full automation of these processes. This was quite a challenge for AWL, as the product to be coated comes in different versions. The 17m crossbeams now move dynamically, with AWL taking control of the power and free system within range of the robots.

AWL has created a continuous flow in the process. Two forklift truck-fed tilting tables turn the panels towards the first robot. This robot secures a panel to a hook every 10 seconds. After 5 panels, the crossbeam moves the width of a panel and carries on suspending the next 5 panels. When the entire crossbeam is filled, it continues towards the coating station. After the panels are coated, a robot removes them from the hooks and places them on pallets. A hook management robot then replaces one of the hooks. It is very important for one of the two hooks to be clean at all times for conductivity reasons. This robot ensures that the hooks are always in the correct position.

Whereas AWL is usually known for integrating welding processes, this project just involves handling. Combining advanced customisation with the human machine interface means that one of Europe's largest powder coating lines can be operated by just two forklift truck operators.
