# Red Bud Hosts Queretaro Seminar

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

Red Bud Industries hosted a coil processing seminar at the Hotel Mision in Juriquilla Queretaro, Mexico, on 17 May 2011. The attendees learned about blanking, multi-blanking, leveling, slitting and a new process (EPS®) for hot roll black material along with learning how to apply new technology, improve tolerances, produce optimum flatness in material and maximise overall efficiency.
