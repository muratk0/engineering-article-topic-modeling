## We highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing technologies and innovations.

“New stamping processes, concepts and press models come under the spotlight in this article”

Metal stamping is a cold-forming process that makes use of dies and stamping presses to transform sheet metal into different shapes. Pieces of flat sheet metal, typically referred to as blanks, are fed into a sheet metal stamping press that uses a tool and die surface to form the metal into a new shape.

It is a complex process that can include a number of sheet metal forming techniques — blanking, punching, piercing, flanging, coining, bending and embossing, to name a few. Stamping presses utilise the force of a moving ram to transmit force, or an amount of tonnage, to specific tooling to achieve a product’s final shape, often with little or no scrap and, whenever possible, with minimal operator intervention. This could be a single-stage operation, where every stroke of the press produces the desired form on the sheet metal part or could occur through a ‘progressive’ series of stages.

A wide variety of parts can be formed in different press sizes and types. Systems include C-frame presses; automatic blanking presses; knuckle-joint presses; servo presses; hydroforming presses or conventional mechanical transfer/progressive die presses. Greater flexibility, shorter cycle times and a focus on energy conservation are just some of the hallmarks of the latest technologies. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing innovations.

## Forming in focus

AIDA is a global specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metal forming automation equipment. Its servo and mechanical stamping presses range in capacity from 30 to 4,000 tons, from 1 to 1,500 strokes per minute, from gap-frame to straight-side, high speed to cold forging, transfer and progressive die, material handling and coil-feeding equipment.

At the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (October 2018), it showcased its MSP high-speed stamping presses for the production of motors such as EV motors, HEV motors and energy-efficient home appliances motors. Dynamic accuracy has been significantly improved with their compact design and multiple suspension point architecture. The presses, which feature Adjust in Motion functionality, are suitable for production of parts with thicknesses of less than 0.20mm.

AIDA-America has released the next generation of its Allen-Bradley-based DSF Series servo press control for servo presses from 315 – 3,500 tons.

AP&T specialises in solutions for efficient sheet metal forming and press hardening of steel. Its new servo hydraulic press (for increased productivity in hot and cold forming) was the company’s main attraction at EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover, Germany. Renovating, maintaining and rebuilding press tools is also a growing business area at AP&T.

Over the past few years, the company has presented several innovations, such as a new production solution for complex parts made out of high-strength aluminium, so that the automotive industry can develop lighter, safer and more fuel-efficient cars.
