# Tata Steel at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

TATA STEEL will use this year’s EuroBLECH as a platform to highlight several extensions to its portfolio of steel products (Hall 17, Stand G38). Citing examples from its hot-rolled, cold-rolled and metallic coated coil product range, Europe’s second largest steel producer will demonstrate how the latest additions are helping to reduce customers’ manufacturing costs, support lightweighting and improve performance.

The steel producer is adding to its hot-rolled range with further portfolio extensions to support growing end-market needs. One example is its range of low sulphur clean steels which improve processability, offering advantages such as enhanced weldability and higher edge ductility.

The company has also developed a tailor-made range of High-Strength Low Alloy (HSLA) steels to support a variety of processing and performance requirements.

It has taken the same approach in cold-rolled strip products, expanding its product range by focusing on product advantages such as improved formability and strength. In this product line, Advanced and Ultra High-Strength steels (UHSS) enable lightweighting whilst maintaining existing performance levels. Developments in the company’s metallic coated range include products whose coating weight is reduced while maintaining or even improving coating durability.

New products include hot-dip galvanised steel developed to help the automotive industry achieve both crash-protection and lightweighting. Other developments have focused on dynamic car styling and lightweighting. For lifting & excavating customers looking for highly formable material that can bear heavy loads, Tata Steel is offering new developments in hot-rolled, high-strength steels. ■

Through the development of new steel chemistries, Tata Steel’s expanded range of heat treatable steels offer re-rollers predictable and consistent steel products
