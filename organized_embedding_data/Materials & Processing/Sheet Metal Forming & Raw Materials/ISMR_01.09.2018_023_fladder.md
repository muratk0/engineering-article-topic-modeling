## AIDA Srl

www.aida-europe.com

AIDA EUROPE is a subsidiary of AIDA Engineering Ltd. in Japan, founded in 1917. AIDA is a global specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metalforming automation equipment and is a global metalforming solutions provider.

"Our MSPs (Multi-Suspension Points Presses) enable users to produce high-precision and high value-added motors such as EV motors, HEV motors and energy-efficient home appliance motors. Dynamic accuracy is significantly improved with their compact design and multiple suspension points architecture. They tread the balance between high rigidity and wide bed area. Moving parts, such as the connecting rod bearings and crankshaft bearings, are all made from special metals specifically selected to avoid heat deformation" AIDA told ISMR.

AIDA MSPs are suitable for the production of parts with thicknesses of less than 0.20mm and offer Adjust In Motion (AIM) functionality.

## AP&T

www.aptgroup.com

AP&T SPECIALISES in solutions for efficient sheet metal forming and press hardening of steel. Its new servo hydraulic press will be the company's main attraction at this year's EuroBLECH.

The award-winning production solution will also be a focus on its booth at EuroBLECH. AP&T's booth will also display other parts of the company's overall offering, including presses, automation, tooling, furnaces and aftermarket services.

## ATHADER, S.L. www.athader.com

ATHADER IS a global producer of coil processing lines. In 2017, it celebrated its 25-year anniversary. The company's main products are automatic slitting lines; cut-to-length (CTL) lines; combined lines (slitting and cut-to-length); automatic banding and packaging lines for sheet and coils plus multi-blanking and recoiling lines.

"Continuous product innovation has allowed us to develop important projects for leading customers such as Tata Steel India, Bluescope Australia, ThyssenKrupp and Outokumpu," said the company.

In 2012, Athader became a member of The Bradbury Group (www.bradburygroup.com), a manufacturer of rollforming, coil processing, levelling and automated production systems. It has an extensive sales network, with manufacturing plants and after-sales service centres all over the world. The Bradbury Group comprises companies in North America, Europe, Asia and Oceania.
