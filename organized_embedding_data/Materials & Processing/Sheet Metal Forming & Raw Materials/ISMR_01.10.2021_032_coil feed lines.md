# Coil feed lines

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Automatic Feed Company (AFCO), which provides blanking and coil feed lines to the automotive industry, has announced its new PRO Series coil processing lines. The new press feed lines are aimed at Tier automotive suppliers which process UHSS, AHSS and aluminium for applications such as inner body panels and stamped parts (which are not surface-sensitive or surface-critical).

Based upon a standard design, each PRO Series line features a single reel and coil cart, peeler, straightener, threading table and press feed, designed for maintainability and reliability.

"Seventy-five per cent of the material being processed today by steel and aluminium mills did not exist ten years ago and suppliers are increasingly being relied upon to process a variety of materials, including high-strength advanced steels," said Joe Andriette, AFCO's director of sales and marketing. "With the PRO Series, we took our industry knowledge and equipment design experience and packaged it into a high-performing, reliable, right-sized solution for their particular needs." The new PRO Series systems are available in three sizes—the PRO 40, PRO 60 and PRO 80—and each can process coil stock from 4" up to 40", 60" or 80" (depending upon the model). All three sizes are capable of handling material thicknesses from 0.02" to 0.25" at up to 160FPM and can process aluminium with yield strengths of up to 350MPa and up to 1200MPa for steel. Each model can handle coil weight of up to 60,000# with an outer coil diameter of up to 80".

These systems incorporate AFCO's leveller technology and include a straightener calculator to assist the operator in making the necessary straightener setting adjustments for coil strip correction, helping to reduce scrap and downtime. The uncoiler reel features a servo motor for back tension control, which can be an issue when processing advanced steels.

"The PRO Series lines can directly feed coil stock to a mechanical stamping press or a laser blanking system. The controls package enables integration between the press and the coil feed line as well as a pedestal-mounted HMI with touch screen control," concluded AFCO.
