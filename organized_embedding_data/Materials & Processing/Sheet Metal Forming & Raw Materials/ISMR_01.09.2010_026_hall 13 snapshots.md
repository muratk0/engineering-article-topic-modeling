## ATTEXOR CLINCH SYSTEMS SA

CH– Hall 13, Stand D82

Website: www.clinchsystems.com The SPOT CLINCH and RIVCLINCH machines allow sheet metal and profiles to be joined with no fumes or sparks, at low maintenance costs with good reproducibility. This clinching process can join several metal sheets (coated or painted) of different thicknesses and of different materials. The join, produced by the interaction of a die and a punch, is hard and water-resistant. ATTEXOR Clinch Systems and BÖLLHOFF, its partner in Europe, offer standard and customised products.

## CEMSA SPA

CEMSA S.p.A. is a leader in resistance welding (spot, projection, seam and flash-butt). It studies and designs special systems with a flexible approach. Resistance, TIG, plasma and laser welding cells, combined with CN motion and/or anthropomorphic management (including loading/unloading units), are a constant application in sectors such as metallic furniture, safety doors and lifts; automotive, railway and aerospace; HVAC; gratings and household appliances.
