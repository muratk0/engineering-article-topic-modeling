# Multi-metal pre-treatment

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

TO meet today’s demands for a cost efficient, low temperature process with unlimited throughput of aluminium substrates, Chemetall has launched the advanced GardoFlex pre-treatment system to the global automotive industry. GardoFlex combines the benefits of a conventional zinc-phosphate technology with optimised process components. The advantage of the multimetal pre-treatment system lies in its’ large number of high-performance modules for the entire process – from cleaning and degreasing to activation and phosphating through to the last rinsing step. These modules are specifically combined to suit each individual production line, enabling increased process efficiency.

“With the GardoFlex system, significant process cost economics can be achieved, for example by reducing the temperature down to 35°C in the zinc-phosphating bath. Field experiences have shown that – depending upon the throughput and plant location – energy costs in the six-digit range can be saved. All process elements are optimally aligned and, together with a distinctly longer bath service life, meaning lower water consumption and smaller effluent volumes, contribute to the resource-saving aspect of this technology,” explained Chemetall.

“GardoFlex is suitable for multi-metal applications without limitations. The modern zinc-phosphate technology can be used for all standard metal materials used in the automotive industry. There are no limits with regard to the throughput of aluminium substrates. And, on high-strength steel, GardoFlex can significantly improve corrosion resistance.” See www.chemetall.com ■
