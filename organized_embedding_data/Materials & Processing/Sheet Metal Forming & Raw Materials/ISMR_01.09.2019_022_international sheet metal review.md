LVD COMPANY www.lvdgroup.com

The Dyna-Cell with bending robot

LVD WILL SHOWCASE its Industry 4.0-ready products designed to help fabricators advance their business, including its Dyna-Cell robotic bending cell; ToolCell automated toolchanging press brake; high-speed Electra 10kW fibre laser cutting machine and CADMAN® software.

Dyna-Cell features the high-speed Dyna-Press Pro electric press brake and robot equipped with LVD's patent-pending universal gripper (able to bend on three sides of a part without regripping). The compact cell automates bending of small- to medium-sized parts in varying batch sizes at speeds of up to 25mm per second.

"Our ToolCell automated tool-changing press brake maximises bending productivity through an integrated storage of tooling, housed within the machine. The Electra FL, with high-power 10kW fibre laser, is an ultra-high-speed laser cutting machine which cuts ferrous and non-ferrous metals as fast as the thermal process allows... CADMAN® makes the virtual factory possible, enabling a constant flow of data between machines and software," added LVD.

## MATE PRECISION TOOLING www.mate.com

MATE PRECISION TOOLING sales engineers will demonstrate features and benefits of the company's full line of solutions for punching at Blechexpo 2019.

"The Mate QCT™ tool-less punch retention system has been expanded to include Metric QCT™, AMX QCT™ for AMADA air blow systems and MXC QCT™ for Wilson Series 90 systems. The QCT system is simple and intuitive to set up. Simply flip the durable latch to remove and snap the new insert into place without any additional tools," explained the company.

Mate Metric QCT is fully compatible with Mate's Ultra® family of guides and canisters, Mate's Original Style guides, spring packs and Rapidset™ canisters. These punches are fully interchangeable with AMADA NCT, NEX and Z-Tooling systems, as well as Wilson Thick Turret Metric punches. AMX QCT™ is fully compatible with Mate AMX™ guides, spring packs and Rapidset canisters, as well as Amada NCT, NEX and Z-Tooling air blow systems. MXC QCT punches are fully interchangeable with Wilson Series 90 systems.

"The Mate Versadie HD™ clamp clearing solution is designed to improve the slitting of thick materials. There is no need to replace the entire die with replaceable die inserts – Versadie inserts are designed to allow for the greatest lengths currently available in the industry, accommodating lengths up to 4.560" (115.82mm) for E-Stations and 3.560" (90.42mm) for D Stations. The new Versadie HD offers improved slitting in thicker materials that require more than 0.016"(0.406mm) total die clearance. The base incorporates a hat die feature to better withstand the punching of heavier materials. The die inserts are fully interchangeable with the standard Versadie," added the tooling specialist.
