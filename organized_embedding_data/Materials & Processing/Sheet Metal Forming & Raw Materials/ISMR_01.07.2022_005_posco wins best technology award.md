# POSCO wins 'Best Technology Award'

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

South Korean materials specialist, POSCO, won the Gold Award in the ISSF-26 technology category at the 26th International Stainless-Steel Forum, hosted by the World Stainless Association, for its highperformance ferrite stainless steel '430RE (Ridging Endurance)'.

The material was developed to meet recent trends in large household appliances by improving the processibility and corrosionresistance of welded parts, compared to existing steel grades. POSCO intends to continue developing R&D in this field.

“As home appliance makers manufacture large-scale washing machines and dryers, it has become more important to improve the formability of the drum bottom and to reduce the stripe pattern defect on the material surface (ridging). In addition, as high-speed rotation of the drum is required to save water and power during spin drying, the welding method (with excellent bonding strength) is preferred over the Lock Seaming method of connecting by folding and compressing the plate material when connecting the centre of the drum," explained POSCO.

POSCO therefore developed 430RE, a specialised steel grade that has features such as enhanced formability, anti-ridging and corrosion-resistance at welds compared to existing steel types, and plans to expand its supply to overseas as well as domestic home appliance companies.

POSCO has won awards 12 times (since the first year) for products such as 470FC (Gold Award in Technology) for hydrogen electric vehicle divider plates in 2018; a fuel tank for PHEVs (Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicle) in 2020 (Silver Award in Technology) and a stainless-steel sealed container (Gold Award in Market Development) in 2020.

The name was changed from the previous ISSF (International Stainless Steel Forum) to the World Stainless Association at this conference. Forty-eight organisations from 25 countries participated in the conference to exchange information on the challenges and developments that the stainless steel industry faces (such as raw materials, new demand and the environment).
