# GEARING FOR GROWTH

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

JEC is investing significantly in new production lines at its factories in Taiwan and China. JEC has been a leading manufacturer of metal mesh products and machinery since 1987. Over the past 30 years, it has not only built its strengths in expanded metal machines and perforated metal machines but also developed angle-bead and plaster stop bead machines, brick mesh machines and various kinds of mesh machines for different applications.

JEC will be celebrating its 30th anniversary in 2017 and plans to celebrate this in March at the TIMTOS exhibition in Taipei. It also plans to announce the launch of a new fine mesh/expanded metal machine, for filtration, electronic components and aviation parts, early next year.

At this October's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, ISMR sat down with its general manager, Vincent Chiu, to uncover more of its plans for 2017.

## An eye on exports

JEC produces two types of metal perforating machines to satisfy different customer requirements. For mass production, its planotype machine achieves 400SPM with an 8-row punching mould to boost productivity. For more flexible production, its C-type machine, processing material widths from 4-8 feet and thicknesses from 0.5mm to 6mm, can adjust to various production requirements from customers.

"All our systems are tailor-made, according to customer requirements. High speed and high stability of production are key strengths of JEC expanded metal machines. Perforated and expanded metal products can be used in applications such as enclosures; protection; support; decoration; ventilation; sound absorption and filtration," Vincent Chiu told ISMR.

"We have around 500 customers and export machines to over 70 countries including the US, Japan, Australia, UAE etc. We also supply customers in Europe. Most of our customers make metal mesh (expanded metal/sheet metal) for construction purposes. Some are manufacturers and some are contractors, working in sectors such as aerospace, construction and industrial (e.g. the chemical industry).

"For the last three years, we have been selling well into the Middle East, Africa and Central/South America. African markets include Kenya; South Africa; the Sudan; Algeria; Morocco; Tunisia and Egypt, Ghana etc. We are actively looking to expand our customer base in Africa. We plan to exhibit at more international exhibitions in 2017 and are appointing new agents in Central and South American countries. We are also trying to increase our market share in Russia and are ready to appoint a new agent there," he added.

## New investments

JEC has invested significantly in new production lines at its factories in Taiwan and China (production and assembly equipment). In 2017, the new lines will be ready.

"We have spent approx. US$ 3 million US dollars on upgrading production lines at both factories," explained Vincent Chiu. "We have been very successful in the Chinese market and it still holds huge potential. This is a big market for us. Our factory in Shanghai is 5.5 years old, so we are a relative newcomer, but we are implementing a three-year plan to invest in new equipment in our China factory so that we can increase capacity there. The Shanghai factory handles expanded metal, perforated metal and other products, producing around 100 metric tons of equipment every month. From China, we can supply customers in South East Asia.

"Our aim is always to attract more customers. EuroBLECH is a very important sheet metal exhibition for us and we have been very pleased with the show so far."

In 2004, JUN-EN sold its first 3100mm expanded metal production line to the US. It was a heavy-duty machine with auxiliary devices for both sheet and coil production. The customer bought another two machines in the next few years and JEC then introduced its machines to more US customers.

"We not only produce the machines but also use our own machines in our own production. As an end-user, our priority is to keep improving JEC machine efficiency for a better user experience. Since all JEC machines are customised, we have been encouraging our R&D team to see every challenge as an opportunity to optimise the machine beyond customers' expectation and enjoy the fruit of the breakthrough as a team," explained Vincent Chiu.

"If we learn something new, or develop a new technology, we will share this with our customers. We constantly innovate and improve our machines – it is an ongoing process. We spend around 5% of our annual turnover on R&D activities. We aim to develop long-term relationships with our customers and share our technologies and developments with them. I think we are heading in the right direction."

Tracto-Technik explained its new Pipe Bending System (PBS) philosophy to ISMR at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition.

It is one of the leading specialists in the pipe bending sector and has positioned itself as an expert for customised piping and the pre-fabrication of media-bearing pipes. Its product range currently covers all-electric and electrohydraulic CNC pipe bending machines, semi-automatic pipe bending machines, machines for pipe end processing and hydraulic joining technology, pipe measuring systems as well as special solutions and software solutions for pipe processing.

For more than 50 years, it has been developing and producing machines, high-precision measuring systems and intelligent software solutions for pipe processing. It has now introduced a new brand for efficient pipe fabrication called Pipe Bending Systems (PBS). PBS is aimed primarily for pipe processers with single-part or small-batch production and large product variety.

## Pipe Bending Systems (PBS)

"Thanks to the interplay between our machines, measuring devices and software solutions in different combinations and levels of deployment, a scalable system emerges that offers our partners an optimal solution for every application," Frank Dobbener, TT PBS sales manager, told ISMR at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover.

"PBS is more than just the pipe bending machine - by combining innovative pipe bending machinery with high-precision pipe measuring technology and efficient piping software solutions, in various expansion stages, it provides customers with the best solution for each specific application. We launched PBS in the middle of the year and the new logo was launched at Tube Düsseldorf earlier this year."

The company's new TUBOMAT has been fundamentally revised and modularly designed. This pipe bender (up to 48.3mm pipe OD) also optionally integrates the sawing, deburring, flaring and cutting-ring assembly function. Various technical improvements have been made to all working functions, such as the tooling concept, handling and control techniques, workflow and energy consumption. The modern control concept provides program operation as well as a “4-fold bending angle pre-selection mode" and also enables bending data import from TT's own PIPEFAB software solution.

For pipe pre-fabrication, TT also offers the semi-automatic TUBOBEND pipe bender series and the fully automatic TUBOTRON CNC pipe bender series. The TUBOTRON 25 MR, an all-electric CNC pipe bending machine with multi-radii capability, rapidly bends pipes with an outside diameter of up to 25mm. TT's optical pipe measuring system, TUBOSCAN S, measures-up pipe figures within seconds. The PIPEFAB workflow management software pack, that performs intelligent networking of all working stations, is the third cornerstone of PBS.

With the leap to version 4.0, the new PIPEFAB intelligent workflow management software accepts pipe and stock data from CAD and ERP systems; optimises pipe material consumption as well as production times and costs; generates work schedules, production lots, sawing and bending lists and is also able to supply various pipe fabrication machines directly with production data.

## Two new prototypes at EuroBLECH

"At EuroBLECH, as well as the PBS system, we are presenting the first two machines of a completely new TUBOBEND family of bending systems (the first up to 16mm and the second up to 42mm pipe OD). These are two prototypes for the show. We use the same machine design and integrate different functions into the design, based upon customer requirements, so we can effectively customise the design for a user," explained Frank Dobbener.

"It has been a good year for us with the new brand and new machine series. We expect similar growth this year to last year. We sell machines all over the world in sectors such as the machine-building industry, industrial pipe solutions, general engineering, HVAC, trucking etc."

The new modular TUBOBEND machine prototypes are optionally available as right-hand or left-hand benders. The exposed bending head, which allows for large bending space, means that they can bend highly complex pipe shapes.

For the semi-automatic pipe bending machines of the TUBOBEND series, the bending function runs automatically while pipe feed and pipe rotation between two bends have to be carried out manually. These machines are particularly used for single and small batch production, for adaption and maintenance works or as mobile bending machines on construction sites.

"Customers want an individual solution (which might be different for each customer) so we will offer a customised pipe bending system. This may mean just supplying our software or the customer may want a combination of our machines (TUBOBEND, TUBOMAT or TUBOSCAN) and software for complete in-house fabrication. We can offer a solution to fit his needs, in line with the PBS principle. The future for us is Industry 4.0 – connecting and interlinking machines and systems for the benefit of our users. We want to grow with our customers and spread the PBS brand into global markets."
