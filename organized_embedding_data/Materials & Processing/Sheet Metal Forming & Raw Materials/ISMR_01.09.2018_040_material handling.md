# Material handling

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

SPECIALIST FORKLIFT MANUFACTURER Combilift is a leading global supplier of customised solutions designed to enable safer, space-saving and productive material handling. It is exhibiting at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover (Stand F156 in Hall 12).

"Whether you handle long, awkward and heavy loads, pallets, containers, extremely oversized loads or a combination of these, there is a Combilift to suit your requirements. We also offer a free warehouse layout service and material flow analysis to help customers to make the best of all available space in any facility," the manufacturer told ISMR.

Celebrating its 20th anniversary this year, Combilift has exported over 40,000 units to over 85 countries. See www.combilift.com
