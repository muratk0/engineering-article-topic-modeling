# SIMULATING HOT FORMING AND QUENCHING PROCESSES

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GMBH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has successfully developed AutoForm-ThermoSolver, innovative software for efficient simulation of hot forming and quenching processes.

“High-strength components with challenging geometrical complexity and minimised springback effects can be engineered in shorter times at lower cost,” the company explained. “Hot forming and quenching processes have recently become important for the automotive industry to meet specific requirements regarding higher levels of crash safety and reductions in overall weight. The components produced by hot forming and quenching are characterised by high strength, complex shapes if required and reduced springback effects.”

AutoForm-ThermoSolver features several innovations. The software supports direct and indirect hot forming and takes into account heat flux and phase transformation during quenching.

“It offers engineers better insight into the structural transformation of material and enables them to have better control over it. The supported tailored tempering process allows for the engineering of local strength properties. Based on the results of AutoForm-ThermoSolver, crash simulation accuracy can be improved by taking the real strength distribution of the hot-formed component into account,” added AutoForm.

“Car manufacturers and suppliers can now reliably design and engineer the processes of hot-stamped parts for their new vehicles, such as A- and B-pillars, tunnels, front and rear bumper beams, side rail members, door beams or roof rails.” FOR MORE INFORMATION, PLEASE SEE www.autoform.com
