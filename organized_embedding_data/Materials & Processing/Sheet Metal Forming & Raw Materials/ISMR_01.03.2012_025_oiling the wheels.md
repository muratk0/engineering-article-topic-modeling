# Oiling the wheels

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

CROWN OIL, a major supplier of lubricants and road fuel to all branches of industry, has become an authorised distributor of advanced lubricants from Q8Oils. The company will now be able to supply a complete range of Q8Oils branded cutting and cooling fluids, lubricants and greases to compliment the Crown Oil range of lubricants manufactured at its blending plant in the UK.

Mark Andrews, Crown Oil Group General Manager, explained, “Q8Oils produce premium quality cutting and cooling fluids for engineering as well as high quality general lubrication products. These products carry extensive technical approvals and meet the demands of manufacturers for high productivity with health and safety.

“Q8Oils’ cutting fluids, for example, have a high cooling capacity, are compatible with multi-metal workshops, suitable for use on high speed modern machining centres, contain no hazardous chemicals and are bio-stable to protect the operator from bio-hazards. This is a new market segment for Crown Oil and one that fits comfortably within our existing business.” See www.crownoil.co.uk

## Mini safety laser scanner

THE NEW compact safety laser scanners from the S300 Mini product family from SICK have been designed with a construction height of 116mm. SICK designed the S300 Mini Standard as a basic technical solution. The range of the 270° protective field is 2m, and operators can program a triple field set with one protective and two warning zones. The device features one pair of OSSDs (output signal switching device) serving as a direct switching output. “By using this device, you can achieve up to 16 triple field sets – suitable for AGV applications but also for automated forklift trucks or rail-mounted transverse distribution cars. With the latest generation of the EFI (enhanced function interface), using the Flexi Soft safety controller, it is possible to combine up to four electro-sensitive protective devices e.g. two S3000 Experts and two S300 Mini Remotes, into a network. By doing so, you can easily implement even complex protective tasks,” said the company. See www.sick.com
