# Up and down folding

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

HASIL KARYA SDN. BHD., a leading importer of sheet metal working machines in Malaysia, placed the PowerBend Professional from Schröder Machines at the centre of its stand at the Metaltech 2016 exhibition in the Putra World Trade Centre in Kuala Lumpur. From 25 to 28 May 2016, Hasil Karya presented the folding machine, with a working length of 3200mm and a bending capacity of 3mm steel sheet, on its booth in Hall 2.

At the trade fair, the Schröder sales partner showcased the benefits of an Up-and-Down folding beam that allows counter folds without having to spend time turning over sheets. Particular emphasis was also placed on the POS 2000 Professional software control that not only controls the machine, but also shows the operator what to do next.

The Schröder Group consists of Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH (Wessobrunn, Germany) and SCHRÖDER-FASTI Technologie GmbH (Wermelskirchen, Germany). Hans Schröder Maschinenbau specialises in the development of modern machine concepts for folding and cutting sheet metal. The Schröder Group is now a leading provider of folding, cutting, crimping, beading and shearing machines. See www.schroedergroup.eu/en
