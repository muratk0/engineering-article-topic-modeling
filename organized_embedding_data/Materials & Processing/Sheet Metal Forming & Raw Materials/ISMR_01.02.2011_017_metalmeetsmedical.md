# METALMEETSMEDICAL

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

Medical technology is one of the world’s expanding markets. Its volume is estimated at US$ 100 to 220 billion, depending on the delimitation adopted. Rising life expectancy and a growing demand for high-quality medical care in many industrialised nations are fuelling advances in medical technology. Ageing populations are also driving growth in the medical device sector, particularly in the orthopaedic and cardiovascular implant markets.

The medical engineering market segment represents a highly attractive customer base. It is highly innovative and places high demands on its suppliers in terms of quality. Those demands include extreme precision and perfect surface finishes along with high process reliability and traceability of all manufacturing steps.

Machine tool manufacturers have responded well – machines and tooling for the machining of high-strength materials used to make dental implants, screws for bone fractures and hip joints, high-quality titanium nails, connecting pins and miniature pump parts for heart surgery or special components for packaging machines, e.g. for the production of ampoules, are all available. The range of products and services also includes laser welding e.g. of cardiac pump housings, and laser marking systems used to mark products and implants.

## THE PROCESS CHAIN
