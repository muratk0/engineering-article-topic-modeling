# PRESSING FOR ACTION

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

We highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing technologies and processes

Stamping includes a variety of sheet metal forming manufacturing processes, such as punching, blanking, embossing, bending, flanging and coining. This could be a singlestage operation, where every stroke of the press produces the desired form on the sheet metal part, or could occur through a series of stages.

Operations include progressive die stamping; deep drawing; hot forming/press hardening (forming metal to make high strength parts through quick quenching of boron steels heated above 900 degrees) and hydroforming (a forming process with an active fluid).

In this review, we highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing innovations.

## The forming equation

AIDA is a global manufacturer of metal stamping presses and continues to develop new and innovative metalforming solutions. The company manufactures a complete line of metalforming presses, including gap, straightside, progressive die, transfer, high speed, cold forging and servo-driven mechanical presses. AIDA also offers complete and total turnkey solutions with feeders and automation.

AIDA-America exhibited a DSF-M2-4000, 400- ton straightside servo press at Fabtech 2015 in Chicago (November 2015). This advanced AIDA DSF Series Servo Press, featuring AIDA’s servo press technology, was part of a complete production line, under power and stamping parts.

AP&T is cooperating with Raufoss Technology, Hydro Aluminium and the SINTEF research institute to develop a new method for forming of aluminium sheet. The goal is for chassis parts that are currently made of steel or die-cast aluminium to be manufactured from aluminium sheet in a press form hardening process, resulting in significantly lower weight. The project’s calculations show that chassis weight could be cut in half if the new method were to be fully applied in new cars. See page 6 for further details.

AP&T recently developed a new method, with Sandvik and Cell Impact, for the costefficient manufacturing of fuel cell plates. At Blechexpo last November, the company focused on lightweight forming – the warm forming of aluminium (fuel cell lines that it believes will grow in the medium term). It showed a 3D simulation with a fuel cell line and a 3D simulation of warm forming of aluminium (6000 and 7000 series) on its booth. AP&T recently started to offer automation equipment from NORDA through AP&T in Germany and Poland.

“Industry 4.0 is a trend I see more and more in our machines i.e. from the service point, in maintenance, for safety on the machines and for communication between the machines. Industry 4.0 is still a bit further into the future but it is, nevertheless, a requirement that we need to incorporate,” Michael Hunger, AP&T’s director and head of sales and marketing, Europe, told ISMR.

“For the automotive industry, the main drive I see now is in lightweight material development. There is also a range of aluminium alloys for more niche car production. Certainly, electric car production is another market trend. Self-driving cars, further in the future, will mean a total change of car design.”

AgustaWestland, an Italian specialist in vertical flight innovation, recently installed a Flexform™ fluid cell press from Avure Technologies at PZL-Swidnik, its helicopter factory in Swidnik, Poland. The Avure model QFC 0.7x1.8-800 press, featuring a forming area of 0.7 x 1.8m (27.5 x 72 inches), provides production capacity of several hundred thousand sheet metal parts per year.

Often compared to sheet hydroforming presses, Flexform fluid cell presses employ ultra-high pressure to form a wide variety of complex shapes. Avure Technologies is a large global provider of ultra-high pressure systems utilised to process advanced materials and to form sheet metal. It has installed over 1,700 high-pressure systems worldwide.

At the FABTECH trade show in November 2015, Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik presented its new BIMERIC servo production and assembly system. The BIMERIC is designed to be a platform for flexible, productive and costefficient sub-assembly production for stamping and forming as well as assembly technology.

“The modular servo system has benefits especially for small and medium batch sizes and for many part variants. With the powerful flow production from raw material (strip or wire) to installation-ready sub-assemblies, users can achieve consistently high product quality. The latest servo and control technology ensures maximum process reliability and extremely short set-up times allowing users to react flexibly to customer requirements,” commented the company.

The high pressure Avure QUINTUS® fluid cell press installed at the AgustaWestland facility in Swidnik, Poland, generates a pressure of 800 bar (11,600psi) over a forming area of 0.7x1.8 metres

At its headquarters in Frasnacht, Switzerland, Bruderer develops fully automated stamping solutions which are exported around the globe. At Blechexpo 2015 in November 2015, it premiered two new models of highperformance stamping presses - the world-first BSTA 410 110, a powerful press which complements its product line in the 40-ton press force range, and a special high-speed version of the BSTA 200 60 HS. This model was equipped with a progressive, 7-stage stamping tool made by the press tool-maker, Fritz Stepper GmbH & Co. KG.

Bruderer has also set up two strategic partnerships with Zani Presses and Zehnder & Sommer. The latter’s high-speed servo feed units can operate up to 2000 strokes per minute.

## Forming in focus

Taiwanese manufacturer Chin Fong’s new single point servo presses (iLS/DLS series) ranging from 80 – 300 tons; its double-point SDS series from 400 up to 1000 tonnes; the compound link LF and KW series for stamping forging composite processes; the long stroke knuckle joint presses KL and warm-hot forging WF series have facilitated its slogan: “Intelligence of Forming, Vision of Forging”.

Chin Fong now has offices in Taiwan, China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and the USA as well as representatives in numerous countries around the world. Its A0 tandem press line for the automotive industry includes stages such as deep drawing, following by trimming, piercing etc. The basic A0 line weighs 2400 tons with 800 tons of weight for the rest of the machines. To gain ground in production efficiency, Chin Fong replaced the conventional hydraulic press with a mechanical press featuring a link drive and long stroke.

Chin Fong’s straightside double crank power press, with 400-ton capacity, has a bed area of 2.5m x 1m and can produce metal stampings from material measuring up to 400mm wide and 3.5mm thick. It has a variable speed of between 30-50 strokes per minute and a maximum shut height of 450mm.

DEES Hydraulic has successfully developed a closed loop, hydraulic, servo-controlled die spotting press. With microprecision control and energy efficiency at the top of its R&D goals, the servo system’s fixed-flow control, as the core technology used in the hydraulic spotting press, can further improve the precision of the conventional design or overcome the ballscrew’s wear and tear to reduce overall maintenance costs.

“The energy-efficient servo motor can save up to 30% energy. Adopting a pulse wave signal handwheel jog down for micro precision of 0.01mm can prevent damage when spotting the punch and die. The closed loop, servo-hydraulic system can reach high slide repeatability levels of +/- 0.02mm,” explained the manufacturer.

Group Rhodes is one of Europe’s largest original equipment manufacturers in its field. The Group actively manufactures and markets over fifty basic ranges of presses/machines, and designs, develops and manufactures bespoke machinery and complete turnkey solutions. Group Rhodes is a double winner of the prestigious Queen’s Award for Enterprise in recognition of its continuous development of Super Plastic Forming and Diffusion Bonding Presses (SPF/DB) specifically designed for the high temperature forming of titanium and aluminium alloys, primarily used in the aerospace and automotive industry.

As well as its two Wakefield sites in the UK, which offer over 18,500m2. of production capability, Group Rhodes also operates divisions in Sheffield, Manchester and Bangalore (India).

Interlaken Technology, a manufacturer of production press equipment for hydroforming, elevated temperature forming and hot stamping systems, has introduced a new four-column hydroforming press system for running high volume production while reducing cycle time. Interlaken offers this new hydroforming press system in clamp forces ranging from 250 to 5000 tons.

Hydroformed parts are stronger and weigh less due to structural integrity and fewer welds or add-on pieces. In addition, costs are reduced and time is saved by eliminating secondary operations, reducing scrap, lowering material and manufacturing costs, and increasing design flexibility.

Established in 1937, Chinese pressmaker, JIER, has produced a variety of mechanical presses from C-frame to straight-sided presses (60t to 7,500t) including automated high speed press lines. It also produces transfer presses, progressive presses, cut-to-length lines and blank washers and has made use of servo press technology and NC hydraulic die cushions in its technical solutions.

Komatsu designs large presses for forming automobile roofs and doors. Its large presses are represented by a tandem press for a variety of wide applications and a transfer press for high productivity. Its hybrid AC servo press, H1F, achieves high performance by combining advanced CNC controls and a hybrid drive mechanism (pat.p).

“Servos offer a variety of benefits, including a major increase in forming performance and productivity due to their free motion mechanism. The accurate linear sensor maintains high bottom dead centre accuracy over a long period of time for strong performance in blanking and precision forming of sheet metal. As a clutch is used, a major reduction in maintenance costs can be achieved. Thanks to the newly developed hybrid drive mechanism, large outputs from small motors have been achieved and power costs are very low,” said the manufacturer.

Its large high speed transfer blanking press for processing car body panels improves productivity by 75%, it told ISMR. Komatsu also provides blanking presses and coil lines.

Lien Chieh Machinery Co. Ltd. was established in 1947. For more than 65 years, it has accumulated practical experience in developing a comprehensive spectrum of presses. Its product portfolio includes die spotting presses, die tryout presses, drawing presses, forging presses, hemming presses, hydraulic presses and others.

The company’s 6,000 - 15,000-ton high capacity, single cylinder hydraulic forging press is designed for the manufacture of wheels for commercial vehicles and automotive industries (press bolster area of 2700 x 2500mm). The Taiwanese pressmaker has sold hydraulic presses directly to Ford Motor, Toyota Motor and Honda Motor.

In 2015, press specialist Nidec Minster announced the acquisition of Spanish pressbuilder, Arisa, located in Logroño. The acquisition allows Nidec Minster, which has been operating in Europe for twenty-five years, the opportunity to expand significantly with a new European production site, add new mechanical and servo press ranges to its product portfolio with immediate effect and enjoy new growth opportunities in Europe. Arisa's target audience and market are primarily European Tier 1 and Tier 2 automotive suppliers.

Based in Minster, Ohio (USA), Nidec Corporation operates Minster service centres in Ningbo (China), Halblech (Germany) and Querétaro (Mexico) and sales offices in Japan, Belgium, Mexico, Spain, China, India, Germany and Brazil. Its servo technology has traditionally been supplied in the range from 100-600 tonnes – it is now working on a new planetary gear box with higher speeds and had a 100-tonne press, with that gearbox, on display at the FABTECH exhibition in Chicago in November 2015. On its stand at Blechexpo in Stuttgart in November 2015, Nidec Minster showcased a high speed Kyori press for the connector market.
