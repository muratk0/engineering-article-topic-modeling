# COIL COATING LINE IN SAUDI

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

HSSAG (Hussein Saleh Saeed Alamoudi Group) has chosen Fitch Metals Engineering (FME) of Sydney, Australia to provide a complete coil coating line for aluminium and steel substrates. In addition, the turnkey project includes a slitting and packaging line and the building and utilities construction for the total site.

Since the establishment of HSSAG, the group has played an important role in the Saudi business community. With over 25 years of experience in trading, construction and services, the company has grown to a position of prominence in its activities and services in imports, exports, food, pharmaceuticals, mining and exploration.

The new CCL line will be designed with FME patented technology for tension levelling, painting, clear coating and hot laminating of 500 – 2000mm wide with a maximum process speed of 120mpm. This will be a three coat – three bake line and will be designed to provide extreme flexibility to produce products for the appliance, architecture, general construction and packaging markets.

The new slitting – packaging line will process the painted coils and tin plate at 100mpm down to a minimum 5mm slit width and 1.5000kg coils. The turnkey site development portion of the contract includes land development, utilities building and storage facilities to house and support the production equipment.

FME’s CCL process technology includes the FME patented Link Coat® design, patented quick product change technology and primary, secondary and tertiary heat recovery. Other key features of the coil coating line include process automation and graphic displays, tension levelling, quick change primer and finish coater, prime and finish air coolers, closed loop coater nip force control, oven solvent monitors, clear coat coating and curing and two hot lamination locations.
