# Trailer and railcar tank manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Tanks manufactured for road and rail transportation are designed to be the lightest possible to increase the payload. The direct consequence of this is the use of thinner and high-quality plates in carbon steel or stainless steel with high-tensile strength.

The production target is therefore to reduce to the minimum the ovality tolerance of the rolled shell and to ensure that the same roundness is then obtained in the heads spun on the flanging machine. It also aims to decrease manual fitting and ensure the repeatability of components.

## An eye on productivity

“To achieve these production objectives, it is important to invest in a tank manufacturing line which features CNC-controlled, four-roll plate rolls equipped with plate-handling and support systems which are fundamental accessories to increase productivity," commented Rino Boldrini of the Faccin Group. Faccin's solution includes:

1. Lateral supports with multiple joints, guiding the plate towards the central support, holding it up during the initial rolling step and leaving free the main overhead crane for other jobs inside the workshop.

2. The cross central support prevents the re-closing of rolled ferrules under their own weight in addition to avoiding the plate-overlap during the second end pre-bending. It has two orthogonal controlled axes to bear the weight of poli-cylindrical (double D) oval, elliptical and cylindrical ferrules with high-diameter thickness ratios. The interpolation between the two axes to handle elliptical or poli-cylindrical ferrules is managed by the CNC system. The cross central ferrule support is an essential device to produce policylindrical ferrules in an automatic single step process.

3. Modern feeding tables are designed to automate plate feeding. While on one side the machine is rolling, on the other side the feeding table is automatically placing the next metal sheet in the same position as the previous one, for immediate introduction and transportation, resulting in valuable time savings. This, said Faccin, is a smart solution for high volumes that is designed to ensure operator safety.

4. CNC dishing press with automated manipulator.

## 5. CNC flanging machine.

“It is also essential to ensure the repeatability of the tank manufacturing line, and this is the reason why the Faccin Group developed CNC software that is designed for results. The success of the CNC is due to the complete redesign of the hydraulic circuit (including the choice of its components) and PLC software ready to receive the CNC input. We therefore implemented the use of high-quality, market-standard technical solutions for hydraulic components and various designs to ensure that all axes of the machines can be operated simultaneously," continued Boldrini.

## Updated hydraulic system

The company has also considered energy and process efficiency to reduce energy consumption and the carbon footprint. The hydraulic system for the Faccin CNC-controlled plate rolls has been updated with the latest components. Precise sensors have been included to monitor the hydraulic valves and the signal to correct the required amount of oil for each axis action.

"This solution, already tested in many machines, is the result of ten years of development and has allowed us to offer the industry a product that is at least 20/25% more efficient than the standard hydraulic packs available in the market. The hydraulic system is an exclusive development by Faccin in close collaboration with a major German manufacturer and is available only upon request," explained Boldrini.

## A focus on flanging

For the head-forming line, the Faccin Group has updated the design of the hydraulic system to reach a higher level of efficiency and rebuilt the PLC software to achieve total control of the forming process and reduce plate thinning (which is extremely important to keep control of the tank weight).

The Group's wide range of flanging machines includes all types of machine configurations to make dished heads with or without the centre hole. Additionally, the option of a tiltable upper beam (a useful feature when flanging wide-diameter long cones) is available.

"This updated control of the forming process also helps the action of the die impact on the plate targeting, giving better uniformity with simultaneous absolute position control. The typical size of a dishing press to produce road tank ends is 300 up to 400 tons (when the range is extended to railcar tanks). For intense production operations, a 600-ton press is the ideal choice to increase the number of blanks/ round plates being pressed together in a single stack," continued Boldrini.

“The height of the flanging machine is generally extended to facilitate the loading and unloading of the maximum head diameter and for flanging cones with vertical axis on a specially dedicated tool, working on a rotary platform. Our flanging machines feature an electric-powered main-rotation system to reduce the energy consumption and to extend the rpms of the main shaft. Obtaining the highest speed achievable is fundamental for the final cosmetic pass to make the outer surface of the dished head very smooth," continued Boldrini.

Experienced manufacturers in the trailer tank industry know how important this is since, after painting the surface, defects will be revealed. The Faccin Group's solution negates the need for costly plastering. Plastering also has a negative environmental impact on the tank construction.

The final step is the flanging operation, which can be done by cold or warm forming, as in the case of aluminium.

"In summary, our updated forming systems reduce the environmental impact of the forming process by cutting unnecessary time and ultimately improving the product quality," concluded Boldrini.
