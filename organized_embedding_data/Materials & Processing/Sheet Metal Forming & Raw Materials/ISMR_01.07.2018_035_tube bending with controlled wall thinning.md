# Tube bending with controlled wall thinning

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

SAFETY IS extremely important in the aviation and aerospace industry and demands are very high, particularly on tube processing, as Transfluid's managing director, Stefanie Flaeper, told ISMR.

"There is often demand for rapid production processes of small batches of complex components. In addition, high and ultra-high strength materials are important for tubes that are more lightweight and that can be subjected to greater loading. What makes the development of tube processing technologies for the aviation and aerospace industry particularly interesting is that there are always new technical milestones that require sophisticated solutions."

One example of this is a project that the company is working on at present for a leading aircraft manufacturer. Its new, specially designed t bend mandrel bending machine is capable of bending titanium, aluminium and stainless steel tubes with a diameter of up to 60mm and a wall thickness of 0.5-1.5mm.

The design makes it possible to achieve a radius of 1xD. There is also an option to control the wall thinning. The wall thickness has to meet specifications after tube bending as the flow through the workpiece and its stability have to be preserved. That is why Transfluid engineers have developed a push bending option for the process. Controlled push bending is applied during the last bend through a controlled inner tube.

"All the electrical axes are equipped with servo-electrical motors and their drives can be neatly synchronised," explained Stefanie Flaeper. "A key benefit is the complete synchronisation of all the moving axes in different sequences, which is what makes our new sequential control possible." This separates each movement of all the axes and moves different units at the right time. This way, it is possible to gradually introduce a bend with ease and either push or slow down with the necessary power.

"All the sequences on this mandrel bending machine are easy to program. The operator can allocate them to the product and retrieve them, as and when needed. In addition to the actual process, the sequencing will also take into account the material, radii and manufacturing processes of the product."

All control aspects can be accessed via the network. The sequence control will store the relevant data for production, as defined by the customer. They can then be recalled and processed with a password and the correct authorisation. The machine's software and all the control components, as well as the control panel (MMI), are compatible with Industry 4.0. These features, added Transfluid, make it possible to set up the machine with almost no operator intervention.
