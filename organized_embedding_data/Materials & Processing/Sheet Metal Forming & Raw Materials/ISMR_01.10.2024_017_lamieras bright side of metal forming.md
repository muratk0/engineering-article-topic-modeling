# LAMIERA's 'bright side' of metal forming

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

From 6-9 May 2025, fieramilano Rho in Milan (Italy) will host the twenty-third edition of LAMIERA, an international exhibition dedicated to sheet metal forming and cutting machine tools/technologies.

Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association) and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU, LAMIERA is the only trade fair in Italy exclusively dedicated to the metal forming sector.

“In the world of sheet metal forming and cutting, Italy holds second position in the world ranking of production and exports (and third in consumption)," said UCIMU.

The trade show's slogan is 'The Bright Side of Metal Forming'. It will feature six technological themes such as ‘Fabbrica Future' (software and technologies for connectivity, data management and automation and digital systems/robotics applied to sheet metal processing); Saldatech (welding) and Fastener Industry (screws, bolts, fastening and clamping systems).

'World of Heat and Surface Treatments' will host machines, systems, accessories and consumables focused on pre- and post-production treatments for ferrous and non-ferrous materials. 'Metrology and Testing' will be centred on testing, measuring and quality-control systems and machines while 'BLECH Italy Service' will cover technical subcontracting and services.

In addition, LAMIERA will offer a 'Revamping and Retrofitting' area focused on machines and equipment that have been revamped and equipped with new functionalities to meet the needs of a market that is complementary to that of “new machinery".

“In the Italian exhibition [world]," said Alfredo Mariotti, general manager of UCIMU, “LAMIERA is the only event dedicated exclusively to machines for cutting, processing and forming sheet metal. The high quality on show and the attention focused, through the innovative exhibition themes, on new leading technologies in the industry make the trade fair, year after year, more relevant and appealing nationally and internationally".

'LAMIALAMIERA', the event area, will offer a programme of meetings on relevant topics in the sector, as well as educational issues.■
