# PLANNING sheet metal processes

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

AUTOFORM Engineering GmbH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has introduced its latest software version, AutoForm plus R5. This latest release streamlines sheet metal forming, providing a wide range of new powerful features and improvements for digital planning and validation of sheet metal processes and parts.

Besides new planning options and tooling features, this release offers several new capabilities to improve the efficiency and reliability of result evaluation. This software also extends systematic process improvement. With AutoForm plus, R5, users can easily evaluate which design parameters influence the part quality and to what extent, in both conventional and hot forming processes. AutoForm plus R5 contains solver improvements for increased result accuracy and shorter computational time.

An improved contact algorithm leads to even more realistic bending behaviour in wall and flanging areas. It also results in a more precise calculation of tool forces and a very realistic representation of wrinkles in the binder area. The new adaptive line bead model takes the current in-die conditions into account. It combines the accuracy of 3D geometric beads with the computing speed of 2D line beads. See www.autoform.com

## The laser equation

PRIMA Electro will introduce its high performance CO2 and Nd:YAG solid state lasers at Laser World Of Photonics India from 12-14 November 2013. The Convergent brand of CO lasers provide beam characteristics that allow for the widest range of industrial material processing applications. These DC exited, fast axial flow laser models are currently available in models with up to 5,000 Watts average power and offer high electrical efficiency. The CX (2.5 – 3 kW) and CV (4 – 5 kW) Series Convergent lasers are designed for precision cutting and welding applications involving a wide range and thicknesses of metal and alloys.

The Convergent Model CL50k Nd:YAG laser is designed specifically for precision hole drilling, percussion drilling, drill-on-the-fly and trepanning. It delivers a combination of high peak power (50kW) and beam quality for drilling deep and high aspect ratio holes at shallow entrance angles. The Convergent Model CL-R is based on the CL50k and is a direct field replacement for older solid state lasers from Prima Electro and other manufacturers such as GSI Group. It provides many laser processing benefits including higher throughput and the ability to produce deeper and larger percussion drilled holes. See www.primaelectro.com

## Hybrid threadform tool

FASTEN two pieces of sheet metal together without tapping or secondary operations using Mate’s hybrid threadform forming tool. The tool is designed with an upper and lower insert machined with a profile that precisely matches the thread helix of the screw head.

Designed for use when the material thickness is greater than the screw pitch, Mate’s hybrid threadform tool thins the material in the centre of the form, creating a threadform helix so that the material fits between the pitch of the screw head in just one direction. It operates rapidly, too, at speeds of 200 strokes per minute.
