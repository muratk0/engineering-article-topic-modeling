# CLINCHING THE DEAL

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

World demand for industrial fasteners is forecast to expand 8.5 per cent per year to US$ 80.5 billion in 2014.

Fasteners for sheet metal provide deep tapped female threads or studs for use in conjunction with thin sheet metals. Once installed, they are permanently attached to the material, allowing the associated nut or bolt to be removed in service without the risk of the fastener becoming dislodged.
