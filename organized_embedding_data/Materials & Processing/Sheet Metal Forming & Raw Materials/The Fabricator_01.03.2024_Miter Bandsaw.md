Advanced Sawing

The AKYAPAK AST Miter Bandsaws are constructed with a sturdy frame for precise automatic material cutting. With a faster cutting speed, these bandsaws can cut materials at 0° or at miters (straight or at angled miter cuts).

The AST series uses top-quality components to reduce friction and enhance performance. They can be used in conjunction with an Akyapak Beam Drilling Line or separately.
