## Flatness in focus

Athader's stretch leveller eliminates visible shape defects such as edge waves; centre buckles; quarter buckles or oil can and mandrel breaks. The Leveltek Process also can eliminate “Memory", the distortion that can occur in cut parts after laser or plasma cutting of visibly flat sheets. The only measurable effect on the coil, it explained, is a slight reduction in material width, and a slight increase in length.

"Most visible flatness or shape defects occur because of length variations which develop during the rolling process. Edge waves occur when the strip is rolled with edges longer than the centre, for example, and centre buckle happens when a strip has a longer centre than edges," it explained.

The stretch levelling process is simple. The coil is decoiled, stretch-levelled and either recoiled or cut-to-length. Through a Leveltek proprietary non-marking process that grips and stretches the unrolled coil, Athader equipment is designed to eliminate shape defects such as edge waves, centre buckle, quarter buckle and mandrel breaks restoring coils that would otherwise end up as scrap metal. It does not mark or scratch the surface being stretched.

“If significant width reduction has not occurred, as in the roller leveller example, permanent correction of length disparities has not taken place," it said.

Leveltek's stretch leveller stretches the strip with the required force to exceed the yield strength and permanently elongate it. Even with materials that can be flattened using other methods, the process can be used for critical or special surfaces that are bright annealed; polished; painted; patterned or plated/coated.

Leveltek can process coils in a wide range of thicknesses and weights. Its stretch levellers can process many different materials including carbon steel; stainless steel; titanium; aluminium; brass; copper; high-temperature alloys; galvanized steel and other ferrous and non-ferrous materials.

## Surface contact

“Leveltek's proprietary non-marking grippers conform to the sheet metal/plate profile to achieve 100% total contact, equally distributed across the entire width of the strip contact area. The entire cross-section is uniformly stretched," commented Athader.

“Enhanced surface structures [are obtained] due to the uniformly aligned grain structures (affecting state, heat conductivity and temperature) and the release/discarding of surface debris/scale from the uniform stretching process," it concluded, adding that no scrap is produced via a threading process.

The Leveltek process is suitable for coils that require exceptional flatness in laser cutting; stamping; slit mults; cut-to-length; perforating and burning. This could be for the manufacture of products such as cathode plates; pipes and tubes; architectural panels; coiled plates; tread plates; refrigeration panels; lithograph plates and food processing equipment etc.

Athader and Leveltek both provide new cut-to-length lines and retrofits of cut-tolength lines. The Leveltek stretch leveller fits into new or existing cut-to-length lines.
