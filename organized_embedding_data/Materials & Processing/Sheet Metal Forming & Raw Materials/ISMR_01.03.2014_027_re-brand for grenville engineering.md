# Re-brand for Grenville Engineering

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

GRENVILLE ENGINEERING, a leading UK-based sheet metal fabrication company, has announced a fresh new brand and website. Following a successful year, Grenville Engineering revamped its branding to a cleaner, contemporary and vibrant look. Its new and improved website also compliments the future strategic direction of the company as a complete manufacturing solution for fabrication requirements, enabling visitors to submit their drawings online for a fast quote. The company offers design and prototyping; metal cutting; machining; drilling, pressing and forming; welding; assembly; grinding; deburring; final assembly and delivery.

The company also celebrates a further milestone in the year, embarking on its 30th anniversary as a sheet metal fabricator. Established in 1984, Grenville Engineering has built a reputation based on high standards of quality and service over a diverse range of sectors. The company attributes much of its success to its stringent quality control process. Grenville Engineering has generated record sales figures over the last year with the future, it says, for the whole sector continuing to look very promising.

"Grenville Engineering will be in Hall 4 at stand 4449a during the MACH exhibition in the UK from 7-11 April 2014," it told ISMR.

For more details, see www.grenville-engineering.co.uk ■
