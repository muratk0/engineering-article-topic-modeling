# POSCO flies high

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

SOUTH KOREAN STEEL producer, POSCO, will accelerate the use of WP (World Premium) steel products, while supplying stainless steel of its own development, as interior and exterior materials for domestic landmark buildings.

It has cut a deal to supply 380 tons of 446M steel (for roofing) and 120 tons of 329LD steel for Incheon International Airport's Passenger Terminal Expansion Project and the construction of Ice Hockey II Stadium for the Pyeongchang Winter Olympic Games.

POSCO is working to expand sales of stainless steel as exterior and interior materials and products with special surface treatment for domestic and overseas construction projects, through the promotion of its brand image. Recently, the company landed an additional order for a curtain wall project for a multi-purpose apartment complex in Vancouver, Canada.

On 23 December 2016, POSCO's World Premium Materials (WPM) Magnesium Business Division held a seminar on magnesium technology at the POSCO Global R&D Centre in Songdo, Incheon by inviting the winners of races of cars made by college students. In attendance were students from Seoyeong, Jeonju and Gacheon Universities who won the Baja (off-road competition), Formula (on-road competition) and EV (electric vehicle) categories of the race as well as Handong University, Dankook University and the Korea University of Technology and Education. They exhibited strong interest in magnesium as an automotive material.■
