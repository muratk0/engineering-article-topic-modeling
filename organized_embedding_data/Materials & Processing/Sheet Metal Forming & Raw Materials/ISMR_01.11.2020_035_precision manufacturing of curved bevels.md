# Precision manufacturing of curved bevels

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

“Our flame cutting experts can cut curved, flame-cut parts from 400mm-thick sheet steel with six different bevels so precisely that these components meet the tightest of tolerances,” the special machine manufacturer, Jebens GmbH, told ISMR.

An example cited were components of 2,770mm long, 960mm wide and 400mm thick, with a final weight of 8.5 tons each, recently supplied to a customer in Germany. Material of this thickness – S355J2 + N with APZ 3.1 – was supplied by Dillinger. As a subsidiary of Dillinger, Jebens has one of the largest stocks of high-quality steels in the thicknesses ranging from 100 to 650mm.

“We are almost unique in Europe in the production of components with this thickness and with such demanding chamfers. To process sheet metal of this thickness, complex and precise temperature control is necessary. We preheat the sheet metal with lances – including all chamfers. This requires not only the necessary infrastructure, but also specific knowledge,” it added.

Jebens first drilled a recess to prevent the flame from hitting the component with too much energy. Otherwise, even the slightest increase in hardness would cause the jet to slip and create a bevel cut, making the entire component unusable. By drilling, it defined the cutting channel which guides the flame. Jebens then applied several chamfers at different levels within the arc-shaped component contour, according to the drawing: 45-degrees laterally at two levels, 60-degree chamfers on the head sides at one level and a 10-degree chamfer in the inner centre of the arc.

The flame cutting specialist from Korntal-Münchingen, Germany, completed the extreme contours of the components within three weeks, and then cut the chamfers with the required precision. The customer did not have the expertise in its own large welding department, so bespoke production of these parts were handed to the flame cutting specialists at Jebens.
