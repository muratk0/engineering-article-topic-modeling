# Remmert improves handling

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

KEEPING A LOW stock volume that is economically sensible and maintaining flexible handling of sheet metal - the BASIC Tower Sheet Metal 4.0 from Remmert GmbH allows manufacturers to do both. It has given its storage tower a new facelift. Customers, it said, will benefit from the shortened assembly and service times and the contemporary design.

For the modernisation of the BASIC Tower Sheet Metal 4.0, Remmert used feedback from its customers and incorporated client comments into the design of the storage tower. It was also able to implement current requirements for economics and sustainability. Thus, in addition to a modernised layout, a new overall design of the tower was created.

"The result is an optimised arrangement of all components, which guarantees shorter assembly times. Individual defective components can also be quickly and easily located and replaced. Thanks to the same part concept, high availability of spare parts ensures more efficient service times," explained Remmert.
