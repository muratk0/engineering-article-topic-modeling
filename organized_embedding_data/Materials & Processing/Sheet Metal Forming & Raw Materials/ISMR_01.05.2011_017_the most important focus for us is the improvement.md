# The most important focus for us is the improvement of business performance in the tool and die sector

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

We also recently issued a new Statistical Year Book (edition 2011) where you can find very detailed statistical data about the global aspects of the tool and die sector. The purpose of the work is to collect, treat, compute and release data concerning production, exports, imports and value unit indices (value / quantity) for the most important classes of products in the industry under analysis, namely:

■ Dies for pressing, stamping, punching and forming

■ Moulds for plastic and rubber

■ Moulds for metal, injection and compression

■ Moulds for metal, excluding injection and compression.

ISMR: CAN YOU COMMENT ON METALLIC MATERIALS BEING USED FOR TOOLING AND HOW THESE WILL CHANGE/DEVELOP OVER THE NEXT FEW YEARS?

JP Tooling steels are under intensive development. There are many solutions in the material base and coatings which can significantly improve tool performance. The major improvements I see are in the areas of fabrication of HSS materials and hot forming in the near future.

## MEMBERSHIP CRITERIA

The Association may admit as a member an organisation of a country and/or its regions representing the interests of manufacturers of the following products:

■ dies for pressing, stamping, punching and forming

■ injection and compression moulds

■ die casting dies

■ patterns and pattern equipment

■ jigs and fixtures

■ standard tooling components

■ precision machining

■ related products produced by toolmakers.

See www.istma.org for further details or email secretariat@istma.org
