# AutoForm opens new office in Shanghai

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GMBH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has opened a new subsidiary office in Shanghai.

"This new office enables us to be closer to the rapidly growing Chinese market to better serve customers with the full range of AutoForm's products and services. With numerous local and global automotive companies in China, the market is still growing rapidly. With this development, the demand for robust and efficient production processes for high-quality sheet metal parts is on the rise. For more than 15 years now, we have been working very successfully with our distribution partner to support customers and their needs in the Chinese market," said the company.

Additional specialists in sheet metal forming are on-site. AutoForm Software (Shanghai) Ltd. is now its main office in the Chinese market. The team of almost 20 experts in sheet metal stamping (in Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, Changchun and Wuhan) will support over 100 customers in China. These customers are local and global automotive OEMs and toolmakers such as Geely; Chery; BYD; Changan; TOM; GAC; BAIC and SAIC.

AutoForm Engineering GmbH AutoForm offers software solutions for the die-making and sheet metal forming industries along the entire process chain. See www.autoform.com ■
