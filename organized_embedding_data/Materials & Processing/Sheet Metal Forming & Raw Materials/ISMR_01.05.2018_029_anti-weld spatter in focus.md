# Anti-weld spatter in focus

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

ALTHOUGH GOOD PRACTICE can reduce weld spatter, by and large it is a fact of life. Henkel, whose brands include LOCTITE®, TEROSON® and BONDERITE®, has developed a range of simple, spray-on anti-weld spatter agents – the latest addition is BONDERITE® S-MA 98.

"This water-based surface treatment product, part of our functional coatings range, is already helping manufacturers to decrease cleaning time and material wastage as well as improve worker safety. Extensively used in automotive manufacturing and now available to the production industry at large, BONDERITE® S-MA 98 is a colourless liquid that is applied directly to the body-in-white panel or workpiece," Henkel told ISMR.

Supplied in 25-litre quantities, BONDERITE® S-MA 98 can be applied by hand or via a TEROSON® pump spray bottle, designed for accurate dispensing of industrial liquids from bulk containers. It provides an infinitely adjustable spray pattern and holds one litre. The product is easily removed in the alkaline cleaning stage of the conversion coating process or can simply be rinsed off with water." See www.bonderite-solutions.co.uk

## FANUC demonstrates open network capabilities

FANUC UK DEMONSTRATED its next-generation automated capabilities, alongside its latest advances in robotics, RoboMachines, drives and controls, at its stand during this April's MACH exhibition in the UK.

"Our theme this year is 'Connectivity'," said Andrew Armstrong, FANUC UK's sales and marketing manager. "Alongside a selection of our most recent product launches, many of which made their UK debut at MACH, we also demonstrated FANUC's capabilities within the emerging markets of artificial intelligence, Industry 4.0 and the IoT."

The stand, which included a range of robot models across FANUC's collaborative, SCARA and machine-tending portfolios, was connected via a single software platform; the MT-Linki. This scalable operation management tool, which was first unveiled at EMO last year, collected, monitored and displayed the data of every machine on the stand in an easy-to-digest format for all visitors (including ISMR) to see.

"The MT-Linki is an easy and cost-effective way to connect and monitor machines on the factory floor," added Armstrong. "The technology is scalable, with a connection capability of up to 100 machines per server. The data, which can be collected via a dedicated Collector PC software, can then be used to optimise production processes, maximise machine usage or diagnose potential problems, all without having to visit the factory floor."

This software was joined by FANUC's Zero Downtime technology, which was also unveiled at EMO. The ZDT demonstrated FANUC's abilities in condition monitoring, especially suitable for manufacturers that are looking to adopt Industry 4.0.

Another FANUC strategic partner, Cyber-Weld, was also on the MACH stand. The company demonstrated Fujicam, a fully-integrated weld seam tracking system that was launched earlier this year. The Fujicam provides 3D analysis of joints for real-time weld and path control, and interfaces exclusively with the FANUC R30iB-Plus controller.

Also making its UK debut was a laser cutting cell featuring the latest integrated CO2 and fibre laser systems from FANUC. They use the same hardware systems, which means that the laser source can be swapped out when required for maximum flexibility. See www.fanuc.co.uk
