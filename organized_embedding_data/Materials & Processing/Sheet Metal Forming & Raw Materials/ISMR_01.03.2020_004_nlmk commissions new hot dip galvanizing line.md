# NLMK commissions new hot dip galvanizing line

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

TENoVA, a Techint Group company specialising in solutions for the metals and mining industries, recently received official notice to proceed with the new Hot Dip Galvanizing (HDG) line for NLMK Group, a leading international manufacturer of steel products, in Lipetsk (Russia).

The new line, with a yearly throughput of 450 kt/y, can process several input materials (in particular, interstitial free (IF) steel, highstrength steel (HSS) and advanced high-strength steel (AHSS) and will help NLMK to strengthen its market share in automotive applications.

The new HDG line includes a hybrid furnace, which combines a horizontal heating portion with a vertical heat treatment and close cooling chamber, to achieve flexibility, even for severe strip annealing recipes, in addition to a new skin pass and tension leveller section required by multi-phase steel grades. A new On-Line Level 2 Mathematical Model is designed to guarantee continuous high quality in different production scenarios.

“Our cooperation over the years with NLMK was always based on the mutual recognition of reliability and trust," commented Roberto Pancaldi, CEO, Tenova Metals. “This new HDG line is proof of this and I am particularly proud that we are participating in this new project.“
