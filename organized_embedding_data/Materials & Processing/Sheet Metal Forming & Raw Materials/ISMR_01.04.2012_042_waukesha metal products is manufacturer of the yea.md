# Waukesha Metal Products is Manufacturer of the Year

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

WAUKESHA METAL Products, an international metalforming supplier with capabilities including metal stamping, sheet metal fabrication and tool design/build, won the 2011 Wisconsin Manufacturer of the Year Award in the Small Business Category.

The 24th annual Wisconsin Manufacturer of the Year Ceremony was held on 24 February 2012 in Milwaukee (USA) to recognize manufacturers of all sizes for their contributions to Wisconsin. With a total of 52 nominees, Waukesha Metal Products was one of eight companies to win the prestigious award.

Waukesha Metal Products has achieved 61% revenue growth over the last several years and expanded capabilities with the acquisition of Parkview Metal Products, Inc. of Lake Zurich, IL and Metal Skills Plus, LLC of Deforest, WI. The company exports nearly 50% of its products to 34 different countries around the world and is committed to promoting skilled trades education for the manufacturing industry.

Waukesha Metal Products was also awarded MMAC’s Future 50 Award, Inc 500|5000 Award and the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) Productivity Award in 2011. ■
