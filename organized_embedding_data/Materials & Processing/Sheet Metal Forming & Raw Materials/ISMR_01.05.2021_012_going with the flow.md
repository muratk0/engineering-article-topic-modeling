# GOING WITH THE FLOW

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 ISMR]

We highlight a selection of new and innovative waterjet cutting technologies and products for sheet metal manufacturers.

Materials are cut with a high-pressure waterjet in a variety of industrial applications. It is used, for example, in aerospace and vehicle workshops to efficiently cut shapes and parts. For very hard materials, a granular abrasive is added to the water jet to increase the cutting force. Almost any material can be cut such as steels; alloys; titanium and copper as well as rubber; plastic; glass; concrete; ceramics and stone.

The cutting process is cold, eliminating the risk of melting, tearing, hardening or deformation. The absence of heat and dust creates an even safer and environmentally friendly working environment. With minimal material waste, low water consumption and total flexibility, the waterjet offers an environmentally friendly solution for profiling material. There are no chemically contaminated pieces, it generates no noxious fumes during the cutting process and requires no cooling or lubricating oils.

The introduction of five-axis cutting heads has triggered massive growth in waterjet cutting systems. No longer confined to cutting out flat objects, waterjets are now able to produce continuous bevels, revolve around the workpiece and cut at angles from 0-90 degrees.

Below is a selection of the latest new and innovative waterjet technologies and products for sheet metal manufacturers, in alphabetical order.
