# AN 'ALL-IN-ONE' FINISH

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

Arbèl, a Dutch sheet-metalworking company, has bought the first, new Q-Fin SER1200 XL Multibrush machine for combined surface-finishing operations.

ISMR SAYS:

"The new SER1200 XL Multibrush machine combines finishing operations for sheet-metal parts (such as deburring; grinding; rounding; oxide layer removal and line finishing) with directionless grinding and 3mm Radius options."

There is increasing demand from sheetmetal consumers in the directionless finishing of cut parts. Arbèl b.v. Las- en plaatwerkindustrie, a manufacturer in Valkenswaard, The Netherlands, which is active in welding and sheet-metal production, noticed this trend, along with requests for further automation of the finishing process. It therefore collaborated with Dutch manufacturer, Q-Fin, on the supply of a new deburring machine to meet these requirements.

Arbèl supplies its customer with high-quality sheet-metal products and fine construction work in stainless steel, aluminium and steel. It produces products from prototypes and single pieces to small and medium large batchwork. This ranges from laser cutting, deburring and finishing to bending, drilling and welding. Arbèl's highly skilled craftsmen and modern machinery support its high-quality precision sheet-metal work.

Q-Fin's SER1200 XL Multibrush deburring machine, now in operation at Arbèl, offers manufacturers directionless finish of cut parts as well as more automation options and Radius 2mm edge rounding on sheet-metal parts up to 1200mm wide.

Barry Noordman, director at Arbèl, highlighted that extensive automation makes operating the new machine relatively easy (image: Link magazine).

## Strong collaboration process

The SER1200 XL Multibrush, with Arbèl's input, combines "all possible processing operations for sheet-metal parts" (such as deburring; grinding; rounding; Radius 2mm; oxide layer removal and line finishing) with directionless grinding.

"All of these processes are automated: the five workstations are software-controlled, making the machine results almost independent of the operator," Q-Fin told ISMR.

Arbèl already offered non-directional finishing of sheet-metal parts but according to Barry Noordman, director at Arbèl, there was a lot of manual labour involved in this process. "It was time-consuming and no longer sufficient, given the increasing demand," he confirmed.

The time had come for an automated alternative, decided Noordman, who did the necessary preliminary research himself before contacting Q-Fin at the end of 2021. At that time, Q-Fin could not offer him a concrete solution. However, partly due to such a concrete request, Q-Fin started working on it. Noordman looks back positively on that process.

“Q-Fin has a lot of finishing knowledge and actively involved us in the development of the machine. We offered our suggestions and made our wishes clear during the design process," he explained.

These requirements were successfully met by the final machine, as demonstrated by Arbèl's investment in the first unit of the SER1200 Multibrush.

## An international debut

The SER1200 XL Multibrush was successfully introduced at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany, in October last year.

Q-Fin has a lot of finishing knowledge and actively involved us in the development of the machine. We can now choose a directionless or line grain finish for our products.

Anton Bax, director of Q-Fin Quality Finishing Machines, commented: "All possible finishes are pre-programmed with their own parameters; the machine calibrates itself" (image: Link magazine).

Inside the SER1200 XL Multibrush.

The first SER1200 Multibrush machine has been operational at Arbèl's factory in Valkenswaard, The Netherlands, since November 2022. The second SER1200 Multibrush machine will be installed shortly. Two further SER1200 machines have also been sold in Germany.

Since its creation in 2013, Q-Fin has focused on the development, production and sales of machines for deburring, edge rounding and finishing of sheet-metal parts. The company, which now has customers all over the world, also builds dust extraction machines to combine with its finishing machines. It has already picked up several awards including the surface-finishing technology prize at EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover and, in 2019, a Blechexpo award for its F200 XL small parts machine. Q-Fin has set itself the task of introducing at least two technology innovations every year.

“Innovation is an indispensable pillar and continuous development is what the customer expects from Q-Fin. So, we went to work on that growing need for a directionless finish, and also searched for a solution to the growing demand for a Radius 2mm edge rounding on parts up to 1200mm wide. Add to that the increasing customer desire to further automate the finishing process, and we believe that the all-in-one SER1200 XL Multibrush meets all three needs," commented Anton Bax, director of Q-Fin Quality Finishing Machines.

## Multi-tasking operations

Barry Noordman, a director at Arbèl, outlined how the new machine is used by the manufacturer.

## FOCUS ON SURFACE FINISHING

"The manual grinding of sheet-metal parts is now a thing of the past. Despite having three finishing/deburring machines already, there was still a desire for a better finish on the products that we make," he explained.

“Since the SER1200 XL Multibrush machine can create both a directionless and line graining finish, we can now be extremely flexible with just one machine so this advanced all-rounder fits our needs perfectly. I can save the work of two employees with it, freeing up those same people to achieve the growth that we need. The extensive automation makes operating the machine relatively easy. With the Multibrush, we can continue to guarantee perfect 'Arbèl finishing' and our craftsmen can focus again on doing other quality work."

## All-in-one machine

Around 20 motors control five stations in the SER1200 XL Multibrush. This ensures an automated process for every possible finish. Features include:

- Automated process for every possible finish.
- ERP production sheet can be scanned for automatic set-up.
- Highly reproducible process, eliminating operator faults.
- Low power consumption, energy saver.
- Savings on manual labour, extra deburring machines and operators.

“The new machine can now process parts with a maximum width of 1200mm and is fully automated. It has a smarter structure, uses much less energy and can also be connected to an ERP package or scanner, so that the machine is no longer dependent on settings by the machine operators," added Q-Fin.

The SER1200 is available to view, and for part finishing demonstrations, at the Q-Fin demonstration centre in Bergeijk, The Netherlands.
