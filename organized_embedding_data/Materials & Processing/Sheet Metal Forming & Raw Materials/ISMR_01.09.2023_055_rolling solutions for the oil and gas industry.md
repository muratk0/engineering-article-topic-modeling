## Pressure vessel geometry and materials

Pressure vessels, according to their geometry, can be divided into spherical pressure vessels or cylindrical pressure vessels. Spherical pressure vessels, as the name suggests, have a spherical shape. This design allows for uniform distribution of stress, making them suitable for high-pressure applications. On the other hand, cylindrical pressure vessels have a cylindrical shape, often with hemispherical, ellipsoidal heads and ASME torispherical heads for the low-pressure range.

The commonly used construction materials for pressure vessels are carbon steel, stainless steel, the Duplex family, nickel alloys, aluminium and titanium. The choice of material depends upon factors such as the nature of the substances being stored, their corrosiveness and the required pressure and temperature levels. ## Automatic dishing lines

Automatic dishing presses with manipulators and flanging machines (with capacities from 2mm to over 80mm in head thickness in cold forming and up to 100mm in hot forming and a diameter of up to 12000mm) can be provided to manufacture hemispherical heads, torispherical heads and/or ellipsoidal heads, an essential part of pressure vessels.

BOLDRINI equipment, a brand of the Faccin Group, has been developed to meet stringent and consistent production requirements. The PAO series is an advanced and high-speed portal frame press equipped with a CNC manipulator that handles multiple blanks, designed for high forming quality.

"A high-tech automatic playback system is included, allowing uninterrupted repetition, ensuring accuracy and efficiency," explained FACCIN.

The RIBO series from Boldrini are flanging machines designed for simple operation and high productivity of large dished and flanged ends. This model can process a dimensional range from 2-80mm thick (cold forming) or up to 300mm (hot spinning).

Alternatively, the group also provides the FACCIN range. The PPM+MA model is a hydraulic press with manipulator. Its range varies from 150 tons to more than 3,000 tons of force. The HPT system minimises deformation of the frame via forged tie rods that keep the frame connected.

The BF model from FACCIN is a hydraulic and automatic flanging machine suitable both for heads with or without a centre hole and for thicknesses up to a maximum of 50mm in cold forming. It features an electro-welded "C" structure and forged alloy steel rolls for durable performance.
