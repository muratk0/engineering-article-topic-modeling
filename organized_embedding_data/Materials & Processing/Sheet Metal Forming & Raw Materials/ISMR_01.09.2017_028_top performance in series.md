## An eye on steel

"The main challenges for the majority of steel companies today are increasing efficiency and reducing costs. In 2017, a number of companies successfully completed complex initiatives aimed at reducing power consumption, commissioning new facilities, increasing production efficiency and substituting imports with competitive domestic shipments," added the show organiser, who confirmed that, by February 2017, more than 85% of exhibition space in hall 75 had been booked by exhibitors.

Key exhibitors at the event include leading Russian and international steel producers; tube and pipe manufacturers; wire mesh producers; non-ferrous producers; final conversion product manufacturers; steel product suppliers and steel service centres as well as construction companies; oil and gas companies; engineering companies etc.

Steel and engineering companies, as well as steel structure manufacturers (including MMK; TMK; Severstal; Mechel; NLMK and EVRAZ), will exhibit at Metal-Expo'2017. Over 250 steel producers, both in and outside the CIS, include ArcelorMittal; SSAB; Metinvest; Belorussian Steel Works; Ansteel; Dneprospetsstal and Aktyubinsk Rails.

Various winners in competitions such as "The Main Event in the Russian Steel Industry 2017", "Best Sales Network 2017", "Best Russian Steel Service Centre 2017", "Metal Vision 2017" and more will also be awarded their prizes at Metal-Expo'2017. The most significant achievements in the ferrous and non-ferrous industries, engineering and steel/metal process equipment manufacturing will be given Metal-Expo'2017 gold and silver medals.

## Meeting new challenges

"Leading steel equipment manufacturers will also exhibit their innovations at the MetallurgMash'2017 exhibition (part of Metal-Expo'2017), endorsed by The International Union of Metallurgical Equipment Producers and the Russian Engineering Union. Industry leaders (including SMS Group; Ficep; Danieli; EZTM and the Urals Engineering Centre) will present their solutions to the steel industry," added the organiser.

Since the construction industry has always been one of the key users of steel products in Russia, steel producers have (through the years) expanded their production and supply of steel structures, and other types of products, for the construction segment. The MetallStroyForum’2017 international exhibition (part of Metal-Expo'2017) therefore focuses on the interests of construction steel producers and suppliers as well as their endusers. The latest solutions will be presented by companies such as MMK Steel Service Centre, Mechel, Severstal, Metallinvest, Steel Structures and more.

The MetallTransLogistik'2017 international exhibition (also part of Metal-Expo'2017) will focus on the most pressing logistical problems and challenges for the mining and metallurgical industries. The exhibition aims to become a major business platform for transportation and logistics companies, vehicle manufacturers as well as mining and metallurgical industries. Since logistical costs account for around 20% of steel product primary cost, there is still scope to streamline logistics initiatives for further cost reductions.

"The four days of Metal-Expo'2017 will be filled with numerous business meetings between steel market participants and their customers, aimed at encouraging new business and strengthening market positions," concluded the organiser.
