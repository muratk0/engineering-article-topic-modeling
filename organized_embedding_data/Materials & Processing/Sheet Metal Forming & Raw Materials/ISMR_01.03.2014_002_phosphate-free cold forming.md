# PHOSPHATE-FREE cold forming

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

“GARDO HYBRID TECHNOLOGY is the first ever cold forming process for steel without phosphates, based on a conversion coating. It replaces the zinc-phosphate layer with a phosphate-free metal-organic one. This innovative conversion layer provides the same excellent properties and behaviours as traditional zinc-phosphate. However, it fulfils more stringent safety and environmental requirements: the product does not contain any heavy metals and the formation of the metal-organic layer consumes less energy,” explained surface treatment specialist, Chemetall.

The company has launched the technology to respond to market requirements for an environmentally-sound cold forming technology, combined with high forming capabilities. Gardo Hybrid can be easily integrated into current cold forming operations since no major modifications to the lubricant, treatment line or application process is needed. The reason for this, says Chemetall, is that the tribological system has not changed; it is still divided into two parts: the conversion layer providing the unique surface and the lubricant on top.

Benefits include lower energy levels due to lower treatment temperature; lower consumption; reduced sludge formation; no heavy metals (except iron) and no need for additional equipment. See www.chemetall.com. ■
