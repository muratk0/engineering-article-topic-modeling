# THE FLAT UNIVERSE

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

Ongoing progress in coil, strip and sheet production has transformed Marcegaglia from a well-established “tube-maker” to a global player with a wider presence in the steel market.

Marcegaglia’s coil processing activities have developed across various plants and facilities, but it has kept its production core at its manufacturing complex in the harbour docks of Ravenna, on the Adriatic Sea. Here, the group has unrolled a massive ten-year investment plan that has strongly enhanced Marcegaglia’s role as a flat steel producer, increasing its overall capacity of cold rolled material and doubling its production of hot dip galvanised and colour-coated steel.

Ongoing progress in coil, strip and sheet production has transformed Marcegaglia from a well-established “tube-maker” to a global player with a wider presence in the steel market. In addition, Marcegaglia Ravenna is also the main logistics centre of the group, with its huge facilities for receiving, storing and shipping coils. Here, using an 800-metre harbour dock (privately owned and managed) equipped with concrete floor and coil cradles, served by five cranes and an internal railway line, Marcegaglia handles about 3.5 million tonnes of steel material per year.

## Stainless steel growth

The latest investment in the group’s headquarters plant in Gazoldo degli Ippoliti (Mantua, Northern Italy) has propelled Marcegaglia even further into the stainless steel business, with the production of stainless steel coils, sheets and flat bars, by doubling the number of dedicated units to widen its offer and add flexibility and just-in-time capability.

A total of two annealing and pickling lines and two cold-rolling and skinpassing lines are now processing hot rolled steel (1D finish from 2 to 6mm in thickness), and cold rolled steel (2B finish) with in-line edge slitting. Downstream, Marcegaglia has facilities to manufacture stainless sheet, strip and flat bar. The new cut-to-length unit in its Gazoldo degli Ippoliti plant can work both hot rolled and cold rolled materials with optional polishing (220-240-320 grit), scotch brite brushing and the option of protection with paper or laser film on each sheet.

“The group’s investments in this area have further reinforced its role as the world’s number one manufacturer of stainless steel welded tubes, supported by production plants in Brazil, USA, China, Russia and Italy,” Marcegaglia told ISMR. ■

## Top and bottom deburring machine

Machine for edge rounding and finishing of parts in a single pass. The oscillating system is equipped with 12 brushes The 6 top and 6 bottom, left and right rotating grinding flap brushes process the parts on both sides simultaneously.

## Multi rotating brush machine

Machine for uniformly deburring rounding edges and fishing of parts. For laser, punched and machined parts, or parts with pockets/protrusions in all directions. The rotating carrousel, equipped with 8 left and right rotating abrasive flap brushes, processes the parts in all directions, no matter how fine the edges of the work pieces are.
