# Edge preparation

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

VAPORMATT has re-designed its high performance wet blasting system, the Jaguar, to further enhance performance. It has re-developed the machine to align it even more closely with needs of its end users, particularly those looking to achieve superior edge preparation on cutting tools.

“The enclosed, automated process of the Vapormatt Jaguar offers important production benefits, including faster processing and more effective and consistent cleaning actions than alternative systems,” said Tim Berry, tooling specialist at Vapormatt. “The new Jaguar boasts eight blast guns that all focus on the tools being processed using varying angles to ensure complete coverage of the critical edges and thorough cleaning of the tool faces.”

The Vapormatt Jaguar incorporates both rinsing and drying stages into its overall process so that that additional steps and machines are not required. The unit is selfcontained and ventilated for low maintenance and ease of access, making it suitable for quiet and tightly controlled environments. See www.vapormatt.com
