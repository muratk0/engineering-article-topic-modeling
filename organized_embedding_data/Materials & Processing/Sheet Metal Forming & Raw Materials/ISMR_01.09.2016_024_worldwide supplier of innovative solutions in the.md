## HANS WEBER MASCHINENFABRIK GmbH (WEBER) www.metallschleifmaschine.de

WEBER grinding technology provides specially developed solutions to meet various sheet metal treatment requirements for surface grinding, edge breaking/rounding, descaling and decorative grinding patterns. Dry or wet grinding machines are designed to carry out these different procedures. All procedures can also be carried out in machine lines with double-sided operation.

"The design of WEBER grinding stations enables the installation of several heads in one machine in a space-saving manner. This means that rounding, descaling and a hairline finish can all be carried out 'in one go' using a single machine," the manufacturer explained.

WEBER DR planetary head technology or WEBER MRB multirotation brush technology are used for rounding and descaling. These machining stations are each made up of individual heads arranged next to each other with widely overlapping areas of effect. This arrangement ensures that the machining quality of edges and cut faces remains even across the entire working width. A definable, consistently even and reproducible result can be achieved, said the manufacturer.

## MARCEGAGLIA

www.marcegaglia.com

IT IS ONE YEAR since the launch of Marcegaglia's new organization that separated its core business activities with carbon steel from those using stainless steel. Three new companies were created and dedicated to specific sectors - Marcegaglia Carbon Steel, Marcegaglia Specialties and Marcegaglia Plates. Now, the Italian specialist is also strengthening its positioning in terms of production, presenting several process and product innovations in the flat steel sector at EuroBLECH 2016.

The first example is an EU-financed project "First worldwide full-scale demonstration of Alternative Current (AC) electrolytic pickling on carbon and stainless coils (ACE PICK)". The intermediate results of the project, which has been running for over 15 months at Marcegaglia's headquarters, demonstrate that the twin targets (improving surface quality of the processed products whilst increasing the productivity and flexibility of the line) have been achieved through the development of an innovative electrolytic pickling process based on Alternating Current (AC), instead of the Direct Current (DC) commonly used.

"The AC electrolytic pickling process can significantly reduce (by up to 50%) the treatment times normally required in conventional processes, on both hot- and cold-rolled products," Marcegaglia explained.

Other company targets concern Advanced High Strength Steels (AHSS) for the automotive industry and the increase in production of hot-dip galvanized dual-phase DP600 steels (manufactured in accordance with EN 10346). Its internal R&D team is also examining higher material grades (DP800 and CP800) and the company has placed a focus on the development of the new grade of semi-processed magnetic steel (M310-50K) through the optimisation of the microstructural characteristics of the semi-finished product using thermomechanical processing (TMP) conditions and cold rolling, annealing cycle and skin-pass processes.

In the pre-painted steels sector, Marcegaglia has developed two new finishes with high aesthetic qualities along with the safety of the steel itself. Its new Wooden Pattern simulates wood surfaces, in four colours and three finishes, through a process of painting and finishing directly onto steel. It has also created a new polyester resin coating, dubbed SHIMOCO.

## BAUST STANZTECHNOLOGIE GmbH www.baust-stanztechnologie.de

AT EUROBLECH 2016, Baust will showcase an all-mechanical rotary punch cassette with a unique design, the BRV 735. The BRV 735 is designed to be combined with a rollformer. As the rollformer pulls the strip of sheet metal through the rotating tools, the punch tools transfer the pulling force into punching power. This enables the BRV735 to function without a motor, although it can run at rapid line speeds and, said Baust, will always stay synchronized.

"Its design and tool size make it suitable for high-speed punching of lightweight profiles for dry walls, ceilings or corner beads. Coil loading and tool change has been made very easy – the tools can be opened, but will always stay synchronized. The BRV735 can be easily integrated into rollforming lines. With a small footprint, and coil-driven, it runs automatically at the same speed as the rest of the line, even at line speeds of up to 100m/min," explained the manufacturer.
