# Northwest Pipe Company

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 The Fabricator]

Northwest Pipe Company

has been in operation manufacturing steel pipe and tubular products since 1966. The products that are manufactured support water works, energy, transportation and construction industries, and are made to strict standards including AWWA, API, and ASME. Most of the facilities have ISO 9000:2008 certifications. Projects are engineered to customer requirements and therefore include a wide variety of internal pipe linings, external coatings, and custom fabricated fittings. Pipe manufacturing processes include Longitudinal ERW, Spiral, and rolled and welded, with diameters ranging from 4.5” through 156” and wall thickness up to 4”. Various end configurations are available, including the proprietary Permalok system. Lining and coating options include a wide variety of paints, cement, hot and cold applied plastics, and coal tar enamel.

The Davi plate rolls have now been in service at the Adelanto, California plant since 2008. The primary products manufactured on these rolls are heavy wall piling and casing, along with a collapsible structural internal lining system that is used to repair and renew ageing concrete pipelines. These “state of the art” plate rolls have been very reliable and produce an exceptionally high quality finished product. They have played a role in helping Northwest Pipe to retain market leadership in the segments that they serve.
