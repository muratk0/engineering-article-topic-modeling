# Precision slitting line

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

CAMU Srl was set up in 1980 by two young brothers and entrepreneurs from Vicenza, Italy; Loris Basso and Fabio Basso. In over four decades, the company has become a specialist in the construction of slitting and cut-to-length lines, thanks to continuous innovation.

“An important service centre recently commissioned us to build a precision slitter for high-strength steels and stainless steel, with strengths of up to 650N/mm², material thicknesses of 0.2-3mm, material widths of 2050mm and speeds of 200m/minute. This slitting line, customised to specific needs, has achieved the goal of efficiency, productivity and quality of the slit coils," said CAMU.

The slitting line features a loading coil car; decoiler; paper rewinder; sliding base, snubber roll holder; telescopic peeler; pinch roll; crop shear; edge guide; PVC applicator; circular shear and automatic blade change. The consolidated CAMU system for automatic change of the cutting set is designed to ensure speed, ease of execution of the operation, productivity and comfort for the operator.

The mobile shoulder of the shear can be opened at 90°; a hydraulic transfer arm allows the rapid expulsion and introduction of the blade set from the shear to the four-arm unloading turnstile (each equipped with two shafts for engagement in the shear arbours) to facilitate cutting formation composition.

“The braking unit with mobile frame, with the option of semi-automatic and automatic operation, enables high levels of efficiency, productivity and flexibility. Below there are press rollers; a scrap recoiler; tables for looping; separator shafts and a tensioner with felt and rolls, which keep the sliding strips in counter-stretch to enable perfect rewinding of the strip (without scratching the surface)," outlined the coil-processing specialist.

"After the tensioner is positioned: an oil separator; deflector rollers; recoiler; separator shaft; coil expulsion plate; paper decoiler; four-arm unloading carousel and unloading L-shaped coil car, equipped with a pressure arm to keep the coils in place, swing into action. Off-line strapping allows rapid restart of the slitter. A high level of automation completes the supply of the new line, together with advanced control systems," it added.

CAMU is present in five continents and 97 countries. It has supplied over 730 installed lines. For over 42 years, it has aimed to supply “the complete coil experience”.■
