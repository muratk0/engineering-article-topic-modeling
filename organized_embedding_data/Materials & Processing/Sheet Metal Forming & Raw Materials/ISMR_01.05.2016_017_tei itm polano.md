On 9 June 2016, the publisher of the “Nowa Stal (New Steel)” magazine will hold its second conference on the domestic aluminium industry. “Aluminium Business Trends and Technologies” is, it says, the only such conference in Poland which also provides an opportunity to present the current condition of one of the fastest-developing branches of the domestic industry. There will be discussions on the state of the Polish, European and worldwide aluminium industries, the international expansion of domestic companies and the applications for aluminium in sectors such as architecture, aviation and renewable energy.

A seminar on “Volume Thermal Processing of Metals,” prepared by the Institute of Precision Mechanics and organized for industry representatives, will be concentrated around the Surfex exhibition, and will also take place at the event. The seminar is aimed at those interested in structure-changing technologies and procedures as well as the improvement of alloy properties and includes the following topics: increasing steel corrosion resistance; nanostructuring of annealing medium alloys – the TRIP effect and rapid prototyping technology for biomedical engineering.

## Science meets technology

As part of the Surfex exhibition, the editors of “Lakiernictwo Przemysłowe (Industrial Varnishing)” magazine will organise a Skills Battlefield. This is an important forum where knowledge and information on the latest surface treatment achievements will be shared. The Skills Battlefield will involve leading producers presenting novel surface preparation technologies for lacquering and coating as well as experts answering questions from visitors.

A two-day event on "3D Solutions”, devoted to 3D printing and scanning technology, will also take place from 8-9 June 2016. This event will provide participants with access to innovative solutions, new devices and knowledge on related topics. Visitors will be able to see 3D printers and print-outs as well as 3D scanners. They can also consult experts, exchange experiences and explore the technology ‘live’.
