# Sheet metal for medical

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

JOHNS HOPKINS HOSPITAL in Baltimore (US), has completed a massive US$ 1.1 billion expansion project that included the construction of two 12-story towers – the Sheikh Zayed Tower for cardiovascular and critical care and the Charlotte R. Bloomberg Children’s Center for paediatric care. It is the largest clinical building of its kind in the United States with 1.5 million square feet.

SMACNA contractor, United Sheet Metal Inc., used four million pounds of galvanized and stainless steel sheet metal for the HVAC systems, more than 14,000 linear feet (2.65 miles) of linear diffuser and custom distribution plenums and collaborated with other trades on this enormous three-year project. USM installed more than 12,000 air devices; 19 custom air handling units; 90 exhaust, return and supply fans; and 3,300 variable air volume boxes for supply, return and exhaust.

The expansion, including the two connected towers, occupies five acres and comprises 17 levels. The new hospital has a main entrance larger than a football field, 560 patient rooms (including both acute and intensive care) 33 modern operating rooms and a new emergency department.
