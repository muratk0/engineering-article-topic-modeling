# The indelible impact of 'thinning engineering'

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

"Don't underestimate the impact of thinning engineering; it goes beyond what you might imagine. In the process of ESG improvement planning, almost all companies focus on increasing green energy, reducing carbon emissions and conserving water resources. However, today we are introducing a new concept: thinning engineering. It involves reducing the thickness of materials used in products without compromising strength and functionality," press specialist, INGYU Precision Industries Co., Ltd., told /SMR.

"For instance, in many aluminium components of tablets and laptops, the thickness has been reduced from 0.3mm to 0.2mm resulting in a 33% reduction in aluminium mining. Further reducing the thickness to 0.15mm achieves a 50% reduction in aluminium mining. The point here is the change in design thinking, which not only reduces aluminium mining but also saves high energy and water resources while freeing up surplus manpower and equipment capacity," it continued.

According to the press specialist, thinning engineering is a strategy that not only increases profits for companies but also serves as the optimal solution for overall ESG environmental improvement, fostering a mindset for the Earth's irreplaceable sustainability.

“The concepts discussed have been validated in numerous world-class electronic products, and we are ready to provide consultation and assistance in this technical and strategic domain," added INGYU.

INGYU Precision Industries Co., Ltd is a Taiwanese precision stamping press manufacturer, established in 1970, that provides high-speed presses for automotive, can-making, motor lamination and complex forming applications.

It launched several new stamping presses last year as flexible solutions to meet new and changing challenges and applications. This includes the INGYU ApexKA series of adjustable stroke, high-speed presses (with stroke lengths ranging from 15-64mm); the HV350-3000 multi-point, high-speed press and the latest SVL series high-speed press with a vertical accuracy of up to 0.001mm.

INGYU highlighted key features of its HV series multi-point high-speed press, where the overall clearance of the machine is extremely small and the bed frame deflection is within 0.05mm/m.

"This ensures the production accuracy of electric vehicle motor cores with a material thickness of less than 0.25mm," explained the press specialist.

Every second counts during the manufacturing process, and INGYU's multipoint, high-speed presses are equipped with a 'slide quick lift system' that can quickly resolve mould issues without time-consuming recalibration, saving users valuable production time.

"The INGYU DDH series double-action cupping press maintains the parallelism of the slide through an excellent precision guide system, ensuring stable BDC repeatability and precision for the mass production of battery cases," concluded the manufacturer.■
