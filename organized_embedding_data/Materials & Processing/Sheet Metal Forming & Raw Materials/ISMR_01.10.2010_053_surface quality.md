# SURFACE QUALITY

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Sheet metal contamination is becoming more of an issue, especially for outer skin panels or softer materials. Dirt or deposits on the conveyor belt have an influence on the quality of the finished product. An integrated belt cleaning device from BTU is able to clean all types and sizes of polymer (toothed) belts e.g. timing belts for a blank stacker as well as transporting or discharging belts, tool conveyor belts, or other applications of polymer belts in metal sheet handling. The belt cleaner has different cleaning modes to choose from during operation such as air vacuum cleaning; air vacuum cleaning with brush and air vacuum cleaning with brush and cleaning fluid. The cleaning device washes and dries the belts in a special closed chamber construction where the pollution is removed from the belts. The principle is a specially developed low pressure cleaning system which works without scrapers.

The BTU blank stacker is often installed in a safety cabin to protect the process against any dust and prevent unauthorised access.

BTU’s roller cleaning unit is designed to clean the tread of (measure) rollers of e.g. metal rolling mills automatically. During the rolling of metals (steel, aluminium), residuals of the metal, in the form of metal slug, will remain on the tread of the rollers. This can cause problems especially with measuring rollers. There are two types of roller cleaning units; the upper-mounted grinding type and the roller-mounted scraper type.
