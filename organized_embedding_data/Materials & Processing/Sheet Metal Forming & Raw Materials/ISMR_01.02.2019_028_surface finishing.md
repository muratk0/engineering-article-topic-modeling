# Surface finishing

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

AS A UK AND Eire supplier of deburring, polishing and finishing machines, Ellesco is opening the doors of its Christchurch, Dorset (UK) facility for a week-long Open-House event. Beginning on 25 February 2019, the mini-exhibition will focus on its Timesavers range and visitors are challenged to bring along components to see how Ellesco and Timesavers can process parts, whether they need basic surface graining, edgebreaking or radiussing.

"This event will be a showcase of surface finishing covering every aspect from deburring, surface finishing and precision grinding through to heavy slag and laser oxide removal," said Vincent Simonis, Managing Director, Ellesco. "We are happy for customers to bring along sample parts and challenge our team to deliver the result they are looking for in terms of surface finish and edge preparation."

Timesavers machines on show, and under power, at the event will cover everything from manual through to fully automated processes and include the: 10-series manual Abrasive Disc and Cup Brush machine; 12-600-W entry level Abrasive Belt machine; 22-1100-WMdMd Belt + Cup Brush machine; 32-1100-WRb Belt + Rotary Brush machine and the company's flagship 42-1350-WRb Belt +Rotary Brush machine.

Ellesco will also showcase a wide range of consumable materials and will demonstrate capabilities from deburring plastic-coated aluminium sheet through to laser oxide removal from cut edges. The event will also be supported by representatives from the Timesavers factory. Email sales@ellesco.co.uk
