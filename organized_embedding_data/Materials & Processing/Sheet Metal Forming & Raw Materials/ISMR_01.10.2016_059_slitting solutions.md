# Slitting solutions

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

TOA DR ENTERPRISE CO., Ltd is a leading manufacturer of slitting machines in Taiwan. Established in 1972, it has devoted itself to developing professional slitting solutions for use across a variety of industries, with an eye on quality, efficiency, economy and originality of design. A large range of accessories are available. Solutions include slitting lines, slitter knives and peripheral accessories.

“Our slitting solutions are simple, effective and easily set up to achieve optimum results," said the manufacturer. “Aerospace, automotive and electronic part industries are just a few of the sectors currently utilising TOA DR slitting solutions in their manufacturing processes. Due to the increasing demand for materials such as BIMETAL; titanium; nickel alloy; silicon steel and non-ferrous alloys, we have developed solutions to meet every client's needs."

The materials for the knife tools are imported high-speed cutting tool steels such as SKD, SKH and tungsten steel. The knife is suitable for the highspeed cutting of various metals and non-iron metals, for example, rolled steel plates, galvanised, aluminium and silicon steel plates. Hardness and precision quality are strictly controlled, said the company, allowing a thickness tolerance of within ±0.001mm.
