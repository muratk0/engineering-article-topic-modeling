# An eye on steel

[TARİH: 01.05.2017 ISMR]

AT THE PARIS Air Show (hall 4, booth C38), Deutsche Edelstahlwerke and Ugitech will be featuring their dedicated product and service offerings for the aerospace industry. The subsidiaries of the SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH Group offer customers stainless and engineering steels in diameters from 5- to 400mm. In a move to bolster competitiveness, the steel group further intends on investing around €17m in a new furnace installation and associated logistics facility to be NADCAP-certified at Ugitech by 2019. This will be the third SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH production site with NADCAP-approved furnaces. Production capacity is to be increased considerably by 2019.

At the Paris Air Show, the affiliates will focus on their consolidated special steel expertise by supplying raw material into the production of safety-relevant components such as drag links and undercarriage components. Customers include stockholders and distributors, metalworking companies, part, component and system suppliers as well as OEMs.

Ugitech and Deutsche Edelstahlwerke are qualified as approved suppliers by OEMs and Tier 1 suppliers, such as manufacturers of aircraft engines and landing systems. The requirement for this is the process reliability of the value-add chain – from steel production to quality assurance to delivery – as well as reproducible high product quality. Other examples of the quality certification of both our steel companies are AS9100, AS9120 and NADCAP for heat treatment," added the manufacturers. See www.schmolz-bickenbach.com ■
