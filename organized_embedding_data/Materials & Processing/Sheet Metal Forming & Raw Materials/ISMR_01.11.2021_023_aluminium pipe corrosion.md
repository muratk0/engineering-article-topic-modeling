# Aluminium pipe corrosion

[TARİH: 01.11.2021 ISMR]

With more than 50 million acres (20,234,282 hectares) of irrigated farmland in the U.S. alone, irrigation plays a major role in the world's food supply. However, irrigation is costly and creates a naturally corrosive environment inside metal irrigation pipes and centre pivot systems. The large percentage of pipes made of aluminium are at even greater risk in areas where groundwater carries a high level of chlorides and sulfates.

To meet this need, Cortec® Corporation recently developed EcoLine® AL-Corr™, a bio-based corrosion inhibitor solution for the protection of aluminium (AL 3003 and AL 1100) irrigation pipes during active and intermittent use.

So, how much protection does EcoLine® AL-Corr™ offer?

"Based on results of ASTM G31 immersion testing, the product is able to reduce corrosion rates by more than 90% in immersion conditions. Testing on two aluminium alloys over five weeks at 104°F (40°C) showed 91.18% protection for AL 3003 metal and 99.03% protection for AL 1100 metal," Cortec told SMR.

Cortec® R&D formulated EcoLine® AL-Corr™ with biodegradable materials approved for use in personal care products and food preservation. It also selected materials with a high percentage of renewable content, enabling EcoLine AL-Corr™ to become a USDA Certified Biobased Product that contains 81% USDA certified biobased content. In this way, Cortec® has taken a double-sided approach to sustainability in the development of a corrosion inhibitor to help water irrigation equipment last longer.
