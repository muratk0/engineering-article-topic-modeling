# FOCUS ON COIL PROCESSING

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Soenen sees a growing demand for high speed, high capacity lines”

ISMR: Please outline current trends, usage and applications of coil processing systems for sheet metal work? Are your customers’ requirements changing? Have you noticed any regional trends in different parts of the world?

PQ: In general, our customers (mainly the larger and internationally-oriented ones) are investing in quality and line capacity. I see a growing demand for high speed and high capacity lines where we are pushing against the physical limits of today’s technologies. A general requirement is, of course, to maintain those increased specifications during three shifts per day, seven days per week.

For example, we recently delivered four high speed lines for cutting and stacking silicon steel, used for transformer core manufacturing. These lines are able to cut and stack transformer sheets at a capacity of 180 sheets per minute or cutting and stacking at three sheets per second (average sheet length of 1350mm, average sheet width of 200mm).

In rollforming, suppliers are searching for solutions where they can implement higher capacities by offering their customers automatic tool change, flying shear cutting and movable punching units to reduce downtime as much as possible. In this respect, material handling is becoming more and more important (since higher capacities mean higher volumes and more raw material and finished products to handle in shorter times) and that’s where our new machine, the Quickstack, is becoming attractive.

On the other hand, we see that customers are investing resources wisely and that they are concerned about controlling production costs because this is directly reflected in their turnover and profit.

I see no regional differences in our customers. Emerging countries currently need a lot of energy installations (transformers cores etc.) and therefore cutting and stacking lines for transformer production are doing well over there.
