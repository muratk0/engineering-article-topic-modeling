# The right FINISH

SAINT-GOBAIN Abrasives, a leading global manufacturer and supplier of performance engineering abrasives, has introduced its portfolio of Norton Rapid Prep products, including Vortex Rapid Prep and Rapid Prep for high performance surface finishing.

“Norton Rapid Prep surface conditioning discs are made of non-woven nylon impregnated with abrasive grain and proprietary smear-free resin for surface finishing applications. The Norton Rapid Prep portfolio is suitable for various applications including the removal of defects, oxides and corrosion, de-burring, mould cleaning and the blending of uneven edges and marks. With its patented advanced design, Norton Vortex Rapid Prep has up to four times the performance of conventional surface finishing products,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

## FLEXIBLE WELDING

The chained welding systems at the CLOOS booth attracted visitors at Schweissen & Schneiden

AT the recent Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition, Carl Cloos Schweisstechnik GmbH showcased its versatile processes and new solutions for manual and automated welding. One of its stand highlights was the fully-automated chained robot welding system. Here, QIROX robots, welding technology and the QINEO power sources, workpiece positioners and handling systems work in a synchronised fashion.

The new 6kg QRH-280 welding robot is part of the automated welding system. This small, light and highly dynamic robot is suitable for CLOOS cell solutions. The compact design saves production area space and guarantees good accessibility to fixtures. CLOOS’s new MasterPlus operating panel offers easy, quick and intuitive control of QINEO power sources. It is suitable for right- and left-handed operators.

Another innovation from CLOOS is wireless operation of welding power sources. Installation of QINEO Data Manager software on a tablet PC allows central operation and monitoring of several power sources via a WLAN connection. Local operating panels at the individual power sources are therefore no longer necessary. See www.cloos.de

## Pressing for action

SEYI Presses Europe will be exhibiting its SD2-200 servo press at the Blechexpo show in Stuttgart. The SD2-200 is a direct drive servo press that includes SEYI’s latest technology for stamping, drawing and forming operations with minimum energy consumption. The press is pictured here with a large-sized bolster and two connecting rods.

“This highly advanced servo press will be operating with a sophisticated tool to show the capabilities – via a live demonstration – of its advanced drawing, stamping and coining operations performed in just one single cycle thanks to the robust design of the SEYI servo motor which enables high torque at low speeds plus a precise and responsive motor controller and control system,” SEYI told ISMR.

With these capabilities, SEYI is focusing on clear cost reductions for users in daily production, improvements in production processes and quality improvements of the parts being manufactured.

Visit SEYI at Hall 8, Booth 8202 at Blechexpo.
