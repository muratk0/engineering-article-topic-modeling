# Slitting and coil packing

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

CAMU, A LEADING manufacturer of coil processing lines and sheet metal coil levelling since 1980, is located in Bressanvido (VI, Italy) and bases its production and assembly in two facilities (a total of 4200m²) with a staff of 48 people. “We design and build innovative machines, 100% made in Italy, such as cut-to-length lines with 4H-6H leveller; slitting lines; recoiling and inspection lines; coil-to-coil; combined lines; packaging lines and feeding lines as well as special purpose machinery to satisfy the particular needs of each customer,” it told ISMR.

It delivered a 25-ton, 1550 x 4 slitting and coil packing line in 2019 to an important service centre supplier in the European automotive sector, which integrates a slitter and packaging system. The line, which incorporates an automatic bladechanging system and processes high resistivity materials, is designed for efficient, productive and compact production. The coil packing line produces around 50/55 small coils per hour, with coil widths ranging from 41-1600mm (maximum number of cuts at 0.5mm are 30, and at 4mm are 12). Maximum strip packing weight is 4000kg. The line is completed by an intuitive electrical panel and software with alarm systems for scheduled maintenance or possible breakdowns.

“ We have incorporated effective systems to reduce line downtime as well as a practical coil car (within reach of operators); blade pack loading; a circular blade shear; precision cutting heads and an unloading recoiler with elevator and coil transfer cut on the 4-arm unloading turnstile,” added CAMU.
