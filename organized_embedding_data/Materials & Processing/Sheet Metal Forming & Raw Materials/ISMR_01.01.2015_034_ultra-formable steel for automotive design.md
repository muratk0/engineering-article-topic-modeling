# Ultra-formable steel for automotive design

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

TATA STEEL, Europe’s second-largest steel producer, has just launched its new, highly formable, hot-dip galvanised steel, DX57-GI Hyperform.

“The product offers excellent deep drawing and stretching properties that enable automotive engineers to design more complex parts for the exterior and interior of the car. This, in turn, allows them to create vehicles that are more appealing to their customers with better functionality. Typical applications include particularly complex exposed automotive parts such as body sides and trunk lids. In addition, DX57 HyperForm can be processed through customers’ existing lines and guarantees a low reject rate, ensuring improved press shop efficiency,” Tata Steel explained.

The development follows and complements its recent launch of SericaTM, a premium hot-dip galvanised surface finish, which can be applied to DX57 HyperForm to achieve a high quality surface finish. With guaranteed low waviness after forming – the long-wave roughness of the surface, which is visible to the human eye – Serica offers customers high surface quality and paint appearance for exposed automotive panels, even for paint systems without primer.

DX57-GI Hyperform is available now from Tata Steel’s DVL3 and Zodiac galvanising lines in The Netherlands and the United Kingdom. The product comes in a range of dimensions, from 0.6-1.2mm thick and up to 1800mm wide and can be supplied in both unexposed and exposed surface quality with a number of different finishes.
