Purging system

Huntingdon Fusion Techniques (HFT®) has introduced the Quick-Purge® II pipe weld purging system.

One of the new features is Purge-Gate™, a device that prevents the inflatable dams from bursting caused by overinflation. No matter how much pressure or flow the operator puts into the system, the device delivers the right amount, eliminating the need for setting up and trials.

The purging system is offered in sizes up to 20 in. dia. with three hoses as standard: the inert gas inflation and purge hose, an auxiliary purge/ exhaust hose, and a polycarbonate tube that can be connected directly to the Weld Purge Monitor®.

For weld purge readings that can be taken from the end of the purging system, the red tube on the monitor joins to the red tube on the purging system by a single quick-fit connection to prevent leaks.

Combined saw/drill unit

Advanced Machine & Engineering Co. (AME) offers the Amsaw R series saw, designed for high-production cutting of high-alloy rails for the railroad industry.

Features include a saw blade changeover time of less than 3 min., hardened spindle gears ground for minimum backlash, a blade guide and dampening device for blade stabilization, and dry operation.

An electronic overload device monitors cutting performance and stops the saw feed when necessary. The heavy machine base is ribbed and braced for rigidity. A chip conveyor conveys chips to the rear of the machine and into a tote box. The saw can be combined with an integrated or inline rail hole drill machine for bolted joints at the rail ends or with infeed and outfeed material handling systems.

Gas mixer

Witt has introduced the MG1000, a gas mixer with a flow rate of up to 44,617 SCFH. The new system generates a mixture from two noncorrosive, nontoxic gases; an EEx version is available for flammable gases.

Covering mix percentages of 0 to 100 percent, any concentration can be mixed and delivered in any volume. Any pressure fluctuations in the gas supply are compensated by the integrated pressure equalizing system.

The electropneumatic proportional mixing valve is controlled by the Gas Control 50 digital controller. This allows remote control of the mixer, including data management and evaluation, as well as export function via various interfaces. The gas mixture is monitored permanently by an analysis module before delivery.

The system is designed to demonstrate, develop, and teach proper welding techniques, robot programming, and welding skills. Its mobile platform facilitates on-site training and transforms any space into a robotic welding training environment.

The cell comprises an Aristo® U5000i power source, KUKA KR6 900 robot and controller, touchscreen robot pendant, expandable cell walls, and interlock safety system. It features a steel tube frame with acrylic windows and heavy-duty casters for mobility.

Hybrid-drive press brake

The HD-ATC press brake from Amada America Inc. features a hybrid drive system engineered for low energy consumption and requiring less hydraulic fluid than conventional machines, the company states.

Repeatability is ±0.00004 in. To maintain bending accuracy while processing materials that vary in type, thickness, and grain direction, the machine is equipped with BI (bend indicator) sensors that use probe (BI-S) and laser (BI-L) technology for trial-free bending. Bend angles are measured and adjusted on-the-fly to ensure correct angles on the first bend and to monitor and adjust angles throughout the run, the manufacturer reports.

It is equipped with an automatic tool changer that can store more than 86 ft. of precision tooling. The system’s 4-axis manipulator selects and places punches and dies from the tool magazine.

The bending system is suitable for small lot sizes, short lead-times, and complex tool layouts. Tool changes are performed in 3 min. or less. An auto-slide, three-position foot pedal helps ensure fast stage bending. The large open height allows for a variety of part geometries to be formed. The variable-slit crowning feature helps ensure consistent angle accuracy.

Amada America Inc. 877-262-3287

www.amada.com

Metalworking fluid for plasma cutting tables

Chemtool Inc. offers RPA Flame Kool, a moderate-duty, synthetic metalworking fluid formulated for general use on plasma cutting tables.

The fluid provides a balance of cooling, wetting detergency, corrosion protection, and workpiece visibility for plasma cutting of ferrous alloys.

The corrosion inhibitor protects against rust and staining of gray iron, cast iron, and ferrous alloys. The fluid is stable in a variety of process water chemistries. It does not deposit a sticky or tacky residue on machine or cut surfaces, and it will not gum up ways or stick switches.

Formulated with additional reserve alkalinity, the product contains no formaldehyde-releasing chemistries. It also can be used as a leak-detection or NDT fluid for ferrous metals and ferrous alloy assemblies.
