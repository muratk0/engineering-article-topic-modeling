# Robust and sustainable spray lubrication

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

"We will present efficient and resource-preserving spray lubrication systems for punching and forming applications at this year's Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2023). Show visitors to our stand 8309 in Hall 8 will see our homogeneous minimum-quantity spray lubrication system using less than 0.5g of oil per m², and a particularly robust variant of the spray.xact c with a new housing structure (among other products)," Technotrans told ISMR.

"We will also provide insights into the development of a system solution for hotmelt applications. Expanding our product portfolio enlarges the application spectrum of our products and provides users with more flexibility. Potential application areas for these spray lubrication systems include cold rolling processes, the forming of aluminium and tool lubrication," it continued.

The manufacturer's solutions work with high-frequency valves, without the use of compressed air, and are designed to ensure precise and mist-free application of oil. An extraction system is not needed and no oil that could otherwise enter the production environment is wasted, explained the company. Machine cleaning and maintenance work are consequently much reduced.

"Sustainability has also become a driving force in punching and forming technologies. The economical use of energy and resources is one of the most important objectives today in metalworking companies," explained Hary Kosciesza, Business Development Manager. "Our spray lubrication systems help users to verifiably reduce their ecological footprints."

At Blechexpo, Technotrans will unveil a robust variant of its spray.xact c, featuring a double-wall housing structure and a spray lubrication width of up to 2,000mm. It is designed to meet demanding requirements that require a high level of stability and resistance, such as cold-rolling processes where impact can occur in the process. Additional collision protection is possible where the upper housing half can be moved vertically by up to 300mm to create more space for passing through coils.

Visitors to the exhibition will also see Technotrans' spray.xact metal, which features a patented nozzle technology that sprays minimum quantities, below 0.5g of oil per square metre, without using compressed air. This allows high-viscosity oils to be evenly applied to blanks and coils.

"The high-frequency pulses of our spray valves ensure gap-free application, preventing material damage," outlined the company.

The application of hot melts to coils for effective protection against corrosion will be another highlight at Blechexpo. A new system solution will feature temperature control and spray lubrication.

"We control the temperature of the entire system, including the line system and spray valves, to ensure that the substance is applied to the coils with precision and in a resourcepreserving manner. Cured at ambient air, hot melts provide optimum protection against environmental influences," concluded Kosciesza.

The company will also present its spray lubrication systems for various application areas. This includes the spray.xact easy, a solution for straightforward metalworking applications, which is adjustable in terms of the applied type and quantity of oil. Its spray.xact reflection has been designed for the indirect oil lubrication of the coils of highspeed blanking presses where typically small electronic components, such as plug contacts, are sprayed with ultra-thin films of oil. It is also presenting its spot-application tool lubrication system. This can be combined with all spray.xact systems and is designed to extend tool service lives.■
