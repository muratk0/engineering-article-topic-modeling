# Press hardening and stamping in spotlight

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Swedish stamping specialist, AP&T, will be participating in the world's largest exhibition for sheet metal processing, EuroBLECH, in Hanover (Germany) from 22-25 October 2024. It can be found on stand H52 in Hall 27.

"We will showcase our total offerings and present production solutions for the efficient manufacturing of large press-hardened automotive components. We will also host seminars in our booth during two of the exhibition days where speakers from ArcelorMittal, GEDIA and fischer Group, as well as ourselves, will share technological insights and the latest industry news," it explained.

"We offer turnkey solutions for fibre and sheet metal forming based on our deep knowledge of processes and machines as well as a long-term dedication to our customers' businesses. Our global offering includes complete production lines with presses, linear automation, tools, heattreatment systems and services such as maintenance, spare part provision, optimisation and rebuilds," it added.

AP&T will also be exhibiting at the FABTECH trade show in Orlando, USA, from 15-17 October 2024 (on stand S30045 in the North/ South Hall).
