# Roll tooling for AG panels

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Bradbury's AG roll tooling is designed to produce high-quality panels and maintain "exceptional production longevity" on its rollforming machine.

"It not only prevents common deformities found in AG panels but also offers a longer lifespan in production," explained the manufacturer.

Bradbury's AG panel tool sets come with a specialised bow correction fixture, eliminating the unsightly banana or canoe appearance of major ribs. Excessive bowing can lead to panels that don't lay flat and have open laps.

"What sets our panel tool sets apart is their inclusion of 212 main forming rolls and ten straightener rolls, which is approximately 40% more tooling pieces compared to [most] other suppliers. This abundance of tooling allows for precise separation of critical over-form bends, giving operators the flexibility to adjust for different steel types," explained The Bradbury Group.

"The AG panel tool sets require a minimum arbour diameter of 3.5 inches. This larger diameter reduces deflection, resulting in uniform formation of high-wear features. The benefit of this uniformity is especially evident in the minor ribs of AG panels, where the tool set produces an identical middle cell to the outer minor ribs. Other suppliers can often use smaller diameter arbours, which are more prone to deflection. This deflection affects the uniformity of the minor ribs, with the middle cells being noticeably less defined," it added.

Designed with seven-inch pitch forming, Bradbury AG panel tool sets necessitate larger outside diameter rolls. These larger rolls provide more footage per revolution, giving the tool set better longevity. The AG panel tool sets are constructed using through-hardened tool steel, allowing for re-cutting without compromising wear resistance.

The Bradbury Group offers various rollforming machine models to meet the needs of its customers and the industries they serve. Its panel roll-forming equipment includes a complete panel roll-forming line solution with configurations such as pre-cut; post-cut; Single High; Double High® and Rafted® roll formers with flying or stop-to-cut shears.
