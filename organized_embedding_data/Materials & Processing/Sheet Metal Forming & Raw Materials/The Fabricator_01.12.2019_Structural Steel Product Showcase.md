Structural steel quality-certification programs focus on preventing errors

AISC offers quality-certification programs for the structural steel industry that focus on the entire process of fabrication and erection with the goal of not correcting errors but preventing them. The certification, which is contingent on the results of an independent, annual audit, can help companies meet the specifications for more jobs, bid on more jobs, and win more jobs, according to AISC. It can help companies stand out from the competition by demonstrating their commitment to producing a quality product on time and within budget.

Four-roll plate bending, section bending machines offered

MG S.r.L. offers the K 3022 D four-roll plate bending machine. The hydraulic machine includes patented linear guide technology: The side rolls move on a rectilinear axis, and the plate gets pinched in less space. The result is the shortest prebending offered, the company states. The top roll moves up and down on a vertical stroke, and the machine can be used like a press bender as needed. The lower central roll moves horizontally for working of more spaces.

The company also offers the ARL 110.4 four-roll section bending machine with linear guide system. The side bending rolls move on a rectilinear axis to reduce space between the rolls.

Thermal profile processor includes cutting robot to reach all material sides

Vertical machining centers deliver long-part machining in single setup

Plate processing system offers two independent spindles

Ficep Corp. has introduced the Gemini model 36 HDE plate processing system with dual-spindle configuration. Each spindle is totally independent with separate X and Y axes, so different drilling, milling, and scribing operations occur on each spindle simultaneously. The addition of a second fully independent spindle with subaxis positioning increases productivity by about 40 percent in the same footprint, says the company.

The dual bridge gantry remains locked in place as just each spindle positions in both axes as required. The stock plate is clamped with four servo-driven material clamps that contact the entire width of the plate on both sides of the spindle.

Robotic beam assembler helps alleviate welding bottlenecks

Tack and weld options on the Peddi-Assembler robotic beam assembler allow fabricators to stay nimble with each future project, says Peddinghaus. Bottlenecks at the welding process can be alleviated with the machine’s tacking function. The most costly tasks can be finished before weld stand operations.

The key to production is to use shop cranes only when absolutely necessary. Each time a piece of material is picked up and rotated with a crane, a business can lose money. The machine makes loading and unloading with fork trucks seamless in the shop flow, according to the company.
