# ARKU InfoDays

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

From 22-26 April 2024, ARKU InfoDays attracted visitors from Germany and overseas to ARKU's headquarters in Baden-Baden, Germany, to experience the latest advances in levelling and deburring. In addition to ARKU's levelling and deburring machines, this year's focus was on ARKU coil lines.

“Visitors were able to experience various coil lines for press feeding and roll forming up close in the assembly hall. This included cutting systems that produce customised blanks for sheet metal processors to modern coil laser systems. The differences in the systems could be seen, for example, in the cross-sections to be processed, the required straightening results or the degree of automation," explained ARKU.

Visitors brought their own sheetmetal parts for testing on ARKU's deburring and levelling machines. ARKU also presented its EdgeBreaker® 3000 NEXT deburrer as well as the EdgeBreaker® 9000 LINE (for thicker flame- or plasma-cut parts and plate). The EdgeBreaker® 9000 LINE removes slag, deburrs and rounds the edges in one single pass. Parts only have to be loaded into the machine once; turning is no longer necessary thanks to double-sided processing. This, said ARKU, reduces throughput times by over 50 per cent by linking all three processes in one single line.

"We focus on communication, training and employee development to promote an open and inclusive corporate culture. The culture of our organisation as a family business helps us to react more flexibly than large corporations," concluded ARKU, pointing to company initiatives such as its Health Day.
