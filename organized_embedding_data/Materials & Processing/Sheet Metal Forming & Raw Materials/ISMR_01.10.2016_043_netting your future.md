# Netting your future

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

JUN-EN (JEC), from Taiwan, has been a leading manufacturer of metal mesh products and machinery since 1987. Over the past 30 years, JEC has not only built its strengths in expanded metal machines and perforated metal machines but also developed angle-bead and plaster stop bead machines, brick mesh machines and various kinds of mesh machines for different applications.

JEC produces two types of metal perforating machines to satisfy different customer requirements. For mass production, its planotype machine achieves 400SPM with an 8-row punching mould to boost productivity. For more flexible production, its C-type machine, processing material widths from 4-8 feet and thicknesses from 0.5mm to 6mm, can adjust to various production requirements from customers.

"All our systems are tailor-made, according to customer requirements. High speed and high stability of production are key strengths of JEC expanded metal machines. Perforated and expanded metal products can be used in applications such as enclosures; protection; support; decoration; ventilation; sound absorption and filtration," the manufacturer told ISMR.

"In 2004, we sold our first 3100mm expanded metal production line to the US. It was a heavy-duty machine with auxiliary devices for both sheet and coil production. The customer bought another two machines in the next few years and we then introduced our machines to more customers of EMMA members across the North American industry. We also supply machines to customers in Japan.

"We not only produce the machines but also use our own machines in our own production. As an end-user, our priority is to keep improving JEC machine efficiency for a better user experience. Since all JEC machines are customised, we have been encouraging our R&D team to see every challenge as an opportunity to optimise the machine beyond customers' expectation and enjoy the fruit of the breakthrough as a team."
