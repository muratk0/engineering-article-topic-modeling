# SHEET METAL VISION

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

On the back of a healthy growth trajectory, the Schröder Group is aiming for total sales of 30 million euros in 2012.

“Schröder sees a strong demand for a wide range of increasingly custom-tailored equipment, including options and tools, as well as for control software”

In 2009, Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH celebrated the company’s 60th anniversary. For over 60 years, it has served as a speciality provider for sheet metal forming solutions and boasts a complete product range from manual shears to an automated sheet-metal processing centre featuring a manipulator and integrated tool changer – from products for craftsmen in the region to industrial enterprises in Asia or the US. Franz Schröder, managing director of Schröder, outlined the company’s vision and future objectives to ISMR.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes/achievements/ awards and the reasons for this?

The past two years have been critical; high-performance folders from the new Evolution series have enjoyed tremendous success with industry customers.

The Schröder Group was able to significantly expand its presence in Germany as well as global markets. The key to our success here is our experience and expertise in software development, control design and the integration of machines into processes and system environments. We also deliver software and hardware from one source which makes our package solutions more flexible and more reliable.

ISMR: What are your views on the current business climate? How did the global economic downturn affect your business?

FS: We have recovered well from the previous economic crisis. The sound financial management practices of a medium-sized family company has served us well during a severe downturn such as the one we are currently experiencing. Our wide range of products – from simple hand-operated machines to automated industrial solutions – and strong international presence through our network of partners have enabled us to weather crises more effectively.

ISMR: Please confirm your turnover for 2011? Has your turnover increased (if so, by what percentage) and what do you project for 2012?

FS: The Schröder Group, consisting of Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH and Schröder FASTI Technologie GmbH, generated EUR 27 million in sales in 2011. On the back of a healthy growth trajectory, we are aiming for total sales of EUR 30 million in 2012.

ISMR: Which market regions do you feel offer the most potential for growth for your products and why?

The upgraded Evolution series enables automated applications and with industrial users, where we had essentially played a minor role in past years.

Our German-speaking home markets, bolstered by strong business activity in the trades, automotive, mechanical engineering and other sectors, currently offer us excellent growth potential. Likewise, the Americas, India and the Gulf states are currently growing the most rapidly amongst the international markets we serve. In addition to the advantages offered by our machines, we score well in these regions in terms of flexibility, having the strong customer focus of a medium-sized company and with the ‘Made in Germany’ seal of quality.

## FOCUS ON METAL FORMING

Schröder SPB Evolution UD swivel bending machine

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus/vision over the next few years? Are there any aims, objectives or initiatives that you can discuss?

FS: Our industry customers are looking to increase the level of automation whilst remaining extremely flexible in how they deploy their machines. For example, folding a single prototype should also be possible even when deviating from existing planning. In this respect, there is strong demand for a wide range of increasingly custom-tailored equipment, including options and tools, as well as for control software that remains easy to use and delivers process stability within a company’s IT landscape.

The Schröder Group is extremely well positioned in this area, and it is here that we are looking to continue our international growth in years to come. As a company that serves a broad customer base of tradesmen and small enterprises, we face the unique challenge of having to maintain a healthy balance in our product range and with our groups of industrial and small business clients. That is why I am so pleased that we were able to present nano Touch, machine control software designed new from the ground up to meet the special requirements of tradesmen and small enterprises, at EuroBlech 2012.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal industries and where do you see the greatest opportunities?

FS: We do not see any radically new trends. Industry is seeking to become more flexible and produce in ever smaller batches – with no increase in unit costs. Machine utilisation rates will also continue to increase, with correspondingly high standards in terms of quality. The software needs to ensure that frequently changing folding programs and modifications to these are quickly and reliably transmitted to the machines.

ISMR: Please give us an overview of your production facilities and any sheet metal operations/activities. Do you plan to add to or augment your production facilities in any way?

The Schröder Group operates two headquarters in Bavaria and one in northwest Germany. As recently as 2000, the company expanded and fully upgraded its production capacity at its head offices in Wessobrunn. Although it was not possible to optimise certain processes, we are currently monitoring trends in utilisation rates and capacity. We will decide on when to expand these facilities further based on this information.

ISMR: Who are your customers? Can you segment them by activity or sector? How many do you have around the world?

FS: For more than 60 years, we have served as a speciality provider for sheet metal forming solutions. No other company to our knowledge is able to offer such diverse products, ranging from small, hand-operated plate shears to industrial sheet metal forming centres. Our customers include everything from a small plumbing firm and workshop near our head offices to an international car company with production facilities throughout the world.

ISMR: Please give details of any new products that you launched this year. Are there any new product launches on the horizon that you can discuss?

FS: We showcased the Schröder SPB Evolution and Schröder MAK 4 Evolution, two new industrial machines with automatic tool changers and productivityboosting up-and-down folding technology at the EuroBLECH trade show. Another strength of the Evolution series is the software. This includes POS 3000, a 3Dcontrol software developed in-house for visualising and simulating all upcoming steps in the folding process. A PC version is also available for use in production planning and programming.

We also launched our fully new nano Touch software designed for our machines targeted at tradesmen and small enterprises. It is extremely easy to use, makes it possible to save bending programs for multiple products and, if required, enables the operator to make step-by-step corrections or adjustments for materials. Machines with POS 3000 and nano Touch both feature hardware and software from one source, all designed to work seamlessly with each other.
