The scanning spray, AESUB green, has been specially developed for matting large surface areas. Using a spray gun, the spray is applied to large-sized, shiny surfaces of, for example, car bodies, aircraft fuselages or the sides of boats.

In a few seconds, a matt, white, homogenous layer with a thickness of approx. 10-20μm can be generated. Reference points adhere to the touch-dry, sprayed layer. After six hours, the applied layer sublimates automatically and the surfaces do not have to be cleaned.

“Because AESUB green is provided in the form of containers, the spray gun can be quickly refilled. AESUB green does not contain any pigments or titanium dioxide, meaning it poses no risk to health," explained the manufacturer.■
