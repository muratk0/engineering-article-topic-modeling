# Sheet metal dragon

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

SA Fabrications was commissioned to create a bespoke dragon sculpture, designed to stand pride of place during a National Open Gardens event in the UK last June. As the sculpture was permanently outside and exposed to the elements, Humber Galvanizing Ltd (part of Wedge Group Galvanizing Ltd) was called upon to protect it against rust and corrosion. Fabricated mainly from sheet metal, the dragon stands at almost two metres by two metres and weighs an incredible 500kg.

A sheet metal dragon sculpture galvanized by Wedge Group Galvanizing Ltd.

"After much planning and preparation, we sent the dragon to Humber Galvanizing where it was hot-dipped in molten zinc at 450C, which ensures that it will be able to withstand the elements for up to 70 years," confirmed SA Fabrications. “Afterwards, it was powder coated in antique silver to enhance the intricate details of the monster."

During the galvanizing process, the molten zinc metallurgically bonds to the steel, which provides a unique and sustainable finish that has great resistance to damage and long-term protection against corrosion.
