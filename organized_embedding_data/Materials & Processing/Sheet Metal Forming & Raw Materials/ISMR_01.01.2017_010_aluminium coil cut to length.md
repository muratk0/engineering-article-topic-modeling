# Aluminium coil cut to length

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

ALUMINIUM KONIN/IMPEXMETAL has introduced an investment programme which has been successfully completed in Poland. Its technology partner, Georg, provided finishing lines as part of the project, designed for efficient cut-to-length processing of coils from thin and soft aluminium for packaging applications. This new Georg line is in addition to an existing Georg cut-to-length line for aluminium coil at the company's factory.

"At the start of October 2016, the new cut-to-length machine started production of aluminium sheet. It enables the processing of strip widths (between 600 and 1250mm at a maximum speed of 100 m/min.)," Georg told ISMR. “The separation length starts at 400mm and reaches up to 2500mm and pile weight of 2 x 5000kg (max. 1600kg/m). The cut-to-length line is equipped with a double decoiler to take-up coils onto steel spools including coil feeding and feeding unit. Beyond that, the Georg machine offers edge-trimming capability so that the aluminium strip can be cut to its desired width."

The line also features a pull-through leveller generating flat strip, a drum shear for cutting to the desired board length and an air-blow-stacker for precise stacking of sheets onto pallets. For further details, see www.georg.com ■
