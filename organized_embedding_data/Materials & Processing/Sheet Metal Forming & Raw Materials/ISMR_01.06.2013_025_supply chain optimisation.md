# SUPPLY CHAIN OPTIMISATION

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

QUINTIQ, a specialist in supply chain planning and optimisation (SCP&O), has confirmed that its platform is being rolled out at the Outokumpu Stainless AB plant in Avesta, Sweden to support the scheduling of cold flow operations.

“The project will be completed by the end of 2013, and Outokumpu’s cold flow area, which produces stainless steel coils and sheets, will experience tangible benefits immediately upon completion,” it told ISMR.

Outokumpu is the largest stainless steel producer worldwide and its Avesta Plant houses cold flow operations including annealing, pickling and cold rolling facilities. To meet an increasing demand for better product properties and tighter process tolerances, Quintiq was approached to recommend a solution to optimise Outokumpu’s complex production challenges.

“In addition to minimising the percentage of product re-work and reducing costs in the production process, other positive impacts of the platform for Outokumpu will include a reduction in lead times and lower inventory levels. Reduced lead times will have a positive impact on customer satisfaction. Reduced inventory will have positive impacts on capital costs,” added Quintiq.
