## BEKAP METAL INS SAN VE TIC AS

## TR – Hall 15, Stand B60

## Website: www.bekap.com

Bekap A.S is a 35-year old company in the iron and steel business based in Turkey and opened its ‘Continuous Electrogalvanized Steel Coating’ facility in 1998. Bekap provides services to approximately 15 different sectors including white goods, automotive and electronics and is currently exporting to over 40 countries. In 2006, its strip production mill’s annual capacity was increased to 40.000 Mtons and is now able to produce strip thicknesses from 0.015” (0.40mm) up to 0.050” (1.27mm) and widths from ½” (12.7mm) up to 1 ¼“ (31.75mm) - also in 1000kg jumbo coils.

## IPG LASER GMBH

## D – Hall 15, Stand C24

IPG Laser GmbH will showcase the Laser Welding Gun LSS for joining sheet combinations from zinc coated steel, high strength steel and aluminium. The Laser Welding Gun offers higher productivity, higher quality and efficient energy balance for the joints. The system works in Class 1 and does not need laser cabins. It replaces spot welding guns 1:1. Laser, chiller and control are included in one cabinet of <1m x 1m. “With this innovative design, weight reductions during the manufacture of metal sheet components as well as considerable reductions in energy cost can be achieved because of the high laser efficiency of IPG Fibre Lasers,“ said IPG Laser GmbH.

## OFFICINE MONTORFANO S.R.L.

## Website: www.montorfano.it
