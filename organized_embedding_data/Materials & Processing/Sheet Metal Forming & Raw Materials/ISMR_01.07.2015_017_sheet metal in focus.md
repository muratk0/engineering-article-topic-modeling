# Sheet metal in focus

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

AT THE most recent edition of its in-house trade fair this June, Competence Days 2015, Bystronic presented its latest sheet metal processing innovations and trends. Bystronic welcomed customers from approximately thirty countries to the Competence Days at its Swiss headquarters in Niederönz. Under the motto "World Class Manufacturing", Bystronic held live shows demonstrating solutions for successful sheet metal processing.

“One of the solutions that particularly drew the guests' attention was the ByOptimizer. With Bystronic's new cloud service, users can order optimized laser cutting plans online. The patented technology automatically groups the parts that are to be cut so closely on the metal sheet that the gaps (i.e. raw material offcuts) are reduced to a minimum,” explained the company.
