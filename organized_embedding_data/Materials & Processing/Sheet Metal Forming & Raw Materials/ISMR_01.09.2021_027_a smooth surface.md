# A smooth surface

[TARİH: 01.09.2021 ISMR]

With the new GARANT Multi Cut, the Hoffmann Group is presenting a new generation of general-purpose burrs.

"The low-vibration, all-rounder burrs machine steel, stainless steel, aluminium and non-ferrous metals with high process reliability and is suitable for use on manually-guided power tools and industrial robot cells. With newly developed tooth geometry, the burrs generate about 30 per cent less vibration than conventional burrs and are particularly free-cutting. The results are plain to see - better surface qualities; 50 per cent longer tool life; 25 per cent higher metal removal rate and up to 60 per cent greater cutting performance," commented the manufacturer.

The new burrs from the GARANT brand feature an optimised carbide substrate and up to ten different profiles, coupled with the option of a TiAlN coating. The latter feature is designed to reduce heat input into the substrate, improve chip evacuation and increase tool life.

"With their innovative tooth pitch, the new GARANT Multi Cut burrs generate extremely low vibration values. That is particularly beneficial on manually-guided power tools, since the tool can be guided significantly more accurately, the surface quality is better and the operator experiences fewer physical effects due to vibration," said the manufacturer.

"They also offer smooth cutting action, even at high cut depths. In addition, the newly developed tooth geometry renders the burrs extremely free-cutting and ensures a high metal removal rate. Specially honed edges protect against premature wear and increase tool life. The defined shaft surface quality prevents any slippage and provides secure clamping in the collet. The burrs are versatile across a wide range of applications," added the Hoffmann Group.
