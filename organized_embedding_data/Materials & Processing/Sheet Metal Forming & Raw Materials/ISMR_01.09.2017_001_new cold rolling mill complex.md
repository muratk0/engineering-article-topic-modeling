# New cold rolling mill complex

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

POMINA 2 STEEL CORPORATION, a branch of the Pomina Steel Joint Stock Company (one of the largest Vietnamese steel manufacturers), has awarded a new cold rolling mill complex to Tenova. It will be installed in Ba Ria Vung Tau Province, Vietnam.

"The cold complex layout has been designed to optimise coil storage, material flow and facility productivity to meet the needs of a firm in possible future expansion. In addition, the project has been conceived to offer a sustainable solution by minimising waste emissions in the acid regeneration plant and in line with stringent regulations for the cold rolling mill complex as a whole," explained Tenova.

The project includes a push-pull pickling line with acid regeneration plant, a six-high cold reversing mill, a roll grinder, a hot dip galvanising line and a colour coating line. The envisaged total production is 375,000 t/year of high quality pre-painted galvanised and cold rolled coils, with the provision for future expansion to almost double the capacity. The incoming hotrolled strip has a width of up to 1250mm with a thickness ranging from 1.6 to 4mm. The output strip will have a thickness ranging from 3 to 0.2mm. "The cold rolling mill has a maximum rolling speed of 1400 m/min. with automatic gauge, flatness control systems and multi-zone cooling. The hot dip galvanising line will be equipped with our own shearing system, offering a complete in-house technological package. The plant will be fully operative by the end of 2018, enabling Pomina 2 to start production of flat products for the domestic and external market," concluded Tenova.
