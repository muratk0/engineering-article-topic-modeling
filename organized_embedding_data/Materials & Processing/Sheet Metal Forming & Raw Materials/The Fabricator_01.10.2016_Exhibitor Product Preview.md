Roll machine performs end bending without need for press machine

Kurimoto offers an end bending roll machine with horizontal top roll motion. According to the company, this method achieves high accuracy and productivity, making it possible for users to attain precise circular fabrication, including end bending, by setting a plain plate at the loading position without any additional correction by press machines or end cutting.

With this patented Koho Mage method, all processes necessary to manufacture cylindrical objects are finished in one machine, without additional material handling. The top roll moves backward and forward horizontally. For the end bending process, the top roll moves in the opposite direction of the workpiece. The bending roll has enough force to perform end bending because the strong top roll works as a press machine, which bends the edges almost completely curved.

According to the company, the machine can achieve the same end bending accuracy as press machines in less than one-third of the operating time.
