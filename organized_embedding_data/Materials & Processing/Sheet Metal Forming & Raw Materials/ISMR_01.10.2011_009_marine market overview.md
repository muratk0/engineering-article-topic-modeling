# MARINE MARKET OVERVIEW

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

ISMR analyses trends and forecasts in global marine markets for manufacturers.

Metallic and alloy products in the marine industry include engines and deck hardware, anchors, deck and galley equipment, fasteners and propellers. The selection of materials for general boat fittings tends to be based on considerations of usage, appearance, weight and workability rather than pure strength.

The action of a ship moving through waves is akin to bending, twisting and wrenching thousands of times each voyage. Fatigue, compression and bending tests must be performed to ensure that metallic materials will not fail under these conditions. Corrosion prevention is also a key factor.

Aluminium, stainless steel and mild steel are just some of the metals used in the marine industry. The role that powder coatings and galvanising can play in ensuring the enduring efficiency of structures is often underestimated. One of the most effective means of corrosion protection is hot dip galvanising. Organic coatings are another highly efficient way of treating steel for marine applications.

## GLOBAL TRENDS

Besides the Middle and Far East, Turkey is also rapidly developing into important new centre for shipbuilding. The Rolls Royce Group has forecast global demand for marine power and propulsion systems valued at US$ 215 billion over the next 20 years.

“Demand will be greatest in the commercial sector where the shipping of raw materials, finished goods and people, in addition to oil and gas exploration and production activity, play crucial roles in the world economy. These activities require large fleets of specialised and increasingly sophisticated ships, which have to be continually renewed and supported to remain operationally efficient. Merchant and offshore markets are rarely at the same stage of the business cycle, which helps to reduce overall volatility. Marine aftermarket services are expected to generate significant opportunities, with demand forecasted at US $125 billion over the next 20 years,” it told ISMR.
