# Metal powder coatings aid sustainability

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Hydro Building Systems Spain, a global manufacturer of aluminium profile systems, is extending its 20-year partnership with AkzoNobel. It is using AkzoNobell's Interpon Anodic range to give architects and designers more choice and flexibility than 'traditional' anodizing with, said the metal coatings specialist, "reduced environmental implications and cost."

Hydro Building Systems Spain has been an Interpo-approved Applicator for many years but was looking for further support to move away from anodizing. Arantxa Oritz Barredo, Hydro Building Systems Spain Purchasing and Powder Management, confirmed that with the Interpon Anodic range, Hydro can create the appearance of an anodized finish but in a powder coating and produce it in-house.

"The metallic effect finishes that we can create are superb, and very well-received by our customers," she said. "The quality of finish is further guaranteed by warranties, and Interpon's accreditation to Qualicoat Class I and II."

Hydro started working with AkzoNobel after building a new manufacturing site in Spain at the start of the new millennium. Today, it offers a choice of more than 175 different Interpon powder coatings in a range of colours and finishes. The Interpon D team works closely with Hydro Building Systems Spain to ensure its finishes are always on trend, enabling the manufacturer to build its own library of additional, bespoke colours for customers.

As well as multiple colours, Hydro Building Systems Spain also offers powder coatings with imaginative textures and finishes and different levels of durability. This is important to protect surfaces from some of the more challenging climates to which its products may be exposed, ensuring that their colour integrity and performance is maintained.

AkzoNobel serves more than 150 countries with a portfolio of paints and coatings including Dulux, Sikkens and International. Its Interpon and Resicoat powder coatings are used in a range of architectural, automotive, industrial, functional and general trade coating applications. With headquarters in The Netherlands and a global workforce of 34,000, AkzoNobel focuses on sustainability through its group-wide 'People Planet Paint' initiative.■
