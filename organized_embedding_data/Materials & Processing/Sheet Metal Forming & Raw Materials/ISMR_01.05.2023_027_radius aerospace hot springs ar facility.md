## About Water Jet Sweden

Water Jet Sweden celebrates its 30th anniversary this year. The company develops, manufactures and markets advanced solutions for water jet cutting and provides an aftermarket offer with service and parts. Today, it has customers in more than 40 countries. It recently released the FiveX Ultra, model name NC5 Ultra, as a compact and standardised version of the FiveX (NC5). The new FiveX Ultra makes advanced waterjet cutting available to a broader market. It comes with the new, compact WJS 5AX Ultra cutting head, which has a > ± 120° cutting angle and process in full 3D.
