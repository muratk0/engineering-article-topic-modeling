# Coil in focus

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

ESTABLISHED SINCE 1957, DIMECO is a well-known French family-owned company with users all over the world of its coil-fed production systems, which are designed to meet demanding market requirements.

As a global specialist in coil-fed flexible fabricating lines, as well as coil handling and press feeding lines, its coilfed fabricating line portfolio ranges from flexible systems (including laser cutting and punching solutions) to cut-to-length, stacking, sorting and roll forming systems for various industrial applications.

“Our punching lines will take users to high levels of productivity whilst ensuring more flexibility, less material waste, lower labour costs and no limits on part lengths," it told /SMR.

"We also offer coil handling and press feeding systems as well as ROTOBLOC quick die change and quick coil change systems, the core of our SMED (Single-Minute Exchange of Dies) methodology, which bring users high returns on investment. They enable users to dramatically reduce their set-up times whilst reducing the risk of accidents and any musculoskeletal problems as a result," it added.
