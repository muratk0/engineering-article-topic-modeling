ISMR: Please give us some background on Soenen. How many people are in the company and what are its main products and technologies (relevant for sheet metal users)?

PQ: Soenen has been - and will remain - a family business since 1924. In the beginning, we manufactured hand–made tooling and smaller press brakes. It was during the 1960s that our focus changed to special machines for the sheet metal industry.

Since the 1970s, all our lines have been CNC controlled and it was during that period that Soenen Controls was established since hardware with certain specifications was not available on the market. Today, Soenen Controls delivers hardware and software to third parties since our all our automatic lines are now automated by Beckhof Automation.

In 2007, ASSEMA was established. ASSEMA stands for“Associated Serbian Manufacturers” and is a fully-owned subsidiary. It organises the supply of mechanical components and sub-assemblies for the Soenen transformer division. Production is done by a dedicated group of manufacturers near Kragujevac, Serbia. They manage the complete process from CAD drawing inspection and supplier selection up to quality control, logistics and custom clearance.

Soenen NV now builds tailor-made machines for the sheet metal industry, located in Belgium, with a turnover of 35 million euros and 125 people in total. We export 95% of our products and have a strong focus on emerging markets. We offer integral project development from design through manufacturing and automation to service after sales – always tailored to the customer’s needs and requirements.

Soenen NV is a global player with sales agencies around the world, a subsidiary in Serbia and a sales and service office in Beijing. We are active in niche markets – from perforating and expanded metal presses to CNC punching lines for truck chassis manufacturing as well as automatic lines for cutting and stacking silicon steel for industrial transformer cores and slitting installations.

ISMR: What are your main objectives and targets over the next few years? How do you intend to achieve them?

PQ: We are aiming for solid growth in all product categories by delivering reliable, well engineered machines and providing excellent service to wherever our customers are located.

We can solve problems within 24 hours by teleservice - a team of highly trained service engineers can arrive on site within 48 hours with the required spare parts. Since 2005, all Soenen machines are equipped with an interactive fault diagnostic system called the TRACKSNIPER. This software registers all actions, displays them on the operator screen and saves them into a log file. In this way, problems can be easily detected and problem solving can be done by the operator assisted by guidelines from our technicians, pictures, data etc.

We are currently looking to improve market share for the Quickstack, our new product for stacking cold formed sections. Our target is to sell 15 machines per year. We also aim to expand our activities in Serbia.

ISMR: Please describe your latest line installations, their approximate value and where/when they were installed.

PQ: We recently delivered a punching line to China. The investment value for the Chinese line is around 2.5 – 3.5 million euros. Soenen is a market leader in punching installations for U-shaped profiles for truck chassis manufacturing. All the leading truck manufacturers are punching holes using Soenen punching lines. Our line is designed to punch holes in plates or profiles with constant or variable sections. One or multiple punching units are set up in line to simultaneously punch holes in different locations on the sheet or profiles.

We also delivered four high speed cutting and stacking lines this year to another customer. The lines cut and stack three sheets per second. The investment value is 1.5 – 2 million euros per line. We also delivered two perforating presses to Permetal Brasil this year.
