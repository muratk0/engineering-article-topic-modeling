# Ornamental Machinery

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 The Fabricator]

Prada Nargesa started Producing Ornamental Machinery in 1970. Over the years Nargesa began to make a big name for themselves and eventually become the Most Trusted and Reliable Ornamental Equipment Manufacturer in the World! The Nargesa Ornamental Machinery Range covers almost every Ornamental Application you can think of. Such as Units that give you the Ability to Produce Fence Railing, Window Grills, Balusters, Tables, Chairs, etc. You can work with countless types of Materials as well, such as: Rounds, Squares, Tube Profiles, Handrails, Flat Bars, and more! Most of Nargesa’s Ornamental Machines can be used in Conjunction with each other - for 1 simple example, the PC16 can Bend the Metal that is heated in the H-Series Gas Forge.

THE MT SERIES

The MT Series has two Working Sites, One for Twisting and another for Bending Cold Metal with No Neck Limit.

THE NOA, NF & PC SERIES

The Nargesa NOA, NF and PC Series are World-Class Ornamental Steel Finishing Machines. Guaranteed to turn heads on any project.

THE H SERIES

The H-Series Gas Forges are Specifically Designed to Heat Metals Efficiently and are one of the Safest Gas Propane Forges in the World.

The Nargesa MT Series can make a great deal of shapes and appliactions utilizing its Twisting, Scrolling and Bending Capabilites.

Certain Units Offer Different Fitting Plates, allowing every Customer to use Nargesa’s designs or their own custom designs.

The Gas Forges are Equipped with an Adjustable Auxiliary Support for the Parts being Heated.

Nargesa Embossing Machines Cold Print and Shape Metal to Emboss Different Designs. (Custom Ornamental Designs are Available.)
