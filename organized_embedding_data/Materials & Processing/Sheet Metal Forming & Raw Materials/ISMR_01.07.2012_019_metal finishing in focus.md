# Metal finishing in focus

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

Homag Group is in the process of joining its subsidiary Buetfering Schleiftechnik GmbH with another one of its affiliates, Weeke Bohrsysteme GmbH. In addition to wood grinding machines, the plant in Beckum also produces metal finishing machines under the Steelmaster name. LISSMAC Maschinenbau GmbH, located in Bad Wurzach, has now assumed ownership of the metal finishing segment. It is also currently expanding its production space in Bad Wurzach.

"The Steelmaster metal finishing and deburring machines will complement and expand our Metal Processing division," Klaus Kiefer of LISSMAC explained. "Our steel brushing, deburring, edge rounding and de-slagging machines work exclusively in dry operation and can process both sides of sheet metal parts in just one single pass, but with some technological limitations. With the Steelmaster machine, we can close that gap by offering a wet grinding process. Customers who have been accustomed to the wet grinding process can rest assured that the Steelmaster metal finishing and deburring machines will continue to be available in the same high quality. To underline that fact we are keeping the brand name Steelmaster.

"With the Steelmaster metal finishing and deburring machines, we can achieve a defined surface grain on a variety of metal sheet and plates. We can achieve smooth, glossy or matte finishes as well as structured surfaces with patterned polishes, according to customer wishes. Usually, however, the edges of the parts will need to be processed before any kind of surface graining is applied. In this respect, the two technologies are perfectly compatible and work well with each other. The customer benefits from getting all the hardware, automation technology, as well as maintenance, service and repair from the same source."

With the collaboration of its three business units (Metal Processing, MT-Handling and Plant Engineering), LISSMAC is capable of automating all aspects of parts processing, from edge treatment of cut sheet metal parts to having them picked up from the respective cutting machine to palletisation according to customer requirements. The conveying, manipulating and automation technology involved can also be used with a wet grinding process as applied by the Steelmaster metal finishing and deburring machines.

As Rolf Angerer explains, "LISSMAC's machine concept is based on processing both sides of sheet metal parts in just one single pass. This is achieved exclusively with a dry operation process. Our focus is on a clean surface with perfectly deburred and rounded edges. The Steelmaster machine, on the other hand, applies a defined surface grain, used for example as a decorative element in highly visible components of products, and focuses on a wet grinding technology. This wet grinding technology reduces heating up of the parts during processing which helps to reduce deformation of heat-sensitive parts like long thin metal sheets.

"Both machine types can process parts made of steel, stainless steel, copper, brass and aluminium. The Steelmaster technology helps us substantially to diversify our established product line and gives us the opportunity to offer solutions for an increased variety of applications."
