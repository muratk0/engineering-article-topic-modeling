For over sixty years, the Timesavers Group has been providing wide-belt grinding and sanding machine solutions for the metal, titanium, molybdenum, zirconium, tantalum, zinc and tungsten and plastic processing industries, as well as for a range of other materials.

From roots reaching back to 1939 when Arie van der Linden started a repair shop for woodworking machinery in Rotterdam, the Dutch Group is now one of the largest and technologically most advanced manufacturers of wide-belt grinding and sanding solutions in the world for belt-widths from 220 to 2600mm. It has delivered over 55,000 machines to almost every country across a multitude of application fields.

“After decades where grinding with wide belts did not change, Timesavers was the first producer of a new grinding system,” explained Paul Hartendorp, General Manager. “In the early eighties, we introduced the first patented ‘Disc’ machines which were, at that time, a revolutionary new concept for metal deburring. With the introduction of the wet version, processing of mixed materials became much easier and created opportunities for a new set of customers. As quality standards increased with the introduction of powder-coating spray systems, deburring was no longer enough and edge-rounding became the new standard.

“To cope with this new market requirement, in late 2009 we introduced the first multi-directional rotary brush (RB) machine. Launched in the Netherlands, home market for Timesavers International BV, it quickly became popular. Over the next two years, the machine was introduced in most European countries and, today, Timesavers has built up an impressive reference list with many contractors and OEMs working with machines from this new product range.”

While laser profiling leaves sheet metal components virtually burr-free, some small burrs and sharp edges still remain which pose a handling risk. To remove these by hand is time-consuming and, under health and safety legislation, poses a risk to operators removing them using vibratory tools. The development of the rotary brush system by Timesavers, based on a rotating orbital brush concept, allows users to achieve a high quality finish and precise radius.

The rotary brush machine produces a high quality edge-rounding result and offers multifunctional capability with pre-grinding and finishing. It is designed to provide a uniform deburring result, finishing the edges of lasercut, punched and machined parts in all directions in one pass, whilst improving the surface with a random or straight finish if desired.

“When designing the machine, a lot of attention was paid to the Human Machine Interface (HMI) as well as the tooling and abrasive solutions required. This resulted in an extremely user-friendly machine with very low production costs per time-unit (brushes with life time > 3.000 hours). With the innovative quick exchange system, the machine can be switched from handling all kind of metals to aluminium applications,” added Hartendorp.

With several decades of experience in ‘sheet-to-sheet’ and ‘coil-to-coil’ finishing of cold rolled stainless steel, the Group has customers in all key segments of sheet metal and stainless steel finishing markets. Timesavers recently introduced heavy-duty machines for finishing hot rolled steel for sheet and coil applications.

“Calibrating metals (from highly exotic material such as molybdenum and titanium to straightforward stainless steel) with a grinding solution is a relatively new process. Besides the heavy-duty machine developed several years ago, we recently introduced a medium-duty machine. These ‘closetolerance’ machines can calibrate material up to 0.01mm using fixtures like magnetic or vacuum tables, custom-made if required, at much higher speeds than traditional stone grinders, and bring much less heat into the material than traditional milling machines,” explained Hartendorp.

“This is a unique innovation, in which we unite all of our knowledge of different machine applications and markets and, together with our partners (in abrasives, coolants, mechanical and electrical components ), serve several customers with solutions they had not imagined would be available,” he added.

## The partnership principle

Timesavers worldwide sales turnover amounted to almost 30 million euros in 2011, spread across all continents, with a focus on North America, Europe and the Far East. For 2012, Hartendorp expects a similar turnover level, with business expected to show growth again in 2013.

“In general, our metal business is still growing,” he told ISMR. “Europe is a stable market, whereas the US market appears to have more growth potential. We have customers in hot and cold rolled metal industries all over the world, in sectors such as stainless steel surface finishing, but we place a particular emphasis on Europe and China at the moment. Calibration (closetolerance finishing) customers represent a relatively new market for us whilst our deburring and edge-rounding machines are in use all over the world.”

Offering users the ability to add value to their customers is a key enabler for Timesavers – it is no longer enough just to have a laser to make them stand out from the crowd. By adding the capability to perform edge preparation with its rotary brush machines, it provides its customers with that little extra over its competitors. The Group’s key philosophy is developing a partnership with its customers for long and fulfilling relationships.

“With a history of nearly 75 years, we have learned what ‘partnership’ means. Some customers go way back, some are brand new in the business. More and more customer intimacy is valued at the point of sale now. Customers increasingly value not just good technical advice, but also provision of an application solution developed with them as well as long-term service,” emphasised Hartendorp.

“We have invested time and money in local sales and service points, with not only showrooms and sales people but also local service technicians who provide service in the customer’s region and language. For example, in Germany we work with nearly 10 local distributors, 5 showrooms and local service organisations. Germany is our number one market with over 75 machines sold last year. Only North America is bigger for us, in terms of sales, and China follows closely.”

Support and inspection is a key part of the partnership package.

“A customer’s investment is not only for just a machine. Besides the machine, they also get a great support team with a proactive plan to keep the machine up and running with genuine Timesavers machine parts and our ‘inspect and repair before failure’ philosophy. It is our goal to maximize return on their Timesavers machine investment by serving them with the benefits and experience of our knowledge in the metal industry. From start to finish, we stand behind every machine we make as well as the service and parts that we provide,” the Group told ISMR.
