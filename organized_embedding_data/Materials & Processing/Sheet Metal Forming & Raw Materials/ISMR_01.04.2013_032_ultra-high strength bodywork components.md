# Ultra-high strength bodywork components

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

LONGITUDINAL MEMBERS, bumpers, side impact protection, crash tubes… these and many other automotive parts can be economically manufactured from ultra-highstrength steels, with strengths of up to 1,500 MPa, on roll forming systems whilst saving weight. In the past, the large process-related fluctuations in the material characteristics of such steels were a problem. The tolerance requirements on such components cannot be achieved using conventional roll forming technology.

Based on its experience with numerous systems it has supplied, Dreistern has developed special forming and straightening processes for these components. In addition, it is possible to make specific changes to particularly critical individual component dimensions in the event of dimensional variations. The numerical simulation of the forming processes plays a key role during development. The result is significantly improved process capability even with fluctuating material characteristics.

“Thanks to their high production speeds, roll forming systems are often not fully utilised with a single product. It is therefore appropriate to manufacture several different products on one system. This is not a problem if the system was designed for different products from the start. However, this is not the case if a system is to be subsequently set up for a new product. This is because, as a rule, additional processes such as perforating, punching, embossing, bending or welding must also be integrated. Often systems are not prepared for the additional requirements and making changes is expensive and time-consuming,” said the manufacturer.

Another new technology from Dreistern, multi-functional roll forming technology, now offers a solution to this problem. It is based on standardised processing modules for punching, bending or welding that can be quickly and easily integrated at any point on the roll forming machine. As a result, it is as quick and straightforward to change such processing modules as it is to change quick-change roll forming tools. Thanks to universal compatibility and the flexible control unit, individual processing modules can also be used on different machines. The prerequisites for operation with a cost-effective product mix are therefore met.

“These new developments in forming and machine tool technology on roll forming systems open up further opportunities for saving weight on bodywork components by using extremely high strength materials. High process reliability and optimal utilisation of the systems, thanks to operations using a product mix, make roll forming an even more attractive alternative for a series of bodywork components,” concluded Dreistern.
