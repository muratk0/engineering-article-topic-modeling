# FOCUS ON WATERJET CUTTING

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

From mass producing parts out of sheetmetal to carving intricate 3D designs in marble, the waterjet has numerous applications and is one of the most versatile cutting systems in the manufacturing industry today. The introduction of 5-axis cutting heads has triggered massive growth in waterjet cutting. No longer confined to cutting out flat objects, waterjets are now able to produce continuous bevels, revolve around the workpiece and cut at angles from 0-90 degrees. Below is a selection of the latest new and innovative waterjet technologies for sheet metal manufacturers.

## Innovation in focus

In thermal/mechanical separation technologies, ANT Applied New Technologies picked up a 2017 Blechexpo Award (presented by Blechnet and MM Maschinenmarkt) for its "Consus", an abrasive remixer that enables faster shaving and savings of the abrasive. To produce an air-free jet, the suspension is pressurised in a high-pressure vessel and forced out of the nozzle via a hose.

Two new waterjet cutting systems were launched in the UK this October by AXYZ International. It follows acquisition by the parent company in Canada of WARDJet, a leading US-based manufacturer of waterjet cutting systems. The new large-format X-Series and the smaller-format A-Series are suitable for a wide range of industries.

The X-Series incorporates a re-engineered lightweight and rigidly constructed modular gantry to hold multiple cutting heads and components and an optional integrated variable water level control system. This enables materials to be processed under water, thereby eliminating splashback and consequent loss of water and drastically reducing the level of noise during the cutting process.

"Providing cutting solutions to a market sector with limited options, the new A-0612 waterjet cutting machine operates at 60,000psi. This enables the processing of any material in the same time as that taken by a full-size machine. Operating at this pressure also reduces the amount of abrasive needed for the cutting process when compared with lower pressure systems, thereby further reducing production costs," said AXYZ International. At the 2017 Blechexpo exhibition, BTG GmBH showcased two high-pressure pumps in its ECOTRON range – the 40.37+ and the 40.45+.

Swiss sheet metalworking machinery manufacturer, Bystronic, has produced a twin-head ByJet Flex for waterjet cutting, which can accept up to two newly developed, 3D cutting heads for producing bevelled edges on components. Upgrading from 2D to 3D technology takes less than an hour, said the manufacturer.

"The ByJet Flex platform can be integrated into a wide variety of production environments and used for both 2D and 3D applications, operating with two CNC heads for straight or bevelled cutting. The basic version is equipped with all prerequisites that allow it to be upgraded with functions such as 3D cutting heads; drill spindles; automatic parts holding during cutting (FixMaster); precise positioning of the cutting head (PositionPointer) and intelligent fine adjustment for rapid, multi-head cutting (PrecisionAligner)," Bystronic told ISMR.

The manufacturer has also introduced a new app that monitors, in real-time, all processes taking place within its laser cutting machines, press brakes and waterjet cutting machines. Called ByCockpit, the software fits neatly with the digital networking requirements of Industry 4.0. It evaluates the collected data and generates status reports, allowing factory output to be optimised.

Luxembourg manufacturer CERATIZIT has produced its Premium+ focusing tubes for demanding waterjet cutting applications, whether users process exotic or easily machinable materials, composite materials, split cuts or high-precision cuts.

Optimised wear protection ensures longer tool life (which supports improved machine utilisation). Improved material quality and processing offer up to a 30% improvement in performance, according to the manufacturer. The tubes are used in a wide range of industry sectors, including furniture, aerospace and food preparation sectors.

At this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Eckert presented its Opal Waterjet Combo patented, rapid CNC waterjet for plasma and waterjet cutting. Equipped with the Pro-X 3D waterjet cutting head, it combines the advantages of ultra-fast plasma cutting and precision waterjet cutting. This solution is patented by Eckert.

"The maximum cutting angle +/- 60 and automatic angle compensation ensure great precision and practically zero angle on the cutting edge. This CNC cutting machine optionally allows cutting with pure water or with abrasive," said the manufacturer.

ECKCAM Professional Plus is modern software for design offices which allows users to choose the preferred cutting technology and decide which elements will be cut by water technology and which by plasma.

Hypertherm, a U.S.-based manufacturer of industrial cutting systems and software, has introduced what it calls ‘the industry’s first predictive maintenance waterjet pumps to substantially reduce maintenance costs and disruption caused by non-planned service’. This new generation of pumps – called HyPrecision Predictive – are equipped with features such as Hypertherm’s Advanced Intensifier Technology.

"It also features new patented technologies such as closed loop proportional pressure control that adjusts for pressure and temperature, oil viscosity and hydraulic system wear parts; along with technology that enables customers to use seals up to 40 per cent longer," Hypertherm told ISMR.

"In addition to technologies to increase performance and reduce system downtime, the HyPrecision Predictive pumps are designed with ease of service in mind. Features such as a colour-coded junction box, electrical cable harnesses, quick-disconnect fittings and an easy access bleed-down valve all make maintenance faster and less expensive."

A clear window cover lets the operator see the intensifier and attenuator. Optional electrical interlocks prevent unauthorised access and can automatically stop the pump when opened. Finally, Seal Maintenance Technology™ helps keep the top deck and shop floor free of oil and water. Hypertherm HyPrecision Predictive pumps are available now in six different models, ranging from 15 to 75 horsepower.

To cut narrow incisions in hard materials with high precision requires a specially developed waterjet cutting process. FAWJ stands for Fine Abrasive Water Jet (micro waterjet, finecut) and was launched in 2008 by Water Jet Sweden. Since then, the technique has been applied to specially developed precision machinery designed to manufacture micro-components. Water Jet Sweden has now broadened the possibilities and launched The FAWJ Micro Head Package for its ordinary machine models. It is designed for customers who wish to cut fine parts with narrow incisions and high precision, without the need for the most extreme micro part tolerances. Its "Micro" (NCM 10) is specially developed for the FAWJ-process and is used for the manufacturing of micro components. Micro waterjet cutting machine is a popular alternative to Micro-EDM and Laser.
