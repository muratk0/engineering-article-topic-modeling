# The final cut

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

AT SUBCON 2012, Henkel unveiled a sustainable solution to an age-old problem. How do you stop a user-friendly, bactericide-free cutting fluid from becoming contaminated with bacteria, resulting in the emulsion splitting and producing bad odours? The answer is with the patented emulsifier technology, the main feature of its new Multan 71-2 cutting fluid.

For example, the tooling manufacturer Walter AG now uses Multan 71-2 in its training and demonstration centre as its former coolant was responsible for a strong build-up of odour when machines were left idle.

By developing a completely new generation of bactericide-free cutting fluids, Henkel has succeeded in banishing the drawback of emulsions splitting in use and the related bad odour nuisance, thus providing a long-awaited answer to a major problem associated with water-miscible metalworking fluids. The new emulsions of the Multan brand have proved themselves in industrial practice.

After intensive development work, a microbiologically robust cutting fluid was formulated that not only contains no bactericides (such as formaldehyde splitters) but does not need any later addition of bactericides to keep it stable. ■

## Suction and filter

THE TEKA FILTERCUBE 2N/2H and FILTERCUBE 4N/4H are a series of BGIA-certified DIN EN ISO 15012-1 stationary central suction and filter units from Flextraction Ltd, suppliers and manufacturers of Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) products and representative for TEKA GmbH, an extraction arms manufacturer.

They are designed for large fabrication shops requiring multi-suction from a number of extraction points within the workshop and are suitable for companies welding and working with non-alloyed metal, precious metals, galvanised materials and aluminium. The main features of both units are fully automatic microprocessor-controlled de-dusting, using ‘Pulse Control-Steering’ with text indicator and cartridge dedusting carried out using a Power Spray system.

“The separation efficiency of both systems is >99% conforming to welding fume category ‘W3’ for high alloyed steels, whilst the sound level is around 72dB(A). The FILTERCUBE 2N/2H has units available with a volumetric fan flow ranging from 2,500m3/hr to 5,000m3/hr. whilst the FILTERCUBE 4N/4H has units available with a volumetric fan flow ranging from 3,500m3/hr to 10,000m3/hr,” Flextraction explained.

Other features include an integrated control system for the fan plus a large dust collection tank with snap fasteners for easy access. E-mail: lkd@flextraction.co.uk

## 2kW fiber laser launch

JK LASERS' newly launched JK2000FL features strong beam quality, high processing powers, stable output power from switch-on, consistent focused spot size, fast modulation and pulse shaping. It can be used with different diameter processing fibers, from 100µm to 300µm, for cutting and welding sheet metal.

The JK2000FL can cut 15mm thick low carbon steel (LCS), 6mm aluminium alloys and 10mm stainless steel (304SS). It can weld 8mm LCS and 304SS and is fitted with JK Lasers' detachable ‘plug in, pre-aligned’ (PIPA) beam delivery fibers which incorporate patented back reflection protection. This protects the laser from damage, especially when processing highly reflective materials such as aluminium, copper, brass and titanium.

“Compared to Nd:YAG lasers, the JK2000FL is up to ten times more energy-efficient, offering manufacturers substantial savings in terms of running costs. In addition, the extremely long mean time to failure (MTTF) of over 300,000 hours for the individual laser pump sources enables extended system lifetimes,” the manufacturer told ISMR. www.jklasers.com

## EFFICIENT NESTING

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of advanced cutting systems, has announced a major version update to its ProNest nesting software, allowing greater productivity enhancements and efficiency for metal fabricators and others in the cutting industry.

“ProNest 2012 contains a number of new features and comprehensive improvements that make it even easier for users to increase the cut quality, productivity and profitability of their cutting operation – whether it be plasma, laser, oxyfuel, waterjet or combination punch/plasma,” Hypertherm told ISMR.

New features and enhancements to ProNest 2012 include a work order processing module (connects to systems, helps to maximise material utilisation, increase productivity and achieve better on-time delivery) and material grade and thickness functionality (increasing the nesting flexibility and costing accuracy when working with parts and plates of varying grades and thicknesses). 2D CAD part creation and editing functionality enables users to create new part geometries for nesting and may be used to edit existing geometries imported from an external CAD program.

ProNest is also a component of Hypertherm’s Integrated Cutting Solutions, providing support for True Hole™ and Rapid Part™ technologies. www.hypertherm.com
