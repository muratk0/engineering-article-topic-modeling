# STAMP OF APPROVAL

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

We highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing technologies and innovations

Above: An AP&T press hardening tool

ISMR SAYS:

"Stamping includes a variety of sheet metal forming manufacturing processes such as blanking; punching; piercing; bend/draw forming; embossing; flanging and coining"

Stamping is the process of placing flat sheet metal, in either blank or coil form, into a stamping press where a tool and die surface forms the metal into a net shape. Stamping includes a variety of sheet metal forming manufacturing processes such as blanking; punching; piercing; bend/draw forming; embossing; flanging and coining. This could be a single-stage operation, where every stroke of the press produces the desired form on the sheet metal part, or could occur through a 'progressive' series of stages.

Stamping presses utilise the force of a moving ram to transmit force, or an amount of tonnage, to specific tooling to achieve a product's final shape, often with little or no scrap and, whenever possible, with minimal operator intervention. A wide variety of parts can be formed in different press sizes and types.

A wide range of materials, including stainless and cold/hot-rolled steel; hardened and tempered steel; aluminium; copper; titanium and specialty alloys, can be processed. In this article, we highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing innovations

## An eye on forming

AIDA ENGINEERING Ltd., a specialist in servo technology for metal forming equipment, opened its new European Technology Centre in Weingarten, Germany on 1 August 2016. AIDA EUROPE GmbH will have sales and engineering functions, as well as project management, purchasing and other activities.

AIDA is a global specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metal forming automation equipment. Its servo and mechanical stamping presses range in capacity from 35 to 4,000 tons, from 1 to 1,500 strokes per minute, from gap frame to straightside, high-speed to cold forging, transfer and progressive die, material handling and coil-feeding equipment

Two AIDA DSF Series servo presses were on display at FABTECH at Las Vegas in November 2016. An AIDA DSF-C1-1500, 150-ton gap frame servo press blanked HSLA material and demonstrated AIDA's Silent Blanking motion profile, which can only be done using a servopress. The second FABTECH demonstration included an AIDA DSF-N2-3000, 300-ton, two-point unitised frame servo press (part of a full production line).

AP&T has also launched a new generation of its Multi Layer Furnace for press hardened vehicle parts. The new generation furnace features improved process stability and availability. At EuroBLECH Hanover in October 2016, Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik introduced its new lean tool concept for more efficient stamping and forming.

"Lean tool is the first modular tooling for the servo stamping and forming machines. RM-NC and GRM-NC. Lean tool will facilitate and speed up the entire process, from the initial planning through to the manufacturing of new tools. Due to the small number of tool components and quick and easy implementation, the cost of new tools on both models (RM-NC and GRM-NC) can be significantly reduced. This leads to a saving of more than 50 per cent, compared to forming tools on mechanical equipment. Furthermore, with the lean tool concept, new products can be brought to market faster, even in very small batch sizes," the company told ISMR.

Taiwanese manufacturer Chin Fong's stamping presses range from 20-ton single crank presses and up to 2400 metric tons of capacity to 4-point eccentric gear-driven, large panel and multistage transfer presses. Forging presses are further diversified by cold, warm and hot forging processes.

Chin Fong now has offices in Taiwan, China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and the USA as well as representatives in numerous countries around the world. Its A0 tandem press line for the automotive industry includes stages such as deep drawing, following by trimming, piercing etc. The basic A0 line weighs 2400 tons with 800 tons of weight for the rest of the machines. To gain ground in production efficiency, Chin Fong replaced the conventional hydraulic press with a mechanical press featuring a link drive and long stroke.

Chin Fong's straightside double crank power press, with 400-ton capacity, has a bed area of 2.5m x 1m and can produce metal stampings from material measuring up to 400mm wide and 3.5mm thick. It has a variable speed of between 30-50 strokes per minute and a maximum shut height of 450mm.

SPC DEES Hydraulic, a hydraulic press manufacturer with factories in Taiwan and China, celebrated its 40th anniversary in 2016. Its main products include tandem lines, deep drawing, hemming and die spotting presses as well as its key interest, hydraulic press technology. Its focus is on three main stamping areas - servo motors, higher speed of hydraulic presses and higher productivity for presses using accumulators for shorter strokes.

Formed in 1976 in Taiwan, it has been present in China since 2000 and, in 2014 moved to a newly built factory in China, which is almost double the size of its previous factory there. It is also building a new factory in Taiwan. The automotive market is a key industry sector for the Taiwanese manufacturer, who supplies customers such as Audi; Volkswagen; Mercedes-Benz; Volvo; HONDA; TOYOTA; BMW and more.

DEES Hydraulic has long been committed to integrating new technologies and has successfully developed a closed loop, hydraulic, servo-controlled die spotting press. With micro-precision control and energy efficiency at the top of its R&D goals, the servo system's fixed-flow control, as the core technology used in the hydraulic spotting press, can further improve the precision of the conventional design or overcome the ball-screw's wear and tear to reduce overall maintenance costs.

INGYU is a precision stamping press manufacturer which has been in operation for 45 years in Taiwan. It aims to provide highprecision, high-speed presses for automotive, can-making, motor lamination and complex forming applications. INGYU presented its new Saga press at the EuroBLECH exhibition in October 2016.

"Our new Saga multi-slide motion mechanical presses enable users to produce high accuracy sheet metal parts, increase the number of products per minute and reduce their tooling cost. With the one-piece frame and rugged plunger and post guiding system, the press's vertical accuracy is within 0.01mm for extended die life and improved part quality. Slide velocity decreases towards Bottom Dead Centre (BDC) to reduce the impact of stamping, noise and vibration. Customers can choose three slide motion modes, including forming, bending and drawing, in one press to maximise productivity," explained the manufacturer.

Interlaken Technology Company, a manufacturer of production press equipment for hydroforming, elevated temperature forming and hot stamping systems, has introduced a four-column hydroforming press system for running high volume production while reducing cycle time. Interlaken offers this hydroforming press system in clamp forces ranging from 250 to 5000 tons. Hydroformed parts are stronger and weigh less due to structural integrity and fewer welds or add-on pieces. In addition, costs are reduced and time is saved by eliminating secondary operations, reducing scrap, lowering material and manufacturing costs, and increasing design flexibility.

Quintus Technologies offers high-pressure sheet metal forming presses plus hot and cold isostatic presses for densification of advanced materials. It has delivered over 1,800 systems with more than 90% sold since the 1960s still in operation. Over the past 40 years, it has grown to become the world's leading producer of fluid cell presses for sheet hydroforming and similar sheet metal forming applications.

In October 2016, Quintus Technologies was selected by Gulfstream Aerospace Corporation, a leading business-jet manufacturer, to provide a modern high-pressure fluid cell press. The Flexform press is destined for Gulfstream's Mexicali, Mexico, facility which produces wiring harnesses, sheet metal components, subassemblies and machined parts. The Flexform process requires only one rigid tool half: the other tool half is a flexible rubber diaphragm under uniform hydrostatic pressure. This design approach generates significant tool cost savings and eliminates several forming operations, intermediate heat treatments and operator dependencies.
