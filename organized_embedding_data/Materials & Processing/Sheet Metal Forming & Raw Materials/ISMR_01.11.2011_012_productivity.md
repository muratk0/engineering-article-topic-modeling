# PRODUCTIVITY

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

Modern sheet metal processing is unthinkable without a flexible form of sheet metal storage, especially without continuous feeding and emptying of the production machines as they operate. When viewed as a whole, the largest potential for improving productivity lies with automated sheet metal storage. Today’s production lines contain expensive processing machines that often make round-the-clock operation necessary to be economically viable.

Lower manpower requirements through fully automated handling and storage in the sheet metal racks, avoidance of expensive warehousing through maximum use of available space per sheet metal rack, high levels of material availability due to storage in close proximity to production lines and central stock management – all of these features characterise modern sheet metal storage solutions.

As sheet metal storage systems and their continuous flow of materials continue to gain importance, the storage system itself becomes almost a form of logistics centre. Additional storage and stacking products from mezzanines to stacking racks to barrier rails are often designed and constructed from structural steel components for optimum strength, rigidity and safety.

## STORAGE AND RETRIEVAL

Do you value optimised warehousing with rapid material transport and short access times? Innovative storage and retrieval systems conforming to customer requirements can perfect efficient storage operations. Automatic storage and retrieval systems form essential components of a cost-optimised logistics chain.

Storage and retrieval machines transfer goods to and from the relevant sections in the storage racks and material flow conveyor systems, according to specific requirements. Whether it’s a short-term buffer with sequencing and high throughput, a warehouse solely used to supply and stock raw material or finished products, or a buffer between the individual steps of production, storage concepts and solutions can fit a wide range of applications.

Examples include automated storage systems, cradles, racking, conveyor and lifting systems as well as handling equipment.

## CRADLES

Flat goods cradles provide a wider full sheet support for materials up to 48” wide. Typically, two standard cradles are required to handle most sheets. Full-length pallet cradles are also available, providing additional centre support for thinner sheet material to prevent sagging.

Up to ten cradles can be stacked with rapid access even to the bottom of the stack. Cradles protect sheet edges without the risk of damage to stored materials. They therefore help to improve worker safety and productivity whilst preventing inventory damage.

Manual and automatic stacking cradle systems are best suited to storing medium to large quantities. Using a spreader beam, several cradles can be re-stacked at the same time. The systems are suitable for bulky goods with storage lengths of up to 24 metres. Further characteristics are easy access and simple operation. Stacking cradle systems can be up to eight metres high and contain approximately 1,000 storage locations.

## RACKING

Portable stacking racks are self-contained units. They consist of a base and removable posts or end frames. These may be entirely removable or attached with hinges or other folding or collapsing mechanisms. Racks can be welded together from steel tubing. Just insert the post or frames into the base sockets and move with forklift or pallet jack. Stacking racks offer:

■ More storage in less space

■ No need to leave aisles

■ No empty shelves, just collapse racks for storage when not in use

■ Maintenance-free

■ Suitable for last-in, first-out inventories. They can be stacked against walls as deep as needed.
