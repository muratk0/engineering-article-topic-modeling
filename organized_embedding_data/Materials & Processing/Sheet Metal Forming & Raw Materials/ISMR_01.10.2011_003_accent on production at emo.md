# ACCENT ON PRODUCTION AT EMO

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

The AEROSPACE INDUSTRY is the innovation driver for many other sectors, not least the machine tool sector. The starting point is the need for development work in response to the growing use of new materials, chief among them titanium, a material that will take on particular importance in future aircraft programmes like the A350 or the A320 NEO.

Product and production development will in future have to cover the entire product lifecycle. (Picture: © Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft, Munich)

Titanium as a material is extremely difficult to machine and poses new challenges for metal-cutting technology. Speed, precision and flexibility have to be increased, while at the same time costs have to be reduced and time savings achieved.

The Special Show of the Machining Innovations Network at the EMO Hannover 2011 (19 to 24 September) will, in Hall 27 Stand C32, be showing new highlights and milestones in metal-cutting production technology for structural components made of titanium. The entire value creation chain of the aviation industry and its partners is represented, from initial research all the way through to the final application.

On 22 and 23 September 2011, the Institute for Production Technology and Machine Tools (IFW) of Leibniz University in Hannover and the Machining Innovations Network (MIN) will be hosting a conference on the subject of “New Production Technologies in Aerospace Applications”.

In parallel to the EMO Hannover 2011, the international congress on “Sustainable Production” is a prestigious meeting point for specialists and managers from the world of production technology. From 20 – 21 September 2011, experts from the industrial sector, the business community, politicians and academics will present the latest technologies, concepts and strategies for energy-economical, resource-efficient production. ## REPKON ANNOUNCES GLOBAL COLLABORATION

REPKON Machine and Tool Industry & Trade, Inc. (Turkey), a designer and manufacturer of advanced metal forming equipment with emphasis on flowforming, shear forming and hot spinning technologies, has formed a collaboration with Hess Industries, Inc. (USA), a global engineering firm and pioneering manufacturer of metal forming machines, cells and integrated systems.

The strategic alliance of the two companies called Metal Smart aims to provide customers with innovative and cost-effective metal forming solutions worldwide.

“This is an exciting collaboration, grounded in thoughtful exploration of new application areas for our patented technologies, that will grant us instant access to a much wider market, while enabling us to immediately expand our product offering for our existing customers,” said Azer Aran, Chairman of Repkon Machine and Tool Industry & Trade, Inc.

Metal Smart aims to offer customers greater resources for metal forming through the ability to create complete systems, utilising innovative research and development, prototyping and service and support on a global scale. It wishes to provide manufacturing capabilities for a much wider range of industries beyond the traditional production domains typically served by either Repkon or Hess individually. ## DON’T MISS BLECH NORDIC

The FIRST BLECH Nordic sheet metal working exhibition will open its doors from 4 – 7 October 2011 at Stockholmsmässan, in Stockholm (Sweden). One hundred companies from 17 countries will exhibit at this launch exhibition dedicated to sheet metal working. Alongside Swedish companies (45% of the exhibitors), there will be exhibitors from all over the world, including Germany, Italy, Finland, Denmark, Great Britain, the USA and China.

“The high percentage of exhibitors from outside Sweden at the first BLECH Nordic event demonstrates the importance of the Nordic market for the sheet metal working industry. After difficult times, companies in this highly dynamic industry sector are looking to develop new business opportunities and to strengthen their position in well-established, high-tech markets such as the Nordic countries,” said Susanne Rauberger, Exhibition Director, on behalf of the organisers, Mack Brooks Exhibitions (organiser of the EuroBLECH exhibition).

“We are particularly delighted to be able to hold BLECH Nordic during Tekniska Mässan, the Scandinavian Technical Fair, Sweden’s most trendsetting technology fair covering a wide range of different technical manufacturing processes.”

A series of sheet metal research and new technology seminars will be held on each exhibition day. The seminars include presentations about the latest developments in sheet metal forming; simulation and process optimisation; laser and waterjet cutting as well as welding processes. Visitors to BLECH Nordic will be able to attend these seminars free of charge.

For visitors who pre-register at www.blechnordic.com, entry to BLECH Nordic is free. Ticket price on-site is SEK 150.
