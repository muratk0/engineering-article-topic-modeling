# An eye on slitting

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

TOA DR ENTERPRISE Co. Ltd. increasingly specialises in the manufacture of metal slitting machines, lines and equipment sales. The Taiwanese manufacturer has also registered several patent rights for its product lines, as a result of continuous research and development.

Its TDS-700 Ultra Precision slitting line transforms coils into electronic connectors (copper alloy 0.1mmT x 30 strips capability).

"One of the leading manufacturers of copper and bronze alloys in Asia, who is also our client, used various well-known coil processing systems from several companies. It was impressed with TOA DR's slitting line, telling us it brought higher efficiency, better quality and user-friendly control design. With multiple mechanical designs and accessories, the TOA DR slitting line has become our

client's main slitting system," the company told ISMR.

"We aim to maximise production efficiency, create extra profit for clients and supply total solutions for most types of materials such as Bimetal; Titanium; Nickel alloy; Silicon Steel and non-ferrous alloys across industries."
