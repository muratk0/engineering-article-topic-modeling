## About Faccin Group

Founded in the 1960s, the FACCIN Group is a global metalforming machine manufacturer headquartered in Visano, Italy. It also has two branches in the U.S. and Germany as well as sales offices, dealers and business partners around the world. It is a specialist in the design, manufacture and sale of plate bending rolls, profile bending machines, dished head machinery and special machines (including ship frame bending rolls and presses as well as plate straightening machines).
