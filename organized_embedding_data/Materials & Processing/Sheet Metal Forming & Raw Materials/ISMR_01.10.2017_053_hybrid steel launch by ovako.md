# Hybrid Steel® launch by Ovako

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

AT THE RECENT Euromat 2017 congress, Ovako introduced a new steel family to challenge traditional steel categorisations – Hybrid Steel. It offers the properties of tool steel, maraging steel and stainless steel, combined with the production economy of engineering steel and, says the company, is designed to enable customers to achieve strong performance and help reduce production costs.

"We are convinced that our hybrid steel is a groundbreaking innovation for the steel industry," said Marcus Hedblom, President and CEO, Ovako. "By using a new alloying philosophy, we have created a steel that has three times the yield and tensile strength of conventional steel at temperatures up to 500°C. These properties are ideal for a wide variety of highly-stressed applications such as engine components, bearings and tools for various purposes."

"Because Hybrid Steel develops its full properties through heat treatment at temperatures that cause very low distortion, manufacturers will be able to adopt new, more efficient processes that eliminate a number of stages. For example, a component might be machined to its final dimensions in a softer condition and then heat-treated to achieve its full strength, a route that could offer a significant reduction in manufacturing cost and complexity," added the company.

Prototype test programmes are already underway with a number of component manufacturers. See www.ovako.com
