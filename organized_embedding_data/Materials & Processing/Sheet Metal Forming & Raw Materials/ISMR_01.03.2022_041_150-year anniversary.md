# 150-year anniversary

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

“Celebrating 150 years of trading in 2022, we are proud to be the UK's longest established provider of metal fabrication machinery," Morgan Rushworth told ISMR. “With one of the most comprehensive ranges of high-performance, precision machinery in the industry, there is a Morgan Rushworth solution for almost every fabrication challenge."

From humble beginnings in 1872 manufacturing plate working machinery, Morgan Rushworth has become a leading supplier of technologically advanced sheet metal and fabrication machinery, built to exacting specifications and standards. It offers a range of press brakes; fibre laser cutters; plasma cutters and guillotines, making it a partner for metal fabrication, engineering and manufacturing businesses UK-wide.

“This machinery delivers productivity improvements, reduces downtime, waste and environmental impacts. We offer live machinery demonstrations in our UK showrooms, customer service from specification to delivery and installation and expert commissioning and service engineering teams. Come to us for specialist advice on the right solution," it added.
