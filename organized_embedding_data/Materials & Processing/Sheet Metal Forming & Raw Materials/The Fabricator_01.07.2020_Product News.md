Flux-cored wires offered in smaller packaging, diameters

Lincoln Electric has expanded its line of hardfacing consumables products with new Lincore flux-cored wires and small, shop-friendly packaging. A number of new and existing hardfacing wires now are offered on 10-lb. spools in 1/16 in. dia.

The new Lincore wires are 70-G, a severe-abrasion metal-cored wire designed for wet abrasion applications; 57-G, a general-abrasion and -impact metal-cored wire for metal-to-metal wear applications; and 420HC-S for overlay deposits that resist metal-to-metal wear and mild corrosion.

In addition, new diameters and packaging have been added for the existing Lincore 50, 55, 55-G, 60-O, and BU-G wires.

Area light is explosion-proof

Air Systems Intl. Inc. has introduced the Air-Light EX explosion-proof, DCpowered LED area light. It deploys rapidly without the use of tools and folds to fit within its integral case.

Exchange platform accelerates EDI implementation for service centers

Invera has announced the release of Stratix/Edix, the EDI-ready exchange platform that natively integrates with Stratix ERP to facilitate and accelerate the implementation of EDI for metal service centers.

Updated welding analyzer enables fast report generation, editing

ESAB Welding & Cutting Products has updated its Quick Weld Productivity Analyzer, a calculator for total weld cost analysis, so users can generate a PDF report. The PDF includes a QR code users can scan with their mobile phone that takes them back to their original calculation and input variables. They then can adjust the variables to create alternative calculations and generate a new PDF report.

The updated calculator includes more filler metal selections for the North American market. Globally, improved links present only filler metals and welding equipment that are available in the country of origin.

The analyzer calculates the net deposition rate, welding speed per pass, weld operation time, and total production cost per foot weld or cost per product.

Wind turbines require large-diameter, heavy-weight flanges and tower sections, typically handled by overhead cranes. New tools added to automatic laser tube cutting systems

BLM GROUP has enhanced its LT Fiber EVO and CO2-type LT722D automatic laser tube cutting systems, which process special sections and open shapes up to 6.0 in. dia., with Active Tools. These tools automatically adapt process parameters for optimal results regardless of operator experience.

They include Active Tilt, which uses focus head movement to provide fast processing of small features; Active Scan, which eliminates errors induced by distorted tubes; Active Weld, which adapts laser cutting parameters to ensure continuity and perfect scrap detachment in consideration of bigger weld seams; Active Focus for error-free cuts regardless of changes of material and thickness; Active Marking for dynamic part marking for production traceability; Active Speed to help ensure high cutting quality on critical jobs; and Active Piercing, which increases piercing speed and reliability even on inconsistent material quality and thickness.

Diode laser offers high output power in compact package

The Coherent HighLight DL HPS is an industrial diode laser that offers 1- to 4-kW output in a compact, selfcontained package for metal cladding, heat treating, brazing, and welding. The laser uses an all-in-one configuration, with the laser head, power supply, water chiller, and heat exchanger contained in a single 670-by 1,050- by 1,760-mm enclosure, making it suitable for space-constrained applications.

The system’s conduction-cooled laser diodes enable the closed-loop chiller to use distilled water rather than deionized water. The near-IR output is delivered through a detachable delivery fiber terminating in a choice of QBH or QD connector. A variety of processing heads, including zoom-focusing units, are available for tailoring the output size, shape, and intensity to the application’s requirements.
