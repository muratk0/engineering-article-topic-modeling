# EFB (Europäische Forschungsgesellschaft für Blechverarbeitung e. V.)

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand C15

The EFB (European Research Association for Sheet Metal Processing) organises industrial research on sheet metal processing. Experts from industry and science work on these joint projects. These are presented, discussed and evaluated in specialist working groups.

www.efb.de

The EFB also advises upon and initiates future-oriented research roadmaps for industries. It ensures the knowledge transfer of publicly funded project results through various channels, such as events and trade fairs. Contacts between business and research partners, as well as users and suppliers, are continuously being developed.

At the “TechnologieForum Blechverarbeitung" EFB stand at EuroBLECH 2024, research institutes and companies will present new developments on pressing issues of sheet metal processing to users.
