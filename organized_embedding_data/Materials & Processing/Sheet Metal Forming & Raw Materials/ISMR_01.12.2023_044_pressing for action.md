# Pressing for action

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Stainless-steel forming examples (left to right: steamer, hob and heat exchanger).

At EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany (25-28 October 2022), Dieffenbacher showcased advanced system solutions for metalforming at the booth co-hosted by the Wirtschaftsförderung Heilbronn-Franken in Hall 15 at the exhibition.

With flexible machine concepts and customised complete plant solutions, even for niche application, Dieffenbacher supports its partners from the metal industry in project phases ranging from planning new press shops to integrating forming lines into existing structures. Its smart concepts for saving energy and increasing material efficiency all contribute to making production processes more sustainable.

Georg Obermaier, head of the company's forming business unit, presented its new manufacturer-independent digitalisation platform, EVORIS, in a half-hour talk at the EuroBLECH Presentation Area (hall 26, booth L60). EVORIS helps plant operators to better understand and control their production and manufacturing processes by means of AI-supported analysis of live data. This, said the manufacturer, allows important decisions to be made more quickly.

"EVORIS is an open and browser-based system. With its modular app structure, it is easily extendable and updateable. EVORIS apps use a modern, intuitive interface and can be securely operated from any mobile or desktop device within a plant's network," said Obermaier. "Based on standardised interfaces, EVORIS collects and processes machine data at a central point. From there, the EVORIS apps are fed relevant data to provide manufacturers with deep insights into their production processes via powerful trending tools and various reporting possibilities."

At EuroBLECH, the manufacturer also displayed fully automated and flexible hydraulic press lines for sheet-metal forming and the production of quality parts made of stainless-steel. The multi-point drawing system Multi-Point-Control (MPC) enables the production of complex geometries through targeted control of material flow.

Its embossing system, designed for stamping plates for stainless-steel heat exchangers, has been designed to meet demand for accurate embossing depth. Its portfolio ranges from the press to feeding and removal equipment to tools and tool-changing systems. The tailored press (with press forces from 1,600 to 10,000kN) completes its metalforming portfolio.

As an independent family business in its fifth generation, Dieffenbacher is a leading manufacturer of press systems and complete production plants for the wood-based panels, forming and recycling industries. It was founded in 1873 by Jakob Dieffenbacher as a small factory for fruit, wine and edible-oil presses.
