## What is **Cut-To-Length Production Line**

Generally, Cut-To-Length Production Line is suitable for steel coil deep-processing enterprises, white home appliance production enterprises, etc. It is an auxiliary processing equipment for steel products. Generally, the investment is relatively large and the output is relatively balanced. A standard production line has a storage table, feeding trolley, auxiliary support, uncoiling Machine, telescopic shovel head, leveling machine, looper, side guide, five-roller rough leveling and measuring machine, shearing machine, conveyor, stacking frame, and discharging trolley. Among them, the specific configuration can be customized according to the needs of customers. Generally, it takes several months for an enterprise to produce products from customer customization to product delivery, so this is non-standard customized equipment.

### Key Functions of a Cut-to-Length Production Line

1. **Uncoiling** – The steel coil is loaded onto a decoiler and gradually fed into the line.
2. **Leveling** – A precision leveler flattens the sheet to eliminate internal stresses and surface irregularities.
3. **Measuring** – The sheet is accurately measured by a high-precision length control system.
4. **Cutting** – A shear (mechanical, hydraulic, or rotary) cuts the sheet into required lengths.
5. **Stacking** – Finished sheets are neatly stacked for easy handling, packaging, or further processing.

## Advantages of Using a CTL Line

- **High Precision:** Consistent length accuracy for every sheet.
- **Productivity:** Continuous, automated operation shortens production cycles.
- **Versatility:** Handles a wide range of coil materials and thicknesses.
- **Labor Saving:** Automation reduces manual handling.
- **Cost Efficiency:** Minimizes material waste and improves yield.
