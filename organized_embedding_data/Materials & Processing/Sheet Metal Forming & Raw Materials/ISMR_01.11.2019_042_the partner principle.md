# THE PARTNER PRINCIPLE

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

ISMR met Joakim Dovrén, CIDAN's sales director, and Gerhard Resch, MD, nuIT, at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart recently to explore their latest new initiatives and products.

The CIDAN Machinery Group, a folding specialist in Sweden producing sheetmetal machinery since 1907, offers a wide range of solutions and products for companies operating in the sheet metal, construction and manufacturing industries. With a dealer and agent network in over 30 countries, it supplies sheet metal folders, metal bending machines, shears, Forstner coil-handling machines and new software to help users bend sheet metal efficiently into various forms.

It has consistently aimed to develop and produce machines with minimal environmental impact. It was an early pioneer in the first step from oils and hydraulics to electricity and pneumatic energy in its machines, in a bid to stop harmful residue/by-products and develop quiet, energy-efficient systems.

## Accent on cooperation

The CIDAN Group has forged strong teaming relationships with other manufacturers to promote its brands and expertise worldwide. A very recent example is the joint development between Thalmann Machinenbau AG with CIDAN nuIT and CIDAN Forstner that led to the launch of "The Base" earlier this year at CIDAN nuIT in St Polten, Austria.

This fully automated folding equipment combines a Thalmann TD folder with a CIDAN Forstner decoiling line and cutting line, CIDAN nuIT software and an automated handling system. "The Base" can fold a set of different parts, with different dimensions, straight from an e-portal based at a customer's location with no direct labour involved in the bending, decoiling and cutting operation. This is a revolutionary development that is marketed by CIDAN nuIT, with support from Thalmann Machinenbau AG.

Nearly 80 customers and retailers visited the Metaflex/nuIT GmbH facility in St. Pölten, Austria, on 11-12 September 2019 to see nuIT, The BASE (Bending and Slitting Engine)

and upgraded innovations in collaboration. In total, more than 500 profiles worth SEK 130,000 were produced during the show, which lasted for eight hours.

"The BASE and nuIT greatly impressed the participants, even though the production rate could have been increased by almost 50 percent. Many people liked the mix of machines and especially appreciated being able to see the nuIT planning tool in operation," Gerhard Resch, MD, nuIT, told ISMR at Blechexpo.

nuIT's nuEVOLUTION package provides tools and services that range from practically simple, such as company-wide networking, to futuristic (e.g. the web-based drawing surface where profiles can be drawn on the fly, calculated and sent to production in realtime). Smart Production facilitates machine-tomachine communication where orders can be combined and the amount of scrap reduced significantly.

"With nuEVOLUTION's Intelligent Management System (IMS), the books become 'a breeze' as the IMS provides realtime, relevant information between divisions or just across your 'intelligent factory' floor. Whether it is materials management, labelling, analysis, document management or the logistics behind day-to-day planning (to include SMS status updates to your customers), the IMS can cover it," explained Gerhard Resch.

## New technology developments

The CIDAN Group promoted several new technology developments on its booth at Blechexpo, including the nuIT software and BASE concept.

Its new FX PLUS folder is an FX folder featuring an automatically movable bending centre with eccentric drive and extra strong lower beam. The ProLink control system for FX is designed, CIDAN told ISMR, to make programming 'easy, intuitive and fast'. On the panel, users can control machine operations, sheet thickness settings and adjustments of the bending centre.

CIDAN's new FORMA Z machine, on the booth in Stuttgart, featured an automatic crowning facility and 'up and down' folding to eliminate the need for an operator to flip the material. This is designed to enable time-efficient folding of complex parts with one operator.

The third new development on display was CIDAN Group's Forstner PSM programmable slitter, which automatically adjusts the slitting width while processing.

"We aim to continue on the Industry 4.0 path, developing communication pathways between machines/software and monitoring machines. We will also continue to add functions. From a sales/marketing perspective, we have a clear growth target aimed at expanding into regions where we have footholds (such as the Alps and Scandinavia) and will continue building that strategy. I fully expect that we will open one or two more sales/technical support offices elsewhere in Europe next year," Joakim Dovrén, CIDAN's sales director, told ISMR.

"We are also making a lot of changes in our own production to streamline our processes and improve our in-house productivity levels. This includes investing in new software to help us optimise our production efficiency."

The Group's avowed intention is to provide high-quality, flexible machines offering ease of use as part of a continuous improvement process. This process is based, it explained, on market requirements and stipulations for innovation, automation and quality – these, it explained, are its guiding principles.

## Markets and customers

The year has been a strong one for the CIDAN, with growth in nearly all its markets.

"We have seen a double-digit increase (10% or more) in our turnover this year, compared to last year. U.S. markets are going very well – we sold around a third of our products to this market this year. We have a U.S. sales/ support/warehouse office in Georgia to support this," added Joakim Dovrén.

## Customers expect us to react flexibly and quickly

"Next year, we are also predicting a higher turnover because we have seen no signs of a downturn in the market. We are also expanding with more salespeople and more service technicians. We aim to penetrate more new markets worldwide. The Alps region has been a growing one for us. Our sister company, Forstner, is based in Austria and we have expanded our sales and service personnel there and have seen a consequent jump in sales. We have also been increasing business in specific regions by working closely with dealers in markets such as Norway, Iceland and most of Europe," he explained.

According to Dovrén, apart from software and integration issues, customers look for strong service support and reliability.

"Customers expect us to react flexibly and quickly. This ties into our Industry 4.0 philosophy because, if the machine can be hooked up to the cloud, we can monitor it remotely and be proactive on maintenance. This answers customer requirements for better reliability and service, and we are doing our best to move in this Industry 4.0 direction. This also helps customers to make the most of their machines," he told ISMR.

In general, Dovrén sees increased demand for automation on lines. Many of CIDAN's customers and end-users are roofers who produce roofing systems themselves. However, he sees this business model changing in the future.

"For example, in the Scandinavian market there is a small shear and power folder in every small roofing workshop. We don't see many companies, at the moment, who are subcontracting to roofers in this market. However, I do foresee a move towards more flexible subcontractors, with more specialised shops, producing the profiles in future for the roofers. This would be an important step change in the market," he explained.

However, CIDAN intends to continue catering for all its customers worldwide, large or small, with a range of differing needs.

"We need to meet the needs of all our customers, which can be wildly different,' concluded Dovrén. "For example, some customers want the nuIT suite with all the production planning, ERP communication bells and whistles and then we have other customers who just want to continue with their existing production equipment. This generally has nothing to do with the overall size of companies – it is more a question of individual philosophies. Our remit is to keep communicating with customers to provide the equipment that they need, when they need it."■
