# FOCUS ON SURFACE FINISHING

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

## An eye on quality and efficiency

The Bavarian company, Rösler Oberflächentechnik GmbH, supplied the wire belt blasting system for descaling, paint stripping, derusting, roughening and cleaning the metal parts. This delivers all-round reproducible blasting results.

The system for deburring and edge rounding (the EdgeBreaker® 9000 LINE) was manufactured by ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH, based in Baden-Baden, Germany.

"This is designed for efficient slag removal, deburring and rounding of metal parts in one single pass through the machine. Compared to single-sided machines where parts pass through several times, this system cuts [processing] time by fifty per cent and increases productivity," confirmed ARKU.

"Liebherr consciously chose European manufacturers not just because of the quality of their products, but also because of comprehensive customer service. This strategic decision reflects Liebherr's commitment to ensuring the highest standards of quality and operational efficiency," it added.

The partners voiced their enthusiasm during a tour of the finished installation.

"We are pleased that our system for slag removal, deburring and edge rounding offers a suitable solution for economical line production that meets the highest quality standards," said Stefan Sauter, International Sales, ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH.

Vasili Schaermann, Global Sales Expert, Rösler Oberflächentechnik GmbH, added, "Our blasting system fits perfectly into [Liebherr's] production process and delivers impressive results."

The shortage of skilled labour was also a key point of discussion during the tour.

"At a time when skilled labour is in short supply, our robot technology can be a crucial component in closing this gap and making work steps more efficient," added Markus Lindörfer, Automation Specialist, Teqram B.V.

Liebherr was finding it increasingly difficult to recruit qualified employees for this area. The deburring line was planned over an extended period, with the reduced need for labour considered early in the process. Ever since implementation of the machines, employees have been able to take on other roles at the production site in Biberach.

This project reflects Liebherr's investment in its Biberach site despite difficult economic conditions. The three manufacturers have already worked together several times in various ways, which has had a positive impact on the project outcome at Liebherr.

"Communication between the companies involved has been outstanding. Everyone was considerate [and accommodating] so we were able to achieve progress quickly," concluded Robert Piesche. The system went into operation in May this year and has been running ever since.
