# POSCO COMPLETES CONSTRUCTION OF 2CGL

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

ON 28 JANUARY 2014, Korean steelmaker POSCO completed construction of a second continuous galvanizing line (2CGL) with an annual 500,000-ton capacity, in Altamira, Tamaulipas state (Mexico).

POSCO established a 900,000-ton scale automobile steel plate production system by completing this 2CGL factory. It complements its 1CGL factory which has an annual 400,000-ton production scale and started operation in 2009. Accordingly, POSCO has now become the largest production factory for plated automobile steel plates in Mexico.

“The main 2CGL product will be galvanized steel plates used in the exterior panels of high-end cars. The ‘GI Ace,’ which has improved coating and processing capabilities, and the ‘780MPa class AHSS steel’ premium grade steel plate (which is lighter yet more resistant to external impact), are increasingly in demand by leading automobile companies and are expected to offer high added value to the local automotive industry,” Posco explained.

Mexico is considered to be an important production region with low labour costs and proximity to the largest automotive market in North America. Global automotive companies including Volkswagen, Chrysler, GM and Renault Nissan have entered the market, while 1,000 or so global parts companies, including Autotech and Benteler Automotive, have also clustered, making it a market with high growth potential. Mexico exported 2.4 million cars last year, making it the world’s 5th largest automobile export country, and production lines for new automobile steel plates are continuing to increase at Honda, Mazda and Nissan ■
