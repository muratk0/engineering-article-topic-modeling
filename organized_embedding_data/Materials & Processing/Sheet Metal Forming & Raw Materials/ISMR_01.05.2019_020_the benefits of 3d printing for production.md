## Sheet metal processing at Ditzingen

TRUMPF's TruConnect – Industry 4.0 was live at INTECH. ISMR joined a guided tour through TRUMPF's sheet metal processing facility at Ditzingen, viewing a multitude of technologies such as its Track&Trace to help optimise material flow. The indoor localisation system provides a clear overview, at all times, and reduces time wasted searching for parts.

TRUMPF also demonstrated its Active Speed Control technology to ISMR, as the path to autonomous laser cutting. A TruLaser Weld live demonstration also highlighted the four steps from the CAD model to the welding programme. New applications were discussed in laser metal deposition as were the implications for sheet metal processing and tube production from the advent of electro-mobility.

We also witnessed the launch of several new TRUMPF products – the TruPrint 5000; TruLaser Cell 7040; TruLaser Center 7030; TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber and the TruMatic 7000.

With the TruLaser Cell Series 7040 laser system, users can process two- or threedimensional components or tubes, changing between cutting and welding. The modular set-up of the machine, as well as the option of individual adjustment and retrofitting, enables users to customise the TruLaser Cell Series 7040 to a modified production environment and to react to changing customer requirements. Processing times are reduced using piercing on-the-fly with FastLine Cell and dynamic cutting optics.

"The fully automatic TruLaser Center 7030 laser cutting system integrates all laser cutting processes into a single machine for the first time. The advantage for users is a drastic reduction in throughput time and processing costs. The full-service machine takes care of the entire laser processing workflow, from the drawing to the sorted part. It also integrates intelligent systems, e.g. SmartGate, SmartLift and SortMaster Speed, for higher reliability," TRUMPF told ISMR.

The new TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber combines the high speeds of a solid-state laser with a broad range of processing capabilities. It can cut tubes and profiles with diameters of up to 254mm and materials as thick as 10mm. In addition, this machine is designed to execute high-quality bevel cuts up to 45 degrees. At the heart of the machine is a four-kilowatt TruDisk 4001, a high-power disk laser manufactured by TRUMPF. A RapidCut function further increases productivity.

The TruMatic 7000 combines the benefits of punch and CO₂ laser processing. The active die, said TRUMPF, provides scratch-free processing and new forming options due to the active forming stroke from below. Parts can be removed via sensor-protected punching and loading flaps. The automatic nozzle changer removes the old nozzle for new orders and inserts a new one, saving time in automated operation. With the SheetMaster, users can load, unload and sort parts automatically.

TRUMPF's Open House in Luton will take place from 4-6 June 2019, where its latest generation machines, lasers and software will be on display. There will be an autonomous laser cutting demonstration with Active Speed Control on the new TruLaser 5030 fiber, 10kW laser cutting machine.
