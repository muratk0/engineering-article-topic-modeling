In the factory, an older slitting line had to be replaced because it could no longer meet growing demands for quality, accuracy and flexibility. The focus is on special high-quality strip of exact dimensional accuracy.

"The highest priority is burr-free cutting and tolerance accuracy," clarified Dr. Staubwasser.

"High dimensional accuracy; cutter shafts with the highest concentricity; a high degree of synchronisation of the drive and precise clamping of the individual plant components are required," added Bernd Kasprowicz. Under no circumstances, he added, should the strip bend.

## Making the decision

After an extensive evaluation round, the construction contract for the new equipment was awarded to Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH. Dr. Staubwasser explained why.

"We have been working with Burghardt + Schmidt for around 20 years and have purchased a lot of equipment from them that has proved to be very reliable over many years of production operation. In addition, out of all the suppliers that we contacted, Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH met our expectations the most."

Bernd Kasprowicz added: "We see Burghardt + Schmidt as a qualified supplier of slitting lines that meet our high-quality standards for zinc strip."

Only through close cooperation with a customer can a project like this be brought to a successful conclusion because there is no standard for precision slitting lines.

Thomas Baral, managing director of Burghardt + Schmidt, clarified:

“Each plant is tailor-made to the customer's requirements. We carefully determine the customer's requirements and advise him on what makes technological economic sense. At the end of this process, we can design and build the desired equipment. For Grillo-Werke AG, a compact slitting line with maximum precision cutting was the optimal solution."
