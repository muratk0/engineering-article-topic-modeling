## 50 years of customised machines

WEMO develops, builds and installs special/customised machines for punching, bending, joining and processing blanks and coiled material. This year, the Dutch company is celebrating its 50th anniversary. The second half-century also starts with new corporate colours (dark grey and orange).

“As a proud family-owned Dutch company, we are happy to bear our national colour, orange. This also represents innovation and the most positive colour. We combine this with dark grey, the colour of steel, our core business,” said managing director, Paul Hoekstra.

Over the years, WEMO has developed dedicated and highly flexible machines which can be combined into one complete machine. Since the early days of the company in 1964, the development and manufacture of tools has been another core task.

“This combination of machine-building and tool development allows us to offer clients one-stop shopping, to include full responsibility for their product design not just by connecting separate machines, but also by fully integrating them. But one thing that won’t change over the next 50 years is that our core focus will stay firmly on wishes of our customers,” added Hoekstra.
