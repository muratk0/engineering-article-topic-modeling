# Through its partnership with AMB Picot, 3A Composites has found an intuitive way for its customers to make ALUCOBOND® claddings.

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

3A Composites has installed a rollbending model from AMB Picot in its new Technicum training and demonstration centre to show its customers how to bend flat ALUCOBOND® panels on the CNC-controlled roll bending machine. 3A Composites knowledge, experience and tests, collated as part of a comprehensive database, enables AMB Picot’s easyroll software to predict material behaviour. This is based upon ALUCOBOND® characteristics (thickness, springback effect, mechanical characteristics etc.) but also upon other factors, such as panel temperature. Easyroll is intelligent rolling software based on springback prediction and material simulation, designed to reach the expected rolling diameter first time, with no corrections needed.

"This important partnership between 3A Composites and AMB Picot means that a beginner can, after just a couple of hours of training, roll an ALUCOBOND® panel with the expected radius (including cone rolling). There is no further need for an in-house roll-bending specialist – a user just launches the easyroll program and follows the intuitive process. Easyroll does the rest, depending upon the composite panel selected. Pre-bending of the edges is automatic and there is no need to adjust the radius after rolling. Precision is ±2% of the diameter (with the springback effect already taken into account). The customer's rolling steadiness can be levelled, even if the operator changes," added Spodar.

AMB Picot launched its new easyroll 2.0 software at EuroBLECH in Hanover in October 2018. It is Artificial Intelligence software for roll bending machines.

"We use simulation, combined with algorithms, to predict and minimise deformation on a sheet. Each sheet is different, so the software needs to adapt. We continuously update the algorithms and offer updates every three months to customers. Easyroll predicts material behaviour and continuously learns from experience. It is as efficient for bending special parts (in manual mode) as it is for large batches (assisted programming) and keeps customers on track for higher productivity," Grégory Conraud, managing director of AMB Picot, told ISMR.

## A strong partnership

The partnership between 3A Composites and AMB Picot has benefited both companies, as well as their respective customers. Besides direct customers, 3A Composites places its focus on decision-makers i.e. architects, builders, building owners etc. Enabling customers to quickly and accurately bend ALUCOBOND® panels on-site is a value-add activity for 3A Composites as a systems solutions provider, as well as saving time and cost for its customers, but it also brings AMB Picot's roll bending technology to the attention of these important users from over 100 countries. The software's ease of use and the machine's intuitive operation were two of the benefits cited by 3A Composites.

"AMB Picot is a very flexible and innovative partner, offering exactly what we were looking for regarding the bending process of roundshaped ALUCOBOND® elements. Its 2m-long CNC-controlled roll bending machine enables unlimited repeatability of the same 2D element, which is very important when creating roundshaped elements on a facade in a homogeneous manner. Its easyroll software includes all the parameters of our materials (hardness, elongation behaviour etc.) and ensures that customers get the results they need," added Guntram Eydner, Head of New Products & Product Management, 3A Composites GmbH.

He told ISMR that approximately 200-million square metres of ALUCOBOND® had already been sold to customers all over the world for fabrication as facades for architectural buildings.

"In general, there is a trend towards freeform facades (comprising round-shaped or parametric shaped facade elements). Our ALUCOBOND® material (4mm thickness) is ideal for this kind of bending, which we demonstrate to customers on the AMB Picot 2m machine in our demonstration and training centre. We are a market leader in this industry and aim to generate new and innovative ideas which we regularly bring to market."
