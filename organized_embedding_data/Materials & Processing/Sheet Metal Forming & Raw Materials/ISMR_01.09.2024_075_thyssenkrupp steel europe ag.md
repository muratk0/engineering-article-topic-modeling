# thyssenkrupp Steel Europe AG

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

www.thyssenkrupp.com

thyssenkrupp Steel will present innovative and sustainable steel solutions at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hannover to "unlock your green potential." These will include tkH2Steel® for carbonneutral steelmaking and CO₂-reduced bluemint® steels to help mitigate climate change.

"We will also present ZM Ecoprotect® Solar high-quality zinc-magnesium-coated steels for effectively protecting high-performance PV mounting systems from corrosion. Also on show will be our high-performance steels for resourceefficient, lightweight automotive construction, enabling weight reductions with improved levels of safety," it told ISMR.

"We offer our customers high-grade stainless, acid-resistant and heat-resistant steels which are used in numerous applications. Their advantages include high corrosion resistance; good processing characteristics (weldability); high thermal resistance; easy maintenance and optimal hygienic properties," it added.

## Marcegaglia Group

Stand B58

www.marcegaglia.com

Marcegaglia is a leading independent industrial group in the global steel processing sector, with a turnover of more than eight billion euros. The group has operations worldwide with 6,600 employees, 60 sales offices, 30 steel plants and 6.2 million tons of steel processed yearly, serving more than 15,000 customers.

Its products range from carbon steels to stainless steel plates and strip, long products to pipes, pre-painted and special products. In 2023, for the first time in over 60 years of history, the Group also entered primary production, acquiring an electric oven steel mill for stainless steels in Sheffield (UK). It invested with a view to shortening and stabilising supply chains and developing an offer of increasingly sustainable and competitive products.

"Strong sectorial diversification and a presence in multiple geographical areas means that Marcegaglia does not depend on the performance of a single business or region, guaranteeing its outstanding and perhaps unique resilience in the steel industry," added the Group.
