# Eminent professor brings vital metals and materials knowledge to Swansea University

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

PROFESSOR of Steel Process Metallurgy, Sridhar Seetharaman, has been recruited to join the Swansea University College of Engineering team.

Professor Seetharaman has held a number of research posts and visiting Professor and Chair positions and received numerous awards for his work. His research and teaching interests lie in materials for clean energy processes and materials processing through clean energy.

Professor Seetharaman's experience will be of vital importance to Swansea University's new Steel and Metals Institute, which was opened by the First Minister of Wales this year.

Welcoming his appointment, Professor Seetharaman said: "I am delighted to take on this part-time position at Swansea University, and be part of the exciting growth that is happening in steel research."

Professor Seetharaman received his PhD from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology in Metallurgy and his undergraduate degree from the Royal Institute of Technology in Sweden in Materials Science. Sridhar is a visiting Professor at USTB in China, and honorary Professor at the University of Warwick. He also holds the Brahm Prakash Visiting Chair at IISC in India. He has graduated over 25 doctoral students and published over 180 journal papers in: secondary refining, casting and mould slags, coal gasification, oxidation and hot-shortness.

He is the editor for AIST Transactions and an associate editor for Metallurgical and Metals Transactions A, B and E. He is also on the International Advisory Board for Steel Research International and has served in that role for ISIJ International.
