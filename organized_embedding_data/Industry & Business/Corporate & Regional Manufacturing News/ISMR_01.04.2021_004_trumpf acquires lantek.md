# TRUMPF acquires Lantek

[TARİH: 01.04.2021 ISMR]

Sheet metal specialist TRUMPF has acquired software house, Lantek.

"TRUMPF is opening up to customers' production ecosystems with this acquisition," said Tom Schneider, managing director of machine tool development at TRUMPF. "Our customers' process is our focus - with Lantek, we comprehensively cover the sheet metal process chain, even with machines from different manufacturers. In this way, we are taking another big step towards efficient and connected sheet metal production and enriching our Smart Factory solution portfolio."

After participating in the development of umati, the open machine data interface, the development of Omlox, the open positioning standard and cooperation with intralogistics expert, Jungheinrich, on automated guided vehicles, the agreement with Lantek is a further step for TRUMPF towards process optimisation and connectivity for sheet metal production of the future, it told ISMR.

Lantek will continue to operate under its current name. Integration under the TRUMPF brand is not planned and Lantek continues 'committed to neutrality and independence from all machine tool builders'. The transaction is expected to close by the end of the month.

Lantek was founded in 1986 and is headquartered in Vitoria-Gasteiz, Spain. The company operates with 20 locations in 14 countries. More than 220 employees develop, implement and maintain software for sheet metal and metalworking with cutting technologies. This includes CAD, CAM, MES and ERP solutions.

In a year of COVID-19 recession, Lantek closed 2020 with record sales figures and customer numbers. Turnover reached 21.3 million euros and the number of active users at the end of the year was 25,747.

Lantek's ability to support remote working was exemplified with the launch of Lantek v2020 under the slogan "Never Stop Manufacturing". This new version virtualises the work of teams and allows customers to continue using Lantek solutions, wherever they might be. Lantek's Solutions Division, focused on the Digital Factory and advanced manufacturing also launched Lantek Analytics and Lantek iQuoting, two operational services hosted in the Cloud environment under the SaaS model.

Lantek recently opened a new Centre of Excellence in Bilbao, Spain, with offices in the Iberdrola Tower.
