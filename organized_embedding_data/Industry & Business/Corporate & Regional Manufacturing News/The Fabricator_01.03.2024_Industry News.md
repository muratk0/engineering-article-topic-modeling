Arlington Capital Partners creates new company from merger of acquisitions

Arlington Capital Partners, a Washington, D.C.-area private investment firm, has announced the formation of Keel Holdings LLC through the merger of existing Arlington portfolio company Pegasus Steel with the newly acquired Metal Trades LLC, Yonges Island, S.C., and Merrill Technologies Group, Saginaw, Mich.

With more than 1 million sq. ft. of manufacturing floor space and about 700 employees, Keel offers design, engineering, fabrication, machining, integration, assembly, finishing, and surface treatment across its nine facilities in Michigan and South Carolina. The company is a Tier 1 provider of large, fabricated structures to OEMs that serve the needs of the U.S. Navy, Army, Airforce, and Department of Defense.

"Keel is supporting the steep production ramp of the Columbia and Virginia class submarine programs by positioning itself to deliver turnkey structures and modules to its customers, empowering them to use valuable shipyard space more effectively," said Ben Ramundo, a principal at Arlington.

Norfolk Iron & Metal installing temper mill cut-to-length line

Metal service center Norfolk Iron & Metal has announced it is installing a Butech Bliss temper mill cut-to-length line at its Port of Catoosa, Okla., processing facility.

The four-high temper mill line elongates, levels, and shears steel up to 5/8-in. Grade 50 and 1/2-in. Grade 100. The line can process thicknesses from 0.060 to 0.625 in., widths from 36 to 96 in., and lengths up to 720 in.

Installation began in 2023, and the line will be fully operational by Q3 2024. Upon implementation of the new line, the facility will have two comprehensive processing lines, a waterjet, and new laser cutting technology that also can process up to 720-in. material.

NBT awards manufacturing scholarships to 31 students

Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs (NBT), the foundation of the Fabricators and Manufacturers Association (FMA), Elgin, Ill., recently granted 31 scholarships totaling $46,500 to students enrolled in manufacturing certificate and degree programs. These awards help defray educational expenses such as tuition fees, books, and other school-related costs for the upcoming semester.

All award winners have strong overall GPAs with high grades in relevant coursework. In as few as six months, some of the scholarship recipients will graduate from their programs and become fulltime employees in manufacturing organizations across the U.S.

"We saw a 71% increase in qualified applications over last year’s spring semester. It is very encouraging to see this growth in interest, and NBT is extremely proud to support these accomplished students in their pursuit of manufacturing careers," said Ed Dernulc, NBT director. "Our sincere belief is that this opportunity will help lay the groundwork for them to become valuable contributors to the ever-evolving metal fabrication industry."

Metal manufacturer and fabricator Solv Metals LLC, a division of Solv Holdings, Fort Wayne, Ind., has announced the acquisition of Wisconsin Metal Parts (WMP) and Lean Manufacturing Products, both in Waukesha, Wis.

WMP’s services include CNC machining, laser cutting, metal stamping, tool- and diemaking, sheet metal fabrication, welding, and assembly. Lean Manufacturing Products specializes in storage racks, carts, and tables to help optimize industrial space utilization and improve workflow.

All Metals Fabricating marks 70th anniversary with $7 million automation plan

Allen, Texas-based All Metals Fabricating, celebrating 70 years in business, has initiated phase one of a $7 million automation plan with the end goal of a fully automated integrated cut-to-bend system.

The company has implemented Industry 4.0 throughout the business, including AI and automation through quoting and back-office accounting, robotics in manufacturing, machine monitoring software for visibility, departmental queue monitors with scheduling and real-time KPIs displayed, and employee performance dashboards for incentive-based bonuses.

The contract manufacturer offers sheet metal fabricating, machining, electromechanical assembly, and powder coating.

AMADA AMERICA to sponsor former Indy 500 winner

Takuma Sato, winner of the 2017 and 2020 Indianapolis 500, returns to Rahal Letterman Lanigan (RLL) Racing for the 108th running of the race. AMADA AMERICA Inc. will be the primary sponsor for the No. 75 Honda-powered entry in the May 26 event.

AMADA was the primary sponsor of the team’s No. 30 entry at the Children’s of Alabama Indy Grand Prix at Barber Motorsports Park on April 30. This will be its first time to participate in the Indianapolis 500.

"We are incredibly excited to partner with the renowned Rahal Letterman Lanigan Racing team at the 108th running of the Indy 500! Our partnership signifies a joint commitment to engineering excellence and innovation. We eagerly anticipate that this iconic race will demonstrate the strong collaboration between our two technology-driven organizations," said Stephen Keating, executive vice president, sales and key accounts, AMADA.

AMADA also will be a major associate sponsor of the No. 30 entry driven by Pietro Fittipaldi for the 2024 Acura Grand Prix of Long Beach, near its Buena Park, Calif., headquarters.

Sato competed for RLL in 2012 and from 2018-2021. He has earned six NTT INDYCAR Series wins, four of which have come with RLL, including the team’s second Indy 500 win in 2020. Most recently, Sato competed in oval races with Chip Ganassi Racing in 2023. The 2024 Indianapolis 500 will mark his 15th time to participate in the race and sixth with RLL.

Angeles Equity Partners acquires Acieta to expand robotics integration platform

Private investment firm Angeles Equity Partners LLC has announced the acquisition of Acieta LLC, a Waukesha, Wis.-based industrial robotics manufacturer and integrator, from Mitsui & Co. (U.S.A.) Inc.

This transaction marks the fourth strategic acquisition by Angeles in the robotics integration sector, complementing its current platform comprising Rōbex, Mid-State Engineering, and +Vantage. Acieta further expands the business’s equipment tending, welding, and palletizing capabilities across a broader set of end markets including welding and fabrication, foundry and die, construction and building products, and agriculture.

Acieta will continue to operate its combined 67,000 sq. ft. of manufacturing space in Council Bluffs, Iowa, and Waukesha.

Lincoln Foodservice Equipment sets up national operations base in Louisiana

Lincoln Foodservice Equipment, a Welbilt brand company that produces commercial-grade foodservice equipment, is establishing a new U.S. operations center at Welbilt’s existing site in Shreveport, La.

The $2.3 million investment will allow the company to create 99 direct jobs and 137 indirect jobs.

The project will add 35,000 sq. ft. of manufacturing space to the existing facility, which manufactures foodservice products for its Frymaster brand. The additional space will support the production of Lincoln Foodservice brand ovens for industry customers nationwide.

Garwood Metal Co. adds compact fiber laser

Sheet metal fabricator Garwood Metal Co. has announced the addition of an AMADA Brevis 1212 AJ compact fiber laser at its facility in Garwood, N.J. The system can process two 4- by 4-ft. sheets seamlessly, manage rectangular parts that require jigs with clamping to fabricate, and cut rectangular tube.

A choice of assist gases—oxygen, nitrogen, or high-compression shop air—is available to meet project specifications. The fiber laser delivers precise cut edge quality in copper, brass, titanium, and exotic metals in flat and 3D shapes, reports the company.
