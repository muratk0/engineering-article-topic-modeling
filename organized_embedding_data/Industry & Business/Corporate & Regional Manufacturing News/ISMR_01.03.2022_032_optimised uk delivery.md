# Optimised UK delivery

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

With the withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the EU, customers and suppliers faced a new and challenging situation. Standard parts manufacturer, Meusburger, familiarised itself with the new requirements and customs formalities at an early stage and is continuously optimising the delivery processes in close coordination with its forwarding agents.

"On 31 January 2022, we switched to a new delivery system in the UK, offering our customers further benefits. We will supply our customers in the UK 'DDP- delivered, duty paid' and take care of the entire customs clearance process as well as tax processing. We will invoice in GBP and include British VAT. In addition, customers will still have the option to make payments in euros," it explained.

"Advantages for our UK customers include faster delivery; no costs or expenses for customs clearance and tax processing; no costs or risks associated with the transport of goods and invoicing and payment in GBP, with option for payment in euros," it added.■
