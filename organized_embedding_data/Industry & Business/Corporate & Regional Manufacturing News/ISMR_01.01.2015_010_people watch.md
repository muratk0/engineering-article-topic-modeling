## New appointments and contract extensions for ThyssenKrupp

AS PART OF THEIR regular meetings, the supervisory boards of ThyssenKrupp Elevator Technology AG, Industrial Solutions AG and Steel Europe AG have extended the appointments of several management board members and appointed two new ones.

In the Elevator Technology business area, the appointment of Gabriele Sons (53) as CHRO and Labour Director was extended. In the Industrial Solutions business area, the appointment of CFO, Jörg Schönewolf (52), was also extended.

Jens Michael Wegmann (49) was appointed as a new member of the business area’s management board with effect from 1 February 2015. Wegmann, who has a degree in electrical engineering, will succeed Dr. Joachim Panek, who retires on 30 June 2015. Following a familiarization period, Jens Michael Wegmann will take over as Chief Operating Officer from April. He was most recently member of the Executive Board and Chief Business Officer of AGT International in Zurich.

In the Steel Europe business area, the appointments of Andreas Goss (50) as CEO and Thomas Schlenz (57) as CHRO and Labour Director were extended. Premal Desai (45) was also appointed as a new member of the management board with effect from 1 January 2015. Desai will take over as the business area’s Chief Financial Officer.

There has also been a change at the helm of ThyssenKrupp’s North American operations. Patrick Bass, becomes CEO of ThyssenKrupp North America, Inc. from 1 January 2015.
