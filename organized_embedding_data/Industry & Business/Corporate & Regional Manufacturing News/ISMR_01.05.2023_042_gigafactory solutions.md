# Gigafactory solutions

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

According to recent estimates, global demand for lithium-ion batteries will more than quintuple to nearly 5,000 gigawatt hours by 2030. To meet the enormous demand, Schuler, together with its latest new acquisition Sovema from Italy, offers machines and lines for mass production of the energy storage devices, especially for electric cars. The two companies will be introducing these solutions at the Battery Show Europe in Stuttgart from 23-25 May 2023.

Schuler's and Sovema's production lines can cover the key process steps. These include solutions for battery cell assembly and formation. During formation, the first charging and discharging processes can be carried out.

In addition, Sovema subsidiary Bitrode will be unveiling its new Sol Bitrode Digital Cycler™M. The BTDC™ is a precision charge/discharge test system specifically designed for high-capacity cells with regenerative capabilities.

Schuler will also be represented one hall away, on joint booth 8-F61, with equipment to produce prismatic and cylindrical battery housings. Dewatering and drying technologies used in the production and also recycling of energy storage systems from ANDRITZ Separation can be seen there e.g. for the recovery of valuable materials such as lithium, cobalt or graphite. Other exhibitors include packaging handling specialist, Jorgensen Engineering, and NPB Automation.

Solutions from production to recycling of battery cells will be shown by Schuler, Sovema and other partners at Battery Europe.

Schuler is a leading manufacturer of systems for sheet-metal forming (automated, mechanical or hydraulic system solutions). With innovations such as ServoDirect technology, Schuler presses can quickly be adapted to changing production conditions and are designed for high productivity with short die change times and modern automation technology from one source.
