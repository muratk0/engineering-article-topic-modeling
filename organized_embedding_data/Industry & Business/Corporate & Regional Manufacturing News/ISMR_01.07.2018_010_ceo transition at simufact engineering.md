# CEO transition at Simufact Engineering

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

SIMUFACT ENGINEERING, AN MSC software company, has announced a change in its top management. Previous CEO and managing director, Michael Wohlmuth, has passed over his position to his co-managing director and CTO, Dr. Hendrik Schafstall. Michael Wohlmuth will take over responsibilities as Vice-President for Strategic Business Development.

"I look forward to meeting this challenging job and I want to thank my partner and friend, Michael Wohlmuth, for these exciting and great 23 years at Simufact Engineering," said Dr. Hendrik Schafstall, CEO and MD of Simufact Engineering. “I am pleased that Michael will remain with his deep knowledge and experience to strengthen strategic business development at Simufact Engineering."

At the same time, Stefan Zimmer (in his role as senior director, global sales and operations) has taken over full responsibility for the operative sales business.

Dr. Hendrik Schafstall and Michael Wohlmuth founded Simufact Engineering in 1995. Since 2015, Simufact has been part of the U.S.- based MSC Software family. See www.simufact.de
