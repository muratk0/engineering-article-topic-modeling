# New chairman for VDW

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

DR. HEINZ-JÜRGEN PROKOP has been elected unanimously as the new Chairman by the Executive Committee of the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders’ Association) and the Sector Association Machine Tools and Manufacturing Systems within the German Engineering Federation (VDMA). He will succeed Martin Kapp, who will be stepping down after six years, from 1 January 2016.

“I am grateful for the confidence placed in me, and am looking forward to my future remit in the VDW,” said Heinz-Jürgen Prokop. “The German machine tool industry is on the right track for success. For the VDW, the task now is to support its member companies in future-shaping issues like internationalisation, energy-efficiency, securing new recruits, additive manufacturing processes and Industry 4.0, and smoothing their path. I want to make a proactive contribution towards this.”

Heinz-Jürgen Prokop is 57 years old and, as a Board Member at TRUMPF Werkzeugmaschinen GmbH + Co. KG in Ditzingen, is responsible for development and purchasing. He has been actively involved in the VDW since 2012, initially on the Technical Committee and, since 2015, also on the VDW’s Executive Committee. ■

## Inspirational acclaim for Ridgeway

GROUP RHODES’ CEO, Mark Ridgway OBE, DL has been named as one of the manufacturing sector’s most inspirational figures for 2015. The 2015 list, unveiled at an awards ceremony at the Birmingham Museum and Art Gallery (UK) on 25 November 2015, identifies the sector’s most dynamic leaders and innovators to help inspire others.

Ridgway is CEO of Group Rhodes, a UK group of seven engineering companies serving the aerospace, metalforming, precision engineering, environmental, heavy ceramics and oil and gas markets. He has helped it to become one of the largest original equipment manufacturers in its field.

Under his direction, it has won the prestigious Queen’s Award for Enterprise in the Innovation category in 2010 and, in 2012, achieved the same accolade for International Trade. Earlier in 2015, the company was ranked 29th in the Sunday Times 2015 top 100 SME exporters in the UK. More recently, Group Rhodes won Silver at this year’s EEF/Aldermore Future Manufacturing Awards, in recognition of the strides it has made in finding exciting new ways to work with education providers to promote engineering and manufacturing careers.

Ridgway is the immediate past president of The Manufacturing Technologies Association (MTA) and a council member of the Metalforming Machinery Makers Association (MMMA). In 2015, he was acclaimed as NE England’s Manufacturing Champion by the UK’s Engineering Employers’ Federation (EEF). ■

CECIMO elects new leadership

DURING ITS MEETING on 3 December 2015 in Brussels, the CECIMO General Assembly elected Luigi Galdabini as president of CECIMO for a period of two years. He has been entrusted with the responsibility of leading CECIMO (the European Association of Machine Tool Industries) that represents 39% of global machine tool (MT) production.

“The European MT sector is moving towards the adoption of disruptive technologies such as intelligent production and additive manufacturing. The sector is accelerating the integration of new technologies like big data and the industrial internet into its equipment and services, laying the foundations of the future’s intelligent and connected factories. This evolution is reflected in CECIMO’s activities,” said Luigi Galdabini.

CECIMO urges EU policy-makers to shape supportive framework conditions for the digitalisation of industry and for the development of additive manufacturing technologies to increase the competitiveness of the European industry as a whole. According to Galdabini, innovation continues to be the motor for growth and lack of investment in manufacturing is its most important obstacle.

“Europe does not have the luxury of lagging behind its competitors in disruptive technologies, like additive manufacturing, which transform the economy,” he added. “We have to aim for global leadership. We hope that the new EU Industrial Policy Roadmap and the Digital Single Market Strategy, for example, will give the necessary attention to additive manufacturing.

“I will ensure that manufacturing and re-industrialisation remain on top of economic policy priorities in the EU. We need a growthfriendly business environment for the European machine tool industry worldwide.”

Since 1990, Galdabini has been managing director of Cesare Galdabini Spa. In June 2012, he was elected President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tool and robotics’ association). He is also chairman of AITA, the Italian association involved in additive manufacturing, and vice president of UNIVA as well as a member of the General Council of Confindustria. ■

## Nic Nixon joins coil processing team

THE BRADBURY CO., Inc. has confirmed Nic Nixon as its technical sales engineer for the coil processing and levelling industry. Nic graduated from Kansas State University with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering. Before joining The Bradbury Co., Inc., he worked for a Mid-West plastics equipment manufacturer as an engineer/sales engineer for approximately sixteen years. He has extensive experience in CAD, technical engineering and process engineering.

The companies of The Bradbury Group are leading manufacturers and worldwide suppliers of roll forming and coil processing equipment for a wide range of industries. See http://bradburygroup.com ■
