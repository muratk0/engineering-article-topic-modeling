# We highlight a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing developments

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Servo drives, warm forming, press hardening and hot stamping come under the spotlight in our stamping review”

As manufacturers seek to meet the challenge of forming lighter, stronger and more flexible parts, today’s press manufacturers have evolved production systems to meet these requirements. We highlight here a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing developments as examples......

AIDA group is the second largest press manufacturer in the world with headquarters near Tokyo, Japan. The AIDA group has now opened a new manufacturing facility in Nantong, China (near Shanghai). In addition, AIDA will expand in Indonesia in 2013 and recently opened new offices in Morocco and Russia.

AIDA’s office in Brazil will be expanded by adding additional service and sales people. To strengthen operations in sales and service in Germany, AIDA has also opened a new office in Augsburg. It is also increasing its manufacturing capacity in Europe by expanding the facilities in Italy. A new office building will be added.

AIDA has independently developed compact low-speed, high-torque servo motors for press metalforming applications and, since 2002, has delivered over 1000 servo presses – ranging in capacity from 80 kN ~ 30000 kN – to its customers worldwide. All its products exhibited at EUROBLECH 2012 were equipped with AIDA servo motors and servo control systems. The world’s largest servo transfer press with 30.000kN press force was supplied by AIDA to a North American Tier 1 company. Class A servo blanking lines have also been sold to European OEMs.

“In the fiscal year under review, the fiscal year ended 31 March 2012, we faced the adversity resulting from natural disasters such as the Great East Japan Earthquake and the severe flooding in Thailand. Against this backdrop, however, capital investment in the automotive industry was brisk, particularly in emerging countries driving a 17.9 per cent year-on-year increase in press machine orders for the Japan Forming Machinery Association, of which AIDA is a member,” Kimi Aida, head of the AIDA group, told ISMR.

“As a company that derives two-thirds of its product sales from the automotive industry, this proved to be a favourable situation for the AIDA Engineering Group. Consequently, our orders grew 21.1 per cent, to ¥58.0 billion, exceeding the overall growth rate for the industry. Due to these factors, consolidated net sales increased 27.4 per cent year-on-year, to ¥52.2 billion; operating income jumped 52.0 per cent, to ¥2.2 billion; and net income surged 131.5 per cent, to ¥2.8 billion, making this our second consecutive year of higher sales and income.”

In the fiscal year under review, AIDA developed and commercialised a new series of servo presses for high-precision, multi-step progressive forming processes, whilst taking other steps to bolster its servo press line-up.

“Automobile-related capital investment has been rising in Asian countries, such as Indonesia, Thailand, and India, as well as in Mexico, Brazil and Russia. Accordingly, we will conduct new order-acquisition activities targeting these countries in the future, while continuing our advancement in China, currently the largest market for automotive products,” the company explained.

## FOCUS ON PRESSES

Above: Schuler's Twin Servo press

The number of orders for general-purpose servo press lines rose 20 per cent, accounting for more than half of AIDA’s orders on a monetary basis. Servo press manufacturers are currently locked in a fierce race to develop new products. Amid this intense competition, AIDA believes that strengthening its ability to create forming systems incorporating presses with peripheral equipment will be key to differentiating it from the competition.

AP&T designs, manufactures and markets automation, presses, tools and complete production lines for the sheet metal and tube forming industry. It specialises in forming technology and automated processes, delivering complete production systems where it assumes complete supplier responsibility – a concept which it calls One Responsible Partner.

At EuroBLECH, visitors learned about its latest manufacturing methods and metal forming solutions such as its concept for press hardening and its new generation of automation. The automotive industry's interest in AP&T's press hardening production solutions is continuing to grow at a rapid pace. The most recent delivery is a complete production line for one of Japan's leading manufacturers of body parts, G-TEKT, with operations all over the world.

The new press hardening line will initially be used for G-TEKT's research and product development work, and it will be in operation at the beginning of 2013. The delivery includes a roller hearth furnace and a 1200 metric ton ODEN press, as well as a very flexible automation solution. Generally one or two SpeedFeeders handle the sheet metal, but this line has four individual units that can be positioned and combined in several different ways.

The Bruderer high performance stamping press BSTA 280, combines precision mechanics, modern electronics and trend-setting innovations. The heart of the machine is the short and rigid main shaft that is mounted in a transverse direction.

The hardened and ground spindles of the ram height adjustment are mounted outside the power flow and enable precise setting of the ram during the punching process. As the mechanism does not generate lateral forces transmitted into the ram guides, the guides are placed exclusively in the strip level which prevents deflection of the punches due to eccentric loads in the die.

Chin Fong Machine Industrial Co., Ltd., established since 1948, is the largest maker of mechanical power presses in Taiwan. Its powerful R&D resources, worldwide service and sales net for customer support with an accent on continuous innovation has given it a strong foothold in the global press market. Chin Fong’s product portfolio includes servo presses, straight side presses, C-type presses, transfer presses, automated tandem press lines and heavy stamping presses along with a range of associated automation.

The Chin Fong Group aims to achieve a NTD 10 billion (US$ 330 million) sales target by 2015. Chin Fong’s new single point servo presses (iLS/DLS series) ranging from 80 – 300 tons; its double-point SDS series from 400 up to 1000 tonnes; the compound link LF and KW series for stamping-forging composite processes; the long stroke knuckle joint presses KL and warm-hot forging WF series have facilitated its slogan: “Intelligence of Forming, Vision of Forging”.

Chin Fong now has offices in Taiwan, China, Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and the USA as well as representatives in numerous countries around the world. Chin Fong’s A0 tandem press line for the automotive industry includes stages such as deep drawing, following by trimming, piercing etc. See Product Reviews for more details.

Award-winning engineering firm, Group Rhodes, finalised a £2.2 million order from Long March (China) at the Farnborough 2012 International Air show. The Chinese company purchased a Rhodes 1200 tonne super plastic forming/ diffusion bonding (SPF/DB) press which will be used to produce equipment and components for new civil aircraft. This is the seventh Rhodes SPF/DB press sold into China over the last five years.

The Rhodes SPF/DB press is specifically designed for high temperature forming of titanium and aluminium alloys. The low density and high strength-to-weight ratio of these alloys places them at the forefront of the race to achieve more “green” fuel-efficient aerospace technologies. In 2010 the Company was awarded the Queen’s Award for Enterprise in the Innovation category for its range of SPF/DB machinery, and in 2012 gained the same honour for International Trade.

Other recent contract wins include a £2.5m order from Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (India) for a 10,600-tonne polymer die press. The press weighing over 136 tonnes will produce components for civil and military aircraft.

HAULICK + Roos was at this year’s EuroBLECH 2012 sheet metal exhibition as an innovative specialist for economical precision stamping and forming presses (press forces from 250kN up to 4000kN). It introduced the next generation of its FSD (Flexible Servo Drive) as well as the new ROP series at the exhibition.
