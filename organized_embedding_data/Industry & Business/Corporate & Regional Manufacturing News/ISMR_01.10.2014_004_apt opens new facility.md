# AP&T opens new facility

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

ON 11 SEPTEMBER 2014, press specialist AP&T inaugurated its new development and production facility in Ulricehamn, Sweden. Speakers, customers and guests attended the ceremony, which was followed by two days of seminars on the forming methods and materials of the future. At the same time, AP&T took the opportunity to celebrate the 50th anniversary of its operations.

“We develop and manufacture equipment for sheet metal forming, and the investment is primarily the result of press hardening’s positive development in the automotive industry. The new facility means that production capacity will increase and become more modern, and, most of all, conditions for research and development will be improved. We will have entirely new opportunities to further develop today’s technology and test solutions for the future,” said Per Josefsson, vice president, sales and marketing, AP&T.

The centre will be used to develop, test and demonstrate tools, presses, automation equipment and control systems. It will also be possible to test different methods for finishing work. During the two days of the inauguration event, there were speeches during the ceremonial inauguration by one of the founders, Tord Andersson, the current CEO and managing director Magnus Baarman and Gianfranco Aimo from vehicle manufacturer, Fiat. Guests were also given the opportunity to go on a guided tour of the 11000m2 facility. The day was concluded with a celebratory dinner.
