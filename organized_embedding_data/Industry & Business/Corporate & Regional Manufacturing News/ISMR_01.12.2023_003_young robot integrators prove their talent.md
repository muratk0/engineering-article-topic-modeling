# Young robot integrators prove their talent

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Promising young robot integrators from Germany, Taiwan and Poland were top of the table, according to the results of the international championship in robot systems integration, which took place in Luxembourg from 17-20 October 2022. Eleven teams from as many countries competed and put their robot programming skills to the test for four days. The competition was jointly organised by the educational organisation WorldSkills Luxembourg and the robot manufacturer FANUC, which has been a Global Partner of the organisation since 2018.

The German team prevailed in a neck-and-neck race against strong competitors from Taiwan, who took second place. Third place was secured by the team from Poland. The international championship of young robot integrators is part of the WorldSkills event, which takes place every two years and aims to convince young people all over the world of the benefits of vocational qualifications. In total, more than 1,100 participants from 57 countries and regions took part in 61 competition disciplines at this year's "WorldSkills Competition 2022 Special Edition".

At the WorldSkills Championships, participants were confronted with a practical task from warehousing. They had to programme a robot to operate a miniature conveyor belt at a set frequency, taking different components from a magazine and filling them into containers. At the end of the conveyor belt, the robot removed the filled containers and stacked them on top of each other on a pallet. Winning criteria was not only the speed at which the robot worked, but also low susceptibility to errors in the system.

WorldSkills Europe promotes vocational, technological and service-oriented education and training across Europe, working with young people, educators, governments and industries to help prepare the workforce and talent of today for the jobs of the future. It brings youth, industries and educators together to give young people the chance to compete, experience and learn how to become the best in their skill of choice.

Together with 32 WorldSkills Europe Member countries, WorldSkills Europe organises EuroSkills – the biggest vocational education and skills excellence event in Europe. Held every two years, EuroSkills sees hundreds of young people under the age of 25 from 32 countries come together to compete for the chance to become the "Best in Europe" in their chosen skill.

In 2023, the next European Skills Championship (EuroSkills) is scheduled for 5-9 September 2023 in Gdansk, Poland. The next International WorldSkills championship is scheduled to take place in Lyon, France, from 10-15 September 2024.
