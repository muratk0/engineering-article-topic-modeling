# QUALITY MATTERS

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

BRÜEL & KJÆR is a leading manufacturer and supplier of sound and vibration measurement systems. It helps its customers to measure and manage the quality of sound and vibration in their products and in the environment.

Ulsan Technopark’s Automotive Innovation Parts Center (APIC) provides test services for customers who are mainly Tier 1 and Tier 2 manufacturers of components. Brüel & Kjær’s PULSE platform, LDS shakers, arrays and transducers are all used at the facility for automotive noise vibration harshness (NVH) as well as environmental and material testing.

The Ulsan Technopark (UTP) is jointly owned by the Korean government and the Ulsan City Authority. The facility includes a hemi-anechoic chamber with a cut-off frequency of 125 Hz and a background noise level of less than 20 dBA, four climatic chambers, a crash test facility, a hydraulic road simulator and a multi-axle hydraulic system for durability testing.

UTP currently has two PULSE systems, which were bought four years ago – a 17-channel C-frame and a 34-channel D-frame – and has plans to invest in more. It also has a non-stationary STSF (Spatial Transformation of Sound Fields) array with scanning robot and a circular array that is used for noise source identification and sound power measurements in UTP’s hemi-anechoic chamber.

Other Brüel & Kjær products used at UTP include an impedance tube for material testing, LDS shakers – a V875 for durability/lifecycle testing and a V9 with a climatic chamber for vibration testing (both R&D and durability/lifecycle). Brüel & Kjær microphones, accelerometers and power amplifiers are also used.
