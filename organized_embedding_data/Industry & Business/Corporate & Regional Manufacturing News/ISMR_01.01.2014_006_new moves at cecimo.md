# New moves at CECIMO

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

LUIGI GALDABINI, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots and automation systems, leaves his position as Technical Committee Coordinator of CECIMO and joins the Board as Vice-President of the European Association of Machine Tool Industries, which represents 15 European national associations in the sector and approximately 1500 companies.

His appointment was decided during CECIMO’s General Assembly in Vienna at the beginning of November.

“The globalisation of the market and increasing competition from Asia, mainly from the Chinese, forces European manufacturers to take a wider and much more open outlook. For this reason, in line with what has been done up to now and even more so as a Member of the Board of CECIMO, I will continue to strengthen my collaboration with the representatives of the other countries, for the definition of a policy that safeguards the position of the European capital goods industry and the ‘Made in Europe’ sector,” he told ISMR.

Coordination of CECIMO’s Technical Committee now falls to Giancarlo Losma, President of LOSMA SpA (Curno) and Federmacchine, the National Federation of Associations of Manufacturers of Capital Goods and Ancillary Products for Industrial and Handicraft Manufacturing Processes. ■
