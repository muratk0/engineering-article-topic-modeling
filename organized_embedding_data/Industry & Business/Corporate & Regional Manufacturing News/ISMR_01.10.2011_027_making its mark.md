# MAKING ITS MARK

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

ISMR profiles Coşkunöz Metal Forming Industry Machinery Co. Ltd, the largest company in the Coşkunöz Holding group with a turnover of almost 150 million euros. Core products include hydraulic presses, press brakes, plasma cutting machines, guillotine shears and resistance welding machines.

Coşkunöz is a market leader in tool manufacturing, stamping presses, assembly parts and resistance welding machines in Turkey. Manufacturing for customers such as Renault, Toyota, Mercedes Benz Turk, Skoda, Honda and Ford Otosan, it has built up an impressive client list.

Coşkunöz Holding is one of the leading Turkish industrial groups in the metal, machinery, heating, defence, aerospace and energy sectors with over 60 years of experience in the business. Today, it is active in seven different industry sectors with its 14 companies using a slogan of “Unbounded Forming”. It currently boasts four overseas partners and more than 2500 employees with a turnover of almost 500 million euros.

“Profits are re-invested in the company and through investments made particularly for die and machinery production,” Musa Saydi, Coşkunöz sales and marketing manager, machinery, told ISMR. “The group continues to serve the Turkish automotive industry with the MACOS brand (short for Machinery Coşkunöz).”

## HISTORY

The group’s founder, technical high school teacher M. Kemal Coşkunöz (1921-2000), started production in 1950 for the textile sector with Turkey’s first patented marking machine. The foundations of the holding began with his production of motorized guillotine shears in 1960. In 1968, Coşkunöz moved to its present location on the Bursa Industrial Estate and started production of dies, press, welding machines and body parts for the automotive industry with Turkish Tractor in 1969 and Oyak Renault in 1973 as its first customers.
