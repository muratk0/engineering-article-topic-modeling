# Bradbury appoints mechanical engineering manager

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

THE BRADBURY CO., Inc. has confirmed the appointment of Ben Bigham to mechanical engineering manager. Employed with Bradbury since 1999, Ben previously held the position of project engineering manager.

In his new role, Ben will be responsible for all aspects of mechanical and hydraulic engineering. He will be charged with ensuring on-time delivery of engineered product designs and conformance to the technical specifications of deliverables. Ben graduated from Kansas State University – Salina with a Bachelor of Science in Mechanical Engineering Technology.

“Ben brings to the position an ability to work across departments to ensure that we deliver a technically advanced product that meets our customer expectations,” commented Greg Smith, Sr. Manager, Engineering.
