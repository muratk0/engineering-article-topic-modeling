# AP&T receives 2017 quality innovation award

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

AP&T, IN ULRICEHAMN, Sweden, has received another award for its production solution for the forming of high-strength aluminium. The 2017 Quality Innovation Award was conferred by SIQ, the Swedish Institute for Quality.

"We are very proud that the results of our most recent innovation are being recognized and rewarded by SIQ. Our new technology for forming high-strength aluminium creates true customer benefit, and allows the automotive industry to meet demands for lighter, safer and more energy-efficient cars," said Dr. Christian Koroschetz, CTO, Technology Development, AP&T.

The committee of judges commented: "By adopting a holistic and systematic approach, which combines many different competencies, a new rational technology for complex forming of high-strength aluminium has been developed. The new technology enables radical reduction of the material's thickness and weight, which leads to lower fuel consumption and less environmental impact whilst crashworthiness increases. This combination makes the innovation an important breakthrough in aluminium production technology."

Earlier in 2017, AP&T received the international 2017 Altair Enlighten Award, which recognizes significant weight-saving innovations for the car industry.
