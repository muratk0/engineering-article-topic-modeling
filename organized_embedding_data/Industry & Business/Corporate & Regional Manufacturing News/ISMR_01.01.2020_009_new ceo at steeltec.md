# New CEO at Steeltec

[TARİH: 01.01.2020 ISMR]

EFFECTIVE FROM 1 JANUARY 2020, Dr. Florian Geiger will be taking over as the new CEO of the bright steel producer Steeltec – a company in the SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH Group. After 14 years at the helm, current CEO, Gerd Münch, has decided to step down from the company.

Dr. Geiger worked for many years as a management consultant to numerous international companies, where he specialised in corporate performance. In 2013, he moved to the SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH Group where, as Vice President for Business Development, he was responsible for enterprise-wide strategic projects and for mergers and acquisitions.

Among other achievements, he led the negotiations for the creation of a joint venture in China and for the acquisition of Ascometal in France. He also successfully implemented a range of projects designed to improve transparency and cooperative work practices within the Group.

“Gerd Münch managed the company with both prudence and foresight. He was never afraid to try new paths or to aim high and by so doing he laid the foundation for the positive development that Steeltec has enjoyed," said Dr. Florian Geiger. As Münch's successor, Geiger is looking to consolidate the company's position.

“In today's economically challenging market environment, I want to work closely with our customers to ensure Steeltec's position as the quality leader in the bright steel sector. This can be achieved through a combination of customer focus, excellent service and by concentrating on innovative products manufactured in what are currently Europe's most modern bright steel production facilities," he told ISMR. ■

## Eva Vitell appointed as HYBRIT's MD

HYBRIT DEVELOPMENT AB, which is owned by SSAB, LKAB and Vattenfall, has appointed Eva Vitell as the new managing director. In her role, Eva will drive development work during an intensive period, develop the organisation and ensure that its ambitious schedule for HYBRIT remains on track.

Eva Vitell joins HYBRIT Development from Vattenfall Electricity Distribution, where she is responsible for customers and market. She was previously head of Vattenfall's Swedish wind power development and head of environment Vattenfall's Nordic operations.

"I am really pleased and excited. Having a chance to be involved in revolutionising the steel industry and contributing to the transition to being fossil-free with highly skilled and motivated colleagues is, in every sense, a fantastic opportunity," she told ISMR.

"Eva's sound experience and ability to combine technological development, business development, economic and ecological sustainability will greatly contribute to the development of HYBRIT. Her strong drive, leadership skills and long experience in the energy sector will be a great asset," said Martin Pei, CTO at SSAB and chairman of HYBRIT Development.

Eva Vitell will take up her new position on 1 February 2020. She has an MSc in Business Administration from the Stockholm School of Economics.

Hybrit Development is a joint venture between the steel manufacturer SSAB, the mining company LKAB and the energy company Vattenfall. Its objective is to develop the world's first fossil-free, ore-based steelmaking process. The project started during the spring of 2016 and the goal is to have an industrial process in place by 2035.

The by-product of using fossil-free electricity and hydrogen in steelmaking, instead of coke and coal, will be water instead of carbon dioxide. The initiative has the potential to reduce Sweden's total carbon dioxide emissions by 10 per cent. The HYBRIT initiative has been granted financial support from the Swedish Energy Agency. ## SigmaTEK welcomes Kevin Ramirez as VP of Sales

SIGMATEK SYSTEMS, a specialist in CAD/CAM software solutions for the fabrication industry, has confirmed that Kevin Ramirez will join the leadership team as Vice President of Sales for North America.

“With an extensive manufacturing background, Kevin brings a strong work ethic of more than 20 years of production, cutting machinery and software experience to this new role. During that time, he has served in many capacities including CNC operator, support and service engineer, technical sales management and global OEM management. Having operated, supported and sold fabrication software and machines, Kevin brings a strong understanding of the technical needs of fabricators," said the company.

Kevin will report directly to SigmaTEK CEO, Robbie Payne, and will help the senior leadership team identify opportunities to drive automation and connectivity within manufacturing technologies.

“I am working closely alongside the SigmaTEK Engineering team to ensure our new product developments truly enhance our customers' experience," added Ramirez. “Additionally, we will continue to expand our local presence with strong sales professionals to maintain our high level of service and support to our customers."

SigmaTEK Systems' product suite covers the full range of fabrication workflow; from quoting to shipping, including integration with a wide range of business systems and the ability to program major laser; waterjet; oxyfuel; plasma; punch; router; shear; press brake and tube machines.
