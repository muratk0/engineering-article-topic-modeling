# Sustainable solutions

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

As well as unveiling its digital pay-per-part business model for the TruLaser Center 7030 at EuroBLECH (reported in our September issue), TRUMPF showcased a range of solutions to make sheet-metal fabrication more sustainable at this year's EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover, Germany. It aims to help its customers make their factories more competitive and eco-friendly.

"Our customers have seen huge increases in the cost of production due to higher energy prices. Our technologies reduce the use of energy-intensive materials and are less powerhungry overall. By offering more sustainable and lower-carbon methods of sheet-metal fabrication, we can help companies fight more effectively against climate change and slash the cost of materials and manufacturing," said Stephan Mayer, Chief Executive Officer Machine Tools, TRUMPF.

The solutions at EuroBLECH included its new 'Eco Cooler' system, developed in partnership with Efficient Energy, that controls the temperature of laser-cutting machines using pure water instead of chemical refrigerants. The new solution will be available for TRUMPF laser-cutting machines from the 1000 to 5000 series.

“This novel cooling solution uses 80 percent less energy than conventional systems and is 100 per cent non-polluting," TRUMPF told ISMR.

## 24kW laser cutting

At this year's EuroBLECH, TRUMPF unveiled the upgraded TruLaser 5000 series fitted with a laser providing twice the power that was formerly standard. With an output of 24kW, the new laser - a TruDisk 24001 - can process sheet-metal parts up to three times more quickly. Depending on the material and the application, the upgraded TruLaser 5000 can handle up to 80 per cent more sheets of metal per hour.

"Increased laser power means that companies can slash processing times while also improving the quality of processed parts. With its patented cutting unit, automated functions and dynamic performance, this technology significantly boosts productivity," said TRUMPF product manager, Patrick Schüle.

With double the laser power, the new machine can cut parts significantly faster. In addition, the more powerful TruLaser 5000 can cut mild steel with a sheet thickness of up to 20mm using nitrogen as the cutting gas. This eliminates the need for reworking that arises when oxygen is used.

## TruBend 1000

TRUMPF also launched a new, entry-level press brake for companies entering the metal-bending market. Featuring the new Right Angle (RA) control system, the TruBend 1000 is easy to program, has a user-friendly interface and comes with TRUMPF's tech data for metal bending. The TruBend 1000 Classic is suitable for companies manufacturing simple and semi-complex parts in medium batch sizes. The initial version weighs 225 metric tons and has a bending length of four metres.

"The machines are every bit as robust and reliable as our high-end models but come with a much lower price tag. We will now be providing even more solutions to help companies grow and manufacture competitively," said Stephan Mayer, chief executive officer for machine tools at TRUMPF.

A variety of machine types are on offer, with press forces ranging from 60 to 320 metric tons and bending edges from two to four metres. It can therefore be used to produce a broad spectrum of bending parts.

The Classic model costs around two-thirds less than the TruBend 5000 and is equipped with the clamping system widely used in this customer segment so that customers can use any bending tools they may already have. The Comfort model comes with the TRUMPF clamp and is therefore compatible with TRUMPF bending tools. Users have access to a demo version of TRUMPF's offline programming software, TecZone Bend. This means the machine can be programmed from the office while in full operation, thereby increasing productivity.

## Laser welding

EuroBLECH marked the launch of new BrightLine Scan technology for TRUMPF's TruLaserWeld 5000 laser-welding machine. TRUMPF launched a new solution at EuroBLECH 2022 to improve the quality and robustness of laser welding. Currently, the only way to guide the laser beam during the welding process is via the welding robot but TRUMPF's new BrightLine Scan technology will also allow users to guide the laser beam using the laser scanner. This combination of robot and scanner makes it possible to supplement the forward motion of the robot with a further, freely programmable movement of the laser in any desired direction.

This “oscillating motion" is facilitated by the use of specially developed lightweight mirrors. By enabling scan frequencies in the kilohertz range, this new technique doubles the thickness of sheets that can be processed in heat conduction welding, upping it from three to six millimetres. The new technology allows users to tailor the parts' bonding surfaces – which are melted directly by the laser beam – to suit each particular application. This, said TRUMPF, makes for a more reliable process and results in higher quality parts. The fact that the beam can now also be guided using the scanner also makes it easier to achieve tighter tolerances.

## Smart Factory Consulting

TRUMPF also presented its new consultancy business at the EuroBLECH trade fair. TRUMPF Smart Factory Consulting was founded in September 2022 with the mission of helping sheet-metal fabricators to optimise their workflows and plan their factories and manufacturing operations. The new company, which is run entirely separately from TRUMPF's machinery sales business, focuses on supporting customers with their production processes.

"As well as giving customers solid, specific advice on how to optimise their factory, we also help them put those suggestions into practice – and that's what sets us apart from other consultants. It's often possible to achieve huge leaps in productivity with relatively simple measures. Our input helps sheet-metal fabricators improve their profitability and create a digitally connected manufacturing environment that is fit for the future. Part of our job is to support customers with the introduction of TRUMPF's Oseon software for production control, which ensures perfect coordination between their processes and software components," said Thomas Rupp, managing director of TRUMPF Smart Factory Consulting, which employs some 20 people at its premises in Ditzingen.

## Versatile multi-gripper

TRUMPF also displayed a new and versatile multigripper designed to deliver greater productivity for automated bending processes. It is fitted to the TRUMPF BendMaster robotic arm. Up to 12 grippers can be positioned in this way. Whereas a conventional gripper has to be modified for each new workpiece, all necessary adjustments for the new multigripper can be made at the system level. This reduces the time needed to set up the bending cell.

“The TRUMPF multigripper makes it economic to use automated bending processes even for smaller batch sizes. Any company – even those already well advanced in the area of automation – can increase their efficiency with the multigripper," said TRUMPF. The gripper is available in two variants: small (S) for the transport of workpieces of up to four kilograms in weight, and medium (M) for workpieces of up to 35 kilograms. The gripper is used with the TruBend Cell 5000.

With the M-variant, each of the eight suckers can be adjusted and replaced by a hex key. Minimal expertise is required to perform this operation. As a result, it is much quicker and easier to set up the gripper for a new workpiece. Each of the suckers can be individually activated. This is useful when handling workpieces with complex geometries. The S-variant features a swivel joint that rotates through 180 degrees. This means that the multigripper can turn over workpieces without having to put them down, reducing cycle times.
