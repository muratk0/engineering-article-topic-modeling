# New management appointments confirmed for thyssenkrupp Steel

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Materials producer thyssenkrupp Steel Europe AG has made key personnel decisions for the realignment of the company at an extraordinary Supervisory Board meeting.

Ilse Henne, member of the Executive Board of thyssenkrupp AG and CEO of the Materials Services business segment, has been appointed as new Chairwoman of the Supervisory Board. Knut Giesler, district manager of IG Metall North Rhine-Westphalia, was appointed to the Supervisory Board of thyssenkrupp Steel Europe AG on behalf of the employees and elected Deputy Chairman.

The other vacant positions on the shareholder side will be completed by Detlef Schotten, Andreas Zinke and Sonja Püskens who work in various functions for the thyssenkrupp Group. On the employee side,

Ali Güzel, Chairman of the Works Council at the Duisburg-Hamborn site, succeeds Tekin Nasikkol.

Above from top: Ilse Henne, Dennis Grimm and Marie Jaroni.

In addition, the Supervisory Board of thyssenkrupp Steel Europe AG has confirmed

Dennis Grimm as Spokesman of the Executive Board. Grimm will assume overall operational responsibility as well as the technological management and development of the company. He is also in charge of the technological implementation of the green transformation. In addition, Grimm is also responsible for the newly tailored sales and innovation department.

Dr.-Ing. Marie Jaroni, previously Executive Vice President Steel at thyssenkrupp AG, was appointed to the Executive Board as of 1 October 2024. In a newly created department, she will be responsible for the strategic development as well as for the performanceoriented management of the company towards enhanced competitiveness.

Philipp Conze was confirmed as Chief Financial Officer. He will also continue to head the HR division until a successor is appointed.
