# Thym heads BMW Plant Landshut

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Thomas Thym succeeded Stefan Kasperowski as Head of BMW Group Plant Landshut on 1 December 2023. The 52-year-old has been at Plant Landshut since 2019 in the role of Vice President Purchasing Quality and Production Cockpit and Door Panels. After almost four years at Landshut, Kasperowski will head up high-voltage battery production at Dingolfing and Regensburg.

Thym began his career at the BMW Group in 1997 as a project manager in vehicle assembly at Plant Regensburg. This was followed by roles at the MINI plant in Oxford, England, and at Group headquarters in Munich, Germany.

After assignments as head of the international purchasing office in India, the Munich-born mechanical engineering graduate took over responsibility for the strategic direction of the BMW Group's purchasing and supplier network. Since 2019, Thym has headed both global purchasing and the supplier network for interior components, as well as production of interior parts such as cockpits and door panels at Landshut and Wackersdorf.
