## Gunning for growth

The Prima Industrie Group has over 1,600 employees and manufacturing sites in Italy, Finland, USA and China. In 2015, the Group's consolidated revenues reached 364.5 million euros, representing an annual growth rate (CAGR) in the last six years of 7.8% (fully organic). Consolidated revenues at 31/03/2016 stood at 79,421 thousand euros, an increase of 12.1% compared to the same period in 2015.

The Group continues to generate a significant portion of revenues in the EMEA region, where sales of 47,023 thousand euros (an increase of 1,712 thousand euros compared to 31/03/2015) have been made. In particular, the Group generates good results in Italy (15.3% of consolidated revenues), Germany (9.8% of consolidated revenues), Northern Europe (9.3% of consolidated revenues) and Spain (7.6% of consolidated revenues).

Prima Power's offering of servo-electric solutions for punching, bending and integrated processes is one of the widest in the world, marketed under the slogan "Energy in Efficient Use", and includes advanced electrical solutions in punching technology. It is a specialist in 3D laser machines, widely used in the automotive, aerospace & energy, white and yellow goods sectors. In the 2D laser sector, Prima Power offers a range of advanced machines which are used in a wide variety of fields.

At the recent LAMIERA exhibition in Bologna, it premiered its new Prima Power BCe Smart – a smart panel bending machine designed to increase productivity. The BCe Smart 2220's large table enables loading and unloading in a single sequence. The machine, Prima Power told ISMR, is easy to use and does not require skilled operators. There are no physical barriers for manual loading (lights and an acoustic warning system are employed) and the table's side sections can be lowered.

"The BCe Smart is very flexible and is also ideal for producing small volumes, parts kits and individual parts with a high quality of bending and high repeatability," Prima Power explained.

The group's automation options are also wide and modular: from the automated loading/unloading/stacking phases to sophisticated Flexible Manufacturing Systems (FMS), which typically automate the entire process of fabricating blank sheets into readybent components. Its combination machines include machines for sheet metal treatment using mechanical tools (punching machines, combined punching/shearing systems and combined punching/laser cutting systems).

## An eye on lasers

Prima Power's factory at Collegno (Turin, Italy) develops, manufactures and delivers the laser cutting and welding machines included in TheLaser product line. In this factory, 2D and 3D laser machines, equipped with CO2 or fibre lasers, are produced. Fibre laser machines are now the most popular models (e.g. the Platino fibre laser). The 10,000-square-metre factory employs 363 people. ISMR took a tour through the factory, cleanroom and its R&D areas, to discover its latest laser developments.

"On our new laser cutting fibre machine, Laser Next, customers can cut hot stamped parts. These are traditionally very hard to cut - presses cannot stamp them with a die, you need to use a laser. You need 3-5 laser machines to cut parts from a complete hot stamping line. The machine is designed for this specific application and linear motors, a high precision rapid turntable and granite frame are some of the features enabling the high productivity of this system," commented Claudio Banchi.

"We still see applications for CO2 lasers, particularly for high quality cutting of thicker material gauges or for accurate thick stainless steel cutting. However, for other applications, fibre lasers have gradually taken the place of CO2 lasers. Maintaining fibre laser machines is also easier – there are not as many moving mechanical parts. CO2 machines can be expensive to maintain."

Prima Power offers laser machines across the entire laser spectrum - from standalone, simple machines to integrated, combination systems. Its 2D and 3D laser machine business represents 30% of the total turnover of the group and is a key differentiator for the company.

"We believe in long-term relationships with our partners, and we think that the real product we deliver to our customer is not just the machine itself, but the production capacity that our customer can achieve with our products and technology. The heart of Prima Power service is the common goal we share with our customer: start, maintain and develop the plant's production capacity and maximize it. Our service covers the whole life cycle of the system and technology and contributes to one goal: maximize the productivity and the profit for our customers," concluded Claudio Banchi.
