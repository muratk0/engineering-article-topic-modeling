# New CEO for tk SE AG

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

The Supervisory Board of tk SE AG has finalised the extension of Bernhard Osburg's appointment as member of the Executive Board of thyssenkrupp Steel Europe for another five years until 30 June 2027. With his appointment, Bernhard Osburg would also be CEO as from 1 October 2021; previously, he was Chairman of the Executive Board.

"Our Steel Strategy 20-30 is an ambitious concept which allows us to create an even more valuable product portfolio while optimising the cost structure. By extending Bernhard Osburg's appointment, we underline this ambition and stand by our goal to create a strong, competitive and viable steel company with sustainable products," said Dr. Klaus Keysberg, CFO of thyssenkrupp AG and Chairman of the Supervisory Board of thyssenkrupp Steel Europe AG.

Bernhard Osburg has been Chairman of tkSE's Executive Board since 1 March 2020. He was appointed member of the Executive Board of thyssenkrupp Steel Europe AG in June 2019 with responsibility among other things, for sales and innovation.

## ASME launches Metrix Connect LLC

The American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) has formed a new subsidiary, Metrix Connect LLC, to accelerate digital transformation in engineering for a variety of industries. Metrix, an ASME Company, will host an integrated events and content platform for engineers and technical professionals in additive manufacturing/3D printing, digital engineering, energy transformation and industrial robotics. The company will also act as the exclusive conduit to ASME members via the Mechanical Engineering® brand of media products.

Deborah Holton will lead Metrix as the company's president, reporting to ASME Chief Strategy Officer Michael Johnson. Holton has served as ASME's managing director of industry events since 2018 and will bring her team to the new company, adding several content and technology specialists. Building on their success with ASME industry events, Metrix launches with eight established event brands.

"The way we do business has changed more in the last 18 months than in the last 18 years. The pandemic has accelerated industrial digital transformation and the need for current technical information, resources and relationships to help engineers succeed," said Holton. "Building a network for cross-industry and interdisciplinary collaboration is more important than ever. I'm excited to lead this effort with Metrix," said Deborah Holton.

Before joining ASME, Holton was vice president of the Society of Manufacturing Engineers' (SME) events and industry strategy, and led the team in doubling gross revenue, launching new products and executing strategic partnerships for leading industry events in North America. She built corporate strategies for emerging technologies including AM/3D printing, led industry collaborations (including the U.S. Department of Defence award-winning Aerospace Automation Consortium) and has driven innovation and growth as acting director of technology transition and outreach for America Makes. She received the American Makes Ambassador award in 2019 and was recognized by Crain's Detroit Business in its 2018 list of 'Notable Women in Manufacturing'.
