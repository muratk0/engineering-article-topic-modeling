# A STEADY GRIP

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

Haeger outlined trends and movements in fastener markets to ISMR during Blechexpo

Fastener insertion technology manufacturer, Haeger, has done well in Europe this year and is positive about European and US markets. ISMR caught up with its general manager, Wouter Kleizen, at Blechexpo in Stuttgart where the company was showcasing two new entry-level fastening machines.

“Global business is up 20% over last year. I am very positive right now about European and US markets. We have had to re-adjust to the reduction in volumes coming from the cooldown in BRIC markets so we have renewed investments in more traditional markets. Our focus is on these now,” he told ISMR.

For over 35 years, Haeger has manufactured machines and tooling systems for inserting every brand or type of self-clinching fastener, it told ISMR. It is constantly creating new technologies for inserting self-clinching fasteners into all varieties of materials. In 1983, Haeger, Inc. became part of the Phillips Corporation.

## New entry-level machines

“We have just released two entry-level fastener insertion machines (618MSPe and 824MSPe). Single-part handling technology is now available on the entry-level models and a new feeding system release is also available for customers who are upgrading to these new systems. We have also upgraded our machine shop in California and added new milling and laser equipment, new heat treatment facilities and new grinding systems over the past twelve months,” explained Kleizen.

On Haeger’s entry-level fastener insertion machines, set-up times used to take 10 minutes per tool station – now it takes less than two minutes per tool station, Kleizen told ISMR. The new machines also feature a minitouchscreen control.

“In terms of our technical focus, we are always aiming to be better at what we do. We implement a lot of small improvements on our fastening machines to make them more reliable and faster, with shorter set-up times,” Kleizen explained. “We have also changed the tooling design over the last few years. A new tooling system, automated set-up, pre-aligned tooling and own software, Haeger Insertion Logic, are all examples of this. Customers have also helped to make this process better – they are asking for these changes…..”

Haeger’s recently launched machine model, 824 OneTouch -4 LITE, has been a success for the company. Demand for this machine model has already been higher than its more basic model 824 WindowTouch -4, Kleizen told ISMR. The ability to automatically feed two different self-clinching fasteners, in any possible combination, with two additional self-clinching fasteners in manual feeding means productivity increases of between 20 and 50% over the 824 WindowTouch -4 machine model.

Besides the 824 OneTouch -4 LITE and two new entry-level machines, Haeger also exhibited five more machine models at Blechexpo in Stuttgart.

## Trends and markets

Kleizen sees a variety of trends in global fastening markets – an increasing variety of self-clinching fasteners in any given electronic enclosure and an increasing demand for the use of self-clinching and self-piercing fasteners in the automotive industry.

“We aim to serve our customers better and faster which means an increased focus on service and training. Customers often have new operators and need more training. Often, the products that they are making are more complex. Mistakes can sometimes be made with manual processes such as fastener insertion, especially with more complex products. Mistakes, e.g. missing fasteners etc., are easier to make with more complex products. We try to offer a total fastener insertion solution that makes the process more accurate, productive and efficient for customers,” he explained.

The company just closed its first quarter FY 2015 accounts. Despite a slow start in the US, it is still up 10% over last year and, Kleizen confirmed, is seeing more orders come through from the automotive market. Sales are generally split - 80% to subcontractors and 20% to OEMs.

“From our standard product offering insertion machines, to our build-to-order products and custom-engineered insertion solutions, we can provide customers with a totally unified solution for most insertion challenges. Whether a shop is new to hardware insertion, or inserts millions of fasteners each year, our solutions-oriented approach will ensure that it gets the correct system for its needs,” concluded Kleizen. ■
