# LATEST MEMBER OF VOESTALPINE MANAGEMENT BOARD

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

Voestalpine’s Supervisory Board has selected Dipl.- Ing. Josef Mülner's successor on the Management Board. The head of the Railway System division retired last after 37 years at Voestalpine. His successor, Dr. Franz Kainersdorfer, will bring comprehensive experience in the area of railway systems in his succession as head of the division and on Voestalpine AG’s Management Board.

After the completion of his degree at the Montanuniversität Leoben, Dipl.-Ing. Dr. Franz Kainersdorfer began his professional life as a university assistant at the Institut für Elektrotechnik (Department for Electrical Engineering, Austria). In 1996, he transferred from the marketing department at Veitsch-Radex AG to Voestalpine Schienen GmbH.

Kainersdorfer held a string of leading positions in Controlling and Accounting at Voestalpine Stahl Donawitz GmbH and then at Voestalpine AG. In 2001, he took over the management of company development at Voestalpine Bahnsysteme GmbH.

From 2003 to 2005 and 2008 to 2009, Kainersdorfer acted as Managing Director of Voestalpine Railpro/Niederlande. From 2006 to 2008, he acted as Executive Vice President of VAE Nortrak North America. In 2009, he took over the position of CEO of Voestalpine Stahl Donawitz GmbH & Co KG, where he is responsible for the areas of controlling and finance, purchasing and sales, personnel and IT.

Voestalpine is a globally active group with a number of specialised and flexible companies that produce, process and further develop steel products. The Group is represented by 360 production and sales companies in more than 60 countries.
