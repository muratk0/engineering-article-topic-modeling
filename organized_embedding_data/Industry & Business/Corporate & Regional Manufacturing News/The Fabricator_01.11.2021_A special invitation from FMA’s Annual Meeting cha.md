# A special invitation from FMA’s Annual Meeting chair

[TARİH: 01.11.2021 The Fabricator]

Carlos Mendizabal

VP, Industrias Selbor SA de CV

Chair, FMA Board of Directors and Annual Meeting Committee

Dear Industry Professionals,

I would like to cordially invite you to FMA’s Annual Meeting in Miami on March 1-3, 2022. This world-class conference provides an exclusive opportunity for you to get in-depth knowledge on market trends, build valuable business relationships, and celebrate the industry with friends and colleagues.

FMA staff have prepared a thought-provoking lineup including keynote speaker Sheryl Connelly, manager of global trends and chief futurist for Ford Motor Company. An exciting panel discussion that I will be joining will talk about North American trade and competitiveness. Along with Tiffany Melvin, president of North American Strategy for Competitiveness (NASCO), panelists from the U.S., Canada, and Mexico will discuss the USMCA (the new NAFTA) and present strategies for improving the competitiveness of the North American supply chain.

I’m honored to be chairman of this year’s Annual Meeting, FMA’s premier industry event, and I look forward to seeing you there.

Thank you,

Carlos Mendizabal
