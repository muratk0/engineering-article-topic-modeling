# SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH opens new location in Buenos Aires

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

THE SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH International Sales & Services network is expanding its presence in the South American market by opening a new site in Argentina. Based in Buenos Aires, SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH Argentina will now drive forward the sales of special long steel products in the country. By taking this strategic step, the Group is expanding its sales network to what is already its fourth country in South America. Alongside Argentina, it already has offices, warehouses and services centers in Brazil, Chile and Colombia.

"The full range of tool steel, stainless steel and engineering steel long products from the SCHMOLZ + BICKENBACH Group mills Ascometal, Deutsche Edelstahlwerke, Finkl Steel, Swiss Steel, Steeltec and Ugitech is now available to the Argentinian market. We are appealing primarily to companies from the automotive, plastic, food and beverage, as well as the oil and gas industries. We offer a wealth of very different special long steel products, such as highalloyed or stainless-steel long products," commented the company.
