# Tool and mould-making success

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

WHILST expanding its range of products, tooling and mould-maker Meusburger was able to increase its turnover to 148 million euros in 2012. This translates to an increase in turnover of 14.5 per cent compared to the previous year for the manufacturer of standard parts. According to the management, this result is mainly due to the opening of new markets in the tool and mould-making sector.

In 2013, Meusburger is not only extending its product range but also investing large sums in the extension of its headquarters in Vorarlberg, Austria. The company will invest about eight million euros, not including machinery, in the largest extension building in its history. A new, three-storey factory building will be built in the north of the premises to consolidate Meusburger's central manufacturing and shipping plant. Around 100 new jobs will be provided.

"This extension building is needed because of the ongoing expansion of sales markets and the resulting increase in turnover,” explained Guntram Meusburger, Managing Partner. The project is being implemented and completion of the new building is scheduled for the end of 2013.

“In future, tool and mould-makers may profit from Meusburger’s long experience in producing high-grade standard parts in many other sectors,” added Guntram Meusburger. “Besides consumables and cutting tools, the product range now also includes surface finishing tools.” ■
