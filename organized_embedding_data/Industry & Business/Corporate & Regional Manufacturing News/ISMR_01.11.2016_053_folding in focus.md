# FOLDING IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

The Schröder Group took ISMR through various new innovations on its booth at this year's EuroBLECH in Hanover.

Hans Schröder Group (the affiliated companies of Schröder-Fasti and Hans Schröder Maschinenbau), felt very positive.

"Compared to our successful EuroBLECH presence in 2014, we were able to generate 13 per cent more leads at this year's show," it told ISMR.

The Group took ISMR through various new innovations on its booth at this year's EuroBLECH in Hanover. The leading manufacturer of folding systems presented a newly developed folding machine, PowerBend Industrial, along with other motorised folding machines and a huge selection of shears.

## Folding in focus

A major attraction at the exhibition proved to be the PowerBend Professional. With this flexible folding machine, Schröder Group has developed an entry-level machine for industrial folding, which is also, it told ISMR, quite popular with larger job shops e.g. for façade manufacturing. With its optional Up-and-Down folding beam, this machine is especially suitable for handling large sheets.

"When the profile of a façade part shows a Z-bend, folding from below and above saves a lot of time as sheets do not need to be turned over by the operator. At EuroBLECH, this machine was exhibited with an extra option, suction cups in the gauge table which complement the back gauge. Visitors were especially interested in the process of gauging the sheet against the folding beam and fixing it pneumatically. From that point onwards, all bends on one side can be done fully automatically," explained the company.

## PowerBend Industrial

Schröder Group has further extended this concept by introducing a new exhibition highlight with its PowerBend Industrial. This new machine extends the PowerBend series portfolio. The new model is equipped with options such as an Up-and-Down folding beam and a suction gauge. It is able to bend up to 6mm thick steel sheets and is designed for continuous operation in industrial sheet metal production.

The dual drives on the clamping beam and the folding beam are designed to increase productivity through rapid closing of the clamping- and folding beam. The clamping beam stroke of 650mm leaves room for tall work-pieces.

"The PowerBend Industrial is positioned between the PowerBend series and the high-end machines of our Evolution series," explained the company.

## Intelligent software

Functions such as radius-step-bending for step-by-step precise bending of curves, and the hydraulic tool clamping on the clamping beam for rapid tool changeover, further extend the application possibilities. If required, sheets also can be gauged from the front of the machine. This is a process that a lot of manufacturing companies use when working with slim sheets and profiles.

"To make this working mode more comfortable, the PowerBend Industrial can optionally be equipped with two touch panels at the front and rear of the machine. In the standard version, the PowerBend Industrial comes with the POS 2000 Professional graphic control. Optionally, users can choose the POS 3000 high-performance software control from the Evolution-Series. Both software controls are in-house developments," explained the Schröder Group.

On the booth, the MAK 4 Evolution UD and SPB Evolution UD folding machines were also exhibited with an automatic tool changer and suction gauge for small batch work and frequently changing orders.

## Making an impact

Schröder-Fasti's program-controlled 134 type roll machine, showcased on the booth, uses a special roll concept for single piece- as well as series production. A further exhibition highlight for the group at EuroBLECH 2016 was the PowerShear Professional hydraulic shear.

"For visitors looking to cut steel sheets of up to 6mm, this machine is ideal. A new feature is the pneumatically controlled sheet support and stacking unit. It allows users to unload and stack the cut material at the back of the machine," the company told ISMR.

As an entry model for customers handling large sheets, Schröder exhibited its MHSU 2000 x 3.0 motorised shear. This shear is also available in working lengths of 1000mm, 1250mm, 1500mm, 2500mm and 3200mm. The PDC-NC (Power Duo Cut), a larger and more powerful dual-drive version of the MHSU, was also showcased on the booth in Hanover.

Founded in 1949, Hans Schröder Maschinenbau GmbH combines the traditional and modern aspects of mechanical engineering. Managed as a quality and customer-oriented family company, Hans Schröder Maschinenbau specialises in the development of modern machine concepts for folding and cutting sheet metal.

With the 2006 integration of the Fasti Company and its workshops, the Schröder Group is now a leading provider of folding, cutting, crimping, beading and shearing machines of all kinds. The Schröder Group now employs more than 240 workers at various domestic and international locations.
