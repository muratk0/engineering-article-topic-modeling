# Promotion at Olympic Steel

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Olympic Steel Inc., a U.S. metals service centre, has announced that Brian C. Harkins has been promoted to Vice President - Aluminium. His promotion supports the company's strategic growth efforts in speciality metals. He will continue to report to Andy Markowitz, President—Specialty Metals.

"Brian's extensive knowledge of the aluminium market has been invaluable in growing the business and supporting our commercial team's success. His expertise has also enabled us to strengthen and enhance our supply base, while increasing market share and firmly establishing Olympic Steel as a growing aluminium distributor and supplier," said Mr. Markowitz. "Brian has a clear vision for the future of our aluminium product line - a future focused on intentional, sustainable growth."

Harkins joined the company in 2019 as director of aluminium with more than 20 years of increasing commercial responsibility in the metals industry. He earned a Bachelor's degree from Marquette University in Milwaukee, USA. Founded in 1954, Olympic Steel is a U.S. metals service centre focused on the direct sale of processed carbon, coated and stainless flat-rolled sheet, coil and plate steel, aluminium, tin plate and metal-intensive branded products. Its CTI subsidiary is a leading distributor of steel tubing, bar, pipe, valves and fittings, and a fabricator of value-added parts and components. Headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio, Olympic Steel operates from 35 facilities in North America.
