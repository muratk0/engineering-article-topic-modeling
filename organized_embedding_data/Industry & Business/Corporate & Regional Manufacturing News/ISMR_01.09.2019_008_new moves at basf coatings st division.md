# New moves at BASF Coatings' ST division

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

CHRISTOPHE CAZABEAU, Senior Vice President, has been appointed head of the global business unit Surface Treatment of BASF's Coatings division, which operates under the Chemetall brand. Effective from 1 August 2019, he succeeds Dr. Martin Jung, who led the Chemetall business since 2017. Martin Jung joined BASF's Performance Materials division in Belgium.

Before assuming his current role, Christophe Cazabeau oversaw the post-merger integration project of the Engineering Plastics business of Solvay into the Performance Materials division of BASF. He studied Business Administration at Sciences Po Paris, France, and holds a Master of Science in Engineering.

He joined BASF in 2009 and held various senior positions of increasing responsibility within the group in France, Germany and overseas, including business manager of the BASF Coatings' business in India. He also held the position of Vice President Business Management for the industrial applications segment of the Performance Materials division in EMEA.
