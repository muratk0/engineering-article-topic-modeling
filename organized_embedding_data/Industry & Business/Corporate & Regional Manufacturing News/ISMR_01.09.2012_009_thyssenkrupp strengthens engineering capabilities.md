# ThyssenKrupp strengthens engineering capabilities

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

THYSSENKRUPP UHDE has acquired Energy & Power Global Limited effective from 2 July 2012. Energy & Power is an engineering consultancy with clients worldwide in the oil, gas and energy industries.The acquisition strengthens ThyssenKrupp’s chemical engineering capabilities in the global oil and gas business.The acquisition is part of the strategic development programme adopted by ThyssenKrupp AG in May 2011 and is a further step in the systematic expansion of the Group’s technology business.

“The acquisition of Energy & Power further expands our portfolio in the acquisition and
delivery of engineering projects for the industry and enables us to provide our customers with even greater advantages in the marketplace,” said Dr. Niclas Müller, CEO of the Plant Technology business area of ThyssenKrupp AG. ■
