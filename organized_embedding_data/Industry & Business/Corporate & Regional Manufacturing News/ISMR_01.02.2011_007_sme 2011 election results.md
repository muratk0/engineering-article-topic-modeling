# SME 2011 ELECTION RESULTS

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

The Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME) has announced the election of six new members to its Board of Directors and four new members to its Member Council for the 2011-12 term; one individual was also elected to serve a one-year term as an international director from January 2011 through to December 2011. All were installed on 13 November 2010, at the SME fall Board of Directors Meeting in Dearborn, Michigan. Their terms officially began on 1 January 2011.

## 2011-12 INTERNATIONAL DIRECTORS

■ Warren R. DeVries, PhD, FSME is dean of the College of Engineering and Information Technology at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County.

■ Wayne F. Frost, CMfgE retired as a production system project manager and product quality systems auditor of John Deere Waterloo Works after a successful 37-year career.

■ Laroux K. Gillespie, Dr Eng, FSME, CMfgE, PE retired as quality assurance manager of Honeywell’s Federal Manufacturing & Technologies Division.

■ Thomas R. Kurfess, PhD, FSME, PE is one of the world’s leading experts in the field of microscale manufacturing metrology, with research focused on the design and development of high-precision manufacturing and metrology systems.

■ Mark L. Michalski is director of operations for MKS Instruments, a leading provider of instrumentation for the manufacture of semiconductor and thin-film products.

■ George E. West Jr. is vice president of manufacturing at PACCAR Inc., which designs and builds light, medium and heavy-duty trucks.

## 2011 INTERNATIONAL DIRECTOR

■ James W. Schlusemann is director of global business integration for Navistar Inc.’s Engine Group, one of the world’s largest truck and engine manufacturers.

## 2011-12 MEMBER COUNCIL

■ Timothy D. Bond, CMfgE, is a manufacturing engineer at Boeing Commercial Airplanes on the 787 programme.

■ Helen Greathouse started her own consulting company, Greenville Design, after retiring from Taco Inc. as a mechanical designer.

■ Brock T. Strunk is currently a senior structures engineer at Erickson Air-Crane Helicopter Inc.

■ Robert L. Wolf, LSME, an SME member since 1967, is a professor and coordinator of the Global Manufacturing Systems Engineering Technology BSET programme at the University of Dayton.
