New industry-recognized standards for various job roles available from NIMS

NIMS has announced a new suite of Smart Standards for job roles in the disciplines of Industry 4.0, industrial maintenance technology, dimensional measurements, additive manufacturing, machining, and metal forming. The standards serve as starting points for employers and educators developing or evaluating training in these sectors.

To develop these standards, subject matter experts in the industry wrote, mapped, and agreed upon all the duties that comprise a given role. However, each employer and each educational institution can build its own job roles with any combination of duties, depending on its specific needs. Employers can select from a repository of industry duties that make, for example, a maintenance technician or CNC operator based on how the company assigns duties. Regardless of how employers build job roles, they are standardized because standardization is at the duty level.

E-book from Hypertherm Associates covers standardization in robotic programming

Hypertherm Associates has released an e-book on the standardization of robotic languages from its Robotmaster brand of offline programming software for robots. "Achieving Standardization in Robot Programming," available at marketing.

robotmaster.com/standardized_robot_programming

, features two technologies to address the market demands for a single universal robot programming system.

Despite the industry’s rapid growth, there are various international standards in the field of robotics governing safety requirements, definitions and terminology, performance, and testing methods. However, there currently are no standard requirements regarding the programming languages used in programming.

The e-book addresses the challenges that arise from the lack of standard programming languages, envisions a world addressing these challenges, and explores why the problem exists while highlighting the technologies available in the market that can overcome these challenges.

Galvan Industries wins 2023 AGA Excellence Award

Galvan Industries of Harrisburg, N.C., has received the Excellence in Hot-Dip Galvanizing Award from the American Galvanizers Association (AGA) in the Building & Architecture category for its work on the new Atrium Health parking structure in Charlotte, N.C. It is Galvan’s 13th win in the nationwide competition.

Several column members and connecting support beams, along with most of the cellular beams used in the project, were designed with lengths of more than 62 ft., requiring progressive dipping in the galvanizing kettle. This process requires the steel members to be dipped into the molten zinc once, then removed for cooling. After the beams have cooled, Galvan team members grind the first dip line, then place the steel beams back into the kettle for the second dip, getting the full zinc coverage required for specification from end to end.

Rockford Systems LLC,

Rockford, Ill., has announced a pledge to provide the safety management program at Slippery Rock University in Slippery Rock, Pa., with classroom training in machine safeguarding, course content development, and safeguarded metalworking equipment as an in-kind gift over the next five years.

, Sterling Heights, Mich., has promoted

Bill Shomo

to the position of machine shop manager. As a vertically integrated company, Coe designs and machines the components used in its coil processing equipment on-site. Shomo has been with the company nearly 25 years, most recently serving as assistant manager.

Engineered Material Handling (EMH)

, Valley City, Ohio, has promoted

Ann Branham

to the position of national sales manager. She has worked for the company since 1994, holding positions as sales and customer service associate and inside sales manager. Before joining EMH, she served in the U.S. Navy onboard the USS Puget Sound AD-38 Destroyer Tender.

Global Shop Solutions

, The Woodlands, Texas, a provider of ERP software for manufacturers, has promoted

Trent Maynard

to director of product and engineering. When Maynard joined the company, he provided software implementation and training for new and existing customers. He then transitioned to an operations and technical consultant and business analyst and served most recently as R&D manager.

Welding wire manufacturer

National Standard

has named

Steve Coker

as district sales manager for Oklahoma, Texas, Arkansas, and Louisiana. Coker has a strong background in welding wire and welding product sales. He served most recently as a territory manager for Lampton Welding Supply and has held sales positions with Fastenal Co., Cone Solvent, and Praxair.

Kassow Robots

, a developer of 7-axis robot and industrial cobot systems for machine tending, material handling, and dispensing, has named

Jim Adamski

as its first regional channel partner manager in the U.S. He has more than 30 years of factory automation experience.

The U.S. team, which is set to expand, will be part of Bosch Rexroth, the company’s majority owner, but will work exclusively on Kassow Robots.

KMT Waterjet Systems

, a Shape Technologies Group company in Baxter Springs, Kan., has named

Brendan Shackelford

, current president of Shape Technologies Waterblast Group, as its new president. The company is a global manufacturer of ultrahigh-pressure waterjet pumps and components used in complete waterjet systems.

Shackelford joined Shape Waterblast Group in November 2016 as aftermarket director and assumed the leadership role in January 2018. Before that, he spent 13 years in the U.S. Navy and five years as regional general manager for the Rosemount Measurement business of Emerson Electric. He holds a bachelor’s degree in psychology and an MBA.

National metals service center

Olympic Steel Inc.

has appointed

Jessica L. Burroughs

to the newly created role of vice president for the organization’s Action Stainless & Alloys subsidiary. Burroughs has more than 18 years of metals industry experience in roles encompassing commercial, sales, and marketing functions. She holds a bachelor’s degree in anthropology and psychology from the University of Toledo and an MBA in international banking and finance from Texas A&M International University.

Sightline Commercial Solutions

, a Minneapolis fabricator and global supplier of architectural railing, metal, glazing, and portable platform systems for the commercial, sports venue, live entertainment, and performing arts industries, has appointed

Tiffany Duncan

as vice president of sales.

She has previously held roles of increasing responsibility at Trane Technologies and Johnson Controls. She holds bachelor’s degrees in political science and history from the University of Missouri and a master’s degree in marketing from Webster University.

Farmington, Conn.-based

TRUMPF Inc.

has appointed

Justin Gasparovich

as the head of multiaxis technical services at its Laser Technology Center in Plymouth, Mich. Gasparovich has been with the company for two and a half years and was previously a project and product manager. Before that, he worked as an engineer at a medical equipment manufacturing company. He holds a master’s degree and bachelor’s degree from the University of Michigan, College of Engineering.

Weiler Abrasives

, a provider of abrasives, power brushes, and maintenance products for surface conditioning located in Cresco, Pa., has named

Arjang "AJ" Roshan-Rouz

as CEO. He succeeds Chris Weiler, who remains with the company and serves as executive chairman.

He started his career as a development engineer with Ford Motor Co. and later was a project manager at Bosch USA. He also has led Umicore’s Asia Pacific business and its global business in energy and surface technologies.
