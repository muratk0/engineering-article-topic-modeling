# 50 years for Meusburger

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

MEUSBERGER, an Austrian manufacturer of standard parts for tool and mould-making, is celebrating an important event: 50 years in the business. Continuous investment in enlarging the facilities, expansion of the product ranges and a targeted sales policy have generated a huge increase in Meusburger’s turnover. In 1999, its annual turnover totalled approx. 28 million euros, whereas in 2013 it was over 160 million euros.

After five years of apprenticeship and travel (1957-1962) throughout America, toolmaker Georg Meusburger returned to Austria and established a one-man business in Dornbirn in 1964. In 1980, the company (with its 35 employees) moved to Wolfurt. In 2007, Guntram Meusburger took over management of Meusburger Georg GmbH & Co KG. In 2010, punching products were launched with products for workshop equipment added in 2011. In 2013, the company launched a separate catalogue for ‘Workshop Equipment for Tool and Mould-Making’. ■

The 2D TUBE GEAR 150 has been designed to optimise the cutting of tube sections up to 152.4 mm. It is equipped with an automated material loader and the unique 3-chuck system for maximum accuracy at high speed on long tubes.

Chinese event attracts 47,654 visitors

THE FIRST EMTE-EASTPO 2014 event concluded successfully on 17 July at the Shanghai New International Expo Centre (SNIEC). Some 650 international manufacturers from 24 countries showcased their machinery to buyers from 54 economies.

The four-day exhibition attracted 47,654 visitors. Local visitors from 32 cities made up the majority of the participants. The biggest group of domestic visitors were from southeastern China namely, Anhui, Jiangsu, Shanghai and Zhejiang. Buyers from India, Japan, Korea and Southeast Asia made up the majority of international visitors.

EMTE-EASTPO is a strategic east-west partnership of two established machine tool show owners from Europe and China – CECIMO, the European Association of Machine Tool Industries, and Shanghai EASTPO Culture Development Co., Ltd. The event featured a total of eight country pavilions and national groups from the Czech Republic, France, Germany, Italy, Korea, Spain, Switzerland and Taiwan. The exhibition brought together some of the world’s leading machine tool manufacturers eyeing the vast market opportunities in China.

Several overseas delegations and the EMTE-EASTPO CEO Summit were key activities held in conjunction with the exhibition. Held on 15 July at Kerry Hotel Pudong Shanghai, the summit attracted some 100 participants to learn and exchange ideas with a panel of distinguished speakers led by keynote presenter, Dr Fritz Klocke, professor and director of laboratory for machine tools and production engineering (WZL) of RWTH Aachen University (Germany).

Other activities included a dialogue on Robotics and Metal Processing Machine Tool Technology Integration and Application held on the eve of the show; a summit on the 1st EU-China machine tool industry capital and a technical cooperation summit as well as a series of technology seminars led by the Shanghai Mechanical Engineering Society held during show days.
