# Novelis opens new aluminium heat treatment line

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

NOVELIS, A SPECIALIST in aluminium rolling and recycling, has celebrated the opening of a new aluminium automotive heat treatment line in Nachterstedt, Germany, to serve European and global automotive customers. Located next to its existing rolling mill and the world’s largest aluminium recycling centre, the US$ 85 million investment will increase production capacity by 120,000 metric tons of automotive sheet annually.

At full production, this line will increase its European automotive sheet capacity to 350,000 metric tons per year and will provide global customers with automotive sheet, including the Novelis AdvanzTM portfolio of alloys used in lightweight vehicle structures and body panels. Since 2011, Novelis has invested more than US$ 550 million globally to triple its automotive sheet capacity.

As a leading supplier of aluminium flat rolled products, Novelis partners with automakers such as Jaguar Land Rover, BMW and Ford to supply the next generation of aluminium vehicles. Novelis aluminium can be found in more than 180 different vehicle models in production today.

The technology used in the new Nachterstedt finishing line is identical to that of the lines recently commissioned in the United States and China. With 56 newly created jobs and 1,200 employees in total, the Nachterstedt complex is the largest facility of the nine Novelis plants across Europe and one of the biggest employers in the state of Saxony-Anhalt.
