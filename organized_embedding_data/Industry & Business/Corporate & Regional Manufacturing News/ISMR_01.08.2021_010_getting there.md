## Get to Fieramilano from motorways:

■From the A7-Genova, A1-Bologna and A4-Torino motorways: A50 Tangenziale Ovest towards Varese, Fieramilano exit

From the A4-Venezia motorway: Pero-fieramilano exit.

From the A8-Varese and A9-Como motorways; from the Milano Nord barrier towards the A4-Venezia and fieramilano exit.

Reach Fieramilano from Milan City:

■A8 motorway towards Varese-Como, Fieramilano exit.

A4 motorway towards Torino, Pero-fieramilano exit.

■ Show parking: Use parking areas P2-P3-P4.
