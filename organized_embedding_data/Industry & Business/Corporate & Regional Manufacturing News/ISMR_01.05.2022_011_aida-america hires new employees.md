# AIDA-America hires new employees

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA) recently hired several new employees.

Dawn Reed was recently hired as accounts payable/ payroll specialist. She has over 15 years' experience managing payroll and accounts payable, in addition to extensive involvement in human resources. In Dawn's new role, she will process payroll; maintain payroll records; prepare reports; obtain set-up information for new vendors; process invoices; generate payables checks; field daily vendor inquiries and maintain customer ERP system records.

Krista Matsumoto was recently hired as sales administrator. She has over 20 years of experience working in a multinational office setting, in addition to expertise in sales and translations. Krista will provide administrative support to the AIDA sales group, including preparing customer proposals; fielding inquiries from field sales personnel; coordinating with project managers regarding cost estimating and maintaining documents in a CRM system.

AIDA is a global specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metalforming automation equipment.
