# SCHULER SHOWCASES NEW INNOVATIONS

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

SCHULER PRESENTED several innovations at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover this October, first and foremost TwinServo technology with its new drive concept and compact design.

”Innovations are hardwired into our DNA, and form an important basis for Schuler’s growth,” said CEO Stefan Klebert at EuroBLECH. He continued by observing that this growth course is being spurred on by the newly created post of Chief Technology Officer, held by Joachim Beyer. ”Also, expansion in Asia and especially in China is contributing to a sustainable boost in sales. What is more, our ‘Growing Together’ strategy programme has enabled us to improve efficiency further.” For the recently concluded business year 2011/12, the press manufacturer is expecting to have achieved its forecasted sales growth to approximately 1.2bn euros, up from 958.5m euros the year before.

Schuler presented five of its ten market segments at EuroBLECH, with innovations such as TwinServo technology, the new Crossbar robot for transporting parts within press lines or the Intra Trans transfer solution. The new Smartline SA-S high-speed press is suitable for manufacturing very thin electric motor laminations with an energy-efficient drive. A new feature is the Large Pipes market segment, which Schuler has added to its portfolio through its takeover of the ATIS engineering bureau. It has confirmed orders for over 250 servo presses since the launch. ■
