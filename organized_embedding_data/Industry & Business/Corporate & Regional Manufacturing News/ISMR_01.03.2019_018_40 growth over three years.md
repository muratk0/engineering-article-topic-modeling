# 40% growth over three years

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

PRYOR MARKING TECHNOLOGY has celebrated a busy and successful 2018, which has contributed to the company achieving a ground-breaking 40% growth over the past three years. This has been mainly due to global expansion and new products.

The multi-million pound industrial marking company has focused on key markets over recent years. These have included the setup of a U.S. subsidiary in October 2016 and rebranding a French subsidiary, Mecagrav Industrie, as a full member of the Pryor family.

"We have been trading in the USA for over 40 years and recognise the growth potential in this market. By establishing our own subsidiary, international customers can now experience the same level of service and complete product range as elsewhere in the world," it told ISMR.

New product introductions have also prompted growth during 2018. The biggest growth areas have been in laser marking, where the company has introduced new electronic controllers, enabling new features such as 'on the fly' high speed marking and touchscreen control. In addition, a hand-held laser marking product, developed to support one of Pryor's charity initiatives with the British Heart Foundation, has opened up a new market segment for the company. The Heart of Steel project for the British Heart Foundation is one example of its bespoke solutions. See www.pryormarking.com
