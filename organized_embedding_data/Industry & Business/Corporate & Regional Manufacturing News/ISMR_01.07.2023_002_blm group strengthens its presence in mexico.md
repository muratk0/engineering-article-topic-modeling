# BLM Group strengthens its presence in Mexico

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

BLM Group has reinforced its presence in Mexico, inaugurating its new office in Mexico City in the municipality of Cuautitlan Izcalli. The company has been present in the Central American state since 1992.

The big Mexican-style celebration was attended by Emanuela Colombo, CEO of the Group. The new office, covering 1,200 square metres and staffed by 39 employees, is equipped with a large exhibition area showcasing examples of the company's product portfolio from Lasertube to sheetmetal cutting machines and from tube benders to tube end-forming and wire benders for various production needs.

"We are very happy to inaugurate our new Mexican headquarters," said Alejandro Gómez, General Manager of BLM Group México, at the ribbon-cutting ceremony. “In Cuautitlan Izcalli, we house the offices and the showroom area at one location, so that we can offer complete advice on our products and solutions. In our showroom, customers can see many types of machinery at work and evaluate the quality of different processes."

The company has already installed over 500 units in Mexico, and is present throughout Mexico with sales consultants and a support service. It offers a range of tube and sheetmetal processing solutions and has its headquarters in Italy and a North American base at Novi, Michigan.■
