ISMR: WHICH MARKET REGIONS DO YOU FEEL OFFER THE MOST POTENTIAL FOR GROWTH FOR YOUR PRODUCTS AND WHY?

TK We continue to rely on the automotive industry and its suppliers. Therefore, we are active in all markets where cars are produced in large quantities. Besides Germany, we therefore concentrate on markets such as Poland, the Czech Republic, Russia, Turkey and India. To provide our customers with the best possible service, we use native speakers in sales, design, assembly and service in those countries.

ISMR: WHAT IS YOUR STRATEGIC AND TECHNICAL FOCUS/VISION OVER THE NEXT FEW YEARS? ARE THERE ANY AIMS, OBJECTIVES OR INITIATIVES THAT YOU CAN DISCUSS?

TK Our major focus will be to increase our worldwide activities by acting as a system supplier by increasing our international network. For this purpose, we will cooperate with strategic partners in several countries not only in sales but also in service.

Beside this international orientation on the automotive industry, we will not forget our small and medium-sized customers in other areas of the sheet metal industry. We will keep on developing smart solutions not only for the large OEMs but also for family-owned companies.

ISMR: WHICH TRENDS DO YOU SEE DEVELOPING IN PRESS AUTOMATION AND STAMPING INDUSTRIES AND WHERE DO YOU SEE THE GREATEST OPPORTUNITIES?

TK We see quite large increases in the implementation of components in cars using high tensile strength steel. This increase in the number of parts will result in stronger demand for fully automated production lines for press-hardening. Therefore, we are developing these types of production lines with strategic partners so that we can be a competent partner in this field in the near future.

ISMR: WHO ARE YOUR CUSTOMERS? CAN YOU SEGMENT THEM BY ACTIVITY OR SECTOR?

TK Our customers work where metal parts are needed and produced in large quantities. The majority of our customers operate in the automotive industry, whether as Tier 1 manufacturers or suppliers. Another sector where we have customers is white goods manufacturing, such as washing machines, refrigerators, cookers, dishwashers etc. But we also have customers in the electrical industry as well as forging part manufacturers.

Wherever a company is forming parts made of steel, our equipment will help to reduce the cycle and downtime of its production.

EVENT PREVIEW ISMR previews the FABEX metal forming exhibition in Saudi Arabia.

Fabex Arabia 2012 is one of the largest exhibitions for steel fabrication, tube and pipe, welding and metal forming in the Middle East. Held from 4 – 7 February 2012 at the Jeddah Centre for Forums & Events (JCFE) in Saudi Arabia, the event has gone from strength to strength.

The event features products; machine tools; plant and equipment for sheet metal; pipes; sections; wire and steel structures; dies and moulds; welding; heat treatment; surface treatments and finishing. Fabex Saudi Arabia 2012 will be held concurrently with Metal & Steel Saudi Arabia 2012, the 3rd international exhibition for the metallurgical industry.

## GROWTH IN SAUDI ARABIA

Saudi Arabia represents one of the fastest growing steel industries in the Middle East. Over the past few years, rapid economic development has led to skyrocketing growth in the construction and infrastructure industry, which has boosted steel demand in the country and caught the attention of global steel giants. Saudi Arabian steel consumption has rapidly surged over the past few years on the back of construction boom, growing investment in real estate and cheap and reliable gas / energy supply. Economic growth has also contributed substantially to raise domestic steel consumption by accelerating business activities. These factors have fuelled the consumption of iron and steel in the Kingdom to reach around 14.8 million metric tons in 2009. Steel consumption in the Kingdom may grow 10.5 per cent CAGR during 2010 – 2013.

Saudi Arabia comprises the largest construction market in the Middle East with multi-billion dollar projects under way and many more in the planning stage by public and private sectors.

The world’s largest producer of oil and an important world player in natural gas, with an oil output of nearly 8 million barrels per day and holding gas reserves of up to 230 trillion cubic feet as of 2010, Saudi Arabia continues to be a dominant player in the world oil and gas markets in spite of its efforts to diversify and encourage greater contributions of other sectors to its GDP.
