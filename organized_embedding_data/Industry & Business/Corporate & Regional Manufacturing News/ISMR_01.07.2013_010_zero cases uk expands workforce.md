# ZERO Cases UK expands workforce

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

PROTECTIVE case manufacturer, ZERO Cases UK, is welcoming a new regional sales manager, Peter Skinner, to its team. Responsible for all sales activities within the UK, his aim is to build on the company’s previous successes and the opening of new markets with the help of new products which are currently being developed in the UK and the USA. ZERO Cases is a global manufacturer of fabricated aluminium and moulded plastic cases, enclosures and assemblies for the defence, aerospace, industrial, medical, electronics and telecommunications markets.

Having worked for some of the market leaders in the field of metal manufacturing, Mr Skinner has a successful track record in design-led solution sales and a strong technical knowledge of metal products thanks to years of involvement with European blue chip organisations.

Part of an international expansion, ZERO Cases UK’s American counterpart, ZERO Manufacturing, has also hired a number of new staff. Following Mike Jewell’s appointment as the new president last month, Greg Polcyn and Terry Snider have also joined the US team as regional sales manager and vice president of sales, respectively. In addition, ZERO Cases (UK) will be exhibiting at the DSEI Show in London from 10-13 September 2013.
