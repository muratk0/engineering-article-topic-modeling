# Porsche takes stake in Rimac

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

PORSCHE AG HAS invested in the technology and electric sports car company, Rimac Automobili, by taking a minority shareholding of ten per cent. The company develops and produces electric vehicle components and manufactures electric super sports cars. As part of its electrification efforts, Porsche is seeking a development partnership with the young company.

In 2009, founder Mate Rimac (30) began working from his garage on his vision of producing electric sports cars that could be fast and offer excitement. Rimac recently presented the latest version of its electric hypercar, the "C Two", at the Geneva Motor Show in March 2018. The two-seater produces around 2,000 hp and reaches a top speed of 412 kilometres per hour. It has a range of 650 kilometres (NEDC Cycle) and achieves an 80% battery charge in 30 minutes through a 250kW fast charging system. The company also engineers and manufactures highperformance electric vehicle powertrain systems and battery systems.

The fast-growing Zagreb-based company employs a total of around 400 employees. Rimac's main focus is on high-voltage battery technology, electric powertrains and the development of digital interfaces between man and machine (HMI Development). Rimac also develops and produces e-bikes under its subsidiary Greyp Bikes, which was founded in 2013.

"This partnership now is an important step for Rimac on our way to become a component and system supplier of choice for the industry in electrification, connectivity and the exciting field of Advanced Driver Assistance Systems," explained Rimac's CEO, Mate Rimac. ■

GONVARRI HAS ONCE again ordered the installation of a hybrid blanking line from Fagor Arrasate for its new plant in Slovakia. The plant specialises in aluminium components and will provide a service to vehicle manufacturers, especially Jaguar Land Rover which has built a plant in the country for its production.

Fagor Arrasate has won the order to supply this hybrid blanking line, in competition with other manufacturers in the sector. Fagor Arrasate will now equip the facilities that will open in Slovakia and will supply parts to Jaguar Land Rover for its project in Nitra. The line will essentially be used for aluminium skin and body panels and is also capable of processing steel.

All the presses that Gonvarri will use for stamping parts for the UK vehicle manufacturer will bear the signature of Fagor Arrasate. To this 800-tonne hybrid blanking line for steel and aluminium processing are also added a full servo G1 line and another hybrid line with servo head that were previously ordered from the Guipuzcoan company.

This last line has a double pressure roller and a capacity for processing material of 0.5 to 4.00mm in thickness and a maximum width of 2150mm The equipment is designed to process both steel and aluminium.

"The set of press lines we supplied for the Gonvarri plant in Slovakia enables the company to stamp high-quality parts for vehicle manufacturers wishing to reduce the weight and increase the safety of their cars," concluded Fagor Arrasate.
