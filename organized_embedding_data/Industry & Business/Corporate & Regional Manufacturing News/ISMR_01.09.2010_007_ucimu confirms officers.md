# UCIMU CONFIRMS OFFICERS

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Called upon to re-elect its Association officers for the two-year period 2010-2011, the AGM of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE has confirmed Giancarlo Losma (LOSMA) as President of the Italian association of machine tool, robot and automation manufacturers.

Vice-Presidents were also elected: Massimo Carboniero (OMERA), Luigi Galdabini (GALDABINI) and Riccardo Rosa (ROSA ERMANDO).

The three Vice-Presidents join the management committee that also includes Immediate Past President Alberto Tacchella (TACCHELLA MACCHINE) and Treasurer Pier Luigi Streparava (STREPARAVA), Past President of the association.

Members of the Board of Directors were also appointed:

Domenico Appendino (PRIMA INDUSTRIE); Sergio Bassanetti (D’ANDREA); Mauro Biglia (BIGLIA); Barbara Colombo (FICEP); Stefano Dal Lago (TECNOCUT); Patrizia Ghiringhelli (GHIRINGHELLI); Giulio Maria Giana (GIUSEPPE GIANA); Veronica Just (MILLUTENSIL); Massimo Mattucci (COMAU); Giuliano Radice (CARNAGHI PIETRO) and Giuseppe Sceusi (MARPOSS).

The Board of Directors also includes Past Presidents Ezio Colombo (FICEP); Cesare Manfredi, Flavio Radice (CARNAGHI PIETRO); Bruno Rambaudi, Andrea Riello (RIELLO MACCHINE) and the director emeritus Dante Speroni (SPERONI).

The Director General of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE is Alfredo Mariotti.
