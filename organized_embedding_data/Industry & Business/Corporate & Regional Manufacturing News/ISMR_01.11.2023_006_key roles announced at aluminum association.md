# Key roles announced at Aluminum Association

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

During its annual member meeting recently, the Aluminum (aluminium) Association in the U.S. announced additions to its Board of Directors and the election of a new Chair and Vice Chair of the organisation.

Henry Gordinier, President & CEO of Tri-Arrows Aluminum, was elected as Chair. He follows Buddy Stemple, President for Muscle Shoals and Bowling Green at Constellium, after his two-year term as Chair. Murray Rudisill, Vice President Operations, Reynolds Consumer Products, was also elected as Vice Chair of the association.

In addition, the association welcomed four new members to the Board of Directors: Gaby Poirier, Alcoa; Ryan Roush, JW Aluminum; Dave Hauser, Skana Aluminum; and Jodi Keller, United Scrap Metal.

The association also honoured David Hazelett, President of Hazelett Corporation, as the 2023 recipient of the Marlan T. Boultinghouse Award for service to the North American aluminium industry. Also honoured was John Lapides, Chairman and CEO of United Aluminum, who received the Chairman's Award in recognition of his lifelong commitment to the aluminium industry.
