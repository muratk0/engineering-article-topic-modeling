# Sales growth for TRÜMPF

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

"Breakthrough Solution of the Year"

AK STEEL and The NanoSteel® Company have received the “Breakthrough Solution of the Year" award from Platts Global Metals Awards for the development of advanced automotive sheet that combines high strength and high formability for automotive lightweighting. AK Steel produced NanoSteel's advanced automotive sheet at its facilities.

The companies, who are joint development partners, believe that this third generation advanced high strength steel constitutes a step change in performance over existing advanced high strength steel products. The award was presented in London on 19 May 2016, as part of the global awards programme honouring excellence and accomplishments in the global metals industry.

AT THE END of Fiscal Year 2015/16 (ending 30 June 2016), the TRUMPF Group successfully registered a 3 per cent increase in sales. According to preliminary calculations, the figure amounts to 2.8 billion euros (previous year: 2.7 billion euros). The order intake, at 2.8 billion euros, lay roughly at the level of the previous year for the manufacturing solutions' provider.

"Even though we were unable to keep sales growth as strong as in the previous fiscal year,' said Dr. Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller, President and Chairwoman of the TRUMPF Managing Board, "this result is still satisfactory in view of the global situation." She mentioned the economic slowdown in China and Brazil, the sanctions against Russia and the strength of the Swiss franc.

She added that, over the past fiscal year, TRUMPF had pressed ahead with several targeted investments in the future, including the development of new machines and business models and the structural expansion of locations in Germany and overseas.

In Spain, for example, TRUMPF achieved double-digit sales growth. Growth in France was also in the double digits, regaining prerecession levels. Sales in Germany grew by around 5 per cent, to almost 600 million euros. TRUMPF also achieved significant sales increases in Japan and the Asia-Pacific region.

In China, however, economic growth cooled. According to preliminary calculations, revenues there totalled roughly 360 million euros – a decrease of around 2 per cent over the previous year (369 million euros).

To date, said the company, the recent Brexit decision in the UK had not seriously endangered its business prospects there over the long term. With its sales company (90 employees) in the UK, TRUMPF generates around 55 million euros in annual sales. Since 2008, with its subsidiary SPI (300 employees, around 70 million euros), TRUMPF has also manufactured lasers in the UK that are sold worldwide.

"We have been paying special attention to the market ever since the referendum," said the TRUMPF President, "but demand for our products has so far remained unbroken."

The end of year results for TRUMPF's UK company confirmed £40.8 million revenue, slightly below last year's performance.
