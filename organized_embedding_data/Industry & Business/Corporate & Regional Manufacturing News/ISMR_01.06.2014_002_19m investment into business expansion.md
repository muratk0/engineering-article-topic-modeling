# £1.9m investment into business expansion

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

UK manufacturer A. E. Oscroft & Sons Ltd (AEO), a specialist in presswork, welding, assemblies and prototypes, has announced expansion and investments totalling £1.9m. The company has moved from its 19,000 sq. ft. premises in Redditch to a new brownfield site in the town comprising some 53,000 sq. ft. It has also made substantial investments into sustainable and energy-saving equipment, a new quality centre and toolroom facility as well as introducing additional staff.

“The new site, increased space and improved workflow system were necessary to keep ahead of the pace of the growing needs of our customers and the emergence of additional requirements for manufacturing capacity with the UK marketplace. AEO is currently consulting with existing and potential customers on their requirements for the next five years,” explained AEO director, Chris Oscroft.

To provide the new range of services, A. E. Oscroft has also hired additional members of staff, including tooling engineers, toolmakers, shop floor staff and senior managers, with plans to hire additional staff as required and introduce an apprenticeship programme in the coming months. The company is now able to offer increased capacities and quicker turnaround times to its customers whilst also maintaining overhead costs.

In addition, the firm has installed solar panels on the roof of its new plant with extra space to increase yield. It has also fitted a dual biomass heating system and introduced low-energy lighting within the facility to reduce its carbon footprint and minimise the impact and dependence on fossil fuels.

A. E. Oscroft’s director, Tim Oscroft, confirmed that the company has plans to acquire businesses outside Britain and bring their production back to the UK. It is actively looking for product partners to assist with its product development and is ready for additional business ventures or acquisitions. See www.aeoscroft.co.uk ■
