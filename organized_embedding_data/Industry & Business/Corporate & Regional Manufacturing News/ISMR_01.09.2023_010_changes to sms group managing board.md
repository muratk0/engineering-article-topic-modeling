# Changes to SMS Group Managing Board

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

The SMS Group is implementing a strategic generational change at the top level of management. On 1 October 2023, Jochen Burg (43), currently Executive Vice President Technical Service, will become CEO and Chair of the SMS Managing Board. He will be taking over the position from Burkhard Dahmen, who has been with the company for more than 33 years, twenty of them on the Managing Board and eleven years as its Chair. Jochen Burg has been with SMS since 2008 and has successfully established and expanded the global service business over recent years.

In addition, Fabíola Fernandez (46) will join the company on 10 October 2023 and will assume the role of CFO on the Managing Board on 1 January 2024. She is taking over from Torsten Heising, who has held various management functions at SMS since 2006 and was appointed CFO in 2017. Fabíola Fernandez will join from Gegenbauer SE & Co. KG, a facility management service company where she currently serves as Chief Financial Officer and co-CEO.

Burkhard Dahmen and Torsten Heising will assist the handover until March 2024 to ensure a seamless transition of all projects.

Chair of the Supervisory Board of SMS Group, Edwin Eichler, said: "Burkhard Dahmen and Torsten Heising have made a major contribution to gearing SMS Group towards the future. In view of the good market situation, now is the ideal time to actively shape this generational change and to usher in a new era. Jochen Burg and Fabíola Fernandez are the ideal fit for this. Together, we intend to focus on the systematic expansion of our service business, the further technological development of our plant and equipment, and the optimisation of our performance.

"My sincere thanks go to Burkhard Dahmen and Torsten Heising who have devoted their professional lives to SMS Group. They have played a tremendous role in successfully shaping and growing the company, which is celebrating its 150th anniversary this year. I wish them all the very best for the future."

SMS Group offers future-oriented technologies and service for the global metals industry. It generates worldwide sales of around 3.1 billion euros. Paving the way for a carbon-neutral and sustainable metals industry is the company's stated goal. As a global player with German roots, SMS has 14,400 employees.
