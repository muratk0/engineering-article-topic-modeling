# About Schletter Inc.

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

Schletter Inc. was founded in Tucson, Arizona in 2008 and designs, develops and produces solar mounting systems made of steel and aluminium for sale in North and South America. The company, which employs about 200 people, offers roof-mounted and ground-mounted systems for photovoltaic systems designed for residential and commercial buildings as well as energy suppliers. Its clients are mainly project developers and solar installers.

Its second production location is at Shelby, North Carolina, (now its headquarters). In that location alone, the company handles material weighing 100 tons every day.

Schletter Inc. is an independent subsidiary of Schletter GmbH of Kirchdorf, near Haag in Upper Bavaria, which has subsidiaries in eleven countries worldwide employing over 1300 people.

PRODUCT REVIEWS ISMR REVIEWS THE LATEST INDUSTRY PRODUCTS

Shorter production times, considerably lower material consumption and high quality are the main features of Cloos narrow gap technology, the company told ISMR

CARL CLOOS SCHWEISSTECHNIK GMBH will be showcasing a wide range of manual and automated welding (booth C 04) at the Hannover Messe (13-17 April 2015). The welding specialist will present its new Tandem Weld generation for quick, comfortable welding. Two separate welding wires are fed and melted simultaneously into one molten pool. The benefits of the process include a high deposition rate, high welding speed and low heat input. The new ZMW 950 Tandem torch is equipped with a powerful coolant integrated in the torch neck and integrated gas nozzle sensor cables. This results in a higher deposition rate, longer service life and simple maintenance of the torch.

“With our Cold Weld process, an alternating current produces a very special pulse form for very low heat input. Due to this type of heat-reduced arc welding, the welding process can be optimally controlled. The increased deposition rate can either be used for more filling volume or higher welding speed. The material is only subjected to minimum heat and the original material properties remain, to a large extent, unchanged,” added Cloos.

Cloos will also be showcasing laser welding on its booth in Hanover. The laser MIG/MAG Hybrid process is the combination of a laser beam with a MIG/MAG welding process in one common process zone, using the benefits of both processes. Considerable savings in production time and filler material are possible. Full-depth welds can be welded without preparation. High weld speeds can be reached when made thin or thick plates.

For thick plate welding, its MIG/MAG Narrow gap technology offers efficiencies for welding thick-walled component parts. The basis of the narrow gap technology is the rectangular narrow gap blade, with lengths of up to 400mm, which guides the wire electrode, the shielding gas and the cooling water. No extensive weld preparation is needed and the quantity of filler material and shielded gas can be reduced.

Another highlight will be the new fully automated current tip and gas nozzle changer, SpareMatic, which enables non-stop welding with operative components. When the welding robot needs new nozzles, the automatic current tip and gas nozzle changer takes the torch consumables and replaces them for new ones. It can be integrated into new and existing systems.

Cloos will also present its new small and dynamic QRH-280 welding robot at Hannover Messe. The robot is equipped with a new tactile gas nozzle sensor which works with a much higher tracking speed. See www.cloos.de ■
