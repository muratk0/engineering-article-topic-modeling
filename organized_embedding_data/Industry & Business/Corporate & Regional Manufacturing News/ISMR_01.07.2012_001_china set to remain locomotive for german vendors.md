# China set to remain locomotive for German vendors

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

WITH A volume of 2.3 billion euros, China is the biggest export market for Germany’s machine tool industry – reason enough for the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders’ Association) to organise three technology symposia in the Middle Kingdom itself.

“Chinese demand for German machine tools has grown so steeply in recent years that we have to seize every opportunity to show the flag in this market on a broad front, so as to maintain and upgrade our fiercely contested position,” emphasised Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, Executive Director of the VDW.

From 14-18 May 2012, fifteen key German machine tool companies specialising in metal-cutting, forming and control technologies showcased their corporate capabilities at Chongqing, Wuhan and Guangzhou. Chinese branch managers of German machine tool manufacturers also attended the symposia. More than 60 German firms are already active in China with sales, service support, purchasing, assembly and production.

Around 770 Chinese customers attended the three days of presentations spotlighting new machines, solutions and services for the Chinese market. They focused primarily on innovative high-tech complete-system solutions, meeting tough stipulations for productivity, flexibility, speed and precision.

“China will continue to be the locomotive for our sector in the future as well,” predicts a confident Wilfried Schäfer. With a share of 37 per cent in global machine tool consumption, 30 per cent of German exports and 36 per cent of German order bookings already, China as a customer is playing a dominant role in many fields and the trend is gathering momentum. The evolution of China’s industrial sector away from cheap-goods producers to manufacturers of top-quality products able to compete on the global markets is boosting demand for sophisticated production technology. ■

## Four F&S predictions

FROST & SULLIVAN has released the results from its customer survey with several hundred companies conducted in December 2011 to discover the top predictions for 2012 for the global automotive and transportation market. Industry participants expect that the auto market in 2012 will grow in all regions of the world by 5-10 per cent except Europe, where it is estimated to decline by 7-8 per cent. It is also expected that increasing fuel prices will accelerate the introduction of plug-in hybrid and battery electric vehicles. In 2012 alone, the industry will witness about 15 new electric vehicle launches in the global market.

“Mobility and integrated transportation are going to be the key trends defining the long term strategy and outlook of the automotive industry. New business models such as car sharing and integrated urban mobility as well as electric vehicles will create new business opportunities,” F&S told ISMR.

“From a technological point of view, integration of the smartphone will also help bring seamless connectivity inside cars, driving the next gen. connected infotainment services and auto-centric application stores. The convergence between automotive and the electronics, information and communication technologies as well as energy markets is also highly significant and relates primarily to the emergence of the smartphone, electric vehicles and e-mobility driving innovation in connectivity, batteries, energy storage, transmission and distribution infrastructure, battery charging and integration of mobility into the smart home.” ■

## Press hardening of chassis parts

USING A method developed by AP&T, TianJin Shun Da Automobile, a Chinese manufacturer of vehicle components, has begun using press hardening in its production of chassis parts for cars.

“This is both a commercial breakthrough for our technology and a strategically important transaction,” said Johan Friberg, Senior Advisor at AP&T. A press hardening line for manufacturing chassis parts was delivered to Shun Da’s factory in TianJin in November 2011. Production is expected to be in full swing in 2012. Thanks to the possibility of combining high strength with low weight, press hardening has become an increasingly common method for manufacturing car body parts but this is the first time the technology has been used commercially to produce chassis parts in China, says AP&T.

“This particular production line will be used to produce torsion bars for rear axles, but many other chassis parts could also be manufactured using similar methods,” explained Johan Friberg. AP&T’s method entails a tube-shaped blank being heated up to 900-930 degrees, being formed and then quickly cooled off to achieve the right degree of hardening and structure. Cooling takes 10 seconds and is done right in the forming tool. The process is optimised to ensure that the different parts of the torsion bar obtain the right qualities; some should be hard and strong while others need to be flexible. The high level of precision obtained during manufacturing also ensures that tolerances do not pose any difficulties.

The production line ordered by Shun Da includes, amongst other things, a six-axis industrial robot to load the blanks, a furnace with a palette system, a press and a tool. The tool in particular represents advanced engineering with its integrated cooling channels for quick and exact cooling. See www.aptgroup.com

## BIEMH in focus

THE 27TH BIEMH International machine tool exhibition closed with positive ratings from participants and over 34,000 trade visitors to Spain’s biggest industrial trade show.

“The 2012 event considerably exceeded expectations (coloured by the current market situation) in terms of the amount of business done, and a significant number of major deals were concluded,” the organiser told ISMR. Overall, visitor numbers from outside Spain were up 9% on the previous show. Visitors from 56 countries attended the event.

BIEMH took place over six days at Bilbao Exhibition Centre. In the words of the Centre’s CEO, José Miguel Corres, there was not only a significant presence of EUbased purchasers but also a large number of specialists from other countries whose markets are among the main destinations of today’s machine tool exports, such as China, Turkey, Ukraine, India, Mexico, Brazil, Algeria, Morocco, Russia, Egypt, Colombia, the Dominican Republic and Venezuela. The sectors rated as most interesting by trade visitors were metal cutting machines, tools and accessories, metal forming machines and automation of production systems.

The next BIEMH event will take place on 2-7 June 2014. ■
