# Dallan celebrates 40 years

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

DALLAN WELCOMED A multitude of guests for its 40th anniversary celebrations at its headquarters in the Veneto region of Italy on 14 March 2018. Celebrating the continuous development of the company, with presentations from its founders, Sergio Dallan and Maria Gomierato, the coil processing specialist also announced the February 2018 opening of a new office in the U.S. The new Dallan America Corp. will operate initially as a sales office, with technical and spare parts service to follow.

"In 40 years, Dallan has grown from being a small technical office to a company of over 150 employees, with a 32 million-euro turnover, more than 450 active customers and over 3000 machines installed worldwide. The past year has brought important results, from the point of view of productivity and development of new technologies, and in terms of new orders for 2018. We are, in fact, already taking new orders for 2019. This is thanks to the efforts of our team, as well as of Dallan agents and historical suppliers who have stood by us and grown together with us," commented Ing. Andrea Dallan, the company's CEO.

"In the past year, we achieved very good results particularly in Europe, North Africa, Argentina and Australia. We work directly in over 80 countries and are consolidating our presence in historical markets, opening new ones and strengthening our relationships with existing customers. We stay focused on the production of roll forming machines and specialised automatic production systems (such as machines for rolling shutters and Venetian Blind aluminium profiles with the Dallan Coil-To-Window system). To maintain this position, continuous investment, effort and innovation are necessary. We have also added laser cutting systems to our range."

Dallan is working to optimise internal working procedures such as material storage. This will culminate in investment for the completion of the Dallan Sistemi building, increasing its working area by 3,500 square metres.

"The international market still covers about 80% of our turnover. I strongly believe in company specialisation and focus, as well as in the talent of our people. I have always believed in research and innovation, which is why every year we register about 12 new patents. Over the last five years, we have registered over 60 patents and utility models, concluded Sergio Dallan, company founder.
