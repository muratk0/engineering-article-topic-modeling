# BRADBURY GROUP EXPANSION

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

The Bradbury Co., Inc., acting through its subsidiary, Bradbury Australia, Pty Ltd, has announced its further expansion into the southern hemisphere. The new company has entered into an agreement to acquire the assets and intellectual property of Hilleng International and Diecraft. The deal is expected to close in the near future. Hilleng International is a leading manufacturer of sandwich panel laminating machinery and systems. The business has been established for more than 35 years, successfully installing over 250 panel manufacturing systems worldwide. Diecraft has been established for more than 25 years and is a leading supplier of rollforming equipment to the rainwater goods market.

“We are pleased to welcome Hilleng International and Diecraft to our family of companies. For the past number of years, we have known and competed with this talented group of people and the quality products they provide. With Martin Cannon and Graeme Clark coming on board as senior managers of Bradbury Australia, the systems they produce will complement our global strategy in the key markets and geographic regions we have targeted for growth,” remarked David Cox, President and Chief Operating Officer of The Bradbury Group.

The companies of The Bradbury Group are leading manufacturers and worldwide suppliers of rollforming equipment and integrated systems designed for metalforming and coil processing applications in a wide range of industries. See www.bradburygroup.com
