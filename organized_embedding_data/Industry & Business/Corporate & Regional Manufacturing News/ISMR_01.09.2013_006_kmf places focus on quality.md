# KMF places focus on quality

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

KMF (Precision Sheet Metal) has appointed Gary Sutton as the company’s UK quality manager. With 15 years’ experience of working within KMF, latterly as a continuous improvement engineer, Gary Sutton is well versed with the company, its customers and procedures. In his previous role, he was tasked with driving the improvement culture within KMF. Among the tasks he was asked to undertake was a six-month secondment to assist in the set-up of the quality system for the company’s manufacturing facility in Slovakia. This involved writing the quality management system and ensuring accreditation to ISO9001.

Upon completion of this project, Gary moved across to the quality department as a quality engineer, before taking up his current role as UK quality manager. His new position will involve him working across all of KMF’s manufacturing facilities, ensuring that the company’s processes are robust and delivering to customers’ expectations. ■
