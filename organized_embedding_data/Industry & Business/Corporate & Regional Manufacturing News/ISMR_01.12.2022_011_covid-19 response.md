# COVID-19 response

[TARİH: 01.01.2022 ISMR]

As the COVID-19 pandemic hit supply chains, ARKU (along with other manufacturers globally) struggled to find available materials and parts.

"Lockdowns and uncertainty in the market in 2020 hit our sales, as customers stopped investing, but from mid-2020, our sales began to recover. The pandemic also changed our working habits as we started using online meeting resources such as MS Teams. It was good to have our local subsidiaries in the U.S. and China so that we could carry out worldwide service during the travel restrictions. In addition, we strengthened our local service partners in other countries who had to work more independently and manage installations with the online support of our service technicians. This, of course, meant providing more training," explained Andreas Hellriegel.

"The pandemic has also streamlined our operations to a degree, without the need to have as many meetings, or travel as much, and relying on our local partners. However, it also caused difficulties as processes were not as efficient as they were normally, trade shows were cancelled etc. In the absence of trade shows, we decided to offer online machine demonstrations, seminars and webinars, along with in-house events when safe to do so. We have learned to plan more around future uncertainty, as a result, and cope with lastminute changes," he added.

## ARKU at Blechexpo 2021

ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH presented its new EdgeBreaker® 6000 deburring machine at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart (October 2021).

"In addition to laser-cut parts, the deburring machine can also process punched parts, regardless of whether they are small or large. If required, the EdgeBreaker® 6000 can handle material thicknesses far beyond the thin sheet range. The machine also covers all processing requirements: deburring, edge rounding and surface finishing, from individual parts to large quantities - the passage width of 1300mm turns processing dreams into reality." it told ISMR.

"In addition to the rotary brushes are a grinding belt and a finishing unit. These three units make the EdgeBreaker® 6000 the most universal deburring machine in the EdgeBreaker® line-up. A quick-change system for the consumables adds flexibility and reduces set-up times," it added.
