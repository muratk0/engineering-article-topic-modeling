# North American sales and service support for KOHLER

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

Wintersteiger, Inc. will provide full North American sales and support for KOHLER Machinenbau GmbH part levelling machines and strip feeding lines for steel service centres, mechanical engineering firms and the automotive industry. Kohler Maschinenbau GmbH is a leading manufacturer of metal processing machinery based in Germany, owned by Wintersteiger AG.

Under this new partnership, Wintersteiger Inc. in Salt Lake City, will provide a comprehensive range of services for KOHLER machinery (including sales, installation, maintenance, repairs and spare parts). This support will be available to all KOHLER customers throughout the United States and Canada.

The partnership with Wintersteiger, Inc. will allow KOHLER to expand its customer base in North America and provide its customers with sales and service support. It will also enable Wintersteiger to strengthen its position as a provider of services for the metalworking industry.

Wintersteiger AG is an international machinery and plant engineering group providing solutions for markets such as snowsports; agriculture; woodworking; metalworking and plant automation. It has provided products and services to customers worldwide since 1953. Wintersteiger, Inc. has been providing products and services to customers in North America for over 20 years.■
