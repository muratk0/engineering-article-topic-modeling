# Moody becomes MD of Bradbury International UK

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

THE BRADBURY GROUP has announced the promotion of Matthew J. Moody to managing director of Bradbury International UK Ltd.

"Matthew has played a fundamental role in the international success of The Bradbury Group of companies. With his focus on new equipment sales and commitment to customer service, he has led our market growth in the United Kingdom and the broader, European market," commented The Bradbury Group.

He began his journey in the roll forming industry in 1997 working for Bronx Engineering, completing a modern apprenticeship during his tenure. He joined Bradbury in 2004 in applications/sales, developing many positive customer relationships. Matthew was promoted to general manager in 2015. In his new role as managing director, he will be responsible for leading the Bradbury International UK business including all customer-facing activities representing The Bradbury Group product lines as well as engineering and service supplied by the UK office to customers and Group companies.

Headquartered in Moundridge, (USA), the companies of The Bradbury Group are manufacturers and worldwide suppliers of roll forming equipment and integrated systems designed for metal forming and coil processing applications in a wide range of industries. Bradbury's product lines include custom designed roll formers; precision rollerlevellers; cut-to-length and slitting lines; insulated panel lines and flexible manufacturing systems.
