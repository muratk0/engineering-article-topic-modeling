# IINVESTING IN QUALITY

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

As a family concern with a long term strategic outlook, GYS then re-invests profits back into the business

SMR SAYS: “GYS, with the largest inverter welding factory in Western Europe, employs a strategy of continuous investment in quality”

ISMR visited the GYS factory in Laval to see its latest welding innovations

In 1997, Nicolas Bouygues acquired GYS, then employing just 40 people. In less than 10 years, through continuous investment in R&D and manufacturing, it grew by a factor of ten. Today, GYS is one of the world’s largest manufacturers of welding machines, battery chargers and car body repair equipment.

The award-winning company, already a recognised brand in Europe with the largest inverter welding factory in Western Europe and a 58 million euro turnover in 2012, employs 500 people (40 of them in R&D) and distributes products to more than ninety countries from its main factory in France and secondary factory in China. It has also opened sales subsidiaries in Germany, UK, India and China. It is an impressive achievement over ten years from what was, essentially, a small inverter manufacturer.
