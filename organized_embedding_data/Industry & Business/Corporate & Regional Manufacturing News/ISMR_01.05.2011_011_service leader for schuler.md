# SERVICE LEADER FOR SCHULER

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

AS OF 1 APRIL 2011, Holger Garbrecht (51) took over global management of the Schuler Group’s central technology field “Service”. At the same time, Mr. Garbrecht was appointed as a Managing director of Schuler Pressen GmbH.

Holger Garbrecht is a sales and service expert with many years of experience in the German and international engineering sector. The business graduate previously worked as a self-employed business consultant for international clients with a special focus on sales and service. Before this, he was President and CEO of KBA North America, Inc., where he was responsible for the North American activities of the printing machine manufacturer, Koenig & Bauer AG.

Before his time in the USA, the qualified manager in wholesale and international trade gathered over ten years of experience with Heidelberger Druckmaschinen AG. After starting in sales, he held various management positions there in marketing, sales and service.

“As a market leader in metalforming technology, Schuler’s global service quality will become an increasingly important differentiation feature,” says Stefan Klebert, CEO of Schuler AG. “With his modern understanding of service and many years of international experience in engineering, Holger Garbrecht will significantly strengthen our team.”

In its “Service” technology field, Schuler pools central services such as the maintenance and modernisation of installed presses or consulting and training of its customers. The division is to be expanded globally as part of the company’s current strategic realignment.

Schuler Pressen GmbH, whose executive board Holger Garbrecht has joined, was created by the merger of the Schuler Group’s two largest subsidiaries, Schuler Pressen GmbH & Co. KG in Göppingen and Müller Weingarten AG in Weingarten, Erfurt and Remscheid.

Schuler supplies machines, production lines, dies, process expertise and services for the entire metal-working industry.
