# New strategic management team at Blackbird

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH, a manufacturer of system solutions for remote laser welding, is growing its leadership team and creating a new area for business field development. Benjamin Bopp is now in charge of sales from April 2024.

The company, based at Garching near Munich (Germany), is repositioning itself to meet the growing requirements of its diverse customer base and to focus more on general future trends. It is adding a new business development department to its organisation. Thibault Bautze-Scherff (39), the previous sales director, took over as head of the new unit on 1 April 2024. Together with his team, he will focus on future technologies, previously unexplored areas of application for laser processing and the expansion of partnerships with research institutions and associations to support the company's medium-term growth.

“Lasers as tools are viewed as one of the key production methods of the future, especially in conjunction with concepts such as artificial intelligence and smart manufacturing," explained Thibault Bautze-Scherff.

Benjamin Bopp (43) is stepping in to fill the gap in the role of global sales manager from April. From his previous work as an application engineer specialising in laser welding, Bopp brings a wealth of professional expertise in technical consultancy. He has over 15 years of experience in international sales from his former roles.■
