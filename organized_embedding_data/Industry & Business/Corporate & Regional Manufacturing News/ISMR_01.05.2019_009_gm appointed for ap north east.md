# GM appointed for A&P North East

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

A&P NORTH EAST (in the UK) has created a new dedicated Fabrication division and appointed a general manager to lead it, as part of its commitment to opening up new markets and leveraging in-house fabrication expertise. Headquartered at A&P Tyne, the new division will be led by Geoff Harmer who joins from Newcastle-based manufacturing business Pryme Group, where he was the managing director.

David McGinley, managing director of Atlantic and Peninsula Marine Services Ltd, said: "Over the last two years, we've invested heavily in the systems, infrastructure and people required to show A&P as a vibrant, forward-thinking company and to re-educate the market about what an engineering business like ours can do.

"The creation of a stand-alone Fabrication division allows us to give this market the dedicated and strategic focus needed to build our presence and client base. There is no other business with our physical size, load-out capability, man-power or specialist engineering expertise in the North East [of England] and certainly no other fabricating blocks for submarines and aircraft carriers. Under Geoff's leadership, the new division will tell that story and leverage the skills, resource and facilities we have at our Hebburn and Tees facilities."

Geoff has more than 40 years' experience in the fabrication industry and has worked in senior management roles in some of the largest fabrication and welding businesses in the North East of England. Geoff's industry experience also includes establishing his own specialist welding and fabrication business for the oil and gas and power generation sectors and he is a time-served metalwork welder.

A&P Group operates seven dry docks across four strategic locations in the UK and delivers global engineering to the commercial shipping, marine, offshore wind, oil and gas, civil nuclear and defence industries. A&P Group is part of Atlantic & Peninsula Marine Services Ltd.■
