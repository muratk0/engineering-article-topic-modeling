# Marcegaglia wins steel excellence awards

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

For the "2020 Global Awards for Steel Excellence", sponsored by Fastmarkets, Marcegaglia Ravenna entered with five applications in three different categories, for two innovative projects, Tensil Pro and Smart Line. The first was conceived and developed entirely by materials specialist Marcegaglia Ravenna, the second is the result of a collaboration with Fives. The awards ceremony took place on 24 July 2020 in a digital forum from New York.

Marcegaglia Ravenna won for "Best Operational Improvements" whilst both Fives and Marcegaglia took the award for "Information Technology Services".

“These awards are the fruits of the strategic role that R&D has taken in the Marcegaglia Group in recent years, focused on the continuous improvement of processes and the development of innovative solutions. For five years, an R&D structure has been implemented in the Ravenna plant, based on skills from bodies of excellence, which supports process engineering for production improvement and researches product solutions which are customised to user needs,” Marcegaglia told ISMR.

Tensil Pro is an online detection system for mechanical characteristics of materials. It was created to improve product quality and process efficiency. Tensil Pro can be applied to all metallic materials as well as magnetic and non-magnetic steels, aluminium or copper alloys, starting from the hot rolling process.

Smart Line, on the other hand, came out of the collaboration between Marcegaglia and Fives. Smart Line integrates all the coil transformation production processes from hot rolling up to the finished product.

Tensil Pro and Smart Line are designed to improve production costs using energy saving and productivity increases to obtain greater homogeneity of mechanical characteristics (in particular for the automotive sector), to study quality and to process trends as input material is variable (to offset the differences between different suppliers/steel-makers).
