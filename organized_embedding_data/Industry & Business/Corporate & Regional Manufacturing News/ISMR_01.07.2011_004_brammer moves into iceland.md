# BRAMMER MOVES INTO ICELAND

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

Michael Wallwork leads the Brammer Island ehf team

Brammer UK, part of Europe’s leading supplier of maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO) products and services, has confirmed its commitment to its growing customer base in Iceland with the formation of a dedicated Icelandic subsidiary.

Based in Reykjavik, Brammer Island ehf is geared to supporting Brammer’s existing Icelandic customers in the metals sector as well as developing new business at other large manufacturing facilities.

The Brammer Island ehf team is led by Michael Wallwork who, before joining Brammer in early 2010, spent more than 15 years in various senior management roles in engineering. With a Masters and Bachelors degree in Business Management and Aerospace Engineering, Michael held general management positions in Canada, Germany and the UK before relocating to Iceland.

The team will support Brammer’s existing contract with the Alcoa Aluminium Smelter on Iceland’s east coast. This relationship has recently been extended following the announcement of Brammer’s new European contract with Alcoa Inc. Brammer is also providing a range of MRO products and on-site services to Rio Tinto Alcan’s aluminium smelting facility near to Reykjavik, complementing Brammer’s existing UK and Europe-wide contracts with this customer.
