# Delpino returns to Metal Craft Industries UK

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

In a strategic move, Metal Craft Industries UK Ltd has announced a major appointment to its management team. Albert Delpino has returned to Metal Craft Industries as its production manager. His journey with Metal Craft Industries began at the age of 16 as a trainee sheet metal worker. Now, after 30 years of traversing different facets of the industry, Delpino returns to the company where he kick-started his career.

"Albert's extensive experience covers sales; finance; purchasing; programming lasers; CAD design and man management, making him a versatile and invaluable addition to the leadership team," commented the manufacturer.

Metal Craft Industries UK Ltd specialises in stainless steel fabrication for the catering, laboratory and storage industries. Delpino's vision for Metal Craft Industries involves leveraging his in-depth knowledge of production and CNC processes to efficiently operate the manufacturing environment. He emphasised the importance of building a team that shares his ambition and dedication.

"Albert's return to Metal Craft Industries signifies not just a reunion, but the convergence of expertise and ambition. His diverse background in the industry aligns seamlessly with our commitment to delivering top-notch products across various sectors. We are thrilled to have him back, and I have full confidence that his leadership will play a pivotal role in shaping the future success of Metal Craft Industries," said Reg Larkin, Managing Director, Metal Craft Industries.■
