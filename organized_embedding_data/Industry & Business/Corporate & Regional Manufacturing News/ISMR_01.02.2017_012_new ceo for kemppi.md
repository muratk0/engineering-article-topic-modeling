# New CEO for Kemppi

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

THE CEO OF Kemppi Oy, Anssi Rantasalo, will leave the company for new challenges on 1 June 2017. He will continue as the CEO until 28 February 2017, taking care of the activity planning for 2017 as well as closing the year 2016. In March-May 2017, he will also finalise the ongoing Asia project, as agreed with the Board of Directors of the company. Mr. Rantasalo has been CEO of the company since 2002.

"Anssi Rantasalo has been a visionary leader, who has guided Kemppi on the road to digitalisation with great steps. He has managed the business successfully and we're very grateful for this time together," said Mrs. Teresa Kemppi-Vasama Chairwoman of the Board of Directors.

Mrs. Teresa Kemppi-Vasama, Chairwoman of the Board of Directors, Kemppi Oy

The ongoing year is the last one in the company's current strategic plan and a new ambitious growth plan, with new challenges, has been prepared under Rantasalo's management.

“Twenty-three years in the same company, with the last fifteen years as CEO, is a long time. It has been great to lead a successful company, even in challenging times. Now the time has come for me to take on completely different challenges, once we have first finalised ongoing projects. Kemppi has a strong strategy for the digital future and an ambitious growth plan that will give the new CEO a solid platform from which to start," added Rantasalo.

The search for a new CEO has been started and, if needed, the CEO's duties will be handled, on a temporary basis, from 1 March 2017 by Mrs. Teresa Kemppi-Vasama.■
