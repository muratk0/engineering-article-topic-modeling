# BOARD MEMBERS CONFIRMED FOR OUTOKUMPU

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

Outokumpu Oyj's Annual General Meeting, held on 30 March 2010 in Helsinki (Finland), decided that the number of Board members would be eight, including Chairman and Vice Chairman.

Evert Henkes, Ole Johansson, Victoire de Margerie, Anna Nilsson-Ehle, Jussi Pesonen, Leena Saarinen and Anssi Soila were reelected as members to the Board of Directors, and Olli Vaartimo was elected as a new member, until the close of the following Annual General Meeting. The Annual General Meeting reelected Ole Johansson as Chairman and Anssi Soila as Vice Chairman of the Board of Directors.

Based on the proposal by the company's largest shareholder, Solidium Oy, wholly-owned by the Finnish State, to form a nomination committee, the Annual General Meeting resolved to form a nomination committee to prepare proposals on the composition and remuneration of the Board of Directors to the next Annual General Meeting.

The Chairman of the Board of Directors, elected as an expert member, and representatives of the three largest shareholders were elected to form the nomination committee. The Annual General Meeting also resolved that Outokumpu Oyj donate EUR 250,000 to the Aalto University Foundation.
