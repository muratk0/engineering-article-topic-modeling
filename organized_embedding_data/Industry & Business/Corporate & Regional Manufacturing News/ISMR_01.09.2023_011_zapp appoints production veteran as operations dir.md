# Zapp appoints production veteran as operations director

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

Zapp Electric Vehicles Group Limited, which is in the process of launching its UK-based, two-wheel electric vehicle brand in Europe, confirmed that Thodsapol Kaewamorn has joined Zapp as operations director to lead its team overseeing engineering and outsourced production. Mr. Kaewamorn has more than twenty years of experience in the automotive industry with a track record of launching new production lines and setting up new plants to manufacture vehicles and automotive products.

For the last five years, he has served as advanced manufacturing engineer and country manager for Thailand at Adient plc, the world's largest automotive seat manufacturer. In that role, he oversaw production operations in Thailand for all Adient's product groups and launched many other projects for the company. Prior to his tenure at Adient, he was an assistant general manager for Nissan Motor overseeing quality assurance and body assembly engineering. Before that, he worked for twelve years at Summit Group, which is Zapp's contract manufacturer, as a project manager and process engineering manager for planning and launching new models.

Swin Chatsuwan, Founder and Chief Executive Officer, Zapp, commented: "Thodsapol brings a long and proven track record of new product launches and quality assurance from his experience at Adient, Nissan and Summit. This will strengthen our team's capabilities and ensure the premium quality and timely delivery of Zapp's products for customers as we establish the i300 as the preferred method of electrified personal urban mobility. We look forward to the successful rollout of the i300 in key markets in Europe on the way towards expansion globally."

Mr. Kaewamorn added: “Electrification in the powered two-wheeler space is inevitable and I believe Zapp is poised to become a major player in this sector. It is an incredible opportunity to be a part of the Zapp team in executing its vision for the conversion of urban mobility to electric vehicles globally."

Zapp's principal operating affiliate is a British company, run by a team of specialists from the mobility industry on a mission to redefine the electric two-wheeler segment. Zapp's debut product, the i300, is an urban electric two-wheeler capable of traditional motorcycle levels of performance in a stepthrough format. The i300 is the first in a suite of electric two-wheelers that Zapp plans to bring to market.

To achieve this, Zapp's design team created a distinctive 'Z'-shaped exoskeleton architecture designed to lower the i300's weight and centre of gravity for better on-road performance and handling (fewer assembly components, reducing weight, maximising efficiency and boosting battery range). Another key innovation is the i300's portable double battery system.■
