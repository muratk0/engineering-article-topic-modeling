# Meet the 2020 FMA Board of Directors Candidates

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 The Fabricator]

Get to know the volunteers who are going to help shape FMA’s future! The candidates will serve threeyear terms beginning October 2020.

Vanessa Heim | Vice President | Brenco Industries Ltd | Delta, British Columbia, Canada

Started by her father and grandfather in 1983, Brenco is a one-stop metal processing and fabrication shop. Vanessa Heim holds a bachelor’s degree in business. After gaining experience in other industries, she returned to Brenco and has grown from project manager to vice president. This will be Vanessa’s first three-year term on the FMA board of directors.

Rakesh Kumar | Vice President of Sales | Cincinnati Inc | Harrison, Ohio

Rakesh started his engineering career in a machine shop and managed machining centers. He spent 22 years with Amada in various roles, including engineering, production, sales, and more. Rakesh has spent the last three years with Cincinnati Inc in an executive role in charge of sales, service, products, and marketing. This will be Rakesh’s first three-year term on the FMA board of directors.

Jason Stadler | BULT Technical Support | John Deere Power Products | Greenville, Tenn.

Jason obtained a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering from the University of Iowa in 1997. In 2005, he joined John Deere as a welding and fabrication manufacturing engineer and in 2010 was promoted to a manufacturing engineering supervisor. In 2015, Jason was promoted to a business unit team leader for technical support. This will be Jason’s first three-year term on the FMA board of directors.

Jay Phillips | Plate Processing Manager | Valley Iron Fabrication | Fresno, Calif.

Jay’s interest in metal fabrication and manufacturing began in high school as an active student in a welding program. While in college at Fresno State, Jay worked part-time at Valley Iron. Jay was an operations manager from 2002-2012. He is currently responsible for the day-to-day sales and operations of all plate processing. This will be Jay’s second three-year term on the FMA board of directors.

Lori A. Tapani | Co-President | Wyoming Machine Inc | Stacy, Minn.

In 1994, Lori joined her sister Traci to become the second-generation owners of Wyoming Machine, founded by their father in 1974. Lori is a member of the FMA Management Advisory Council and has participated and chaired the steering committee that plans the FMA Annual Leadership Summit. This will be Lori’s second three-year term on the FMA board of directors.

Lisa Wertzbaugher | Sales Manager and National Accounts | Superior Tube Products | Davenport, Iowa

Lisa leads the sales and marketing strategy for Superior Tube Products and manages all national accounts. Superior Tube Products was started by Lisa’s parents in 1991. Lisa worked in the family business growing up but went on to pursue a career in the medical device industry. She returned to STP in 2014. This will be Lisa’s second three-year term on the FMA board of directors.
