BENTELER is a group that develops, produces and distributes products, systems and services for the automotive, industrial and energy sectors. Now owned by the fifth generation of the founding family (it was founded in 1876 by Carl Benteler), the group is managed by BENTELER International AG based in Salzburg, Austria. The company employs 23,000 people and is represented at 86 sites in 26 countries.

As of 2024, business operations are organised into four divisions: BENTELER Automotive Components (BAC), BENTELER Automotive Modules (BAM), BENTELER Steel/ Tube (BST) and HOLON. The Automotive business, which is organised into the two BAC and BAM divisions, develops and produces components and modules for chassis, body and engine and exhaust systems as well as system solutions for electric vehicles. BENTELER Steel/Tube develops and produces steel and seamless and welded quality steel tubes for automotive, energy and industrial markets. It provides customised tube solutions from material development to tube application. BENTELER covers the entire supply chain from its own steel production to the tube and processing of the finished component.

HOLON is a subsidiary of the BENTELER Group. It is an OEM for autonomous movers and, through HOLON, BENTELER aims to redefine passenger transport in public and private sectors. BENTELER Glass Processing Equipment is a supplier of flat glass processing equipment. BENTELER Mechanical Engineering develops and manufactures continuous furnaces, production equipment for chassis components and structural parts as well as equipment for automated loading and unloading of furnaces and presses.

## 2023 results in focus

2023 was a pivotal year for BENTELER. It secured

refinancing at an early stage and successfully completed the restructuring of the group. It also confirmed a record order intake of more than €11 billion in its automotive business.

Despite a slight decline in sales last year, BENTELER increased its EBITDA by €79 million to €782 million in 2023. Revenue in BENTELER's Automotive Division increased by around €200 million despite lower inflationary effects. EBITDA of €452 million also significantly exceeded the previous year's figure by around €100 million. It has also invested in numerous sites—for example, its sites in Schwandorf (Germany) and Kariega (South Africa)—and established a new site in Bratislava (Slovakia).

At an operational level, it is focusing on digitalisation. Using automated quality control, artificial intelligence and Big Data analytics, it increased production efficiency in 2023.

Dr. Tobias Braun, CFO of the BENTELER Group, added: "Our hard work over the past few years is bearing fruit. Both of our divisions achieved remarkable results in 2023. The increase in earnings in our automotive business is particularly pleasing. We took advantage of the upturn in the market to generate EBITDA of €452 million. We also built on the previous year's good result in our steeltube business with EBITDA of €332 million. The consistent implementation of our strategy is clearly proving successful."

## Tube and pipe outlook

BENTELER's automotive tube solutions include rotor shafts; fluid routing lines; prop shafts; seatbelt tensioners; airbag generator sleeves and tubes for axle and steering solutions. Its products for industry applications include tube for pistons, hydraulic lines and cylinders. It also offers a wide range of tubes for energy applications.

With a relatively small proportion of standard products, BENTELER's Steel/Tube division offers over 85% customised and tailor-made products. Sustainability and electric mobility are also of crucial importance. By increasing the billet size at its Dinslaken plant (Germany), it has increased productivity and expanded its product range. With larger billets, it can produce more sophisticated and diverse tube products to better meet the needs of the oil and gas sector.

BENTELER has over 44 hot-forming lines worldwide. With this technology, high-strength products can be manufactured using the minimum of material, thereby ensuring high safety with minimal weight.

In addition, it can offer design solutions made of high-strength aluminium or steel that reduce weight by up to 30%. High-strength steel enables a thinner-walled design of tubes. Substituting solid material solutions with tube solutions or designing tube wall thicknesses according to load factors also contributes to a significant reduction in weight, a topic that is on the long-term agenda at BENTELER Steel/Tube where investigations are carried out on an ongoing basis.

BENTELER HYRESIST

Intensive efforts are being made worldwide to reduce greenhouse gases: Europe plans to be CO₂-neutral by 2050 and Germany wants to achieve this goal as early as 2045. To achieve this, new energy sources are needed. For example, ‘green' hydrogen which, because it's produced using renewable energy, is CO₂-free.

BENTELER has now added seamless, hot-rolled tubes and pipes for the safe distribution of hydrogen to its portfolio: BENTELER HYRESIST. Designed for the construction of hydrogen distribution networks, the range helps make the alternative energy source more readily available and the targeted CO₂ neutrality more quickly achievable. These hydrogen line pipes support industry in building the infrastructure for the energy source of the future.

## BENTELER CliMorebrand

In September 2023, BENTELER's Steel/Tube Division presented its new CliMore® product brand to the public. It covers all CO₂-reduced steels and steel tube products in the division and categorises them into three sustainability categories: Advantage, Ambition and Excellence. These indicate how the steel is produced and which energy sources were used for it.

“In each of the three levels, lowemission steel is produced from scrap using the electric arc furnace. This recycling alone saves up to 75% of CO₂ emissions when compared to new steel produced from iron ore in the classic blast furnace route," explained BENTELER.

“For the Ambition level, green electricity is used in the tube mills and for the operation of the electric arc furnace. This results in a reduction of up to 85% in CO₂ emissions. In addition, the highest level, Excellence, will focus in future on electrification of the plants or use of green hydrogen as an energy source for process energy in the production of steel and its processing into tubes," concluded BENTELER.

CliMore family products are available as tube solutions directly from BENTELER Steel/Tube and through the company's steel trading partners.

"Our Steel/Tube Division also once again achieved a high level of profitability. Our North American business was affected by the ongoing exceptional economic situation in the first quarter and a subsequent normalisation of demand and price levels. As expected, it was unable to repeat the results of the previous year. Nevertheless, we coped well with the fluctuations by flexibly adjusting our capacities. Our European steel tube business, on the other hand, exceeded the previous year's very good result despite falling demand and also posted a record year," confirmed BENTELER in its latest Annual Report.

"Along with our good economic performance, we continued to focus on sustainability. We expanded our green product portfolio and maintained our pioneering role in sustainable steel and steel tube solutions. Our CliMore®range, introduced in 2023, groups our CO₂-reduced steels and steel tubes into three different sustainability categories. This creates transparency and helps our customers to decarbonise their own supply chains. EcoVadis, the world's largest provider of sustainability ratings, awarded our Steel/Tube Division its gold medal at the end of the year. This puts us in the top five per cent in our industry," it added.
