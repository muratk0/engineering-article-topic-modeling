# A STRONG FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Matthias Huber, CEO, RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH, outlines his strategic and technical focus to ISMR along with his views on sheet metal market trends and new RAS technology launches to support metal folding operations.

RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau is a leading manufacturer of machines for sheet metal processing. Headquartered in Sindelfingen, Germany, the company has been in business for over 85 years.

Its extensive portfolio includes folding machines, swing beam shears and forming machines as well as automated and customised manufacturing solutions for various industries worldwide.

In 2022, RAS launched the MEGAbend folder for UpDown bending of thick materials. It expanded its successful relationship with Euclid Labs in 2023 by joining it as a shareholder. This October, it launched the MiniBendCenter 2 (with automatic loading and unloading—sorted and unsorted) at the EuroBLECH trade fair in Germany. Other product launches at EuroBLECH included new Tool Support for its XLTbend folder and a new sheet metal lifting system (MetalLift) for heavy parts on its MEGAbend folder.

Matthias Huber, CEO, RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH, outlined his strategic and technical focus to ISMR along with his views on sheet metal market trends and new RAS technology launches.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals? What about in 2025?

MH: There have been very high, above-average growth rates in recent years, especially in the USA and Canada. The European market is recovering hesitantly. We assume that this situation will be resolved over the course of 2025.

The EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover this October was a great success for us. There were many discussions about future projects in the medium term and high demand for investment.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements?

MH: RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau is celebrating its 85th anniversary this year. This milestone reflects the company's many years of experience and market leadership in sheet metal processing. A new subsidiary recently opened in France, further strengthening our international presence and optimising access to European customers.

Notably, recent innovations in automation stand out. It is now possible to retrofit our Multibend-Center with automation solutions, and the new MiniBendCenter 2 offers increased efficiency for bending small parts.

Ergonomics and cost-efficiency have also been further improved: our XLTbend folder features a practical Tool Support, and our MEGAbend system has been enhanced with a MetalLift which increases productivity and user-friendliness.

ISMR: Please explain your strategic and technical focus/vision over the next few years?

MH: Strategically, we are focusing on markets and customer service/support. Through targeted strategic developments, we also aim to explore new industries and further expand our market position.

With our partners, we aim to further expand our existing markets internationally, particularly in regions such as South America and Australia. We also want to enter countries where we are currently not represented through targeted partnerships. In our core markets (such as Germany, France, Italy and the Netherlands) we will continue to strengthen our presence to achieve even greater market penetration.

We place great emphasis on comprehensive after-sales service, regular training and technical support for our partners. This helps us to maintain long-term customer relationships and maximise machine uptime.

As regards our technical focus, we continuously invest in the further development of our machines and software solutions to offer our customers precision, speed and flexibility. Our technical focus is on automation and optimising our existing product portfolio. Additionally, we plan to strengthen our collaboration with Pivatic in line production.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal markets?

MH: In response to digitalisation and the skilled labour shortage, we rely on our userfriendly Bendex software and the extensive automation capabilities of our machines which allow them to be used up to 24/7.

The Bendex machine control for RAS machines automatically generates the appropriate bending programs without requiring bending experience or complex programming. In addition, programs can be prepared in the office while the machine continues to operate productively. This combination of ease of use and advanced automation increases efficiency and flexibility in the production process, relieves skilled personnel and enables our customers to fully optimise their production capacities.

RAS unveils the new MiniBendCenter 2 at EuroBLECH

At this October's EuroBLECH in Hanover, RAS Reinhardt Maschinenbau GmbH presented the MiniBendCenter 2, a fully automated solution for precise single and series production of bent parts. The folding centre is specifically designed for small parts. It combines advanced robotics with a software solution for high levels of flexibility and efficiency. The new MiniBend-Office software and the integrated robot for fully automated loading and unloading make the system suitable for 24/7 operation.

The new MiniBend-Office software is a standalone solution that replaces previously used CAM software and SolidWorks. Through a partnership with Euclid, the flexibility of the software has been significantly enhanced, allowing for fully integrated control. Using the MiniBend-Office software, only one software solution is now required (handling both programming and simulation) and uniting the entire process on a single platform.

The intelligent robotic system, with servo-electric controls, handles loading and unloading of workpieces. The vacuum suction unit features four individually controllable suction cups for handling of parts from stacks or bulk material. The finished bent parts are stacked. Pallet change takes place during operation.

The MiniBendCenter 2 enables the processing of sheets up to 600 x 600mm and a material thickness of up to 3mm in mild steel. Automatic bending technology in both directions means that part turning is not necessary. Combined with automatic tool set-up, the machine can run continuous 24/7 production in ‘ghost shifts' with batches and individual parts.

The new MiniBendCenter 2 offers increased efficiency for bending small parts.

ISMR: Are there any products/technologies/ software that you have released or are releasing this year (including at EuroBLECH) that you would like to discuss?

MH: The RAS MiniBendCenter 2 is a unique folding centre specifically designed for small parts. It combines advanced robotics with an innovative software solution to achieve the highest levels of flexibility and efficiency. The new MiniBend-Office software and the integrated robot for fully automated loading and unloading make the system ideal for 24/7 operation.

The RAS MetalLift complements the MEGAbend and sets new standards in ergonomics and efficiency. The system enables effortless loading and unloading of heavy and large blanks up to 6mm mild steel. This makes material handling easier and supports the automated bending process of the MEGAbend ergonomically and safely.

At EuroBLECH in Hanover recently, we also introduced our Tool Support for the first time. This system is designed to enable ergonomic operation on the XLTbend and MEGAbend. The Tool Support simplifies the handling of upper beam tools up to 300mm (XLTbend) and 400mm (MEGAbend) for large box heights. Tools can be positioned effortlessly and almost "weight-neutrally" or stored in the integrated tool magazine, which reduces fatigue and, importantly, lowers the risk of injury from repetitive heavy lifting.

ISMR: What is your approach to technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Augmented Reality, Digital Twins and Virtual Reality?

MH: Our Bendex software makes it possible to create a 'digital twin' that enables precise analysis of bent parts and therefore realistically determines the optimum bending strategies, without blocking production at the machine. Programming can be carried out conveniently in the office, including collision detection.

Using this simulation, we can automatically suggest suitable bending strategies, which makes the entire process much more efficient. This not only improves planning accuracy, but also optimises the entire production process.

In addition, we are currently working on the first approaches to predictive maintenance with the aim of permanently monitoring the condition of the machine and being able to carry out maintenance work proactively and in line with requirements.

ISMR: How are you handling sustainability in both your products and organisation (including carbon footprint calculations etc.)?

MH: We have significantly reduced the energy consumption of our machines by dispensing with hydraulic components as far as possible and using servomotors in various machine types instead. This allows us to substantially lower power consumption and reduce the environmental footprint of our products.

Another approach is the refurbishment of used machines. We buy back older RAS machines, recondition them and put them back on the market. In this way, we extend the lifecycles of our products.

Our company is committed to sustainable energy solutions. With our photovoltaic system, we generate a significant portion of our electricity needs on-site. Additionally, we use the process heat generated to warm our buildings. This approach allows us to reduce our carbon footprint and actively contribute to environmental protection.

We also collaborate with an energy consultancy that actively supports both our customers and ourselves in promoting energy consumption optimisation.
