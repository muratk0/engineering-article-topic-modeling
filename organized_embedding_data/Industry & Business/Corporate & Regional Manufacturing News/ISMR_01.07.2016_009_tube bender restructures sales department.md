# Tube bender restructures sales department

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

SCHWARZE-ROBITEC, a leading manufacturer of tube bending machines, has said goodbye to its sales manager, Jürgen Korte, who retired at the end of April 2016. A new alignment, from the bottom up, has been implemented throughout the entire management level of the company. One major objective is to expand the company's market position.

Schwarze-Robitec has also restructured its sales department. Heike Ahlers is its new sales and marketing manager and now functions as the extended arm of the company's management. Hired in 2013 by Schwarze-Robitec, she was significantly involved with the opening of the current subsidiary in the USA in 2015. The objective of the restructuring is to ensure that the company can adapt to ever-changing international markets over the long term.

"In the future, we will increase our sales activities beyond Europe and raise our high profile worldwide," commented Bert Zorn, managing director of Schwarze-Robitec. See www.schwarze-robitec.com.

## Norsk Titanium focuses on quality

NORSK TITANIUM AS, a global provider of aerospace-grade, titanium additive manufacturing technology, has announced the appointment of Annette M. Jussaume to the position of Vice President of Quality. In her new role, she will report to Carl O. Johnson, Senior Vice President – Technology, Engineering & Quality.

She joins Norsk Titanium from Pratt & Whitney, a United Technologies Company, where her 29-year career was highlighted by leading the execution of the PW1524G engine development programme for the Bombardier C-Series commercial aircraft, as well as establishing and leading quality readiness for the Lockheed Martin F-35 Joint Strike Fighter and F-22 Raptor engine programmes. In her most recent role, she provided worldwide executive leadership of quality programmes at all Pratt & Whitney-owned facilities.

“Annette is a wonderful and necessary addition to our leadership team," said Norsk Titanium CEO, Warren M. Boley Jr. “Her background in leading collaborative customer partnerships and implementing best-in-class aerospace quality systems will significantly enhance Norsk Titanium's portfolio of commercial and military OEM part programmes."

Ms. Jussaume holds a mechanical engineering degree from Western New England University.
