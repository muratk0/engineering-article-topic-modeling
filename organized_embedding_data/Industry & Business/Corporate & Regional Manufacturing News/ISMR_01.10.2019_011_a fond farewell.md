# A fond farewell

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

DAVID LARCOMBE, who recently retired after 40 years' service (latterly as managing director, Bystronic Laser AG in the UK) was invited with his wife Alison to say goodbye to his colleagues at the Swiss company's headquarters in Niederönz. After the event, the couple were treated to a weekend in the Swiss Alps near the Italian border, beside Lake Lugano.

As well as his tenure at Bystronic AG, David Larcombe spent considerable time and effort on co-founding and promoting the Association of Industrial Laser Users (AILU), the UK's active voice for laser processing.

"I feel extremely privileged to have had the career I have enjoyed within the engineering industry. I have a large number of very special memories, especially the many years working for Bystronic. I have made many great friendships both within Bystronic worldwide and in the UK sheet metalworking industry. I wish everyone the very best for the future," he told ISMR.
