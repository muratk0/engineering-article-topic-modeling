Prima Power is the machinery division of Prima Industrie, a Group with over 1600 employees worldwide and manufacturing plants in Italy, Finland, the USA and China as well as a specialised sales and service network in over 80 countries.

At this October's EuroBLECH exhibition, ISMR sat down with Prima Power's managing director, Ezio Basso, and its executive VP of sales, Claudio Banchi, to discover new innovations being unveiled and the Group's strategy for the future.

## Strategy and targets

"This has been a good year for the Prima Group - we expect to register reasonable growth at the end of our financial year. Prima Power growth should be higher than the overall growth in the Group. We have made significant investments in fibre lasers and have set up a direct presence in many emerging markets in the last few years. We have also introduced a new model into all our product lines. Here, at EuroBLECH, we have renewed all our product lines," Ezio Basso told ISMR.

"We also opened our new Technology Centre and HQ this year at Collegno (near Turin), Italy. This was a significant investment for us. We have a huge array of products now and a target for the group of half a billion euros in 2019. We are working hard to achieve this goal and have a lot of projects in development at the moment to support this growth. We need to complete our product range first. For example, we are currently mapping the complete range of skills for our service technicians around the world so that we can arrange the necessary training for them to optimise our services."

The Prima Group, having acquired different companies with various ERP systems over the years, is following a defined IT strategy with a core ERP model capable of capturing and processing data from different companies in the Group. The aim is to unify and coordinate all the systems into one. This, Basso told ISMR, will be one of the pillars that allow the Group to grow.

We expect to register approximately 10% growth at the end of our financial year.

With different logistics centres for different sheet metal parts (punching, bending etc.) around Europe, another key target for the Prima Group is to integrate these to create major logistics/machine diagnostics centres in Europe, Asia and the US. Prima plans to connect these to a system which can track Prima stocks and spares worldwide in real-time to ensure local stock supplies for customers.

“The aim is to send spare parts and engineers separately, and as quickly as possible, to minimise response times for customers. The engineer will have diagnosed the issue before visiting the customer," concluded Basso.

We have a huge array of products now and a target for the group of half a billion euros in 2019.

Prima Power products are "Industry 4.0 Inside", ready for the smart manufacturing era, with complete connectivity to production monitoring, diagnostics, analysis and service through advanced solutions such as Fleet Management, and a new app for simple, smart use of the machine's Tulus MUPS (Machine User Personal aSsistant). A demonstration on Prima's EuroBLECH stand showed various Prima Power machines communicating with operators.

## The first cut

At EuroBLECH, Prima Power exhibited a revised version of its 2D Laser Genius fibre laser cutting system, featuring a new Prima cutting head, with an adaptive collimator and a new 4kW CF laser resonator developed by Prima Power. The machine was exhibited with another new feature – a loading/unloading tower system, Combo Tower Laser, for automated operation.

In Hanover, Laser Genius was exhibited with the Open Cabin - fully opening sliding doors granting accessibility and userfriendliness for the operator. Laser Genius is managed by the new Prima Power Open Laser2D CNC, featuring smart gas pressure management, improved grid cutting speed and innovative software compensation solutions to further enhance cutting quality.

"We now have total control over laser technology," explained Claudio Banchi, Executive VP Sales, Prima Power. "Laser source and head, automation, hardware and software: all the essential machine elements are developed, manufactured and maintained by Prima Power directly. This really makes a difference for our customers, who can count on a single-point-of-contact. With our comprehensive range of technologies and products, we are now a 'one-stop shop' partner for any sheet metal processing need."

EuroBLECH 2016 was also the global Launchpad for Prima's new Laser Next 2130 3D laser cutting machine for larger parts, such as the new door ring concept. Laser Next 2130 has a considerable working volume of 3050 x 2100 x 612mm with a dynamic 5m turntable.

We are now a ‘one-stop shop' partner for any sheet metal processing need.

"We are introducing the new 3D Laser Next system, with 2 metres in the y axis, so it can cut the door ring (the component out of which a ring of the car door is manufactured). This part is made out of different blanks, tailorwelded together and then stamped. As the part is so large, it wouldn't fit the previous range of dynamic standard laser cutting 3D machines, so we had to enlarge the machine up to two metres.

This will open new opportunities for us in the hot stamped steel market, which is a very good market for 3D laser cutting, Banchi told ISMR.

Hot stamped parts are increasingly replacing traditional metal formed parts in automotive frame design and this trend will be prevalent for the next few years. A recent trend is the adoption of a new door ring concept, hot stamped as one part instead of the four parts which are usually required. This minimizes production costs, reduces weight and increases the performance of this component.

"Laser Next boasts the best dynamic performance on the market for a 3D laser machine (208m/min. trajectory speed and 2.1g acceleration) and can be equipped with 3kW or 4kW high brilliance fibre laser. The model exhibited in Hanover features the new fibre laser source developed and manufactured by Prima Power," explained Claudio Banchi.

The latest version of Platino Fiber, Prima Power's 2D laser machine, was also on display at EuroBLECH 2016 with some important new features such as the Prima Power CNC Open control.

## Punching and combination systems

Prima Power also launched a complete range of new generation, servo-electric, punch-laser combination machines at EuroBLECH 2016. The Combi Genius 1530 Dynamic version was shown with Compact Express automation and new Prima Power CF4000 fibre laser source. The new technology is also available in the Punch Genius turret punch press range. Numerous improvements have been made to the automation range, sheet position axis and process monitoring for unmanned production.

The customised turret concept allows the availability of up to 384 tools or 128 index tools simultaneously in turret, which practically eliminates the tool set-up. A new option is the Intelligent Ram which shortens tool change time and increases the number of tools in turret, especially for index tools. More value can be added by using special tools for tapping, bending, roll forming, marking etc. A new Hole Check system increases process reliability in unmanned production. A further option is the indexable, servo-electric, 200kN upforming unit for high and complex forms.

As the laser source, a new Prima Power CF fibre resonator of either 3kW or 4kW can be chosen to provide higher speed and quality cutting results. Laser Plasma Monitoring (LPM) follows the cutting process quality and re-starts the cutting if any problems appear. A Lens Condition System (LCS) monitors the cutting lens condition, informs the operator and stops the process before any further damage occurs.

Prima Power also renewed its Shear Genius models. These new models include the latest new features of the new generation Punch Genius and add many new ideas for shearing and automation.

A new upgraded solution for flexible manufacturing, with the new Shear Genius, was a key attraction on the Prima booth at EuroBLECH. Prima Power PSBB (short for Punching, right angle Shearing, Buffering and Bending) is a fully automatic manufacturing line for processing sheet into ready-bent components. The version on the booth included many improvements and the new Shear Genius as the punching-shearing module to raise manufacturing speed and productivity. The integrated line produced a shoe box, on the booth at EuroBLECH, which visitors could take away with them.

## New bending innovations

After its successful launch at the LAMIERA show in Bologna, the new BCe Smart servo electric panel bender made its international debut at EuroBLECH in Hanover. It is equipped with a range of options to adapt it to specific applications such as automatic tool change, additional short blades for symmetrical or asymmetrical positioning, the UBC to minimize bending profile width and an additional screen for selflearning mode. Bending force is 32 tons.

"The large working table that allows loading and unloading in a single sequence increases productivity. The machine is extremely easy to use and does

queues. High ergonomics and safety levels are obtained through features such as the absence of physical barriers for manual loading; warning lights and acoustic signals to synchronize loading and unloading and the additional interactive display that guides the operator e.g. orientation of work-pieces etc. The working table side sections can also be easily lowered," Banchi told ISMR.

At EuroBLECH 2016, Prima Power also presented the bestseller in its eP press brake family - the eP1030. The model on display (105 tons x 3060mm) had been revamped with the new Prima Power design and the latest safety device by Lazer Safe.

All the eP-series press brakes use servo electric technology; feature an O-frame construction and open tooling concept; the Prima Power touch-screen numerical control and, from now on, the new Lazer Safe IRIS for operator and tool safety.

It does not require skilled operators. Thanks to the barcode reader and the Tool Changer system, its set-up is automatic, takes place in masked time and activates dynamic production.

The global Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC) market is expected to grow steadily at a CAGR of 7% from 2016-2020

ISMR SAYS:

“We analyse trends and drivers in global HVAC markets”

The heating and air-conditioning contractors' industry is heavily influenced by activity in the construction market, with the majority of income generated from Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning (HVAC) installations in new residential and nonresidential structures. This industry comprises companies that primarily install and service heating; ventilation; air conditioning (HVAC) and refrigeration equipment. Operators perform tasks that include new installations, additions, alterations, maintenance and repairs.

The industry is characterised by moderate barriers to entry. Barriers include completing an apprenticeship and gaining a license, facing intense competition from established industry contractors in local areas, as well as establishing working relationships with property managers, general contractors and local retailers.

## A global outlook

Market analyst, ReportsnReports, predicts that the global HVAC market will grow steadily at a CAGR of 7% from 2016-2020. The increase in construction activities worldwide is a key growth driver for this market. New constructions in the residential, commercial and industrial sectors have increased the demand for HVAC systems, which improves building efficiencies, generating high revenue for the market.

"Government regulations introduced by countries worldwide also compel HVAC system vendors to comply with industry standards. For instance, the Commercial Building Disclosure outlined by the Australian government provides a high incentive for improving the performance and efficiency of HVAC systems. It requires building owners selling or leasing office spaces larger than 2,000 square meters to comply with energy-efficiency standards. This, in turn, has been encouraging the installation of efficient HVAC systems," said the analyst.

"Advances in monitoring systems and intelligent technology are expected to aid the growth of the HVAC market. Monitoring systems help in tracking the energy consumption of a building while intelligent technology controls the energy output of an HVAC system. For instance, fans used in the heating system of computing systems are switched on only after the computing system reaches the set temperature, making the system energy-efficient. The integration of the Internet of Things (IoT) with HVAC systems is terms of revenue."

According to the analyst, the HVAC equipment segment dominated the market during 2015, with a market share of around 69%. Growing populations and industrialisation, rising sales of domestic and commercial buildings, increasing construction expenditure and the expanding purchasing power of the middle class are the key growth drivers for this segment. The nonresidential segment accounted for 67% of market share during 2015. Healthcare and educational institutions, manufacturing and industrial buildings, as well as offices and retail stores are the non-residential users of HVAC systems. The increase in construction activities and rapid industrialisation, as well as the need for enhancing energy-efficiency in buildings, are driving the growth of HVAC systems.

APAC accounted for 51% of market share during 2015 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 7% during the forecast period. The analysts expect the rise in investments in real estate to boost the HVAC market in APAC. The report further anticipates China to retain its market leadership until the end of the forecast period.

## Residential HVAC market

According to analyst Technavio, after the recession of 2009, there has been a strong increase in the construction of residential buildings, especially in APAC countries such as India and China, which is driving the market for residential HVAC. Technavio's market research analysts expected the global residential HVAC market to grow tremendously from 2015-2019, posting a CAGR of more than 10%.

"The growing concern for indoor air quality is one of the main reasons driving the growth of the HVAC market. Daily exposure to multiple chemicals with an indoor presence adds to the increasing prevalence of asthma, medically unexplained symptoms. Thus, people have become more aware of these concerns in the recent past and have started adopting measures to prevent these problems, leading to an increase in the sales of HVAC equipment," said the analyst.

"The global residential HVAC by air conditioning segment is growing rapidly and is expected to reach US$ 84.38 by 2019, growing at a remarkable CAGR of over 12%," added the analyst. Technavio further expects this segment to multiply significantly during the forecast period due to rising demand for split, single-packaged, window and portable air conditioners.

"In terms of geography, the APAC region held the largest share with over 52% of the total market share in 2014, and this region is expected to maintain its dominance during the forecast period. Rapid urbanisation and infrastructure developments are the major factors contributing to the growth of the market in this region," explained Technavio.

## HVAC in the US

A new report "Global and US HVAC Market (Heating, Ventilation and Air Conditioning) Research Report, 2016-2017", from RnR Market Research, confirms that the US HVAC industry is gradually recovering. In the residential sector, according to the report, it should result in outsized volume growth due to low unemployment and gas prices, added to high consumer confidence and depressive demand, while continued price and commodity tailwinds boost margin expansion. "The commercial HVAC market in the US is at a more steady status, with continuous nonresidential construction recovery which still has some room to run," said the analyst.

Replacement rates have steadily risen over the past few years following historically low levels coming out of the recession, and are now accelerating towards the high end of historical ranges as potential replacement demand is released into the system. This will be further boosted by a positive fundamental set-up with high consumer confidence and low unemployment and gas prices, which have historically correlated strongly with replacement rates. It is estimated that the US HVAC market would achieve an 11% growth in 2016 and 5% in 2017.

It also reported that the US commercial HVAC market is stable - based on a 5% growth rate, ongoing non-residential construction recovery and stable 3% growth in replacement, it is estimated that this market would achieve 4% growth in 2016 and 2017 respectively. "2015 was a strong recovery year for non-residential construction while 2016 could most probably witness some slowing. While most HVAC players' revenue have recovered on regulatory change and pricing, commercial HVAC volumes are still 20% below peak, which means there is still some room to run in this HVAC market," said the report.

'Ductless' continues to be a key strategic theme in the HVAC market, which could represent 15% of the industry for both residential (about 9%) and commercial (about 6%). While key obstacles such as first cost, lack of installed base and aesthetics limit the penetration gains in what should remain largely a ducted unitary market, it is starting to show better adoption rates as energy efficiency gains and flexibility in installation outweigh the higher first cost in many instances. Almost all US OEMs have built up JVs/ partnerships with foreign suppliers, and a well above market average growth rate is expected in 'ductless'.

"With most OEMs boasting new capabilities, inverter technology is another key strategic theme. Inverter compressors appear to be contained solely within the high-end systems at present, with key HVAC market players expressing interest in spreading this technology in more mainstream systems. In the near-term, there should be no dramatic change here," added the analyst.
