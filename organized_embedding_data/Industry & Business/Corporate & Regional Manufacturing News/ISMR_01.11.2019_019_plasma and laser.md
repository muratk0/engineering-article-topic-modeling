# PLASMA AND LASER

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

Kjellberg's Finsterwalde's MD highlighted Industry 4.0, smart technologies and the Smart Factory to ISMR on the company's booth at Blechexpo.

Kjellberg invested several million euros and years of research into the latest 'Q' generation of its plasma power source. The investment paid off for the plasma and laser cutting specialist, as it won the winner's trophy for the "Q" in the category of separation technology at the Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart in November.

The Industry 4.0-compatible inverter power source combines precision plasma cutting with the complex requirements of digitised production. In the future, systems can communicate between all stages of the value chain in a smart factory. With the slogan "Always on", the "Q" series was officially launched less than two months ago.

Real-time communication between plant components and process data exchange of networked machines were two of the innovations that visitors were able to experience at the company's stand at Blechexpo via a live broadcast of Kjellberg's Competence Centre Plasma 4.0 from Kjellberg's headquarters in Finsterwalde. Using the browser-based Q-Desk operator interface, visitors were able to see information such as FAQs or video tutorials directly on-site.

ISMR spoke with Kjellberg's new managing director, Thomas Kagemann, on the company's booth at Blechexpo who highlighted its focus on Industry 4.0, smart technologies and the Smart Factory. With its new technology launches, and clear embrace of Industry 4.0 principles in its product development, the future looks bright for this German cutting specialist.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements?

TK: I am very proud of the number of deliveries we made last year and this year. In 2017, we saw strong demand for power sources – and customers wanted us to deliver more of these. We therefore hired more people as well as reorganising and restructuring to meet this demand.

In the long run, we decided to expand even further so we are even better structured now for future growth.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals?

TK: This year, we intend to improve on last year's turnover – it looks to be 4 to 5 per cent more than expected. We are happy with the situation this year and looking forward to 2020. The European market has been strong for us this year. Despite the shrinkage of the automotive industry, production issues and the move to electric vehicles, the European and Asian sectors have been strong for us this year. Our business tends to be in thicker materials, rather than thinner ones, so we have not been quite as affected by the shrinkage of the automotive industry as our competitors. Germany still takes a high percentage of our business. Europe is our largest market country-wise but, worldwide, China is actually our largest market.

We have considered expansion into new regions and countries – this includes Canada and the U.S. We accordingly decided to exhibit at FABTECH in Chicago this year and showcase our plasma and laser products.

The tariff situation between the U.S. and China is uncertain and it is not clear what effect this will have on wider industry in Asia. Although Chinese market growth has decreased slightly, we do have an advantage in the Chinese market as our German/European product is not currently affected by Customs tariffs.

ISMR: What are you showcasing here at Blechexpo?

TK: Our presence here is a little different this year. It is the first time that we have not shown live cutting on our booth. However, our large screen is showing live cutting demonstrations from our Finsterwalde headquarters.

Our focus at Blechexpo is on our new Q modular inverters (the third series, after our Smart Focus and HiFocus series). Q was launched on 11 September 2019 and we are now selling Q 1500 and Q 3000 to OEMs and end-users. These inverters offer a high output of digital information to users.

We are highlighting Industry 4.0, smart technologies and the Smart Factory on our booth at Blechexpo. Our systems offer customers high levels of data that can be combined with a range of other data from different machines to users.

We have been very focused on plasma cutting and have now started with laser cutting, which we will expand in the future. We are also presenting welding here on the booth with OSCAR (our development partner for welding and laser) and our welding company, which works on very special projects. We also have a welding electrode business within the Group. You will also see on our booth a 3D-printed gas shoe for high-quality welding (our In Focus inverter combined with the gas shoe).

The 3D-printed gas shoe on the Kjellberg booth at Blechexpo

ISMR: Please explain your strategic and technical objectives?

TK: The next two steps that we will take in 2020 concern the new Q 4500 and a new SmartBox. We plan to launch the new Q 4500 inverter in Q3 next year, so it will officially be launched at EuroBLECH in Hanover.

We also plan to launch a SmartBox in Q3 next year to prepare our customers for the digital world. The products have smart algorithms to equip customers for Industry 4.0 and the required levels of connectivity needed. We do not need to change customers' plasma sources but can retrofit/upgrade their sources with the SmartBox to offer this level of digital connectivity and data to the customer.

We have a stable presence in Asian markets but we are placing more of a strategic focus on these markets. With this is mind, we have decided to set up a bonded warehouse in Saigon to store our power sources for Asian markets. We intend to open it in December this year. It is a clear signal of our commitment to Asian markets and means quicker service and delivery for our Asian customers.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers and how are you addressing these issues for them?

TK: Although Industry 4.0 and digitalisation are very important next steps for the industry, the main consideration for our customers is the provision of high-quality, high-precision cutting and a stable process. Our aim has always been to offer the most stable process in the world.

They also want faster, cheaper and more productive systems, of course. We have set the main consideration for our customers is the provision of high-quality, high-precision cutting and a stable process.

We have set up small teams which focus on different areas i.e. customer demands, service, marketing etc. This helps us to become better prepared and more responsive to our customers' needs.

ISMR: What about the environmental aspects of your systems?

TK: Our Silent Cut plasma cutting technology offers very low noise levels. It is available now for all smaller power sources (HiFocus and Smart Focus ranges) so is a good option for customers who care about the level of noise emissions in their factories. When cutting mild steel with a material thickness of between four and 30mm, and currents ranging between 60 and 160 A, the sound pressure level can be reduced by up to 10 dB(A). Silent Cut includes modifications not only of the nozzle design but also of the cutting parameters.

Another aim we have in the next two years is to stop ventilators running in the machines immediately after cutting to save energy. We will also work on reducing gas consumption for our customers and the environment.

ISMR: What is your strategic focus/ vision over the next few years?

TK: We intend to keep our focus on our OEM partners and not sell directly to end-users although we are visiting more end-users, with our OEM partners, to better gauge their needs now and in the future. It is important to be nearer to end-users and get their technical feedback and ideas on our products. We want to create demand from end-users for our power sources.

This is also a bridge to our technical focus on digitalisation. Our new Q power source shows that we are ready for Industry 4.0 and we now need to start 'living' Industry 4.0. Everyone has their own idea and vision of Industry 4.0 but we want to start practically implementing this, and hopefully set the benchmark for it. We already have our first customer, from Switzerland, for this product from the Industry 4.0 business network and are discussing the development of a smart factory for them. Kick-off will start in Q1 next year.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

TK: The greatest opportunities come from respecting our OEMs' business models and visiting end- users with them. This will yield even greater opportunities for us both. We are very focused on this business concept and keeping our strong partnerships with OEMs.

ISMR: Which exhibitions will you be attending over the next few months?

TK: We will be in Bangkok, Thailand. We will also be at SteelFab in Dubai with our distributor and intend to exhibit at IMTEX in India in January 2020 and presenting the Q for the first time in Asia.
