# New CEO for AutoForm

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

Dr. Waldemar Kubli, Founder and Chief Product Officer (CPO), AutoForm Group and Olivier Leteurtre, Chief Executive Officer (CEO), AutoForm Group

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GMBH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has appointed Olivier Leteurtre as Chief Executive Officer of the AutoForm Group. Dr. Waldemar Kubli, Founder and former CEO, will now take up the role of Chief Product Officer. He will continue to contribute to the success of the AutoForm Group as a member of the Board of Directors as well as the Executive Committee.

As a mechanical engineer with an MBA from INSEAD, Mr. Leteurtre has many years of international experience having held various leading positions in the software sector in Europe, Asia and USA. Before joining the AutoForm Group, he was the MD of Dassault Systèmes EUROWEST (France, Iberia, North Africa and the Middle East) for ten years. Before that, he was Executive Vice President and Executive Board Member at Tecnomatix/ Siemens PLM. In his new position as AutoForm's CEO, he is responsible for the continued business growth of the AutoForm Group.

AutoForm Engineering GmbH offers software solutions for the diemaking and sheet metal forming industries along the entire process chain. With over 400 employees dedicated to this field, it is a leading provider of software for product manufacturability, tool and material cost calculation, die face design and virtual process optimisation. Besides its headquarters in Switzerland, AutoForm has offices in Germany; The Netherlands; France; Spain; Italy; Czech Republic; Sweden; USA; Mexico; Brazil; India; China; Japan and Korea. AutoForm is also present through its agents in more than 13 other countries. See www.autoform.com
