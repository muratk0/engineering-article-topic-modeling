# ARKU recently opened its new 1,600m2 production building in Baden-Baden

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

On 6 June 2013, more than 220 customers, business partners and representatives from politics and press accepted an invitation from ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH to view its new production facility in Baden-Baden, Germany. The leveller and coil line manufacturer inaugurated its new production building with the motto “How can engineering companies and suppliers take advantage of globalisation?”

“The sun is shining over Baden and we have made every effort to ensure this as well,” said ARKU CEO, Albert Reiss, with a smile. “It is fantastic to welcome so many guests and friends to Haueneberstein where my grandfather founded his sheet metal workshop in 1928. Our efforts during this long preparation have paid off.”

In the 1960s, Artur Reiss, father of the current CEO Albert Reiss, transformed the small sheet metal workshop into an industrial sheet metal production company. The company began working in the engineering sector in 1967 when technicians built its first leveller which was sold to Argentina. Production of ARKU coil lines started in the 1970s.

## A level view

The event combined an interesting programme with exciting discussions. In the afternoon, guest speaker Hermann Simon, Ph. D., gave a presentation on the opportunities and challenges provided by hidden champions in Asia. The internationally acclaimed management consultant, author and educator talked about the macroeconomic contribution made by hidden champions and why mid-sized German companies represent a driving force for exports.

In the podium discussion chaired by Stefanie Tücking (SWR 3), the guests discussed Prof. Simon’s presentation and contributed interesting practical examples. Alongside ARKU’s CEO, Albert Reiss, representatives from the industrial sector and the financial press were also present on the podium.
