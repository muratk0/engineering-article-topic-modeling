# INNOVATION IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“To further the concept of futureoriented manufacturing, AMADA is also using the AGIC to introduce new technologies, software and machine tools to its customers.

Amada, the AMADA Group includes around 90 companies including sales branches and production sites. It employs over 8,000 people worldwide. AMADA offers a comprehensive range of solutions for sheetmetal processing and has set up a network of production sites in strategic areas: Japan, Europe, North America and China.

Based on the concept of a "place for cocreating the future of metalworking," it also set up the new AMADA Global Innovation Centre (AGIC) this year. It was opened by AMADA President & CEO, Tsutomu Isobe, on 3 February 2023 at the company's head office in Isehara City, Kanagawa Prefecture (Japan). The AGIC facility has earned Science-Based Targets Initiative (SBTi) certification to support research and verification.

"AGIC has been established to solve numerous issues faced by customers around the world, including their requests for 'support for new materials and processing technologies', 'improvement of automation and productivity' and production innovation. The existing Solution Centre was fully transformed to create a facility with a total floor space of approximately 30,000m² and the latest functions," AMADA told ISMR.
