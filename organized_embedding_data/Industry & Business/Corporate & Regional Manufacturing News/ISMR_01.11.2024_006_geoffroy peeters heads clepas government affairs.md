# Geoffroy Peeters heads CLEPAs Government Affairs

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

CLEPA, the European Association of Automotive Suppliers, has appointed Geoffroy Peeters as Head of Government Affairs.

Geoffroy brings a wealth of experience in EU public affairs and a robust track record in institutional relations across the automotive industry. He will play a key role in representing CLEPA's interests at European level, advocating for the needs of the automotive supply sector during a pivotal time of technological transformation and regulatory evolution," it said.

Geoffroy Peeters' career spans over two decades, with prominent positions at major automotive corporations. He most recently served as Director EU Affairs at Stellantis and held senior roles at FIAT Chrysler Automobiles, Toyota Motor Europe and the Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association.

Commenting on his new role, he said: “It's a great pleasure to join CLEPA’s team at a crucial moment for the mobility industry in Europe, which is full of challenges and opportunities. Promoting competitiveness and technology innovation of our members will be my motivations in our interaction with EU stakeholders."

CLEPA's Secretary General, Benjamin Krieger, added: "Geoffroy's appointment strengthens our leadership team and underlines CLEPA's

commitment to fostering strong relationships with EU institutions and the mobility ecosystem. His experience will be instrumental as we continue to advocate for a regulatory framework that supports innovation, sustainability and competitiveness for Europe's automotive suppliers.
