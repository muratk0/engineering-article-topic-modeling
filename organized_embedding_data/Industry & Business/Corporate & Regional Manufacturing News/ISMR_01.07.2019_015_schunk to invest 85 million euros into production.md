# SCHUNK to invest 85 million euros into production sites

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

SCHUNK Intec USA in Morrisville is the largest subsidiary for gripping systems and clamping technology. The new building has a total area of 4,000 square metres.

SCHUNK is investing almost 40 million euros in its Competence Centre for Gripping Systems in Brackenheim-Hausen

WITH A BROAD-BASED investment programme, automation specialist SCHUNK is preparing for the next step of its global growth programme with around 85 million euros to be invested at locations in Brackenheim-Hausen, Mengen, St. Georgen and Morrisville in the USA by mid-2020. This will create around 42,000 square metres of additional production and administration space. It all starts with the U.S. plant in the state of North Carolina, where the new buildings were officially handed over a few days ago.

In addition to expansion of the production area, SCHUNK Intec USA created a new administrative building that, through its open architecture, is designed to 'encourage and inspire creativity and lateral thinking'. In its newly-created Customer Centre, users can experience SCHUNK's components live and receive additional expertise in technology forums and workshops. Under the motto "One Team – One Family – One Future", the approximately 4,000 square metre new building expansion was inaugurated in early May with an official ceremony followed by a Family Day.

"On-site production ensures short delivery times and high flexibility, especially when it comes to specific components that are produced specifically for the American market. Today, the U.S. site plays a leading role within the group of companies for the entire North and South American market. SCHUNK has invested a total of almost 10 million euros into the expansion of the site," added the company.

In addition, 40 million euros is being put into its Competence Centre for Gripping Systems in Brackenheim-Hausen, just five km from its headquarters in Lauffen. The expansion covers an area of 22,000 square metres and represents a doubling of the existing production area. SCHUNK is investing a further 30 million euros into its Competence Centre for Lathe Chuck Technology and Stationary Workholding in Mengen, in the district of Sigmaringen. Here, 12,000 square metres are to be added for production, research and development.

Five million euros were invested at its St. Georgen site in the Black Forest, where the production area was doubled with an increase of 4,200 square metres. The Chief Executive Officer, Henrik A. Schunk, sees the total volume of 85 million euros as a consistent investment in the future of the company.

"In the coming years, we will experience a boom in automation and digitisation worldwide and we'll only be able to handle this by having the right capacities," said the CEO. Henrik Schunk expects high growth rates, especially for mechatronic and increasingly intelligent clamping devices and gripping systems.

SCHUNK also recently announced its cooperation with AnotherBrain, one of the world's leading specialists in Artificial Intelligence. Schunk operates 34 directly owned subsidiaries and nine plants around the world. See www.gb.SCHUNK.com
