# 123 Insight confirms new appointments

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 ISMR]

123 INSIGHT LIMITED has appointed Simon Badger as managing director (designate) and Emma Richards as customer services manager.

Formerly a 123insight user in 2003, Simon Badger has a degree in Business Studies and joined 123 Insight Ltd. in 2011 as the first customer care manager and was promoted to customer services manager in 2015. Emma Richards joined the company in 2014 as part of the customer care team with a wealth of experience gained over ten years and having held several roles within various manufacturing business.

Guy Amoroso, co-founder of the company, will continue in the role of managing director until 2020, working alongside Simon Badger during this period. After the changeover, he will take on the role of Chairman.
