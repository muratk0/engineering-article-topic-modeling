# 20-year anniversary for GSS CFO

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

GLOBAL SHOP SOLUTIONS, a leading producer of ERP software for manufacturing companies around the globe, has announced that its Chief Financial Officer, Barry Klein, has reached an impressive 20-year anniversary with the company.

Klein joined Global Shop Solutions in 2000 after serving as Corporate Counsel for Perry Homes, a home-builder in the state of Texas. In addition to overseeing the financial side of Global Shop Solutions, Klein also manages the accounting team, including tax and regulatory compliance. According to company CEO & President, Dusty Alexander, Klein's contributions to the company over the past twenty years go far beyond expectations.

“We hired Barry because he had everything we were looking for in a CFO – a business degree, a law degree, a customer-first attitude and previous manufacturing ownership experience," said Alexander. “However, we had no idea at the time he would play such a key role in our global growth over the past 20 years.

“Barry led the way in helping Global Shop Solutions become a truly global organisation by setting up our international corporations in the U.K., Mexico, Singapore and New Zealand. He also oversees the purchasing and management of the technologies we need to keep our business and our customers at the forefront of the ERP industry."

The company's headquarters in The Woodlands, Texas, includes an R&D facility and Global Shop Solutions training centre. Through its offices in the U.S., Mexico, Indonesia, Singapore, Australia, New Zealand and the United Kingdom, the company supports thousands of manufacturing facilities in over 25 countries and nearly 30 industries.
