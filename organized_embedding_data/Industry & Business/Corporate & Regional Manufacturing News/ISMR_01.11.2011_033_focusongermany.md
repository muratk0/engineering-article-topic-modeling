# FOCUSONGERMANY

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

Schuler now offers its POWERSAVE services for the energy optimisation of press systems – regardless of brand. Schuler’s POWERSAVE experts can show customers exactly which areas of the existing line offer energy-saving potential. Customers can then choose either individual measures or a complete concept of tailored solutions for optimising energy. Following this, the machines are made fit for the future in terms of energy consumption – thus ensuring that press systems are optimised for energy efficiency throughout their life cycle.

“A Schuler environmental certificate documents the benefit for the environment. After all, energy savings not only have a positive effect on a company’s balance sheets, but also on its corporate image. In future, major corporations in particular will pay increasing attention to the commitment of their partners with regard to environmental protection,” Schuler told ISMR.

## NEW TWIN-ROD HIGH-SPEED BLANKING PRESS

With a performance rating of 300 strokes per minute, the new MCF twin-rod high-speed blanking press not only offers considerably more speed, but also increases productivity. The high-speed press from Beutler Nova, the Schuler Group’s Swiss subsidiary, also features a special slide guiding system which guarantees a high degree of precision during blanking.

“We’ve already designed and built numerous customised cells with similar performance specifications on request. We have now taken the decisive next step: with immediate effect, we are offering fast blanking cells with up to 300 strokes per minute and press forces of 630 kN to 2,000 kN,” explained Benedikt Schenker, Technical Director of Beutler Nova. “The new high-speed blanking presses are particularly suitable for the production of precise parts with a high cutting accuracy”.

The slide’s pre-loaded and play-free roller bearing guiding guarantees a high degree of precision during blanking. This enables the production of small parts with tight die clearances and a wide variety of parts in quick succession.
