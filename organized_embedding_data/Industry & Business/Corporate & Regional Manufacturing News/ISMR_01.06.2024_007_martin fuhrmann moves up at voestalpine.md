# Martin Fuhrmann moves up at voestalpine

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

After almost 20 years with the voestalpine Group, Martin Fuhrmann (51) has joined the Management Board team of its High-Performance Metals Division on 1 April 2024. He is now responsible for the division's sales and service area, which includes around 75 companies at over 130 locations on five continents.

Fuhrmann has taken over the responsibilities of Reinhard Nöbauer who has been appointed to the Management Board of voestalpine AG and is now head of the High Performance Metals Division. The High-Performance Metals Division specialises in the production and processing of tools and special steels and generated revenue of 3.8 billion euros in the 2022/23 business year, with around 13,700 employees.

After completing his studies in social and economic sciences at the Vienna University of Economics and Business Administration (WU), Martin Fuhrmann began his professional career in the retail division of a large European electronics retailer and then moved to the Austrian branch of a leading global logistics company as Head of Controlling. In 2005, he joined the former Böhler-Uddeholm IT subsidiary “Intesy” as Head of Controlling. Between 2008 and 2014, he held various management positions in the voestalpine Böhler Welding Group. In 2014, Martin Fuhrmann took over as managing director of voestalpine High Performance Metals International GmbH and head of the International Region within the Sales and Service Division.

The voestalpine group is a leading steel and technology group which operates globally and has around 500 group companies. It is also a specialist in railway systems, tool steel and special sections. It has a plan for decarbonising steel production with its greentec steel programme. In the business year 2022/23, the Group generated revenue of 8.2 billion euros, with an operating result (EBITDA) of 2.5 billion euros. It has around 51,200 employees worldwide.■
