# Amada GmbH

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

www.amada.eu

AMADA offers a range of sheet-metal solutions including laser-cutting; punching; bending; laser welding; shearing; combination and grinding machines. It also develops software, Industry 4.0 and automation systems. At EuroBLECH, it will present its latest highlights from the world of sheet-metal processing, with live demonstrations shown throughout the event.

“We offer a fully comprehensive range of solutions for sheet-metal processing. More than just a manufacturer of machine tools, we offer innovative solutions to boost the technological edge of our customers," it explained.

Established in 1946 in Japan by Isamu Amada, the AMADA Group now has some 90 companies including sales branches, production sites and employs over 8,000 people worldwide. Combining all activities, the AMADA Group operates in Europe with ten branches in 13 countries and eight production sites, employing 1,500 people serving approximately 30,000 customers.

## CMS SpA

## www.scmgroup.com/en/cmsmetal

CMs Metal Technology offers complete waterjet-cutting systems, pressure intensifiers and dry or wet deburring and satin finishing machines. Since the 1990s, thanks to the acquisition of Tecnocut and constant internal developments, CMS has completed over 1,500 installations worldwide and become a partner of leading industries in sectors such as automotive, aerospace, machining, furniture and industrial architecture.

"Tecnocut Aquatec is an advanced, versatile, highperformance abrasive waterjet cutting system, able to accommodate the most diverse production demands in different application fields. The machine consists of a mobile bridge moving along separate high-thickness steel supports, fastened to the floor and providing outstanding structural rigidity. This structure delivers high performances, even for the most demanding tasks," it explained.

In a separate initiative, CMS has also devised a JetShield solution to keep the circuit constantly at cutting pressure, even when loading and unloading the material to be cut "without compromising conditions of maximum safety for the operator". Stand G16

## Bystronic

## www.bystronic.com

"We are launching a groundbreaking software solution for sheet-metal manufacturing customers. With the new BySoft Suite, customers can holistically digitalise their business, from quote to delivery," Swiss sheet metal specialist, Bystronic, told ISMR.

## Combilift

## www.combilift.com

Irish specialist manufacturer, Combilift, will be exhibiting a selection of models from its extensive range of material-handling solutions at EuroBLECH. Products range from small pedestrian stacker trucks to the Combi-SC Straddle Carrier, which can lift loads of over 100t.

“Whatever you need to move around production facilities, yards or warehouses, we have a suitable solution, designed and engineered to our ethos of safe, space-saving and productive handling," it explained.

The machines on the stand will reflect the diversity of Combilift's offering: the original multidirectional forklifts of the C-Series, with lift capacities from two up to 25t, will be joined by the Combi-CB (a compact, counterbalance design, multidirectional truck). Pedestrian-operated trucks are now a popular replacement for ride-on forklifts and Combilift offers a number of these, featuring its patented awardwinning multi-position tiller arm designed for safer operation, maximum operator visibility and narrow aisle performance.

## Hypertherm Associates

Stand H80 (also Hall 13, stand E158)

LVD is a leading manufacturer of sheet-metalworking equipment including laser cutting systems; punch presses; press brakes; guillotine shears and automation systems, integrated to and supported by its CADMAN®software suite. ## Inspecvision

## www.inspecvision.com

InspecVision specialises in automated 2D and 3D measurement systems for inspection and reverse engineering of sheet metal parts at every stage of production (flat, folded, welded or assembled). OEMs and sub-contract manufacturers use InspecVision technology for quality control, improving efficiency and reducing waste.

"The systems are high speed, low maintenance, can be supported remotely and are available in a wide range of sizes and accuracies. Products include Planar 2D, the world's fastest measurement tool for 2D parts, and Opti-Scan 3D (the world's first 3D inspection system that can measure surfaces and edges in 3D)," it told ISMR.

InspecVision will showcase, for the first time at EuroBLECH, its new Area of Interest (AOI) upgrade which measures countersinks, bevels, chamfers, blind holes and surface features. InspecVision is a TRUMPF Partner and was recently awarded the prestigious UK 'Queen's Award for Enterprise in International Trade'.
