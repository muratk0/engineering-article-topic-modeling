# MATE SPEARHEADS EUROPEAN SALES

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

## NEW FOIL SPECIALIST

INNOVAL TECHNOLOGY, a leading provider of independent technical expertise to the global downstream aluminium industry, has confirmed the appointment of Vicente Martin to its Process Improvement team.

Vicente joins the company from Inasa Foil in Spain where he held the position of Technology Manager. Having also previously held this position at Alcoa Sabinanigo, Vicente brings a wealth of experience in foil production and products to Innoval Technology.

MATE PRECISION Tooling, a leading manufacturer of CNC punch press tooling, has announced the appointment of Arkadiusz (Arkady) Skolyszewski to the position of European Sales Manager.

In announcing the appointment, Jim Thomas, Vice President of Mate's International Sales, confirmed that Mr. Skolyszewski will be responsible for dealer and direct sales for Mate in Poland, the Baltics, CIS countries, Ukraine, Russia, Belarus, Czech Republic, Bulgaria, Hungary, Rumania, Slovakia, Slovenia (Balkans) and Israel.

"Arkady has a proven track record for delivering international growth and he understands the needs of customers internationally," stated Mr. Thomas. "His appointment will further accelerate Mate's growth providing products and services to important European manufacturing industries. He will also serve on Mate's European Advisory Committee."

Mr. Skolyszewski has a very comprehensive sales background in Europe. Since 2005, he managed sales for Luna Export AB headquartered in Sweden. Luna is a leading branded manufacturer and distributor of machinery, tools and accessories to the major manufacturing countries of Europe. Mr. Skolyszewski has a Masters Degree in Physics from the University of Krakow and speaks fluent Russian and English. He will work from a home office in Krakow, Poland.

Founded in 1962 and headquartered in Anoka, Minnesota, Mate Precision Tooling is a leading global manufacturer of original and replacement tooling for CNC punch presses with manufacturing facilities in Minnesota and Germany. It holds numerous patents worldwide.
