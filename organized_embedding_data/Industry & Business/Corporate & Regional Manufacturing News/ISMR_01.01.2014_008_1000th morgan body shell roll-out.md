# 1000th Morgan body shell roll-out

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

UK SHEET metal powerhouse, Premier Group, reached a historic milestone recently, producing the 1,000th body shell for the Morgan Motor Car Company’s legendary Morgan 3 Wheeler. The iconic design of the Morgan 3 Wheeler has been updated with 21st century technology and Premier Group has been instrumental in assisting the car body shell design and manufacture.

In 2010 the Morgan Motor Company decided to re-visit an icon that had not been produced for 60 years, by re-engineering the much-loved Morgan 3 Wheeler. Taking inspiration from the original car but with modern technological influences, the initial plan was to build 400 Morgan 3 Wheelers but its incredible global success has now seen the production of the 1,000th Morgan 3 Wheeler.

Premier Group have been working with The Morgan Motor Company for the past five years. This particular contract has seen Premier Group work with Morgan throughout the initial design stage of the 3 Wheeler to ensure all body panels could be manufactured to the standards expected by Morgan’s loyal customer base. Premier Group also manufactures the bespoke coach-built bonnets and boots for Morgan’s other iconic production vehicles.

Based in Coventry across several major sites, Premier Group’s client list ranges from Bentley Motors, Jaguar Land Rover and The Ford Motor Company to Alstom, GKN and Bombardier. The firm’s automotive prototype production is underpinned using the latest CAD design, CAE virtual development and CAM manufacturing techniques, generating significantly reduced lead-times when it comes to production. ■
