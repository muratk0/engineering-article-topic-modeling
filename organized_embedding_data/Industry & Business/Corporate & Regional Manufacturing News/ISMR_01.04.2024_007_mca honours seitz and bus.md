# MCA honours Seitz and Bus

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

The Metal Construction Association (MCA) in the USA presented its annual Larry A. Swaney Award to Tom Seitz, and its firstever Lifetime Achievement Award to Dick Bus. The awards were announced on 30 January 2024 during MCA's 2024 Winter Meeting at the Scottsdale Plaza Resort & Villas in Scottsdale, Arizona (USA).

The Larry A. Swaney Award is bestowed upon an industry professional for contributions to the success of the association and the betterment of the metal construction industry. This year's recipient, Tom Seitz, is the former director of strategic accounts for 3A Composites Americas, where he managed business development for its largest customers.

Seitz has served extensively within MCA, including as a Board Member from 2016 until 2020. He also co-chaired the Metal Composite Material Council and chaired the Metal Composite Material Alliance. In January 2020, Seitz became the executive director of the Metal Composite Material (MCM) Alliance.

"Our new Lifetime Achievement Award was created to recognize an individual’s lifetime contributions and accomplishments to the association and metal construction industry. Dick Bus earned the award for the breadth and significance of his involvement and impact on the metal construction industry,“ commented the MCA.

"Bus is the President of ATAS International and served on MCAs Board for 18 years, including as Vice Chair from 2000 to 2006 and Chair from 2007 to 2009. Bus's accomplishments include playing a major role in the growth of the U.S. and Canadian residential metal roofing industry. Bus was also instrumental in the creation of the Metal Roofing Alliance," added the association.

In 2013, Bus was inducted into the Metal Roofing Hall of Fame. In 2017, he received the MCA Triumph Award and was recently named as one of the “Power 100” by the Lehigh Valley Business Journal. ■

Brenden O'Brien, Regional Sales Manager, AIDA-America.
