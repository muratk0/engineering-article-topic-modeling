Last month the FMA board of directors announced the election of a new chairman, a new member, and a re-elected member.

Gregg Simpson is FMA’s incoming chairman. Gregg has been president of Ohio Laser since 2001, which specializes in higher-tolerance engineered solutions to specific fabricated metal parts. He is also president of Ten.Six Industries Inc, a company that provides technical and managerial knowledge to industrial laser material processing users.

Gregg is a valued member of the FMA’s Industrial Laser Technology Council. He has spoken at and chaired several FMA conferences.

Phil Kooima has been re-elected as an FMA board of directors member. Phil started Kooima Company in 1988, which provides fabrications for the construction, agriculture, and recreational vehicle industries. This is Phil’s second three-year term on the, FMA board.

Scott Myran is a new FMA board of directors member. Since 1988 Scott has worked at Mississippi Welders Supply (MWS) Company and became president in 2014. MWS provides welding equipment and tools, gases, abrasives, and industrial supplies.

Scott currently serves as a distributor advisory board member for Hypertherm and a distributor focus group member for Lincoln Electric. This will be Scott’s first three-year term on the FMA board.
