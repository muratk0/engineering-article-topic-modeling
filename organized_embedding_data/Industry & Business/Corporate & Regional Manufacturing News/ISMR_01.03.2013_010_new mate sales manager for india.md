# New Mate sales manager for India

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

MATE PRECISION TOOLING, a leading manufacturer of CNC punch press tooling, has announced the appointment of Amit Pande as India Sales Manager. In announcing the appointment, Jim Thomas, vice president of Mate’s International Sales, stated that Mr. Pande will be responsible for Mate’s customer relationships in India.

Amit Pande has a comprehensive background of achievement in the Asian sheet metal industry. From 2008 through 2012, he worked as product manager of the Yawei and Prima Power punch press, press brake and laser product lines at Electronica Hitech Machines Pvt. Ltd., a branded distributor of metal cutting and metal forming machines. He speaks fluent English, Hindi and Marathi and holds a Master’s Degree in Instrumentation Science from Pune University.
