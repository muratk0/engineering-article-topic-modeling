# NEW BLOOD AT KMF

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Already reporting record turnover for the first quarter of its financial year, UK-based KMF (Precision Sheet Metal) Ltd has appointed Trevor Leadbeater (right) and Radim Vaculin (below) as business development managers. Both recruits bring extensive experience in the precision sheet metal sector.

Trevor Leadbeater’s main role will be to focus efforts to support the growth of KMF business through developing closer working relationships with customers.

Radim Vaculin’s role will focus on central Europe with emphasis on the industrial, electronics and aerospace sectors.
