# Sustainable energy management

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

EPICOR Software Corporation, a specialist in business software solutions for manufacturing, distribution, retail and services organisations, has unveiled Epicor Energy Management, formerly Epicor Carbon Connect. The rebranded Epicor Energy Management suite provides companies with the ability to identify, analyse, audit, track, manage, benchmark and report on energy consumption, environmental impact and carbon emissions.

“With economic uncertainty, organisations must have a valid means of measuring energy management initiatives to justify continued investment. Epicor Energy Management helps customers to provide automated reports that demonstrate measurable savings and meaningful measurement of sustainability programmes. This validation translates to justification of further investment and expenditures to support environmental sustainability,” said the manufacturer.

As part of the next generation enterprise resource planning (ERP) solution, Epicor Energy Management is available on-premise or on-demand. This solution allows companies to calculate their expected emissions output and budget for their environmental cost factors. For more information, see www.epicor.com/Solutions/Pages/Energy-Management.aspx ■

## Cassette-type roll former

CHIN MINN started machinery design and manufacture thirty years ago. Its first roll forming machine (for steel ball slides) was designed and manufactured in 1983. Since then, it has delivered hundreds of roll forming machines to customers all over the world. The majority of ball slides and undermount slide makers in Taiwan and China use Chin Minn machinery, it told ISMR.

Chin Minn is now a leading manufacturer of roll forming machines for draw slides, or steel ball slides. From strip material loaded onto the decoiler, it carefully designs all the rollers (from entrance guide rollers, forming rollers and straightening rollers, to punch and cut-off dies).

“If a customer wants to run different cross sections on the same machine, we have designed a cassette-type roll forming machine. This cassette-type of roll forming machine has divided roller stands into three sections, and each set of roller stands are bolted onto a steel plate. For roller change, you simply lift each steel plate which holds several roller stands. You then move and change another cassette of rollers to the machine – dowel pins confirm the mounting positions. The change is fast and easy,” Chin Minn explained.
