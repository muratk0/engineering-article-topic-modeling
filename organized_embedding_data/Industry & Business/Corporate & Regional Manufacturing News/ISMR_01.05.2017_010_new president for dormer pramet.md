# New President for Dormer Pramet

[TARİH: 01.05.2017 ISMR]

STEFAN STEENSTRUP HAS been appointed the new President of global cutting tool manufacturer, Dormer Pramet. Previously the company's global sales director, he took over from Fabrizio Resmini, who retired on 1 January 2017.

Having worked for the Sandvik Group for the last 20 years, Stefan has held various senior positions after initially joining Dormer as technical specialist. In 2007, he became managing director of Dormer Italy, before moving into the company's global management team.

Stefan, who speaks six different languages, was previously director of business development at Dormer Pramet following the merger of Dormer and Pramet Tools in 2014, becoming global sales director one year later. Originally from Denmark, Stefan, aged 46, will continue to be based in Milan, Italy.

Olympic Steel promotes Brace Moran

OLYMPIC STEEL INC., a metals service centre in the US, has promoted Brace Moran to general manager of Integrity Stainless – Latrobe, effective from 3 April 2017.

"With the rapid growth and increasing market share experienced by our Specialty Metals' segment, we have created a GM position to specifically oversee our Latrobe facility," said Andy Markowitz, President – Specialty Metals. "In his new role, Brace will be responsible for continuing the profitable growth of our stainless steel business. In addition, he will oversee stainless steel flat bar products, which have been recently re-deployed to Latrobe. We look forward to his future contributions in this new role."

Jerry Gideon remains general manager of the Integrity Stainless – Streetsboro, Ohio, facility, a position he has held since 2014. Both Gideon and Moran will report to Markowitz. See www.olysteel.com
