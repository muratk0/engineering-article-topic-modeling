# AIDA confirms senior management appointments

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA) has announced that Mr. Toshihiko Suzuki was recently promoted to global CEO of AIDA ENGINEERING, LTD. in Japan. He joined AIDA in 2011. Mr. Suzuki has held numerous engineering, sales, customer service and executive officer positions, both at AIDA ENGINEERING and sister divisions, throughout his tenure.

Additionally, Ms. Kiyoe Kado was recently appointed as AIDA ENGINEERING's first female director. She serves as an Outside Director. Ms. Kado is Director of Trust Forum Foundation and Professor Emeritus at Rikkyo University College. She specialises in corporate, financial and general business law.

AIDA is a global specialist in the design; manufacture; sale; service and support; refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metalforming automation equipment. It has a worldwide network of sales and service locations in nearly 40 cities across 20 countries and press installations exceeding 80,000 units in over 60 countries.
