# New Acting President for MTA

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

The UK's Manufacturing Technologies Association (MTA) has appointed NIKKEN's CEO and President, Tony Bowkett, as its latest Acting President. He aspires to promote and develop the MTA to engage and network with its members and associates “to ensure that UK manufacturing technologies are recognised as critical parts of government economic strategy."

"With more than 30 years of experience as managing director and founder of NIKKEN Kosakusho Europe Ltd, Tony is responsible for the success of NIKKEN's activities across Europe and beyond. This includes multiple subsidiary companies within the NIKKEN Europe Group, together operating in over 23 countries," NIKKEN told ISMR.

Tony Bowkett is a former financial director and vice president of the MTA. He succeeds outgoing MTA President, Dave Burley, and is a staunch advocate for the manufacturing technologies sector. He is a founder industrial partner and non-executive industrial Board member of Sheffield's Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre (AMRC) and has also held other advisory and voluntary roles.

"It is a great honour and privilege to serve our association members, and we now proudly represent over 300 companies. Together, we generate an annual turnover of more than £2 billion and employ over 20,000 people collaborating with a vast UK supply chain, generating many millions in real wealth for the UK economy. Through our live MACH exhibition, education, training, legislation and standards teams, we aspire to reach all of the UK's manufacturing industries. During my presidency, I would like to make it my mission to engage with our members and represent their best interests on local, national and international platforms," commented Tony Bowkett.■
