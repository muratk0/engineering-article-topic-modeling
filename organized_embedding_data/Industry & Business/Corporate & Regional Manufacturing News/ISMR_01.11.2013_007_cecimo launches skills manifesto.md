# CECIMO launches skills manifesto

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

CECIMO (the European Association of Machine Tool Industries) has published a manifesto to identify the skills-related needs of the European machine tool industry and put forward recommendations to respond to new challenges facing the sector. The manifesto has been drawn up following enquiries and workshops organised among machine tool companies and associations represented at the CECIMO General Assembly in June 2013. These recommendations primarily address regional, national and European authorities, as well as education and training suppliers who can provide essential support to the companies’ existing activities to develop skills.

“The European machine tool industry is a global leader. Following an 8% output growth in 2012, Europe’s share in global production is forecasted to increase from 31% to 34% in 2013. European exports have also reached an all-time-high level in 2012, to 18.8 million euros. The secret behind the performance of European machine tool builders is a combination of engineering know-how and a highly skilled workforce which enable a strong innovation capacity and quality production tailored to customer needs,” explained Filip Geerts, CECIMO Director General. However, he points out that the skills issue has recently been high on the agenda, as the narrowing down of the skills pipeline in Europe risks hindering growth and innovation in the sector.

As modern machine tools are characterised by a high level of technology integration, technical skills requirements in machine tool companies are becoming more demanding. As the machine tool business is increasingly globalised, companies need to connect to global supply chains and serve customers globally. This requires beefing up the skills set of the existing and future workforce with new competences in management, marketing, languages and communication. The CECIMO manifesto calls for adapting education and training programmes to build occupational and transversal skills which respond to the needs of the 21st century. Overall, the recommendations’ focus is on improving the skills supply in Europe quantity-wise and quality-wise.

CECIMO brings together 15 national associations of machine tool builders which represent approximately 1500 industrial enterprises in Europe, over 80% of which are SMEs. CECIMO covers 98% of total machine tool production in Europe and about one third worldwide. Email information@cecimo.eu for further details.
