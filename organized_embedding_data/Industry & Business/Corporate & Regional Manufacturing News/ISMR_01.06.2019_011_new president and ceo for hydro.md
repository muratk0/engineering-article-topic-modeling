# New President and CEO for Hydro

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 ISMR]

HILDE MERETE AASHEIM has assumed her position as Hydro's new President and CEO, aiming to raise profitability and accelerate the company's drive to become a leading provider of advanced, low-carbon products and solutions.

Aasheim introduced several measures in support of her business agenda, which include renewing the company's corporate management board, restructuring and strategic review of the Rolled Products business area, ensuring stricter capital discipline and capital allocation as well as revitalising on-going improvement programmes and identifying new improvement targets in all business areas and for personnel. A reinforced improvement agenda for the company will be developed and presented during the third quarter.

"While we remain optimistic about the longterm prospects for aluminium and Hydro as the world moves towards the low-carbon, circular economy, we must acknowledge the challenges that must be addressed to strengthen our performance and cash generation," said Aasheim, adding that Alunorte must be returned to full production as soon as possible to allow for normal operations in Brazil.

"We will increase our targets for operational excellence and ensure stricter financial discipline going forward, and we will explore all options to improve earnings from Rolled Products for the future," she added.

Eivind Kallevik will take over as head of primary metal after Hilde Merete Aasheim, effective from 15 August 2019. Einar Glomnes, former head of global joint ventures in Primary Metal, is replacing head of rolled products Kjetil Ebbesberg, effective immediately, while Pål Kildemo will take over as CFO after Eivind Kallevik, effective from 15 August 2019. In the interim period, Kildemo will be acting head of primary metal in addition to his current role as head of finance in primary metal.

According to Aasheim, Hydro will also increase its efforts on advanced, low-carbon aluminium products and solutions, based upon a more sustainable footprint through the value chain and life-cycle of aluminium.

"Customers are increasingly seeking advanced solutions with the lowest possible impact, from building and construction to automotive, packaging and electronics. This enables us to commercialise our best-in-class footprint and leading positions within process technology and product innovation," explained Aasheim.

"Hydro is already the only aluminium company able to provide the global building and construction sector with solutions containing at least 75 per cent post-consumer scrap through its certified 75R alloy, and also offers low-carbon primary aluminium with a maximum CO₂ footprint of 4 kilos per kilo aluminium."

Automated transfer unit

Misati names new UK technical sales manager

MISATI, THE SPANISH manufacturer of standard elements to automate transfer presses, recently appointed John Walker as its new representative in the UK. John has over 40 years' experience in stamping technology and dealt with OEMs such as Nissan, Toyota, JLR and General Motors.

"He is a fully qualified engineer and is equally at home dealing with tooling managers, tooling buyers, tooling designers and process/production engineers, ensuring Misati offer a full technical and commercial support package to all areas of our customers' requirements," commented the company.

With this appointment, Misati wants to offer a better and closer service to its English customers, including technical assessment in the engineering (design of the end-of-arm tooling), assembly and running-in phase of a transfer. Misati is a specialist in automating 3-axis transfer presses. Founded in 1985, the company designs and manufactures pneumatic grippers and structural elements in its factory near Barcelona, Spain.
