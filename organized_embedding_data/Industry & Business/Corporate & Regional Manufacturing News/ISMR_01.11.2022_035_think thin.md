# THINK THIN

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

"The new EXN coil-fed punching machine and coil management app were two highlights of DALLAN's booth at EuroBLECH in Hanover."

At this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, DALLAN's CEO unveiled its latest products, new initiatives and 2023 plans to ISMR.

DALLAN SpA has been designing, producing and installing advanced systems to manufacture products from steel coil, including punching machines, rollformers, laser-cutting and packaging machines. The family-owned company operates worldwide on all five continents across 83 countries. Its machinery is used in the manufacture of profiles for applications in sectors such as interior finishing (false ceilings); sun shading (Venetian blinds and roller shutters); HVAC (heating, ventilation and air conditioning); metal furniture and lighting, amongst others.

The Northern Italian manufacturer's specialisation in pre-painted and pre-finished materials means that it can study and create, with its customers, tailor-made equipment for manufacturing punched and folded sheet metal products. This equipment is supplied complete with assembly and packaging systems, and Dallan continues to innovate in coil-processing and rollforming markets.

The company will celebrate its 45th anniversary in 2023. At this year's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany, its CEO, Andrea Dallan, unveiled its latest products, new initiatives and 2023 plans to ISMR.

ISMR: What are you promoting here on your booth at EuroBLECH in Hanover, Germany?

Andrea Dallan (AD): This year, we have a lot of technologies to present. Over the past few years, we have focused on the topic of efficiency so here, at EuroBLECH, we wanted to provide tools that really put efficiency in focus.

We are well known for roll forming thin sheet metal. However, we are presenting technology here at EuroBLECH for coil-fed punching which we are convinced has been a little overlooked over the past few years. In fact, our experience with this technology has been very successful because it enables manufacturers to generate huge savings in raw materials. Raw material prices have been rising so we have received a lot of requests from companies who want to make better use of the material that they buy. This has moved the focus of the market onto coil-fed punching and laser cutting.

At the last EuroBLECH show, we showcased our laser-cutting machine from coil and, at this event, we wanted to highlight the new DALLAN EXN coil-fed punching machine. We have redesigned it from scratch and have cooperated with Siemens in Germany to develop the first EXN coil-fed punching machine with Siemens drives, controls and motors. Here, on the stand, you can see the new servo-electric DALLAN EXN with Siemens controls.
