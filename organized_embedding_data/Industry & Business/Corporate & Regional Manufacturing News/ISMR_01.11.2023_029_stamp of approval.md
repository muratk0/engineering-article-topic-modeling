# STAMP OF APPROVAL

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Werner Borth, CSO, Nidec SYS GmBH, spoke to ISMR about the company's new investment plans and latest initiatives.

ISMR SAYS:

“The Nidec Press & Automation brand brings together Minster, Arisa, Kyori, Vamco, CHS, SYS and Automatic Feed products to the market, established a single-source solution for machinery, services and technology. The Nidec Press & Automation brand brings together Minster; Arisa; Kyori; Vamco; CHS; SYS and, most recently, Automatic Feed Company products to the market, allowing combined synergies to offer efficient solutions to manufacturers.

NP&A group companies include more than 1,400 employees from 13 locations worldwide. The group operates a shared global customer service network with major service centres in Minster, Ohio (USA); Mexico; Germany, Spain and China. This group of companies represents a combined 526 years of manufacturing experience and is a full-service pressroom provider for businesses in more than 90 countries and on six continents.

In 2019, Nidec Minster Corporation acquired 100% ownership of privately owned German company, Systeme + Steuerungen GmbH and its group companies (collectively 'SYS'). SYS is well known in the market for its high-speed, precision feeding equipment for presses. With 130 employees, Nidec SYS products include high-speed servo feeds, related automation like in-die cutting and welding; spindle presses; integrated controls with production management and monitoring software along with specialised high-speed press remanufacturing.

ISMR: Please highlight any particular successes and achievements, as well as any significant targets reached, over the past two years (of which you are particularly proud)?

WB: SYS is really growing and in a very exciting time right now. We are in the process of a significant campus expansion at our facility in Grafenau, Germany, while also launching several new products to the EMEA market (such as our Kyori high-speed presses). I am proud that SYS is a key player in the global growing EV/lamination market.

ISMR: Which investments have you made over the past two-three years (machine tools, factory, staff etc.)?

WB: We have embarked on a 16-million-euro 3,600 square metre campus expansion to increase both our production and support staff spaces. We have also introduced new milling machinery and initiated new production processes to maximize shop efficiency.
