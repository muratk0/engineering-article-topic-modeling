# About the Liebherr Group

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

The Liebherr Group is a family-run technology company with a widely diversified product programme. The company is one of the largest construction equipment manufacturers in the world. It also provides high-quality, user-oriented products and services in a wide range of other areas. The Liebherr Group includes over 150 companies across all continents. In 2023, it employed more than 50,000 staff and achieved combined revenues of over 14 billion euros. Under the slogan ‘75 years of moving forward’, the Group is celebrating its 75th anniversary in 2024.

## About ARKU

ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH was founded in 1928 as a family-owned company. It is a global specialist in roller leveller and press feeding technology with nearly 60 years of experience. It offers an extensive range of high-capacity and precision levellers, as well as deburring and edge-rounding machines plus parts handling solutions for levelling and deburring machines. With its headquarters in Baden-Baden, Germany, and ISO-certified facilities in Cincinnati (USA) and Kunshan (China), the company operates in more than 30 countries worldwide.
