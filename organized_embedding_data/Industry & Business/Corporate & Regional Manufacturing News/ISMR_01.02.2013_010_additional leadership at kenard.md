# Additional leadership at Kenard

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

Kenard Engineering Ltd, a privately-owned precision engineering company based in the UK, has confirmed the appointment of Terry Paterson as managing director of the company’s Tewkesbury facility. This is a new position within the company and the appointment will support and strengthen the existing management structure at the facility and is a further demonstration of Kenard’s growing status as a leading provider of a full range of manufacturing and engineering services across a wide range of industries.

Paterson has a wealth of experience in the aerospace industry, backed by 18 years of senior management experience working with major organisations in the UK as well as international assignments in Europe and North America.

Keith Ellis, managing director of the Kenard Engineering Group, commented: “As an accomplished practitioner of lean manufacturing, Just-in-Time manufacturing, DLF, Kanban and Total Quality Management, Terry brings more than theory to Kenard; he comes as someone with a wealth of real-world experience in a range of best practice manufacturing solutions.”

Established in 1964, Kenard Engineering Group is a sub-contract precision manufacturing organisation operating two manufacturing sites in the UK. Kenard’s strategy is to balance its customer base across four industry sectors - aerospace, defence, communications and oil and gas. The Kenard Group also operates a software division which operates under the brand Seiki Systems Ltd. ■
