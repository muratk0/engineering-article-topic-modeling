# SigmaTEK names new VP

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

SigmaTEK Systems, LLC, a leading CAD/CAM nesting solutions provider, has confirmed the appointment of Dan Barr as the new VP of Sales and Marketing of SigmaTEK. In his role with SigmaTEK, Dan is responsible for guiding the overall strategic direction and organisational leadership of sales and marketing activities for the company.

"Dan brings a unique combination of solid leadership, proven success and enthusiasm," explained SigmaTEK President and CEO, Ben TerreBlanche. "People are the lifeblood of any organisation. While we certainly continually strive for technical superiority it is the quality of our people that truly enables SigmaTEK to solidify its market leadership position. We welcome Dan to the organization and look forward to his contributions in the coming years."

SigmaTEK Systems, LLC develops and sells SigmaNEST, a comprehensive software solution for nesting, NC programming and cutting of wood, steel sheet, plate and tube materials. SigmaNEST is a leading CAD/CAM nesting system for plasma, laser, punch, oxyfuel, waterjet, router, knife, tube/pipe and combination cutting machines.
