# AP&T has new CEO

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

AFTER 20 YEARS, Tord Andersson is handing over the reins at sheet metal forming specialist, AP&T, to Magnus Baarman, the former CEO of Frauenthal Automotive GmbH.

"We have experienced fantastic development at AP&T since the company was founded in 1989 and are now taking additional strides. We have an aggressive marketing plan for the next three years. We have our sights set on improving our sales by 50 per cent to reach SEK 1.5 billion. With strong support from Fairford, our principal owner, we decided in 2013 to make our largest investment ever in a new facility in Ulricehamn. It is under construction and will be completed this year. It feels like a good time to hand over the reins now," explained Tord Andersson.

During Andersson's time as CEO, AP&T progressed from being a local Swedish manufacturer of automation, presses and tools to a world leader in the market. One main reason for the company's success is its targeted investment in press hardening for the automotive industry.

Magnus Baarman’s most recent position was CEO of Frauenthal Automotive GmbH in Vienna. He has worked at the Frauenthal Group since 2003 – the past four years as the CEO of subsidiary Frauenthal Automotive. Baarman, who was born in Helsinki, Finland, has previously held leading positions at Imatra Steel Group and began his career at Ovako Steel, a Nordic steel group. ■
