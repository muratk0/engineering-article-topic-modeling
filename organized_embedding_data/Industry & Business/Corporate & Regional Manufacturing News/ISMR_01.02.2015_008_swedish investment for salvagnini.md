# Swedish investment for Salvagnini

[TARİH: 01.02.2015 ISMR]

THE SALVAGNINI Group has decided to make an investment in Sweden by building a new modern and functional head office for Salvagnini Scandinavia AB. Since 1988, the Swedish branch has completed approximately 300 installations on its territory.

“This Northern European branch is managing sales for Sweden, Denmark, Norway and Iceland as well as customer service for Salvagnini machines and systems in Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Iceland, Finland and the Baltic countries. The company has a total of 23 employees,” said Salvagnini.

Salvagnini Scandinavia offers technical assistance and a range of spare parts. The new building, with a total of 1000 sqm with offices on two floors, is twice as big as the former office and offers a new and dedicated training area as well as a showroom for metal samples, panels and machines. The inauguration ceremony of the new branch will be held on 5-7 May 2015, at the same time as the local “Värnamo Industriexpo” event. www.salvagnini.se
