# Porsche raises turnover and results

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

IN THE first quarter 2016, Porsche increased its turnover, results and deliveries compared to the previous year. The sports car manufacturer improved its turnover to 5.4 billion euros (+6%) and operating results to 896 million euros (+17%). Deliveries reached 55,974 vehicles (+10%). The operating return on sales was 16.7%. Porsche therefore believes it is 'the most profitable car manufacturer in the world'.

"Our aim is not to achieve record deliveries. We have our sights firmly placed on value-added growth based on respectable returns and safe jobs," affirmed Oliver Blume, Chairman of the Executive Board, Porsche AG. At the end of the first quarter 2016, the work force had grown to 25,081 employees. One year ago, this figure was 22,945 (+9%).

Lutz Meschke, Deputy Chairman of the Executive Board and Member of the Executive Board responsible for Finance and IT, is convinced that Porsche will again achieve its strategic profitability target of 15% by the end of Fiscal Year 2016. He expects a slight increase in deliveries and turnover for the entire Fiscal Year 2016, with results on a par with the previous year – despite very high levels of site investments, development expenditure and its billion-euro investment in the first pure battery-powered Porsche model, the Mission E.

Porsche will also soon be producing all two-door sports cars at its headquarters, with the successful production launch of the third generation of the 718 Cayman mid-engine sport coupé that will be produced at the Stuttgart plant. Up to mid-August, the company will continue to use its production capacities at the Volkswagen plant in Osnabrück, where the mid-engine sport coupé's previous generation has been manufactured to date.

Manufacturing of all two-door sports cars at the Porsche headquarters will ramp up production there to around 240 vehicles per day by August. Currently, more than 220 units come off the assembly line each day in Zuffenhausen.

## New venue for LAMIERA

THE EIGHTEENTH edition of the LAMIERA sheet metal exhibition, attended by ISMR at BolognaFiere from 11–14 May 2016, registered a 10% increase in the number of visits (compared to the 2014 show). There were 20,395 visits officially registered at the show, 857 of which were from outside Italy.

LAMIERA 2017 will change its venue and date in the international schedule of trade shows, choosing Milan and odd (rather than even) years. From 17-20 May 2017, the modern exhibition centre of Fieramilano Rho will host LAMIERA, said the organiser, and the event will feature new exhibition areas focused on products and solutions related to metal forming technologies which do not yet have dedicated events in Italy (e.g. fasteners and Blech Italy Service, which is centred on technical subcontracting and services to the industry).

"Operators were attracted by the product offering from 386 companies, of whom 30% were from outside Italy, covering an exhibition area of 35,000 square metres," the organiser told ISMR. Lamiera is promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the Italian machine tool, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association, and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU.

Lamiera's Lambda 4.0 showcase this year at LAMIERA was dedicated to illustrating the commitment of the sector to the study of Industry 4.0. The comprehensive conference programme involved 400 operators. Among the workshops were themes such as "The Smart Factory" and "Machines for Laser Processing: Industry 4.0 is Here!"

There were also numerous in-depth discussions on economic-technical topics including a presentation of the results from a survey carried out by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE every ten years on "Total machine tools and production systems in the Italian industry. Focus Emilia-Romagna" and of the competitiveness study, which compares systems and industries in Italy and Germany. For further details, see www.lamiera.net.
