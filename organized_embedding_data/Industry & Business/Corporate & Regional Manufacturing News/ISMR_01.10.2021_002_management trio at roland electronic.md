# Management trio at Roland Electronic

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Roland Electronic GmbH is a leading international provider of highly specialised sensor systems used in factory automation and quality control. Its products and solutions are used in the automotive industry and by their suppliers, as well as for metal packaging; sheet metal and tube processing; household appliances; wire and cable; tyres; pharmaceuticals; batteries and photovoltaics.

Joining the existing managing partners (Joachim Manz and Ralf Wilms), Marcus Bartle (48) started at the company in January this year as a further managing partner.

"This rejuvenation at management level is an important step to secure the future of the company." said Ralf Wilms and Joachim Manz. From 2012 to 2019, Marcus Bartle was at ARKU Maschinenbau (machine builders) GmbH in Baden-Baden as managing director with a focus on sales and technology.

Mr. Jahnke, in charge of technical management at Roland Electronic for over two years now, was recently nominated for the additional post of chief technology officer (CTO) and member of the management.
