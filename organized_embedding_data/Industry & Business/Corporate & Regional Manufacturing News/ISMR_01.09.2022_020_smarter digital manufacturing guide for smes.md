# Smarter digital manufacturing guide for SMEs

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

A digital transformation specialist has partnered with a leading alliance of trade associations to create an Industry-4.0 guide specific to the needs of its SME members.

LMAC Consulting is a certified assessor for Smart Industry Readiness Index (SIRI), the internationally recognised standard for Industry 4.0 planning and endorsed by the World Economic Forum, who tailor a Smarter 4.0 roadmap and data analytics pathway "while delivering a tangible bottom line benefit." EAMA (Engineering & Machinery Alliance) constitutes ten bodies working with government and others to strengthen the machinery and component supply chain in the UK.

The guide summarises the different pillars of Industry 4.0 and the impact that they have on costs and KPIs, as well as giving examples in action. SIRI measures a business's current maturity and prioritises the digital transformation dimensions that will have the greatest impact on an SME business, aligned to its strategic priorities.

EAMA Secretary, Jack Semple, explained: "This insightful guide is fit-for-purpose for our members and is long overdue help and advice after the delays caused by Brexit and COVID. It's both a training and educational solution that gives them quick productivity improvement wins, but also the longer-term framework for sustainable change."

LMAC CEO, Alastair Crawford, added: "Industry-4.0 principles and technology enable manufacturers to keep their advantage and grow in a market constrained by available skills and rising costs – without wasting time and misguided direction [whilst] factoring in the change management issues that arise.

“There is much to be gained from going digital in today's tougher economic climate and we'll also be giving EAMA leaders some of the skills to do it themselves. Equally, by combining our expertise and benchmarking, we can shape best-practice ideas and also look to influence funding, policy and digital support going forward," he added.

The guide is also available to interested parties and businesses that are not part of EAMA.■
