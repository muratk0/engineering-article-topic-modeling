# INVEST IN SUCCESS

[TARİH: 01.05.2014 ISMR]

visited Amada’s newly refurbished Northern European Technical Centre to view its latest sheet metal machine tool technologies

The new Technical Centre will serve as a core base from which Amada carries out its activities in the UK, Spain and Scandinavia.

an automated spare parts’ warehouse and a school for technical training were also shown to ISMR at the refurbished facility. Around 97% of its tooling for the UK market is manufactured here. The visitor experience will be boosted further with a 60-seat seminar theatre and 100-seat restaurant.

Amada sells between £5-8 million worth of pre-owned machine tools per year and has now set up a used machine tool showroom on-site. Another new unit is destined to be a refurbishment unit for pre-owned machines with its own paint shop.

The entire site refurbishment is based on an eco-friendly design boasting minimal carbon footprint due to the installation of the latest sustainable technologies such as low energy and sensor control lighting, air source heating and cooling, and thermally-efficient cladding and glazing. Interestingly, most of the sheet metal parts in its new showroom and kitchen have been fabricated by Amada customers.

“There are over 60 companies in the Amada Group. Turnover was 190 billion yen last year (1.4 billion euros). We are currently in an aggressive growth period, growing in two ways – organically, by exploiting our expanded product range, as well as through acquisition. We recently acquired Miyachi, a specialist company for automotive welding. Sheet metal is approximately 75% of our business,” Alan Parrott, Amada UK’s managing director, told ISMR.

“Our ethos is always to look long term. We have always invested in our staff. We have 160 people in the UK (over 100 are engineers) and some of these are Scandinavian and Spanish staff. We see service as the most important thing we can do for our customers. We have also provided finance for our customers in the UK since 1990s – our current finance book is 48 million euros for 80 customers (about 60% of our business). It is important to us that our customers are successful.”

Turnover in the UK was 48 million euros in 2013 – Parrott aims to boost this to 90 million euros in 2014. Amada UK now supports the Northern European block of UK, Spain and Scandinavia and has trebled turnover in Spain by putting in place direct sales teams and de-centralising service and application support in Spain. Employed by Amada UK, Spanish engineers have been living and working in the UK for a minimum of six months to hone their engineering skills and learn English.

“In terms of trends, we see increasing automation as a requirement for UK and Scandinavian markets. Labour costs are a significant factor in Scandinavia – however, in the UK market, HR issues and restrictive labour practices will boost more automation. We have significantly reduced running costs on the fibre laser with thicker fibre laser cutting – taper is the biggest issue. That is our next challenge. The edge quality is fine. By expanding our range of the products, we can offer our customers more choice,” Alan Parrott told ISMR.

He sees strong potential in countries such as Poland, Czech Republic, the Baltic countries, Spain and Turkey for further sales this year.

## Innovation in focus

Seven new sheet metal machine tool models were demonstrated to ISMR in Amada’s newly refurbished showroom. These ranged from robotic press brakes and fiber laser welding cells to high performance punch presses, laser inspection units and new CO2 and fiber laser cutting systems.

The LCG-3015 series lasers are purpose-built for productivity, based on one machine platform with a choice of CO2 or fibre laser sources. Designed for cutting a range of thin to mid-thick materials at traverse speeds of 170m/min, the machine offers a host of innovative features. The CO2 machine can process mild steel up to 20mm thick, along with 10mm stainless steel and 8mm aluminium. The LCG series is also available equipped with an Amada-developed fiber engine.
