# STAINLESS STEEL EXECUTIVE OF THE YEAR

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Antonio Marcegaglia, CEO of the Italy-based Marcegaglia group, with headquarters in Gazoldo degli Ippoliti, Mantova, has been awarded Stainless Steel Executive of the Year 2010.

A panel of experts in the sector together with Steel & Metals Market Research and Metal Bulletin gathered in Rome as part of the 9th Stainless & Special Steel Summit to award the prize to the entrepreneur at the helm of the Marcegaglia group.

The prize comes at the highest point of a growth path to success in the stainless steel sector, said Marcegaglia. A young Antonio Marcegaglia, at the start of his career, set out to create and develop the stainless steel branch within the group: Marcegaglia is regarded today as a leading global producer of stainless steel tubes.

“Marcegaglia currently manufactures and develops its stainless steel product range (austenitic and ferritic grades, duplex steel and special alloys) from seven plants in Italy, Brazil, USA, Russia and China,” Antonio Marcegaglia told ISMR.

The Gazoldo degli Ippoliti facility hosts the manufacturing lines for stainless steel cold rolled products, cold-drawn bars are produced in Contino, while the Forlì plant specialises in the stainless tube sector with a yearly capacity of over 250,000 tons.

Marcegaglia is a leading industrial group worldwide in the steel processing sector, with a yearly output of 5.3 million tons. The group has operations worldwide with 6,500 employees, 51 sales offices, 210 representations and 50 manufacturing plants covering 6 million square metres where 5,500 kilometres of carbon and stainless steel products are manufactured every day, serving more than 12,000 customers.
