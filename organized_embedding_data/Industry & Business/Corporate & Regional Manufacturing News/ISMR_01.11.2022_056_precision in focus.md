# PRECISION IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Electrically conductive materials can be cut with Kjellberg's Q power source.

Kjellberg Finsterwalde's new management team sat down with ISMR to outline the company's latest projects, technology launches and strategic priorities.

On 9 September 2022, Kjellberg Finsterwalde celebrated its centenary in Finsterwalde, Germany. The company develops and manufactures sustainable plant equipment for the metalworking industry, with a portfolio that includes products and technologies for plasmacutting, welding and customised specialpurpose machines.

For more than 60 years, the manufacturer has been a market specialist in plasma cutting and has committed itself to advancing the digitalisation of the metalworking industry with innovative industrial products.

Fresh from its success at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, the company's new management team sat down with ISMR to outline the company's latest projects, technology launches and strategic priorities.

New product launches

In October 2022, Kjellberg Finsterwalde Plasma und Maschinen GmbH (the largest company in the Kjellberg Finsterwalde group) extended its Management Board to position itself for the future. Falk Tzschichholz now acts as managing director, alongside Dr. Jörg Eßmann, to form the new management structure of Kjellberg Finsterwalde Plasma und Maschinen GmbH. The management partnership has been additionally strengthened by Dr. Martin Hertel, who was appointed as a further authorised signatory (alongside Jens Friedel) and oversees research and development.

As a management team, they take lead responsibility for production, sales and development. The team faces the common challenge of maximising the benefits of plasma technology for users in face of the increasing complexity of products and technologies. The aim is to continue to develop the company sustainably and globally as well as to be prepared for the future.

“This year has been a very special one for us; we are celebrating 100 years of Kjellberg in Finsterwalde. It all started in 1922 with a small manufacturing site for welding electrodes and, later, welding generators. Today, one century later, Kjellberg Finsterwalde is a global player. Our products and technologies are used all over the world. We are very proud of this and celebrated our 100th anniversary with our customers and partners at the recent EuroBLECH exhibition in Germany. Without them, we would not be where we are today," Falk Tzschichholz told ISMR.

The manufacturer also presented its new plasma power source, Q 4500, at the EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover from 25- 28 October 2022. It is the most powerful plasma-cutting system in its Q-Series range, combining high-precision plasma cutting with the complex requirements of Industry 4.0 standards.

"With a cutting current range of up to 450 A, Q 4500 is our most powerful plasma power source for digitised production. It combines precise plasma cutting and marking and enables the user to make precise plasma cuts of up to 120mm. High cutting speeds reduce cost and increase efficiency. The browser-based HMI 'Q-Desk' provides process data and information on all system components in real-time on standard devices (i.e. smartphones and tablets), regardless of location. Via the Ethernet interface, users can transfer and process all data using the MQTT protocol. Kjellberg eService ensures reliability in production through remote diagnostics, remote monitoring and regular updates," added Falk Tzschichholz.

Kjellberg's Q-Hole technology enables precise holes in mild steel with a diameter-to-material-thickness ratio of 0.75:1 via an extensive range of cutting technologies. The patent-pending 'ProPierce' process is offered exclusively for the Q-Series. It is designed to increase the previous maximum piercing capacity by 10mm in mild steel and by up to 30mm in stainless steel and aluminium.

"The new technological process offers the user more flexibility. No process change, additional equipment or new consumables are required," confirmed Tzschichholz.

As the smallest model in the Q-Series, the Q 1500 and Q 1500 plus cut material thicknesses of up to 60mm with a maximum cutting current of 150A. The Q 3000 and Q 3000 plus plasma-cutting systems cut and mark material thicknesses of up to 80mm with a maximum cutting current of 300A. Because of their modular design, machine performance of the Q 1500 plus and Q 3000 plus can be upgraded to suit user requirements.

## Strategic and technical focus

The management team confirmed its key strategic and technical priorities to ISMR.

“We have committed to advancing our technologies and providing innovative and high-quality products, even though the core product has changed over the years. So, our current target is to further develop the plasma-cutting process. Our customers want even more powerful machines to be equipped as flexibly as possible for future tasks, combined of course with digitisation. This creates completely new user requirements and expectations which we must meet with our technologies," the management team told ISMR.

The management team at Kjellberg Finsterwalde Plasma und Maschinen GmbH. From left to right, Falk Tzschichholz and Dr. Jörg Eßmann as well as authorised signatory, Dr. Martin Hertel.

"On the other hand, digitisation provides us with new opportunities to further develop our products, especially for linking complete production chains and systems. We talk about the digitisation of production which goes beyond mere automation. Another aim for the future is to expand our marketing activities. We want to establish representative offices across the world and start using further digital tools for our marketing activities," added the team.

Digitisation has been a strong focus for Kjellberg's product development. Process communication of the new power sources in the Q-Series takes place via EtherCAT to enable efficient communication of the power source with the guiding system and all other components in the production process. Updates and diagnostic tasks can be performed by the manufacturer as remote service via a secure VPN connection. All new features are aimed at the networking and automation of the production process as well as improving system availability.

"Additionally, users are provided with tools for visualisation, operator support, preventive maintenance and troubleshooting tips which they can access worldwide on all devices (i.e. smartphones or tablets) in a protected area," added the German specialist.

As a member of the Industry Business Network 4.0 e.V. association, Kjellberg Finsterwalde also works on solutions for existing equipment, in addition to the development of new products. The aim is to enable users of existing power sources to also benefit from the advantages of digitisation, networking and operation.

## Challenges and opportunities

Kjellberg Finsterwalde has successfully navigated global headwinds in worldwide manufacturing markets and an increasingly challenging business climate.

“As is the case with all companies worldwide, we have been severely tested by the COVID pandemic which is not over yet. The war in Ukraine, as well as the current COVID situation in China, poses new challenges. International markets are very important in our industry. It is a daily challenge to ensure the activities and success of our own products, regardless of political changes. Other great challenges are to tackle supplychain issues and maintain delivery capability. This is one of our most important targets. At present however, in our business, we see no general restraint regarding new investments," explained Falk Tzschichholz.

"One of the greatest challenges at present is to maintain our delivery capability. However, challenges also present new opportunities. For example, we have the advantage of high production depth; from assembly units to parts. This has kept us very robust in the past, particularly with respect to supply chains. Extensive stocks are also helpful to be able to respond to fluctuations in the market," he added.

In the future, Kjellberg Finsterwalde intends to cover a large part of its electricity demand with its own photovoltaic plants. This will make it less dependent on the public electricity network and is an effective way for it to save energy costs going forward.

“Reliability and efficiency are among the main objectives for our customers. They expect today's plasma-cutting machines to provide maximum availability and the highest productivity levels. The variable costs of a cutting system are very important in this context," added the management team.

## Focus on welding

In addition to its TIG welding processes (InFocus and ARCLINE®PP), solutions for submerged arc welding and the retrofit of welding systems, Kjellberg Finsterwalde also offers a wide range of welding electrodes. Its ProFocus direct diode laser complements the portfolio with a system for additive manufacturing of 3D parts and the repair of worn workpieces.

The TIG welding torches developed by Kjellberg Finsterwalde support the InFocus and InFocus Pluspole automated processes. With InFocus, amperages of up to 1000 A can be achieved (depending upon the respective welding task). The process is suitable for single-layer keyhole welding of sheets with thicknesses of up to 10mm as well as for productive heat conduction welding with extremely stable arc. InFocus Pluspole is designed to enable low-emission, singlelayer TIG welding of aluminium sheets with a thickness of up to 8mm.

Customised welding systems can also be designed to specific user requirements e.g. SAW pipe welding equipment for inner and outer seams, double T-beam welding equipment and double-hinged arm automation for box profiles.

## Trends and directions

The German specialist is well placed to spot developing trends in plasma-cutting and welding markets and has used its market knowledge to regularly introduce new products and processes.

“The main industry trends right now are sustainability and digitalisation. Innovations have shaped the history of our company. We are continuously working on optimising our plasma-cutting machines for use in smart factories," outlined Falk Tzschichholz.

With a separate Executive Board and Board of Trustees, the Kjellberg Foundation funds research and applications for innovative material-processing technologies and the improvement of working environments.

OSCAR PLT is the Foundation's research company, researching and developing new and innovative plasma and laser technologies for cutting, welding and additive manufacturing, as well as solutions for digitisation in the industrial environment.

"Another important target is, of course, the sustainable use of our machines," the management team told ISMR. For years, Kjellberg Finsterwalde has been committed to making plasma cutting as efficient and sustainable as possible. In addition, the plasma power sources are being developed in order to be able to work in an even more energy- and cost-saving way.

"Our latest inverter power source concepts and torch technology developments ensure consistent reductions of energy and gas consumption – also when compared to other cutting technologies. We pay particular attention to low CO₂ emissions. I think that, with our Q-Series, we are making very good progress towards achieving these goals," concluded Falk Tzschichholz.
