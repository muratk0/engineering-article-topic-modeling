# ARKU honoured by KIRCHHOFF Automotive

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH has received the "Supplier of the Year 2022" award from one of its customers, KIRCHHOFF Automotive. The two companies have mutually benefited from their partnership of over 20 years.

ARKU is a family-run company and levelling technology specialist founded in 1928. It offers a comprehensive range of precision levellers as well as deburring and rounding machines. It also runs its own business unit focusing on parts handling for levelling and deburring machines. ARKU coil lines are in operation in KIRCHHOFF Automotive factories on three continents. An important cornerstone is the worldwide presence of ARKU's sales and service. From its three sites in Baden-Baden, Cincinnati and Kunshan, ARKU also ensures a rapid supply of spare parts and maintenance for the coil lines.

From left: Franck Hirschmann; Albert Reiss; Hans-Peter Pflüger and Harald Kohl accept the "Supplier of the Year 2022" award from KIRCHHOFF Automotive.

ARKU was nominated in the 'Capital & Tooling' category. Due to the current political situation, the award ceremony took place online. ARKU employees Albert Reiss (President & CEO); Hans Peter Pflüger (former senior employee); Franck Hirschmann (key account manager) and Harald Kohl (project manager) accepted the award.

Albert Reiss, President & CEO, ARKU Maschinenbau, commented: "This award is of particular importance to us. It shows that high-quality, continuous innovations and a customer-oriented focus pays off greatly for both sides in a long-term partnership. Such values are clearly part of ARKU's DNA."

ARKU's headquarters are in Baden-Baden, Germany, and it maintains ISO-certified subsidiaries in Kunshan (China) and Cincinnati (USA), serving markets in more than 30 countries. It employs approximately 200 people worldwide.■
