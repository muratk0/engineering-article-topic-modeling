# WORLD STEEL ASSOCIATION ELECTS NEW OFFICERS

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

The Board of directors of the World Steel association (worldsteel) has elected the following new officers for 2010/2011:

■ CHAIRMAN: Hajime Bada, President & CEO, JFE Holdings

■ VICE CHAIRMEN: Paolo Rocca, Chairman and CEO, Techint Group and Xiaogang Zhang, President, Anshan Iron & Steel Corporation

■ TREASURER: John Surma, Chairman and CEO, United States Steel Corporation.

The new officers were elected until October 2011. The Board of Directors met on the first day of World Steel’s 44th annual conference in Tokyo, Japan.

On behalf of the Board of Directors, the Director General of the World Steel Association, Ian Christmas, thanked Paolo Rocca, Chairman for 2009/2010, for his leadership and support over the past year.

Ian Christmas said: “We welcome Hajime Bada as the new Chairman, Paolo Rocca and Xiaogang Zhang as Vice Chairmen and John Surma as Treasurer. We are also delighted to welcome our new members on board.”

The World Steel Association (worldsteel) is one of the largest and most dynamic industry associations in the world. It represents approximately 180 steel producers (including 19 of the world’s 20 largest steel companies), national and regional steel industry associations and steel research institutes. Worldsteel members produce around 85 per cent of the world’s steel.

## AWL-TECHNIEK APPOINTS LASER SAFETY OFFICER

Working with advanced laser applications is not without danger. Since AWL uses high-power lasers, which entails great risks in the case of misuse or lack of knowledge, laser safety is an extremely important issue for AWL. To ensure that safety is embedded in the organisation, a Laser Safety Officer (Dennis Broekhof) has been appointed.

The purpose of this expert is to implement the policy on laser safety and to ensure that employees uphold the laser safety regulations. The Laser Safety Officer defines, together with the laser safety commission, internal policy in regard to laser safety. He also compiles the laser safety report, stating the dangers and the measures taken; the goal being that employees are not exposed to laser radiation. In addition, he provides a safe lay-out for the laser welding facilities and also registers and instructs new laser operators.

By working in this way, AWL hopes to firmly secure laser safety within the organisation and facilitate a safe environment for working with lasers.
