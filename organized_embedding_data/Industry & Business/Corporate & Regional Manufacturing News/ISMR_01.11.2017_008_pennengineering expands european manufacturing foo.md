# PennEngineering® expands European manufacturing footprint

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

A NEW, 80,000 FT² facility, developed by PennEngineering®, a specialist in innovative fastening technologies and solutions, has recently begun production in Galway, Ireland. The firm has experienced considerable growth over its 75 years in business, and has invested in the new development in Ireland to increase its European production capabilities and demonstrate its commitment to providing manufacturing solutions for its EU customers.

"The new facility expands the primary manufacturing site for all PEM clinch fastening products sold in Europe. In addition to the onsite manufacturing facilities, the site has a Research and Development Centre and an Application Engineering Centre, offering full custom fastener and installation design and test capabilities. The extended capabilities of the new site will make Galway a 'centre of excellence' focusing on developing new innovative solutions to meet the European market needs," the manufacturer told ISMR.

The new facility has been built to have a low environmental impact, designed with the latest sustainable and ecological technologies including sensor controlled auto-dimming lights throughout office and factory areas, and waste oil and heat recovery systems.

The Galway workforce comprises over 60% highly trained engineers working across all functions, supporting customers at each stage of the manufacturing process. All employees are graduates from local educational institutes which are key, longstanding partners. The new building is PennEngineering's sixth building to open at the site in Galway and has been named after Chairman Charles 'Charlie' Grigg, who has a long history with PennEngineering in Ireland.

The new facility expands PennEngineering's capabilities to produce over 1.5 billion fasteners annually for its customer base. PennEngineering's regional strategy is centred around producing fastening solutions closer to its customers, in the same time zones, speaking the same languages, which enhances key relationships.

In the last 12 months, Penn has launched four new products and moved its entire core product to "lead-free". The expansion will bring the total footprint of PennEngineering operations in Galway to more than 200,000 ft². From this base PennEngineering, which celebrates its 75th anniversary, will be focus on growing markets in Europe.
