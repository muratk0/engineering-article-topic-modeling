# Filtermist launches ‘S’ Series at AMB

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

FILTERMIST INTERNATIONAL launched its ‘S’ Series filters at the AMB Exhibition for International Metalworking in Stuttgart, Germany. The S Series features a number of developments including a quick release keyless bushing system and new silencer design. For the first time the whole range, including the smallest S200 model, will feature high efficiency after-filters. The addition of afterfilters will help complete its comprehensive range of small centrifugal filters.

“The S Series offers increased efficiency across the range thanks to the use of a specially developed new filter pad material,” the company explained. “Effective oil mist extraction is a vital consideration for all factory owners, both in the UK where we have strict H&S guidelines and overseas. After all, a healthy workforce is a productive workforce!” www.filtermist.com
