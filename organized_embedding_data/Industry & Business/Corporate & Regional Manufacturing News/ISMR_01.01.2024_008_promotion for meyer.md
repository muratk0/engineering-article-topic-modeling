# Promotion for Meyer

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

Since 1 November 2023, Nikolas Meyer has been the new head of sales and industry management at Weil Technology GmbH in Müllheim, South Baden (Germany). He now heads the sales, industry management and TechCentre departments at Weil Technology. The company produces laser cutting, welding, pressing and stamping systems (including rollformers).

Nikolas Meyer has many years of experience in sales management for mechanical engineering. He has been working as a technical sales manager with technical and disciplinary leadership in an international environment for over ten years.

“Most recently, he was Business Unit Head of Sales and Applications and therefore jointly responsible for the entrepreneurial management of EMAG LaserTec GmbH. In addition to his sales expertise, he brings with him in-depth technical expertise for our systems and applications," commented Weil Technology.■
