# Alcoa to acquire RTI International Metals

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

LIGHTWEIGHT, high-performance metals specialist, Alcoa, has signed a definitive agreement to acquire RTI International Metals, Inc., a global supplier of titanium and speciality metal products and services for commercial aerospace, defence, energy and medical device markets. Alcoa will purchase RTI in a stock-for-stock transaction with an enterprise value of US$ 1.5 billion.

With RTI, Alcoa will grow its value-add businesses and further strengthen its aerospace portfolio. RTI will expand Alcoa’s range of titanium offerings and add advanced technologies and materials.

“Innovation and scale are critical to winning in both the titanium and aerospace industries today, which is why this transaction is such a natural strategic fit for both RTI and Alcoa,” said Dawne Hickton, Vice Chair, President and Chief Executive Officer of RTI International Metals.

This transaction is expected to enable Alcoa to capitalise on strong growth in the commercial aerospace sector. Alcoa projects a compounded annual global aerospace market growth rate of 5 to 6% through to 2019 and sees a current nine-year production order book for commercial jets at 2014 delivery rates.

In September last year, Alcoa signed a long-term contract to supply aluminium sheet and plate products to Boeing, the world’s largest aerospace company and a leading manufacturer of commercial jetliners and defence, space and security systems. The multi-year contract, valued at more than US$1 billion, is the largest ever between the two companies. ■

## European standardisation strategy

THE INGRESS (INdustry GRoup on European Standardisation Strategy) platform has now been launched by its sixteen founding European industry associations. The multi-stakeholder platform aims to facilitate representation and advocacy of industry interest groups on European standardisation policy matters, given that industry is the main contributor (funding 95% of standards’ development costs) and user of European standards.

“Regulation EU 1025/2012 on European standardisation (14 November 2012) grants the legal basis for the European Commission to request the three European standardisation organisations – CEN, CENELEC and ETSI – to develop standards which have the potential to support EU legislation and policies, provided that they are used by interested stakeholders. It also provides the status of observer to four categories of stakeholders in the regulatory Committee on Standards (CoS). The CoS is responsible for taking implementing decisions, such as on new standardisation requests, implementation guidance or discussions on the possible review of the Regulation. Unfortunately, industry’s participation in this Committee was not foreseen by the European regulator,” explained CECIMO, the European Association of Machine Tool Industries.

“One of INGRESS’ primary objectives is to be recognised by the European Commission as a relevant stakeholder to participate, as an observer, in the Committee on Standards (CoS). As European standards are voluntarily used by companies to primarily support their needs, it is necessary to ensure that the European Commission’s standardisation requests will be equally attractive to industry experts who participate in the standards’ development process and, eventually, offer the best chances to a broader uptake of harmonised standards by companies.” ■

## Blechexpo on course for growth

THE PRACTICAL trade fair duo of Blechexpo and Schweisstec takes place over a two-year cycle at the state trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and covers the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology. This year’s event will be held from 3 to 6 November 2015 with exhibitors from over 25 countries to date.

The combination of sheet metal, profile and pipe processing technology plus cutting, joining and fastening technology throughout the entire process sequence – from machining and fabrication up to module assembly – is the event’s strength.

Originally launched for visitors in south-eastern Germany, Blechexpo is now ranked the world’s number two trade fair for sheet metal processing. Today, the catchment area for expert visitors extends well beyond Central, South and Eastern Europe. The event, organised by P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, has enjoyed continuous growth in domestic and overseas exhibitor numbers, as well as an expanding expert audience who travelled to Stuttgart from more than 100 countries for the last event. According to the project management team, Blechexpo and Schweisstec have already matched the floor space figures reached at the last event in 2013, so additional halls have now been allocated.

To date, roughly 860,000 square feet have been reserved with several months to go before the trade fair opens. The show remit has also been expanded to include relevant areas for processing directly from the strip coil or the wire coil for electrical and electromechanical products.

“The percentage of overseas manufacturers and distributors is heading for record-breaking numbers (exhibitors are expected from 30 countries around the globe), so that even the successful figures achieved in 2013 will be topped,” added the organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.
