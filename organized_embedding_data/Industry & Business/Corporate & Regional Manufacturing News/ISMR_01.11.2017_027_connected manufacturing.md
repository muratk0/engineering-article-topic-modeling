"Digital technology controls the value chain, from the producer of raw materials to the final customer"

Jonathan Riegel, Member of the Board of Management, KASTO Maschinenbau GmbH & Co. KG

Jonathan Riegel, Member of the Board of Management, KASTO, discusses industry trends, energy-saving concepts and the company's focus on Industry 4.0 with ISMR.

KASTO Maschinenbau GmbH & Co. KG, based in Achern, Germany, specialises in sawing and storage technology for bar stock. The company is a global specialist in the manufacture and sale of metal-cutting saws and semi-automatic and automatic storage systems for bar stock and sheet metal. It is also a leading manufacturer of automatic handling systems for metal bars, sheet metal and pre-cut parts, as well as the corresponding software.

One of Europe's oldest family-owned companies, KASTO celebrated its 170th anniversary in 2014. It has registered 170 patents, delivered more than 140,000 sawing machines to all parts of the world and installed over 1,900 automatic storage facilities. In addition to a branch in Schalkau, Thuringia, KASTO has subsidiaries in England, France, Switzerland, Singapore and the USA.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes, achievements or awards and the reasons for this?

JR: For our automatic bar stock and sheet metal storage systems, we have developed a concept in which excess kinetic energy can be converted into electric current, stored temporarily and then used flexibly as required. Consumption of electric power can be reduced by as much as 40 per cent compared to conventional drive systems and the connected load can even be cut by more than 50 per cent. This reduces operating and investment costs and cuts CO₂ emissions.

This solution has won KASTO an environmental technology distinction, in the "energy-efficiency" category, awarded by the German state of Baden-Württemberg. The Ministry for Environment, Climate and Energy Management has awarded this distinction every two years since 2009 for outstanding, innovative products in environmental technology.

We are one of 16 companies to have received an award as part of the competition "100 places for Industry 4.0 in Baden-Württemberg". This places the focus on innovative concepts that achieve success through the intelligent networking of production and value-added processes. KASTO succeeded in convincing the jury with its in-house solutions in switch cabinet assembly and production logistics. This is because, for us, Industry 4.0 is an all-embracing topic and the reason why we consistently exploit possibilities for networking and automation in our in-house operations.
