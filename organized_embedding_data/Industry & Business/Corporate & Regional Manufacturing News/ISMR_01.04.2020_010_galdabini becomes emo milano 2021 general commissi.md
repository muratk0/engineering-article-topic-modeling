# Galdabini becomes EMO Milano 2021 General Commissioner

[TARİH: 01.04.2020 ISMR]

LUIGI GALDABINI IS the new General Commissioner for EMO MILANO 2021, the global machine tool and metalworking exhibition which will be hosted at the fieramilano Rho Exhibition Centre in Milan, Italy, from 4-9 October 2021.

The new General Commissioner was appointed during the General Assembly of CECIMO, the European Association of Machine Tool Industries. Luigi Galdabini will supervise the organisation of EMO MILANO 2021, participating in the promotion of the event through a roadshow of press conferences which will reach capital cities across the world.

EMO attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors, from every part of the world, to Milan. Alternately held in Hanover (Germany) and Milan, EMO in Italy is developed by EFIM-ENTE FIERE ITALIANE MACCHINE, a specialist in sector exhibition organisation, and promoted by FONDAZIONE UCIMU, the Foundation associated with the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association.

Metal forming and metalcutting machine tools; production systems; enabling technologies; solutions for interconnected and digital factories and additive manufacturing will be among the products and solutions spotlighted at EMO MILANO 2021. It is intended, says the show organiser, 'to transform fieramilano Rho into the biggest digital factory ever set up within an exhibition complex'.

Born in 1958, Luigi Galdabini is CEO of Galdabini S.p.A., a company in Cardano al Campo (Varese) which specialises in the production of metal forming machine tools and machines for mechanical tests. Past President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE and still a Board member of the association, Luigi Galdabini is also a member of the Italian Delegation of CECIMO, European Association of Machine Tool Industries, for which he was President from 2015-2017. EMO MILANO 2015 hosted 1,600 exhibiting companies over an exhibit area of 120,000 sq. m. and registered over 155,000 visits of operators from 120 countries.
