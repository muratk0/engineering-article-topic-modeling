# Vision Engineering opens new global HQ

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

VISION ENGINEERING LTD., an award-winning manufacturer of inspection and measurement microscopes, has opened its new global headquarters in Surrey, United Kingdom. The 129,200 sq. ft. building has all company departments, including manufacturing, under one roof. The main entrance is circular, designed to resemble a microscope lens. Many of the materials used in its construction are sustainable.

Vision Engineering was founded in 1958, by Rob Freeman, MBE, a toolmaker who worked as a race mechanic with the Jaguar Racing Team where he developed a borescope for inspecting internal race engine parts. Now aged 87, Rob Freeman is still Chairman of the growing company and led the grand opening of his building in the presence of the mayor of Guildford, the Lord Lieutenant of Surrey, invited guests and Vision Engineering employees from around the world.

Vision Engineering holds several patents on its high-tech optical systems. Currently, 120 employees are based within the new Freeman Building. While over 80% of the products are designed, developed and manufactured in the new building, the Mantis Family of eyepiece-less microscopes will continue to be manufactured at its U.S. headquarters in New Milford, CT.
