Wind Energy in the U.S.

In March 2021 the U.S. government announced its plan to install 30 gigawatts— enough to power 10 million homes—of offshore wind capacity in U.S waters by 2030, creating in the process thousands of jobs for manufacturing to install, maintain, and service hundreds if not thousands of offshore wind turbines.

Reaching 30 GW is a massive challenge. Thousands of wind turbines will have to be built together with factories, dedicated ports, and ships to transport them, as well as the infrastructure to connect them to the grid.

At the Delaware River in southern New Jersey, the first factory of this kind is being built to fabricate 400-ft.-long, 40-ft.-wide monopiles for installation in the Atlantic seabed. The monopile is the most common foundation model because of its strong design, mass production, and simplicity of installation. Forming thick steel plates into these columns that will be the foundation of a great source of electricity requires the right tools: powerful bending technology and automation systems.

Plate Rolling Technologies

Faccin specializes in manufacturing plate forming machines for wind power—specifically for wind tower foundations such as monopiles, jackets, and tripods, together with their transition pieces.

Bending heavy-duty steel plates 14 ft. wide and 6 in. thick into 40-ft. diameters for offshore wind tower foundations requires strong, powerful bending machines capable of achieving tight tolerances of roundness and able to perform this repetitive task 24/7.

The company has supplied top players of offshore foundations with its optimized HAV machines, 3-roll with variable geometry, ideal for rolling and calibrating cylindrical and conical shapes. In addition, the 4-roll models 4HEL and 4HEP are the ideal machines when high productivity is required by the wind tower manufacturer. Their heavier versions are optimized for the construction of thick cones and cans for monopiles, jackets, and TPs.

Must-have Features

A rolling machine for foundations must work 24/7 and be highly reliable, ensuring minimum downtime. An oversized structure and components, efficient cooling system, and centralized lubrication system make the Faccin 4HEL and 4HEP series the most steadfast rolling machines on the market. The company is widely known for the quality of its products, continuously supplying machines that perform what has been promised under heavy loads.

Reliability must go hand in hand with speed and accuracy, and the job must be stress-free for the operator. Thanks to the Siemens PGS-Ultra CNC, it is possible to control automatically not only the machine but also the handling system.

A good rolling also means saving time in the subsequent steps of the wind tower assembly and welding. With their mammoth forces, Faccin machines can perform a top-quality rolling and prebending of the plates, supplying perfect rolled cones to the welding stations.

The Ultimate Rolling System

Rolling hundreds of wind tower sections per month can be accomplished with Faccin’s Wind Tower Automation System, which combines:

Powerful, super-sized Faccin plate rolling machine.

Strong side support with clamps that permits the correct alignment of the plate before tack welding.

Sturdy top support for conical sections covering the full width of the plate.

Totally motorized feeding table with alignment device to quickly prepare the plate for rolling.

Fully programmable Siemens CNC designed to automate the most complex rolling projects.

Solutions for Foundation Manufacturers

The foundation business is very competitive, so the challenge is to enhance the cycle to cut production time. The company’s rollers, thanks to the groundbreaking "Faccin Advantage Package," have a superfast hydraulic system and high power to reach the floor-to-floor production time of 20 min. per rolled section. The machines’ geometry and controls are designed to reach the tightest tolerances and to minimize the rerolling percentage of the cones, which is a common challenge of the foundation manufacturers.
