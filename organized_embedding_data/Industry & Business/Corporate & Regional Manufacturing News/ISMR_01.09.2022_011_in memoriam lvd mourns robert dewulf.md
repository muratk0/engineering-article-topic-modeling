# In memoriam: LVD mourns Robert Dewulf

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

LVD has announced the passing of Baron Robert Dewulf, one of the founding fathers of LVD, who died on 11 August 2022 at the age of 93.

As one of the driving forces, the 'D' in LVD, he made a significant contribution to the success of the company that he helped set up in 1954 (alongside Jacques Lefebvre and Marc Vanneste). The three

managers complemented each other, with Mr. Dewulf acting as the bridge-builder to both government and industry. "As a result of his efforts, LVD built a reputation as a respected manufacturer and good employer in the region. Under his direction, LVD grew into an international company. His entrepreneurship and his vision for the future put LVD on the map as an innovative global player decades ago," LVD told ISMR.

"Even though he had been retired for many years and had passed the torch to the younger generation, he remained genuinely concerned with the company's fortunes up to the very last moment. If possible, he was very happy to come to the company and still showed great interest in our latest products and developments. He loved meeting new people at LVD but also remained very concerned about the wellbeing of all employees of the company. He will be greatly missed," LVD continued.

LVD is a leading manufacturer of sheetmetalworking equipment including laser cutting systems; punch presses; press brakes; guillotine shears and automation systems, integrated to and supported by its CADMAN® software suite. Its Industry 4.0-ready technology is geared towards smart manufacturing.
