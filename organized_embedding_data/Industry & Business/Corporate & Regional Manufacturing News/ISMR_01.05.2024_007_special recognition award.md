# Special recognition award

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Croatian-American entrepreneur and owner of Cortec® Corporation, Boris Miksic, has received special recognition for 50 years of dedicated work at NACE-International, the largest global organisation focusing on developing standards for corrosion prevention. Cortec® is a global corrosion inhibitor manufacturer and a specialist in green corrosion protection technologies.

Boris Mikšić emigrated to the USA in the 1970s, where he founded Cortec® in his garage. From modest beginnings in Hugo, Minnesota (USA), Cortec® now boasts nine plants, warehouses and laboratories. It is reportedly the world's largest producer of VCI, VpCI® and MCI® products and services, operating in over 100 countries.

"I have dedicated my life to developing new green technologies in the corrosion industry, and this award is a great honour. All my investments have a sustainability focus, and my vision from the beginning was to develop environmentally safe solutions. Our plants operate on the circular economy principle, and we recently started utilising our energy resources by installing solar power plants," confirmed Miksic.

"Most of our products are bio-based and recyclable. We reprocess them in-house, significantly reducing the amount of waste for disposal. This is how we demonstrate not only responsibility towards our customers but also towards our communities. For me, this is equally important. Cortec® is an example of a modern company that is based on sustainability principles," he added.

Over his 50-year career, he has received 66 patents and played an important role in the National Association of Corrosion Engineers (NACE, now AMPP). He has won numerous awards and chaired NACE symposiums and working groups, helping to develop numerous industrial testing standards. His discoveries on vapour-phase corrosion inhibitor technology have been published in various scientific journals.■
