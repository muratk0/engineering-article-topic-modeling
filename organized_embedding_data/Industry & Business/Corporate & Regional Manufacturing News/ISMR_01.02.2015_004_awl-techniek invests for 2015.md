# AWL-Techniek invests for 2015

[TARİH: 01.02.2015 ISMR]

DUTCH MANUFACTURER AWL-Techniek has grown from a regional player into a global partner. With branches in the Netherlands, the Czech Republic and China, and a strategic partnership with JR Automation in the United States, AWL is approaching a new phase. The company grew during the past year, achieving a turnover of more than 70 million euros, and intends to increase this amount to 100 million euros within five years. Hans Velten was appointed Commercial Director on 1 January 2015 to help the company to achieve this growth.

In 2014 AWL achieved nearly 20% of its turnover with specials for the general metalworking industry. This is a trend which is expected to continue in 2015. The company is investing heavily in R&D to ensure that it can continue to serve the demanding automotive industry, now and in the future. With the development of the Smartline and the Quick Motion, amongst other things, AWL is noticing an increase in the number of projects using these cells.

“During the past year we have integrated more than 200 robots in our projects, which is an absolute record. This increase is expected to continue in the years to come, as is the case for far-reaching automation,” explained Van’t Hof, CEO of AWL.

Laser welding will also continue to contribute to AWL’s further future growth, now that the company has set its sights set on expanding its position as a supplier for the automotive industry for chassis components and complex exhaust components.
