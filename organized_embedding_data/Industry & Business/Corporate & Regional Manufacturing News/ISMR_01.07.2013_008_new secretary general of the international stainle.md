# New Secretary General of the INTERNATIONAL STAINLESS STEEL FORUM

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

THE INTERNATIONAL Stainless Steel Forum (ISSF) has appointed John Rowe as Secretary General and Member of its Executive Board. He replaced Pascal Payet-Gaspard who stepped down at the end of 2012. John was formerly CEO of the ThyssenKrupp Group as well as Vice Chairman of the Shanghai Association of Enterprises with Foreign Investment.

John has made his career in metals and minerals, including working in the stainless steel industry for the last 30 years. From 1991 to 2003, he was director of international sales and marketing at Middelburg Steel and Alloys, during which time he was also a member of the Executive Boards of the South African Stainless Steel Development Association (SASSDA) and of the South Africa Iron and Steel Institute.

The International Stainless Steel Forum is a representative association of the steel industry based in Brussels. It has 76 members from 26 countries, representing almost all of the world’s stainless steel producers. For more information, see www.worldstainless.org
