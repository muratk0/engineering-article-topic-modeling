# DREISTERN celebrates 75th anniversary

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

Roll forming specialist, DREISTERN GmbH & Co. KG, founded in 1949 and celebrating its 75th anniversary this year, has established itself as one of the market leaders worldwide.

At EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, Germany (22-25 October 2024), it will present its skinpass station for the finishing of cold rolled steel strip, its new intelligent straightening device and upgraded sensing drive shaft. Visitors can meet the company on its stand G60 in Hall 27, where it will be showcasing its profiles.

"Using seven profiles from our customers, we will show EuroBLECH visitors where and how the profiles produced on DREISTERN rollforming systems are used in their end products in different industry sectors," it told ISMR.

In the age of Industry-4.0, Dreistern also offers RF4.0 (Roll Forming 4.0) and SERVICES4.0. More than 2,000 units from DREISTERN are in use, producing over 10,000 different profiles with more than 40 patents already in the system. Dreistern has 185 employees in Schopfheim (Germany) and Telford (USA).

Roll forming systems are required in a wide variety of sectors including the automotive and transportation industries; warehousing and logistics; the electrical, furniture and construction industries and in roll-forming plants. Special profiles are used everywhere.

"The famous Dreistern cognac (a rare, high-quality treat) was the inspiration for our name—literally a 'schnapps idea' that is still worth its weight in gold today," said the company.
