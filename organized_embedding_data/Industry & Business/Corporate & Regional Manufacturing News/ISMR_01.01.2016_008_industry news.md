## German manufacturers push sales to Indonesia

Successful VDW symposium in Jakarta. Group picture of the organisers (source: VDW).

Official opening of the VDW’s Technology Symposium in Indonesia (source: VDW)

INDONESIA RANKS among the world’s top 20 markets for the machine tool industry. Japanese vendors dominate the market, while Germany ranks fifth among the vendor nations for machine tools. For German manufacturers, there is plenty of scope for upsizing their sales so German producers of machine tools intend to vigorously intensify their business operations in Indonesia. The kick-off came at the VDW’s Technology Symposium in Jakarta on 25 November 2015.

Twelve German manufacturers of machine tools took the opportunity to showcase their products, solutions and services at the event. 113 high-ranking representatives of Indonesia’s industrial sector responded to the VDW’s invitation. They came primarily from the automotive industry and its component suppliers, the aviation sector, metalworking companies and the general mechanical engineering sector.

“In 2014, Indonesia ranked 19th in the world, with machine tool consumption of 742 million euros. The country is the third-largest market in the ASEAN region, behind Thailand and Vietnam. During the period 2010 to 2013, the Indonesian market performed exceptionally well. Consumption doubled to more than 900 million euros. Last year, however, the market suffered a downturn of 17 per cent. The most important supplier of machine tools is Japan, which accounts for almost 60 per cent of machine tool imports. It is followed in second, third and fourth places by Taiwan, China and South Korea. In 2014, German manufacturers exported machine tools worth 24 million euros to Indonesia, which corresponds to a market share of 2 per cent. The task for German companies now is to devote more vigorous attention to the Indonesian market,” commented the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders’ Association).

Besides the construction industry and the food-processing sector, it is primarily the automotive industry that is booming in Indonesia at present. The Association of the Indonesian Automotive Industry (Gaikindo) is predicting that, between 2014 and 2017, the local automotive industry will be upsizing its production capacities from 1.3 to 2.2 million units. Japanese manufacturers such as Suzuki, Mitsubishi, Toyota and, most recently, Honda and Nissan intend to upgrade Indonesia into an export hub for the ASEAN Economic Community (AEC).

## Sheet metal in Asia

ASIABLECH, the International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, will open its doors at the Suzhou International Expo Centre in China from 11-13 May 2016. It is a central business hub for suppliers and manufacturers of sheet metal working technology in Asia, providing a platform that appropriately reflects the specialised nature of this industry sector.

“We have developed relationships with sheet metal associations and trade bodies across all provinces of China, as well as Korea, Taiwan, Japan and the whole of South East Asia and the ASEAN region. AsiaBLECH aims to build the platform for international sheet metal working manufacturers in China and across Asia. We have also made significant connections in Asian vertical sectors such as automotive supply, electronics, shipbuilding etc. This has given us a depth of reach for AsiaBLECH, domestically and internationally, that has not been attempted before,” said David Tellett, managing director of organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd.

“AsiaBLECH is on track to be at least double the size of the previous event last year. That show attracted well over 4000 sheet metal professionals and buyers from 30 countries, so we’re always aiming to grow that in terms of numbers, quality of visitors and geographical reach. China has become a highly sophisticated engineering market and has gained a regional and global reach beyond its borders.”

There will be networking and educational areas at the event (with Chinese and international keynote speakers), as well as workshops for European exhibitors/visitors on how to connect and do business in China and a technology-focused seminar, led by the associations. There will also be a presentation area for exhibitors. Sheet metal awards for the show are currently under discussion. For further details, see www.asiablech.com

David Tellett, managing director, Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd
