## CSI rotates CEO & Chairman

THE SHAREHOLDERS of California Steel Industries, Inc. (CSI) have elected to rotate the CEO and Chairman of the Board positions of the company, in keeping with historical practices. Mr. Toshiyuki (“Ted”) Tamai, long-time executive at CSI, became President and Chief Executive Officer of the company from 1 August 2012, replacing Mr. Vicente Wright who has been appointed as CSI’s Chairman of the Board of Directors, replacing Mr. Kaoru Okamoto.

CSI’s two shareholders are Vale S.A. and JFE Steel Corporation of Japan. Each shareholder owns 50% of the shares of CSI. The leadership changes follow a longstanding practice where one shareholder company has appointed the CEO and the other the Chairman of the Board position, and then vice versa upon the next rotation.

California Steel Industries, Inc. is a leading producer of flat rolled steel products in the western United States (the 11 states west of the Rocky Mountains) based on tonnage billed, with a broad range of products including hot rolled, cold rolled and galvanized sheet and electric-resistant welded pipe. CSI has nearly 1,000 employees. www.californiasteel.com

## SMA names new VP, Policy and Communications

THE STEEL Manufacturers Association (“SMA”) has promoted Adam B. Parr to the position of Vice President of Policy & Communications. The decision was announced during SMA’s recent Board of Directors meeting in Washington, DC.

Mr. Parr joined the SMA in February 2005. His areas of policy focus have included international trade, workforce safety, transportation and infrastructure, raw materials and corporate tax. He has worked extensively with SMA’s Safety, Plant Operation and Transportation Committees as well as SMA’s Trade Advocacy, Energy and Scrap ad hoc groups.

A native of Richmond, VA, Mr. Parr has a Bachelor of Arts in Government & American Politics from the University of Virginia, and a Master of Business Administration from the Robert H. Smith School of Business at the University of Maryland.

## ALERIS LEADERSHIP CHANGES

ALERIS RECENTLY announced the establishment of the Commercial & Defense Plate global market segment and a centralised sustainability initiative, both key strategic areas for the company. Aleris is a global leader in aluminium rolled and extruded products, aluminium recycling and specification alloy manufacturing.

Stephen S. Stone, formerly senior vice president and general manager, Rolled Products North America (RPNA), assumes the new role of vice president, Commercial & Defense Plate. Kelly R. Thomas, formerly vice president and Treasurer, has been named senior vice president and general manager, RPNA, replacing Stone. Guido Weber, formerly vice president, Operations, RPNA, becomes vice president, Sustainability. Thomas Brackmann, most recently president of Nichols Aluminum, has joined Aleris as vice president, Operations, to replace Weber. See www.aleris.com
