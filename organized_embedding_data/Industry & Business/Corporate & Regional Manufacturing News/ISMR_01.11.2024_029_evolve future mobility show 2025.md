# EVOLVE FUTURE MOBILITY SHOW 2025

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Expo Centre Sharjah (ECS) has signed a partnership agreement with MIE Events to launch the EVOLVE FUTURE MOBILITY SHOW 2025 from 15-18 October 2025 at the Centre. Over 100 global brands are expected to participate.

This event aims to highlight the future of sustainable transportation, showcasing electric, hydrogen and hybrid vehicles and electric bikes, alongside modern solutions and technologies in the electric transport sector.

The exhibition will feature components and systems of electric vehicles such as motors, electronic control units and battery management systems. Providers of charging services and integrated renewable energy solutions will also participate, highlighting developments in the EV (electric vehicles) industry in general.

It aims to bolster the presence of electric vehicle manufacturers in UAE markets and accelerate the pace of EV adoption, helping to reduce the transportation sector's carbon footprint.

This supports the “UAE Net Zero by 2050” strategic initiative and the circular economy, while also enhancing economic development.
