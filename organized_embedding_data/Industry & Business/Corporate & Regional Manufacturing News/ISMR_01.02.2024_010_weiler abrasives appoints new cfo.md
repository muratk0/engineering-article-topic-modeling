# Weiler Abrasives appoints new CFO

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Weiler Abrasives (a provider of abrasives, power brushes and maintenance products for surface conditioning) has appointed Ms. Mary Beth Martino as the new Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and a member of Weiler Abrasives' Executive Committee.

In this role, Martino will be responsible for the global financial activities of the company and its subsidiaries. In addition, she will work closely with Weiler Abrasives CEO A.J. Roshan and the Executive Committee to develop, implement and optimise the company's global strategy aimed at delivering sustainable growth and transformation. In addition to leading the Global Finance Team, Martino will support various leaders and functions across the organisation to ensure operational and strategic alignment.

Before joining Weiler Abrasives, Martino spent 25 years with international abrasive manufacturer, Winterthur Wendt USA, serving as both CFO and CEO during her tenure. More recently, she was CFO for Henkels and McCoy, a large infrastructure contractor, and has held CFO and CEO roles in several private-equity-backed manufacturing and construction businesses. Martino has an MBA from Saint Joseph's University and a Bachelor's Degree in Accounting and Finance from La Salle University.
