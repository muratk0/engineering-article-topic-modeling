# Smart ideas made smarter

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

SMART FACTORY EXPO, which took place on 14-15 November 2018 in Liverpool (UK), brought together a host of talent from across the manufacturing sector. From the large manufacturing companies and solution providers to a range of SMEs exhibiting in Innovation Alley and several interesting talks, the conference attracted almost 6,000 people over the two days.

The conference started with an announcement from UK Business & Industry Minister, Richard Harrington MP, and Chair of the Made Smarter Review, Juergen Maier, who unveiled the details of the £20m 'Made-Smarter North West' pilot which will test the most effective ways to engage with manufacturers and encourage them to adopt digital technologies. Working through five North West Growth Hubs, the £20m pilot aims to test the most effective ways to engage with manufacturers and encourage them to adopt technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Virtual Reality, IoT and sensors, 3D printing and robotics.

A core part of the announcement was grants of up to £20,000 to help small businesses in the UK to adopt and embrace industrial digital technologies (IDTs). This follows the UK Government's budget statement on 29 October 2018 announcing that £121m will be invested in the Industrial Strategy Challenge fund, also under the moniker "Made Smarter". This activity builds on extensive engagement with Innovate UK and its partners – including KTN, the Catapults and the Research Councils.

Sponsored by Innovate UK and organised by the Knowledge Transfer Network (KTN) in partnership with the Manufacturer, Innovation Alley brought together solution providers from across the UK with innovative concepts and technologies that will help manufacturers achieve their goals of creating 'a smarter digital manufacturing process'.

The Digital Readiness Level, DRL, supported by KTN, also had an excellent amount of engagement at the show. DRL is a community interest company set up to help businesses benchmark their readiness to make the most of digital technologies and prioritise the steps along their journey to digital maturity. The DRL assessments are free to use, and the team can connect businesses to the right organisations, giving advice on how to embrace the new digital revolution.■
