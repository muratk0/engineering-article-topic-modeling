# Formtek confirms new appointments

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

Formtek has confirmed the promotion of Rick Poots to service manager. Rick previously was a service technician for seven years with Formtek's Winpro product line, specialising in fenestration applications. He has serviced machines throughout the world. His new duties will expand on over 20 years of manufacturing experience by coordinating service for all product lines.

Bob Shaffer has transitioned to the role of plant manager for Formtek, Inc., responsible for the Cleveland and Hill Engineering facilities. He previously served as the director of customer service. Bob has 30 years of prior experience as the engineering manager at Yoder Manufacturing as well as engineering positions with Royalton Industries and Loopco, specialising in roll forming, tube mill and coil processing systems. ■
