# Beam rolling mill contract

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

SMS Group will build the new beam rolling mill for Duferco’s steel division in San Zeno Naviglio (Brescia, Northern Italy). The supply contract for the new steel rolled products plant, which will crown a 25-yearold industrial dream, was signed recently by Antonio Gozzi, President of Duferco Italia Holding and Franco Monteferrario/Thomas Massmann, Executive VP, SMS GmbH, as well as Marco Asquini, CEO of SMS Group.

The new rolling beam, integrated with the steel plant, will give Duferco, an Italian producer of steel beams, an efficient system at the heart of the European and Italian market. The mill will focus on digitisation, artificial intelligence and automation for production and logistics.

The total investment, facility and infrastructure cost amounts to €180 million and, when fully operational, it will create 150 jobs, not including those related to ancillary industries. The entire rolling mill capacity of the Group is expected to reach 1.5 million tons worth of long products. The commitment is to create a more sustainable factory, with low environmental impact and high safety standards.

“The entire power supply of the new rolling mill will be supplied by renewable energy, thanks to the first green PPA signed by a steel company in Italy. This will allow us to build the first steel plant fully powered by green energy,” commented Duferco.

“The new beam rolling mill in San Zeno Naviglio will greatly increase our competitiveness. This will provide a strong foundation for a long-term strategy that combines environmental, social and economic sustainability through technological innovation, maximising market opportunities,” concluded Augusto Gozzi, Duferco Member of the Board.

Duferco Group is an international holding company historically operating mainly in the steel industry. Headquartered in Luxembourg, the Group operates globally with more than 100 operating offices and industrial sites across the world.
