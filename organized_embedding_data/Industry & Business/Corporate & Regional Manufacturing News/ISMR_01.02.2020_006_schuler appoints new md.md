# Schuler appoints new MD

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

ON 1 JANUARY 2020 Frank Klingemann took over management of the Industry division at forming specialist, Schuler. He is now responsible, among other things, for fully automated mechanical ProgDie and transfer presses, which are mainly used in the automotive and supplier industries, blanking lines, systems for production of electrical motor laminations and coin minting presses.

The graduate engineer has 30 years of experience in the industry, which he gained in various management positions at robot manufacturer, Kuka. Most recently, he was head of the Robotic Automation Business Unit at HLS Engineering Group GmbH in Augsburg. At Schuler, he is based in Weingarten.

For the new managing director, the objective is clear: "Innovative technology and the associated customer benefits must always be paramount," he said, believing that the move to electro-mobility and digital transformation offer great opportunities. "Schuler must play a pioneering role here," he added.

Founded in 1839 at its headquarters in Göppingen, Germany, Schuler AG has approximately 6000 employees at production sites in Europe, China and the Americas, as well as service companies in over 40 countries. The company is majority-owned by the Austrian ANDRITZ Group. See www.schulergroup.com
