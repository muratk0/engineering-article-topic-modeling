# THE PARTNERSHIP PRINCIPLE

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

THALMANN Maschinenbau AG and CIDAN Machinery discuss their view of global sheet metal markets, as well as their partnership and technology objectives, with ISMR.

THALMANN Maschinenbau AG can trace its history back to the one-man business founded by Otto Thalmann in 1948.

As a pioneer in long-length bending machine construction, the Swiss innovator has been awarded with the Bavarian State Gold Medal for excellent workmanship. It is constantly innovating with new products and technologies aimed at simplifying working practices in sheet metalworking plants and industries.

THALMANN has forged a strong partnership with Cidan Machinery, a folding specialist in Sweden producing sheet metal machinery since 1907, to jointly market each other's technologies in Scandinavia, Sweden, Switzerland, Asia and the US. The two companies have a long history of cooperation, as they offer complementary sheet metal processing technologies.

Both THALMANN Maschinenbau AG and its partner, CIDAN Machinery, presented a range of folding innovations at this year's EuroBLECH. ISMR spoke to both partners at the exhibition this October to discuss their view of global sheet metal markets and their partnership and technology objectives.

## New folding innovations

THALMANN showcased its TD double folder and the newly launched TZ single folder at EuroBLECH this year.

"Kinetic control shaft technology delivers maximum angular accuracy and parallelism of the parts; a new VFD (Vertical Force Drive) construction principle of the TZ model guarantees maximum force; an energy-efficient DFT-drive system (Dynamic Folding Technology) is part of the system as is new tool geometry and intuitive CNCs," Marco Cappello, THALMANN's CEO, told ISMR. "These are all key factors for economic and profitable sheet metal processing."

The innovative TZ model emerged as a further development of two preceding models, the THAKO and ZR series. Its VFD (Vertical Force Drive) design principle produces a large amount of vertical clamping force for hemming. The integration of kinetic control shaft technology offers added value by distributing the output from all of the machine stands evenly over the clamping and folding beams. Additional free space and flexibility are provided by the newly designed clamping beams and the offset folding beam.

"With modern drive technology and 'Stop and Go' functionality, the TZ provides measurably improved energy efficiency with considerably higher folding dynamics. Torsion-free profiles are created by a smooth-running, servo-drive-controlled dynamic crowning system which compensates for any edge-pressure effects which may occur. The new longitudinal slitter concept is detached from the folding beam to prevent disruptive influences during the folding process," explained Marco Cappello.

The safety concept, which includes advanced laser technology for the clamping area and slitting device, is designed to provide protection and safety for the workplace.

Automatic radius adjustment is performed using muted servo motors. The same drive also controls the dynamic crowning function, which prevents the profile ends from being folded too far. The fully-automatic back gauge provides a working range of 5mm - 1250mm (0.19"- 49.21"). A tapered back-gauge function is optionally available.

"We are very happy with the amount of visitors here at EuroBLECH, and especially in the interest shown in our double folder with its new features such as the loading table (which helps to reduce labour costs as the machine needs fewer operators or can be completely automated). It is a big step forwards in folding. I demonstrated to customers here how to operate this machine on my own, whilst folding three parts at the same time," explained Cappello.

"We recently developed a next-generation model in the TD series that can process harder (higher-tensile strength) material as more and more stainless steel and other steel alloys are being processed. The customer can save money because the folders can process thinner, but stronger, sheet metal. Alongside our new folders, process management software is one of the key innovations that we are showing at EuroBLECH. We developed this as an open and flexible software platform, in close cooperation with our partner, with Industry 4.0 principles in mind."

CIDAN Machinery also launched several new innovations at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition. It showed ISMR its redesigned PRO Z Up/Down folder for folding complex parts with reduced handling. The patented OED is designed to ensure maximum pressure and capacity, regardless of tool height. Automatic CNC sheet thickness and folding centre adjustment, without requiring crowning, is a key feature of the machine.

"We also launched our new PIVOTAL folding machine which is now able to bend 360° - we are focusing on a concept folder that can produce a four-sided part to reduce welding," Peter Tafazoli, CIDAN's CEO, told ISMR. "On our booth, you will also see our new 3100 x 3.0mm FORMA folding machine with new clamping beam drive technology and redesigned side frames. Also on display the new CTL-line COMPACT-12, which is now even more compact and has a lot of new features. Our machine range at EuroBLECH is rounded off with our new EVO guillotine shear, with new updated blade gap adjustment."

## A joint strategy

Both CIDAN and THALMANN's product ranges are complementary, which has formed the basis of their successful partnership for many years.

"More and more companies are looking at their overall production processes now, not just the individual machines. By combining our product ranges, we can develop a joint concept that improves our customers' overall performance. This can be introduced, for example, in different steps. The software manages everything in between. Geographically and customer-wise, we are a strong and complementary team," Peter Tafazoli told ISMR.

"I am seeing more automation and semi-automation in the market as well as demands for better bending quality and flexibility. Of course, the focus is also on software," he added.

Marco Cappello agreed, adding that he is also seeing more complex geometries being bent that require special manufacturing processes and tools, as well as more flexibility.

"Customers always want to take cost out of the process. The fastest way of doing this, of course, is by eliminating labour costs and this is done through automatic processes and software. Safety is also increased as people are not close to the machine or moving parts," he told ISMR.

"There will still be human involvement in these processes, of course," added Tafazoli. "Machine programming needs to be simple and not over-complicated, with the relevant data supplied. It is no longer easy to find skilled operators. Machines need to be simple and easy to operate."

## Working for growth

Both companies are re-sellers of each other's products through their headquarters, subsidiaries and dealer networks. Together, they can reach and service a wider customer base. Both Cappello and Tafazoli reported a strong and optimistic climate in the industry this year and good results from their joint strategy.

"It has been a good year for us. It is not always just about price. Companies are starting to think about sustainable investment and quality – they are making more long term investments and quality is coming into play again," explained Cappello. "Traditionally, the US has been our largest market. Germany has always been a good market for us, as has the Italian market this year. We are on track for 15-20% growth in 2016."

Tafazoli has high expectations for 2017, as both companies' order books look strong.

"The US, Scandinavian and UK markets (despite Brexit) are very strong. France has also been a good market for us this year although Asia has been slow. I am happy about the joint service that we can offer our customers, in combination with our partner Forstners. We are a team, we trust each other and we have the same goals."
