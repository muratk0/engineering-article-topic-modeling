# DREISTERN GmbH & Co. KG

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand G60

www.dreistern.com

The roll forming specialist, founded in 1949 and celebrating its 75th anniversary this year, has established itself as one of the market leaders worldwide.

At EuroBLECH, it will present its skin-pass-station for the finishing of cold rolled steel strip, its new intelligent straightening device and upgraded sensing drive shaft.

"Using seven profiles from our customers, we will show EuroBLECH visitors where and how the profiles produced on DREISTERN roll forming systems are used in their end products in different industry sectors," it told ISMR.

In the age of Industry 4.0, Dreistern also offers RF4.0 (Roll Forming 4.0) and SERVICES4.0. More than 2,000 units from DREISTERN are in use, producing over 10,000 different profiles with more than 40 patents already in the system. Dreistern has 185 employees in Schopfheim (Germany) and Telford (USA).
