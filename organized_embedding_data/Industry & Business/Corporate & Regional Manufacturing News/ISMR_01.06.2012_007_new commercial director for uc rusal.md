# New Commercial Director for UC RUSAL

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

UC RUSAL, a leading global aluminium producer, has confirmed the appointment of Evgeny Ovchinnikov as Commercial Director of UC RUSAL. Ovchinnikov has been working for UC RUSAL for over ten years and, since April 2010, has been Director of By-Products Sales in RUSAL’s Commercial Directorate. As Commercial Director, Evgeny Ovchinnikov will be responsible for ensuring uninterrupted supply process within the company and optimisation of costs for the procurement of raw materials and services for the transportation of raw materials and finished products. He will be also responsible for decision-making in sales of by-products with the aim of maximising profits and to form transparent reporting systems, as well as for overall byproducts’ sales management. This appointment follows the transfer of Pavel Ovchinnikov who previously held the position of UC RUSAL’s Commercial Director to a new managerial post in Basic Element. ■
