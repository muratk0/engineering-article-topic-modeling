# of Italian-Chinese Chamber of Commerce

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

PIER LUIGI STREPARAVA, past President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robotics and automation, is the new President of the Italian-Chinese Chamber of Commerce. This institute, founded in 1970, was one of the first Western institutions to open a dialogue with China.

The entrepreneur from Brescia, born in 1942, is Chairman of Streparava SpA (Adro), a leading manufacturer of machine tools and components for the automotive sector, and is currently also Treasurer of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE as well as a Member of the Board of the association. See www.ucimu.it
