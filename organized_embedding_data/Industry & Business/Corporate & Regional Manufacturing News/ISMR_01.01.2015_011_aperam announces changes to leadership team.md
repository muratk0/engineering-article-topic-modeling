# Aperam announces changes to leadership team

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

## Management change at Novelis Europe

NOVELIS, EUROPE has announced a change in the management of its Automotive and Can value streams. Pierre Labat, who previously served as Vice President and General Manager, Can Europe, assumed the function of Head of the Automotive Value Stream from 1 September 2014. He most recently served as Vice President and General Manager for the can business of Novelis in Europe.

APERAM has appointed Frederico Ayres Lima as Chief Operating Officer, Stainless & Electrical Steel, South America as of 1 December 2014, following the resignation of Clenio Guimarães.

Philippe Darmayan, CEO of Aperam, said: “Together with Tim di Maulo, who is taking my succession as Aperam’s CEO from 1 January 2015, I would like to extend our gratitude and appreciation for Clenio’s achievements as Chief Operating Officer, Stainless & Electrical Steel, South America. Clenio has successfully focused on implementing the Leadership Journey® and improving the competitiveness and performance of our Brazilian operations. We wish Clenio all the best in his new career and welcome Frederico aboard.”

Michael Hahne, formerly Sales Director Automotive Europe, succeeds Pierre Labat as the new Vice President and General Manager, Can Value Stream, Europe. Mr. Hahne joined Novelis in 2008 as Key Account Manager Automotive. Most recently, he was the Director Automotive Sales in Europe and Global Account Director, Daimler.

Aperam is a global player in stainless, electrical and specialty steel with customers in over 40 countries. The business is organized into three primary operating segments: Stainless & Electrical Steel, Services & Solutions and Alloys & Specialties. It has 2.5 million tonnes of flat stainless steel capacity in Brazil and Europe. ■

## SMDI appoints new head of automotive programmes

Novelis Inc. is a global leader in aluminium rolled products and the world's largest recycler of aluminium. It supplies aluminium sheet and foil products to various markets throughout North America, Europe, Asia and South America. ■

THE STEEL MARKET DEVELOPMENT INSTITUTE (SMDI), a business unit of the American Iron and Steel Institute (AISI), has confirmed that Dr. Jody N. Hall, former technical integration engineer at General Motors Company Global Body Manufacturing Engineering Centre in Warren, Michigan, will succeed Ronald P. Krupitzer as Vice President, Automotive Market for the Institute.

Dr. Hall received her doctorate in Materials Science and Engineering from the University of Michigan, under a General Motors Fellowship in 1994, and has spent a large part of her career in applying new steels and manufacturing techniques in automotive applications. Among other accomplishments, she has evaluated new auto technologies for implementation including new sheet steel grades and manufacturing processes, developed business cases and action plans for deployment of key new technologies and led global project implementations.
