# UCIMU elects new President

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

MASSIMO CARBONIERO, an entrepreneur from Veneto, is the new President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association). The Members Meeting elected him to lead the Association for the two-year period, 2016-2017.

Appointed as a member of the Board of Directors of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE in 2004, he was Vice-President from September 2008 to June 2016. General Manager and co-owner of OMERA, a specialist in metal forming machine tools, Massimo Carboniero is an active member of other associations and holds other Board memberships.

He is currently President of the Mechanics, Metallurgy and Electronics Department of Confindustria Vicenza and a member of the General Council of Confindustria (General Confederation of Italian Industry) and of Federmeccanica's National Executive. He is also a member of the Italian delegation of CECIMO, the European Association of Machine Tool Industries, as well as being managing director of FAIB, a company specialising in hot and cold die manufacturing for the automotive sector.

"We will continue asking the Italian government authorities to introduce a system of incentives for the upgrade of machinery installed in Italy, so that machines and factories can meet the new requirements for productivity, digitisation, energy-saving and compliance with EU regulations on safety in the workplace," said the new UCIMU President.

"UCIMU will also continue to highlight the needs and proposals of the sector to government authorities and to contribute, on the basis of the same institutions' demands, towards directing industrial policy proposals which concern the entire manufacturing industry. The next test bench will be the discussion on Industry 4.0. This discussion runs alongside the necessity for upgrading production machines and the introduction of the latest production systems in factories. And UCIMU is ready to play its part....."

## Roland Junck joins British Steel

ROLAND JUNCK, who once oversaw ArcelorMittal, has been named executive chairman of the new British Steel, the UK steelmaker has announced.

“Roland's extensive industry experience will be most valuable to British Steel and its turnaround plan," said Marc Meyohas, a partner with Greybull Capital, which acquired the business from Tata Steel earlier this year. British Steel was Tata's European long products' operation.

Junck has been advising British Steel's senior management team since mid-June. The company is continuing to search for a permanent chief executive and, once one is named, Junck will become British Steel's non-executive chairman. He is a steel industry veteran and had worked with Spain's Aceralia and successor company, Arcerlor. After Arcelor merged with Mittal Steel, he became the company's first chief executive.
