# SMACNA confirms new President and VP

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

The Sheet Metal and Air Conditioning Contractors' National Association (SMACNA), in the USA, has confirmed that Carol Duncan, CEO and owner of General Sheet Metal, has been elected by the Board of Directors to serve as President. Duncan's year-long term in office began at the close of SMACNAs 79th Annual Convention in Phoenix, USA, on 18 October 2023.

Duncan began her career in the sheet metal industry in 1982 at General Sheet-Metal, a Clackamas, Oregon-based company specialising in mechanical, architectural and HVAC fabrication, installation and repair services for commercial and industrial markets. She became the company's CEO in 2000 and has helped to grow the company from 25 employees to more than 180. She has been active on the national legislative front for over 25 years, serving as the Political Action Committee (PAC) Chair and Legislative Chair.

An active member of SMACNA both nationally and locally, Duncan has served in many roles and committees including Products & Programmes Coordinating Committee and SMACNA/SMART Best Practices Market Expansion Task Force. She has also been heavily involved in her local chapter, SMACNA Oregon & Southwest Washington.

“In her new role as President, Duncan will be focused on the BE4ALL initiative, a joint effort of SMACNA, SMART and the International Training Institute (iTi) to positively impact the industry's work culture and meet the real-world demand for the best, and most qualified, workforce available," commented SMACNA.

The association also confirmed that Scott Vidimos, President of Vidimos, Inc., has been elected by the Board of Directors to serve as Vice President. His year-long term in office also began at the close of SMACNA's 79th Annual Convention in Phoenix.

Vidimos began his career in the sheetmetal industry in 1979 as a project manager with Vidimos, Inc., an East Chicago, Indiana-based company specialising in duct collection, steel fabrication and industrial ventilation systems. He became its President and third-generation leader in 2005.

An active member of SMACNA both nationally and locally, Vidimos has served in many roles and committees including SMACNA's Industrial Contractors Council Steering Committee; Technical Resources Committee; Budget and Finance Committee; Director Nominating Committee and as a SMACNA-IFUS Trustee. Vidimos has also been heavily involved in his local chapter, the Northern Indiana Sheet Metal Contractors Association.

SMACNA also announced the winners of its annual Contractor of the Year and Chapter Executive of the Year awards at its 79th Annual Convention in Phoenix, Arizona (USA). Contractor of the Year is Kevin Gill Jr., President of McCusker-Gill, and the Chapter Executive of the Year award was presented to James T. Strother Jr., Executive Director of SMACNA of Western Pennsylvania.

SMACNA is an international trade association representing 3,500 contributing contractor firms and promotes quality and excellence in the sheet-metal and airconditioning industry. It has national offices in Chantilly (VA) and on Capitol Hill in the USA.
