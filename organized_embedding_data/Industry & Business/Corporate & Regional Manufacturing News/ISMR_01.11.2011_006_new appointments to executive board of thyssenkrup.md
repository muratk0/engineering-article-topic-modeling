# NEW APPOINTMENTS TO EXECUTIVE BOARD OF THYSSENKRUPP STEEL EUROPE AG

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

IN ITS MEETING on 10 August 2011, the Supervisory Board of ThyssenKrupp Steel Europe AG made some personnel decisions that became necessary as Dr. Ulrich Jaroni is to leave the Executive Board and retire in 2012. The ‘Production’ directorate is being split into two directorates, ‘Metallurgy’ and ‘Rolling and Metallic Coating’.

Dr. Herbert Eichelkraut, currently member of the management board of the Steel Americas business area and also CEO and COO of ThyssenKrupp CSA in Brazil, is to join the Executive Board of ThyssenKrupp Steel Europe AG as head of ‘Metallurgy’ effective 1 January 2012. It has not yet been decided who will succeed him in his current positions. Dr. Heribert Fischer, currently head of the ‘Duisburg Hot & Cold Rolling and Galvanizing’ corporate department, will become the Executive Board member responsible for the ‘Rolling and Metallic Coating’ directorate effective from 1 October 2011. The ‘Research and Development’ department will now also fall under the responsibility of this directorate.

By resolution of the Supervisory Board, Peter Urban, Chief Financial Officer of ThyssenKrupp Steel Europe AG, has additionally been appointed head of ‘Controlling’ on the management board of the Steel Americas business area with immediate effect. The post became vacant when his predecessor, Reinhard Florey, switched to the management board of the Stainless Global business area.
