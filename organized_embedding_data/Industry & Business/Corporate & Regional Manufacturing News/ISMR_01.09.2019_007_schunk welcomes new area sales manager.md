# Schunk welcomes new area sales manager

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

SCHUNK, A SPECIALIST in gripping and clamping systems, has welcomed a new area sales manager to its expanding UK team. As the latest addition to the team, Jason Hardwidge will be covering the East Midlands region of the UK.

"Jason is a self-motivated sales professional with a strong engineering background. With extensive manufacturing expertise, he will be a key addition to the existing sales team at Schunk, as the company continues to capitalise on its rapid growth and the re-location to a new and larger UK headquarters," said the company.

"With fifteen years in the industry, Jason has worked with a number of leading engineering and cutting tool manufacturers."

SCANLAB GMBH, AN OEM manufacturer of laser scan systems, has appointed Christian Sonner as Head of Research & Development (R&D).

The experienced control expert is expected to strengthen the company's market position in digital products and control solutions for the photonics industry. Under his leadership, the R&D department and its circa 70 employees will increasingly focus on highly integrated scan solutions. Sonner has extensive expertise in developing system solutions, and in his new role he will drive forward interdisciplinary collaboration at SCANLAB.

Christian Sonner has been the company's software development manager since 2013. Under his leadership, scan control solutions for precSYS and XL SCAN were developed. After earning an applied mathematics degree, he initially specialised in industrial robot controllers, focusing on functionality for redundant kinematics at previous employers, such as KUKA.
