# INDIAN OFFICE FOR LOSMA

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

Losma s.p.a. announced the recent opening of its Indian subsidiary, Losma India Pvt. Ltd. This is its fourth international subsidiary, in addition to the ones in USA, Germany and UK.

“Through our assembly and sales activities, we are responding to the strong market demand in India for eco-compatible solutions in workshops and workplaces,” Losma told ISMR. “The Indian subsidiary, which currently has a staff of five with more planned, will also develop new activities in an area of rapid economic growth.”

Losma, with its head office based in Curno in the province of Bergamo, Italy, is an international leader in air and coolant filtration for machine tools.
