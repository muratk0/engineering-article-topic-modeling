# Management changes for DENSO

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

DENSO CORPORATION HAS announced management changes, effective from 1 April 2018, as follows:

Toshiyuki Kato was appointed as executive vice president. Kazuaki Fujitani, Yukihiro Shinohara and Yoshitaka Kajita were appointed as senior executive directors. Hidehiro Yokoo; Koichi Nagaya; Hirotaka Yato; Kazuhiro Iwai; Hiroshi Kondo; Seiji Maeda and Eiji Inoue have become executive directors. Norio Fujimori became an executive fellow.

Yoshikazu Makino, Michio Adachi and Yukihiko Murakami resigned as senior executive directors and Hiroyuki Murayama resigned as an executive fellow.

DENSO also decided the following changes to members of the Board as well as Board Members on its audit and supervisory board. These changes will be effective after the 95th Annual General Meeting of Shareholders, 2018.

Shoji Tsuzuki will be appointed as a Member of the Board. Currently, he is a senior executive director. Nobuaki Katoh, Koji Kobayashi and Yoshikazu Makino will resign as Members of the Board. Motomi Niwa will be appointed as Member of the Audit & Supervisory Board. Masato Iwase will resign as a Member of the Audit & Supervisory Board.
