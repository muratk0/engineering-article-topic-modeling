# New safety innovations on show at SIL 2024

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

Advanced Microwave Engineering (AME), a provider of occupational safety technology, showcased its latest advancements at SIL Barcelona in Spain (5-7 June 2024). AME's latest offerings, Amesphere Vista and Amesphere One, reinforce its commitment to providing active safety solutions.

AME CEO, Filippo Bonifacio, said: “We were excited to connect with industry leaders and present our latest safety innovations at SIL Barcelona. In Spain, a profound emphasis is placed on preemptive safety protocols, a paradigm that has catalysed our substantial growth trajectory over the years. Our expertise in technology plays a key role in strengthening safety standards for vehicles and operators, and we are enthusiastic about driving its development in the Spanish market."

AME offers a comprehensive safety ecosystem designed to create a safer working environment inside complex industrial settings. At its core is Amesphere Alpha, the flagship product, offering real-time plant safety monitoring through wearable tags and in-vehicle sensors. These sensors transmit pedestrian locations to a designated driver's tablet, known as the 'Amesphere tablet'. The data generated is then processed through the cloud-based Amesphere platform, where advanced algorithms analyse it to produce the Efficiency Safety Indicator (ESI). This metric offers insights into both safety and operational efficiency, providing a holistic view of workplace safety.

AME recently expanded the Amesphere ecosystem with two additions:

■ Amesphere Vista: This solution enhances the existing Local Positioning System (LPS) functionality of Amesphere by utilising integrated AI cameras for precise detection of pedestrians, regardless of whether they are wearing tags.

■ Amesphere One: This is designed specifically for smaller vehicles and offers a compact, all-in-one sensor with customisable detection zones for advanced collision avoidance.

“Amesphere Vista and Amesphere One further strengthen our vision to provide comprehensive safety solutions across industries. Our solutions are contemporary and is designed to help users to continuously analyse data using algorithms to assess risks, trends and insights and transform raw data into actionable insights. With our latest offering, we hope to help businesses to take proactive steps towards a safer and more efficient workplace," added Bonifacio.

AME Srl specialises in workplace safety technologies with headquarters in Florence (Italy) and offices in Stuttgart (Germany) and Virginia (USA) as well as more than 1100 customers and partners in over 20 countries on all continents. It has 25 years of experience in the research and development of integrated Safety & Security solutions.
