# Material hardness testing

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

Since October 2020, Buehler – ITW Test & Measurement GmbH, a manufacturer of metallographic and hardness testing equipment, consumables and accessories, has been cooperating in a strategic partnership with OMNILAB-Laborzentrum GmbH & Co. KG, one of Germany’s largest independent specialist laboratory distributors.

The objective of this collaboration, for the EMEA region, is to boost efficiency and increase customer satisfaction. With an expanded inventory, response times for the delivery of equipment, spare parts and consumables have been shortened. Buehler’s sales, service, marketing and application teams will continue to serve existing customers in Europe from its European headquarters in Esslingen and further sites in Germany, as well as in France and the United Kingdom.

OMNILAB, founded in 1935 and headquartered in Bremen, serves the research, industrial and scientific markets and is today a globally active laboratory equipment supplier delivering products, consulting expertise and individual solutions for laboratory work. The group has some 140 employees in Germany and three international subsidiaries.

As CEO Nils Herrmann explained, “Doubling the number of products held in stock and extending shipping hours will make Buehler synonymous with rapid fulfilment and seamless customer service throughout Europe and the Middle East and parts of Africa too.”

Buehler is part of the Test and Measurement segment of the U.S. company Illinois Tool Works (ITW), with some 800 decentralised divisions in 52 countries and around 45,000 employees.
