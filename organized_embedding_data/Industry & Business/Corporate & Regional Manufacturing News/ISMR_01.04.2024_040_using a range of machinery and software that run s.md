METAFLEX serves various markets in Austria including the roofing, industry and industrial buildings' sectors. The industry segment has been the fastest growing sector for the company, spanning HVAC production to custom metal fabrication.

## Smart automation

Gerhard Resch's journey with CIDAN Machinery Group began in 2019 with his investment in "The Base," a comprehensive production line that operates from coil to finished trim with no human intervention. It was equipped with automation and powered by smart nuEVOLUTION software.

"A single employee can operate 'The Base', which produces up to 500 tons of metal sheet per year (running for ten hours per day). The operator doesn't need to program anything; the preparatory steps are handled seamlessly in the nuEVOLUTION software, thanks to the efforts of just 2.5 administrative employees handling 3,500 customer orders per year," commented bending specialist, CIDAN.

"In 2020, METAFLEX added the durable THALMANN TZ long metal folder to its machinery portfolio. The same year, it acquired the CIDAN FORMA folding machine, a versatile and user-friendly solution. We also delivered the CIDAN EVO DUO shear, driven by two lownoise gear motors," added CIDAN.

In 2021, Gerhard's Resch's commitment to meeting customer demands led to the acquisition of the FORSTNER nuSLIT, a versatile slitting machine with touch-screen control for various functions.

In 2023, METAFLEX continued its quest for smart and efficient production solutions. It added the rapid CIDAN F32 folding machine with XSPACE. The CIDAN RAPIDO SSM shear, designed for cutting soft or scratch-sensitive materials, and the FORSTNER STRAIGHT for efficient coil cutting were also integrated into METAFLEX operations.

## A productivity boost

Thanks to its strategic investments over the past four years, METAFLEX has witnessed a significant boost in its productivity. In 2006, with just two employees, it produced 100 tons of metal sheet. By 2010, with five employees, that number rose to 300 tons. As of 2023, with six employees, it has scaled up production to 800 tons of metal sheet.

Gerhard Resch emphasized the importance of machines and software that run seamlessly, and praised CIDAN Machinery Group for providing a comprehensive portfolio of solutions that align with his needs. He particularly values the ability to connect all his machines, enabling fast and high-quality profiles for his customers. Because of this, he received fourth prize in the ALC (Austria's Leading Companies) Awards 2023 for companies who register up to ten million euros of turnover.

He also highlighted the partnership aspect of working with CIDAN Machinery Group.

"CIDAN has supported me from the very beginning and has continued to be a strong partner as my business has grown, meeting the increasingly demanding requirements of my customers. This collaborative approach has been pivotal to METAFLEX's success story," he concluded.■

## About CIDAN Machinery Group

The CIDAN Machinery Group is a full-range supplier of sheet-metal machinery and control solutions for multiple industries. Its headquarters are in Götene, Sweden. It operates manufacturing locations in Sweden, Switzerland and Austria, with office locations in several European countries, the United States and China.

Its acquisition in March 2022 of Swiss folding specialist, Thalmann Maschinenbau AG, has added another company to the Group that includes CIDAN Machinery Austria GmbH (formerly coil-processing line specialist, Forstner Maschinenbau GmbH) and nuIT GmbH (which manufactures control software). CIDAN itself (formerly Göteneds) specialises in sheet metal folders, shearing systems and rollformers.
