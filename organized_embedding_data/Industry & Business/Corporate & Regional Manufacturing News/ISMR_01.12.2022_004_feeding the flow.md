# FEEDING THE FLOW

[TARİH: 01.01.2022 ISMR]

Michael Kron, Sales Director EMEA & Senior Vice President Market Division Northern Europe, Bystronic, outlined his views on sheet metal markets, automation technologies and Smart Factory trends to ISMR.

Bystronic is a leading global company in sheet metal processing. It has over 3,000 employees worldwide at over 40 sites and generated a net revenue of CHF 801 million in 2020. Its wide portfolio includes laser cutting, tube processing, bending and automation products and services, as well as a range of software and Smart Factory solutions for the networked manufacturing environment. Its focus is on automation of the complete material and data flow of the cutting and bending process chain.

Bystronic's operational headquarters are at Niederönz, Switzerland. Additional development and production locations are in Sulgen (Switzerland); Gotha (Germany); Cazzago San Martino and San Giuliano Milanese (Italy); Tianjin and Shenzhen (China); and Hoffman Estates (USA). It is represented by its own sales and service subsidiaries in more than 30 countries and by agents in numerous other countries.

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus for the company?

MK: We are transitioning from a machine builder to a software company. Networking of individual production cells through to entire production leads to optimisation of the flow of goods. This year, we acquired software specialist Kurago and, together, we have developed customised software that enables our customers to also integrate the machines and processes of our competitors into their production lines (from stand-alone machines to the fully networked factory). These are exactly the solutions that we want to offer our customers.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals? What about the climate in 2022?

MK: The market will clearly move towards digitalisation in our industry. Increased efficiency and quality, increased production speed and precision can only be achieved by automating manufacturing. In laser cutting machines, there is a trend towards more laser power which is why our R&D initiatives are also moving in this direction. Customers also want automation in the bending process. For all this, we need powerful software solutions that coordinate and optimise these processes. Digitalisation is, therefore, a powerful force in our industry.

ISMR: How has the COVID-19 crisis affected your operations and which steps have you taken in response?

MK: The pandemic also did not spare Bystronic. Luckily, we made the decision to keep all our employees and continued to work on all customer projects. The COVID-19 virus helped us to further drive digitalisation within our organisation; we increased digital meetings and training in response. Our colleagues displayed admirable flexibility and commitment throughout this period.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers right now and how are you addressing these issues for them?

MK: Our customers are facing more competition, smaller batch sizes, higher deadline pressure and shorter delivery times. They are required to produce even faster now. These are significant challenges to overcome. To meet these challenges, our customers need to optimise their production and make it even more efficient. Customers come to us to help them increasingly automate and optimise their production.

ISMR: What, in your view, are important industry trends, challenges and opportunities? How are you responding to these?

MK: The trend is clearly towards automation. Many of our customers already own more than one machine from us. Initially, they may have ordered a laser cutting machine or a press brake. Later, a loading and unloading system would have been added. When production is automated, efficiency can be significantly increased. With our scalable system solutions, we combine machines, automation and software to create a flexible network of intelligent components. If other elements are also integrated, we now start talking about a smart factory. We are currently rolling out our Smart Factory software to test customers and plan to launch it in the first quarter of 2022, helping our customers to stay ahead of the competition.

ISMR: Which products, technologies or services have you released this year?

MK: This year, our tube processing division introduced the ByTube Star 130, a high-end tube laser cutting system with fully automatic set-up for open profiles and ellipses, ensuring greater flexibility and quality in production. Since 85 per cent of the market potential in the segment is in small tubes, the ByTube Star 130 is equipped to process tubes with diameters of 10 to 130mm and raw material lengths of up to 8.5 metres.

## ByTube Star 130

Bystronic's new ByTube Star 130 tube laser cutting system handles metallic materials from 10 to 130mm and raw material lengths of up to 8.5 metres. The automated system reduces manual intervention to a minimum and has a loading capacity of up to 17kg per metre.

"Available in two power levels, two or three kilowatts, the fibre laser unit of the ByTube Star 130 offers high energy efficiency as well as consistently even cutting quality. With its 2D cutting head, 90 per cent of all customer requests can be fulfilled, since vertical cuts make up the largest market share," Bystronic told ISMR. Additional options can be configured individually.

Automatic weld seam detection enables automatic alignment of the weld seam to the required position. With Laserscan, the function independently recognizes and compensates for geometric deviations in tubes, to ensure the accuracy of cutting processes regardless of raw material quality. With the ByVision Tube operating system, users progress from receiving orders to delivering finished parts.

Bystronic also provides the ByTube Star 130 laser cutting system in a design with a 'mirrored' configuration upon request. This enables users to select the best layout for their individual production flow.

Our 15kW XXL laser cutting systems in the ByStar Fiber family were extremely well received by the market. In terms of software, we launched our own BySoft CAM software. Modern sheet metal production is no longer conceivable without powerful software. BySoft CAM offers a comprehensive range of functions and yet is easy to use. Our customers can, with BySoft CAM, always maintain an overview of their production and complete orders quickly, cost-effectively and reliably.

We are also working on the expansion of our after-sales service and will continue refining our service offering next year.

ISMR: How are you leveraging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Big Data, VR, robotics etc. for your business. How have you positioned Bystronic with respect to Industry 4.0?

MK: Innovation is the lifeblood of Bystronic. Over 50% of our revenue comes from products that are no more than three years old.

How do we do this? We have an agile innovation process consisting of three phases: spotting, developing and creating. We use our trend radar to spot early trends in the market. Using the Bystronic Venture Fund, we can invest in and collaborate with start-ups. And, most importantly, we have a co-creation approach to our innovation. More than 80% of our innovation is co-created with customers.
