# AISI AWARD WINNER

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

CCFSS Director Roger LaBoube was recently a winner of the 2010 Market Development Industry Leadership Award in Boca Raton, Florida at the American Iron and Steel Institute’s (AISI’s) General Meeting.

“In the US, the name of Dr Roger LaBoube is synonymous with cold-formed steel construction. He is the recognized authority on cold-formed steel, with extensive industrial and academic experience in design, teaching and research," said AISI Chairman, Dan DiMicco.

Roger A. LaBoube, Ph.D. (centre) receives the 2010 Market Development Industry Leadership Award from AISI Chairman, Dan DiMicco.

“Roger has contributed significantly to the body of research that undergirds AISI S100, the North American Specification for the Design of Cold-Formed Steel Structural Members, which is the primary reference for cold-formed steel construction. Roger has also been a driving force behind the success of AISI’s Committee on Framing Standards.”

As Director of the Wei-Wen Yu Center for Cold-Formed Steel Structures, Dr LaBoube has promoted the use of cold-formed steel construction through technical service, engineering education, research and professional activities.
