## Epicor eyes EMEA

EPICOR SOFTWARE Corporation, a leader in business software solutions for manufacturing, distribution, retail and services organisations, has announced the appointment of Keith Deane as senior vice president and general manager for Europe, Middle East and Africa (EMEA). Reporting to Epicor President and Chief Executive Officer, Pervez Qureshi, Deane will lead operations in EMEA including all sales, services, support and marketing across the region.

In this new role, Deane will be focused on revenue growth and expanding the market for Epicor enterprise resourcing planning (ERP) solutions throughout the region. Deane brings more than twenty years of sales, operations and software industry experience to Epicor, having worked for leading ERP, SaaS, e-commerce, middleware, infrastructure, process management and business intelligence software providers during his career. Under Deane’s leadership as the head of EMEA operations at Infor, revenue more than quadrupled in the region. ■

## New appointment at MMK Metalurji

MMK METALURJI, part of the MMK Group, has appointed Sergei Sulimov, deputy general director for company relations at MMK Group, as acting general director of MMK Metalurji. Yury Bodyayev, previously general director of MMK Metalurji, will return to his position as director of production at MMK.

MMK CEO Boris Dubrovsky said: “These changes are driven by the new goals facing MMK Metalurji. Yury Bodyayev oversaw changes to production at MMK Metalurji, in particular the introduction of 1mm hot-rolled coil. Now our main priority is to build effective business processes and boost the company’s efficiency. Sergei Sulimov will take charge of achieving this.”

Construction of the facility in Turkey began in March 2008. The facility has a production capacity of 2.3m tons of flat steel per year, and is located at two production sites in Iskenderun and Istanbul. Total investment in the project came to around US$ 2.1 billion.

MMK Metalurji currently operates the electric arc furnace facility, the cold-rolling mill, galvanizing and colour coating lines and two service centres – in Iskenderun and Istanbul. To supply raw and input materials to the Iskenderun plant and ship its products to customers, a sea port capable of catering ships of up to 100,000 dwt was built and operates next to the production site. The electric arc furnace facility in Iskenderun was commissioned in March 2011. The first hot-rolled coil was produced in May 2011.

MMK is one of the world’s largest steel producers and a leading Russian metals company. ■
