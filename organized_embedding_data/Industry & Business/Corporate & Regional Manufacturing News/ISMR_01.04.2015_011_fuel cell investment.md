# Fuel cell investment

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

Mats Wallin, CEO of Cell Impact (left) and Magnus Baarman, CEO and managing director of AP&T (right)

PRESS SPECIALIST AP&T and Cell Impact AB have signed a long-term cooperative agreement with a focus on production lines for formed plates for heat exchangers, fuel cells and electrolyzers. The cooperation initiative entails the integration of Cell Impact’s High Velocity Impact Units with AP&T’s solution for volume production of heat exchanger and flow plates.

“This enables a competitive solution for bending and forming flow plates to be offered to the energy and automotive industry. The competitiveness of the solution is based on lower manufacturing costs and improved plate quality. The agreement will initially apply through to 31 December 2017 and thereafter be extended for two years at a time,” explained AP&T.

“AP&T has been developing fully automated production lines for heat exchanger plates for several years. By integrating Cell Impact’s High Velocity Impact Units, we can increase the level of quality and improve plate tolerance further. With the growing market for fuel cells, we believe that the solution can enable production lines for high volumes at a very favourable cost. Formalising the previous declaration of intent with Cell Impact in this cooperation agreement signals our belief that the time has come to invest in the fuel cell industry,” added Magnus Baarman, CEO and managing director of AP&T.
