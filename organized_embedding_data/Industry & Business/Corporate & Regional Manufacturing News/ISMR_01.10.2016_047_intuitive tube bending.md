# Intuitive tube bending

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

ON BOOTH N7100 at this year's FABTECH (16-18 November 2016) in Las Vegas (USA), German tube bending machine manufacturer, Schwarze-Robitec, will be dedicating its focus to customer service. After reorganising its marketing management division in the spring of 2016 and its subsequent focus on international markets, the ITA (International Tube Association) member plans to further expand its services at its US site in Michigan.

Since last year's opening of its US subsidiary, Schwarze-Robitec America Inc., the team has worked continuously together with experts from Germany to expand its services.

"To support our customers in the key markets of the US, Canada and Mexico even better, and to supply them with spare parts, we are expanding our network of experts and increasing our storage capacity in the US," said Bert Zorn, Managing Director, Schwarze-Robitec. The focus is on the individual customer's benefits.

Its new CNC 80 E TB MR tube bending system, with optimised cutting tool, will be on display at its FABTECH booth. This machine was developed for the series production of tube systems, commonly used in the automotive industry. It is equipped with innovative tool solutions and was developed in close cooperation with the customer. A disconnecting device, integrated into the bending tool, makes it possible to bend and separate tube components at random lengths in a single step.

"Benefits include a decrease in scrap material of up to 90 per cent and reduced production times. An additional highlight of the machine is the intuitive CNC system. During implementation of the current process, the system is already preparing the next step for efficient operation," explained the manufacturer. See www.schwarze-robitec.com
