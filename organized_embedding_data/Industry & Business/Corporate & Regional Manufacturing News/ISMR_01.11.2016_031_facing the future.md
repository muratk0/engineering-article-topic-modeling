# FACING THE FUTURE

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: “The Turkish manufacturer is re-positioning for the future"

A. Vasif Inan, Managing Director, MVD Makina Sanayi AS

MVD Makina's managing director, A. Vasif Inan, discusses his company's growth objectives with ISMR at EuroBLECH

MVD Makina has been manufacturing machines since 1950 and sheet metal processing machine tools, such as press brakes, shears, punching and laser machines, since 1971. In 1994, it told ISMR, it produced the first tandem press brake in Turkey. The family-owned company exports to over 80 countries and aims to be one of the largest manufacturers of sheet metal machine tools in the European market.

ISMR caught up with its managing director, A. Vasif Inan, on its booth at this October's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, to find out the latest new company developments.

## Re-positioning for the future

"Over the past year, we have restructured our company, re-organised many of the departments, re-allocated responsibilities, started an ERP system and improved our CRM system. We have also improved our product quality control system, delivery time reliability and the speed of product development/R&D. New training programmes in the company have been initiated and we have spent the time positioning ourselves for the future," Inan told ISMR.

"Our targets for next year are to increase global sales and to add new products to our product lines – we expect a 20% growth in sales. The main markets for us are Europe (west and east), the Middle East and America. Currently, we have around 50 distributors worldwide and are planning to open an office and showroom in the US."

At this year's EuroBLECH exhibition, the company was showcasing new features and options on its press brakes as well as its new laser cutting system (new controllers, higher power levels etc.). It also introduced its new "fast Shear" (iShear series) to the European market.

Its product portfolio for press brakes, lasers and shears are grouped across three clear brands - iBend, iLaser and iShear (A, B and C series). Its iLaser uses an IPG fibre laser source and Precitec cutting head, features an automatic loading/unloading shuttle table. Its iBend C series of press brakes can configure up to a maximum of 12 axes, has large throat depth and stroke, a height-adjustable control panel and automatic height-adjusted front support system. Its iShear B series features two cutting speeds, automatic back gauge and blade gap adjustments, cut length programming and multiple sheet support.

## An eye on growth

"The EuroBLECH exhibition is very useful for us - we meet our distributors and suppliers here. We welcome new contacts and are always on the look-out for new dealers in countries. The quality of the visitors this year is much better - we have made lots of new contacts in Europe, the Far East and the US," explained Inan.

Industry 4.0 is also a key area for the company. It is discussing, with customers and software suppliers, how best to meet the future needs of its users in a 'connected' way. It is already offering more software options on its machines and has been developing robotic applications on its bending machines, with programmed production.

"This has been a growth year for us. There have been some issues with markets in Russia and Turkey – the drop in oil prices has also affected markets in the Middle East. However, we have increased sales volumes from our existing customers and have also sold into new countries. I think we will be looking at a 15% growth in production quantity and around 20% growth in turnover this year. There is still a lot of production in Turkey going on so I believe that the Turkish market will also grow next year," concluded Inan.■
