# Herren Frères & Cie has expanded its range of industrial sheet-metal processing services with its new ByStar Fiber 3015 20kW automated, high-speed laser cutting machine.

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

“A philosophy of continuous investment and innovation, with a focus on quality and service, are integral parts of the Swiss manufacturer's approach."

ISMR SAYS:

Switzerland's Yverdon-les-Bains, was founded in 1955 by brothers Robert and Marcel Herren as a craft business for sanitary technology and construction plumbing. Over the years, and with the arrival of the next generation, it has developed into a group of companies that today provides sanitary, plumbing and solar systems, as well as industrial sheet metalworking, laser-/waterjet cutting and real estate.

The operational activities of Herren's current 85 employees are underpinned by its guiding principle of what it describes as "quality-oriented services to private, trade and industrial customers from a wide range of sectors"

The brothers recognised early on that it was not just their own sanitary and plumbing business that repeatedly needed individual sheet-metal parts, but other tradesmen also needed these parts. Consequently, they invested in the production of sheet-metal parts and, from that point onwards, also operated as a service provider. This developed further into industrial sheet-metal processing, laser and waterjet cutting, for which Alexandre Herren is responsible.

To better serve its trade and industry customers, a decision was made as early as 1992 to continuously invest in the latest technology and it became one of the first companies in western Switzerland to invest in laser cutting.

## Continuous investment culture

Herren likes to take on challenges, evolve, innovate and meet the multiple and varied needs of its customers. Quality, service and well-trained staff, as well as a culture of continuous investment in technical equipment, are integral parts of its approach.

The acquisition of a laser cutting machine from Swiss manufacturer Bystronic, with a laser power of 2500W, unveiled new possibilities for sheet-metal and tube processing, which increase Herren to expand its range of services and increase its appeal to other industrial companies. Alexandre Herren commented on his company's technology and investment philosophy.

Above and below: The ByTrans automatic loading and unloading system on the new laser cutting machine for self-sufficient, fully automatic order production in all batch sizes. On the right is the pallet loading/unloading station.

"My father, Robert, had already distinguished himself as a technology pioneer when he entered CNC technology as early as 1984 and his company was the first Swiss SME to buy a CNC punching machine. We aim to stand out from the competition and provide our customers with the latest technologies.

Anyone who is familiar with Swiss sheet-metal processing companies knows about the need to differentiate oneself through exceptional performance, strong productivity, economic efficiency and high quality to survive in the market over the long run," he explained.

Industrial sheet metal activities

Today, Herren Frères & Cie has a machinetool portfolio that encompasses all the necessary technologies. Bystronic Laser AG has been a very important partner to the company since 1992 and, over the years, has installed three laser cutting machines with 2.5-, 4- and 6kW laser power, two high-pressure waterjet cutting systems and ten press brakes. The most recent example of this successful cooperation over the years is a new, high-performance fibre laser cutting centre (ByStar Fiber 3015) with 20kW laser power, which replaces Herren Frères & Cie's older 2.5kW laser cutting machine.

## Accent on productivity and quality

To move further ahead in terms of production, efficiency, quality and increasing competitive strength, Alexandre Herren's philosophy moved to embrace fibre lasers with even higher powers and high degrees of automation. Its visit to Blechexpo 2021 (in Stuttgart, Germany), where Bystronic introduced the ByStar Fiber 3015 fibre laser cutting machine with 20kW laser power to a broad trade audience, ultimately led to an order for this laser cutting system.

"This high-end laser cutting system processes sheets in the 3000 x 1500mm format and offers simultaneous positioning speeds of up to 170m/min with highly dynamic X/Y axis drives with a repeat accuracy of 0.025mm. Equipped with a shuttle table system, table-change time is a rapid 25 seconds so that unproductive downtime is reduced to a minimum. The laser cutting machine is equipped with a loading and unloading system, enabling the fully automatic processing of orders of all sizes," outlined Bystronic.

"The most important component of the system is, of course, the 20kW fibre laser which can cut steel sheets of up to 25/50mm thick; stainless steel of up to 40mm thick; aluminium of up to 40mm thick; brass of up to 20mm thick and copper of up to 20mm thick. Using the new cutting head, consistently high quality is also guaranteed with high laser powers for demanding cutting applications," it added.
