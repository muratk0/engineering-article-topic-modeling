# Schuler sells technology centre in Tianjin to Feintool

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

THE SCHULER GROUP has sold its technology centre in the North Chinese city of Tianjin to the Swiss company, Feintool. Based in Lyss, Switzerland, Feintool plans to produce precision components for the automotive industry in Tianjin using Schuler presses. Schuler itself continues to expand its market position in China as a leading manufacturer of metalforming equipment. The purchase agreement is expected to be closed at the end of March 2017. Feintool intends to expand production capacity in Tianjin during the course of the year with the use of Schuler technology.

The Chairman of the Board of Directors of Feintool, Alexander von Witzleben, explained that Feintool's investment in China was in response to customer demands for global presence and world-wide expertise. "This will enable us to close a strategic gap in Asia," he said.

Feintool will retain all 30 or so employees and the infrastructure of the plant in Tianjin, as well as existing customer orders.

## Success for SteelFab

THE 13TH STEELFAB 2017, which was held at Expo Centre Sharjah in the UAE from 16-19 January 2017, featured 342 exhibitors (197 overseas exhibitors and 145 local companies) who displayed more than 1000 international brands from 43 countries. The event attracted 7,950 visitors from 70 countries during its four-day run.

According to the show organiser, Expo Centre Sharjah, the Middle Eastern market is showing signs of a pick-up with the last two months of 2016 and the beginning of 2017 reflecting this sentiment. It told /SMR that exhibitors and the industry, in general, are optimistic of improving oil prices and more work from World Expo 2020 projects.

"Visitors from countries such as Afghanistan; Algeria; Armenia; Azerbaijan; Egypt; Ghana; Kazakhstan; Kenya; Libya; Nigeria; Sudan; Tanzania; Tunisia; Uganda and Ukraine indicated the acceptance of the show as a regional sourcing platform. SteelFab also covers the evolving African economies and Central Asian countries and is a 'must-visit' trade show for metal manufacturing and steel fabrication," Expo Centre Sharjah told /SMR.

Trade visitor pre-registration crossed the key 5,000-mark for the first time. With a series of special segments such as Stainless Steel, Wear Resistant & Hard Facing Cladding, Welding & Cutting, Machine Tools and Pipe & Tube Machinery, SteelFab 2017 became a gathering point for fabricators to shop for tools and machinery and discover new solutions for metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs. There were large international country pavilions from Germany and Taiwan and strong participation from countries such as Turkey, China and India.

"According to the latest research by the National Bank of Abu Dhabi, the UAE is expected to lead economic growth in the GCC this year amid higher spending spurred by recovering oil prices, while another report forecast that Saudi Arabia and the UAE will have the lion's share of projects spending between US$112 billion and US$ 151 billion in 2017, compared to US$ 109 billion in 2016. An immediate rise in orders for regional steel fabricators are likely to materialise through recently launched projects such as the multi-billion dollar Dubai Harbour project, and the US$ 15.6 billion infrastructure spending by Kuwait, among others," added the show organiser.

The 14th edition of SteelFab is scheduled to be held from 15-18 January 2018.■
