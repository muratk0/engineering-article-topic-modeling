# CAM Process Technologies confirms new VP

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

CAM PROCESS TECHNOLOGIES, LLC (CAM Pro), the fabrication entity of CAM Integrated Solutions, LLC (CAM) has confirmed the appointment of Douglas Fieldgate as the Vice President.

In Fieldgate's new role as Vice President of CAM Process Technologies, he will sit on CAM's Executive Leadership Team and will oversee all manufacturing operations. His career spans over 29 years of manufacturing, engineering and construction experience in the upstream, midstream and downstream oil and gas industries. His areas of expertise include business management; safety management; project sponsorship; business development; project management; capital project management; financial forecasting; operations and maintenance management.

Before joining CAM, Fieldgate served as the Senior Vice President of AECOM Oil and Gas where he led its United States Engineering, Fabrication, Operations and Maintenance and Mechanical Construction divisions.

Craig Pierrotti, CEO of CAM, added; "I have personally followed Doug's career as a proven industry leader and have seen him build and grow a new fabrication facility and business in West Africa from the ground up. Doug has a strong-minded safety culture and will look to build on CAM's already growing safety initiatives. This is a huge step for CAM Pro and I know that this is the right move as we continue to ramp up production."

CAM Process Technologies, the fabrication arm of CAM Integrated Solutions, allows CAM to offer a wide range of fabrication services specialising in ASME pressure Vessels, process piping, structural steel and modular fabrication for the upstream and midstream markets. The 82,000-square foot manufacturing facility in Katy (TX, USA) enables CAM to provide turnkey solutions for the engineering, fabrication and packaging of process and production equipment.
