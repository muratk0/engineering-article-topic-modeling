# Europe must focus on quality, not quantity

[TARİH: 01.04.2011 ISMR]

THE CONFERENCE ON the future of the Competitiveness and Innovation Programme (CIP) entitled “Ready to Grow?” brought together MEPs, European Commission representatives and other interested parties in an interactive discussion forum, enabling numerous questions to be asked and answered. European industrial focus should be on quality, not quantity, with the spotlight being steered towards SMEs to translate political commitments of the European Commission into real possibilities for supporting and building upon innovation policy. This was the key message from Antonio Tajani, Vice President of the European Commission and Commissioner for Industry and Entrepreneurship.
