# FORD’S FLEMING joins The Manufacturing Leadership Council Board

[TARİH: 01.03.2013 ISMR]

THE MANUFACTURING LEADERSHIP COUNCIL, a global business network for senior industrial executives, has announced the election of John Fleming, Executive Vice President of Global Manufacturing and Labour Affairs at Ford Motor Company, to the Manufacturing Leadership Council's Board of Governors.

In a unanimous vote, the Board of Governors, which steers the Manufacturing Leadership Council and its global community of more than 5,000 industry executives, approved Fleming's nomination at its quarterly meeting. With his appointment, The Board of Governor's ranks expand to 16 members, who hail from organisations such as Lockheed Martin; Dell; Cisco Systems; UCLA; MIT; L'Oreal and GlaxoSmithKline.

"Having spent my whole career in manufacturing, I have seen first-hand how the industrial sector is vital to the health of the global economy," said Fleming. "I look forward to working with the Manufacturing Leadership Council and my fellow Board members to explore new ways that industrial companies can create value, and a better future for manufacturing in these challenging times."

At Ford, Fleming is responsible for overseeing the entire global operations of 80 assembly, stamping and powertrain plants, plus engineering support for stamping, vehicle and powertrain manufacturing, and Ford's Material Planning and Logistics, Ford Production System, Manufacturing Business Office and Labour Affairs organisations. ■
