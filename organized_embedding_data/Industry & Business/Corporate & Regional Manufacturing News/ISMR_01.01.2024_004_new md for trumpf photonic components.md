# New MD for TRUMPF Photonic Components

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

TRUMPF subsidiary, Photonic Components, has a new managing director as of 1 December 2023. 58-year-old Dr. Thomas Harrer took over the role from Dr. Berthold Schmidt, who has been managing the business in Ulm (Germany) on an interim basis, in addition to his duties as a member of the Management Board of TRUMPF SE + Co KG and CTO.

Thomas Harrer began his career at TRUMPF in 1999 as an advisor to the Group Managing Director for Research and Development. After earning his doctorate in electrical engineering, he held various management positions within the TRUMPF Group. He is currently responsible for the Optical Systems Business Field and the Laser Application Centre within the Laser Technology Division.

"I am very pleased that we were able to win Thomas Harrer for the management of TRUMPF Photonic Components. He brings with him a high level of technological expertise and has a very good network in the industry and within the TRUMPF Group," said Berthold Schmidt, CTO of TRUMPF.

In his new role as managing director of TRUMPF Photonic Components, Dr. Thomas Harrer will continue to expand the VCSEL and photonics business and the technological expertise of the TRUMPF subsidiary. TRUMPF Photonic Components has been part of the TRUMPF Group since 2019 and is a global supplier of VCSEL and photodiode solutions for consumer electronics, data communications, automotive, industrial sensors and heating systems.

Approximately 300 people are employed worldwide at its headquarters in Ulm (Germany) and at locations in Aachen, the Netherlands; China; Korea; Taiwan and the USA.
