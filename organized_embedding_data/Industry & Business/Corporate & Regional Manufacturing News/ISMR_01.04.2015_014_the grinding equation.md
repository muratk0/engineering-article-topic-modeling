# The grinding equation

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

Estefano Zselics (Production Director, ZEMA), Rochus Mayer (CEO of the JUNKER Group) and Osnir Carlton (Development Director, ZEMA) celebrate their future cooperation

THE JUNKER GROUP is growing with the incorporation of Brazilian grinding machine manufacturer, ZEMA, as part of the corporate group. ZEMA was founded in 1953 and now has over 80 employees. It manufactures CNC grinding machines with conventional grinding wheels – up until now mainly for the Brazilian market. With the takeover, the JUNKER Group expands its expertise as a complete supplier in the grinding sector and now offers CBN grinding machines, corundum grinding machines and air filters.

The JUNKER Group, based in Nordrach in Germany, is a market leader in CBN high-speed grinding machines. To add to the portfolio, it has now secured a qualified majority share (over 75%) in ZEMA. ZEMA already supplies global players such as Bosch, ThyssenKrupp and Fiat in Brazil. ■
