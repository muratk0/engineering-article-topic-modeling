# Getting there

[TARİH: 01.03.2016 ISMR]

By air: Guglielmo Marconi International Airport in Bologna is directly connected to BolognaFiere by the AEROBUS BLQ shuttle.

By train: Bologna’s Stazione Centrale is 10 minutes from BolognaFiere. Bus lines 35, 35 and 38 link the station to the exhibition centre, P.zza Costituzione and Viale Aldo Moro entrances.

Railway Station -> BolognaFiere = lines 35 and 38
BolognaFiere -> Railway Station = lines 35 and 39
Centre <-> BolognaFiere and returning = line 28

By car: From Florence, Milan and Ancona: exit at the new “BOLOGNA FIERA” toll booth motorway A14. From Padua, ring road exit 8 for Nord, Michelino, Moro entrances and Michelino car park (ring road exit 7 for Costituzione entrance and car park). See www.autostrade.it/en/home

By bus: The Bologna Exhibition Centre can be reached every day with Tper buses 28 – 35 and 35 – 38 – 39. For information, see www.tper.it/ percorsi-orari/servizio-bacino-di-bologna
