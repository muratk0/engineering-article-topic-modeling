# MEDICAL FORUM FOR TRUMPF

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

Sheet metal specialist, TRUMPF Medical Systems, recently organised an expert forum near Stuttgart, Germany to discuss the trends and issues affecting the medical sector, as well as future technology requirements. On 20 July 2010, seven representatives from different hospitals and companies networked with 100 architects and planners on hybrid operating room trends, with a focus on hygiene and ergonomics.

“The first three expert fora are aimed at architects and planners. In the future, we want to offer topics for medical technicians or nurses. The next date is planned but the exact dates and topics will be posted on the company’s website,“ said Gabrielle Münch, Head of Sales Europe at TRUMPF Medical Systems.

The TRUMPF Group has a turnover of €1.66 billion. Under the umbrella of a holding company, there are three divisions – Machine Tools / Power Tools, Laser Technology / Electronics and Medical Technology. The core business is machine tools for flexible sheet metal processing for punching and forming, laser processing and bending. For industrial lasers and laser systems, the company is headquartered near Stuttgart in Ditzingen.
