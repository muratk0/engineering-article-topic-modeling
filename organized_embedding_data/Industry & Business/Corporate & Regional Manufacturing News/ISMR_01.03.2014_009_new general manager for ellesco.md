# New general manager for Ellesco

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

DEBURRING, finishing and polishing specialist, Ellesco, has announced the appointment of Guy Newton as its new general manager. As UK dealers for the deburring and surface finishing machine tools such as Timesavers, Kuhlmeyer, Autopulit and Sugino, the Christchurch company has appointed Mr Newton to review all aspects of the business and deliver growth and enhanced market presence for the business with a fresh and modern approach.

Since joining the company, Mr Newton has worked to support the modernising and streamlining of the business, enhancing the sales and service operation as well as driving the company's extensive consumable range into the marketplace. His appointment will enable company owner, Vincent Simonis, to develop the robotic, subcontract and consumable divisions of the company. ■
