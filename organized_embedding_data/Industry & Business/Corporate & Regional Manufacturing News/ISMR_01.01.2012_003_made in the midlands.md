# MADE IN THE MIDLANDS

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

THE UK’S Midlands region, a key manufacturing centre, has reported strong results. Made in the Midlands is an independent manufacturing collective of nearly 300 Midlands-based manufacturing and engineering SMEs with the sole purpose of collaborating, networking and furthering the regional agenda for the sector.

“It is the continued investment in plant, machinery and people that is sustaining the manufacturing sector and enabling it to grow in the future. It is not just the big names that report increased sales and increasing export orders. Small and medium-sized enterprises in the Midlands are also showing their hardiness and bucking the trend of dismal growth forecasts. Made in the Midlands continues to be the hub that brings these successful companies together to continue this,” commented Made in the Midlands’ Commercial Director, Charles Addison.

Midlands-based Professional Polishing Services is one such company, recently announcing the investment of nearly a quarter of a million pounds on new capital plant expansion. Analysts have been quick to temper previous optimism from UK manufacturers’ quarter one performance with a focus on apparently dwindling numbers in July. Some have attributed the decline to Olympics-related disruption and closedowns during the Jubilee. Nonetheless, the summer has seen phenomenal success in the UK aerospace industry, showcased recently at the Farnborough Air Show. The show saw the UK government announce a £120 million investment from government and industry in aerospace research and technology. The investment is a reflection of future plans to secure billions of pounds worth of new contracts for the aerospace sector during the next 15 to 20 years. ■

## New CCL in Turkey

BRONX RECENTLY commissioned a colour coating line for a customer in Turkey involved in the building and construction industry. Its continued success and demand for product led to a recent expansion including a second composite line and a Bronx Continuous Coil Coating Line (CCL) to produce its own colour-coated coil.

Bronx commissioned a 1600mm-wide colour coating line, designed to process 0.2mm to 0.8mm thick aluminium at speeds of up to 40mpm. The customer, Alcoat Metals, required a high quality aluminium coil coating line. Its product range includes PVDF, metallic and flurocarbon paint systems as well as modern pattern coatings. Bronx has worked closely with Alcoat to ensure a successful start-up and ongoing support through its Bronx Club to meet ongoing needs. ■

## EuroBLECH flies high

FROM 23 – 27 October 2012, the 22nd International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition will open its doors again in Hanover, Germany.

“As the world’s No. 1 exhibition and business barometer for the sheet metal working industry, the show offers its audience a comprehensive idea of the prevailing technological trends and economic situation. Currently the economic outlook for the industry sector is cautiously positive. Despite some uncertainty due to the global economic situation and the currency crisis in the Euro zone, the sheet metal working industry is stabilised and confident,” the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibition, explained.

A total of 1,525 exhibitors from 39 countries have already booked a net exhibition space of 85,000 square metres at EuroBLECH 2012. Compared to the previous event in 2010, this is an increase of 4% in exhibitor numbers and of 7% in exhibition space. A total of 61,500 trade visitors from all over the world attended the previous show.

Featuring a large number of live machine demonstrations, EuroBLECH is suitable for sheet metal working professionals to find smart solutions and equipment for their companies. With this year’s theme ‘For a sustainable future’, EuroBLECH puts a special focus on energyefficient technology, environmentally-friendly manufacturing processes and intelligent use of material.

“Major developments in sheet metal working relate to lightweight construction and processing of hybrid structures,” added Nicola Hamann, Exhibition Director EuroBLECH, on behalf of the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions. “To reflect this development, processing of sheet metal/plastic hybrid structures has now been included in the EuroBLECH exhibition profile that covers the complete sheet metal working technology chain.”

49% of this year’s exhibitors come from outside Germany. Asia and the Americas have now become an inherent part of the exhibition. Italy, China, Turkey, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Spain, Austria, France, Taiwan, UK and the USA are the largest exhibitor countries after Germany. EuroBLECH 2012 covers eight halls of the Hanover exhibition grounds. The exhibition will showcase a wide range of machines, tools and systems for sheet metal working, including high tech as well as conventional machinery and tools.
