# SCANLAB targets further growth

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

SCANLAB GmbH, a leading OEM manufacturer of laser scanning solutions, is expanding its management team at the start of 2022. To be better positioned to achieve its ambitious goals for its future and growth, it is expanding its operative management team. Long-standing head of finance, Dirk Thomas, and head of research & development, Christian Sonner, have been newly appointed to the management board.

Dirk Thomas has been part of the company's workforce for more than 20 years. As a new managing director, he will be responsible for Administration, Controlling, Human Resources and the IT department. Currently, digital transformation is a core element of SCANLAB's future strategy.

R&D director Christian Sonner has also been appointed to the Board of Managing Directors. He has been part of the SCANLAB family for nine years. In his new role, he will continue to drive the digitalisation of the company's product portfolio and the development of highly integrated scan systems.

SCANLAB CEO, Georg Hofner, has worked for the company since 1995. All three managing directors believe that a distinct focus on customers and partnerlike relationships with industry and scientific institutes are the company's most important guiding principles.

The previous COO, Christian Huttenloher, has left the company to focus on new challenges.
