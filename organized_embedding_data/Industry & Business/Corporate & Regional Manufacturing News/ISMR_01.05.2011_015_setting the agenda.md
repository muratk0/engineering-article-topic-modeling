# SETTING THE AGENDA

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

Janez Poje, presented the main goals for his mandate recently during ISTMA Europe’s meeting in Bologna, Italy. The intensification of contacts, promotion and visibility of the tool and die industry in Europe and within its policy makers as well as the repositioning of the sector within European strategic manufacturing priorities are top of his agenda.

He also intends to reinforce the relationship with the ManuFuture Initiative and the development of cooperation and networking activities with OEMs, technological and educational organisations and suppliers of the industry. Janez Poje has also highlighted the enlargement of ISTMA Europe’s membership, the organisation of the III and the IV European Tooling Forum and the activation of more companies into the reporting process as priorities.

During his Presidency, the Secretariat will be based in Portugal at Cefamol (the Portuguese Mouldmakers Association).

ISMR: PLEASE GIVE US SOME BACKGROUND DATA ON ISTMA– WHEN WAS IT FORMED, HOW MANY MEMBERS IT HAS, WHERE YOU ARE BASED ETC.

JP ISTMA was officially established in Frankfurt, in 1973 as ISTA. For around 32 years, a firm partnership for stamping tools, die and mould making, precision machining and special machines has evolved.

ISTMA is now an international association representing 24 special tooling and machining associations throughout the world. Whether a member is a toolmaker in Tokyo, a mould or die maker in Europe, a machinist in Canada or a special machine builder in the United States, the ISTMA represents them all.

Industry associations, member companies and employees benefit from ISTMA membership for its networking capabilities. Collectively, ISTMA member associations represent over 8,000 companies and about 40 billion euros in annual sales. United, under this ISTMA global roof, are the three regional chapters of the association: Americas, Asia and Europe.

ISMR: HOW DO YOU SEE THE ISTMA DEVELOPING OVER THE NEXT FEW YEARS? ARE THERE ANY MARKET OR TECHNOLOGICAL TRENDS THAT WILL AFFECT THIS?

JP ISTMA is an association that helps members to better understand the sector, gives benchmark indicators, facilitates member and institution networking, provides information regarding market trends and is active in studies on how to improve EU competitiveness and long term industrial policy etc.
