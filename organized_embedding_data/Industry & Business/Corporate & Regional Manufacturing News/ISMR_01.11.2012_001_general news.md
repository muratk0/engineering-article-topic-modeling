## On track for growth

MACHINE TOOL manufacturer Leifeld Metal Spinning AG has ambitious plans for the future according to the company’s CEO, Chairman of the Board, Gerhard Glanz. The Austrian who, in the past, propelled well-known companies such as UNION Werkzeugmaschinen Chemnitz and EMCO Group Österreich to international performance levels would like to double the revenue of Leifeld to over 50 million euros by 2017 and provide up to 100 new jobs in Ahlen.

Leifeld develops, manufactures and sells machine tools for chipless metal forming. As a global market leader for spinning and flowforming machines, it has completed more than 6,000 installations in over 60 countries.

“Leifeld is a well known international machine tool manufacturer whose name stands for innovative technologies, progress and outstanding productivity” says the 53-year-old who moved with his family to Ahlen.

Strategic growth, he adds, ought to follow as the Munsterlander machine tool manufacturer wants to trade on its international reputation and a new price performance ratio for its machines and services. Oliver Reimann, Board Member for Sales, believes that leadership in the company’s target markets will be achieved through the company’s adaptation to market shifts over the last three years.

“In the future, our machine series will be designed and built according to the principle of design-to-cost. Target prices will therefore be achieved and underbid which are market requirements. This principle and a clearly built and manageable market development concept will lead us to success,” he told ISMR.

“After the two mixed business years in 2010 and 2011 caused by the aftermath of the economic crisis, Leifeld will gain considerable benefits in 2012. The envisaged growth has already begun; eleven new jobs have already been provided,” concluded Gerhard Glanz. See www.leifeldms.com ■

## Porsche Consulting opens base in China

WITH THE establishment of a location in China, the Porsche Consulting GmbH management consultancy firm, based in Bietigheim-Bissingen (Germany), continues its systematic international growth strategy. With headquarters in Shanghai, Porsche Consulting Ltd. will be launched at the onset of 2013. After Italy, Brazil and the United States, it will be the fourth overseas branch of the consultancy firm.

“To be present with our own office in Shanghai allows us to provide support to current and future clients in China and other countries in the Far East far better and even more directly,” said Eberhard Weiblen, President and CEO of Porsche Consulting GmbH. In Asia, Porsche Consulting is focusing primarily on the automotive industry, aeronautics and space as well as machine building at present.

The team in Shanghai will be managed by Dr. Heiko Rauscher, who sees the need for consultancy clearly: “We provide support to companies in China in mastering their strong growth in organisational terms while boosting productivity at the same time,” commented the future Shanghai CEO.

In the meantime, the number of employees of Porsche Consulting GmbH continues to grow steadily. “By the end of 2012, we will have hired a total of 50 new employees. This augmentation of our capacity allows us to meet persistently high demand,” explained Weiblen.

Industrial engineers, engineers with a focus on automotive, aeronautics and space as well as machine building plus economists and change management experts round off the shopping list of what is needed. The requirement profile includes several years of professional experience, a focus on implementation and international training. The new Shanghai location is still seeking staff as well. Beyond the requisite professional qualifications, candidates should have knowledge of the Chinese language and culture.

Porsche Consulting, a specialist in lean processes, is a subsidiary of the sports car manufacturer Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG, Stuttgart.

Founded in 1994, it now has 330 employees. In August 2012, Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG delivered a total of 10,912 vehicles to customers around the world, an increase of 20.8 per cent compared to the same month the previous year. Looking back over the previous year, the Stuttgartbased sports car manufacturer sold 92,474 vehicles (+15.0%).

“Currently, automotive markets are developing quite differently: uncertainty in Europe, competitive pressures in China, confidence in the US, growth in the Middle East and South-East Asia,” said Bernhard Maier, Member of the Executive Board Sales and Marketing at Porsche AG. “Thanks to our strong brand, along with an attractive range of models, we are still on course for growth.” ■

## New moves at KMT

KMT Waterjet Systems has announced two new senior appointments and the opening of a new European HQ in Bad Nauheim, Germany.

Kevin McManus is the new Global President of KMT Waterjet Systems, having previously headed KMT’s Robotic Solutions organisation. Bernd Schrum has taken up the position of Vice President for Technical Sales and Marketing for Europe, stepping up to the role after many successful years with the company. Joining Bernd at Vice President level in Europe is Thorsten Kilb, who now heads Human Resources and Finance.

These key appointments coincided with the opening of a new European HQ for the company in Bad Nauheim, Germany.

“With much larger warehousing and storage space, the new facility reflects the increase in demand across Europe for KMT Waterjet Systems products,” the company told ISMR. There are also new contract cutting and customer demonstration areas, with the latter being regularly used by UK customers to test cut a diverse range of products and to view the advantages of the waterjet process.”

For further details, please see www.kmt-waterjet.com ■
