# MADE IN SWITZERLAND

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

By Hansjörg Egger (with photos by Markus Forte)

Designed, constructed and built in Switzerland,   
Marenco’s Swisshelicopter contains numerous innovations and   
technological refinements.

ISMR SAYS:

“ Marenco invested approximately half a million Swiss francs into Bystronic water jet and press brake systems for a wide range of material cutting and bending applications”

With its Swisshelicopter, Marenco has achieved success with a light, compact helicopter in the 2.5-ton class. The market opportunities for a high-quality product in small series at an advantageous price are strong in today’s market, the manufacturer explained, with vast potential.

Worldwide, there are only approximately eight Western helicopter manufacturers. The overall volume in the market for civil turbine helicopters is approximately 1,200 machines per year. At least half of the machines sold belong to the sub-2.5-ton class. Some analysts even forecast an increased demand of up to 900 machines per year for lightweight helicopters.

Marenco CEO, Martin Stucki, estimates that his company could realistically manage series production of between 80 and 100 machines per year. He expects continued growth in the European economic area, in the USA, in South America, especially in Brazil, and in China. Southern China and the Himalayas region are mountainous and there is a lack of air transport capacities. In these regions, there are entrepreneurs on the lookout for machines, for whom Stucki believes the versatile Swisshelicopter is the perfect solution.

However, there are also companies in Switzerland (where approximately 60 Airbus AS-350 helicopters, three Bell 407s and one Koala A-119 from Agusta are currently registered) for whom the Swisshelicopter could represent an alternative. For Rega, the Swiss air rescue service, however, it is not an option. In Switzerland, rescue helicopters are required to have twin engines, as opposed to the USA where, for example, there are no such requirements.

## Cold cuts

Marenco invested approximately half a million Swiss francs in two Bystronic machines – a ByJet Flex waterjet cutting system, with two 2D cutting heads, and an Xpert 60 press brake.

Cutting without creating heat in the material has proved to be a great advantage, because heat could change the material structure – which in turn would mean an increased risk of cracks. In combination with the bending machine, Marenco now has sheet metal processing facilities that offer an almost closed process chain for cutting and bending.
