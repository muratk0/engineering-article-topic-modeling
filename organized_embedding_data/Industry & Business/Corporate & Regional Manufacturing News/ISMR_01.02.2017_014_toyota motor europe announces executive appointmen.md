# Toyota Motor Europe announces executive appointments

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

TOYOTA MOTOR EUROPE (TME) has announced several executive level appointments at its European headquarters and affiliates. These changes follow Toyota's executive and management rotations that take place regularly at global, pan-European and national levels. These appointments were effective from 1 January 2017.

Shigeru Teramoto was appointed as Chairman, TMUK, and Chairman, TMMF

Marvin Cooke was appointed as managing director, TMUK

Didier Gambart, was appointed as President & CEO, TFR

## Toyota Motor Europe (TME)

Mr. Hideo Takano was appointed as Vice President Quality, TME, replacing Mr. Necdet Senturk.

Mr. Kiyoshi Hashikabe was appointed as Vice President, Research & Development 1, TME, replacing Mr. Tetsuji Sano, who will end his assignment.

Mr. Toshihiko Ishikawa, currently Vice President Supplier Breakthrough Engineering, TME, will end his assignment.

Toyota Motor Manufacturing France (TMMF)

Mr. Luciano Biondo was appointed as President, TMMF, replacing Mr. Koreatsu Aoki.

## Toyota Peugeot Citroen Automobile (TPCA)

Mr. Koreatsu Aoki was appointed as President TPCA, replacing Mr. Kenta Koide, who will end his assignment

Toyota Motor Manufacturing UK (TMUK)

Mr.Shigeru Teramoto was appointed as Chairman, TMUK, and Chairman, TMMF. He will be based in the UK

Mr. Marvin Cooke was appointed as Managing Director, TMUK.

Toyota Motor Manufacturing Turkey (TMMT)

Mr.Necdet Senturk was appointed as Senior Vice President, Manufacturing, Finance & Administration, TMMT

Toyota Deutschland (TDG)

Mr. Masaki Hosoe, currently Chairman, TDG, ended his assignment.

Toyota Motor Kazakhstan (TMKZ)

Mr. Kit Simmons was appointed as President, TMKZ, replacing Mr. Etsuo Hashino, who will end his assignment.

Toyota France (TFR)

Mr. Didier Gambart was appointed as President & CEO, TFR, replacing Mr. Pascal Ruch, who will be appointed as Director within Lexus Europe, TME.
