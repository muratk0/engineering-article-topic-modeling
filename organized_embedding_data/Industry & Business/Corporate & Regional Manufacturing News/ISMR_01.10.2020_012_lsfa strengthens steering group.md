# LSFA strengthens Steering Group

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

Melanie Davies, Market Manager, Etex Building Performance.

AFTER APPOINTING Mike Fairey as its new Chair, the Light Steel Frame Association (LSFA) has also welcomed Melanie Davies, market manager for supply chain member Etex Building Performance, to the Steering Group.

“Melanie brings a wealth of supply chain expertise to the LSFA Steering Group. As a specialist in market monitoring for consumer demands and building regulation implications, Melanie has her finger on the pulse of emerging design trends,” said the LSFA.

Mike Fairey, Director, Fusion Building Systems.

Mike Fairey, Chair of the Light Steel Frame Association, added: “We embrace the opinions of supply chain members - Melanie will be a great asset to the LSFA Steering Group. Her energy and enthusiasm, together with great industry experience particularly relating to new trends and emerging markets, will be valued.

“Since inauguration in Spring 2019, the LSFA is gathering momentum and now includes some of the most prominent names in the sector and wider construction industry. Our collective power will drive growth and promote specification but only if we work closely with supply chain members to gain a wider industry perspective.”

The Light Steel Frame Association (LSFA) has been established to influence legislation and regulation, and to support the overall objectives and growth of the steel framing sector in the UK. It aims to provide unified representation of the UK steel framing industry.
