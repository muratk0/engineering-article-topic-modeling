# FURTHER EXPANSION AT KMF

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Such are the encouraging signs at UK-based KMF (Precision Sheet Metal) in the first quarter of its financial year that it has committed to further expansion of its manufacturing plant.

Continuous investment in people, technology and manufacturing processes are at the core of KMF’s success story. This success is continuing into the company’s new financial year with record sales of £6 million being recorded during April, May and June.

“These increased sales figures continue what has been a positive trend for KMF,” says Gareth Higgins, KMF’s managing director. “They have put us firmly on track for 15 per cent growth in the current financial year, with much of that being generated through enhancing the partnerships with our existing customers. In particular, we are extremely pleased by the increase we are seeing in the aerospace and medical sectors.”

In the aerospace sector, KMF is manufacturing the chassis of complex aircraft seats. This increased demand from the aerospace and medical sectors has resulted in the purchase of an additional 10,000 ft2 (919 m2) of manufacturing space. This new unit will be dedicated to the manufacture of high technology components and assemblies.

Business and first class aircraft seats for which KMF (Precision Sheet Metal) is providing sheet metal components, assembly and integration of electrical systems for its customer. Production of these seats will be moving to the new manufacturing facility.
