# SMOOTHING THE EDGES

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

German deburring and sawing specialist, RSA, is moving ahead with new products, a new plant, a re-branded corporate image and new acquisitions. Its CEO, Thomas Berg, explains all to ISMR…..

ISMR SAYS: “RSA’s modular, customisedapproach to systems integration aims to reduce overall processing times providing optimisedcosts percutforits customers”

It has been a productive year for German deburring and sawing specialist, RSA. It has energised its brand with a series of product portfolio enhancements, new headquarters, a new company acquisition and a move into new market sectors.

Supplying professional sawing and deburring machines and tools (for bars, tubes and sheet metal) to a range of customers worldwide, it has introduced services such as mobile demonstration centres and workpiece testing on customer sites. Its main aim is to reduce overall processing times for its customers (particularly for ancillary operations) so that they stay competitive with minimal costs per cut.

ISMR caught up with its CEO, Thomas Berg, to discover the company’s aims and objectives for the rest of the year and uncover his view of sheet metal and tube market trends.

We are aiming to increase our turnover this year to 20 million euros, which is around a 15-20 per cent increase on last year.
