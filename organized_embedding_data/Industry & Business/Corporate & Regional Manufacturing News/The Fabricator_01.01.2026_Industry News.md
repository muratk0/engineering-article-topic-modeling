Ryerson and Olympic Steel announce merger agreement

Ryerson Holding Corp., a value-added processor and distributor of industrial metals, and metals service center Olympic Steel Inc. have entered into a definitive agreement to merge.

As part of the transaction, Michael D. Siegal, executive chairman of Olympic Steel’s board of directors, will be appointed chairman of the board of the combined company, and Olympic Steel will appoint three other mutually satisfactory directors to the combined 11-member board. Eddie Lehner, president/CEO of Ryerson, will serve as CEO of the combined company, with Richard T. Marabito, CEO of Olympic Steel, serving as president/COO.

Ryerson has operations in the U.S., Canada, Mexico, and China, with about 4,300 employees in approximately 110 locations. Olympic Steel operates from 54 facilities, providing direct sale and value-added processing of carbon and coated steel sheet, plate, and coil products; stainless steel sheet, plate, bar, and coil; aluminum sheet, plate, and coil; and pipe, tube, bar, valves, and fittings.

Sargent Metal expanding operations in South Carolina

Sargent Metal, a provider of precision metal fabrication services, will expand its operations in Anderson County, S.C. The company expects the $9 million investment to create 20 jobs over the next five years.

With capabilities including laser cutting, welding, powder coating, and assembly, Sargent Metal serves OEMs in a variety of markets.

The expansion is expected to be completed over the next few years.

Bystronic to acquire Coherent’s tools for materials processing business unit

Bystronic, an international provider of sheet metal processing technology, has entered into an agreement to acquire the tools for materials processing business unit of U.S.-based Coherent Inc. Located in Gilching, Germany, the business unit employs about 400 people.

This acquisition will help Bystronic expand into additional markets such as medical devices, semiconductors, and general manufacturing. In addition, new laser applications such as micro material processing, marking, labeling, and drilling will be added to the Bystronic portfolio.

With this acquisition, Bystronic also will obtain the rights of the Rofin laser brand and will integrate this business into a new Bystronic Rofin business unit.

Titletown Manufacturing achieves ISO certification

Titletown Manufacturing LLC, a Green Bay, Wis., provider of OEM assembly and contract machining services, has achieved ISO certification. This certification validates the company’s adherence to internationally recognized standards for quality management systems. The company serves the packaging, processing, food, and industrial equipment industries.

Intella Parts Co. opens new headquarters, distribution warehouse

Intella Parts Co. LLC, a supplier of forklift and aerial lift parts, has announced the opening of its new corporate headquarters and distribution warehouse in Holland, Mich. The building features 11,000 sq. ft. of offices and warehouse space.

The company sells parts through two websites targeted to the U.S. and Canada, respectively. Starting in 2026, a website will be added for Mexico. The company will operate in Mexico as Intella Parts S.A. de C.V.

In addition to selling parts to customers online, the company has developed a nationwide network of more than 120 independent forklift mechanics across the U.S. who typically work from home. "Customers who need parts often need technical help as well, and we match them with a mechanic," said Marcel VandenTop, owner and founder.

Mid Atlantic Machinery to host 24th annual Steel Tech event

Mid Atlantic Machinery will host its 24th annual Steel Tech technology showcase Feb. 10-12, 2026, at its facility in Harrisburg, Pa.

The event will feature more than 40 machines under power, representing manufacturers across precision sheet metal processing, structural and plate fabrication, routing, and finishing. Educational seminars tailored for shop owners, experienced fabricators, and new team members will be offered.

Virginia Transformer’s Georgia expansion to create 400 jobs

Virginia Transformer Corp. will invest $40 million to expand its existing manufacturing facility near Rincon, Ga., creating more than 400 jobs.

The plant, which operates as Georgia Transformer, manufactures custom-designed, large power transformers for a variety of industries, including the utilities, data centers, and renewable energy sectors. The 250,000-sq.-ft. facility produces transformers from 30 to 500 MVA at up to 525 kV.

Construction on the expansion is expected to begin in January 2026.

Hudson Industries to invest more than $1.7 million in Missouri plant

Hudson Industries, doing business as Green’s Welding and Sales, a manufacturer of livestock feeding and handling equipment, has announced plans to expand in Appleton City, Mo., with an investment of more than $1.7 million. The project will add 18,900 sq. ft. to the company’s facility, nearly doubling production and creating seven jobs.

GWS manufactures a variety of livestock feeding and handling equipment, including bulk feed bins, creep feeders, UTV feeders, feed bunks, portable panels, chutes, bale rings, and bale movers. The company has more than 100 active dealers in 21 states.

Triple-S Steel Holdings acquires Industrial Material Corp.

Triple-S Steel Holdings Inc., Houston, has acquired the assets of Industrial Material Corp., a Galveston, Texas, supplier of steel tubing, angle, sheet, plate, and hardware and accessories.

Triple-S Steel is a family-owned steel service center with more than 50 locations across North America and Colombia. It offers structural steel, tubing, specialty metals, and value-added processing to customers in construction, manufacturing, and infrastructure.

Lock Joint Tube acquires Tectron Tube product line and facility

Lock Joint Tube, a manufacturer of mechanical and structural grade steel tubing in South Bend, Ind., has acquired the Tectron mechanical tube product line and manufacturing facility in De Pere Wis., from Atkore Inc.

The 140,000-sq.-ft. facility and all employees will now operate as Lock Joint Tube Wisconsin. With the acquisition, LJT operates six locations across Indiana, Ohio, Tennessee, Texas, and Wisconsin that serve fabricators, distributors, and OEMs nationwide.

The acquisition enhances LJT’s small-diameter tubing capabilities and broadens its range of products for the agricultural, healthcare, furniture, automotive, and other transportation industries.

Scout Motors to create 1,200 jobs in North Carolina

Truck and SUV manufacturer Scout Motors Inc. has announced plans to establish its corporate headquarters in Charlotte, N.C. The nearly $207 million project will create 1,200 jobs.

Reiter’s Custom Welding expanding in Arizona

Reiter’s Custom Welding Inc., a provider of steel fabrication, processing, and contract manufacturing services, is expanding with a 40,000-sq.-ft. facility in Glendale, Ariz.

"We’re investing heavily in automation, advanced equipment, and a more streamlined workflow," said Donald Reiter, vice president. "The new space allows us to modernize how we build—faster setups, cleaner processes, and better integration across engineering, production, and quality control."

"This new facility … gives us the space, technology, and layout we need to take on larger projects, increase output, and continue setting the standard in turnaround times for our customers," said Ross Reiter, director of operations and business development.

The plant will include automated laser cutting, waterjet cutting, press brake forming, manual and robotic welding, and fabrication systems, along with expanded space for engineering, quality control, and logistics.

The company expects to be fully operational in the new facility by mid-Q1 2026. During the transition, operations will continue without interruption.

Socomec to create 300 jobs in Georgia

Electrical component and system manufacturer Socomec will invest nearly $10 million in a new facility in Suwanee, Ga., creating 300 jobs. Hiring for production and operations roles is expected to begin in early Q1 2026.

A family business that has grown into an international group, Socomec employs more than 4,800 people worldwide. The company is a provider of power switching, monitoring, conversion, and energy storage technology and service.

Angeles Equity Partners acquires majority interest in Technique

An affiliate of Angeles Equity Partners LLC, a private investment firm that partners with niche manufacturing, critical industrial services, and specialty distribution businesses, has acquired a majority interest in Technique Inc., a provider of complex, high-mix, low-volume metal fabrication.

The Jackson, Mich.-based fabricator offers prototyping and ultralow-volume production with capabilities including metal forming, stamping, tube bending, welding, assembly, and finishing. It delivers highly complex, value-added components and integrated assemblies to OEMs in the aerospace and defense, power generation, and motorsports industries.

Technique operates six manufacturing facilities totaling about 500,000 sq. ft. in Michigan and North Carolina.

In partnership with Technique founder and CEO Ronnie Johncox, Angeles will support continued investment in the company’s operational infrastructure, advanced manufacturing capabilities, and strategic initiatives. Johncox will continue to lead the company alongside the existing management team.

Butting USA breaks ground on $61 million North American facility

Butting USA, a global stainless steel pipe manufacturer, has broken ground on its first U.S. production facility in Loxley, Ala. The $61 million facility is expected to create up to 100 jobs.

The 58-acre site will be developed in two phases. Phase 1 will establish local administrative operations, engineering, and fabrication of stainless steel spools, components, and cryogenic pipe systems. Phase 2 will expand to include two welded stainless steel pipe mills and additional fabrication capabilities.

Sightline Commercial Solutions expands manufacturing facilities

Sightline Commercial Solutions, a fabricator and global supplier of architectural railing, metal, glazing and portable platform systems, recently expanded and reconfigured its manufacturing facility in Minneapolis to help boost welding and machining capacity and increase efficiency.

Outfitted with new TIG welding machines and advanced racking systems, the upgraded space streamlines workflows, giving crews the room and resources to meet demanding project schedules. The company serves the commercial, sports venue, live entertainment, and performing arts industries.

Morton Industries acquires Superior Tube Products

Morton Industries, a manufacturer of precision tube fabricated components and welded assemblies, has announced its acquisition of Superior Tube Products, a precision tube fabrication company in Davenport, Iowa. As part of this strategic expansion, all STP operations will transition into Morton Industries’ facilities in Morton, Ill., and Dunlap, Tenn., with production shifting fully by Jan. 1, 2026.

"We’re excited to welcome the STP business into the Morton Industries family," said Christopher Ober, president/CEO of Morton Industries. "This strengthens our ability to serve both existing and new customers with a wider range of high-quality, precision-formed components and welded assemblies."

STP serves OEMs in the agriculture, HVAC, construction, and in-dustrial equipment industries. The acquisition brings STP’s work into Morton’s vertically integrated operations to offer expanded CNC and large-OD tube bending, advanced 5-axis and inline laser cutting, robotic and manual welding, and in-house tooling and fixture design and engineering support.

Andrew Rommann, former president of STP, now serves as vice president of strategic transformation at Morton Industries.

The expansion, which is expected to create 74 jobs, will add 30,000 sq. ft. to the company’s existing footprint to manufacture stainless steel tub dishwashers.

Imperial Systems acquires third Pennsylvania location

Air filtration equipment manufacturer Imperial Systems Inc. has announced plans to expand its operations in Pennsylvania with the addition of a location in Hadley. The company has two existing facilities in Williamstown and Mercer.

ISI will be able to process its own steel coils with the addition of a high bay extension, which will add 17,000 sq. ft. at the existing 38,000-sq.- ft. facility in Hadley. The $8 million expansion is expected to create at least 47 full-time jobs over the next three years.

John Bouchard & Sons marks 125 years in business

John Bouchard & Sons Co. is celebrating its 125th year in business. Headquartered in its original Nashville, Tenn., location, with its foundry facility only a few blocks away, JBS operates branch offices in Knoxville, Tenn.; Elizabethtown, Ky.; and Evansville, Ind.

Begun in 1900 as a machine shop, the company incorporated in 1913. Through four integrated divisions, it provides equipment sales and service, iron construction castings, and construction and machine services to commercial, institutional, industrial, and municipal customers.

The fifth-generation, family-owned company is a certified Woman Business Enterprise.

Pipe and Steel Industrial Fabricators announces Louisiana expansion

Pipe and Steel Industrial Fabricators Inc., a family-owned industrial construction company, will invest $5.1 million to expand its steel processing and fabrication facility in Denham Springs, La., to increase beam cutting capacity.

The project, which is expected to create 20 jobs and retain 70 current positions, will add a steel processing center, advanced equipment, and electrical infrastructure upgrades.

Construction is scheduled for completion in June 2026. The company recently added 8,000 sq. ft. to its structural steel shop and 14,000 sq. ft. to its pipe fabrication facility, along with a new permanent covered blasting and painting area.

Pipe & Steel is a Native American family-owned and certified Louisiana veteran-owned business and industrial construction company that serves the petrochemical, agricultural, fertilizer, gas, and oil industries with pipe and structural fabrication, civil construction, and general field construction.

Ice Industries opens new Louisiana facility

Ice Industries, Sylvania, Ohio, recently celebrated the opening of its new $6 million manufacturing facility in Lacassine, La. The 115,000-sq.-ft. facility features advanced production systems, modern quality control measures, and upgraded safety and environmental protocols designed to enhance throughput and precision, the company reports.

The plant will supply components to First Solar Inc.’s manufacturing facility in New Iberia, La.

Since its opening, the facility has created nearly 70 jobs and added several employees relocated from its Bowling Green, Ohio, facility.

Tate to locate its largest North American facility in Kentucky

Tate, a manufacturer of data center infrastructure products headquartered in Columbia, Md., will locate its largest North American facility in Glasgow, Ky. The $61.2 million project will create 400 fulltime manufacturing jobs once the 764,000-sq.-ft. facility is fully operational.

A subsidiary of Ireland-based company Kingspan Group PLC, the company employs more than 1,800 employees across 21 facilities globally. The Glasgow plant will manufacturer thermal management and airflow systems for data centers.
