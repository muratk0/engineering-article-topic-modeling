# OKAY INDUSTRIES, INC.

[TARİH: 01.01.2020 ISMR]

OKAY Industries, Inc., is a stamping, machining and prototyping company that specialises in complex metal stampings with extremely tight tolerances. It produces precision parts for medical and surgical devices as well as components for the defence and firearms industries. It also produces other highly sophisticated parts for specialty, industrial, and automotive markets.

The company has a long history of continuous improvement. It has achieved its ISO certification and has had to comply with new ISO standards and requirements.

Over time, OKAY's management found itself with 18 different databases which integrated with each other but not with the company's UNIX server. It was difficult to quickly compile the detailed information management required to continually improve the operation's efficiency.

## Starting the search

“We needed real-time information that was accessible to everyone on the production floor,' stressed its President, Jason Howey, at the 100,000-square-foot facility in New Britain, Connecticut (USA). "We wanted one system on one platform that would give us real-time work-in-process information and allow us to analyse production costs down to the minute. The UNIX system was accessed by administrators with terminals, but no one out on the floor could access information.

Howey learned about Plex Cloud ERP at a Precision Metalforming Association show in Chicago. Intrigued, he began researching Plex and other systems.

“It got to the point where we knew within five or six questions whether a system could do what we wanted," Howey said.

## A focus on quality

“We started implementing Plex with the quality functions because OKAY's parts have to be perfect," said Mig O'Brien, Plex project manager. “We made some changes to the system so it would keep track of weights up to seven decimal places.'

“OKAY's customers, suppliers, part numbers and more were housed in separate databases — we had a whole slew of databases within our quality system," Howey added. “It took student interns about ten weeks to input part numbers, routings and more. Once all the data was in the new system, we started using Plex strictly for quality monitoring. Everything went smoothly. It was a great way to start."
