# Major milestone at MINI plant

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

Above: MINI will celebrate a milestone in 2013

IN 2013 MINI UK and its parent company, BMW Group, will celebrate 100 years of car-making at the site of MINI Plant Oxford (in the UK). The focus date for the celebrations will be 28 March 2013, which is 100 years to the day when the first Bullnose Morris Oxford rolled off the line.

Since 1913, tens of thousands of people – 26,000 at the peak in the 1950s and 1960s – have worked on the site building over 11 million cars under brands including Austin Healey; MG; Wolseley; Riley; Austin; Mini; Vanden Plas; Princess; Triumph; Rover; Honda; Sterling; the founding marque Morris and over two million MINIs since 2000.

Today, Plant Oxford manufactures the MINI hatchback, Convertible, Clubman, Clubvan, Roadster and Coupe, 80 per cent of which are exported to over 100 global markets. It is currently undergoing a major investment in preparation for the third generation of new MINIs. A centenary exhibition at the plant will incorporate stories and pictures from employees past and present. To share a story or a photograph please contact oxford100@mini.co.uk.

As a global company, the BMW Group operates 29 production and assembly facilities in 14 countries and has a global sales network in more than 140 countries. In 2011, the BMW Group sold about 1.67 million cars and more than 113,000 motorcycles worldwide.
