# FMA welcomes incoming board chair and elects new directors

[TARİH: 01.11.2021 The Fabricator]

Last month, FMA elected a new chair along with other directors and officers to its board.

Carlos Mendizabal

James Rogowski

Jason Hillenbrand

Sarah Richards

Steven Dobos

Matthew Sharpe

Carlos Mendizabal is FMA’s incoming chairman. Carlos is VP of Industrias Selbor SA de CV in San Luis Potosí, Mexico. Established in 1981, SELBOR provides design, and manufacturing of sheet metal fabrication and enclosures based on specific needs, from appliances, electrical, and electronics.

In addition to Carlos, other board officers for 2021-2022 are James Rogowski, VP of technical services at TRUMPF Inc as 1st vice-chairman; Jason Hillenbrand, general manager for Amada America Inc as 2nd vice-chairman; and Sarah Richards, the president and CEO of Jones Metal Inc as secretary / treasurer.

Two new members began serving their first term as a director on the FMA Board: Steven Dobos, president and CEO of The Butler Weldments Corporation, and Matthew Sharpe, president and CEO of Cameron Manufacturing & Design.
