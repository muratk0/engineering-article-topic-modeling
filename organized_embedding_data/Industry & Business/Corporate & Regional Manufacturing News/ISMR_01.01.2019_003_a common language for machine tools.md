# A common language for machine tools

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

VDW, THE GERMAN Machine Tool Builders Association, and the OPC Foundation has established a Joint Working Group to move Umati (the universal machine tool interface) towards becoming a generally accepted standard. Interested parties are cordially invited to participate with immediate effect.

"The group will start its work in January 2019," reported Dr. Alexander Broos, project manager for umati at VDW. "Interested members of the OPC Foundation, the VDW or affiliated associations who want to cooperate in the joint development of umati towards a Companion Specification of the OPC Foundation are welcome to contact us."

The VDW aims to ensure that the worldwide requirements of customers and machine tool manufacturers are included in the standard. This will increase umati's acceptance as a globally accepted connectivity standard.

"As is common with international standards, a draft of umati has been presented to the (professional) public for inspection, feedback and comments. Comments received will be incorporated to the greatest possible extent, so that a final standard can be adopted. In contrast to classical standardisation organisations, OPC UA also includes configuration files that facilitate the set-up of OPC servers, which need to be implemented in the machine and then handle the actual communication," explained the VDW.

Umati passed a first crucial test in a showcase during the AMB 2018 trade show in Stuttgart, Germany. Fifteen machines from nine manufacturers with five control platforms were connected to numerous communication partners. The exchange of data was based on an extract of the first parameter set that had been jointly defined up to then. The participating companies confirmed that set-up of the communication network took between eight and 16 hours.

"The next important milestone for umati is the presentation of a more extensive showcase at EMO Hannover 2019. At the world's leading trade fair for metalworking, we want to present a significantly larger number of international partners participating in umati," added Broos.

The VDW is engaged in intensive discussions with important market partners all over the world. In addition to planned collaboration with companies, the VDW is working closely with its sister associations in Europe, the USA, Japan, China, South Korea and others. Further information is available at www.umati.info Those interested in collaboration can email info@umati.info
