# Elevators and escalators for metro

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

THYSSENKRUPP ELEVATOR won the bid to provide the first metro in a district-free city – a metropolitan region with a surrounding major region – in Western China with 234 elevators and escalators. With Metro Line 1, the Chinese City of Luoyang (one of the four historical capitals where 13 dynasties ruled) has officially entered the "metro era". The metro will be put into operation in 2021.

Luoyang was also China's first university city as well as an ancient residence for the philosophers Laozi and Confucius.

With a total length of 23km, Luoyang Metro Line 1 has 19 stops and covers the main passenger flow in the east-west direction to the north of the Luohe River. It will address the issue of east-west passenger transportation in the downtown area to the north of the Luohe River and strengthen the connection between areas north of the river.

Thyssenkrupp Elevator has been chosen to become the largest supplier of escalators for this project. The installation of all escalators is expected to be completed by August 2020. After that, thyssenkrupp Elevator will continue to provide Luoyang citizens with mobility experiences.

In 2018, thyssenkrupp Elevator inaugurated its new China test tower and production facility in Zhongshan. The launch demonstrated the company's continued development in the region following the opening of its Multiple-Purpose Facility Building in Shanghai in 2016. Today, thyssenkrupp Elevator runs four factories with around 11,000 employees in China. The service network comprises nearly 200 different locations. With sales of €8 billion in fiscal 2018/2019 and customers in over 100 countries, thyssenkrupp Elevator has become one of the world's leading elevator companies in just over 40 years.■

## Porsche increases deliveries in 2019

VEHICLES PRODUCED BY PORSCHE, the Stuttgart-based sports car manufacturer, are more popular than ever. In 2019, Porsche delivered 280,800 vehicles to its customers – ten per cent more than in the previous year.

“The Cayenne and Macan model series, in particular, saw significant growth: the Cayenne was delivered 92,055 times worldwide, an increase of 29 per cent year-on-year. The current model has been available in all markets since 2019, and as a plug-in hybrid recently. Spring also saw the market introduction of the Cayenne Coupé, a more sporting derivative. A total of 99,944 Macan vehicles were delivered to customers – an increase of 16 per cent compared to 2018," Porsche told ISMR.

“We are delighted with this great result and are also proud that we have further strengthened the appeal of our brand and the customer experience with new approaches," said Detlev von Platen, Member of the Executive Board for Sales and Marketing at Porsche AG. “We are optimistic that we can maintain the high levels of demand in 2020 thanks to a host of new models and full order books for the Taycan."

In the company's domestic market and throughout the European market, Porsche generated its strongest growth levels in 2019 (+15 per cent, in each case). 31,618 vehicles were delivered in Germany and 88,975 vehicles throughout Europe.

The sports car manufacturer also recorded an increase in its two largest markets. In China, 86,752 vehicles were handed over to customers, an increase of eight per cent compared to 2018. In the USA, Porsche also grew its delivery figures by eight percent to 61,568. The sports car manufacturer has therefore defied the trend towards a weakening overall economic climate in both markets. 116,458 Porsche vehicles were delivered to customers in the Asia-Pacific, Africa and Middle East regions – an increase of seven per cent compared to 2018.
