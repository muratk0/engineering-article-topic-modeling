# New HQ for Creaform

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

CREAFORM, A LEADING global provider of 3D measurement solutions and engineering services, has officially opened its new factory headquarters at Innoparc Lévis in Lévis, Quebec (Canada), in the presence of key partners and customers. The 76,000 square-foot facility will play a crucial role in Creaform's growth strategy in the years ahead. Built at a cost of C$ 20 million, it triples production capacity and doubles the space available for the company's fast-growing workforce.

"For an innovation-based company like ours, human talent is asset number one," said Catherine Thomas, Director of Human Resources, Creaform. "Now that the new factory headquarters is open, we'll be moving full speed ahead to fill over 30 positions currently available in R&D, engineering and production as we work to meet our target of 150 new employees within five years."

R&D, manufacturing and assembly, engineering services and administrative operations have all been relocated to the new building, along with part of the sales and support team.

"We are absolutely thrilled with the new facility," said Creaform founder and business unit manager, Martin Lamontagne. "Our aim is to double sales and significantly expand our workforce over the next five years. Adding space and production capacity was an essential step on the road to that goal. The new headquarters will also provide a home base for our international employees and the growing number of clients who come to us for product training."

In addition to upgrading its production infrastructure and technology, Creaform also incorporated green building techniques into the project to reduce the facility's environmental footprint and create a luminous and welcoming workplace that qualifies for LEED certification.

The new facility at 4700, rue de la Pascaline has been in operation since 20 November 2017. See www.creaform3d.com
