## Stefan flies higher

AFTER four years managing the development of operations at its KMF Advanced Solutions division, Stefan Rduch is taking on a new challenge in business development at KMF (Precision Sheet Metal). His new role will focus on developing sales in the aerospace sector, which is seen as a significant market for KMF’s expertise in sheet metal manufacture and electronic integration.

“At KMF, we are well placed to work in partnership with leading aerospace manufacturing companies to provide them with a totally outsourced sheet metal fabrication and integration service, something that we already do for several customers. My new role will be to develop these new partnerships and ensure that KMF is first choice when these businesses are looking to expand their manufacturing capacity,” Stefan told ISMR.

Stefan is a product of KMF’s highly successful apprentice training programme, where his enthusiasm was recognised at an early stage. On completion of his apprenticeship, he went on to complete further training, gaining a HNC and HND in Mechanical Engineering, along with level five of the Chartered Institute of Purchasing and Supply’s qualification. Having spent time managing a budget of over £3.5 million in the purchasing department, he was hand-picked to head the new KMF Advanced Solutions’ division. ■
