# AUTOMATE TO ACCUMULATE

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

Folding specialist, Thalmann Machinenbau AG, has taken major steps in process automation for its customers. Its managing director, Marco Cappello, outlined its approach to product and market development to ISMR.

We believe that a continuous focus on cost reduction for our customers will benefit the sheet metal processing industry in general

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes or achievements?

MC: Thalmann Machinenbau AG has continued building the business, with great success, after I (as managing director) and Martin Thalmann (finance and administration director) acquired the company from its previous owners in 2016. Our success is underpinned by a strong focus on streamlining the product line and extending product developments, resulting in exciting new innovations as well as increasing capacity and establishing more efficiencies in the supply chain.

New innovations have been focused on reducing operational costs for Thalmann customers. One example, which was demonstrated at Blechexpo and at Baltimat, is the fully automated sheet metal side-loading option on a Thalmann TD folder. Less labour is required to feed the sheet into the machine and the process increases the effective use of the equipment for folding. Initial feedback from our customers has exceeded our expectations. We expect significantly increased interest when the first machines are installed with this side-loading device.

Another example is the joint development between Thalmann Machinenbau AG with Cidan NuiT and Cidan Forstner that led to the launch of "The Base" earlier this year at Cidan NuiT in St Polten, Austria. This fully automated folding equipment combines a Thalmann TD folder with a Cidan Forstner decoiling line and cutting line, Cidan NuiT Software and an automated handling system. "The Base" can fold a set of different parts, with different dimensions, straight from an e-portal based at a customer's location with no direct labour involved in the bending, decoiling and cutting operation. This is a revolutionary development that is marketed by Cidan NuiT, with support from Thalmann Machinenbau AG.

We are very proud to be taking these major steps in process automation for our customers.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals?

MC: We see the present business climate as mixed. Demand for folding machines remains high and, for Thalmann, this is confirmation that the industry is still 'going strong'. We also see the effect of low interest rates, with companies needing favourable conditions to make capital investments in new machinery.

There are, of course, different uncertainties in the market that might lead to reduced building activity or general economic slowdown. Brexit, the economic slowdown in the main European markets and a slowdown in China might negatively affect the processing of sheet metal. On the other hand, demand for folded sheet metal parts in the U.S. remains very strong.

We remain very confident that our business will continue to grow, as we believe that a continuous focus on cost reduction for our customers will benefit the sheet metal processing industry in general. We have initiated developments that increase the scope of dimension of folded parts and the ability to produce at lower costs, which expands the applications and markets for bent sheet metal.

ISMR: Please explain your strategic and technical objectives?

MC: Thalmann Machinenbau AG has a clear strategy based on three pillars:

1) Focusing on innovations that our customers need and ask for e.g. automation and operational cost reductions.

2) Unlocking markets that traditionally use different technologies for folding sheet metal. We have recently started initiatives to expand in selected markets in the Asian region.

3) Further optimising the supply chain and processes to be able to manufacture machines in a way that safeguards high quality standards at an acceptable cost base.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers at the moment and how are you addressing these issues for them?

MC: Depending upon the region, we see that customers are prepared to invest in folding machines that a) deliver parts safely; b) consistently deliver accurate parts with the smallest possible tolerances; c) deliver parts at the lowest operational cost and d) deliver parts using a machine that has the highest uptime and low levels of maintenance.

The safety of our machines is guaranteed by complying fully with, or even exceeding, CE norms. Fully automated laser monitoring of critical areas during operation protects machine operators. The design of the Thalmann machine and our "Torsion Shaft Technology" is our basis for guaranteeing the highest possible accuracy of folded sheet metal parts. The "cropped" design of the folding beams are designed to increase the dimensional freedom of folded sheet metal; this gives architects and designers greater scope to use sheet metal in their designs. Innovations, such as automating sheet metal feeds to Thalmann machines, is a clear example of how sheet metal processing can reduce labour costs and speed up production.

For years, Thalmann Machinenbau AG has built a reputation on delivering folders that operate flawlessly. The robust design and unique Torsion shaft technology is our guarantee that customers can make folded parts without interruption and with accuracy. Thalmann machines are built to last and their design is extremely robust. All parts are manufactured in-house to the highest standard.

ISMR: What is your strategic focus/ vision over the next few years?

MC: Thalmann Machinenbau AG's strategic vision is to keep a strong focus on the innovations that customers need. We expect these needs to focus on further automation and features that deliver cost reduction benefits. In addition, we are working on novel operating software that allows customers and operators to better understand part dimensions in relation to operating the machine.

In addition, we have a clear opportunity to unlock under-developed markets for folding technology. It is Thalmann's strategy to be a first mover in such markets, either from a geographic or an application perspective.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal folding/ bending markets?

MC: We expect that architects and product designers will gradually discover and better understand the possibilities offered by folding technology to cost-effectively bend sheet metal, producing a wider scope of dimensions than ever before. Industrial applications in automotive or even office furniture sectors will also be markets to discover that long folding technology is a cost-effective and excellent alternative to press brakes, for example.

ISMR: Where do you see the greatest challenges and opportunities?

MC: We continuously work on how folding technology can either reduce costs or how it can introduce new technology to new markets. The challenge that we see, doing this, is to focus on the right opportunities. We are normally not short of ideas, being so close to our customers, nor are our customers short on ideas to help them grow into new regions or applications but, with a lean/cost-effective operation, it can be a challenge to make the right choices.

In 2020, Thalmann will be at Swissbau in Basel, Switzerland, and Dach&Holz in Stuttgart, Germany, followed by the IRE (International Roofing Expo) in the USA.
