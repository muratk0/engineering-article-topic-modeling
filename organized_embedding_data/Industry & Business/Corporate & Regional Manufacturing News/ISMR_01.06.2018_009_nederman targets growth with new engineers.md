# Nederman targets growth with new engineers

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

To FURTHER ESTABLISH its position as a leading supplier and developer of environmental technology solutions for the manufacturing sector, Nederman has expanded its UK team with two new appointments. It has appointed a new product and solutions sales manager for Ireland and a sales manager for solutions for the North of England. The two appointments will strengthen its position in key geographical areas whilst enhancing support for the existing network of customers and supply chain partners, it told ISMR.

Clive Dalgliesh joins Nederman as the new product and solutions sales manager for Ireland, armed with decades of expertise in the HVAC, ATEX, vacuum and dust and fume extraction sectors. He also served for 20 years with the Royal Navy as a weapons engineer.

Aiming to increase market share and brand awareness in the North of England, Nederman has appointed Graham Murray as the new sales manager for solutions in the area. He is an engineer with extensive expertise in project management, design engineering and filtration systems.
