# CT metrology partnership

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

The WENZEL Group and VisiConsult signed a cooperation agreement for a development and sales partnership at the recent Control exhibition in Stuttgart. This is intended to jointly expand successes achieved to date in computed tomography (CT)-based metrology solutions.

“The key aspects of our partnership lie in joint development towards a product range tailored for industrial metrology based on CT technology," explained Lennart Schulenburg, Managing Partner, VisiConsult. "We see enormous advantages for users by bundling the expertise of both companies for the development, production and realisation of this joint product portfolio."

“By using existing synergies in purchasing, development and production and concentrating on the respective core competencies of VisiConsult and WENZEL, the attractiveness of the CT solutions we offer to customers will be significantly increased," added Dr. Heike Wenzel, MD, WENZEL Group. She hopes that this will result in a significantly higher market share for both partners in the rapidly growing CT metrology market.

The goal of the two medium-sized familyowned companies is long-term cooperation in the design, production and marketing of holistic CT solutions, in which measurement technology plays an essential role in the system solution.

Lennart Schulenburg, VisiConsult, and Dr. Heike Wenzel, WENZEL, sign the cooperation agreement at Control in Stuttgart, Germany.

VisiConsult, a family-owned company in Northern Germany, is a specialist in X-ray image processing and automation. The WENZEL Group is a leading supplier of industrial metrology and styling solutions.
