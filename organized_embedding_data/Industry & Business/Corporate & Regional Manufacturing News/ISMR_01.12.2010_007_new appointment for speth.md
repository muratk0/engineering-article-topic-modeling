# NEW APPOINTMENT FOR SPETH

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

Dr. Ralf Speth, CEO, Jaguar Land Rover was appointed as a Non-Executive Director on the Tata Motors Board with effect from 9 November 2010.

Dr Speth was appointed to the post of Chief Executive Officer at Jaguar Land Rover on 18 February 2010. He is on the Board of Jaguar Land Rover Limited, UK and is also the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the two wholly-owned subsidiary companies, Jaguar Cars Limited and Land Rover in the UK.

## NEW DEPARTMENT HEAD AT LASER ZENTRUM HANNOVER E.V.

Dr.-Ing. Stephan Barcikowski is the new head of the Material and Processing Technologies Department at the Laser Zentrum Hannover e.V. (LZH) Dr. Barcikowski studied chemistry at the TU Braunschweig (Brunswick) and the Leibniz Universität Hannover before he started working at the LZH. In 2003, he completed his doctorate degree with honours and in the years that followed he built up the Nanomaterials Group at the LZH.

Dr. Barcikowski is a founding member of the German Research Foundation (DFG) excellence cluster “From Regenerative Biology to Reconstructive Surgery REBIRTH” and he is the leader of the special young nanoparticles researcher group. Dr. Barcikowski was also the initiator and head of the programme committee of the new conference on the topic “Laser Ablation and Nanoparticle Generation in Liquids” (ANGEL 2010), that took place for the first time this year in Engelberg, Switzerland. His main fields of interest are the material aspects of laser material processing as well as laserbased generation of nanomaterials.
