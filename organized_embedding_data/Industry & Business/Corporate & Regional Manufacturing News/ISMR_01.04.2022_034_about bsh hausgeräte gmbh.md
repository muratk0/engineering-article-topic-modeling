# About BSH Hausgeräte GmbH

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

BSH Hausgeräte GmbH was founded in Munich as a joint venture of Robert Bosch GmbH (Stuttgart) and Siemens AG (Munich) and is one of the world's leading companies in the industry, as well as the largest household appliance manufacturer in Europe.

BSH produces the entire range of modern household appliances in 39 factories worldwide. Its product portfolio ranges from cookers, ovens and cooker hoods to dishwashers, washing machines, tumble dryers, refrigerators and freezers to small household appliances such as vacuum cleaners, fully automatic coffee machines or kitchen machines. Since 2015, the company has been fully owned by the Bosch Group and currently employs around 60,000 people.

Since 1949, household appliances have been built in Traunreut near Lake Chiemsee – firstly by Siemens and, since 1967, by BSH Hausgeräte GmbH. In Traunreut, BSH develops and produces products and innovations for modern households on 430,000m² floor space.

In addition to the development and production of ovens and hobs, its competence centre for small household appliances (such as fully automatic coffee machines) is also located here. Around two million household appliances leave the production facilities annually. Around 3,000 people are employed at the BSH site at Traunreut.

## About b+s

Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH (b+s) was founded in 1945 and manufactures machines and systems for cutting and levelling metal strip. The products are tailored to the needs of customers.

The company has many years of experience in the thin strip sector and specialises primarily in slitting lines; stretch-bend levellers; packaging lines; traverse winders; levellers; cut-to-length lines and strip processing lines. At its German site in Remchingen, systems are developed and manufactured with the “Made in Germany” quality feature.

The roots of Schnutz GmbH go back to a blacksmith's shop founded in 1846 by Martin Schnutz, whose focus increasingly shifted to the design and construction of sheet metal working machines as the sheet metal industry in the Siegerland region grew at the turn of the 20th century. In 1882, Schnutz manufactured its first levellers. The most recent innovations include the Schnutz-Multidrive multi-motor drive and a further development of its electric motor support systems. Since 2014, the company has belonged to the b+s group.

b+s AUTOMATION GmbH is a medium-sized automation specialist, founded in 1995 as DTA DELTA TECHNIK Automation GmbH. Offering automation solutions for initial plant equipment and plant modernisation, the company has expertise in drive/ safety technology and precision controls. Since 2018, it has been a member of the b+s group and, with a change of name in 2021 to b+s AUTOMATION, delivers technology for conveyor systems.
