# New Board of Directors and CEO for Prima Industrie

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Prima Industrie (a global group in the laser systems, sheet-metalworking systems and electronic components for industrial applications sector) has announced that, following the closing operation by Femto Technologies S.p.A. (a company whose share capital is indirectly owned by Alpha Private Equity and Peninsula funds) a new Board of Directors has been elected and a new Chief Executive Officer has been appointed.

The new Prima Industrie S.p.A. Board of Directors includes Gianfranco Carbonato (Executive Chairman); Domenicc Peiretti (Deputy Chairman); Giovanni Negri (CEO); Ezio G. Basso (Director); Donatella Busso (Independent Director); Francesca Cirrincione (Independent Director); Nicola Colavito (Director); Francesca de Fraja Frangipane (Independent Director); Edoardo M. Lanzavecchia (Director); Barbara Poggiali (Independent Director) and Lisa Marie Tan (Director).

Giovanni Negri has also been appointed CEO of Prima Industrie S.p.A. With a degree in Management Engineering and a Master's in Business Administration from the Politecnico di Milano, Negri has been CEO of CMS SpA since November 2018. CMS SpA (part of the SCM Group) specialises in the production of numerically controlled multi-axis machining centres. He previously held several positions of increasing responsibility in SCM Group, Porsche Consulting and Siemens in Germany and Italy.

The former CEO, Ezio Basso, will remain on the Board of Directors and will actively cooperate with Giovanni Negri on an effective handover in the Group.

Company Executive Chairman, Gianfranco Carbonato, commented: "I wish to thank Ezio Basso, even on behalf of past and new shareholders, for the long-lasting collaboration that allowed our Group to flourish and become what it is today. I welcome Giovanni Negri, wishing him all the best for the new challenge, conscious that significant opportunities for further growth are within our reach."
