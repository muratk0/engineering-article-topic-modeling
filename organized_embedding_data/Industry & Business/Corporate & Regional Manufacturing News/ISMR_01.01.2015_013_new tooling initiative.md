# New tooling initiative

[TARİH: 01.01.2015 ISMR]

NOVELIS, a global leader in aluminium rolling and recycling, has announced the commissioning of a new coating line for beverage can end stock and an expanded recycling centre at its Pindamonhangaba (SP), Brazil complex. The company has invested approximately US$ 106 million in the combined projects that will add 100,000 metric tons of coating capacity and 190,000 metric tons of recycling capacity when fully operational.

"These critical expansion projects increase our capacity to meet the growing demand for beverage cans in Brazil and enable Novelis to process even higher levels of recycled material as we continue to increase the recycled content in our products," said Tadeu Nardocci, Senior Vice President, Novelis and President of Novelis South America.

"Brazil is one of the most rapidly-growing regions where Novelis operates around the world, highlighted by recent growth driven by the World Cup. As we look ahead, we expect the anticipated increase in the population's disposable income and the upcoming Olympic Games to drive additional demand in the region."

The addition of this 100,000-ton coating line doubles the market's capacity to supply coated can end stock and enables Novelis to deliver directly to its can customers in the region, a function previously provided by a third-party partner. Novelis expects to begin shipping commercial product from the new line in coming months.

The investment in the recycling centre supports the company's long-term commitment to increasing the recycled content in its products to 80 per cent by 2020. With this expansion, the plant's recycling centre, already the largest in South America, will nearly double its capacity from 200,000 to 390,000 metric tons per year when fully commissioned. Over 100 new jobs were created to support the expanded operations. ■

## Change of date for BLECH Russia 2015

EXHIBITION ORGANISER, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, has announced that the dates for BLECH Russia 2015 have been rescheduled. The event will continue to take place in the same week as planned, but the opening date is now a day later. The three-day show will now take place from 25-27 March 2015. The venue for the event remains unchanged at the recently completed ExpoForum in St Petersburg.

## Large scale stamping in Mexico

MERCURY PRODUCTS’ acquisition of a new 1,000-ton stamping press, at its Saltillo, Coahuila (Mexico) facility, fulfils customer demand for larger stampings required for many automotive, heavy truck, off-highway and other industrial equipment manufacturing needs.

“Our customers can now consolidate multiple parts orders that were previously placed with several suppliers. The new largeton and bed stamping press complements existing capabilities including metal stamping and fabrications, tube assemblies, welding, polishing and buffing, and everything in-between,” explained the manufacturer.

The company provides global service capabilities with manufacturing facilities in Schaumburg, Illinois; Englewood, Colorado; Windsor, Ontario, Canada; and Saltillo, Mexico; in addition to supply chain fulfilment in Hong Kong, China. ■
