## Integrated tube processing

TRUMPF OFFERS solutions for every stage of the tube processing line: welding and cutting tubes, joining cut tube sections and marking. The TruLaser Cell 1100 welds strips of metal into tubes and profiles. The TruLaser Tube 7000 cuts the tubes and profiles to form the required parts. The TruLaser Robot 5020 joins the parts to create the complete component. And, in a final step, markings can be written on the components, either as a means of ensuring traceability or for decorative effect.

“For each stage of this process, TRUMPF has the appropriate laser tools and systems.

The end-to-end use of lasers for welding, cutting and marking tubes and profiles offers new possibilities that conventional processing techniques cannot provide. Advantages include the flexibility to produce an endless variety of contours and seams in many different materials, and reduced set-up times, enabling you to move on rapidly to the next processing job,” TRUMPF explained.
