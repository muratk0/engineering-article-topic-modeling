## EMO General Commissioner appointed

PIER LUIGI Streparava is the new General Commissioner of EMO MILANO 2015, the global machine tool exhibition that will take place from 5-10 October 2015 at the Fieramilano exhibition centre. He is the president of Streparava S.p.A., based in Adro (Brescia), and past president of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tool, robot and automation manufacturers’ association). He is still a Member of the Board of the association as well as President of the Italian-Chinese Chamber of Commerce.

Mr. Streparava will supervise the organisation of EMO MILANO 2015. ■

## Independent director for China Precision Steel

CHINA PRECISION Steel, Inc., a niche precision steel processing company principally engaged in producing and selling high precision, cold-rolled steel products, has appointed Mr. Wei Hong Xiao to its Board of Directors and the audit committee (effective from 1 November 2013).

“On behalf of China Precision Steel, I am very pleased to welcome Mr. Xiao to this appointment as we believe his in-depth experience of China’s steel industry and business acumen will help guide the company through this challenging period and strengthen our competitive position. Additionally, we feel Mr. Xiao will contribute to maintaining a high standard of corporate governance,” commented Mr. Hai Sheng Chen, CEO of China Precision Steel.

Mr. Wei Hong Xiao has served as Professor, Department of Technology, China Iron and Steel Association since 1998. Prior to 1998, he spent over 25 years at the Ministry of Metallurgical Industry in various engineering and management positions. ■

KMF (Precision Sheet Metal) apprentices, Harley Brian and Ryan Murphy, took a step closer to being part of Team UK at the next World Skills competition, after taking gold and silver medals respectively in the Advanced Sheet Metal category during the recent Skills Show at the NEC in the UK.

Harley and Ryan, who are just entering their third year as apprentices at KMF, hope to follow in the footsteps of their mentor, and former KMF apprentice, Matt Page who was part of the team representing the UK at World Skills Leipzig 2013 in the sheet metal category. The recent Skills Show competition saw almost 700 apprentices competing for places in the Team UK training squad for Brazil 2015, representing more than 40 diverse skill groups. Harley and Ryan will now join other medal winners on an intense two year programme which will see them compete against each other for a coveted place on the plane to Brazil in 2015.

Sheet metal technology was only reintroduced to the list of skills in 2009 after a long absence. The sheet metal technology category is a wide-ranging remit, with apprentices challenged in skills that can be seen in everyday products found in sectors such as automotive, aerospace structures, construction, shop-fitting and catering equipment. ■
