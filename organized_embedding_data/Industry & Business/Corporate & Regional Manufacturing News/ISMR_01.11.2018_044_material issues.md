[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

CERATIZIT demonstrated its latest innovations to ISMR at EuroBLECH in Hanover.

ISMR SAYS: "CERATIZIT stormed into EuroBLECH with an accent on Industry 4.0"

Mustafa Turkoglu, Country Manager Germany – Industrial Wear Parts, CERATIZIT

## CERATIZIT GROUP

developed hard material products for cutting tools and wear protection. The privately-owned company, based in Mamer (Luxembourg), develops and manufactures highly specialised carbide cutting tools, inserts and rods made of hard materials as well as wear parts. It is a specialist in several wear part application areas, and successfully develops new types of carbide, cermet and ceramic grades.

With over 9,000 employees at 34 production sites and a sales network of over 70 branch offices, the Group is a global player in the carbide industry. Its international network also includes the subsidiaries KOMET, WNT and Becker Diamantwerkzeuge, as well as the joint venture CB-CERATIZIT. The company is a partner for manufacturers wishing to optimise applications for wear protection, metalforming or tool construction.

ISMR sat down with CERATIZIT's country manager for Germany for industrial wear parts, Mustafa Turkoglu, at this October's EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover to find out about its latest innovations.

## Material issues

"As a specialist in materials technology, we continuously invest in research and development and hold over 1,000 patents. Our innovative carbide solutions are used in mechanical engineering and tool construction and many other industries including the automotive, aerospace, oil and medical sectors. We are active in the market through our seven competence brands – Hard Material Solutions by CERATIZIT; Toolmaker Solutions by CERATIZIT; Tool Solutions by CERATIZIT as well as Cutting Solutions by CERATIZIT, KOMET, WNT and KLENK," Mustafa Turkoglu, Country Manager Germany – Industrial Wear Parts, CERATIZIT told ISMR at this October's EuroBLECH exhibition.

With over 9,000 employees at 34 production sites and a sales network of over 70 branch offices, the Group is a global player in the carbide industry.

CERATIZIT is an amalgamation of two companies in 2002 (one based in Austria, and the other based in Luxembourg). It also has seven factories in Germany, one in Italy (Como) and four factories in the USA as well as a production site and a JV with several production sites in China (CB CERATIZIT). Over the last four years, it has acquired several companies including cutting tool manufacturer, KOMET (based near Stuttgart).

"We have experienced growth of around 10% on average each year. This year was particularly strong for us in terms of new orders and new customers. In the last three years, we have grown by over 10% per year in the Group, as a whole. Our Board continuously spends almost 20% of its revenue for process and product innovations each year. This is spent not just on equipment and R&D but also on people (training, education etc.). We hold a lot of technical seminars for our customers. At our unit near Stuttgart, we recently held an e-mobility seminar for customers," continued Turkoglu.

## An eye on Industry 4.0

CERATIZIT stormed into EuroBLECH with an accent on Industry 4.0 for its Configurator system, which was highlighted on its booth. Visitors at the show were given the opportunity to test out the userfriendliness of the online configurator for nibs and blanks.

"Our Configurator gives our customers price, delivery time and process updates for our products. There is no need for customers to enquire at our office, they can go online and even use the Configurator to receive a quotation or place their order. It is connected to our E-Techstore where customers can order products and are given an account for the Configurator. Customer reaction has been very encouraging, as it saves them (and us) a great deal of time," Turkoglu told ISMR.

"In the future, our customers will also be able to receive all information on their product and they can then provide feedback to us on the Configurator so that we can continuously improve and refine this system."

Industry 4.0 will have an enormous impact on manufacturing, feels Turkoglu. Saving time and money, as well as connecting manufacturing operations, are key benefits. This runs hand-in-hand with trends affecting the industry, such as e-mobility.

"E-mobility is a huge trend in the automotive sector. A lot of our customers cut sheet for the engines used in electric cars. We supply special carbide grades for that. We are working also with specific universities (in the form of research partnerships) on e-mobility. For example, we have a partnership with the Technical University of Aachen for fine blanking. The rise of the electric car means that more copper will be used and worked, and we need to consider its effects on tungsten carbide tools," he added.

## Waterjet cutting

The new Premium+ focusing tube, from Hard Material Solutions by CERATIZIT, is designed for demanding waterjet cutting applications, whether exotic or easily machinable materials, rough-cut or high precision-cut.

"Long tool life and reliability of the focusing tubes ensure minimum machine downtime, and therefore maximum productivity and cost savings. Premium+ sets new standards for water-jet cutting," said the Luxembourg manufacturer.

"Premium+ focusing tubes are suitable for demanding waterjet cutting applications, whether users process exotic or easily machinable materials, composite materials, split cuts or high-precision cuts. Long tool life and reliability of the focusing tube keep machine downtime to a minimum. This is the way to guarantee maximum productivity and cut costs. Premium+ sets new standards for water-jet cutting," CERATIZIT told ISMR.

Optimised wear protection ensures longer tool life (which supports improved machine utilisation). Improved material quality and processing offer up to a 30% improvement in performance, according to the manufacturer. The tubes are used in a wide range of industry sectors, including furniture, aerospace and food preparation sectors.

## Into the future

"We are focusing our efforts in two areas: firstly, to continuously improve our carbide grades (via R&D projects) and, secondly, to improve our levels of service in the after-sales market. At the local service level, we have our own sales offices and product managers who work closely with the customers on technical issues. We often work with our customers on projects to improve or increase the lifetimes of our material grades, for example," he told ISMR.

These initiatives include working on new application fields for tungsten carbide. The company's R&D departments in Luxembourg and Austria are not only working on improving tungsten carbide grades (the company has 150), but also on new grades for fields where tungsten carbide is not currently used. In the next two to three years, said Turkoglu, it should be able to introduce new grades.

A swift cut
The CERATIZIT Group presented its restructured cutting tools division for the first time, including the new competence brand structure, at the AMB 2018 exhibition in Stuttgart, Germany, recently. Under the umbrella brand CERATIZIT Group, the cutting tool specialist was represented with the four Cutting Solutions Competence Brands by CERATIZIT, KOMET, WNT and KLENK. The international company used its acquisition of precision tool manufacturer, KOMET GROUP, to pool all its cutting tools activities and align them to the needs of its customers. In the course of this process, KOMET was fully integrated into the structures of the CERATIZIT Group.

The CERATIZIT Group inaugurated the CERATIZIT Jacques Lanners Halle on the first day of the AMB trade fair. The company has secured the naming rights for Hall 3 as part of a ten-year marketing partnership with Messe Stuttgart. The hall is named after Jacques Lanners, grandson of one of the company's two founders and long-standing Chairman of the Group Executive Board.

"The product portfolios of CERATIZIT and KOMET complement each other perfectly," explained Thierry Wolter, Member of the Executive Board "Together, we can now offer our customers comprehensive, highquality solutions from a single source. While CERATIZIT occupies a very strong position in turning, milling and grooving, for example, and offers solutions for various applications, KOMET is a specialist for all machining steps involving bore holes and, among other things, provides strong solutions for the automotive industry. The Group presented joint solution packages for the automotive powertrain sector in the CERATIZIT Jacques Lanners Halle at AMB."

At present, as much as 40% of the Group's cutting tools are less than five years old. But its ambitions go even further. The pace of innovation is to be further increased in the coming years, to around 50 per cent, according to Wolter. One of its tools, FreeTurn (one single tool for all traditional turning operations) also picked up an award during the AMB exhibition in Germany.

The cutting tool streamlining activities has also extended to sales. There is now only one sales organisation per country which is responsible for the products of all four competence brands.
