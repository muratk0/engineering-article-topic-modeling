# Servosteel appoints new commercial director

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

Servosteel in the UK has confirmed the appointment of Steve Dickens as its commercial director from 1 December 2021, bringing the appointment forward from April 2022.

“We had previously discussed a date of April 2022 for this appointment but, given the progress and impact that Steve has made since his return to the business, we are delighted to bring the appointment forward. We would like to congratulate Steve and wish him every success in his new role," said the company, a major UK independent steel toll processor (and one-stop steel shop) with an annual capacity of over 500,000 tonnes. Its processing facilities include three slitting lines; two decoiling lines; a coil pickling line; a blanking line and "the UK's only coil recovery line" In recent years, the company has made significant investments in laser services.■
