Lincoln Electric promotes Hedlund

THE LINCOLN Electric Company, a subsidiary of Lincoln Electric Holdings, Inc., has promoted Steven B. Hedlund to Senior Vice President and President, Global Automation. Steven Hedlund previously served the company as Senior Vice President, Strategy and Business Development.

The new role expands executive leadership of Lincoln Electric’s global automation initiative, which is a key growth programme in the company’s ‘2020 Vision and Strategy’ targeting accelerated growth in sales, profitability and returns by 2020. Its global automation offering is a broad portfolio of automated arc welding and cutting solutions. The initiative is focused on achieving US$ 500 million in revenue by 2020. As President, Mr. Hedlund will oversee the implementation of all strategic, operational and commercial aspects of the global automation organisation.

## The Bradbury Co., Inc. has announced several new promotions

Aaron Woods has accepted the position of field completion coordinator within the Customer Service Department. Aaron has worked in hydraulic assembly, most recently as a test technician specialising in Bradbury eDrive® Levelers. He will be responsible for coordinating equipment installations and start-ups through to completion.

Mike Nuzum is the new field service technician for the customer service department. After serving the company in electrical assembly, he will be responsible for assisting customers and Bradbury field technicians in troubleshooting equipment. Mike brings a strong electrical background to the department with an Associate’s Degree in Applied Science and Electrical Technology along with a Journeyman’s license from North Central Kansas Technical College.

Shawn Fields has accepted the position of parts and sales technician within the customer service department. He will be responsible for assisting customers and field technicians in placing part orders, upgrades, and repairs.

The companies of The Bradbury Group are leading manufacturers and worldwide suppliers of roll forming and coil processing equipment for a wide range of industries. www.bradburygroup.com

## CHANGES AT HYPERTHERM

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, appointed, as of 1 January 2015, current President Evan Smith as CEO from the founder Dick Couch, who will retain the position of Executive Chairman of the Board and Trustee of the Hypertherm Stock Ownership Plan.

The current Director of Corporate Social Responsibility, Jenny Levy, will assume the role of Vice President, Corporate Social Responsibility from Barbara Couch, who will retain her roles as board director and chair of its Corporate Social Responsibility Committee. She will also remain as President of the HOPE Foundation and sponsor of Hypertherm’s HOPE Team.

## Top team at Semta

THE FUTURE of engineering and advanced manufacturing skills is in the hands of a new dynamic executive team at Semta. Semta, which is engineering skills for the future and represents 138,000 companies in the sector, has appointed an all-female team to combat the skills gap and champion vocational training. Semta is employer-led and responsible for equipping the UK’s vital engineering, science and manufacturing technologies sectors with the skills to compete on the global stage.

Ann Watson, Chief Executive Officer, and Al Parkes, the newly appointed chief operating officer, are spearheading the organisation that acts as a vital conduit between industry and its prospective workforce. With a requirement of 100,000 STEM graduates per year and at least two million engineers within the next ten years, the challenges ahead are significant.

CEO, Ann Watson, said: “Semta will inspire the next generation of engineers, scientists and technicians, creating the competent and skilled workforce that UK plc needs to compete effectively on the global stage. We will continue to create employer-led skills’ solutions and support partnerships with industry, government and other key stakeholders.” www.semta.org.uk
