# Caterpillar announces new appointments

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

JIM UMPLEBY BECAME Caterpillar's Chief Executive Officer on 1 January 2017. Tom Pellette, 53, became group president of Energy & Transportation and Billy Ainsworth, 60, became senior vice president and strategic advisor to the Caterpillar Executive Office on the same date. In addition to his new responsibilities, Ainsworth will continue to have responsibility for Caterpillar's Rail Division.

After more than 38 years with Caterpillar Kent Adams, 62, President and CEO of Caterpillar Financial Services Corporation and Caterpillar vice president of the Financial Products Division, has elected to retire. Dave Walton, 54, will replace Adams as President and CEO of Caterpillar Financial Services Corporation and Caterpillar vice president of the Financial Products Division.

Tom Pellette has been appointed Caterpillar Inc.'s group president of Energy & Transportation

Bob De Lange is now Caterpillar Inc.'s group president of Construction Industries

Billy Ainsworth is now senior vice president and strategic advisor to the Caterpillar Executive Office

Mike DeWalt, 59, vice president of the Finance Services Division, has also elected to retire. The company has appointed Joe Creed, 41, to replace DeWalt as vice president of the Finance Services Division. Creed is currently chief financial officer for Energy & Transportation. Bob De Lange will become group president of Construction Industries and Zack Kauk, 49, will become vice president of the Excavation Division

Jill Daugherty, 46, currently Caterpillar's corporate controller, is now chief audit officer. Kyle Epley, 44, currently chief financial officer for Customer & Dealer Support, will become Caterpillar's corporate controller.
