Strategic moves at Hypertherm

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of advanced metal cutting systems, has confirmed that Vice President and General Manager, Evan Smith, will immediately assume the role of President from Hypertherm founder and CEO, Dick Couch. Mr Couch will remain as Chief Executive Officer of Hypertherm and continue to chair Hypertherm's Board of Directors. In addition, Hypertherm CFO, Carey Chen, is expanding his duties and will now oversee Hypertherm's air plasma and related product lines as CFO and Vice President, General Manager of Light Industrial Businesses.

Mr Smith was named Vice President and General Manager of Hypertherm in 2001, overseeing the company's strategic business units and global sales organisation. During that time, and under Mr Smith's guidance, Hypertherm's business has grown substantially with revenue 'more than tripling'.

Mr Chen has served as Hypertherm's Chief Financial Officer since 2006. In this capacity, he has also led the company's information services, legal, corporate development, advanced R&D and various customer-related service functions.

Hypertherm's product line includes handheld and mechanised plasma and laser systems, consumables, as well as CNC motion and height controls and CAM cutting software. ■

## New MIDEST President

EVERY OTHER YEAR, the presidency of the MIDEST event alternates between a representative of the trade associations in France and one from the chambers of commerce. For 2012 and 2013, the chambers of commerce have nominated Jérôme Delabre, Vice-President of the CCIT (regional chamber of commerce and industry) for the coastal zone of Normandy and Picardy with responsibility for industry.

Mr Delabre, who is 45 years old and a member of the UIMM (Union of Metal Industries and Trades) office in Vimeau, is very familiar with MIDEST as the Chairman and Managing Director of Etablissements Delabre, cutting and stamping specialist, and Delabre France Tôlerie, which is involved in sheet metal work and fabrication. These have been exhibitors since 1994 on the Picardy regional stand.

The 42nd MIDEST exhibition, a leading industrial subcontracting show, will be held from 6 to 9 November at the Paris Nord Villepinte Exhibition Centre, France. ■

## New addition for AWL-Techniek's Board of Directors

“AWL had an excellent year in 2011, during which the company continued to grow even further," the company told ISMR. To manage this growth and to promote a clear corporate vision, Harald Lubbinge joined the Board of Directors on 1 January 2012.

Mr Lubbinge has worked with AWL since 2007 and in the last couple of years has filled the positions of both Operations Manager and Project Management Manager. By joining the Board, he also takes on a new function, namely that of Chief Operating Officer. In this position, he has ultimate responsibility for the assembly, logistics, process quality and project management departments.

AWL completed its strategic plan for the medium-term in 2011 and “operational excellence” is an important part of this, for which Harald Lubbinge is also responsible.

“In its policy plan, AWL anticipates the turnover to double within the next five years, although the company is not looking to take on a similar proportion of new employees to achieve this. More efficient deployment of our resources, improved management of our supply chain and strategic outsourcing of our activities will play a much bigger role,” Harald Lubbinge added. ■

## JK Lasers appoints German sales manager

TO SUPPORT PLANS to grow its presence in Germany, industrial laser manufacturer JK Lasers has appointed a new German sales manager. Dipl.-ing Michael Haase trained as a mechanical engineer and has ten years' experience in the laser industry. After working for the R&D Institute at the Laser Zentrum, Hannover, he established his own laser company, H2B Photonics. When the company was acquired by ROFIN-BAASEL Lasertech, Michael went on to work as its solar industry sales manager.

He was instrumental in the development and commercialisation of new laser processing methods within these roles, and consequently has a broad knowledge of laser technology, including fiber and Nd:YAG.

Based in Planegg, near Munich, Michael will provide sales and technical support to customers throughout Germany. JK Lasers, part of the GSI Group, manufactures Watt to kiloWatt fiber lasers, Nd:YAG lasers and process tools that operate around-the-clock in industrial environments. A new range of powerful multi-kiloWatt class fiber lasers will be added later this year. ■
