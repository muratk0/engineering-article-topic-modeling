# AMADA showcases innovation

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

THE Amada Innovation Fair 2013 Europe, Amada’s largest private event in Europe, was held for five days from 24-28 of September at the Technical Centre of Amada S.A. in the suburbs of Paris, France. Approximately 1,400 visitors and 700 companies attended from countries such as Europe, Russia, Turkey etc. ISMR flew over to see the latest technologies and processes from the Japanese sheet metal machine tool specialist.

“We are showcasing the ‘ultimate digital sheet metal automated factory’ here,” explained Amada CEO and President, Mitsuo Okamoto. “It includes the essence of technical innovation, together with Amada’s most advanced laser automation and automated innovation. The aim is to reinforce the Amada brand and double our market share in Europe.”

Amada has amassed over 3,000 patents in more than sixty years of overall operation. Founded in 1946 by Isamu Amada, and with 81 subsidiaries throughout the world, its new European HQ in Paris will strengthen the synergy between its European units.

## Schuler streamlines organisation

Dr. Markus Ernst stepped down from Schuler AG’s Board of Management on 30 September 2013. He had been a member of the Board since 2007 and was responsible for five technology fields in the Group’s non-automotive business. His responsibilities will be assumed by Schuler’s CEO, Stefan Klebert.

Dr. Wolfgang Porsche, Chairman of the Supervisory Board of Porsche AG

As of 1 October 2013, the Board of Management of Schuler AG now consists of Stefan Klebert (CEO – Chief Executive Officer), Joachim Beyer (CTO – Chief Technology Officer), Norbert Broger (CFO – Chief Financial Officer and Personnel Director) and Peter Jost (COO – Chief Operating Officer).

The Board of Management and Supervisory Board are currently examining possibilities to streamline the complex Group structure, especially in Germany, which has resulted from a number of company acquisitions over the years. See www.schuler.com

Stefan Klebert, CEO, Schuler AG

## Porsche extends key contracts

The Supervisory Board of Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG, Stuttgart, has appointed Executive Board members Wolfgang Hatz (54) and Uwe-Karsten Städter (57) for a further five years. The new contract for Wolfgang Hatz, who took over as Member of the Executive Board Research and Development department in February 2011, is due to start on 1 February 2014. Uwe-Karsten Städter has been responsible for the procurement department since April 2011 – his new contract begins on 1 April 2014.

Chairman of the Supervisory Board of Porsche AG, Dr. Wolfgang Porsche, paid tribute to the performance put in by both Executive Board members over the last three years.

“The contribution from Mr Hatz and Mr Städter has been pivotal in creating the integrated automotive group with Volkswagen, thereby helping to set the course for a successful future for Porsche AG,” he explained.

## AWS appoints new marketing and technology chiefs

The American Welding Society (AWS), the world’s largest organisation dedicated to advancing the science, technology and application of welding, has announced the appointments of William “Bill” Fudale as chief marketing officer and Dennis D. Harwig as chief technology officer.

In his role, Fudale will lead AWS’s global expansion strategy by overseeing sales operations, including show sales, advertising sales and international business development. In addition, Fudale also oversees operations of AWS marketing and communications.

Dennis Harwig joins the AWS team as chief technology officer where he oversees AWS’s technical and education services departments. Before coming to AWS, Harwig worked for the Edison Welding Institute for more than five years. He also served as vice president of global product engineering for Thermadyne Industries (now Victor Technologies) before returning to EWI.

Harwig holds a B.S. and M.S. degree in welding engineering from The Ohio State University and a Ph.D. in welding engineering technology from Cranfield University.

## Precision blanking line

HADAYA Engg & Metal Services of Dubai, United Arab Emirates, recently installed a Red Bud Industries’ 3mm (0.118”) x 1524mm (60”) x 20,412kg (45,000lb.) precision blanking line. Hadaya operates a service centre in Dubai that specialises in processing stainless steel.

Featured in the line is Red Bud’s dual motor precision grip feed which enables Hadaya to produce blanks with length tolerances of +/-0.127mm (0.005”). The line includes Red Bud’s high speed hydraulic shear and 3-side blank support drop stacker. The drop stacker mounts directly to the back of the shear which allows material to enter the drop stacker before shearing. Once the material is sheared, it drops straight down onto the waiting stack table.

The new line features a coil lift; decoiler; peeler breaker; dual cassette 6-high roller leveler; pop-up flatness table; thread system; dual motor grip feed system; shear; drop stacker; automatic lowering stack table; end discharge roll-outs and pallet loader.

## MAKING THE CUT

GONVAUTO Thuringen has ordered the supply, installation and commissioning of a new cutting line with blanking press to a consortium integrated by three Spanish companies: SALICO HISPANIA, ARISA and BIELE. The line is designed to cut blanks for the automotive sector, handling material of up to 3mm in thickness with maximum dimensions of 4500mm x 2500mm.

The feeding line, supplied by SALICO, can receive coils of up to 30 tons (maximum OD of 2.000mm), with coil eye of either 508 or 610mm. Once the coils are placed on the entry storage saddles, loading onto the decoiler is automatic. Mandrel design allows handling of both eye dimensions without generating marks or damaging the last wraps of the coil.
