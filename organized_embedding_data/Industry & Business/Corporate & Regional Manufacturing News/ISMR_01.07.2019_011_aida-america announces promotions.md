# AIDA-America announces promotions

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

PRESS SPECIALIST AIDA-AMERICA recently promoted several employees and thanked them for their dedication. Bob Southwell, who began his career with AIDA-America in August 1998, has been promoted to executive VP. He has held many positions with AIDA-America, including regional sales manager, market specialist, national sales director and VP of sales & marketing. In his new role, Bob will still retain all VP of sales & marketing responsibilities

Mike West and Al Wichner have both been promoted to purchasing group leaders. West joined AIDA-America in March 2012 as supply chain coordinator. Wichner has been employed by AIDA-America for 12 years and previously served as supply chain coordinator. In their new roles, they will assist in directing the purchasing of materials, supplies and services and are responsible for controlling total direct and indirect materials' costs.

Ben Shepherd, who joined AIDA-America's engineering department in May 2012, has been promoted to mechanical engineering group leader. His previous positions include mechanical engineer and senior mechanical engineer. In his new position, Ben is now responsible for managing mechanical engineering department associates, drawings and the department's master schedule.

## Davide Faconti joins Blue Ocean Robotics

BLUE OCEAN ROBOTICS, a robot venture factory that develops, produces and sells service robots for healthcare, hospitality, construction and agriculture has hired Davide Faconti as its senior robot architect.

"Faconti is an internationally recognized expert in humanoid robots, modeldriven development of robot software and the C++ programming language. He is also very active in the open source robotics community, where he is one of the organisers of and a frequent speaker at C++ meetups. At Blue Ocean Robotics, Faconti is now responsible for developing new types of robots designed to improve quality, working environments and productivity in current and potential Blue Ocean Robotics markets, such as healthcare, hospitality, construction and agriculture," said the company.

Before Blue Ocean Robotics, Faconti spent two years at the Eurecat technology centre, where he developed software algorithms and programming tools for a variety of robot projects, including agricultural robots and mobile robot arms and hands. Before that, in 2004, he became known for starting the company Pal Robotics, one of the leading manufacturers of humanoid robot platforms. While there, he led project management and design of hardware and software, which included the design of four generations of humanoid robots and the development of real-time controllers and simulators for legged robots.

Faconti has participated in several projects in academic and industrial sectors. As a member of the Institute for Human and Machine Cognition team (IHMC) in 2015, he was part of the DARPA Robotic Challenge, which focused on hierarchical inverse kinematics and control of hyper-redundant articulated robots. He graduated with an Electronic Engineering degree from Tonio Mallia University in Malta and holds a Master's degree in Complex Adaptive Systems from Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg. ■
