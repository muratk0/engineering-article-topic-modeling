# Swift-Cut Automation wins Queen's Award for Enterprise

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

Dr Liam Fox (second from right) opens the new factory

SWIFT-CUT AUTOMATION has won the highly coveted 2018 Queens Awards for International Trade. The Queens Awards for Enterprise are awarded to businesses who excel in one of four categories: International Trade, Innovation, Sustainable Development and Promoting Opportunity.

"The International Trade Award recognises the outstanding success we have had since our mission to expand globally began. Since our inception in 2011, we have risen from a company designing and manufacturing just one CNC plasma cutting table in the UK to a global success story - launching in 35 countries, exporting over 80% of our business globally and producing a range of extremely efficient cutting machinery with an enviable reputation for innovation, affordability and quality. This is a recognition of the highest order from the Prime Minister and the Queen," said Swift-Cut Automation.

Swift Cut's international global development director, Neil Smith, added: "We are thrilled that our international growth and success has been recognised by the Queens Award, arguably the most prestigious business award in the UK and a testament to our product, our team and our vision."

Dr Liam Fox, UK Secretary of State for International Trade, recently opened Swift-Cut's new UK factory. Swift-Cut is a leading UK manufacturer of CNC plasma and waterjet cutting systems to the light and medium industrial fabrication, construction and manufacturing sectors. Its range of cutting systems include the PRO range, the smaller Swifty and the latest addition, the Swift-Jet waterjet cutting system. See www.swift-cut.co.uk
