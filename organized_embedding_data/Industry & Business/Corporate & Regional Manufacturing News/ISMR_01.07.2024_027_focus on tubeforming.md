# FOCUS ON TUBEFORMING

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

The company successfully established product innovations such as BENTELER HYRESIST®hydrogen line pipes in 2023. In 2023, the Steel/Tube Division also made several improvements to its own product portfolio. These included, for example, expanding its portfolio at the Shreveport site (USA). ## An eye on sustainability

As a company with close to 150 years of tradition, long-term thinking is part of BENTELER's DNA.

“Our goal is to create a more sustainable future for all of us—our employees, our customers, society. In 2023, we once again demonstrated our commitment to sustainability. Among other things, we significantly reduced our energy consumption. We also concluded declarations of intent for the purchase of CO₂-reduced raw material. Our Steel/Tube Division was awarded the gold medal by EcoVadis, the world's largest provider of sustainability ratings. The success of our activities is also reflected in the CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project) rating. We have been participating with the Automotive Division since 2012 and have improved continuously since then. In 2023, we achieved an 'A' rating in the Climate Change category for the first time," said BENTELER in its 2023 Annual Report.

Its customers are increasingly concerned about the carbon footprint of materials to achieve their own sustainability goals. As part of the Product Carbon Footprint calculations (PCFs), the phases from material procurement to when a product leaves the BENTELER premises are analysed ("From Cradle to Gate").

Along with its good economic performance in 2023, the group set further priorities in sustainability. Direct and indirect emissions (Scope 1 and Scope 2) were once again significantly reduced at group level, down around ten per cent on the previous year. Emissions in the supply chain (Scope 3) also fell year-on-year. The same applies to the amount of waste.

At the same time, BENTELER expanded its sustainable product portfolio, maintaining its pioneering role in the field of sustainable steel and tube solutions. With the CliMore® range, introduced in 2023, the company has grouped CO₂-reduced steels and steel tubes into three sustainability categories. This creates transparency and helps customers to decarbonise their own supply chains.

“As a global company in an energy-intensive industry, we are aware of our responsibility to the environment and future generations," commented Dr. Tobias Braun, CFO of the BENTELER Group and Managing Director and CFO of the Steel/Tube Division.

“Sustainability is an important part of our corporate strategy. We are consistently reducing CO₂ emissions and focus on the circular economy. Steel production in our electric steel mill in Lingen produces around 75% less CO₂ than steel production via the conventional blast furnace route, an important trump card in the implementation of our sustainability strategy. By 2045, we aim to become CO₂-neutral in the Steel/Tube Division. That goes for the supply chain as well as production and our products. In the latter area, especially, we have established new benchmarks for more sustainable steels and steel tube products with the introduction of our new CliMore® product range," he continued.

Sustainability is also a factor in research and pre-development projects, for example, through lightweight construction and resource conservation. An innovation process exists in the Automotive Division for this that includes the phases of idea management, concept, laboratory and series maturity.

## Electric steel mill celebrates anniversary

BENTELER Steel/Tube's electric steel mill in Lingen celebrated its 50th anniversary in April 2024. Since its commissioning on 1 April 1974, the plant has produced high-quality steel from scrap steel. According to the company, by using an electric arc furnace and recycling scrap, the site currently produces around 75 per cent less CO₂ during steel production than steel production using the traditional blast furnace method. The use of renewable energies can further reduce emissions.

Thomas Michels, Chief Operating Officer of BENTELER Steel/Tube, explained: "Our electric steel mill in Lingen is a symbol of our pioneering spirit at BENTELER. Fifty years ago, we were already relying on our own steel production for our seamless tubes. Today, this investment has become the key to our sustainable production of steel, which we are continuously developing. The key drivers for this are approximately 280 employees at the site, who develop and implement new ideas with great dedication and commitment.

"With continuous modifications to the processes, we have increased the annual production capacity from around 75,000 tons in 1974 to around 650,000 tons today," added Thomas Michels. "To produce the electric steel, we use 100% scrap steel from which new, high-quality steel is produced again and again without any loss of quality. The Lingen site is therefore extremely important for our sustainability efforts. This will remain the case in the future, as Lingen is currently evolving into one of the most important hydrogen centres in Germany and therefore offers significant locational advantages."

The CO₂-reduced steel for all three levels of BENTELER's CliMore® product range is produced in the electric arc furnace in Lingen.

"With our plant in Lingen, we are not only making a decisive contribution to sustainability at BENTELER but are also supporting our customers on their path towards climate neutrality with our CliMore® products. To support our customers' sustainable transformation in the long term, we are also planning to use green hydrogen or biogenic coal in the future," confirmed plant manager, Andreas Stadtherr.

## Looking to the future

In May 2024, the BENTELER Group officially opened its new plant in Bratislava, Slovakia. A total of around 50 guests attended the opening ceremony including employees, the management team as well as representatives of its customer and suppliers. The site will produce rear axles for Volkswagen.

BENTELER is committed to smart production, digitalisation and efficient logistics. The new plant is located just 1.5km from the customer's site. This brings corresponding advantages: in addition to more efficient production and assembly, logistics costs are minimised and rapid response to product and order adjustments is possible.

Over a production area of 5,300 sqm, BENTELER will produce automotive parts in highly flexible and scalable facilities to meet the growing demands of its customers for model diversity and just-in-sequence production. The new location, BENTELER's second automotive plant in Slovakia, will create more than 70 additional jobs. The company has operated a facility in Malacky with around 380 employees since 2009.

In June 2024, BENTELER officially inaugurated a new paint line at its plant in Schwandorf. The cathodic dip coating system strengthens sustainable production at the site. Thanks to the intelligent use of resources, energy and water consumption is reduced and the waste heat for the entire process is reused. In future, the plant can be operated entirely CO₂-neutrally by using biogas.

As part of the new process, components made of steel, galvanized steel and aluminium are dipped in an aqueous, electrically conductive, epoxy-based paint. A voltage field is applied and the paint is deposited on the workpiece. This enables continuous and uniform thickness, even in cavities and on edges.

"We have introduced a separate pretreatment line for aluminium with the new system and are thereby achieving new quality benchmarks for our customers, particularly in the premium segment. At the same time, we are further optimising and stabilising the corrosion protection of the steel," explained the company.

The 2,000m² facility can treat a surface area of up to 7.8 million square metres annually, equivalent to around 1,000 football fields.

In Schwandorf, BENTELER produces battery trays as well as structural and chassis components for German automobile manufacturers.

Digitalisation has revolutionised processes at BENTELER. Investments made early on enable prompt optimisation of workflows and production. By using IoT technologies in predictive maintenance, it can avoid production stoppages. Automated quality control and the use of artificial intelligence save a significant amount of time and increase efficiency. In Big Data analytics, it has been using its own Smart Production Data Platform (SPDP) for several years now. This helps with predictive maintenance, for example. In 2023, the platform was used at 16 additional locations worldwide.

To remain successful in the long term, BENTELER promotes the training and further education of its own workforce. For example, the company has developed specialised employee training programmes to further improve their skills and make best use of technologies on-site.

To make vehicle entry easier, for example, the metal processing specialist has developed a new door concept that removes the need for a B-pillar. This production-ready product, BENTELER confirmed, is lighter than existing solutions in the market and offers improved safety and material features.

"As part of our BENTELER Strategy 2025+, we are constantly developing our futureoriented and agile business model. In 2023, we continued to consistently pursue our vision as leading metal processing specialists. We are focusing on continuous improvement within our three strategic core elements: customer orientation, process efficiency and innovative strength," concluded BENTELER in its 2023 Annual Report.■
