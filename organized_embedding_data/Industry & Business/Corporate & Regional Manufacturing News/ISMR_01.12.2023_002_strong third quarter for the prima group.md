# Strong third quarter for the Prima Group

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

The Board of Directors of Prima Industrie S.p.A., a company operating in the high-tech sector of laser systems and sheet-metal processing and electronics components for industrial applications, has approved the Interim Financial Report at 30/9/2022.

The Company Executive Chairman, Gianfranco Carbonato, commented: “Performance in the first nine months of the year confirmed a positive trend within the Group's main markets. In particular, the third quarter of 2022 was the highest ever both in terms of turnover and order acquisition. To date, we therefore confirm the positive trend also for the last part of the year, both in terms of revenues and profitability.

“The excellent order backlog (which grew to around 303 million euros as of 31/10/22) also allows us to face 2023 with confidence although, at a macroeconomic and political level, strong instability remains on energy costs and procurement, as well as the Russia-Ukraine conflict," he added.

Group turnover, as of 30/9/2022, grew by 16.2% compared to 2021. It increased in the SEMEA region (+22.8%), in Northern Europe (+13.3%) and in the Americas (+22.4%) but slightly decreased in the APAC region (-2.7%).

Its main customers in the SEMEA region were based in Italy (19.5% of consolidated revenues), Poland and Eastern Europe (6.1% of consolidated revenues) and Spain and Portugal (3.8% of consolidated revenues). Main customers in the Northern European region were based in Finland and the Baltic countries (7% of consolidated revenues), DACH countries (5% of consolidated revenues) and Benelux (4.6% of consolidated revenues). Revenues went up in the Americas, with a marked growth in new orders. In the APAC region, revenues decreased by 2.7% compared to 2021.

The Group reportedly kept its position in the Chinese market thanks to strong machinery deliveries to the automotive sector (mainly electric cars).

During the first nine months of 2022, Group order acquisition (including aftersales service) reached 393.9 million euros, up 14.1% compared to 30/9/2021. The consolidated order backlog (not including after-sales service) as of 30/9/2022 was 285.2 million euros, up from 187.9 million euros on 30/09/2021. The backlog further increased to 302.7 million euros as of 31/10/2022.■
