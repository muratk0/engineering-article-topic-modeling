# AN EYE ON INNOVATION

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

Amada outlines the latest trends and developments in sheet metal markets as well as its plans for the coming year

ISMR SAYS:

“A £5 million pound facility investment in the UK, new product launches and an eye on innovation are cornerstones of Amada's focus”

Amada is making plans to upgrade and re-vamp its UK facility in Kidderminster, servicing and selling into UK and Spanish markets – a major £5 million refurbishment project that is due to be completed by September 2013. The Technical Centre is specifically designed for machinery demonstrations, showcasing the latest technology in lasers, punches, press brakes, tooling, automation and CADCAM software.

ISMR drove to Kidderminster to discuss Amada’s expectations, plans and focus for the coming year.

“The revamp is purely to make the customer’s visit experience here more in-depth and detailed so that we can do a better job for him/her. Our relationship with existing customers is key, but we also get new customers in too. To be able to take a part from a customer and produce it in front of him, we need to upgrade our facilities for that seamless flow of information,” explained Matthew Wood, Amada’s technical consultant manager.

“Rather than using memory sticks for the customer files, as part of the revamp all the machines will be connected wirelessly to a central server, for example, for a smooth flow of information. In the new presentation rooms, we will be able to talk them through the machinery in more detail and show them better videos. We will update the show room, particularly as more people want bespoke demonstrations nowadays, bringing a part drawing that they want made in the showroom, particularly if they are buying a machine on the back of a contract for that sheet metal part.....”

“We are looking at an initial £5 million investment with 20% more space for machines as well as more and bigger machines with more automation – all geared around the customer,” added Chris Williams, Amada’s press brake consultant.

## The year in focus

Amada’s first business term this year has just finished and was very productive, it told ISMR. Wood expects a further increase in the current term and felt that the year is on track for a similar growth level to last year.

“It is difficult to project because the simple addition of automation and a few machines to a customer order could add £1 or £2 million to an order’s value,” explained Williams. “The first six months of this year have been good and there seems to be more interest in re-investing in new machinery from customers.....”
