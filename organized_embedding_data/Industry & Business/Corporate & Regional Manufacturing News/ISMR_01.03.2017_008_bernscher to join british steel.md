# Bernscher to join British Steel

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

BRITISH STEEL HAS announced the appointment of Peter Bernscher as the company's new Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Peter, who will officially take up the position on 2 May 2017, has 30 years of experience in the steel industry, having held numerous board and director positions with Voestalpine.

Peter, who is 48 years old, has a wide and varied knowledge of the steel sector. His most recent post is as Executive Board Member of Voestalpine's Metal Forming Division. Previous positions in the company include a CEO role in the Dutch Voestalpine Polynorm Group and a Managing Director position in the Steel Division's International Service Centre Organisation. He spent several years as Executive Board Member of Metalservice Group – a Voestalpine joint venture in Italy. Peter obtained a Master's in Business Administration (MBA) in 2006.

When Peter joins British Steel, Roland Junck will continue in his current role as Executive Chairman until the end of 2017. They will work in tandem during this period of handover, after which Roland will take up the position of Non-Executive Chairman and continue to provide his experience and strategic support to the business.

British Steel recently announced that it ended Q3 2016 in profit following its first seven months of independence and is already executing the next stage of its turnaround plan. The company has committed over £39m in capital expenditure this financial year and taken on over 350 new employees since its launch.■

## New CEO for LIMO

ON 1 FEBRUARY 2017, Dr. Guido Bonati took over the reins at LIMO Lissotschenko Mikrooptik GmbH as the company's new CEO. Dr. Paul Harten and Dr. Chung-En Zah will continue to serve as managing directors.

Dr. Bonati has over 20 years of extensive experience in the laser industry. After serving for many years as a managing director of several Jenoptik Group companies, he most recently held the position of Director, Business Development & Product Line Management and was a Member of the Executive Board at Coherent GmbH in Göttingen, Germany, a subsidiary of the US laser manufacturer that goes by the same name.

Dr. Guido Bonati is the new CEO of LIMO (Lissotschenko Mikrooptik GmbH)

Bonati has a doctorate in engineering and, in addition to over 16 years of management experience in product and corporate development, is also a technology expert and authority on international markets for lasers and optics, with a strong customer- and market-oriented focus.

2017 marks a 25th anniversary year for LIMO. The company ranks among the world's leading specialists for laser beam shaping. See www.limo.de ■

## Paul Warndorf retires from AMT

PAUL WARNDORF, FORMERLY AMT Vice President -Manufacturing Technology and more recently Vice President - Strategic Planning, has retired after 20 years with AMT (Association of Manufacturing Technologies in the US).

Paul joined AMT in 1996 and became Vice President - Manufacturing Technology in 2003. He led in the development of AMT's Technology Roadmap for the Machine Tool Industry, benchmarking the future performance needs of equipment. He also led the start-up of the Smart Machine Platform Initiative aimed at developing technologies to meet those needs.

In 2007, he managed the creation of the MTConnect open standard, which allows collection of shop-floor data and communication between equipment and devices. He formed the MTConnect Institute for AMT to carry forward the further development of the standard and to meet ongoing data collection requirements.

Before joining AMT, he held leadership positions at General Research Corporation and the Institute of Advanced Manufacturing Sciences in Cincinnati, Ohio. He began his manufacturing career at the age of 18 as an electrical apprentice at Cincinnati Milacron. He graduated from the University of Cincinnati with a degree in electrical engineering, and also received an MBA from Xavier University.

Tim Shinbara, Paul's successor as AMT Vice President - Manufacturing Technology, noted that "he was an engineer with an MBA before it was cool, he understood connectivity to be the issue while folks only thought connectivity regarded the length of an AC cord."■
