# Fontijne Grotnes Group expands in Korea

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

THE Fontijne Grotnes Group has strengthened its international presence by opening a Korean sales and service office in the Changwon district in Korea. With this office, it has strengthened its position in the Korean market and improved flexibility for its service activities in the country.

Fontijne Grotnes Korea serves the wheel industry (design and manufacture of steel wheel production lines for the automotive industry) and the tyre and wheel industry (tyre and wheel assembly lines). It also provides canning and spinning equipment for tubular construction; carries out process development and prototyping of spinformed hollow tubes shapes and assemblies and serves the pipe industry (pipe-ends and full length pipe expanders). The Group also supplies sizing and forming equipment for metalforming applications in the domestic appliance, aerospace, automotive and forging sectors.

Fontijne Grotnes is headquartered in the Netherlands with a sales and manufacturing office. The company has two sales and manufacturing offices in the USA (Michigan and Illinois) as well as sales and service offices in China, Russia, India, Japan and Korea with a total staff of around 150 employees.
