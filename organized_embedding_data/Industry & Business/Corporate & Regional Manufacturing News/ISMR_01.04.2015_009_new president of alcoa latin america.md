# New President of Alcoa Latin America

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

LIGHTWEIGHT METALS SPECIALIST, Alcoa, has confirmed that José Aurélio Drummond joined the company as President of Latin America on 9 March 2015. Mr. Drummond (50) will lead Alcoa’s Latin America region, supporting the company’s transformation strategy. Mr. Drummond currently serves on Alcoa’s Brazil Advisory Board and previously held global leadership roles at Whirlpool Corporation for 14 years. He will be based in São Paulo, Brazil.

“José brings significant knowledge of Alcoa and experience managing and growing global operations, making him uniquely qualified to lead our Latin America region where we are building our value-add businesses,” said Alcoa Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Klaus Kleinfeld.

Mr. Drummond holds a Bachelor’s degree in industrial and metallurgical engineering from Faculdade de Engenharia Industrial in São Paulo and completed the Executive Development Programme at the Wharton School of Business at the University of Pennsylvania. ■
