# European contract win for British Steel

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

BRITISH STEEL CELEBRATED the tenth anniversary of rail production at its Scunthorpe plant in December 2016 with the news that it has secured another contract in Europe.

On 7 December 2006, the company replaced the old rail facility in Workington with a new one in Scunthorpe. It has since rolled more than 1.5 million tonnes of rail – enough to lay track between London and Delhi. The landmark has been made even more significant by the announcement that the Finnish Transport Agency (FTA) has chosen to use rails made by British Steel for the sixth consecutive year.

Peter Smith, British Steel's Managing Director Rail, said: "This is an important contract for us in a very competitive market place. The full contract will be split 50/50 between British Steel and another supplier, with our half of the contract seeing 5,500 tonnes of steel being delivered by April 2017. This will be produced at our Scunthorpe Rail and Section Mill, which is now 10 years old.

The first deliveries will be made to the FTA over the coming weeks."

The steel will be used for the agency's annual maintenance work, which will see the British Steel rails used across multiple sites.

"As we look forward, we continue to see a great future in rail. It's exciting that the UK Government is talking about an industrial strategy and improving transport infrastructure, particularly in the north of the country with projects like HS2, for which we expect to be a significant supplier," added Peter Smith.■
