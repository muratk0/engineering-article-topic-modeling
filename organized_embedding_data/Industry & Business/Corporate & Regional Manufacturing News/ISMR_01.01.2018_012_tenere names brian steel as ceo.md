# Tenere names Brian Steel as CEO

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

TENERE INC., a provider of complex metal and plastic components and assemblies for Fortune 500 companies, has named Brian Steel as its new CEO. Steel most recently served as VP, Global Sales and Marketing, Tenere.

Before joining Tenere, Steel was VP, Global Sales, at PCH International and previously served in sales leadership positions at supply chain services provider, Zomax, and at outsourced hosting and technical services vendor, Agiliti.

"We're thrilled to have Brian at the helm at Tenere," stated Robert Ackerman, Partner, Watermill Group (the private equity owner of Tenere). "With the company's expected global growth following its recent expansion in Mexico, the opportunities for Tenere are tremendous. Brian's deep experience with growth acceleration and strategy implementation, along with his strong customer focus, will help keep Tenere on a strong growth trajectory."
