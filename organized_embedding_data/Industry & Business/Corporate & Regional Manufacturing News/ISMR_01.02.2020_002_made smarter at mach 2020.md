# Made Smarter at MACH 2020

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

Donna Edwards, director of the North West Made Smarter pilot

THE MACH 2020 exhibition, which will take place from 20-24 April 2020 at the NEC in Birmingham (UK), has confirmed that the Made Smarter programme will be exhibiting at the UK's national manufacturing showcase. Its stand (446 in Hall 6) at the exhibition aims to help UK SMEs start their digital journey. The biennial exhibition attracts more than 25,000 visitors and over 600 exhibitors.

"The Made Smarter North West pilot is already working with UK SMEs to help them introduce digital tools and technologies to boost productivity and growth. So far, 300 North Western small and medium-sized manufacturers have secured support, including specialised advice and £1.6m of funding, in the first year of the programme. The North West pilot is being overseen by the Made Smarter Commission – a partnership between the UK Department for Business, Energy & Industrial Strategy (BEIS) and industry leaders. The programme aims to kick start a new industrial revolution for the manufacturing sector and turn the country into a world leader in digital technologies. These technologies can help close the productivity gap in the UK and cost-effectively boost production," commented the UK's Manufacturing Technologies Association (MTA), which organises MACH.

The Made Smarter team is looking to implement what it has learned in the North West Pilot to help roll the scheme out to other areas and regions of the UK. Made Smarter is bringing this support to MACH 2020 to reach more makers and signpost them towards taking their first steps into Industry 4.0.

James Selka, DL, CEO of the MTA, said "We are delighted to welcome Made Smarter to MACH 2020. We have been a huge supporter of the initial review and subsequent launch of the North West Pilot. Our members and exhibitors create the technology that will power this new industrial revolution. We know the benefits that SMEs across the UK will get from adopting cutting-edge digital technology to help them boost productivity and take manufacturing forward in this country."

Donna Edwards, director of the North West Made Smarter pilot, added: "In its first year, Made Smarter has helped 300 SME manufacturers and we are keen to build on that success. MACH 2020 provides the perfect opportunity to showcase these achievements and encourage other SMEs to start their digital journey. Our specialist advisers can talk through your business challenge, help you understand the opportunities and talk through the next steps."
