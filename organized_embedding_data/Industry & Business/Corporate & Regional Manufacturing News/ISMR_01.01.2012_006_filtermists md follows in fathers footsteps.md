# Filtermist’s MD follows in father’s footsteps

[TARİH: 01.01.2012 ISMR]

FOLLOWING MANAGING director Mike Stansfield’s retirement at the end of June, oil mist extractor manufacturer, Filtermist International, has reported that new MD, James Stansfield, has presided over a successful first month in office.

“Export sales have continued to grow despite difficult global trading conditions and Filtermist’s UK business, which includes sales of Absolent oil smoke extraction products, is also prospering,” it told ISMR.

James began his career at Filtermist 14 years ago, starting as sales co-ordinator in 1998 and working his way up through the organisation to become the sales and marketing manager in 2010. James has extensive knowledge of all aspects of Filtermist and has played an integral role in the continued expansion of the company - specifically in its international export strategy.

James Stansfield said, “I am extremely proud to be able to take the helm at Filtermist to ensure its expansion continues. We are developing a number of products and services to ensure our UK clients comply with COSHH legislation as well as oil mist extractors and accessories specifically designed for the export market.”
