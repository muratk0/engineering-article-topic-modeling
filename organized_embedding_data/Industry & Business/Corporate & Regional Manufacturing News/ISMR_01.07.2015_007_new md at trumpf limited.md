# New MD at TRUMPF Limited

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

EFFECTIVE FROM 1 July 2015, Annette Doyle will take on the role of managing director of TRUMPF Limited (UK). She succeeds Scott Simpson who will leave TRUMPF after more than 20 years to pursue his own individual projects.

Annette Doyle comes from TRUMPF Inc. in Farmington, US where she served as head of its customer training centre as well as assembly department manager for TRUMPF’s TruLaser machines. In her role at TRUMPF Inc., Doyle has been recognised by The Manufacturing Institute, Deloitte, University of Phoenix and the Society of Manufacturing Engineers with a Woman in Manufacturing STEP (Science, Technology, Engineering and Production) Award for excellence and leadership in manufacturing.

Doyle holds a Bachelor degree in Business Administration and Mechanical Engineering as well as a Master of Science in Technology Management. In her new role as managing director, she will be responsible for all TRUMPF business operations in the United Kingdom including product sales, customer service, technical training, product sales and applications.

## Management change at ROFIN

ROFIN-SINAR TECHNOLOGIES INC., one of the world's leading developers and manufacturers of high-performance laser beam sources and laser-based solutions, has confirmed that Thomas Merk, Chief Operating Officer, ROFIN Laser Micro and Marking Group, succeeded Günther Braun as President and Chief Executive Officer of the company from 1 July 2015.

Mr. Braun will also, at that time, resign as a director of the company and Mr. Merk will assume the vacant position and become a member of the company’s Board of Directors. Mr. Braun will remain with the company for a period of time to assist with the transition of duties.

“I am excited to lead ROFIN-SINAR in its next chapter of growth and development,” said Thomas Merk. “As the company with one of the greatest customer reaches in the industry and a strong product portfolio that includes some of the most advanced fibre laser technology as well as a wide ultra-short-pulse laser portfolio in the market, there is significant opportunity ahead to drive growth and further margin improvement. I am looking forward to building on Günther Braun’s leadership, solidifying our strong market position and continuing to create long-term shareholder value.”

## Sheet metal tooling sales

MATE PRECISION TOOLING, a leading global manufacturer of sheet metal fabrication tooling, has hired sales veteran Kevin Jordan as vice president of North American sales. ## Asian sales appointment for OMAX®

OMAX CORPORATION a global specialist in the design and manufacture of advanced abrasive waterjet cutting technology, recently named William Wei as its new sales manager for Asia. Wei will support the company’s distribution partners and serve customers throughout China and Taiwan.

Wei has over 20 years of experience in international sales and marketing as well as product marketing and business development for the electronic manufacturing and plumbing hardware industries. Most recently, he worked for Delta Faucet Company/Masco Corporation as the sales and marketing director for China. He holds a Bachelor of Science in electrical engineering from Northern Jiaotong University and earned his Master’s degree in business administration from Illinois State University.

## Lifetime achievement award for Nardocci

TADEU NARDOCCI, Senior Vice President, Novelis Inc. and President, Novelis South America, was honoured recently with the Lifetime Achievement Award, for his contributions and dedication to advancing the aluminium industry globally over the past three decades, at the Platts 2015 Global Metals Awards ceremony in London (UK).

His commitment to advancing the industry was exemplified in his role as Chairman of the European Aluminium Association (EAA) Board in 2011 and 2012 and Vice President of the Brazilian Aluminium Association (ABAL) in 2008, providing strategic direction for the industry on two continents.

Novelis was also honoured as a finalist for the Recycling Leadership Award for its recycling programme, the Breakthrough Innovation of the Year for its automotive sheet finishing lines in Oswego, New York, USA and Changzhou, China and the Corporate Social Responsibility Award.
