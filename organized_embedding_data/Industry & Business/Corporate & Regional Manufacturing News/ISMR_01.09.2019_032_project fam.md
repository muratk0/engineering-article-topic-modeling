# PROJECT / FAM

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

Project Name: FAM Head Office, Magdeburg, Germany
Façade Material: ALUCOBOND® A2 Sunrise Silver Metallic, Sky Blue
Construction Type: Riveted/Screwed
Planning (Architect): pbr Planungsbüro Rohling AG, Stuttgart, Germany
Fabricator/Installer: WF Fassadenbau GmbH, Barleben, Germany
Year of construction: 2016
Copyright pictures: Christian Bierwagen, Fotodesign

An example of futuristic curvature. Glass, silver cladding and a multitude of curves determine the futuristic appearance of the Magdeburg Förderanlagen und Baumaschinen GmbH (FAM) corporate head office. It reflects FAM’s values and corporate identity on many levels.

"The futuristic new building in the south of Magdeburg not only communicates quality and the power of innovation, it also makes an architectural statement for industrial buildings. This modern structure, featuring a large number of curves, was conceived as an architectural counterpoint to the historical company villa," commented the architects.

Large-format, silver-grey ALUCOBOND® panels determine the façade design and, in conjunction with the building contours, create a futuristic image. Deliberately employing large format panels, in conjunction with a curved geometry, has simply and deftly created the modern appearance of the structure. Ribbon windows structure and divide the new fivestorey mullion-transom facade construction. FAM's corporate colour is featured in the blue window reveals and the entrance area.

Attached to the foyer, a light-bathed atrium links all five storeys and creates a luminous ambience at the centre of the new building. A lift, designed and built by FAM, is housed within a steel structure (also in the blue corporate colour). The glass roof allows daylight to flood in from above and reach the ground floor, creating a bright atmosphere in the centre of the new building.

## PROJECT/ Peters Ice Cream

Project Name: Peters Ice Cream
Location: 254-294 Wellington Road Mulgrave VIC 3170, Australia
Façade Material: ALUCOBOND® PLUS Anthracite Grey + Dark Grey Metallic
Construction Type: Fixed Cassette
Planning (Architect): Gray Puksand Pty Ltd
Fabricator/Installer: Alclad
Year of construction: 2016
Copyright pictures: Peters Ice Cream

Butterfly roof with a bullnose edge. The new head office for Peters Ice Cream by Gray Puksand, in Melbourne, makes the most of the existing landscape and takes additional inspiration from its natural surroundings. Slender steel columns imitate gum trees to create the illusion of a two-storey structure that lightly connects the ground.

The project demonstrates a different office typology by the way it is positioned into the natural terrain. The most striking feature is a butterfly-shaped roof made of wide ALUCOBOND® eaves that floats over the car park and forms a bullnose edge.

"By using the existing topography, we lifted up the building to create a dramatic entry with the tilted butterfly roof and a car park below the offices," said Kelly Wellington, an associate at Gray Puksand.

On the south side, angular pre-cast panels maintain thermal comfort through passive design. The building was also designed to achieve a minimum 4.5-star NABERS Energy Rating.

"It is a real opportunity for us to work with this company," Grégory Conraud, President of AMB Picot, told ISMR.

"We put one of our machines at its disposal so that it can do trials with different type of panels and organise demonstrations for its customers. In return, it feeds our material database with rolling data from actual situations, which helps us to improve our easyroll software (Artificial Intelligence for roll-bending machines). It really is a win-win partnership for us both. Our exchanges are cordial and fruitful and our joint project is moving along quickly."

3A Composites will take delivery of a new 3m roll bending machine from AMB Picot, equipped with a cassette, at the end of this year. This, it believes, will give its clients further bending and folding flexibility for facade fabrication.

"Our aim is to improve our technology to simplify our customers' business," added Conraud. "We are working on the 'rolling' algorithms to simplify the use of the numerical command; employ high elastic limit materials (Hardox); use composite materials such as ALUCOBOND®; increase productivity by more autonomous management of the laser scanning area and offer automatic restart of programs when leaving the zone."

To see a demonstration of easyroll technology, meet AMB PICOT at this year's Blechexpo in Stuttgart, Germany (5-8 November 2019) in Hall 1, Stand 1706-2 and on stand A5723 in the South Hall of McCormick Place at FABTECH in Chicago, USA (11-14 November 2019). ■
