# LANTEK opens new branch in Holland

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

LANTEK, Spanish specialist in the development and marketing of software solutions for the sheet metal and structural steel industry, has just opened a new branch office in Holland which will cover the needs of more than 600 customers for the country and its neighbours, Belgium and Luxembourg.

The EMEA region is one of Lantek’s main markets. The Dutch branch joins the eight branches that the company already has in the European, African and Middle Eastern regions, where it has offices in France, United Kingdom, Poland, Germany, Italy, Spain and Turkey.

Lantek was founded in 1986 in the Basque Country and has head offices in Vitoria (Alava). The company currently has over 13,000 customers in over 100 countries and offices in 15 countries, in addition to an extensive network of distributors across the world. See www.lanteksms.com

## Flexform press for aerospace applications

SOME 50 visitors representing aerospace manufacturers from 15 countries met at Avure’s European headquarters in Västerås, Sweden, to participate in an Avure Open House event. Among the highlights of the one-day event was the launch of a new high-pressure Flexform™ sheet metal forming press, practical forming demonstrations and a technical seminar involving process advantages. Visitors were also given tours of the extensive manufacturing facility, the shop floor, research and testing operations in the high-pressure lab as well as a demonstration of Avure’s QUINTUS® wire-winding system for strengthening pressure vessels.

“The workshop tour included the presentation and launch of the Flexform press, type QFC 0.7 x 1.8-800, a new cost-efficient fluid cell press. The new press is designed to meet the needs of mainly lower-tier suppliers, which are today fabricating most of the sheet metal components for the aerospace industry,” the company told ISMR. A production unit was on display and visitors witnessed a demonstration of the complete forming operation, yielding multiple parts in a single one-minute cycle. The unit, the first in production of several already firm orders, is on schedule to be delivered to Kale Aero in Turkey.

“Features include a capacity of up to one million parts per year, a fast, 60-second processing cycle, low running costs, a spacesaving small footprint and easy installation directly at floor level with no foundation or pit required,” added the manufacturer.
