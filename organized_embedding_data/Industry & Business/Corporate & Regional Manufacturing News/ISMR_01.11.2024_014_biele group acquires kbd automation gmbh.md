# Biele Group acquires KBD Automation GmbH

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Biele Group, a manufacturer of turnkey projects for automated production plants based in Spain, has acquired a majority stake in KBD Automation GmbH, based in Cologne (Germany). KBD Automation's activity focuses on the development of specialised engineering projects in packaging lines.

Biele Group consists of five companies: Biele (Azpeitia, Gipuzkoa); Marrodán y Rezola (Navarrete, La Rioja); Biele Digital (San Sebastián, Gipuzkoa); Biele USA (Atlanta, Georgia) and Biele China (Kunshan, Jiangsu). It employs more than 350 people.

The operation, for which Biele Group has had the legal and financial advice of Agem Consultores Russell Bedford, is part of the strategic plan of the Gipuzkoan firm with which it intends to strengthen its position in Central Europe. The integration of KBD Automation into Biele Group complements the technical structure of the group and will allow it to provide services more closely to customers in Central Europe, while also accessing potential suppliers based in that region.

"The Biele Group portfolio on metal packing lines (including plate and quality control packing lines, coil packing lines, slit packing lines and stack packing lines) will be now expanded with new technological solutions," confirmed the Group.■
