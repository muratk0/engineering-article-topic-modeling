# New President for ECTA

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

For the next four years, ECTA (the European Cutting Tools Association) will be led by an Italian President, Federico Costa, who is also the managing director of FEBAMETAL. The Turin company specialises in the production and sales of standard and special tools. Costa was elected in November 2023.

He highlighted industry challenges such as international geopolitical uncertainty and “the complexity of new EU regulatory requirements".

Costa added that ECTA's focus would be firmly on its annual event and meetings to 'provide companies with food for thought and insight, as well as opportunities for exchanging views."

"As President of ECTA, I will work to improve the image of the European industry in the sector by proposing initiatives that can also help to durably strengthen its international positioning. After participating in the next World Conference on Cutting Tools, in Osaka this year, the association (supported by UCIMU) will organise the ECTA Conference to be held in Italy in 2025," he concluded.
