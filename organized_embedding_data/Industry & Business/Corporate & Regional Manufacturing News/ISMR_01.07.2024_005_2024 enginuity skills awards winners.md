# 2024 Enginuity skills awards winners

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

Wednesday 5th June saw leaders from across the UK's engineering and manufacturing sector gather at the 10th Annual Enginuity Skills Awards to celebrate UK engineering and manufacturing talent and the organisations championing skills development in the UK.

Run by Enginuity, the charity dedicated to closing skills gaps in UK engineering and manufacturing, the ceremony was hosted in Birmingham by Honorary Fellow of the Royal Academy of Engineering and award-winning broadcaster, Steph McGovern, at the Hilton Birmingham Metropole next to the National Exhibition Centre (NEC) during ‘Smart Manufacturing and Engineering Week'. The ceremony was attended by West Midlands Deputy Lieutenant, Professor Sir Nigel Thrift.

Jamie Clarke, a maintenance apprentice at LISI Aerospace Rugby, picked up not only the 'Advanced Level Apprentice of the Year' trophy but also the biggest prize of the evening, the 'Best of British Engineering Award’. He has overcome challenges such as dyslexia and health issues to pursue his dream of becoming an engineer.

Yusuf Mumtaz, Jaguar Land Rover, won the 'Graduate, Degree or Higher-Level Apprentice of the Year' award (sponsored by Teesside University). The 'T-Level Student of the Year' award, sponsored by BAE Systems, went to Elliot Barton of Barnsley College. Siemens' Virtual Work Experience Programme won the 'Equality, Diversity & Inclusion Award' (sponsored by Rolls-Royce).

The Siemens' programme addresses under-representation by engaging a diverse range of students, including females, those from diverse socio-economic backgrounds, individuals with special educational needs or disabilities, and carers.

Gloucestershire Engineering Training, who successfully addressed a skills gap in the Forest of Dean by opening a training centre in September 2023, won the 'Training Partner Skills Champion of the Year' award (sponsored by EAL). Brandauer was awarded the 'SME Employer Skills Champion of the Year' award (sponsored by Innovate UK). Brandauer launched the UK's first Precision Tooling Academy to ensure the transfer of essential skills from an ageing workforce to a younger generation. It has invested £1 million to create a commercial toolroom and reshaped its apprenticeship approach, contributing to business growth and increased turnover.

The 'Large Employer Skills Champion of the Year'—sponsored by FANUC—went to KMF Precision Sheet Metal Limited for its collaborative efforts, work experience programmes and apprenticeship offerings. In 2010, KMF set up a ‘Young Engineer of the Year’ programme working with Year 10 students across all the high schools in Stoke-on-Trent, UK. Over the past 14 years, it has worked with more than 25,000 young people while investing over £1 million into STEM projects and equipment for schools.

Other supporting partners of the awards include EDT; The Manufacturer; Siemens plc; KMF; Manufacturing Technology Centre and Kontent.ai.■
