# £140k funding boost for ‘Factory of the Future’

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

The Heart of the South-West Local Enterprise Partnership (LEP) in the UK has announced £144,500 to support high-value engineering 'Factory of the Future' digitisation for SMEs. The funding will allow the iAero Centre at Yeovil to install an Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre (AMRC) Factory+ Demonstrator and equipment, to create a dedicated digital data demonstration space. The project will focus on harnessing the power of data to drive innovation and collaboration in the aerospace and advanced manufacturing sectors.

iFACTORe will allow companies in the industry to collect and analyse vast amounts of data, with the potential to enhance efficiency and productivity whilst also fostering innovation and competitiveness.

Karl Tucker, Chair of the Heart of the South West LEP, said: "This sector is vital to the region's growth, so funding for projects like these is pivotal. iFACTORe at the iAero Centre signifies the importance of the aerospace and advanced manufacturing industry in the region."

Austin Chick, head of iAero Centre, added: "This funding offers complementary capabilities at iAero that will benefit SMEs in the aerospace and high-value manufacturing sector looking to collaborate on new projects, technologies and skills."

The Heart of the South-West Local Enterprise Partnership (covering Devon, Plymouth, Somerset and Torbay) is one of 38 LEPs in England. It is a business-led partnership between the private sector, local authorities, universities and colleges.■
