## Lantek appoints new CCO

AS PART of the implementation of its Strategic Plan 2016-2018 and effective from 4 April 2016, sheet metal software specialist, Lantek, has appointed Juan José Colas to the role of chief commercial officer. Juan José will take a seat on the Board of Directors and will be responsible for global commercial strategy and business development for the corporation. He will have direct responsibility, and development plans, for two key markets: the United Kingdom and France.

Juan Jose Colas, Lantek

"Juan José has successfully managed operations in several countries over the last few years. He takes this position at a time when Lantek is well poised for growth and the team worldwide is fully geared to leverage new market opportunities and contribute to the growth of software solutions for the sheet metal industry," explained Lantek CEO, Alberto Martínez.

## Getting a grip

SCHUNK, a specialist in gripping and clamping systems, has welcomed a new area sales manager to its expanding UK team. Steven Betts, who has a strong engineering background with extensive manufacturing expertise, will be covering the East Midlands area.

Schunk's new area sales manager, Steven Betts

Martin Kent, general manager of Schunk Intec Ltd, commented: "I am delighted to introduce Steven as our latest addition to the team. Steven joined us the week of the MACH 2016 exhibition and his timely arrival gave him the opportunity to meet the rest of the Schunk team and, more importantly, customers from his region.

“Customer service is a key focus point for Schunk and I believe our new area sales manager, with his deep industry knowledge, will be able to service the wide variety of requests we receive from our customers in the post-MACH period and beyond."

## A true entrepreneur

PAUL EBERHARD SCHALL, the founder of trade fair organiser P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG (which runs, amongst others, trade fairs such as Blechexpo, Fakuma, Control and Motek) passed away on 19 February 2016, shortly before his 77th birthday.

The company will be continued by the trade fair team under the direction of Mr Schall's wife and sole managing shareholder, Bettina Schall.

"He was always driven by unbridled curiosity, esprit, pragmatism and the joy of life - even when the wind was sharp," said the Schall Group.

## Leadership moves at Thalmann

AS OF 1 May 2016, CEO Marco Cappello and Martin Thalmann have officially taken charge of Swiss folding manufacturer, Thalmann Maschinenbau AG.

Martin Thalmann is the son of outgoing CEO, Peter Thalmann. An electrical engineer and technical salesman, Martin started his career in electro-mechanical engineering and has, since then, held a variety of management roles with a leading international manufacturer of sensors and systems. In his new role, Martin will assume responsibility for administrative oversight.

“We have always strived for continuous improvement and this is still our mission statement - to remain at the forefront of innovation and provide our customers with a sustainable, competitive advantage," said the company.

## New AISI Chairman

THE BOARD of Directors of the American Iron and Steel Institute (AISI) has elected John Ferriola, Chairman, President and CEO of Nucor Corporation, to serve as Chairman of the Institute until May 2017.

"John is a dedicated and outspoken advocate for our industry, and has engaged his employees and the supply chain to speak out on behalf of steel with their elected officials, especially on the issue of unfair trade," said Thomas J. Gibson, President and CEO, AISI.

The election took place recently at the AISI Board Meeting as part of the Institute's 124th General Meeting at the Grand America Hotel in Salt Lake City, Utah (USA).

## Young Professional of the Year

(Second from right) Jaimie Larson of Jet Edge

THE I-94 West Chamber of Commerce (USA) recently honoured Jet Edge sales and marketing assistant, Jaimie Larson, as its 2016 Young Professional of the Year.

Larson, who performs a wide range of sales and marketing duties for the waterjet systems manufacturer, was recognised for her commitment to professional growth and leadership development, as well as for her involvement in the Chamber's numerous business and community development initiatives. Larson is a co-chair of the Chamber's Ambassador programme and is very active in Chamber activities, including the Elevate for Success leadership programme. She also completed the Chamber's 2015-2016 Business Institute and is currently enrolled in its Marketing Academy.

This is the fourth time in four years that Jet Edge has been recognised by the Chamber of Commerce. Larson was named Chamber Ambassador of the Year in 2013, and Jet Edge received the Chamber's Manufacturing Excellence Award in 2012 and 2015.
