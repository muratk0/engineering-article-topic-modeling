# Andreas Hellriegel, ARKU Vice President of Business Development, outlined the company's latest new initiatives and product launches, as well as its focus on automation and Industry 4.0 for networked production.

[TARİH: 01.01.2022 ISMR]

Germany, and facilities in Cincinnati (USA) and Kunshan (China), ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH operates in nearly 30 countries. Its product range comprises precision levellers, deburring and edge rounding machines for parts; automated parts handling via robots; in-line levellers; coil-laser lines; press feeding lines and coil entry lines for roll formers.

In 2018, ARKU celebrate its 90-year anniversary. Over the last 30 years, it has grown an average of 10% per year. In the last few years, it has been voted several times among the top 50 'most innovative mid-sized companies in Germany' and one of the top 500 market leaders worldwide.

ARKU stormed into the Blechexpo event in Stuttgart (October 2021) with new deburring and precision levelling company's latest new initiatives and product launches as well as its focus on automation and Industry 4.0.
