# Strategic growth at KMF

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

KMF (PRECISION SHEET METAL) has strengthened its senior management team by appointing Jon Eeles as Finance Director. While managing financial relationships with existing partners, Jon has also been tasked with involvement in the strategic growth of what is already the UK’s largest supplier of fully integrated bespoke metal work, precision sheet metal fabrication and electrical integration, with customers from a wide range of industry sectors including retail, vending, rail, aerospace, medical and defence.

Jon became a chartered accountant while working at global accountancy firm KPMG and has developed a broad range of experience through working with businesses in the FMCG and manufacturing sectors.

“This is a fantastic opportunity to join a dynamic business with a vision that will see significant growth in the current £35 million turnover and 385 employees. The modern working practices and drive to invest in the latest manufacturing technology highlight to me the direction that KMF is determined to travel and I intend to use my experience to enhance the already very strong management team to help make the future aspirations of the business become reality,” said Jon Eeles. ■
