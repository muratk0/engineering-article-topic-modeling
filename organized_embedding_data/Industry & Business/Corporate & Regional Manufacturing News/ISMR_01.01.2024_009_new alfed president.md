# New ALFED President

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

The Aluminium Federation (ALFED) in the UK announced, on 6 December 2023, that Mechatherm's sales director, Mark Allen, will be its new President. The current presidency of Mike Dines is coming to an end. Mark Allen will join the ALFED Board, "helping to lead the line in driving ALFED forwards between now and the close of 2025." ALFED represents over 150 members across the entire supply chain.

"With over 25 years of experience in the aluminium industry and his role at Mechatherm that sees him leading a globally successful sales team into some of the biggest aluminium projects in the world, we have no doubt that Mark will be an invaluable addition to the ALFED Board," commented the Federation.

Mechatherm International Limited is a major global aluminium furnace supplier, with installations in 35 countries supporting a vast variety of applications from primary smelters, advanced recycling centres and the ancillary equipment used in aluminium casting and heat treatment processes.
