# New press hardening line

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

At EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover, Germany (22-25 October 2024), Swedish press specialist, AP&T, introduced its latest innovation, AP&T®SkyLines. It has been designed as a compact and efficient presshardening line, specially developed to handle large and complex components.

"The industry is increasingly moving towards sustainability and energy efficiency, and the new AP&T®SkyLines clearly meets the market's requirements. This new line combines precision with short cycle times, making it attractive to customers looking to improve both efficiency and quality in their production. With our broad range of services, SkyLines also provides high performance over time, which creates long-term business value for our customers," commented Dr. Christian Koroschetz, Head of Products and Marketing, AP&T.

In connection with the launch of SkyLines, AP&T also held its own seminars on its booth at EuroBLECH for the first time. The seminars, which attracted physical and digital participants, were led by AP&T specialists and included industry stalwarts from companies such as ArcelorMittal, GEDIA and fischer Group. Together, they shared insights on press hardening, hot forming of high-strength aluminium and various sustainable solutions for the future.

"These seminars offered a valuable platform to discuss forthcoming production challenges and technological advances in sustainability and energy efficiency," explained AP&T.

“We couldn't be more pleased about the positive response we received and all the new contacts we made. Our focus on sustainable production and quality solutions is really in line with the needs of the industry," concluded Dr. Christian Koroschetz.

For the first time, AP&T held seminars on its booth at EuroBLECH. The seminars were also broadcast online.

AP&T celebrates sixty years in operation this year. Tool manufacturer, VIBAB, was founded in Blidsberg in 1964. Lagan Press began to manufacture presses in Lagan the same year, and Tranemo Hydraulmaskiner was formed in 1970. In 1989, these three companies merged under the name AP&T (Automation, Presses & Tooling) and set their sights on the international market.
