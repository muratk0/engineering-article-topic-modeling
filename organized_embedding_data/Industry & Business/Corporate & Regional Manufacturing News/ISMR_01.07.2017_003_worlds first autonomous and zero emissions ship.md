# 'World's first' autonomous and zero emissions ship

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

Svein Tore Holsether, President and CEO, YARA

THE VESSEL "YARA Birkeland" will be the 'world's first fully electric and autonomous container ship, with zero emissions' according to YARA and Kongsberg who are partnering on the project. Operation is planned to start in the latter half of 2018, shipping products from YARA's Porsgrunn production plant to Brevik and Larvik in Norway.

YARA will reduce diesel-powered truck haulage by an estimated 40,000 journeys per year. Kongsberg will integrate sensor, control, communication and electrical systems.

"Named YARA Birkeland after YARA's founder, the famous scientist and innovator Kristian Birkeland, the vessel will be the world's first fully electric container feeder. The new vessel will reduce NOx and CO2 emissions and improve road safety by removing up to 40,000 truck journeys in populated urban areas," explained YARA.

YARA Birkeland will initially operate as a manned vessel, moving to remote operation in 2019 and is expected to be capable of performing fully autonomous operations from 2020. The new zero-emission vessel is expected to be a game-changer for global maritime transport contributing to meet the UN sustainability goals.

"As a leading global fertilizer company with a mission to feed the world and protect the planet, investing in this zero-emission vessel to transport our crop nutrition solutions fits our strategy well. We are proud to work with Kongsberg to build the world's first autonomous, all-electric vessel to enter commercial operation," said Svein Tore Holsether, President and CEO of YARA.

"Every day, more than 100 diesel truck journeys are needed to transport products from YARA's Porsgrunn plant to ports in Brevik and Larvik where we ship products to customers around the world. With this new autonomous battery-driven container vessel, we move transport from road to sea and thereby reduce noise and dust emissions, improve the safety of local roads and reduce NOx and CO₂ emissions," he added.

Kongsberg is responsible for development and delivery of all key enabling technologies on YARA Birkeland including the sensors and integration required for remote and autonomous operations, in addition to the electric drive, battery and propulsion control systems.
