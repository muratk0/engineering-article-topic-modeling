# Renault-Nissan Alliance invests in Argentina

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

The Renault-Nissan Alliance is preparing to launch its first dedicated pickup truck manufacturing line to produce vehicles for Nissan, Renault and Alliance partner, Daimler. The new line represents an investment of US$ 600 million. It will also mark the first time that Nissan will build vehicles in Argentina.

Renault Argentina also announced it will invest another US$ 100 million in a new model to be produced at the Santa Isabel plant in Córdoba. This latest investment follows a US$ 100 million investment announced in March 2015 for the production of Renault Sandero, Sandero Stepway and Logan models at the plant.

"The Renault-Nissan Alliance is deeply committed to Argentina and the local partners who are joining these ambitious projects," Carlos Ghosn, Chairman and CEO of the Renault-Nissan Alliance, said. "As the third-largest automotive market and the centre of pickup manufacturing in Latin America, Argentina is key to the regional growth plans of our brands."

The Renault-Nissan Alliance is a strategic partnership between Paris-based Renault and Yokohama-based Nissan. The companies, which have been strategic partners since 1999, sold 8.5 million cars in nearly 200 countries in 2015. The Alliance also operates strategic collaborations with other automakers, including Germany's Daimler, China's Dongfeng and Japan's Mitsubishi Motors. The Alliance has a majority stake in the joint venture that owns Russian automaker, AVTOVAZ.
