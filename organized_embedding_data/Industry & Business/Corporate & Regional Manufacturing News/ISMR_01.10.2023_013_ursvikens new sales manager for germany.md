# Ursviken's new sales manager for Germany

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Swedish sheet-metal bending specialist Ursviken has appointed Henrik Häseler as sales manager responsible for the German market. He has over 20 years of sales experience in the sheet-metal processing industry and direct experience with sales of punching machines, laser cutting systems and press brakes as well as tooling for punching machines and press brakes.

"At Ursviken, I am looking forward to continuing my collaborative, fair and solution-oriented customer advice and support. Together with my Swedish colleagues, I offer customers solutions even for complicated tasks," confirmed Mr. Häseler.

“We welcome our new colleague and are looking forward to strengthening our presence in the German market," concluded Ursviken.

With over 135 years of industry experience, Ursviken Technology AB is a leading manufacturer of CNC press brakes, shears, hydraulic presses and straightening presses.■
