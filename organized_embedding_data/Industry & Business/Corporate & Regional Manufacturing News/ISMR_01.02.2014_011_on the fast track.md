# ON THE FAST TRACK

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

Constellium will invest up to €200 million to add significant body-in-white production capacity in Europe and has created a JV in North America for aluminium sheet

ISMR SAYS:

Constellium N.V. plans to invest up to €200 million over the next three years to further grow its European Body-in-White (BiW) business. The company currently offers a portfolio of aluminium flat rolled products to the automotive industry targeted to meet stringent requirements in terms of surface quality, strength, crash and formability for BiW applications such as hoods, doors, roofs and the structural parts of cars.

The past few years have seen a notable increase in the use of aluminium flat rolled products in premium car models in Europe.

“Constellium believes that the use of aluminium in body-in-white structures will keep growing…”

Constellium believes that this growth will continue in light of stringent European legislation on CO2 emissions and resource efficiency. The main advantages of aluminium components in automotive manufacturing are its resistance to corrosion and its low weight.

“We expect to benefit from strong market growth as the integration of aluminium components continues to expand not only in premium vehicles but in the high-volume car segment,” commented Laurent Musy, President of Constellium’s packaging and automotive rolled products business unit.

## An eye on capacity

Constellium’s investment is expected to run through to the end of 2016. In Phase 1, Constellium plans to increase production capacity at Neuf-Brisach (France) and to start BiW production at Singen (Germany) by revamping its continuous annealing line. By 2016, Constellium expects to add up to 40,000 metric tons to its current capacity with the first BiW coils produced in Singen as early as mid-2014.

In Phase 2, it plans to add a new continuous annealing and conversion line in Europe with a targeted capacity of 100,000 metric tons, with the aim to start commissioning in the second half of 2016.

In light of increasing emissions regulations and consumer demand, automotive OEMs are exploring ways to lower CO2 emissions and reduce fuel consumption. Constellium is helping automotive manufacturers to respond using intelligent material lightweighting in areas including body-in-white, heat exchangers, equipment, chassis and other sheets. It has a large portfolio of high quality products such as aluminium extrusions, tubes, bars and sheets in a wide range of automotive alloys.

## FOCUS ON MATERIALS

Laurent Musy, President of Constellium’s packaging and automotive rolled products business unit

The lightness and strength-weight ratio of aluminium make it the material of choice for lightweighting in automotive applications. This can translate into lower fuel consumption and fewer emissions over the duration of a vehicle’s lifetime.

Constellium’s solar surface self-clean panels

“We provide a wide range of body-in-white aluminium sheets, form sections and coil solutions for automotive body-in-white (interior and exterior parts and panels). Our high-strength alloys have strong surface quality. They also incorporate downgauging possibilities, excellent strength-to-weight ratio, formability properties and full recycling compatibility,” the company told ISMR.

Side-impact beams are designed to preserve the integrity of the passenger compartment on collision, and using aluminium for these parts provides significant weight reductions. Constellium supplies aluminium side impact beams that meet with all structural requirements.

Automotive manufacturers install Constellium’s other aluminium solutions, such as front-end structures and lower path energy absorption systems, to satisfy structural requirements and enhance crash performance.

In recent years, the company has heavily invested in upgrading its production unit at Neuf-Brisach (France) to supply the European market. Its Voreppe research centre in France covers the entire automotive value chain. The centre provides a specialist knowledge of aluminium applications and experience acquired through collaborative projects in highly innovative sectors such as aerospace. It has also significantly invested in surface inspection and chemical conversion when upgrading production facilities.

## US joint venture

Last month, Constellium and UACJ Corporation announced a plan to create a Joint Venture in the United States to produce aluminium body-in-white aluminium sheet for the North American automotive industry.

To achieve this goal, Constellium and UACJ, through Tri-Arrows Aluminum Inc. (TAA), (UACJ’s subsidiary with Sumitomo Corporation and Itochu Group), intend to create a joint venture company in the United States, as an equal partnership, to serve the North American market. The JV is expected to include a continuous heat treatment and conversion line with an initial target capacity of 100,000 metric tons supplied by cold rolled coils from both partners’ rolling mills. The planned facility is designed to allow for expansion beyond 100,000 tons. The total joint investment by both parties is expected to amount to approximately US$ 150 million.

Current Corporate Average Fuel Economy (CAFE) regulations require significant reductions in fuel consumption by vehicles. The intrinsic lightness of aluminium makes it a prime material for the automotive industry to meet these improvements.

Pierre Vareille, CEO of Constellium

“Automakers in the United States have already started to convert key models from steel to aluminium which we believe should translate into a significant increase in demand. We estimate that the US market for body-in-white aluminium rolled products could grow from less than 100,000 tons in 2012 to approximately 1 million tons in 2020,” commented Laurent Musy. “Constellium expects to benefit from this significant growth potential. UACJ is the ideal partner for us to serve US customers with high quality automotive sheets for body-in-white applications like hoods, doors, roofs and structural parts of cars and trucks.”

“Automotive is a strategic market for Constellium and we believe this US initiative will strengthen the company’s position as a global leader,” added Pierre Vareille, CEO of Constellium. “Both companies will approach this joint venture, in which Constellium will own 51% of the equity, as an equal partnership. Each partner has a crucial contribution to make through coil supply, customer relationships and technical expertise.”

Constellium and UACJ are working towards signing a definitive agreement in the first half of 2014.

## Sheet and coil

Constellium’s comprehensive range of hot- and cold-rolled aluminium coils and sheets can be customised according to customer specifications. These are available in thicknesses of up to 4mm and widths of up to 1800mm.

It works with industrial and consumer products markets to produce various aluminium applications. These include hotrolled coils that enable further rolling and downgauging operations.

With their combination of corrosion resistance, formability, welding, deep drawing and wear behaviour, its sheets and coils can be used for many complex applications including electrical cases; ladders and scaffolding; furniture; orthopedic equipment; antennae and telescopic handles.

## Sustainability prize

Constellium has been awarded the Silver Recognition Level rating by EcoVadis, a rating agency that specialises in sustainable development and ethics. EcoVadis operates a collaborative platform, allowing companies to assess the environmental and social performance of their suppliers on a global scale.

“This rating recognises our progress in Constellium’s sustainability roadmap, and reflects a high level of transparency with respect to our governance and our partnerships throughout the supply chain,” commented Laurent Musy.

Transparency is also about giving facts and evidence. In this respect, reporting to the Carbon Disclosure Project has enabled Constellium not only to make all its greenhouse gas emissions data openly available, but also to identify areas of improvement on climate change. Similarly, but on a broader range of sustainability parameters, Constellium has published its first Sustainability Report according to the Global Reporting Initiative principles, with an application level check A.

In its Sustainability Report entitled ‘Altogether More Sustainable’, Constellium has set measurable targets for governance, people, operations and products, endorsing industry targets to emphasize the need for increased cooperation throughout the aluminium value chain.

Constellium is a founder member of the Aluminium Stewardship Initiative that aims to set an environmental, social and governance standard for companies involved in the aluminium value chain, from bauxite extraction to consumer goods manufacturing.
