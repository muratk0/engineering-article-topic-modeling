# TRUMPF Expands Management

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

The TRUMPF Group is expanding its management. Dr. Lars Grünert (41) was appointed with effect from 1 January 2010 as additional Managing Director of TRUMPF GmbH + Co. KG, the management holding company of the TRUMPF Group. Its remit covers the commercial management of the Laser Technology / Electronics, the corporate functions IT and processes, and organizational development.

In addition, he is responsible, within the Group Managing Director function, for the activities of TRUMPF in the North American market. The management of the family firm is chaired by Dr. Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller from a total of seven members.

Dr. Lars Grünert is an industrial engineer. Prior to his appointment to the management holding company he has been, since 2005, commercial director of its subsidiary, TRUMPF Laser GmbH + Co. KG in Schramber. In 2008, he became CFO of SPI Lasers, Southampton (UK). He was previously responsible for controlling the TRUMPF Werkzeugmaschinen GmbH + Co. KG in Ditzingen. From 1993 to 2002, he worked as a project manager at the consulting firm Horváth & Partner, Roland Berger Strategy Consultants.

## LZH Expands Board of Directors

The curators of the Laser Zentrum Hannover e.V. (LZH) decided to expand the board of directors of the LZH at its last meeting in November. Prof. Dr.-Ing Ludger Overmeyer, head of the Institute of Transport and Automation Technology at the Leibniz Universität Hannover (LUH), and Prof. Dr.-Ing. Volker Wesling, head of the Institute for Welding Technology and Separation Processes at the TU Clausthal, are the new board members, both dedicated to guiding the non-university research institute.

Professor Overmeyer has a PhD in mechanical engineering and knows the LZH inside out. He was staff member and department head at the LZH from 1991 until 1997, when he started working for industrial companies. Since 2002, Professor Overmeyer has been head of his institute at the Leibniz Universität Hannover.

Professor Wesling studied in Clausthal where he also received his PhD. After working in the mechanical engineering industry for nine years, including the former Mannesmann group, he was appointed to a professorship at the TU Clausthal in 2002. Since September 2009, he has also been the vice president of the university. Through the appointment of both new board members, the LZH is emphasizing its engagement as an important partner in the Network of the Lower Saxony Technical University (NTH).
