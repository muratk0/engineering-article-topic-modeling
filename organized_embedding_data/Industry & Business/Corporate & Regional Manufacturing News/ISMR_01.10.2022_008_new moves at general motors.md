# New moves at General Motors

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

General Motors has appointed Jack Uppal as President and Managing Director, GM Africa & Middle East (GM AMEO).

Effective from November 2022, current President and Managing Director, GM Africa & Middle East, Luay Al Shurafa, will transition to take on the role of vice president of vehicle sales, service and marketing, GM China and SAIC-GM. Al Shurafa replaces Mike Devereux who has been named vice president of digital for GM International and will repatriate to the United States.

GM Senior Vice President and President, GM International, Shilpan Amin, said: "Uppal's track record of commercial success and deep knowledge of electrification are valuable assets as our business in Africa and the Middle East leads the transition of the industry to an all-electric future. Under Luay Al Shurafa's leadership, GM AMEO has not only delivered significant growth, but is also leading the transformation of the automotive industry in the region as evidenced at our Exhibit Zero Arabia forum last November.

"I sincerely thank Luay for his leadership. He was the first locally born and raised leader for GM Africa & Middle East and has taken the business to new heights. He successfully led the team through the global pandemic, built on close relationships with local governments and our valued dealer partners and accelerated the profitable growth of our business. He also helped to forge several strategies and partnerships to introduce many firsts in the electric and autonomous vehicle space, as well as nurturing entrepreneurs, women in leadership and STEM," Amin concluded.

Uppal has held several leadership roles with General Motors across sales, marketing and strategic business planning in China, India, Singapore, the Middle East and his home country of Canada. He was a driving force behind GM China's Chevrolet transformation toward an electric vehicle portfolio and supporting distribution channels, with a key role in product development and design. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) from the University of Toronto, specialising in Finance & Marketing.■
