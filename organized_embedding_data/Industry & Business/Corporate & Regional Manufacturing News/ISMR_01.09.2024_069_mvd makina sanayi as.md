# MVD Makina Sanayi A.S.

Since 1950, Turkish manufacturer MVD Machinery has been present in the sheet metal working machinery industry. It places a strong focus on quality and innovation.

MVD Machinery's product range includes hydraulic press brakes; hybrid press brakes; belt- and screw-driven servo press brakes; fibre laser cutting systems; guillotine shears; plasma-oxy cutting systems and punch presses.

"We have solidified our position in the industry with high-quality products as well as fast, reliable and lifelong service. We deliver unmatched quality and innovation in 95 countries through a network of 50+ distributors and representatives," it explained.

"Driven by a commitment to innovation and quality, we continuously evolve to meet the needs of our global customers. Come to our stand and experience the difference!" it added.
