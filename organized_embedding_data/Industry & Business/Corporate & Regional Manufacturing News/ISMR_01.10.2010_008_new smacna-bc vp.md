# NEW SMACNA-BC VP

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

The British Columbia Sheet Metal Association (SMACNA-BC) Board of Directors has appointed Darryl Gordon, of Austin Metal Fabricators L.P., Burnaby, BC, to the position of Vice-President for 2010-2011.

Mr. Gordon’s service to SMACNA-BC includes service on the Board of Directors, Chapter Councillor as well as a previous tour through the chairs of Vice-President, President-elect, President and Past President/Secretary-Treasurer.

“It is important for an individual to step up and take the responsibility of filling the position and advancing the SMACNA-BC Chapter. I believe my 27 years of experience in the sheet metal industry will be an asset to the organisation,” Mr. Gordon said.
