# Margareta Mahlstedt to lead marketing at Porsche Cars GB

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

PORSCHE CARS GB welcomes Margareta Mahlstedt as she is appointed General Manager, Marketing – succeeding Ragnar Schulte, who takes up a new role at Porsche AG in Stuttgart.

"Margareta Mahlstedt, who has held senior positions at BMW/MINI and at Volvo Cars, leaves her current role as director of marketing at Porsche Cars Canada to relocate to the UK – reporting to Alexander Pollich, managing director of Porsche Cars GB," said the company.

Ragnar Schulte, who has held the position of General Manager, Marketing at Porsche Cars GB for six years, moves to Stuttgart to take up the position of Director, Experiential Marketing – a global role in the Central Marketing area.

Margareta Mahlstedt will join Porsche Cars GB on 2 July 2018 and will oversee not only all marketing operations, but also the Porsche Experience Centre Silverstone and the Porsche Carrera Cup Great Britain.
