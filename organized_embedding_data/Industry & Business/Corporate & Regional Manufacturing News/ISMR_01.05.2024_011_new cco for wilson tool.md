# New CCO for Wilson Tool

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

Wilson Tool International, the largest independent manufacturer of tooling systems for punch presses, press brakes and punch and die components for the stamping industry, has announced the appointment of Jason Semerad as its Chief Commercial Officer (CCO).

"As CCO, Jason will be ensuring enterprise-wide alignment of the Wilson Tool business model, sales, marketing and customer engagement strategies, identifying [important] new product development opportunities and serving as a critical leader on the M&A team," it told ISMR.

"With a career spanning over 25 years in prominent global corporations, Jason brings a wealth of expertise in marketing, strategy, commercialisation, product lifecycle management and business development. Known for his keen focus on customer needs and market dynamics, Jason is poised to lead Wilson Tool into its next phase of growth," it added.

“Starting this new chapter as Chief Commercial Officer at Wilson Tool International is truly an honour. With a foundation built on decades of customer-focused innovation, quality and forward-thinking vision, my dedication is unwavering: to nurture strategic partnerships, drive accelerated growth and deliver unmatched value to our stakeholders. With this exceptional team, we will continue to deliver market-leading tooling solutions and service to help our customers solve their most challenging fabrication challenges," commented Jason Semerad.

Jason Semerad graduated from the Indiana University, Kelley School of Business with a 'Master of Business Administration' in Marketing and New Venture/Business Development, as well as a 'Bachelor of Science' in Marketing, Management and International Business.

Wilson Tool products are manufactured at the company's world headquarters at White Bear Lake, Minn., USA, as well as at manufacturing facilities in England; Canada; Brazil and Italy.
