# Leadership promotions at Esmark Steel

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Luis Landois has joined Esmark Steel International as President, leading the growing team and reporting to Esmark Steel Group CEO, Roberto Alvarez. Landois has more than 30 years of experience in the Mexican steel and industrial markets. He earned a B.A. in Business Administration from the ITESM in Monterrey, Mexico, and an M.B.A. from National University. Has been a Board Member of AISI and CANACERO and lives in Monclova.

Dominic Grossmann, currently chief operating officer of Esmark Steel Group, has been named President of Esmark Steel Group. He will report to Alvarez. He joined Esmark in 2008 as an inside sales representative and has had increasing roles of responsibility over 15 years. He graduated with a B.S. from Purdue University.

John Dergentis, currently VP of operations, has been promoted to VP and general manager at Esmark Steel Group. In this role, he is responsible for the Chicago Heights and University Park, Ill. Division operations and will report directly to Grossmann. Dergentis joined Esmark in 2019 after 30 years in management and operations. He earned a B.A. from Northern Illinois University and an M.B.A. from De Paul University.

Patrick Frey, currently VP and general manager of Esmark Steel Group University Park, Ill. Division, has been promoted to senior VP of purchasing and will report to Grossmann. He has more than 35 years of experience in the steel industry and joined Esmark in 2005. He studied at Indiana University and Purdue University.

Jim Bouchard, CEO and Chairman of Esmark, Inc., also confirmed that Esmark Steel Group has established Esmark Steel International, a new business entity focused on supplying the Mexican industrial steel market. Based in Monclova, Coahuila, Esmark Steel International will service steel mill customers to supply flat rolled steel products for the construction; metal products; rail cars; auto machinery; tin plate and electrical equipment markets. Esmark Steel International is a whollyowned subsidiary of Esmark Steel Group.

Esmark Steel International's flat roll steel products include hot rolled; cold rolled; tin plate; metallic coated; painted and digital-printed coils.
