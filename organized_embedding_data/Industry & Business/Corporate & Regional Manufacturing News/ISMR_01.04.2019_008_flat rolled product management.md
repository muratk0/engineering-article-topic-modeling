ALLEGHENY TECHNOLOGIES INCORPORATED has confirmed that, from 1 April 2019, Kimberly A. Fields will join ATI as Executive Vice President with full P&L responsibility for the Flat Rolled Products Group. She will succeed Robert S. Wetherbee, who held this role until becoming ATI's President and CEO.

As Executive Vice President of the FRP Group, Ms. Fields will be responsible for all aspects of the group's business strategy, sales and operations, and functional and business line leaders, to drive growth, earnings and cash flow. ATI is a producer of stainless-steel sheet, specialty plate and specialty coil. Ms. Fields will report directly to Mr. Wetherbee, ATI’s President and CEO.

Kimberley A. Fields brings 20 years of global experience with a focus on growth and operational excellence. In her role at IDEX Corporation, she served as Group President for Industrial and Energy with full P&L responsibility for US$ 750 million of revenue in businesses operating globally across 15 manufacturing locations and sales offices. She led a team of 2,000 and dramatically improved profitability and accelerated growth in the business portfolio.

She joined IDEX in April 2014 as President of GAST Manufacturing and, in 2015, she was promoted to Group President. Previously, she served as Executive Vice President for the integrated global steel producer, EVRAZ, where she had full responsibility for all activities for the US$ 2 billion North American Flat Products business. From 2008 to 2011, Ms. Fields was General Manager of Industrials for GE Energy, with global responsibility for growing GE's penetration in metals, petrochemicals and mining segments. She has also held commercial, manufacturing and strategic leadership positions with Alcoa, The Boston Consulting Group and Owens Corning Fiberglass. Ms. Fields was a founding member of the online metals trading site, MetalSpectrum.

Ms. Fields earned a BS in Ceramic Engineering from the University of Illinois at Champaign-Urbana and an MBA from the Kellogg Graduate School of Management at Northwestern University.

## New attendance record for Southern Manufacturing

SOUTHERN MANUFACTURING & Electronics 2019 has highlighted a new record attendance, according to figures released recently by the show organiser. The exhibition, held in Farnborough (UK) earlier this year, attracted a total of 8,644 unique visitors, an increase of around 13% on the previous year. Around 800 companies from all over the world took part in the event, occupying all the available exhibition space at the recently-opened 20,000m² Farnborough International Exhibition and Conference Centre.

"Visitor statistics revealed significant increases in the number of visitors from the medical equipment, pharmaceutical and metal processing sectors. The largest increase was in visitors from the rail and public transportation sector, which leapt tenfold from 2018. Amongst the exhibiting firms, bookings from non-UK companies increased 26% over 2018," the organiser told SMR.

"Exit polls reveal that visitor interests remain broadly consistent with previous years, but with interest in assembly systems; automation & robotics; machine tools; tooling and inspection systems all showing positive growth. There was also heightened interest in the full range of subcontract services present at the show, including contract electronics manufacturing; fabrication; machining; laser processing and plastics."

Southern Manufacturing & Electronics 2020 returns to Farnborough International from 11-13 February 2020. Admission to the show is free. See www.industrysouth.co.uk

Industry 4.0 leverages record-breaking turnover at Hi-Tech Steel

UK STEEL STOCKHOLDER and processor company, Hi-Tech Steel Services (HTSS), has seen a £3 million increase in turnover in 2018, a 12 per cent rise over the previous year, as a result of introducing new industrial digital technologies through the LCR 4.0 regional support programme.

"Established for over 25 years and processing over 5,000 tonnes of material per month, HTSS works with companies across the UK and Europe in sectors including automotive, construction and ventilation. Recognising a change in customer demand for a more digital approach to steel processing, following an increase in requests for lasercut materials and flat sheet metal products, HTSS approached part-ERDF funded business support programme, LCR 4.0.

Andy Flatt, General Manager, Hi-Tech Steel Services, explained: "Digital transformation is making waves through every industry, so it was no surprise to us that our customers were looking for more advanced solutions than we had traditionally offered. We knew the technology that we needed to introduce, and we knew that there was a significant growth opportunity for us to expand our offer. But what we didn't know was how to implement it without disruption, so it was great that the LCR 4.0 team at LJMU was there to help."

The Liverpool City Region Local Enterprise Partnership (LEP) put HTSS in touch with LCR 4.0 partner, Liverpool John Moores University (LJMU), which provided access to specialist academic expertise and a dedicated resource. The result was the development of a commissioning strategy to fully integrate new laser cutting equipment into the factory to meet customer demand and requirements quicker than before.

In addition to this, four trained operatives were also hired at HTSS to offer a CAD design service. Installing CAD files into the new machinery allowed the company to ensure that metal is cut and processed exactly as required, offering customers a quick and bespoke solution.

All of the new machinery is autonomous and can cut independently, freeing up staff time to be spent on other areas of the business and elements of delivery. The new equipment collects performance data which is automatically fed back into the business to provide insight for further improvements, enabling HTSS to be more sustainable in a competitive market place.

"The results of going through our own digital transformation have already been tremendous," Andy continued. "We have increased turnover by £3 million this year and expanded our customer base significantly. We have also entered new tiers within our supply chain, which will encourage further growth well into the future. LCR 4.0 is most definitely one of the best projects with which I’ve been involved – it has been a fantastic experience."

Hi-Tech Steels Services recently purchased an AMADA FOM2 Flatbed laser. The company can now laser cut sheets up to 20mm thick with a maximum sheet size of 3070 x 1550mm. The maximum sheet weight is 950kg. For further details of the programme, see http://lcr4.uk ■

ALLITE Super Magnesium TM Concept Bike

ALLITE® INC., THE material science company behind the ALLITE® Super Magnesium™ line of branded alloys, has partnered with bicycle manufacturer, Weis, to co-develop 'the first track frame made entirely from the premium metal'. The one-of-a-kind build was showcased at the North American Handmade Bicycle Show (NAHBS) in Sacramento, California (USA) from 15-17 March 2019.

An exclusive collaboration, the project merges ALLITE®'s new alloys with the design and geometry of Weis' bikes. Most recognized for its "Hammer" line, which includes a track or road option, the Brooklyn-based bike manufacturer will use ALLITE® Super Magnesium™ tubes to create a lighter version of its popular Weis Hammer Track bike.

"After connecting with several independent bicycle manufacturers following the launch of ALLITE® Super Magnesium™ in 2018, it became clear that Weis was the ideal partner for this collaboration," said Morten Kristiansen, Vice President, Products and Marketing, ALLITE® Inc.

"Weighing 30 per cent less than aluminium, while also being stiffer and stronger, pound for pound, ALLITE® Super Magnesium™ is the lightest of all structural materials. The revolutionary new alloy, which has been utilised in classified defence and aerospace applications, can also be leveraged in a variety of applications, products and components in the cycling industry and beyond. In certain applications, it is just as strong and effective as carbon fibre at a much more affordable cost," continued the company.

Furthering its appeal for an increasingly environmentally aware consumer base, magnesium is one of the most eco-friendly and sustainable metals in the world, as it is 100 per cent recyclable and has widespread natural occurrence with unlimited reserves.

ALLITE® Super Magnesium™ is the first material to go-to-market from Ohio-based company ALLITE® Inc. Founded in 2018, ALLITE® Inc. is a full-service material sciences organisation that develops and manufactures high-performance metal alloys for industries around the world. See www.alliteinc.com ■
