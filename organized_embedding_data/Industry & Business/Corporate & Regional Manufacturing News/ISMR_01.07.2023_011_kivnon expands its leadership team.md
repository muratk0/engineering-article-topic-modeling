# Kivnon expands its leadership team

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Kivnon, a global specialist and provider of autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and automated guided vehicles, has appointed a new UK country manager and a sales director to help meet product demand in the United Kingdom. Jose Pantaleao will be its new UK country manager while Meir Ziskin has been appointed as the UK sales manager.

"Jose Pantaleao, who has been Kivnon's France country manager since May 2020, will also manage the Kivnon UK subsidiary that provides AGV/AMR vehicles and related maintenance services to the UK. He will provide project management, sales, purchasing and technical services to support demand for Kivnon solutions in the UK and France," commented Kivnon.

Mr. Pantaleao brings many years of global automation expertise gained in logistics, e-commerce, automotive, goods-to-persons and PLC programming positions including projects in China, Spain, India and Denmark.

“Meir Ziskin is an accomplished sales and business development professional with more than ten years of experience in the logistics and automation industries. He has a proven track record of driving business growth and establishing solid relationships with clients in various sectors including automotive, e-commerce and pharmaceuticals. Before joining Kivnon, he held multiple sales roles at companies such as K2 Automation and Su-Pad in Israel for Universal Robots and MiR," added Kivnon.

“Global digital transformation is intensifying interest in our AGV and AMR technologies and the UK has been one of our fastest growing regions. We are pleased to announce that Jose and Meir are joining Kivnon to help us take full advantage of these emerging opportunities," commented Thierry Delmas, Managing Director, Kivnon.

Kivnon is an international company dedicated to mobile robotics with its head office in Barcelona (Spain). Since its beginning as a family-owned business in 2009, it has been offering customers technological solutions for the implementation of automated guided vehicle (AGVs/AMRs) systems. Its comprehensive projects in mobile robotics include the design, prototyping and manufacture of AGVs/AMRs, their installation and launch, and technical assistance and customer service. Kivnon exports to more than 17 countries and continues to extend through subsidiaries in Europe, North America and South America.
