# EMEA leadership at Yaskawa

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Yaskawa Electric Corporation has announced the executive leadership change of the Yaskawa EMEA Region. Marcus Mead succeeded the retiring Bruno J. Schnekenburger and was appointed to Executive Officer Regional Manager Europe, Chairman and President YASKAWA Europe GmbH from 1 March 2024.

"Marcus Mead (53) is stepping into the regional executive head position at Yaskawa Europe GmbH. The British executive joined Yaskawa in 2018 as Vice President of Operations, following a career built across Aerospace Engineering with British Aerospace (Commercial Aircraft) Airbus Division, the automotive Industry with Magna International and industrial automation with Parker Hannifin. Since September 2018, he has been leading our European Robotics Division in Allershausen, near Munich.

Additionally, since March 2023, Mead has held the position of President EMEA at our headquarters in Hattersheim, near Frankfurt," explained the company.

Throughout his tenure, Mead has actively driven Yaskawa's European development, particularly in robotics. In 2019, Yaskawa inaugurated its first European robot factory in Kočevje, Slovenia, which now supplies approximately 80% of the European demand for Motoman robots. In 2023, Yaskawa significantly increased its capacity for robotics system engineering and assembly via a 10,000-sqm expansion in Allershausen, Germany. Its goal was reportedly to achieve European leadership in this domain.

"Bruno J. Schnekenburger has shaped Yaskawa's successful direction in EMEA, and for that we are immensely grateful, commented Mead.

Since 2014, Schnekenburger has held responsible positions at Yaskawa, initially as President of the European Robotics Division, then as Chief Operating Officer and, since 2018, as President and Regional Head. Additionally, since 2020, Schnekenburger has served as Senior Executive Officer of the Japanese parent company, Yaskawa Electric Corporation.
