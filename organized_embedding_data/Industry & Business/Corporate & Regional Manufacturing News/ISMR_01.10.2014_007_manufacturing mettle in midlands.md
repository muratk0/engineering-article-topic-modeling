# Manufacturing mettle in Midlands

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

SKILLED ARTISANS and the latest equipment are driving growth for a UK Midlands’ manufacturer making sheet metal components for cars, trains, tractors and aircraft.

A new £600,000 Engineering Centre opened recently by the Lord Mayor of Coventry, Councillor Hazel Noonan, is one component of privately-owned Premier Group’s £3m investment in staff, equipment and facilities over the last four years. In that time, the company has increased staff numbers by 30 per cent and has seen its sales book grow by 170 per cent from £5.5m in 2010/2011 to just under £15m in 2013/2014.

The privately-owned firm employs 164 people, including 22 apprentices, and is looking forward to welcoming its next intake of young trainees in September. Its growing list of clients includes Bentley, BMW, Jaguar, Land Rover, Ford and Aston Martin in the car industry with Bombardier, Alstom and GKN Aerospace among its customers in rail and aerospace. The metal bodies for the Morgan three-wheeler car are made by its craftsmen, who also turned their hand to the task of manufacturing 11,500 Olympic torches for London 2012. ■
