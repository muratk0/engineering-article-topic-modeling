# Solid Print acquires 3DVerkstan

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Solid Print3D, 3D printing and scanning specialists in the UK, acquired 3DVerkstan (a Stockholm-based supplier of 3D printing and solutions, with a subsidiary in Denmark). 3DVerkstan Nordic AB has been a driving force for additive manufacturing in Sweden and Scandinavia since 2013.

Solid Print3D is owned by Solid Solutions and part of the TriMech Group. Solid Solutions is one of the largest SolidWorks & CAD resellers in the world and, with Solid Print3D, supports 20,000+ engineering companies in the UK and Ireland.

3DVerkstan Nordic AB was placed into administration on 21 April 2023. Since then, the estate has been in an intensive process to select the best-suited buyer of the business located in Stockholm, Sweden. Solid Print3D stepped in and worked quickly to secure the deal, an important step in its European growth strategy for Solid Solutions and Solid Print3D.

“There are clear synergies between 3DVerkstan and Solid Print 3D from their incredible team, products, culture to the fantastic leadership. I believe that, together, we can build quickly to deliver positive results, allowing the 3DVerkstan team to continue to deliver the amazing support and service to their customers," commented Neil Sewell, Managing Director, Solid Print3D.

Solid Print3D is a sister business to Solid Solutions, a large UK SolidWorks reseller and one of the largest SolidWorks resellers in the world. It supports over 19,000 UK design and engineering companies. Solid Print works with leading brands in 3D printing; 3D scanning; waterjet cutting; laser cutting and desktop CNC.
