# New CFO for BENTELER Group

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

The Supervisory Board of BENTELER International AG has appointed Dr. Tobias Braun, managing director and chief financial officer (CFO) of BENTELER Steel/Tube GmbH, as the new CFO of the BENTELER Group. He assumed the position on 1 September 2022. Braun will also continue in his role as managing director and CFO of BENTELER Steel/Tube.

Frank Jehle handed the mantle to his internal successor and left the company on 31 August 2022. The financial expert, with many years of experience in the automotive industry, was appointed to the Executive Board by the BENTELER Group in September 2020.

Ralf Göttel, BENTELER Group CEO, commented: “With Tobias Braun, an experienced leader from our own ranks is taking over the important position of CFO of the BENTELER Group. Over the past four years, Tobias has successfully implemented the transformation of the Steel/Tube Division in a dynamic market and made an important contribution to the overarching transformation strategy of the BENTELER Group."

In addition to Ralf Göttel, CEO, and Tobias Braun, CFO, Michael Baur will continue to be responsible for the operational implementation of the ongoing transformation programme as the third member of the Executive Board.

BENTELER is investing, amongst other areas, in e-mobility. This includes projects such as the expansion of the BENTELER e-mobility location in Schwandorf, the planned production of its people-mover and the targeted expansion of lightweight construction expertise.

“The focus on sustainability will also be further intensified. The Steel/Tube Division will, therefore, continue to focus on the decarbonisation of steel tube production," it told ISMR.

In July 2022, the BENTELER Group detailed further goals and initiated numerous measures as part of a comprehensive sustainability strategy.■
