# BENTELER Group invests to tap new markets

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

FISCAL YEAR 2014 was a successful year for the BENTELER Group. After adjustment for currency effects, the company group increased its revenue by 3.2 per cent to €7,556 million (2013: €7,425 million). At €133.3 million, consolidated earnings before taxes were also €129.0 million higher than in the previous year. This equated to an earnings improvement of €58.6 million. The BENTELER Group generated earnings after taxes of €102.0 million after a consolidated net profit of €3.1 million in 2013.

BENTELER Automotive accounts for 75.8 per cent of Group revenues. The BENTELER Steel/Tube division contributed 13.1 per cent to total revenues, while the BENTELER Distribution division contributed 11.1 per cent.

“Despite some challenges such as currency fluctuations, uncertain oil price development and political crises, we look to 2015 with confidence,” said Hubertus Benteler, CEO of the BENTELER Group. “In recent years, we have created a launch pad for further growth in all divisions. In doing so, we have focused especially on tapping new markets with innovative products and services.”

As in the previous year, the BENTELER Group invested heavily in the further development of its global business in fiscal year 2014.

The total investment amount was €489 million (2013: €349 million). The largest investment project continues to be the construction of a new hot rolling mill in the US state of Louisiana, which is expected to open by the end of fiscal year 2015 as the first production site of BENTELER Steel/Tube. The total investment volume for this project is around US$ 1 billion.

BENTELER Automotive recorded 2014 revenue of €5,865 million, corresponding to a slight year-on-year decrease of 0.7 per cent. Adjusted for currency translation effects, this figure is 1.0 per cent or €61 million above last year’s level. BENTELER Automotive develops and produces ready-to-install modules, components and parts for bodies, chassis and engines. As a full-service supplier, it supplies virtually all major automotive manufacturers around the world.

BENTELER Steel/Tube division generated revenue of €1,009 million in the reporting period, corresponding to an increase of 5.2 per cent compared with the previous year. This is due to a large extent to higher sales volumes and rising price levels in the US market. BENTELER Steel/Tube is one of the leading international producers of high-quality tube products.
