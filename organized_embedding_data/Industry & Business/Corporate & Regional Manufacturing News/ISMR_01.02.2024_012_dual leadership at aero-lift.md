# Dual leadership at AERO-LIFT

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

AERO-LIFT Vakuumtechnik GmbH, a manufacturer of vacuum-lifting devices and solutions, confirmed that Ms Katja Haak was appointed managing director as of 1 January 2024. Together with Tobias Pauli, who continues to act as managing director, she will continue to drive the company forward.

"Ms Haak has extensive expertise in financial management, corporate management and strategic planning. During her time as commercial director and authorised signatory, she has made a decisive contribution to making AERO-LIFT fit for the future and increasing operational efficiency," commented the company.

The decision to divide the management between two heads emphasises the strategic orientation of AERO-LIFT to "master the variety of entrepreneurial challenges more effectively and further increase the company's innovative strength."

The family business was founded in Geislingen-Binsdorf/Baden-Württemberg (Germany), where its headquarters are still located. It supplies a range of products from tube lifters; vacuum lifters and area grippers for automation technology to components; crane systems and services.

New dual leadership in the management of AERO-LIFT: Katja Haak and Tobias Pauli will jointly manage the company from 1 January 2024.
