# New head of business development at KMF Group

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Adam Ellis has been appointed the new head of business development at UK sheet-metal specialist, KMF Group. KMF is a leading subcontract manufacturer of precision sheet-metal components, enclosures, machined components and electro-mechanical assemblies. Its facilities span 255,000 sq. ft. of metal manufacturing floorspace.

In 2011, Adam Ellis joined KMF at the young age of eighteen and enrolled in KMF's four-year apprenticeship scheme. Ellis worked in a range of departments, including laser cutting, metal forming, punching and welding. By joining the apprenticeship scheme, he gained valuable 'on-the-job' experience as well as professional qualifications.

After completing his apprenticeship, Adam Ellis was awarded NVQ and VRQ Level 3 qualifications in metal fabrication and welding.

Adam Ellis's first role at KMF's Advanced Solutions was as a press-brake operator. He was then promoted to sales engineer and involved in planning the manufacturing process. Once customer drawings had been received, it was his duty to provide the best method of manufacture to clients. The next stage of his career saw him advance to business development manager, involved in supervising key customer accounts and tasked with finding new business opportunities.

As the new head of business development, he is now responsible for managing and nurturing members of the team as well as taking part in regular management meetings to discuss current sales, forecasted figures and new business opportunities.■
