# PACKING A PUNCH

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Brabantia has expanded its production of rectangular bins with a new punching line.

As of June 2022, Brabantia has a new WEMO punching line for rectangular bins at its production facility in Overpelt, Belgium. Discussions on this project first started in 2020, when Brabantia was developing a new product. Expectations for the product were high, so extra capacity was needed. The versatile and rapid punching line from WEMO has now been installed and is already in production at Brabantia.

Brabantia is a privately owned Dutch company which manufactures items for the home such as waste bins, laundry racks, food storage containers and other products. It was founded in 1919 by a group of 15 people in the small Dutch town of Aalst, North Brabant (The Netherlands) under the name van Elderen & Co, a company specialising in sheet metal fabrication. The name Brabantia comes from the province of North Brabant and was an early Van Elderen trade name.

It offers a range of some 1,000 houseware products in five core categories: waste storage; food storage; food preparation; laundry care and bathroom. The company now has four production units employing 1,000 employees across 85 countries and an annual turnover of around €150 million. Still using steel as its prime manufacturing material, as well as a variety of high-grade plastics, products continue to be made in-house in Belgium, Latvia and China, as well as at an assembly and finishing plant at Nailsea in the UK.

## Quality is key

"We've always been a family company that believes in creating products that are made with integrity by people who care about quality. Ideas that are made to last – and designed for living," explained Brabantia.

The company has owned subsidiaries in all key European markets including the United Kingdom; Belgium; France; Germany; Denmark; Switzerland; Spain; Italy and the United States with an export department covering other world markets. It markets its products based on their supposed toughness and durability. According to its mission statement, Brabantia aims to develop products that are "designed for living"

WEMO Special Projects designs, builds and maintains tailor-made production lines for the fully automated processing of sheet metal: punching, bending, joining and software. WEMO has worked with Brabantia since 2015, when it developed a punching solution for Brabantia's rectangular waste bins. That solution called for a specific speed and a process in which plates had to be produced in matt black, brushed stainless steel and glossy chrome steel without any blemishes or scratches on the material.

"This is highly sensitive material, and even the slightest damage is unacceptable on the visible side of the end product. We developed a concept with a used, refurbished sheet-metal punching machine and turning station with new machine parts. This was completed and installed in 2016," WEMO told ISMR.

## Planning the line

WEMO is a family business, based in The Netherlands, with 60 years of experience in sheet-metal processing and machine building. It develops, builds and installs fully automated metalworking machines for punching, bending and joining sheet metal and coil material into semi-finished or finished products for customers all over the world. Customisation is, it explained, the winning hand.

It held conversations with Brabantia about its new punching line well into 2021.

"A combination of new and used machinery was not an option as there were no used punching stations available. As a result, the solution became more costly but Brabantia already had several years of experience with the WEMO process and with our technology. It knew that the speed, quick switch times, high machine availability and zero loss of product would ensure a very good payback period," explained WEMO.

In the meantime, the first completed line was equipped with a shuttle system that can service the different rolling shutter cover bending machines. The WEMO punching machine supplies the punched plates to the CNC rolling shutter cover benders. With the high output of the WEMO punching machine, Brabantia opted for an automated shuttle system on the line.

## A strong partnership

“Ever since our first project with WEMO, we have had a lot of faith in the partnership and the solutions offered by WEMO. I like working with WEMO. We have open and clear communications. We all honour our commitments and have hardly experienced any disruptions or errors on the punching machine. WEMO brainstorms with us and is creative. We believe that we now have a production solution that best meets our expectations," commented Dirk-Jan Daams, Engineering Manager, Brabantia

The key properties of the new WEMO punching line are its speed, punching with no material damage and a flexible connection between the punching solution and the three CNC rolling shutter cover benders in the line.

"This achieves the capacity that we need with one WEMO punching machine, so we have a great return on investment and we can keep loss of product (due to damage) down to a minimum," continued Brabantia.

“We are so pleased that Brabantia is also keen on a long-term partnership," added WEMO. “That is something that we strive for and do everything we can to achieve. Of course, that takes two or more parties."
