# The collaboration between Lucid, AP&T and the fischer Group on AP&T's new aluminium technology has helped to reduce the weight of the Lucid Air luxury electric car

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Making vehicles more efficient and safer is a challenge for the entire automotive industry. U.S. electric car manufacturer, Lucid Motors, chose a new and innovative technology to address the challenge. The Lucid Air, launched in 2021, uses several unique, lightweight components made of hot-formed, high-strength aluminium designed, manufactured and industrialised in collaboration with AP&T and the fischer Group.

"We are extremely proud to be given the chance to contribute to the building of this fantastic car. Lucid Motors is among the first car manufacturers in the world to use hot-formed, high-strength aluminium components made using our technology. It makes their success an important milestone for us as well," said Dr. Christian Koroschetz, Chief Sales Officer, AP&T.

Lucid's mission is to inspire the adoption of sustainable energy by creating advanced technologies and "the most captivating luxury electric vehicles centred around the human experience." The company's first car, Lucid Air, is a luxury sedan with a California-inspired design that features luxurious, full-size interior space in a mid-sized exterior footprint. Lucid recently reported Q3 revenue of US$ 195.5 million on deliveries of 1,398 vehicles.

## An exciting challenge

In 2015, Lucid was in pursuit of the latest technology for the manufacturing of crash-critical stampings, such as door rings, B-pillar stiffenings and battery pack protection components. It reached out to AP&T.

Lucid presented AP&T with several parts and process requirements—pushing the team towards new and advanced processes, which would ultimately yield low weight, high formability, strength and ductibility products at high volumes.

At the time, AP&T was deeply engaged in innovation projects geared towards investigating how high-strength aluminium could be used to produce low-weight, high-strength car body components. As a direct result of these projects, Lucid selected AP&T as a supplier for the Lucid Air.
