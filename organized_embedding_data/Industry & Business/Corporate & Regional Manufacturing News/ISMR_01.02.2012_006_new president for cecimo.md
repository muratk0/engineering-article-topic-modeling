# New President for CECIMO

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

Martin Kapp is the new President of CECIMO, the European Association of Machine Tool Industries

The CECIMO Fall General Assembly, which met on 12 November 2011 in Zurich, elected Martin Kapp as new President of CECIMO for a period of two years. He has been entrusted with the responsibility of leading the European Association, which represents 33% of the world's machine tool production.

After his election Martin Kapp stated, "We need to focus on how to boost the competitiveness of the European machine tool industry in facing the challenges of globalisation, the fight against climate change, environmental protection, the importance of market surveillance and enforcement, the specific needs of SMEs and the lack of a skilled technical labour force. We need to encourage a research and innovation strategy driven by the present and future needs of the market."

His most important goal is to point out to European stakeholders the importance of machine tools for industrial production in Europe. His chief occupation is as Managing Partner of the Kapp Group, Coburg. He has been the Chairman of VDW, the German Machine Tool Manufacturers Association, since 2010. ■
