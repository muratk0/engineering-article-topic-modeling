# WEMO focuses on Asian sales

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

Veikko Moilanen joined the WEMO sales team for WEMO Special Projects as Vice President, Business Development for the Asian Market, on 1 November 2022. He has over 25 years of experience in the sheet-metal industry and will be responsible for further business development in the Asian market.

"We are delighted with Veikko's arrival. He fits WEMO's culture very well and his focus and vast knowledge and experience in this market is of great importance to take advantage of opportunities in Asia," said WEMO.

“The Asian sheet-metal processing industry faces a huge need for automation. The market is dealing with a lot of challenges; the lack of skilled labour forces; increasing material prices and component scarcity. Our tailor-made sheetmetal bending machines fulfil this need and can achieve rapid cycle times and low costs per part," it added.

Veikko Moilanen worked for over 20 years at Finn Power/Prima Power, where he was responsible for system sales in Asia. He then started Vaski Oy in Finland, a company specialising in flat bar machinery, cut-to-length machinery and multi-coil systems.

"Veikko's focus on the Asian market will enable us to make great steps in the further development of this market. Our solutions for industry sectors in this market include elevator panels; shelving; electrical enclosures; wall and ceiling panels and steel doors as well as a range of automotive and HVAC applications," continued WEMO.

Veikko Moilanen will cover countries such as China; Japan; South Korea; Taiwan; Thailand; Indonesia; Malaysia; Vietnam; Australia; the Philippines; New Zealand and India.
