# IFRELECTS NEW PRESIDENT

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

Photo: Dr. Shinsuke Sakakibara (left) is the new IFR President

After its General Assembly meeting, the IFR Executive Board elected Dr. Shinsuke Sakakibara, Managing Counsellor, FANUC Corporation, Japan, as the new President of the International Federation of Robotics (IFR).

Dr. Sakakibara announced that he will continue to strengthen the IFR as an important organisation for the world robotics community.

“The key focus areas will be continuing strong publication relation activities, the acquisition of member associations of emerging robot markets and the implementation of a new concept of the worldwide robotics conference, the ISR. I am looking forward to close and fruitful cooperation with our members and the IFR Secretariat.”

Arturo Baroncelli of Comau Robotics, Italy was elected as IFR Vice President.
