## Moving into gear

INDUSTRIAL GEAR unit specialist, Hansen Industrial Transmissions NV (HIT), acquired by Sumitomo Heavy Industries Ltd. (SHI) in March 2011, has confirmed that its European assembly centre, based in the Antwerp gear plant, will integrate the engineering, production, assembly, sales and service of the PARAMAX industrial gearboxes of Sumitomo Heavy Industries (SHI) for the EMEA region.

In the framework of global synergy projects, HIT’s product offering for the EMEA region has been extended by integrating the PARAMAX industrial gearbox activity of SHI. As such the company has forged, together with the Power Transmission & Controls group of SHI, a customer-oriented sales and service platform. Alongside, a clear product strategy for the EMEA region has been developed, offering its customers and end users a more accurate selection of gear units to serve high and low torque applications.

One year after the acquisition by SHI, the global integration of HIT into SHI to create a leading player in the power transmission market is still ongoing in most parts of the world but has now been finalised in Europe to serve the EMEA region.

Combining the strengths of HIT’s P4 and SHI’s PARAMAX gear units, a complementary match of industrial gear units ranging between 3 and 1100 kNm is now available from one single source. www.hansenindustrialgearboxes.com

## An eye on Morocco

THE SECOND SISTEP-MIDEST, the Moroccan trade show for industrial subcontracting, machinery, equipment and services, will take place at OFEC, the Casablanca Exhibition Centre, from 19 - 22 September 2012. It is a major industry event for Morocco, which occupies a strategic geographical position that enables it to attract professionals in search of partnerships in the new markets of North Africa and elsewhere on the continent.

SISTEP-MIDEST offers a complete range of exhibits divided into six major sectors: machine tools, sheet metal, electronics, services, plastics and subcontracting. In 2011, 207 exhibitors (including 59% from overseas) represented a dozen countries and occupied 6,626 m² of exhibition space. These exhibitors welcomed nearly 4,000 decision-making professionals drawn from 25 countries and prestigious international groups such as Aircelle, Alstom, Bombardier, Lafarge, Renault, Safran, Suzuki, Thales, ThyssenKrupp, Volkswagen, Zodiac Aerospace etc.

To date, the following national pavilions have already been confirmed: France (through Ubifrance with sixty exhibitors); China (with thirty exhibitors); Spain (with fifteen exhibitors) and Hungary (ten exhibitors).

“Morocco offers real opportunities for industry. The Kingdom has put in place a major National Charter for Industrial Emergence, which is designed to be a tool for modernising its economy through the development of this sector. It is launching major projects in sectors such as railways, aerospace, motor vehicles and energy, and it is continuing to modernise its road, port and airport infrastructure,” said the organiser.

“All this requires the establishment of industries and, in particular metal, plastic and electronics subcontracting… The major groups will not let such opportunities pass them by and they draw in their wake a large number of industrial partners ready to follow them and profit from these development opportunities.” www.sistep-midest.com

## Queens Award for Rhodes

UK ENGINEERING company, Joseph Rhodes Limited (part of Group Rhodes) has won the prestigious Queen’s Award for Enterprise in the International Trade Category in recognition of outstanding achievements in overseas markets. During the MACH 2012 exhibition, the company also won the MWP Advanced Engineering Award 2012 for Best Metalforming and Fabrication Equipment for its range of superplastic forming and diffusion bonding (SPF/DB) machinery.

The need to improve profitability through competitive advantage has emerged as a clear strategic objective for Joseph Rhodes. It has therefore focused on developing its international business with a strong customer orientation and clear recognition of the need for an equally strong platform of innovation. The company’s R&D department is developing ‘patent pending’ environmental systems to create energy from household waste, with the UK as the initial target market but with significant future export potential. In 2010, the Company was awarded the Queen’s Award for Enterprise in the Innovation category.

Joseph Rhodes has demonstrated success in penetrating overseas markets and experienced significant growth and expansion on the international scene. It is one of Europe’s leading Original Equipment Manufacturers in its field, designing and developing an extensive range of machinery for metalforming, material handling, environmental and special purpose applications.

It operates in over thirty countries but has been particularly successful in China, India and the Middle East. Exports represent approximately 30% of the company’s turnover and include multi-million pound projects such as the installation of ceramic pipe plants in Saudi Arabia and aluminium blanking lines in India. The company also manufactures machinery for UK-based projects, such as the Eurofighter Typhoon and Joint Strike Fighter aircraft programmes, which subsequently will contribute significantly to the UK’s international balance of trade.

Recent export successes include the sale of capital plant and machinery for the manufacture of components as varied as coins for the South Korean Mint to aerosol cans in Thailand. In addition, assemblies manufactured by Rhodes at its Wakefield site are being used in support of Allied forces in Iraq and Afghanistan and over US$ 1.5m sales of technical fabrications have been recently supplied to the US Navy for installation on the new class of DGD Destroyer.

Major target markets for its metalforming business continue to include China, Brazil and India, where this year an office has been opened. Confirmed orders of over US$ 4m have recently been secured from the aerospace and aluminium processing sectors in these countries. An example of this export success is a US$ 3m contract signed in 2011 with Hindalco of India, a leading global aluminium processing company. www.grouprhodes.com

## Live furniture production

The design of the Tom Dixon Chair in sheet metal is reminiscent of the hood for the legendary Citroën 2CV automobile. A TruPunch 3000 and a TruBend 5130 were used to manufacture the chair.

DESIGNER Tom Dixon has joined forces with TRUMPF to create an extraordinary design project. Dixon drafted different products made from sheet metal for Milan DesignWeek (17 – 22 April 2012). These were fabricated on TRUMPF machines during the recent Milan Furniture Fair. The machines were set up in Milan’s Museum of Science and Technology.

Machine operators and sales personnel from TRUMPF Homberger s.r.l., TRUMPF’s Italian subsidiary near Milan, were on hand in the museum during the six days of the fair. They manufactured a lamp shade (Stamp Lamp) and a prototype chair, reminiscent of the hood for the legendary Citroën 2CV automobile. The chair was fabricated on the TruPunch 3000 and the TruBend 5130. Stamp Lamp is a flat-packed shade, made from the wasted sheet metal used to create the chair. Lamp and chair are manufactured from 1mm thick galvanized mild steel.

This design project was also on display during LAMIERA, the Italian machine tool fair, which took place in Bologna from 9 – 12 May 2012. See www.trumpf.com
