# Prima Power opens new plant in Finland

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

PRIMA POWER HAS announced the opening of a new manufacturing site in Seinäjoki, in Western Finland. Finn-Power Oy, part of the Prima Industrie Group, which manufactures Prima Power turret punch presses, combined machines and systems, has relocated its manufacturing plant and Tech Centre (previously based at Kauhava) to the new facility at Seinäjoki.

"The 20,000 square metre facility was commissioned by Seinäjoen Yrityskiinteistöt (a company owned by the City of Seinäjoki) and built by Lujatalo Oy for an overall investment of around €20m. The new factory was specially designed and built for Finn-Power Oy purposes, which has rented it on a long-term basis," Prima Industrie told SMR.

The new location, in operation since November 2018, was officially inaugurated on 21 March 2019 in the presence of the Speaker of the Finnish Parliament (Paula Risikko); the Mayor of the city of Seinäjoki (Jorma Rasinmäki); the Italian Ambassador (Gabriele Altana); the Chairman of Prima Industrie Board (Gianfranco Carbonato); the managing director of Finn-Power Oy (Juha Mäkitalo) as well as other authorities; international customers; partners and press representatives. An Open House dedicated to Finnish Prima Power customers took place the following day.

"For a company, it is very rare to be able to build a completely new factory," explained Juha Mäkitalo. "We have now done this and are very pleased and grateful. For Finn-Power Oy, the opening is also the occasion to celebrate our 50-year anniversary. A book on the history of the company was launched at the opening event.

Over 250 guests took part in the two-day event and attended presentations and speeches with a guided tour of the factory, where 400 people are employed. Finn-Power Oy is strongly focused on Innovation and 20% of its workforce is dedicated to R&D activities. The Seinäjoki plant manufactures all Prima Power punching; punching/shearing; punching/ laser-cutting and automation equipment as well as systems for the global market. Around 450 machines and automation equipment are delivered from the Finnish factory annually.

"The new premises consists of over 20,000 square metres, including a production plant (13,530 m²), offices (5,585 m²) and a showroom (1,550 m²). Compared to previous facilities, production capacity in the new plant has increased by 40%, with a 30% increase in production space; improved internal logistics; modern technology and increased building height of the building (where tall storage systems can be assembled in every part of the hall and machines can be hoisted with cranes)," added the Prima Industrie Group.

"The new Finnish production plant in Seinäjoki also has high energy efficiency and was built with innovative 'green' technologies, equipped with an efficient heat recovery system, lowenergy windows and solar panels. A modern lighting system adapts to daylight so that the best possible working conditions are always granted. Compared to the previous facilities, we see a reduction of 40% in heating energy.

After the official event, on 23 March 2019 the plant hosted 700 people for the "Family Day". Employees and their families celebrated the new location and 50 years of the Finnish company. See www.primapower.com
