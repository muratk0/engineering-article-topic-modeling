# AIDA-America promotions

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA) has promoted Brenden O'Brien to regional sales manager (RSM) in Ohio; North Carolina; South Carolina and Virginia. George Munschauer remains regional sales manager in these states. As Co-RSMs, Brenden and George are the AIDA-America points of contact for all metal stamping and complete forming system needs in these states.

Mason Round has been promoted to RSM in Kentucky; New Mexico; Oklahoma; Tennessee and Texas. Greg Bonczkowski remains regional sales manager in New Mexico; Oklahoma and Texas. As Co-RSMs, Greg and Mason are the points of contact for all metal stamping system needs in New Mexico, Oklahoma and Texas.

Rand Mellinger remains regional sales manager in Kentucky and Tennessee. As Co-RSMs, Mason and Rand are the points of contact for all metal stamping system needs in Kentucky and Tennessee.
