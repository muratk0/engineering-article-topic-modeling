## RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT

To further its goals, Coşkunöz has built R&D centres in Turkey. Coşkunöz Member of the Board, Mrs Oya Coşkunöz Yoney, is driving to develop higher value-add projects in sectors such as automotive and machinery. Technological production is an inescapable element of global competition today.

The Coşkunöz R&D centre undertakes all research and development activities for the Coşkunöz Holding groups.

Coşkunöz R&D started out with the slogan ‘Cheaper, Lighter, Safer’ and its focus was on new products, materials and production techniques. The development of highly engineered products and advanced technology machinery and equipment is its main target. Coşkunöz R&D also works in close collaboration with universities and local and foreign research facilities.

“We currently have many new projects in our R&D centre, one of which is a laser cutting and welding machine. We design laser cutting and welding cells for automotive and white goods applications for prototyping and serial production. We will also develop cutting and welding technology with laser technology. We also produce servo hydraulic green machines and exhibited a MACOS servo hydraulic press, using 50 per cent less electricity and oil, at the last EuroBLECH exhibition. The press can reach speeds of 750mm/sec. with 200mm/sec. pressing speeds. This is the greenest technology available for stamping applications,” added Musa Saydi.

## SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

The Bursa Coşkunöz Educational Foundation, founded by M. Kemal Coşkunöz in 1988, has a “public-benefit status” and 80 per cent of its income, which comes from the profits of the Coşkunöz Holding companies, is used for technical education. Since the day it was established, the Coşkunöz Educational Foundation has contributed to the country’s progress by keeping up with all changes in vocational education; it provides technical training as needed by the private and public sectors and society and continues activities as an institute of benchmarking levels nationally and internationally.

The Coşkunöz Educational Foundation aims to provide education and skills for Turkey’s youngsters and ultimately help them find employment. It has three main areas of activity:

■ Vocational and specialist education

■ Cooperation for raising the quality of education

■ Scholarships and support.

In 1997, the Coşkunöz Educational Foundation built the M. Kemal Coşkunöz Anatolian Technical High School in the Nilüfer district of Bursa and its aim is, through strategic cooperation with the National Vocational High School Reinforcement Foundation, to encourage more support to education. The Bursa Coşkunöz Educational Foundation also provides support to many departments of Uludağ University. It has backed academics with congress sponsorships and for various scientific works. Every year, cash grants are also given to 50 successful high school and university students and grants for accommodation are given to 25 students. ■
