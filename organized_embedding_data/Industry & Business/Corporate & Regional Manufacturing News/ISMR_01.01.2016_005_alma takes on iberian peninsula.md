# Alma takes on Iberian Peninsula

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

SOFTWARE SPECIALIST Alma has announced the opening of a branch in Irun (Gipuzkoa region, Spain) to distribute its almaCAM sheet metal CAD/CAM and nesting software in Spain and Portugal.

“Alma is well represented in Europe and throughout the world. However, aside from a few partners or clients, we were lacking in presence in the Iberian Peninsula. This market is similar and close to ours - it is made up of many small enterprises and subcontractors working with sheet metal. Therefore, it holds strong potential. Thanks to our long-lasting presence in Latin America, it has been easier to implement our software and meet our Spanish and Portuguese clients’ needs,” explained Philippe Rouzeau, Alma’s distribution network manager.

The new Irun-based joint venture company, Metalcam Sheet Metal Software S.L. (Alma Ibérica), was officially created on 1 September 2015. Alma has taken minority shares in the entity and is financially supporting the launch. In Spain and Portugal, Alma will focus on spreading its 2014 almaCAM software suite. See www.almacam.com
