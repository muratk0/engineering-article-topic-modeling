# Dyas Makina San. VE Ticaret A.Ş.

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Stand G137

www.dyas.com.tr

“Dyas/Guney Taslama creates innovative solutions for the steel industry with CNC beam and plate drilling lines that date back to 2008.

Wide production lines are supported by expert staff teams equipped with the latest technology, equipment and facilities in a closed production area of 6000 square metres in Bursa (Turkey)," Dyas told ISMR.

Today, Dyas exports to 22 countries with 20 distributors as business partners. Approximately 60% of the machinery it manufactures are delivered to buyers in countries such as the USA, EU members and Australia. The company entered the steel processing sector in Turkey in 2008 with CNC sheet and profile processing machinery.

"All the materials and components used in our products groups are from world-class suppliers. Installation and aftersales is backed up by professionals who respond 24/7, regardless of time difference. Control units and automation systems offer quick support to customers, wherever the machines are installed," it concluded.
