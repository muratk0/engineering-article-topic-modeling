2022 is a pivotal year for the auto industry, as nearly every legacy automaker has launched at least one electric vehicle. Ford, GM, Subaru, Stellantis, Toyota, Nissan, Hyundai, BMW, Volkswagen, and Kia have launched EVs this year to compete with Tesla, Canoo, Fiskar, Lordstown, and Rivian.

So what does this mean for stamping manufacturers supplying the auto industry? Attend FMA’s second annual Stamping in an EV World Conference this June to hear from auto industry insiders and prominent players. Join fellow company owners, plant and department managers, and metal fabricators to learn how to navigate this electrified new road.
