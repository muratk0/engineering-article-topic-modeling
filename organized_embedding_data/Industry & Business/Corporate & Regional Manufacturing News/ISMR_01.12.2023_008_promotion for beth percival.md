# Promotion for Beth Percival

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press manufacturer in Dayton, OH (USA) recently promoted Beth Percival to Senior Director of Finance.

"Beth has been part of the AIDA-America team for over 17 years. During her tenure, she has worked as a general accountant; senior accountant; project controller and controller. In her new role, Beth develops, controls and maintains an integrated plan for the effective control of all financial operations at AIDA-America, while also ensuring all financial programmes meet corporate and regulatory requirements," said AIDA-America.

AIDA is a global specialist in the design; manufacture; sales; service and support; refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metalforming automation equipment. With a worldwide network of sales and service locations in nearly 40 cities across 20 countries, its press installations exceed 80,000 units in over 60 countries.
