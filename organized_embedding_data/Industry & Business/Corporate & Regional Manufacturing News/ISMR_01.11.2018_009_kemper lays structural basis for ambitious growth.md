# KEMPER lays structural basis for ambitious growth strategy

[TARİH: 01.11.2018 ISMR]

KEMPER GMBH IS laying the structural basis for its ambitious growth strategy. To do this, the extraction and filtration system manufacturer is restructuring its management board and successively increasing its number of employees. It takes this action after seeing its sales double over the last eight fiscal years. The past twelve months have been the best in the company's history and 2018 will most likely be the most successful year in its history, the company told ISMR. In addition, the company has set itself ambitious targets by 2025. The new CEO, Marketing and Sales, Frederic Lanz, will be actively shaping this development as of 2019.

To support this path in the future, Björn Kemper, CEO of KEMPER GmbH, says that corporate structures need to be adapted: "The restructuring has already started. We're taking on new staff practically every week and are continuously expanding our capacities."

Whilst Michael Schiller will continue to have responsibilities for production, the technical department, sourcing and controlling, Björn Kemper will in future focus on the strategic development and global expansion of the KEMPER Group. Currently, the family-owned business has over 350 employees on its payroll – and this number is rising.

Bjorn Kemper, CEO, Kemper GmbH

"Our solution- and user-oriented products plus the growing importance of a healthy work environment will continue to accelerate our growth worldwide", Björn Kemper points out. The manufacturer intends to raise its sales again – this time by 50% until 2025.

KEMPER is not only changing management responsibilities, it is also extending its management board. As of 1 January 2019, Frederic Lanz will become the new CEO Marketing & Sales, an important role which was previously held by Björn Kemper. He will focus on further developing the KEMPER brand internationally.

"I am very much attracted by this fresh challenge in a company with a pioneering spirit, a trail-blazing portfolio and a powerful team," said Lanz. "The industry's future lies in digitalisation, closely linked with industrial safety and employee satisfaction. It's now a matter of getting the most out of the company's potential."
