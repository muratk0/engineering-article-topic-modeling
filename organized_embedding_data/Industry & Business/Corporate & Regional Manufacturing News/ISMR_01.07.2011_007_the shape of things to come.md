ISMR interviews Walter Suber, Vice President of Australia NSW’s Sheet Metal Industry Association

WALTER SUBER is the Vice President of the Sheet Metal Industry Association for New South Wales, Australia. He has been involved with the SIA for the last 11 years in various capacities and works for OneSteel George Ward Sheet Aluminium in Australia. 
He outlined challenges and opportunities in the Australian market for sheet metal fabricators and formers to ISMR…

ISMR SAYS

"An eye on emerging technology, safety and future entrants into the industry are key drivers for Australia’s NSW sheet metal association."

ISMR: PLEASE GIVE US SOME BACKGROUND DATA ON SIA – WHEN WAS IT FORMED, WHO ARE YOUR MEMBERS, HOW YOU SUPPORT THE SHEET METAL MARKET ETC.?

WS The SIA was established over 25 years ago by a group of businesses that wanted to secure the future of the sheet metal industry by getting involved with our educators at our local TAFE technical and further education facilities.

It lobbied them to keep up with the emerging technology that keeps our businesses abreast of imported manufactured products. So, the partnership is vital to the long-term health of our industry and the potential of attracting young people into our high tech world.

The SIA focus is in the State of New South Wales and our membership is mainly concentrated in the wider Sydney region. We do have some members that are country-based. The SIA have been considering a national-based approach, representing all of our manufacturers, but that is a future project in the next five years. The newly formed SIA committee is made up of a number of industry owners, suppliers and service providers. We are the voice for our industry.
