# Getting There

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

By Air: Poznań’s Ławica airport currently services flights from almost 20 European cities. It also services flights from Warsaw and Cracow. More information can be found on the airport's website at http://www.airport-poznan.com.pl/pl/ 
Bus Connection to the Airport: "L" rapid bus line running between Main Railway Station and Ławica airport 
Bus line no. 59 running between Bałtyk (Rondo Kaponiera) – Ławica airport 
Bus line no. 48 running between: Bałtyk (Rondo Kaponiera) – Ławica airport 
Night bus line no. 242 running between Main Railway Station and Ławica airport The grounds of Poznań International Fair and the Main Railway station are near the "Bałtyk" bus stop.

By Car: Some important international road routes intersect in Poznań: E-30 – Berlin – Warsaw – Moscow and E-261 – Gdańsk – Wrocław – Prague. By Train: PKP (Polish Railways) offers direct connections from Poznań to Berlin; Düsseldorf; Frankfurt on the Main; Munich; Nuremberg; Cologne; Amsterdam; Basel; Kiev; Moscow; Odessa; Minsk; St. Petersburg and Krym. PKP Intercity is a partner of MTP. See http://rozklad-pkp.pl/bin/query.exe/pn

The second edition of the FOCAST Foundry Forum, an event organized for foundries, machine and device producers, component suppliers, service providers and science representatives, will also take place at the same time as ITM Poland. This synergy will be further boosted by the Industrial Subcontracting Exhibition and its accompanying matchmaking Subcontracting ITM meetings.

“At a time when the pressure to optimise processes, improve productivity, quality and industrial efficiency is consistently a factor in the minds of manufacturers and company owners, the ITM Poland Fair is a trigger for development and a driving force for future success. ITM Poland means modern technologies, development innovations and advanced machinery – all in one place, in a nutshell, but on a grand scale,” said the event organiser, MTP.

For further details, see the event website at http://technologie.mtp.pl/en/itm/about_itm/
