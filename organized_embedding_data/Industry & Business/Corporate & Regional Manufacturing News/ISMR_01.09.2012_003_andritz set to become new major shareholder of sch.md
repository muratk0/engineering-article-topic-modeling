# Andritz set to become new major shareholder of Schuler

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

THE AUSTRIAN company Andritz AG, based in Graz, is set to become the new major shareholder of Göppingen-based press manufacturer, Schuler. The current main shareholder, Schuler-Beteiligungen GmbH, is selling its complete 38.5 per cent shareholding to the technology and engineering group, Andritz.

The Board of Management and Supervisory Board of Schuler AG are unanimously recommending that the shareholders accept the takeover offer put forward by the Andritz Group. Both bodies feel that the price offered, at 20.00 euros per share, is appropriate in financial terms. Schuler-Beteiligungen GmbH is owned by the founding family Schuler-Voith, which will retire completely from the business of Schuler AG and thus regulate the question of succession. Andritz submitted an unsolicited takeover bid after the company’s founder, the Schuler-Voith family, sold its stock package to Andritz at the end of May 2012. That package accounts for 38.5 per cent of the outstanding shares. The takeover bid is subject to approval by several antitrust authorities. Andritz previously held almost 25 per cent of the shares in Schuler AG. ■
