# Olympic Steel hires Steven Wasil

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

OLYMPIC STEEL INC, a leading US metals service centre, has confirmed that Steven R. Wasil was named Vice President – Specialty Metals from 1 January 2017.

"Steve's commercial experience and proven success in business development make him a valuable addition to our specialty metals management team. We look forward to his contributions as we work to expand our client base and market participation," said the company.

Wasil brings more than 25 years of metals industry experience to Olympic Steel, with an emphasis on specialty metals. Most recently, he was Senior Vice President, North America at ThyssenKrupp Materials NA, formerly Acciai Speciali Terni (AST). In that role, he helped to re-launch AST's brand in North America, focusing on premium anti-fingerprint and other surface-critical applications.■
