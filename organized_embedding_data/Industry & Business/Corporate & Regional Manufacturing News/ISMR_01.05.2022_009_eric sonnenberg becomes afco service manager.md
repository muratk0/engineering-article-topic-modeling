# Eric Sonnenberg becomes AFCO service manager

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Automatic Feed Company (AFCO) has promoted Eric Sonnenberg to the position of service manager, responsible for overseeing and coordinating the service department activities and sales of replacement and spare parts.

“The service department plays a critical role in our company," said President, Nathan Weaks. “This team contributes significantly to developing and maintaining client relationships, as well as to our revenue. We know that Eric will do a great job in leading this department."

Before this appointment, Sonnenberg had been the project management coordinator. His original tenure with Automatic Feed began in 1997. During his time with AFCO, he has worked as a mechanical design engineer, a service department engineer and a project manager. He has also been instrumental in several successful AFCO equipment installations.

Sonnenberg has an associate degree in Mechanical Design/Drafting from Owens Community College (Toledo) and continued his education at The University of Toledo and Northwest State Community College, an AFCO partner in the Northwest Ohio Learning Centre for Manufacturing Sciences.

The Automatic Feed Field Service team collectively has over 200 years of experience with additional support provided by its electrical and mechanical engineers.
