# Prima opens new Technology Centre in Munich

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

The new Prima Power GmbH Tech Centre in Neufahrn, near Munich, Germany

Paolo Musante, Managing Director, Prima Power GmbH

PRIMA POWER HAS announced the official opening of its new Technology Centre in Neufahrn, near Munich. The new location is in the heart of the D-A-CH area. As a result, Prima Power is now able to better serve the entire German-speaking market and be much closer to the regions where its machines are already in use or where the group sees the highest growth potential.

The manufacturer is a supplier in the hightech field of laser and sheet metal working machinery. Its product portfolio includes 2D and 3D laser machines for cutting, welding and drilling; punching machines; combined punching/laser and punching/shearing systems; press brakes; panel benders; bending centres and Flexible Manufacturing Systems (FMS). Prima Power is the Prima Industrie Machinery Division, a group with more than 1,700 employees worldwide, manufacturing facilities in Italy, Finland, USA and China and a sales and service network in over 80 countries.

Above: The servo-electric bending cell, BCe Smart

Paolo Musante, Prima Power GmbH's managing director, stated: "The new Technology Centre will serve as a centre of excellence and will have a strong focus on customer satisfaction, since from this location we can offer our customers highly efficient support, particularly for the industrial areas of Southern Germany, Austria and Switzerland."

"Munich, as an international transport hub, is well connected to the European railway and motorway network and has the second largest airport in Germany. This is very important for us as a globally active company, especially as it means we can also now be reached relatively quickly and easily by our customers in other European countries."

Prima Power GmbH in Neufahrn employs 55 people (of whom 35 are dedicated to customer support) and covers an area of 1,800m². Around half of the area hosts the Demonstration Centre, where live demonstrations of its latest technologies and products can be viewed by guests. Two conference rooms are available for events, customer visits, meetings, seminars and also training for Prima Power engineers and employees in general.

In line with Prima Power GmbH's growth strategy, the aim is to have a team of 70 employees in the new subsidiary by 2020.

Prima Power GmbH is part of Prima Industrie, a Group with more than 1700 employees around the world and €450m revenues, 5% of which is in the D-A-CH area. In 2017, the group celebrated 40 years of activity and a record year for all main financial indicators, with a 14% increase in revenues, +18% in order intake and +83% in net profit. The opening of the new facility in Munich is part of a larger plan of investments to increase its presence in strategic markets and to expand and improve its set of Technology Centres for a better and more complete customer experience.

## Above: The servo-electric press brake, eP-1030

As part of the opening event, guests were shown a live demonstration of the Technology Centre's capabilities and attended a series of lectures on key trends in sheetmetal processing, such as 3D fiber laser cutting and Industry 4.0. Theory and practice were combined to give a wide overview of the sector.

## Above: Fibre laser cutting system, Laser Genius

In the new Technology Centre, the latest innovations in Prima Power product portfolio were on display: the rapid 3D fibre laser cutting machine, Laser Next 1530 (the smaller sister of the recently launched 2141 version); the high-performing 2D fibre laser machine, Laser Genius 1530; the combined punching and fibre laser system, Combi Genius 1530 with servoelectric technology; the servo-electric bending cell, BCe Smart, and the servo-electric press brake, eP-1030. All products are Industry 4.0 Inside for digital, interconnected and datadriven manufacturing.
