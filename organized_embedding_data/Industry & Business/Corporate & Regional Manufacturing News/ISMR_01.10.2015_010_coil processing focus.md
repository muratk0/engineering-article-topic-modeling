# Coil processing focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

THE BRADBURY CO., Inc. has announced the appointment of Alex D'Alfonso to ‘Sales Director - Coil Processing Systems’.

“With his knowledge of these systems and his past success, he will be responsible for sales-related activity for cut-to-length lines, slitting and levelling lines. He will be directing the Bradbury sales team for this industry and will also work with Harkaitz Luengo, Managing Director, Athader, to have input into the activities of the Athader sales team,” said the company.

The Bradbury Group has a complete line of entry equipment, levelling machines, hydraulic, mechanical, flying and rotary shears, surface critical lines capable of processing bright finish and stainless steel, as well as stacking and material handling solutions.
