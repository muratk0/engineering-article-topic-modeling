# Regional business award for Rhodes

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

Mark Ridgway OBE, DL the managing director of Group Rhodes, a UK capital equipment manufacturer, has won the Manufacturing Champion Award in the EEF/Aldermore Future Manufacturing Awards.

The award celebrates the achievements of individuals who, through vision, dedication, hard work and tireless involvement, have made a significant contribution to UK manufacturing. He will now go on to find out if he has won the national title at an awards gala dinner in London in April.

Underlining his long-standing commitment to manufacturing, Mark is the current President of the Manufacturing Technologies Association (MTA), the trade association for companies operating in the UK’s engineering-based manufacturing sector, and is a Council member of the Metalforming Machinery Manufacturers’ Association (MMMA).

He also sits on UKTI’s Aerospace Growth Partnership, having previously sat on the Advanced Engineering Sector Advisory Board, which covered the UK aerospace, automotive and engineering sectors. In 2010, as Interim Chair of the Board, Mark represented the sector during the World Trade Fair in China, and in 2011 he led UKTI’s Aerospace Mission to India. In 2012, Mark accepted an invitation to join the Advanced Manufacturing Supply Chain Initiative (AMSCI) Investment Board.

Developing his interest in athletics, Mark was appointed the UK’s Advanced Engineering Sector Champion for the London 2012 Olympics, his role being to encourage business engagement within this sector and to develop a business legacy. Mark himself ran internationally as a student and held several Yorkshire distance titles. He is also a qualified sky diver and open water diver. Mark was awarded an OBE in the Queen’s Birthday Honours List 2011 for Services to Industry and in 2012 was appointed a Deputy Lieutenant to the West Yorkshire Lieutenancy. ■

## Simufact expands sales in Europe

Simufact Engineering, a software provider for the simulation of production processes for manufacturing industries, is strengthening its sales team. From 1 February 2015, Michel Pereme (pictured) will be responsible for the further development of sales activities in the EMEA sales region (Europe – Middle East – Africa) as a senior sales manager. He will be closely cooperating with Stefan Zimmer, Director of EMEA APAC Operations, who holds overall responsibility for Europe and Asia. From an operating perspective, Stefan Zimmer will concentrate on the business development of the region Asia Pacific (APAC) in the future.

As the responsible manager for EMEA, Pereme will lead and support the company’s growing network of resellers and distribution partners in the region. He will also manage key forming and welding simulation accounts himself. ■

## New roles at SIFCO

SIFCO Applied Surface Concepts (ASC), a leading supplier of selective plating technology and solutions, has appointed Kristinko Cik as European Sales Manager. Based in Vienna, Kristinko will help continue the development and growth of SIFCO ASC into new markets and countries in the EMEA region.

Before joining SIFCO ASC, Kristinko worked for its Austrian partner, Lahner KG, for eight years. At Lahner, he was responsible for developing new business in Austria, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Hungary, Slovenia and Croatia, and worked with many customers using the SIFCO Process® – a portable method of selectively electroplating localised areas of metal surfaces.

SIFCO ASC recently became part of the Norman Hay Coatings Group, with sister company Surface Technology. The new Coatings Group will be headed by group managing director, Lee Shelton, who joined Norman Hay in 2000. Lee, a fluent Japanese speaker, started working for Norman Hay in its impregnation business, Ultraseal International, as director of international business, based in Japan. Following several years in key business development and leadership roles for Norman Hay in the Asia Pacific region, Lee relocated to the US in December 2012 as MD of SIFCO ASC. ■

## Wilson Tool welcomes back Palick

Wilson Tool International (www.wilsontool.com) has announced the return of Ron Palick as the organisation’s new director of sales for North America. In his new position, Palick will be responsible for building and leading Wilson Tool’s industry presence through strategic sales development initiatives throughout North America. Palick previously served as Wilson Tool’s vice president of sales and marketing from 2000 – 2006, then held executive positions at Prima Finn-Power and Haco / Atlantic before returning to Wilson Tool.

“We’re proud to welcome Ron back to the Wilson Tool family,” said Brian Robinson, CEO of Wilson Tool. “His depth of experience and proven success in strategic business development and execution is truly an asset to our national and international sales endeavours.”

Palick began his sales career with Wilson Tool twenty years ago as a national sales manager. He shares a patent for machine tool cartridge development with two other inventors. Wilson Tool International® is a leading provider of tooling solutions to enhance the performance of sheet metal fabricating machinery worldwide. ■
