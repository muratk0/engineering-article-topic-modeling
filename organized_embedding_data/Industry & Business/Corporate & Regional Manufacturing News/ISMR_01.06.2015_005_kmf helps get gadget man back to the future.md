# KMF helps get gadget man ‘back to the future’

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

WHEN THE Gadget Show presenter, Jason Bradbury, decided to buy an original Delorean sports car and convert it into a replica of the car from ‘Back to the Future’, he turned to KMF (Precision Sheet Metal) to help him.

KMF and Jason have worked together previously, with the TV star being an enthusiastic judge and compere for KMF’s Young Engineer of the Year Awards. KMF was therefore tasked with the engineering skills and enthusiasm to deliver the project on time and as close as possible to the original car from the movie, including its very own Flux Capacitor!

“We are accustomed to working to time constraints, but have to admit that this is the first time we have been called on to make a time machine,” said Terry Stanway, KMF’s purchasing director. “The project called on a bit of imagination as well as the skills of the people involved. The team had to locate and order items that it had never ordered before, and probably never will again, to make the car as authentic as possible. However, for the help and exposure that Jason brings to the KMF Young Engineer of the Year Awards it was a pleasure to be able to help him to realise this dream.”

The KMF team had a tight deadline to meet with the car due to be unveiled as part of the Gadget Show Live event which was held at the National Exhibition Centre, Birmingham (UK) from 8-12 April 2015.

“I suppose we did have a bit of leeway on delivery. If we had slipped behind schedule, we could have run the car up to 88MPH and let the technology do the rest...!” said KMF.
