# Manufacturing remit for Uguz

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Fisker, Inc., a creator of sustainable electric vehicles and advanced mobility solutions, has appointed Alpay Uguz as its senior VP of global manufacturing (reporting to Chairman and CEO, Henrik Fisker) from 14 July 2022.

In his role at Fisker, Uguz will oversee the company's global manufacturing as Fisker grows towards its goal of producing one million vehicles annually in 2027. He will focus on lean manufacturing and innovative techniques to reduce parts, starting from the earliest development phase.

"Alpay's wealth of hands-on, global manufacturing experience will boost our already ultrafast development process, creating greater efficiency by incorporating the latest in new manufacturing methods," Fisker Chairman and CEO, Henrik Fisker, commented. "Alpay and his team will integrate advanced manufacturing processes early in the vehicle design and engineering phases. We are developing a seamless approach from design to production with faster assembly times, ultimately providing high-quality vehicles optimised for cost and service to our customers."

“I'm excited to apply my knowledge of lean manufacturing deployment, new product launches and appreciation for innovation in Fisker's fast-paced environment, utilising my cross-cultural experience working in China, Germany, the UK and the U.S," added Uguz.

Before joining Fisker, Alpay Uguz worked at BMW's production facility in Spartanburg, South Carolina (USA), as the general manager of the brand's SUVs.

Fisker brought its all-electric Fisker Ocean SUV to Pebble Beach Automotive Week at Pebble Beach Concours d'Elegance from 18-21 August 2022.

The Fisker Ocean five-passenger, emissions-free SUV was at Concours Village, where visitors could see its latest technology and features. The Fisker Ocean is on track to start production in November 2022 at a carbonneutral factory in Austria.

The first 5,000 vehicles will be a limited launch edition Fisker Ocean One.■
