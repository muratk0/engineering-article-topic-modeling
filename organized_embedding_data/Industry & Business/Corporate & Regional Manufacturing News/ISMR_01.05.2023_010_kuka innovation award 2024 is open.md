# KUKA Innovation Award 2024 is open

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

The new round of the KUKA Innovation Award calls for creativity in robotics for the skilled trades.

“The best teams can implement their ideas with a KUKA robot, present them at a large trade show and win €20,000 at the end. Apply now!" German robotics specialist, KUKA explained.

"The motto of the annual innovation competition in 2024 is 'Robots for the People'. What challenges in craft init Research teams and young entrepreneurs from all over the world are invited to submit creative and innovative ideas on collaborative robotics for small and medium-sized enterprises," it continued.

Concepts can be submitted to KUKA until the end of June 2023. An international jury of experts will evaluate the best ideas and select the finalists. They will then implement their ideas with the sensitive KUKA lightweight robot LBR iisy and the new KUKA iiQKA operating and ecosystem. The robot will be provided free of charge throughout the competition. Finalists will also receive training and coaching throughout the competition.

“The final of the Innovation Award traditionally takes place at a major trade show. There, the teams present their applications to a large professional audience from industry and research as well as media representatives and investors. At the end of the trade show, the winner of the €20,000 prize is chosen," said KUKA.

"Many tasks in small and medium-sized, craft-related companies are not automated yet. The finalists' contribution shall simplify the engineering, set-up and programming or execution of tasks and processes for companies and craftsmen. In that way, the iiQKA and the LBR iisy should be made usable as a flexible tool for machine tending, glue dispensing, quality inspection or other typical automation tasks. Unleash your creativity and revolutionize how people interact and use robots in their daily work life, continued KUKA.

"Send us your innovative idea, describing use cases where robots today face major challenges in interacting with their real-world environment, including human-robot collaboration. The focus should be on introducing and demonstrating new and interactive methods to personalise and improve usability with our robot. The introduced concept should be versatile and in no way limited to one technical approach to solving a specific use case. Participants are encouraged to present ideas that may lead to or are already close to commercialisation," it added.

Applications (on PDF with max. 12 pages in English) will be accepted by e-mail to innovationaward@kuka.com up to 28 June 2023.

The LBR iisy is a cobot for all production areas. It uses the iiQKA.OS operating system, making it easy to install, configure and program. Tasks can be taught by manual guidance. Using the smart teach pendant, smartPAD pro, any additional programming tasks are, said KUKA, “as easy as using a smartphone."■
