Special Invite From the New NBT Chairman

I would like to express how honored I am to serve as the chairman of the board of directors for the Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT) Foundation for the next 12 months. In the 20 years I have spent in the steel industry, I have come to know one thing for sure: We must invest in the future in order to succeed in the present.

This year will bring new opportunities to support the future of manufacturing. I would like to cordially invite you to attend NBT’s GOLF4MFG

®

South this spring in Charlotte, North Carolina. The event is an inaugural golf outing that helps expand our reach into different parts of the country.

Since inception, NBT has awarded over $2 million in scholarships and camp grants. We hope that you will join us in making this year even more successful for NBT and the manufacturers of tomorrow.

I’ll see you on the golf course,

David Pettigrew

Work Hard, Play Harder in Charlotte

Join NBT and hit the links for a good cause at Ballantyne Country Club in Charlotte, North Carolina, April 27. GOLF4MFG South will take place at this 18-hole championship, par 72 course that offers challenging play to golfers at all skill levels. Enjoy a superior, top-quality golf course facility designed by the legendary Rees Jones.

Proceeds of GOLF4MFG South support the programs of NBT, the foundation of the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

®

, (FMA). The summer manufacturing camp program introduces students to basic manufacturing skills. NBT awards scholarships to students who have the interest and desire to pursue manufacturing careers. Both programs are steps toward solving the skilled labor shortage epidemic.

Experience a full day of golf, competition, and camaraderie. $250 includes greens fees, cart, on-course games, prizes, food, and beverages.
