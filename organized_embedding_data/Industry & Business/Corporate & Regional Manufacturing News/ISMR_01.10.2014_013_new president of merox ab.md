# New President of Merox AB

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

SSAB is creating a new Group-level role with operational responsibility for the subsidiary Merox AB, and a coordinating role for the company’s sustainability work. Maria Långberg will take up the position as President of Merox AB on 1 November 2014 and assumed responsibility for the Group´s sustainability work on 1 September 2014, reporting directly to SSAB’s President and CEO, Martin Lindqvist.

In addition to operational responsibility for Merox AB, the position also includes responsibility for coordinating the Group’s sustainability work and public affairs, including business ethics issues, environmental and energy issues, sustainability reporting and ethical investments, personnel-related sustainability issues, sustainability strategy and dialogue with politicians and members of society regarding sustainability issues.

Merox AB is a wholly-owned subsidiary of SSAB, tasked with optimising SSAB’s management of by-products, scrap and waste, with a focus on sustainability. Merox covers the entire chain, from R&D and production to marketing and sales. The operations primarily consist of three parts: recycling of material to the metallurgy process within SSAB, processing and selling material and products externally, and dealing with waste which cannot be recycled or processed into new products. Merox AB´s external sales in 2013 amounted to SEK 217 million.

Hanna Friberg, currently President of Merox AB, will take up the position as head of business development at Merox on 1 November.

SSAB’s purchase of Rautaruukki was completed on 29 July 2014. One important task will be to achieve seamless integration of Merox with Ruukki’s operations for the management of by-products, scrap and waste in the new Group. In 2013, Ruukki recorded external sales of SEK 293 million from this business. ■
