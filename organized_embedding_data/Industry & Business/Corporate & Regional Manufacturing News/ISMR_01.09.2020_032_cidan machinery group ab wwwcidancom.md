# CIDAN Machinery Group AB www.cidan.com

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

CIDAN Machinery Group is a leading manufacturer in the sheet metal industry with a strong market position in USA, Europe and other key markets. The company produces and markets the brands CIDAN, FORSTNER, nuIT and GÖTENEDS worldwide. The extensive machine portfolio ranges from power folders, coil processing and mechanical shears to software. CIDAN Machinery Group has extensive production facilities in Götene, Sweden and in Feldkirch and St. Pölten in Austria. It also has its own sales and service offices in the USA, China and Denmark.

## CAMU Srl www.camu.it

CAMU, a leading manufacturer of coil processing lines and sheet metal coil levelling since 1980, is located in Bressanvido (VI, Italy) and bases its production and assembly in two facilities (over a total of 4,200m²) with a staff of 48 people.

“We design and build innovative machines, 100% made in Italy, such as cut-to-length lines with 4H-6H leveller; slitting lines; recoiling and inspection lines; coil-to-coil; combined lines; packaging lines and feeding lines as well as special purpose machinery to satisfy the particular needs of each customer,” it told ISMR.

## CMS SpA www.cms.it

CMS Metal Technology is a brand dedicated to the production of metalworking machines and technical products, offering a wide range of complete waterjet cutting systems, pressure intensifiers and dry or wet deburring and satin finishing machines.

“Since the 1990s, thanks to the acquisition of Tecnocut and constant internal developments, CMS Metal Technology has gained strong international prestige, boasting more than 1,500 installations worldwide. It is a reliable partner to leading industries in various divisions such as automotive, aerospace, machining, furniture and industrial architecture,” said CMS SpA.

## Ficep SpA www.ficepgroup.com

FICEP offers a complete plate processing range for all size fabricators and manufacturers.

“Our Gemini systems handle light to heavy plates for thermal cutting with oxy-fuel and plasma, marking, milling, drilling, bevelling and more. Kronos are gantry thermal cutting lines to manufacture plates, gussets, stiffeners etc. from raw material, with all cutting processes in one single operation, (no interim handling and one single operator). Gemini and Kronos work as stand-alone solutions or are combined in one system for high levels of productivity; flexibility; safety; capacity; reduced total investment; operating labour; handling time and floor space,” said the Italian manufacturer.
