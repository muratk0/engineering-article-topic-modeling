# EFENDIEV RETURNS TO URAL STEEL

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

Nazim Efendiev was appointed managing director of Ural Steel (a subdivision of Metalloinvest Holding). He took over the office on 8 July 2010.

“Nazim Efendiev is an experienced and competent leader, he has excellent knowledge of the plant. Today, Ural Steel faces the serious task of keeping its share of traditional markets while expanding into new ones. The plant needs to continue modernization, increasing quality and profitability,” said Eduard Potapov, general director of Metalloinvest Management Company.

Mr Efendiev was most recently Chairman of the Board of CJSC Machine-Building Corporation, Uralmash, and was general director of Ural Steel in 2004.
