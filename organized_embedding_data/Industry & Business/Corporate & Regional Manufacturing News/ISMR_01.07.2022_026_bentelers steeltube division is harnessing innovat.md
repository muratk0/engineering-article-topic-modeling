# BENTELER's Steel/Tube division is harnessing innovation to develop new sustainable products for hydrogen distribution networks and e-mobility.

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

“BENTELER has made significant investments in a new tube drawing line at Paderborn, plant expansion at Schwandorf, an ambitious sustainability strategy and smart factory adoption.“

owned by the fourth generation of the founding family. Founded in 1876 by Carl Benteler, today the company is owned by Hubertus Benteler Ges.m.b.H. and Dr. Ing. E.h. Under the management of the strategic holding company, BENTELER International AG registered in Salzburg, Austria, business operations are organised into two divisions: BENTELER Automotive and BENTELER Steel/Tube.

BENTELER Steel/Tube develops and produces steel and seamless and welded quality steel tubes. It offers customers

worldwide solutions along the entire value chain from material development to tube applications. It creates customised tube products for the automotive, energy and industry market segments. BENTELER covers the entire supply chain from its own steel production to the tube and processing of the finished component.

Today, the BENTELER Group has around 25,000 employees in 92 locations (27 countries) worldwide. The Steel/Tube segment contributes to around 15% of its overall revenues. As a metal process specialist, BENTELER is active worldwide for customers in the automotive technology, energy and mechanical engineering sectors.

Its automotive tube solutions include rotor shafts; fluid routing lines; prop shafts; seatbelt tensioners; airbag generator sleeves and tubes for axle and steering solutions. Its products for industry applications include tube for pistons, hydraulic lines and cylinders. It also offers a wide range of tubes for energy applications.

## Driving future mobility

"We're not just metal processing specialists; we're also inventors. That is why we continuously ask ourselves how we can make tubes, components and systems even lighter, more durable and more resource-efficient worldwide, with high quality at fair prices. In short, how we can make mobility lighter, safer and more sustainable," said BENTELER.

The Minister for Economic Affairs, Innovation, Digitalisation and Energy of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, Prof. Dr. Andreas Pinkwart, visited BENTELER's Paderborn factory in April 2022. Also pictured are Ralf Göttel, CEO of the BENTELER Group, and Marco Kollmeier, Managing Director, BENTELER EV Systems.

It has now added seamless, hot-rolled tubes and pipes for the safe distribution of hydrogen to its portfolio, dubbed BENTELER HYRESIST. Designed for the construction of hydrogen distribution networks, the range helps to make the alternative energy source more readily available and, it hopes, Germany's targeted CO₂ neutrality goal of 2045 more quickly achievable. Green hydrogen is considered an attractive energy option in industry. As a result, the rapid expansion of nationwide distribution networks for hydrogen is pressing, both in Germany and throughout the EU.

"The expansion of our portfolio also underlines our sustainable orientation. We have initiated a programme aimed at reducing our CO₂ footprint in tube production. In addition to procurement and production, the product portfolio also plays a strategic role in this," emphasised Christian Wiethüchter, CSO and COO, BENTELER Steel/Tube.

BENTELER harnessed the expertise of both its divisions, Automotive and Steel/Tube, to expand its portfolio of tubes for rotor shafts to include individual, lightweight component solutions. A current example is its entry into the rotor shaft component business, which includes e-mobility applications.

The core element of an electric powertrain is the motor, the main component of which is the rotor shaft. By using tube instead of solid material, significantly less material is required in the manufacture of rotor shafts, which saves weight. BENTELER Steel/Tube produces both welded-drawn and seamless-drawn precision steel tubes for the manufacture of rotor shafts. Because of the hard steel used and individual tube thicknesses, these solutions are particularly light, safe and flexible to adapt.

"In addition to the product, innovative manufacturing processes are also becoming increasingly important. Our modular approach to finished rotor shafts offers maximum flexibility and innovative mechanical machining," added Dr. Rainer Lübbers, Head of Business Unit Engine & Exhaust Systems, BENTELER Automotive, about the strategic development.

The metal processing specialist has also developed a new car door concept that removes the need for a B-pillar. This production-ready product is lighter and offers improved safety and material features. It is based on an aluminium structure made of AlSiMg grade alloy.

Despite sharply increased raw material prices, the Steel/Tube division ended 2021 successfully.

## New tube line investment

After two years of construction, BENTELER opened a new drawing line at its Paderborn steel tube mill in Germany on 22 June 2022. It has invested a double-digit million euro sum into the new plant. With the commissioning, up to 50 jobs in parallel will be created at the new production facility for precision steel tubes. The Steel/Tube division is therefore strengthening the Schloss Neuhaus and Paderborn manufacturing cluster and expanding its market position in individual tube solutions for highly specialised applications in the automotive industry.

The new production line for welded tube applications has an annual capacity of 12,000 tons. A new, innovative infrastructure was created in the production hall for the drawing bench, which includes an integrated testing facility. More than 1,150 tons of concrete, around 31 tons of reinforcing steel and more than 1.7km of cable for supply lines weighing 7.7 tons have been installed over the past two years.

The tubes that BENTELER produces on the new line will predominantly be used in the automotive sector.

"Products that function faultlessly and have a long service life are very important in the automotive sector because they ensure greater safety for vehicle occupants. Our precision tubes meet these expectations safely and reliably, for example thanks to their excellent surface quality. Their homogeneous microstructure and tight tolerances also ensure high stability in subsequent processing. This makes us the ideal partner for suppliers to the automotive industry," explained Christian Wiethüchter, CSO and COO, BENTELER Steel/Tube. “We are delighted to strengthen Paderborn with this investment."

## Schwandorf plant expansion

BENTELER has also expanded the production area again at its Schwandorf (Germany) site, this time by around 11,500m². The ground-breaking ceremony took place on 2 June 2022.

The plant, part of BENTELER's Automotive division, produces (among other things) battery trays for electric vehicles as well as other structural and chassis components for German automotive manufacturers. BENTELER is one of the largest employers in Schwandorf. The internationally active group currently employs around 650 people at the Schwandorf site. The new expansion will also increase the number of employees.

The extension is due to be completed by January 2023, with production expected to start in September 2023. This is the third expansion within a few years. BENTELER expanded its production area in Schwandorf by around 50% in October 2018, and in October 2020 the group increased its production area by a further 7,500m².

With this latest expansion, the company is now investing once again in the production of battery trays in Germany as well as in the growth sector of electromobility.

## Sustainability champion

EcoVadis, one of the world's largest providers of sustainability ratings, awarded its silver medal to BENTELER's Steel/Tube Division in June 2022. By improving from bronze to silver status, the company is now among the top 25% of companies certified by EcoVadis in the "Manufacture of basic iron and steel" category.

"We are delighted to receive this award, which is significant in terms of our strategy. The independent rating underlines our responsible actions and sustainable management, which are central components of our corporate policy. This confirms that, with our 'Carbon Neutral Tubes' initiative, we are on the right path to transformation as a CO₂-neutral manufacturer of steel tubes," said Ralf Brunnert, Vice President of SHE & Operation Services at BENTELER Steel/Tube and Carbon Neutral Tubes programme manager.

BENTELER development engineers dive deep into the chemical composition of the materials of tomorrow.

“By 2045, we want to become CO₂ neutral and have transformed our supply chain, production and products. Independent awards such as those from EcoVadis are testimony to our sustainable actions and motivate us to continue driving our ambitious sustainability strategy with full commitment," he added.

The silver medal from EcoVadis recognizes BENTELER Steel/Tube for its activities in sustainability according to the ESG criteria of environment, social and governance. Since its foundation in 2007, EcoVadis has become the world's largest provider of sustainability ratings for companies.

The EcoVadis score is based on a defined assessment catalogue that provides a comparable overview of EcoVadis-certified companies. The assessment focuses on twenty-one criteria grouped into four thematic areas: environment, labour and human rights, ethics and sustainable procurement. These criteria are based on international CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) standards such as the ten principles of the U.N. Global Compact; the conventions of the International Labour Organisation (ILO); the standards of the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI); the ISO 26000 standard; the CERES principles and the U.N. Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights, also known as the “Ruggie Framework".

## Into the future

The future looks bright for BENTELER. Earlier this year, BENTELER EV Systems, Beep Inc., and Mobileye, an Intel Company, announced a strategic collaboration to develop and deploy automotive-grade, fully electric, autonomous movers in public and private communities across North America.

BENTELER has been investing in its innovation management as well as its own Industry 4.0 strategy for years. These include networked machines and robots, machine learning for predictive maintenance and autonomous intelligent vehicles.

In April 2022, it completed the comprehensive networking project for production processes at its Smart Factory in Mos, Spain. All processes in the factory are digitally networked and aligned with each other, from material receipt to delivery of the final product to the customer. With the network now fully completed, BENTELER can exploit the full potential of its Smart Factory.
