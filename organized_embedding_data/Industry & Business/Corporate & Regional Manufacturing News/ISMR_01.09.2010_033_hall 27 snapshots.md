## ASSERVIMENTI PRESSE SRL

I– Hall 27, Stand B 37 Website: www.asservimentipresse.it The company offers simple and double decoilers with capacities of up to 20 tons and outer diameters up to 2000mm and straighteners for material widths of up to 2000mm and strip thicknesses of up to 10mm. ## DIMECO

F – Hall 27, Stand H54

Website: www.dimeco.com

## IRON SPA

I-Hall 27, Stand C32

Website: www.iron.it

IRON SpA, the Italian family-run coil processing specialist, recently developed a new modular line for flexible punching and bending from coil (FBP). The line incorporates a punch press (with thick turret tooling), a part-turning device (after punching) with positive and negative bends possible on the punched parts. It can handle part dimensions ranging from 650mm width by 1500mm length to 1500mm width by 3600mm length. “The strip holder and bending process is completely automated – each station has four servo motors (16 in all) for faster operation and better process control. The line itself is very complex, with different coil-handling stations: a punching unit; a shear; a buffer; a tilting device and two bending stations and is designed for high production volumes,“ said the company.

## SERAPID DEUTSCHLAND GMBH

‘QDX’ (Quick Die eXchange), SERAPID’s product and engineering programme, is adapted to the general process of the quick tool exchange on stamping presses. QDX solutions improve safety and ergonomics for operators while increasing productivity and thus enhance the efficiency of the entire operation: die clamping, die loading and positioning. Besides products such as die transport equipment, die clamping systems and rollway devices, it offers complete solutions including design, configuration and support. At EuroBLECH 2010, SERAPID will show a large range of its standard products and will introduce its new GPO truck offering a larger range of options.

## WEMO NEDERLAND B.V.

## NL– Hall 27, Stand H42

## Website: www.wemo.nl

WEMO develops and builds special machines for punching, bending and joining of blanks/coil material. It can also integrate automatic vision control systems in its machines. At EuroBLECH 2010, the production of heater covers in several sizes will be shown. Starting from pre-painted coil, the material is punched and cut. During cutting, the head side is also bent and joggled. De-burring is done by rolling and double-folding. The sides will be double-folded and then bent with a small and large radius. A mounting rail is positioned into the product and is attached by self-clinching studs. Further handling will be done by a robot.
