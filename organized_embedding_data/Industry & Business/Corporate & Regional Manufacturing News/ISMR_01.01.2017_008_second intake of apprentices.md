# Second intake of apprentices

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

PRESS SPECIALIST SCHULER'S Cedual training centre in Mexico now also trains specialist staff for the stamping industry on an 18-month course.

“Those armed with a qualification from Cedual rest assured of the best employment prospects. This also applies to the second intake of industrial engineers and toolmakers, who were celebrated leaving our dual training centre in the Mexican city of Puebla recently. Over the past three years, the new graduates have acquired the expertise and capabilities that are in demand in the rapidly growing automobile and supplier industry in Mexico," Schuler told ISMR.

Alongside Schuler management, the Sub-Director of the Mexican Chamber of Commerce, Andreas Müller, as well as the Chairman of the Supervisory Board of Allgaier Werke GmbH, Prof. Dr. Dieter Hundt, joined in the celebrations.

“Dual training, according to the German model, shall continue to form the basis at Cedual in future. Nevertheless, it has also transpired that there is a need for shorter training courses. With this in mind, we now also offer a course spanning just 18 months, like the 'Specialist in Metal Technology' qualification recognised by the German Chamber of Industry and Commerce which will continue to meet the high German standard of quality," explained Cedual manager, Irving Maciel.

A customer workshop was set up to uncover customer requirements. Automotive manufacturer, Ford, has already decided to sign up 21 of its Mexican employees for a basic course at Cedual. Additional companies such as Audi, Allgaier, ThyssenKrupp, LuK, Gestamp and PWO are also letting their junior team members attend courses at Cedual. The training centre is equipped with drilling, turning, milling and grinding machines, numerous workstations, an audiovisual lecture theatre and three classrooms, and offers a total of 90 spaces. Theoretical and practical phases are alternated, in a similar manner to the dual training concept driven in Germany. See www.schuler.mx
