## New Chairman for AIMHE

A MAJOR announcement during the recent BIEMH machine tool exhibition in Bilbao was made by the Association of Spanish Sector Importers (AIMHE), which held its annual AGM to renew the association’s Board of Directors.

With representation from most of the 30 associated companies, Jorge Garcia of FEGEMU was appointed new Chairman by majority vote. Through this appointment, two of the AIMHE main drivers from the onset, Antonio Postigo of Maquinser and Xabier Aranbarri of Delteco, now pass the torch to a new Board. The new Board includes Eduard Farran, of Emuge Franken, as Vice-Chairman; Eduardo Matesanz, of DTC Tecnología, as Secretary and Antonio Jover, of Daunert Máquinas-Herramienta, as Treasurer.

With a total number of 1,314 exhibitors from 27 countries, almost 800 high-performance machine tools and 300 news releases and keynote speeches, the 28th Spanish Machine-Tool Biennial Exhibition also attracted 35,500 visitors. ■

## Photofabrication appoints technical director

PHOTOFABRICATION LIMITED, a UK chemical etching company, has confirmed that Tom Pounds has joined the company as technical director. Tom will initially concentrate on further improving internal processes using LEAN thinking, Six Sigma reduction in variation techniques and tools. He will also advise on technical projects to help to resolve customer issues and develop fitting solutions for both prototype and production requirements.

The privately-owned company is a specialist in the development of photochemical technology to produce intricately-shaped components from thin sheet metal. More than 60,000 different components have been produced to date. Services offered include, chemical etching (also known as photo etching and chemical milling), plating, laser cutting, metal stamping and wire erosion.

Tom spent the last 13 years at Cambridge Display Technology (CDT) where he led process development and was a member of the management team. Prior to CDT, Tom spent two years at Proctor & Gamble as a member of the upstream process innovation team, evaluating emerging technologies for future business application and new FMCG products.

See www.photofab.co.uk for further information. ■

## New leadership for MIDEST

THE PRESIDENCY of MIDEST alternates every two years between a representative from the trade associations and a representative from the chambers of commerce. For the 2014 and 2015 shows, the associations have therefore nominated Patrick Munini to take over from Jérôme Delabre. Besides roles in FIM, the Mechanical Engineering Federation, he is the founder of Mondragon Assembly, an SME specialising in vision and robotics applied to special assembly and process machinery, and the President of Amics-E&pi, the machining and special machinery trade association.

The new director of MIDEST, Jean-François Sol-Dourdin, is also in charge of Expoprotection, the risk prevention and management show, and APS – Alarmes Protection Sécurité. He took over the reins of MIDEST in January, when he became director of the new Industry, Safety and Security Division, and replaces Sylvie Fourn, who is taking on new roles in Reed Expositions France but remains linked to the show as the director of the Industry, Construction, Environment, Health and Safety cluster.

The 44th MIDEST show will run from 4-7 November 2014 at the Paris Nord Villepinte Exhibition Centre and now has a new president, in Patrick Munini, and a new director, Jean-François Sol-Dourdin. It will also be shining a spotlight for the first time on a North African country, Tunisia, and focusing on a sector that is a driving force for European industry, aerospace. ■

## German moves for AutoForm

AUTOFORM Engineering GmbH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has opened a new office in Stuttgart. This office will enable AutoForm to be closer to its customers in the local region and allow for more efficiency in the supply of AutoForm’s range of products and services. In addition, it recently appointed a new general manager, Michael Rembrink, to head its offices in Germany.

Mr. Rembrink holds a Master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering and a Master’s degree in Business Administration from IESE Business School in Barcelona. Before joining AutoForm, he was employed in the operations and management of various leading vendors in the PLM industry. After joining AutoForm Engineering in 2008 as a consulting team manager, he moved on to become the general manager of AutoForm USA. In his new position as the general manager in Germany, he will be responsible for the successful and continued business growth of AutoForm Engineering Deutschland GmbH in Stuttgart.

See www.autoform.com ■

## New Board appointments

EUROBLECH’S show director, Nicola Hamann, has been appointed to the Board as managing director for Mack Brooks Exhibition’s extensive German business. She has been show director of several international trade exhibitions at Mack Brooks Exhibitions for ten years, including leading brands such as EuroBLECH, inter airport Europe and ICE Europe International Converting Exhibition, all held in Germany. Her sphere of responsibility now covers the company’s complete portfolio of exhibitions held in Germany, which has strongly expanded over the past six years. This year’s EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition will take place in Hanover, Germany from 21-25 October 2014.

Mike Cooke has joined the Board as consultant on strategies to expand the company’s business in the USA. The other Board positions at Mack Brooks Exhibitions remain unchanged, with Simon Adkins as finance director, David Tellett as managing director and Hakan Gershagen as consultant. Chairman of the Board is company owner, Stephen Brooks.

The Mack Brooks Exhibitions Group has its headquarters in St Albans, England and maintains offices in selected regional markets, such as the USA, Brazil, China, India, Russia and Thailand.

See www.mackbrooks.co.uk or www.euroblech.com ■
