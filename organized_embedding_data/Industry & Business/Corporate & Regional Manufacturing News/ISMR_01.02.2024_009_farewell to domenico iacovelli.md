# Farewell to Domenico Iacovelli

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

The ANDRITZ Supervisory Board and Schuler CEO, Domenico Iacovelli, mutually agreed upon amicable terms on his departure as of 31 December 2023. Within the ANDRITZ Group, the President and CEO of ANDRITZ, Dr. Joachim Schönbeck, is now responsible for the Metals business area, to which Schuler belongs, and will be working closely with managing directors Thomas Kamphausen and Dr. Peter Jost.

"We sincerely thank Domenico Iacovelli for his commitment and outstanding achievements especially in the successful restructuring of the Schuler Group, the strategic development of battery technology for the electric vehicle industry and Andritz's entry into the green hydrogen market," said Joachim Schönbeck, President and CEO of ANDRITZ. Schuler offers customised technology in all areas of forming from the networked press to pressshop planning. Its products also include automation, dies, process expertise and service for the entire metalworking industry. The company is part of the international technology group, ANDRITZ.
