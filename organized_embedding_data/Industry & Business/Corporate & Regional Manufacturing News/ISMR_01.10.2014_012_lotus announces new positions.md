# Lotus announces new positions

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

LOTUS recently announced two new appointments.

Jean-Charles Lievens has joined the Cars Commercial team to develop the Lotus network in France, Italy, Spain and Portugal. He has 37 years of experience in the car industry and distribution, having occupied senior positions at Toyota Europe and KIA Europe. His mission is to strengthen the network with qualified and professional partners to optimise and increase the presence of the Lotus brand in the Southern European region.

Jean Marc Gales, Chief Executive Officer of Group Lotus plc, said, “We are growing our dealer network throughout Asia, North America and Europe. The appointment of Jean-Charles Lievens comes at an appropriate time. We are looking to increase our presence in Southern Europe where there is a high demand for Lotus sports cars.”

It also announced the appointment of Miguel Fragoso as Engineering Director. He will have overall responsibility for all aspects of engineering at Lotus and be based at Lotus’ Headquarters in Hethel, Norfolk reporting to the CEO of Group Lotus plc, Jean-Marc Gales.

A Chartered Engineer and Fellow of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers, Miguel has held a number of senior roles with the automotive industry, most recently as CEO of Millbrook Proving Ground where he grew and diversified Millbrook from a proving ground and test site to a complete vehicle engineering development centre. Prior to the role at Millbrook, he held senior positions in engineering and corporate planning at General Motors in the UK, mainland Europe and Japan.

Lotus Cars announced a 31% rise in global sports car sales for the first financial quarter of 2014, its best for three years. ■

## KMT extends service network

IN the first six months of 2014, KMT Waterjet Systems expanded its service team with three new service engineers. The new engineers are responsible for sales areas in Italy, Russia and Eastern Europe.

“To guarantee smooth operation of a waterjet cutting machine, regular servicing of the high pressure components of the system is recommended. If, however, a repair becomes necessary, it is important that the responsible service technician reacts quickly so that machine downtime is kept to a minimum. We maintain a global network of service offices that can provide their customers with qualified and quick 24/7 support for all issues covering high pressure components for waterjet cutting,” KMT told ISMR. ■

## Leadership change at Applied Robotics

APPLIED ROBOTICS, INC., a leading global manufacturer of robotic end-of-arm tooling and connectivity solutions, has announced the appointment of John Serfilippi as President and Interim Chief Executive Officer upon the retirement of Michael F. Quinn, P.E. effective from 1 August 2014.

Quinn, who joined Applied Robotics, Inc. after 45 years of senior management and technical experience at the Knolls Atomic Power Laboratory supporting the US Naval Nuclear Programme, has played a substantial leadership role in re-shaping ARI for long term success.

“Applied Robotics will benefit from John’s expertise and dedication to the employees and company as well as his strong commitment to the success and growth of ARI and our joint venture in Europe, ARE. I am absolutely confident that the company is in good hands,” said Quinn.

Mr. Serfilippi has over 30 years of corporate experience, seven years with ARI and, until recently, was the director of operations. Serfilippi brings a broad-based experience to the position which includes engineering, manufacturing and sourcing management. See www.appliedrobotics.com ■
