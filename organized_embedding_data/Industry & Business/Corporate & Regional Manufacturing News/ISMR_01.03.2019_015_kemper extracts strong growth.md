# KEMPER extracts strong growth

[TARİH: 01.03.2019 ISMR]

KEMPER GMBH CONTINUES on its growth course; 2018 became the most successful financial year in the company's history. For the first time, the manufacturer of extraction units and filter systems surpassed the sales mark of 40 million euros. It boosted sales of central filter systems by more than 25 per cent compared to 2017. The family-owned company increased its sales with a 13 per cent jump compared to 2017. With a total turnover of more than 40 million euros, the manufacturer recorded the best year in the company's history in terms of revenues.

"Over the past eight years, we have doubled turnover – an outstanding development in an industry that is characterised by rather moderate growth rates. We intend to build on this trend and increase sales by half their current level by 2025," KEMPER told ISMR.

While Germany remained its most important sales market for extraction technology, the European markets of the Czech Republic; UK; France; The Netherlands; Poland and Spain, in particular, contributed to the company's growth in 2018. Compared to 2017, sales of extraction plants and filter systems grew by 25 per cent.

The shortage of skilled workers is one of the main drivers behind the development of KEMPER. "This gives the issue of occupational health and safety new relevance in manufacturing companies," said Frederic Lanz, new CEO of Marketing & Sales at KEMPER. "Effective extraction technology means companies that use technology such as ours are signalling that the health of their employees is important to them."

By further digitalising its portfolio, the company aims to create added value for its customers. In 2019, KEMPER is bringing new IoT-compatible solutions to market readiness. The company will present these solutions at trade fairs in Switzerland and abroad – the next is at BLE.CH in Berne from 5–7 March 2019. See www.kemper.eu
