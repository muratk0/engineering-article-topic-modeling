# Board of Directors elected for VDMA's WG Laser

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

Management and Board of Directors of the Working Group Laser and Laser Systems for Material Processing. Image source: VDMA.

At the Autumn meeting of the VDMA Working Group Laser and Laser Systems for Material Processing (WG Laser), the members held an election to complete the Board of Directors. The Chairman and Deputy Chairman were also confirmed.

Dr. Hagen Zimer, Member of the Management Board and CEO Laser Technology, TRUMPF, and Nikolas Meyer, Head of Sales and Industry Management and Member of the Executive Board, Weil Technology, were newly elected to the Board.

Dr. Christoph Ullmann was confirmed as Chairman of the WG Laser and Dr. Stefan Ruppik was confirmed as his deputy. The newly elected Board of Directors is made up as follows:

- Nikolas Meyer, Head of Sales and Industry Management/Member of the Executive Board, Weil Technology GmbH.
- Dr. Stefan Ruppik, Vice President and General Manager High Power CO₂ Lasers & Profile Welding Systems, Coherent.
- Dr. Christoph Ullmann, General Manager, Laserline GmbH.

■Dr. Hagen Zimer, Member of the Management Board and CEO Laser Technology, TRUMPF SE + Co KG.

The VDMA Working Group Laser and Laser Systems for Material Processing (WG Laser) represents the interests of 50 companies from Germany, Austria and Switzerland vis-à-vis politicians, business partners, the interested public and the media.

The circle of member companies includes manufacturers of beam sources; laser processing systems; suppliers of components with direct reference to the end application as well as research institutes and universities. WG Laser has been active since 1988 as an organisationally independent grouping within the VDMA.

As the largest industrial association in Europe, the VDMA (with more than 3,600 members and liaison offices in Brazil, China, India, Japan and the United States) has a worldwide network in mechanical engineering and its customer sectors.■
