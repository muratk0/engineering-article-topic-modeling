# Management change at Bystronic

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

Long-time Bystronic CEO, Alex Waser, has elected to retire as CEO. He will be succeeded by Domenico Iacovelli, who will assume the role of CEO from 1 July 2024.

Under Waser, Swiss sheet-metal specialist Bystronic more than doubled its revenues in the last ten years. It grew organically and through strategic acquisitions, including China-based machine producer, DNE, and Spanish software company, Kurago. Under Waser's leadership, Bystronic has evolved from a company manufacturing sheet metal machines to a global full-solution provider in the sheetmetal industry.

Iacovelli brings extensive management and leadership experience, having spent 13 of his 20 years in the sheet metal industry in CEO roles. In 2018, he was named CEO of Schuler AG and, before that, CEO of Andritz Soutec. In April 2022, he was given additional responsibilities and named a Member of the Executive Committee for ANDRITZ, as well as Head of Business Area Metals and Group Head of IT and Automation & Digitalisation.

"Alex executed successfully on Bystronic's growth strategy, expanded our global footprint and strengthened the company's market offerings and services. We are grateful for his many contributions," said Bystronic Board Chairman, Heinz Baumgartner. "We look forward to supporting Domenico in his new role and ensuring a smooth transition. His industry knowledge and market experience will strengthen Bystronic and will be great assets as we continue to execute on our growth strategy."

Bystronic will publish its Annual Report 2023 on 29 February 2024. The company's operational headquarters are at Niederönz (Switzerland). Additional development and production sites are in Sulgen (Switzerland); Gotha (Germany); Cazzago San Martino and San Giuliano Milanese (Italy); Tianjin, Shanghai and Shenzhen (China) and Hoffman Estates (USA).
