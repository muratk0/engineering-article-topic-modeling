Marcegaglia, which is celebrating its 60th anniversary, has introduced new steel grades and finishes to its range of flat steel products, as well as a new app.

Marcegaglia is a leading industrial group in the steel processing sector, with a turnover of over €5.2 billion. The group operates worldwide with 6,500 employees, 60 sales offices and 24 steel plants, serving more than 15,000 customers. It is currently celebrating its 60th anniversary.

Founded in 1959, Marcegaglia is an independent player in the steel industry, driven (it told ISMR) by strong values: innovation, high-quality products, customer care, human capital and sustainability.

Recently, the company website (www.marcegaglia.com) was enhanced with two new areas - quality and EEHS (Environment, Energy, Health and Safety) - and a new app is now available to update everyone on the group's innovations and initiatives.

Over the last sixty years, Marcegaglia has built up its competitive position among leading steel producers by offering a wide range of carbon steel and stainless steel products, cold-drawn bars and heavy quarto plates, with global annual production of 5.8 million tons.

## New grades and finishes

"Marcegaglia Carbon Steel's processed flat rolled division is one of the largest independent service centres in terms of volume delivered and product range," explained Lorenzo Biagi, its sales and marketing director (pictured above).

"While respecting the role of distribution and continuing to sell commodity products, we are enlarging our clientele to large, mill direct end-users. Using cross training, better product knowledge and our ability to offer new solutions, we have been able to significantly grow our presence in premium markets such as automotive or yellow goods, creating added value for Marcegaglia and more efficient business models designed to reduce the total cost of ownership for our customers."

"As we predicted a few years ago," Biagi continued, "the market is becoming much more structured than in the past, with very few imports and the more dominant presence of a few domestic players. In this context, we play an important role as one of the largest, but still lean, independent alternatives to the newly-formed conglomerates."

In this evolving scenario, Marcegaglia Carbon Steel has added new grades and finishes to its wide range of flat steel products.

In particular, hot rolled micro-alloys strips have been introduced, complying with EN 10149-2, with high yield strength for cold forming in a wider range of grades from S280MC up to S700MC, able to provide tolerances for very narrow thicknesses, compared to EN 10051.

Its production range of cold rolled products has also been extended, with the introduction of low carbon steel for enamelling by vitrification, complying with EN 10209, in EK1 and EK4 grades. Its semi-finished range of steels for magnetic applications has also been developed, responding to the needs of a market that is increasingly concerned about energy efficiency. New grades of hot-galvanized strips for structural uses have also been introduced, currently available from S220GD to S550GD with zinc coatings up to Z800.

## Expanding its portfolio

Marcegaglia Carbon Steel is now able to offer stock availability of pickled sheets S235JR / DD11 (thicknesses from 1.5 to 15mm – widths of up to 2000mm) and the new S355J0WP grade (thicknesses from 2 to 10mm – widths of 1,500mm); S355K2 + N black sheet (thicknesses from 3 to 15mm – widths of up to 2,000mm) and extended grades of up to S700MC. It also offers LASER SHARP®, a range of structural sheets for laser applications suitable for special finishes (thicknesses from 1.5 to 15mm - widths of up to 2000mm).

The company is a premium partner for the automotive industry: it developed the HCT780X (DP800) galvanized grade, for which it is still the only Italian manufacturer. The company is also able to supply HCT490X and HCT590X grades complying with EN 10346, and has developed Bake-Hardening with the galvanized HX220BD grade for use on unseen parts.
