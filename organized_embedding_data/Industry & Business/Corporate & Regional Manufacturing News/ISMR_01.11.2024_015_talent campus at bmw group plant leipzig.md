# Talent Campus at BMW Group Plant Leipzig

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Every year, 65 young people start their vocational training at BMW Group Plant Leipzig (Germany). In the future, more than 6,200 employees a year will also develop their skills in specifically tailored courses and seminars. For the first time, Leipzig's new Talent Campus brings together vocational training and skills development under one roof. The new building officially opened on 25 October 2024.

The Talent Campus offers 6,000m² of space in total across two floors. For those doing their vocational training, it is equipped with automotive, CNC, mechatronics and mechanical workshops as well as spaces for pneumatics, mechatronics and IT/AR/VR. For employees undergoing skills development, it offers e-mobility workshops for SPS control technologies, robotics and automation as well as practical training multifunctional spaces and seminar rooms. The building is designed to be accessible and, with equipment, clocked up an investment of 16 million euros.

Nicole Haft-Zboril, Head, BMW Group Real Estate Management, commented: "This new Talent Campus is an investment in a flexible, needs-focused training infrastructure at our Leipzig site. The construction project has been successfully completed—thanks in large part to our collaboration with our partner, the City of Leipzig—and the building has been handed over in time for the start of the academic year."

The Talent Campus offers training and development for a wide range of participants, from apprentices to managers. Courses and programmes centre on working with highvoltage electricity for new battery systems and on digitalisation, with a particular focus on artificial intelligence, to develop the skills that will be vital in the car industry of the future.

BMW Group Plant Leipzig is steadily growing in terms of employees, buildings and production volume. When it opened in 2005, it employed around 2,600 people. Today, there are over 6,000. Plant director, Petra Peterhänsel, explained: "Investments in training and development are key to the transformation of our industry. We are securing the future of our site by fostering young talent and offering attractive skills development options to keep our longer-standing employees with us on our journey to electric and digital mobility."

Over the last three years, the training rate at Plant Leipzig has risen by 44 per cent. The site currently offers five apprenticeships: production mechanic, IT system electronics technician, automotive mechatronics technician, mechatronics technician and industrial mechanic.

"This year, BMW Group Plant Leipzig is also home to the state of Saxony's Best Apprentice," concluded the manufacturer.■
