# Uwe Keiter joins VDMA's main Board

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Rockwell Automation, Inc., with a focus on industrial automation and digital transformation, has announced that Uwe Keiter, managing director and OEM sales director of Rockwell Automation in Germany, was recently elected to the main Board of the VDMA (machinery and plant engineering industry association in Germany).

“It is a great honour and responsibility, for Rockwell Automation and myself, to have been elected to the VDMA main Board," said Keiter. "The machinery and plant engineering industry is facing major challenges. We can contribute our expertise in automation, digitalisation and artificial intelligence to help shape the future of German industrial production engineering and therefore strengthen the industry's competitiveness."

The election took place on 11 October 2024, during the VDMA general assembly.

“Keiter has many years of experience in the automation industry and a comprehensive understanding of the challenges and opportunities of digitalisation in the industry. His priorities will include driving digital transformation in manufacturing, fostering a highly skilled automation technology workforce and enabling sustainable production through the integration of intelligent technologies," outlined Rockwell Automation.

Rockwell Automation also recently appointed Gustavo Zecharies as its president for the Europe, Middle East and Africa (EMEA) region. Zecharies will report to Scott Genereux, SVP and Chief Revenue Officer, and succeeds Susana Gonzalez, who leaves Rockwell to pursue other opportunities.

In this role, based in Spain, Zecharies will lead and provide strategic direction for sales, services and solutions in the EMEA region with a focus on growing the original equipment manufacturer segment, which accounts for a significant portion of the region's business. He will drive revenue growth, strategic initiative performance, improved sales force effectiveness, and people development in the organisation. Most recently, he served as regional vice president, Latin America, in Global Sales & Marketing for Rockwell Automation.

Rockwell Automation, Inc. and Microsoft Corp. recently announced an expanded strategic collaboration aimed at "revolutionising industrial transformation. Together, the two companies will provide manufacturing customers with advanced cloud and AI solutions to deliver data insights, streamline operations and enhance scalability.
