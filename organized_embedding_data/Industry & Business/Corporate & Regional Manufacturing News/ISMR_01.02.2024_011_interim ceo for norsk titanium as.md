# Interim CEO for Norsk Titanium AS

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Norsk Titanium AS has announced the appointment of its Chief Technology Officer and industry veteran, Carl Johnson, as Interim Chief Executive Officer. He has been the CTO of Norsk Titanium since 2016 and is closely involved in developing RPD®, the company's proprietary additive manufacturing process that delivers structural titanium parts to aerospace, defence and other industries.

"Carl was instrumental in getting the first RPD® parts on the Boeing 787 programme and continues to play a leading role in the ongoing qualification efforts with Airbus and other customers. Before joining Norsk Titanium, he had a distinguished 30+ year career at Northrop Grumman. He is a two-time recipient of the prestigious Robert J. Collier Trophy, presented to those who have made 'The greatest achievement in aeronautics or astronautics in America'. Carl holds a BSME from Northern Arizona State University, an MSME from Colorado State University and an MBA from the University of California, Los Angeles," confirmed the company.

Mike Canario will leave Norsk Titanium and assume the position as Chief Executive Officer of Qarbon Aerospace, a manufacturer of composite components and assemblies.

"We want to thank Mike for his contributions during his more than five-year tenure. We wish him the best of luck with his next endeavour," added Andersen. The Board of Directors has initiated the search for a permanent CEO.
