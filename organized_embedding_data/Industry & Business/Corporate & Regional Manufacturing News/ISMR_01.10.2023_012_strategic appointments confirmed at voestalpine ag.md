# Strategic appointments confirmed at voestalpine AG

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Left to right: Herbert Eibensteiner, Hubert Zajicek, Franz Kainersdorfer and Reinhard Nöbauer.

At its meeting on 29 August 2023, the Supervisory Board of voestalpine AG reached a decision to extend the mandates of three members of its Management Board, and appointed two new members.

Herbert Eibensteiner remains the Chairman of the Management Board of voestalpine AG for another five years. He is currently responsible for the "classic" CEO functions such as strategy; strategic environmental management/sustainability; R&D and M&A. He has been a Member of the Management Board of voestalpine AG since 2012 and its chairman since 2019.

The mandates of Hubert Zajicek, as Head of the Steel Division, and Franz Kainersdorfer, as Head of the Metal Engineering Division, were also extended by a further five years each.

Reinhard Nöbauer will become a Member of the Management Board from 1 April 2024, and the new Head of the High-Performance Metals Division. He succeeds Franz Rotter, who will retire on 31 March 2024. His time on the Management Board will initially run for three years.

The Supervisory Board will decide promptly on a successor to Robert Ottel, who declined an offer of the Supervisory Board to extend his contract as CFO. Ottel's period in office ends on 31 March 2024. The successor to Peter Schwab who, at his own request, will not be extending his Management Board contract beyond the current period in office (expiring on 31 March 2024) will be announced shortly.

"Herbert Eibensteiner has successfully guided the voestalpine Group through very challenging times and presented record figures in the past two business years. His remit continues to cover the Group's long-term strategic orientation. Sustainability, digitalisation and diversity remain—more than ever—in focus. Under his leadership, voestalpine is taking the first big step toward green steel production with greentec steel. The Supervisory Board is certain that the Management Board team will convincingly accomplish this challenge under his leadership," commented Wolfgang Eder, Chairman of the Supervisory Board of voestalpine AG.

Voestalpine is a leading steel and technology group which operates globally and has around 500 Group companies and locations in over 50 countries on all five continents. The company is also a specialist in railway systems, tool steel and special sections. It has a clear plan for decarbonising steel production with its greentec steel programme. In business year 2022/23, the Group generated revenue of 18.2 billion euros, with an operating result (EBITDA) of 2.5 billion euros.■
