# Oil mist extraction

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

UK-BASED oil mist extraction manufacturer, Filtermist International, will be introducing its latest product development to the UK market at Southern Manufacturing in Farnborough (13 – 14 February 2013). The CSX was launched in the US market by its distributor Royal Products at the IMTS show in Chicago last September.

Filtermist will be showcasing the new CSX portable oil mist extraction system as well as its latest range of small centrifugal oil mist extractors – the ‘S’ Series. It will also be displaying products from parent company, Absolent AB.

The CSX from Filtermist is a low level combined stand that includes an integral cyclone separator, making it suitable for heavy duty applications such as wet grinding. The system draws the air containing the coolant and grinding dust into the low level stand, where it can be easily removed – the pre-filtered air then passes through the extraction unit as usual, increasing the efficiency of the operation.

The new stand has been developed to enable ongoing servicing to be carried out at ground level, removing the need to use ladders, hoists or platforms to maintain the equipment. Its compact footprint makes it suitable for manufacturing facilities where space is an issue and the integral separator in the CSX increases the service intervals by removing large amounts of oil and debris before it enters the Filtermist unit.

Filtermist will also be presenting a range of its oil smoke filters specifically designed for the heavy smoke and fine mist produced by high pressure coolant systems, die casting and heat treatment applications.
