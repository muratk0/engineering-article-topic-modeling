# Schuler holds Tech Day in Mexico

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

Press specialist, Schuler, has developed solutions for the digitisation and networking of forming technology with its new MSP 400 servo press and its process monitoring system for hot stamping systems. They are part of the "Smart Press Shop," Schuler's answer to Industry 4.0 ("Industrial Internet of Things", IIoT). The main benefits for press operators were discussed with visitors at Schuler's Tech Day at the end of November in Puebla, Mexico.

"The digital transformation of the press shop is already well underway," said Carlos Valdés, CEO of Schuler in Mexico. "Not just major car manufacturers, but medium-sized suppliers can also use the Smart Press Shop for more efficient production and fewer rejected parts. We are therefore putting forming technology on the fast track to the digital future."

In the morning, Schuler celebrated its fourth group of apprentices completing their vocational training at the Cedual Training Center.

The 16 industrial engineers and toolmakers prepared themselves for their job with theoretical and practical phases based on the German model. The training centre, which Schuler founded in 2012, is equipped with a teaching workshop as well as an audiovisual lecture theatre and three classrooms.
