# CECIMO is part of new KET group

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

A NEW High Level Group (HLG) on KETs (Key Enabling Technologies) was launched recently to assist the European Commission in the implementation of its European strategy to boost the industrial production of KETs-based products in Europe. KETs cover nanotechnology, micro-/nanoelectronics, industrial biotechnology, advanced materials, photonics and advanced manufacturing systems, and they play an essential role in enhancing the competitiveness of a wide range of sectors including, amongst others, automotive, aerospace, energy and health in Europe.

CECIMO was invited by the Commission to become a member of the group as representative of the advanced manufacturing technologies sector. Javier Eguren, former CECIMO President, was appointed as member and Filip Geerts, CECIMO Director General, in his capacity as Sherpa. CECIMO is the European Association of the Machine Tool Industries which brings together 15 national associations of machine tool builders, which covers more than 97 per cent of total machine tool production in Europe and about one third worldwide.

KETs and the industrial production of KETs-based products in Europe. A first HLG on KETs delivered a report in June 2011 setting out a shared long-term strategy for the deployment of KETs in Europe. The report constitutes the basis of the European Commission’s strategy on KETs adopted in June 2012.

The scope of issues to be discussed by the High Level Group is broad and covers research and innovation, financing, trade and competition, skill and regional policies. Many measures have already been put in place by the Commission to implement the KETs’ strategy. An MoU was also signed between the European Commission and the European Investment Bank (EIB) to pave the way for improved access to finance for investments in key enabling technologies. ■

## THE ITALIAN TECHNOLOGY

CENTRE was inaugurated on 6 March 2013. The new Centre, with its office in Pune (India), was created for the promotion of the Italian capital goods industry and supports eleven machine tool producers/companies that have signed a networking agreement.

ITC-PUNE supports these eleven Italian companies in their efforts to penetrate the Indian market. Promotion is also shared by UCIMU-Sistemi Per Produrre and ASSOCOMAPLAST, two Italian manufacturing associations.

At the ceremony, attended by Massimo Carboniero, VP, UCIMU-Sistemi Per Produrre and owner of OMERA, one of the project partner companies, many Italian and Indian representatives of the institutions and the companies were also present.

The eleven companies taking part in the project are: BLM SpA (tube processing machines); Colgar SpA (press brakes, boring/milling machines); Guiseppe Giana SpA (CNC lathes and boring/honing machines); Istech Segratrici SpA (band saws); Losma SpA (air/coolant filtration systems); Millutensil SpA (die and mould spotting presses, die splitters, press equipment, coil lines, CTL, strip cutting); OMERA Srl (hydraulic/mechanical presses, trimming/beading machines, punching machines); Produtech Srl (cutting/punching/fiber laser lines from coil); Rosa Ermando SpA (grinding machines); Streparava (automotive components) and Tria SpA (granulators).

During the initial period, the Centre will deal with principal activities such as the promotion of the partner and ITC brands, the development of new contacts with Indian institutions and the creation of a first post-sale assistance point. ■
