# Metallic bipolar plate line

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

From 4-5 October 2022, the German Fuel Cell Cooperation (GFC) showcased “a highly productive production line" for metallic bipolar plates to the public for the first time at the Hydrogen + Fuel Cells Europe 2022 event in Hanover, Germany.

By combining their respective competencies, the three companies—Von Ardenne (Dresden), Weil Technology (Müllheim) and Zeltwanger (Tübingen)—as partners in the GFC, offer manufacturers of fuel-cell systems and electrolysers expertise for the efficient production of metallic bipolar plates.

The three equipment providers combine their respective expertise along the entire process chain to support the production of fuel-cell systems and electrolysers. They bring decades of experience from their core business and combine this to form one seamless process.

Von Ardenne provides its expertise in the development and production of vacuum coating systems, which are used to functionalise the surfaces of metal strip (pre-coating) or bipolar plate (post-coating). Weil Technology is available as an experienced partner with its knowledge of laser cutting and laser welding of metal bipolar plates. Zeltwanger's expertise in automated assembly and testing processes, leak testing and laser applications is useful at various points along the production chain.

Fuel-cell stack.

"The concept of a fully coordinated production line plays a pioneering role in the market. With an annual production capacity of several million bipolar plates, it serves the current needs of the industry," they told ISMR.■
