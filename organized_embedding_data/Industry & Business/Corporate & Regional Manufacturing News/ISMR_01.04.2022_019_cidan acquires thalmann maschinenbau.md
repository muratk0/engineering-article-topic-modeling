# CIDAN acquires Thalmann Maschinenbau

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

CIDAN Machinery Group has acquired Swiss long folding specialist, Thalmann Maschinenbau AG. This acquisition adds another brand to its portfolio, which already includes CIDAN, Forstner and nuIT.

"Thalmann's headquarters in Switzerland will remain the major hub for long folder innovation and production. The 50 Thalmann employees will be joining the CIDAN Machinery Group and remain employed at Frauenfeld. We are committed to building on the strengths of the existing team with the aim of further growing the long folders business within the group," CIDAN explained.

The acquisition creates a sheet metal equipment manufacturer/software developer integrating all unit operations from de-coiling to folding (including long folders).

CIDAN has long been a supplier to the sheet metal industry. The group operates manufacturing locations in Sweden and Austria and has office locations in several European countries, the United States and China. The headquarters of the company, founded in 1907, is based at Götene, Sweden.

Thalmann Maschinenbau is a long folding specialist, with a strong track record of bringing innovations to the market that are designed for significant efficiency improvements, cost reductions and higher precision/flexibility of the folding process. The company was founded in 1948 and operates from Frauenfeld, Switzerland.

Petter Hjelmqvist, CEO and President of CIDAN Machinery Group commented: “CIDAN and Thalmann already share a great history together. Over a period of many years, both companies have established a number one position for long folders in the Americas. With Thalmann now part of the CIDAN group, I am convinced that we will make each other stronger. We look forward to growing the CIDAN business and the business of our customers."

CIDAN's strategy is to become a “One-Stop-Partner" for the sheet metal processing industry.

From now on, sheet metal processors will have one point of contact to purchase a broader range of sheet metal processing equipment software solutions and have one team taking care of service and after sales support. The combination of CIDAN, Forstner, nuIT and Thalmann in one group will offer great opportunities to further integrate a complete sheet metal production line.

Marco Cappello, former CEO/co-owner of Thalmann Maschinenbau AG, said: "Martin Thalmann and I are very pleased that with CIDAN, as the new owner of Thalmann, we will safeguard the long-term future. CIDAN is a well known, powerful and innovative company that has put customers at the centre of its strategy, much like Thalmann has been doing over the years."

Marco Cappello will be remaining active for CIDAN, focusing on long folder customers and distributors/agents. He will strategically support CIDAN and contribute to the growth of the portfolio as well as bring new solutions and benefits to the sheet metal processing industry and building sector.

After three generations of Thalmann in the leadership of the company, Martin Thalmann will leave the company with a positive new chapter in its future. The team in Frauenfeld will be strengthened directly on-site with Gerhard Zech (Forstner operations manager and CEO, CIDAN Germany) who becomes the new CEO.
