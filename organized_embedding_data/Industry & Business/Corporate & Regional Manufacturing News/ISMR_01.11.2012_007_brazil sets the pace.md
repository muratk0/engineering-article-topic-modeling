# BRAZIL SETS THE PACE

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

AP&T is set to begin operating in the rapidly growing Brazilian market. There is a great deal of interest in Brazil for press hardening and it is primarily in this field that AP&T recognizes abundant potential.

Per Josefsson, global sales & service manager at AP&T

“Brazil is an exceptionally vibrant and vigorous market, with a large automotive industry that is interested in the opportunities afforded by press hardening technology,” said Per Josefsson, global sales and service manager at AP&T.

AP&T is reinforcing its presence in Brazil by expanding its cooperation with Sofir, an Italian company, which has operated in the country for a long time.

“We have successfully cooperated with Sofir in Italy since 2008 and together we have completed several large assignments for Fiat, amongst others. We will now continue developing our joint offering in Brazil as well,” added Josefsson.

AP&T and Sofir organized a seminar on advanced press hardening in Belo Horizonte on 31 October 2012. www.aptgroup.com ■

## Three new BLECH events

FACED WITH a changing world economy, suppliers of sheet metal machines, tools and systems are recognising the opportunities presented by growth markets.

Mack Brooks Exhibitions, the organiser of EuroBLECH, combines the expertise of organising successful industry events with experience in Russia, India and China to deliver exhibitions geared towards the demands of these specific regions.

BLECH Russia will be held from 12-14 March 2013 in St. Petersburg, Russia, concurrently with the St Petersburg Technical Fair. Initiatives by the Russian government, such as the multibillion US$ investments in infrastructure for the 2014 Winter Olympic Games and the 2018 World Cup, present great business opportunities for suppliers of sheet metal working technology.

The BLECH India exhibition will be held from 17-20 April 2013 in Mumbai, India. Steel production in India is still growing strongly due to rapid urbanisation and infrastructure investment. In addition, there is an increasing domestic demand for consumer goods such as cars, home electronics and white goods by the soaring Indian middle class.

Mack Brooks’ BLECH China event, on 14-16 May 2013 in Suzhou, completes the chain as investment in manufacturing in China continues at a very high level across all end-user sectors for sheet metal technologies and products.
