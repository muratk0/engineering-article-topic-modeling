# AUTOMATION IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

KASTO Maschinenbau GmbH & Co. KG, based in Achern-Gamshurst, Germany, specialises in sawing and storage technology for bar stock and sheet metal. The company is a specialist in the manufacture and sale of metal-cutting saws and semi-automatic and automatic storage systems for bar stock and sheet metal. It is also a leading manufacturer of automatic handling systems for metal bars, sheet metal and pre-cut parts.

One of Europe's oldest family-owned companies, KASTO celebrated its 170th anniversary in 2014. In the course of its history, it has registered 160 patents, delivered more than 140,000 sawing machines to all parts of the world and installed more than 1,700 automatic storage facilities. In addition to a branch in Schalkau, Thuringia, KASTO has subsidiaries in the UK, France, Switzerland, Singapore and the USA. In 2015, the company's roughly 700 employees achieved revenues of approximately 120 million euros.

ISMR visited its booth at this October's EuroBLECH to see its latest new tower storage system, the KASTOecostore.

## The storage principle

At EuroBLECH 2016, KASTO launched its new KASTOecostore tower storage system for storing pallets and flat goods of various sizes.

The standard version of the KASTOecostore accommodates materials with a width of up to 1,524mm and a length of up to 4,000mm. The system height of up to eight metres can be chosen, as needed. Users can specify up to three different loading heights of between 50 and 950mm. The lifting gear drive is equipped with modern frequency converters and the motor is stationary to allow precise, dynamic loading and unloading. KASTOecostore comes as standard with the KASTOsmartControl machine control system, which has an intuitive user interface and touchscreen for easy operation.

Above: KASTOecostore

“System benefits include its low space requirement, high storage density and short access times," Martin Stöckle, KASTO's sales manager Group 1, told ISMR. "The operator has direct access to the stacker crane, which moves up and down at the front of the tower, taking out the pallet to load and unload the material (it can store 15-30 different kinds of materials). KASTOecostore is available in three sizes (one, two and three metric tons and a height of 8m) so it offers more flexibility, more height and more capacity to users."

KASTO's storage portfolio starts with small tower storage systems, KASTOecostore and KASTOunitower, the stacking cradle KASTOunibloc and then, depending upon demand, in-line storage systems such as KASTOuniline, the KASTOuniportal and KASTOunitop bar storage systems or KASTOunigrip and KASTOunicompact honeycomb storage systems. All these automated storage systems are modular in design, based on standardised components, and are delivered tailor-made in different versions according to the number of storage locations needed.

KASTOunitower single- or double-sided systems are designed for lower volume storage. They have a small footprint for high capacity, with tower heights of up to 25 metres. Versatility of use is enhanced because storage is possible in cassettes, on pallets or on special slave pallets. Loads of between one and five tonnes per storage location can be accommodated.

KASTOunibloc systems are intended for the manual or automatic handling of stacking cradles, up to a maximum of three at a time, up to five metres high. They are designed for material lengths from five to 24 metres and for maximum loads of six or nine tonnes per cradle.

"Space is at such a premium on a shop floor. Customers want to save space and call-off material in a specific order. They also want faster access to the material, avoiding the need to search for and possibly damage the material by handling it lots of times. Having the material right next to the machine, with load/unload automation, also make sense, in terms of logistics," explained Martin Stöckle.

"Everybody is talking about Industry 4.0 and this includes the automated loading and unloading of machines. We supply a range of these solutions - from small systems for manual loading/unloading of machines up to very sophisticated solutions to load every type of laser cutting/punching machines. They may use vacuum or mechanical solutions for loading and unloading. Ultimately, these systems can be connected to nesting programs and/or sheet metal processing machines for lights-out, unmanned operation."

KASTOuniline is designed for single-deep flexible, longitudinal storage of cassettes or pallets of sheet material, from small to extra-large. Suitable for narrow buildings, the modular block design starts at 200 storage locations and system heights can reach 25 metres. The installation can either be of silo construction, with roof and wall cladding to save warehouse space, or a stand-alone unit in existing buildings.

KASTOunigrip and KASTOunicompact storage systems are designed as two adjacent storage blocks in honeycomb design with an operating gantry crane moving between them. Very high storage density and fast access to stock, due to short travel distances, are hallmarks of the designs. Storage solutions with more than 500 locations for cassettes or pallets and system heights of up to 30 metres are also offered, with dynamic crane movement for up to 60 picks per hour.

Stöckle emphasised the ecological friendliness of KASTO storage solutions through the use of optional energy recovery. Surplus energy from braking and lowering of a load is converted into electricity and stored temporarily for subsequent use, for example to move and lift the picking crane. Not only are energy costs reduced, but the quality of the electricity supply is improved because power is drawn more evenly.

## Sawing strategies

KASTO launched its new KASTOwin pro-AC 5.6 sawing solution at AMB in Germany this year. It features two drives to feed the machine and carbide-tipped blades.

"Our KASTOwin series was launched two or three years ago. However, we made some modifications on the series this year. The KASTOwin 10.6 machine is now available with a moveable vice or a table for feeding unwieldy blocks through the machine," Stöckle told ISMR.

The tube cutting saw moves upwards through the material

At MACH 2016, a new bandsaw, the KASTOwin tube A 5.0, was presented for processing tubular material. The blade cuts from the bottom upwards, unlike other bandsaws where the reverse is the case. This novel action, for which patent applications have been made, reduces wear on the band and avoids damage to the teeth that often occurs when a blade travels downwards into swarf that has accumulated inside the bottom of a tube. TCT blades may now be used without fear of premature wear, resulting in greatly increased productivity.

The tube saw is the sixth model to be introduced to the KASTOwin range of bandsaws. The original five fully-automated bandsaws have maximum cutting capacities of 330 to 1,060mm and are suitable for processing a variety of materials, especially steels, in stockholding and manufacturing. The various sizes of saw are similarly constructed and the components used are largely identical.

The bandsaws feature a frequency-controlled drive that enables cutting speeds of 12 to 150 metres per minute. For all machine sizes, the saw band is hydraulically tightened and looped around hydraulic band guides, which now have interchangeable carbide inserts. The latter saves 80 per cent of the previous cost of replacing an entire carbide guide.

To reduce idle times, an innovative feature called KASTOsense eliminates the need for a 5mm stand-off from the material surface, which can waste a lot of time when the saw is programmed to cut tough materials at slower rates. Instead, the saw blade descends straight into the material and stops within microseconds to continue the cut at the programmed infeed rate, without damaging the teeth.

At the end of last year, a variant of the largest model in the range was introduced. Called KASTOwin F 10.6, it has a moveable, 2,600mm long support table that allows heavy, bulky stock such as ingots, large tools or forged eccentric shafts to be fed into position easily, ready for sawing.
