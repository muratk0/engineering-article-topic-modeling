# ArcelorMittal Board movers

[TARİH: 01.03.2010 ISMR]

ArcelorMittal announced that Georges Schmit stepped down from his position as a member of the Board of Directors on 31 December, due to his appointment as Consul General of Luxembourg based in San Francisco.

To replace Mr. Schmit, the Board has co-opted Jeannot Krecké as an interim board member starting from 1 January 2010. Mr Krecke's full appointment to the Board will be proposed to shareholders at the Company's Annual General Meeting scheduled for 11 May 2010. Like Mr Schmit, Mr. Krecké, who is Luxembourg's Minister of the Economy and Foreign Trade, will serve on ArcelorMittal's Board as a shareholder representative.

ArcelorMittal has also confirmed the appointment of Peter Kukielski to its Group Management Board (GMB), with responsibility for the Group's global mining operations, effectve from 1 January 2010. Philippus F. (Phil) Du Toit also joined its Management Committee from 1 January 2010. Mr. Du Toit has been Executive Vice President (EVP) of ArcelorMittal and Head of Mining Projects and Exploration since November 2008 and he reports to Peter Kukielski.

ArcelorMittal is a leading steel company, with operations in more than 60 countries.

## PEKKA ERKKILÄ TO JOIN OUTOTEC

Dr. Volker Wesling (left) and Dr.-Ing. Ludger Overmeyer (right), new members of the board at the LZH

The Executive Vice President - General Stainless and a member of Outokumpu Group's Executive Committee, Pekka Erkkilä, will join the Executive Committee of Outotec, the Finnish-based company specialized in mining and metallurgical technology. As the head of General Stainless, he has been responsible for the business units Tornio Works and Long Products as well as the R&D, energy, safety, environment and quality functions. Mr. Erkkilä has also had temporary management responsibility for Outokumpu's Tornio Works from 15 September 2009. The recruitment process for the new head of Tornio Works is currently ongoing. Mr. Erkkilä will start at Outotec on 1 May 2010 at the latest to ensure the smooth transition of Tornio Works' management.
