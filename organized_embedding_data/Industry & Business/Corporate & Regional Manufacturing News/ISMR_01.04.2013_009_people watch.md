## Board changes for Schuler

The completion of a majority takeover by the Austria-based Andritz Group has led to changes in the composition of Schuler AG’s Supervisory Board. The press manufacturer’s controlling body has appointed the CEO of Andritz AG, Dr. Wolfgang Leitner, as successor to the Supervisory Board Chairman, Dr. Robert Schuler-Voith. Together with a further member of the Supervisory Board, Helmut Zahn, Dr. Robert Schuler-Voith had agreed to step down from his position.

On application by the Board of Management, Andritz CEO Leitner and Prof. Christian Nowotny, a member of the Supervisory Board of Andritz AG, were appointed by the court as new members of the Supervisory Board. The appointment was made for the period up to the end of the Annual General Meeting on 18 April 2013. The Supervisory Board of Schuler AG is composed of six shareholder representatives and six representatives of the company’s employees.

“Dr. Schuler-Voith is handing over the reins to a global and technological leader in the most successful year of Schuler’s 174-year history,” said Stefan Klebert, CEO of Schuler. In April 2012, Schuler-Voith sold his shareholding (38.5 per cent) to Andritz in the course of succession planning.

Dr. Peter Jost (48) also joined press manufacturer Schuler AG as Chief Operating Officer (COO) on 6 March 2013. The Supervisory Board appointed Jost for this newlycreated position as the press manufacturer’s fifth Board of Management member. In this position, the doctor of mechanical engineering will be responsible for the procurement, production and logistics divisions of the Schuler Group.

Born in Austria, Jost comes from the Graz-based technology group Andritz where he held various management positions at different facilities – mostly in manufacturing and procurement – since 1997. In his last position, he headed the Group Operations Management division of the Andritz Group and thus held global responsibility for all purchasing and manufacturing processes. ■

## New CFO for SSAB

SSAB has appointed Håkan Folin as its new Chief Financial Officer. He has previously held a number of senior positions in the company and will take up the position of CFO on 1 May 2013.

Håkan Folin came to SSAB in 2006 from the property company Stronghold and was initially involved in SSAB´s strategic development, as well as its subsequent acquisition of the North American company, IPSCO. During his time at SSAB, he has also served as CFO of Tibnor and SSAB APAC. He returned to Sweden last year and, since the beginning of the year, has been the company´s Chief Controller. He holds an M.Sc. in Industrial Management from the Royal College of Technology in Stockholm and is married with two children.

SSAB is a global leader in value-added, high-strength steel. It has employees in over 45 countries and operates production facilities in Sweden and the US. ■

## Canavesio joins UCIMU Board

During the February Meeting of UCIMU Sistemi Per Produrre (the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots, automation systems and auxiliary products), the Board of Directors appointed Davide Canavesio (SAET, Leinì, TO) as a co-opted member of the Board.

Proposed by Luigi Galdabini, President of the association, Davide Canavesio, managing director of SAET, becomes a member of the Board, replacing the resigning Carlo Garoldi.

The Board of Directors consists of: Luigi Galdabini (Galdabini SpA, Cardano al Campo VA), President of the association, assisted by vice presidents Massimo Carboniero (OMERA, Chiuppano VI); Giulio Maria Giana (Giuseppe Giana, Magnago MI) and Riccardo Rosa (Rosa, Rescaldina MI).

Board members also include Past Presidents: Ezio Colombo (Ficep, Gazzada Schianno VA); Cesare Manfredi; Flavio Radice (Carnaghi Pietro, Villa Cortese MI); Bruno Rambaudi; Andrea Riello (Gruppo Riello Sistemi, Minerbe VR); Alberto Tacchella (IMT, Casalecchio di Reno BO) and Director Emeritus Dante Speroni (Speroni, Sostegno di Spessa Po PV). The General Director is Alfredo Mariotti. ■

## Simufact Engineering forms subsidiary in India

Dr. Gabriel McBain, new director of Simufact India

The Hamburg-based software company, Simufact Engineering, has formed the Indian subsidiary Simufact India Private Limited. Dr. Gabriel McBain (38) has been appointed as director of Simufact India. The registered office of the new company is the city of Bangalore, in which leading component suppliers and aerospace companies are based.

With its software solutions, Simufact primarily targets metalworking and metal-processing companies as well as plant and mechanical engineering companies and sectorrelated suppliers. The software simulates forming, welding and mechanical joining processes and supports companies to rapidly design and optimise their production processes. For several years, Simufact has been successfully operating in the Indian market.

For his new position, Gabriel McBain contributes years of scientific and industrial experience in forming technology. After graduating in metallurgy and materials engineering from RWTH in Aachen, he worked as a research employee in several research projects on incremental forming technology and bulk metal forming at the Institute of Metal Forming for over five years. In 2009, he obtained his doctorate with a thesis on numerical modelling of innovative bulk metal forming processes. Gabriel McBain gained industrial experience as a project manager at Acut Aachener Umformtechnik GmbH and at Simufact since 2010. ■
