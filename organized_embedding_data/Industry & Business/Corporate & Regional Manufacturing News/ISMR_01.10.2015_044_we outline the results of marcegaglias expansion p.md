# We outline the results of Marcegaglia’s expansion programme in the flat products and tube sectors

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

Antonio Marcegaglia, Chairman and CEO, Marcegaglia

Marcegaglia is the leading industrial group worldwide in the steel processing sector, with a yearly output of five million tons. The Group has operations worldwide with 6,500 employees, 60 sales offices, 210 representations and 43 manufacturing plants covering six million square metres, where 5,500 kilometres of carbon and stainless steel products are manufactured every day, serving more than 15,000 customers.

Today, Marcegaglia is considered to be one of the key steel service centre players in the steel industry – from carbon steel tube production (with an extremely wide range of products) to the stainless steel tube sector (and a leading position in terms of global production) as well as in carbon and stainless steel flat products and heavy (quarto) plates. The company is an independent player that can supply other service centres as well as specialist customers and offers a wide range of products in terms of steel grades and typical applications, a mix of services and rapid response times with stock availability in standard sizes.

Activities in the steel sector and other businesses generated over 4 billion euros of turnover for the group in 2014. Founded in 1959 and fully owned by the Marcegaglia family, the Group is headquartered in Gazoldo degli Ippoliti, Mantova (Italy).

## A platform for growth

The Italian specialist started an expansion programme about 15 years ago in the flat products sector, pursuing a successful strategy that it followed in the tube sector (where it is a leader in stainless steel welded tubes) to create a diversified network of sourcing-production distribution with horizontal, downstream and upstream growth.

“As a result, the group is now a unique player in the European and global steel industry, guided by key values such as synergies (raw material purchasing, processing, logistics, market), economies of scale, production flexibility and diversification of products and markets,” Antonio Marcegaglia, the company’s Chairman and CEO, told ISMR. “All these values are translated into flat product production (carbon and stainless steel coil, sheet and narrow strip) as well as colour-coated steel production and heavy (quarto) plates.”

Within the flat products field, the group has placed its focus squarely on Marcegaglia's core business of steel processing, developing its product portfolio focusing on the three main families (stainless steel and special products, welded tubes and flat carbon steels).

This strategy has led the Italy-based group to develop new high-grade quality and niche products, based on new capacity at its Ravenna and San Giorgio di Nogaro (Italy) plants. The setup and revamping of new lines have expanded production capacity, especially in terms of range and grade feasibility, with better control of production processes and the capability to supply more and more specialised products.

## Production, quality and logistics

Production, quality and logistics are keywords for Marcegaglia. On the production side, new high-grade products and niche products can count on, for example, high-grade new flat rolled specialties. These were expanded with new high carbon grades for heat treatment, deep drawing grades, semiprocessed magnetic steels and extra-smooth surface galvanized material and new cut-to-length processing capacities to meet the specific requirements of target sectors for the manufacturing of mechanical and precision components.

Marcegaglia has also increased its flexibility in zinc coating up to Z800 (also allowing a different coating between sides), and new grades have been developed such as dual phase DP600, DP800 and DP1000. The coil colour-coating units can count on more than 25 standard painting cycles that can be combined for specific surface requirements using more than 1200 colours. The production programme of heavy (quarto) plates was also enriched and a wide array of end uses are now covered, with specific certifications for boilers, pressure vessels and naval construction (ASTM 131, grades A, B, D, RINA approval) among others.

Another focus for the company is the efficiency of its distribution network connecting manufacturing plants with each other and with customers. Marcegaglia has been able to establish its presence in new areas through the use of intermodal terminals (hubs), strategically located in harbours on the Mediterranean Sea, but also on the Atlantic Ocean, the North Sea and the Baltic Sea, as well as storage areas near important railway connections, significantly cutting delivery times as well as carbon emissions. A network of more that 10 logistic hubs across Europe have significantly enhanced flexibility and just-in-time service.

At the heart of this network, the company’s Ravenna plant (Italy) operates as the main production and shipping hub for the group’s flat product chain, with its handling capacity (now 500 trucks per day) significantly upgraded thanks to the completion of new internal railway tracks and the expansion of the harbour docks area (220m added for a total length of 800m, 9.5m draught) for ship access. Last year in Ravenna, the group completed a massive ten-year investment plan that has strongly enhanced Marcegaglia’s role as a flat steel producer, increasing its overall capacity of cold rolled material and doubling its production of hot dip galvanized and colourcoated steel.

The plant in San Giorgio di Nogaro (Italy), which specialises in heavy plate production, is conveniently located with easy access to sea and rail freight along the same Eastern coast of Italy.

The stainless steel business of Marcegaglia has also been expanded with the production of flat materials (coil and cut-to-length sheet) at the Gazoldo degli Ippoliti plant which manufactures hot-rolled steel (1D finish) from 2 to 6mm thickness and cold-rolled steel (2B finish). These coil processing lines can also supply in-line edge slitting. Downstream, Marcegaglia can count on the integrated production of stainless sheet and strip but also flat and angle bars. The cut-to-length line can work both hot-rolled and cold-rolled materials with optional polishing (220-240-320 grit), scotch brite brushing and paper or laser film protection on each sheet.

“The group’s investments in this area have further reinforced our role as the world’s leading manufacturer of stainless steel welded tubes, supported by production plants in Brazil, USA, China, Russia and Italy,” Antonio Marcegaglia concluded.
