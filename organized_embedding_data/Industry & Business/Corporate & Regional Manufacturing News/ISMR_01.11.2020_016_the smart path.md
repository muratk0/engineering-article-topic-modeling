# THE SMART PATH

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

Marcegaglia has placed a strong focus on investment, innovation, diversification and cost reduction to drive growth.

ISMR SAYS:

“The Italian materials specialist highlights its latest R&D projects and process developments.”

Marcegaglia is the largest independentglobal operator in the steel processing industry, processing 6.2 million tons per annum. With a turnover of more than 5.5 billion euros, the materials specialist is a leading global player in the transformation of steel.

Continuing down the path of recent years, Marcegaglia is focusing its attention on strengthening its core business at national and international levels. In what is a challenging COVID-19 period, Marcegaglia has shown overall resilience in its results through strong diversification of its business segments, geographical markets and industry sectors.

“We have taken advantage of different trends as well as our broad and specialised customer base; business types (by trade and use); size and sectors; a vast raw material purchasing network (optimising prices) and continuous attention to costs and operational, as well as logistical, efficiencies,” Marcegaglia told ISMR.

## New investments

Marcegaglia Carbon Steel is a group company which manufactures flat products (coil, strip and sheet), carbon and pre-painted (PPGI) steel and carbon steel welded tubes.

In 2019, the Marcegaglia Group approved investments totalling 150 million euros, now under construction, for Marcegaglia Carbon Steel Flat Products. These include, at its plant in Ravenna (Italy), a new highly automated rolling mill for the production of special steels; the extension of the covered area by 20,000 square metres; a logistics automation project using overhead cranes and intelligent shuttles; a 33MW cogeneration plant and the revamp of a galvanizing line to improve quality and productivity. At its plant in Gazoldo degli Ippoliti, investments include a second 22 MW cogeneration plant, the revamp of a pickling line and an increase in slitting lines.

“Special attention is being paid to research and development projects. We believe that the consolidation of industry leadership in processes and products is always the basis of any investment plans that we launch,” it added.

## Innovation in focus

As far as process development is concerned, Marcegaglia’s Tensil-Pro project is a continuous measurement system of mechanical and microstructural characteristics, which has now reached its final stage of industrial application on processing lines in Ravenna (pickling, cold rolling and galvanizing). It was created to improve product quality and process efficiency: now it is applied to all metallic materials, magnetic and non-magnetic steels as well as aluminium or copper alloys, starting from the hot rolling process.

At the 2020 Global Awards for Steel Excellence, Marcegaglia Ravenna won the award for ‘Best Operational Improvements’ with Tensil-Pro, while Fives and Marcegaglia won the ‘Information Technology Services’ award with the Smart Line project. Smart Line was the result of collaboration between Marcegaglia and Fives and integrates all coil transformation production processes from hot rolling up to the finished product.
