# Chemetall acquires shares of Shanghai JV

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

CHEMETALL, a business unit of Albemarle Corporation, has completed the acquisition of all shares of its Shanghai Chemetall joint venture and is now the sole owner of the entity. This transaction is in line with Chemetall’s strategic market approach to further enhance its global footprint as a surface treatment supplier.

China is a key growth market for Chemetall. “With our very strong focus on the automotive market and our long-term relationship with leading automotive manufacturers, we are ideally positioned to further develop the business, provide advanced innovative technologies to the market and create value for our customers in this region”, said Jin Hui, Managing Director, Shanghai Chemetall.

Today, China is the largest automotive market in the world. With its broad portfolio of surface treatment technologies, Chemetall expects to actively participate in the dynamic growth of automotive-related businesses, such as automotive components, coil and coldforming industries.

Established as a joint venture with two Chinese partners in 1995, Shanghai Chemetall today houses the recently modernised, equipped and expanded regional R&D and Technical Centre, administrative offices and over 220 dedicated employees to support customers in the region.

Chemetall was recently awarded the highest supplier honour in the Airbus ”Materials & Parts SQIP” (Supply Chain & Quality Improvement) programme. See www.chemetall.com ■
