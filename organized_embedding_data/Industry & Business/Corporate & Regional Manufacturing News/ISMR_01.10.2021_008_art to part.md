# ART TO PART

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

Matt Fowles, LVD's group marketing director, discussed its latest new product and technology updates and 'art to part' material flow improvements with ISMR.

Managed Companies' for the fourth consecutive year by Deloitte, winning the programme's Gold label, LVD Company nv is a global solutions provider for sheet metalworking fabrication and serves twelve core industries. It is a leading manufacturer of sheet metal/plate working equipment including laser cutting systems; punch presses; press brakes; guillotine shears and mid-level automation systems, integrated to and supported by its CADMAN® software suite.

The LVD Group is represented in 47 countries, with 19 subsidiaries and multiple manufacturing facilities across Europe and the U.S. Its main factory and headquarters is at Gullegem, Belgium, with a central European factory in Slovakia and automation factory in Italy (formerly COMPAC), as well as a factory in the U.S. and factories in China (Hubei province) for its two Chinese Joint Ventures.

Despite the challenges of the global COVID-19 pandemic, the company introduced some of its most high-technology sheet metalworking machinery to date, including an auto tool-changing robotised bending cell. Matt Fowles, LVD's group marketing director, discussed its latest new product and technology updates and 'art to part' material flow improvements with ISMR.

ISMR: Please highlight any notable successes and achievements over the past two/three years.

MF: As a family-owned business, we are very dynamic and adapted quickly to the changing COVID-19 situation. Business has been very good for us over the past two-three years, and we have seen good growth levels. It has been particularly good this year and we have seen some record months. LVD has achieved strong year-on-year growth, despite the COVID-19 pandemic. Users still need to invest in technology and manufacturing remained very resilient.

With COVID-19 restrictions on air travel, we have seen more home construction projects, along with more purchases of consumer goods etc., which has pumped more money into local economies, with economic stimuli in some countries for capital investment. Manufacturing, in general, has benefited from that. Sheet metal job shops in western Europe, particularly, are benefiting and are very busy trying to maintain lead times. Material supply and rising steel prices are issues right now.

In the last two-three years, we acquired an Italian automation designer and manufacturer, COMPAC. We knew we needed to expand our automation portfolio, for laser cutting and punching in particular, because we could see that customers not only wanted to increase productivity (including for small-medium batches), but also wished to resolve material flow issues. This acquisition was a strategic move, and we have since integrated this within our product portfolio and manufacturing network. Our sales of automated systems (particularly lasers) have grown, as a result.

In terms of automation, we also introduced the Dyna-Cell automated bending cell whose automated programming wizard takes users from art to part in 20 minutes (digital file to completed 3D part). We have now also launched Ulti-Form, the next level of automation for bending, so that users can handle more complex and larger parts. It guarantees art to part in under 20 minutes. We will continue developing our automation strategy to meet market demand.
