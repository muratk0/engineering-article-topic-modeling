# FAST, FLEXIBLE AND FOCUSED

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

With a significant Investment Plan for its Ravenna plant, funding for a new cogeneration plant and new R&D initiatives, Marcegaglia is gearing up for Industry 4.0

Marcegaglia is a global industrial group which is a leading global independent player in 'the transformation of steel'. This is the result of a long, solid and steady growth path, with 5.8- million tons of steel processed each year and a turnover of more than 5.2 billion euros.

Over the course of sixty years, Marcegaglia has built up its position among leading steel producers, processing a wide range of carbon steel products (coil, strip, sheet, pre-painted steel and welded tube), stainless steel products (coil, strip, sheet and welded tube), cold drawn bar and heavy quarto plates.

## The Ravenna plant

The company has set up the largest of its steel metallurgy plants in Ravenna (Italy) which is also the nerve centre for integrated logistics to supply each production and distribution facility in the Marcegaglia Group.

Marcegaglia Ravenna covers an area of over 540,000 square metres, between the left bank of the port-channel and the industrial area that runs along the sea to the north of the city. It is equipped with modern plant engineering facilities and advanced production technologies for chemical pickling, cold rolling, batch annealing, galvanizing and pre-painting of coils, as well as a service centre for levelling and cutting carbon steel coils.

"Our main flat rolled plant in Ravenna is directly on the sea and benefits from inbound and outbound sophisticated logistics. We have positioned ourselves uniquely in the market, trying to combine the economies of scale of a mill with the flexibility of a service centre, to provide our customers with a one-stop-shop package," Daniele Papa, Sales and Marketing Director, Coils, Marcegaglia's Flat division, told ISMR.

## The Ravenna investment plan

In June 2019, Marcegaglia presented a significant Investment Plan for the Ravenna plant.

Marcegaglia Ravenna has contracted the SMS group for the complete supply of a twostand reversing cold mill, of the Compact Cold Mill (CCM®) type. The CCM® is an important step towards digitalisation and the ability to adapt to future needs. It can process a large material range, including high-carbon and duplex steels, chrome-manganese alloyed steels and silicon steels, with an annual capacity of about 550,000 tons. Depending upon the product mix, it will roll strip with widths of between 750 and 1,570mm and entry thicknesses ranging from between 0.60 and 6.00mm. The minimal achievable final thickness is 0.23mm. The maximum thickness achievable for cold rolled is 4.00mm.

Among the projects in the Investment Plan is another major innovation, the new cogeneration plant for production and consumption of energy. The plant will be built to replace the three existing older generation steam generators, reducing national grid and environmental emissions. The construction will guarantee 67% of the plant's electricity requirements whilst 100% of the self-produced thermal power will also be consumed by the plant itself.

## Innovation in focus

"Marcegaglia recently registered a patent for its new Tensil-Pro system, the mathematical model that (through a system of physical equations) is able to evaluate mechanical and microstructural properties based upon data from the skin passing and rolling processes. This feature of Tensil-Pro can be applied to both hot and cold rolled strip products," explained Daniele Papa.

In terms of digitalisation and Industry 4.0 Marcegaglia is carrying out a complete overhaul of its supply chain 'to further improve its response to the market', starting with planning through to production scheduling and process optimisation. It expects the improvement to its supply chain to be accompanied by more efficient production cycles and an increase in overall knowledge of what is happening on the shop floor, in the market and throughout Marcegaglia's ecosystem.

“The Industry 4.0 model provides the entire company and its partners with activities and information on models and collaborative services as much as possible in real-time, " added Daniele Papa.

Recently, the Marcegaglia group signed a seven-year loan agreement with the European Investment Bank (EIB) for 100 million euros to cover the costs of digitalisation (56%) and energy efficiency (44%) of its production plants. This will support expansion and development programmes for its industrial activities. The loan will also be used to build two thermal energy plants and some photovoltaic plants for the independent production of electricity and heat designed to meet 75% of Marcegaglia's internal needs, with a significant reduction in costs and greenhouse gas emissions into the air.

## An eye on the market

The steel market has changed over the last few years, with the consolidation of producers and growing protectionism in mature countries. Despite everything, 'risks relating to external factors, including global trade tensions and significant trade policy uncertainty, will remain the key challenge for the EU during 2019-2020. However, until now (third quarter of 2019), apart from the business directly linked to the motor vehicle sector and mechanical engineering, distribution appears to maintain the volume seen in 2018 without significant reductions,' according to Papa.

"For the future, we foresee an unclear market outlook where steel buyers will try to find suppliers who are able to ship steel quickly. Markets will become more 'regionalised' with limited 'steel tourism', so the intermediate activity in the steel business will become more difficult, with steel mills trying to go straight to steel-end users. There will be more improvements to steel grades, aimed at delivering better material performance in steel processing. Marcegaglia AHSS is one example enabling engineers to profit from lighter pieces and new configurations. The DP600-HF (high forming ability) and D780 grades are the key products for the automotive market developed between 2018 and 2019 at the Marcegaglia Ravenna plant. At present, Marcegaglia is the only Italian manufacturer to supply these steel qualities," explained Daniele Papa.

"Changes in the market will affect the way we approach business; we believe that the greatest challenge in the near future will be steel sourcing in a more sophisticated and disciplined market. Faced with such a challenge, there is an opportunity for companies who are able to move, change and act rapidly, in line with such a volatile market."

Compared to the past, the order book for distribution is now based around just a few weeks and this, as Marcegaglia told /SMR, makes it necessary to choose flexible and responsive strategic partners.

“On this path, as a relatively young and very flexible player in the steel business, we will be able to gain more market share as the last independent steel processor," concluded Daniele Papa.
