# SUTOR APPOINTS NEW CTO

[TARİH: 01.10.2011 ISMR]

SUTOR TECHNOLOGY GROUP LIMITED, a leading China-based manufacturer and distributor of high-end finished steel products and welded steel pipes used by a variety of downstream applications, has confirmed the appointment of Mr. Jiuling Li as its new Chief Technology Officer (CTO). Mr. Li will be responsible for providing overall guidance for the company's research and development, project planning and implementation as well as supervision of facilities integration and employees training.

Mr. Jiuling Li is a founder of hot-dip galvanisation technology for wide steel strips in China. He currently serves as chief expert and senior member of the Hot-dip Galvanisation Committee of China Society for Corrosion and Protection (CSCP). Prior to joining Sutor, Mr. Li was chief engineer at Wuhan Iron and Steel (Group) Corporation, one of the largest steel companies in China. Mr. Li authored the first book on hot-dip galvanisation technology in China and published 38 articles in various professional journals.

CHANGES FOR BRADBURY GROUP

The Bradbury Co., Inc., a leading manufacturer of rollforming and coil processing equipment, has appointed Brad Foster to its automated production systems sales / applications team. Foster’s responsibilities include promoting sales for automated production systems with a special emphasis on sectional steel garage door manufacturers and steel foam panel producers.

The Bradbury Group also recently appointed Neil Rogers as Operations Manager at Bradbury Machinery Shanghai (BMS). Rogers has been with the Group for 12 years and has served in many capacities, including European Sales Manager with responsibilities in India and Middle East Sales Manager. His responsibilities in his new appointment will include all facets of the business, with special focus on ensuring that the finished machines and automated lines produced by BMS fulfill customer production objectives and standards.

Rick McVey recently joined The Bradbury Group as the North American Product Manager of Rainwater Goods for Bradbury Group Australia. McVey, a 36-year industry veteran, has extensive experience within the metal forming, rainwater goods industry.

## SIMPSON TO RUN TRUMPF UK

TRUMPF UK has confirmed the appointment of Scott Simpson to the post of managing director. Formerly the company’s technical director, Scott has taken over from Hartmut Pannen who returns to TRUMPF Japan to spearhead customer support in the wake of the country’s devastating earthquake and tsunami.

Scott Simpson started his career with TRUMPF as a service engineer and, within ten months, became its area sales manager in Scotland. In 2000, he was promoted to general sales manager, a post he held for six years before becoming managing director of TRUMPF India. Under Scott’s leadership, this company moved a purpose-built facility with a turnover of 15 million Euros and a workforce of 50 people, all within three years.

Since his appointment as technical director at TRUMPF UK last year, Scott has radically reformed the company’s service programme to ensure the rapid and efficient response that is so vital in today’s supply chain. This critical element of the TRUMPF operation is now the responsibility of Brian Harper, the newly confirmed national services manager, and his deputy, Tony Mattingley.
