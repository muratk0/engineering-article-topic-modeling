# Management change at TRUMPF

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

EFFECTIVE 31 December 2011, Friedrich Kilian left the Board of Management of TRUMPF GmbH + Co. KG. where he was responsible for Central Purchasing as well as research and development in the machine tools’ business field.

“We thank Friedrich Kilian for over 25 years’ untiring work for TRUMPF, during which time he made our company even more successful with numerous innovations,” said Dr. Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller, President and Chairwoman of the Board of Management.

The new head of Machine Tools Development is Dr. Jürgen Prokop. He was appointed a CEO of TRUMPF Werkzeugmaschinen GmbH + Co. KG from 1 January 2012. Responsibility for Central Purchasing on the Group’s Board of Management has been assumed by Dr. Lars Grünert, whose range of tasks there will continue to include information technology and organisational development.

With sales totaling €2,024 billion and 8,550 employees, the TRUMPF Group ranks among the leading manufacturing companies for production technology worldwide. Its core business is machine tools for flexible sheet metal processing for punching, forming, laser processing and bending. www.trumpf.com

## JK LASERS PINPOINTS SALES

industrial laser manufacturer JK Lasers has appointed a new sales director for North America. Andrew Dodd joined the company on 16 January 2012. With 28 years’ experience in the industrial laser industry, he will be responsible for sales and technical support. Immediately before joining JK Lasers, he ran the Mid-West sales operation for Miyachi.

As part of his role, Andrew will oversee the establishment of a dedicated sales and service facility in South East Michigan. The new site will incorporate specialist applications laboratories, enabling JK Lasers to demonstrate products and processes to customers. Products include a range of Watt to kiloWatt fiber lasers, Nd:YAG lasers and process tools. A new range of high performance multi-kiloWatt class fiber lasers will be added. See www.jklasers.com
