# APW EXPANDS UNDER NEW OWNERSHIP

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

APW, A manufacturer of perforated sheet products, based in Warrington (near Manchester, UK) is under new ownership. As a part of varomexx Beteiligungen GmbH, apw will now be flanked by two sister companies: Mevaco and Solvaro. Together, they offer a comprehensive and diverse range of perforated products, such as perforated sheets and expanded metals as well as metal-based engineering solutions for a wide range of applications. The focus of apw, in the short-term, will be the upcoming construction of a new production hall and the expansion of its product range to include products in metric sizes.

The merger with varomexx Beteiligungen GmbH provides apw and its sister companies, Mevaco and Solvaro, with the opportunity to supply a wide range of customers with perforated sheet and expanded metal products or metal industrial components. Despite joining forces, each of the companies still specialises in its own specific segment.

After the notice of termination for the previous apw premises, the company’s future, which will involve a move to a new site and construction of a new production hall close to the current location, was secured when it was bought by varomexx Beteiligungen GmbH. This, in turn, offers an opportunity to modernise the machinery which will have moved to its new premises by December 2012. ■
