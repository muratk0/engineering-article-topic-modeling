# LIGHTS-OUT PRODUCTION

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

In 1959, Bob Allsop set up a small but efficient fabrication shop on the outskirts of Huddersfield, in the UK, providing a high quality, high capacity service catering for the needs of a booming industrial Yorkshire.

Paul Goodwin became managing director of Allsops in 1993 and his commitment to investment in new technology (plant and machinery) has seen it grow to become a company that now employs 120 people with an annual turnover in excess of £10 million and a respected reputation in the production of high quality sheet metal components and fabrications. In 2009, it celebrated its half century.

ISMR talked to Stephen Fennell, Allsops' Business Development Director, on the company's strategic objectives, its view of the markets, recent investments and its increasing fabrication capabilities.

ISMR: Please can you review Allsops' achievements and successes in 2009? Can you confirm turnover figures in 2008 and 2009?

SF: Our achievements and successes in 2008/09 include the successful integration of new plant/machinery (2 x new Bystronic Byspeed 3015 lasers, a Pullmax 720 punch press and a new paint line) – worth just short of £2 million at beginning of 2008 – as well as the fact that we are still here after the worst recession in 90 years. We are now gearing towards true lights-out manufacturing with our new equipment investments – they have taken us to a completely new level where we can take on all types of new jobs.

We expanded the factory by building a new extension which is given over solely to assembly work over the last couple of months. We also recently purchased a new Arc Mate 120i R-J3 Mig welding application robot welder, which enables us to take on more volume work. In 2008, our turnover was £10,009,487. In 2009, it was £7,623,178.

ISMR: Please give a short potted history of Allsops. How large is your facility, which services can you offer and how many staff do you have?

SF: Occupying a factory covering over 80,000 square feet in Holmfirth, our capabilities include CAD design and punching (the latest technology includes Pullmax's new 720 punch and 126 machines). The Pullmax 720 has a maximum working range of 3090mm x 1725mm easily accommodating the standard sheet size 3000mm x 1500mm. The axis speed is 128m/min. with an accuracy of 0.1mm per metre, producing precision components rapidly.

We use the 720 to punch up to 3mm but it is capable of punching to 8mm with a press capacity of 220KN-300KN (24-33 tonnes). The tool-head can hold between 20 and 200 tools added to its sheet loading and part picking abilities which gives us the option of running lights-out facilities and passing the reduced costs on to our customers.

"Allsops is actively targeting volume work with new customers, seeking partnership style projects on long term contracts including potential partnerships from abroad"

We also offer laser cutting - the latest machines on the market supplied by Bystronic with 24/7 facilities and equipped with sheet loaders to enable unmanned running gives us a competitive edge in the laser cutting market place. Two Bystronic Byspeed 3015 lasers have a cutting range of 3048mm x 1524mm and can cut section up to 100mm high. The maximum sheet thickness that can be cut is 25mm in mild steel, 20mm in stainless steel and 12mm in aluminium with an accuracy of 0.1mm.

We also have 14 press brakes including the Bystronic Edwards Pearson P250 with 250 tonne and 4-metre bed capabilities. We also have what is considered by many to be the Rolls Royce of press brakes - the Hämmerle P130 with 3-point bending and an accuracy of 0.5 degrees over 3 metres.

We also offer automated CNC folding - our Motoman Robot and Salvagnini P4 enable optimised and consistent pressing with unmanned production. The P4 Salvagnini takes minutes to set up for a new part, can be programmed for an unlimited number of bends and is up to five times faster than conventional press brakes, producing a part that is consistently accurate to tight tolerances. The P4 can accommodate bend lengths of up to 2500mm long with a bend height of up to 127mm and a maximum bend thickness of 2mm for mild steel, 1.25mm stainless steel and 2.5mm aluminium.
