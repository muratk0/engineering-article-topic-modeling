# Forming solutions

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Group Rhodes, one of Europe's largest Original Equipment Manufacturers, will be showcasing its innovations in superplastic forming and diffusion bonding at the Advanced Engineering exhibition (1-2 November 2023) in Birmingham (UK).

The company will also highlight its extensive range of machinery for the hot and cold forming of metals and composites as well as complete turnkey solutions for specialised machinery in the aerospace industry.

Group Rhodes' operations director, James Gascoigne, outlined: "The Advanced Engineering exhibition is a great opportunity for our team to demonstrate the innovative work that is being undertaken at Group Rhodes. It is the perfect environment for us to meet with industry experts and discuss all the aspects of our ever-growing portfolio."

Group Rhodes manufactures and markets over 50 ranges of machinery from its facilities in Wakefield, West Yorkshire (UK).

The company's capabilities include the design, development and manufacture of bespoke equipment and complete turnkey solutions. The main trading divisions of the Group consist of Rhodes Interform, Craven Fawcett and Hallamshire Engineering Services.

"Rhodes is celebrating its 200th anniversary next year and, notwithstanding the fact that the business has evolved enormously since 1824, we are still proud to nurture a dedicated workforce in a traditional yet dynamic manufacturing environment," it told ISMR.

The Advanced Engineering exhibition is a large annual gathering for engineering and manufacturing professionals in the UK and a hub of innovation, collaboration and development for all sectors and areas of the engineering supply chain. The event attracted around 9,000 attendees and over 400 exhibitors in 2022.
