# Hyundai Motor Group announces new business priorities

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

HYUNDAI MOTOR GROUP'S Executive Vice Chairman (EVC) Euisun Chung has laid out the Group's top priorities for the year ahead, through a New Year's address to employees at the Group's headquarters. He identified 'enhancing business competitiveness', 'strengthening future-readiness' and 'innovation of management and organisational systems' as the three pillars to propel the organisation forward and make strides in 2019.

The EVC stressed the role of the regional headquarters in improving the Group's profitability in automotive businesses and explained plans to grant them greater autonomy. Strengthening business fundamentals and optimising operations, as measures to spur sustainable growth across the board, will also be on the automotive group's agenda, he said.

EVC Chung had high expectations for the 13 new models at Hyundai, Kia and Genesis brand vehicles set for launch this year. He added that these models will not only serve to revitalise the Group's business in key markets, notably the U.S. and China, but also to strengthen operations in emerging markets such as India and ASEAN countries.

According to EVC, the Group will refocus its business portfolio, maximising efficiency through a 'selection and concentration' approach, to strengthen the viability of operations in the global market.

His expectations for non-automotive affiliates were also specific. Hyundai's EVC is counting on the Group's components businesses to spearhead its technological advancements, while steel affiliates 'should continue to lead the development of advanced materials'. As for the construction business, he made clear that strengthening the core fundamentals of the industry, such as design and engineering, is imperative.

According to EVC Chung, Hyundai Motor Group has no plans to slow down its investment in new growth areas and expects to accelerate technological advancements to make itself an Industry 4.0 leader. The Group aims to speed up transition towards Clean Mobility and position itself as a global leader in driving this change in the industry. It will continue developing a wide array of electrified powertrains, hybrid, electric and hydrogen fuel cell-powered vehicles and, by 2025, aims to offer 44 electrified models, targeting annual sales of around 1.67 million units. By 2030, it expects to have invested around KRW eight trillion (about US$ seven billion) in fuel cell technologies including FCEVs.

With the goal of launching a pilot fleet of autonomous 'robo taxis' in Korea by 2021, Hyundai Motor Group will pursue and explore partnerships with global industry leaders to develop autonomous driving technologies that are 'safe and innovative', added its EVC. In addition, the Group will combine capabilities to create its own mobility service business model. Its plans involve developing business opportunities that integrate manufacturing and services, as well as expanding the Group's global reach through partnerships with third-party experts.

The EVC emphasised that an advanced management system and flexible corporate culture are crucial to accomplishing business objectives for the New Year. To establish more transparent and swift decision-making processes, the Group plans to strengthen the diversity, expertise and independence of the Board of Directors at each affiliated company and maximise shareholder value and customer value through proactive communication with shareholders and the market.

EVC Chung also vowed to fulfil social responsibilities, create jobs and achieve win-win opportunities and harmonious cooperation across the business. Driving change and innovations in the way employees think is the imperative in achieving such changes, he added.
