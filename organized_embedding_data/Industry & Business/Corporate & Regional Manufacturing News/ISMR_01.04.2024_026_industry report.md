## Market trends

According to Mordor Intelligence, the refrigerator segment accounts for a significant market share of the global home appliance market.

"Refrigerators are considered a safe, easy and convenient way to preserve food and food products. Rising disposable income, the availability of smart and energy-efficient refrigerator units and a widening base of nuclear families are leading to an upsurge in demand for refrigerators for household applications. Singledoor refrigerators are the most widely used door type as they are cost-efficient, easy to use and may or may not contain an inbuilt freezer. The French door refrigerators, on the other hand, are rapidly gaining popularity owing to the ease of usability and smart features introduced in these refrigerators. Nevertheless, market players are trying to come up with new models that have a minimum impact on the environment," outlined the analyst.

The analyst also confirmed that the Asia-Pacific region dominates the market for home appliances.

"Regional brands (including Haier; Midea; Gree; Hisense from China; Samsung; LG from Korea; Panasonic; Sharp; Hitachi from Japan and Videocon from India) largely dominate the market for home appliances in the Asia-Pacific region. The smart appliances market in Asia-Pacific is expected to grow at a rapid rate. Developed countries (such as Japan, Singapore, Hong Kong and Indonesia) are expected to register an increase in the sales of smart appliances, mostly owing to the rising energy and labour costs, the high purchasing power of consumers and greater awareness about smart cities and smart integrated appliances. China has the highest demand for conventional air conditioners, by volume, across the globe owing to government policies that favour energysaving appliances, a growing property market and hot weather," confirmed Mordor Intelligence.

The home appliance market, it added, is highly fragmented with many players. In terms of market share, major international players include Whirlpool; Haier; Samsung Electronics; LG Electronics and Bosch. However, with technological advances and product innovations, mid-sized to smaller companies are increasing their market presence by securing new contracts and tapping into new markets.

## Heating and cooling

In 2024, the Bosch brand is expected to launch the first air-to-air heat pump that can be used to heat and cool buildings.

"As it meets the requirements of the Federal Subsidy for Efficient Buildings (BEG), it is eligible for subsidies like any other Bosch heat pump," explained the manufacturer.

Carbon-neutral cold local heating networks, which are being set up in

Germany and other countries, can make an important contribution to a carbon-neutral building stock. In the North Frisian municipality of Leck, the first phase of such a network is currently being built to supply 153 residential and commercial buildings, each of which is connected to a Bosch brine-to-water heat pump, with heat and hot water. A central collector field extracts heat from the ground, providing a particularly efficient energy supply for the neighbourhood.

Starting in 2024, Bosch's Buderus brand will offer hybrid heat pump systems that also enable existing older apartment buildings in urban neighbourhoods to meet the requirements of the German Buildings Energy Act. The heat pump takes on the main load of the supply and the supplementary condensing boiler is only switched on during peak demand. These hybrid systems can supply sustainable heat to neighbourhoods of up to 100 residential units.

From summer 2024, all Bosch's air purifiers and mobile air conditioners will be controlled via the HomeCom Easy app, which is also used to control heat pumps.

"Modern energy management systems ensure an efficient and sustainable power supply for all consumers in the home. By distributing PV power intelligently and automatically, they can reduce electricity costs by up to 60 per cent," commented the manufacturer.

On 6 February 2024, Panasonic announced the launch of the WhisperComfort® 60 Energy Recovery Ventilator (ERV). HVI-certified for energy recovery and sound, the new addition to Panasonic's family of ERVs furthers the company's dedication to providing whole-house ventilation in an energy-efficient way. The new ERV provides a tempered fresh air supply and balanced exhaust air flow to maintain neutral pressure throughout the home.

LG Electronics (LG) presented its latest heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC) solutions at Mostra Convegno Expocomfort (MCE) 2024 in Milan, Italy, this March. LG's booth showcased the company's energy-efficient HVAC solutions for home and business, including the new DUALCOOL™ residential air conditioner indoor unit, residential heat pump solutions and commercial hydronic solutions employing low-Global Warming Potential (GWP) refrigerants.

LG's air conditioner is equipped with a comfort-enhancing Soft Air™ function, which provides customised and indirect breeze, and an innovative DUAL Vane™ system, sending warm or cool air in multiple directions that is designed to deliver rapid temperature control. Engineered for the European market, the DUALCOOL leverages the DUAL Inverter Heat Pump Compressor™ that is designed to deliver efficient heating and cooling. Another feature designed to reduce energy consumption is the kW Manager™, which lets users set electricity usage targets and monitor the air conditioner's estimated electricity usage via the ThinQ™ app.

DUALCOOL also features a Human Detecting Sensor that automatically puts the air conditioner in energy-saving mode if no-one is detected in the room. The Window Open Detecting feature can prevent energy waste as well, activating energy-saving mode if the temperature in the room suddenly rises or falls. The Auto Cleaning™ feature automatically dries the DUALCOOL's interior, boosting hygiene by removing any moisture and preventing mould from forming. The Freeze Cleaning™ feature defrosts the heat exchanger to wash away bacteria, dust, debris and odour-causing contaminants.

## In conclusion

"We see a clear trend across all age groups that a more sustainable lifestyle is playing an increasingly important role. In addition to the environmental aspect, financial savings through better energy efficiency is also a strong factor in choosing an appliance," explained Alexander Dehmel, Head of CSM Germany & Austria at GfK (research analysts), who also spoke at Electrolux Group's international media conference at IFA 2023 in Berlin.

"The industry's commitment to sustainability extends beyond appliances in customers' homes, applying also in factories and offices and to all aspects of manufacturers' operations. It includes design, materials and manufacturing to recycling appliances and recovering materials. The sector makes certain all phases of an appliance's life cycle contribute to net zero targets and to ultimately achieving a circular economy. Manufacturers also ensure, in the latter stage of an appliance's life, that recycling feeds into the economy as recovered materials and enables a new cycle to begin," commented AMDEA (the Association of Manufacturers of Domestic Appliances).

AMDEA is supporting the new 'Kitchen of the Future' zone at the inaugural 'Installer Kitchens and Bathrooms' zone at the Installer Show 2024 to be held at Birmingham NEC (UK) on 25-27 June 2024. This new initiative will explore the concept of multigenerational kitchens and futureproofing as well as how kitchen retailers, designers, appliances and installers can play a pivotal role in shaping the kitchens of tomorrow.

It is clear to see that today's consumers are more empowered, have greater expectations and are open and adaptable to new technology. Sustainable product innovation, coupled with smart functionality and ease of use, to fit consumer lifestyles will therefore continue to gain ground in the global home appliance market.
