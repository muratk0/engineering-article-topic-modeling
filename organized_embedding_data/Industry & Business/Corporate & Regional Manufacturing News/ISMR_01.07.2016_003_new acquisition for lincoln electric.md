# New acquisition for Lincoln Electric

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

LINCOLN ELECTRIC HOLDINGS Inc. has acquired Vizient® Manufacturing Solutions, a privately held robotic integrator specialising in custom engineered tooling and automated arc welding systems for general and heavy fabrication applications.

“We are excited to welcome Vizient into our growing automation portfolio," said Christopher L. Mapes, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. “Its system design capabilities not only complement our current offering, but its focus on quality and customer service aligns with our values at Lincoln Electric. Vizient will also help to diversify our end-market exposure and broaden growth opportunities globally."

Vizient is headquartered and operates in Bettendorf, Iowa (USA) and has operations in Brazil. Annual sales are approximately US$ 40 million. Terms of the transaction were not disclosed. Lincoln Electric is a global specialist in the design, development and manufacture of arc welding products, robotic arc welding systems, plasma and oxy-fuel cutting equipment and has a leading global position in the brazing and soldering alloys market.
