# New investments from General Motors

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

GENERAL MOTORS will start construction of a US$ 174 million stamping facility that will create or retain about 145 jobs. The new facility, part of the company’s Lansing Grand River Assembly Plant, will produce stamping components for the Cadillac ATS and Cadillac CTS family of vehicles and a future product.

The new facility, which opens in 2016, will save about US$ 14 million per year in logistics costs tied to material handling. Built in 2001, Lansing Grand River is GM’s second-newest US assembly plant and the manufacturing home of the Cadillac ATS, CTS and the CTS V-series performance versions.

Including this investment, GM has announced Lansing facility investments of more than US$ 530 million since 2009. In 2012, it announced that Lansing Grand River would produce the next-generation Camaro and, in 2013, it invested US$ 44.5 million and 200 jobs for a new logistics optimisation centre at LGR. Since 2009, GM has announced nearly US$ 11 billion of investment in US facilities, including US$ 777 million so far this year.

In a separate initiative on 14 August 2014, General Motors do Brasil announced to Brazilian President, Dilma Roussef, that it would invest US$ 2.9 billion in Brazil from 2014 to 2018. The investments will help to fund the development of new products, technologies and employee training, in addition to localising components with Brazilian suppliers.

"This investment will allow the Chevrolet brand to continue to renew its line-up with a focus on technology and quality. Another major purpose of this contribution is to raise the percentage of nationalisation of the components of the cars made in Brazil, in an action that also involves suppliers installed in the country,” confirmed Jaime Ardila, president of General Motors South America. ■
