# Marco Cappello, CEO, Thalmann, discusses new company developments, products and trends in global bending markets.

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

Thalmann Maschinenbau AG can trace its history back to the one-man business founded by Otto Thalmann in 1948. As a pioneer in long-length bending machine construction, the Swiss innovator has been awarded with the Bavarian State Gold Medal for excellent workmanship. It is constantly innovating with new products and technologies aimed at simplifying working practices in sheet metalworking plants and industries.

ISMR caught up with its CEO, Marco Cappello, to discuss the company's latest developments and his views on the market.

ISMR: Please outline any recent successes and achievements?

Mc: Thalmann has had major success in the last two years creating a very strong market position in the high-end double folding market, with its TD range. This success has been based on true measurable values that the TD model brings to Thalmann customers.

Firstly, customers can save significant production time and costs. The TD folder can produce bent parts at a significant higher speed and with less manpower. Secondly, customers can increase their sales as the TD can produce an extended range of products. The range of dimensions against which parts can be bent on a TD is significantly higher compared to other machines in the market. Designers and architects are now able to propose new designs that previously were not possible to produce. Several of our customers who now work with the TD folder increased their sales because of their investment in this technology. Thirdly, customers buy more capacity with their investment in a TD bender compared to other products available.

Another major milestone is that, in 2016, company ownership changed hands. Martin Thalmann, who previously worked in the electronics industry as a process manager, and myself (I manage sales and marketing activities for Thalmann) acquired all shares in the company from the previous Thalmann generation.

With this acquisition comes major initiatives to develop the company further.

These initiatives are well underway with the aim of serving customers and markets even better in the future. The focus is on increasing efficiencies, lowering the cost base, increasing capacities and further developing new products and customer benefits. This will include a completely new product line in the near future. This milestone will guarantee the continuity of Thalmann as a premium supplier of folders in years to come.

ISMR: What are your views on the current business climate for sheet metal professionals?

MC: After many years in the industry, we clearly see a more positive climate and outlook. Customers are investing again and there is clearly more confidence in the market as the global economic climate registers growth again.

We also see that companies who invest in innovative solutions and technologies are able to obtain governmental grants and tax relief. Several of our customers have, for these reasons, invested in a TD folder (for example).

The way that building and architectural styles are developing recently shows a clear shift towards the use of metal façades. Architects that opt for metal facades have a broader scope of designs, shapes and colours available. It is exactly here that we are putting a lot of effort. We want architects and builders to have more options available for their product offerings.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers and how are you addressing these issues for them?

MC: We see that customers are focusing on costs and equipment reliability, as well as working with suppliers who understand.
