# A350 XWB for Thailand

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

THE FIRST DELIVERY of Airbus’ latest generation widebody airliner to THAI is scheduled in mid-2016. Manufacturing of the first A350 XWB for Thai Airways International (THAI) is advancing well, with major components progressing towards the start of final assembly in October 2015. THAI will acquire a total of 12 A350 XWB aircraft, with four on direct order and eight on lease. The airline will be able to operate these aircraft on long-haul services to Europe and in Asia.

The ‘forward fuselage’ was manufactured and assembled by Premium Aerotec in Augsburg, Germany, and delivered to Airbus in Hamburg, Germany for equipping (including brackets, tubes, pipes and systems). This section will then be flown from Hamburg to Saint-Nazaire, France, on board Airbus’ Beluga transport aircraft.

The ‘nose fuselage’ section, manufactured by Stelia Aerospace, has been delivered to Airbus in Saint-Nazaire for assembly and equipping. These two sections (‘nose fuselage’ and ‘forward fuselage’) will be joined to form the front fuselage, which will subsequently be shipped from Saint-Nazaire to the A350 XWB Final Assembly Line in Toulouse (France). n

## Levelling and deburring

THE INTEGRATION of Paul Ernst Maschinenfabrik GmbH from Eschelbronn, Germany, into the Austrian Wintersteiger Group created the conditions for pushing forward and expanding the technology of the Ernst and Kohler Maschinenbau GmbH companies. The motto is “sheet metal deburring and levelling from a single source”.

Levelling machine specialists Kohler and Ernst are a good match – every levelling machine customer also needs a deburring machine. For this reason, Kohler and Ernst have merged their sales and will now cooperate jointly with retailers and representatives in all markets. The companies also merged their demonstration centres at the newly established Kohler facility in Lahr in the Black Forest in Germany, directly on the A5 Karlsruhe-Basel motorway.

Their first joint trade fair exhibition took place in mid-June 2015 at the ITM (a trade fair for industrial technology and capital goods) in Poland. This was followed, at the end of June, by a joint in-house exhibition in Lahr, Germany. Visitors were given the opportunity to immerse themselves in every aspect of levelling and deburring technology with product tests of various models, tours of the company and expert lectures. Another focus was on the services offered by the contract levelling and deburring service center in Lahr.

“Deburring and levelling from a single source – this helps us generate synergies for ourselves and major benefits for our customers,” said Markus Lindörfer, CEO of Ernst. n
