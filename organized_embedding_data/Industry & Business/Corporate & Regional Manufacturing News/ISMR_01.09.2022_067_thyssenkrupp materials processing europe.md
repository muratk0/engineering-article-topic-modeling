## Stampitalia Srl

www.stampitalia.eu/it/

This Italian manufacturer specialises in the design, manufacture and test of highprecision dies. These include lamination dies for electric motors; blanking and bending progressive dies plus progressive dies in carbide and special steels for production of magnetic, interlocked, helical or simple laminations.

The company also manufactures dies for high-precision components. Dies can be used mainly in the electronic, electric, automotive and aerospace sectors for high-standard production of small metal items in various materials, thicknesses and sizes.

"We are a young and dynamic company specialising in the design and manufacture of dies for blanking and bending metal sheet," it told /SMR. "On request, we can also manufacture high-precision steel or carbide spare parts, also without drawings, and special treatments for those. We use high-quality materials e.g. for plates, metallurgical powders or carbide for the blanking elements."
