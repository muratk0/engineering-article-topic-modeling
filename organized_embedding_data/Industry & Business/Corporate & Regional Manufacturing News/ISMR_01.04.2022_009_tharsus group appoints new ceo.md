# Tharsus Group appoints new CEO

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Joni Rautavuori is the new CEO of the Tharsus Group. The Group comprises Tharsus Ltd, a designer and manufacturer of advanced machines and commercial robotics, and Universal Wolf, a specialist in complex sheet metal fabrication.

"Joni brings many years of experience running complex automation organisations and scaling them in multiple territories. His appointment represents an important point in the strategy to create a long-term sustainable future for our business, as well as drive the important next phase of growth," commented the Group.

Joni's commercial experience is a strong complement to the engineering capability of the existing Tharsus Group team. He led ABB's Global Robots & Solutions business from the UK between 2015 and 2020 and headed several commercial sustainable technology businesses in the roles of CEO and founder. During his 20 years at ABB, he worked across multiple roles in end-to-end value chains in Finland, China and the UK. Joni also holds a Master's degree from the University of Cambridge Manufacturing Leaders Programme.

Tharsus Group's founder, Brian Palmer, will continue to lead strategy and innovation, whilst supporting Joni in running the business.

"Joni is the ideal person to deliver the next phase of growth for Tharsus Group as its new CEO and contributes impressive commercial experience in the manufacturing and robotics space. Joni is also adept at building and leading collaborative teams. His expertise will help shape the company as we expand our core business areas and build new offerings," Brian Palmer explained.■

## New GM for KEF

The directors of Killeen Engineering Fabrication have announced the immediate appointment of Brian Cunningham to its new role of General Manager.

Managing director, Ben Killeen, commented: “It gives me great pleasure to announce the promotion of Brian Cunningham to general manager for our Killeen Engineering business. Brian has worked across the Killeen Engineering and Sensys Technology business in most departments and has been a key contributor to our growth over the past few years. He has built up years of experience in the industry, gaining his technical and leadership skills with great commercial insight."

Brian Cunningham will be responsible for, and oversee, the management of company sales revenue; marketing; operations and service; production; fitting; technical; HR and finance.

Killeen Engineering is a metal fabrication and welding business based in Enfield, Co. Meath (Ireland). The business is managed by father and son, Des and Ben Killeen. With over 35 years of experience in the trade, Killeen Engineering provides steel products and services to construction and residential customers. A wide range of metal fabrication and steel products, and services from gates and railings to steel fire escapes, balconies, structural steel and steel sales, are supplied.■

## Contract extensions at TMS

The Supervisory Board of thyssenkrupp Materials Services confirmed the reappointment of Martin Stillger to the segment's Executive Board for another five years from 1 January 2022 to 31 December 2026. He was also appointed chief executive officer at the same time (he previously held the post of chairman).

In addition, the Supervisory Board approved the extension of the contract of Daniel Wodera, Chief Financial Officer of thyssenkrupp Materials Services, by a further five years from 1 October 2022 to 30 September 2027.

The appointments give the Executive Board of the mill-independent materials distributor and service provider a strong mandate for the strategic transformation path launched in 2019 with "Materials as a Service." As part of its strategic further development, "Materials as a Service", the company is focusing on the supply of raw materials and materials as well as products and services in supply chain management.

Martin Stillger has been Chairman of the Executive Board of Materials Services since 6 December 2019. Daniel Wodera joined the Executive Board as CFO on 1 October 2019.
