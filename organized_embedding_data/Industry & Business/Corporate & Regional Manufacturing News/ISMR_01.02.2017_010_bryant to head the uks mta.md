# Bryant to head the UK's MTA

[TARİH: 01.02.2017 ISMR]

THE UK'S MANUFACTURING Technologies Association (MTA) held its Annual General Meeting in London on 6 December 2016. The meeting, which was preceded by an MTA Members' Forum, saw over 50 members come together to discuss the MTA's future with potential new events and the Association's response to Brexit at the forefront of the debate.

The AGM saw the MTA Presidency pass from Guy Mollart to Geoffrey Bryant, managing director of Citizen Machinery UK.

MTA CEO, James Selka, said: “It is fantastic to welcome Geoff as the President of the MTA Board. Guy Mollart has been a great advocate for the MTA over the course of his two years at the helm and I would like to extend my sincere thanks for all the help and support he has given. We look forward to drawing upon Geoff's wealth of experience to help guide the association over the next two years."■
