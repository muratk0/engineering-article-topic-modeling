# In memoriam: a tribute to Alex D'Alfonso

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Coil processing specialist, The Bradbury Group, has confirmed that Alexander D'Alfonso passed away at home in Burlington, Ontario (Canada) on 3 August 2022 surrounded by his family. He was 67 years old. In 2015, he became the Group's sales director of coil processing systems.

"Alex is survived by his loving wife of 27 years, Angelica, and his children Tanya; Tyler; Barbara; Joel; Ana and Drew, along with their partners, and twelve grandchildren. Alex graduated from the Mohawk College Control System Engineering Programme in 1975 in Hamilton, ON, Canada, and spent his entire career in the steel industry in various roles," The Bradbury Group told ISMR.

Alexander D'Alfonso joined Stelco in 1975 for ten years before moving to New Zealand Steel in 1986 as a hot-strip mill electrical engineer. He returned to Canada in 1987 with Reliance Electric Company in service and then moved to sales. Alex joined PRO-ECO (SMS Demag PRO-ECO) in 1991 as manager of Mexican and Latin American sales until 2003. He was VP of sales with Avon Engineering from 2003 to 2007. In 2007, he joined The Bradbury Group as director of process lines and Latin American sales before, in 2015 becoming its sales director of coil processing systems.

"He will be missed by many colleagues in the steel industry," The Bradbury Group told ISMR.

"Alex was an accomplished sales professional and an expert in the coil processing industry. He worked for The Bradbury Group of companies for 17 years, sharing his knowledge with colleagues and customers around the world."■
