# AIDA-America confirms new hires

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

AIDA-America, a metal stamping press specialist in Dayton, Ohio (USA), recently announced new hires.

John Reynolds is a new project manager, after market solutions (AMS). He has more than ten years of experience with equipment rebuilds, updates and relocation, as well as project management. In his new position, John now manages assigned customer AMS projects, such as equipment upgrades and retrofits, from start to finish. He coordinates between internal departments and external suppliers, serving as a single point of customer contact.

Javed Gardezi was recently hired as project manager, after market solutions (AMS). He brings over 33 years of engineering experience, in addition to extensive project management experience, to his new position. He now manages assigned customer AMS projects, such as equipment upgrades and retrofits, from start to finish. He coordinates between internal departments and external suppliers, serving as a single point of customer contact.

Rob Maley was recently hired as marketing coordinator. He has over six years of marketing experience in the equipment industry, including designing marketing materials; promoting company products and initiatives via the company's website, social media platforms and news releases; and planning customer events. In his new position, he shares responsibility for all AIDA-America marketing efforts, as well as coordinating globally with other AIDA group companies.

AIDA is a global specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metalforming automation equipment.
