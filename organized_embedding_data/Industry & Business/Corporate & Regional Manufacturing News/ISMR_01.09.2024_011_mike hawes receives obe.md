# Mike Hawes receives OBE

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Mike Hawes, the Chief Executive of the Society of Motor Manufacturers and Traders (SMMT) in the UK, has been awarded an Officer of the Order of the British Empire (OBE) for services to the automotive industry in the King's Birthday Honours List.

Mike joined SMMT as chief executive in 2013, after several years in the automotive sector in the UK and overseas. He was charged with leading the UK industry's engagement with governments, stakeholders and the media internationally.

"Over more than a decade, Mike has led the industry through a period of technological, structural and geopolitical change, working tirelessly to ensure the UK sector's global competitiveness and reputation are enhanced. Many of the issues that have beset the sector during his tenure have required consensus building among the SMMT's diverse membership, something he has done with tact and respect," said the SMMT.

"From the Brexit trade negotiations, in which he was relentless in advocating a deal for automotive, to COVID-19 and the subsequent global supply chain shortages, the energy crisis and the unprecedented technological transformation to net zero, he has sought to safeguard jobs and businesses, promote the environmental benefits of new vehicle technology and help secure millions of pounds of inward investment," it added.

One of the greatest challenges facing the sector is the need to attract new talent. Mike was an influential advocate in the creation and work of Mission Automotive, which places ex-service personnel in jobs across the sector, signed SMMT to the Armed Forces Covenant and saw the organisation receive a Gold Award under the Defence Employer Recognition Scheme.

Mike also plays a pivotal role in the Automotive Council—a partnership formed in 2009 to foster collaboration between government and the automotive industry. As a main Board Member and Chair of the UK Competitiveness and Business Environment working group, he has driven work to enhance the UK's appeal and delivered the UK's first Automotive Diversity Charter, which counts every major UK-based automotive manufacturer as a signatory.

Mike joined SMMT from Bentley Motors, where he held director positions with responsibility for global communications, government affairs and corporate social responsibility. Before that, he held senior government and corporate affairs positions at Toyota, in the UK and Europe.
