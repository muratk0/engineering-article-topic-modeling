Dear OEM Supplier,

The COVID-19 pandemic has no doubt disrupted the global supply chain across the U.S. manufacturing industry. In an environment when speed-to-market is so critical, having a stable supply base is vital to the ongoing operations and the ability to meet current demand, launch new products, react to advances in technology, and most importantly, exceed customer expectations. Many OEMs are already reconsidering their supply chain strategies to include more localized suppliers and I want to assure you the members of the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

®

(FMA) are ready to support you.

Our technical affiliate partner, the Association of Steel Distributors (ASD), includes member distributors who can quickly provide the various metals your company needs to make agricultural equipment, medical diagnostic products, and food manufacturing equipment, among countless other products that are the lifeblood of your organization.

FMA job shop members are located across the U.S. and stand ready to provide the high precision sheet metal components, parts, and subassemblies that your company can rely on to be delivered in a cost-effective and timely manner that will enhance efficient production and profitability.

If your company’s product designs include tube and pipe specifications and components, members of our technical affiliate partner, the Tube & Pipe Association (TPA), are ready to work with your company to provide reliable and local supply chain partners to ensure your production and manufacturing operations are able to meet demand and respond to customer needs.

For the past 50 years, FMA’s members have supplied OEM manufacturers with the highest degree of professionalism in a trusted and reliable manner and they are ready to work with your organization to advance your success both in the short and long term. Please visit

fmanet.org/membership/directory

to search for FMA, ASD, and TPA members closest to your organization.
