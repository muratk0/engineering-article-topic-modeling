# Mobile robotics on display in Hannover

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Kivnon, a global specialist in the production of Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMRs) and Automated Guided Vehicles (AGVs), participated at Hannover Messe 2024 in April 2024 alongside the Spanish Association of Robotics and Automation (AER).

This collaboration marked a significant milestone in promoting mobile robotics innovation and excellence to the global audience at the trade fair.

"We were thrilled to debut our collaboration with the Spanish Robotics Association (AER) at Hannover Messe 2024," said Thierry Delmas, Managing Director, Kivnon. “Similar to Germany, sales of mobile robots in the Spanish market largely depend upon the automotive industry, and our combined expertise will help to fuel Kivnon's growth in the German market. This partnership underscores our commitment to driving innovation and empowering businesses to thrive in an increasingly competitive landscape."

Kivnon's AGVs are designed to optimise material handling processes and streamline operations. With the support and expertise of AER, Kivnon is looking to energise its regional expansion and technology offerings in Germany.

The international company has its headquarters in Barcelona, Spain. Since its inception as a family-owned business in 2009, it has offered advanced solutions for the implementation of Automated Guided Vehicle (AGV/AMR) systems. The company's projects in mobile robotics encompass the design; prototyping; manufacturing; installation; launch and ongoing technical assistance; and customer service. It serves customers in over seventeen countries and continues to expand through subsidiaries in Europe, North America and South America.

AER Automation, the Spanish Association of Robotics and Automation, is a non-profit association founded in 1985 and brings together players in the automation and industrial, service and educational robotics market. This includes manufacturers; distributors; engineers; integrators; technology centres; startups; universities; training centres and user companies. AER is a founding member of the International Federation of Robotics (IFR, 1987) and, since 2022, has been a member of CEOE, representing the robotics and automation sectors in Spain.■
