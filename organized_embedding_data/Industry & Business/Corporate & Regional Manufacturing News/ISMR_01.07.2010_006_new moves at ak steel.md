# NEW MOVES AT AK STEEL

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

AK Steel’s Board of Directors has approved the promotion of several members of its executive management team and other management changes. The executive management promotions are:

• David C. Horn, 58, to Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Secretary, from Senior Vice President, General Counsel and Secretary

• John F. Kaloski, 60, to Executive Vice President and Operating Officer, from Senior Vice President, Operations

• Albert E. Ferrara, Jr., 61, to Senior Vice President, Finance and Chief Financial Officer, from Vice President, Finance and Chief Financial Officer.

In addition, the company announced three new vice president positions - Keith J. Howell, 44, Vice President, Carbon Steel Operations; Kirk W. Reich, 42, Vice President, Specialty Steel Operations and Roger K. Newport, 45, Vice President, Business Planning and Development. In addition, Richard S. Williams, 51, has been named Controller and Chief Accounting Officer.

AK Steel, based in the US, produces flatrolled carbon, stainless and electrical steels, primarily for automotive, appliance, construction and electrical power generation and distribution markets.
