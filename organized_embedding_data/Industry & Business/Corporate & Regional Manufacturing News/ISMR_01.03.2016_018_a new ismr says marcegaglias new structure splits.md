# A NEW ISMR SAYS: “ Marcegaglia’s new structure splits off its diversified operations from the company’s core business of steel processing” STRUCTURE

[TARİH: 01.03.2016 ISMR]

Marcegaglia has reorganised its corporate structure to better serve international markets with its steel products and services

At the end of 2015, Italian manufacturer, Marcegaglia, launched a corporate reorganisation and new structure, focused on its core business of steel processing, to reach its objective of greater leadership in the steel industry. This is a deep-rooted internal change from its long-term vision which dates from the time Marcegaglia started to focus its financial and managerial resources on its core business, prioritizing the full use of its main assets in Italy and abroad.

“With the aim of greater transparency and clarity,” Chairman and CEO, Antonio Marcegaglia, told ISMR, “we have completed the split-off of our non-core equity holdings (tourism, white goods components, building etc.) from our core business, steel processing, which we aim to focus on and strengthen.”

Its new structure aims to split off its diversified operations from the company’s core business of steel processing, which the group intends to strengthen, so that it can develop ‘the widest range of steels in terms of thickness and grades in Italy and the world’.

## Greater transparency, greater growth

In over 50 years of operation, Marcegaglia has created a strong production mix strictly linked to the structure and organisation of the entire group, ranging from the processing of carbon and stainless steel tubes and flat products through to verticals in the building and construction industry (with the production of insulated panels and crash barriers, for example), and in electromechanical components, composites, energy and DIY sectors, to name a few. Its corporate reorganisation, completed on 1 November 2015, has led to a regrouping of Marcegaglia’s three main businesses – Marcegaglia Carbon Steel (carbon steel tubes and flat products), Marcegaglia Specialties (stainless steel tubes and flats and cold-drawn bars) and Marcegaglia Plates (heavy plate manufacturing) – in separate companies owned by one holding corporation, Marcegaglia Steel.

The operation had two main aims – on the one hand, greater transparency within and outside the group, and on the other, “our industrial objective is to achieve industry-leading growth, potentially by merging or forming alliances with other players, in a process which I believe may also involve the consolidation of the first and second transformation phases. We want to be actively present, we want to grow and we want to do it as the industry leader and, rather than adding capabilities, we believe this process based on external lines is better for the business’s profitability and sustainability,” Chairman, Antonio Marcegaglia, told ISMR.

## The flat universe

Production, quality and logistics are keywords for Marcegaglia. In the flat products sector, the group started an expansion path approximately 15 years ago pursuing a successful strategy in the tube sector to create a diversified network of sourcing-production-distribution for horizontal, downstream and upstream growth.

“As a result, today, we can be considered as a unique player in the European steel industry and on a world scale, guided by key values such as synergies (raw material purchasing, processing, logistics, market), economies of scale, production flexibility and diversification of products and markets,” the company explained. “All these values are translated into flat production (carbon and stainless steel coils, sheet and narrow strip) including colour-coated steel production and heavy (quarto) plates. We are an independent player who can supply other service centres as well as specialist customers, offering a wide range of products in terms of steel grades and typical applications, a unique mix of services and quick response times with stock availability in standard sizes.”

On the production side, Marcegaglia has developed new high-grade quality and niche products, increased its flexibility in zinc coating up to Z800 (allowing a different coating between sides) and developed new grades such as dual phase DP600, DP800 and DP1000. The coil colour-coating units can count on more than 25 standard painting cycles that can be combined for specific surface requirements with over 1200 colours.

Marcegaglia’s production programme of heavy (quarto) plates has also been optimised and a wide array of end uses are now covered, with specific certifications for boilers, pressure vessels and naval construction (ASTM 131, grades A, B, D, RINA approval), amongst others.

Another focus for the group is the efficiency of its distribution network, connecting manufacturing plants with each other and with customers. Marcegaglia has been able to establish its presence in new areas through the use of intermodal terminals (hubs) strategically located in harbours on the Mediterranean Sea, but also on the Atlantic Ocean, the North Sea and the Baltic Sea, as well as storage areas near important railway connections. This network has significantly cut delivery times as well as carbon emissions: a network of more that ten logistic hubs across Europe has considerably enhanced its flexibility and just-in-time service.

Marcegaglia’s new corporate structure will be formally presented to the industry at the forthcoming Tube exhibition in Düsseldorf, Germany (4-8 April, 2016). See Marcegaglia on its stand D30 in Hall 3. ■
