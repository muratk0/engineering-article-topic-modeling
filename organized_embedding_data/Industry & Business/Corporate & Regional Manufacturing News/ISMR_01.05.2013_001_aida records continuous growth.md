# AIDA records continuous growth

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

AIDA, with one hundred years of experience and a leading position in manufacturing servo and mechanical stamping presses, as well as complete metalforming automation systems, has strengthened its positioning through a process of continuous product development and growth. It has successfully combined the power of its global resources with the advantages of local services and after-sales support.

AIDA has increased manufacturing capacity and sales and support offices globally, including Japan, China, Malaysia, the United States and Europe; thanks to these expansions, it has now doubled its global manufacturing area.

“An example of our continuous growth is the headquarters for AIDA’s activity in Europe, located in Pavone Mella near Brescia, which is undergoing an important expansion. In a 55,200–square metre total area, we are building new offices spanning over 1600 square metres which will be in addition to the existing 2100 square metres of office space. The expansion also includes a new 190-square metre automatic warehouse (for spare parts) and a 1900-square metre covered area. The project also includes a 5800-square metre enlargement of the press assembly department. These facility improvements will be completed in Spring 2013,” the manufacturer told ISMR.

AIDA is also increasing the number of employees in several departments including research and development, engineering, production, after-sales service and marketing and sales.
