# STAMP OF APPROVAL

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

STEINEL has evolved from a standard parts supplier to a tooling solutions partner. Quality, affordability and reliability have become its watchwords, it told ISMR.

It has been a busy year for STEINEL, the German tooling solutions specialist. ISMR caught up with Christoph Neudörffer (CEO), Christian Pape (Head of Sales/Marketing) and Christel Schlenker (responsible for product management and marketing) on STEINEL’s booth at this year’s Blechexpo exhibition in Stuttgart, to uncover its latest initiatives and product developments.

“We installed a new warehouse management system at the beginning of the year to improve our delivery times to customers – we currently have over 20,000 products in the catalogue. This was a big investment for us and has helped us to improve our delivery and warehousing processes,” Christoph Neudörffer told ISMR.

“We also invested towards the end of this year in a second measuring machine from Zeiss for manufacturing special steel plates for die sets which have already been installed. It is an important step to improving the quality of our production processes and products.

## An eye on quality

Whether milling; water jet cutting; grinding; electrical discharge machining (EDM) or honing, STEINEL performs almost all of the in-house operations required for the production of its tooling. Its products are tested at various stages of development using technology such as measurement microscopes with image processing, surface roughness measuring instruments, 3D measuring units and hardness testers.

“We are a standard parts supplier as well as a system solutions partner. We deliver and manufacture nitrogen systems, small tools etc. We have expanded our product range in discussions with our customer as a trusted partner and supplier. We offer the complete range of tooling products necessary for STEINEL toolmakers in stamping and pressing industries, starting from guiding units up to high-end tooling for high speed presses,” explained Christian Pape.

In addition to standard parts, STEINEL also offers nitrogen cylinders, manifold plates and tapping units. It produces sophisticated active elements and tool assemblies at its secondary location in Halblech/Buching, Germany.

“Our main markets in Europe are Germany and Switzerland,” continued Pape. “We are also trying to sell into other regions. We have our own sales organisations in Germany and Switzerland - we cooperate with distribution partners elsewhere. Last year, we were at FABTECH in Atlanta and we were at the show in Chicago last month. This year, we have placed special emphasis on North American and Asian markets.”

Globalisation, Christoph Neudörffer added, is both a challenge and an opportunity. To develop from a standard parts supplier to a system partner has been a very demanding process, he told ISMR.

“Our main target is not quick levels of growth – we are more interested in sustainable, steady and solid levels of business. We need to keep innovating as much as possible and are completely dedicated to researching the solutions needed by our customers,” he explained.

## STEINEL at Blechexpo

With a booth space of 120m² at Blechexpo in Stuttgart, as well as numerous product highlights and innovations, STEINEL showcased a cross-section of its extensive services portfolio, from standard parts and tool components through to complete tool systems for progressive stamping as well as punching and bending technology. This year, the main focus of its presentation was on guide units, nitrogen systems, roll taps and tool technology.

In addition to its standard products, STEINEL placed particular emphasis on customised solutions and presented itself as a troubleshooter for guide units during its demonstration. Guides that were custom-made for users across all industries, as well as their special features, were shown for the first time.

We are a standard parts supplier as well as a system solutions partner

“Our high precision standard guide units for the tool shop have been established on the market for years. Less well known is that we also develop and produce individual guide units across all industries. With our own presentation platform on the subject, we wanted to show visitors our expertise in this field,” explained Christel Schlenker.

Further tool sector innovations were on display at the booth. At this year's Blechexpo, STEINEL presented its newly-designed module system - available as a standard product.

Frank Muckenfuss, project manager for tool technology at STEINEL, added: “Our module system combines the advantages of a standardised tool with the highest possible flexibility for individual parts production. It comprises a standardised basic design and individual tool modules that are interchangeable. As this intelligent modular system is now also available as a standard product, our customers are guaranteed a quick overview and short delivery times.”

In addition to application examples, multimedia presentations showcased the use of STEINEL components in the tool as well as their distinctive features and functions. The theme island provided insights into nitrogen systems. In addition to nitrogen cylinders and composite systems, a manifold plate that provided visitors with an extensive insight into pressure equipment technology was displayed in the cross-section.
