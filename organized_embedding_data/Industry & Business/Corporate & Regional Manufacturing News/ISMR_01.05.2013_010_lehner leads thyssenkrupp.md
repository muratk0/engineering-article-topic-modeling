# Lehner leads THYSSENKRUPP

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

PROF. DR. ULRICH LEHNER, who took over as Chairman of the Supervisory Board of ThyssenKrupp AG from 1 April 2013, has decided to resign from a total of three directorships to concentrate on his duties at ThyssenKrupp.

Effective as of 31 March 2013, Prof. Lehner will step down as member of the supervisory board of Henkel Management AG. In addition, he will resign from his seat on the advisory board of Dr. August Oetker KG and his supervisory board directorship with Porsche Automobil Holding SE as part of an orderly succession process.
