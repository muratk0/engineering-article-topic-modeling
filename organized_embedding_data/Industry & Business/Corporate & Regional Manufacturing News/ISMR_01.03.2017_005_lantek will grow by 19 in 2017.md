# Lantek 'will grow by 19% in 2017'

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

GLOBAL REPRESENTATIVES OF sheet metal management software specialist Lantek, gathered from 24-26 January 2017 in Miñano (Araba/Álava) to share results, ideas, reflections and targets for 2017. The Lantek team confirmed its growth strategy and the most important changes for this year, including the opening of new offices.

Lantek, a specialist in the development, commercialisation and integration of management software solutions (CAD/CAM, MES, ERP, Advanced Manufacturing) for the sheet metal industry, began 2017 with growth forecasts of 19% on its current turnover. This increase, it told ISMR, will be most evident in European and U.S. markets, where the company has already achieved significant increases in turnover.

"Lantek's growth is mainly due to the increase of its turnover in the solutions area, more than 68% compared to last year, and also in CAD/CAM, where it hopes to continue growing at a figure close to 7%. Europe continues to be the market where large growth is predicted, especially in countries such as Spain, Germany, France and the United Kingdom. Another country where a significant increase in turnover is predicted is the USA," explained the company.

Alberto Martínez, CEO of Lantek, commented on the growth forecasts: "They add to the good results achieved by our company throughout 2016, with increases which have exceeded 20% in areas such as Spain and Portugal. In this way, we have already achieved three consecutive years with significant increases in our turnover, which have enabled us to reach more than 18,000 customers on five continents.

"We also predict a significant increase in maintenance services and the consolidation and increase of large projects such as those with John Deere and Salzgitter. We will also enhance current alliances with our partners and will work to form new ones within the framework of Advanced Manufacturing. Finally, we will encourage new agreements with global OEMs."

During 2016, Lantek significantly increased its turnover in many of the countries in its network. The increase in markets such as France (24%), Spain (22%), Italy (19%), Canada (17%) and China (11%) was particularly highlighted. The company plans to incorporate around 30 new people into its workforce.
