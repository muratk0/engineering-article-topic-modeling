# CECIMO confirms new leadership roles

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

During its General Assembly, the European Association of Manufacturing Technologies (CECIMO) announced the appointment of François Duval as its new President for a two-year term, succeeding Dr. Heinz-Jürgen Prokop. This leadership transition also sees Riccardo Rosa take on the role of Chairman of the Technical Committee.

CECIMO delegates also confirmed Marcus Burton as Chairman of the Economic Committee and Michael Merkle as Chairman of the Communication and Advocacy Committee, for a further term of office. Spain holds one of the permanent vice presidencies of CECIMO, represented by José Pérez Berdud, President of AFM Cluster.

François Duval, recognised for his contributions to the machine tool industry, reiterated his appreciation for the new role: “I’m truly honoured to take on the role of CECIMO President for the next two years, and I look forward to working together on our shared goals. Especially in these challenging times, my main priorities are supporting innovation in the manufacturing sector and ensuring that European regulations allow businesses to thrive and remain competitive," he said.

Mr. Duval's commitment to industry development underscores the interdependence of innovation and skilled talent.

“As manufacturing solutions become more complex, the need for expertise becomes paramount. To truly reap the benefits of the innovations we offer, we must provide our customers with unwavering support and guidance. Complex innovation requires a skilled and talented workforce and a commitment to helping customers realise their full potential," he highlighted.

With a degree in engineering from the Institut Catholique d’Arts et Métiers (ICAM) in Lille, Mr. Duval's career spans almost three decades within the FIVES.

Group. Throughout his career, he has held prominent management positions. He has been managing director of GF Machining Solutions France since July 2020. He has over two decades of experience in the machine tool industry, including six years as President of the Machine Tool Manufacturers and Importers Group in the French trade association, SYMOP (now EVOLIS).

Mr. Duval began his career in the machine tool sector as managing director of Cinetic Machining, where he oversaw the development of Rouchaud milling and laser welding solutions for the automotive powertrain, aluminium extrusion milling and routers for the aerospace industry, and machine tool reconditioning.

Duval’s subsequent acquisition of Metrap, the largest remanufacturer in France, marked a significant milestone in his career. By merging Metrap with Cinetic Machining, he created Fives Machining, a testament to his strategic vision and leadership.

He has also participated and gained experience in powder bed fusion and direct energy deposition as a Board Member of AddUp, a JV between Fives and Michelin. He has a broad international career with extensive experience in Eastern Europe, Asia and Australia.
