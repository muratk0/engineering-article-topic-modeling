## New CTO for Schuler

DR.-ING. STEPHAN ARNOLD (52) will become the new Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of Schuler AG on 1 April 2016. The engineer has been head of group-wide development at Miele & Cie. KG since 2010. He previously held various management positions for the Volkswagen Group in Germany and the USA over a period of thirteen years. Amongst other things, he led projects on electro-mobility, headed a technology centre and was responsible for the assembly of prototype vehicles. He gained his PhD at the RWTH Aachen University, Germany.

Dr. Arnold will succeed Joachim Beyer (60) who is leaving the company, at his own request, when his contract expires on 7 April 2016. Beyer served the company for thirteen years – eleven of which was as a member of the Executive Board. He previously held management positions at Müller Weingarten AG, which now belongs to the Schuler Group. He will continue to be available for Schuler in an advisory capacity for the next few years. n

## Kent Franklin joins JETCAM

JETCAM INTERNATIONAL S.A.R.L. has announced that Kent Franklin has joined the company to support its US dealers and customers as a JETCAM technical support engineer. Kent is employed by JETCAM’s US entity, JETCAM USA Inc, and is based in Wisconsin.

“Kent has accumulated 21 years of experience using and support JETCAM Expert across a wide range of punching and profiling equipment. His role is focused on providing assistance to JETCAM’s dealer and customer base in the Americas and Canada, along with working with the JETCAM technical team on assisting with postprocessor development and testing of new product functionality,” commented the CADCAM software company.

Said Kent; “As a longstanding JETCAM user for many years, it’s great to be on the other side of the fence, share my knowledge and help others. It’s very exciting to see what’s coming in the development process and get to learn and test it ahead of general releases.” n

## Mate expands global presence

MATE PRECISION TOOLING, a leading manufacturer of tooling solutions for sheet metal fabricators, has named Michael Brown to the position of 'Vice President, International Sales'. A seasoned sales veteran with more than 20 years of experience, Brown will be responsible for planning and managing all of Mate's international sales activities.

With significant experience in managing key accounts and knowledge of selling via dealers, Brown's focus will include developing strategies and team capabilities to further strengthen customer relationships.

"Michael brings a wealth of strategic insight and international sales experience to our senior leadership team," said Kevin Nicholson, Mate President. "We look forward to his contributions to expand our strong international presence and introduce our proven tooling solutions to more companies around the world."■

## Driving sales at ST/SIFCO

MARK CATTLE has been appointed as sales director of Norman Hay’s specialist Coatings Group, formed of Surface Technology and SIFCO ASC. After formalising the collaboration this year, the organisation recognised the need to appoint a sales director to maximise synergies between the businesses which aim to provide a single global access point in surface coating technology.

“Mark, who also leads sister company NHE’s commercial function, has a track record of proactively driving sales growth in new and established sectors and managing complex sales and buying processes in product and service environments. He will be responsible for the sales and new business development team of the Coatings Group, identifying opportunities to share best practices across customer groups,” the organisation told ISMR.
