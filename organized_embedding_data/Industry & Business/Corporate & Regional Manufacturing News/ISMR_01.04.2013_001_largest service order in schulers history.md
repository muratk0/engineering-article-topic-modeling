# Largest service order in Schuler’s history

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

SCHULER recently received an order from a major German car manufacturer to carry out a retrofit on two press lines from 1998. With a value of approximately €18 million, it is the largest service contract that Schuler has ever been awarded.

“Our expertise in conducting such large-scale projects was a compelling argument for the customer,” said Holger Garbrecht, Head of the Technology Field Service. The two compact crossbar press lines at two different locations in southern Germany will each receive a new electric transfer system. The service team will also be overhauling the blankloaders and draw cushions, modernising the electrical and mechanical elements and adapting the electric control system. Retrofit of the first press line will begin at the end of the year and is due to be completed by mid-2014 – the second line retrofit will then follow.

“The retrofit will not only raise the productivity of both lines considerably, but also improve their energy efficiency,” added Holger Garbrecht. The press lines are used to produce car body and structural parts for mid-range and premium models.

A further retrofit is currently being conducted on another press line for this car manufacturer – Schuler is overhauling its electrical, mechanical and hydraulic systems. The line was disassembled in November last year and is expected to be ready for production again as early as Spring. ■
