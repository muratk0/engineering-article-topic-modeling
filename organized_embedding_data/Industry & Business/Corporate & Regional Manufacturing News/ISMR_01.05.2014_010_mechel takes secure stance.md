# Mechel takes secure stance

[TARİH: 01.05.2014 ISMR]

MECHEL OAO, one of the leading Russian mining and metals companies, has confirmed a new executive appointment. Valery Sheverdin, 50, is Mechel’s new vice-president for corporate security.

“In the seven years that Valery has been working in our company, he has proved himself an excellent professional and experienced leader, capable of tackling the most complicated tasks. I am sincerely happy to welcome him to our top management and wish him further successes in his work,” Mechel OAO’s chief executive officer ,Oleg Korzhov, noted.

Mechel is an international mining and steel company which employs over 70,000 people. Its products are marketed in Europe, Asia, North and South America and Africa. Mechel unites producers of coal, iron ore concentrate, steel, rolled products, ferroalloys, heat and electric power. ■
