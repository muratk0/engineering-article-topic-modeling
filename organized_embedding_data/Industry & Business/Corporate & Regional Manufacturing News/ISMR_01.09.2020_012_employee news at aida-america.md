# Employee news at AIDA-America

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

PRESS SPECIALIST AIDA-America has been expanding several departments across its organisation and taking on new associates. Josephine Bell was recently hired as sales administrator/receptionist, with over seven years of customer service experience, including completing customer orders on time in a detail-oriented environment, as well as managing and training a team.

“Josephine prepares customer proposals, assists the Sales Administration team in taking care of AIDA’s customers in many facets, answers calls on the main phone line and greets all our visitors,” AIDA-America told ISMR.

Jill Post was recently hired as project manager.

“Jill brings more than 20 years of engineering and project management experience, as well as proven success with cross-functional teams, to our Project Management Team. Jill will be a single, dedicated point of contact coordinating with internal departments and external vendors throughout the life of each assigned customer press project,” added the company.

AIDA is a specialist in the design, manufacture, sale, service and support, refurbishment and modernisation of metal stamping presses and metal forming automation equipment. AIDA servo and mechanical stamping presses range in capacity from 30 to 4,000 tons, from 1 to 1,500 strokes per minute, from gap frame to straightside, high speed to cold forging, transfer and progressive die, material handling and coil feeding equipment.
