# Marina Bill becomes IFR Vice President

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Marina Bill has been appointed as new Vice President of the International Federation of Robotics (IFR) with immediate effect. She represents IFR along with IFR President, Milton Guerry.

The IFR Executive Board elected Marina Bill as IFR Vice President following Klaus Koenig's resignation from his IFR positions. Marina Bill is Global Head of Marketing and Sales Robotics, ABB, as well as a member of the IFR Executive Board and current Chair of the Robot Suppliers' Committee.

"There hasn't been a more exciting time to be in our industry than right now," stated Marina Bill after her election. "We are in a period of unprecedented transformation, for robotic automation itself and in the rapid acceleration and adaptation of its use across a wide range of industry sectors. I'm looking forward to helping steer that transformation, through the IFR."
