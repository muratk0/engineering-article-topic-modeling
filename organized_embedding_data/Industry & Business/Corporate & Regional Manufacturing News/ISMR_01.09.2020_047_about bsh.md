# About BSH

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

BSH Hausgeräte GmbH was founded in Munich in 1967 as a joint venture between Robert Bosch GmbH (Stuttgart) and Siemens AG (Munich) and is one of the world’s leading companies in the industry and the largest home appliance manufacturer in Europe. BSH produces the entire spectrum of modern household appliances in 40 factories worldwide. Its product portfolio ranges from cookers, ovens and extractor hoods to dishwashers, washing machines, dryers, refrigerators and freezers, as well as small household appliances such as vacuum cleaners, fully automatic coffee machines and food processors. Since 2015, the company has been fully owned by the Bosch Group and currently employs some 58,000 associates.

## About Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH

Burghardt + Schmidt GmbH was founded in 1945 and manufactures machines and equipment for cutting and levelling metal strips. The products are tailored to the needs of the customers. The company has many years of experience in the thin strip sector and specialises in slitting lines; stretch-bend-levelling lines; packaging lines; traverse winders; levelling machines; cut-to-length lines and strip processing lines.

At its German factory in Remchingen, systems are developed and manufactured with the quality feature “Made in Germany“. With the acquisition of other companies, the b+s group was founded in 2014.

Through its subsidiaries Schnutz GmbH and Delta Technik GmbH, Burghardt + Schmidt’s previous product range had been expanded into levelling machines, strip feeding systems and automation technology.
