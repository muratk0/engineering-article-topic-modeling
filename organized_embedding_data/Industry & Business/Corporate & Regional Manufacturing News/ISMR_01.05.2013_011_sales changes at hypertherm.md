# Sales changes at HYPERTHERM

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

Marco Mostert, Hypertherm's new OEM sales director, Europe

Witold Szpakowski, Hypertherm consumable sales manager for Central Eastern Europe and Russia

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of advanced cutting systems, has announced its new OEM sales director, Europe, as Marco Mostert.

Marco joined Hypertherm on 1 February 2013 and will be responsible for the management of the European OEM sales team, which is charged with the ongoing development and growth of Hypertherm’s mechanised business with OEMs operating in the region. Hypertherm’s mechanised product offering includes mechanised plasma, fiber laser and waterjet products as well as torches and consumables, automation products and CAM software.

For the last 12 years, Marco worked for Nordson, a leading manufacturer of products and systems for dispensing of adhesives, coatings, sealants and biomaterials. In his roles as regional sales manager and market development manager, Marco was responsible for the EMEA region.

In March, the company also confirmed the addition of Witold Szpakowski to its European sales team. Mr. Szpakowski joined Hypertherm on 1 February 2013 as the consumable sales manager for Central Eastern Europe and Russia. His primary focus will be to expand the company’s channel partner network for its consumable product line and to support its partners with all questions relating to its torches and consumables, technologies and upgrade opportunities.

Witold Szpakowski holds a degree in political science from the University of Gdansk. In addition to his native Polish, he is fluent in Russian and English. Witold will work from his home office in Gdansk, Poland.
