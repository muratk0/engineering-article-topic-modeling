Reshoring Award co-winners announced

GE Appliances and Marlin Steel have been named co-winners of the 2025 National Metalworking Reshoring Award.

The award—made possible by the Reshoring Initiative, the Precision Metalforming Association, AMT– The Association For Manufacturing Technology, SME, the National Tooling and Machining Association, and the Fabricators and Manufacturers Association (FMA)—honors companies that have contributed to the reshoring trend by effectively reshoring products, parts, or tooling made primarily by metal forming, fabricating, casting, machining, or additive manufacturing.

GE Appliances was recognized for leadership and continued investment in U.S. manufacturing. In June 2025, the company announced a $490 million investment to reshore production of washers and washer/dryers from China. This move is expected to create 800 full-time jobs in 2027. It was the first phase of a more than $3 billion plan to shift production of refrigerators, gas ranges, and water heaters out of China and Mexico over the next five years.

Marlin Steel accepted the award in recognition of leadership in reshoring and domestic manufacturing resilience. The company reshored multiple production lines from diverse industries, including medical device, food processing, and aerospace, repeatedly demonstrating that automation, engineering, quality, and delivery can enable reshoring even of high-volume commoditized products.

Robinson Inc. to open facility in Iowa

Robinson Inc., a custom metal manufacturer based in De Pere, Wis., has announced it will lease 560,000 sq. ft. of newly constructed, build-to-suit manufacturing space outside of Altoona, Iowa, creating 100 jobs.

The city of Altoona is investing $9 million for infrastructure improvements, and Robinson will invest $76 million for upgrades to the building. Occupancy is targeted for January 2027.

The facility will provide flexible production spaces for metal bending, large-format cutting, metal forming, welding, fabricating and assembly, and electrical integration. This expansion gives the manufacturer close to 1.4 million sq. ft. of manufacturing space for customized production in both Wisconsin and Iowa.

"Expansion outside of our home state of Wisconsin is critical to provide a dependable supply chain for our growing customer base," said Sam Thomas, president. "This site is conveniently located near Interstate 80, a major supply route for our customers in the energy sector."

SencorpWhite establishes U.S. headquarters in Ohio

SencorpWhite, a provider of end-to-end automated storage systems for the manufacturing, medical, and retail industries, has selected Hamilton, Ohio, for its new U.S. headquarters. The project will create 230 jobs.

The company, which manufactures vertical carousels, horizontal carousels, vertical lift modules, and proprietary software, is transferring highly automated manufacturing operations from Sweden to Ohio. This shift will reduce lead times and ocean freight costs and increase global production capacity for its Weland product line.

Fraser Steel adds laser tube cutting system

Tube fabricating company Fraser Steel has installed an LT7 Lasertube cutting system from BLM GROUP USA at its 80,000-sq.-ft. facility in Albertville, Minn. With advanced laser tube cutting capabilities and seamless automation, the machine offers increased turnaround speed and accuracy, the company reports.

Kloeckner Metals announces start of operations in Iowa

Kloeckner Metals Corp., Roswell, Ga., has started operations at the former Bauer Built Manufacturing site in Paton, Iowa. Retaining the Bauer workforce, the 300,000-sq.-ft. facility enhances the company’s ability to deliver heavy fabrication, welding, assembly, and complex finishing services for agricultural and industrial customers.

The site, operating as Kloeckner Manufacturing Services, includes the company’s first in-house industrial painting capability, allowing for the delivery of painted subassemblies. It also offers in-house engineering and fixturing capabilities to support custom designs, modifications to existing prints, and the manufacture of products from pre-existing prints. Kloeckner Metals Corp. is a metal processor and service center with about 2,800 employees at 45+ branches across the U.S. and Mexico.

Salvagnini America adds 28 jobs at North American hub

Salvagnini America, a division of Italy-based Salvagnini Group, will expand its presence in Hamilton, Ohio, adding 28 jobs while retaining 118 existing positions, including 50 positions across the country.

Steelhead Technologies announces $84 million growth capital investment

Calumet, Mich.-based Steelhead Technologies, a provider of all-in-one ERP software made for manufacturing job shops, has announced an $84 million growth investment from Mainsail Partners, a growth equity firm that partners with vertical SaaS businesses. Manufacturing Corp. of America announces joint venture launch

Manufacturing Corp. of America has announced the launch of MetalPeak Fabrication in Hunker, Pa., a joint venture between MetalOne Fabrication (a wholly owned MCA company) and EHC Industries.

The new company combines EHC’s customer relationships and fabrication expertise with the scale, advanced machinery, and large-format manufacturing capabilities of MetalOne Fabrication. It offers endto end capabilities spanning prototype-to-production sheet metal fabrication, welding, rolling, forming, punching, laser cutting, tube and pipe processing, and finishing.

Equipment includes flat sheet plate punching and laser cutting systems, multiple press brakes for bending up to 320 tons, a 27-ft. fiber tube laser with pipe and tube bending capabilities, and on-site hardware insertion and assembly.

The venture will serve industries including transportation, technology, aerospace, HVAC, manufacturing, air filtration, nuclear medicine, electrical enclosures, and data center infrastructure.

Titan Abrasive Systems installs blast room at Fox Tank Co.

Titan Abrasive Systems, a designer and manufacturer of surface preparation, surface finishing, and abrasive blasting equipment, has installed a blast room at Fox Tank Co.’s Coshocton, Ohio, facility. Fox Tank is a manufacturer of tanks, high-pressure vessels, and modular equipment packages for the oil and gas industry.

The 16- by 16- by 50-ft. Titan blast room replaces an older, makeshift blasting setup that had reached production capacity. With the recent consolidation of its three Texas locations into the expanded Kerrville, Texas, facility, along with the Coshocton plant, Fox Tank sought an industrial-grade blasting system that could deliver higher throughput, longer-lasting components, and a more consistent blast profile.

Midwest Equipment Manufacturing to locate in Kentucky

Midwest Equipment Manufacturing Inc. has announced plans for a $15 million expansion of its operations in Maysville, Ky., creating 66 skilled jobs. The expanded facility will serve as a hub for manufacturing and help alleviate labor shortages and production backlogs affecting other locations.

The strategy includes continued production of existing product lines while relocating several key operations to Maysville. The Tru-Cut mower product line and the 500 series Brown Bear composter will be moved from Thorntown, Ind., to Maysville. Following that, the Trac Vac lawn vacuums, Tru-Cut reel mowers, Brown Bear aerator, Broyhill golf course equipment, Packer Brothers plate compactors, PowerTek rental wood chipper, Broyhill Load and Pack refuse equipment, and Arrow Master concrete breaker product lines also will be transitioned to the new facility.

Epic Piping expands Louisiana operations
