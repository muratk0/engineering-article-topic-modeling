# Industrial cooling

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

Seeley International is a designer and manufacturer of evaporative cooling products. Evaporative cooling is becoming a very efficient option for cooling large areas, especially industrial and commercial spaces. Traditional air conditioning in warehouses or industrial plants can sometimes rack up considerable capital and running costs.

"Most industries with high heat loads have no cooling system installed, and this impacts on workers' morale and productivity, as well as on their health. In all these examples, Direct Evaporative Cooling could be the right solution," commented the manufacturer.

"Seeley coolers do not use any sort of chemical refrigerants to cool the air, just a pump to supply water to the wetted media where evaporation occurs. A small amount of electricity is required for the pump and the fan which pushes the air inside the building. Evaporative air cooling uses up to 87% less energy than conventional air conditioning," it added.

Indoor air quality (IAQ) is high on the agenda to maintain a healthy and comfortable environment.

"Our products supply 100% fresh filtered outside air; we do not recirculate so spaces are effectively being purged when our coolers are being used, removing dust, dirt, allergens and airborne viruses. With zero environmental impact from chemical refrigerants and low carbon running, that all adds up to very responsible environmental solution. With a warranty of up to 25 years, customers can consider this an investment for the long term," commented Seeley.

If the ambient air (outside the building) is cold and the temperature inside the building is hot, which is the case for many manufacturing companies with high heat-emitting machinery, evaporative coolers can also work in 'fan only mode' to provide "free cooling" (which reduces water consumption while maintaining a comfortable environment). Spot cooling can also be used for any small areas inside the space that need cooling.
