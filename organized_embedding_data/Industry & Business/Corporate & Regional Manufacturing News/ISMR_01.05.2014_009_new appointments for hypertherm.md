# New appointments for Hypertherm

[TARİH: 01.05.2014 ISMR]

HYPERTHERM, a manufacturer of plasma, laser and waterjet cutting systems, recently announced two new appointments.

Georgio Audisio becomes its new waterjet sales manager, Europe. Since the acquisition of AccuStream in 2013, Hypertherm has been determined to grow its waterjet business in the EMEA region. Giorgio Audisio will be responsible for building, expanding and managing the network of Hypertherm’s HyPrecision™ waterjet accounts within the EMEA region. For the past 10 years, he served Hypertherm as the OEM sales manager for Italy, Spain, Portugal and Greece and has, since 2014, been engaged on an intensive training programme including familiarisation with Hypertherm’s HyPrecision waterjet offering, travelling to its Minnesota-based waterjet production and R&D centre. Email giorgio.audisio@hypertherm.com

Walter Eiselt is the successor for Giorgio Audisio. He is now responsible for the OEM business for Hypertherm’s mechanised product offerings including mechanised plasma as well as torches and consumables, automation products and CAM software. Walter Eiselt joined Hypertherm in 2008 as the distribution sales manager for Italy. He was responsible for managing Hypertherm’s distribution network in Italy and building sales for Hypertherm’s manual system product line and consumables. Email walter.eiselt@hypertherm.com ■
