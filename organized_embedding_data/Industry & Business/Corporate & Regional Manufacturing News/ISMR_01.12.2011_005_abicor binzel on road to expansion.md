# ABICOR BINZEL ON ROAD TO EXPANSION

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

WITH THE official opening of its first owned branch in the Middle East, gas-shielded welding specialist, ABICOR BINZEL, laid the cornerstone for expansion in the Middle East growth region.

"To offer local partners fast delivery and ‘service on the spot’, ABICOR BINZEL Middle East FZE with its large logistics centre will now serve as the hub for the entire Middle East region. Because of its central location, customers in the oil and gas industry as well as the ship-, tank- and steel construction business can be reached more rapidly. As a result, the normally enormous freight costs can be greatly reduced. In addition, training, seminars and practical demonstrations on-site are now also possible.

“We are the first manufacturer of gas-shielded torches to open a branch in the booming Middle East region. In the coming years, the main focus will be put on the expansion of the retailer network and the development of new markets," it told ISMR. FOR MORE INFORMATION, PLEASE SEE www.binzel-abicor.com

Prof. Dr. Emil Schubert, managing director of the Alexander Binzel Schweisstechnik GmbH & Co. KG in Alten-Buseck, and Manfred Stöhr, business development and key account manager international at ABICOR BINZEL, opened the subsidiary ABICOR BINZEL Middle East FZE in the Ras Al Khaimah Freezone earlier this year.

## SEE ‘INSIDE’ A PROCESS

Output data from the Innoval Ingot Preheating Model

MATHEMATICAL MODELS, developed by Innoval technology, make it possible to ‘see inside’ a working process and obtain parameters which are physically impossible to measure. The models, which are being launched for the aluminium rolling industry, will greatly enhance process optimisation.

For example, the Innoval Ingot Preheating Model makes it possible to obtain the temperature at any position within an ingot, and at any time, during a heating cycle without having to use thermocouples. This ‘internal vision’ means that process engineers can really understand what is happening within their process and optimise it to increase productivity, to improve product quality or to save energy.

The five process models, covering ingot preheating, rolling, mill vibration, spray cooling and coil heating and cooling, are available for purchase under a license agreement from Innoval technology. FOR MORE INFORMATION, PLEASE VISIT www.innovaltec.com/processmodelsoverview.htm
