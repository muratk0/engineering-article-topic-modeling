# Olympic Steel confirms new appointments

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

OLYMPIC STEEL INC., a U.S. metals service centre, has announced the appointment of Vanessa L. Whiting to the Company's Board of Directors. She will serve as an independent director. Whiting is president of A.E.S Management, the Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen franchisee in Northeast Ohio. She is also an attorney, who has been named one of the top 100 lawyers by Northern Ohio Live, with extensive experience in real estate and small business enterprise law. A 2019 Cleveland Crain's Women of Note honouree, Whiting has been recognized for her local civic involvement in addition to her professional success.

Olympic Steel Inc. has also announced the appointment of Daniel E. Harnois to General Manager, Berlin Metals (effective from 1 November 2019). In this position, Harnois will be responsible for driving profitable growth for Berlin Metals, and will report directly to Andrew Wolfort, Regional Vice President and General Manager. He most recently served as the Director of Sales at Outokumpu, a global provider of stainless steel. Berlin Metals is an Olympic Steel company that specialises in tin mill products, stainless steel strip, cold rolled steel and galvanized steel.

Olympic Steel Inc. focuses on the direct sale of processed carbon; coated and stainless flat-rolled sheet, coil and plate steel; aluminium; tin plate and metal-intensive branded products. Headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio, it operates from 30 facilities in North America. See www.olysteel.com.
