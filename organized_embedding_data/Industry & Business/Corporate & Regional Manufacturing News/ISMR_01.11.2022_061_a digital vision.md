# A DIGITAL VISION

[TARİH: 01.11.2022 ISMR]

Harkaitz Luengo, managing director of ATHADER S.L., outlined his vision and strategy for the coil-processing specialist's continued growth to ISMR.

ATHADER S.L., based in Spain and part of The Bradbury Group, specialises in the design and manufacturing of slitting; cut-to-length; levelling; multi-blanking and recoiling lines as well as coil/sheet automated packaging systems. The company is celebrating its 30th anniversary. With second-generation family members at the helm, it pursues a strategy of continuous improvement and modernisation.

The coil-processing specialist has two strategic partners: Leveltek International LLC — Stretch Leveling Equipment and Samuel Nelson Technology – Steel Pickling Equipment. Over the last two years, with Leveltek International, it has successfully supplied and installed five stretch-levelling lines, one in Australia and four in Europe.

“Due to the sustained increase in our order book, two strategic decisions were made in 2022. We increased our manufacturing facilities in the Basque Country with the acquisition of three additional workshops. We also opened a new branch office in North America to assist the customer service and sales support team in the area, due to the significant number of installations on the continent," it told ISMR.

## Supporting growth

"ATHADER has experienced substantial growth, especially over the last ten years. Our turnover in 2022 is five times our turnover in 2012 and we supplied three times more coil-processing lines this year than ten years ago," its managing director, Harkaitz Luengo, explained to ISMR.
