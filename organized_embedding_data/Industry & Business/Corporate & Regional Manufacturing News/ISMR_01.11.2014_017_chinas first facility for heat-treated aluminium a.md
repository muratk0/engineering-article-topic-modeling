# China’s first facility for heat-treated aluminium automotive sheet

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

NOVELIS, a leader in aluminium rolling and recycling, officially opened China’s first plant dedicated to the production of heat-treated aluminium automotive sheet on 21 October 2014. The opening makes Novelis the only company to produce automotive aluminium sheet in all three of the world’s major automobile-producing regions – Asia, Europe and North America.

The wholly-owned facility, located in the Changzhou National Hi-Tech District, represents a US$ 100 million investment by Novelis to expand its global footprint to serve the rapidly growing market for automotive aluminium. With an annual capacity of 120,000 metric tons, the new facility will assist China in meeting its environmental goals and provide customers in Asia and abroad with high quality automotive sheet for use in lightweight vehicle structures and body panels such as hoods, doors, fenders and lift gates.

Since 2011, Novelis has invested more than US$ 550 million globally to triple its automotive sheet capacity to 900,000 metric tons by 2015. Demand for aluminium is rapidly increasing as automakers strive to design a new generation of lighter, more fuel-efficient vehicles with reduced life-cycle emissions. Each 10% weight reduction can result in 5 to 7% fuel savings.

“The opening of our Changzhou plant marks an important milestone for Novelis as our first manufacturing facility in China,” said Shashi Maudgal, Senior Vice President, Novelis and President, Novelis Asia. “This new facility will be integrated with our existing operations in Korea, strengthening Novelis’ leadership in the Asian market, the largest and fastest growing region in the world for aluminium.”

Currently, Novelis aluminium products are now featured in more than 180 models of vehicles produced by leading automakers around the globe. Novelis forecasts that the global automotive market will expand by approximately 30% each year through to the end of the decade.

The company was recently awarded two prestigious prizes at the 2014 European Aluminium Awards during the ALUMINIUM 2014 Trade Fair. In the Building and Architecture category, Novelis was awarded the winning prize for the Blue Planet aquarium building in Copenhagen constructed with Novelis falzonal® pre-painted aluminium for roof cladding.

Novelis also received a special prize for its online measuring tool for thin coatings on aluminium coils. The method, developed in cooperation with the University of Leipzig, determines coating thickness and content even with process velocities of up to 200m./minute without touching the surface.
