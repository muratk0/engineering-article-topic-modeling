# UC RUSAL and Omen to form automotive JV

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

UC RUSAL, a leading, global aluminium producer, has signed a Memorandum of Understanding with Israeli company Omen High Pressure Die Casting (“Omen”) which specialises in the production of automotive components from non-ferrous metals. Together the companies intend to create a joint venture in Russia to produce automotive components from aluminium, to be supplied to carmakers in Russia and the CIS.

The JV, which is to be held in equal share (50/50), will be based on a production site at one of RUSAL’s Aluminium Division West smelters. RUSAL will supply the liquid aluminium alloys and Omen will set up automotive component production. The financing will be provided on a parity basis, with the option of external borrowing. The project is to be considered by the parties over the next three months.

‘The launch of the automotive components’ production joint venture is in line with our stated strategy to switch production at our Aluminium Division West smelters to value-added products, providing an excellent development opportunity in a challenging market. The Division's smelters currently produce value-added products for Russia’s automotive industry which has experienced strong growth in the last few years. This is a trend we expect to continue over the next seven-eight years,’ said Alexey Arnautov, UC RUSAL’s director for Aluminium Division West.
