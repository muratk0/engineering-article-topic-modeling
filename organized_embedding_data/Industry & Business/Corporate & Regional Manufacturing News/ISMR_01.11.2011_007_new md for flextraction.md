# NEW MD FOR FLEXTRACTION

[TARİH: 01.11.2011 ISMR]

FLEXTRACTION LTD. SUPPLIES and manufactures Local Exhaust Ventilation (LEV) products – it has confirmed that Lee Darton has been appointed managing director with immediate effect.

Lee has many years of experience in the fume and dust extraction equipment industry. He has been with Flextraction for over 12 years as sales manager and most recently as general manager (products) and this promotion comes at a time as the company continues to further develop its range of products and increase its market share in the UK and Europe.

Working with the UKTI (UK Trade and Investment) to find defined partners in Europe has led to the recent appointment of a distributor in France for its HPD products (Hood Positioning Devices). This in turn has resulted in increased production of HPDs at Melton Mowbray.

“We are continuing to drive our export business forward and are currently looking at defined partners in Germany and Spain. My appointment as managing director will help us take the company forward as part of our ongoing marketing and investment strategies, both in the long and short term,” said Darton.

## SMMT ANNOUNCES NEW VPs

THE SOCIETY OF Motor Manufacturers and Traders (SMMT) has appointed Tim Abbott, Managing Director, BMW UK, Ralf Speth, CEO, Jaguar Land Rover and Robin Woolcock, Managing Director, Volkswagen Group (UK) as Vice Presidents of the Society, joining existing Vice Presidents John Neill CBE, Chief Executive and Deputy Chairman, Unipart Group and Bill Parfitt CBE, Chairman and Managing Director, GM UK.

Commenting on the new appointments, SMMT President, Nigel Stein, CEO GKN Driveline, said, “This is an important time for the UK automotive sector as it continues to lead economic recovery, attract inward investment, develop new ultra-low carbon technology and manufacture products for global export. Our challenge is to ensure government focus remains fixed on sustaining growth, rebalancing the economy and strengthening UK business and consumer confidence. Automotive is a sector with incredible growth potential and I am delighted that our new Vice Presidents have committed to supporting SMMT in its efforts to maximise the opportunities for all its members.”

SMMT supports and promotes the interests of the UK automotive industry in the UK and overseas. Working closely with member companies, SMMT acts as the voice of the motor industry, promoting its position to government, stakeholders and the media. Around 1.5 million cars and commercial vehicles and 3 million engines are produced annually in the UK, accounting for 9 per cent of the UK’s total exports. With an annual turnover of 40 billion and a workforce of over 700,000 people, the UK is a key global player within the automotive sector and the industry is a significant contributor to the UK’s economy.
