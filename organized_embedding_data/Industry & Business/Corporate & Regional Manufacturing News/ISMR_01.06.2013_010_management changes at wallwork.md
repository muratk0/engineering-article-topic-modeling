# Management changes at WALLWORK

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

SENIOR MANAGEMENT changes have been announced by Wallwork Heat Treatment, the UK metal surface engineering specialist. Manchester site director Richard Burslem is to step down from the Board ahead of his retirement but will continue to work part-time with the company. Richard’s role will become the responsibility of production director, Ian Griffin.

Carole Chettoe is to join the senior management team as company secretary. Sales and marketing director, Simeon Collins, will take on the additional role as site director for the Tecvac subsidiary in Cambridge. Mike Allen will continue as site director for Wallwork Heat Treatment in Birmingham working alongside fellow director, Tim Pelari.

Commenting on his new role, Richard said, “I have been a director for Wallwork Heat Treatment for 23 years and worked for the company for almost 35 years. I now feel that it is right to look for a better work and life balance. I will continue on a part-time basis, working with our technical director Peter Carpenter on several development projects. I am happy to make a continuing contribution to the business and ensure continuity as I step back from a front-line role.”

The Wallwork group provides comprehensive metal heat treatment and surface coating services to aerospace, automotive, oil and gas, medical and general engineering clients. In addition to a well-established foundry and production of heat-resistant castings, the company undertakes world-class research with leading universities and manufacturers. The company also engineers bespoke vacuum equipment for the application of advanced metal coatings.

## Viessman chooses AP&T

THE VIESSMANN GROUP, one of the world's leading heating system manufacturers, has invested in a new hydraulic press and a new tool changing system from AP&T. The equipment will be used to produce high-capacity boilers at Viessmann's facilities in Berlin, Germany.

"This is a strategically important order that will help us reinforce our position in the German market where competition is fierce. We are very proud that Viessmann selected our products," said Magnus Svenningsson, sales manager at AP&T Press Systems.

In the middle of 2011, Viessmann decided to replace one of the older presses at its competence centre for medium and large boilers in Berlin. The procurement demanded, for example, high press force, high production speed and short tool-change times.

"With the new press, we received a very powerful and compact machine with twice the press force as previously, which provides many more opportunities to form high quality parts. We can also increase production speed since the press time per part is almost cut in half," explained Markus Leutloff, technical manager at Viessmann in Berlin.

The new press, an ODEN with 4000 tons of press force and a 1.6 x 1.4m bolster will be used to manufacture heat exchanger parts in condensing boilers (80kW to 1.4MW). Boilers of this type and size are used in both office and apartment buildings.

"We naturally wanted to keep our existing tools, as well as be able to replace them more quickly and more easily than we can today. AP&T was able to offer this type of solution," said Markus Leutloff.

AP&T's system for tool changes is entirely integrated with the press, and every tool change is monitored by the machine's control system to ensure that the process is quick and secure.

The new equipment from AP&T was installed at Viessmann in Berlin at the beginning of the year and is now in full operation.
