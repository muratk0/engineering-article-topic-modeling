# New press investment by BENTELER

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

The opening ceremony for the new transfer press at BENTELER's Burgos factory.

BENTELER opened a new 3,200-ton, coldforming transfer press at its automotive plant in Burgos in October 2022. For the new press, it invested more than 12 million euros into the plant. It enables the automotive supplier to adapt to the new stamping challenges required by its customers, such as stamping components from very thick sheet metal with a high yield strength directly from coil. The President of the Junta de Castilla y León, Alfonso Fernández Mañueco, was also at the opening ceremony.

Ricardo García, Executive Vice President Southern Europe, BENTELER Automotive, commented: “The BENTELER plant at Burgos is in the process of transformation into an intelligent plant. This new press will be monitored with Big Data technologies, so that with advanced data analysis techniques we can optimise the process, costs and maintenance strategy, moving from corrective to predictive maintenance."

Built 33 years ago, the Burgos plant was the first BENTELER plant constructed outside Germany. With approximately 600 workers, it produces chassis and structural vehicle components. Its technology includes hot and cold forming; automatic 3D laser cutting and assembly welding among others. In total, up to 16 million components are produced per year over an area of 99,000m².

Burgos is also home to the headquarters of BENTELER's Southern Europe Region, where 19 production plants are integrated. Spain, with eight plants, is a key market for BENTELER. The BENTELER Group has two divisions: BENTELER Automotive and BENTELER Steel/ Tube. The holding company, BENTELER International AG (based in Salzburg, Austria), controls the strategic orientation of the group.
