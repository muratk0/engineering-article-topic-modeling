# Schuler opens demonstration centre in Tianjin

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

SCHULER HAS LAUNCHED its first technology centre for forming technology systems in China with an innovation day. Schuler supplies presses, automation solutions, dies, process expertise and service for the metalworking industry and lightweight automotive design.

The demonstration and reference centre in the Northern China industrial metropolis of Tianjin offers automotive manufacturers, suppliers and other companies from the sheet metal processing sector the opportunity to try out a new generation servo press for themselves. The Servo TechCenter Tianjin is Schuler's first demonstration centre across all of Asia and forms a key part of the company's strategy, to be ranked ‘among the leading providers in China, as the largest press market in the world, in all the key price-performance sectors’. Schuler's customers can, for example, perform their own stamping and die tests on the TwinServo press.

"We support our customers in optimising their productivity and therefore becoming even more successful", commented Chief Operating Officer, Dr. Peter Jost, at the innovation day. "Our first TechCenter in China has not only put our technological leading position to the test, but also underlines the significance of the Chinese market for Schuler's future growth."

"This country has enormous potential for us." added China Chief Executive Officer, Oemer Akyazici.

Schuler has been operating in China with a number of sites since the early 1990s and, just a few months ago, acquired leading Chinese press manufacturer, Yadon, in the middle price-performance segment.

"With the products from Yadon under our belt, we are now optimally positioned to cover further market segments."

Around 140 visitors saw the new press in action at the Servo TechCenter and listened to presentations on topics such as TwinServo technology, with which the 1,600-ton system is equipped.

"Customers can not only make their production processes more efficient, but also resort to back-up production or book practical training for employees," explained Schuler.

In addition to Tianjin, Schuler also offers customer-specific consulting services at its German sites in Göppingen, Erfurt, Gemmingen and Helßdorf as well as at the US TechCenter. See www.schulergroup.com ■

THE MINIMALLY INVASIVE surgical instruments' market is projected to reach US$ 18.14 billion by 2021 from US$ 11.16 billion in 2016, at a CAGR of 10.2% according to a new report by Markets and Markets.

The global medical instruments market has been a growing market for specialist sheet metal manufacturers. The major drivers for the minimally invasive surgical instruments' market include the benefits of minimally invasive surgeries over traditional open surgeries; the reduction in healthcare costs and time; the increasing number of surgeries among the aging population; the increasing prevalence of lifestyle disorders and technological advancements.

In this report, the global minimally invasive surgical instruments' market is segmented based on product, application, end user and region. By product, the market includes handheld instruments; electrosurgical instruments; inflation systems; guiding devices; auxiliary instruments and cutter instruments.

Based upon application, the market is segmented into cardiothoracic surgery; cosmetic surgery/bariatric surgery; gastrointestinal surgery; orthopaedic surgery; gynaecological surgery; vascular surgery; urological surgery and others. The other segments include ENT/respiratory surgery; neurological surgery; dental surgery and paediatric surgery. Based on the end-users, the market is divided into government hospitals, private hospitals and surgical clinics, and academic and research institutes.

Based on region, the market is segmented into North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and the Rest of the World (RoW). The North American market is further segmented into the US and Canada. See www.marketsandmarkets.com■
