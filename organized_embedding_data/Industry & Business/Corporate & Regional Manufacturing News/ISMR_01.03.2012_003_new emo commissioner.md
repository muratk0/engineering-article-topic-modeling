# New EMO Commissioner

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

THE CECIMO General Assembly has appointed Carl Martin Welcker as General Commissioner for EMO 2013, which will take place in Hannover.

EMO is a premier exhibition for metal working technology. VDW, the German Machine Tool Builders Association and member of CECIMO, will organise EMO 2013 from 16 to 21 September in Hannover Germany, in partnership with Deutsche Messe.

Mr. Welcker, in his capacity of General Commissioner, will supervise the organisation of EMO 2013 and will be driving promotion of the event, which will include a series of press conferences in major world capitals over the next two years. The General Commissioner reports to the CECIMO General Assembly regarding the action plans for the organisation of EMO.

Mr. Welcker served as Chairman of the German Machine Tool Builders’ Association (VDW) from 2004 to 2010. As a board member of the German Engineering Federation (VDMA) since 2001, Vice President of the VDMA and member of the executive board of the Federation of German Industry (BDI), he has been a CECIMO delegate since 2005. He is also President and CEO at Alfred H. Schütte GmbH & Co. KG in Cologne, Germany, which manufactures multi-spindle automatic lathes and 5-axis CNC grinding machines. ■
