# Eder now chairs voestalpine AG Supervisory Board

[TARİH: 01.06.2022 ISMR]

The Supervisory Board of voestalpine AG (the steel and technology group) elected Wolfgang Eder as its new chairman as of 1 April 2022, the start of the new business year. Eder succeeds Joachim Lemppenau, who stepped down after 18 years as chairman of the supervisory body. Lemppenau will continue to serve the Group as a member of the Supervisory Board until his current term of office officially ends at the 2024 Annual General Meeting.

Wolfgang Eder has worked at voestalpine for over 40 years, 15 of which (2004 to 2019) were spent as chairman of the Management Board. He was elected to the Supervisory Board in July 2019.

Voestalpine, which operates globally, has around 500 Group companies and locations in more than 50 countries on all five continents. In business year 2020/21, the Group generated revenues of 11.3 billion euros, with an operating result (EBITDA) of 1.1 billion euros.
