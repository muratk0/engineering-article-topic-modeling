# About EHG Stahlzentrum

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Holding a stock of around 45,000 tonnes, EHG offers a range of goods including more than 15,000 items of steel and other metals in over 140 grades and countless dimensions. It makes the facility one of the largest in Europe and ensures a high degree of availability.

The ability to provide a wide range of services is just as important to EHG as holding a large variety of stock.

"We don't think in terms of products, but rather in terms of solutions. It means that we supply items already cut-to-size and just in time directly to our customers."

This service encompasses a broad geographical area, not just Austria, Germany and Switzerland but also Italy, Slovenia, Hungary, Slovakia, the Czech Republic and Romania. The company network comprises ten sites employing about 360 employees, serving around 6,000 customers.
