## Senior appointments at Sertec

LEADING AUTOMOTIVE SUPPLIER, Sertec, has made two significant additions to its senior management team. Steve Dunn joins the Sertec Group Board as engineering production director and Carol Henderson takes tenure as senior commercial manager with immediate effect.

Joining from a major Tier One Body In White (BIW) and chassis manufacturer, Steve brings 37 years of automotive experience in leadership technical product development and process improvement. Carol Henderson joins Sertec from Covpress Assembly and brings with her 25 years of automotive purchasing and new model experience. She will play an integral part in attracting new customers to the Group.

Group Managing Director, Dave Steggles, said: "Having recently spent £16 million in our new heavy stampings facility, we are very fortunate to have enlisted Steve, who brings with him the calibre and skills set essential to capitalising on our investment. Whilst he will concentrate on heavy stampings at our Coleshill factory, his experience in assessing and implementing new strategies and processes will be of great benefit to the group as a whole, both in the UK and for our European operations.

"Carol has a wealth of engineering and commercial skills which, coupled with an extensive knowledge of the automotive industry, will add a new perspective to our commercial activities for a wider customer portfolio."

Since acquiring Wild Automotive twelve months ago, Sertec has shown commitment to each of the new factories with investment and senior management support. New factory layouts and plant additions have benefited the two Midlands sites, their site in Germany has enjoyed from £1 million worth of plant refurbishment and lighting upgrades and the Hungary operation is being fitted with new presses and equipment to the value of £11 million over a two-year period. This investment is backed up with the current roll-out of a new £1 million strategic investment in OEM-recognised 'Industry 4.0' manufacturing IT systems across the Group.■

## Baton handover at Blackbird Robotics

REMOTE LASER WELDING technology specialist, Blackbird Robotersysteme GmbH, is positioning itself for further international growth. From 1 September 2017, the addition of Karl Christian Messer to the Executive Board temporarily enlarged it to four people. After an extensive transition phase, co-founder and current CEO, Dr. Wolfgang Vogl, will leave the company at year-end to establish a start-up in the Industrial Internet of Things (IoT) environment. For Blackbird Robotics, this step underscores the company's transformation into a mid-sized SCANLAB Group enterprise.

Karl Christian Messer (51) was appointed CEO of Blackbird Robotersysteme from 1 September 2017. He has previously worked at KUKA AG, PTR Präzisionstechnik and Linde AG in various sales management, production management and controlling capacities, as well as performing the role of CEO at mid-sized companies. He studied mechanical engineering at Munich Technical University and has certifications as a technical business economist, safety engineer and European welding engineer.
