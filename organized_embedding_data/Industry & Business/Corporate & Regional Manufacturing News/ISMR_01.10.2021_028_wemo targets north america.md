# WEMO targets North America

[TARİH: 01.10.2021 ISMR]

After successfully presenting its automated panel bending line at the FABTECH exhibition (Chicago, USA) in October 2019, and navigating the challenges of COVID-19, WEMO is now reinforcing its strategic position in the North American market. The Dutch machinery specialist is partnering with AMI-service and MetalReady machinery to develop sales and support infrastructure in the United States for WEMO products.

"AMI-service is already servicing the installed base of machinery that WEMO has built up in the past 22 years in the U.S. market. Our new strategic partner, MetalReady Machinery, will further shape our proactive market approach from a commercial perspective and help customers, in close cooperation with WEMO's head office, to find a solution that best fits their business case," WEMO told ISMR.

"At FABTECH, visitors confirmed that there is a huge need for sheet metal processing automation, high output and flexible products in the U.S. market. Our initial growth plans for the U.S. market were hampered by COVID-19 restrictions but confidence in the economy has now returned and exponential market growth is expected," it added.

WEMO offers solutions to the North American market in areas such as shelving; steel cabinets; steel doors; ceiling tiles and wall systems. It designs, builds, sells and services fast and flexible production lines for processing sheet metal: punching, bending, joining and software. It has been organising WEMO Demo Days over September and October 2021 at its facility in Hertogenbosch (The Netherlands), where manufacturers can see their products bent live on the WEMO Panel Bending Line (PBL).

"I am very excited to further increase our footprint in the U.S. market with MetalReady and AMI-service. This agreement facilitates the ever-growing demand for automated bending and punching solutions in the U.S. market. We take great pride in the solutions we have built over the last decades and it would make us prouder to extend this heritage into the U.S. market for decades to come," concluded René van Zon, WEMO's area sales manager for U.S and Canadian markets.
