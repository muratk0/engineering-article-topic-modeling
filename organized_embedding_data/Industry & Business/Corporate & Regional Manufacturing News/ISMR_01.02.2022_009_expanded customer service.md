# Expanded customer service

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

The Bradbury Group has announced the addition of Thomas Hofstetter to its North American customer service and sales support team. As a service technician for The Bradbury Group, he will focus on servicing Athader equipment and customers. Athader (in Renteria, Spain) is a company within The Bradbury Group and supplier of advanced, customised slitting and cut-to-length lines. With hundreds of coil processing lines operating on five continents, Athader has focused on customer productivity and coil processing operations for 30 years.

Hofstetter, working from The Bradbury Co., Inc. in Moundridge, KS (USA), will assist Athader customers with equipment troubleshooting, technical instruction, remote service, spare parts, equipment upgrades and more. Along with his degree in welding technology, he brings five years of experience in Weapons Test and Assembly in the U.S. Navy and four years as a shipboard install subject matter expert for all naval aircraft carriers.

Hofstetter joins Athader's North American service team working with Steve Baker (Athader sales area manager); Eduardo Maldonado (Mexico sales manager); Jim Sugars (industry sales leader, coil processing lines) and Jason Bartel (technical sales engineer). He will also work closely with Athader service in Spain and Bradbury service in the USA.

The Bradbury Co., Inc., in Moundridge, KS, is expanding its test and assembly facility by approximately 40% to increase manufacturing capacity and provide additional space for testing custom-built production lines before shipping to customers' facilities. It has also been expanding its workforce since June 2021. Bradbury's multi-phase expansion process was expected to be complete by February 2022.

Athader joined The Bradbury Group of companies in 2012. Bradbury Group's equipment consists of slitting lines; cut-to-length lines; stacking and packaging lines; rotary shears; slitting heads and levelling technology, including the patented e-drive® leveller.
