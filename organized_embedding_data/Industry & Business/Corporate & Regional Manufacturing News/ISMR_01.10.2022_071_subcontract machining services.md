# Subcontract machining services

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

Founded in 1966, Partheniou S.A. is one of Greece's leading subcontract and precision machine shops, specialising in CNC and manual machining services. It is based close to Athens International Airport, not far from the port of Piraeus.

“We pride ourself on offering a wide range of services from one-off, custom-machined part projects through to large subcontracting projects for a diverse range of industries such as defence and metallurgy to renewables; aerospace and marine. We supply custom parts; new components; precision parts and steel structures," it explained.

“The company is managed, compliant and operational to ISO-9001:2015 standards. Our manufacturing team includes experienced professionals, providing high-quality, on-time projects at competitive prices," it added.

The family-run business has grown from a small workshop in Piraeus to a modern 2000m² engineering facility in Mandra-Attica. It offers complete solutions from concept and design to product development and production (mainly in steel, stainless steel and aluminium).
