# One year of the TRUMPF Bank

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

It was exactly one year ago that the laser manufacturer and mechanical engineering company, TRUMPF, announced the establishment of its own bank. The experience gained since, it confirmed, has been extremely positive.

“A specialised credit institution with a high level of sector and industry expertise provides a good counterpart to the established banking market. TRUMPF customers see it that way as well, which is why they place their trust in us,” explained Diane Zetzmann-Krien, who has been managing director of the TRUMPF Bank since February 2015. She is responsible for market succession (“Marktfolge”) there, which includes risk management and corporate management.

About 80 per cent of TRUMPF machines are acquired through financing models, around half of them are loan-based and the remainder are lease-based. As part of the activities of TRUMPF Financial Services to date, more than 7,000 machines with a total volume of about 1.6 billion euros have been financed, the majority of them from the machine tool sector.

Thanks to its full banking license, TRUMPF Financial Services GmbH has expanded its leasing business in Europe significantly over the past year in markets including the UK, Spain and Denmark. This is because the banking license is accompanied by EU Passport, which allows the product portfolio from the home country to be offered EU-wide without the need for additional approval processes. The grant of loans to finance TRUMPF customers is now possible as well. With its accreditation by the L-Bank, the TRUMPF Bank is also now a contact partner for development loans. Here, the focus is on promoting energy and material efficiency as well as environmental technology.

At the start of 2015, the TRUMPF Bank entered the deposit business – it offers German employees savings accounts and fixed-deposit accounts that are managed via online banking. In the first eight weeks following the launch, ten per cent of German TRUMPF employees opened an account with their company’s own bank.

“All in all, I can certainly imagine our model being a blueprint for other industrial companies,” said Diane Zetzmann-Krien, who runs the TRUMPF Bank with Hans-Joachim Dörr. She has more than 20 years of experience in the banking sector as an operational and strategic analyst, working internationally and in top management.
