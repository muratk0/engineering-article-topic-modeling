# Autonomous vehicle maker to open first U.S. production plant

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

IN A JOINT announcement, NAVYA with project collaborators Michigan Economic Development Corporation (MEDC) and Ann Arbor SPARK, announced that its Michigan plant will be located in the city of Saline, Michigan (USA) with a 20,000+ sq. ft. facility. NAVYA plans to build approximately 25 of its ARMA fully autonomous, electric shuttle vehicles at the plant before the end of 2017.

The driverless transport vehicles can carry up to 15 people and are equipped with sensor technology for 3D vision which enables them to map out the environment, detect obstacles in their path and identify traffic and road signs. The vehicle aims to provide a complement to current public transport systems over distances that are too short to go by car or are too long on foot. NAVYA's breakthrough ARMA shuttle has become a global leader in first and last mile transportation solutions with a fleet of 45 vehicles deployed worldwide and more than 170,000 passengers transported to date.

With the addition of its new plant in Saline, the company will be able to boost production significantly to serve the growing North American market. The project is expected to generate a total capital investment of more than US$ 1 million and create 50 jobs, resulting in a US$ 435,000 Michigan Business Development performancebased grant.

NAVYA, created in 2014, is a French company specialising in developing innovative, smart and sustainable mobility solutions. See www.navya.tech

## AMB Iran hits new high

THIS YEAR'S SECOND AMB Iran exhibition attracted 5736 trade visitors and 202 company exhibitors from 17 countries. The joint event was run by Messe Stuttgart and the German Machine Tool Builders' Association (VDW) from 23–26 May 2017 at the Shahr-e-Aftab Exhibition Centre in Tehran. AMB Iran was described by the Iranian Minister of Industry, Mine and Trade, Mohammad Reza Nematzadeh, as the first non-governmental trade fair, a clear signal of the event's importance for the Iranian economy.

"Exhibitors came from 17 countries (2016: 12) to renew existing customer contacts and tap into new customer groups in Iran. The presence of 42 Iranian exhibitors shows that AMB Iran is also a very good platform for the domestic market. Germany topped the participation figures with 88 exhibitors, followed by Iran (42), Italy (27), China (12), Switzerland (7), France (5) and Spain (4)," explained the organisers.

"86 per cent of visitors were from industry, mainly the automotive sector (23%), component suppliers (14%), general mechanical engineering (11%) and tool construction and mouldmaking (11%). 30 per cent of trade fair visitors came from further afield than 300km, from industry centres such as Isfahan or Tabriz."

The 'Automation Iran' dedicated area of the exhibition attracted particular attention - 21% of visitors said that they had come specifically for information about automation. Fardad Daliri, Vice-President Investment Development, IDRO (Industrial Development & Renovation Organisation of Iran) reported: "AMB Iran is an important trade fair and machine tools and automation are particularly interesting for Iran's highly diversified industry. I took part in promising meetings and discussions; we have high hopes of cooperation between international companies and Iran, and I can see major opportunities especially in the automotive, energy, power station and rail sectors.

The next AMB Iran will take place in Tehran from 26-29 June 2018. See www.messe-stuttgart.de ■

ALMA and ASCO DATA join forces

ALMA AND ASCO DATA, two major European players in the sheet metal CAD/CAM sector with head offices in Grenoble (France) and Bremen (Germany) respectively, have announced that ALMA GmbH, the German subsidiary of ALMA, has purchased ASCO DATA's share capital.

This will enable the ALMA-ASCO group to reach a critical size in Germany, offer a broader range of sheet metal CAD/CAM software products and free resources to finance the innovation and development of new products that will be part of Industry 4.0, in an international environment.

The aim of this operation is the eventual merger of the two German companies as well as the ALMA and ASCO DATA product ranges. Several actions have already been taken to bring the companies closer together.

"For several years, the CAD/CAM market has been going through a process of consolidation. Therefore, only the leading companies will be able to survive and continue to innovate. In this context, closer links between the two companies will allow us to achieve several objectives," the companies told ISMR. "These objectives include becoming a leading group in German-speaking countries (DACH); building a single offering to meet the challenges of Industry 4.0 and building an independent group."■
