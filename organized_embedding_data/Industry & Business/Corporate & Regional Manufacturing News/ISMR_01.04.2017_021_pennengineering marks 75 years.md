# PennEngineering® marks 75 years

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

PENNENGINEERING is celebrating 75 years in 2017 as a global specialist in fastening technologies and solutions. "Founded in 1942, we pioneered the first-ever self-clinching fastener for thin metal assemblies and have since achieved dramatic growth from an increasingly diverse portfolio of fastening products, a focus on customers and world-class technical expertise. Strategic acquisitions over the years have further extended our footprint as a problem-solver for an expanding universe of customer industries," the company told /SMR.

The manufacturer continues to enhance its global manufacturing capabilities, ongoing new product development, focused technical services and engineering support as well as online resources and custom solutions tailored for application demands. Fastener and installation equipment brands under the PennEngineering umbrella include PEM®, microPEM®, PEMSERTER®, PROFIL®, Atlas®, PennAuto™, LinkTool™ Group and Heyco®.

The acquisition of Heyco Products in 2016 exemplified the company's evolution with the launch of a new "Engineered Plastics" platform. As a result, PennEngineering now offers a range of solutions for metal or plastic component assemblies. See www.pemnet.com
