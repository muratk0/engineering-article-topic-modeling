# ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH was founded in 1928 and is a specialist in levelling technology, with almost 50 years of experience, and a wide range of precision levellers. It recently expanded its service portfolio to include innovative deburring technology. ARKU's headquarters are in Baden-Baden, Germany and the company maintains ISO-certified subsidiaries in Kunshan (China) and Cincinnati (USA), serving markets in almost 30 countries. Its product range covers precision levellers and deburring machines for parts, cut-to-length and slitting lines, press feed lines and coil preparation lines for profile manufacturers.

However, ARKU's product portfolio now features several new machines. At EuroBLECH 2016 from 25-29 October 2016 in Hanover, the company took two booths to showcase new capabilities in deburring and grinding on one booth and levelling, on the other. It presented its EdgeBreaker® 2000 Plus deburring and rounding machine as well as its new EdgeGrinder system, along with the EdgeRacer® the Finisher for surface processing.

Its new FlatJack® flatness control, a system that now completes ARKU's levelling portfolio, also received the EuroBLECH Award in the measurement and testing technology category.

ARKU presented new products for levelling, deburring and grinding processes at EuroBLECH 2016

## Innovation in focus

“At our headquarters in Baden-Baden this May, we displayed a fully automated EdgeBreaker®2000 deburring and rounding machine linked with the precision leveler FlatMaster® 55. A robot lays sheet metal parts on the EdgeBreaker® inlet conveyor. Once the deburred parts exit the EdgeBreaker, a conveyor pushes them onto the FlatMaster® inlet conveyor. From there, the parts move automatically through the leveller to emerge almost 'stress-free'. The conveyor belt then transports the parts directly onto the storage unit. We are pleased to say that we have now sold the first connected lines in Europe, which is a huge step for us as well as an important step towards Industry 4.0. Processes such as bending and/or welding are easier with parts that have been levelled and/or deburred," ARKU told ISMR at EuroBLECH in Hanover.

"Some customers tell us that they save 20-30% of their time using our deburring and/or levelling machines in their automated production lines. Feedback on our new machines here at EuroBLECH has also been very good – the audience is very qualified as there are a lot of decision-makers here."

ARKU presented another new levelling development with live demonstrations at its EuroBLECH booth, witnessed by ISMR. The award-winning FlatJack® automated flatness control system checks the evenness parameters of a variety of different parts. The testing system can handle punched, laser-cut and flame-cut parts with complex geometries. It uses lasers to examine the surface, regardless of the panel's orientation and position. These are integrated into the in-feed rollers on the FlatMaster® 88130 precision leveller, which was also on display at EuroBLECH. The results are automatically transferred to the control panel. Because of this, said ARKU, the FlatJack® guarantees complete documentation together with reproducible results.

ARKU's smallest precision leveller model, the EcoMaster® 12, is designed to work with more delicate sheet metal parts (thicknesses from 0.1mm to 1.2mm) in precision sectors such as watch-making, the electro-technical industry and the textile industry.

## An eye on deburring

At EuroBLECH 2016, ARKU presented its EdgeBreaker® and EdgeRacer® deburring machines for stamped, laser, plasma or oxy-fuel cut metal parts. They are designed for quick and cost-effective two-sided deburring, edge-rounding and oxide removal.

The EdgeBreaker® 2000 Plus is designed to provide smooth panels. The deburring machine deburrs and rounds thick panels on both sides and can also be combined with the FlatMaster® leveller. The brushes remove undesirable burrs while a transverse rounding unit produces rounded edges on both sides.

"You can change the tools on the EdgeBreaker® to deburr, grind, round edges or do all three," Thomas Weinfurtner, ARKU's sales manager, told ISMR. "The new feature that we are promoting at EuroBLECH is that tools can now be changed which means more applications for users and added value because this machine can now deburr and also grind. You can easily and quickly operate the machine thanks to the intuitive control unit.

"In line with Industry 4.0, a robot system can be placed in front of the machine (loading/ unloading). The maximum thickness of material that it can process is 80mm. The machine is also easy to clean – it takes 30 minutes, on average. Consumable costs are low as the parts are built to last."

ARKU also presented two new machines belonging to the EdgeRacer® series from Wöhler Brush Tech. The EdgeGrinder deburring machine is designed for single-sided deburring. With its circumferential grinding heads, it removes irregularities from cut pieces of up to 50mm thick.

The EdgeRacer® the Finisher refines deburred surfaces with thicknesses of up to 50mm. A special scotch bride roll enables sanding processes without removing material. This creates a surface, ARKU told ISMR, with both good grip and an appealing look, thanks to the matt effect. The EdgeRacer® the Finisher and the EdgeGrinder modular design enable integration into existing production lines or use as a standalone solution.

"EdgeRacer® offers perfect rounding and deburring on each corner - this is a rounding machine for top and bottom rounding in one pass but you can also add modules to this machine e.g. an EdgeGrinder or an EdgeRacer® the Finisher, so that you can grind a burr away. A user can perform an entire operation – deburring, grinding, rounding and finishing - in one pass. The machines can be connected for an Industry 4.0 interfaced solution, perhaps with a robotic solution for loading and unloading machines," explained Weinfurtner.

"2016 has been a very successful year for us. The US market has been very strong this year. Asia and China are dynamic markets for us, despite the slight slowdown in China. Our customers come from diverse sectors such as automotive, construction, rail, shipbuilding (processing thick sheet metal parts) as well as the medical technology sector (operation room baskets for tools)," concluded ARKU.
