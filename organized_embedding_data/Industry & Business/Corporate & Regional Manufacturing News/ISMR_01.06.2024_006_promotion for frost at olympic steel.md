# Promotion for Frost at Olympic Steel

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Olympic Steel Inc., a U.S. metals service centre, recently announced the promotion of George Frost to Vice President – Aluminium. This change supports the company's specialty metals' strategic growth efforts. Mr. Frost will report directly to Andy Markowitz, President — Specialty Metals.

"George's thorough understanding of the specialty metals market, particularly aluminium, has been an effective resource for our commercial team and our customers. His extensive expertise will help us continue to increase our market share and firmly establish Olympic Steel as a preferred aluminium distributor and supplier," said Markowitz. “His leadership will contribute to the continued growth of our aluminium product line."

Mr. Frost received his Bachelor of Science degree from Western Carolina University and his Master of Business Administration from the University of Phoenix in the United States. He serves on the Metals Service Center Institute (MSCI) Aluminum Products Board and is also a member of the MSCI Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI) Task Force.

Founded in 1954, Olympic Steel is headquartered in Cleveland, Ohio. It operates from 47 facilities. The company reported sales for the first quarter of 2024 totalling US$ 527 million, compared with US$ 573 million in the first quarter of 2023.
