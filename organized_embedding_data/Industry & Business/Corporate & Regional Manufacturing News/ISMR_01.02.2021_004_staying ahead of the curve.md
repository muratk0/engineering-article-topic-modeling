# STAYING AHEAD OF THE CURVE

[TARİH: 01.02.2021 ISMR]

"As in 2020, TIMTOS 2021 Hybrid offers an important opportunity for the Taiwan machine tool industry," explained Jimmy Chu, CEO of Fair Friend Group, during the recent press conference.

James Huang, Chairman of TAITRA, told ISMR: "Taiwan is facing a lot of challenges right now. The machinery industry had a tough year in 2020. Sales went down, and we saw the rupture of the global supply chain as well as geopolitical issues. We not only had to manage our companies well, but also face other challenges. We are living in a time of uncertainty, but we are seeing a recovery in the machinery industry from the fourth quarter of 2020.

"In the future, forging ahead, stimulating the global economy and recovering from the pandemic is a shared mission for us all globally and the machinery industry (as the foundation of many other different industries) will play a leading role in this. We need to get back on our feet to inspire other industries to do the same. TAITRA and TAMI will be doing all we can to ensure that TIMTOS 2021 is held as scheduled. It carries a lot of industry expectations. TIMTOS has become the third largest machinery show in the world and I believe that it can also be a driving force for Taiwan's economic growth," he added.

## Market recovery and industry trends

Alex Ko, Chairman of TAMI, added: "We lived through the U.S.-China trade war and the pandemic last year. Taiwan could be a role model of pandemic prevention in the world. Even though we are experiencing an economic slump, machinery and machine tools are so important. A challenge can be turned into an opportunity; this is an opportunity to upgrade our supply chain and manufacturing equipment. From the second half of 2020, we have seen some industry recovery (orders coming back). However, we still face shortages in human resources and rising shipping costs.

"We will stick very closely to pandemic prevention guidelines to ensure that TIMTOS goes ahead - it will be a great platform for us to show the world our strengths. We have not seen a large-scale international trade show in the world since the second half of last year. I believe that this kind of hybrid exhibition will become the norm so this is also an opportunity for members of the machinery industry to learn, upgrade and transform," he concluded.

"Since the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, demand in China has been rising since May 2020 and throughout the third quarter of 2020. China is now making a strong comeback. However, the aerospace and automobile industries have suffered the most in Europe. After the inauguration of President Biden, we hope that the automotive and aerospace industries in the U.S. will see a revival. I believe 2021 is a hopeful year in Asia and the economy will not suffer as severely."
