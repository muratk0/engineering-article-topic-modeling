# Reed becomes EVP/COO

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

Alcoa Corporation has appointed Matt Reed as Alcoa's Executive Vice President and Chief Operations Officer from 1 January 2024. Reed, who will be based in Western Australia, will lead Alcoa's global operations including bauxite mines, alumina refineries and aluminium smelters. He will be accountable for operational excellence focusing on safety, environmental management, productivity, cost management and long-term strategic planning.

Reed joined Alcoa in June 2023. He will continue as Vice President Operations, Australia, and President, Alcoa of Australia, until 31 January 2024, at which point an interim Vice President Operations for Australia will be appointed. A permanent Vice President Operations for Australia will be named after a competitive process.
