# Aerospace fabricator, Radius Aerospace, has ordered a new, customised FiveX waterjet cutter for delivery from Water Jet Sweden.

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

Radius Aerospace manufactures a broad portfolio of aircraft and aerospace structures, components, sub-assemblies and systems. It works across the aerospace industry including commercial, private and military aviation as well as the industrial gas turbine market. Its work is focused on core manufacturing specialties of aircraft structures; engines; rotorcraft; defence applications and turbines.

Its six active manufacturing facilities work independently and in combination with one another to deliver aerospace and energy products. The manufacturer's fabrication sites in the United States are in Hot Springs, Arkansas (also its headquarters); Phoenix, Arizona; Shelbyville, Indiana and Fort Worth, Texas. In the UK, it has facilities in Sheffield and Shrewsbury. The company boasts more than 1,000 employees and almost one million square feet of manufacturing space.

The manufacturer focuses on several core services including forming; welding; bonding; machining; processing and assembly. Its facilities work strategically and in concert with each other to fulfil the forming; bonding; fabrication; welding and structural assemblies needs of major aerospace and aviation brands and companies. Specialty capabilities include specialty alloy welding; Titanium hot forming and SPF; bulge forming; complex assembly; chemical milling; Titanium Sol-Gel; metalto-metal and composite bonding; complex machining; hydroforming; stretch-forming; processing and assembly.

## Focus on waterjet

Radius Aerospace has used waterjet technology for many years and is continuously improving its production processes. The company decided to investigate new application areas for waterjet cutting and contacted Water Jet Sweden, a Swedish waterjet specialist, in early 2022. A dialogue started on how to optimise a machine design to best support its ideas, based on the FiveX, a 3D waterjet cutting system from Water Jet Sweden.

"Since both our companies have complementary area of expertise, the specification of a machine like this is always a joint effort," explained Christian Svensson, U.S. Sales Manager, Water Jet Sweden.

"When it comes to a FiveX machine, the size, features and fixtures are bespoke to fit the customer's application and area of usage.

Before Summer 2022, we agreed how to design the set-up and the machine order was placed. In December 2022, the new machine was ready for delivery and it was time for a thorough two-day FAT (Factory Acceptance Test)," he continued.

The FAT was successful and, in early 2023, the new machine will be installed at the Radius Aerospace facility in Hot Springs, Arkansas, USA.

"It is key for us to meet customers' expectations. There should be no problems or questions left when the machine leaves our production hall," added Christian Svensson. “Securing machine performance before delivery is standard procedure for us."
