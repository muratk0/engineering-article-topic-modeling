# 50-year anniversary for TRUMPF UK

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

TRUMPF Ltd in the UK is celebrating its 50-year anniversary this year. The company has been busy in recent weeks hosting celebratory events with customers, partners and employees. ISMR attended the company's Open House event on 2-3 July 2024, at its headquarters in Luton (UK), where it provided existing and prospective customers access to its latest machine tool, software and laser technologies. In addition to the festive gala for customers and partners, TRUMPF Ltd held a special 50th anniversary celebration with employees on 13 July 2024.

"In 1974, TRUMPF Machine Tools Ltd began trading in St Albans with a team of just five employees. The company demonstrated success and growth and, in 1989, relocated to Luton to form the new UK headquarters of TRUMPF Ltd. Today, more than 100 employees work at the Luton facility, which includes a 1,000m² showroom for hosting holistic customer experience activities such as time studies, feasibility studies and sample creation," TRUMPF UK explained.

To mark such a milestone, TRUMPF Ltd organised a festive gala in Luton on 4 July 2024. Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller (CEO) and Mathias Kammüller (CDO) from TRUMPF's parent company in Germany attended the celebrations. Addressing over 50 invited guests, Nicola Leibinger-Kammüller said: "I would like to thank you, our customers and partners, for your loyalty over the years. Without you, none of us would be here today."

Lee Moakes, managing director of TRUMPF Ltd who has led the subsidiary since 2018, echoed this sentiment: "As we celebrate our 50th anniversary, we especially celebrate our customers. They are the reason we strive to give our best every day. To ensure our customers receive optimal solutions and support, we also have a duty to keep investing in our employees, processes and products."■
