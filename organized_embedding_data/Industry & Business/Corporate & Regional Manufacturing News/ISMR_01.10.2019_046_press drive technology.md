# Press drive technology

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

PRESS MANUFACTURER SIMPAC and Baumüller, a specialist in drive and automation solutions, have signed a cooperative agreement for the supply of drive units for Simpac's new MX and SX series presses.

Simpac's MX and SX series servo presses were presented for the first time at the EuroBLECH 2018 trade fair in Hanover. Baumüller, a Nuremberg-based specialist in drive and automation systems, will in future supply Simpac not only with highly dynamic high-torque motors but also with complete power electronics and industrial PCs, including software such as motion control and cam generation.

"For us, this is a further step in the standardisation process that will not only benefit Simpac. In addition to a highly efficient drive concept, our customers will also benefit from maintenance and service in the future," explained Bernd Moritz, Chief Technology Officer, Simpac Inc. in South Korea and Managing Director, Simpac Europe GmbH.

"By drastically reducing the number of component variants, Baumüller is able to make all spare parts available from stock. For motors, we are limiting ourselves to three performance classes for presses from 3,000 to 30,000kN. For converters, there is one single model and its dimensions are set purely on the number of units. This means that the customer can either keep a spare part in stock for all tonnages, or exchange converters between different presses to bridge the gap until the spare part is available."

Baumüller's product range also includes capacitors for active energy management. Servo presses without integrated energy management can lead to additional investments by the customer to absorb system perturbations. The capacitor units, installed as standard in the Simpac MX and SX series servo presses, make this additional energy management superfluous by absorbing approx. 80% of the power peaks. This, said Simpac, means that the load on the supply system is even lower than with conventionallydriven presses.

"Simpac customers will receive key account support from us in the future," explained Joachim Weissenberger, authorised signatory and sales manager at Baumüller. "This guarantees access to our 24/7 hotline with a response time of 24 hours. Our service concept is designed to provide comprehensive support over the entire life cycle of the machines – worldwide."

A further advantage of the drive concept from a single source is the ability to analyse the entire system using remote maintenance in the event of a fault. This Industry 4.0 solution, said Baumüller, contributes to comprehensive diagnostics and a more efficient process for troubleshooting, resulting in higher plant availability.

"We believe that in times of approximately comparable products, the real advantages for our customers lie in individual, competent process consultancy and a comprehensive service concept with rapid response times," added Bernd Moritz.
