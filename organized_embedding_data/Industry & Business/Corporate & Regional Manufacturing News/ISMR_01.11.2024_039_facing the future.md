# FACING THE FUTURE

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

ISMR sat down with Bystronic's new CEO, Domenico Iacovelli, at EuroBLECH in Hanover recently to discuss the company's latest innovations, as well as his market views and strategy for the Bystronic Group going forward.

Bystronic is a technology specialist, headquartered in Switzerland with over 3,500 employees worldwide at 40 locations. Additional development and production sites are in Sulgen (Switzerland); Gotha (Germany); Cazzago San Martino and San Giuliano Milanese (Italy); Tianjin, Shanghai and Shenzhen (China); and Hoffman Estates (USA). The Bystronic Group generated a net revenue of CHF 930 million in 2023.

Bystronic's focus is on the automation of the complete material and data flow of the cutting and bending process chain. The intelligent connectivity of its laser cutting systems and press brakes with automation, software and service solutions is the key to comprehensive digitalisation in the sheet metal industry, it told ISMR.

Bending automation; laser automation; a new laser-cutter configuration system; electric bending; software, services and sustainability were just some of Bystronic's highlights on its booth at the EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition this October in Hanover, Germany.

ISMR therefore sat down with Bystronic's new CEO, Domenico Iacovelli, at EuroBLECH to discuss the company's latest innovations, as well as his market views and strategy for the Bystronic Group going forward.

## ByCell Bend Star M

At EuroBLECH 2024, Bystronic presented its compact ByCell Bend Star M, the bending cell with the ByBend Star 120 press brake for small and medium-sized parts.

"This new, fully automatic bending cell with the 'Optical Part Detection' function offers small and large production companies efficiency and flexibility in bending," said Bystronic.

“The ByCell Bend Star M is suitable for processing job lists as well as for changing orders, from small batch sizes to large series. In combination with the new ByBend Star 120, the robot automatically completes bending jobs error-free," it added.

At the heart of the automation system is the latest-generation KUKA IONTEC robot.

ISMR: What are your key technology highlights here at EuroBLECH?

DI: The world is moving away from single manually operated machines towards automation. Our new fully automated ByCell Bend Star M bending cell is one of this year's highlights. The smaller ByBend Star 80 was a great success- we sold over 300 systems and now we want to scale-up.

We have completely changed the way that we configure laser cutting machines, by introducing our new ByCut Packages. Now, customers can choose from various configurations to better serve their production requirements.

As an innovation leader, we collaborate with customers, experts and partners to build a sustainable future. In our "Solution Forum" at EuroBLECH, visitors can learn about the world of green steel and discover how artificial intelligence and the industrial metaverse are revolutionising the industry.

## Mobile Bending Cell

If manufacturers need a bending cell in small spaces, the Mobile Bending Cell 80 offers a press force of up to 80 tons over a bending length of around 1.5m.

“The powerful ByBend Star 80 press brake can be easily upgraded to a fully automated Mobile Bending Cell, if users already have a manual press. The Mobile Bending Robot can simply be docked onto the ByBend Star and connected and referenced within ten minutes," Bystronic told ISMR.

ISMR: What are your views on the current global business climate for sheet metal professionals? What about in 2025?

DI: We are all in a very difficult market environment. You can read about this in the newspapers every day and many businesses are experiencing tremendous pressure. While we are adapting to changed market dynamics, we also expect a recovery in some markets relevant for Bystronic such as, for example, agriculture, job shops, white goods etc.

But, more profoundly, we must admit that mechanical engineering in the “Western world" has a structural problem. The largest sales market for years, China, is breaking away and it remains to be seen when, and if, the market will come back. China is increasingly able to cover its own production with machines and, thus, seeks to export into the world market. We must position ourselves accordingly so that we can defend against these attacks... I believe in local business by local suppliers and Bystronic is well positioned in this respect.

I have been on board at Bystronic for three months now. We are facing a very challenging global market. Bystronic has a great deal of knowledge as well as fantastic people with lots of expertise, so we have all the ingredients for success when the economy comes back.

In terms of profitability, I expect a much better year for us next year. In terms of volume, I do not expect a huge rebound, but I do see a recovery.

ISMR: Now that you have joined Bystronic, what will be your strategic direction for the company?

DI: I believe that Bystronic has the right strategy to become a full systems supplier, rather than a single machine supplier. We are building on this strategy and are currently building a divisional more customer-centric organisation which will help us on our journey to become one-stop-shop for our customers.

We have formed two divisions: Systems and Service. With the Systems division, we are placing a greater focus on the machine business; combining machines, automation and software. Functions that were previously managed decentrally, will now be integrated into the division (such as engineering, product management and sales). This will enable us to align our product development more closely with market requirements, optimise customer-focused project management and improve overarching communication with our customers.

ISMR: Where will you be directing your investments next year?

DI: We will definitely be investing more into automation and diversification. Automation around the laser is very interesting and we see some new opportunities. In addition, we will launch new tube technology next year. It is our plan to expand the tube business. More announcements on this will be made in 2025.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in global sheet metal markets?

DI: Customers require an end-to-end system in a full solution (e.g. connecting cutting to loading and storage etc.). If you don't incorporate software and digitalisation into that solution, you are out of the market. Some manufacturers are managing their own path on digitalisation, whilst others are cooperating on projects only.

Artificial intelligence (AI) will play a much bigger role in the future, and it is mandatory that manufacturers build on it. Without AI, we will not have the speed to develop the types of systems that we want. It will play a huge role in teaching, programming and adapting machines, as well as proactive maintenance.

## ByCut Eco laser with ByLoader Flex

Bystronic has developed a new entrylevel solution for laser automation; the ByCut Eco laser cutter in combination with the ByLoader Flex automation solution.

“Simple, robust, affordable; these are the attributes of the ByCut Eco. The new Bystronic ByCut Eco 3015/4020 with a choice of 3-, 4- or 6 kilowatts of laser power is the right choice for entrepreneurs who want to get into the laser cutting business or want an uncomplicated fibre laser without many extras," outlined Bystronic.

The ByLoader Flex automation system handles sheet metal on a Bystronic laser cutting system. If customers have more than one laser cutting system, the “2-for-1” principle of the ByLoader Flex means that two laser machines can be managed simultaneously by one ByLoader Flex.

“Simple operation and low price are the plus points of this cutting cell. Customers can leave the loading and unloading process to the ByLoader Flex and take care of more important tasks," concluded Bystronic.

## A new approach: ByCut packages

Bystronic is taking a new approach to its laser cutting systems. Customers no longer select pre-defined machines in various classes with existing features, but instead assemble their Bystronic laser cutting system themselves according to their production requirements.

“The ByCut laser cutter forms the basis. Depending upon requirements, an Autonomy Package; a Power Package; a Dynamic Package; a Quality Cut Package or a Convenience Package can be added. This means that customers receive exactly what they really need. The packages can also be combined," highlighted Bystronic at EuroBLECH 2024.

The laser cutting cell, with laser packages on show at EuroBLECH, included a complete automation system with the ByCut 30kW laser cutter, a third shuttle table (Shuttle Table Extension) and the ByTower Compact combined loading and unloading system (including storage tower).

ISMR: How do you see technologies such as Augmented Reality, Digital Twins and Virtual Reality developing for your business?

DI: As an example, applications such as predictive maintenance will be thriving with the support of digital technologies, so we can help customers solve a problem without a Bystronic technician on-site. And with the digital-twin technology, our customers were able to explore our new ByTube Star 330, without seeing the physical product at our booth. There will be many more applications where we will be able to benefit from these technologies.

ISMR: What is your view on sustainability?

DI: There are two ways to handle sustainability: you do it because you have to do it, or you do it because you are convinced that it is necessary. We really live this topic, and that makes all the difference in our company and products.

Bystronic has developed an ambitious sustainability strategy that is consistently implemented across all areas.

We believe that the biggest impact comes from the performance of our machines. We are constantly improving energy and resource efficiency and have implemented circularity into our product development processes.

It is our goal to make our customers more sustainable with our technologies and innovation.

## ByBend Smart E

The new compact ByBend Smart E is the next generation of bending technology designed for the clean and efficient bending of sheet metal using an electric drive.

"With a bending capacity of 350 or even 550 kilonewtons (kN), which corresponds to a press force of 35.7 or 56.1 tons respectively, users in the sheet metal working industry can reduce the cycle time in their production and increase their productivity," commented Bystronic at EuroBLECH 2024.

"Sustainability is now a top priority, so new machines with better energy consumption have an advantage. The ByBend Smart E scores here: thanks to its intelligent electric drive, energy consumption can be significantly reduced," it added.

The ByBend Smart E's pneumatic clamping for upper and lower tools, (RF-A/XPT quick clamping with T15 table) assists users, particularly during tool changes. It also features an optional laser-based 'Fast Bend M Safety System'. Automatic lubrication of the upper beam helps to keep the press brakes in prime condition, without requiring manual maintenance.

## Software and services

With the BySoft Suite, manufacturers can digitise their sheet metal processing business from quotation preparation to delivery of the end product. The software developed specifically for the sheet metal processing industry won the EuroBLECH Award in the “Handling and Automation” category at EuroBLECH 2022. Whether with individual modules for the respective application area, the “Digital Plans, the “Digital Solutions” or the entire package for the complete digitalisation of production: the BySoft Suite is designed to optimise and accelerate the sheet metal working process.

Whether laser cutting, bending, automation or software, experienced Bystronic specialists can support customers throughout the entire lifecycle of their systems. Bystronic Care plans offer a comprehensive range of services. Digital tools are also offered such as eProActive Service or ePartsFinder, which are designed to simplify the life of sheet metal workers and extend the life of the machines.
