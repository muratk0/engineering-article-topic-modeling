# New Chairman for Open Industry 4.0 Alliance

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

The Open Industry 4.0 Alliance has reorganised. At its Annual General Meeting on 9 December 2022, Dr. Christian Liedtke (responsible for strategic alliances at KUKA) was elected Chairman of the Board of the Alliance and Spokesman.

“In 2023, we will enter a new phase with the Alliance," said Dr. Liedtke. "In particular, we will continue to drive forward the internationalisation of the alliance. Our initial focus is on Europe. Following the successful launch in the Netherlands, Denmark, Belgium and Italy will follow. The alliance also continues to intensify its cooperation with German government organisations, associations and federations."

Hans Huber (Managing Director, Industrial Internet of Things, Endress+Hauser) and Marius Grathwohl (Vice President Digital Products and Transformation, Multivac) support Liedtke as part of the Executive Board. Georg Kube (Global Vice President, SAP) succeeds Nils Herzberg as a new Executive Board member.

A new service company, Open Industry 4.0 Implementation GmbH, will now be managed by Ekrem Yigitdoel (MD of the Open Industry-4.0 Alliance).
