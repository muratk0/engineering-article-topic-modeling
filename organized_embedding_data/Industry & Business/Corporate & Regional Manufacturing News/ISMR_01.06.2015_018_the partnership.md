# THE PARTNERSHIP

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

Polish innovator Eckert, celebrating its 25th anniversary this year, has focused its expertise on metal cutting technologies such as plasma, oxy-fuel, waterjet and laser.

The award-winning Eckert Company has installed almost 1200 cutting system installations worldwide over its 25-year history. Its clients include Daimler Chrysler Rail Systems, Rolls-Royce, Becker Warkop, Manesmann-Siegen, H. Cegielski, Siemens-Chemnitz, Huta Stalowa Wola, Tagor Tarnowskie Góry, Kuka, Crist, Vistal and GEA.

Employing 130 specialists, and celebrating 25 years of innovation this year, it has focused its expertise firmly on cutting, and combination cutting, technologies such as plasma, oxy-fuel, waterjet and laser for metal sheet and tube. The family-owned company has launched a range of innovative cutting systems, such as the patented WaterJet Combo combination waterjet-plasma cutting system (which now features a new 3D bevel cutting head for waterjet operations) and the Diamond Fiber Laser 2 cutting system.

Its head office is in Legnica, Poland, and a further branch is located in Zwickau (Germany). Across its 25,000 m² site in Poland, there are four production halls, warehouse and office buildings. The company plans to expand its infrastructure in the near future. Eckert has gained an important foothold in the highend, combination machine tool market in Europe and sees further growth opportunities in Europe and Asia for sophisticated machine tool technologies.

## An eye on growth

“We see more market demand for customised, high-end machines, certainly in Europe,” Rafael Eckert, Global Sales Director and Co-Owner, told ISMR. “EU investment funds are awarded to manufacturers for the implementation of innovative, high-end manufacturing solutions. This has spurred demand for our high-end machines and we work in partnership with our customers to help them get this funding. Generally, we see an improved economic situation in Europe this year – and 10-15 per cent growth for us this year.”

Eckert is one of the few European CNC cutting machine producers with expertise across all four cutting technologies – oxy-fuel, plasma, laser and waterjet cutting technologies. It recently launched an innovative Piercing Control System (PCS) for its Diamond Fiber Laser 2 cutting machine which substantially reduces piercing time from 10 seconds to two seconds, as well as cutting gas/energy costs. The technology received many awards, including from MLP – ‘Global Leaders of the Future’, the Gold Medal of Trade at the Innovation Technologies Machine Fair 2014 and this year’s STOM Blech exhibition prize.

“We will be celebrating our 25th anniversary on our stand (51-52, Hall 5) at the ITM Poland (Mach-Tool) exhibition in Poznan this June. We will also be showcasing our new Diamond Fibre Laser 2 machine, the WaterJet Combo with new 3D bevel cutting head as well as the new design of our Sapphire plasma cutting machine. We are also working on some new cutting innovations that we will announce later in the year, possibly at Blechexpo,” continued Rafael Eckert.

“Our sales today are split 50 per cent to the Polish market and 50 per cent to the international export market. Exports are rising mainly because of the demand for high-end technology machines. This means that we need more production space and more people – as a result, we will be adding a fifth production hall to our factory at Legnica in Poland later this year.”

## Trends in sheet metal markets

Eckert sells to a number of countries inside and outside Europe. Other export countries/regions include the UAE, Kazakhstan, Russia and Mongolia. Its clients are giants such as power plants, steel mills and coal mines, and its machine tools are involved in creating stadiums, ships, bridges, machine builders and flyovers. Rafael Eckert highlighted a number of growing trends that he sees in sheet metal markets to ISMR.

“We see rising market share for sheet metal producers of innovative products. Customers need more automation and precision in their operations. Their requirements for this are growing so fast that manufacturers can often struggle to keep up with them… We are receiving more requests from companies to partner with them because they are not able to keep up with the technical demands of their customers,” he explained.

“Material thickness is also a key point. The processing of very thin materials calls for machine tools with different dynamics. Machines need to be flexible to cope with different types and thicknesses of materials. The working areas of machine tools are rising as customers are increasingly using larger metal sheets. Consequently, machine tool constructions are getting larger and need to be more flexible (processing sheet metal and tube, for example). Customers are also focused on more intelligent solutions so requirements for automation and safety are also growing.”

This demand for increased precision, safety, flexibility and automation from customers means that Eckert now produces more bevel machines than straight waterjet machines. Its most popular machine is the Sapphire plasma cutting machine. The company is focusing on features such as user-friendliness (regardless of operator experience) and safety in its machine tool designs. It is also able to modify its CNC software to work with a customer’s dedicated CNC software in the interests of flexibility.

“Customers also require more integrated operations, greater productivity and efficiency, saving time and cost,” continued Rafael Eckert. “They want a customised, integrated solution, or an all-in-one combined solution, for their cutting operations. In the eyes of the customer, there is now no gap between us, as the manufacturer of the machine, and our CAD/CAM software partners, for example, as we now offer just one point of contact to the customer for any queries he may have..”

## A strategic focus

Selling one-off solutions is not enough any more, the company told ISMR. Customers are looking for partnerships for the entire lifetime of a machine (20 years and longer). Eckert is therefore developing a customer loyalty programme to bring them closer into the Eckert family and encourage deeper and longer lasting relationships.

“Strategically, we want to develop more long term relationships with customers and then focus on high-end markets here in Europe before moving deeper into Asian markets for our high-end portfolio of machine tools (3D plasma and waterjet etc.),” explained Rafael Eckert.

“We wish to offer more than just a machine to a customer – we want to develop longstanding relationships with customers based on trust and a partnership principle. We offer training, service, advice and customisation options centred around a customer’s requirements. We have 25 years of innovation to offer…”

Eckert is using its 25th anniversary to launch this new initiative to customers in its mission to become one of the leading cutting manufacturers in Europe. Its aim is to get closer to customers, covering the overall lifecycle of the machine, support, training and deepening the long term relationship with customers as well as partners. This is its long term view and constitutes a strong strategy for the future.

This emphasis on placing the customer at the centre of its focus has also influenced the company’s philosophy on future development. Rafael Eckert is adamant that he will continue to focus on high-end sales in Europe before moving his attention to markets further east in Asia.

“It is very important to ensure that we can cover all customer needs (i.e. service, supply, training etc.) before we start selling into a region. We may need to open a new factory or spend time finding the right partners to support us in those regions before we begin operations there. The most important focus for me is serving our customers in the right way and providing the right support for them for the complete lifecycle of our machines,” he told ISMR.

The future looks bright for this award-wining Polish innovator with its continued focus on innovation and customer care.
