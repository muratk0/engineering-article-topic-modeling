# BEP acquires Plating Solutions in the UK

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

BEP Surface Technologies, a leading electroplating and surface finishing specialist in the UK, has announced the acquisition of consultancy, Plating Solutions, to accelerate its growth and innovation strategy.

“Based in Huddersfield, Plating Solutions is a specialised metal finishing plant design and equipment consultancy launched 20 years ago by industry luminary, John Torr. The business boasts a raft of UK and international clients and is a chemical stockist for plating solutions," it explained.

For an undisclosed sum, the strategic acquisition marks BEP's significant expansion and positions it as a forerunner in the UK's metal plating and specialty chemicals trading. It enhances BEP's capabilities to serve diverse industries. Established more than half a century ago, BEP operates in the engineering and manufacturing; plastics; defence; energy; power generation and nuclear sectors, offering solutions to contemporary challenges in industries including EV battery production. The deal will allow for the eventual retirement of Torr, who will take a consultant's role and lead a team of commercial staff and chemical engineers.

Commenting on the acquisition, Andrew McClusky, Managing Director BEP Surface Technologies, said: "We are thrilled to welcome John Torr and the Plating Solutions' clients into the BEP family. John's unparalleled expertise and dedication to advancing surface technologies perfectly align with our mission to drive innovation and excellence in engineering."

This acquisition marks another significant milestone for BEP, which recently launched BEP Solutions to accelerate international R&D partnerships to solve global challenges in surface coating.

"Our recently launched innovation arm, BEP Solutions, will benefit from Jon Torr's deep understanding of process chemistry and commitment to excellence, which have earned him recognition as a leader in the field," added BEP.

BEP has a strong track record of collaborating with international governments, academia and businesses to address challenges within existing and emerging sectors. This includes developing a novel graphene-impregnated copper coating in partnership with the Graphene Engineering Innovation Centre (GEIC) in Manchester; chill roll process optimisation for a leading global food packaging manufacturer; surface coatings research for a major defence supplier and driving research into the development of copper-coated containers for global nuclear waste management organisations.

BEP Surface Technologies specialises in electroplating, metal finishing and coating. Based in Radcliffe, Manchester, the 54-year-old company works with hard chrome, nickel and copper. BEP is certified 'Fit For Nuclear' and holds ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001.
