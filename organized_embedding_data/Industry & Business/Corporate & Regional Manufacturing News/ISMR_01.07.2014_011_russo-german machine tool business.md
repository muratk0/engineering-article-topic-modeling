# Russo-German machine tool business

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

AT Metalloobrabotka 2014, the international trade fair for the metalworking sector held in Moscow from 16 to 20 June 2014, the German machine tool industry showcased its capabilities with 134 exhibitors over a net exhibition area of around 4,000 m².

“That’s a new record and shows that German manufacturers are determined to show the flag in the important Russian market,” said Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, Executive Director of the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders’ Association), speaking to Russian journalists during a press conference.

At the Metalloobrabotka exhibition, Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, Executive Director of the VDW, discussed bilateral machine tool business with Russian trade journalists.

However, he pointed out that the business had become significantly more difficult. Due to high capital outflows and rising interest rates, he said, the Russian economy is in crisis. Consequently, user industries, chief among them automakers and precision mechanics/optics, including medical technology and metal producers, are dramatically downsizing their capital investment levels. British researchers at Oxford Economics, the VDW’s forecasting partner, are expecting a figure of minus 6 per cent in Russia’s machine tool consumption during the current year.

Modern-day production technology, Schäfer continued, is urgently needed to enable domestic demand to be more effectively met in the short term and in the global market to successfully reduce the country’s dependence on raw material exports in the medium term. Russia is the third-largest export market for Germany. With a volume of around 518 million euros and growth of 8 per cent, Germany was the most important supplier of production technology for Russia’s industrial sector last year. ■
