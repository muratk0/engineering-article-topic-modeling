# A firm fixture

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

TR FASTENINGS (TR), a global specialist in the design, engineering, manufacture and distribution of industrial and Cat C fastenings, returned to Mexico City in July to exhibit at the INA PAACE Automechanika Mexico (10-12 July 2019) show for the second year in a row.

TR has 32 business locations in the UK, Asia, Europe and the USA including eight high-volume manufacturing sites. It showcased its full range of automotive fastener products at the event, as well as highlighting its capability to supply many of the components that are used in the manufacture of electric vehicles, batteries and charging units. Own brand products include rivet bushes; self-clinch fasteners; screws for plastic; blind rivet nuts; self-locking nuts; security fasteners; plastic fasteners; cable management products and its new range of enclosure hardware.

Chris Black, Director of Automotive Business Development, TR, commented: "Our growing and highly successful global footprint means we are well positioned to support customers in the fast-evolving automotive landscape in Mexico and the surrounding regions. We finished a very successful show at Automechanika Birmingham 2019, where we held extremely positive meetings with longstanding, new and prospective customers."
