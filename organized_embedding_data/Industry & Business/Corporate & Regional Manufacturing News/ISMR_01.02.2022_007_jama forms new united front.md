# JAMA forms new united front

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

On 18 November 2021, the Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association (JAMA) held an online press briefing to introduce its new executive structure from May 2022. It has put together a structure for developing a 'full line-up' of representation for the Japanese automotive industry.

Akio Toyoda, Chairman of JAMA and President, Toyota Motor Corporation, has accepted an extension of his term at JAMA.

President Katayama of Isuzu will represent commercial vehicles and President Hidaka of Yamaha will represent motorcycles, in their roles as vice chairmen. In addition to President Uchida of Nissan and President Mibe of Honda, President Suzuki of Suzuki has also accepted the post of vice chairman as a representative of mini-vehicles.

"I believe that carbon neutrality is a theme that needs to be addressed by maximizing our full line-up of diversified technologies and approaches under research and development, as well as by maximizing the strengths of each company and field," commented Toshihiro Mibe, Incoming Vice Chairman, JAMA (and President and CEO, Honda Motor Co., Ltd.).

Makoto Uchida, Incoming Vice Chairman, JAMA (and President and CEO, Nissan Motor Co., Ltd.), added: “The strength of the Japanese automobile industry lies in its ability to produce innovative ideas that create new value and in the high level of technology that makes such ideas possible."

"I look forward to working together with all JAMA members to make proposals and take actions not just for vehicles but also for infrastructure development to achieve carbon neutrality," added Toshihiro Suzuki, Incoming Vice Chairman (President, Suzuki Motor Corporation)

"With our new executive structure, we are determined to work together with a strongerthan-ever ‘all Japan' stance and achieve greater innovation," said the association.
