# Intelligent production

[TARİH: 01.06.2013 ISMR]

For the first time, the “Producing More Intelligently” conference will take place during EMO Hannover. Based on the theme “Inspired by Technology”, everything at the conference on 16 and 17 September will revolve around the possibilities afforded by intelligent production. The organiser is the German Engineering Federation, VDMA. The congress will be supported by VDW, the German Machine Tool Builders' Association, which is also the organiser of EMO.

“The partnership with EMO is ideal,” emphasised Carl Martin Welcker, managing partner of Alfred H. Schuette GmbH & Co. KG and EMO General Commissioner. “The world’s largest international platform for production technology offers us the opportunity to make the congress even more significant internationally.”

“This year, the EMO has the guiding theme of ‘Intelligence in Production,’” added Dr. Wilfried Schaefer, Executive Director of VDW. “The congress is therefore the perfect supplement since trade show visitors can deepen their specialist knowledge.”

Solutions for the challenges of today and tomorrow in manufacturing, the latest manufacturing technologies as well as current trends in the machinery and plant engineering sector will be presented at the 4th congress on “Producing More Intelligently”. Amongst other things, the thematic focus will be on the topics of: excellence through lean production and flexible value chains, efficiency through sustainable and resource-saving production and ‘Industry 4.0’, respectively intelligence and communication in manufacturing.

See www.emo-hannover.de for more details.
