# DIVERSIFY TO SUCCEED

[TARİH: 01.05.2011 ISMR]

ONE HUNDRED AND FIFTY delegates from the UK attended a successful conference entitled ‘A Balanced Portfolio: Future Opportunities and Challenges across the Advanced Engineering and Material Sector’, which was held at Castle Park in Doncaster recently. The event, organised by NAMTEC (National Metals Technology Centre) on behalf of AEM Yorkshire, featured a world class line-up of speakers, each of whom highlighted potential opportunities to join the supply chain in their specialist sectors. These sectors included nuclear, carbon capture and storage, aerospace, defence, renewable energy, oil and gas.

Dr Alan Partridge, Chief Executive of NAMTEC (The National Metals Technology Centre) opened the conference with his presentation outlining the need for companies to diversify and seek new markets to protect against any future setbacks in particular sectors.

Dr Partridge said, “In today’s global marketplace, where unpredictable events or accidents can have a significant impact on a whole sector, it is absolutely vital for businesses to reduce the risks by joining several separate supply chains. This conference has been designed to give UK manufacturing companies the support they need to investigate new markets and widen their customer base.”

The speakers were from leading UK and international companies and organisations including The Nuclear Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre (NAMRC), Rolls-Royce, Doosan Babcock, Scottish and Southern Energy, Subsea UK, RenewableUK, Pelamis Wave Power and Alstom.
