## New President for PMA

THE PRECISION METALFORMING Association has confirmed that Roy Hardy joined the organisation as President on 15 May 2017. As President, he will spearhead the association's work including leading PMA's staff; directing strategic planning, membership development and outreach; managing finances and leading domestic and international advocacy efforts.

Roy takes the helm at PMA after eight years as President of the Forging Industry Association. In this position, he represented some 200 member companies ranging in size from multi-billion-dollar, publicly traded corporations to small, privately-held forging shops. Roy succeeds Bill Gaskin, who retired at the end of May after 40 years with the PMA staff, including the last 17 years as President.

The Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) is the full-service trade association representing the US$ 137 billion metalforming industry of North America. Its nearly 900 member companies also include suppliers of equipment, materials and services to the industry. Its events include the FABTECH and METALFORM Mexico tradeshows.

## AutoForm appoints new COO

AUTOFORM ENGINEERING GMBH, a leading supplier of software solutions for the sheet metal forming industry, has appointed Dr. Detlef Schneider as Chief Operating Officer (COO). In this new position at AutoForm, Dr. Schneider will be responsible for operations and will help to lead the company towards successful and continuous business growth.

Dr. Schneider holds both a Master's and a PhD in Mechanical Engineering. Before joining AutoForm, Dr. Schneider worked at Altair, a company focused on the development and broad application of simulation technology. After holding several different positions at Altair, he became Senior Vice President EMEA in 2014, responsible for the management of sales, technical support and marketing.

## SAE honour for Dr. Jwo Pan

DR. JWO PAN, Mechanical Engineering Professor at the University of Michigan, is the recipient of the 2017 SAE International Medal of Honour. Dr. Pan received his medal at the annual Awards Ceremony during the SAE International WCX: World Congress Experience, held from 4-6 April 2017 in Detroit, USA. Dr. Pan has published two books, "Mechanics Modelling of Sheet Metal Forming," published by SAE International, and "Fatigue Testing, Analysis and Design: Theory and Applications," published by Elsevier. He has produced 180 journal papers and 130 conference papers on various research topics and has made original contributions to the development of plasticity, fracture and fatigue theories for the modelling of automotive materials and components. He has also generated new innovative modelling tools for various types of welds; crankshaft fillet rolling; sheet metal forming; vehicle/barrier crashes; bearing design and lithium-ion battery cells and modules. Dr. Pan has been recognised as a leading expert in the diverse automotive engineering areas of welding/joining, crash, fatigue/durability and sheet metal forming.

## CEO of the Year for 'steel transformation'

INTERNATIONAL ENTREPRENEUR, SANJEEV GUPTA, has scooped a top global metals award in recognition of his drive to transform the steel industry in the UK and beyond. Mr Gupta, executive chairman of the Liberty House Group, received the title 'CEO of the Year' at the S&P Global Platts' Global Metals Awards, attended by senior industry figures from across the world, at London's Grosvenor Square Marriott on 19 May 2017.

He was chosen from a shortlist of ten top metal industry executives, who lead prominent businesses in Europe, Asia, North America and Australia. The judges' citation said that they were 'impressed by his track record of successful acquisitions and his role as a motivator willing to swim against the tide of pessimism prevalent in the industry'.

The high-profile honour follows an intensive 18-month period during which the Liberty leader has invested more than £500m to acquire and turn around metals, energy and engineering operations across the UK, saving more than 5,000 jobs in the process. He has subsequently gone on to pursue major mining and metals assets in the USA and Australia.

Since mid-2015, Liberty has moved from being largely a trading entity to become one of the UK's largest industrial employers and owner of the country's last aluminium smelter, its largest steel plate mill and largest scrap melting capacity. It recently completed the acquisition of Tata's speciality steels business – now Liberty Speciality Steels – which supplies high-value steels to automotive, aerospace, industrial and energy clients worldwide.

In leading Liberty through this transformational phase, Mr Gupta has applied the GREENSTEEL business model designed to secure a competitive, low-carbon future for metal manufacture, through recycling, use of renewable energy and vertical integration with value-added engineering activities.

His success in securing the S&P Global Platts' CEO title comes in the Liberty Group's 25th anniversary year. From its beginnings as a trading operation, run from student halls of residence at Cambridge University, it has grown into a global metals and industrials enterprise expected to gross around US$ 7 billion this year. ■
