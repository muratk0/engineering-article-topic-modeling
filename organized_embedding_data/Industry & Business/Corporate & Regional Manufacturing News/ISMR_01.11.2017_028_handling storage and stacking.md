# HANDLING, STORAGE AND STACKING

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

The company's energy recovery and storage concept has won KASTO an environmental technology distinction in the "energy efficiency" category awarded by the German state of Baden-Württemberg.

ISMR: What are your views on the current business climate for sheet metal professionals?

JR: The increasing trend towards digitalisation and global networking is providing companies with new opportunities and new challenges. Competition is becoming more international and more intense. At the same time, expectations on the market are increasing. Customers are demanding more and more different products, and these must be produced in smaller batches. Production must be fast, free of defects, flexible, inexpensive and reliable. Machines and tools must therefore not only be powerful, efficient and versatile in use but must also guarantee the necessary precision and quality.

ISMR: Which issues are of prime importance for your customers and how are you addressing these issues for them?

JR: The issue of energy efficiency is becoming ever more important in storage technology.

Ultimately, increased levels of automation mean that users are also considering power consumption as a decisive cost factor. The requirements placed on machines and systems are therefore not only growing in terms of flexibility, speed and precision, but also for the savings that they can bring. To meet these needs, KASTO's portfolio includes efficient energy recovery and storage methods that allow users to reduce the electricity costs from system operation and, at the same time, improve the quality of the power supply.

Many of our storage systems offer a high level of automation, even in the standard versions, and can be integrated without difficulty into a uniformly controlled materials' flow. At the software level, we also supply intelligent solutions that are adapted to industry needs, for example in the form of our Warehouse Management System. As a result, I believe that we have the right answers when it comes to the issues of digitalisation and networking, which are topics that are also gaining ground in the sheet metalworking industry.

ISMR: What is your strategic and technical focus/vision over the next few years?

JR: We are a global market and technology leader in the manufacturing of systems for sawing and storing bar stock and sheet metal. Our success is based on innovative, high-quality products on the one hand and expert, personal service on the other. Our products range from compact, stand-alone storage towers through to fully automatic high-rack honeycomb systems with thousands of storage cassettes for bar stock, sheet and residual materials which are returned to storage after machining.

There is a huge demand for innovative and high-quality sawing and storage technology and efforts are being stepped up in all industries to deploy networking and automation to rationalise production processes. This is where we can provide users with support. We help companies to achieve significant improvements in production efficiency whilst reducing their costs – two outcomes which, in today's economically challenging climate, are in great demand.

The operators of our sawing and storage systems also expect quick, comprehensive and personal service from the manufacturer – this is why we are constantly expanding our network. We support users in every phase of a project; from planning and development of a plant through to construction and commissioning and all the way up to maintenance and servicing.

ISMR: Which trends do you see developing in sheet metal/storage and cutting markets?

JR: Connectivity and automation are increasing. Machines, goods, raw materials, load carriers, transport equipment and locations are no longer isolated; they are globally linked and interconnected by information networks. Production and logistics are merging, and the integration of processes is increasing. Handling tasks are becoming more and more automated. Digital technology controls the value chain, from the producer of raw materials to the final customer.

Other important trends include a greater emphasis on safety in materials handling and machine control, which is why we particularly focus on developing effective solutions.

KASTO continuously exploits the possibilities for networking and automation, including in its in-house production operations.

ISMR: What was the focus of your booth presence at Blechexpo/ FABTECH? Did you launch any new products at these shows this year?

JR: We presented highlights from both of our business segments at these trade fairs, such as the KASTOecostore space- and energy-saving tower storage system. Besides being suitable for storing sheet metal in a wide range of sizes, it can also hold Euro-pallets and skeleton containers, for example. Special advantages of the automated tower storage system include its low space requirement, high storage density, flexible clamping and fastening, short access times and excellent value for money.

The standard version accommodates materials with a width of up to 1,524mm and a length of up to 4,000mm. The system height can be up to eight metres and can be flexibly selected as required – and the same applies to loading heights. Users can specify up to three different heights between 50- and 950mm per storage system. This makes the storage tower suitable for applications involving many varied materials – in small businesses, for example, and in workshops and maintenance shops. It can also be used as a buffer store between production areas.

We also presented our flexible, multi-purpose KASTOwin line of band saws. These fully automatic machines are designed for the serial and production sawing of solid materials, tubes and sections. The KASTOwin series consists of five fully automatic band saws, covering a cutting range from 330- to 1,060mm.

The machines are suitable for use in areas such as mechanical engineering, tool-making, the steel and automotive industries and aerospace engineering. They are true all-rounders, offering efficient, standardised solutions for a wide range of customer requirements. The KASTOwin is also available with a moveable material support table for heavy workpieces and in a special version for machining tubular material. The KASTOwin pro AC 5.6 is optimised for use with bimetallic and carbide blades, ensuring particularly short cutting times and a long service life.
