# RUN4MFG 5K Charity Run

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 The Fabricator]

FABTECH Partners Joined Forces to Support Manufacturing Education

Pictured above: Mark Ullstrup, Paula Keats, Jennifer Egan, and Matt Bollinger.

Over 120 runners and walkers helped raise funds for the educational foundations of AWS, FMA, SME, PMA, and CCAI on Sunday, Nov. 17, 2013, at the

RUN4MFG 5K

held in Lincoln Park, Chicago. The five associations collaborated to plan this second annual event to support the future of the manufacturing industry and to shed light on the need for skilled workers in the industry.

“The RUN4MFG is an important part of a nationwide push to get more young people involved in manufacturing by providing financial resources to students with a desire to become part of our industry. We have to realize the future of our nation and our prosperity are directly affected by our manufacturing capabilities,” said Frank Fontana, vice president of sales at KNUTH Machine Tools USA Inc. and member of FMA’s Young Professional Council. “We need the next generation to continue the hard work and build on the success of American manufacturers so that our future generations can enjoy the same lifestyle we have.”

Thank you to all who participated and sponsored. A special congratulations to the top male finishers Matt Bollinger of JMT USA and Mark Ullstrup of Superior Die Set and top female finishers Jennifer Egan of TCI Powder Coatings and Paula Keats of Keats Manufacturing!
