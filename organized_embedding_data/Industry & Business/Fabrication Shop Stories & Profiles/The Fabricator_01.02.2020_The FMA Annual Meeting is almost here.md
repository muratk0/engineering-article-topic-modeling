FABTECH Mexico Will Bring the Best in Manufacturing to Mexico City

FABTECH

®

Mexico, Latin America’s largest collaboration of technology, equipment, and knowledge in the metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing industries, will showcase the latest industry innovations at Mexico City’s Centro Citibanamex, May 12-14, 2020.

The event provides exhibitors with an opportunity to showcase metal manufacturing equipment and services to influencers from Latin America. With an expected attendance of over 13,000, FABTECH Mexico 2020 will generate the leads you need to succeed.

FABTECH Mexico is made possible by its five event partners, all of which represent the diverse makeup of the manufacturing industry. They are the American Welding Society (AWS), FMA, SME, the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA), and the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI).
