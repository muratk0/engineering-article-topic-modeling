As we reach the end of 2021, Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT) reflects on a year that again brought its share of ups and downs to the industry. Lingering effects from the global pandemic continued to have a direct impact on the students who benefit from NBT’s summer manufacturing camp and scholarship programs. Schools were slow to open, didn’t open at all, or faced the continued challenge of virtual classes.

With the unwavering help of the foundation’s supporters, NBT was able to take this opportunity to learn, adapt, and thrive. In 2021, NBT continued to support students in their pursuit of a career in manufacturing. Nearly 2,000 middle to high school-aged students attended one of the 121 NBT manufacturing camps held across the country, and 56 manufacturing students received scholarships this year.
