# Congratulations, 2018 FMA/CNA Safety Award Winners

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 The Fabricator]

News and information from the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

®

The FMA/CNA Safety Award program recognizes metal fabrication companies that have an ongoing commitment to excellence in safety. Award winners are determined by the FMA Safety Council and the awards are sponsored by CNA. The awards were presented at a recognition dinner during the 10th Annual Safety Conference, which was held April 18-19, 2018, at FMA’s headquarters in Elgin, Ill.

Forty-seven facilities received awards for achieving safety records better than the OSHA recordable rate for their North American Industry Classification System (NAICS) codes. Each year, company-level members are eligible to apply by submitting their OSHA 300A forms.

AK Tube LLC Receives the 2018 Rusty Demeules Award

AK Tube LLC of Columbus, Ind., has been named the winner of the 2018 Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence. This award, FMA’s highest safety honor, was created by FMA’s Safety Council to recognize a company with a demonstrated safety culture. It is named in honor of Rusty Demeules, a significant volunteer to FMA and president of the FMA Safety Committee from its inception in 1989 until 1996.

Management commitment is evident within the AK Tube LLC program, which reflects the safety plan of its parent company, AK Steel. "FMA is pleased to present the Rusty Demeules Safety Award to AK Tube," said Edward Youdell, president and CEO, FMA. "Beginning with its formal safety orientation program for new employees, dedicated recordkeeping, ongoing management commitment, and the CEO’s signature on its safety policy, it’s clear AK Tube is a worthy recipient of this award."

Roger Wilson, assistant vice president and industry leader for manufacturing, CNA, said, "We are proud to sponsor the Rusty Demeules Award and are very proud to recognize AK Tube as the 2018 award recipient for their conscious work to create a culture of safety."

Congratulations to all award winners on their accomplishments!
