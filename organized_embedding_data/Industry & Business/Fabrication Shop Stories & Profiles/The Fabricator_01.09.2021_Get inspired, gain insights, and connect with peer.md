FMA Fall Training Programs and Conferences

FMA events give you the opportunity to obtain innovative information and rub shoulders with leading industry professionals, enabling you to achieve business objectives so you can thrive in the upcoming year and beyond.

From improving technical skills to building invaluable relationships, take your career or business to the next level by attending FMA’s fall training programs and conferences.

Oct. 6-7 | Tampa, Fla.

Want to improve your roll forming process and apply best practices? FMA-sponsored sessions will provide updates, new innovations, and practical information you can integrate into your operations to increase productivity.

ASD Executive Networking Event

Oct. 27-28 | Michigan City, Ind.

World-Class Roll Forming Workshop

Nov. 2-3 | Pittsburgh

Experience two days of interactive sessions, panel discussions, plant tours, networking, and Roll Forming Jeopardy. Take a tour of the Edgar Thompson Plant! It’s one of Mon Valley Works’ integrated steelmaking operation and is located about 10 miles southeast of Pittsburgh in Braddock, Pennsylvania, where basic steel production occurs.
