(NBT) silent auction at the FMA Annual Meeting was a thrill-a-minute as the room at the Hyatt Regency in San Antonio came alive. The March 3 auction offered unique items such as a vacation timeshare, golf experiences, autographed sports memorabilia, and techie gadgets. Auction proceeds support the summer manufacturing camp and scholarship programs of NBT.
