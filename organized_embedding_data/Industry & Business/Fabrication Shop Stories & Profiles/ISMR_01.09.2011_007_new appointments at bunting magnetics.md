# NEW APPOINTMENTS AT BUNTING MAGNETICS

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

BUNTING MAGNETICS, headquartered in Newton, Kansas (USA), has made two appointments to its manufacturing team. “We are experiencing a large increase in sales and as a result needed additional talent to help balance our workload,” said Barry Voorhees, general manager for Bunting’s main production facility. “We anticipate this growth continuing throughout the first half of 2011 and we will be adding more headcount in our technical services department soon.”

Matt Anderson, manufacturing engineer, will handle the overall workflow of active orders between the technical services group and the plant floor operations. He has several years of experience in sheet metal fabrication and shop floor planning along with experience in CAD/CAM systems, job routing and tooling design. Anderson has designed and engineered new products for Weckworth Manufacturing in Haysville, Kansas and Hehr International in Newton, Kansas.

Kenton Stumps, appointed as a design engineer for Bunting’s material handling production line, holds a Bachelor of Science degree in mechanical engineering from Wichita State University. He has experience in the aerospace, construction and manufacturing sectors, as well as a deep background in Catia V5, Solid Works and AutoCad software programs.

The company recently announced the addition of Simon Ayling, as the new Production Manager, and Steve Lacy, as a new Technical Sales Executive, for its European manufacturing and sales operation centre based in Berkhamsted, UK. This location is responsible for all sales in the United Kingdom, Europe and Africa. This operation has begun to manufacture equipment that is part of the company’s Magnetic Separation product line.

## MCA ELECTS 2011 BOARD OF DIRECTORS AND OFFICERS

THE METAL CONSTRUCTION ASSOCIATION (MCA) in the US elected its 2011 Board of Directors and Officers at its annual meeting in San Diego, CA.

Jeff Irwin, Kingspan Benchmark, Columbus, OH will serve his second term as Chair (previous title of President), Todd Miller, Classic Products Inc., Piqua, OH will serve as Vice Chair and Karl Hielscher, Metl-Span LLC, Lewisville, TX, will serve as Treasurer.

John Bernardi, vice president of business development for Firestone Building Products in Indianapolis, IN and Jim Tuschall, president and CEO of Tuschall Engineering in Burr Ridge, IL were newly elected to the MCA Board of Directors.

In addition to Mr. Bernardi and Mr. Tuschall, those serving on the 2011 MCA Board of Directors are: Ken Buchinger, MBCI, Houston, TX; Bill Croucher, Fabral, Lancaster, PA; Jerry Hatley Jr, Roll Coater Inc., Indianapolis, IN; Bill Hippard, Precoat Metals, St. Louis, MO; Ed Karper, AkzoNobel, Columbus, OH; Ted Miller, Miller Clapperton, Austell, GA; Brian Partyka, Drexel Metals Corp., Ivyland, PA; Sid Peterson, Alcoa Architectural Products, Lisle, IL; Renee Ramey, Steelscape Inc., Kalama, WA; Randy Ridenour, Atlas Bolt & Screw, Ashland, OH; Norbert Schneider, Umicore Building Products USA Inc., Raleigh, NC; and Roger Sieja, Wismarq Corp. Inverness, IL.

The Metal Construction Association (MCA) is an organisation of manufacturers and suppliers whose metal products are used in structures throughout the world. The association promotes the use of metal in construction through education, marketing support, technical programmes, monitoring of industry issues and achievement awards. MCA’s 2011 Semi-Annual Meeting is held from 1–3 August 2011 at The Umstead in Raleigh, NC (USA).

## ESSAR STEEL ALGOMA SELECTS NEW CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS of Essar Steel Algoma Inc. is pleased to confirm that James Hrusovsky officially assumed the role of Chief Executive Officer of the company on 24 June 2011 having received final regulatory approval.

Mr. Hrusovsky, 54, has held a number of senior executive positions in the steel industry, most recently as Chief Executive Officer, Severstal Columbus.

Mr. Malay Mukherjee, CEO Essar Steel Business Group, said, “We are very pleased to attract an individual with Mr. Hrusovsky’s skills to serve as the company’s CEO. Mr. Hrusovsky is one of the leading executives in the steel industry with over 29 years of experience in nearly all aspects of steel making including research, quality, product development, engineering, capital projects, sales and profit centre management.”

Essar Steel Algoma Inc. is based in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario. Revenues are derived primarily from the manufacture and sale of rolled steel products, including hot and cold rolled sheet and plate.
