# Straightpoint recruits logistics expert

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

Simon Paine, Logistics Supervisor (centre) is welcomed by Alfie Lee, Operations Manager (left), and David Ayling, Director

STRAIGHTPOINT has named Simon Paine as its logistics supervisor, who brings to the role three decades of Tier One supply chain experience from the automotive sector. The force measurement, load monitoring and suspended weighing load cell manufacturer prides itself on short lead times, putting Paine at the forefront of one of the most important departments at the company.

He said: “That doesn’t faze me at all. I am used to less than 24-hour lead times in certain instances. I intend to use the skills and tools that I have learned during more than 30 years in logistics. Initially, I will review working practices and subsequently consult with the team to understand its issues, concerns and roadblocks.”

Paine explained that inventory reduction and delivery are two key performance indicators by which to measure his success. He added: “My role will centre on ensuring shipments are made on time, whilst overseeing receipt of goods and supplied parts in addition to tracking shipments and arranging transportation quotes.”

He drew synergies between his old and new manufacturing environments. “Moving parts are moving parts,” he said. “It’s the strict adherence to quality and effective SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) that ensures one gets it right first time. In both the automotive and force measurement sectors, there are complex parts and pieces of kit, short lead times and issues to work through with suppliers.” ■

## Hervé Boyer joins Nexteer Automotive

NEXTEER AUTOMOTIVE, which specialises in intuitive motion control, has appointed Hervé Boyer to lead the growth efforts of the company’s Europe & South America Division. Taking the role of Vice President, Chief Operating Officer – Europe and South America, Boyer will also be a member of Nexteer’s global strategic council.

Hervé joins Nexteer from NBHX where he held the role of Group Executive. Before that, he gained experience at the Faurecia Group where he served as Vice President of the French, US & Japanese customer divisions, as well as the company’s Southern Europe region. While there, Hervé also served in the capacity of President & CEO of North America for Faurecia Interior Systems.

These previous roles have given Hervé extensive experience in sales, product line management and operations. He also holds an engineering degree from the Ecole Centrale de Nantes and is certified in Advanced/Leadership programmes from both Harvard Business School and INSEAD. ■

## Koslowski moves from Gartner to Porsche

WITH IMMEDIATE EFFECT, Thilo Koslowski is assuming a key role in the area of digital mobility at Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG. He joins Porsche from leading international technology advisory company, Gartner Inc., and is known for his extensive knowledge of the automotive as well as Internet and technology industries. He will report directly to the Chairman of the Executive Board, Oliver Blume.

At Gartner, Koslowski most recently advised global automakers and technology companies on transforming new market trends and technologies into innovative products, customer experiences, business models and partnerships.

In cooperation with all departments involved, Koslowski will assume a leading role at Porsche in implementing innovative technologies, especially in the areas of connectivity, digital customer experiences, smart mobility and autonomous vehicles. His responsibilities also include corporate venturing, seeking suitable partners in the process of digital transformation of Porsche, as well as the implementation of innovative customer offerings.

Koslowski studied business management with a major in Marketing, European Economy and Production Engineering at the RWTH Aachen University. He subsequently worked in various marketing positions at Audi. ■

## NEXTSENSE appoints new sales and marketing director

HARALD HOPFGARTNER has been appointed sales and marketing director for NEXTSENSE, the Austrian specialist in optical sensor technologies. He succeeds Franz Schiep, who has retired after the successful establishment of an international sales network.

Before joining Nextsense in 2009 as international sales manager, he worked as a consultant and Nextsense was amongst his customers. Over the last seven years, he has been in charge of the worldwide acquisition of customers and sales partners and took over representation from Franz Schiep.

Besides the worldwide distribution of measurement devices, Hopfgartner is responsible for the strategy development of sales and marketing. Within nine years, the workforce of Nextsense has grown from four to more than 50.

"By 2020, we want our turnover to be tripled. In addition, we intend to increase our number of employees to a total of 150,”said Hopfgartner. "The most important thing is to meet the expectations of our customers with our unique products. To achieve these objectives, we don’t want to swim with the tide - we still want to focus on niche markets. I look forward to the challenge and the ambitious growth plans."

Nextsense GmbH is a specialist in solutions for profile measurement and surface inspection. All solutions are based on non-contact measurement technology. See www.nextsense.at ■
