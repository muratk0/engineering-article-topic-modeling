# Bradbury welcomes six interns

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Every year, The Bradbury Group welcomes back many new and returning interns spending their summer in the heartland of Kansas. They will be working from The Bradbury Group's headquarters in Moundridge, Kansas (USA).

"Whether it is in the office or on the shop floor, we welcome interns throughout our facility who learn from trusted full-time employees. Over the summer, interns gain valuable work experience while pursuing our vision, mission, values and beliefs. This summer, we are fortunate to welcome three new interns, as well as three returning interns," commented The Bradbury Group, introducing the new interns as:

■Dillon McGinn, a software engineering Intern, attending Kansas State University.

■Taylor Stucky, an electrical engineering Intern, attending Kansas State University.

■Mateo Roth, an assembly intern, will be attending Kansas State University. Returning interns are:

■Riley Shelton, an electrical engineering intern, attending Kansas State University.

■Cedon Yoder, a mechanical engineering Intern, attending Eastern Mennonite University.

■Kolton Sims, a roll-tooling engineering intern, attending Kansas State University.

These six interns have completed orientation and already begun work in their assigned departments.

The Bradbury Group is a global group of companies dedicated to producing productivity-enhancing metal processing equipment. The companies within the Bradbury Group manufacture customised rollforming systems; precision levellers; coil processing lines; equipment control solutions and automated production systems for more than a dozen industries.
