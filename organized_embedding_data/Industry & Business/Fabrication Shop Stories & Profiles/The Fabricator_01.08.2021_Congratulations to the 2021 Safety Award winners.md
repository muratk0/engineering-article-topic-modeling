# Congratulations to the 2021 Safety Award winners

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 The Fabricator]

This year, a record-breaking 67 FMA Business members are receiving awards for achieving safety records better than the OSHA recordable rate for their NAICS codes. The FMA / CNA Safety Award winners will be honored at a networking reception and awards dinner Oct. 20, 2021, in North Charleston, South Carolina.

Rusty Demeules Award

This award honors long-time former FMA volunteer Rusty Demeules’ passion for creating safe work cultures and is presented to one company each year that best exemplifies a dedication to safety. The 2021 Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence will be presented to Tampa Tank Inc, Gibsonton, Florida, for their outstanding commitment to ensuring a safe culture in their facility.
