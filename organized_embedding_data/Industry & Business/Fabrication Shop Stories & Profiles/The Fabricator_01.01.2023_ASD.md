Dear Fellow Metals Industry Professionals,

The Association of Steel Distributors (ASD) was founded in 1943 and became a part of FMA in 2018. The ASD affiliation brings a combination of benefits and unique perspectives to both fabricators and steel suppliers — providing a platform to exchange unrealized ideas and market information to help advance your career and business.

Today, I’m writing to let you know of a new and exciting direction with the Association of Steel Distributors. Since our merger with FMA almost five years ago, we knew we had an opportunity to expand our member base and work with the fabricators in our industry more directly. We have recognized the need to become more inclusive of the entire metals industry and not solely steel distributors.

The ASD is committed to education, training, professional networking, and fundraising. Through these actions we know we can make a positive impact on the industry, individuals, and companies who are involved with us. We work with mills, distributors, processors, and service centers to provide content and events that are valuable to each of you.

I’m inviting you to join us as we kick off the NEW ASD in February during the FMA Industry Awards night in Las Vegas, Nevada, where we will honor the 2022 ASD Steel Executive of the year.

If you are interested in getting more involved with us, visit our website and sign up for membership today.

Thank you,

Michael Barnett Association of Steel Distributors Chairman

Patrick J. Simon Director of Membership Fabricators and Manufacturers Association
