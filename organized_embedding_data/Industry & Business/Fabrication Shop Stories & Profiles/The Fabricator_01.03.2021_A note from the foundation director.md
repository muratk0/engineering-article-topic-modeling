(NBT), the foundation of FMA, is getting ready to kick off our summer manufacturing camps. These fun and interactive camps, for ages 12-16, are one of NBT’s programs that help young people explore career paths that lead them to promising positions in manufacturing.

From 2010 to 2020, the manufacturing camp program has grown from 16 camps to almost 10 times that number. The growth has been an amazing accomplishment that has a powerful impact on the industry.

Since inception, NBT has awarded over $1.4 million in camp grants to support the future of manufacturing. And we couldn’t have done it without your generous donations. We are grateful for every single dollar, as it is an investment in the next generation of the manufacturing workforce.

Thank you for your support, Edward C. Dernulc
