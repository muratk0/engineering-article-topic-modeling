## Women in manufacturing study overview

In 2017, The Manufacturing Institute, APICS and Deloitte surveyed more than 600 women in manufacturing and conducted nearly 20 manufacturing executive interviews to explore how effectively manufacturing companies are attracting, recruiting and retaining women, and what should be done to close the gender gap. The women surveyed were well-educated (90 per cent with a bachelor's degree or above), experienced (71 per cent had been working over 15 years), held a variety of senior positions and were employed by large companies (52 per cent worked in organisations with annual revenue of over one billion dollars.)
