An Invitation From Foundation Leadership

I’m thrilled that for the first time, Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT), the foundation of the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

®

(FMA), will host an awards gala on October 24 in the Chicagoland area. The gala will celebrate manufacturing, and NBT will recognize some of the industry’s finest contributors.

Proceeds from the gala will go to NBT’s summer manufacturing camp and scholarship programs to encourage manufacturing’s next generation and help ensure a future workforce of highly skilled and motivated professionals.

Having hosted camps at my school, I have had the privilege of seeing many students discover a lifelong passion for creating things with their hands and identify the educational pathways that can lead to prosperous manufacturing careers.

I hope to see you at the gala,

Mike Cattelino

Nominate Industry Contributors

Avanté Banquets & Conference Center • Fox River Grove, Ill.

NBT’s inaugural awards gala will acknowledge individuals and companies for their generosity and commitment to ensuring a thriving manufacturing future. Since the foundation’s inception more than 25 years ago, NBT has been committed to supporting the industry’s workforce needs. Thanks to the continued support of generous donors, the foundation has provided over $1 million in scholarships and over $1 million in camp grant funding, impacting more than 6,000 students.

One of the ways notable contributors can be honored for their part in reaching these remarkable milestones is by receiving an award at the gala. Three prestigious awards will be presented: Power of Industry, Impact on Industry, and Future of Industry. Nominations for award recipients are being accepted through August 31.
