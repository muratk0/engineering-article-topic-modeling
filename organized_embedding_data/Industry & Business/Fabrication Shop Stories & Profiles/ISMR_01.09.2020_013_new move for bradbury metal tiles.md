# New move for Bradbury Metal Tiles

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

BRADBURY METAL TILE Roofing Solutions (BMTRS) has welcomed Justin Pressnall to its U.S. sales team.

“Justin will work with Lance Eagle (Business Development Sales Manager, BMTRS) on the development of marketing and sales for the North American market. He will handle enquiries and sales for metal tile production lines in the USA and Canada. Justin will remain on the Bradbury sales team, focusing on the metal building and steel framing industries,” explained the company.

Justin Pressnall has been with The Bradbury Group since 2006. He began in the shop as a machine operator at Group Headquarters in Moundridge, KS (USA). He quickly progressed to the production planning department, where he was a planner/scheduler for six years before being promoted to master scheduler. He held that role for another three years before transitioning to the sales department in 2017 where he became a technical sales leader.
