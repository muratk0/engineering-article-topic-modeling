# Join the mission to support tomorrow’s workforce

[TARİH: 01.03.2021 The Fabricator]

The goal of NBT’s camp program is to introduce young people to the excitement, challenge, and pride of building something with their own hands and using advanced technology to inspire them to pursue careers in manufacturing.

The benefits companies can gain from a growing number of successful summer camps goes beyond the present circumstances. The 2021 camp program will impact an estimated 2,700 students, their parents, school instructors, community colleges, technical schools, and local businesses.

You can help the next-generation workforce to discover manufacturing by supporting a camp in your area. Offer to host a plant tour of your manufacturing facility, volunteer as a guest speaker or camp mentor, or give financial support as a camp sponsor.
