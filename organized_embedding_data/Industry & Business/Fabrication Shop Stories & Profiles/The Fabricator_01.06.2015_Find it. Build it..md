Find and Build Business Worth at the Drivers of Business Valuation Conference

One of the most fundamental tasks of business ownership and management is increasing the value of your business. Find out what drives business value and how you can manage it at the

Drivers of Business Valuation Conference

Aug. 24-25, 2015, held in conjunction with the GOLF4MFG NBT Open in Lake Geneva, Wis.

Uniquely created for owners of smaller, privately held specialty manufacturing, fabrication, and related businesses, the Drivers of Business Valuation Conference will provide the insight and knowledge business owners need to lead their companies toward the top of the valuation scale by learning

why

and

how

to manage for valuation.

Topics include the art and science of private company M&A, transaction types and financial (including tax) implications, considerations for family-owned businesses, and the impact of the economy on business value.

Whether you are interested in selling, buying, or just staying the course, this event is a rare opportunity to fully understand what influences your company’s most important metric: its

worth
