# Safety award for Waukesha Metal Products

[TARİH: 01.05.2013 ISMR]

WAUKESHA® Metal Products of Grafton, Wisconsin (USA) was recently chosen to receive a 2013 Safety Award of Honour from the Fabricators and Manufacturers Association, International (FMA). Open to all FMA and Tube and Pipe International (TPA) company members, the FMA/CNA Annual Safety Awards recognises metal fabrication companies that adhere to excellence in safety.

Sponsored by CNA, the endorsed business insurance carrier for FMA, the awards are designed to promote safety in the industry and winners are selected by the FMA Safety Council. The Safety Award of Honour is given to companies with perfect safety records of no recordable injuries or illnesses for the reporting period.

“By winning an FMA/CNA Safety Award of Honour, Waukesha Metal Products has demonstrated its commitment to recognising employees’ safe work habits, stimulating interest in accident prevention and promoting safety in the workplace,” said Edward Youdell, President and CEO of FMA. “It’s clear that they place a premium on ensuring their employees safely return home to their families each night. We want all companies to operate in this manner.”

From humble beginnings as a two-man tool and die shop, Waukesha Metal Products has developed into an international full-service metalforming manufacturer, delivering parts and assemblies in the industry since 1971. Waukesha Metal Products is an international full service provider of metalforming services including custom metal stampings, sheet metal fabrications and inhouse precision tooling. It has two locations in the greater Milwaukee area of southeastern Wisconsin and 40 years of manufacturing experience. See www.WaukeshaMetal.com
