# Service Package Reduces Maintenance Burden for Complex Structural Component Manufacturer

SITUATION

Northern Stamping is a Cleveland-based producer of complex structural components and welded parts for a number of industries, including automotive. Its facility has 70 welding robots and peripheral equipment, an array requiring a network of 28 dust-collecting units.

While the units were effective, as their number increased, the company experienced more challenges with regular preventive maintenance. Northern Stamping preferred to keep its maintenance staff focused on maintaining the company’s production machinery. They weren’t experts in RoboVent equipment.

That kind of maintenance "isn’t one of our core competencies," explained Scott Sheffield, vice president of operations. It might have even required hiring a new staff member.

RESOLUTION

RoboVent presented a client care package to Northern Stamping designed to meet its maintenance and budgetary needs. Under the plan, RoboVent sends service representatives once a month to change filters, conduct routine maintenance, and troubleshoot emerging issues. The visits are coordinated with facility staff to work around production schedules and minimize downtime.

This level of preventive maintenance not only helps extend the life of the equipment, but also reduces the burden on the company’s maintenance staff. "I have more time to maintain the other equipment we’re using in our facility," said Greg Degan, maintenance manager. "It saves labor hours."

Monthly reports from the RoboVent team identify potential issues and provide recommendations. Problems with the machinery now are rare, and if something does come up, replacement parts are sent within a day, and a team is there within one to two days for the installation. Under the client care package, the firm is guaranteed service while avoiding costs of buying replacement filters, hiring additional maintenance staff, and budgeting for and stocking replacement parts.
