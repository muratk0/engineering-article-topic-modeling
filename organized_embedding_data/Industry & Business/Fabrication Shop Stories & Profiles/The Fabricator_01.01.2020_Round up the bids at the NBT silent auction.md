The FMA and CNA awards program recognizes several levels of success with numerous awards. All company-level members who qualify receive an award! Applying is easy! Simply submit the OSHA Form 300A to

nancyh@fmanet.org

.

The highest level of recognition in FMA’s Safety Award program is the Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence, celebrating one company each year that excels beyond OSHA recordable incidence rates. The recipient will receive complimentary registration and travel reimbursement for one company representative to accept the award at a special reception and dinner. An engraved crystal award will be presented, and the company will be recognized in FMA publications.

All awards will be presented at FMA’s 12th annual Safety Conference April 22-23 in North Charleston, South Carolina. The deadline to apply for awards is February 28.
