# FMA and TPA Present Member Companies With 2014 Industry Awards

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 The Fabricator]

Congratulations to two longtime FMA/TPA members — DeWys Manufacturing Inc. and Superior Tube Company Inc. — on becoming

The FABRICATOR®

and

TPJ-The Tube & Pipe Journal®,

respectively, 2014 Industry Award honorees. These awards are bestowed upon an individual or company within the industry that has successfully improved operations, attained business success, and contributed to both their local and business communities.

DeWys Manufacturing Inc. is featured as the cover story in this issue of

The FABRICATOR,

and Superior Tube Company Inc. will receive the same recognition in the January/ February 2014 edition of

TPJ

.

“Manufacturing has been the backbone of America, and we feel it’s appropriate to honor the heroes of the industry by paying tribute to these companies that exemplify success,” said James Warren, senior director of member services, education & foundation for FMA. “Congratulations to the management and staff.”

Celebrate with the recipients of

The FABRICATOR®

and

TPJThe Tube & Pipe Journal® 2014 Industry Awards

in a ceremony during

The FABRICATOR’s 9th Annual Leadership Summit,

which begins Feb. 26 at the Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa in Austin, Texas. Leaders of the two companies will each make brief remarks about their companies’ accomplishments qualifying them for these prestigious awards.
