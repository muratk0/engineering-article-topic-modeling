# Generational Strategist to Keynote FMA Annual Conferences, Feb. 26-28, 2014, in Austin, Texas

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 The Fabricator]

IN THE KNOW

Co-located events to focus on the next generation of manufacturing and advanced high-strength metals

Get a glimpse into the future at FMA’s annual meetings Feb. 26-28, 2014, at the Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa in Austin, Texas. Both

The FABRICATOR®’s Leadership Summit

and

18th Annual Toll Processing Conference

will be very forward-looking, featuring a keynote presentation by learning strategist Diane Thielfoldt.

Thielfoldt, co-founder of the Learning Cafe, will present strategies for leading the multiple-generation workforce. Thielfoldt specializes in resolving issues related to the four-generation workforce and helps organizations bridge the generation gap at work. She has learned from the age-diverse workforce by engaging in primary research, analyzing secondary research, capturing trends, teaching in organizations, and observing workplace behavior — to build a strong knowledge base of the four generations’ commonalities and differences. Her thought-provoking presentation will educate the audience on the importance of flexing to meet the needs of a changing workforce — and give practical ways to do so.

Diane Thielfoldt, co-founder, the Learning Cafe,

“Manufacturing: The Next Generation” will be the focus of discussions at

The FABRICATOR’®s Leadership Summit.

Planned by executives of progressive job shops, the conference exists to invigorate metal fabricators with an annual injection of knowledge, skill development, inspiration, and networking opportunities for the advancement of the greater manufacturing community. Anchors of the program are noncompetitive, peer-to-peer roundtable discussions. Some of the 2014 topics are: Employee Engagement, Lean Manufacturing in the Job Shop, Robotic Technology & Automation, Sales & Marketing, and Strategic Planning.

The theme of the

2014 Toll Processing Conference

is “Advanced High-Strength Metals — Ready or Not, Here They Come!” Participants will hear about the progressively stronger and lighter materials being developed and get advice on how best to prepare for the oncoming processing challenges these new metals will create. In addition, attendees will gain steel and aluminum market insight from industry experts and world-class speakers.

Valuable networking time during plant tours, golf tournaments, and fun evening receptions round out the events, making them “must attend” for those working to move ahead in today’s competitive market.
