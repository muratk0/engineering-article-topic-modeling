# A note from the president of FMA

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 The Fabricator]

News and information from the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

∗

Ed Youdell

President and CEO

Fabricators & Manufacturers Association

I am pleased to announce the recipients of this year’s Safety Awards. Open to all FMA Business members, the program acknowledges metal fabrication companies committed to safety excellence.

FMA, along with CNA, are honored to share that a record number of winners will be recognized for their safety achievements during an awards dinner at the 13th annual Safety Conference, Oct. 20-21, 2021.

Sponsored by CNA, the conference will be in person where attendees can connect with other safety personnel from across the country and learn about the latest safety regulations, machine safety best practices, response plans, and more.

Congratulations to this year’s Safety Award winners. We look forward to celebrating your safety achievements this fall.

Thank you,

Edward Youdell
