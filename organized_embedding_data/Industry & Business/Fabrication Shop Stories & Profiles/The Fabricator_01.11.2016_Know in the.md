Chief Economist/Director of Research for Consumer Technology Association to Give Keynote at FMA Annual Meeting

FMA is excited to announce that Dr. Shawn DuBravac, chief economist for the Consumer Technology Association (CTA), will deliver the opening keynote presentation of the 2017 Annual Meeting happening March 8-10 in New Orleans. DuBravac has been widely published and appeared in

The Wall Street Journal, The New York Times

, the

Financial Times

, the

Los Angeles Times

, and

Barron’s

and on CNN, MSNBC, and other media outlets.

The CTA, which represents more than 2,200 consumer technology companies, organizes the wildly popular Consumer Electronics Show, a global tradeshow that takes place every January in Las Vegas. In his role as CTA’s chief economist, DuBravac provides crucial economic analysis to industry leaders regarding the health of the technology sector. In his presentation to FMA he will share which emerging technologies will stick and disrupt business models and discuss how business innovation has and will continue to affect the metal manufacturing industry.

Manufacturing Day 5th Anniversary Event Resounding Success — Especially in Metal Fabricating Industry

The 5th annual Manufacturing Day

SM

was celebrated Oct. 7 with a presidential proclamation, and even one of the most powerful hurricanes of this century couldn’t dampen the spirit of the event.

One might think that an event like Manufacturing Day couldn’t capture the attention and imagination of the nation while competing with a contentious U.S. presidential campaign and Hurricane Matthew — but it did!

Trending at No. 5 on Twitter

, and with events in all 50 states, Puerto Rico, and Canada, MFG DAY was a force to be reckoned with.

More than 2,740 events in 2016 broke all previous records

. When the tallying was done, based on industry classifications, more events were held by companies in the fabricated metal sector than any other single manufacturing sector.

While the attendance numbers had yet to be verified at the time this magazine went to press, the anecdotal evidence was trending strongly in favor of last year’s record being broken. The Science Channel ran commercials for two weeks prior to the event to encourage the fans of

"How It’s Made"

to go and see how things are made in their own communities, and the annual radio media tour, which is conducted a few days before MFG DAY, reached more than 14 million potential attendees with information about what to do and where in their own communities. But in the end, it’s all about the dedication of the companies that open their doors to the community and issue invitations to local schools that makes

MFG DAY a resounding success

. Thank you to everyone in the metal fabrication community for your efforts!

$57,000 in Grants Awarded in 2016!

In 2016 Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT), the foundation of FMA, had the largest summer manufacturing camp season in years. Check out the entire list of camps, locations, photos, news stories, videos, and projects on the NBT website at

nbtfoundation.org/camps

.

A camper shares her project with Ed Youdell, FMA and NBT president & CEO.

Since 2003 NBT has awarded grants annually to schools and community or technical colleges to host a summer camp program for girls and boys ages 12-16. Summer camps have been a successful way of introducing middle and high school students to the fascinating, high-tech career choices available to them in today’s automated manufacturing industry.

Grant awards follow a tiered structure:

First-year award: $2,500

Second-year award: $1,500

Third-year award: $1,000

The 2017 camp grant deadline is approaching; applications are due Nov. 15, 2016. Access the application online at

or call 888-394-4362.
