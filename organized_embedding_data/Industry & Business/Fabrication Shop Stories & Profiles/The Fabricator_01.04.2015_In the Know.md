22nd Annual ALAW: Advanced Laser Applications Workshop

June 1-3, 2015

The Inn at St. John’s Conference Center, Plymouth (Detroit), Mich.

"I have always found ALAW to be a very insightful and worthwhile experience. It’s a great way to learn and keep current with the latest manufacturing uses of lasers from both a product and process standpoint."

— Mark Williamson, Project Manager - Adv Mfg Engr Johnson Controls Inc.

The theme for this year’s ALAW is

Lasers: Navigating an Advanced Multi-Material World

. Lasers are becoming more versatile and efficient. As the use of lasers becomes more widespread, product designers and engineers are using various metals during the laser welding process. They also must learn how to design components, assemblies, and systems for successful implementation of lightweight parts. These topics are addressed in-depth, along with practical examples and case studies to help build a foundation in laser design and implementation.

This workshop is designed for metal fabrication job shops, manufacturers, suppliers, automotive and aerospace engineers, and anyone interested in learning more about laser material processing systems to reduce costs, improve quality, save material and reduce weight, and provide flexible laser manufacturing.

In addition to the keynote presentation by Blair Carlson of GM, 26 presentations will be offered on the latest topics in laser welding of multi-materials, remote laser welding, lightweighting, and laser additive manufacturing, just to name a few. Two world-class open houses featuring live welding and cutting demos will be held at Fraunhofer USA-Center for Laser Applications and TRUMPF’s Laser Technology Center. Registration for both open houses is free to ALAW attendees.

Attendees will also have the opportunity to network during the receptions and visit the following tabletop exhibitors (confirmed as of 3/10/15) during the refreshment breaks: Abicor-Binzel, Fraunhofer USA, Blackbird Robotics, Dinse Inc., Genesis Systems Group, IPG Midwest Operations, KUKA Systems USA, Laserline Inc., Laser Mechanisms Inc., Ophir Laser Measurement Group, Plasmo USA, Precitec Inc., and TRUMPF USA.
