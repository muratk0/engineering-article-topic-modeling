# Fabricator’s agile manufacturing model expedites production process for OEM

[TARİH: 01.04.2026 The Fabricator]

Applications

SITUATION

Lead times are critical to overall performance for fabricating shops. Manufacturers are facing increasing pressure from global competition to hit shorter lead times. It’s a major competitive advantage to lessen the overall cost of production and increase customer satisfaction.

A manufacturer of power generation products was experiencing a rapid increase in orders with demand outpacing production. In addition, significant power grid outages had spurred legislation that required backup power for telecommunication towers.

This OEM needed to produce base frame weldments while facing the challenges of time-driven deadlines, constraints in both material and labor, and a lengthy initial sample order lead time. The manufacturer began looking for a supplier that could quote, build samples, and accelerate shipment for multiple product versions simultaneously.

RESOLUTION

The OEM contacted Creative Metal Products to help generate a production plan for designing and manufacturing base frame weldments with an extremely high-volume demand and a dynamic and fluctuating timeline.

Understanding the complexities and the ambitious nature of the project, the CMP team collaborated closely with the OEM to construct a comprehensive production plan using CMP’s robotic welding cell with custom-designed and -built fixturing. The goal of this integration was to provide maximum output and reduce previously extended lead times.

The plan included:

Refining the OEM’s existing design by adding locating tabs and slots to aid assembly.

Formulating fixturing for maximum productivity within the robotic welding cell.

Enhancing the production schedule to substantially increase the weekly quantity shipped to meet the customer’s continuously increasing demand.

The CMP team checked in with the OEM weekly to discuss consistent demand changes for parts and to shift production requirements accordingly. In addition, raw material sourcing necessitated ongoing creative utilization of both CMP’s supply partners and the customer’s existing contracts.

With all hands on deck, CMP was able to complete the sample orders on multiple parts in less than four weeks, and production parts began shipping less than two weeks later.

The OEM was able to open up internal capacity by outsourcing to CMP, thereby mitigating the backlog. The manufacturer also realized a 30% reduction in the time interval for the first order sample production lead time and a 50% increase in parts shipped.
