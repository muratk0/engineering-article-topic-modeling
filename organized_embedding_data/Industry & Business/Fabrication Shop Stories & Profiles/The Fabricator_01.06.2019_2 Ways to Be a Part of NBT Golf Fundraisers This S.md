See How You Benchmark Against Your Peers

For 24 years, FMA has collected anonymous salary/wage and benefit data for the metal fabricating, processing, and forming industry. The survey results offer detailed looks at production and administrative positions including both hourly and salaried workers. Results are viewable by demographic criteria including: number of employees, region, population, and sales by volume — giving you the ability to match up to company data that is most similar to your operation.

Each year, the survey is open electronically from mid-May until the end of July, with companies from all over the U.S. and Canada participating. The compiled results will be available in the fall.

Survey participation is open until July 31.
