[TARİH: 01.09.2019 The Fabricator]

One question that has become a refrain across the manufacturing industry demonstrates the difficulties manufacturers are experiencing when trying to fill jobs with skilled workers: Why can’t we find the skilled workers we need?

Growing production demand has caused manufacturers to create new positions and new titles, only to see these jobs sit unfilled for extended periods because none of the applicants they receive hold enough of the core skills of the job.
