As we safely come together to celebrate the holidays with family and friends, we would like to express our sincere gratitude to the generous donors and supporters who have enabled Nuts, Bolts & Thingamagigs

®

(NBT) to make a positive impact, especially in 2020.

Manufacturing has become the backbone of the U.S. economy, providing countless careers and leading in technological advancements. However, the lack of a skilled workforce in the coming decade threatens the industry’s bright future.

Now, more than ever, the industry relies on the next generation of skilled workers, trained and ready for tomorrow’s advanced manufacturing jobs. NBT provides grant funding to schools and organizations to host summer manufacturing camps that expose students ages 12-16 to manufacturing career options in their communities. NBT also awards scholarships to high school graduates seeking manufacturing-related skills training and study programs.

We could not do it without your help! NBT is truly grateful for the impact your generosity has made. Make a gift today to help NBT continue to provide more scholarships and camp grants next year and beyond.
