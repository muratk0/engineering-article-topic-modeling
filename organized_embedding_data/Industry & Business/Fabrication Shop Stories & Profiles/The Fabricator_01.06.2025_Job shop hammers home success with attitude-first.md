# Job shop hammers home success with attitude-first hiring

[TARİH: 01.06.2025 The Fabricator]

Applications

Situation

Hammer MetalWerks, a full-service, custom metal fabrication and powder coating shop in Indianapolis, provides small to medium production runs and parts for a variety of industries, from construction to health care, entertainment, military, and government.

In response to skilled labor shortages, the shop decided to take a new approach to hiring staff, preferencing work ethic and attitude over existing skills. Management has found that they can train and upskill an employee with a good attitude, which benefits them and the business.

"Our laser operator and brake press operator had both never used those machines in their life before working here," said President Derek Hammer. "But they were intelligent, familiar with computer systems, and had a positive attitude to learning, so we hired them based on that.

"Rather than looking for highly skilled—and more expensive—operators, which are in short supply, we looked for technology that could make the operator’s job easier and help them get up to speed faster."

Resolution

One technology the job shop chose to help its new staff and overall efficiency was ToolBox by Tempus Tools. The multifunctional laser cutting quoting software is calculation-based and runs on a Software as a Service (SaaS) model. On the shop’s 3-kW fiber laser, the software can be set up with information on material price, cutting time, labor, and other relevant information to generate quotes quickly and accurately.

"ToolBox has been highly valuable for us. It provides work orders to the shop floor with lots of details that the operators don’t have to find or calculate themselves. One example for us is that sheet sizes are all metric, and we use imperial gauges in the U.S., which can often lead to errors if not converted correctly," explained Hammer.

"Moreover, 11-ga. aluminum is not the same thickness as 11-ga. steel, and our laser machines are all metric, so having all the right measurements through [the software] reduces the risk of any errors."

"We’ve designed ToolBox to provide benefits to job shops of all shapes and sizes," explained Tempus Tools CEO Bart Jesman. "What Derek has done is take this a step further and used it to improve his hiring process by making the operator’s job easier. It’s an intelligent approach."

In addition to metal fabrication and laser cutting, Hammer MetalWerks offers additional services and secondary processes including design, bending, welding, powder coating, packaging, tapping, rivets, and hardware.

"With so many secondary processes, it’s so helpful that we can quote them all using [the software], and they can all be included in the work order that goes out to the shop floor," Hammer said.

The work order also can be sent to a label printer to produce a label that includes a thumbnail of each item. The label then provides a visual cue to shop floor staff to further enhance their accuracy and efficiency and prevent mix-ups.

"With the skilled labor shortages in the U.S., and common issues around the world, having the right technology is crucial," Hammer continued. "We now hire employees that fit well with our work culture and have a good attitude – and it’s working out well for our business."
