Be Among the Leading Metal Manufacturers at FABTECH in Mexico City

FABTECH

®

Mexico, Latin America’s largest collaboration of technology, equipment, and knowledge in the metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing industries, will showcase the latest innovations at Mexico City’s Centro Citibanamex, May 2-4, 2018.

The event will feature 575 exhibiting companies; cover 140,000 net square feet of floor; and offer an anticipated 13,000 attendees from Mexico and Central America unlimited opportunities to make side-by-side product comparisons, discover innovations, and connect with industry experts.

With a highly developed industrial base and strong growth projected in the near- and long-term, Mexico continues to be a strategic manufacturing market. Don’t miss this exclusive opportunity to meet face-to-face with high-level decision makers who buy the products and services your company delivers.

FABTECH Mexico is made possible by its five co-sponsors, all of which represent the varied and diverse makeup of the manufacturing industry. They are the American Welding Society (AWS), Fabricators & Manufacturers Association International (FMA), SME, Precision Metalforming Association (PMA), and Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI).

To find out how your company can be part of this exciting industry event or to register to attend, visit

mexico.fabtechexpo.com

Panel: Perspectives on the Steel Industry

Industry experts will provide insight into the current state of the steel industry and where it is going in the future.

Ladd Hall

Executive vice president flat-rolled products Nucor Corp.

Ladd Hall has been with Nucor Corp. for 34 years. He started his career at Nucor Steel Utah in 1981. From there he held a variety of positions and went on to become vice president and general manager at Nucor Steel Berkeley until being appointed to his current position of executive vice president of flat-rolled products.

Donalad R. McNeely, PhD

President/CEO Chicago Tube & Iron

Don McNeeley is the president and CEO of Chicago Tube & Iron Co., a steel distribution and fabrication company founded in 1914 with headquarters in Chicago and 10 subsidiaries. In 2011 McNeeley was the architect of a merger of his company with Olympic Steel Inc. The combined forces of $1.5 billion in sales and 30 locations position the company as one of the top 10 in the nation.

Johan Packard

participant/CEO Steel Market Update

John Packard has been an active participant in the North American flat-rolled steel business since 1977. He has held positions in the service center, steel mill, and trading company segments of the flat-rolled steel industry. Packard founded Steel Market Update Inc. in August 2008, although the newsletter has been produced since 2004 when it was used to convey market information to his customers.
