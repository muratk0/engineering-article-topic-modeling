2018 Safety Awards—Now Accepting Applications

An FMA/CNA Annual Safety Award

is a great way to recognize your company’s improved safety record or to acknowledge continuing success in that area. Several levels of recognition — awards of honor, merit, and honorable mention — are presented each year. All companies that qualify receive an award.

2017 Safety Award Winner Standard Iron

2017 Safety Award Winner Cerro Flow

It’s easy to apply. You must be a company-level member of FMA, complete OSHA Form 300A (Summary of Workrelated Injuries and Illnesses), and send it to Nancy Hatten at

nancyh@fmanet.org

by Feb. 28, 2018. Not a member?

Call customer service at 888-394-4362 or visit

fmanet.org/join

to learn more about membership levels and benefits.

The Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence

This award is the highest honor in FMA’s Safety Award program, presented to one company each year that demonstrates it has an established safety culture in its workplace. It is named in honor of Rusty Demeules, a longtime FMA volunteer and the first chair of the FMA Safety Council. An application and essay must be submitted by Feb. 28, 2018, to be considered for this award.

All safety awards will be presented at FMA’s Annual Safety Conference April 18-19, 2018, in Elgin, Ill. The conference will feature a tour of UL. Additional information about the event is available at

fmanet.org/calendar

.

NEW Technical Seminars in Mexico

Improve your stamping, fabrication, and coil processing operations in Mexico at the Mexico Technology Conference on Feb. 27 - March 1 at the Safi Valle Hotel in Monterrey, N.L.

Attendees will gain the technical knowledge, best practices, and technology advancements they need to efficiently process, fabricate, and stamp parts. This comprehensive conference will cover an array of topics such as stamping tool and die design; press operations; press brake technology and tool selection; and coil processing and cut-to-length lines. Attendees can choose to attend one, two, or all three days.

Conference fees: US$125 per person daily rate. Group discounts are available. Sessions will be conducted in Spanish or with simultaneous translation service.

New FABTECH Logo Reflects an Industry Evolution

The five FABTECH

®

co-sponsors unveiled a new logo and brand identity for the FABTECH series of events at the 2017 show in Chicago. The industry is evolving, and the new logo reflects the innovation and emerging manufacturing technologies showcased at FABTECH. With its more modern look and feel, the new logo design better conveys the current and future direction of the FABTECH brand.

"The manufacturing industry is entering a new age and experiencing an industrial revolution that is changing the way we manufacture,"

said John Catalano, FABTECH show co-manager and SME senior director.

"And in the midst of this, FABTECH is also evolving to stay at the leading edge of the industry. We are excited to launch a new logo that demonstrates the evolution of the FABTECH brand."

"While we usher in a new visual identity, FABTECH remains committed to delivering a high-quality, innovative event experience,"

added Mark Hoper, FABTECH show co-manager and FMA senior vice president of media and expositions.

"The show will continue to meet the demands of the industry and showcase not only what’s new, but what’s next."

FABTECH represents the leading events in North America exclusively focused on advancing the metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing industries. The new logo mark and simple letter structure offer a more relevant, forward-thinking image while still honoring and preserving FABTECH’s rich history.

The new logo is the first piece of a strategic branding initiative to bring a consistent, cohesive, and contemporary look and feel to all FABTECH events.
