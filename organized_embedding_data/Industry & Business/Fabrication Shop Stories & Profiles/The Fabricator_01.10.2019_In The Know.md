Mark Your Calendar for FMA’s Annual Meeting

FMA’s Annual Meeting is headed to San Antonio! This is the industry-exclusive networking and educational event for metal manufacturing professionals that you won’t want to miss.

Each year the event attracts hundreds of metal fabricators, processors, and distributors for three days of invaluable business presentations and discussions, expert-led keynotes, exclusive plant tours, fun outings like golf, and other excellent networking functions.

The venue is the Hyatt Regency Hill Country Resort — a rustic-style retreat nestled in the scenic Texas Hill Country. Guests will have a memorable experience of genuine Texas hospitality combined with the comfort and charm of a country ranch house. The resort is conveniently located 20 minutes from downtown San Antonio.

FMA’s discounted group room block is $255/night through January 31, 2020. Call 877-803-7534 and refer to "FMA 2020."
