# NBT Summer Manufacturing Camps Announced for 2014

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 The Fabricator]

It may still feel like winter in much of the U.S., but 19 colleges are preparing for and recruiting students to participate in summer manufacturing camps they will produce under grants from Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs®, the Foundation of the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association (FMA).

Some of the camps are general in their manufacturing focus, others are very specialized with regard to a particular technology or sector of the industry, but they all have the same endgame: to get young people interested in the career options that manufacturing offers and to inspire them to complete coursework in the subjects that will matter most when they are ready for the work world. Most camps focus on students in the 12-14 age range, but some accept students through age 16.

“These camps have a profound impact on youth and how they view manufacturing,” said NBT Board Chairman Bryan Hawkins, president and CEO, Hawkeye Industries. “For many of the participants it is the first time they have ever made anything, and there is a great sense of pride in that accomplishment. I never tire of seeing the gleam of wonder that kids get in their eyes when they do something they’ve never done before — like operate a CNC press brake or cut metal with a million-dollar laser. For others it is the first time they’ve ever really imagined themselves in a career like manufacturing. And I certainly never tire of reading notes from parents that express how the camp experience has forever changed their child’s outlook on life.”
