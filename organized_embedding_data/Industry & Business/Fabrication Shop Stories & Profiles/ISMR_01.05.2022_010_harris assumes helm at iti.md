# Harris assumes helm at ITI

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Michael Harris, a long-time staffer at the International Training Institute (ITI), has taken the reins as its administrator. The ITI's mission is to develop, maintain and distribute training resources for union workers in the sheet metal; heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC); industrial/welding; architectural and building service industry throughout the United States and Canada.

A second-generation sheet metal worker, Harris got his first taste of the industry at age 16 when he spent the summer working in a sheet metal shop. Initially opting for college, Harris later changed course and worked in a custom fabrication shop alongside his father before joining the International Association of Sheet Metal, Air, Rail and Transportation (SMART) workers Local 20's apprenticeship programme in 1991. During the fourth year of his apprenticeship, Harris began teaching part time at the Local 20 training centre and, in 1997, began teaching full time. By 2000, Harris took over as director of training for the statewide training trust.

In 2005, Harris joined the ITI staff as a welding assessor and, in 2009, he took on the role of programme administrator, overseeing programming and instructor training and managing field staff. After the retirement of James Page in 2020, Harris assumed many of the responsibilities of ITI administrator in an acting capacity, assisting funds administrator Daniel McCallum in running the day-to-day operations of the organisation. Harris is now ITI administrator.

Harris hopes to continue to build on ITI's success, adding new instructional offerings and developing programmes for the sheet metal workers of tomorrow.
