(NBT), the foundation of FMA, is proud to be supporting 175 manufacturing camps held at schools throughout the U.S. and Canada this summer! It’s at these camps where students are inspired to be the next-generation skilled workforce. Many children discover their passion for hands-on skills through computer-aided welding, robotics, 3D printing, and other manufacturing-related projects.

Since inception, NBT has awarded nearly $1.4 million in camp grants to support the future of manufacturing. And we couldn’t do it without your generous donations. We are grateful for every single dollar, as it is an investment in the next generation of the manufacturing workforce.
