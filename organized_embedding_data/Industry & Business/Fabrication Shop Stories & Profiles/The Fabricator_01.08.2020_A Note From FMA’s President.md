# A Note From FMA’s President

[TARİH: 01.08.2020 The Fabricator]

In The Know

News and information from the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International®

Edward Youdell

President and CEO Fabricators & Manufacturers Association

2020 has turned out to be a year with staggering challenges that no one expected. Here at the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International’ (FMA), we remain focused on helping metal fabrication professionals advance their careers and the industry to continue delivering on our mission: to advocate for the growth and sustainability of the North American metal processing, forming, and fabricating industries.

As we navigate this difficult time, the top priority of our dedicated staff and event partners is to provide safe and secure experiences to everyone who attends any future FMA in-person event by following national and local health guidelines.

We are confident in our ability to ensure safe experiences now and we’re ready to resume business, better than ever, in the near future. I leave you with a Winston Churchill quote: "It’s not enough that we do our best; sometimes we have to do what’s required."

Thank You,

Edward Youdell
