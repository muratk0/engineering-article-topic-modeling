As we kick off the 50th anniversary of the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

®

(FMA), I would like to take a moment to look back at where it all began and where we are today. Fifty years ago FMA was founded by Milo J. Pitkin, a businessman and entrepreneur who pioneered technologies in welding automation, meat processing equipment, and ergonomic office equipment.

In 1970 Milo wanted to create an association that truly served metal fabricators. After several months of relentlessly reaching out to industry leaders, he formed FMA with industry colleagues. Since then the association has both helped over 50,000 metal fabrication professionals advance their careers and grow the market into the $153 billion industry

1

it is today.

For half a century FMA’s mission has remained the same: to advocate for the growth and sustainability of the North American metal processing, forming, and fabricating industries.

I look forward to celebrating FMA’s 50th anniversary with you throughout the year.

Sincerely,

Ed Youdell

FMA’s 50th Anniversary Festivities Are Underway

The FMA 50th anniversary is a true cause for celebration! To start things off, the big milestone is being honored with signage at FMA’s corporate headquarters in Elgin, Illinois.

Throughout 2020 FMA will recognize the association’s dedication to 50 years of helping metal manufacturers thrive and move the industry forward.
