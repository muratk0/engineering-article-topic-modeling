Leadership DISCovery™ for AMPlifying Success

William Brashers, PhD Bartell & Bartell

There is one constant in every conversation you have: You’re in it! Understanding yourself, your motivations, and your "hot buttons" is the most effective way to improve your interpersonal communication. On April 28, 2015, FMA will host a one-day seminar in Palatine (Chicago), Ill., to help you uncover the strengths you can leverage as a leader.

Facilitated by William "Bill" Brashers, PhD, of Bartell & Bartell, Leadership DISCovery™ is a fascinating exploration into what makes you, your employees, and even your family members tick! Anyone interested in better understanding who they are and communicating more effectively with others is encouraged to attend. Prior to the workshop you will be asked to complete online assessments, tools that will provide you with a personalized, data-driven, and powerful learning opportunity to help you retain top talent and ensure organizational health.

WORLD-CLASS ROLL FORMING

Workshop 2015

The most roll forming experts during one training workshop.

Is Heading to Chicago

This year’s World-class Roll Forming Workshop is blowing into the Windy City April 21-22, 2015, with updated, enhanced, revised, and new sessions to give you an edge on your roll forming processes. Plus, for the first time, a motivating and dynamic keynote address will be presented featuring Ray Leathers, president of Roll Forming Corporation.

Packed with 13 session options, World-class Roll Forming is designed to provide small-group workshops that allow meaningful interaction with roll forming experts and other attendees. During the two-day program, in addition to the new keynote address, you can attend nine sessions that best fit your objectives and enjoy a networking reception to continue earlier conversations, interact with colleagues, and experience a rousing round of Roll Forming Jeopardy!

Plus, Samson Roll Formed Products Company will open its facilities for an exclusive tour for workshop attendees. World-class Roll Forming features the most roll forming experts in one training workshop, and they are ready to answer your questions; look at your parts, blueprints, and drawings; and solve your roll forming challenges.
