Epic Piping acquires BendTec

Epic Piping, a turnkey industrial pipe manufacturing firm in Baton Rouge, La., has acquired Minnesota-based pipe fabricator BendTec Fabrication & Bending. The investment marks Epic’s entrance into union label projects and expands its capabilities in the power, renewable, and infrastructure sectors.

The transaction also expands Epic’s production capabilities with the addition of BendTec’s existing operations and plants, including the capability to produce pipe bends up to 66 in. dia.

Skyline Fabricating to add 22 jobs in Virginia

Skyline Fabricating Inc., a metal fabrication company specializing in structural steel construction, will invest more than $675,000 to expand its operations. The company will construct a 25,000-sq.-ft. facility in Southern Gap, Va., to meet increased demand for its steel and aluminum fabricated products and expanded customer base outside Virginia. Skyline Fabricating specializes in the construction of bridges, walkways, and handrails, as well as aluminum fabrication, mobile welding, and plasma cutting.

Harris acquires FTP, Shoals Tubular

Harris Products Group, a manufacturer of cutting, welding, brazing, and soldering equipment and a Lincoln Electric company, has strengthened its presence in the HVAC/R manufacturing market with the acquisition of two manufacturers: Fabricated Tube Products (FTP) and Shoals Tubular Inc.

The two companies manufacture copper and aluminum headers, distributor assemblies, and other complex brazed assemblies in the U.S. and Mexico for the HVAC/R sector.

"We now have a good footprint with facilities in Tennessee and Alabama in the U.S., as well as a plant in Monterrey, Mexico, so we can be very close to our customers," said Kyle Smith, director of global OEM for Harris Products Group.

Unison installs Breeze pipe benders at Stauff

(From left) Steve Chambers of Unison Ltd. with Stauff’s Ken Cleal

Stauff, a manufacturer and supplier of precision fluid power products, has purchased two new Unison Breeze 35-mm (maximum pipe diameter) left- and right-hand pipe bending machines. One machine has been installed at Stauff’s manufacturing site in Sheffield, U.K., the other at its production facility in Sydney, New South Wales.

The machines are used to manufacture small-run, complex components, such as hydraulic lines for braking systems. They bend aluminum, corrosion-resistant steel, and exotic alloys in both clockwise and counterclockwise directions. The benders are equipped with Opt2Sim, Unison’s CAD tube bending simulation software.

Tampa Tank-Florida Structure Steel announces expansion

Tampa Tank-Florida Structure Steel, a Precision Build Solutions LLC company, has announced plans for a $3 million expansion of its Gibsonton, Fla., location. By the end of 2022 it expects to add 60 welders, fitters, machinists, and equipment operators to its workforce.

The company provides design, shop fabrication, shipment, field erection, inspection, and testing of welded steel products.

Hooper opens new Wisconsin headquarters

Hooper has relocated from Madison, Wis., to a new campus on 50 acres in DeForest, Wis. As part of the move, the company has combined Hooper Corp. and its wholly owned subsidiary General Heating and Air Conditioning into one company under the name Hooper.

About 200 employees that previously worked in separate offices and various warehouses have joined together on the new campus, under construction since October 2020. The campus includes a 68,860-sq.-ft. office building and 207,226-sq.-ft. fabrication facility.

The company offers fabrication and installation of complex plumbing, process piping, fire protection, and HVAC systems, as well as construction of substations and high-voltage transmission and distribution lines for utilities.

MEC expands Arkansas facility, adds 70 jobs

Mayville Eng. Co. Inc. has expanded its capacity in Heber Springs, Ark. As part of the expansion, the company will continue to invest in equipment and process improvements and will add about 70 new jobs over a four-year period.

MEC has operated its 190,000-sq.-ft. facility in Heber Springs since acquiring the former Defiance Metal Products Co. in 2018. Current employment at the facility is approximately 200.

A provider of prototyping and tooling, production fabrication, coating, assembly, and aftermarket components, the company has 20 facilities in seven states.

Camfil breaks ground for new manufacturing facility

Camfil Air Pollution Control, a manufacturer of industrial dust-, fume-, and mist-collection systems for industrial processes, has broken ground for its new manufacturing and office facility in Jonesboro, Ark. Camfil plans to invest more than $37 million in the facility, which will add about 67 new jobs within five years to an existing workforce of 237 people.

The company has been headquartered in Jonesboro for more than 20 years. In early 2020 Camfil was already planning to develop a new manufacturing site when a tornado destroyed its facility. Since then it has been operating out of three separate leased locations throughout the city.

The new, 290,000-sq.-ft. facility will occupy 34 acres and is scheduled to open in fall 2022. In addition to space for manufacturing industrial dust collectors and filter cartridges, the facility will also house sales offices, warehousing, and shipping.

Fox Valley Metal-Tech expanding in Wisconsin

Fox Valley Metal-Tech (FVMT) has announced it is expanding its Green Bay, Wis.-based facility to accommodate growth and increase its precision metal fabrication, assembly, and finishing capabilities for its military and commercial customers.

The 74,800-sq.-ft. addition is expected to be completed in April 2022, bringing the total facility footprint to more than 175,000 sq. ft. FVMT currently leases two locations for additional warehouse space and its painting and finishing services. Those operations will be moved to the expanded facility.

Team Industries recognized as Bechtel top supplier

Team Industries Inc. has received the Bechtel 2020/2021 Supply Chain Excellence Award in recognition of its exceptional work on Bechtel projects. Companies were evaluated on multiple factors including overall performance; ability to deliver quality services/supplies on time; ability to work collaboratively to meet milestones; and meeting or exceeding project expectations for safety, performance, technical expertise, and environmental compliance.

The company’s past awards received from Bechtel are the 2011 Supplier of the Year, the 2012 Key Supplier, the 2013 Supplier of the Year, and the 2018/2019 Supply Chain Excellence.

With locations in Kaukauna, Wis., and Port Arthur, Texas, Team Industries is a provider of shop-fabricated pipe spools and pressure vessels, offering welding and fabrication, project management, engineering, quality control, and surface preparation and coating capabilities.

K-TIG expands North American distribution arm

Australia-based welding manufacturer K-TIG has expanded its reach across North America with the appointment of MITUSA Inc., Maywood, Calif., as a new Tier 1 distributor to supply its products in the U.S. and Mexico.

MITUSA’s appointment aligns with KTIG’s strategy to further develop thirdparty sales channels to complement its direct sales force.

K-TIG Managing Director Adrian Smith said specialist automation integrators were essential in incorporating the company’s technology into a turnkey welding automation system across industry.

"MITUSA is a highly regarded welding automation company specializing in automated seamers, offering both technical and logistical expertise for customers in North America," Smith said. "We are confident in MITUSA’s management team, led by its president, Leo Valerio."

Valerio began his career in 1970 at Eaton Leonard Robolix, integrating electronic equipment. He gained additional expertise at Valco Precision Works before founding MITUSA in 2017.

"A K-TIG distributor agreement primarily enables us to deliver productivity gains to our customers in a unique way with leading-edge technology," Valerio said.

McDougall Processing adds second TruLaser Tube system

McDougall Processing has installed a second TRUMPF TruLaser Tube 7000 fiber laser system in its Nashville, Tenn., metal processing facility.

The 6-kW laser handles tubing up to 10 in. dia. and ½ in. thick.

Georgia Trade School students capture first place at the Xcel North Georgia Welding Competition

Georgia Trade School students Jordan Adcox, Cade Cordle, Kelly Myers, and Skylar Voeltner won first place in the Xcel North Georgia Welding Competition. They custom-fabricated a smoker/cowboy cooker to resemble the classic Lincoln Electric tombstone welder, using recycled materials from paper mills, railroad spikes, and other miscellaneous metal. Cade Cordle also placed first in two individual welding competitions.

Certified Welding Inspectors Stephen Leone and Kyle Lockwood prepared and coached the team.

Super Steel expands in Wisconsin

Super Steel LLC has announced its move into a new, 29-acre, 220,000-sq.- ft. manufacturing campus in Mequon, Wis. The move is expected to be completed by year-end and includes the addition of employees, manufacturing capabilities, and services.

In addition to the company’s existing full-service metal fabricating and engineering platform, it has added in-house, large-format/high-tonnage equipment, including a 12- by 30-ft. batch powder painting system, a 60-ft. wet paint booth, a 60-ft. blasting booth, a 1,000-ton by 22-ft. press brake, a 72-by 108-in.-travel boring bar, and a 12-by 47-ft. plasma/oxyfuel burning table. The new facility also has 30-ton crane capability throughout the manufacturing area.

Diversified Ulbrich of Canada achieves AS 9120 B certification

Diversified Ulbrich of Canada has been awarded AS 9120 B certification for sales and distribution of highquality metallic alloys and nonmetallic materials for the aerospace and nonaerospace industries.

The metals service center offers stainless steel, aluminum, and specialty metals in sheet, plate, bar, strip, tubing, and structural angle.

KKR to acquire Bettcher Industries

Global investment firm KKR has signed a definitive agreement to acquire Bettcher Industries, a manufacturer and supplier of food processing equipment and associated aftermarket parts and consumables, from MPE Partners.

Based in Birmingham, Ohio, Bettcher is a provider of semiautomated, hand-held tools and automated equipment for beef, pork, and poultry processing plants.
