# FMA’s president calls on the industry to join the Build Now movement

[TARİH: 01.04.2021 The Fabricator]

Dear Industry Professionals,

With the American Society of Civil Engineers (ASCE) report card giving the U.S.’s infrastructure a C- grade, now is the time to bring our infrastructure up to the standards its people deserve.

Not only is public safety a top concern due to our decaying infrastructure, improving the substandard conditions of it will both provide countless jobs for the metal fabrication industry and demonstrate how crucial fabricated metal is to create safe and sound infrastructure systems.

Our industry has a skilled workforce prepared and ready to make the necessary and long-lasting improvements to America’s failing infrastructure. Join me in supporting the Metals Service Center Institute (MSCI) Build Now movement to help create momentum and awareness for sustained investments in America’s infrastructure.

Learn more and join the Build Now movement at

build-now.org

Thank you,

Edward Youdell

President and CEO
