# NBT’s 2021 Summer Manufacturing Camp Program Is Kicking Off

[TARİH: 01.05.2021 The Fabricator]

Advancing Metal Fabrication Together™

NBT is poised and ready to safely kick off the 2021 summer manufacturing camp season beginning in May. The camps will introduce middle and high school-aged youth to career paths in manufacturing as well as practical applications of math, science, and engineering principles. Students will tour local manufacturing companies, work directly with technology, and design and manufacture a product they can take home.

NBT awards grant funding to community colleges, technical schools, and high schools to host youth summer camps focused on manufacturing. 137 programs across the U.S. will receive support in 2021.

The summer camp program continues to help individuals discover their passion for manufacturing. The camps are instrumental in building the foundation for a qualified skilled workforce and ensuring the future of American manufacturing.
