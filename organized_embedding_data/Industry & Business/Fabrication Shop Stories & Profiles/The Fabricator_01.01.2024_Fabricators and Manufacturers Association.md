Dear Fellow Metals Industry Professionals,

The Association of Steel Distributors (ASD) was founded in 1943 and became a part of FMA in 2018. The ASD affiliation brings a combination of benefits and unique perspectives to both fabricators and steel suppliers — providing a platform to exchange unrealized ideas and market information to help advance your career and business.

We have recognized the need to become more inclusive of the entire metals industry and not just steel distributors. As the industry has changed over the year’s we understand we cannot keep doing the same things we have always done.

Our new name, the Association of Metals Processors and Distributors was established earlier this year. We are committed to Education, Training, Professional Networking and Fundraising. Through these actions we know we can make a positive impact on the industry and the individuals and companies who are involved with us. We work with Mills, Distributors, Processors and Service Centers to provide content and events that are valuable to each of you.

The AMPD is proud to bring you this directory once again this year. We hope that you will find it useful and informative.
