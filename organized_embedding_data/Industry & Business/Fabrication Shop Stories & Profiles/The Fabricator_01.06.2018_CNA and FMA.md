The following highlighted coverages are part of CNA’s comprehensive suite of insurance solutions designed to meet the needs of today’s manufacturers.

Equipment Breakdown

Property insurance coverage has its limits. Many policies don’t cover the risk of mechanical breakdown, electrical arcing, power surges, short circuits or deformation/cracking of pressure containing items. CNA Equipment Breakdown not only covers these risks, but also offers coverage for lost income, equipment rental and additional expenses associated with getting back up and running.

CNA Equipment Breakdown goes above and beyond service contracts and warranties offering coverage for:

Production Machinery

Electrical Systems

Elevators, Security Systems and Computer-controlled Equipment

Heating and Cooling Systems

Steam Systems

And more

Cyber Liability for Advanced Manufacturing

As manufacturers increase their reliance on technology, they face a greater risk of viruses and security breaches. Cyber Liability coverage from CNA offers coverage for both online and offline data in addition to a wide range of risks such as:

Privacy rights violations

Theft of trade secrets

Third party network damage caused by you

Defamation and copyright infringement

Payments of credible extortionist’s demands

Theft through your network

Cost to restore pre-loss conditions

And more
