# Scrap and remnant re-use

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 ISMR]

WITH THE ONGOING instability in material costs, maximizing scrap and remnant use is a rising priority for fabricators. SigmaTEK Systems, a developer of fabrication workflow software, such as SigmaNEST, SigmaMRP, SigmaTUBE and others, has long focused on increasing profitability through greater material utilisation and increased scrap and remnant use. Whether metal, composite, wood or other material, FMA's annual survey reports an average reuse rate on scrap/remnants as 1.5% of sales.

"With tools such as SigmaNEST and SigmaMRP, fabricators can potentially double that, with savings going directly to the bottom line. Our robust, scaleable, modular tools for process, material and throughput optimisation allow customers to align production goals with cash flow and profitability objectives, remnant and scrap use being critical," said SigmaMRP's Valter Bonelli. "We partner with them to configure what they need to grow, then grow with them."

SigmaTEK Systems' modular implementation means setting a feasible, profitable vision achieved in scaleable steps toward the desired level of integration and automation. Customised configurations of modules (such as SigmaNEST, SigmaMRP, SigmaMXP, SimTRANS and others) on top of existing or emerging ERP systems are crucial, driving better performance with, said the company, no downtime.

In 1993, SigmaTEK Systems launched its SigmaNEST™, a comprehensive CAD/CAM software platform solution developed by mechanical engineers specifically for fabrication facilities worldwide. With a robust nesting engine, NC programming and integrated MRP and MES modules on one scalable platform, SigmaTEK technology and innovation have helped to bring the "Smart Factory" to life.
