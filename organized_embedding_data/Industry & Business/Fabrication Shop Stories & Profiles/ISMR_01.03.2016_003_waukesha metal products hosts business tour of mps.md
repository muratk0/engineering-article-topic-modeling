# Waukesha Metal Products hosts business tour of MPS students

[TARİH: 01.03.2016 ISMR]

WAUKESHA METAL PRODUCTS is participating in a programme launched by the Council of Small Business Executives (COSBE) aimed at giving nearly 1,800 MPS students the chance to get an inside look at the diverse businesses that help to grow the Milwaukee area economy.

COSBE, the small business arm of the Metropolitan Milwaukee Association of Commerce, is partnering with 50 small businesses representing a wide variety of industries to provide guided company tours where students will meet and interact with employees in a host of different jobs. On 8 February 2016, Waukesha Metal Products brought approximately 20 students from Golda Meir School to its facility.

“Providing career exploration opportunities, like the ‘Be the Spark’ business tours, helps establish a link between the students and our businesses,” said the company. “We need these students to fill the talent pipeline for the region’s economic vitality!”

“The ‘Be the Spark’ programme gives kids a chance to learn not only what a company makes and does, but also what it takes to get those jobs,” added Mary Steinbrecher, COSBE executive director. “COSBE is dedicated to helping our region ensure a strong and sustainable workforce. The key to the success of our businesses – and the entire region – is building a pipeline of future talent.”

From humble beginnings as a two-man tool and die shop, Waukesha® Metal Products has been a full-service manufacturer of parts and assemblies in the industry since 1971. With facilities in Grafton and Sussex, Wisconsin, and San Luis Potosí, Mexico, Waukesha Metal Products focuses on process excellence to provide metalformed products that are engineered for durability and strength.
