Commitment to Customers Drives Innovative Customization

It happens more than you think. Globally renowned fabrication machinery manufacturers refuse to take on tough assignments.

Either they can’t do it, don’t think it is worth the effort or there’s just not enough margin. On the other hand, Boschert USA and its portfolio of multi-generational family-owned fabricating machinery brands’ first consideration is how to meet their customer’s toughest demands.

Boschert USA represents three fabricating manufacturers which consistently push the envelope to bring new technology and creative customization to solving some of the industry’s most interesting challenges—Boschert Precision Machinery, Stierli-Bieger and Boschert Gizelis. Each of these examples show how they adapt to deliver strength, accuracy, flexibility and repeatability:

Customization with a Punch

A major petroleum industry supplier needed a combination laser and punch with 50 tons of punching force. The manufacturer needed to laser cut 5′ × 10′ sheets with a 4kW fiber laser and punch, tap and form bridges with the same machine. Some of the forms were 14mm deep and required not only the pressure, but with a vertically moveable die, a unique Boschert Precision Machinery feature. Others wouldn’t touch it, but Germany-based Boschert adapted its Combi-Laser Multi-Punch to meet each demand. The Combi-Laser turned out to be a knock-out.

Bending on the Flat Edge

A major turbine manufacturer needed to replace an aging machine that specialized in edge-forming copper flat rectangular and round hollow tubes in varying radii from 40″-60″—all with very tight tolerances. Tubes specs ranged from 1.6″ round to 1.5″ × 4″ rectangular bars. After several no-bids, including from the original machine’s manufacturer, they approached Boschert USA. Boschert USA tailored a Swiss-manufactured Stierli 820 CNC horizontal bender with very specialized feeding equipment and tooling to accurately form each of their parts. The Stierli is bending the hard way to this day.

Small but Mighty

A large Canadian fabrication shop required a significantly powerful 225-ton × 10′ 6-axis press brake. But it had very limited height capacity. The machine also needed to satisfy strict CSA electrical requirements. Boschert USA worked with its Greek manufacturer, Boschert-Gizelis to modify an existing press brake with not only the latest in tooling, electronics, safety and control technology, but that would handle the load and fit in the workspace. The Boschert Gizelis has taken on big jobs in small spaces.

Boschert USA and its top-quality fabricating machinery partners are delivering innovation, strategic design counsel, and service resources that continue to put customers first and meet the toughest challenges.
