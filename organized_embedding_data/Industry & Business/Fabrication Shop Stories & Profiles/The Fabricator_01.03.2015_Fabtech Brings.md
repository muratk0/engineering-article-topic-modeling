FABTECH

®

is the place where the industry’s most influential buyers come to see the latest and greatest in metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing. This year more than 40,000 attendees will visit FABTECH to seek out new technologies and products to make their business more productive and profitable. No other event offers a better face-to-face opportunity to showcase equipment and services to decision-makers from around the world.

The Fabricators & Manufacturers Association Intl. (FMA) encourages industry participation in the 2015 "Financial Ratios & Operational Benchmarking Survey." The results will provide comprehensive data for company owners and managers in the metal forming and fabricating industry.

2015 "Financial Ratios & Operational Benchmarking Survey" Now Open

The survey combines financial ratio details with additional operational data used to determine a company’s financial health. Survey results will allow comparisons of liquidity, profitability, asset and debt management, operational benchmarking, value proposition, and marketing effectiveness with other companies. Comparisons will be made based on such demographic similarities as company size, sales volume, plant size, and geographic region. A sample page from the 2014 report is available to review at

fmanet.org/surveys

.
