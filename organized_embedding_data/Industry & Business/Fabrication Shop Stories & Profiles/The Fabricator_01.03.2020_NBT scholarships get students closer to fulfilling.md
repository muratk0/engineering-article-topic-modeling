# NBT scholarships get students closer to fulfilling careers

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 The Fabricator]

Fourteen manufacturing students have been awarded scholarships for the spring 2020 semester from Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT). These bright-minded, career-focused individuals are taking the next steps to join the skilled labor workforce.

"At heart, I am a mechanical engineer, but I have many interests outside of traditional engineering. Additive manufacturing is one of the fastest-growing areas of mechanical and manufacturing engineering — the possibilities are almost limitless. I would love to take my interests in fabrication and materials science and combine them together! [Additive or 3D printing], in either a manufacturing or research standpoint, would excite me every day as there is so much that has yet to be discovered."

— Nathanael C., Mechanical Engineering, The University of Texas at Austin

Since 1990 NBT has awarded more than $1 million in scholarships to over 500 students.

Fall 2020 applications are being accepted through March 31

.
