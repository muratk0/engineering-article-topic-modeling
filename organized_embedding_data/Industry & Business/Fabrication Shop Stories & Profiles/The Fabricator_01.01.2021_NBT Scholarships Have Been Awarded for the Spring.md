# NBT Scholarships Have Been Awarded for the Spring 2021 Semester

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 The Fabricator]

Advancing Metal Fabrication Together™

Twenty-five students pursuing manufacturing careers received scholarships for the spring 2021 semester from Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT), the foundation of FMA. The scholarships will help these deserving students reach their academic goals and learn the skills needed for lucrative and fulfilling jobs in the manufacturing industry.

"As a female in the STEM program, it is a great opportunity to be [on this career path and] a part of this association. As an African-born African American and first-generation college student on my maternal side of the family, it is a great honor to receive this scholarship. It has given me a sense of accomplishment and encouragement to continue working hard in my studies."

Since 1990, NBT has awarded nearly $1.1 million to 537 students who will or have already become the next generation of highly skilled manufacturing professionals. Applications for fall 2021 scholarships are being accepted through March 31.
