Take Away Big Ideas From Dynamic Sessions

These 2020 Annual Meeting keynote speakers will share key industry data and economic insights that will help you plan and meet your professional goals.

Jeffrey Rosensweig, Associate Professor of International Business and Finance, Emory University

Financial Trends and Business Strategies in a Global Economy

An international business and finance professor, Dr. Jeffrey Rosensweig focuses on global investing and specializes in financial, macroeconomic, and business forecasting. A frequent keynote speaker on topics related to global economic forecasts and financial trends, Jeff is known for his unique ability to combine original and useful business content with an engaging and dynamic speaking style.

Chris Kuehl, FMA Economic Analyst and Managing Partner, Armada Corporate Intelligence

Fabrinomics in 2020: A Clear Vision for Manufacturing

Dr. Kuehl shares relevant business and economic stories from around the world accompanied by commentary interpreting the impact on manufacturing in North America. He delivers the news that matters most to you, without the hype typically found in consumer media. For a distilled perspective on what’s happening in economies at home and around the globe, you won’t want to miss this presentation.
