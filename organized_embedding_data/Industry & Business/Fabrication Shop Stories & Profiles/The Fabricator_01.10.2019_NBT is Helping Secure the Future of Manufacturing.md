# NBT is Helping Secure the Future of Manufacturing — You Can Too

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 The Fabricator]

To make the largest impact on students to ensure a highly skilled future workforce, introducing them to manufacturing at the middle school or high school level is key — and Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs® (NBT) summer camps do just that.

You can help ensure the future of manufacturing too by teaming up with local schools that will introduce students to NBT’s summer manufacturing camps. NBT provides funding and support to community colleges and technical high schools that host these camps for students, ages 12-16.

Here’s how you can help introduce manufacturing to students in your area:

Contact the manufacturing program faculty at a local community college and/or technical high school and direct them to

nbtfoundation.org/host-a-camp

for information on becoming a camp host.

Offer tours of your company for NBT summer camps in your area and in October to celebrate Manufacturing Day on

mfgday.com

.

Donate to NBT’s summer camp program at

nbtfoundation.org/donate

or sponsor your local camp by donating materials or financial support.

NBT’s recent GOLF4MFG® was a great success; net proceeds reached a total of $45,000. GOLF4MFG has been instrumental in the success of NBT camp and scholarship programs. Save the date for GOLF4MFG South on April 27, 2020, at Ballantyne Country Club, Charlotte, N.C.
