# Husband and wife team at AliMet Fabrications

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

A new appointment to AliMet Fabrications (UK) sees its first husband and wife team at the company. Gemma Morris has taken up the role of sales co-ordinator, joining spouse Anthony who is general manager for pressed metals at the company.

Gemma's job involves status managing all live projects to ensure that orders are sent to production and dispatched within timeframes, alongside managing relationships with new and potential customers.

"Anthony is really passionate about his work with AliMet Fabrications so I have heard a lot about the company over the past couple of years. As a relatively young company, it has a lot of potential and that's what really attracted me to the role as well as working with a good group of people, including my husband," outlined Gemma Morris.

She added that she was really looking forward to “growing" her job, which is a new role within the company, and creating “a great customer service experience as well as helping to co-ordinate sales and operational activity."

Steve Marshall, Managing Director, AliMet Fabrications, welcomed Gemma to the team: "It is wonderful to have Gemma on board. She will no doubt become the 'glue' that the company needs right now as it continues to grow and develop key commercial partnerships," he said.

AliMet Fabrications manufactures architectural aluminium doors, windows, shopfronts and curtain walling. The company (based in Bridgnorth, Shropshire) was founded in 2020 and also specialises in pressed metal work including 4M press brake work. Among its solutions is the Series 100 profile system which can be used in the fabrication of windows, doors and screens for new or refurbished commercial and industrial buildings.
