# Leadership and Trust

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 The Fabricator]

By William “Bill” Brashers, Ph.D.

Hi and welcome back. Last time we explored what it might take to win a follower. Remember, no follower no leader. make me Your company can but your manager, it can’t make me your leader. When do I become your leader (if I ever do)? I become your leader if and when you choose to follow me. Not before, and not unless.

We know that followers follow leaders because leaders make it worthwhile to the follower in ways besides just dollars. Great, we’re there. Now for another crucial factor:

trust.

How many times have you heard employees use that word? If you haven’t heard it, you need to get out more (or you understand leadership so well you could teach it).

Of course, sometimes we all have to obey to get our pay, but ask yourself this question: Would I follow someone

from the heart

that I didn’t trust? Probably not. Nobody would. So the next question arises: Who do I trust? Some say it is those who have integrity. But how do you define integrity? For example, if I say I’m going to skin you alive and hang you from your heels, and then I do it, do you trust me? Didn’t think so. Wrong criterion.

shown We trust a pattern people of helpfulness who have to us, and we distrust those who haven’t.

True, no? In a world where every- one is looking for magic keys, here’s a great big one: Helpfulness wins followers; helpfulness grows followers; helpfulness retains followers; and helpfulness earns trust.
