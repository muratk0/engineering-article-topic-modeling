# US$ 4.6m in new projects for Morris Sheet Metal

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Alpine 4 Holdings, Inc., an operator/owner of small market businesses, confirms that its subsidiary, Morris Sheet Metal (MSM) of Indiana, USA, has been awarded several new projects totalling US$ 4.6 million in the educational and healthcare facilities space.

Tom Laubhan, President of Morris Sheet Metal, commented: "We are eager to begin these new projects as they serve to demonstrate how integral we are to the Indiana and Midwest region. I believe we were awarded these projects [partly because of how] we handled volatile market conditions in 2021. Unprecedented inflation pushed the construction services market into a negative margin position on many projects due to material costs on items like steel, which rose to over 200%.

"While it is not easy to absorb such material cost changes, we stood by our customers and honoured contracts, completing prior work on time and on budget. The integrity with which we conducted ourselves as a company in this difficult market has led to our customers rewarding this team with the quality projects that we proudly build for our community."

Morris Sheet Metal (MSM) was established in 1992 and is a full-service HVAC contractor offering design, fabrication and installation services in addition to custom metal fabrication services. MSM is an active member of SMACNA and the AFE Association for Facilities Engineers. Its workshop is approximately 50,000 sq. ft. and equipped with shearing and braking capacities of 1/4" x 10, with a high-definition, 6 x 20 ft. automated plasma machine that will cut up to 1" plate. Kent Wilson, Alpine 4's CEO, commented, "With the volatility of 2021, it's confidence-inspiring to see some rebalancing in construction, culminating in this newly awarded work. Morris Sheet Metal has garnered a stellar reputation in the area, and we anticipate the flow of more work as supply chain issues continue to ease."■
