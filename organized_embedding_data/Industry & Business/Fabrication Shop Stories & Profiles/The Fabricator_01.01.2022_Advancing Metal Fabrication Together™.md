(NBT) is pleased to announce scholarship winners for the spring 2022 semester. Twice each year, NBT awards scholarships up to $2,500 to deserving students on manufacturing career pathways.

The spring 2022 scholarship winners could be employed in rewarding manufacturing careers in as few as five months! By joining the skilled labor workforce, they will fill crucial positions open in manufacturing.

To date, NBT has awarded over $1.2 million in scholarships to help more than 600 manufacturing students follow their dreams.

Scholarship applications for the fall 2022 semester are being accepted through March 31, 2022. Spread the word to any students who are pursuing manufacturing-related degrees!
