FABTECH 2014 ATLANTA SHOW PREVIEW

By Amanda Carlson, Contributing Editor

Building or improving upon your business requires an investment in both equipment and employees. In today’s increasingly competitive industry, it is essential that your manufacturing environment has the right tools and your staff is up-to-date on knowledge currently available about their craft.

According to an economic report published in late August by the National Association of Manufacturers (NAM), “Manufacturing production increased 1.0 percent in July, helping to lift the year-over-year pace of manufacturing output to 4.9 percent, its fastest annual pace since June 2012. The gain is due largely to increased motor vehicle production, with all but three of the major manufacturing sectors notching higher output levels for the month. At the same time, the utilization rate for manufacturers increased to 77.8 percent, nearly reaching pre-recessionary capacity levels.”

Those positive industry statistics are reflected in this year’s FABTECH®, the premier forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing exhibition. The show is co-organized by the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association Intl. (FMA), SME, American Welding Society (AWS), Precision Metalforming Association (PMA), and Chemical Coaters Association Intl. (CCAI). It is a one-stop shop for metal fabricators to browse metalworking tools and technology; network with other professionals; and learn about technical and leadership concepts during educational conferences, panel discussions, and forums.

For the second time in its history, the show will be held in Atlanta. The show follows up a 2013 Chicago campaign that had a record number of attendees (40,667), exhibiting companies (1,573), and total square footage (632,256). While it is unlikely the Atlanta showing will equal those numbers, they have already well exceeded those of 2010, the last time FABTECH was held in Atlanta. As of late August, attendee pre-registrations were 80 percent ahead of where they were at the same time in 2010. Organizers expect upwards of 1,400 exhibitors and 27,000 attendees filling roughly 500,000 net sq. ft. of exhibition space.

Special Events

Keynote presentations, panel discussions, and special presentations are a hallmark of FABTECH. Different events are geared toward a varied target audience and cover current industry trends, hot-button issues, and leadership topics.

Keynote Presentation: Creating U.S. Jobs and Bringing Manufacturing Home

Tuesday, November 11

9:00 – 10:00 a.m.

Thomas Murphy Ballroom

Cindi Marsiglio, vice president, U.S. manufacturing at Walmart, will discuss the company’s commitment to spending $250 billion over the next 10 years on U.S.-made products. This is an effort that will create thousands of manufacturing jobs as well as new business for both existing and new Walmart suppliers. The company is currently working on engaging hundreds of suppliers and manufacturers from specific states in an effort to overcome the challenges involved in bringing manufacturing jobs back to the U.S. Marsiglio’s presentation will reflect on why this work is important, both in terms of rebuilding the middle class and providing customers with the best quality and prices possible.

Bridging the Manufacturing Skills Gap With Veterans

Tuesday, November 11

12:30-1:30 p.m.

Room C101

Thousands of veterans and National Guard members are exiting the military and looking for civilian careers at the same time that U.S. manufacturers are scrambling to find people to fill skilled positions. This panel will discuss the gap between available jobs and the highly skilled veterans who possess desirable leadership qualities seeking employment. Learn about the organizations and resources available to connect both sides, and how veterans could be the key to bridging the skills gap.

Moderator: Shane Hicks, VP global talent, Epicor

Panelists: Michael J. Aroney, MS, MBA, principal consultant, Allied Reliability Group; Joseph C. Barto III, founder and president, TMG Inc.; Steve Nowlan, president and CEO, Center for America; Hernan Luis y Prado, president, Workshops for Warriors

Keynote Presentation: Be the Best You Can Be

Wednesday, November 12

9:00 – 10:00 a.m.

Thomas Murphy Ballroom

Rocky Bleier, four-time Super Bowl champion and Vietnam War veteran, motivates audiences to strive for greater accomplishments. Bleier sees parallels between the challenges he faced in the past and the challenges all businesspeople face in today’s competitive marketplace. Using real-life stories, Bleier will provide attendees with the drive to achieve their goals and improve their business.

State of the Industry Executive Outlook

Wednesday, November 12

12:30-1:30 p.m.

Room C101

A panel of industry professionals will discuss the notable rebound of U.S. manufacturing activity and whether the sector might be experiencing a renaissance or just an uptick. Drivers such as health care costs, labor, government regulations, energy prices, and exchange rates are just some of the factors affecting many FABTECH attendees. Hear from manufacturing industry leaders who are facing these issues and how they navigate these challenges to remain competitive in today’s economy. Join an interactive Q&A afterward to offer your perspective on the future of the industry. Walk away with new insights that will help guide your business decisions for 2015 and beyond.

Moderator: Edwin Stanley, VP sales and finance, GH Metal Solutions

Panelists: Todd Henry, operations manager, Caterpillar Inc.; Dave Whelan, SVP supply chain, Bluebird Corp.; Raj Bartra, president, Industry Automation Division, Siemens.
