# 2014 Safety Awards: Now Accepting Applications

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 The Fabricator]

If your company has an outstanding safety record, your efforts can be recognized with an FMA Annual Safety Award. There are several levels of recognition, from awards of honor to honorable mention. It’s easy to apply. You must be an FMA Advantage-level member, complete OSHA Form 300A (summary of work-related injuries and illnesses), and send it to Nancy Hatten at

nancyh@fmanet.org

by Feb. 28, 2014. Not a member? Call customer service at 888-394-4362 or visit

fmanet.org/membership

for a complete listing of membership levels and benefits.

The 2014 Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence

This award, the highest level in FMA’s Safety Award program, is presented to one company each year that demonstrates it has an established safety culture in its workplace. The award is named in honor of Rusty Demeules, a longtime FMA volunteer and the first chairman of the FMA Safety Council. An essay must be submitted by Feb. 28, 2014, to be considered for this award.

All safety awards are presented at FMA’s Annual Safety Conference, which is planned for April 23-24, 2014, in Rock Island, Ill. (Quad Cities). The conference will feature a tour of the John Deere Harvester Works Plant.

To find out more about the awards and to apply online, visit

fmanet.org/safetyawards
