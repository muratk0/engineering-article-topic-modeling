Gregg Simpson

FMA Board of Directors Outgoing Chair

When I became the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

®

, (FMA) chairman last fall, we experienced an exceptional year topped off with record attendance at FABTECH

®

Chicago. 2020 has been quite a year. Early on, there were remarkable highs followed by some unexpected lows. Back in March, we celebrated FMA’s 50th anniversary at the Annual Meeting in San Antonio.

Shortly after the Annual Meeting, we quickly responded to the pandemic’s disruptions in the manufacturing sector by providing member-exclusive resources to all industry professionals at no cost to them. We’ve also pivoted to virtual events that give attendees access to educational sessions and insightful leaders from the comfort of their homes or offices.

As my time as the FMA chair comes to an end, I would like to thank our board members, FMA staff, event partners, and generous donors who have provided tremendous support to the association throughout the year. Real greatness is best demonstrated during difficult times, and FMA is committed to staying the course to ensure a bright future for manufacturing.

Thank you,

Gregg Simpson
