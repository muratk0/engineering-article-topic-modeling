The Fabrication and Metals Industries’ Leadership Conference

300+ top-level executives from industry-leading companies will come together for three days of incomparable learning and collaboration in a relaxed atmosphere.

Here’s a sneak peek at some of the subject matter experts…

Sheryl Connelly

Manager of Global Trends and Chief Futurist, Ford Motor Company

Tracking changes across social, technological, economic, environmental, and political arenas and shifts in consumer attitudes and behaviors, Sheryl Connelly will share insights on the factors that will most affect your business in the years to come.

Lourenco Goncalves

Chairman, President and CEO, Cleveland-Cliffs Inc

Hear what’s next on the horizon for the steel industry and Mr. Goncalves’ first-hand perspective on Cleveland-Cliffs’ expansion.

Dr. Chris Kuehl

FMA Economic Analyst and Managing Partner, Armada Corporate Intelligence

A returning conference favorite, Dr. Kuehl will share relevant business and economic stories with a touch of humor.

Omar Nashahibi

Founding Partner, The Franklin Partnership

Washington, D.C.-based lobbyist, Omar Nashashibi will provide an insider’s view into the recent developments that impact the industry and what manufacturers can expect in 2022 and beyond from their not-so-silent partner — the federal government.
