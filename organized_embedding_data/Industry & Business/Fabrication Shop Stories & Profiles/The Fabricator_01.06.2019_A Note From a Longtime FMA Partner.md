News and information from the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International

®

Roger Wilson Assistant Vice President of CNA

Last month attendees gathered for the 11th annual Safety Conference in Lexington, Kentucky. The program included insights from industry experts and peers about best practices in plant safety.

I am pleased to share that FMA’s most prestigious safety honor, the 2019 Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence, was presented to Tempel Steel for its conscious work to create a culture of safety. Additionally, 49 company members received awards for their achievements in obtaining safety records better than the OSHA recordable rate for their NAICS codes.

As FMA’s endorsed business insurance provider for more than 30 years, CNA is proud to sponsor FMA’s annual Safety Conference and Awards, recognizing companies that are dedicated to high standards for safety excellence.

Thank you,

Roger Wilson
