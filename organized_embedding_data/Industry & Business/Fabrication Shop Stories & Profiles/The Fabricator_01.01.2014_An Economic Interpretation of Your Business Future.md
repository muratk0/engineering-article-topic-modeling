# An Economic Interpretation of Your Business Future

Attend FMA’s annual meetings for an economist’s assessment of the ever-changing business landscape

Dr. Chris Kuehl, FMA Economic Analyst

FMA’s economic analyst, Chris Kuehl, PhD, will headline both

The FABRICATOR®’s 9th Annual Leadership Summit

and

FMA’s 18th Annual Toll Processing Conference.

These co-located events will be held at the Omni Barton Creek Resort & Spa, Austin, Texas, Feb. 26-28, 2014.

Readers of the “Ask the Dr.” column in

The FABRICATOR

(page 86) and Fabrinomics® e-newsletter know that Dr. Kuehl shares relevant business and economic stories accompanied by commentary that interprets the impact on manufacturing. He delivers the news without the hype typically found in consumer media. Kuehl’s vast knowledge, wit, and clever observations make for an entertaining presentation.

The FABRICATOR’s Leadership Summit

will begin with group tours of local manufacturing facilities on Wednesday morning, Feb. 26, followed by a luncheon. Kuehl will share his view of the economy and the manufacturing segment performance—giving the comprehensive analysis needed to make sound business decisions. Before Kuehl’s presentation, case studies will be presented by the recipients of

The FABRICATOR

and

TPJ-The Tube & Pipe Journal®’s Industry Awards.

A d d iti o nal highlights of the program include a technology update on equipment advances available to improve production velocity and the speed of success, as well as many peer-to-peer learning opportunities such as roundtable discussions on business growth. Operational improvement topics — employee engagement, lean manufacturing in the job shop, robotic technology and automation, sales and marketing, and strategic planning — will also be featured.

“Advanced High-strength Metals — Ready or not, here they come!” is the theme of the 2014

Toll Processing Conference.

Steel and aluminum producers will share insight on the growing demand for lighter and stronger metals, while a panel of equipment specialists will highlight technology innovations for processing materials that are now available and explore what will be needed for the next generation of metals still in development.

In addition to hearing world-class keynote speakers, attendees will see steel market analyst Glenn Kidd and Chris Kuehl share the stage once again. Following their enlightening and entertaining presentation, they will each lead a deep-dive breakout session.

Attendees will have plenty of opportunities for networking at golf outings, plant tours, and exciting evening receptions. Don’t miss out on this incredible opportunity to expand your network and strengthen existing business relationships. Conference registration fees are $895, FMA/TPA/ OPC members or $1,145, nonmembers.
