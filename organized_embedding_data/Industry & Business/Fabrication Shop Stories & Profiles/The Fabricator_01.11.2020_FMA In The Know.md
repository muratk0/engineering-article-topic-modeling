A Special Invitation From FMA’s Board Chair

Dear Industry Professionals,

As our top priority this year has been to safely serve, interact with, and educate industry professionals, I would like to cordially invite you to the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International’s

®

, (FMA) first-ever virtual Annual Meeting, March 3-4, 2021. This is an unprecedented opportunity to virtually interact with your fellow heroes of manufacturing who have braved the 2020 storm to come back stronger than ever.

The FMA staff has prepared an impressive lineup. Throughout two interactive days you will:

Engage with hundreds of metal fabricators, processors, and distributors to discuss common business challenges and successes.

Experience insightful keynotes, including FMA Economic Analyst Dr. Chris Kuehl, who will shed light on the state of manufacturing and what to expect in the coming months.

Interact with and hear from industry experts who will discuss the latest fabrication and metals processing technologies.

The Annual Meeting has been a premier FMA event for many years now. I’m especially looking forward to the 2021 event, as it will be a groundbreaking virtual experience for all of the manufacturing heroes who attend. We look forward to celebrating you there!

Sincerely, Bill Isaac
