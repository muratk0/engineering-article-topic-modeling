Summer Manufacturing Camps Are Growing in Popularity

What this means for manufacturing companies

This summer, NBT will support a record number of 134 camps, a substantial increase from last year’s high of 85. The aim of the camp grant program is simple: to introduce young people to the excitement, challenge, and pride of building something with their own hands and using advanced technology to inspire them to pursue careers in manufacturing.

You may be wondering, why is this important? What does this mean for my company? How does this affect us?

The benefits companies can gain from a significant increase in summer camps go beyond the present circumstances. The 2019 camp program will have an impact on 2,000 students, their parents, school instructors, community colleges, technical schools, and local businesses.

At this rate every year, thousands of students will be working with their hands to make something on their own. This knowledge and experience can influence their future and the possibility of joining the manufacturing skilled labor workforce. The more interested students, the more likelihood of skilled workers to be employable by your company.

You can help the next-generation skilled workforce discover manufacturing by supporting a camp in your area. Offer to host a plant tour of your manufacturing facility, volunteer as a guest speaker or camp mentor, or give financial support as a camp sponsor.
