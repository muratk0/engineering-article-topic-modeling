# Sheet metal showcase

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

DON’T MISS THE EUROBLECH 2014 sheet metal showcase, from 21-25 October 2014, in Hanover, Germany. A total of 1,600 exhibitors from 38 countries have booked their stands, covering 86,500m2 of net floor space across eight halls of the Hanover Exhibition Centre. This represents a 5 per cent increase in exhibitor numbers compared to the previous show. Over 60,000 registered visitors attended the event in 2012.

Major exhibitor countries next to Germany are Italy, China, Turkey, the Netherlands, Spain, Switzerland, Austria, the US and the UK. Half of all exhibiting companies come from outside Germany. This high percentage of international exhibitors once again confirms EuroBLECH’s position as the world’s No 1 exhibition for the sheet metal working industry. This year’s event will also feature the new ‘Sheet Metal Champion’ awards. The official awards ceremony is open to all exhibitors and visitors and will take place on 22 October 2014, at 15.00 hrs, up in the gallery in Hall 13.

“The sheet metal working industry is currently facing an economic environment of diverse regional market situations within our globalised world. In addition, there is a demand for an ever-growing choice of products which needs to be met by using innovative and flexible fabrication processes.

In this challenging but highly promising environment, it is essential for companies in the sheet metal working industry to invest in innovative technologies and tailor-made machines and systems. Intelligent process chains and efficient networks are indispensable,” explained Nicola Hamann, managing director for Mack Brooks Exhibitions’ extensive German business.

“As the technological showcase and business barometer of the sheet metal working industry, EuroBLECH offers its audience a comprehensive overview of the prevailing technological trends. For companies in sheet metal working looking to find suitable machinery and smart solutions for modern production, the exhibition is the key global event,” she added.

The show profile of the 23rd International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition represents the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; handling; separation; forming; flexible sheet metal working; joining; welding, tube/section processing; surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; quality control; CAD/CAM systems; factory equipment and R&D. EuroBLECH 2014 will once again be held in halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 27 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds.

With its dedicated exhibition profile, EuroBLECH targets all sheet metal working specialists at every management level in small and medium-sized companies as well as large enterprises. Visitors include engineers, production managers, quality managers, buyers, manufacturers, technical directors and experts in associations and R&D.

The EuroBLECH show website also features the EuroBLECH Job Board (www.euroblech.com/english/job-market). Specialists in sheet metal working looking for a new challenge in their career can reach employers worldwide. Job vacancies and candidate profiles are advertised on the EuroBLECH website, through the exhibition app which will be live two weeks before the show, as well as on-site at the exhibition.

The EuroBLECH app offers information about all exhibitors and their products along with a map of the exhibition grounds. The app also offers ‘myEB’ – a personal show planner which offers many useful functions. Visitors can register tickets and generate a mobile ticket which will transform the smartphone into an entrance ticket. ‘EuroBLECH 2 go’ is available around two weeks before the exhibition. Smartphone users can access the app via www.eb2go.de; it is also free to download as an Android or iPhone app.

See www.euroblech.com ■

## Apprenticeship awards for Group Rhodes

Group Rhodes’ managing director, Mark Ridgway OBE, DL with the apprentice award winners. In the foreground is the Apprentice of the Year winner, Anthony Smith

GROUP RHODES hosted its Apprenticeship Awards 2014 in the UK on 12 September 2014. In addition to highlighting key successes and personal development, the awards recognised apprentices who have made a significant contribution to their role and department. Group Rhodes is one of Europe’s largest manufacturers of special purpose machinery for advanced applications in aerospace, metalforming, heavy ceramics, composites, environmental and deep-sea oil and gas sectors, and currently operates from four factories in the north of England.

“Approximately 9 per cent of Group Rhodes’ employees are apprentices. Their focus on training and professional development has this year been rewarded by qualifications at every academic level from NVQs and ONCs through to First Class Degrees and even an M.Phil. Group Rhodes’ strength is in our product differentiation and this, in turn, stems from the quality of the people that we train and employ,” the company explained.

The company has a successful track record of working closely with learning centres. Local colleges and the Advanced Manufacturing Research Centre training school in Rotherham form the main source of apprenticeship training, and are pivotal to continuous education and personal development initiatives. The company also has strong working relationships with Sheffield Hallam University and Leeds Metropolitan University.

A new Group Rhodes’ purpose-built facility in Bangalore, India was officially inaugurated on 27 August 2014 by UK Deputy Prime Minister, Nick Clegg.
