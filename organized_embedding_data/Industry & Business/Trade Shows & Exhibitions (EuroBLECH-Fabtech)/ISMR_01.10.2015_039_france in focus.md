We preview the combined Tolexpo, MIDEST and Maintenance Expo events in Paris from 17-20 November 2015.

Tolexpo, the sheet metal exhibition which takes place from 17 – 20 November 2015 in Paris (France), is celebrating its tenth anniversary this year. Tolexpo is a key event in France dedicated to sheet metal, coil, tube and section equipment. Tolexpo, Midest (a leading industrial subcontracting show) and Maintenance Expo will take place together at Paris Nord Villepinte and are expected to attract 2,000 exhibitors and 50,000 professional visitors.

“The entire production chain (users and suppliers) together under one roof, from cutting to assembly through to final surface treatment, not forgetting the maintenance of equipment,” explained the organiser. “The last joint event attracted more than 200 exhibitors (46% from abroad) and 64 new product launches. Approximately 71% of exhibitors have supported Tolexpo since it was created in 2005.

“Tolexpo targeted visitors are looking for new equipment and technological innovations, whether in relation to renewal or modernization of their existing equipment, adoption of new technologies and strengthening of their productivity or their compliance with standards and regulations in the sector - all resulting in an increase in their creativity and competitiveness.”

## The Tolexpo trophy

Launched for the first time this year, the Tolexpo Trophy – which is part of the Midest Trophy awards – is the only one in France dedicated exclusively to manufacturers of production equipment for sheet, tubing and profile metal working. The Tolexpo Trophy is jointly run by the Tolexpo and Midest organisers. It is open only to Tolexpo exhibitors involved in the Tolexpo new products campaign.

“Created in 2009, the aim of the new products campaign is to promote major new products from the sector during the period between Tolexpo shows (November 2013 to November 2015). After studying each entry, a panel of journalists, engineering centre representatives and Tolexpo and Midest consultants will issue a shortlist of nominees in early October. The winner will be announced at the traditional Midest trophy award ceremony to be held at the Midest trade show after its official opening on Tuesday 17 November 2015,” explained the organiser.

The list of nominees will be published in mid-October 2015 once the panel has finished its deliberations. This initiative will showcase new products in the sheet metal sector (strengths and performances) presented by equipment manufacturers and retailers.

“The last show in 2013 saw no fewer than 64 new products exhibited at Tolexpo, including one international launch,” added the organiser.
