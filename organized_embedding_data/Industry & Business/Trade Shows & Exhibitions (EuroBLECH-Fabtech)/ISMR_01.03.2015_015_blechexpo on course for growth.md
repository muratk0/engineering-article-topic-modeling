# Blechexpo on course for growth

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

THE PRACTICAL TRADE FAIR duo of Blechexpo and Schweisstec takes place over a two-year cycle at the state trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and covers the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology. This year’s event will be held from 3 to 6 November 2015 with exhibitors from over 25 countries to date.

The combination of sheet metal, profile and pipe processing technology plus cutting, joining and fastening technology throughout the entire process sequence – from machining and fabrication up to module assembly – is the event’s strength. Originally launched as a national trade fair targeted primarily at expert visitors in south-eastern Germany, Blechexpo is now ranked the world's number two trade fair for sheet metal processing. Today, the catchment area for expert visitors extends well beyond Central, South and Eastern Europe.

The event, organised by P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, has enjoyed continuous growth in domestic and overseas exhibitor numbers, as well as an expanding expert audience which travelled to Stuttgart from more than 100 countries for the last event. According to the project management team, Blechexpo and Schweisstec have already matched the floor space figures reached at the last event in 2013, so additional halls have now been allocated. To date, roughly 860,000 square feet have been reserved with several months to go before the trade fair opens. The show remit has also been expanded to include relevant areas with regard to processing directly from the strip coil or the wire coil for electrical and electromechanical products.

“The percentage of overseas manufacturers and distributors is heading for record-breaking numbers (exhibitors are expected from 30 countries around the globe), so that even the successful figures achieved in 2013 will be topped,” added the organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.

ISMR is a media partner and will be exhibiting at the event. For further details, see www.blechexpo-messe.de/ ■
