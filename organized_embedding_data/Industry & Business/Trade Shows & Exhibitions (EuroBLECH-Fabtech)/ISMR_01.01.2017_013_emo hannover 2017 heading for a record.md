# EMO Hannover 2017 heading for a record

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

JUST UNDER A year before the show begins, the figures for official registration at EMO Hannover 2017 are looking strong. More than 1,700 companies from 40 different countries have registered for the metalworking sector trade fair, booking a total stand area of 152,000 square metres for the period from 18-23 September 2017 in Hanover.

“It looks very much as if EMO Hannover 2017 will end up with a record number of exhibitors," declared EMO General Commissioner, Carl Martin Welcker.

The principal focus of EMO Hannover is on metal cutting and forming machine tools; production systems; high-precision tools; automated material flows; computer technology; industrial electronics and accessories. Overall, Asia currently accounts for more than a fifth of EMO's exhibitors. In 2013, the fair attracted over 2,130 exhibitors, and around 143,000 trade visitors from more than 100 countries.

Under the banner 'Connecting Systems for Intelligent Production', the forthcoming trade fair for the metal working sector will focus on the megatrend of digitisation and networking under the aegis of Industry 4.0.

“Numerous companies can identify with this slogan, and will be showcasing their Industry 4.0 solutions either on their own stands or in the Industry 4.0 area," commented Carl Martin Welcker.
