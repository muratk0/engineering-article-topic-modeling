# STEELFAB IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

Don't miss the 19th SteelFab exhibition from 8-11 January 2024 in Sharjah, UAE.

ISMR SAYS: "Visitors can interact with over 250 exhibitors from 35 countries displaying over 600 brands. Over 7,000 trade visitors from 65 countries are anticipated to attend.

A key showcase for the UAE and region's economic and industrial growth, the SteelFab trade fair is gearing up for its 19th edition at Expo Centre Sharjah (UAE) from 8-11 January 2024. It has become an important trade show in the Middle East and North Africa region, dedicated to metalforming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs.

"The United Arab Emirates (UAE's) strong economic performance and the extraordinary resurgence of its construction industry are poised to set the tone for this forthcoming trade event for the metalworking, metal manufacturing and steel fabrication industry, the show organiser, Expo Centre Sharjah, told ISMR.

"SteelFab has played a significant role in the remarkable progress achieved by the UAE's industrial sector as well as in the adoption of advanced technology and promotion of innovation in the industry. The show has provided a shot in the arm for the country's industrial initiatives, resulting in higher industrial exports and a rise in the sector's contribution to the national economy," added H.E. Saif Mohamed Al Midfa, CEO, Expo Centre Sharjah.

## UAE in focus

"The UAE is accelerating the adoption of Fourth Industrial Revolution technologies in the industrial sector under the UAE Industry 4.0 programme. This has generated huge interest among manufacturers and distributors at SteelFab to tap the rise in demand for the latest technologies and sustainable solutions. The construction industry, which is the main demand driver for the fabrication sector, is set to grow on the back of high oil prices and the country's development push, keeping the orders books busy for the metalworking industry," outlined Mr. Sultan Shattaf, Commercial Director, Expo Centre Sharjah.

For the forthcoming 19th edition in 2024, SteelFab will host leading global manufacturers and dealers through special segments such as Welding & Cutting; Metal Cutting; Machine Tools; Metal Forming; Steel Machinery; Pipe Construction Equipment and Surface Preparation & Finishing. SteelFab has increased its focus on several industrial segments in the manufacturing industry and topics such as AI & Robotics (a recent addition to its core focus areas) are key attractions at the event.

"Visitors can interact with over 250 exhibitors from 35 countries displaying over 600 brands. We look forward to attracting over 7,000 trade visitors from 65 countries. The trade show will also feature the 'Best-Welder @SteelFab' competition as well as a huge live display of the latest welding and cutting machinery/equipment; a hosted buyers programme and an online B2B matchmaking platform along with the 8th edition of Fasteners World Middle East," explained the show organiser.

Country pavilions; live machinery displays/ demonstrations and a dedicated vertical for the fastener and fixing industry (Fasteners World Middle East) are key features of next January's event. The show's visitor footprint extends across the entire GCC, Middle East, North Africa, Indian sub-continent and Central Asian region. Entry is free to trade visitors and there is free parking at the venue.

Innovation is key. With the latest technological advances, such as IoT and automation, the metal fabrication industry is undergoing rapid changes. From product research and development, quality assurance and everything in between, technological advances are changing the manufacturing process. Smart sensors, immersive technology gadgets, sustainable materials and green energy sources are being embraced widely.

"According to the Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology figures, the UAE has managed to increase the industrial sector's contribution to national gross domestic product to US$ 50 billion, a 38% increase from 2020. Multiple foreign consultancies and financial agencies (including the International Monetary Fund) has projected the UAE to emerge as the best performing economy in the GCC in 2024, which augurs well for the industry," added the show organiser.

## An eye on welding

SteelFab, with ESAB and the Middle East Industrial Training Institute (MEITI) will present the third edition of the "BestWelder@SteelFab" competition at SteelFab 2024. The competition will be held from 8-11 January 2024 at Expo Centre Sharjah, UAE.

Welding industry professionals from the region will showcase their welding skills and compete to win this title. Space is limited so the show organiser urges candidates/participants to register as quickly as possible. The submission deadline is 15 December 2023.

"SteelFab was the first event that exclusively catered to the complete welding and cutting requirements of the steel fabrication industry in the region. The formidable manufacturing and industrial sectors in this region were the main reason behind the rising demand for welding and cutting equipment in the region. Heavy fabrications and a focus on renewable energy usage also provided impetus for growth in welding markets," outlined the show organiser.

"Other sectors that contribute to the overall market growth of welding equipment, consumables and services include the energy, construction and ship building industries, as well as emerging sectors such as aerospace and defence. The construction of new power plants and repair and maintenance activities in the region are also likely to boost demand for welding consumables. Returning investments in oil and gas industries would further augment demand for welding consumables," it added.

The welding and cutting segment is diverse, pointed out the show organiser, and the regional industry consists of fabrication yards and engineering workshops and not "welding shops".
