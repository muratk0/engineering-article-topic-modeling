## The FABTECH educational programme

The Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, Int'l (FMA); SME (formerly known as Society of Manufacturing Engineers); Precision Metalforming Association (PMA); American Welding Society (AWS) and Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI) co-sponsor the sessions on cutting; laser; finishing; forming & fabricating; management; marketing/sales; lean; automation; smart manufacturing; stamping; workforce development and 3D/additive manufacturing.

All sessions are 90 minutes, offering practical knowledge that can be used right away. Sessions with Tech Tours combine classroom instruction with expert-led guided tours on the show floor to see technology operating in designated booths.

## Getting there

Transportation is convenient in and around Atlanta. Fly into Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport and reach hotels and the Georgia World Congress Centre (GWCC) by car, taxi, train or a variety of ground transportation services.

A free shuttle bus service is provided between official FABTECH hotels and the Georgia World Congress Centre (US $30 value). Note: Attendees must book hotel rooms through onPeak, the official housing provider for FABTECH 2022, to receive the complimentary shuttle service.

FABTECH is partnering with Delta Airlines to offer potential discounts on return air fares to FABTECH. Book your flight using meeting code: NMVC3.
