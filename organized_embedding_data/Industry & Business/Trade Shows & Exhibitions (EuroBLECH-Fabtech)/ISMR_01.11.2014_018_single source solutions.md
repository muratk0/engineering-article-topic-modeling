# Single source solutions

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

THE Blechexpo/Schweisstec international trade fairs for sheet metal processing and joining technologies will take place from 3-6 November 2015 in Stuttgart, Germany. ISMR will be a media partner at the event, which unites the fields of sheet metal, pipe and profile processing, as well as thermal and mechanical joining and off-the-coil production technologies. Organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, has just announced over 70% stand bookings at this early stage as well as greater international coverage – exhibitors from 25 countries to date will be at the 2015 event. Over 32,000 expert visitors from nearly 100 countries attended the last event in 2013.

“The customer trend is plainly moving away from suppliers of all sizes who process sheet metal and other materials, and is moving inexorably towards complete solutions from a single source. This means concentration on just a few suppliers who offer accordingly higher levels of performance, as well as technology partners who will have the necessary equipment and qualified personnel at their disposal, to counter competition in the long run,” the show organiser explained.

“Expressed in other terms, stamping and bending specialists will supplement their portfolios to include laser technologies, laser job shops will expand into bending and forming technology and manufacturers of stamped products will supplement their operations with thermal and mechanical joining technologies. All wish to be seen by their customers as expert technology partners.

“The logical consequence will be increased investment in machinery, tooling, auxiliary equipment and qualified personnel on the one hand. On the other hand, these efforts will be thwarted by a growing lack of qualified personnel, which will further propel manufacturers of machine tools and peripheral equipment to make their systems and solutions fit for simple, flexible operation by trained personnel through the inclusion of more intelligence and automation.”
