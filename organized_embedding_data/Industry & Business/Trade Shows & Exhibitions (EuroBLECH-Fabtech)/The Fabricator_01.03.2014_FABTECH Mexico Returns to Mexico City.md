# FABTECH Mexico Returns to Mexico City

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 The Fabricator]

May 6-8, 2014, Centro Banamex

FABTECH® Mexico,

co-sponsored by the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association Intl. (FMA) and SME, co-locates with AWS Weldmex and METALFORM Mexico once again in 2014. COATech will join the event, adding to the finishing technology offered and further expanding the show. The largest metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing exposition in Mexico anticipates 550 exhibiting companies, 100,000 net square feet of floor space, and more than 12,000 attendees from Mexico and Central America.

FABTECH Mexico features bending and fabrication products, including laser and plasma cutting, coil processing, roll forming, plate and structural fabricating, saws and cutoff machines, tooling, press brakes, shears, punching, and tube and pipe equipment. AWS Weldmex showcases welding and cutting products, including thermal spray, metal finishing, and safety equipment. METALFORM Mexico offers metal forming products, including tool and die, metal stamping, forming, and assembly. COATech will highlight finishing technology including paint and powder, coatings, and corrosion control.
