# BIEMH storms into 2020

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

EXHIBITORS FROM MORE than 23 countries have booked over 90% of the exhibiting space planned for the 31st BIEMH International Machine Tool Exhibition. The event, which will take place from 25-29 May 2020 at Bilbao in Spain, aims to showcase a huge amount of innovative technology and solutions over a large exhibiting area.

The exhibition area will cover tools, accessories and components (35%), machine tools (28%), production systems automation (11%), metrology and production services (13%) and other machines and equipment (13%).

The VIP Buyer Invitation programme (Hosted Buyers Programme) for national and international buyers will once again be a key feature. The visitor team is already working on a list of priority countries such as Germany; the U.S.; Mexico; France; Poland; Portugal; China; India; Italy; Turkey and Vietnam.

“Hall 4 at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre will hold parallel trade shows during BIEMH and become Hall 4.0, with more exhibitors than the last show. They will be displaying essential technologies to successfully tackle the transition to the new industry. Hall 4.0 will host the third edition of BeDIGITAL, a showcase of the latest solutions and tools for advanced digitalisation," said the show organiser.

“At the same time, innovative industrial solutions in additive manufacturing and 3D printing will be on show at the fifth edition of ADDIT3D. It will include the latest features in systems and machinery, industrial applications and materials. Hall 4.0 will also hold the third edition of IMIC, Industrial Maintenance Innovation Conference, on 26-27 May 2020. This will cover the latest trends and developments in industrial maintenance, the management of assets and facilities, and production reliability within the framework of advanced maintenance. The portfolio is completed with WORKinn, an Industrial Employment Fair, on 27-29 May 2020 for industrial companies looking to employ workers with specific skills."
