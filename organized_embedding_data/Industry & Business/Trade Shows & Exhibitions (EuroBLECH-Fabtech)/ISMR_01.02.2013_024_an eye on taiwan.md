# AN EYE ON TAIWAN

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

The 24th TIMTOS (Taipei International Machine Tool Show) will feature around 960 exhibitors with more than 5,300 booths.

ISMR SAYS:
“TIMTOS will run from 5 to 10 March 2013 in Taipei, Taiwan and is co-hosted by TAITRA (Taiwan External Trade Development Council) and TAMI (Taiwan Association for Machinery Industry)”

This year, the TIMTOS exhibition in Taiwan is expected to break records in terms of number of exhibitors, scale and visitors. It now ranks among the top five machine tool events in the world. TIMTOS 2011 attracted 928 exhibitors (with 5,152 booths). The organisers predict that, this year, more than 5,500 overseas visitors and 40,000 domestic visitors will visit the event.

TIMTOS will run from 5 to 10 March 2013 in Taipei, Taiwan and is co-hosted by TAITRA (Taiwan External Trade Development Council) and TAMI (Taiwan Association for Machinery Industry). The exhibition will cover a total space of 90,000 square metres at Taipei World Trade Centre Halls one, two and three as well as the NANGANG exhibition hall. An accompanying seminar programme will also be held from 2–4 March 2013.

“One touchstone for success has been that exhibitor applications for TIMTOS, which started last April, were fully booked within a week,” the organisers told ISMR. “Along with strong participation from across Taiwan, well known machine tool manufacturers from 17 other countries have also signed on for this event. Countries include leading machine tool manufacturers from Switzerland; Germany; Italy; Japan; the USA; Austria and several other nations.”

## Inspiring innovation

The show’s constant drive to inspire quality and innovation is typified by this year’s "Taiwan Machine Tools Industry Award 2013 for Excellence in Research and Innovation". Around 58 businesses have entered 61 products in the competition. Products compete in four separate categories: machining centres, CNC lathes, other NC machine tools and key components for CNC machine tools. This year, a new category dedicated to "key components" for CNC machine tools has been added. All competing products in all categories undergo three months of strict review and evaluation, with the winners being announced in March at TIMTOS.

An exhibition app, available to download in February, supports all functions related to the floor plan/exhibitors/products searches, daily schedule planning, follow-ups on past negotiations and event/shuttle bus scheduling.

“The 24th TIMTOS will reach record scale to become Taiwan’s largest and Asia’s second largest machine tool event,” added Reyming Tu, Deputy Executive Director, Exhibition Department, TAITRA at the pre-show press conference on 27 November 2012. “TIMTOS 2013 joins exhibitors from 17 countries – Germany; Italy; Japan; Switzerland; Spain; the USA; South Korea; India; Australia; Singapore; Malaysia; China; Hong Kong; Turkey; Vietnam; Finland and (of course) Taiwan. There are 196 exhibitors who are based outside Taiwan. The Trade Office of Swiss Industries (TOSI) will set up its national pavilion for the 11th consecutive year. A 20-booth German Pavilion and a 32-booth China Pavilion has also been added to the show.”

In the first eight months of 2012, Reyming Tu explained, Taiwan machine tool exports rose 9.3 per cent over the same period in 2011.

“The first eight months of exports in 2012 totalled almost US$ 3 billion, with almost a third of that going to China. Meanwhile, Taiwanese exports to emerging markets saw the strongest growth. For example, in the first eight months of 2012, Taiwanese machine tool exports to Thailand grew 59 per cent on the previous year. And, over the same period, the Turkish market grew by more than 19 per cent. Indonesia grew by almost 29 per cent and Malaysia by 6.3 per cent," he explained.

“Last year was a prosperous year for main machine tool manufacturers, especially in Germany, USA, Japan and Taiwan. In 2011, Taiwan’s overall production value as well as its export and import value for the machine tool industry achieved significant increases. Production value grew a quarter over last year to reach US$ 5.04 billion,” added Alan Lu, Chairman of TAMI’s Machine Tool Committee, during the press conference. “China continues to be Taiwan’s main export destination, with January to October 2012 export value reaching US$ 1.2 billion US dollars, a 2.8 per cent growth over the same period in the previous year. The US is Taiwan’s second largest export destination of machine tools; with export value to the USA reaching US$ 0.4 billion, 68 per cent growth on the same period in the previous year.”

ISMR SAYS: “Punched holes in random wave lines create ‘architecture as art”

Embossing aluminium panels with punched holes in random wave lines is another example of creating “architecture as art” using Mate’s special application tooling. Consistency of the embossing, not speed, was key to the success of this project.

NES Architecture in the UK created one hundred and twenty, ten-foot high aluminium panels mounted side-by-side on the walls of a newly constructed electrical substation building. Using its CNC turret punch press equipped with Mate tooling, NES punched all of the panels with lines of holes creating a spectacular building facade. The thousands of holes displayed a novel visual effect of motion and waves.

Specialising in creative architectural metalwork for distinctive, high-end buildings, NES Architecture has a reputation for award-winning design work. It utilises Radan sheet metal CAD/CAM software for its designs as well as a full range of fabricating equipment including CNC turret punch presses equipped with Mate tooling. Its finished products include eye-catching cladding, flashings, copings and rain-screen panels, as well as windows, doors and curtain walls. Typical projects include architectural diamond panels for a Liverpool University building which won a RIBA North West Architectural Award and creation of the UK's largest flat panel LED light installation around the internal walls of the Westfield Shopping and Lifestyle Centre in Stratford, London.

According to Patrick Holt, NES architecture operations director, his company was commissioned to produce the artistic metalwork cladding for the electrical substation building. With a long track record for high quality niche architectural metalwork, NES set out to design the finely polished aluminium panel surfaces with a vibrant feeling of flowing motion. ■
