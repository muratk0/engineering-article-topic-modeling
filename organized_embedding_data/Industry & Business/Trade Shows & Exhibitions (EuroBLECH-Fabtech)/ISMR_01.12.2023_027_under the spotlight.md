# UNDER THE SPOTLIGHT

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

We preview the Southern Manufacturing & Electronics exhibition in Farnborough, UK, from 7-9 February 2023.

ISMR SAYS:
“Southern Manufacturing is an extremely accessible show for the rich eco-system of engineering SMEs across the UK and, increasingly, continental Europe."

Southern Manufacturing & Electronics 2023, due to take place in Farnborough (UK) from 7-9 February 2023. Sheet and tube machining, manipulation, metrology and inspection will form an important part of the exhibition, which will be held at the Farnborough International Exhibition and Conference Centre (a new £35-million 20,000m² venue offering modern facilities, easy access and free onsite parking).

It is a comprehensive annual industrial exhibition in the UK and a major pan-European showcase for new technology in machinery; production equipment; electronic production and assembly; tooling and components as well as subcontract services.

Southern Manufacturing & Electronics 2023 will co-locate once again with AutoAero, a specialist theme within the exhibition devoted to aerospace and automotive engineering. ISMR is a media supporter of the event and will distribute its February issue throughout the show.

## Innovation in focus

"Companies looking for new ideas to bolster their order books and keen to find new business opportunities will do well to visit one of the most important engineering shows in the UK calendar. Many stands will reflect a renewed interest in the reshoring of production as well as new technologies in green energy, the exhibition organiser, European Trade & Exhibition Services (ETES), told ISMR.

“Hundreds of firms will highlight a vast array of high technology products and services. Although it is a multidisciplinary event, that does not mean it is fragmented. There are no physical barriers between areas, so people can move easily between stands showing many diverse engineering disciplines. Visitors say this is one of the show's main attractions, as it often leads to the discovery of companies and technologies that might otherwise be missed. Firms with expertise in similar areas are grouped together, allowing Technology Trails to guide visitors logically around the showground," it added.

Chinese firm Jinan Bodor CNC Machine Co. will display the high-power, fibre laser cutting machines that it builds in its 30,000m² factory in Jinan. AMOB UK will show its specialised equipment for tube and pipe bending, ranging from simple manual machines to the eMOB range of complex, fully electric CNC systems. Additional tube bending products will be exhibited by Portuguese manufacturer, VLB-Group.

Various sheet-metal subcontractors will also promote their services at Southern Manufacturing & Electronics 2023. Fife Fabrications will explain the extent of its supply of precision sheet metalwork, machined components and electro-mechanical assemblies, with continued investment in facilities and equipment.

“Wood plc is another established Scottish subcontract manufacturer of sheet-metal components that will focus visitors' attention on its ability to supply welded fabrications and assemblies to the automotive, oil and gas, nuclear and process and packaging sectors. It constantly invests in the latest fibre laser cutting technology, which is operated by a highly skilled workforce," added the show organiser.

It also highlighted other UK sheet-metal subcontractors such as BIZ Engineering and RHH Franks.

“Design for manufacture in sheet metal is the province of the support team at Potters. The company says that it is receiving an increasing number of enquiries from all manner of industries asking if parts previously created using diverse processes (from plastic vacuum forming to prismatic machining) may alternatively be made from sheet metal," ETES told ISMR.

Other exhibitors include the Confederation of British Metalforming, the leading UK trade association for manufacturers of fasteners, forgings and pressings, cold-rolled and sheetmetal products.

## Seminars and lectures

There will be an extensive technical seminar programme alongside the three days of the exhibition. It is designed "to give visitors and exhibitors valuable learning opportunities, with a particular focus on the technical, managerial, supply-chain and environmental issues facing manufacturers today" according to the show organiser.

The programme will include lectures on lean continuous improvement; design for test; marketing; team motivation; additive manufacturing and business succession planning. The manufacturing sessions will focus, amongst other things, on management techniques; CE and UKCA marking; the role of IP in commerce; social media for lead generation and other marketing strategies; commercialisation of ideas and testing. A full list of sessions and pre-registration are available on the show organiser's website.

The programme is a complementary blend of technical and business topics covering a wide range of issues facing manufacturing businesses today. All seminars can be booked online on the show website at www.industrysouth.co.uk

“There are several benefits to preregistering for the exhibition," added the show organiser. "You can gain faster access into the show with a QR code and you also receive a show preview magazine in advance to help you plan your visit. You are also given a free show catalogue when you arrive (which would otherwise cost £10) and you can guarantee yourself a seat on a free technical seminar. Avoid those inevitable queues!"

## Getting there

From modest beginnings as a regional event, Southern Manufacturing has evolved into a fully-fledged national show, with increasing influence across continental Europe and further afield.

In 2018, it entered its third decade with a move into its new permanent home at the Farnborough International Exhibition and Conference Centre.

The site is easy to access via private transport, with 3,500 free on-site parking spaces.

For those travelling by train, there is a dedicated free bus service running from Farnborough's Main and North Camp railways stations directly to the show. Admission and parking are also free of charge.

The venue itself features a high standard of facilities including a free WiFi service in the foyer area and a selection of catering outlets.
