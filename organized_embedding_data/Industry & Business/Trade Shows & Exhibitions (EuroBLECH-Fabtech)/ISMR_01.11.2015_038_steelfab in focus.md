# STEELFAB IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

## SteelFab 2016 is likely to feature more than 370 exhibitors as well as extra content and a new concurrent fasteners show

Meet ISMR on its booth at next year’s SteelFab exhibition in Sharjah, UAE – the regional steel fabrication and metal working industry’s well-established trade event. The event promises the regional industry a robust growth in new content, a rise in foreign participation and a new concurrent show.

The twelfth edition of the trade exhibition, from 17-20 January 2016 at Expo Centre Sharjah in the United Arab Emirates, targets steel fabricators and the metal working and metal manufacturing industry and features participation from steel fabricators, steel contractors and manufacturers. Exhibitors in this segment can expect to meet visitors such as main contractors, construction companies, oil & gas industry, shipbuilding industry and heavy manufacturing industries.

“SteelFab 2016 is likely to feature more than 370 exhibitors representing nearly 1000 brands from across the world, besides new special focus areas, special pavilions, live displays, technical seminars and product presentations,” the organiser, Expo Centre Sharjah, told ISMR.

## Strong support and new content

“At SteelFab 2016, the regional industry can explore one of the biggest showcases of cutting-edge equipment, advanced technologies, new products and applications for all its metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs,” said Mr Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, CEO, Expo Centre Sharjah.

Prominent among the new additions to SteelFab 2016 is a new vertical “Fasteners World Middle East” which will be a dedicated trade fair for the fastener and fixing industry. As a precursor to Fasteners World ME, a fasteners’ pavilion was introduced during the previous show which paved the way for the launch of the new concurrent show.

Increasing foreign participation has always been a strong point of SteelFab and this has been underscored by the confirmation of the German and Taiwan pavilions at the show.

“In coordination with the Association of the German Trade Fair Industry (AUMA) and the German Engineering Federation (VDMA), a large number of exhibitors have already confirmed their presence in the German Pavilion. The Taiwan Association of Machinery Industry (TAMI) will be facilitating the participation of leading machinery makers at the Taiwan Pavilion,” added Mr Midfa.

Apart from Germany and Taiwan, there will be strong participation from Italy, Turkey, China and India. At its previous show, SteelFab featured 22 stalls in the German Pavilion, while the show saw the participation of more than 30 Italian exhibitors and 16 in the Taiwan Pavilion. This time, the German and Taiwan pavilions will be featuring 24 and 20 exhibitors, respectively.

“New technology and machinery have always been a mainstay of SteelFab and this year visitors can expect some of the latest innovations from the world’s leading manufacturers and dealers. The number of new exhibitors has gone up by about 15% already, confirming that new content is set to grow in space by more than 25%,” added Mr Midfa.

SteelFab 2016 will also see the launch of a new special focus area, “Wear Resistant & Hard Facing – Cladding”. The new segments will help regional fabricators to optimise their production for fabrications used in rigorous environments such as construction equipment, municipal equipment, recycling, quarries, cement industry etc.

The regular special focus areas, such as “Welding and Cutting”, “Machine Tools” and “Pipe and Tube Machinery” will continue to be attractions for the industry.

## Markets in the UAE

“The construction industry in the UAE and the region is on full swing, with almost all segments showing significant growth as statebacked firms and others have begun to spend on new infrastructure, commercial, residential, tourism and retail projects,” the show organiser told ISMR.

“Construction projects with a combined value of US$ 67.6 billion were completed in 2014 in the GCC. Construction projects worth US$72 billion are slated to be completed in the region by 2015. Deals worth US$ 103 billion were set to be awarded across the year, up 21.2% compared to 2014. Overall, US$ 2.87 trillion worth of projects are in the design, bid or construction stage in the GCC up to 2025, with US$ 1.53 trillion worth of real estate projects under construction," it added.

Manufacturing is another industry that is keeping the order books full for the steel fabrication industry. The industrial base of the region is witnessing major expansion and the push for economic diversification is seeing more dollars being pumped into the industry by governments. Regional governments are boosting industrial development by providing adequate infrastructure, setting up industrial cities and zones and several other incentives.

With an expanding population and a thriving economy, and the UAE and Qatar playing host to two of the largest events globally in 2020 and 2022, there is a serious need to invest heavily in infrastructure. The World Bank estimates that GCC states need to invest an estimated US$121 billion into projects during 2015-2019 to build new power plants and expand existing units to face growth demand.

Despite the recent decline in oil prices, the oil & gas industry remains the economic engine of the GCC. The number and value of new projects in the energy sector surpasses that of any other sector in the region, and this is not likely to change as regional governments invest to boost production and diversify their economies by pushing into downstream industries. ■

## A POLISHED PERFORMANCE

Timesavers International is based in Goes, The Netherlands, with regional offices around the globe in Shanghai (China), Taichung (Taiwan), Kuala Lumpur (Malaysia) and Timesavers Inc. in Minneapolis (USA). The company has more than 200 employees worldwide supported by a network of local dealers and partners. It has built and sold over 55,000 machines worldwide, with customers in nearly every country.

Knowledge is one of Timesavers’ strong points, a culmination of 70 years of experience in the field. It does not just construct machines but offers suitable solutions for deburring, calibration, edge radiussing, finishing and surface finishing on stainless steel sheet and coil.

At this year’s Blechexpo, it launched its new top bottom grinder, the first in the Timesavers range, to join its family of deburring machines. This ergonomic machine is the result of a collaborative effort between Timesavers, the TU in Eindhoven and a design office in Amsterdam. ISMR joined its marketing and PR manager, Annemarie van de Velde, at the exhibition in Stuttgart for a demonstration of the new machine.

## Top and bottom deburring machine

If you process or machine shear, plasma cut, water jet, punch, laser or mill, your material will generally have burrs. There are no current industry standards for deburring. To some, a part is considered deburred when the vertical burr is removed. To others, a part isn’t fully deburred until the edge of the part has a radius. With thousands of machines in service and decades of experience, Timesavers International designs systems to meet a varying range of standards.

“Our new top and bottom deburring machine is for edge rounding and finishing of parts in one single pass. It also features a grinding wheel. The oscillating system is equipped with twelve brushes. The six top and six bottom left and right rotating grinding flap brushes process the parts on both sides simultaneously,” she told ISMR.

“It is suitable for processing a large range of parts - the feed speed, shuttle speed, rotation speed, depth adjustment and the choice of brushes can be set independently of each other. It is also possible to process just one side at a time.”

Rapid tool exchange and automatic calibration adjustment means that no time is lost when processed mixed materials and the machine can be operated 24/7. All aspects of the machine are easily accessible and the intuitive PLC control, knobs and gauges make the machine handy to operate.

“It is possible to combine the machine with a grinding belt in front, so that parts with big burrs can also be processed,” continued Annemarie van de Velde. “The addition may also be a grinding belt after the machine, which ensures that the material will be processed with a nice finish. Customers can also opt for an automatic cleaning function.”

The machine is particularly suited to processing materials with heavy burrs and features new brushes for the top/bottom part. It can process material of up to 25mm. Advanced servo technology optimises movements and keeps energy and tooling consumption to low levels, which maximizes output. The high-tech design, including the visual operator control through LED lighting, makes the machine easy to use. The machine was developed with funding from the European Fund of Regional Development “Op Zuid”.

“This machine handles a lot of operations, which previously needed several machines, and saves users a lot of time by covering all these operations in one single pass,” concluded Annemarie van de Velde.

## Multi-rotating brush machine and manual grinder

On its booth at Blechexpo, Timesavers also displayed its multi-rotating brush machine for uniformly deburring, rounding edges and finishing parts. It processes laser, punched and machined parts, or parts with pockets/protrusions in all directions. The rotating carousel, equipped with eight left and right rotating abrasive flap brushes, processes the parts in all directions, not matter how fine the edges of the work-pieces. The company also showed ISMR its flexible manual grinder.

“Our manual grinder is a cost-effective alternative to conventional grinding and deburring. Its head, consisting of a grinding wheel and a flap brush, is tiltable by about 180˚. The head can easily be moved on the workpiece by the stabilized arm, resulting in a uniformly rounded edge, and even smaller parts can be processed,” explained van de Velde.

“Finding the right wide belt grinder and/or deburring machine for your metal working factory is not only a financial investment, but an investment into the quality of product that your factory produces,” she added.
