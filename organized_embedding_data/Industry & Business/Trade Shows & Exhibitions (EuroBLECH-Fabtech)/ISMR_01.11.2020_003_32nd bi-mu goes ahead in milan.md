# 32nd BI-MU goes ahead in Milan

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

At the fieramilano Rho exhibition grounds in Milan (Italy), Saturday 17 October was the last day of the 32.BI-MU exhibition, dedicated to machine tools, robotics, automation, digital and additive manufacturing, as well as auxiliary technologies. The event took place during the health emergency.

“Over 9,000 visits were registered over the four exhibition days. This is a satisfactory number, in line with the expectations for an event that took place during the health emergency, but fully respecting all rules to contain the spread of COVID-19,” the show organiser told ISMR.

Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers’ association, and organised by EFIM-ENTE FIERE ITALIANE MACCHINE, 32.BI-MU obtained Compliance Certification (released by ICIM) on conformity, with safety protocols to combat and contain the spread of COVID-19.

Barbara Colombo, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, stated: “Particular care was taken over the definition of all organisation details concerning safety, to further reassure attending operators throughout the four days of the event. 32.BI-MU was the very first trade fair to obtain compliance certification. We think that this encouraged visitors to attend the exhibition, and not just Italian operators.”

Of the total visitors, 3.5% were overseas visitors representing 27 countries. Germany, Switzerland, France and Austria were the most represented countries. Student attendance was limited due to new Italian Government directives communicated just before the opening of the event.

With over 350 exhibiting companies, 30% of whom were from overseas, two exhibition halls and a varied technological product offering, 32.BI-MU also included a full programme of meetings focused on topical issues of interest for the manufacturing industry. About 50 events were held in the BI-MUpiù arena. Attended by an average of 20 people, but with peaks of 90 attending guests, speeches were followed through the live streaming service (activated to extend promotion and communication for exhibitors beyond public visitors to the trade show). Over one thousand users followed the conferences remotely.

The BI-MUpiùAdditive area, organised by AITA-Italian association of additive technologies, showcased industrial applications for additive manufacturing, with components for automotive, aerospace and biomedical sectors. A particular highlight was BI-MUpiùDigital, a demonstration area set up in Hall 11 to highlight the potential of the digital factory.

Through BI-MUonline, the event continues beyond the end of the exhibition. As a virtual version of the real trade show, BI-MUonline will feature the technological products offered during the exhibition on the BIMU website. Available from the beginning of November until the 2022 show, the virtual exhibition offers videos, video clips and interviews directly from the stands.

The next edition of BI-MU will take place at fieramilano Rho in Milan, Italy, from 12-15 October 2022. n
