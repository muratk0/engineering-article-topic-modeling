# RUSSIA CALLING

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“The 2011 exhibition attracted 150 exhibitors from 22 countries as well as 4050 trade visitors”

BLECH Russia is a key trade exhibition aimed exclusively at the sheet metal industry in Russia and showcases international suppliers of machines, tools and services with a sales and marketing platform to access the Russian market, as well as to expand market share and introduce new product innovations.

Scheduled to take place at the Lenexpo Exhibition Centre in St. Petersburg from 12 – 14 March 2013, the exhibition profile encompasses all core sectors of the sheet metal working process: sheet metal; finished and semi-finished products; forming; separation and joining technology; flexible sheet metal working; machine elements; handling; surface treatment as well as tools and dies.

“This biannual show attracts attendance from production specialists, buyers, designers and quality controllers in the sheet metal working industry. Visitors are industry professionals from key industries including the automotive industry and its suppliers; mechanical engineering; sheet metal products and components (white and brown goods); iron and steel production; steel and aluminium construction; rolling mills and, of course, ship- building,” the organiser, Restec-Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

Currently, 122 exhibitors from 14 countries have confirmed their attendance at BLECH Russia 2013 in St Petersburg, one of the fastest growing industrial regions in Russia.

## Russia in focus

“In comparison to other economic regions, Russia is looking forward to above-average economic growth, originating in high domestic investment activities together with growing domestic demand. Ongoing modernisation of industry and infrastructure requires continuous investment; high import duties for ready-made products are an incentive for domestic production offering countless opportunities and a high demand for international sheet metal technology,” Restec-Brooks Exhibitions explained.

St. Petersburg, with its 4.6 million inhabitants, is the second-largest city in Russia and a key business centre. The Russian government is investing heavily in the region, aiming to turn the city into the country’s foremost international traffic hub, trade and technology centre by 2025. Numerous international companies have already established successful branches or production units in the area, influenced by favourable economic condition in the special economic zones created around St. Petersburg which offer discounted tax and customs conditions, reduced administrative procedures as well as discounted property prices for companies new to the area.

St. Petersburg is internationally known as an automotive production centre with resident companies such as Toyota, General Motors, Nissan, Suzuki, Hyundai, Ford, Scania, MAN and Magna.

Next to the automotive industry, local manufacturing traditionally focuses on shipbuilding and has today grown to include emerging industries such as optical technologies, IT and innovative electronics, metal working and new environmental technologies.

## EVENT PREVIEW

## A proven profile

BLECH Russia 2011 was a great success: 150 Russian and international exhibitors from 22 countries together with 4050 trade visitors reflected the strong sheet metal industry interest in Russia. 93 per cent of visitors were from Russia, 4.5 per cent were from Europe with 2.3 per cent from other CIS countries. Key visitor sectors included metalworking; mechanical engineering; shipbuilding; steel and aluminium construction; sheet metal products, components, assemblies; automotive industry; rolling mills; iron and steel production; construction; heating/ventilation/air conditioning; machine tool construction; non-ferrous metal production; aerospace; precision engineering/optics and agricultural machinery.

The exhibitors demonstrated modern machine tools, software and the latest technologies for cutting, welding, bending, forming, fastening and stamping sheet metal. The exhibition featured well-known brands representing 21 countries over the world: ThyssenKrupp, Galika, Schuler, Metalforme, Corrada, Kjellberg, Rosenberger, Bystronic Laser, Abamet, CL Engineering, Robur and others.

Over three days, the trade show was visited by over 4,000 trade specialists working in sectors such as the automotive industry, railway engineering, shipbuilding, construction and metal structure manufacture, machine tool building, metallurgy, manufacture of ventilation and heating systems as well as home appliances.

BLECH Russia 2011 business events included the Assembly of Top Technologies dedicated to advanced technologies in sheet metal working. Experts discussed the most current issues concerning the manufacture of high quality material for the automotive industry, the development of new methods of contact welding for automotive body sheet, modern laser technologies etc. The business programme also included corporate presentations from exhibitors demonstrating their latest achievements in sheet metal production, processing and process quality control.

One important element of the programme was a working meeting by Mikhail Oseevskiy, Vice Governor of St. Petersburg, Head, St. Petersburg Governor’s Administration and participants from industrial clusters in St. Petersburg. The meeting was attended by representatives of General Motors, Hyundai Motor, Bosch, Magna and others. The most pressing industry issues and problems were announced for St. Petersburg government authorities to comment. A business seminar entitled “Doing business in Russia: legal and tax aspects” was organised for the overseas participants of BLECH Russia 2011. ■
