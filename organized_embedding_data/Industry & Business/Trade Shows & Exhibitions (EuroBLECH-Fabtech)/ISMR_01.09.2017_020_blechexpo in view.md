# BLECHEXPO IN VIEW

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

Over the next few pages, ISMR previews selected highlights from the 13th Blechexpo and 6th Schweisstec exhibition that will take place at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre from 7-10 November 2017

A wide selection of thermal and mechanical sheet metal, profile and pipe processing innovations plus thermal and mechanical welding, joining and fastening technologies will be showcased at the 13th Blechexpo and the 6th Schweisstec exhibition.

The Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal processing and the Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology are set to take place in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2017) and have so far registered 15% growth in comparison to the last event. More than 1300 exhibitors from nearly 40 countries are expected to occupy around 1,130,000 square feet of overall exhibition floor space.

According to project manager Georg Knauer, of event organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, both Blechexpo and Schweisstec are demonstrating strong growth rates from German and overseas exhibitors, as well as occupied booth floor space: "During and shortly after the event in 2015, initial bookings were taken and registration is still booming today. At our current level of overall exhibition floor space, we'll be setting a new record. And this certainly won't be the end of the story, because all of the available halls at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre are nearly full as well. We hope to fill the entire trade fair centre for the first time. We are also attracting a growing number of manufacturers and distributors from outside Germany," he said.

## The sheet metal process sequence

Blechexpo and Schweisstec offer a "process sequence" trade fair concept. Visitors are presented with worldwide product and service offerings to set up the production and processing of components, modules and devices made of sheet metal, pipe and profiles. The complementary Schweisstec trade fair supplements and extends the process sequence for sheet metal, pipe and profile processing with thermal and mechanical welding, joining and fastening technologies.

"This results in numerous synergies for the visitors, because they're provided with the opportunity of looking beyond their own horizons. Blechexpo and Schweisstec showcase all of the hardware and software required for the efficient production of sheet metal, pipe and profile components, as well as the modules and devices made from them, ranging from materials warehousing, parts processing and automated assembly joining technology, right up to surface finishing," added the organiser
