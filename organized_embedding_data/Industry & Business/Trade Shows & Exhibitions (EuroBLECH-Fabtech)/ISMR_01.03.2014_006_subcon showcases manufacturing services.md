# SUBCON showcases manufacturing services

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

SUBCON, the UK’s only dedicated national exhibition for contract and subcontract manufacturing, returns to the NEC from 3-5 June 2014. This year’s event is set to be the biggest yet, with over 300 subcontractors exhibiting and stronger than ever participation by UK exhibitors. The show has built strong bonds with the associations and institutions representing manufacturers.

Leading trade associations supporting Subcon include the Confederation of British Metalforming; the Association of Industrial Laser Users; the Engineering Industries Association; the Institute of Cast Metal Engineering; the British Turned Parts Manufacturers Association and the Institute of Cast Metal Engineering.

A well-established annual event, Subcon covers every aspect of outsourced manufacturing services – from design and prototyping to machining, casting, moulding, fabrication, composites, finishing and testing. A significant number of the exhibitors will be showcasing their sheet metal fabrication, metal forming, press-working and profiling capabilities. New for this year is the Subcon Electronics Zone – building on the growing number of contract electronic manufacturers at the show and providing a focus for future growth.

Subcon will also host The Engineer Conference, a high-level event focused on innovation and design in manufacturing, which will also include the popular Subcon Seminar programme. Top speakers already lined up include Dr Hamid Mughal, Director of Manufacturing at Rolls-Royce plc and Steven Barr, Head of the MAS (The Manufacturing Advisory Service).

“Subcon has doubled in size in the last five years and is set to attract over 4000 visitors in 2014,” the organiser, Centaur Exhibitions, told ISMR. See www.subconshow.co.uk ■
