# EVENT PREVIEW

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

"We are returning to the ITM fair with our cyclical MM Speakers Corner event. We have changed the formula of our industry talks to make them even more attractive. Every show day, show visitors will be able to listen to interesting conversations on the most current topics in Polish industry. We will analyse the current situation in the Polish and global industry, as well as discuss the greatest challenges faced by Polish companies," emphasised Paweł Kruk, editor-in-chief of MM Magazyn Przemysłowy monthly.

## Live test zones

A new highlight of the trade fair will be special test zones for specific Industry-4.0 automation solutions. This is where visitors can check live products and technologies supporting production processes in factories.

"Show participants will learn new skills and can also take advantage of the opportunity to test solutions that can significantly improve their company efficiency. Our idea is to enable the initial 'tailoring' of a product to a specific recipient. The show is a good place to connect producers of such solutions with their potential customers, who will be able to test them in our spaces and see whether such innovations offer the appropriate support for their companies," added Anna Lemańska-Kramer.

The trade show will also feature extensive exhibits of robotics as well as foundry, welding and surface treatment systems plus software for the industrial sector. A new highlight of the trade fair will be special test zones for specific Industry-4.0 automation solutions.

## Subcontracting 2024

The Subcontracting 2024 fair, which runs alongside the ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE exhibition, is a meeting place for subcontractors and industrial partners from Poland and overseas.

"It offers visitors the opportunity to establish long-term business relationships, acquire new customers and gain a competitive advantage. For four days, the exhibition will present solutions and offers from companies with spare production capacity, technological and human resources potential, offering services in metal processing and industrial solutions. Lectures aimed at buyers are also planned, providing expert knowledge on improving supply chains," explained the show organiser.

An integral element of the trade fair is the opportunity for two-day subcontracting meetings. Visitors can plan them using a special online platform, where they can read the profiles of entrepreneurs and then directly arrange 30-minute meetings.

"Subcontracting 2024 is a three-level communication platform for industrial clients and subcontractors. In addition to the traditional trade fair exhibition, we also enable contact between contractors before the trade fair. A few weeks in advance, our exhibitors describe their production capabilities and interested buyers can make arrangements with them via our system. This saves time and the trade fair becomes more effective," added the MTP Group.

Another element is a programme in which experts are invited to share their knowledge in purchasing, production, logistics and warehouse management. This attracts not only specialists, but also management staff.

"As part of Subcontracting 2023, 62 companies from six countries (Finland, Spain, Lithuania, Germany, Poland and Ukraine) presented their subcontracting offers, while as many as 100 participants from 15 countries discovered the powers of networking during cooperative meetings. We are not slowing down and, this year, we want more," emphasised Robert Męcina, director of the Subcontracting trade show.

## Modernlog and Smart Warehouse

The June event is also a meeting place for experts from the logistics, warehousing and transport industries. At the Modernlog trade show, visitors will see modern technologies and innovative solutions for logistics as well as being able to take part in industry meetings and good practice exchanges.

"For several years now, a permanent feature of the Modernlog programme has been the Smart Warehouse Conference, attracting a group of professionals including representatives of companies known and respected in Polish and overseas markets. EUROPA SYSTEMS; Sealed Air; GAWRONSKI; WDX; LOGISYSTEM and Bekuplast have already confirmed their participation this year," added the MTP Group.

The conference in 2024 focuses on supporting warehousing processes and the latest future technologies, emphasising practical solutions for representatives of the logistics industry using the potential of modern tools. Experts will 'take a closer look' at, among others, topics such as:

■ Digitisation and IoT – support in warehousing processes.
■ Smart Warehouses – Automation and Transformation
■ Robots and Technologies of the Future
■ Fulfilment e-Commerce – Growing Needs, New Challenges.

The conference will also open with a panel entitled “Managers Facing the Challenges of Future Logistics."
