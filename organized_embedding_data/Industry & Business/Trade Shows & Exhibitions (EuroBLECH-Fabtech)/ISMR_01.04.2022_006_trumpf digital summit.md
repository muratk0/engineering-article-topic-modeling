# TRUMPF Digital Summit

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

Following the success of its virtual BlechExpo Innovation Days last year, sheet metal specialist TRUMPF is repeating the formula on Wednesday 6 April 2022 with its Digital Summit. It will focus on nine topics, each of which will occupy a 30-minute presentation slot across the day.

Technology experts in each field will address current challenges, introduce the latest TRUMPF solutions and the opportunities that they present. As well as the live talks, there will be Q&A sessions for in-depth professional exchange. All will be conducted in English.

The main highlights will include entry-level machines for laser cutting to bending; optimising production processes with integrated cutting and bending; an introduction to a new shopfloor software solution for sheet metal processing; automated panel bending; smart production with tailored solutions and automated welding.

A highlight of the day will be the Keynote speaker, Dr. Peter Leibinger (TRUMPF's Chief Technical Officer), who will provide insights on the company's latest research into quantum computing for sheet metalworking.

For more information and to register, see https://digital-events.trumpf.com/en-EN/innovation_insights.
