# Taiwan calling

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

TIMTOS x TMTS 2022, Taiwan's 'largest and only co-branded machine tool show', will be held at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre (Halls 1 and 2) from 21-26 February 2022. Around 950 exhibitors with 5,200 booths are expected.

"It will not only be the largest trade show in Taiwan since the outbreak of the pandemic, but one of the most significant events for the global machine tool industry in early 2022," the show organiser told ISMR.

Aside from metal cutting machine tools, machining centres and metalforming machine tools, the TIMTOS x TMTS 2022 joint event will also showcase a line-up of smart machines. Smart machinery is part of the Taiwanese government's "five plus two" innovative industries plan.

In response to travel restrictions, TIMTOS x TMTS 2022 will also launch various online services and activities in conjunction with the physical exhibition, including an on-site guide for online visitors; Live Tour @ Showground; Live Stage for product launches; virtual displays and one-on-one online procurement meetings.

Taiwan, which is ranked as the world's ninth-largest manufacturing nation and the fifth-largest machine tool exporter, is a major producer in the machine tool industry with about 1,950 companies based on the island and over 43,000 people employed in the sector.■
