Generous Donors and Bidders Help NBT Raise $24,700 to Support Manufacturing Students

The 2018 FMA Annual Meeting in Scottsdale, Ariz., was the site of the most successful Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT) silent auction to date. Conference attendees had the opportunity to outbid each other on a collection of interesting items and unique experiences during the opening welcome reception on March 7 at the Talking Stick Resort.

The auction raised a record $24,700 to benefit the scholarship and manufacturing camp programs of NBT. Online bidding and Buy-It-Now features allowed auction participants to place bids online for weeks before the live auction on-site.

A few notable items kept the bidding momentum going throughout the night, such as a hot air balloon ride across the Arizona landscape or through the scenic hills in northeast Wisconsin. Other experiences donated to the auction included pit passes at NASCAR, golfing at Medinah Country Club, a pheasant hunting trip in Iowa/South Dakota, and tickets to a Green Bay Packers game at Lambeau Field.

Verify and Validate Technical Skills With FMA Certificate Courses

FMA’s certificate programs help employers and employees verify and validate skills through training and on-the-job experience. These in-person courses cover a specific machine or process in two days or less, and include certificate testing on completion. These courses are valuable for all levels of experience, as well as cross-training of individuals in related disciplines.

NEW — Safety Fundamentals for Metals Manufacturing

The new Safety Fundamentals for Metals Manufacturing class, developed by national safety experts and industry leaders, is your go-to primer for key regulations, recommended standards, and best practices. The two-day certificate course is based on the FMA/CNA Safety & Loss Control Manual and includes guidance and instruction on:

Facility Safety

Safety Documentation

Personnel Safety Accountability/Employee Engagement

This comprehensive course is geared toward newly designated safety reps, experienced personnel looking for a refresher on current procedures, and teams seeking expert guidance on company safety programs.
