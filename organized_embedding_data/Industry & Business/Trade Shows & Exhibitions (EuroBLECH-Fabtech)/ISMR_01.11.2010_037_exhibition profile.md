As with previous years, Machine Tool Indonesia will be the biggest component of the show. Market leaders and leading Indonesian distributors including Mori Seiki, Mitutoyo, First Machinery, Trumpf, Ficep, Multi Mayaka and Yakin Maju exhibited last year alongside national/ regional groups from Austria, China, Germany, Korea, Singapore, Taiwan and the UK. Last year, for the first time, Manufacturing Indonesia welcomed a large number of companies from India as part of its colocation with INDEE 2009, the Indian Engineering Exhibition organised by the EEPC India [formerly known as Export Promotion Council of India]. Selected as a Partner Country, INDEE was organised at the most opportune time for India to foray into Indonesia as the bilateral agreements (FTA) between India and the ASEAN countries provided novel opportunities to Indian exporters. A total of 116 Indian exporters of engineering items participated in the exhibition to showcase their products and expertise to Indonesian buyers.

Over the last 30 years, Indian engineering has seen a major change. It is the largest segment in Indian industry and employs about 4 million skilled and semi-skilled workers. The huge growth powered by large investments in power projects and infrastructure development and the growth in India’s manufacturing sector has seen the development of cutting-edge capabilities in product development and advanced manufacturing technology.

Indonesia is India’s third largest trading partner in ASEAN and second largest export destination in ASEAN with a US$20 billion – bilateral trade target in the next five years. Engineering export constitutes 29% of India’s total export to Indonesia. Total trade between India and Indonesia has grown significantly to over US$ 9 billion during 2008-09 and engineering exports to Indonesia aggregates to US$ 554.3 during the same period.

The Taiwan Association of Machinery Industry, TAMI, will again be bringing a large number of companies to the exhibition. Plastics and Rubber and ProPak Indonesia will also again be held in conjunction with Manufacturing Indonesia.

## STRONG NUMBERS

Indonesia’s leading machinery trade exhibition, Manufacturing Indonesia 2009, was held on 2-5 December at the Jakarta Exhibition Centre, Kemayoran. The exhibition incorporated Machine Tool Indonesia, Industrial Automation Indonesia and Vehicle Manufacturing Indonesia. The exhibition occupied all halls at the exhibition venue, covering over 35,000 sq. m. of exhibition space.

Held within the Manufacturing Indonesia series of exhibitions, Machine Tool Indonesia is one of the largest and most important trade exhibitions of its kind in the market, consistently attracting over 35,000 qualified trade visitors and more than 1,400 participating companies. The 2010 exhibition will be held at the Jakarta International Expo Centre, Indonesia from 1-4 December 2010.
