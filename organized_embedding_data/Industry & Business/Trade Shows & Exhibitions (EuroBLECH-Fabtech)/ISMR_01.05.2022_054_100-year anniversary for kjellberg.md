# 100-year anniversary for Kjellberg

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

2022 is a very special year for the Kjellberg family. Exactly 100 years ago, on 4 April 1922, Kjellberg Elektro-Maschinen GmbH was established in Finsterwalde, Germany, by Oscar Kjellberg. Kjellberg Finsterwalde has been among the world's leading manufacturers of welding electrodes, welding techniques and plasma cutting systems for decades. Its portfolio also includes coaxial direct diode laser (through research company, OSCAR PLT GmbH).

Kjellberg's management surprised all staff members at their workplaces with individual birthday cakes along with personal words of thanks. On 25 October 2022, the 100th anniversary will be celebrated with its European customers and partners at the EuroBLECH trade fair in Hanover, Germany.

Kjellberg has set itself challenging goals for the future to remain successful. This includes, for example, the challenges connected with digitisation. Its latest products include conventional plasma cutting combined with HotWire, a patented method of cutting interrupted structures like gratings. The principle behind the method is indirect plasma cutting.

"With the HotWire process, different contours can be cut in any direction without additional preheating and without the use of a rotating head. Indirect plasma cutting offers a variety of possible applications. Depending on the power source, the user can choose between cutting currents of 280 A or 360 A. Cutting speeds of up to 1.1 m/min. are possible with good cutting quality," Kjellberg told ISMR.■ https://www.kjellberg.de/de/historie.html
