Demonstrating the Future

What does the future hold for metal manufacturing? FABTECH® supplies the answer to that question Nov. 11-13, 2014, at the Georgia World Congress Center (GWCC) in Atlanta. From the latest trends and end-to-end solutions in metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing to the industry’s leading education and peer-to-peer networking, this year’s event provides the perfect opportunity to capitalize on the future. Attendees can find the answers and know-how they need for the challenges of tomorrow at FABTECH.

More on the Show Floor

North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing event continues to break records each year by offering visitors the latest and greatest innovations the industry has to offer. Attendees can look forward to more than 1,400 exhibitors showcasing 14 million tons of equipment, pushing the expansion of the show to over 600,000 nsf of floor space and into three halls at the GWCC. Over 100 technical and management sessions are available, with topics covering cutting, forming and fabricating, finishing, management, stamping, tube and pipe, and welding. Plant Tours and Networking Opportunities Highlight These May FMA Workshops

PlateFab Workshop & Tours – May 6-7, 2014 - Hampton, Va. Plant tours for this workshop are of Newport News Shipbuilding, for 127 years the nation’s leading builder of U.S. Navy aircraft carriers and submarines, and Liebherr Mining Co. (pictured), known for fabricating and assembling some of the world’s largest dump trucks.

The workshop portion covers instruction on the latest plate fabrication techniques for handling, cutting, bending, and welding. Attendees will learn how to get more flexibility from their equipment, maximize the use of material to reduce scrap, and gain practical solutions to challenges from facilitated roundtable discussions with industry peers.

Registration is $495 for FMA/TPA Members and $645 General.

LeanFab Workshop & Tours – May 28-29, 2014 – Farmington, Conn.

Plant tours for this workshop are of TRUMPF Inc., ebm-papst, and Legrand Wiremold. Attendees will see firsthand how an OEM, a fan and motor manufacturer, and an electrical and information system specialist have implemented continuous improvement to improve their business and manufacturing processes.

The workshop portion of the program includes presentations, case studies, and roundtable discussions on how to better apply, implement, and sustain lean manufacturing. Attendees will return to their operation armed with best practices and practical resources that will take their operation to the next stage in the lean journey.

Registration is $495 for FMA/TPA Members and $695 General.

Educators and Employers Meet to Solve Skilled-labour Chanllenges at T.E.A.M Summit

Diane Thielfoldt

Leadership are cornerstones of this year’s

T.E.A.M. Summit

, where technology educators from colleges and trade schools will join the hiring and training specialists from fabricating and manufacturing companies to learn from each other about methods that work in recruiting, hiring, and training manufacturing personnel. The conference takes place May 19-21, 2014.

The shortage of skilled machine operators and shop floor personnel remains a serious concern, but now it’s also about finding and grooming next-generation leaders for the supervisory and management roles that baby boomers currently fill. T.E.A.M. Summit 2014 will address both of these areas of concern for employers.

Speakers

The conference welcomes keynote speaker Diane Thielfoldt, co-founder of The Learning Café. Her presentation on Four Generations in the Workplace and how to motivate and develop young Gen Y and Gen Z employees will lead into a panel discussion of real-life challenges being faced in several FMA member job shops as current company owners work to transition their leadership to a new generation.

Featured speaker Mike Lindsey, manager of human resources for Continental Corp. in Newport News, Va., will focus on the difference bet- ween technicians and technologists and why modern manufacturing facilities need both. Jason Scales, welding education specialist for Lincoln Electric, will present on new approaches to developing learning systems and the role for manufact- urers in this process. A full schedule of speakers and topics can be found at

fmanet.org/teamsummit2014

.

Location

The 2014 summit is being hosted by Ogden-Weber Applied Technology College in Ogden, Utah, an FMA Certified Education Center that operates on one of the most unique and forward-thinking program models in the country. Conference attendees will have an opportunity to learn firsthand how their competency-based programming provides trained employees for local employers in a timely manner while being cost-effective for the learners. It offers many ideas that could be implemented elsewhere around the country. In addition to a tour of facilities and a pre- sentation on methodology from system level to student level, participants will also tour Barnes Aerospace and JD Machine.
