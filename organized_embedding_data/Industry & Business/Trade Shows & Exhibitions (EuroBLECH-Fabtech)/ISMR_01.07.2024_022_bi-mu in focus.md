# BI-MU IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

Don't miss the 34th edition of BI-MU in Milan, Italy, from 9-12 October 2024.

The 34th edition of BI-MU (the biennial exhibition of machine tools; robots; automation systems; digital and additive manufacturing; auxiliary and enabling technologies) will be held at Fieramilano Rho in Milan (Italy) from 9-12 October 2024.

Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association) and organised by EFIM-ENTE FIERE ITALIANE MACCHINE, it is managed and organised according to the principles of environmental, economic and social sustainability (with ICIM ISO 20121 certification).

Last held in 2022 around machines and production systems, BI-MU 2024 features a format based on eight exhibition themes to represent "all faces of innovation" (as described in its slogan). The exhibition's product displays will be complemented by a cultural, thematic insight, developed through a rich programme of meetings, arranged by the show organisers and exhibitors and hosted in the BI-MUpiù arena.

## Eight themes at BI-MU

The eight exhibition themes at BI-MU 2024 are listed below:

■RobotHeart: this area will be dedicated to robotics; automation; related technologies; systems and artificial intelligence for all manufacturing sectors.

■piùAdditive: this area will be focused on machines, materials and software for production using additive technologies.

■BI-MU Digital: centred on software, technologies for connectivity and data management, cybersecurity and sensors.

■Metrology and Testing: measurement instruments; testing machines; computer vision and quality control.

■Power4Machines: this area will offer mechatronic solutions, systems and components for power transmission.

■ Heat and Surface Treatments: showcasing machines, equipment and systems for surface and heat treatments.

■The Composites: A new exhibition area, this will spotlight manufacturers of composites and machines for processing them.

■Consulting and Certification: Consulting services for digitalisation; cybersecurity; sustainability and environment; and financial services.

In addition, a new project area (Education and Job) will aim to bring the world of education closer to companies during the event. It will encourage contact between professionals looking for new job opportunities and companies in the sector, which strongly need to rely on skilled, motivated young people trained to operate in the factories of the future.

Alfredo Mariotti, General Manager of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, stated: "BI-MU is the only trade fair in Italy, and not just in Italy, with such a longstanding history. In its 65 years of activity, it has proved able to illustrate the technological evolution of the industry, identifying in advance the trends and segments in the world of machines, characterised by the most interesting development dynamics."

The last edition of BI-MU in 2022 attracted approximately 50,000 visitor admissions.

## RobotHeart attracts key industry players

Less than five months before the second edition of RobotHeart at BI-MU 2024, key players in the robotics industry have already confirmed that they will exhibit in the halls. These include companies such as ABB; ABL; Automazione; Alto Robotics; Comau; DCM; EWM; Fanuc Italia; IFM; KUKA; MA Robotica; Schunk; SIRI; Staubli; Tecnorobot and Universal Robots.

Alfredo Mariotti, General Manager, UCIMU.

"The satisfaction expressed by RobotHeart exhibitors at BI-MU in 2022," said Alfredo Mariotti, General Manager, UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, "has been confirmed by the high number of applications received to exhibit this October. To date, all the big players in the robotics sector have already confirmed that they will participate, to add to the quality of innovation on show. On this basis and considering the availability of Italian Government incentives for the acquisition of the latest technologies, we can only expect excellent results from BI-MU this year. We are confident that RobotHeart will enjoy even greater success than it did in the first edition.

"According to the latest official data from IFR (International Federation of Robotics), Italy ranked sixth in the world in 2022 for the number of robots installed. It had a 16% market share in the European Union and an 8% increase in installations, accounting for 11,475 units," concluded Alfredo Mariotti.
