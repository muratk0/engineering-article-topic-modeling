# Subcon co-locates with two shows

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

SUBCON, THE UK'S subcontract manufacturing supply chain show, has confirmed that it will return to the NEC in Birmingham for Subcon 2019 from 4-6 June 2019. Now in its 43rd year, Subcon is a key event for subcontract manufacturing professionals looking to source new products and partners.

"In a first for the show, Subcon will be co-located with the Advanced Manufacturing Show and The Engineer Expo. Over three days, the three events will showcase the complete manufacturing solution, covering design, prototyping and development, contract and subcontract products and services through to machine tools, systems and equipment.

The Engineer Expo is dedicated to the advanced engineering that drives UK manufacturing and design. Building on the calibre of the 2018 programme, 36 free-to-attend sessions will lift the lid on the latest industry developments and interesting engineering projects. Advanced Manufacturing Show is the global platform for next-generation technologies, showcasing innovative solutions that enhance advanced manufacturing performance.

All visitors will benefit from free access to all three events. For more details or to register your interest, please visit https://www.subconshow.co.uk/
