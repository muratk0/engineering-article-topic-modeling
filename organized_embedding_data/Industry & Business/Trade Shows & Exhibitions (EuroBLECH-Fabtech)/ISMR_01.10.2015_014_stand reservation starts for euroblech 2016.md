# Stand reservation starts for EuroBLECH 2016

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

STAND RESERVATION for the 24th International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, EuroBLECH 2016, is now in full swing by the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions. EuroBLECH 2016 will take place from 25 – 29 October 2016 at the Exhibition Grounds in Hanover, Germany.

“As the leading international trade exhibition for the sheet metal working industry, the coming show will be a platform for interlinked sheet metal working production. The Internet of Things, or Industry 4.0 as it is called when it refers to manufacturing production, poses big challenges for the sheet metal working industry and raises a multitude of questions. We are currently experiencing a revolutionary development in industry production and for manufacturers and suppliers of machines and systems - it is vital to be actively participating in this development. The exchange of expertise with users and the build-up of networks play a significant role for smart production, as the increased use of automation and software requires customised solutions that fit precisely into each individual networked fabrication plant,” explained Nicola Hamann, managing director of the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

“Manufacturers of machines, IT systems and tools for sheet metal working have to position themselves now, in order to rank first as a provider of expertise and innovation for this new era of industry production. EuroBLECH 2016 thus offers a unique opportunity to be involved in designing the future of sheet metal working,” she added.

An exhibitor brochure with detailed information about the exhibition and stand options is now available from the organisers. The revamped show website www.euroblech.com offers detailed information about the international event, such as an exhibition profile, facts and figures about the show, details for stand bookings, an interactive floor plan as well as video footage and photos from the previous event. With its modern responsive design, the new show website is user-friendly for desktop and mobile use.

“As in previous years, we see a large number of returning exhibitors at EuroBLECH 2016 and thus recommend all companies interested in exhibiting to reserve their stand spaces as soon as possible, to ensure that the company is placed within the relevant technology sector. We are delighted to say that we have already received numerous stand reservations and, therefore, might quickly run out of stand space in some halls,” explained Nicola Hamann.

EuroBLECH presents the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; handling; separation; forming; flexible sheet metal working; joining; welding; tube/section processing; surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; machine elements; quality control; CAD/CAM/CIM systems; factory equipment and R&D.

EuroBLECH 2014 attracted 1,573 exhibitors from 38 countries over a net exhibition space of 86,500m2 and 59,618 trade visitors from 105 countries. It ended with a 5 per cent growth in exhibitor numbers and a 3 per cent growth in floor space compared to the previous event. The exhibition was, once again, more international: 52 per cent of exhibitors and 37 per cent of visitors were from outside Germany. There was a substantial gain in visitors from Europe and North America. See www.euroblech.com

## BRILAMET research project comes to successful conclusion

THE SUCCESSFULLY concluded BRILAMET joint research project demonstrated that high-power diode lasers are suitable for the industrial-strength precision cutting of thick metal sheets (stainless steel with a thickness of 6.0mm). The project afforded LIMO an opportunity to enhance its ability to optimise beam-shaping systems for high-power lasers.

The BRILAMET (Brilliant High Power Diode Lasers for Metal Processing) joint research project was carried out between July 2012 and June 2015 as part of the German Federal Ministry of Education and Research programme entitled “KMU-Innovativ, Optische Technologien” (“optical technologies in innovative small and medium-sized enterprises”). During this period, the Laser Centre at the Münster University of Applied Sciences (LFM) teamed up with the Dortmundbased LIMO Lissotschenko Mikrooptik GmbH to conduct extensive experiments in precision cutting for thick metal sheets.

“Using lasers to cut sheet metal is something that has typically been handled in the realm of CO2 and fibre lasers, although this may not be the case for very much longer. Diode lasers are increasingly emerging as the light source of choice for a large number of applications, as can be seen from the BRILAMET research project that has just come to a successful conclusion,” commented LIMO.

Initially, the project involved the use of a 2.5kW high-power diode laser (HPDL) that was coupled to industrial CNC machining equipment. LIMO had used special microoptics to optically connect the HPDL beam source modules. Through an optical fibre, the HPDL beam was then guided to the cutting head, which was also developed by LIMO. LFM and LIMO employed the “Design of Experiments” (DOE) methodology to optimise the cutting process by experimenting with different nozzle geometries and diameters, as well as with other aspects. As the project progressed, the experiments were expanded to include a 4kW HPDL beam source, which likewise consisted of two laser units coupled together.

Among other things, the project showed that, although a high power diode laser operates at a relatively modest brilliance, it is well suited for the rapid and precise fine cutting of 6mm thick stainless steel (speed >2.0 m/min., roughness Rz < 30 µm).

“The key ingredient here is not only the asymmetrical laser beam geometry but also the model for predicting machining results, which was developed during the project,” explained Dr. Jens Meinschien, Vice President Innovations Management at LIMO. “By making further laser beam-shaping adjustments, cutting speeds of 2.5 m/min. can even be achieved with kW diode lasers.” But the experts at the Dortmund-based company were interested in more than just using HPDL beam sources to optimise high-precision laser cutting processes.

“Over the past three years of close cooperation with the laser specialists at the Münster University of Applied Sciences, we have acquired a great deal of additional expertise,” Dr. Meinschien noted. “We now know how to improve the components and, most importantly, the beam-shaping systems for kW beam sources using an adapted optical, mechanical and thermal design.”

LIMO has presented the results of the project at international trade fairs and conferences as well as in trade journals, and the findings have caught the attention of experts throughout the field. The Dortmundbased company is currently pursuing additional laser-cutting research alongside internationally renowned experts as part of the EU “Lashare/Nextcut” sub-project.

## 25 years for Minster

CELEBRATING 25 YEARS IN GERMANY, press specialist Nidec Minster Corporation continues to expand its global customer service and sales support network. Established in October 1990, Minster Germany began as a small, one-man sales office with two field servicemen to provide faster response times. Before that, most of the service support for Europe, the Middle East and Africa was dispatched from Minster U.S.A.

Since then, Minster Germany has expanded and networks with Nidec Minster’s service centres around the world to offer a full suite of servicerelated solutions. By 1997, Minster Germany was expanding with additional field service personnel, technical support operators available in the Central European Time Zone, and inventoried repair parts. As business expanded, the repair parts inventory located at the German facility increased tenfold.

In 2003, Minster Germany relocated to Halblech, sharing a major manufacturing facility with strategic partner Otto Bihler Maschinenfabrik. This provided Nidec Minster with component and press rebuild capabilities as well as improving response times for emergency repairs.

Today, Minster Germany dispatches field service personnel from six strategic locations in Europe. Earlier this year, Nidec Minster acquired Spanish press builder, Arisa.
