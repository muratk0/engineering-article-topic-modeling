# PRESSING FOR ACTION ISMR SAYS: “Today’s stamping solutions are more flexible than ever”

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

Press manufacturers are under constant pressure to provide solutions that offer greater versatility, efficiency and accuracy as well as crucial energy and cost savings. Processes that offer greater control over production and a more flexible range of parts, in different (sometimes lighter) materials, are the result of painstaking R&D programmes.

Today’s stamping solutions are more flexible than ever and here we present a selection of the latest new global stamping and pressing developments to highlight this. We also include some of the new press launches that you can see at this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover this October.

## New stamping developments

Built in Europe, ADIRA’s BlueBender press was inspired by the BlueCompetence concept, an initiative by the largest European manufacturers to maintain their production processes with an eye on sustainability, ecological and social responsibility.

“BlueBender is more accurate and boasts faster cycles than its hydraulic predecessors – from 0.01mm down to 0.005mm with no oil leaks, no pollution and less energy waste. In other words, it runs green – a simple mechanical system providing efficiency, accuracy and easier maintenance. It can also be integrated with a robot cell for material handling to give further cost savings,” ADIRA told ISMR.

ANDRITZ KAISER has spent decades of experience (over 8,000 presses delivered) and proven design principles to build presses with high press body rigidity, guidance accuracy and parallelism between press table and slide. It provides stamping and forming presses from 630 to 25,000kN. The table lengths range from 1,000 to 6,000mm. In addition to the classic eccentric drive, it also provides link drive systems and servo presses.

Swedish manufacturer AP&T develops, manufactures and markets complete production systems, automation, presses, tools and related aftermarket services for manufacturers of formed metal parts. It placed a strong focus on press hardening at the BLECH China 2014 exhibition in Suzhou this May. On 11 September 2014, AP&T will inaugurate its new development and production facility in Ulricehamn.

Its product portfolio includes automation equipment such as blank feeders, press robots and transfer feeders for weights of up to 200kg and lines for conveyor belt feeding with conveyor belt widths of up to 1,600mm. Hydraulic presses for punching, deep drawing, embossing, hydromechanical forming and hydroforming are also supplied as well as tools for tube and sheet forming, from punching to hydroforming. At this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition, visitors will get the chance to experience a 3D tour inside an advanced AP&T press hardening line.

Spanish manufacturer, ARISA S.A., designs and distributes mechanical and servo presses from 1,600kN to 30,000kN, accessories for the manipulation and treatment of sheet metal as well as customised machinery and devices, automation and assembly of subsets with electric, hydraulic and pneumatic installations. The company has been in existence since 1940.

Avure Technologies, a leading provider of high-pressure Flexform™ pressing equipment, has introduced a new fluid cell press and a new hydroforming deep-draw press family. The new equipment is tailor-made to suit demands for low volume sheet metal fabrication. The hydroform/Flexform™ process requires just one rigid tool half, the other being a flexible rubber diaphragm, leading to significant tool cost savings.

At EuroBLECH 2014, Balconi Presse will showcase the latest technological versions of its high-precision mechanical presses and servo presses. Each machine is customised with technical solutions to suit customer needs.

Swiss manufacturer, BRUDERER, is a leading supplier of high-performance stamping presses. EuroBLECH 2014 will see the launch of its new fully automatic high performance stamping press, BSTA 510-150, with a toolloading area of 1500mm.

Chin Fong Machine Industrial Co., Ltd. is a key provider of mechanical power presses in Taiwan. It has accumulated a number of sales records for tandem press lines in the automotive industry since 1986 with Toyota. Over years of technical cooperation in Europe and Japan, it now manufactures an A0 press line up to 54,000kN with automation equipment. Its customers include Toyota, Ford, Honda, Yamaha and Nissan.

At the TIMTOS exhibition last year, it showcased its straightside, 2-point, direct drive servo presses; energy-saving, link motion servo presses; semi-warm hot forging presses and knuckle presses developed for forging thick sheet metal, as well as variable-slide stroke, single crank power presses.

DEES HYDRAULIC was established in 1976 as a manufacturer of hydraulic presses in Taiwan, offering safe and simple processes for use in the sheet metal working environment. With press capacities from 10 to 6,000 tonnes, it has ISO 9001 and CE accreditation and exports to over seventy countries. Its main products include deep drawing presses; die spotting presses: press brakes and related hydraulic presses. Customers include Bosch-Siemens, Electrolux, Ariston, Audi, Mercedes-Benz and Honda.

ebu Umformtechnik focuses on the development of large presses and servo drive technology. Innovative drive concepts are integrated into ebu servo presses e.g. the highperformance, double servo torque drive for press forces of over 1,000 tons. High dynamics and increased application rates require powerful motors with top outputs of >3.2MW. The company also supplies an intelligent energy management system with kinetic energy accumulators and capacitor banks.

For more than 50 years, S. DUNKES has focused on press technology. Hydraulic drives, hydraulic speed-controlled servo direct drives and electro-mechanical servo spindle drives are amongst its products. DUNKES press systems are built to customer requirements and are suitable for blanking/forming and assembly processes, internal/external high-pressure forming, massive forming, press hardening and straightening tasks.

Italian manufacturer, Galdabini SpA (founded in 1890), specialises in hydraulic deep drawing presses, material testing machines and automatic and manual straightening machines for steel and aluminium forming.

Gigant Italia Srl produces several families of hydraulic presses, designed in different power ranges from a minimum of 400 up to 25,000kN, for high-rate, deep drawing processes in several industrial application areas for aluminium and stainless steel parts (e.g. automotive, house ware, cookware and others). It can offer handling systems for metal sheets in coils (such as a decoiling/straightening unit) positioned at a side entrance of the presses. Alternative feeding systems may be automatic-piece loaders where, in lieu of coils, disks or square-shaped metal sheets are fed into the presses.

Hydraulico develops and builds hydraulic presses for deep draw, cold and hot forging, blanking and stamping, R&D etc. The hydraulic presses are used to produce items such as wheelbarrows; suitcases; containers; sinks; bathtubs; automotive parts; gas cylinders; pots and pans; window fitting; rails etc. The press capacities range from 100 to 5,000 tonnes and presses are all tailor-made to customer specifications. In addition, Hydraulico offers complete press upgrades and preventative press maintenance for any press brand.

Heilbronn Group offers a broad spectrum of press technology for the packing, pharmaceutical and sheet metal processing markets from single machines up to complete production lines. It focuses on ‘automotive and stamping’, for which it manufactures O-Presses, C-Presses or complete blanking lines, on ‘Packaging’ in the production of lines for manufacturing shells and ends as well as on special solutions for the pharmaceutical market.
