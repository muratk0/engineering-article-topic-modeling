## Exhibition organisers

The organisers of this new exhibition are CECIMO and EASTPO. Since its creation in 1950, CECIMO has committed itself to the promotion of the machine tool industry worldwide and a European machine tools exhibition was created for this reason in 1951. It was organised for the first time under the name of EMO® in 1975 in Paris. EMO® is a registered trademark by CECIMO. It receives the support of its 15 CECIMO member associations which represent and promote the interest of approximately 1,500 European companies, accountable for 99 per cent of the European production of machine tools and 31 per cent of the world production of machine tools.

More than three quarters of CECIMO’s production is shipped abroad, whereas half of it is exported outside Europe. For more information, see www.cecimo.eu

Shanghai EASTPO Culture Development Co., Ltd is a leading exhibition company in China, specialising in providing quality platforms for trade and information exchange for the mechanical industries. It has been the organiser of the Shanghai International Machine Tool Fair (EASTPO) since 1999. The show is well-known among machinery industry exhibitors and buyers. For more information, see visit www.eastpo.net

MP Organisation Pte Ltd is a fully owned subsidiary of the MP International group. Established in 1987, MP specialises in organising exhibitions, conferences and special events. The group has organised and managed over 1,000 national, regional and international events, including the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) 2009 Leaders’ Week. It is a leading international organiser of trade exhibitions, including ITMA and ITMA ASIA. MP is headquartered in Singapore with a global footprint across Asia and Europe (www.mpinetwork.com). ■
