# Metal meets medical

[TARİH: 01.04.2013 ISMR]

DOCTORS, medical technology producers, machinery manufacturers and researchers met at the second “Metal meets Medical” conference in Frankfurt am Main on 11 April 2013. It was hosted by the VDW (German machine tool builders association), Frankfurt am Main, with the Institute for Production Management, Technology and Machine Tools (PTW) at Darmstadt University.

“With a volume of around 300 billion euros and still rising, the market for medical technology is growing dynamically. According to our latest customer structure survey, the machine tool industry is delivering about two per cent of its production output to this customer grouping. The total value of machines sold has tripled over four years up to 2011 alone which means the rapidly expanding market for medical technology is now much more than an insider’s tip,” said VDW.

“The order of the day in medical technology is high quality customisation at affordable prices,” added Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, executive director of the VDW. “At our conference, top-ranking experts discussed how the principles of series production can also upgrade potential in a highly individualised market.”

The event was themed around the requirements of doctors and medical technology producers, and showcased solutions with various machining and metrological processes used in manufacturing prostheses, in dental technology and implant production. ■
