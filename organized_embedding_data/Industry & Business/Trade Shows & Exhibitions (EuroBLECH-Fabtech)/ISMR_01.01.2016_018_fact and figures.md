# Fact and Figures

“Turkey is one of the world’s largest markets with a population of 78 million and a labour force of 29 million. Half of the population is under the age of 30. Turkey has the highest youth population and fourth largest labour force compared to EU-27 countries,” said the show organiser, Hannover Messe Bilesim Fuarcılık A.Ş.

“Planning to achieve US$ 100 billion worth of exports, the machine industry plays a major role in Turkey’s future, as the country aims to achieve an export volume of US$ 500 billion by 2023 and rank among the top ten economies worldwide. Known as the metal processing and metal forming exhibition of Eurasia, the WIN Eurasia Metal Working exhibition offers a compact view on the metal processing operations’ chain together with surface processing technologies.”

“Turkey has risen to become Europe’s largest home appliances’ manufacturer. Turkey’s largest white goods export market is Europe which is led by the UK, France and Germany. Turkish brand Beko is the second largest white goods brand in Western Europe, and one of Europe‘s fastest growing large home appliance brands. Turkey is also the largest commercial vehicle and second largest bus manufacturer in Europe and the 16th biggest motor vehicle producer in the world. Today, there are 17 companies, including Fiat, Honda, Hyundai, Renault and Toyota, Mercedes-Benz and M.A.N, manufacturing various types of vehicles in Turkey. Turkey also provides automotive parts for brands such as GM, Mercedes, BMW, Opel, Toyota, Fiat and Ford. Turkey is also the number one TV manufacturer in Europe,” added Hannover Messe Bilesim Fuarcılık A.Ş.

Turkey, in effect, has access to 1.5 billion customers in Europe, Eurasia, the Middle East and North African markets with a total of US$25 trillion GDP.

## Strong support

Last year’s WIN Eurasia Metal Working event was held from 12-15 February 2015 and brought together three international trade fairs (Metal Working Eurasia, Surface Treatment Eurasia and Welding Eurasia) under a single roof. The exhibition was organized over an area of 16,953 net sq. m. and 622 companies from Austria; Bulgaria; China; Denmark; France; Hungary; Italy; Germany; South Korea; The Netherlands; U.A.E; UK and Turkey exhibited their products at the event. Approximately 29,016 visitors came from Europe, North Africa, Near and Central Asia and the Gulf states besides Turkey.

Germany, Italy and South Korea each took country pavilions. WIN Eurasia Metal Working 2015 attracted visitors from the Middle East, Europe and other regions. In cooperation with the Turkish Chambers of Commerce and other agencies, 46 delegations from 32 regions in Turkey visited the exhibition. International delegations also attended, under a “Hosted Buyer Programme” organized with the support of various trade ministries in 19 countries. ■
