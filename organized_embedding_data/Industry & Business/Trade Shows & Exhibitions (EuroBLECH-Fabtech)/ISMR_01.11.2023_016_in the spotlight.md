# IN THE SPOTLIGHT

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

We report from the Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023 sheet metal and joining technologies trade fair which took place in Stuttgart, Germany, from 7-10 November 2023.

ISMR SAYS:

With visitors from 100 countries, 40,103 visitors and nine busy halls, Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023 was an international showcase for innovation and customer benefits in sheet metal and metal processing. Its central themes revolved around automation; sustainability; energy efficiency; skilled labour shortages, higher productivity; employee protection and customer service. The latest sheet metal, pipe and profile processing; cutting; joining; forming and welding technology developments were showcased at the event.

The trade show, held from 7-10 November 2023 in Stuttgart (Germany), offered practical insights and solutions for more efficient production; better material utilisation and higher part quality as well as reductions in energy consumption and CO₂ emissions. As the international media partner for the event, ISMR had a stand in Hall One and copies of its October issue were given out at the show entrances.

"With the goal of making steel production and sheet-metal processing more sustainable, Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023 provided significant impetus and demonstrated that even small businesses can successfully embrace automation. The winners of the best-Award 2023 impressively showcased what is possible with technical innovation and inventiveness," explained show organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.

## An innovation showcase

This key meeting place for global players in sheet metalworking takes place every two years at Messe Stuttgart. It featured a range of new products; world premieres; process expertise and innovation advances.

Around 45 per cent of the exhibitors were from outside Germany. The majority of overseas exhibitors came from Italy; Switzerland; Turkey; China and the Benelux countries. Visitors to the trade fair could see first-hand the steps taken towards achieving the sheet metal industry's sustainability and environmental goals.

"In individual dialogue between exhibitors and trade fair visitors, many forward-looking production projects were evaluated and valueadded investment projects were launched at Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023," explained the show organiser. "The trade fair duo presented complete process sequences for modern, future-proof, sheet-metal processing.

This key meeting place for global players in sheet metalworking takes place every two year at Messe Stuttgart.

An impressive supplementary programme included presentation of the 'best Award 2023', the 'Blechexpo Steel Convention 2023', the 'futureSteel' congress and an expert forum."

Sheet metal, pipe and profile processing technologies were in Halls 1, 3, 5 and 9. Halls 4 and 6 were reserved for stamping technologies. Cutting, joining and fastening technologies were presented in Hall 7. Hall 8 focused on pressing and forming technologies. Steel and metal services, as well as surface finishing technology, were addressed in Hall 10.

"The newly restructured and more clearly defined exhibition hall layout made visiting the trade fair more efficient for expert visitors. The range of topics covered by Blechexpo/Schweisstec included efficient process technologies for modern laser technology; fully automated robotic sheet metal processing machines; precision tools and systems for punching, bending, braking and forming; as well as innovative microwaterjet cutting technologies," added the show organiser.

The "Blechexpo Steel Convention 2023" took place in the forum area in Hall 10 on the first day of the trade fair and focused on "Challenges in the Steel Markets in 2023." The convention was organised by the Stahl-Kommunikation Agency. A “futureSteel" congress was also organised on the second day of the trade fair in cooperation with Marketsteel Media from Düsseldorf. A particular focus was on the challenge of transformation towards a climate-neutral steel industry.

The daily Exhibitor Forum was further expanded; speakers from exhibiting companies held presentations on all trade fair days at various fora aligned to the thematic structure of the exhibition halls for the first time at Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023. This included the Stamping Forum in Hall 4 (1FO-01), the Schweisstec Forum in Hall 7 (7515), the BEST Forum in Hall 9 (9315) and the Steel Forum in Kongress West. Additional special fora with the ZVEI, IBU and EFB associations were held on the third day of the trade fair.

## Award winners

Together with trade magazines Blechnet and MM Maschinenmarkt, event organiser Schall presented innovation awards at Blechexpo/ Schweisstec for the sixth time. This year, the "best-Award 2023" brand was introduced for this purpose, incorporating the trade fair slogan "Be part of the best".

Innovations submitted had to be completely new developments or further developments/ improvements that differ significantly from previous ones. The innovations had to be shown at the trade fair and not be prototypes. The evaluation criteria included degree of innovation; added value for the user; unique selling point of the respective solution and economic efficiency.
