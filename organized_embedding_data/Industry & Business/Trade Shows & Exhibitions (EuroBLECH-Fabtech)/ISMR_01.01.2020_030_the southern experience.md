# The Southern Experience

[TARİH: 01.01.2020 ISMR]

The Southern Manufacturing & Electronics Exhibition returns to Farnborough, UK, from 11-13 February 2020. As in previous years, the 2020 exhibition will welcome an exhibitor list from high-tech machine tools, Industry-4.0 solutions and workshop equipment to components and consumables, together with a similarly broad range of subcontract services. Firms from around the UK will be taking part, accompanied by a number of offshore exhibitors from across Europe, Asia and the Far East.

"Southern Manufacturing is a major fixture in the UK's annual engineering event calendar. The size of the show – we expect the 20,000m² Farnborough International to be fully sold out by the time doors open – means that many major vendors regard the event as one of their leading annual showcases – particularly so, given the importance of the region to the UK's £32bn aerospace manufacturing sector," the event organiser, European Trade & Exhibition Services, told ISMR.

"Not simply is it one of the largest exhibitions of its type in the UK, it is also the first big show of the New Year, offering those considering investing in new equipment the first chance to see and evaluate, side by side, the most recent offerings from numerous major manufacturers. This happy formula ensures that the variety, as well as the amount of hardware, on display is exceptional," it continued.

## A sheet metal focus

For those working in sheet metal, the show has a lot to offer. Sheet metal specialist, TRUMPF, returns to Southern Manufacturing after a two-year hiatus. Along with its range of machine tools and laser systems for flexible sheet and tube processing the company will be highlighting its Smart Services. Customers can order parts via an app, monitor the condition of a machine and act proactively for a smooth production process, TRUMPF told ISMR.

TRUMPF's range includes bending, punching, combined punch laser processes, 2D and 3D cutting applications and laser welding and it models itself as a single-source supplier of all relevant laser technologies for additive manufacturing. Fellow exhibitor, Bystronic UK, is another well-known brand synonymous with laser cutting, bending and automation systems, together with software designed to streamline the process chain, minimise waste and increase profit.

TLM Laser offers products from leading names in laser technology including Foba; IPG; Univet; Swisstec; Alphalaser; ULS; LasX; 4Jet and Bofa. On show at Southern 2020 will be products from FOBA and Alpha, emphasising FOBA's IMP (Intelligent mark positioning system) through lens vision, designed to reduce scrap and improve throughput by part recognition.

Workshop equipment on show will include tooling, measuring, workholding and essentials such as compressors, CAD/CAM and ERP software, storage and industrial air cleaners. Blum Novotest will be showcasing the latest software advances for its recently introduced Digilog probe series, designed to bring the integration and everyday use of fully automated solutions within reach of even modest workshops. Faro will highlight its recently launched 8-Axis Quantum ScanArm, which integrates an eighth axis into the company’s Quantum FaroArm to make it, so the firm claims, ‘the world’s first eight-axis portable co-ordinate measuring machine (PCMM)’. When combined with the company’s Blu Laser Line probe or Prizm Laser Line probe, it becomes a 3D laser scanning system.

ITC is a specialist tooling supplier and manufacturer of high precision cutting tools. The company supplies WIDIA products and is the UK distributor for all BIG KAISER products. Guhring will exhibit a number of new products for the first time, including its RF Speed milling range that has been developed for the machining of very tough materials.

E-Max Systems will highlight the latest additions to its ERP and MRP software, designed specifically for manufacturing and engineering businesses which provide make-to-order or discrete product manufacturing. The ERP software includes typical features such as CRM; estimation and quotation; sales order processing; production control (including MRP); work orders; job cards and routing; purchasing; quality control and traceability; warehouse management; shop floor data collection and financials. This year, several developments feature new financial functionality, including ‘Making Tax Digital’ and standard costing as well as a new ‘speeds and feeds’ calculator.

## Find a subcontractor

Southern Manufacturing is one of the key marketplaces for subcontract services, and the 2020 show will welcome many firms with expertise in the sheet metal sector.

Award-winning complex sheet metal fabricator, Universal Wolf, will be highlighting its work with brand-leading companies across a variety of sectors, from transport and robotics to security and defence.

Havant Sheet Metal is an ISO 9001-2015 accredited manufacturer of quality sheet metal components and fabrications in stainless steel, mild steel and aluminium. Its capabilities encompass fine limit sheet metal; welding; machining; tube forming; assembly; powder coating and stove enamelling.

Jenks & Cattell Engineering is amongst the most established metalwork companies in the UK offering laser cutting, fabrication, presswork and welded assemblies with manual and robotic welding facilities.

## The seminar programme

“The show’s free seminar programme covers a wide variety of technical, as well as commercial, topics and will appeal to anyone involved in engineering or manufacturing, from practical business counselling to a look at the latest advances in manufacturing technology. The programme for 2020 provides its usual high standard of presenters drawn from business, academia as well as commerce,” explained the show organiser.

A total of 33 sessions over three days have been added to date, including a detailed review of advanced manufacturing together with 3D printing; Industry 4.0; CE Marking; digital transformation in manufacturing; Lean and more. All the sessions are free of charge, although pre-booking is definitely recommended. A complete listing of sessions, as well as the online reservation form, is available at www.industrysouth.co.uk
