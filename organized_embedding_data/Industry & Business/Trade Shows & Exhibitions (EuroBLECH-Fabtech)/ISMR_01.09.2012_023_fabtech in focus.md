# FABTECH IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

The three-day FABTECH event in November offers products and services for metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing

ISMR SAYS: “More than 400,000 net square feet of floor space will be dedicated to live equipment demonstrations”

North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event heads to the Las Vegas Convention Centre, USA on 12-14 November 2012. The conference and exhibition brings buyers and sellers of metal forming and fabricating technologies together in an environment that facilitates and accelerates business relationships.

The conference will feature programmes on a variety of metal forming, fabricating and welding technologies and processes plus related business topics.

## Network for success

More than 1,100 leading manufacturers and 25,000 attendees will converge at the Las Vegas Convention Centre for FABTECH 2012. The three-day event is a convenient one-stop shop to source products and services for metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing. More than 400,000 net square feet of floor space will be dedicated to live equipment demonstrations, offering visitors the opportunity to see, touch and compare products side-by-side and find cost-saving solutions. Simultaneously, the FABTECH educational conference will offer insights and expert speakers who will discuss the hottest topics in manufacturing.

FABTECH 2011 shattered all records of previous shows, with the number of buyers walking the floor, educational conference attendance and the overall square footage of exhibit space. The four-day exhibition and conference at Chicago’s McCormick Place welcomed a historic 35,457 attendees; a 35 per cent increase over 2009, the last time FABTECH was held in Chicago. The show had more than 1,300 exhibitors, filling a record 522,000 net square feet and attendees from more than 80 countries.

“FABTECH is so much more than a trade show, it’s a year-round experience,” said John Catalano, show co-manager. “Attendees have the opportunity to see new products and new technologies in action and leave the expo with new ideas, new suppliers and new solutions for working smarter and becoming more competitive. They also join a network of other attendees that fosters knowledge and contacts needed to stay current with industry developments in the year to come.”

FABTECH’s annual expo comes as manufacturing continues to lead the American economy out of the recession. Manufacturers in the US added 12,000 jobs in May 2012, the eighth-straight month of gains in the sector. Since 2010, half a million manufacturing jobs have been created in the US. Offshored work is returning home and profits at manufacturing companies increased 25 per cent in 2011, said the show organiser.

“The slow but steady economic recovery makes attending FABTECH more important than ever for manufacturers who want to stay ahead of the competition and discover the latest technologies from around the world, forge partnerships, share ideas and, of course, connect with potential buyers and suppliers,” said Mark Hoper, show co-manager. “The connections made and knowledge shared at FABTECH will help fuel the engine behind the current economic recovery - the manufacturing sector.”

The five FABTECH co-sponsors represent a wide variety of expertise and include: the American Welding Society (AWS), the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA), the Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME), the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI).

The Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME) along with the Fabricators and Manufacturers Association (FMA) and American Welding Society (AWS) introduced FABTECH into Canada for the first time this March. The result was a success. Thousands of manufacturing professionals from around the country including Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba and Quebec attended FABTECH Canada to walk the show floor and source over 75 new products, assess the latest equipment, machines and technologies, learn from the varied educational conference sessions and meet with new and existing suppliers. The three-day exhibition and conference at the Toronto Congress Centre welcomed over 5,800 visitors and more than 200 exhibitors.

## Event highlights

FABTECH highlights include a special featured session on ‘Lean Manufacturing for Managers’ (Sunday 11 November, 1:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m.) This session will give an overview on lean methods that can be used to minimize all forms of waste and maximize value for the customer.

A ‘Manufacturers’ Executive Outlook’, on Monday 12 November, will give insights from leaders representing job shops, contract manufacturers and other service providers who are dealing with the same issues of supply, demand, labour and changing business sectors that affect survival in today’s economy. Join an interactive Q & A lead by Chris Kuehl, FMA Economist, to ask questions and offer your perspective on the future of the industry.

Check out the welding skills of the six top qualifying National Skills USA student welders.

The excitement heats up throughout the show as they compete for the top three Team USA spots in the 2012 Invitational Weld Trails taking place on the show floor.

A variety of networking events will also be held. To guide attendees to products and services easily, the show floor is organised into Pavilions that are geared toward specific industries and technologies. FABTECH will encompass both Central and North Halls at the Las Vegas Convention Centre. The Central Hall houses Pavilions dedicated to Forming & Fabricating, Laser, Metalforming, Tool/Die, Tube & Pipe and Finishing. The North Hall houses Pavilions dedicated to Welding and Thermal Spray.
