Experience how metal connects

our lives at FABTECH, held Nov. 9-12, 2015, in Chicago at McCormick Place. From action-packed exhibits to industry-leading educational sessions and networking opportunities, this event highlights the best of all your metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing operations.

As an industry, we make stuff. We provide safety, convenience, comfort, energy, and much more. The products you make improve daily life — and once a year you have full access to the people, processes, and solutions you need to make it all possible.

Learning opportunities

go beyond the show floor. The FABTECH Education Program features over 100 technical, operational, economic, and managerial sessions led by industry experts providing information on the latest advancements in metal manufacturing. Whether you’re a seasoned veteran or a newcomer to the industry, you’ll find ideas to help your operation: Form knowledge, fabricate solutions, weld relationships, and finish strong.

Whether you’re on the show floor visiting the 1,500 exhibiting companies or in the classroom, the processes and solutions to drive your productivity can be found at FABTECH. Visit

fabtechexpo.com

for complete event details and to register.

FABTECH

is brought to you by the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association Intl., SME, American Welding Society, Precision Metalforming Association, and Chemical Coaters Association Intl.

The national co-producers of Manufacturing Day — Fabricators & Manufacturers Association (FMA), National Association of Manufacturers (NAM), The Manufacturing Institute (MI), and NIST’s MEP program of the Department of Commerce — each year have produced more and more support tools and generated significantly more national media interest in this annual celebration of manufacturing.

This year it is a series of webinars for hosts and community organizers, including the community and technical colleges that have assumed leadership roles in many communities. The first host webinar was held June 8, and more than 250 companies participated. In mid-July a best practices webinar for colleges will be held to provide insights on all the ways a community or technical college can participate. It will be led by a panel of experienced educators who have shown their creativity in their previous events. Coming up in early August will be a best-practices host webinar with multiple experienced host companies sharing their experiences and suggestions.

"We want to make sure that all manufacturing companies — no matter how big or small — get inspired to participate in MFG DAY in a way that makes sense for them and their community," said Ed Youdell, president and CEO of FMA. "With the generous support of our many sponsors, the coproducer team is able to create these kinds of inspirational and practical event planning tools," he continued. "People who may have thought about doing a Manufacturing Day event but aren’t sure how to start or who to invite will get wonderful ideas through these webinars. Each webinar will be produced live and recorded for 24/7 access on

mfgday.com

."

Social media activity directed at the MFG DAY audience is also exploding this year. In addition to its presence on Facebook, Twitter, Google , and LinkedIn, the program can now be found on Instagram. "The old adage that a picture is worth a thousand words is especially true when it comes to Manufacturing Day," said FMA board member Teresa Beach-Shelow, president of Superior Joining Technologies Inc. in Machesney Park, Ill. "We still have an image problem in manufacturing, but this program is definitely providing the content to overcome the out-of-date images people may still have in their heads. And, when you see the interest expressed on the faces of the thousands of students who have gotten to tour manufacturing facilities on MFG DAY, those expressions tell you that our message is getting across and we are changing perceptions."

Tools for Human Resource Management

Retaining and rewarding top employees. Creating a culture that attracts the best and the brightest. Identifying the candidates with the right skills for the job. FMA recognizes that HR professionals have a tough job. It can be burdensome to gather the pertinent data — the facts that relate specifically to your company, your type of employees, and your employment situation — let alone to automate work and save administrative time. But it doesn’t have to be that way with resources that FMA offers!

Benchmark Compensation Package With "Salary/Wage & Benefit Survey"

For 20 years FMA has collected anonymous salary/wage and benefit data for the industry. Survey results offer a thorough look at compensation for 67 production and administrative positions. Results are broken down by demographic criteria, allowing you the opportunity to view data that is most similar to your own company’s. Detailed benefits information for both hourly and salaried workers is also included.

Advantage-level members who complete the survey receive the compiled anonymous results at no cost; Basic members who complete the survey may purchase the results for $75; and nonmember companies that complete it may purchase the results for $250. The more companies that participate in this study, the more comprehensive the resulting data will be, so be sure to fill out the survey for your company before the July 31 deadline.

Don’t know if this tool is for you? Visit

fmanet.org/surveys

and review details from the 2014 results.

Pre-Employment Assessment Provides Quick Way to Evaluate Potential Shop Hires

Tired of sorting through pools of job applications searching for the best qualified candidate? Streamline your hiring process with FMA’s Pre-Employment Assessment for Metal Fabrication Shops, a convenient online screening tool, designed to help you validate hiring decisions for entry-level metal fabrication positions.

The Pre-Employment Assessment evaluates a candidate’s aptitude in math fundamentals, metal manufacturing basics, visual speed and accuracy, as well as spatial recognition and reasoning. There is even a section that considers a candidate’s motivation and behavior.

Test out this resource with a FREE trial in July.

The assessment tool is available on a subscription basis for 24-hour-a-day access with an unlimited number of uses. One-, six-, and 12-month subscription plans are available. AdvantageTraining members enjoy complimentary access to the tool. During July only, employers and HR managers can try out this valuable hiring tool for FREE with a one-week trial subscription.
