# NEW ASIAN HUB

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

11 – 13 May 2016 Suzhou, China

Leading global sheet metal exhibition brand takes new direction for its focus in Asia

ISMR SAYS:

Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd, organiser of the leading global sheet metal working event EuroBLECH, has announced the rebranding of the BLECH China series of exhibitions. Now known as AsiaBLECH 2016, an international sheet metalworking trade show, it will take place from 11-13 May 2016 at Suzhou International Expo Center, China, on a two-year cycle.

This is a significant step which mirrors the development history of EuroBLECH, the world’s leading sheet metal exhibition.

In this world-exclusive, the Chairman of Mack Brooks Exhibitions, Stephen Brooks; Managing Director, David Tellett and Dan Wu, Exhibition Director for Mack Brooks’ (Shanghai) operations tell ISMR more…

ISMR: Why rebrand BLECH China as AsiaBLECH?

Stephen Brooks: It really goes back to the fundamentals of where EuroBLECH came from, as a show and as a brand. Since the early 1970s, BLECH in Germany has shown the great value of a truly specialised audience. In many ways, it was one of the very first ‘vertical trade exhibitions’, leading the way in terms of attracting a uniquely engaged, dedicated audience of visitors.

The BLECH show in Germany was rebranded some twenty-five years ago as EuroBLECH and the event has gone on to prove itself as the world’s number one specialised exhibition for sheet metal engineering. Since that time, the brand has acquired a global reach, attracting an international audience. This growth demonstrated the benefits for visitors of a clarity of approach; the clear knowledge that everything on show would be relevant to them and their businesses. Visitors found the specialised, stand-alone nature of what’s on show to be extremely compelling.

With AsiaBLECH we are drawing a clear parallel between EuroBLECH and AsiaBLECH, which we believe has the potential to follow a similar growth path in Asia.

ISMR: What are the benefits for exhibitors?

Stephen Brooks: Well, for exhibitors, this market-focused ‘vertical’ concept provided much greater efficiency in finding new customers and maintaining contact with existing customers. To meet exclusively with buyers from a specialised sector means that no time is mis-spent dealing with those whose interest lies elsewhere. It means that exhibitors could avoid ‘timewasters’ and benefit from a much higher strike-rate in meeting buyers.

There is nothing worse for exhibitors than spending time speaking to visitors who are not relevant and EuroBLECH has always recognised this.

David Tellett: The rebranding of BLECH China as AsiaBLECH is significant in that it is designed to follow the same path, in Asia, as EuroBLECH followed in Europe. The rebranding has been undertaken with the backing of exhibitors who value and understand the specialised nature of EuroBLECH and who now require exhibitions in China with very clear, dedicated concepts, to avoid the pitfalls of taking part in too generic, too vague or loosely defined ‘engineering’ or ‘metals’ or ‘metallurgical’ events.

Stephen Brooks: Yes, there are a great many general metalworking events in China to choose from, but the real point of AsiaBLECH is to provide the same purity of approach and the same specialised sheet metal audience that EuroBLECH offers in Europe, except that it pivots around China.
