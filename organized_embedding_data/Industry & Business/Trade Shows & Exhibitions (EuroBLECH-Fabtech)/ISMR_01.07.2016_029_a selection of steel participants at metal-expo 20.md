# A selection of steel participants at Metal-Expo 2016

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

NLMK; MMK; TMK; Severstal; EvrazHolding; Mechel; UMMC; ChTPZ; KUMZ; Metinvest-Holding; ArcelorMittal; BMZ; OMK; SSAB; OMZ-Spetsstal; VSMPO-AVISMA; Centravis; Stupino Steel Company; GMH-Holding; TEM-PO; Uraltrubprom and more.

Metal-Expo'2016, the 22nd International Industrial Exhibition (from 8-11 November 2016, VDNH, Moscow) will exhibit the entire range of ferrous and nonferrous products, contemporary solutions and the latest equipment.

Over 600 companies from 30 countries will showcase products and technologies at the VDNH exhibition grounds whilst more than 30,000 professionals in the ferrous; non-ferrous; construction; heavy engineering; fuel and energy; transportation and logistics; steel trading and other industry sectors will visit the event.

## Network for success

Metal-Expo'2016 will bring together leading steel companies; tube and pipe producers; wire mesh product manufacturers; non-ferrous products processors; aluminium rolled stock and alloy manufacturers; steel products suppliers and traders; builders; machinebuilders; oil and gas representatives and other industry sectors.

"Leading steel producers and holdings; equipment manufacturers and suppliers; engineering companies and the biggest steel traders are among the regular Metal-Expo exhibitors. Steel equipment producers and suppliers consider the event the main platform to present their technologies and promote innovative engineering solutions in the Russian market. Solutions and equipment suppliers such as SMS Group; Ficep; Metalforme; BLM Group; Dango&Dienenthal; Vernet Behringer; Fives Oto; Gustav Eirich; Bradbury; ORMETO-YUMZ; Electrostal Heavy Engineering Works; Vi-Mens; KAMI and more will present their latest expertise at the exhibition," the organiser told ISMR.

Steel company executives and state officials in charge of the steel industry visit the show on a regular basis. The congress includes more than 50 conferences, seminars and roundtables to enable market participants the opportunity to discuss pressing problems in the industry including supply chain balancing, long-term contracts etc. and to plan their long and medium-term strategies

"Metal-Expo provides all market participants with the perfect opportunity to meet with their customers, expand sales, promote their corporate image among partners, test new product lines and conclude profitable contracts. Estimated contracts totalling over 100 billion roubles are concluded among Metal-Expo exhibitors and visitors (long-term and new partners) every year. During the four days of the exhibition, managers and industry professionals hold hundreds of meetings with long-term and potential customers," added the organiser.

Metal-Expo'2015 was held from 10-13 November 2015 in Moscow, Russia. 570 companies from 34 countries participated in the event while some 27,000 steel industry professionals visited the exhibition. Steel endusers and traders met with managers and professionals of leading steel and heavyengineering companies to discuss ways to cooperate as well as issues such as materials and rolled stock supplies.

Along with national companies, Metal-Expo'2015 also featured more than 200 international companies from 34 countries. The top four largest international exhibitor segments were Chinese (51 exhibitors), German (32), Italian (30) and Ukrainian (14). Despite the downturn in the national economy, many new contracts were signed at the exhibition.
