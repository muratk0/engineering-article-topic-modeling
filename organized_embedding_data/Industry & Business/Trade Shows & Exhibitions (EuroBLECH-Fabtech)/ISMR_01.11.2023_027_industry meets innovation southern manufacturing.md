Southern Manufacturing & Electronics exhibition 2024 (6-8 February 2024), which has rapidly changed from a regional to an international event. Last year's event attracted 9,144 visitors and 460 exhibitors (representing 600 companies).

"With a record-breaking number of enquiries and interest from overseas companies, next year's event is set to shine. In early February, this prestigious showcase of industrial innovation will capture global attention at the Farnborough International Exhibition & Conference Centre in the UK. This new 20,000m² venue offers modern facilities, easy access and free onsite parking," the show organiser, European Trade & Exhibition Services (ETES), told ISMR.

The comprehensive annual industrial show is a major platform for new technology in production and inspection equipment, components and subcontract services across a wide spectrum of industries. Professionals engaged in design, manufacturing and procurement can explore a wide array of equipment including machine tools; tooling; workholding; production hardware; automation and robotics; test and measurement equipment; quality control solutions; engineering software; materials supply and other diverse technologies.

In addition, the exhibition showcases a range of more general products and services including oil and coolant supply; workshop equipment; dust and fume extraction; humidity control; workplace storage systems; bespoke case and foam manufacture; injection moulding; hand tools; adhesives; waste removal and recycling; training; freight services and financial consultancy. ISMR is a media supporter of the event and will distribute its December 2023/January 2024 issue throughout the show.

## Forum and seminar

"Southern Manufacturing & Electronics is not only a draw for big-name manufacturers but equally welcomes smaller engineering firms. This inclusivity, and the show's effectiveness as a business forum, result in a vibrant atmosphere and encourage exhibitors to return year after year," explained ETES.

In addition to the exhibition, the event includes a free technical seminar programme over the three days. Presentations will include advice for small manufacturers looking to improve their relationship with customers; opportunities for those seeking financial support and tips on how to enhance a sales team's performance. CE and UKCA marking compliance; sustainable practices in manufacturing; the importance of brand awareness and creative campaigns; management skills coaching and digital process management will all come under the spotlight.

The programme is a complementary blend of technical and business topics covering a wide range of issues facing manufacturing businesses today. All seminars can be booked online on the show website at www.industrysouth.co.uk

It is also an accessible show for the rich ecosystem of engineering SMEs operating across the UK and, increasingly, continental Europe. This makes it one of the most effective shows for addressing multiple sourcing issues in a very time-efficient way.

The event will co-locate again with AutoAero, a specialised theme within the exhibition devoted to aerospace and automotive engineering. Farnborough is, of course, well-known for its links to the UK's aviation industry. BAE Systems' and Lockheed Martin's UK headquarters are both within five minutes' walk of the show. Across the region as a whole, 22% of the UK's £20 billion aerospace industry is reportedly located within the show's catchment area.

## Sheet metal in focus

Various sheet-metal subcontractors will also promote their services at Southern Manufacturing & Electronics 2024. Glenrothes-based Fife Fabrications will explain the extent of its supply of precision sheet metalwork, machined components and electro-mechanical assemblies. Its extensive capabilities allow it to work as an integral part of a customer's project.

Indian firm, Adar Industries, will promote its subcontract manufacturing expertise in tool production and stamping of brass, bronze, carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium. Aerospace AS9100D-certified Easton Sheet Metal will present examples of its CNC laser cutting, punching and forming, plus welding and finishing. It operates from a 50,000 sq. ft. manufacturing site in Harlow, Essex (UK).

PRV Engineering in Pontypool operates a one-stop-shop solution to meet customers' requirements. Multiple services include waterjet cutting; fabrication; toolmaking; sheet metal working; shotblasting; electroplating; painting; powder coating; structural and architectural steelwork; site installation and maintenance.

Advanced Chemical Etching (ACE) uses chemical etching processes to manufacture precision components, frequently out of sheet metal. Materials processed include titanium; copper and aluminium for electrification and hydrogen fuel cell systems; while it also routinely manufactures components in stainless steel; nickel alloys; copper; beryllium copper; phosphor bronze; brass; nitinol and elgiloy. On its stand, the company will highlight battery interconnect and fuel cell component manufacture, important to the development of energy management in existing and future electric vehicles and alternative fuel systems.

BIZ Engineering specialises in fine-limit sheet metal and complex welded fabrications. In-house capabilities include sheet metalforming; tube forming; MIG and TIG welding and fabrication; CNC laser cutting of sheet and tube, punching and assembly. The company mainly processes aluminium, mild steel and stainless steel.

Potters also supplies precision sheetmetal work and fabricated assemblies from its 33,000 sq. ft. facility in Maidenhead, UK. RHH Franks supplies turnkey manufacturing solutions across a range of industries, encompassing air and fluid transfer; tubes; ducts; heat exchangers; water extractors and complex fabrications. Tube manipulation; TIG, MIG and spot welding; salt bath dip brazing; fabrication; power pressing and heat treatment are within its remit.

## Getting there

The site is easy to access via private transport, with 3,500 free on-site parking spaces. For those travelling by train, there is a dedicated free bus service running from Farnborough's Main and North Camp railways stations directly to the show. Admission and parking are also free of charge.

The venue itself features a high standard of facilities including a free WiFi service in the foyer area and a selection of catering outlets.

Admission to the exhibition is free of charge – registrations can be made on www.industrysouth.co.uk/register/

Parking at the venue is free, as is the shuttle bus service between Farnborough's train stations directly to the venue.
