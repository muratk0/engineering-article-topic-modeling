# Date and opening hours

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Date: 20-24 June 2022 Venue: Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre Opening hours: Mon - Thurs: 09:00 a.m. - 6:00 p.m. Friday: 09:00 a.m. - 5:00 p.m.

For those driving, the A44 motorway goes directly past the exhibition centre. Leave at Junction 29, signposted "Messe/Arena," and head for the two large car parks, P1 and P2. Free shuttle buses are available from the car parks to the trade fair entrances. The Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre can be reached from Cologne within about one hour and from Dortmund and Aachen within about an hour and a quarter. The exhibition centre can also be reached from neighbouring Neuss, Krefeld, Mönchengladbach and Moers in 30 minutes.

For arrival by local transport, take tram no. U78 ESPRIT arena/Messe Nord or U79 Messe Ost (exit at MesseOst/Stockumer Kirchstr.) or bus no. 722 (exit at Messe Ost or Messe Süd/CCD). There are two major transport hubs where you are likely to change: Hauptbahnhof (central station) and Heinrich-Heine-Allee. Nearly all destinations in and around Düsseldorf can be reached from these stations. During the trade fair, Rheinbahn also provides an additional airport shuttle bus (no. 896).
