# Tube 2026 focuses on new tech, sustainable industry

[TARİH: 01.03.2026 The Fabricator]

Show Preview

Biannual showcase highlights new one-day event for stainless and nickel alloys

By

Lincoln Brunner

Tube bending machines will once again play a central role in the machinery featured at Tube 2026.

T

he Tube 2026 expo in Düsseldorf, Germany, promises a more diverse array of offerings than ever before, including a first-ever event devoted specifically to provide insights and networking for the stainless steel and nickel alloys sector of the market.

In conjunction with wire 2026, the world’s largest event focused on tube and pipe is being held April 13-17 at the Düsseldorf fairgrounds. The event features 1,100 exhibitors from dozens of countries occupying nearly 600,000 sq. ft. of floor space in Halls 1 to 7 at the fairgrounds.

The tens of thousands of attendees expected at the event will of course see machinery and equipment for metal tube forming and fabrication, but they’ll also find a Plastic Tube & Pipes area in Hall 1. Vendors with forming technology equipment will be in Hall 5, while pipe processing machinery can be found across parts of Halls 5, 6, and 7a.

In addition, Chinese pipe manufacturers and processors will present at the Meet China’s Expertise exhibit in Hall 7.0.

STAINLESS AND NICKEL ALLOYS TAKE CENTER STAGE

Perhaps the most notable new event at Tube 2026 is the Stainless Steel & Nickel Alloy Tube & Pipe Market Insights Day. Scheduled for Monday, April 13, at the Congress Center South near Hall 1 and organized by Messe Düsseldorf partner SMR Events, the one-day mini-conference will feature talks and panel discussions on seamless products, the oil/gas and energy markets, and welded tube and pipe, as well as keynote speakers addressing geopolitical realities, expectations for the entire stainless and nickel alloy sector, and the outlook specifically for India and China.

According to organizers, the stainless and nickel alloy event will give attendees the chance to interact personally with international experts and gain insights that will help them customize their business strategies and appointments at Tube.

"We actually participate at the SMR conferences not just to kill time, but there is actually a very important outcome of it," said Damstahl Group CEO Michael Lund, one of the event’s speakers and part of the morning’s panel discussion on the big picture in the industry. "It is a fantastic opportunity to meet colleagues from around the world. It’s a great place to meet suppliers, and it’s a fantastic place for networking."

FOCUS AREAS ACROSS THE BOARD

All week, Tube 2026 will offer participants a look at the full spectrum of tube-related industries, including cutting equipment, new materials, hydrogen-related machinery, e-mobility, welding, and fastening and joining.

Both wire and Tube 2026 also will include expert forums, industry get-togethers, and ecoMetals demonstrations about the latest ideas in sustainability efforts like emissions reduction and efficient energy use. The forum stage in Hall 1 will focus on topics like renewable energies, artificial intelligence, and robotics. On top of that, a hydrogen and e-mobility congress and High Potential Day will offer platforms for networking, promotion of young talent, and sustainable industrial approaches.

"Wire and Tube are the leading international marketplaces for our industries," said Daniel Ryfisch, Messe Düsseldorf’s director of metal forming and fabrication technologies. "Düsseldorf offers the necessary infrastructure and international reach to showcase technological developments and industrial trends."
