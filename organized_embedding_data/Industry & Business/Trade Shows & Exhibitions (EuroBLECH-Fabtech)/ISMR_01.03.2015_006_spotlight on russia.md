# Spotlight on Russia

[TARİH: 01.03.2015 ISMR]

DON’T MISS this year’s BLECH Russia from 25-27 March 2015 at the recently completed ExpoForum in St Petersburg, at the heart of Russia’s fastest growing industrial region. It will be the fourth in a successful series of trade shows. Nearly 4,400 trade visitors attended the 2013 exhibition in St Petersburg.

The exhibition profile encompasses all core sectors of the sheet metal working process: Sheet metal; finished and semi-finished products; forming; separation and joining technology; flexible sheet metal working; machine elements; handling; surface treatment as well as tools and dies. This biannual show attracts attendance from production specialists, buyers, designers and quality controllers in the sheet metal working industry. Visitors are industry professionals from key industries including the automotive industry and its suppliers, mechanical engineering, sheet metal products and components (white and brown goods), iron and steel production, steel and aluminium construction, rolling mills and ship building. See www.blechrussia.com

A Brooks 2014 publication on ‘Russia's Sheet Metalworking Sector – A Market Overview’ gives more in-depth analysis of the market. See www.brooksreports.com ■
