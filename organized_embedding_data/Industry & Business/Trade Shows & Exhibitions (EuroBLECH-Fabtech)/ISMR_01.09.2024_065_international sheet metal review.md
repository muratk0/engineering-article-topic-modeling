Acknowledged as the number one international sheet metal forming and fabrication journal, International Sheet Metal Review (ISMR) magazine offers truly global and independent coverage. Celebrating its 25th anniversary this year, its success lies in a unique blend of technology, analysis and business issues presented in a fresh and lively format. Each issue, both print and digital, reaches over 60,000 qualified registered readers. The print issue is published ten times per year.

ISMR offers news, views, product reviews, technical articles, case studies and interviews from across the world. An interactive digital version of the magazine, as well as a regular newsletter, is also published.
