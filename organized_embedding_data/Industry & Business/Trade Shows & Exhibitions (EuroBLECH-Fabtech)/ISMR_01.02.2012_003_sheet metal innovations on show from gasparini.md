# Sheet metal innovations on show from Gasparini

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

Gasparini unveiled the new X-200 press brake at its Open

GASPARINI INDUSTRIES, based near Treviso (Italy) inaugurated its first Open House on 19 October 2011. Three brief presentations, by managing director, Simone Ferrari, sales and marketing director, Frank Heyerick, and research and development director, Nicola Florian, presented the company's strategic and business plan. The company then unveiled its three flagship products: the new Gasparini shear, its new X-press press brake and new plasma cutting system. See our product review page 41 for further information.

The industrial plant covers 40,000 m2, which include the workshop (10,000 m2), offices (2,400 m2), show room (700 m2) and the warehouse and logistics areas. Space and production flow have been rationalised and the display area has been completely renovated. A dedicated in-house Tech Centre has been recently added, with a team of specialists to analyse clients' specific requirements and design technical solutions.

Gasparini Industries manufactures press brakes, shears and plasma cutting machines. Advanced improvements have been made to the entire 2011 Collection, such as the LED lighting systems that have been installed at crucial processing points to favour proper work ergonomics and operator safety.

All the new machines have also been completely redesigned to integrate important high-tech solutions. The new "Telelink" Suite assistance service allows Gasparini technicians to remotely connect to the customer's machinery, analyse its operating status, verify any problems that may be present and solve real-time situations that would otherwise involve maintenance interventions. ■

## BAOSTEEL LAUNCHES NEW HYDROFORMING AND HOT STAMPING ACTIVITIES

ON 18 October 2011, the commissioning ceremony for Shanghai Baosteel Hot Stamping Parts Co., Ltd.and Shanghai Baosteel Hydro-forming Parts Co., Ltd. affiliated to Baosteel Metal was held in Baoshan Industrial Park. Shanghai Baosteel Hot Stamping Parts Co., Ltd. and Shanghai Baosteel Hydroforming Parts Co., Ltd. are both located at Baoshan Industrial Park in Luodian.

The designed capacity of the Phase 1 project of Baosteel Hot Stamping Parts Co., Ltd. is 1 million pieces, with a total investment of RMB 230million yuan. Its main products are safety-critical components, including the A pillar, B pillar, C pillar, door impact bar, front and rear bumpers, roof-strengthened beams etc. The Phase 1 project of Shanghai Baosteel Hydro-forming Parts Co., Ltd. has a production capacity of 500,000 pieces, with a total investment of RMB 220 million yuan, mainly for the design and development, production, sales and relevant technical services for hydro-formed parts, relevant moulds, fixtures, supporting components and general assemblies, and the research and development of tube hydro-formed tubes technology.

Hot stamping and hydro-forming technology are important techniques to achieve automotive lightweighting. Five years ago, Baosteel devoted itself actively to the research and development of forming technology for high strength automotive steel and reached advanced levels in terms of mastering key technologies, equipment configuration and product development. ■
