# ADVANCED ENGINEERING

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

Over 700 exhibitors and more than 175 speakers will support the Advanced Engineering UK show from 4-5 November 2015

More than 700 leading international suppliers have booked stands for Advanced Engineering UK, which opens its doors on 4 and 5 November 2015 at Birmingham’s NEC (UK). These exhibitors are using the UK’s large annual trade show and open conference for the high performance engineering sectors to launch new products and services to the wider engineering market. Advanced Engineering UK 2015 integrates multiple show exhibit zones with the UK’s largest free-to-attend engineering conference programme. The show spans key sectors including aerospace, automotive, motorsport, marine, civil engineering and more. It will also feature over 700 exhibitors and more than 175 speakers, delivering sessions from six purposebuilt auditoriums at the show.

The event features four ‘shows within a show’ – Automotive Engineering Show, Aero Engineering Show, Composites Engineering Show and Performance Metals Engineering.

## Deepening support

SAE International, a global association made up of more than 137,000 engineers and technical experts in the aerospace, automotive and commercial-vehicle industries, will be hosting its own co-located technology symposium in a separate location at the NEC, Birmingham. It will also run four expert speaker sessions as part of Advanced Engineering’s free-to-attend open conference programme, located on the exhibition show floor itself.

SAE International’s Augmented and Virtual Reality (AR/VR) Technologies Symposium will detail how to integrate design, manufacturing and final assembly of augmented and virtual technologies into the digital thread of organisations. The practical symposium will also look at how to show an economic value proposition for using AR/VR within any advanced manufacturing enterprise, and it will examine the future outlook of AR/VR for the mobility industry. Speakers from international names, including Boeing, Ford Motor Company, Jaguar Land Rover and Airbus, will be sharing their insights and experiences of AR/VR being utilised in an array of industries.

Global Tier 1 automotive supplier, Magna International, will also be increasing its involvement at this year’s show. Magna International, supported by the Knowledge Transfer Network, will host and sponsor the Enabling Innovation Poster Zone – a major area at the show – where the ideas and concepts from the UK’s up and coming innovators will be showcased. Magna will also run 1-2-1 meetings with interested innovators, deliver a keynote in the show’s conference programme and take an expanded exhibition stand.

The Enabling Innovation Poster Zone will provide an opportunity for early stage innovators to showcase breakthrough ideas, products and processes to the UK’s advanced engineering audience. The ‘poster’ submissions will come from incubator-level organisations, university students, research-based companies and startups. The Zone will give the 13,000 visitors attending Advanced Engineering UK the chance to uncover potential technologies, partners and untapped capability.

Magna’s R & D team will also invite potential innovation partners for 1-2-1 meetings. The sessions will offer innovators the opportunity to discuss new concepts and products, with opportunity for direct investment, collaborative programmes and potential licensing agreements with Magna’s R&D decision makers. Magna is very active in the UK technology community interfacing with venture capital firms to locate opportunities, actively working with universities on new technologies and collaborating with multiple start-ups leading to a recent joint venture agreement.

## New product launches

A number of manufacturers will be launching new products and systems at the show. A selection of these are below.

Royal Tool Control will be launching its new Tool Master ‘BASIC’ pre-setter live on the Advanced Engineering UK show floor. The previously unseen product, which is based on its Tool Master Quadra pre-setter, offers an entry level tool pre-setter, benefitting from EyeRay Hawk image processing software and an “All-In One PC” with 15.6” touch screen monitor, for rapid and easy operation.

Alicona is using the show to present its optical metrology robot measuring system to the UK market for the first time. This 3D measurement sensor can be successfully implemented into a range of different industries, providing users with a flexible measurement solution for quality assurance applications. It saves time during the manufacturing process and allows quality assurance to be included, whilst providing users with real-time information.

Perceptron will be exhibiting its new ScanR. It bills it as ‘the world’s first green laser for use with portable and fixed coordinate measuring machines’. Features include an enhanced dynamic range, improved material coverage, increased accuracy and edge detection.

Sia Abrasives will be showcasing its new ‘Sianet’ range of flexible abrasives. On display will be the 7900 and 7500 CER series which deliver high performance stock removal, with a long lifetime.

ETPS Ltd, a leading provider of power supplies and test systems, will be presenting its new super capacitor emulation GUI. It allows the power supply to accurately emulate the electrical characteristics of a real super capacitor stack. It is designed to reduce preparation time, eliminate operator error and result variations, and minimise external variables.

Trotec will be displaying its Speedy 360, the latest addition to its Speedy line of laser engravers. It has been designed to improve accessibility with a fully flexible front cover.

Nordson EFD is introducing its xQR41 Series MicroDotTM needle valve. This new valve features a 60% smaller form factor in comparison to standard valves, an exchangeable modular design and a quick release clasp.
