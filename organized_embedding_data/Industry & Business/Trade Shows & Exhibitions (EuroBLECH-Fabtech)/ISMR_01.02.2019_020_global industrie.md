# GLOBAL INDUSTRIE

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

Don't miss Global Industrie, taking place in Lyon (France) from 5-8 March 2019, at the Eurexpo Lyon. This international event receives support, said the show organiser, from French public institutions and authorities, and particularly from the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Region where the show will be held.

"The success of our Paris show in 2018 is well documented and the Lyon exhibition is already more than 90% full. The Lyon shows are always dynamic, attracting high visitor numbers in a region where industrial identity is key," the show organiser, GL Events, told ISMR.

The show in 2018, in Paris, attracted 40,800 visitors over four days. Global Industrie 2019 brings together four leading manufacturing exhibitions under one banner. These are:

- INDUSTRIE, the production technology and equipment show.
- MIDEST, representing expertise in industrial subcontracting, returning to Lyon after 30 years.
- SMART INDUSTRIES, a show focused on Industry of the Future. It is the first time that this exhibition will be held in Lyon.
- TOLEXPO for metalworking sheet and rolls, tubes and profiles.

## Innovation and awards

Global Industrie 2019, said the show organiser, is expected to attract participants from:

- The entire industrial ecosystem: startups; product/solution providers; equipment suppliers; subcontractors; contracting parties; large groups; regions etc.
- The whole value chain: research & innovation; design; production; services; maintenance; training etc.
- User markets: such as transport & mobility; energy; food processing; infrastructure; consumer goods; chemicals; cosmetics; pharmacies; mechanical; defence/military; metallurgy; steel and more.

"Global Industrie Lyon will bring 2,500 exhibitors and represented brands (including 30% international) over a floor space of 110,000m² and will welcome 45,000 visitors (including 20% international)," added GL Events. "It doesn't just bring all the players from the industrial world together; it offers a set of services that meet the needs of all visitor profiles.

"Monitoring technology and searching for innovations are the primary reasons given by 66% of visitors attending the show. The Global Industrie awards will also help visitors to identify unmissable innovations at the exhibition in the following categories: Industrial Transformation; New Technology; Productive Performance; Exemplary Manufacture; Collaborative Success and Responsible Solution. In 2019, a special award category for start-ups has been created."

All exhibitors can enter free of charge by completing the entry form via their exhibitor portal. The winners of these awards will be unveiled during the grand evening reception on 5 March 2019, where 1,600 figures from the industrial world will gather. In 2018, 180 entries were submitted by exhibitors and the awards were presented before an audience of 1,500 people.

## Employment, training and gender equality

Finding the right skills to increase capability is a priority now for businesses and industrial countries. Global Industrie will therefore showcase a special 'campus area' of over 1,500m² dedicated solely to employment and training. This area will serve as a practical gateway to training and employment, aiming to give young people, teachers, parents and job-seekers (whether looking for their first job or retraining) a complete overview of what the industry can offer.

"Supported, advised or even guided if necessary, everyone will have the means to identify tomorrow's jobs easily; choose the direction that suits them (college, training, placements etc.); discover training courses or industries that are recruiting and even meet and talk to manufacturers and potential future employers," explained GL Events.

"Global Industrie is also addressing one of the major social problems of our times, gender equality at work. This is a particular problem in industry because many of its trades have a very male image. Things, however, are changing and institutions, training organisations and companies are launching initiatives to combat these stereotypes. We will pick up on these efforts in all the themes and events throughout the show."

## Factory of the Future

Global Industrie's flagship display, the 'Connected Factory', is an immersive dip into the heart of Industry of the Future. Over a floor space of 1,100m², the operating Industry 4.0 Production Line will practically demonstrate innovations which manufacturers can implement in their factories to improve production.

"In the age of Industry 4.0, Global Industrie's goal is to help visitors learn and explain what is happening; help them understand the changes that are taking place; demonstrate their benefits and show them the upheavals that will result which will help them to innovate their production. We aim to focus on the attractions and the benefits of the Industry of the Future. In short, making sure that manufacturers are not left standing on the platform…"

The Global Industrie conferences/seminars aim to tackle the challenges and solutions affecting the industrial sector today. On the show's two TV stages, debates; roundtables; keynote speeches and masterclasses etc. will cover topics such as Industry of the Future; Artificial Intelligence; environment; open innovation; additive manufacturing; robotics; cybersecurity etc.

"This technical expertise, presented for the first time at Lyon, will (even more than in Paris) highlight interconnectivity and automation; co-bots; enhanced reality; predictive maintenance; production digitisation; customisation and more," explained the show organiser.

## FOCUS ON PLASMA CUTTING

BSK Engineering has seen explosive growth with new plasma cutting machine purchases.

“An Irish construction company digs for deeper productivity gains with plasma cutting”
