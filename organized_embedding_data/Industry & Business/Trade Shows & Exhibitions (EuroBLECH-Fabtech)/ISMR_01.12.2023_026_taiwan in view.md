# TAIWAN IN VIEW

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

Don't miss the TIMTOS machine-tool show in Taipei, Taiwan, from 6-11 March 2023. Key themes are green machine tools; smart machinery; flexible manufacturing and digital simulation.

Taipei International Machine Tool Show, also known as TIMTOS, will return from 6-11 March 2023 at Nangang Exhibition Centre (Halls 1 and 2 and TWTC Hall 1) in Taipei, Taiwan.

"With 1,000+ exhibitors in nearly 6,100 booths, even larger than in 2022, TIMTOS 2023 will be the largest trade show ever recorded in Taiwan after it recently lifted all its border controls since the COVID-19 outbreak," the show organiser explained.

"At present, overseas professionals are free to travel to Taiwan to participate in trade shows as usual. As a result, there has been increasing demand for larger booth space at TIMTOS 2023 and delegations to the show will arrive from countries such as Vietnam; Turkey and India etc.," added TAITRA, one of the show organisers.

Total exports of Taiwan machine tools from January to November 2022 reached US$ 2.763 billion, an increase of 9.4% over the same period last year. Smart solutions that integrate the concepts of digitalisation and carbon reduction will be highlighted at the show.

## TIMTOS x TMTS 2022 report

TIMTOS × TMTS 2022, the first co-branded machine-tool mega show in Taiwan, was held from 21-26 February 2022. The physical exhibition was held in Taipei Nangang Exhibition Halls 1 and 2 over six consecutive days while the online exhibition remained open until 21 March 2022. The show attracted more than 40,000 online and offline visitors in Taiwan and overseas. The online exhibition reached visitors from more than 20 countries/regions.

"With 950 exhibitors in 5,100 booths, TIMTOS x TMTS was not only the largest trade show in Taiwan since the outbreak of the pandemic but also the world's first machine-tool mega show in 2022. In response to border controls, the show featured an array of online services for international visitors and media including an onsite guide for online visitors; 'Sourcing Taiwan Machinery'; onsite guide for media tour; 'Live Tour @ Showground'; 'Media Eye on TIMTOS x TMTS'; 'Podcast Live' and TIMTOS x TMTS Online," outlined the show organisers.

“Live Tour @ Showground”, “Media Eye on TIMTOS x TMTS" and "Podcast Live" delivered daily live updates from the show floor. The videos and podcast episodes have enjoyed 25,000+ views and listens. In addition, heavyweight attendees were guided to the showground and made real-time connections with selected exhibitors during 500 online sessions. Processing machines, multi-axis machining centres and laser-cutting machines were amongst the most sought-after items for overseas buyers, according to the show organisers.

"The machine-tool industry keeps up with the development of future trends and accelerates digital transformation and upgrades. New models and solutions have targeted emerging business in sectors such as semiconductors; green energy; electric vehicles; healthcare and aerospace. TIMTOS x TMTS welcomed a wide spectrum of visitors from different industries. Major domestic visitors included TSMC; AIDC (Aerospace Industrial Development Corporation); Formosa Heavy Industries; Hon Hai Precision Industry and CSMC (China Steel Machinery Corporation)," they added.

Aside from metal-cutting machine tools, machining centres and metalforming machine tools, the TIMTOS x TMTS 2022 joint event also showcased a line-up of smart machines. Smart machinery is part of the Taiwanese government's “five plus two" innovative industries plan.
