NBT has been sponsoring summer manufacturing camps for 10 years. Collectively, 2,384 students have enjoyed 121 summer camps in 31 states. Students are exposed to a wide range of manufacturing techniques during their chosen camp. Advanced camps have been added to make it possible for students who have completed a beginner camp to go deeper with their exploration of the industry. Projects are taken from design to completion.
