## An eye on MIDEST

In 2013, France’s industrial subcontracting sectors achieved total sales of €66.63 billion. This total represents the activities of 30,302 companies of all sizes, employing 485,050 people. For the year as a whole, companies employing at least 20 people recorded overall sales of €54.62 billion. It has been estimated that, in 2013, there were 24,610 subcontractors employing fewer than 20 people and their total workforce amounted to 106,174 people. They achieved sales of €12 billion.

The automotive sector remains the number one customer sector for French subcontracting, even though its relative importance has been declining for the last nine years. In 2004, it accounted for 40% of orders whereas, in 2013, it was down to 28.8%.

MIDEST, the global show for industrial subcontracting, will be hosting a series of lectures, round tables and special events on the problems that manufacturers are currently facing as well as those that will affect their future. It will also host the Industrial Subcontracting Awards, a set of awards specifically for the sector’s companies, players and expertise. According to MIDEST research, the subcontract manufacturing sector in the European Union has an annual turnover of nearly half a trillion euros.

“This year’s show aims to help manufacturers to anticipate the changes that will form the industry of tomorrow. The much talked about Industry of the Future, a real revolution, will be covered through themes such as robotization, additive manufacturing, energy efficiency and the major challenge of training and recruitment. These topics, which are of crucial importance and where it is necessary to act right away, will be covered by MIDEST 2015,” added the organiser.

CETIM, the mechanical engineering technical centre, will also be organizing its flash conferences in the Place de la Mécanique, which it will host along with the Mechanical Engineering Federation. The b2fair – Business to Fairs® business meetings will bring together subcontractors and customers who are looking to forge concrete partnerships in a specific sector, market or geographical region. This year, the TV Studio and conference venue have been brought together which will allow discussions to be filmed. It will welcome major figures from industry who are attending the show, and these highlights will be available to view on the www.midest.com website and on the MIDEST TV YouTube channel.

In 2014, MIDEST attracted 1,678 exhibitors from 45 countries. Specific sectors covered will include metal processing; electronics and electricals; surface and thermal treatments and finishes; additive manufacturing (new); industrial fastenings and industrial maintenance.
