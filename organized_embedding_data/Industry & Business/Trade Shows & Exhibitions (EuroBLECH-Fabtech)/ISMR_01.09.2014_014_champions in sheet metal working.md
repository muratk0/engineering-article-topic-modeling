# Champions in sheet metal working

[TARİH: 01.09.2014 ISMR]

EUROBLECH 2014, the 23rd International Sheet Metal Working Exhibition in Hanover this October, is presenting a new online competition to recognise high standards, best practice, innovation and excellence in sheet metal working. The competition, called “Champions in Sheet Metal Working”, will have five winners in total, one winner for each of the following five categories: Production in a record time, solution for a complex problem, outstanding customer service, production of an unusual product made of sheet metal and excellent teamwork.

Winners will be formally presented with an award during a ceremony at EuroBLECH 2014, held from 21-25 October 2014 in Hanover, Germany. Each of the five winners will also receive a EuroBLECH Mini Football Table.

Detailed information about the competition is now live on the show website at www.euroblech.com and companies of all sizes in any country are invited to participate. The entries of the participating companies will go live on the EuroBLECH website, followed by an open online vote to elect the winners. All industry professionals are invited to vote for their favourite candidate.

EuroBLECH is renowned as a worldwide industry barometer and marketplace for the latest sheet metal working technology. Live demonstrations of machinery are a main attraction of the exhibition. A total of 1,550 exhibitors from 40 countries will present an impressive range of machines, tools and systems across eight exhibition halls. ■
