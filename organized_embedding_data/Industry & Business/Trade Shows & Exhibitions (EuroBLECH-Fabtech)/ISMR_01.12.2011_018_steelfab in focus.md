# STEELFAB IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

Material handling will be a key focus at SteelFab 2012.

SteelFab 2011, held in January, showcased more than 600 brands and recorded a 20 per cent increase in the number of visitors compared to last year.

The seventh edition of SteelFab featured 206 direct exhibitors, against 183 in the previous year, registering an increase of nearly 13 per cent. It was held over a total exhibition area of 16,000 square metres, featuring the Middle East’s largest display of machinery, machine tools, welding equipment, supplies, consumables and ancillary equipment used in the pre- and post-treatment of steel.

Leading industry experts who exhibited at SteelFab voiced their optimism for the future of the industry. Apart from attracting leading manufacturers in the industry from all over the world, SteelFab boasted a visitor footprint covering the Middle East, Africa, Central Asia and the Indian sub-continent.

SteelFab 2012, the eighth edition of the regional show for steel fabrication, metalworking and metal manufacturing industry, will be held from 9 – 12 January 2012 at Expo Centre Sharjah in the United Arab Emirates. It will have a new special focus – material handling in steel fabrication.

## MIDDLE EAST SPURS GROWTH

The UAE spent 32.8 per cent of its gross domestic product in 2010, the highest level since the global financial crisis, which helped the world’s third-largest oil exporter spur economic growth and further diversify away from oil. The UAE has also recorded one of its best economic and financial eras over the past decade, with its nominal GDP, revenue and trade nearly quadrupling to emerge as one of the fastest-growing economies, a semi-official study has shown.

This has resulted in the UAE becoming one of the most attractive investment destinations. According to AT Kearney’s 2010 Foreign Direct Investment Confidence Index, the UAE is seen as the most attractive investment destination in the Middle East in the next three years and ranks 11th worldwide.

This expected rise in foreign investment would also help boost economic recovery and enhance investment in the infrastructure sector, which could throw up strong opportunities for the metal working and steel fabrication sector.

An emirate of the seven-emirate federation, Sharjah overlooks the Arabian Gulf from the West and Gulf of Oman from the East. One part of the emirate is surrounded by the emirates of Dubai and Ajman, while the other extends east to the Gulf of Oman. The emirate, however, borders all the other emirates of the UAE.

Sharjah is located in the centre of the UAE, with Dubai being a 15-minute drive away (i.e. 15km approx.) by one of the most advanced road networks in the country. Abu Dhabi, a 90-minute drive away, is about 200km from Sharjah and 185km from Dubai.

MATERIAL HANDLING FOCUS Growing awareness on ‘HSE’ issues will boost demand for the right material handling equipment, said the organisers of next year’s SteelFab 2012 event. About 3.9 million employees are disabled at work in any given year and one work-related injury occurs every eight seconds, according to Ergoworks Consulting, injury prevention and ergonomic specialists.

“The right material handling equipment with the correct load ratio ensures reduction in injuries and prevents fatal accidents,” said Mr Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, Director-General of Expo Centre Sharjah, organisers of the SteelFab show.
