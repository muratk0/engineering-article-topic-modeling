# An eye on METAV

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

Peter Bole, Director of the VDW-Nachwuchsstiftung (the VDW’s Youth Foundation)

THE PREPARATIONS for METAV 2014 from 11 to 15 March in Düsseldorf, Germany, are now in full swing. Around 600 international vendors of production technology will be showcasing their innovations to a trade public from all over the world and from all industrial user sectors. Special shows, seminars and technology conferences will address topical issues. At the last METAV in 2012, around 700 exhibitors from 26 different countries showcased their products, solutions and service capabilities. They attracted around 40,700 experts from more than 30 different countries.

The VDW Technology Day on 11 March 2014, during METAV 2014, will examine the safety of machine tools from the perspective of machinery manufacturers, component suppliers and occupational safety experts. The speakers will map out the requirements needed for modern-day machine tools and how these are currently being met. The VDW’s Youth Training Foundation, the VDW-Nachwuchsstiftung, is inviting more than 3,000 schoolchildren from North Rhine-Westphalia and about 1,000 vocational college teachers and trainers to METAV in Düsseldorf.

“Teachers have to communicate to their students their enjoyment of engineering and the MISE subjects (mathematics, informatics, science, engineering), which are indispensable pre-conditions for a career in the machine tool industry,” commented Peter Bole, Director of the VDW-Nachwuchsstiftung.

For the first time at the show, a clamping technology forum on 12 March will be held. A METAV Technology Forum on ‘Tools and Surface Technology’ on 12 and 13 March will also see machinery and tool manufacturers report on how they will be deploying new products, technologies and services to meet and master the challenges of the future. They will be showcasing the latest advances and applications for machine tools and high-precision tools, particularly on subjects such as machining difficult-to-cut materials and machining complex surfaces and geometries.

The 5th Dortmund Grinding Seminar will also be held on 13 and 14 March 2014 during METAV. Other events at METAV will include ‘Generative Production in Medical Technology’ in Hall 15, Stand F11, supplemented by the lecture forum ‘Metal meets Medical’ from 11 to 14 March 2014, Hall 15, Stand F11. A Congress on ‘Mechatronics and Industry 4.0’ will also be held on 12 March 2014.

Sustainability in industrial production operations is a major issue at METAV 2014 in Düsseldorf. Under the label Blue Competence, 27 companies and two industry associations will be showing users from the industrial sector how they can raise potential here. The firms will be spotlighting a broad spectrum of solutions for an energy-autonomous, emission-neutral, ergonomical production technology. For further details, see www.metav.com ■
