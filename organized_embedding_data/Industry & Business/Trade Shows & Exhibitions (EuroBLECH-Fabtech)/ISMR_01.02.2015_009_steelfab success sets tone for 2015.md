# SteelFab success sets tone for 2015

[TARİH: 01.02.2015 ISMR]

THE 11TH edition of the MENA region’s major metal working, metal manufacturing and steel fabrication trade fair – SteelFab 2015 – has given a boost to Expo Centre Sharjah in the new year.

SteelFab, which was the first show to be held in 2015 at Expo Centre Sharjah from 26-29 January 2015, registered a nearly 15% rise in the number of visitors – from 9,022 in 2014 to 10,324 this year. ISMR was present with a booth at the event.

Visitor pre-registrations before the show had touched 4,042 from 72 countries compared to the previous year’s 3,165, recording a surge of 27.7%.

During its four days, SteelFab featured 350 exhibitors representing nearly 900 brands from across the world, a series of special focus areas, special pavilions, new segments, live displays, technical seminars and product presentations. Industry experts were expecting demand drivers for the steel fabrication and metal working industry to remain intact due to the continuing buoyancy of the core sector in the region.

A decision to expand SteelFab was also announced during the show. “We have expanded and spruced up every edition of the show based on exhibitor and visitor feedback. The special focus areas and pavilions seen today were added over the years, catering to the demands of a growing industry. SteelFab 2015 saw us announcing the launch of a new vertical – Fastener World Middle East,” said the show organisers.

Fastener World ME will run alongside SteelFab next year and aims to be a dedicated trade fair for the fastener and fixing industry.

“As a precursor, we introduced the Fasteners Pavilion this time and, going by the visitor interest generated by the pavilion, I am sure the Fastener World ME will soon become an important event for the regional fastener and fixing industry,” added the organiser. SteelFab 2016 will be held at the Expo Centre Sharjah from 11-14 January 2016. www.steelfabme.com
