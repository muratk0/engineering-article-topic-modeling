Earn an industry-recognized credential in precision press brake operation

Master the fundamentals of precision sheet metal bending with an understanding of the math, equipment, and tools of the trade. This intensive two-day seminar will teach you the theory and math fundamentals behind the machine to uncover the secrets to working with press brakes. The principles behind quality sheet metal bending will be taught with interactive instruction and sample work problems.

Through easy-to-understand exercises, you will learn the skills needed to calculate accurate bend deductions, select the best tooling for the job, and determine the correct V-die opening to avoid part distortion.

Who should attend?

Metal fabrication operators with at least 6 months’ experience

New and minimally skilled press brake operators

Experienced operators, engineers, designers, or programmers who want to improve their skills on the press brake.

FMA Member Price: $495.00

General: $695.00

Date: April 4 - 5, 2017

Location: TRUMPF Inc., Farmington, Conn.

Are you properly safeguarding your press brakes?

In this one-day seminar, lessons are based on real-world shop applications and cover the risk assessment process, ANSI standards, OSHA requirements, and application-specific safeguarding system selection. You’ll hear case studies of optimally safeguarded press brake systems and learn about current technologies that maximize compliance, safety, and productivity. Upon completion of the course and a passing score on the exam, you can earn an industry-recognized certificate.

Who should attend?

All levels of press brake operators or supervisors

Owners, operators, managers, safety personnel, or anyone responsible for safety measures at their company.

FMA Member Price: $295.00

General: $495.00

Date: April 18, 2017

Location: Harper College, Palatine (Chicago), III.
