# BLECH China 2014 delivers new sheet metal business

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

AFTER three busy exhibition days, the second BLECH China exhibition, held in Suzhou, closed its doors. The show, held at the Suzhou Exhibition Centre from 7-9 May 2014, attracted more than 4,000 sheet metal professionals, buyers and decision-makers from the Yangtze River Delta, China’s largest economic region, along with delegates from more than 30 countries and from most provinces across China.

“The show attracted a healthy mix of domestic Chinese and international companies, including suppliers from Germany, Italy, Switzerland, Sweden, Finland, Canada, USA, Russia, India, Japan and South Korea,” the show organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, explained. Blech China also featured a well-attended ‘Meet the Buyer’ programme and Technical Seminar.

A wide range of exhibitors presented a range of innovative machinery and products for both sheet metal forming and fabrication, with a firm focus on increasing productivity and output quality. BLECH China will return to the Suzhou Exhibition Centre in May 2016. See www.blechchina.com

‘China’s Sheet Metal Working Sector – a Market Overview’, from Brooks Market Intelligence Reports, examines activities within the complex Chinese economy and infrastructure. It addresses important factors including the state’s current attitude towards operating in a market-driven global economy, trends in machine tools and metalworking as well as the demographic and geographic issues affecting economic performance.

See www.brooksreports.com and select ‘China’s Sheet Metal Working Sector’. ■

## Simufact opens Japan office

ON 15 May 2014, Simufact Engineering opened a Representative Office in Tokyo, Japan. With this step, it enlarges its business engagement as a provider of process simulation software for manufacturing industries in the Japanese market.

For seven years now, Simufact has maintained a close partnership with NTT Data Engineering Systems Corporation (NDES), a leading provider of CAD/CAM/CAE software in Japan. As a Channel Partner of Simufact, NDES has been responsible for the distribution and technical support of the simulation software Simufact.forming in the Japanese market since 2007.

“The cooperation with NDES has been very successful,” confirmed Stefan Zimmer, Director Channel Operations of Simufact. “NDES has been able to enlarge the user base for Simufact software to more than 80 companies in Japan in the last few years.” See www.simufact.com ■

## Quality award for Stadco Powys

STADCO POWYS, the UK manufacturing site of major international automotive supplier Stadco, has been recognised by Jaguar Land Rover for the quality of the products that it supplies to its British-made vehicles.

The JLRQ Award was presented by Terry Sampson, Jaguar Land Rover’s Supplier Technical Assistance Director, at a special ceremony held at the Powys plant on 5 June 2014. Each of Stadco’s four facilities in the UK already has the ISO/TS16949 Quality Award, ISO14001 Environmental accreditation and OHSAS18001 Health and Safety accreditation. The Powys site now joins a select group of Jaguar Land Rover suppliers to qualify for the prestigious JLRQ award.

Stadco is a key supplier in the UK for Jaguar Land Rover, supplying parts for every vehicle it produces, including the Jaguar XF Sportbrake, Land Rover Defender, the new Range Rover and the Jaguar F-TYPE. ■
