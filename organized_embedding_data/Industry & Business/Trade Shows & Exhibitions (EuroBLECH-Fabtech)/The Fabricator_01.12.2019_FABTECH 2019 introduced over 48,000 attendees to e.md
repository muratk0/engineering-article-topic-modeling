# FABTECH 2019 introduced over 48,000 attendees to emerging technology during the November expo

[TARİH: 01.12.2019 The Fabricator]

FABTECH

2019, North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing event, welcomed over 48,000 attendees from around the world November 11-14 in Chicago. Over 120 new education sessions, interactive show floor features, and three exhibit halls full of new products and technology showcased emerging trends and manufacturing innovations.

Over 1,700 exhibiting companies showcased 500+ new products on the show floor. Attendees were able to witness the latest technology and products to make their businesses more productive and profitable, providing the best face-to-face opportunity to touch and demo emerging equipment and services. The new Robotics and Industrial Automation Pavilion was a huge success with over 60 exhibiting companies showing off the latest technology that is transforming the manufacturing industry.

FABTECH 2020 is set for November 18-20 in Las Vegas.
