# Stand reservation starts for EuroBLECH 2016

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

STAND RESERVATION for the 24th International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, EuroBLECH 2016, is now in full swing at the organiser's offices of Mack Brooks Exhibitions. EuroBLECH 2016 will take place from 25 – 29 October 2016 at the Exhibition Grounds in Hanover, Germany.

“As the leading international trade exhibition for the sheet metal working industry, the coming show will be a platform for interlinked sheet metal working production. The Internet of Things, or Industry 4.0 as it is called when it refers to manufacturing production, poses big challenges for the sheet metal working industry and raises a multitude of questions. We are currently experiencing a revolutionary development in requires customised solutions that fit precisely into each individual networked fabrication plant,” explained Nicola Hamann, managing director of the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

“Manufacturers of machines, IT systems and tools for sheet metal working have to position themselves now, in order to rank first as a provider of expertise and innovation for this new era of industry production. EuroBLECH 2016 thus offers a unique opportunity to be involved in designing the future of sheet metal working,” she added.

An exhibitor brochure with detailed information about the exhibition and stand options is now available from the organisers. The revamped show website, www.euroblech.com, offers detailed information about the international event, such as an exhibition profile, facts and figures about the show, details for stand bookings, an interactive floor plan as well as video footage and photos from the previous event. The new show website is user-friendly for desktop and mobile use.

EuroBLECH presents the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; handling; separation; forming; flexible sheet metal working; joining; welding; tube/section processing’ surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; machine elements; quality control; CAD/CAM/CIM systems; factory equipment and R&D.

EuroBLECH 2014 attracted 1,573 exhibitors from 38 countries over a net exhibition space of 86,500m2 and 59,618 trade visitors from 105 countries. It ended with a 5% growth in exhibitor numbers and a 3% growth in floor space compared to the previous event. The exhibition was, once again, more international: 52% of exhibitors and 37% of visitors were from outside Germany. There was a substantial gain in visitors from Europe and North America.
