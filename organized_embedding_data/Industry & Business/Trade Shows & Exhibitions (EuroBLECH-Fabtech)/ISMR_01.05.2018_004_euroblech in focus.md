# EuroBLECH in focus

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

THE 25TH EDITION of EuroBLECH, the International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, will take place from 23–26 October 2018 in Hanover, Germany. Already, over 1,300 companies from 39 countries have secured their stands.

"Industry experts, manufacturers and suppliers will come to Hanover to discover the latest technological developments in sheet metal working and processing, invest in new machinery for modern manufacturing and exchange expertise," the show organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

"As the world's leading exhibition for the sheet metal working industry, EuroBLECH offers a global platform for the presentation of the latest technology to a specialised audience of the industry's key purchasers and decision-makers."

EuroBLECH provides an overview of the full spectrum of conventional machines available, covering the entire sheet metal working technology chain. Across eight exhibition halls, a range of machines, tools and systems will be on display from 15 technology sectors.

Industry 4.0 will be one of the major focus points at EuroBLECH 2018 as the industry adjusts to move towards smart manufacturing and digitalisation. A total of 60,636 visitors from 102 countries attended EuroBLECH in 2016 and 1,505 companies from 41 countries showcased their products and services over a record net exhibition space of 87,800m².

See www.euroblech.com for further details.
