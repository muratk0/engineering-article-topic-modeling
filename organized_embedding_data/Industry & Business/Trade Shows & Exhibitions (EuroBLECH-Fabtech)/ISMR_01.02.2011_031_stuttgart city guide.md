# STUTTGART CITY GUIDE

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

Charmingly situated in the heart of one of Germany’s largest wine-growing regions, this vibrant metropolis fascinates visitors from all over the world. The state capital of Baden-Württemberg delights tourists not only by virtue of its magnificent panorama, impressive gardens and parks, fine squares, splendid palaces and buildings in a wide range of architectural styles, but also by its cultural diversity. A big draw for visitors to Stuttgart is the sensational Mercedes-Benz World as well as the magnificent Porsche-Museum. Discover the myth of those world-famous premium cars in Stuttgart’s automobile museums.

## TRANSPORT

Stuttgart’s public transportation (S-Bahn, subway and street cars) operates daily from about 5am to 1am. A book with all the schedule information for the entire transportation network can be purchased at the main train station as well as at the VVS ticket counters. The main train station is protected as a national monument and is located in downtown Stuttgart, in close proximity of the Königstraße (Stuttgart’s shopping district) and the Schlossgarten which is also known as the “Green U” of Stuttgart. Stuttgart airport in Leinfelden-Echterdingen (STR) is located approx. 13 km (8 miles) from downtown Stuttgart. The airport’s terminals welcome around 8.8 million passengers each year.

## STATE GALLERY

While the Old State Gallery contains works from the Middle Ages to the 19th century, the New State Gallery contains art from the 20th century, including works from Braque, Chagall, Dubuffet, Klee, Kokoschka and Nolde. The Gallery also has one of the most important collections of Picasso’s work. In addition to the featured paintings, sculptures and photography, the largest collection of European drawings and printed graphics within Germany, with more than 400,000 papers, can also be viewed.

## OLD PALACE

During the 13th century, a castle with a moat stood here. This castle was later the headquarters for counts and dukes in the 14th century. Under the supervision of Dukes Christoph and Ludwig, the castle was reconstructed in the middle of the 16th century into a renaissance castle. The inner courtyard, with its splendid arcades, is a result of the modification and is considered to be a showpiece of renaissance architecture. In the 18th century, the moat around the castle was removed. The entire building was heavily damaged in the Second World War. During the reconstruction, the premises of the castle was also converted into the Württemberg Museum.

## FESTIVALS

Throughout the year, Stuttgart celebrates many festivals: the Spring Festival, the Summer Festival, the Wine Village, the Beer Festival and the Stuttgart Christmas Market are the largest of the festivals which take place in the entire region.

## THE ARTS

The State Theatre features ballet, opera and theater performances and is the largest of its kind in Europe. It is located within the spacious Opera House (Großes Haus), which was built between 1909 and 1912 by architect Max Littmann. The Opera House was one of the few buildings in Stuttgart that was not damaged during the air raids of the Second World War.

## SHOPPING

The Breuninger fashion and lifestyle emporium offers an exclusive selection of great clothes, the latest in shoes, the newest bags, sparkling jewellery, scented beauty products and lingerie. At Germany’s luxury store, you’ll find all the modern brands such as Hermes, Fendi, Gucci, Dior and Marc Jacobs. Why not also enjoy the Königsbau Passagen right opposite the New Palace. It boasts 27,000 m2 full of different shops and delicious eating places. There are three Galeria Kaufhof stores in Stuttgart, each with a wide range of goods. There are also two Karstadt stores in Stuttgart. Store opening hours from Monday to Friday are 9:30am – 8:00pm. Smaller shops close at 6:30 pm. On Saturday, stores are open until 8:00 pm. Smaller shops and travel agencies close at 1:00 pm. For more information see www.stgt.com or www.stuttgart-tourist.de

## HOW TO GET THERE

## BY CAR

■ Directions from the A8 Motorway From the direction of Stuttgart: leave the motorway at the “Echterdinger Ei” junction and follow the access road towards the airport and trade fair centre. From the direction of Munich: exit the motorway at junction “Messe/Flughafen”. This leads you to the multi-storey car park over the motorway.

## ■ Directions from the B27

The car parks at the western edge of the trade fair centre are accessible from both directions, Stuttgart or Tübingen. The multi-storey car park over the A8 motorway is also accessible.

## BY AIR

The airport terminals are approx. 200 metres from the trade fair centre and can easily be reached on foot. There you also find the suburban trains (S-Bahn) S2 and S3 to Stuttgart main railway station. Travelling time is 27 minutes. For further information on arriving by air visit www.stuttgart-airport.com

## BY TRAIN

Stuttgart is directly connected to 13 European capitals via the rail network (ICE, IC and Inter Regio trains). From the main railway station to the new Stuttgart Trade Fair Centre, take the S2 or S3 suburban trains, direction “Stuttgart Flughafen/Neue Messe Stuttgart”. Travelling time is 27 minutes. For more train travel information, visit www.bahn.de
