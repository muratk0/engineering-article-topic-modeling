# BEACON OF THE NORTH

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

Mack Brooks Exhibitions is holding a new trade exhibition, geared towards the Northern European sheet metal working industry. The first BLECH Nordic exhibition for sheet metal working will be held from 4 – 7 October 2011 at Stockholmsmässan (Stockholm), Sweden. The event will be held alongside the Scandinavian Technical Fair, Sweden’s most important manufacturing trade exhibition which attracts around 25,000 visitors.

BLECH Nordic is part of BLECHevents, a series of dedicated sheet metal working exhibitions for important regional markets, organised by Mack Brooks Exhibitions every other year. Besides BLECH Nordic, two further events will take place in Russia and India in 2013. Both of these exhibitions – BLECH Russia and BLECH India – have already been held before, with good results.

## DEDICATED ACTIVITIES

Like the other two BLECH events, BLECH Nordic covers all sectors of the sheet metal manufacturing process, including sheet metal supply; tubes and sections; metal-forming and bending solutions; stamping; pressing; automation; flexible manufacturing systems; cutting; punching; joining/welding; tooling; laser technology; CAD/CAM; finishing/coating; quality assurance and material handling.

BLECH Nordic is a specialised sheet metal working exhibition for the Nordic countries. Leading suppliers of sheet metal machinery, tools and systems as well as manufacturers of sheet metal parts and finished products will display their latest exhibits to the industry’s key decision makers. Over 100 Nordic and international companies have already secured their space.

The organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, is hosting a programme of seminars in the presentation area over all four days of the show. The latest sheet metal research and new technologies will be presented by independent researchers as well as from a range of exhibitors. Presentations are free for visitors and are held in English or Swedish.

## THE NORDIC COUNTRIES

“In an expert market with an increased awareness for environmentally sustainable and energy-efficient production processes, there is huge potential for cutting-edge technology and innovative solutions,” said Susanne Rauberger, Exhibition Director, BLECH Nordic.

“Our dedicated trade exhibition BLECH Nordic, now offers manufacturers and suppliers of machines, tools, materials and services for sheet metal working an ideal platform to meet high profile trade visitors in Northern Europe.”

The Nordic countries are generally regarded as a highly stable economic region with excellent infrastructure and the latest logistics centres. Metal production is a traditional industry there and several major manufacturers of sheet metal working machinery are based in this region. The most important customers of machine tools for sheet metal working are the shipbuilding and offshore industries, construction, civil engineering and the automotive industries.
