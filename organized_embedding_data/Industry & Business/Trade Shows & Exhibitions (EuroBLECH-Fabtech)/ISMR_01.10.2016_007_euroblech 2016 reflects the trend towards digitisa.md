# EuroBLECH 2016 reflects the trend towards digitisation and automation in modern sheet metal processing

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

Discover the latest technologies for a new generation of sheet metal working at this year's EuroBLECH exhibition (25-29 October 2016) in Hanover, Germany. More than 1550 exhibitors from 40 countries are expected to present their latest developments, from all areas of sheet metal working, at this international showcase.

With "Industry 4.0" as a key topic in the manufacturing industry, EuroBLECH 2016 will focus on the smart manufacturing approach. With this year's theme "The New Generation of Sheet Metal Working", EuroBLECH 2016 reflects the trend towards digitisation and automation in modern sheet metal processing. Data exchange along the entire production value chain paves the way for optimised manufacturing processes, improved planning reliability, more flexibility and higher product quality.

"Sheet metal working professionals are currently seeking to adjust their production lines in order to stay competitive. The smart manufacturing approach is, to some extent, already implemented in large corporations but in most small and medium-sized companies, this new trend has not been realised yet. Therefore, it is more important than ever for companies to gain a comprehensive overview of prevailing trends and innovative processing solutions," the show organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

## Network for success

Mack Brooks Exhibitions announced a further increase in exhibition space for this leading trade show. A total of 1,550 exhibitors from 40 countries have already secured their stand space at this year's EuroBLECH, the 24th International Sheet Metal Working Technology exhibition, covering a net exhibition space of 89,000 square metres across eight halls (halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 27) at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds.

"This represents an increase in net floor space of 3% compared with the previous exhibition and reflects the fact that exhibiting companies have booked bigger stands to demonstrate an even larger choice of enhanced machinery and cutting-edge solutions. Major exhibitor countries are Germany; Italy; China; Turkey; the Netherlands; Switzerland; Spain and Austria," commented the organiser.

EuroBLECH is a highly international event and the world's largest exhibition for the sheet metal working industry. The event is viewed as a trend barometer for this industry sector. At the last exhibition in 2014, 52% of exhibitors and 37% of visitors came from outside Germany. A total of 59,618 trade visitors from 105 countries attended the event in 2014.

EuroBLECH will present a large variety of tailor-made solutions for optimising and modernising production processes. Exhibitors will display a comprehensive range of machinery, tools and systems for the entire value chain of sheet metal processing. The exhibition profile includes sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; welding and surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; quality control; CAD/CAM/ CIM systems and R&D.

"Many exhibiting companies have booked bigger stands this year and there are 20% of new exhibitors at our exhibition. This shows that the overall situation in the sheet metal working industry is very positive. Spurred by new technical developments, such as Industry 4.0, companies are now planning ahead to upgrade their production processes and to achieve a competitive advantage. At this year's EuroBLECH, there will be an above average percentage of new products live on display on bigger stands, and many new companies have joined the show," explained Nicola Hamann, Managing Director, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

EuroBLECH 2016 will also host the 8th Training Centre for Hot Stamping Technology and the 5th PHS Suppliers Forum, which will take place on 26 and 27 October 2016 in the Convention Centre at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds.

## Sheet metal champions

The second EuroBLECH online competition, which will take place during EuroBLECH 2016, rewards outstanding performance in sheet metal working. The entire sheet metal working community was invited to participate in the voting for winners (in five categories) on the EuroBLECH website, starting in summer. The categories are "Factory of the Future", "Academic Excellence", "Original Design", "The Young Generation" and "Clean Technology".

The theme for this year's online competition is "The New Generation of Sheet Metal Working". The online competition will reward five organisations or individuals in the sheet metal working industry for best practice, innovation, excellence and outstanding performance in five categories. The competition categories focus on recent developments and trends in the sheet metal working industry.

The first category, "Factory of the Future", will reward organisations or individuals for the clever implementation of smart manufacturing methods.

Leading university projects in science and engineering, involving sheet metal technology, can compete in the "Academic Excellence" category. The "Original Design" category is open to companies producing unusual products made from sheet metal. "The Young Generation" category seeks outstanding performance from junior employees and young inventors, and the "Clean Technology" category will reward eco-friendly solutions and sustainable manufacturing methods.
