# Blechexpo Travel Directions

[TARİH: 01.09.2017 ISMR]

## BY CAR

The new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre is 13km from downtown Stuttgart and right next to Stuttgart Airport. Follow the signs to the airport. The new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre can be accessed directly from the A8 motorway and the B27 highway.

## Access from A8 motorway

From the Stuttgart direction, exit the motorway at the Stuttgart/Degerloch junction and travel on the Messe and airport slip road. Then take the direct access road to the car park via the A8. If approaching from the Munich direction, there is a direct exit from the A8 to the car park over the motorway just before the trade fair centre.

Access from B27 highway from Stuttgart/Tübingen Car parks can be accessed from both directions at the west perimeter of the trade fair and congress grounds. Or continue on the L 1192 into the car park over the A8.

Input for satellite navigation systems Flughafenstraße/Flughafen, 70629 Stuttgart or, for older systems, 70629 Leinfelden-Echterdingen

## BY AIR

The airport terminals are roughly 200 metres from the trade fair and congress grounds and can be reached comfortably on foot. There is also a direct S-Bahn connection to Stuttgart central station with the S2 and S3. Travel time: 27 minutes.

## BY RAIL

Stuttgart is linked directly to 13 European capital cities via the railway system (InterCity Express, InterCity and Interregional). Stuttgart Central Station is located in the city centre, approximately 13km from the Stuttgart Airport and the new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre.

From central railway station to the exhibition centre From Stuttgart central station, travel with the S2 or S3 suburban railway lines (S-Bahn) in the direction of 'Flughafen' (airport) (Filderstadt) / Messe Stuttgart. The journey time between Stuttgart central station and Messe Stuttgart is 27 minutes. The S-Bahn operates from the central station from 04.55 (or 05.25 on Sundays and public holidays) until 00.25 and runs every 10 to 20 minutes.

The S-Bahn (urban rail) runs from Messe Stuttgart in the direction of central station between 05.08 (or 05.48 on Sundays and public holidays) and 00.08.

## BY SHUTTLE BUS

For large public trade fairs and specialist trade fairs, the 78 shuttle bus line operates between Degerloch and the trade fair grounds. You can reach Degerloch via rail lines U5, U6 or U8, with the cog railway or with the bus, from the bus stop Degerloch/B27 (north side of Löffelstrasse). Line 78 then takes you directly to the trade fair centre (West bus terminal).

## BOSCHERT GmbH www.boschert.de

WITH THE LAUNCH of its new fibre laser cutting machine, Boschert has developed a compact solution with a range of practical benefits. The machine offers a fixed table, sheet transport (X-axis) and a "flying" laser head (Y-axis).

"With high application flexibility, the machine table is easily accessible for loading and unloading. It also has a flap for the immediate removal of small, cut parts. These workpieces can be up to 350mm wide and 1,500mm long and can be discharged over the whole width of the table. The precise cutting system and the practical clamping concept also enable leftover metal sheet (as small as 500 x 50mm) to be processed, resulting in a very high degree of material utilisation," said the manufacturer.

## BYSTRONIC

www.bystronic.com

BYSTRONIC'S NEW XPERT 80 press brake offers increased power for versatile bent parts, increased bending length for complex individual parts and large batches as well as increased dynamics for rapid cutting sequences.

With the new Xpert 80, Bystronic is extending the concept of the mobile press brake, whose smaller version, the Xpert 40, has already established itself in the market. Bystronic product manager, Karsten Trautvetter, explained: "The great success of the Xpert 40 shows that there is a demand amongst users for fast, but also mobile, bending systems.

Compared to the smaller Xpert 40, the Xpert 80 offers an increase in bending length and press capacity. This widens the machine's spectrum of applications. Over a bending length of approximately 1.5m, the Xpert 80 generates a press capacity of 80 tons with a footprint of less than three square metres. The press brake can achieve rapid bending speeds of up to 25mm per second, according to the company.

## DALLAN SpA www.dalcos.com

IN JUNE 2017, Dallan SpA received official confirmation of its patent for the LaserVision system applied to coil processing lines. The Dallan vision system employs a high-resolution camera that identifies both the strip/panel edges and the pre-cut holes.

"The way it works is extremely simple for the operator, since operation is 100% transparent and the algorithm developed by Dallan software engineers takes care of the measuring and calibrating. This exclusive system is available in all Dalcos LXN and ELXN laser cutting systems and is designed to ensure high-performance, high-precision produced parts and flexible processing," the Italian manufacturer told ISMR.

The system can process long parts (up to 14m long), can start processing from coil (often directly from the steel mill) and pre-cut sheet, works with no side guide, can be rapidly set-up and is also able to process pre-punched panels.

## FLADDER DANMARK A/S www.fladder.com

AT BLECHEXPO 2017, Fladder Danmark A/S will present the Fladder-200/GYRO deburring machine with energysaving accessories. The accessories are designed to reduce customers' energy costs as well as reduce wear on the machine. All motors in the machine are regulated by frequency converters, especially when using the machine's vacuum system (to hold items during the deburring process).

"Normally, the machine is used at maximum vacuum i.e. 7.5kW, which easily holds items the size of a business card. However, full vacuum is only needed on very small items so users now have now the opportunity to work with reduced power (e.g. 4kW, which is sufficient for 'common' items). This reduces the noise level significantly. In addition, the machine is equipped with an energy-saving system that automatically shuts down the machine when not in use," explained the manufacturer.

## HANS WEBER MASCHINENFABRIK GmbH

AT THIS YEAR'S Blechexpo in Stuttgart, WEBER will be presenting deburring technology. Four separate product ranges of grinding and brushing machines for surface finishing, deburring, rounding and scale removal will be presented.

"One special feature of our grinding and deburring machines is their compact design. In the standard version, up to four machining stations can be set up in a space-saving and customised arrangement. For years, we have been using time-tested planetary head technology for edge rounding. The tools are arranged in a compact machining station in groups on planetary heads. The ideal tool arrangement ensures evenness in the rounding process," WEBER told ISMR.

The multi-rotation brush system machines parts with rim holes and slots from all sides with, according to the company, even machining results along the entire machine width. Both systems are also used for scale removal in heavy plate machining. Where large pieces require an even surface structure, WEBER machines use a large tool carrier with various machining heads. The machines are controlled via a touch screen with an intuitive user interface. WEBER I-Touch enables systematic navigation and direct input.
