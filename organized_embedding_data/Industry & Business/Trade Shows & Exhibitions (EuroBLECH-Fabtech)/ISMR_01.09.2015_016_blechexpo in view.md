# BLECHEXPO IN VIEW

[TARİH: 01.09.2015 ISMR]

Over the next few pages, ISMR previews selected highlights from the 12th Blechexpo and the 5th Schweisstec exhibition that will take place in Stuttgart from 3-6 November 2015

Don’t miss the 12th edition of the Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal, profile and pipe processing and the 5th edition of the Schweisstec international trade fair for joining and fastening technologies at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre in Germany from 3-6 November 2015. More than 1,000 exhibitors from 35 countries have already registered – a 14 per cent increase in exhibitor numbers and 21 per cent more exhibition floor space.

“We are pleased to confirm that over 100 new exhibitors have decided to present their companies’ products and services to national and international expert visitors for the first time at Blechexpo and Schweisstec,” commented the organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.

“A large number of well known manufacturers and distributors from fields such as measurement technology, image processing and vision systems have also registered to participate at Blechexpo and Schweisstec. As a result, the process sequence goes full circle because, for example, the digitisation of models and prototypes as well as metrological inline quality inspection have become indispensable building blocks in the process sequence for the processing of sheet metal, pipe and profiles – including the feedback of measurement data and correction values for production programs.”

International exhibitors from Germany (just under 670), Italy (approx. 105), Switzerland (36), the Netherlands (23), Turkey (22), France (19) and China (16) currently comprise the largest show delegations.
