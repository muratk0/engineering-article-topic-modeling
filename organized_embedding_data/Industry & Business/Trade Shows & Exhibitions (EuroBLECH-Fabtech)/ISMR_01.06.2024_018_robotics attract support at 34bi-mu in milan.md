# Robotics attract support at 34.BI-MU in Milan

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Less than six months before the debut of the second edition of RobotHeart, key players in the robotics industry have already confirmed that they will exhibit at the 34.BI-MU manufacturing trade show at Fieramilano Rho (Milan, Italy) from 9-12 October 2024. RobotHeart is dedicated to industrial robots and a range of manufacturing sectors.

Sponsored by SIRI, the Italian Robotics and Automation Association, and organised by EFIM-ENTE FIERE ITALIANE MACCHINE, the second edition of RobotHeart will attract, along with manufacturers and integrators, universities and representatives of the academic and research world; spin-offs and start-ups.

"To complement the manufacturing technology on display at the 34.BI-MU exhibition, there will be several thematic in-depth events, organised mostly on 'Robotics Day' on Friday 11 October 2024 in the BI-MUpiù arena. In addition to presentations by exhibitors, a rich programme of meetings will be held by the main organisations representing the sector such as IFR (International Federation of Robotics); SIRI; I-RIM (Italian Institute of Robotics and Intelligent Machines); AIIA (Italian Association for Artificial Intelligence) and UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE," commented UCIMU (the Italian machine tool, robot and automation systems manufacturers' association).

The BI-MU exhibition covers metal cutting; metal forming and additive machines; robots; digital manufacturing and automation; enabling technologies and subcontracting. For further information on the 34.BI-MU manufacturing trade show, see page 31 of this issue.■
