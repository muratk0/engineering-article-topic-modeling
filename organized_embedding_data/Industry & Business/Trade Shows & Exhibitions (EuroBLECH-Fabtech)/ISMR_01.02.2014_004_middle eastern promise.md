# Middle Eastern promise

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 ISMR]

EXPO CENTRE SHARJAH, which hosted the 10th SteelFab exhibition from 20-23 January 2014 that caters to the metal working, metal manufacturing and steel fabrication industry in the region, recorded a massive 35 per cent rise in visitors over the first two days of the show.

Riding on the back of a growing economy and the positive sentiment in the core sector due to the winning bid to host the World Expo 2020, the show attracted 5,979 trade visitors in its first two days compared to the 7,296 over all four days in 2013. The three new segments at SteelFab 2014 were Steel-Tech, Fabricators Pavilion and Steel Showcase while the regular special focus areas included Welding & Cutting, Material Handling, Machine Tools and Pipe & Tube Machinery as well as a section on Used & Re-Conditioned Machinery. ISMR had a booth at the show and its latest issue was distributed throughout the halls.

“On average, SteelFab has attracted about 7000 visitors every year. The high visitor turn-out this year is an indication of a return to the pre-recession years for SteelFab. I believe that, apart from the positive market sentiment, the expansion of the show by adding a new hall and new segments has played a key role in making the show much more attractive for the regional fabrication industry,” said the organiser.

“Along with the 50 per cent surge in exhibition space and a 35 per cent rise in the number of exhibitors, we are sure that the regional steel fabrication industry is in for a bright future.”

Apart from the official figure of US$ 24 billion earmarked for building infrastructure and other facilities for World Expo 2020, it has emerged in an HSBC report that the event would also attract an additional US$ 10 billion of private sector spending, positively impacting the fabrication industry. The construction and oil & gas sectors continue to be key demand drivers for the fabrication industry in the GCC, with the construction sector seeing nearly US$ 52 billion of contracts awarded during 2013 and the oil & gas sector planning to invest US$ 666 billion to boost its capacities, as oil prices continue to remain bullish. ■
