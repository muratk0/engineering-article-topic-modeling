# Here are key details on EuroBLECH 2016 in Hanover to help you plan your visit

[TARİH: 01.09.2016 ISMR]

EuroBLECH is a highly international event and the world's largest exhibition for the sheet metal working industry. More than 1500 exhibitors from 40 countries are expected to present their latest developments, from all areas of sheet metal working, across eight halls (halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 27) at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds. The event, which will be held from 25-29 October 2016, is viewed as a trend barometer for the sheet metal sector.

Exhibitors will display a comprehensive range of machinery, tools and systems for the entire value chain of sheet metal processing. The exhibition profile includes sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; welding and surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; quality control; CAD/CAM/ CIM systems and R&D.

## Sheet metal expertise

Comprehensive information for visitors planning their trip to EuroBLECH 2016 is now available. A visitor brochure in twelve languages can be ordered via the multi-lingual show website, www.euroblech.com. The leaflet includes the show profile, a plan of the exhibition halls, practical information about the exhibition as well as useful information on travel and accommodation.

The online exhibitor list, available at www.euroblech.com, is regularly updated and provides plenty of information on exhibiting companies. The online newsletter "EuroBLECH Bulletin" offers the latest news about the show, its exhibitors and the industry sector. EuroBLECH can also be followed on Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn and YouTube. The official hashtag is #euroblech.

The official EuroBLECH 2016 online show preview, with detailed information on many exhibitors and their products, is now available. With this user-friendly online preview, visitors will be able to create and print their personalised show preview, with company and product descriptions sorted by hall or category.

Additionally, new functions also allow sharing selected entries via social media. See http://www.euroblech.com/preview/english/

The EuroBLECH website is again offering a job board that allows specialists in the sheet metal working industry to search for a new professional challenge and to reach employers worldwide. Job listings are published on the EuroBLECH website. Participation as a jobseeker is free of charge. More information is available in the visitor section of the exhibition website, see http://www.euroblech.com/english/job-market

The EuroBLECH 2016 catalogue can be purchased on-site during the exhibition. Entrance tickets for EuroBLECH 2016 are now available via the website ticket shop at https://www.messe-ticket.de/DMAG/EuroBLECH2016/Shop?culture=en. Tickets can also be purchased on-site during the exhibition. The world's largest sheet metal working technology exhibition attracts top industry professionals from all over the world. It predominantly targets all sheet metal working specialists at all management levels in small and medium-sized companies as well as large enterprises. Visitors come from all key industry sectors. A total of 98% of visitors to EuroBLECH 2014 were trade visitors.
