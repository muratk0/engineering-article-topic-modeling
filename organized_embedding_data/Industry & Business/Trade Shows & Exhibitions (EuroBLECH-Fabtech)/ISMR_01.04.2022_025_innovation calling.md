# INNOVATION CALLING

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE in Poland will take place from 31 May – 3 June 2022 and features the Industry Next Congress.

This year's edition of ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE from 31 May – 3 June 2022. The trade fair will take place as a physical exhibition (with a series of supporting events, conferences and zones) in Poznań (Poland).

Three additional events will be held alongside ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE, namely the MODERNLOG Logistics, Warehousing and Transport Fair; the SUBCONTRACTING Industrial Cooperation Fair and the FOCAST Foundry Forum.

The trade fair traditionally combines industry, business and science. It is attended by nearly 1000 exhibitors who present innovative machines and solutions for industries such as metalworking; metallurgy; welding; surface treatment; varnishing; automotive; transport and railways.

Overseas companies traditionally account for nearly 50% of its exhibitors from countries which include Austria; Belgium; China; Czech Republic; Denmark; France; Netherlands; India; Germany; Switzerland; Sweden; Taiwan; Turkey; Ukraine; Hungary; the UK and Italy.

This year's ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE will also highlight current financial support openings and opportunities for companies who are operating in the industrial sector and planning to invest in the latest new technologies, confirmed the show organiser.

## Industry NEXT Congress

The first Industry Next Congress was well received last year at ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE. The Industry Next Congress this year will take place on the first and second day of ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE.

One of the key topics at the event will be the industrial strategy announced by the Polish Ministry of Development and Technology. Its scope is to reflect the current economic situation in Poland and respond to the challenges faced by industrial processing industries and, particularly, the situation in the energy market.

"During the Congress, we will discuss the situation in Poland and look for solutions to provide the Polish economy with stable conditions for further development," said Anna Lemańska-Kramer.

The congress will also be a discussion platform on the challenges faced by companies in the framework of EU energy and climate policy, and disruptions in the supply chain and intermediates. It will serve as a forum to exchange experiences on the application of Industry 4.0 and the Internet of Things by Polish and global manufacturers.

'Industry Decarbonisation' will also be another discussion panel at the Industry Next Congress. The need for decarbonisation is becoming more and more urgent. The Paris Agreement assumes zero-emission of the entire Old Continent until 2050, and one of the important documents already adopted is the MCP (Medium Combustion Plants) directive, which sets emission standards for sulphur dioxide (SO₂), nitrogen oxides (NOx) and particle solids (dusts).

"Newly constructed MCP facilities must already comply with these standards, and existing ones have time to adapt by 1 January 2025 for installations with a capacity greater than 5MW, and by 1 January 2030 for installations with a rated thermal input of at least 1MW and no more than 5MW. According to the McKinsey&Company report, 'Emission-Neutral Poland 2015', in 2017 the industrial sector was responsible for 22% of all emissions. This was mainly the result of fuels, cement, chemical and steel production," commented the show organiser.

"Industrial development will create an opportunity to use low-emission technologies in newly established plants. According to experts and the latest research, unfortunately, Polish production companies from various industries are only just starting the decarbonisation process. Therefore, we consider this subject to be extremely urgent and necessary," added Anna Lemańska-Kramer.

One of the key tools supporting sustainable development and green transformation initiatives are green bonds. Unlike traditional debt instruments that are available on international markets, they can only be issued to obtain financing for environmentally-friendly investments.

"By supporting eco-initiatives in the industrial sector, we see this subject as an opportunity for fruitful discussion at the Industry Next Congress," explained Anna Lemańska-Kramer. "For several years now, there has been a huge interest in automation, digitalisation and robotisation among managers of manufacturing companies. We are planning not only panel discussions with the biggest leaders in this sector, but also a diverse exhibition of solutions and devices dedicated to smart factories."

## New zones

"What matters at the ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE trade fair are innovations that support the development of companies, technologies that are the future of the industry and machines that are an implementation example of the latest solutions in the industry," said the show organiser.

For the show this year, a continuation of the Smart Factory Zone, which is rich in intelligent solutions for factories, is planned. Visitors will be able to see production processes in smart factories using the latest tools and solutions in line with the fourth industrial revolution. Examples will be based on IoT, digitisation and robotisation. The Smart Factory zone will be open throughout the duration of the exhibition.

Near the Smart Factory zone, there will be a stage where companies will be able to present their products or solutions in the "smart" spirit.

The Start-Up Zone last year was open for pioneering solutions "in the spirit" of Industry 4.0, which have already passed the testing phase.

"The idea of a space for start-ups in the industrial sector was all the more important as more and more 'young' initiatives based on technologies appear: digital twin, AI, Big Data or 5G intended for companies operating in the industry. We want to enable successful launches of new applications, technologies or machines and initiate successful business ideas," confirmed Anna Lemańska-Kramer.

The latest developments in metalforming, including product launches and the latest market releases will be highlighted at ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE.

"It is here that visitors will see machines in operation and meet the leaders of innovative technologies," added the show organiser.

Technologies on display will include machining; automation and robotics; tools; industrial metrology; software; maintenance; hydraulics; pneumatics and drives.

Copies of International Sheet Metal Review magazine will also be distributed throughout the show-we look forward to seeing you there!■

## Getting there

Poznań is situated in the centre of Europe, just a 2.5-hour drive from Berlin and three hours from Warsaw. It is served by Ławica international airport and a wide range of national and international rail connections.

MTP Poznań EXPO is on Głogowska Street, in the city centre. Several large hotels are in the immediate vicinity of the showgrounds. Show visitors can reach the railway station on foot in five minutes. Ławica Airport is 6km from the showgrounds.

Poznań International Fair and the main railway station are served by the “Bałtyk" bus stop. There is a bus connection to the airport via the “L" rapid bus line; bus line no. 59; bus line no. 48 and night bus line no. 242 (running between the main railway station and Ławica airport).
