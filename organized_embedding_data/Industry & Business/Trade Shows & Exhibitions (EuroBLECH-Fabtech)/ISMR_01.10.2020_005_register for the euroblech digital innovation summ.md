# Register for the EuroBLECH Digital Innovation Summit

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

27-30 October 2020

REGISTRATION IS NOW open for the virtual EuroBLECH Digital Innovation Summit, a four-day online trading, networking and webinar event for professionals from the entire sheet metal working technology chain. Held from 27-30 October 2020, the event will be a virtual hub for the global sheet metal industry following the cancellation of EuroBLECH, the world’s largest sheet metal working exhibition, this year. Visitor participation is free and online visitor registration opened in October 2020 on https://euroblech.eventnetworking.com/register?dm_i=4ZZT,EVIR,1UB647,1QLGO,1

“The Virtual Presentation Theatre features 15-minute exhibitor presentations on their latest product innovations and technologies, followed by a live Q&A session. Virtual Product Showcases create a virtual stand experience and allow users to explore new products and services. The Exhibitor Directory offers an easy starting point to exhibitor information, including product showcases, product videos, contact information and more,” the show organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

A new matchmaking system is powered by the latest AI technology and helps suppliers and buyers make smart networking decisions by suggesting relevant people to meet. Participants can tailor their networking activities by requesting and pre-scheduling their meetings before the event. Arranging a meeting is easy and intuitive, said the organiser, with no third-party software required.

“A daily programme of webinars provides an opportunity to gain useful insights into the latest market developments as well as technical expertise about new industry applications and solutions. A special series with a focus on the sheet metal working industry in key geographical areas offers an in-depth analysis on the current challenges and strategies to adapt to the COVID-19 pandemic,” added the show organiser. “This Digital Innovation Summit will provide our exhibitors with a platform to demonstrate their latest machines and solutions and arrange virtual meetings with international visitors. We believe it is our duty to assist the industry through these extraordinary times by offering a marketplace this year as a platform for innovations and a starting point for the recovery of the industry,” commented Nicola Hamann, managing director of Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

## COVID-19 crisis sparks innovation in sheet metal working

The EuroBLECH Digital Innovation Summit comes at a crucial time when sheet metal production must deal with the massive impact of the global pandemic and its disruption to industrial production in general. The “new normal” requires companies to adapt to new regulations and restrictions, such as social distancing rules, and to master an increasingly volatile market with sudden drops and surges in demand. All this poses a huge challenge for an industry that has tried to bring down lead times and manages complex supply chains.

“The key for post-crisis growth is agility, and this includes the knowledge and ability to find the right balance between justin-time, lean inventory and resilience,” explained Evelyn Warwick, Exhibition Director, EuroBLECH. “The industry will recover, but companies who want to survive and ultimately thrive really need to use the coming months to adapt and strengthen for the future. In fact, this crisis is a big moment for innovation, as barriers that once took years to overcome will become irrelevant within a matter of weeks, opening up new opportunities for those willing to adapt and meet shifting customer needs.”

Industry experts agree that the rise of formerly disruptive forces, in particular digitalisation, robotics and automation, has been significantly accelerated by the current crisis. The survival and long-term success of manufacturers will ultimately depend on how quickly they can deploy this new technology to boost their productivity and ensure full scalability of their operations.

“Digitalisation has certainly set the tone at the previous EuroBLECH 2018,” added Evelyn Warwick. “The last couple of years have brought noticeable technological advancement, and many exhibitors have already demonstrated how well the industry is prepared for digitalisation. At the same time, this technology is changing so fast that it is better to engage today as businesses start moving forward.“
