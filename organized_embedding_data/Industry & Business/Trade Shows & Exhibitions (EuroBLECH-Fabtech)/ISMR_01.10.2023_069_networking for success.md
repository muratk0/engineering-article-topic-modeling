# Networking for success

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

Turkish sheet-metal machinery specialist, MVD Makina Sanayi A.S. (MVD), has now returned from FABTECH in Chicago, USA, where it enjoyed considerable success. MVD and WYSONG have forged a partnership for the U.S. market, capitalising on almost two centuries of combined experience in machine manufacturing.

"Visitors from diverse American states, as well as our friends from the North in Canada and the South in Mexico, gravitated towards our booth at FABTECH. They were impressed by our comprehensive product line-up. The enthusiasm from loyal clients and prospective customers after the event has been truly inspiring and has played a crucial role in our success. We look forward to reuniting with our North American clientele at future exhibitions such as FABTECH Mexico, FABTECH Orlando and FABTECH Canada," MVD explained.

MVD is among the leading Turkish companies in the sheet-metal processing technology sector. It offers a range of press brakes such as the B100-3100 iBend hydraulic bending system and D320-4100 press brake, its H135-3100 (hydraulic and electric) model and the electric E100-3100 iBend press brake.

It also offers its F8-3015 laser-cutting system, with a special bridge developed for high-speed movements, the iLaser fibre laser-cutting machine and its B06-3100 iShear guillotine shear model. The company also manufactures the iPunch (punch press); iPlasma (plasma-oxy cutting); iExpand (expanded metal press) and associated bending tool systems. It has developed digital services as part of its digital transformation remit.

"The MVD family fully adopts the principles of sustainability in the light of continuous education and continuous development. Our principles of respect for people and nature are closely linked not just to our machine development but also to issues such as sustainability, improving our company culture and reducing our carbon footprint. In this context, we have made major improvements and implemented various projects and investments across all our production stages, and will continue to do so," it told ISMR.■
