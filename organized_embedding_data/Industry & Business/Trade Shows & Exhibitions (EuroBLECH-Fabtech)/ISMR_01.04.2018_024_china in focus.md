## CHINA’S MANUFACTURING SECTOR ADDED VALUE GREW BY 6.8% IN 2016

MIAO Wei, Minister of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, presented a report on promoting supply side structural reform to hasten the transformation and upgrade of the manufacturing industry. The report showed that, in 2016, the year-on-year added value of manufacturing industry grew by 6.8%, laying solid foundations for the country's GDP growth of 6.7%.

During the "12th Five-Year" period, said the report, the manufacturing sector in China achieved an average annual growth rate of 9%, becoming an important pillar to withstand the global economic downturn and maintain steady development of the macroeconomy. However, the overall characteristics of China's manufacturing industry are large and weak, said the report, and the development model based on large-scale expansion has not yet been fundamentally reversed. Therefore, said the Minister, ‘we must take the strategic transformation from quantity expansion to quality enhancement as the main direction to promote the structural reform of the supply side’.

## An international showcase

CIMES provides an international showcase and trading platform for the global advanced manufacturing industry in China, the largest global machine tool production and consumption market. Advanced manufacturing technology, numerical machine tool controls and automatic and intelligent factories will be showcased, allowing visitors to preview digital "Industry 4.0" changes and see the latest products, technologies and solutions to improve production efficiency and competitiveness.

For 30 years, CIMES has brought machine tool manufacturers and distributors together. The event organiser arranges face-to-face meetings with selected buyers from automotive production, the medical equipment sector, aviation and aerospace, electronics production and the rail industry with show exhibitors. For CIMES 2018, the event organiser intends to attract more quality buyers from key industries. CIMES also attracts nearly 100 additional buyer delegations from different industries. The event provides matchmaking services to pair buyer delegations and recommended exhibitors.

More than 1300 companies from over 30 countries and regions will attend this year's exhibition, with an expected audience of up to 150,000 (overseas buyers will account for more than 30% of total buyers), added the organiser.

"CIMES 2018 will include a summit, forum, technical seminars and other activities to provide visitors with opportunities to learn about advanced technology and investigate industry development. At the exhibition, they can meet global industry colleagues. Many well-known manufacturers will release new models, upgrade products and bring the latest technical information to the event."

GETTING THERE The New China International Exhibition Centre (NCIEC) is in the Tianzhu Airport Industrial and Commercial Zone in Shunyi District, Beijing. It features eight exhibitions halls and a gross indoor exhibition area of 100,000 square metres.

With convenient transport facilities, NCIEC is near the Capital International Airport in the east and Wenyu River Ecological corridor in the west. There is a highway network around the NCIEC and it is convenient to reach by bus, subway, taxi and airport shuttle bus. A 24-hour parking area is opposite the NCIEC.

Take subway line No.15 to the China International Exhibition Centre Station and walk 1.1km to the New CIEC. The organiser will set up special shuttle bus lines to the exhibition centre between 22-26 June 2018.
