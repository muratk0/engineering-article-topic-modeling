# Around 1,100 exhibitors from 33 countries and more than 35,000 visitors are expected to attend Metalloobrabotka 2019 in Russia

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

Metalloobrabotka, the international exhibition for equipment, instruments and tooling for the metalworking industry, is a major international show for the machine tool and metalworking industry. The show is organised by Expocentre AO and Stankoinstrument (the Russian association of machine tool manufacturers) and supported by the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade, the Council of the Federal Assembly of Russia and the Russian Engineering Union, under the auspices of the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

Taking place at the Expocentre AO in Moscow, Russia, from 27-31 May 2019, it showcases innovative research trends and developments in machine tool building and the latest solutions for the machine-building industry. The 2019 event is expected to attract over 1,100 companies from 33 countries.

The trade show features a strong presence from both Russian and international stakeholders. Nine national pavilions will be showcasing capabilities from countries such as Belarus; China; Taiwan; Czech Republic; Germany; Italy; Switzerland; the UK and the Slovak Republic. They are organised and supported by major industry associations and entities such as VDW (Germany); SWISSMEM (Switzerland); SST (Czech Republic); UCIMU (Italy); TAMI (Taiwan); CMTBA (China); Ministry of Industry of the Republic of Belarus and the MTA- the Manufacturing Technologies Association (UK). Other machine building associations will also be represented.

## New developments for 2019

This year's event will place a strong focus on additive technologies. A 3D Printing Sector will be in Pavilion No.5 to showcase raw materials for 3D printing; metal printing equipment and technologies; rapid prototyping; 3D scanning; hardware and software. Welding and related technologies, as well as robotics and technologies, will also be highlighted at Metallobrabotka 2019.

This year's Metalloobrabotka will also feature the 9th edition of the International Forum on Modern Trends in Technologies and Design of Metalworking Equipment, organised by the Stankoinstrument Association and Expocentre AO.

## An eye on 2018

Last year’s Metalloobrabotka event, held in May 2018 in Moscow over a total exhibition area of 41,000 square metres, attracted 1,097 company exhibitors and 32,497 professional visitors from 33 countries.

Eight country pavilions and group stands, presented by industry associations, dotted the event (Belarus; China; Czech Republic; France; Germany; Italy; Switzerland and, for the first time, the UK). Other participating machinebuilding associations were KOMMA (South Korea); MIB (Turkey); JMTBA (Japan); AFM (Spain); TMBA (Taiwan) and TIAD (Turkey).

A separate pavilion was occupied by 80 machine building companies from China whilst a group stand of machine builders from Taiwan featured Hiwin; Honor Seiki; Palmary; Quaser; SEYI; SOCO; Taiwan Taikisawa; Tongtai and YCM.

The first Additive Technologies 3D-Printing Sector showcased raw materials for 3D-printing, metal printing equipment and technologies, rapid prototyping, 3D scanning, hardware and software. The Stankoinstrument Association organised the Science, Vocational Education and Production section where leading technical universities and colleges introduced training programmes for the machine building industry. The participants included Bauman Moscow State Technical University; Ogarev Mordovia State University (Saransk); Moscow Polytech; Blagonravov Mechanical Engineering Research Institute (National Research University) and Ufa State Aviation Technical University etc.

The Industry Development Fund participated for the first time. It presented a VR simulator developed jointly with VRTech for training and evaluation as part of the development of the State Information System of Industry (SISI).

The first Job Centre at Metalloobrabotka gave information about current job offers from exhibitors, posted CVs and offered professional career development consultations. The centre was arranged by the Association of Personnel Selection Consultants and the Stankoinstrument Association.

The Expocentre for Counterfeit-Free Exhibitions project, aimed at minimising counterfeit products highlighted at exhibitions, was showcased online. The Stankoinstrument Association and ITO Publishing House also prepared special issues of the Metalloobrabotka newsletter (Russian only) devoted to the Russian exhibition and the most important challenges facing the industry.

A number of supporting and associated events were also held during the exhibition, such as the Russian Export Centre's 'Consultation Session on Export Support for the Metal Working Industry' organised by the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Russian Export Centre and Expocentre. The 8th edition of the 'International Forum on Modern Trends in Technologies and Design of Metal Working Equipment' was also organised by the Stankoinstrument Association and Expocentre AO.
