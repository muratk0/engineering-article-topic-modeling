# Empowering innovation

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

A spectacular display of innovations, high levels of visitor interest from all over the world and a lively atmosphere in the halls characterised EMO Hannover 2023 in Germany (18-23 September 2023).

"After the four-year break, the relaunch has been a huge success for EMO Hannover," summed up EMO Commissioner General, Carl Martin Welcker, after six highly eventful days in Hannover. "We saw everything here for the future of production: new solutions for automation, networking within the factory and sustainable production. When digitalisation finds its way into the factory, there is no end to the potential for new solutions and increased efficiency. This was impressively demonstrated by the exhibitors. There was a positive mood at the event, despite the tense economic conditions," he continued.

Around 1,850 exhibitors attended the trade show with approximately 70 per cent from 45 different countries (including China; Italy; Taiwan; Switzerland and Japan). Of the approximately 92,000 trade visitors, 54 per cent came from 130 countries. The five largest visitor countries were Turkey; China; the Netherlands; Italy and Poland. Around one third of trade visitors came from Asia.

The visitor survey revealed that 30 percent of visitors gave "obtaining information about innovations and trends" as their top priority. A further goal was "finding concrete solutions for their specific problems."

## Automation, digitalisation and sustainability

The shortage of skilled workers was a further reason why automation was a clear focus of the trade fair. Over one-third of visitors cited this as a key issue for the industry. Almost a quarter mentioned digitalisation and networking.

Several hundred robots were at EMO. It is no longer necessary to have programming skills to use cobots (collaborative robots) for different applications such as loading and unloading, quality control, painting, washing and connection to measurement devices.

Cobots were one of several key technologies at EMO Hannover 2023 (image: Deutsche Messe).

The cobots are equipped with sensors that mimic the human sense of touch. This allows them to compensate for workpiece tolerances or work around obstacles in the workspace. In collaboration with employees, this facilitates enclosure-free operation, for example.

"Cobots are continuing to enjoy rising popularity in manufacturing, especially in smaller companies that are now struggling to find staff," said Nils Tersteegen, marketing manager at Japanese robot specialist, Fanuc.

Another focus was on connectivity. The main emphasis here was on the open exchange of data based on OPC UA, for example. This is the basis for the Companion Specification OPC UA for Machine Tools under the umati umbrella. Retrieving large amounts of data from digital controls without affecting the process is a key factor. The availability of transparent process data is crucial for process monitoring and related quality management. See page 64 for further details on umati.

'The Future of Sustainability in Production' was high on the agenda for 68 per cent of visitors. A key aspect of this was efficiency.

## First-time visitors

"More than half of the visitors to EMO were attending for the first time. This dovetailed nicely with the exhibitors' goal of attracting new customer business. Around one fifth of the exhibitors were also at the event for the first time," commented the show organiser.

"EMO is a trade fair for executives and decision-makers from the mechanical engineering, automotive and supplier industries; metal processing; precision mechanics; optics; the aerospace industry and more. Almost 60 per cent of the visitors are executives or come from top management. Just under half have decision-making authority for purchasing and procurement. Half of the trade visitors actually stated that they came to EMO with concrete investment plans. On average, these visitors planned to invest just under three million euros. More than a quarter said that they had placed orders at the show," it added.

The next EMO Hannover show will be held from 22-27 September 2025 in Germany.
