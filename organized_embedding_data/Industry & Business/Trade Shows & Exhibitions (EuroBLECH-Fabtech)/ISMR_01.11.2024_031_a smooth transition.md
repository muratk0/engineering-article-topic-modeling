# A SMOOTH TRANSITION

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“ARKU held live part levelling and deburring demonstrations on its stand at EuroBLECH this October. The surface finishing specialist showcased its new generation EdgeBreaker® deburring system and FlatMaster® part-leveller as well as a laser-cutting line from coil.”

Gottfried Sihler, the new Chairman of ARKU's Management Board, and Andreas Hellriegel (Vice President, ARKU), outlined the company's strategic and technical priorities to ISMR at EuroBLECH in Hanover.

The EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition in Hanover, Germany (22-25 October 2024), is an important international launch pad for technology innovations and new product launches in the sheet metal-working process chain.

Highlights on ARKU's stand at EuroBLECH included an invitation to visitors to experience the future of levelling and deburring. They could experience levelling live, with demonstrations on ARKU's FlatMaster® part-levelling machine. ISMR also watched a live deburring demonstration on ARKU's latest EdgeBreaker® generation machine. The surface-finishing specialist also showcased laser cutting from coil on its stand, a project that it is pursuing with TRUMPF in Germany.

"We are very happy with the number and quality of leads (including new leads) at EuroBLECH this year. Feedback on our new machines has been great. Our proximity in Hall 11 to our partner TRUMPF's booth has also given us the opportunity to meet new customers," Andreas Hellriegel, Vice President, ARKU, confirmed to ISMR during the exhibition.

ARKU Maschinenbau is a longstanding family-run company, headquartered in Baden-Baden, Germany, with a second site in Bühl (producing its deburring and smaller straightening machine models).

Its Levelling and Deburring Centres in Baden-Baden (Germany), Cincinnati, Ohio (USA) and Kunshan (China) demonstrate practical sheet metal solutions for customers, drawn from practical experience based on real applications and tests.

Double-sided deburring

The new EdgeBreaker® 3000 NEXT deburring machine for punched and laser-cut parts was the focus of ARKU's presence at EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover. This machine deburrs and rounds laser-cut parts on both sides in one single pass.

“Thanks to a new drive solution, users save up to 40% on energy consumption. Visitors have a direct view of the process through a large glass front, which significantly improves process control. Additional functions increase productivity, which is already 50% higher than previous versions," the company told ISMR.

“The EdgeBreaker® 3000 NEXT removes hard burrs from both sides of the sheet metal in a single pass. With its secondary brush unit, it also edge-rounds from both sides. The double-sided deburring machine removes not only the strongest laser burrs e.g. from fibre laser cutting, but also removes splatters on the surface. The brush units deburr and edge-round sheet metal parts so that production can continue quickly, preventing bottlenecks and saving money," added ARKU.

ISMR also saw the universal deburring machine, EdgeBreaker® 6000, live in action on ARKU's booth. It has been designed for deburring, edge rounding and surface finishing operations. With rotating brushes, it can achieve a uniform edge rounding result of up to 2mm radius, according to ARKU. Its flexibility makes it an all-rounder for a wide range of processing results, shapes and dimensions (as used in job shops). "Our new EdgeBreaker® series are strong and good-looking machines, offering easy maintenance and very high deburring power as well as significant energy savings. We aim to further enhance our product portfolio in the years to come," confirmed Gottfried Sihler, the new Chairman of ARKU's Management Board, to ISMR.

Almost all ARKU levelling and deburring machines now incorporate the ARKU Wizard, an intelligent control system that adjusts the machines to achieve the best results. The software automatically selects the best machine settings for a respective processing task. The operator enters the known workpiece data into the control system (e.g. material, size of burrs, material thickness, desired edge rounding etc.). The software then automatically selects the best machine settings for the respective processing task. The levelling or deburring machine adjusts itself automatically.

## Live levelling demonstrations

ARKU also held demonstrations during EuroBLECH on its FlatMaster® 55 levelling machine, showing how important it is to level sheet metal parts after cutting to reduce unevenness and residual stresses.

The FlatMaster® series of levellers is designed to level parts, sheet and heavy plate (with thicknesses from 0.6 up to 60mm, plus high-strength parts (AHSS) and entire sheet/plate with cut-outs) with low residual stress. The part leveller incorporates ARKU's 60 years of experience in manufacturing levelling machines and more than 15 years of contract

velling. The part, sheet or plate is subjected to a series of alternating bends in the hydraulic machine. The material is formed quickly, allowing stamped, laser-cut or plasma-cut parts to be levelled in seconds. In comparison, manual flattening may require anywhere from several minutes to several hours. Using ARKU's FlatJack®, the flatness control system will automatically measure the flatness directly on the leveller.

## Laser cutting from coil

One highlight from the coil line area of ARKU's booth was laser cutting from coil, a project in collaboration with sheet metal giant, TRUMPF. It is sold under the name 'TruLaser 8000 Coil Edition'. The coil lines feed material to punching or forming presses and roll-forming systems.

"Our coil-line projects have, in the past, been primarily automotive-driven and optimised for high output. We have now made these lines more flexible and customised with our partner, TRUMPF," explained Gottfried Sihler.

“The laser-cutting-from-coil process enables sheet metal processors to flexibly produce even medium-batch sizes from coil. This results in much less waste when cutting parts compared to laser cutting from standard blanks. By eliminating the format limits, sheetmetal parts can be nested more efficiently. This enables material savings of up to 30% for large parts," ARKU explained.

In the ARKU leveller on the coil line, rollers plastically deform the sheet metal with the bend becoming smaller and smaller towards the outlet. The size of the levelling rollers ultimately determines which material can be levelled on the machine.

"The advantages for users are higher productivity due to the elimination of blankchanging times, lower material costs and less material waste. TRUMPF has calculated that the purchase prices for coil material are around 15 per cent lower than for comparable blanks. In addition, material utilisation is improved by 20 per cent thanks to optimal nesting," confirmed ARKU.

With increasing laser speeds and more frequent changes of material types and thicknesses, laser cutting from coil is becoming feasible for even medium batch sizes. Coil processing is well established amongst press shops; it enables identical high-volume products to be produced economically.

## Strategic and technical focus

ARKU's new Chairman of the Management Board, Gottfried Sihler, also shared his development strategy with ISMR.

“We are hoping that the German market, which has been struggling, will register a turnaround by the middle of 2025. In the meantime, our intention is to further internationalise our business i.e. develop more sales and service power in other regions of the world," he outlined.

"We aim to localise further i.e. increasing our presence in regions where we are not yet fully present. We will increase our footprint and sales power in those regions which are still growing. Will we fully change our product portfolio? No, I don't think so. It will be more of an evolutionary approach because sheet metal is still the core of our business. We will not change that. However, we will make it as easy as possible for any of our customers to have ARKU as the central access point for their machinery," he continued.

Andreas Hellriegel agreed with this outlook, adding: "We are the only European surface-finishing specialist with a direct company presence (our own service and sales staff) in the United States and China. We aim to further expand our presence in the United States, adding to our base in Cincinnati with a second site in Spartanburg (Greenville, North Carolina) later this year. It will be a combined levelling and demonstration centre. That should help us to offer contract levelling, faster service times and a sales force that is closer to the customer."

## Pinpointing market trends

Both Gottfried Sihler and Andreas Hellriegel pinpointed key trends in global sheet metal markets.

"The market is shifting. Some of our customers are still buying stand-alone machines but other high-end customers are choosing OSEON or other high-end connectivity tools to connect their machines to MES systems. I think we have an advantage there, at least for TRUMPF customers who are already using OSEON as it gives them a future-proof solution," commented Gottfried Sihler.

In its new production facility at Bühl, ARKU relies on an assembly line for the deburring machines and a fixed-station assembly for the precision levellers.

Andreas Hellriegel emphasised the popularity of fibre laser cutting systems and ease of operation in machine design. "Customers today want systems that offer easy operation: it is so hard now to find qualified workers, with labour shortages globally. That is why we offer solutions such as the ARKU Wizard intelligent machine control system on our systems," he told ISMR.

"Our HMI still differentiates us. Its ease of use and flexibility (i.e different rights management for users etc.) really make it easier for customers to operate our machines and avoid unnecessary breakdowns. Our aim is to design machines that will eventually become 'infallible' by making them as easy as possible for customers to operate in an environment of labour shortages," added Gottfried Sihler.

Artificial intelligence (AI) integration was one of the highlights at several booths at this October's EuroBLECH exhibition. It is a technology that ARKU is also developing in several key areas.

"We do use AI, to some extent, more generally in our organisation. This is for internal processes as well as to enhance the customer journey with us (i.e. in our service and sales offerings). As regards our product portfolio, we already offer an automation solution that incorporates AI. I think AI will gain in importance over the next few years, and we will definitely release more solutions that incorporate this technology," confirmed Gottfried Sihler.

## A sustainable future

With its ESG approach (Environment, Social and Governance), ARKU is pursuing a holistic concept to ensure that its actions “have a long-term positive impact on the environment, make a contribution to society and promote entrepreneurial thinking."

From an environmental standpoint, ARKU is increasing the energy efficiency of its machines, processes and sites, promoting the electrification of mobility and focusing on renewable energies. It has already implemented measures to reduce its carbon footprint and remains committed to increasing these efforts. Over a third of its vehicle fleet is electrified, so that many of its routes and transport are emission-free.

"We have just issued our new sustainability report, which can be accessed on the ARKU website. Our ESG approach is well advanced on the use of green power in our production as well as the steps we have taken of the machines that we produce. Our machines are built for durability, which feeds into sustainability. We also offer retrofits, particularly on our coil lines, which substantially increases the life of lines (typically, by around ten years)," outlined Gottfried Sihler.

From a social and governance standpoint, the company has invested in young people with a training rate of 11.9%, to ensure that it continues to have highly qualified staff in the future. ARKU offers apprenticeships in an environment designed to promote their personal and professional development and give them an opportunity to fully develop their skills. Every year, skilled workers also move up from ARKU's own training and dual study programmes. The company actively promotes its employees to fill vacant positions through further qualifications.
