# Singapore eyes value-added manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

Mr Lee Yi Shyan, Singapore’s Senior Ministry of State for Trade and Industry and National Development, examines a car model

AS THE INDUSTRY undergoes restructuring and gears itself towards high value-added and high-tech production, companies are confronted with a pressing need to rise above traditional boundaries, by embracing new technologies and acquiring new knowledge. Technologies for 3D printing and robotics have been earmarked by the Singaporean government as the future of manufacturing.

At MTA2015 in Singapore this April, exhibitor showcases dedicated to demonstrate these capabilities were well-received. In addition, equipment and solutions designed to cater to high-value sectors of aerospace, automotive and oil & gas sectors were also highly sought after. Over 20 group delegations from Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore attended MTA2015. More than 12,000 attendees from 41 countries/regions also visited the event.

“The 3D printing technology tours were very popular with attendees and highlighted the flexibility of the technology for applications across industries such as aerospace, automotive and oil and gas. Besides displaying a model tilted rotor airplane and a concept car, academic institutions – the Additive Manufacturing Innovation Centre (AMIC) at Nanyang Polytechnic (NYP) and the Singapore Centre for 3D Printing at Nanyang Technological University (NTU) – also showcased products that were wholly 3D printed from materials such as metal,” said the organisers.

Mr. Lee Ah See, President of the South Johor Foundry & Engineering Industries Association (SJFEIA), said, “MTA2015 had a good representation of exhibitors including group pavilions from Germany, Korea, Singapore, Taiwan and the United Kingdom. Besides the key industry manufacturing solutions providers, our members also had a chance to source from new exhibitors who cater to their engineering and machining needs. The association has always made it a point to form group delegations to visit the MTA exhibition and will continue to support the next show.”

Featuring case studies on digital manufacturing, Industrie 4.0 and industrial automation, the inaugural “Future Factories and Manufacturing Asia Conference” provided in-depth knowledge and insight into the evolving business and industry landscape for Asian manufacturers.
