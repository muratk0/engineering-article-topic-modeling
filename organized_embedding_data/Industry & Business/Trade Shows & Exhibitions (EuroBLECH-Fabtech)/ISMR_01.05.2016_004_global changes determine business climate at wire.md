# Global changes determine business climate at wire and Tube

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

GLOBAL ECONOMIC upheaval, a worldwide steel crisis, new climate regulations spanning the globe and a classic industry on its journey into the new Industry 4.0 digital age – that’s the business climate in which trade fairs wire and Tube took place for the fifteenth time in Düsseldorf from 4 to 8 April 2016.

More than 2,600 companies unveiled the latest machinery, equipment and products from the wire, cable and tube processing industries. A total net exhibition area of 110,900 square metres was occupied across 16 exhibition halls – a new record. A combined total of about 69,500 trade visitors from more than 130 countries attended the two events.

On over 51,100 square metres of net exhibition space, Tube – the International Tube and Pipe Trade Fair – covered the full range of pipe and tube manufacturing, processing, treatment and trade. Pipelines, OCTG technology, profiles, machines and the Plastic Tube Forum (PTF) supplemented the line-up of 1,278 exhibitors from 51 countries.

The strongest exhibitor contingents at Tube also came from Italy; the UK; the Netherlands; Austria; Switzerland; Poland; Spain; Turkey and Germany. The greatest share of overseas exhibitors came from the US; India; South Korea and China.

The two trade fairs, wire and Tube, will take place concurrently again in Düsseldorf from 16 to 20 April 2018. See www.wire.de and www.Tube.de ■
