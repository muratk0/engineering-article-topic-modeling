# BLECHEXPO IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

Over the next few pages, ISMR previews selected highlights from the 14th Blechexpo and 7th Schweisstec exhibition that will take place at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre from 5-8 November 2019.

ISMR SAYS: "Approximately 1500 exhibitors will showcase their technologies at Blechexpo-Schweisstec over nine exhibition halls in Stuttgart."

This year's 14th Blechexpo, in combination with the 7th Schweisstec, from 5-8 November 2019 will welcome exhibitors and expert visitors to Stuttgart to showcase sheet metal, pipe and profile processing technologies. The two complementary industry events will highlight comprehensive product and service offerings dealing with all facets of thermal and mechanical processing, as well as welding, joining and fastening technology.

The Blechexpo-Schweisstec trade fair duo takes place once every two years. Five months before the event, its project manager Georg Knauer of P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, reported that the event had already registered more exhibitors, more exhibition floor space and higher levels of international interest for Blechexpo and Schweisstec than two years ago. This increase, he told ISMR, correlated to over 150 primary exhibitors and a 20% rise in exhibition floor space as opposed to its recordbreaking year in 2017.

Approximately 1500 exhibitors will participate in this year's Blechexpo-Schweisstec, showcasing their products over nine exhibition halls in Stuttgart.

## Sheet metal still dominates

"As a structural material – along with pipe and profile materials – sheet metal has evolved into a common alternative to conventional cast iron, steel and plastic constructions. FEM optimised lightweight design and modular component systems characterise the image of modern, material, weight- and cost-saving vehicle, machinery and equipment manufacturing – all the more reason to focus on precision cutting, stamping and forming of steel, aluminium and non-ferrous sheet metal at Blechexpo and Schweisstec," the event organiser told ISMR.

"Expert visitors will be presented with solutions and systems covering all aspects of pre-processing and final processing, module assembly and surface finishing for sensitive, visible components. Lightweight design; design engineering solutions; handling; quality assurance; automation and digitalisation in sheet metal processing and joining technology are also featured at the event."

For the fourth time, "blechnet" and "MM-MaschinenMarkt" will present the "Award for Blechexpo" at Blechexpo in Stuttgart for the most innovative exhibits on display at the international trade fair. Five categories will be awarded. These are stamping and forming technology; separation technology; connection technology; surface technology and handling and automation.

## Blechexpo Travel Directions BY CAR

The new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre is 13km from downtown Stuttgart and right next to Stuttgart Airport. Follow the signs to the airport. The Stuttgart Exhibition Centre can be accessed directly from the A8 motorway and the B27 highway.

Access from A8 motorway Coming from Stuttgart, exit the motorway at the Stuttgart/Degerloch junction and travel on the Messe and airport slip road. Then take the direct access road to the car park via the A8. If approaching from the Munich direction, there is a direct exit from the A8 to the car park over the motorway just before the trade fair centre.

Access from B27 highway from Stuttgart/Tübingen Car parks can be accessed from both directions at the west perimeter of the trade fair and congress grounds. Or continue on the L 1192 into the car park over the A8.

Input for satellite navigation systems Flughafenstraße/Flughafen, 70629 Stuttgart or, for older systems, 70629 Leinfelden-Echterdingen.

## BY AIR

The airport terminals are about 200 metres from the trade fair grounds, and can be easily reached by foot. There is also a direct S-Bahn suburban railway connection to Stuttgart Central Station (line S2 or S3, travel time: 27 minutes).

## BY RAIL

Stuttgart is linked directly to 13 European capital cities via the railway system (InterCity Express, InterCity and Interregional). Stuttgart Central Station is located in the city centre, approximately 13km from Stuttgart Airport and the new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre.

From central railway station to the exhibition centre From Stuttgart central station, travel with the S2 or S3 suburban railway lines (S-Bahn) to ‘Flughafen' (airport) (Filderstadt) / Messe Stuttgart. The journey time between Stuttgart central station and Messe Stuttgart is 27 minutes. The S-Bahn operates from the central station from 04.55 (or 05.25 on Sundays and public holidays) until 00.25 and runs every 10 to 20 minutes. The S-Bahn (urban rail) runs from Messe Stuttgart to central station between 05.08 (or 05.48 on Sundays and public holidays) and 00.08.

## BY SHUTTLE BUS

For large public trade fairs and specialist trade fairs, the 78 shuttle bus line operates between Degerloch and the trade fair grounds. You can reach Degerloch via rail lines U5, U6 or U8, with the cog railway or with the bus, from the bus stop Degerloch/B27 (north side of Löffelstrasse). Line 78 then takes you directly to the trade fair centre (West bus terminal).

## AMB PICOT SAS www.ambpicot.com

AMB PICOT HAS been designing and manufacturing roll bending machines for over 150 years and is constantly expanding its range to create value for its customers. The company manufactures 2-, 3- and 4-roll bending machines.

"Our roll bending machines can produce material with perfect pre-bent edges, with no lamination, and can roll any metal, any thickness and any form (including Aluminium composite material). The machines are easy to use with our new easyroll patented CNC software. This intelligent rolling software uses springback prediction and simulation to enable users to reach their expected rolling diameter on the first attempt, with no corrections needed," explained Gregory Conraud, President, AMB Picot.

The company's double pre-bending plate bending machine features driven and synchronised rolls, with automatic and hydraulic synchronisation of roll parallelism. Other benefits, it said, include progressive speed and nowear components.

"THE BEST COST per cut part and short delivery times are decisive. A laser cutting system must therefore enable high processing speeds, a reliable cutting process and low maintenance costs. This means more cutting jobs and gradually increasing productivity levels, which build the foundation for growth. The high-end laser cutting system now features new 12-kilowatt performance, designed to boost profitability and speed of the cutting process even for high material thicknesses. To help ensure a reliable cutting process with very high laser outputs, Bystronic has systematically advanced the design of the cutting head. It is also introducing the new "BeamShaper" function for flame cutting. ## ARKU MACHINENBAU GmbH www.arku.com

After deburring and edge-rounding with the EdgeBreaker® 3000, a laser-cut part made of stainless steel looks like a completely different piece.

ARKU'S NEW DEBURRING machine, EdgeBreaker® 3000, features two sequential processing units which deburr and round laser-cut parts on both sides in a single pass. Its patented brush change click-system is designed to enable quick and easy replacement of the abrasives, leading to rapid and flexible adaptation to various deburring tasks.

"The automatic calibration system measures the abrasive consumption and compensates for this automatically. Visitors can see the new EdgeBreaker® 3000 live at our booth at Blechexpo. ARKU levellers ensure flat metal sheets and parts with reduced internal stresses – for parts as thin as 0.1mm (0.005") up to a max. thickness of 60mm (2.40"). See the FlatMaster® precision leveller in action on our booth at Blechexpo!" said the manufacturer.

ARKU also offers turnkey coil lines and handling solutions for sheet metal parts.

## DURMAZLAR MAKINA A.S.

www.durma.com.tr

LASER CUTTING and sheet metal processing company, Durmazlar Sheet Metal Machinery, has developed 3D sheet cutting technology designed to 'revolutionise sheet metal cutting'. Its new 3D laser cutting machine with advanced features is designed to accurately process 3D metal cut-outs.

"Durma 3D laser cutting machine cuts through metal alloy parts with thicknesses of between 0.5mm-8mm. It is mainly used in the automotive industry for drilling and perimeter cutting of pre-formed materials which cannot be punched. The 4kW Brilase Durma laser power source, developed by Durmazlar Machinery, is also used in 3D laser cutting machines to increase productivity" said the Turkish manufacturer.

"Thanks to its high cutting speed, the machine meets the needs of sheet metal processing sectors such as defence, aviation, white goods and the automotive industry."

## FACCIN SpA GROUP

www.faccin-gmbh.de

LASER CUTTING and sheet metal processing Faccin SpA Group combines a number of leading brands – Faccin, Boldrini and Roundo – in plate-rolling, profile bending and dished-head manufacturing, all united under one roof and offering a wide range of options in the metal forming market.
