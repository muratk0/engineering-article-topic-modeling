Show Preview International Manufacturing Technology Show

A comprehensive conference program targets job shops and those interested in additive manufacturing

Additive manufacturing technologies greeted attendees as they worked their way onto the IMTS show floor in 2018.

A

fter missing 2020 because of the pandemic, the International Manufacturing Technology Show (IMTS) returns to Chicago Sept. 12-17. The 33rd edition of this manufacturing technology show is expected to attract more than 2,000 exhibiting companies, occupying most of the McCormick Place campus. In 2018, IMTS had 129,000 people register to attend the event.

Although IMTS is known mostly for its collection of machining technology providers, including mills and lathes, many fabricators still might have interest in attending the event because The FABRICATOR’s own readership data suggests that almost 75% of metal fabricators who get the publication have some sort of machining activities in their facilities. Their chipping machines might be focused more on value-added activities rather than supporting machining-only work, but that still means plenty of metal fabricating companies have some interest in the main focus of IMTS.

The six-day event also hosts exhibitors from other areas of manufacturing as well. In addition to the Metal Removal Pavilion, where the machining technologies can be found, pavilions also cover:

Abrasive Machining/Sawing/Finishing

Additive Manufacturing

Controls and CAD/CAM

Fabricating and Lasers

Gear Generation

Machine Components/Cleaning/Environmental

Quality Assurance

Tooling and Workholding Systems

A Collection of Conferences

IMTS is a bit unique with this year’s iteration because of the plethora of conferences being offered.

"Content is key in the information era, and we have organized the widest array of conference topics ever at IMTS 2022 to help visitors move their people, business, and technology forward," said Bonnie Gurney, vice president, strategic partnerships for AMT – The Association For Manufacturing Technology, which owns and produces IMTS.

The IMTS 2022 Conference, Sept. 12-15 in the West Building of McCormick Place, is the largest collection of speakers and presentations, with more than 70 different sessions. Attendees can learn about process innovation, plant operations, quality/inspection, automation, improving productivity, improving part quality, and developing a competent workforce. Registration for each day of the conference also gives the attendee full access to the IMTS exhibit halls and a lunch ticket. One- and four-day passes are available.

IMTS is hosting a specialty program targeted at job shops. Job Shops Workshop, Sept. 12-13 in the South Building, features a presentation on workforce development and panel discussions with job shops implementing digital manufacturing technologies and workforce best practices on the first day and an indepth investigation of the shops that won Top Shop awards from Modern Machine Shop, also the sponsor of the two-day conference. Registration for these two half-day events also includes access the show floor and a boxed lunch each day.

To help IMTS visitors fully grasp digital technology possibilities, IMTS 2018 featured an Emerging Technology Center on digital transformation.

The Additive Manufacturing Conference is focused exclusively on additive technologies for industrial part production. The conference, organized by Gardner Business Media and taking place in the West Building, takes a practical, applications-based look at the machines, materials, and methodologies being used to create end-use tools and components. Registration to this full-day event on Sept. 14 and half-day session on Sept. 15 gives attendees access to the IMTS exhibit halls, breakfast and lunch, a ticket to a reception on Wednesday, and access to the conference presentations after the show.

3D Printing Workshop for Job Shops, a concentrated half-day seminar on Sept. 15, provides companies the opportunity to learn how machining facilities are applying 3D printers for tooling and for runs of parts that do not make sense on traditional machining centers. The focus will be on 3D printers at the price point of midrange machine tools on down to desktop machines, applied specifically in the context of machine shop work. Registration for this event in the West Building provides the individual access to the IMTS show floor and conference presentations after the show.

In total, more than 2,500 visitors attended conference sessions during the week of IMTS in 2018.

The Industrial Laser Conference, presented by the Laser Institute of America, focuses on laser processing for manufacturing, including marking, cutting, welding, heat treating, remote welding, cleaning, and additive manufacturing. Anyone registering for the Sept. 14 all-day conference in the West Building gains access to IMTS exhibits and the 2022 conference guide with presentation and speaker information. Lunch and snacks are provided on the event day.

Three new events are also part of the mix in 2022:

The IMTS Women Make Manufacturing Move

program is designed to inspire women in the manufacturing technology community through curated sessions and networking. Registration includes a Monday night reception, Wednesday luncheon, and a Friday afternoon mixer.

The IMTS Investor Forum

connects visitors with advanced manufacturing experts who provide insight about the $65 billion manufacturing technology industry. Guided tours of the trade show floor afford an inside experience with the industry. The tours will take place on Sept. 13 from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., with a reception to follow, and on Sept. 14 from 8:30 a.m. to 3:45 p.m.

The inaugural

Smarter Sourcing Symposium

on Sept. 16 is targeted at OEMs, tier suppliers, and contract manufacturers looking to establish or improve domestic supply chain performance. Symposium registrants will benefit from first-hand demonstrations and panel discussions on new tools and proven techniques for leaner, more agile, and more profitable domestic sourcing.
