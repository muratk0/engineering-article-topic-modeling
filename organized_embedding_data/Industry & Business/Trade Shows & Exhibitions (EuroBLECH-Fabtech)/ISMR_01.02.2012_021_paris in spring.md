# PARIS IN SPRING

[TARİH: 01.02.2012 ISMR]

Industrie Paris is expected to attract 30,000 visitors and 1200 exhibitors

ISMR “We preview the Industrie Paris exhibition taking place SAYS: from 26-30 March 2012”

Industrie Paris will take place from 26-30 of March at Paris Nord Villepinte in France. The event is expected to bring together 1,200 exhibitors, spread over nine complementary sectors, as well as 30,000 suppliers and contractors. The nine industrial sectors are Assembly – Mounting; Control; France Form & Tôle (Sheet Metal Trade Show); Industrial Computing; Machine Tools; Tools; Robotics; Surface/ Thermal Treatment and Welding.

## An industrial approach

The Ile de France region is a key economic area in France, boasting 8 competitive clusters with 16% overall of French industry. Approximately 35% of international investments made in France are in this region which has attracted 36,000 industrial companies employing 505,000 people (31% in the automotive industry including Renault and PSA, 26% in aeronautics including EADS, Dassault Aviation, Thalès etc. and 15% in the railway industry).

INDUSTRIE Paris 2012 has chosen to target four of today's most dynamic sectors: automotive, aeronautics, energy and medical. A range of presentations will be staged including "New hybrid and electrical engines in the automobile sector", "A reduction in fuel consumption in the aeronautics sector", "The evolution of wind power and nuclear in the energy sector" and "Specific requirements for precision material and surface coverings in the medical sector".

By encouraging opportunities for inter-sector technology transfer, INDUSTRIE Paris, a multisector fair dedicated to the production and design of equipment, has found a niche. It has therefore integrated a range of special initiatives to further this goal:

■ Expert sector guides, who will help point out exhibitors with specific expertise

■ Application workshops in which customers of exhibitors and experts will participate

■ A conference sponsored by a leading specialist magazine will be dedicated to each of the four main strands

■ The INDUSTRIE Intelligence Unit provides market research carried out exclusively for INDUSTRIE Paris 2012

INDUSTRIE Paris 2012 will be seeking out professionals in key production segments. A chartered plane will bring professionals in the aeronautics sector from the Toulouse basin to Paris on 28 March 2012 whilst a TGV train will be chartered from Marseille and Lyon on 27, 28 and 29 March 2012. Shuttles will serve the north of France (Charleville Mézières; Abbeville; Velenciennes; Béthune; Pont Audemer) from 26 to 29 March 2012.
