# Advice on sourcing materials

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

MATERIALS COMPARISON SITE, Matmatch, is inviting suppliers, design engineers and product designers to try its online materials database at MatX 2018. The company can be found on the ground level of NCC West at Bayern Innovativ’s international conference on materials innovation in Nuremberg from 27–28 June 2018.

Matmatch will be demonstrating its free-to-access materials database to visitors, to show how anyone involved in the designing, engineering and manufacturing of a product can source the right material and supplier efficiently and easily. Featuring over 80,000 materials from suppliers such as Thyssenkrupp, VDM Metals and Plansee, attendees will be shown how they can filter by application, category and properties to find the best-suited material for their project.

MatX will host a series of seminars during the event tackling the prominent topics and challenges in the materials industry at the moment. This includes the demand for producing and sourcing costeffective materials for large-area processable electronics.

"As material requirements and demands constantly change, design engineers need to have an effective tool to help them choose the best material for their product," said Melissa Albeck, CEO of Matmatch. "Our database helps engineers do exactly that and we want to show MatX visitors how they can benefit from using Matmatch, including how the online tool can help to improve internal processes.

"With Matmatch, engineers no longer have to scour through heaps of online data to find the right material. By using our intuitive database and search functions to streamline the process, engineers can save time and reduce costs by finding the right supplier for their requirements."

"MatX is also an opportunity for suppliers developing new and innovative materials to test the database's functionalities for themselves and find out how they can work with Matmatch to increase their own brand awareness. Being listed on Matmatch's database can connect suppliers with thousands of people searching for new materials to generate business leads."

The start-up, which is based in Munich, will be joined by fifty other exhibitors including FlexHyJoin, the ACAD group and the Bavarian Research and Innovation Agency. See https://matmatch.com/
