## A MARKET WORTH 1,500 MILLION EURO

Italy is the leading European consumer (and second world-wide) of metal forming machines and home to LAMIERA - one of the most qualified 
international shows in the field. The fifteenth edition will be held 12-15 May 2010 in the Bologna Exhibition Centre. The event promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE will focus the attention of operators in the world user industry on exhibitors at the show by acting as an essential occasion for defining new and more rewarding technical-commercial relationships. In 2008, LAMIERA was visited by 24,370 operators, 7% of which travelling to Bologna from 67 
international countries: production of machinery (28.4%), metallic constructions (24.9%) and the automotive sector (12.2%) emerged as the most prominent operating spheres, ahead of electric household appliances (6.4%), sundry engineering (3.7%) and aeronautics (2.6%). 
The protagonists at the 2008 edition of LAMIERA were 565 companies over an exhibition area equal to 27,632 square metres.
