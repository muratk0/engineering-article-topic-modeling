# BLECH EVENTS IN 2011

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

After its successful launch in November 2009, the dates for the second BLECH India trade exhibition for sheet metal working have now been published: BLECH India 2011 will take place from 14- 17 April 2011 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai.

“Companies interested in exhibiting will find extensive information on the new show website: this includes photos and facts about the previous show, featuring 50 exhibitors from 8 countries and 3,600 trade visitors. The new exhibitor brochure can also be ordered,” said Inter Ads – Brooks Exhibitions (India) Pvt Ltd.

BLECH Russia 2011, which will take place from 15-17 March 2011 at the Lenexpo exhibition centre in St. Petersburg, also has a new website with information and important facts for companies interested in the complex growth market of Russia. At BLECH Russia 2009, 170 companies from 20 countries presented their products and services to an audience of 3,500 trade visitors. See http://www.blechindia.com and http://www.blechrussia.com
