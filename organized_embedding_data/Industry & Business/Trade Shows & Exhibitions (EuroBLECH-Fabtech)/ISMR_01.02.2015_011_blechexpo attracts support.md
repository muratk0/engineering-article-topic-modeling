# Blechexpo attracts support

[TARİH: 01.02.2015 ISMR]

THE PRACTICAL trade fair duo of Blechexpo and Schweisstec takes place over a two-year cycle at the state trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and covers the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology. This year’s event will be held from 3 to 6 November 2015 and is over 70% booked, in terms of exhibition space, with exhibitors from over 25 countries to date.

Originally launched as a national trade fair targeted primarily at expert visitors in south-eastern Germany, Blechexpo is now ranked the world’s number two trade fair for sheet metal processing. Today, the catchment area for expert visitors extends well beyond Central, South and Eastern Europe, although these core markets receive the most attention where marketing communication is concerned.

The event, organised by P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, has enjoyed continuous growth in domestic and overseas exhibitor numbers, as well as an expanding expert audience which travelled to Stuttgart from more than 100 countries for the last Blechexpo/Schweisstec. ISMR is a media partner and will be exhibiting at the event. www.blechexpo-messe.de
