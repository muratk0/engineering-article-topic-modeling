# wire Tube

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

Key players in the tube, wire and cable industries will meet at Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre in Germany from 30 March to 3 April 2020 to present the latest machinery and equipment for wire and tube manufacturing; finishing and processing; finished products and services at wire and Tube 2020. Around 2,650 exhibitors from 54 countries, over 16 exhibition halls, will support the event. The trade fair duo expects over 70,000 visitors from 130 countries to register for attendance in 2020.

The show organiser has posted a strong increase in attendance for wire 2020 from Turkey, India and Taiwan, already exceeding final exhibitor numbers for 2018. Strong participation from the traditional exhibitor regions of Italy, Spain, Germany, Austria, Switzerland and Central and Eastern Europe point to new, record exhibitor figures for this year.

Düsseldorf

"For the first time, the new multi-purpose Hall 1, which holds up to 10,000 people

over 12,027 square metres, will offer Tube exhibitors new presentation options. More room also means potential for more growth: exhibitors can now book even bigger, versatile and digitally equipped exhibition stands to present themselves and their product portfolio. Finished products will be featured for the first time in the portfolio range of Wire 2020. Fasteners and springs will be on display in Halls 16 and 17," the show organiser, Messe Düsseldorf, told SMR.

## New features and initiatives

New start-ups will also be showcased at the event. Young, innovative start-ups from Germany, as well as international companies, will – for the first time – be promoted and supported selectively at the show. This includes the BMWi Pavilion for German start-ups and a joint stand for international start-ups.

Sustainable, environmentally friendly, energy-saving and innovative: this is how most manufacturing companies want to come across to the public. But the road to achieving this is a long and winding one, especially for resource-intensive technology companies in the wire, cable and tube industries.

Messe Düsseldorf is therefore focusing on a new ecoMetals Campaign for wire and Tube. The event will feature, for the first time, guided tours or 'ecoMetals-trails'. These will guide visitors to the stands of exhibitors who offer sustainable, resource-saving and lowemission production.

## Two show premieres at Tube Düsseldorf

Sawing and industrial cutting technologies will be specially signposted, under the banner of 'Saw EXPO', as part of Tube. Saw EXPO will be held as a separate trade fair from 8-11 June 2021 at the Friedrichshafen fairgrounds.

Interested companies can register online at www.sawexpo.de.

"This specialinterest trade fair is all about sawing machines and alternative cutting technologies; trowel equipment; deburring tools; accessories and consumables as well as peripheral machinery and systems. Saw jobbers; second-hand machinery; associations; publishing houses; service providers; science and research will complement the ranges," said the show organiser.

## wire 2020

Exhibitors at wire 2020 will present machinery and equipment for wire production; wire processing and finishing; process technology tools and auxiliaries; materials; glass fibre technologies; special wires and cables; measuring; control technology and inspection engineering; meshwelding machines and fastener/spring technology over 66,000m² of exhibition space.

“For the first time, finished products i.e. technical springs and fasteners, will also be presented. This means that the entire value chain, from machinery and equipment for wire and cable production and their processing and finishing all the way down to the finished products, will be on show,“ the show organiser explained.

## Tube 2020

Tube 2020 will showcase the complete process chain of the tube industry – from machinery and equipment for tube production; tube finishing and processing through to raw materials; tubes and accessories; second-hand machinery; process technology tools; auxiliaries to measuring and control technology and inspection engineering.

Tube trade; tube bending and sawing; OCTG technology; pipelines; profiles; machines and Plastic Tubes complement the broad, comprehensive range. It will be held over 52,000 square metres of exhibition space.

WTT-Expo will also make its premiere as a special-interest fair for industrial heat exchangers and heat transfer technology. It will be held in Düsseldorf, Germany, for the first time from 30 March – 1 April 2020.

"Energy-efficient systems for heat transfer are in high demand as energy increasingly becomes a cost factor for companies. As a result manufacturing companies, in particular, will start investing more in energy efficiency again.

According to the current Energy Efficiency Index (EEI), nearly three quarters of all companies spend more than ten per cent of their total investment on this," added the show organiser.

## Dynamic future markets

Over a period of 30 years, wire and Tube Düsseldorf have developed into leading trade fairs in their industries and, on an international scale, there are also eleven satellite shows on the themes of wire, cable and tube.

"Market leaders in their respective regions, they provide a boost for local industries and have high growth potential. Satellites in Russia, Brazil, China, Thailand, India and the USA therefore now form part of the portfolio of our Metal and Flow Technologies Trade Fairs," concluded the show organiser.
