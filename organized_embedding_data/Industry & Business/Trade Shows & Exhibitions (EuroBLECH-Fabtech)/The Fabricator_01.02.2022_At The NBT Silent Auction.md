[TARİH: 01.02.2022 The Fabricator]

Advancing Metal Fabrication Together™

Help NBT advance its mission while promoting your company among business colleagues! Donate an item or experience to one of the foundation’s most important fundraising events of the year — the silent auction at the FMA Annual Meeting in Miami. It takes place on Tuesday, March 1, 2022, at the welcome reception.

Your donation helps support the next generation of skilled manufacturers. Proceeds from items donated and sold help fund the summer manufacturing camp and scholarship programs of NBT. Both programs introduce and support students in their pursuit of rewarding manufacturing careers.

Show your support by donating or bidding on an item or making a cash donation. You don’t have to be present to win! Review the list of donated items and enter your bid online, set a max bid, or make a purchase using the Buy Now feature.
