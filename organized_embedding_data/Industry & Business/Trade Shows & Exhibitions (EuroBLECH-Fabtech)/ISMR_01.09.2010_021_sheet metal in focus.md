# SHEET METAL IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

The 21st EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition will be held from 26 - 30 October 2010 in Hanover (Germany). This year, the world’s largest trade exhibition for this industry sector will focus upon innovative technologies for the future of sheet metal working. Enhanced fabrication processes for adapting to changing demand, intelligent systems for energy saving and environmentally sustainable production and new materials will be on display.

With 1410 exhibitors from 42 countries, and a net exhibition space of 78,000m2, this year’s EuroBLECH is bigger than the show in 2006, a very successful year for the sheet metal working industry. This year will be the 10th anniversary for EuroBLECH at the Hanover exhibition grounds. The exhibition was launched in 1969 in London, moved to Zurich in 1974 and has been held in Germany since 1978 (firstly in Essen and then, in 1992, in Hanover).

On Thursday 28 October, a one-day seminar on Hot Sheet Metal Forming of High-Performance Steel will be held in the Convention Centre from 09.15 – 19.00. For further details, see http://www.euroblech.com/_downloads/pdf/EB2010_CHS2_Seminar.pdf

## INTERNATIONAL PARTICIPATION

International participation at this year’s EuroBLECH has risen again slightly, with 46% of exhibitors coming from outside Germany. Following Germany and Italy, Turkey now represents the third largest group of exhibitors. The number of exhibitors from Asia, particularly from China, Taiwan and India, has also increased substantially for this year’s show.

Traditionally, EuroBLECH is also a highly international event as regards visitor numbers. At the last exhibition, 35% of the trade visitors came to Hanover from outside Germany. A total of 69,400 visitors from 98 countries attended EuroBLECH in 2008. A total of 99.5% of visitors to EuroBLECH 2008 were trade visitors. Around 68% of visitors had decision-making capacity and 44% had concrete investment plans.

Visitors to EuroBLECH 2008 came from key industry sectors, such as mechanical engineering; sheet metal products, components and assemblies; steel and aluminium construction; automotive industry and its suppliers; electrical engineering/ appliances; heating, ventilation, air conditioning; electronics; rolling mill; iron and steel production; aerospace; non-ferrous metal production; precision engineering / optics and shipbuilding.

“EuroBLECH now boasts 1410 exhibitors from 42 countries and a net exhibition space of 78,000m2 “

“The current situation, a period of economic regeneration, demands a great deal of innovative capacity from every company,” Nicola Hamann, Exhibition Director of EuroBLECH at Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR. “After a difficult time, it is now important to set the course for the future by sourcing the right equipment. For industry experts all over the world, EuroBLECH 2010, with its theme ‘Time for Innovation’, is therefore the first choice when it comes to gaining a comprehensive overview of trends and innovative technology, and to finding suitable machinery and tailor-made solutions.”
