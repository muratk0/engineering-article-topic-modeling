# The Many Ways to Participate in Manufacturing Day

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 The Fabricator]

know in The

Manufacturing Day 2015 is officially Oct. 2.

Hundreds of companies across the U.S. have already registered their event on the

mfgday.com

website and are inviting guests to see for themselves what 21st century manufacturing looks like. Is your company included in that group? If not, consider this list of options for what you might still do:

Host a celebration of your workforce. Have a cookout or an ice cream social and invite them to bring their family members and friends to see where they work and learn what they do. Make sure they include their children.

If someone on your staff is a leader with a local Boy Scout, Girl Scout, or Indian Princess group, invite them to bring their group to your plant for a field trip and a talk about careers.

Contact a local school, perhaps one where a number of your employees’ children attend. Offer to host a class of eighth-graders for a field trip or go to them to do a classroom presentation about manufacturing careers. Check out the free Rock MFGDAY Kit available at

mfgday.com/resources

and use the available video and PowerPoint resources.

If your company belongs to a local chamber of commerce or regional manufacturing association or economic development organization, you can be the catalyst to bring a public showing of American Made Movie

®

to your town and educate members of the community about the importance of manufacturing to their local economy.

Contact your local community or technical college’s manufacturing technology department and offer to host a Job Shadow Day for their students who are preparing for work in your community.

Invite your mayor or congressional representative to visit and see the kind of work that is being accomplished by their constituents. You’ll have a chance to show them how the decisions they make when passing bills and approving regulations affect your ability to do business.

There are many ways to share the importance of manufacturing with those who may not know anything about it (other than what they hear or see in the news). Any day can be Manufacturing Day, so pick a day that works best for your company and your community and take just a few hours to share what your company and its employees do to make the world a better place. You just might be the inspiration that sets a young person on the path to a future career in the industry!

Register your event on the national calendar.

Complete information about Manufacturing Day can be found at

mfgday.com

or by calling 815-399-8700 or 888-394-4362. Manufacturing Day co-producers include the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association (FMA), the National Association of Manufacturers (NAM), the Manufacturing Institute (MI), and the NIST-MEP program of the Department of Commerce.
