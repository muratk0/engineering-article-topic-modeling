# Innovation in focus at Wire and Tube 2024

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Trade fairs for the wire, cable, tube and pipe industries will be staged from 15-19 April 2024 at the Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre in Germany. Trend fora, expert meetings and themed pavilions for stainless steel; hydrogen; other regenerative energy carriers; separating and cutting, plastic pipes and tubes plus finished products for fastener and spring-making technologies will be in focus. Some 2,600 exhibitors over more than 119,000 square metres of net exhibition space are expected to attend.

"For the first time, the innovations showcased by industry players in the exhibition halls will be accompanied by an extensive programme of supporting events tuned to the market on all five days," the show organiser, Messe Düsseldorf, told ISMR.

With 1,200 exhibitors over 52,000 occupied square metres in eight exhibition halls, Tube Düsseldorf 2024 is ready for take-off in April. The trade fair represents the entire process chain of the tube and pipe industry and is supported by trade associations such as the BDS (the trade association for German steel distribution) and ITA (the International Tube Association).

“Sustainability and energy efficiency are even more in focus this year. Daily ecoMetal trails touring the exhibition halls are aimed at convincing visitors of our exhibitors' novel, clean production methods," the show organiser, explained.

In addition to the classic themes, such as machinery and equipment for wire, cable and tube production, processing and end products, Wire and Tube places a strong focus on topics such as stainless steel, hydrogen, plastic tubes, cutting and slitting technologies as well as e-mobility.
