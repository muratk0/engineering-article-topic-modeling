# Wire and Tube focus on sustainability

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

A total of 2,700 exhibitors were in Düsseldorf, Germany, in April over five trade fair days on around 120,000 square metres' net of exhibition space for the Wire and Tube trade shows. Almost 1,500 exhibitors from 60 countries over 67,400 square metres contributed to the largest Wire show in its almost 40-year Düsseldorf history. The Tube show (with around 52,200 square metres) attracted 1,200 exhibitors from 54 countries. Energy efficiency and sustainability were major themes at the trade shows.

"Wire, cable and tube/pipe industries, as well as their upstream suppliers, are ready for future energy-related challenges," said the show organiser, Messe Düsseldorf.

"For the first time, a packed programme comprising lectures, presentations, special shows and digital guided tours accompanied the hustle and bustle in the exhibition halls. Special Areas and digital trails on hot topics such as ecoMetals; hydrogen; plastic pipes; separating; cutting; sawing and stainless steel provided information on their news and trends," it continued.

Specialists from numerous exhibiting firms presented their companies' sustainability strategies, discussed paths towards the green transformation and energy and climate policy with the audience or presented successes achieved on the way to decarbonisation.

Trends in wire and cable machines, in wire and cable manufacture, as well as the latest developments in glass fibre technologies, mesh welding machinery, spring-making and connection and fastener technologies were also presented.

"The exhibition stands in Halls 1 to 7.1 made it clear that the tube industry not only 'thinks big' but also boasts convincing, sustainable concepts for the use of renewable energies and the reduction of CO₂ emissions. The sectors using tubes and pipelines are as highly diverse as the requirements for the material properties and sorts/grades," added Messe Düsseldorf.

Apart from Germany itself, trade visitors also came from Italy; Spain; Belgium; France; Austria; the Netherlands; Turkey; Switzerland; UK; Sweden; Poland; the Czech Republic; the USA; Canada; South Korea; Taiwan; India and Japan.

"Noticeably more visitors than before came from Central and North America, Turkey and Asia," added the show organiser.

Wire and Tube will again fly their flags in Düsseldorf, Germany, from 13-17 April 2026.■
