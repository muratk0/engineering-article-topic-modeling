# An eye on India

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

BLECH
Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working 22-25 April 2015 Mumbai, India

BOTH BLECH India and Fastener Fair India will return to the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai in Spring 2015. Both events are co-located there with BLECH India taking place from 22-25 April 2015 and Fastener Fair India being held from 23-24 April 2015. The Bombay Exhibition Centre (BEC) in Mumbai is one of the largest exhibition centres in India and is close to airports, train stations and the city centre. ISMR is a media partner at BLECH India.

BLECH India encompasses all core sectors of sheet metal working technology such as sheet metal; finished and semi-finished products; forming; separation and joining technology; machine elements; handling; surface treatment and tools/dies. It attracted 5686 trade visitors (98% from India) and 187 exhibitors from 16 countries in 2013.

Fastener Fair India covers a wide product range of industrial fasteners and fixings, assembly and installation systems, storage and logistics services, fastener manufacturing technology and construction fixings. In 2013 it attracted 4,028 trade visitors and 145 exhibitors. The majority of exhibitors were Indian companies and almost a third of the exhibitors were from outside India, namely China, Taiwan, Germany, the UK, Italy, Vietnam and Korea.

India’s positive investment climate and vast consumer markets have resulted in a decade of outstanding growth. The country has witnessed significant development over the last few decades with the manufacturing and automotive sectors growing rapidly. The growth is set to continue over the next few years as the Indian government continues to develop its country’s public infrastructure. Continuing demand for consumer goods, such as cars, white goods, electronic appliances and electronic equipment from its domestic market, is contributing to the growth of the sheet metal working industry.

For further information on these events, see www.blechindia.com and www.fastenerfair.com/mumbai/english/

## New PMA Chairman of the Board

BILL SMITH, CEO OF TERMAX CORP. (Lake Zurich, IL, USA) was elected the 2015 PMA Chairman of the Board during the annual meeting of the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) in the US on 5 March 2015 in Orlando. His theme for the year – ‘To Whom Much is Given, Much is Expected’ – revolves around three core areas: mentoring, education and advocacy.

Ron Lowry and Dayton Rogers (Minneapolis, MN) and Dave Arndt (Pentaflex Inc., Springfield, OH) join Bill Smith with leadership roles on the board. Special thanks were given to outgoing Chairman, Jody Fledderman, for his leadership in 2014.

The association is also inviting interested members to submit an abstract for consideration for its 2015 Stamping Conference sessions at FABTECH (9-12 November 2015) in Chicago, IL (USA). PMA is the full-service trade association representing the US$ 113 billion metalforming industry of North America – the industry that creates precision metal products using stamping, fabricating, spinning, slide forming and roll forming technologies, plus other value-added processes.
