The 2019 Rusty Demeules Award for Safety Excellence

This award honors longtime former FMA volunteer Demeules’ passion for creating safe workplaces and is presented to one company each year that best exemplifies this dedication to safety.

The 2019 winning company will receive complimentary registration and travel reimbursement for one company representative to attend the 2019 Safety Conference April 10-11 in Lexington, Ky. An engraved crystal award will be presented at the Safety Awards dinner April 10, and the company will be recognized in FMA publications.
