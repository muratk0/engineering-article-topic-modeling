# Blechexpo in focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

A WIDE SELECTION of thermal and mechanical sheet metal, profile and pipe processing innovations plus thermal and mechanical welding, joining and fastening technologies will be showcased at the 13th Blechexpo and the 6th Schweisstec exhibition. The Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal processing and the Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology are set to take place in Stuttgart, Germany (7-10 November 2017) and has registered 15% growth in comparison to the last event. More than 1300 exhibitors from nearly 40 countries are expected to occupy around 1,130,000 square feet of overall exhibition floor space.

According to project manager Georg Knauer, of event organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, both Blechexpo and Schweisstec are demonstrating strong growth rates from German and overseas exhibitors, as well as occupied booth floor space. In cooperation with Vogel Business Media and its trade journal Blechnet, the Blechnet Award will be presented at Blechexpo and Schweisstec. Prizes conferred in several categories will be presented on the first day of the trade fair (7 November 2017) beginning at 4 p.m. at the exhibitor forum. See www.blechexpo-messe.de for further details.
