# Don't miss Blechexpo 2019

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

THE 14TH BLECHEXPO, in combination with the 7th Schweisstec, will welcome exhibitors and expert visitors to Stuttgart, Germany, covering all aspects of sheet metal, pipe and profile processing from 5-8 November 2019.

"The two complementary industry events provide expert visitors with comprehensive product and service offerings dealing with all facets of thermal and mechanical processing as well as welding, joining and fastening technology," explained the show organiser, promoters P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.

"The Blechexpo-Schweisstec trade fair duo takes place once every two years. Roughly five months before the event opens its doors, it is already registering more exhibitors, more exhibition floor space and higher levels of international participation than Blechexpo and Schweisstec two years ago. An increase amounting to more than 150 primary exhibitors and a 20% rise in exhibition floor space has already been verified, and it's becoming apparent that the results from our record-breaking year in 2017 will be topped," commented project manager, Georg Knauer.

A total of approximately 1500 exhibitors will set the tone at Blechexpo-Schweisstec over nine exhibition halls in Stuttgart. Bookings and reservations from overseas have doubled, compared to 2017.

As a structural material – along with pipe and profile materials – sheet metal has evolved into a common alternative to conventional cast iron, steel and plastic constructions. FEM-optimised lightweight design and modular component systems characterise the image of modern, material, weight- and cost-saving vehicle, machinery and equipment manufacturing. Blechexpo-Schweisstec focuses on precision cutting, stamping and forming of steel, aluminium and non-ferrous sheet metal.

"Expert visitors are presented with solutions and systems covering all aspects of preprocessing and final processing, module assembly and surface finishing for sensitive, visible components. The issues of lightweight design, design engineering solutions, handling, quality assurance, automation and digitalisation in sheet metal processing and joining technology are also extensively covered," added the organiser.

Stahlnews.de GmbH & Co. KG, in cooperation with the trade fair promoters, will organise the 4th Stahlnews Expert Meet on the evening of the first day of the trade fair, on 5 November 2019, to promote an intensive exchange of ideas and knowledge transfer. See www.blechexpo-messe.de/en/
