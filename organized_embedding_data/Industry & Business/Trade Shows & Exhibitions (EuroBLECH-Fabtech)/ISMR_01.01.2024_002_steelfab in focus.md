# SteelFab in focus

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

A key showcase for the UAE and region's economic and industrial growth, the SteelFab trade fair is gearing up for its 19th edition at Expo Centre Sharjah (UAE) from 8-11 January 2024. It has become an important trade show in the Middle East and North Africa region, dedicated to metalforming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs.

"The United Arab Emirates (UAE's) strong economic performance and the extraordinary resurgence of its construction industry are poised to set the tone for this forthcoming trade event for the metalworking, metal manufacturing and steel fabrication industry, the show organiser, Expo Centre Sharjah, told ISMR.

"SteelFab has played a significant role in the remarkable progress achieved by the UAE's industrial sector as well as in the adoption of advanced technology and promotion of innovation in the industry. The show has provided a shot in the arm for the country's industrial initiatives, resulting in higher industrial exports and a rise in the sector's contribution to the national economy," added H.E. Saif Mohamed Al Midfa, CEO, Expo Centre Sharjah.

Visitors can interact with over 250 exhibitors from 35 countries displaying over 600 brands. Over 7,000 trade visitors from 65 countries are anticipated to attend. SteelFab will host leading global manufacturers and dealers through special segments such as Welding & Cutting; Metal Cutting; Machine Tools; Metal Forming; Steel Machinery; Pipe Construction Equipment and Surface Preparation & Finishing.

SteelFab has increased its focus on several industrial segments in the manufacturing industry and topics such as AI & Robotics (a recent addition to its core focus areas) will be key attractions at the event.

The trade show will also feature the 'Best-Welder @SteelFab' competition as well as a huge live display of the latest welding and cutting machinery/equipment; a hosted buyers programme and an online B2B matchmaking platform.

Country pavilions; live machinery displays/ demonstrations and a dedicated vertical for the fastener and fixing industry (Fasteners World Middle East) are major features of January's event.

The show's visitor footprint extends across the entire GCC, Middle East, North Africa, Indian sub-continent and Central Asian region. Entry is free to trade visitors and there is free parking at the venue.■
