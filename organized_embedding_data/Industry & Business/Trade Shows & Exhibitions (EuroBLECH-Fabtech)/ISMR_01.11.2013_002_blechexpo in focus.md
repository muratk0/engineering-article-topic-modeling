# Blechexpo IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

DON’T MISS this year’s 11th Blechexpo and 4th Schweisstec/Coilex which will be held by private trade fair promoters, Paul E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre from 5-8 November 2013. The practical trade fair duo of Blechexpo and Schweisstec takes place every two years at the trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and covers the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology.

1137 exhibitors from 32 countries have already confirmed their participation at the 2013 event. A total of roughly 915,000 square feet of net floor space was occupied in 2011, and these figures have already been exceeded this year with over 430,000 square feet of net floor space or roughly 970,000 square feet overall.

A new special show entitled “Top of Automobile” will be held at the exhibition. Participants at this will include more than 20 companies from the technological disciplines for the production of vehicle components such as stampings, body components, body shells, exhaust systems, wheels etc. as well as the manufacturers themselves who will present their products.

ISMR is the international media partner for the event – meet us on our stand number 1301-2 in Hall 1. ISMR’s September issue holds a preview of the exhibition with selected new product highlights.
