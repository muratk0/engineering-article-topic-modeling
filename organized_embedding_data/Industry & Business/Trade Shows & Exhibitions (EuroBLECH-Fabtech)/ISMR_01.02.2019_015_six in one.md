## WINOVATION competition

Deutsche Messe's Turkey office, Hannover Fairs Turkey, will be holding a competition for the WIN EURASIA event. Organised in collaboration with ENOSAD, aimed at adding value to Turkey's industrial sector, the WINOVATION competition will reward projects that are focused on technical innovation and involve original designs.

Nominations can be submitted in one of the various categories such as products; services; design; technology; technical infrastructure; solutions; business models; organisation and social environment.

"The deadline for applications for the WINOVATION contest, which WIN EURASIA 2019 participants can enter with their completed projects with a brand or commercial value, is 31 January 2019. Projects ranking the highest will be rewarded at a ceremony which will take place on 14 March 2019 during WIN EURASIA.

"Projects which rank the highest will be chosen by a jury of ten members, consisting of professors who are experts in their fields, sector representatives and ISDER (the Materials Handling, Storage & Industrial Equipment Association of Turkey). At the evaluation stage of the projects, however, the criteria to be taken into consideration will include: uniqueness and authenticity; user benefits; innovations offered; openness to development and applicability; export potential; R&D quality; technological aspects; contribution to the environment; contribution to society and hightech and commercial features," said Hannover Fairs Turkey.

Projects which rank amongst the top three will be awarded an incentive for participation in the WIN EURASIA 2020 exhibition, with promotional and marketing support available throughout the year. The project that wins first place will receive, in addition to the previously mentioned support, a return ticket to the Hannover Messe 2019 exhibition which will take place from 1-5 April 2019 in Germany, as well as three days' accommodation, free of charge.
