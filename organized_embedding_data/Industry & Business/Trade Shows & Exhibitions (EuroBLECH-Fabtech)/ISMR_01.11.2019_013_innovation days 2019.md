# Innovation Days 2019

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

DURING ITS 'Innovation Days 2019' event, which hosted more than 300 visitors at Prima Industrie's headquarters in Collegno (TO, Italy), the Group opened the doors of its Advanced Laser Centre. This new facility of about 4000m² is dedicated to the research and innovation of advanced laser manufacturing systems. The premises are built with the latest sustainable building criteria and use geothermal systems and solar panels to minimise environmental impact.

The second edition of Innovation Days, Prima's international event dedicated to innovative technologies for Additive Manufacturing and sheet metal processing, was held in the new Advanced Laser Centre. Over 300 guests, including customers, universities and research centres, were welcomed to the event.

The new Advanced Laser Centre is part of a wider programme for the renewal of the group's sites in the Torino area, which began in 2016 with construction of the new Collegno Headquarters and Tech Centre which will be completed within the next year with the construction of the new production plant for laser machines, currently located in another area of the city.

Total investment is around 25 million euros and will allow Prima Industrie to increase efficiency, bringing the sites closer together in a single large complex, improving customer experience in the areas dedicated to visitors and profiting from more modern and organised work environments.

## New AM systems

During Innovation Days 2019, a new generation of systems for the additive manufacturing of metal components by Prima Additive, the Division of the Prima Industrie group specialising in turnkey solutions for Additive Manufacturing, were presented. The innovations shown during the event cover both AM technologies: Powder Bed Fusion (PBF) and Direct Energy Deposition (DED).

The new systems have been designed following the group's Open Innovation approach, which focuses on the needs and applications of customers, ensuring a close partnership with end-users of the technologies and strong adherence to market demands. Another important drive for the group's innovation projects is sustainability.

During its Innovation Days, Prima Industrie presented the Print Genius 250, the new PBF machine with a construction volume of 262 x 262 x 350mm, suitable for the production of medium-sized components. Print Genius 250 is suitable for high-productivity metal printing applications thanks to its dual 500 W single-mode laser, which reduces production time, combined with intelligent software for rapid part orientation and for the definition of machine parameters.

Print Genius 250 is suitable for the processing of a wide range of materials: stainless steel; maraging steel; high-temperature nickel-base alloy (Inconel); titanium; cobalt-chromium; aluminium and copper alloys, and high strength steel. Guests were also able to see the Print Sharp 250 PBF machine at work, with a construction volume of 250 x 250 x 300mm. The system is equipped with the Convergent CS450 proprietary fibre laser.

As part of Direct Energy Deposition technology, the Laserdyne 430 DED, a compact laser metal deposition platform with a working volume of 585 x 400 x 500mm, a high build rate (max 40-50 cm³/h - typical 20 cm³/h), suitable for 3D fabrication, reworking and repairing was also presented. It is equipped with the Advanced Head, a multi-purpose DED laser head of modular design that can be mounted in different configurations to adapt to multiple laser processes. The Advanced Head can be mounted on all Prima Industrie Laser Metal Deposition products, and is also available as part of Prima Additive's Laser Metal Deposition Kit, which includes the head, a high-efficiency laser source, a flexible powder feeder and CAM software for offline programming. The kit can be integrated into any machinery and robotic system.

The flexibility of the Laserdyne 430 DED can be further increased with the addition of an optional roto-tilting table, which adds two more axes for more complex applications. See www.primaindustrie.com
