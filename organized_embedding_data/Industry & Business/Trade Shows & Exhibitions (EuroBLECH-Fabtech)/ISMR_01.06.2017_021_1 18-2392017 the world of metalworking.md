## Supporting events at EMO Hannover

Various supporting events will also take place around EMO Hannover.

"We shall be organising numerous events designed to address, and indeed progress, important technical and commercial issues in the world of metalworking," reported Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, Executive Director, VDW. "They complement the manufacturers' range of exhibits, and communicate to visitors a comprehensive picture of trends and issues themed around metalworking.

These are exemplified by the "Industry 4.0 Area" with its presentation forum addressing various aspects of the motto "Connecting systems for intelligent production"; the "India Day"; the conference on additive manufacturing; the seminar on machine safety and the special show on aerospace technology

Other activities themed around youth recruitment, a match-making initiative and a special stand featuring international start-ups will also be features of the event.

Trade visitors to EMO come from all major sectors of industry, such as machinery and plant manufacturers; the automotive industry and its component suppliers; the aerospace sector; precision mechanics and optics; shipbuilding; medical technology; tool and die manufacture; steel and lightweight construction.

CONTACT

"Innovations are unveiled here which makes EMO Hannover a key point of reference for decision-makers in the production technology segment," said the organiser. "EMO Hannover presents an unparalleled width and depth of products and services throughout all production areas, from machine tools and production systems for cutting and forming (which form the heart of machining); precision tools; accessories and controller technology, to system elements and components for automated manufacturing."

Its principal focus is on metal cutting and forming machine tools; production systems; high-precision tools; automated material flows; computer technology; industrial electronics and accessories.
