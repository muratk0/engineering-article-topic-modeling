# Getting there

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Poznań is situated in the centre of Europe, just a 2.5-hour drive from Berlin and three hours from Warsaw. It offers a wide range of national and international rail connections. Ławica Airport is 6km from the showgrounds.

MTP Poznań EXPO is on Głogowska Street, in the city centre. Visitors are invited to the newly opened underground car park (Parking PWK). Several large hotels are in the immediate vicinity of the showgrounds. Show visitors can reach the railway station on foot in five minutes.

Poznań International Fair and the main railway station are served by the "Bałtyk" bus stop. There is a bus connection to the airport via the "L" rapid bus line; bus line no. 59; bus line numbers 48 and 59 plus a night bus line no. 242 running between the main railway station and Ławica airport.
