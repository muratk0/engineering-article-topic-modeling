# Growth for Blechexpo 2019

[TARİH: 01.10.2019 ISMR]

THE 14TH BLECHEXPO and the 7th Schweisstec will focus on 'outstanding process competence' for processing sheet metal, pipe and profiles. Held in Stuttgart, Germany, from 5-8 November 2019, the two complementary industry events are expected to break records, according to the show organiser.

“Exhibitor numbers are approaching 1500 and booked booth floor space has increased by 15%! The international flavour of exhibitors has also risen, compared to 2017. Bookings and reservations from overseas have doubled," P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, the show organiser, told ISMR.

A total of roughly 1500 exhibitors will set the tone at Blechexpo-Schweisstec over nine exhibition halls in Stuttgart, which represents an increase of more than 150 primary exhibitors. The 15% increase in floor space corresponds to the addition of roughly 1½ exhibition halls. The figures from 2017 for technology companies from Italy, China, Turkey and Spain have already been exceeded.

“The record-breaking numbers achieved in 2017 will be topped this year. The reason for this boom is the strictly practice-oriented concept of the trade fair duo: Blechexpo-Schweisstec covers all relevant issues and trends relevant to the digitalised, automated world of sheet metal processing and metalworking," said the show organiser.

The Blechexpo/Schweisstec trade fair duo features product and service offerings covering all aspects of thermal and mechanical processing of sheet metal, pipe and profiles, as well as welding, joining and fastening technologies. The technical event takes place every two years.

Special emphasis will be placed upon precision cutting, stamping and forming of steel, aluminium and non-ferrous sheet metal at Blechexpo and Schweisstec. Visitors will be presented with solutions and systems covering all aspects of pre- and final processing, as well as module assembly and surface finishing for sensitive, visible components. The issues of lightweight design, design engineering solutions, handling, quality assurance, automation and digitalisation in sheet metal processing and joining technology are also covered in the show.

In cooperation with the trade fair promoters, stahlnews.de GmbH & Co. KG will organise the 4th Stahlnews Expert Meet on the evening of the first day of the trade fair on 5 November 2019. Starting at 6:15pm, it will promote the beneficial exchange of ideas. This event will be held under the banner: “The Steel Industry in Times of Globalisation and Industry 4.0”. ZVEl's Schweisstec Day will take place on 7 November 2019.
