# SUCCESS FOR EUROBLECH

[TARİH: 01.11.2010 ISMR]

More than ever, this year’s EuroBLECH was the focus of the global sheet metal working industry for its role as the world’s leading trade exhibition and trend barometer for this industry sector. After a difficult time, the sheet metal working industry is now on the up again. Large increases in business activities were reported by many of the exhibiting companies, and the general atmosphere at the show was very positive.

In total, 61,300 trade visitors came to visit the 21st International Technology Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working. 1,455 exhibitors from 43 countries displayed a wide range of innovative solutions, cutting edge technology and an enormous amount of live machine demonstrations were held.

EuroBLECH 2010 was more international than ever. 46% of the exhibitors and 39% of the visitors came from outside Germany. 99.9% of all visitors were trade visitors. Almost every second visitor came to the show with the definite intention of buying, the organiser (Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd) told ISMR.

EuroBLECH 2012 will take place from 23 – 27 October 2012 in Hanover, Germany. Next year, Mack Brooks Exhibitions will again hold a series of sheet metal working exhibitions in selected target markets. BLECH Russia 2011 will take place from 15 – 17 March 2011 in St. Petersburg, BLECH India from 14 – 17 April 2011 in Mumbai and the first BLECH Nordic exhibition will be held in Stockholm, from 4 – 7 October 2011.
