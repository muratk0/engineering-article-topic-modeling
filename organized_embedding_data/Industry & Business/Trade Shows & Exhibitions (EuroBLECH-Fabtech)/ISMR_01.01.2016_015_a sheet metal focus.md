# A SHEET METAL FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

## We report on our visits to the Tolexpo and MIDEST events held in Paris from 17-20 November 2015

Sheet metal, coil, tube and section equipment professionals (including the ISMR team) turned up in force at Tolexpo, which took place from 17 to 20 November 2015 at the Parc des Expositions de Villepinte in Paris, France. The sheet metal event celebrated its tenth anniversary, sponsored by the French Ministry for the Economy, Industry and the Digital Sector.

“Exhibitors, visitors, companies, journalists and partners present at the 2015 show expressed their support in the wake of the tragic events in Paris on 13 November 2015,” the show organiser, DBR Events, explained.

Visitor numbers were affected by the tragic events in Paris. All in all, 8830 professionals from 49 countries (15.5% were from outside France) attended Tolexpo, the International Exhibition of Sheet Metal, Coil, Tube and Section Equipment, which took place alongside Midest, the global show for industrial subcontracting expertise, and Maintenance Expo, the maintenance solutions show. Entry badges entitled visitors and exhibitors free entry to the three exhibitions. Two hundred exhibitors (48% from outside France) also supported Tolexpo.

## New Tolexpo initiatives

The Tolexpo Trophy, one of the “Midest Trophy” awards, was staged for the first time at this November 2015 event. The Tolexpo Trophy is jointly run by the Tolexpo and Midest organisers. It is open only to Tolexpo exhibitors involved in the Tolexpo new products campaign.

“This is the only award in France dedicated exclusively to sheet metal, tube and section equipment manufacturers. The winner of the 2015 Trophy was Jean Perrot Industries with its Maneo Premium LCS – a press brake with automated bending measurement and control,” DBR Events told ISMR. Further details of this system can be found on page 56.

On the booths at Tolexpo 2015

Tolexpo also rolled out its new products campaign, which promotes major new products presented by Tolexpo exhibitors (products announced or marketed between two editions of the show i.e. between November 2013 and November 2015). This initiative, highlighting the sector's capacity for innovation – Tolexpo’s main driving force – also enables buyers looking for new equipment to glean information before the show and prepare their visits more effectively. Approximately seventy new products were showcased at Tolexpo 2015, including three ‘world firsts’.

“This year, AFORP, the industrial and technological training centre, took part in Tolexpo for the first time,” added the show organiser. “It has been training boilermakers for half a century and, for the past four years, has been running a creative workshop with a visual artist. Every year, its participants dream up and produce a large number of works, some of which were on display at Tolexpo.”

“Tolexpo targeted visitors are looking for new equipment and technological innovations, whether in relation to renewal or modernization of their existing equipment, adoption of new technologies, strengthening of their productivity or compliance with standards and regulations in the sector - all resulting in an increase in their creativity and competitiveness.”

## An eye on MIDEST

MIDEST, the global show for industrial subcontracting expertise, took place from 17 to 20 November at the Paris Nord Villepinte Exhibition Centre but, this year, it was very far from being business as usual. It was a show charged with emotion following the horrifying attacks and their consequences that had plunged Paris into mourning on the week-end before its opening.

“Out of the 1,603 exhibitors who had booked only some fifty, including around forty international exhibitors, did not occupy their stands. The cautious stance adopted by their governments or managements was fully understandable. On the visitor front, there was a 17% drop in numbers. Whilst certainly significant, initial feedback suggests that this was not as severe as it might at first seem in the light of a very high quality customer profile,” the organiser, Reed Expo, told ISMR.

This year, MIDEST’s exhibitors were drawn from 39 countries. Although France remains the most highly represented country, accounting for over 62% of the exhibitors, the show also featured a strong international presence made up of 572 companies active in all the major sectors of industrial subcontracting. A total of 34,070 professionals from 68 countries, planning specific projects, attended the event and 15% of them came from outside France.

The next Tolexpo event will take place in November 2016.

“For the first time this year, and reflecting our determination to offer professionals the benefits of actual experience, we put together a strong conference programme focusing on themes that are of central importance to industry. It was an encouraging start and the dozen lectures that were delivered attracted a good audience,” continued Reed Expo.

To reinforce and flesh out the themes addressed by this conference programme, MIDEST also set up, for the first time, four themed information points. Amongst other things, these allowed professionals to project themselves into a virtual world and to play with robots (Industry of the Future), to have themselves scanned with the chance to win a colour statuette of themselves (Additive Manufacturing), to undertake online vocational tests and to review job offers (Employment/Training) or to vote for the winners of the awards chosen by the public (Industrial Subcontracting Awards).

Wednesday was devoted to the major challenge of employment and training and it allowed manufacturers to make contact with young people undergoing vocational training. This day was staged in association with Fabrique de l’Industrie and involved numerous.

The automotive sector remains the number one customer sector for French subcontracting partners. Several events took place over the course of the day, including a number of speed meetings in which the joint President of Fabrique de l’Industrie, Louis Gallois, took part. There were also meetings between young people.

Amongst other events on offer to both visitors and exhibitors, the Industrial Subcontracting Awards shone a spotlight on outstanding pieces of work and the b2fair – Business to Fairs® business meetings provided a forum for subcontractors and/or customers looking to forge partnerships in a particular sector, market or geographical region.

## French subcontracting sector

In 2013, France’s industrial subcontracting sectors achieved total sales of €66.63 billion. This total represents the activities of 30,302 companies of all sizes, employing 485,050 people. For the year as a whole, companies employing at least 20 people recorded overall sales of €54.62 billion. It has been estimated that, in 2013, there were 24,610 subcontractors employing fewer than 20 people and their total workforce amounted to 106,174 people. They achieved sales of €12 billion.

The automotive sector remains the number one customer sector for French subcontracting, even though its relative importance has been declining for the last few years. In 2004, it accounted for 40% of orders whereas, in 2013, it was down to 28.8%.

## Midest goes global

MIDEST 2015, which ran in conjunction with TOLEXPO, the international exhibition of equipment for working metal sheet, coil, tubes and sections, was also an opportunity to present the fifth MIDEST MAROC. This major industrial event took place in Casablanca from 9-12 December 2015 and featured six major sectors - machine tools; subcontracting; plastics; services; electronics and sheet metal.

MIDEST 2015 also served as the venue for the official launch of MIDEST India, which will be held for the first time from 8-11 July 2016 in New Delhi.

At the show, the United Nations Industrial Development Organization (ONUDI) and Reed Exposition France signed a mutual interest agreement that will unlock new opportunities through the group’s events and reinforce economic links between the French private sector and ONUDI’s global networks e.g. the subcontracting and partnership exchange (SPX) network, the investment and technology promotion offices (ITPO) and the network of investment promotion agencies. As a result, MIDEST welcomed a delegation of subcontractor exchanges from the ONUDI network (Senegal, Cameroon, Kuwait) and 15 Cameroon companies coordinated by the Cameroon SPX, with the support of ONUDI.

The next MIDEST and MAINTENANCE EXPO will be held from 6-9 December 2016. ■
