FABTECH 2018 Introduced Over 33,000 Attendees to Emerging Technology

North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing event welcomed 33,755 attendees from around the world to FABTECH 2018 Nov. 6-8 in Atlanta. New education sessions, interactive show floor features, and three exhibit halls full of new products and technology showcased emerging trends and manufacturing innovations.

Over 1,500 exhibiting companies showcased new products and technology set to advance the manufacturing industry in the coming year. Attendees were able to discover the latest technology and products to make their businesses more productive and profitable, providing the best face-to-face opportunity to touch and demo emerging equipment and services.

The new Smart Manufacturing Hub featured displays and presentations on advanced manufacturing technologies including IoT, 3D printing, and more. An expanded 3D/Additive Manufacturing Pavilion introduced a Knowledge Bar designed to provide answers to 3D/additive manufacturing challenges.

FABTECH 2019 is set for Nov. 11-14 at McCormick Place in Chicago.
