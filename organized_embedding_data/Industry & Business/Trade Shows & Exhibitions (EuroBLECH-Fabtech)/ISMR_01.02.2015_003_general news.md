# GENERAL NEWS

[TARİH: 01.02.2015 ISMR]

BLECH RUSSIA takes place from 25 to 27 March 2015 at the recently completed ExpoForum in St Petersburg, at the heart of Russia’s fastest growing industrial region. It will be the fourth in a highly successful series of trade shows. Nearly 4,400 trade visitors attended the 2013 exhibition in St Petersburg, generating very positive feedback from participating firms.

Leading companies from around the world, as well as Russian firms specialising in the supply of machinery and products to the sheet metal market, have already secured stands at the show. They will be presenting their products and latest developments to buyers and engineers from leading manufacturing sectors. They are expected to include visitors from the automotive industry, mechanical engineering, sheet metal products and components suppliers, iron and steel producers, steel and aluminium fabrication companies, rolling mills and shipbuilding.

A new World Bank Group report finds that in the past year, the Russian Federation made it easier for local entrepreneurs to do business by implementing regulatory reforms in the areas of starting a business and registering property.
