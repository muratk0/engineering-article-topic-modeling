# Strong showing at BEC

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

“HIGHLY SATISFACTORY” is the rating given to the five industrial trade-shows – FERROFORMA, SUBCONTRATACIÓN, FITMAQ, PUMPS & VALVES and MAINTENANCE – held at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre, Spain from 26-29 May 2015. More than 18,500 trade specialists from 65 countries and from almost all of Spain’s autonomous regions attended the shows, making this the year’s biggest industrial trade event in southern Europe.

The countries from which most visitors came were Morocco; Algeria; Colombia; Mexico; Peru; India; Germany; Belgium; France; the Netherlands; Italy; Portugal; the UK and Turkey. Around 48% of domestic trade visitors to the event came from outside the Basque Autonomous Community, mainly from Andalusia; Madrid; Catalonia; the Community of Valencia; Cantabria; Galicia and Navarre. A total of 1439 people took part in the various seminars organised over the course of the week.

Almost 1200 exhibitors showed their products at this industrial platform. Taking advantage of these five trade shows, the Basque Government Department of Economic Development and Competitiveness held a press conference to present the results of its Industry Plan 2014, where Basque Government Minister Arantxa Tapia reported that the plan had directly impacted 3627 industrial projects, created 3490 new jobs in industry and had helped to maintain at least a further 16,121. Five projects had also been carried out with foreign direct investment to the tune of €44.5 million under the strategy “Invest in the Basque Country”.

A total of 592 exhibitors from 24 countries took part in this year’s SUBCONTRATACIÓN trade show, with the main participants from Portugal; Italy; France; Germany; Morocco; Belgium; Romania and Switzerland.

This year, two new areas were incorporated into the exhibition – the additive manufacturing area was organised by ADDIMAT (Spanish Association of Additive Manufacturing and 3-D), which also took the opportunity to hold its first governing board meeting during the event. The show also hosted the European Industrial Subcontracting Event, during which 1500 toplevel meetings were arranged. Overseas purchasers from automotive firms, amongst others, took part in the event which was reportedly the largest of its kind held in Spain to date. Aeronautics firms also had their own programme of prearranged meetings, presentations and round tables at AEROTRENDS.
