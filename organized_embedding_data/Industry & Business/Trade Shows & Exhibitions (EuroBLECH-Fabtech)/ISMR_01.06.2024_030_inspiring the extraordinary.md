# INSPIRING THE EXTRAORDINARY

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

Don't miss the IMTS 2024 manufacturing technology show in Chicago, USA, from 9-14 September 2024.

IMTS SAYS:

This year, the IMTS manufacturing technology show will be held from 9-14 September 2024 at McCormick Place in Chicago, USA. With more than 1.2-million square feet of exhibit space, it attracts visitors from over 110 countries. In 2022, the trade show confirmed 86,307 registrants (including more than 12,000 students) and 1,816 exhibiting companies, saw over 7,600 people attend educational events and included a Student Summit that introduced the next generation to manufacturing.

The event offers an opportunity to discover unexpected solutions at more than 1,500 booths, including more than 65 first-time exhibitors offering innovative solutions.

Examples include haptic feedback to improved remote robot operation and digital training; quality control software that can manage millions of data points; additive manufacturing powders and gases; business services to address labour issues via an app; and many more.

"IMTS covers manufacturing technology and related issues such as supply chain, workforce development and education. It showcases innovations in CNC machining; automation; robotics; additive manufacturing; software; inspection and transformative digital technologies that drive our future forward," explained the show organiser, AMT (The Association For Manufacturing Technology).

"The International Manufacturing Technology Show is where the creators, builders, sellers, and drivers of manufacturing technology come to connect and be inspired. It is the largest manufacturing technology show and marketplace in the Western Hemisphere. Be the change at IMTS 2024—our motto is 'Inspiring the Extraordinary'," it added.

## Conference registration

At the beginning of March, IMTS 2024 announced the opening of registration for more than ten conferences at IMTS 2024, an enhanced conference format and new IMTS Elevate programmes. The conference schedule runs from Monday 9 September to Thursday 12 September. All conferences are co-located with IMTS at Chicago's McCormick Place.

Conferences at IMTS provide deep dives into the technology showcased on the IMTS exhibit floor, as well as the opportunity to network with other professionals focused on the topic.

"We organised the conferences to fit into focused blocks of time that give visitor and exhibitor personnel more schedule flexibility," explained Bonnie Gurney, Vice President — Strategic Content and Partnerships, AMT. "Topics in 'The IMTS 2024 Conference', produced by AMT and GIE Media, cover the technologies exhibited on the floor, while the IMTS Elevate programmes bring to the forefront issues that are important to the manufacturing community."

All conferences require registration.

New events in 2024 include IMTS Elevate LATAM, a half-day conference presented in Spanish for the Spanish-speaking manufacturing technology community. The conference focuses on transformative technologies, sustainable manufacturing and regional trends in the high-growth markets of Mexico, Latin America and Spain.

Registering in advance provides a discount and includes full access to the IMTS exhibit halls.
