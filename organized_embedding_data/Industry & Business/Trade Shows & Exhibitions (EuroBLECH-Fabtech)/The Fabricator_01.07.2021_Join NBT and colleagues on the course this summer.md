OPC Processors Silent Auction

NBT will host the Processors Silent Auction at the Motor City Slitters golf outing on July 12, 2021, at the Detroit Golf Club. The auction benefits the Outside Processors Council (OPC) scholarship fund through NBT to support students preparing for careers in the metals industry.

Safety First

No zeros, only heroes here!

Has your shop gone an impressive number of days without a safety incident? If so, bask in your safety excellence with your colleagues at FMA’s 13th Annual Safety Conference sponsored by CNA in North Charleston, South Carolina, Oct. 20-21, 2021.

This year’s conference will be in person with a record number of winners being recognized for their safety achievements. Connect with metal manufacturing EHS professionals from across the country and toast to your strong safety culture.

Attend informative sessions led by industry experts to learn about the latest safety regulations, machine safety best practices, response plans, and more. FMA members receive a 20% discount and award winners receive a special discounted rate.
