# Don't miss EuroBLECH 2022

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

EuroBLECH 2022, the 26th International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, takes place from 25-28 October 2022 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds in Germany. Around 1,300 exhibitors from 39 countries have already secured their stand space at the leading trade exhibition for the global sheetmetalworking industry.

Read our EuroBLECH preview on page 37 of this issue of ISMR with the exhibition introduction, visitor guide and selected hall highlights. ISMR is a EuroBLECH media partner and copies of its October issue will be distributed at the show entrances. You can also meet ISMR on its stand H92 in Hall 14 at the show.

Currently, major exhibitor countries at EuroBLECH are Germany; Italy; Turkey; China; Switzerland; the Netherlands; Spain; Belgium; Poland; Austria; Portugal and the USA. Exhibiting companies have already secured a net exhibition space of over 88,000 square metres.

“A lot of innovations have been developed in the past few years, with a focus on cost and resource efficiency. While digitalisation and Industry 4.0 were topics that only large companies were achieving at the previous EuroBLECH, these key drivers have now reached the factories of small and medium-sized companies," commented Evelyn Warwick, Event Director, EuroBLECH, on behalf of the organiser Mack-Brooks Exhibitions.

EuroBLECH is a key event for design engineers; production managers; quality managers; buyers; manufacturers; technical directors and experts from associations and R&D to discover the latest trends and machinery in sheet metalworking. Visitors to this year's show can expect the complete spectrum of intelligent solutions and innovative machinery for modern production in sheet metalworking, which are presented in the form of numerous live demonstrations at the exhibition stands.

EuroBLECH is the largest exhibition for the sheet-metalworking industry worldwide and is the marketplace to discover and source the latest innovative manufacturing solutions. Numerous live demonstrations at the exhibition stands offer trade visitors the opportunity to experience machines and systems from all areas of sheet-metalworking in action. The EuroBLECH exhibition profile covers fifteen technology sectors and covers the entire sheet-metalworking technology chain.

On social media, EuroBLECH can be followed on LinkedIn and YouTube. The official hashtag is #euroblech.
