# RUSSIA CALLING

[TARİH: 01.04.2016 ISMR]

The Metalloobrabotka International Exhibition will take place in Moscow from 23-27 May 2016

Metalloobrabotka was launched in 1984 to become the largest metalworking and machine-building show in Russia and the Commonwealth of Independent States. It showcases the latest solutions and machinery to upgrade the Russian machinebuilding industry. This year, the event will be held at the Expocentre Fairgrounds in Moscow on 23-27 May 2016.

Minister of Industry and Trade of the Russian Federation, Denis Manturov, has sent a welcome address to the participants, organisers and visitors to the 17th edition of the International Exhibition for Equipment, Instruments and Tools for the Metalworking Industry – Metalloobrabotka 2016.

Machine tools and associated manufacturing technologies that are covered by the show include the following:-

- Metal cutting machinery.
- Metal forming machinery.
- Foundry equipment.
- Welding equipment.
- Thermal and coating equipment.
- Metal cutting tools.
- Control and measurement machinery, instruments and tools.
- Accessories, equipment, components, tooling, hardware and software.
- Repair and modernisation of equipment, spare parts and services.
- Modern information CALS technologies.
- Scientific research projects, technological and design developments, production management.
- Materials for metal working.
- Scientific and research papers and information; advertising in metal-working.
- Subcontracting and investment projects.
- Science, vocational education and production.

## An eye on 2015

Last year’s Metalloobrabotka trade show was supported by the Council of the Federation of the Russian Federal Assembly, the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade, and the Russian Engineering Union. It ran under the auspices of the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the Moscow City Government. Various international industry associations traditionally supported the show.

Around 949 companies from 32 countries showcased their developments, technologies and services in the machine tool industry and machinery manufacturing over a total exhibition space of more than 80,000 square metres. Ten national pavilions were organised at the trade show with the support of various professional associations. They represented companies from Belarus; China; the Czech Republic; France; Germany; Italy; Slovakia; Spain; Switzerland and Taiwan. Taiwanese manufacturers of machine tool products almost doubled their exhibition space. Russian machine tool industry accomplishments were showcased by 474 companies in the Russian regions.

An important event during the 2015 show for the Russian machine tool industry was the signing of agreements between the Stankoprom tool-making/holding and Russian machine tool companies. The agreements were aimed at start-up of more than ten new high-tech plants producing machine tools and machine tool products based on Russian engineering and technical achievements, in particular for the application of nanotechnologies.

“Metalloobrabotka 2015 attracted a large number of professionals, including CEOs of companies and enterprises. Within five days, more than 26,000 industry experts attended the trade show,” the organiser explained.

The 2015 Forum was organized by Stankoinstrument Association and assisted by Expocentre ZAO. Participants in the Forum were from different companies, associations and research institutes in the tool-making industry, among them KZTS (Krasnodar Heavy Machine Tool Plant), ChelyabNIIcontrol, Kirovgrad Hard Alloy Plant, VNIIALMAZ and others. One of the Forum’s hot topics was import substitution as a tool to develop the machine tool industry. ■
