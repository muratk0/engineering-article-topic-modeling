# A SHEET METAL FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

Over 39,280 visitors and 1336 exhibitors took part in the 13th Blechexpo and 6th Schweisstec 2017 in Germany.

The international trade fair for joining technology, was held for the sixth time from 7–11 November 2017 in Stuttgart, Germany. Product and service offerings, presented by 1,336 exhibitors from 35 countries, met with strong interest on the part of over 39,280 visitors from more than 116 countries.

"On the one hand, the international focus of offerings from Europe and Asia increased once more. We also saw an increased international element in visitors, which rose again significantly from 24% at the last event to 30% i.e. just less than one third of all visitors," the event organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, told ISMR.

As an international media partner at the event, distributing copies of its magazine at both show entrances, ISMR was also present with a booth at the event in Hall 1.

## An eye on manufacturing

With a range of issues targeted at the relevant industry segments (manufacturers and their suppliers), the event also featured a forum with a range of presentations on a variety of manufacturing topics. Over three days, just under 500 experts participated in 22 different presentations.

"More and more attention is focused on composite designs and hybrid solutions, not least of all as the result of lightweight design which, in actual practice, necessitates interdisciplinary work across individual fields of technology," commented the event organiser.

The "Schweisstec Session" which took place on the second day of the trade fair, inspired its target audience with eight presentations dealing with, amongst other things, Industry 4.0; welding technology and work safety; studwelding; laser welding and pipe processing.

"Inclusion of the 'Schweisstec Session' in the forum programme is strictly aligned to actual industrial practice. And industrial practice has virtually no more boundaries amongst the individual disciplines, not least due to indispensable, consistent automation of materials' handling as well as parts made of sheet metal, pipe and profiles," added the organiser.

The event also featured the newly built hall 10 (which has increased exhibition floor space at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre by 160,000 square feet to a total of 1,290,000 square feet), as well as associated infrastructure improvements (e.g. restructuring of the West entrance area).

"The entire world of thermal and mechanical sheet metal, profile and pipe processing is at Blechexpo, whilst Schweisstec is dedicated to all aspects of welding, joining and fastening technology. They have both shown 15% growth in comparison to the last time the events were held in 2015. Both exhibitions have increased overseas and domestic exhibitor numbers, as well as occupied booth floor space," confirmed P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.

## Forming trends and statistics

"The global market for forming technology had a total value of 20.5 billion euros in 2016 – a 4% increase compared to the previous year. With a share of more than 36%, China is by far the largest market for this technology, followed by the USA with a share of one-tenth. The German market has demonstrated 14% growth and currently holds third place, followed by Italy and Mexico," explained Sylke Becker, Director, PR for the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association) at the press conference on the opening day of Blechexpo 2017.

"In Germany, forming technology amounted to roughly 26% of overall machine tool production last year with a value of 11.1 billion euros. This figure does not include parts, accessories or services. Forming technology with a value of 2.9 billion euros was produced in 2016. This was 7% more than the previous year, driven mainly by large orders and project business with the automotive industry. Presses comprise the largest group of machines, followed by sheet metal processing with bending and press brakes, as well as stamping machines."

She added that German manufacturers of forming technology exported just under 60% of their production output last year. In 2016, exports rose by 1% and, during the first half of 2017, they increased by an additional 2%. China, she confirmed, is the most important market for Germany with a volume of 337 million euros last year and a market share of just under 20%.

"With a market share of nearly 19%, Germany is at the top of the list of international exporters of forming technology, followed by Italy, Japan, China and South Korea (ranking second through to fifth, respectively). It takes fourth place in the international rankings of the largest importers of forming technology after China, the USA and Mexico. German imports rose by 7% in 2016. Imported machines came mainly from Switzerland, where numerous subsidiaries of German companies are located, as well as from Italy, Austria and Japan," added Becker.

Technical trends in forming technology tend to be the same as those in the industry sector, as a whole. Issues such as digitalisation and networking, increased efficiency, sustainability and the need to recruit young talent are important. They were also mentioned by many of the manufacturers with whom ISMR spoke and interviewed during Blechexpo.
