# ASIAN PROMISE

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

Don't miss the MWCS 2016 show in Shanghai from 1-5 November 2016

ISMR SAYS:

"Access the vast Chinese metalworking market at MWCS 2016"

As one of the most representative industrial shows in China, the Metalworking & CNC Machine Tools Show (MWCS) offers 230,000 square metres of display space for the advanced manufacturing industries. Over 120,000 buyers from around the world are expected to attend this event, bringing new challenges to process upgrading, production line procurement as well as design engineering and solutions.

Organised by Hannover Milano Fairs Shanghai Ltd, Shanghai Eastbest International (Group) Co., Ltd, Deutsche Messe Worldwide and Hannover Fairs International GmbH, MWCS China will take place from 1-5 November 2016 at the National Exhibition and Convention Centre in Shanghai (China). The show's co-organisers are the China National Machine Tool Corporation; the Society of Automotive Engineers of China and the Taiwan Association of Machinery Industry. Concurrent shows, running alongside MWCS 2016, will be IAS 2016 (the industrial automation show) and the Energy Show.

MWCS opens the doors to the vast Chinese metalworking market and offers a trading platform with a global network. Over 600 exhibitors from China and overseas, on approximately 60,000 square metres, will display all kinds of innovative products, technologies and solutions for industrial development.

## 2015 in focus

MWCS 2015 came to a successful close on 7 November 2015 at the National Exhibition and Convention Centre (Shanghai Hongqiao). MWCS and IAS followed the Industry 4.0 trend and kept pace with the 'Made in China 2025' strategy, celebrating a grand slam in exhibition scale and enterprise investment. The 5-day shows attracted 136,000 visits.

As one of the most influential international metalworking exhibitions in the Asia-Pacific area, MWCS 2015 was held for the first time in Hall 2 and Hall 3 of the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai). It focused on several categories including metal cutting machine tools, metal forming machine tool and special machine tools, promoting the transformation and upgrade of China's machine tool industry.

MWCS 2015 attracted 469 exhibitors within a display area of 60,000 square metres, as an international show for machine tool, sheet metal and piping processing as well as the smart manufacturing of mould and tools.

"IAS (its concurrent event) is the largest and most influential industrial automation show in Asia, covering four categories including system control, sensor connector, motors and accessories for chassis and cabinet and robotics. IAS 2015 presented a feast of smart manufacturing in which 545 well-known companies demonstrated the splendid development of industrial automation with brand new features and top technology within a display area of 50,000 square metres," said the show organiser.
