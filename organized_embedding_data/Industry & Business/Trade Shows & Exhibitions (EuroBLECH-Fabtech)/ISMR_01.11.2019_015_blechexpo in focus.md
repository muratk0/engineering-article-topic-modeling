# BLECHEXPO IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.11.2019 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“With nine occupied halls and a strong international focus, this year's Blechexpo highlighted key themes of digitalisation and major research areas

We report from this November's record-breaking Blechexpo in Stuttgart, which attracted 1498 exhibitors and 41,152 visitors.

Visitors brought themselves up to date with the latest sheet metal, pipe and profile processing, as well as cutting, joining, forming and developments.

The 14th Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal working, together with the 7th Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology, held from 5-8 November 2019 closed successfully with 1498 exhibitors from 36 countries. 41,152 visitors from a total of 113 countries took advantage of the trade fair duo, which has now grown to cover 108,000 square metres of exhibition floor space. Visitors brought themselves up to date with the latest sheet metal, pipe and profile processing, as well as cutting, joining, forming and welding technology developments.

The Blechexpo/Schweisstec trade fair duo broke several records this year: 1498 exhibitors from 36 countries (as opposed to 1339 in 2017) were on hand in Stuttgart.

"45% of exhibitors came from outside Germany," Georg Knauer of Blechexpo organiser P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, told ISMR. "This year's Blechexpo/Schweisstec was more international than ever before."

With a total of 108,000 square metres, roughly 15% more exhibition floor space was booked than in 2017. For the first time, Blechexpo/ Schweisstec occupied a new exhibition hall 10.

## Trends and directions

"For us, this technical event is a leading trade fair of very high significance," remarked Wolfgang Wiedenmann, deputy sales manager at press manufacturer Andritz Kaiser.

Blechexpo/Schweisstec highlighted the thermal and mechanical processing of sheet metal, pipe and profiles as well as processing machines and systems for stamping, bending, bevelling and forming, up to peripherals including controllers, software and solutions for handling and quality assurance. Subject matter covering digitalisation and automation also dominated the event. To an even greater extent, emphasis was placed on modern cutting technologies such as laser cutting (presented by 56 exhibitors) and high-pressure waterjet cutting (25 further exhibitors).

Held once every two years, the Blechexpo/ Schweisstec trade fair duo is a strictly practice-oriented concept and covered aspects affecting the world of sheet metal processing and metalworking, which is now more digitalised and consistently automated than ever before. The Industrieverband Blechumformung (industry association for sheet metal forming) organised a communal booth for its members.

"The economy is cooling down," pointed out Cornelius Eich, managing director of the German Electrical and Electronic Manufacturers' Association (ZVEI). "And our industry sector won't be spared from trade wars."

Nevertheless – or perhaps precisely for that reason - technical discussions were lively and interest was intense, because issues which are of overriding importance in many areas (such as digitalisation, sustainability and process efficiency) are driving current development in the sheet metal working sector. The trade fair's topics included trends, innovations and future-proof solutions in the wake of a rapidly changing production environment. For example, modern joining and fastening technologies are becoming increasingly important because many new and hybrid materials can't be cut or joined using thermal processes – as a result, more attention is being focused on mechanical joining technology in lightweight design.

## Conference programme

The conference programme over the three days also covered aspects of digitalisation. This was also the subject of the stahlnews.de/Blechexpo Expert Meet on the evening of the first day of the trade fair. Organiser Hans Diederichs welcomed roughly 50 interested parties to the event in keeping with the motto: 'The Steel Industry in Times of Globalisation and Industry 4.0'.

Amongst other things, the presentations covered major areas of research such as steel forming, new process approaches, e-commerce and Internet platforms as an opportunity for the metals markets, the learning steel plant and data harvesting.

With a total of 108,000 square metres, roughly 15% more exhibition floor space was booked than in 2017.

The "Blechexpo Award" was presented on the afternoon of the second day of the trade fair by the "blechnet" trade journal, in cooperation with event promoters P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG. The prize was awarded to the most innovative exhibits in five different categories.

With its MC 125 automatic stamping press, Schuler was the winner in the stamping and forming technology category. Kjellberg Finsterwalde Plasma und Maschinen won in the cutting technology category with its Q 3000 plasma cutting system. The winner in the joining technology category was TRUMPF Laserund Systemtechnik with its TruLaser Station 7000 – a 3D laser welding machine.

The award for surface finishing technology went to Q-Fin Quality Finishing Machines for its SER600 Super Edge Rounder (a deburring and edge rounding machine). Last but not least, Cenit was highlighted in the handling and automation category for its cross-manufacturer cutting and joining software, Fastsuite Edition 2.

International Sheet Metal Review magazine was a media supporter of the event and was present in Hall 1 with a stand. Copies of the magazine were given out at both entrances of Blechexpo.
