# Manufacturing Day — 800% Growth in Three Years

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 The Fabricator]

October has become Manufacturing Month in many states, which means that Manufacturing Day celebrations were scheduled throughout the entire month. But the official date was Oct. 3, and results for this third annual celebration exceeded all expectations. More than 1,650 events were held across all 50 states, three Canadian provinces, and Puerto Rico. This doubled the number of events held in 2013.

Fori Automation, a global provider of assembly, testing, and welding equipment located in Shelby Twp., Mich., hosted 80 students from the tech ed program of Chippewa Valley School District for a tour and handson activities.

ZenTech Mfg., a Baltimore contract manufacturer specializing in electronics, hosted electronics technology students from Anne Arundel Comm. College and ITT Technical Institute. In addition, they greeted U.S. Ambassador Michael Froman (pictured) and Under Secretary of Commerce Mark Doms.

President Obama attended his first Manufacturing Day event, visiting FMA member company Millennium Steel in Princeton, Ind. He also issued a proclamation declaring Oct. 3 officially as Manufacturing Day for the U.S. His proclamation reinforces dozens of others issued by mayors, county board chairmen, and state governors to commemorate the day, a week, or a full month of celebrating manufacturing. Manufacturing Day was recognized on one of the big screens in Times Square, New York City, as shown above, as well as in hundreds of media reports across America.

Stories have been pouring in to Manufacturing Day headquarters at FMA, along with photo galleries that have been posted on the official

mfgday.com

website to commemorate activities. Attendance estimates at this time exceed 50,000 visitors. Final numbers will be available in early November after evaluations from host companies have been received and tallied.
