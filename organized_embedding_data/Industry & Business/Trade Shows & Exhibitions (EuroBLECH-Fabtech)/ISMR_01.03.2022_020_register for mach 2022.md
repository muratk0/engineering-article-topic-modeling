# Register for MACH 2022

[TARİH: 01.03.2022 ISMR]

MACH 2022 (4-8 April 2022 at Birmingham's National Exhibition Centre, UK) has demonstrated the strong momentum being generated in the UK manufacturing sector by selling almost all its exhibition space. The landmark exhibition is now more than 90% allocated.

Show organiser and national trade association, the Manufacturing Technologies Association (MTA), welcomed this success. The show attracts around 25,000 visitors and over 600 exhibitors. Co-located at MACH 2022 will be subsidiary exhibitions Air Tech; Fluid Power & Systems; Drives & Controls; Plant & Asset Management, the Smart Industry Expo and the three-day Engineering Supply Chain Show.

James Selka, CEO of the MTA, commented: "MACH is the UK's national manufacturing and engineering show and, as a content-led event, brings together the latest advanced engineering and manufacturing technologies in operation under one roof...

"Showcasing live, digital production systems in one space, it is not to be missed. Hundreds of millions of pounds worth of business is discussed, secured and completed at the exhibition, making it the must-attend event in the UK manufacturing calendar," he added.

For 2022, there are extra incentives in place for companies looking to invest in the latest machine technology, principally the Super Deduction initiative announced by the UK Chancellor last year. Running until 31 March 2023, the scheme means that UK companies investing in qualifying new plant and machinery assets will be able to claim a 130% superdeduction capital allowance on their plant and machinery investment, plus a 50% first-year allowance for qualifying special rate assets.

The UK Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR) has predicted that the scheme could encourage manufacturers to bring forward investment plans originally scheduled for much later in the decade.

The MTA is collaborating with other associations in the sector, many of which are taking pavilions at the MACH show. These include the MMMA, which represents the needs of the UK metalworking sector.

"MACH is not just a showcase for the manufacturing technologies sector, but a celebration of the manufacturing industry at its best- the development of the digital factory, new automation solutions and connected manufacturing processes, power by the hour and the cost efficiency solutions that will dramatically improve production processes and help shape the industry over the next decade," explained the MTA.

Other themes during the show will be decarbonisation, new opportunities for the UK manufacturing supply chain and the drive to secure a net zero economy. The MTA believes a more sustainable approach to the UK supply chain, with more intelligent sourcing of product from within the UK, will benefit the entire economy.

"Those visiting MACH can optimise their show experience by using the new online platform, Map Your Show Planner," added the MTA.

## Flexible Beam Patterns

## LBC Technology can enable performance comparable to 6kW with only 4kW of power.

LBC (Locus Beam Control) is AMADA's unique technology which gives the ability to manipulate the beam movement freely and flexibly without the need to defocus the power density. The optimum beam pattern is thus selected based on each material type and thickness. This provides the best cutting performance in the 4kW class in combination with the high-brightness laser oscillator. Performance comparable to 6kW can be achieved.
