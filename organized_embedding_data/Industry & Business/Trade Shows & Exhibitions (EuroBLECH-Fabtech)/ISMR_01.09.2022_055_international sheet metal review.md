Acknowledged as the number one international sheet-metal forming and fabrication journal, International Sheet Metal Review (ISMR) magazine offers truly global and independent coverage. Its success lies in a unique blend of technology, analysis and business issues presented in a fresh and lively format. Each issue, both print and digital, reaches over 40,000 qualified registered readers. The print issue is published ten times per year.

ISMR offers news, views, product reviews, technical articles, case studies and interviews from across the world. An interactive digital version of the magazine, as well as a regular newsletter, is also published. Launched in February 1999 by the Mack-Brooks Group, owner and organiser of EuroBLECH, the magazine is now published by In2 Publishing Ltd.

## SafanDarley BV

https://safandarley.com

“The E-volution in sheet metal working is moving increasingly towards the automatically controlled factory. We will therefore be presenting our latest innovations in sheet metalworking including a complete programme of automated solutions at EuroBLECH 2022," Dutch bending specialist, SafanDarley, told ISMR.

"Automated bending solutions will include our E-Brake 35T Mini Cell and R-Brake 130T, supplied with sheet metal parts via automatic infeed and discharge systems. This enables production to run 24/7 with minimal operator intervention. The E-Brake C 200T Tool Mate is an electronic press brake with automatic tool-changer. Intelligent software in the Tool Mate means that the only changes necessary are made to the tool arrangement. This results in significantly shorter change-over times and greater time savings when producing small batches," it continued.

SafanDarley develops press brakes and shears for thin and thick sheet metal. It has two production facilities, a head office in Lochem and a branch in Eijsden.
