# Wire and Tube trade fairs are cancelled

[TARİH: 01.11.2020 ISMR]

Following close consultation with exhibitors and partners, Messe Düsseldorf has taken the decision to cancel the VALVE WORLD EXPO and Wire and Tube trade fairs, which were scheduled for December 2020, because of the current COVID-19 infection developments. The events will be held in 2022 in accordance with their cycle in Düsseldorf, Germany. Other events from Messe Düsseldorf which are planned for 2021 are not affected by this decision.

“Following the decision of the German federal and state governments on 28 October 2020, trade fairs cannot be held until the end of November (for the time being). This means that the last physical events of this year will not be held at our exhibition grounds here in Düsseldorf. Due to the increasingly critical pandemic situation in Germany and European neighbouring countries, the situation had to be reassessed. Against this backdrop and the current high level of uncertainty from exhibitors and visitors, we have taken the decision to call off the trade fairs,” explained Wolfram Diener, President & CEO, Messe Düsseldorf GmbH.

“Exhibitors and visitors can continue gathering information on the latest news from the industry, as well as trends and product innovations, on our online platforms. With this in mind, we are constantly developing our digital content. The decision taken today is an event and industry-specific decision that does not affect other events planned by Messe Düsseldorf. We are in close contact with the authorities and partners of the respective trade fairs, which whom we will assess the situation,” added Diener.
