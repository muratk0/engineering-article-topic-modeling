## AsiaBLECH aims to build the platform for international sheet metal working manufacturers in China and across Asia

AsiaBLECH, the International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, will open its doors at the Suzhou International Expo Centre in China from 11-13 May 2016. It is the central business hub for suppliers and manufacturers of sheet metal working technology in Asia, providing a platform that appropriately reflects the specialised nature of this industry sector.

ISMR caught up with David Tellett, managing director of organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd, for the latest news and updates on the event.

ISMR: What was the spur for AsiaBLECH and your strategic vision for the event over the next few years?

DT: AsiaBLECH is really the embodiment of our belief that the sheet metal industry in China requires a focused “sheet metal only” event, which is distinct from the more general metalworking events which already exist. Our hopes are that the event will eventually become as significant in the Asian market as our other international sheet metal exhibition, EuroBLECH, is now to European markets and beyond. Of course, the two shows are at very different points in their evolutions but, like EuroBLECH, AsiaBLECH is 100% dedicated to sheet metal forming and fabrication, connecting with sheet metal visitors for pure sheet metal networking in the region.

The sheet metal industry in China is clearly significant enough to demand a dedicated trade show and, now that access into China from across Asia is so much easier, China has become a natural hub. Beyond that general picture, the quality of contact between the right suppliers and buyers at any trade show is, of course, what we constantly aim to achieve.

With that in mind, one of the keys has been to connect with the right people and trade bodies – we have therefore developed relationships with sheet metal associations and trade bodies across all provinces of China, as well as Korea, Taiwan, Japan and the whole of South East Asia and the ASEAN region. We have also made significant connections in Asian vertical sectors such as automotive supply, electronics, shipbuilding etc. This has given us a depth of reach for AsiaBLECH, domestically and internationally, that has not been attempted before.

Growth will not happen overnight, it is a long term investment like any undertaking in China but, if the desire is there from visitors and suppliers, we believe that we can work with the entire industry to build, year-on-year, an Asian show (structured on a two-year cycle) which is exclusively focused on sheet metal.

So, clearly structured and subdivided into twenty technology sectors, AsiaBLECH showcases the entire sheet metal working technology chain. For companies ready to invest in the latest machines, tools and systems for sheet metal working, AsiaBLECH aims to be the first point of contact when it comes to sourcing the right equipment. For suppliers and manufacturers of sheet metal working technology, the exhibition is a central hub for business on a global scale.

ISMR: How is AsiaBLECH developing? Are there any new benefits and features of next year’s event that you would like to highlight?

DT: It’s going very well overall. It is on track to be at least double the size of the previous event last year. That show attracted well over 4000 sheet metal professionals and buyers from 30 countries, so we’re always aiming to grow that in terms of numbers, quality of visitors and geographical reach.

What is noticeable is the very good response from the Chinese domestic market, in particular. Although economic growth levels in China have been comparatively depressed recently, there are still strong growth rates of between 5 and 7% of GDP forecast in China, which is very sustainable in the longer term. AsiaBLECH is a long term project, so I believe that steady growth will be good news for the industry generally, and the exhibition will mirror that.

We are working very hard to put several networking and educational areas onto the floor (with Chinese and international keynote speakers) and are liaising on this with a very wide range of supporting trade associations and industrial bodies. There will be at least one workshop for European exhibitors/visitors on how to connect and do business in China and a technology-focused seminar, led by the associations, as a forum to discuss issues for their members and other sheet metal users.

We’re also planning a presentation area for exhibitors to explain the intricacies and advantages of the technology that they will have at the show, as well as new trends not on display. This has been driven by requests from Chinese visitors to BLECH China for information on new and forthcoming technologies. We are also looking at the possibility of holding sheet metal awards at the show.

Many companies have already booked their stand spaces at AsiaBLECH 2016, representing a top-class range of innovative machinery and systems for sheet metal working. AsiaBLECH is attracting a widespread mix of domestic and international companies including suppliers from Germany, Italy, Switzerland, Sweden and Belgium. Many exhibitors have also returned to the event (such as Gasparini Spa, Kohler Maschinenbau GmbH, Messer Cutting Systems and Schuler Pressen). Other well-known companies have decided to join AsiaBLECH 2016 for the first time this year, like Panasonic Welding Systems and Prima Power.

ISMR: What is the significance of Suzhou as the location for AsiaBLECH?

DT: Suzhou is very close to Shanghai, located within reach of Shanghai Pudong international airport and Shanghai Hongqiao international airport. As such, it offers a balance between easy access for Chinese domestic visitors and international visitors, as well as first class facilities and lower accommodation costs than central Shanghai. Suzhou is known for its international business park – indeed, a number of large sheet metal manufacturers are located nearby, such as TRUMPF and Bruderer.

The sheet metal industry in China is clearly significant enough to demand a dedicated trade show.

International visitor access is important, of course, for an international show. China has become a highly sophisticated engineering market and has gained a regional and global reach beyond its borders. This is a trend that is sure to continue. More than ever, to stay at the forefront of productivity and quality, local and domestic manufacturers are looking to invest in the world’s best sheet metal technologies, equipment and expertise. AsiaBLECH 2016 gives providers of machinery, tools and services the opportunity to meet with decisionmakers and to find business partners within the highly developed sheet metal working industry in China and other Asian countries.

Of course, it is difficult to do business and build a presence anywhere in Asia if you are not there in person to build relationships – this is the same as anywhere else… We aim to facilitate that process for our visitors and exhibitors in the best possible way.
