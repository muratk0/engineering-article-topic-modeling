# Around 1,000 exhibitors from 33 countries and 31,000 visitors are expected to attend Metalloobrabotka 2018 in Russia.

[TARİH: 01.04.2018 ISMR]

Metalloobrabotka, the 19th international exhibition for equipment, instruments and tooling for the metalworking industry, is a major international show for the machine tool and metalworking industry. The show is organised by Expocentre AO and Stankoinstrument (the Russian association of machine tool manufacturers) and run under the auspices of the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade, the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the Russian Engineering Union.

Approved by the Global Association of the Exhibition Industry (UFI) and taking place at the Expocentre AO in Moscow, Russia, from 14-18 May 2018, it showcases innovative research trends and developments in machine tool building and the latest solutions for the machine building industry. The 2018 event is expected to attract over 1,000 companies from 33 countries over a total area of about 80,000 sq. m. The international level of participation in Metalloobrabotka is expected to exceed 50%.

The trade show features a strong presence from both Russian and international stakeholders. National pavilions and joint stands, with the assistance of major industry associations such as VDW (Germany); SWISSMEM (Switzerland); SST (the Czech Republic); UCIMU (Italy); TAMI (Taiwan) and CMTBA (China), can be found at the show. The Belarus pavilion is organised by the national Ministry of Industry. This year will also mark the debut of the UK's national pavilion, organised with the support of MTA (the UK's Manufacturing Technologies Association). Other machine tool associations, such as MIB (Turkey); TIAD (Turkey); JMTBA (Japan) and AFM (Spain), will also be represented.

## An eye on 2017

Last year's Metalloobrabotka event, held in May 2017 in Moscow over a total exhibition area of 39,500 square metres, attracted 1,042 company exhibitors (of which over 500 were from outside Russia) and 31,432 professional visitors from approximately 33 countries.

Various country pavilions and group stands, presented by industry associations dotted the event. During the exhibition, the Moscow City Government and Niles-Simmons-Hegenscheidt (NSH), Germany, signed a cooperative agreement. First Deputy Minister of Industry and Trade of Russia, Gleb Nikitin, also visited the event and took part in the signing ceremony of a Memorandum of Cooperation between the State Marine Technical University of St. Petersburg and BeAM Machines (France).

Switzerland also sent an official delegation, headed by Jürg Stahl (President of the National Council). More than 70 Chinese machine tool builders also occupied a separate pavilion.

This year will also mark the debut of the UK's national pavilion, organised with the support of MTA (the UK's Manufacturing Technologies Association).

Vocational education and the training of highly qualified personnel was once again the focus of attention at the event. The traditional Science, Vocational Education and Production Section featured leading universities and colleges which introduced programmes about the integration of the educational process. The Expocentre for Counterfeit-Free Exhibitions project, aimed at minimising counterfeit products showcased at exhibitions, was also highlighted.

Metalloobrabotka 2017 also featured a number of associated events, such as the '7th International Forum on Modern Trends in Technologies and Design of Metalworking Equipment' (organised by the Stankoinstrument Association and Expocentre AO). The first 'Consultation Session' of the Russian Export Centre on export support for the metalworking industry was also arranged by the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Russian Export Centre and Expocentre AO.

The 'International Forum on Digital Machinery Production: Problems and Solutions' was organised by Moscow State Technological University Stankin and Expocentre AO jointly with the Russian Engineering Union, Stankoinstrument Association and the Office for Development and Exchanges in the Italian Embassy in Russia (ICE). Participants were from Italy, the Czech Republic, Poland, Spain and Russia. The forum included an award ceremony for students for the 'The Best Innovative Metalworking Project 2017' competition. The eight winners received a prize training package in Europe.

A series of educational workshops devoted to digital production and automation for high-school students and teachers of supplementary education, and the international school for young scientists and professionals in robotics, manufacturing technology and automation, was also arranged by Moscow State Technological University Stankin.

The event also included a conference on 'Industry 4.0 in Metalworking', organised by Next Business Media and Sandvik Coromant, as well as a 'Virtual Reality' press conference from Taiwanese machine tool builders.

## 2018 in focus

This year's Metalloobrabotka will also feature several associated events, such as a conference on 'Industrial Lubricants and Fluids in Metallurgy, Metal Processing and Machine-Building 2018' and 'VIII International Forum on Modern Trends in Technologies and Design of Metalworking Equipment' (organised by the Stankoinstrument Russian Association of Machine Tool Manufacturers and Expocentre AO).
