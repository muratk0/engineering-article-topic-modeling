# THE MOVE TO MILAN

[TARİH: 01.04.2017 ISMR]

LAMIERA, the international biennial exhibition dedicated to the metalforming machine tool industry and innovative technologies related to the sector, is (almost) fully booked. Approximately 440 companies have already applied to exhibit at the event which will be held at Fieramilano Rho, from 17 to 20 May 2017, in halls 13 and 15.

Over 40,000 square metres of total exhibition area, exhibitors will display products and technology relating to sheet metal forming machinery; bar-; tube-; section-working machines; presses; blanking and punching machines; lasers; robots; automation; moulds and dies; heat treatment; surface treatment and finishing; wires and metal carpentry as well as subcontracting.

The core of the trade show will be represented by sheet metal forming and cutting machines; laser cutting and welding. In particular, the number of exhibitors manufacturing presses, robots and components has doubled, according to the show organiser compared to the last show in Bologna.

## New themes and focus areas

New themed areas, focusing on products and solutions relating to metal forming technologies, will complement the exhibition promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robotics and automation association) and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU.

## Building on success

"There are already over 30 companies taking part in the new Fastener Industry area, dedicated to screws, bolts, fastening and clamping systems. More than 20 are companies exhibiting within Ecocoatech - an area created in cooperation with ANVER, the Italian association of industrial coaters - presenting all the most innovative interoperational processes for sheet metal coating and painting. Blech Italy Service, centred on technical subcontracting and services for the industry, will complete LAMIERA's offering," said the show organiser.

The number of exhibitors who have booked, to date, at this year's LAMIERA has validated the decision to move the exhibition venue to Milan, scheduling it in the odd years. This year, LAMIERA moves from Bologna to Fieramilano Rho.

## An eye on Italy

"Italy plays a primary role in the international rankings of the sector. Third among manufacturers, second among exporters and fourth in the ranking of consumers, Italy can rely on leading companies that specialise in sheet metal forming and machining. It can also count on lively, dynamic demand," commented Massimo Carboniero, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE.

"LAMIERA is founded on these two assumptions, as well as on the competence of local manufacturers and domestic market demand, and already attracts overseas exhibitors whilst taking advantage of a particularly favourable economic environment for this year's show. In Italy, the consumption of production systems is expected to grow in 2017, supported by incentive measures included in the National Plan Industry 4.0 for the benefit of those who will exhibit at LAMIERA."

Alfredo Mariotti, General Manager, UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, confirmed that the number of exhibitors has grown (+12%), as well as the booked exhibition area (+15%), and the level of exhibition content has also increased. The event, he said, will be able to attract an even higher number of visitors, thanks to the offerings on show and numerous side initiatives.

Free admission is reserved for operators and press. Free entrance is also offered for minors, university students and disabled visitors. The free entrance ticket may be collected at the reception desks. Using online pre-registration, entrance tickets can also be obtained free of charge. The entrance ticket can also be bought at the LAMIERA reception during the exhibition. Daily ticket cost is €12.00.

## Keeping current

In keeping with tradition, LAMIERA will feature an in-depth conference/speaker programme on issues of interest for the operators in the sector, included in the list of conferences and meetings of the Lambda Academy. Nine events have already been scheduled in the agenda. They will be dedicated to: screws; bolts; alternative technologies for surface treatment; machinery safety; nanotechnologies and coatings; Industry 4.0 and robotics.

Organised in cooperation with Randstad and Ecole, Pianeta Giovani Lavoro will be an area focused on meetings around the demand and supply for jobs, where young students visiting the trade show will be able to find information about opportunities offered by companies in the sector. The area will be a welcome point for over 200 students from 10 technical institutes, accompanied by tutors, on a guided tour of the exhibition stands. This initiative is part of a wider "Alternanza Scuola-Lavoro" (work-related learning) project defined by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE in agreement with the Italian Ministry of Education, University and Research.

Networking between exhibitors and visitors will also be encouraged through a special project, promoted by the association in cooperation with the Italian Ministry of Economic Development and ICE-Italian Trade Agency. This initiative involves inviting 13 delegations of foreign operators from dynamic and promising markets: Algeria; Brazil; Canada; China; Germany; India; Iran; Morocco; Mexico; Poland; Russia; Serbia and Turkey. The 120 attending buyers will be able to arrange B2B meetings at the Italian exhibitors' stands.

For its first show in Italy's 'capital city of design', LAMIERA will propose "LAMIERALIVING" which will display a selection of design objects that won the prestigious "ADI Compasso d'Oro" international Award and were totally or partially made by metal forming machines and technologies. The initiative is carried out in cooperation with the Lombard delegation of ADI, the Italian association for industrial design.
