The seventh event in North America’s largest plant tour series takes place in Cincinnati Sept. 29-30. It features the unique opportunity to tour eight fabricating shops in a day and a half that will showcase their lasers, press brakes, robotic welders, automation, finishing processes, and more. SigmaTEK Systems LLC will host the event.

Transportation will be provided to and from all tours. Day one includes tours of Tri-State Fabricators (Amelia, Ohio), Paragon Metal Fabricators Inc. (Cincinnati), Cincinnati Sub-Zero (Cincinnati), and Fecon Inc. (Lebanon, Ohio). Fortis Security Products (Lawrenceburg, Ind.), Western Hills Fabricators, Metalworking Group, and Intelligrated (all in Cincinnati) will be toured on day two.

Dick Kallage

of KDC & Associates LLC and regular columnist for

The FABRICATOR

®

will present on "What to Look for During a Plant Tour" before the tours and provide a summary of the plant tours each day.

Valuable networking will occur during the Cincinnati Reds vs. Chicago Cubs game on Tuesday, Sept. 29, at Great American Ball Park.

New this year is the addition of the Lasers 101 Certificate Course offered on Monday, Sept. 28, from 7 a.m.- 4 p.m. The course covers three topic areas: laser basics, laser cutting, and laser welding. The course includes a written exam.
