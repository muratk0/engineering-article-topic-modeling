# High note for MTA HANOI

[TARİH: 01.05.2012 ISMR]

## MTA HANOI 2012, the Vietnam capital’s machine tools and metalworking trade exhibition, closed on 30 March 2012. The event attracted more than 4,000 attendees which included trade visitors, exhibiting staff, conference delegates and speakers, and members of the press.

More than 100 conference delegates gathered at the MTA HANOI 2012 conference to network with industry peers and experts and share their views on the latest developments taking place in the manufacturing sector. Sessions focusing on the Toyota Production System (TPS), Lean Management and Lean Six Sigma concepts and methodology saw high attendance amongst the Vietnamese industry professionals.

It was a productive trip for the Vietnamese group delegates as well. Over 30 contingents from local manufacturing establishments visited the event over the three days and many had the chance to network with exhibitors, as well as gain insights into the latest technologies on the show floor.

MTA HANOI will return from 1 – 3 April 2014 at the International Centre for Exhibitions (ICE). It is organised by Singapore Exhibition Services Pte Ltd (SES) and co-organised by VCCI Exhibition Service Co Ltd. www.mtahanoi.com

THE 22ND International Sheet Metal Working Technology exhibition, EuroBLECH 2012, will take place from 23 – 27 October 2012 in Hanover, Germany. The event is a leading trade exhibition for this industry sector. The current booking status of this year’s show reflects a significant stabilisation of the sheet metal working industry, the organiser told ISMR. A total of 1,235 exhibitors from 38 countries have already reserved their stand space, covering a net exhibition space of 83,000 square metres (some 5,000 square metres more than the total exhibition space at the previous event).

“The trend, which had already become apparent during the previous exhibition, has now been confirmed: the industry sector is investing again, and, after one of the worst crises for this industry, manufacturers and suppliers of machines, materials and systems for sheet metal working are finally able to, once again, witness some planning reliability,” explained Nicola Hamann, Exhibition Director, EuroBLECH on behalf of the organisers, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

“Apparently, the sheet metal working industry has recovered, and now presents itself as stabilised, confident and thoroughly positive. Despite some uncertainties due to the currency crisis in the Eurozone or recent developments in some north African and Middle Eastern countries, there are generally positive sales prospects for suppliers to the sheet metal working industry; not only in the growth markets of Asia and South America but also in many European countries as well as in the USA.”

Almost half of the exhibiting companies that have already booked their stands come from outside Germany, with Italy, Turkey and Switzerland being the largest exhibitor countries after Germany. According to the current booking status, there are also significantly more exhibitors from the USA and China than at the previous exhibition.

EuroBLECH 2012 will again occupy halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 27 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds. The exhibition profile represents the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal, semi-finished and finished products; handling; separation; forming; flexible sheet metal working; joining; welding and surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; quality control; CAD/CAM systems and R&D.

A total of 61,500 trade visitors from all over the world attended the previous show. www.euroblech.com
