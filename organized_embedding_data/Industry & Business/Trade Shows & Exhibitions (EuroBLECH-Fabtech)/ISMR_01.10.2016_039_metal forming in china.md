# METAL FORMING IN CHINA

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

## MetalForm China 2017 will take place from 19-22 September 2017 at the Shanghai World Expo Centre

MetalForm China is organised by CCMI (China National Machine Tool Corporation and the China Metalforming Industry Association), which is focused specifically on the metalforming industry in China. It showcases machinery, dies and moulds, material and metal formed parts which cover the entire chain of metal stamping, fabricating and forging. MetalForm China 2017 will take place from 19-22 September 2017 at the Shanghai World Expo Centre. Every year, approximately 10,000 companies take part in the event and its concurrent conferences.

One such concurrent conference will be the 19th ICOSPA Congress, which will be held from 19-22 September 2017 in Suzhou, China. Suzhou, in the south east of the Jiangsu Province and the central section of the Yangtze River Delta, is an important manufacturing centre about 110km from Shanghai. ICOSPA (the International Council of Sheet Metal Presswork Associations) was founded in the 1960s by the sheet metal associations from France, Germany, the UK, the USA and Japan, which the Dutch and Chinese sheet metal associations joined later. It is the first time that this event will take place in China.

MetalForm China ranks among the top three events in the metal forming industry in China.

"We have achieved steady growth of floor space, exhibitors and visitors by consistently focusing on solutions which are aligned to process sequences of forging, stamping and sheet metal fabricating - and the automation of processes," said CCMI, the MetalForm China show organiser.
