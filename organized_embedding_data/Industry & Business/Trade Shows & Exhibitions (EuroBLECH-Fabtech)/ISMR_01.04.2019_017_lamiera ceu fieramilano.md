# Lamiera CEU fieramilano

[TARİH: 01.04.2019 ISMR]

Don't miss this year's LAMIERA exhibition from 15-18 May 2019 in Milan.

MADE IN ITALY

To the sheet metal field, LAMIERA attracts visitors from all major user sectors. The international biennial exhibition dedicated to the metal forming machine tool industry and innovative technologies relating to the sector has attracted over 450 companies and will be held at Fieramilano Rho, from 15 to 18 May 2019, in Halls 13 and 15. A new Hall 18, the Innovation Hall, is also part of LAMIERA this year.

The exhibition area is already 30% larger compared to the space booked by exhibitors in February 2017 (40,000 square metres of total exhibition area). Exhibitors will display products and technology relating to sheet metal forming machinery; bar-; tube-; section-working machines; presses; blanking and punching machines; lasers; robots; automation; moulds and dies; heat treatments; surface treatment and finishing; wires and metal carpentry as well as subcontracting.

"The number of direct exhibitors has already grown by 10% to date. Around 27% of organisations who sent in their applications will take part in LAMIERA for the first time or will come back and exhibit again after several editions of the event," explained UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robotics and automation association) and show organiser CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU.

## New innovation areas

"What we expect," stated Alfredo Mariotti, director of the exhibition, "is to improve our 2017 results, which were already very positive, by further expanding and enhancing the event.

To this end, we will continue working on traditional sectors – they represent the core of the exhibition and are in a strong position, as Italian demand for machine tools is still very high. In 2019, the industry will confirm results achieved in 2018, a record-breaking year for the sector.

"On the other hand, work will continue on the new innovation areas focused on robotics, IoT and consultancy, which are having an increasingly strong impact on the industry and a greater presence in manufacturers' factories."

LAMIERA will showcase technologies for integrated factories, displaying international product ranges for stand-alone machines and complex systems as well as a comprehensive overview of automation systems, robotics (ROBOT PLANET), technologies linked with the digital world (FABBRICAFUTURA) and consultancy (BOX CONSULTING).

Machines, systems and equipment for the machining of sheet metal, tube, section, wire and metal structural work; presses; dies; welding; treatment and finishing; technical subcontracting and fasteners are just a few of the 'traditional' technologies on show. In addition, there will be a wide and varied offering of robots, automation, enabling technologies and consultancy.

This year, LAMIERA is also running a special initiative, LAMIALAMIERA, a programme of in-depth meetings arranged by exhibitors and organisers, throughout the event. Organised on different themes, the meetings will last 20 minutes each and will be hosted in a special area, set up as a conference arena in one of the exhibition halls.

"The idea," explained Alfredo Mariotti, "is to offer exhibitors a further chance for promotion outside their stands, free of charge. However, the aim is also to provide visitors with technical-cultural, in-depth opportunities, so that they can make the most of their own presence at the exhibition."

## An eye on 2017

In 2017, 480 companies exhibited at LAMIERA (25% more compared to the 2016 edition, with an occupied exhibition surface area of 40,000 square metres). The exhibition registered 23,579 visits.

"The uniqueness of the event and its numerous side initiatives make the exhibition an unmissable occasion for the industry: for major Italian and overseas manufacturers of machines, robots and automation; for the SMEs specialising in super-customised solutions; for subcontractors; for companies specialising in accessories and auxiliary technologies but also for players in the digital world and of consultancy, whose presence is increasing within the sector," added the show organiser, CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU.

## Record-breaking year for Italian machine tools

According to UCIMU, 2018 was a record-breaking year for the Italian industry for manufacturing machine tools, robots and automation systems, which registered double-digit increases for all economic indicators. 2019, it said, will instead be characterised by 'general stability'.

"As shown by the preliminary year's data processed by the Studies Dept. of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, in 2018 production grew to 6,900 million euros, highlighting a 13.4% upturn compared to the previous year. This is the fifth consecutive year of growth and, in terms of absolute values, this is the new record for the Italian industry sector. The outcome was due both to the excellent performance of deliveries by Italian manufacturers in the domestic market, which increased by 21.1% to 3,270 million euros, and to the positive trend of exports, up by 7.2% to 3,630 million euros," explained Massimo Carboniero, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association.

"Based on the ISTAT data processed by UCIMU, in the first eight months of the year (latest available survey), the main destination countries for Made in Italy products in the sector were the following: Germany 246 million euros (+11.6%); China 237 million euros (+7.1%); United States 223 million euros (+9.5%); Poland 143 million euros (+49.8%) and France 135 million euros (-4.6%). Thanks to these increases Italy strengthened its role internationally, highlighted not only for the competence expressed by the industries in the sector (measured by production and export data) but also for the dynamism of its demand, supported and stimulated by incentive provisions for competitiveness, such as Super- and Hyper-Depreciation, included in the Industry/Enterprise 4.0 programmes."

## LAMIERA 2017

In particular, in 2018, machine tool, robot and automation consumption in Italy grew by 25.9% to 5,620 million euros, said Mr Carboniero. Italian users' propensity to invest in new technology, as well as in digital products, led Italian manufacturers to focus on the domestic market, shown by the decrease in the export/production ratio, which changed from 55.6% in 2017 to 52.6% in 2018.

"In 2019, however, the Italian industry sector should reinforce its 2018 performance, enjoying a positive trend with deliveries in overseas markets expected to increase. A clear slowdown is shown in the domestic market, whose growth trend seems to have lost the momentum we were used to seeing over the last few years," explained Massimo Carboniero. "We are satisfied with the 2018 result: the year closed with excellent results, both in Italy and abroad. Moreover, considering sales on the whole i.e. not only of machines, but also the production of parts, tools and numerical controls (which are not included in the total of Italian machine tools), we can confirm that turnover exceeded nine billion euros.

"For 2019, although we are confident about maintaining the results of 2018, we are noticing a slowdown of investment in new machines by Italian users. Of course, we understand that the growth we were used to cannot continue uninterruptedly with the same pace and intensity. However, it is important to keep in mind that a recent survey conducted by FONDAZIONE UCIMU and EUMETRA shows that only 50% of Italian metalworking and engineering organisations made investments in the period between 2017 and the first part of 2018."
