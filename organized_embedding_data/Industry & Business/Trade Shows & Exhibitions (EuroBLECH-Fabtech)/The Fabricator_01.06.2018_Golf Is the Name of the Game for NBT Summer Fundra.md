Supporting the summer camp and scholarship programs of Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT), the foundation of the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, is a breeze when you’re a golfer. Two exciting Midwest golf outings, held at different courses, should top your "to-do" list this summer.

Monday, July 16 –

Processors Auction at the Motor City Slitters

golf outing will be held at the Detroit Golf Club. Up to 250 golfers from the metals industry will hit the links on two premier courses designed by Donald Ross and enjoy networking at the evening reception and dinner. Register to golf at

motorcityslitters.com

.

The

Processors Auction

held during the Motor City Slitters reception will benefit the John Grossheim Memorial Scholarship program. The scholarship is named after a longtime Outside Processors Council (OPC) member who was passionate about encouraging the next generation of workers to consider a career in the metals industry. The fundraiser is not your typical silent auction as attendees can bid on industry-related services such as slitting, pickling, and transportation services.
