# Trade fair trio comes to Milan

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Three trade fairs kick-off concurrently in Milan, Italy, in the second week of October 2022. The 33.BI-MU international biennial exhibition of machine tools, robotics and automation systems, additive manufacturing, digital and auxiliary technologies as well as the Xylexpo biennial exhibition of woodworking technology and components for the furniture industry take place from 12-15 October 2022. Viscom Italia, a European visual communication trade fair, will be held from 13-15 October 2022.

The organisers' decision to co-ordinate the overlap of their event calendars aims to enable trade fair visitors to maximise their opportunities and participate in all three events.

Riccardo Gaslini, head of the organising secretariat of BI-MU, organised by EFIM, commented: “BI-MU and Xylexpo have been working together for two years now to offer event participants a comprehensive and rewarding visitor experience. Joined by Viscom Italia, this will certainly help to attract greater visitor numbers, benefiting everybody participating at the fair. [We also extend] thanks for the enhanced services provided by the Rho-fieramilano exhibition centre to meet the needs of the larger number of operators in each of the halls."
