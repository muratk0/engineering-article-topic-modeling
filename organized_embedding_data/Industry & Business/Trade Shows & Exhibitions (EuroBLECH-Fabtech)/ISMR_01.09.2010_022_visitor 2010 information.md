# VISITOR 2010 INFORMATION

[TARİH: 01.09.2010 ISMR]

ISMR outlines essential visitor information for this year’s 21st EuroBLECH exhibition (26-30 October 2010) in Hanover

With a comprehensive range of products, EuroBLECH is the event for everyone involved in sheet metal production, working or trading, from all management levels in small, medium-sized or multinational companies. Visitors include production specialists, designers, plant managers, manufacturers, engineers and buyers as well as association experts and students, Mack Brooks Exhibitions told ISMR.

A comprehensive exhibition preview, with detailed information on exhibitors and products, will be available from September 2010. The preview includes a complete exhibitor list, an area map, an article on the latest trends in laser technology as well as useful tips for travel and stay. The exhibition preview can be downloaded from www.euroblech.com or can be ordered on the website as a print version free of charge.

The EuroBLECH Bulletin brings you the latest news about the forthcoming exhibition, as well as short reports on markets, research, products and trends in sheet metal working. Published four times per year, it keeps exhibitors informed of the most important deadlines and of the services available.

As the exhibition gets closer, visitors will find useful tips about the show and information about the products and services on display at EuroBLECH 2010.

You can subscribe to the free EuroBLECH Bulletin at http://www.euroblech.com/english/bulletin/
