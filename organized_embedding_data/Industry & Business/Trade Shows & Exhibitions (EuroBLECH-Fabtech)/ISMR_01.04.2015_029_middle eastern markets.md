# MIDDLE EASTERN MARKETS

[TARİH: 01.04.2015 ISMR]

MTE 2015 (Machine Tools Exhibition) will take place at the Dhahran International Exhibitions Centre in Dammam, the Eastern Province of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, from 19-21 May 2015. An hour’s drive from Kuwait and Bahrain, and a 30-45-minute flight from the U.A.E. and Qatar, the halls are already, reportedly, 85 per cent booked in terms of exhibition space.

The exhibition is free to attend – over 150 companies from 20 countries will be present, showcasing their respective tools and technology to current and prospective clients. Represented sectors include flexible manufacturing; transfer and special production machines; precision tools; work-piece and tool holders; metrology; grinding machines; surface technology; sheet metal working machines; forming machines; robotics; welding etc.

“MTE 2015 has been established to meet the requirement for the largest machine tools market in the Middle East to host its own focused machinery show. The Eastern Province provides the best access to business opportunities. It is the largest province in the Kingdom and the perfect strategic gateway for international trade. MTE 2015 will attract high level technical experts, key industry professionals, specialists and decision-makers in various sectors of the industry,” said the event organisers, IES & SGE.

## Machinery in focus

“Saudi Arabia is one of the fastest growing economies in the world and the largest and most influential economy in the Middle East. It is considered to offer a wealth of business opportunities with multi-billion dollar projects,” added the organisers. “The Kingdom is also listed as one of the Top 20 destinations for foreign direct investment.

“One of the rapidly emerging sectors in Saudi Arabia is industry – recent developments have created an unmatched demand for the full range of industrial machinery, machine tools and spare parts. MODON, the Saudi Industrial Property Authority, started with 14 industrial cities in 2007 – now there are 22. The government is setting up new towns and expanding existing cities located in the neighbourhood of industrial cities to boost local industry. MODON is setting up 40 industrial cities throughout the Kingdom.

“Market sales of machine tools from 2007 doubled in 2012 and 95 per cent of local demand is served by imports, from North America, European countries and China. In the industry sector, metalworking machines remain the largest and the best performer category (source: Euromonitor – ISIC Report 2922).”

In 2014, the Council of Ministers, chaired by Crown Prince Salman Bin Abdulaziz (Deputy Premier and Minister of Defence) reportedly approved the creation of a new company to invest in manufacturing industries. According to the show organiser, the new Saudi Arabian Company for Industrial Investment will have capital of SR2 billion ($533 million). It will focus on conversion industries that rely on petrochemicals, plastics, fertilisers, steel, aluminium and basic industries that achieve economic diversification. The new company is a joint venture between the Public Investment Fund, Saudi Aramco and Saudi Basic Industries Corp (SABIC). MODON is a strong indicator of how the Kingdom’s authorities, public and private authorities are boosting local industry developments. ■
