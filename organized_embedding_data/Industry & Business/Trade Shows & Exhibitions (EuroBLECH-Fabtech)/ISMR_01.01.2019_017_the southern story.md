# THE SOUTHERN STORY

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

Southern Manufacturing & Electronics, the UK's leading annual engineering technology show, returns to Farnborough from 5–7 February 2019. The move to a new and permanent location last year proved to be a milestone in the show’s two-decade history and the drive for further growth in 2019.

The event is a comprehensive annual industrial exhibition in the UK and a major pan-European showcase for new technology in machinery; production equipment; electronic production and assembly; tooling and components as well as subcontract services across a wide range of industry. It is located at Farnborough International Exhibition and Conference Centre, a new £35m 20,000m2 venue, offering modern facilities, easy access and free onsite parking.

The show's location in one of the most important regions for the UK’s £22 billion aerospace business, and the supply chain that supports it, is also important for machinery vendors; an average year will see deals (worth millions of pounds sterling) closed on the show floor. 9000 visitors and 800 exhibitors are anticipated to take part in the show. Southern Manufacturing is co-located with AutoAero, a highly specialised event focusing on suppliers to the UK's aerospace and automotive engineering sectors.

## Partners in progress

"Exhibitor reservations for February's show are more than 20% over levels one year ago, which indicates that the exhibition will easily reach its maximum capacity. Now moving into its third decade, the show continues to exert considerable appeal to visitors from a wide variety of manufacturing enterprises due to the variety of companies taking part," the event organiser told ISMR.

## We preview Southern Manufacturing in Farnborough UK from 5-7 February 2019.

"Exhibitors range from component and consumable vendors to major international machinery manufacturers. There is also a broad offering of subcontract service providers, specialising in everything from fabrication to electronics assembly. A significant number of AS9100-rated subcontractors are also exhibiting. But, potentially more interestingly considering the current concerns over Brexit, the 2019 show also hosts a significant group of non-UK vendors, numbers of which have multiplied no less than 26% over 2018."

As well as a record volume of exhibitors anticipated, the event has formalised links with several important industry organisations. These include the Confederation of British Metalforming; Farnborough Aerospace Consortium; the Gauge and Toolmakers' Association; The British Gear Association; the Society of Motor Manufacturers and Traders and Composites UK.

"These partnerships will enable the roll-out of new show features and have added greatly to opportunities to access authoritative technical expertise through the show’s comprehensive and free technical seminar programme," added the event organiser.

Some of the new topics to be tackled, as part of the 2019 conference programme, will include the benefits of incremental sheetforming (ISF) processes, explained by Roger Onions, Business Development Director, GTMA. Stewart Williams, Professor of Welding Science and Engineering, Cranfield University, will discuss the large-scale additive manufacture of engineering components using Wire + Arc Additive Manufacture (WAAM) – a technique that is currently arousing great interest in many sectors of industry.

Peter Harris and Joseph Bellis of Matsuura Machinery will also examine applications in 3D Printing and Component Post Processing using the HP Multi-Jet Fusion. Steven Barr of Edge Digital will look at digital readiness Tool and Industry 4.0 – how to maximise value from the right digital technologies for your business. With Brexit looming large on the horizon, Dr. Chris Robertson of RINA Consulting will discuss what's next in trade relations and product regulation.

## Machinery demonstrations

For those interested in the latest machinery, Southern Manufacturing also offers an opportunity to see the offerings from many wellknown brands demonstrated live at the show.

Bystronic will demonstrate its Xpert 40 press brake with Mobile Bending Robot. The robot is available for feeding the press brake, turning it into a compact, automated bending cell for producing 3D sheet metal components.

Above and below: Scenes from Southern Manufacturing 2018

"Retrofittable to any Xpert 40 press brake, the robot is suitable for subcontractors as it allows automatic production for larger runs of components, or rapid access for manual bending of smaller batches and one-offs by quickly setting the robot module aside. As the automation module is on wheels and the press brake itself is small enough to be moved by fork lift truck, the entire assembly can be relocated anywhere in a factory to suit variable production needs," explained the Swiss manufacturer.

High speed stamping presses and preowned presses will be highlighted by Bruderer UK. The pre-owned machines are rigorously checked and then refurbished, as required, to ensure that they meet all manufacturer’s tolerances. The company also offers a range of other presses, ranging from six to over 4,000 tons.

"Visitors to Southern Manufacturing & Electronics 2019 will notice many other familiar names, most notably C. Dugard; Matsuura; Haas Automation; Unison; Hurco and Rolla-V, as well as some noteworthy returnees to the event. Citizen Machinery UK will exhibit for the first time since 2007, joined by other important previous exhibitors that include Mitutoyo (UK) and LNS Turbo," explained the event organiser.

Laser processing machines are also well represented at Southern 2019. Lasercutters and engravers will be exhibited by Boxford. The company's latest range of laser cutters incorporate many features as standard, including integrated chiller cooling systems; auto-focus; air assist c/w integrated compressor; integral red dot pointer and honeycomb and 'knife-edge' cutting tables. TLM Laser will showcase products from leading names in laser technology. Products on show from FOBA and Alpha will highlight FOBA's Intelligent mark positioning system.

Southern Manufacturing also gives visitors the opportunity to meet a full range of business service providers, including subcontractors and services such as asset finance. Close Brothers Asset Finance will be on hand to discuss the range of finance options available to businesses seeking to obtain the equipment they need to grow.

Subcontractors represented at the show include everything from fabrication, to precision sheet metalwork, finishing and electroplating. A full list of exhibiting companies can be found on the newlyrevamped Southern Manufacturing website at www.industrysouth.co.uk

## GETTING THERE

Admission and car parking is free. Farnborough International Conference and Exhibition Centre is easily reached by road, air or public transport.

## By Road

From the M3, take junction 4 and follow the A331 to Farnborough. The show will be clearly AA signposted on all roads approaching the venue. Car parking is available for 3,500 vehicles.

## By Rail

Farnborough North Camp is the nearest station but Farnborough Main and Aldershot stations are also nearby. A regular free shuttle bus service operates from both of Farnborough’s mainline railway stations directly to the show. For train timetable information, call National Rail Enquiries on 0845 7484950.

## By Air

Farnborough Airport is operated by TAG Aviation and, as airport operators, can accommodate arrivals by small, fixed-wing aircraft. The nearest UK airport is London Heathrow.
