# THE FIRST CUT

[TARİH: 01.08.2017 ISMR]

Don't miss SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN from 25-29 September 2017.

ISMR SAYS:

"Over 1,000 exhibitors from 40 countries will showcase their products and technologies over seven halls at this year's SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN event."

## opens its doors from 25-29 September 2017, international

trade visitors can expect an interesting programme at this leading international trade fair for joining, cutting and surface technologies. Exhibitors from around the world will present the complete value chain for joining, cutting and surface technologies – with products and services ranging from shaping, pre-treatment and post-treatment to equipment inspection. Due to modernisation work at Messe Essen, the event will take place at Messe Düsseldorf this year only.

For the first time, the team has reorganised the halls for SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN 2017. They will now be structured on thematic focal areas, which will make it easier for trade visitors from all over the world to find their way around. Numerous conferences, seminars and competitions will also take place during the event, such as the robot welding competition which will be held for the first time at the exhibition.

Over 1,000 exhibitors from 40 countries will showcase their products and technologies over seven halls. The show organiser, Messe Essen GmBH, and its longstanding partner, the German Welding Society (DVS), are expecting around 55,000 visitors from more than 130 countries, with decision-makers from automobile and vehicle construction; shipbuilding; mechanical and installation engineering; pipe fabrication.

Participation will be nearly 63 per cent - almost one in three of the exhibitors will arrive from Asia or overseas. SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN demonstrated a truly international reach in joining, cutting and surface technologies, commented Messe Essen GmbH.

Six official joint participations will show country-specific innovations. China (Hall 11 and Hall 15) and the USA (Hall 10 and Hall 11) will both be represented with two booths each. French exhibitors will be present in Hall 9. Further country pavilions are from Korea (Hall 12), Taiwan (Hall 14) and Japan (Hall 14).

The halls this year have been reorganised with the main focal points as welding; welding: pre- and postweld machining (with occupational safety, workshop, gases, accessories and filler materials); cutting; robotics; automation and production equipment (with gases and auxiliary materials); information technology (with gas, auxiliary materials, adhesive bonding, sealing applying, quality and testing) and services (with organisation, education, promotion of young welders and thermal spraying).

Numerous conferences, seminars and competitions will also take place during the event, such as the robot welding competition which will be held for the first time at the exhibition.

The exhibition will also cover pipeline construction; mining as well as the building trade, the chemical industry and the manufacture of metal products.

## Network for success

"All the market leaders have already registered, including Air Liquide; ESAB; FANUC; Fronius; KUKA; Lincoln Electric; Messer; MicroStep; TRUMPF; Voestalpine Böhler Welding and Yaskawa. The largest group of exhibitors will come from Germany with 368 companies, followed by China (215 exhibitors), Italy (89) and the USA (32). The United Arab Emirates will be represented for the second time. Australia will be amongst the exhibitors, too. In total, overseas exhibitors will be present."

"The exhibits will include the latest devices, installations and materials - not only for welding and cutting but also for associated processes such as brazing/soldering, heat treatment or thermal spraying. In this respect, attention will centre on efficient, cost-saving and sustainable processes for manual welding, mechanised welding as well as automated welding. The range on offer will include, amongst others, productivity-raising special welding processes for the manufacture of cars, new types of remote laser cutting technologies for integration into fabrication as well as lining systems for the economically viable manufacture of engine parts," explained the show organiser.

"The exhibition will also present ultramodern high-tech solutions for the globalised economy. It will meet the rising demand for flexible and location-independent production processes with the latest trends in matters relating to automation and intelligent production. The range on offer will extend from interlinked robot welding installations and CNC flame cutting machines via laser robots for 3D metal cutting right up to externally controllable automated welding cells. In part, they will be shown at the exhibition as world premieres..."

Alongside the exhibition, experts will also introduce research results, market developments and solutions for manufacturers and users of joining, cutting and surface technologies at the DVS Congress 2017. It will take place in the Congress Centre Düsseldorf (abbreviated as CCD) East at Messe Düsseldorf (26–29 September 2017), with the Annual Welding Conference (GST) and the DVS Student Congress. DVS's joint booth in Hall 15 will offer a programme with roundtables, lectures and more.

Newcomers will present pioneering solutions on the joint booth for "Young Innovative Companies" in Hall 15. The joint booth for "Thermal Spraying" in Hall 15 will also provide information about new surface technology processes.

## Exhibition Areas

Equipment for the joining, cutting and surface of metallic and non-metallic materials and material composites; Equipment for heat treatment and other production processes; Fully mechanised and automated equipment; Industrial robots; Data processing; Control and instrumentation technology; Production equipment for fillers and consumables; Workshop and workplace equipment; Safety devices; Occupational health and safety; Accessories, fillers, consumables and auxiliary materials; Measuring and testing technology; Quality assurance; Industry services; Organisations; Research; information, literature and bonding.

## Competitions and 'Young Welders Forum'

After its successful premiere two years ago, the DVS robot welding competition or the 'Robot Welding Contest' will take place in Hall 13 this year. As one of the event highlights, this international competition will take place at Messe Düsseldorf from 25-28 September 2017. Before an international expert public, participants will pit themselves against each other in programming, teaching and, of course, welding on robots and welding power sources.

Robot operators all over the world can register for the second DVS robot welding competition up to 31 July 2017. At seven different stations, using a combination of robots and welding power sources, the participants will introduce their abilities in programming, teaching and welding. The Halle Welding Training and Research Institute (SLV Halle GmbH) will check the welded components directly 'in situ'.

For the best young welders from Germany and overseas, three contests will take place in Hall 14 during the event. The prelude will be the 12th National DVS "Young Welders" Competition in which Germany's most talented young welders will be sought in four disciplines: Gas Welding, Manual Metal Arc Welding, Metal Active Gas Welding and Tungsten Inert Gas Welding.

"The WELDCUP with European participants as well as the International Competition with young welders from all over the world will take place at the show," explained Messe Essen GmbH.

"In terms of promoting young welders, the DVS Student Congress on 26 September 2017 will again offer students and young engineers, dealing with joining technology, a platform to introduce their scientific papers and to put themselves in front of an international expert public. The two best presentations will be awarded with the DVS Young Welders Award. This event will take place in Hall R in CCD East. The offers made to young professionals on the DVS joint booth in Hall 15 'Network and Career' area will include insider tips for successful job applications. Coaching will be free of charge for them."

The DVS Young Welders' competition in Hall 14 will search for Germany's most promising welding talent.

## Getting There

By Air: Düsseldorf Airport is three kilometres away from the exhibition grounds, which can be reached in a few minutes on the 896 bus route or by taxi. Further information can be found at: www.duesseldorf-international.de

By Car: Arrive via the A44 motorway. Signposts to the exhibition grounds will guide visitors to the large P1 and P2 car parks. From there, travel to the show entrances free of charge on shuttle buses (shuttle bus 897).

By Train: The main railway station at Konrad-Adenauer-Platz is in a city centre location. From there, visitors can travel to the exhibition grounds on the U78 and U79 underground railway routes.
