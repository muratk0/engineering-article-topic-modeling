# Blechexpo 2023 is fully booked

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

Preparations for the 16th Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metalworking, together with the 9th Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology, are running “at full bore." The trade fair duo will take place from 7-10 November 2023, showcasing modern, forward-looking sheet metal processing and entire process sequences. The event is supported by a supplementary programme including “Best-Award 2023" and the “Blechexpo Steel Convention 2023".

Trade fair promoter, P. E. Schall, has now announced a full house for the show. The nine halls at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre are fully booked, reported Georg Knauer, project manager of Blechexpo/Schweisstec.

"Interest in Blechexpo/Schweisstec is once again enormous," confirmed Knauer. “Most exhibitors booked again immediately after the last event in 2021, and even requested additional booth floor space for 2023."

The trade fair team has structured the current layout of the nine halls for Blechexpo/ Schweisstec in a targeted manner. Sheetmetal, pipe and profile processing will be in halls 1, 3, 5 and 9. Halls 4 and 6 are reserved for stamping technologies. Cutting, joining and fastening technologies will be presented in hall 7. Hall 8 will focus on pressing and forming technologies. Steel and metal services, as well as surface finishing technology, will be addressed in hall 10.

Held every two years, the trade fair presents issues covering all aspects of thermal and mechanical sheet-metal processing, along with system peripherals including controllers and software, as well as solutions for handling and quality assurance.

“The sheet metalworking industry is currently facing the challenges of energy efficiency, resource conservation and costeffectiveness, which is why high priority is being placed on ever greater degrees of digitalisation and automation to optimise processes," commented the show organiser.

The "Best-Award 2023" will be presented for the sixth time in cooperation with trade journals Blechnet and MM Maschinenmarkt. The five award categories are: 1) sheet metal, pipe and profile processing 2) stamping technologies 3) cutting, joining and fastening technologies 4) pressing and forming technologies and 5) steel and metal services, as well as surface finishing technology. Exhibitors are invited to submit their entries to www.blechnet.com. Submission is free of charge. The winners will be chosen and the innovations unveiled at a ceremony on the first day of the trade fair.

The "Blechexpo Steel Convention 2023" will take place as a highlight at 1 p.m. on the first day of the trade fair. In keeping with the theme "Challenges in the Steel Markets in 2023," attention will be focused on the issues of climate protection, the green transformation, decarbonisation and the costs of steel production. Speakers from leading companies with international operations will address these issues. Interested parties are requested to register bindingly with full name and telephone number by 15 October 2023 at hgd@stahlkommunikation.de

A “futureSteel" congress has also been scheduled for the second day of the trade fair, in cooperation with Marketsteel Media from Düsseldorf. The event will focus on the future of the steel industry and the issue of green steel. Research institutes will also present their projects in theory and in experimental practice.
