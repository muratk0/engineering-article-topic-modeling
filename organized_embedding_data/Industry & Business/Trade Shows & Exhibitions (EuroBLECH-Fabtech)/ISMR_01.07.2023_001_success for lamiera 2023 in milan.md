# Success for LAMIERA 2023 in Milan

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Extremely positive results were logged for the 22nd edition of LAMIERA, the international exhibition of sheet metal, tube and section cutting, forming and processing technologies which took place at fieramilano Rho, Milan (Italy), from 10-13 May 2023.

"Over a total floor space of 40,000 square metres, 400 exhibiting companies (27% of which were from overseas) represented 22 countries. Visits of professional operators registered during the four exhibition days totalled 19,000. In addition, over 4,000 visitors from the Made in Steel trade show also expressed interest in visiting LAMIERA. The agreement signed with Made in Steel, (which was held in the nearby halls) included a free passage from one exhibition to the other," explained the show organiser.

Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association) and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU, LAMIERA was unusually held just one year after the previous edition, which had been postponed for 12 months due to COVID-19.

Barbara Colombo, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, commented: "We are very pleased with the results we obtained: even if the exhibition took place just twelve months after the previous one, it not only confirmed the outcome of the last show but, on some indicators, was even better."

Out of the total number of visitors, 7.5% were from overseas (67 countries). The largest delegations came from Albania; Austria; China; Croatia; France; Germany; UK; Spain; Netherlands; Poland; Portugal; Czech Republic; Romania; Slovenia; Switzerland; Tunisia; Turkey and Hungary.

"In the aisles of the two exhibition halls 13 and 15, among numerous visitors were also about fifty operators from Egypt; India; Morocco; Mexico; Czech Republic; Turkey; the United States; Uzbekistan and Vietnam. They were invited as part of the delegation initiative organised by UCIMU, in cooperation with MAECI-Ministry of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation and the ICE-Italian Trade Agency. There was also an independent delegation of over 30 Turkish end users from Manisa. The ICE-Italian Trade Agency set up an area at the trade fair that was dedicated to start-up companies.

A programme of about 40 meetings was held at LAMIALAMIERA, the arena promoted by UCIMU FOUNDATION and developed with the Lombardy Region. Digitalisation and sustainability were among the most discussed topics during the conferences arranged by the exhibitors and trade-show organisers.

“Eight-hundred operators took part in the events hosted at the LAMIALAMIERA arena, in addition to 3,500 users that were remotely connected via live streaming," added UCIMU.

The trade show also hosted around one thousand students, invited as part of the UCIMU Academy initiative. Accompanied by their teachers, students participated in free visits and stand tours guided by tutors from the association.

The next edition of LAMIERA is scheduled to take place in Milan, Italy, from 7-10 May 2025.■
