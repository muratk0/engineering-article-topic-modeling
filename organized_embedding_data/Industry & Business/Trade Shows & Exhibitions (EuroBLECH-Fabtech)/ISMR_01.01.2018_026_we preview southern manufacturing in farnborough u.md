# We preview Southern Manufacturing in Farnborough UK from 6-8 February 2018.

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

Southern Manufacturing celebrates its 20th anniversary when it returns to Farnborough, UK, from 6-8 February 2018. Even more significantly for the show's anticipated 9,000 visitors, 2018 will see the event move into its new permanent home, the newly constructed Hall 1 complex at Farnborough International Conference & Exhibition Centre.

"Southern Manufacturing will be the first big show to be held in the £30m development, an ultra-modern 20,000m² venue offering world-class facilities, easy access and free on-site parking for 3000 cars," the event organiser told ISMR.

The show's location in one of the most important regions for the UK's £20bn aerospace business, and the supply chain that supports it, is also important for machinery vendors; an average year will see deals (worth millions of pounds sterling) closed on the show floor.

This year's show also features a free seminar programme of one-hour sessions that cover key manufacturing and supply chain issues, presented by top speakers in their industry fields. Subjects such as CE marking; 3D printing; lean thinking; patent issues; cobots and the Industrial Internet of Things are on the agenda. These can be booked in advance via the website.

## An industrial focus

Southern Manufacturing, and the co-located Southern Electronics and AutoAero shows, is an important annual industrial technology event in the UK, catering to a wide range of engineering and manufacturing enterprises. Its comprehensive selection of exhibitors gives the event extremely broad appeal, serving equally well as a showroom for those looking to invest in the latest machinery and as a lively marketplace for components and subcontract engineering services of all descriptions.

As a multi-discipline event, it presents visitors with the chance to address multiple sourcing issues in a single visit. With an expected 800 companies from all over the world taking part, it is this diversity that visitors consistently rate as one of the show's best aspects.

"Machinery/production hardware is one of the main features of the show, with numerous major manufacturers giving live demonstrations of their latest offerings. As the first big engineering show of the year, it is a good opportunity for

## An eye on exhibitors

Bystronic returns for 2018, highlighting a range of production technologies and automation solutions including laser cutting, waterjetcutting and bending systems along with software that streamlines the process chain and minimises waste to enhance profitability.

Another well-known name returning for 2018 is Bruderer UK, celebrating its 50th anniversary. On show will be a wide range of products including Fibro 'gas springs' and tooling components; quick tool-change and work holding devices; spotting presses; try-out presses and die turnover units; roller and spray lubrication systems; OEE systems (Overall Equipment Effectiveness); press and tool protection monitoring systems and Zani high-tonnage mechanical presses.

Structural bonding products from Henkel Adhesives will also be on display at the show – its LOCTITE Universal Structural Bonders are designed to solve a broad variety of design and assembly challenges (booth H165). See page 54 for further details.

The event will also welcome other types of production hardware including 3D print and laser processing. IPG Photonics will be demonstrating its Multi-Axis Workstation, a Class 1 laser system that is fully compatible with the full range of welding laser sources at powers ranging from 150 to 8,000 Watts. The system has been optimised for the manufacture of small parts. Side panels can be removed to accommodate automated feeds, with a maximum working envelope of 500 x 300 x 300mm. Up to 5-axis operation is possible and a range of standard options such as vision and power measurement are also featured. The system may be configured to order for various specific fibre laser processing applications.

Tooling is another area of particular strength, with several notable vendors exhibiting in 2018. Berger Tools is a UK supplier of standard parts for machine equipment press and mould tools and associated components, providing both standard and bespoke parts. On the stand this year will be a series of hygienic standard elements that meet EHEDG requirements and the 3-A Sanitary Standard. The range is optimised for minimal contamination, using stainless steel with ground or polished surfaces. Hygienically safe FDA-compliant elastomer seals sit flush with the surface, preventing dirt ingress. Smooth surfaces of below Ra 0.8μm ensure that dirt particles cannot adhere and are removed during cleaning, said the company. The parts are designed to reduce cleaning time in food production, medical technology, pharmaceutical industries, dispersion paint manufacturers and other sectors.

Wilson Tool will launch its Express Rail 2000 and Express Air press brake clamping systems in the UK at Southern Manufacturing 2018. These clamping systems are designed to reduce set-up times and increase productivity with quick tool locking and automatic tool alignment. Other products on show will include its QuickTap tapping tool for thick turret punching machines and the Smart-X Storage 4.0 addition to the Wilson Xtra Accessories' range. Integrated tool management software allows tools to be managed as well as stored, with features such as passcode entry and automatic ordering.

GETTING THERE Admission and car parking is free. Farnborough Exhibition and Conference Centre is easily reached by road, air or public transport.

## By Road

From the M3, take junction 4 and follow the A331 to Farnborough. The show will be clearly AA signposted on all roads approaching the venue. Car parking is plentiful and free.

## By Rail

Farnborough North Camp is the nearest station but Farnborough Main and Aldershot stations are also nearby. For train timetable information, call National Rail Enquiries on 0845 7484950. A complimentary coach shuttle service will operate from both North Camp and Farnborough Main stations.

## By Air

Farnborough Airport is operated by TAG Aviation and, as airport operators, can accommodate arrivals by private jet/ charter aircraft. The nearest major UK passenger airport is London Heathrow.
