# A SHOWCASE FOR INNOVATION

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Fifteen zones and thirteen theme villages will showcase a range of technologies, processes, equipment and expertise across multiple sectors at the trade show."

This year's Global Industrie trade show in Paris, France, expects to attract 2,300 exhibitors and 40,000 visitors, with 1,500 machines in operation over four days.

The sixth edition of Global Industrie returns to Paris Nord Villepinte, France, from 25-28 March 2024. This global showcase for industrial innovation is under the patronage of the French President, Emmanuel Macron. It will host several French Ministers, elected officials, ambassadors and representatives of large industrial European professional organisations.

"We expect to attract 2,300 exhibitors over 100,000m² of exhibition space with 1,500 machines in operation over four days," the show organiser, GL Events, told ISMR. "Approximately 40,000 visitors are expected to attend. The trade show will highlight a range of solutions to address multiple challenges facing industry (such as the environmental and ecological transition; recruitment; social and societal responsibility; training; financing; Industry 5.0 etc.)," it added.

Previous editions of the show have been held in even years in Paris and odd years in Lyon, France. This geographical diversification enables it to enhance both its offer and the variety of its visitors.

## Zones, villages and awards

Fifteen zones and thirteen theme villages will showcase a range of technologies, processes, equipment and expertise across multiple sectors at the trade show.

"Machinery demonstrations will be at the heart of 'the biggest factory in France' to enable visitors to access the latest innovations and industrial processes. This will also be an opportunity for them to identify the trends that will transform tomorrow's industry. The GI Awards, for their part, will highlight the most innovative discoveries identified among exhibitors," continued GL Events.

For those who wish to promote their expertise or find the innovations that can improve their production systems, the Gi Awards are one of the highlights at Global Industrie. Each year, the GI Awards select examples of innovation presented at the exhibition. All exhibitors can take part after filling in their application form.

An initial selection will be made by a jury of experts who will select the entries that have demonstrated their innovative nature, performance etc. Selected companies then present their entry in an oral examination before a jury of manufacturers, experts and specialist journalists.

Following this, nine prizes are awarded, including a "Start-up" award presented to a start-up company which is less than three years old. The exhibition then invites its visitors to participate with the creation of a "Visitors' Favourite" award.

## GI Avenir

The issues of training, employment and the better representation of women in the industry remain central to reindustrialisation and its sustainability. Global Industrie will include several activities and facilities designed to "make industry attractive" for the younger generation who will become the future talents of manufacturing.

"GI Avenir and the Golden Tech, the competition for industrial excellence, will be the tools we use to make jobs attractive and meet recruitment needs. More than 5,000 young people and job seekers are expected at this 2024 edition," said GL Events.

"Companies have increasing difficulty in finding their future employees. There were more than 180,000 vacancies in industry in 2023. To raise awareness of jobs in industry among the younger generation, the GI Avenir area will be completely redesigned and will operate in synergy with Golden Tech. Two areas will be set up in Hall 6 to create a visitor trail," it added.

The first area of GI Avenir will aim to throw off the obsolete image of industry, "make industry attractive" and set the younger generation dreaming by presenting multiple professions and sectors of industry to them in a fun and informative way. This area will contain various demonstration areas such as Worldskills; the French Robotics Cup (Planète Sciences); Digital Smile; a Jobs and Qualifications Campus and pitches (providing advice through interviews, feedback and first-hand accounts).

It will also be the starting point for specific visitor trails to meet professionals such as the Fédération des Industries Mécaniques and its partners and an 'Elles Bougent' section which invites girls (middle school, high school and university students) to discover exciting jobs in industry and talk to "godmothers" and representatives of the association. Walk-throughs will also be organised for teachers.

## Golden Tech

"Golden Tech is the place where visitors come to see those vying to become the future industrial elite. It is an opportunity for companies to meet special talents to recruit and for the public to marvel at their professional excellence or even envision taking part themselves in the future," explained the show organiser.

This last GI Avenir section will contain areas providing support for students and job applicants with coaching sessions and job dating to meet HR managers from companies that are recruiting etc.

Golden Tech promotes the attractiveness and diversity of jobs in industry through a competition offering an overall vision of industry. For four days, some one hundred of the men and women who are forging today's industry will compete in 16 rings, representing 16 professions and skills. They will be evaluated on their dexterity and technical skills.

To link these different professions, the competition topics will focus on the theme of industry and sport to show how industrial excellence serves sporting excellence. In a unique tour, visitors will be given an overview of industry via the various "rings" organised according to industrial logic (from design to production management and logistics by way of production and control plus robotics/automation).

Three professions featured for the first time in 2024 will be lathe operators, mechatronics and cybersecurity managers. After seeing the demonstrations, visitors will be able to participate in a friendly competition using an augmented reality game. For further information, see www.goldentech.fr

## Innovation exemplified by sport

As a showcase for industrial expertise, Global Industrie will highlight two major events that will focus the world's attention on France in 2024: the Olympic and Paralympic Games. It will spotlight the innovative spirit of industry through examples that have capitalised on the most advanced technologies in different sectors (such as transport; infrastructure; measurement; materials; 3D printing etc.) to advance sport in our everyday lives.

It has partnered for the occasion with France's National Institute of Sport, Expertise and Performance (INSEP) which uses the latest technology to train athletes who will compete at the Olympic and Paralympic Games. At the exhibition, a stand bringing together the expertise of INSEP and the Carnot Institutes will highlight the overlaps between sport, research and industry.

This sporting spirit will also be in evidence at the exhibition in events such as the Golden Tech awards, where the professional tests will align with this theme. For example, the lathe operators will work on a steel rugby ball, the milling machine operators will work on mechanical parts for a Golden Dream motorcycle, the metrologist will test competition standard foil boards and the robotics engineer will have to define the path of a canoe held by a robot arm etc.
