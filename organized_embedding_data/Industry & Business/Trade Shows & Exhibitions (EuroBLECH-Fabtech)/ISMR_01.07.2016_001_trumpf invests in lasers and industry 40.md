# TRUMPF invests in lasers and Industry 4.0

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

MACHINE TOOL and laser manufacturer, TRUMPF, is building a demonstration factory in Chicago, USA that is designed, from the ground up, as a flexible, digitally connected production plant. The entire “sheet metal process chain" – from ordering of a sheet metal part to its design, production and delivery – is intelligently interlinked according to the very latest principles. With construction costs estimated at about 13 million euros, the demo factory was designed by the Berlin architectural office of Barkow Leibinger and its official opening is planned for the summer of 2017.

“Unlike the classic show rooms of the TRUMPF Group, where the emphasis lies on individual machines, the Chicago plant will be focusing on the entire customer process, complete with material and information flow. Covering an area of roughly 5,500 square metres, the new site aims to demonstrate the interaction of people, machines, storage equipment, automation, software and Industry 4.0 solutions," TRUMPF told ISMR.

In a separate initiative, TRUMPF has invested nearly 30 million euros in new development and production facilities for solid-state lasers. The new production facility in Schramberg will offer 12,000 square metres of additional floor space, enlarging the total available production area to nearly twice its current size. The new facility is due to begin production by the end of 2017. The company plans to expand its development centre in Schramberg by adding an additional floor covering an area of 1800 square metres.
