# Success for SteelFab 2016

[TARİH: 01.06.2016 ISMR]

STEELFAB 2016 once again proved that it was a must-attend event for the Middle Eastern regional steel fabrication and metal working industry. It featured 376 direct exhibitors and attracted 8,176 trade visitors from 83 countries. More than 900 brands were exhibited during the four-day show, compared to 350 exhibitors at the last edition. It attracted a higher visitor footprint from the Indian Subcontinent, North Africa and Central Asia, while visitors from the GCC also showed a steady growth.

“Indications of the show's global acceptance was visible from the steady increase of visitors from Afghanistan; Algeria; Armenia; Azerbaijan; Egypt; Ghana; India; Kazakhstan; Kenya; Libya; Nigeria; Sudan; Tanzania; Tunisia; Uganda; Ukraine and the GCC countries," commented the organiser. “Economic diversification has become the crux of the development strategy for the GCC region. Policies and guidelines are being announced frequently to inject fresh vigour into the non-oil sector. Investments are also pouring in to sectors like infrastructure and manufacturing due to forthcoming mega events such as Expo 2020 and FIFA World Cup 2022. The immediate beneficiary of these moves is the core sector that propels the steel fabrication and metal working industry." 
Next year's SteelFab will take place in Sharjah, UAE from 16-19 January 2017. For more information, see www.steelfabme.com

## Nederman opens new facility

NEDERMAN, a specialist in industrial air filtration, has now re-modelled its UK headquarters in Preston (UK) to offer a comprehensive demonstration and training facility for manufacturers aiming to reduce the environmental impact from production processes.

The grand opening of the new facility was conducted by Nederman's Senior Vice President and Head of the EMEA Division, Hans Dahlen. The opening of the new demonstration area coincided with the UK launch of Nederman Filtac, a division dedicated to oil mist filtration and extraction for machine tools.

To announce the arrival of the Nederman Filtac line in the UK, the newly refurbished demonstration area includes a new operational oil mist filtration unit. To demonstrate the capabilities of this new unit, Nederman has invested in an oil mist generating machine, which enables customers to witness the oil mist extraction rates and efficiency levels of this new line. The Nederman Filtac range utilises patent-pending FibreDrain media technology, which makes it possible to capture small sub-micron droplets. Its training facility can seat up to 30 delegates. 
See www.nederman.com for more details.
