# On the upswing

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

Above and below: Scenes from Blechexpo 2013

THE TRADE FAIR ALLIANCE comprising Blechexpo, Schweisstec and Coilex is registering a 7 per cent growth in exhibitor numbers in comparison to the results of the last event in 2013. Blechexpo and Schweisstec open their doors from 3 to 6 November 2015, and private trade fair promoter Paul E. Schall is counting on over 1000 companies to support the events based on the growth in thermal and mechanical cutting, joining and fastening technology.

“Blechexpo and Schweisstec will experience more than 15 per cent growth in exhibition floor space and are headed for record results in other areas as well,” said the organisers of this year’s shows. “Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal processing and the Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology, as well as the second Coilex technology park for the production of electromechanical components, will once again present the entire process sequence for sheet metal, pipe and profile processing – plus processing directly from the coil – and will play a special role in 2015.”

Blechexpo and Schweisstec take place over a two-year cycle at the state trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and cover the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology. Blechexpo is now ranked the world’s number two trade fair for sheet metal processing. Today, the catchment area for expert visitors extends well beyond Central, South and Eastern Europe.

ISMR is a media partner and will be exhibiting at the event. For further details, see www.blechexpo-messe.de
