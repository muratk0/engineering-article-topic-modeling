# AN EYE ON EXPANSION

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS:

“Steel demand in the MENA region is projected to reach a new height of 70 million metric tonnes by 2020”

SteelFab, a keenly anticipated trade fair for the global steel fabrication industry, has successfully crossed regional borders to increase its geographical bandwidth. What started as a dedicated platform for the metal working, metal manufacturing and steel fabrication industry for the Middle East has grown to attract visitors and buyers from as far as the Indian Subcontinent, North Africa and Central Asia. Next year’s event will be held at Expo Centre Sharjah from 20-23 January 2014.

The ninth edition of SteelFab, the region’s comprehensive trade event for the steel fabrication, metal working and metal manufacturing industry, was organised by Expo Centre Sharjah (with support from the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry) from 14-17 January 2013. During the four-day run, the exhibition hosted around 250 direct exhibitors from 24 countries representing more than 600 brands of steel machinery, welding equipment and surface preparation and attracted 7296 visitors.
