# 2015 AIST Steelmaker of the Year

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

Mario Longhi, President and Chief Executive Officer, United States Steel Corporation, was named “Steelmaker of the Year” at AISTech 2015, the Iron and Steel Technology conference and exhibition, managed by the Association for Iron & Steel Technology (AIST). The Steelmaker of the Year Award is presented annually by AIST to recognise notable leaders for their impact on the steel industry.

The AIST Steelmaker of the Year Award was presented on 5 May 2015 during the President’s Award Breakfast, one of the signature events of AISTech 2015 (4-7 May at the Cleveland Convention Centre, Cleveland, Ohio, USA). The breakfast programme featured the presentation of several awards, followed by a keynote presentation by Lourenco Goncalves, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer, Cliffs Natural Resources Inc., entitled, “Global Iron and Steel Outlook”.

Mario Longhi joined US Steel in July 2012 to serve as executive vice president and chief operating officer. He was appointed president and chief operating officer in June 2013, and was elected president and chief executive officer in September 2013. Under his leadership, US Steel has engaged in a multi-year transformational journey, “The Carnegie Way”. In 2014, the company realised US$ 575 million in Carnegie Way benefits and achieved its best financial performance since 2008, including US$ 1.5 billion of cash flow from operations.

Longhi earned a bachelor’s degree in metallurgical engineering from the Institute Mauá de Tecnologica in São Paulo. He serves on the executive committee of the World Steel Association.

## European service manager

On 1 April 2015, Eberhard Heide became the service manager for AP&T’s European market, with the exception of the Nordic area. With the press specialist’s office in Germany as his base, he will develop Central European operations as a whole, which are currently located in Germany, Denmark, Poland and the UK.

Eberhard Heide is a certified mechanical engineer. He most recently worked at a major European press manufacturer as the service manager. He will begin working at AP&T at a time when the company is developing its service offering as a means to provide proactive services that help maintain the customer’s production performance during the life-cycle of the entire installation.

“We are extremely pleased that we managed to recruit Eberhard Heide to our service organisation. He has in-depth expertise of our product area, solid understanding of the market and customer needs, and he is a successful and committed leader who will play an important role in the exciting development to come,” said Michael Hunger, head of AP&T’s European market and Martti Jalava, director of aftermarket services at AP&T.
