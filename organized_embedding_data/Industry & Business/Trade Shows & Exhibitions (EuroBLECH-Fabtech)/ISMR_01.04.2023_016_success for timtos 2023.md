# Success for TIMTOS 2023

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

TIMTOS 2023 in Taipei, Taiwan, concluded successfully on 11 March 2023. It attracted over 45,000 on-site visitors from Taiwan and overseas (including nearly 6,000 international visitors from more than 100 countries). Delegations were from Turkey; Vietnam; Indonesia; Spain and Algeria. The top five countries with the highest turnout were Japan; Malaysia; India; South Korea and Thailand.

More than 1,000 exhibitors from 18 countries/regions hosted nearly 6,200 booths across the Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre Halls 1 & 2 and TWTC Hall 1 from 6-11 March 2023. Three new themed areas were added in TWTC Hall 1 to feature "Advanced Metalworking Technology," "Additive Manufacturing" and "Future Manufacturing." TIMTOS also launched two major events: "ESGaction!" and “ESG in TIMTOS" that brought together exhibitors and consulting organisations to provide new insights on carbon neutrality. Among them, “Dr. Net Zero" joined senior industry consultants to offer free carbon footprint consultations for show attendees.

The TIMTOS opening ceremony included distinguished guests such as President Tsai Ing-Wen; Vice Premier Wen-Tsan Cheng; Director Sandra Oudkirk of the American Institute in Taiwan, Taipei Office; and TIMTOS organisers, TAITRA and TAMI.

“TIMTOS 2023 is starting a new chapter for the global machine tool community after the COVID-19 pandemic," commented TAITRA Chairman, James Huang. He expressed excitement about the dynamic return of international attendees, including four national pavilions (USA, Switzerland, Germany and Denmark) and delegations led by industry associations in India; Malaysia; Thailand; Vietnam; Japan and Turkey. Huang also pointed out that this year's TIMTOS highlighted ESG trends and their impact on the machine tool industry's transition towards making manufacturing more efficient, smarter and more sustainable.

TAMI Chairman, Larry Wei, gave a warm welcome to international visitors at TIMTOS 2023. Machine tool manufacturers nowadays, he said, are devoted to reducing carbon emissions instead of just reducing costs. The optimisation of equipment and services to achieve net-zero goals will be the key driving force for order growth, he added.

“Many business deals were made during show hours, with several exhibitors delighted with considerable orders secured on-site. It was the largest trade show ever recorded after Taiwan lifted all its border controls since the COVID-19 outbreak. The online exhibition will continue to run until 6 April 2023 and has already attracted 7,000 visitors from nearly 15 countries," the show organisers told ISMR.

"The line-up of events; the Future Manufacturing Forum; ESG Seminars; Maker Workshop; Insights Talk; Marine & Offshore Machinery Seminar and Metalworking Ecosystem Guided Tours accommodated roughly 1,000 attendees. For those unable to attend in person, show highlights were easily accessible through the "ManufacturersWin Podcast" and the co-branded online video coverage presented by Modern Machine Shop, "Media Eye on TIMTOS 2023, which have already enjoyed more than 20,000 views and listens," it added.

TIMTOS is scheduled to return in March 2025.
