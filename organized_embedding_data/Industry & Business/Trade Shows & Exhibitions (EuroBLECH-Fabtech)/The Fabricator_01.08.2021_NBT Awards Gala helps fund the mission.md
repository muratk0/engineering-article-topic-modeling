# NBT Awards Gala helps fund the mission

[TARİH: 01.08.2021 The Fabricator]

Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT) invites you to attend the awards gala on October 21 at 6 p.m. CDT. Along with presenting sponsor AMADA AMERICA, INC., NBT will celebrate some of manufacturing’s finest champions as three industry awards are presented to recognize and honor the contributions made by an individual or family, a company, and a summer manufacturing camp.

Join your friends, colleagues, and NBT as we applaud those who share their passion for manufacturing and hear uplifting stories of the progress NBT is making to build tomorrow’s skilled manufacturing workforce.

Proceeds from this event support NBT’s summer manufacturing camp and scholarship programs. NBT awards scholarships of up to $2,500 to students pursuing manufacturing-related degrees. Students can apply for spring 2022 scholarships now through Sept. 30, 2021. Up to 25 scholarships will be awarded this season. Learn more at
