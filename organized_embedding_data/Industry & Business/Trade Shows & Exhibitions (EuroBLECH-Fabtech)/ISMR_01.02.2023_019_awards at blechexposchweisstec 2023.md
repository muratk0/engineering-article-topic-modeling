# Awards at Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

A special highlight at the 16th Blechexpo (international trade fair for sheet metalworking) and the 9th Schweisstec (international trade fair for joining technology), from 7-10 November 2023 in Stuttgart, Germany, will be the presentation of the “Best Award 2023" for outstanding innovation. The deadline for submitting entries is 31 May 2023.

Trade fair promoter Schall is organising these awards for the sixth time with trade journals Blechnet and MM Maschinenmarkt. "The halls will be full in 2023," observed Georg Knauer, project manager of the Blechexpo/Schweisstec trade fair duo. "They're already as good as fully booked out. Only a few spaces remain."

Sheet metal, pipe and profile processing will be the thematic focus in halls 1, 3, 5 and 9. Halls 4 and 6 will be devoted to stamping technology. Cutting, joining and fastening technologies will be presented in hall 7. Hall 8 is reserved for pressing and forming technology. Steel and metal services, as well as surface finishing technology, will be presented in hall 10.

Blechexpo/Schweisstec covers the entire process sequence for sheet metalworking. The line-up includes efficient process technologies for modern laser technology; fully automated robotic sheet-metal processing machines; precision tools and systems for punching; bending; braking and forming as well as innovative micro-waterjet cutting technologies.

System peripherals, including controllers and software, as well as solutions for handling and quality assurance are also covered. Important investments in digitalisation and automation are on the agenda. The challenges of energy efficiency, the conservation of resources, economic efficiency and sustainability are also fuelling new approaches and future-proof technologies which will be showcased at Blechexpo/Schweisstec.

“Many new first-time exhibitors will present their companies at Blechexpo/ Schweisstec 2023," added Georg Knauer. “It's also interesting to note that quite a few exhibitors who had interrupted their trade fair activities in recent years are now coming back."
