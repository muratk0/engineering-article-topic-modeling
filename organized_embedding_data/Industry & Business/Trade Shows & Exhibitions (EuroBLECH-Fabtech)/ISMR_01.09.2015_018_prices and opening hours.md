## Blechexpo travel directions

## BY CAR

The new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre is 13km from downtown Stuttgart and right next to Stuttgart Airport. Follow the signs to the airport. The new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre can be accessed directly from the A8 motorway and the B27 highway.

## Access from A8 motorway

Coming from Stuttgart, exit the motorway at “Echterdinger Ei” to the exhibition centre / airport access road. From there continue to the car park, to which direct access is provided. Coming from Munich, there is a separate exit for the car park shortly before the exhibition centre.

Access from B27 highway from Stuttgart/Tübingen The parking areas at the western perimeter of the trade fair grounds can be accessed from either direction or you can continue via the L 1192 to the car park exit from the A8 motorway.

## BY AIR

The airport terminals are about 200 metres from the trade fair grounds, and can be easily reached by foot. There is also a direct suburban railway connection to Stuttgart Central Station (line S2 or S3, travel time: 27 minutes).

## BY RAIL

Stuttgart is linked directly to 13 European capital cities via the railway system (InterCity Express, InterCity and Interregional). Stuttgart Central Station is located in the city centre, approximately 13km from Stuttgart Airport and the new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre.

From central railway station to the exhibition centre Suburban railway lines S2 and S3 operate from the central railway station to Stuttgart Airport and the new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre. Travel time from Stuttgart Central Station to the new Stuttgart Exhibition Centre is 27 minutes.

## ARKU Maschinenbau GmbH

www.arku.com

ARKU has been active in the deburring sector since May 2015, following the successful development of new deburring and edge rounding machines. Thanks to a cooperative agreement with Wöhler Brush Tech GmbH, ARKU is also able to offer the complete EdgeRacer® machine series. These are designed for processing various different materials such as aluminium, steel, stainless steel, titanium and more.

“EdgeBreaker® is the latest machine series developed specifically for processing thicker parts. Fitted with an oscillating roll, the EdgeBreaker® effectively removes, burrs and grinds thick slags from laser-cut, plasma-cut and flame-cut parts, while the transverse rounding units with their grinding heads round the edges from both sides. The EdgeBreaker® series is now available to customers for test and contract work in the ARKU levelling and deburring centre in Baden-Baden,” ARKU told ISMR.

Thinner stamped and laser-cut parts are processed by ARKU on the EdgeRacer®. Fitted with special disk brushes, these machines deburr and round sheet parts of up to 2,000mm in width.

## LVD Company N.V.

www.lvdgroup.com

LVD will be presenting its latest developments in bending and laser cutting technology with its advanced CADMAN software solutions.

“CADMAN® suite brings deeper process integration and creates maximised throughput in the workshop. Its modules include interacting with ERP, efficiently classifying and grouping jobs, offline work preparation, intuitive touch controls and workshop visualisation. The suite comprises an intelligent CADMAN core database combined with CADMAN-JOB, CADMAN-B, -L and -P, TOUCH-B and -L and TOUCH-i4,” commented LVD.

LVD will also highlight its ToolCell hydraulic press brake with automated tooling. The system is designed to minimise press brake set-up operations and ensure consistency from the first to the last using its onboard Easy-Form® Laser adaptive bending system. For fibre laser cutting, LVD will showcase its Electra FL-3015, complete with 4kW source and integrated automatic nozzle changer.

## EHRT

www.ehrt.de

EHRT will be in Hall 1 on booth 1902 at Blechexpo in Stuttgart. The EHRT product range is a complete processing solution designed for the efficient cutting, punching and bending of bus bars for the electrical industry (e.g. switchgear production).

Manufactured in Germany, EHRT’s 50 years of experience brings the latest CNC technology to bus bar manufacturing. Its solutions include automation, MultiTool 6-in-1, high speed punching machines and either Standard or Professional lines. Its new gantry system for fully integrated storage is dubbed the “Smart Factory”.

PunchPRO production planning software is for EHRT punching and bending machines. Nesting software avoids the waste of long scrap pieces. After the production process, users receive evaluations and reports on production material usage as well as production times.

## Friedrich Remmert GmbH

www.remmert.de

At Blechexpo 2015, Remmert is devoting its booth presence to guaranteeing efficiency in the production environment. On its stand 1710 in Hall 1, the company will present its new robotic application, Laser FLEX, a rapid solution for fully automated laser equipment feeding. Two automation units decouple the supply and disposal of manufacturing machines. The result – material change times of 60 seconds.

Other Remmert highlights include the BASIC Tower sheet and the Remmert sheet storage system. Both storage systems are individually configured and expanded by connecting Laser FLEX to fully automated sheet manufacturing units.

## SafanDarley B.V.

www.SafanDarley.com

SafanDarley has expanded its E-Brake Ergonomic series of press brakes. These are compact, portable, high speed electric press brakes, with a 40-ton model that offers higher capacity and a longer working length of 1600mm. The Ergonomic 40-ton press brake has an open height of 590mm standard and a stroke of 300mm standard.

The Ergonomic series has been specifically designed for bending small parts. Rapid acceleration and deceleration of the electrical servo-driven ram delivers approach speeds up to 20mm/sec. SafanDarley will show this machine at Blechexpo in Stuttgart as a highlight as well as the E-Brake 50T 5020, the E-Brake 150T 3100 and the hybrid M-Shear 310-6.

## Haeger Europe BV
## www.phillipscorp.com/haeger

Haeger’s recently launched machine model 824 OneTouch -4 LITE has, said the company, been a great success.

“Demand for this machine model has already been higher than for our more basic model 824 WindowTouch -4. The ability to automatically feed two different self-clinching fasteners, in any possible combination, with two additional selfclinching fasteners for manual feeding enables productivity increases up to 50% over the 824 WindowTouch -4 machine model,” the company explained.

Besides the 824 OneTouch -4 LITE, Haeger will also be exhibiting five more machine models at Blechexpo, including two new entry level models with a mini-touchscreen control and single part handling ability.
