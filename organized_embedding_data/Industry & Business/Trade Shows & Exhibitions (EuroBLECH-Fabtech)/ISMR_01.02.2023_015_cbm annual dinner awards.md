# CBM Annual Dinner & Awards

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

The Confederation of British Metalforming (CBM) in the UK will be holding its Annual Dinner and Awards evening on 30 March 2023 at The Grand Hotel in Birmingham (UK). The annual industry awards recognise excellence within the CBM membership.

"The annual CBM Metalforming Awards are a wonderful way to promote a company. As the voice of [the UK's metalforming] industry, we can't wait to recognise accomplishments and show the wider supply chain and business community what has been achieved," the CBM told /SMR.

The four categories this year are: 'Company of the Year'; 'Apprentice of the Year'; 'HSE Initiative of the Year' and ‘Energy Efficiency Initiative'. Sponsorship opportunities are available.

“Each category will have its own independent judging panel (whose decision is final). Submission deadline is Thursday 9 February 2023, with the shortlist announced on 1 March 2023. The winners will be announced on 30 March 2023 at the CBM Annual Dinner & Awards," added the CBM.

Entries and supporting documentation can be emailed to melinda.jean@thecbm.co.uk
