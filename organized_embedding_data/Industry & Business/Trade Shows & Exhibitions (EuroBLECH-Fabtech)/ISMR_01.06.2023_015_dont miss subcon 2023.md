# Don't miss Subcon 2023

[TARİH: 01.06.2023 ISMR]

The UK's largest subcontract manufacturing supply chain show, Subcon, returns for its 46th year to Hall 12 of the NEC, Birmingham, in the UK from 7-8 June 2023. Visitors can source, subcontract, benchmark and learn all under one roof.

“Over 200 exhibitors will be displaying their products and latest innovations. There will also be plenty of dedicated networking areas at the show, including a VIP zone. The exhibition will cover everything from assembly work; CNC machining; metal fabrication; electronics; component parts and more," explained the show organiser, MA Exhibitions.

Alongside the exhibition, two conference theatres will provide topical content on the challenges, pain points and opportunities for the manufacturing industry. Topics include optimising the supply chain; AI and automation; agility and resilience; additive manufacturing; cybersecurity; sustainability; digitalisation and more.

“With the industry facing a multitude of challenges, from soaring costs to supply chain shortages, there has never been a more critical time for suppliers and visitors to meet," added the show organiser.

Industry sectors covered by Subcon include automotive; electronics; aerospace; materials; energy; rail; oil and gas and defence/security. Last year, Subcon attracted over 4000 visitors. This year, Subcon is co-sponsored by the OMNIDEX Group and Turkish Machinery (Machinery Exporters' Association).

ISMR is a media partner at the event and its June issue will be distributed at Subcon.■
