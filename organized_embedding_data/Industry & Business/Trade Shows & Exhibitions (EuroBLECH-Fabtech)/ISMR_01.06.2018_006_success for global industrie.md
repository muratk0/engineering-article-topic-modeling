# Success for Global Industrie

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 ISMR]

THE NEW GLOBAL Industrie exhibition in France this March attracted 40,800 visitors and 2,700 exhibitors. Global Industrie represented a combination of four leading exhibitions - INDUSTRIE, MIDEST, SMART INDUSTRIES and TOLEXPO - at the heart of Manufacturing Week. The French President, Emmanuel Macron, invited nearly 200 manufacturers to dine at the Élysée palace before the opening day, and three ministers and the French Secretary of State visited the exhibition. Flagship French manufacturers, including French Fab companies, were also represented at the event.

Europe was represented by several delegations and the 2nd Stakeholder Forum. The Connected Factory, which included demonstrators from the four exhibitions, drew more than 8,000 visitors. The 5,900 students and more than 1,000 jobseekers who visited the exhibition show that manufacturing is once again a promising sector for young talent and future generations. The free Business Meetings service resulted in 1,041 business meetings.

The four-day event showcased 18 international pavilions (including Portugal, Italy, Algeria, and South Korea) and visits by foreign delegations and distinguished guests, such as Hideaki Omura, the Governor of Aichi Province in Japan.

The Stakeholder Forum 2018, organised by the Economy Ministry and the European Commission, was held as part of Global Industrie. During the three half-days, 400 people (ministers, general managers, CEOs of large companies, local authorities etc.) participated in workshops dealing with various aspects of the sector's digital transformation (financing, digital skills, public/private partnerships, digital platforms, digital innovation hubs).

The Global Industrie Awards were also held during the event. The six winners selected by the jury from 180 competing innovations were announced to the 1,500-strong audience at the Global Industrie Awards event on 27 March 2018. These were:

Industrial Transformation: MESHROOM for its virtual reality software MESHROOM VR Studio.

New Technology: AUTODESK FRANCE and RAMLAB, for the on-demand hybrid production of the Waampeller propeller.

Production Performance: CATALCAD-BRICSCAD, for its BRICSCAD V18 software combining parametric CAD and direct modelling technology.

Outstanding Achievement: FONDEX, for the creation of parts by sectioning and press stamping, which increases the performance of a car's electronic package.

Collective Success: EDEIS, for the construction of an open-air test bench to develop, validate and qualify engines for next-generation civil aircraft.

Responsible Solution: INOSPRAY, for DITYSPRAY, a propellant-free alternative to aerosols and cartridges using compressed air.

"A 1,000m² complete manufacturing chain (the Connected Factory) in operation was showcased at an exhibition. It provided an overview of progress in automated production, specifically using technologies from the Industry of the Future (cobotics, augmented reality, predictive maintenance, digitisation of production, customisation etc.). The 80 participating companies were coordinated by the engineering firm ADI, providing a concrete illustration of the synergy between the four constituent exhibitions and showcasing almost all the sectors present at the event. With more than 8,000 visitors and engraved medals distributed, the technical challenge was clearly met and surpassed," explained the event organiser.

The event also featured a Hackathon, eight newly created expertise centres (electro technologies, surface treatments, plastics processing, smelting, metal shaping, machining and large-size mechanical engineering) and televised panels (over 200 expert speakers).
