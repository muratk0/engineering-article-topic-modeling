# Don’t miss the WIN Eurasia Metal Working exhibition in Istanbul from 11-14 February 2016

[TARİH: 01.01.2016 ISMR]

This year’s WIN Eurasia Metal Working (Metal Working and Surface Treatment) exhibition will take place from 11-14 February in Istanbul (Turkey). Designed as a platform for the entire sheet metal working and surface treatment technologies’ chain, the 21st edition of the show will be held at the Tuyap Fair Convention and Congress Centre in Istanbul. Organized jointly with Bileşim Fuarcılık A.Ş. since 2002, the exhibition this year is a 100% Deutsche Messe AG brand as all its shares have been acquired.

WIN Eurasia Metal Working will bring together the sheet metal working and surface treatment exhibitions under the same roof. Organized last year for the first time, the Safe@Work special section and the Robotic Forum will also be held this year during the exhibition. At the international pavilion of the Parts2Clean brand, industrial part and surface cleaning technologies will be displayed together.

## Turkey in focus

Turkey is also the fastest emerging market in Europe and the OECD, added the show organiser, pointing out that Turkish GDP grew by 2.2% in 2012, 4% in 2013 and 2.9% in 2014.
