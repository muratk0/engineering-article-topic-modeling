# AN EYE ON INNOVATION

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

EMO Hannover 2017, organised by the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association) on behalf of the European Association of Machine Tool Industries (CECIMO), will be a major highlight of the year for the metalworking sector. From 18-23 September 2017 at Messe Hannover (Germany), it will be an international meeting point for trade visitors interested in production technology. With the motto of "Connecting systems for intelligent production", the focus will be on networking and digitisation.

"So far, around 2,000 exhibitors from 42 countries have registered. That means that, this year, the event is well on the way to topping the record figures achieved by EMO Hannover in 2013. Major exhibitors in 2017 include Mazak; Fanuc; FFG; Grob; Doosan, Makino and Siemens. In 2013, it attracted more than 2,130 exhibitors, and around 143,000 trade visitors from more than 100 different countries," the organiser told ISMR. With approximately 60% of exhibitors from outside Germany, EMO Hannover is a truly international trade fair for metalworking.

EMO Hannover will be showcasing sophisticated metalworking technology, which is the heart of every industrial production process, including the latest machines; efficient technical solutions; product-supportive services; sustainability in the production process and more. Its principal focus is on metal cutting and forming machine tools; production systems; high-precision tools; automated material flows; computer technology; industrial electronics and accessories.
