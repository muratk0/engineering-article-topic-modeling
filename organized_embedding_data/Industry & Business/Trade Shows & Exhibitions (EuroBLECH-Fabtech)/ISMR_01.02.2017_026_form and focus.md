## Don't miss Southern Manufacturing 2017 from 21-23 March 2017 at Farnborough (UK)

Southern Manufacturing and Electronics 2017 returns to FIVE, Farnborough, from 21-23 March 2017. The UK's longest-running annual engineering technology show provides a key opportunity to see and compare the latest hardware from many of the world's top manufacturers.

"Southern Manufacturing is a major event by any measure; around 800 exhibitors gather in an 18,000m² purpose-built venue on the site of the iconic Farnborough Airshow in Hampshire. Now in its 19th year, the show drew over 8,600 visitors in 2016 from all over Europe. With exhibition space for 2017 nearly sold out, we are confident of once again pushing visitor numbers to record highs." Phil Valentine, MD of show organiser, European Trade & Exhibition Services, told ISMR.

## Discover the event

The event itself has three main aspects; Manufacturing, Electronics and a specialist Automotive/Aerospace engineering zone that covers all activities. Further subdivisions group the exhibitors into informal areas covering Machinery, Production & Test, Electronics Assembly and Subcontracting. Technology Trails help to guide visitors who are interested in specific disciplines through the different areas of the show, linking all suppliers with expertise in particular areas such as Aerospace or Medical Technology.

"However, as there are no physical boundaries between any of the areas, visitors are free to discover many new ideas along the way. The sheer diversity of firms taking part virtually guarantees encountering hitherto undiscovered products and services, and is one feature of the event that regular tops visitor polls as its best aspect," added the organiser.

"For many visitors, the impressive Machinery demonstration area provides the highlight of their visit. The size of the event ensures that many top manufacturers view it as one of their main showcases for the UK market and accordingly commit to a significant presence among them Bruderer, Matsuura, Yamazaki Mazak and Haas Automation. Bystronic will showcase its mobile automation module for the Xpert 40 press brake as a compact solution for automated and manual bending."

## Sheet metal in focus

The show is also a key event for machinery and tooling dealers such as XYZ Machine Tools; AMF Andreas Maier; Roemheld; Wilson Tool International; Dormer Pramet; C.Dugard; Rolla-V; Laser Lines; TLM Laser and others. Production and metrology vendors include Faro Technologies; Nikon Metrology; Mitutoyo and Renishaw. Bowers Group will highlight several new products developed in-house, alongside examples from suppliers such as Trimos; Sylvac; Wyler, Innovatest Gagemaker and Accretech.

"Along with the hardware, Southern Manufacturing is a crucial event for the subcontracting industry, providing one of the primary marketplaces for engineering services in the UK. Once again, a wide range of expertise is represented. Leading sheet metal specialists exhibiting include Barlow Sheet Metal; Fife Fabrications; Falcon Precision; Jenks & Cattell Engineering; ICEE Managed Services and Batten & Allen. Other specialist services on show include plating from Twickenham Plating and Corodine, and specialist finishes from Applied Coating Technologies and Fluorocarbon Surface Technologies." explained the organiser.

## GETTING THERE

## By Road

■ From the M3, take junction four and follow the A331 to Farnborough. The show will be clearly signposted on all roads approaching the venue. Parking is free.

## By Rail

■ Farnborough North Camp is the nearest station but Farnborough Main and Aldershot stations are also close. For train timetable information, call National Rail Enquiries on 0845 7484950. A complimentary coach shuttle service will operate from both North Camp and Farnborough Main stations.

## By Air

■ Farnborough Airport is operated by TAG Aviation and, as airport operators, can accommodate arrivals by fixed wing aircraft.

"Visitors can learn from the free technical seminar programme as well as see innovations, new products and live demonstrations. They can also resolve any manufacturing issues during their visit and bring plans and technical drawings for an instant response," said the organiser.

Entrance to the exhibition and technical seminar programme is free, and FIVE Farnborough offers free on-site car parking. There is straightforward access by road from the M3 or by public transport. A free shuttle bus runs between the exhibition and both of Farnborough's railway stations. To register for tickets, see www.industrysouth.co.uk
