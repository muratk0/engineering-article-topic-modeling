# INDIANSHEET METALSUCCESS

[TARİH: 01.07.2011 ISMR]

WITH 137 EXHIBITORS from 16 countries and more than 7,000 trade visitors, mainly from India, the second BLECH India Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working was a huge success. Held from 14-17 April 2011 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai, the exhibition more than doubled in size as well as in exhibitor and visitor numbers compared with the launch show in 2009. BLECH India 2011 showcased a large variety of machines, tool and solutions for the complete sheet metal forming and fabrication process; many live machine demonstrations were held.

India is considered one of the world’s key growth markets for sheet metal working. In particular, the automotive industry and its suppliers as well as the construction industry are a driving force for continuously positive growth figures. As a result of recent trade liberalisation measures, such as India’s trade agreement with the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), the country’s export figures for cars and white goods are significantly growing.

Co-located with this year’s BLECH India, the first Fastener Fair Mumbai took place from 16-17 April 2011. With 90 exhibitors and 4,800 visitors, the trade exhibition for the fastener and fixings industry was a highly successful launch event. BLECH India and Fastener Fair India will return to the Bombay Exhibition Centre in April 2013. BLECH India 2013 will take place from 17-20 April 2013 and Fastener Fair India, Mumbai will be held from 17-18 April 2013. See www.blechindia.com

With BLECH Nordic, Mack Brooks Exhibitions offers a new trade exhibition geared towards the Northern European sheet metal working industry. The first BLECH Nordic, Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working, will be held from 4 - 7 October 2011 at Stockholmsmässan in Stockholm, Sweden. See www.blechevents.com

WATER JET SWEDEN recently announced its largest order received to date from China through wholly-owned subsidiary, WJS Pacific Company Ltd., Beijing, for seven machines; five with cutting tables of 2.5 metres width x 7 metres length, one of 4m x 9m and one 5-axis machine of 4m x 6m. This follows an order placed a year ago for four machines from the same customer.

“We have now established a firm foothold for the future in the potentially massive Chinese market,” said WJS CEO and managing director, Lennart Svensson.

WJS’s recent Open House featured group training sessions in advanced waterjet cutting techniques using the most recent new technologies and software. New applications and operating techniques were demonstrated to increase the speed of production and precision accuracy, while simultaneously reducing operator time and labour costs. A 10-man group from Russia attended and one placed an order for a new machine. A 14-man group from Poland also attended, one of whom also placed an order.

A great attraction was the latest version of the new FINECUT Fine Abrasive Waterjet Machining Centres from Finecut AB, part of the Water Jet Sweden Group. Visitors saw demonstrations of the ultra-high, micro-precision accuracy accomplished in the machining of small and miniature components.

Said Tony Ryd, representing the 54 percent holding and family participation in Water Jet Sweden AB: “2011 so far has seen our best start in any year since 1993, which is very encouraging in view of the recent few years of recession.” For more information, please E-mail: info@water.se

## TRI-COUNTIES SHEET METAL RECOGNIZED FOR EXCELLENCE

THE TRI-COUNTIES Sheet Metal and Air Conditioning Contractors National Association (SMACNA) and Sheet Metal Local 273 Joint Apprentice Training Centre (JATC) in the US was recently presented with gold level accreditation by the International Training Institute (ITI) JATC Accreditation Board.

Tri-Counties SMACNA training facility completed a stringent audit by the ITI and complied with accreditation standards in 13 categories to earn the gold level of accreditation. The California Association of Sheet Metal and Air Conditioning Contractors, National Association (CALSMACNA) is a non-profit trade association representing over 600 union sheet metal and air conditioning contractors who employ more than 25,000 union men and women throughout the state of California.

The gold level accreditation certificate states that Tri-Counties Sheet Metal Local 273 has met the international standards conforming to the prescribed level of performance excellence, integrity and quality training for the unionized Sheet Metal and Air Conditioning Industry. The accreditation is valid until September 2014.

The Sheet Metal Workers Local Union 273 Apprenticeship and Training Programme is a Joint Labour-Management apprenticeship programme offering free training and on-the-job training, allowing apprentices to earn while they learn to become skilled sheet metal workers. For more information, visit www.SMACNA.org

## BEAKBANE INVESTS IN TRUMPF

The TruMatic 6000 features Adaptive Stroke Calibration, which automatically monitors and corrects in real time the depth of forming regardless of material thickness variations

UK-BASED BEAKBANE Ltd, a leading manufacturer of bellows and machinery protection systems, has invested £500,000 in a new TruMatic 6000 high performance CNC punch-laser combination machine from TRUMPF. The large format (3050 x 1550mm) set-up, installed in June 2011, will provide for improved material utilisation at a time when steel prices are at a premium, thereby enabling efficiency gains through a reduction in waste.

“The acquisition of this machine completes a major investment programme during the past four years to improve the company’s metal cutting, punching and folding capabilities,” said Beakbane’s manufacturing manager, Roy Legg.

“If you take the strengths of the punch, in terms of accurately forming material, coupled with the speed of punching holes, and you take a part that has sweeping contours and irregular shapes where smooth edges without burrs is important; and you then put those two types of geometry into one part – that’s the perfect combination machine component,” says Mr Legg.

The TRUMPF TruMatic 6000 is a rugged, all-round machine based on an open C-frame design that is able to process sheet of up to 8mm thick. Intelligent punching, a wear-free ram guidance system and 360° tool rotation are combined with a choice of three lasers (2, 2.7 or 3.2 kW) and simultaneous (XY) cutting speeds of 108m/min.

Mr Legg indicated that cycle times would almost certainly reduce due to fewer handling operations and the ability to complete parts in one hit. Beakbane will be able to tap, countersink, form and bend in a single set-up, thus reducing the non value-added time associated with secondary operations.
