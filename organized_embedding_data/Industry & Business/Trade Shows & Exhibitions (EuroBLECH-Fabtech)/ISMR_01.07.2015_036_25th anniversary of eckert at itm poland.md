# 25th anniversary of Eckert at ITM Poland

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

INNOVATIONS TECHNOLOGIES MACHINES MACH-TOOL in Poznan (Poland) is one of the largest trade fairs in the European calendar. During this year's event, Polish innovator Eckert celebrated its 25th anniversary.

“During the exhibition, we showcased the latest version of our Sapphire (plasma/oxygen cutting machine) equipped with the multiprecision 3D head. This device is the most commonly chosen by European investors. The machine is designed for continuous operation in the toughest conditions and is precise and efficient for heavy duty tasks. We also displayed the Diamond Fiber Laser 2 (laser cutting system) and Opal WaterJet COMBO,” Eckert told ISMR.

Eckert also won this year's Acanthus Aureus prize for the best designed exhibition booth during the event. See www.eckert.com.pl
