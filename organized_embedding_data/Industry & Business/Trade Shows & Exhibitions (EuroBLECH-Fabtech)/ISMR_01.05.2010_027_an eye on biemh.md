# AN EYE ON BIEMH

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

ISMR previews the BIEMH machine tool exhibition which will be held from 31 May – 5 June 2010 in Bilbao, Spain

## T he 26th edition of the BIEMH machine tool exhibition is to be held from 31 May – 5 June 2010 in Bilbao, Spain. Jointly organised by BEC and AFM, it is widely considered to be the third most important exhibition in Europe in its sector. Manufacturers and distributors will meet visitors from around the world to discover the latest developments and technologies from leading companies in the industry.

"Over 30,000 trade specialists have expressed an interest in visiting the event," the organisers told ISMR. "We have prepared the first set of layout drawings for the event, which on paper involves 60,000 m² of exhibition space. In all, 924 firms have so far booked space at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre for BIEMH, the 26th Spanish Machine Tool biennial exhibition."

As usual, the exhibition will be broken down by sectors. This year it will feature twelve areas: cutting machines will occupy the whole of Hall 1, while Hall 2 will be divided up between welding, handling and robotics. Hall 3 will house tools and accessories, automation and hydraulics & pneumatics, and Hall 5 will be home to shaping machines, laser and marking technologies, metrology and CAD/CAM/CAE companies.

## VISITOR CAMPAIGN

The campaign to attract visitors to BIEMH is gathering pace and, to date, more than 30,000 trade specialists from 54 countries have expressed an interest in attending the event.

Representatives of the organisers travelled to India and China at the end of February and are scheduled to visit Mexico, Brazil and Argentina shortly to promote the event through presentations aimed at machinetool-using industries (railways, shipbuilding, domestic appliances, aeronautics, wind power, the automotive industry etc).

"The programme to attract delegations (a major feature of the campaign) is continuing in more than 100 countries all over the world, thanks to the efforts of the trade liaison offices at ICEX (Spanish Institute for Foreign Trade), the network of offices of the Basque Governmentdependent association SPRI (Association for the Promotion and Restructuring of Industry) and the overseas agents of the Bilbao Exhibition Centre itself," added the organisers.

Qualification and training is essential in the machine tool sector, so the exhibition has carefully promoted itself as a reference training area to answer this need. Over 80 vocational training and higher education centres across Spain have already confirmed their participation in the event.

## AN EYE ON EXHIBITORS

Current exhibitors at BIEMH include leading brands such as Fagor Automation, Geka, Goratu Máquinas Herramienta, Danobat Group, Hoffman, Mazak, Motofil, Zayer, Zeiss and Kuka, among others.

"Countries with strong machine tool markets (Italy, Germany, Switzerland...) as well as new/emerging markets with wide potential (Taiwan, China, Czech Republic, India, Russia...) and leading brands annually take part in the BIEMH exhibition," the organizers explained. "The sector-based support provided by associations such as AWEX, GAIA, AER, ASCAD, AEFTOP, Chamber of Commerce of Taiwan, etc. and led by AFM, together with AMT, is crucial to assemble this high-tech international window showcasing innovation and development in all machine tool-related sub-sectors."

In 2008, BIEMH was attended by 50,712 trade visitors and recorded a significant increase in overseas visitors (10.4%) representing 66 countries. A total of 1,761 exhibitors attended the event, 57% of which were based outside Spain, across 788 stands. The 25th biennial event in 2008 brought together leading manufacturers and purchasers from 36 countries in an international showcase event that occupied all six exhibition halls at the venue.

## SHOW PREVIEW

## HOW TO GET TO BEC

The Bilbao Exhibition Centre stands in a privileged location in the main hinterland area of the Atlantic Rim of Europe, the line that runs between Bordeaux in France and Oporto in Portugal along the north coast of Spain. It is in the province of Vizcaya, which belongs to the Autonomous Community of the Basque Country. It is also within easy reach of France, Portugal and the Spanish regions of Aragón, Asturias, Cantabria, Castilla, León, La Rioja and Navarra.

## BILBAO CITY GUIDE

Access to the city has become faster and easier with the opening of the new La Paloma terminal at Sondika airport, which can handle 4 million passengers per year and 20 flights per hour, and the revival of the city's tramways (EuskoTran) that have linked the historical and modern areas of Bilbao along 5km of track since 2002. Special mention must also be given to the city's Metro system, designed by Sir Norman Foster, which has decongested traffic and enabled several areas to be pedestrianised

The new heart of the city is the area comprised by Paseo de Abandoibarra, the Pedro Arrupe footbridge and the Avenida de las Universidades boulevard, connected by a promenade decorated with sculptures by renowned artists including Eduardo Chillida, Ángel Garraza, Salvador Dalí and José Zugasti.

After years of economic uncertainty, Bilbao has recovered its energy as a service-based city regenerating its environment and its urban fabric, hence the city has reshaped itself into an attractive form for visitors. The impressive Guggenheim Museum and the Palacio Euskalduna Concert Hall and Congress Centre are the flagships of Bilbao's new style, and more and more areas continue to be recovered for the enjoyment of the city's people.

## SHOPPING

The economic robustness of the Basque bourgeoisie and the British influence is palpable in Bilbaos shops, which tend to be clustered around a few streets. Casco Viejo has the traditional family-run shops. The bespoke tailors, prestigious jewellers and the big chain stores tend to be located in Ensanche and Indautxu. On both sides of Gran Vía, there are top-end international firms. There are also some lively street markets in some of the most traditional parts of town.

Opening hours for the small shops tend to be 10:00- 14:00h and 17:00-20:00h, although the large chain stores, department stores and shopping centres open continuously from 10:00 to 20:00h

Casco Viejo - the traditional shopping district of Bilbao, today the Casco Viejo can cater to the whole spectrum of shoppers, with alternative boutiques, chain stores, and traditional retailers
Ensanche - it has been called the “golden mille" for the high concentration of international designer shops, although it also has plenty of national and local retailers.
Indautxu - also a good shopping area.
Zubiarte shopping centre - it was designed by Robert Stern, and provides the Bilbao mall experience, with shops, cinemas and
Corte Inglés - one of the best known department stores in the country, it is centrally located on Gran Vía, next to Plaza Circular.

## EATING OUT

Bilbao is well known for its excellent gastronomy, and it is not difficult to eat well in Bilbao as there are plenty of restaurants catering to all tastes and budgets. Pintxos are a must! They are small delicious food portions served on a slice of bread - best enjoyed with some wine, cider or txakoli and can be found in numerous bars around the city. Food markets held in and around Bilbao also provide an excellent opportunity to enjoy the local produce.

In Bilbao, even the average restaurant is of high quality. Fish like cod, seabream and hake – cooked in a variety of ways – is the highlight, together with grilled T-bone ox steak, served in the grill restaurants. Local produce, such as the Gernika peppers and the Ibarra chilies, are classic side dishes.

It is also worth mentioning the porrusalda – a delicious leek soup – and the Gernika and Tolosa red beans, always accompanied by black sausage, chorizo, ribs and tocino.
