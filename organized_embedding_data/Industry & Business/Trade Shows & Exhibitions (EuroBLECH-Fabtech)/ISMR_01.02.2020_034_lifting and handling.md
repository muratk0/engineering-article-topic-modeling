# Lifting and handling

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

EUROTECH WILL BE presenting its tube lifters, lifting devices and handheld grabbers at Fensterbau Frontale 2020 in Nuremberg, Germany, from 18-21 March 2020.

Every second year, visitors attend this trade fair for windows, doors and facades to find out about the latest profile systems; fastening technologies; architectural glass; safety engineering; machines and systems. Eurotech, a specialist in vacuum lifting technologies, will be presenting two new devices from its eT-Lift tube lifter series at the event. It will also be exhibiting its lifting devices from the eT-Hover series and the battery-powered, handheld grabber Nemo.

“Trade show visitors will be able to test the devices at booth 3-208 in hall 3. Our experts will also offer advice for using the vacuum components, handling devices or the complete vacuum solution along with the crane,” Eurotech told ISMR.

Its two new versions of the eT-Lift tube lifting and positioning devices can be used for most applications and are designed to be easy to handle, equipped with an ergonomic one-hand control system. Featuring a quick-swap system, tool change takes just a few steps. The graded carrying capacities range from 20kg up to 250kg in two varieties; a pneumatic 90° turner and a manual 180° turner.

The eT-Hover-univac line, a more compact and lightweight version of the eT-Hover-univac, will also be on show at the trade fair. The lifting device has a two-circuit system and four suction plates arranged in line. It is suitable for lifting and moving large-surface loads made of glass, wood, metal, stone or plastic. In vertical mode, the lifting device can lift loads with a weight of up to 360kg; in horizontal mode, up to 500kg. It is suitable for all applications that involve lifting and moving narrow loads. These loads have dimensions of up to 2,500 x 1,000mm. Multiple security and warning systems, in accordance with DIN EN 13155, are designed to prevent operational errors and hazards.

“The portable, electric vacuum handheld grabber ‘Nemo’, for lifting and transporting flat items, is another product in our portfolio. The handheld device can attach itself to virtually any surface. It achieves a holding force of up to 170kg, allowing it to move and securely place down any material. An integrated vacuum meter with red/ green area is used for monitoring. The vacuum pump installed in the handle generates the necessary vacuum. Once the vacuum is established, the pump turns off. If the vacuum in the suction device drops, the pump automatically switches on again,” added the manufacturer.
