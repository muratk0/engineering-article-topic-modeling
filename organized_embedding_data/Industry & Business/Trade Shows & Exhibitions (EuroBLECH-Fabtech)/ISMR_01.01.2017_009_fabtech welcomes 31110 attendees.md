# FABTECH welcomes 31,110 attendees

[TARİH: 01.01.2017 ISMR]

FABTECH 2016, North America's largest collaboration of technology, equipment and expertise in the metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing industries, welcomed 1,500 exhibiting companies and 31,110 attendees from over 120 countries in November 2016 to the Las Vegas Convention Center.

"FABTECH's exhibit space spanned more than 575,000 net square feet, giving attendees ample opportunity to see manufacturing's latest innovations, interact one-on-one with key vendors and learn about new industry trends. Throughout the three-day event, attendees also had access to more than 100 educational sessions and expert-led panel discussions. A variety of special programming was featured as well, including a powerful opening keynote from boxing legend and entrepreneur, Sugar Ray Leonard," commented show organiser, FMA.

Hundreds of live product demonstrations took place daily on the exhibit floor. Special event and panel discussion topics covered important matters relevant for today's manufacturers. They included additive manufacturing/3D printing, how to attract more women to manufacturing careers, improved product development strategies and how the 2016 presidential election results could impact manufacturing around the world.

For more information about FABTECH 2016 or FABTECH 2017, taking place in Chicago on 6-9 November 2017, see www.fabtechexpo.com/
