## Innovation in focus

"TIMTOS will open at the Taipei World Trade Centre, Exhibition Halls 1 and 3 and Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre, Halls 1 and 2. It will host 1,230 exhibitors from 26 countries (on 7,000 booths), creating a new record high in the show’s history, and making it the world's third largest machine tool trade show," the event organisers told ISMR.

"Most of the major machine tool companies from home and overseas will be participating at the show. Exhibitors from the U.S.A., Denmark and Turkey will participate in country pavilions for the first time, alongside longstanding Swiss and German pavilions. TIMTOS is one of the most important trade shows to showcase the latest products and technologies."

TIMTOS in 2019 will focus on "Industry 4.0 & Smart Manufacturing" and "Artificial Technologies". Visitors will be able to see various digitalised solutions which integrate machine tools, industrial robots, sensors, IoT, big data analysis, machine learning and AI technologies into factory operations to help manufacturers achieve "human-machine collaboration" and make automated production lines more efficient and resilient.

To show visitors the latest industry trends, the show organisers will hold the second TIMTOS Summit and invite industry experts and top executives from firms such as ABB, Dassault and Siemens to provide insight and market trends on topics such as "Machine Intelligence and AIoT", "Additive Manufacturing", "Automotive Manufacturing Revolution" and "Innovation in Aerospace Manufacturing".

More smart machines incorporated with industrial robots, IoT, big data analysis, remote control and cloud computing can be expected at TIMTOS 2019.

## Taiwan's machine tool industry

Gardner Research has published its rankings for the top machine tool producers, importers and exporters in 2017 by country, in its latest issue. With rising demand from China for midrange products and automated manufacturing cells, Taiwan's annual output value reached US$ 4.29 billion, ranked seventh in the world, and annual export value reached US$ 3.35 billion, once again ranked the fourth largest exporter in the world.

Consumption of machine tools in Taiwan reached US$ 1.78 billion in 2017, ranked ninth in the world, while annual import value was US$ 840 million.

"Due to strong growth in North American and Chinese markets, the output value of Taiwan’s machine tool industry in Q1 2018 was US$ 1.05 billion and the export value was US$ 0.83 billion, up 7.6% and 20.3% year-on-year, respectively. The machine tool industry in Taiwan is export-oriented, with exports accounting for 80% of the output. The main export countries are China (accounting for 33.2% of total exports), the U.S. (accounting for 11%), emerging countries in Asia and some parts of Europe," said the analyst.

The Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA) was founded in 1970 to help promote foreign trade and is a key nonprofit trade promotion organisation in Taiwan. Jointly sponsored by the government, industry associations and several commercial organisations, TAITRA assists Taiwanese businesses and manufacturers to expand their global reach. The organisation has a staff of 1,300 specialists and operates five local offices in Taoyuan, Hsinchu, Taichung, Tainan and Kaohsiung, as well as 60 branches worldwide.

Together with the Taipei World Trade Centre (TWTC) and Taiwan Trade Centre (TTC), TAITRA has formed a global network dedicated to promoting world trade.
