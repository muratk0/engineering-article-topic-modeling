# Back to the future

[TARİH: 01.02.2022 ISMR]

Southern Manufacturing & Electronics will be returning to Farnborough (UK) from 8-10 February 2022. It is a comprehensive annual industrial exhibition in the UK and a major pan-European showcase for new technology in machinery, production equipment, electronic production and assembly, tooling and components as well as subcontract services.

The event will be held at Farnborough International Exhibition and Conference Centre, a new £35m 20,000m² venue offering modern facilities, easy access and free onsite parking. Co-located with Southern Manufacturing & Electronics, AutoAero is a highly specialised event focused on suppliers to the UK's aerospace and automotive engineering sectors. International Sheet Metal Review magazine is a media supporter of the event and will distribute its February issue throughout the show.

"The show's eagerly-awaited revival comes as engineering firms across the UK are getting back to business and are keen to reinvigorate their order books. Many of the show's regular participants have weathered the turbulent conditions of the last 20 months and are now eager to pitch their stalls. As in previous years, the show features an incredible array of hightech production, mechanical and electronics' components; tooling; industrial hardware; business and subcontract services from all over the UK, continental Europe and beyond," said the show organiser, European Trade & Exhibition Services (ETES).

ETES expects the show to be fully booked by the time it opens and is confident of 'coming near 2020's pre-COVID, recordbreaking attendance of roughly 9,000 people'.

With its wide range of exhibitors, Southern Manufacturing aims to provide answers to 'a variety of procurement challenges in one visit' – perhaps even more important given the current difficulties of travel and engagement in face-to-face business.

"Wide-ranging anti-infection precautions, including fresh air ventilation throughout the building, help to make Farnborough International Exhibition a comparatively low-risk environment in which to conduct face-to-face business," ETES told ISMR.

In line with government guidelines and on event industry recommendations, COVID-19 status for exhibitors, visitors, contractors and event staff will be validated on arrival at the event.

"All attendees are kindly asked to respect the space of others, particularly in crowded areas. From 10 December 2021, face coverings must be worn in indoor settings. Spare masks will be available from us. Shields will be installed in busy areas involving frequent face-to-face contact. Attendant staff will be provided with face visors/face coverings. Dedicated hand hygiene stations will be located within all high traffic areas throughout the hall. Farnborough International will also ensure a rigorous cleaning regime of all public areas throughout the entire event," confirmed the show organiser.

Alongside the trade show and demonstration areas, the free seminar programme will return for 2022 in a socially-distanced format. Subject areas include reducing energy consumption for manufacturers; new technology; innovation; supply chain and business strategy along with an evaluation of current industrial and post-Brexit regulations.■
