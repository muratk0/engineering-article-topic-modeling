# Enter the Machine Tools Innovation Awards

[TARİH: 01.06.2024 ISMR]

The Machine Tools Innovation Awards (MTI Awards) 2024 edition from CECIMO is open for entries until 2 September 2024. It recognises ground-breaking projects that have a positive impact on the machine-tool ecosystem.

“The MTI Awards recognise and celebrate outstanding contributions to the machine-tool industry. Our goal is to spotlight the remarkable achievements of industry players and showcase best practices that drive productivity, growth and profitability," said CECIMO (European Association for Manufacturing Technologies).

Winners will be announced at the CECIMO Brussels Forum in Brussels on 2 December 2024. This event is officially supported by EIT Manufacturing; European Factories of the Future Research Association (EFFRA); ERT — European Round Table for Industry and EMO Hannover 2025.

“The MTI Awards programme is open to all European players (EU + UK + EFTA + Turkey) of all sizes in the machine tool industry that drive the sector forward, highlighting achievements in areas such as sustainability, digitalisation and overall innovation in manufacturing," commented CECIMO.

Winners will not only receive recognition for their contributions but will also gain industry visibility through access to EMO Hannover 2025, along with a network of top industrialists in the sector.

Entry is free and manufacturers can apply on www.cecimo.eu/mti-awardsapplication/
