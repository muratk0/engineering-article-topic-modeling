## INDUSTRIE LYON 2015 show from 7-10 April in France

ISMR SAYS:

“Around 850 exhibitors and 22,000 visitors are expected to attend this year’s Industrie Lyon event”

Around 850 exhibitors at this year’s Industrie Lyon trade fair, at Eurexpo Lyon in France from 7-10 April, will showcase a wide range of equipment, consumables and services for production and subcontracting. With its complimentary sectors focused on competitiveness and industrial performance, Industrie Lyon will take place over 50,000 square metres of exhibition space in Lyon and around 20,000 visitors are expected to attend.

“All our exhibitors will introduce innovative solutions designed for the end-user sectors of industrial fabrication, during the four days of the event,” the organiser told ISMR.

Nine sectors will cover industrial equipment and requirements for assembly handling/forming; cutting-sheet metal working/industrial IT; machine tools; robotics; surface finishing and heat treatment; tools; vision-control-quality assurance and welding. One sector will promote expertise in sub-contracting and a new addition to the event is the Additive Manufacturing village where visitors can discover 3D printing innovations and technologies. The Industrie Academy is a training village that supports young people to find careers, jobs and professional training opportunities in manufacturing and the industrial world.

“Industrie Lyon showcases the equipment, products, consumables and services that are necessary for any industrial production factory. It unveils the innovations that allow SMEs, agencies and large groups to source the tools, expertise and solutions to develop their performance and their competitiveness,” the organiser explained.

Located each even-numbered year in Paris, and each odd-numbered year in Lyon, the event takes place over four days. Lyon and the Rhône-Alpes region in France is a primary subcontracting area in France with 30,500 local companies, amounting to 40 per cent of the national total. It ranks in the top five trade fairs in Europe. ■
