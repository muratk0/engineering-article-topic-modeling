# UK Metals Expo 2024 profiles supply chain

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

UK Metals Expo took place on 11-12 September 2024 at the NEC exhibition centre in Birmingham, UK.

“The event wrapped up with resounding success, surpassing all expectations. The overwhelmingly positive feedback has laid a strong foundation for an even larger show next year. It attracted 300 exhibitors and over 7,000 professionals from 51 countries. Many attendees secured new partnerships and contracts. It also fostered valuable connections across the entire metals supply chain and saw a 51% increase in attendance with a satisfaction score of 8.3 out of 10," announced the show organiser.

“The diversity of industries represented— from aerospace and automotive to construction and renewable energy—demonstrates the Expo's central role in connecting the entire metals supply chain. This year's exhibitors showcased modern solutions tailored to a wide variety of sectors," it added.

The 2025 Expo will include new features such as custom workshops, live demonstrations and a greater focus on emerging technologies such as AI and automation. UK Metals Expo 2025 will return to the NEC, Birmingham (UK) on 10-11 September 2025.
