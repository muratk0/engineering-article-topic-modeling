Press Brake Training Opportunities

FMA offers individuals and companies the ability to gain knowledge and hone skills through its many professional development programs. From on-demand e-learning courses to custom training in your shop, training is available in a variety of formats to suit the different needs of adult learners. As the leading education resource for the forming and fabricating industry, FMA is also your source for technical seminars on the essential sheet metal manufacturing process of bending.

FMA’s Precision Press Brake Certificate Course gives you or your employees the opportunity to earn an industry-recognized credential with in-depth classroom instruction. The instructor is Steve Benson, a nationally known expert and author of

The Art of Press Brake

. This two-day seminar is dedicated to mastering the fundamentals of precision sheet metal bending. Metal fabricators with six months’ experience or more will learn the theory, principles, and math fundamentals behind the machine, applying what they learn to a final exam at the course’s conclusion. FMA’s Precision Press Brake Certificate Course is being offered this October in Atlanta and Greensboro, N.C.

New this September, FMA and Harper College have partnered to offer a one-day Bending With Press Brakes hands-on workshop. Novice fabricators will have the opportunity to gain basic skills through classroom instruction and hands-on exercises. Given individualized attention and expert coaching, attendees will follow the steps from flat layout to finished part in Harper’s manufacturing lab. (This course also is offered in Spanish.) This workshop will be held in Palatine, Ill.
