# MTA VIETNAM returns to Ho Chi Minh City

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

THE MAIN international trade event for Vietnam’s manufacturing industry will be back once again at the Saigon Exhibition & Convention Centre (SECC). MTA Vietnam 2012 will showcase the latest technologies, products and solutions in precision engineering, machine tools and metalworking from 3-6 July 2012.

MTA Vietnam 2012 will incorporate Metrology Vietnam 2012, ToolTec Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam International Exhibition of Tools and Tooling Technology), SubCon Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam Contract Manufacturing and Subcontracting Exhibition), Automation Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam International Industrial Automation Exhibition) and WeldTech Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam International Welding, Surface Treatment & Joining Technology Exhibition.)

The exhibition is organised by Singapore Exhibition Services Pte Ltd (SES) and local organiser VCCI Exhibition Service Co. Ltd (VCCI). www.mtavietnam.com

## Bang & Olufsen collaborate with AP&T

AP&T HAS received a new order from Bang & Olufsen in Denmark. One press and over 50 tools were delivered in 2011 and an additional press is on the way. Equipment from AP&T is being used to produce aluminium speaker grills for BMW and Mercedes, amongst others.

“It is naturally extremely gratifying to cooperate with one of the world’s most recognized brands. Business has developed quickly ever since we delivered the first press. In just a short space of time, we have produced over 50 different tools for advanced forming of speaker grills for various makes of cars and, in March 2012, we installed another press at B&O’s factory in Struer on Jylland,” said Per Josefsson, Global Sales and Service Manager at AP&T.

“AP&T was the supplier best able to satisfy our demands for presses and tools. For example, accessibility is very important to us. We run short series and need to change tools five or six times a day – and it has to be done quickly and easily,” added Carsten Jensen, Purchasing Manager at B&O. ■

## US finished steel imports rise

BASED ON preliminary Census Bureau data, the American Iron and Steel Institute (AISI) has reported that the US imported a total of 2,546,000 net tons (NT) of steel in January 2012, including 2,060,000 NT of finished steel (up 23% and 31%, respectively, from December final data). Annualised total and finished steel imports in 2012 would be 30.6 and 24.7 million NT, up 7% and 13%, respectively, vs. 2011.

Finished steel import market share in January was an estimated 23%, up 1 percentage point from full-year 2011 and up 4 percentage points from December. Key finished steel products with significant import increases in January 2012 compared to December are line pipe (up 60%), cold rolled sheets (up 59%), reinforcing bars (up 58%), oil country goods (up 46%), cut length plates (up 42%) and standard pipe (up 42%).

In January vs. the month before, the largest volumes of finished steel imports from offshore were from South Korea (314,000, up 55%), Turkey (142,000 NT, up 71%), Japan (128,000 up 0.2%), Germany (107,000 NT, up 25%) and China (100,000, up 15%). ■

## Alcoa and CPI finalise JV agreement

ALCOA AND China Power Investment Corporation (CPI) have finalised an agreement to establish a joint venture company to produce high-end fabricated aluminium products for the Chinese market. Alcoa Chairman and CEO Klaus Kleinfeld and CPI President Lu Qizhou signed the agreement in Beijing at the Great Hall of the People. Also attending the signing ceremony was US ambassador to China, Gary Locke. The new joint venture company, Alcoa CPI (China) Aluminum Investment Co. Ltd, will be majority-owned and managed by Alcoa and based in Shanghai. The announcement is the outcome of a Letter of Intent between the two companies signed in September 2011 to form the joint venture company.

“This is a significant milestone in the partnership we have forged with CPI leveraging our combined strengths to capture opportunities in the high-growth Chinese aluminium market,” said Alcoa Chairman and CEO, Klaus Kleinfeld. “This alliance will enhance Alcoa’s competitiveness and further position our businesses for continued profitable growth.”

The joint venture company will serve as a leading manufacturer of high-end fabricated aluminium products for the aerospace, automotive, commercial transportation, consumer electronics and packaging markets in China. www.cpicorp.com.cn

## Hot-rolled blanks

THE PROCESSING of blanks of laser quality with plasticity degrees of up to 80% inside the material to be levelled and high relief of stresses for further processes were some of the challenges to be mastered by Heinrich Georg GmbH Maschinenfabrik for Tata Steel Service Centre UK. Together with its daughter company, Heinrich Georg UK Ltd, GEORG finishing lines developed a new high performance leveller for hot-rolled steel blanks with an extremely rigid machine frame for the required high plasticity degrees. The maximum bending of the entire machine frame under its highest load is 0.2mm. GEORG attempted to ensure optimum levelling values for laser quality for sustainable blank production.

“Unfortunately we ran into problems with our previous leveller. Due to the remaining residual stresses, the blanks could not be used for the subsequent laserwelding process. Deliveries were rejected by our customers. With the new leveller made by GEORG, we could overcome such problems and increase the quality considerably,” explained Mark Davenport, General Manager of Tata Steel Service Centre UK.

Thanks to intelligent GEORG technology, steel strips with thicknesses of between 3 and 6mm and tensile strengths of up to 600 N/mm2 and strip widths of 1.000 – 2.000mm can be precisely cut. A levelling grade of laser quality has been achieved so that the blanks are almost free from stresses. ■
