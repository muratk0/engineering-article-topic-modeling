# What’s new for Subcon 2014?

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

THE Subcon exhibition, from 3-5 June 2014 at the NEC in the UK, boasts several new features including the Subcon Electronics Zone as well as more free sessions over three days at The Engineer Conference.

A survey, carried out by the Subcon exhibition, showed that 47.3 per cent of visitors to the Subcon 2013 show planned to increase the amount of work they placed with UK subcontractors in the next 12 months. The strong tendency was to place more work with local suppliers. When the question was opened up to cover placing work with all subcontractors, regardless of location, this figure only increased to 47.8 per cent. Subcon visitors were most positive about the outlook for their own companies, with 68 per cent expecting business to improve in the next 12 months.

“Over 300 subcontractors in machining; sheet metal; materials; design and prototyping; casting and forging; testing and inspection; plastic mouldings; metrology; software solutions; electronics and more will be at the event. The Engineer Conference will offer a mix of inspiring keynote speakers, industry and supply chain insights as well as ‘Show and Tell’ sessions,” the organiser told ISMR.

In 2015, Subcon will run alongside The Advanced Manufacturing Show, the biennial event for machine tools, tooling and metrology equipment and a new annual event, The Engineer Design and Innovation show, that will focus on the tools, service and products that are essential to bring a product to market. The three shows will take place on 2-4 June 2015 at the NEC in Birmingham. See www.subconshow.co.uk ■
