# A FIRM GRIP

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

ISMR previews the Fastener Fair Stuttgart exhibition which will take place in Germany from 26-28 February 2013

Fastener Fair Stuttgart is Europe’s leading exhibition for the fastener and fixing industry. From 26th – 28th February 2013, Fastener Fair Stuttgart will once again open its doors in Stuttgart, Germany.

Representatives from the entire fastener and fixing industry meet at Fastener Fair Stuttgart: distributors, suppliers, component buyers and other industry experts come every two years from all parts of the world to be part of the industry event. In 2011, Fastener Fair Stuttgart established itself as a key international meeting place for the industry with 667 exhibitors and 8300 trade visitors from 86 countries.

Fasteners are everywhere: without them our modern world would literally fall apart. From relatively simple DIY screws to the most sophisticated complex joining technologies, fastener and fixing technologies provide some of the most diverse and varied industry solutions worldwide. The increasing globalisation of markets and worldwide availability of high-quality industry-leading brands mean that the fastener and fixing industry is facing new challenges.

Continuous development of new products and technologies with improved precision and productivity are as important as location, expertise and an international network.

## The fastener supply chain

Major exhibitor countries at Fastener Fair Stuttgart include Germany, Italy, the United Kingdom and the Netherlands. Fastener Fair Stuttgart 2013 will once again occupy halls four, six and eight at the new Stuttgart Trade Fair Centre. The three halls offer a total exhibition space of 15,500m² net.

The exhibition represents the entire supply chain of the fastener and fixing industry. The show profile includes industrial fastener and fixings, construction fixings, assembly and installation systems and fastener manufacturing technology. Storage, distribution, factory equipment and information services round off the exhibition profile.

The Stuttgart edition of Fastener Fair mainly focuses on a traditional trade and manufacturing audience; the show predominantly targets distributors, wholesalers and suppliers.

The venue is conveniently located next to Stuttgart Airport and is 13 kilometres away from Stuttgart city centre. The airport terminals are approximately 200 metres from the venue and can easily be reached on foot. There, suburban trains (S-Bahn) S2 and S3 can also be taken to Stuttgart main railway station and take approximately 27 minutes of travelling time.

## EVENT PREVIEW

The Fastener Fair Stuttgart exhibition represents the entire supply chain of the fastener and fixing industry

The previous Fastener Fair Stuttgart, held in February 2011, was the largest Fastener Fair ever. It covered an exhibition space of 12,500m² net. Some 8,300 industry professionals, an increase of 38 per cent compared with the previous exhibition, visited the show.

Related events next year include Fastener Fair Russia (12-14 March 2013), Fastener Fair India (17-18 April 2013) and Fastener Fair China (14-16 May 2013). Later in the year, Fastener Fair Thailand will be held in Bangkok from 9-11 October 2013.

For more information on these events, see info@fastenerfair.com
