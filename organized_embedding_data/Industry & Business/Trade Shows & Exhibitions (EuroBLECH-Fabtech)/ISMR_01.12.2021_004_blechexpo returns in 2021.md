# Blechexpo returns in 2021

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

Together with the 8th Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology, the 15th Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal processing will be held in Stuttgart, Germany, from 26-29 October 2021.

“Exhibitors and expert visitors will be able to see the latest technologies in sheet metal processing and metalworking, in combination with joining technology, at an international level,” the organiser, P. E. SCHALL GmbH & Co. KG, told ISMR.

Once every two years, the Blechexpo/ Schweisstec trade fair duo presents standalone and system solutions for the process sequences associated with the fabrication of parts made from sheet metal, profiles and pipe. In 2019, exhibitors from 36 countries and visitors from 113 nations attended the event.

“Our project team is planning booth enlargements for all exhibition halls. Digitalisation, sustainability, process efficiency and future-proof solutions in a rapidly changing production environment are amongst the dominant driving issues in the sheet metal processing industries – which was already evident in 2019. Advanced mechanical joining and fastening technologies are also of great interest because new plain and hybrid materials are finding their way into the production environment, for example within the context of lightweight design. Modern cutting technologies, such as high-pressure waterjet cutting, are also attracting attention again,” added the show organiser.

Exhibitors in grinding technology can be found in hall 5, where a centre for grinding technology will be set up. The grinding technology segment will be colour-coded in the digital hall floor plans. As sheet metal process steps are being networked and automated to an even greater extent, a consistently digitised setting for sheet metal processing and metalworking will also be one of the core issues at Blechexpo/Schweisstec in 2021.
