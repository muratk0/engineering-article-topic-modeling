# Don't miss Southern Manufacturing & Electronics

[TARİH: 01.01.2024 ISMR]

Registration is now open for the Southern Manufacturing & Electronics exhibition 2024 (6-8 February 2024) which is now an international event. Last year's event attracted 9,144 visitors and 460 exhibitors (representing 600 companies).

"With a record-breaking number of enquiries and interest from overseas companies, the event is set to shine. In early February 2024, this prestigious showcase of industrial innovation will capture global attention at the Farnborough International Exhibition & Conference Centre in the UK. This new 20,000m² venue offers modern facilities, easy access and free onsite parking," the show organiser, European Trade & Exhibition Services (ETES), told ISMR.

The comprehensive annual industrial show is a major platform for new technology in production and inspection equipment, components and subcontract services across a wide spectrum of industries.

Professionals engaged in design, manufacturing and procurement can explore a wide array of equipment including machine tools; tooling; workholding; production hardware; automation and robotics; test and measurement equipment; quality control solutions; engineering software; materials supply and other diverse technologies.

In addition, the exhibition showcases a range of more general products and services including oil and coolant supply; workshop equipment; dust and fume extraction; humidity control; workplace storage systems; bespoke case and foam manufacture; injection moulding; hand tools; adhesives; waste removal and recycling; training; freight services and financial consultancy.

The event also includes a free technical seminar programme over the three days. Presentations will include advice for small manufacturers looking to improve their relationship with customers; opportunities for those seeking financial support and tips on how to enhance a sales team's performance. CE and UKCA marking compliance; sustainable practices in manufacturing; the importance of brand awareness and creative campaigns; management skills coaching and digital process management will all come under the spotlight.■
