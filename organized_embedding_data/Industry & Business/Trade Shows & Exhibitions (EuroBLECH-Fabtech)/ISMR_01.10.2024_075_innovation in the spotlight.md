# Innovation in the spotlight

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

When EuroBLECH, the industry's flagship trade fair, opens its doors in Hanover, Germany, from 22-25 October 2024, AMADA will present and demonstrate its wide portfolio of solutions on booths D06/ F06 and D02/F02 in Hall 12. These include laser cutting, stamping, bending, welding and automation technologies. It will also focus on sustainability issues.

"At EuroBLECH, we will be explaining how our European service structure is to be redesigned to further enhance its high quality. Visitors will have access to first-hand information about the benefits and synergies that will result, with immediate effect, from our new Welding Technology Centre in Italy," AMADA told ISMR.

The AMADA Group is one of the world's leading manufacturers of sheet metal working machines. It offers a range of cutting, bending, punching and laser technologies, complemented by modular automation components, software applications and a wide range of tools and services. The AMADA Group was founded in 1946 in Japan by Isamu Amada.

## Laser cutting and punching

New versions of AMADA's laser range will be on display at EuroBLECH 2024, incorporating AMADA's original beam control technologies and Laser Integration System functions for higher autonomy and profitability.

"Fiber Silky Cut for very high-quality stainless-steel processing and the ultra-highspeed, small-hole processing LBC Flash Cut feature will be combined with new developments for reduced running costs. Simple operation and higher productivity will be enhanced by various automation systems, including storage towers and part picking modules," said AMADA.

The EM ZRTe punching machine will also be exhibited, with capacity for 300 tools, linked to an intelligent tool grinding system to extend tool life.

## New line of press brakes

Building on the success of its EG bending machine, AMADA has developed a new series of servo-electronic press brakes. These range from ergonomic and stand-alone benders, available with or without automatic tool changer, to robotic models. This new range of machines complements the existing HRB hydraulic series.

## Focus on welding

The AMADA Group will also present "everything from sheet metal part marking and welding to resistance welding and weld monitoring" at EuroBLECH.

"The new mid-range FLW3000Le robotic fibre laser welding cell will feature a new set-up function for increased throughput. The flexible, expandable Jupiter system can be utilised for resistance and fibre laser micro welding. A revolutionary weld monitoring system, which can be integrated into a production line, will be on display as well as a desktop, high-speed fibre laser marking system," confirmed AMADA.

"We will also announce the opening of Europe's first Fibre Laser Welding Centre, in Piacenza (near Milan), Italy. This marks a significant step for the European welding industry and underlines our ongoing commitment to innovation, quality and sustainability," it added.

## Managing the flow

AMADA will also promote the European market debut of its software solution, LIVLOTS, at EuroBLECH.

“At the core of LIVLOTS is its ability to seamlessly integrate and manage the entire production workflow, from order intake to final product delivery. This comprehensive digitalisation streamlines operations, eliminates inefficiencies and optimises resource allocation, paving the way for unprecedented productivity gains and cost savings," commented AMADA.

## An eye on customer service

"Building on our customer service and support across Europe, we will present solutions aimed at ensuring all customers receive the same high level of support whatever their location. Enhanced solutions, such as our webshop, will make it easy to place orders 24/7. New Proset tooling packages, IoT proactive machine management and improved remote support options, including automatic alarm response, will enhance support for our customers," concluded AMADA.■
