# MTA VIETNAM returns to Ho Chi Minh City

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

THE MAIN international trade event for Vietnam’s manufacturing industry will be back once again at the Saigon Exhibition & Convention Centre (SECC). MTA Vietnam 2012 will showcase the latest technologies, products and solutions in precision engineering, machine tools and metalworking from 3-6 July 2012.

MTA Vietnam 2012 will incorporate Metrology Vietnam 2012, ToolTec Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam International Exhibition of Tools and Tooling Technology), SubCon Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam Contract Manufacturing and Subcontracting Exhibition), Automation Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam International Industrial Automation Exhibition) and WeldTech Vietnam 2012 (Vietnam International Welding, Surface Treatment & Joining Technology Exhibition.)

The exhibition is organised by Singapore Exhibition Services Pte Ltd (SES) and local organiser VCCI Exhibition Service Co. Ltd (VCCI). www.mtavientnam.com

## Bang & Olufsen collaborate with AP&T

AP&T HAS received a new order from Bang & Olufsen in Denmark. One press and over 50 tools were delivered in 2011 and an additional press is on the way. Equipment from AP&T is being used to produce aluminium speaker grills for BMW and Mercedes, amongst others.

“It is naturally extremely gratifying to cooperate with one of the world’s most recognized brands. Business has developed quickly ever since we delivered the first press. In just a short space of time, we have produced over 50 different tools for advanced forming of speaker grills for various makes of cars and, in March 2012, we installed another press at B&O’s factory in Struer on Jylland,” said Per Josefsson, Global Sales and Service Manager at AP&T.

## US finished steel imports rise

BASED ON preliminary Census Bureau data, the American Iron and Steel Institute (AISI) has reported that the US imported a total of 2,546,000 net tons (NT) of steel in January 2012, including 2,060,000 NT of finished steel (up 23% and 31%, respectively, from December final data). Annualised total and finished steel imports in 2012 would be 30.6 and 24.7 million NT, up 7% and 13%, respectively, vs. 2011.

Finished steel import market share in January was an estimated 23%, up 1 percentage point from full-year 2011 and up 4 percentage points from December. Key finished steel products with significant import increases in January 2012 compared to December are line pipe (up 60%), cold rolled sheets (up 59%), reinforcing bars (up 58%), oil country goods (up 46%), cut length plates (up 42%) and standard pipe (up 42%).

In January vs. the month before, the largest volumes of finished steel imports from offshore were from South Korea (314,000, up 55%), Turkey (142,000 NT, up 71%), Japan (128,000 up 0.2%), Germany (107,000 NT, up 25%) and China (100,000, up 15%). ■
