# MACH attack

[TARİH: 01.06.2014 ISMR]

THE MACH 2014 exhibition in April reaffirmed MACH’s position as Britain’s biggest manufacturing technologies event. Over 23,000 visitors attended and a record 612 exhibitors took space in Halls 4 and 5 of the NEC Birmingham.

The exhibition had a roaring launch from former world land speed record holder, Richard Noble OBE, who then went on to tour the exhibition and gave an exciting presentation on progress with his Bloodhound Supersonic Car project. Iain Wright MP, the UK Shadow Minister for Manufacturing, and UK Business Secretary, Dr Vince Cable MP, also toured the show. Dr Cable held a round table with business leaders to discuss investment, skills, Europe and the problems experienced by SMEs trying to fund growth projects. He concluded his visit by delivering a speech to exhibitors, visitors and the media in a packed Seminar Theatre. ■
