## EVENT PREVIEW

Barbara Colombo, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, added: "2021 was extremely positive for the Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots and automation systems. Only the lack of some electrical and electronic components and of raw materials hindered full recovery of the loss registered at the end of 2020, in conjunction with the outbreak of the pandemic."

"While experiencing slower recovery of business in overseas markets, owing to the restrictions on people's mobility, there is a great liveliness of Italian demand supported, at least partially, by the Italian government incentives for new investments in production technology."

## Getting there

By Air: LINATE airport (bus no. 73, Duomo M1-M3 terminus and Metro Red line 1: Rho-Fieramilano stop).

MALPENSA airport (Malpensa Express Train to Milano Centrale station – Milano Porta Garibaldi stop, then Metro Lilac line 5 towards San Siro Stadium, Lotto stop and interchange with Metro Red line 1 for Rho-Fieramilano. Alternatively, from Milano Porta Garibaldi S-Line trains S5, S6 or S11 to Rho-Fieramilano. The Malpensa Express Train can also be taken to Milano Cadorna station, then Metro Red line 1 towards Rho Fieramilano.

ORIO AL SERIO airport (a free shuttle bus service is available from/to Milano Centrale).

By Car: From the A7-Genova, A1-Bologna and A4-Torino motorways: A50 Tangenziale Ovest towards Varese, Fieramilano exit. From the A4-Venezia motorway: Pero-Fieramilano exit. From the A8-Varese and A9-Como motorways: From the Milano Nord barrier towards the A4-Venezia and Fieramilano exit.

By Train: Choice of train services. During the days of the event, the direct stop at the Rho-Fiera railway station, just near the exhibition centre, will be open. From the stations of Milano Centrale, Milano Porta Garibaldi and Milano Lambrate: Take Metro Green Line 2 towards Abbiategrasso-Assago; at the Cadorna stop, take the Metro Red line 1 towards Rho-Fieramilano. From Milano Cadorna station: Take Metro Red line 1, direction and stop Rho-Fieramilano.

Other: Metro Red line 1 to Rho-Fieramilano stop. Visitors need a Mi1-Mi3 fare range ticket which costs two euros per trip. 
A choice of taxis is also available.
