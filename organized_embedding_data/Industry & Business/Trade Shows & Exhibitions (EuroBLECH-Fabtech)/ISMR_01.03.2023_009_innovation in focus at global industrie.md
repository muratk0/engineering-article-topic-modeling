# Innovation in focus at Global Industrie

[TARİH: 01.03.2023 ISMR]

Global Industrie will once again take place at Eurexpo in Lyon (France), from 7-10 March 2023, as a global showcase for industrial innovation with more new developments and solutions. The event is under the patronage of the French President, Emmanuel Macron, and plays host to several ministers, elected officials, ambassadors and representatives of the largest European professional organisations in the world of industry.

"Global Industrie brings together all industry players in one place. Divided into 26 zones, it specialises in fields such as digitalisation and automation; electronics; additive manufacturing & 3D printing; measurement; robotics; energy and sustainable development (to name a few)," the show organiser commented.

"Global Industrie will be honoured to host not only the Chambers of Commerce and Industry of Bourgogne Franche-Comté and Seine-et-Marne and CCI France Internationale (Union of French CCls abroad) but also the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Pavilion; Aveiro Regional Industrial Association (Portugal) and AIMMAP (Associação Dos Industriais Metalúrgicos Metalomecânicos E Afins De Portugal)," it added.

It incorporates five complementary exhibitions: MIDEST (industrial subcontracting); SMART INDUSTRIES (smart, efficient and digital industry); INDUSTRIE (manufacturing technologies and equipment); TOLEXPO (solutions and equipment for sheet metalworking) and MEASUREMENTWORLD. The Global Industrie awards honour new exhibitor innovations.

This year, Global Industrie expects to welcome 2300 exhibitors and an expected 40,000 visitors from 85 countries. Previous editions of the show have been held in even years in Paris and odd years in Lyon, France. This geographical diversification enables it to enhance both its offer and the variety of its visitors.■
