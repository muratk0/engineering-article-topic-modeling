# EuroBLECH 2012 on track

[TARİH: 01.06.2012 ISMR]

THE 22ND International Sheet MetalWorking Technology Exhibition, EuroBLECH 2012, will take place from 23 – 27 October 2012 in Hanover, Germany. The event is a leading trade exhibition for this industry sector. The current booking status of this year’s show reflects a significant stabilisation of the sheet metal working industry, the organiser told ISMR. A total of 1,340 exhibitors from 37 countries have already reserved their stand space, covering a net exhibition space of 84,000 square metres (a floor space increase of more than 6% compared with the previous exhibition). Almost 48% of the companies that have already booked their stands come from outside Germany.

“This reflects a further recovery of the sheet metal working industry and a thoroughly positive outlook for this year’s EuroBLECH. The trend, which had already become apparent during the previous exhibition, has now been confirmed: the industry sector is investing again,” explained Nicola Hamann, Exhibition Director, EuroBLECH on behalf of the organisers, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

Visitors to EuroBLECH 2012 can look forward to a large number of machine demonstrations throughout the eight exhibition halls. Comprehensive information for visitors planning their trip to EuroBLECH 2012 is now available. The visitor brochure is available in twelve languages and can be ordered via the show website.

EuroBLECH 2012 will again occupy halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 27 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds. The exhibition profile represents the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal, semi-finished and finished products, handling, separation, forming, flexible sheet metal working, joining, welding and surface treatment, processing of hybrid structures, tools, quality control, CAD/CAM systems and R&D. A total of 61,500 international trade visitors attended the previous show.

BLECH Russia 2013 will take place from 12-14 March 2013 in St Petersburg. BLECH India will be held in Mumbai from 17-20 April 2013. See www.euroblech.com

## JCB award for Tata Steel

CONFIRMING ITS position as one of the leading suppliers to the off-road construction equipment industry, Tata Steel has received a Supplier Performance Award 2012 at JCB’s Supplier Conference in Rochester, UK.

The annual conference was attended by over 240 of JCB’s key suppliers and recognised those that particularly excelled during the year. The Supplier Performance Awards assess five key areas of business, including quality, cost, innovation, delivery and management. Tata Steel and JCB are also working together on new product designs that incorporate high strength steels, and are experimenting with alternative welding techniques for major fabrications. ■
