# GEARING UP FOR ITM POLAND

[TARİH: 01.05.2015 ISMR]

For suppliers of sheet metal machines, tools and systems, the Polish growth market provides excellent sales opportunities

The ITM Poland (Innovations Technologies Machine Tool exhibition) in Poznań, Poland (9-12 June 2015), which includes the Mach-Tool exhibition, is a significant industrial event in the European machine tool calendar and the only such large scale industrial event in Poland. It brings together all the sectors associated with the metal industry.

The brand has strengthened over the years as the exhibition has grown, in relation to the number of exhibitors and visitors at the event.

“In 2014, hundreds of companies from 30 countries presented their products in Poznań and the exhibition was visited by 16,728 guests from Poland and overseas. This year’s event promises to be even more interesting, so it is worth finding out why you should visit Poznań in June this year,” the show organiser, MTP, told ISMR.

## Poland in focus

For foreign investors, Poland is strategically important within Eastern Europe and is often seen as the gateway to markets in Russia and the Ukraine. As the largest of the Eastern European Union countries, Poland has become an attractive location for industries and supply chains in sectors such as automotive.

Poland has excellent links with its EU neighbours and the USA. As a result, English is the industry’s most important foreign language, alongside German. Poland’s major trade partners are, among others, Germany; Italy; Russia; France; Hungary; Ukraine; Spain and China.

A high level of education, knowledge and skills are available on the market. Another important benefit is the demonstrably professional attitude and work ethics of employees. Technical studies are increasingly popular. This is mainly as a result of government-funded programmes and powerful media campaigns. Polish intellectual capital is one of the key factors that make Poland an attractive place for FDI (foreign direct investment). Highly-qualified workers and well-educated specialists are easily available in Poland.

Numerous small and medium-sized component manufacturers, such as producers of pressed and punched parts, have started new businesses or re-located their production to Poland. Poland is now one of the largest European locations for the assembly of electrical and household appliances.

For suppliers of sheet metal machines, tools and systems, the Polish growth market provides excellent sales opportunities. The country has firmly established itself as a centre for the steel, car and electrical appliance industries and there is a readiness to invest in modern production facilities.

And whilst, in the past, sheet metal parts for the production of appliances were predominantly imported, they are now increasingly produced and finished in Poland. Achieving higher productivity has therefore become a major factor for competitive advantage in the country. This is noticeable in the growing proportion of new machinery used by Poland’s manufacturing industries.

## Technological avant-garde

Innovation has always been at the centre of the ITM Poland exhibition. Most of the exhibitors ensure that the offer they present features the most modern machinery, services and technologies.

“Several industry leaders have already announced their participation in the exhibition, including for example the SECO/WARWICK Group, one of the world’s leading manufacturers of equipment for thermal processes that will present, amongst other things, the production technology of large format graphene using the GraphMaster reactor. This high-temperature device with screen thermal insulation and vacuum system, based on a turbo molecular pump, enables the production of graphene in sizes of up to 500 x 500mm,” the organiser explained.

As part of this year’s exhibition, at the MTP Poznań (Poznań International Fair), there will be nine halls and seven theme exhibitions (Mach-Tool, Metalforum, Surfex, Welding, Hape, Science for the Economy, Industria Health and Safety), visitors will have the opportunity to interact with industry players in the machine tool; automotive; welding; varnishing; munitions; energy and railway sectors. In the halls at Poznań, machinery and equipment for metalworking, welding or paint coatings; hydraulics; pneumatics and metallurgy will all be showcased. Industrial robots and manipulators will be also displayed.

The Mach-Tool exhibition, part of ITM Poland, will focus on the following industrial areas of activity:
