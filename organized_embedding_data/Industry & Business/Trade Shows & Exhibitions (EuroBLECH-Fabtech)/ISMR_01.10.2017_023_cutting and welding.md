# CUTTING AND WELDING

[TARİH: 01.10.2017 ISMR]

Over 1,000 exhibitors from 41 countries and over 50,000 visitors from 120 countries attended Schweissen & Schneiden from 25-29 September 2017

SMR flew over to this year's Schweissen & Schneiden event at Messe Düsseldorf, Germany, from 25-29 September 2017. This international trade fair for joining, cutting and surface technologies attracted exhibitors from around the world, with products and services ranging from shaping, pre-treatment and post-treatment to equipment inspection. The show organiser, Messe Essen GmBH, and its longstanding partner, the German Welding Society (DVS), reported that 50,000 visitors from over 120 countries attended the event. 1,035 exhibitors from 41 countries showcased their products and technologies over seven halls and overseas exhibitor participation was nearly 64 per cent (in 2013: 61 percent). Approximately 66.3 per cent (in 2013: 47.6 per cent) of visitors arrived from outside Germany. The show organiser also reported that the density of decision-makers at the event was, at 83 per cent, higher than the density at the previous event (81.2 per cent).

The show team reorganised the halls for Schweissen & Schneiden 2017 which were structured on thematic focal areas to make it easier for trade visitors to find their way around. Numerous conferences, seminars and competitions also took place during the event. Under the overall responsibility of DVS (the German Welding Association), the promotion of young welders was highlighted at the show. The DVS Student Congress for young scientists took place at Schweissen and Schneiden as well as the Young Welders' Competitions – three competitions for young welders from Germany, Europe and other nations which have been staged live for visitors to the event since 2013.

## Highlighting industry trends

"76 per cent of the exhibitors (in 2013: 66) and 71 per cent of visitors (in 2013: 64) rated the current economic situation of their companies as 'favourable to very favourable'. The former showed that they were extremely satisfied that trade visitors were therefore very willing to invest. One in three has already ordered machines, materials or consumables at the fair or are planning concrete post-fair business," the show organiser told ISMR.

"One trend which emerged at the fair this year was trade visitors' growing interest in automation and robotics, also in the case of manual welders. In general, requirements are heading towards connectivity because Industry 4.0, as a topic, is permeating through the sector. One of the reasons for this is that corresponding systems are becoming increasingly affordable," it added.

To the same extent, said the organiser, this applies to additive manufacturing whose significance is increasing even further in sectors with long product life cycles. It has already gained acceptance in the automotive, aviation and heavy machinery industries. The areas of application for additive manufacturing, e.g. using arc technology, have also grown continuously in the last four years. Dr. Jörg Hildebrand from the Ilmenau University of Technology confirmed that the manufacturing of large and complex structures is a trend which will continue in the future.

A second trend highlighted at the event was the processing of different materials into hybrid structures. At the exhibition, global players as well as newcomers introduced their innovations on this subject e.g. a machine for surface treatment using surface welding as well as a new procedure for manufacturing metallic components.

"With the 3D metal printing process, the revolutionary production technology of 3D metal printing now offers the advantages of three-dimensional printing for metal, too," said the organiser.

## Showcasing new technologies

Six official joint participations showed countryspecific innovations. China (Hall 11 and Hall 15) and the USA (Hall 10 and Hall 11) were represented with two booths each. French exhibitors were present in Hall 9. Further country pavilions were from Korea (Hall 12), Taiwan (Hall 14) and Japan (Hall 14).

Overall, the exhibits included the latest devices, installations and materials – not only for welding and cutting but also for associated processes such as brazing/ soldering, heat treatment or thermal spraying. Attention centred on efficient, cost-saving and sustainable processes for manual welding, mechanised welding as well as automated welding. The range on offer included, amongst others, productivity-raising special welding processes for the manufacture of cars, new types of remote laser cutting technologies for integration into fabrication as well as lining systems for the economically viable manufacture of engine parts.

"The exhibition also presented ultra-modern high-tech solutions for the globalised economy. It met the rising demand for flexible and locationindependent production processes with the latest trends in matters relating to automation and intelligent production. The range on offer extended from interlinked robot welding installations and CNC flame cutting machines via laser robots for 3D metal cutting right up to externally controllable automated welding cells. Some were shown at the exhibition as world premieres….," added the organiser.

## Planning for the future

Messe Essen has been paving the way for companies from sectors dealing with joining, cutting and surfacing technologies to gain access to growth markets in China, India, Russia and, from January 2018, with the Essen Welding and Cutting Pavilion at the SteelFab exhibition in Sharjah, the United Arab Emirates. The new regional partnership in the UAE was announced on the first day of Schweissen & Schneiden 2017. SteelFab takes place from 15-18 January 2018 in Sharjah, UAE (see details below).

"The success of Schweissen & Schneiden is also the result of the partnership between DVS and Messe Essen which has lasted over 60 years," said DVS General Manager, Dr. Roland Boecking. "Even now, we are already pleased that the next event will return to its regular location in Essen in four years. We will again offer a comprehensive programme and various competitions then, too."

In September 2021, the event will move back to the New Messe Essen. When the current modernisation work on the exhibition site in Essen has been concluded, exhibitors and visitors will find a new foyer covering an area of 2,000m² and a new Hall 6. The modernisation work will finish in 2019.
