# Getting there

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

Visitor opening times at the Fieramilano Rho exhibition centre for the 34.BI-MU (from Wednesday 9 October to Friday 11 October 2024) are from 09.30 to 18.00. On Saturday 12 October 2024, opening hours are from 09.30 to 17.00. Entrance is free through pre-registration.

## By Metro/Bus

The Fieramilano Rho exhibition centre can be reached on the underground M1 Line, whose terminal (Rho-Fiera) is located at the “Porta Est” Entrance, or by high-speed trains and buses connecting with Milan's city centre.

A fast and extensive network of urban transport services connects the exhibition complex with the city centre of Milan, with railway stations and neighbouring airports, such as Linate and Malpensa. On exhibition days, high-speed trains will stop directly near the exhibition area.

From Milan Linate airport: Take the Metropolitana line 4 blu (Linate station) towards San Babila (Metro Red line 1 for Rho-Fieramilano). Visitors can also take bus number 73 Duomo M1-M3 terminus and Metro Red line 1, Rho-Fieramilano stop.

From Milan Malpensa airport: Visitors can take the Malpensa Express Train to Milano Centrale station (Milano Porta Garibaldi stop), then the Metro Lilac line 5 towards San Siro Stadium, Lotto stop, and interchange with Metro Red line 1 for Rho-Fieramilano (or from Milano Porta Garibaldi S-Line trains S5, S6 or S11 to Rho-Fieramilano). They can also take the Malpensa Express Train to Milano Cadorna station, then the Metro Red line 1 towards Rho Fieramilano.

From Orio Al Serio airport: A payable shuttle bus service is available from/ to Milano Centrale.

## By Car

The exhibition centre can also be easily reached by car via the Milan motorway ring roads. Its car parks have capacity for over 10,000 parking places.
