# SUCCESS in Stuttgart

[TARİH: 01.01.2014 ISMR]

THE BLECHEXPO international trade fair for sheet metal processing and Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology in Stuttgart from 5-8 November 2013 attracted 1153 exhibitors from 32 countries as well as 32,599 expert visitors from 96 nations. ISMR was the official international media partner for the event and was distributed at the show entrances and around the exhibition.

“330 exhibitors from outside Germany once more represented the latest in sheet metal processing and joining technology and introduced themselves to demanding markets in German-speaking countries and their European neighbours with high-tech products, as well as stand-alone and system solutions,” the organiser, P E Schall GmbH & Co. KG, explained.

Blechexpo and Schweisstec 2013 unveiled a large number of world premieres in sheet metal processing machines as well as joining and fastening processes. Focal points such as sheet metal processing machines, cutting and forming technology, pipe and profile processing plus sheet metal, pipe and profile materials at Blechexpo, as well as thermal cutting and machining, thermal and mechanical joining, fastening technologies and processes plus welding and cutting automation at Schweisstec, enjoyed the attention of interested expert visitors with decision-making authority.

The supplementary programme, with its sold-out exhibitor forum, was attended by just under 850 experts. Other highlights included the Coilex technology park, the “Top of Automobile” special show and the presentation of the first “Blechnet Award”.

The next Blechexpo and Schweisstec will take place from 3-6 November 2015. ■
