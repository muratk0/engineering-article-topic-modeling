# ALUMINIUM AWARDS

[TARİH: 01.05.2010 ISMR]

In preparation for ALUMINIUM 2010 (14 -16 September 2010, Essen Exhibition Centre), the industry is again focusing on new ideas for the efficient and progressive use of aluminium. Since 1998, the European Aluminium Award for Industrial Design & Engineering has been one of the most important awards in Europe. This year, the award will be presented for the seventh time.

Six prizes altogether will be awarded in two categories. The Industrial Products category deals with aluminium applications in Automotive & Transport, Building & Construction, Mechanical Engineering & Electronics and Production Techniques. The search is for aluminium components that make a positive contribution to the finished product. In the Consumer Products category, the jury will award prizes to finished products for Design and Innovation.

For other new developments in the industry, two special jury awards will be granted. For the first time, the award for Young Designers will specifically encourage young talents. In this category, students and designers in the under-30 age group present creative ideas and new approaches. The Overall Jury Award focuses on the best contribution in the area of environmental compatibility, sustainability and energy efficiency.

The award is given every two years and is open to engineers, design engineers, developers, designers and their clients. The competition is organized by the Aluminium Centrum (Houten) in cooperation with EAA European Aluminium Association, GDA Gesamtverband der Aluminiumindustrie and ALUMINIUM 2010. The deadline for interested companies and individuals to enter the award competition is 1 July 2010.
