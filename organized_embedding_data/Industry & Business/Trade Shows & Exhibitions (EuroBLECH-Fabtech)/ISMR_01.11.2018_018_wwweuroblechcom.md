## Looking to the future

In addition to EuroBLECH, Mack Brooks Exhibitions is organising a range of sheet metalworking exhibitions in different markets next year. The next BLECH India will take place from 25-27 April 2019 in Mumbai. AsiaBLECH 2019 will be held in Chengdu City from 20-22 November 2019. The first BLECH France will also take place from 21-23 January 2020 in Paris, France.
