# FABTECH heads to Florida this October

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

North America's largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event, FABTECH, heads to Orange County Convention Centre in Orlando (USA) from 15-17 October 2024. With over 800,000 square feet of exhibits, the trade show will encompass two halls where attendees will find a variety of technology pavilions dedicated to Forming & Fabricating; Tube & Pipe; METALFORM; Welding; Finishing; 3D/ Additive Manufacturing and Automation.

“FABTECH provides a convenient 'onestop shop' venue where visitors can meet suppliers, see the latest industry products and developments, and find the tools to improve their productivity, increase profits and discover new solutions to metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs," said the show organisers.

FABTECH is made possible by its five event partners, all of whom represent the varied and diverse make-up of the manufacturing industry in North America. They include the American Welding Society (AWS); the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI); the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association International (FMA); the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and SME.

“Registration is now open to attend FABTECH and see the most comprehensive showcase of sheet metal manufacturing processes and the latest developments in fabrication, from over 1,300 suppliers, in what is considered the tenth best state for manufacturing as presented in Florida's 2023 Manufacturing Report. Last year's event in Chicago welcomed over 45,000 attendees representing 80 countries and all 50 states," added the show organisers.

To support the future workforce in the manufacturing discipline, new on the FABTECH agenda this year is an Emerging Leaders programme for young professionals including networking, a workshop and a keynote address. Tailored for individuals aged 20-30, the Emerging Leaders programme aims to inspire, challenge and prepare participants to become influential leaders in their fields. 'Women of FABTECH' will return in 2024 featuring an inspiring keynote speaker who will share how to further careers in the industry.

The FABTECH Conference combines 60-90 minutes sessions and half-day workshops covering the latest in advanced fabrication and welding technology, workforce and management topics. Led by some key powerhouse speakers, the conference will provide insights, spotlight innovative strategies and offer solutions to manufacturing and production challenges.

FABTECH events include the Weld-Ed conference, seminars, professional programme, society events and the FABx Tech Talks at the FABTECH Theatre.

“Technical education sessions, dynamic keynotes, expert panel discussions and other special events keep show visitors returning to FABTECH every year. Admission to the FABTECH 2024 exhibit floor is complimentary for industry professionals who register before 12 October 2024," concluded the show organisers.
