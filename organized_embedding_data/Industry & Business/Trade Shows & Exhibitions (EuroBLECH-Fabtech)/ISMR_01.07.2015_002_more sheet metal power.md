# More sheet metal power

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

MEET ISMR at this year’s Blechexpo and Schweisstec trade fair from 3 – 6 November 2015 in Stuttgart. ISMR is a media partner and will be exhibiting at the event.

“Blechexpo and Schweisstec will experience over 15% growth in exhibition floor space and are headed for record results in other areas as well! The Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal processing and the Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology, as well as the 2nd Coilex technology park for the production of electromechanical components, will once again present the entire process sequence for sheet metal, pipe and profile processing – plus processing directly from the coil – in all of its facets and will thus play a special role in 2015,” the show organiser, P E Schall GmbH & Co. KG, explained.

The practical trade fair duo of Blechexpo and Schweisstec takes place over a two-year cycle at the state trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and covers the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology. Over 7% growth in exhibitor numbers, in comparison with the results of the last event in 2013, has already been registered. The show organiser is counting on over 1000 companies as exhibitors at the event. For further details, see www.blechexpo-messe.de
