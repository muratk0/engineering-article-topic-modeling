# Don't miss Industrial Spring 2025 in Poland

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

From 25-28 March 2025, the Kielce Exhibition and Congress Centre (Targi Kielce, Poland) will host the Industrial Spring, an event dedicated to the industrial sector in Poland and Central and Eastern Europe.

“Industrial Spring is a combination of key exhibitions on metalworking technology, automation, welding, 3D printing and other modern industry solutions. This industrial cycle includes STOM-BLECH&CUTTING; STOM-TOOL; STOM-LASER; STOM-ROBOTICS; STOM-FIX; KIELCE FLUID-POWER; EXPO-SURFACE; WELDING; 3D PRINTING DAYS; CONTROL-STOM; TEiA and WIRTOPROCESY,” outlined the show organiser.

"Exhibitors will present what is in most significant demand with industry specialists; machine tools; cutting machines; bending machines; complete robotic lines; industrial robots; specialised machines for foundry; welding machines and microscopes," it added.

As well as a showcase for modern industrial technologies, the Industrial Spring cluster also offers educational and information opportunities. Each exhibition includes specialist conferences highlighting current issues from various industry fields. A permanent element of the agenda is the Laser Processing Seminar at STOM-Laser which includes various types of laser technologies commonly used for material processing.

“The Kielce exhibition includes machine launches/premieres as well as technological novelties. For four days, Targi Kielce's exhibition halls become a gigantic factory working live, with various devices in operation," concluded the show organiser.■
