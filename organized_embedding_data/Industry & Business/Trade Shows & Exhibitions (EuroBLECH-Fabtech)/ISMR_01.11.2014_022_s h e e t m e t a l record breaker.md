# S H E E T M E T A L RECORD BREAKER

[TARİH: 01.11.2014 ISMR]

A record number of 1,573 EXHIBITORS from 38 COUNTRIES, as well as 59,600 VISITORS, supported this year’s EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover

## EVENT REPORT

ISMR SAYS:

“There was lively trading activity in EuroBLECH’s eight exhibition halls in Hanover”

ECH 2014, the 23rd International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, closed its doors in Hanover, Germany, this October after five successful exhibition days. A total of 59,600 trade visitors came to visit the world’s largest sheet metal working show to uncover the latest technology trends for sheet metal processing.

A record number of 1,573 exhibitors from 38 countries put a wide range of innovative solutions, cutting edge technology and an enormous amount of live machine demonstration on display, on a total net floor space of 86,500 square metres.

While the exhibitor numbers were up by 5% compared to the previous show, and the exhibition space increased by 3%, visitor numbers remained basically the same. The results of the preliminary visitor survey showed a further shift towards highly qualified trade visitors.

## Lively trading activity

“The prevailing mood at the show was positive throughout. There was lively trading activity in the eight exhibition halls and many exhibiting companies reported large numbers of international business contacts and buoyant sales figures. A first analysis of the exhibitor survey shows that participating companies were highly satisfied with the outcome of EuroBLECH 2014. The vast majority of exhibitors were able to fully or largely reach their target groups; they praised the quality of trade visitors and the internationality of the audience”, explained Nicola Hamann, managing director of the show organisers, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

The busy atmosphere at EuroBLECH 2014 reflects the overall positive outlook of the sheet metal working sector, said the show organiser. The exhibition survey showed that companies assess the current business climate as more favourable than two years ago. Approaching new markets was the main aim for the exhibitors at this year’s exhibition and EuroBLECH 2014 was more international than the previous exhibition.

“We are delighted to be able to report that 52% of our exhibitors came from outside Germany compared to 48% last time; and we could also increase our traditionally high percentage of international visitors. 38% of this year’s visitors came from outside Germany compared to 34% at the previous show,” added Nicola Hamann.

The first visitor survey data showed that top visitor countries outside Germany were the Netherlands; Austria; Switzerland; Italy; Poland; Sweden; Turkey; the Czech Republic; Spain; Great Britain; Belgium and the USA .

The quota of visitors from top management was very high (41% of all visitors), and there was a high percentage of visitors with decisionmaking capacity (81% of all visitors). Almost half of all visitors came to EuroBLECH 2014 with the definite intention of buying.

“EuroBLECH 2014 also received excellent evaluations from visitors and exhibitors, from inside and outside Germany. Participants were highly satisfied, particularly with the comprehensive range of products on offer and with the international flavour of the exhibition. More than two thirds of all exhibiting companies intend to exhibit again at the next show. EuroBLECH 2016 will take place from 25 – 29 October 2016, in Hanover, Germany,” added the organiser.

## Solution to a complex problem F.LLI VIMERCATI S.N.C. Italy

This machine is a special project created for a prestigious Italian company for facilitating its production of electric cabinets.

“The press brake has been built so that it can automatically replace top and bottom tools, operate the bending-flattening tool and change the position of the photocells for creating the finished component in one bending station,” the supplied explained.

“Thanks to this system, the customer company reaped many benefits:

a. It bought a compact machine with low tonnage: Saving money and saving space in the factory. (With a standard product, it should choose a bigger and longer machine to work on more stations). b. It reduced manufacturing time and increased productivity: The operator does not have to shift to different working stations, but completes the component in one bending station on-site. c. It improved the operators' operational procedures: More quality and less physical effort for the operators.”

## Production of an unusual product made of sheet metal Jnius Design Finland

“We designed a size 5 scale 1:1 football made of 32 separate pieces and connected to each other just using the parts themselves and no extra tools, weldings, screws etc.

“The ball is constructed so that it could be used like an original ball so it will not fall apart or break when handled. It is used as an award by the Finnish Football Association to honour the best players, coaches and referees in the country. This has been the case for almost five years and they love the product. It is possible to use different materials,” explained Jnius Design.

“The parts are made on a Prima Power punching machine with special upforming tools and punches. The assembly of this 3D puzzle takes about one hour. Most people are interested in how the last part is assembled, as you cannot see which part is the last one," it added.
