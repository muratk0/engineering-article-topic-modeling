# LASYS meets Central Europe in Slovakia

[TARİH: 01.03.2017 ISMR]

MESSE STUTTGART AND the German-Slovakian Chamber of Industry and Commerce (AHK Slowakei) are jointly organising the event "LASYS meets Central Europe" for the first time from 30-31 May 2017. This event will focus on laser applications for the automobile industry and will be held in Trnava in Slovakia. It will bring together suppliers of laser production systems, laser-specific machine sub-systems, components, processes and services, and industrial users.

The Trnava region is home to a large number of industrial companies, particularly from the automotive production sector which is rapidly expanding throughout Slovakia.

"We are also enabling exhibitors at the international trade fair LASYS to access a future-oriented user market," said Gunnar Mey, Department Director Industry, Messe Stuttgart. This is because economic activity in the Slovakian automobile industry, which produced more than one million cars in 2016 for the second year in succession, is leading to rising demand for laser technology.

Dr. Guido Glania, Managing Director, AHK Slowakei, added: "Industry in Slovakia is continuing its growth course. The expansion of the Volkswagen plant in Bratislava and the start of production at a new Jaguar-Land Rover factory in mid-2018 have provided a further boost to the investment plans of local companies. However, expansion plans and relocations are also playing a significant role outside the automobile industry, for example in the electrical engineering industry. In other words, there are excellent market opportunities, as a whole."

Further information is available online at www.lasys-meets.com
