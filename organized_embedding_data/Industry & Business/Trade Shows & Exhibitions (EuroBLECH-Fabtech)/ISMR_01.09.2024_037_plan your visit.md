# PLAN YOUR VISIT

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Here are key details on EuroBLECH 2024 in Hanover to help you plan your visit.

EuroBLECH is a truly international event and the world's largest exhibition for sheet metal processing, covering the entire supply chain in 15 different technology sectors across nine exhibition halls. The event, which will be held from 22-25 October 2024 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds in Germany, is viewed as a key barometer for the global sheet metal sector.

In 2022, at the previous EuroBLECH event, 1,300 exhibitors from 39 countries showcased their latest developments, from all areas of sheet metal working. As a vertical trade show, EuroBLECH covers the entire sheet metal working technology chain and offers the industry sector a marketplace for knowledge transfer, individual manufacturing solutions and worldwide business contacts.

Read this section of our EuroBLECH Preview to help you plan your visit to EuroBLECH this October. Don't forget to meet the ISMR team on stand H92 in Hall 14 while you are there!

## Sheet metal portal

The world's largest sheet metal working technology exhibition attracts top industry professionals from all over the world. It predominantly targets all sheet metalworking specialists at all management levels in small and medium-sized companies as well as large enterprises. Visitors come from all key industry sectors.

EuroBLECH 2024 EXHIBITION PROFILE

Exhibits cover the entire sheet metal working technology chain in the manufacturing of metal prototypes, industrial components and consumer parts.

Categories are sheet metal, tube, sections (ferrous and non-ferrous); finished products, components, assemblies; handling; separation and cutting; forming; flexible sheet metal working; tube/section processing; machine elements; processing of sheet metal/ plastic hybrid structures; joining, welding and fastening; additive manufacturing; surface treatment of sheet metal; tools and dies; controlling, regulating, measuring and testing; quality assurance; data processing, hardware and software; factory and warehouse equipment; environmental protection and recycling; safety at work and Research and Development.

For a flavour of the latest products at the exhibition, visitors can access the Online Show Preview on the EuroBLECH website. It provides an overview of the wide variety of innovations, technologies and systems on display at the event. See www.euroblech.com/en-gb/visit/show-preview.html

EuroBLECH 2024 will be held in nine halls (halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 26 and 27) at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds in Germany. New since EuroBLECH 2022, Hall 26 will host exhibitors of joining technologies, as well as surface and tool technologies, which were previously based in Hall 13.

The exhibitor list, available on the EuroBLECH website, is regularly updated and provides plenty of information on exhibiting companies such as exhibitor profiles, company videos and contact details. EuroBLECH e-news, which is sent out frequently ahead of the show, offers the latest news about the event, the exhibitors and the industry sector.

Visitors requiring a visa to travel are recommended to start their preparation as early as possible. Further information is available in the Travel hub on the EuroBLECH website. Further detailed information about the exhibition, as well as travel and accommodation, is available on the show website at www.euroblech.com

Entrance tickets for EuroBLECH 2024 are available via the website ticket shop. Tickets can also be purchased on-site during the exhibition.

## Getting there

Direct flights to Hanover are available from numerous major cities. An exclusive EuroBLECH shuttle service will be available throughout the trade fair period for €25 each way. The shuttle bus, which is not bound to a schedule, will run as required.

The taxi journey from the airport to the exhibition grounds takes approximately 30 minutes, depending upon traffic.

Alternatively, the suburban railway (S-Bahn) line 5 (located between Terminals B and C) will take you from the airport to the main railway station (approximately 16 minutes). 'Hannover Messe/Laatzen' is the exhibition grounds' own station. From there, the covered Skywalk will take you to entrance West 1.

German Railways (DB) is offering the DB-Event Ticket (train ticket) for a fixed price from any DB station to Hannover exclusively to EuroBLECH visitors. Check the special DB Event Ticket for EuroBLECH 2024 from just €55.90 on www.veranstaltungsticketbahn.de/?event=14154&l=en

You can also reach the exhibition grounds quickly and easily with the local transport network. The nearest stops are:

■ For entrance West 1: 'Hannover Messe/ Laatzen' S-Bahn (suburban train) stop.

■ For entrance Süd 1: 'Laatzen/ Gutenberstraße' bus stop.

■ For entrance Ost 3: 'Messe/Ost (EXPO PLAZA)' tram stop.

■ For entrance Nord 1+2: 'Messe/Nord' tram stop.

Alma GmbH
https://almacam.com

Alma is a leading developer of CAD/ CAM software for sheet metal working, cutting and robotics. Over 40 years, it has built up a reputation as a leading specialist in the automatic nesting of complex shapes for cutting. It offers a comprehensive range of CAD/CAM software for cutting in every form, sheetmetal working and mechanical welding: cutting by fluid jet; punching; milling/routing; five-axis cutting; robotic cutting; cutting of circular and oblong tubes; folding/ unfolding; robotised arc welding etc.

These applications are completed by a set of modules centred on production management and control (quotes, planning etc.) and integration into a manufacturer's information system. Its software products link CAD, ERP and workshops in the digital chain for sheet metalworking and machine welding. It is also commercialising its cutting optimisation algorithms in the form of software components. It will showcase, amongst others, its Nest&Cut web application at EuroBLECH.
