# Fabtech 2015 is Going to be Bigger than Ever!

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 The Fabricator]

North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing event heads to McCormick Place in Chicago

Nov. 9-12, 2015. FABTECH

®

2015 will be actionpacked with even more to experience on an expanded show floor. Attendees have the opportunity to make side-by-side comparisons and uncover new ideas and solutions to drive business to unparalleled levels of productivity. Highlights include over 1,500 exhibits encompassing more than 650,000 net square feet of floor space; an anticipated 40,000 visitors; and over 100 educational sessions, including content for every role.

Featured Presentations

The event kicks off in the fast lane on Monday, Nov. 9, with a keynote presentation from NASCAR

®

legend and champion Rusty Wallace. With insights from his racing experience, Wallace demonstrates that everything is possible through the power of teamwork. Also on Monday, a featured panel session, What Additive Manufacturing /3-D Printing Means for the Metal Fabrication Industry, will give attendees a new perspective on how this innovative technology can help improve their business. Attendees can also visit the Additive Manufacturing/3-D Printing Pavilion at the show to see the exciting capabilities of 3-D printing firsthand.

AWS Professional Welders Competition

Professional welders will demonstrate their skills to earn the title of Best Welder in America during the Professional Welders Competition, Monday, Nov. 9, and Tuesday, Nov. 10. Cheer on the contestants as they make a single-pass shielded metal arc weld with E7018 on low-carbon steel. Speed and quality will be the criteria. Winners will be announced on Wednesday, Nov. 11, at 11 a.m. Professional welders interested in competing can sign up on-site to compete for a $2,500 first prize, a $1,000 second prize, and a $500 third prize.

Networking Opportunities Network during show hours and after. Have fun and unwind at the annual FABTECH Industry Night on Tuesday, Nov. 10. Enjoy food, drinks, bowling, billiards, and more all while connecting with old colleagues and new friends at Lucky Strike in downtown Chicago. We expect this to be a soldout event, so get your tickets early when you register for the show.
