Just added to the speaker lineup:

Lourenco Goncalves, chairman, president and CEO of Cleveland-Cliffs, will discuss the state of the steel industry on Tuesday, March 1. Responsible for transforming Cleveland-Cliffs into the largest flat-rolled steel mill in North America, Mr. Goncalves will bring insight and perspective for the future of steel during the Annual Meeting’s opening session.

Connections (and FUN!) are on the Horizon

Attendees report their number one reason for attending the FMA Annual meeting is networking! Connect with other industry professionals at these events and activities:

Meet customers and make new friends at the Welcome Reception

Support the future of manufacturing by participating in NBT’s silent auction

Hit the links at Miami Beach Golf Club and Normandy Shores Golf Club

Enjoy a night out where legends are made at the Hard Rock Café Miami

Visit local manufacturing facilities during the Bus ‘n’ Bagels Tours

Gain industry contacts visiting tabletop exhibits and networking during meals and breaks

Whatever activities you choose — you’ll be in good company!
