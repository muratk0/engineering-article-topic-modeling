# PLAN YOUR VISIT

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

Here are key details on EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover to help you plan your visit.

EuroBLECH is an international event and the world's largest exhibition for the sheet-metalworking industry. Around 1,300 exhibitors from 39 countries are expected to present their latest developments, from all areas of sheet metalworking, across nine halls (halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 26 and 27) at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds. The event, which will be held from 25-28 October 2022, is a key barometer for the global sheet metal sector.

Visitors can expect a huge amount of live machine demonstrations and technological world premieres. There will be plenty of opportunities to watch innovative machinery in action, allowing attendees to assess and discuss the potential benefits for their own production processes.

"We are really looking forward to the buzz and excitement on the show floor, said Evelyn Warwick, Event Director, EuroBLECH, on behalf of Mack-Brooks Exhibitions. "In the end, nothing beats human interaction. A face-to-face conversation, a live demonstration, a fist bump – all this is important to establish good relationships and build trust. We are delighted that EuroBLECH will once again provide a physical platform where tech buyers can meet and connect with worldleading suppliers and innovators in the industry."

Exhibitors will display a comprehensive range of machinery, tools and systems for the entire value chain of sheet metal processing.

## Sheet metal in focus

EuroBLECH will be held at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds in Germany from 25-28 October 2022. The exhibition profile covers fifteen technology sectors and encompasses the entire sheet-metalworking technology chain. It is a key venue for showcasing the latest sheet metal product launches, new technologies and efficient processes.

The exhibitor list, available on the EuroBLECH website, is regularly updated and provides plenty of information on exhibiting companies such as exhibitor profiles, company videos and contact details. EuroBLECH e-news, which is sent out frequently ahead of the show, offers the latest news about the event, the exhibitors and the industry sector. The subscription form is on the EuroBLECH website.

Visitors requiring a visa to travel are asked to start their preparation as early as possible. Further information is available in the Travel hub on the EuroBLECH website. Further information about the exhibition, as well as travel and accommodation, is available on the show website.

Entrance tickets for EuroBLECH 2022 are now available via the website ticket shop. Tickets can also be purchased on-site during the exhibition.

## EXHIBITION PROFILE

Exhibits cover the entire sheetmetalworking technology chain in the manufacturing of metal prototypes, industrial components and consumer parts.

Categories are sheet metal, tube, sections (ferrous and non-ferrous); finished products, components, assemblies; handling; separation, cutting; forming; flexible sheet metalworking; tube/section processing; machine elements; processing of sheet metal/plastic hybrid structures; joining, welding, fastening; additive manufacturing; surface treatment of sheet metal; tools, dies; controlling, regulating, measuring, testing; quality assurance; data processing, hardware and software; factory and warehouse equipment; environmental protection, recycling; safety at work and research and development.

## Getting there

The world's largest sheet-metalworking technology exhibition attracts top industry professionals from all over the world. It predominantly targets all sheet metalworking specialists at all management levels in small and medium-sized companies as well as large enterprises. Visitors come from all key industry sectors.

Shuttle buses operate from Hanover airport (Terminal C) to the exhibition grounds (Entrance West 1). The journey lasts approximately 30 minutes. The taxi journey from the airport to the exhibition grounds takes approximately 30 minutes, depending upon traffic. Alternatively, the suburban railway (S-Bahn) line 5 (located between Terminals B and C) will take you from the airport to the main railway station (approximately 16 minutes).

If you are travelling by train, 'Hannover Messe/Laatzen' is the exhibition grounds' own station. From there, the covered Skywalk will take you to entrance West 1.

You can also reach the exhibition grounds quickly and easily with the local transport network. The nearest stops are

- For entrance West 1: 'Hannover Messe/ Laatzen' bus stop.

- For entrance Süd 1: 'Gutenberstraße' bus stop.

For entrance Ost 3: 'Messe/ Ost' tram stop.

■For entrance Nord 1+2: 'Messe/ Nord' tram stop.
