# Strong showing for LASYS 2018

[TARİH: 01.08.2018 ISMR]

LASYS, RUN BY Messe Stuttgart, ended recently on a high. The positive mood in the market for laser material processing was apparent on all three days of the exhibition at the Stuttgart Trade Fair Centre in Germany. Around 5,800 visitors interacted with 189 exhibitors from 23 countries on the use of lasers in industrial production.

Gerhard Hein, Head of the Laser and Laser Systems Working Group and Manager of the Economics and Statistics Division of the Machine Tools and Manufacturing Systems at the VDMA, summarised the event as follows: "The exhibitors at this year's LASYS were delighted to report that many visitors came from nearly all target sectors. The response to invitations was remarkably good. Exhibitors were able to welcome purchasers of complete system solutions and integrators of various expansion stages at their stands. They also reported various discussions with production planners from the automobile industry, relating to current projects or future procurement requirements. Finally, we noted that the use of lasers in electromobility was discussed intensively and with positive expectations for the future."

He added that, to combat skills shortages in the industry, a young talent campaign had been driven with the Federal Ministry of Education and Research at LASYS to show school-leavers exhibits and try to inspire them with a fascination for laser technology and photonics. The hands-on workshops, where electrical components could be tried out, met with a very positive response from the young target group.

Visitors to LASYS came from 39 countries (24% international visitors) – besides Germany, primarily from Switzerland, Austria, Italy, France and the UK. Over three quarters had concrete intentions to invest or purchase and one third of the purchasers planned to invest over €200,000, said the organiser.

The conference programme included the Stuttgart Laser Technology Forum, which took place for the tenth time and registered over 300 participants. In addition, leading experts from market research and industry gave presentations at the Stuttgart Laser Marketplace. The practically-oriented forum "Lasers in Action" in the trade fair hall, as well as several specialist workshops and seminars, rounded off the programme.

The next LASYS will be held at the Stuttgart Trade Fair Centre from 16-18 June 2020. Before then, on 29 November 2018, "LASYS meets Central Europe" will take place in Budapest.
