Calling All Educators and Camp Directors — Reap the Benefits of Hosting a 2022 Summer Manufacturing Camp

NBT manufacturing camps are held at schools across the U.S. to inspire middle and high school students to pursue manufacturing careers. Ideally, students who attend camp will continue their skills training by enrolling in a manufacturing program at the same school where their camp was hosted!

Apply for grant funding and start a camp at your school!

Here are the reasons why:

Camps help promote a positive image of the school in the community

Positive camp experiences can lead to increased enrollment in the school’s technical and manufacturing programs

Camps help strengthen the school’s relationships with manufacturers in the community

Customized T-shirts are given to every student who attends a camp

Students receive a one-year license to SolidWorks CAD software

Marketing materials and other valuable resources are provided by NBT
