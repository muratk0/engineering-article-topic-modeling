(NBT), the foundation of FMA, invites you to join the virtual awards gala on October 22 at 6 p.m. CDT. Along with presenting sponsor AMADA AMERICA, INC., NBT will shine a spotlight on manufacturing’s brightest stars. Three industry awards will be presented during this entertaining and celebratory program to recognize and honor the contributions made by an individual or family, a company, and a summer manufacturing camp.

The virtual gala will feature inspiring stories about NBT camps and scholarships plus an online art auction. You’ll be able to bid on many beautiful pieces donated by professional and student artisans from across the country. Enjoy entertainment, raffles, prizes, and more on this exciting, fun, and special evening! RSVP for this no-cost event to receive your free gift while supplies last.

Proceeds from this event support NBT’s summer manufacturing camp and scholarship programs. Through the generosity of donors, NBT has awarded 539 scholarships and 566 camp grants impacting nearly 10,000 students across the country.
