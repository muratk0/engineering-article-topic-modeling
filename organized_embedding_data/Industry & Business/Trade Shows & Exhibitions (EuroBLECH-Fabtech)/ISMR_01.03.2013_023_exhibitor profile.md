## An eye on exhibitors

More than 103 companies have already booked exhibition space at Fastener Fair India 2013; this is more than the total exhibition space of the previous show. Alongside India and other Asian exhibitor countries, such as Taiwan and China, a large number of German and Italian companies will again exhibit at India’s dedicated exhibition for the fastener and fixings industry.

Fastener Fair India 2013 will once again be co-located with BLECH India, Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working. The third edition of this highly successful trade fair will display machines, tools and systems for the entire sheet metal manufacturing process, including metal forming and bending solutions, stamping, pressing, automation, flexible manufacturing systems, metal supply, cutting, punching, joining/welding, tooling, laser technology as well as finishing and coating.

Fastener Fair India 2013 is open from 17 and 18 April 2013 from 10:00 – 17:00 hrs. Entrance is free for pre-registered visitors as well as those arriving on the day. Detailed information is available on the show website at www.fastenerfair.com/mumbai/

## MAJOR FASTENER MARKETS

India’s major fastener markets include sectors such as automotive, rail, road, telecoms, domestic appliances, energy and construction.

“Passenger vehicle sales and production are expected to grow by 14 per cent to 16 per cent between 2011 and 2021, reaching over 9 to 10 million units annually, and commercial vehicles sales at 18-20 per cent,” said Inter Ads - Brooks Exhibitions (India), adding that India’s auto component exports are expected to reach US$ 26 billion by the end of this decade.

The Auto Component Manufacturers Association (ACMA) estimates that sales will grow exponentially to 10 million cars, 30 million two wheelers and 2.2 million commercial vehicles by 2020. The automotive sector accounts for 60 per cent of machine tool demand in the country.

“In 2012, India’s budget for highway construction was 550 billion rupees and the road construction sector was expected to grow at about 35 per cent between 2012-2013. The Indian government is pushing to build 20km of roads each day,” added the event organiser.

India has become the world’s second largest mobile handset market with sales expected to reach 206 million units in 2014.
