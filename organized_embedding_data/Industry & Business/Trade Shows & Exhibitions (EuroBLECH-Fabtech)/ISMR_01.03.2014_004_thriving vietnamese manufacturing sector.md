# THRIVING VIETNAMESE manufacturing sector

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

FOLLOWING its successful showing in 2013, MTA VIETNAM is set to return with a comprehensive display of metalcutting and metalforming machinery as well as tooling systems that cater to Vietnam’s robust manufacturing industry. The next edition of MTA VIETNAM will be held from 8 – 11 July 2014.

The Vietnamese manufacturing sector has gained momentum this year, with January seeing its strongest rise in output since April 2011. There is a sharp increase in pledged FDI, mostly channelled into the manufacturing sector, which is expected to further accelerate exports in the coming year. This reflects the country’s competitiveness in labour-intensive manufacturing.

Retaining its position as a leading trade event for Vietnam’s manufacturing industry, MTA VIETNAM2014 will return to the Saigon Exhibition & Convention Centre (SECC) in Ho Chi Minh City from 8 – 11 July 2014. The event will welcome group pavilions from Germany, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Taiwan and Thailand, as well as local supporting government organisations. See www.mtavietnam.com ■
