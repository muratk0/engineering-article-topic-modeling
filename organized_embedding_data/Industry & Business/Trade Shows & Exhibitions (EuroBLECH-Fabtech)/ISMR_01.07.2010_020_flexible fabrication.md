# FLEXIBLE FABRICATION

[TARİH: 01.07.2010 ISMR]

Last year’s economic trough has definitely been left behind and the tube business is picking up again, says the organiser of the International Tube and Pipe Trade fair held recently in the halls of the Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre.

Almost 69,200 trade visitors from over 100 countries attended the two global trade fairs, International Wire and Cable Trade Fair, and Tube, International Tube and Pipe Trade Fair, from 12 to 16 April 2010 to gather information on the latest machinery, equipment and products from the wire, cable and tube processing industries.

Summing up Joachim Schäfer, the managing director in charge of Wire 2010 and Tube 2010 at Messe Düsseldorf GmbH, said: “Companies are now investing again so as to gear up for future competition or re-pitch themselves.”

## INDUSTRY NEWS

In March last year, tube bending and endforming technology specialist, AddisonMckee announced an agreement to merge with Eagle Precision Technologies, a leader in muffler manufacturing solutions.

“AddisonMckee is changing everything we do in terms of bringing our products to market in a simpler, more efficient and streamlined way, at a reduced cost, and more quickly than ever”, stated Jim Sabine, President, AddisonMckee. “Indeed, we are in the midst of unveiling a new, next-generation, all-electric tube bending product line that is geared toward these three fundamental principles.”

The Addison showcase was highlighted by the pre-release unveiling ‘beta’ release of OMEGA VII, its new easy-to-use, full-featured bender control technology.

“The challenge in controls has become how to offer our customers ultimate flexibility for optimising tube production to suit their applications while still keeping the machines simple to program and operate. This, combined with superior diagnostics for real-time process monitoring and troubleshooting, has been the driving force behind OMEGA VII,” Addison told ISMR.

BLM Group is a technological and market leader in the design, manufacture and sales of tube processing machines such as laser tube cutting systems for tubes of up to 508mm outside diameter, sawing machines for tube, bar and profile (ADIGE and ADYGE-SYS) as well as tube and wire benders (BLM). With 498 employees and an export share of over 65 per cent, in 2008 the BLM Group made a turnover of € 128 million.

The takeover of Montorfano Sas last year allowed BLM to further increase its tube, wire and metal bending portfolio.

BLM’s SMART is its new all-electric (8 axes) tube bending machine with its own 3D visual programming software (VGP3D). The multistack SMART can be freely and quickly switched into right-hand or left-hand configuration, its automatic load and unload function allows lights-out operation and it is suitable for bending tubes up to diam. 28mm.

Above: BLM cutting machinery (Sidhil) Below right: Examples of bent tube on the BLM stand at MACH (Simon Latter)

SMART is particularly suitable for the bending of tubes with complex shape and multiple bending radii, even preassembled (fittings, endforms, etc.), mostly used in the automotive branch (hydraulic pipes, air conditioning, power supply) as well as HVAC. BLM also recently introduced its ELECT 150 new all-electric bending machine (12 controlled axes) for the bending of medium/large tubes (up to 150mm).

Harco Metal Products (Harco), an industry leader in tube fabrication and systems integration, recently purchased new, advanced machinery. Both the Wafios BMZ42 and the Accurex TubeInspectHD were selected by Harco. The BMZ42 was chosen for its ability to bend free form geometry tubing requiring high tolerance and repeatability.

Harco bought the TUBE InspectHD machine because of its accuracy in optical measuring, coupled with its speed and reporting details.

Offering a vast array of custom metal fabrication and tube bending services, along with complete welding, stamping, assembly, and design services, Harco provides customers with everything needed to complete a project, under one roof.

With over 35,000 sq. ft. of manufacturing space in both Arizona and Alabama, and staff working around the clock, Harco has quickly become a giant in the US metal fabricating industry.

"We tackle projects of any scope for several major industries, taking on even the most challenging designs," it told ISMR.

Herber Engineering AB (Värnamo, Sweden) has formed a strategic partnership with Innovative Tube Equipment Corp. (ITEC) in Rockford, Illinois and Montreal, to be its exclusive sales, applications engineering, technical support/service and installation group for the North American market.

Herber designs and builds tube and pipe bending machines. Its multi-stack, all-electric, CNC tube bending machines have a centreline booster system to help minimize scrap and secondary operations on automotive exhaust and other precision tubular components.

## REDUCING TIME AND COST

“Less weight, lower costs, better material utilisation, fewer parts and a one-piece flow,” is how Klaus Löfler, Head of Branch Management at TRUMPF Laser Systems, describes the benefits of tube and profile design in automotive manufacturing over traditional laser welding and cutting. “It allows flange-free structures that can be manufactured and easily joined together using an interlocking feature. No special equipment is needed to accurately join the tubes.”
