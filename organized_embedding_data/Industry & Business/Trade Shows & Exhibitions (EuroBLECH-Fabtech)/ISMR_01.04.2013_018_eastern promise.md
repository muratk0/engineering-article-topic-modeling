## Don’t miss the BLECH China exhibition in Suzhou from 14–16 May 2013

An important new sheet metal exhibition, BLECH China 2013, will take place at Suzhou International Expo Centre from 14-16 May 2013. BLECH China 2013, organised by Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd, is an opportunity to showcase equipment, technology and services under the well-known BLECH brand, in one of the most exciting and dynamic manufacturing markets in the world for the first time. Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd. is the organiser of EuroBLECH, the world's largest sheet metal working technology exhibition.

In recent years, China's economy around the Yangtze River Delta has become the engine of China's flourishing economy. The region generates 26 per cent of the country's GDP, fulfils 37 per cent export volume, attracts 52 per cent foreign investment and has consistently shown double digit growth per year. It is the economic centre of China’s National Development Programme and an important international gateway to the Asia-Pacific region and beyond.

Next to Shanghai, Suzhou is the second largest industrial city with the fifth largest GDP in China. With a population of 10 million, it has grown to become a hub of international and domestic investment. It lies just to the west and within easy reach of Shanghai with access to both Shanghai Pudong and Shanghai Hongqiao international airports.
