# FABTECH announces expanded education programme

[TARİH: 01.11.2012 ISMR]

FABTECH, North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event, has released a schedule for its newly expanded education programme which will be held alongside the show in Las Vegas (12-14 November 2012). The line-up will feature more than 100 educational sessions that offer unique insights from top industry experts on hot topics in manufacturing.

FABTECH is expected to attract more than 25,000 manufacturers to the Las Vegas Convention Center to view 1,100 exhibits, including live equipment demonstrations covering more than 400,000 net square feet of floor space. Simultaneously, the education programme features technical, operational, economic and managerial sessions where participants can discover solutions to advance their career and their businesses.

“This year’s line-up will be like no other,” said John Catalano, the show’s co-manager. “We have added more than 50 new sessions, including presentations in Spanish, and expanded our welding, management and lean manufacturing tracks.”

Several new special events have been added to the programme such as a Workshop on Lean Manufacturing for Managers that will be offered on Sunday 11 November 2012. There will also be a panel discussion with seasoned Washington DC government relations experts on how the presidential and congressional election results will impact US manufacturing; a special roundtable with five manufacturing CEOs on “The State of the Industry: Manufacturers’ Executive Outlook” and various new product presentations taking place daily in the FABTECH Theatres.

The five FABTECH co-sponsors represent a wide variety of expertise. The Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA), the Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME), the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI) co-sponsor the sessions on cutting, finishing, forming & fabricating, lean, management, stamping and tube & pipe. The American Welding Society (AWS) will present a comprehensive line-up of welding education. www.fabtechexpo.com ■
