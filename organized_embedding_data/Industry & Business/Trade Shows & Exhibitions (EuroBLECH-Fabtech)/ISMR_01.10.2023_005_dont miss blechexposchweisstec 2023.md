# Don't miss Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

ISMR previewed the 9th Schweisstec international trade fair for joining and the 16th Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metalworking in its September issue. This key meeting place for global players in sheet metalworking will be held from 7-10 November 2023 at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre (Germany). The event encompasses nine halls which are now fully booked. The Blechexpo/Schweisstec event takes place every two years at Messe Stuttgart.

"The trade fair duo presents complete process sequences for modern, futureproof, sheet-metal processing. An impressive supplementary programme includes presentation of the 'best Award 2023’, the 'Blechexpo Steel Convention 2023' the 'futureSteel’ congress and an expert forum," the show organiser, P. E. Schall, outlined.

Sheet metal, pipe and profile processing technologies can be found in Halls 1, 3, 5 and 9. Halls 4 and 6 are reserved for stamping technologies. Cutting, joining and fastening technologies will be presented in Hall 7.

Hall 8 will focus on pressing and forming technologies. Steel and metal services, as well as surface finishing technology, will be addressed in Hall 10.

“The newly restructured and more clearly defined exhibition hall layout makes visiting the trade fair more efficient for expert visitors. The range of topics covered by Blechexpo/Schweisstec includes efficient process technologies for modern laser technology, fully automated robotic sheet metal processing machines, precision tools and systems for punching, bending, braking and forming, as well as innovative micro-waterjet cutting technologies. Expert visitors will experience the entire process sequence for sheet metalworking with high levels of practical relevance in a pleasant working atmosphere at Blechexpo/Schweisstec," added the show organiser.

The “Blechexpo Steel Convention 2023" will take place in the forum area in Hall 10 at noon on the first day of the trade fair and will focus attention on “Challenges in the Steel Markets in 2023." The convention will be organised by the Stahl-Kommunikation Agency. A “futureSteel” congress has also been organised for the second day of the trade fair in cooperation with Marketsteel Media from Düsseldorf.

The daily Exhibitor Forum has now been further expanded; speakers from exhibiting companies will hold presentations on all trade fair days at various forums aligned to the thematic structure of the exhibition halls for the first time at Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2023. This includes the Stamping Forum in Hall 4 (1FO-01), the Schweisstec Forum in Hall 7 (7515), the BEST Forum in hall 9 (9315) and the Steel Forum in Kongress West. Additional special forums with the ZVEl, IBU and EFB associations will be held on the third day of the trade fair on 9 November 2023.

Meet the ISMR team on booth 1400 (Hall 1) at the event. ISMR is the show's official international media partner and copies of the October issue of ISMR will be given out at the show entrance, press centre and on its booth at the show. ISMR's Blechexpo/Schweisstec preview includes a selection of halls and products that will be showcased at the exhibition.

## DISCOVER THE NEW AIDA DSF-NE2 SERVO PRESSES

AIDA is pleased to introduce the new line of monolithic servo presses DSF-NE2, awarded Product of the Year at the EuroBLECH Awards 2022 in Hannover, that combine the high rigidity of the monobloc frame with the flexibility of the freely programmable servo movement of AIDA DSF® technology. Each frame component is designed and verified with FEM systems and manufactured to reduce deflection under load to less than 0.1 mm/m. This series of presses, capable of reaching speeds of 120 strokes per minute, expresses performance of absolute excellence.

## EXPERIENCE THE BENEFITS OF THE NEW AIDA TRANSFERS

AET (AIDA Electronic Transfer) is the after-market solution developed by AIDA to allow movement of blanks and parts in multi-station or interconnected presses. AET are conceived for retrofit installations and may be easily integrated to all brands of mechanical and servo presses. Thanks to the high-rigidity structure, high-precision preloaded roller guides and high-performance servo motors, AET assure maximum output performance.
