More than 11,500 attendees visited the three-day event in May, with attending companies including Ford, Chrysler, GM, Caterpillar, Delphi, John Deere, Lear, Whirlpool, Mabe, Magna, Bombardier and Volkswagen. Visitors represented some of the fastest-growing industries in the Mexican manufacturing market, such as aerospace, automotive appliance, contract manufacturing, construction, and agriculture.

“Considering these factors, Mexico is positioned well to support this positive growth...”

The Mexican manufacturing sector continues to grow at a faster pace than has been the case in the last few years. The market has experienced an increase of over 154 percent in foreign direct investment equaling a total of $1.893 billion in the past .

“With reshoring and nearshoring, the rising cost of manufacturing in China and India, along with transportation and energy, more manufacturing is coming back to North America,” said Mark Hoper, vice president of expositions and media, FMA, co-sponsor of FABTECH Mexico. “Considering these factors, Mexico is positioned well to support this positive growth and will continue to prosper in the future. This puts FABTECH Mexico in a very good position to continue its average 25 percent yearly growth.”
