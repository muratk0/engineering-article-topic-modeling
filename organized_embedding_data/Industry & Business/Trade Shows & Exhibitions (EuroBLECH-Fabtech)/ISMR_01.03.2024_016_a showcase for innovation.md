# A showcase for innovation

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

The sixth edition of Global Industrie returns to Paris Nord Villepinte, France, from 25-28 March 2024. This global showcase for industrial innovation is under the patronage of the French President, Emmanuel Macron. It will host several French Ministers, elected officials, ambassadors and representatives of large industrial European professional organisations.

"We expect to attract 2,300 exhibitors over 100,000m² of exhibition space with 1,500 machines in operation over four days," the show organiser, GL Events, told ISMR.

"Approximately 40,000 visitors are expected to attend. The trade show will highlight a range of solutions to address multiple challenges facing industry (such as the environmental and ecological transition; recruitment; social and societal responsibility; training; financing; Industry 5.0 etc.)," it added.

Fifteen zones and thirteen theme villages will showcase a range of technologies, processes, equipment and expertise across multiple sectors at the trade show.

"Machinery demonstrations will be at the heart of 'the biggest factory in France' to enable visitors to access the latest innovations and industrial processes. This will also be an opportunity for them to identify the trends that will transform tomorrow's industry. The Gi Awards, for their part, will highlight the most innovative discoveries identified among exhibitors," continued GL Events.

Global Industrie will also include several activities and facilities designed to “make industry attractive" for the younger generation who will become the future talents of manufacturing.

"GI Avenir and the Golden Tech, the competition for industrial excellence, will be the tools we use to make jobs attractive and meet recruitment needs. More than 5,000 young people and job seekers are expected at this 2024 edition," said GL Events.

Golden Tech promotes the attractiveness and diversity of jobs in industry through a competition offering an overall vision of industry. For four days, some one hundred of the men and women who are forging today's industry will compete in 16 rings, representing 16 professions and skills.

Numerous discussion areas will provide opportunities for information, exchange and technology monitoring. The Big Stage, between the two exhibition halls, will address key industrial themes and host well-known industry personalities. These include current and future-oriented themes (reindustrialisation; Industry 4.0; green industry; recruitment; financing; carbon reductions etc.) via debates, knowledge-sharing and best practice.
