## Don’t miss this year’s Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition from 16-21 September in Essen

The Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition, a premier global showcase for joining and cutting technology, will be held at Messe Essen in Germany from 16-21 September 2013. The latest technologies, devices, machines, materials and installations for welding and cutting as well as for the large number of allied processes such as adhesive bonding, brazing/soldering, thermal spraying or heat treatment will be under the spotlight during the show.

Visitors will be able to find the latest data on, for example, fully mechanised and automated processes such as industrial robots, data processing, testing systems and process controllers. The last Schweissen & Schneiden exhibition in 2009 attracted over 52,000 visitors and 1,016 exhibitors.

A comprehensive overview of the latest innovations in joining, cutting and coating technology awaits international trade visitors. Around 1,000 exhibitors from over 40 countries will present their technologies, services and materials at Messe Essen – many of them will be introduced to the trade public for the first time at the exhibition. The organiser, Messe Essen, and its partner, DVS – Deutscher Verband für Schweißen und verwandte Verfahren e.V. (“German Welding Society”), are expecting around 55,000 visitors from over 100 countries.

## Making the right connections

The largest group of exhibitors will be from Germany with 379 companies followed by China (170 exhibitors), Italy (96) and the USA (41). Overseas participation will be around 61 per cent, according to the organiser.

This year, there will be eleven international cooperative booths at the exhibition where trade visitors will be able to obtain information about country-specific ranges for welding and joining technologies. The USA will be represented with two booths and Chinese exhibitors will be present in Halls 7, 8.1 and 9.1. Other cooperative booths will be from France, South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, Brazil and Italy.

A twelfth cooperative booth will be devoted to thermal spraying.

On Booth A 120 in Hall 5, 13 member companies from GTS (Gemeinschaft Thermisches Spritzen e.V. – Association of Thermal Sprayers) will present their latest developments, including nine German companies, three Swiss companies and one Dutch company. On around 200 square metres, they will demonstrate the efficiency and diversity of thermal spraying technology. Areas of application are, for example, the manufacture of turbine blades, sealing rings and printing rollers. The objects which will be seen on the booth will include thermal spraying torches, innovative coating systems for agriculture and the automotive sector as well as turnkey installations for surfacing.

In Hall 9, an area of around 140 square metres (Booths A 133 – A 145) will be reserved for eight young companies and their innovative portfolios. The exhibited technologies will cover the entire spectrum of joining technology. The exhibits will include, for example, welding gap clamps for root welding, special nozzles and mobile lasers which can also be utilised on ships or drilling rigs. The companies will be promoted by the German Federal Ministry of Economic Affairs.

## A welding showcase

The exhibition is also a networking platform for the welding industry. Conferences, congresses, seminars, workshops and competitions will all take place during the exhibition. Immediately before the fair, the International Institute of Welding (IIW) will invite people to its Annual Assembly in the Congress Centre, Essen on 11-15 September 2013. Around 1,000 experts from all over the world will be expected at this event as well as at the two-day IIW Conference entitled “Automation in Welding” (16-17 September 2013). With 54 member countries, IIW is the largest worldwide network for welding technologies and allied processes.

At the DVS Congress 2013, during Schweissen & Schneiden, specialists will also introduce research results, market developments and solutions for manufacturers and users of joining, cutting and surfacing technology. The DVS Congress 2013 will be held at the DVS/GSI cooperative booth in Hall 12. The DVS (Deutscher Verband für Schweißen und verwandte Verfahren e. V. – German Welding Society) Students’ Congress, during Schweissen & Schneiden, will offer young scientists a platform for students and young engineers dealing with joining technology the opportunity to introduce their scientific papers in lectures.

In the young welders’ competitions, talented young welders will also be able to provide proof of their abilities in three competitions. The 10th DVS national “Young Welders Competition” will start on the first three days of the exhibition. Youngsters from 15 European countries will compete against each other in the WELDCUP competition. See www.ewf.be/weldcup/index.html for more details. An international competition with Chinese participation will also be on the agenda on the Friday of the exhibition.

Schweissen & Schneiden is also represented in the most important growth markets in the world with international sister events e.g. in Brazil, China, India, Russia and, from January 2013, in the United Arab Emirates. For example, the Brazil Welding Show (1-4 October) will be staged in 2013 and India Essen Welding & Cutting (28-30 October) is planned for 2014. According to the cycle, Arabia Essen Welding & Cutting will take place again in two years’ time (10-13 January 2015).

KASTO’s FutureDays 2013 attracted visitors from across Europe, including the UK, as well as Russia, India, South Africa and the USA

More than 600 trade visitors plus 152 journalists from across Europe, Russia, India, South Africa and the USA attended KASTO’s FutureDays 2013 open house exhibition. KASTO held the event at its factory and headquarters in Achern, southern Germany, from 14-17 May 2013. The company is well known for the manufacture of metal sawing machines, which can be linked to various types of automated materials handling and storage systems for bar, other long stock, sheet, plate and cut pieces.

Its computer-controlled storage systems are also produced as stand-alone products for stockholding and for feeding machine tools in factories, not just saws but also other types of machine such as lathes and laser profilers. The product range encompasses storage towers in compact, stand-alone design up to fully automatic honeycomb systems with thousands of storage cassettes.

## A global footprint

KASTO has delivered over 140,000 saws and 1,450 storage systems worldwide. Its factories in Achern and Schalkau employ 600 people, 10 per cent of whom are apprentices. 90 engineers work in R&D and 5 per cent of Kasto sales is invested in new product development, particularly with the BRIC countries.

Turnover in 2012 was 122.9 million euros, its third-best figure in the last ten years, while, in the current fiscal year, global sales are so far holding up despite weakened demand in Europe. Around half of its production is exported from Germany, including to the UK through its Milton Keynes-based daughter company. Subsidiaries are also located in the USA and France.

A key message at the FutureDays 2013 event was the continuous optimisation of lean production, logistics and business practices throughout the factory, together with the adoption of a new shop floor management system and a move towards larger batch manufacture to reduce unit production costs.

## Innovations on display

Sawing machines that were showcased ranged from the Workshop/Dealer range of bandsaws, circular saws and hack saws to a giant KASTOmaxcut AM 6x16 bandsaw for cutting aluminium blocks of up to 2200mm wide by 800mm high. This machine deployed a horizontal blade cutting horizontally to take slices off the top of the material to produce sheets, which were supported during sawing.

Other innovations included the latest evolution of the KASTOtec AM4 aluminium saw, capable of 1,200m/min band speed and 800mm/min infeed, and the KASTOtec AC 7 and 7x10 KPC bandsaws for use with carbide blades. There was an accent on aluminium cutting at the event, with plate and block saws (-plate and -cross) presented along with the -tec, -bbs and -maxcut machines.

On the storage system side of the business, FutureDays 2013 provided an opportunity to see the modular KASTOunitower C automated storage system for long stock, such as bar and tube. It is an entry-level tower store, joining its similarly named counterpart for accommodating sheet metal. The exhibition tower was connected directly to a saw, illustrating how manual handling could be eliminated. There were a total of seven storage and handling systems on show.

Aluminium was again a focus, with KASTOunitower, -portal, -top, -line and -compact systems promoted for storing long stock and/or sheet. Other handling highlights included a pedestal-mounted 6-axis robot for automated loading/unloading of a KASTOvariospeed circular saw, and a similar set-up where a robot performed deburring of cut pieces.

## Software, measurement and automation

The latest developments from KASTO in control technology; software including system simulation, virtual commissioning and value analysis; and human-machine interfaces for sawing machines and warehousing were also presented. KASTO has implemented over 300 systems worldwide that interface with customers’ computerised enterprise resource planning systems.

New, camera-based equipment for identification and measurement of cut pieces and other stock, various ‘green’ initiatives for reducing power consumption of KASTO products and a new internet tele-service were all introduced by a succession of expert speakers from the company.

Further material flow and automation units that were on display included sheet handling options, buffer stations and material magazines for lights-out production.
