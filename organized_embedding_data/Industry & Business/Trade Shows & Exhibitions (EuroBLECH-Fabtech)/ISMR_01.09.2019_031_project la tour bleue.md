# PROJECT / La Tour Bleue

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

Project: Tour Bleue des Cerclades, Parvis des 3 Fontaines, 95000 CERGY, France
Façade material: ALUCOBOND® A2 Teinte Spectra Spéciale : "Blaugoldeffekt"
Construction system: Tray Panels Special Design
Building Owner: France Habitation
Architects: Felletin Architecture
Fabricator / Installer: Vetisol, Stim Technibat
Year of construction: 2017
Copyright pictures: Thibault Savary

A 'blue' touch. At Cergy in France, la Tour Bleue (the Blue Tower) is an apartment building from the 1970s that has been given a modern aesthetic make-over with ALUCOBOND® A2 material, giving a blueish reflection to the building.

"There is a lot of colour change on the facade, as there are special pigments in the colour. Much depends upon the light and the position of those viewing the Blue Tower, as the colour will move and change (from light blue to grey, for example) as viewers move around the Tower. The round-shaped elements were bent on the 2m AMB Picot roll bending machine. The ALUCOBOND® A2 material is non-combustible and also offers good UV and wind/weather protection," Guntram Eydner, Head of New Products & Product Management, 3A Composites GmbH, told ISMR.
