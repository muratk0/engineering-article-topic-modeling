# Blechexpo in focus

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

ISMR enjoyed a strong presence at this year’s Blechexpo (sheet metal processing exhibition) and Schweisstec (trade fair for joining technology) from 3-6 November at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre in Germany. The organiser confirmed that the event exceeded all previous targets, attracting a total of 1234 exhibitors from 36 countries.

Overall exhibition floor space amounted to 90,000 square metres and 38,136 expert visitors from 110 countries visited the events. The number of exhibitors from inside and outside Germany increased by 7%, overall exhibition floor space rose by 6% and expert visitor numbers grew by 14%.

“Presentations covering the issues of cutting and forming technology, pipe and profile processing, as well as tooling and surface finishing, were met with great interest by experts from all levels who are involved with sheet metal, pipe and profile processing,” added the organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.

The next Blechexpo-Schweisstec trade fair will take place from 7 to 10 November 2017 and is expected to occupy the entire facility at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre. For the full show report, see page 16. Interviews from the show start on page 26.
