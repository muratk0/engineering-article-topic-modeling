# TR celebrates 50 years

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

The TR Fastenings' team was back exhibiting at Fastener Fair Global (21-23 March 2023 in Stuttgart, Germany) after four years since the previous event. This is a special year for TR, a Trifast plc Group Company, as it celebrates its 50th anniversary so the entire team was at the exhibition to mark the special occasion.

TR's stand carried a 50th golden theme to acknowledge this major milestone. Dedicated seating areas facilitated more in-depth discussions face-to-face with customers and two large TV screens showcased how fasteners are made from wire through to the completed product, explaining the different processes including heat treatment and finishings. These videos were produced in-house for training purposes and included animations illustrating where TR products are used. These are also available in the media library on the TR website.

“This show was very important to the whole TR team, who were keen to meet up with its 35 Master Distributors located throughout mainland Europe and thank them for their support in distributing our proprietary products including Hank Self-Clinch parts, Plastic and Rubber commodities and Fasteners for Plastic. There was also interest shown in our recently expanded manufacturing site, TR VIC, in Italy," the fastening specialist told ISMR.

Managing Brexit, to ensure that there were no supply issues, necessitated opening a central warehouse hub in Verl, Germany. This is close to the TR Kuhlmann site, and TR Kuhlmann managing director, Peter Henning, oversaw this successful project. He enhanced his sales team to manage the increased business and it continues to work with TR's distributor team in the UK, which has long-term relationships with many of these Master Distributors.

To thank them for their support, and the growth that TR is seeing, TR hosted a 50th dinner celebration for 50 people during one of the evenings. TR Board members attended and Adam John, European Distribution Sales Manager, hosted the event.

“Traditionally, this exhibition has been a distributor show and the team was very pleased to see high-profile OEMs attending, particularly from the automotive sector. More than 350 visitors spent time with our teams, and we have already secured sizeable new business wins and forged new relationships," TR concluded.

Established in 1973, TR Fastenings is the trading business of Trifast plc and is a global specialist in the design, engineering, manufacture and distribution of industrial fastenings and Category ‘C' components (principally to major global assembly industries). Its own brand products include Hank®Rivet Bushes; Hank® Self Clinch Fasteners; Blind Rivet Nut; Binx® Self-Locking Nuts; Security Fasteners; Cable Management and Enclosure Hardware products. Worldwide operations span 34 locations in the UK, Asia, Europe and the USA including seven highvolume manufacturing sites.■
