# WIN EURASIA 2018

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

Venue:
TUYAP Fair Convention and Congress Centre, Istanbul, Turkey 
Dates:
15-18 March 2018

Visiting Hours:
10:00-18:30 (15-17 March 2018)   
10.00 – 17.30 (18 March 2018)

The six trade fairs under the WIN EURASIA Metalworking and WIN EURASIA Automation banner this year are:-

- CeMAT EURASIA: For transport, handling and storage solutions. Exhibitors from around the world will present logistics systems; cranes and forklift trucks; packaging technologies; shelving systems; logistics IT and electronics in intralogistics at CeMAT EURASIA.

- Electrotech EURASIA: Electrical energy generation systems, power supply, transmission, distribution and storage come under the spotlight at Electrotech EURASIA. Various products, such as smart and refined safety relays and electronic components, will be showcased throughout the fair.

- Metalworking EURASIA: According to IGEME (Export Promotion Centre of Turkey), Turkey's exported machine tools are mainly metal forming systems (77% share of machinery exports), with the rest as metal cutting systems.
Metal working industry products will be showcased here (metal forming, metal cutting and metal working sectors).

- SurfaceTechnology EURASIA: This focuses on all core solutions for surface treatment and finishing. The exhibitor product range varies from cleaning to pre-treatment; paint; polish and coating systems to hardware and software solutions.

- Integrated Automation, Motion & Drives (IAMD): IAMD will feature a full range of industrial automation, transmission and fluid power solutions.

## Welding EURASIA: The significance and competitive power of the welding, shearing and joining sector, a significant cornerstone of industry in Turkey and Eurasia, will be showcased here.

## Special display areas

Young and innovative companies will get together at the STARTUP Special Area at this year's WIN EURASIA exhibition. It will be arranged this year as an area where companies can present their ideas and entrepreneurship activities and develop potential collaborations. There will also be a German pavilion at the show.

The Industry 4.0 Festival will also take place at WIN Eurasia Automation 2018 in Hall 14. Siemens is back as the "Industry 4.0 Solution Partner", whose joint venture increased visitor interest at the event in 2017. New additions such as a robotic area; fora; panels and associations' (ENOSAD, Industry 4.0 platform) "Industry 4.0" panel with global keynote speakers will also be held.

The Digital Factory - IIoT Special Area is a new area for digital visionaries in manufacturing. Big Data; Cloud-based services and M2M technologies; integrated products; new solutions and services will be exhibited in the Digital Factory - IIoT Special Area. Visitors can also discover compressed air and vacuum technologies at a Special Display Area at WIN EURASIA, along with the parts2clean pavilion.

For a rounded view of manufacturing in Eurasia, visit the WIN EURASIA event this March.
