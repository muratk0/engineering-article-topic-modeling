## Sustainability and energy reduction

In addition to innovative technologies and technical highlights in the exhibition halls, the Wire and Tube Expert Meeting will be held for the first time on 20 June 2022 (2pm). International experts will report from their companies and present their machines, systems and the latest production methods.

The motto of the expert meeting organised by Messe Düsseldorf is: “The steel industry is forging ahead with the green transformation". The event is organised by the Stahl-Kommunikation agency and will be held in Room 15, South Entrance, Hall No. 1 of Messe Düsseldorf.

Sustainability and green issues are of key importance at the trade fairs.

“How green are the wire, cable, tube and pipe industries? How sustainably do they produce and how environmentally friendly is the handling of the materials used? These are questions that the new ecoMetals campaign wants to demonstrate through guided tours. During the Wire and Tube trade fair, trade visitors will be offered daily guided tours, or ecoMetals-trails, to exhibitors for whom the terms sustainability, energy efficiency and resource conservation are lived practice," explained Messe Düsseldorf.

"At their exhibition stands, they will demonstrate how new technologies, machines and systems in their production facilities improve the energy and carbon dioxide balance to minimise their companies' ecological footprint. They will also explain to visitors how they manage to reconcile economy and ecology in their production and process chains. This seems to be more important than ever to survive in international competition and to remain a sought-after business partner," it added.

The meeting point for all free tours is the ecoMetals information counter at the North Entrance of the Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre.

## Versatile applications

Tubes and pipes are some of the most versatile products and design elements in general. Whether in the energy sector; mechanical; power; automotive engineering; chemical; petrochemical; construction or other areas, tubes and tube designs in different shapes, sizes and weights can be found everywhere. Steel tubes and pipes cover a particularly broad spectrum, as these products are conquering new applications partly due to the innovative strength of manufacturers. However, thanks to new and further developments, they are also maintaining a strong position in traditional applications.

In the energy sector, pipes have become indispensable for the transportation of oil and gas, while also playing a major role as design elements. In fact, most structural foundations of offshore wind turbines consist of pipe elements. Pipes also fulfil important functions in the offshore production of oil and gas.

"Take, for example, new developments for automotive engineering. In non-motor applications, tubes with variable wall thicknesses can be used to support load-optimised lightweight construction," commented Messe Düsseldorf.

## Automotive sector opportunities

Electric vehicles are gaining strong momentum in the automotive market. High growth rates in new registrations are already driving the cable and wire industry to invest in more modern technology.

In Europe alone, electric vehicle sales have nearly tripled since 2019, reports the "AlixPartners Global Automotive Outlook 2021." Investment is rocketing as governments promote the switch to electromobility. Fourteen EU countries and China reportedly plan to ban the sale of internal combustion vehicles by 2035 at the latest.

“The market potential is huge," exhibitor Wafios underlined. It develops, designs and manufactures machines for the wire and tube processing industry, as well as for cold forming, and is accelerating its transformation in modern drives by investing ten million euros in a new e-mobility campus at its former Südwerk site in Reutlingen, Germany. In the future, Wafios' electromobility activities will be bundled in the 1,800-square-metre campus "to jointly offer the global automotive industry complete turnkey production lines for hairpin stators."

Sheet metal and laser specialist TRUMPF also sees electromobility as a growth-promoting stimulus. Copper is considered a key material for conducting electricity and is indispensable in an electric car. TRUMPF's laser can be used to weld copper for the high-performance electronics of electric cars.

"The shift towards electromobility offers great opportunities for the German industry," emphasised Christian Schmitz, Managing Director for Laser Technology, TRUMPF.
