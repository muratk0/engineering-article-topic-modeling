# ITM Industry Europe unveils 2022 plans

[TARİH: 01.11.2021 ISMR]

Preparations are now underway for the next edition of the ITM Industry Europe trade fair in Poland, with its slogan 'Industry of the Digital Age'. It covers machine tools for metals; industrial automation; robotics; tools; foundry; surface treatment; additive technologies; software and industrial metrology.

"The event-in line with the industry's expectations - will be held in late spring, as in previous years. From 31 May to 3 June 2022, the exhibition halls at the MTP Poznan Expo will be filled with modern machinery and equipment for Industry 4.0," the organiser told ISMR.

Alongside the event, visitors will also be able to visit exhibitions such as the Modernlog Logistics, Warehousing and Transport Fair; the Subcontracting Industrial Cooperation Fair and the Focast Foundry Forum. On the first and second day of the trade fair, the second edition of the 'Industry Next' congress will take place.

This year, it was the ITM Industry Europe trade fair that kicked off the industry meeting season in Europe at the end of August. It was the first exhibition event for the sector after a break of several months caused by the pandemic. Over 340 exhibitors from Poland and overseas showcased their products and services at the four-day event.

"Market data confirm that industrial robotics and digitisation are doing well and, indeed, have helped many companies to survive in these difficult times. There will certainly be no shortage of these topics at ITM. For several years now, there has been a huge interest in automation, digitalisation and robotisation among managers of manufacturing companies. We are already planning not just panel discussions with the biggest leaders in this sector, but also a diverse exhibition of solutions and devices dedicated to smart factories," Anna Lemarska-Kramer, the event's director, told ISMR.

Robotisation is on the rise. The digital factory combines existing production technology with the intelligent technology of networked communication and data processing systems. The cyber-physical systems used in it monitor processes and can make decisions - it is a vision of a digital factory that is slowly becoming a reality around the world.

"We will certainly show these trends at the Fair. We realize that there is much to be done in robotisation in Poland, and the costs associated with digitisation are high. However if the government's plans are confirmed, robotisation relief will be part of a package of legislative changes. According to current assumptions, it will entitle manufacturers to a deduction from the tax base of 50% of tax-deductible costs incurred in a given year for this purpose. We are keeping our finger on the pulse and taking note of current solutions," commented Anna Lemanska-Kramer.

"We will invite decision-makers to discuss this topic as part of the Industry Next Congress, the first edition of which was a great success this year. The next edition will also feature current opportunities for financial support for companies, operating in the industrial sector, who are planning to invest in the latest technologies," she added.

For the 2022 edition of the trade fair, a continuation of the SMART FACTORY zone rich in intelligent solutions for factories is planned. The STARTUP ZONE will be open for 'pioneering solutions in the spirit of Industry 4.0', which have already passed the testing phase.

"More and more 'young' technology initiatives will appear such as digital twin, AI, Big Data or 5G for companies operating in the industry. We want to enable successful launches of new applications, technologies or machines and initiate successful business ideas," explained Anna Lemarska-Kramer.
