ANOTHER SUCCESSFUL GOLF4MFG FUNDRAISER

Aug. 24 marked the third annual Golf4MFGfundraiser benefiting the programs of Nuts, Bolts & Thingamajigs

®

(NBT), the foundation of FMA. Eighty-four golfers played on the Arnold Palmer course at Geneva National Resort in Lake Geneva, Wis. Six corporate sponsors made this event possible: Turner Construction, Mitsubishi Laser, FREEMAN, CNA, Hawkeye Energy Solutions, and Northland Laser.

One Fabulous Day on the Course Means More Scholarships and Summer Camps for Students

The day began with a morning tour of Dalco Metals in Walworth, Wis. This one-stop resource for steel processing features a fully automated laser with storage tower. Afterward Geneva National’s golf pro shared her tips with participants to help them hone their skills before hitting the links. Lunch was served in the pavilion, followed by the shotgun start.

Players spent the rest of the day enjoying strategic golf through the natural wetlands and water features of the Palmer course. Prizes were awarded and dinner was served in the Trevino room, where NBT recognized three donor companies with a "Big Check" presentation. A very special thank you to the CNA Foundation, Kelly Services, and Production Tube Cutting Inc. for their generous gifts. A special thank you goes to Turner Construction for donating their foursome to veterans of the armed forces.

Congratulations to the winning team of the golf scramble: Craig Summers, Gregg Simpson, Sarah Richards, and Troy Berg. Other contest winners were Tim Triplett, straightest drive; Dave Kennedy, closest to the pin; Sarah Richards, longest drive; and Tim Knauf, longest putt. To view more photos, visit NBT’s Facebook page.

The real winners are the kids who participate in NBT summer manufacturing camps and the young adults who can start training for their manufacturing careers because of an NBT scholarship. Thank you to everyone who participated and believes in the mission of the foundation. If you were not able to join us, please consider making a tax-deductible charitable donation of your own at

nutsandboltsfoundation.org/donate

.

20th annual salary/wage & benefit report set to be released

One hundred twenty-three companies in the metal processing, forming, and fabricating industry completed an extensive survey on employment practices, benefit packages, and salaries and wages for 67 production and administrative positions. This is the 20th year FMA has collected the anonymously reported information. Broken the country, and area population, the "Salary/Wage & Benefit Survey" allows you to make valid comparisons with your competitors who share similar demographics.
