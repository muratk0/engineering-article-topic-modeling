# FABTECH tackles future workforce strategies

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

FABTECH, a North American event for metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing, will take place in Orlando, Florida (USA), from 15-17 October 2024.

"This year's special events at FABTECH will feature a dynamic line-up of industry leaders, innovators and visionaries who will challenge attendees to rethink their approaches, explore new technologies and embrace innovative practices. The sessions will explore the rise of women in manufacturing leadership; advanced manufacturing techniques; digital solutions; AI and robotics; steel production and strategies for workforce development, while also offering insights into global economics and the current state of the industry," said the show organiser.

Tracy Garcia, CEM, Group Director-FABTECH Series, SME, said: "FABTECH 2024 is designed to ignite change and drive advancements across the manufacturing sector. With an extensive line-up of influential speakers, we aim to provide attendees with the knowledge and inspiration to embrace new technologies and innovative practices."

FABTECH offers a comprehensive conference with 60-90-minute sessions as well as full- and half-day workshops covering advanced fabrication and welding technology, workforce development and management topics. Attendees can customise their agenda from 14 tracks, gaining practical knowledge to address specific manufacturing and production challenges.

FABTECH's Leadership Exchange Panel will explore current trends, challenges and innovations in metal fabrication. Leaders will discuss the impact of global economic dynamics, processing technology and AI on the industry. Attendees will gain insights into how companies are navigating these challenges and preparing for the future.

"The new FABTECH Emerging Leaders Programme has been designed to equip young professionals and rising stars with the tools and confidence to shape their future and drive positive change in their organisations and the industry. Featuring an opening keynote luncheon, interactive workshop, guided show floor tours, networking opportunities and more, the three-day programme will act as a launchpad for those ready to elevate their careers to new heights," added the show organiser.

FABTECH 2024 will feature an expansive exhibition floor with over 1,500 exhibitors showcasing their latest products and technologies. The 800,000 square-foot exhibit space offers an opportunity to explore innovations in metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing. Attendees can see live demonstrations, interact with suppliers and discover new developments designed to improve productivity and increase profits.
