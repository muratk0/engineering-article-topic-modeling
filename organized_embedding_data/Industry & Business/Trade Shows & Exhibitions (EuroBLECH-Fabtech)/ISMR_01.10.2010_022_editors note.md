# EDITOR’S NOTE

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

VDW is the organiser of the EMO Hanover exhibition in 2011.

From 19 to 24 September 2011, international manufacturers of production technology will be presenting “More than Machine Tools” at the EMO Hanover exhibition. The entire spectrum of modern metalworking technology is covered - the fair includes the latest machines and efficient technical solutions, product-related services, sustainability in manufacturing and much more.

The main focus of the exhibition is on cutting and forming machine tools, manufacturing systems, precision tools, automated material flow, computer technology, industrial electronics, and parts and accessories.

Registration documents for EMO Hannover 2011 have just recently been sent out to more than 60 countries worldwide, and the registration deadline for exhibitors has been set for December of this year. For the first time ever, exhibitors can also register online at www.emo-hannover.de.
