# TIMTOS 2023 welcomes global visitors

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

The Taipei International Machine Tool Show in Taiwan, also known as TIMTOS, will return from 6-11 March 2023 to Nangang Exhibition Centre, Hall 1 & 2 and TWTC Hall 1. ISMR is a media partner at the show and its issues will be distributed at the show entrances.

"With 1,000+ exhibitors in nearly 6,100 booths, on an even larger scale than 2022, TIMTOS 2023 will be the largest trade show ever recorded after Taiwan recently lifted all its border controls since the COVID-19 outbreak. At present, overseas professionals are free to travel to Taiwan to participate in trade shows as usual. As a result, there have been increasing demand for larger booth space at TIMTOS 2023 and delegations to the show will arrive from countries such as Vietnam; Turkey and India etc.," added TAITRA, one of the show organisers.

In a press conference for TIMTOS 2023 recently, the show organisers (Simon Wang, President & CEO, TAITRA) and Larry Wei (Chairman of TAMI) unveiled the highlights of TIMTOS 2023.

TAITRA CEO Wang pointed out that the new slogan for TIMTOS 'Keep Rolling in Metalworking' highlighted the show's claim “to link up the global machine tool ecosystem to make metal processing smarter, more efficient and more sustainable."

Taiwan's machine-tool industry has become more resilient since the pandemic, continuing to address intelligent transformation and adding value to its products. TIMTOS, which gathers most Taiwanese players in the machine-tool sector, is a key event for those looking for innovative products or services developed by Taiwanese manufacturers.

TIMTOS 2023 will accommodate more than 6,000 booths to showcase a full line of metal-cutting machines; controllers; key components; smart manufacturing solutions and more. Taiwan is a major producer in the machine-tool industry with around 1,950 companies based on the island and more than 43,000 people employed in the sector. It is well known for its customisation and innovation.

“Taiwan ranked third in the world's machine-tool exports in 2021. Total exports of Taiwan machine tools in 2021 reached US$ 2.78 billion, a phenomenal increase of 29.1% compared with 2020. In addition, over 50% growth was recorded in Turkey, the Netherlands and Italy. Unsurprisingly, in anticipation of continuing global demand, Taiwan's machine-tool outputs will increase in the years to come, while supply chain restructuring has generated new business opportunities," added the show organisers.

TIMTOS 2023 will debut three new themed areas featuring “Advanced Metalworking Technology," "Additive Manufacturing" and “Future Manufacturing”. Events at TIMTOS 2023 include the Future Manufacturing Forum; Corporate Carbon Footprints Consultation; Podcast Live; Maker Workshop and Guided Tours.■
