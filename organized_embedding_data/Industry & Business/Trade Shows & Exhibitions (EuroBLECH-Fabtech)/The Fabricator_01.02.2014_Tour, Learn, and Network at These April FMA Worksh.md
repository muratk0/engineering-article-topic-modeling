Roll With the Experts at World-Class Roll Forming Workshop

Strengthen your skills and knowhow with a comprehensive industry update at FMA’s World-Class Roll Forming Workshop April 21-23, 2014, in Cleveland, Ohio.

Designed with smaller workshop sessions for more interaction, World-Class features roll forming experts who are committed to answering questions, providing solutions, and giving insights to help improve your operation.

You can take full advantage of the presenters’ experience when spending an evening with them at a Networking With the Experts Reception. Bring your parts, drawings, and challenges to get the solutions you need. Added this year are special showroom tours at Formtek Inc. and at Roll-Kraft, the exclusive sponsor of the workshop. Also included is an insider’s look at American Roll Form Products and Superior Roll Forming Co. during two additional tours.

Registration is $695 for FMA/ TPA members and $895 general.

Share Best Practices at the 6th Annual Safety Conference

The 2014 Safety Conference will be held in Rock Island/East Moline (Quad Cities), Ill. April 23-24, 2014. An excellent forum for sharing the successes and challenges of your company’s safety program with industry peers is provided through interactive presentations and a reception and dinner honoring FMA/ CNA Annual Safety Awards winners.

Additional highlights include a plant tour of John Deere Harvester Works, where Deere’s largest farm equipment is manufactured, and an opening conference address by Deere’s associate director of global work and life safety. Learn how you can improve the safety record at your company from those who share the same challenges!

Registration is $395 for FMA/ TPA members and $595 general.

Upgrade Your Bending Operation by Optimizing Management and Technology Processes

BendFab Workshop & Tour will be hosted by FMA member company BEGNEAUD Manufacturing Inc. — a comprehensive sheet metal service and solutions provider, passionate about technology, that pushes the envelope in design and fabrication — April 24, 2014, in Lafayette, La.

This event is intended to be a strategic learning opportunity for owners and team leaders who make decisions about bending equipment and procedures. During a plant tour, general sessions, panel discussions, and roundtable exchanges, you’ll pick up the latest in production practices. Find out what your peers in the panel bending/press brake/ folding industry are doing!

Registration is $295 for FMA/ TPA members and $495 general.

See Advanced Technology in Action at the 2014 Coil Processing Workshop & Tours

Gain a fresh perspective on today’s competitive market by attending FMA’s 2014 Coil Processing Workshop & Tours April 28-30 in Merrillville, Ind., three days packed with five plant tours and multiple technical presentations from industry leaders.

Whether your expertise is in slitting, blanking, or leveling, you’ll acquire technical know-how and discover practical solutions to your toughest processing challenges. Industry experts will share the newest innovative techniques.

The five plant tours in the Portage/Burns Harbor, Ind., area include a steel mill and several processing plants, giving attendees a chance to see equipment advancements in action.

Registration is $595 for FMA/ TPA members and $795 general.
