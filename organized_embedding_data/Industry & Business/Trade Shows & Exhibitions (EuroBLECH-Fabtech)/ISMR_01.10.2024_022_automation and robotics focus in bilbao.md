# Automation and robotics focus in Bilbao

[TARİH: 01.10.2024 ISMR]

In response to the growing influence of robotics and automation in different sectors of advanced industry, weAR is an international specialist event aimed at solving technical problems in user industries for smart manufacturing. The event, taking place every two years, will be held for the first time from 3-5 June 2025 at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre (Spain) as part of +INDUSTRY.

"The new event will provide a great opportunity for automation and robotics companies to promote themselves and make contact with a wide range of user industries. It will be held as part of the +INDUSTRY trade fairs, with leading sectors of advanced manufacturing represented, at BeDIGITAL; ADDITßD; MAINTENANCE; PUMPS&VALVES; SUBCONTRATACIÓN and weAR," commented show organisers, STECH (the Spanish Association of Intelligent Technologies for the Manufacturing Industry) and the Bilbao Exhibition Centre.

"Likewise, the WORKinn employment forum will continue to respond to the challenge of finding talent in the industry.

With a format that includes an exhibition area, workshop area, demonstrations, technical visits and networking spaces, weAR aims to become a benchmark for robotics and automation. It will represent the full spectrum of intelligent and digital automation," they added.

The weAR exhibition area will mainly host companies from the Iberian Peninsula, France and North Africa and will showcase new technologies and innovations.■
