# Immersive cutting experience at EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.09.2024 ISMR]

Hypertherm Associates, a US-based manufacturer of industrial cutting systems and software, will be exhibiting at EuroBLECH, the world's largest trade exhibition for the sheet metal working industry.

"From 22-25 October 2024, at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds in Germany, our multi-technology booth will deliver an immersive experience to visitors. It will explore Hypertherm® plasma, the versatility of OMAX® waterjet and the integration of sophisticated software, all designed to enhance productivity and performance in metalworking. After the immersive experience, eventgoers will be guided to our partner booths to see live cutting, ask questions and exchange ideas," explained the company.

"We are excited to connect with our valued partners and end users at this year's show," added André Rombouts, Hypertherm Associates' regional director for Europe, the Middle East, India and Africa. “Our booth is more than just a showcase; it is a dynamic digital experience designed to inform visitors and leave a lasting impression. Together with our partners, we are dedicated to guiding our customers to the ideal industrial cutting solutions tailored to their requirements and optimising their operations with our advanced automated systems."

At EuroBLECH 2024, Hypertherm Associates will bring together its technologies under one roof in Hall 13 (stand: E158) where visitors can explore its full spectrum of industrial cutting solutions. This includes the new XPR460® plasma system with X-Definition® technology for cutting versatility/power; the Powermax45 SYNC® (part of the plasma series), with a new single-piece consumable cartridge designed to decrease operating costs with, according to Hypertherm, up to five times longer consumable life.

In the OMAX waterjet range, EuroBLECH visitors will also discover the OptiMAX® and ProtoMAX® waterjet systems. Various software solutions for industrial cutting will also be on display, such as ProNest® CAD/ CAM software (designed to streamline nesting and improve cutting efficiency in plasma, waterjet and laser cutting operations). It features SureCut™ technology with embedded expertise for XPR and HPR® plasma systems.

'Robotmaster® robotic software offers advanced robot programming capabilities to boost automation and productivity in manufacturing. It is used for programming various applications including cutting, welding, grinding and more," concluded Hypertherm.

Founded in 1968, Hypertherm Associates is a 100 per cent Associate-owned company, employing approximately 2,000 Associates, with operations and partner representation worldwide.
