# EUROBLECH GOES DIGITAL! Connect with the global sheet metalworking industry at the EuroBLECH Digital Innovation Summit from 27-30 October 2020.

[TARİH: 01.09.2020 ISMR]

THE EUROBLECH DIGITAL Innovation Summit is a new online meeting place for the global sheet metal working community. This interactive networking event will take place from 27-30 October 2020. Exhibitors will present innovative products, machinery and technologies whilst matchmaking features will create business opportunities by enabling users to connect and conduct business with companies in the sheet metal working industry around the world.

“The Digital Innovation Summit is a week packed with interactive opportunities to meet, conduct business, discover latest machinery and hear about the latest trends and developments on a custom-designed digital platform. All activities are grouped into three main focus areas: Discover, Connect and Learn,” EuroBLECH organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

“Visitor participation is free and registration for the event starts on 1 October 2020. Opening times are 8am to 4pm GMT."

## Network for success

EuroBLECH is the largest exhibition for the sheet metal working industry worldwide and a key global marketplace for visitors to discover and source the latest innovative manufacturing solutions. Numerous live demonstrations at exhibition stands offer trade visitors the opportunity to experience machines and systems from all areas of sheet metal working in action.

At EuroBLECH in 2018, a total of 56,307 visitors from around the world attended the show in Hanover for an overview of the latest innovations and digital technologies for sheet metal working and to invest in new manufacturing machinery. A total of 1,507 companies from 40 countries exhibited at the event. The next EuroBLECH exhibition in Hanover, Germany, will take place from 9-12 March 2021.

The EuroBLECH exhibition profile covers fifteen technology sectors and covers the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; handling; separation; forming; flexible sheet metal working; tube/ section processing; joining; welding; additive manufacturing; surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; quality control; CAD/CAM/CIM systems; factory and warehouse equipment as well as R&D.

The new Digital Innovation Summit for EuroBLECH this year places these opportunities onto a virtual platform, in line with the industry’s movement towards digitisation and automation in modern sheet metal processing.

“Digital transformation is playing a major role in the industry, enabling increased efficiency and higher levels of automation and predictive maintenance. Industry 4.0 and Smart Factory have become major topics in sheet metal working and are important areas for small and mediumsized companies planning to invest in these technologies in the future to gain competitive advantage,” said Evelyn Warwick, Exhibition Director, EuroBLECH.

At the new EuroBLECH Digital Innovation Summit, we have grouped the key elements of the event under the three pillars of ‘Discover’, ‘Connect’ and ‘Learn’.

## DISCOVER

AT THE EUROBLECH virtual stand and presentation theatre during the Summit, exhibitors will promote their business, products, contacts and links to their website and social media platforms.

Using an exhibitor directory and virtual stands, visitors will be able to browse company profiles and discover which companies are offering the right solutions for their business requirements. Exhibitors’ products and services, product videos, contact information and more will be available. Visitors can request meetings or use the chat facility to find out more about machines, products and services.

Discover the latest innovations by viewing the product showcases of EuroBLECH exhibitors. They contain details of the products and services provided. This allows visitors to quickly find the products that they need and make direct contact with the respective companies offering those services.

## CONNECT

“THE MATCHMAKING system, during the Summit, uses the latest AI technology, providing visitors with relevant recommendations and potential business contacts,” explained Mack Brooks Exhibitions. Visitors to the Digital Innovation Summit will be able to schedule 20-minute, one-to-one meetings with companies before the event – with no third-party software required. Once a meeting has been agreed by both parties, a time can be set for a video call and files can be shared via the chat feature.

## LEARN

“EUROBLECH webinars will run daily from 27-30 October 2020 and will provide an opportunity for the global sheet metal working industry to gain insights into the latest industry knowledge, technical expertise, applications and solutions,” added the event organiser.

In addition, some sessions will focus on the sheet metal working industry in key geographical areas combined with in-depth overviews of the challenges these areas are facing and how they are adapting during the COVID-19 pandemic.

These sessions will last around one hour each and consist of a number of short presentations followed by a live Q&A session.

The presentation theatre will be open every morning and afternoon for up to two hours. Each scheduled 15-minute presentation will feature a live Q&A session for questions. After the session has closed, presentations will be available to stream on demand.
