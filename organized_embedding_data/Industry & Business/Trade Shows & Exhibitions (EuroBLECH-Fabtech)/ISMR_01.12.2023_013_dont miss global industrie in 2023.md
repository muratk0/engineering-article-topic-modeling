# Don't miss Global Industrie in 2023

[TARİH: 01.01.2023 ISMR]

From 7-10 March 2023, Global Industrie will once again take place in Lyon as a French showcase for industrial innovation with more new developments and solutions.

"Global Industrie brings together all industry players in one place. Divided into 15 zones, specialising in fields such as digitalisation and automation; electronics; additive manufacturing & 3D printing; measurement; robotics; energy and sustainable development (to name a few), our 2,300 exhibitors can be relied on to be at this large international gathering," the show organiser commented.

"Global Industrie will be honoured to host not only the Chambers of Commerce and Industry of Bourgogne Franche-Comté and Seine-et-Marne and CCI France Internationale (Union of French CCls abroad) but also the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Pavilion; Aveiro Regional Industrial Association (Portugal) and AIMMAP (Associação Dos Industriais Metalúrgicos Metalomecânicos E Afins De Portugal)," it added.

The Global Industrie awards will honour new exhibitor innovations. The show's priority in 2023, the organiser told ISMR, will be “to provide concrete answers to the contemporary needs of its players." Concrete solutions could include innovations developed by companies, new ideas from overseas or aid and support schemes offered by the state and local authorities (whether reshoring; job creation; the environmental transition; regions or industry appeal to young people).

“This Lyon edition will be a genuine platform [to showcase] the efforts of manufacturers battling ever more inventively against the crisis gripping Europe, and will answer the huge question: how can we respond to such a complex international situation whose repercussions directly affect French production chains?" it explained.

“At a time when everyone is facing growing problems every day, Global Industrie is committed to supporting manufacturers and will offer numerous avenues for industry to emerge stronger. The industry is mobilising. The industrial sector responded, from the start of the COVID-19 crisis and supported the national production effort, just as it responded to the recovery plan presented by the French government in the process. Its actors, all sectors combined, have also been invested in innovation for many years to meet the challenges of tomorrow," it concluded.■
