# Voortman experience centre

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

VOORTMAN Steel Machinery has opened a new showroom and training facility in Rijssen, the Netherlands. All the capabilities of Voortman beam, plate, flat and angle processing machinery can now be demonstrated in the 2500m2 showroom. Next to the showroom, the Voortman Experience Centre houses 1100m2 of office space with modern meeting rooms as well as an auditorium for presentations to bigger groups.

Throughout the year, Voortman will organise several events focusing on specific issues and processes which are of interest to visiting groups. It will also organise training days for machine operators. See http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ce1C7t5v1pk ■
