# FABTECH IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

Don't miss FABTECH 2022 in Atlanta, USA, from 8-10 November 2022.

North America's largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event heads to the Georgia World Congress Centre in Atlanta from 8-10 November 2022. Live equipment demonstrations, featured keynotes, expert panels and other special events will allow attendees the opportunity to interact with a large gathering of technical experts and visionaries to learn what's new and what's next in manufacturing.

There are five FABTECH Event Partners: the American Welding Society (AWS); the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA); SME; the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and the Chemical Coaters Association International.

“FABTECH provides a convenient 'one-stop shop' venue where you can meet with world-class suppliers, see the latest industry products and developments, and find the tools to improve productivity, increase profits and discover new solutions to all of your metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs," the event organisers told ISMR.

## Show highlights

FABTECH 2022 will encompass three halls at the Georgia World Congress Centre where attendees will find a variety of technology pavilions dedicated to Forming & Fabricating, Tube & Pipe, METALFORM, Welding, Finishing, 3D/Additive Manufacturing and Robotics & Industrial Automation.

FABTECH 2021 welcomed over 24,000 attendees from across the United States to Chicago with long-awaited opportunities to experience product evaluation, in-person networking, education and collaborative discussions on key topics impacting the industry. Three exhibit halls hosted close to 1,000 exhibitors (including 93 new exhibitors), demonstrating over 400 new products and highlighting emerging trends and technologies.

FABTECH also offers a comprehensive conference with targeted technical, operational, economic and managerial sessions. A variety of networking events and competitions also form part of FABTECH.

The American Welding Society (AWS) will present a comprehensive lineup of welding education. Led by top professionals, programmes will focus on best practices and new commercial developments in welding and thermal spray. Events include conferences; seminars; RWMA Resistance Welding School; a professional programme; society events and more.

"Individuals who attend AWS education programmes are awarded 1 PDH (Professional Development Hour) for each hour of education programme attendance. Individuals seeking FMA Recertification Credits will be awarded two credits for each conference session attended (forming & fabricating, cutting or finishing tracks) plus an additional two credits for attending the show. Individuals who attend SME education programmes may be eligible to receive one credit per hour attended towards their SME-managed re-certification requirements," commented the FABTECH organisers.

FABTECH also offers a series of quarterly short webinars, featuring a diverse and dynamic line-up of expert speakers who will discuss relevant topics to help manufacturers navigate through 2022 and beyond. Each webinar will be 30-45 minutes, followed by time for Q&A with presenters.
