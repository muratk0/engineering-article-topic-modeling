# FABTECH Canada returns for first time in four years

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

FABTECH Canada, the country's largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event, has announced its return from 14-16 June 2022 at the Toronto Congress Centre.

"Over three days, FABTECH Canada attendees will connect with suppliers, see the latest industry products and developments, find the tools to improve productivity and discover the latest manufacturing solutions," the show organiser told ISMR.

John Catalano, SME Senior Director, FABTECH, commented: "More than ever before, our goal is to deliver a show that encourages collaboration and networking, equips manufacturing professionals with the latest technological advancements and provides an immersive education platform to discover new techniques and trends that will improve productivity and the bottom line."

FABTECH Canada's 80,000 square foot show floor will give attendees access to over 200 exhibiting companies showcasing new innovations covering close to 40 technology focus areas (including cutting; lasers; press brakes; robotics and welding machines).

“Attendees will experience live, hands-on equipment demonstrations and connect with thousands of experts and peers during the event's networking activities," added the organiser.

FABTECH Canada offers a line-up of keynote speakers, panel discussions and educational programmes. Around 50+ conference sessions and workshops will be led by industry thought-leaders.

This year, FABTECH Canada is partnering with the CWB Group, an industry-supported private sector organisation providing welding certification, management systems registration and training services, who will hold its CanWeld 2022 Conference in conjunction with FABTECH Canada on 15-16 June 2022. The goal for this year's conference is to educate attendees on the latest welding technologies as well as current issues affecting the welding industry.
