# Meet ISMR at SteelFab

[TARİH: 01.01.2018 ISMR]

THE 14TH SteelFab 2018 will be organised and hosted by Expo Centre Sharjah (UAE), with the support of the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry, from 15-18 January 2018. It will showcase machinery and equipment in metalforming; surface preparation & finishing; grinding & cutting; machining and other allied engineering disciplines. It will also feature Italian, German and Taiwan pavilions, and enjoys support from leading industry associations from Italy, Germany, Taiwan, the UK and the UAE.

Above and below: Scenes from SteelFab 2017

SteelFab 2017 attracted 342 exhibitors from nearly 30 countries as well as 7,950 visitors from 83 countries, during its four-day run. SteelFab 2018 aims to attract large numbers of steel industry professionals and decision-makers among exhibitors and visitors, and also features a seminar programme. SteelFab 2018 will also offer special segments such as Wear Resistant; Welding & Cutting and Machine Tools; seminars and technical presentations and live displays. The Third Fasteners World Middle East will also be held, a dedicated vertical for the complete range of industrial fasteners and fixings, assembly and installation systems, fastener manufacturing technology as well as storage and logistics services.

"For the first time, SteelFab will be bringing German excellence in technology, engineering and innovation to the region through our tie-up with Messe Essen and the International Tube Association (ITA)," the event organiser, Expo Centre Sharjah, told ISMR.

"Foreign partnerships have always been the mainstay of SteelFab. In our latest endeavour, the tie-up with Messe Essen will see the launch of a special pavilion, the Essen Welding & Cutting Pavilion, while the partnership with ITA will help us focus more on pipe and tube machinery and tools. Both the tie-ups are important to us since they underscore our commitment to introduce better technology and machinery to the region," added H.E. Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, CEO, Expo Centre Sharjah.

The event is an opportunity for visitors, investors and experts to review the latest equipment and machines for steel fabrication. The event attracts worldwide attendance and participation from companies and leading international brands. ISMR is an international media partner for the event and will be distributed throughout the exhibition - you can also meet ISMR on booth 3052 in Hall 5 at the event.
