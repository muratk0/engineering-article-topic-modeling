# Advanced Engineering round up

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

BRINGING TOGETHER THOUSANDS of attendees from OEMs, Tier 1 manufacturers and supply chain partners, Advanced Engineering is the UK's largest annual advanced engineering and manufacturing event. With a two-day attendance of some 15,000 engineering professionals at the NEC in Birmingham, Advanced Engineering promotes supply chain business and technology transfer across aerospace, automotive, medical technology, energy and any sector that involves high-value manufacturing, R&D and innovation.

"The most recent edition of Advanced Engineering in October 2018 enjoyed a 10% rise in OEM and Tier 1 attendance. The advanced engineering industry is constantly evolving with new technologies, regulations and more. Advanced Engineering 2018 held six CPD-accredited open forums, filled with over 200 sessions covering hot topics in the industry," the show organiser told /SMR.

Fora included Aero Engineering; Automotive Engineering; Composite Engineering; Connected Manufacturing; Nuclear/Open Engineering and Composites/Advanced Materials. For further details, see www.easyfairs.com/advanced-engineering-2019/advancedengineering-2019/
