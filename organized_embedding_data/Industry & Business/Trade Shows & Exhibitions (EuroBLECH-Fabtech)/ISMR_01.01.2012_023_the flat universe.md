## At the core of flat products

Marcegaglia coil processing plants have been developed through the years mainly within the manufacturing complex in the harbour docks of Ravenna (Italy), on the Adriatic Sea. Here, the group has focused on a massive campaign of investment across ten years that has taken Marcegaglia much further in the production of flat products – increasing overall capacity and substantially doubling the production of hot dip galvanized and prepainted steel.

Today, the Italy-based group can count – across all plants – on a global capacity of 2.5 million TPY (tons per year) of cold-rolled products and of more than 2 million TPY in the galvanized sector. Pre-painted product output has reached a quota of 500,000 TPY, from two modern dedicated lines developing more than 25 standard organic coating systems on both surfaces, from fluoro-carbon to epoxy-polyesters, on a zinc-coated or cold-rolled base.

This means continuous progress for the group in total flat product sales: exports have sharply increased and today Marcegaglia has gradually transformed itself from a well-established tube-maker to a global player with a strong presence in the flat products market.

In addition, Marcegaglia Ravenna is also the main logistics centre of the group worldwide. The manufacturing unit has ample facilities for receiving, storing and shipping coils. Facilities have also been hugely upgraded during the years and, today, the group boasts 700 metres of harbour docks, privately owned and managed, finished with modern concrete floor and coil cradles served by five cranes and an internal railway. This hub handles approximately 3.5 million tonnes of steel material every year.

“Although I’ve been a passionate supporter of consolidation for all the group's Italian production sites, my passion and curiosity for the world and its various cultures has led me to open up to the global market, by exporting our methodology, expertise and business culture as well as by learning from local environments,” commented Antonio Marcegaglia, CEO.

This has resulted in further investments in recent years: in addition to Ravenna, all Marcegaglia service centres in Italy, Brazil and Poland have been strengthened and today more than 1.3 million TPY of narrow strip and around 1.1 TPY of decoiled sheet are processed.

Processing technologies and raw materials (such as high-yield strength steel grades) guarantee specific levels of tolerance, surface finish and mechanical properties as requested by the most demanding industrial sectors such as automotive.

With an investment of over 120 million euros, the Kluczbork plant in the south of Poland (the second Marcegaglia plant in the country) started operations with two slitting lines – from 0.3 to 6mm material thickness – and two cut-to-length facilities 'processing' 0.4 to 12.7mm as well as six new carbon steel tube mills; a new production and logistics hub, spanning an overall surface of 427,000 sqm. (80,000 covered) now acting as a cornerstone of Marcegaglia’s strategic expansion towards Eastern and Northern Europe.

The Brazilian plant of Garuva (in the southern state of Santa Catarina) has also been upgraded, doubling its manufacturing area to 120,000 sqm. covered and capacity of over 200,000 TPY of carbon steel sheet in addition to its range of stainless and carbon steel tube-certified specialties.

The presence of Marcegaglia is completed by heavy plate production for applications in the building and infrastructure sectors, heavy steel construction and industrial engineering. The plant in San Giorgio di Nogaro (Italy) has been recently refurbished with a new quarto rolling mill and a new unit for thermal treatment, bringing total capacity up to 400,000 TPY with maximum rolling widths of 2700mm.

## The stainless steel challenge

The Gazoldo degli Ippoliti plant, where the group's headquarters are located, has started new production of stainless steel hot rolled coil and sheet (1D finish) from 2 to 6mm thickness with 500,000 TPY capacity and cold rolled (2B finish) from 0.6 to 3mm with 250,000 TPY capacity, thanks to two annealing-pickling lines and two cold rolled-skinpass lines.
