# Teesside student wins UK sculpting competition

[TARİH: 01.08.2019 ISMR]

A FINE ART student from Teesside University (UK) has been announced as the winner of metals4U's annual Student Sculptors Competition. Cameron Lings, 20, wowed the judges with his metalwork sculpture, titled 'Turbine'. Lings, a second-year student at Teesside University's MIMA School of Art, has scooped a prize of attending an exclusive metal sculpture course at Yorkshire Sculpture Park.

As well as attending the course, Lings, who is from Scunthorpe, will also receive guidance from experienced metal artist, Brian Fell, who runs the Midsummer Metal Sculpture Course at Yorkshire Sculpture Park. His winning sculpture will also be put on display in the grounds of the sculpture park.

Lings' winning entry, 'Turbine', is inspired by ever-developing industrial day-to-day life. When describing his piece, Lings said: "To develop new ways of creating energy, we have looked to our natural environment to produce electricity from its kinetic movements. The sculpture demonstrates how our natural elements are being industrialised and harvested to their full extent. In this case, the wind is captured and portrayed in hardened metal, representing how engineering processes are making us look at nature from a different, and clearly useful, point of view. The sculpture also questions the future of the relationship between industry and the environment, and how it will further develop for our own benefit."

The Student Sculptors Competition, now in its third consecutive year, is hosted by Yorkshire-based metals4U. Fell chose Lings' work based on his entry, as well as images of past and current work. Metals4U was founded in 2002 as the UK's first online supplier of metals, plastics, tube clamps and engineering consumables. It now supplies grades of steel, copper, bronze and brass, power and hand tools, PPE, workwear and engineering products to retail, wholesale, hobbyists, artists, DIY, construction and industrial sectors.

Paul McFadyen, managing director of metals4U, said: "We were delighted with the standard and variety of work submitted to our third annual Student Sculptors Competition. We are very excited to see what Cameron creates on the course with support and inspiration from Brian Fell and his team."
