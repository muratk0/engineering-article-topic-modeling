# Sheet metal in India

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

PREPARATIONS at the offices of organiser Inter Ads – Brooks Exhibitions (India) Pvt. Ltd. for the third edition of the BLECH India sheet metal working exhibition are in full swing. BLECH India 2013 will take place from 17 – 20 April 2013 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai. Co-located with BLECH India 2013, the second Fastener Fair Mumbai will also take place from 17 – 18 April 2013. The exhibition presents a comprehensive range of industrial fasteners, construction fixings, assembly and installation systems as well as fastener manufacturing technology.

Six months before BLECH India, a total of 75 exhibitors from ten countries had already reserved their stands, and as much as 2,600 m² of net exhibition space had been booked. Compared to the same period before BLECH India 2011, this represents almost 50 per cent more booked exhibition space.

“Many exhibiting companies will present themselves on bigger stands this time and put an even larger amount of machines, tools and systems on display. More than half of the exhibiting companies that have already booked their stands at BLECH India 2013 come from India; the largest exhibitor countries from outside India are Germany and Italy. India is considered one of the world’s key growth markets for sheet metal working. In particular the automotive industry and its suppliers as well as the construction industry are a driving force for India’s continuously positive economic outlook,” the organiser told ISMR.

As a dedicated trade exhibition, exclusively focused upon sheet metal working, BLECH India covers all sectors of the sheet metal manufacturing process: metal forming and bending solutions, stamping, pressing, automation, flexible manufacturing systems, metal supply, cutting, punching, joining/welding, tooling, laser technology, CAD/CAM, finishing/coating, quality assurance and material handling.

The previous exhibition, held in April 2011, was highly successful, with 139 exhibitors from 18 countries and more than 7,000 trade visitors attending.
