## EVENT PREVIEW

"To achieve a better future, we need to combine rigorous methods, efficient organisation and business logics with human empathy, wisdom, courage and creativity.

"Made in Steel offers opportunities for business and ideas, relationships and knowledge. Besides being a showcase, it is also a culture hub which, through conferences, fora and round tables, hopes to further competitive collaboration and the exchange of information, two vital ingredients for business growth," added the show organiser.

## A record showing for 2019

With 20% more exhibition space sold this year, 19% overseas exhibitors and the increasing number of buyers, the 2019 event is already outstripping its last edition.

"In December 2018, five months before the close of registrations, the 12,000 square metres of exhibitions space sold is 20% higher than the total floor space for the previous show. Meanwhile, around 80% of 2017 exhibitors have confirmed their participation in 2019 (10% more than the average for similar international exhibitions). So far, the event has attracted exhibitors from 19 countries," confirmed the show organiser.

Alongside the major European steel manufacturers, such as Germany, France and Spain, are other rapidly expanding giants such as India (the world's second largest steel producer in 2018 with 106.5- million tons) and Turkey (the eighth largest steel producer with 37.3- million tons). Overseas exhibitors represent 19% of the total. These figures are already higher than the final numbers for the 2017 event.

"Also growing is the number of authorised buyers; at more than 40, they will multiply the business opportunities available over the three days of the event," explained Siderweb

The 2019 online ticket office is now open and offering entry tickets at special rates (€10 for one-day ticket and €25 for a threeday entry ticket)

## Competition and awards

This year, Made in Steel and Digital Magics are also launching the 'MADE IN STEEL 2019 – STEEL ON STAGE' contest, which aims to find new products and services with the right potential to create innovation in the steel industry and, at the same time, be environmentally sustainable. Applications can be submitted to https://innovation.madeinsteel.it by no later than 14 April 2019

"The best start-ups and SMEs, selected by a panel of experts, will have the opportunity to discuss their projects during Made in Steel 2019 to a qualified public as well as to European and international decision-makers in the steel industry, from production to distribution. The jury will then award a reward of 5,000 euros to the most innovative start-up or SME," explained Siderweb.

During every Made in Steel show, an expert jury also gives the Made in Steel Awards. Prizes are given to exhibitors and stand designers for the forcefulness and flair of their message, and for the most effective, ground-breaking presentations of their products and services.

There are three award categories: Best Communication Stand, (for the most effective advertising effort); Most Friendly Stand (for the most welcoming stand) and Best International Stand (for the stand with the most international flavour).

## The event in 2017

Made in Steel 2017 – a three-day event for the Italian and international steel industry – recorded increased figures, up 15% compared to the 2015 edition, with total participants at 14,714. The previous edition, for the first time at fieramilano, was visited by 13,000 participants (up 17% compared to the 2013 event).

Total exhibition area sold was up 5% on the 2015 edition, over a gross exhibition surface area of 34,000 square metres. It also registered double-digit growth in terms of stand sales (+11%). Overseas exhibitors accounted for 18% of the total, with a sharp increase in the total number of exhibiting countries to 18. Show visitors came from 74 countries.

"Steel was the spark that ignited the process that led to the setting up of the European Union we enjoy today," said Made in Steel CEO, Emanuele Morandi. "The entire process required a great deal of cooperation, a value we are trying to pass onto our audience with this event. We chose the title ‘Stronger Together’ for the 2017 edition and are convinced that it is only by working together, with a shared approach that goes beyond the idea of competition and embraces a really collaborative way of working throughout the entire value chain, that we will be able to face and overcome the big challenges affecting the global steel industry," added the organiser.

"Made in Steel has also welcomed many international guests from the steel industry, public institutions and the academic and research world. This is because we make a point of focusing on innovation and knowledge sharing, which is just the sort of cross-fertilisation that triggers new ideas and pushes back horizons."
