# THE FRENCH CONNECTION

[TARİH: 01.11.2013 ISMR]

FROM 19 TO 22 NOVEMBER, MIDEST (a major exhibition exclusively dedicated to industrial subcontracting and face-to-face meetings) will take place at the Paris Nord Villepinte Exhibition Centre in France.

“Once again this year, the show is launching numerous new initiatives. It will be focusing on a sector for the future – energy – and, for the first time, it has chosen a country from outside Europe, South Africa, as its country of the year. This will open up prospects for fresh cooperation for those companies looking to export. Algeria, another country that is rapidly industrialising, will also be the subject of a special programme designed to enable companies to forge partnerships.

“These initiatives will reinforce the array of increasingly successful events established over recent years such as the MIDEST Awards, the TV Studio, the technology centres and the conference programme. Finally, of course, there is the mobile app,” added the organiser.

Around 39,347 professionals visited the event in 2012. Approximately 16% of them were international buyers from 78 countries and 38% of visitors were new. MIDEST attracted 1,721 exhibitors in 2012 – of these, 188 exhibitors showcased their semi-finished products, metals and processes expertise, 483 exhibitors presented technologies for metal shaping and cutting and 244 exhibitors presented fabrication and metal structure products. Around 109 exhibitors displayed industrial fastening technologies.

MIDEST 2013 will be held in conjunction with MAINTENANCE EXPO, the total maintenance solutions show, and TOLEXPO, the international exhibition of equipment for working metal in sheet, coil, tubes and sections. See www.midest.com
