# EuroBLECH 2012 flies high

[TARİH: 01.12.2012 ISMR]

AFTER FIVE successful exhibition days at the Hanover exhibition grounds, EuroBLECH 2012, the 22nd International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, closed its doors on 27 October 2012. On a net exhibition space of 84,000 square metres, a total of 1,520 exhibitors from 39 countries presented a range of innovative products for the complete sheet metal working chain. With 60,500 trade visitors from all over the world, the number of visitors at EuroBLECH 2012 equals the visitor number at the previous exhibition, while the number of exhibitors at this year’s show grew by 5 per cent and the floor space by 7 per cent.

Initial analysis of the visitor survey showed a significant shift towards highly qualified trade visitors. Some 75 per cent of the visitors came from the industry, and the percentage of visitors from companies with more than 500 employees has increased by 20 per cent, said Mack Brooks Exhibitions. More visitors from top management attended and 40 per cent of all visitors came to EuroBLECH 2012 with the definite intention of buying, added the organiser.

Almost half of the exhibitors (48 per cent) and 36 per cent of the visitors at this year’s EuroBLECH came from outside Germany. The most important exhibitor countries, after Germany, were Italy, Turkey, China, the Netherlands, Switzerland, Austria, France and the USA. Top visitor countries from outside Germany were Austria, the Netherlands, Sweden, Switzerland, Great Britain, Italy, Denmark, the Czech Republic, Poland and India.

“Some 70 per cent of the exhibitors have indicated on site that they intended to exhibit again at the next exhibition. EuroBLECH 2014 will take place from 21st – 25th October 2014 at the Hanover exhibition grounds.

“Next year, we will organise, once again, dedicated sheet metal working exhibitions in selected growth markets. BLECH Russia 2013 will be held from 12th - 14th March 2013 in St. Petersburg; BLECH India 2013, from 17th - 20th April 2013 in Mumbai and BLECH China 2013, from 14th - 16th May 2013 in Suzhou,” explained the organiser. www.euroblech.com or www.blechevents.com
