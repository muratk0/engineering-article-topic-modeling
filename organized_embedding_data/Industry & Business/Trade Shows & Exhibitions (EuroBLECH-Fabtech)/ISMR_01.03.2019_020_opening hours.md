Mumbai, the gateway to India, is a solid location for BLECH India. The city, in the state of Maharashtra, is the financial capital of India and a leading industrial state. Many overseas companies have already settled in the Mumbai region and the administration is familiar with the needs and requirements of new investors. Maharashtra also introduced the 'Make in Maharashtra' scheme to increase foreign direct investment and local investment in the region by making it easier for international companies to conduct business.

## A strong grip

Co-located, once again, with BLECH India 2019 is Fastener Fair India 2019, the trade show exclusively dedicated to fastener and fixing technologies. Taking place for the seventh time from 25–26 April 2019, the exhibition covers a wide product range of industrial fasteners and fixings; assembly and installation systems; storage and logistics services; fastener manufacturing technology and construction fixings.

Fastener Fair India is part of the Fastener Fair series of targeted trade exhibitions for the fastener and fixings industry which includes Fastener Fair Italy; Fastener Fair France; Fastener Fair USA and the flagship event Fastener Fair Stuttgart, Europe's leading international trade fair for this industry sector.
