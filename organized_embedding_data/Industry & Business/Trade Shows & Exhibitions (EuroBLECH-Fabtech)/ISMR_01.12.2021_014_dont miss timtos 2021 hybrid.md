# Don’t miss TIMTOS 2021 Hybrid

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

The TIMTOS 2021 Hybrid show will debut in a new format as an online-offline showcase for the Taipei International Machine Tool Show (TIMTOS), which will be held physically at the TWTC Hall 1 and Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre Hall 1 and Hall 2, from 15-20 March 2021.

“The parallel online version of the show, characterised by its friendly interaction interface and personalised user experiences, will provide buyers with superb experiences, while exhibitors may interact with buyers in real-time and take advantage of business opportunities in a timely manner,” explained show organiser, TAITRA.

The online version of TIMTOS 2021 Hybrid was opened for registration from 7 December 2020. To acquaint exhibitors with the online exhibition, the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA) launched a video on 18 December 2020, explaining in detail its plans and packages, service items, promotion channels and business opportunities, in the hope of encouraging online exhibitors to ‘ride the digital wave’. New content is also to be released, including more clips from the TIMTOS Salon and TIMTOS Glimpse series, which were both launched in August 2020.

At the TIMTOS Salon on 19 November 2020, Somwang Boonrakcharoen (President of the Thai-German Institute) shared his insights on the current status of Thailand’s manufacturing industry and why it needs to move towards intelligence and automation while improving its domestic manufacturing capabilities to reduce overseas dependence.

On 12 November 2020, TIMTOS Glimpse introduced Shiel Hong Technology, a machining centre manufacturer in Taiwan. On 26 November 2020, the finale featured two manufacturers: Keyarrow (Taiwan), a high-profile supplier of flexible telescopic shields, and Ying Han Technology, a dedicated laser cutting machine, pipe bending machine and intelligent workstation maker.

According to the preliminary import and export customs trade statistics published by the Customs Administration, Ministry of Finance, Taiwan’s machine tool export value was US$ 3.064 billion for 2019, of which metal cutting machine tools accounted for approximately US$ 2.538 billion while metalforming machine tools accounted for US$ 52.6 million. Taiwan retains its place as the world’s top five machine tool exporter.

Coupled with TIMTOS’s physical exhibition in March 2021, the TIMTOS online exhibition is now open for online registration. To access up-to-date information, visit www.timtos.com.tw
