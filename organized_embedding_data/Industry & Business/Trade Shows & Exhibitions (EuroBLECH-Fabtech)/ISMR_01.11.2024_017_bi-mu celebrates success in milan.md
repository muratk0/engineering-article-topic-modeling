# BI-MU celebrates success in Milan

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

The 34.BI-MU, the international biennial exhibition for machine tools, robots, automation, digital and additive manufacturing/subcontracting, was held at Fieramilano Rho (Milan, Italy) from 9-12 October 2024. Organised by EFIM-ENTE FIERE ITALIANE MACCHINE and promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots, automation systems and auxiliary products manufacturers' association), 34.BI-MU featured product offerings from over 750 companies. According to the show organiser, 37% of exhibiting companies were from outside Italy.

Around 35,000 trade operators were at the exhibition (5% came from 62 countries). The most represented countries were Germany; Switzerland; France; Spain; Slovenia for Europe; the USA and Brazil for the Americas and China; South Korea; Japan; Taiwan and Turkey for Asia. Among overseas visitors were also 80 operators, mostly users and some journalists, from 16 countries.

## Thematic areas and events

A total of 4,000 students took part in BI-MU through free and guided tours entrusted to 55 young tutors from Higher Technological Institutes. In addition to students from high schools, for the first time the exhibition opened its doors to students from lower secondary schools, a crucial time in deciding which educational path to take after 'compulsory schooling', commented the show organiser.

"All students were welcomed in the ROBOTGAMES area, with its special education and job initiatives project designed to encourage dialogue between education and industry to attract talent. A meeting point for matching job demand and supply was offered by Randstad, which also offered job orientation and gamified light self-assessment activities. The real area attraction was ROBOTGAMES, the new automation and robotics contest for high school students, promoted by UCIMU Academy through UCIMU Foundation and organised by EFIM, with the support of Fiera Milano Foundation. The final of the contest, which took place on Friday 11 October 2024, saw eight teams from different institutes compete from all over Italy," it added.

Alfredo Mariotti, general manager of UCIMU, stated: "Despite particularly complex [market] economic circumstances, BI-MU registered an increase in the number of its direct and indirect exhibitors, compared to a partial reduction in exhibition space. Thematic areas, such as RobotHeart, piùAdditive and BI-MUDigital, contributed to enhancing the value of the thematic and product offerings of our trade show."

Over eighty events were organised by exhibitors and organisers, most of which were held in the BI-MUpiù Arena (promoted by UCIMU Foundation) with the support of the Lombardy Region, the sponsorship of Banca Generali and the patronage of TM.

## Hot topics for industry

Sixty speeches hosted in the BI-MUpiù Arena involved over 1,400 people in attendance and around 5,000 users connected online via a live streaming service from the home page of the exhibition's website.

“The Robotics Day included organisations representing the segment such as IFR (International Federation of Robotics), SIRI (Italian Robotics and Automation Association), I-RIM (Italian Institute of Robotics and Intelligent Machines) and AIxIA, (Italian Association for Artificial Intelligence). These organisations, with UCIMU, took part in an open discussion on the potential for development and application of new technologies in all markets. IFR also chose BI-MU as the venue for its annual assembly as well as a series of in-depth meetings dedicated to its members.

Riccardo Rosa, President of UCIMU, concluded: "These initiatives, as well as the commitment to young people through our UCIMU Academy, are essential elements for the success of the trade show [which includes] activities designed to foster the growth and development of the sector."

In October 2026, the 35.BI-MU will celebrate its 70th anniversary.
