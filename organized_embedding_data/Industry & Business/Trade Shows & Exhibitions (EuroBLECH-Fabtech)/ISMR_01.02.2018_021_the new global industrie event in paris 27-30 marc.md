# The new GLOBAL INDUSTRIe event in Paris (27-30 March 2018) aims to bring together the entire manufacturing ecosystem

[TARİH: 01.02.2018 ISMR]

Paving the way for the creation of a large-scale European event in France that would be focused on the 'Industry of the Future', GL Events has therefore taken the opportunity to bring together all these stakeholders at the Paris Nord Villepinte exhibition centre from 27-30 March 2018 as part of the new GLOBAL INDUSTRIe event.

"For the first time in France, the exhibition aims to draw visitors from the entire manufacturing sector. GLOBAL INDUSTRIe will be the highlight of the Manufacturing Week and is backed by the highest national authorities, the Industry of the Future Alliance, local communities and professional organisations. It provides a unique opportunity to meet all the stakeholders in the sector, find new partners and explore the manufacturing world of the future! We are very confident that the event will attract more than 50,000 visitors over four days," the event organiser told SMR.

The event aims to bring together the entire manufacturing ecosystem (startups; providers of products/solutions; parts manufacturers; subcontractors; purchasers and large companies). It will also bring together the entire value chain: research & innovation; design; production; services and training. Different sectors are also represented e.g. transportation & mobility; energy; food processing; infrastructure; consumer goods; chemistry; engineering and other (defence/military, metallurgy, iron and steel etc.)

## Sectors at GLOBAL INDUSTRIe

Start-ups; product providers; solutions providers; equipment manufacturers; subcontractors; purchasers; SMEs; mid-cap companies; large companies; competitive clusters; research centres and incubators.

## Four exhibitions in one

GLOBAL INDUSTRIe's four specialised exhibitions will offer solutions to a wide range of specialised needs. All four of these exhibitions come under the GLOBAL INDUSTRIe banner and represent different areas of manufacturing.

MIDEST is the main international event for subcontracting expertise.

SMART INDUSTRIES showcases digitalisation and the industry of the future.

INDUSTRIE is where technology meets production equipment.

TOLEXPO brings together solutions and equipment for sheet metal work.

50,000 people are expected to visit the 100,000m² exhibition.

"The 'Industry of the Future' is changing the world, and France can play a leading role. It enjoys unprecedented support from the political world, which sees modernisation as a national priority. The goal is to promote the French manufacturing sector at home and abroad by highlighting its top performers and regional success stories," added the event organiser.

"With more than 50% of new jobs expected within the next 15 years, the future is being defined now. This event will showcase the fourth industrial revolution that is taking place right now.

The innovative events will feature machinery, technologies and training courses, as well as a series of conferences combining case studies and feedback from leading manufacturers. This will help you stay (or get) up to date with the latest developments and find employees who will move your company into the future."

## Event awards

The GLOBAL INDUSTRIe Awards acknowledge and reflect the variety and modernity of the sector. The awards will be presented on the evening of 27 March 2018 at a ceremony attended by the press and manufacturers.

The six original award categories reflect the diversity and modernity of the manufacturing sector. All exhibitors at Paris-Nord Villepinte can compete in the six categories below:

- Productive performance will showcase innovations that provide significant gains in productivity, key ratios or performance.

- New technology will display innovations resulting from a technology transfer or a major technological innovation.

- Collective success will highlight a project, product or service that came about via collaboration between various manufacturers.

- Outstanding achievement will showcase remarkable expertise in the production of parts, services, subassemblies and other systems.

- Industrial transformation will highlight providers of services and technologies who support companies in their transformation, and have managed to adapt themselves.

- Responsible solutions will showcase innovations that incorporate energy efficiency, the circular economy, CSR, ergonomics, eco-design etc.

A special demonstration award will go to one of the exhibitors whose booth particularly illustrates the current revolution that is taking place in the manufacturing sector. Award submissions will be considered by a panel of manufacturers and journalists from the trade press who will compile a list of nominations. The nominees will then present their innovation orally to the panel in Paris.

## The Connected Factory

After the success of its paint chain ("Peinture en Live") – a first at an exhibition in France – the next logical step for the event organiser was to set up an entire production line, from design to final delivery, for an object that visitors could take home with them (The Connected Factory). The challenge is to use proven technologies to demonstrate the progress being made in the automation of production, alongside the technologies of the industry of the future that enable the product to be customised. This temporary factory will also bring together most of the techniques and services presented at the four exhibitions.

"A part will be designed and manufactured in front of the public, then degreased, painted and assembled using additive manufacturing. An automated assembly station will place the object in a cut and painted box for visitors to take with them. Visitors will be able to choose from several patterns and colours to demonstrate the machines' ability to adapt. The manufactured objects will be checked and measured. The entire connected factory will be highly automated (AGV link between manufacturing stations, robot loading and unloading machines etc.) and progress will be monitored live," commented GL Events.

"Technologies such as predictive maintenance, augmented reality and cobotics will also be showcased. The event will also demonstrate connectivity, the flexibility of the production chain, the flow of information between workstations and data protection (cybersecurity). Visitors will be able to follow the entire production process while safely moving around the connected factory."
