# FACTORIES OF THE FUTURE

[TARİH: 01.02.2020 ISMR]

Six trade fairs under one roof. Manufacturing processes and technological innovation will merge at WIN EURASIA 2020.

WIN EURASIA, a leading industrial exhibition in Eurasia, will take place from 12-15 March 2020 at the TÜYAP Fair and Exhibition Centre in Istanbul, Turkey. It is being supported by the Association of Turkish Machine Manufacturers. The event, organised in Istanbul by Deutsche Messe’s Istanbul office - Hannover Fairs Turkey, is a leading B2B trade fair for access to international, as well as Turkish and Eurasian, markets. The Turkish Ministry of Trade is supporting WIN EURASIA 2020 with a free hosted buyer programme (application deadline is 4/2/2020).

“Innovations ranging from metalworking to automation, motion and drive systems and from energy and electrical-electronics to intralogistics, as well as from surface treatment to welding technologies will be showcased at the 27th International WIN EURASIA 2020. It will present the entire ecosystem required for the factories of the future from steel plate work to metal production technologies, from automation services to bonding and welding technologies and from hydraulic and pneumatic services to on-site logistics. It will also offer participants the opportunity to closely experience latest technological developments including automation, digitalisation, the Internet of Things (IOT) and Augmented Reality,” the show organiser told ISMR.

Manufacturing processes and technological innovation will come together at WIN EURASIA 2019, and the Industry 4.0 concept and future vision for the industry will be discussed by experts in seminars during the event. The event encompasses six trade fairs under one roof – CeMAT EURASIA; IAMD EURASIA; Industrial Energy Systems EURASIA; Metalworking EURASIA; Surface Technology EURASIA and Welding EURASIA. WIN EURASIA 2020 will showcase a range of technologies from sheet metal processing to metal forming technologies; automation services to electric and electronic equipment and hydraulic & pneumatic services to intralogistics.

## The show in 2019

WIN EURASIA 2019, from 14-17 March 2019, featured a Smart 4.0 production line. Five manufacturers came together at the Industry 4.0 Festival Area to showcase a smart production line in Turkey for the first time. The event also featured 51 panels, 156 speakers and the WINovation: Innovative Technologies Contest. B2WIN, a Business-to-business matchmaking service supported by Enterprise Europe Network (EEN), was also part of the show.

In all, 1,376 exhibitors from 20 countries showcased their products and services over eleven halls located on 33,110m2 of net stand space. The top five international exhibitor countries were China, Germany, Taiwan, South Korea and Italy. Four country pavilions highlighted national competencies for China, Germany, South Korea and Taiwan. The event also welcomed 77,40 visitors from 111 countries. The top five international visitor countries were Iran, Bulgaria, China, the Russian Federation and Germany. In each of the halls, a variety of Industry 4.0 advances were also showcased. Turkey’s industrial sector has increased its exports of technological industrial products in recent years.

In 2018, significant visitor increases were also registered from Italy; the United Kingdom; Poland; the Russian Federation; South Africa; U.A.E and U.S.A compared to previous years.

Besides Turkish exhibitors, WIN EURASIA also hosted international exhibitors from Canada; France; India; Japan; Poland; Russia and Spain. Exhibitors presented innovations in their products and services while making new agreements through bilateral business talks.

## WINOVATION competition

Deutsche Messe’s Turkey office, Hannover Fairs Turkey, will be holding a competition for the WIN Eurasia event. Organised in collaboration with ENOSAD, aimed at adding value to Turkey’s industrial sector, the WINOVATION competition will reward projects that are focused on technical innovation and involve original designs. Nominations can be submitted in one of the various categories such as products; services; design; technology; technical infrastructure; solutions; business models; organisation and social environment.

The deadline for applications for the WINOVATION contest, which WIN EURASIA 2019 participants can enter with their completed projects with a brand or commercial value, is 14 February 2020. Projects ranking the highest will be rewarded at a ceremony which will take place on 12 March 2020 during WIN EURASIA.

“Projects which rank the highest will be chosen by a jury of ten members, consisting of professors who are experts in their fields, sector representatives and ISDER (the Materials Handling, Storage & Industrial Equipment Association of Turkey). At the evaluation stage of the projects, however, the criteria to be taken into consideration will include: uniqueness and authenticity; user benefits; innovations offered; open-ness to development and applicability; export potential; R&D quality; technological aspects; contribution to the environment; contribution to society and high-tech and commercial features,” said Hannover Fairs Turkey.

Projects which rank amongst the top three will be awarded an incentive for participation in the WIN EURASIA 2020 exhibition, with promotional and marketing support available throughout the year.
