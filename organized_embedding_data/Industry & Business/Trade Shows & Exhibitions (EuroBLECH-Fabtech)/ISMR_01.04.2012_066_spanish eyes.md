# SPANISH EYES

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

ISMR previews BIEMH 2012, the biennial international machine tool show, in Spain

ISMR SAYS:

“Manufacturers of Spanish machine tools represent the third largest producers in the European Union and the ninth worldwide”

The 27th BIEMH, the international biennial machine tool show, is to be held from 28 May to 2 June 2012. Jointly organised by the Bilbao Exhibition Centre (BEC) and the Spanish Machine Tool Manufacturers’ Association (AFM), the last BIEMH in 2010 attracted 35,263 people from 54 countries, many of them strategic machine tool consumers. A total of 1,085 international manufacturers, importers and distributors exhibited advanced machinery and the latest technological innovations in the pavilions at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre.

“BIEMH is now considered to be the third most important exhibition in Europe in its sector,” the organiser told ISMR. “Leading manufacturers and distributors will meet visitors from all around the world so that they can see and learn about the latest developments and technologies. May is set to be a strategic month for machine tool companies and associated sectors keen to strengthen their participation in international markets.”

Sectors covered by the event will include machine tools (metal cutting and metal forming), other machines (welding, oxycut, surface treatment), machine tooling, parts, components and accessories, production systems automation, metrology and quality control and services.

## Support from over 1000 manufacturers

As the number of overseas exhibitors at BIEMH increases, with 1022 manufacturers, importers and distributors from 25 countries already signed up for the event, the campaign to attract visitors is also taking an international focus. Promotional actions have already reached out to over 67,000 industry specialists worldwide, with forthcoming trade missions scheduled for Turkey, Germany, Mexico, Colombia, China, India, Morocco and Algeria.

“The effect of the event as a platform for globalising business will be strengthened this year with the incorporation of new, online applications and spaces to facilitate direct links between users, exhibitors and potential visitors, information sharing and discussion on various levels,” added the organiser.

At EMO last year, BIEMH’s exhibition manager, Mari Carmen Gorostiza, explained that the BIEMH organising team (aware of how the current economic climate is affecting the capital goods industry) had decided to offer firm support to companies to make the most of the BIEMH 2012 and ‘to help them to reinforce their commercial activity in the most effective way possible, guaranteeing advantageous economic conditions of participation’.

To achieve this goal, both the services and commercial divisions offered exhibitors personalised management, as well as providing the chance to use direct BEC promotion services. Additional benefits depending on the amount of space rented have been considered, ranging from 5% to 20%. Other benefits available include preferential reservations during certain registration periods, easy payment terms and special stand decoration packs, hotel accommodation, meals, meetings, parking etc.

BIEMH’12 is supported by the commercial offices of the Spanish Institute of Foreign Trade (ICEX), the network of agents of the Society for the Industrial Promotion and Reconversion (SPRI) and Bilbao Exhibition Centre’s own agents abroad, to coordinate delegations of purchasers in countries from five continents.

Manufacturers of Spanish machine tools represent the third largest producers in the European Union and the ninth worldwide. www.biemh.com for more details.
