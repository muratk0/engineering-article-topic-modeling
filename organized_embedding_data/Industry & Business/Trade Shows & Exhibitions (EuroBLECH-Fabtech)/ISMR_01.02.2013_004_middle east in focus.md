# Middle East in focus

[TARİH: 01.02.2013 ISMR]

A RECENT STUDY forecasts that the regional steel industry in the Middle East is expected to continue growth in 2013, bolstered by increasing infrastructure spend, greater industrialisation and a higher demand for homes fuelled by a growing population and falling family sizes.

The ninth edition of SteelFab, which is the region’s comprehensive trade event for the steel fabrication, metal working and metal manufacturing industry, was organised by Expo Centre Sharjah with the support of the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry from 14 to 17 January 2013. During the four-day run, the exhibition hosted around 250 direct exhibitors from 24 countries representing more than 600 brands of steel machinery, welding equipment and surface preparation and attracted 7296 visitors.

German participation at SteelFab 2013 generated keen interest. The German pavilion brought 16 exhibitors to SteelFab 2013 under the aegis of VDMA (Verband Deutscher Maschinen- und Anlagenbau - German Engineering Federation), which is one of the key association service providers in Europe and is the largest engineering industry network in Europe.

The exhibitors in the German pavilion included AGTOS; A.S. Exclusiv; Bio-Circle Surface Technology; FlexiCAM; Häussler; Honsberg Metallsägen; Kahrl + Wiemann; Kaindl; Kaltenbach; Knuth; KULLEN-KOTI; PFERD and Wieland.

GENERALLY REGARDED as the most significant global economic success story of the past 30 years, China's economy is now reckoned to be the second largest in the world. World Bank data shows that investment in buildings, roads, bridges, railways, airports, industrial equipment and machinery rose to 45.4 per cent of GDP from 36.3 per cent between 2002 and 2010.
