# TUBE & STEEL Istanbul 2023

[TARİH: 01.04.2023 ISMR]

The second edition of the TUBE & STEEL Istanbul 2nd Pipe, Profile, Wire, Steel Manufacturing and Technology Specialised Fair will be held at TÜYAP Istanbul from 24-27 May 2023.

Organised by Tüyap Tüm Fuarcılık Yapım Inc., in cooperation with the Turkish Steel Pipe Manufacturers Association (CEBID) and the Steel Foreign Trade Association, it also has the support of the Flat Steel Import and Industry Association (YISAD); Turkish Steel Exporters' Association; the Turkish Constructional Steelwork Association (TUCSA); Galvanized and Coated Coil Manufacturers (SOGAD) and Steel Orbis.

"The exhibition will bring together more than 15,000 qualified visitors from 70 countries over four halls. Hundreds of participating companies from the international arena will support this event, which will also contribute significantly to Turkey's export balance with new business connections," said the show organiser.

Tuyap Fairs Production Inc. General Manager, ilhan Ersözlü, added: “We are very happy to organise the TUBE & STEEL Fair, which is the only specialist fair in Turkey for pipe, profile and wire steel, for the second time.“

The exhibition will showcase products and technologies for steel-tube-profile-wire; flat steel; structural steel; steel service centres; steel processing technology; tube-profile-wire processing technology; tube-profile coating; tube-profile and fittings; pipelines; tube-profile-wire-steel production machinery; tube-profile-wire test and control technologies; fasteners; pumps/valves and accessories.

Visiting hours are between 10:00 and 19:00 on the first three days and up to 18:00 on the last day.

Business, state and local officials joined U. S. Steel CEO and President David Burritt to break ground at Big River Steel Works in Osceola, Arkansas (USA).
