# SPRING INTO 2024

[TARİH: 01.02.2024 ISMR]

The Industrial Spring cycle, from 19-22 March 2024 in Kielce (Poland), showcases the latest industrial products and solutions with highly specialised exhibitions such as STOM-TOOL.

The Industrial Spring is a series of exhibitions at the Kielce Exhibition and Congress Centre in 'the heart' of Poland from 19-22 March 2024. The previous industrial exhibition cluster took up all seven halls so a further temporary exhibition hall has been added for the 2024 event. The city of Kielce is part of the capital of the Świętokrzyskie province in southern Poland and a major industrial centre in the region.

The Industrial Spring cycle showcases the latest products and solutions with highly specialised exhibitions such as STOM-TOOL; STOMBLECH & CUTTING; STOM LASER; STOMROBOTICS; STOMFIX; WELDING; WIRTOPROCESSES; EXPO-SURFACE; KIELCE FLUIDPOWER; TeiA Electronics and Automation Expo; CONTROLSTOM and 3D PRINTING DAYS.

“The industry events series in the Kielce exhibition and congress centre is always held in the first quarter of the year; every consecutive expo breaks records. The 2024 Industrial Spring promises to be the largest in its history. Exhibition space has increased by as much as 10% compared to previous events. Industrial Spring is the place to view hundreds of machines operating live in all seven halls of the Kielce exhibition and congress centre," explained show organiser, Targi Kielce S.A.

## A networking platform

Last year's STOM events attracted 600 exhibitors and co-exhibitors from 21 countries (206 from outside Poland).

"STOM's 2023 series and accompanying events hosted 19,000 business visitors over four days. The comprehensive exhibition covers several industry sectors which is particularly interesting for visitors. Industrial Spring has mainly hosted representatives of manufacturing companies (72%) in sectors such as machinery (45%), automotive (24%) and armaments (10%)," confirmed the show organiser, Targi Kielce S.A.

Visitors came from 24 countries across Europe, Asia and North America. The trade show organiser confirmed that 29% of visitors were new, and not returning, arrivals.
