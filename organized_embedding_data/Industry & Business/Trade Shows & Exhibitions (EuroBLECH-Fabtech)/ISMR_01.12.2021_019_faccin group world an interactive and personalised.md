# Faccin Group World – an interactive and personalised experience

[TARİH: 01.01.2021 ISMR]

Faccin Group has invested in building closer relationships with its customers by launching the interactive “Faccin Group World” project.

“As trade shows are on standby, we are bringing the world to our factory,” Andrea Ceretti told ISMR. “Thanks to an important investment in terms of resources and planning, we held our first live event dedicated exclusively to the dished heads and pressure vessels manufacturing sector on 16 July 2020 at our headquarters. It was attended by over 250 customers worldwide.”

The event enabled participants to enter Faccin Group’s factory while remaining at home, participating in a technical presentation which, said Ceretti, reaped significant results in terms of customer involvement, interactivity and in-depth understanding. In short, an interactive and personalised experience.

“Customers dived into our factory discovering machines and their operations, asking questions and getting live answers. In the studio, speakers conversed with the participants, answering questions and explaining our industrial and technological solutions,” added Ceretti.

“We will not stop exhibiting at trade shows in the near future, but we will certainly intensify this new method of communication which offers important opportunities to be closer to our customers and offers valuable experiences that go beyond a simple commercial presentation,” he concluded.
