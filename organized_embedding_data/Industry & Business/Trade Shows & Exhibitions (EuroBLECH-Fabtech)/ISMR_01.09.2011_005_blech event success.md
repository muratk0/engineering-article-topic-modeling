# BLECH EVENT SUCCESS

[TARİH: 01.09.2011 ISMR]

LEARN EVERYTHING ABOUT the latest product innovations and market developments at this year’s BLECH Nordic event (4 – 7 October 2011 in Stockholm, Sweden) for the manufacturing industry thanks to two co-located exhibitions and seminar programmes.

For the first time BLECH Nordic, exclusively dedicated to the sheet metal working industry in the Northern countries, will complement the Scandinavian Technical Fair, the leading event for the manufacturing industry in the Nordic and Baltic region. See pages 22 – 24 for our full BLECH Nordic preview or access www.blechnordic.com

Stand reservation for EuroBLECH 2012, the 22nd International Technology Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working, has already begun. Taking place from 23 – 27 October 2012 in Hanover, Germany, the exhibition will occupy eight halls at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds. The exhibitor brochure, published in five languages, is now available and includes important information for companies interested in participating in one of the leading international trade shows for the sheet metal working industry.

With a total of 1,455 exhibitors from 43 countries and 61,500 visitors from 98 countries, EuroBLECH 2010 was more international than ever and covered a net exhibition space of 78,600m2. EuroBLECH is audited by FKM, Society for Voluntary Control of Fair and Exhibition Statistics. The show had an above-average increase in visitors from outside Germany, in particular from Asia, North America and the Middle East. EuroBLECH therefore reflects the general development of an ever more international orientation of this industry sector.

‘Sustainable success in a global market’ is the theme for exhibiting companies at EuroBLECH 2012. To reflect the growing importance of hybrid structures in sheet metal working, processing of sheet metal / plastic hybrid structures has now been included in the EuroBLECH exhibition profile as a separate category.
