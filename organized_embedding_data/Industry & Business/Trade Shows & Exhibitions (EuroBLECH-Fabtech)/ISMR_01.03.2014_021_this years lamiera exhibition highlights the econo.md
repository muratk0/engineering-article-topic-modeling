# This year’s LAMIERA exhibition highlights the economic benefits of sustainability

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

LAMIERA 2014, the international exhibition dedicated to the metal forming machine tool sector and associated technologies, will take place in Bologna, Italy from 14-17 May 2014. A total of 451 exhibiting companies – 42% from outside Italy – supported LAMIERA 2012 over a total area of 40,000 sqm. In 2012, 18,192 visits were registered at the event.

LAMIERA is the Italian exhibition showcasing solutions for optimising the management of machines and processes, providing systems with dynamic performance and operating flexibility and for making production innovation investments safer and more effective. Sheet metal is a highlight of the show. Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tool and robotics association) and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU, it also features a specially themed area for materials and carpentry; metal sheets and strips; tubes and profiles plus semi-finished and finished products.

“The solutions offered by LAMIERA can be applied to all industrial sectors: from automotive to metalworking machines and equipment in the manufacturing sector, from electro-technics/electronics to pharmaceuticals; from cosmetics to medicals; from the food sector to the packaging sector; from chemicals to plastics; from optics to the manufacturing of interior design components and from transport to telecommunications and sensors,” the organiser told ISMR.

## Sustainability in focus

According to provisional data, Italian production of machine tools, robots and automation systems will increase by 4.6% in 2014, reaching almost 5 billion euros. However, 2014 will generally be the year of recovery for Italian domestic consumption, which will show positive results with a 4.4% increase, starting a growth trend that will become even stronger during 2015 and 2016. It will also bring an increase in the consumption of those materials that are typical in the steel supply chain,” the association told ISMR.

LAMBDA SOSTENIBILITÀ (LAMBDA SUSTAINABILITY) is the new showcase for LAMBDA, hosted by LAMIERA. Focusing on sustainable development, LAMBDA SOSTENIBILITÀ showcases the commitment of the metal forming sector to the creation of machinery, production systems and auxiliary technologies capable of meeting the requirements of social, environmental and economic sustainability.

“With the aim of reducing manufacturing costs, end users are becoming more interested in finding innovative machines, capable of operating by rationally exploiting resources and minimising the use of energy and raw materials,” commented UCIMU. “Through the LAMBDA SOSTENIBILITÀ demonstration area, LAMIERA puts the spotlight on how sustainable production can generate economic benefits, promoting the business potential it offers for operators in the machine tool sector.”

Using debate and comparisons between the players involved – manufacturers, end users, research centres and knowledge development centres – LAMBDA SOSTENIBILITÀ will emphasize the needs and opportunities generated by new sustainable development models, and highlight their impact on manufacturing design and production.

The space allocated inside one of the exhibition halls will host start-ups, research centres and universities that are interested in meeting potential partners from the exhibitors and visitors to LAMIERA and to highlight, through the 50 programme presentations, initiatives and projects dealing with the issue of sustainable systems.

“In addition, LAMBDA SOSTENIBILITÀ will feature an area dedicated to the ‘UCIMU Blue Philosophy’ brand, under which the companies (that have been granted this brand) will showcase their best eco-compatible production. The distinctive mark of the most qualified Italian production of the sector, applicable to the company rather than the product, the UCIMU Blue Philosophy brand is granted to member companies that can guarantee commercial reliability, financial solidity, utmost attention to safety and testing, customer care and sustainable production,” explained UCIMU.

Alfredo Mariotti, general manager of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, stated: “Cost reduction, from activities aimed at improving energy efficiency and the containment of costs associated with the rational use of raw materials, are some of today’s determining factors in the sustainability strategy of end user companies. For this reason, the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots and automation systems, promoter of the exhibition, wanted to facilitate comparison on this issue through the new edition of LAMBDA”. ■

## HOW TO GET THERE

## Trains: Under an agreement between BolognaFiere and Trenitalia, you can travel with “Le Frecce” saving 40% on the basic rate of 1st and 2nd class or Business/Premium/Standard level of service on Frecciarossa/Frecciargento and Frecciabianca trains. For further details, see www.lamiera.net/en/visitors/come-arrivare/

Bologna’s Stazione Centrale is only 10 minutes from the BolognaFiere exhibition grounds. Bus lines 35, 35/ and 38 link the station to the exhibition centre (P.zza Costituzione and Viale Aldo Moro entrances). See Trenitalia.

Air: Guglielmo Marconi International Airport is connected directly to the BolognaFiere exhibition grounds by the AEROBUS BLQ shuttle. The service is active for all exhibitions.

Car: On the motorway, from Florence, Milan and Ancona: Exit at the new “BOLOGNA FIERA” A14 toll booth motorway. From Padua: Ring road exit 8 for Nord, Michelino, Moro entrances and Michelino car park; ring road exit 7 for Costituzione entrance and car park.

Bus: The Bologna Exhibition Centre can be reached every day with ATC buses (numbers 28-35-35/-38-39) at the regular city rate. There is also an Appennino shuttle bus service from Florence to Bologna airport.
