# Over 19,000 visits to LAMIERA in Milan

[TARİH: 01.08.2022 ISMR]

LAMIERA (the international trade show of sheet metal forming machines, tools and related technologies) took place in Milan, Italy, from 18-21 May 2022. Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association, and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU, LAMIERA returned after three years, postponed for twelve months due to the pandemic. ISMR also visited the show.

“Over 19,000 entries were registered at the turnstiles during the four exhibition days. Apart from the number of visitors, which was higher than expected, it was the quality of visits that distinguished the audience of operators attending LAMIERA 2022. Most exhibitors, interviewed at their stands, reported the presence of knowledgeable visitors and received visits mainly intended for purchases," the show organiser told ISMR.

"Of the total visitors, 12% were from overseas (representing around 60 countries). The most numerous delegations came from Austria; China; Croatia; France; Germany; The Netherlands; Poland; Slovenia; Spain; Switzerland; Turkey and the United States."

There were 365 exhibiting companies, whose stands were set up in both halls, covering a total exhibition area of over 40,000 square metres. Thirty overseas delegates of the business mission were made up of end-users and journalists from Brazil; France; India; Poland; Romania; Turkey and the United States. The mission was organised by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, in cooperation with the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs and of International Cooperation and the ICE-Italian Trade Agency, which also helped to create the exhibition area dedicated to start-up companies.

Over 100 journalists were accredited at the trade show, and 724 students registered to visit LAMIERA 2022 (stopping at the UCIMU Academy area specially reserved for them). The show organiser focused on guided tours led by tutors of the Association. In addition, there were also free visits, guided by teachers of visiting schools.

"Over 30 meetings were hosted at LAMIALAMIERA, an arena set up in Hall 13. The meetings were attended by around 700 operators, in addition to more than 400 remotely connected users who followed the speeches streamed on the lamiera.net website," UCIMU explained.

The next edition of LAMIERA is scheduled to be held again at Fieramilano Rho in Milan from 10-13 May 2023.■
