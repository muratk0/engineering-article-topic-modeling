Since 2007, the two complimentary trade fairs, BlechExpo and SchweissTec, have taken place every two years at the New Trade Fair Centre at Stuttgart. The Centre’s unique location – directly adjacent to Stuttgart International Airport, right of the motorway, and linked to local public transportation as well as to the ICE (intercity express) railway network in the future – gives it unparalleled advantages.

The 10th BlechExpo 2011 (sheet metal exhibition) and 3rd Schweisstec (trade fair for joining technology) will return to Stuttgart from 6 – 9 June 2011. For the first time, BlechExpo will cover the field of hybrid technology, a key focus for the sheet metal field. The organiser, P E Schall, expects an increase in visitor numbers on the 2009 event (which attracted 957 exhibitors and over 25,000 visitors). VDW (the German Machine Tool Builders’ Association) will be the sponsor for this year’s BlechExpo event. The declared goal of both partners is introduce synergies to progress the ‘internationalisation’ of this exhibition. The aim is to extend joint activities onto the European stage.

## SHEET METAL PROCESSING

“Record breaking participation figures are already becoming apparent for this edition of the BlechExpo international trade fair for sheet metal processing – with regard to both exhibition floor space and exhibitor numbers. To date, 900 exhibitors have booked available floor space in Halls 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 for the complementary trade fair duo and thus will occupy a total of roughly 750,000 square feet,” the organiser explained.

The 5th Schweisstec alone, with roughly 100 exhibitors and 105,000 square feet booked to date, will occupy hall 6 in its entirety where the joint BlechExpo – Schweisstec forum will also be held.

The 900 exhibitors registered to date (over 800 for BlechExpo and roughly 100 for Schweisstec) cover all the relevant technologies throughout the process sequence for efficient sheet metal processing – from raw materials to joining parts made from sheet metal, profiles and pipe into assemblies. This year’s Schweisstec has been expanded to include the technically relevant topics of mechanical fastening and joining technologies.

“The exhibitions will take into account new requirements with regard to joining and fastening modern materials, as well as hybrid materials,” said the organiser. “After all, for example, it’s frequently impossible to use thermal joining processes for combinations consisting of high-strength sheet metal with coatings, which applies to an even greater extent to innovative hybrid solutions comprised of steel and aluminium sheet, for which only mechanical processes such as clinching, riveting screwing etc. can be used reliably and efficiently. The issues of energy efficiency and increased productivity are also now at the forefront.”
