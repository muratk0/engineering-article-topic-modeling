Biele Group offers engineering and automation systems, designing and manufacturing complex turnkey projects for different industrial sectors. The Biele Metal Division is focused on the metal industry, supplying solutions for ferrous and non-ferrous materials and tailor-made solutions for material handling, stacking and packaging.

"Our technical scope includes hydraulic press technology, vacuum-, magnetic- and robotic-stackers, as well as complete packing lines for steel, stainless steel or aluminium. We will be exhibiting at the 2024 EuroBLECH exhibition in Hannover, presenting our latest innovations and most relevant turnkey projects," outlined the Group.

"We will also present our latest developments in customised systems that optimise production processes through manufacturing management software, advanced data analysis tools and computer vision systems," it added.
