# Success for three BLECH Events

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

BETWEEN MARCH and May 2013, three successful BLECH sheet metal events took place in Russia, India and China. While BLECH China was the initial launch, the dedicated exhibitions for sheet metal working in Russia and India took place for the third time.

The third edition of the BLECH Russia exhibition for sheet metal working technology, held in March 2013 at the Lenexpo Exhibition Centre in St. Petersburg, attracted 4,387 trade visitors. A total of 127 exhibitors from 18 countries presented their products and services over a net exhibition space of 2,900m². Compared to the previous event, this was an increase of 5% in floor space and 7% in visitor numbers. Visitors to the event were mainly from Russia (90%); the rest were trade visitors from European countries and from the CIS. BLECH Russia 2013 attracted sheet metal working specialists at all levels, with the majority working in production (56%) or having managerial responsibility (24%).

“The third BLECH India, held in April 2013 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai, grew by more than a third in floor space, from some 3,000m² in 2011 to more than 4,000m² in 2013. The show attracted 187 exhibitors from 16 countries and 5,686 trade visitors. The visitor survey revealed that the quota of visitors from top management was very high, with 36% of the visitors being company owners, 20% being senior department heads and other employees with managerial responsibilities, and 27% being managing directors, Board Members or department heads,” the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

The first BLECH event at Suzhou, China in May 2013 attracted 4,497 sheet metal working professionals from Jiangsu province, along with delegates from over 30 countries and most regions across China. BLECH China attracted a widespread mix of domestic Chinese and international companies including suppliers from Germany, Italy, Switzerland, Turkey, Austria and the Netherlands.

BLECH Russia and BLECH India will return in spring 2015 to St Petersburg and Mumbai. After the successful launch of BLECH China, there are now plans in place for the second BLECH China at Suzhou Exhibition Centre from 7 - 9 May 2014.
