# Don't miss Metal Expo 2023

[TARİH: 01.09.2023 ISMR]

The fifth edition of Metal Expo 2023 wil be held from 27-30 September 2023 at the Istanbul Expo Centre in Turkey. Focusing on the metals industry, and particularly iron and steel innovations, it spans three halls and 16,000 square metres.

"Metal Expo brings together well-known names in the industry and construction sectors, serving as a major meeting point. It is an ideal platform to explore the latest developments and innovations in the sector, establish connections with leading suppliers and forge collaborations. Industry leaders will showcase the latest iron and steel products and technologies; organise business tours and share valuable insights," the show organiser, Sky Trade Fairs, told /SMR.

Metal Expo also offers a conference programme featuring panels, seminars and special presentations. As part of the trade fair, a metal workshop will be held on 28 September 2023, bringing together the Turkish steel industry and manufacturing sectors. It will highlight topics such as increased value-added production; localisation in the industry and environmental transformation, focusing on decarbonisation and low carbon consumption. "Metal Expo hosted more than 10,000 domestic and international visitors (from 62 countries) in 2022. Turkey's Deputy Minister of Industry and Technology, Hasan Büyükdede, visited the exhibition and chatted with participants. Visitors from 54 provinces in Turkey also spent time at the show," concluded Sky Trade Fairs.
