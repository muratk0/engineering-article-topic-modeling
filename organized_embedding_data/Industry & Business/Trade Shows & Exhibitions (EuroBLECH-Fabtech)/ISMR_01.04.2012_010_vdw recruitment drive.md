# VDW recruitment drive

[TARİH: 01.04.2012 ISMR]

METAV, THE international exhibition for manufacturing technology and automation, focused specifically on finding new recruits this year. VDW, the German machine tool association, is a co-organiser of METAV. The VDW recruitment foundation is campaigning for excellent training and better communication between vocational colleges, the schools responsible for general education and industry. The aim is to improve the image of machine manufacturing as a profession and thus to encourage more young people to opt for careers in metal processing fields. Another major project by the VDW recruitment foundation is to train and qualify instructors and teachers, so that apprenticeships in the metal processing industry are made more attractive.

At the press conference to mark the opening of the exhibition on 28 February 2012, the VDW recruitment foundation presented the results achieved from work done over the past three years. Dr.-Ing. Jan Braasch stressed that small and medium-sized companies in Germany are suffering to a particularly large extent from a lack of skilled staff. They are unable to find enough suitable candidates for apprenticeships. In 2010/2011, for example, there were more than 2,000 too few applicants for the apprenticeships available. The situation is similar where jobs in the engineering field are concerned: there were only 3,960 applicants for 5,177 vacancies.

Dr Wilfried Schafer, Executive Director of the VDW

VDW recently published record results for Germany’s machine tool industry: production output grew by 33 per cent in 2011, the highest figure ever achieved in the sector’s history. For 2012, production output is expected to rise by another 5 per cent.

“The high level of demand and full order books are particularly encouraging for the German manufacturers, who with approximately 490 firms constitute the biggest single exhibitor grouping,” reported Dr Schäfer of VDW. “Even if demand for capital goods may be taking a slight breather in 2012 (as some economic pundits are predicting) there are plenty of issues still awaiting a resolution,” he added, citing keywords like energy-efficiency and resource-economy in the production process, new drive concepts in the automotive industry or the use of new materials in innumerable application categories. ■
