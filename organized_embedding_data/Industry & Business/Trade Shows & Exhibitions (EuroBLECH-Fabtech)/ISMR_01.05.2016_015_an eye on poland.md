# AN EYE ON POLAND

[TARİH: 01.05.2016 ISMR]

TM (Innovations-Technologies-Machines) Poland in Poznañ will take place from 7-10 June 2016. Exhibitors and visitors in fields such as production automation; metallurgy and metal processing and modern industrial equipment are set to enjoy a rich programme of events.

The number of exhibitors and visitors at the event has been constantly increasing. In 2014, hundreds of companies from 30 countries presented their products in Poznañ, and the exhibition was visited by 16,728 visitors from Poland and abroad. Nearly 50% of exhibitors at the show last year were from outside Poland.

This year's exhibition will be organised over nine halls and incorporates seven themed exhibitions (MACH-Tool; Metalforum; Surfex; Welding; HAPE; Science for the Economy and Industrial Health and Safety). Visitors can expect to see innovations in industry sectors such as machine tools; automotive; welding; varnishes; energy and rail.

Machinery and equipment for metalworking, welding or paint coating; hydraulics; pneumatics and metallurgy as well as industrial robots and manipulators will be on show in the halls at Poznañ. The Industrial Subcontracting Exhibition, and its associated matchmaking meetings, will also run alongside the ITM Poland Fair.

## Conferences, seminars and panels

Several events will run alongside the ITM Poland Fair. The Polish Union of Steel Distributors (PUDS) is preparing a conference entitled “The Construction Steel Technical Forum” which will be held on 8 June 2016 at the Poznañ International Fair during ITM Poland 2016. The event is aimed at representatives of companies operating in construction steel production, machining and distribution as well as all companies using these products in their own production processes. The event’s scope includes construction steels for industries such as construction; offshore; automotive and transportation; and also for producers of machines and devices.

The Stainless Steel Association (SSN) will also be holding a seminar on “Cleaning and Preservation of Stainless Steel” on 8 June 2016. It will highlight the rising number of stainless steel applications, with both producer and user perspectives, and discuss methods to ensure durability. A variety of interesting solutions, ideas and possibilities will be presented.

MM Magazyn Przemysłowy (Industrial Magazine) is inviting attendees to take part in discussions at the MM Speakers’ Corner. Here, on 8 June 2016, managers of Poland’s largest industrial companies, with science and administration representatives, will be debating the state of Polish industry and the direction of its development. They will also try to pinpoint a variety of solutions which, if implemented, will boost the competitiveness of domestic companies in domestic and global markets.
