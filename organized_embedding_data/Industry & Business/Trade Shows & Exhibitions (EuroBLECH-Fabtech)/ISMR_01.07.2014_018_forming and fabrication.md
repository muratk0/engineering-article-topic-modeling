# FORMING AND FABRICATION

[TARİH: 01.08.2014 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: “FABTECH is the largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event in North America”

## We preview this year’s FABTECH exhibition from 11-13 November 2014 in Atlanta

North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event will be held at the Georgia World Congress Center, Atlanta, GA, USA. The event, to be held from 11-13 November 2014, is expected to cover more than 500,000 net square feet and is anticipated to attract over 27,000 attendees and 1,400 exhibiting companies.

FABTECH provides a convenient ‘one-stop shop’ venue where visitors can meet suppliers, see the latest industry products and developments, and find the tools to improve productivity, increase profits and discover new solutions for metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs. The event also provides learning opportunities beyond the exhibits with over 100 educational sessions and expert-led presentations on the latest industry trends and technology in the metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing industries.

This year also saw several regional FABTECH shows held in different parts of the world. FABTECH Canada took place from 18-20 March 2014 at the Toronto Congress Centre. On 10-12 April 2014, the inaugural FABTECH India, co-located with the India Institute of Welding’s Weld India Exhibition, was held in New Delhi. From 6-8 May 2014, FABTECH Mexico took place at the Centro Banamex in Mexico City.

## Show highlights

The five FABTECH co-sponsors represent a wide variety of expertise and include: SME, the American Welding Society (AWS), the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA), the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI). Together, these associations bring technical proficiency and industry insight to FABTECH.

To guide attendees to products and services easily, the FABTECH 2014 show floor is organised into Pavilions that are geared toward specific industries and technologies. FABTECH will encompass Building B (Forming & Fabricating, Finishing, METALFORM) and Building C (Welding, Tube/Pipe/Wire technology) at the Georgia World Congress Center.

“FABTECH serves the smallest job shops looking for a competitive edge to the largest Fortune 500 manufacturer seeking innovative solutions to complex problems. High-level company executives, manufacturing and engineering professionals and business owners from major industries, including aerospace, agriculture, appliance, automotive OEMs and their suppliers, construction, heavy equipment, military and defence, mining and utilities, recreational equipment, and non-automotive transportation, attend FABTECH to discover new technologies and source products to improve their business,” the organiser explained.

This year’s educational sessions at FABTECH offer over 100 targeted technical, operational, economic and managerial sessions. The Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, Int’l (FMA), SME, Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI), co-sponsor the sessions on cutting; finishing; forming & fabricating; management; job shop solutions; automation & robotics; stamping and tube & pipe. All sessions are two hours in length, offering practical knowledge that can immediately be used. Sessions with Tech Tours combine classroom instruction followed by expert-led guided tours on the show floor to see technology operating in designated booths.

The American Welding Society (AWS) will also present a comprehensive line-up of welding education. Led by the industry’s top professionals, programmes focus on best practices and new commercial developments in welding and thermal spray.

For further details of the 2014 FABTECH exhibition, see www.fabtechexpo.com

## A strong pedigree

FABTECH 2013 in Chicago set records for numbers of attendees and exhibitors as well as for floor space. A total of 40,667 attendees visited 1,573 exhibitors to see live equipment demonstrations at product displays spread across 650,000 square feet of exhibits at McCormick Place. The FABTECH educational conference, held simultaneously with the four-day expo, included a number of expert-led sessions on some of the hottest topics in manufacturing.

“While breaking our own records is impressive, most important were the reports we received from attendees and exhibitors,” said John Catalano, FABTECH show co-manager. “Attendees were impressed with the record size and scope of the show and the number of new products and innovative technologies on display. Exhibitors were enthusiastic and reported that sales activity was brisk and leads were plentiful.”

FABTECH attendees and exhibitors in Chicago also raised more than US$ 15,000 for the victims of the severe storms and tornados that devastated Washington, Illinois and other areas of the state on 17 November 2013. The five FABTECH co-sponsors made a financial commitment to start the campaign with all funds collected going to the American Red Cross to help victims of the storms. ■
