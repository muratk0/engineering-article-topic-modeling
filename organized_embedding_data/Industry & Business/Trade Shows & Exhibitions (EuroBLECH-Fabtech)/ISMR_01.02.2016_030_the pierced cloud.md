# THE PIERCED CLOUD

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

Two aluminium plates, stacked one upon another, are riveted at various points and arch to form the image of a cloud made of metal in Montpellier, France

“What’s floating in there?” ask passersby who glance into a courtyard off the Rue des Trésoriers de la Bourse in Montpellier (France). The answer to this question is as simple as it is unique - two aluminium plates, stacked one upon another, are riveted at various points and arch to form the image of a cloud made of metal.

The “Festival des Architectures Vives”, over two weeks, lured artists and architects to Montpellier at the beginning of June 2015. They presented their works throughout the city. Included was the floating “Cloudscape” by FAKT, an office of architects in Berlin.

## Form and structure

The four young architects described their artwork: “We made a conscious decision in favour of aluminium. It is relatively light in weight and thus easily shipped. It offers a ‘hovering feeling while the viewer perceives – through its spatial and structural curves – varying effects and reflections from every viewpoint.”

The expertise and machinery needed to create this Cloudscape, 6.4 x 4.5m in size and almost 200kg in weight, were provided by the Karl Dieringer Company. Christopher Kern, junior executive at Dieringer, worked with the young team of architects to find the appropriate solution.

“To cut thousands of holes in this aluminium sheet, two millimetres thick, we used our TRUMPF TruLaser 3060. It encountered no problems with cutting holes, from 6 - 80 millimetres in size, in the work-pieces with their mirrored foil.”

An art installation of this type was also a ‘first’ for the founders of the FAKT office.

“Such experimental and elaborate projects can be successful only with strong collaboration. In this case, Dieringer supported us with the manufacture and AMAG Austria Metall contributed the material,” explained FAKT. ■
