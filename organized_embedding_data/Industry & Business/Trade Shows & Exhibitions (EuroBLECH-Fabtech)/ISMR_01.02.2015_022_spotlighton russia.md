BLECH Russia takes place from 25 to 27 March 2015 at the recently completed ExpoForum in St Petersburg, at the heart of Russia’s fastest growing industrial region. It will be the fourth in a highly successful series of trade shows. Nearly 4,400 trade visitors attended the 2013 exhibition in St Petersburg, generating very positive feedback from participating firms.

The exhibition profile encompasses all core sectors of the sheet metal working process: Sheet metal; finished and semi-finished products; forming; separation and joining technology; flexible sheet metal working; machine elements; handling; surface treatment as well as tools and dies.

This biannual show attracts attendance from production specialists, buyers, designers and quality controllers in the sheet metal working industry. Visitors are industry professionals from key industries including the automotive industry and its suppliers, mechanical engineering, sheet metal products and components (white and brown goods), iron and steel production, steel and aluminium construction, rolling mills and ship building.

## A leading showcase

Leading companies from around the world, as well as Russian firms specialising in the supply of machinery and products to the sheet metal market, have already secured stands at the show. They will be presenting their products and latest developments to buyers and engineers from leading manufacturing sectors. They are expected to include visitors from the automotive industry, mechanical engineering, sheet metal products and components suppliers, iron and steel producers, steel and aluminium fabrication companies, rolling mills and shipbuilding.

“The St. Petersburg region is the fastest growing industrial area in Russia, offering high investment potential together with a large base of already established international production companies. The third BLECH Russia in 2013 reflected the positive growth prospects for Russia’s sheet metal working industry with an increase in visitor numbers and floor space. 127 exhibitors from 18 countries presented their products and services to 4,387 trade visitors,” the organiser, Restec Brooks, told ISMR.

“BLECH Russia 2015 continues to attract exhibitors who are confident about the longterm business opportunities on offer in Russia’s sheet metal working sector. International participants already include Dayton Progress, a global leader in precision press tool components and accessories and II-VI Deutschland, specialist in engineering materials and opto-electronic components. Exhibiting from Turkey will be Ajan Elektronik; well established in the Russian market as a supplier of high-tech sheet metal machinery. Also seeking opportunities in Russia are Chinese firms, Dongguan Vision Tool & Mould Co. and Shandong Juli Welding Co.”

Some of Russia’s leading sheet metal firms are also participating in the show. They include St Petersburg-based Tochnaya Sila/Precise Power Group, a major manufacturer of punching presses, punching tools and automatic lines for steel fabrications and Abamet, supplier of own-brand metal working equipment as well as products such as lathes and milling equipment.

Despite the current economic and political issues in the region, a new World Bank Group report finds that in the past year, the Russian Federation made it easier for local entrepreneurs to do business by implementing regulatory reforms in the areas of starting a business and registering property. ■

## Russia’s sheet metal working sector

With a predicted annual GDP growth of 1.1-1.2 per cent through 2014-2015 Russia, as one of the BRIC countries, offers plenty of business opportunities, such as the supply of energy efficient manufacturing technologies and equipment including machine tools and tooling.
