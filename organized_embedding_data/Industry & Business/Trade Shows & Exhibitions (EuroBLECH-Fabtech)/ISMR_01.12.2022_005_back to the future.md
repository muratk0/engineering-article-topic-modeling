## SOUTHERN MANUFACTURING

Farnborough International Exhibition Centre 8th to 10th February 2022

We preview Southern Manufacturing in Farnborough, UK, from 8-10 February 2022.

Southern Manufacturing & Electronics will be making a welcome return to Farnborough (UK) from 8-10 February 2022. It is a comprehensive annual industrial exhibition in the UK and a major pan-European showcase for new technology in machinery, production equipment, electronic production and assembly, tooling and components as well as subcontract services.

For those involved in design, engineering production and procurement, it offers the latest in production hardware, components, consumables and technology serving a wide range of industries (including aerospace/ defence; automotive; electronics assembly/ production; industrial machinery; packaging/ handling/logistics; energy; marine; medical; mechanical engineering and metal processing).

The event will be held at Farnborough International Exhibition and Conference Centre, a new £35m 20,000m² venue offering modern facilities, easy access and free onsite parking. Co-located with Southern Manufacturing & Electronics, AutoAero is a highly specialised event focused on suppliers to the UK's aerospace and automotive engineering sectors. ISMR is a media supporter of the event and will distribute its February issue throughout the show.

ISMR SAYS:

"Southern Manufacturing is an extremely accessible show for the rich eco-system of engineering SMEs across the UK and, increasingly, continental Europe."

## Business in focus

From modest beginnings as a regional event, Southern Manufacturing has evolved into a fully-fledged national show, with increasing influence across continental Europe and further afield. In 2018, it entered its third decade with a move into its new permanent home at the Farnborough International Exhibition and Conference Centre.

"The show's eagerly-awaited revival comes as engineering firms across the UK are getting back to business and are keen to reinvigorate their order books. Many of the show's regular participants have weathered the turbulent conditions of the last 20 months and are now eager to pitch their stalls. As in previous years, the show features an incredible array of high-tech production, mechanical and electronics components; tooling; industrial hardware; business and subcontract services from all over the UK, continental Europe and beyond," said the show organiser, European Trade & Exhibition Services (ETES).

ETES expects the show to be fully booked by the time it opens and is confident of 'coming near 2020's pre-COVID, record-breaking attendance of roughly 9,000 people'. With its wide range of exhibitors, Southern Manufacturing aims to provide answers to 'a variety of procurement challenges in one visit' - perhaps even more important given the current difficulties of travel and engagement in face-to-face business.

## Safety guidelines

"Wide-ranging anti-infection precautions, including fresh air ventilation throughout the building, help to make Farnborough International Exhibition a comparatively low-risk environment in which to conduct face-to-face business," ETES told ISMR.

In line with government guidelines and on event industry recommendations, COVID-19 status for exhibitors, visitors, contractors and event staff will be validated on arrival at the event. ETES will continue to follow all local and national government guidelines and will communicate changes and updates to its COVID policy via the website and by email.

"All attendees are kindly asked to respect the space of others, particularly in crowded areas. From 10 Dec 2021, face coverings must be worn in indoor settings. Spare masks will be available from us. Shields will be installed in busy areas involving frequent face-to-face contact. Attendant staff will be provided with face visors/face coverings. Dedicated hand hygiene stations will be located within all high traffic areas throughout the hall. Farnborough International will also ensure a rigorous cleaning regime of all public areas throughout the entire event," confirmed the show organiser.

## Exhibition showcases

The Machinery Zone is traditionally its most popular and 2022 will probably be no exception, as visitors flock to see new production hardware from many of the UK's top vendors. This includes machinery companies such as C. Dugard; Haas Automation; Hurco Europe; MACH Machine Tools and YMT Technologies. XYZ Machine Tools will be revealing its new XYZ SS 65 twin/sub-spindle turning centre for the first time at a UK exhibition. Laser hardware

will also be well represented at Southern 2022. Laser Lines will be highlighting examples of its solutions for industrial processes such as marking, engraving, drilling, welding, soldering, brazing and cladding. The company will be demonstrating some of its smaller laser welding and laser marking equipment at the show. Other well-known vendors in this space include TLM Laser (distributors for Foba), BODOR, Univet, Swisstec, Alphalaser, Universal, LasX, 4Jet, Innolas, Bofa, and Boxford (with its latest range of laser cutters, markers and engravers including fibre and CO2 metal cutting versions).

"Ancillary hardware such as workholding, tooling and metrology will also be showcased, with hundreds of well-known firms such as Thame Workholding, Faro, Guhring, Loctite-Henkel, Nikon Metrology and others. Aberlink will be showcasing the Extol CNC CMM with automation, claimed to be the world's first CMM to utilise a delta mechanism, and the Axiom CNC CMM (complete inspection systems)," added the show organiser.

"The show is also a great destination for sourcing production and planning aids, hardware and workplace products. Total Airtool Services returns to the exhibition for 2022, alongside Kanya UK and workplace storage equipment supplier, Bott. MIE Solutions will showcase its Trak Pro ERP software system for all manufacturing industries. E-Max Systems will be demonstrating its E-Max ERP, a system to manage all the critical elements of manufacturing and engineering, from initial enquiry to invoicing, tailored to fit individual needs," it added.

Subcontract services is the other main aspect of the show. UK sheet metal specialists exhibiting in 2022 include Hampshire-based fine tolerance precision sheet metal fabricator Barlow Sheet Metal, which specialises in servicing customers in the electronics & electromechanical industry sectors.

ISO 9001-2015-accredited Havant Sheet Metal specialises in the manufacture of quality sheet metal components and fabrications in stainless steel, mild steel and aluminium, with particular expertise in fine limit sheet metal, welding, machining, tube-forming, assembly, powder coating & stove enamelling.

## New seminar programme

Alongside the trade show and demonstration areas, the free seminar programme will return for 2022 in a socially distanced format. Subject areas include reducing energy consumption for manufacturers, new technology, innovation, supply chain and business strategy along with an evaluation of current industrial and post-Brexit regulations.

The programme is a complementary blend of technical and business topics covering a wide range of issues facing manufacturing businesses today. All seminars will take place at stand M310 and attendance at the seminars can be booked online via the show website at www.industrysouth.co.uk

Admission to the exhibition is free of charge – registrations can be made on www.industrysouth.co.uk/register/ For further details, see www.industrysouth.co.uk
