# Don't miss Global Industrie 2022

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

For its fourth edition, following the success of its September edition in Lyon, Global Industrie returns to Paris Nord Villepinte in France from 17-20 May 2022. Around 1,500 machines will be in operation on the exhibition floors and fifteen industrial sectors will be represented at the event.

"Global Industrie is the only event in France to cover the whole of industry. It brings together the entire ecosystem (start-ups; large companies; subcontractors; manufacturers of industrial equipment or solutions; competitive clusters; research centres; incubators etc.), as well as the whole value chain (R&D; design; production; maintenance; service; training etc.) and all its user markets (transport; energy; mechanical engineering etc.)," the organiser, GL Events, told ISMR.

It incorporates four complementary exhibitions: MIDEST (industrial subcontracting); SMART INDUSTRIES (smart, efficient and digital industry); INDUSTRIE (manufacturing technologies and equipment) and TOLEXPO (solutions and equipment for sheet metalworking). This year, it expects to welcome 2300 exhibitors and an expected 40,000 visitors from 85 countries.

The event is under the patronage of the French President, Emmanuel Macron, and hosts ministers, elected officials, ambassadors and representatives of major European professional industrial organisations. Launched in 2021, eight channels in the GI Channels programme made it possible for visitors and exhibitors to participate in the event both physically and digitally.

During the exhibition itself, four major events will also be held for visitors to enjoy. The Golden Tech 2022 Challenge rewards those who excel in nine selected professions. GI Avenir is a dedicated area for employment, training and job opportunities. GI Awards recognise exhibitors who have developed an innovative product, service or expertise. GI Conferences will host over 160 experts and manufacturers at a series of events such as masterclasses, Keynotes and roundtables.■
