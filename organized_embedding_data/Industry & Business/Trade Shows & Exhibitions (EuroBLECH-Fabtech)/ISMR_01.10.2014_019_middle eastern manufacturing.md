# Middle Eastern manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

STEELFAB 2015 (26-29 January in Sharjah, UAE) is the region’s main metal working, metal manufacturing and steel fabrication trade show. It has succeeded in taking advantage of a growing economy and the euphoria surrounding the winning bid to host the World Expo 2020 in Dubai, UAE.

“SteelFab 2014 achieved phenomenal growth, adding 50 per cent more exhibition space to accommodate a 25 per cent rise in the number of exhibitors. The increase in visitor turnout too saw a significant surge of 25 per cent, an unprecedented rise in the 10-year history of SteelFab,” the organiser told ISMR.

SteelFab will be even bigger with a new hall added to the show offering 24,000 square metres of exhibition space. Over 270 direct exhibitors from 26 countries representing 700 plus manufacturers will be exhibiting at the show.

Most exhibitors display running machines and demonstrations including laser punching, bending, welding displays, robotic cutting displays, orbital welding displays and CNC profile cutting metals.

Also showcased on the show floor will be surface preparation and painting; galvanizing; steel & special steel; metal forming machinery (coil handling, coil forming); punching; press brakes; shearing; grinding and cutting; tooling for machinery material handling and related tools and accessories.

According to latest industry reports on the GCC construction market, Saudi Arabia continues to have the highest market share of 44.3 per cent with projects valued at US$ 1.1 trillion. The UAE is in the second place at 30.3 per cent with a US$ 717.7bn project value, and Qatar has projects worth US$ 270.1 billion, accounting for 11.2 per cent.

While this takes into account all the projects that are currently under way, the volume of announced and planned projects in the UAE in 2014 alone is expected to be valued at US$ 315 billion, with the government announcing a number of major development projects and increased spending on social infrastructure development.
