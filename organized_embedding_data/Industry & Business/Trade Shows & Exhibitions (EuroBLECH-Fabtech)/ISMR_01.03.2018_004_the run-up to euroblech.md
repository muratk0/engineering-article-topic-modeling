# The run-up to EuroBLECH

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

This year will see the 25th edition of EuroBLECH, the International Sheet Metal Working Technology Exhibition, which will take place from 23–26 October 2018 in Hanover, Germany. Already, more than 1,300 companies from 39 countries have secured their stands.

"Industry experts, manufacturers and suppliers will come to Hanover to discover the latest technological developments in sheet metal working and processing, invest in new machinery for modern manufacturing and exchange expertise," the show organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

"As the world's leading exhibition for the sheet metal working industry, EuroBLECH offers a global platform for the presentation of the latest technology to a specialised audience of the industry's key purchasers and decision-makers."

Industry 4.0 will be one of the major focus points at EuroBLECH 2018 as the industry adjusts to the development towards smart manufacturing and digitalisation.

A total of 60,636 visitors from 102 countries attended EuroBLECH in 2016 and 1,505 companies from 41 countries showcased their products and services over a record net exhibition space of 87,800m².

See www.euroblech.com for further details.
