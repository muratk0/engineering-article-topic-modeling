# Fabtech Mexico 2018 Brings Latest Manufacturing Innovations To Mexico City

[TARİH: 01.06.2018 The Fabricator]

FABTECH

®

Mexico, Latin America’s leading manufacturing event for metal fabricating, forming, welding, and finishing, hosted more than 11,700 committed equipment specifiers and buyers May 2-4, 2018, at Mexico City’s Centro Banamex facility. With more than 550 exhibitors, the threeday show spanned some 147,000 square feet of floor space, featuring the latest metal manufacturing and welding equipment for focused attendees coming primarily from Mexico and Central and South America. In addition to viewing live manufacturing product demonstrations, attendees met with foremost industry experts and attended educational sessions covering automation, welding, cutting, forming, and fabricating for all facets of metal manufacturing.

"FABTECH Mexico has grown in popularity and influence each year during its 10-year run," said Matthew Rubin, American Welding Society director of conventions and meeting services. "Providing a central meeting place for attendees to explore new products, learn, and interact has quickly made FABTECH Mexico the premier show for metal manufacturing in Latin America."

The event is jointly sponsored by the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association Intl. (FMA), SME, the American Welding Society (AWS), the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA), and the Chemical Coaters Association Intl. (CCAI).

FABTECH is known throughout North America as the leading metal fabricating event with shows in the U.S., Canada, and Mexico. The U.S. shows rotate each year among Chicago, Atlanta, and Las Vegas. FABTECH Mexico rotates each year between Monterrey and Mexico City, and FABTECH Canada is held every two years in Toronto.
