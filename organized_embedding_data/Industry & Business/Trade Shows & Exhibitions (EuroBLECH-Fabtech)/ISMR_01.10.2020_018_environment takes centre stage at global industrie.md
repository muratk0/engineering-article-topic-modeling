# Environment takes centre stage at Global Industrie

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

“AT THIS TIME of ecological and energy transition and the recovery plan funds earmarked for it, Global Industrie is going greener than ever in 2021. The Lyon edition of the exhibition in France, which will take place from 16-19 March 2021 at Eurexpo Lyon, will be an ideal opportunity to come and meet the main players in the sector and attend several events, particularly conferences, which will be specifically dedicated to it,” said show organiser, GL Events.

Thirty billion euros i.e. one third of the value of the recovery plan set up by the French government, will be devoted to the ecological transition. This illustrates the importance placed on ecology and on economic opportunities offered by the energy efficiency, transport and energy generation sectors. This “green plan” aims to encourage a new growth model based on a low-carbon economy, energy saving and green innovations.

At the roundtable devoted to Industrial Territories, organised on 2 July 2020 as part of Global Industrie Connect, several projects were unveiled. Global Industrie 2021 in Lyon will include an area entirely dedicated to the environment where a complete and expanded offer will be presented. This area will feature the main players in the sector, in fields as varied as air; instrumentation – metrology; water and waste water; pipes – networks; pumps – valves; waste treatment - recycling and re-use; collection vehicles; sites and soil; lighting; energy – energy recovery; smart grids; carbon management; mobility; institutions – research and regional and international collectives.
