# Success for Global Industrie 2023

[TARİH: 01.05.2023 ISMR]

ISMR attended the Global Industrie event at Eurexpo in Lyon, France, from 7-10 March 2023. It is a showcase for industrial innovation with more new developments and solutions. The event is under the patronage of the French President, Emmanuel Macron, and plays host to several ministers, elected officials, ambassadors and representatives of some of the largest European professional organisations in the industry.

Despite widespread strikes across France, the event attracted 2,300 exhibitors over 100,000 square metres of exhibition floor area. According to the show organiser, around 38,700 visitors attended the event (up 75% on its 2021 figures for the event).

“Global Industrie brings together all industry players in one place. It was divided into 15 zones, specialising in fields such as digitalisation and automation; electronics; additive manufacturing & 3D printing; measurement; robotics; energy and sustainable development," commented the show organiser.

It incorporated five complementary exhibitions: MIDEST (industrial subcontracting); SMART INDUSTRIES (smart, efficient and digital industry); INDUSTRIE (manufacturing technologies and equipment); TOLEXPO (solutions and equipment for sheet metalworking) and MEASUREMENTWORLD. Previous editions of the show have been held in even years in Paris and odd years in Lyon, France. This geographical diversification enables it to enhance both its offer and the variety of its visitors.

The show's Golden Tech challenge was to evaluate the skill, technical knowledge, expertise and creativity of candidates for fifteen selected professions. It combined novel features such as individual technical tests and collective creative tests. The theme for 2023 was ‘Hydrogen Cars' and two concept cars were on display; the NamX French hydrogen concept SUV and the H2 24h Le Mans hydrogen concept car. Golden Tech winners were selected at the show.

A series of events during the show were held around the theme 'The industry is mobilising'. These included masterclasses, Keynotes by factory directors and technical managers as well as roundtables. Topics included reindustrialisation and sovereignty; energy; green growth; 15 zones covering over 100,000 square metres technologies and skills. The next Global Industrie event will take place from 25-28 March 2024 in Paris Villepinte.
