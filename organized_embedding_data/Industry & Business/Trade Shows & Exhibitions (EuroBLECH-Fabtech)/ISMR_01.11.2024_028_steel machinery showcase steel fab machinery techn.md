# STEEL MACHINERY SHOWCASE STEEL FAB MACHINERY TECHNOLOGY EQUIPMENT 20TH EDITION

[TARİH: 01.11.2024 ISMR]

Meet ISMR at the 20th SteelFab exhibition from 13-16 January 2025 in Sharjah, UAE. Show highlights include a welding competition, hosted buyer programme and two-day conference.

Emirates is set to host the 20th edition of SteelFab, a key Middle Eastern event for metalworking, metal manufacturing and steel fabrication, from 13-16 January 2025.

"The exhibition is expected to see extensive participation from local and international companies and manufacturers, as it will feature more than 800 major global brands from 35 countries showcasing their latest technologies and innovations in the metalworking field," show organiser, Expo Centre Sharjah, told ISMR.

Last year's event attracted over 6500 trade visitors from 65 countries, representing a 16% increase on the previous year's show.

## International participation and local players

The SteelFab trade show brings together well-known local and international players in the iron and steel industry to showcase modern machinery, equipment and tools for the metal sector, welding and cutting, pipe manufacturing and other related industries.

"Participants will represent steel industry leaders and major companies from countries such as the UK; Germany; India; Taiwan; China; Italy and Turkey. The exhibition will also host some of the most prominent UAE-based companies," explained Expo Centre Sharjah.

"SteelFab is the region's premier event for the metal manufacturing and steel fabrication industry. It serves as an important platform for manufacturers and companies operating in the field to showcase their latest products and services and connect with potential clients and partners," commented HE Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, CEO of Expo Centre Sharjah.

He also highlighted the success of the exhibition over the past twenty years, reflected in the high participation rates over the years. The 2025 show is expected to attract over 125 international companies and 170 local firms, an increase in the number of exhibitors compared to previous years. He also expects to see more visitors representing various industrial sectors.

## Focus on advanced technologies

The SteelFab 2025 exhibition places a special emphasis on the latest technologies and equipment used in the iron and steel industries. The trade show will spotlight the latest trends and advances in the manufacturing, iron and steel sectors, featuring extensive displays of modern laser equipment and metal cutting systems.

Welding and cutting; power tools; machine tools; pipe and tube machinery; stainless steel; AI (artificial intelligence) and robotics will be key attractions at the event. It constantly realigns with industry requirements, with AI and robotics being a recent addition to its core focus areas.

With the latest technological advances, such as IoT (Internet of Things) and automation, the metal fabrication industry is undergoing rapid changes. From product research and development, quality assurance and everything in between, technological advances are changing the manufacturing process. Smart sensors, immersive technology gadgets, sustainable materials and green energy sources are being embraced widely.

“Now, with the UAE government adopting policies to speed up digital transformation of the industrial sector in the country, SteelFab has emerged as a game changer for the regional metal working industry. Its role is helping regional players to develop digital capabilities that would help them gain an edge in a hypercompetitive market," explained the show organiser.

## Pipe and tube machinery

Pipe and tube machinery is an interesting growth segment at the next edition of SteelFab.

“Rising requirements from industries such as oil & gas; pipelines; refineries; power & water; MEP; food manufacturing; electrical; marine & offshore; furniture; construction & infrastructure; heavy industries and manufacturing has made the pipe and tube machinery segment an indispensable part of SteelFab. It will help us to bring more machinery, exhibitors, visitors and potential investors from across the world," explained Expo Centre Sharjah.

“Since the region has the largest concentration of energy resources, governments are investing continuously in their pipeline networks to ensure a high volume-to-weight ratio which sustains demand for pipes and tubes," it added.

The MEP (mechanical, electrical and plumbing) industry, another sector that sees heavy pipe and tube consumption, is expanding rapidly on the back of new tourism developments and investments flowing into the construction market. With demand for electricity increasing and regional governments planning to invest billions of dollars for additional generating capacity, pipes and tubes are also finding higher usage in power generation markets.

## Economic outlook

On 24 October 2024, Jihad Azour (director of Middle East and Central Asia Department, International Monetary Fund) pointed out that GCC countries have adapted, over the past few years, to several shocks and challenges in the region and across the world. This included the COVID-19 pandemic and oil shocks. He added that oil countries and GCC countries have maintained a certain level of growth, "despite OPEC+ and its agreements."

“For 2024, our projections are better than 2023. The growth is about 1.2 per cent in 2024 and will improve in 2025 to reach 4.2 per cent in 2025. The main driving force behind the growth in GCC countries is the development of the non-oil economy. This is a very important element. The development of the non-oil economy was a main lever for growth and the Gulf countries maintained a good level of growth ranging between three to four per cent for non-oil growth, under our investments that are aimed to develop other economic sectors in the future," he added.

These include renewable energy as well as technology which contributes to increasing the capacity of these countries to increasing revenues, diversifying the sources of revenue for economies and adapting to economic changes all over the world.

This year has been challenging, with conflicts causing devastating human suffering and economic damage. Oil production cuts are contributing to sluggish growth in many economies in the MENA region. The recent military escalation in Lebanon has also increased uncertainty in the region.
