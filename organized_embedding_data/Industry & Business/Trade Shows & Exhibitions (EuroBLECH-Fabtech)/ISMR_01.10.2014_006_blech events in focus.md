# BLECH events in focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2014 ISMR]

Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working 24-26 March 2015 St. Petersburg, Russia

Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working 22-25 April 2015 Mumbai, India

DON’T MISS TWO regional sheet metal events in Russia and India next year, organised by joint ventures with Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd.

BLECH Russia is a leading trade exhibition exclusively for the sheet metal industry in Russia. Last year’s event attracted 127 exhibitors from 18 countries and 4,387 trade visitors. The next BLECH Russia will take place in St. Petersburg from 24-26 March 2015 and is co-located with the new Fastener Fair Russia 2015 exhibition at the ExpoForum Exhibition Centre. See www.blechrussia.com/english/

Fastener Fair Russia 2015 is a trade show dedicated to the Russian fastener and fixing industry. It covers a comprehensive range of industrial fasteners, construction fittings, assembly and installation systems as well as manufacturing technology. See www.fastenerfair.com/russia. The St Petersburg Tech Fair will also be held from 24-26 March 2015.

India is on the way to becoming a manufacturing location of global status. BLECH India, the technology exhibition for sheet metal working, will be taking place for the fourth time from 22-25 April 2015 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre, Mumbai. With 187 exhibitors from 16 countries and 5,686 trade visitors, BLECH India 2013 benefitted from strong market growth in key industry sectors in India such as the automotive and construction industries. The outlook for the Indian sheet metal market was assessed as very positive by exhibitors at last year’s show. See www.blechindia.com

Having gained accession to the World Trade Organisation after 18 years of negotiation, Russia is keen to diversify its oil-dependent economy and presents a wide range of investment opportunities for overseas companies. A new Brooks Market Intelligence report on the sheet metalworking sector in Russia covers the main sheet metal and sheet metal equipment user industries.

See www.brooksreports.com and select ‘Russia's Sheet Metalworking Sector 2014’. ■
