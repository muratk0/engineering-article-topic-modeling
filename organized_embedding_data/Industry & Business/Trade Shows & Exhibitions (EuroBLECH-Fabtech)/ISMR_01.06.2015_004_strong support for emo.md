# Strong support for EMO

[TARİH: 01.06.2015 ISMR]

AFTER SIX years, EMO, the worldwide exhibition of machine tools, is back in Italy. Promoted by CECIMO, the European Association for Machine Tool Industries, the trade show will take place from 5-10 October 2015 at the Fieramilano exhibition hall (Milan).

“More than 110,000 square metres of net exhibition space has already been booked and over 1,300 companies have already registered for EMO MILANO 2015. The leaders in the company rankings are the Italians, closely followed by the Germans and the Taiwanese, followed by Chinese and Swiss exhibitors,” said the show organiser.

“The halls of the exhibition centre will host the biggest factory in the world, where machine tools, robots, automation, additive manufacturing, mechatronic solutions and auxiliary technologies will be exhibited, representing an industry that accounts for 64 billion euros.”

150,000 visitors from approximately 100 countries are expected for EMO MILANO 2015 which takes place alongside EXPO 2015.

“In the first quarter of 2015,” confirmed Alfredo Mariotti, Director of EMO MILANO, “the index, produced by the Studies Dept. of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, of the quantity of machine tool orders received by Italian manufacturers, registered an increase of 2.2 per cent compared to the same period in 2014, making this six positive quarters in a row. The increase in the internal market (+15.4 per cent) has been instrumental to this positive trend. This information confirms a new period of development and increase for the use of machine tools in Italy. This will materialise with stronger and stronger increases: +5.3 per cent in 2015, +5 per cent in 2016 and +7.4 per cent in 2017 (Oxford Economics’ figures) for the benefit of EMO MILANO 2015.

“This data, together with the positive forecast for the global use of machine tools that will reach 60 billion in 2015, 63 billion in 2016 (+5.2 per cent) and 66 billion in 2017 (+4.8 per cent) are good indications for a successful exhibition.”

## Patent award on levellers

THE BRADBURY Co., Inc. has been awarded Patent #8893537 for its advanced e•Drive® roller leveling technology. Bradbury has produced over 50 levelling machines equipped with this controlled tension technology in gauges ranging between 0.015” to 1”.

With its focused tension accelerator, the e•Drive® system is available on new levellers as well as being a retrofit to existing Bradbury levellers.
