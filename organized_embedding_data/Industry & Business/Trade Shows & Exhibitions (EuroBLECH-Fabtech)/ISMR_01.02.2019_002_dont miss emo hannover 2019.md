# Don't miss EMO Hannover 2019

[TARİH: 01.02.2019 ISMR]

BY 1 DECEMBER 2018, 1,780 exhibitors from 41 countries had registered for EMO Hannover 2019, which will take place in Hanover, Germany, from 16-21 September 2019.

"We are delighted with the fantastic response that EMO Hannover, the world's leading trade fair for metalworking, has again received from all sides," said EMO General Commissioner, Carl-Martin Welcker. "The exhibitor directory shows a clear plus in comparison to the last EMO and already reads like a who's who of the international machine tool industry. It's also good to see that many exhibitors have increased their stand space and are now even more representative than last time."

With the motto 'Smart technologies driving tomorrow's production!', EMO Hannover will feature a new exhibition area, 'IoT in production' to highlight networking in production.

Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, managing director of EMO organiser, VDW (German Machine Tool Builders Association), explained: "IoT in production provides a complete overview of the central aspects of networking, including data security, data analysis, process-monitoring, predictive maintenance, smart data management and more."

The new OPC-UA open interface standard, umati, will also draw a lot of attention in the 'IoT in production' area, as the new project is expected to 'go live' at EMO Hannover 2019. The first concrete OPC UA Companion Specification, based on a realistic set of parameters, will be presented at EMO Hannover 2019. The new open interface standard is being designed to connect machine tools to higher-level IT systems.

Visitors will see a large-scale demonstration of how umati works on the umati stand in Hall 9 at EMO Hannover. It is planned to link up at least 100 machines from national and international manufacturers, and to present the latest developments and internationally agreed draft of the Companion Specification. The VDW's umati interface (universal machine tool interface) is based on OPC UA and aims to ensure that data from machines, equipped with different control systems, is routed through an open, standardised connection. It is comparable to a USB stick.

Other EMO Hannover features will include the special Industry 4.0 area, which will bundle expertise in an expert forum for direct dialogue in cooperation with Konradin Verlag (as in 2017), as well as scientific presentations and activities by the WGP (Wissenschaftliche Gesellschaft für Produktionstechnik) and Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft.
