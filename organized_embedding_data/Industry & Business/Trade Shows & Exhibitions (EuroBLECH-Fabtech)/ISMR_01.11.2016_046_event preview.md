# EVENT PREVIEW

[TARİH: 01.11.2016 ISMR]

The US$ 116 million development of Almarkaz light industrial real estate project, the launch of the second phase of the Khalifa Industrial Zone Logistics Park - Non Free Zone warehousing and the opening of Renault Trucks in Saudi Arabia also signifies continued growth and work for steel contracting in the region.

"Steel fabricators can be assured of a steady rise in requirements from different sectors. SteelFab will help them explore and acquire the latest equipment, advanced technologies, new products and applications for all their metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs," added Mr. Midfa.

## An eye on engineering

Providing a comprehensive sourcing platform, the 13th SteelFab 2017 will feature a vertical - Fasteners World Middle East, special focus areas such as Welding & Cutting, Metal Cutting & Machine Tools, Tube & Pipe, Stainless Steel and Wear Resistance & Hard Facing. The show will also incorporate overseas pavilions, seminars and technical presentations as well as live displays.

SteelFab 2017 will be held at Expo Centre Sharjah, United Arab Emirates, from 16 to 19 January 2017 (opening timings will be from 10am to 7pm daily). Admission is free and restricted to trade and professional visitors only. It is organised and hosted by Expo Centre Sharjah with the support of the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry. The event will showcase machinery and equipment in metal forming, surface preparation and finishing, grinding & cutting, machining and other allied engineering disciplines.

The 12th SteelFab 2016 was held last January and featured more than 370 exhibitors and 8,176 visitors from 83 countries. Visitors from countries such as Afghanistan; Algeria; Armenia; Azerbaijan; Egypt; Ghana; Kazakhstan; Kenya; Libya; Nigeria; Sudan; Tanzania; Tunisia; Uganda and Ukraine viewed the show as a regional sourcing platform. The opening ceremony of the 2016 event also saw the launch of a new vertical- Fasteners World Middle East - besides the introduction of two special focus areas - Stainless Steel and Wear-Resistant & Hard Facing Cladding.
