# JOINING AND CUTTING SCHWEISS & SCHNEIDEN

[TARİH: 01.08.2023 ISMR]

Don't miss SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN from 11-15 September 2023 in Essen, Germany.

ISMR SAYS: "Digitalisation is in the spotlight at SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN."

At Messe Essen in Germany, more than 700 companies from 36 countries will present innovative products, technologies and services at the SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN exhibition (11-15 September 2023). This key event for joining, cutting and surfacing has attracted industry exhibitors.

such as Lincoln; ESAB; Microstep; ABICOR BINZEL; EWM; Fronius; Messer Cutting, and voestalpine, among others. Niche players, start-ups, research institutions and associations will also participate.

"Trade visitors can expect a complete overview of the entire industry at the exhibition. Networking, establishing business contacts, placing orders and preparing purchasing decisions are the main goals of many trade visitors at the show. They come from far and wide to Essen; about every second visitor is from overseas," Messe Essen told ISMR.

The show organisers have optimised the hall layout to provide exhibitors and visitors with “numerous synergies and a seamless experience." Halls 1 to 5 will be dedicated to companies from the welding sector, while Halls 6 to 8 will house exhibitors presenting cutting products and services.

“This strategic arrangement ensures that related products (such as surface technology; heat treatment; gas; consumables and filler materials; equipment; safety; health; quality assurance; service and digitalisation) are conveniently located in close proximity. Short distances and a perfect fit between exhibitors and visitors enhance the overall efficiency of the event," explained Messe Essen.

## Events and themed areas

Apart from the exhibition itself, there will be a comprehensive programme of events that provide added value for attendees.

The trade fair will feature a special show on digitalisation, in collaboration with the Industry Fusion Foundation IFF and the German Welding Society (DVS). Through a digital rally, trade visitors will gain insights into practical applications and the advantages of digital solutions.

"This innovation, facilitated by a dedicated app, will enable exhibitors and visitors to connect and engage in meaningful discussions about the integration of machine, man, AI and software in the industry. By showcasing the potential of digital solutions, the exhibition highlights the efficient use of resources, materials and manpower in welding, cutting and surfacing processes," explained the show organiser.

The impetus for putting digitisation solutions in the spotlight at SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN came from the Industry Fusion Foundation (IFF). The association is committed to the intelligent networking of the manufacturing industry.

Igor Mikulina, President of the IFF Foundation Board, commented: "Digital solutions in welding and cutting technology have the potential to raise productivity to an entirely new level of efficiency. Against this background, the non-profit Industry Fusion Foundation, in cooperation with DVS and Messe Essen, will focus on the concrete added values of digitised product solutions right through to the immense benefits of cross-manufacturer networking of the entire manufacturing process."

Visitors will also find themed areas at the trade fair:

■ The underwater technology section in Hall 7 will provide in-depth insights into this rapidly growing field.

■ International joint stands will allow attendees to explore products and services from specific countries including China, France, Japan, Korea, Taiwan and the USA.

The Young Innovative Companies programme, sponsored by the German Federal Ministry of Economics and Technology, will provide German startups with an affordable platform to present their latest solutions.

## DVS Congress

During SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN, the DVS Congress will also be held at Messe Essen. The event includes lectures on operational practice and numerous application-oriented, current research results from the completed projects of the Forschungsvereinigung Schweißen und verwandte Verfahren e.V. im DVS (German Welding Society).

"Guests from small and medium-sized companies, from industry, the skilled trades and science are cordially invited to exchange information on many new developments in joining, cutting and coating technology and to take valuable practices back to their professions. Another symposium will be devoted to the topic of underwater technology. With the DVS CAMPUS, students from joining, release and coating technology will have their own forum to present current results from their Bachelor, Master or Diploma theses to the professional world," commented the show organiser.

"The event not only showcases the latest innovations but also creates a platform for professionals to connect, exchange ideas and address the industry's evolving needs. Its emphasis on digitalisation highlights the industry's commitment to embracing technological advances and optimising processes for increased efficiency and sustainability," it added.

## In conclusion

SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN has been working with other major trade shows, such as STEELFAB in Sharjah (UAE), for several years. With BEIJING ESSEN WELDING & CUTTING and INDIA ESSEN WELDING & CUTTING, SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN is also present in the important markets of China and India.

## Getting there

By Car: North Rhine-Westphalia's motorway network plan makes travel by car easy and comfortable. Take the major Rhine-Ruhr motorways A3, A40, A42 and A52 to reach Messe Essen. Address for GPS navigation systems: Norbertstraße 2, 45131 Essen. The signs for the exhibition centre will guide you to the site where there are ample parking spaces. From there, you can reach Messe Essen on foot or with the exhibition centre bus. From there, the journey is five minutes with the Underground line 11.

By Train: The well connected Essen Central Railway Station is a hub for over 120 daily ICE, InterCity, EuroCity and InterRegio connections in all directions. From there, the journey is just five minutes with the Underground line 11.

By Air: Excellent connections to Düsseldorf Airport enable quick and convenient travel. Düsseldorf Airport is around 20 minutes' drive from the Essen exhibition grounds and is well connected by rail. There is a separate information counter for Messe Essen in the middle of the arrivals level of Terminal B at the airport.

On all trade fair days, the Airport Express bus departs every 30 minutes from the Arrivals level in front of the hall, Terminal A/B bus station 2. The trip takes about 20 minutes. The Airport Express will take visitors non-stop to Messe Essen for €25.00/€35.00 (and back to the airport).

For executives and specialists from large and medium-sized enterprises, the Essen/Mülheim airport is a convenient alternative. From there, Messe Essen can be reached by car or taxi within 10 minutes.
