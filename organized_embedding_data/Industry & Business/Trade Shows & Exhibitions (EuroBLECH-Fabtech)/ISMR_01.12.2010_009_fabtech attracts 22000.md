# FABTECH ATTRACTS 22,000

[TARİH: 01.12.2010 ISMR]

Some 22,000 visitors attended the Fabtech 2010 conference and exhibition at the Georgia World Congress Center in Atlanta (USA) from 2-4 November to experience the latest manufacturing innovations and discuss industry issues – an increase of 12% per cent from the 2006 Atlanta show.

Co-sponsored by the American Welding Society (AWS), Fabricators & Manufacturers’ Association Int’l (FMA), Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME), Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI), FABTECH is the largest tradeshow in North America dedicated to showcasing a full spectrum of metal forming, fabricating, welding, stamping, tube and pipe, and finishing equipment and technology. Filling nearly 371,000 square feet of booth space, the three-day show included 1,138 exhibitors, an increase of 23% per cent from the first show in Atlanta. The event also featured the unveiling of more than 500 new products.

“Our 2010 show brought together industry leaders from more than 50 countries to share the best and the

brightest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing ideas,” said Mark Hoper, FABTECH show co-manager. “This year’s show also included the successful debut of the Finishing Technologies Pavilion & Conference that allowed visitors to review finishing equipment, processes and services, and gain valuable technical insight.”

The 2010 show included three fully functional stamping lines in the METALFORM Technology Area; Keynote sessions on how manufacturers can gain a competitive advantage by going green and on how diversification is critical to survival; Solutions Showcase business improvement sessions; the 2010 Weld Trials Competition and more than 100 conferences, seminars, professional programmes and technical sessions.

The 2011 exhibition will be held in Chicago from 13-16 November. For more information, visit www.fabtechexpo.com
