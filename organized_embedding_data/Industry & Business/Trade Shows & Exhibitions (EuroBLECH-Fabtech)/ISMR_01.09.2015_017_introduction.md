## Sheet metal meets joining technology

The practical trade fair duo of Blechexpo and Schweisstec takes place every two years at the trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and covers the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology. In 2013, the event attracted 1153 exhibitors from 32 countries, as well as 32,599 expert visitors from 96 countries.

Visitors can gather information on modern cutting, joining, forming and stamping technologies at the event which now ranks second in the world on the list of trade fairs for sheet metal processing. The Schweisstec international specialist trade fair for joining technology is a strong complement, showcasing the relevant mechanical and thermal joining and connecting procedures as well as thermal welding processes. The show remit has also been expanded to include relevant areas for processing directly from the strip coil or the wire coil for electrical and electromechanical products.

The continuing success of the Blechexpo/Schweisstec trade fair group is due not least to its consistent focus as a meeting place for the industry in the primary European market. Based in the heart of industrial Europe, the large consumer and user market is a benchmark for the manufacturers of products, machines, systems and accessories in hardware and software for sheet metal component production.
