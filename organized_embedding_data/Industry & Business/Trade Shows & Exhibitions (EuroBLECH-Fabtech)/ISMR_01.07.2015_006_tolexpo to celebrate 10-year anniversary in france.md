# Tolexpo to celebrate 10-year anniversary in France

[TARİH: 01.08.2015 ISMR]

FROM 17–20 November 2015, Tolexpo (France’s leading sheet metal working event) will celebrate its ten-year anniversary with support from the French Ministry for the Economy, Industry and Digital Media.

The event, at the Paris Nord Villepinte Exhibition Centre, will feature the first ever Tolexpo Trophy. The Tolexpo Trophy, part of the Midest Trophies, is the only one in France dedicated exclusively to manufacturers of production equipment for sheet, tubing and profile metal working.

The Tolexpo Trophy is jointly run by the Tolexpo and Midest organisers. It is open only to Tolexpo exhibitors involved in the Tolexpo new products’ campaign. Created in 2009, the aim of this campaign is to promote major new products from the sector during the period between Tolexpo shows (November 2013 to November 2015). After studying each entry, a panel of journalists, engineering centre representatives and Tolexpo and Midest consultants will issue a shortlist of nominees in early October. The winner will be announced at the traditional Midest trophy award ceremony to be held at the Midest trade show after its official opening on Tuesday 17 November 2015. The list of nominees will be published in mid-October 2015 once the panel has finished its deliberations.

This initiative will showcase new products in the sheet metal sector (strengths and performances) presented by equipment manufacturers and retailers.

“The last show in 2013 saw no fewer than 64 new products exhibited at Tolexpo, including one international launch,” the organiser told ISMR. “Tolexpo is the French benchmark event dedicated to sheet metal, coil, tube and section equipment. More than 200 exhibitors (46% from outside France) from 65 countries were at the last event. There are 11,000 targeted visitors and we have a partnership agreement with the Midest and Maintenance Expo events. This complementary partnership makes the venture a unique event which brings the whole production chain together under one roof, from cutting to assembly through to final surface treatment, not forgetting the maintenance of equipment.”
