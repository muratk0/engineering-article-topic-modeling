After nearly two years of missed in-person opportunities, I am very pleased to cordially invite you to FABTECH

®

, North America’s largest metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing event. FABTECH will be the first large-scale manufacturing trade show headed back to McCormick Place in Chicago, September 13-16, 2021. Remember, as advised by FABTECH exhibitors and attendees, FABTECH in Chicago will no longer be held in November. Our new dates for all future FABTECH events held in Chicago will now be in September.

FABTECH will reunite exhibitors and attendees to get an unparalleled glimpse into the industry’s future and learn innovative methods to improve individual business performance.

At FABTECH you will experience the best in-person opportunity to:

Get up close to emerging equipment and services in cutting-edge exhibits

Learn about advanced technology and products that drive productivity and profitability

Obtain exclusive insights from education sessions and expert-led presentations covering new trends and technology

Join me this September at FABTECH when Chicago welcomes back the North American metal fabrication industry.
