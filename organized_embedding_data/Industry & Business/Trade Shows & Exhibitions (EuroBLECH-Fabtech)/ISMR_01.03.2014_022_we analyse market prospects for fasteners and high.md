# We analyse market prospects for fasteners and highlight a selection of recent technology developments

[TARİH: 01.03.2014 ISMR]

From relatively simple DIY screws to the most sophisticated complex joining technologies, sheet metal fastener and fixing technologies provide some of the most diverse and varied industry solutions worldwide. The industry is continuously developing an array of fastening products to suit diverse requirements in sectors such as aerospace, automotive, telecommunications, electronics, white goods, lighting etc.

Events such as Fastener Fair Hannover (which takes place from 8-10 April 2014), Fastener Fair Stuttgart and Fastener Fair Turkey (20-21 November 2014) have an important role as broad marketing platforms where the entire fastener and fixing industry can meet. Fastener Fair Stuttgart recently attracted 10,600 visitors and 811 exhibitors.

## A promising outlook

TechNavio's analysts forecast the global aerospace fasteners market to grow at a CAGR of 7.75 per cent over the period 2013-2018. One of the key factors contributing to this market growth is the increasing demand for commercial aircraft in the APAC region. The global aerospace fasteners market has also been witnessing the rise in demand for LCCs, said TechNavio. However, the increase in raw material prices could pose a challenge to the growth of this market.

According to a recent report by RnR Market Research, US demand for industrial fasteners is forecast to increase 4.3 per cent per year to US$ 14.8 billion in 2017.

“Sales growth will be supported by rebounding levels of motor vehicle manufacturing, along with rising aerospace equipment and machinery shipment levels. Fastener producers will benefit from renewed strength in both construction activity and fixed investment spending. In addition, gains will be bolstered by fastener manufacturers offering highly engineered, advanced fastener products that can be sold at a premium. On the other hand, demand will be held back by competition from producers in lower-cost regions, and from moderating raw material costs, both of which will serve to restrict industrial fastener price increases. These products will continue to face competition in a number of applications from alternative joining technologies, such as adhesives, clinching and welding,” said the analysts.

“In many cases, motor vehicle manufacturers will continue attempts to decrease the number of fasteners per vehicle to reduce weight and simplify production. However, fastener producers will find opportunities for growth by offering lighter and more durable product designs, as well as fasteners specifically for the increasing electric and hybrid vehicle market.”

“The market for standard fasteners is expected to outpace aerospace-grade fastener sales through 2017. This will largely be a reflection of the improved conditions in the motor vehicle market, which uses primarily standard fasteners. Externally threaded and application specific standard fasteners will post the fastest gains through 2017, although the outlook is positive for every major product category. While not forecast to match standard products, aerospace-grade fastener sales will fare better than they did during the 2007-2012 period,” concluded the analysts.

## New fastener technologies

A number of new fastening and joining technology developments have taken place over the past year. Here, we highlight a selection of innovations.

Actiri/8-Tree have introduced FastCheck as a rapid, repeatable way to inspect fasteners for depth and fitting angle. It supports common aerospace fasteners such as Hi-Lok, rivets and shaved-head fasteners. Up to 30 fasteners can be checked simultaneously – the system can also measure max./min. steps at the edge of the fastener.
