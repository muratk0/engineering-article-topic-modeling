# Don't miss EuroBLECH 2022 in Hanover

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

The world's leading trade exhibition for the sheet-metalworking industry is back and opens its doors from 25-28 October 2022 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds in Germany. A total of 1,300 exhibitors from 39 countries are ready to showcase their latest innovative manufacturing solutions covering a total net exhibition space of 88,000 square metres. Meet ISMR's team on its booth H92 in Hall 14 at the show!

Reflected in this year's motto 'Your gateway to a smarter future', EuroBLECH 2022 focuses on intelligent solutions and the ongoing automation and digitalisation of the manufacturing chain to enable an increase in output and efficiency.

As the world's leading exhibition for the industry, EuroBLECH 2022 provides the opportunity to finally catch up with the latest innovations that have been developed in the past few years, as well as meeting with new and existing business contacts.

“Experiencing machines and systems in action, fostering business relationships and discovering what the future of sheetmetalworking, live and in-person, is what EuroBLECH is about!" said Evelyn Warwick, Event Director, EuroBLECH, on behalf of the organiser Mack-Brooks Exhibitions.

"Covering the entire sheet-metalworking technology chain, exhibitors will present everything they have on offer to innovate and digitalise the manufacturing process. Major exhibitor countries are Germany; Italy; Turkey; China; Switzerland; The Netherlands; Spain; Belgium; Poland; Austria; Portugal and the USA," she added.

In addition to the innovations and numerous live demonstrations at the exhibition stands, attendees can look forward to the launch of the EuroBLECH 2022 Presentation Area. Located in hall 26 (stand L60), the new show feature delivers around 20 sessions throughout the four exhibition days.

"It will offer exciting insight into innovative companies and projects, interesting discussions and valuable networking opportunities. Attendance is free of charge to all registered exhibitors and visitors and further information will be released closer to the show," said Mack-Brooks Exhibitions.

The new EuroBLECH App for Android and iPhone has been available to download via the App Store since mid-September. It includes an interactive area map and a function to search for exhibitors and plan your visit to the show, as well as further useful features for visitors to EuroBLECH.

EuroBLECH offers students the opportunity to visit EuroBLECH free of charge. On Friday 28 October 2022, students will be given free entry to the show when presenting a valid student ID at the entrance. During all other show days, students are invited to visit at a reduced student fare of €18 per day ticket.

EuroBLECH 2022 will be held in halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 26 and 27 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds in Germany. Opening times are from 09:00 to 18:00. Entrance tickets for EuroBLECH 2022 are available via the online ticket shop at a discounted price. Tickets can also be purchased on-site throughout the duration of the exhibition.

Both the EuroBLECH Exhibitor List tool and the Online Show Preview, with detailed information on the many exhibitors and products, are regularly updated and available on the EuroBLECH website.■
