# FASTENER FAIR STUTTGART

[TARİH: 01.02.2011 ISMR]

ISMR previews the 2011 Fastener Fair Stuttgart, the trade show for fastener and fixing technology.

## Fastener Fair Stuttgart 2011, the trade show for fastener and fixing technology, is now larger than ever.

“Since important industry sectors such as the machine tool, automotive and construction industries are picking up again, there is an increasing demand for fastener and fixing technology. As a major international industry event, Fastener Fair Stuttgart 2011 comes, therefore, exactly at the right time,” says Susanne Rauberger, Exhibition Director, Fastener Fair Stuttgart on behalf of the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

From 22–24 February 2011, the trade show will open its doors at the exhibition grounds of Messe Stuttgart, Germany. A total of 623 exhibitors from 38 countries will showcase a comprehensive range of products and services including industrial fasteners and fixings; construction fixings; assembly and installation systems; fastener manufacturing technology and storage/logistics services.

## EXHIBITION INFO VENUE AND ENTRY

The Messe Stuttgart show grounds, situated directly at Stuttgart’s international airport, are easily accessible. As an important industry hub in southern Germany, Stuttgart offers excellent infrastructure and hotel accommodation of all ratings. The entry ticket price on-site is 30 euros – visitors who pre-register online can save 10 euros, see www.fastenerfair.com/stuttgart for more information.

## EXHIBITOR FACTS

Fastener Fair Stuttgart 2009 was a record event, the biggest trade exhibition for the fastener and fixing industry held in Europe so far. 625 exhibitors from 33 countries attended the 2009 Fastener Fair – 56 per cent of exhibitors came from Europe. More than two thirds of the exhibitors rated the quality and number of visitors as very good or good, according to the organiser. Top exhibitor countries were China; Germany; India; Italy; Korea; The Netherlands; Spain; Taiwan; Turkey and the United Kingdom.

6,000 trade visitors from 75 countries visited the 2009 event. 62 per cent of the visitors came from outside Germany and more than two-thirds of the visitors had purchasing capacity, said Mack Brooks Exhibitions. Top visitor countries were Czech Republic; France; Germany; Italy; The Netherlands; Poland; Spain; Sweden; Switzerland and the United Kingdom.

## AN EYE ON INNOVATION

“Fastener Fair Stuttgart is Europe’s largest trade exhibition for the fastener and fixing industry. The 2011 event will cover 12,500m2 in net exhibition space, and the show now stretches over three halls (Halls 4, 6 and 8) at Messe Stuttgart,” Mack Brooks Exhibitions told ISMR.

Visitors at the Fastener Fair are distributors, suppliers, engineers, specifiers and other professionals in the fastener and fixing industry. Alongside traditional products, the Fastener Fair puts an emphasis on the latest innovations in fastening and fixing technology. The ‘Route to Fastener Innovation’, a competition to promote innovations, highlights all the stands where innovative solutions and technologies can be found.

The show’s profile ranges from materials and semi-finished products via production equipment, surface technology and quality assurance to finished products in diverse materials and modern bonding processes. Storage, distribution and information services are also included.

Stuttgart, the venue of Fastener Fair, is the capital of Baden-Württemberg, one of Germany’s most prosperous business regions. A large number of machine tool manufacturers, construction companies as well as leading car manufacturers are based in this region.

International show organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions Group, announced the acquisition of the entire shareholding of Fastener Fairs Limited for an undisclosed figure in February 2010. Fastener Fair India is scheduled to take place in Mumbai on 16 – 17 April 2011. With Fastener Fairs Limited, Mack Brooks also acquired the ‘Fastener+Fixing Magazine’, read by more than 30,000 fastener professionals worldwide.
