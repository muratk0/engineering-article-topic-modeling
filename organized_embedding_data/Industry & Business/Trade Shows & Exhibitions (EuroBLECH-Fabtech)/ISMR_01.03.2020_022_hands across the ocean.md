# HANDS ACROSS THE OCEAN

[TARİH: 01.03.2020 ISMR]

Global Industrie, in Paris from 31 March-3 April 2020, incorporates four leading manufacturing exhibitions under one banner.

## EDITOR'S NOTE:

Global Industrie has just advised that the show will be postponed to 23-26 June 2020, and be held at the Paris Nord Villepinte Exhibition Centre. See www.global-industrie.com for more details.

The show is taking place this year in Paris Nord-Villepinte (France) from 31 March-3 April 2020. The show's last edition in 2018, in Paris, attracted 40,800 visitors over four days.

The 110,000m² exhibition area is expected to host 2,500 international exhibitors, covering the entire industrial sphere. Worldwide in its scope, the show brings together the entire industrial ecosystem (product/solution providers, equipment suppliers, subcontractors, start-ups, primary contractors and major corporations); the whole value chain (Research & Innovation, Design, Production, Services, Training etc.) and all user sectors (transport & mobility, energy, food, infrastructures, consumer goods, chemicals, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, defence/military, metallurgy, iron and steel etc.).

## New features for 2020

"Among new features of the 2020 show are strengthened international leadership, a strong theme centred on ecological transition and sustainable industry and a new competition, the Golden Tech challenge, which will identify and reward the best professionals (of all ages) in all industrial jobs," said the show organiser, GL Events.

Golden Tech will see around ten teams of professionals with industrial expertise competing against each other. By refocusing industry on the men and women who embody it on a daily basis, this challenge also aims to make the general public aware of the wealth and diversity of professions in the industry.

Among the various thematic visitor trails proposed by Global Industrie, the "Sustainable industry" trail will identify the stands of exhibitors selected for their ecological and energy policies. The show cited the circular economy, energy efficiency, mobility and sustainable development as crucial challenges which are guiding companies' actions today.

"The 2020 edition of the show is also reorganising around 20 major industrial sectors to make the event more easily understandable. However, the four exhibitions which make up Global Industrie (MIDEST, INDUSTRY, TOLEXPO and SMART INDUSTRIES) will still retain their specific identities," said GL Events.

INDUSTRIE, the production technology and equipment show.

MIDEST, representing expertise in industrial subcontracting.

SMART INDUSTRIES, a show focused on Industry of the Future.

■TOLEXPO for metalworking sheet and rolls, tubes and profiles.

Visitors looking more specifically for partners in regions, or in specific countries, can visit various national Pavilions. Around ten of France's thirteen regions and around twenty countries will be represented at the show in 2020.

The show's medium-term objective is to double its number of foreign visitors and increase the number of countries represented. The organiser is therefore setting up numerous actions aimed at a number of countries in Europe (Italy; Spain; Portugal; Belgium; Switzerland; Germany; Hungary; Czech Republic and Poland); Africa (Algeria; Tunisia; Morocco; Cameroon; Senegal and Ivory Coast) and Asia (China and Turkey). It will host numerous international delegations of visitors and international speakers at its conferences and round-tables.

A new Artificial Intelligence Village will also be created at Global Industrie 2020. Covering a floor area of 1,000 – 2000m² in the SMART INDUSTRIES section, this village is expected to bring together 100 to 150 companies specialising in Artificial Intelligence (AI).

"In this area, pavilions will present national companies (large corporations, SMEs and start-ups) at the forefront of AI in Germany; the Netherlands; Italy; Sweden; Finland; Spain; Poland; France; the Czech Republic and other countries. At the heart of the Village, a pitch room will host talks by companies and various political personalities who will attend the show," added the show organiser.

## Global Industrie Campus

The Global Industrie Campus will highlight professional expertise, the wide range of sectors and careers in manufacturing, the jobs available and the professions of the future. An immersive educational pathway, it facilitates exchanges between professionals, university students, school students and job seekers.

"Contrary to preconceived ideas, French industry is a teeming breeding ground for jobs, many of which still too often remain unfilled," said the show organiser. "For the third consecutive year, the Global Industrie Campus is going to help to emphasise professional expertise, demonstrate the wealth of career possibilities; highlight the jobs available and promote the professions of the future."

At the Campus, visitors can find out about professions and careers in industry and talk about training with specialist organisations.

It also aims to help young people find their 'dream job'. For four days, Global Industrie will enable access to hundreds of jobs in the sector and facilitate meetings with companies who are recruiting. Services include:

■ The recruitment portal which lists several hundred job offers and allows you to submit your CV to make yourself visible to potential recruiters.

■ Job-dating – 30-minute interviews give you a chance to talk on the spot with companies which have real recruitment needs.

■ Express coaching sessions organised by the Arts & Métiers engineering school.

An association created in 2005, “Elles Bougent", which aims to attract girls in schools and universities to jobs in engineering (IT, aerospace, automotive and railways) will also join the Campus in 2020. Around fifty girls, accompanied by their sponsors, will attend Global Industrie on Tuesday 31 March 2020 and take part in a video competition linked to their visit to the show.

## Conferences and round-tables

Placed under the patronage of the President of France, Global Industrie has the active support of national and regional public authorities and socio-professional organisations. On Thursday 2 April 2020, it will once again host a meeting of the Conseil National de l'Industrie (CNI), organised by the French Ministry of Economy and Finance.

Major captains of industry, factory managers, technical managers, experts etc. will take part in the master classes, keynote addresses and technological/strategic roundtables which make up the Global Industrie conference programme.

On the afternoon of Thursday 2 April 2020, the Global Industrie 2020 TV studio will host a large round-table, in which several industrial heads (who have already committed their companies to green policies) will share their experiences. A session led by Cetim will analyse 'The challenge of the energy and environmental transition and opportunities for SMEs'.

## Global Industrie Awards

The awards programme this year includes six categories (Productive Performance; New Technology; Collaborative Success; Exemplary Production; Digital Transformation and Responsible Solution) as well as three special prizes (the Start-Up Prize and two new ones: the Women in Industry Award and the Ecological Transition Award) to reward noteworthy solutions from the show's exhibitors.

The awards will be held on 11 March 2020. A special prize will be awarded for a noteworthy action by a company in 2019 on ecological transition: electrification/hybridisation, circular economy, energy efficiency etc.

All exhibitors can enter free of charge by completing the entry form via their exhibitor portal. The winners of these awards will be unveiled during the grand evening reception, where 1,600 figures from the industrial world will gather. In 2018, 180 entries were submitted by exhibitors and the awards were presented before an audience of 1,500.

## Personalised portal

Designed for Global Industrie exhibitors and visitors, 'My GI' is an interface available on the Internet and on smartphone.

"Preparing for your visit before the show opens, organising your meetings, arranging for your visit during the show, assessing your business meetings, technological discoveries and information collected at the show... all of this and more is available everywhere and at any time with My GI," explained the show organiser.

French industry is a teeming breeding ground for jobs, many of which still too often remain unfilled.

As they would on social media, visitors and exhibitors will have access to a profile which can be personalised with their profile photo, social media, areas of interest and time slots when they will be available during the show.

Visitors will be able to search in the list of exhibitors for the companies in which they are interested, add them to their favourites and contact their sales reps directly to make appointments. The interface's artificial intelligence will automatically suggest companies linked to the areas of interest that they have indicated in their profile. The exhibitors' sales teams will be able to search in the list of users registered at the show for the companies and profiles that they have targeted as potential prospects. Contact details and messages will only be activated if both parties mutually accept their invitations.

All users will be able to scan their correspondent's badge or business card. This information will be added to their list of contacts. The contact forms of people met can also be personalised via hashtags or notes, and all information concerning appointments (dates, times, contact details, notes etc.) will be exportable after the show. All of this information will remain confidential for each user.

Visitors and exhibitors will be able to add the conferences in which they are interested to their favourites. These conferences will then be automatically saved in their general “My Visit" programme. For appointments, a notification will be sent to them ten minutes before the start of the appointment.
