Registration opens for Powder Coating Week 2024

PCI’s Powder Coating Week 2024 returns to the Renaissance Orlando at SeaWorld in Orlando, Fla., March 11-13. The program, comprising four separate events, combines keynote presentations, technical sessions, tabletop exhibits, roundtable discussions, expert panels, and social activities.

The Powder Coating 101 Workshop kicks off the event Monday and Tuesday, March 11-12. The Custom Coater Forum will open with a luncheon at noon on Monday, with the program to follow. The Tabletop Exhibition will be open Monday evening and during Tuesday’s lunch and evening reception.

The Powder Coating Technical Conference begins with breakfast and an emerging markets panel discussion on Tuesday. A general session keynote will be delivered by Chris Kuehl with Armada Corporate Intelligence, followed by a variety of topical breakout sessions that run through Wednesday, March 13. The conference will close with a lunch and roundtable discussions.

Alto-Shaam constructs Innovation Center at Wisconsin headquarters

Food service equipment manufacturer Alto-Shaam has started construction of an Innovation Center at its headquarters and manufacturing facility in Menomonee Falls, Wis. Completion is planned for 2025. The expansion, more than 100,000 sq. ft. connected to the existing building, includes an R&D lab as well as engineering, product management, shipping, and finished goods spaces.

Avid Boats expanding Mississippi facility

Aluminum fishing boat manufacturer Avid Boats is renovating and expanding its facility in Amory, Miss. The $7.96 million investment will create 50 jobs.

The company is rebuilding and expanding from its original 85,000-sq.-ft. facility, which suffered significant tornado damage in March, to 100,000 sq. ft. Construction is expected to be completed by December 2024, with new jobs filled over the next five years.

Anco Eng. to expand in Connecticut

Anco Eng. Inc. will expand its fabrication facility in Shelton, Conn., according to the Shelton Herald. The company plans to add 53,200 sq. ft. to its current 142,000-sq.-ft. plant.

"Blue Heron" by Angellos Glaros

Metal Supermarkets announces 2023 winners of Metal My Way Contest

Toronto-based Metal Supermarkets, a supplier of small-quantity metals, has announced the six winners of its annual Metal My Way contest. Showcasing the versatility and craftsmanship of metalworking, fabricating, and design, the international photo and video submission contest invites entrants to submit projects through which they have transformed ordinary metal into inventive creations, innovative tools, or works of art.

This year’s competition received more than 870 international submissions. Both professional and amateur metalworkers were invited to submit a photo or video of their innovative metal-made projects. Winning submissions were chosen based on complexity, detail, intricacy, uniqueness, creativity, craftsmanship, quantity of metal used, and ingenuity.

The 2023 winners are:

Grand Prize: Angellos Glaros, Nanaimo, B.C.— "Blue Heron"

Best Furniture and Home Project: Noah Khoury, Altamont, N.Y.—"Forged and Fabricated Steel Doors"

Best Knives Project: Justin Lamoureux, Winnipeg, Man.—"Wheel of Time Dagger"

Best Industrial or Shop Project: Matthew Mitman, Bowman, S.C.—"Pneumatic Power Hammer"

Best Vehicle Project: Tim Strochine, Cincinnati— "Airsoft Combat Vehicle"

Best Artistic and Design Project: Steve Nielsen, Oroville, Calif. – "Polished Stainless Steel Eagle"

Alongside title sponsor Metal Supermarkets, the contest was sponsored by Airgas, Inc., Cosen Saws, Ferric Machinery, Haco-Atlantic Inc., and Space Aid Mfg.

Herd adds laser welding to Winnipeg facility

Herd, a producer of trucking accessories including truck guards and grille guards, has integrated handheld laser welding capabilities at its Winnipeg, Man., production facility. The IPG LightWeld XR uses a laser to fuse a variety of metals up to four times faster than TIG welding and with deeper penetration than MIG welding, the company reports. Optimized presets make it easy to use for staff without previous welding experience or training.

Bad Boy Mowers to create 300 jobs in Arkansas

Bad Boy Mowers has announced it will expand its campus in Batesville, Ark., creating 300 jobs over the next five years. As part of the expansion, the company will build a new paint facility and shipping center while continuing to add manufacturing equipment. Vander-Bend Mfg. and subsidiaries become Vantedge Medical

Vander-Bend Mfg. Inc., San Jose, Calif., and its subsidiaries TMK Mfg., JL Haley, Swiss Precision Machining, and Omni Components, all portfolio companies of Aterian Investment Partners, have announced a strategic transformation to become one company, Vantedge Medical.

For more than 40 years, Vander-Bend has offered complex precision manufacturing and assembly services to medical technology markets including robotic-assisted surgery and radiation oncology.

Over the last four years, the organization has acquired the four subsidiaries because of their proximity to medical technology innovation hubs, breadth of manufacturing expertise, and experience supporting medical device customers. These acquisitions were strategic to make this evolution to one company possible.

The new company has a combined 550,000 sq. ft. of manufacturing space, offering development and production of metals-based medical components and assemblies, from rapid prototyping to full-scale manufacturing. Capabilities include complex precision micro to large-format machining, sheet metal fabrication, assembly, and engineering.

SAF to relocate headquarters in Georgia

SAF has announced plans to relocate its corporate headquarters into Carroll County, Ga. The company will invest $17 million to establish an eco-friendly facility.

SAF fabricates, finishes, and distributes metal products for the commercial building industry.

Prostar Manufacturing launches three new divisions

Prostar Manufacturing Inc. has introduced three new manufacturing divisions. The creation of these divisions—Reels, Racks, and Platforms—is a strategic move to diversify the company’s offerings and cater to a wider range of market needs.

The Reels division will specialize in precision-engineered reels for various industrial applications. The Racks division will deliver storage systems, while the Platforms division will focus on the production of elevated work platforms.

Located in Pasadena, Texas, the company is a producer of ASME vessels, skids, vapor recovery units, and customized products.
