# Fastener Fair Hannover 2012

[TARİH: 01.03.2012 ISMR]

The forthcoming Fastener Fair Hannover, from 24 - 26 April 2012, has been well received by companies in the fastening and fixings sector. A total of 304 exhibitors from 28 countries have already booked their stands at the first edition of Fastener Fair Hannover. The exhibition will be held in Hall 21 at the Hannover Exhibition Grounds in Germany.

Fastener Fair Hannover 2012 covers a net exhibition space of some 5,000 square metres. The majority of exhibiting companies are European with Germany and Italy being the largest European exhibitor countries, followed by Turkey, UK, the Netherlands and France. Companies from Asia and North America are also represented at the show.

A total of 55 companies have never exhibited at any Fastener Fair before and will therefore add value to the fastener and fixings show with their new range of products and services.

## Adding value

Fastener Fair Hannover 2012 will take place alongside Hannover Messe, internationally renowned as a trendsetting technology fair covering all areas of the industrial value chain.

“In addition to the traditional manufacturing and trade audience at Fastener Fair, we expect a greater end user audience from all over the world in Hannover,” said exhibition director, Susanne Rauberger, on behalf of Mack Brooks Exhibitions. “Co-located to the world’s key event for industrial technology, Fastener Fair Hannover will be able to offer its participants a wide range of new business contacts in additional industry areas, such as the automation, energy and mobility sectors.”

Hannover offers excellent national and international travel connections by air, rail and road as well as a wide range of accommodation.

## EVENT PREVIEW

The exhibition profile of Fastener Fair Hannover covers industrial fasteners and fixings, construction fixings, assembly and installation systems, fastener manufacturing technology as well as storage and logistics services. The exhibition provides buying and networking opportunities for distributors, suppliers, engineers and industry professionals.

Information on travel and accommodation can be found in the travel section of the exhibition website www.fastenerfair.com

## KEY FACTS

Visitors to Fastener Fair Hannover 2012 who register in advance at the show can enjoy a reduced price of 20 Euros instead of 30 Euros, the ticket price on-site. Registration is now open. Visitors holding an entry ticket to Hannover Messe get free entry to Fastener Fair Hannover 2012. The exhibition catalogue is available on-site and included in the entry price.

Fastener Fair Hannover 2012 is open from 24 April 2012 to 26 April 2012, from 9.00 – 17.00 hrs. The nearest entrance is Entrance West 3 of the Hannover Exhibition grounds.

Detailed information about the exhibition including an up-to-date exhibitor list is available on the show website at www.fastenerfair.com
