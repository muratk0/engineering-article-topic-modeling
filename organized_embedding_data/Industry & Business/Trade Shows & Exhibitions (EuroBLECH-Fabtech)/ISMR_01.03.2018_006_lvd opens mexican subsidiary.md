# LVD opens Mexican subsidiary

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

THE LVD STRIPPIT Mexico S. de R.L. de C.V. sales and service subsidiary in Querétaro, Mexico, was formally inaugurated on 1 December 2017 at a grand opening and open house ceremony attended by almost 100 guests. His Excellency Antoine Evrard, Ambassador of Belgium to Mexico, and Wim Van Cauteren, Commercial Attaché of Flanders Investment and Trade, helped to cut the ribbon and officially launch the new office, LVD's 18th dedicated subsidiary. The opening ceremony was followed by a tour of the facility, demonstrations of a range of sheet metal fabrication equipment that is permanently housed there, and a seminar focused on Industry 4.0.

"LVD Strippit Mexico offers readily-accessible, locally-based sales and technical support for the complete range of LVD Strippit laser cutting, punching, bending and software products. The new subsidiary will strengthen our already established operations in Mexico and provide the company with a more active presence in this growing market," LVD told SMR.
