# BLECH China 2014 will take place at Suzhou International Expo Centre from 7-9 May 2014

[TARİH: 01.04.2014 ISMR]

BLECH China 2014 will take place in Hall 4 at Suzhou International Expo Centre from 7-9 May 2014. It is a dedicated sheet metal event in the heart of the Yangtze River Delta hub. Visitors will be drawn from the booming Yangtze River Delta and right across China to see and experience sheet metal equipment, services, technologies and solutions for their business.

“China’s manufacturing base is facing huge growth potential and more competition than ever. Keeping at the forefront of productivity and quality is key to sustained growth. BLECH China 2014 provides the perfect showcase of the leading metal forming and fabrication technologies, with visitors drawn from all the key end-user markets – from the automotive sector to micro-electronics,” explained the organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd.

Powered by the international BLECH brand, BLECH China 2014 focuses on the entire sheet metal buying chain in the Shanghai/Suzhou regions and across China. In a booming industrial region and with excellent links to all parts of China, BLECH China 2014 will attract key buyers.

## Sheet metal in focus

The ‘Meet the Buyer’ programme and technology seminars at BLECH China 2014 will invite national and international key buyers and key players to discuss hot topics in the industry. BLECH China 2014 will showcase the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal, semi-finished and finished products, handling, separation, forming, flexible sheet metal working, joining, welding and surface treatment, processing of hybrid structures, tools, quality control, CAD/CAM systems and R&D.

BLECH China 2013, held at Suzhou Exhibition Centre from 14-16 May 2013, attracted 5,000 sheet metal professionals, buyers and decision-makers from Jiangsu province, along with delegates from more than 30 countries and most regions across China.

In recent years, China’s economy around the Yangtze River Delta has become the engine of China’s flourishing economy. The region generates 26 per cent of the country’s GDP, fulfils 37 per cent export volume, attracts 52 per cent foreign investment and has consistently shown double-digit growth per year. It is the economic centre in China’s National Development Programme and an important international gateway to the Asia-Pacific region and beyond.

## Press technology innovations

There will be a strong focus on press hardening when AP&T exhibits at BLECH China 2014 in Suzhou. The rapidly growing Chinese market is showing a great deal of interest in the opportunities afforded by press hardening technology; this applies both to car manufacturers and their subcontractors.

During the event, AP&T (booth B15) will demonstrate simulations of various press hardening processes using advanced 3D technology. Amongst other things, visitors will be able, by means of virtualization, to study in detail an entirely new press hardening line with very high capacity.

AP&T is also introducing new energy-saving technology in its hydraulic presses. The system, dubbed the “Frequency Drive System”, is similar to the start/stop function found in modern vehicles. The system controls the speed of the electrical engines, which then control the load-sensing hydraulic system found in AP&T’s presses. This means that the system can quickly reduce the speed of the electrical engines as well as quickly start them. The principle is similar to the start/stop system found in some modern passenger cars.

“The test results are positive. Since utilisation of the electrical engines is optimised to when they are really needed, it is possible to save up to 40 per cent of the energy costs for the press. The amount that can be saved primarily depends on the customer’s electricity contract,” the company explained. –

## New COMBO storage features

New capabilities have been developed for the Prima Power COMBO automatic sheet storage systems. A special gripper is now available which makes it possible to load single sheets at a time from the sheet storage system to the machine. The sheet storage crane therefore has a dual function: handling sheet stacks on cassettes and loading individual sheets with the gripper.

The new capability adds flexibility and production speed, as material flows are fast and material changes can be made at short intervals. Loading material into the storage system is rapid as sheet stacks are loaded. COMBO can be integrated with one or more machines and cells, either using the standard connection (in which material stacks are loaded to the machine) or the rapid sheet-at-a-time connection when special response times are required to meet production targets.

## Loading, stacking and flexible manufacturing

The LST loading and stacking robot, previously available only for the PLATINO® laser cutting machine, can now be connected to the high-end ZAPHIRO® laser cutting system. ZAPHIRO® features a 4- or 5- kW CO2 laser source. Linear motors bring positioning speed up to 240m/min. (X/Y). The work area is designed for sheet sizes of 3000mm x 1500mm. Positioning accuracy and repeatability of 0.03mm is possible, Prima Power told ISMR.

The LST eliminates manual separation of cut parts from the skeleton, reducing manual operations, increasing part quality and enabling a higher level of productivity to be reached using unmanned operations. The robot takes the parts directly from the cutting head one-by-one and they are accurately stacked on the table or wagons, or sorted into boxes. Parts weighing up to 200kg can be picked up as well as very small parts (when their geometry is suitable). The RALC (robot-assisted last cut) option prevents the sticking of even intricate components to the sheet as the part is held by the robot when it is separated from the sheet.
