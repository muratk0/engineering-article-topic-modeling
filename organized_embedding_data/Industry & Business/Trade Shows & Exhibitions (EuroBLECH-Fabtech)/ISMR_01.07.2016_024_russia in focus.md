# RUSSIA IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.08.2016 ISMR]

We review this year's Metalloobrabotka exhibition in Moscow

Held for the 17th time at the Moscow Expocentre, Russia (23–27 May 2017), Metalloobrabotka 2016 has not only remained in its position amongst the top five European international machine tool exhibitions, but also showed a slight increase in the floor plan and number of exhibitors compared to the previous show," the event organiser, Expocentre AO, told ISMR.

According to the general manager of Expocentre AO, Sergey Bednov, "All this could be due to the unique infrastructure of the Expocentre exhibition grounds. All kinds of energy-intensive and large equipment can be demonstrated and operated, which was a great benefit of Metallobrabotka 2016. Our show is often compared to a machine-building plant where hundreds of machines operate simultaneously."

Nearly 1,000 companies and organisations from 32 countries showcased their latest developments, technologies and services in machine tool and machinery manufacturing on a total exhibition area of more than 83,000 square metres. Over five days, more than 28,000 people attended the show, representing a 10% growth level. According to the organiser, show surveys confirmed that 93% of visitors achieved their goals at the show and 89% intend to visit the next exhibition. Statistical data also indicated that 87% of regular exhibitors participate in the show every year, including companies from all over the world.

## An eye on innovation

Metalloobrabotka was launched in 1984 to become the largest metalworking and machine-building show in Russia and the Commonwealth of Independent States. It showcases the latest solutions and machinery to upgrade the Russian machine-building industry. At the 2016 event, Minister of Industry and Trade of the Russian Federation, Denis Manturov, sent a welcome address to the show's participants, organisers and visitors. He also toured the Metalloobrabotka 2016 exhibition and met journalists.

"Such a keen interest in the trade show testifies not only to the increasing demand for machine tool products in the Russian market but also to the development of the industry's investment potential in Russia. Despite the economic lag, our machine tool industry enjoys qualitative and quantitative growth. Last year, the production of cutting machines increased by almost 20%. Moreover, the production structure is changing and the output of high tech equipment is growing. Last year, the CNC machine sector increased by 60%," said the Minister.

"The trade show is the largest innovation project in Russia and the CIS," noted President of the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Sergey Katyrin. He stated that 500 out of 950 Metalloobrabotka 2016 exhibitors were Russian companies and that their number had increased by 100 compared to the previous year.

Despite the economic lag, our machine tool industry enjoys qualitative and quantitative growth

General director of Expocentre AO, Sergey Bednov, commented that Metalloobrabotka was not only a show but a place where people made contacts. To make this process easier, the organiser arranged the Inter-Regional Subcontracting Exchange. The Expocentre for Counterfeit-Free Exhibitions project also took place again.

Participants in the opening ceremony were Chairman of the State Duma Committee on Industry, Sergey Sobko; temporary acting Governor of the Ulyanovsk Region, Sergey Morozov; State Secretary, Deputy Chairman of the Bank of Russia, Alexander Torshin and Rector of Moscow State University of Technology ("STANKIN"), Sergey Grigoriev

"The show's success can also be explained by an extensive event programme devoted to key issues around the machine tool industry's development. The programme featured fora, seminars and presentations from participating companies where industry professionals networked and exchanged best practice tips. Organised by the Stankoinstrument Association and Expocentre AO, the 6th International Scientific and Technical Forum took place as part of the event programme. It was geared towards modern trends in technologies and the design of metalworking equipment," explained the organiser.

Metalloobrabotka 2017 will be held at Moscow Expocentre from 15-19 May 2017.
