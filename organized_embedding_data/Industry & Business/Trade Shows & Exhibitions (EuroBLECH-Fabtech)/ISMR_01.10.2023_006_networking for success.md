# Networking for success

[TARİH: 01.10.2023 ISMR]

With 826 exhibitors from 40 nations and 40,000 trade visitors from 124 countries, the SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN trade show for joining, cutting and surfacing technology took place over five intensive days from 11-15 September 2023 at Messe Essen, Germany. The event revolved around innovations, investments, networking and knowledge transfer.

"The atmosphere in our exhibition halls was excellent. After the pandemic-related break, it was noticeable how much the industry appreciated this platform. Many companies reported concentrated and successful sales talks to us; they are also expecting good post-exhibition business," said Oliver P Kuhrt, CEO of Messe Essen. "In addition to the high international quotient, the procurement competence of trade visitors was particularly impressive," he added.

Overall, 66 per cent of exhibiting companies came from outside Germany. Countries included China; France; Japan; South Korea; Taiwan and the USA. Almost 150 Chinese exhibitors, occupying an area of 3,500 square metres, were at the trade show. A time-lapse video of the trade fair set-up and a compilation of impressions garnered over 7.8 million views and more than 206,000 likes on Messe Essen's Instagram feed.

## Visitors in focus

Around 83 per cent of all trade visitors were involved in purchasing decisions in their companies, said the trade show organiser.

"Various specialists from the steel, mechanical engineering and vehicle construction sectors (as well as from the wholesale, service and energy sectors) visited the show to discover new solutions or to place orders. A third of all visitors used the trade fair to make or prepare investments, and the average order volume increased significantly compared to the previous event. Innovations from numerous areas were in demand. After all, the industry is the driving force for many other sectors of the economy, such as automotive or architecture," it added.

"In 2022, the production value of German welding technology alone amounted to a total of 4.11 billion euros; a new record."

## Digitalisation and innovation

Digitalisation and networking play a decisive role in the industry's future orientation. Smart robot systems that work hand-in-hand and carry out diverse process steps efficiently were on display, as were systems that create digital twins and enable optimisation using real-time data.

Igor Mikulina, President of the Board of Trustees of the IndustryFusion Foundation, commented: "Our industry is highly innovative. The trade audience was able to experience numerous digitalisation solutions live at exhibitors' stands. The IndustryFusion Foundation also recorded a high number of visitors at its stand. Our open-source networking solution, IndustryFusion-X, interested end users as well as machine and plant manufacturers and software providers."

Traditionally, SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN is where companies present their innovations to the public.

"A world premiere was celebrated, for example, by oxyacetylene welding machines that are powered by hydrogen and make an important contribution to more climate protection, occupational safety and CO₂ reduction. International and German startups supported by the German government used SCHWEISSEN & SCHNEIDEN 2023 as a global platform to present their own ideas and solutions to the market. Among them was Steeldate, based in Bad Nauheim, Germany, which brings together order and material with its resource-saving matchmaking tool. MX3D, on the other hand, showed additive manufacturing from the 3D printer, a technology which the Dutch start-up has already used with a 3D-printed footbridge in Amsterdam," commented Messe Essen.

## Conferences and competitions

The supporting programme included the DVS Congress from Monday to Thursday, which attracted almost 450 participants. It combined the Large Welding Conference, which focused on current topics such as "Welding in Electromobility" or "Artificial Intelligence in Joining Technology", the DVS Campus (where students presented their final theses) and, for the first time, the Underwater Technology Conference, which highlighted challenging work in wet depths. Trade visitors also experienced practical application examples in a diving container and were able to observe an expert at work.

The industry's young talents also demonstrated their skills live on-site. The best German welders were selected in the DVS national competition "Jugend schweißt" ("Welding Youth") and teams with young talent from eleven countries competed against each other in the Young Welders' Competitions.
