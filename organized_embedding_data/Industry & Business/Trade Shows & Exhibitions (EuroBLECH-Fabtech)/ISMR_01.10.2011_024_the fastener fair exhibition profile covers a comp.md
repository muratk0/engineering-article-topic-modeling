## VENUE AND EXHIBITION

Fastener Fair Brazil will take place in São Paulo, the largest city in Brazil and the seventh largest metropolitan area in the world. São Paulo itself accounts for 41 per cent of Brazil’s domestic industrial production as well as 40 per cent of automotive plants and 59 per cent of the car parts manufacturers in the country.

The venue is conveniently situated close to Tietê Bus terminal and Subway Station and with direct access to the Marginal Tietê Expressway. It has hotels and facilities nearby. Center Norte Shopping Mall with cinemas, restaurants, foreign exchange centre and shops is 600 metres from the venue.

The Fastener Fair exhibition profile covers a comprehensive range of products and services for the fastener and fixing industry such as Industrial Fasteners and Fixings; Construction Fixings; Assembly and Installation Systems; Fastener Manufacturing Technology; Storage, Distribution and Factory Equipment; and Information, Communication and Services.

Information on Fastener Fair Brazil can be found at www.fastenerfair.br.com ■
