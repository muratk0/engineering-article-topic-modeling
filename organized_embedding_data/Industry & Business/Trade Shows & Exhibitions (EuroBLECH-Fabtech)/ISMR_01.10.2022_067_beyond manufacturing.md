# 'Beyond manufacturing'

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

EuroBLECH (25-28 October 2022) is the world's largest sheet metalworking technology exhibition, featuring a clear focus on technological excellence. Salvagnini will present its latest innovations and a new concept, oriented towards the future, at the event in Hanover, Germany.

"Digitalisation, technological evolution and the fourth industrial revolution have led to cultural and industrial changes. The parameters have changed: we are more interested in ideas than things, the mind more than material, bytes more than atoms. We have embraced this change: the concept of 'Beyond manufacturing' represents exactly this synthesis between production and the future. 'Beyond manufacturing' is a vision that goes beyond: beyond the image of the industry and mechanical processes, open to ideas, thoughts, changes, challenges, mankind. It is a vision of the future consistent with the past that merges with design, dialogue and active consulting in the sheet metalworking industry. It is a vision that is not just directed towards sheet metal, but goes beyond it," it explained.

Salvagnini has gradually eliminated routine and repetitive tasks/activities with low added value and increased the importance of operators to roles where they manage and control the machines and systems. The market, which is increasingly oriented towards a make-to-order and just-in-time strategy against the backdrop of the recent pandemic, is continuing to push the Italian group in this direction.

“The technologies that we offer allow operators to produce only what is needed, when it is needed, making the most of the functionality of the machines which react to everything happening around them, both upstream and downstream, within the production cycle. As sustainability is one of the main Salvagnini keystones, all the systems have been further evolved to run with the minimum consumption possible, reducing any kind of waste – whether it is of raw materials, supplies, quality, time or efficiency – and allowing operators to work with total safety and ergonomics," said Salvagnini.

The company also pays close attention to consulting, sharing ideas and experience, and offering tailored solutions and services to meet all customers' needs. Its 'Beyond manufacturing' concept includes not only products but also pre- and post-sales services, designed to support customers in their path towards innovation, sustainability and continuous growth.

Salvagnini's complete range of products will be exhibited at its booth in Hall 11 at EuroBLECH. Two panel benders, the P2 and P-Robot (a new application that combines a P1 panel bender with a robot to autonomously produce both batches and single parts) will be on display. The L5 laser system; the B3 pressbrake and a flexible manufacturing system (featuring an S1 punch-laser combi machine and a P4 panel bender) will also be showcased. A wide range of handling devices and storage towers, as well as software (modular and automated) will be on the booth. Salvagnini will also present solutions beyond the 4.0 paradigm, with a variety of services exploiting advanced technologies for manufacturing optimisation and maintenance.■
