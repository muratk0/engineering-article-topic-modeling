# Over 900 exhibitors for 11th Blechexpo and 4th Schweisstec

[TARİH: 01.08.2013 ISMR]

AS A EUROPEAN industrial centre, Stuttgart will once again be dominated by the Blechexpo-Schweisstec trade fair duo for industrial sheet metal processing and complementary joining and cutting technologies from 5-8 November 2013. Currently, over 900 exhibitors have firm bookings – 844 for the Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal processing and 65 for the Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology. ISMR is the official international media partner for the event.

“Just under 150 new exhibitors will participate at Blechexpo or Schweisstec for the first time. Exhibitors from around 30 countries will journey to Stuttgart. Although, purportedly, there is mounting evidence that various markets are stagnating, there are no current signs of this trend in sheet metal processing and joining technology. On the contrary, markets in the US, South and Central America, Russia and other parts of Asia are still prospering and, in some cases, experiencing a significant upsurge, for example in the USA and Russia,” the show organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, told ISMR.

With over 1000 exhibitors from more than 30 countries and a gross exhibition space of 70,000 m² in 2011, the trade fair duo attracted around 26,000 specialist visitors from nearly 90 states to the Stuttgart trade fair centre.

## Seminars and fora

In addition to strong participation from Italian manufacturers and distributors, a special Italian forum will also be held, in cooperation with a trade journal publisher from Milan, at which companies can present their technologies, products and services to international expert visitors.

“A further highlight in the supplementary programme for Blechexpo and Schweisstec will be organised by the DVS (German Association for Welding Technology), a one-day event addressing all aspects of joining technology. In the age of lightweight construction and efficient use of resources, this is gaining in significance in many ways: on the one hand, for joining/fastening different materials (hybrid solutions) and, on the other hand, as regards energy efficiency and reduced energy consumption through the use of alternative joining/fastening solutions instead of conventional welding and soldering processes, as well as in connection with process reliability and quality assurance with complete production documentation,” added the organiser.

Further collaborations with associations and institutions, as well as a targeted marketing and advertising campaign in the German-speaking countries as well as Europe, Eastern Europe, Turkey and beyond, is expected to generate the large influx of visitors.
