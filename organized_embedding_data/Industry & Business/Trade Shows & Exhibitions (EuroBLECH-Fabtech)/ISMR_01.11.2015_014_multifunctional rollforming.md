We preview this year’s Blechexpo in Stuttgart (2-6 November) and highlight a selection of new and upgraded technology launches at the show

ISMR ENJOYED a strong presence as international media partner at this year’s Blechexpo (sheet metal processing exhibition) and Schweisstec (trade fair for joining technology) from 3-6 November at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre in Germany (see page 00). The organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH &. Co. KG., confirmed that the event exceeded all previous targets, attracting a total of 1,234 exhibitors from 36 countries.

Overall exhibition floor space amounted to 90,000 square metres and 38,136 expert visitors from 110 countries visited the colocated events. The number of exhibitors from inside and outside Germany increased by 7%, overall exhibition floor space rose by 6% and expert visitor numbers grew by 14%, said the organiser.
