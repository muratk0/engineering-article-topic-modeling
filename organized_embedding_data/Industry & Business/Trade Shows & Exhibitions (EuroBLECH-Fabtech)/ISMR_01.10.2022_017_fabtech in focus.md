# FABTECH in focus

[TARİH: 01.10.2022 ISMR]

North America's largest metalforming, fabricating, welding and finishing event heads to the Georgia World Congress Centre in Atlanta from 8-10 November 2022. Live equipment demonstrations, featured keynotes, expert panels and other special events will allow attendees the opportunity to interact with a large gathering of technical experts and visionaries to learn what's new and what's next in manufacturing.

The FABTECH Event Partners include five associations: the American Welding Society (AWS); the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA); SME, the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI).

"FABTECH provides a convenient 'one-stop shop' venue where you can meet with world-class suppliers, see the latest industry products and developments, and find the tools to improve productivity, increase profits and discover new solutions to all of your metalforming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs," the event organiser told SMR.

The event encompasses three halls at the Georgia World Congress Centre where attendees will find a variety of technology pavilions dedicated to Forming & Fabricating; Tube & Pipe; METALFORM; Welding; Finishing; 3D/Additive Manufacturing and Robotics & Industrial Automation. It also offers a comprehensive conference with targeted technical, operational, economic and managerial sessions. A variety of networking events and competitions also form part of FABTECH.
