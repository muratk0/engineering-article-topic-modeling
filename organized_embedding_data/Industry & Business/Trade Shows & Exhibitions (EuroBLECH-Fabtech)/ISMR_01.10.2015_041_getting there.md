# Getting there

[TARİH: 01.10.2015 ISMR]

## BY CAR

The exhibition centre is at the crossroads of the A1, A3, A86 and A104 motorways. From Paris, take the A1 or A3 motorway, then the A104 access road (towards Soissons). Follow signs for Parc des Expositions. In the event of congestion on the A1, take the Le Bourget exit, then the N2, N370 and CD40 roads to the exhibition centre. The address is: ZAC Paris Nord 2 - 93420 Villepinte.

From south eastern Paris, Orly, A6, take the A4, then the A86 (towards Bobigny) and A3 (towards Lille) motorways. In the event of congestion at the A86 exit, continue to the A104, towards Roissy-Charles de Gaulle, as far as the Villepinte - Parc des Expositions' exit.

From Paris-CDG and Lille, take the A1 towards Paris, then the A104 to Parc des Expositions.

From Nancy/Metz/Luxembourg/Germany, from the A4, take the A104 towards Roissy-Charles de Gaulle as far as Villepinte-Parc des Expositions’ exit.

## BY TRAIN

Fourteen TGVs serve Roissy station every day, which is 10 minutes by direct RER from Paris-Nord Villepinte. Trains link Roissy-Charles-de-Gaulle to numerous large towns in France and many other European cities.

Gare du Nord Parc des Expositions (about 30 minutes): Take RER B towards “Aéroport CDG 2 TGV” and get off at "Parc des Expositions". Gare de Lyon Parc des Expositions (about 50 minutes): Take RER D towards “Orry-le-Ville” and get off at “Châtelet-Les-Halles” station. Take RER B towards “Aéroport CDG 2 TGV” and get off at "Parc des Expositions". Gare de l'Est Parc des Expositions (about 40 minutes): Walk to the RER B station "Gare du Nord"; then take RER B towards “Aéroport CDG 2 TGV” and get off at "Parc des Expositions"

Gare Montparnasse Parc des Expositions (about 1 hour): Take the metro line 6 towards “Nation” and get off at "Denfert-Rochereau" station. Take RER B towards “Aéroport CDG 2 TGV” and get off at "Parc des Expositions".

## BY AIR

From Paris-CDG airport: The Exhibition Centre is 5 minutes from Paris-CDG airport. Take the RER line B, towards Paris, and get off at the first stop after the airport: “Parc des Expositions”.

From Orly airport: Take ORLYVAL to Antony, then change to RER line B, towards Roissy CDG. Get off at “Parc des Expositions”.
