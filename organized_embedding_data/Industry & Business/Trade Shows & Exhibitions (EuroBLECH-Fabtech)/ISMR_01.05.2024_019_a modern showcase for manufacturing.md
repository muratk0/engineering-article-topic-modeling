# A modern showcase for manufacturing

[TARİH: 01.05.2024 ISMR]

This year, ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE promises a wealth of new products and ten halls with working machines; robots and solutions for intralogistics plus a range of meetings. This year's trade show will be held from 4–7 June 2024 at MTP Poznań Expo in Poland. As well as stands showcasing the latest technologies, it will feature various demonstrations, conferences, roundtables, debates and workshops aimed at resolving industrial challenges.

Visitors will also be able to visit two other exhibitions alongside the show: the Modernlog Trade Fair of Logistics, Transport and Storage and the Subcontracting Industrial Cooperation Fair. The three trade fairs will highlight various industrial trends such as robotics; automation; Industry-4.0; industrial logistics etc. The available floor space at this year's ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE will also be larger.

"For years, the event has been a key venue for meetings and technology/innovation exchange among innovation sector leaders. People come to Poznań to see the latest live solutions for various industry sectors and take part in conferences, debates and meetings with experts," commented show organiser, the MPT Group.

A new highlight of the trade fair will be special test zones for specific Industry-4.0 automation solutions. This is where visitors can check live products and technologies supporting production processes in factories.

"The largest TECH STAGE in Poland will once again be created in Poznań, prepared by DBR77 and Grupa MTP, and will take up twice as much space this year," announced Anna Lemańska-Kramer, Director, ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE.

"This year's exhibition will certainly surprise with its scale and will be enriched with interactive show spaces. Listening to visitors, we are significantly expanding the Surface Treatment Showroom. We invited leading companies in this sector to participate," she added. Almost forty companies have already confirmed their participation in the zone.

In the Science and Startups Zone, participants will learn about inventions, scientists and new ventures. Innovations in industry and ways of obtaining subsidies for research and development will also be presented.

Last year, the trade show hosted 15,156 professionals (a 30% increase on 2022 figures). Modern industry solutions were presented by 690 companies (a 20% increase in the number of exhibitors compared to 2022).
