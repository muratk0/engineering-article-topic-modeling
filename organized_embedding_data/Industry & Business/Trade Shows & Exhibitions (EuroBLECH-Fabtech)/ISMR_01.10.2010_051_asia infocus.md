# ASIA INFOCUS

[TARİH: 01.10.2010 ISMR]

METALEX 2010 is the ASEAN’s (Association of Southeast Asian Nations) largest international machine tools and metalworking technology exhibition. The exhibition will be held from 24 to 27 November 2010 at the Bangkok International Trade and Exhibition Centre (BITEC) in Thailand.

METALEX 2010, housed in 45,000 square metres of exhibition space, is one of the main Asian events in the manufacturing calendar. At this year’s event, there will be 2,700 exhibitors and 11 international pavilions. No less than 50 countries will be represented amongst the exhibitors, and the exhibition organiser is expecting the show to be attended by around 55,000 ASEAN visitors. The exhibition, now in its 24th year, is organised by Reed Tradex, a leading exhibition and conference organiser in Thailand and Vietnam. Last year, METALEX 2009 welcomed 62,510 visitors and was recently certified by the UFI as an international standard trade show.

Although there will be 500 conference and seminar sessions at the ‘METALEX Forum,’ which constitutes a series of specialist lectures given by members of leading state and private companies from the metalwork and manufacturing industry, the official METALEX website indicates that the fora will be conducted in the Thai language.

INNOVATION ON DISPLAY METALEX 2010 will see the introduction of 4,000 new machinery and technology innovations, with metalworking technology on display in various zones within the exhibition centre.

The ‘Super Dome’ will house mould and die, WIRE tech, TUBE tech, machine tools, tools and tooling, while a series of six halls (101- 106) will include metrology, tools and tooling, machine tools, mould and die, foundry, factory automation, pump and valve, material handling, accessories, machine tools and metalworking, sheet metalworking and welding technology. METALEX is also running a welding competition during the exhibition.

## BY ROAD

The exhibition venue, BITEC, is located in the east of the capital and vehicles travelling from Bangkok can leave the motorway at the Samrong flyover which takes them directly to the newly constructed entrance 3 of the exhibition centre, avoiding traffic congestion on the Bang Na-Trad Highway.

BY RAIL/BUS The BTS skytrain operates in Bangkok and visitors to the exhibition should get off at On Nut station where a free shuttle bus has been organised to convey visitors to the exhibition centre. The shuttle bus will provide a link between the On Nut BTS station and the BITEC centre during the exhibition. There are also a number of buses conveying visitors to within 100 metres of the exhibition.

VIP RED CARPET SERVICE Anyone planning to fly to Bangkok to attend the exhibition will have the opportunity to opt for the METALEX ‘red carpet’ service for overseas visitors, which facilitates a Fast Track option at Suvarnabhumi Airport. The METALEX VIP option also includes, amongst other benefits, a guided tour of the exhibition and a briefing session upon arrival, unlimited access to a VIP lounge for the duration of the exhibition and special offers on recommended hotels. For further details, e-mail tradeadmissions@reedtradex.co.uk.

## ECONOMIC GROWTH

The METALEX Exhibition is being held at an important juncture for the construction industry as Thailand is due to receive a timely enhancement from a number of key sources. Figures supplied by Kasikorn Research Centre (KResearch) indicate that Thailand’s construction industry is expected to grow between 3 and 6.5% this year - an increase of between 10 - 14% on last year - due in part to government incentives and initiatives (source: The Nation).

Thailand’s motor industry is also enjoying a period of significant growth; the monthly sales figures for June 2010 showed an increase of 62.6% over the same period last year while, at the same time, figures were at a record ten-year high. In the first half of 2010, passenger vehicle sales were up by 59.6% and commercial vehicle sales showed an increase of 50.3% (source: The Nation).

At an international press conference in Tokyo on 1 September, held jointly by Thailand Convention & Exhibition Bureau (TCEB) and Reed Tradex Company to promote the METALEX Exhibition, the significance of the ASEAN manufacturing industry was underlined. Many leading car manufacturers and electronics firms have relocated their businesses to this region and it was revealed that, in the ASEAN region, vehicle parts and accessories generated figures of over US$ 40m, whilst electric machinery, equipment and parts reached figures of over US$ 300m.

Germany has been demonstrating its confidence in Thailand as an area of economic growth with significant investment potential. One of the leading German automotive parts suppliers, Continental, opened an engine plant in Thailand in 2009 and has plans to increase its revenue from 30m euros to 60m euros by 2015 (source: Bangkok Post).

Mr Krisada Wechwitayaklung, Deputy Director (Economic & Investment) Thailand Board of Investment Office revealed that, “Thailand is currently the largest producer of one-tonne pick up trucks and the seventh largest auto exporting country in the world.” He went on to state that the country was ‘the world’s largest HDD component manufacturing bases and one of the top ten air conditioning producers and exporters.’ He also announced, perhaps most relevant to the METALEX exhibition, that Thailand, bolstered by Japan as its foremost investor, is positioned to ‘offer a large and prolific market for Japan to expand machine tools and metalworking industries to Thailand and other countries in the ASEAN’.

Recent figures released by the Iron and Steel Institute of Thailand reveal that the demand for steel this year is predicted to increase from last year’s figure of 10 million tonnes, to between 11.5 – 12 million tonnes. A breakdown of this figure indicates that 60% will be directed towards construction of property, roads and rail links, while the remaining 40% is designated for automotive, electrical and other industries (source: The Nation).

Thailand is a major exporter of aluminium products, with Japan purchasing over US$ 400m, the US more than US$ 130m, and India over US$ 80m annually. The aluminium products most often exported were aluminium structures plates, rods, profile tubes and pipes. At the same time, Thailand is importing from Australia, Japan and China in excess of US$ 900m of mostly unwrought aluminium (source: The Nation).

Bangkok is a vibrant modern city. Commercial Siam Square has smart hotels and malls, while Sukhumvit Road is lined with quality hotels, restaurants and clubs. Thonburi, on the west bank of the Chao Phraya River, is navigable by canal – take in one of the floating markets!

On the Bangkok Skytrain, the light green line traverses the Sukhumvit road towards northern Bangkok, terminating near the Chatuchak Weekend Market (35-acre flea market). From Silom, the red line intersects the green at Siam Square terminating at the National Stadium by the MBK shopping mall. Numerous river ferries serve areas of Bangkok not connected by the Skytrain providing a joined-up transportation service.

Look for metered taxis and avoid taxi touts. Ensure the meter is running or agree a price before starting your journey and have your destination written in Thai as drivers often speak limited English. Bikes are permitted on pedestrian routes - a good way to visit temples, markets and parks.

Admire Bangkok’s skyline at sunset with stylish rooftop dining at State Tower’s Sirocco or Banyan Tree’s Vertigo Grill and Moon Bar. Visit the Island of Rattanakosin (Old City) for Bangkok’s best temples - Wat Pho, Wat Aran and The Grand Palace. Wat Phra Kaew (Temple of the Emerald Buddha) is the most sacred Buddhist temple in Thailand. River cruises are a scenic way to dine whilst enjoying the sights; glimpse floodlit temples cruising down the Chao Phraya river. Check vaccination requirements and avoid tap water.
