# Cleaning parts and surfaces

[TARİH: 01.11.2015 ISMR]

THE CLEANLINESS of surfaces and components and the removal of all contaminants is a critical factor for the quality of subsequent processing steps, and of the finished product. Consequently, the demand for cleaning solutions that are efficient, sustainable and produce consistently reliable results is on the rise around the globe. The parts2clean industrial parts and surface cleaning exhibition, which will take place in Stuttgart, Germany from 31 May to 2 June 2016, will cover this market.

“The quota of decision-makers attending parts2clean is higher than average. Around 80 per cent of the visitors come to the show with a view to investing in new technology,” explained Olaf Daebler, the director in charge of parts2clean at Deutsche Messe.

With an average of more than 25 presentations, which are simultaneously translated (German - English), the Forum offers valuable expertise on a variety of topics of importance to every stage of the process chain for cleaning industrial parts and surfaces. The 2016 event will offer separate sessions on cleaning approaches, upstream and downstream processes and analytics. Presentations may be submitted at www.parts2clean.de under “Program / Expert Forum”.

In parallel with parts2clean, several other trade fairs (in which part cleaning plays a key role) are also taking place at the exhibition centre in Stuttgart. The various coating processes featured at O&S, the International Trade Fair for Surface Treatments and Coatings, all require a scrupulously clean substrate. LASYS, the International Trade Fair for Laser Materials Processing, showcases laser applications across all sectors of industry and for all types of materials. Also, for many of those attending AUTOMOTIVE Expo (a group of five trade fairs covering different aspects of the automotive industry), the cleaning of parts and surfaces is of great importance for manufacturing operations. See www.parts2clean.de

## Ovako starts restructuring programme

STEEL PRODUCER, Ovako, plans to implement a restructuring programme to adapt to current and expected future market demand. The programme affects 300 employees. The annual cost saving for the entire programme is estimated to be 45 million euros, with full effect in 2018. Negotiations with local unions will start immediately.

"We are operating in a market with low expected growth over the next three years. The European production capacity in engineering steel was shaped for the market peak in 2007, and this is reflected at Ovako too in terms of over-capacity. This is not sustainable in the long term. Hence, we have decided to take action now in order to reduce costs and ensure the future strength of our business," said Tom Erixon, CEO at Ovako.

The restructuring programme will be implemented over the next two years and includes changes across the group. The main structural initiatives are:

■ Closure of the rolling mill in Hällefors, with production volumes moved to other Ovako units. The closure is planned to be finalised by summer 2017.

■ Closure of the Forsbacka production and distribution unit, with volumes moved to other Ovako units. The closure is planned to be finalised during 2016.

■ Closure of the distribution centre in Turenki and consolidation of Ovako’s distribution in Finland to newly acquired facilities in Tampere. The closure is planned to be finalised during 2016.

■ Reduction of shifts from four to three for the steel and rolling mill in Hofors, starting on 1 December 2015. The tube mill in Hofors is moving to three shifts during the fourth quarter of 2015.

The programme will also include a maintained focus on continuous improvements in production, administration and purchasing across the group. Detailed planning of the implementation and negotiations with local trade unions will commence immediately.

The total programme targets cost savings of 45 million euros annually, with 300 employees affected and a net reduction of 250 in the group. The total restructuring cost for the programme is estimated to be 10 million euros. In addition, capital expenditure of approximately 10 million euros will be required to adjust the production facilities, which will be handled within normal capex levels. During and after the transition, Ovako will continue to offer all of its current product lines to its customers. See www.ovako.com

## Asian appointments for AP&T

PRESS SPECIALIST AP&T’S continued investment in Asia involves renewing its leadership in China and Japan. Kari Rannisto became the new general manager of AP&T in Shanghai on 1 July 2015. He will continue developing AP&T's business in China and Korea with a strong focus on growth. Since its start in 2008/2009, AP&T's operations in China have grown substantially and the number of employees has grown tenfold from three to thirty people.

Kari Rannisto has worked a total of 18 years in Asia (15 of those in China) for industrial companies such as SKF, Atlas Copco and SSAB. He has also held several leading positions in both sales and manufacturing.

"Kari has in-depth understanding of the Chinese business climate as well as comprehensive experience in heading major projects and building strong sales organisations and distribution networks. He also speaks Chinese fluently. We are extremely pleased to welcome him," said Per Josefsson, Vice President, Sales and Marketing, AP&T.

Tatsuo Inoue has been AP&T’s new managing director in Japan since mid-August 2015. Under his leadership, AP&T will continue to develop its offer and reinforce its presence in the Japanese market.

Japan is a strategically important market for AP&T with its high level of technological expertise and stiff competition which drive development of new innovation. Several of AP&T's major customers are also based there. AP&T's ambition is to continue growing new sales of production solutions. AP&T has had sales and service operations in Japan since 2002. The company's office is in Yokohama. See www.aptgroup.com ■

## New moves at Porsche

THE SUPERVISORY BOARD of Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG appointed Dr. Oliver Blume (47) as the Chairman of the Executive Board of the sports car manufacturer from 1 October 2015. Since the start of 2013, Blume has been a member of the Porsche Executive Board responsible for production and logistics.

Oliver Blume will take the helm in Zuffenhausen at an exciting time. Porsche is investing over 1.1 billion euros in its current production sites over the next five years. For instance, a new engine plant and a new body manufacturing plant will be built at the company’s main business site. The assembly line in Zuffenhausen will also be extended. In the future, not only will all 911 and Boxster cars come off the assembly line here, but also Cayman models starting in summer 2016.

Another new appointee is Detlev von Platen (51) who will be a member of the Porsche Executive Board for Sales and Marketing. Von Platen comes to Zuffenhausen from the USA where he was Head of Porsche Cars North America for just over seven years. The new head of sales assumed his post on 1 November 2015. On the same date, his predecessor, Bernhard Maier (55), was reassigned from Zuffenhausen to Mladá Boleslav in the Czech Republic where he will assume the position of Chairman of the Board of Management of Škoda.

Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG also appointed Klaus Zellmer (48) to head its North American subsidiary from 1 November 2015. As the new President and Chief Executive Officer of Porsche Cars North America, Inc. (PCNA), Zellmer succeeds Detlev von Platen (51) who takes over responsibility for the sales and marketing division as member of the Executive Board of Porsche AG.

The Supervisory Board also appointed Executive Board Member for Finance, Lutz Meschke (49), as the Deputy Chairman of the Executive Board. ■

## Stephen Bruner joins OMAX

OMAX® CORPORATION, a global specialist in advanced abrasive waterjet systems, recently welcomed Stephen Bruner to its senior management team as vice president of marketing. Bruner joins the Washington-based company with nearly 20 years of B2B and vertical market experience executing successful integrated product marketing strategies that drive business growth.

He has oversight of the company’s marketing, technical training and inside sales teams and will work closely with staff on programme planning, brand development and lead generation and nurturing. He will also apply his analytical skills and process management expertise to implement new integrated product marketing techniques that further enhance the sales and support of the company’s abrasive waterjet solutions, which include OMAX and MAXIEM® JetMachining® Centers.

Seattle Business Magazine recently named OMAX Corporation “Manufacturer of the Year” as part of the 2015 Washington Manufacturing Awards. The company is increasing its equipment asset base, expanding its operations and developing new technologies.

Bruner has a Bachelor’s degree from the University of Colorado Boulder and Master’s degree in business administration from the Graziadio School of Business and Management at Pepperdine University. ■
