# Don't miss BLECH India 2017

[TARİH: 01.10.2016 ISMR]

BLECH INDIA 2017, India's exhibition exclusively dedicated to sheet metal working, returns to Mumbai for the fifth time in 2017. It will take place from 27-29 April 2017 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre.

Located in the state of Maharashtra, Mumbai, the 'Gateway To India' is the country's commercial capital. The state is both culturally and historically rich, whilst also being India's most industrialised state, and contributes 13% of the country's industrial output. In terms of development, Maharashtra is one of the fastest growing states in India.

Many Indian companies have established their head offices in Mumbai, recognising the city's status as India's commercial capital.

In addition to this, numerous international companies have also established themselves within the region. The Mumbai/Pune car manufacturing cluster contributes to 33% of the Indian market.
