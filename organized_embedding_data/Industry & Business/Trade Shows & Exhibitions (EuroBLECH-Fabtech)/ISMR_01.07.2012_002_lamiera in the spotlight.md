# LAMIERA in the spotlight

[TARİH: 01.08.2012 ISMR]

THE SIXTEENTH edition of the LAMIERA metalforming exhibition and conference, which took place in Bologna (Italy) on 9-12 May 2012 recorded a total of 18,192 visits, 787 of which were from outside Italy. 451 companies exhibited at the event (42% of whom were from outside Italy) occupying a total exhibition surface area of 40,000 square metres.

The international character of the event, promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots, automation and auxiliary technologies) was upheld by the presence of companies from countries such as Germany, Switzerland, Great Britain, USA, France, Holland, Turkey, China, Japan, Belgium, Spain, San Marino and the Principality of Monaco.

“These figures”, said Giancarlo Losma, President of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE “when compared with those of the previous show, confirm once again the central role of the exhibition for operators in the sector and highlight an increase in participation that shows the healthy condition of LAMIERA and the sector it represents.”

Lamiera’s RTDI-IN-FORMING section highlighted the latest international research initiatives and was a key meeting point for research centres and companies in the metalforming sector. Approximately 200 operators took part in the accompanying conference programme.

In view of the interesting shortmedium term opportunities, Turkey was chosen as the guest of honour country for the sixteenth edition of LAMIERA. The initiative, developed with the support of the Regione Emilia-Romagna and Confindustria Emilia-Romagna, enjoyed the presence of a delegation of Turkish operators. The next edition of LAMIERA will take place at BolognaFiere on 14-17 May 2014. See www.lamiera.net

## ON THE CASE

A REQUEST for super light cases with a very short lead time to carry valuable aircraft servicing-related parts, presented such a challenge that protective cases expert Zero Cases (UK) Limited used its 35 years of case-making expertise to provide a solution for a new customer.

“We were given the challenge of building a case which had to be very light, robust and able to meet Didsbury Engineering’s aerospace-related demands”, explained Mike Dimmack, UK plant manager.“The dimensions were 1.5m x 1.1m x 0.85m and the case,made of aluminium,needed to be strong,durable and the internal layout specifically designed to meet the industry sector leading product to be transported .”

Zero Cases (UK) innovative design created the case with minimal joints, parts and fittings to reduce the weight and meet the design scope.The company developed the case by enhancing an existing design, rolling the sheet aluminium and extending the design around the products the case would be protecting.

Zero Cases (UK) was given three weeks to design, manufacture and ship.Work on the first five cases started in the middle of April 2012 and by the middle of the third week in May 2012 it had been delivered to the customer.Another five cases have now been made and delivered. Demand for cases that are customised to meet specific needs has become so important to Zero Cases (UK) Limited that it has been able to develop new product lines based on such requirements. EUROBLECH is the world’s largest exhibition for the sheet metal working industry.With this year’s theme “For a Sustainable Future”, EuroBLECH puts a special focus on energy-efficient technology, environmentally-friendly manufacturing processes and intelligent use of material.

Currently, a total of 1,340 exhibitors from 37 countries have booked their stands at EuroBLECH 2012, from 23-27 October 2012 in Hanover, Germany, covering a net exhibition space of more than 84,000 square metres.This is an increase in floor space of more than 6% compared with the previous show.

“Visitors to the exhibition can look forward to a large number of machine demonstrations throughout the eight exhibition halls, as live presentations of innovative machinery and systems is traditionally a major focus for participating companies at EuroBLECH,” explained Nicola Hamann, Exhibition Director, EuroBLECH, on behalf of the organisers, Mack Brooks Exhibitions.

Almost 48% of the companies that have already booked their stands come from outside Germany, with Italy,Turkey, China, Switzerland, the Netherlands and Spain being the largest exhibitor countries after Germany. The exhibition profile represents the entire sheet metal working technology chain: sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; handling; separation; forming; flexible sheet metal working; joining; welding; surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; quality control; CAD/CAM systems and R&D. See www.euroblech.com
