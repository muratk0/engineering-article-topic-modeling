# FORM AND FUNCTION

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

ISMR previews IMTEX Forming 2012 to be held from 19-24 January 2012 in Bangalore, India.

IMTEX FORMING 2012 – the international forming technology exhibition will take place from 19–24 January 2012 at the Bangalore International Exhibition Centre (BIEC) in Bangalore, India. It is a specialist business fair featuring ‘live’ recent trends in all forming technologies – metals, plastics, ceramics, composites and advanced materials from across the globe. IMTEX FORMING will showcase the latest technological developments of these products from India and other developed and developing countries.

Tooltech 2012 is a concurrent exhibition which will showcase the latest range of dies and moulds, forming tools, tooling systems, machine tool accessories and metrology as well as computer aided design and manufacturing software.

## INTERNATIONAL PARTICIPATION

IMTEX FORMING 2012 and Tooltech 2012 will be one of the largest fairs run by IMTMA (Indian Machine Tool Manufacturers Association) and will be held over an exhibition space of around 32,500 sq. m., with around 500 exhibitors from various parts of India and leading brands in the global machine tool industry.

There will be representation from 23 overseas countries, including three country-group participations from the Republic of China, Germany and Taiwan. Other major overseas countries represented at the exhibition will include France, Italy, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Spain, Switzerland, Turkey, United Kingdom and the United States of America.

IMTEX FORMING 2012 and Tooltech 2012 will provide an opportunity to see a wide range of innovations, solutions and technologies showcased by Indian and overseas machine tool manufacturers.

## TOOLTECH IN FOCUS

Whether it is moulds, forming tools, machine accessories, metrology or CAD/CAM, both of these shows will present the latest machinery, equipment, processes, tools, accessories, software and raw materials and feed stocks required by manufacturers to produce formed parts. The previous IMTEX FORMING exhibition was held from 21 – 26 January 2010 and featured the participation of 427 companies. IMTEX FORMING 2010 along with Tooltech 2010 attracted participation from 26 overseas countries, including 4 country-groups from Belgium, China, Taiwan and Germany. Other major overseas countries represented at the exhibition included Australia, France, Italy, Japan, Korea, Singapore, Spain, Switzerland, Turkey, UK and the USA.

“The exhibition was host to around 44,000 business visitors from across the Indian manufacturing industry. Interaction with business visitors led to a virtual sell-out of all machines displayed at the exhibition and generated serious business enquiries to the tune of Rs.12 billion. Expectations are high about an upbeat investment environment in India around the time when IMTEX FORMING 2012 and Tooltech 2012 are scheduled.

“The Indian economy and its industry are poised to take India to a new league at the global level in 2012. This in turn spells very high opportunities for the machine tools and manufacturing solutions industry,” the event organiser told ISMR. ■
