# MADE IN ITALY

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

The LAMIERA sheet metal exhibition returns to Milan, Italy, from 18-21 May 2022.

LAMIERA 2022 will be able to offer an innovative visit experience, with an eye on the future of the sheet metalworking sector.

LAMIERA 2022, the international exhibition dedicated to the industry of sheet-metal forming machine tools and innovative technologies, will take place at Fieramilano Rho in Milan (Italy) from 18-21 May 2022. It will be followed by the Milan Furniture Fair (Salone del Mobile), which will be held in June.

Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (the Italian machine tools, robots and automation systems manufacturers' association) and organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU, LAMIERA has already received over 300 applications to exhibit. It will be the third edition of the show to be held in Milan.

The 21st edition of the trade fair aspires to repeat the success of the two previous editions held in the same venue, which saw the biennial event continuously grow (in terms of technologies on show, exhibition area, exhibiting companies and visitors).

## Innovation areas

The Italian government has set up special fiscal incentives, which consist of tax credits up to 50%, for Italian companies investing in new machine tools; robots; automation systems; Industry 4.0 and digital technologies. It has now extended the Industry 4.0 implementation incentives which will remain in force over 2022. This is, said the show organiser, "a great business opportunity for all exhibitors that will meet end-users at the next edition of LAMIERA."

General manager of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, "confirms the importance and the high value of trade shows. To see machinery first-hand and meet partners and customers in person are wishes shared by all operators in this sector. We are working intensively to offer a trade fair rich in novelties, technological innovations and side initiatives and we believe that it will be a success."

Several Innovation Areas will be open to visitors during the trade show, as listed below:

■ Fabbricafutura is dedicated to software and technologies for connectivity and data management; analysis and security; remote assistance and predictive maintenance; energy efficiency and production process streamlining.

■ Box Consulting is a space for consultants, integrators and trainers and is dedicated to expertise, certification and professional education/training.

■ Fastener Industry is centred on screws, bolts, fastening and clamping systems.

EcoCoatech is dedicated to the world of industrial painting and coating and their treatments. Created in cooperation with ANVER, the Italian Association of Industrial Coaters, it will show innovative inter-operational processes for sheet metal painting and coating.

Blech Italy Service is reserved for technical subcontracting and services in the parallel visit tour "Make" for manufacturers and “Buy" for suppliers of subcontracting and services and those who wish to purchase semi-finished products and outsourced work.

■ Saldatech is focused on the welding sector. Welding machines and equipment, robots and consumables will be exhibited.

"Machines, systems and equipment for the machining of sheet metal, tubes, sections, wire and metal structural work; presses; dies; welding; surface treatment and finishing; tooling; technical subcontracting and fasteners are just a few of the 'traditional' technologies on show. In addition, there will be a large and varied offering of robots; automation; control and quality systems; metrology; software; micromachining; additive technologies and solutions for the digital factory and energy/production efficiency," the show organiser told ISMR.

"Wide product ranges will be on show, including stand-alone machines and complex production cells. LAMIERA 2022 will also offer several in-depth-analysis and entertainment initiatives to make the most of the presence of operators taking part in the event. For example, the LAMIALAMIERA initiative will be a forum for conference sessions dedicated to specific issues and side events that will host discussions among opinion leaders and experts, as well as presentations of innovative technologies arranged by exhibitors," it added.

## Strong market indicators

“2021 was an extremely positive year for the Italian machine tools, robotics and automation industry, which reported double-digit increases for all key economic indicators. This trend should continue into 2022, the year when all the ground lost in 2020 should be recovered," UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE told ISMR.

Barbara Colombo, the association's president, outlined these strong economic indicators during its traditional end-of-year press conference.

"As highlighted by the preliminary year's data processed by the Studies Dept. & Business Culture of UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, in 2021 production marked a 22.1% upturn compared to the previous year. This outcome was due to the excellent trend in Italian manufacturer deliveries to the domestic market, which increased by 27.8% as well as to positive export performance (17.4% more than in the previous year)," she explained.

Based on ISTAT data processed by UCIMU, from January-September 2021 (latest available data), Italian exports of machine tools started to grow again in almost all destination countries. Sales to Germany, a primary market for "Made in Italy" exports, increased by 38.4%. Other major destination countries were the United States, (+9.7%); China (-5.3%); Poland, (+29%) and France (+1.2%).

"The domestic market was extremely lively, as shown by consumption data, and increased by 30.4% in 2021 compared to the previous year. The export/production ratio went down by two percentage points, standing at 53.1%. In line with the upswing reported by these indicators was also the growth of turnover, exceeding 9 billion euros in 2021 after collapsing to 7.5 billion euros in 2020," said the association.

Despite dynamic demand and this improvement, in 2021 the Italian machine tool, robotics and automation industry of the sector was not able to make a full recovery (after the downturn in 2020) because of difficulties in sourcing components and raw materials. This caused a delay between the collection of orders and the actual delivery of machinery.

"According to the survey conducted last October by the UCIMU Economic Studies Department & Business Culture on a representative sample of businesses in the sector, 95% of the companies confirmed that there were late supplies. The average delay is three months which, added to the usual one-and-a-half months of normal waiting time, increases the delivery time of supplied components and materials to four-and-a-half months. According to the businesses questioned, the impact of this delay on machinery delivery time is estimated to be four months. As a result, the average delivery time of machinery is currently nine months (as opposed to the five months which manufacturers habitually use as a guarantee to their customers)," said the association.

## Forecast for 2022

However, UCIMU believes that the positive trend in 2021 should continue into 2022, the year when it says there should be "a full recovery from the collapse due to the outbreak of the pandemic."

According to the forecasts processed by UCIMU, in 2022 production should grow by +10.9% versus 2021, driven by the upswing in exports which should increase by +7.7% and the upturn of manufacturers' deliveries in the domestic market, which should rise by 14.5%.

"Consumption should also keep growing to 12.1% more than in 2021. Imports should also benefit from the dynamism of domestic demand, marking a 7.7% rise (versus 2020). The export/production ratio should go down again, standing at 51.6%," confirmed the association.
