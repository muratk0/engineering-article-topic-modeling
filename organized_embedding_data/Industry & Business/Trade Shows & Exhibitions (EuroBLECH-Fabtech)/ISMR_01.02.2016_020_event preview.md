# EVENT PREVIEW

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

## Seminars and networking

There will be networking and educational areas at the event (with Chinese and international keynote speakers), as well as workshops for European exhibitors/visitors on how to connect and do business in China. A new technology-focused seminar, led by the associations, will also be held during the event with a presentation area made available for exhibitors.

“The Keynote speaker of our seminar will be Werner Stelzer, the former Chairman (and Co-Chairman) of the Rollers Council of the European Aluminium Association (EAA) and senior Alcan executive. He continues now as senior executive adviser to Alcan (now Constellium) and holds several board appointments in international associations,” Dan Wu, AsiaBLECH’s exhibition director, told ISMR.

“There will also be three days of workshops on topic sectors such as construction machinery; white goods; automotive; ship-building; elevators; agricultural machinery and hot topics in China. We are also organising a Meet the Buyer programme at AsiaBLECH.”

AsiaBLECH has also confirmed partnerships with key trade associations across the Asian region.

“We have developed relationships with sheet metal associations and trade bodies across all provinces of China, as well as Korea, Taiwan, Japan and the whole of South East Asia. AsiaBLECH aims to build the platform for international sheet metal working manufacturers in China and across Asia. We have also made significant connections in Asian vertical sectors such as automotive supply, electronics, shipbuilding etc. This has given us a depth of reach for AsiaBLECH, domestically and internationally, that has not been attempted before,” explained David Tellett, managing director of organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd.

## Sheet metal in focus

The show’s previous incarnation (BLECH China) attracted over 4000 sheet metal professionals and buyers from 30 countries. The rebranded AsiaBLECH aims to substantially increase visitor numbers and offer a variety of new visitor and exhibitor experiences.

“Many companies have already booked their stand spaces at AsiaBLECH 2016, representing a top-class range of innovative machinery and systems for sheet metal working. AsiaBLECH is attracting a widespread mix of domestic and international companies including suppliers from Germany, Italy, Switzerland, Sweden and Belgium. Many exhibitors have also returned to the event (such as Gasparini Spa, Kohler Maschinenbau GmbH, Messer Cutting Systems and Schuler Pressen). Other well-known companies have also decided to join AsiaBLECH 2016 for the first time this year, such as Panasonic Welding Systems and Prima Power,” explained David Tellett.

Suzhou International Expo Centre is located just to the west of Shanghai and easily reached from both Shanghai Pudong and Hongqiao international airports, both of which support access for international and domestic attendees. It also offers a balance between easy access for Chinese domestic visitors and international visitors, as well as first class facilities and lower accommodation costs than central Shanghai. Suzhou is known for its international business park – a number of large sheet metal manufacturers are located nearby, such as TRUMPF and Bruderer.

“China has become a highly sophisticated engineering market and has gained a regional and global reach beyond its borders. This is a trend that is sure to continue. More than ever, to stay at the forefront of productivity and quality, local and domestic manufacturers are looking to invest in the world’s best sheet metal technologies, equipment and expertise. AsiaBLECH 2016 gives providers of machinery, tools and services the opportunity to meet with decision-makers and to find business partners within the highly developed sheet metal working industry in China and other Asian countries,” concluded David Tellett. ■
