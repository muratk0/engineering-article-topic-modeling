# ROBOT HEART THE ART OF SMART ROBOTICS

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

Sponsored by SIRI, the Italian Robotics and Automation Association, and organised by EFIM-ENTE FIERE ITALIANE MACCHINE, the second edition of RobotHeart will also attract, along with manufacturers and integrators, universities and representatives from the academic and research world, spinoffs and start-ups. A winning synergy, said UCIMU, between robotic technology, artificial intelligence and creativity.

Finally, to complement the technological offerings at the exhibition, there will be several thematic in-depth events organised mostly on Robotics Day (Friday 11 October 2024) in the BI-MUpiù arena. In addition to a battery of speeches by exhibiting companies, a full programme of meetings will be offered by the main organisations representing the sector such as IFR (International Federation of Robotics); SIRI; I-RIM (Italian Institute of Robotics and Intelligent Machines); AIIA (Italian Association for Artificial Intelligence) and UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE.

"RobotHeart is not 'just' an exhibition area for matching supply and demand for automation technologies, but is also a laboratory of ideas, expertise and culture production, a point of reference and latest updates for those working in this sector," outlined UCIMU.

## IFR chooses RobotHeart

Marina Bill, President of IFR (the International Federation of Robotics), after her visit to BI-MU in 2022, chose RobotHeart as the venue for the IFR's Annual Assembly (2024). RobotHeart targets all manufacturing industries. In an interview last year, after a favourable experience at BI-MU 2022, she explained the reasons for her choice.

“We chose Italy because of its increasingly decisive role in the industrial robotics market, its ongoing commitment to issues such as education, training and research, its high-level offering in terms of innovative solutions and the presence of numerous start-ups in the area focused on service-robotics. These are the elements that have most influenced our decision," she commented.

"We decided to organise our meeting at RobotHeart, the area dedicated to robotics and hosted by 34.BI-MU in Milan. With our presence, we intend to highlight the importance of Italy in the sector, thus honouring its growth and innovative contribution. In addition, the Assembly will take place almost at the end of my term as President of IFR and therefore this meeting will be even more valuable and meaningful for me," she added.

The robotics sector, she said, is now a wide and varied ecosystem including multiple figures operating in a variety of disciplines. RobotHeart at BI-MU 2022 hosted manufacturers, system integrators, research organisations, universities and visitors from different fields, in an atmosphere where sharing was encouraged. She wished this aspect of the event to be further enhanced, commenting that “RobotHeart can and must be a co-creation event, the result of constant dialogue and discussion among the attendees, a place for exchanging ideas and presenting technological innovations, a balanced mix of exhibition and cultural insight. Because this is the fundamental basis for the development of further innovations in the world of robotics..."

Marina Bill, President of the International Federation of Robotics, at the 33rd edition of BI-MU.

## ROBOTGAMES for young innovators

ROBOTGAMES is a new contest of robotics and automation aimed at young students from scientific high schools, technical/technological institutes and professional schools. It is designed to stimulate young people to try their hand at making robot automation prototypes, with an eye on application in the world of industry.

“The key players in the contest will be young people from upper secondary schools (belonging to scientific high schools, technical/technological institutes and professional institutes) organised in teams. Each team must include between two and five students and must be coordinated by a teacher," outlined UCIMU.

“The prototypes eligible to compete in ROBOTGAMES must involve the integration of at least two technical disciplines including mechanics, automation, electronics, physics and mathematics-IT. They should be able to perform different tasks and respond to different commands such as, for example, moving objects, detecting elements in the field, moving in space etc. An essential requisite for the admission of a prototype to the contest is its industrial application," it continued.

ROBOTGAMES is promoted by UCIMU Academy, an initiative that groups together all the activities carried out by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, with the aim of bringing young people closer to the manufacturing industry whose digital transformation is ongoing. Sponsored by the UCIMU Foundation and with the contribution of Fiera Milano Foundation, ROBOTGAMES makes its debut in 2024 at 34.BI-MU (9-12 October 2024).

On Friday 11 October 2024, the final stage will see a competition among the ten best projects submitted. This will be held in an area dedicated to ROBOTGAMES and set up in one of the halls of 34.BI-MU. The judging committee will select the top three winners, who will receive their awards during an ad hoc ceremony in the BI-MUpiù Arena during BI-MU on Saturday 12 October 2024.

Prizes totalling 20,000 euros, made available by EFIM-ENTE FIERE ITALIANE MACCHINE, will be divided among the three winning teams.

## The additive manufacturing boom

"With a turnover that reached the recordbreaking figure of 16 billion euros in 2022, additive manufacturing's most important markets are in the United States and in Europe. In this global context, Italy is among the Top 10 for the number of patent applications and second place in the European Union in terms of industrial installations. These positive results show that Italy is a market with very high potential for the development of additive manufacturing, which finds application in numerous and varied fields including pharmaceuticals, healthcare, biomedical engineering, as well as aerospace and transport," commented UCIMU.

All these industry sectors will be represented at piùAdditive, the initiative promoted at 34.BI-MU. For several years now, piùAdditive has become a key event for manufacturers, distributors and users of additive solutions. Of the 50,000 operators visiting the last edition of BI-MU (2022), 26% specifically indicated additive manufacturing as an area of interest.

Capable of ensuring better management of energy resources and materials, today's additive manufacturing is at the centre of the sustainability debate, one of the themes at BI-MU 2024 along with digitalisation.

"All this will make piùAdditive even more attractive for operators in the sector, as it will offer interesting business opportunities to those who exhibit in its area. Indeed, piùAdditive will combine the exhibition with networking and cultural insights, thanks a rich programme of events which will be hosted on the stand belonging to AITA-ITALIAN ASSOCIATION OF ADDITIVE TECHNOLOGIES, the sponsor of the initiative," concluded UCIMU.
