# BLECH India 2017 hits new heights

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

THE FIFTH EDITION of BLECH India took place from 27-29 April 2017 at the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai. Over 140 companies exhibited at this sheet metal working exhibition, run by Inter Ads-Brooks, a joint venture between the Indian-based Inter-Ads Exhibitions and Mack Brooks Exhibitions (organiser of EuroBLECH, a leading exhibition for the sheet metal working industry in Hanover, Germany).

The majority of BLECH India exhibitors were Indian-based companies. However, many also came from outside India, namely from Germany; Italy; USA; China; Turkey; UK and the Netherlands. The exhibitors showcased a comprehensive range of machines, tools and solutions for the complete sheet metal forming and fabrication process, as well as live machine demonstrations.

4,568 visitors attended the three-day event, an increase of over 20% compared to the previous edition of BLECH India in 2015. A high percentage of the visitors to the exhibition were company owners or had buying and specifying responsibilities. The exhibition attracted visitors from many key industry sectors, including mechanical engineering, sheet metal products, components, assemblies and the automotive industry.

"As in previous years, BLECH India 2017 was co-located with Fastener Fair India 2017. The largest ever Fastener Fair India was a massive success, welcoming exhibitors and visitors from India and around the world. BLECH India will return to Mumbai in 2019 – see www.blechindia.com," the organiser told ISMR.
