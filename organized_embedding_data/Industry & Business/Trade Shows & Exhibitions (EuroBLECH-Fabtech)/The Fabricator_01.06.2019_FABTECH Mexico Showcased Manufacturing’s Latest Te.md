# FABTECH Mexico Showcased Manufacturing’s Latest Technologies

[TARİH: 01.06.2019 The Fabricator]

FABTECH

®

Mexico, the leading manufacturing event in Latin America for the metal forming, fabricating, welding, and finishing industries, brought the latest manufacturing innovations to Monterrey, Mexico, May 7-9.

Spanning 140,000 net square feet of exhibit space, FABTECH Mexico provided attendees the opportunity to see live demonstrations, explore the latest manufacturing and welding technologies, meet with industry experts, and attend educational sessions covering technical topics such as automation, cutting, forming and fabricating, lasers, lean management, software, finishing, stamping, and welding.

"Giving businesses and customers the opportunity to come together in one place to learn, network, explore new products and technology, and make lasting connections is what has made this show the largest of its kind in Latin America," said Mark Hoper, FMA senior vice president of expositions and media, co-sponsor of FABTECH.

The next FABTECH event will be held Nov. 11-14, 2019, in Chicago.
