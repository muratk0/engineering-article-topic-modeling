FULL HOUSE FOR BLECHEXPO

ISMR SAYS: “Approximately 1,100 exhibitors will showcase their technologies at Blechexpo-Schweisstec over nine exhibition halls in Stuttgart”

Together with the 9th Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology, the 16th Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metalworking will be a key meeting place for global players in sheet metalworking. Held from 7-10 November 2023 at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre (Germany), the event encompasses nine halls which are now fully booked (by over 1,100 exhibitors).

"The trade fair duo presents complete process sequences for modern, future-proof, sheet-metal processing. An impressive supplementary programme includes presentation of the 'best Award 2023', the 'Blechexpo Steel Convention 2023', the 'futureSteel' congress and an expert forum," the show organiser, P. E. Schall, told ISMR.

The Blechexpo/Schweisstec event takes place every two years at Messe Stuttgart. In 2021, the event attracted 953 exhibitors as well as 30,000 visitors from 83 countries.

## Efficient production processes

"On the whole, process automation is being driven further and further in sheetmetalworking," explained project manager, Georg Knauer. "Methods based on datadriven, artificial intelligence algorithms and machine learning are being exploited to an even greater extent in production operations to optimise processes and, as a result, reduce lead times. Efficient sheet-metal processing necessitates flexible machine tools, modern software and virtual simulation. These issues will be addressed intensively at the trade fair. The challenge is sustainability and in sheet-metal processing, this means optimum workpiece utilisation and the avoidance of waste."

The range of topics covered by Blechexpo/ Schweisstec includes efficient process technologies for modern laser technology; fully automated robotic sheet-metal processing machines; precision tools and systems for punching, bending, braking and forming; as well as innovative micro-waterjet cutting technologies. The current shortage of qualified production personnel is fuelling further automation across the industry, for example in materials handling and machine operation. Planning, implementation and analysis of production processes are becoming more efficient thanks to the growing integration of production data.

The trade fair showcases numerous issues covering all aspects of thermal and mechanical sheet-metal processing, along with system peripherals including controllers and software as well as solutions for handling and quality assurance.

"The sheet-metalworking industry is currently facing the challenges of energy efficiency, resource conservation and costeffectiveness which is why high priority is being placed on ever greater degrees of digitalisation and automation to optimise processes," commented the trade fair organiser.

## A packed programme

Blechexpo/Schweisstec will again feature a packed supplementary programme.

The “Blechexpo Stahl Convention 2023" will take place in the Forum area in Hall 10 at noon on the first day of the trade fair. It will focus attention on "Challenges in Steel Markets in 2023. Organised by the Stahl-Kommunikation Agency, it will highlight and expand upon climate protection, the green transformation, decarbonisation and the costs of steel production. Speakers from leading companies with international operations will address these issues and discuss them with convention visitors.

## Award presentations

The "best Award 2023" will be presented for the sixth time at the trade fair, in cooperation with trade journals Blechnet and MM Maschinenmarkt.

The award organisers have specified five submission categories conforming with the themes assigned to the exhibition halls, so that applicants can find the most suitable area for their innovations.

"An expert jury with well-known representatives from the industry sector will select the winners, who will receive their 'best Award' during a festive event on the afternoon of the first day of the trade fair," explained the show organiser.

"Blechexpo/Schweisstec, and above all the 'best Award 2023', will once again showcase lots of innovations and solutions for digitalisation, sustainability and health protection this year," added jury member, Stefanie Wiesner (managing director of the Industrial Division of the German Electric Welding Equipment Association).

Jury member Florian Kellner, production engineering designer at Siemens' equipment manufacturing facility in Amberg, Germany, is also looking forward to the award submissions.

"We can expect technological highlights in the form of future-oriented solutions that link the digital world to the real world in terms of Industry 4.0," he said.

The submission deadline for the "best-Award 2023" has now been extended to 15 September 2023. To make your nomination, see: www.maschinenmarkt-awards.de/blechexpo

## Pivatic Oy

## https://pivatic.com/en/

Pivatic offers a new coil punching and shearing solution.

"Pivatic systems give you multiple options to run your parts. Using advanced nesting, users may choose the part type, amount and the order in which they want the parts to be produced. All parts of the same material can be produced from the same coil width, nested side-by-side. After the Pivatic double punching process, material is cut to length (CTL) from the coil by a shear," it explained.

To nest parts side-by-side on a strip, Pivatic uses a CTW (Cut-to-Width) station to shear and separate the nested parts. All parts are stacked or buffered automatically in piles on top of pallets for easy transportation or transferred directly to follow-up processes. Waste is minimised by filling the coil with nested parts. By separating each process step, it aims to deliver significant leaps in productivity.
