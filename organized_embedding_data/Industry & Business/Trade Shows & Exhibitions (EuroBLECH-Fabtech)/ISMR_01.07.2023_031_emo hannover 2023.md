## EMO Hannover 2019

EMO Hannover 2019 attracted about 117,000 production specialists from 150 countries.

"The mood in the halls was positive, with many exhibitors pleasantly surprised at the high volume of visitor traffic at their stands. At the start of the trade fair, there was some worry about the news that many customers were cutting their travel budgets. Yet the percentage of company executives among trade show attendees was about as high as two years before the event, at 58 per cent. Around half of these came armed with capital investment plans. For overseas visitors, this figure was even higher, at more than 62 per cent," the show organiser explained.

"Approximately 25 per cent of attendees either placed orders during the trade fair or planned to do so in the near future. A further 20 per cent intended to spend money downstream from the trade fair," it added.

The main focus was on expansion and replacement investments in flexible manufacturing, production machines, tools and automation. More than half of all attendees came from overseas, split almost evenly between other European countries and overseas. A 20 per cent growth in attendance from overseas in comparison with the 2017 event was particularly noteworthy. This included a high percentage of Asian guests, who accounted for almost one third of visitors from overseas. China, Japan, Taiwan and India headed the rankings. This trend was clearly related to a higher number of Asian exhibitors at the event in 2019, who encouraged their customers to visit them in Hannover.

Other countries with strong representation at the event included Italy; Poland; Sweden; Russia and Turkey.

Digitalisation and automation also gathered momentum at the event, as it featured the first AI applications in the Start-Up area and at the stands of trailblazing companies. Along with the strong interest in AI and machine learning, visitors' appetite for future visions was reflected in the accompanying events and fora. Topics included not only AI but also additive processes, the industrial internet of things (IIoT), 5G and OPC UA or umati, the new standard interface between machine tools and overarching IT systems.

The standout attraction was the large umati showcase, which included 110 machines from 70 international firms and partners, demonstrating for the first time that the universal interface between machines and IT systems can function across all product types.

## Solutions to challenges

Many exhibitors will be unveiling new products, technologies and software at EMO Hannover 2023.

"European manufacturing companies are faced with multiple challenges when competing internationally. The hurdles that must be overcome range from high energy and personnel costs to shortages of skilled workers and unstable supply chains for a wide variety of components," explained the show organiser, VDW.

"However, these very same difficulties are also giving rise to some remarkable innovations. The smart use of digitalisation – including artificial intelligence – in combination with robotics and automation solutions can bring about major leaps in productivity and help raise competitiveness on a long-term basis.

Cutting-edge solutions will be on show at the EMO Hannover 2023; practical examples from industry and research show the most effective ways of combining the different technologies," it added.

The manufacturing industry is currently having to contend with decreasing batch sizes and increasing levels of variance in parts. The situation is further exacerbated by the shortage of skilled workers in all areas. This is forcing companies to keep their machines in operation for as long as possible.

The effective linking of automation, robotics and digitalisation represents an established solution when it comes to optimising process flows in the production environment. Now this approach is increasingly being enriched by digital twins and artificial intelligence.

## Showcasing trends on joint booths

Joint booths keep production experts up to date with the latest in production technology. They can experience live and on-site the latest trends at a total of four joint booths at EMO Hannover 2023. These booths will be focusing on additive manufacturing; connectivity; open space cobot solutions and sustainability.

Martin Göbel, Director, Exhibitions at EMO organiser VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association) knows the value of a visit.

“Nowhere else can production specialists experience the sector's innovations so close up, presented in thematic packages over the entire process chain and up to date at all times. So, if you're coming to EMO Hannover 2023 in September, you shouldn't miss out on the joint booths," he said.

Companies will be presenting pioneering concepts from the entire bandwidth of the additive process chain, whether direct and indirect 3D printing technologies, engineering materials or rapid product development (RPD).

The Future of Connectivity area focuses on new applications; automation processes; smart production; Industry 4.0; machine learning; predictive maintenance; IIoT (Industrial Internet of Things) and many other aspects. The editorial series 'Future of Connectivity' will also be reporting on these subjects in the run-up to EMO Hannover.

Many exhibitors will be unveiling new products, technologies and software at EMO Hannover 2023.

The Open Space Cobot Solutions area is fully dedicated to the interactions between humans and industrial robots and their actual and potential applications. Manufacturers will be presenting their automation solutions based on cobots and their innovative use: grippers; image processing; measurement systems; software; industrial electronics; feed systems and more can be experienced up close.

At EMO Hannover 2023, the Future of Sustainability in Production area offers visitors the opportunity to learn about current trends in energy efficiency; the integration of renewable energies; circular economy and lifecycle concepts.

Climate change, environmental protection, energy and material efficiency; sustainability is now an integral part of our daily lives. It will also feature prominently at EMO Hannover 2023.

## How to get there

BY CAR

Directions: (Address: Messegelände, Hermes Allee, 30521 Hannover)

Take the A2 motorway from North Rhine-Westphalia and Berlin. The A7 motorway takes you directly to Hannover, whether you are coming from Hamburg or Munich. On the “Gesellschaft für Verkehrsförderung mBH“ (GfV) website, you will find information about parking facilities on the exhibition grounds. The parking fee for cars is 15 euros.

BY TRAIN

North Entrance: via Hannover Main station

For your day visit, use the regular services of Deutsche Bahn AG. The exhibition grounds can be reached from Hannover main station by underground line 8 or 18 in approx. 18 minutes (entrances NORD 1 and 2). Subway stop: Hannover Messe/Nord

West Entrance: via Hannover Messe/Laatzen station

The Hannover Messe/Laatzen station is approximately 400m from the WEST 1 entrance to the grounds. You can reach the grounds on foot by walking over the covered Skywalk transport system, which connects the station with the exhibition grounds. During the trade fair, German Railways will extend its schedule with special train services at Hannover Messe/Laatzen station.

Railway stop: Hannover Messe/Laatzen. There will also be additional metronom and erixx stops at Hannover Messe/Laatzen station.

FlixTrain

FlixTrain also offers train connections to Hannover.

BY PUBLIC TRANSPORT

From the city centre, you reach the exhibition grounds by tram line, U-Bahn, suburban railway or from the south by bus. Entrance NORD 1 + 2. Tram lines 8 and 18.

From Hannover Main Station via stations Kröpcke, Aegidientorplatz, Altenbekener Damm and Bothmerstraße. Entrance OST 3. Tram lines 6 and 16.

Via stations Kröpcke, Aegidientorplatz, Braunschweiger Platz, Bult, Tierärztliche Hochschule and Kronsberg. Entrances: South + West 1. Bus stop lines 340 and 341. Bus stop at the exhibition grounds - Gutenbergstraße stop (South area) and Hannover Messe/Laatzen stop (West 1 area).

You can also use FlixBus for your journey.

BY AIR

Train: From Hannover Airport via Hannover Main Station to the exhibition grounds by local railway connection (S-Bahn) Suburban Railway S5. Travel time is 12-15 minutes.

Bus shuttle: During the trade fairs, shuttle buses provide regular service between the airport and the exhibition centre.
