# ASIAN PROMISE

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

This year’s AsiaBLECH in Suzhou from 11-13 May 2016 is a platform for international sheet metal working manufacturers in China and across Asia.

Don’t miss this year’s AsiaBLECH at Suzhou International Expo Centre in China from 11-13 May 2016. Held over a two-year cycle, it is a central business hub for suppliers and manufacturers of sheet metal working technology in Asia. The event predominantly targets all sheet metal working specialists at all management levels in small- and medium-sized companies as well as large enterprises from all key industry sectors. Clearly structured and subdivided into 20 technology sectors, AsiaBLECH shows the entire sheet metal working technology chain.

“The power of AsiaBLECH, as with EuroBLECH in Europe, is a clear established focus on sheet metal forming, fabrication and manufacturing ensuring that, unlike many general engineering events, AsiaBLECH attracts only visitors from this sector who are focused on sheet metal technologies,” explained David Tellett, managing director of Mack Brooks Exhibitions Ltd which also organises EuroBLECH, Europe’s largest sheet metal exhibition.

“Following the same successful model, AsiaBLECH is an international event for a pan-regional international audience drawing specialist visitors and buyers from each of the countries across the Asia Pacific region. China has become a central trade event hub in the region, hosting many leading international exhibitions. We expect around 100 exhibitors and 5000 sheet metal visitors, from China, Taiwan, South Korea, Southeast Asia and other parts of the world.”

Online registration for the event has started and visitors can now register at www.asiablech.com/en
