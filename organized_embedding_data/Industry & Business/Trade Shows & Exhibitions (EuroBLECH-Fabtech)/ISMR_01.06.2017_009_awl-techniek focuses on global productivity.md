# AWL-Techniek focuses on global productivity

[TARİH: 01.06.2017 ISMR]

On 17-18 May 2017, AWL organised its ninth series of 'AWL Tech Days'. Customers from all over the world travelled to Harderwijk, The Netherlands for a 'Lunch2Lunch' meeting.

"As production is becoming increasingly more global and the world continues to change at a rapid pace, we see that new developments, cultural differences, the economic climate and politics all have a huge impact on our day-to-day business. The key is to stay on track, now and in the future. During the AWL Tech Days, customers gained interesting insights and were able to contribute to discussions. With interesting speakers, innovative projects in the AWL R&D Fieldlab, an interactive shop floor tour and great networking opportunities, the AWL Tech Days were a great success," the company told ISMR.

Presentations from AWL were given on topics such as machine software and modular fixture design and demonstrations were also shown in the Fieldlab on Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality, HMI (Human Machine Interface) design and messaging etc. AWL also discussed the world ahead, identifying future game changers and advising manufacturers how to adapt to them.

Guest speakers were also invited from TRUMPF, SKS, Brose Automotive and Joep Hofhuis, Professor at the Erasmus University in Rotterdam. They all focused on dealing with production on a global scale, including how manufacturers can overcome cultural differences, local requirements and legislation to be successful around the globe.

Customers were given a tour of various projects in which AWL is now involved. A large number of automotive projects were shown including machines for the production of body structural parts such as the door frame, A-pillar, wishbone and rear seats. Finally, the CTO (Configure to Order) principle was explained by showing a modular laser cell used for the production of various different car seat elements.

AWL-Techniek recently welcomed its 500th employee at its production site in Napajedla, the Czech Republic. With branches in the Netherlands, the Czech Republic, China, Mexico and soon in the U.S., AWL considers committed employees to be 'the organisation's greatest asset'.
