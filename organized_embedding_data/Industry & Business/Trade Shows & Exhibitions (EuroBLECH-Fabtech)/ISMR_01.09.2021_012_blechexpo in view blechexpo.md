# BLECHEXPO IN VIEW Blechexpo

[TARİH: 01.09.2021 ISMR]

Together with the 8th Schweisstec international trade fair for joining technology, the 15th Blechexpo international trade fair for sheet metal working will open its doors in Stuttgart, Germany, on 26-29 October 2021. The latest technologies for sheet metal processing and metalworking, including joining technology, are on the agenda. Exhibitors and expert visitors will discuss stand-alone and system solutions for complete process chains involving the fabrication of parts made from sheet metal, profiles and pipe – live and in person.

"The specialist audience will meet at Blechexpo/Schweisstec 2021 to remain at the cutting edge and draw inspiration from efficient, up-to-date process technologies in sheet metal processing and joining applications. Digitalisation, sustainability and process efficiency, within the context of a rapidly changing production landscape, are driving further development in the sheet metal processing industry," commented the show organiser, P. E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG.

Over the next few pages, ISMR previews selected highlights from the 15th Blechexpo and the 8th Schweisstec exhibition that will take place in Stuttgart, Germany, from 26-29 October 2021.

ISMR SAYS: "In 2019, the event attracted 1498 exhibitors from 36 countries, as well as 41,152 visitors from 113 countries."

Exhibition topics include stand-alone and system solutions for process sequences involving the fabrication of parts made from sheet metal, profile and pipe, as well as associated thermal and mechanical processing: cutting technologies; processing machines; equipment for stamping; bending, press braking and forming; system peripherals including controls and software; and solutions for handling and quality assurance.

A number of new innovations - products, services and technologies - will be showcased at Blechexpo/Schweisstec, which attracts an international audience.

## Networking measures, demos and presentations

"High booking levels in recent months for the live event in Stuttgart from 26 to 29 October 2021 underscore just how much the industry sector cherishes the event as a unique, globally recognised marketplace," commented Georg Knauer, Blechexpo/ Schweisstec project manager. "Exhibitors and expert visitors are looking forward to this Autumn's trade fair highlight and to face-to-face meetings at the on-site event."

P. E. Schall aims to fulfil all the prerequisite measures for providing exhibitors and expert visitors with suitable conditions for optimised networking at Blechexpo/Schweisstec, and has endorsed the hygiene concept adopted by the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre. This has been extensively coordinated with German state authorities and is based on the valid Corona Ordinance of the State of Baden-Württemberg.

"Blechexpo/Schweisstec also provides an excellent opportunity for collaboration because the issue of networking is at the top of the agenda for progressive companies - in terms of process engineering and digital technology. Networking is by no means just a fashionable buzzword and will shape future business. Exhibitors and expert visitors will benefit from the synergy of the trade fair duo and from the strictly practice-oriented event concept. The trade fair demonstrations of fully automated sheet metal processing machines with robotics and gantry solutions, as well as system peripherals with controls and software, assist visitors on the vital decisions they have to make on investment projects," added the show organiser.

The event will also feature an exhibitor forum, with presentations by exhibitors to visitors. Research institutes will also introduce theoretical and practical projects to visitors via presentations at Blechexpo. Innovation at the event will also be celebrated when Blechnet and MM MaschinenMarkt present the "Blechexpo Award", for the most innovative exhibits at the international trade fair, at 5pm on 26 October 2021.

## Sheet metal meets joining technology

The Blechexpo and Schweisstec event takes place every two years at the trade fair centre in Stuttgart (Landesmesse Stuttgart) and covers the complementary technologies of sheet metal processing and joining technology. In 2019, the event attracted 1498 exhibitors from 36 countries, as well as 41,152 visitors from 113 countries. Advanced mechanical joining and fastening technologies will also be showcased at the event because production is working to a greater extent with new plain and hybrid materials, for example within the context of lightweight design. Modern cutting technologies, such as high-pressure waterjet cutting, will also be in the spotlight at Blechexpo/Schweisstec. Exhibitors from the grinding technology sector will come together in Hall 5 in the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre. Hall 7 will showcase new scalable solutions for visitors seeking individual production layouts for special joining and fastening tasks.

"Sheet metal handling and processing is of central importance in every branch of manufacturing because the production process begins with coil handling, followed by punching, bending, pressing and braking. As these process steps are being networked and automated to an even greater extent, a consistently digitalised setting for sheet metal processing and metalworking will also be one of the core issues at Blechexpo/Schweisstec in 2021," added the show organiser.
