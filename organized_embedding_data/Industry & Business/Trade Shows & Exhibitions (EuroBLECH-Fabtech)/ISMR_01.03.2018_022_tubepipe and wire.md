# TUBE,PIPE AND WIRE

[TARİH: 01.03.2018 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: “Tubes and pipes are some of the most versatile products and design elements

Over 2,500 key players from the industry will present innovations from the wire, cable and pipe industries at Wire and Tube 2018 in Düsseldorf from 16-20 April 2018.

"The wire, cable and tube industries look to the future with high expectations. Rising investment in global markets are encouraging for the steel industry worldwide. Industries are therefore looking to the metal trade fair summit

Over 2,500 key players from the industry will present innovations from the wire, cable and pipe industries on 118,000 square metres of net exhibition space (16 halls) over the five days of the trade fair.

Wire and Cable Trade Fair, and Tube, the International Tube and Pipe Trade Fair, will open their doors at the Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre in Germany. The event will showcase innovative technologies, new machinery and equipment as well as products and services.

"We look forward to April 2018 with optimism as the sectors enjoy a stable investment climate," the show organiser, Messe Düsseldorf, told ISMR.

## Versatile applications

Tubes and pipes are some of the most versatile products and design elements in general. Whether in the energy sector, mechanical, power or automotive engineering, in the chemical or petrochemical industry, in construction or in one of many other areas, tubes and tube designs in different shapes, sizes and weights can be found everywhere.

The event will showcase innovative technologies, new machinery and equipment as well as products and services.

Steel tubes and pipes cover a particularly broad spectrum, as these products are conquering new applications, partly due to the innovative strength of manufacturers.

In the energy sector, pipes have become indispensable for the transportation of oil and gas, while also playing a major role as design elements. In fact, most structural foundations of offshore wind turbines consist of pipe elements. Pipes also fulfil important functions in the offshore production of oil and gas.

"Other growth areas that will be supported by research and development projects over the next few years will focus on the customised optimisation of pipe and tube properties. Take, for example, new developments for automotive engineering. Although the combustion engine has been written off by many, automotive manufacturers are not alone in believing that it will remain an important form of propulsion for many years to come. However, this makes it necessary to achieve further reductions in consumption and in harmful emissions – an area where precision tubes can help to achieve higher injection pressures that will warrant a more eco-friendly combustion process. In non-motor applications, tubes with variable wall thicknesses can be used to support load-optimised lightweight construction," commented Messe Düsseldorf.

Lightweight automotive construction is also at the focus of research and development at Benteler AG. Its latest innovations in automotive tube solutions included the prototype of a front axle carrier for electric vehicles. Thanks to a new innovative steel design, the weight has reportedly been reduced by 35 per cent.

The automotive industry should also soon benefit from ultra high-strength tubes which can be used as innovative automotive suspension systems. Compared to series production today, these chassis solutions should save up to 30 per cent in weight.

Wire and Tube has grown steadily in Düsseldorf since the mid-1980s. Every two years, Düsseldorf becomes the meeting place for key players in these industries. The demand for equipment, machinery and products from the cable, wire and tube industries is also growing in other countries. Now, Wire and Tube satellite trade fairs can be found in Russia; India; China; Thailand and Brazil.

"2017 was the first year of our active involvement in Iran. It was the first time that Messe Düsseldorf acted as an overseas associate at Iran Wire. We've been targeting and supporting all international companies (except US companies) that would like to present themselves as exhibitors at the trade fair in Tehran," commented Friedrich-Georg Kehrer, Global Portfolio Director for Metals and Flow Technologies, Messe Düsseldorf GmbH.

## Getting there

Düsseldorf Airport (DUS) is only 3km from the exhibition centre, with direct links to 200 destinations in 50 countries on four continents. It takes a few minutes to get to the exhibition centre, either by bus (no. 896) or by taxi. The airport is also very close to the centre of Düsseldorf.

For those driving, the A44 motorway goes directly past the exhibition centre. Leave at Junction 29, signposted "Messe/Arena," and head for the two large car parks, P1 and P2. Free shuttle buses are available from the car parks to the trade fair entrances. The Düsseldorf Exhibition Centre can be reached from Cologne within about one hour and from Dortmund and Aachen within about an hour and a quarter. The exhibition centre can also be reached from neighbouring Neuss, Krefeld, Mönchengladbach and Moers in 30 minutes.

For arrival by local transport, take tram no. U78 ESPRIT arena/Messe Nord or U79 Messe Ost (exit at MesseOst/Stockumer Kirchstr.) or bus no. 722 (exit at Messe Ost or Messe Süd/CCD). There are two major transport hubs where you are likely to change: Hauptbahnhof (central station) and Heinrich-Heine-Allee. Nearly all destinations in and around Düsseldorf can be reached from these stations.

During many of Düsseldorf’s trade fairs, Rheinbahn provides an additional airport shuttle bus (no. 896).

## Wire 2018

"Wire 2018 will be held in exhibition halls 9 to 16. On display will be machinery and equipment for wire manufacturing and finishing, process technology tools and auxiliary materials for process engineering as well as materials, special wires and cables. In addition to this, innovations measuring and control technology, as well as test engineering and specialist areas, will be showcased. There will be an even stronger focus on glass fibre cables. With this move, we are responding to the increasing use of fibre in the energy, construction and communications sectors," explained event organiser, Messe Düsseldorf.

The wire, cable and glass fibre machinery segments will be presented in Halls 9 to 14 and 16, with fastener technology following on from this area in Hall 15. Mesh welding and spring-making will be in Hall 13.

Traditionally well represented are exhibitors from Italy; Belgium; France; Spain; Austria; The Netherlands; Switzerland; Turkey; UK; Sweden and Germany. From overseas, many companies are expected to come from the USA; South Korea; Taiwan; India and China.

## Tube 2018

Tube 2018 will take place in Halls 3 to 7.0 and Halls 16, 17 and 18. Its extensive range includes machinery and equipment for pipe and tube production, finishing and processing as well as raw materials; tubes and accessories; second-hand machinery; process technology tools; auxiliary materials; measuring and control technology as well as test engineering.

This is complemented by pipeline and OCTG technology, profiles and machinery as well as plastic tubes. In response to the sustained high demand from China, this event will once again host a China Pavilion. Under the banner 'Meet China's Expertise', Chinese companies from the wire, cable and tube and pipe sectors will be present in Hall 16.

Tube accessories can be found in Hall 17 while tube trade and tube manufacturing can be found in Halls 3 and 4. Visitors can also see tube bending and forming technology innovations in Hall 5. Machinery and equipment, as well as tube and pipe processing machinery, will be on display in Halls 6 and 7a. Profiles and plastic tubes can be found in Halls 3 to 7.0 as well as Halls 16, 17 and 18.

"At Tube 2018, the countries represented with the most exhibitors are Italy; The Netherlands; Spain; Turkey; France; UK; Austria; Switzerland; Poland and Germany. Most overseas exhibitors will come from India; the USA; China and South Korea," added the event organiser.
