# A strong finish

[TARİH: 01.11.2021 ISMR]

A positive mood prevailed at DeburringEXPO, which was held as an on-site trade fair from 12-14 October 2021. Most of the 1100 expert visitors came to the Karlsruhe Exhibition Centre in Germany with clear aims, with some bringing sample parts or drawings, and a great need for information.

"This resulted in excellent contacts, valuable RFQs and interesting projects for almost all of the 105 exhibiting companies. Roughly 50% of the expert visitors took advantage of the opportunity to gather information about current trends, the latest developments and best practice solutions in deburring and the production of precision finishes at the specialist forum," the event organiser told ISMR.

"As expected, fewer visitors attended the trade fair this year than in previous years, but the quality was very good. Visitors are coming to our booth from widely differing industries with very specific tasks and projects," explained Michael Striebe, sales manager for barrel finishing technology at Rösler Oberflächen GmbH.

The best represented sectors, from a visitor standpoint, included machinery and equipment manufacturing; the automotive industry; medical technology; metalworking and metal processing; hydraulics; tool and mould-making as well as aviation and aerospace.
