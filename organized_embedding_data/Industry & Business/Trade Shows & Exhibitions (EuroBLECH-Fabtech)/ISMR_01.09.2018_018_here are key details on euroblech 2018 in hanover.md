# Here are key details on EuroBLECH 2018 in Hanover to help you plan your visit.

[TARİH: 01.09.2018 ISMR]

EuroBLECH is a highly international event and the world's largest exhibition for the sheet metal working industry. More than 1500 exhibitors from 40 countries are expected to present their

latest developments, from all areas of sheet metal working, across eight halls (halls 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17 and 27) at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds. The event, which will be held from 23-26 October 2018, is viewed as a key barometer for the sheet metal sector. Exhibitors will display a comprehensive range of machinery, tools and systems for the entire value chain of sheet metal processing.

## EuroBLECH Exhibition Profile

The exhibition profile includes sheet metal; semi-finished and finished products; handling; separation; forming; flexible sheet metal working; tube/section processing; joining; welding; additive manufacturing; surface treatment; processing of hybrid structures; tools; quality control; CAD/CAM/CIM systems and R&D.

## Sheet metal in focus

The exhibitor list, available on the EuroBLECH website, is regularly updated and provides plenty of information on exhibiting companies, such as exhibitor profiles, online press boxes, company videos and contact details. The subscription form for EuroBLECH News is also available on the website www.euroblech.com

Job listings are published on the EuroBLECH website. Participation as a job seeker is free of charge. More information is available in the visitor section of the exhibition website – see www.euroblech.com/2018/english/visitors/job-vacancies/

The EuroBLECH 2018 catalogue can be purchased on-site during the exhibition.

The world's largest sheet metal working technology exhibition attracts top industry professionals from all over the world. It predominantly targets all sheet metal working specialists at all management levels in small and medium-sized companies as well as large enterprises. Visitors come from all key industry sectors.

Shuttle buses operate from Hanover airport (Terminal C) to the exhibition grounds (Entrance West 1). The journey lasts approximately 30 minutes. The taxi journey from the airport to the Exhibition Grounds takes approx. 30 minutes, depending upon traffic. Alternatively, the suburban railway (S-Bahn) line 5 (located between Terminals B and C) will take you from the airport to the main railway station (approx. 16 minutes).

If you are travelling by train, 'Hanover Messe/Laatzen' is the exhibition grounds' own station. From there, the covered Skywalk will take you to entrance West 1. Alternatively, from the main railway station ('Hauptbahnhof'), tram lines 8 and 18 provide a direct connection to the exhibition grounds (approx. 18 minutes).

## FRAUNHOFER-GESELLSCHAFT

AT EUROBLECH 2018, six research institutes in the Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft will present the results of their latest research activities in sheet metal working. The focus will be on implementation of the IIoT for forming technologies, cutting, welding and joining of metals and hybrid materials, thermal-assisted processing with progressive or transfer tools and measurement technologies for the measurement of coatings and for automated, non-destructive testing.

The Fraunhofer-Gesellschaft is a leading organisation for applied research in Europe. Around 70 per cent of its contract research revenue of two billion euros is from contracts with industry and publicly financed research projects.

## INSPECVISION Ltd.

INSPECVISION WAS formed by sheet metal manufacturers in 2004 to create a complete sheet metal inspection and reverse engineering solution. The system can automatically inspect or reverse-engineer sheet metal parts at every stage of production (flat, folded, welded or assembled). Flat parts are measured with its Planar machine, which takes up to 40 million measurements in 0.1 seconds. The measurements are then automatically compared to a CAD file to create the inspection report.

For folded and assembled parts, InspecVision developed the OptiScan. "It is the only 3D scanner in the world which can measure surfaces and edges in 3D. 3D edges are critical when measuring sheet metal parts as their geometry is mostly defined by their edges, not by their surfaces," commented Dr. Jan Antonis, Managing Director, InspecVision. Available in a range of flexible configurations, the Planar and OptiScan integrate to create a complete sheet metal inspection solution.

## MATE PRECISION TOOLING www.mate.com

AT THE Mate Precision Tooling stand at EuroBLECH 2018, visitors will see Mate's greatly expanded QCT™ punch press tooling product line (including QCT Metric™, AMX QCT™ for AMADA air blow systems and MXC QCT™ for Wilson Series 90 systems). The new Mate Versadie HD™ is designed to improve the slitting of thick materials, taking clamp clearing solutions to a new level with several new design features. First, there is no need to replace the entire die with replaceable die inserts.
