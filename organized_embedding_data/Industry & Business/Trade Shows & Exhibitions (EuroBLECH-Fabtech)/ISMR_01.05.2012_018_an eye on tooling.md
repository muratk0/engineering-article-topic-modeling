# AN EYE ON TOOLING

[TARİH: 01.05.2012 ISMR]

ISMR previews the MACH TOOL/ITM POLAND trade fair in Poznan

ISMR SAYS:

“This year's MACH TOOL/ ITM POLAND exhibition will take place from 29 May to 1 June 2012”

The 2011 MACH TOOL/ITM POLAND event was attended by over 900 exhibitors from 32 countries, and more than 100 European and world market premieres were presented at the exhibition. The innovative event was visited by 16,800 people from Poland and abroad.

Czech Republic, Finland, South Korea, Russia, Turkey, Japan, Taiwan, Australia, China, Denmark, France, India, USA, UK, Portugal, Tunisia and Ukraine – these were just some of the 32 countries of origin of the exhibitors at the 2011 trade fair Innovations-Technologies-Machines Poland (ITM POLAND).

45 per cent representation of foreign companies ranks ITM Poland as a leading international trade fair featuring innovative solutions for industry in Central and Eastern Europe. The ITM Poland 2011 trade fair (held in Poznan, 14-17 June 2011) brought together over 900 exhibitors and represented companies that showcased their offerings over 20,000 square meters of space.

"The 2012 trade fair from 29 May to 1 June 2012 will be the venue to present high-tech machinery and equipment for industries such as welding, metallurgy, plastic processing of metal, metallurgy, varnish manufacture, transport as well as health and safety. These solutions are often developed by teams of engineers, representatives of scientific institutions and even architects and designers. The goal is simple: to increase the parameters of the devices in terms of performance, precision, modernity, and... be the best," said the organiser.

The PIF Gold Medal Competition facilitates market competition, symbolises the product's values, its modern features and innovative character. The companies that have gained the right to place the sign on their products confirm its marketing value. These can be found throughout the exhibition. See http://www.binte.pl/
