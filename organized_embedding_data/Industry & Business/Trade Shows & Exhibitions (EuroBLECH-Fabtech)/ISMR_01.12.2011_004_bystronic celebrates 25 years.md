# BYSTRONIC CELEBRATES 25 YEARS

[TARİH: 01.12.2011 ISMR]

BYSTRONIC, THE Swiss specialist for laser technology and supplier of complete solutions for sheet metal processing, is a quarter of a century old. The anniversary celebrations took place mainly during the Competence Days, in which customers from around the globe participated.

During a three-week period in September, Bystronic Competence Days were held at its headquarters in Niederönz, Switzerland. During the total of eleven individual events, some one thousand guests from almost 50 countries participated.

The programme consisted of technical presentations and discussions about current issues in the field of sheet metal processing, of live presentations and a tour of the production plant. It also featured an exhibition about the 25-year history of Bystronic, in which the still young but nevertheless turbulent years of the company’s history were brought to life.

At the various events, Bystronic’s CEO Ferdi Töngi expressed his opinion that the company will continue to maintain its track record but pointed out that challenges will not decrease in the future. “In a changing environment, our task will continue to be the satisfaction of the requirements of a discerning group of customers,” he stated.

Bystronic is one of the pioneers in the development and manufacture of laser cutting systems, and today is one of the leading suppliers of complete solutions for sheet metal processing. It currently employs a workforce of 1400 employees worldwide.

In Niederönz, the prime output is laser and waterjet cutting systems for the worldwide market. Other development and production centres are based in Gotha (Germany), Tianjin (China) and Brașov (Romania). Gotha and Tianjin share responsibility for the production of press brakes. Tianjin is additionally responsible for the manufacture of laser cutting systems for the Chinese market. In Brașov, on the other hand, secondhand machines are refurbished for sale on the secondhand market. Additionally, the company has sales and service subsidiaries in 25 countries.
