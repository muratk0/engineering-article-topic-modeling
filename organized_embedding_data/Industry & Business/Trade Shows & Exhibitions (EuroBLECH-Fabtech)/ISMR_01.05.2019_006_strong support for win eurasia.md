# Strong support for WIN EURASIA

[TARİH: 01.05.2019 ISMR]

WIN EURASIA, ORGANISED by Deutsche Messe subsidiary, Hannover Fairs Turkey, once again brought leading companies from 122 countries in the manufacturing industry together from 14-17 March 2019 in Istanbul, Turkey. The event was opened by Turkish Republic Ministry of Industry and Technology Deputy, Mehmet Fatih Kacir, and participating organisations predominant in the manufacturing sector. It also attracted almost 1,400 participants from 20 countries with 77,403 visitors from 111 countries, with six trade fairs under the WIN- EURASIA banner.

Thousands of exhibitors and visitors attended the trade fairs (under one roof) of Metalworking EURASIA; Surface Technology EURASIA; Welding EURASIA; IAMD EURASIA; Electrotech EURASIA and CeMAT EURASIA. Digital Transformation in manufacturing was a key focus at the event. Industrial products and services from sheet metal processing to metal forming technologies; automation services to electric and electronic equipment; hydraulic and pneumatic services to intralogistics were brought together under the digital transformation banner. ISMR was also distributed throughout the exhibition and exhibited at the show.

"Organised with the support of the Ministry of Economy, Ministry of Industry and Technology and KOSGEB (Small and Medium Industry Development Organisation), as well as Hannover Fairs Turkey, the event served as a platform for numerous innovations in its 26th year. The entire eco-system required by factories of the future, ranging from steel plate processing to metal forming technologies; automation services to electrical and electronic equipment; and from hydraulic and pneumatic services to on-site logistics, came together at WIN EURASIA," explained the show organiser.

According to the organiser, the event hosted more than 500 delegations from 34 countries within the scope of its 'International Buyers' Delegation Programme'. Delegations arrived from France; Italy; Spain; Greece; Belarus; Morocco; Kyrgyzstan; Kazakhstan; Russia; Serbia; Slovenia; Tajikistan; Ukraine; Tunisia; Algeria and Lebanon, signing off on new agreements with over 1400 exporters.

Held for the first time at WIN EURASIA, in collaboration with ENOSAD, top ranking projects in the "WINOVATION" contest were also rewarded. Project applications were examined by a jury of university academicians, professors and sector representatives. Brands ranking the highest in terms of innovation in a range of categories were presented with their awards during the show.

WIN EURASIA was organised in collaboration with organisations and institutions such as MIB; MAIB; MAKFED; ISDER; ENOSAD; AKDER; ETMD; ROBODER; VDMA and ZVEI. With the Chambers of Industry and Commerce and the Trade Associations' collaboration, WIN EURASIA also hosted Anatolian delegation groups from 41 cities around Turkey. The 27th edition will be held from 12-15 March 2020.
