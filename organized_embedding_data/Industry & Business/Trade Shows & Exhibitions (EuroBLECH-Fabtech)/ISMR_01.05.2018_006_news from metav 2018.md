# News from METAV 2018

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

METAV 2018, THE 20th International Exhibition for Metalworking Technologies, ended in Düsseldorf on 24 February 2018 after five eventful days.

"The excellent economic state of the industry was demonstrated, as expected, at METAV 2018 too. In the visitor survey, about one third of visitors indicated that they are planning investments, primarily in machine tools, measuring technology, manufacturing and process automation and precision tools.

"This shows us that we are exactly right to focus METAV on the core area of metalworking and the supplementary topics in the areas, said Dr Wilfried Schäfer, Director of VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association), which organised METAV.

"There is a downside to the booming economy for us too, however. Company capacity utilisation is very high. It was therefore only logical that many companies find it difficult to delegate staff. As a result 26,500 trade visitors came instead of the 30,000 that we expected." added Schäfer. The proportion of visitors from countries outside Germany remained constant at 11 per cent, however. They came from about 50 different countries, with the largest contingents arriving from the Netherlands, Switzerland, Belgium and Austria.

As expected, three quarters of German visitors came from West and North Germany. About one fifth came to Düsseldorf from South and South-West Germany. The area concept, which was launched two years ago and focuses on specific topics (quality, additive manufacturing, tool and mould production and medical technology) is now fully established with exhibitors and visitors. The areas highlight topics and products. Additional information is provided in the associated fora.

Industry 4.0 also played a central role. Relevant solutions were presented in the theme park with the same name as well as on many of the 560 exhibitors' stands. VDW took advantage of the trade fair environment to report on its industry project to develop a joint interface for linking machines to higher-level IT systems that is not tied to a specific manufacturer. "A great deal of work still lies ahead of us, but many suppliers and users in small and medium-sized companies are interested in this," added Schäfer.

"In view of the digitisation process and the changes associated with Industry 4.0, sustainability in recruitment development is the key to corporate success in future. These were among the central issues in the talks we held with instructors and teachers at the special youth show," explained Peter Bole, head of the Machine Manufacturing Recruitment Foundation.

Additional events at METAV 2018 were focused on additive manufacturing, grinding and clamping technology and fire protection. One of the highlights was the Inside 3D Printing conference. The programme included 45 presentations by international speakers and discussion and interaction with experts.

"All in all, METAV 2018 confirmed the strong economic position of the industry and the willingness of industrial customers to invest, concluded Wilfried Schäfer from VDW. The next METAV will take place in March 2020.
