# FABTECH Canada Returns to Toronto

[TARİH: 01.02.2014 The Fabricator]

The manufacturing industry is abuzz with anticipation of FABTECH® Canada returning to the newly renovated Toronto Congress Centre on March 18–20, 2014. The only event of its kind in Canada, FABTECH is a one-stop, all-encompassing venue for the latest technologies and trends in fabricating, welding, metal forming, stamping, coating, and finishing. The 2014 event is significantly expanded in both size and scope.

“The decision to expand the show is based on the success of the 2012 premiere event, which was the first time the world-renowned FABTECH brand launched a show in Canada,” said Janine Saperson, show manager with SME, which is hosting FABTECH Canada 2014 with other industry leaders FMA (Fabricators & Manufacturers Association Intl.), AWS (American Welding Society), PMA (Precision Metalforming Association), and CCAI (Chemical Coaters Association Intl.).

Part of the 2014 Canadian expansion is the addition of key industry partners PMA and CCAI; a larger conference and welding area; brand-new finishing and stamping pavilions featuring a wide range of exhibiting companies making their debut at the show; and extensive, interactive educational and networking opportunities.

“In Canada there is growing demand for the technical expertise and industry insight provided by an event like FABTECH, which is evident by the fact that the exhibitor floor is already 80 percent sold,” Saperson said. “In Ontario alone there are 1.7 million manufacturing professionals — the largest amount of manufacturing employees of any jurisdiction in both Canada and the U.S. — and the show is geared towards the needs of these employees, from industries such as automotive and energy to transportation and construction, as well as businesses that either produce or rely on equipment and machinery in their day-to-day operations.”

The Canadian show comes on the heels of the massive, record-setting FABTECH 2013, which took place in Chicago last November. The event drew over 40,000 visitors and 1,500 exhibitors. FABTECH expands its reach in 2014 with shows in Mexico and Atlanta after the Canadian event.

“Canada is a key market in the metal forming, fabricating, and welding industry, and the introduction of a major finishing and coatings component to the Canadian show is a testament to the growth of this sector and the increased need for the latest finishing and coatings solutions and technologies that will benefit businesses in both this country and beyond,” said Anne Goyer, executive director, CCAI.

“The success of the FABTECH brand, both in Canada and around the world, is the result of the strong combination of the five partners, each of which brings a slightly different perspective to the show,” said Mark Hoper, vice president expositions and media, FMA. “Together, we are excited to bring an expanded event to such a key market as Canada in 2014.”
