# Gearing up for FABTECH

[TARİH: 01.09.2022 ISMR]

North America's largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event heads to the Georgia World Congress Centre in Atlanta from 8-10 November 2022. Live equipment demonstrations, featured keynotes, expert panels and other special events will allow attendees the opportunity to interact with a large gathering of technical experts and visionaries to learn what's new and what's next in manufacturing.

The FABTECH Event Partners include five associations: the American Welding Society (AWS); the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA); SME; the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI).

"FABTECH provides a convenient 'one-stop shop' venue where you can meet with worldclass suppliers and see the latest industry products and developments as well as find the tools to improve productivity, increase profits and discover new solutions to all of your metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs," the event organisers told ISMR.

FABTECH 2022 will encompass three halls at the Georgia World Congress Centre where attendees will find a variety of technology pavilions dedicated to Forming & Fabricating, Tube & Pipe, METALFORM, Welding, Finishing, 3D/Additive Manufacturing and Robotics & Industrial Automation.

FABTECH 2021 welcomed over 24,000 attendees from across the United States to Chicago with opportunities to experience product evaluation, in-person networking, education and collaborative discussions on key topics impacting the industry. Three exhibit halls hosted close to 1,000 exhibitors (including 93 new exhibitors), demonstrating over 400 new products and highlighting emerging trends and technologies.

FABTECH also offers a comprehensive conference, educational programme and related events, webinars and competitions.■
