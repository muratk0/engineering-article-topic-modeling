With a total of 1,550 exhibitors from 40 countries presenting the latest machines, tools and systems for sheet metal working, EuroBLECH 2014 (the 23rd International Technology Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working) is set to be the business barometer for the global sheet metal working industry.

This year’s EuroBLECH occupies eight halls offering even more space for live demonstrations of innovative machinery and future production trends. At EuroBLECH 2012, 1505 exhibitors from 39 countries showcased a wide range of innovative solutions, technologies and live machine demonstrations. ## AMB PICOT S.A.S. www.ambPICOT.com

## BOSCHERT
www.boschert.de

BOSCHERT WILL SHOWCASE its Model CuProfi (copper, aluminium and steel bar CNC punching machine) at this year’s EuroBLECH. It features hydraulic material clamping from the sides to eliminate waste and ensure exact positioning before punching. A feeding system pulls the material through the punch head for complete processing with minimal waste.

The machine accepts thick turret Amada-style tooling and readily available punches and dies. An automatic part removal system is standard and consists of a full-length table that supports finished parts before separation and sorting. An optional tapping head fits in one punching station and there is an optional marking station.

## DALLAN SpA (DALCOS) www.dallan.com

AFTER INVESTING two years in research and development, Dalcos (Dallan coil punching division) will introduce its most advanced coil fed cutting system at EuroBLECH. Covered by international patent, the Dalcos LXN Laser system is suitable for the flexible production of medium and large batch parts fed from coil. The machine is powered by a 1kW fiber laser source, suitable for processing thin materials with optimal speed, and can be upgraded to 4kW operation.

Any coil widths up to 1000mm for the LXN 1000 and 1500mm for the LXN 1500 can be processed. Both machines cut up to 1000mm in the X axis. With these capacities, the LXN systems have a very compact footprint. Installation can be made over less than 30sqm including straightener and feeder. The LXN is also available in the combo punch/laser version, the Dalcos ELXN.

## INSPECVISION LTD www.inspecvision.com

INSPECVISION was formed in 2004 to create a complete sheet-metal inspection and reverse engineering solution. The system can automatically inspect or reverse engineer sheetmetal parts at every stage of production – flat, folded, welded or assembled. Flat parts are measured with the Planar machine which takes up to 40 million measurements in 0.1 seconds. The measurements are then automatically compared to a CAD file to create the inspection report. For folded and assembled parts, InspecVision developed the OptiScan.

“Optiscan is the only 3D scanner in the world which can measure surfaces and edges in 3D. 3D edges are critical when measuring sheet metal parts as their geometry is mostly defined by their edges, not by their surfaces,” explained Dr. Jan Antonis, MD of InspecVision. Available in a range of flexible configurations, the Planar and OptiScan integrate to create a complete sheet metal inspection solution.

## RESATO INTERNATIONAL BV www.resato.com

## DUTCH WATERJET MANUFACTURER

Resato will present its new Flex-zone at EuroBLECH. Most traditional waterjets use light screens surrounding the cutting table to comply with safety regulations. Resato has found a way for these light screens to be placed onto the bridge where they move along with the cutting heads, to provide the necessary safety only where needed: in the cutting zone. The Flex-zone option enables safe loading and unloading of material while the waterjet cuts. This reduces downtime to a minimum, rendering a productivity increase of up to 40%, says Resato.

With this innovation, Resato says that it further reduces the competitive gap between waterjet cutters and both laser cutters and plasma cutters. The Flex-zone option can be retrofitted to most ACM cutting tables due to the modular concept of the ACM-line.
