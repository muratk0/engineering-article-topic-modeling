# FOCUS ON TUBE

[TARİH: 01.02.2016 ISMR]

The next concurrent staging of Wire Düsseldorf, the International wire and cable trade fair, and Tube Düsseldorf, the international tube and pipe trade fair, will take place from 4 – 8 April 2016 at the exhibition grounds in Düsseldorf, Germany. Over 2,500 exhibitors will showcase the latest technologies from the wire, cable, tube and pipe processing industries. Tube Düsseldorf is held every two years.

So far, 1,212 companies from 50 countries have registered for Wire 2016 (59,000 square metres). Over 1,190 exhibitors from 51 countries (over 50,000 square metres) at Tube 2016 will give a broad overview of developments in raw materials; tubes and accessories; tube manufacturing machinery; rebuild and reconditioned machinery; process technology tools and auxiliaries as well as measuring, control and testing technology; pipeline and OTG technology; profiles and machinery. The special “Plastic Tube Forum” exhibition will also be part of Tube 2016. Parts of Hall 13 will also be open for Wire 2016.

“At Tube 2016, U.S. and Canadian companies will also have the opportunity to exhibit within a North American Pavilion, organized by Messe Düsseldorf North America (MDNA) with the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA) and the Society of Manufacturing Engineers (SME) as co-sponsors,” the show organiser told ISMR.

## An eye on tube

Tube Düsseldorf, International Tube and Pipe Trade Fair, is the leading international event for its sector and a central international platform where global market leaders, innovators and other industry experts meet to gather information on current technologies and future trends in machinery and equipment for tube manufacturing and processing, tube accessories and the tube trade. Every two years, over 1,200 exhibitors and 33,500 visitors from around the world take part in Tube Düsseldorf.

At the last staging of Wire and Tube in 2014, more than 2,583 international exhibitors presented their product innovations to 72,000 trade visitors from 104 countries. The wire exhibitors occupied approximately 639,680 square feet of space – a 2% increase compared with 2012 figures. At Tube 2014, 538,190 booked square footage of total exhibition space represented a 4% increase compared with the event in 2012.

“Industry and our everyday lives are inconceivable without tubes and pipes, whether they be rolled, drawn, cast or welded. At the same time, the performance, standard of quality and cost-effectiveness of tubes and the systems used for manufacturing them are more crucial than ever before,” added the show organiser, Messe Düsseldorf GmbH.

At Tube 2016, the countries with the largest numbers of exhibitors are again Italy; the UK; the Netherlands; Austria; Switzerland; Poland; Spain; Turkey and Germany. The largest participation of overseas exhibitors are from the U.S.; India; South Korea and China.

“Tube 2016 covers the full range of pipe and tube manufacturing, processing, treatment and trade. The line-up also includes pipelines; OCTG technology; profiles and machinery and the Plastic Tube Forum (PTF). Halls 1 and 2 will focus on pipe accessories, while the pipe trade and pipe manufacturing can be found in Halls 2; 3; 4; 7.0 and 7.1. Like last time, the China Pavilion will be located in Hall 2,” added the show organiser.

Metal forming will occupy Hall 5, while Halls 6 and 7a will feature tube processing machinery. Mechanical engineering and construction will be presented in Hall 7a. Halls 1 to 7.0 will also include profiles for a wide range of applications. The special PTF show will be presented in Hall 7.1. ■
