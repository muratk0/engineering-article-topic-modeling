# TIMTOS CALLING

[TARİH: 01.01.2019 ISMR]

Don’t miss the 2019 Taipei International Machine Tool Show (TIMTOS) in Taiwan from 4-9 March 2019

The 2019 Taipei International Machine Tool Show (TIMTOS) will be held at the Taipei World Trade Centre and the Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre in Taiwan from 4-9 March 2019. The biennial show is the third largest machine tool trade show in the world and a key sourcing hub for metal and machinery professionals globally. The show includes an industry summit with international experts sharing their insights into the latest trends and is expected to host over 1,200 exhibitors and 55,000 visitors.

TIMTOS, co-organised by the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA) and the Taiwan Association of Machinery Industry (TAMI) is held once every two years. It is the second largest exhibition in Asia and the largest in Taiwan. As a comprehensive showcase for machine tools in Taiwan, TIMTOS is also one of the most important trade shows for buyers from all over the world to find products to suit their purchasing needs. To meet the demand and challenges from Industry 4.0, TIMTOS promotes innovative research and development, smart manufacturing and smart factories in Taiwan's machine tool industry. With the construction of Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre (Hall 2 will be completed in time for TIMTOS 2019), 1,700 more booths will be added to the show, increasing its exhibition scale to around 6,500 booths (the largest ever in its history). More smart machines incorporated with industrial robots, IoT, big data analysis, remote control and cloud computing can be expected at TIMTOS 2019.
