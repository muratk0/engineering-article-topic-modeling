## EVENT PREVIEW

In 2021, Italy produced 24.41 million tonnes of steel (source: Federacciai). It also imported 30 million tonnes of raw materials and steel products and exported 18.3 million tonnes.

## The show background

The 2019 show, according to figures validated by ISF (Institute for Certification of Trade Fair Statistical Data), was its best event with the largest number of exhibitors and visitors in the history of the event. The conference and exhibition at fieramilano Rho hosted over 12,000 square metres of exhibition space (+10% compared to the 2017 show) and over 300 exhibitors (+56% of overseas companies compared to the event in 2017).

It also registered over 16,500 visitors, up 12.3% compared to the 2017 edition. Overseas attendance reached 26% of the total. According to Grs Explori, overall satisfaction with participation in the event was positive for 83.2% of visitors and 91.1% of exhibitors.

Show organiser, siderweb, provides information, analysis and consultancy specialising in the steel sector. Established in 2001, it offers steel producers, distributors and industry players information, services and advice through its website and has gathered a community that includes the protagonists of the Italian steel industry.

The siderweb Research Department also generates in-depth studies, analysis and consultancy in the steel sector. Since 2009, it has been surveying the prices of 55 raw materials and steel products on the national market every week, with the method certified by the Laboratory of Statistics Data Methods and Systems of the University of Brescia. Since 2022, it has published five weekly indexes on scrap, carbon steel, long and flat products and stainless steel.

For the 2021 edition of Made in Steel, it provided live siderweb TV, streaming all the main events from the threeday conference and exhibition on the siderweb YouTube channel. This included in-depth analysis from steel experts; the Conference Room; interviews with exhibitors and visitors and daily discussions with operators. It streamed more than 20 hours of conferences live from the Conference Room, featuring interviews with the protagonists of the steel supply chain as well as videos by exhibitors. The YouTube channel from siderweb was viewed more than 7,000 times.

Digital MIS – Expoplaza (the Made in Steel 2021 digital platform supporting business), a virtual meeting place for visitors and exhibitors and the Made in Steel website were accessed by a total of 42,679 users, generating 134,086 page hits.■

## Made in Steel Awards

At every edition of the event, a specialist jury will confirm the winners of the Made in Steel Awards. Prizes are given to exhibitors and stand designers for the forcefulness and flair of their message and for the most effective, ground-breaking presentations of their products and services.

There are three award categories: Best Communication Stand, for the most effective advertising effort; Most Friendly Stand, for the most welcoming stand and Best International Stand, for the stand with the most international flavour.

The 2021 Made in Steel award winners are listed below:

Best Communication Stand: AFV Beltrame Group

Most Friendly Stand: Feralpi Group

Best International Stand: Acciaierie Venete
