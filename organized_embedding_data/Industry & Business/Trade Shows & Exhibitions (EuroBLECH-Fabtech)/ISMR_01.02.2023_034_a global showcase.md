# A GLOBAL SHOWCASE

[TARİH: 01.02.2023 ISMR]

Don't miss this year's Global Industrie event in Lyon, France, from 7-10 March 2023.

ISMR SAYS: “It incorporates five complementary exhibitions: MIDEST (industrial subcontracting); SMART INDUSTRIES (smart, efficient and digital industry); INDUSTRIE (manufacturing technologies and equipment); TOLEXPO (solutions and equipment for sheet metal working) and MEASUREMENT WORLD."

The event will take place at Eurexpo in Lyon, from 7-10 March 2023, as a global showcase for industrial innovation with more new developments and solutions. The event is under the patronage of the French President, Emmanuel Macron, and plays host to several ministers, elected officials, ambassadors and representatives of the largest European professional organisations in the world of industry.

"Global Industrie brings together all industry players in one place. Divided into 26 zones, specialising in fields such as digitalisation and automation; electronics; additive manufacturing & 3D printing; measurement; robotics; energy and sustainable development (to name a few), our 2,300 exhibitors can be relied on to be at this large international gathering," the show organiser commented.

"Global Industrie will be honoured to host not only the Chambers of Commerce and Industry of Bourgogne Franche-Comté and Seine-et-Marne and CCI France Internationale (Union of French CCIs abroad) but also the Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes Pavilion; Aveiro Regional Industrial Association (Portugal) and AIMMAP (Associação Dos Industriais Metalúrgicos Metalomecânicos E Afins De Portugal)," it added.

It incorporates five complementary exhibitions: MIDEST (industrial subcontracting); SMART INDUSTRIES (smart, efficient and digital industry); INDUSTRIE (manufacturing technologies and equipment); TOLEXPO (solutions and equipment for sheet metalworking) and MEASUREMENTWORLD.

Previous editions of the show have been held in even years in Paris and odd years in Lyon, France. This geographical diversification enables it to enhance both its offer and the variety of its visitors.

The Global Industrie awards will honour new exhibitor innovations. The show's priority in 2023, the organiser told ISMR, will be "to provide concrete answers to the contemporary needs of its players." Concrete solutions could include innovations developed by companies' new ideas from overseas or aid and support schemes offered by the state and local authorities (whether reshoring; job creation; the environmental transition; regions or industry appeal to young people).

## Meeting new challenges

This year, Global Industrie expects to welcome 2300 exhibitors and an expected 40,000 visitors from 85 countries.

“This Lyon edition will be a genuine platform [to showcase] the efforts of manufacturers battling ever more inventively against the crisis gripping Europe, and will answer the huge question: how can we respond to such a complex international situation whose repercussions directly affect French production chains?" it explained.

“At a time when everyone is facing growing problems every day, Global Industrie is committed to supporting manufacturers and will offer numerous avenues for industry to emerge stronger. The industry is mobilising. The industrial sector responded, from the start of the COVID-19 crisis, to supporting the national production effort, just as it responded to the recovery plan presented by the French government in the process. Its [companies], in all sectors combined, have also been invested in innovation for many years to meet the challenges of tomorrow," it concluded.

## A showcase for innovation

Global Industrie will highlight and reward the latest new developments and technologies in Lyon this year.

The show also allows visitors to see, touch and understand innovations and the latest new industrial processes in several 'experimentation' areas and 26 different zones.

"The event will enable visitors to monitor industrial trends and emerging signs that will help them to anticipate industry transformations more effectively. Global Industrie facilitates the creation of partnerships or alliances, links between research and industry, good relations between customers and suppliers and meetings between talented young people and companies," added the organiser.

Launched in 2021, eight channels in the GI Channels programme enabled hybridisation of the event, in which it was possible for visitors and exhibitors to participate both physically and digitally via the live broadcasting of highlights, conferences, feedback, first-hand accounts etc.

“This afforded an additional opportunity to do business, make video appointments with manufacturers unable to make the trip and promote activity more effectively. This initiative was very successful, with 40,000 views including 21,000 connections over the entire duration of the exhibition two years ago. There were video meetings on the MyGi portal and a range of channels broadcast live from the exhibition on the website," explained GL Events.

To assist visitors in organising their trips to the event, Global Industrie has also set up its Welcome World service. This also helps visitors to plan their trip and discover the exhibition as well as prepare a visit schedule. This includes a hospitality area and translation services for meetings.

Global Industrie will also be highlighting an event which will identify and reward talent in all industrial professions, the Golden Tech challenge.
