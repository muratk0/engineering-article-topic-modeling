# AMB Iran 2018 (26–29 June 2018) is an opportunity to develop new business and find new contacts in Iran.

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

ISMR SAYS: “There is great demand for modernisation and technical expertise in Iran”

Messe Stuttgart and the German Machine Tool Builders' Association (VDW) are organising a joint trade fair, AMB Iran, in Tehran from 26–29 June 2018. The aim of the two partners is to establish an international platform for manufacturing technology for the Iranian market and neighbouring countries now that sanctions have been lifted in Iran. AMB Iran will be held at the new trade fair grounds at the Shahr-e-Aftab Complex. Spread over 25,000m², they are modern trade fair grounds in Tehran with links to the airport, three major highways and the subway.

AMB Iran is an offshoot of AMB, the international exhibition for metalworking, one of the top five trade fairs worldwide for metal cutting technology. ISMR will be at the event as a media partner. AMB Iran 2017 surpassed expectations, with 5,736 specialist visitors visiting the stands of 202 exhibitors from 17 countries.

"Participation in AMB Iran is worthwhile. The number of exhibitors has almost doubled from 111 companies at the first event to 202 exhibitors in 2017; the number of visitors has tripled (2017: 5,736). 86 per cent of the exhibitors would recommend the trade fair. AMB Iran also struck the right chord with the visitors. 95 per cent plan on visiting the trade fair again and 93 per cent would recommend the event," the organisers told ISMR.

## Positioning for growth

Reiner Fries, sales director for Schwäbische Werkzeugmaschinen GmbH, discussed his motivation for going to Iran: "We have been active in Iran since 2016 and operate there with a local machine tool manufacturer as a partner. We still do not expect quick success. After the sanctions against Iran were lifted, we forged some initial contacts with potential partners. The reason for this is that we again regard the market as interesting for our company in the long term."

Sandvik Coromant will exhibit for the first time at AMB Iran. The market specialist for tools and tool solutions has already been active in Iran for many years and is represented by local partner, SAP (Sakht Abzar Pars Co).

"The objective of our participation in AMB Iran is to strengthen our customer relationships and build our brand for future business opportunities," said Valerio Rolando Vertua, Sales Manager Middle East, Sandvik Coromant.

AMB Iran 2017 surpassed expectations, with 5,736 specialist visitors visiting the stands of 202 exhibitors from 17 countries.

Ulrich Kromer von Baerle, President of Messe Stuttgart, confirmed that a large number of bookings had been received from local exhibitors: "Our presence and status as the most important exhibition for metal working companies are well accepted on the Iranian market. We have never had so many bookings from Iran at such an early stage. We are naturally monitoring current political events, but are confident about the economic development of Iran. It was pleasing to see that only recently Italy and Iran signed a general loan agreement."

The German Federal Ministry of Economics and Energy (BMWi) has again included AMB Iran in the official foreign trade fair and exhibition programme of the German federal government. Based on this financial promotion, exhibitors at AMB Iran will have the opportunity to present their company and their products in a German pavilion under the motto "Made in Germany".

Dr. Wilfried Schäfer, managing director of the German Machine Tool Builders Association (VDW), commented: "In the light of general conditions, developing sustainable business with Iran will only be successful by adopting a long-term approach. The opportunity to present their products and services in a German pavilion is therefore a great help for companies. It will enable them to get a foot in the door and forge and maintain contacts, even if sales are only slow at the beginning."

Companies from Turkey and China will also be represented by a pavilion at AMB Iran. Country delegations from Italy, under the auspices of the Association of Italian Machine Tool Manufacturers' Association (UCIMU), France and Taiwan have already confirmed that they will be taking part in the exhibition.

## Vocational education and further training

At AMB Iran, great importance will be attached to vocational education and further training. Messe Stuttgart and the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders' Association) are organising a special show on vocational education. At the prominently positioned special area, additional qualifications and opportunities for further training in the machine tool industry will be the main focus. The programme will be complemented by a forum and panel discussions with providers from the German education sector.

There is great demand for modernisation and technical expertise. This applies, in particular, to the automotive industry, oil and natural gas extraction, the energy industry, the construction industry and the banking sector. In a study, iMOVE confirms that, in the mediumterm, Iran has great potential for the export of German education.

Kristine Faenger, Regional Manager Iran at iMOVE, reported: "The German dual system, with its practical training elements, is a formula for success for Iranian companies and can support them in modernising training in Iran and adapting it to the requirements of industry. In Iran, great importance is attached to education. 'Made in Germany' has always been and still is a guarantee of quality for Iranian business partners."

Until now there have been only a few international providers in Iran. In addition to modern teaching equipment and technology, consulting services are also in demand. The current topics for international education providers also include the development of curricula and standards, teacher training and the implementation of training centres in Iran, as well as offers for online training.

In December 2017, a new law for comprehensive vocational education came into force in Iran, which also applies to nonuniversity training courses. With the targeted improvements in the cooperation of all involved and the introduction of a qualification framework, Iran is responding to the challenge of high unemployment, especially among young people. An important reason for this is 'the unsuitable or non-existent vocational education of young Iranians', according to iMOVE.

AMB Iran is supported by the Iranian Ministry of Education and the Technical and Vocational Training Organisation Iran (TVTO). Training programmes from the TVTO, the most important provider of technical and vocational education, strongly emphasise practical elements, unlike higher vocational schools," said the event organiser.

## EU matchmaking programme

AMB Iran 2018 is supported by Business Beyond Borders (BBB), a matching initiative of the European Commission. It is coordinated by Eurochambres, the Association of European Chambers of Commerce and Industry, with support from EMECA (European Major Exhibition Centres Association) and UFI (Union of International Fairs).

"Visitors and exhibitors should be given the opportunity to find appropriate partners to initiate business according to their needs and profiles, thus enabling all exhibitors and visitors to maximise the benefits of attending the show. We are confident that the BBB matchmaking event during AMB Iran will make a real difference," explained Barbara Weizsäcker, Secretary General of EMECA (European Major Exhibition Centres Association) and a member of the BBB consortium.

Exhibitors and visitors can register on an online platform in advance and create a profile. Appropriate business partners are brought together on request. Then, at the trade fair, the meeting can take place in a separate matchmaking area.

Ulrich Kromer, President of Messe Stuttgart, added: "For our international exhibitors, participation in AMB Iran means being present in the Iranian market again, being able to maintain existing customer contacts but also increasing the brand awareness for new business. With the matchmaking programme, we offer the opportunity to use the trade fair participation even more intensively and meet the right and, above all, qualified customers."

A joint German pavilion, as part of the German overseas exhibition programme under the auspices of the VDW (German Machine Tool Builders Association), will be part of the trade fair for the second time. In 2017, 68 companies used the opportunity to test the Iranian market on the pavilion's 1,000 square metres. It is now possible to register for the German pavilion in 2018. All conditions and registration documents are available online: www.amb-iran.german-pavilion.com/content/en/home/home.php
