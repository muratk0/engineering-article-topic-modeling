From software to machine tools, components and assembly, the entire industrial chain will be represented at Industrie Paris, the forthcoming industrial exhibition. The 50,000 m2 gross exhibition will be held from 22-26 March 2010 (Paris-Nord Villepinte, France) and is expected to attract 25,000 visitors and over 1,000 exhibitors.

“With over 1,000 exhibitors expected, Industrie Paris offers all the solutions in equipment, components, products and services for all stages of manufacture from design to production,” the organiser told ISMR.

The previous event in the series, INDUSTRIE Lyon, succeeded in retaining the confidence of its exhibitors who invested heavily in the exhibition. The event opened on 10 March 2009 with almost 1,000 exhibitors over a net exhibition surface area of 23,500 sq. m. Visitors filled the aisles from the first day and, by the time the exhibition closed on Friday 13 March, it had attracted a total of 21,000 professional visitors (an increase of 3% over 2007) – 8% of them international visitors from Switzerland, Italy, Germany and Belgium.

A meeting place for key players in the French mechanical-engineering sector, headed by Fédération des Industries Mécaniques chairman Yvon Jacob, the 2009 exhibition was honoured with a visit from French Secretary of State for Industry and Employment, Luc Chatel. ## TEN-IN-ONE

INDUSTRIE Paris comprises ten complementary and targeted exhibitions:

■ Machine Tool: International exhibition of production equipment for the mechanical industries

■ Form & Plate: International exhibition for the working and forming of sheet-metal and tube

■ Welding: International Exhibition devoted to welding solutions and technologies

■ Control France: International exhibition for measurement and quality assurance

■ Inter Expo Tool: International exhibition of cutting / shaping tools for metals, plastics and composites

■ SITS: The international exhibition for surface treatments and coatings
