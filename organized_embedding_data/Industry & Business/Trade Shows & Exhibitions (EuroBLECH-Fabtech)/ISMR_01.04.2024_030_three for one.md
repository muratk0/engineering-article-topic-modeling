# THREE FOR ONE

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

Live test zones; equipment demonstrations; conferences; roundtables; debates and workshops will characterise this year's ITM Industry Europe trade show.

ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE this year promises an arena of new products; working machines; robots and solutions for intralogistics plus a range of meetings. This year's edition of the trade show will be held from 4–7 June 2024 at MTP Poznań Expo in Poland. As well as stands showcasing the latest manufacturing technologies, it will feature various demonstrations, conferences, roundtables, debates and workshops aimed at resolving industrial challenges.

Visitors will also be able to visit two other exhibitions alongside the show: the Modernlog Trade Fair of Logistics, Transport and Storage and the Subcontracting Industrial Cooperation Fair. The three trade fairs, collectively, will highlight various industrial trends such as robotics; automation; Industry-4.0; industrial logistics etc.

"For years, the event has been a key venue for meetings and technology/innovation exchange among innovation sector leaders. People come to Poznań to see the latest live solutions for various industry sectors and take part in conferences, debates and meetings with experts," commented the show organiser, the MPT Group.

## An eye on growth

"Innovations, Technologies and Machines, or ITM in short, fully reflects the spirit of the ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE trade show. This is the heart of our trade show; there is never silence as you can hear the noise of working machines used every day in factories. Investors come here to see robotic workplaces, as well as technological innovations. We will build stages in the halls for shows, competitions and debates as well as prepare a tailor-made programme. The largest TECH STAGE in Poland will once again be created in Poznań and will take up twice as much space this year," announced Anna Lemańska-Kramer, Director, ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE.

The exhibition area/available floor space at this year's trade show will also be larger.

"We are grateful that most of our exhibitors included participation at the trade show in their marketing strategy much earlier. This allowed us to dedicate a larger number of halls. This includes industry specialists such as ASTOR; TRUMPF; FANUC; MAZAK; DEMATEC; AMADA; KIMLA and many other leading companies that set high standards in the industry," added Anna Lemańska-Kramer.

Over four days, the ITM Industry Europe trade show in 2023 hosted 15,156 professionals (a 30% increase on 2022 figures). Modern industry solutions were presented by 690 companies (a 20% increase in the number of exhibitors compared to 2022). Visitors represented manufacturing companies, mainly the machine and metal industry (31%), the automotive industry (12%) and the construction sector (10%).

## Key industry topics

The show organiser also highlighted key industry topics at the trade fair, including industrial automation and Industry 4.0.

"Industrial automation solutions are an integral part of virtually all production processes and largely determine the development of the global industry. The need to develop automation in Polish companies results from the desire to increase competitiveness, improve working conditions, develop sustainably and adapt to the dynamically changing business environment. Research and innovation in this area is crucial to achieving economic success and maintaining high quality production," explained the MTP Group.

At Speakers Corner, the event's formula of meetings with experts on the stand taken by the editorial office of MM Magazyn Przemysłowy monthly publication, will be a feature of ITM INDUSTRY EUROPE. The MM Speakers Corner will take place every day from 1-4pm.

On a specially prepared stage, representatives from the editorial office of MM Magazyn Przemysłowy monthly will address managers of some of the largest industrial companies in Poland about individual industrial sectors in the country, as well as the latest trends in industrial production, automation and cooperation between companies.
