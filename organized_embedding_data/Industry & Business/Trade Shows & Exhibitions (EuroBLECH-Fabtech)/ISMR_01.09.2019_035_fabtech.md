# FABTECH

[TARİH: 01.09.2019 ISMR]

North America's largest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing event heads to McCormick Place in Chicago from 11-14 November 2019. FABTECH provides a convenient 'one stop shop' venue where visitors can meet with suppliers, see the latest industry products and developments, and find the tools to improve productivity, increase profits and discover new solutions to metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs.

More than 48,000 attendees and over 1,700 exhibiting companies will gather to showcase the industry's latest trends and technologies. A number of special events are also scheduled for FABTECH. While at the show, make plans to attend the keynote presentations, expert panel discussions and networking events, including FABTECH Industry Night.

The FABTECH Event Partners include five associations: the American Welding Society (AWS); the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association, International (FMA); SME, the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI).

## Show highlights

FABTECH will encompass three halls at McCormick Place to showcase a wealth of innovation and technology solutions that are transforming the metal manufacturing industry.

Specialty Pavilions will offer a first-hand introduction to the machinery and fabricating trends that are transforming everything from small job shops to the largest Fortune 500 manufacturers. The show floor will host two featured pavilions which boast innovations in 'Robotics & Industrial Automation' and '3D/ Additive Manufacturing'.

##   Robotics & Industrial Automation

Pavilion: Robotics and automation are fuelling the future of manufacturing. "Of all the industries that utilise robotics, the fabricated metal parts and welding industries have the strongest correlation to robots used in manufacturing processes. This pavilion will showcase new technology and products making businesses more productive and profitable," explained FABTECH.

##   3D/Additive Pavilion: Leading additive manufacturing technologies – including 3D printing, rapid prototyping and direct digital manufacturing (DDM) – will be on full display in this pavilion. Advances in this technology have seen its use become far more widespread, offering exciting possibilities for future development.

"Over the past several decades, major shifts in the manufacturing environment have proved beneficial for domestic manufacturers. Industry statistics show that there is a rise in non-traditional manufacturing operations, especially among small and medium-sized companies. Much of this work continues to require human beings in conjunction with automation. FABTECH will give you insights on current trends in workforce development, provide insights into how to manage workforce needs and shifting skills, and report on solutions to the workforce gap," explained the show organiser.

"Reshoring – bringing operations back to United States’ shores – is becoming increasingly common among manufacturers. Attend FABTECH to talk with sources about the economic impact of manufacturing in the US, the growing interest in working with U.S.-based manufacturers, the supply chain impact and more."

## IoT, tariffs and gender diversity

Manufacturers are increasingly leveraging the Internet of Things (IoT), which entails the interconnection of devices within an existing Internet infrastructure, to achieve a variety of goals including cost reduction, increased efficiency, improved safety, meeting compliance requirements and product innovation. FABTECH aims to give visitors the latest updates on factors driving the growth of IoTs – widely available Internet access, smaller sensors and cloud computing.

"Economists are split on the impact of the effects of newly-implemented tariffs. Many believe that tariffs will have a negative impact on manufacturing, while some believe they could positively contribute to the reshoring of manufacturing jobs. Meet with FABTECH's industry experts to get their perspective and quotes on this looming issue," added FABTECH.

The show floor will host two featured pavilions which boast innovations in ‘Robotics & Industrial Automation’ and ‘3D/Additive Manufacturing’

"Manufacturing has seen a steady rise in women playing key roles in the industry from production and assembly lines to the C-suite. Today, women make up nearly one-third of the industry workforce and are shaping the next generation of manufacturing to be more gender-diverse, innovative and rewarding. Visit FABTECH to meet these prominent women shaping the manufacturing industry and discover creative ways to attract and retain a diverse workforce."

## Keynote speakers

Several keynote presentations will also be held during FABTECH. These include the FABx Tech Talks. These TED Talk-inspired sessions will honour Veterans Day with a powerful line-up of visionary military veterans.

"Drawing on their unique experiences, each speaker will captivate and inspire you with their frontline stories focused on motivation, team building, innovation and how to achieve excellence in the business battlefield," added FABTECH.

"Renowned international cybersecurity and fraud-prevention expert, Frank Abagnale, will also present a two-part keynote. During the first part of his speech, appropriately titled 'Catch Me If You Can', Abagnale will provide entertaining insight into his life as the notorious imposter of the 1960s. He will then instruct attendees on how to protect themselves and their businesses from fraud during the second part entitled the 'Art of the Steal'," it told ISMR.

Grant Imahara, former host on Discovery’s Mythbusters, will also provide a behind-the-scenes look at his work on various sets and productions while also touching on the importance of advancing the engineering/ manufacturing industry.

## Leadership exchange sessions

A number of leadership exchange sessions will also be held at FABTECH. These include:

##   Emerging & Advanced Technologies for Manufacturing 2020: Advanced manufacturing is transforming the economy and jobs, using the latest technology and new manufacturing processes to accelerate innovation. Learn how companies are using advanced technologies to stay at the forefront of manufacturing.

##   The Impact of Disruptive Technology and the Future of Manufacturing: As technology has advanced and become embedded in plants, warehouses and throughout the supply chain, businesses have needed to adapt to stay competitive. Many already have, but the path towards digital transformation is different for every company. A panel of experts will cover disruptive technologies from 5G, IoT to AI, Robotics and Automation, share case studies and explore the challenges and benefits of implementation.

##   21st Century Workforce: Manufacturers in the 21st century must make it a precedence to have a workforce that is educated, diverse and motivated. Organisations need to produce a more globally competitive workforce that can meet the demands of an evolving digital economy and adapt to the trends and challenges of managing a multigenerational workforce. Experts will share their strategies and some actions to address the challenges for the future.

## Professional welders' competition:

Professional welders will demonstrate their skills and compete for cash and other prizes during the AWS Professional Welders Competition on 11 and 12 November 2019. Participants will go head-to-head as they complete and clean a single-pass fillet weld in just five minutes.
