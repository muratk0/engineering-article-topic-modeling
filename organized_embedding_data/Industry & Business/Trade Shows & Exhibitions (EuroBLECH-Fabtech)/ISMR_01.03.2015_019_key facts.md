“India is on the way to becoming a manufacturing location of global status and takes sixth place on the list of the world’s largest machine tool markets. The country’s rising importance means that many users and manufacturers of machine tools focus resources on developing their position in India,” said the organiser, Inter Ads Brooks Exhibitions (India) Pvt Ltd.

“Next to the huge market potential, the high level of investment by foreign companies is a good indicator of the subcontinent’s attractiveness not only to global players but also to mid-tier suppliers of sheet metal machines, tools and systems.”

BLECH India has made significant progress since its inaugural show. Between 2009 and 2013, exhibitor participation has doubled. In 2009, the first edition of the show, 90 exhibitors took part in BLECH India. At the 2013 show, some 187 exhibitors from 16 countries showcased the latest machinery and solutions for the sheet metal working industry. The exhibition had grown by more than 300 per cent in floor space, from 855m2 in the 2009 show to more than 4,000m2 at the 2013 event. BLECH India 2013 attracted 5686 trade visitors (98 per cent from India).

“BLECH India 2013 showcased a large variety of machines, tool and solutions for the complete sheet metal forming and fabrication process; an enormous amount of live machine demonstrations were on display. The majority of the exhibitors were Indian companies; major exhibitor countries from outside India were Germany, Italy, China and Switzerland. For foreign exhibitors, this dedicated trade exhibition for India’s sheet metal working industry proved an opportunity to gain new contacts and develop their business relations in the buoyant Indian market,” explained the organiser.

Key industry sectors to which the visitors at BLECH India 2013 belonged included sheet metal products (27%), mechanical engineering (25%), the automotive industry (6%), steel and aluminium construction (6%), electrical engineering/appliances (5%) as well as iron and steel production (5%).

BLECH India 2015, with its specialised exhibition profile, targets specific trade visitors from industry sectors including mechanical engineering, sheet metal products, the automotive industry and its suppliers, iron and steel production, household appliances, steel and aluminium construction, rolling mills as well as shipbuilding and aerospace.

## Fastener Fair India

Fastener Fair India, the trade show exclusively dedicated to fastener and fixing technologies, returns to the Bombay Exhibition Centre for the third time from 23-24 April 2015. The exhibition covers a wide product range of industrial fasteners and fixings, assembly and installation systems, storage and logistics services, fastener manufacturing technology and construction fixings.

“The outlook for the fasteners market in India remains positive. The Indian economy is constantly evolving, benefitting from an emerging middle class, cost competitiveness and strong domestic consumption. Poised to become a global innovation hub, India is predicted to become the world's fifth largest consuming country and the third largest economy in the world by 2020,” said the organiser, Inter Ads-Brooks Exhibitions.

The show is poised to attract a large number of Indian as well as international companies to the Bombay Exhibition Centre in Mumbai. Optimism for doing business in India is rising and the popularity of the show has driven the organiser to extend the show floor space. Companies exhibiting from outside India include Germany, Italy, Turkey, China, Taiwan and Malaysia.

Fastener Fair India 2013 attracted 4,028 trade visitors and 145 exhibitors. The majority of exhibitors were Indian companies and almost a third of the exhibitors were from outside India, namely China, Taiwan, Germany, the UK, Italy, Vietnam and Korea.

Visitors at Fastener Fair India 2013 were mainly fastener and fixings manufacturers (36%); users (29%); distributors/suppliers to users (19%) and wholesaler/supplier to resellers (16%). Key industry sectors to which the visitors belonged were the automotive industry (16%); mechanical engineering (15%); hardware/DIY retailing (9%); electrical products/domestic appliances (8%) and construction (6%).

Fastener Fair India is part of the Fastener Fair series of targeted trade exhibitions for the fastener and fixings industry which includes Fastener Fair Brasil, Fastener Fair Turkey, Fastener Fair Russia and the flagship event Fastener Fair Stuttgart, Europe’s leading international trade fair for this industry sector. ■
