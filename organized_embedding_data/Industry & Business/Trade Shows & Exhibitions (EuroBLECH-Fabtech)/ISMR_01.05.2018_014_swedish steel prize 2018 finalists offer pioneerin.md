# Swedish Steel Prize 2018 finalists offer pioneering designs

[TARİH: 01.05.2018 ISMR]

THE SWEDISH STEEL Prize 2018 finalists have been named, revealing four innovative design concepts that utilise high-strength steel to achieve high performance. The winner will be announced at a ceremony in Stockholm on 24 May 2018.

"We have nominated four finalists with quite different perspectives on innovation that the jury found mind-blowing. They range from completely new product features to applications which challenge traditional design by using modern materials," said Eva Petursson, chair of the Swedish Steel Prize jury and head of SSAB's strategic R&D.

The four finalists are:

CTE Spa, Italy, has developed a small truck-mounted sky-lift of great versatility, height and reach. The compact boom system has an elevated slewing mechanism that provides the added capability of manoeuvring around corners, giving access to confined spaces. A carefully considered design as well as the optimisation of all boom cross sections and use of high-strength steel made this possible.

JAK Metalli Oy, Finland, has invented a cutting principle for the cleansing of roadside vegetation that provides 'a much more controlled operation for small trees than conventional chain crushers'. The innovative design utilises the potential of high-strength and wear-resistant steel. This has resulted in a lightweight cutter head that allows for high productivity and a safer work environment, combined with extremely low energy consumption as well as operational costs, said the Swedish Steel Prize jury.

Mantella S.r.l., Italy, has made a design and innovative solution for a trailer and body that represents an entirely new generation. It was made possible by skilled design work and smart production methods in combination with high-strength and wear-resistant steel. This new lightweight trailer has improved stability, low operational costs as well as high transport efficiency, explained the Swedish Steel Prize jury.

With a modular design concept using almost no welding, Trufab Global, Australia, has created a flexible and lightweight grain auger wagon that enables final assembly to be done on-site. By utilising modules, different sizes can be delivered, while maintenance is also simplified. This new chaser bin 'reduces cost and increases production efficiency for producers as well as end-users'.
