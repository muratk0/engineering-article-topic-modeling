# Turkish technologies

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

WIN EURASIA 2022 at the Istanbul Expo Centre in Turkey (8-11 June 2022) encompasses six trade fairs under one roof – CeMAT EURASIA; IAMD EURASIA; Industrial Energy Systems EURASIA; Metalworking EURASIA; Surface Technology EURASIA and Welding EURASIA.

“Exhibitors and visitors to WIN EURASIA will have a unique opportunity to see and experience the 360-degree manufacturing industry. From sheet metal processing to metal forming technologies; automation services to electric and electronic equipment, hydraulic & pneumatic services to intralogistics, WIN EURASIA will bring together all the ecosystems needed for the future's factories," said the show organiser.

"Metalworking parts (which include metal forming and shaping, surface treatment technologies and machine elements) now come under the one umbrella heading of 'Industrial Manufacturing Machines' at the show. The Compressed Air Systems and Compressors sector, which will be at the exhibition after a two-year hiatus, is also preparing to present its latest technologies to visitors. Turkish machinery manufacturers have shown particular interest in the event," it added.

Turkish machinery exports reached US$ 22.4 billion in 2021. The WIN EURASIA show organiser aims to contribute to the sector's export target achievements with its Special Qualified Hosted Buyers' programme. It will be held this year in cooperation with MAiB (Machinery Exporters' Association), MAKFED (Turkish Machinery Federation) and Deutsche Messe.

WIN EURASIA Hybrid 2021 was Turkey's first hybrid industry event held from 10-13 November 2021. Around 467 physical and 80 digital exhibitors from 16 countries met 30,728 visitors from 78 countries over the four days of the show. With the support of the Hosted Buyer Delegation Programme from the Ministry of Trade and the global network of Deutsche Messe, more than 250 buyers from 16 countries (such as Russia; Egypt; Tunisia; Morocco; Iraq; Bulgaria; Czech Republic; Iran and Qatar) met Turkish manufacturers to discuss new export deals.
