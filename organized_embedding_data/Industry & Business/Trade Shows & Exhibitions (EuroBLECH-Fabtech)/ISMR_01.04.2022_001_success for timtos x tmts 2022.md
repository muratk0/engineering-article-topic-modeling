# Success for TIMTOS x TMTS 2022

[TARİH: 01.04.2022 ISMR]

TIMTOS x TMTS 2022, the first co-branded machine tool mega show in Taiwan, was hailed as a “huge success" by its organisers. The physical exhibition was held in Taipei Nangang Exhibition Halls 1 and 2 over six consecutive days, while the online exhibition remained open until 21 March 2022. The show has attracted more than 40,000 online and offline visitors in Taiwan and overseas. The online exhibition reached visitors from more than 20 countries/regions.

"With 950 exhibitors in 5,100 booths, TIMTOS x TMTS is not only the largest trade show in Taiwan since the outbreak of the pandemic but also the world's first machine tool mega show in 2022. In response to border controls, the show featured an array of online services for international visitors and media, including an on-site guide for online visitors; 'Sourcing Taiwan Machinery'; an on-site guide for the media tour; 'Live Tour @ Showground'; 'Media Eye on TIMTOS × TMTS'; 'Podcast Live' and TIMTOS x TMTS Online," explained the show organiser, TAITRA.

“Live Tour @ Showground, Media Eye on TIMTOS x TMTS and Podcast Live delivered daily live updates from the show floor. The videos and podcast episodes have enjoyed 25,000+ views and listens. Heavyweight attendees were guided to the showground and made real-time connections with selected exhibitors during 500 online sessions. Processing machines, multi-axis machining centres and laser cutting machines were amongst the most sought-after items for overseas buyers," it added.

The Taiwanese machine tool industry keeps up with the development of future trends and accelerates digital transformation and upgrades. New models and solutions have targeted emerging business in sectors such as semiconductors, green energy, electric vehicles, health care and aerospace. This year's TIMTOS x TMTS welcomed a wide spectrum of visitors from different industries.

TIMTOS is scheduled to return in March 2023.
