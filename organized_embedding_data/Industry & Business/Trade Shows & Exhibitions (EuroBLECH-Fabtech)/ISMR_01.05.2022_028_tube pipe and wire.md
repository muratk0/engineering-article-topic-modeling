# TUBE, PIPE AND WIRE

[TARİH: 01.05.2022 ISMR]

Innovations from the wire, cable and pipe industries can be seen at Wire and Tube in Düsseldorf from 20-24 June 2022.

ISMR SAYS:

"Exhibitors will present new machines and equipment as well as sustainable solutions for e-mobility, digitalisation and Industry 4.0."

Innovations in wires, cables, tubes and pipes will be presented at leading trade fairs 'Wire and Tube' from 20-24 June 2022 at the Düsseldorf Fairground in Germany. Exhibitors at Wire will present their technological highlights in exhibition halls 9 to 15, whilst Tube exhibitors will be in halls 1 to 7a (a combined show space of 100 square metres).

Over 1,700 key players from the industry will showcase technologies to thousands of visitors from the wire, cable and pipe industries over the five days of these busy trade fairs.

Guest event METAV, International Trade Fair for Metalworking Technologies, will be held from 21-24 June 2022 in exhibition halls 16 and 17. METAV is organised by the VDW, German Machine Tool Builders' Association.

## Key products and services

“There will be a lot of innovations in Düsseldorf from end products in fasteners and springs, an ecoMetals campaign with guided tours to exhibitor stands to expert meetings with keynote speeches by key industry players. Exhibitors will present not just new machines and equipment but also increasingly sustainable solutions for e-mobility, digitalisation and Industry 4.0. Start-ups with their ideas and collaborations for wire, cable, tube and pipe production complement the comprehensive range of products and services in the 13 exhibition halls," the show organiser, Messe Düsseldorf, told ISMR.

More than two thirds of the exhibitors come from overseas; exhibitors from 67 countries came to the 2018 pre-events. Trade visitors from over 130 countries meet at the Düsseldorf Fairgrounds at peak times.

“The show app is ideal for preparing visits to the trade fair and, more importantly, it can be used anytime and anywhere," explained the show organiser. "The interactive site map and hall plans are guides for finding your bearings at the Exhibition Centre. A simple touch of a button takes you into the individual halls, allows you to gradually zoom into individual stands and then view an exhibitor's entire data as well as the products that they offer. All exhibitor and product details on the entire Tube database can be searched by a full-text search routine," it added.

The free app can be downloaded on Google Play or the Apple App store.
