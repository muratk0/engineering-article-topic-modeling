# TAIWAN CALLING

[TARİH: 01.01.2022 ISMR]

Don't miss the new TIMTOS x TMTS 2022 machine tool show in Taipei, Taiwan, from 21-26 February 2022.

TIMTOS x TMTS 2022, Taiwan's 'largest and only co-branded machine tool show', will be held at Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre (Halls 1 and 2) from 21-26 February 2022. Around 900+ exhibitors are expected to pack the exhibition halls with 5,200 booths.

"It will not only be the largest trade show in Taiwan since the outbreak of the pandemic but one of the most significant events for the global machine tool industry in early 2022," the show organiser told ISMR.

Aside from metal cutting machine tools, machining centres and metalforming machine tools, the TIMTOS x TMTS 2022 joint event will also showcase a line-up of smart machines. Smart machinery is part of the Taiwanese government's "five plus two" innovative industries plan.

In response to travel restrictions, TIMTOS x TMTS 2022 will also launch various online services and activities in conjunction with the physical exhibition, including an on-site guide for online visitors; Live Tour Showground; Live Stage for product launches; virtual displays and one-on-one online procurement meetings.

## A key milestone

For the first time ever, two major machine tool exhibitions in Taiwan, the Taipei International Machine Tool Show (TIMTOS) and Taiwan International Machine Tool Show (TMTS), will be held at the same time, as TIMTOS x TMTS 2022.

"The show will create a '1+1 is greater than 2' synergy for the industry and boost the international status of Taiwan's machine tool industry," the organiser explained.

Total exports of Taiwanese machine tools from January to November this year reached US$ 2,525 billion, a massive increase of 29.5% over the same period last year. TIMTOS and TMTS, two major exhibitions in Taiwan, recently reached consensus to work together to host both shows at the same time, at the same venue, with the common goal to help Taiwanese companies explore global markets.

Over the years, the two trade shows have been held separately in Taipei and Taichung and kept growing in scale. However, due to the pandemic and venue issues in Taichung, both TIMTOS 2021 and TMTS 2020 were presented online instead of as physical exhibitions.

"As the global machine tool industry is recovering from the pandemic and demand is increasing, industry players are eager for physical events. TIMTOS and TMTS are working together to maximize trade opportunities for exhibitors and visitors.

Undoubtedly, this cooperation marks a milestone of unity for Taiwan's machine tool industry. The alliance between TIMTOS and TMTS in 2022 represents the development and R&D capacity of Taiwan's smart machinery industry." added the show organisers.

## TIMTOS 2021

Taiwan, which is ranked as the world's ninth-largest manufacturing nation, is a major producer in the machine tool industry with about 1,950 companies based on the island and over 43,000 people employed in the sector.

Although the COVID-19 pandemic has had a great impact on various industries globally, Taiwan's machine tool industry is built on strong foundations. Combined with the smart manufacturing transformation and the interconnection of upstream and downstream resources, the industry occupies an important position in the global industrial supply chain.

Nearly 1,000 exhibitors participated in the TIMTOS 2021 Online event. Machine tool manufacturers present their latest products and various seminars, workshops and recorded tutorials actively promoted this new type of exhibition format.

Taipei in Taiwan (Shutterstock.com).

In 2019, TIMTOS hosted a record 1,230 exhibitors from 26 countries with 7,000 booths.

Exhibitors used 3D, VR and videos to display their latest products and connect with global buyers through online procurement conferences, online real-time matching, video conferences etc. Industry fora and a Factory tour were also held during the event. The virtual show offered intuitive user interfaces; interactive search functionality; onsite tours; live streams from the summit; online procurement meetings; digital fora; a guide for online visitors; the e-official directory and, for pre-registrations, virtual gold coins for a lucky draw.

The event was originally planned as 'TIMTOS 2021 Hybrid' in a pioneer format as a virtual and physical trade show. Around 7000 online visitors and 40,000 onsite visitors, and over 200 procurement meetings, were expected by the show organisers at the TIMTOS 2021 Hybrid combination. However, COVID-19 concerns led to the cancellation of the physical trade show, although the virtual show (TIMTOS 2021 Online) still went ahead.

In 2019, TIMTOS hosted a record 1,230 exhibitors from 26 countries with 7,000 booths, positioning it among the top three machine tool trade shows in the world. The six-day exhibition attracted a total of 53,777 professional visitors, of whom 7511 were from overseas.

## Manufacturing in focus

At an international press conference for TIMTOS × TMTS 2022 on 2 December 2021, the show organisers—James C. F. Huang, Chairman of TAITRA (Taiwan External Trade Development Council); Larry Wei, Chairman of TAMI (Taiwan Association of Machinery Industry) and Harbor Hsu, Chairman of TMBA (Taiwan Machine Tool & Accessory Builders' Association)—sat next to two industry giants (Jimmy Chu, Chairman of Fair Friend Group and Jui-Hsiung Yen, Chairman of TTGroup).

They all took part in a CEO panel discussion on the theme "Impacts, Challenges and Opportunities for the Global Machine Tool Industry after the Pandemic."

Chairman Huang of TAITRA confirmed that "Taiwan plays a crucial role in supporting the global machine tool supply chain, as Taiwan is renowned for its customisation and innovation." Huang pointed out that the TIMTOS x TMTS mega show will showcase a wide array of R&D innovations from high-profile manufacturers in Taiwan.

TAMI's Chairman Wei added that the supply chain has fundamentally changed since the global pandemic and "created an opportunity for Taiwanese players to explore new business in the post-pandemic era." Meanwhile, TMBA's Chairman Hsu encouraged continuous progress towards intelligent manufacturing, SaaS and interface unity.

Taiwan, the world's fifth-largest machine tool exporter, provides smart total solutions to the world with its solid foundation in IoT, AI and Big Data technologies. In the post-pandemic era, Taiwan continues its journey towards Industry 4.0. Taiwan's machine tools have introduced not only intelligent technologies and flexible solutions but also green concepts to smart machinery. Sustainability is a key concern and Taiwan's machinery industry is committing to the promotion of sustainable technology.

Emerging industries, for example electric vehicles, have had a great impact on the global machine tool supply chain to date. Jimmy Chu, Chairman of Fair Friend Group, and Jui-Hsiung Yen, Chairman of TTGroup, shared their experiences and options for enterprise transformation in the wake of such opportunities and challenges, demonstrating the resilience of Taiwanese companies when dealing with supply chain issues.

Aside from Fair Friend Group and TTGroup, other exhibitors at the 2022 event will include HIWIN; PMI; Victor Taichung; YLM; SHINBU; KFO; CHMER; FANUC; MAZAK; YOU JI; TAKISAWA; YCM; TRUMPF; Delta; Mitsubishi and Siemens.

"The machinery industry has become Taiwan's third trillion-dollar industry, behind the semiconductor and panel industries. With increasing COVID-19 vaccination rates and growing demand, it is expected that Taiwan's machine tool industry exports will hit a new record in the next few months," added the show organisers.

"Industry 4.0 is a key industry trend. Taiwan is the third largest exhibitor country at the EuroBLECH sheet metal exhibition in Germany (out of 50 countries). More than 60% of Taiwanese exhibitors at EuroBLECH are already doing smart manufacturing so our industry offers a lot of smart manufacturing benefits. 5G and smart manufacturing is the way to go for Taiwan's industry," added Jimmy Chu, CEO of Fair Friend Group.

## An eye on smart machinery

In response to emerging global trends, Taiwan Excellence (supported by Taiwan's Bureau of Foreign Trade) invited five Taiwan Excellence Awardwinning machine tool manufacturers to demonstrate their intelligent manufacturing solutions at a webinar held alongside the EMO Milano 2021 international trade fair for machine tools. Companies participating in the webinar were Tongtai Machine & Tool; Fair Friend Enterprise (FFG); Buffalo Machinery (AXILE); L&L Machinery and HIWIN Technologies. Entitled 'Beyond Smart', the webinar welcomed 48 media outlets and 171 buyers from around the world. A total of 47 Taiwanese manufacturers signed up to attend the physical EMO Milano 2021 exhibition (including HIWIN, AXILE and FFG).

During the webinar, Mr. Yung-Hsiang Lai, Director of Taiwan's Smart Machinery Promotion Office, highlighted that the COVID-19 pandemic has enhanced the importance of smart manufacturing and concepts such as intelligence and automation are now key trends.
