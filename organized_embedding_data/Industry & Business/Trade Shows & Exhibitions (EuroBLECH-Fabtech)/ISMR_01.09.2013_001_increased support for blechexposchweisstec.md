# Increased support for Blechexpo/Schweisstec

[TARİH: 01.09.2013 ISMR]

AS OF 25 August 2013, 1029 companies had confirmed their participation at the 11th Blechexpo and 4th Schweisstec event (5-8 November 2013 at the Stuttgart Exhibition Centre). A total of roughly 430,000 square feet of net floor space was occupied in 2011 (915,000 sq. feet overall), and these figures have already been exceeded this year with over 430,000 square feet of net floor space and roughly 970,000 square feet overall.

“This can be traced back to considerably more overseas exhibitors – Italy takes second place to Germany in this respect with just under 100 exhibitors. As usual, Switzerland is comparatively well represented with more than 40 exhibitors, followed by Turkey with 20. All in all, the 11th Blechexpo and the 4th Schweisstec have added more than 200 new exhibiting companies,” the organiser, Paul E. Schall GmbH & Co. KG, told ISMR.

A new special show entitled “Top of Automobile” will be held during the 11th Blechexpo and 4th Schweisstec. Participants at this will include more than 20 companies from technological disciplines for the production of vehicles components such as stampings, body components, body shells, exhaust systems, wheels as well as the manufacturers themselves who will present their products.

As a further highlight, TRUMPF (a leading international manufacturer of sheet metal processing machines) will present the world of stamping technology at a large, special booth also located in Hall 4, covering simple equipment for newcomers right on up to high-end stamping technology for complex sheet metal parts as components of multifunctional assemblies.

For further details, see www.blechexpo-messe.de or www.schweisstec-messe.de
