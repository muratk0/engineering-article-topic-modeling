# Don't miss Poland's Industrial Spring

[TARİH: 01.11.2023 ISMR]

The Industrial Spring is a series of exhibitions at the Kielce Exhibition and Congress Centre in Poland from 19-22 March 2024. The previous industrial exhibition cluster took up all seven halls so one further temporary exhibition hall has been added for the 2024 event.

The Industrial Spring Cycle showcases the latest products and solutions at highly specialised exhibitions such as STOM-TOOL; STOM-BLECH & CUTTING; STOM LASER; STOM-ROBOTICS; STOM-FIX; WELDING; WIRTOPROCESSES; EXPO-SURFACE; KIELCE FLUID-POWER; TEiA Electronics and Automation Expo; CONTROL-STOM and 3D PRINTING DAYS.

"2023's STOM series and accompanying events hosted 19,000 visitors, and the show lasted four days. The comprehensive exhibition covers several industry sectors which is particularly interesting for visitors. Industrial Spring has mainly hosted representatives of manufacturing companies. The industries most often represented are machinery, automotive and armaments," confirmed the show organiser, Targi Kielce S.A.

"The Exhibition of Metal Processing Technologies includes presentations of modern CNC machines, machine tools and milling machines, complemented with displays of sheet-metal forming and processing equipment; metal cutting, laser and laser technology equipment; welding and industrial robots plus 3D printers. At this 2024's Industrial Spring, there will also be seminars on metrology, lasers and technologies for Industry-4.0," it concluded.
