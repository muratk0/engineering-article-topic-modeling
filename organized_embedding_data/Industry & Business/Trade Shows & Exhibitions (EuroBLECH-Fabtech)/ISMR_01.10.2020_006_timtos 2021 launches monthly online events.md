# TIMTOS 2021 launches monthly online events

[TARİH: 01.10.2020 ISMR]

THE TAIPEI INTERNATIONAL Machine Tool Show (TIMTOS) will be held on 15-20 March 2021 in the Taipei Nangang Exhibition Centre (Halls 1 and 2) and the World Trade Centre Hall 1. With the support of domestic and overseas machinery manufacturers, the number of booths is expected to reach 7000.

In the run-up to the show, the organisers, the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA) and the Taiwan Association of Machinery Industry (TAMI), will regularly launch online events with different themes every month.

“Starting from this August, TIMTOS Glimpse and TIMTOS Salon will be aired on a regular basis to assist industry players to promote new models, grasp key global market trends and catch up with global business opportunities,” the show organisers told ISMR.

Already aired this August was the first episode of TIMTOS Glimpse, in which Hiwin Technologies Corp., a ball screw manufacturer, was interviewed. Then, broadcast on 10 September 2020, was an in-depth report on the 70-year old hydraulic press equipment manufacturer, Lien Chieh Hydraulic Industrial Co., Ltd. (LCH).

The next instalment, released on 24 September 2020, featured Buffalo Machinery Co., Ltd., a firm specialising in the development and manufacturing of high-end machine tools.

“TIMTOS Salon events focus on inviting important machine tool association representatives from around the world to share first-hand information on local markets. After a successful launch discussing the current situation in the U.S. and Turkish markets in August, the TIMTOS Salon continues its virtual trip around the world in September.

“On 17 September 2020, the third episode of TIMTOS Salon featured Mr. V. Anbu (Chairman of the Indian Machine Tool Manufacturers’ Association - IMTMA), who discussed the current situation and trends in the Indian manufacturing industry during the COVID-19 pandemic. India is the third largest export destination for Taiwan’s machine tools,” added the show organisers.

From October to November 2020, TIMTOS Salon and TIMTOS Glimpse will continue to release new videos on Thursdays from the second to the fourth week of each month which are easily accessible.

TAITRA has also been intensively preparing for a TIMTOS online exhibition to meet the needs of exhibitors and buyers. Expected to be launched soon, the online version of TIMTOS will debut in an O2O mode together with the physical exhibition.

For the latest information, see the TIMTOS website at www.timtos.com.tw and the TIMTOS Facebook page.
