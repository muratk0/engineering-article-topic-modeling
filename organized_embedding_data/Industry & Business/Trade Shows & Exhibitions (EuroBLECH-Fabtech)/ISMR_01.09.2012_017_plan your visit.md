# PLAN YOUR VISIT

[TARİH: 01.09.2012 ISMR]

With a total of 1,500 exhibitors from 41 countries presenting the latest machines, tools and systems for sheet metal working, EuroBLECH 2012 (the 22nd International Technology Exhibition for Sheet Metal Working) is set to be the business barometer for the global sheet metal working industry.

Bigger than the previous event, this year’s EuroBLECH occupies eight halls offering even more space for live demonstrations of innovative machinery and future production trends. At EuroBLECH 2010, 1,455 exhibitors from 43 countries showcased a wide range of innovative solutions, technologies and live machine demonstrations. In total, 61,500 trade visitors from 98 countries visited the event.

## Sheet metal hub

EuroBLECH is the central hub for the sheet metal industry on a global scale. From 23-27 October 2012, in Hanover (Germany), suppliers of products and services for sheet metal working will converge with trade visitors from all over the world, ready to invest.

Comprehensive information for visitors planning their trip to EuroBLECH 2012 is now available. The recently published visitor brochure includes key show details, a site map, the exhibition profile as well as useful travel information. The leaflet is available in twelve languages, and can be ordered via the exhibition website at www.euroblech.com

The monthly online newsletter, ‘EuroBLECH Bulletin’, includes news about the upcoming event, as well as short reports on markets, research, products and trends in sheet metal working. As the exhibition gets closer, visitors will find useful tips about the show and information about the products and services on display at EuroBLECH 2012. Free subscription to the EuroBLECH Bulletin is available at www.euroblech.com

The EuroBLECH 2012 catalogue can be purchased on-site during the exhibition. You can also order the catalogue in advance via the online shop from August 2012 at www.euroblech.com

Entrance tickets for EuroBLECH will be available at the online shop from August 2012. Tickets can also be purchased on-site during the exhibition.

The world’s largest sheet metal working technology exhibition attracts top industry professionals from all over the world. It predominantly targets all sheet metal working specialists at all management levels in small and medium-sized companies as well as large businesses: i.e. design and production engineers, quality managers, buyers, manufacturers, technical directors and experts from research and associations. They come from key industry sectors, including mechanical engineering; sheet metal products, components, assemblies; steel and aluminium construction; automotive industry and related suppliers; heating, ventilation, air conditioning; electronics industry; precision engineering; medical technology; aerospace and shipbuilding. 99% of visitors to EuroBLECH 2010 were trade visitors. An impressive 64% of visitors had decision-making capacity and 48% had concrete investment plans, the organiser confirmed.

## New for 2012

“For the first time ever, EuroBLECH is hosting a Job Board for vacancies and job seekers in the sheet metal working industry. Participating in this career market will enable job seekers to reach a worldwide employer base within the sheet metal working technology industry. To make use of this opportunity, candidates can register and, upon approval, post their details on the EuroBLECH website. Exhibitors can post vacancies via their Online Marketing Packages. For job seekers, this service is free of charge,” EUROBLECH organiser, Mack Brooks Exhibitions, told ISMR.

For more details, please see www.euroblech.com/english/job-market

EuroBLECH 2012 is also planning to launch a special new exhibition area focusing on “Protection against Product Piracy, Trademark and Patent Law”. Suppliers of product protection technologies as well as associations, governmental bodies, consultants and lawyers can now apply for stand space in this special exhibition area.

EuroBLECH 2012 will host the second International Seminar on Hot Sheet Metal Forming Technology. This seminar will be held from 24-25 October 2012 at the Hanover Exhibition Grounds. It will take place at the Convention Centre within the exhibition grounds of Deutsche Messe AG. Registration for this seminar is only valid in conjunction with the purchase of an admission ticket to EuroBLECH 2012.
