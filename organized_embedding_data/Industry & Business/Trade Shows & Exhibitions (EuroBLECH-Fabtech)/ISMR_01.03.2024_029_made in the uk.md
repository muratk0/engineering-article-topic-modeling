# MADE IN THE UK

[TARİH: 01.03.2024 ISMR]

This April's MACH 2024 trade show will feature live machinery demonstrations, new Knowledge Hubs, a seminar programme and a range of different zones.

Don't miss MACH 2024 in Birmingham, UK, from 15-19 April 2024, is an important showcase event for

the UK's engineering-based manufacturing technologies, bringing key decision-makers under one roof across a five-day period. Over 26,000 visitors and 500 exhibitors are expected to attend the event, sponsored by Lloyds Bank, at the NEC exhibition centre in Birmingham, UK. It will showcase live, digital production systems and a range of machinery, software and systems for modern manufacturing.

"Resolving the current challenges facing the industry—soaring energy prices; rising labour costs; skills shortages; the escalating price of raw materials; supply chain disruption and the need to effectively tackle the green agenda by seizing the opportunities posed by decarbonisation will all receive special focus. Entry to MACH is free and, once inside the exhibition halls, visitors will find the UK's latest metalforming, metalworking and manufacturing technologies," said show organiser, the Manufacturing Technologies Association (MTA).

"MACH will feature the largest display of new machinery and manufacturing technologies under one roof in the UK. MACH's ability to connect manufacturing engineers, decision-makers, buyers and specifiers with suppliers of new technology, equipment, services and processes is key to its success. It is why the event consistently attracts important stakeholders in such large numbers. Attracting an audience of more than 26,000 people to meet and network with around 500 exhibitors, business attributed to MACH in 2022 amounted to more than £180m," added the association.
