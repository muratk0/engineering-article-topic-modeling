# MADE IN SPAIN

[TARİH: 01.04.2024 ISMR]

The 32nd edition of BIEMH, the International Machine Tool Exhibition, will take place from 3-7 June 2024 at the Bilbao Exhibition Centre (BEC) in Bilbao, Spain. BIEMH is a leading industrial trade fair and an international show for advanced manufacturing. This global event will showcase a range of machinery, and large machines in operation will be the stars of the show. BIEMH 2024 is organised by AFM; Machine Tool Manufacturers; AIMHE; Machine Tools Importers and the Bilbao Exhibition Centre, with the support of SPRI. The Basque Government is an Institutional Partner.

The event has already registered more than 1,300 exhibitor companies from 29 countries (including Germany; Italy; Portugal; the USA; Japan and Taiwan). In addition, around 2,500 products and equipment from the main sectors have already been confirmed. These include the manufacture and distribution of starting and forming machinery; metrology; production services; components; tools and accessories as well as robotics, automation and digitalisation. Robotics, automation and digitalisation will be allocated special areas at BIEMH.

Visitors will be able to see products, practical demonstrations and specialised presentations in Halls 4 and 6 of the BEC using an easy and sector-specific route. In addition, to facilitate direct contact and encourage interaction between exhibitors and visitors, specific activities such as guided tours, networking meetings and other initiatives have been designed to enrich the experience of all participants.

## Programme and show features

The BIEMH 2024 organisers have negotiated a solid network of alliances and work closely with the main associations in the user sector, as well as key players in the industry.

The biennial event also features a varied programme of activities, including networking spaces, guided tours and a series of BIEMH

Talks over three days. The latter will take place on 4, 5 and 6 June, bringing together international experts from leading companies to discuss technical and strategic issues and delve into the trends, challenges and solutions in automation & robotics, additive manufacturing and digitalisation. Through a series of conferences, keynotes and roundtables, these experts will also provide their own experiences and present their success stories first-hand.

"Mexico will be the 'Invited Country' at the 32nd edition of BIEMH. This country usually has a very significant presence at the event and represents a strategic market for the industry. This position has now been enhanced following the signing of the T-MEC agreement and the nearshoring policy it is implementing. About 50 Mexican purchasing companies have already confirmed their participation. Most of them represent the automotive sector in various states, but professionals from the aerospace, appliance, moulding and distribution sectors have also been confirmed," said the show organiser.

The alliance is the result of the intense work that BIEMH's organising team has been doing in Mexico in collaboration with sectoral associations, local companies and the Basque Trade & Investment (BTI) office in that country. The purpose of these contacts is to cement the link between the event and Mexico, and to promote long-term business relationships between companies exhibiting at the show and Mexican buyers, and vice versa.

Within this framework, BIEMH's organisers will offer a webinar about Mexico and the business opportunities that it offers weeks before the show opens.

This event also aims to optimise the results of any show visits.

During the trade show, BIEMH TV will also broadcast live interviews; testimonials from professionals; summaries of the conference; show images; company videos and an agenda of events.

BIEMH targets professionals working in automotive; aerospace; aeronautics; capital goods; metal construction; energy generation; domestic appliances; household goods; rail; cutting & plate-work subcontracting; mould and dies as well as the gas & oil industry.

"The visitor promotion campaign is currently being held in around 140 countries. As part of this effort, the Hosted Buyers' Programme will once again be a major attraction at the event. This initiative includes the requests of exhibitors, guaranteeing the presence of hundreds of strategic professionals with whom to close business meetings," the show organiser told ISMR.

Alongside BIEMH, new editions of BeDIGITAL (a forum dedicated to the application of digital technologies) and AdditED (an international trade fair for additive manufacturing and 3D printing) will also take place simultaneously in the BEC halls. In addition, true to its commitment to the development of talent, WORKinn Talent Hub (a meeting point between talent and industry) will also be held.

In the previous edition of WORKinn Talent Hub held by BIEMH, more than 100 new employment contracts were signed by young professionals as well as professionals looking for new job opportunities.

As sustainable events, the three gatherings (BIEMH, BeDIGITAL and AdditED) will focus on six action areas: mobility; energy; water; procurement; infrastructure and waste.
