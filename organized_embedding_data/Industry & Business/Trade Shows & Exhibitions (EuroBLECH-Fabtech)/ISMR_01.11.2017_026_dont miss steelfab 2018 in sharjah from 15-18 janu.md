# Don't miss SteelFab 2018 in Sharjah from 15-18 January 2018

[TARİH: 01.11.2017 ISMR]

The 14th SteelFab 2018 will be organised and hosted by Expo Centre Sharjah (UAE), with the support of the Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry, from 15-18 January 2018. It will showcase machinery and equipment in metalforming; surface preparation & finishing; grinding & cutting; machining and other allied engineering disciplines. It will also feature Italian, German and Taiwanese pavilions, and enjoys support from leading industry associations from Italy, Germany, Taiwan, the UK and the UAE.

SteelFab 2017 attracted 342 exhibitors from nearly 30 countries, as well as 7,950 visitors from 83 countries, during its four-day run. SteelFab 2018 aims to attract large numbers of steel industry professionals and decisionmakers among exhibitors and visitors, and also features a seminar programme.

The event is an opportunity for businessmen, investors and experts to review the latest equipment and machine tools for steel fabrication. The event attracts worldwide attendance and participation from companies and leading international brands. ISMR is an international media partner for the event and will be distributed throughout the exhibition - you can also meet ISMR at the event on booth 3052 in Hall 5.

## New global partnerships

"For the first time, SteelFab will be bringing German excellence in technology, engineering and innovation to the region through our tie-up with Messe Essen and the International Tube Association (ITA)," the event organiser, Expo Centre Sharjah, told ISMR.

"Foreign partnerships have always been the mainstay of SteelFab. In our latest endeavour, the tie-up with Messe Essen will see the launch of a special pavilion, the Essen Welding & Cutting Pavilion, while the partnership with ITA will help us focus more on pipe and tube machinery and tools. Both the tie-ups are important to us since they underscore our commitment to introduce better technology and machinery to the region," said H.E. Saif Mohammed Al Midfa, CEO, Expo Centre Sharjah.

The tie-up with Messe Essen was announced at the Schweissen and Schneiden exhibition in Düsseldorf recently. Messe Essen and ITA join the list of supporting overseas industry associations such as UCIMU SISTEMI PER PRODURRE (Association of Italian Manufacturers of Machine Tools, Robots, Automation Systems and Ancillary Products), DVS and AUMA for the official German pavilion, the Taiwan Association of Machinery Industry and the UAE Contractors' Association.

"Messe Essen organises the Schweissen & Schneiden fair, one of Europe's largest gatherings of welding, cutting and finishing industry professionals, while the ITA is the world’s largest and most influential association for the tube and pipe industries. They are names to reckon with globally and add great value to SteelFab. The deals with Essen Messe and ITA will go a long way to helping us attract bigger names in the industry, not only from Germany but also from across Europe," added H.E. Al Midfa.

"Non-oil trade between the UAE and Germany stood at more than US$ 15.5 billion in 2015, and US$ 10.9 billion in the first nine months of 2016, according to figures by the UAE Ministry of Economy. In 2015, the UAE attracted US$ 2.8 billion in foreign direct investment from Germany," added the show organiser.

The association with ITA is expected to elicit a good response, with the Pipe & Tube Machinery segment showcasing a wider spectrum of international suppliers and machinery makers for visitors from industries such as oil & gas; pipeline; power & water; MEP; food manufacturing; electrical, marine & offshore; furniture; construction & infrastructure; heavy industries and manufacturing. The tie-up with ITA will help the association’s 900 members from more than 70 countries to present equipment, innovations, machines and technology for the regional tube and pipe industry.

"Increasing foreign support will also boost participation from countries such as Italy,
Taiwan, Turkey, India and China, as well as Germany," added the organiser.

## Strong economic tailwinds

SteelFab 2018 will also feature special segments such as Wear Resistant; Welding & Cutting and Machine Tools; seminars and technical presentations and live displays. The Third Fasteners World Middle East will also be held, a dedicated vertical for the complete range of industrial fasteners and fixings, assembly and installation systems, fastener manufacturing technology as well as storage and logistics services.

"The show has a lot to gain from strong economic tailwinds that include a projected jump of 14% in non-oil contribution to its GDP in four years due to rapid diversification of its economy – the UAE remains the most competitive economy in the region and 17th most competitive economy globally – and increasing spending on mega events like the World Expo 2020," added the organiser.

"According to the International Monetary Fund, the UAE's economic growth is set accelerate to 4.4% in 2018, driven by a rebound in investment, manufacturing and trade, the fastest in the region."

Technology for Pipe and Tube will be reflected on the show floor with a complete array of pipe forming; pipe mills; tube bending; bevelling; orbital welding; cold cutting; induction heating and more being showcased. The pipe and tube machinery section formed an integral part of SteelFab 2017, featuring nearly 60 exhibitors from Italy; the Netherlands; Portugal; France; Japan; the US; Germany; Turkey; Finland; Canada; Taiwan; Poland; the UK; Denmark; India and the UAE, accounting for one-sixth of total exhibitors at the show.
