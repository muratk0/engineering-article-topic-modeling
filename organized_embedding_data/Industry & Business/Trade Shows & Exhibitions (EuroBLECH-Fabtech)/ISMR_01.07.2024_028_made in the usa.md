# MADE IN THE USA

[TARİH: 01.08.2024 ISMR]

FABTECH will be held for the first time in Orlando, USA, from 15-17 October 2024. Two halls, and a comprehensive conference programme, will showcase the latest metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing technologies, processes and practices.

ISMR SAYS:

“Events at FABTECH 2024 will include the Weld-Ed conference; seminars; a professional programme; society events and the FABx Tech Talks at the FABTECH Theatre."

FABTECH

The leading fabricating, welding and finishing event, FABTECH, heads to Orange County Convention Centre in Orlando (USA) from 15-17 October 2024. With over 800,000 square feet of exhibits, the trade show will encompass two halls where attendees will find a variety of technology pavilions dedicated to Forming & Fabricating; Tube & Pipe; METALFORM; Welding; Finishing; 3D/Additive Manufacturing and Automation.

“FABTECH provides a convenient 'onestop shop' venue where visitors can meet suppliers, see the latest industry products and developments, and find the tools to improve their productivity, increase profits and discover new solutions to metal forming, fabricating, welding and finishing needs," said the show organisers.

FABTECH is made possible by its five event partners, all of whom represent the varied and diverse make-up of the manufacturing industry in North America. They include the American Welding Society (AWS); the Chemical Coaters Association International (CCAI); the Fabricators & Manufacturers Association International (FMA); the Precision Metalforming Association (PMA) and SME.

"Registration is now open to attend FABTECH and see the most comprehensive showcase of sheet metal manufacturing processes and the latest developments in fabrication, from over 1,300 suppliers, in what is considered the tenth best state for manufacturing as presented in Florida's 2023 Manufacturing Report. Last year's event in Chicago welcomed over 45,000 attendees representing 80 countries and all 50 states," added the show organisers.

## A new venue

"We are excited to take FABTECH to Orlando this year and let the growing Florida manufacturing base see the event up close for the first time," said Tracy Garcia, CEM, FABTECH group director at SME. "Orlando is a perfect hub for manufacturers from across the United States to attend FABTECH to see live equipment demonstrations, build strategic partnerships, find solutions and stay at the forefront of what's next in manufacturing."

To support the future workforce in this manufacturing discipline, new on the FABTECH agenda this year is an Emerging Leaders programme for young professionals including networking, a workshop and a keynote address. 'Women of FABTECH' will return in 2024 featuring an inspiring keynote speaker who will share how to further your career in the industry.

"The education offered at FABTECH truly adds value to every attendee's experience," said Mark Hoper, FMA senior vice president of expositions & media. “The event gives professionals, from small job shops to the largest Fortune 500 manufacturers, first-hand access to the expertise and knowledge they need to expand skill sets and ensure the future success of their business."

## FABTECH conference

FABTECH offers a comprehensive conference to transform careers and business operations. The FABTECH Conference combines 60-90 minutes sessions and half-day workshops covering the latest in advanced fabrication and welding technology, workforce and management topics. Pick and choose the sessions right for you from 14 tracks and build an agenda to provide the key insights and solutions to your manufacturing and production challenges.

Led by some key powerhouse speakers, the FABTECH Conference will provide insights, spotlight innovative strategies and offer solutions to manufacturing and production challenges.

"Each day will feature a morning keynote speaker and an afternoon panel discussion to provide insights, spotlight innovative strategies and offer solutions to ongoing workforce challenges. As part of the conference, professionals will also be able to earn credit toward professional certifications offered by SME, the Fabricators and Manufacturers Association and the American Welding Society by attending educational sessions during the event," added the show organisers.

The American Welding Society (AWS) will present a comprehensive line-up of welding education. Led by top professionals, programmes will focus on best practices and new commercial developments in welding.

FABTECH events include the Weld-Ed conference, seminars, professional programme, society events and the FABx Tech Talks at the FABTECH Theatre. This year's FABx will feature a powerful line-up of speakers who are embracing change and navigating through uncertainty. These experts will discuss how the key to success lies in transforming fear and doubt into innovation and growth.

The Leadership Exchange Panel is entitled "Advanced Manufacturing: Integrating New Technologies & Materials for The Fabrication Industry". This dynamic discussion will delve into the innovative practices and materials redefining the manufacturing landscape. Expert panellists will unpack the complexities and advantages of integrating these advances into current manufacturing processes.

## In conclusion

"The FABTECH series of events welcomes more than 65,000 total visitors from over 90 countries every year. High-level company executives, manufacturing and engineering professionals as well as business owners from a range of major industries attend FABTECH to discover new technologies and source products to improve their business," the show organisers explained.

"Technical education sessions, dynamic keynotes, expert panel discussions and other special events keep show visitors returning to FABTECH every year. Admission to the FABTECH 2024 exhibit floor is complimentary for industry professionals who register before 12 October 2024," it continued.

FABTECH is held annually in the United States, rotating between Las Vegas, Chicago and Orlando. FABTECH Mexico also occurs annually rotating between Mexico City and Monterrey. FABTECH Canada takes place biennially in Toronto.■
