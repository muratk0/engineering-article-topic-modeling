RUSSIA IN VIEW

Don't miss Metallobrabotka 2020 from 10-14 August 2020 in Moscow, Russia.

Metalloobrabotka, the international exhibition for equipment, instrument and tooling for the metalworking industry, is a major international show for the machine tool and metalworking industry. It is not only the largest trade show in Russia and the CIS for the machine tool and metalworking industry, but also ranks amongst the world's top ten leading machine tool trade shows.

The show is organised by Expocentre AO and Stankoinstrument (the Russian association of machine tool manufacturers) and supported by the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade, the Council of the Federal Assembly of Russia and the Russian Engineering Union, under the auspices of the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry.

Taking place at the Expocentre AO in Moscow, Russia, from 10-14 August 2020, it showcases innovative research trends and developments in machine tool building and the latest solutions for the machine-building industry. The 2020 event is expected to attract over 1,100 exhibiting companies from 33 countries.

## Pavilions and processes

“Metalloobrabotka is remarkable for its wide range of material processing aspects. Key showcases include metalworking machines; forging and press equipment; tooling; new-generation intelligent machine tool systems; technological equipment and components and the latest software etc.," the show organiser, EXPOCENTRE AO, told ISMR.

Technical equipment will be demonstrated in the halls to visitors, so that visitors can see it in action. In 2019, the exhibition was split into two sections: 'Welding and Related Technologies' and 'Robotics and Industrial Automation'. The 'Additive Manufacturing/ Industrial 3D Printing sector' pavilion has also grown, according to the show organiser.

The trade show features a strong presence from both Russian and international stakeholders. Nine national pavilions will be showcasing capabilities from countries such as Belarus, China, Czech Republic, Germany, Italy, Slovakia, Switzerland, Taiwan, UK.

## Employment opportunities

The Employment Centre at the 2020 exhibition, created by EXPOCENTRE jointly with the Association of Recruitment Consultants with support from the Vybor Group, offers promising opportunities for both exhibitors and visitors to the exhibition.

Exhibitors are able to place a company's job vacancies on the Centre's stand, read the CVs from applicants, find the necessary specialists and obtain professional advice on labour market conditions.

The exhibition also features the 'Expocentre for Counterfeit-Free Exhibitions' project aimed at minimising the violation of exclusive rights to intellectual property. In 2020, for the first time, the exhibition will expand and move the 'Industry 4.0' and 'Surface Processing Equipment and Technologies' sections to a separate zone.

## An eye on 2019

Last year's Metallobrabotka exhibition featured an exhibition area of 42,385 square metres (+3%, compared to 2018) and attracted 1,186 exhibiting companies from 33 countries and 35,096 visitors (+8%, compared to 2018).

The official opening ceremony was attended by Russian Deputy Minister of Industry and Trade, Vasiliy Osmakov, as well as President of the Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Sergey Katyrin. Several heads of machine tool associations also attended such as Georgy Samodurov, President of the Stankoinstrument Russian association of machine tool manufacturers, and Christoph Blattler, Secretary General of the Swiss machine tool manufacturer association (SWISSMEM). Klaus-Peter Kuhnmunch, Manager of General Affairs at VDW (the German machine tool builders' association), Mao Yufeng, President of CMTBA (China machine tool & tool builders association) and Arten Bolgen, Deputy Secretary General at the MIB Association of Turkish Machine Manufacturers were also present, among others.

Ambassador of India to the Russian Federation, D. Bala Venkatesh Varma, and Ambassador of Switzerland to the Russian Federation, Yves Rossier, also visited the exhibition.

The joint stand of the Taiwan External Trade Development Council (TAITRA) showcased the latest equipment and technologies from major Taiwanese companies. The stand also hosted various events, speeches from representatives from leading companies and a VR press conference.

The Mosprom centre for support of foreign trade activities in manufacturing and agricultural industries organised a stand for Moscow machine building companies under the 'Made in Moscow' brand. The stand brought together 16 leading Moscow companies to demonstrate their latest metalworking products, lasers, additive manufacturing and products and solutions for industrial automation. Twenty technologies were showcased. In association with the Moscow City Government, representatives from Moscow industrial manufacturers held 110 business meetings with potential customers, investors and technological partners.

In 2019, Metalloobrabotka presented two new sectors: 'Robotics and Industrial Automation' and 'Welding and Related Technologies'. The Additive Manufacturing/Industrial 3D Printing sector has also grown. These three showcases, said the show organiser, were a great addition to supporting events on promising trends in Russian metalworking and machine building enterprises.

The 'Science, Vocational Education and Production' section also significantly expanded. In 2019, it showcased seventeen of Russia's leading universities including the Bauman Moscow State Technical University and the Moscow State Technological University Stankin. Stankin premiered its design of a digital unmanned production facility, universal CNC systems AxiOMA Control and Perspektiva, as well as a 3D model of a milling and engraving machine developed by the staff and students at the university.

The Employment Centre at Metalloobrabotka offered the latest job offers from exhibiting companies, posted CVs and offered professional career development consultations. The centre was run by Expocentre AO, the Association of Recruitment Consultants and Stankoinstrument, and powered by Vybor Group.

The Expocentre for Counterfeit-Free Exhibitions project, aimed at minimising counterfeit products showcased at exhibitions and illegal copying of new exhibited products and developments, also operated online.

The Stankoinstrument Association and ITO Publishing House prepared special issues of the Metalloobrabotka newsletter (in Russian only) on the Russian exhibition and the most important challenges facing the industry.

Metalloobrabotka 2019 featured around 30 supporting events with participation from representatives of the Russian authorities, relevant ministries, large and small businesses, leading industry associations and research centres, and prominent experts. The main event was the 9th International Forum on Materials Processing Technology, Robotics and Industry 4.0, organised by the Stankoinstrument Association and Expocentre AO, supported by the Russian Ministry of Industry and Trade, and assisted by the Moscow State Technological University Stankin and the Russian Association of Robotics (RAR).

The Moscow State Technological University Stankin and the Association of Digital Innovations in Machine Building organised the 'Digital Innovations in Machine Building' discussion platform, with the support of Expocentre AO and the Stankoinstrument Association. The main topic at the event was digitalisation. The Association of Digital Innovations in Machine Building signed cooperative agreements with several Russian companies. The parties agreed to cooperate on technologies and digital innovations in machine building.

The Moscow State Technological University Stankin organised events on industrial additive manufacturing. One important event was the 'International Conference on Automation and IT'. Its participants included students, postgraduate students and young researchers at Stankin, Russian Technological University MIREA and the Bauman Moscow State Technical University.

The Moscow State Technological University Stankin also held the 'Russian Conference on Digital Economy: Technologies, Management, Human Assets'.

The State Marine Technical University of St. Petersburg took an active part in the exhibition. The University's Institute of Laser and Welding Technology presented its innovative developments. It also took part in two supporting events: 'The Latest Laser Technologies for Materials Processing' and 'Advanced Solutions for Welding Production'.

The Russian Chamber of Commerce and Industry, Russian Export Centre AO and Expocentre AO organised a Consultation Session on Export Support for the Metalworking Industry.

In 2019, one of Metalloobrabotka's newcomers was welding specialist, Kemppi. The company organised the 'Kemppi Day: Industry 4.0: Transformation of Welding Production'. It was devoted to the industry's global trends, welding of the future, digitalisation, robotics and quality control. Kemppi also organised masterclasses by sculptor, Yury Barsukov, who showed visitors how to create amazing metal figurines using a welding machine.

Sandvik Coromant also showcased several solutions. The 'Sandvik Coromant Day' conference was devoted to new tooling solutions from Sandvik Coromant, factors that increase the productivity of end milling machines, online applications, the 4.0 digital revolution and the latest solutions and technology from Sandvik Coromant for industry.

Other supporting events included the '5th Russian Symposium on Metal Forming Technologies'; the 'Round Table on Industrial Lubricants and Fluids in Metallurgy, Metal Processing and Machine Building 2019'; the 'Conference on Industry 4.0' and 'Tebis CAD/CAM Programme Conference on Industry 4.0: Transforming Assembly Plant into Smart Factory Workshops' for exhibiting companies.
