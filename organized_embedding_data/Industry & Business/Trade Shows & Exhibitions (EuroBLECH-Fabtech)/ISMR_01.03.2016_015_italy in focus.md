# ITALY IN FOCUS

[TARİH: 01.03.2016 ISMR]

Don’t miss this year’s LAMIERA exhibition from 11-14 May in Bologna

This year’s LAMIERA will take place from 11-14 May at the Fiera Bologna exhibition ground in Bologna, Italy. Promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, the association of Italian manufacturers of machine tools, robots and automation systems, the exhibition is organised by CEU-CENTRO ESPOSIZIONI UCIMU.

The international exhibition dedicated to the metal forming machine tool industry and all related technologies, with a focus on sheet metal, also boasts a themed area dedicated to materials and carpentry, sheet metal, steel tapes, metal tubes and sections, semi-finished and finished products. This is designed to give visitors a wide and comprehensive overview of the sector, from materials to treatment and machining.

ISMR SAYS:

The event offers a full programme of conferences as well as Lambda 4.0, an area dedicated to the topics connected with “smart management” of factories, Industry 4.0 and all possible applications for SMEs (small and medium-sized enterprises) working in the metal forming technology sector. There will also be B2B meetings with qualified foreign operators and end-users, including a delegation from Mexico (the “Country of Honour” for LAMIERA 2016). These focused meetings will encourage more contact between exhibitors and potential customers.

## A sheet metal focus

“In 2014, the world production of metal forming machine tools reached almost 20 billion euros, remaining at the same level as the previous year,” said the event organiser. “With an 11 per cent production increase (2,180 million euros), Italy achieved the most remarkable growth among major players in the sector, affirming its competence and strengthening its third place in the international rankings.”

The last LAMIERA, which took place at BolognaFiere from 14-17 May 2014, ended on a positive note. With 18,620 visitors (826 of which were from outside Italy) and 370 exhibitors, 40 per cent of whom were from outside Italy, the event occupied an overall exhibition space of 35,000 square metres.

The international character of the event, promoted by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE, was confirmed by the strong presence of exhibitors from Germany; UK; Spain; the USA; Switzerland; Holland; Austria; France; Turkey; Belgium and Ireland as well as Australia; China; Denmark; Finland; Japan; Greece; Monaco; Romania and Taiwan.

“BLECH ITALY, an area dedicated to the steel, metal alloy and non-ferrous metal manufacturing chain, and in which approximately 50 companies took part, was introduced at the event. Conceived to showcase two contiguous branches, the machine tool and the steel and alloy processing machine sectors, the area proved to be extremely interesting,” added the event organiser.

Populated by prestigious international entities from the academic, research, enterprise and start-up worlds, the LAMBDA SOSTENIBILITÀ area, designed to demonstrate the commitment of the sector towards eco-compatible production, fuelled debate on the opportunities and methods for machinery and connected technologies, capable of combining social, environmental and economic sustainability needs. The main attraction was the Alfa Romeo 4C, designed by the Centro Ricerche Fiat, displayed at the heart of the area as the representative object of these new production techniques. The conference programme, run alongside LAMIERA, was attended by approximately 170 operators.

The number of bilateral meetings, involving about 40 Italian companies and many operators from foreign delegations, reached approximately 400. Invited by UCIMU-SISTEMI PER PRODURRE and ICE-Agenzia, the event was attended by representatives from Brazil; India; the United States and Turkey. Also invited independently by the association were delegations from Canada, Croatia and Serbia. These were joined by the delegation from Russia, the country of honour of LAMIERA 2014, invited under the auspices of the ‘Machines Italia in Russia’ project, promoted by the Italian Ministry for Economic Development and FEDERMACCHINE. ■
